### 4. MATERIALS OF CONSTRUCTION

**Reference:** 4-SPC-0001 (Material Specification), 4-SPC-0002 (Pump Specification), 5-LST-0003 (Approved Vendor List)

*   **Casing:** Stainless Steel 316L
    *   *Justification:* Wetted part. Although fluid is non-corrosive, 316L SS is specified as a robust standard for process fluids to ensure longevity, compatibility with potential minor impurities, and future process changes, as per B Star Chemetics standard 4-SPC-0001 for general process service.
*   **Impeller:** Stainless Steel 316L
    *   *Justification:* Wetted part. Consistent with casing material for hydraulic stability and corrosion resistance.
*   **Shaft:** Stainless Steel 316L
    *   *Justification:* Wetted part. Provides strength and corrosion resistance.
*   **Baseplate:** Carbon Steel
    *   *Justification:* Non-wetted part. Standard construction material for pump baseplates.
*   **Special Requirements:** None. Fluid is non-corrosive, and no active risk flags are identified.

### 5. MECHANICAL SEAL SPECIFICATION

*   **Type:** Cartridge Mechanical Seal
*   **API Plan:** API Plan 11 (Flush from pump discharge)
    *   *Justification:* Provides adequate cooling and lubrication for the seal faces using the process fluid, suitable for clean, non-corrosive fluids.
*   **Seal Faces:** Silicon Carbide (SiC) vs. Silicon Carbide (SiC)
    *   *Justification:* Excellent hardness and wear resistance for reliable operation with MEG.
*   **Secondary Seals (Elastomers):** EPDM
    *   *Justification:* Proven compatibility with Monoethylene Glycol (MEG) and suitable for the design temperature.
*   **Barrier Fluid:** Not required (API Plan 11 utilizes process fluid flush).
