### 6. VENDOR REQUIREMENTS

**6.1. Vendor Confirmation:**
The Vendor shall confirm the following:
*   Pump performance (TDH, flow, efficiency, NPSHr) at rated conditions.
*   Specific material grades for all wetted parts (Casing, Impeller, Shaft) compatible with 95% MEG at 65°C, referencing B Star Chemetics Standard 4-SPC-0001 and 5-LST-0003.
*   Motor sizing and compliance with API 610 margin (1.25x BHP).
*   Mechanical seal arrangement and barrier fluid system (API Plan 53B) details, including barrier fluid type, pressure, and temperature.
*   Compliance with API 610 (latest edition) for centrifugal pumps in petrochemical service.

**6.2. Vendor Submittals (Minimum Requirements):**
The Vendor shall submit the following documents for review and approval:
*   Certified pump performance curves (Head vs. Flow, Efficiency vs. Flow, BHP vs. Flow, NPSHr vs. Flow).
*   General Arrangement (GA) drawings of the pump and motor assembly, including dimensions, weights, and nozzle orientations.
*   Detailed Material Specification List (MDS) for all pump components, including specific grades of stainless steel for wetted parts.
*   Mechanical seal drawings, data sheets, and P&ID for the API Plan 53B barrier fluid system.
*   Motor data sheet, performance curves, and certified drawings.
*   Coupling data sheet and drawings.
*   Baseplate drawings.
*   Sound level data at rated conditions.
*   Vibration analysis report.
*   Quality Control Plan (QCP) and Inspection and Test Plan (ITP).

**6.3. Testing Requirements:**
The Vendor shall perform the following tests:
*   **Hydrostatic Test:** Casing hydrostatic test as per API 610.
*   **Performance Test:** Full performance test (flow, head, power, efficiency, NPSHr) at rated conditions and across the operating range as per API 610.
*   **Vibration Test:** Vibration measurements as per API 610.
*   **Witnessed Tests:** All major tests (hydrostatic, performance, vibration) shall be witnessed by B Star Chemetics or their appointed third-party inspector, as per B Star Chemetics Standard 4-SPC-0002.
*   **NPSHr Test:** NPSHr curve verification.
