**Project Handoff Brief: MEG Transfer Pump Skid (B Star Chemetics)**

**1. Project Overview**
This project involves the engineering design and specification of a MEG Transfer Pump Skid for B Star Chemetics' facility in Sarnia, Ontario. The primary objective is to deliver a fully specified horizontal centrifugal pump system capable of transferring 95% Monoethylene Glycol at defined process conditions, adhering to stringent industry standards.

**2. Process Engineer Tasks**
The Process Engineering team is responsible for establishing the fundamental design basis for the MEG Transfer Pump Skid. This includes performing a comprehensive fluid property assessment and defining the process design criteria as per internal procedure 1-PRC-0001. Based on these criteria, you will then execute the necessary process calculations, documented under 1-CAL-XXXX, which will serve as critical inputs for the Mechanical Engineering team's subsequent pump sizing and selection activities.

**3. Mechanical Engineer Tasks**
Mechanical Engineering will lead the detailed design and specification of the horizontal centrifugal pump. Your work will commence upon receipt of the Process Design Criteria (1-PRC-0001) and Process Calculations (1-CAL-XXXX) from the Process team. Key tasks include developing the Pump Calculation Procedure (4-PRC-0007), performing detailed Pump Calculations (4-CAL-0001), and generating comprehensive Pump Data Sheets (4-DST-XXXX). You will also be responsible for preparing the General Equipment Requirements (4-SPC-0001) and the specific Horizontal Centrifugal Pumps specification (4-SPC-0002), ensuring all aspects comply with API 610, API Plan 11 for the mechanical seal, and a TEFC motor enclosure. The final deliverable will be the Equipment Bid Tabulations (4-TAB-XXXX), consolidating all technical requirements for procurement.
