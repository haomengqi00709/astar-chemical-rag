**Project Handoff Brief: MEG Transfer Pump Skid (B Star Chemetics)**

**1. Project Overview**
This project involves the detailed engineering design and selection of a MEG Transfer Pump Skid for our client, B Star Chemetics, located in Sarnia, Canada. The primary objective is to specify a horizontal centrifugal pump capable of handling 95% Monoethylene Glycol at a flow rate of 45 m³/h, 10 bar pressure, and 65 °C, ensuring compliance with all relevant industry standards and client requirements.

**2. Process Engineer Tasks**
The Process Engineering team is responsible for establishing the fundamental process design basis and fluid properties for the MEG transfer system. Your key deliverables include the Process Design Criteria (1-PRC-0001), which must incorporate a thorough fluid property assessment, and the Process Calculations (1-CAL-XXXX). These documents will serve as the foundational inputs for the Mechanical team's subsequent pump sizing and selection activities. Specifically, 1-PRC-0001 must detail the process design basis and fluid property assessment as per internal guidelines.

**3. Mechanical Engineer Tasks**
The Mechanical Engineering team will take the process outputs to perform the detailed hydraulic design and pump sizing. You are required to develop the Pump Calculation Procedure (4-PRC-0007), perform the Pump Calculations (4-CAL-0001), and generate the Pump Data Sheets (4-DST-XXXX). Additionally, you will be responsible for preparing the Horizontal Centrifugal Pumps specification (4-SPC-0002), the General Equipment Requirements (4-SPC-0001), and the Equipment Bid Tabulations (4-TAB-XXXX). It is critical that the hydraulic design and pump sizing adhere to 4-PRC-0007, and pump and motor selection must be in accordance with API 610, specifying a mechanical seal API Plan 11. Please note that Mechanical engineering cannot commence pump calculations (4-CAL-0001) until both the Process Design Criteria (1-PRC-0001) and Process Calculations (1-CAL-XXXX) have been finalized and issued by the Process team.
