## 3.0 Purpose

The Document Register is a key planning and control document to record all documents that will be produced on a project. It is developed as part of project planning and maintained throughout the project by the respective disciplines. This register ensures that all required deliverables are identified, assigned, and tracked against their due dates, facilitating effective project execution and control.
