## 1.0 Introduction

### 1.1 Purpose

This Project Execution Plan (PEP) is a formal deliverable (WBS 0-2) that outlines the comprehensive strategy and methodology for executing the MEG Transfer Pump Skid project. It serves as a foundational document for all project participants, ensuring a common understanding of project objectives, scope, organization, and control mechanisms.

### 1.2 Scope

This document defines the framework for project execution, encompassing project management, engineering, procurement, and construction (EPC) oversight activities. It details how the project will be managed from initiation through closeout, including key deliverables, scheduling, control processes, and stakeholder responsibilities. This PEP applies to all personnel and contractors involved in the MEG Transfer Pump Skid project.

## 2.0 Project Overview

### 2.1 Project Identification

The project involves the design, procurement, fabrication, and delivery of a Monoethylene Glycol (MEG) Transfer Pump Skid.

### 2.2 Client and Location

*   **Client:** B Star Chemetics
*   **Location:** Sarnia, Ontario, Canada

### 2.3 Technical Data

The primary equipment for this project is a horizontal centrifugal pump designed for MEG transfer.

*   **Fluid:** Monoethylene Glycol (MEG), 95% by weight
*   **Flow Rate:** 45 m³/h
*   **Discharge Pressure:** 10 bar
*   **Operating Temperature:** 65 °C

### 2.4 Key Project Dates

*   **Project Execution Plan Due Date:** 2026-03-28
*   **Overall Project End Date:** 2026-08-01

### 2.5 Risk Assessment Summary

As of the issuance of this document, there are no active risk flags identified that require immediate mitigation actions beyond standard project management practices. A comprehensive risk management process will be maintained throughout the project lifecycle.

## 3.0 Project Organization and Responsibilities

The project will be managed by a dedicated Project Management team, supported by specialized engineering disciplines including Process, Mechanical, and Instrumentation/Control. ## 6.0 Project Control and Reporting

### 6.1 Progress Monitoring and Reporting

Project progress will be monitored weekly through team meetings and reported monthly to the client. Reporting will include progress against schedule, earned value analysis, expenditure tracking, and identification of any deviations or potential issues.

### 6.2 Change Management

All changes to the approved project scope, schedule, or budget will be managed through a formal Change Management Procedure. This procedure ensures that all changes are properly documented, assessed for impact, approved by relevant stakeholders, and communicated to the project team.

### 6.3 Risk Management

A continuous risk management process will be implemented to identify, assess, mitigate, and monitor project risks. While no active risk flags are currently identified, potential risks related to technical challenges, procurement delays, or external factors will be proactively managed.

### 6.4 Quality Management

The project will adhere to established quality management procedures to ensure that all deliverables meet the specified technical requirements and client expectations. This includes design reviews, vendor quality surveillance, inspection and test plans (ITPs), and final documentation verification.

## 7.0 Procurement and Subcontracting Strategy

Procurement and subcontracting activities are critical to the successful delivery of the MEG Transfer Pump Skid. A structured approach will be followed to ensure timely acquisition of materials and services, cost-effectiveness, and quality assurance.

### 7.1 Procurement Activities

Procurement activities will cover all necessary equipment, materials, and services required for the project. This includes:

*   **Vendor Prequalification:** Assessment of potential suppliers based on technical capability, financial stability, and past performance.
*   **Request for Quotation (RFQ) / Tender Process:** Issuance of detailed specifications and commercial terms to qualified vendors.
*   **Technical and Commercial Bid Evaluation:** Thorough review of proposals to ensure compliance with project requirements.
*   **Purchase Order (PO) Issuance:** Formal contracting with selected vendors.

### 7.2 Subcontract Activities

Subcontracts will be issued for specialized services such as fabrication, testing, and potentially installation support. The management of subcontracts will follow a similar rigorous process to procurement, ensuring clear scopes of work, performance monitoring, and adherence to project standards.

### 7.3 Expediting

Expediting will be a key activity across Process Engineering, Mechanical & Equipment Engineering (Including Material Engineering), and Instrumentation/Control disciplines to ensure the timely delivery of critical equipment and materials. This involves:

*   **Regular Communication:** Maintaining close contact with vendors and subcontractors.
*   **Progress Monitoring:** Tracking manufacturing progress against agreed schedules.
*   **Issue Resolution:** Proactively addressing any potential delays or bottlenecks.
*   **Site Visits:** Conducting inspections at vendor facilities as required.

## 8.0 Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE)

The health and safety of all personnel involved in the project, as well as the protection of the environment, are paramount. The project will operate under a strict HSE management system, ensuring compliance with all applicable regulations, client requirements, and industry best practices. All project activities will be planned and executed with a "safety first" mindset.

## 9.0 Project Closeout

Upon completion of all project activities, a formal project closeout process will be initiated. This includes:

*   **Final Documentation:** Compilation and submission of all project records, including as-built drawings, operation manuals, and maintenance procedures.
*   **Financial Closeout:** Reconciliation of all accounts and final payments.
*   **Lessons Learned:** Conducting a review to identify successes and areas for improvement for future projects.
*   **Client Handover:** Formal transfer of the completed MEG Transfer Pump Skid to B Star Chemetics.
