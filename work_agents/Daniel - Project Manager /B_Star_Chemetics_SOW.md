# Statement of Work
## Monoethylene Glycol (MEG) Transfer Pump Skid

---

**Project Title:** MEG Transfer Pump Skid
**Project Number:** BSC-2026-001
**Client:** B Star Chemetics
**Location:** Sarnia, Ontario, Canada
**Project Type:** Pump Station
**Prepared By:** A Star Chemical Engineering
**Date:** March 2026

---

## 1. Background

B Star Chemetics operates a glycol processing facility in Sarnia, Ontario. As part of a capacity expansion, a new pump skid is required to transfer monoethylene glycol (MEG) from an intermediate flash separator to the MEG regeneration column at elevated pressure.

---

## 2. Scope of Work

Design, procure, and commission a MEG transfer pump skid consisting of one (1) duty and one (1) standby horizontal centrifugal pump to transfer monoethylene glycol from the flash separator to the regeneration column.

The scope includes:

- Hydraulic design and pump sizing per 4-PRC-0007
- Pump and motor selection in accordance with API 610 (latest edition)
- Preparation of pump calculation sheet (4-CAL-0001)
- Preparation of pump data sheet (4-DST-0001)
- Process design basis and fluid property assessment per 1-PRC-0001
- Document register and project execution plan

---

## 4. Equipment

- **Pump type:** Horizontal centrifugal, API 610 OH2
- **Configuration:** 1 × duty + 1 × standby (100% installed spare)
- **Driver:** Electric motor, TEFC enclosure, 50 Hz
- **Shaft seal:** Mechanical seal, API Plan 11

## 8. Constraints and Assumptions

- The MEG flash separator and regeneration column nozzle sizes and elevations are fixed.
- Piping isometrics and detailed friction loss calculations to be finalized at detailed design stage; preliminary friction losses are as stated in Section 3.
- Pump vendor to confirm NPSHr; preliminary NPSHr estimate used for initial sizing.
- Motor voltage: 480 V, 3-phase, 60 Hz.
- Ambient temperature: 20 °C (design basis for motor rating).
