# 6-PRC-0002 — Procedure


[TABLE: 6-PRC-0002_table_1]


## Purpose
Project personnel will prepare documentation as described in this procedure to provide a record of insulation and heat tracing design, and to provide a complete description of technical requirements to be issued to Vendors.
This procedure assigns responsibilities for document preparation and approval.

## Document Requirements

[TABLE: 6-PRC-0002_table_2]


### Insulation Design Criteria
6-PRC-0001: Insulation Design Criteria will contain the following elements as applicable:
Statutory requirements
Owner requirements
Reference to the appropriate  specification
Any additional  general requirements for insulation selection for the plant.
Insulation Design Criteria will be prepared by Mechanical
Insulation Design Criteria will be checked by Project Engineer
Insulation Design Criteria will be approved by the Chief Mechanical Engineer

### Insulation Specification
will contain the following elements as applicable:
Surface preparation requirements
Installation requirements
Insulation Specification will be prepared by Mechanical
Insulation Specification will be checked by the Project Engineer
Insulation Specification will be approved by Mechanical

### Equipment Insulation List
6-LST-0001: Equipment Insulation List will be issued to summarize equipment insulation.
Equipment Insulation List will be prepared by Mechanical
Equipment Insulation List will be checked by Process
Equipment Insulation List will be approved by Project Engineer
