# 9-PRC-0020 — Procedure


[TABLE: 9-PRC-0020_table_1]


## Introduction
The electrical systems contractor checkout procedure outlines the procedure to be used to verify that all electrical devices have been installed in accordance with the drawings and specifications and are ready for the electrical systems field checkout by a Aker Kvaerner Chemetics specialist.  The Check Lists will form part of the electrical completion package.

## Sequence of Execution
During construction, the site sub-contractor checks for completeness of the electrical system installation, before approaching Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site supervisor for his review.
Each electrical system item is inspected and signed-off in accordance with the attached Electrical Systems Contractor Check Lists.  Findings are recorded on a separate sheet attached to the Check Lists.
All deficiencies are recorded and given to the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site supervisor.
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site supervisor visually reviews the installed electrical systems against the general arrangement and installation instructions. When the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site supervisor is satisfied the sub-contractor has taken the work as far as can be done at that time, he signs the completed Electrical Contractor Check Lists.
He then notifies the Project Manager that the equipment is ready for a final review by a Aker Kvaerner Chemetics specialist.
The site supervisor works with a deficiency crew supplied by the site sub-contractor to correct all deficiencies identified by the sub-contractor or the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site supervisor.

Completed electrical installation.
