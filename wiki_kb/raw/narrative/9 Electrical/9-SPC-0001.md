# 9-SPC-0001 — Specification


[TABLE: 9-SPC-0001_table_1]


## Purpose
The purpose of this Standard is to define the workmanship and quality requirements for Aluminium and Aluminium alloy welded BUS and Associated Connections purchased by/or on behalf of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics

## Scope
This Standard applies when called for on purchase orders and referenced documents.
This Standard covers the welding and other fabrication requirements for shop and field fabricated BUS systems in the ASME P-21 Material Classification.

## Definitions
Codes, etc., shall be taken to mean latest edition ruling at date of contract.
Agreement in Writing - Where prior approval in writing is required by this Standard, this shall be taken to mean submission in writing by letter and not merely by inclusion in approval drawings.
Inspector - Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Quality Assurance Inspector or other Authorized Representative.

## General Requirements
Supervision
The fabricator shall ensure that there is regular and systematic supervision of all work both in-shop and on-site by competent staff.
Procedure Submittal
The welding procedure(s) shall be provided by the fabricator for review and agreement by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Q.A. & Materials Group prior to commencement of any work.
Once the procedure parameters are established, a change in essential variables as outlined in ASME Code, Section IX or CSA W47.2 shall not be permitted.
Procedure shall show joint preparation and weld bead sketch.
Welders Qualifications
All welders to be employed on Aker Kvaerner Chemetics work shall be experienced in the welding of Aluminum and shall be suitably qualified in accordance with relevant codes and standards for every procedure to be used
For field welding, any uncertified welder shall pass the Qualification Tests laid out in CSA W47.2 prior to any production welding.
The fabricator shall, upon request, supply Aker Kvaerner Chemetics with objective evidence of current welder qualifications.
All qualified welders shall be allocated an identification symbol.
Cleanliness
The surface and edges of any Aluminum or Aluminum alloy which is to be heated for any purpose (e.g. hot forming or welding) shall be cleaned free from all traces of foreign matter (e.g. marking paint, oil, etc.) by solvent cleaning with acetone.
The fabricator shall take the controls necessary to prevent contamination of weldments at all stages of fabrication.  These controls shall include the segregation of wire brushes, grinding wheels, etc., for use on Aluminum and it alloys only.
Before welding all weld surfaces and the base metal one inch from the weld joint shall be solvent cleaned with acetone and wire brushed thoroughly with a stainless steel wire brush.
On completion of work, items shall be cleaned internally and externally.  All fabrication markings, weld spatter, etc., shall be removed prior to shipment.
Drawings
All work shall be carried out in accordance with the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics "Approved For Fabrication" shop detail drawing(s).  Deviations from the weld geometry as shown on approved drawing(s) are not permitted without the prior approval of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

## Materials
Base Materials
All materials used shall be as specified in the purchase order and referenced documents.
Material shall be in accordance with ASTM B209 or B236.
Type ll00 or l350 materials are considered acceptable for BUS systems.  H112 temper is normally used.
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics must be advised in writing regarding proposed use of alternate materials.
Filler Materials
All weld filler metals shall be selected from, and meet the requirements of, The American Welding Society and ASME Code.
All filler wire/electrodes shall be in accordance with AWS A5.10.
It is recommended that Type 4043 or ll00 filler wire be used, with 4043 being the preferred choice as it is easier to use and provides good penetration at bus bar thickness’.
All fabricators shall ensure and demonstrate to the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics representative that the manufacturer's instructions regarding the storage, drying and use of weld consumables are being fully implemented both in the shop and on site.
The filler wire/electrode used on production welding shall be the same as that used in the Procedure Qualification Test.
The specific AWS filler metal grade must be shown on every weld procedure.
Certification
Certification showing the results obtained from analysis and other tests carried out in accordance with the relevant material specification shall be supplied to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for all materials including welding consumables.
Traceability
When required by purchase order, fabricator shall maintain full traceability of materials back to original mill certification.
As a minimum the fabricator's internal controls shall be such as to prevent the use of unspecified materials.
Lack of adequate identification or certification shall be cause for rejection of the specific component or item.

## Preparation Of Edges For Welding
Cutting to size
Plate and pipe shall be cut to size by mechanical methods only.  Bevel preparation by machining, shearing, grinding is acceptable.
Forming
Material shall be cold formed as far as practical.  When hot forming is necessary, the procedure to be adopted shall be agreed to in writing by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics prior to commencement of work.

## Welding Processes
General
The following processes are the preferred methods of welding Aluminium or its alloys.  Manual metallic arc welding process is not acceptable.
Gas Tungsten Arc Welding	(GTAW)
Gas Metal Arc Welding	(GMAW)
Gas Tungsten Arc Welding
Preferred electrode is thoriated tungsten as per AWS-A5.12, Class EWTh-2.
Consumable electrodes shall fall within the filler metal category F21 of Section IX of the ASME Code.
High frequency (HF) voltage shall be used to start the arc.
Where necessary, a back-up gas shall be used to prevent root pass oxidation and contamination.  This gas may be argon, helium or a mixture of the two.
Shielding gas and flow rate shall be specified.
Gas Metal Arc Welding
GMAW may be used for butt welds or fillet welds.
Fabricator must provide satisfactory evidence of continuous shop use of this process in the fabrication of Aluminum or its alloys.
Wire spools must be tagged for easy identification.
Shielding gas and flow rate shall be specified.
Preheat and Interpass Temp.
Preheat is not normally required.  However, when adverse weather conditions give rise to condensation or wetness on the surfaces of the component, the material shall be dried prior to welding by heating to a temperature just sufficient to prevent condensation.  It may also be necessary to preheat to facilitate elimination of cold starts (poor penetration).
Heat input during welding should be kept to a minimum.  Interpass temp. should not exceed 250F. in order to minimize heat build-up and distortion.

## Workmanship
General
Procedure Tests
The welding procedure shall be agreed upon (see Para 5.2) before any production welding is begun.  It is recommended that weld procedures be organized as set out in Section IX of the ASME Code.
Welding Position
As far as practical, all welds shall be made in the downhand (flat) position.  Vertical welds shall be made vertical up (uphand).
Weather Protection for Welding on Site
Suitable enclosures shall be provided to keep welds and welders free from drafts, rain or other inclement weather.  Welding shall not be performed on wet surfaces.
Fusion Faces
The fusion faces and the adjacent metal, both internal and external, shall be free from moisture, scale, paint, grease or other foreign matter immediately prior to welding.
Alignment of Butt Joints
Misalignment of welding edges at butt joints shall be held to a minimum.
Fit-up must be accurate and checked frequently in order to minimize possible distortion and weld penetration difficulties.
It is essential that considerable care is exercised in joint preparation.  Bevel angles and thickness’ shall be accurate and true to facilitate good welding.
Assembly for Welding
To maintain the specified alignment and gap (where used) during welding, the parts to be welded shall be securely held in position by mechanical means, welded-on bridge pieces or by tack welding.
Tack Welds
Tack welds employed for assembly purposes may form part of the completed weld provided that complete fusion is effected at the root of the weld and that the welding procedure used is the same as that employed for the remainder of the root run.
Sound tack welds must be thoroughly fused into the root run of the main weld.  It is sometimes necessary to thin down the ends of tack welds to make sure this is possible.
Where this is necessary tack welds should preferably be at least one inch long to ensure that the remaining part of the tack weld is strong enough to hold the plate in place.
Cracked and unsound tack welds shall be cut out prior to welding.
Backing Bars
The use of permanent back-up rings or strips or consumable joint inserts is not permitted unless specifically approved in writing by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' Q.A. and Materials Engineering.
Striking of Welding Arc
Every effort shall be made to confine arc strikes to the surface on which weld metal is to be deposited.  If such a strike should occur outside this area the spot will require to be ground, PT examined per Para. l0.3 and, if necessary, repaired.
Burn marks from grounding clamps shall be treated as arc strikes.
Cleaning and Re-Starting
Longitudinal welds shall use run-off pads for starting and finishing each pass. Run-off pad material shall be of the same material designation as the parent material.
Each run of weld metal shall be completely fused and thoroughly cleaned of all slag and foreign matter before the next run is deposited.  On re-starting, care shall be taken to ensure proper fusion with the weld metal previously deposited. The Arc shall be broken gradually to eliminate craters and cracks.
Electrode Touch Down
If a tungsten electrode accidentally makes contact with the work, the section of weld and the tip of the electrode should be examined.  Where tungsten loss has occurred, the tungsten pick-up in the weld shall be ground out and the electrode reground to the correct profile before re-starting.
Peening and Hammering
Peening of weld runs and hammering of finished welds is not permitted.
Form of Welds
Each weld shall be uniform in width and size throughout its full length.  Each layer of welding shall be smooth and free of slag inclusions, cracks, porosity, lack of fusion, and undercut except to the extent permitted in the referenced codes.  In addition, the cover pass shall be free of coarse ripples, irregular surface, non-uniform bead patterns, high crown, and deep ridges or valleys between beads.
Buttwelds shall be slightly convex, of uniform height, and have full penetration with no lack of sidewall fusion.
Welding of butt joints shall alternate from both sides in order to minimize distortion.
As far as is practicable, fillet welds shall be deposited in not less than two layers and their surfaces shall be free from marked irregularities.  Fillet weld dimensions shall be in accordance with the approved drawing.
Back gouging of root passes of butt welds shall be performed whenever possible.
Dressing of Welds & Repair Areas
Grinding or gouging shall be carried out in such a manner as not to reduce the adjacent base metal thickness.
Welds shall be thoroughly cleaned between passes by wire brushing.  Power brushing is preferred.
Grinding wheels shall be iron-free and of the resin bonded alumina or silica type.
Wire brushes or chipping tools shall be made of austenitic stainless steel and must not have been previously used to clean other surfaces.

## Quality Control
General
The Inspector shall have free access to the Vendor's work and to his Subvendor's, if work is sublet by approval, at all reasonable times.
He shall be at liberty to inspect the work during fabrication at any stage in manufacture and to reject any material or workmanship, which does not conform to this Standard or the applicable code.
Inspection by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics does not relieve the fabricator or erector of responsibility for providing materials, components and workmanship in accordance with the requirements of this Standard, engineering design and the applicable code.
Chemical Analysis
Supplier must make available to the Inspector copies of chemical certification for batch of electrodes or wire coils used.  If electrodes, wire, etc., cannot be identified to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' satisfaction, samples shall be removed for chemical analysis.  This shall be to supplier's account.
When required by purchase order, a spot check analysis shall be performed on production welds.
The weld(s) to be checked shall be selected at random by the Inspector.
Where results of chemical analysis show that a specific weldment falls outside of the approved specification or code requirements, the supplier shall promptly advise Aker Kvaerner Chemetics in writing of his proposed course of action.  Such action shall include details of any additional testing necessary to fully define the extent of the non-conformance.  All repairs and retests shall be to the supplier's account.
Dye Penetrant/Radiography
Test Pieces shall be radiographed in accordance with CSA W47.2 to ascertain quality of the welds prior to commencement of shop or site welding.
The quality of the production welds shall be monitored by the use of spot radiography and/or dye penetrant inspection.
Welds shall be rejected if:
Radiographs reveal lack of fusion, cracks or other linear defects or gross porosity as outlined in the attached sketch - Page 14.
Dye penetrant inspection reveals linear defects such as cracking or lack of fusion.
It shall be the responsibility of the fabricator to notify the purchaser immediately upon the discovery of any unacceptable defects.  Repair of these defects shall be to the fabricators' account.
Accessible field butt welds shall be spot radiographed (25 percent), and rejection may be cause for dye penetrant inspection in addition to the mandatory extra radiography.  Welds which cannot be radiographed e.g. fillet welds, shall be dye penetrant inspected.
Painting and Marking
No paint shall be applied, except for identification purposes.  The order number and/or other identification marks shall either be painted in lead-free paint on each item or tagged as specified.
Documentation
The supplier shall provide Aker Kvaerner Chemetics with a documentation package covering all relevant tests and inspections performed on the system.  This shall include certification per section 6.3.
