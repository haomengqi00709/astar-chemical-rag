# 9-PRC-0003 — Procedure


[TABLE: 9-PRC-0003_table_1]


## Purpose
Project personnel will prepare documentation as per project requirements as described in this procedure providing a complete:
Record of electrical equipment sizing and design for this project
Description of technical requirements to be issued to vendors and subcontractors.
This procedure assigns responsibilities to project personnel for document preparation, checking and approval.

### Detail Drawings
The detail drawings will show the methods of installing electrical equipment and indicate the various components required for the overall installation of the equipment.  It is the intention to use the details shown on the drawings as a guide during the installation of the equipment.  The details will include a full description of the materials and components required for the installation.
Grounding
The grounding details will show the following as applicable, but not limited to:
Grounding conductors used for main ground grids, branches to equipment and buildings;
Grounding electrodes used, material types, length and method of fastening to the grounding conductor;
Connectors for fastening the grounding conductors to equipment, building columns, ground electrodes and to other grounding conductors.
Lighting
The lighting details will show the following as applicable, but not limited to:
Lighting fixtures and the method of supporting from ceilings, walls, along walkways, under walkways, roadways, etc.
Mounting hardware will include the junction boxes, fixture hangers, conduit boxes, cable fittings, nuts and bolts;
The various ways of installing fixtures according to the reflectors used;
Cable Trays
The cable tray details will show the following as applicable, but not limited to:
Cable tray supports on pipe racks, along walls, under walkways, vertical runs between horizontal runs;
Cable tray and support materials will be indicated;
Cable tray supports for multiple layers in straight runs horizontally, vertically and bends;
Lightning Protection
The lightning protection details will show the following as applicable, but not limited to:
The lightning electrode mounting hardware required when the electrode is located on a building roof, outdoor vessel, vent pipe or other tall structure;
Method of installing the grounding conductor from the lightning electrode to the buried grounding electrode.
Type of grounding electrode required and any interconnection with other grounding systems;
Wiring
The wiring details will show the following as applicable, but not limited to:
Conductor and cable numbering system;
The necessary wiring accessories such as spade lugs or closed loop lugs;
Conductor and cable marking materials and the locations of the identifiers;
Methods of bundling the conductors and supporting the bundle within an enclosure;
Terminal block numbering system will be shown and the materials identified.
Motor Installations
The motor installation details will show the following as applicable, but not limited to:
Power cable connection to the motor junction box;
Control cable connection to the local control station located at the motor;
Grounding conductor connection to the motor, pump and the common frame.
Cabling to other motor junction boxes such as space heater, winding & bearing RTDs, vibration and speed switches.
The various components needed to complete each type of installation.
Boxes, Cabinets, Panels
The installation details for boxes, cabinets and panels will show the following as applicable, but not limited to:
Methods of installing and supporting these items as necessary and the items will be identified;
Methods of securely fastening these items to floors, walls, building columns, on stand-alone support frames, etc.;
Locations of cables entering these boxes, cabinets and panels will be identified;
Methods of terminating the cables at these boxes, cabinets and panels will be shown and the items identified;
The details will be applicable to the project and not generic;
The details will satisfy all of the necessary mounting and installation methods required for the project.
Bus Bar Layout
The bus bar installation details will show the following as applicable, but not limited to:
Support brackets required for the bus bars located between the T/R and the cells;
Support brackets required for the bus bars between the cells as necessary;
Insulating materials required on the brackets to isolate the bus bars from the bracket materials.
Methods of supporting the brackets from the floors, walls or ceiling of the building;
The various styles of support brackets required and the quantities of each type;
Details will be project specific and not generic.
Pushbutton Stations
The pushbutton station details will show the following as applicable, but not limited to:
Methods of supporting the pushbutton station on walls, building columns and at motor bases where a wall or building column do not exist;
Mounting brackets and support steel required for the above three possible locations;
Identify each component required to attach the pushbutton station to the mounting bracket or support steel;
Location of the cable entry to the pushbutton station;
Cable terminating components required to fasten the cable to the pushbutton station;
The various types of pushbutton stations will be identified and any installation differences will be shown;

Location of the warning marker tape

Depth of burial
Distance between the cable and all other items located in the trench.

The necessary supporting materials.
Wall penetration details to identify the materials required to seal the opening after conduit installation.

To Section 3.1 : Electrical Design Criteria
This is a procedure and will be document number: 9-PRC-0002.
The electrical design criteria will be a compilation of the Owner’s electrical design criteria and specifications;  electrical design criteria and specifications; and the electrical codes, standards, regulations and local codes of the location of the project. To Section 3.2 : Process Data Sheets
These sheets are process data sheets will be document numbers: 4-PDS-XXXX.  The XXXX will be the electrical equipment number.
The process department determines specific requirements such as the DC current and voltage of electrical equipment such as a transformer/rectifier and CPU required for the electrolyzer cells.  The  process department is responsible for the electrolyzer cell output design and they determine the current and voltage required from a transformer/rectifier to achieve their design output.

The temperature limits will be applied to pipes, vessels, pumps, etc. as determined by the process and the materials of the equipment to be heat traced. To Section 3.4: Equipment Specifications – Hazardous Area Classification Requirements
