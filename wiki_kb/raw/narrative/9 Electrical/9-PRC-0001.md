# 9-PRC-0001 — Procedure


[TABLE: 9-PRC-0001_table_1]


## Purpose
This procedure describes the requirements for standard identification numbers and names for electrical equipment.
The standard identification numbers will be used for the development of all related numbering systems such as cost codes, document numbers, etc.
The standard identification numbers and names will be used on all projects unless specific Owner requirements dictate otherwise. In such cases, the project team will maintain a dual system on the project equipment list to provide a cross-reference.

## Number Format
The number format is similar to that used for plant equipment (4-PRC-0001: Plant Equipment Identification) as follows:
1016E
10:	Process Area
16:	Serial Number
E:	Identifier for Electrical Equipment

## Electrical Equipment Associated with Plant Equipment
For electrical equipment associated with plant equipment, such as motors or resistance heaters, the process area and serial number will be identical to that assigned to the plant equipment (4-PRC-0001: Plant Equipment Identification). The equipment name will then be derived from the plant equipment name. For example:
The Lixator Pump Motor 1016E is associated with the Lixator Pump 1016P.
The Brine Heater Element 1070E is associated with the Brine Heater 1070H.

## Electrical Distribution Equipment
For electrical distribution equipment, the “Process Area” is 99.
Standard serial numbers and electrical equipment names are on the appended list.

## Application for Duplicate Items
Where multiple pieces of the same equipment are installed, a suffix is used for identification.
Duplicate equipment that is part of parallel processing systems will be identified with a numeric suffix (e.g. -1, -2, etc.).
Duplicate equipment within a single processing system will be identified with a letter suffix
(e.g. -A, -B, etc.).
Combinations of suffixes may also be used where appropriate (e.g. –1A, -1B, etc.).
