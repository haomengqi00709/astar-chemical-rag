# 9-PRC-0004 — Procedure


[TABLE: 9-PRC-0004_table_1]


## Introduction
The electrical field checkout procedure for electrical systems outlines the procedure to be used to verify that all the electrical devices have been installed in accordance with the drawings and specifications, are functioning correctly and are ready for water batching and commissioning.  The Check Lists will form part of the electrical completion package.

## Sequence of Execution
During construction the site sub-contractor checks for completeness of the electrical system installation before approaching Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site supervisor for his review.
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site supervisor visually reviews the installed electrical systems against the general arrangement and installation instructions. When the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site supervisor is satisfied the sub-contractor has taken the work as far as can be done at that time, he signs the applicable Electrical Contractor Check Lists (attached to procedure 9-PRC-0020) and then notifies the Project Manager that the equipment is ready for a final review by a Aker Kvaerner Chemetics  specialist.
The Aker Kvaerner Chemetics specialist accepts the signed Check Lists from the  site supervisor and now conducts the inspection of the electrical systems against the engineering documents and applicable Electrical System Check List, to confirm the installation is in accordance with the documents.
Each electrical system item is inspected and signed-off in accordance with the attached Electrical System Check Lists.  Findings are recorded on a separate sheet attached to the Check Lists.
All deficiencies recorded are given to the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site supervisor.
The site supervisor works with a deficiency crew supplied by the site sub-contractor to correct all deficiencies identified by the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics specialist as well as any outstanding punch list items from the initial review by the sub-contractor.

## Checkout Prerequisites
Fully installed and checked DCS Hardware as per procedure 8-PRC-0014
Completed field wiring including remote I/O sub systems
DCS Control Functions ready to be checked out as per procedure 8-PRC-0004
