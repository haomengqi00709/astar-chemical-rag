- Commentary
- 1.0 | Five documents define process and mechanical design conditions for piping and equipment within the Plant:
- 1-DST-0001 | Site Design Conditions
- 1-LST-0001 | Battery Limits Conditions
- 5-LST-0001 | Line List
- 5-LST-0002 | Tie Point List
- 4-DST-XXXX | Equipment Data Sheet (or 4-PKG-XXXX Package Data Sheet)
- 1-LST-0001 Battery Limits Conditions documents the process conditions agreed to between Chemetics and Owner.  This document is issued to the Owner to confirm the process conditions.
- 1-DST-0001 Site Design Conditions documents site information such as wind and seismic design requirements, voltages, rainfall, etc.  The utility fluid process and mechanical design conditions for the project also appear on the document for convenient dissemination to equipment vendors.  This document is issued to the Owner to confirm the site conditions.
- 5-LST-0001 Line List defines all mechanical design conditions (temperature, pressure, relief, insulation, etc.) that are required to select pipe materials and components.  Mechanical design conditions on the Line List will be co-ordinated with utility fluid design conditions on the Site Design Conditions data sheet.
- 5-LST-0002 Tie Point List defines the mechanical requirements (size, location, pressure relief) of each pipe connection at the battery limit.
- 4-DST-XXXX Equipment Data Sheets (or 4-PKG-XXXX Package Data Sheets) define all process and utility conditions for the equipment.  Equipment Data Sheets may reference Site Design Conditions to specify utility design requirements.  Equipment design conditions should be conservative enough that Line List and Tie Point List details (which come later) will not affect them.
