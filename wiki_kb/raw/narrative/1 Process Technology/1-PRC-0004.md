# 1-PRC-0004 — Procedure


[TABLE: 1-PRC-0004_table_1]


## Purpose
This procedure outlines the manner and timing in which Hazard and Operability Studies will be conducted for projects. The procedure also identifies how changes to the design will be controlled.

## Methodology
The HazOp Study is conducted using the “Guide Word” method under the direction of a trained study leader. The HazOp Study is used to identify all possible deviations, explore the consequences of deviations, and identify potential hazards and operability problems. The study is conducted on the basis of reviewing design intent statements and information for discrete parts of the plant as designated on Piping and Instrument Diagrams.
Work assignments are made to develop solutions to remove or contain the problems identified so that a competent design is produced.

## Supporting Documentation
The HazOp Study can be conducted once the following project documentation is available with status “Approved for Design” except for Piping and Instrument Diagrams which will be at status “Approved for Design (HazOp)” (0-PRC-0003: P&ID Development and Approval):
1-DST-0001: Site Conditions
1-LST-0001: Battery Limit Conditions
1-PFD-XXXX: Process Flow Diagram
1-PID-XXXX: Piping and Instrument Diagrams
1-PRC-0001: Process Design Criteria
4-LST-0001: Equipment List
5-LST-0001: Piping Line List
5-LST-0002: Battery Limit List
8-SPC-0020: Logic Narrative
8-SPC-XXXX: Instrumentation Specification
To ensure a permanent record of the design as studied, a copy of the published versions of all of this documentation will be stored in P:\1-Process-Technology\HAZ.

## Study Team Members
At a minimum the HazOp team will consist of a Leader, representatives from the Process Department, Mechanical Department , and Design Office.  One of the study team members will be assigned the role of recording secretary.
The Chief Process Engineer will assign the Leader of the HazOp Study team when advised by the Project Engineer that the supporting documentation is ready. In consultation with the Leader, the Project Engineer will assign the other members of the HazOp Study team from among the project team.

## Preparation
Process  and the HazOp Study Leader will divide the plant into parts to be studied, marking the boundaries on copies of the published, checked and approved Piping and Instrument Diagrams, and assigning a sequential identification number. The marked-up copies of the Piping and Instrument Diagrams will be filed in P:\1-Process-Technology\HAZ.
Prior to commencing each HazOp Study session Process  will record the following data for each part of the plant to be studied using 1-HAZ-0001: [Template] HazOp Record, assigning one excel worksheet for each part:
Purpose / Design Intent
Materials / Inputs
Activities
Sources
Destination
Using the same form, prior to commencing each HazOp Study session  Mechanical  will record the following data for each part of the plant to be studied:
Mechanical Design

## Study Execution
The HazOp study results will be recorded on 1-HAZ-0001: HazOp Record, prepared as described in Section 5. All guide word deviations studied are to be numbered sequentially using the part number as the common element (for example 10.3 refers to the third deviation studied for part 10). Each non-trivial guide word deviation is to be identified. A trivial deviation is one in which the deviation has no context, or is impossible, or the consequence is trivial.
All causes for each non-trivial guide word deviation are to be identified. Causes that result in potential hazardous situations are to be recorded. In situations where there is insufficient information available to assess whether the guide word deviation is non-trivial the lack of information is to be recorded and a question will be generated. Deviations that will not result in hazardous situations and are deemed to have no significant consequence are to be recorded as such (NSC). Safeguards that have been included in the design, or mitigating circumstances, are to be identified but not recorded for each cause.
Questions or problems associated with the cause of the deviation will be written in a concise manner and a discipline assigned to respond.
Portions of utility subsystems (packages) that are not designed by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics will be studied as a single part. Details of the internals of the packages will not be studied.

## Use of Previous HazOp Studies
Parts of the plant that are identical in form (but not necessarily in size), and that have identical design intent, to parts of a plant that has previously received a HazOp Study can use the information generated from that previous study.
In such a case, the relevant sections of the following published documents of the previous HazOp Study will be added as a subfolder of the record of the present HazOp Study, in P:\1-Process-Technology\Haz:
1-DST-0001: Site Conditions
1-LST-0001: Battery Limit Conditions
1-PID-XXXX: Piping and Instrument Diagrams (Approved for Design (HazOp))
1-PID-XXXX:Piping and Instrument Diagrams (As Built)
1-PRC-0001: Process Design Criteria
1-HAZ-0001: HazOp Record
1-HAZ-0002: HazOp Response
8-SPC-0020: Logic Narrative
The HazOp leader and the Technology Manager will be responsible for confirming that the part of the plant is identical in form and identical in design content to the previously studied plant. This confirmation will be noted on the HazOp Record.
The relevant sections of the HazOp Record and HazOp Response  are to be reviewed during the HazOp session to determine whether any of the questions need to be recorded in the current HazOp.

## Records
At the conclusion of each HazOp Study session, the record of that session will be published, checked and approved (0-PRC-0006: Publishing of Project Documentation). The record will be checked by the Study Leader and approved by the Technology Manager.
The HazOp record will therefore exist as a number of discrete files, and they will be numbered as sheets / sections of 1-HAZ-0001: HazOp Record.

## Study Responses
At the end of the HazOp Study, each deviation that generated a question will be transferred onto a single sheet / section of document 1-HAZ-0002: [Template] HazOp Response by the recording secretary. The Project Engineer will create a log of the questions to be answered using 1-HAZ-0003: [Template] Response Log, indicating the discipline assigned to respond, and will advise the Discipline Leads the deadline for completion of the responses.
The Discipline Leads will provide the required responses and advise the Project Engineer and the Technology Manager when they are complete.
The Technology Manager will review each response. If acceptable, and if it does not impact the HazOp Study of that part of the plant, then that sheet / section of 1-HAZ-0002: HazOp Response will be published and digitally signed by the Technology Manager as approved for implementation.
In the case where a response creates changes to the design that require further HazOp Study, the Technology Manager will work iteratively with the Discipline Leads to modify the design such that any remaining HazOp Study questions are satisfactorily answered. The revised sheet / section of 1-HAZ-0001: HazOp Record will be published, checked and approved as for the original HazOp study, and the resulting final sheet / section of 1-HAZ-0002: HazOp Response will be published and digitally signed by the Technology Manager as approved for implementation.

## Implementation
The Project Engineer will direct the implementation of any design changes required by the HazOp Study Responses following approval by the Technology Manager.
Following implementation the Technology Manager will review the design changes and if they meet the intent of the HazOp Study Responses, then the sheet / section of 1-HAZ-0002: HazOp Response will then be digitally signed by the Technology Manager as complete.
If the Technology Manager believes that the design changes do not meet the intent of the HazOp Study Responses, then the Technology Manager will proceed as described in the last paragraph of Section 9.

## Subsequent Changes to Design
Following the HazOp Study, any changes to design unrelated to the study responses will require the approval of the Technology Manager to ensure that the integrity of the HazOp Study is maintained.
For the purposes of interpreting the application of this section, a change to design will be interpreted to mean any action that requires a revision to the content of one of the documents listed in Section 3.
Draft changes to design will be prepared under the direction of the responsible Discipline Lead by marking-up the published version of the documents listed in Section 3, or any subsequent published versions after revision to incorporate the HazOp Study responses, and submitted to the Technology Manager for review.
The Technology Manager will review each draft change to design. If acceptable, and if it does not impact the HazOp Study of that part of the plant, then the document describing the draft change will be digitally signed by the Technology Manager as approved for implementation.
In the case where the draft change will require further HazOp Study, the Technology Manager will advise the Project Engineer who will arrange for the necessary personnel to conduct the study. The procedures of Sections 8, 9 and 10 will be followed.
The Project Engineer is responsible for ensuring that no changes of design are implemented following HazOp Study without review and approval as described by this Section.
