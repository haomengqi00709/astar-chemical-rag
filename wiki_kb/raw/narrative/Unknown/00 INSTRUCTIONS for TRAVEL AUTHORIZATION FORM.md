Ticket Booking Information:
Criteria for Around the World Tickets
In some cases your travel may allow you to take advantage of the “Around the World” fares which can be much cheaper.  FCm try to book these for us when it fits in with the planned request but they will not always be aware of what other travel you may be considering.  The following is what is required:
Business Class
- Reservations and ticketing must be done 7 days prior to departure
- All flights must stick to airline partners (for example, all Star Alliance or all One World)
- Must be at least three stops
- Minimum time of travel - 10 days
- Maximum time of travel - 12 months
- Change to date/time - Free (except no change allowed to first flight within 7 days of departure)
- Change to routing - $150
- Must travel over Pacific and Atlantic oceans (only once allowed)
- No back tracking (for example, can't fly to Europe, then Asia, then Europe again) - Availability - There must be sufficient availability in Business Class - even though there are Business Class seats available, there must be enough to receive the RTW discount - amount varies with specific airline
- Price - $8700, $10,000, or $11,700 - depending on amount of miles traveled
Typical route would be:
Vancouver to Perth to Singapore to Frankfurt to Vancouver
(at least three stops of at least one night, minimum 10 days total trip, and cross both oceans)
