# 7-PRC-0002 — Procedure


[TABLE: 7-PRC-0002_table_1]


## Purpose
Project personnel will prepare documentation as described in this procedure to provide a record of coating selection, and to provide a complete description of technical requirements to be issued to Vendors.  Coating requirements for all disciplines are described.
Coatings are defined as thin film coatings (such as paint and plating) and do not include thick film coatings such as equipment and floor lining systems.  Design and specification of linings is not covered in the scope of this procedure.

## Document Requirements
Project documentation of coating requirements will consist of:
Coatings Design Criteria
Coating Specification
Coating ColourSpecification

### Coating Design Criteria
7-PRC-0001: Coating Design Criteria will contain the following elements as applicable:
Statutory requirements
Owner requirements
Reference to the appropriate  coating specification
Any additional  general requirements for coating selection for the Plant.
Coating Design Criteria will be prepared by Mechanical
Coating Design Criteria will be checked by the Project Engineer

Commentary
To ection 2.1: In the absence of statutory or Owner requirements, the Design Criteria will reference the standard  Coating Specifications applicable to the Plant  also include guidance on items such as where Vendor coatings are acceptable.
Standard equipment and materials such as Type A and B mechanical equipment (e.g. pumps), electrical equipment and the like may use Vendor’s standard coating systems provided that they are suitable for the intended service, clearly specified by the Vendor and checked by the responsible Discipline Engineer.  In case of doubt of the suitability of Vendor’s standard coating, the iscipline ngineer will check with the Materials Engineer.
Some materials may have coating requirements specified as part of the applicable specification; i.e. plating or galvanising for bolting, electrical conduit.
To ection 2.2:
In the absence of statutory or Owner requirements, Mechanical will issue the  standard Coating Specification.  The standard choices are as follows:
7-SPC-0001: Protective Coating Electrochemical & Pulp & Paper
7-SPC-0002: Protective Coating Acid Plants (Shop Applied)
7-SPC-0003: Protective Coating Acid Plants (Field Applied)
7-SPC-0004: Protective Coating Calcium Hypochlorite Plants
7-SPC-0005: Protective Coating Underground Piping
If applicable, Mechanical will modify the standard coating specification to incorporate statutory and Owner requirements.  Mechanical will obtain input from the Materials Engineer and other Discipline Leads as required.  Note that modified specifications must be re-checked and re-approved as required by 0-PRC-0001: Approval and Use of Standards
To ection 2.3:
The coating colour / system specification will define the coating colour and system for all components of the project.  Having this information in a single location will help assure consistency for colour and paint systems, minimise the number of source documents, and simplify project checking.  The coating colour and system will be back checked on the vendor documents when they are submitted.
Coating system recommendations will be made by the appropriate Discipline Engineer and directed to the Project Engineer.
