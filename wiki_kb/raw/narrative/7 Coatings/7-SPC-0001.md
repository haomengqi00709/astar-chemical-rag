# 7-SPC-0001 — Specification


[TABLE: 7-SPC-0001_table_1]


## Purpose
This specification defines the minimum painting requirements for assemblies or components to be installed in corrosive environments such as acid plants and electrochemical plants.  It is not the intention that the selected paint systems described in this specification will be suitable for immersion in either acid or alkaline environments.  Spills must be washed down with water immediately.

## Scope
The Vendor will be responsible for all the requirements relating to the painting of process equipment, piping, pumps, tanks and structural steel including non-galvanized items.  Copies of the paint manufacturer’s data sheets will be provided to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics prior to paint application.  The following surfaces will not be painted:
Nameplates
Shaft seals
Stainless steels, and other high alloys except where specifically stated otherwise
Rubber, plastic and glass
Valve stems
Movable linkage on equipment
Grease fittings
Compressor or pump shafts
Pressure gauges
Instrument glasses and other similar items
Machined surfaces such as flange gasket faces
Any surfaces on equipment that are normally lubricated or have close working tolerances
Structural steel which has been galvanized or is to be fireproofed will not be painted.

## Surface Preparation and Cleaning

### General
All compressed air used for blasting and spray application will be free of moisture and oil.  Suitable air filters and traps will be installed on all blasting pots and spray equipment to completely remove oil and water.
All abrasive medium will be free of grease.  Samples form each lot will be immersed in clean water to test for grease.  Oil on the water surface will be cause for rejection of that lot of blasting medium.

### New, un-primed surfaces
Where necessary, all fins at saw cuts and burrs will be removed and sharp edges ground smooth.
Unless stated otherwise, surfaces to be painted will be blast cleaned to NACE No. 3 / SSPC SP-6 "Commercial Blast Clean".
If contaminated with grease and/or oils, the articles will first be solvent cleaned in accordance with the SSPC SP-1 "Solvent Cleaning".
Galvanized surfaces which are to be painted will be pre-treated with an etchbond primer (Maximum DFT 12m (0.5 mil) above surface profile).
The Vendor will use SSPC-VIS1 standard to ensure surface preparation requirements are satisfied.

### Previously Primed Surfaces
After erection and/or installation but before top-coating, items previously primed will be examined for damage and for unprimed areas.
All oil and grease will first be removed in accordance with SSPC SP-1.  Following solvent cleaning all loose dirt, chalk, and other contaminants will be removed by high-pressure water wash or other approved techniques.
Un-primed and damaged areas will be power tool cleaned in accordance with SSPC SP-11, and re-coated prior to application of finish coat(s).

## Curing Time before Service
A minimum curing time of 2 days at 21C (70F) and 50% relative humidity is recommended before exposing any newly painted parts to service conditions, unless stated otherwise on the coating manufacturers data sheet.
Several high temperature coatings are extremely soft until they have reached a temperature of 150°C (300F).

## Restrictions
Equipment must be primed within 8 hours of blast cleaning.  In areas of high humidity this time will be reduced accordingly, and under no circumstances will equipment which shows signs of rust or condensation be painted.
Painting will not be done when the surface temperature of the metal substrate is less than 3C (5F) above the dew point.
Surface temperatures of metal substrates will not exceed 38C (100F) during the painting operation.
Painting will not be attempted during periods of rain, fog, snow or high winds, nor should paint be applied to moist surfaces.

## General Requirements
Surfaces which have been previously painted, and must be subsequently welded, will have all paint coatings completely removed from the areas to be welded for a minimum of 50mm (2”) on either side of the repair area.
When deemed necessary, an adhesion test patch will be carried out before applying paint or coating to a previously painted surface to ensure that the previous coating will not lift and is well adhered to the surface.
Edges and sharp discontinuities will be “stripe coated” to ensure adequate coverage.
Where more than one topcoat is required, the intermediate and final coats will have a different colour to ensure that no areas are missed during the application of the topcoats.

## Inspection and Repair
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, or their designated representative, reserves the right to inspect at any time.  This does not relieve the Vendor of their responsibility to provide a properly inspected and acceptable protective coating in accordance with this specification.
Each operation of preparation or coating application will be visually inspected for uniform quality and coverage.  Holidays, variations in gloss or texture, or other defects will be repaired to provide uniform coverage of all surfaces.
Painted surfaces will be protected from damage throughout the construction period.
Damaged or abraded areas will be promptly repaired.  Material and application for repairs will be as specified for new coatings and will provide protection equal to the undamaged areas.
Shop and field touch up of damaged paint surfaces will be carried out using the following criteria:
No rusting but prime coat exposed:	Apply one or two finish coats to regain		specified thickness.
No rusting but prime coat damaged:	Clean area to base material and reapply		the specified coating.
Rust areas:	After cleaning to the original specified		standard of surface preparation, reapply		the specified coating.
Field welds:	Power tool clean (or spot sandblast) and		apply the specified paint system.

## Quality Documentation
The Vendor will provide a QA documentation package, which will include a paint inspection report covering the items painted.  The paint inspection report will include the following information:
Total DFT (above surface profile)
DFT measurements for each coat.  SSPC PA-2 will be used as a guide for acceptance
Number of coats, dates and times of application,
Name of painting contractor
Item(s) painted
Substrate material
Surface condition
Surface preparation, including date and time
Paint system(s) used
Application method
Conditions during application and drying of paint film
Location (i.e. shop/field, inside/outside, heated, etc.)
