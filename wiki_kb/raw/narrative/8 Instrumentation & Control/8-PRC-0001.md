# 8-PRC-0001 — Procedure


[TABLE: 8-PRC-0001_table_1]


## Purpose
This procedure describes the requirements for standard identification numbers for plant instrumentation.
It is the responsibility of each Technology Manager to develop and maintain standard identification numbers for plant instrumentation for their assigned technologies. These numbers will be recorded in the form of standard Piping and Instrument Diagrams.
The standard identification numbers will be used for the development of all related numbering systems such as document numbers, etc.
The standard identification numbers will be used on all projects unless specific Owner requirements dictate otherwise. In such cases, the project team will maintain a dual system on the project instrument list to provide a cross-reference.

## Number Format
LSH-780302
LSH:	Device Type
7803:	Associated Equipment Number
02:	Serial Number

## Device Type
A multi-character tag constructed as follows:
L:	Process Parameter (e.g. L: level, T: temperature)
S:	Device Type (e.g. S: switch, T: transmitter, V: valve)
H:	Action Modifier (e.g. H: high, L: low)
Standard character assignments are given in Section 7 below.

## Associated Equipment Number
This is the standard equipment number (4-PRC-0001: Plant Equipment Identification) of the equipment which the instrument is controlling or monitoring, or of the associated equipment number of the line (5-PRC-0001: Plant Pipe Numbering) which the instrument is controlling or monitoring.

## Serial Number
A unique two-digit number is assigned, one per instrument loop.

## Application for Duplicate Items
Duplicate instrument devices within a single loop will be identified with a letter suffix  to the “device type” portion of the number.
(e.g. LV/A-780301, LV/B-780301, etc.)
Instrumentation within the first of any parallel systems will be given the standard serial numbers as described above.
Instrumentation within any subsequent parallel systems will be assigned unused serial numbers as available. A cross-reference to the standard serial numbers will be maintained in the project instrument list.
