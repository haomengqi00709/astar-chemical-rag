# 8-PRC-0011 — Procedure


[TABLE: 8-PRC-0011_table_1]


## Introduction
The instrument approval tag procedure outlines the procedure to be used for tracking the receipt, installation and testing of field instrumentation and to indicate that the instruments are ready for water batching and commissioning.  The instrument approval tags will form part of the mechanical completion package.  This procedure assumes that site work is carried out as follows:
The subcontractor has been provided with construction issued documentation including but not limited to Instrument Specification Sheets, Instrument Index and Instrument Loop Drawings.
The subcontractor has been provided with Chemetics “INSTRUMENT APPROVAL TAG’S”.  See Figure 1 for example of instrument approval tag.
It is the responsibility of the subcontractor to permanently affix (Ty-Rap) an instrument approval tag to each instrument on receipt of the instrument.  Both the primary sensor and associated transmitter require individual tags.  If at any time during the construction/commissioning phase of the project, the instrument approval tag should become detached, it is the responsibility of the subcontractor to immediately replace the tag and confirm the accuracy of the information recorded on the tag.   All data recorded on the Instrument Approval Tag will be printed in a clear and concise manner.
The primary purpose of the instrument approval tag is to provide a complete record of an instrument received through to and including loop checkout.

## Stage No. 1 / Receive & Inspect
On receipt of the instrument the subcontractor will inspect the instrument for any sign of physical damage.  In the event of evidence of physical damage, the subcontractor will in writing, immediately inform the Chemetics site supervisor.
The subcontractor will confirm that the instrument is provided as per the Instrument Specification Sheet.  The subcontractor will confirm that the engraved instrument Tag No. is equivalent to the Tag No. on the specification sheet.  The subcontractor will record the model number directly from the instrument and not from the Instrument Specification Sheet.  Should there be a variance in these model numbers the subcontractor will make note of the variance in the comments section of the Instrument Approval Tag.  The subcontractor will record the serial number of the instrument directly from the instrument.
On completion of Stage No. 1, the subcontractor will sign and date the Instrument Approval Tag, Stage No. 1, as “Inspected”.

## Stage No. 2 / Field Calibration
After completion of Stage No. 1 the subcontractor will confirm the calibration of the instrument as follows.
If the instrument manufacturer has provided a certified calibration certificate, the subcontractor will confirm the calibration is as per the instrument specification sheet.  The subcontractor will record the calibration range or setpoint and units on the Instrument Approval Tag and indicate “By Manufacturer”.  Note: Calibration range or setpoints indicated directly on the field instruments is not considered to be certified.  The certified calibration certificates will be maintained on file by the subcontractor and will be turned over to the Chemetics site supervisor at mechanical completion.
All other field instruments shall be bench checked by the subcontractor to confirm the calibration is as per the instrument specification sheets. The subcontractor will make the necessary calibration adjustments to meet the instrument specification sheet requirements.  The subcontractor will maintain calibration check sheets on file for each instrument checked.  The calibration check sheets will make note where calibration adjustments have been made.
Control valve and on/off valve fail positions shall be confirmed against the instrument specification sheets.  The subcontractor will record the fail position on the Instrument Approval Tag “Valve/Xtr Fail Pos.”.
Where transmitters have a fail high/low position embedded in their software, the subcontractor will record the configured fail position on the Instrument Approval Tag “Valve/XTR Fail Position”.
If the instrument is provided with K numbers or calibration numbers (i.e.: Mag Flow Meters), they will be recorded in the Stage No 6  (COMMENTS) section of the Instrument Approval Tag by the subcontractor.
On completion of Stage No. 2, the subcontractor will sign and date the Instrument Approval Tag, Stage No. 2, as Calibrated By.

## Stage No. 3 / Installation
After completion of Stage No. 2 the instrument is ready for installation by the subcontractor.  On completion of the installation including all supports, tubing and wiring the subcontractor will sign and date “Ready for Checkout”.  The E&I subcontractor will co-ordinate the installation of all inline instruments with the piping contractor.  The E&I subcontractor will be responsible for signing and dating the “Ready for Checkout” of all inline instruments.
When the subcontractor has signed the “Ready for Checkout” the Chemetics site supervisor or his representative will be responsible for verifying the installation meets all engineering documentation requirements and manufacturer instructions.  The Chemetics site supervisor or his representative will sign and date “Checked By”.

## Stage No. 4 / Instrument Function Checkout
After completion of Stage No. 3 the instrument is ready for the functional checkout.  Local instruments such as pressure gauges, temperature gauges, rotameters etc. will be functionally checked during water batching.  The subcontractor will sign “Ready for Checkout” on these devices immediately after completion of Stage no. 3.
The subcontractor will check all instruments with electrical wiring.  The subcontractor will check each wire continuity with an ohmmeter, buzzer or equivalent apparatus to confirm the accuracy of the terminations as per the Instrument Loop Diagrams.  The subcontractor will sign “Ready for Checkout” on completion of these continuity tests.
When the subcontractor has signed the “Ready for Checkout” the Chemetics site supervisor or his representative will be responsible for verifying the instrument function meets all engineering documentation requirements.  The Chemetics site supervisor or his representative will sign and date “Checked By”.

## Stage No. 5 / Mechanical Complete
After completion of Stage No. 4 and all deficiencies have been corrected to the satisfaction of the Chemetics site supervisor or his representative, the subcontractor will sign and date “Ready for Checkout”.
The Chemetics site supervisor or his representative will sign and date “Checked By” when he is satisfied that the installation and loop check is complete with respect to the engineering documents and manufacturer instructions.  In addition the Chemetics site supervisor or his representative will attach (Ty-Rap) a plain green tag to provide a visual representation that the instrument is mechanically complete and ready for water batching.

## Stage No. 6 / Comments
The subcontractor may place any comments with respect to the associated instrument in this location (i.e.: Magnetic Flowtube Calibration Number).
Should there be a requirement to record owner acceptance of individual instruments, the owner will sign and date the card in this location.
