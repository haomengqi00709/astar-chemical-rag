Commentary
To Section 3: Document Requirements
As per the project contract documents, the FIE and the DDE will determine those documents that apply to  Scope of Supply requirements.  For some projects, all documents listed will apply, for other projects only a few documents will apply. The project engineering and procurement procedures, for document preparation, checking and approval will remain the same.
To Section 3.1: Instrument Design Criteria
Where customer provided instrument design requirements are provided, the DDE will review and clarify as required.   The DDE will provide the Instrument and Control Design criteria as per the owners requirement and as per standard engineering practices.
Where the customer has not provided instrument design requirements in the contract documents, the DDE will provide the Instrument and Control Design criteria as per  standard 8-PRC-0002 and as per standard engineering practices.
Project Engineer is to provide an opportunity to FIE to review and comment on the Instrument Design Criteria prior to final publication and issue.
To Section 3.2: Plant Instrument Numbering
Where applicable the Plant Instrument Numbering will follow Standard-8-PRC-0001.  If project requirements dictate the use of a Client numbering procedure, the DDE will revise Standard-8-PRC-0001 to include the Clients requirements and publish accordingly.  The modification to include the Client numbering system will be an add-on to the standard document with a statement clearly defining the added section as the procedure to be used on the project.  The standard portion of the document is not to be revised.
To Section 3.3: Project Drawing List – Field Instrument
The FIE is responsible for creating and maintaining a current project drawing list.  The drawing list is to include contract deliverables.  The project document, “0-LST-0001: Document List” will be provided to the FIE to be used to form the basis of the document list.  The FIE will update the list and return it to the Project Engineer.  The Project Engineer will update and maintain the project file accordingly.  The FIE will update the document on a regular basis and provide it to the Project Engineer as required.
To Section 3.4: Project Drawing List – Detail Design Drawings
The DDE is responsible for creating and maintaining a current project drawing list.  The drawing list is to include contract deliverables.  The project document, “0-LST-0001: Document List” will be updated by the DDE.  The DDE will maintain this document in a current state.
To Section 3.5: Piping and Instrumentation Drawings (P&IDs)
Process department will assign standard  Instrument Tag/Loop numbers on the Stage I thruough to Stage III P&ID’s.  When required by contract, the FIE will be responsible for assignment of the clients Instrument Tag/Loop numbers on the Instrument Index, cross referencing to the  standard numbers as per Stage III P&ID’s.  See 8-PRC-0001: Plant Instrument Numbering for details of Instrument Tag Numbering.  The FIE is responsible for maintaining this cross-reference for the duration of the project.   Design Office will be responsible for updating the P&ID’s with the client Instrument Tag Numbers.
To Section 3.6: Instrument Index
The FIE will commence the Instrument Index after Stage III P&IDs have been issued Approved for Design.  The FIE (where required) will use the clients instrument tag number as the key field and  instrument number will be for reference.  Where clients instrument numbers are not required,  standard instrument number will be the key field.
The FIE is responsible for all Instrument Tag updates to the Instrument Index and to manage the dual numbering requirements as per the project contract documents.
Note that it is in the best interest of the Project to delay commencement of the Instrument Index database as late as possible.  The later Instrument Index work is commenced, the less rework will be required through the project design period due to Mechanical/Process revisions.
To Section 3.7: Instrument Hydraulic Requirements
Hydraulic calculations will be performed by mechanical or process.  Results of Hydraulic Calculations will be incorporated in the Process Data List (PDL) as required
Hydraulic calculations are usually performed late in the project, once PID's are firm and the pipe models are sufficiently complete.  However, for long lead instruments such as control valves, the hydraulic information is required as soon as practicable.  Critical pathing, scheduling and expediting of activities for long lead instruments requiring hydraulic calculations should be co-ordinated by the project engineer with input from the FIE and design office.
To Section 3.8: Instrument Process Data List (PDL) - Preliminary
FIE will produce the preliminary process data lists seeded only with Instrument Tag Numbers, Service Description and P&ID reference. It is the intention that the FIE need only provide this document once and that the record information provided remain unchanged.
The accuracy of the preliminary PDL’s is dependent on the accuracy of the P&ID’s.  Any changes to the P&ID’s will cascade through the various documents, PDL, Specification Sheets, Vendors Quotation, Purchase Requisition, Bid Tab sheets and the Purchase Order.   Once the Instrument Process Data List – Preliminary has been published and provided to , revisions to the PDL will be the responsibility of  Process Engineer.
To Section 3.9: Instrument Process Data List (PDL) - Final
The Process Lead is responsible for completing and publishing the final Instrument PDL’s
Required process information and Instrument Hydraulic information will be compiled by Process and included in the PDL to meet the needs of the Instrument Specification.  Process department is responsible to ensure the PDL’s are maintained in a current state and relate to the current issue of the P&IDs.  Process will co-ordinate with  Mechanical and Design Office to ensure that data is complete.
Where the PDL allows for 3 basis of information (ie: Minimum/Normal/Maximum), the Process Engineer is required to fill in all fields.  If the information is not applicable the Process Engineer will enter the data N/A.  If the information is duplicated between basis, the process Engineer will enter the equivalent data in both fields.
Note that the Pipe specification and size will be as per Piping Line List and P&IDs.  This information is not to be duplicated in the Instrument PDL’s.
The FIE will transfer the detailed information from the Final Instrument PDL’s to the Instrument Specification Sheets.
To Section 3.10 : Pipe Specifications
The project pipe specifications will be provided to the FIE.  In the event that the pipe spec’s are revised during the course of the project, they are to be immediately reissued to the FIE.   The FIE will use the pipe specifications and the Standard ANSI Piping Connections for Instrumentation in the design of the field instruments.
To Section 3.11 : Standard ANSI Piping Connections for Instrumentation
Standard ANSI Piping Connections for Instrumentation will provide the PROCESS PIPING connection sizes for the various instrument types, based on Piping Specifications.  The actual Instrument fitting size may vary from the Standard Process Connection.  Where possible the Instruments will be specified to mate to these standards.  Where it is impractical to provide the mate to these standards, Instrument Installation details will specify the appropriate adapters required.
Where the FIE is unable to provide instruments that mate directly to the sizes as listed in the Standard ANSI Piping Connections for Instrumentation, they will immediately inform  Instrument Engineer of the variance.   Instrument Engineer will immediately inform the DDE of any variances.  The DDE will provide the necessary adapters on the Instrument Installation Details to complete the installation.
To Section 3.12 : Instrument Specification Sheets
Where required, FIE will create Instrument Specification Sheets for field provided instrumentation.  Instruments provided with mechanical packages will not have  Instrument Specification sheets created.
Specification sheets are developed from Stage III P&ID’s, Process Data Lists, Site Design Conditions and Piping Specifications.  The process engineer is responsible for checking the input of data as per the Process Data Lists and the Site Design Conditions.  To assist the checking requirements of the Process Engineer, the FIE will provide all applicable information in a spread sheet format obtained from the instrument database.
The pipe specification and size will be as per Piping Line List and P&IDs.
Process connections sizes will be obtained from the Piping Specifications and the Standard ANSI Piping Connections for Instrumentation spread sheet.
Expedient development of the Instrument Specification sheets is dependent on providing the FIE with complete and accurate feed documents.

To Section 3.15: Instrument Location Drawings
The DDE will provide instrument Location Drawings.  Instrument Location Drawings will depict the location of all field mounted instrumentation, the elevation of the instruments, electrical power supply requirements and instrument air requirements.  Plant GA’s and elevations will be utilised in the production of the Instrument Location Drawings.
All in-line type instruments and vessel trim instruments will be input into the PDMS model by Design Office.  All other instruments and Junction Boxes will be input into the PDMS model by the DDE.
To Section 3.16: Instrument Junction Box Drawings
The DDE will provide Instrument Junction Box Drawings.  Instrument Junction Box drawings will provide all details required to supply and fabricate completed boxes.  Quantity of Instrument Junction boxes is dependent on the plant layout and Instrument locations as per the Instrument Location Drawings.  DDE will determine the optimum number of Instrument Junction Boxes required for the project.
When Instrumentation discrete signals are 120 VAC or 220 VAC, separate junction boxes will be allowed for, segregating the Analog signals from the discrete signals.  When Instrumentation discrete signals are a low voltage DC signal (ie: 24 VDC), separate junction boxes separating the power supplies from the analog signals is preferred however when there is a small quantity of signals, a single box with segregated terminal strips will be permitted.  This will allow for separate multiconductor cables to the plant control system.
To Section 3.17: Instrument Loop Diagrams
The DDE will provide Instrument Loop Diagrams.  Instrument Loop Diagrams will contain all wiring details of field installed instruments as per the Instrument Index.  In addition where Mechanical Packages are supplying instruments shipped loose, the DDE will provide loop diagrams.
When the client maintains DCS responsibility, DCS I/O details may not be shown on loop diagrams.  The DDE will determine the DCS contractual requirements from the contract package documents.

To Section 3.28: Vendor Documentation Requirements (VDR) – Field Instruments
The FIE will provide a “8-VDR-8XXX: Vendor Documentation Requirements” form (VDR), for each instrument requisition.
VDR forms will generally be quite typical however some exceptions may be required to facilitate Hazardous Area Certificates, Calibration Certificates etc.
Calibration Certificates are required wherever it is possible to have the vendors provide this certification.  Exceptions will generally be simple devices such as pressure gauges and temperature gauges.  The project engineer is to be advised when Calibration Certificates may increase the cost of the Purchase Order by an excessive amount.  Note that if the Calibration Certificates are waived, the site installation costs will increase due to instrument calibration requirements on site for instruments without calibration certificates.
