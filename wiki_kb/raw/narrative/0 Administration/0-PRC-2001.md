# 0-PRC-2001 — Procedure


[TABLE: 0-PRC-2001_table_1]


## Purpose
This procedure describes how personnel will be assigned to the positions of responsibility for a project.

## Responsibilities
All procedures assign project execution responsibilities to departments or design disciplines.
A general description of project execution responsibilities is given in 0-PRC-2005: Project Responsibility. The Project Manager may modify this document to reflect specific responsibility assignments for a project. It will be checked by the Director of Projects and approved by the Director of Engineering.

## Project Manager
The Director of Projects will assign the Project Manager.

## Project Engineer
The Director of Projects and the Project Manager will decide whether the project is of sufficient size and complexity to require a Project Engineer
When a Project Engineer is not assigned, the relevant responsibilities will be assigned to other project team members (see Section 2).

## Discipline Lead
Each design discipline will have a Discipline Lead assigned by the Discipline Manager / Chief Engineer.
The Discipline Leads will either directly, or indirectly by assigning tasks to a discipline team, execute all work assigned for which they are responsible. The Discipline Leads will check the work assigned (0-PRC-7004: Checking and Approval of Design).

## Discipline Approver
Each design discipline will have a Discipline Approver assigned by the Discipline Manager / Chief Engineer.
The Discipline Approvers will approve the work assigned (0-PRC-7004: Checking and Approval of Design).

## Commissioning Manager
The Director of Technology will assign the Commissioning Manager, with the agreement of that person’s Discipline Manager / Chief Engineer.

## Other Personnel
The respective Department Managers will assign other project personnel as required.

## Project Responsibility List
The assignment of key personnel will be recorded on the form 0-LST-2004: Project Responsibility by the Project Manager. The list will be checked by both the Director of Engineering and the Director of Projects, and will be approved by the President.
The Project Manager will record new assignments or reassignments by revision of form 0-LST-2004: Project Responsibility. The revisions will be checked and approved as above.
Commentary
To Section 2 and 4: Responsibilities may be reassigned to allow balancing of individual workloads, personal development, or to accommodate the specific needs of each project. For example responsibilities may be assigned or shared between the Project Manager and the Project Engineer, or in the case where no Project Engineer is assigned, the responsibilities may be distributed among the Project Manager and one or more of the Discipline Leads.
To Section 5: Assignments will be made on the basis of experience, maturity, and demonstrated accuracy. A single individual may be assigned multiple responsibilities in their discipline assuming adequate experience, and appropriate project size and scope.
To Section 6: There may be multiple assignments of Design Discipline Approver for different types of work (e.g. for documentation for different types of equipment). It will be usual for the Discipline Lead to be assigned Discipline Approver responsibilities. This will mean that the Discipline Lead will approve her own work, which is permissible providing she has sufficient experience, maturity, and demonstrated accuracy. If the Discipline Lead is not assigned Discipline Approver responsibilities, they will generally be taken by the Discipline Manager / Chief Engineer.
