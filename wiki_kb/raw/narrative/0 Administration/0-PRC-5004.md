# 0-PRC-5004 — Procedure


[TABLE: 0-PRC-5004_table_1]


## Purpose
This procedure describes how commercial terms will be presented for Requests for Quotation and for Purchase Orders, and assigns responsibility for preparation, checking and approval.

## Standard Conditions
These terms are not project- or order-specific, and are presented in one of two forms:
0-SPC-5001: Standard Conditions for Purchase (Short Form)
0-SPC-5002: Standard Conditions for Purchase (Long Form)
The standards are checked by the Director of Projects, and approved by Legal.
The documents will be issued for the project without modification (0-PRC-0001: Approval and Use of Standards).
0-SPC-5001: Standard Conditions for Purchase (Short Form) may be used under the following conditions:
Order value less than CAD 200,000, and
No conditions imposed by the Order Risk Assessment (0-PRC-5003: Bid Evaluation and Approval), and
No site work.

## Special Conditions
These terms are presented in 0-SPC-5003: Special Conditions for Purchase, and they are terms that are normally project-specific. They will always be used when 0-SPC-5002: Standard Conditions for Purchase (Long Form) are used.
The standard version of this document contains clause titles only, a description of the circumstances when the clause may be required and in some cases optional wording. The descriptive material is set out in square brackets and is deleted before the document is issued.
Special Conditions for Purchase may be identified as being required by the project risk management strategy.
0-SPC-5003: Special Conditions for Purchase will be prepared specifically for each project by Procurement, checked by the Director of Projects, and approved by Legal.

## Order-Specific Conditions
These terms are inserted directly into the body of the Request for Quotation or Purchase Order, and they are terms that are normally order-specific. Typical subjects covered by Order-Specific Conditions are:
Terms of payment
Liquidated damages for delay
Liquidated damages for performance
Performance bonds or bank guarantees
Standard clauses for these subjects are appended to this procedure. This text may be varied by modification of this procedure and issued for the project (0-PRC-0001: Approval and Use of Standards). The revised document will be approved by Legal.
Order-Specific Conditions will be applied to an order as directed by the Order Risk Assessment (0-PRC-5003: Bid Evaluation and Approval).

Free Issue Materials.
Free Issue Materials are materials provided by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics to the Vendor free of charge, for incorporation in the Goods and Services.  Free Issue Materials remain the property of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
The Vendor protects Free Issue Materials and maintains them in the condition in which they were provided.  The Vendor only uses the Free Issue Materials for incorporation into the Goods and Services.
Any loss, waste of or damage to the Free Issue Materials after they have been provided to the Vendor is the Vendors risk.  Any surplus Free Issue Materials will be dealt with as the Project Buyer or Lead Engineer instructs.
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ risks include loss of or damage to any Free Issue Materials until the Vendor has received and accepted them.
The Vendor includes the amount advised by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for the replacement of any materials provided by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics in the insurance taken out by the Vendor for loss of or damage to the Goods and Services.
