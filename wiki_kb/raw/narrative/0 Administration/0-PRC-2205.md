# 0-PRC-2205 — Procedure


[TABLE: 0-PRC-2205_table_1]


## Purpose
This procedure describes how payment terms and commercial terms will be presented for Engineering Subcontracts, and assigns responsibility for preparation, checking and approval.

## Standard Conditions
These terms are presented in 0-SPC-2201: Standard Conditions for Engineering Subcontract, and they are terms that are not normally project- or order-specific.
0-SPC-2201: Standard Conditions for Engineering Subcontract are in the form of the NEC Professional Services Contract published for the Institution of Civil Engineers by Thomas Telford Publishing, London.  An electronic copy of the document will be reviewed and approved for the project (0-PRC-0001: Approval and Use of Standards).  The conditions of use of this copyright document are set out in 0-PRC-2206: Issue of Engineering Subcontract.  Any modification necessary is dealt with in 0-SPC-2202: Special Conditions for Engineering Subcontract and must be approved by Legal.

## Special Conditions
These terms are presented in 0-SPC-2202: Special Conditions for Engineering Subcontract.  The Special Conditions include:
Full text for special conditions which will always be used to modify the NEC form for use by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics; and
Special conditions which are project-specific and are presented as clause titles only, a description of the circumstances when the clause may be required and in some cases optional wording.
The descriptive material is set out in square brackets and is deleted before the document is issued.
Special Conditions for Engineering Subcontract may be identified as being required by the project risk management strategy.
0-SPC-2202: Special Conditions for Engineering Subcontract will be prepared specifically for each project by the Project Engineer, checked by the Director of Projects, and approved by Legal.

## Subcontract-Specific Conditions
These terms are inserted directly into the body of the Agreement and they are terms that are normally subcontract-specific.  Typical subjects covered by subcontract-specific Conditions are:
Data associated with pricing and payment
Identification of applicable Secondary Option Clauses of 0-SPC-2201: Standard Conditions for Engineering Subcontract
Data associated with Secondary Option Clauses:
Parent company guarantee;
Delay damages;
Security;
Limitation of Liability.
Standard clauses for these subjects are appended to this procedure. This text may be varied by modification of this procedure and issued for the project (0-PRC-0001: Approval and Use of Standards). The revised document will be approved by Legal.
The subcontract-specific Conditions to be applied will typically be selected as part of a project risk management strategy, and will be indicated on the Project Execution Plan (0-PRC-3001: Project Planning). Additional conditions may be applied to a subcontract as a result of the bid evaluation process (0-PRC-2204: Engineering Subcontractor Evaluation and Approval).
