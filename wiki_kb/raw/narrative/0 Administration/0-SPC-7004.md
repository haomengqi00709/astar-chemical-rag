# 0-SPC-7004 — Specification


[TABLE: 0-SPC-7004_table_1]


## Purpose
This specification describes the requirements for Engineering Subcontractor’s quality assurance.

## Quality Plan
Subcontractor will establish, implement, and maintain a Quality Plan to ensure that all services comply with the requirements of the Subcontract. Preferably the Quality Plan will reference Subcontractor’s existing Quality Management System.
The Quality Plan will include the elements listed below, which are considered the minimum requirements and do not relieve Subcontractor from considering and complying with any other criteria deemed necessary to obtain an effective Quality Plan.
It is anticipated that Subcontractor might have previously received approval by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics of his Quality Management System and referenced documents. In this case the Quality Plan need simply refer to these approved documents and should certify that no revisions have been made since their approval.

### Management and Control
Subcontractor will identify the groups or persons within his organisation responsible for the establishment and effective implementation of the Quality Plan and for the verification of Quality Plan compliance.
Subcontractor will document the organisational structure, functional responsibilities, and levels of authority and reporting lines for activities affecting quality.

### Documents
Subcontractor will control documents referenced by the Quality Plan such as procedures, instructions, specifications, and drawings, to ensure that they are reviewed for adequacy, approved for release by authorized personnel, and distributed appropriately. Any changes to such documents will receive the same degree of review and approval as the originals.
Subcontractor will retain all documents that furnish evidence of the quality of the services.

### Resources
Subcontractor’s personnel assigned to perform the services will be competent on the basis of education, training, skills and experience.
Subcontractor will provide any additional specific training in the requirements of the Quality Plan and its implementing documentation, and will record the training given.

### Services
Subcontractor will have documented procedures for the provision of the services and for review of the services for completeness, adequacy, and conformance with the requirements of the Subcontract.
Design change control procedures will be consistent with the controls applied to the original design.

### Defects / Non-Conformances
Subcontractor will have documented procedures for identifying and documenting Defects in the services or other non-conformances with the Quality Plan, for taking appropriate corrective action, and for enacting necessary changes to the Quality Plan or its referenced documents to prevent their recurrence.

## Quality Plan Approval
Subcontractor will submit the Quality Plan together with all referenced documents for the approval of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics as set out in the Subcontract Data. Any changes to the Quality Plan or any referenced documents will require the approval of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics before being implemented.

## Quality Audit
The services will be subject to audit for assurance of their accuracy and compliance with the requirements of the Subcontract. On request, and for the purpose of audit, Subcontractor will transmit to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics documentation that has been properly checked and approved in accordance with the Subcontractor’s Quality Plan.
Following a review of Subcontractor’s documentation, the documents will be returned to Subcontractor as Adobe Portable Document Format copies either with Defects marked-up as such, or marked as “Audited: No Defects Noted” and digitally signed by the person conducting the audit.
Failure of an audit to identify a Defect in the services will not relieve the Subcontractor of his obligations to perform the services in accordance with the Subcontract.

## Audit Response
Upon receipt of audited documents with Defects noted, the Subcontractor will follow the steps for correction and prevention of recurrence set out in the Quality Plan or its referenced documents.
