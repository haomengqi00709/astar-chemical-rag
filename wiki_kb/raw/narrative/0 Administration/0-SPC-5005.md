# 0-SPC-5005 — Specification


[TABLE: 0-SPC-5005_table_1]


## Purpose
This Specification defines the minimum requirements for the pre-shipment preparation and packaging of goods for marine transportation.

## Scope
This specification describes general requirements; specific packaging requirements may be called up on the Purchase Order.

## Responsibilities Of Vendor
The Vendor will ensure that all goods are adequately packaged to provide protection against physical damage, temperature extremes, corrosion, pilferage, condensation, rain, and salt water spray.
All packaging materials become the property of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and are non-returnable.
Packing must be adequate for extended storage.  The Vendor will advise Aker Kvaerner Chemetics of any particular storage or handling requirements at the time of order placement.
The Vendor will comply with all regulations governing the transportation of dangerous goods. The Vendor will advise Aker Kvaerner Chemetics of dangerous goods at the time of order placement.

## Pre-Shipment Preparation
The Vendor will drain, dry, and clean the goods prior to packaging.
Flange faces will be covered with an adequate shipping gasket and a 12mm wood or 5mm steel cover and secured with bolting.  Pipe or nozzle openings prepared for field welding will have protective covers secured by adhesive tape. All other openings will be plugged, capped or otherwise closed.
The vendor will provide blocking, bracing or other support necessary to prevent damage caused by shock loading or vibration.
In general, goods will be packaged and shipped fully assembled ready for the intended use. The Vendor may disassemble the goods as necessary to prevent damage during shipment, or to comply with allowable shipping dimensions or weights.

## Over-Dimensional Loads
Four weeks in advance of the scheduled shipment date the Vendor will supply transport information for any goods exceeding the capacity of a standard forty-foot ocean container; approximately 1200 cm in length, 230 cm in width and 230 cm in height. This may include but is not limited to detailed transport drawings, lifting and handling instructions and estimated transportation weights and dimensions.

## Packaging
The type of packaging will be one of the methods described in the following sections, as called up on the Purchase Order.
The Vendor will package the goods in a manner consistent with the size, weight and characteristics of the goods, to allow an even distribution of weight, and to allow the packages to be handled using cranes, forklift trucks or bogies/dollies.
Wooden packaging will be made of hardwood or conifer timbers of good quality, without cracks, loose knots or other defects. Plywood is acceptable, but no other manufactured sheeting is permitted. Conifer timbers must be free of bark, grub holes (holes larger than 3mm across), visible signs of living pests, and have a moisture content of less than 20 percent.
Use of hay, straw, flax combining, palm packing fibre, brown coconut fibre, used hessian bags or hessian linings in packing is not permitted.

### Wooden Crating
The goods will be fully enclosed by wooden crating fastened securely using spiral type galvanised nails.
The goods will be strapped to base pallets using steel bands applied with stretching tool and secured with crimped steel seals.
Items within a single crate will be partitioned, braced, blocked or secured to the base to prevent harmful contact with each other.

### Palletised
The goods will be secured to racks, cradles, or saddles and protected by draping or wrapping with a water-resistant medium.
The Vendor will design and supply all items required for shipment and handling, such as supports, special bracing, and spreader bars. The Vendor will provide drawings showing the details of all such items for the approval of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics before proceeding with their manufacture. Shipping saddles will be designed to withstand all imposed loads and to prevent damage or permanent distortion to the goods. The minimum contact arc length will be 120°.
Tensioned steel bands, straps, wire rope or other suitable means will be used to keep the goods securely attached to the supports during handling and transporting. Protection will be provided at band locations to prevent damage to painted items.

### Ocean Freight Containers
The goods will be placed within standard twenty and forty-foot ocean freight containers.
Containers will be loaded using as much of the interior capacity as possible, and will be loaded so as to allow unloading using standard forklifts where practical.
Where the size of items requires them to be dragged while loading or unloading the container, the Vendor will mount them on skids, sleds, or runners.
Bulk commodities will be shipped in bags or boxes, or will be shrink-wrapped and strapped to pallets or skids.
The vendor will block and brace the container contents to prevent shifting while in transit.
Where a Vendor-supplied shipping container is called up on the Purchase Order, it will not be returned to the Vendor; on delivery it will be considered a part of the packaging supply and the property of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. The container must have an independent marine surveyor’s certificate of seaworthiness, and must have all marks and numbers not pertaining to the contract removed.

## Shipping Marks
Packages will be clearly identified by painting or dye stencilling on the top and two sides of the exterior. Lettering will be black, at least 8 centimetres high. When palletised packaging is used the marks will be applied to a securely mounted plywood placard. For example:
For Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Purchase Order Number 96W11100-4006 delivered in a total of six packages, each will be identified as: 96W11100-4006-1/6, 96W11100-4006-2/6, etc.
The following marks will be applied:
Handling, lifting and storage instructions/restrictions supplemented by standard international pictorial symbols will be clearly stencilled on both sides of each crate. For example:
All packages exceeding 2,000 kg will have the centre of gravity marked on the projected location on three sides, with the letters "C.G." and shipping weight adjacent to the marks.

## Packing Lists
The Vendor will provide a separate packing list for each package. The Vendor will follow the line item numbering and equipment descriptions shown on the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics purchase order. In cases where the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics purchase order is less detailed than the Vendors itemised list, the Vendor’s more detailed list will be used.
General line items descriptions such as “one lot assembly nuts and bolts” are not acceptable. The packing list will detail the number and unit type of each item.
The Vendor will attach the packing slip to the outside of each crate under a protective metal plate and place a duplicate packing slip, in a waterproof package clearly marked “Packing Slip”, inside the crate.
On large , the packing slip will be located in a position that will permit easy access by a person standing at ground level.

## Shipping Notification
Vendors will fax or email a shipping notice two weeks in advance of readiness for shipment. The shipping notice will contain the following information and attachments:
Address of factory, contact name, phone, fax and email address.
Estimated number of packages in shipment
Estimated weights and dimensions (kg & cm) of each package
Type of packing
Special handling instructions
Material safety sheets for hazardous goods
Vendors will fax or email a final shipping notice twenty-four hours in advance of readiness for shipment. The shipping notice will contain the following information and attachments:
Final number of packages in shipment
Final weights and dimensions (kg/cm) of each package
Final packing list for each package
Shipping notices will be faxed or emailed to:
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Logistics Group
Phone 604 734-1200
Fax 604 737-4408
Email: logistics@kvaerner.com
The vendor will allow two business days for Aker Kvaerner Chemetics to complete shipping arrangements.

## Contents Inspection
Prior to closing packaging the Vendor will notify Aker Kvaerner Chemetics that the goods are packaged and ready for an open-package inspection. This open-package inspection may be waived at the discretion of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

## Shipping Documentation
The Vendor will contact the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Logistics Group as above for instructions on the distribution of the following shipping documents:
Ocean Bills of Lading
Certificates of Origin
Free Trade Certificates
Insurance Certificates
Customs Invoices
Fumigation, Heat or Pressure Treatment Certificates
