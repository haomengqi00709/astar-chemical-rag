# 0-SPC-7002 — Specification


[TABLE: 0-SPC-7002_table_1]


## Purpose
This specification describes the requirements for Vendor’s quality assurance for the supply of bulk goods, and should be read in conjunction with the Quality Data Sheet for the goods.

## Quality System
The Vendor will have a system of controls in place to accomplish the following requirements:

### Procurement Control
Vendor will follow documented procedures for the control of subcontractor/supplier selection.

### Training and Qualification
Vendor’s personnel responsible for performing quality related activities will possess the necessary experience, background and training to perform their assigned functions.
Personnel responsible for selecting goods to supply against a customer’s order will be familiar with

### Handling And Storage
Storage and handling of the goods will be accomplished in a manner that will prevent damage or deterioration.

### Identification and Control
Identification will be maintained on the or in documentation identifiable to the from initial receipt providing traceability to appropriate quality records such as Material Test Reports.

### Receiving Inspection
All goods will be inspected for damage and for conformance to purchase order requirements. Marking of goods must comply with the relevant specification. will be verified and correlated with markings on the goods. Any non-conforming goods will be returned to or quarantined until the non-conformance is rectified.

### Quality Records
Vendor will retain all documents furnishing evidence of the quality of the goods.

## Quality Surveillance
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics

### Level 0 – No Quality Surveillance
No quality surveillance will be carried out.

### Level 1 – Final Quality Surveillance
Surveillance will be carried out in a single visit upon completion of the manufacture of the goods, or at some other agreed stage prior to packaging for shipment.

## Reporting Requirements
Vendor will immediately report to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics any event that has or could have significant impact on quality

## Inspection Release
Vendor will not release the goods for packaging or shipping until all the activities specified in the Quality Data Sheet have been satisfactorily completed, and the quality documentation has been sent to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
For goods with Surveillance Level 1 the Vendor must receive an Inspection Release from the Inspector before proceeding with packaging or shipping.
