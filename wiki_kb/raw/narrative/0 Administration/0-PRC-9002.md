# 0-PRC-9002 — Unknown


[TABLE: 0-PRC-9002_table_1]


## Purpose
This procedure describes how to request business travel, the authorisations required, and how the bookings are made.

## Group Travel Procedure
All business travel will comply with the requirements of the 11.1 PR 2 Group Travel Procedure which can be accessed through the eNet.
The 11.1 PR 2 Group Travel Procedure requires travellers to “behave as though you were paying for the journey yourself”, including the use of the most cost-effective airlines and routing.

## Travel Request
Each traveller will complete 0-FRM-9002: Travel Request.
The traveller will indicate the following for each destination:
Latest required arrival dates and times
Earliest required departure dates and times
Business or Economy Class
Requirement for a hotel booking
Requirement for a rental car
The traveller will state the purpose of the trip and the cost-code(s) to be charged.
The traveller will indicate the need for and the Company will assess the requirements for:
US Immigration letter
Business or visitors visa
Assignment Uplift entitlement
Cash advance
If requesting travel bookings that deviate from the 11.1 PR 2 Group Travel Procedure the traveller will state the deviation requested and the reasons for it. This will be most common in the case where the traveller’s plans are subject to possible change and the ticket is required to be refundable, which is not usually the lowest-cost option.

## Business Class Travel
Business class travel is only allowed for journeys of more than nine hours continuous flying, or twelve hours total flying time.  Note: Business class is not allowed on assignment uplifts (1 month or more), employee development, training, home leave or R&R.

## Authorisation
The traveller will be responsible for obtaining the following authorisations for the journey:
the traveller’s direct supervisor (Dept. Manager), and
the person responsible for the budget being charged (Budget Manager)
If a deviation from the 11.1 PR 2 Group Travel Procedure is requested the traveller will be responsible for obtaining the following additional authorisation:
the traveller’s senior line manager (Director or equivalent)

## Use of the Travel Request Form
The travel request form can be completed and signed digitally using Adobe Acrobat, in which case it should be circulated by email. Alternatively it can be printed out and completed and signed manually then scanned and forwarded as a .pdf file attached to an email.

## Travel Bookings
The fully approved travel request must be emailed by the final approver to the distribution list DL-ECVAN-Travel with a copy to the traveller. The distribution list includes:
the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics designated travel agency
Human Resources
the HSE Manager
Accounting
the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics designated travel medical services provider
The Aker Kvaerner Chemetics designated travel agency will advise the traveller of the travel itinerary options. The Aker Kvaerner Chemetics designated travel agency has been instructed to provide options that:
Comply with the 11.1 PR 2 Group Travel Procedure or any authorised deviation;
Are within the lesser of 10% or $500 of the most economic carrier and routing;
Are within mid-range business accommodation costs for accommodation.
If the traveller wants a change to the itinerary that does not comply with the above, then the traveller must revise the Travel Request Form stating the explicit deviation requested and have it re-authorised.

## Travel Health Considerations
The Aker Kvaerner Chemetics designated travel medical services provider will provide vaccination and medical services in accordance with 0-PRC-8102-R1 Travel Health and Safety.

## Travel Documentation
Human Resources and/or the Travel Coordinator will advise the traveller of any requirements for visas or other travel documentation, and the Company will arrange to obtain them.

## Changes to Original Travel Authorization Form
The method for making changes is to contact the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics designated travel agency, and copy your Aker Kvaerner Chemetics travel coordinator.
If the traveller is requesting a change to a restricted ticket then they should determine the cost of that change and ensure that it is a justifiable business expense. Changes often cost more than the original journey and should be evaluated in the context of “behaving as though you were paying for the journey yourself”.
