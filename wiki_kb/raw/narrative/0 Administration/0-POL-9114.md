## Objective
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics believes that vacation time spent away from your job is both necessary and desirable for your physical and mental health. In accordance with Aker Kvaerner Policy 11.1 Group People Policy, we actively support the creation of an appropriate balance between work and life outside work.

## Eligibility
This policy applies to all regular employees of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ Vancouver office.

## Vacation Year
The vacation year is January 1 to December 31, and vacation will accrue on a monthly basis.
Employees may take their vacation during the year in which it is earned

## Pay-out of Unused Vacation
Unused vacation can be carried forward to the next vacation year.  Any carried-forward vacation time may be paid out at the employee’s request.
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics

## Vacation Entitlement
Employees earn and receive vacation according to the greater of their position Hay Point level and their length of service, according to the following chart.

[TABLE: 0-POL-9114_table_2]

Entitlements will be pro-rated to promotion dates or to anniversary dates.

## Statutory Holidays
All British Columbia Statutory holidays are observed in the Vancouver office. Employees are allowed 3 additional days off with pay between the Christmas statutory holiday and the New Years statutory holiday.

## Scheduling Of Vacations
Scheduling of vacation will be the joint responsibility of the employee and their supervisor and will be subject to operational requirements.
An employee will submit vacation requests by email to their supervisor and obtain approval before vacation can be taken. The supervisor should copy the Office Services Co-ordinator when approving requests for time off so that central absence records can be updated.

## On Termination
Employees leaving Aker Kvaerner Chemetics will be paid their earned vacation entitlements that have not been taken as a lump sum with the employee’s final pay. If an employee has taken vacation in excess of the amount earned this excess will be deducted from their final pay.
