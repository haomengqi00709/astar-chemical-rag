# 0-POL-8001 — Unknown


[TABLE: 0-POL-8001_table_1]


## Purpose
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics (“Chemetics”) is committed to providing safe workplaces for its employees and maintaining high standards of conduct to ensure health, safety and productivity.  Therefore, the purpose of this policy is to minimize the risk of impaired performance due to substance use in order to ensure a safe, healthy and productive workplace.

## Policy
While on Chemetics business or on any Chemetics Work Site:
The unauthorized use or possession of any alcoholic beverage is expressly prohibited.
The use, possession, distribution, or offering for sale of any Controlled Substance and the distribution or offering for sale of any alcoholic beverage is expressly prohibited.
Employees shall ensure that their work performance is not impaired by alcohol or substances which may affect the Employee’s mental or physical abilities.

## Scope
This policy applies to all Employees, Consultants, and Agency Subcontractors providing services to Chemetics.  For ease of reference throughout this policy, the use of the term “Employees” shall also include Consultants and Agency Subcontractors.
Clients may have their own drug and alcohol policy for work on Clients’ own sites, which Client implements and enforces.  Employees need to be familiar with such Client policies when working at any Client work site.  Should an Employee have any concerns regarding Client policies, the Employee should raise such concerns with Human Resources as soon as Employee is aware of the Client policies.

## Definitions

### Employee
Means any individual on the payroll of Chemetics working on a permanent/temporary and/or full-time/part-time basis whose pay is quoted hourly, weekly, bi-weekly, semi-monthly, monthly or annually.

### Consultant
Means any independent contractor hired by Chemetics to provide a specific service for a specific period of time.

### Agency Subcontractor
Means any individual under Chemetics’ direction and control whose services are retained through a third party for a contracted period of time, or for assignments of short duration, or for a specific service.

### Client
Means any individual, company, organisation, or other entity for whom Chemetics is performing work.

### Chemetics Work Site
Means any Chemetics, Client, or vendor work site location where Chemetics is performing work. This includes but is not limited to offices, construction sites, plant sites, and other places where business is being conducted.

### Illegal Drug
Means any drug not legally obtained or legally obtainable, which can include prescription drugs obtained illegally or not being used for their prescribed purposes.

### Prescription Drug
Means any drug which is legally prescribed to an individual and which is being used for the purposes for which it was prescribed or manufactured.

### Non-Prescription Drug
Means any drug which may be legally bought without a medical prescription and which is not illegal to possess.

### Controlled Substance
Means any (a) Illegal Drug or (b) any Prescription Drug obtained and used without a prescription or which is used in excess of the therapeutic dosage prescribed by a doctor.

### Reasonable Cause
Means a belief based on observed, specific and objective facts.

### Safety-Sensitive Position
Means a position with limited or no supervision in which impaired performance could result in direct and significant risk of injury to the Employee, others or the environment; or a supervisory position where the Employee is supervising individuals in  positions as noted above.  A Safety-Sensitive Position may include a temporary assignment of an Employee even where an Employee’s regular job position is not a Safety-Sensitive Position.  Safety-Sensitive Positions will normally include only positions or assignments on location at or directly controlling activities at a plant site.

## Explanation
Chemetics provides prevention, rehabilitation and aftercare support and resources for Employees who suspect they have a substance dependency or an emerging alcohol or drug problem.  Employees concerned about or experiencing alcohol or drug-related problems are encouraged to seek assistance from Chemetics’ Employee and Family Assistance Program (“EFAP”), their personal physician, or appropriate community service before job performance is affected or violations of this policy occur.  Once a problem has been identified, the employee should contact Human Resources who can assist in arranging appropriate support.  No prejudicial or disciplinary action will be taken against an Employee based on participation in EFAP or other support program.  However, Employees who breach this policy will still be subject to normal disciplinary action in accordance with nature and degree of the violation.

### Prescription and Non-Prescription Drugs
Employees taking Prescription Drugs or Non-Prescription (over-the-counter) Drugs are responsible for being aware of any potential effect such drugs may have on their reactions, judgment, or ability to perform their duties, and to ensure that their performance is not impaired by the use of such drugs.  If an Employee suspects that work performance may be impaired by use of such drugs, he/she should discuss alternate job duties or other appropriate actions with his/her manager.

### Travel while on Chemetics business
For the duration of travel while on Chemetics business, Employees are expected to act responsibly in his/her ‘off hours’ so as to be able to perform his/her work duties, protect his/her own safety and the safety of others, and not compromise Chemetics’ interests.

## Non-Compliance Procedures
Employees who breach this policy will be subject to normal disciplinary action as a job performance issue.  Chemetics response to breach of this policy by an Employee may include but is not limited to, requiring counseling or treatment, increased supervision, paid leave, unpaid suspension, probation, or termination.  Chemetics may require that the Employee undergo drug and alcohol testing in certain situations, in accordance with Clause 6.2.
Chemetics’ determination of an appropriate response to breach of this policy by an Employee will take into account all applicable circumstances, including but not limited to:
the health and safety of the Employee and others,
circumstances of the incident,
prior violations,
supervisory nature of Employee’s position,
if the Employee is in a Safety-Sensitive Position,
Clients’ requirements,
work productivity and schedules,  and
any other relevant considerations.

### Investigation
Chemetics will carry out investigations into the circumstances surrounding a breach of this policy by an Employee.
In post-incident or “near miss” situations, the Employee shall fully cooperate with the investigation and shall ensure that they do not ingest any substance or take any action which may affect the results of any substance testing required under Clause 6.2.

### Drug and Alcohol Testing
Chemetics may require that an Employee undergo drug or alcohol testing in the following situations:
Drug testing:
Post-incident or “near miss”, where there is Reasonable Cause to believe that drug impairment contributed to the incident circumstances, but only as part of a larger assessment and investigation.
Where there is Reasonable Cause to believe that an Employee’s work performance is impaired by drugs, but only as part of a larger assessment and investigation.
Post-reinstatement only as part of a larger assessment of the Employee.
Alcohol testing:
Post-incident or “near miss” where there is Reasonable Cause to believe that alcohol impairment contributed to the incident circumstances.
Where there is Reasonable Cause to believe that an Employee’s work performance is impaired by alcohol.
Post-reinstatement only as part of a larger assessment of the Employee.
Random testing for Safety-Sensitive Positions by a method which indicates impairment.
Drug and alcohol testing will be performed by a medical doctor by conducting a physical examination and by taking of blood and/or urine samples for testing to support the medical observations. The samples will be analysed by an accredited laboratory, and as directed by the examining medical doctor. The examining medical doctor will be required to make a report to Chemetics on his findings and express a professional opinion of the degree of impairment found.

## Applicable law
Nothing in this policy is intended to contradict the BC Human Rights Code or other applicable legislation.  To the extent that any clause or portion of a clause conflicts with any applicable law, such conflicting portion of this policy shall not apply.
