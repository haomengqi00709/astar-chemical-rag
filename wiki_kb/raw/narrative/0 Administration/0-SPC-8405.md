# 0-SPC-8405 — Specification


[TABLE: 0-SPC-8405_table_1]


## Purpose
This specification describes the minimum health safety and environment (HSE) standards that Aker Kvaerner Chemetics construction subcontractors must meet in order to be eligible for the award of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics construction subcontracts.

## Aker Kvaerner Chemetics HSE Policy
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics is committed to:
Providing a safe and healthy workplace for all employees, subcontractors and neighbours.
Conducting operations in an environmentally sound manner.

## Value of Subcontractor Safety Record
Only those subcontractors who have demonstrated management leadership and systems resulting in excellent environmental, safety and health performance will be considered to participate on our construction projects.

## Subcontractor HSE Qualification Requirements
To qualify to perform site work for Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, subcontractors must:
Have a written Corporate Commitment to Health, Safety and Environment
Have a documented Health Safety and Environment program that meets government and construction safety organizations having jurisdiction in the locale of the project
Have a company substance abuse policy
Ensure strict compliance with these construction safety procedures, as well as with all applicable local, state, and federal regulations.
Agree that all subcontractors employed by the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Subcontractor on Aker Kvaerner Chemetics work will meet the requirements listed here.
Submit to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics the response to Aker Kvaerner form 0-REP-8405-R0 “Subcontractor Survey”
Agree that all subcontractor employees communicate with a level of proficiency, and to the extent necessary to ensure safety.
Provide supporting Health Safety & Environment information as requested, to verify the Subcontractor’s ability to comply with applicable health safety and environment requirements.

## Accepted Performance Values
The following performance values, for each of the preceding 5 years, will be used to gauge performance whenever possible:
Total number of construction man-hours for year
Number of fatalities
Number of recordable injuries or illness with lost workdays
Number of recordable injuries or illness without lost workdays
Number of days away from work due to non-work related sick leave.
The Recordable Incident Rate (RIR) is to be computed using the following formula:
(c+d) x 200,000 / (Total-number-of-worked ) = Incident rate
The Lost Time Injury Rate (LTIR) is to be computer using the following formula:
(c) x 200,000 / (Total-number-of-worked ) = Incident rate

### Subcontractor HSE Performance Standards
Subcontractor is to submit the above values for the previous five years.
Subcontractor will meet or better the average health and safety performance of the construction industry in the region where the project is located.
Subcontractors should:
Have a Workers’ Compensation Experience Modification Rate (EMR) of 1.0 or less for the last 3 years. Must be verified by workers’ compensation insurance provider, via a letter on their letterhead.
Have a Recordable Injury Frequency Rate (RIR) equal to or less than national average.
Have a Lost Time Injury Frequency Rate (LTIR) equal to or less than national average.
If a subcontractor fails to pass under the criteria prescribed above, the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Safety Department will analyze and evaluate other aspects of the Subcontractor’s safety program to make a determination if the program can become qualified.  Qualification under this scenario may require monitoring of Subcontractor’s safety program on other current projects.

## Construction Site Safety General
The Subcontractor will, at its own expense, cooperate with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the owner in all reasonable safety precautions in connection with the project.  The Subcontractor shall comply with all the requirements of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or the owner (or any governmental authority having an interest in the work) concerning the secrecy of any part or aspect of the work or of the project and will not permit any person to enter upon the premises of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or the owner at the site of the work or elsewhere except in accordance with the safety and security requirements of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, the owner, or such governmental authority having an interest in the work.

## Owner’s Safety Manual

#### Subcontractor will strictly adhere to the Owner’s Environment, Health and Safety requirements.

## Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Construction Safety Manual

#### Subcontractor will also strictly adhere to the corporate Safety Manual of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, issued by Aker Kvaerner.  A compact disk version of the manual will be made available to the Subcontractor.

## Subcontractor Safety Manual
The Subcontractor will ensure that its own safety procedures are updated to incorporate the requirements listed under this Subcontract.  Where the safety procedures of the Subcontractor are less stringent than the ones listed in this Specification, the more stringent will apply.

## Site Emergency Preparedness and Evacuation Plan
The main construction Subcontractor will prepare a site emergency evacuation plan prior to sending trade workers to the work site. This plan is to be submitted to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Owner for review and acceptance.
The plan will contain the following elements:
Signals and Alarms
Evacuation routes
Assembly points
Responsibilities of emergency supervisory personnel.
Emergencies covered by the plan:
Weather emergency
Earthquake
Fire
Hazardous materials handling
Any site specific emergencies

## Subcontractor Responsibilities during Construction
Following all regulations, codes and other legal obligations regarding the performance of the work.
Ensuring that employees and subcontractors have received appropriate training and are competent to perform assigned work.
Providing personal protective equipment (PPE) and other necessary equipment to all employees working in areas/operations requiring PPE.
Briefing employees on any unique or unseen hazards that they may encounter on their job site.
Using a permit system to authorize potentially hazardous activities.
Proactively use Job Hazard Analysis (JHA) and Task Safety Awareness (TSA) to promote the safety mindset.
Supplying copies of Material Safety Data Sheets for all chemicals prior to bringing on-site.
Providing all equipment necessary to perform the contracted work and ensuring that that equipment is properly maintained in good working order.
Removing (and properly disposing of) all waste, chemicals, and empty chemical containers brought on-site.
Conduct regular safety audits of the work site.
Hold regular safety meetings (minimum weekly frequency) where representation is 50% management or supervisory employees, and 100% are non-supervisory trade employees.
Hold toolbox safety meetings.
Immediately reporting any hazards, accidents or incidents to the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Construction Manager, and follow up with a written report within 7 days of the incident.
Provide Aker Kvaerner Chemetics with monthly HSE performance report in a format designated by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

## Monitoring Subcontract Safety Programs
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics will monitor the subcontractor’s safety program to ensure compliance with applicable Health and Safety standard for the worksite.  The subcontractor is expected to report all incidents, and to submit a monthly HSE report to the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Site Manger.
