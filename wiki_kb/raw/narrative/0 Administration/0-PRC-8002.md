## Purpose
This procedure outlines the process used to collect Health Safety and Environmental performance statistics.  This procedure ensures compliance with the Health Safety and Environment Policy of Aker Kvaerner, and Group Procedure 2.2 PR 1 “HSE Reporting Procedure”.

## Definitions
HSE – Health Safety and Environment

## Reporting Process and Responsibilities
Each Business Unit shall record the required HSE data (defined under 3.2) into the Aker Kvaerner Synergi database.
The HSE data shall be quality checked by an HSE function and approved by the BU/BA president each month.
Each BU and BA is required to have quality checking routines in place.
The dates for submission of the verified HSE data shall follow the financial reporting timetable.
For BA’s where Synergi is not in use for the BA as a whole, the BA shall submit the Quarterly HSE Report to Group HSE, complete and authorized, on behalf of all companies within their area, on a quarterly basis.

## Company Standard Key Measures of Performance
Lagging Indicators
Number of deaths or permanent disability
Lost Time Injury Frequency  (LTIF)
Total Recordable Injury Frequency (TRIF)
Sick Leave as a percentage of worked hours
LTIF and TRIF indicators are based on one million man-hours and the sum of the last 12 months of exposure hours (rolling average).
Sick leave is based on the sum of exposure hours year-to-date.
Leading Indicators
Number of task risk analysis per employee during the month (multiplied by 12)
Number of risk observations per employee during the month (multiplied by 12)
Number of near-misses per employee during the month (multiplied by 12)
Inspections per employee during the month (multiplied by 12)
Safety Training as a percentage of worked hours (based on monthly man-hours total)
All information required for the calculation of these key measures is to be collected and reported at the end of each month by the designated person at each work site.

## Synergi – Management of Health and Safety Cases and Statistics
Synergi is the AK Group database used to record OHS information for the purpose of knowledge sharing across the Group, and for producing HSE performance statistics.
As instructed in Group guidelines AK Chemetics HSE managers will:
Enter HSE incidents into the database within 7 days of occurrence.
Enter monthly exposure hours and HSE performance statistics within 7 days after month end.
Where a site HSE manager is not able to enter information directly into the database, the Company HSE Manager or designate will perform the task of behalf of the field HSE Manager.

## Responsibilities
The collection of HSE performance statistics is the responsibility of the HSE Manager at the Works site, or his designate.  At sites where there is no full-time HSE Manager, the person having responsibility for HSE matters will also have the responsibility for collecting HSE performance statistics.
The HSE Manager will have management systems in place to ensure that HSE performance information is collected and tallied accurately.
At the end of the month the HSE will report the information to the Company using the standard reporting document.

## Month End
HSE Statistics are collected at the end of each month.  Month-end is defined by the accounts department of Group, and it is generally the end of last Saturday of a given month.  Group accounts department publishes a calendar of month end dates at the beginning of each accounting year.

## Scope of Inclusion
The Company HSE Statistics report will include the HSE performance of:
Direct employees without regard for the worksite where the exposure occurs.
All employees at worksites where the Company is in direct control of operations.
Subcontractors and indirect subcontractors at worksites where the Company is in direct control of operations.

## Collection of Information
The HSE Manager will have management systems in place to ensure that HSE performance information is collected and tallied accurately. Where necessary the HSE will collect HSE performance data from subcontractors and audit that information on a regular basis.

## Reporting
The HSE performance data for the site is collated and communicated to the Company HSE Manager in the Standard reporting Spreadsheet, not later than 4 days after month end day.
An Excel spreadsheet is provided to facilitate the HSE exposure rate calculations.  Consult company HSE Manager to acquire the latest version of the spreadsheet.
