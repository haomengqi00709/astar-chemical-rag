# 0-PRC-8012 — Procedure


[TABLE: 0-PRC-8012_table_1]


## Purpose
This procedure outlines the guidelines to be followed when requesting a variance to an approved Health Safety and Environment procedure or guideline found in the Aker Kvaerner or in the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Health and Safety manuals.

## Responsibilities
The designated work site Health and Safety Manager, or the work site Manager is responsible for completing the request and providing any information required to support the case for a variance.
In the case where there is no designated Aker Kvaerner Chemetics HSE Manager, the senior Aker Kvaerner Chemetics employee will make the request.

## Requirements
Variances will only be approved if the variance request results in an equally effective or better protection of health, safety and environment.

## Procedure Process
Required variances to established Health Safety and Environment procedures should be discussed in advance with the Company HSE Manager.
Variances will be approved by the Company HSE Manager, and the new HSE procedure will be documented prior to implementation.  All variance requests will be considered for permanent inclusion in the established HSE policies and procedures.
