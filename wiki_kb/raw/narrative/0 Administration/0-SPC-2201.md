## Compensation events

### The following are compensation events.
(1)	Aker Kvaerner Chemetics gives an instruction changing the Scope.
(2)	Aker Kvaerner Chemetics does not provide something which he is to provide by the date for providing it required by the Accepted Schedule.
(3)	Aker Kvaerner Chemetics changes a decision which he has previously communicated to the Subcontractor.
(4)	Aker Kvaerner Chemetics does not reply to a communication from the Subcontractor within the period required by this Subcontract.
(5)	Aker Kvaerner Chemetics gives an instruction to resolve an ambiguity or inconsistency in or between the conditions of Subcontract and the Scope and Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ interpretation is not one that could have been reasonably anticipated by the Subcontractor.
(6)	A breach of contract by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics which is not one of the other compensation events in this Subcontract.
(7)	The services are affected by any of the following events
war, civil war, rebellion, revolution, insurrection, military or unsurped power,
strikes, riots and civil commotion not confined to the employees of the Subcontractor and SubSubcontractors.
ionising radiation or radioactive contamination from nuclear fuel or nuclear waste resulting from the combustion of nuclear fuel,
radioactive, toxic, explosive or other hazardous properties of an explosive nuclear device,
natural disaster,
fire and explosion,
impact by aircraft or other aerial device or thing dropped from them.

## Quotations for compensation events

### Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may instruct the Subcontractor to submit alternative quotations based upon different ways of dealing with the compensation event which are practicable. The Subcontractor submits the required quotations to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and may submit quotations for other methods of dealing with the compensation event which he considers practicable.

### Quotations for compensation events comprise proposed changes to the Prices and any delay to the Completion Date assessed by the Subcontractor. The Subcontractor submits details of his assessment with each quotation. If the Schedule for remaining work is affected by the compensation event, the Subcontractor includes a revised Schedule in his quotation showing the effect.

### The Subcontractor submits quotations within one week of being instructed to do so by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics replies within one week of the submission. His reply is
an instruction to submit a revised quotation,
an acceptance of a quotation,

### Aker Kvaerner Chemetics instructs the Subcontractor to submit a revised quotation only after explaining his reasons for doing so to the Subcontractor. The Subcontractor submits the revised quotation within one week of being instructed to do so.

### The cost of preparing quotations for compensation events is not included in the assessment of compensation events.

## Assessing compensation events

### The changes to the Prices are assessed as the effect of the compensation event upon
the Time Charge for the work already done, and
the forecast Time Charge for the work not yet done.

### A delay to the Completion Date is assessed as the length of time that, due to the compensation event, planned Completion is later than planned Completion as shown on the Accepted Schedule.

### Assessments are based upon the assumptions that the Subcontractor reacts competently and promptly to the compensation event, that the additional Time Charge and time due to the event are reasonably incurred and that the Accepted Schedule can be changed.

### The Subcontractor includes in his quotation for a change to the Scope, proposed staff rates for people for whom there are no staff rates, consistent with Prices in the activity schedule.

### The following are deducted from the assessment of compensation events
the cost of events for which this Subcontract requires the Subcontractor to insure and
other costs paid to the Subcontractor by insurers.

### Assessment for changed Prices for compensation events are in the form of changes to the activity schedule.
