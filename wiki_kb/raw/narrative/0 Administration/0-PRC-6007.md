# 0-PRC-6007 — Procedure


[TABLE: 0-PRC-6007_table_1]


## Purpose
This procedure describes how to maintain commercial control of construction subcontracts.

## Responsibility
The Subcontract Administrator is responsible for the control of construction subcontracts with the following objectives:
Timely identification and quantification of all changes in the Works Information
Accurate forecasts of the total cost of the works
Minimal close-out effort due to retrospective cost claims

## Reference
Sections 26 to 29 of 0-SPC-6001: Standard Conditions for Construction Subcontract deal with Compensation Events, which are generally defined as events which will result in a change (generally an increase) to the Prices, and / or a change (generally an extension) to the Completion Date.

## Compensation Events
The most commonly encountered compensation events will be where Aker Kvaerner Chemetics gives an instruction changing the Works Information. This will include any of the following:
Change in quantity of the works caused by changes to documents issued by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics between status “Issued for Quotation” and status “Approved for Construction”
An extension to the Completion Date caused by late delivery of Equipment and Materials by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics
Modification of work already completed caused by changes to documents issued by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics with status “Approved for Construction”
A Field Order providing instruction to rectify an error or omission in documentation issued by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics
Issue of an explicit or implicit instruction by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics by e-mail message or as minutes of meetings
0-SPC-6001: Standard Conditions for Construction Subcontract describes potentially lengthy steps for review and quotation in response to the issue of an instruction. In order to ensure that there are no undue delays in delivery of the works, the more specific steps described in this procedure will be followed.

## Issue of Revised Documents
The Subcontract Administrator will monitor the Document Control Database for revisions to documents already issued to the Subcontractor with status “Approved for Construction” (0-PRC-0006: Control of Project Documents). The Subcontract Administrator will then periodically direct Document Control to issue revised documents to the Subcontractor, the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Site Manager, and to the Owner.
Upon issue of the revised documents, the Subcontract Administrator will issue a notice to the Subcontractor with copy to the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Site Manager as follows:
“Our communication KCI-SUB-[024] contains Document Transmittal [KC-SC-0047].
The documents referenced by the Document Transmittal contain revisions to the Works Information and may be a compensation event. The Subcontractor will immediately proceed with the work shown on the revised documents. The Subcontractor will submit a notice and quotation, including a revised schedule, for any compensation event resulting from the revised documents in accordance with Sections 27 and 28 of 0-SPC-6001: Standard Conditions for Construction Subcontract.”

## Request for Additional Work
Additional work will be handled separately from revisions to documents already issued because as an alternative, it could be let to another subcontractor, and it may be acceptable for it to have a different Completion Date from that already agreed for the subcontract.
The Subcontract Administrator will then direct Document Control to issue documents describing additional work to the Subcontractor and to the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Site Manager.
Upon issue of the documents, the Subcontract Administrator will issue a notice to the Subcontractor with copy to the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Site Manager as follows:
“Our communication KCI-SUB-[028] contains Document Transmittal [KC-SC-0053].
The documents referenced by the Document Transmittal contain a proposed revision to the Works Information. The Subcontractor will submit a quotation, including a revised schedule, for the work shown on the documents in accordance with Section 28 of 0-SPC-6001: Standard Conditions for Construction Subcontract.”

## Issue of a Field Instruction
Field Instructions are issued to the Subcontractor in response to technical queries (0-PRC-6092: Construction Technical Queries). Often the Field Instruction will direct the Subcontractor to “Proceed with the work described immediately” and to “Determine the actual cost of the work and submit a claim for compensation”. The Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Site Manager will approve the claim for compensation.
At each assessment date the Subcontract Administrator will execute a Change Order that compiles all Field Instructions completed since the previous assessment date and for which cost claims have been approved.

## Issue of Communications
Any communication issued by the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Site Manager or by the Subcontract Administrator to the Subcontractor containing an instruction will have one of the following texts appended:
“The foregoing is an instruction to the Subcontractor given by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics which may change the Works Instructions and may be a compensation event. The Subcontractor will immediately proceed with the instruction. The Subcontractor will submit a notice and quotation, including a revised schedule, for any compensation event resulting from the revised documents in accordance with Sections 27 and 28 of 0-SPC-6001: Standard Conditions for Construction Subcontract.”
“The foregoing instruction is a proposed revision to the Works Information. The Subcontractor will submit a quotation, including a revised schedule, for compliance with the instruction in accordance with Section 28 of 0-SPC-6001: Standard Conditions for Construction Subcontract.”

## Response to Notice of Compensation Event
The Subcontract Administrator will respond to any notice from the Subcontractor of the occurrence of a compensation event. The response will be either acceptance or rejection of the claim that the action is a compensation event. If the decision is made to accept the claim that the action is a compensation event, the Subcontract Administrator will request a quotation.

## Acceptance of Quotation
Section 28.3 of 0-SPC-6001: Standard Conditions for Construction Subcontract describes the possible actions by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics following receipt of a quotation for a compensation event. Should the quotation be accepted a formal notification will be issued in the form of 0-CHG-6XX0: [Template] Subcontract Change Order.
Change Orders will be prepared by the Subcontract Administrator and will be numbered (0-PRC-0002: Document Numbering) as for example 0-CHG-6X1YYY where “X” is the equivalent digit used for the Subcontract Agreement (0-PRC-6002: Form of Subcontract Agreement), and “YYY” is a sequential addendum.
Change Orders will be published (0-PRC-0005: Publishing of Project Documents) with status “Approved for Construction” and will be checked by the Manager of Construction and approved by the Project Manager.
The approved Change Order will be printed and physically signed by the same authorised signatory of the original Subcontract. The Subcontract Administrator will forward the signed and authorised paper copy of the Change Order to Document Control for issue to Subcontractor (0-PRC-0006: Control of Project Documentation).

## Assessing Compensation Events
If the Subcontractor does not provide an acceptable quotation for a compensation event then Aker Kvaerner Chemetics assesses the compensation event in accordance with Section 28.4 of 0-SPC-6001: Standard Conditions for Construction Subcontract.
The Subcontract Administrator determines the increase in cost and extension of time resulting from the compensation event and issues a Change Order accordingly. The Subcontractor’s only recourse under these circumstances is to put the matter into dispute.

## Forecasts
The Subcontract Administrator will prepare forecasts of the total cost of the Subcontract as requested by the Project Manager. The forecasts will take into account any instructions issued to Subcontractor for which quotations have not yet been received or accepted.
