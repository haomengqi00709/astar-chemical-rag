# 0-PRC-2101 — Procedure


[TABLE: 0-PRC-2101_table_1]


## Purpose
This procedure describes how Project Execution Model (PEM) Control Level Milestone Gate Reviews are to be carried out, recorded and reported.

## Application
This procedure applies to projects larger than CAD $3,000,000 or 3,500 home-office hours.

## Responsibility

### PEM Steering Committee
Primary responsibility for applying this procedure rests with the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ PEM Steering Committee consisting of the President, the Director of Engineering and the Director of Projects.
The PEM Steering Committee is responsible for:
Ensuring that PEM is used on all applicable projects
Approving final reports from Milestone Gate Reviews
Facilitating the sharing of best practice and lessons learned from PEM application.
Facilitating independent auditing of the Milestone Reviews results to maintain consistency and accuracy

### PEM Gate Review Facilitator
PEM Gate Review Facilitator will be assigned to each project in accordance with:
0-PRC-2001: Assignment of Project Responsibilities
0-LST-2004: Project Responsibility
The facilitator is responsible for:
Preparing template for the control object status check list
Leading the Review with the project team
Preparation of the Summary of Findings at the conclusion of the Review

### Project Responsibility
The Project Manager is responsible for:
Planning and organizing the Milestone Gate Reviews.
Inviting participants, from both within and outside the project team.
Expediting review preparation with Discipline Leads and Department Managers.
Ensuring all documentation required for the review is available.
Producing Action Plan (prepared by Project Engineer)and Risk Mitigation Plan (prepared by Project Manager) following the Review
Producing the Report to the PEM Steering Committee
Tracking actions resulting from the approved Report (expediting by project Engineer)

## Planning of Milestone Gate Reviews
For each individual project the dates of PEM Milestone Gate Reviews will be established from the start and published as follows:
The PEM will be fully integrated in the project schedule by coding individual activities to reflect the PEM Control Level Stage(s) in which they occur.
The schedule will include a milestone for each applicable PEM stage.
Milestone Gate Reviews dates will be published as part of the 0-DAT-3001:Project Schedule

## Minimum Requirements for Milestone Gate Reviews

### Process of the Gate Review:
Preparation:
PEM Gate Review  Facilitator prepares the template of control object status check list.
Discipline Leads report actual status of the control objects on the check list.
Validation of the check lists by the Discipline Manager
Updates of Project Managers Report, Project Schedule, Cost Reports, Risk Reports, Change Notifications, HSE Status and Action Plan from the previous gate review.
Review:
Review of reports (as detailed above) by Project Manager, Project Engineer and PEM Gate Review Facilitator. Others will be requested to attend as appropriate.
Selective probing and spot checks
Discussion and agreement of major findings and actions required
Report:
Preparation and issue of report as described in section 6.0.

### Schedule
A ‘typical’ Gate Review timing is as follows:
Preparation – 1 – 3 days depending upon size of project.
Review – 1 – 2 days depending upon size of project
Summary of Findings – Issued within 2 days of the Review
Action Plan and Risk Mitigation Plan - Issued within 3 days of the Summary of Findings
Report to PEM Steering Committee – Issued within 7 Days of the Review

### Scope of Gate Review:
Reviews shall be carried out to establish project performance relative to:
Applicable Milestone requirements as specified in the Corporate Common PEM for each applicable Project Execution Key Activity Group i.e.
Concept Execution
HSE in Design
System Engineering
Procurement
Layout & 3D Model
Sub-contracting
Fabrication / Construction
Civil works and buildings
Completion
Applicable Milestone requirements as specified in the Corporate Common PEM for each applicable Project Management Key Activity Group i.e.
Concept Management
Project Execution Strategy
HSE Management
Cost Estimating / Control
Planning & Scheduling
Risk Management
Weight/Quantity Management
Information Management
Quality Management
Contract / Sub-contract Management
Change Management
Any approved changes to these requirements made by the PEM Steering Committee

## Requirements for Milestone Gate Review Reports

### Report to PEM Steering Committee will include as a minimum, the following:
Executive Summary including:
Significant PEM Milestone requirements which have not been achieved
Risk Mitigation Plan addressing risk / cost / schedule
Recommendations and timescales for corrective action
Detailed Findings including:
Detailed report, by exception for each Project Execution and Project Management Key Activity Group.
Detail Action Plan with nominees and dates for completion agreed within the project team.

## Tracking Actions from the approved Report
It is vital that the status of the actions from the milestone gate review is tracked in order to ensure completion by the agreed date. The Project Engineer will maintain the Action Plan current, updating it accordingly as the actions are completed. Completion of all actions constitutes clearing of the PEM gate.
