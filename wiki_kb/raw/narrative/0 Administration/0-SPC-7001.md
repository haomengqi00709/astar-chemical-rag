# 0-SPC-7001 — Specification


[TABLE: 0-SPC-7001_table_1]


## Purpose
This specification describes the requirements for Vendor’s quality assurance, and should be read in conjunction with the Quality Data Sheet for the goods.

## Quality Plan
Where required in the reference Quality Data Sheet, Vendor will establish, implement, and maintain a Quality Plan in accordance with this Section. The Quality Plan will document the commitment and implementation of the Quality Plan, and will detail the procedures used to ensure that all items, activities, or services comply with the requirements of the Purchase Order. Preferably the Quality Plan will reference Vendor’s existing Quality Management System.
The Quality Plan will include the elements listed below, which are considered the minimum requirements and do not relieve Vendor from considering and complying with any other criteria deemed necessary to obtain an effective Quality Plan.
It is anticipated that Vendor might have previously received approval by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics of their Quality Management System and implementing procedures. In this case the Quality Plan need simply refer to these approved documents and should certify that no revisions have been made since their approval.

### Organization
Vendor will identify the groups or persons within his organisation responsible for the establishment and effective implementation of the Quality Plan and for the verification of Quality Plan compliance.
Vendor will document the organisational structure, functional responsibilities, and levels of authority and reporting lines for activities affecting quality.

### Design Control
Vendor will have documented procedures for the design of the goods and for review of the design for completeness, adequacy, and conformance with the requirements of the Purchase Order. Design change control procedures will be consistent with the controls applied to the original design.

### Process
All Vendor activities affecting quality of the goods will be executed in conformance with documented instructions, procedures and drawings. The documents will include appropriate acceptance criteria for determining that the activities have been satisfactorily accomplished.

### Document Control
All quality related documents will be reviewed for adequacy, approved for release by authorized personnel, and distributed appropriately. Documents will include, but are not limited to procedures, instructions, specifications, drawings and procurement documents. Changes to documents will receive the same degree of review and approval as the original documents.

### Procurement Control
Vendor will have documented procedures for subcontractor/supplier selection, bid evaluation, review and approval of subcontractor/supplier quality plan. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics will be advised of sub-contracted items or equipment.
Applicable requirements of this specification will be included in the procurement documents. Vendor will perform periodic audits or surveillance of its subcontractor/supplier quality plans, and source or receipt inspections to verify conformance of purchased items.

### Identification and Control of Items
Only correct and accepted materials and components (items) will be used in the goods. Identification will be maintained on the items or in documentation identifiable to the items from initial receipt or fabrication until installation or use. Such identification will provide traceability to appropriate specifying documentation and quality records. Vendor identification and control procedures will preclude the use of any items after their useful life has expired.

### Training and Qualification
Vendor’s personnel responsible for performing quality related activities will possess the necessary experience or background to perform their assigned functions. Personnel will receive appropriate training, certification or qualification and be familiar with the requirements of the Purchase Order.

### Inspection and Test
The Quality Plan will incorporate the Inspection and Test Plan described in Section 3.

### Measuring and Test Equipment
Vendor procedures will control measuring and test equipment calibration, certification and release to the shop floor.

### Handling and Storage
Vendor procedures for the packaging, handling, and storage of materials, items, and the goods while fabrication is in progress will prevent damage, loss or deterioration.

### Non-Conformances
The Quality Plan will incorporate procedures for handling non-conformances as described in Section 5.

### Quality Records
Vendor will retain all documents that furnish evidence of the quality of the goods.

## Inspection and Test Plan
Vendor will plan, implement and document inspections and tests to verify conformance of materials, components, or the goods to specifications. This program will be provided to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics in the form of an Inspection and Test Plan. As a minimum, the Inspection and Test Plan will include the activities listed on the reference Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Quality Data Sheet.
Inspection or test results will be documented and conformance with specifications will be evaluated and approved by Vendor’s authorised personnel.

## Quality Surveillance
The goods and any included materials or components will be subject to quality surveillance by designated Inspectors, either Aker Kvaerner Chemetics staff, or the personnel of Subcontractor surveillance agencies. Additionally, they may be subject to quality surveillance by the Owner or Owner’s representative. These individuals will be granted access to any and all parts of Vendor and Sub-Vendor facilities where work is being executed. This will include access to manufacturing, quality control and engineering documents.
The level of quality surveillance will be as stated in the reference Quality Data Sheet.

### Level 0 – No Quality Surveillance
No quality surveillance will be carried out.

### Level 1 – Final Quality Surveillance
Surveillance will be carried out in a single visit upon completion of the manufacture of the goods, or at some other agreed stage prior to packaging for shipment.

### Level 2 – Limited Scope Quality Surveillance
Surveillance of activities noted on the reference Quality Data Sheet will be carried out upon notification by the Vendor of readiness to inspect.
Where activities are denoted as witness points, Vendor will advise Aker Kvaerner Chemetics in writing at least five (5) working days in advance of the operation, each time the operation is performed, so that it may be witnessed. Vendor may proceed with the work past the witness point if Aker Kvaerner Chemetics is not available at the appointed time.
Where activities are denoted as hold points, Vendor will advise Aker Kvaerner Chemetics in writing at least five (5) working days in advance of the operation, each time the operation is performed, so that it may be witnessed. Vendor will not proceed with the work beyond the hold point without witness by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics except by written agreement from Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
Inspection or test activities which are not denoted as witness or hold points will be verified or monitored by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics during scheduled shop inspection visits.

### Level 3 - Full Scope Quality Surveillance
Similar to level 2, with more witness and hold points identified, and continued regular monitoring visits by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

### Level 4 – Resident Quality Surveillance
Requires continuous surveillance by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or designated third party inspector resident in Vendor’s facility.

## Non-Conformances
Vendor will report any non-conformances to the Inspector as soon as they have been discovered. Similarly, any non-conformances identified by the Inspector during surveillance visits will be identified to the Vendor in writing.
The Vendor will promptly propose corrective action, including any inspection or testing, to remedy the non-conformance and will further propose action to prevent recurrence of the non-conformance.
The Vendor will not proceed with the corrective or preventive actions until Aker Kvaerner Chemetics approves them in writing, indicating whether any inspection or testing will be a witness or hold point.
Following completion of the corrective action, the Vendor will forward to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics documented results of any inspection or testing indicating conformance with specifications as evaluated and approved by Vendor’s authorised personnel.

## Reporting Requirements
Vendor will immediately report to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics any event that has or could have significant impact on quality, cost or schedule.
Vendor will issue to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics a status report at the frequency stated in the reference , containing a summary of the following activities:
Revisions to Quality Plan or implementing procedures.
Status of preventive actions for non-conformances
Changes to the production schedule.

## Inspection Release
Vendor will not release the goods for packaging or shipping until all the activities specified in the approved Quality Data Sheet and Inspection and Test Plan have been satisfactorily completed, and the quality documentation is available and acceptable to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
For goods with Surveillance Level 1,2,3, or 4 the Vendor must receive an Inspection Release from the Inspector before proceeding with packaging or shipping. The Inspection Release only applies to the goods stated in the title, and will list any remaining work required by the Vendor prior to packaging the goods.
