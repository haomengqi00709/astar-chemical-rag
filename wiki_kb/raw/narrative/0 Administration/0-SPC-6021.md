# 0-SPC-6021 — Specification


[TABLE: 0-SPC-6021_table_1]


## Purpose
This procedure describes the methods by which Construction Subcontractors will schedule the works to set out the sequence of activities, and to provide the means for measuring progress and forecasting outcome.

## Application
The Subcontractor will prepare and update a schedule in the form of a bar chart using SureTrak Version 5.0 or later.
For works larger than 3,500 direct labour hours, the schedule will be developed to the levels described in this procedure.
Works below this threshold will have schedules developed at levels determined by the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Site Manager as fit for the purpose.

## Nomenclature
The level of detail provided in a schedule is denoted by the following nomenclature:

### Level 1 Schedule
Also known as a Summary Schedule, one activity bar is shown for each major work division. Major project milestones are shown.
The activities in a Level 1 Schedule are derived as summaries of the corresponding activities in a Level 2 Schedule.

### Level 2 Schedule
Also known as a Control Schedule, grouped work activities are shown for items (equipment, pipes, instruments, etc.) grouped by type and/or by functional area, typically giving up to 20 activities for each major work division. All project milestones are shown.
The activities in a Level 2 Schedule are derived as summaries of the corresponding activities in a Level 3 Schedule.

### Level 3 Schedule
Also known as a Detailed Schedule, all work activities are shown for items (equipment, pipes, instruments, etc.) grouped by type and/or by functional area. All project milestones are shown.
The status of activities in a Level 3 Schedule are derived as summaries of the status of corresponding activities in a Level 4 Schedule.

### Level 4 Schedule
Also known as a Work Plan, all work activities are shown for all items (equipment, pipes, instruments, etc.). This may be prepared as a bar chart, but is more usually prepared as a tabular checklist. Work actually completed is checked off on the list, and percent completions derived by count and scheduled resources of work completed.

## Schedule
The schedule for the works will be structured into three sub-schedules as follows:
Design
Procurement
Construction
The activities within and between the sub-schedules will be linked by logical dependencies to establish critical paths and to enable the accurate forecasting of the effects of delays or changes.
Linkage between the sub-schedules will utilise intermediate milestones to allow independent assessment and control of float within each sub-schedule.

### Design
Bar chart scheduling of design will be prepared at Level 3.
The sub-schedule will show all design work activities executed by Subcontractor’s personnel, and will show the activity durations for review and approval by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

### Procurement
Bar chart scheduling of procurement will be prepared at Level 3.
The sub-schedule will show as single activities the manufacture and shipment of each item or type of Equipment and Materials to be supplied by the Subcontractor. The sub-schedule will also show as milestones the delivery to the Subcontractor of all Equipment and Materials to be supplied by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or by the Owner.
The sub-schedule will show the off-site prefabrication of sub-assemblies such as structural steelwork or piping spools also at Level 3. Activities will have trade responsibility and direct labour hours entered to allow the preparation of resource loading histograms. The Subcontractor will also develop Level 4 Work Plans.

### Construction
Bar chart scheduling of construction will be prepared at Level 3, with Level 4 Work Plans also developed. Activities will have direct labour assignments and man-hours entered together with Construction Equipment requirements to allow the preparation of resource loading histograms.
Dates for access to the site or to portions of the site for tie-ins or other activities will be shown as milestones.

### Preparation and Acceptance
The Subcontractor will prepare the schedule within two weeks of subcontract award, and submit it to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for acceptance. Within two weeks of receipt, Aker Kvaerner Chemetics will either accept the schedule or will notify the Subcontractor of reasons for not accepting it. A reason for not accepting the schedule is:
the Subcontractor’s plans which it shows are not practicable
it does not show the information which the subcontract requires
it does not represent the Subcontractor’s plans realistically
it does not comply with the Works Information
The accepted schedule will be saved as the target, and all subsequent revisions compared to the target.

### Updating
The schedule will be updated at regular intervals as set out in 0-SUB-6X00: Construction Subcontract Agreement. Updating requires the following inputs:
add or delete activities and dependencies to reflect revised execution plan
actual activity start and completion dates
revised resource hours based on latest count of work quantities
Level 3 Procurement activity % completion derived from supplier schedules or Level 4 Work Plans in the case of prefabricated items
Level 3 Construction activity % completion derived from Level 4 Work Plans
The Subcontractor will submit the updated schedule to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics at the same time as submitting the monthly application for payment. Should the schedule show that Completion is delayed, then the Subcontractor will submit for Aker Kvaerner Chemetics acceptance an acceleration plan to remedy the delay.

## Site Logs
The Subcontractor will keep logs showing the following for each workday:
for each classification of direct labour, headcount on the site together with hours worked
for all indirect labour personnel, headcount on the site together with hours worked
a listing of each item of Construction Equipment on the site
The logs will be submitted to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics weekly.

## Direct Labour Analysis
The Subcontractor will prepare an analysis of direct labour utilisation at the same intervals as for the schedule update, submitting it at the same time.
The analysis will show the following cumulative data as a single graph for each classification of direct labour:
Target direct labour utilisation
Forecast of direct labour to be used
Actual direct labour used
Earned direct labour based on the originally allocated hours for all work completed
