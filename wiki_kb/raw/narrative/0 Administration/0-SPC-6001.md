# 0-SPC-6001 — Specification


[TABLE: 0-SPC-6001_table_1]

Based on "THE ENGINEERING AND CONSTRUCTION SHORT CONTRACT" 1ST Edition, a New Engineering Contract published by Thomas Telford for the Institution of Civil Engineers July 1999. A copy with amendments tracked will be provided to subcontractors familiar with the Standard Form upon request.
General

## Actions

### Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Subcontractor shall act as stated in this subcontract and in a spirit of mutual trust and co-operation.

## Identified and Defined Terms

### In these conditions of subcontract, terms identified in the Subcontract Data are in italics and defined terms have capital initials.

### (1)	The Parties are Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Subcontractor.
(2)	The Agreement is the document headed Construction Subcontract Agreement signed by the Parties.
(3)	The Owner is the person with whom Aker Kvaerner Chemetics has contracted to provide work including the works.
(4)	The Head Contract is the contract between Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Owner which relates to the works.
(5)	To Provide the Works means to do the work necessary to complete the works in accordance with this subcontract including all incidental work, services and actions which this subcontract requires.
(6)	Subcontract Data is information which describes the commercial terms of the subcontract and is in Section 1 of the Agreement and the documents referenced in that section.
(7)	Works Information is information which either specifies and describes the works or states any constraints on how the Subcontractor Provides the Works and is in Section 2 of the Agreement and the documents referenced in that section.
(8)	Site Information is information which describes the site and its surroundings and is in Section 3 of the Agreement and the documents referenced in that section.
(9)	Equipment and Materials are items to be included in the works.
(10)	Construction Equipment is items provided by the Subcontractor, used by the Subcontractor to Provide the Works and not included in the works.
(11)	The Completion Date is the completion date unless later changed in accordance with this subcontract.
(12)	Completion is when the Subcontractor has completed the works in accordance with the Works Information except for correcting notified Defects which do not prevent Aker Kvaerner Chemetics from using the works.
(13)	A Defect is a part of the works which is not in accordance with the Works Information.
(14)	The Defects Certificate is either a list of notified Defects which the Subcontractor has not corrected at the defects date or a statement that there are no such Defects.
(15)	The Prices are the amounts stated in the Price column of the Price List. Where a quantity is stated for an item in the Price List, the Price is calculated by multiplying the quantity by the rate.
(16)	The Price for Work Done to Date is the total of
the Price for each lump sum item in the Price List multiplied by the percent complete of that lump sum item and,
where a quantity is stated for an item in the Price List, an amount calculated by multiplying the quantity which the Subcontractor has completed by the rate.
(17)	Subcontractor’s Cost is the amount of payment made by the Subcontractor in Providing the Works (excluding any tax which the Subcontractor can recover) for
people who are employed by the Subcontractor,
Equipment and Materials,
sub-subcontracted work and
Construction Equipment
The amount for Construction Equipment includes amounts paid for hired Construction Equipment and an amount for the use of Construction Equipment owned by the Subcontractor which is the amount included in the Price List or, if no amount is included in the Price List, a reasonable amount which is no more than the amount the Subcontractor would have paid if the Construction Equipment had been hired.

## Interpretation

### In this subcontract the order of precedence of contract documents, for the purposes of determining the obligations of the Parties is:
any Special Conditions for Construction Subcontract;
Standard Conditions for Construction Subcontract;
Other documents referred to in the Construction Subcontract Agreement, read together.

## Communications

### Each communication which this subcontract requires has effect when it is received in writing at the last address notified by the recipient for receiving communications.

### If this subcontract requires Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or the Subcontractor to reply to a communication, unless otherwise stated in this subcontract, the responder replies within the period for reply.

## Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ Authority and Delegation

### The Subcontractor obeys an instruction which is in accordance with this subcontract and is given to the Subcontractor by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

### Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may give an instruction to the Subcontractor which changes the Works Information.

### Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ acceptance of a communication from the Subcontractor or of the Subcontractor’s work does not change the Subcontractor’s responsibility to Provide the Works or the Subcontractor’s liability for the Subcontractor’s design.

### Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, after notifying the Subcontractor, may delegate any of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ actions and may cancel any delegation. A reference to an action of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics in this subcontract includes an action by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ delegate.

## Access to the Site and Provision of Services

### Aker Kvaerner Chemetics allows access to and use of the site to the Subcontractor as necessary for the work included in this subcontract.

### Aker Kvaerner Chemetics provides services and other things as stated in the Works Information.

## Early Warning

### The Subcontractor or Aker Kvaerner Chemetics gives an early warning by notifying the other as soon as either becomes aware of any matter which could
increase the total of the Prices,
delay Completion or
impair the performance of the works in use.

### The Subcontractor and Aker Kvaerner Chemetics co-operate in making and considering proposals for how the effect of each matter which has been notified as an early warning can be avoided or reduced and deciding and recording actions to be taken.

## Law

### This subcontract is governed by the law of the country where the site is.
The Subcontractor’s Main Responsibilities

## Providing the Works

### The Subcontractor Provides the Works in accordance with the Works Information.

### The Subcontractor designs the parts of the works which the Works Information states he is to design.

### The Subcontractor submits the design to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for acceptance. A reason for not accepting the Subcontractors design is that
it does not comply with the Works Information or
it does not comply with the applicable law.

### The Subcontractor does not proceed with the relevant work until Aker Kvaerner Chemetics has accepted his design.

### The Subcontractor indemnifies Aker Kvaerner Chemetics against claims, compensation and costs due to the Subcontractor infringing a patent or copyright.

### Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Owner may use and copy the Subcontractor’s design for any purpose connected with construction, use, alteration or demolition of the works.

## Legislative Requirements, Codes and Standards

### The Subcontractor complies with all requirements imposed by law in respect of Providing the Works which includes:
The requirement to obtain any permit, authority, licence, consent, certificate and administrative decision;
Requirements to give notices or report something to a government authority;
The requirements under any Approvals obtained by the Owner or Aker Kvaerner Chemetics listed in the Works Information.

### The Subcontractor complies with all relevant codes and standards in respect of the works.

## Sub-subcontracting and People

### If the Subcontractor further subcontracts work, the Subcontractor is responsible for Providing the Works as if the work had not been further subcontracted.

### This subcontract applies as if a sub-subcontractor’s employees and Construction Equipment were the Subcontractor’s.

### The Subcontractor submits the name of each proposed sub-subcontractor to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for acceptance. A reason for not accepting the sub-subcontractor is that his appointment will not allow the Subcontractor to Provide the Works.

### The Subcontractor submits the proposed conditions of contract for each sub-subcontract to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for acceptance unless it has been agreed that no submission is required.

### The Subcontractor does not appoint a sub-subcontractor on the proposed conditions until the conditions are accepted. A reason for not accepting them is that
they will not allow the Subcontractor to Provide the Works or
they do not include a statement that the parties to the sub-subcontract shall act in a spirit of mutual trust and co-operation.

### Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may, having stated reasons, instruct the Subcontractor to remove an employee. The Subcontractor then arranges that the employee immediately has no further connection with the work included in this subcontract.

## Access for Aker Kvaerner Chemetics

### The Subcontractor provides access for Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and others notified by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics to work being done for this subcontract and to stored Equipment and Materials.

## Security

### The Subcontractor gives Aker Kvaerner Chemetics security. The security is provided by a financial or insurance institution Aker Kvaerner Chemetics accepts, for the amount stated in the Subcontract Data and in a form acceptable to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. The security must be given within 2 weeks of the starting date. No payment to the Subcontractor is made until the security has been given. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics has recourse to the security if a payment to which it is entitled remains unpaid for a period of five days after Aker Kvaerner Chemetics has notified the Subcontractor that it intends to have recourse to the security.

## Liens

### The Subcontractor gives Aker Kvaerner Chemetics satisfactory evidence that all amounts owing to any person related to the works have been paid.

### The Subcontractor gives Aker Kvaerner Chemetics a full and complete release from any person who has filed or registered any notice of lien, security, security interest or charge, UCC or PPSA, or its equivalent in any jurisdiction or who has provided labour or materials in relation to the works.

### If the Subcontractor does not provide the evidence or give the releases Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may assess an amount to be retained from the Subcontractor as security against any claims by any person who has provided labour or materials in relation to the works.

## Delivery of Equipment and Materials Off Site

### The Subcontractor delivers the Equipment and Materials to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics as described in the Works Information.

### Aker Kvaerner Chemetics retains title to any free issue materials.

### Whatever title the Subcontractor has to the Equipment and Materials passes to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics if it has been delivered.

### Whatever title the Subcontractor has to any part of the Equipment and Materials passes to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics if Aker Kvaerner Chemetics has made a payment which included the Price or part of the Price for the part of the Equipment and Materials.

### A payment of the Price or part of the Price for a part of the Equipment and Materials before it is delivered is a part payment and not a deposit.

### When a part payment is made, the Subcontractor holds the part of the Equipment and Materials in trust and as bailee for Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. The Subcontractor keeps the part of the Equipment and Materials free from all claims, charges and other encumbrances. The Subcontractor authorises Aker Kvaerner Chemetics to give notice of or register a charge, lien, security or security interest, make a UCC or PPSA filing, or its equivalent in any jurisdiction, evidencing Aker Kvaerner Chemetics interest in the part of the Equipment and Materials.

### The Subcontractor properly stores, adequately protects and labels as property for the Project each part of the Equipment and Materials whether or not title is retained by or has passed to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

### The Subcontractor does not deliver any Equipment and Materials which the Works Information states are to be tested or inspected before delivery until Aker Kvaerner Chemetics has notified the Subcontractor that they have passed the test or inspection.
Time

## Starting and Completion

### The Subcontractor does not start work until the starting date and does the work so that Completion is on or before the Completion Date.

### The Subcontractor submits a forecast of the date of Completion to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics each week from the starting date until Completion.

### Aker Kvaerner Chemetics decides the date of Completion and certifies it to the Subcontractor within one week of the date.

### Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may instruct the Subcontractor to stop or not to start any work and may later instruct the Subcontractor to restart or start it.

## Delay Damages

### The Subcontractor pays delay damages at the rate stated in the Subcontract Data for each day:
from the date by which a document is required to the date on which the document is provided; and,
from the Completion Date until Completion.

## Schedule

### The Subcontractor submits schedules to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics as stated in the Works Information.
Defects

## Warranties

### The Subcontractor gives the following warranties to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics:
The works are fit and suitable for the purposes for which they are to be used as described in the Works Information;
The Subcontractor acknowledges that Aker Kvaerner Chemetics relies on the Subcontractor’s skill and judgement in relation to the fitness and suitability of the works;
The works are of merchantable quality and free of defects; and
Unless otherwise stated in the Works Information, all materials used in the works are new and of best quality.

### The warranties benefit Aker Kvaerner Chemetics successor’s and assigns and the Owner. The warranties are in addition to warranties arising by operation of law.

## Searching for and Notifying Defects

### Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may instruct the Subcontractor to search for a Defect.

### Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may notify a Defect to the Subcontractor at any time before the defects date.

## Correcting Defects

### The Subcontractor corrects Defects whether or not Aker Kvaerner Chemetics notifies the Subcontractor of them.

### After Completion, the Subcontractor corrects notified Defects before the end of the defects correction period. This period begins at the later of Completion and when the Defect is notified.

### Aker Kvaerner Chemetics issues the Defects Certificate to the Subcontractor on the later of the defects date and the end of the last defects correction period.

## Uncorrected Defects

### If the Subcontractor has not corrected a notified Defect within its defects correction period, Aker Kvaerner Chemetics assesses the cost of having the Defect corrected by other people and the Subcontractor pays this amount.

## Repairs

### Until the Defects Certificate has been issued and unless otherwise instructed by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, the Subcontractor replaces loss of Equipment and Materials and repairs damage to the works, Equipment and Materials.
Payment

## Assessing the Amount Due

### Unless stated otherwise, the price is exclusive of any value-added tax, goods and services tax or other tax of a similar nature.

### The Subcontractor assesses the amount due as at each assessment day and applies to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for payment. There is an assessment day in each month from the starting date until the month after the Defects Certificate has been issued.

### The Subcontractor’s application for payment includes details of how the amount has been assessed. The first application for payment is for the amount due. Other applications are for the change in the amount due since the previous payment.

### The amount due is
the Price for Work Done to Date
plus other amounts to be paid to the Subcontractor (including any tax which the law requires Aker Kvaerner Chemetics to pay to the Subcontractor)
less amounts to be paid by or retained from the Subcontractor.

### Aker Kvaerner Chemetics corrects any wrongly assessed amount due and notifies the Subcontractor of the correction before paying the Subcontractor.

### The Subcontractor pays delay damages for each day from the Completion Date until Completion.

### An amount is retained from the Subcontractor in the assessment of each amount due until Completion. This amount is the retention applied to the Price for Work Done to Date. The amount retained is halved in the first assessment made after Completion and remains at this amount until the assessment day after the Defects Certificate is issued. No amount is retained in the assessment made after the Defects Certificate has been issued.

### If Aker Kvaerner Chemetics requires a schedule to be submitted, one quarter of the Price for Work Done to Date is retained in assessments of the amount due until the Subcontractor has submitted a first schedule to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics showing the information which the Works Information requires.

## Payment

### Aker Kvaerner Chemetics pays within the later of four weeks after receipt of the application for payment by the Subcontractor, or two weeks after the next assessment day which follows receipt of an application for payment by the Subcontractor.
Compensation Events

## Compensation Events

### The following are compensation events:
(1)	Aker Kvaerner Chemetics gives an instruction changing the Works Information unless the change is in order to make a Defect acceptable.
(2)	A difference between the final total quantity of work done and the quantity stated for an item in the Price List.
(3)	Aker Kvaerner Chemetics gives an instruction to stop or not to start any work.
(4)	Aker Kvaerner Chemetics changes a decision which Aker Kvaerner Chemetics has previously communicated to the Subcontractor.
(5)	Aker Kvaerner Chemetics instructs the Subcontractor to search for a Defect and none is found.
(6)	Aker Kvaerner Chemetics does not allow access to and use of the site to the Subcontractor as necessary for the work included in this subcontract.
(7)	Aker Kvaerner Chemetics does not reply to a communication from the Subcontractor within the period required by this subcontract.
(8)	Aker Kvaerner Chemetics does not provide something which Aker Kvaerner Chemetics is to provide by the date for providing is stated in this subcontract.
(9)	Aker Kvaerner Chemetics notifies a correction to an assumption about a compensation event which Aker Kvaerner Chemetics has previously stated.
(10)	A loss of Equipment and Materials or damage to the works, Equipment and Materials not the fault or responsibility of the Subcontractor.
(11)	The Subcontractor is prevented by weather from carrying out all work on the site for periods of time, each at least one full working day, which are in total more than one seventh of the total number of days between the starting date and the Completion Date. In assessing this event, only the working days which exceed this limit and on which work is prevented by no other cause are taken into account.
(12)	The Subcontractor encounters physical conditions which
are within the site,
are not weather conditions and
which an experienced subcontractor would have judged, at the date of signing the Agreement to have such a small chance of occurring that it would have been unreasonable to have allowed for them.
(13)	An event which
delays Completion by more than two weeks,
the Subcontractor could not have prevented or controlled,
an experienced subcontractor would have judged, at the date of signing the Agreement to have such a small chance of occurring that it would have been unreasonable to have allowed for it and
is not one of the other types of compensation event stated in this subcontract.
In assessing this event, only the delay in excess of two weeks is taken into account.

### In judging the physical conditions, the Subcontractor is assumed to have taken into account
the Site Information,
publicly available information referred to in the Site Information,
information obtainable from a visual inspection of the site and
other information which an experienced subcontractor could reasonably be expected to have or to obtain.

## Notifying Compensation Events

### The Subcontractor notifies to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics an event which has happened or which the Subcontractor expects to happen as a compensation event if
the Subcontractor believes that the event is a compensation event,
it is less than two weeks since the Subcontractor became aware of the event.

### An event that is not notified by the Subcontractor as a compensation event within two weeks of the Subcontractor becoming aware of the event is deemed to have no effect upon the Subcontractor’s Costs or upon Completion. The Subcontractor releases and indemnifies Aker Kvaerner Chemetics against any claim, cost or loss arising out of the event.

### The Prices and the Completion Date are not changed if Aker Kvaerner Chemetics decides that an event notified by the Subcontractor
arises from a fault of the Subcontractor,
has not happened and is not expected to happen,
has no effect upon the Subcontractor’s Cost or upon Completion or
is not one of the compensation events stated in this subcontract.
If Aker Kvaerner Chemetics decides otherwise, Aker Kvaerner Chemetics instructs the Subcontractor to submit a quotation for the event. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics notifies the decision to the Subcontractor or instructs the Subcontractor to submit a quotation within one week of the Subcontractor’s notification to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics of the event.

### If Aker Kvaerner Chemetics decides that the Subcontractor did not give an early warning of the event which the Subcontractor could have given, Aker Kvaerner Chemetics notifies that decision to the Subcontractor when instructing the Subcontractor to submit a quotation.

### If Aker Kvaerner Chemetics decides that the effects of a compensation event are too uncertain to be forecast reasonably, Aker Kvaerner Chemetics states assumptions about the event when instructing the Subcontractor to submit a quotation. Assessment of the event is based on these assumptions. If any of them is later found to have been wrong, Aker Kvaerner Chemetics notifies a correction.

### A compensation event is not notified after the defects date.

## Quotations for Compensation Events

### A quotation for a compensation event comprises proposed changes to the Prices or rates and any delay to the Completion Date assessed by the Subcontractor. The Subcontractor submits details of the Subcontractor’s assessment with each quotation. The Subcontractor submits a quotation within one week of being instructed to do so by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or, if no such instruction is received, within two weeks of the notification of a compensation event.

### Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may instruct the Subcontractor to submit a quotation for a proposed instruction or a proposed changed decision. The Subcontractor does not put a proposed instruction or a proposed changed decision into effect.

### Aker Kvaerner Chemetics replies within one week of the Subcontractor’s submission. For a proposed instruction or proposed changed decision, Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ reply is
notification that the instruction or changed decision will not be given,
notification of the instruction or changed decision as a compensation event and acceptance of the quotation or
notification of the instruction or changed decision as a compensation event and notification that Aker Kvaerner Chemetics does not agree with the quotation.
For other compensation events, Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ reply is
acceptance of the quotation or
notification that Aker Kvaerner Chemetics does not agree with the quotation.

### If Aker Kvaerner Chemetics does not agree with the quotation, the Subcontractor may submit a revised quotation within one week of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ reply. If Aker Kvaerner Chemetics does not agree with the revised quotation or if none is received, Aker Kvaerner Chemetics assesses the compensation event and notifies the assessment.

### Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may instruct the Subcontractor to submit alternative quotations for a compensation event.

## Assessing Compensation Events

### For a compensation event which only affects the quantities of work shown in the Price List, the change to the Prices is assessed by multiplying the changed quantities of work by the appropriate rates in the Price List.

### For other compensation events, the changes to the Prices are assessed by forecasting the effect of a compensation event upon the Subcontractor’s Cost or, if the compensation event has already occurred, the assessment is based upon the Subcontractor’s Cost due to the event which the Subcontractor has incurred. Effects on Subcontractor’s Cost are assessed separately for
people,
Equipment and Materials,
sub-subcontracted work and
Construction Equipment.
The Subcontractor shows how each of these effects is built up in each quotation for a compensation event. The percentages for overheads and profit stated in the Subcontract Data are applied to the assessed effect of the event on the Subcontractor’s Cost.

### The effects of compensation events upon the Subcontractor’s Cost are assessed at open market or competitively tendered prices with deductions for all discounts, rebates and taxes which can be recovered. The following are deducted from the assessment of compensation events:
the cost of events for which this subcontract requires the Subcontractor to insure and
other costs paid to the Subcontractor by insurers.

### A delay to the Completion Date is assessed as the length of time that, due to the compensation event, Completion is forecast to be delayed.

### If Aker Kvaerner Chemetics has decided and notified the Subcontractor that the Subcontractor did not give an early warning of a compensation event which an experienced subcontractor could have given, the event is assessed as if the Subcontractor had given an early warning.

### Assessment of the effect of a compensation event includes risk allowances for cost and time for matters which are at the Subcontractor’s risk under this subcontract.

### Assessments are based on the assumptions that the Subcontractor reacts competently and promptly to the compensation event and that additional cost and time due to the event are reasonably incurred.

### A compensation event which is an instruction to change the Works Information in order to resolve an ambiguity or inconsistency is assessed as if the Prices and the Completion Date were for the interpretation most favourable to the Party which did not provide the Works Information.

Termination and Disputes

## Termination and Reasons for Termination

### If a Party wishes to terminate, that Party notifies the other giving details of the reason. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics issues a termination certificate promptly if the reason complies with this subcontract. After a termination certificate has been issued, the Subcontractor does no further work to complete the works.

### Either Party may terminate if the other Party has
become insolvent or its equivalent (Reason 1) or
assigned this subcontract (Reason 2).

### Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may terminate if Aker Kvaerner Chemetics has notified the Subcontractor that the Subcontractor has defaulted in one of the following ways and the Subcontractor has not stopped defaulting within two weeks of the notification.
Substantially failed to comply with this subcontract (Reason 3).
Substantially hindered Aker Kvaerner Chemetics (Reason 4).
Substantially broken a health or safety regulation (Reason 5).
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may terminate for any other reason (Reason 6).

### The Subcontractor may terminate if
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics has not made a payment within ten weeks of the assessment day which followed receipt of the Subcontractor’s application for it (Reason 7) or
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics has instructed the Subcontractor to stop or not to start any substantial work or all work for a reason which is not the Subcontractor’s fault and an instruction allowing the work to restart or start has not been given within eight weeks (Reason 8).

## Procedures on Termination

### On termination, Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may complete the works or employ other people to do so. The Subcontractor leaves the site and where the Subcontractor has terminated the subcontract for reason 1, 2, 7 or 8, removes the Construction Equipment.

## Payment on Termination

### The amount due on termination includes
an amount due assessed as for normal payments,
the cost of Equipment and Materials provided by the Subcontractor which are on the site or of which the Subcontractor has to accept delivery and
any amounts retained by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

### If Aker Kvaerner Chemetics terminates for Reason 1, 2, 3, 4 or 5, the amount due on termination also includes a deduction of the forecast additional cost to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics of completing the works, and does not include any amount in respect of costs incurred by the Subcontractor in expectation of completing the whole of the works, whether or not they are costs to which the Subcontractor is committed.

### If the Subcontractor terminates for Reason 1, 2, 7 or 8 or if Aker Kvaerner Chemetics terminates for Reason 6, the amount due on termination also includes 5% of any excess of a forecast of the amount due at Completion had there been no termination over the amount due on termination assessed as for normal payments.

## Settlement of Disputes

### A dispute under or in connection with this subcontract which the Parties have not resolved is referred to the tribunal.

### Unless otherwise stated in the Subcontract Data, where the dispute does not relate to the Head Contract the tribunal is arbitration by a single Arbitrator appointed by the British Columbia International Arbitration Centre under its Rules.

### Unless and until the Arbitrator has given a decision on the dispute, the Parties proceed as if the matter was not disputed.

### If a dispute arises between:
the Subcontractor and Aker Kvaerner Chemetics which relates to the Head Contract;
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Owner which relates to the works,
the Subcontractor irrevocably and unconditionally submits to the non-exclusive jurisdiction of the tribunal stated in the Subcontract Data.
