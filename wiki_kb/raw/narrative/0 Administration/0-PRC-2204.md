# 0-PRC-2204 — Procedure


[TABLE: 0-PRC-2204_table_1]


## Purpose
This procedure describes the evaluation of Engineering Subcontractor’s bids to ensure that Subcontractors selected are technically and commercially able to supply the services in compliance with the Subcontract.

## Assignment
Once bids are received from Subcontractors in response to a Request for Quotation the Project Engineer will direct the appropriate project personnel to evaluate the bids, setting deadlines for the evaluations. Assignments will generally be as set out in this procedure.

## Format and Numbering
Bid Evaluations will be numbered (0-PRC-0002: Document Numbering) using the same identification number as for the original requisition. The evaluation will consist of four sections:
Commercial Evaluation
Performance Evaluation
Financial Evaluation
Summary Record
The four sections will be considered sheets of a single document, numbered and filed accordingly (0-PRC-0003: File Structure).

## Commercial Evaluation
Commercial evaluations will be prepared in the form of 0-TAB-XXXX-3: [Template Commercial Evaluation] by the Discipline Lead.
The evaluation will consist of a comparison of all elements of the bids to the requirements of the Subcontract, the project schedule, and the project budget.
All cost evaluations must be done in Canadian dollars using project hedge rates where applicable, forward rates when not hedged, and allowing for the cost of collapsing currency hedges where a bid is in a currency different from that budgeted.
The evaluation will list the deviations proposed by each Subcontractor, comparing them with the approach the other Subcontractors are taking to the same items.
Where clarification of a Subcontractor’s bid is necessary, the Discipline Lead will direct the query to the Subcontractor.
Cost comparisons and schedule comparisons require no further classification. For the remainder of the commercial evaluation criteria, the Discipline Lead will rate each Subcontractor as being one of:
Commercially satisfactory as tendered
Commercially satisfactory with listed qualifications
Commercially unsatisfactory
The evaluator will publish the Commercial Evaluation (0-PRC-0005: Publishing of Project Documents), and the Discipline Chief Engineer will approve the evaluation.

## Performance Evaluation
Performance evaluations will be prepared in the form of 0-TAB-XXXX-4: [Template Performance Evaluation] by the Discipline Lead, for any values larger than CAD 50,000.
In the interests of expediency the Project Engineer may direct the Performance Evaluation to be conducted only on a shortlist of Subcontractors.
The evaluation will consist of a summary of the last two Subcontracts placed with each Subcontractor for similar services, together with an evaluation of quality: did the Subcontractor meet requirements with respect to schedule and specification. Any failures to meet requirements should be detailed.
For Subcontractors who have done no work for Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for the previous three years, then a survey will be conducted by the discipline Chief Engineer or by the Discipline Lead. The scope of the survey and the format for the subsequent report will be as set out in 0-REP-XXXX: [Template Engineering Subcontractor Survey]. This document will be numbered (0-PRC-0002: Document Numbering) using the same identification number as for the original requisition. Surveys for multiple Subcontractors will be considered as sheets of a single document. The Subcontractor Survey will be summarised on the Performance Evaluation.
The Discipline Lead will rate each Subcontractor as being one of:
Performance satisfactory
Performance satisfactory with listed qualifications
Performance unsatisfactory
The evaluator will publish the Performance Evaluation (0-PRC-0005: Publishing of Project Documents), and the discipline Chief Engineer will approve the evaluation.

## Financial Evaluation
Financial evaluations will be prepared in the form of 0-TAB-XXXX-5: [Template Financial Evaluation] by Finance, for any values larger than CAD 100,000.
In the interests of expediency the Project Engineer may direct the Financial Evaluation to be conducted only on a shortlist of Subcontractors.
The evaluation will examine the audited operating accounts of the Subcontractor for the previous fiscal year to ensure that the risk that the Subcontractor may suffer a liquidity or credit crisis is minimal.
Finance will rate each Subcontractor as being one of:
Financial status satisfactory
Financial status satisfactory with listed qualifications
Financial status unsatisfactory
The evaluator will publish the Financial Evaluation (0-PRC-0005: Publishing of Project Documents), and the Chief Financial Officer will approve the evaluation.

## Negotiation and Selection
The objective of Subcontractor selection is to simultaneously minimise risk and minimise cost. This would be achieved by the Subcontractor with the lowest total cost to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics having a rating of “satisfactory” in all evaluations.
Practically, this will generally require the imposition of additional requirements on the selected Subcontractor to reduce risk. These requirements will be identified in the evaluations as the qualifications associated with a rating of “satisfactory with qualifications”.
This will usually necessitate soliciting modified bids from Subcontractors to accept additional Subcontract-Specific Conditions (0-PRC-2205: Commercial Terms for Engineering Subcontract). This should be done during the evaluation process where practical, or may be done during final negotiations with the short-listed Subcontractors.

## Summary Record
The Project Engineer will prepare a summary record of the evaluations of all Subcontractors in the form of 0-TAB-XXXX-6: Summary Record. All evaluation qualifications will be listed together with bid modifications or Subcontract-Specific Conditions agreed with the selected Subcontractor.
The Project Engineer will publish the Summary Record (0-PRC-0005: Publishing of Project Documents), and it will be approved by the most senior person listed in Section 7 within the limits stated. When this approval is by the President, he may insert additional instructions to mitigate remaining risks.
The approved Summary Record provides authorisation to issue the Subcontract (0-PRC-2206: Issue of Engineering Subcontract).
Commentary
To All: Note the role of the Project Engineer in orchestrating the evaluation process. The objective is that the finally selected Subcontractor has received a full and complete evaluation, but that excessive time is not expended on early rejects. It is intended that the process as described is a series of related and parallel activities, with ratings changed as bids are clarified or modified. Publishing and approval of the individual evaluations will probably only be completed immediately prior to approval of the Summary Record.
