# 0-PRC-9003 — Unknown


[TABLE: 0-PRC-9003_table_1]


## Purpose
This procedure describes how to request reimbursement of travel or other business expenses paid directly by the employee.

## Travel Expenses
All business travel expenses will comply with the requirements of the Group Travel Policy as applicable. This policy can be accessed through the eNet.
Employees will act reasonably in incurring expenses while travelling on company business, frequenting establishments consistent with their purpose for travelling and with the Group Travel Policy, which requires travellers to “behave as though you were paying for the journey yourself”.
In general, employees will be reimbursed for all reasonable expenses that they would otherwise not have incurred if they were not travelling. When in doubt, employees should check the reasonableness of intended expenses with their supervisor.
Employees may claim CAD 20 for each trip away from their home base in compensation for the wear and tear on luggage and briefcases.

## Credit Cards
Employees who normally directly pay in excess of CAD 500 per year for travel or other business expenses will be reimbursed for the annual fee for a credit card to use for these transactions to a maximum fee of CAD 55.

## Expense Reports
Travel or other business expenses to be reimbursed will be listed on an Expense Report similar to that appended to this procedure.
Expenses will be entered against the date actually incurred, and in the currency of expenditure as shown on the receipts to be submitted. The exchange rate to Canadian Dollars will be clearly shown on the Expense Report. The Expense Report will be totalled in Canadian Dollars.

## Receipts
All expenses to be reimbursed will be supported by itemised receipts. This will often require both a register receipt plus a credit card slip.
Expenses for which no receipts are available will be described in detail on the Expense Report and will be reimbursed as the exception, not the rule.

## Review and Approval
The employee will sign the completed Expense Report and pass it to the Finance Department together with the following attachments:
all receipts
the authorised Travel Request Form
all ticket stubs
any unused tickets to be refunded
Finance will check the Expense Report for accuracy and compliance with this procedure and with the Group Travel Policy. Any discrepancies will be highlighted for action by the approver who will discuss and agree the treatment of the items with the employee.
Expense Reports will be approved by:
the person responsible for the budget being charged
In addition, any expenditure for items to be retained for future use such as cameras, library books, etc. will be approved by:
for items < CAD 500, the employees senior line manager (Director or equivalent)
for items > CAD 500, the Vice President Finance or the President

## Payment
The employee will be reimbursed in CAD for approved expenses by check or by electronic funds transfer into the employee’s bank account.
In any case where the amount reimbursed is not identical to the Expense Report submitted, Finance will provide the employee with a marked-up copy of the Expense Report showing the changes made.

## Cash Advances
Employees may request a cash-advance prior to travelling when they do not have a credit card for which they have been reimbursed for the annual fee, or when travelling to a location with limited acceptance of credit cards.
Employees will indicate on their Travel Request Form (0-PRC-9002 Travel Authorisation) any requirements for a cash advance. They will provide a copy of the authorised Travel Request Form to Finance who will make the arrangements for the cash advance.
Cash advances will be paid by check or by electronic funds transfer into the employee’s bank account. Cash advances will be limited to the following:
for travel within Canada: 	CAD 200 per day away
for travel outside Canada: 	USD 200 or CAD 300 per day away

## Personal Vehicles
The use of personal vehicles for business purposes will be reimbursed at the rate of CAD 0.45 per kilometre. This rate is inclusive of all costs including fuel, maintenance, suitable insurance and depreciation.

## Personal Travel
Where acceptable to the employee’s direct supervisor, an employee may combine business travel with personal time such as vacation, use of banked time or weekend days. Financial offsets as described below must be identified and agreed before commencing the personal portion of the trip.
Any additional travel costs incurred by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics over and above the amount payable for the business travel alone will be shown as a credit on the employee’s Expense Report.
Any reduction in travel costs incurred by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics below the amount payable for the business travel alone may be used to offset the employee’s costs for hotels and meals while on personal time. To the extent reimbursed, these costs will be shown on the employee’s Expense Report as for any other business travel expense, and receipts provided as described in Section 5.
To support any additional costs credited or costs reimbursed while on personal time, the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Travel Arranger will provide fare quotations for the base business travel case plus for the travel actually booked which will be attached to the employees Expense Report.
