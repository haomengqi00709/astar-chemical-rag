# 0-PRC-2203 — Procedure


[TABLE: 0-PRC-2203_table_1]


## Purpose
This procedure sets out how to prepare the Scope for inclusion in an Engineering Subcontract.

## Definition
Note the following definition (0-SPC-2201: Standard Conditions for Engineering Subcontract):
The Scope is information which:
Specifies and describes the services or
States any constraints on how the Subcontractor Provides the Services
and is
In the documents which the Subcontract Data states it is in
In an instruction given in accordance with the Subcontract

## General
The Subcontract Data is a section of the Subcontract Agreement (0-PRC-2202: Form of Engineering Subcontract).
The Subcontract Data will refer to the following document(s), which will be prepared specifically for each Subcontract:
0-SPC-22XX: [Major Work Division] Engineering
The Subcontract Data will also refer to the following documents, which exist as standards that will be used as-is, or will be modified for the project as necessary (0-PRC-0001: Approval and Use of Standards):
0-SPC-0002: Engineering Subcontractor Document Supply
0-SPC-7004: Engineering Subcontractor Quality Assurance
0-SPC-2204: Engineering Subcontractor Coordination

## [Major Work Division] Engineering Specification
These specifications will be prepared (one for each Major Work Division) by the responsible Discipline Lead using 0-SPC-22XX: [Template] [Major Work Division] Engineering.
The specifications will be numbered (0-PRC-0002: Document Numbering) with an identification number of 8YXX-Y where Y is the Major Work Division identifier (0-PRC-0002: Document Numbering) and where XX is identical to the sequential series used for the Engineering Subcontract Agreement (0-PRC-2202: Form of Engineering Subcontract).
The specifications will be published with status “Approved for Design” and will be checked by the Project Engineer and approved by the Discipline Chief Engineer.

## Engineering Scope Checklist
0-SPC-22XX: [Major Work Division] Engineering will directly reference 0-LST-22XX: Engineering Scope Checklists which will itemise the work and documents to be prepared by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and by the Subcontractor.
These checklists will be prepared (one for each Major Work Division) by the responsible Discipline Lead using 0-LST-2001: Engineering Scope Checklist as a basis.
0-LST-22XX: Engineering Scope Checklist will be used to state:
the dates for document deliveries by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics to Subcontractor;
the dates for other Aker Kvaerner Chemetics actions necessary for the Subcontractor to provide the services;
the dates for any document deliveries by Subcontractor prior to the Completion Date stated in the Subcontract Agreement.
The checklists will be numbered (0-PRC-0002: Document Numbering) with an identification number of 8YXX where Y is the Major Work Division identifier (0-PRC-0002: Document Numbering) and where XX is identical to the sequential series used for the Engineering Subcontract Agreement (0-PRC-2202: Form of Engineering Subcontract).
The checklist will be published (0-PRC-0005: Publishing of Project Documents) with status “Approved for Design” and will be checked by the Project Engineer and approved by the Discipline Chief Engineer.

## Document Templates
Subcontractor will be provided with document templates as required for him to prepare the documents listed in 0-LST-8YXX: Engineering Scope Checklist. Templates will be listed in 0-LST-8YXX: Engineering Scope Checklist.
Document Templates must be issued to Subcontractor in native file format with the word “[Template]” appearing in the document title and file name.

## Sample Documents
Subcontractor will be provided with sample documents where doing so clarifies the content of documents that will be provided to him by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, or clarifies the content of documents that will be prepared by Subcontractor. Sample documents will be listed in 0-LST-8YXX: Engineering Scope Checklist.
Sample documents will retain the document number of the originating project, will be published (0-PRC-0005: Publishing of Project Documents) as Revision A with status “Sample Document (Subcontract [Number])” and will be checked by the responsible Discipline Lead and approved by the Project Engineer.
