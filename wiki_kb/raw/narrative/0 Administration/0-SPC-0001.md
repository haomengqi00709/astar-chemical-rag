# 0-SPC-0001 — Specification


[TABLE: 0-SPC-0001_table_1]


## Purpose
This specification describes the supply of documentation by Vendors.

## Scope
Documents titled “Vendor Document Requirements” (VDR’s) will be referenced by the Purchase Order, and will contain the following information:
List of documents to be supplied
Required delivery dates of the documents
Identification of hold documents
Documents to be bundled as manuals

## Content
Document content will be prepared in the [English] language. Vendor may as an alternative, prepare bilingual documents in both [English] and the Vendor’s working language.
Document content will be prepared using [SI] units.

## Revisions
Vendor will highlight revisions by clouding drawings, and by marking textual documents with a vertical line in the left-hand margin. Following revision, Vendor will increment his own revision number, will update the status if applicable, and will re-issue the document to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

## Document Status
Vendor will mark the status on each document prior to issuing it to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. The status describes the permitted use of the information provided in the document as follows:
Approved for Design: The information provided may be used for the design of ancillary components not supplied by Vendor such as support structures or foundations, piping, power supply, etc.
Issued for Review: Only to be used on a hold document if Aker Kvaerner Chemetics has not yet given approval to proceed with the work as described in Section 12.
Approved for Fabrication: The information provided will be used to manufacture the goods. If a hold document, this status will only be shown when all revisions requested by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics have been incorporated and Aker Kvaerner Chemetics has given approval to proceed with the work as described in Section 12.
Approved Instruction: The information provided may be used by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or by Owner for the installation, operation, or maintenance of the goods.
As Built: The information provided is a record of the condition of the goods as delivered.
The word “Approved” as applied to document status means approved by Vendor for the use stated in accordance with the Quality Plan.

## Numbering
Vendors may use their own document titles and numbering. Vendors will provide a 75mm x 45mm block on each document for insertion of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics numbers. For example:

Vendor will insert the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Purchase Order Number and Tag Number. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics will insert all other numbers.

## Format
Vendor will deliver documents in one of the following formats. Maximum document size will be D (22” x 34”) or A1 (594mm x 841mm).
In native digital format (AutoCAD, Microstation, Word, etc.)
In digital format, published in Adobe Portable Document Format (PDF)
Printed paper originals

## Quantities
Documents delivered in printed paper format will be in the following quantities:
Documents delivered during the course of project execution: [one]
Operating, Erection, and Maintenance Manuals: [ten]
Quality Manuals: [five]

## Storage
Digital documents may be stored on one of the following media:
CD-ROM capable of being read on a PC running Microsoft Windows 95/NT.
Floppy disk, either 1.44 MB or LS-120.
Files may be compressed only by using the PKZIP format.

## Transmittals
Documents delivered to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics will be accompanied by a Transmittal containing the following information in tabular form:
Purchase Order Number
Vendor’s document titles
Vendor’s document numbers
Vendor’s document revision number
Vendor’s document status
In the case of documents delivered by e-mail, the Transmittal will be in the form of a Microsoft Word or Excel file named “Transmittal”.

## “Hold” Documents
Vendor may not commence any work shown on a document marked as “hold” on the VDR until the document has been reviewed by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and approval given to proceed.
If a hold document has been revised, work on the revised portion may not commence until the document has been reviewed by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and approval given to proceed.
Upon receipt of a hold document, Aker Kvaerner Chemetics will respond by returning a marked-up copy of the document in Adobe Portable Document Format to Vendor within 7 working days containing one of the following directions:
No Revisions Required; Proceed with Work Shown
Revise as Marked and Reissue; Proceed with Work Shown
Revise as Marked and Reissue; Do Not Proceed with Work Shown

## “Non-Hold” Documents
Upon receipt of a “non-hold” document, Aker Kvaerner Chemetics will only respond in the case where it is found not to be in compliance with the requirements of the Purchase Order by returning a marked-up copy of the document in Adobe Portable Document Format to Vendor containing the following direction:
Revise as Marked and Reissue; Proceed with Work Shown
