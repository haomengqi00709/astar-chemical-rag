# 0-PRC-2202 — Procedure


[TABLE: 0-PRC-2202_table_1]


## Purpose
This procedure describes the format to be used for an Engineering Subcontract.

## Content
The Engineering Subcontract consists of an Agreement signed by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Subcontractor, which contains all subcontract-specific information, and references the various documents that in total contain the description of the services and the conditions under which they are provided. The Agreement is in the form of 0-SUB-22XX: [Template] Engineering Subcontract Agreement.

## Agreement Details
Will state the date of the Agreement, the name and address of the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics entity or business name, and the name and address of the Subcontractor.

## Subcontract Data
The Subcontract Data is broken down into seven parts described further below.

### Part 1: Services
Will reference one or more of 0-SPC-22XX: [Major Work Division] Engineering which provide the primary definition of the services.
Will also state the following information as defined in 0-SPC-2201 Standard Conditions for Engineering Subcontract:
the health and safety requirements are identified by reference to any document which sets out particular safety rules or standards applicable to performance of the services
the key persons are identified in 0-LST-22XX-2: Engineering Subcontractor Staff Rates

### Part 2: Documentation
Will reference 0-SPC-0002: Engineering Subcontractor Document Supply.

### Part 3: Time
Will state the following dates and periods that are defined in 0-SPC-2201 Standard Conditions for Engineering Subcontract:
the starting date
the completion date for the whole of the services
the period for submission of the first Schedule expressed as weeks after the starting date.
the period for reply to a communication
the period for retention of documents
the defects date expressed as weeks after Completion.

### Part 4: Quality Assurance
Will reference 0-SPC-7004: Engineering Subcontractor Quality Assurance.
Will state the period for submission of the quality plan expressed as weeks after the starting date.

### Part 5: Payment
Will reference 0-LST-22XX-1: Engineering Subcontract Activity Schedule.
Will also state the following information as defined in 0-SPC-2201 Standard Conditions for Engineering Subcontract:
the assessment interval
the currency of the subcontract
the period within which payments are made
the expenses to be reimbursed by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics
the frequency at which Subcontractor is to submit forecasts of total expenses
Will further reference 0-LST-22XX-2: Engineering Subcontractor Staff Rates

### Part 6: Commercial
Will list all documents that define the commercial conditions. Will also contain any Order-Specific Conditions (0-PRC-2205: Commercial Terms for Engineering Subcontract).
Will also state the following information as defined in 0-SPC-2202 Special Conditions for Engineering Subcontract:
the rate for delay damages

### Part 7: Coordination and Communication
Will reference 0-SPC-2204: Engineering Subcontractor Coordination.
Will list the authorised representatives of both parties.

## Numbering
Engineering Subcontract Agreements will be numbered (0-PRC-0002: Document Numbering) with an identification number of 22XX where XX is one of a sequential series 01, 02, 03…For example: 98C12300-0-SUB-2201-RA
