# 0-PRC-2207 — Procedure


[TABLE: 0-PRC-2207_table_1]


## Purpose
This procedure describes how to control the cost and schedule of services provided by Engineering Subcontractors.

## Responsibility
The Subcontract Administrator is responsible for the control of cost and schedule. The objectives of controlling cost and schedule are as follows:
Delivery of the services as required by the project schedule
Accurate forecasts of total cost of the services
Minimal close-out effort in the form of cost claims

## Reference
Clauses 25 to 30 of 0-SPC-2201: Standard Conditions for Engineering Subcontract deal with Compensation Events, which are generally defined as events which will result in a change (generally an increase) to the Prices, and / or a change (generally an extension) to the Completion Date.

## Compensation Events
The most commonly encountered compensation events will be where Aker Kvaerner Chemetics gives an instruction changing the Scope. This will include any of the following:
Increase in quantity of services caused by changes to documents issued by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics between status “Issued for Quotation” and status “Approved for Design”
Extension of total duration of the services caused by late delivery of documents by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics
Revision of services already completed caused by changes to documents issued by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics with status “Approved for Design”
Issue of an explicit or implicit instruction by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics by e-mail message or as minutes of meetings
0-SPC-2201: Standard Conditions for Engineering Subcontract describes potentially lengthy steps for review and quotation in response to the issue of an instruction. In order to ensure that there are no undue delays in delivery of the services, the steps described in Sections 5 and 6 will be followed.

## Issue of Documents
Immediately following issue of documents by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics to Subcontractor, the Subcontract Administrator will issue a communication as follows:
“Our communication KCI-SUB-[024] contains Document Transmittal [KC-SC-0047].
The documents referenced by the Document Transmittal contain revisions that may be considered by Subcontractor to be instructions given by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics changing the Scope. Subcontractor will immediately proceed with the services based on the instructions contained in the revised documents, keeping a separate record of the direct costs applicable to any change in Scope. Subcontractor will submit a notice and quotation, including a revised schedule, for any compensation event resulting from the instructions contained in the revised documents in accordance with clause 27 of 0-SPC-2201: Standard Conditions for Engineering Subcontract.”

## Issue of Communications
Any communication issued by the Discipline Lead or by the Subcontract Administrator to Subcontractor containing an instruction will have the following text appended:
“The foregoing is an instruction to Subcontractor given by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics which may change the Scope. Subcontractor will immediately proceed with the services based on the instruction, keeping a separate record of the direct costs applicable to any change in Scope. Subcontractor will submit a notice and quotation, including a revised schedule, for any compensation event resulting from the instruction in accordance with clause 27 of 0-SPC-2201: Standard Conditions for Engineering Subcontract.”

## Response to Notice of Compensation Event
The Subcontract Administrator will respond to any notice from Subcontractor of the occurrence of a compensation event within one week. The response will be either acceptance or rejection of the claim that the instruction is a compensation event. If the decision is made to accept the claim that the instruction is a compensation event, the Subcontract Administrator will request a quotation.

## Acceptance of Quotation
Clause 27.3 of 0-SPC-2201: Standard Conditions for Engineering Subcontract describes the possible actions by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics following receipt of a quotation for a compensation event. Should the quotation be accepted a formal notification will be issued in the form of 0-CHG-22XX: [Template] Subcontract Change Order.
Change Orders will be prepared by the Subcontract Administrator and will be numbered (0-PRC-0002: Document Numbering) using the same identification number as for the Subcontract, with each specific Change Order considered as a sheet of a single document. Change Orders will be published (0-PRC-0005: Publishing of Project Documents) with status “Approved for Design” and will be checked by the responsible Discipline Lead and approved by the Project Engineer.
The approved Change Order will be printed and physically signed by the same authorised signatory of the original Subcontract. The Subcontract Administrator will forward the signed and authorised paper copy of the Change Order to Document Control for issue to Subcontractor (0-PRC-0006: Control of Project Documentation).

## Forecasts
The Subcontract Administrator will prepare forecasts of the total cost of the Subcontract and of the delivery of the services as requested by the Project Manager. The forecasts will take into account any instructions issued to Subcontractor for which quotations have not yet been received or accepted.
