# 0-POL-9113 — Unknown


[TABLE: 0-POL-9113_table_1]


## Purpose
The nature of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' business will at times require work in excess of the normal hours of work. This policy is intended to ensure that employees are fairly compensated for this work.

## Normal Hours of Work
In accordance with 0-POL-9106: Hours of Work, normal hours of work are 75 hours per two-week period minus 8.25 hours for every local statutory holiday falling within that period.

## Positions Evaluated at 639 Hay Point or Lower
Employees in positions evaluated at Hay point 639 or lower will be paid for hours worked in excess of the normal hours of work.

### “Exempt” Employees
These are employees in positions that are not subject to the statutory provisions governing the payment of overtime contained in the Employment Standards Act. At Aker Kvaerner Chemetics these positions include supervisors at Hay point 450 or higher, managers, engineers, “Engineers in Training”, and employees in equivalent professional positions to engineers.
Compensation will be at the employee’s hourly rate of pay (annual salary divided by 1,950).

### “Non-Exempt” Employees
These are employees in positions that are subject to the statutory provisions governing the payment of overtime contained in the Employment Standards Act. At Aker Kvaerner Chemetics these are all employees not described in Section 3.1
Compensation will be as follows:
For time above 37.5 but under 40 hours per week: at the employee’s hourly rate of pay
For time above 40 hours per week: at 1.5x the employee’s hourly rate of pay
For time in excess of 12 hours in a day: at 2x the employee’s hourly rate of pay
Where employees are following a flexible work schedule in accordance with 0-POL-9106: Hours of Work, overtime will only be paid for hours worked outside the approved flexible work schedule.

## Positions Evaluated at Hay Point 640 or Above
Employees in positions evaluated at Hay point 640 or above will not normally be paid for hours worked in excess of the normal hours of work.

## Banked Time
Employees eligible for compensation for hours worked in excess of the normal hours of work may request that time equivalent to the compensation be banked to be taken as time off at a future date. Employees will not hold more that 75 hours in their time bank. Employees requesting time to be banked on their timesheets will be paid for any time in excess of the 75-hour cap.

## Time While Travelling on Business
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics will provide reasonable compensation for time spent away from home while travelling on business. It is intended to reach a sensible balance between compensation for lost personal time while avoiding complex accounting of time differences and world date lines, and precise definitions of worked time with respect of time spent in airports, in aeroplanes, waiting for appointments or meetings, etc.
Employees travelling on business will record a maximum of 8.25 hours per day for any days spent in transit.  In general, 8.25 hours should be recorded for the day that they leave and the day that they return, and all days in between except for any weekend days spent at an away location. Time actually worked at the away location in excess of the normal hours of work will be compensated as set out in this policy.  Employees are not expected to schedule travel outside of normal working hours.

## Recording Overtime
Employees eligible for compensation of overtime will record hours worked in excess of the normal hours of work on their weekly timesheets.
