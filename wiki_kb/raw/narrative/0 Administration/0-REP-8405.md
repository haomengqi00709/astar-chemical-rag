SUBCONTRACTOR'S SAFETY RECORD
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics places a high emphasis on a Subcontractor's safety performance record as part of the criteria for being awarded work on our projects.  Aker Kvaerner Chemetics will emphazise safety continually, from the very inception of the bid process, throughout construction and/or contract execution and post contract completion.  Aker Kvaerner Chemetics requires our Subcontractors to be fully committed to safety and to meet performance criteria before being allowed to participate on our construction projects.
During the Pre-Bid meeting, Aker Kvaerner Chemetics will emphazise Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' commitment to safety and explain specific contractual safety and health requirements.  Prior to contract award, Aker Kvaerner Chemetics will review a safety preconstruction checklist with the Subcontractor's Field Supervisor to assure that he fully understands the criteria to which he must comply.  Aker Kvaerner Chemetics requires that an officer of the Subcontractor execute the "STATEMENT OF SAFETY COMMITMENT" at the end of this form.  He shall personally assume responsibility for the safety of their employees and all lower tier subsubcontractors.
Upon completion of the subcontract, the safety performance of the Subcontractor will be evaluated.  The Subcontractor will be judged on attitude, compliance, responsiveness and injury or incident occurrence.  The evaluation results will be noted in the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics data base and used as the basis for future bidders list.  Only Subcontractors with favourable safety and performance records shall be allowed to bid future work.
