# 0-PRC-5006 — Procedure


[TABLE: 0-PRC-5006_table_1]


## Purpose
This procedure describes the format to be used for the use of an Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Procurement Department – Royal Bank Visa, Purchase Card (“the Card”), and assigns the responsibility for providing the information, approval and records keeping as required.

## General Information
The use of the Card is to help eliminate the need for petty cash, cash advances, and the need to prepare and issue internal purchase orders covering approved purchases having a value of < $250.00.
The use of the Card is not intended to avoid or bypass appropriate purchasing or payment procedures or policies.
The Card is not intended for any personal use.
The Card will be used for both project approved purchases having a value of < $250.00 as well as procurement departmental approved requests such as:
Approved Staff – Personal Development Programs, (Seminars, Conferences, Accredited Courses, etc.)
Approved Procurement Subscriptions, Departmental Reference Books and Magazines, etc.)
Approved Office Supplies and forms
Approved Catering or dining services, (such as “lunch and learns”, etc.)
Approved miscellaneous expenditures.
The Card can be used for both in-store purchases, (will require signature of cardholder), as well as for mail, email or fax orders.
The Card may be used by any vendor or supplier who accepts Visa throughout Canada or any other country.
The Card is intended for the use by and for the Procurement Department and/or its Staff and used only for approved company business in compliance with this procedure.
No transaction may exceed $2,500.00 in value, unless approved by the Vice President of Finance and manually approved by the Royal Bank.
The misuse of the Card or any use out of compliance with this procedure will result in severe consequences, up to and including termination of employment.
The “cardholder” will be identified as the Manager of Procurement.
The cardholder will be responsible for the security of the Card, its compliance with these procedures, preparation of any required reporting on a monthly basis, and to the Card’s return in accordance with appropriate “close-out” procedures.

## Defined Levels of Authority
The use of the Card will be in compliance with the following levels of authority:
Individual approved purchases < than $2,500.00 will be approved by the Manager of Procurement
Individual approved purchases > than $2,500.00 will be approved by the Vice President of Finance or by their approved designate.

## The General Rules for use of the Procurement Purchase Card
All orders for a purchase will be by phone/fax, email, mail or pickup.  Orders will be placed by the cardholder, or as designated by the cardholder.  Designates will always be approved Procurement Staff only.
All proposed non-project expenditures must be detailed in writing and covered by an “expense request form” (see attachment).  This form will identify in clear terms the purpose for the expenditure/s as well as provide:
Name and address of Vendor
Description of the purchase to be made
Quantity to be purchased
Unit price of each item
Amount of Sales Tax to be paid
Shipping Charges, etc., if applicable.
If the proposed expenditures are for “Project” related items, the expenditure must be covered by an approved project material requisition, providing appropriate cost code allocations for all items to be purchased.
The cardholder will maintain all appropriate records regarding the use of the Card.  These records will be subject to internal financial audits as may be determined by the company’s Finance Department.

## Supporting Documentation
The cardholder will collect and maintain all supporting documentation attached to each and all transactions. This includes all receipts and invoices.

## Payment
The Royal Bank will issue on a monthly basis, a detailed statement of charges applied against the Card.  The cardholder will review all expenditures to ensure compliance with this procedure, assign appropriate cost code allocations against each expenditure identified, and forward the monthly statement to the Finance Department for payment.
