This direction will be through the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Site Construction manager and the Subcontractors and Owners normal supervisory structures.

### Training
Training of the Owner’s operations personnel is the first step in the commissioning process. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics will prepare the following documents:
1-MAN-0001: Operating Manual
1-MAN-0002: Analytical Manual
1-MAN-0003: Training Manual
A series of lesson plans are prepared covering the following topics:
Normal start-up and shutdown procedures
Routine operation control and monitoring
Troubleshooting of commonly encountered problems
Upset response and recovery
Emergency response and shutdown procedures
Plant purging and preparation for maintenance
These lessons are delivered in the form of lectures given by one of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics process engineers. The lessons are preferably given at the site to allow plant walk-throughs and familiarisation.
The trainees will be given a written test with additional training given to cover common lack of understanding.
Owner will prepare detailed operator instructions and checklists, and will deliver training on these and on safety procedures.
Continuing hands-on training is delivered through the remaining phases of the commissioning of the plant.

### Mechanical Completion Testing
Mechanical completion testing is the final set of inspections and tests to ensure that the plant has been completed in accordance with the design and is ready to commence commissioning. The inspections and tests are typically conducted by the commissioning team under the direction of the Commissioning Manager.
The inspections and tests are conducted in accordance with the following:
4-PRC-0004: Mechanical Checkout Equipment
5-PRC-0007: Mechanical Checkout Piping
8-PRC-0005: Instrument Systems Field Checkout
8-PRC-0006: DCS Control Function Checkout
8-PRC-0011: Instrument Approval Tag
8-PRC-0014: DCS Field Hardware Checkout
9-PRC-0004: Electrical Systems Field Checkout
Each procedure has a number of associated checklists to record the completion of the inspections and tests.
Incomplete work is identified to the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Site Construction Manager in the form of deficiency lists. Priority for remedy of the defects will be agreed between the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Site Construction Manager and the Commissioning Manager, with agreement on what if any work can be done while water and air trials are in progress.

### Water and Air Trials
Water and air trials are processes whereby the plant is operated using water and air in the place of the process fluids, and with all utilities energised. It is intended to demonstrate the following:
Operability of the utility systems and controls
Hydraulic function of the process piping and equipment
Operability of the process controls
Functionality of interlocks and emergency shutdown systems
The water and air trials are conducted in accordance with the following:
1-PRC-0006: Water and rials
This procedure requires that the plant be divided into sub-systems for the execution of the water and air trials. These sub-systems are identified in marked-up Piping and Instrument Diagrams, including any requirement for temporary bypasses, jumpers, and hoses.
Records in the form of checklists and marked-up Piping and Instrument Diagrams are kept as water and air trials are carried out and system operability and function are demonstrated.
During water and air trials utility systems are energised, and water is pumped through the process piping and equipment at elevated pressures and temperatures. This increases safety risks to any construction personnel remaining in the plant, and to operating personnel.
To control these risks the following procedures are enforced under the direction of the Commissioning Manager.
1-PRC-0007: Isolation and Preparation for Maintenance
1-PRC-0009: Safe Work Permits
9-PRC-0019: Field Lockout and Tagout

### Chemical Commissioning
When water and air trials are complete, agreement is reached between the Owner and the Commissioning Manager that the plant is complete and ready for the introduction of raw materials and treatment chemicals. This agreement will be made and recorded in accordance with the protocols set out in the Contract.
At this time, responsibility for the safety management of the plant is transferred to the Owner, and all Owners safety regulations will be in force.
Raw materials and treatment chemicals are introduced into the plant in a sequential and systematic manner, fully or partially operating as many process unit operations as possible, troubleshooting them, and tuning controls.

### Production Energisation
When chemical commissioning is complete, agreement is reached between the Owner and the Commissioning Manager that the plant is complete and ready for energisation and commencing production. This agreement will be made and recorded in accordance with the protocols set out in the Contract.
DC Power will be applied to the electrolysers, which will immediately cause the production of chlorine gas, hydrogen gas, and sodium hydroxide. The plant will be operated at minimum allowable loads to continue the operation, troubleshooting, and tuning of controls in downstream process unit operations.
DC Power will be applied to the electrolysers in increasing increments, demonstration the operation and stability of all process unit operations at all rates up to full production.

### Plant Modification

These modifications also require careful control and regulation to ensure that they do not compromise the quality or the safety of the plant. All plant modifications during commissioning will be designed and executed in accordance with the following:

### Acceptance Testing
Plant performance guarantees will be demonstrated in one or more Acceptance Tests in accordance with the following:
1-PRC-0008: Performance Testing
The Owners personnel will operate the plant during the performance testing under the direction of the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics commissioning team as assigned by the Commissioning Manager.
Upon completion of the Acceptance Testing, agreement is reached between the Owner and the Commissioning Manager that the plant has passed the Acceptance Tests and is Accepted. This agreement will be made and recorded in accordance with the protocols set out in the Contract.
Should the plant not pass the Acceptance Tests, and Aker Kvaerner Chemetics decides that modification of the plant is necessary, the modifications will require the same controls as described in Section 8.9, as well as the Owner approvals as set out in the Contract.
