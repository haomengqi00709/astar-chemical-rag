# 0-PRC-7007 — Procedure


[TABLE: 0-PRC-7007_table_1]


## Purpose
This procedure describes the roles and responsibilities of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics personnel in assuring the quality of services supplied by Engineering Subcontractors.

## Subcontractor Requirements
Subcontractors will be issued 0-SPC-7004: Engineering Subcontractor Quality Assurance to set out the quality requirements for the services that they are supplying.

## Subcontract Quality Plan
Subcontractor is required to prepare and submit a Subcontract Quality Plan for the services together with copies of all referenced documents.
The Subcontract Quality Plan and the referenced documents will be logged and published (0-PRC-0006: Control of Project Documents). They will then be reviewed by the Discipline Lead responsible for the work, and by the Project Engineer. Any required changes will be marked-up on the Adobe Portable Document Format documents in the project electronic file and they will be returned to Subcontractor.
When approved, the Adobe Portable Document Format versions of the documents will be marked as “Approved for Design”, and digital signatures attached by the Discipline Lead as Checker, and by the Project Engineer as Approver.

## Quality Audit
The services provided by Subcontractor will be audited by review of documentation prepared by Subcontractor that has been properly checked and approved in accordance with the Subcontract Quality Plan.
Audits will be carried out by the Discipline Lead responsible for the work to the extent necessary for assurance of the accuracy of the services and compliance with the requirements of the Subcontract. Audits will be scheduled so as to provide early warning to Subcontractor of the necessity for corrective or preventive action.

## Audit Reporting
Documents submitted by Subcontractor for audit will be logged and published (0-PRC-0006: Control of Project Documents).
Any Defects in the services will be marked-up on the Adobe Portable Document Format documents in the project electronic file and copies will be returned to Subcontractor.
If no Defects are found, the Adobe Portable Document Format documents in the project electronic file will be marked as “Audited: No Defects Noted” and digital signature attached by the Discipline Lead. Copies of the documents will be returned to Subcontractor.

## Defects / Non-Conformances
Defects in the services or other non-conformances with the Subcontract Quality Plan may be identified by audit, or by Subcontractors own reviews.
The Discipline Lead responsible for the work will ensure that Subcontractor undertakes corrective action as required by the Subcontract Quality Plan, conducting additional audits as required.
The Discipline Lead responsible for the work will ensure that Subcontractor proposes appropriate action to prevent recurrence of the non-conformance. This proposal is subject to approval as required by the Subcontract Quality Plan. Any required modifications to the Subcontract Quality Plan or its referenced documents will be subject to checking and approval as described in Section 3. Further auditing will be conducted as necessary to evaluate the effectiveness of the modified Subcontract Quality Plan and its referenced documents
