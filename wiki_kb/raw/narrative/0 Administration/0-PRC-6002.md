# 0-PRC-6002 — Procedure


[TABLE: 0-PRC-6002_table_1]


## Purpose
This procedure describes the contents of a Construction Subcontract Agreement.

## Content
A Construction Subcontract consists of an Agreement signed by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Subcontractor, which contains all subcontract-specific information, and references the various documents that in total contain the description of the works and the conditions under which they are provided. The Agreement is in the form of 0-SUB-6X00: [Template] Construction Subcontract Agreement.

## Agreement Details
Will state the date of the Agreement, the name and address of the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics entity or business name, and the name and address of the Subcontractor.

## Subcontract Data
The Subcontract Data provides commercial information and is broken down into eight parts described further below.

### Part 1.1. General
Will state a general description of the works and of the site.

### Part 1.2. Time
Will state the following dates and periods that are referenced by 0-SPC-6001 Standard Conditions for Construction Subcontract:
the starting date
the completion date
the period for reply to a communication
the defects date expressed as weeks after Completion
the defects correction period expressed as weeks

### Part 1.3. Payment
Will state the total of the Prices using the appropriate currency code.
Will reference 0-LST-6X11: Construction Subcontract Price List to give either the breakdown of a lump sum subcontract or the complete listing of units and prices for a unit price subcontract.
Will also state the following information as referenced by 0-SPC-6001 Standard Conditions for Construction Subcontract:
the assessment day
the retention as a percentage
the security as a percentage of the Price, and the method of provision
the delay damages as a fixed cost per day.

### Part 1.4. Assessment of Compensation Events
In the case of a lump sum subcontract, will reference the following as the basis for assessing future compensation events:
0-SPC-6011: Construction Subcontract Pricing: Lump Sum
0-LST-6X12: Construction Subcontract Labour Rates
0-LST-6X13: Construction Subcontract Crew Rates
0-LST-6X14: Construction Subcontract Indirect Labour Schedule
0-LST-6X15: Construction Subcontract Equipment Schedule
In the case of a unit price subcontract, will reference the following as the basis for assessing future compensation events:
0-SPC-6012: Construction Subcontract Pricing: Unit Price
0-LST-6X12: Construction Subcontract Labour Rates
0-LST-6X13: Construction Subcontract Crew Rates
0-LST-6X14: Construction Subcontract Indirect Labour Schedule
0-LST-6X15: Construction Subcontract Equipment Schedule
Will also reference the Subcontractor’s quoted mark-ups on costs.

### Part 1.5. Liability and Insurance
Will state what insurance will be supplied by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or by Owner.
Will state the insured amount for the Subcontractor’s liability for loss of or damage to property and for bodily injury to or death of a person arising from or in connection with the Subcontractor’s Providing the Works.

### Part 1.6. Disputes
Will describe the form of tribunal for a dispute under the subcontract as arbitration, stating the location it is to be conducted, the governing rules and law, and the number of arbitrators.
Will state the form of tribunal for a dispute under the Head Contract.

### Part 1.7. Commercial
Will list the two documents that define the commercial conditions.

### Part 1.8. Coordination and Communication
Will reference 0-SPC-6003: Construction Subcontractor Coordination to define the requirements for reporting, communication, site coordination meetings, notification of Compensation Events, etc.
Will also list the authorised representatives of both Parties.

## Works Information
The Works Information provides the description of the works and is broken down into six parts described further below.

### Part 2.1. General
Will reference one or more of 0-SPC-6X9X: [Major Work Division] Erection that provides the primary definition of the works.
Will also reference 0-SPC-6091: Construction Subcontractor Document Supply if applicable to the works, and 0-SPC-6041: Construction Subcontractor Materials Control.

### Part 2.2. Applicable Documents
Will reference the Document Transmittal (0-PRC-0006: Control of Project Documentation) to provide a listing of all applicable drawings and specifications, including the Revision Number and Status of each.
Because all administrative specifications, lists, etc. are listed in the Document Transmittal, direct references in the Agreement do not require the Revision Number to be shown.

### Part 2.3. Schedule and Progress Measurement
Will reference 0-SPC-6021: Construction Subcontract Scheduling to define the requirements for planning, scheduling, and progress measurement.
Will also state the frequency of measuring progress and updating the schedule.

### Part 2.4. Management and Programs
Will reference some or all of the following management systems as are required by the project:
0-SPC-6051: Construction Subcontract Safety Management
0-SPC-6061: Construction Subcontract Environmental Management
0-SPC-6071: Construction Subcontract Quality Assurance
0-SPC-6081: Construction Subcontract Industrial Relations Management
Also references 0-LST-6X16: Construction Subcontract Supervision Schedule to name the Key Personnel plus set out overall levels of management and supervision.

### Part 2.5. Constraints
States the dates of delivery to the site of any Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or Owner supplied Equipment and Materials in the form of 0-REP-5001: Expediting Report obtained from the Procurement and Logistics Database.
Will reference 0-GAD-6X01: Construction Facilities Layout to show the location available for the Subcontractor’s temporary facilities such as offices, lunchrooms, field workshops, etc.
Will list any other constraints on the Subcontractor that will dictate the sequence or methodology of working such as dates of shutdowns, etc.

### Part 2.6. Services by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics
Will reference Aker Kvaerner Chemetics 0-LST-6X90-2: Construction Subcontract Field Services Scope that will state what services will be supplied to the Subcontractor at the site by either Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or by Owner.
Will reference 0-GAD-6X01: Construction Facilities Layout to show the location of the connection points for the services supplied at the site by either Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or by Owner.

## Site Information
Will list any information about the site which is necessary for the Subcontractor to make his bid, such as soil conditions, contamination of soil or any items to be demolished or moved, existence of flood plains, etc. This is particularly important to describe conditions that cannot be determined from visual inspection during a visit to the site.

## Numbering
Construction Subcontract Agreements will be numbered (0-PRC-0002: Document Numbering) with an identification number of 6X00 where X is one of a sequential series 1, 2, 3…For example: 98C12300-0-SUB-6100-RA.
