# 0-SPC-6091 — Specification


[TABLE: 0-SPC-6091_table_1]


## Purpose
This specification describes the supply of documentation by the Construction Subcontractor.

## Content
Document content will be prepared in the [English] language. The Subcontractor may as an alternative, prepare bilingual documents in both [English] and the Subcontractor’s working language.
Document content will be prepared using [SI] units.

## Revisions
The Subcontractor will highlight revisions by clouding drawings, and by marking textual and tabular documents with a vertical line in the left-hand margin. Following revision, the Subcontractor will increment the revision number, and will update the status.

## Document Status
The Subcontractor will mark the status on each document prior to issuing it to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. The status describes the permitted use of the information provided in the document as follows:
Approved for Design: The information provided may be used for the design of ancillary components not supplied by the Subcontractor such as support structures or foundations, power supply, etc.
Issued for Review: Only to be used if Aker Kvaerner Chemetics has not yet given approval to proceed with the work shown as described in Section 11.
Approved for Construction: The information provided will be used to manufacture and erect the work. This status will only be shown when all revisions requested by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics have been incorporated and Aker Kvaerner Chemetics has given approval to proceed as described in Section 11.
Approved Instruction: The information provided may be used by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or by Owner for the operation or maintenance of the work.
As Built: The information provided is a record of the condition of the work as completed.
Limitations on the use of the document may be made by adding a qualifier to the status. For example: “Approved for Design (Layout only)”
The word “Approved” as applied to document status means approved by the Subcontractor for the use stated in accordance with his Quality Plan.

## Numbering
The Subcontractor may use his own document titles and numbering. The Subcontractor will provide a 75mm x 45mm block on each document for insertion of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics numbers. For example:

[TABLE: 0-SPC-6091_table_2]

The Subcontractor will insert the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Subcontract Number and Tag Number. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics will insert all other numbers.

## Format
The Subcontractor will deliver documents in one of the following formats. Maximum document size will be D (22” x 34”) or A1 (594mm x 841mm).
In native digital format (AutoCAD, Microstation, Word, etc.)
In digital format, published in Adobe Portable Document Format (PDF)
Printed paper originals

## Quantities
Documents delivered in printed paper format will be in the following quantities:
Documents delivered during the course of project execution: [one]
Operating and Maintenance Manuals: [ten]
Quality Manuals: [five]

## Storage
Digital documents may be stored on one of the following media:
CD-ROM capable of being read on a PC running Microsoft Windows 95/NT.
Floppy disk, either 1.44 MB or LS-120.
Files may be compressed only by using the PKZIP format.

## Transmittals
Documents delivered to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics will be accompanied by a Transmittal containing the following information in tabular form:
Subcontract Number
Document File Name
Document Number
Document Title
Document Revision Number
Document Status
In the case of documents delivered electronically, the Transmittal will be in the form of a Microsoft Excel file named “Transmittal”.

## Review By Aker Kvaerner Chemetics
The Subcontractor will not commence any work shown on a design document prepared by the Subcontractor until the document has been reviewed by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and notice given to proceed.
If a design document has been revised, work on the revised portion may not commence until the document has been reviewed by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and notice given to proceed.
Upon receipt of design document, Aker Kvaerner Chemetics will respond by returning a marked-up copy of the document in Adobe Portable Document Format to the Subcontractor within 7 working days containing one of the following directions:
No Revisions Required; Proceed with Work Shown
Revise as Marked and Reissue; Proceed with Work Shown
Revise as Marked and Reissue; Do Not Proceed with Work Shown
The Subcontractor will comply with the direction given. When directed, reissue of documents will be within 7 working days.
