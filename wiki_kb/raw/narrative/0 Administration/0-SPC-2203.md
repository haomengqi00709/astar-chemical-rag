# 0-SPC-2203 — Specification


[TABLE: 0-SPC-2203_table_1]


## Purpose
This specification describes the conditions under which the Proposer provides to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics an offer to provide the Services described in the Agreement.

## Acknowledgement
Within five working days of receipt of the request, Proposer will acknowledge receipt and state his intended response.

## Reference
The Agreement number will appear prominently on all correspondence and documentation submitted by Proposer.

## Verification of Documents
Proposer will verify that he has received all documents referenced in the Agreement. If not, he will promptly advise the Subcontract Administrator who will issue the missing documents.

## Queries and Clarifications
Proposer will submit all queries or requests for clarification in writing to the Subcontract Administrator. All proposers will be provided with a verbatim copy of the query plus the response.

## Validity of Offer
Proposer’s offer to supply the Services will be irrevocable for a period of 60 days following the submission date.

## Form of Offer
The Proposer will provide the following documentation, completed in accordance with the instructions given below:
0-LST-22XX-1: Engineering Subcontract Activity Schedule
0-LST-22XX-2: Engineering Subcontractor Staff Rates
0-REP-22XX: Engineering Subcontractor Survey
Proposer will supply the following additional documentation to assist in evaluation of his offer as follows:
Deviations to any part of the Subcontract
Resumes or C.V.’s for nominated Key Persons
[Preliminary schedule]
[Quality Management Manuals]
Proposer may supply other documentation he considers is required to fully describe his offer. Documentation supplied should be concise and clearly indicate its purpose. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics is not obliged to consider documentation supplied other than that listed above.

### Activity Schedule
The Subcontractor is paid a lump sum for the services. The activity schedule is a list of the activities that the Subcontractor will carry out in Providing the Services. The lump sum for each activity is the price paid by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for that activity, and the total of the lump sum prices on the activity schedule is the Subcontractor’s lump sum price for the whole of the services.
The Proposer will complete the activity schedule provided (0-LST-22XX-1: Engineering Subcontract Activity Schedule), listing quantities for each activity which have not already been given, and listing lump sum prices for each activity. Any part of the services not specifically identified as an activity will be included in the other activities.

### Staff Rates
The Proposer will include in his tender staff rates that must be consistent with the priced activity schedule and will be used to value compensation events.
The Proposer will complete the list provided (0-LST-22XX-2: Engineering Subcontractor Staff Rates), listing categories of employees and the hourly rate to be charged for each, inclusive of all overhead charges and profit.
The Proposer will nominate key persons that will be engaged to Provide the Services, listing their names on 0-LST-22XX-2: Engineering Subcontractor Staff Rates.

### Survey
The Proposer will complete the survey provided (0-REP-22XX: Engineering Subcontractor Survey). Aker Kvaerner Chemetics will contact the references directly.

### Deviations
Proposer’s offer to supply the services will be considered to be in full compliance with the Agreement and all referenced documents unless deviations or exceptions are explicitly stated and described.

## Evaluation and Disposition of Offers
Offers will be evaluated in the sole discretion of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics as to overall performance, delivery and value. The evaluation will consider any deviations and exceptions included in the offer in the manner described by these instructions.
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics reserves the right to accept or reject any offer in whole or in part, or to reject all offers with or without notice or reasons, or to accept an offer other than that having the lowest price, or to accept an offer not in full compliance with the Request for Tender. If no offer is acceptable, the work may be abandoned or performed in any manner selected by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics makes no representation that any Proposer is pre-qualified or is acceptable merely because they have received or responded to a Request for Tender.
Unsuccessful Proposers will be advised within ten working days of the award of a Subcontract for the services to another party.
Costs incurred by Proposer, or any Supplier, Subsubcontractor, or Others, in preparing Proposer’s offer to supply the services are the responsibility of the Proposer and will not be reimbursed by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

## Confidentiality
Proposers will take reasonable measures to maintain the confidentiality of the Request for Tender and all referenced documents. Proposer’s Suppliers or Subsubcontractors must only be given sufficient information to provide an offer to Proposer.
The Request for Tender and all documents provided to Proposer by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics remain the property of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. Originals and all copies must be returned to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or destroyed on demand.
