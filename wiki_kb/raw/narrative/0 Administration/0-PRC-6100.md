# 0-PRC-6100 — Procedure


[TABLE: 0-PRC-6100_table_1]


## Purpose
This document describes the responsibilities of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, a division of Aker Kvaerner Canada Inc. (Aker Kvaerner) construction site advisor.

## Interface
The Aker Kvaerner construction site advisor will interface directly with the client’s construction manager and the Aker Kvaerner home office.  Direct interfacing with Aker Kvaerner engineering subcontractor, Aker Kvaerner suppliers and logistics providers may also be required.

## Responsibilities
The Aker Kvaerner construction site advisor will advise the client’s construction contractor in the following areas that relate to Aker Kvaerner engineering and design scope of supply:
Construction activity sequencing;
Installation advice related to Aker Kvaerner proprietary design;
Material delivery dates related to Aker Kvaerner scope of supply;
Document technical interpretation;
Technical query resolution;
Construction to commissioning transition.
The advice provided by the Aker Kvaerner construction site advisor does not relieve the client or the client’s construction contractors of the obligations to timely complete and ensure the quality and correctness of the installations in accordance with design documents and jurisdictional requirements.
The Aker Kvaerner construction site advisor is not responsible for direct supervision or management of client construction contractors.
