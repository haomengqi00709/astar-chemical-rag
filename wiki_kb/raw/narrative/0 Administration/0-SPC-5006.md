# 0-SPC-5006 — Specification


[TABLE: 0-SPC-5006_table_1]


## Purpose
This specification describes the conditions under which the Bidder provides to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics an offer to supply the Goods and Services described in the Request for Quotation.

## Forms
Bidder is provided with the following listed forms to use as directed by this specification:
Acknowledgement
Offer Summary
Packaging Summary

## Acknowledgement
Within five working days of receipt of the Request for Quotation, Bidder will complete and return the Acknowledgement form, stating his intended response.

## Reference
The Request for Quotation number will appear prominently on all correspondence and documentation submitted by Bidder.

## Verification of  Data
Bidder will verify that he has received all documents referenced in the Request for Quotation. If not, he will promptly advise the Project Buyer who will issue the missing documents.

## Queries and Clarifications
Bidder will submit all queries or requests for clarification in writing to the Project Buyer. All bidders will be provided with a verbatim copy of the query plus the response.

## Documentation
Bidder will supply documentation to assist in evaluation of his offer as required by the Request for Quotation, Goods and Services Information, Section 2.
Bidder may supply other documentation he considers is required to fully describe his offer. Documentation supplied should be concise and clearly indicate its purpose. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics is not obliged to consider documentation supplied other than that required by the Request for Quotation.

## Validity of Offer
Bidder’s offer to supply the Goods and Services will be irrevocable for a period of 60 days following the submission date.
Bidders offer will be valid in respect of each line item for which a separate price is quoted, whether or not any or all of the offers for other line items are accepted.

## Offer Summary
Bidder will complete the Offer Summary form to describe his offer to supply the Goods and Services in accordance with the following directions.

### Pricing
Bidder will quote a separate fixed price for each line item of the Goods and Services.
Bidder will quote in single or multiple source currencies for each line item so as to assume no risk for exchange rate fluctuations.

### Terms of Payment
Where Bidder is offering other than payment upon delivery, he will state his proposed progress payments, listing milestone events to which payments will be tied, the value of each payment, and the estimated duration in weeks after award that the milestone will be achieved.

### Documentation
Bidder will list all specific documents that are required by the Request for Quotation, Goods and Services Information, Section 2. A Delivery Date will be stated for each document as required by Section 9.4.

### Delivery
Bidder will offer fixed Delivery Dates for each of the Goods and Services, stated as weeks after award. Bidder will qualify these dates by stating any forecast manufacturing shutdown or closure periods that may be applicable.
Bidder will allow for all required documentation reviews and for all Quality Surveillance by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or Others in offering the fixed Delivery Date.
Bidder will state the trade union affiliations of their workforce together with the expiry dates of their collective agreements.

### Shipping
Bidder will state the method of shipping to the point of delivery.

### Deviations
Bidder’s offer to supply the Goods and Services will be considered to be in full compliance with the Request for Quotation and all referenced documents unless deviations or exceptions are explicitly stated and described.

## Packaging Summary
Bidder will complete the Packaging Summary form to describe the size and weight of all packages in which he will supply the Goods.

## Submission
Bidder will submit his offer to supply the Goods and Services on or before the date set out in the covering letter. Submission may be by e-mail, telefax, mail, courier, or hand delivery. Submissions by e-mail or telefax will be followed by paper format received by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics within five calendar days.

## Evaluation and Disposition of Offers
Offers will be evaluated in the sole discretion of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics as to overall performance, delivery and value. The evaluation will consider any deviations and exceptions included in the offer in the manner described by these instructions.
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics reserves the right to accept or reject any offer in whole or in part, or to reject all offers with or without notice or reasons, or to accept an offer other than that having the lowest price, or to accept an offer not in full compliance with the Request for Quotation. If no offer is acceptable, the work may be abandoned or performed in any manner selected by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics makes no representation that any Bidder is pre-qualified or is acceptable merely because they have received or responded to a Request for Quotation.
Unsuccessful Bidders will be advised within ten working days of the award of a Purchase Order for the Goods and Services to another party.
No costs incurred by Bidder, or any Supplier, or Others, in preparing Bidder’s offer to supply the Goods and Services will be reimbursed by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

## Confidentiality
Bidders will take reasonable measures to maintain the confidentiality of the Request for Quotation and all referenced documents. Bidder’s Suppliers will only be given sufficient information to provide an offer to Bidder.
The Request for Quotation and all documents provided to Bidder by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics remain the property of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. Originals and all copies must be returned to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or destroyed on demand.
