# 0-PRC-5005 — Procedure


[TABLE: 0-PRC-5005_table_1]


## Purpose
This procedure describes the steps required to issue a Request for Quotation or Purchase Order.

## Form of Request for Quotation or Purchase Order
The form will be as detailed in 0-PRC-5001: Form of Purchase Order.

## Initiation
Procurement will proceed with the issue of a Request for Quotation upon receipt of the approved Requisition (0-PRC-5002: Requisition for Quotation or Purchase Order).
Procurement will proceed with the issue of a Purchase Order upon receipt of the approved Requisition (0-PRC-5002: Requisition for Quotation or Purchase Order), the approved Cost Tabulation (0-PRC-5003: Bid Evaluation and Approval), and for orders of value over CAD 10 k, the approved Order Risk Assessment (0-PRC-5003: Bid Evaluation and Approval).

## Technical Requirements
Procurement will create the following sections of the Request for Quotation or Purchase Order by copying the relevant parts of the Requisition into the Purchasing Database.
Line Items 1.x (Scope of Supply)
Line Items 2.x (Documentation)
Text Section 1: Scope of Supply
Text Section 2: Documentation

## Line Items
Procurement will insert Line Items 3.x (Delivery) stating the appropriate type of packaging and delivery point (as defined by INCOTERMS 2000).

## Text Section 3: Delivery
The requirements for packaging and shipping will be as set out in the project Logistics Plan (0-PRC-3001: Project Planning). Procurement will insert the required references to packaging specifications, for Requests for Quotation will state “TBA” for the delivery date, and for Purchase Orders will insert the date agreed with the Vendor.

## Text Section 4: Quality Assurance
Procurement will insert the required references to Vendor Quality Assurance specifications and to the relevant Quality Data Sheets (0-PRC-7005: Vendor Quality Assurance).

## Text Section 5: Commercial
Procurement will insert the required references to conditions for purchase, and will insert Order-Specific Conditions (0-PRC-5004: Commercial Terms for Purchase).

## Text Section 6: References
In the case of a Request for Quotation, Procurement will insert the required references to 0-SPC-5006: Instructions to Bidders.
In the case of a Purchase Order, Procurement will insert the required references to Vendor-issued documentation (the latest revision of the quotation and any clarifications or explanations).

## Text Section 7: Contacts
Procurement will insert their own particulars as the commercial contact, the particulars of the responsible Discipline Lead as the technical contact, and the particulars of the responsible Inspector.
Procurement will insert the particulars of the Vendor’s responsible personnel.

## Issue of Request for Quotation
Procurement will publish the Request for Quotation (0-PRC-0005: Publishing of Project Documents).
Procurement will prepare covering letters to each of the Vendors listed in the Procurement Plan (0-PRC-3001: Project Planning), and will forward the letters to Document Control for issue to the Vendors (0-PRC-0006: Control of Project Documentation).

## Issue of Purchase Order
Procurement will publish the Purchase Order (0-PRC-0005: Publishing of Project Documents). The Project Engineer will check, and the Project Manager will approve the Purchase Order, affixing digital signatures. Procurement will print a paper copy.
Procurement will present the paper copy of the Purchase Order for authorisation as set out in Section 13 together with a copy of the approved Cost Tabulation and a copy of the approved Order Risk Assessment (0-PRC-5003: Bid Evaluation and Approval).
Following authorisation of the Purchase Order, Procurement will advise the Vendor in writing that he has been awarded the order, establishing the Start Date. Procurement will advise Document Control to issue a complete document package to the Vendor including the (unsigned) published version of the Purchase Order.
Procurement will forward the authorised paper copy of the Purchase Order directly to the Vendor.

## Authorisation of Purchase Order
Purchase Order will be signed by authorised personnel as follows
Procurement (Buyer)	< CAD100,000
Manager of Procurement	< CAD 500,000
Director of Projects	< CAD 2,000,000
President	> CAD 2,000,000
