# 0-PRC-8004 — Procedure


[TABLE: 0-PRC-8004_table_1]


## Purpose
This procedure lists the minimum job site postings required to communicate vital HSE information to all workers.

## Scope
Unless noted, these requirements will apply across all Aker Kvaerner Chemetics operations, design offices, shops, research facilities, and construction sites.

## Responsibilities
It is the responsibility of the designated Heath and Safety Manager for the worksite to post the following informational items to ensure that this information is communicated throughout the jobsite.

## Minimum Posting Requirements
A copy of each notice described below will be posted at each Aker Kvaerner Chemetics work establishment.  The notices shall be prominently displayed, preferably on a bulletin board, at one or more locations that will provide greatest exposure to all employees.
Aker Kvaerner Health and Safety Leadership Commitment poster
Emergency Information List
Post this list in conspicuous locations near each telephone and show current emergency numbers for medical assistance, fire, police, ambulance, and hospital.
Emergency Evacuation Plan
A one page summary showing the worksite emergency evacuation plan
Workers’ Compensation Notice
Each Province or State Workers’ Compensation agency requires the posting of a notification poster to inform all site personnel of their Workers Compensation benefits accorded to them by local State or Provincial law.
Work Site Safety Rules
Work sites (with the exception of the engineering office environment) will have posted near the employee entrance the most important site rules. Section 5 of this document outlines the minimum safety rules requirement.
Hazard Communication Notice (where required)
A notice shall be posted to notify all site personnel of their rights under the Hazard Communication Standard. Required for work sites where chemicals are being handled.
OSHA 300A; Summary of Work Related Injuries and Illnesses (where required)
The OSHA 300A Summary shall be completed signed and posted on an annual basis no later than February 1st and shall remain posted until April 30th.
The corporate office shall retain this document for a period of five years.
Note: This is only applicable to jobsites in the United States, and other jurisdictions where Client, or local regulations require it.
Minutes of Site Safety Meetings
Minutes of Safety Meetings will be posted until replaced by the subsequent minutes of the same meeting.  After the initial posting, minutes of safety meetings will be kept for a period of two years.
The minutes of the meetings may be kept on-line if all employees have access to view them.

## Worksite Safety Rules Requirement
Worksites (with the exception of the engineering office environment) will have posted near the employee entrance the most important site rules.  These rules will be clearly readable from a distance of four meters on a construction site, and two meters in a laboratory or shop setting.
The following sections list the minimum safety rules for each type of work site.

### Construction Site Rules
Hard hat and safety boots will be worn by all workers entering the work site.
All personnel entering the work site for the first time will report to the site office first thing.
Consuming or possession of illegal drugs or alcohol on this project site is prohibited.
First Aid treatment shall be obtained immediately for any injury.
Unsafe conditions, safety observations and incidents will be reported to your supervisor immediately.
Clothing that covers arms and legs will be worn (long sleeves and long pants).
Only tools that are in good repair meeting industry standards, with all guards and safety devices, will be used.
Fighting, horseplay, practical jokes or interference with other workers is prohibited.
Theft, vandalism or abuse or misuse of Company property is prohibited.

### Commissioning Site Rules

## Hard hat, safety glasses, and safety boots will be worn by all workers entering the job site
All personnel entering the work site for the first time will report to the site office first thing.
Consuming or possession of illegal drugs or alcohol on this project site is prohibited.
First Aid treatment shall be obtained immediately for any injury.

## Unsafe conditions, safety observations and incidents will be reported to your supervisor immediately.
Clothing that covers arms and legs will be worn (long sleeves and long pants).
If in place, and if they meet or exceed Aker Kvaerner Chemetics standards, the client’s safety rules and regulations will be followed.
By-passing, or forcing, interlocks will only be done with the permission of the Commissioning Manager.
Comply with all Aker Kvaerner Chemetics (Aker Kvaerner) safety rules and regulations.

### Workshop Rules
Personal protective equipment shall be worn in accordance with the Company’s written procedures.
All visitors and contractors are required to sign-in and follow safety rules as instructed by the Company.
Consuming or possession of illegal drugs or alcohol on company premises is prohibited.
First Aid treatment shall be obtained immediately for any injury.
Unsafe conditions, safety observations and incidents are to be reported immediately to your supervisor.
Only company provided work wear shall be worn when working in the workshop.
Only tools that are in good repair meeting industry standards, with all guards and safety devices, will be used.
Fighting, horseplay, practical jokes or interference with other workers is prohibited.
Theft, vandalism, abuse or misuse of Company property is prohibited.

### Laboratory Rules
Personnel are responsible for their own safety, for the safety of others, and for keeping their work area clean.
Safety glasses must be worn in the Laboratory (office area excepted).
Consuming or possession of illegal drugs or alcohol on company premises is prohibited.
Preparation and consumption of food or drink are strictly forbidden in the Laboratory working area.
Smoking is strictly prohibited inside the building.
Working alone in the Laboratory is prohibited (office area excepted).
Dispose of chemical wastes safely and promptly.
All Chemicals are to be correctly and clearly labelled and stored in accordance to WHMIS guidelines.
All spills must be reported to the CTC Supervisor and be promptly and completely neutralized, absorbed, and disposed of in accordance to the “Hazardous Laboratory Chemicals Disposal Guide”.
Be Familiar with emergency procedures in the CTC Laboratory Safety Manual.
All injuries, no matter how slight, must be reported to the CTC Supervisor immediately.
Guests are not allowed in the Laboratory working area without the prior permission from the CTC Supervisor or Manager. Permitted guests will be accompanied at all times by a Laboratory employee.
