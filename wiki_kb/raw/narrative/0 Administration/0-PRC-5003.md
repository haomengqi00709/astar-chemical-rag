# 0-PRC-5003 — Procedure


[TABLE: 0-PRC-5003_table_1]


## Purpose
This procedure describes the evaluation of Vendor’s bids to ensure that Vendors selected are able to supply the Goods and Services in compliance with the Purchase Orders at minimum business risk to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

## Format and Numbering
The evaluation is a single document prepared in four separate sections:
Cost Tabulation
Technical Bid Tabulation
Commercial Bid Tabulation
Order Risk Assessment
The sections will be numbered (0-PRC-0002: Document Numbering) using the same Major Work Area as for the Goods and Services being ordered, and using the same Identification Number as for the intended Purchase Order. For example: 99C10500-4-TAB-4005-1-R2.

## Work Sequencing
The evaluation of Vendor’s bids is set out in flowchart format in 0-SCM-5003: Bid Evaluation and Approval. This procedure describes only the major elements of the work and should be read in conjunction with the flowchart.

## Preliminary Bid Evaluation
The Requisitioning Discipline will conduct a preliminary evaluation of all bids received documenting it in the form of 0-TAB-5XXX-1: [Template] Cost Tabulation.
This will tabulate the pricing of each line item of each bid received, including any alternates. The pricing will be itemised and totalled in the currencies quoted. The itemised pricing and the totals for each Vendor will be converted to Canadian dollars using project hedge rates where applicable or forward rates when not hedged. The cost of collapsing currency hedges will be assigned as an additional cost to any bid quoted in a currency different from that budgeted. The budget in Canadian dollars will be included for each line item tabulated including a notation of the budgeted currency. For tabulations where not all Vendors have quoted for all line items, a sub-tabulation will be prepared for the common items to allow an equalised comparison.
The Requisitioning Discipline will list on the tabulation any additional cost elements such as inspection, quality surveillance, freight, associated structures, piping, utility connections, erection costs, etc. that will need to be quantified to properly compare the total cost to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics of each bid. The applicability of each additional cost element to each bid will be indicated by a checkmark.
The Requisitioning Discipline will list on the tabulation each Vendor’s quoted delivery duration comparing with the requirements of the project schedule. The Requisitioning Discipline will conduct a preliminary review of the technical merits of each Vendor’s bid and indicate on the tabulation whether they are acceptable or not. The Requisitioning Discipline will indicate on the tabulation whether the Vendor has been used for the supply of similar Goods and Services to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics previously.

## Orders of Value < CAD 10 k
The Requisitioning Discipline will conduct a risk assessment using the form described in Section 8 as a guideline (this process will not be documented). The objective of this process is to select the lowest cost bid that presents acceptable business risks to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. The selected Vendor will be identified as such on the Cost Tabulation.
If the Requisitioning Discipline is of the opinion that a more extended review of the bids is required then the procedure described in Section 6 will be followed.
The Requisitioning Discipline will publish the Cost Tabulation (0-PRC-0005: Publishing of Project Documents) and the Requisitioning Discipline Approver will approve the tabulation. The approved Cost Tabulation provides authorisation to issue the Purchase Order (0-PRC-5005: Issue of Request for Quotation or Purchase Order).

## Orders of Value < CAD 200 k
The Requisitioning Discipline will meet with the Project Engineer to review the Cost Tabulation, to conduct a risk assessment as described in Section 8, and to select the Vendor to receive the order.  The objective of this process is to select the lowest cost bid that presents acceptable business risks to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. The selected Vendor will be identified as such on the Cost Tabulation.
The Project Engineer will direct that the order be split among two or more Vendors if an overall lower cost to the project will result. In this case the Cost Tabulation will be copied and re-numbered for each order to be placed, and a Risk Assessment completed for each order.
If the Project Engineer is of the opinion that a more extended review of the bids is required then the procedure described in Section 7 will be followed.
The Requisitioning Discipline will publish the Cost Tabulation (0-PRC-0005: Publishing of Project Documents) and the Project Engineer will approve the tabulation. The approved Cost Tabulation provides authorisation to issue the Purchase Order (0-PRC-5005: Issue of Request for Quotation or Purchase Order) provided the conditions described in the Order Risk Assessment are met.

## Orders of Value > CAD 200 k

### Initial Review
The Requisitioning Discipline will meet with the Project Engineer and the Project Manager to review the Cost Tabulation and to select a short-list of Vendors whose bids will be further evaluated. The Project Manager will further direct the Requisitioning Discipline as follows:
identify the Vendors which will require a shop survey
identify the Vendors which will require a technical reference check
identify which other disciplines will conduct a technical evaluation
identify which additional cost elements will be quantified to complete the Cost Tabulation
The Requisitioning Discipline will record these directions on the Cost Tabulation, will publish it (0-PRC-0005: Publishing of Project Documents), and the Project Engineer will approve the tabulation.
If the lowest bid of the short-listed Vendors is over budget, the Project Manager will decide whether or not to proceed with the procurement of the Goods and Services. If the Project Manager decides not to proceed with the procurement then she will direct the Project Engineer to work with the project team to determine alternate design or procurement strategies to reduce the cost. This direction will be by memorandum copied to the relevant Discipline Leads and to the Director of Projects. If the Project Manager decides to proceed with the procurement and the lowest bid of the short-listed Vendors is more than 10% over budget, then she will issue a memo to the Director of Projects advising of the decision made, attaching the Cost Tabulation, and explaining why no further work to reduce cost will be done.

### Technical Evaluation
The Requisitioning Discipline will conduct a detailed technical evaluation of the short-listed bids documenting it in the form of 0-TAB-5XXX-2: [Template] Technical Bid Tabulation.
As directed by the Project Manager, the Requisitioning Discipline will solicit technical evaluations from other disciplines, providing them copies of the Cost Tabulation and the short-listed Vendors’ bids. This process should be initiated by a meeting between all participants to ensure that no work is being duplicated and that all evaluation criteria are being covered.
The evaluation will consist of a comparison of the technical information provided against the requirements of the Scope of Supply. The comparisons will be made in as many characteristics as necessary to indicate clearly whether the Goods and Services offered comply with the requirements, and to highlight any superior characteristics of one Vendor’s offer as compared to the others.
Where appropriate, the evaluation will include a comparison of the process performance of the Goods and Services offered (throughput, efficiency, power and other utility consumption, etc.) and the impact on any performance guarantees held by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
Where appropriate, the evaluation will include a summary of observations or evaluations based on previous experience with the Vendors’ Goods and Services. As directed by the Project Manager Vendor references will be contacted and the evaluation will include a summary of the information received.
Where clarification of a Vendor’s bid is necessary, the Requisitioning Discipline will direct the query to the Vendor, copying the other technical evaluators and Procurement.
The final Technical Bid Tabulation will be prepared by the Requisitioning Discipline. It will be a compilation of the evaluations of all participating disciplines and will include each discipline’s ranking of the Vendors evaluated.
The Requisitioning Discipline will publish the Technical Bid Tabulation (0-PRC-0005: Publishing of Project Documents), all other discipline evaluators will check the tabulation and the Requisitioning Discipline Approver will approve the tabulation.

### Final Cost Evaluation
The Requisitioning Discipline will conduct a detailed cost evaluation of the short-listed bids documenting it in the form of 0-TAB-5XXX-1: [Template] Cost Tabulation.
This will be a revision of the previously prepared Cost Tabulation, with the pricing of line items revised as necessary to provide a complete and accurate record of each Vendor’s offer as it is changed in response to requests for clarification or modification.
The Cost Tabulation will compare the total cost to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics of each short-listed bid including an estimate of any additional cost elements such as inspection, quality surveillance, freight, associated structures, piping, utility connections, erection costs, etc.
Sections of the previously prepared Cost Tabulation dealing with the technical merits of each Vendor’s bid and Aker Kvaerner Chemetics experience with each Vendor will be deleted since these issues will be dealt with more thoroughly in the Technical Bid Tabulation.
Following the review meeting described in Section 7.7 the selected Vendor will be identified as such on the Cost Tabulation. The Requisitioning Discipline will publish the Cost Tabulation (0-PRC-0005: Publishing of Project Documents) and the Project Manager will approve the tabulation. The approved Cost Tabulation provides authorisation to issue the Purchase Order (0-PRC-5005: Issue of Request for Quotation or Purchase Order) provided the conditions described in the Order Risk Assessment are met.

### Financial Evaluation
Finance will examine the financial information of the short-listed Vendors for the previous fiscal year to ensure that the risk that the Vendors may suffer a liquidity or credit crisis is minimal. The financial information will be from the parent company where the Vendor is a subsidiary.
Where the required Vendor financial information is not already available to Finance, it will be expedited by Procurement.
The financial evaluation and recommendations will be issued by Finance to Procurement by way of memorandum.

### Commercial Evaluation
Procurement will conduct a detailed commercial evaluation of the short-listed bids documenting it in the form of 0-TAB-5XXX-3: [Template] Commercial Bid Tabulation.
Procurement will list the commercial deviations proposed by each Vendor, comparing them with the approach the other Vendors are taking to the same items.
Procurement will provide on the Commercial Bid Tabulation the following additional information for each of the short-listed Vendors:
the evaluation provided by Finance
any outstanding claims or disputes between the Vendor and Aker Kvaerner Chemetics
any previous claims or disputes between the Vendor and Aker Kvaerner Chemetics
the total value of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ Purchase Orders current with the Vendor
Where clarification of a Vendor’s bid is necessary, Procurement will direct the query to the Vendor, copying the technical evaluators.
The evaluator will publish the Commercial Bid Tabulation (0-PRC-0005: Publishing of Project Documents), and the Manager of Procurement will approve the tabulation.

### Shop Survey
For custom fabricated Goods and Services, if a short-listed Vendor has done no work for Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for the previous three years, then a shop survey will be conducted by a Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Quality Assurance Inspector. The scope of the shop survey and the format for the subsequent report will be as set out in 0-REP-5XXX: [Template] Vendor Shop Survey. This document will be numbered (0-PRC-0002: Document Numbering) using the same identification number as for the original requisition. Surveys for multiple Vendors will be considered as sheets of a single document.
The Shop Survey will include recommendations by the Inspector as to additional Tests and Inspections or a higher level of Quality Surveillance required for the Vendor.
The Inspector will publish the Shop Survey (0-PRC-0005: Publishing of Project Documents), and the Manager of Quality Assurance will approve the survey. A copy of the approved survey will be forwarded to the Requisitioning Discipline who will account for the additional cost elements in the Cost Tabulation.

### Final Review and Vendor Selection
The Requisitioning Discipline will convene a meeting with the following personnel:
the Project Engineer
the Project Manager
the Project Buyer
the Discipline Leads involved in the Technical Bid Tabulation
The meeting will review the Cost and Bid Tabulations, will conduct a risk assessment as described in Section 8, and will select the Vendor to receive the order. The objective of this process is to select the lowest cost bid that presents acceptable business risks to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
Should any of the meeting participants feel that the selected Vendor will result in unacceptable business risks to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics that are not adequately mitigated by the instructions on the Order Risk Assessment then they may request that the meeting be reconvened to include the Directors of Technology, Engineering and Projects. If the Directors cannot agree that the business risks to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics are acceptable then the matter will be referred to the President.
The Project Manager will direct that the order be split among two or more Vendors if an overall lower cost to the project will result. In this case the Cost Tabulation and Bid Tabulations will be copied and re-numbered for each order to be placed, and a Risk Assessment completed for each order.

## Risk Assessment
A risk assessment will be carried out for the selected Vendor and in the case of orders of value > CAD 10 k the assessment will be documented in the form of 0-TAB-5XXX-4: [Template] Order Risk Assessment.
The template provides a checklist guide to assessing business risks and also provides the means to instruct Procurement what contractual conditions must be obtained during negotiation with the Vendor to mitigate the risks identified.
In cases where the Vendor has not received a satisfactory evaluation from Finance then the Requisitioning Discipline and the Project Manager will consult with the Director of Projects and the V.P. Finance as to what if any contractual conditions will mitigate the risks and will incorporate these measures in the instructions to Procurement.
The Requisitioning Discipline will publish the Order Risk Assessment (0-PRC-0005: Publishing of Project Documents), for orders of value < CAD 200 k the Project Engineer will approve the assessment, and for orders of value > CAD 200 k the Project Manager will approve the assessment.
Should the Vendor not agree during negotiations to the commercial conditions required by the instructions contained in the Order Risk Assessment then the review meeting described in Section 7.7 will be reconvened to include the Directors of Technology, Engineering and Projects. If the Directors cannot agree that the business risks to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics are acceptable then the matter will be referred to the President.
