# 0-PRC-7004 — Procedure


[TABLE: 0-PRC-7004_table_1]


## Purpose
All project documents will be checked and approved in accordance with this procedure in order to be published with the following status:
Issued for Owner Approval
Approved for Design
Approved for Fabrication
Approved for Construction

## Discipline Responsibilities
Discipline responsibilities for preparation, checking, and approval of project documents are set out in the following procedures:
1-PRC-: Flowsheet Development and Approval
1-PRC-: P&ID Development and Approval
2-PRC-0002: Civil Works Design and Specification
3-PRC-0002: Structural and Architectural Design and Specification
4-PRC-0003: Equipment Design and Specification
5-PRC-0003: Piping Design and Specification
5-PRC-0004: Line List Development and Approval
6-PRC-0002: Insulation Design and Specification
7-PRC-0002: Coatings Design and Specification
8-PRC-0003: Field Instrument Design and Specification
8-PRC-0004: Control System Design and Specification
9-PRC-0003: Electrical Design and Specification

## Project Responsibilities
Each design discipline will have an individual assigned (0-PRC-2001: Assignment of Project Responsibilities) as a Discipline Lead. The Discipline Leads will check all work as assigned by the procedures listed in Section 2.
Each design discipline will have an individual assigned (0-PRC-2001: Assignment of Project Responsibilities) as a Discipline Approver. The Discipline Approver will approve all work as assigned by the procedures listed in Section 2.

## Checking
Checking will verify that the correct input data has been used in the work being checked. For this reason, it is usually assigned to the discipline responsible for producing the documentation that sets out the input.

## Approval
Approval will signify that the work has been done accurately in accordance with appropriate procedures and the requirements of the Contract, and that it has been checked as required. The approver has the ultimate responsibility for the accuracy and compliance of the work.
In the case where documents are being approved which have been derived from the output of calculations, then the approver is further signifying that the supporting calculations have also been checked and approved.

## Revisions
All revisions to documents originally checked and approved in accordance with this procedure will further be checked and approved before re-issue.
