# 0-PRC-8005 — Procedure


[TABLE: 0-PRC-8005_table_1]


## Purpose
This procedure establishes a standard format for health and Safety orientations and lists the information to be reviewed with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics employees upon hire. This procedure extends to visitors, contract employees, indirect employees, and Vendor employees on Aker Kvaerner Chemetics work sites.

## Personnel definitions
Employees – Are permanent employees, contract employees or temporary employees of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
Escorted Personnel (visitors) – Are visiting personnel who are not direct employees Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and who are on site for more than one week, will have minimal exposure to construction or operating hazards, and who will be escorted at all times by a Aker Kvaerner Chemetics employee.
Unescorted Personnel  – Are Subcontractor employees, or indirect employees, or visiting personnel who are not direct employees of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, who are on site for one week or less, and who are expected to work independently.

## Design Office Orientations

### Design Office HSE Responsibilities
It is the responsibility of the Manager of Human Resources to give a health and safety orientation to all new personnel coming into the office for a period of more than one week and who will be required to work independently (and unescorted) in the office environment.
Managers will:
Ensure that their employee has received an initial Health and Safety orientation prior to starting work.
Conduct an orientation with their employee(s) reviewing the potential hazards of the specific operations in the work area.

### Employees
All Aker Kvaerner Chemetics employees or contract employees receive a pre-job orientation, the content of which is determined by Human Resources and the Division doing the hiring.  Health and safety will be part of the pre-job orientation. This health and safety component may be given as part of the Company orientation or as a separate orientation, but it is to be given before starting the job.
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics employees will be considered “Unescorted Personnel” and will go through the same orientation procedures as the “Unescorted Personnel”, section 3.4 below.

### Escorted Personnel
The safety of escorted Personnel is the responsibility of the primary Aker Kvaerner Chemetics employee who is receiving the visiting person(s).
The employee will present the visitor with the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics office Health and Safety Card and answer any questions the visitor may have about health and safety. Any health and safety questions the employee cannot answer will be directed to the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics HSE manager.
In the event of an emergency the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics employee will act to safeguard the safety of his guest by ensuring that the guest follows Aker Kvaerner Chemetics emergency procedures, and/or by providing the guest with assistance in case of an emergency.

### Unescorted Personnel
Unescorted personnel are expected to follow all Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, Aker Kvaerner and Client safety policies, and will participate fully in the health safety and environment awareness programs being used at the work site.
The Design office health and safety orientation will follow the minimum outline given in Appendix 1.
All individuals attending the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics design office safety orientation will receive:
0-MAN-8100-RA Office Safety Manual
All individuals receiving the safety orientation will sign an orientation attendance log which will contain the following information as a minimum:  Date, Name, Company, Department, and Social Security number.
Personnel who have been absent from the office for more than one year (and who regularly visit the office) will receive a new orientation.

## Construction Site Orientations

### Site Responsibilities
The “Site Health and Safety Manager” is responsible for giving Health Safety and Environment orientations to all personnel entering a work site.
Construction Manager will:
Ensure that each employee has received an initial Health and Safety orientation prior to starting work.
Conduct an orientation with their employee(s) reviewing the potential hazards of the specific operations in the work area.
Supervise the instruction and training of a new employee, either personally or through delegation to experienced employees, until the new employee is able to satisfactorily demonstrate his/her ability to perform work safely.

### Employees
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics office employees or Contract employees traveling to a project site receive a safety orientation prior to departure to site as outlined in 4.2.1 below.
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics employees will be considered “Unescorted Personnel” and will go through the same orientation procedures as the “Unescorted Personnel”, section 4.4 below.

#### Orientation Prior to Departure for Site
The Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Health and Safety manager will provide each employee or contract employee traveling from the office (or through the office) and going to the worksite with a safety orientation.  In the absence of the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Health and Safety Manager this responsibility will be transferred to the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Manager or Construction Services (for construction personnel) or to the Director of Technology (for commissioning personnel).
The safety orientation prior to travel to site will follow the minimum outline given in Appendix 2.

### Escorted Personnel (on construction site)
The orientation of escorted personnel (visitors) will consist of the following as a minimum:
Provide guest with necessary personal protection equipment and instruction on how to use it.
Overview of site emergency response plan
First aid contact information.
Site specific high risk hazards.
In the event of an emergency the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics escort will act to safeguard the safety of his guest by ensuring that the guest follows Aker Kvaerner Chemetics emergency procedures, and/or by providing the guest with assistance in case of an emergency.

### Unescorted Personnel (on construction site)
This class of personnel are expected to act independently and will therefore receive a full safety orientation.
These employees are expected to follow all Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, Aker Kvaerner and Client safety policies, and will participate fully in the health safety and environment awareness programs being used at the site.

#### Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Site Orientation
The Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site safety orientation may include audio/visual and oral discussion. The exact means of delivery is left to be decided by the site Health and Safety Manager. Every worker entering the site for an unescorted visit will sign the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics safety orientation log which will contain the following information as a minimum:  Date, Name, Company, Department, and Social Security number.
All employees attending the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics safety orientation will receive a copy of the “Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Construction Safety Handbook”.
The worksite health and safety orientation will follow the outline given in Appendix 3.03 of the Aker Kvaerner HSE Manual.

#### Client / Owner Orientation
After completion of the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics / Aker Kvaerner safety orientation, the new worker may be required to attend the Client / Owner safety orientation. If this is a project requirement instructions will be provided during the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site orientation.

## Research Laboratory Orientations

### Employees
All employees receive a pre-job orientation, the content of which is determined by Human Resources.  The Health and Safety component will be part of the pre-job orientation and will be given at the Technology Centre as a separate orientation before starting the job.
The Technology Centre health and safety orientation will follow the minimum outline given in Appendix 3.  All individuals receiving the safety orientation will sign the Acknowledgement Form (found in the AKCTC Safety Manual, Appendix D).

### Escorted Personnel
The safety of escorted Personnel is the responsibility of the primary AK Aker Kvaerner Chemetics employee who is receiving the visiting person(s).
The employee will brief the visitor on the Technology Centre emergency plan; brief the visitor on basic safety precautions needed during the visit, and answer any questions the visitor may have. Questions that the employee cannot answer will be directed first to the AKCTC supervisor, then to the AK Aker Kvaerner Chemetics HSE manager.
In the event of an emergency, the AKCTC employee will act to safeguard the safety of his guest by ensuring that the guest follows all emergency procedures and by providing the guest with assistance as required.

### Unescorted Personnel
It is the joint responsibility of the AKCTC Manager or Supervisor to give a health and safety orientation to all new personnel coming into the Technology Centre for a period of more than two days and who will be required to work independently (and unescorted) in the laboratory environment.  Personnel, who have not visited the Technology Centre for one year or more, will receive a new orientation.  The orientation will follow the minimum outline given in Appendix 3, and individuals who receive it will sign the standard AKCTC Acknowledgement Form.
