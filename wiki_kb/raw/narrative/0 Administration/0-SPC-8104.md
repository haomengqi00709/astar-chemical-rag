# 0-SPC-8104 — Specification


[TABLE: 0-SPC-8104_table_1]


## Purpose
This specification describes the minimum Health and Safety standards that Vendor employees will meet when working at a Aker Kvaerner Chemetics work site.

## Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Health and Safety Policy
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics is committed to:
1) Providing a safe and healthy workplace for all its employees.
2) Conducting operations in an environmentally sound manner.
Everyone working on a Aker Kvaerner Chemetics work site is expected to cooperate and contribute toward the success of the company HSE program by performing their jobs in the safest manner prescribed, and by conducting themselves in a way that enhances their personal safety and that of others.

## Responsibilities of Vendor Employees on Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Worksite
Vendor safety concerns, if any, are to be considered before Vendor’s employee travels to construction sites.
Vendor employees will review all construction HSE specifications prior to site visit
Vendor employees will attend a Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site safety briefing upon arrival
Vendor employees will use required minimum personal protection equipment (PPE) at all times in the work worksite
Vendor employees will follow Aker Kvaerner Chemetics HSE policies and procedures while on work site
Any work procedure must be approved by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site manager prior to commencement of work
Vendor employees will report safety infractions and safety incidents to the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics HSE Manager on the worksite.

### Personal Protection Equipment (PPE)
Vendor employees visiting Aker Kvaerner Chemetics worksites are required to wear the following minimum PPE equipment:
Clothing that covers arms and legs (long sleeves and long pants minimum)
Safety work boots which meet CSA Z195 Standard, or ANSI Z41 standard.  A green patch in the shape of either a rectangle or a triangle indicates CSA approval
Hard hat which meets the CAN/CSA Standard Z94.1-92 and associated standards, or ANSI Z89.1 standard
Glasses with side shields, or goggles.  All protective eyewear must meet CSA Z94.3 Standard, or ANSI Z87.1 standard
Note: PPE equipment of equal or superior will be acceptable.
Vendor will ensure that his employees are aware of the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics minimum personal protection equipment requirements.
Vendor employees are responsible arriving at the worksite wearing clothing and footwear as per above standards.  Aker Kvaerner Chemetics will provide a hardhat, eyewear and other PPE required to meet the minimum requirements above.
Specialized PPE required by the Vendor for his employees to wear during the performance of the contracted work is the responsibility of the Vendor.
