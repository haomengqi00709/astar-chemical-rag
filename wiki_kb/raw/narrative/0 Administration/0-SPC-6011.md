# 0-SPC-6011 — Specification


[TABLE: 0-SPC-6011_table_1]


## Purpose
This specification describes how the Subcontractor is to allocate costs in 0-LST-6X11: Construction Subcontract Price List, and in quotations for compensation events.

## Labour Rates
The Subcontractor will complete 0-LST-6X12: Construction Subcontract Labour Rates to derive a total cost per hour worked for each classification of person to be engaged in Providing the Works. The labour rates will include the following costs:
Wages, salaries, premiums and allowances paid directly to employees
Payments made on behalf of employees to government or other agencies such as income taxes, social security taxes, pension contributions, benefit premiums, union dues, etc.
Payments made directly to employees or on behalf of employees for travel to the site and for living expenses while working at the site.
Redistribution of paid non-productive time such as meal breaks, public holidays, leave or vacation, sick leave, contingency for inclement weather, etc.
Consumable supplies such as small tools and consumables, personal protective equipment, office supplies, etc.

## Direct Work
Direct work is the supply of all permanently installed materials and the labour required to fabricate, erect, and install those materials.

### Crew Rates
The Subcontractor will complete 0-LST-6X13: Construction Subcontract Crew Rates to show an average total cost per hour worked for each direct labour work crew to be engaged in Providing the Works. The crew rates will be calculated using the labour rates determined per Section 2.

### Direct Labour Costs
Direct labour is the effort required to fabricate, erect, and install the Equipment and Materials.
The Subcontractor will enter direct labour costs in the worksheet “Direct Costs” in 0-LST-6X11: Construction Subcontract Price List, using the work breakdown entered by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
The Subcontractor will show the man-hours of direct labour required, and will use the crew rates determined per Section 3.1 to calculate the total cost of direct labour.

### Direct Materials Costs
Direct materials costs includes the delivered cost of Equipment and Materials supplied by the Subcontractor plus the delivered costs of any materials such as formwork consumed in the fabrication, erection and installation of the Equipment and Materials.
The Subcontractor will enter direct materials costs in the worksheet “Direct Costs” in 0-LST-6X11: Construction Subcontract Price List, using the work breakdown entered by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

## Indirect Work
Indirect work is all work which does not become a permanent part of the project but which is necessary for the orderly completion of the project.

### Indirect Labour Schedule
The Subcontractor will complete 0-LST-6X14: Construction Subcontract Indirect Labour Schedule to show the weeks on the site for all site-based indirect personnel, as well as the weeks engaged directly on the Subcontract for all home office-based personnel.
Site-based indirect personnel include the following:
all levels of supervision
all administrative and support staff
clerical and janitorial workers
storemen and material handlers
installers / removers of field service facilities
erectors, operators and maintainers of Construction Equipment
first aid attendants
health and safety staff and workforce representatives
union shop stewards

### Equipment Schedule
The Subcontractor will complete 0-LST-6X15: Construction Subcontract Equipment Schedule to show the weeks on the site for all items of Construction Equipment. This will include all items of equipment used for the fabrication, erection and installation of the Equipment and Materials such as cranes, welding machines, scaffolding, etc, as well as field services items such as those listed on 0-LST-6X90-2: Construction Subcontract Field Services Scope.

### Indirect Labour
The Subcontractor will enter indirect labour costs in the worksheet “Indirect Costs” in 0-LST-6X11: Construction Subcontract Price List, using a separate row for each position or classification identified on the Indirect labour Schedule per Section 4.1.
The Subcontractor will show the man-hours of indirect labour required, and will use the labour rates per Section 2 or the crew rates determined per Section 3.1 as applicable to calculate the total cost of indirect labour.
Data for erectors, operators and maintainers of Construction Equipment will be combined on the same rows as for the Construction Equipment.

### Construction Equipment
The Subcontractor will enter costs for mobilisation / demobilisation, rental, fuel, and maintenance in the worksheet “Indirect Costs” in 0-LST-6X11: Construction Subcontract Price List, using a separate row for each item identified on the Equipment Schedule per Section 4.2.

## Overhead
Overheads are cost items that are proportional to the total Price of the subcontract or are corporate-based, the recoveries of which are derived from all projects that the Subcontractor undertakes.
The Subcontractor will enter overhead costs in the worksheet “Cover Sheet” in 0-LST-6X11: Construction Subcontract Price List, expressed as percentages of the total of Direct Work Items and Indirect Work Items.

## Sub-subcontractors
Sub-subcontractor’s costs will be listed as if incurred by Subcontractor, i.e. broken down into Direct Labour, Direct Materials, Indirect Labour, Construction Equipment and Overheads.

## Compensation Events
Subcontractor’s quotations for compensation events will be presented as:
The Prices for Direct Work Items
The Prices for Indirect Work Items
The Prices for Insurance, Financing and Security at the same rates as given on 0-LST-6X11: Construction Subcontract Price List
The Price for Overhead and Profit determined using the rates given in 0-SUB-6X00: Construction Subcontract Agreement.
A quotation for Indirect Work Items will only be approved where the compensation event is shown to cause an extension of time on the site for the items claimed.
A quotation for Indirect Labour will only be approved where the person is identified on the Indirect Labour Schedule. The costs of personnel not identified will be assumed to be included in Overhead.

## Progress Payments
Section 24.4 of 0-SPC-6001: Standard Conditions for Construction Subcontract states that the amount due to the Subcontractor is the Price for Work Done to Date. This in turn is defined in Section 2.2 (16) of 0-SPC-6001: Standard Conditions for Construction Subcontract to be the Price for each lump sum item in the Price List multiplied by the percent complete of that lump sum item and, where a quantity is stated for an item in the Price List, an amount calculated by multiplying the quantity which the Subcontractor has completed by the rate.
In order to determine the percent complete of each lump sum item in the Price List, each lump sum item must be represented by one or more activities on a level 3 schedule (0-SPC-6021: Construction Subcontract Scheduling). Prior to the first assessment day the Subcontractor will provide Aker Kvaerner Chemetics with a proposed revision to 0-LST-6X11: Construction Subcontract Price List that provides additional subdivision of the line items on the Price List to provide this correspondence with the accepted schedule.
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics will approve and re-issue 0-LST-6X11: Construction Subcontract Price List to show the work breakdown that the Subcontractor will use to claim payment due.
