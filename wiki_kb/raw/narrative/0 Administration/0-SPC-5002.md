## Assessing ompensation vents

### The increase or decrease in the Prices are assessed as the effect of the compensation event upon :
the cost to the Vendor of the work already done;
the forecast cost to the Vendor of the work not yet done; and
any resulting adjustment to an allowance for margin.

### The assessment of a compensation event which is or includes subcontracted work does not include any margin or profit on payments by the Vendor to the Suppliers.
