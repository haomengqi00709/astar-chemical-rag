# 0-PRC-6001 — Procedure


[TABLE: 0-PRC-6001_table_1]


## Purpose
This procedure provides guidelines for conducting Plant Constructability Reviews on major projects with an EPC scope of supply.  With minor modifications, this procedure may be used on smaller projects with other scopes of supply.

## Timing
The Plant Constructability Review will be conducted at the earliest opportunity to enable design concepts to be incorporated without major cost expenditure.
The Review will be held during development of the Plot Plan and Equipment Layouts and prior to commencement of detail design.

## Review Objectives
The objectives of the Review are to ensure that the detail design is carried out with an understanding of how equipment will be installed, accessed, maintained, and how the Plant will be constructed.

## Responsibilities
The Project Engineer will act as Review Leader.  The Review will be attended by the Project Manager, Manager of Construction and Discipline Leads.
The Project Engineer is responsible to ensure that information required for the Review is available, that the Review is conducted and that actions are assigned and followed-up.  The Project Engineer will interpret the results of the Review into clear instructions for the Discipline Leads to carry out the detail design.

## Information Required for Review
Preliminary Plot Plan
Preliminary Equipment Arrangement / Sections
Equipment List
Procurement Plan
Logistics Plan
Information as indicated with (X) on the Plant Constructability Review Checklist

## Review Methodology
Plant Constructability Review Checklist will provide guidance for the Review.  The Review Leader will determine the review method and time spent on each item.   Participants will decide on the applicability of any particular item on the Checklist and will raise any additional applicable items for review.
Information presented at the review will be recorded under “Record” on the Checklist.  Follow-up actions and responsibilities will also be recorded.
