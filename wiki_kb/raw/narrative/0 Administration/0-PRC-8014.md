# 0-PRC-8014 — Procedure


[TABLE: 0-PRC-8014_table_1]


## Purpose
This procedure provides the guidelines for the reporting, investigation and follow-up of all accidents and near miss Incidents with the aim of
providing an effective system to enable personnel to notify the HSE Department and appropriate line management in the event of an accident or near miss;
ensuring suitable arrangements are made for prompt reporting of accidents and near miss incidents to appropriate person(s), Senior Management, and external authorities;
ensuring effective communication to enable a prompt response to accidents and near miss incidents, and to ensure an effective investigation; and
to comply with Aker Kvaerner Group HSE Norms and procedures as detailed in
2.2 NO 1 Incident Follow-up Norm
2.2 PR 1 HSE Reporting Procedure
2.2 PR 2 Incident Investigation Procedure
2.2 NO 4 Just Culture Norm

## Definitions
Company – Aker Kvaerner Chemetics
Group – Aker Kvaerner Group companies
HSE – Health Safety and Environment
Near Miss – A discharge of energy (or release of hazardous chemical) that had the potential to cause injury to an employee, damage to the environment, or a loss.
Accident – An event that affected the Health or Safety of employees, that caused damage to the environment, or that resulted in property loss.
Incident – Accident or a near-miss incident.

## Scope
This procedure applies to all work sites where the Company is in control of operations, and to other work sites to the extent that the Company can enforce the application of HSE management processes.

## Responsibilities

### Employees
Report all incidents and to his/her supervisor or manager immediately after they occur, or as soon as practicable thereafter;
Ensure that the scene is not disturbed until the situation can be controlled;
Assist in the incidents investigation as required; and
Assist and co-operate in the implementation of any preventive measure(s) as deemed necessary from the investigation(s).

### Management Personnel
Report immediately (or as soon as practicable thereafter) all incidents to the HSE Department;
Ensure the incident scene is not disturbed until the incident is investigated;
Must take a key role in the accident investigation and determination of corrective and preventive measures;
Ensure that the preventive measure(s) are implemented in a timely manner;
Ensure that their staff are notified of investigation results and any preventive measures;
Review Risk Assessments and Safe Work Procedures as part of the investigation; and
Enter incident data in Synergi, including underlying root causes and follow-up actions required to close out the case.

## When There is an Incident
In the event of any incident causing personal injury requiring greater attention beyond First Aid (i.e. a Recordable Incident) or a Near Miss with the potential for such, the Area Supervisor/Manager must ensure the area is quarantined awaiting arrival of a member of the HSE Department, and where appropriate the arrival of the Authorities and emergency services (e.g. Police, Paramedics etc.).  Disturbance of the accident site is only permissible in order to prevent further harm or deterioration from unstable circumstances.
Report the incident to the HSE Manager of the Business Unit using Standard-0-REP-8014 “Incident Notification Form” within the reporting timeframe established in the “Incident Follow-up Norm”.

## Incident Follow-up Process
Incident follow-up Process is as specified in:   2.2 NO 1 Incident Follow-up Norm
