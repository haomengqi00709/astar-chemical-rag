# 0-SPC-6002 — Specification


[TABLE: 0-SPC-6002_table_1]


## Purpose
This specification describes the conditions under which the Proposer provides to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics an offer to provide the works described in the Agreement.

## Acknowledgement
Within five working days of receipt of the request, the Proposer will acknowledge receipt and state his intended response.

## Reference
The Agreement number will appear prominently on all correspondence and documentation submitted by the Proposer.

## Verification of Documents
The Proposer will verify that he has received all documents referenced in the Agreement. If not, he will promptly advise the Subcontract Administrator who will issue the missing documents.

## Queries and Clarifications
The Proposer will submit all queries or requests for clarification in writing to the Subcontract Administrator. All proposers will be provided with a verbatim copy of the query plus the response.

## Validity of Offer
The Proposer’s offer to supply the Services will be irrevocable for a period of 90 days following the submission date.

## Form of Offer

### Price List and Basis for Pricing
The Proposer will provide the following documentation, completed in accordance with the instructions given in 0-SPC-6011: Construction Subcontract Pricing: Lump Sum:
0-LST-6X11: Construction Subcontract Price List
0-LST-6X12: Construction Subcontract Labour Rates
0-LST-6X13: Construction Subcontract Crew Rates
0-LST-6X14: Construction Subcontract Indirect Labour Schedule
0-LST-6X15: Construction Subcontract Equipment Schedule

### Survey
The Proposer will complete the survey provided (0-REP-6X0X: Construction Subcontractor Survey). Aker Kvaerner Chemetics will contact the references directly.

### Additional Documentation
The Proposer will supply the following additional documentation to assist in evaluation of his offer as follows:
A List of all proposed sub-subcontractors
Site Management Organisation Chart
Resumes or C.V.’s for Key Persons named in 0-LST-6X14: Construction Subcontract Indirect Labour Schedule
Proposed percentages for overhead and profit to be used in assessing Compensation Events
The Proposer may supply other documentation he considers is required to fully describe his offer. Documentation supplied should be concise and clearly indicate its purpose. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics is not obliged to consider documentation supplied other than that listed above.

### Deviations
The Proposer’s offer to supply the works will be considered to be in full compliance with the Agreement and all referenced documents unless deviations or exceptions are explicitly stated and described.

## Evaluation and Disposition of Offers
Offers will be evaluated in the sole discretion of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics as to overall performance, delivery and value. The evaluation will consider any deviations and exceptions included in the offer in the manner described by these instructions.
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics reserves the right to accept or reject any offer in whole or in part, or to reject all offers with or without notice or reasons, or to accept an offer other than that having the lowest price, or to accept an offer not in full compliance with the Request for Tender. If no offer is acceptable, the work may be abandoned or performed in any manner selected by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics makes no representation that any Proposer is pre-qualified or is acceptable merely because they have received or responded to a Request for Tender.
If unsuccessful, the Proposer will be advised within ten working days of the award of a Subcontract for the works to another party.
Costs incurred by the Proposer, or any Supplier, Subsubcontractor, or Others, in preparing the Proposer’s offer to supply the works are the responsibility of the Proposer and will not be reimbursed by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

## Confidentiality
The Proposer will take reasonable measures to maintain the confidentiality of the Request for Tender and all referenced documents. The Proposer’s suppliers or sub-subcontractors must only be given sufficient information to provide an offer to the Proposer.
The Request for Tender and all documents provided to the Proposer by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics remain the property of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. Originals and all copies must be returned to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or destroyed on demand.
