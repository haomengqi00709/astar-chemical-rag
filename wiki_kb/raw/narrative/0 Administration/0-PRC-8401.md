# 0-PRC-8401 — Procedure


[TABLE: 0-PRC-8401_table_1]


## Purpose
This specification summarizes the responsibilities of site management personnel in Health Safety and Environment (HSE) matters on Aker Kvaerner Chemetics EPC projects.

## Health Safety and Environment Policy
Our targets are zero injuries, zero occupational illnesses, zero material loss and zero effect on the environment.
Our commitment to health safety and the environment is set out in:
Standard-0-POL-8000 Aker Kvaerner HSE Policy

## Scope
Site health and safety management is required on all Aker Kvaerner Chemetics project work sites.  The level of health and safety management required is as follows:

### Construction
When Aker Kvaerner Chemetics has direct responsibility for construction, the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Construction Services Manager will appoint a full-time Health and Safety Manager, who will be responsible for health, safety and the environment on site.

### Construction Advisement
When Aker Kvaerner Chemetics is responsible for providing only construction advisors, the health and safety management responsibilities of field advisor are as set in:
Standard-0-PRC-8400 HSE Management for Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Construction Site Advisors

### Commissioning
When Aker Kvaerner Chemetics is responsible for Commissioning, the health and safety management responsibilities of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for its employees will be fulfilled by the Commissioning Manager; however, the Director of Technology may appoint a full-time health and safety manager for the site.

## Preparation for Site Visit
Authorisation to travel will be sought as per procedure:
Standard-0-PRC-9002 Travel Authorisation
Employee will make preparations for travel in accordance with:
Standard-0-PRC-8102 Travel Health and Safety

## Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Site Construction Manager
The Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Site Construction manager has overall responsibility for all activities on the construction site, and the site Health and Safety Manager responds directly to the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Construction Services Manager.
In addition to the responsibilities of section 6 of this document, the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Site Construction Manager is responsible for ensuring that the Project Health, Safety and Environment plan is implemented in a timely manner, and that the plan is being followed by all Aker Kvaerner Chemetics subcontractors.

## Site Health and Safety Manager

### General Responsibilities
Reads, understands and carries out the general requirements of the Aker Kvaerner Health and Safety procedures, as well as the project HSE plan:
Standard-0-PRC-8000 Administration of Health, Safety and Environment
Standard-0-MAN-8400 Aker Kvaerner Health and Safety Policy
Project-0-PRC-8405 Project Health, Safety and Environmental Plan (project specific)
Ensures all site employees are familiar with the Policy and adhere to its requirements. Reviews Client HSE procedures and guidelines as well as governmental regulations with employees to help ensure understanding and compliance.
Performs safety orientations for employees and contractors arriving to work on site.
Formulates safe work procedures and methods and ensures that the procedures and methods are used by the general work force.
Ensures that all new or transferred employees are given safety orientation training before assignment to a supervisor.  Where necessary, specific job training will be carried out for personnel engaged in work involving the use or operation of such equipment as cranes, lift trucks, mobile equipment, powder-actuated tools, scaffolds, ladders, man-lifts and other high-risk equipment.
Audits Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Subcontractor health safety and environment plans and procedures, and audits the implementation of those plans and procedures on the site.
Cooperates with non-Aker Kvaerner Chemetics subcontractors to ensure common safety issues are treated appropriately.  Provides subcontractors with necessary information, which may affect them.
Sets a personal example by wearing appropriate personal protective equipment/clothing and ensuring that all necessary protective equipment and clothing is available for issue as and when required.
Reports and investigates all employee injuries, equipment/property damage and public liability incidents to their superiors immediately.  Takes appropriate corrective actions to prevent recurrence.
Carries out informal physical condition inspections during the normal work day and takes corrective action on substandard conditions and unsafe personal acts noted.  Any items noted outside of his control will be reported immediately to his supervisor.
Actively promotes Health and Safety on the work site.

### Reporting of Health and Safety Statistics
The designated Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Health and Safety Manager will collect health and safety statistics and prepare a monthly report as required by company procedure:
Standard-0-PRC-8002 HSE Status Reporting

### Reporting Incidents and Accidents
The urgency of incident notification varies depending on the severity of the incident. Incidents that resulted in an injury that can lead to a permanent disability must be to the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Vancouver office within one hour of occurrence.  The full incident reporting guidelines are set out in:
Standard-0-PRC-8013 Health and Safety Incident reporting

## Construction Site Staff Personnel
Project Managers, project advisors, project controllers, subcontract administrators and field engineers will have the following responsibilities:
Responsible for Safety and Health activities carried out within their area of control.
Reads, understands and carries out the general requirements of the Policy.
Follows planned safe work procedures and methods ensuring that any alteration to the work program does not affect safe working conditions.
Participates in the formulation of safe work procedures and methods and ensures that the Job Hazard Analysis procedure (0-PRC-8007) is applied in all areas of operations.
Gives proper instruction and adequate supervision to employees and others under their control and actively encourages them to report defects or other substandard conditions affecting site safety so that appropriate and timely corrective action can be taken.
Cooperates with other contractors and with subcontractors to ensure common safety issues are treated appropriately.  Provides subcontractors with necessary information, which may affect them.
Sets a personal example by wearing appropriate personal protective equipment/clothing and ensuring that all necessary protective equipment and clothing is available for issue as and when required.
Carries out informal physical condition inspections during the normal work day and takes corrective action on substandard conditions and unsafe personal acts noted.  Any items noted outside of his control will be reported immediately to his supervisor.
