# 0-PRC-8400 — Procedure


[TABLE: 0-PRC-8400_table_1]


## Purpose
This procedure establishes the minimum Health, Safety and Environment (HSE) requirements for Aker Kvaerner Chemetics construction site advisors at construction sites (Site).

## Applicable Sites
This procedure applies to construction sites where Aker Kvaerner Chemetics is providing construction advisory services for the erection of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics designed process plants.

## Responsible Personnel

### Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Vancouver Office
The Aker Kvaerner Chemetics safety manager will determine the personnel responsible for safety indoctrination and advice prior to travelling to site.

### Site
The dvisor is responsible for safety indoctrination at Site.

## Preparation for Site Visit
Advisors expected to be on site for a period greater than one week will complete an HSE training program approved by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.  Applicable advisors will complete training programs every two years.  Training program information can be obtained from the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics afety anager.
The advisor will obtain a copy of the following documents and be familiar with the specific site HSE issues such as first aid locations, ambulance availability, evacuation procedures, etc.
0-MAN-8400 Aker Kvaerner Construction Safety Manual
0-REP-8400 Construction Site Safety Health and Environmental Analysis
0-REP-8401 Construction Site Safety Checklist
Advisors will complete the 0-REP-8402 Personal Data Form for Construction Site Visits.  This form will provide the advisors with emergency contact HSE information as well as provide Aker Kvaerner Chemetics with emergency HSE information about the advisor.
Advisors are required to bring steel toe safety shoes, long sleeve shirts, long pants and other PPE as required.  In some cases, PPE is available at ite, however the person providing the safety indoctrination will advise if additional PPE is required to be carried with the advisor.

### Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Vancouver Office
Prior to travelling to Site, the advisors will receive an HSE indoctrination, which will include a review of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Construction Safety Handbook.  At a minimum, the review will include safe work practices and required PPE.

### Other Locations
Prior to travelling to Site, the advisors will receive by email the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Construction Safety Handbook.  They will also receive verbal or email review that will include safe work practices and required personal protective equipment.

## Site Duties

### Orientation
Each site will have its specific HSE orientation.
Prior to entering the construction areas the advisor will obtain site-specific HSE indoctrination.  The owner generally gives this indoctrination upon the advisors initial arrival at site.

### Activities
Advisors will be familiar with HSE procedures and specifications that are applicable to their activities on site.  If advisors are not familiar with applicable HSE procedures and specifications, they will obtain training as noted above.  Specifications and procedures that are typically required at Site are listed in 0-MAN-8400 Aker Kvaerner Construction Safety Manual.

### Safety Meetings
The advisor will attend the owners’ safety meetings if specifically requested by the owner.

### Reporting
As soon as practical, advisors will report any HSE incident that occurs on Site to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Vancouver afety anager and anager of onstruction and stimating.

### Job Analysis
Prior to any potentially hazardous duty by the advisor, the advisor will ensure a job analysis (JA) report has been completed and reviewed

### Transportation
If site conditions require the need for advisors to operate a motor vehicle such as automobile, truck or scooter, the advisors will be properly trained and licensed as applicable to operate the vehicle.  Advisors will wear proper PPE while operating vehicles.

## Conclusion
HSE is considered priority above all.  No job will be performed without the proper HSE plans in place.  If unsure about the status of a particular task, advisors will ask a qualified person or refuse to perform the task until uncertainty is removed.  If an advisor is witnessing an unsafe situation, they should notify the parties involved.
