## Issuing of Documentation
Documents issued to Owner or third parties will be published, logged and issued by Document Control.  Documents received from Owner or third parties will be logged and distributed by Document Control.  Documents issued for internal use do not require publishing.  Requirements are shown in:
0-PRC-0006: Control of Project Documentation
Documents provided to Owner for review or approval are listed in the Document List.  Owner is required to review or approve documents within [XX] calendar days.  Document Control will send a notification of deemed approval to Owner in XX calendar days if a document or approval notification is not received.
Document Control will issue Document Status Reports listing the Vendor documents for expediting and return.  The Project Buyer is responsible for expediting Vendor documents from Vendors.  The Project Engineer is responsible for expediting the internal review and return of Vendor documents.
