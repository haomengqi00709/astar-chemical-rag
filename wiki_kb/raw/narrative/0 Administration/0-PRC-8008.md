# 0-PRC-8008 — Procedure


[TABLE: 0-PRC-8008_table_1]


## Purpose
To provide a process by which Management personnel and employees can cooperate to fulfill their joint duties to ensure that the best Health Safety and Environmental practices are applied at the work site, and to promote and maintain the safety mindset among the workforce of the work site.

## Requirements
A joint health and safety committee will be established at any work site where 10 or more workers are regularly employed and in any workplace where a joint committee is required by law or required by the Company HSE Manager.
Where the Company has fewer than 10 employees, the recommendation is that one of the employees participates in the owner’s or prime contractor’s health and safety committee.
Notwithstanding the above, the formation of a Joint Health and Safety Committee is highly recommended at all workplaces.

## Composition of Joint Health and Safety Committee
The committee will be composed of an equal number of management employees and non-management employees.
The Company most senior Management person at the work site will attend all safety meetings.  In case he is not able to attend he will designate another attendee to act on his behalf.
The committee will have a minimum of 4 members, or greater where required by regulation.
At a construction site the committee will include from each subcontractor one management employee and one worker employed by the subcontractor.
The non-management employee representatives shall be elected by their peers in a democratic election process (where multiple nominations exist).
The committee will have 2 co-chairs on selected by the worker representatives of the committee and the other selected by the management representatives of the committee.

## Responsibilities of Joint Health and Safety Committee
A joint safety committee has the following duties and responsibilities:
Identify situations that may be unhealthy or unsafe for workers and advise on effective measures to control exposure;
Consider and expeditiously deal with complaints related to health safety and the environment;
Make recommendations to employer for improvements to health safety and environment matters at the work site;
Advice employer on programs and policies required under regulation and monitor the effectiveness of those programs;
Plans improvements to existing health and safety rules and regulations;
Assesses the implications of changes in work tasks, operations and processes;
Advise employer on proposed changes to health and safety policy;
Participate in inspections to detect unsafe conditions, unsafe work practices, hazardous materials, and environmental factors;
Monitors the effectiveness of health and safety recommendations and procedures;
Participates in the investigation of workplace accidents, and
Studies and analyses workplace accident and injury data
Perform regular workplace inspections.

## Joint Committee Procedure
The joint committee will follow the guidelines of the local Health and Safety Board as mandated by local law.
The joint committee may establish its own rules of procedure by mutual agreement.
The joint committee will meet regularly at least once per month. At construction sites the committee will meet at least every two weeks.
If a joint committee is unable to reach agreement on a matter relating to health and safety at the workplace, one of the co-chairs will report the matter to the Company HSE Manager and attempt to resolve the issue.

## Response to Joint Committee Recommendations
Company management representatives will take action to effectively deal with committee recommendations and report back to the committee on the subsequent meeting.
For any matter requiring management authority beyond the managers in the committee, the employer must respond in writing 21 days to the committee, either indicating acceptance, or the reasons for not accepting the recommendation.
In the event that the Company rejects a joint committee recommendation, the co-chair of the committee may report the issue to the health and safety regulatory body having jurisdiction of the work place.

## Joint Committee Reports
After each meeting the committee will prepare a report of the meeting and provide a copy to the Company HSE Manager, and all approved organizations having an interest in the work site health and safety program.
Meeting reports will be posted at a location (or locations) that are easily accessible by employees.
Copies of the meeting reports will be retained for the duration of the project, or for a minimum of 2 years in the case of permanent work sites, and these shall be readily accessible to all employees and others having appropriate authorization.

## Postings
At each work site where there is a functioning health and safety committee:
the Company will post a list of the joint committee members along with their contact information;
the reports of the 3 most recent committee meetings; and
copies of orders issued by local health and safety regulatory body over the last 12 months.

## Other Health and Safety Committees
This document does not preclude the formation of other HSE committees as required by Company Management, or required by local regulations.  Minutes of these committees will be posted in a publicly assessable location.
