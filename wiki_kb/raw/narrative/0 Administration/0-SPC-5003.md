## Price adjustment for inflation
[Provides for prices to be adjusted at each payment due date for the effects of rise and fall.  A table of relevant indexes for each category of Goods and Services must be included in the Purchase Order.]

### Defined Items
The Base Date Index (B) is the latest available index before the base date.
The Latest Index P is the latest available index before the date of assessment of an amount due.
The Price Adjustment Factor is the total of the products of each of the proportions stated in the Purchase Order multiplied by (L - B)/B for the index linked to it.

### Price Adjustment Factors
If an index is changed after it has been used in calculating a Price Adjustment Factor, the calculation is repeated and a correction included in the next assessment of the amount due.
The Price Adjustment Factor calculated at the Completion Date for the whole of the Goods and Services is used for calculating price adjustment after this date.

### Compensation events
The Cost of Providing the Goods and Services for compensation events is assessed using:
the costs current at the time of assessing the compensation event adjusted to base date by dividing by one plus the Price Adjustment Factor for the last assessment of the amount due; and
the costs at base date levels for amounts calculated from rates stated in the Purchase Order for employees and the Vendor’s plant and equipment.

### Price adjustment
Each amount due includes an amount for price adjustment which is the sum of:
the change in the amount due since the last assessment of the amount due multiplied by the Price Adjustment Factor for the date of the current assessment;
the amount for price adjustment included in the previous amount due; and
correcting amounts, not included elsewhere, which arise from changes to indices used for assessing previous amounts for price adjustment.

## Site Works

### Insurance and Risk
The additional insurance provided by the Vendor is stated in the Insurance Table.  The insurances are in the joint names of the Parties and provide cover for events which are at the Vendor's risk from the starting date until the Defects Certificate has been issued.
