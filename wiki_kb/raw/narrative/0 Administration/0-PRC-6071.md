# 0-PRC-6071 — Procedure


[TABLE: 0-PRC-6071_table_1]


## Purpose
This procedure describes the roles and responsibilities of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics personnel in assuring the quality of works supplied by Construction Subcontractors.

## Subcontractor Requirements
Subcontractors will be issued the following documents to set out the quality requirements for the works that they are supplying:
0-QDS-6XYZZ: [Template Quality Data Sheet]
0-SPC-6071: Construction Subcontract Quality Assurance

## Quality Data Sheets
Quality Data Sheets will be prepared for each type of equipment (tanks, pumps, etc.), instrument (pressure transmitter, control valve, etc.), electrical equipment (motor, switchgear), and for each type or class of bulk material (concrete, pipe material class PV6, etc.) used in the works of the Subcontract.

### Content
Quality Data Sheets will describe the following requirements:
Inspections and tests to be included in the Subcontractor’s Inspection and Test Plan
Inspections and tests to be witnessed points
Inspections and tests to be hold points
Inspection and test result records to be provided

### Responsibility
The Quality Data Sheets are prepared by the responsible discipline, checked by the Manager of Construction Services, and approved by the responsible Discipline Approver (0-PRC-2001: Assignment of Project Responsibilities).

### Numbering
For example 0-QDS-6XYZZ, where “X” is the equivalent digit used for the Subcontract Agreement (0-PRC-6002: Form of Subcontract Agreement), “Y” is the Major Work Division (0-PRC-0002: Document Numbering), and “ZZ” is a sequential addendum.

## Specification
The Quality Data Sheets will refer to the following specification:
0-SPC-6071: Construction Subcontract Quality Assurance
This document will be issued for the project (0-PRC-0001: Approval and Use of Standards).

## Subcontractor’s Quality Plan
The Subcontractor will prepare a Quality Plan as required by 0-SPC-6071: Construction Subcontract Quality Assurance. This document will be checked by the responsible Discipline Approvers (0-PRC-2001: Assignment of Project Responsibilities), and will be approved by the Manager of Construction Services.

## Surveillance
Surveillance will be carried out by suitably qualified Inspectors under the direction of the Site Quality Supervisor.
The scope of the surveillance will be as described in the Quality Data Sheets, by 0-SPC-6071: Construction Subcontract Quality Assurance, and by this procedure.

### Surveillance Reporting
The Quality Data Sheets are formatted to allow surveillance activities to be checked off as completed. The Inspector will do this updating directly in the native format version of the Quality Data Sheet in the project site file.
The original document numbering will be retained, but the document title (and file name) will be amended to refer to the specific item inspected, using tag numbers, line numbers, etc. wherever possible.
Surveillance reports will be prepared by publishing the updated Quality Data Sheets (0-PRC-0005: Publishing of Project Documents), marking the status as “As Built (In Progress)” with the revision numbers incremented. The final version will have the status marked as “As Built (Complete)”. The published documents will be approved by the Site Quality Supervisor.

## Defects
Defects in the works may be identified by the Subcontractor, or by the Inspector during surveillance.
A Defect is a part of the works that is not in accordance with the Works Information.

### Reporting
For each Defect, the Inspector will prepare a report in the form 0-NCR-6XXX: [Template] Construction Non-Conformance Report, containing the following information:
Description of the Defect
The Subcontractor’s proposed action to correct the Defect
The Subcontractor’s proposed action to prevent recurrence of the Defect
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics approval of proposed actions with any qualifications
Results of re-inspection after corrective action
Any part of the works that fails to meet the requirements of the inspections and tests set out in the Subcontractor’s Quality Plan will be reported as a Defect.
Any part of the works that is not yet considered complete by the Subcontractor should not be reported as a Defect.

### Responsibility
The Subcontractor’s proposed actions will be checked and approved by the same personnel as for the reference Quality Data Sheet. The checker will ensure the acceptance of other disciplines as required.
The checking and approval will be recorded by digital signatures on the published Construction Non-Conformance Report (0-PRC-0005: Publishing of Project Documents), marked as “Approved for Construction”, with the revision number incremented.

### Numbering
Construction Non-Conformance Reports will be numbered (0-PRC-0002: Document Numbering) and named identically to the reference Quality Data Sheets. Multiple Non-Conformance Reports for the same item will have an incremental number included in the title.

### Revisions
Revisions of the Construction Non-Conformance Reports will be issued, as information is developed, to advise personnel of status. Generally, the following two versions will be formally issued with status “Approved for Construction”:
To denote approval by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics of the Subcontractor’s actions
Following recording of results of re-inspection to provide a final record of disposition
