# 0-PRC-2206 — Procedure


[TABLE: 0-PRC-2206_table_1]


## Purpose
This procedure describes the steps required to issue an Engineering Subcontract.

## Technical Requirements
The technical requirements will be developed in accordance with 0-PRC-2203: Engineering Subcontract Scope Preparation. All time related obligations will be consistent with 0-DAT-3001: Project Schedule.

## Commercial Requirements
The commercial requirements will be developed in accordance with 0-PRC-2205: Commercial Terms for Engineering Subcontract.
0-SPC-2201: Standard Conditions for Engineering Subcontract is in the form of the NEC Professional Services Contract published for the Institution of Civil Engineers by Thomas Telford Publishing, London. The published document is subject to copyright. An electronic copy of the document has been created for ease of reference, however to comply with copyright requirements a copy of the document must be purchased through the library on each occasion that an Engineering Subcontract is issued. The Subcontract Administrator will advise the library when an Engineering Subcontract has been awarded, and the library will record copies purchased against project number for audit purposes.

## Issue of Request for Tender

### Activity Schedule
The Subcontract Administrator will prepare a draft version of 0-LST-22XX-1: Engineering Subcontract Activity Schedule based on 0-LST-8XXX: Engineering Scope Checklist(s). In general, a separate activity will be shown on 0-LST-22XX-1: Engineering Subcontract Activity Schedule for each item checked as being the responsibility of Subcontractor on 0-LST-8XXX: Engineering Scope Checklist(s).
The activity schedule will be numbered (0-PRC-0002: Document Numbering) with an identification number identical to that used for the Engineering Subcontract Agreement (0-PRC-2202: Form of Engineering Subcontract).
The activity schedule will be published (0-PRC-0005: Publishing of Project Documents) with status “Issued for Quotation” and will be checked by the Discipline Lead(s) and approved by the Project Engineer.

### Staff Rate List
The Subcontract Administrator will prepare a blank version of 0-LST-22XX-2: Engineering Subcontractor Staff Rates.
The rate list will be numbered (0-PRC-0002: Document Numbering) with an identification number identical to that used for the Engineering Subcontract Agreement (0-PRC-2202: Form of Engineering Subcontract).
The rate list will be published (0-PRC-0005: Publishing of Project Documents) with status “Issued for Quotation”. No checking or approval is required.

### Agreement
The Subcontract Administrator will prepare the Engineering Subcontract Agreement with the assistance of the Discipline Lead in accordance with 0-PRC-2202: Form of Engineering Subcontract.
The agreement will be published (0-PRC-0005: Publishing of Project Documents) with status “Issued for Quotation” and will be checked by the Project Engineer and by Legal and approved by the Project Manager.

### Instructions to Tenderers
0-SPC-2203: Instructions to Engineering Subcontractors for Tender exists as a standard that will be used as-is, or will be modified for the project as necessary (0-PRC-0001: Approval and Use of Standards).

### Letter of Invitation
Engineering Subcontractors who will be invited to tender for the work will be identified by the project team (0-PRC-2003: Project Execution Plan).
The Subcontract Administrator will prepare covering letters to each of the Engineering Subcontractors, referencing 0-SPC-2203: Instructions to Engineering Subcontractors for Tender and 0-REP-8XXX: [Template Engineering Subcontractor Survey].
The Subcontract Administrator will forward the letters of invitation to Document Control for issue to the Engineering Subcontractors (0-PRC-0006: Control of Project Documentation).

### Document Package
The Subcontract Administrator and the Discipline Lead(s) will forward a published copy of all required documents to Document Control for issue to the Engineering Subcontractors (0-PRC-0006: Control of Project Documentation).

## Issue of Engineering Subcontract

### Activity Schedule
The Subcontract Administrator will revise 0-LST-22XX-1: Engineering Subcontract Activity Schedule based on the tender of the successful Engineering Subcontractor.
The activity schedule will be published (0-PRC-0005: Publishing of Project Documents) with status “Approved for Design” and will be checked by the Discipline Lead(s) and approved by the Project Engineer.

### Staff Rate List
The Subcontract Administrator will revise 0-LST-22XX-2: Engineering Subcontractor Staff Rates based on the tender of the successful Engineering Subcontractor.
The rate list will be published (0-PRC-0005: Publishing of Project Documents) with status “Approved for Design” and will be checked by the Discipline Lead(s) and approved by the Project Engineer.

### Agreement
The Subcontract Administrator will revise the Engineering Subcontract Agreement as needed to ensure that all references to other documents are correct as to revision number, and to comply with any modifications agreed as part of the negotiation with the successful Engineering Subcontractor.
The agreement will be published (0-PRC-0005: Publishing of Project Documents) with status “Approved for Design” and will be checked by the Project Engineer and by Legal, and approved by the Project Manager.
The Subcontract Administrator will print a paper copy and arrange for it to be signed by the person authorised under Section 6, presenting it together with a copy of the approved Engineering Subcontractor Evaluation Summary Record (0-PRC-2204: Engineering Subcontractor Evaluation and Approval).
The Subcontract Administrator will forward the signed and authorised paper copy of the Engineering Subcontract to Document Control for issue to the Engineering Subcontractor (0-PRC-0006: Control of Project Documentation).

### Document Package
The Subcontract Administrator and the Discipline Lead(s) will forward copies of all required documents to Document Control for issue to the Engineering Subcontractor (0-PRC-0006: Control of Project Documentation).
