# 0-PRC-8501 — Procedure


[TABLE: 0-PRC-8501_table_1]


## Purpose
The purpose of this procedure is to ensure that any work on a plant under commissioning or operation is properly authorized and that all safety precautions have been taken prior to initiating the work.
This regulation will be followed by the Commissioning Team (Aker Kvaerner Chemetics personnel, Contractors and Vendors rep) after a circuit, any system or equipment is handed over mechanically completed for commissioning by the Construction Manager to the Commissioning Manager.
When available, a Client’s regulation may be followed if the requirements and guidelines spelled out on Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ procedures are fully covered by the Client’s regulation.
Note: This procedure is based on Aker Kvaerner’s Work Permits procedure issued in January of 2003 and found in the AK’s Safety Policy Manual section 7, item 7.02.

## Scope
Safe work permits are to be issued by the Commissioning HSE Manager to control the work in areas under commissioning or operation and ensure that operator/maintenance/construction personnel are aware of safe work practices.  Safe work permits are generally mandatory for the following operations:
Line breaking
Pump repairs
Making tie-ins to existing equipment
Working in hazardous area
Work on equipment used for corrosives
Hydro-blasting
Flame or electric welding
Electric power tools or motor
Open flame devices
Excavation
Tank entry and/or lockout
Tank entry into a hazardous area

## Responsibilities

### Commissioning HSE Manager
The Commissioning HSE Manager, who is will be appointed for each Site, issues work permits.
The Commissioning HSE Manager shall ensure that the Commissioning Team (Aker Kvaerner Chemetics personnel, Contractors and Vendors rep) knows and understands the required safety procedures applicable to the work that is going to be carried out on the areas under commissioning or operation.
The Commissioning HSE Manager ensures that the Commissioning Team knows and understands the location and the type of work being carried out on the areas under commissioning or operation.
The Commissioning HSE Manager shall ensure that the Commissioning Team is familiar with the requirements for obtaining work permits.
In case the Commissioning HSE Manager is temporarily away from site (i.e., R&R, site leave, sick leave, etc.),  the Commissioning Manager will take over the administration of all safety related activities.

### Commissioning Team and Work Supervisor
The Commissioning Team (Aker Kvaerner Chemetics personnel, Contractors and Vendors rep) shall become thoroughly familiar with the Clients regulations for obtaining work permits, if available.  A Client’s regulation may be followed if the requirements and guidelines spelled out in Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ work permit procedures are fully covered by the Client’s regulations.
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics work permit procedures will be used when clients have inadequate permit programs.
The Commissioning Team shall coordinate all work with the Commissioning HSE Manager on site ensuring that:
A work permit is issued.
The hazards and limitations at the site are identified.
A joint inspection of the work area is conducted and positive definition of the work to be accomplished is obtained.
All precautions are taken and safe practices are followed.
All precautions are verified by a competent Work Supervisor before commencement of work.
It is the responsibility of all personnel entering operating areas to obtain the proper Client authorization and appropriate work permits.
Upon issuance of the proper work permit, the Work Supervisor shall sign the work permit verifying he has reviewed the permit and understands the limitations.
Upon receiving the permit, it is the responsibility of the Work Supervisor to review the permit with his employees / crew explaining the precautions and limitations of the proposed task.  The employees shall also be notified that if any changes in conditions of the permit take place, the work shall stop and the Client notified.
The Work Supervisor shall ensure that the employees understand terms that may be used which include inert, hole watch, toxic, etc.
