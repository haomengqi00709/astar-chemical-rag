# 0-SPC-5004 — Specification


[TABLE: 0-SPC-5004_table_1]


## Purpose
This Specification defines the minimum requirements for the pre-shipment preparation and packaging of goods for shipment .

## Scope
This specification describes general requirements; specific packaging requirements may be called up on the Purchase Order.

## Responsibilities of Vendor
The Vendor will ensure that all goods are adequately packaged to provide protection against physical damage, temperature extremes, corrosion, pilferage, condensation, and rain.
All packaging materials become the property of  and are non-returnable.
Packing must be adequate for extended storage.  The Vendor will advise  of any particular storage or handling requirements at the time of order placement.
The Vendor will comply with all regulations governing the transportation of dangerous goods. The Vendor will advise  of dangerous goods at the time of order placement.

## Pre-Shipment Preparation
The Vendor will drain, dry, and clean the goods prior to packaging.
Flange faces will be covered with an adequate shipping gasket and a 12mm wood or 5mm steel cover and secured with bolting.  Pipe or nozzle openings prepared for field welding will have protective covers secured by adhesive tape. All other openings will be plugged, capped or otherwise closed.
The vendor will provide blocking, bracing or other support necessary to prevent damage caused by shock loading or vibration.
In general, goods will be packaged and shipped fully assembled ready for the intended use. The Vendor may disassemble the goods as necessary to prevent damage during shipment, or to comply with allowable shipping dimensions or weights.

## Over-Dimensional Loads
Four weeks in advance of the scheduled shipment date the Vendor will supply transport information for any goods exceeding the capacity of a standard forty-foot ocean container; approximately 1200 cm in length, 230 cm in width and 230 cm in height. This may include but is not limited to detailed transport drawings, lifting and handling instructions and estimated transportation weights and dimensions.

## Packaging
The type of packaging will be one of the methods described in the following sections, as called up on the Purchase Order.
The Vendor will package the goods in a manner consistent with the size, weight and characteristics of the goods, to allow an even distribution of weight, and to allow the packages to be handled using cranes, forklift trucks or bogies/dollies.
Wooden packaging will be made of hardwood or conifer timbers of good quality, without cracks, loose knots or other defects. Plywood is acceptable, but no other manufactured sheeting is permitted. Conifer timbers must be free of bark, grub holes (holes larger than 3mm across), visible signs of living pests, and have a moisture content of less than 20 percent.
Use of hay, straw, flax combining, palm packing fibre, brown coconut fibre, used hessian bags or hessian linings in packing is not permitted.

### Wooden Crating
The goods will be fully enclosed by wooden crating fastened securely using spiral type galvanised nails.
The goods will be strapped to base pallets using steel bands applied with stretching tool and secured with crimped steel seals.
Items within a single crate will be partitioned, braced, blocked or secured to the base to prevent harmful contact with each other.

### Palletised
The goods will be secured to racks, cradles, or saddles and protected by draping or wrapping with a water-resistant medium.
The Vendor will design and supply all items required for shipment and handling, such as supports, special bracing, and spreader bars. The Vendor will provide drawings showing the details of all such items for the approval of  before proceeding with their manufacture. Shipping saddles will be designed to withstand all imposed loads and to prevent damage or permanent distortion to the goods. The minimum contact arc length will be 120°.
Tensioned steel bands, straps, wire rope or other suitable means will be used to keep the goods securely attached to the supports during handling and transporting. Protection will be provided at band locations to prevent damage to painted items.

## Shipping Marks
Packages will be clearly identified by painting or dye stencilling on the top and two sides of the exterior. Lettering will be black, at least 8 centimetres high. When palletised packaging is used the marks will be applied to a securely mounted plywood placard. For example:
For  Purchase Order Number 96W11100-4006 delivered in a total of six packages, each will be identified as: 96W11100-4006-1/6, 96W11100-4006-2/6, etc.

## Packing Lists
The Vendor will provide a separate packing list for each package. The Vendor will follow the line item numbering and equipment descriptions shown on the  purchase order. In cases where the  purchase order is less detailed than the Vendors itemised list, the Vendor’s more detailed list will be used.
General line items descriptions such as “one lot assembly nuts and bolts” are not acceptable. The packing list will detail the number and unit type of each item.
The Vendor will attach the packing slip to the outside of each package and place a duplicate packing slip inside the package. The packing slip will be in a waterproof package clearly marked "Packing Slip".
On large packages, the packing slip will be located in a position that will permit easy access by a person standing at ground level.

## Shipping Notification
Vendors will fax or email a shipping notice twenty-four hours in advance of readiness for shipment. The shipping notice will contain the following information and attachments:
Address of factory, contact name, phone, fax and email address.
Number of packages in shipment
Weights and dimensions (kg &cm) of each package
A brief description of packing (i.e. pallets, box, pails etc.)
A brief description of any special handling instructions
Material safety sheets for hazardous goods
The packing list for each package
