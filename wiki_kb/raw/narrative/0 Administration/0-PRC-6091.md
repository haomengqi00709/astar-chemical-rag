# 0-PRC-6091 — Procedure


[TABLE: 0-PRC-6091_table_1]


## Purpose
This procedure sets out how to prepare the documents that provide the primary description of the works as referenced by the Construction Subcontract Agreement.

## [Major Work Division] Specifications
Section 2.1 of the Construction Subcontract Agreement (0-PRC-6002: Form of Construction Subcontract) refers directly to the following specifications that will be prepared as required for each Subcontract:
0-SPC-6X92: Civil Construction and Site Preparation
0-SPC-6X93: Structural and Buildings Construction
0-SPC-6X94: Equipment Erection
0-SPC-6X95: Piping Erection
0-SPC-6X96: Insulation Erection
0-SPC-6X97: Coatings Erection
0-SPC-6X98: Instrumentation and Control Erection
0-SPC-6X99: Electrical Erection

### Responsibility
These specifications will be prepared (one for each Major Work Division) by the responsible Discipline using 0-SPC-6X9X: [Template] [Major Work Division] Construction.
The specifications will be numbered (0-PRC-0002: Document Numbering) with “X” replaced by the same digit as for the Subcontract Agreement (0-PRC-6002: Form of Construction Subcontract).
The specifications will be published with status “Approved for Construction” and will be checked by the Manager of Construction and approved by the Project Engineer.

### Specific Requirements
Section 6 of each [Major Work Division] Specification is entitled “Specific Requirements” and is intended to provide additional description of the work over and above that provided on the referenced drawings, specifications, and other documents such that a generally experienced Subcontractor can quantify the works. Topics typically covered in these sections are:
Assembly of items disassembled for shipping
Installation of equipment internals or components supplied separately
Any requirements or procedures not described in a specification

## Scope Checklists

### Supply and Erection
Each 0-SPC-6X9X: [Major Work Division] Construction directly references the following scope checklist that will itemise which Party will supply the Equipment and Materials and what erection / construction work will be by the Subcontractor and what will be by others.
0-LST-6X90-1: Construction Subcontract Supply and Erection Scope
This checklist will be prepared by Construction using 0-LST-2002: Supply and Erection Scope Checklist as a basis.
The checklist will be numbered (0-PRC-0002: Document Numbering) with “X” replaced by the same digit as for the Subcontract Agreement (0-PRC-6002: Form of Construction Subcontract).
The checklist will be published (0-PRC-0005: Publishing of Project Documents) with status “Approved for Construction” and will be checked by the responsible Disciplines and approved by the Project Engineer.

### Field Services
Section 2.6 of the Construction Subcontract Agreement (0-PRC-6002: Form of Construction Subcontract) directly references the following scope checklist that will itemise the field services (water, power, offices, etc.) supplied to the Subcontractor by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or by Owner, and any charges for consumption:
0-LST-6X90-2: Construction Subcontract Field Services Scope
This checklist will be prepared by Construction using 0-LST-2003: Field Services Scope Checklist as a basis.
The checklist will be numbered (0-PRC-0002: Document Numbering) with “X” replaced by the same digit as for the Subcontract Agreement (0-PRC-6002: Form of Construction Subcontract).
The checklist will be published (0-PRC-0005: Publishing of Project Documents) with status “Approved for Construction” and will be checked by the Project Engineer and approved by the Manager of Construction.

## Facilities Layout
Sections 2.5 and 2.6 of the Construction Subcontract Agreement (0-PRC-6002: Form of Construction Subcontract) refer directly to the following drawing that will show the location of connection points for the field services (water, power, offices, etc.) supplied to the Subcontractor, and where the Subcontractor may locate his temporary offices, lunchrooms, employee parking, etc.
0-GAD-6X01: Construction Facilities Layout
This drawing will be numbered (0-PRC-0002: Document Numbering) with “X” replaced by the same digit as for the Subcontract Agreement (0-PRC-6002: Form of Construction Subcontract).
The drawing will be prepared by Design, will be published (0-PRC-0005: Publishing of Project Documents) with status “Approved for Construction” and will be checked by the Manager of Construction and approved by the Project Engineer.

## Applicable Documents
All drawings, specifications and other documents applicable to the subcontract are listed on an accompanying transmittal and referenced by Section 2.2 of the Construction Subcontract Agreement (0-PRC-6002: Form of Construction Subcontract).
If any document contains an interface between work done by the Subcontractor and work done by others, the document should clearly show the location of the interface, indicating which portion will be done by the Subcontractor.
Reference to these documents by 0-SPC-6X9X: [Major Work Division] Construction is not necessary unless it accompanies some further description of the works to improve clarity.
