# 0-PRC-5002 — Procedure


[TABLE: 0-PRC-5002_table_1]


## Purpose
This procedure describes the steps required to issue a Requisition to initiate the issue of a Request for Quotation or a Purchase Order.

## Form of Requisition
The Requisition will be in the form of 0-REQ-: [Template Requisition]. The requisitioner will indicate on the form whether the Requisition is for the issue of a Request for Quotation or a Purchase Order.
The content of the Requisition is as follows:
Line Items 1.x (Scope of Supply)
Line Items 2.x (Documentation)
Text Section 1: Scope of Supply
Text Section 2: Documentation
It is intended that this content will be duplicated in the equivalent sections of the Request for Quotation or Purchase Order. Refer to the sample Purchase Order text in 0-PRC-5001: Form of Purchase Order.

## Numbering
The identification number of a Requisition will be identical to that assigned on the Procurement Plan (0-PRC-3001: Project Planning) for the subsequent Purchase Order.
Requisitions for the issue of a Request for Quotation and subsequent Requisitions for the issue of a Purchase Order will have the same document number with the exception of the revision numbers as follows:
the Requisition for issue of a Request for Quotation will have letter revisions (RA, RB….)
the Requisition for issue of a Purchase Order will have number revisions (R1, R2….)

## Referenced Documents
All references to documents in a Requisition must show the correct revision number.
Documents referenced by a Requisition for issue of a Request for Quotation must be published (0-PRC-0005: Publishing of Project Documents). If available with status “Approved for Fabrication” then that version will be referenced. If not, then the requisitioner must have the documents published with status “Issued for Quotation”, and those versions will be referenced.
Prior to preparation of a Requisition for issue of a Purchase Order, referenced documents will be modified as necessary to reflect any changes agreed as part of the bid evaluation process.
Documents referenced by a Requisition for issue of a Purchase Order must have status “Approved for Fabrication” prior to issue of the Requisition.
Following issue of a Requisition for issue of a Purchase Order, any revisions to documents referenced by it will require the issue of a revision to the Requisition containing the updated references.

## Restructuring
Evaluation of bids (0-PRC-5003: Bid Evaluation and Approval) may require that  be ordered in different groupings than those used in the Requisition for issue of a Request for Quotation. Restructured Requisitions for issue of a Purchase Order will be prepared accordingly.

## Publishing, Approval and Issue
The requisitioner will publish the requisition (0-PRC-0005: Publishing of Project Documents), and the responsible Discipline Approver (0-PRC-2001: Assignment of Project Responsibilities) will approve it.
The Discipline Lead will advise Procurement that the Requisition has been published and approved, and Procurement will then proceed with issuing the Request for Quotation or the Purchase Order (0-PRC-5005: Issue of Request for Quotation or Purchase Order).
