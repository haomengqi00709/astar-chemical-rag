# 0-PRC-3002 — Procedure


[TABLE: 0-PRC-3002_table_1]


## Purpose
This procedure describes the methods by which projects are scheduled to set out the sequence of work, and to provide the means for measuring progress and forecasting outcome.

## Application
A schedule will be prepared and updated for every project in the form of a bar chart.
For projects larger than CAD 3,000,000 or 3,500 home-office hours, the schedule will be developed to the levels described in this procedure.
Projects below this threshold will have schedules developed at levels determined by the Project Manager to be fit for the purpose, as approved by the Director of Projects.

## Nomenclature
The level of detail provided in a schedule is denoted by the following nomenclature:

### Level 1 Schedule
Also known as a Summary Schedule, one activity bar is shown for each major work division for each of engineering, procurement, and construction, and a single activity bar for commissioning. Major project milestones are shown.
The activities in a Level 1 Schedule are derived as summaries of the corresponding activities in a Level 2 Schedule.

### Level 2 Schedule
Also known as a Control Schedule, grouped work activities are shown for items (equipment, pipes, instruments, etc.) grouped by type or by functional area, typically giving up to 20 activities for each major work division. All project milestones are shown.
The activities in a Level 2 Schedule are derived as summaries of the corresponding activities in a Level 3 Schedule.

### Level 3 Schedule
Also known as a Detailed Schedule, all work activities are shown for items (equipment, pipes, instruments, etc.) grouped by type or by functional area. All project milestones are shown.

## Project Schedule
The Project Schedule will be structured in four sub-schedules as follows:
Engineering
Procurement
Construction
Commissioning
The activities within and the sub-schedules will be linked by logical dependencies to establish critical paths and to enable the accurate forecasting of the effects of delays or changes.

### Engineering
The engineering schedule will show all work activities executed by home-office personnel.

### Procurement
The procurement schedule will show all work activities executed by Vendors and shippers.
Procurement will be scheduled at Level 2. Work by Vendors will be grouped by Purchase Order, with activities grouped to show Design – Fabrication – Testing as applicable. Shipping will be grouped by Purchase Order or by consolidated shipment as applicable.
When requested to provide schedules, Vendors will prepare them at Level 3. Vendor schedule updates will be summarised as % completion on the Level 2 activities.

### Construction
The construction schedule will show all work activities executed by construction Subcontractors.
Construction will be scheduled at Level 2. Work will be grouped by physical area and by equipment or material class or type. Major equipment items will be shown as individual activities. Activities will have trade responsibility and direct labour hours entered to allow the preparation of resource loading histograms.
Activities will be assigned to Subcontracts as set out in 0-PRC-2003: Project Execution Plan.
Subcontractors will be required to schedule at Level 3, with Level 4 Work Plans developed in the form of checklists. Subcontractor’s schedule updates will be summarised as % completion on the Level 2 activities.

### Commissioning
Initial scheduling of commissioning will be at Level 2, to establish the required overall duration.
Final scheduling of commissioning will be at Level 3, with Level 4 Work Plans developed in the form of checklists.

## Initial Preparation
Project Controls will prepare the initial Project Schedule using information generated as part of the planning of the project (0-PRC-3001: Project Planning), and in consultation with the project team.
Checking of the initial Project Schedule will be a group activity lead by the Project Manager, involving all project team members listed on 0-LST-2004: Project Responsibility, with department heads sitting in where assignments have not yet been made. Following this review, the schedule will be published as the base-line for the project. The Project Manager will sign the base-line schedule as the checker, and the Director of Projects will approve it.

## Updating
The Project Schedule will be updated at regular intervals as set out in 0-PRC-2002: Project Instructions. Updating requires the following inputs:
Add or delete activities and dependencies to reflect revised execution
Actual activity start and completion dates
Revised resource hours based on latest count of work quantities
Engineering activity % completion derived from the Project Resource Management System
Procurement activity % completion derived from Vendor schedules or expediting reports
Construction activity % completion derived from Subcontractor schedules
Commissioning activity % completion derived from the field checklists

## Reporting
Standard schedule management reports are as follows. These are generally prepared immediately following each schedule update for inclusion into the Project Managers reports in accordance with 0-PRC-2002: Project Instructions.

### Engineering Task Lists
These are prepared for each department, listing engineering activities required to be completed over the subsequent reporting period showing early completion dates, and listing preceding and succeeding activities. These provide a convenient guide to the Project Engineer to manage the engineering work of the project.

### Level 2 Schedule
This is the format to be used for reporting to Owner and to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics senior management.

### Commissioning Task Lists
These list commissioning activities required to be completed over the subsequent reporting period showing early completion dates, and listing preceding and succeeding activities. These provide a convenient guide to the Commissioning Manager.
