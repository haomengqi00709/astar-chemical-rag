# 0-SPC-7003 — Specification


[TABLE: 0-SPC-7003_table_1]


## Purpose
The purpose of this  is to ensure that Agencies supplying Third Party Inspection Services to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics submit/complete the required documentation.

## Scope
The scope of this  describes the documents Agencies supplying Third Party Inspection will submit and/or complete.

## Reference
The following reference documents form part of this :
Quality Data Sheets (example)
Nonconformance Report (example)
Inspection Release (example)
Wire Payment Authorization Form (example)

## Inspection
After completion of any inspection activities, the Inspection Agency will:
issue an Inspection Report after each inspection visit that details the Inspector’s assessment of current delivery and inspection findings.
maintain/complete all Quality Data Sheets (Figure 1 & 2),
issue Non-Conformance Reports (Figure 3) as required
issue Inspection Releases (Figure 4) upon satisfactory completion of an inspection.
Note, any text shown in square brackets on Aker Kvaerner Chemetics forms is instructional and is intended to be deleted when using the form.
Inspection Reports, Nonconformance Reports and Shop Survey reports will be issued within two days of the activity. Inspection Reports will be on Inspection Agency letterhead, and sent to ChemeticsQA@akerkvaerner.com

### Inspection Report
A formal inspection report in the English language will be issued using the Agency’s normal style or format, and sent by e-mail to ChemeticsQA@akerkvaerner.com Accepted formats are:
Micosoft Word (.doc)
Microsoft Excel (.xls)
Adobe Acrobat (.pdf)
When digital photographs form part of the report, accepted formats will be: “.jpg”, “.gif” or “.tif”.
Inspection Reports must reference the following:
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics PO Number on the Agency.
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics PO Number on the Equipment Vendor.
Name of the contact at Equipment Vendor.
Date of visit.
Equipment Description(s).
Equipment Tag Number(s).
Inspection Reports must include the following:
The Inspector’s assessment of fabrication status, and whether this is in agreement with the current schedule.
Details of any delays, including those claimed by vendor to be due to slow approvals by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

### Quality Data Sheet (QDS)
All activities shown on the QDS will be identified as Witness, Hold, Review, Verify or Monitor as appropriate.
Unless otherwise advised:
Activities shown as Witness points may, by agreement between Vendor and Agency, be exempted from the normal five working days advance notification.
Where multiple instances of the same activity are being carried out at the same vendor, Witness requirements may be waived by the Agency in favour of Monitoring.
Hold points may not be waived without written approval from Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
When an activity has been completed the Agency inspector will initial and date the appropriate columns in the QDS. When inspection of the item(s) listed in a QDS is finished the completed QDS will be retained for submittal with the associated Inspection Release and any Nonconformance Report. See examples of “As Issued” (figure 1) and “Final” (figure 2) revisions of a QDS.

### Nonconformance Report (NCR)
The NCR number is derived from the QDS number. When equipment fails to comply with the requirements of the Purchase Order, and cannot be made to comply by further work, then an NCR will be issued. If the nonconformance is first identified by the Equipment Vendor then the Vendor’s own system and documentation will be used, with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics included in the review and approval process. If the nonconformance is first identified by the Agency inspector then the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics form is used. This will be completed by the Agency inspector and forwarded to ChemeticsQA@akerkvaerner.com together with any Vendor proposal(s) to correct the nonconformance. ### Inspection Release (IR)
The IR number is derived from the equipment Purchase Order number. When more than one IR is required the IR’s will be numbered sequentially.
Any equipment that requires work to be completed where another inspection visit has to be completed, or an outstanding Nonconformance Report exists, an IR will not be issued. When the work to be completed does not require another inspection visit, an IR will be issued by the Inspector, containing details of the work to be completed. The Vendor will be instructed to sign and date the IR as “All noted work completed” and forward it to ChemeticsQA@akerkvaerner.com
When the work to be completed does not require another inspection visit, the Vendor will:
Complete the work described on the applicable Inspection Release
Sign and date sections B and C
Forward the original or a copy to the Inspector and Aker Kvaerner Chemetics
While it is intended that the Vendor will have the Quality Documentation Manual completed at the time of final inspection, it is permissible for the Inspector to issue a suitably endorsed IR where the Manual is not in its final configuration, but its contents are complete and acceptable to the Inspector. ## Invoicing
Where funds other than CDN$ are the form of payment the Agency must send a completed and signed Wire Payment Authorization Form (Figure 5) to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics to enable payment(s) to be made. The e-mail address for all accounting communication is Accounts.Payable@akerkvaerner.com
All invoices must be numbered, dated, and reference the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Purchase Order (PO) Number on the Agency. Additionally, if more than one Vendor PO is covered by the invoice then costs must be allocated accordingly.
When work on an Agency PO is complete the invoice will state that it is the “Final Invoice for Purchase Order Number 0XXXXXXX-07XX”.
When work on an Agency PO extends over a period exceeding two months, invoicing will be submitted monthly.
The price of services shall not exceed the price stated on Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ PO without prior written approval. This approval will be documented on a Change Order.
Failure to comply with the above will result in a delay in payment until the missing information is supplied.
