## Purpose
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics supports an appropriate balance between work and life outside work, and allows a flexible work schedule as described in this policy.

## Normal Hours of Work and Schedule
Normal hours of work are 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. with a 45 minute unpaid lunch break (8.25 hours) Monday through Thursday, and 8:00 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. (4.5 hours) Friday, totalling 37.5 hours per week.
The total workweek is reduced by 8.25 hours for every local Canadian statutory holiday within that week.

## Flexible Hours of Work and Schedule
Employees are permitted to establish a flexible work schedule whereby they arrive and leave earlier or later than the normal work schedule providing they work 75 hours per two-week period, minus 8.25 hours for every local Canadian statutory holiday within that period.
Guidelines for the flexible work schedule are:
it will allow the employee to fulfill all operational requirements of their position;
it will include the core hours of 9:30am to 3:30pm, Monday through Thursday;
it will include a minimum 30 minute unpaid lunch break; and
it will include no more than 10 hours worked on any day.

## Approval of Flexible Work Schedules
Employees may request a flexible work schedule by e-mail addressed to their supervisor.  In considering operational requirements, the supervisor must ensure that both departmental and project needs are considered.  Providing the flexible work schedule meets the guidelines in Section 3, the supervisor will approve the request by return e-mail copying both Finance and Human Resources.

## Site Hours of Work
Due to the nature of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ business, employees required to work on site will be subject to distinct regular hours of work during site assignments.
Regular site hours will be set on a case-by-case basis, and may be up to 60 hours per week with a minimum one day of rest per week.  The regular site hours are a target and staff may be required to work additional hours within the following parameters:
Maximum 75 hours in any one week period
Maximum 16 hours in any one day, but never on consecutive days
Minimum 8 hours rest period between work periods
Minimum 1 rest day in any two-week period
All time worked in excess of 37.5 hours per week will be paid in accordance with 0-POL-9113-R2 Compensation of Overtime.
Department Managers, Project Managers, Construction Managers and Commissioning Managers are required to manage in accordance with this policy and ensure that appropriate staff levels are maintained on site.
