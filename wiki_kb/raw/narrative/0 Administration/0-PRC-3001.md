# 0-PRC-3001 — Procedure


[TABLE: 0-PRC-3001_table_1]


## Purpose
This procedure describes the activities required for effective planning of project execution, and communication of the plans to project team members and to the supporting organisation. The activities will be carried out in accordance with the appended schedule.

## Initiation and Responsibility
Upon issue of an approved project authorisation (0-PRC-1004: Initiation of Project Execution), the Director of Projects will assign the Project Manager.
The Project Manager will be responsible for all planning activities, delegating to the Project Engineer and to support groups such as Project Controls as required.

## Contract Hand-Over
The Proposal Manager and the Sales Manager (or Director) are responsible for completion of the following documents to describe the as-sold project:
Contract	Sales Manager
Scope Checklists	Proposal Manager
Battery Limits Conditions	Proposal Manager
Estimate	Proposal Manager
Proposal Risk Analysis	Sales Manager
Immediately on initiation of the project as described in Section 2, the Proposal Manager and the Sales Manager (or Director) will meet with the Project Manager to provide a briefing of the requirements of the project and to hand over the above documents. If the documents are not yet updated to as-sold status, preliminary versions will be provided and a schedule for completion will be agreed.
The Project Manager will prepare minutes of the contract hand-over briefing, which will be checked by the other attendees to ensure accuracy.

## Management Briefing
The Project Manager will prepare a summary of project requirements and will convene a meeting to brief the operations department heads on them, and on the scope of work to be delivered by each department. The Project Manager will advise the department heads of the immediate need for personnel to be assigned to the project.

## Project Team Assignment
See 0-PRC-2001: Assignment of Project Responsibilities.

## Internal Kick-Off Meeting
The Project Manager will issue preliminary scope and schedule information to assigned Discipline Leads, together with copies of relevant parts of the Contract.
The Project Manager will arrange a meeting of key assigned project team members, proposal, and marketing personnel to facilitate a complete and orderly hand-over of all project requirements from proposal to project. Planning priorities and short-term work priorities will be agreed with project team members.

## Project Execution Plan
Using the template on Q Drive, the Project Manager will prepare the:
0-PRC-2003: Project Execution Plan
The plan will be checked by the Manager of Project Controls and approved by the Director of Projects.

## Scope Description
The Project Execution Plan refers to the following documents to define the scope of supply by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. They are generally based on the similar documents provided by the proposal team, but will be verified for accuracy, will contain additional information as described, and will be issued with status ”Approved for Design”.
1-LST-0001: Battery Limits Conditions
Prepared by Process, checked by Project Engineer, approved by Director of Technology.
0-LST-2001: Engineering Scope Checklist
Prepared by Project Engineer, checked by Project Manager, approved by Director of Projects.
Includes the following additional information:
The requirement for document review and approval by Owner
The split in scope between Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and engineering contractor(s)
Any contractually required document submission dates
The requirement for document review and approval by a locally registered engineer
0-LST-2002: Supply and Erection Scope Checklist
Prepared by Project Engineer, checked by Project Manager, approved by Director of Projects.
Includes the following additional information:
Delineation of supply by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or by Subcontractors
The work distribution amongst Subcontracts
0-LST-2003: Field Services Scope Checklist
Prepared by Project Engineer, checked by Project Manager, approved by Director of Projects.
0-LST-2006: Project Risk Analysis
Prepared by Project Manager, checked by Director of Projects, approved by President.
The basis is the original analysis of contract risks and mitigating strategies considered in the preparation of the estimates and proposals. The final contractual requirements are appended together with any modifications or additions to the mitigating strategies deemed to be required.

## Project Administration
To provide assistance to the Project Manager in administering the Contract, Legal will prepare:
0-LST-2005: Key Contract Provisions
The list will be will be checked by the Sales Manager and approved by Legal.
The Project Manager will prepare the following procedures covering internal administrative details and Owner communications:
0-PRC-2002: Project Instructions
0-PRC-2004: Project Co-ordination
The procedures will be checked by the Manager of Project Controls and approved by the Director of Projects.

## Quality Plan
The Project Manager will evaluate the quality requirement of the project and will propose modifications to the standard:
0-PRC-7003: Quality Plan
The Director of Engineering, the Director of Projects, the Director of Technology, and the President will approve the Quality Plan.

## Document List
The Project Engineer will forward to Document Control all Owner design documents referenced by the Contract, and they will publish, log, and file them (0-PRC-0006: Control of Project Documents).
If dual Owner and Aker Kvaerner Chemetics document numbers are required, the Project Engineer will modify and issue for the project:
0-PRC-0002: Document Numbering
The Discipline Leads and the Project Engineer will develop an initial listing of all project documents, 0-LST-0001: Document List, based on 0-LST-2001: Engineering Scope Checklist.

## Procurement Plan
The Project Manager will develop 0-LST-5001: [Template] Procurement Plan, a preliminary listing of all Purchase Orders to be placed, stating the scope of each by reference to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics standard equipment identification numbers, bulk material types or classes, instrument types, etc.
Potential bidders will include, but not be limited to, any nominated suppliers in the Contract, and the suppliers used in the estimate.
Procurement will enter this preliminary information into:
0-DAT-5001: Purchasing and Logistics Database

## Logistics Plan
For the potential bidders listed in the Procurement Plan, shipping routes should be established with the following noted:
Vendor packaging requirements
Exporting country shipping method
Port of export
Consolidation / repackaging requirements
Port of import
Carrier type
Importing country shipping method

## Project Schedule
Project Controls will schedule the project in accordance with 0-PRC-3002: Project Scheduling to create:
0-DAT-3001: Project Schedule
The initial schedule will be checked by the Project Manager and approved by the Director of Projects.

## Project Budget
Immediately on initiation of the project as described in Section 2, Project Accounting will open all engineering cost codes. Project Accounting will prepare the Project Budget in accordance with 0-PRC-4002: Project Budget and Cost Forecasting to create:
0-DAT-4001: Project Budget
The budget will be checked by the Project Manager and approved by the Director of Projects.

## Project Resource Management System
Project Controls will set up the Project Resource Management System for the project in accordance with 0-PRC-3003: Project Resource Management to create:
0-DAT-3002: Project Resource Management System
The PRMS will be checked by the Project Manager and approved by the Director of Projects.

## Owner Kick-Off Meeting
The Project Manager will arrange a kick-off meeting with the Owner to ensure that the respective responsibilities of the Project Team and Owner are clear, scope delineation is understood and that both parties will follow:
0-PRC-2004: Project Co-ordination Procedure.
