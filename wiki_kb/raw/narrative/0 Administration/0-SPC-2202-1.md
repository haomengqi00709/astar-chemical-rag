# 0-SPC-2202-1 — Specification


[TABLE: 0-SPC-2202-1_table_1]

Additional Conditions of Contract
The following additional conditions of contract have precedence over the Standard Conditions for Engineering Subcontract.

## Definitions
The Subcontract Data is information required to fully ascertain the obligations of the parties under the Subcontract and is in:
The Agreement;
The documents referenced in the Agreement;
The Special Conditions for Engineering Subcontract.
The Agreement is the document headed “Agreement” and executed on behalf of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Subcontractor.

## Project Specific Definitions

## Early Warning Meeting
The Early Warning Meeting may be conducted by telephone or any other means which enables its objectives to be met.

## Revised Schedule
The Subcontractor submits a revised Schedule to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics at maximum intervals of one calendar month whether or not Aker Kvaerner Chemetics has instructed him to.

## GST
The reference to value added tax in clause 22.3 of the Standard Conditions for Engineering Subcontracts includes any goods and services tax or other tax of a similar nature.

## Interest
Clauses 23.4 and 23.5 of the Standard Conditions for Engineering Subcontracts do not apply.

## Compensation Events
An event that is not notified by the Subcontractor as a compensation event within 2 weeks of the Subcontractor becoming aware of the event is deemed to have no effect upon the Subcontractor’s costs or planned Completion.  The Subcontractor releases and indemnifies Aker Kvaerner Chemetics against any claim in respect of the event.
An acceptance of a quotation is by Change Order signed on behalf of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics by the same person who executed the Subcontract.

## Disputes

### When a dispute arises under or in connection with this Subcontract, either party may submit the dispute to the Adjudicator by following the procedure under the contract, but adjudication is not a pre-condition to the reference of a dispute to the tribunal.  If neither party submits a dispute to the Adjudicator, either party may at any time refer the dispute to the tribunal.

### Unless a dispute relates to the Head Contract, the Adjudicator will be appointed by agreement between the parties and the tribunal will be arbitration by a single Arbitrator appointed by the British Columbia International Arbitration Centre under its Rules.

## Termination
Clause 40.3 is deleted and the following is substituted:
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may terminate by notifying the Subcontractor
immediately if the Subcontractor has substantially failed to comply with his obligations and has not put the default right within 2 weeks of notification by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics of the intention to terminate;
with 1 weeks notice for any other reason.
Clause 41.3 is amended by adding at the end:
“and does not include any amount in respect of costs incurred by the Subcontractor in expectation of completing the whole of the services whether or not they are costs to which the Subcontractor is committed.”

## Limitation on Liability
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Subcontractor will not be liable under this Subcontract, in law or equity, including for negligence, in respect of any liability arising in any way in relation to the services for:
Loss of profit, loss of use, loss of production, loss of business or any similar loss;
Any indirect or consequential damage; and
Special, exemplary or punitive damages.

## Security
The Subcontractor gives Aker Kvaerner Chemetics security.  The security is provided by a financial institution Aker Kvaerner Chemetics accepts, for the amount stated in the Subcontract Data and in a form acceptable to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.  The security must be given within 2 weeks of the starting date.  No payment to the Subcontractor is made until the security has been given.  Aker Kvaerner Chemetics has recourse to the security if a payment to which it is entitled remains unpaid for a period of five days after Aker Kvaerner Chemetics has notified the
Subcontractor that it intends to have recourse to the security.

## Delay Damages
The Subcontractor pays delay damages at the rate stated in the Subcontract Data for each day:
from the date by which a document is required to the date on which the document is provided; and,
from the Completion Date until Completion.
