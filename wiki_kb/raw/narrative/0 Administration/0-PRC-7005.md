# 0-PRC-7005 — Procedure


[TABLE: 0-PRC-7005_table_1]


## Purpose
This procedure describes the roles and responsibilities of  personnel in assuring the quality of goods supplied by Vendors.

## Vendor Requirements
Vendors will be issued the following documents to set out the quality requirements for the goods that they are supplying:
0-QDS-7XXX: [Template Quality Data Sheet
0-SPC-700X: Vendor Quality Assurance [Equipment] / [Bulk Materials]

## Quality Data Sheets
A Quality Data Sheet will be prepared for eachequipment item listed in the Purchase Order or for each type or class of bulk materials listed in the Purchase Order.

### Content
The Quality Data Sheet describes the following requirements:
The level of surveillance by
Inspection and test result records to be provided
Inspections and tests to be included in Vendor’s Inspection and Test Plan
Inspections and tests to be witnessed points
Inspections and tests to be hold points
Numbering

### Revisions
The Quality Data Sheet is the controlling document for surveillance by . It will be revised to incorporate any elements of the Vendor’s Inspection and Test Plan that  intends to survey.

## Specification
The Quality Data Sheets will refer to one of the following specifications:
0-SPC-7001: Vendor Quality Assurance (Equipment)
0-SPC-7002: Vendor Quality Assurance (Bulk Materials)

## Surveillance
Surveillance will be carried out by suitably qualified Inspectors under the direction of . Inspectors will be either  staff, or be the personnel of Subcontractor surveillance agencies.
The scope of the surveillance will be as described in the Quality Data Sheets, by 0-SPC-7001: Vendor Quality Assurance, and by this procedure

## Surveillance Reporting
The Quality Data Sheets are formatted to allow surveillance activities to be checked off as completed. The Inspector will do this updating directly in the native format version of the Quality Data Sheet in the project file.
Surveillance reports will be prepared by publishing the updated Quality Data Sheets (0-PRC-0005: Publishing of Project Documents), marking them as “Issued for Progress Report” with the revision numbers incremented.

## Non-Conformances
Vendor non-conformances may be identified by the Vendor, or by the Inspector during surveillance.

### Definition
A non-conformance is the failure by the Vendor to fulfil a requirement of the Purchase Order. As such, the term is only applicable to work that the Vendor considers being complete that does not meet the requirements of the Purchase Order.
This non-conformance procedure will also be used to document defect repair that is not covered by procedures already approved as part of the Vendor Quality Plan.

### Reporting
For each non-conformance, the Inspector will prepare a report in the form 0-NCR-7XXX: [Template Non-Conformance Report], containing the following information:
Description of the non-conformance
Vendor’s proposed action to correct the non-conformance
Vendor’s proposed action to prevent recurrence of the non-conformance
approval of proposed actions with any qualifications
Results of re-inspection after corrective action
Numbering
Non-Conformance Reports will be numbered (0-PRC-0002: Document Numbering) with the identification numbers identical to those of the reference Quality Data Sheets. Multiple Non-Conformance Reports for the same goods will be numbered as multiple sheets / sections of the same document.

### Revisions
Revisions of the Non-Conformance Reports will be issued, as information is developed, to advise personnel of status.
recording of results of re-inspection to provide a final record of disposition

## Inspection Release
In the case of items with Surveillance Level 1,2,3, or 4 the Inspector will provide the Vendor with approval in the form of 0-REL-7XXX: [Template Inspection Release] to permit the Vendor to package the goods ready for shipment.
The Inspection Release will list any remaining work required by the Vendor prior to packaging the goods. The remaining work listed will only be of a minor nature, not requiring re-inspection. Non-Conformances will be dealt with as described in Section 7, and an Inspection Release will not be issued until the goods have been re-inspected.
The Inspection Release will be numbered (0-PRC-0002: Document Numbering) with the identification number identical to that of the reference Quality Data Sheet. Multiple Inspection Releases for the same item or type of bulk materials will be numbered as multiple sheets / sections of the same document.
The Inspector will place the correct date in the electronic file version of the Inspection Release, will print it, sign it using blue ink, and hand it to the Vendor. The electronic file version will be marked “Signed in the original by [Name]” above the signature line and placed in the project electronic file.
