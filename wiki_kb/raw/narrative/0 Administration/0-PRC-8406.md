# 0-PRC-8406 — Procedure


[TABLE: 0-PRC-8406_table_1]


## Purpose
This procedure details the minimum requirements to ensure that hazardous materials purchased by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics are clearly identified during shipping, and that Material Safety Data Sheets (MSDS) for those materials are collected, compiled and distributed to the site where material will be used.

## Scope
Unless noted, these requirements will apply to all materials purchased by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or its subcontractors and to be shipped to a work site or shipped directly to a client.

## Hazardous Materials Identification
It is the joint responsibility of the person making the requisition and the Purchasing Agent to flag items being purchased that may be hazardous to people or the environment.
Every purchasing agent is to request information from the supplier about the properties of items being bought, and identify the possible hazards posed by the material during shipping, handling and storage; hazards posed while the material is being used; and hazards posed on improper disposal.

## Identification Requirement
All hazardous goods are to be clearly tagged and labeled in accordance to the Workplace Hazardous Materials Information System (WHMIS) guidelines and clearly labeled in English and in the language of the country of destination.
Material Safety Data Sheets (MSDS) or material safety handling instructions will be a separate line item in the purchase order.
The purchase order will clearly flag hazardous goods so that it becomes apparent to anyone looking at the purchase order, under which conditions the goods pose a hazard:
Shipping/handling/storage
During actual use or installation
Disposal

## Shipping Considerations
Hazardous goods that may pose a danger if improperly handled, will be properly marked and identified with the appropriate labels, and will have attached on the outside in a secure manner, instructions for the proper handling and storage of the package.  In addition, the goods will have a copy of Material Safety Data Sheets (MSDS) or handling instructions in a suitable pouch attached to the goods inside the shipping crate.
These instructions will be written in English and in the language of the destination country, and will at minimum include the Material Safety Datasheet Sheet for the material.
Hazardous items of different composition in a single purchase order will be segregated and packaged separately.
The Purchasing Agent will consult with the Shipping Agent to ensure that the hazardous items are adequately packaged to comply with the IATA (International Air Transport Association) guidelines and TDG (Transportation of Dangerous Goods) regulations for air shipment, or TDG for ground and ocean freight.

## Material Use Considerations
Goods that only pose a hazard when spent (expended) will have Material Safety Data Sheets or handling instructions in a suitable pouch attached to the goods inside the shipping crate.

## Receiving Materials
The Receiving Agent at the field will check all purchase orders and crates for hazardous goods identifications, and ensures that the goods are handled and managed according to MSDS handling instructions, specific vendor recommendations, and safe work practices.
The Receiving Agent is responsible for collecting a copy of the Material Safety Data Sheets attached to any crates; to place these in a reference binder to be kept in the field office; and to alert the HSE manager of the nature of the goods received.
