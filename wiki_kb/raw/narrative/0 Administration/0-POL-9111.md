## Purpose
The nature of our business will at times require that employees travel to project sites for extended periods of time where they may be subject to additional hardship due to climate, communications and other living and working conditions.  This procedure is intended to ensure that employees are fairly compensated for performing site work under these conditions and for the costs associated with being away from home for extended periods of time.
This procedure is not designed to provide compensation in lieu of adequate on-site health and safety precautions or work practices, or for insurable risks more appropriately covered by the Company's group health and insurance benefits.
This procedure applies to all Regular employees of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, Canadian Shared Services and AK Chemetics Offshore Services Inc. (collectively referred to as ”The Company”) with the exception of Assignment Uplift provisions which will only apply for employees of the Company in positions below Hay Point 640.  This limitation may be waived by the President under exceptional circumstances.
This procedure does not apply to independent consultants contracted by the Company.
This procedure does not normally apply to temporary employees engaged primarily for the purpose of site work, in which case their rate should be set to provide fair compensation.  This procedure will be applied to all other temporary employees required to travel on behalf of the Company.

## Application and Activities
This procedure will be applied to short term assignments that are less than 6 months.  For international assignments that are longer than 6 months, 11.1 PR 1 Group International Assignment Procedure will apply.
This procedure applies to the following categories of short term site assignments.  Note that this list is intended to cover the typical types of site assignments and is not intended to be exclusive.
Construction site services
In-plant technical service and customer training assignments
Plant commissioning
Quality assurance and expediting assignments
Assignments to other Engineering offices
Assignment Uplift will only be paid if the time on site is in excess of 30 consecutive days (excluding travel time).  Should the planned trip be expected to be more than 30 days then Assignment Uplift will commence immediately.  If the planned trip is for less than 30 days and the trip is extended for more than 30 days, Assignment Uplift will be retroactively paid from the day the employee arrived on site.
Travel for less than 30 days will be treated as a business trip and expenses will be reimbursed in accordance with 0-PRC-9003 Business Expense Reimbursement.

## Short Term Assignment Elements
This procedure addresses three elements to recognize the particular circumstances of the short term site assignment, namely:
Assignment Uplift
Home Leave / R & R Considerations
Meals and Incidental Expenses
It is the responsibility of the employee and the Department Manager to ensure that the travel requisition requests Assignment Uplift if the planned trip is more than 30 days.  The Project Manager must approve the travel requisition, and once the approved travel requisition is received, this will trigger communication with Human Resources and Finance to apply this procedure.   If the planned trip is less than 30 days but the assignment is extended beyond 30 days, the Project Manager must advise Human Resources of this change.

### Assignment Uplift
An Assignment Uplift will be determined in accordance with Group norms and the International Assignment Procedure, including obtaining uplift factors from Employment Conditions Abroad (“ECA”), which is a consulting firm that specialises in measuring living standard differences between countries and work locations.  The Assignment Uplift will be applied to base salary for the duration of the assignment, excluding travel time.
In addition to compensating for site location and conditions, the Assignment Uplift provided under this procedure is intended to compensate for additional home and living expenses incurred due to extended periods of absence.  Examples of expenses that are intended to be covered by the above premium include: child care arrangements, home maintenance expenses such as lawn mowing, pet care arrangements including kennel boarding fees, entertainment expenses such as fitness or theatre costs during assignment, costs associated with mail forwarding or mail pick-up, or local membership fees such as gym memberships.  Such expenses will not normally be reimbursed as part of your travel expenses under 0-PRC-9003 Business Expense Reimbursement.
Payments made under this section are taxable earnings, but are not considered earnings for either pension or benefits purposes.

Every effort must be made to adhere to the scheduling of these leaves.  Additional time off will require approval from the Project Manager.  If an R&R leave cannot be taken due to unforeseen situations including technical or client difficulties, the employee will be granted 2 days off with pay in lieu.  Time off in lieu must not be used for two consecutive R&R leaves.
Travel for R&R or Home Leaves must be booked and approved in accordance with 0-PRC-9002 Travel Authorisation.  Travel costs, including excursion airfare and other reasonable travel related expenses will be paid by the Company.
Assignment Uplift During Leave
The Assignment Uplift does not apply during normal leaves in accordance with this section.  Timesheets must reflect the R&R/Home Leave charge code.  Additional time off must be taken as vacation or banked time and is also not subject to Assignment Uplift.
Spousal and Family Visits in Lieu
An employee may request that a spouse or family member may visit in lieu of a trip home.  Written approval from Human Resources must be received before any travel arrangements are made.  The Company will pay for airfare and direct travel expenses, to a maximum equal to the travel expenses that the Company would have paid if the employee had returned home using an excursion class airfare.
Statutory and Local Holidays
Employees on short term assignments will be eligible for paid local statutory holidays.  If an employee works on-site on a home office statutory holiday, they will be able to bank 8.25 hours in lieu unless they took an equivalent local statutory holiday during the assignment.

### Meals and Incidental Expenses
A per diem will be set to cover meals and incidental expenses in accordance with reasonable local costs.  Housing will be considered a Business Expense.  The per diem will take into consideration whether meals and housing are provided with the assignment.  If a per diem is not established for the Short Term Assignment, 0-PRC-9003 Business Expense Reimbursement will apply.  The Company will make efforts to ensure that we are in compliance with any Canada Revenue Agency guidelines to avoid personal tax liability for employees.

## Overtime
Employees will be eligible for overtime pay while on site in accordance with 0-POL-9113-R2: Compensation of Overtime.  Overtime hours must be approved by the Project Manager or their designate.
Department Managers, Project Managers, Construction Managers and Commissioning Managers are required to ensure that appropriate staff levels are maintained on site and in accordance with the Site Hours of Work outlined in 0-POL-9106 Hours of Work.  Any exceptions must be approved in advance by the President or a Director.

## Timesheet Administration
It is essential that employees complete timesheets as fully and accurately as possible and submit timesheets to the Project Manager or designate. If there is a designate on site responsible for approving timesheets, that designate will forward approved timesheets to the Department Manager and copy the Project Manager.  The Department Manager is responsible to ensure that timesheets are submitted electronically on behalf of the employees on-site.
For reimbursable or time and materials work, the Project Manager or designate will ensure that timesheets are approved by the Client representative on site.

## Statutory Requirements
To the extent that any statutory requirements provide higher overall compensation than the aggregate provisions of this Policy and 0-POL-9113-R2 Compensation of Overtime, the statutory requirements shall prevail.
