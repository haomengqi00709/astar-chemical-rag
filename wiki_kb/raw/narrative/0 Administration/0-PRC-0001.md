# 0-PRC-0001 — Procedure


[TABLE: 0-PRC-0001_table_1]


## Purpose
This procedure describes the creation and use of standard documents.

## Administration
Standards will be stored in native format on the network in Q Drive. Only the most recent version of any standard will be used.
Write-access to Q Drive is only given to the Standards Administrator. The Standards Administrator: will ensure that documents are properly numbered and approved; will update the Standard Document Number Index; place them in the correct location on Q Drive. All employees of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics will have read-access to Q Drive, which allows them to copy the standard documents for their use.

## Initial Issue
The Checker and the Approver will review all first drafts of standard documents to ensure conformity and compliance with all existing standards.
Following this review, the draft standard will be marked Rev 0, status shown as “Comments Requested”, and placed in Q Drive. The Standards Administrator will notify all employees of the draft standard, and will invite comments over a one-month period. The comments will be e-mailed to the Standards Administrator who will collate them.
Following the comment period, the author of the standard, the Checker and the Approver will review the comments and revise the standard as required. It will be marked Rev 1, status shown as “Standard”, and placed in Q Drive.  The signed “Approval” PDF of the standard will also be stored.  The Standards Administrator will notify all employees of the availability of the standard. The Standard Document Number Index will be updated.

## Revision
All revisions to standards will be flagged, and authors of revisions will include a note in the commentary briefly stating the details of the revision.  The Checker and Approver will review all revisions.  The revision number will be incremented, the status shown as “Standard”, and the document placed in Q Drive. The signed “Approval” PDF of the standard will also be stored.  The Standards Administrator will notify all employees of the revision of the standard. The Standard Document Number Index will be updated.
A copy of the previous revision will be retained for revision history on Q drive. This copy will be marked “obsolete” and stored in the “Old Files” folder.

## Training
All employees will be trained in the use and requirements of standard documents as applicable to their assigned work. The training will be accomplished by means of group presentations and discussion delivered by the Approver or delegate.
The applicable Business Directors or delegate will ensure that a log of training received by each employee in their department is maintained.

## Use of Standards
All work executed by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics will comply with all applicable standard documents marked Rev 1 or later.
Other than as described in Sections 7.2 and 7.3, non-compliance with standard documents will only be permitted with the approval of the applicable Business Director.
This Section is not intended to apply to the normal use of any standard documents that are templates for the insertion of data by the project team e.g. Lists, Data Sheets, etc. It does apply to the modification of the form of any templates.

### Use without Modification
When a standard document is to be used on a project without modification, the project team will remove any commentary and will replace the prefix “Standard” with the project number (0-PRC-0002: Document Numbering).
The document will be marked as Rev A or Rev 1 for the project, and the appropriate status shown followed by “(S-Rx)” where “x” is the revision of the originating standard document. The revision entries of the standard will be removed from the revision log.
The document will be published (0-PRC-0005: Publishing of Project Documents), and will be approved by the responsible Discipline Approver (0-PRC-2001: Assignment of Project Responsibilities). For documents with major work division 0: Administration, this person is the Project Engineer.

### Use with Modification
When a draft standard is to be used on a project, or where a standard document requires modification for a project in order to meet contractual requirements, the project team will author the necessary changes, will remove any commentary, and will replace the prefix “Standard” with the project number (0-PRC-0002: Document Numbering).
If any sections of the standard are deleted, the section number and title will be retained, and the text replaced with the words “Not Used”.
The document will be marked as Rev A or Rev 1 for the project and the appropriate status shown followed by “(MS-Rx)” where “x” is the revision of the originating standard document. The revision entries of the standard will be removed from the revision log and the changes from the originating standard will be flagged.
The document will be published (0-PRC-0005: Publishing of Project Documents). Procedures and templates will be checked and approved by the personnel assigned in Section 3. Other documents in work areas 1 through 9 will be checked and approved by the applicable Chief Engineer.

### Complete Revision
In the case where a standard document requires complete re-writing for a project in order to meet contractual requirements, the project team will author the new document and will number it with the project number as prefix and the same identification number as the replaced standard.
The document will be marked as Rev A or Rev 1 for the project, and the appropriate status shown.
The document will be published (0-PRC-0005: Publishing of Project Documents). Procedures and templates will be checked and approved by the personnel assigned in Section 3. Other documents in work areas 1 through 9 will be checked and approved by the applicable Chief Engineer.
