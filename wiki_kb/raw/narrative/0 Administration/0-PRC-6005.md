# 0-PRC-6005 — Procedure


[TABLE: 0-PRC-6005_table_1]


## Purpose
This procedure describes the steps required to issue a Construction Subcontract.

## Technical Requirements
The technical requirements will be developed in accordance with 0-PRC-6091: Construction Subcontract Scope Preparation.

## Commercial Requirements
The commercial requirements will be developed in accordance with 0-PRC-6004: Commercial Terms for Construction Subcontract.
0-SPC-6001: Standard Conditions for Construction Subcontract is in the form of the NEC Engineering and Construction Short Contract published for the Institution of Civil Engineers by Thomas Telford Publishing, London. The published document is subject to copyright. An electronic copy of the document has been created for ease of reference, however to comply with copyright requirements a copy of the document must be purchased through the library on each occasion that a Construction Subcontract is issued. The Subcontract Administrator will advise the library when a Construction Subcontract has been awarded, and the library will record copies purchased against project number for audit purposes.

## Issue of Request for Tender

### Agreement
The Subcontract Administrator will prepare the Construction Subcontract Agreement based on the standard template 0-SUB-6X00: [Template] Construction Subcontract Agreement. Information contained in square brackets in the template will be modified to reflect the specific requirements of the project with the exception of the words “by Subcontractor”.
The Agreement will be published (0-PRC-0005: Publishing of Project Documents) with status “Issued for Quotation” and will be checked by the Project Engineer and by Legal and approved by the Project Manager.

### Administrative Specifications
Administrative specifications directly referenced by the Construction Subcontract Agreement are as follows:
0-SPC-6003: Construction Subcontract Coordination
0-SPC-6011: Construction Subcontract Pricing: Lump Sum
0-SPC-6012: Construction Subcontract Pricing: Unit Price
0-SPC-6021: Construction Subcontract Scheduling
0-SPC-6041: Construction Subcontractor Materials Control
0-SPC-6051: Construction Subcontract Safety Management
0-SPC-6061: Construction Subcontract Environmental Management
0-SPC-6071: Construction Subcontract Quality Assurance
0-SPC-6081: Construction Subcontract Industrial Relations Management
0-SPC-6091: Construction Subcontractor Document Supply
These documents exist as standards that will be used as-is, or will be modified for the project as necessary (0-PRC-0001: Approval and Use of Standards). They should be published with status “Approved for Construction” and checked and approved (0-PRC-0001: Approval and Use of Standards).

### Price List
The Subcontract Administrator will prepare 0-LST-6X11: Construction Subcontract Price List.
In the case of a lump sum subcontract, the Price List will define how the price is to be broken down by the Subcontractor.
In the case of a unit price subcontract, the Price List will set the quantities for each unit of the works, allowing for the standard unit definitions contained in 0-SPC-6005: Construction Subcontract Pricing: Unit Price.
The Price List will form the basis for the Subcontractor’s progress claims.
The Price List will be numbered (0-PRC-0002: Document Numbering) with an identification number identical to that used for the Construction Subcontract Agreement (0-PRC-6002: Form of Construction Subcontract).
The Price List will be published (0-PRC-0005: Publishing of Project Documents) with status “Issued for Quotation” and will be checked by the Project Engineer and approved by the Project Manager.

### Other Pricing Lists
Other pricing lists directly referenced by the Construction Subcontract Agreement are as follows:
0-LST-6X12: Construction Subcontract Labour Rates
0-LST-6X13: Construction Subcontract Crew Rates
0-LST-6X14: Construction Subcontract Indirect Labour Schedule
0-LST-6X15: Construction Subcontract Equipment Schedule
These lists are all to have information inserted by the Subcontractor to better define the basis for his bid. The standard templates contain sample data showing how they are to be completed and 0-SPC-6002: Instructions to Construction Subcontractors for Tender contains instructions on their use.
The lists will be numbered (0-PRC-0002: Document Numbering) with “X” replaced by the same digit as for the Subcontract Agreement (0-PRC-6002: Form of Construction Subcontract).
The lists will be published (0-PRC-0005: Publishing of Project Documents) with status “Issued for Quotation”. No checking or approval is required.

### Instructions to Tenderers
0-SPC-6002: Instructions to Construction Subcontractors for Tender exists as a standard that will be used as-is, or will be modified for the project as necessary (0-PRC-0001: Approval and Use of Standards). Any changes to Sections 8 and 9 must be approved by Legal.

### Letter of Invitation
Construction Subcontractors who will be invited to tender for the works will be identified by the project team (0-PRC-2003: Project Execution Plan).
The Subcontract Administrator will prepare covering letters to each of the Construction Subcontractors, referencing 0-SPC-6002: Instructions to Construction Subcontractors for Tender.
The Subcontract Administrator will forward the letters of invitation to Document Control for issue to the Construction Subcontractors (0-PRC-0006: Control of Project Documentation).

### Document Package
The Subcontract Administrator and the Project Engineer will forward a list of all required documents to Document Control for issue to the Construction Subcontractors (0-PRC-0006: Control of Project Documentation).

## Issue of Construction Subcontract

### Agreement
The Subcontract Administrator will revise the Construction Subcontract Agreement to comply with any modifications agreed as part of the negotiation with the successful Subcontractor and to insert any information provided by the Subcontractor in his bid.
The Agreement will be published (0-PRC-0005: Publishing of Project Documents) with status “Approved for Construction” and will be checked by the Project Engineer and by Legal and approved by the Project Manager.
The Subcontract Administrator will print a paper copy and arrange for it to be signed by the person authorised under Section 6, presenting it together with a copy of the approved Construction Subcontractor Evaluation Summary Record (0-PRC-6003: Construction Subcontractor Evaluation and Approval).
The Subcontract Administrator will forward the signed and authorised paper copy of the Construction Subcontract to Document Control for issue to the Construction Subcontractor (0-PRC-0006: Control of Project Documentation).

### Administrative Specifications
Administrative specifications directly referenced by the Construction Subcontract Agreement as listed in Section 4.2 will not be further modified. Any exceptions or deviations agreed as part of the negotiation with the successful Subcontractor will be handled by means of appropriate text inserted into 0-SPC-6X01: Special Conditions for Construction Subcontract.

### Price List
The Subcontract Administrator will revise 0-LST-6X11: Construction Subcontract Price List based on the tender of the successful Subcontractor, including any modifications agreed as part of the negotiation.
The Price List will be published (0-PRC-0005: Publishing of Project Documents) with status “Approved for Construction” and will be checked by the Project Engineer and approved by the Project Manager.

### Other Pricing Lists
Other pricing lists directly referenced by the Construction Subcontract Agreement will be revised by the Subcontract Administrator based on the tender of the successful Subcontractor, including any modifications agreed as part of the negotiation.
The lists will be published (0-PRC-0005: Publishing of Project Documents) with status “Approved for Construction” and will be checked by the Project Engineer and approved by the Project Manager.

### Document Package
The Subcontract Administrator and the Project Engineer will forward a list of all required documents to Document Control for issue to the Construction Subcontractor (0-PRC-0006: Control of Project Documentation).
