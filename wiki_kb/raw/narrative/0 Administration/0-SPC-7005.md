# 0-SPC-7005 — Specification


[TABLE: 0-SPC-7005_table_1]


## Purpose
The purpose of this specification is to ensure that Agencies supplying Third Party Inspection Services to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics (AKC) have a clear understanding of the services normally expected to be provided.

## Scope
This specification list the minimum extent of activities normally expected to be provided by Third Party Inspection Agencies. The range of activities may be modified for a particular assignment by instructions issued by AKC and confirmed in writing, usually by E-mail.

## Reference
This specification should be read in conjunction with AKC Standard Specification 0-SPC-7003 Third Party Inspection Agency Documentation Requirements.

## Inspection
Upon receipt of a Purchase Order (PO) from AKC for Inspection Services the Agency will follow the sequence shown under unless advised otherwise by AKC:
Make contact with the equipment vendor and advise him that the Agency will represent AKC in surveillance and inspection matters, and will be responsible for issuing an Inspection Release covering the equipment.
Confirm the location of manufacture.
Establish if any sub-contract of manufacture is planned. If so, advise AKC the details.
a	If Final Inspection (Inspection Level 1) is required arrange for vendor to notify in advance a date when all equipment will be available and all Quality Data Sheet (QDS) inspection requirements can be met. This includes review of all QA documentation and, where specified, witnessing hydrostatic testing. A brief review of vendor’s relevant manufacturing and inspection processes should be carried out during the visit unless this has already been done within the past year. Any serious problems should be reported to AKC immediately.
b	For reporting and other documentation requirements refer to 0-SPC-7003.
a	If Stage Inspection (Inspection Level greater than 1) is required then, based upon the current Production Schedule(s), establish a tentative visit plan that will enable the requirements of the QDS(s) to be met within the budget constraints of the Agency PO.
b	Execute inspection visits according to the plan, revised as necessary to accommodate schedule change or quality problems encountered. During the visits monitor vendor’s relevant manufacturing and inspection processes, and establish if progress is in accordance with the current schedule.
c	For reporting and other documentation requirements refer to 0-SPC-7003.

## Expediting
When shop expediting visits are requested, the Agency will utilize experienced shop or field expeditors and not inspectors, unless specifically agreed to by AKC. In expediting matters, direction will be provided by the Procurement Department.
