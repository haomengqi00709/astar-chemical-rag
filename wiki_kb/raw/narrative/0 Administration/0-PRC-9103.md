# 0-PRC-9103 — Unknown


[TABLE: 0-PRC-9103_table_1]


## Objective
Employees are expected to represent Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and Aker Kvaerner with integrity, honesty and trustworthiness and in accordance with Aker Kvaerner Policy 1.2: Business Ethics.
Recognizing that it is impossible to define proper behavior in every situation, the purpose of this procedure is to provide direction to employees as to what represents an acceptable standard of behavior and conduct while acting as an employee of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
Employee conduct that is not in compliance with this policy may lead to disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment.

## Regulatory Compliance
Employees will at all times, and in all dealings both internal and external, comply with all applicable laws and regulations of the countries in which the employee is performing his or her duties.  No employee will knowingly commit or condone an illegal act or instruct another employee, supplier or customer to do so.
Employees are expected to be sufficiently familiar with any legislation that applies to their work and to recognize situations that present potential corporate liability.  If in doubt, employees should ask for guidance from the appropriate Senior Manager or Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ Legal Counsel.

## Conflicts of Interest
Employees are expected to perform their duties conscientiously and not to put themselves in a position in which their personal interests and those of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics might conflict.  In general, a conflict of interest exists for employees who use their position at Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or business information of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for personal gain or benefit to themselves, friends or families, whether directly or indirectly.
Employees must avoid any situation in which there is, or appears to be, potential conflict which could interfere with the employee’s judgement in making decisions in Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ best interests.
Some conflicts of interest are readily identifiable, but others are less obvious.  Aker Kvaerner Chemetics recognizes that its employees have perfectly legitimate outside interests; however there may also be situations that could be seen as a conflict of interest no matter how innocent the intentions of the employee.  Employees should err on the side of caution.
Employees must disclose all circumstances that constitute an actual or potential conflict of interest to the appropriate Senior Manager or Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ Legal Counsel.

## Outside Business Interests
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics employees may hold outside jobs or engage in modest self-employment activities in their own time, using their own resources, provided the performance of their duties for Aker Kvaerner Chemetics is not adversely affected.  Employees should not become involved in any activity that would affect their objectivity when carrying out their normal duties.

## Entertainment, Gifts & Favours

### Business entertainment is an appropriate way to build valuable business relationships.  At the same time, however, Aker Kvaerner Chemetics has an obligation to ensure that business entertainment is carried out appropriately and in conformance with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics standards.
Employees are expected to exercise sound business judgement in incurring expenses for which the employee expects reimbursement from the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.  The purpose of business entertainment is to enhance business.  Before any business entertainment expenses are incurred, a clear potential for business development must exist.
When arranging business entertainment, employees should exercise good judgement in selecting the individuals to attend.  A group should consist of a balanced mixture of employees and clients.  Spouses/partners should not be included unless the occasion warrants, such as when a client is travelling with their spouse/partner.
At all times, the selected business entertainment must not negatively impact Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ corporate image or principles.  Examples of appropriate business entertainment are lunches, dinners, theatre, golf and other sporting events.  Aker Kvaerner Chemetics will not tolerate any form of “adult” entertainment, even in foreign cultures where this is seemingly acceptable to the other business party.
If an employee has any doubt as to whether a proposed form of entertainment is in accordance with this policy, he or she should first seek guidance from the appropriate Senior Manager or Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ Legal Counsel..

### When entertaining clients in circumstances where alcohol is served, employees should exercise good judgement in terms of how much alcohol is offered or consumed, and must not drive while impaired.  In these circumstances Aker Kvaerner Chemetics will cover the cost of alternate transportation.

### The purchase of gifts for clients is restricted to Sales and Project Managers.  Any gifts with a value of over $200 must have the prior approval of the appropriate Senior Manager.  All gifts must be reported on an expense report and must indicate the recipient, position and organization, as well as the prior approval obtained where required.

### Employees and members of their immediate families may not accept gifts, cash payments, free services, privileges, entertainment or favors of more than a nominal value from customers or suppliers or other business contacts.  Entertainment and/or gifts should be reasonable and never lead to a sense of obligation upon the employee; they should not be of such a type that they could be construed by an impartial observer as a bribe, pay off or improper incentive.  Employees should not accept any entertainment that could not be justified on a Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ expense report were they offering it rather than receiving it.  All gifts and entertainment must be disclosed to the appropriate Senior Manager or Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ Legal Counsel.

## Confidential Information
As a term of their employment agreement, all employees are under a contractual obligation to safeguard the confidential information of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.  Confidential information must not be disclosed or made available to non-employees.  Employees may only use confidential information in the course of their employment.
The requirement to safeguard and protect confidential information from disclosure applies not only to sensitive technical information, such as that generated through our research and development efforts or associated with our proprietary technologies, but also to any information which Aker Kvaerner Chemetics has a business interest in protecting.  This includes such things as Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ business strategies, marketing plans and prospects, pricing information, methods of estimating, sources of supply, customer lists, research plans, forecasts and results, and also includes the confidential information of third parties such as clients, prospective clients and development partners.
Employees are expected to perform their duties in accordance with these obligations and with due regard for Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ interests.  Any employee who is uncertain as to whether certain information can be disclosed or made available to a person outside of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, or whether a certain activity will allow a third party access to confidential information, should seek the advice of the appropriate Senior Manager or Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ Legal Counsel.

## Electronic Mail / Internet Use
Electronic mail (email) is a highly effective means of communicating both within and outside Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.  Access to email and the Internet is provided to employees to assist them in performing their work in the most efficient way possible.
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ Internet access and email systems are provided for official business use.  Occasional personal use is permitted but personal messages will be treated no differently under this policy from business messages.
Neither email nor the Internet may be used for a) illegal, unethical or immoral uses; b) sending, receiving or accessing offensive, objectionable, abusive, pornographic (including nudity), obscene, sexist, racist, harassing or provocative messages, images or other materials, including adult–oriented Web sites or news groups; c) defamatory, or derogatory or false messages; d) political activities; e) other commercial or business uses other than in pursuit of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics business; f) unauthorized access to other user’s email, data, or communications; g) uses that infringe on copyright or other intellectual property rights; h) unsecured disclosure of confidential or privileged information; i) uses that may compromise system integrity or degrade system performance; j) deliberately propagating viruses.
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics reserves the right to access, monitor and control all employee usage of the Internet and any message sent or received over Aker Kvaerner Chemetics email system.
