# 0-PRC-8103 — Procedure


[TABLE: 0-PRC-8103_table_1]


## Purpose
This procedure describes the minimum Health and Safety precautions that Aker Kvaerner Chemetics employees will follow during a shop visit.

## Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Health and Safety Policy
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics is committed to:
1) Providing a safe and healthy workplace for all its employees; and
2) Conducting operations in an environmentally sound manner.
All Aker Kvaerner Chemetics employees are expected to cooperate and contribute toward the success of the company HSE program by performing their jobs in the safest manner prescribed, and by conducting themselves in a way that enhances their personal safety and that of others.

## Safety Orientation
Find out in advance what the shop safety requirements are. Employees may need to carry their own safety equipment.
Upon arrival at the Vendor workshop ask for a safety orientation.  The minimum topics of discussion are to be:
Personal protection equipment requirements
Emergency signals and safe meeting location
Location of emergency wash stations
How to summon first aid
Typical shop hazards and ongoing work that may present a hazard (ex: radiography, hydro testing, etc.)

## Personal Protection Equipment
Employees visiting a fabricating shop are required to wear the following minimum personal protection equipment:
Clothing that covers arms and legs (long sleeves and long pants)
Work boots which meet CSA Z195 Standard.
Glasses with side shields, or goggles.  All protective eyewear must meet CSA Z94.3 Standard, which is indicated by the CSA Logo.
Other safety equipment as required by Vendor or shop owner.
For specific recommendations on type of equipment and conditions of use, see specification: Standard-0-8015 Personal Protection Equipment

### Recommended Personal Protection Equipment
Hard hat which meets the CAN/CSA Standard Z94.1-92
Hearing protection (required if exposed to noisy environments)
Respirator (required if exposed to paint fumes, FRP resin vapors, or dust)
Gloves
If the following hazards are present, a hard hat is required to be worn:
Bumping hazard
Fork lift operations
Any possibility for falling objects
People working above

## Confined Space Entry Procedure
The Aker Kvaerner Chemetics employee will be fully familiar with the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Confined Space Entry Procedure:  Standard-0-PRC-8502-R1 Confined Space Entry
The Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Employee is empowered to determine if the safety conditions have been met to attempt entry of a confined space.
The Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Confined Space Entry procedure will be used in the event that the Vendor does not have a procedure that meets the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics safety standard.

## Responsibilities of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Employees on Fabricating Worksite
Ask your host about the safety procedures you are required to observe
Stay within designated walkways and enter work areas only when guided by your host.
Be extra cautious in machine shop areas. Stay clear of boring mills, lathes, drills, or any other moving machinery.
Avoid grinding areas. Do not stand in direct line with grinding or cutting operations to avoid grinding dust or being struck by a broken stone or disk.
Wear appropriate respirator if you will be exposed to dust, FRP resin vapors, or paint fumes.
Do not look directly at welding or cutting arcs.
Avoid areas where arc cutting is being used.  These operations generate hot flying metal, and are very noisy.
Watch for overhead crane movements. Never be under a load.
Do not enter areas where radiography is being conducted even when told it is safe.
Do not assume that workers within the plant are aware of your presence and will act to protect you.
If unsure … ask your host.
