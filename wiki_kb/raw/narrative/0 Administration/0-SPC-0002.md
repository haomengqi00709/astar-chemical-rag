# 0-SPC-0002 — Specification


[TABLE: 0-SPC-0002_table_1]


## Purpose
This specification describes the supply of documentation by Engineering Subcontractors.

## Format
Subcontractor will deliver documents in native digital format using the following software:
AutoCAD
Microsoft Office
Aker Kvaerner will provide document templates for use with the software listed providing borders, title blocks, style elements, etc. Subcontractor may insert their name and reference number in the locations provided.

## Numbering
Subcontractor will insert Aker Kvaerner document numbers and titles, and Owner document numbers, in accordance with 0-PRC-0002: Document Numbering.
Subcontractor will assign document file names in accordance with 0-PRC-0003: File Structure.
Subcontractor will not use any proprietary document or file numbering systems.

## Content
Document content will be prepared in the [English] language. Subcontractor may as an alternative, prepare bilingual documents in both [English] and the Subcontractor’s working language.
Document content will be prepared using [SI] units.

## Revisions
Subcontractor will highlight revisions by clouding drawings, and by marking textual and tabular documents with a vertical line in the left-hand margin. Following revision, Subcontractor will increment the revision number, and will update the status.

## Document Status
Subcontractor will mark the status on each document prior to issuing it to Aker Kvaerner. The status describes the permitted use of the information provided in the document as follows:
Issued for Owner Review: Published specifically for this purpose.
Issued for Owner Approval: Published specifically for this purpose.
Issued for Quotation: Published specifically for this purpose.
Approved for Design: The information provided may be used for further design.
Approved for Fabrication: The information provided may be used to manufacture the items shown or described.
Approved for Construction: The information provided may be used to construct the items shown or described.
Approved Instruction. The information provided may be used by the Owner for the operation or maintenance of the items shown or described.
As Built. The information provided is a record of the condition of the plant as constructed.
Limitations on the use of the document may be made by adding a qualifier to the status. For example: “Approved for Design (Layout only)”
The word “Approved” as applied to document status means approved by Subcontractor for the use stated in accordance with the Quality Plan.
