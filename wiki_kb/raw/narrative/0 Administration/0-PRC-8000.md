# 0-PRC-8000 — Procedure


[TABLE: 0-PRC-8000_table_1]


## Purpose
This procedure describes the management system by which Aker Kvaerner Chemetics ensures compliance with the Health Safety and Environment Policy of Aker Kvaerner as described in Group Policy Number: 2.2.

## Definitions

### HSE
Means Health, Safety, and Environment

### Company
Refers to the corporate entity Aker Kvaerner Chemetics

## Policy Statement
In Aker Kvaerner HSE is a core value, expressed in our HSE Mindset:
All incidents can be prevented. We strive continuously for zero harm to personnel, material and non-material assets.
We focus on employee health and on continuously improving the work environment.
We conduct our operations through efficient use of materials and energy, with minimum waste and damage to the environment.
We design products and services to have no undue environmental impact, to be safe and to be efficient in consuming energy and natural resources. We seek to ensure that our products can be recycled or disposed of safely.
To live up to our HSE Mindset, we will:
Require every employee to take personal responsibility for HSE by focusing on own behavior.
Apply a systematic and measurable approach to continually improve our HSE culture and performance.
Bring with us our high HSE standards wherever we do business. Complying with applicable laws and regulations is only the minimum.
Openly communicate HSE issues and performance, and share and learn from HSE best practices, internally and externally.
Include HSE performance in the selection, appraisal and reward of our staff.
Integrate HSE in all business processes.
Require line management to provide HSE leadership and implement the policy.

## Governing Safety Policies and Procedures
Health Safety and the Environment will be protected by supporting and enforcing the Occupancy Health and Safety (OHS) regulations of the Workers Compensation Board of British Columbia (WCB), Aker Kvaerner Group Health and Safety Manual, and Company specific safety policies and procedures.
Company safety policies and procedures will meet or exceed the requirements of both Aker Kvaerner policies and procedures, and WCB policies and procedures. In case of conflict the policy or procedure deemed to the higher standard will be used.
When performing work in foreign jurisdictions, the Company will comply with applicable legislated law, Client HSE Standards and contact documents, and will strive toward higher standards.

### HSE Operating System
Group Policy 2.2 outlines the seven elements of an effective HSE Management System that will be applied throughout the organization.
Each of these management elements has sub-elements with clearly stated expectations and a list of the minimum enabling arrangements that must be in place to achieve the expectations.
The Operating System performance of each Business Unit is evaluated on an annual basis by using a self-assessment and a peer review.

### HSE Performance

Standard-0-PRC-8002  HSE Performance Reporting

Leading Indicators

Synergi is the Group database used to record occupancy health and safety incidents, and statistics, for the purpose of knowledge sharing across the Aker Kvaerner Group of companies, and for producing HSE performance statistics.
The Company will follow Group guidelines to enter the occupancy health and safety information into the Synergi database.

## Management and Responsibility

### Management Responsibility
The President of the Company has overall management responsibility for HSE within all of its operations with responsibility for specific segments of operations delegated as described in this procedure.
All line managers and supervisors are expected to provide HSE leadership and to implement the Group Policy and this procedure within their areas of administrative control.

### Individual Responsibility
All employees of the Company are required to take personal responsibility for HSE by focusing on their own behavior, and will comply with this procedure, and all other Company HSE procedures and policies.

### HSE Management Committee
An HSE Management Committee will be formed comprising the following members:
President of Company	Chair
HSE Manager	Alternate Chair
Administration Services Coordinator	Responsible for Vancouver Office
President of Equipment Division	Responsible for Toronto Workshop
Laboratory Supervisor	Responsible for Burnaby Laboratory
Manager of Construction & Estimating	Responsible for Construction Sites
Manager of Process & Mechanical	Responsible for Commissioning Sites
The committee will meet monthly to review HSE performance within each of the areas of responsibility outlined above. Attendance by personnel located outside the Vancouver Office may be by teleconference. A record of the meeting will be kept in the form of an Action List, with the status of previous actions updated and new actions noted with assignment of responsibility. The Action List will be posted for access by all employees in the Company data network.

### HSE Manager
The Company HSE Manager will report to the President. The duties and responsibilities of the HSE Manager include:
Champion HSE initiatives and promote ongoing safety awareness;
Coaching and mentoring of senior management on HSE leadership;
Development of HSE policy and procedures and strategies for implementation;
Maintenance of the HSE System documentation;
Review of supporting procedures and practices to ensure conformity;
Conducts health and safety audits;
Ensures follow-up on HSE cases;
Gathers HSE performance data;
Assists accident investigations and/or performs accident investigations;
Initiation and implementation of incident investigation; and
Evaluation and audit of the HSE Management System and Project HSE Management Plans.

## Vancouver Office

### Management Responsibility
Responsibility for HSE at the Vancouver Office as set out in this Section 8 is assigned to the Administration Services Coordinator.

### Vancouver Office Joint Health and Safety Committee
The Vancouver Office Joint Health and Safety Committee (JHSC) will be formed in accordance with the requirements of the OHS regulations of British Columbia.  The JHSC will have equal representation from management and non-management employees who will be elected by their peers.  The management representatives in this committee will be, at minimum, the Administration Services Coordinator, the HSE Manager, and the Human Resources Manager.
This committee will assist the Administration Services Coordinator to ensure that all the responsibilities for HSE at the Vancouver Office are fulfilled.
The committee will meet monthly to review HSE performance within the Vancouver Office. A record of the meeting will be kept in the form of an Action List, with the status of previous actions updated and new actions noted with assignment of responsibility. The Action List will be posted for access by all employees in the Company data network.

### Health
The Company will provide a workplace environment to minimize any deleterious impact on employee health. This requires the following facilities/actions:
Heating, Ventilation and Air Conditioning to comply with local codes and regulations;
Sufficient and sanitary facilities;
Appropriate levels of ambient and task lighting;
Refrigerators for the storage of food;
Furniture designed to minimize repetitive strain injury;
Promotion of employee health and fitness.

### Safety
The Company will minimize risk to employees working at the engineering office. This requires the following facilities/actions:
Following the health and safety guidelines of the Workers Compensation Board of B.C.;
Maintaining First Aid facilities and trained first aid attendants;
Maintaining an effective office emergency response plan that is tested on a regular basis;
Maintaining employee awareness of ergonomic factors;
Engage employees in reporting near-misses and risk observations; and
Take proactive actions eliminate unsafe conditions, and to reduce clutter and accumulation of unnecessary materials.

### Environment
The Company will take a leading role in following the principles of care for the environment embodied in the slogan “Reduce Recycle Reuse”.

## Chemetics Technology Centre (Burnaby Laboratory)
Responsibility for HSE at the Burnaby Laboratory as set out in this Section 10 is assigned to the Laboratory Supervisor.

### Chemetics Technology Centre Joint Health and Safety Committee
A Laboratory Joint Health and Safety Committee (JHSC) will be formed in accordance with the requirements of the OHS regulations of British Columbia.  The JHSC will have equal representation from management and non-management employees who will be elected by their peers.  The management representative in this committee will be, at minimum, the Laboratory Supervisor.
The committee will meet monthly to review HSE performance within the Division. A record of the meeting will be kept in the form of an Action List, with the status of previous actions updated and new actions noted with assignment of responsibility. The Action List will be posted for access by all employees in the Company data network.

### Health
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Technology Centre will provide a workplace environment to minimize any deleterious impact on employee health. This requires the following facilities/actions:
Premises that comply with B.C.’s codes and regulations for heating, ventilation, air conditioning, and sanitary facilities;
Smoke-free working environment;
Appropriate levels of ambient and task lighting;
Appropriate means for storage of food (such as refrigerators);
Office and laboratory furniture designed to minimize repetitive strain injury;
Protective clothing and equipment when working in pilot area or adjacent labs; and
Promotion of employee health and fitness initiatives.

### Safety
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Technology Centre will take the followings actions to minimize risk to employees working at the Burnaby Laboratory:
Follow the health and safety guidelines of the Workers Compensation Board of B.C. ;
Implement and enforce all rules and internal safety procedures highlighted in the Center’s Safety Manual.
Provide appropriate instruction and training in the proper handling and usage of laboratory chemicals and equipment;
Maintain First Aid facilities and ensure that all employees hold valid First Aid Certificates;
Maintain an effective laboratory emergency response plan that is tested on a regular basis;
Maintain an effective Workplace Hazardous Materials Information System (WHMIS) program with database updated annually;
Perform risk assessments or process audits for all new experimental setups;
Perform safety inspections/audits of safety equipment and operations on a minimum monthly basis;
Maintain employee awareness of ergonomic factors;
Engage employees in reporting near-misses and risk observations; and
Take proactive actions to eliminate unsafe conditions, and to reduce clutter and accumulation of unnecessary materials.

### Environment
Hazardous chemicals will be stored in compliance with the Workers Compensation Board WHMIS guidelines, and in compliance with local municipal guidelines.
Waste chemical discharges will be monitored and will fall within the local municipal discharge limits.  Waste chemicals that cannot be discharged safely will be safely stored and disposed periodically using an approved chemical disposal services provider.
All accidental releases or spills will be controlled immediately and cleaned up by personnel knowledgeable of the hazards involved.  The spill control and clean-up will be done in accordance with all applicable regulations, including WCB and/or “Spill Reporting Regulation” of the Waste Management Act, if applicable.
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Technology Centre will take a leading role in ensuring the principles of care for the environment embodied in the slogan “Reduce Recycle Reuse” are implemented and adhered to in a responsible manner.

## Commissioning Sites
Responsibility for HSE at Commissioning Sites as set out in this Section 13 is assigned to the Manager of Process and Mechanical.

### Duties and Responsibilities of Manager of Process & Mechanical
The duties and responsibilities of the Manager of Process and Mechanical will include:
Maintains operating plant HSE procedures compliant with Aker Kvaerner HSE guidelines and guidelines of local regulatory authorities;
Designate HSE manager for commissioning works;
Supervises the commissioning HSE Managers throughout plant commissioning;
Ensures technical change does not impact safe operation of a plant;
Ensures that workplace and worksite hazard assessments are done;
Ensures HAZOP study is performed on all new plant designs;
Maintain Workplace Hazardous Materials Information System (WHMIS);
Reviews HSE management plans for each commissioning works.

### Hazard Operability Study (HAZOP)
A hazard operability study will be performed on all new plant designs (and design modifications) to insure that the plant has been designed to reduce to an acceptable level operability risk and risk to human health, and to comply with local environment and community regulations.
The HAZOP will be led by a qualified study leader using the HAZOP method recognized by the American Society of Chemical Engineers as detailed in:
Standard-1-PRC-0004 Hazard & Operability Studies
Standard-5-PRC-0005 Plant Layout Review

The staff responsibilities for health and safety management at a Company commissioning site  will be fulfilled by the Commissioning Manager; however, the Manager of Process and Mechanical may appoint a full-time HSE Manager for the site, or opt to have the construction HSE Manager continue through the commissioning phase or the project.
