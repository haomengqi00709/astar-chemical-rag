# 4-PRC-0003 — Procedure


[TABLE: 4-PRC-0003_table_1]


## Purpose
Project personnel will prepare documentation as described in this procedure to provide a record of equipment sizing and design, and to provide a complete description of technical requirements to be issued to vendors.
This procedure assigns responsibilities for document preparation and approval.

## Classification
Equipment is assigned one of five classifications according to the engineering required.  This assignment will be made by Mechanical, and is recorded on 4-LST-0001: Equipment List.

### Type A Equipment: (Vendor Standard Equipment)
Includes pumps, fans, agitators, plate heat exchangers, etc.
Design and manufacture are by vendor in accordance with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics documentation, referenced national standards, and vendor’s standard practices.

### Type B Equipment: (Process Packages; Vendor Proprietary Design)
The term “process package” is used to describe a collection of equipment that is specified to perform a singular overall process duty.
Design and manufacture are by vendor in accordance with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics documentation, referenced national standards, and vendor’s standard practices.  The vendor determines the duty and sizing of the equipment items within the package.  Examples are water or brine demineralizers, chlorine refrigeration units, acid plant blowers, etc.

### Type C Equipment: (Custom Fabricated Equipment; Vendor Design)
Includes tanks, pressure vessels, shell and tube heat exchangers, etc.
Detailed mechanical design, including any shop drawings and cutting plans required, and manufacture are by the vendor in accordance with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics documentation, referenced national standards, and vendor’s standard practices.

### Type D Equipment: (Custom Fabricated Equipment; Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Design)
Includes tanks, pressure vessels, shell and tube heat exchangers, reactors, etc., that are designed by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, often using specialised or proprietary methods.
The vendor will generally produce any shop drawings and cutting plans required, and will manufacture the equipment in accordance with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics documentation and referenced national standards.

### Type E Equipment: (Proprietary Equipment)
Includes items of proprietary design with significant after-market commercial value, and where details of the design are not disclosed to the user.
Classification of items as Type E requires the approval of the President.

### Outline Drawing
Outline Drawingswill initially contain dimensional criteria in the form of equipment elevations and plan views, and will contain itemised references to any required standard details provided on Detail Drawings.
Drawings for Type D equipment will contain fully dimensioned equipment elevations and plan views, and will contain itemised references to any required fabrication details provided on Detail Drawings.
Outline Drawings will contain dimensioned outlines in the form of equipment elevations and plan views that provide sufficient information to allow the design of surrounding and supporting facilities.
Outline Drawings will contain the following elements as applicable:
A virtual copy of the equivalent Equipment Data Sheet
Dimensioned equipment elevations and plan views
Internal components in outline
Itemised references to Detail Drawings
Plant elevation reference
Gasket dimensions
Equipment Part Numbers (EPN) as required.  See 4-PRC-0005:  Equipment Part Numbering.
Outline drawings are considered a “controlling document” for Aker Kvaerner Chemetics plant design:
Dimensions will be kept updated with any changes that may result from vendor detail design, or fabrication considerations
Outline Drawings will be used for Equipment manuals (Vendor drawings will not be used).
Outline Drawings will be prepared by Design
