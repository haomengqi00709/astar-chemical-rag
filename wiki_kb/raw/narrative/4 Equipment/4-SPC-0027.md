# 4-SPC-0027 — Specification


[TABLE: 4-SPC-0027_table_1]


## Purpose
This specification defines the minimum workmanship and quality requirements for duplex stainless steel welded fabrications purchased by/or on behalf of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics

## Scope
This specification applies when called for on purchase orders and referenced documents.
This specification covers the welding and other fabrication requirements for vessels, heat exchangers, tanks, piping heater coils and miscellaneous welded items having UNS designation S32205 (alloy 2205).
This specification is only valid when the minimum design metal temperature (MDMT) is greater than -30°C.

## General Requirements

### Good stainless steel welding and electrode handling practices will be used (good cleanliness, dry electrodes and fillers, iron contamination avoidance, and proper alignment and fitup, etc.)

### The 2205 stainless steel material will be fabricated in accordance with the recommendations of the alloy producer and fabricator, but the contents of this specification will take precedence.

### Completed welds will have their process exposed surfaces pickled/acid cleaned of all heat tint/oxides formed during welding.  Grinding or brushing is sufficient cleaning for non-process exposed welds and the pickling requirements is in addition to any grinding that may be required.

### All weld spatter, arc strikes and slag will be completely removed from the finished equipment.

### No end grain containing surfaces on the pressure envelope 2205 material will be exposed to the chemical process.  Nozzle neck pipe end grain must be covered, but vessel internal support ring edges do not require weld capping.

### When/if inert gas shielded wire welding processes are used, conservative torch and back-side shielding is extremely important, as oxygen pick-up strongly influences weld properties.

### Double sided welds will be used whenever possible.  All one sided chemical process exposed welds will be highlighted/clearly indicated on the fabricator’s drawings.

### All welding procedures used on the equipment will be submitted by the fabricator for review and agreement by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ Materials and Quality Assurance Group prior to commencement of any work (PQR and WPS to be included).
Autogenous welds are not allowed.

## Base Materials

### All product forms of Alloy 2205 base materials used in the fabrications will have minimum chromium and nitrogen contents of 22% and 0.15% respectively.  Only 2205 material certified as UNS S32205 will be acceptable.

### The 2205 material (all product forms) will be tested and found acceptable for intermetallic phase content by ASTM A923 (latest edition) or certified sigma phase free by the manufacturer.  The test results or certification statement will appear on the material’s mill test certificate.  All mill test certificates will be made available to the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics inspector.

### Welded 2205 heat exchanger tubing must have the weld bead cold worked before annealing to provide a fully recrystallized weld structure.  The manufacturer’s test certificate will show this and it must be made available to the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics inspector.

### 2205 plate materials thicker than 10mm will be Charpy V-Notch impact tested by the manufacturer and the lateral expansion results must indicate good impact loading toughness and ductility.  These results will be stated on the materials mill test report (or other suitable report) and made available to the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics inspector.

## Filler Materials

### SMAW and SAW flux coatings and fluxes will be “Basic” type only, to reduce oxygen pick-up in the weld metal.

### Welding consumables and filler metals used will be:
GTAW:	Alloy 2209
SMAW:	Alloy 2209 Basic Flux type
SAW:	Alloy 2209 Basic Flux Type
FCAW:	Alloy 2209 (Non Process-contact welds only)
Mill test certificates will be made available to the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics inspector.

### 309MOL filler material will be used for Alloy 2205 to carbon steel or Alloy 2205 to 300 series stainless steel welding.  The process exposed pressure envelope of the equipment will be constructed entirely of Alloy 2205 and 2205 poison pads will separate the pressure containing shell from all carbon steel and stainless steel welded attachments.

## Welding Process and Joint Requirements

### FCAW will not be used for any pressure retaining weld, as weld metal from this process has a potentially inferior chemical composition.  All FCAW welds will be clearly indicated on the fabricator’s drawings.

### Only basic coated electrodes will be used in the SMAW welding process.

### If the SAW process is utilized, the basic flux will be recommended by the alloy and consumable manufacturers for optimum corrosion resistance, and its technical details will be provided to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for review.

### Straight stringer beads will be used and the maximum interpass temperature is 150°C.

### Weld heat input is critical to achieving the correct weld metal ferrite/austenite phase balance and duplex stainless steel alloys require more heat for an acceptable weld than other stainless alloys.  As a guide, the fabricator will use heat inputs in the 20-24 kJ/cm range, subject to approval by the alloy and welding consumable supplier.  The fabricator must tailor the heat input with material thickness, with higher heat inputs better for thicker sections and lower ones better for thinner sections.
Maximum interpass temperature is 150°C.
Preheat is not required.  Work may be heated prior to welding only sufficiently to drive off moisture.

## Weld Inspection and Testing

### All welds are to be 100% LPE inspected per the ASME pressure vessel code.

### All pressure retaining welds will be subject to a minimum of spot radiography per the ASME pressure vessel code.

### Magnetic ferrite testing will be performed on a representative sample of pressure retaining welds to ensure the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics inspector that weld ferrite contents are within the 30 - 60% range.  The exact number and locations of the tests are to be agreed with the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics inspector.

### The fabricator will provide Aker Kvaerner Chemetics with welded test coupons during fabrication that have been produced in an identical manner to the equipment’s primary pressure envelope welds.  The use of run out tabs for the coupons is preferred.  The weld coupons will be used for corrosion and metallurgical evaluations.  The coupon type and number are to be agreed with the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics inspector.
