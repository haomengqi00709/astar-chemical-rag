## Purpose
This specification defines Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ requirements for welding and associated procedures for vessels, equipment and pipe spools made of austenitic stainless steel in the ASME P-No. 8 classification.

## Codes and Standards
4-SPC-0040: Shop Fabricated Metallic Vessels and Metallic Vessel Components lists the codes and standards for design and fabrication.

## Special Requirements
When specified, pickling and/or passivation will be performed in accordance with approved cleaning and passivation procedures.  In general, passivation of stainless steel will conform to the requirements of ASTM A380.

## Welding Process

### General
The following processes are acceptable:
Shielded Metal Arc Welding	(SMAW)
Gas Tungsten Arc Welding	(GTAW)
Gas Metal Arc Welding	(GMAW)
Submerged Arc Welding	(SAW)
Plasma Arc Welding	(PAW)
Flux Core Arc Welding	(FCAW)

### Shielded Metal Arc Welding
SMAW electrodes will satisfy the requirements of SFA-5.4.
Heat input will be minimized by using the stringer bead technique.

### Gas Tungsten Arc Welding
GTAW rods will satisfy the requirements of SFA-5.9.
Weld metal thickness will not exceed 3mm (⅛") per pass.
Heat input will be minimized by using the stringer bead technique.
Gas backing is required where second side welding is not carried out.

### Gas Metal Arc Welding
GMAW electrodes will satisfy the requirements of SFA-5.9 or SFA-5.22.
GMAW will be used for the root pass of butt welds only when fill passes are to be made from both sides.
Gas backing of the root pass is required when GMAW is used to fill a butt weld from one side.
The Vendor must provide satisfactory evidence of continuous shop use of the short circuiting arc process if it is to be used.

### Submerged Arc Welding
SAW electrodes will satisfy the requirements of SFA-5.9.
Weld metal thickness will not exceed 13mm (½") per pass.
Run-on and run-off tabs of the same material specification as the base material will be used on all longitudinal seams.
The Vendor must provide satisfactory evidence of continuous shop use of this process.
A chemical analysis of the finished deposit must be included as part of the weld procedure qualification.
Filler metal type ER310 and ER320 will not be used with this process.
Since the flux choice is critical, any change in flux will require that a chemical analysis of the finished deposit be performed and submitted to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

### Plasma Arc Welding
PAW electrodes will satisfy the requirements of SFA-5.9.

### Flux Core Arc Welding
FCAW electrodes will satisfy the requirements of SFA-5.22.
A chemical analysis of the finished deposit must be included as part of the weld procedure qualification.
Only electrodes supplied by Kobe, ESAB, Sandvik, Lincoln and Air Liquide are acceptable.  Electrodes from other producers may only be used if written approval is given by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics before work is started.
The Vendor must provide satisfactory evidence of continuous shop use of this process.

## Preheat & Inter-pass Temperature
Preheat is not required.
Heat input during welding will be kept to a minimum.  Inter-pass temperature will not exceed 150°C (300°F).

## Inspection and Testing

### Hydrostatic Test
Water used for testing austenitic stainless steel equipment will contain less than 50 ppm chlorides.
After testing, equipment will be either wiped dry or blown dry with air.

### Radiographic Examination
Piping will have 5% random radiography as defined in ASME B31.3 paragraph 341.4.1(b) as a minimum.
Shop fabricated pipe made from sheet or plate will have one spot radiograph at each longitudinal seam.  No additional radiography of the longitudinal seam is required unless defects are revealed when the pipe is cut into smaller lengths.
When RT results are inconclusive, welds will be liquid penetrant or ultrasonic examined.

### Liquid Penetrant Examination
The following welds will be examined for 25% of the total weld length for each welder or welding process or both:
Cover pass of nozzle attachment welds.
Cover pass of butt welds.
Cover pass of skirt to shell welds.
The following welds will be examined for 100% of their length:
Root pass and cover pass of dissimilar metal welds.
Tube to tubesheet welds.
Weld repair surfaces including weld built-up excavated areas.
For each pipe size, ¼ of the production welds will be 100% examined.
