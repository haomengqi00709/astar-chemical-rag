# 4-SPC-0046 — Specification


[TABLE: 4-SPC-0046_table_1]


## Purpose
The purpose of this Standard is to define and design, fabrication, erection and quality requirements for the procurement of shop fabricated sulphuric acid storage tanks.

## Scope
This Standard together with the drawings, purchase order and referenced documents, covers the design, fabrication, field erection and quality requirements for welded steel storage tanks for sulphuric acid in the 93-98% concentration range.
Sulphuric acid is a hazardous fluid which must be safely confined in a tank of unquestionable integrity.  The materials specified herein are compatible with the acid only at specific combinations of temperature and concentration which must be carefully defined in advance.
For this reason, consultation with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' Quality Assurance and Materials Engineering Group will be necessary before utilizing this specification for sulphuric acid service.
When phenolic coating of the internal tank surface is required this will be so noted on drawings etc., and requirements will be covered in a separate document.
Where required - API, ASTM, ASME, Federal, State and Local regulations will apply.

## General
In cases where this Standard and/or Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' drawings conflict with API and all other applicable codes, this Specification and Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' drawings will govern.  However, in all cases, Federal State & Local laws and regulations must be maintained.
All site conditions, drawings, specifications/Standards and instructions will be examined by the manufacturer and any errors, disagreements or inconsistencies will be reported in writing to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics before any physical work has started in the shop or at the site.
No part of this contract will be sublet without Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' prior approval.
Deviations from this Standard are acceptable only as approved in writing by the designated Aker Kvaerner Chemetics engineer.
The quality of welding required must be achieved by rigid  adherence to the specified procedures.  Welding slag is not compatible with sulphuric acid and therefore it is essential that all welds be free of slag and any defects which could cause a leak.  There will be no compromise made as to poor fit-up, inadequate dryness and cleanliness or other conditions that may adversely affect the welding.
The manufacturer must select welders who are qualified as per paragraph 10.4.2 and who are consistently able to produce this high quality of work to the satisfaction of the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' inspector.
The manufacturer will ensure that a copy of this Standard will be available at all times for consultation in the shop and field.
All precautions must be taken to prevent contamination of plates by oil or other hydrocarbons.  This precludes the use of vehicles, including cranes, aerial buckets, etc., over floor plates before they are welded.
If the requirements of this Standard are not followed, Aker Kvaerner Chemetics reserves the right to stop work and any costs thus incurred will be at the expense of the manufacturer.

## Foundation
The foundation to be built by others according to a separate specification, in general, will be of well-compacted granular fill with a concrete wall pit below the bottom outlet nozzles.  Copies of the foundation drawings may be obtained on request.

## Materials
All materials used in the manufacture of tanks conforming to this Standard will be to API 650 and detailed on the drawing and/or purchase order.  Where steel to ASTM A36 or A285 is specified, it will be made using other than rimmed or capped steel practices.
The manufacturer will provide Aker Kvaerner Chemetics with one copy of all mill test certificates for plates before fitting and rolling and for other materials before shop or field work starts.

## Design
The manufacturer is responsible for the design of the tank except as specified otherwise on the purchase order.  The basic design will be in accordance with API Standard 650 Appendix "D" although the API approval is not required.  Overall dimensions and other construction details of the tank will be as shown on Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' drawings attached to the request for quotation/purchase order.
The design shell thickness will be computed on the following basis:
The tank is filled with sulphuric acid of a given specific gravity as specified.
The nominal corrosion allowance will be 1/8 inch.  Tanks which are to be coated or protected internally will not require a corrosion allowance.
In no case will the total shell thickness (structural plus corrosion allowance) be less than 3/8 inch, or greater than 1 1/4 inch.
Annular ring plates will have a thickness of at least one-half of the nominal design thickness (including corrosion allowance) of the lowest shell course, but in no case will they be less than 1/2 inch in thickness.  This thickness includes corrosion allowance.  The annular ring in all cases will be at least 4 feet wide.
Bottom floor plates will be a minimum thickness of 1/2 inch.  This thickness includes a corrosion allowance.
The bottom of the tank will slope in agreement with the foundation's drawings, generally 1" per 10 feet, cone up.
Floor and first course shell plates containing openings will be shop fabricated.  The welds will be radiographed in accordance with Section 6 of API 650, and the results and repairs will meet the requirements of Sections 5 and 6 of API 650.
All nozzles and manholes will be seal welded inside the tank.
The neck of a side manhole will be a minimum 3/4 inch thickness.
Roof inlet nozzles will project 6 inches inside the roof and be located at least 12 feet from the shell as per referenced drawings.
The roof will be self-supporting and designed for a minimum of 25 psf.
Roof construction will be double lap or double butt welded.  The joint between the roof plates and the eave angle will contain no cavities and will be seal welded flush on the inside of the tank.  Internal support ribs will be seal welded on the inside of the tank and external support ribs will be designed to prevent any hold-up of water, ice or snow.
Supporting columns, if required, will not be less than 5/16 inch wall thickness.  The bottoms of columns will be welded to support plates with a continuous seal weld.  Support plates will be circular in plan and of a sufficient size and thickness to distribute the column load within the bearing capacity of the foundations.
Supporting columns and support plates will have a nominal corrosion allowance of 1/8 inch minimum.
The manufacturer will certify in writing that tanks built to this specification comply with all applicable laws and regulations of the local authority having jurisdiction.  This also applies to such appurtenances as access stairs, ladders, handrails, etc.
The tank will be equipped with an overflow nozzle located in the top of the shell such that the full height of the tank can be utilized, a manhole in the first course of the shell and nozzles as shown on the drawings.

## Tolerances
The tank fabricator will check the foundation levels before and during erection and will report any changes from the established datum.  Any damage to the surface of the foundation will be repaired by the tank fabricator at his own expense, to match the original foundation in design and materials.
All tolerances regarding plumbness, roundness, peaking, banding and the foundations of the tank will be in accordance with API 650 Paragraph 5.5.

## Welding

### General
The following processes are the preferred methods of welding:
Shielded Metal Arc Welding	(SMAW)
Gas Tungsten Arc Welding	(GTAW)
Gas Metal Arc Welding	(GMAW)
Submerged Arc Welding	(SAW)
The manufacturer's welding procedures complete with qualification records must be submitted for review by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics prior to construction.

### Process

#### Shielded Metal Arc Welding
Unless specifically requested otherwise by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics all shielded metal arc welding of carbon steel will be done using low hydrogen type electrodes; e.g., E7018, which satisfy the requirements of SFA-5.1.
Direct current, reverse polarity is preferred.

#### Gas Tungsten Arc Welding
Gas Tungsten Arc Filler Rods will satisfy the requirements of SFA-5.18.
Preferred electrode is thoriated tungsten as per SFA-5.12, Class EWTh-2; ground to a sharp point.
Weld metal thickness will not exceed l/8"/pass.  Heat input should be kept low by using stringer bead technique.
Where necessary a back-up gas will be used to prevent root pass oxidation and contamination.

#### Gas Metal Arc Welding
Solid wire electrode will conform to SFA-5.18 classification ER70S-2 or ER70S-6.
For flux cored arc welding, electrodes will conform to SFA 5.20, classification E60T-8, E70-Tl, E70-T5 or E70T-6.  A chemical analysis of the weld metal will be provided with the P.Q.R.
Fabricator must provide satisfactory evidence of continuous use of this process.
A minimum of two passes is required on all thickness’.
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics will be advised formally of the location of all flux core welded seams prior to commencement of welding.
This process will only be used when approved in writing by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

#### Submerged Arc Welding
SAW electrodes and fluxes will satisfy the requirements of SFA-5.17.
Maximum weld deposit per pass will be limited to l/2".
Starting and run off pads of the same material as the base material will be used on all longitudinal seams.
The flux and wire combination used on production welds will be the same as that used for weld procedure qualification.
Wire spools will be tagged for easy shop identification.
Wire and flux combinations recommended for single pass welding will not be used for multiple pass welds.
A chemical analysis of the finished deposit must be included as part of the weld procedure qualification.
Fabricator must provide sectioned samples and satisfactory evidence to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics of continuous shop use of this process in the fabrication of Low Temperature, high carbon or high Manganese steels.

### Preheat

#### Preheat & Interpass Temperature
Preheat and interpass temperatures will be in accordance with the applicable codes or specifications and the parameters shown in the reviewed welding procedure(s).
These temperatures will be controlled by the use of temperature indicating crayons, contact pyrometers or other suitable means.
Preheat will be used to remove condensation from the weld joint area and adjacent (2"-3") material.

### Fabrication
Preheating and storage of electrodes will conform to the requirements set forth by the electrode manufacturer.  In addition, the fabricator will ensure that low hydrogen electrodes are dry and warm immediately prior to use by means of portable electrically heated containers.
All welders will be qualified on procedures for all position welding on plate up to the maximum thickness used in the tank, in accordance with Section IX of the ASME Boiler and Pressure Vessel Code (latest edition), prior to fabrication.
All welds will be clearly identified with the welder's identification number on the outside of the tank adjacent to the welds.
Surface and edges to be welded will be smooth, uniform and free from fins, tears, cracks and other defects which adversely affect the quality or strength of the weld.
Welding surfaces will be dry and protected from rain, snow and strong winds.  If the temperature of the metal surfaces is below 350F (20C), the base metal within 3 inches of the place where the welding will start will be preheated to a temperature warm to the hand, in accordance with Section 5.2 of API Standard 650.
Construction of the floor of the tank will be as follows:
Radial joints between annular ring plates and between annular ring and bridge plates will be double butt welded with full penetration.
If the annular ring plates or the bridge plate is a different thickness than the bottom plate, the joint between the plates will be double butt welded with full penetration and the inside surface will be flush.
Bottom plates will be joined by either one of the following methods:
Double butt welds with full penetration.
Butt weld with full penetration using 2 inch wide, 1/4 inch thick backing strips.
In the second case, where bottom plates are butt welded using backing strips, the backing strips will be maintained firmly in contact with the underside of the bottom plates at all points and properly centered on the joints.  They will be field tack welded to one plate before the second plate is fitted.  Care will be taken to ensure that there are no gaps at joints between adjacent strips and that there is a solid backing for the weld at all points.
Bottom plates will be prepared with single "V" grooves and, before welding, all grooves will be wire brushed and vacuum cleaned to remove all foreign material.  All welds have a minimum of four layers of weld metal to minimize the danger of a leak path developing.  The root gap before welding will be 1/8 inch minimum, 1/4 inch maximum.
The bottom-to-shell joint will have full penetration welds with reinforcing fillets and will be balanced to minimize cocking or tilting.
Horizontal and vertical joints between shell plates will have full penetrating butt welds.  Plates 5/8" or over in thickness will have double V-grooves.  Thinner plates will have single V-grooves and will be completed with a back gouge and final pass from the inside of the tank.  Reinforcement will be within the welding procedure and API specifications.
All erection lugs, brackets, arc strikes, craters, etc. must be removed to leave a uniform full thickness surface, free from possible stress raisers, or crevices where dilute acid condensation can collect.

## Quality Control
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or its representative will have access to work at all times for inspection purposes to ensure compliance with this specification, approved drawings, purchase order, etc.  The manufacturer will provide adequate notice and facilities for inspection.
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics inspection does not relieve the manufacturer of responsibility for the proper execution of the work.
After the bottom plates have been fitted and tack welded, no further welds on the bottom will be carried out until accepted by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or its representative.
The manufacturer will provide and schedule radiographic inspection as follows:
All vertical welds in all courses will be 100% radiographed.
All T-joints between horizontal and vertical shell welds will be radiographed.
Horizontal welds between shell courses will be spot radiographed as specified in API Standard 650, Paragraph 6.1.2 with at least eight radiographs per horizontal seam.
All radial welds on annular ring and bridge plates will be 100% radiographed.
All T-joints on double butt welded floors, if such construction is  used, will be radiographed as well as 5% of the remaining floor welds.
At least  one radiograph will be made randomly of the work of each welder after he has completed his first 10 feet of welding in each position.
Tanks to be internally protected with a phenolic coating will be subject to the spot radiography requirements as outlined in Section 6 of API 650.
The manufacturer will retain all radiographic results, unless required by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, for a period of at least 5 years, after which the manufacturer will ascertain whether Aker Kvaerner Chemetics wants the radiographs for its files.
Radiograph locations will be marked temporarily by paint, crayon or similar means on the tank.  A permanent record of locations will be provided to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
Hydrostatic testing of the tank is not required.  Other tests as specified under Section 5.3 of API Standard 650 will be carried out.

## Clean-Up
All interior steel surfaces including structural members, will be blast cleaned to SSPC-SP6, broom swept and vacuum cleaned to remove all residual scale, slag, rust and other residue after erection and immediately prior to making final connections to product lines and services.  If all the connections are not ready to be made by others, the openings will be blind flanged after the vacuum clean-up.
Tanks to be internally protected with a phenolic coating will be blast cleaned to SSPC-SP5 (white metal finish).

## Drawings And Records
Copies plus sepias of the manufacturer's shop and erection drawings as required by purchase orders as well as two copies of the welding procedures, will be submitted to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for review before work commences.  Two copies of the design calculations will also be submitted for review.  One reviewed copy of each will be returned to the manufacturer.
The manufacturer will supply copies plus sepias of final certified drawings in a form suitable for micro-filming.
Review by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics of the manufacturer's drawings, design calculations and procedures will not relieve the manufacturer of any responsibility for the proper and efficient design of the tank or for checking of the drawings.
On completion of work, the manufacturer will supply Aker Kvaerner Chemetics with two sets (in booklet form) of the following quality records:
Mill Test Reports (Paragraph 7.2)
Non-Destructive Examination reports including location of radiographs
P.Q.R. form (QW484) for all welders used
Certificate of tests carried out per API 650, Section 5.3

## Painting
All external surfaces of shop fabricated tanks, including floor, will be primed in accordance with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' Standard ES44\C-03.  For site fabricated items, all steel will arrive at the site unpainted unless specifically noted otherwise elsewhere.

## Nameplates
The fabricator will affix to all equipment a nameplate, to be supplied by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. Maximum size of this nameplate is 6½" wide x 4" high.
All nameplates will be securely fastened with stainless steel bolts or stainless steel rivets, to a bracket welded to the vessel as shown on Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' drawings.
