# 4-SPC-0044 — Specification


[TABLE: 4-SPC-0044_table_1]


## Scope
This Standard covers the manufacture, testing, inspection and supply of glass-lined carbon steel reaction vessels including agitated and jacketed or split pipe coil types to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Inc.
The vessels shall be manufactured in accordance with the requirements of the ASME Boiler & Pressure Vessel Code, Section VIII, Division 1, the Manufacturer’s standard procedure for glass-lined vessels, and the contents of this document.
In the event of conflict between the Codes and Standards, in all cases, the more stringent requirement shall govern.
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' Packaging and Shipping Standards ES44\S-01 & \S-02 are applicable to all glass-lined equipment.

## General Requirements
All codes, Standards, etc., referred to herein, shall be interpreted as meaning the latest published revisions of such references at time of quotation.
The Fabricator shall be responsible for obtaining the approval of design and/or fabrication and registration of pressure equipment by the appropriate jurisdiction.  The cost of external inspection services that may be required to obtain these approvals shall be included in Fabricator’s proposal.
Vessel type, Engineering Standard and designation shall be provided for each vessel by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. Fabricator’s suggested alternative materials shall be approved in writing by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics prior to use.
Fabricator’s proposal shall contain the following specific information:
Type of glass-lining proposed.
Minimum thickness of vessel lining.  The absolute minimum thickness of glass-lining shall be 35 (890 microns) mils, achieved by that number of layers (including ground coat) necessary to obtain optimum adhesion and thermal properties.  The following items shall have a minimum glass thickness of 30 mils (760 microns): Blind Flanges, Agitators, Baffles, Covers, Dip-pipes, Spargers Internal Valves and Instrumentation.
On-site spark testing by Supplier.
Refer also to paragraph 13.1.

## Materials

### General
The materials of construction shall be those defined in the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' approved vessel engineering drawings.  No substitution of materials, including internals is permitted without approval in writing from Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
All materials must be clearly identified in accordance with ASME Code Section VIII, Div. 1.  Lack of adequate identification of flanges and fittings, etc., shall be cause for rejection.
The acid resistant glass-lining shall be of the highest quality.  The coating shall therefore be continuous and complete, without cracks or flaws and applied on a prepared, clean surface.  In the case of nozzles, the glass-lining shall be continuous over the gasket face.

### Certification
Certification is required for all materials used in the manufacture of Vessels and Agitators, including Welding Consumables, except that Agitator drive components are exempted.

## Design

### Responsibility for Design
Fabricator is responsible for the design and he shall, unless stated otherwise, submit within three weeks of the receipt of an order two sepias of detailed approval drawings and one copy of design calculations all in accordance with the Purchaser's order, this Specification and any standards or drawings which the Purchaser may supply as references. Manufacture shall not commence until the Purchaser has approved, in writing, the Fabricator’s drawings. Fabricator shall allow, in his programme, two weeks for this approval per vendor data requirements, schedule attached.
No deviation from these requirements shall be permitted without the written agreement of the Purchaser.

### Design Temperature and Pressures
Unless otherwise stated on the Purchase Order/Vessel Data Sheet, the design temperatures and pressures shall be as follows:
Temperatures:	From -250C to 2300C  (-130F to 4460F)
Pressures:	Up to 140 psig (965 kPag) or full vacuum in the vessel, coincident with up to 55 psig (380 kPag) or full vacuum in the jacket. Vendor shall be consulted if operation outside these limits is contemplated.
Note: Upper temperature limit shall be reduced to Vendor’s recommended safe design temperature if upper limit of maximum continuous temperature can affect the performance of the PTFE enveloped gasket.

### Design Code
The vessel shall be designed and fabricated in accordance with the ASME Boiler and Pressure Vessel Code, Sec. VIII, Div. 1 (latest edition).
A Design Code other than that specified above may be used only after written agreement between the Purchaser and Fabricator.
The jacket and vessel compartments on jacketed vessels must be designed to independently withstand the design temperature and the design pressure stated for the jacket and the vessel on the individual vessel drawings.  Jacketed vessels shall also satisfy the requirements of Appendix IX of Section VIII, Division 1, ASME Code.
Cooling or heating jackets shall be supplied as called for on the vessel drawings.  Inspection openings shall be located in the jackets as required by ASME Code.  These openings shall be 2-inch (50mm) minimum size, with flanged cover, suitable gaskets, studbolts, and nuts.  Impingement baffles shall be located opposite the jacket nozzles.
Each jacket shall include a spiral baffle or special inlet nozzles which will produce a spiraling effect.  Water or steam will be circulated through the jackets.  The design flow rate shall be given in the vessel data sheets or drawings.
Jacket closing welds are to be in accordance with the ASME Code, Section VIII, Div. 1, Appendix IX, requirements.

### Corrosion Allowance
In the non-jacketed portion of the vessel shell, the glass-lining provides the corrosion allowance; therefore, no extra corrosion allowance is required.
For jacketed vessels a 1/16" (1.5mm) corrosion allowance shall be used on outside of vessel shell (jacketed section) and on inside of jacket.
Steam condensate outlet nozzles on jackets shall have 1/8" (3mm) corrosion allowance.  A 1/16" (1.5mm) corrosion allowance shall be used for all other jacket nozzles.

### Wind and Earthquake Loading
The earthquake and wind loads to be used for design shall conform to the appropriate National Standards.  Earthquake zone shall be specified on data sheet and/or vessel drawings.

### Vessel Heads
The top and bottom heads for all vessels shall preferably be ellipsoidal. Where necessary, openings in the top head shall be arranged to permit maintenance access to mixer and driver.  Bottom heads shall have a minimum number of openings.

### Nozzles and Manholes
Except for the radial manhole nozzle, all nozzles in the vessel heads shall be Fabricator‘s standard vertical or radial type nozzles. Gaskets shall be provided to match 10K JIS flanges.  The flanges may be split or solid type.
Gaskets in contact with glass surfaces shall be suitable for design pressure service without excessive flange bolt loading.
The external loads shall be kept to an absolute minimum on vessel nozzles.
Each vessel shall have an 18" (450mm) circular manhole in the top head, unless otherwise called for on data sheets.  The necessary swing-clamps to secure the joint against the design pressure shall be included.

### Gaskets
Gaskets shall be non-graphite containing.
A machined or channel type envelope gasket style is acceptable but filler (asbestos or Vendor standard) must be approved by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. Elastomer fillers and slit style gaskets with heat sealed I.D.'s are not acceptable.
All gasket material in contact with glass surfaces shall be made of Teflon (Virgin PTFE).  Teflon enveloped gaskets shall satisfy the requirement of ASTM F336 as modified above.

### Baffles and Supports
When internal baffles are required, Fabricator shall provide information on the number, width and materials required.  This information shall be shown on the drawing or data sheet for each vessel. The method of attachment shall be specified in the quotation.
Internal supports, such as clips, brackets, studs, bolts, plates, nuts and washers, shall be designed and supplied by the Vessel Fabricator Vendor. Suitable materials for the above items shall be shown on the data sheets or drawings.
Normally, each vessel shall be supported by either (a) lugs, (b) continuous ring, or (c) saddles.  The Vessel Fabricator shall design the support and furnish the design calculations if agreed previously between Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and Supplier.
Vessel fabricator shall provide insulation support details as called for on the vessel drawings.

### Nameplate
A stainless steel nameplate attached to each vessel by a bracket projecting beyond any insulation shall be furnished by Manufacturer. The nameplate shall be located immediately below the vessel manhole and show the item number, service and minimum data required by ASME Code and the appropriate Provincial Department of Labour in Canada or National Board in US.  In addition, a Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' supplied nameplate as shown in Appendix B shall be attached to the same bracket.  The vessel shell shall not be stamped unless otherwise specified.

## Fabrication

### Fabrication Tolerances
Vessel tolerances shall be in accordance with ASME Code, Section VIII, Div. 1 and as outlined in Paragraph 13 of this Specification.  In case of conflict between ASME Code and Paragraph 13, the contents of Paragraph 13 shall govern.

### Forming and Assembly
Heads which have a central hole as a result of manufacture, shall have it completely welded and radiographed to ASME Code, Section VIII, Div. 1 Paragraph UW-51, unless a nozzle is to be welded to the head at this location.
All nozzles or manway openings in the shell or heads shall be swaged.
Plate edges for welding shall be prepared by shearing, machining, grinding or flame cutting.  Plate thickness greater than 1¼" (31mm) shall be preheated prior to flame cutting.
The maximum thickness of non-bevelled plate shall not exceed 5/16" (6mm).
Each shell section shall be completely welded longitudinally and corrected for out of roundness and peaking of the weld seam prior to assembly.
All rerolling or forming of the shell sections shall be completed prior to any radiographic examination.
For vessels equipped with close fitting internal parts (baffles, etc.) all nozzles that may obstruct the entrance into the vessel or assembly of such parts shall be flush and ground to a smooth curvature suitable for the application of the glass-lining.
All flange bolt holes shall straddle the centre line of the vessel.

### Welding

#### General
All welding procedures for pressure retaining parts shall be reviewed by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics prior to fabrication.  The ASME IX P.Q.R. Form 483 shall form part of the welding procedure package.  The procedures typically used by Fabricator shall be provided to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for record purposes.
All longitudinal and circumferential seams shall be double butt welded with full penetration.
Internal structural parts of non-pressure retaining parts in alloy materials which require welding shall be welded to an approved welding procedure by qualified personnel.
Welding shall be performed in dry, clean surroundings.
Backing strips shall not be used, except as allowed on jacket closing welds.

#### Weld Processes
Shield electric arc processes shall be used with the following restrictions:
Automatic Submerged Arc Welding
Maximum weld deposit per pass shall be limited to ½" (12mm).
Starting and run-off pads of same material as base material shall be used on all longitudinal seams.
The flux and wire combination used on production welds shall be the same as used in the procedure qualifications.
Submerged arc welding procedures shall utilize the consumable manufacturer's recommended wire and flux combinations, which do not result in excessive manganese or silicon build-up in the weld.
Wire and flux combinations recommended for single pass welding shall not be used for multiple pass welds.
Wire spools shall be tagged for easy, in shop, identification.
Shield Metal Arc Welding
The electrode used shall be the same type and brand as used in the qualification procedure.
Once electrode cans are opened, the electrodes shall be stored in a cabinet heated in accordance with the manufacturer's recommendations.
Wet electrodes shall be discarded.
Back welding of screwed fittings is not permitted.
Electroslag, Electrogas, Short Arc & Fluxcore
These processes shall not be used unless approved in writing by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.  The Pfaudler and De Dietrich organizations have this approval.

## Non-Destructive Testing (Prior to Glassing)

### Radiography
Vessels (excluding jackets) fabricated to this Standard shall as a minimum be spot radiographed to Paragraph UW-52 of ASME Code, Division 1, Section VIII.
All radiographs taken during fabrication shall be available for examination by the Inspector or authorized representative.
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics reserves the option to review final radiographs prior to glassing of vessel.  This review will be performed in such a way that normal shop production activities will not be affected.
If considered necessary, spot radiography or some form of alternate N.D.T., shall be performed on selected jacket butt welds.  Jacket closing seams shall also be spot radiographed where access is possible.

### Dye Penetrant (P.T.)
Welds on internal components fabricated from corrosion resistant metallic materials shall be 100% P.T. inspected to ASME Code, Section V.
NOTE:	Welding repairs required after radiography or P.T. inspection shall be re-inspected in accordance with the requirements of this specification.

## Finish of Equipment Prior to Glass-Lining
All surfaces to be lined shall be smooth and all corners shall be to the radii specified on the drawing.
Welds shall be thoroughly inspected and repaired where necessary and the vessel normalized prior to lining.
The surfaces to be lined shall then be grit blasted and visually examined for defects by Vendor.  Surface defects shall be ground out and where necessary repaired by welding.  Weld repairs shall be thoroughly inspected by the non-destructive method used for the original weld.  A final grit blasting or mechanical grinding shall be carried out prior to lining.

## Application of Glass-Lining
The lining shall consist of:
a layer or layers (ground coat(s)) to provide a strong metal to glass bond, on top of which shall be
highly corrosion-resistant cover coats.  Application of the constituent coatings shall be carried out by the Wet Spray Process or the Hot Dust Process or a combination of the two.

## Quality of Glass-Lining

### Grade
The lining shall be the best grade of chemical and thermal shock-resistant glass, currently available, suitable for an operating temperature range of minus 25C (-13F) to 230C (446F) and shall be either free from defects or within the specified plugging standard (see Table in Para. 10.4). Fabricator shall be consulted if operation outside this temperature range is contemplated.  The Purchaser's Inspector shall ascertain from Fabricator, the grade of glass used and shall record this information on the Glass-lining Inspection Record shown as Appendix A to this Standard.

### Thickness
The thickness of the lining shall be within the limits specified in Paragraph 12.3 and shall be recorded in Appendix "A".

### Test Data
The Fabricator shall, on request, provide the Purchaser with test data giving the resistance of the grade of glass to be used for the Purchaser's order against the following conditions:
Impact, hot caustic soda solutions, boiling hydrochloric acid, boiling water/steam and sudden temperature changes or permissible temperature differentials.
Sulphuric and nitric acid mixtures at boiling temperatures.

## Tantalum Plugging of Defects

### Number, Size and Location
The number, size and location of any proposed repairs to defects exposed under the spark test and which are outside the scope of the agreed Standard shall be referred to the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

### Nozzle Repairs
Nozzle repairs utilizing a metallic liner are unacceptable and fall outside the scope of this specification.

### Repairs to Linings
Before carrying out repairs to the lining which are outside the scope of the agreed Standard, the Supplier shall inform Aker Kvaerner Chemetics of the number and position of the defects and the type of repair proposed including the composition of any cement to be employed.  Aker Kvaerner Chemetics will indicate approval or otherwise to the proposals in writing.
Plugs will normally be of tantalum with PTFE sealing washers either of the single or three-piece type.  Silicate cement shall be used in oxidizing environments e.g. nitric acid.  This cement should not be used in alkaline solutions where the pH exceeds 9.

### Defect-free Locations
The following locations shall be defect-free:
Covers for nozzles of:
24" and 20" (600 & 500mm) diameter and 18"x16" (450 x 400mm) manholes
18"x14" (450 x 350mm) inspection openings
30", 36", 38", 40" and 42" (750, 800, 850, 900, 950mm) diameter openings
Dished heads of two-piece vessels of 420 gal. (1600 Litres) and below:
Agitators (standard configuration through 4000 gal. (15,000 Litres) vessel capacity)
Baffles
Thermowell nozzles
Flange surfaces
Radii of 3" (75mm) and below
Agitator nozzles
Bottom outlet nozzles
Nozzles 6" (150 mm) nominal size and below

### Minimum Spacing
There shall be a minimum of 4" (100mm) of undamaged glass between the edges of adjacent plugs.

### Position of Plugs

## Pressure Testing
The hydrostatic test pressure for the glassed vessel shall be the maximum allowable working pressure in accordance with the ASME Code, Section VIII, Paragraph UG-99 (k) of Division 1.  The jacket shall be hydrotested at its design pressure with the vessel empty.
No vessel or its component parts shall be given a proof hydrostatic test (Paragraph refer UG 101) unless approval is obtained in writing from Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.  If a proof test is necessary, it shall be witnessed in its entirety by the Project Engineer or his designate.
All cover plate hinge pins shall be in place during the test and shall not bind on the hinges.
Gaskets and bolting that are to be supplied with the vessel may be used when pressure testing the vessel.  A spare set of gaskets for all flanges shall be supplied with the vessel.
The use of shellac, glue, compounds, lead, etc., on gaskets is not permitted.
Welded attachments provided with tell-tale holes shall be pneumatically tested with 10 psig (68 kPag) air prior to heat treatment and/or hydrostatic test. Tell-tale holes shall not be plugged during the final hydrostatic test of the vessel.
The temperature of water used for hydrostatic tests shall be maintained at 60F (15C)  throughout the testing cycle, in accordance with the intent of the ASME Code.
In the case of vessels containing mixers, the Fabricator shall assemble the vessel mixer and drive system for final hydrotest at the design pressure.  The mixer shall be operated during the hydrotest for a period of one hour (continuous) at a speed that will not overload any part of the mixer system (preferably maximum agitator speed).
Upon completion of the hydrotest and running test, the vessel lining shall be Holiday-tested prior to shipment to site.
All hydrostatic tests shall be made in the presence of an Authorized Inspector and with his approval.
The release of a vessel by the Inspector shall not relieve the Fabricator of responsibility and does not alter the conditions of the guarantee.
The tests shall be recorded and test certificates shall be supplied to the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ Inspector.

## High Voltage Spark Testing and Visual Examination of Glass-Lining in Fabrication Shop

### High Voltage Testing
The lining, other than where ground, shall be tested for continuity and shall show no metal contact when the test probe, at a voltage of 17 kV dc peak, is moved twice over the glass surface.  For ground surfaces the voltage shall be reduced to l0 kV dc.  A defect is indicated by a spark or a visual/acoustic signal.
Auxiliary components - e.g. agitators, baffles, etc., shall be tested at l5 kV. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics shall be advised of the timing of these tests.

### Visual Examination
After completion of the hydrostatic test, the glass shall be visually examined for defects by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. The lining shall show a smooth uniform surface free from defects such as:
Hairlines caused by excessive local thermal stresses during firing. In particular, lines caused by fully or partially forced-up ground coat or by cracked ground coat and in consequence forced-down cover coat, shall not be present. The detection of hairlines is facilitated by a light held obliquely to the glass surface.
Haircracks.
Chipping or flaking.
Contamination by scale and fire brick. In the case of the latter:
Separate particles to a maximum of three for up to and including 680 gallons (2500 litres) vessels and a maximum of five for vessels of 1100 gallons (4100 litres) and over, are permitted provided that their length, which must be parallel to the glass surface, is not greater than .125" (3mm). Particles must be isolated by a distance of 24" (600mm) for those having a maximum length of .125" (3mm), and 12" (300mm) for particles of .062" (1.5mm) maximum length.  Particles smaller than .062 (1.5mm) shall be permitted in greater number but must also satisfy the 12" (300mm) spacing requirement.
The result of the Visual Examination shall be entered on the Glass-lining Inspection Record (Appendix A).

### Glass Thickness
The thickness of the lining shall be measured using a non-destructive technique; e.g. a magnetic thickness gauge, at regular intervals appropriate to the equipment under examination.  The glass-lining applicators procedure for performing this operation shall be reviewed by the purchaser.
The thickness shall be free from any sudden variations and shall be within the following limits:
Vessel surfaces general 35-90 mils (890 - 2300 microns).
Agitators, baffles, thermowells, pipes and radii in vessels up to and including 4" (100mm) 30-90 mils (760 - 2300 microns).
Ground surfaces, e.g. agitator shaft in way of gland or mechanical seal 30-90 mils (700 - 2300mm).
The thickness range measured shall be recorded and the locations of any areas outside the limits shall be specified on the Glass-lining Inspection Record shown as Appendix A.

### Silicate Cement
Silicate cement shall be used in conjunction with the tantalum plug when repairing vessels for nitrating environments.

### Statiflux Test
This test is used to show up the existence of hairline cracking using dry magnesium carbonate powder.  This test should be used if there is reason to believe the lining may have suffered mechanical damage.

## Cleaning and Protection Against Corrosion and Damage in Transit and Storage
The unlined surfaces shall have all loose scale and rust removed and a protective primer coating shall be applied.  All machined surfaces shall be coated with temporary metal protective, after cleaning as necessary.  Set screws and washers or plastic protective caps shall be fitted in tapped holes for corrosion protection.  Jackets shall be completely emptied to prevent damage due to freezing in cold weather.
The Supplier shall ensure that the completed items are adequately protected against damage in transit and suitable for possible long periods of storage in the open.  Vessels with open tops shall be adequately protected in store by fitting a robust wooden cover.  All joint faces and parts such as agitators, stuffing boxes, mechanical seals and thermowells shall be provided with adequate protection to prevent damage in transit.  Loose backing flanges shall be either secured to their respective nozzles (van stone type) or shipped separately.  All nozzle flanges shall be protected by caps of rubber, plastic or gasketted plywood.
Installed vessels shall have a suitable external top or finish coating to prevent the ingress of corrodents, particularly acids to the metal surface.  The type of coating shall be agreed between Vendor and Purchaser.
See also Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' Packaging & Shipping Specifications ES44\S-01 and ES44\S-02.

## Quality Assurance Documentation
The Supplier shall prepare three sets of Q.A. documentation for each vessel.  These packages shall consist of:
Mill Test Certification
Calculations
Welding Procedures
Affidavit of Manufacture
Report of major repairs prior to glassing
Spark Testing Report identifying plug locations
Dimensional Report including glass surface inspection and thickness
Inspection/Shipping Release Form
The Aker Kvaerner Chemetics inspector or authorized representative shall review each package for completeness and accuracy prior to acceptance.

## Inspection on Arrival at Jobsite
All glass-lined items shall be inspected on arrival at the site.  This inspection shall normally comprise a thorough visual examination by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics supplemented by a spark test performed by the Supplier at a voltage of 5 kV A.C. maximum.  Any proposed deviations to the spark test shall be reviewed and approved by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.  The result of the spark test shall be recorded on the Receiving Report.  If it is not carried out, the Report shall be endorsed to this effect.
Any defects shall be repaired by the Supplier, to an agreed method, free of charge.
