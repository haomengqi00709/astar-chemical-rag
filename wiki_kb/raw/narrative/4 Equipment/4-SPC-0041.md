# 4-SPC-0041 — Specification


[TABLE: 4-SPC-0041_table_1]


## Purpose
This Specification defines Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ manufacturing requirements for radial flow gas/gas heat exchangers.

## Machining
The inside edges of all tube holes in tubesheets and baffles will be free of burrs.

## Fabrication
All welds in tubesheets and expansion plates will be radial butt welds.
As an aid to fit up prior to welding, the tubes may be roller expanded into the tubesheet with taper rollers, using a degreased tool and no lubricant.  Tack welding of tubes to tubesheets is not permitted.
Prior to welding all tubesheet holes, faces, and tube surfaces within 50mm (2”) of the weld will be degreased with a solvent such as acetone or isopropyl alcohol
Expansion of the tube to tubesheet joint after welding in not permitted.
