- Core OD | ID | = | 1e-05 | mm
- Poisson's Ratio | n | = | 0.305
- Unit Load | q | = | 1.0 | N/mm^2
- 2) | CALCULATIONS
- Plate Outside Radius | a | = | 0.5*OD
- = | 5e-06 | mm
- Plate Inside Radius | b | = | 0.5*ID
- = | 5e-06 | mm
- Geometry Constants | C1 | = | 0.5*(1 + n)*(b/a)*ln(a/b) + 0.25*(1 - n)*(a/b - b/a)
- = | 0.0
- C3 | = | 0.25*(b/a)*(((b/a)^2 + 1)*ln(a/b) + (b/a)^2 - 1)
- = | 0.0
- C7 | = | 0.5*(1 - n^2)*(a/b - b/a)
- = | 0.0
- C9 | = | (b/a)*(0.5*(1 + n)*ln(a/b) + 0.25*(1 - n)*(1 - (b/a)^2))
- = | 0.0
- L11 | = | (1/64)*(1 + 4*(b/a)^2 - 5*(b/a)^4 - 4*((b/a)^2)*(2 + (b/a)^2)*ln(a/b))
- = | 0.0
- L17 | = | 0.25*(1 - 0.25*(1 - n)*(1 - (b/a)^4) - ((b/a)^2)*(1 + (1 + n)*ln(a/b)))
- = | 0.0
- Unit Shear Force @ Core | Qb | = | q*a*(C1*L17 - C7*L11)/(C1*C9 - C3*C7)
- = | 7 | N/mm
- Unit Shear Force @ Shell | Qa | = | abs(Qb*b/a - q/(2*a)*(a^2 - b^2))
- = | 7 | N/mm
- Reaction @ Core | Fb | = | Qb*2*p*b
- = | 7 | kN
- Reaction @ Shell | Fa | = | Qa*2*p*a
- = | 7 | kN
- Fraction Carried by Core | db | = | Fb/(Fa + Fb)
- = | 7
- Fraction Carried by Shell | da | = | 1 - db
- = | 7

- COMMENTARY
- This spreadsheet calculates the distribution of axial load between the shell and core of a converter.  Load distribution is calculated
- per Roark Table 24, case 2c (annular plate under uniform load).  The results are used to determine the proportion of weight and
- pressure loads carried by the shell and the core.
