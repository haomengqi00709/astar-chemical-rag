- COMMENTARY
- This spreadsheet calculates the distribution of axial load between the bottom vestibule and core.  Load distribution is calculated
- per Roark Table 24, case 2c (annular plate under uniform load).  The resultant axial load on the core (f2) is used to check the
- cone to cylinder junction in the bottom vestibule (see 4-PRC-0009: Gas/Gas Exchanger Mechanical Calculations).
