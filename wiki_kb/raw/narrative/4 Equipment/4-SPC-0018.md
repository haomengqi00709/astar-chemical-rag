# 4-SPC-0018 — Specification


[TABLE: 4-SPC-0018_table_1]


## Purpose
The purpose of this Standard is to define the workmanship and quality requirements for heat resistant  UNS S30815 welded fabrications purchased by/or on behalf of .

## Scope
This Standard applies when called for on purchase orders and referenced documents.
This Standard covers the welding and other fabrication requirements for vessels, heat exchangers, tanks, piping and miscellaneous welded items in the ASME P8 Material Classification Group 2.

## Definitions
Codes, etc., will be taken to mean latest edition ruling at date of contract.
Agreement in Writing - Where prior approval in writing is required by this Standard, this will be taken to mean submission in writing by letter and not merely by inclusion in approval drawings.
Inspector -  Quality Assurance Inspector or other Authorized Representative.

## General Requirements

### Sub-Contracting
No Sub-Contracting of welding is permitted without  written approval.

### Supervision
Fabricator will ensure that there is regular and systematic supervision of all work both in-shop and on-site by competent staff.

### Procedure Submittal
All welding procedures will be submitted by Fabricator for review and agreement by  Materials and Quality Assurance Group prior to commencement of any work.  The ASME Section IX PQR Form 483 will form part of this package.
A change in essential variables as outlined in ASME Code. Section IX will require re-qualification and re-submission of procedure(s).

### Welders Qualifications
All welders to be employed on  work will be suitably qualified in accordance with relevant codes and standards for every procedure he is to use.
Fabricator will, upon request, supply  with objective evidence of current welder qualifications.
All qualified welders will be allocated an identification symbol which will be identified on parent material adjacent to his work.

### Cleanliness
The surface and edges of any austenitic steel which is to be heated for any purpose (e.g. fusion cutting, forming or welding) will be cleaned free from all traces of foreign matter (e.g. marking paint, oil, etc.) by grinding, wire brushing with a stainless steel brush and/or non-chlorinated solvent cleaning immediately prior to the application of heat.
Fabricator will take the controls necessary to prevent contamination of weldments at all stages of fabrication.  These controls will include the segregation of wire brushes, grinding wheels, etc., for use on stainless steels only.
On completion of work, items will be cleaned internally and externally. All fabrication markings, oxidation and ferroxyl testing, weld spatter, etc., will be removed prior to shipment.
Passivation, where required by Purchase Order/referenced documents, will be carried out in accordance with written procedures which will be approved by  prior to use.

### Drawings
All work will be carried out in accordance with the  "Approved For Construction" shop detail drawing(s).  Deviations from the weld geometry as shown on approved drawing(s) are not permitted without the prior approval of ' Q.A. & Materials Group.

## Materials

#### Base Materials
All materials used will be as specified in the purchase order and referenced documents and conform to ASTM/ASME Codes.

### Filler Materials
All weld filler metals or consumables will be Avesta 253 MA®

#### Certification
Mill Test Certification will be supplied with all materials including welding consumables.

### Traceability
The fabricator will maintain full traceability of materials back to original mill certification.
As a minimum the fabricator's internal controls will be such as to prevent the use of unspecified materials.
Lack of adequate identification or certification will be cause for rejection of the specific component or item.

## Preparation Of Edges For Welding

### Cutting to Size
Material will be cut to size by:
Machining
Shearing
Material over  thick will have extra material left on sheared edge to allow machining, grinding, etc., to ensure that any defects caused by shearing are removed. Material over  thick will not be sheared.
Thermal Cutting
It is preferable that thermal cutting will be carried out by the Plasma Arc Process and the welding edges lightly ground to bright metal.  Any other process will require that on completion of thermal cutting a minimum of  of metal be removed by mechanical means to ensure that all slag, scale, other irregularities and contaminants, including thermal effects, have been removed.
Grinding

### Forming
Material will be cold formed as far as practical.  When hot forming is necessary, the procedure to be adopted will be agreed to in writing by  prior to commencement of work.  A solution anneal will be necessary after the hot working operation.

## Welding Processes

### Welding Methods
The following processes are the preferred methods of Other process will be evaluated on submission of full details including procedure and exact location of proposed weldments.
Gas Tungsten Arc Welding	(GTAW)
Gas Metal Arc Welding	(GMAW)

### Gas Tungsten Arc Welding
Preferred electrode is thoriated tungsten as per SFA-5.12, Class EWTh-2.
High frequency (HF) voltage will be used to start the arc.
Weld metal thickness will not exceed pass.  Heat input will be kept low by using stringer bead technique.
Back-up gas (normally 100% argon with 99.9% purity) will be used to prevent root pass oxidation and contamination.  This requirement is mandatory for root pass welds on piping or other items where access to the internal weld surface is limited.

### Gas Metal Arc Welding
GMAW will only be used for the root pass of butt welds and for fillet welds unless specifically agreed otherwise.  Gas backing (argon) is required for root pass welds where accessibility to the internal weld surface for grinding etc. is not possible.

### Preheat, Interpass Temp. & Postweld Heat Treatment
Preheat is not required.  However, when adverse weather conditions give rise to condensation or wetness on the surfaces of the component, the material will be dried prior to welding by heating to a temperature just sufficient to prevent condensation.
Heat input during welding should be kept to a minimum.  Interpass temp. should not exceed .
Postweld Heat Treatment is seldom required.  In the event that PWHT is specified the fabricator will submit his procedure in writing for approval prior to commencement of work.  This procedure will include information on the number and location of thermocouples and supports.

## Workmanship

### General

#### Procedure Tests
The welding procedure will be agreed upon (see Para 5.3) before any production welding is begun.

#### Welding Position
As far as practical, all welds will be made in the downhand (flat) position.
Vertical welds will be made vertical up (uphand).

#### Weather Protection for Welding on Site
Suitable enclosures will be provided to keep welds and welders free from draughts, rain or other inclement weather.  Welding will not be performed on wet surfaces.

#### Fusion Faces
The fusion faces and the adjacent metal, both internal and external, will be free from moisture, scale, paint, grease or other foreign matter immediately prior to welding.

### Alignment of Joints

### Assembly for Welding
To maintain the specified alignment and gap (where used) during welding, the parts to be welded will be securely held in position by mechanical means, welded-on bridge pieces or by tack welding.  Reinforcing rods and other shaped items will closely conform to their matching curvature.

#### Mechanical Means
Where a jig or other suitable device is used, the construction of the jig will allow adequate access for welding and cleaning and will be such that external loading is not applied to a joint during welding.

#### Bridge Pieces/Support Spiders
Welded-on bridge pieces or support spiders will be of 253 MA on the surface welded in contact with the vessel.
Where welded-on bridge pieces or support spiders are used, the plate surface will not be left in a damaged condition after they are removed.

#### Tack Welds
Tack welds employed for assembly purposes may form part of the completed weld provided that complete fusion is effected at the root of the weld and that the welding procedure used is the same as that employed for the remainder of the root run.
Cracked and unsound tack welds will be cut out prior to welding.
Sound tack welds must be thoroughly fused into the root run of the main weld. It is sometimes necessary to thin down the ends of tack welds to make sure this is possible.
Where this is necessary tack welds should preferably be at least one inch long to ensure that the remaining part of the tack weld is strong enough to hold the plate or pipe in place.

#### Backing Bars
The use of permanent back-up rings or strips or consumable joint inserts is not permitted unless specifically approved in writing by  Q.A. and Materials Group or shown on the  Drawings.

### Striking of Welding Arc
Every effort will be made to confine arc strikes to the surface on which weld metal is to be deposited.  If such a strike should occur outside this area the spot will require to be ground, PT examined per Para. 10.3 and, if necessary, repaired. Burn marks from grounding clamps will be treated as arc strikes.

### Cleaning and Re-Starting
Each run of weld metal will be thoroughly cleaned and all slag and foreign matter removed before the next run is deposited.  On re-starting, care will be taken to ensure proper fusion with the weld metal previously deposited.

### Electrode Touch Down
If a tungsten electrode accidentally makes contact with the work, the section of weld and the tip of the electrode will be examined.  Where tungsten loss has occurred, the tungsten pick-up in the weld will be ground out and the electrode reground to the correct profile before re-starting.

### Peening and Hammering
Peening of weld runs or hammering of finished welds is not permitted except for light peening to remove slag.

### Form of Welds
Each weld will be uniform in width and size throughout its full length.  Each layer of welding will be smooth and free of slag, inclusions, cracks, porosity, lack of fusion, and undercut except to the extent permitted in the referenced codes.  In addition, the cover pass will be free of coarse ripples, irregular surface, non-uniform bead patterns, high crown, and deep ridges or valleys between beads.
Buttwelds will be slightly convex, of uniform height, and have full penetration.
Crevices will not be permitted on the process side of completed weldments.
As far as is practicable, fillet welds will be deposited in not less than two layers and their surfaces will be free from marked irregularities. Dimensions of fillet welds will be in accordance with the approved drawing.

### Dressing of Welds & Repair Areas
Grinding or gouging will not be carried out in such a manner as to reduce the adjacent base metal thickness.
Grinding wheels will be iron-free and of the resin bonded alumina or silica type.
Wire brushes or chipping tools will be made of austenitic stainless steel and must not have been previously used to clean surfaces other than stainless steels.

## Quality Control

### General
The Inspector will have free access to Vendor's work and to his Subvendor's, if work is sublet by approval, at all reasonable times.
He will be at liberty to inspect the work during fabrication at any stage in manufacture and to reject any material or workmanship, which does not conform to this Standard or the applicable code.
Inspection by  does not relieve Fabricator of responsibility for providing materials, components and workmanship in accordance with the requirements of this Standard, engineering design and the applicable code.

### Chemical Analysis
Supplier must make available to purchaser's inspector copies of chemical certification for batch of electrodes or wire coils used.  If electrodes, wire, etc., cannot be identified to ' satisfaction, samples will be removed for chemical analysis.  This will be to supplier's account.

### Dye Penetrant Inspection (PT)
Unless otherwise specified in the purchase order, approved drawings, specifications/codes, etc., all welds will be PT examined 100%, as far as is possible.
The PT exam will be carried out in accordance with a written procedure and in general, the limit of acceptability will satisfy the ASME Code Section VIII, Division 1, Appendix VIII, except that all linear indications will be considered unacceptable.
All excavated areas will be weld built-up flush with the surface of the base material using the welding procedure previously approved by  for the main welding details.
Weld repair surfaces will be PT examined per the above requirements.
reserves the right to accept or reject any doubtful or questionable indications.

### Radiographic Inspection  (Rt)
Where specified on ' Engineering drawings, items fabricated in accordance with this Standard and the ASME Code will be spot radiographed in accordance with Para.  UW-52(b) of Section VIII, Division 1 of the ASME Code which clearly defines the following requirements:
Minimum extent of Spot Radiographic Examination.
Standards for Spot Radiographic Examination.
Evaluation and Retests.
For piping fabricated in accordance with ASME Codes and this Standard, the minimum standard of radiography will be 5 percent random radiography as defined in Para.  341.4.1(b) of ASME B31.3.
RT Exam will be carried out in accordance with a written procedure by personnel qualified to appropriate national standards.
Where additional spot radiography is required, as defined in UW-52 Sub-para.  (d)(2), costs of this radiography including all repair costs and re-radiography will be to the fabricator's account.
Where more than one welder or welding operator has been used, spot radiography will be performed in such a manner that the welding of each welder or welding operator can be examined.
Junctions between circumferential and longitudinal seams will be considered as preferred locations for spot radiography.
