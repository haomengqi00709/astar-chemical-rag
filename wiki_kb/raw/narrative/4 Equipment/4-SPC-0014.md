# 4-SPC-0014 — Specification


[TABLE: 4-SPC-0014_table_1]


## Purpose
The purpose of this Standard is to define the workmanship and quality requirements for Columbium Stabilized High Nickel Austenitic stainless steel welded fabrications purchased by/or on behalf of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.  For unstabilized alloys refer to Appendix A.

## Scope
This Standard applies when called for on purchase orders and referenced documents.
This Standard covers the welding and other fabrication requirements for vessels, heat exchangers, tanks, piping and miscellaneous welded items in the ASME P-45 Material Classification. (Stabilized)
Alloys such as Carpenter 20 Cb-3 or Nicrofer 3620 fall into the above classification.
For unstabilized P45 alloys such as 904L & 254 SLX refer to Appendix "A".

## Definitions
Codes, etc., shall be taken to mean latest edition ruling at date of contract.
Agreement in Writing - Where prior approval in writing is required by this Standard, this shall be taken to mean submission in writing by letter and not merely by inclusion in approval drawings.
Inspector - Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Quality Assurance Inspector or other Authorized Representative.

## General Requirements

### Sub-Contracting
No Sub-Contracting of welding is permitted without Aker Kvaerner Chemetics written approval.

### Supervision
Fabricator shall ensure that there is regular and systematic supervision of all work both in-shop and on-site by competent staff.

### Procedure Submittal
All welding procedures shall be submitted by Fabricator for review and agreement by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics QA & Materials Group prior to commencement of any work.  The ASME IX PQR Form 483 shall form part of this package.
The procedure shall be qualified on the same grade of material as that selected for fabrication.
A change in essential variables as outlined in ASME Code Section IX will require resubmission of procedure(s).

### Welders' Qualifications
All welders to be employed on Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' work shall be suitably qualified in accordance with relevant codes and standards for every procedure he is to use.
Fabricator shall, upon request, supply Aker Kvaerner Chemetics with objective evidence of current welder qualifications.
All qualified welders shall be allocated an identification symbol which shall be stamped on parent material adjacent to his work.  A weld map(s) may be substituted for stamping, subject to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics approval.

### Cleanliness
The surface and edges of any austenitic steel which is to be heated for any purpose (e.g. fusion cutting, forming or welding) shall be cleaned free from all traces of foreign matter (e.g. marking paint, oil, etc.) by grinding, wire brushing with a stainless steel brush and/or non-chlorinated solvent cleaning immediately prior to the application of heat.
Fabricator shall take the controls necessary to prevent contamination of weldments at all stages of fabrication.  These controls shall include the segregation of wire brushes, grinding wheels, etc., for use on stainless steels only.
On completion of work, items shall be cleaned internally and externally. All fabrication markings, weld spatter, heat tinting, slag, etc., shall be removed prior to shipment.
Passivation, where required by Purchase Order/referenced documents, shall be carried out in accordance with a Cleaning and Passivation Procedure approved by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. In general passivation of stainless steel shall correspond to the requirements of ASTM A380.
Ferroxyl testing where required, shall require similar procedural approval by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

### Drawings
All work shall be carried out in accordance with the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics "Approved for Fabrication" shop detail drawing(s).  Deviations from the weld geometry as shown on approved drawing(s) are not permitted without the prior approval of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

## Materials

### Base Materials
Plate, sheet, pipe, tube and forging shall be purchased in the stabilize-annealed condition (UNS N08020) to ASME SB463, SB729 or SB462.
Plate and sheet shall be supplied in the hot rolled, annealed and descaled condition for maximum corrosion resistance unless specified otherwise on the purchase order.

### Filler Materials
All weld filler metals shall meet the requirements of Section 8 of this Standard (Refer to Appendix 'A' for Non Stabilized Grades).
Fabricator shall ensure and demonstrate to the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' representative that the manufacturers' instructions regarding the storage, drying and use of weld consumables are being fully implemented both in the shop and on-site; in particular that all wet electrodes are discarded and not baked and re-used.
Fabricator shall prior to commencement of work, furnish Aker Kvaerner Chemetics with the brand name of the filler materials actually to be used.

### Certification
Certification showing the results obtained from analysis and other tests carried out in accordance with the relevant material specification shall be supplied to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for all materials including welding consumables.

### Traceability
Fabricator shall maintain full traceability of materials back to original mill certification.
As a minimum, Fabricator’s internal controls shall be such as to prevent the use of unspecified materials.
Lack of adequate identification or certification shall be cause for rejection of the specific component or item.

## Preparation Of Edges For Welding

### Cutting to Size
Material shall be cut to size by:
Machining
Shearing:
Materials over 3/l6" thick shall have extra material left on sheared edge to allow machining, chipping, etc., to ensure that any defects caused by shearing are removed. Material over l" thick shall not be sheared.
Thermal Cutting: It is preferable that thermal cutting shall be carried out by the Plasma Arc Process and the welding edges lightly ground to bright metal.  Any other process shall require that on completion of thermal cutting, a minimum of l/8" of metal be removed by mechanical means to ensure that all slag, scale, other irregularities and contaminants, including thermal effects, have been removed.
Grinding - See also Para 9.9.

### Forming
Materials shall be cold formed as far as practical.  When hot forming is necessary, the procedure to be adopted shall be agreed to in writing by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics prior to commencement of work.

## Welding Processes

### Welding Methods
The following processes are the preferred methods of welding Columbium stabilized austenitic steels to ASME P45 classification.  Other processes will be evaluated on submission of full details including procedure and exact location of proposed weldments.
Shielded Metal Arc Welding	(SMAW)
Gas Tungsten Arc Welding	(GTAW)
Gas Metal Arc Welding	(GMAW)
The general welding parameters of current, voltage, electrode size, etc., as well as special requirements or precautions, should be obtained from the electrode supplier.

### Shielded Metal Arc Welding
Shielded metal arc welding electrodes shall satisfy the requirements of SFA-5.4.
The electrodes shall be Columbium stabilized Grade E320 or E320 LR to ASME F5 classification.
Use of Type l5 for normal fillet and butt welds and Type l6 for weld overlay is recommended.
Minimum practical amperage, stringer beads rather than weave and minimum arc length are recommended in order to minimize heat input.
Single sided welds shall have the root protected by gas backing during welding.
Gas Tungsten Arc Welding
Gas Tungsten Arc Welding Rods shall satisfy the requirements of SFA-5.9.
The filler wire diameter shall be kept small preferably l/l6" and shall be Columbium stabilized Grade ER320 or ER320 LR to ASME F6 classification.
Preferred electrode is thoriated tungsten as per SFA-5.12, Class EWTh-2; ground to a sharp point.
High frequency (HF) voltage shall be used to start the arc.
Direct current straight polarity shall be used (D.C.S.P. - Electrode negative).
Weld metal thickness shall not exceed l/8"/pass.  Heat input shall be kept low by using stringer bead technique.
Single sided welds shall have the root protected by backing gas during welding.

### Gas Metal Arc Welding
GMAW shall only be used for the root pass of butt welds and for fillet welds unless specifically agreed otherwise.
Direct current reverse polarity shall be used (D.C.R.P. -Electrode positive).
Fabricator must provide satisfactory evidence of continuous shop use of this process in the fabrication of austenitic stainless steel.  The use of ER320 LR filler metal is mandatory and the mode of transfer must be stated.

### Submerged Arc Welding
Use of this process must be reviewed and approved in writing by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics prior to start of fabrication.
Since the flux choice is critical, a chemistry check of the finished deposit must be included as part of the weld procedure qualification.
Fabricator must provide sectioned samples and satisfactory evidence of continuous shop use of this process in the fabrication of the austenitic stainless steel.

### Preheat, Interpass & Postweld Heat Treatment
Preheat is not required.  However, when adverse weather conditions give rise to condensation or wetness on the surfaces of the component, the material shall be dried prior to welding by heating to a temperature just sufficient to prevent condensation.
Heat input during welding should be kept to a minimum.  Interpass temp. should not exceed 200o F.
Postweld Heat Treatment is seldom required.  In the event that PWHT is specified, Fabricator shall submit his procedure in writing for approval prior to commencement of work.  This procedure shall include information on the method of water quenching and the number and location of thermocouples and supports.

## Workmanship

### General
Procedure Tests. The welding procedure shall be agreed upon (see Para 5.3) before any production welding is begun.
Welding Position. As far as practical, all welds shall be made in the downhand (flat) position. Vertical welds shall be made vertical up (uphand).
Weather Protection for Welding On-site. Suitable enclosures shall be provided to keep welds and welders free from draughts, rain or other inclement weather.  Welding shall not be performed on wet surfaces.
Fusion Faces. The fusion faces and the adjacent metal, both internal and external, shall be free from moisture, scale, paint, grease or other foreign matter immediately prior to welding.

### Alignment of Butt Joints.
The alignment of welding edges at butt joints shall be in accordance with relevant code, specification, etc., but maximum values for misalignment (High - Low) shall be as follows:
For plates up to and including 1/4" in thickness, 1/16"
For plates above 1/4" in thickness, 25% of the plate thickness or 1/8" whichever is smaller.
For pipe, shall be in accordance with ASME B31.3.
For Converters and Gas/Gas Exchangers, 20% of the plate thickness or 1/16", (1.5mm), whichever is smaller.
Prior to welding a slip-on flange, the sum of the gaps on any diameter shall not exceed 3/16" (5mm).

### Assembly for Welding.
To maintain the specified alignment and gap (where used) during welding, the parts to be welded shall be securely held in position by mechanical means, welded-on bridge pieces or by tack welding.  Reinforcing pads and other shaped items shall closely conform to their matching curvature.
Mechanical Means. Where a jig or other suitable device is used, the construction of the jig shall allow adequate access for welding and cleaning and shall be such that external loading is not applied to a joint during welding.
Bridge Pieces/Support Spiders
Welded-on bridge or support spiders shall be of austenitic steel on the surface welded in contact with the vessel or heat exchanger.
Where welded-on bridge pieces or support spiders are used, the plate surface shall not be left in a damaged condition after they are removed.
Tack Welds
Tack welds shall be performed by ASME code qualified welders.  Tack welds employed for assembly purposes may form part of the completed weld provided that complete fusion is effected at the root of the weld and that the welding procedure at the root of the weld and that the welding procedure used is the same as that employed for the remainder of the root run.
Cracked and unsound tack welds shall be cut out prior to welding.
Sound tack welds must be thoroughly fused into the root run of the main weld. It is sometimes necessary to thin down the ends of tack welds to ensure that complete fusion is achieved.
Where this is necessary, tack welds should preferably be at least one inch long to ensure that the remaining part of the tack weld is strong enough to hold the plate or pipe in place.
Backing Bars
The use of permanent back-up rings or strips or consumable joint inserts is not permitted unless specifically approved in writing by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics QA. & Materials Group.

### Striking of Welding Arc
Every effort shall be made to confine arc strikes to surfaces on which weld metal is to be deposited.  If such a strike should occur outside this area the spot will require to be ground, PT examined per Para 10.3 and, if necessary, repaired. Burn marks from grounding clamps shall be treated as arc strikes.

### Cleaning and Restarting
Each run of weld metal shall be carefully examined to ensure that it has been thoroughly cleaned.  All slag and foreign matter shall be removed before the next run is deposited. This may be achieved by either grinding or chipping followed by wire brushing. On restarting, care shall be taken to ensure proper fusion with the weld metal previously deposited.

### Electrode Touch Down
If a tungsten electrode accidentally makes contact with the work, the section of weld and the tip of the electrode should be examined.  Where tungsten loss has occurred, the tungsten pick-up in the weld shall be ground out and the electrode reground to the correct profile before restarting.

### Peening and Hammering
Peening of weld runs and hammering of finished welds is not permitted except for light peening to remove slag.

### Form of Welds
Each weld shall be uniform in width and size throughout its full length.  Each layer of welding shall be smooth and free of slag, inclusions, cracks, porosity, lack of fusion, and undercut except to the extent permitted in the referenced codes.  In addition, the cover pass shall be free of coarse ripples, irregular surface, non-uniform bead patterns, high crown, and deep ridges or valleys between beads.
Buttwelds shall be slightly convex, of uniform height, and have full penetration.
Crevices shall not be permitted on the process side of the weld.
As far as is practical, fillet welds shall be deposited in not less than two layers and their surfaces shall be free from marked irregularities. Dimensions of fillet welds shall be in accordance with the approved drawing.

### Dressing of Welds & Repair Areas
Grinding or gouging shall be carried out in such a manner as not to reduce the adjacent base metal thickness.
Grinding wheels shall be iron-free and of the resin bonded alumina or silica type.
Wire brushed or chipping tools shall be made of austenitic stainless steel and must not have been previously used to clean surfaces other than stainless steels.

## Quality Control

### General
The Inspector has free access to the Vendor's work and to his Subvendor's, if work is sublet by approval, at all reasonable times.
He shall be at liberty to inspect the work during fabrication at any stage in manufacture and to reject any material or workmanship which does not conform to this Standard or the applicable code.
Inspection by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics does not relieve Fabricator of responsibility in accordance with the requirements of this Standard, engineering design and the applicable code.
Material found to be unsatisfactory shall be rejected or repaired to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics satisfaction, at Vendor’s cost, despite any prior approval of material or certification.
Dimensional tolerances shall be as stated on Aker Kvaerner Chemetics drawing or equipment specification.  In the absence of such a reference, Aker Kvaerner Chemetics ESDO/001 shall apply.

### Chemical Analysis
Supplier must make available to purchaser's inspector copies of chemical certification for batch of electrodes or wire coils used.  If electrodes, wire, etc., cannot be identified to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' satisfaction, samples shall be removed for chemical analysis.  This shall be to Supplier’s account.
When required by purchase order, a chemical analysis shall be performed on production welds of critical equipment such as pressure vessels, heat exchangers, pressure piping and certain proprietary items.  This shall also include non-pressure retaining equipment in dangerous or demanding services.
The weld(s) to be checked shall be selected at random by the Inspector.
Where results of chemical analysis show that a specific weldment falls outside of the approved specification or code requirements, Supplier shall promptly advise Aker Kvaerner Chemetics in writing of his proposed course of action.  Such action shall include details of any additional testing necessary to fully define the extent of the non-conformance.  All repairs and retests shall be to Supplier’s account.

### Dye Penetrant Inspection (PT)
Items fabricated in accordance with the ASME Code Section VIII, Division l, shall be 100% PT examined on all welds.
Unless otherwise specified in the purchase order, approved drawings or specifications, non-coded items shall have butt and fillet welds, nozzle attachment welds including skirt to shell welds, etc., PT examined at least for 25% of each welder/process used, for cracks, porosity, slag inclusions, significant undercutting or other major defects.  It should be noted that in the case of piping, 25% refers to l00% PT examination of 25% of the production welds in each pipe size.
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics shall reserve the right to select the location of the above inspection.
Unless otherwise specified in the purchase order or approved drawings, all pressure welds between dissimilar metals shall be l00% PT examined.
Tube to tubesheet welds shall be l00% PT examined.
The PT exam shall be carried out in accordance with a written procedure and in general, the limit of acceptability shall satisfy the ASME Code Section VIII, Division 1, Appendix VIII, except that linear indications shall be considered unacceptable.
All excavated areas shall be weld built-up flush with the surface of the base material using the welding procedure previously approved by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for the main welding details.
All weld repair surfaces shall be l00% PT examined per the above requirements.
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics reserves the right to accept or reject any doubtful or questionable indications.

### Radiographic Inspection (RT)
Equipment fabricated in accordance with the ASME Code shall have all butt welds 100% radiographed in accordance with Para. UW-5l of Section VIII, Division 1 of the ASME Code where the plate or shell thickness at the welded joint(s) exceeds 3/8".
Equipment fabricated to the ASME Code, but not exceeding 3/8" plate thickness shall be spot radiographed in accordance with Para. UW-52(b) of Section VIII, Division I.
For piping fabricated in accordance with ASME Codes and this Standard, the minimum standard of radiography shall be random radiography as defined in Para. 334.4.1 (b) of ASME B31.3, except that 20% of all butt welds shall be radiographed.
For tankage fabricated in accordance with API Standards 620 and 650 and this Standard, the minimum requirements shall be spot radiography.
Items other than those described above, fabricated to this Standard only (i.e. Non-Code), shall if required by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics engineering drawings be spot radiographed in accordance with UW-52 (b) or 20% random radiographed to B31.3 in case of piping.
10.4.6.RT Exam shall be carried out in accordance with a written procedure by personnel qualified to appropriate national standards.
Where additional spot radiography is required, (as typically defined in UW-52 Sub-para. (d2)), costs of this radiography including all repair costs and re-radiography shall be to Fabricator’s account.
Junctions between circumferential and longitudinal seams shall be considered as preferred locations for spot radiography.

### Helium Mass Spectrometer Test
When required by purchase order or referenced drawings tube/tube sheet joints and tube bundles shall be tested using the helium mass spectrometer (reverse probe method) per ASME Code Section V, Article 10.

### Documentation
Fabricator shall furnish a report containing all the data pertaining to each vessel or item as follows:
Prior to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' final inspection of each unit, Fabricator shall have prepared in finished form, three sets of records, except final test data, as set forth in this paragraph.
Material mill test reports
Nameplate rubbing
Hydrostatic test report
A copy of all inspection and test reports
These reports, together with final test data, shall be assembled into three complete sets for each item.  In no case shall a documentation package pertain to more than one vessel or item number.
The Inspector will at the time of witnessing the final tests and certifying correctness of forms, collect the appropriate number of documentation packages for each item tested.
