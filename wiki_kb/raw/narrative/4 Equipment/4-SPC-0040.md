# 4-SPC-0040 — Specification


[TABLE: 4-SPC-0040_table_1]


## Purpose
This specification defines Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ requirements for the shop fabrication of metallic process vessels (e.g. pressure vessels, shell and tube heat exchangers, atmospheric storage tanks) and metallic vessel components.
Structural components (e.g. skids, bases, ladders, platforms) are exempt from the requirements of this specification.  These components will be fabricated in accordance with the structural welding code specified in the equipment requisition or purchase order.

## Design
Provision will be made for skirt venting where no other suitable openings exist.
Reinforcing pads will be provided with a ¼” NPT hole located at the lowest point on the pad.
Where a split reinforcing pad is necessary, the weld will be oriented parallel to the circumferential direction of the shell.
The corrosion allowance specified on drawings or data sheets will be added to:
both sides of all fixed internals that come in contact with the process fluid;
one side of shell and tube heat exchanger baffles;
calculated anchor bolt diameter.
The corrosion allowance will not be added to removable internals or flange faces.
All category A & B joints (as defined in the ASME Code) will be full penetration butt welds.  Permanent backing rings or consumable joint inserts are not permitted.
Longitudinal seams of adjacent courses will be offset a minimum of 75 mm (3”) or 5 times the material thickness, whichever is greater.
All wall, floor and roof joints will be full penetration welds.
All welds on supports will be continuous (i.e. not stitch welds)

### Certification and Traceability
Mill test certificates are required for all materials, including weld consumables.  Mill test certificates must show the country of origin of the primary material.
Identification and traceability is required to the minimum requirements of the ASME Code.
Attachments welded to equipment will be of a Code approved material of the same P-No. as the equipment.  Where attachments are of a different P-No., a poison pad of the same P-No. as the equipment will be used.

### Contamination
Fabrication of carbon steel will be completed in an area which is segregated from stainless steel and other alloys.
The Vendor will use an effective system to identify and segregate different materials to prevent inadvertent use.
Contamination of stainless steels, nickel based alloys, titanium, zirconium and tantalum alloys by carbon steel or other incompatible materials is not permitted, and will be cause for rejection.
Grinding wheels, wire brushes and other hand tools will not be used on more than one type of material.

## Welding

### General
All welding will be completed at the location specified on the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics purchase order.  Alternate locations MUST be aprroved by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
All welders and welding procedures will be qualified in accordance with the ASME code or Aker Kvaerner Chemetics approved alternate.
All weld consumables will meet the requirements of ASME Section II, Part C.
On equipment with support skirts, no tack welds will be permitted between the head and skirt on the inside of the skirt.  The skirt attachment weld will not cover the bottom girth seam.
Heads which have a central hole as a result of manufacture, will have it completely seal welded and 100% radiographed to the ASME code.  This requirement will not apply if a nozzle is to be welded to the head at this location.
Shell or head plate cut-outs made at wrong locations will be repaired by either installing a nozzle through the plate and performing a full penetration weld or installing a “flush” plate insert with full penetration weld and 100% radiography to the ASME code.
Vertical welds will be made in the up-hand (vertical up) position.
Weld surfaces will be protected from contamination before and during welding.
Use of paste type shielding products (i.e. Solar Flux®) as a substitute for inert gas shielding is not permitted.

### Assembly Prior to Welding
Butt joint misalignment will not exceed that allowed by the relevant code or as specifed below:
Plates <= 6mm (1/4”) - 1.5mm (1/16”)
Plates > 6mm (1/4”) - the smaller of 3mm (1/8”) or 25% of plate thickness
Slip on flanges - the sum of gaps on any diameter will not exceed 5mm (3/16”)
To maintain the specified alignment and gap (where used) during welding, the parts to be welded will be securely held in position by mechanical means, welded-on bridge pieces or by tack welding.  Welded-on bridge pieces or support spiders will be of the same material as the component to which they are welded.  The Equipment surface will not be left in a damaged condition after removal of such pieces.
Where a jig or other suitable device is used, the construction of the jig will allow adequate access for welding and cleaning and will be such that external loading is not applied to a joint during welding.
Tack welds will be performed by ASME qualified welders to an ASME approved procedure.
Tack welds employed for assembly purposes may form part of the completed weld provided that complete fusion is achieved at the root of the weld and that the welding procedure used is the same as that employed for the remainder of the root pass.
Cracked and unsound tack welds will be removed prior to welding.
Sound tack welds must be completely fused into the root pass of the main weld.  It is sometimes necessary to thin down the ends of the tack welds to achieve this.  Where this is necessary, tack welds will be at least 25mm (1”) long to ensure that the remaining portion of the tack weld is strong enough to hold the pieces in place.

### Arc Strikes
Arc strikes will be confined to surfaces on which weld metal is to be deposited.  If an arc strike occurs elsewhere, the location will be ground smooth, dye penetrant (PT) or magnetic particle (MT) examined and, if necessary, repaired.
Burn marks from grounding clamps will be treated as arc strikes.

### Cleaning and Restarting
Each run of weld metal will be thoroughly cleaned of slag and foreign matter before the next run is deposited.  On restarting, care will be taken to ensure proper fusion with the weld metal previously deposited.
If a tungsten electrode makes contact with the work, the section of weld and the tip of the electrode will be examined.  Where tungsten loss has occurred, the tungsten pick up in the weld will be ground out and the electrode reground to the correct profile.

### Form of Welds
Welds will be uniform in width and size throughout their full length.  Each pass of weld will be smooth and free of slag, inclusions, cracks, porosity, lack of fusion and undercut except to the extent permitted by ASME.  In addition, the cover pass will be free of coarse ripples, irregular surface, non-uniform bead patterns, high crown and deep ridges or valleys between beads.
Butt welds will be convex, uniform in height and have full penetration.  Weld reinforcement will not exceed the limits permitted by ASME.
Nozzle to shell and floor to shell welds will be full penetration unless otherwise shown on approved drawings.
Crevices and/or undercut are not permitted on process exposed welds.
Peening of weld runs or hammering finished welds is not permitted.

### Chemical Analysis
The Vendor will make available to the Inspector copies of chemical certification for consumables used.  If filler metal cannot be identified to the Inspector’s satisfaction, samples will be removed for chemical analysis.  This will be at the Vendor’s cost.
When evidence of poor electrode control indicates a specific weldment could fall outside the approved specification or Code requirements, the Vendor will complete an analysis of the suspect welds.  All costs for repairs and retests that result from this investigation will be the Vendor’s responsibility.

## Post Weld Heat Treatment (PWHT)
When PWHT is required, the Vendor will submit a PWHT procedure to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for review and approval before PWHT is completed.
Post weld heat treatment will satisfy the requirements of the applicable codes and standards.
The use of direct flame impingement heating, exothermic heat treatment or localized heat treatment is not permitted without Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ prior approval.
All welding, including clips, structural parts, etc., will be completed prior to PWHT.
Soaking period will be based on the heaviest welded section including reinforcing pads.
Flange faces will be suitably protected from oxidation.
Thermocouples will be attached to the equipment to indicate the metal temperature.  Sufficient numbers of thermocouples will be used and distributed such that the range of metal temperatures is recorded.  Temperature records will be required.
Any required hydrostatic/pneumatic testing, air/soap testing or magnetic particle inspection will be carried out after PWHT.

## Forming
Materials will be cold formed and, where necessary, stress relieved or annealed according to the applicable code and Aker Kvaerner Chemetics specifications.
When hot forming is necessary, it will be carried out in accordance with a written procedure.

## Workmanship
Operations such as tube rolling and welding will be carried out in accordance with a written procedure.
Reinforcing pads and other attachments will closely match the mating contour.
In addition to ASME Code requirements, all Equipment will satisfy the tolerances given in Section 13 of this specification.
Poor workmanship will be reason for rejection.  Flaws or defects which cannot be reasonably repaired, excessive repairs, poor fit-up, nozzles requiring relocation and damaged surfaces, etc., will be considered evidence of poor workmanship.

## Nameplates
In addition to any nameplates required by codes (ASME/TEMA etc.), the Vendor will affix an Aker Kvaerner Chemetics nameplate to equipment.
All nameplates will be securely fastened with stainless steel bolts or rivets to a bracket welded to the vessel shell as shown on the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics drawing.
Nameplate brackets will be sized to accommodate the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics nameplate  (150mm x 90mm (6” x 3½”)), and where required, Vendor’s nameplate.  Additionally, for Code stamped Equipment where metric pressures and temperatures are required, the Vendor will install a supplementary metric nameplate.

## Cleaning and Painting
Equipment will be drained and thoroughly cleaned inside and outside and will be free from rust, grease, weld spatter, scale, slag and any other foreign matter.
Interior surfaces will not be painted, coated or lined unless otherwise specified.
When an Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or Owner paint specification is applicable, the Vendor will include a report demonstrating satisfactory compliance to the specification in the quality assurance documentation package.

## Inspection And Testing

### Pressure Test
When specified by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or required by Code, equipment will be subject to a hydrostatic or pneumatic test.
Testing will be in accordance with the applicable code or Aker Kvaerner Chemetics requirements.
Hydrostatic testing will be done using potable water at 18-24oC (65-75oF) unless otherwise limited by ASME code.  If sweating occurs, the water will be warmed sufficiently to allow inspection of dry surfaces.
The test pressure will be maintained for one hour per 25mm (1”) of maximum shell thickness.  Minimum holding time will be one hour.  A calibrated pressure gauge will be installed on the equipment during the test.
Hydrostatic testing of internal coils will be performed after all preliminary operations and N.D.T. have been carried out.  (e.g. forming, heat-treatment, descaling, etc.)  Minimum holding time will be two hours, after which the coil(s) will be inspected, thoroughly drained and dried.

### Radiography
When required or specified, radiography will be performed in accordance with:
ASME Section V, Article 2 for vessels fabricated to ASME.
API 620 paragraph 7.15.1 for tanks fabricated to API 620.
API 650 paragraph 6.1 for tanks fabricated to API 650.
Radiography will not be performed until all work required to correct out-of-roundness and peaking has been completed.
Location of spot radiographs will normally be selected by the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Inspector.  In all cases junctions between circumferential and longitudinal seams will be considered as being preferred locations.
Where more than one welder/welding operator has been used, spot radiography will be performed in such a manner that the welding of each welder/welding operator is examined.
When additional radiography is required as defined in ASME Section VIII, Division 1, paragraph UW-52(d)(2), or for radiography of repairs, all costs will be the responsibility of the Vendor.
Radiographic inspection reports will include a sketch or map of the locations of the radiographs.
Personnel performing radiographic examination will be qaualified to SNTC-1A.
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics reserves the right to accept or reject any detected indications.

### Liquid Penetrant Examination
When required or specified, liquid penetrant examination will be performed in accordance with ASME Section V, Article 6.
Acceptance standards will be per ASME Section VIII Division 1, Appendix 8, except that linear indications will be considered unacceptable.
Welds will, as a minimum, be dressed sufficiently so as to permit proper evaluation of defects.
Where internal welds are specified to be ground smooth, any required liquid penetrant examination will be carried out after grinding.
Liquid penetrant examination will be performed on both surfaces of completed butt welds and all welds exposed to the process.

### Magnetic Particle Examination
When required or specified, magnetic particle examination will be carried out in accordance with ASME Section V, Article 7.
Acceptance standards will be per ASME Section VIII, Division 1, Appendix 6.

### Leak Tests
The welds of each reinforcing pad or segment, together with associated nozzle welds, will be given an Air/Soap solution test.  Test hole(s) will be left open after the test has been completed.
Unwelded tube to tubesheet joints will be given an Air/Soap solution test.  The shell side will be pressurized to 35kPag (5 psig) max.
Flat-bottomed tanks will have any weld splices in the floor given a vacuum box test.  For non-API tanks this requirement is waived where the floor splice welds are subjected to 100% radiographic or dye penetrant examination.
When helium testing of tube to tubesheet joints is specified, it will be done using the Helium Mass Spectrometer Reverse Probe method.

The quality document package will be available for review prior to inspection release.

## Fabrication Tolerances (see also sketch on next page)

### Nozzles
The circumferential location of nozzles will be within 3mm (1/8”) of the specified axis as measured at the face of the nozzle.
The out of squareness of nozzle faces, measured at the inside of the bolt circle will be within 1.5mm (1/16”) for connections DN200 (8”) and under and within 2.5mm (3/32”) for connections over DN200 (8”).
Outside diameter of nozzle necks rolled from plate; ± 1.5mm (1/16”) for connections DN300 (12”) and under and ± 3mm (1/8”) for connections over DN300 (12”).

### Shop Fabricated Flanges
Bolt circle diameter:	± 1.5mm (1/16”)
Bolt hole circumferential spacing:	± 1.5mm (1/16”)
Eccentricity of bolt circle with respect to flange bore:	± 1.5mm (1/16”)
Flange minimum thickness:	+ 3mm (1/8”) / -0
For slip on and lap joint flanges, the clearance between the inside diameter of the flange and the outside diameter of the cylinder will not exceed:
1.5mm (1/16”) for nominal diameter <= DN300 (12”)
3mm (1/8”) for nominal diameter <= DN1200 (48”)
5mm (3/16”) for nominal diameter > DN1200 (48”)

### Lap Rings
Outside diameter of lap ring; ± 1.5mm (1/16”) for connections DN600 (24”) and under and  ± 3mm (1/8”) for connections over DN600 (24”).
Lap ring minimum thickness; + 1.5mm (1/16”) / -0

### Insulation and Vacuum Rings
Datum line to first ring;	± 5mm (3/16”)
Distance between any two adjacent rings;	± 12mm (1/2”)

### Supports
Radial clearance between outside of head and inside of skirt will not exceed 1.5mm (1/16”).
Base of skirt/legs/support lugs will be true and square with respect to the reference lines within the following limits:
± 2.5mm (3/32”) for nominal vessel diameter <= 2150mm (7’-0”)
± 3mm (1/8”) for nominal vessel diameter <= 3050mm (10’-0”)
± 5mm (3/16”) for nominal vessel diameter > 3050mm (10’-0”)
Anchor bolt circle diameter; ± 3mm (1/8”)
