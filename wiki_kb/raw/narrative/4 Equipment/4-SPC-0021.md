# 4-SPC-0021 — Specification


[TABLE: 4-SPC-0021_table_1]


## Purpose
The purpose of this Standard is to define the workmanship and quality requirements for nickel/copper (UNS N 04400 also known as Monel 400) welded fabrications purchased by/or on behalf of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Inc.

## Scope

### This Standard applies when called for on purchase orders and referenced documents.

### This Standard covers the welding and other fabrication requirements for vessels, heat exchangers, tanks, piping and miscellaneous welded items in the above material.

## Definitions

### Codes, etc., shall be taken to mean latest edition ruling at date of contract.

### Agreement in Writing - Where prior approval in writing is required by this Standard, this shall be taken to mean submission in writing by letter and not merely by inclusion in approval drawings.

### Inspector - Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Quality Assurance Inspector or other Authorized Representative.

## General Requirements

### Subcontracting
No Subcontracting of welding is permitted without Aker Kvaerner Chemetics written approval.

### Supervision
The fabricator shall ensure that there is regular and systematic supervision of all work both in-shop and on-site by competent staff.
The proposed location of site welds shall be evaluated by the Contractor at an early stage to ensure that there is adequate access for welding, cleaning, inspection and non-destructive testing.

### Procedure Submittal
All welding procedures shall be submitted by the fabricator for review and agreement by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics QA & Materials Group prior to commencement of any work.  The ASME IX PQR Form 483 shall form part of this package and be the basis for procedural approval
A change in essential variables as outlined in ASME Code, Section IX will require re-submission of procedure(s).

### Welders Qualifications
All welders to be employed on Aker Kvaerner Chemetics work shall be suitably qualified in accordance with relevant codes and standards for every procedure he is to use.
The fabricator shall, upon request, supply Aker Kvaerner Chemetics with objective evidence of current welder qualifications.
All qualified welders shall be allocated an identification symbol which shall be marked on the parent material adjacent to each welder's work, allowing traceability during fabrication.
Subject to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics approval, fully marked-up weld maps may be substituted for stamping.

### Cleanliness
The surface and edges of any nickel material which is to be heated for any purpose (e.g. thermal cutting, forming or welding) shall be cleaned in two stages, as follows:
Stage 1 - All surfaces and edges which are to be heated shall be lightly ground to white metal.  Where it is not practical to grind the surface e.g. pipe bore, the surface shall be chemically cleaned using an agreed procedure.
Stage 2 - Immediately prior to the application of heat, the ground surfaces shall be cleaned with suitable organic solvent to remove lubricants, greases, oils, marking crayons paints, dye penetrants etc.
The fabricator shall take the controls necessary to prevent contamination of weldments at all stages of fabrication.  These controls shall include the segregation of wire brushes, etc., for use on nickel materials only.
On completion of work, items shall be cleaned internally and externally. All fabrication markings, weld spatter, etc., shall be removed prior to shipment.  Sulphur, phosphorous or lead bearing paints or crayons shall not be used for marking or identification purposes.

### Drawings
All work shall be carried out in accordance with the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics "Approved for Fabrication" shop detail drawing(s).  Deviations from the weld geometry as shown on approved drawing(s) are not permitted without the prior approval of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

## Materials

### Base Materials
All materials used shall be as specified in the purchase order and referenced documents.
All nickel/copper products shall be purchased in the annealed condition and satisfy ASME SB 127, 163, 164, and 165.
External or internal attachments shall be of the same material specification as the parent material unless agreed otherwise by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics QA & Materials Engineering Group.

### Filler Materials
All weld filler metals shall meet the requirements of the ASME Code.
Nickel/copper materials shall be welded with coated electrode #190 and/or filler wire #60 supplied by International Nickel Co.
All fabricators shall ensure and demonstrate to the Inspector that the manufacturers' instructions regarding the storage, drying and use of weld consumables are being fully implemented both in the shop and on site; in particular that all wet electrodes are discarded and not baked and re-used.

### Certification
Certification showing the results obtained from analysis and other tests carried out in accordance with the relevant material specification shall be supplied to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for all materials including welding consumables.

### Traceability
Fabricator shall maintain full traceability of materials back to original mill certification.
As a minimum the fabricator's internal controls shall be such as to prevent the use of unspecified materials.
Lack of adequate identification or certification shall be cause for rejection of the specified component or item.

## Preparation Of Edges For Welding

### Cutting to Size
Material shall be cut to size by:

### Machining
Shearing - material over 3/16" thick shall have extra material left on sheared edge to allow machining, chipping, grinding, etc., to ensure that any defects caused by shearing are removed.
Material over 1" thick shall not be sheared.
Thermal Cutting - it is preferable that thermal cutting shall be carried out by the Plasma Arc Process and the welding edges lightly ground to bright metal.  Any other process shall require that on completion of thermal cutting a minimum of 1/8" of metal be removed by mechanical means to ensure that all slag, scale, other irregularities and contaminants, including thermal effects, have been removed.
Grinding – Silicon carbide wheels shall be used.

### Forming
Materials shall be cold formed as far as practical.  When hot forming is necessary, the procedure to be adopted shall be agreed to in writing by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics prior to commencement of work.  Refer Para. 5.5.1.
Pipe bends shall be in accordance with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Dwgs. and shall not utilize low melting point metals or alloys for the purpose of filling the pipe for bending.

## Welding Processes

### Welding Methods
The following processes are the preferred methods of welding unalloyed nickel materials.  Other processes will be evaluated on submission of full details including procedure and exact location of proposed weldments.
Shielded Metal Arc Welding	(SMAW)
Gas Tungsten Arc Welding	(GTAW)
Gas Metal Arc Welding	(GMAW)
Submerged Arc Welding	(SAW)

### Shielded Metal Arc Welding
Coated electrodes shall satisfy the requirements of SFA-5.11 Class ENiCu7.
Minimum practical amperage and minimum arc length are recommended in order to control the weld puddle.  Stringer beads rather than weaving is generally desirable.

### Gas Tungsten Arc Welding
Gas tungsten arc welding rods shall satisfy the requirements of SFA-A5.14 Class ER NiCu7.
Preferred electrode is thoriated tungsten as per SFA-A5.12, Class EWTh-2.
High frequency (HF) voltage shall be used to start the arc.
Weld metal thickness shall not exceed 1/8"/pass.  Heat input shall be kept low by using stringer bead technique.
Where necessary an inert backing gas shall be used to prevent root pass oxidation and contamination.

### Gas Metal Arc Welding
GMAW shall only be used for the root pass of butt welds and for fillet welds unless specifically agreed otherwise.
Fabricator must provide satisfactory evidence of continuous shop use of this procedure in the fabrication of nickel components.

### Submerged Arc Welding
Use of this process must be reviewed and approved in writing by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics prior to start of fabrication.
Since the flux choice is critical, a chemistry check of the finished deposit must be included as part of the weld procedure qualification.
Acceptable wire/flux combination is Inco filler metal #60 and Incoflux #5.
Fabricator must provide satisfactory evidence of continuous shop use of this process in the fabrication of nickel materials.

### Preheat, Interpass Temp. & Postweld Heat Treatment
Preheat is not required.  However, when adverse weather conditions give rise to condensation or wetness on the surfaces of the component, the material shall be dried prior to welding by carefully heating to a temperature just sufficient to prevent condensation.
Heat input during welding should be kept to a minimum.  Interpass temperature should not exceed 200F.
Postweld Heat Transfer is seldom required.  In the event that PWHT is necessary, the fabricator shall submit a written procedure for approval prior to commencement of work.  This procedure shall include information on the number and location of thermocouples and supports.

## Workmanship

### General
Procedure Tests. The welding procedure(s) shall be agreed upon (see Clause 5.2) before any production welding is begun.
Welding Position. As far as practical, all welds shall be made in the downhand (flat) position.
Vertical welds shall be made vertical up (uphand).
Weather Protection for Welding on Site. Suitable enclosures shall be provided to keep welds and welders free from draughts, rain or other inclement weather.  Welding shall not be performed on wet surfaces.
Fusion Faces. The fusion faces and the adjacent metal, both internal and external, shall be free from moisture, oxide, paint, grease or other foreign matter immediately prior to welding.

### Alignment of Butt Joints
The alignment of welding edges at butt joints shall be in accordance with relevant code, specification, etc., but maximum values for misalignment (High-Low) shall be as follows:
For plates up to and including 1/4" in thickness, 1/16"
For plates above 1/4" in thickness, 25% of the plate thickness or 1/8" whichever is smaller.  The misaligned surface shall be chamfered accordingly.
For pipe, shall be in accordance with ASME B31.3.
Note that run-off pads, in the same material as the parent plate, shall be used at the beginning and end of each plate weld and longitudinal pipe weld.

### Assembly for Welding
To maintain the specified alignment and gap (where used) during welding, the parts to be welded shall be securely held in position by mechanical means, welded-on bridge pieces or by tack welding.
Mechanical Means. Where a jig or other suitable device is used, the construction of the jig shall allow adequate access for welding and cleaning and shall be such that external loading is not applied to a joint during welding.
Bridge Pieces/Support Spiders
Welded-on bridge or support spiders shall be of nickel material/weld metal where in contact with the vessel, heat exchanger or pipework.
Where welded-on bridge pieces or support spiders are used, the plate surface shall not be left in a damaged condition after the bridge pieces or support spiders have been removed.
Tack Welds.
Tack welds employed for assembly purposes may form part of the completed weld provided complete fusion is effected at the root of the weld and that the welding procedure used is the same as that employed for the remainder of the root run.
Cracked and unsound tack welds shall be cut out prior to root pass or full seam welding.
Where "feathering" is necessary tack welds should preferably be at least one inch long to ensure that the remaining part of the tack weld is strong enough to hold the plate or pipe in place.
Sound tack welds must be thoroughly fused into the root of the main weld.  It is sometimes necessary to thin down or feather the ends of tack welds to ensure  that complete fusion is achieved.
Backing Bars. The use of permanent back-up rings or strips or consumable joint inserts is not permitted unless specifically approved in writing by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics QA & Materials Engineering.

### Striking of Welding Arc
Every effort shall be made to confine arc strikes to surfaces on which weld metal is to be deposited.  If such a strike should occur outside this area, the spot will require to be ground, PT examined per Para. 10.3 and, if necessary, repaired.
Burn marks from grounding clamps shall be treated as arc strikes.

### Cleaning and Re-Starting
Each run of weld metal shall be thoroughly cleaned and all slag and foreign matter removed before the next run is deposited.  On re-starting, care shall be taken to ensure proper fusion with the weld metal previously deposited.
All restarts in the root bend shall be preceded by "feathering" out the area of the stop by grinding or filing, followed with wire brushing to remove any abrasive residue.

### Electrode Touch Down
If a tungsten electrode accidentally makes contact with the work, the section of weld and the tip of the electrode should be examined.  Where tungsten loss has occurred, the tungsten pick-up in the weld shall be ground out and the electrode reground to the correct profile before re-starting.
Should the heated end of the filler rod become contaminated (i.e. fall outside of the inert atmosphere at the weld puddle), it shall be snipped off and discarded.

### Peening and Hammering
Peening of weld runs and hammering of finished welds is not permitted.

### Form of Welds
Each weld shall be uniform in width and size throughout its full length. Each layer of welding shall be smooth and free of slag, inclusions, cracks, porosity, lack of fusion, and undercut except to the extent permitted in the referenced codes.  In addition, the cover pass shall be free of coarse ripples, irregular surface, non-uniform bad patterns, high crown, and deep ridges or valleys between beads.
Buttwelds shall be slightly convex, of uniform height, and have full penetration.  In the case of pipe welds, internal reinforcement shall be subject to the limitations of the governing code.
Crevices or undercut shall not be permitted on the process side of the weld.
As far as is practicable, fillet welds shall be deposited in not less than two layers and their surfaces shall be free from marked irregularities.
Dimensions of fillet welds shall be in accordance with the approved drawing or specified size, with uniform legs and full throat.

### Dressing of Welds & Repair Areas
Grinding or gouging shall be carried out in such a manner as not to reduce the adjacent base metal thickness.  Note that complete removal of weld defects shall be verified by non-destructive examination prior to repair welding.
Rubber bonded grinding wheels or abrasive discs shall not be used.
Wire brushes shall be made of austenitic stainless steel and must not have been previously used on other metals.

## Quality Control

### General
The Inspector shall have free access to the Vendor's work and to his Subvendor's, if work is sublet by approval, at all reasonable times.  Access shall also be given to Authorized Representatives of the End-User.
The Inspector shall be at liberty to inspect the work during fabrication at any stage in manufacture and to reject any material or workmanship which does not conform to this Standard or the applicable code.
Inspection by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics does not relieve the fabricator or contractor of responsibility for providing materials, components and workmanship in accordance with the requirements of this Standard, engineering design and the applicable code.

### Chemical Analysis
Supplier must make available to the Inspector copies of chemical certification for batch of electrodes or wire coils used.  If electrodes, wire, etc., cannot be identified to the Inspector's satisfaction, samples shall be removed for chemical analysis.  This shall be to supplier's account.
When required by purchase order, a spot check analysis shall be performed on production welds of critical equipment such as pressure vessels, heat exchangers, pressure piping and certain proprietary items. This shall also include non-pressure retaining equipment in dangerous or demanding services.
The weld(s) to be checked shall be selected at random by the Inspector.
Where results of chemical analysis show that a specific weldment falls outside of the approved specification or code requirements, the supplier shall promptly advise Aker Kvaerner Chemetics in writing of his proposed course of action.  Such action shall include details of any additional testing necessary to fully define the extent of the non-conformance.  All repairs and retests shall be to the supplier's account.

### Dye Penetrant Inspection (PT)
Unless otherwise specified in the purchase order, approved drawings, specifications/codes, etc., nozzle attachment weld surfaces, butt weld surfaces including skirt to shell welds shall be PT examined for 25% of each welder/process used for cracks, porosity, slag inclusions, signification undercutting or other major defects.
It should be noted that in the case of piping, 25% refers to 100% PT examination of 25% of the production welds.
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics shall reserve the right to select the location of the above inspection.
Unless otherwise specified in the purchase order or approved drawings, all pressure welds between dissimilar metals shall be l00% PT examined.
Tube to tube sheet welds shall be 100% PT examined.
The PT exam shall be carried out in accordance with a written procedure, and in general the limit of acceptability shall satisfy the ASME Code Section VIII, Division 1, Appendix VIII except that linear indications shall be considered unacceptable.
All excavated areas shall be weld built-up flush with the surface of the base material using the welding procedure previously approved by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for the main welding details.
All excavated areas and weld repair surfaces shall be 100% PT examined per the above requirements.
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics reserves the right to accept or reject any doubtful or questionable indications.
Where access is limited (e.g. process side of small piping) spot radiography shall be performed in accordance with the relevant code.

### Radiographic Inspection (RT)
Where specified on Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Engineering drawings, items fabricated in accordance with this Standard and the ASME Code shall be spot radiographed in accordance with Para. UW-52 (b) of Section VIII Division 1 of the ASME Code which clearly defines the following requirements:
Minimum extent of Spot Radiographic Examination
Standards for Spot Radiographic Examination
Evaluation & Retests
For piping fabricated in accordance with ASME Codes and this Standard, the minimum standard of radiography shall be random radiography defined in Para. 341.4.1 (b) of ASME B. 31.3 except that 20% of all butt welds shall be radiographed.
For tankage fabricated in accordance with API Standards 620 and 650 and this Standard, the minimum requirements shall be spot radiography as required by these standards.
In the absence of other governing codes, items fabricated to this Standard, and requiring Radiography, shall meet the requirements of Paras 10.4.1 or 10.4.2 as appropriate.
RT Exam shall be carried out in accordance with a written procedure by personnel qualified to SNTC-1A.
Where additional spot radiography is required, as defined in UW-2 Sub-para. (d) (2), costs of this radiography including all repair costs and re-radiography shall be to the fabricator's account.
Junctions between circumferencial and longitudinal seams shall be considered as preferred locations for spot radiography.

### Helium Mass Spectrometer Test
When required by purchase order or referenced drawings, tube/tubesheet joints and tube bundles shall be tested using the helium mass spectrometer (reverse probe method) per ASME Code Section V, Article 10.

### Painting or Marking
No paint shall be applied either externally or internally to any item or component manufactured to this Standard unless agreed to in writing by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

### Documentation
The fabricator shall furnish a report containing all the data pertaining to each vessel or item as follows:
Prior to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' final inspection of each unit, the fabricator shall have prepared in finished form, three sets of records, except final test data, as set forth in this paragraph.
Material mill test reports
Nameplate rubbing
Hydrostatic test report
A copy of all inspection and test reports
These reports, together with final test data, shall be assembled into three complete sets for each item.  In no case shall a documentation package pertain to more than one vessel or item number.
The Inspector will at the time of witnessing the final tests and certifying correctness of forms, collect the appropriate number of documentation packages for each item tested.
