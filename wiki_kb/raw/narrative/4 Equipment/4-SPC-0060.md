# 4-SPC-0060 — Specification


[TABLE: 4-SPC-0060_table_1]


## Purpose
This specification defines Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ requirements for welding and associated procedures for vessels, equipment and pipe spools made of carbon steel in the ASME P-No. 1 classification.

## Codes and Standards
4-SPC-0040: Shop Fabricated Metallic Vessels and Metallic Vessel Components lists the codes and standards for design and fabrication.

## Special Requirements
Weldments for low temperature service will meet the requirements of ASME Section VIII, Division 1, paragraph UCS-67.

## Welding Process

### General
The following processes are acceptable:
Shielded Metal Arc Welding	(SMAW)
Gas Tungsten Arc Welding	(GTAW)
Gas Metal Arc Welding		(GMAW)
Submerged Arc Welding	(SAW)

### Root Passes
The Vendor will give close consideration to root pass quality.  Acceptable welding processes for process exposed root passes are:
GTAW
GMAW
SMAW

### Shielded Metal Arc Welding
SMAW electrodes will satisfy the requirements of SFA-5.1.
SMAW of carbon steel will be done using E7018 or E7028 electrodes
Pipe root passes may be done using E6010 or E6011 electrodes.

### Gas Tungsten Arc Welding
Weld metal thickness will not exceed 3mm (⅛") per pass.
Heat input will be kept low by using stringer bead technique.

### Gas Metal Arc Welding
GMAW may be used for the root pass of butt welds and for fillet welds.
GMAW may be used for complete welding of other joint details.
The Vendor must provide satisfactory evidence of continuous shop use of this process.
A minimum of two passes is required on all thicknesses.
The short circuit mode of transfer will not be used unless the specific application is approved by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

### Submerged Arc Welding
Weld metal thickness will not exceed 13mm (½") per pass.
Run-on and run-off tabs of the same material specification as the base material will be used on all longitudinal seams.
Wire and flux combinations recommended for single pass welding will not be used for multiple pass welds.
A chemical analysis of the finished deposit must be included as part of the weld procedure qualification.
When high carbon or high manganese fluxes are used for the fabrication of equipment for low temperature service, Vendor must provide sectioned samples (which may be from run-on/off tabs) and satisfactory evidence of continuous shop use of this process.  This is due to the possibility of microcracking.

## Preheat & Inter-pass Temperature
Preheat and inter-pass temperatures will be in accordance with ASME Section VIII, Division, 1 Appendix R.
The temperatures will be controlled by the use of temperature indicating crayons, contact pyrometers or other suitable means.

## Post Weld Heat Treatment (PWHT)
PWHT temperature will not exceed 649 ºC (1200 ºF).

## Inspection and Testing

### Radiographic Examination
Butt welds on materials in low temperature service will be 100% RT examined.
Piping will have 5% random radiography as defined in ASME B31.3 paragraph 341.4.1(b) as a minimum.
Shop fabricated pipe made from sheet or plate will have one spot radiograph at each longitudinal seam.  No additional radiography of the longitudinal seam is required unless defects are revealed when the pipe is cut into smaller lengths.
When RT results are inconclusive, welds will be magnetic particle examined.

### Magnetic Particle Examination
When required or specified, magnetic particle examination will be performed in accordance with ASME Section V, Article 7.
Acceptance standards will be in accordance with ASME Section VIII Division 1, Appendix 6.
Surfaces will be demagnetised after magnetic particle examination.
