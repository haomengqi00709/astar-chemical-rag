# 4-SPC-0048 — Specification


[TABLE: 4-SPC-0048_table_1]


## Purpose
This specification defines Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ requirements for carbon steel heat exchanger tubes which are to be used in the fabrication of gas to gas heat exchangers in acid plants.  The specification applies to ASTM A178/SA178 Grade A tube material.

## Codes and Standards
ASTM A178/SA178 (latest version), and the applicable standards to which it refers.
Dimensional tolerances will conform to ASTM A450 (latest version).

## NDE Requirements
Material testing and general requirements as described in ASTM A178 (and A450) are mandatory
The Hydrostatic test
A -ring gauge test will be done on 100% of the tubes. A 50 mm long ring gauge with ID to match the maximum allowable OD of the tube will be passed over the of all the tubes.  This ensures that the tube is not too large to fit into the hole in the tube-sheet of the heat exchanger.
Commentary
To Section 1: This specification is written solely for the procurement of electric resistance welded (ERW) carbon steel gas/gas heat exchanger tubing.
To Section 3: These tests will be done on all tubes irrespective of the source
To Section 4: These tests will be done on tubes from suppliers who have not supplied tubes to  previously.  These requirements MAY be waived on subsequent orders from the same supplier only after consultation with and approval from  Materials and QA group.
