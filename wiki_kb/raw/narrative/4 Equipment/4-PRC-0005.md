# 4-PRC-0005 — Procedure


[TABLE: 4-PRC-0005_table_1]


## Purpose
This procedure describes the requirements for standard identification numbers for equipment detachable parts.
The standard identification numbers will be used for the development of all related numbering systems such as drawing numbers, etc.
The standard identification numbers will be used on all projects unless specific client requirements dictate otherwise.

## Definition
An equipment part is any part of equipment that is detachable and commonly replaced after a period of time in service.  For example, items that are not purchased from the main vendor, such as plastic components in a metal tank or vice versa such as:
Gaskets:	Manway, Body Flange, Special Types used on Explosion Hatches or Sight Glasses, etc.
Nozzle:	Spray Nozzle, Sleeve (PTFE, Titanium), Spargers, Dip Tubes, Vent Goose Necks etc.
Support Plates:	Packing, Distributer, Mist Eliminater, etc.
Internals:	Packing, Distributer, Mist Eliminater, Floating Roof, etc.
Misc.:		Spacers, Rupture Disc., Stacks, Insulators, Slide Pads, Grounding Device, Pressure Relief, Assemblies, etc.
Bolting:	Special Fasteners (Bolts, Studs, Washes, Nuts).

## Numbering Format for Equipment Part Number
EPN1234-02
EPN:	Equipment Part
1234:	Associated Equipment Number
02:	Serial Number

## Associated Equipment Number for Equipment Parts
This is the standard equipment number as described in 4-PRC-0001: Plant Equipment Identification of the equipment to which the equipment part belongs.

## Serial Number
A unique two-digit number is assigned to each equipment part that belongs to the same equipment.
