# 4-SPC-0016 — Specification


[TABLE: 4-SPC-0016_table_1]


## Purpose
The purpose of this Standard is to define the workmanship and quality requirements for austenitic stainless clad steel welded fabrications purchased by/or on behalf of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

## Scope
This Standard applies when called for on purchase orders and referenced documents.
This Standard covers the welding and other fabrication requirements for vessels, heat exchangers, piping and miscellaneous welded items utilizing 300 series stainless steel cladding.  Other austenitic stainless steel cladding such as Alloy 20-Cb3 (UNS N08020) is also covered by this Standard.
Roll bonded or explosion clad plate will be acceptable cladding methods.

## Definitions
Codes, etc. will be taken to mean latest edition ruling at date of contract.
Agreement in Writing - Where prior approval in writing is required by this Standard, this will be taken to mean submission in writing by letter and not merely by inclusion in approval drawings.
Inspector -  Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Quality Assurance Inspector or other Authorized Representative.

## General Requirements

### Sub-Contracting
No Subcontracting of welding is permitted without Aker Kvaerner Chemetics written approval.

### Supervision
Fabricator will ensure that there is regular and systematic supervision of all work both in-shop and on-site by competent staff.

### Procedure Submittal
All welding procedures will be submitted by Fabricator for review and agreement by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' Q.A. & Materials group prior to commencement of any work.  The ASME IX PQR Form 483 will form part of this package.  Refer also to Para 6.4.2.
A change in essential variables as outlined in ASME Code, Section IX will require re-submission of procedure(s).

### Welders Qualifications
All welders to be employed on Aker Kvaerner Chemetics work will be suitably qualified in accordance with relevant codes and standards for every procedure he is to use.
Fabricator will, upon request, supply Aker Kvaerner Chemetics with objective evidence of current welder qualifications.
All qualified welders will be allocated an identification symbol which will be stamped on parent material adjacent to his work.

### Cleanliness
The surface and edges of any austenitic steel which is to be heated for any purpose (e.g. fusion cutting, forming or welding) will be cleaned free from all traces of foreign matter (e.g. marking paint, oil, etc.) by grinding, wire brushing with a stainless steel brush and/or non-chlorinated solvent cleaning immediately prior to the application of heat.
Fabricator will take the controls necessary to prevent contamination of weldments at all stages of fabrication.  These controls will include the segregation of wire brushes, grinding wheels, etc. for use on stainless steels only.
On completion of work, items will be cleaned internally and externally.  All fabrication markings, oxidation, weld spatter, etc. will be removed prior to shipment.
Passivation and ferroxyl testing of the stainless steel surface, where required by Purchase Order/referenced documents or engineering drawing will be carried out in accordance with written procedures, which must be approved by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics prior to use.

### Drawings
All work will be carried out in accordance with the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics "Approved for Fabrication" shop detail drawing(s).  Deviations from the weld geometry as shown on approved drawing(s) are not permitted without the prior approval of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

## Materials

### General
All materials will be as specified in the purchase order and referenced documents.
Weld overlay will be applied to carbon steel flange faces exposed to the process environment.
Small diameter nozzle necks will be solid seamless stainless steel Sch. 80 min.
Small diameter flanges will be slip-on or lap joint type.

### Clad Plate
All clad plate will be in accordance with SA264 or SA265.
The base metal (carbon steel) will satisfy specification SA516.
The alloy cladding will have a minimum thickness of 1/8" (3mm) conforming to specification SA240 for 300 series stainless steel and SB463 for Alloy 200-Cb3 (UNS N08020)
Mill ultrasonic inspection will be performed in accordance with ASME A578 Level II.  Repairs will be in accordance with supplementary requirement S6.
All clad plate will be subjected to the shear test outlined in the material specification SA264 or SA265.
Nozzle cut-outs to be examined by  fabricator for lack of bond and retained for customer inspection.

### Filler Materials
All Filler Materials will conform to the appropriate SFA Specification.
Fabricator will ensure and demonstrate to the Inspector that the manufacturer’s instructions regarding the storage, drying and use of weld consumables are being fully implemented both in the shop and on site; in particular that all wet electrodes are discarded and not baked and re-used.
Refer to Appendix A for typical stainless weld consumables.

### Certification
Mill test certification showing the results obtained from analysis and other tests carried out in accordance with the relevant material specification will be supplied to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for all materials including welding consumables.
Welding procedures (including weld overlay) will provide a chemical analysis of the cover pass on the clad side.  The analysis will satisfy the requirements of the ASME code.

### Traceability
Fabricator will maintain full traceability of materials back to original mill certification for all pressure containing or major load bearing elements or materials exposed to the process environment.
As a minimum Fabricator’s internal controls will be such as to prevent the use of unspecified materials including weld consumables.
Lack of adequate identification or certification will be cause for rejection of the specific component or item.

## Preparation Of Edges For Welding

### Cutting to Size
Material will be cut to size by machining, shearing, thermal processes such as plasma arc and grinding.  All contaminated edges will be removed prior to rolling or working of plate.

### Forming
Material will be cold formed as far as practical.  When hot forming is necessary, the procedure to be adopted will be agreed to in writing by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics prior to commencement of work.

## Welding Processes

### Welding Methods
The following processes are the preferred methods of welding stainless clad steels.  Other processes will be evaluated on submission of full details including procedure and exact location of proposed weldments.
1)	Shielded Metal Arc Welding		(SMAW)
Note:	This is the recommended weld process for the process side of welds.
Refer to Appendix A for recommended welding electrodes.
2)	Gas Tungsten Arc Welding		(GTAW)
3)	Gas Metal Arc Welding		(GMAW)
4)	Submerged Arc Welding		(SAW)

### Preheat, Interpass Temp. & Postweld Heat Treatment
Preheat is not required.  However, when adverse weather conditions give rise to condensation or wetness on the surfaces of the component, the material will be dried prior to welding by heating to a temperature just sufficient to prevent condensation.
Heat input during welding should be kept to a minimum.  Interpass temp. should not exceed 3000 F on 300 series stainless steel or 2000 F. on Alloy 20-Cb3.
Postweld Heat Treatment is not generally a requirement, however, in the event that PWHT  is specified, Fabricator will submit his procedure in writing for approval prior to commencement of work.  This procedure will include information on the number and location of thermocouples and supports.

## Workmanship

### General

#### Procedure Tests
All welding procedures and testing will be agreed between the purchaser and Fabricator  (see Para 5.3) before any production welding is begun.

#### Welding Position
As far as practical, all welds will be made in the downhand (flat) position. Vertical welds will be made vertical up (uphand).

#### Fusion Faces
The fusion and the adjacent metal, both internal and external, will be free from moisture, scale, paint, grease or other foreign matter immediately prior to welding.

### Alignment of Butt Joints
The alignment of welding edges at butt joints will be in accordance with relevant code, specification, etc., but maximum values for misalignment (High - Low) will be as follows:
For plates up to and including 1/4" in thickness, 1/16"
For plates above 1/4" in thickness, 25% of the plate thickness or 1/8" whichever is smaller.
The specific welding detail for clad plate shown in Appendix "B" will be adhered to by Fabricator.

### Assembly for Welding
To maintain the specified alignment and gap (where used) during welding, the parts to be welded will be securely held in position by mechanical means, welded-on bridge pieces or by tack welding.

#### Mechanical Means
Where a jig or other suitable device is used, construction of the jig will allow adequate access for welding and cleaning and will be such that external loading is not applied to a joint during welding.

#### Bridge Pieces/Support Spiders
Welded-on bridge or support spiders will be of austenitic steel on the surface welded in contact with the vessel or heat exchanger.
Where welded-on bridge pieces or support spiders are used, the plate surface will not be left in a damaged condition after they are removed.

#### Tack Welds
Tack welds employed for assembly purposes may form part of the completed weld provided that complete fusion is effected at the root of the weld and that the welding procedure used is the same as that employed for the remainder of the root run.
Sound tack welds must be thoroughly fused into the root run of the main weld.  It is sometimes necessary to thin down the ends of tack welds to make sure this is possible.
Where this is necessary, tack welds should preferably be at least one inch long to ensure that the remaining part of the tack weld is strong enough to hold the plate or pipe in place.
Cracked and unsound tack welds will be cut out prior to welding.

### Sequence of Welding
Substantially all carbon steel welding will be completed in each joint before stainless welding on the joint is started.
No increase in thickness of stainless steel root pass(es) or decrease in number of stainless steel layers is allowed, by comparison with the analysed test piece.

### Striking of Welding Arc
Every effort will be made to confine arc strikes to surface on which weld metal is to be deposited.  If such a strike should occur outside this area, the spot will require to be ground, PT examined and, if necessary, repaired. Burn marks from grounding clamps will be treated as arc strikes.

### Cleaning and Re-Starting
Each run of weld metal will be thoroughly cleaned and all slag and foreign matter removed before the next run is deposited.  On re-starting, care will be taken to ensure proper fusion with the weld previously deposited.

### Electrode Touch Down
If a tungsten electrode accidentally makes contact with the cladding, the section of weld and the tip of the electrode should be examined.  Where tungsten loss has occurred, the tungsten pick-up in the weld will be ground out and the electrode reground to the correct profile before re-starting.

### Peening
Peening of weld runs other than light peening for slag removal is not permitted unless specifically shown as a requirement on the "Approved for Construction" fabrication drawings.

### Form of Welds
Each weld will be uniform in width and size throughout its full length.  Each layer of welding will be smooth and free of slag, inclusions, cracks, porosity, lack of fusion, and undercut except to the extent permitted in the referenced codes.  In addition, the cover pass will be free of coarse ripples, irregular surface, non-uniform bead patterns, high crown, and deep ridges or valleys between beads.
Buttwelds will be slightly convex, of uniform height, and have full penetration.
Crevices will not be permitted on the process side of the weld.
As far as is practicable, fillet welds will be deposited in not less than two layers and their surfaces will be free from marked irregularities. Dimensions of fillet welds will be in accordance with the approved drawing.

### Dressing of Stainless Welds & Repair Areas
Grinding or gouging will not be carried out in such a manner as to reduce the adjacent metal thickness tolerance for clad plate.
Grinding wheels will be iron-free and of the resin bonded alumina or silica type.
Wire brushes or chipping tools will be made of austenitic stainless steel and must not have been previously used to clean surfaces other than stainless steels.
All internal weld surfaces will be ground smooth.

## Quality Control

### General
The Inspector will have free access to the Vendor's work and to his Subvendor's, if work is sublet by approval, at all reasonable times.
The Inspector will be at liberty to inspect the work during fabrication at any stage in manufacture and to reject any material or workmanship which does not conform to this Standard or the applicable code.
Inspection by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics does not relieve Fabricator of responsibility for providing materials, components and workmanship in accordance with the requirements of this Standard, engineering design and the applicable code.

### Ultrasonic Inspection
Evidence of lack of bond revealed by nozzle cut outs, edge preparation etc. will require that the suspect areas be ultrasonically examined and the extent of the delamination determined.
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics will be advised of the extent of the delaminated surfaces and of the repair method proposed by the fabricator.
All repairs to clad surfaces will be ground smooth, dye penetrant inspected and 100 percent scanned with the ultrasonic probe.  This will include adjacent clad plate to ensure that no further delamination has occurred during welding.
Repairs will be performed in accordance with the ASME code and satisfy the specific requirements of SA578 Level II supplementary requirement S6.  A report of these repair areas will be provided to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
Where load bearing or stressed attachments are to be welded directly to the cladding, Fabricator will ultrasonically check the area concerned for lack of bond prior to welding.

### Chemical Analysis
Supplier must make available to purchaser's inspector, copies of chemical certification for each batch of electrodes or wire coils used as well as plate material etc.  If electrodes, wire, etc. cannot be identified to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' satisfaction, samples will be removed for chemical analysis.  This will be to Supplier’s account.
A spot check analysis will be performed on production welds of equipment such as pressure vessels, heat exchangers and certain proprietary items.  This requirement will also apply to non-pressure retaining equipment.
The weld(s) to be checked will be selected at random by the Inspector or designated representative.
Where results of chemical analysis show that a specific weldment falls outside the approved specification or code requirements, Supplier will promptly advise Aker Kvaerner Chemetics in writing of his proposed course of action.  Such action will include details of any additional testing necessary to fully define the extent of the non-conformance.  All repairs and retests will be to Supplier’s account.

### Dye Penetrant Inspection (PT)
Unless otherwise specified in the purchase order, approved drawings, specifications/codes, etc. weld surfaces will be 100 percent PT examined for cracks, porosity, slag inclusions, significant undercutting or other major defects after grinding smooth.
Unless otherwise specified in the Purchase Order or approved drawings, all welds between dissimilar metals will be 100% PT examined on the root pass and completed weld surfaces.
Tube to tubesheet welds will be 100% PT examined.
The PT exam will be carried out in accordance with a written procedure and in general, the limit of acceptability will satisfy the ASME Code Section VIII, Division 1, Appendix VIII, except that linear indications will be considered unacceptable.
All excavated areas will be weld built-up flush with the surface of the base material using the welding procedure previously approved by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for the main welding details.
Internal weld repair surfaces will be ground smooth and PT examined per the above requirements. Machined surfaces e.g. weld overlaid flange faces will also be 100 percent PT examined.
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics reserves the right to accept or reject any doubtful or questionable indications or weld surfaces which have not been ground smooth.

### Radiographic Inspection (RT)
Carbon steel weld radiography of each seam will be complete before starting to weld the stainless side of the seam.
Items fabricated in accordance with this Standard and the ASME Code will be spot radiographed in accordance with Para. UW-52(b) of Section VIII, Division 1 of the ASME Code which clearly defines the following requirements:
Minimum extent of Spot Radiographic Examination
Standards for Spot Radiographic Examination
Evaluation and Retests.
RT Exam will be carried out in accordance with a written procedure by personnel qualified to SNTC-1A.
Where additional spot radiography is required, as defined in UW-52 Sub-para. (d)(2), costs of this radiography including all repair costs and re-radiography will be to Fabricator’s account.
Where more than one welder or welding operator has been used, spot radiography will be performed in such a manner that the welding of each welder or welding operator can be examined.
Junctions between circumferential and longitudinal seams will be considered as preferred locations for spot radiography.

### Passivation
All internal weld surfaces will be passivated in accordance with a written procedure, which must be approved by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics prior to its use.  (Refer to ASTM A380 "Cleaning & Descaling Stainless Steel Parts, Equipment & Systems").

### Ferroxyl Test
All internal surfaces will be given a Ferroxyl Test for Carbon Steel Contamination.

### Documentation
Fabricator will furnish a report containing all the data pertaining to each vessel or item as follows:
Prior to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' final inspection of each unit, Fabricator will have prepared in finished form, three sets of records, except final test data, as set forth in this paragraph.
Mill test reports including reference to shear and ultrasonic testing.
Nameplate rubbing
A copy of all inspection and test reports (UT, PT, X-Ray, Chemical Analysis Passivation, Ferroxyl Test)
Affidavit of Manufacture
Hydrostatic Test
These reports, together with final test data, will be assembled into three complete sets for each item.  In no case will a documentation package pertain to more than one vessel or item number.
The Inspector will at the time of witnessing the final tests and certifying correctness of forms, collect the appropriate number of documentation packages for each item tested.
