# 4-SPC-0001 — Specification


[TABLE: 4-SPC-0001_table_1]


## Purpose
This document describes the general requirements for the design, manufacture and supply of equipment.

## Regulations, Codes and Standards

### General
All equipment manufactured will be in accordance with the latest edition of applicable statutes, regulations, codes, standards and specifications.  The Vendor will state the specific codes and regulations on which his tender is based.

### Codes and Standards
Codes and Standards required by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics will be listed on the data sheets, drawings or specifications.
It is the Vendor’s responsibility to obtain relevant codes and standards.

## Site Conditions
Site data, including climatic information, is given in 1-DST-0001: Site Design Conditions.

## Utilities
Design data for utilities is given in 1-DST-0001: Site Design Conditions. In some cases additional information may be on the equipment data sheets.

## Equipment Requirements

### General
Equipment will be designed to prevent the accumulation of spilled material, dust and water.
Equipment will be designed for operation under the full range of operating conditions including non-steady state, start-up, shutdown and turndown.
Permanent facilities for pump-out, steam-out, purging and flushing will be included on all equipment where necessary.

### Loadings
Design loads for equipment will be the most adverse combination of loadings, which will include, but not necessarily be limited to, the following:
Dead, live and wind loads.
Earthquake loads.
Pressure induced loads.
Loads applied by machine action (e.g., torque).
Acceleration or deceleration (inertia) loadings (e.g., braking forces).
Impact loads.
Loadings induced by expansion and contraction of materials of construction.
Loadings produced by material spillage or abnormal operation (e.g., conveyor spillage onto adjacent walkways, blocked chutes).
Loadings produced during the course of plant maintenance (e.g., resting of equipment on adjacent platforms, leak/hydro testing of vessels).
Design loads for fatigue failure analysis (where applicable) will be determined from those loads, applied to, or produced by, a machine whilst operating at its maximum rated capacity assuming the machine's prime mover(s) are operating at their maximum continuous rating.
Equipment will be designed to accommodate the design wind loading and earthquake loading as specified in 1-DST-0001: Site Design Conditions.  Load combinations will be in accordance with the applicable codes.

### Safety and Environmental
The supply of all devices required for the safe operation of the equipment will be the responsibility of the Vendor.  The Vendor will provide Aker Kvaerner Chemetics with details concerning any required connections (e.g. relief valve discharges to drain).

### Handling for Equipment Packages
Provision will be made for the assisted lifting of all items of equipment, plant and products weighing more than 25 kg (55 lb).  All equipment and major sub-assemblies will have designated lifting points.
All items of equipment which may require removal for regular maintenance (once or more per year) and weighing more than 50 kg (110 lb) will be readily retrievable from its installed position by wheeled trolley, forklift, monorail trolley hoist or crane, onto road transport at grade level or to a designated position where maintenance can be performed.

### Noise
All equipment will meet a noise limit of 85 dB(A) measured one metre from the equipment.
Equipment that operates during plant excursions only (such as relief valves) will be designed to minimize noise levels, having particular regard for personnel safety.  Noise from this equipment will comply with a limit of LC, peak 140 dB(C) (a C-weighted sound pressure level of 140 dB(C), referenced to 20 micropascals) measured at any location that has personnel access.
The Vendor will submit expected noise levels and data for the equipment with the quotation.  If noise levels are expected to exceed the specified requirements, the Vendor will propose a noise reduction system, complete with cost, for Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ consideration.

### Vibration
The Vendor will provide relevant information on inertial loads and any theoretical dynamic loads caused by an imbalance in the equipment.  The Vendor will specify installation requirements for vibration control or isolation, and will provide vibration absorption pads if required.
Vibration limits for fans and pumps will conform to ISO standard 10816-1, zone A.  The Vendor will provide installation information for mounting and foundations that will be required to achieve these vibration limits.

### Lubrication
Equipment will be designed such that lubrication points are easily accessible and that lubrication is possible while the equipment is operating.  The Vendor will recommend lubricants and will provide a lubrication schedule.  Gearboxes will be drained of oil prior to shipping.  Sufficient lubricant for a first fill will be provided and shipped with the equipment.  Greased bearings will be greased at the factory.
Splash lubrication is preferred over forced circulation oil lubrication.
All gearboxes will include a replaceable breather, magnetic sump element, dust-tight dipstick or sight glass and plugged oil drain point.
Rotating shaft packings and seals subject to dust and process loading, and thus decreased effective life, will be protected by additional coarse sealing, e.g. - labyrinth seals in addition to any mechanical seal.

### Protective Coatings
For Vendor standard equipment such as pumps, fans, agitators, plate heat exchangers, and bulk materials etc. the Vendor’s standard coating system for chemical service will be used. The Vendor will submit details of the coating system with their quotation for Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ approval.

### Insulation
Insulation will conform to the applicable Aker Kvaerner Chemetics specification.  For packaged equipment, where insulation of equipment or piping is required, the Vendor’s standard insulation system will be used. The Vendor will submit details of the proposed insulation system with their quotation for Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ approval.

### Shipping
Large, heavy, temperature sensitive or fragile equipment will require the following as required:
Internal bracing for large tanks.
High lifting capacity cranes and spreader bars for heavy equipment.
Climate controlled containers or routing through warm climates for temperature sensitive equipment.
Vibration/shock isolation packaging including “no shunting” when shipped by rail for fragile equipment.
Shipping configuration drawings for the equipment listed above detailing supports, lifting attachments, weights, dimensions and any restrictions must be submitted for review.  This review must be completed before packaging this equipment.

### Nameplates
Each piece of equipment will be provided with a stamped or engraved stainless steel manufacturer’s nameplate which lists the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics equipment number, Owner’s equipment number as well as pertinent manufacturer information (make, model number, serial number, rating, etc.).  Nameplate information will be in the English language.

## Mechanical Requirements

### Fasteners
[Fasteners will be imperial in accordance with American National Unified thread standard in either Unified National Coarse (UNC) or Unified National Fine (UNF) sizes].

### Piping (General)
Unless noted otherwise, piping and components will conform to [ASME B31.1 or B31.3] piping standards as applicable.
Drains will have plugs, caps, or blind flanges as appropriate.
Heavy Hex nuts will be used.  Bolts and studs will have a minimum of 2 threads projection to a maximum of 4 threads projection.
[Screwed pipe threads will be American standard taper pipe threads (NPT) per ASME B1.20.1].
Pipe flange sizes and drilling will be in accordance with [ASME B16.5].
All tie points will be anchored, flanged connections. Thermal expansion is not allowed past any tie-in points.
Piping will be adequately supported.  Types and locations of supports will be submitted to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for approval.
All plastic piping will have an ultra-violet (UV) inhibitor in the base material or the exterior of pipe and fittings will be fully coated with a suitable UV inhibitor.

### Grounding Connections
Small individually mounted equipment will be provided with one grounding clip of approximate size 50mm x 50mm complete with a 12mm diameter hole.  Skid mounted equipment will be provided with two grounding clips located at opposite ends of the skid.
Each metallic vessel, tank and heat exchanger will be equipped with two (2) grounding lugs.  Lugs on vertical vessels will be located on vessel supports located 180 degrees apart.  Lugs on horizontal vessels will be located near each saddle support.

### Drives
Where the equipment may stall occasionally due to the nature of the service, suitable overload protection devices such as shear pins, zero speed switches, or slip clutches will be incorporated.  High inertia load devices will be equipped with centrifugal clutches or fluid couplings.  The following will also apply:
Couplings will be selected to transmit the design starting torque with a service factor of 1.2 and will be manufactured from SG cast iron, cast or fabricated steel.
Couplings will be mounted to their respective shafts using Taper-Lock bushings and rectangular parallel keys for drives up to and including 150 kW.
V-belt sizes will be 3V, 5V or 8V wherever possible.  Belts will be supplied in matched sets.  Single groove sheaves are not acceptable.  Service factor will be minimum 1.4 times the motor nameplate HP.
All drive guards (for chains, couplings, belt drives, etc.) will be supplied with the equipment and will meet [OSHA] requirements.  Belt guards will be split, open-mesh, stainless steel, and totally enclosed.  Guard openings must be smaller than a finger.  Belt guards will have a hinged opening to allow for checking belt tension.  All guards will be provided with a tachometer opening.
For the selection of gear reducers, a service factor will be applied to the motor nameplate HP rating to determine mechanical rating.  The following service factors will be used:
Normal duty	1.50
Heavy duty	1.75
Gear reducers will be sized for continuous duty 24 hours per day, 365 days a year.  External cooling of gear reducers is not permitted.

### Anti-Friction Bearings
Bearings for all mechanical equipment will be ball or roller anti-friction type.
Minimum basic rating bearing life will be L10h = 100,000 hours unless specified otherwise.

### Shaft Seals
Single mechanical seals will have bypass flush or internal flush.
Double mechanical seals will be a balanced, self-aligning, double-seal type.

### Assembly Tolerances
Assemblies having rotating shafts or loose fittings with set screws are not acceptable.  Surfaces will be machined for an interference fit in accordance with ANSI B4.1 or in accordance with ISO or DIN standards.  Taper-lock bushings are permitted.

## Requirements for Specific Equipment

### Pressure Vessels and Heat Exchangers
Pressure vessels will be designed, fabricated and tested in accordance with ASME Section VIII, Division 1.  Heat exchangers will be in accordance with ASME Section VIII, Division 1 and in accordance with TEMA (Tubular Exchanger Manufacturers Association) Class “C”.  All equipment will be “U” stamped.

### Boilers, Superheaters, Economisers
Boilers (and the tube side of superheaters and economisers) will be designed, fabricated and tested in accordance with ASME Section I.  All equipment will be “S” stamped.

### Tanks
Metallic vessels (other than flat bottom tanks) operating at less than 100 kPag (15 psig) will be designed and fabricated in accordance with ASME Section VIII, Division 1.
Flat bottom metallic tanks operating at less than 100 kPag (15 psig) but greater than 17 kPag (2½ psig) will be designed and fabricated to API 620.  Flat bottom metallic tanks operating at less than 17 kPag (2½ psig) will be designed and fabricated to API 650.
Anchor bolts will be designed in accordance with the applicable codes.

### General Requirements for All Tanks, Vessels and Heat Exchangers
The minimum size of access manways considered safe and practical for general access requirements is 600 mm diameter.  If equipment is too small in diameter to comply with this manway size requirement then top entry will be provided via a full diameter removable tank top.
Vessels, heat exchangers and tanks will be fitted with lifting lugs and/or trunnions.  Each individual lug or trunnion will be designed to support the total vessel weight.
The weight of the vessel including all internals, ladders, platforms, etc. and the maximum weight of the specified liquid contents, will be calculated and shown on the general arrangement drawing.
Flanges will be in accordance with [ASME B16.5] and the ratings will be specified on the data sheets and general arrangement drawings.  Flanges beyond the limit of [ASME B16.5] or where the rating is not specified will be designed to meet the specified design code requirements.
All flange faces for pressure connections will have a surface finish compatible with the selected gaskets.   Flange bolt-holes will straddle vessel natural centerlines.
Liquid filled vessels or sections of vessels will be either self draining and venting or fitted with drain and vent connections.

## Materials
All materials will be new and of merchantable quality.
The Vendor will consider both internal and external environments, fugitive emissions and plant fluid spills when selecting materials and protective coatings.  The components of all equipment that is not permanently sealed from the atmosphere will be reviewed and selected for compatibility with the environment, including all fugitive emissions.
Aluminium alloys will not be used without the approval of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics except for standard items such as panels and instrument housings.  Standard aluminium alloy items with a protective coating will be permitted.
Asbestos containing materials are prohibited.
Galvanic action via contact of dissimilar metals will be prevented.

## Spares
The Vendor will provide recommended spare parts lists, with pricing valid for one year, for the following:
Spare parts required for start up.
Spare parts required for two years of continuous operation.
The lists must include the recommended quantity of spares, item identification number, description, item manufacturer, unit price, and delivery time. The unit price will be based on the assumption that the spares will be ordered separately from the equipment.
Spare parts will be clearly tagged with the words "Spare Part For" followed by the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics equipment number, and if applicable, with a part number which is referenced in the manufacturer's maintenance documentation.
