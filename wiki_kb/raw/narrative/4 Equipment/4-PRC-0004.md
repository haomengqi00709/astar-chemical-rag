# 4-PRC-0004 — Procedure


[TABLE: 4-PRC-0004_table_1]


## Introduction
The mechanical checkout procedure for equipment outlines the procedure to be used to verify that equipment has been installed in accordance with the drawings and specifications and is ready for water batching and commissioning.  The mechanical checkout documents will form part of the mechanical completion package.  This procedure assumes that site work is carried out as follows:
During construction the site sub-contractor checks for completeness before approaching Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site supervisor for site supervisor review.
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site supervisor visually reviews installed equipment against the general arrangement and installation instructions. When the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site supervisor is satisfied the sub-contractor has taken the work as far as can be done at that time, he notifies the Project Manager that the equipment is ready for a final review by a mechanical engineer from the office.
The engineer from the office conducts a visual inspection of the equipment against the equipment drawings and P & I D to confirm the installation is in accordance with the construction documents.
Each equipment item is inspected in accordance with the attached guidelines.  Findings are recorded on the Mechanical Equipment Check-out List.
All deficiencies are recorded and given to the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site supervisor.
The site supervisor works with a deficiency crew supplied by the site sub-contractor to correct all deficiencies identified by the engineer.
Outstanding work such as painting, insulating and installing flange guards is undertaken by the deficiency crew after cleaning, flushing and hydrotesting has been completed.
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site supervisor may carry out the check for vessel internals (packing material, size, etc.).  In this case the engineer will obtain a signed form stating that the internals have been checked and conform to the design.
The primary purpose of check-out is to ensure that the plant has been constructed in accordance with the design documents (drawings and specifications).  Other modifications and changes identified during check-out shall be treated as design deficiencies.  A design deficiency is not a punch list item and shall be documented and forwarded to the Project Manager for resolution.

## Vessels and Tanks
Equipment:	Equipment covered in this section will be designated by the suffix:
T – Tanks, Receivers, Drums and Sumps
D – Cyclones, Precipitators and Dust collectors
J – Crystallizers and Evaporators
K – Decanters, Settlers and Clarifiers
R – Reactors and Converters
U – Towers, Columns, Strippers and Absorbers.
Documents:	P & I D, Vessel drawing, Equipment data sheet; Boiler code certification
Nameplate:	Nameplate data and record matches the outline drawing/data sheet information
Nameplate tag matches tag drawing
Foundation:	Confirm that the base has been grouted and grouting is good
Confirm that the elevation height is consistent with the design
Anchoring:	Check all anchor bolts are installed and tight
Check that all anchor chair connections are installed as per outline drawing
Check that the nuts of any slide pads are loose, allowing for expansion.
Confirm that the slide pads are not grouted.
Access:	Check that the manway is accessible and can be opened without obstruction
Check the manway gasket material against the outline drawing.
Check that vessel ladder is adequately support and all bolts are tight
Check that the ladder has enclosed cage.
Paint:	Check that carbon steel vessels have been primed and painted
Check for gouges, cuts, scratches etc.
Lining:	Check that lining is continuous
Check that lining inserts are sealed
Check that lining seams are smooth and continuous
Grounding:	Check grounding cable connection is tight
Check that the cable is connected to the main grounding loop
Overflow:	Check overflow nozzle is connected and bolts are tight.
Check overflow piping is connected to ground, trench or seal pot
See notes on nozzle
Vents/Drains:	Check that both vent and drain are clear of debris and open
Check that screens are provided where required
Check drain valve is connected and lever has unobstructed rotation
Insulation:	Check tank insulation material against specification
Check insulation thickness
Check insulation sheet covers the surface and all connections are made
Internals:	Check that support plates are installed correctly and fit.
Check that the correct material has been supplied and the size of packing is correct, etc.
Check that packing or catalyst level is at the correct height and correct volume is installed
Check that bed limiter plate is the correct material, installed and bolted down
Check that distributor tray or piping is the correct material installed and clean and free of debris
Check that the mesh pads or mist eliminators are the correct materials, clean and free of debris
Check that mesh pads, mist eliminators are supported and bolted in place
Inserts and
Special Items:	Check that vortex breakers are installed, where P & I indicates such and gasket position complies with special item drawing
Check that dip legs, nozzle inserts, etc. are installed as per outline drawing with correct gasket material and correct design dimensions
Check that internal piping is installed and all connections are tight.
Check that plastic piping is cemented
Check that overflow seal pot dimensions are correct
Nozzles:	Nozzle size and orientation matches outline drawing
Nozzle gasket and thickness matches outline drawing/data sheet
Nozzles are open from the inside of vessel and no foreign debris is present
Check that items connected to nozzle (instrument, valve, etc) are present
Valve flange is aligned to piping
Flange bolts are adequately tightened
Flange bolts have not been overtightened (i.e. damage to gasket)
The nut has at least three rounds of pipe thread protruding past the nut
Check that all spare nozzles have installed blinds of the correct material.
Cleanliness:	Check that the vessel is flushed, walls are clean and free of debris
Carbon steel tanks should be cleaned of rust.
Static Test:	Check that hydro test has been carried out and signed as acceptable

## Rotating Equipment
Equipment:	Equipment covered in this section will be designated by the suffix:
C – Blowers, Compressors and Fans
G – Grinders, Crushers and Classifiers
P – Pumps
V – Vacuum pumps
Documents:	P & I D, Manufacturers documentation, Equipment data sheet
Confirm that the elevation height is consistent with the design
Nameplate:	Nameplate data and record matches the equipment drawing/data sheet information
Nameplate tag matches manufacturer information
Motor nameplate matches the manufacturer information
Foundation:	Confirm that the equipment is horizontal
Confirm that the base has been grouted
Confirm that equipment is bolted to base
Check installed shock absorbers for damage
Confirm that installed shock absorbers are clean and move smoothly through their travel range.
Alignment:	Confirm that the motor alignment has been completed and signed off.
Motor:	See motor installation field check in electrical checklist
Confirm that grouting does not obstruct the sliding base, where applicable
Determine if the motor has sealed bearings or whether lubrication is required.
Paint:	Check that equipment has been coated per the job requirements
Check that coupling guard has been coated per the job requirements
Coupling, Guards
and V-Belts:	Check that coupling is installed
Check that the shaft rotates freely by hand
Check that guard is installed and bolts tight
Check that the correct size of v-belt has been installed
Check the v-belt tightness using the installation instruction method.
Check that the v-belt guard has been replaced and is tight.
Grounding:	Check grounding cable connection is tight
Check that the cable is connected to the main grounding loop
Housing:	Ensure that all housing nuts and bolts are tight
Nozzles:	Check equipment size and material against manufacturers information and data sheet
Check nozzles and piping are free of debris
Check nozzles are not strained by connected piping and that adequate pipe supports are installed (may require piping to be disconnected)
Oilers and
Oil Seals:	Ensure that the oilers are installed (if required).
Check that the oiler and oil seal for leakage.
Check that the oil is the correct specification for the equipment.
Check that the oil casing contains oil to the fill line.
Bearings:	Check the manufacturer's installation instructions, bearings may have shipping grease.
Check that bearings have been lubricated
Seals:	Check that correct seal has been installed
Check seal flush piping is connected
Air Supply:	Check that the air supply line, regulator and oiler are installed
Drains:	Check that drain plug is installed and clear of debris
Strainer:	Check that start-up strainer is installed (if required)
Compressors:	Check that all valves open and close fully and easily
Check that the manufacturer's instructions for lubrication have been followed.  The oil within the compressor casing may be shipping oil
Check supplied instrumentation and seal fluids versus the specifications.

Check hot pressure and temperature rating

Confirm that the base has been grouted

Paint:	Check that heat exchanger has been coated per the project specifications

Check that the cable is connected to the main grounding loop

Ensure nozzles and piping are free of debris

Drains:	Check that the drain valve is installed and clear of debris
Check that the steam trap has correct orientation and flow direction

Removal:	Check that no obstructions prevent the removal of heads, tubes, plates etc.
Check that piping has adequate spool pieces, which can be removed to assist in tube or head removal
Safety Shields:	Check that the safety shield is installed, and that the bolts are tight (for plate heat exchangers).
Materials:	Check plate material versus specification
Check gasket material versus specification
