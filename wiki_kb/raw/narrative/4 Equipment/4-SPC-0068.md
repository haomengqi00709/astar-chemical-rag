# 4-SPC-0068 — Specification


[TABLE: 4-SPC-0068_table_1]


## Purpose
This specification defines Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ requirements for welding and associated procedures for vessels and equipment made of unalloyed nickel in the ASME P-No. 41 classification.

## Codes and Standards
4-SPC-0040: Metallic Vessel Fabrication lists the codes and standards for design and fabrication.

## Special Requirements
The surface and edges of any material which is to be heated for any purpose (e.g. fusion cutting, forming or welding) will be cleaned in two stages as follows:
Stage 1 – All surfaces and edges will be lightly ground to white metal.  Where it is not practical to grind the surface (e.g. pipe bore), the surface will be chemically cleaned using and approved procedure.
Stage 2 – Immediately prior to the application of heat, the ground surfaces will be cleaned with suitable organic solvent.

## Filler Materials
All weld filler metals will meet the requirements of ASME Section II, Part C.

## Welding Process

### 5.1	General
The following processes are acceptable:
Shielded Metal Arc Welding	(SMAW)
Gas Tungsten Arc Welding	(GTAW)
Gas Metal Arc Welding		(GMAW)
Submerged Arc Welding	(SAW)

### 5.2	Shielded Metal Arc Welding
SMAW consumables will satisfy the requirements of SFA-5.11, classification ENi-1.
Use of consumables other than Special Metals welding electrode 141 requires ’ approval.

### 5.3	Gas Tungsten Arc Welding
GTAW consumables will satisfy the requirements of SFA-5.14, classification ERNi-1.
Use of consumables other than Special Metals filler metal 61 requires ’ approval.
Weld metal thickness will not exceed 3mm (⅛") per pass.
Heat input will be kept low by using stringer bead technique.

### 5.4	Gas Metal Arc Welding
GMAW consumables will satisfy the requirements of SFA-5.14, classification ERNi-1.
Use of consumables other than Special Metals filler metal 61 requires ’ approval.
The Vendor must provide satisfactory evidence of continuous shop use of this process.

### 5.5	Submerged Arc Welding
Weld metal thickness will not exceed 13mm (½") per pass.
Run-on and run-off tabs of the same material specification as the base material will be used on all longitudinal seams.
The Vendor must provide satisfactory evidence of continuous shop use of this process.
Acceptable wire/flux combination is Special Metals filler metal 61 and Special Metals Incoflux 6.
Since the flux choice is critical, a chemical analysis of the finished deposit must be included as part of the weld procedure qualification.

## Preheat & Inter-pass Temperature
Preheat is not required.
Heat input during welding will be kept to a minimum.  Inter-pass temperature will not exceed 93°C (200°F).
