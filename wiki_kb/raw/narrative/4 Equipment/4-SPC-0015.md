# 4-SPC-0015 — Specification


[TABLE: 4-SPC-0015_table_1]


## Purpose
The purpose of this Standard is to define the workmanship and quality requirements of welded fabrications in nickel-base alloys for use in aggressively corrosive environments.

## Scope
This Standard applies when called for on purchase orders and referenced documents.
This Standard covers the welding and other fabrication requirements for vessels, heat exchangers, tanks, piping and miscellaneous welded items.  The material is grouped under No. P45 of the ASME Code.
Huntington Alloys Inc. Material Designated Incoloy 825® (UNS N08825) is specifically covered by this Standard.

## Definitions
Codes, etc., will be taken to mean latest edition ruling at date of contract.
Agreement in Writing - Where prior approval in writing is required by this Standard, this will be taken to mean submission in writing by letter and not merely by inclusion in approval drawings.
Inspector - Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Quality Assurance Inspector or other Authorized Representative.

## General Requirements

### Sub-Contracting
No Sub-Contracting of welding is permitted without Aker Kvaerner Chemetics written approval.

### Supervision
Fabricator will ensure that there is regular and systematic supervision of all work both in-shop and on-site by competent staff.

### Procedure Submittal
All welding procedures will be submitted by Fabricator for review and agreement by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics QA & Materials Group prior to commencement of any work.  The ASME IX PQR Form 483 will form part of this package.
The procedure will be qualified on the same grade of material as that selected for fabrication.
A change in essential variables as outlined in ASME Code Section IX will require resubmission of procedure(s).

### Welders' Qualifications
All welders to be employed on Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' work will be suitably qualified in accordance with relevant codes and standards for every procedure he is to use.
Fabricator will, upon request, supply Aker Kvaerner Chemetics with objective evidence of current welder qualifications.
All qualified welders will be allocated an identification symbol which will be marked on the parent material adjacent to each welder's work, allowing traceability during fabrication.

### Cleanliness
The surface and edges of the material which is to be heated for any purpose (e.g. fusion cutting, forming or welding) will be cleaned free from all traces of foreign matter (e.g. marking paint, oil, etc.) by grinding, wire brushing with a stainless steel brush and/or non-chlorinated solvent cleaning immediately prior to the application of heat.
Fabricator will take the controls necessary to prevent contamination of weldments at all stages of fabrication.  These controls will include the segregation of wire brushes, grinding wheels, etc., for use on nickel-base materials only.
On completion of work, items will be cleaned internally and externally.  All fabrication markings, weld spatter, etc., will be removed prior to shipment.  Sulphur, phosphorous or lead bearing paints or crayon will not be used for marking or identification purposes.
Passivation, where required by Purchase Order/referenced documents, will be carried out in accordance with a written procedure, which must be approved by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics prior to its use.

### Drawings
All work will be carried out in accordance with the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics "Approved for Fabrication" shop detail drawing(s).  Deviations from the weld geometry as shown on approved drawing(s) are not permitted without the prior approval of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

## Materials

### Base Materials
Plate, sheet, pipe, and tube will be purchased in the stabilized condition to ASME SB424, SB423 and SB163 respectively.
Plate and sheet will be supplied in the hot rolled, annealed and descaled condition for maximum corrosion resistance unless specified otherwise on the purchase order.
Pipe and tube will be seamless grade and supplied in the annealed and pickled condition.

### Filler Materials
All weld filler metals will meet the requirements of Section 8 of this Standard.
Fabricator will ensure and demonstrate to the Inspector that the manufacturers' instructions regarding the storage, drying and use of weld consumables are being fully implemented both in the shop and on-site; in particular that all wet electrodes are discarded and not baked and reused.
Fabricator will, prior to commencement of work, furnish Aker Kvaerner Chemetics with the brand name of the filler materials actually to be used.

### Certification
Certification showing the results obtained from analysis and other tests carried out in accordance with the relevant material specification will be supplied to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for all materials including welding consumables.

### Traceability
Fabricator will maintain full traceability of materials back to original mill certification.
As a minimum, Fabricator’s internal controls will be such as to prevent the use of unspecified materials.
Lack of adequate identification or certification will be cause for rejection of the specific component or item.

## Preparation Of Edges For Welding

### Joint Design
The joint design for nickel-base alloys is slightly different from other materials due to the characteristics of the molten metal.  Material thicker than 0.1" will be bevelled to form a 'V', 'U' or 'J' groove.

### Cutting to Size
Material will be cut to size by:
Machining
Shearing:
Where necessary materials over 3/l6" thick will have extra material left on sheared edge to allow machining, grinding, etc., to ensure that any defects caused by shearing can be removed. Material over l" thick will not be sheared.
Thermal Cutting:
It is preferable that thermal cutting will be carried out by the Plasma Arc Process and the welding edges lightly ground to bright metal where necessary.  Any other process will require that on completion of thermal cutting, a minimum of l/16" of metal be removed by mechanical means to ensure that all slag, scale, other irregularities and contaminants, including thermal effects, have been removed.
Grinding:
Silicon carbide wheels, dedicated to nickel-base alloys will be used.

### Forming
Materials will be cold formed as far as practical.  When hot forming is necessary, the procedure to be adopted will be agreed to in writing by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics prior to commencement of work.

## Welding Processes

### Welding Methods
The following processes are the preferred methods of welding nickel-base alloys.  Other process will be evaluated on submission of full details including procedure and exact location of proposed weldments.
Shielded Metal Arc Welding 	(SMAW)
Gas Tungsten Arc Welding 		(GTAW)
Gas Metal Arc Welding 		(GMAW)
The general welding parameters of current, voltage, electrode size, etc., as well as special requirements or precautions, should be obtained from the electrode supplier.

### Shielded Metal Arc Welding
Shielded metal arc welding electrodes will satisfy the requirements of SFA-5.11.
The electrode will be Incoloy Welding Electrode 135.
Minimum practical amperage, and minimum arc length are recommended in order to control the weld puddle.  A weave pattern is generally desirable and should not be wider than 3x electrode core diameter.

### Gas Tungsten Arc Welding
Gas tungsten arc welding rods will satisfy the requirements of SFA-5.14.
The filler wire diameter will be kept small preferably l/l6" and will normally be Incoloy Filler Metal 65, designation ER Ni Fe Cr-1.  Incoloy 625 or equivalent (designation ER Ni Cr Mo3) may also be used provided prior approval has been granted by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
Preferred electrode is thoriated tungsten as per SFA-5.l2, Class EWTh-2; with a tapered tip for good arc stability and penetration.
The power supply will incorporate a high frequency (HF) voltage to start the arc, and a current decay unit.
Direct current straight polarity will be used (D.C.S.P. - Electrode negative) for both manual or machine welding.
Weld metal thickness will not exceed l/8"/pass.
A back-up gas will be used to prevent root pass oxidation and contamination.  e.g. Piping or where root pass is inaccessible.

### Gas Metal Arc Welding
GMAW will only be used for the root pass of butt welds and for fillet welds unless specifically agreed otherwise.
Short-circuiting transfer will be used for material up to 1/8" thick, and spray or pulsed arc transfer for thicker material.
Direct current reverse polarity will be used (D.C.R.P. - Electrode positive).
Fabricator must provide satisfactory evidence of continuous shop use of this process in the fabrication of nickel-base alloys.

### Submerged Arc Welding
Use of this process must be reviewed and approved in writing by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics prior to start of fabrication.
Fabricator must provide sectioned samples and satisfactory evidence of continuous shop use of this process in the fabrication of nickel-base alloys.

### Preheat & Postweld Heat Treatment
Preheat and postweld heat treatment are not required.  However, when adverse weather conditions give rise to condensation or wetness on the surfaces of the component, the material will be dried prior to welding by heating to a temperature just sufficient to prevent condensation.

## Workmanship

### General

#### Procedure Tests
The welding procedure will be agreed upon (see Para 5.3) before any production welding is begun.

#### Welding Position
As far as practical, all welds will be made in the downhand (flat) position. Vertical welds will be made vertical up (uphand).

#### Weather Protection for Welding On-site
Suitable enclosures will be provided to keep welds and welders free from draughts, rain or other inclement weather.  Welding will not be performed on wet surfaces or with wet or damp electrodes.

#### Fusion Faces
The fusion faces and the adjacent metal, both internal and external, will be free from moisture, scale, paint, grease or other foreign matter immediately prior to welding.

### Alignment of Butt Joints
The alignment of welding edges at butt joints will be in accordance with relevant code, specification, etc., but maximum values for misalignment (High - Low) will be as follows:
For plates up to and including 1/4" in thickness, 1/l6".
For plates above 1/4" in thickness, 25% of the plate thickness or 1/8", whichever is smaller.
For pipe, will be in accordance with ASME B3l.3.

### Assembly for Welding
To maintain the specified alignment and gap (where used) during welding, the parts to be welded will be securely held in position by mechanical means, welded-on bridge pieces or by tack welding.

#### Mechanical Means
Where a jig or other suitable device is used, the construction of the jig will allow adequate access for welding and cleaning and will be such that external loading is not applied to a joint during welding.

#### Bridge Pieces/Support Spiders
Welded-on bridge or support spiders will be of austenitic steel on the surface welded in contact with the vessel or heat exchanger.
Where welded-on bridge pieces or support spiders are used, the plate surface will not be left in a damaged condition after they are removed.

#### Tack Welds
Tack welds will be performed by qualified welders.  Tack welds employed for assembly purposes may form part of the completed weld provided that complete fusion is effected at the root of the weld and that the welding procedure used is the same as that employed for the remainder of the root run.
Cracked and unsound tack welds will be cut out prior to welding.
Sound tack welds must be thoroughly fused into the root run of the main weld.  It is sometimes necessary to thin down the ends of tack welds to ensure that complete fusion is achieved.
Where this is necessary, tack welds must be crack free and should preferably be at least one inch long to ensure that the remaining part of the tack weld is strong enough to hold the plate or pipe in place.

#### Backing Bars
The use of permanent back-up rings or strips or consumable joint inserts is not permitted unless specifically approved in writing by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics QA. & Materials Group.

#### Run-off Pads
Run-off pads, in the same material as the parent plate, will be used at the beginning and end of each plate weld.

### Striking of Welding Arc
Every effort will be made to confine arc strikes to surfaces on which weld metal is to be deposited.  If such a strike should occur outside this area the spot will require to be ground, PT examined per Para 10.3 and, if necessary, repaired. Burn marks from grounding clamps will be treated as arc strikes.

### Cleaning and Re-starting
Each run of weld metal will be carefully examined to ensure that it has been thoroughly cleaned and is defect free.  All slag and foreign matter will be removed before the next run is deposited.  This may be achieved by grinding followed by wire brushing.  On re-starting, care will be taken to ensure proper fusion with the weld metal previously deposited.
All re-starts in the root bead will be preceded by "feathering" out the area of the stop by grinding or filing, followed with wire brushing to remove any abrasive residue.

### Electrode Touch Down
If a tungsten electrode accidentally makes contact with the work, the section of weld and the tip of the electrode should be examined.  Where tungsten loss has occurred, the tungsten pick-up in the weld will be ground out and the electrode reground to the correct profile before restarting. Should the heated end of the filler rod become contaminated (i.e. fall outside of the inert atmosphere at the weld puddle), it will be snipped off and discarded.

### Peening and Hammering
Peening of weld runs and hammering of finished welds is not permitted.

### Form of Welds
Each weld will be uniform in width and size throughout its full length. Each layer of welding will be smooth and free of slag, inclusions, cracks, porosity, lack of fusion, and undercut except to the extent permitted in the referenced codes.  In addition, the cover pass will be free of coarse ripples, irregular surface, non-uniform bead patterns, high crown, and deep ridges or valleys between beads.
Buttwelds will be slightly convex, of uniform height, and have full penetration.  In the case of pipe welds, internal reinforcement will be subject to the limitations of the governing code.
Crevices or undercut will not be permitted on the process side of the weld.
As far as is practicable, fillet welds will be deposited in not less than two layers and their surfaces will be free from marked irregularities or concavity. Dimensions of fillet welds will be in accordance with the approved drawing.

### Dressing of Welds & Repair Areas
Grinding or gouging will not be carried out in such a manner as to reduce the adjacent base metal thickness.  Note that complete removal of weld defects will be verified by non-destructive examination prior to repair welding.
Grinding wheels will be iron-free and of the resin bonded silicon carbide type.
Wire brushes or chipping tools will be made of austenitic stainless steel and must not have been previously used to clean surfaces other than Incoloy 800 or 825.

## Quality Control

### General
The Inspector will have free access to the Vendor's work and to his Subvendor's, if work is sublet by approval, at all reasonable times. Access will also be given to Authorized Representatives of the End-User.
The Inspector will be at liberty to inspect the work during fabrication at any stage in manufacture and to reject any material or workmanship which does not conform to this Standard or the applicable code.
Inspection by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics does not relieve Fabricator of responsibility in accordance with the requirements of this Standard, engineering design and the applicable code.

### Chemical Analysis
Supplier must make available to the Inspector copies of chemical certification for each batch of electrodes or wire coils used.  If electrodes, wire, etc., cannot be identified to the Inspector's satisfaction, samples will be removed for chemical analysis.  This will be to supplier's account.
When required by purchase order or where the fabricator cannot identify the weld material, a spot check analysis will be performed on production welds of critical equipment such as pressure vessels, heat exchangers, pressure piping and certain proprietary items.  This will also include non-pressure retaining equipment in dangerous or demanding services.
Normally the weld(s) to be checked will be selected at random by the Inspector.
Where results of chemical analysis show that a specific weldment falls outside of the approved specification or code requirements, the supplier will promptly advise Aker Kvaerner Chemetics in writing of his proposed course of action.  Such action will include details of any additional testing necessary to fully define the extent of the non-conformance.  All repairs and retests will be to the supplier's account.

### Dye Penetrant Inspection (PT)
Items fabricated in accordance with the ASME Code Section VIII, Div. l, will be PT tested on all welds.
Unless otherwise specified on the purchase order, approved drawings or Standards, non-coded items will have butt and fillet welds, nozzle attachment welds, etc., 100 percent PT examined on all surfaces exposed to the process, for cracks, porosity, slag inclusions, significant undercutting or other major defects.
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics will reserve the right to select the location of the above inspection.
Unless otherwise specified in the Purchase Order or approved drawings, all welds between dissimilar metals will be l00% PT examined.
Tube to tubesheet welds will be l00% PT examined.
The PT exam will be carried out in accordance with a written procedure and in general, the limit of acceptability will satisfy the ASME Code Section VIII, Division 1, Appendix VIII, except that linear indications will be considered unacceptable.
All excavated areas will be PT examined prior to building up flush with the surface of the base material using the welding procedure previously approved by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for the main welding details.
All weld repair surfaces will be l00% PT examined per the above requirements.
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics reserves the right to accept or reject any doubtful or questionable indications.

### Radiographic Inspection (RT)
Equipment fabricated in accordance with the ASME Code will have all butt welds 100% radiographed in accordance with Para. UW-5l of Section VIII, Division 1 of the ASME Code where the plate or shell thickness at the welded joint(s) exceeds 3/8".
Equipment fabricated to the ASME Code, but not exceeding 3/8" plate thickness will be spot radiographed in accordance with Para. UW-52(b) of Section VIII, Division 1.
Spot radiography will also be the minimum requirement for equipment not fabricated to the ASME Code.
For piping fabricated in accordance with ASME Codes and this Standard, the minimum standard of radiography will be random radiography as defined in Para. 341.4.1 (b) of ASME B3l.3 except that 20% of all butt welds will be radiographed.
For tankage fabricated in accordance with API Standard 620 and this Specification, the minimum requirements will be spot radiography.
Items other than those described above, fabricated to this Standard only (i.e. not ASME, etc.), will if required by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics engineering drawings be spot radiographed in accordance with UW-52 (b) or 20% random radiographed B3l.3 in case of piping.
RT Examination will be carried out in accordance with a written procedure by personnel qualified to appropriate national standards.
Where additional spot radiography is required, as defined in UW-52 Sub-para. (d), costs of this radiography including all repair costs and re-radiography will be to Fabricator’s account.
Junctions between circumferential and longitudinal seams will be considered as preferred locations for spot radiography.

### Helium Mass Spectrometer Test
When required by purchase order or referenced drawings, tube/tube sheet joints and tube bundles will be tested using the helium mass spectrometer (reverse probe method) per ASME Code Section V, Article l0.

### Documentation
Fabricator will furnish a report containing all the data pertaining to each vessel or item as follows:
Prior to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' final inspection of each unit, Fabricator will have prepared in finished form, three sets of records, except final test data, as set forth in this paragraph.
Material mill test reports
Nameplate rubbing
Hydrostatic test report
A copy of all inspection and test reports
These reports, together with final test data, will be assembled into three complete sets for each item.  In no case will a documentation package pertain to more than one vessel or item number.
The Inspector will at the time of witnessing the final tests and certifying correctness of forms, collect the appropriate number of documentation packages for each item tested.
