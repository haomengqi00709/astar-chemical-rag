# 4-SPC-0067 — Specification


## Purpose
This specification defines Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ requirements for welding and associated procedures for vessels and equipment made of titanium in the ASME P-No. 51 and P-No. 52 classifications. Specifically, this specification covers titanium with the following UNS numbers: R50250 (Grade 1), R50400 (Grade 2), R52400 (Grade 7), R52402 (Grade 16) and R53400 (Grade 12).

## Codes and Standards
4-SPC-0040: Metallic Vessel lists the codes and standards for design and fabrication.

## Filler Materials
All weld filler metals will meet the requirements of ASME Section II, Part C.
Filler metals for grades 1, 2, 7 and 16 will satisfy the requirements of SFA-5.16 and fall within the F-No. 51 filler metal group.
Filler metals for grade 12 will satisfy the requirements of SFA-5.16 and fall within the F-No. 52 filler metal group.
The grade of filler metal will match that of the base metal except that grade 7 filler metal will be used to join grade 16 to grade 16 and grades 7 or 16 to grades 1 or 2.
If the part geometry is such that the completed weld is openly exposed to the process and does not form part of a crevice or a gasketted joint, Grade 2 filler may be used to join Grades 7 or 16 to Grades 1 or 2.
Filler metal for nitric acid service will not contain more than 0.05% iron and 60 ppm hydrogen.

## Welding Process

### General
Welding will be done using either the gas tungsten arc welding (GTAW) or Plasma Arc welding (PAW) process.

### Shielding Gas
The shielding gas will be moisture free argon, helium or a mixture of the two.
The gas handling hoses will be of Tygon® or vinyl plastic material.  Rubber hoses are not acceptable.
Gas shielding is required until the weld zone has cooled to below 426°C (800°F).
For pipe and fittings, a complete internal gas purge is necessary before starting the root pass.

## Fabrication
All titanium welding will be carried out in a clean, segregated area within the facility.

### Forming
Bend tests will be performed on both surfaces of each plate selected for formed head construction.  Plates which crack during bend testing will not be used in head construction.
Forming tools which have been previously used for other materials will be cleaned to remove surface contamination.  All burrs and indentations will be ground smooth.
Suitable protective layers will be used between forming equipment and the titanium to minimize metallic pick-up.  Iron pick-up is of particular concern where the component is to be used in nitric acid service.
