- COMMENTARY
- This spreadsheet calculates the reinforcement required for openings in vessel skirts.  The moment of inertia of the reinforcing
- ring must be equal to or greater than the moment of inertia of the skirt section removed.  Skirt section properties are per Roark
- Table 1, case 19 (hollow circular sector) with simplifications for thin cylinders (t/R << 1) and small opening angles.
