# 4-SPC-0069 — Specification


[TABLE: 4-SPC-0069_table_1]


## Purpose
This specification defines Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ requirements for welding and associated procedures for steam piping made of carbon steel in the ASME P-No. 4 and 5 classifications.  Specifically 1 ¼ Cr ½ Mo and 2 ½ Cr 1 Mo alloy steels.

## Codes and Standards
ASME B31.1.  Power Piping.

## Special Requirements
Final hardness (after post-weld heat treatment) of welds on will be no higher than 2 Brinell for P No.4 material and no higher than 2 Brinell for P No. 5 material.

## Filler Materials
All weld filler metals will meet the requirements of ASME Section II, Part C.

## Welding Process

### General
All welding will be done in accordance with ASME B31.1 Power Piping code. The following processes are acceptable:
Shielded Metal Arc Welding	(SMAW)
Gas Tungsten Arc Welding	(GTAW)
Gas Metal Arc Welding		(GMAW)
Submerged Arc Welding	(SAW)
Other processes will be considered by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics if proposed by the Vendor and subsequently approved in writing by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

### Root Passes
GTAW will be used for root passes.

### Shielded Metal Arc Welding
SMAW electrodes will satisfy the requirements of SFA-5.5.

### Gas Tungsten Arc Welding
GTAW electrodes will satisfy the requirements of SFA-5.28
Weld metal thickness will not exceed 3mm (⅛") per pass.
Heat input will be kept low by using stringer bead technique.

### Gas Metal Arc Welding
GMAW electrodes will satisfy the requirements of SFA-5.28
GMAW may be used for fillet welds.
The Vendor must provide satisfactory evidence of continuous shop use of this process.
A minimum of two passes is required on all thicknesses.
The short circuit mode of transfer will not be used unless the specific application is approved by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

### Submerged Arc Welding
SAW electrodes will satisfy the requirements of SFA-5.23
Weld metal thickness will not exceed 13mm (½") per pass.
Run-on and run-off tabs of the same material specification as the base material will be used on all longitudinal seams.
Wire and flux combinations recommended for single pass welding will not be used for multiple pass welds.
A chemical analysis of the finished deposit must be included as part of the weld procedure qualification.

## Preheat & Inter-pass Temperature
Preheat and inter-pass temperatures will be in accordance with ASME B31.1 Para.131
The temperatures referred to above will be controlled by the use of temperature indicating crayons, contact pyrometers or other suitable means.

## Post Weld Heat Treatment (PWHT).
Post weld heat treatment will be done on completed welds.
Heat treatment will be done to the requirements of ASME B31.1 Para.132.  No welding is allowed after PWHT.

## Weld Examination
Weld examination will be done in accordance with ASME B 31.3.
In addition to examination that is required by the Code, Aker Kvaerner Chemetics requires that all butt welds are examined by 100 % RT.
Commentary
To Section 1: This is a specification was written for steam piping in sulphuric acid plants.

To Section 3: Hardness testing serves as a check that PWHT has been done correctly.  High hardness indicates insufficient time at temperature.
