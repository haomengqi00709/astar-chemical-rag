# 4-SPC-0105 — Specification


[TABLE: 4-SPC-0105_table_1]


## Purpose
This specification defines Aker Kvaerner Chemetics requirements for installing refractory brick linings.
The Scope of Work will be defined in the Subcontract documents.

## Installation
Installation will be done in accordance with the refractory Vendor’s drawings and detailed installation instructions.
No metallic shims will be used in the lining.

### Preparation of Metal Surface
All interior metal surfaces to be lined will be thoroughly cleaned by the lining installer.  All loose scale, rust, dirt, grease and all other foreign material will be removed.

### Insulating Fire Brick
All insulating fire brick selected for installation will be free of cracks and voids.
The thickness of mortar joints in insulating fire brick will be kept to a minimum by using a combination of standard and special shape bricks.  Brick which must be fitted will be accurately laid out and wet cut with a diamond blade.  Chipping or breaking with a hammer is not permitted.
Insulating fire brick will be laid up with thin mortar joints.  All joints will be completely filled with the mortar.  Vertical joints will be staggered.  Courses will be held straight to line, true to straight edge, and flush on the surface.

### Refractory Fire Brick
Bricks that must be fitted will be accurately laid out and wet cut with a diamond blade.
Bricks will be laid up with thin mortar joints.  The joint thickness will average 1.6mm.  Joints that are 3mm or thicker are not acceptable.  Each brickwork joint will be completely filled with mortar except where expansion joints are provided.  The inside face of the brick will be left reasonably free of mortar.  Vertical joints will be staggered.  Courses will be held straight to line, true to straight edge, and flush on the surface.

### Clean-Up
The completed lining will present a clean, workmanlike appearance.  The lining installer will remove all loose mortar, bricks, scaffolding and debris from the inside of the equipment and from the general work area around the equipment.

## Inspection
The work will be inspected by a representative of the purchaser at the following stages:
Before application of insulating fire brick to ensure that the metal surfaces have been prepared in accordance with this specification.
At stages during the brick work installation to ensure that the requirements of this specification are being followed, and that high standards of workmanship are being maintained.
After installation is complete to check that clean-up is in accordance with this specification.
Lining installations which are not in accordance with this specification or the lining installer’s drawings, or in which poor workmanship, injurious defects, improper application, excessive repairs are indicated will be subject to rejection.

## Curing of Lining
After the installation is complete and has been inspected the lining will be cured in accordance with the instructions provided by the Vendor of the lining.
