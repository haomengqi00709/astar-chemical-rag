# 4-PRC-0001 — Procedure


[TABLE: 4-PRC-0001_table_1]


## Purpose
This procedure describes the requirements for standard identification numbers and names for plant equipment.
It is the responsibility of each  to develop and maintain standard identification numbers and names for plant equipment for their assigned technologies.
The standard identification numbers will be used for the development of all related numbering systems such as cost codes, document numbers, etc.
The standard identification numbers and names will be used on all projects unless specific  requirements dictate otherwise. In such cases, the project team will maintain a dual system on the project equipment list to provide a cross-reference.

## Number Format
1016P
10:	Process Area
16:	Serial Number
P:	Equipment Type

## Process Area
Each technology will be subdivided into process areas. Each process area will be a section of plant that fulfils a distinct process function. Each process area will typically be located within a continuous physical area of a plant.
Each process area will be given a two-digit identification number. Numbers beginning with 0 will not be used. Numbers may be repeated across technologies.

## Serial Number
A unique two-digit number is assigned, one per equipment item. Individual equipment items within “equipment packages” will be given unique numbers; the packages will not be numbered.
In general, serial numbers should ascend in the direction of major process flow.
The serial number is unique per basic process function, but may be assigned different equipment type identifiers if there are different methods of achieving that function:
1056U	Weak Brine Dechlorinator (Packed Column type)
1056T	Weak Brine Dechlorinator (Drum type)

## Equipment Type
A single letter designates the type of equipment item. Equipment type identifiers are as follows:
A:	Dispersion:	Agitator, Mixer, Blender, Aerator
B:	Distribution:	Distributor, Spray Nozzle, Spray Pipe
C:	Gas Motive:	Blower, Compressor, Fan
D:	Gas Cleaning:	Cyclone, Precipitator, Dust Collection
E:	Electrical:	Transformer, Motor Control Center, Switchgear
F:	Combustion:	Furnace, Fired Boiler, Incinerator, Kiln, Stack
G:	Diminution:	Grinder, Crusher, Classifier
H:	Heat Transfer:	Heater, Cooler, Cooling Tower, Economiser
J:	Evaporation:	Crystalliser, Evaporator
K:	Separation:	Filter, Decanter, Clarifier, Mesh Pad, Candle Filter
M:	Material Handling:	Conveyor, Elevator, Bin, Hopper, Crane
P:	Liquid Motive:	Pump
R:	Reaction:	Reactor, Converter, Electrolyser, Ion Exchange
S:	Services:	Safety Shower, Fire Fighting
T:	Storage:	Tank, Receiver, Drum, Sump
U:	Gas-Liquid Transfer:	Tower, Column, Stripper, Absorber
V:	Vacuum:	Vacuum Pump, Ejector
X:	Miscellaneous

## Application for Duplicate Items
Where multiple pieces of the same equipment are installed, a suffix is used for identification.
Duplicate equipment that is part of parallel processing systems will be identified with a numeric suffix (e.g. -1, -2, etc.).
Duplicate equipment within a single processing system will be identified with a letter suffix
(e.g. -A, -B, etc.).
Combinations of suffixes may also be used where appropriate (e.g. –1A, -1B, etc.).

## Equipment Names
Equipment names will indicate process function, and should be generic rather than specific as to type e.g. Sulphur Pit Heater rather than Sulphur Pit Steam Coil.
COMMENTARY
To Section 1: The benefit is to provide a consistent numbering system to use as the basis for collecting project information for future estimating, for effective referencing in tech-service reports, and for a referencing base for design procedures.
The structure of the numbering system has been chosen to provide a basis for related numbering systems for piping and instrumentation as described in 5-PRC-0001: Plant Pipe Numbering and 8-PRC-0001: Plant Instrument Numbering. In assigning standard numbers to equipment, it is important to ensure that the intent of all three documents can be realised.
