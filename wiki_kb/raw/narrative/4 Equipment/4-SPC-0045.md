# 4-SPC-0045 — Specification


[TABLE: 4-SPC-0045_table_1]


## Purpose
This specification defines Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ requirements for welding and associated procedures for vessels and equipment made of tantalum alloys with UNS number R05252.

## Codes and Standards
4-SPC-0040: Shop Fabricated Metallic Vessels and Metallic Vessel Components lists the codes and standards for design and fabrication.

## Special Requirements
The tantalum alloy grades listed in this specification are approved for use.

## Materials
Only tantalum 2.5% tungsten alloy UNS R05252 (Ta 2.5% W) is approved for use.  Approved suppliers are H.C. Starck and Cabot Corporation.  Alternative alloys or suppliers require prior written approval from Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.  Tubes will be conform to ASTM B521 (latest edition).  Matching filler metals from the alloy suppliers will be used for all welds.

## Welding Process

### General
Only gas tungsten arc welding (GTAW) is acceptable.

### Shielding Gas
The shielding gas will be moisture free argon, helium or a mixture of the two.  Gas handling hoses will be of Tygon® or vinyl plastic material.  Rubber hoses will not be used.

## Fabrication
All tantalum welding will be completed in a clean, segregated area within the Vendor’s facility. Provision will be made to prevent oxidation or contamination of ALL tantalum surfaces during welding and cooling operations

### Forming
Forming tools which have been previously used for other materials will be cleaned to remove surface contamination.  All burrs and indentations will be ground smooth.

### Grinding and Brushing
Grinding wheels and wire brushes will be clean, and will not have been previously used on any other material.  Grit for abrasive materials will be alumina only.  Brushes will be fabricated from austenitic stainless steel.

## Heat Treatment
Stress relief of formed parts will not be done unless specifically requested in writing by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

## Tubing Non-Destructive Examination Requirements
Each length of tubing will be examined as follows:

### Visual Examination
100% external examination for surface irregularities and damage.

### Dimensional
Out of tolerance dimensions will be cause for rejection.

### Ultrasonic Examination
Ultrasonic examination (immersion type) is the preferred method.
When ultrasonic examination cannot be performed, Aker Kvaerner Chemetics will be so advised at the quotation stage.
Ultrasonic examination will be completed by an Aker Kvaerner Chemetics approved agency.
The calibration standard will be of the same diameter, wall thickness and grade of material as the tube being tested.  Internal and external longitudinal and transverse notches will be 6mm (1/4”) maximum in length, 0.125mm (0.005”) nominal width, with depth not to exceed 10% of the nominal wall thickness.
The indication from the calibration defects will be set at 80% of full screen height.
The alarm level will be set at not less than 50% of the minimum indication from the calibration defects.
All indications causing an alarm will be investigated and manually maximized.
Indications exceeding 75% of the minimum calibration indication will be cause for rejection.
An appropriate multi-channel strip chart recorder will be used for all ultrasonic examination.
Copies of all strip chart recordings and examination reports will be submitted to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics prior to any tube being used for manufacture.
The ultrasonic examination procedure will be submitted to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics prior to commencement of the test.
The Vendor or N.D.E. contractor will demonstrate adequate proficiency using the proprosed procedure prior to commencement of the test.
Rejected tubes will be classified as to defect type, since further evaluation may be necessary.

### Radiographic Examination
When ultrasonic examination is not available, radiographic examination is required.
Tubes will be 100% radiographed to an Aker Kvaerner Chemetics approved procedure showing established defect limits.
The technique used will maximize sensitivity to weld defects.
A report will be submitted to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics prior to any tube being used for manufacture.
In addition to radiographic examination, 20% of tubes will be internally examined by boroscope.  The percentage will be increased to 50% upon detection of any defect.  Visual defects will be followed up by exploratory radiography.

### Hydrostatic Test
Hydrostatic tests will be completed in accordance with ASTM B521 (latest edition).

### Pneumatic Test
A pneumatic proof test, when specified, will be performed after the hydrostatic test has been accepted.  The pneumatic proof test pressure will be held for 30 seconds minimum.
Alternatively, a helium leak test of all tubes can be performed provided the Vendor has all the standard testing equipment and procedures available.  This test will satisfy ASME Code requirements.
