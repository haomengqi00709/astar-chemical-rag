# 4-SPC-0047-2 — Specification


[TABLE: 4-SPC-0047-2_table_1]


## Purpose
This specification defines Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ requirements for stainless steel heat exchanger tubes which are to be used in the fabrication of gas to gas heat exchangers in acid plants.  The specification applies to ASTM A249/SA249 types 304/304L and 304H.

## Codes and Standards
ASTM A249/SA249 (latest versions), and the applicable standards to which it refers.
Dimensional tolerances will conform to ASTM A1016 (latest version).

## NDE Requirements
Material testing and general requirements as described in ASTM A249 (and A1016) are mandatory
ASTM A249 supplementary requirement S3 - the air-under water pressure test (A1016 para. 27) - is required on 100% of the tubes.
The Hydrostatic test (A1016 para. 26) is required on 100% of the tubes
A249 supplementary requirement S7 (weld decay test) with a corrosion ratio of 1.2 or less is required on each heat of tube
The reverse bend test (A249 para.11) will be done a minimum of once every 100 tubes
A -ring gauge test will be done on 100% of the tubes. A 50 mm long ring gauge with ID to match the maximum allowable OD of the tube will be passed over 100% of the  length of all the tubes.  This ensures that the tube is not too large to fit into the hole in the tube-sheet of the heat exchanger.

## Additional Requirements (Mandatory for first- time suppliers)
All tube ends (both ends of each tube) will be PT tested on the inside diameter upto a minimum of 50mm inside the tube bore.  PT testing will be will be done in batches of 50, starting as soon as the first 50 tubes are completed.  Indications on any of the 50 tubes will be investigated by slitting the tube longitudinally and PT testing the entire tube length to investigate the severity of the problem.  Indications will be reported to  immediately.
Every 100th tube, starting at tube 1, will be cut lengthwise and PT tested.  Indications will be reported to  immediately.
