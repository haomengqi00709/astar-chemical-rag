# 4-SPC-7540 — Specification


[TABLE: 4-SPC-7540_table_1]


## Purpose
This specification defines Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ requirements for the shop fabrication of converter components.

## Scope of Supply

### Goods and Services by Vendor:
All stainless steel and carbon steel components of the converter including shell, heads, nozzles, internals, etc.
Non-standard field erection items such as grid / baffle installation braces, tier bracing beams and temporary lifting lugs
Material for field weld run off tabs.  Material will be of the same specification and grade as the parent material.
Trial fitting, match marking and tagging as described below
Packaging and preparation for shipment
Documentation listed on 4-VDR-7540: Vendor Data Requirements.

### Goods and Services by Others:
Ceramic Saddles
Quartz pebbles
Wire mesh for grid plates
Catalyst
Thermo wells
Standard field fit up lugs, strong backs, spreader bars
Insulation support hardware, insulation, cladding
Field welding consumables

## Fabrication Tolerances
Tolerances will be per Appendix A and as noted below
Variation in circumferential strapping between adjacent courses will be such that the maximum offset will be within ASME tolerances.
If a conflict occurs in the out of roundness tolerances, the lowest number will govern.
Concentricity and out of roundness tolerances are not cumulative.

## Plate Tolerances
As per applicable ASTM material tolerances for purchased plate size.

## Extent of Pre-Fabrication
Vendor will provide the largest size pieces that can be economically shipped.  Generally this will mean plates of up to 2.4m x 12m (8’ x 40’) size.  Vendor to consider most economical alternative and discuss with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
General guidelines:
Shop fabricated subassembly sizes to be maximized within shipping restrictions
Fabrication of Converter to be maximized in shop so as to minimize field fabrication/erection
All field weld edge preps will be done in shop per the Vendor’s approved weld procedure(s)
All stainless steel to carbon steel welds to be done in shop
All subassemblies to be trial fitted, match marked and disassembled for shipping
Shell plates to be rolled
Head plates formed, trial assembled and match marked
Baffle and grid plates rolled and joining flat bars shop welded to one side.
Nozzles prefabricated complete with transition section
Rolled shell courses will be trial fitted (complete circle), checked for dimensional and level accuracy and match marked course by course.
Core completely fabricated in shippable sections
All parts will be tagged as outlined in the converter field erection manual.
