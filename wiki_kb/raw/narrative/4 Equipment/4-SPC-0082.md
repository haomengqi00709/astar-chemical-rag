# 4-SPC-0082 — Specification


[TABLE: 4-SPC-0082_table_1]


## Purpose
This specification defines Aker Kvaerner Chemetics requirements for refractory brick linings installed in steel vessels and ducting.

## Scope of Supply

### Goods and Services by Vendor
Detail design of refractory brick lining system including but not limited to:
Mechanical design of brick lining, considering radial thermal expansion of lining, radial thermal expansion of steel shell, and the interaction between the two.
Design of longitudinal expansion joints in the brick
Thermal design of thermal insulation external to the vessel
Design criteria for the vessel in which the lining will be installed
Installation and curing instructions
Dry-out (curing) curves for the refractory lining system
Supply of all materials and consumables including but not limited to:
Refractory brick
Insulating brick
Castable
Mortar
Ceramic paper
Ceramic fibre (e.g. for expansion joints)
Anchors

### Testing by Vendor
Vendor will test all materials in accordance with ASTM requirements.

### Spare Parts by Vendor
Vendor will supply sufficient extra material to allow for typical breakage/wastage during shipping and site installation.

Curing of completed refractory lining.

## Process Requirements

### System Description
The Regeneration Furnace is used to decompose spent sulphuric acid/ammonium bisulphate compounds coming from an MMA generation process.  Feeds to the furnace are:
Spent sulphuric acid
Ammonium bisulphate
Water and organics
Molten Sulphur
Strong sulphuric acid containing nitric acid (“candle drips”)
Process vent gases containing organics
Furnace ash conditioner – liquid “alum” (aluminum sulphate)
Burner combustion gases
Furnace Burners include a fuel oil burner and a combination fuel oil/organics burner. The purpose of the burners is to provide the heat required to dissociate the acid compounds, combust the organic compounds, produce an SO2 process gas, and heat the combustion products to the required exit conditions.
Conditions at the exit of the furnace are 980 oC to 1100 oC and 1.5 % O2 (by volume, wet basis).  The SO2 content is about 5 to 6 vol% (wet basis).
Both burners will be able to run at a range of fuel to air ratios, bounded by the conditions:
15 % excess air
55 % excess air
Secondary air is added at the front of the furnace, and is used to control % O2 at the exit of the furnace.
Spent acid will be injected through acid guns into the burner flame from above the centreline.  Other furnace feeds (candle drips, sulphur, vents, etc) will be injected through sprays at the front of the furnace.

### Design of Lining
Refractory linings will be a minimum of 228mm thick mullite bonded high alumina firebrick backed by 114mm thick insulating firebrick.  Additional requirements may be shown on vessel and ducting drawings.
Final lining detail design will be by the Vendor and performance of the lining will be his sole responsibility.  The Vendor will clearly state where his recommendations differ from the basic design criteria set by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
The brick lining for equipment and ductwork will be designed so that spacing between mitres and/or appurtenance allows at least one 114mm wide brick in the parent lining between such mitres and/or appurtenances.
Lining in heads will be dished or cone keyed (not flat walls).
Nozzle opening will be designed using “bulls-eyes” of shaped brick wherever possible.
The refractory lining system must be designed with sufficient expansion joints to accommodate thermal expansion of the lining system anticipated at the maximum gas operating temperature shown on the equipment drawings.  The Vendor will submit calculations to demonstrate that the thermal expansion of the lining has been accounted for and accommodated in the design.

### Materials
High alumina fire brick will conform to the latest edition of ASTM C27
Insulating brick will conform to the latest edition of ASTM C115
Burner inlet nozzle rings will be recommended by the Vendor
Mortar for all fire brick and insulating brick will be air setting with a minimum temperature limit of 1650ºC.  Fire brick mortar will be phosphate bonded, high alumina mortar or approved equal.
Insulating brick mortar will be alumina/silica mortar or approved equal.
See equipment drawings for additional requirements

## Guarantee
The Vendor will guarantee that the lining system will be correct for the application, will require no repairs for 5 years and will have a life of 20 years with minimal maintenance repairs.
