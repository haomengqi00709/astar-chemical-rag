# 4-SPC-0003 — Specification


[TABLE: 4-SPC-0003_table_1]


## Purpose
This specification defines the general requirements for the design, fabrication and supply of horizontal centrifugal magnetic drive pumps.

## Scope
The Vendor will supply the equipment fully assembled with all items required for a complete functional unit including the following major components:
Pump assembly
[Motor]
[Power monitor]
Baseplate

## Process Requirements
The pumps will meet the process requirements as detailed in the individual Pump Data Sheets.  If a Pump Data Sheet states a flow rate value + ‘Vendor Minimum Continuous Flow’ (MCF) the Vendor will state their recommended MCF rate for the pump they are selecting, and add this MCF to the flow rate value given on the Data Sheet.  For example, if the Pump Data Sheet states a 10 m3/h + ‘Vendor Minimum Continuous Flow’, and the recommended MCF rate for the pump being selected is 1 m3/h, then the duty point will become 11 m3/h.
The pumps will have stable head/capacity curves that continuously rise to shut-off.
The pumps will not have a design duty point that is to the right of the head/capacity curve Best Efficiency Point unless approved by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

## Mechanical Requirements
The Equipment will be dynamically balanced in accordance with ISO1940: Mechanical vibration - Balance quality requirements of rigid rotors - grade [G6.3].
The Vendor will provide the estimated noise level of the pumps.
The selected impeller diameter will neither be greater than 90% of the maximum size available for the selected pump casing nor less than 110% of the minimum size without approval from Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.  Once a pump casing has been selected, the Vendor will proceed with the maximum size impeller for the casing, and will not trim the impeller until after the final size is confirmed by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, in writing.
The maximum operating speed will be less than the synchronous speed of a two-pole motor.
The pump suction and discharge connections will be flat faced flanged and will match the [DIN PN10] standard.  A casing drain will be provided with [an NPT] plug.
Pumps will be designed for back pull out and close-coupled assembly with flange mounted motor.
Baseplates will be [painted carbon steel] and will be large enough to accept motors one size larger than the size selected.
Impellers will be closed unless noted otherwise on the individual Pump Data Sheets.
Vendor will state the length of time that the pumps can run dry.
Motors will be sized for the run-out condition at the selected impeller size. Magnetic drive couplings will have torque ratings greater than or equal to the maximum motor torque the motor is rated for.
[Vendor] will supply a power monitor for pump protection.
General equipment requirements will be per 4-SPC-0001: General Equipment Requirements.
