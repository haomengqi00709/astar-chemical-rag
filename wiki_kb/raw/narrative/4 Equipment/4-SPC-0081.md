# 4-SPC-0081 — Specification


[TABLE: 4-SPC-0081_table_1]


## Purpose
This specification details Aker Kvaerner Chemetics requirements for:
Fabricated steel components that will be rubber lined
Sheet rubber selection, application, curing, testing and repair.

## Codes and Standards
4-SPC-0040: Metallic Vessel Fabrication lists the codes and standards for design and fabrication of the vessel to be lined.
NACE RP0298-98 “Sheet Rubber Linings for Abrasion and Corrosion Service”.
NACE RP0178-98 “Fabrication Details, Surface Finish Requirements and Proper Design Considerations for Tanks and Vessels to be lined for Immersion Service”.
Note: Both of the above NACE publications can be obtained at nominal cost from www.nace.org.

## Additional Requirements
The type and thickness of rubber lining will be agreed upon between the Vendor and Aker Kvaerner Chemetics before lining work is started.
Rubber lined equipment will be clearly marked on the outside "Rubber Lined - Handle With Care - Do Not Weld”. This will be done in paint stencil with minimum 75mm high letters.
It is the responsibility of the Vendor to ensure that the vessel or component fabricator has taken all the precautions necessary for good rubber lining installation.  It is anticipated that the Vendor of the completed equipment is also the lining applicator.  It is therefore the Vendors responsibility to ensure that the component steel surfaces to be rubber lined are acceptable for rubber lining.
