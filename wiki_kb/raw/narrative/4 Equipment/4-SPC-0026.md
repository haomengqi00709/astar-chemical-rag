# 4-SPC-0026 — Specification


[TABLE: 4-SPC-0026_table_1]


## Purpose
The purpose of this Standard is to define the workmanship and quality requirements of welded fabrications in nickel/molybdenum/chromium alloys for use in aggressively corrosive environments.

## Scope
This Standard applies when called for on purchase orders and referenced documents.
This Standard covers the welding and other fabrication requirements for vessels, heat exchangers, tanks, piping and miscellaneous welded items.  The material is grouped under No. P44 of the ASME Code.
Hastelloy C-276 & C-22 have the UNS designations N10276 and N06022, respectively.

## Definitions
Codes, etc., shall be taken to mean latest edition ruling at date of contract.
Agreement in Writing - Where prior approval in writing is required by this Standard, this shall be taken to mean submission in writing by letter and not merely by inclusion in approval drawings.
Inspector - Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Quality Assurance Inspector or other Authorized Representative.

## General Requirements

### Sub-Contracting
No Sub-Contracting of welding is permitted without Aker Kvaerner Chemetics written approval.

### Supervision
The fabricator shall ensure that there is regular and systematic supervision of all work both in-shop and on-site by competent staff.

### Procedure Submittal
All welding procedures shall be submitted by the fabricator for review and agreement by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Q.A. & Materials Group prior to commencement of any work.  The ASME code section IX PQR Form 483 shall form part of this package.
The procedure shall be qualified on the same grade of material as that selected for fabrication.
A change in essential variables as outlined in ASME Code Section IX will require resubmission of procedure(s).

### Welders' Qualifications
All welders to be employed on Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' work shall be suitably qualified in accordance with relevant codes and standards for every procedure he is to use.
The fabricator shall, upon request, supply Aker Kvaerner Chemetics with objective evidence of current welder qualifications.
All qualified welders shall be allocated an identification symbol which shall be marked on the parent material adjacent to each welder's work, allowing traceability during fabrication.

### Cleanliness
The surface and edges of the material which is to be heated for any purpose (e.g. fusion cutting, forming or welding) shall be cleaned free from all traces of foreign matter (e.g. marking paint, oil, etc.) by grinding, wire brushing with a stainless steel brush and/or non-chlorinated solvent cleaning immediately prior to the application of heat.
The fabricator shall take the controls necessary to prevent contamination of weldments at all stages of fabrication.  These controls shall include the segregation of wire brushes, grinding wheels, etc., for use on Hastelloy materials only.
On completion of work, items shall be cleaned internally and externally.  All fabrication markings, weld spatter, etc., shall be removed prior to shipment.  Sulphur, phosphorous or lead bearing paints or crayon shall not be used for marking or identification purposes.
Passivation, where required by Purchase Order/referenced documents, shall be carried out in accordance with a written procedure, which must be approved by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics prior to its use.

### Drawings
All work shall be carried out in accordance with the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics "Approved for Fabrication" shop detail drawing(s).  Deviations from the weld geometry as shown on approved drawing(s) are not permitted without the prior approval of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

## Materials

### Base Materials
Plate, sheet, pipe, and tube shall be purchased in accordance with ASME specifications SB574, SB575 and SB619, SB622 and SB626.
Plate and sheet shall be supplied in the solution annealed condition for maximum corrosion resistance unless specified otherwise on the purchase order.

### Filler Materials
All weld filler metals shall meet the requirements of Section 8 of this Standard.
All fabricators shall ensure and demonstrate to the Inspector that the manufacturers' instructions regarding the storage, drying and use of weld consumables are being fully implemented both in the shop and on-site; in particular that all wet electrodes are discarded and not baked and reused.
The fabricator shall, prior to commencement of work, furnish Aker Kvaerner Chemetics with the brand name of the filler materials to be used.

### Certification
Certification showing the results obtained from analysis and other tests carried out in accordance with the relevant material specification shall be supplied to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for all materials including welding consumables.

### Traceability
Fabricator shall maintain full traceability of materials back to original mill certification.
As a minimum, the fabricator's internal controls shall be such as to prevent the use of unspecified materials.
Lack of adequate identification or certification shall be cause for rejection of the specific component or item.

## Preparation Of Edges For Welding

### Joint Design
The joint design for nickel/molybdenum/chromium alloys is slightly different from other materials due  to the characteristics of the molten metal.  Material thicker than 0.125" shall be bevelled to form a 'V', 'U' or 'J' groove and shall follow the material suppliers welding practice with respect to root opening and land thickness.

### Cutting to Size
Material shall be cut to size by:
Machining
Shearing
Where necessary materials over 3/l6" thick shall have extra material left on sheared edge to allow machining, grinding, etc., to ensure that any defects caused by shearing can be removed.
Material over l" thick shall not be sheared.
Thermal Cutting
It is preferable that thermal cutting shall be carried out by the Plasma Arc Process and the welding edges lightly ground to bright metal where necessary.  Any other process shall require that on completion of thermal cutting, a minimum of l/16" of metal be removed by mechanical means to ensure that all slag, scale, other irregularities and contaminants, including thermal effects, have been removed.
Grinding
Silicon carbide wheels, dedicated to Hastelloy Alloys shall be used.

### Forming
Materials shall be cold formed as far as practical.  When hot forming is necessary, the procedure to be adopted shall be agreed to in writing by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics prior to commencement of work.

## Welding Processes

### Welding Methods
The following processes are the preferred methods of welding nickel-base alloys.  Other process will be evaluated on submission of full details including procedure and exact location of proposed weldments.  Submerged arc welding is not recommended.
Shielded Metal Arc Welding 	(SMAW)
Gas Tungsten Arc Welding 	(GTAW)
Gas Metal Arc Welding 	(GMAW)
The general welding parameters of current, voltage, electrode size, etc., as well as special requirements or precautions, should be obtained from  the electrode supplier.

### Shielded Metal Arc Welding
Shielded metal arc welding electrodes shall satisfy the requirements of SFA-5.11.
The electrode shall be Designated AWS Classification ENiCrMo-4 for C-276, ENiCrMo-10 for C-22.
Minimum practical amperage, and minimum arc length are recommended in order to control the weld puddle.  A straight stringer bead is generally desirable.

### Gas Tungsten Arc Welding
Gas tungsten arc welding rods shall satisfy the requirements of SFA-5.14.
The filler wire diameter shall be kept small preferably l/l6" and shall be designated AWS Classification ERNiCrMo-4 for C-276, ERNiCrMo-10 for C-22.
Preferred electrode is thoriated tungsten as per SFA-5.l2, Class EWTh-2; with a tapered tip for good arc stability and penetration.
The power supply shall incorporate a high frequency (HF) voltage to start the arc, and a current decay unit with foot control to eliminate or minimize crater cracking.
Direct current straight polarity shall be used (D.C.S.P. - Electrode negative) for both manual or machine welding.
Weld metal thickness shall not exceed l/8"/pass.
A back-up gas shall be used to prevent root pass oxidation and contamination.  e.g. Piping or where root pass is inaccessible.
The Tungsten Arc Torch shall have a gas diffuser screen for optimum gas coverage and the gas cup size should be as large as practical.

### Gas Metal Arc Welding
GMAW shall only be used for the root pass of butt welds and for fillet welds unless specifically agreed otherwise.
Short-circuiting transfer or the Pulsed Arc transfer process shall be used.
Direct current reverse polarity shall be used (D.C.R.P. - Electrode positive).
The gas cup size shall be as large as practical.
Fabricator must provide satisfactory evidence of continuous shop use of this process in the fabrication of nickel-base alloys.

### Submerged Arc Welding
Use of this process must be reviewed and approved in writing by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics prior to start of fabrication.
Fabricator must provide sectioned samples and satisfactory evidence of continuous shop use of this process in the fabrication of Hastelloy C-276 & C-22.

### Preheat & Postweld Heat Treatment
Preheat and postweld heat treatment are not recommended.  However, when adverse weather conditions give rise to condensation or wetness on the surfaces of the component, the material shall be dried prior to welding by heating to a temperature just sufficient to prevent condensation.  It should be noted that interpass temperatures shall not exceed 200F.

## Workmanship

### General

#### Procedure Tests
The welding procedure shall be agreed upon (see Para 5.3) before any production welding is begun.

#### Welding Position
As far as practical, all welds shall be made in the downhand (flat) position.
Vertical welds shall be made vertical up (uphand).

#### Weather Protection for Welding On-site
Suitable enclosures shall be provided to keep welds and welders free from draughts, rain or other inclement weather.  Welding shall not be performed on wet surfaces or with wet or damp electrodes.

#### Fusion Faces
The fusion faces and the adjacent metal, both internal and external, shall be free from moisture, scale, paint, grease or other foreign matter immediately prior to welding.

### Alignment of Butt Joints
The alignment of welding edges at butt joints shall be in accordance with relevant code, specification, etc., but maximum values for misalignment (High - Low) shall be as follows:
For plates up to and including 1/4" in thickness, 1/l6".
For plates above 1/4" in thickness, 25% of the plate thickness or 1/8", whichever is smaller.
For pipe, shall be in accordance with ASME B3l.3.

### Assembly for Welding
To maintain the specified alignment and gap (where used) during welding, the parts to be welded shall be securely held in position by mechanical means, welded-on bridge pieces or by tack welding.

#### Mechanical Means
Where a jig or other suitable device is used, the construction of the jig shall allow adequate access for welding and cleaning and shall be such that external loading is not applied to a joint during welding.

#### Bridge Pieces/Support Spiders
Welded-on bridge or support spiders shall be of austenitic steel on the surface welded in contact with the vessel or heat exchanger.
Where welded-on bridge pieces or support spiders are used, the plate surface shall not be left in a damaged condition after they are removed.

#### Tack Welds
Tack welds shall be performed by qualified welders.  Tack welds employed for assembly purposes may form part of the completed weld provided that complete fusion is effected at the root of the weld and that the welding procedure used is the same as that employed for the remainder of the root run.
Cracked and unsound tack welds shall be cut out prior to welding.
Sound tack welds must be thoroughly fused into the root run of the main weld.  It is sometimes necessary to thin down the ends of tack welds to ensure that complete fusion is achieved.
Where this is necessary, tack welds must be crack free and should preferably be at least one inch long to ensure that the remaining part of the tack weld is strong enough to hold the plate or pipe in place.

#### Backing Bars
The use of permanent back-up rings or strips or consumable joint inserts is not permitted unless specifically approved in writing by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Q.A. & Materials Group.

#### Run-off Pads
Run-off pads, in the same material as the parent plate, shall be used at the beginning and end of each plate weld.

### Striking of Welding Arc
Every effort shall be made to confine arc strikes to surfaces on which weld metal is to be deposited.  If such a strike should occur outside this area the spot will require to be ground, PT examined per Para l0.3 and, if necessary, repaired.
Burn marks from grounding clamps shall be treated as arc strikes.

### Cleaning and Re-starting
Each run of weld metal shall be carefully examined to ensure that it has been thoroughly cleaned and is defect free.  All slag and foreign matter shall be removed before the next run is deposited.  This may be achieved by grinding followed by wire brushing.  On re-starting, care shall be taken to ensure proper fusion with the weld metal previously deposited.
All re-starts in the root bead shall be preceded by "feathering" out the area of the stop by grinding or filing, followed with wire brushing to remove any abrasive residue.

### Electrode Touch Down
If a tungsten electrode accidentally makes contact with the work, the section of weld and the tip of the electrode should be examined.  Where tungsten loss has occurred, the tungsten pick-up in the weld shall be ground out and the electrode reground to the correct profile before restarting.
Should the heated end of the filler rod become contaminated (i.e. fall outside of the inert atmosphere at the weld puddle), it shall be snipped off and discarded.

### Peening and Hammering
Peening of weld runs and hammering of finished welds is not permitted.

### Form of Welds
Each weld shall be uniform in width and size throughout its full length. Each layer of welding shall be smooth and free of slag, inclusions, cracks, porosity, lack of fusion, and undercut except to the extent permitted in the referenced codes.  In addition, the cover pass shall be free of coarse ripples, irregular surface, non-uniform bead patterns, high crown, and deep ridges or valleys between beads.
Buttwelds shall be slightly convex, of uniform height, and have full penetration.  In the case of pipe welds, internal reinforcement shall be subject to the limitations of the governing code.
Crevices or undercut shall not be permitted on the process side of the weld.
As far as is practicable, fillet welds shall be deposited in not less than two layers and their surfaces shall be free from marked irregularities or concavity.
Dimensions of fillet welds shall be in accordance with the approved drawing.

### Dressing of Welds & Repair Areas
Grinding shall not be carried out in such a manner as to reduce the adjacent base metal thickness.  Note that complete removal of weld defects shall be verified by non-destructive examination prior to repair welding.
Grinding wheels shall be iron-free and of the resin bonded silicon carbide type.
Wire brushes or chipping tools shall be made of austenitic stainless steel and must not have been previously used to clean surfaces other than Hastelloy Alloys.

## Quality Control

### General
The Inspector shall have free access to the Vendor's work and to his Subvendor's, if work is sublet by approval, at all reasonable times.  Access shall also be given to Authorized Representatives of the End-User.
The Inspector shall be at liberty to inspect the work during fabrication at any stage in manufacture and to reject any material or workmanship which does not conform to this Standard or the applicable code.
Inspection by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics does not relieve the fabricator or erector of responsibility in accordance with the requirements of this Standard, engineering design and the applicable code.

### Chemical Analysis
Supplier must make available to the Inspector copies of chemical certification for  each batch of electrodes or wire coils used.  If electrodes, wire, etc., cannot be identified to the Inspector's satisfaction, samples shall be removed for chemical analysis.  This shall be to supplier's account.
When required by purchase order or where the fabricator cannot identify the weld material, a spot check analysis shall be performed on production welds of critical equipment such as pressure vessels, heat exchangers, pressure piping and certain proprietary items.  This shall also include non-pressure retaining equipment in dangerous or demanding services.
Normally the weld(s) to be checked shall be selected at random by the Inspector.
Where results of chemical analysis show that a specific weldment falls outside of the approved Standard or code requirements, the supplier shall promptly advise Aker Kvaerner Chemetics in writing of his proposed course of action.  Such action shall include details of any additional testing necessary to fully define the extent of the non-conformance.  All repairs and retests shall be to the supplier's account.

### Dye Penetrant Inspection (PT)
Items fabricated in accordance with the ASME Code Section VIII, Div. l, shall be PT tested on all welds.
Unless otherwise specified on the purchase order, approved drawings or Standards, non-coded items shall have butt and fillet welds, nozzle attachment welds, etc., 100 percent PT examined on all surfaces exposed to the process, for cracks, porosity, slag inclusions, significant undercutting or other major defects.
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics shall reserve the right to select the location of the above inspection.
Unless otherwise specified in the Purchase Order or approved drawings, all welds between dissimilar metals shall be l00% PT examined..
Tube to tubesheet welds shall be l00% PT examined.
The PT exam shall be carried out in accordance with a written procedure and in general, the limit of acceptability shall satisfy the ASME Code Section VIII, Division 1, Appendix VIII, except that linear indications shall be considered unacceptable.
All excavated areas shall be PT examined prior to building up flush with the surface of the base material using the welding procedure previously approved by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for the main welding details.
All weld repair surfaces shall be l00% PT examined per the above requirements.
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics reserves the right to accept or reject any doubtful or questionable indications.

### Radiographic Inspection (RT)
Equipment fabricated in accordance with the ASME Code shall have all butt welds 100% radiographed in accordance with Para. UW-5l of Section VIII, Division 1 of the ASME Code where the plate or shell thickness at the welded joint(s) exceeds 3/8".
Equipment fabricated to the ASME Code, but not exceeding 3/8" plate thickness shall be spot radiographed in accordance with Para. UW-52(b) of Section VIII, Division 1.
Spot radiography shall also be the minimum requirement for equipment not fabricated to the ASME Code.
For piping fabricated in accordance with ASME Codes and this Standard, the minimum standard of radiography shall be random radiographed as defined in Para. 341.4.1 (b) of ASME B3l.3 except that 20% of all butt welds shall be radiographed.
For tankage fabricated in accordance with API Standard 620 and this Specification, the minimum requirements shall be spot radiography.
Items other than those described above, fabricated to this Standard only (i.e. not ASME, etc.), shall if required by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics engineering drawings be spot radiographed in accordance with UW-52 (b) or 20% random radiographed to B3l.3 in case of piping.
RT Exam shall be carried out in accordance with a written procedure by personnel qualified to SNTC-1A.
Where additional spot radiography is required, as defined in UW-52 Sub-para. (d), costs of this radiography including all repair costs and re-radiography shall be to the fabricator's account.
Junctions between circumferential and longitudinal seams shall be considered as preferred locations for spot radiography.

### Helium Mass Spectrometer Test
When required by purchase order or referenced drawings tube/tube sheet joints and tube bundles shall be tested using the helium mass spectrometer (reverse probe method) per ASME Code Section V, Article l0.

### Documentation
The fabricator shall furnish a report containing all the data pertaining to each vessel or item as follows:
Prior to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' final inspection of each unit, the fabricator shall have prepared in finished form, three sets of records, except final test data, as set forth in this paragraph.
Material mill test reports
Nameplate rubbing
Hydrostatic test report
A copy of all inspection and test reports
These reports, together with final test data, shall be assembled into three complete sets for each item.  In no case shall a documentation package pertain to more than one vessel or item number.
The Inspector will at the time of witnessing the final tests and certifying correctness of forms, collect the appropriate number of documentation packages for each item tested.
