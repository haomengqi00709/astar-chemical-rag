# 4-SPC-0042 — Specification


[TABLE: 4-SPC-0042_table_1]


## Purpose
This Specification defines the requirements for shop-fabricated fibreglass reinforced plastic (FRP) process tanks or vessels that are not classified as pressure vessels.
This Specification gives Aker Kvaerner Chemetics requirements in the form of exceptions and additions to RTP-1 (1999 Edition of ASME RTP-1: Standard for Reinforced Thermoset Plastic Corrosion Resistant Equipment) referred to herein as RTP-1, and its Mandatory Appendix M-14, Dual Laminate Vessels.  Due to the present lack of RTP-1 accredited fabricators RTP-1 accreditation is not required to fabricate equipment to this Specification.

## General Requirements
Vessels will be designed to RTP-1, except where indicated on drawings and with the exceptions noted in this specification:

### User's Basic Requirements Specification (Para. 1-200)
This Specification and the information contained in the specifications, drawings or data sheets form the basis for design.  The User's Basic Requirements Specification (UBRS) will not be completed.

### Service Restrictions (Para. 1-210)
This Specification will not be used for vessels which are in a critical service.

### Accreditation (Para. 1-520)
Formal accreditation by ASME is not required to supply vessels to this Specification.

### Nameplates (Para. 1-540)
The fabricator will engrave and affix a Aker Kvaerner Chemetics nameplate to each vessel.  The nameplate will be supplied by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

## Materials
Materials comprising the glass fibre reinforced resin laminates and the laminates will substantially be in accordance with Part 2 - Materials.  The fabricator will have adequate methods and procedures in place for receiving reinforcement and matrix materials, therefore, formal adherence to Mandatory Appendices M-2 - Reinforcement Materials Receiving Procedures and M-3 - Matrix Materials Receiving Procedures is not a requirement of this Specification.

### Post Cure
Post cure requirements are specified in the specifications, drawings or data sheets.

### Laminates (Subparts 2A and 2B)
Unless otherwise specified in the specifications, drawings, or data sheets, the fabricator will select either standard contact moulded or non-standard construction for each vessel.  Standard contact moulded construction for all major vessel components will be Type II (mat-woven roving-mat) only.  ype I (all mat) laminates will only be used when specified in the specifications, drawings or data sheets.  Non-standard or Type X laminates including filament would require lamination analyses.

### Inner Surface (para. 2A-221 and 2B-110)
The inner surface exposed to the contents will include flange faces and all surfaces of internal components.  The surface veil is specified in the specifications, data sheets or drawings as single or double C-glass or polyester.  C-glass will be 0.25mm (0.010 in.) thick  grade, chemical resistant veil.  Polyester will be 0.38mm (0.015 in.) thick aperture type, Nexus surfacing fabric manufactured by Precision Fabrics Group or Aker Kvaerner Chemetics approved equal.  Unless otherwise specified in the specifications, drawings, or data sheets the inner surface will consist of one layer of resin saturated surface veil.

### Interior Layer (Para. 2A-222 and 2B-120)
Only 0.46 kg/m2 (1½ oz/ft2.), 1.1mm (0.043 in.) thick, commercial grade, E type (electrical) or ECR type (electrical-chemical) chopped strand mat is permitted in the secondary corrosion liner interior layer. Chopped roving (spray-up) is not permitted.

### Structural Layer (Para. 2A-223 and 2B-130)
Unless otherwise agreed by , the FRP structural armouring for dual laminate vessels will be, Type II (mat-woven roving-mat) laminate only.
Only mat and 0.82 kg/m2 (24 oz/yd2) commercial grade, E type woven rovings are permitted in the structural layer of Type II laminates.  In addition to mat and woven roving, commercial grade, E type continuous filament strand roving is permitted in the structural layer of Type X laminates.  Chopped roving (spray-up) is not permitted in the structural layer of any laminates.

### Outer Surface (Para. 2A-224 and 2B-140)
The outer surface which is a paraffinated resin coat and a resin rich C-glass surfacing veil will be applied over the final mat ply of the structural layer.  An ultraviolet absorber, a fire retardant synergist and pigmentation (pigmentation colour to be specified in the specifications, data sheets or drawings) will be incorporated in the outer surface.  All additives will be used as recommended by the resin manufacturer.  As a minimum, the final mat ply of the structural laminate requires a fire retardant resin system and synergist. If additional laminate areas require fire protection, these will be specified in the project specifications, data sheets, or drawings.

### Lamination Analysis Method (Para. 2B-200(c))
The use of commercial software such as Superlam by Fiberglass Canada Ltd. are permitted to determine the laminate properties using Lamination Analysis.  Lamination analysis calculations will be submitted to  for review.

## Design

### Design will substantially be in accordance with Part 3 - Design and Non mandatory Appendices NM-2, NM-3, NM-4, NM-5, NM-12 and NM-13.

### Wall Thickness (Part 3 and Non-mandatory Appendix NM-13)
All calculations for references to wall thickness will apply to the structural layer of the laminate only.

### Minimum Thickness (Para. 3-200(f))
The minimum average structural wall thickness of any pressure containing part is to be 7mm (9/32”).

## Fabrication

### Fabrication will substantially be in accordance with Part 4 - Fabrication regardless of vessel size or if Subpart 3B design rules are employed.

### Flat Bottom Tank Head to Shell Joint (Fig. 4-3)
For  tanks, unless otherwise indicated, the bottom head FRP will be in accordance with only Fig. 4-3 (c) or (d). Fig. 4-3 (d).
For any vessel supplied to this Specification, the number of FRP shell joints (regions where the FRP structural laminate is discontinuous/spliced together) will be kept to the absolute minimum practicable.

### Flanged Nozzles (Para. 4-410) and Installation of Nozzles (Para. 4-430)
Flanged nozzles will have FRP with integral construction only (as in Fig. 4-7 (b)). Nozzle flange, integral hub and neck FRP structural layers will be constructed from Type II laminate only. Nozzle attachment and reinforcing pad FRP structural layers will be made from Type II laminate only. Nozzles less than 50mm (2”) dia. will have the same FRP flange and neck structural thickness as for 50mm (2”) dia. flanges.

### Nozzle Gussets (Fig. 4-11)
Nozzle gussets will be in accordance with Fig. 4-11 (a), right side sketch only.  Nozzles 80mm (3”) dia. and smaller will be supported with four equally spaced gussets.

### Manways (Para. 4-500)
Manway requirements will be the same as for nozzles.

### Tolerances (Para. 4-700)
Fabrication tolerances will be in accordance with Figs. 4-1, 4-5, 4-12 and NM7-1.

### Internal and External Components
External pipe support, lifting or hold down lugs and embedded external components will be embedded 300 series stainless steel or solid FRP.

### Lifting lugs
Lifting lugs will be the fabricator’s standard design, but each lug must be capable of lifting the empty vessel plus internals on its own. Lift lug calculations and details are to be submitted to  for approval.

## Inspection and Tests
Inspections and tests will substantially be in accordance with Part 6 - Inspections and Tests.

### Resin Cure Test (Para. 6-910 (b)(1))
The minimum number of Barcol test readings in any vessel area will be as recommended in ASTM D 2583, or as agreed between the fabricator and Inspector. The locations of the Barcol test readings will cover all major vessel areas and be at the Inspector's discretion.

### Laminate Burnout Tests (Para. 6-930 (c))
Laminate burnout tests are required only when requested by .

### Proof Tests (Para. 6-930 (d))
Proof tests are required only when requested by .

### Allowable Laminate Imperfections (Para. 6-940 (a))
The maximum allowable visual defects will be in accordance with Table 1. “Maximum Allowable Visual Defects Quality Level 2”, not Table 6-1 in RTP-1.

### Pressure Tests and Acoustic Emission Tests (Para. 6-950)
Acoustic emission tests are required only when requested by .
