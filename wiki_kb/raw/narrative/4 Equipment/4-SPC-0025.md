# 4-SPC-0025 — Specification


[TABLE: 4-SPC-0025_table_1]


## Purpose
The purpose of this Engineering Standard is to define the workmanship and quality requirements for High Silicon Austenitic stainless steel welded fabrications purchased by/or on behalf of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

## Scope
This Standard applies when called for on purchase orders and referenced documents.
This Standard covers the welding and other fabrication requirements for vessels, heat exchangers, tanks, piping and miscellaneous welded items.
VEW-Bohler A610 is the only stainless steel covered by this Standard.

## Definitions
Codes, etc., shall be taken to mean latest edition ruling at date of contract.
Agreement in Writing - Where prior approval in writing is required by this Standard, this shall be taken to mean submission in writing by letter and not merely by inclusion in approval drawings.

## General Requirements

### Supervision
The fabricator shall ensure that there is regular and systematic supervision of all work both in-shop and on-site in accordance with the fabricator's ASME approved quality control system manual.

### Procedure Submittal
All welding procedures shall be submitted by the fabricator for review and agreement by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Q.A.  & Materials Group prior to commencement of any work.  The ASME IX PQR Form 483 or equivalent shall form part of this package.
The welding procedure submittal package shall also include the following additional test results.  These tests may already have been performed for previous jobs and as such would satisfy Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' requirements.
100% Radiography
100% Dye Check
Chemical Analysis (weldmetal)
Ferrite level in weld metal including root pass
Huey Test
The Huey Test shall be qualified on the same heat of material as that used for fabrication and shall identify the method used to rapidly cool the root pass or "process" side of weld.  Refer Para 11.3.3 on production weld corrosion tests.
A change in essential variables as outlined in ASME Code Section IX will require resubmission of procedure(s).

### Welders' Qualifications
All welders employed on Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' work shall be experienced in welding high silicon austenitic steel and qualified in accordance with each procedure he is to use and the ASME Code Section IX.
The fabricator shall, upon request, supply Aker Kvaerner Chemetics with objective evidence of current welder qualifications.
All qualified welders shall be allocated an identification symbol which shall be etched on the parent material adjacent to his work. An alternative method of identification which has been used successfully in the past will also be considered.

### Drawings
All work shall be carried out in accordance with "Approved for Fabrication" shop detail drawing(s).  Deviations from the weld geometry as shown on approved drawing(s) are not permitted without the prior approval of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

## Materials
Acceptable base and filler materials shall be supplied by VEW and the necessary test and certification documents shall be available for inspection by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics prior to commencement of welding.

### Base Materials
Plate, sheet, pipe, tube and forgings shall be purchased in the solution annealed condition and satisfy ASME SA240, SA312, SA268 and SA479 respectively.
Acceptable material is designated VEW-Bohler A610 to German Werkstoff No. 1.4361 grade X2 CrNi Si 1815.
Corrosion test results (Huey Test) shall accompany all mill test certificates.

### Filler Materials
All weld filler metals shall contain high silicon (4.7 percent maximum) and conform to Bohler Fox EAS-2Si for manual metallic arc welding.  The use of EASN-2Si for tungsten inert gas welding is not acceptable unless specifically agreed by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics QA and materials engineering representative.
Electrodes shall have a ferrite content of between 7-11 percent in the undiluted fused filler metal, measured by a Fischer Penescope.
All fabricators shall ensure and demonstrate to the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' representative that the manufacturers' instructions regarding the storage, drying and use of weld consumables are being fully implemented in the shop (and on-site if applicable) in particular that all wet electrodes are discarded and not baked and reused.

### Certification
Prior to commencement of welding, certification showing the results obtained from chemical analysis and other tests carried out in accordance with the relevant material specification shall be supplied to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for all materials including welding consumables.

### Traceability
Fabricator shall maintain full traceability of materials back to original mill certification.
As a minimum, the fabricator's internal controls shall be such as to prevent the use of unspecified materials.  Substitution of materials shall not be permitted without prior agreement in writing by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
Lack of adequate identification or certification shall be cause for rejection of the specific component or item.

## Preparation Of Edges For Welding

### Edge Preparation
Material shall be cut or prepared by mechanical means such as machining or shearing with close attention to cleanliness and accuracy.
Plasma cut surfaces shall have the area affected by heat completely removed.  This requirement does not apply to underwater plasma cutting.
Other methods of thermal cutting high silicon stainless steel shall not be permitted.
Single or Double "V" grooves shall have a maximum depth of 6 mm on the process side.  Exceptions to this specific requirement shall be reviewed between VEW and Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

### Cleanliness
The fabricator shall take the controls necessary to prevent contamination of weldments at all stages of fabrication.  These controls shall include the segregation of wire brushes, grinding wheels, etc., for use on stainless steels only.
On completion of work, items shall be cleaned internally and externally.  All fabrication markings, weld spatter, etc., shall be removed prior to shipment.

## Welding Process
The following process is the preferred method for welding High Silicon Austenitic steel.  Other processes will be evaluated on submission of full details including procedure and exact location of proposed weldments.
Shielded Metal Arc Welding	(SMAW)
Welding parameters of current, voltage, electrode size, etc., as well as special requirements or precautions, such as recording of interpass temperature and method(s) of cooling welds, shall be documented and monitored during equipment fabrication.

### Shielded Metal Arc Welding
Shielded metal arc welding electrodes shall be Bohler Fox EAS-2Si or agreed equivalent.
Welding should be performed in the downhand or flat position where possible (in accordance with DIN 1912 Part 2).
Stringer beads are preferred but weaving of electrode is permitted up to twice the core diameter as long as interpass temperature of 120oC is not exceeded.
Heat input during welding shall be limited to between 4 and 6 K.J.

## Thermal Requirements

### Preheat and Interpass Temperature
Preheat is not required.
Interpass temp. shall be monitored and recorded during equipment and piping fabrication.

### Solution Heat Treatment and Water Quench
When required by the Purchase Order or approved fabrication drawing, furnace heat treatment shall be performed between 1100oC - 1150oC followed by rapid water quenching.
The operation shall be covered by a written procedure outlining method(s) of controlling distortion, particularly during the cooling cycle.

### Forming
Plate, sheet, pipe and tubes shall be cold formed at all times.  Hot forming for alignment when welding or assembling components or adjusting for distortion is not permitted and may result in rejection of the part or component.
After forming and final heat treatment on dished heads, the severely deformed knuckle areas shall be dye penetrant inspected both internally and externally around the entire circumference.
Hot forming of dished heads shall not be performed unless specifically agreed by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
All dished heads shall be solution treated and water quenched after forming.

## Workmanship

### Tack Welds
When aligning parts for welding, the use of clamping devices is preferred to tacking.
Tack welds should therefore be avoided but if necessary shall be small and performed by qualified welders.  Tack welds employed for assembly purposes shall not form part of the completed weld. Instead they must be ground out as the main weld progresses.
Given accessibility, tacking from the outside of the groove is advantageous, because the tacks can be completely removed by grinding before welding up the outside surface.
Cracked and unsound tack welds shall be cut out prior to installing the root pass.

### Striking of Welding Arc
Every effort shall be made to confine arc strikes to surfaces on which weld metal is to be deposited.  If such a strike should occur outside this area, the arc strike shall be ground out to a maximum depth of 0.012" (0.3mm), and the area dye penetrant examined.
Burn marks from grounding clamps and weld spatter shall be treated as arc strikes.
Weld repairs shall not be carried out on the process side of the weld unless prior approval has been obtained from Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

### Cleaning and Restarting
Each run of weld metal shall be carefully examined to ensure that it has been thoroughly cleaned and is defect free.  Craters left from the previous pass shall be ground out before commencing the next pass.  On restarting, care shall be taken to ensure proper fusion with the weld metal previously deposited.

### Peening and Hammering
Peening of weld runs and hammering of finished welds is not permitted.

### Distortion
Distortion from welding or solution annealing and water quenching shall be minimized at all times.
Tolerances after welding or heat treatment shall comply with the requirements of Appendix B.

### Form and Cooling of Welds
Each weld shall be uniform in width and size throughout its full length.  Each layer of welding shall be smooth and free of slag, inclusions, cracks, porosity, lack of fusion, and undercut except to the extent permitted in the referenced codes.
Buttwelds shall be slightly convex, of uniform height, and have full penetration.
End grains on tubes, nozzle plate, etc. shall be fused for nitric acid service.
Heat generated by welding shall be removed quickly by appropriate cooling measures.  Welding under intensive water cooling has proven an effective means of heat elimination and is recommended.
Discolouration produced by welding shall be removed by grinding after each pass.
In case of root pass welding, other suitable measures may be taken to achieve the fastest cooling. (e.g. compressed air for weld joints without gaps or intensive cool shielding gas for pipe seams). The requirement for effective water cooling applies especially to the layers on the product side.  In order to achieve adequate cooling with running water, the weld material should not be too thick (max. 6 mm).  Filling of the groove shall be carried out while water cooling from the opposite side.
For the same reason the double sided groove with wall thicknesses greater than 6 mm, shall be welded first on the product side.  The thickness of weld metal on the product side shall not exceed 6 mm in order to achieve satisfactory cooling rates.
Pipe welds should, if possible, be welded by multi pass welding after depositing the root pass using cooling with running water.
Crevices shall not be permitted on the process side of the weld.

### Dressing of Welds & Repair Areas
Grinding shall be carried out in such a manner as not to reduce the adjacent base metal thickness or produce heat tinting. Grinding with water cooling may be necessary to avoid grinding cracks.
Wire brushes shall be made of austenitic stainless steel and must not have been used previously to clean surfaces other than stainless steels.

## Quality Control

### General
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Quality Assurance Inspectors or authorized representatives shall have free access to the Vendor's work and to his Subvendor's, if work is sublet by approval, at all reasonable times.
He shall be at liberty to inspect the work during fabrication at any stage in manufacture and to reject any material or workmanship which does not conform to this specification and/or the applicable code.
Inspection by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or delegated representative shall not relieve the fabricator or erector of responsibility in accordance with the requirements of this Standard, engineering design and the applicable code.

### Chemical Analysis
Supplier must make available to purchaser's inspector copies of chemical certification for electrodes used.  If electrodes cannot be identified to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' satisfaction, samples shall be removed for chemical analysis and other related identity tests.  This shall be to supplier's account.
Where the fabricator cannot identify the weld material, chemical analysis and other related tests shall be performed on production welds to ascertain extent of the problem.
Weld(s) to be checked shall be selected by agreement between the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' representative and the fabricator.

### Corrosion Testing (Huey Test)
Corrosion testing for Intergranular attack shall be carried out in accordance with ASTM A262 Practice "C".  (5 x 48 hrs in boiling 65% nitric acid).
This test shall have been performed on all heats of parent material and shall be provided with mill test certification.
Production weld corrosion tests shall be performed to ensure that production welds by different welders or after heat treatment are acceptable for nitric acid service.
Each vessel exchanger shall be represented by a production test sample removed from a longitudinal seam.  Each welder used shall also be covered by the test samples.  In the case of heat treated and water quenched equipment, the production test samples shall be removed after the water quench operation.
No individual weight loss shall exceed 1 g/m2 h (1.1 mm/yr)
Weight loss results in excess of 1 g/m2.h shall require the vessel or component to be either:
Solution annealed and water quenched and the corrosion tests repeated or
Weld(s) and heat affected zone(s) completely cut-out, seam(s) rewelded and the corrosion tests repeated.
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics shall be advised of the extent of the problem and proposed corrective action.
Where possible, corrosion tests shall be performed on weld run-off tabs taken from longitudinal seams.
Corrosion test coupons shall be carefully identified with welders' symbol.  Coupons shall be checked prior to leaving fabrication shop.
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics reserves the right to independently test the corrosion resistance of any weld joint.

### Dye Pentrant Inspection (PT)
Items fabricated in accordance with this specification shall be 100% dye penetrant tested on all welds including root passes. This requirement shall also cover dissimilar welds.
The PT exam shall be carried out in accordance with a written procedure and in general, the limit of acceptability shall satisfy the ASME Code Section VIII, Division I, Appendix 8, except that linear indications shall be considered unacceptable.
Repairs shall not be undertaken without prior consent of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.  Consent to carry out repairs shall be based on prior acceptance of VEW"s repair procedures by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
Weld repair surfaces shall be l00% PT examined per the above requirements and the areas identified on a sketch or drawing.
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics reserves the right to accept or reject any doubtful or questionable indications.

### Radiographic Inspection (RT)
Items fabricated in accordance with this specification shall have all butt welds spot radiographed in accordance with Para. UW-52 of Section VIII, Division I of the ASME Code.
Junctions between circumferential and longitudinal seams shall be considered as preferred locations for spot radiography.
Piping operating at wall temperatures greater than 70oC shall be 100 percent radiographed (heat-treated and water quenched piping).
Piping operating at wall temperatures between 40oC and 70oC shall be 25 percent radiographed.
Piping operating at wall temperatures less than 40oC shall be 10 percent radiographed.
Repairs shall not be undertaken without prior consent of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.  Consent to carry out repairs shall be based on prior acceptance of VEW's repair procedures by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
Weld repairs and the nature of the defects found shall be identified on a sketch or drawing.
Ferrite Content
The ferrite content of weld deposits shall be not less than 4-5 percent.
The ferrite content of each batch of electrodes shall be checked prior to use.

### Quality Records
The following documentation (3 sets) shall be provided to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics prior to release of the fabricated items:

Corrosion test records
Affidavit of manufacture
Nameplate rubbing or facsimile
Copies of approved welding procedures
List of approved welders and their symbols
A copy of test charts
N.D.T. reports showing repair locations
Post weld heat treatment charts
"As Built" drawing marked up to show actual recorded dimensions

## Nameplates
In addition to any nameplates required by codes (ASME/TEMA), fabricator shall, where shown on the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics drawing, affix a Aker Kvaerner Chemetics nameplate to equipment.
Details of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' nameplates are shown in Appendix A to this Standard.
All nameplates shall be securely fastened with stainless steel bolts or stainless steel rivets to a bracket welded to the vessel shell as shown on the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics drawing.
The Aker Kvaerner Chemetics nameplate will be supplied to the fabricator by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
