## Membrane
The acid resistant membrane employed on all strong acid equipment will be a nominal 3mm layer of Pecora Corporation, Pecora A-103 chemical resistant mastic, overlaid with 0.125mm (2 layers) or 0.25mm (1 layer) sodium etched virgin PTFE sheet.
The Pecora/PTFE membrane applied  two layers of 0.125mm thick PTFE sheet overlapped 50% to give an effective 0.25mm  thickness.
Overlap joints in the lower layer of 0.125mm PTFE will be a minimum of 150mm wide and must be firmly stuck down with mastic.
Pecora mastic will be applied to the entire sandblasted surface of the dished bottoms, but it is acceptable to stick down only the edges of the upper PTFE sheet. .  The number of PTFE sheet seams will be minimized and  be staggered.

Drying tower gas inlet membranes will be 6mm thick chlorobutyl rubber, applied in one or two layers. The membrane will cover the nozzle flange face and extend to the edge of the nozzle to shell junction. The rubber membrane will be applied before the Pecora mastic/PTFE vessel membrane and the Pecora/PTFE will cover the rubber in the mouth of the inlet nozzle for a minimum length of 450mm.

## Brick Lining
All brick bonding surfaces will be thoroughly and evenly buttered. The resulting brick joints, bedding layers and ceramic rope packed joints will be completely full and solid. When placing the bricks into position a sliding movement will be used to remove voids. When in place the bricks will be pounded into final position using a rubber mallet in order to obtain thin, uniform joints. All courses must be straight, level and tightly wedged into place. Joints between layers and courses will be staggered for maximum strength. A course of brick work once started must be finished before the mortar has part set. No more than one brick course will be worked on at any one time.
The bricks must be handled carefully to avoid chipping and cracks. Damaged/chipped bricks will only be used for cuts.

## Inspection
inspectors will have free access to the installation job site at all times during the lining . The inspectors will be  to inspect the work in progress at any stage and to reject any material or workmanship which does not conform to this or normal industry practice.
During the installation work in progress, at the end of each shift (approx. every 8 hours) and at the completion of the lining work in each vessel, the finished brick work will be thoroughly visually inspected. The requirements of this Standard must be complied with. Mortar joints will be 100% full (including the bedding layer/back side of the brick) or touch up is required, any tears in the PTFE sheet must be repaired with overlap patches, and all partially disbonded joints in the PTFE/Pecora membrane are to be firmly re-stuck with Pecora mastic and flattened. The completed lining and membrane sections will be uniform and neat in appearance, with consistent joint widths, workmanship and level brick courses.
