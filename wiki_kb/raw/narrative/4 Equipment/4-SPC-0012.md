## Brick Lining

### Acid Resistant Brick
Acid resistant bricks will conform to ASTM C-279 type III and be free from blow holes, cracks, chips, voids and other defects. Both fireclay and red shale brick are acceptable, but if red shale acid bricks are employed in the strong acid vessel linings, absorber tower gas inlet nozzle brickwork and associated nozzle to vessel Bullseye block will be constructed of fireclay acid brick. Details of the vendor's selected acid brick materials will be included in his proposal.

### Packing Supports
Domed packing supports are required in the drying and absorption towers. Steuler Industriewerke Gmbh is the preferred packing support supplier, but other suppliers and types of packing supports will be considered if the lining supplier can prove considerable acid plant experience and provide demonstrative technical literature/data (manufacturer's name, percentage free area, geometry and ceramic details are minimum information for evaluation). If a stepped dome is not offered, the vendor will comment in his proposal on how the stability of the packed bed will be maintained.
