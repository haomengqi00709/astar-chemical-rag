# 5-SPC-0122 — Specification


[TABLE: 5-SPC-0122_table_1]


## Purpose
To define the quality requirements for the fabrication of carbon steel piping.

## Scope
This Standard applies when referenced on purchase orders, drawings or other documents.
This Standard is applicable to both shop and field fabricated piping.
This Standard is applicable to carbon steel steam, process and utilities piping, and supplements ASME B31.3 requirements where these are applicable.

## Definitions
Codes, etc., will be taken to mean latest edition ruling at date of contract.
Agreement in Writing - Where prior approval in writing is required by this Standard, this will be taken to mean submission in writing by letter and not merely by inclusion in approval drawings.
Inspector - Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Quality Assurance Inspector or other Authorized Representative.

## Manufacture
Sub-Contracting
No Subcontracting of welding is permitted without Aker Kvaerner Chemetics written approval.
Materials
No deviation from the specified or approved type/grade/class of material will be permitted without the written approval of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
Fabricator will use an effective system to identify different materials and prevent substitutions.  All materials must show some form of visual identification such as stenciling or stamping.
Seamless grade of piping is preferred.  Steam piping will be ASTM A106 or A53 unless otherwise requested.
Filler materials will conform to the appropriate AWS/ASME SFA-5 series specification, and will be stored in accordance with manufacturer's recommendations.
When material certification is specified, or when piping is in accordance with ASME B31.3, all materials including weld consumables will be identified and traceable to original mill certification.
Fabrication will be undertaken by an experienced pipe fabricating Contractor, using premises devoted to this type of work and which have adequate supervision and inspection.
Welding
All welding will be in accordance with ASME qualified procedures, which will be made available for review by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics prior to production use.
Weld procedures will state permissible variations in weld preparation dimensions, alignment, etc.
For low temperature service, (design temperature -20° F or less) weld procedure qualification will include impact testing of weld metal coupons to ASME B31.3.
Where specified by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, the weld procedure will be qualified on the same grade of material as the piping.  This situation will apply to low temperature service piping e.g. liquid chlorine service.
Adequate protection from the weather will be provided during field welding.
All low hydrogen electrodes will be kept dry at all times, particularly during field work and stored in accordance with manufacturers recommendations.
Wet shielded metal arc electrodes will not be oven baked or dried for reuse, instead they will be discarded and new electrodes obtained.
All shielded metal arc welding will use E 7018 electrodes except root pass welding which may use other classifications.
Flux cored arc welding and M.I.G. welding are only permissible where evidence of extensive, satisfactory use is available and prior approval by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics has been obtained.
Weld deposit chemical analysis will be provided for flux core & submerged arc welding processes.
Normally M.I.G. welding is acceptable for root pass only.
Preheat and interpass temperatures will be controlled within the range specified on the weld procedure(s) specification.
All Butt/Groove welds in pipe including branch connections will be full penetration.
Welder Qualification
Only experienced welders who are qualified by certification or recent test will be used.  Repeated testing of a welder who fails a test is not permissible.
All welds will be traceable to the welder responsible.
Fabricator will maintain a list of welders and their qualifications.

## Inspection/Quality Control
Fabricator will systematically inspect work in progress to check fit-up, workmanship, and ensure that correct procedures are being followed.
Where radiography is specified or required by code, the minimum standard will be spot radiography (5 percent) as defined in para. 341.1.1(b) of ASME B31.3.  Butt welds in low temperature service will be 20% radiographed.
Spot radiography, where required, will cover the welding of each welder and operator and will be performed on the first-off butt welds completed by each welder or operator as early as possible during production.
The requirement for spot radiography is mandatory for welds performed by M.I.G. or flux cored arc welding.
Dimensional tolerances for individual spool pieces will be such as to maintain the accuracy of the overall line as specified on the isometric drawing (normally ± 1/4").
Fabricator will inspect finished spools prior to shipping, checking materials, workmanship, finish, flange protection and loading.  Carbon steel piping for cryogenic service will be clearly marked or painted for easy on site identification.

## Post Weld Heat Treatment (P.W.H.T.)
Where P.W.H.T. is required, it will be carried out in a furnace, unless otherwise approved by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.  A procedure will be provided showing location of thermocouples and supports.
Furnace charts are required to be submitted for records purposes.

## Pressure Testing
Unless specifically excluded, pressure testing of all pipe spools except those which require field welding will be carried out by the fabricator in accordance with ASME B31.3, para. 345.4.

## Painting/Marking/Shipping
Each spool piece will have its identity number painted on it in 1" or larger numbers where possible.  No paper or cardboard tags or tags attached by cord or string will be used.
All necessary precautions such as plugging openings, protecting flange faces, use of shipping supports, etc., will be taken by fabricator prior to shipping.
Priming etc., where required, will be carried out in accordance with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' painting Standard only after all welding and testing has been completed.
