# 5-SPC-0121 — Specification


[TABLE: 5-SPC-0121_table_1]


## Purpose
The purpose of this Standard is to define the quality requirements for the shop and field fabrication of austenitic stainless steel piping (300 series) for low pressure corrosive service (such as that typically found in pulp & paper mills and chemical plants).  Appendix "A" extends the coverage of this specification to include Alloy 20 (UNS N08020) stainless steel piping fabrication.

## Scope
This Standard applies when referenced on purchase orders, drawings or other documents.
This Standard is applicable to both shop and field fabricated piping & fittings.
Piping with a design pressure of 15 psig or greater will satisfy the requirements of ASME B31.3 for Chemical Plant & Petroleum Refinery Piping in addition to this Standard.
For piping with a design pressure of up to 15 psig ASME B31.3 paragraph 300.1 will be used to determine the applicability of that Standard.

## Definitions
Codes, etc., will be taken to mean latest edition ruling at date of contract.
Agreement in Writing - Where prior approval in writing is required by this Standard, this will be taken to mean submission in writing by letter and not merely by inclusion in approval drawings.
Inspector - Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Quality Assurance Inspector or other Authorized Representative.

## General Requirements
Sub-Contracting
No Subcontracting of welding is permitted without Aker Kvaerner Chemetics written approval.
Supervision
The fabricator will ensure that there is regular and systematic supervision of all work both in-shop and on-site by competent staff.
Procedure Submittal
All welding procedures will be submitted by the fabricator for review and agreement by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics prior to commencement of any work.  The ASME IX PQR Form 483 will form part of this package.
A change in essential variables as outlined in ASME Code. Section IX will require re-submission of procedure(s).
Welders Qualifications
All welders to be employed on Aker Kvaerner Chemetics work will be suitably qualified in accordance with relevant codes and standards for every procedure he is to use.
The fabricator will, upon request, supply Aker Kvaerner Chemetics with objective evidence of current welder qualifications.
All qualified welders will be allocated an identification symbol which will be stamped on parent material adjacent to his work.
Subject to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics approval, fully marked-up weld maps may be substituted for stamping.
Cleanliness
The surface and edges of any austenitic steel which is to be heated for any purpose (e.g. fusion cutting, forming or welding) will be cleaned free from all traces of foreign matter (e.g. marking paint, oil, etc.) by grinding, wire brushing with a stainless steel brush and/or non-chlorinated solvent cleaning immediately prior to the application of heat.
The fabricator will take the controls necessary to prevent contamination of weldments at all stages of fabrication.  These controls will include the segregation of wire brushes, grinding wheels, etc., for use on stainless steels only.
On completion of work, all weld splatter heat tinting, tack welds etc; will be removed.
Drawings
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics will provide the Fabricator with an isometric drawing for each line.
Each drawing will show Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Piping Specification, fluid service, design pressure/temperature and bill of material where necessary quality control requirements will also be identified.
The Fabricator will normally provide the required shop spool drawings, which will include spool identification numbers and location of field welds.  Alternatively, spool drawings may be provided by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics as shown on the purchase order.
Where field welds are shown on Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' drawings or determined by the Fabricator to suit shipping arrangements, at least one field weld on any pipe run will have a 3 inch trim allowance added.

## Materials
Base Materials
Only low grade of will be used Fittings which do not require welding may be standard grade.
No deviation from the specified or approved type/grade/class of material will be permitted without the written approval of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
Effective segregation of stainless steels from and contamination by carbon steel will be maintained in order to optimize corrosion resistance of the finished welds and adjacent areas.
e.g.	-	no grinding or arc air gouging of carbon steel nearby
-	wire brushes and chipping hammers to be stainless steel
-	grinding wheels only used for stainless steel
All base metal will be in the solution annealed condition.
Fabrication should be undertaken by an experienced pipe fabricating contractor, using premises devoted to this type of work and which have adequate supervision and inspection.

Certification
Mill Test Certification showing the results obtained from analysis and other tests carried out in accordance with the relevant material specification (e.g. ASME, ASTM etc.) will be supplied for all materials including welding consumables.
When required by purchase order, Standard or certified drawings, a chemical analysis will be performed on production welds made by submerged arc, flux cored arc welding or weld overlaid surfaces.
The weld(s) to be checked will be selected at random by the inspector.
Where results of chemical analysis show that a specific weldment falls outside of the approved specification or code requirements, the supplier will promptly advise Aker Kvaerner Chemetics in writing of his proposed course of action.  Such action will include details of any additional testing necessary to fully define the extent of the non-conformance.  All repairs and retests will be to the supplier's account.
Traceability
When required by purchase order, fabricator will maintain full traceability of materials back to original mill certification.
As a minimum the fabricator's internal controls will be such as to prevent the use of unspecified materials.
All consumables will be readily identifiable.  Lack of adequate identification will be cause for rejection of the weld(s).

## Welding
All welding will be in accordance with ASME Code qualified procedures, which will be made available for review by The Inspector prior to production use.  In general, these procedures will satisfy the requirements of ASME B31.3 for Chemical Plant and Petroleum Refinery Piping.
Weld procedure(s) will state permissible variations in weld preparation dimensions, alignment, etc.
All welding will be carried out by experienced welders in possession of current appropriate certification or who have been qualified directly on the procedure to be used.  Repeated testing of a welder who fails a test is not permissible.
All welds will be traceable to the welder responsible.
All butt and groove welds will be full penetration.  For one sided welds, the root pass will be TIG with gas shielding on the inside of the piping.
Solar flux will not be used
When mitred bends are shown on Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' drawings each section will not exceed 30o unless specifically approved in writing.
Weld consumables will be stored in accordance with manufacturer's recommendations.  This will apply in particular to field welding.
The use of backing rings is not permitted.  Consumable inserts may only be used with prior written approval of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
Suitable enclosures will be provided to keep welds and welders free from draughts, rain or other inclement weather.  Welding will not be performed on wet surfaces.

## Inspection/Quality Control
The Inspector will have free access to the Vendor's work and to his Subvendor's, if work is sublet by approval, at all reasonable times.  Access will also be given to Authorized Representatives of the End-User.
The responsibility for, and cost of carrying out all required inspection is borne by the shop fabricator or field contractor as appropriate.
Fittings will not be formed from pipe by the piping fabricator unless specifically approved by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
Fabricator and where appropriate site representative will inspect work  in progress to ensure that fit-up is satisfactory and that Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' approved weld procedures are being followed at all times.
All welds will be visually inspected and repaired where necessary.
Where pipe fabrication is not subject to radiography, dye penetrant examination will be carried out on 10% of finished butt welds, with acceptance standards in accordance with ASME B31.3 Table 341.3.2A and paragraph 341.3.2.
This examination will be performed on the first off welds completed by each welder in order to establish welder performance and weld quality.
Where access permits, dye penetrant inspection will be performed on the process side of selected welds as well as the external side.  Where access to the process side of the weld is not available the root pass and cover pass on the external side will be inspected.
Lines in slurry service will be visually inspected and spot radiographed to ensure that internal weld projections such as "icicles" etc. are identified and completely removed.
Butt and groove welds (including branch connections) using solar flux, (refer to para 7.6) will be subject to random radiography (10 percent) as defined in ASME B31.3, paragraph 341.4(b).
Random radiography, where specified, will cover the welding of each welder and operator, and will be performed on the first-off butt welds (full circumference) completed by each welder or operator as early as possible during production.
Dye penetrant and radiographic examinations will be subject to supervision by the inspector.
Dimensional tolerances for individual spool pieces will be such as to maintain the accuracy of the overall line as specified on the isometric drawing (normally ± 1/4").
Fabricator will inspect finished spools prior to shipping, checking materials, workmanship, finish, flange protection, and loading.
Fabricator will ensure that where specified, MSS type lap joint stub ends have been installed and not rolled angle stub ends.
Where purchase order specifies "Inspection Required Prior To Shipping" adequate notice must be given to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics to allow scheduling of inspection without delaying shipping.  A formal release must be obtained prior to shipping.

## Pressure Testing
Where pressure testing is required, this will be conducted in the field, in accordance with ASME B31.3, para. 345.4 unless otherwise specified.  Potable water will be used and the piping drained and dried upon completion of the test.  The test pressure for each line will normally be specified by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
Potable water will be used for hydrostatic testing (ess than 50 ppm chlorides).  All lines will be drained and blown dry.
For piping  where supports are not capable of flooded weights use, a pneumatic soap test in accordance with ASME B31.3 para 345.5 will be performed. All leaks will be rectified.

## Passivation
When specified
Painting/Marking/Shipping
Each spool piece will have its identity number painted on it in 1" or larger numbers where possible.  No paper or cardboard tags or tags attached by cord or string will be used.
Carbon steel back-up flanges, etc., will be given a coat of zinc based epoxy shop primer unless otherwise specified (applied after any pickling).
All necessary precautions such as plugging openings, protecting flange faces, use of shipping supports, etc., will be taken by fabricator prior to shipping.
