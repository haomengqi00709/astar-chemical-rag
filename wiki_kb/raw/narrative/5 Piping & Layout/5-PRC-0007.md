# 5-PRC-0007 — Procedure


[TABLE: 5-PRC-0007_table_1]


## Introduction
The mechanical checkout procedure for piping outlines the procedure to be used to verify that the piping has been installed in accordance with the drawings and specifications and is ready for water batching and commissioning.  The mechanical checkout documents will form part of the mechanical completion package.  This procedure assumes that site work is carried out as follows:
During construction the sub-contractor punch lists the isometrics for completeness before approaching Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site supervisor for site supervisor review.
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site supervisor visually reviews installed piping against the isometric, and contractor punch list. When the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site supervisor is satisfied the contractor has taken the work as far as can be done at that time, and that the punch list is correct, he notifies the Project Manager that the piping is ready for a final review by a designer from the office.
The designer conducts a visual inspection of the piping against the latest isometrics and P&ID to confirm the installation is in accordance with the construction documents.
The designer identifies any deficiencies on a set of isometrics and hands these deficiencies to the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site supervisor.
The Site Supervisor works with a deficiency crew supplied by the sub-contractor to correct all deficiencies identified by the designer as well as any outstanding punch list items from the initial review by the sub-contractor.
Outstanding work such as painting, insulating and installing flange guards is undertaken by the deficiency crew after cleaning, flushing and hydrotesting has been completed.
The primary purpose of check-out is to ensure that the plant has been constructed in accordance with the design documents (drawings and specifications).  Other modifications and changes identified during check-out will be treated as design deficiencies.  A design deficiency is not a punch list item and will be documented and forwarded to the Project Manager for resolution.

## Piping
Documents 	-	P&ID, Isometrics, Line List, Pipe Material Specification, Pipe Support Specification
Materials	-	Visually confirm that line materials match the specification
Routing	-	Visually confirm pipe routing matches isometric
Branches	-	Visually confirm piping branches and take-offs are in correct location as identified on the isometric and P&ID
-	Confirm that the branch connection material /type matches the isometric
Slope	-	Visually confirm pipe slope is in correct direction
-	Visually confirm presence of shims or supports on pipe bridge
-	Visually confirm that piping joints (couplings etc) are not resting on supports, causing piping to bend upwards
Critical Dimensions	-	Measure and confirm critical dimensions identified on P&ID (seal legs, pots and internals)
-	Visually confirm straight runs in pipe length are correct for orifice plate and flowmeter operation
Supports	-	Confirm supports exist as identified on the isometric (correct type/correct location)
-	Confirm support is adequate – move/ shake the pipe
-	Check the support connection to the pipe for correct tightness of bolts, correct installation of wear pads.
-	Visually confirm that anchor supports and guides appear to be adequately sized and correctly located
-	Visually confirm that hot lines have room to expand and u-clamps are not tight
-	Confirm that travel stops are removed from spring hangers.
Insulation	-	Visually confirm insulation material, thickness and cladding (if installed), punch list if not completed.
Paint	-	Visually confirm painting (where required on the line list), punch list if not completed.
Flange Guards	-	confirm flange guards are installed (where required on the line list), punch list if not completed.

## In-Line Items
Flanges
Documents:	-	P&ID, Pipe Specification, Isometric, Gasket Colour Code Chart
Gaskets 	-	with flashlight visually confirm installed gasket material matches isometric
-	visually confirm that gasket is centred
-	visually confirm flange has not been overtightened (gasket forced out)
Flanges	-	visually confirm that the flange type and material are to specification
-	visually confirm flange alignment is satisfactory
-	check flange tightness by hand turning nuts, shaking pipe
-	confirm flange clearance is adequate for operation/maintenance
Nuts/Bolts	-	visually confirm that the bolting material are to specification
-	confirm bolt length is adequate
-	confirm washers are present (when required)
Manual Valves
Documents 	-	P&ID, Valve Specification, Isometric
Flanges	-	complete check as described above for flanges
Installation	-	confirm valve type and tag matches P&ID and isometric
-	confirm valve installation is accessible for operation and maintenance
-	if applicable confirm valve handle can rotate 900 without interfering with anything
-	confirm valve handle orientation (open and close valve) matches isometric
-	confirm valve has been installed in the correct flow direction, (where applicable)
-	confirm valve body has been correctly and adequately supported(where applicable)
-	for butterfly valves confirm that there is no gasket interference with valve when fully open
-	rotate valve handle on all valves from fully closed to fully open to confirm operation
-	confirm that spacers have been installed (where applicable)
Bellows
Documents 	-	 P&ID, Specialty Item Specification, Isometric
Flanges	-	complete check as described above for flanges
Installation	-	confirm bellows type and tag matches P&ID and isometric
-	measure installed length and confirm
-	check visually for misalignment. Confirm that the misalignment does not exceed vendor limits
-	measure limit bolt settings on tie rods and confirm that it matches the specification
-	confirm there is no interference for expansion or contraction
-	confirm sleeves are in bellows (if applicable)
-	confirm bellows do not have obvious damage (punctures, cuts)
-	confirm that the pipe is adequately supported adjacent to bellows
Sight Glasses
Documents 	-	 P&ID, Specialty Item Specification, Isometric
Flanges	-	complete check as described above for flanges
Installation	-	confirm sight glass type and tag matches P&ID and isometric
-	confirm all components have been installed correctly (i.e. shroud)
-	For critical services in metal lines, disassemble piping around glass sight glasses to check for piping misalignment.  Confirm that the piping arrangement does not put stress on sightglass body
-	Confirm which site glasses have recommended torque and check that this torque is correct
Strainers, Check-Valves, Steam Traps
Documents 	-	P&ID, Specialty Item Specification, Isometric
Flanges	-	complete check as described above for flanges
Installation	-	confirm type and tag matches P&ID and isometric
-	confirm installation is accessible for operation and maintenance
-	confirm installed with the correct flow direction
Pressure Relief Valves
Documents 	-	P&ID, Valve Specification, Isometric
Flanges	-	complete check as described above for flanges
Installation	-	confirm valve type and tag matches P&ID and isometric
-	confirm vendor valve tag has correct model number, set pressure etc.
-	confirm valve installation is accessible for operation and maintenance
-	confirm valve has been installed in the correct flow direction
-	confirm valve body and discharge piping has been adequately supported (where applicable)
-	for valves with testing lever, confirm that lever operates and is accessible
Rupture Discs
Documents 	-	P&ID, Specialty Item Specification, Isometric
Flanges	-	complete check as described above for flanges
Installation	-	confirm rupture disc type and tag matches P&ID and isometric
-	confirm vendor tag has correct model number, burst pressure, material etc.
-	confirm installation is accessible for maintenance
-	confirm rupture disc has been installed in the correct flow direction
-	confirm materials of rupture disc holders (where applicable)
Restriction Orifices
Documents 	-	P&ID, Restriction Orifice List, Isometric
Flanges	-	complete check as described above for flanges
Installation	-	confirm restriction orifice tag number matches P&ID and isometric
-	confirm material and type and bore matches restriction orifice list (if possible).
-	confirm restriction orifice has been installed in the correct flow direction
-	confirm adequate upstream and downstream straight runs (where applicable)
Pipe Grounding Ring
Documents 	-	P&ID, Specialty Item Drawing, Isometric
Flanges	-	complete check as described above for flanges
Installation	-	confirm grounding ring is installed in the correct location and orientation
-	confirm that the grounding wires are firmly bolted to the grounding ring
-	confirm that electrolytic cellroom piping and structure is not  grounded or touching any other structure
-	Check with electrical personnel for checkout and electrical continuity
Piping Specialty Items
Documents 	-	P&ID, Specialty Item Drawing, Isometric
Flanges	-	complete check as described above for flanges (where applicable)
Installation	-	confirm materials and installation are in accordance with the specialty item drawing.
