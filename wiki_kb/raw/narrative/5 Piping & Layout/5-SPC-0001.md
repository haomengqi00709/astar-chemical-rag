# 5-SPC-0001 — Specification


[TABLE: 5-SPC-0001_table_1]


## General
Nominal metric equivalent pipe diameters for ASME standard pipe sizes will be as shown in Section 3.
Material specifications preceded by “(V)” (e.g. (V) TT4) indicate a line in vacuum service.  Required stiffening rings are specified with the respective branch connection table in Section 5.
Materials will be as defined in the listed specifications in Section 4.
Piping will conform to the requirements of ASME B31.3, latest edition.
Pipelines will be given unique numbers based on the following:

[TABLE: 5-SPC-0001_table_2]

Example:  100-CDG-(V)TT4A-800101

## Definitions
In-Line Items:	An item mounted in the run of a pipeline.  Examples include strainers, expansion joints, and in-line instruments such as magmeters and control valves.
HP:	High Point Vent.
LP:	Low Point Drain.
Instrument Connections:	Connection provided as a branch from a pipeline for mounting an instrument.  For example, instrument connections are provided for pressure gauges and thermowells.
