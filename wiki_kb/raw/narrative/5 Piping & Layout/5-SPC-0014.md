# 5-SPC-0014 — Specification


[TABLE: 5-SPC-0014_table_1]


## Purpose
This specification defines Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ requirements for the design, fabrication, assembly and testing of metal butterfly dampers, including automation as defined in Scope of Work.

## Scope of Work

### Goods and Services by Vendor
The complete control damper will consist of but not be limited to:
Fabricated damper body
Reinforced blade
Drive & tail shafts
Stuffing boxes
Supply of actuation equipment
Declutchable gear box and hand wheel
Mounting and assembly of actuation equipment onto damper body
Instrument calibration
Functional test

### Testing by Vendor
The Vendor will fully shop assemble and test the Equipment.  Tests will include but not be limited to:
Non destructive testing as outlined in the Quality Data Sheet
Actuator performance tests

### Goods and Services by Others
Installation of damper bodies into ductwork
Supply and installation of insulation and cladding

## Process and Automation Requirements
Process requirements are specified in 5-LST-[00XX]: Metal Dampers.  Automation requirements are specified in the following documents:
5-LST-[00XX]: Metal Dampers
8-DST-[5XXX]: Damper Automation

## Equipment Requirements

### General
The Equipment will be suitable for installation and continuous operation in the outdoor environment specified in 1-DST-0001: Site Design Conditions.
Each damper will be supplied with 316 stainless steel shafting and self-aligning outboard anti-friction bearings.  The support bearings will be complete with 316 stainless steel tubing & fittings for grease insertion.  Tubing will be routed to an accesible location on the damper body, external to any insulation, or as close to external as practical.  The bearings will be greased with Rhotium HT-1000 synthetic grease.
Each damper will be supplied with local indication of blade position at the “dead” end of the shaft.  The indicator will be 300mm (12”) long and painted yellow.

### Welding
Welding will be per the Vendor’s welding procedures, in accordance with 4-SPC-0060: Equipment Welding Carbon Steel or 4-SPC-0061: Equipment Welding Austenitic Stainless Steel as applicable.  All welds on the damper body and packing gland will be continuous and gas tight.  Additionally, damper body longitudinal seams will be full penetration.

### Damper Body
5-LST-[00XX]: Metal Dampers indicates the diameter and thickness of the duct into which the damper body will be welded.  This thickness is not to be taken as the required damper body thickness.  The damper body thickness will be determined by the Vendor giving consideration to all the specified process and climatic conditions plus the effect of operating forces inherent to the Equipment.  The body thickness will not be less than the duct thickness.  The damper body outside diameter will match the outside diameter for the duct.  The inside wall of the damper body will be tapered 3:1 to match the duct thickness.
The damper body will be designed with sufficient thickness to prevent distortion under all operating conditions.
Damper body length will not exceed 500mm (20”).  Longer body lengths require approval by Aker Kvaerner Chements.
Lifting lugs will be provided for the installation and removal of the damper body.

### Stuffing Box
Stuffing boxes will be supllied with the following details:
Two sets of packing and two lantern rings in series.  Each set of packing will have a minimum of two rings of packing material.  Packing material will be Garlock 1300 or approved equal.
Lantern rings will be 316 stainless steel.  Spring rings are not acceptable.
The stuffing box follower will be a spring/bolt tightening design to prevent over tightening of the packing.
Stuffing boxes will be provided with two connections.  One connection will be used for insertion of grease.  The other will be available for air purging of the stuffing box, but will not be used under normal operating conditions.  Both connections will be routed to accesible locations on the damper body, external to any insulation, or as close to external as practical.  The stuffing box will be greased with Rhotium HT-1000 synthetic grease.

## Painting
External surfaces of carbon steel damper bodies will be shop primed.
Actuation equipment will be painted per the Vendor’s standard paint system for severe service.
Loose structural components and hardware.

## Inspection and Testing
Butt welds in the damper body will be subject to spot radiographic examination in accordance with ASME Code Section VIII Division 1, paragraph UW-52.
All welds exposed to the process will be subject to liquid penetrant examination.
The Vendor will perform functional checks to prove correct operation of the damper.  These checks will demonstrate:
That the damper blade moves accurately and smoothly over its full range.
The speed and direction of movement is correct for the application.
The damper blade moves to the correct position in the event of supply failure.

## Performance Guarantee
The Vendor will guarantee the leakage rate based on the maximum flow rates and maximum inlet pressures specified in 5-LST-[00XX]: Metal Dampers.

## Preparation for Shipment
Packaging and shipping will be in accordance with 0-SPC-5004: Packaging Land Transport or 0-SPC-5005: Packaging Marine Transport, and the following:
After functional checks, the actuation equipment will be demounted and clearly tagged with identification corresponding to the appropriate valve body.
Damper bodies and actuation equipment will be shipped separately.
All grease and air purge connections will be plugged.
Commentary
This specification assumes damper bodies and actuation equipment are sourced together.  It must be modified if actuation equipment is sourced separately.
