Notes:

Gasket colour codes (spray paint outer edge of gasket in 3 places):
C - EPDM, Peroxide Cured	yellow
D - Butyl Rubber	red
T - Viton B	blue
V - Plasticized PVC	green
Spiral wound 304 SS with graphite filler, 304 SS inner/outer ring, uncompressed thickness 4.5 mm, compressed thickness 3.2 mm.
5.	Spiral wound 316 SS with graphite filler, 316 SS inner ring, carbon steel outer ring, uncompressed thickness 4.5 mm, compressed thickness 3.2 mm.
6.	For pipe sizes above DN600 use GORETM GR for metallic flanges.  For non-metallic flanges above DN600 use elastomer gaskets appropriate for the service as specified on    5-LST-0003: Piping Service List.
7.	Gasket G is supplied by the glass-lined steel pipe manufacturer.  Continuous U-type PTFE envelope with non-asbestos acid resistant filler and stainless steel corrugated metal insert.  Gasket thickness:  DN250 and less, 7 mm uninstalled, 5 mm compressed.  DN300, 10 mm uninstalled, 7 mm compressed.
8.	Gasket K is supplied by the ductile iron pipe manufacturer.  Step ring design consists of a 1.6 mm thick outer ring for centering and 3.2 mm thick inner ring for sealing.
