## Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Requirements

### General
The following procedures will apply:
0-PRC-7003: Quality Plan
5-PRC-0003: Piping Design and Specification
Safety and Environmental
The consideration of personnel safety during construction, ongoing operation and maintenance, in the design, selection and installation of plant piping is paramount.
Safety showers and eye washes will be designed, specified and located to suit the need and maximise their effectiveness.  Water temperature, flow rate and coverage, supply pressure, accessibility and remote indication of use will be considered.  The water will be tepid and will not have a temperature of greater than 40°C.  In circumstances where the chemical reaction is accelerated by water temperature, medical advice will be sought on the optimum temperature for each application.  The heating effect of sunlight on stagnant lines will be considered.
Cross-contamination of utilities and the safety consequences presented will be avoided by the specification of mutually exclusive fittings. For example, hose couplings unique to the service fluid type will be specified.
Teflon tape will not be used on natural gas piping.
Piping requiring fire protection will be insulated with a suitable medium to provide adequate fire resistance (i.e. at flanged connections on hydrocarbon lines).
Safeguarding in accordance with ASME B31.3 Appendix G will be considered in the design of Normal Fluid Service applications, especially for plastic pipe.

### Material and Component Selection
Material and component selection will be in accordance with 1-PRC-xxxx: Piping Service Index and 5-PRC-0003: Piping Design and Specification.
The applicable standard Aker Kvaerner Chemetics specifications will be updated to include all requirements of this design criteria.
Where the rate of external corrosion is assessed to affect the integrity of the piping over the design life of the Plant, a corrosion protection system will be applied.
PVC, CPVC and FRP plastic piping will have UV (ultraviolet light) protection by way of suitable internal pigment.  PP pipe will be painted for UV protection.  PVDF pipe will be supplied with internal pigment if required to protect liquid contents from UV.
Consideration will be given to using CPVC instead of PVC where solar gain will increase the temperature of the pipe above the design temperature/pressure rating.  This is more cost effective than painting the PVC pipe to increase reflectivity.
Insulating flange sets will be provided between dissimilar materials as required.

### Design
The design of piping will consider the rationalisation of pipe material classes and valves.
ANSI type flanges will be used wherever possible.
Sizing
Piping system sizing will be in accordance with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics standard 5-PRC-xxxx.
Freeze Protection
Outdoor process lines will be protected against freezing by electric heat tracing.
Outdoor utility water lines 2” and smaller will be freeze protected.  Lines 3” and larger will generally not be freeze protected unless they can be stagnant in normal operation.
Layout and Arrangement
Operational and maintenance requirements will be incorporated into the layout and arrangement of piping.  Crane and vehicle access, set-down space, fire-fighting access and clearances and emergency routes will all be considered. Special consideration of equipment and piping that requires regular cleaning or maintenance may necessitate additional space and clearances. Clear overhead access should be provided for equipment handling and removable spools provided where necessary.
Condensate lines and process sample points will be brought to grade and routed to common locations for operations and maintenance convenience. Commonly used valves and instruments will be easily accessible.
Piping will be designed with a minimum number of high points and low points. Where they do occur, the piping system will be provided with vent and drain connections of minimum DN20 (3/4”).  Utility piping vent and drain connections will be threaded couplings with plugs.
Piping conveying corrosive or harmful process fluids will have vents, drains and bleeds routed to a safe location and provided with valves and blinds.
Minimum clearances for piping around equipment such as pumps will be in accordance with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics standard 5-PRC-xxxx.  To avoid flow-induced vibration, check valves will be located a minimum of 6 pipe diameters downstream of the “expander” on pump discharges.
Control valve station piping layout will be in accordance with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics standard 5-PRC-xxxx.
Dead ends will be avoided, especially for piping where solids or fluids may congeal or form corrosive condensate.
Permanent access will be provided for all routine operations (i.e., at sampling points, control valving and isolation valving). Access by mobile platform is acceptable for isolated valving and piping items requiring infrequent access.
Instrumentation and valving will be located to permit operator visibility, maintenance, inspection, repair and replacement.
Underground piping below roads and concrete areas will be avoided. However, where pipes are laid under roads or areas subject to heavy traffic, these will either be laid in suitable unsealed ducts, solidly encased in concrete at least 150 mm thick, or laid at suitable depth to ensure protection of the piping.
Where underground pipes are laid near to or across buried electric power cables they will always be laid at a level lower than the cables. Pipes carrying hot liquids will be kept away from cables, where the heat dissipated could have an adverse effect on the cable’s plastic sheathing.
Where valves, meters and other similar fittings are used on buried lines, they will be housed in a suitable brick chamber or equivalent, with proper access from the surface.
Piping will be arranged so that it does not obstruct a way of exit from any point in a building, elevated equipment, or structure in the case of an emergency.
Provide utility stations such that a single 15 m length of hose can be used to reach the entire working area from the station.
Provide water outlets at grade level only, in pump areas, and near equipment that will be water washed during operation and maintenance.
Provide air outlets in areas where air-driven tools are used such as at heat exchangers, air cooler platforms, compressor area, maintenance platforms on reactors, on columns and on tanks, so that each manway to be serviced is within the reach of a 15 m hose. Also provide air outlets adjacent to valves fitted with pneumatic actuators for ease of operation.
Drain valves will be so positioned as to allow rodding out of the drain through the valve if required.  Drain nipples near the edge of paved areas will be positioned to allow draining onto the concrete slab.
Isolation
Plant equipment and piping will be configured to enable isolation mechanically, electrically and chemically from energy sources to ensure that it is safe to service, repair, remove or replace items of equipment.
Isolation will be provided in accordance with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics design experience with various fluid services and in accordance with normal good industry practice.
System design will provide for drainage from the equipment prior to an initial break-in.  The process of flushing/purging of residual fluid from the isolated system will also be considered.
Any single flange valves to be used for isolation will have threaded lugs.
Spectacle blinds will be accessible from grade or platform. Blinds located in a pipeway are considered accessible. Blinds that weigh over 25 kgs will be accessible by mobile equipment. Where this is not possible, provide davits or hitching points.
Spectacle blinds will be of the non-lugged type so as to minimise the effort required to swing the blind.
Pressure Relief
Pressure relief will be provided for lines that are not otherwise protected by pressure relief on equipment.  An example of this is lines that can be blocked in and heated by the sun.  Provision of pressure relief will be documented on the line list.
Flexibility, Restraint and Support
Piping will be analyzed for stress and movement and supported in accordance with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics standard 5-SPC-xxxx: Pipe Support Specification.
The use of expansion joints in pressurized lines will be minimized.  Wherever possible, piping flexibility will be used to accommodate thermal movement of equipment.  Hoses will be used to accommodate the large thermal and load deflection movements of thermoplastic (i.e. polyethylene) tanks.
Install flat face flanges against flat face white cast iron valves and equipment.
In addition to flexibility analysis, piping systems will be reviewed for the possibility of sanding up, loads applied by spilled materials, flow induced vibration, pressure pulse induced vibration, equipment induced vibration and hydraulic-surge.
Pipe Racks
Standard parametersfor design are:
6 meter baylines with 2 meter intermediate bents
Process piping on bottom level, utility piping above, cables on top level
Minimum 1.5 meters between (parallel) levels (decks)
Avoid flat turns
