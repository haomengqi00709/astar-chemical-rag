# 5-SPC-0124 — Specification


[TABLE: 5-SPC-0124_table_1]


## Purpose
This specification defines Aker Kvaerner Chemetics minimum requirements for fabrication and associated non-destructive examination (NDE) procedures for piping systems made of SARAMET 23 (UNS S30601) and SARAMET 35 (UNS S32615) stainless steels.

## Codes and Standards
4-SPC-0025: Equipment welding SARAMET Stainless Steel
ANSI/ASME B31.3 (latest edition)

## Piping System Requirements
Unless stated otherwise, SARAMET piping systems will be fabricated and tested in accordance with ANSI/ASME B31.3 (latest edition).  The requirements for Normal FIuid Service will apply addition:
Elbows and fittings will be smooth flow wherever possible.  Mitred elbows and fittings will be accepted only after written approval from Aker Kvaerner Chemetics has been given.
Vendor will supply as built drawings of all SARAMET piping systems .

## Fabrication Requirements

### For SARAMET 23
All welding procedures and qualifications (WPS's and PQR's) will conform to ASME requirements and be verified by a third party.
All materials and welds will have full traceability, including mill tests certificates for the material and weld maps that identify the welder and process used for each weld
Only SARAMET 23 run-off tabs, fit up pieces etc. are allowed
Fabricator must perform fit-up (prior to welding) and dimensional checks on all spools/pieces either made in house or procured from third party suppliers.

### For SARAMET 35
Welding of SARAMET 35 will be kept to an absolute minimum.  All fabrication requirements for SARMET 23 (above) apply with the following additional requirements:
Only SARAMET 35 run-off tabs, fit up pieces etc. are allowed.
All temporary weld attachment points must be blended and PT examined.

## NDE Requirements
The nature of SARAMET requires that NDE requirements over and above those laid out in ANSI/ASME B31.3  - including the random X-ray (RT) testing for Normal Fluid Service - are required to ensure that the piping system is supplied free of significant defects, specifically:

### For SARAMET 23
RT will be done in accordance with  ANSI/ASME B31.3.  Minimum radiography will be 5% of long seams and 1% of  circumferential seams – weld tees are preferred locations for RT
Film used for spot RT will be minimum 430 mm (17 inches) long
Final condition liquid penetrant testing (PT) of all weld surfaces (inside and outside) is required.
Hydro testing is required.  Hydro-test water will be drained and the piping system and components will be blown dry with air immediately after final hydrotest.

### For SARAMET 35
All NDE requirements for SARMET 23 (above) apply with the following additional requirements:
PT will be done with solvent removable penetrant only.
Minimum dwell time for penetrant (soak time) is 1 hr.
All interpretation of PT will be done by certified inspectors only (equivalent to CGSB lev. 2 minimum.
All weld tees will be RT tested along the long axis of the pipe.
Hold time for Hydro-testing will be a minimum of 24 hours.
