# 5-PRC-0003 — Procedure


[TABLE: 5-PRC-0003_table_1]


## Purpose
Project personnel will prepare documentation as described in this procedure to provide a record of pipe sizing and design, and to provide a complete description of technical requirements to be issued to vendors and construction contractors.
This procedure assigns responsibilities for document preparation and approval.

## Classification
Piping components are assigned one of five classifications according to the engineering required.

### Type A Piping component (Standard piping and pipe fittings)
Design and manufacture are in accordance with referenced national standards.

### Type B Piping component (Vendor standard design)
Includes valves and speciality piping.  Design and manufacture are by vendor in accordance with vendor’s standard practices, and referenced national standards.

### Type C Piping Component (Custom Fabricated Piping Component; Chemetics Design)
Includes pipe speciality items and special spools that are designed by Chemetics. Design and manufacture are by the vendor in accordance with referenced national standards, and vendor’s standard practices.

### Type D Piping Component (Pipe Support)
Vendor standard design: Design and manufacture are by vendor in accordance with vendor’s standard practices.
Chemetics design: Custom fabricated.

### Acid Plant Ducting
The Vendors will generally produce any shop drawings and will manufacture the hardware in accordance with Chemetics documentation.
