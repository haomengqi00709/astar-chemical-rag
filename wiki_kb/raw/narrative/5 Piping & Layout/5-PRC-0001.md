# 5-PRC-0001 — Procedure


[TABLE: 5-PRC-0001_table_1]


## Purpose
This procedure describes the requirements for standard identification numbers for plant pipes.
It is the responsibility of each  to develop and maintain standard identification numbers for plant lines for their assigned technologies. These numbers will be recorded in the form of standard Piping and Instrument Diagrams.
The standard identification numbers will be used for the development of all related numbering systems such as document numbers, etc.
The standard identification numbers will be used on all projects unless specific requirements dictate otherwise. In such cases, the project team will maintain a dual system on the project line list to provide a cross-reference.

## Number Format
100-SAS-CS6B-780302
100:	Nominal Diameter
SAS:	Code
CS6:	Pipe Material Specification
B:	Gasket Code
7803:	Associated Equipment Number
02:	Serial Number

## Nominal Diameter
A number as shown in the example is in millimeters. If the number is shown in inches, show as e.g. 4”.

## Code
The code designates the fluid present in the pipeline. It is the responsibility of each  to develop and maintain standard codes for their assigned technologies.

## Pipe Material Specification
This references a specification that states the material of construction, wall thickness, connection type, etc. for the components to be used in fabricating the pipeline.

## Gasket Code
This references a specification that states the material of construction, thickness, type, etc. for the gaskets to be used in assembling the pipeline.

## Associated Equipment Number
This is the standard equipment number 4-PRC-0001: Plant Equipment Identification of the equipment to which the pipeline “belongs”, whether that item of equipment is present in the plant or not.
In the case of pipelines interconnecting many items of equipment:
A distribution header belongs to the “primary delivering equipment” (Boiler, Compressor, Tank, etc.).
A collection header belongs to the “collecting equipment” (Sump, Tank, Cooling Tower, etc.).
Branches off the header belong to the “served equipment” (Heat Exchanger, Tower, Tank, etc.).
In the case of pipelines connecting only two items of equipment:
The pipeline belongs to the upstream equipment item.
In the case of pump discharge and suction pipelines:
Pump suction and discharge pipelines belong to the equipment item upstream of the pump.

## Serial Number
A unique two-digit number is assigned, one per pipeline. The serial number must change when there is a change in piping material or in service condition (design pressure or temperature).
In general, serial numbers should ascend in the direction of major process flow.
Equipment trim is also assigned a line number in accordance with this procedure. In this case, the serial number assigned will be the associated nozzle designation with a zero inserted as necessary to create two characters e.g. 78030A, 7803AA.

## Application for Duplicate Items
Piping within the first of any parallel systems will be given the standard serial numbers as described above.
Piping within any subsequent parallel systems will be assigned unused serial numbers as available. A cross-reference to the standard serial numbers will be maintained in the project line list.
COMMENTARY
To Section 1: The benefit is to provide a consistent numbering system to use as the basis for collecting project information for future estimating, for effective referencing in tech-service reports, for a referencing base for design procedures, and to facilitate the re-use of past projects.
The structure of the numbering system has been chosen to allow for future growth by inserting new numbers within those already selected. It is the intention of the system that line number will remain identical for similar services between projects e.g. a caustic distribution header should have the same number whether it is supplied from a head tank or from a pump tank and pump.
