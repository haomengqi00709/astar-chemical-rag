# 5-PRC-0004 — Procedure


[TABLE: 5-PRC-0004_table_1]


## Purpose
The purpose of the Line List is to document the piping design conditions and requirements, and provide the fabrication and testing requirements to the construction sub-contractor.  The Line List is developed alongside the P&ID.  See 1-PRC-0003: P&ID Development and Approval.
This procedure assigns responsibilities for document preparation, checking and approval.

## Standard Form
5-LST-0001: Piping Line List will serve as the basis for a project Line List.

## Design Procedure

### Stage 1 Line List (Layout and Process Data)
Stage 1 Line List will contain the following data for all process lines and utility lines:
Step 1
P&I D reference	By Design from Stage 2 P&ID
Line origin and destination	By Design from Stage 2 P&ID
Fluid code	By Design from Stage 2 P&ID
Line identifier	By Design from Stage 2 P&ID
Preliminary material code	By Design from Stage 2 P&ID
Estimated line size	By Design from Stage 2 P&ID
Type of insulation and heat tracing	By Design from Stage 2 P&ID
Stage 1 Line List will be prepared by Design
Stage 1 Line List will be checked by Process
Stage 1 Line List will be approved by Design

### Stage 2 Line List - Approved for Design (Pipe Study)
Stage 2 Line List will contain the following additional data:
Design flow rate	By Process
Fluid density	By Process
Velocity criteria	By Process
Estimated operating pressure - Maximum	By Process
Estimated operating pressure - Minimum	By Process
Operating temperature – Maximum	By Process
Operating temperature – Minimum	By Process
Stage 2 Line List will be prepared by Process
Stage 2 Line List will be checked by Technology
Stage 2 Line List will be approved by Process

### Stage 3 Line List - Approved for Design (HAZOP)
Stage 3 Line List will contain the following additional data:
line size	By Mechanical
Final gasket material selection	By Mechanical
Design pressure - Maximum	By Mechanical
Design pressure – Minimum	By Mechanical
Design temperature – Maximum	By Mechanical
Design temperature – Minimum	By Mechanical
B31.3 fluid code	By Mechanical
Pressure relief device	By Mechanical
Pressure relief setting	By Mechanical
Insulation thickness	By Mechanical
Stage 3 Line List will be prepared by Mechanical
Stage 3 Line List will be checked by Process
Stage 3 Line List will be approved by Mechanical

### Stage 4 Line List - Approved for Construction
Stage 4 Line List will contain following additional data:
Test pressure	By Mechanical
Test method	By Mechanical
NDT requirements	By Mechanical
Paint	By Mechanical
Stage 4 Line List will be prepared by Mechanical
Stage 4 Line List will be checked by the Project Engineer
Stage 4 Line List will be approved by Mechanical

## Publishing of Line List
Each stage of Line List will be published in accordance with Standard-0-PRC-0005: Publishing of Project Documents.

To Section 3.1:
P&ID reference: P&ID that shows line source
Line number: Refer to 5-PRC-0001: Plant Pipe Numbering Procedure
Design flow: 100 % Design flow from Process flow diagram.
Velocity criteria: Preliminary line size will be calculated using standardized velocity criteria and design flow.
To Section 3.2: The utility header information will come from piping studies and hydraulic network analysis.
To Section 3.3:
Estimated maximum operating pressure: The pressure expected during normal operation including expected process excursions, but excluding mal-operation and equipment failure. The pressure is specified with respect to the lowest point of piping.
Estimated minimum operating pressure: The minimum pressure expected during normal operation including expected process excursions, but excluding mal-operation and equipment failure.  The pressure is specified with respect to the lowest point of piping.  The main concern is vacuum operation – show N/A if no possibility of vacuum.
Operating temperature: Operating temperature is the maximum and minimum temperature expected during normal operation including expected process excursions, but excluding mal-operation and equipment failure.
Insulation type: Process will provide the preliminary insulation requirements e.g.:
HC	Heat Conservation
CC	Cold Conservation
AS	Anti-Sweat
PP	Personnel Protection
ET	Electric Heat Tracing
Pipe and gasket material: Refer to 1-PRC-0005: Piping Service.
Design pressure – Maximum: Refer to ASME B31.3 Section 301.2.  Design pressure will not be less than the pressure at the most severe condition of coincident internal or external pressure and temperature (minimum or maximum) expected in service.   Design pressure is specified with respect to the lowest point of piping.  Maximum operating pressure, pump dead head, set pressure of relief device, battery limit pressure, etc, will be considered.
Design pressure – Minimum: Minimum operating pressure, blocked-in vertical liquid lines, set pressure of vacuum relief devices, etc, will be considered.
Design temperature – Maximum: Refer to ASME B31.3 Section 301.3.   Design temperature is the temperature at which, under the coincident pressure, the greatest thickness or highest component rating is required.  Verify that the design temperature is covered by the pipe specification.  Maximum fluid operating temperature, heating medium temperature, ambient temperature, solar radiation, and all sources of heating, etc, will be considered.
Design temperature – Minimum: Minimum fluid operating temperature, auto-refrigeration, ambient temperature, and all sources of cooling, etc, will be considered.
B31.3 fluid code: Refer to ASME B31.3, Section 300.2, fluid service.
D	Category D
M	Category M
NFS	Normal Fluid Service (other than Category D or M)
Pressure relief: Identify the pressure relief device, where applicable.
PDH	Pump dead head
Tag No.	Relief device (rupture disc, PSV, etc)
BL	Battery limit pressure
ATM	Vent to atmosphere
S	Source
D	Destination
Pressure relief setting:
Refer to pressure relief device data sheet.
Record pump dead head value
To Section 3.4:
Test method: Refer to ASME B31.3, Section 345
Hydro	Hydrostatic Leak Test
Pneumatic	Pneumatic Leak Test
Service	Initial Service Leak Test
Flood	Service Leak Test for equipment trim
Chlorine	Special cleaning and testing for chlorine lines
Test pressure is specified with respect to the lowest point in the pipe.
Paint: Show paint system only if applicable.
