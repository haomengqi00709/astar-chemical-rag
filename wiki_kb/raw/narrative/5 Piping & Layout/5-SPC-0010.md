# 5-SPC-0010 — Specification


[TABLE: 5-SPC-0010_table_1]


## Purpose
This specification, in combination with the details shown on the ductwork drawings, defines Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ requirements for the fabrication of metal ductwork.

## Scope of Work

### Supply
The  will furnish all labour, supervision, materials, consumables, and services required for the fabrication, assembly, inspection, testing, painting, and preparation for delivery of the ductwork in accordance with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics drawings and specifications, including all:
Ductwork supports, guides
Manways, blind flanges, spectacle and blind plates
Bolting and gaskets for bolted connections
Weld bands for all ductwork field joints
Lifting lugs required for the shipping and installation of ductwork spools.  The  is responsible for the design and location of all lifting lugs required for shipment and installation.  The material of lifting lugs will be the same as that of the ductwork spool to which it is attached
Temporary bracing and supports required for the shipping and erection of the ductwork
Spare parts

### Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Supply
The  will install the following components supplied "free issue" by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics:
Butterfly control damper bodies.  Control damper actuators will be shipped directly to site for installation by others
Expansion joints and hinges.  Expansion joint hinges are supplied loose

### Supply and Installation by Others
The following components shown on Aker Kvaerner Chemetics drawings will be supplied by others and installed at site.
Flanged valves and in-line items
Constant and variable force spring hangers, spring supports, and support hardware
Tie-rod assemblies including all clevises, turnbuckles, pins, cotter pins, split pins, jam nuts, washers, beam welding lugs, or clamps required
Control damper actuators
Consumables, including weld rod material, required for all field joints

## Codes and Standards
All work performed and all materials furnished under this specification will be in accordance with the code or standard listed below:
ASME	American Society of Mechanical Engineers, Boiler and Pressure Vessel Code
ASTM	American Society for Testing Materials

## Fabrication

### Shop Fabrication
Field joints are by the split welding band design shown on the duct detail drawings.  Field joints required for construction are shown on the drawings without regard to shipping limitations.  The  may add field joints where required, but the intention is to minimise the number of additional field joints.
Duct expansion joints may be supplied with bracing installed to prevent joint movements during shipping and installation.  These braces will be left in place in preparation for shipping of the ductwork.  If the expansion joints are furnished without braces, or the supplied braces are inadequate for shipping, the  will provide additional braces.
For significant lengths of attached ducts, (4) pieces of 100 x 100 x 10 mm ”angle welded to the inside of the duct should be used as a minimum to brace each expansion joint.  Stainless steel angle, or alternatively carbon steel angles with stainless steel poison pads will be used to brace expansion joints in stainless steel ducts.
Expansion joints are typically provided with internal hinges.  The  will ensure that hinges are oriented in the duct as shown on the drawings.  Expansion joint hinge positions will be clearly marked on the outside of the duct section by the .

### Welding
Welding will be per the ’s welding procedures, in accordance with 4-SPC-0060: Welding Carbon Steel, and 4-SPC-0061: Welding Austenitic Stainless Steel.

### Workmanship
Poor workmanship will be reason for rejection.  Flaws or defects which cannot be reasonably repaired, excessive repairs, poor fit-up, nozzles requiring relocation and damaged surfaces, etc., will be considered evidence of poor workmanship.
All pads, backing plates, manhole necks, and similar attachments will fit closely to and conform with the curvature of the surface to which they are attached.
Manways, nozzles and their reinforcement will not be located within 75mm of any weld.

### Tolerances
The butting edges of plates at all seams will not be out-of-alignment by more than 20% of the thickness of the plate, or 1.6mm whichever is less.
The difference between the maximum and minimum diameters as measured against any cross section will not exceed 0.5% of the nominal diameter.

### Painting
The  will paint all carbon steel surfaces in accordance with 7-SPC-0001: Protective Coating, including all:
External support brackets, lugs, support saddles, etc., attached by welding to any ductwork.
Loose structural components and hardware.
Lifting lugs and temporary expansion joint bracing will be painted yellow.
The following will not be painted:
Ductwork to be covered with insulation
Stainless steel
Internal carbon steel surfaces

## and Testing
Butt welds in the ductwork will be spot radiographic examination in accordance with ASME Code Section VIII iv 1, UW52.
Welds between expansion joints, their hinges and the ducting will be subject to liquid penetrant examination.

### Radiography
Radiography will be performed in accordance with ASME Section V, Article 2.
Weld intersections will be the preferred locations for radiographs.
Radiography locations will be selected in such a manner that the welding of each welder/welding operator is examined.

### Liquid Penetrant Examination
Liquid penetrant examination will be performed in accordance with ASME Section V, Article 6.  Acceptance standards will be per ASME Section VIII, Division 1, Appendix 8.

### Magnetic Particle Examination
When required or specified, magnetic particle examination will be performed in accordance with ASME Section V, Article 7.  Acceptance standards will be per ASME Section VIII, Division 1, Appendix 6.

### Testing
Unless otherwise specified, all ducting will be leak tested by others, after installation, under initial service conditions.  The  will be responsible for remediation of any defects in the fabricated duct spools discovered during the initial service leak test.

## Preparation for Shipment
Packaging and shipping will be in accordance with 0-SPC-004: Packaging Land Transport or 0-SPC-005: Packaging Marine Transport, and the following
The  will verify all spool weights prior to lifting, including verification of weight of components supplied free issue and assembled into the ductwork.  The  will mark the weight on the outside of each duct spool.
The  will maintain a dry condition inside of all ductwork spools throughout the duration of the fabrication.  All openings in the ductwork spools will be covered and sealed prior to shipment.
The spool number will be clearly marked on each duct spool in two places, at opposite sides of each end.
Exposed machine surfaces, including flange faces, will be coated with commercial rust preventative.
Duct spools will be adequately braced at the open ends to maintain roundness tolerance during shipping.
Flanges will be blanked with bolted or steel-strapped wood or metal covers.
Tapped connections will be fitted with threaded steel pipe plugs.  Threaded ends will be provided with steel thread protectors.
All other openings will be suitably covered to prevent the entry of unwanted material or water.
