# Inspection and Documentation Requirements for 0-QDS-7404-5

This page details the specific inspection, witness, hold, and documentation requirements for the Electrostatic Precipitator Shell & Head Sections-FRP, as outlined in Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7404-5. These requirements ensure comprehensive quality control throughout the manufacturing process.

**Inspection and Documentation Activities:**

*   **Material Test Certificates:**
    *   Requires an [[0-QDS-7404-5_quality_record_definitions|FP quality record]].
    *   Chemetics **will Witness** this activity.

*   **Filament Winding Pattern:**
    *   Chemetics **will Witness** this activity.

*   **Certificate of Compliance:**
    *   Requires an [[0-QDS-7404-5_quality_record_definitions|FP quality record]].

*   **Barcol Hardness Test:**
    *   Requires an [[0-QDS-7404-5_quality_record_definitions|FP quality record]].
    *   Chemetics **will Witness** this test.
    *   Chemetics **will Hold** this test, meaning work cannot proceed without Chemetics' approval.

*   **External Coating/Lining Inspection:**
    *   Requires an [[0-QDS-7404-5_quality_record_definitions|FP quality record]].
    *   Chemetics **will Witness** this inspection.
    *   Chemetics **will Hold** this inspection.

*   **Internal Coating/Lining Inspection:**
    *   Requires an [[0-QDS-7404-5_quality_record_definitions|FP quality record]].
    *   Chemetics **will Witness** this inspection.
    *   Chemetics **will Hold** this inspection.

*   **Dimensional Final Inspection:**
    *   Requires an [[0-QDS-7404-5_quality_record_definitions|FP quality record]].
    *   Chemetics **will Witness** this inspection.
    *   Chemetics **will Hold** this inspection.

*   **Acetone Wipe Test:**
    *   Requires an [[0-QDS-7404-5_quality_record_definitions|FP quality record]].
    *   Chemetics **will Witness** this test.
    *   Chemetics **will Hold** this test.

*   **Surfaces Free From Defects:**
    *   Chemetics **will Witness** verification of surfaces.
    *   Chemetics **will Hold** verification of surfaces.

*   **Cleanliness:**
    *   Chemetics **will Witness** cleanliness checks.
    *   Chemetics **will Hold** cleanliness checks.

*   **Review QA Documentation:**
    *   Chemetics **will Witness** the review of Quality Assurance Documentation.
    *   Chemetics **will Hold** the review of Quality Assurance Documentation.

*   **Packaging:**
    *   Chemetics **will Witness** the packaging process.

*   **Packing List:**
    *   Requires an [[0-QDS-7404-5_quality_record_definitions|AP quality record]].
    *   Chemetics **will Witness** the packing list.

These requirements are critical for ensuring the quality and integrity of the supplied components. For related procedures, refer to [[vendor_inspection_release]] and [[third_party_inspection_reports]].
