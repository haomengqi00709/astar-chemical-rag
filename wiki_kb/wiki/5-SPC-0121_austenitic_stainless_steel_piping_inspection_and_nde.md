# Inspection and Non-Destructive Examination for Austenitic Stainless Steel Piping (5-SPC-0121)

This section outlines the mandatory requirements for inspection and non-destructive examination (NDE) of austenitic stainless steel piping during fabrication.

**Access and Responsibility:**
*   The [[5-SPC-0121_austenitic_stainless_steel_piping_specification_overview#inspector|Inspector]] **must** have free access to the Vendor's work and to his Subvendor's (if work is sublet by approval) at all reasonable times. Access **must** also be given to Authorized Representatives of the End-User.
*   The responsibility for, and cost of carrying out all required inspection is borne by the shop fabricator or field contractor.

**Visual Inspection:**
*   All welds **must** be visually inspected and repaired where necessary.
*   Lines in slurry service **must** be visually inspected.
*   Internal weld projections (e.g., "icicles") **must** be identified and completely removed in slurry service lines.

**Dye Penetrant Examination (PT):**
*   Where pipe fabrication is not subject to radiography, dye penetrant examination **must** be carried out on 10% of finished butt welds.
*   Acceptance standards for dye penetrant examination are in accordance with [[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping|ASME B31.3]] Table 341.3.2A and paragraph 341.3.2.
*   Dye penetrant examination **must** be performed on the first-off welds completed by each welder to establish welder performance and weld quality.
*   Where access permits, dye penetrant inspection **must** be performed on the process side of selected welds as well as the external side.
*   If access to the process side of the weld is not available, the root pass and cover pass on the external side **will** be inspected.
*   Dye penetrant examinations **must** be subject to supervision by the [[5-SPC-0121_austenitic_stainless_steel_piping_specification_overview#inspector|Inspector]].

**Radiographic Examination (RT):**
*   Lines in slurry service **must** be spot radiographed.
*   Butt and groove welds (including branch connections) using solar flux **must** be subject to random radiography. The random radiography for these welds is 10 percent.
*   Random radiography requirements are defined in [[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping|ASME B31.3]], paragraph 341.4(b).
*   Random radiography, where specified, **must** cover the welding of each welder and operator.
*   Random radiography **must** be performed on the first-off butt welds (full circumference) completed by each welder or operator.
*   Random radiography on first-off butt welds **must** be performed as early as possible during production.
*   Radiographic examinations **must** be subject to supervision by the [[5-SPC-0121_austenitic_stainless_steel_piping_specification_overview#inspector|Inspector]].
