# Confined Space Entry: Pre-Entry Procedures

Prior to any entry into a confined space, a series of critical pre-entry procedures **must** be meticulously followed to ensure the safety of all personnel. These steps include thorough preparation of the space, isolation of energy sources, and comprehensive atmospheric testing.

**1. Hazard Assessment:**
*   Before entering a confined space, a written [[0-REP-8007b_confined_space_hazard_assessment_form|Confined Space Hazard Assessment form]] **must** be prepared by the Commissioning HSE Manager for the space of interest.
*   It is the responsibility of the designated “Competent Person” to perform the task safety analysis.
*   If an existing confined space entry protocol is available and applicable, that plan **must** be reviewed by the “Competent Person” on the confined space entry team.
*   The entry protocol **shall** include, but not be limited to:
    *   Assignment of responsibilities.
    *   Written safe work procedures for entry into and work in the confined space, addressing:
        *   Identification and entry permits.
        *   Lockout and isolation.
        *   Verification and testing prior to entry.
        *   Cleaning, purging, venting or inerting, and ventilation.
        *   Standby persons.
        *   Rescue.
        *   Lifelines, harnesses, and lifting equipment.
        *   Personal protective equipment and other precautions.
        *   Coordination of work activities.
        *   Personal protective equipment and lifeline/harness system.

**2. Space Preparation (Cleaning and Ventilation):**
*   The vessel **should** be emptied and made clean and void of residue according to the nature of its contents. Cleaning methods include hot or cold water flush, steaming, chemical neutralization, or air purge.
*   Harmful vapours from cleaning **should** be vented safely to the outdoors where practicable.
*   The vessel **should** be thoroughly ventilated, preferably by some positive method of mechanical exhaust. Ventilation by mechanical exhaust **should** avoid recirculating contaminated air.
*   All ventilation or air moving equipment **shall** be grounded to the vessel to avoid static sparks.
*   After the vessel is cleaned and ventilated, the mechanical exhaust system **should** be kept operating.

**3. Isolation and Lockout/Tagout:**
*   Should decontamination involve flammable liquids, vapours, gases, or dusts, all sources of ignition **shall** be eliminated.
*   The vessel **should** be completely isolated from all other systems and equipment.
*   Positive and adequate measures **must** be taken to prevent harmful material (solids, liquid, or vapour) from entering the vessel while workers are inside.
*   In all cases where lines have contained hazardous fluids or inert gas, or where they contain fluids at high pressures or temperatures, the lines **should** be physically disconnected.
*   Lines **should** be isolated by placing a blank in the lines as close to the tank as practical.
*   Drains or overflow lines **should** be disconnected or blanked if they extend into a sewer from which steam or fumes could be conducted back into the vessel, or if they connect to other equipment or extend to a remote location.
*   All lines to be disconnected or blanked or capped to prevent flow from disconnected ends entering a vessel opening or draining onto assistants on the outside.
*   All blanks **shall** be of sufficient thickness and tensile strength to withstand maximum pressures that may be imposed.
*   Blanks **shall** be of material which will withstand corrosion of chemicals to which they may be exposed.
*   Motor control center disconnect switches supplying power to any mechanical mixers, agitators, pumps, and cover or door actuators **should** be tagged and locked in the "OFF" position. It is **not adequate** to lock a local push-button station.
*   The man in the vessel **should** keep the key. Only the man in the vessel **shall** be authorized to unlock the switch and remove the tag on completion of the job.
*   Where more than one man is exposed, each **should** place his own lock on the switch.
*   In certain cases, it may be advisable to have an electrician pull the line fuses in the electrical circuit in addition to lock and tag requirements.
*   Refer to procedure [[0-PRC-8504-RA_lockout_and_tagging]].

**4. Gas Testing:**
*   Before entry is made into any vessel, a test for oxygen and both flammable and toxic gases **shall** be made.
*   The last substance contained in the vessel **must** be considered for complete gas testing, as residue can leave or generate toxic fumes that normal O2/combustible gas checks will not detect.
*   Under no condition **will entry be made** until this procedure is carried out and all potential gases and contaminants are checked for.
*   Gas testing **will be done** prior to any entry.
*   Gas testing **will be done** at intervals of one hour during the work/inspection of the confined space.
*   All atmospheric testing equipment **will be calibrated** according to the manufacturer's recommendations, tested, and certified before each use with calibration gas.
