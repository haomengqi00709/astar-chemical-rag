# Project Change Evaluation

The Project Change Evaluation phase is a critical step in the [[0-PRC-4003-R1_project_change_management_flow_diagram|Project Change Management Flow Diagram]], where the potential impacts of a proposed change are thoroughly assessed. This phase involves multiple project disciplines and specialists to ensure a comprehensive understanding of the change's implications on cost, schedule, resources, and risks.

The evaluation process is coordinated by the [[change_management_roles_and_responsibilities_0_prc_4003|Project Control Specialist]], who performs the following key actions:
*   The Project Control Specialist **must** register the incoming Project Variation Notice (PVN) or Project Change Notice (PCN).
*   The Project Control Specialist **must** distribute the PVN/PCN to relevant stakeholders for a detailed change impact evaluation.

Key personnel involved in the evaluation and their responsibilities include:
*   **[[change_management_roles_and_responsibilities_0_prc_4003|Discipline Leads]]**: These individuals are responsible for evaluating the impact of the proposed change on man-hours and the project schedule within their respective disciplines.
*   **[[change_management_roles_and_responsibilities_0_prc_4003|Estimator]]**: If required, the Estimator **must** estimate the total cost of the proposed change, providing a financial assessment.
*   **[[change_management_roles_and_responsibilities_0_prc_4003|Project Risk Management]]**: This team reviews the changes, assesses associated [[risk_assessments|risks]], and determines any necessary [[project_risk_analysis_0_lst_2006|contingency requirements]]. They **must** update the [[risk_register]] if necessary to reflect new or modified risks.
*   **[[change_management_roles_and_responsibilities_0_prc_4003|Project Engineer]]**: The Project Engineer plays a coordinating role, ensuring that the evaluations of man-hours impacts and schedule impacts are properly conducted and integrated.
*   **[[change_management_roles_and_responsibilities_0_prc_4003|Engineering Management]]**: This entity is involved in the overall review and guidance of the evaluation process, ensuring technical feasibility and adherence to engineering standards.

After all impacts have been assessed by the respective teams, the Project Control Specialist **must** incorporate all impact assessments into a comprehensive document. Subsequently, the Project Control Specialist **must** issue a [[variation_report]] for approval. This report summarizes all findings and provides the necessary information for decision-making in the [[project_change_approval|change approval]] phase.

For detailed procedures on assessing change impacts, refer to [[pvn_pcn_impact_analysis_and_evaluation]]. The registration and documentation management of PVNs/PCNs are further elaborated in [[pvn_pcn_log_and_documentation_management]]. For definitions of the documents mentioned, please see [[change_management_document_types]].

This phase involves multiple project disciplines and specialists to thoroughly assess the potential effects on project scope, schedule, cost, and resources.
Upon initiation of a PVN/PCN, the Project Control Specialist registers the document and distributes it for change impact evaluation. Discipline Leads are responsible for evaluating man-hours and schedule impacts. An Estimator estimates the total cost of the change if required. Project Risk Management reviews changes, assesses associated risks, and determines any contingency requirements. The Project Engineer plays a coordinating role, overseeing the evaluation of man-hours and schedule impacts. Finally, the Project Control Specialist incorporates all impacts assessments into a comprehensive report.
