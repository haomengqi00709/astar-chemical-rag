# 4-SCR-0031 Anchor Bolt and Nut Material

This section specifies the material requirements for the anchor bolts and nuts used in the support of the Plate & Frame Heat Exchanger on Steel, as outlined in document 4-SCR-0031.

**Material Specifications:**
*   **Anchor Bolt Material:** 4.6
*   **Nut Material:** 4

These materials are selected to ensure the structural integrity and longevity of the equipment's support system, in conjunction with the dimensions specified in [[4-SCR-0031_equipment_dimensions_and_anchor_details]].
