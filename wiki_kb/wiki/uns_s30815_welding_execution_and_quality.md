# Welding Execution and Quality for UNS S30815 Fabrications

This page outlines the critical requirements for the execution of welding and the standards for weld quality and finishing for UNS S30815 fabrications. Strict adherence to these procedures ensures the structural integrity and aesthetic quality of the final product.

**Welding Environment and Surface Preparation**
*   As far as practical, all welds **WILL** be made in the downhand (flat) position. Vertical welds **WILL** be made vertical up (uphand).
*   Suitable enclosures **WILL** be provided to keep welds and welders free from draughts, rain, or other inclement weather for welding on site. Welding **WILL NOT** be performed on wet surfaces.
*   The fusion faces and the adjacent metal, both internal and external, **WILL** be free from moisture, scale, paint, grease, or other foreign matter immediately prior to welding.
*   The surface and edges of any austenitic steel to be heated for any purpose **WILL** be cleaned free from all traces of foreign matter (e.g., marking paint, oil, etc.). Cleaning methods include grinding, wire brushing with a stainless steel brush, and/or non-chlorinated solvent cleaning. Cleaning **MUST** be done immediately prior to the application of heat.

**Alignment and Fit-Up**
*   The alignment of welding edges at butt joints **WILL** be in accordance with relevant code, specification, etc.
*   Maximum values for misalignment (High - Low) for plates up to and including in thickness, .
*   Maximum values for misalignment (High - Low) for plates above in thickness, 25% of the plate thickness or whichever is smaller.
*   For pipe, misalignment **WILL** be in accordance with [[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping]].
*   Prior to welding a slip-on flange, the sum of the gaps on any diameter **WILL NOT** exceed .
*   Parts to be welded **WILL** be securely held in position by mechanical means, welded-on bridge pieces, or by tack welding to maintain the specified alignment and gap.
*   Reinforcing rods and other shaped items **WILL** closely conform to their matching curvature.
*   Where a jig or other suitable device is used, its construction **WILL** allow adequate access for welding and cleaning, and **MUST** be such that external loading is not applied to a joint during welding.
*   Welded-on bridge pieces or support spiders **WILL** be of 253 MA on the surface welded in contact with the vessel. The plate surface **WILL NOT** be left in a damaged condition after their removal.

**Tack Welds and Back-Up Rings**
*   Tack welds employed for assembly purposes **MAY** form part of the completed weld if complete fusion is effected at the root of the weld, and the welding procedure used is the same as that employed for the remainder of the root run.
*   Cracked and unsound tack welds **WILL** be cut out prior to welding.
*   Sound tack welds **MUST** be thoroughly fused into the root run of the main weld. It is sometimes necessary to thin down the ends of tack welds to ensure thorough fusion. Where thinning is necessary, tack welds **SHOULD** preferably be at least one inch long to ensure the remaining part is strong enough to hold the plate or pipe in place.
*   The use of permanent back-up rings or strips or consumable joint inserts is **NOT permitted** unless specifically approved in writing by Chemetics' Q.A. and Materials Group or shown on the Drawings.

**Arc Strikes and Weld Cleaning**
*   Every effort **WILL** be made to confine arc strikes to the surface on which weld metal is to be deposited.
*   If an arc strike occurs outside the weld metal deposition area, the spot **WILL** require to be ground, PT examined per Para. 10.3 (see [[uns_s30815_inspection_and_nde]]), and repaired if necessary. Burn marks from grounding clamps **WILL** be treated as arc strikes.
*   Each run of weld metal **WILL** be thoroughly cleaned before the next run is deposited. All slag and foreign matter **WILL** be removed.
*   On re-starting, care **WILL** be taken to ensure proper fusion with the weld metal previously deposited.
*   If a tungsten electrode accidentally makes contact with the work, the section of weld and the tip of the electrode **WILL** be examined. Where tungsten loss has occurred, the tungsten pick-up in the weld **WILL** be ground out, and the electrode **WILL** be reground to the correct profile before re-starting.
*   Peening or hammering of weld runs is **NOT permitted**, except for light peening to remove slag.

**Weld Appearance and Finishing**
*   Each weld **WILL** be uniform in width and size throughout its full length. Each layer of welding **WILL** be smooth and free of slag, inclusions, cracks, porosity, lack of fusion, and undercut, except as permitted by referenced codes.
*   The cover pass **WILL** be free of coarse ripples, irregular surface, non-uniform bead patterns, high crown, and deep ridges or valleys between beads.
*   Buttwelds **WILL** be slightly convex, of uniform height, and have full penetration.
*   Crevices **WILL NOT** be permitted on the process side of completed weldments.
*   Fillet welds **WILL** be deposited in not less than two layers, as far as is practicable. Fillet weld surfaces **WILL** be free from marked irregularities. Dimensions of fillet welds **WILL** be in accordance with the approved drawing.
*   Grinding or gouging **WILL NOT** be carried out in such a manner as to reduce the adjacent base metal thickness.
*   Grinding wheels **WILL** be iron-free and of the resin bonded alumina or silica type.
*   Wire brushes or chipping tools **WILL** be made of austenitic stainless steel and **MUST NOT** have been previously used to clean surfaces other than stainless steels.

**Surface Preparation and Contamination Control**
*   The surface and edges of any austenitic steel which is to be heated for any purpose will be cleaned free from all traces of foreign matter (e.g., marking paint, oil, etc.).
*   Cleaning methods include grinding, wire brushing with a stainless steel brush, and/or non-chlorinated solvent cleaning. Cleaning must be done immediately prior to the application of heat.
*   The Fabricator will take the controls necessary to prevent contamination of weldments at all stages of fabrication. Controls will include the segregation of wire brushes, grinding wheels, etc., for use on stainless steels only.

**Welding Environment and Position**
*   When adverse weather conditions give rise to condensation or wetness, the material will be dried prior to welding by heating. Drying temperature must be just sufficient to prevent condensation.
*   As far as practical, all welds will be made in the downhand (flat) position. Vertical welds will be made vertical up (uphand).
*   Suitable enclosures will be provided to keep welds and welders free from draughts, rain, or other inclement weather for welding on site.
*   Welding will not be performed on wet surfaces.
*   The fusion faces and the adjacent metal, both internal and external, will be free from moisture, scale, paint, grease, or other foreign matter immediately prior to welding.

*   Parts to be welded will be securely held in position by mechanical means, welded-on bridge pieces, or by tack welding to maintain the specified alignment and gap.
*   Reinforcing rods and other shaped items will closely conform to their matching curvature.

**Jigging and Temporary Attachments**
*   Where a jig or other suitable device is used, the construction of the jig will allow adequate access for welding and cleaning. Jig construction will be such that external loading is not applied to a joint during welding.
*   Welded-on bridge pieces or support spiders will be of 253 MA on the surface welded in contact with the vessel. Where used, the plate surface will not be left in a damaged condition after they are removed.

**Tack Welds**
*   Tack welds employed for assembly purposes may form part of the completed weld. For tack welds to form part of the completed weld, complete fusion must be effected at the root of the weld, and the welding procedure used must be the same as that employed for the remainder of the root run.
*   Cracked and unsound tack welds will be cut out prior to welding.
*   Sound tack welds must be thoroughly fused into the root run of the main weld.
*   It is sometimes necessary to thin down the ends of tack welds to ensure thorough fusion. Tack welds should preferably be at least one inch long (where thinning is necessary) to ensure that the remaining part of the tack weld is strong enough to hold the plate or pipe in place.

**Back-up Rings and Arc Strikes**
*   The use of permanent back-up rings or strips or consumable joint inserts is not permitted unless specifically approved in writing by Q.A. and Materials Group or shown on the Drawings.
*   Every effort will be made to confine arc strikes to the surface on which weld metal is to be deposited. If an arc strike occurs outside the weld metal deposition area, the spot will require to be ground, PT examined per Para. 10.3, and repaired if necessary. Burn marks from grounding clamps will be treated as arc strikes.

**Interpass Cleaning and Welding Technique**
*   Each run of weld metal will be thoroughly cleaned before the next run is deposited. All slag and foreign matter will be removed from each run of weld metal before the next run is deposited.
*   On re-starting, care will be taken to ensure proper fusion with the weld metal previously deposited.
*   If a tungsten electrode accidentally makes contact with the work, the section of weld will be examined, and the tip of the electrode will be examined. Where tungsten loss has occurred, the tungsten pick-up in the weld will be ground out, and the electrode will be reground to the correct profile before re-starting.
*   Peening of weld runs is not permitted except for light peening to remove slag. Hammering of finished welds is not permitted except for light peening to remove slag.

**Weld Quality and Appearance**
*   Each weld will be uniform in width and size throughout its full length.
*   Each layer of welding will be smooth and free of slag, inclusions, cracks, porosity, lack of fusion, and undercut, except as permitted by referenced codes.
*   The cover pass will be free of coarse ripples, irregular surface, non-uniform bead patterns, high crown, and deep ridges or valleys between beads.
*   Buttwelds will be slightly convex, of uniform height, and will have full penetration.
*   Crevices will not be permitted on the process side of completed weldments.
*   Fillet welds will be deposited in not less than two layers, as far as is practicable. Fillet weld surfaces will be free from marked irregularities.
*   Dimensions of fillet welds will be in accordance with the approved drawing.

**Post-Welding Cleaning and Finishing**
*   Grinding or gouging will not be carried out in such a manner as to reduce the adjacent base metal thickness.
*   Grinding wheels will be iron-free and of the resin bonded alumina type or resin bonded silica type.
*   Wire brushes or chipping tools will be made of austenitic stainless steel and must not have been previously used to clean surfaces other than stainless steels.
*   On completion of work, items will be cleaned internally and externally. All fabrication markings, oxidation and ferroxyl testing, weld spatter, etc., will be removed prior to shipment.
*   Passivation, where required by Purchase Order/referenced documents, will be carried out in accordance with written procedures. Passivation procedures will be approved by [Company Name] prior to use.
