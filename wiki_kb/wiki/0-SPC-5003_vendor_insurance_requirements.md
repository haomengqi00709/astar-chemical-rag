# Vendor Insurance Requirements (0-SPC-5003)

Under Specification **0-SPC-5003**, specific requirements for vendor insurance are outlined to manage [[risk_allocation_and_indemnities|risks]] associated with the provision of Goods and Services.

Any additional [[insurance_requirements|insurance]] that **must** be provided by the Vendor **shall** be explicitly stated in the [[0-SPC-5003_insurance_table|Insurance Table]].

All required insurances **shall** be held in the joint names of both Parties.

These insurances **must** provide comprehensive coverage for events that are deemed to be at the Vendor's risk. This coverage period extends from the project's starting date until the official Defects Certificate has been issued, ensuring continuous protection throughout the project lifecycle and beyond initial completion.
