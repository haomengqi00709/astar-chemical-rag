# Electrical Design Criteria

The Electrical Design Criteria document (9-PRC-0002) serves as a foundational guide for all electrical design work on a project. It compiles essential requirements and references to ensure compliance and consistency.

**Content of Electrical Design Criteria**
The Electrical Design Criteria document **shall** contain:
*   Statutory requirements relevant to the project location.
*   Owner requirements, including specific codes, standards, regulations, typical details, and equipment specifications.
*   References to applicable specifications and data sheets.
*   Any additional general requirements pertinent to Plant electrical design.

**Compilation and Compliance**
The electrical design criteria is a comprehensive compilation of:
*   Owner’s electrical design criteria and specifications.
*   Electrical design criteria and specifications.
*   Electrical codes, standards, regulations, and local codes specific to the project location.

For example, for a project located in Brisbane, Australia, the electrical codes, standards, regulations, and local codes in effect for Brisbane, Australia, **must** be used and followed.
