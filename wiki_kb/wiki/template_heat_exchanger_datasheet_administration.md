**General Fields:**
The following fields are to be completed for specific equipment:
*   **Tag No.:** Unique identifier for the equipment.
*   **Location:** Physical location of the equipment within the plant.
*   **Quantity:** Can be specified as "Operating" or "Installed Spare".
*   **Case Description:** A brief description of the operating scenario.
*   **Design Basis:** The fundamental principles or data used for the design.
*   **Design Case:** The specific operating case for which the equipment is designed.
*   **Location (for Minimum Excess Area):** The specific area where the minimum excess area requirement applies.
