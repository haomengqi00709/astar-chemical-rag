# Wire Payment Authorization Form

This page describes the Wire Payment Authorization Form, which is a critical document for processing payments in currencies other than Canadian Dollars (CDN$).

**Purpose**
The Wire Payment Authorization Form is used to provide Aker Kvaerner Chemetics with the necessary banking details and authorization to process payments via wire transfer when the payment currency is not CDN$.

**Usage**
Agencies supplying Third Party Inspection Services **must** complete and sign this form when requesting payment in a currency other than CDN$. The completed form **shall** be sent to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics as part of the invoicing process.

**Related Information**
For details on when this form is required and where to send it, refer to [[third_party_inspection_invoicing_and_payment]].
