# Agitation and Mixing Equipment Checkout Requirements

This page details the mandatory requirements for the mechanical checkout of agitation and mixing equipment, designated by suffix 'A' (Agitators, Mixers, Blenders, and Aerators). This process is a key part of the overall [[mechanical_equipment_checkout_procedure]].

**Required Documents:**
The following documents are mandatory for the checkout of agitation and mixing equipment:
*   [[piping_and_instrumentation_drawings|P&ID]]
*   Manufacturers documentation
*   [[equipment_data_sheets_4_dst_xxxx|Equipment data sheet]]

**General Installation and Structural Checks:**
*   **Mandatory:** Nameplate data and record for Agitation and Mixing Equipment **must** match the pump drawing/data sheet information.
*   **Mandatory:** Nameplate tag for Agitation and Mixing Equipment **must** match manufacturer information.
*   **Mandatory:** Motor nameplate for Agitation and Mixing Equipment **must** match the manufacturer information.
*   **Mandatory:** Agitator **must** be firmly mounted.
*   **Mandatory:** Bolting for agitator mounting **must** be reviewed if required.
*   **Mandatory:** Agitator shaft **must** be straight.
*   **Mandatory:** Agitator **must** be mounted at the correct height.
*   **Mandatory:** Propeller **must** be away from the pump suction nozzle or [[vortex_breaker_installation|vortex breaker]].
*   **Mandatory:** Agitator **must** be accessible.
*   **Mandatory:** Sufficient room **must** be available for agitator removal/maintenance.
*   **Mandatory:** Motor installation field check is in the [[electrical_systems_field_checkout_9_prc_0004|electrical checklist]].
*   **Mandatory:** Determine if the motor has sealed bearings or whether lubrication is required.

**Surface Treatment and Lining:**
*   **Mandatory (where applicable):** [[coating_and_lining_inspection|Lining]] **must** be continuous.
*   **Mandatory (where applicable):** Lining inserts **must** be sealed.
*   **Mandatory (where applicable):** Lining seams **must** be smooth and continuous.
*   **Mandatory:** Agitator **must** be coated per project specification.

**Coupling and Guards:**
*   **Mandatory:** Coupling **must** be installed.
*   **Mandatory:** Shaft **must** rotate freely.
*   **Mandatory (if required):** Guard **must** be installed.
*   **Mandatory (if required):** Guard bolts **must** be tight.

**Grounding:**
*   **Mandatory:** [[grounding_installation_details|Agitator grounding cable]] connection **must** be tight.
*   **Mandatory:** Agitator grounding cable **must** be connected to the main grounding loop.

**Nozzles and Piping:**
*   **Mandatory:** Agitator **must** be clear of the tank opening.
*   **Mandatory:** Agitator nozzles **must not** be strained by connected piping.
*   **Mandatory:** Adequate pipe supports **must** be installed for agitator nozzles.

**Lubrication and Sealing:**
*   **Mandatory (where required):** Agitator [[bearing_lubrication_and_inspection|bearings]] **must** be greased.
*   **Mandatory:** Gearbox oil filling **must** follow the installation procedure.
*   **Mandatory:** Gearbox **must** be full to the fill line.
*   **Mandatory:** Gearbox oil **must** be the correct specification.
*   **Mandatory:** Correct seal **must** be installed.
*   **Mandatory:** [[seal_flush_system_piping|Seal flush piping]] **must** be connected.
