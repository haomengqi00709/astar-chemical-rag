# FRP Tank Packing and Shipping Requirements (0-QDS-7414)

This page outlines the packing and shipping requirements for FRP Tanks, as specified in Quality Data Sheet **0-QDS-7414**. Proper packing and documentation are essential to ensure the safe and undamaged delivery of the tanks to the project site.

**Mandatory Packing and Crating Requirements:**
*   **Packing/Crating** is a mandatory requirement and **must** be included in the Vendor's Inspection and Test (I & T) Plan. This ensures that the methods used for protecting the FRP tank during transit are planned and executed correctly.

**Specific Requirements:**

*   **Packaging:**
    *   Requires **Chemetics Witness** surveillance. A Chemetics representative **must** be present to observe the packaging process, ensuring it meets the specified protection standards.

*   **Packing List:**
    *   Requires Quality Record **AP** (As Prepared). The packing list **must** be prepared by the vendor.
    *   Requires **Chemetics Witness** surveillance. A Chemetics representative **must** be present to verify the contents and accuracy of the packing list.

These requirements ensure that the FRP tanks are adequately protected during transportation and that all necessary documentation for logistics and customs is accurately prepared and verified. For general packing and shipping guidelines, refer to [[0-QDS-7411_packing_and_shipping_requirements]].
