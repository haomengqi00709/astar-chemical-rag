# Material Certifications (0-QDS-7416)

For the Plate & Frame Heat Exchanger project (**0-QDS-7416**), the following material certifications are required:

*   **Mill Test Certificates:** These certificates are required as **FP** (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package). Chemetics surveillance for Mill Test Certificates is **Complete**.
*   **Certificate of Compliance:** This certificate is required as **FP** (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package). Chemetics surveillance for the Certificate of Compliance is **Complete**.

The definitions for **FP** can be found in [[0-QDS-7416_quality_record_definitions]]. The extent of Chemetics' surveillance is further detailed in [[0-QDS-7416_chemetics_surveillance_level]].
