# Technical Bid Tabulation Template (0-TAB-5XXX-2)

This page describes the template 0-TAB-5XXX-2, which is used for documenting the detailed technical evaluation of Vendor bids.

**Purpose:**
The [[technical_bid_tabulation_template]] is a standardized form used by the Requisitioning Discipline and other participating disciplines to compare the technical information provided by short-listed Vendors against the project's Scope of Supply requirements. It facilitates a structured assessment of technical compliance, performance characteristics, and potential impacts on project guarantees.

**Content:**
The template typically includes sections for:
*   Comparison of technical specifications and data.
*   Assessment of compliance with project requirements.
*   Highlighting superior technical characteristics of a Vendor's offer.
*   Evaluation of process performance (e.g., throughput, efficiency, utility consumption).
*   Analysis of impact on Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' performance guarantees.
*   Summary of observations based on previous experience with the Vendor.
*   Information gathered from Vendor references.
*   Documentation of clarification queries and Vendor responses.
*   Ranking of Vendors by each evaluating discipline.

**Usage:**
This template is primarily used during the [[detailed_technical_bid_evaluation]] process for orders valued greater than CAD 200,000. The final Technical Bid Tabulation, compiled using this template, represents a comprehensive technical assessment from all involved disciplines.
