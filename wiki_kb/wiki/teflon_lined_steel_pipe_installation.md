# Teflon Lined Steel Pipe Installation

Teflon lined steel piping systems offer excellent chemical resistance for many plant streams but require specific installation procedures due to the material's properties.

**System Components and Gaskets:**
A Teflon lined steel piping system consists of individual sections flanged at each end and flanged fittings, all bolted together. The mating faces of each flanged joint consist of the same relatively soft Teflon material as the lining. Kvaerner Aker Kvaerner Chemetics has specified that gaskets **must** be installed between the Teflon-faced flanges. Special attention to the sequence and torque applied to the bolt tightening operation is required due to these soft facing surfaces.

**Flange Face Protection and Component Inspection:**
Flange face protectors **must** be left on Teflon lined pipe and fittings at all times, except for inspection. After inspection, flange face protectors **must** be reinstalled and removed just before assembly. Each Teflon lined component **must** be individually inspected to ensure the inside is clean, the flange face is flat and not scratched, gouged, or otherwise damaged, and no cracks appear in the lining, particularly at the radius between the flange face and the bore. Some surface imperfections on the face of the flange, as designated by Kvaerner Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' site representative, may be polished out by hand using fine abrasive cloth or paper. However, cracking at the radius or in any other location is cause for rejection. Any Teflon lined part or component with cracking **must not** be used. Each Pipefitter **should** visually inspect each component at the time of erection and reject damaged components for subsequent repair or replacement. Gaskets used for Teflon lined pipe are Teflon; each gasket **will** be examined before installation for surface imperfections, scratches, or gouges. Damaged gaskets **must not** be used.

**Welding Prohibition:**
Welding is **not permitted** on any lined pipe or fitting.

**Pre-assembly and Temporary Supports:**
Line assembly for Teflon lined pipe starts with in-place pre-assembly of the total line, including all components and branches. During pre-assembly, all bolts are inserted and tightened "finger tight" using no tools. Bolts **should** be coated with a suitable "anti-seize" lubricating compound. All specified pipe supports, hangers, and guides **must** be in place for pre-assembly. Temporary supports made of wire, wood, etc., are usually required for each horizontal pipe spool length during pre-assembly.

**Length Adjustment and Bellows:**
An additional gasket can be inserted between flanges to increase length in increments of 1/16 inch (2 mm) per gasket joint. Teflon spacers can be used where additional gasket length increase is insufficient. At any location, there **will** never be more than one spacer and/or two gaskets. Each Teflon bellows expansion joint has a very narrow range of acceptable dimensions in the "cold" position; the piping **must** be adjusted to allow the bellows to be installed within its small range.

**Bolting Tools and Sequence:**
Tools required for bolting Teflon lined pipe are a standard open-end wrench and a torque wrench with a socket. Adjustable "crescent" type wrenches **must not** be used. Bolting starts at a fixed anchor point, usually a flanged connection to equipment or a tank. Each joint is made progressively, torquing up to one half of the torque value recommended by the manufacturer. The bolting sequence is important due to the soft flange facing. After completing the first connection to one half of the recommended torque, the next flange is tightened, and work proceeds progressively down the line.

**Alignment Correction:**
If "bends" or slight misalignment is evident, nuts on the "tight" side **should** be released by one half turn, and nuts on the "loose" side tightened by one half turn, repeating until alignment is achieved. Spacers may be required to be added or removed to adjust dimensions. To correct for major angular misalignment, a standard 1/2 inch (13 mm) thick Teflon spacer can be machined on the face to create a wedge-sloped spacer (approximately 1/2 inch (13 mm) thick on the wide side and not less than 3/8 inch (9 mm) on the narrow side). The use of tapered spacers **must** be reviewed and approved by Kvaerner Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' site representative before installation.

**Final Alignment, Torquing, and Supports:**
After achieving satisfactory alignment, each joint **will** be re-torqued to the value recommended by the manufacturer. Re-torquing **must** follow the same sequence of flanges and the same balanced sequence of bolt tightening. Temporary supports **should** be removed after final alignment and torquing. Permanent supports **should** be adjusted where necessary to correctly guide and support the finished line.

**Leak Testing and Post-Hot Operation Checks:**
During leak testing with water, every connection **must** be inspected to ensure a tight joint. If a leak occurs, **do not** tighten bolts further on Teflon lined piping. Instead, bolts on the opposite side **should** be loosened by one half turn, and bolts on the leaking side tightened by the same amount. If leaking still persists, bolts **should** be removed, and flange faces inspected for damage. Scratches or dents not exceeding 20% of the liner face thickness can be repaired by hand polishing with fine abrasive cloth or paper. If repair measures fail to produce a leak-tight joint, one or more components **must** be replaced. For safety, **do not** tighten or loosen any nut or bolt while a Teflon lined piping system is under pressure or more than 30°F above ambient temperature. After the first 24 hours of "hot" operation, the system **should** be shut down. Alignment of expansion bellows **should** be rechecked and adjusted, and all bolts **should** be checked for proper torque.
