# Dry Tower Document Revision History

This page outlines specific modifications and additions made during Revision 1 of the Dry Tower documentation.

**Revision 1.0 Modifications**
*   Sketches were modified in Rev 1 to show more clearly the transition to stainless steel.
*   Sketches were made visible in Rev 1.
*   Instructions for modification were added in Rev 1.
*   The minimum distance above the mesh pad was added in Rev 1.
*   The default velocity for the acid inlet was changed to 4.0 ft/s for the header (not the pipe) in Rev 1.
*   The default atmospheric pressure was changed to 14.7 psia in Rev 1.
