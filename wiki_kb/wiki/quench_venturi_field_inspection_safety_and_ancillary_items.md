# Quench Venturi Field Inspection - Safety and Ancillary Item Requirements

This page outlines the mandatory requirements for safety features and other ancillary items for the Quench Venturi, as specified in the Field Inspection Checklist **4-FIC-XX21-R0**. These elements are crucial for ensuring personnel safety, accessibility, and overall plant operational integrity.

**Mandatory Safety and Ancillary Item Checks:**

*   **Platforms, Hand Rails, Walkways, Stairs & Ladders:** All platforms, hand rails, walkways, stairs, and ladders **must** be installed. These access structures are essential for safe operation, maintenance, and inspection of the Quench Venturi, particularly for elevated components or areas requiring regular access. Their installation **must** comply with relevant safety codes and ergonomic standards.
*   **Safety Gates:** Safety gates **must** be installed and functioning correctly at all access points to platforms or elevated areas. These gates are a critical safety feature to prevent accidental falls and control access to hazardous zones.
*   **Grounding:** Grounding **must** be completed. Proper grounding is vital for electrical safety, protecting personnel from electric shock and equipment from lightning strikes or static electricity buildup. It is noted that grounding may be required in multiple locations on the equipment and supporting structures. Refer to [[grounding_installation_details]] for comprehensive guidelines.
*   **Flange Guards:** Flange guards **must** be installed if applicable. Flange guards are protective covers placed over pipe flanges to contain potential leaks of hazardous fluids, preventing spray-outs that could injure personnel or damage surrounding equipment. Their necessity is determined by the fluid properties and operating conditions.

These safety and ancillary items are fundamental to creating a safe working environment around the Quench Venturi and ensuring that all operational and maintenance tasks can be performed securely. Any non-compliance **must** be addressed immediately.
