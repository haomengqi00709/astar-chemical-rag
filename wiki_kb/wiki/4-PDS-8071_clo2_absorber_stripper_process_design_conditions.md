# ClO2 Absorber-Stripper Process Design Conditions (4-PDS-8071)

This page outlines the process design conditions for the ClO2 Absorber-Stripper (Tag No. 8071U), covering both normal and maximum operating scenarios. The equipment is designed for selective absorption of ClO2 from Cl2 in chilled water, utilizing HCl addition and air stripping.

**General Process Data**
*   **Quantity Operating:** 2.0
*   **Quantity Installed Spare:** N/A
*   **ClO2 Absorption Efficiency:** 0.995
*   **Turn Down Required:** 0.33 (Note 11)

**Normal Case Process Design Conditions**
*   **Design Basis (Norm):** Nom.FS, Aspen run+ClO2 Vac.Gen. Design Book
*   **Process Design Conditions Note (Normal Case):** Note 8
*   **Fluid Descriptions and Locations:**
    *   **Gas Feed:** From Condenser, Location/Tray Position VTA
    *   **Liq Feed:** Absorber, Location/Tray Position VTA
    *   **Exit Gas:** Top of Abs
    *   **Chilled Water:** Note 1
    *   **Abs. Liq to Stripper:** Note 3
    *   **Stripper Gas to Abs:** Note 4
    *   **Stripper Air:** Note 2
    *   **Vac.Pump Drains:** VTA
    *   **Product Solution:** Note 5
*   **Tower Operating Temperatures:**
    *   **Maximum Operating Temperature:** 20.0 oC (Note 6)
    *   **Normal Operating Temperature:** 8-12 oC
    *   **Minimum Operating Temperature:** 6.0 oC
*   **Tower Operating Pressures:**
    *   **Maximum Operating Pressure:** Note 12 kPa(g)
    *   **Normal Operating Pressure:** -0.72 to -0.76 kPa(g)
    *   **Minimum Operating Pressure Unit:** kPa(g)
