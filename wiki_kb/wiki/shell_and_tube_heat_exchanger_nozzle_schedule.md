# Shell and Tube Heat Exchanger Nozzle Schedule

This page details the nozzle specifications for the Shell and Tube Heat Exchanger (Chemetics Equipment Number 10-10H, "Water Heater"). These nozzles are critical interfaces for process fluid entry and exit, utility connections, and essential safety and maintenance functions, ensuring the proper operation and integrity of the heat exchanger.

For material specifications of these nozzles, refer to [[shell_and_tube_heat_exchanger_material_selection]]. For process-related design conditions, see [[shell_and_tube_heat_exchanger_process_design_conditions]].
