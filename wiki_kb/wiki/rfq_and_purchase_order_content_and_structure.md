# RFQ and Purchase Order Content and Structure

This page details the specific content requirements for Requests for Quotation (RFQs) and Purchase Orders (POs) as part of the issuance procedure.

**Line Items and Text Sections**
Procurement **shall** create Line Items 1.x (Scope of Supply) and 2.x (Documentation) by copying relevant parts of the [[requisition_content_and_structure|Requisition]] into the [[0-dat-5001_purchasing_and_logistics_database|Purchasing Database]]. Similarly, Text Section 1 (Scope of Supply) and Text Section 2 (Documentation) **shall** be populated from the Requisition.

**Delivery Information (Line Items 3.x)**
Procurement **shall** insert Line Items 3.x for Delivery. These line items **must** state the appropriate type of [[packaging_marine_transport|packaging]] and the delivery point, as defined by INCOTERMS 2000. Requirements for packaging and shipping **shall** be as set out in the [[project_planning_0_prc_3001|project Logistics Plan (0-PRC-3001)]]. Procurement **shall** insert required references to packaging specifications.
For Requests for Quotation, Procurement **shall** state “TBA” for the delivery date. For Purchase Orders, Procurement **shall** insert the date agreed upon with the Vendor for delivery.

**Quality Assurance**
Procurement **shall** insert required references to [[vendor_quality_assurance|Vendor Quality Assurance]] specifications. References to the relevant [[quality_data_sheet_4_qds_4520|Quality Data Sheets (0-PRC-7005)]] **shall** also be inserted.

**Conditions for Purchase**
Procurement **shall** insert required references to general conditions for purchase. [[order_specific_conditions_for_purchase|Order-Specific Conditions (0-PRC-5004: Commercial Terms for Purchase)]] **shall** also be inserted.

**Referenced Documentation**
For Requests for Quotation, Procurement **shall** insert required references to [[instructions_to_bidders|0-SPC-5006: Instructions to Bidders]].
For Purchase Orders, Procurement **shall** insert required references to Vendor-issued documentation, including the latest revision of the quotation and any clarifications or explanations.

**Contact Information**
Details regarding contact information for RFQs and POs are provided in [[rfq_and_purchase_order_contact_information]].
