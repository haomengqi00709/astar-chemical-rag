# Emergency Contacts and First Aid

This page outlines the procedures for obtaining First Aid attention and reporting emergencies within the office, including critical contact information.

**During Office Hours**
To obtain First Aid Attention or to report a fire during regular office hours, **you must** contact the person answering at the reception desk.
*   The person answering at the reception desk **will** contact the First Aid Attendant on duty.
*   The person answering at the reception desk **will** call 911 if necessary.

**Outside Regular Office Hours**
To obtain First Aid Attention or to report a fire outside of regular office hours, contact information is available in [[0-PRC-8010_table_3]].

**Making an Emergency Call**
When making a call to emergency services, **you must** concisely and calmly state the problem. An example of a clear emergency call is:
"This is <your_name>, I have a <Medical / Fire / Bomb threat> emergency at 1818 Cornwall in Vancouver. I am located at the intersection of Cornwall Avenue and Burrard Street."

**Serious Illness or Injury**
If one of your co-workers loses consciousness, experiences anything that seems like heart, stroke, or other potentially serious illness or injury, **you must** use the Emergency Contact information (found on the first page of this manual) to get help.

**Emergency and Non-Emergency Contact Numbers**
A comprehensive list of emergency and non-emergency contact telephone numbers is identified in [[0-PRC-8010_table_4]].
