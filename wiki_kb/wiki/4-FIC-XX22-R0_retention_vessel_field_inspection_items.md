# Retention Vessel Field Inspection Items

This page details the mandatory inspection items for a retention vessel, as specified in document 4-FIC-XX22-R0. Adherence to these requirements is crucial for ensuring the proper installation, safety, and operational readiness of the vessel.

**Mandatory Inspection Items:**
*   Correct Nameplate **must** be installed.
*   Foundation/anchor bolts **must** be secure and tightened.
*   All platforms, hand rails, walkways, stairs & ladders **must** be installed.
*   Safety gates **must** be installed & functioning.
*   Vessel internals (dip pipes, stand pipes, baffles, vortex breakers etc) **must** be installed. For general requirements on internal components, see [[vessel_internals_installation_requirements]].
*   Correct spray nozzles **must** be installed in inlet & outlet.
*   All Non-Destructive Testing (NDT) inspections **must** be completed and records available. Refer to [[0-QDS-7411_non_destructive_examination_requirements]] for general NDE requirements.
*   Vessel **must** be clean and empty.
*   Final internal inspection **must** be completed with the client.
*   A closure certificate **must** be attached for the final internal inspection.
*   Manway covers **must** be installed & tightened. See [[manway_covers_installation_and_inspection]] for detailed procedures.
*   Accessories (gauges, sight glasses, sample ports, vents, drains etc) **must** be installed.
*   Isolation valves **must** be installed on drains.
*   External painting **must** be completed.
*   Grounding **must** be completed (possible multiple locations). Refer to [[grounding_installation_details]] for installation specifics.
*   Flange Guards **must** be installed (if applicable).

Any deviations or issues identified during these inspections **must** be documented, potentially leading to a [[non_conformance_report_0_ncr_7]].

For administrative details of this checklist, refer to [[4-FIC-XX22-R0_retention_vessel_field_inspection_checklist_overview]].
For sign-off requirements, see [[4-FIC-XX22-R0_retention_vessel_field_inspection_sign_off_requirements]].
