# 4-SPC-0003 Horizontal Centrifugal Magnetic Drive Pump Specification Overview

This specification, identified as Document ID 4-SPC-0003, outlines the general requirements for the design, fabrication, and supply of horizontal centrifugal magnetic drive pumps. Its primary purpose is to clearly communicate the main requirements to suppliers, facilitate the addition of special contract requirements, minimize the need for extensive data sheet editing, and allow data sheets to highlight special cases by exception.

General equipment requirements for these pumps are detailed in [[4-SPC-0001_general_equipment_requirements]].
