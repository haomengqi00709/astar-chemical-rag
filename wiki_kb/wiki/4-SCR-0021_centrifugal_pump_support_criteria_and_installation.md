# Centrifugal Pump Support Criteria and Installation (4-SCR-0021)

This page details the specific support criteria and installation requirements for the centrifugal pump, as outlined in document 4-SCR-0021. Adherence to these criteria is mandatory to ensure the structural integrity, stability, and safe operation of the pump assembly on its concrete and grout foundation.

**Load Application:**
*   All unfactored equipment loads, as detailed in [[4-SCR-0021_centrifugal_pump_unfactored_equipment_loads]], **shall** be applied at the designated Datum point. This ensures that structural calculations accurately reflect the intended load distribution and stress points on the foundation and anchoring system. Proper load application is critical for preventing localized stress concentrations and ensuring uniform load transfer.

**Anchor Bolt Installation Requirements:**
*   **Minimum "Bolt to Edge" Distance**: A minimum distance of 100 mm **must** be maintained between the center of any anchor bolt and the edge of the concrete foundation. This requirement is crucial for preventing concrete spalling or cracking under load, ensuring adequate concrete cover, and maintaining the structural capacity of the anchor system. This aligns with general [[4-SCR-0004_anchor_bolt_installation_requirements]].
*   **Anchor System Type**: An epoxy adhesive anchor system **must** be used for securing the centrifugal pump to the concrete foundation. This type of anchor system provides superior bond strength and resistance to dynamic loads and vibrations compared to mechanical anchors, making it suitable for rotating equipment like centrifugal pumps.
*   **Anchor Bolt Material**: The specific material for the anchor bolts **shall** be as indicated in the [[4-SCR-0021_centrifugal_pump_support_dimensions]] and on the referenced equipment drawing. The material selection is critical for ensuring adequate tensile strength, shear resistance, and corrosion resistance in the operating environment. For general guidelines on anchor bolt materials, refer to [[4-SCR-0007_anchor_bolt_and_nut_material]].
*   **Nut Material**: The specific material for the nuts used with the anchor bolts **shall** be as indicated in the [[4-SCR-0021_centrifugal_pump_support_dimensions]] and on the referenced equipment drawing. Compatibility between bolt and nut materials is essential to prevent galling and ensure proper torque retention.

**Basis for Support Criteria:**
The detailed basis for these support criteria, including specific design considerations and calculations, refers to **Equipment Drawing Number 4-VEN-XXXX, Revision X**. This drawing provides the graphical representation and additional notes necessary for the complete understanding and implementation of these requirements. It is imperative to consult this drawing in conjunction with this document.

These installation requirements are integral to the overall [[equipment_support_criteria]] and contribute to the safe and reliable operation of the centrifugal pump. For general concrete surface requirements, refer to [[4-SCR-0004_concrete_surface_requirements]].

*   Anchor Bolt Material

**Load Application:**

**Installation Requirements:**

*   An epoxy adhesive anchor system must be used for anchoring.
