# Mist Separator Mechanical Design Requirements

This page specifies the mechanical design temperature requirements for the Mist Separator, as outlined in Document ID **4-DST-XXXX-R0**, "Template Mist Separator Datasheet". These parameters are crucial for ensuring the structural integrity and safe operation of the equipment under various thermal conditions.

**Temperature Design Limits:**
*   **Element Design Maximum Temperature:** This is a critical mechanical design requirement, specified in degrees Celsius (°C). The mist separator elements **shall** be designed to withstand this maximum temperature without degradation of material properties or structural failure. This temperature accounts for potential upset conditions, process excursions, and normal operating maximums. It directly influences the selection of materials of construction and the design of internal supports.
*   **Element Design Minimum Temperature:** This is also a critical mechanical design requirement, specified in degrees Celsius (°C). The mist separator elements **shall** be designed to operate safely down to this minimum temperature. This is particularly important for processes that may experience low ambient temperatures, auto-refrigeration, or cold start-up conditions, which can lead to brittle fracture in certain materials.

These temperature limits are fundamental considerations in the [[general_mechanical_design_requirements_0_spc_0010]] for all process equipment. They are recorded in [[equipment_data_sheets_4_dst_xxxx]] to guide fabrication and material procurement. Similar design considerations apply to other pressure-containing equipment, such as those detailed in [[converter_vessel_design_conditions]], ensuring that all components are robust enough for their intended service temperatures.
