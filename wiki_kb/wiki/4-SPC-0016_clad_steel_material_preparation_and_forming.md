# Clad Steel Material Preparation and Forming

Proper preparation and forming of clad steel materials are essential steps in the fabrication process to ensure the quality and integrity of the final welded components.

**Cutting and Edge Preparation**
Material **must** be cut to size using methods such as machining, shearing, thermal processes (e.g., plasma arc), and grinding. All contaminated edges **must** be removed prior to rolling or working of the plate. This is crucial to prevent impurities from being incorporated into the material during subsequent processing.

**Forming Operations**
Material **must** be cold formed as far as practical. When hot forming is necessary, the procedure **must** be agreed to in writing by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics prior to the commencement of work. This ensures that any thermal effects on the clad material are properly managed and approved.

**Bevelling and Cladding Protection**
For detailed preparation guidelines, refer to Appendix 'B', which provides [[4-SPC-0016_appendix_b_clad_base_metal_detail]]. Where bevelling is performed on the clad side of the plate, the cut-back **must** be measured from the edge of the bevel.

Throughout normal handling, rolling, and welding operations, the cladding **must** be diligently protected from:
*   Weld spatter
*   Surface indentations
*   Scoring
*   Iron contamination

These protective measures are vital to preserve the corrosion resistance and surface finish of the stainless steel cladding.

**Cladding Protection**
Cladding **must** be protected from weld spatter, surface indentations, scoring, and iron contamination during normal handling, rolling, and welding operations.

**Bevelling**
Where bevelling is done on the clad side of the plate, the cut-back **must** be measured from the edge of the bevel.
