# United Nations Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods (CISG)

This page clarifies the non-application of the United Nations Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods (CISG) in specific project contracts.

**CISG Overview**
The United Nations Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods (CISG) is a multilateral treaty that establishes a uniform framework for international commerce. It governs contracts for the international sale of goods, aiming to reduce legal obstacles to international trade by providing substantive rules that apply directly to contracts between parties from different signatory countries.

**Non-Application in Purchase Orders**
For Purchase Order [[0-SPC-5001_purchase_order_overview|0-SPC-5001]], the United Nations Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods **does not** apply. This explicit exclusion means that the legal framework for international sales contracts will be governed by the specific terms and conditions of the Purchase Order itself, along with the designated [[applicable_law_and_compliance|Applicable Law]] (e.g., the law of British Columbia, Canada, as specified in 0-SPC-5001), rather than the provisions of the CISG. This ensures that the contractual relationship is solely defined by the agreed-upon Purchase Order terms and the chosen national law.
