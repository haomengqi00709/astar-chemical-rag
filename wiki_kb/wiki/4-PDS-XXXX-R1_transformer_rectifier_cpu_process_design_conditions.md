# Transformer Rectifier CPU Process Design Conditions

This page details the specified process design conditions for Transformer Rectifier (T/R) and Central Processing Unit (CPU) equipment, as outlined in Document ID 4-PDS-XXXX-R1. These parameters are crucial for the accurate sizing, selection, and operation of T/R and CPU systems in chemical processes.

**T/R Case Descriptions:**
The datasheet supports design for various chemical processes, including:
*   Chlorate
*   Chloralkali

**Process Design Basis Parameters:**
The following parameters define the operational and performance requirements for the T/R and associated electrolytic cells:

*   **Number of Cells (electrolyzers):** Specifies the total count of electrolytic cells in the circuit, particularly for NaClO3 production.
*   **Cell Production NaClO3:**
    *   tonnes/day
    *   %
*   **Cell Production NaOH:**
    *   tonnes/day
    *   %
*   **Cell Current Efficiency:**
    *   New cells: %
    *   Old cells (Note 1): %
*   **Calculated Cell Current:**
    *   Design (new cells): kA
    *   Clean design (Note 2): kA
    *   Dirty old cells (Note 1): kA
*   **K factor:**
    *   Clean
    *   Dirty
*   **Calculated Cell Voltage:**
    *   Dirty clean new cells: VDC
    *   Dirty old cells (Note 1): VDC
*   **Estimated Bus Losses:** VDC
*   **Allowance for Future Expansion:**
    *   VDC
    *   kA
*   **Design Circuit Voltage:** VDC
*   **Design Circuit Voltage - cells bypassed:** VDC
*   **Design Circuit Current (Note 2):** kA
*   **Minimum Operating Current:** kA
*   **Number of DC Taps:** Specifies the number of direct current taps available for voltage adjustment.
*   **DC Taps Voltages:** VDC
*   **T/R Efficiency at Start-up (Note 3):** %
*   **Method of Eff'y Measurement:** Describes the technique used to determine efficiency.
*   **Type of Current Measurement:** Specifies the method employed for current measurement.

For further context on related process design principles, refer to [[process_design_criteria_1_prc_0001]] and specific equipment datasheets such as [[bipolar_electrolyzer_process_design_conditions]] or [[chlorate_reactor_process_design_and_composition]].

**Additional Design Basis Parameters:**

The following parameters further define the design basis for the T/R and CPU systems:

*   **Cell Current Efficiency old Note 1:** Specified in kA.
*   **K factor:** Specified in VDC.
*   **Design Circuit Voltage - cells bypassed:** Specified in kA.
*   **Number of DC Taps:** Specified in VDC.
*   **DC Taps Voltages:** Specified in %.
*   **Number of Cells (electrolyzers):** Specifically for NaClO3 production.
