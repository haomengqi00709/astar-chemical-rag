# AKCTC Safety Manual Appendix O - Table 1: Determination of Materials and Agencies Requiring Notification

This page describes the content and purpose of Table 1, titled “Determination of Materials and Agencies Requiring Notification”, which is located in Appendix O of the AKCTC Safety Manual.

**Purpose:**
This table serves as a critical reference for AKCTC personnel to:
*   Determine if a chemical spill or gas release is reportable according to regulatory requirements.
*   Identify which specific agency or agencies **should** be notified in the event of a spill or gas release.

**Application:**
The information within Table 1 is directly referenced in the [[chemical_spill_response_akctc]] and [[gas_release_response_akctc]] procedures to ensure compliance with the "Spill Reporting Regulation" (B.C. Reg. 166/93) from the B.C. Waste Management Act.

Personnel involved in managing or reporting environmental releases **shall** consult this table to ensure all necessary notifications are made to the appropriate authorities.
