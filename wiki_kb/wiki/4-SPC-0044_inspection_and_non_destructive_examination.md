# Glass-Lined Vessel Inspection and NDE Requirements (4-SPC-0044)

This section outlines the mandatory inspection and non-destructive examination (NDE) requirements for glass-lined carbon steel reaction vessels, covering radiography, penetrant testing, visual examination, and spark testing, as specified in 4-SPC-0044.

**Radiography (RT)**
Vessels (excluding jackets) fabricated to this Standard **shall** as a minimum be spot radiographed to [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels|Paragraph UW-52 of ASME Code, Division 1, Section VIII]]. All radiographs taken during fabrication **shall** be available for examination by the Inspector or authorized representative. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics reserves the option to review final radiographs prior to glassing of the vessel, ensuring normal shop production activities are not affected. If considered necessary, spot radiography or some form of alternate N.D.T. **shall** be performed on selected jacket butt welds. Jacket closing seams **shall** also be spot radiographed where access is possible.

**Penetrant Testing (PT)**
Welds on internal components fabricated from corrosion resistant metallic materials **shall** be 100% P.T. inspected to [[ASME_section_v_non_destructive_examination|ASME Code, Section V]]. Welding repairs required after radiography or P.T. inspection **shall** be re-inspected in accordance with the requirements of this specification.

**Visual Examination**
After completion of the hydrostatic test, the glass **shall** be visually examined for defects by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. The lining **shall** show a smooth uniform surface and be free from hairlines caused by excessive local thermal stresses during firing. Lines caused by fully or partially forced-up ground coat or by cracked ground coat and in consequence forced-down cover coat **shall not** be present. The detection of hairlines is facilitated by a light held obliquely to the glass surface. The lining **shall** also be free from haircracks, chipping or flaking, and contamination by scale and fire brick.

**Fire Brick Particle Limits:**
*   For vessels up to and including 680 gallons (2500 litres), a maximum of three separate fire brick particles are permitted.
*   For vessels of 1100 gallons (4100 litres) and over, a maximum of five separate fire brick particles are permitted.
*   Permitted fire brick particles **must** have a length, parallel to the glass surface, not greater than .125" (3mm).
*   Particles having a maximum length of .125" (3mm) **must** be isolated by a distance of 24" (600mm).
*   Particles of .062" (1.5mm) maximum length **must** be isolated by a distance of 12" (300mm).
*   Particles smaller than .062" (1.5mm) **shall** be permitted in greater number but **must** satisfy the 12" (300mm) spacing requirement.

The result of the Visual Examination **shall** be entered on the Glass-lining Inspection Record (Appendix A).

**Spark Testing (Holiday Testing)**
Upon completion of the hydrotest and running test, the vessel lining **shall** be Holiday-tested prior to shipment to site. On-site spark testing by the Supplier is required. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **shall** be advised of the timing of high voltage tests.

Lining, other than where ground, **shall** be tested for continuity and **shall** show no metal contact when the test probe is moved twice over the glass surface. The test voltage for lining (other than ground) is 17 kV dc peak. For ground surfaces, the test voltage **shall** be reduced to 10 kV dc. Auxiliary components (e.g., agitators, baffles) **shall** be tested at 15 kV. A defect is indicated by a spark or a visual/acoustic signal during high voltage testing.

Site inspection **shall** normally comprise a thorough visual examination by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, supplemented by a spark test performed by the Supplier. The spark test at the jobsite **shall** be at a voltage of 5 kV A.C. maximum. Any proposed deviations to the jobsite spark test **shall** be reviewed and approved by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. The result of the jobsite spark test **shall** be recorded on the Receiving Report. If the jobsite spark test is not carried out, the Receiving Report **shall** be endorsed to this effect.

**Statiflux Test**
The Statiflux test, using dry magnesium carbonate powder, is used to show the existence of hairline cracking. This test **should** be used if there is reason to believe the lining may have suffered mechanical damage.

**Thickness Measurement**
The thickness of the lining **shall** be measured using a non-destructive technique (e.g., a magnetic thickness gauge). Thickness measurement **shall** be at regular intervals appropriate to the equipment under examination. The glass-lining applicator's procedure for performing thickness measurement **shall** be reviewed by the purchaser. The thickness range measured **shall** be recorded, and locations of any areas outside the limits **shall** be specified on the Glass-lining Inspection Record (Appendix A).

**Radiography (RT)**
*   All radiographs taken during fabrication shall be available for examination by the Inspector or authorized representative.
*   If considered necessary, spot radiography or some form of alternate N.D.T. shall be performed on selected jacket butt welds.
*   Jacket closing seams shall also be spot radiographed where access is possible.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' review of final radiographs will be performed in such a way that normal shop production activities will not be affected.

**Penetrant Testing (PT)**
*   Welds on internal components fabricated from corrosion resistant metallic materials shall be 100% P.T. inspected to ASME Code, Section V.

**General Inspection**
*   Welding repairs required after radiography or P.T. inspection shall be re-inspected in accordance with the requirements of this specification.

**Spark Testing**
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics shall be advised of the timing of high voltage tests.

**Visual Examination**
*   After completion of the hydrostatic test, the glass shall be visually examined for defects by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Thickness Measurement**
*   The glass-lining applicators procedure for performing thickness measurement shall be reviewed by the purchaser.
