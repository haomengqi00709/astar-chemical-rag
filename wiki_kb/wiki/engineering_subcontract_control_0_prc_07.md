# Engineering Subcontract Control (0-PRC-07)

This document, 0-PRC-07, describes the roles and responsibilities of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics personnel and their interaction with the Subcontractor in the context of engineering subcontracts. It establishes the framework for managing and overseeing subcontracted engineering activities.

This procedure is essential for ensuring effective communication, coordination, and control over engineering subcontractors, thereby contributing to the overall quality and timely delivery of the Project.
