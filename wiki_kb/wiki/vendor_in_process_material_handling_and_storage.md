# Vendor In-Process Material Handling and Storage

To prevent damage, loss, or deterioration of materials, components, and the goods during fabrication, Specification [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment|0-SPC-7001]] requires the Vendor to implement specific procedures for handling and storage.

**Prevention of Damage and Deterioration**
The Vendor **shall** have documented procedures for the packaging, handling, and storage of materials, items, and the goods while fabrication is in progress. These procedures **must** be designed to prevent:
*   **Damage:** Physical harm to materials or components.
*   **Loss:** Misplacement or disappearance of items.
*   **Deterioration:** Degradation of material properties due to environmental factors (e.g., corrosion, contamination, exposure to unsuitable conditions).

Effective in-process handling and storage are crucial for maintaining the quality and integrity of the goods throughout the manufacturing cycle, ensuring that only conforming items proceed to subsequent stages.
