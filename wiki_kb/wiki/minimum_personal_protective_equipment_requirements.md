# Minimum Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) Requirements for Vendors

This page details the mandatory minimum Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) requirements for all [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_name_change_history|Vendor]] employees working at Aker Kvaerner Chemetics worksites. Adherence to these standards is crucial for maintaining a safe working environment and is a fundamental aspect of [[0-SPC-8104_vendor_hse_standards_overview|Vendor Health and Safety Standards]].

Vendor employees visiting Aker Kvaerner Chemetics worksites **are required** to wear the following minimum PPE:

*   **Clothing:** Long sleeves and long pants are the minimum acceptable attire, providing basic protection for arms and legs against minor abrasions and environmental elements.
*   **Safety Work Boots:** These are essential for foot protection. Safety work boots **must** meet either the CSA Z195 Standard or the ANSI Z41 standard. CSA approval is visually indicated by a green patch, which can be in the shape of either a rectangle or a triangle.
*   **Hard Hat:** A hard hat is mandatory for head protection against falling objects or impacts. It **must** meet either the CAN/CSA Standard Z94.1-92 and associated standards or the ANSI Z89.1 standard.
*   **Protective Eyewear:** Eye protection is critical. This includes glasses with side shields, or goggles. All protective eyewear **must** meet either the CSA Z94.3 Standard or the ANSI Z87.1 standard.

PPE equipment of equal or superior quality to the specified standards will be considered acceptable.

Aker Kvaerner Chemetics will provide a hardhat and eyewear to meet these minimum requirements for Vendor employees. Additionally, Aker Kvaerner Chemetics will provide any other PPE required to meet the general minimum site requirements. However, specialized PPE that is specifically required by the Vendor for their employees to wear during the performance of the contracted work remains the sole responsibility of the Vendor to supply.

Vendor employees **are responsible** for arriving at the worksite already wearing the appropriate clothing and footwear as per these standards. The Vendor **shall** ensure that their employees are fully aware of these minimum [[personal_protective_equipment_policy|personal protection equipment requirements]].
