# Purchase Order Signing Authority Levels

This page defines the financial thresholds and corresponding personnel authorized to sign Purchase Orders (POs). These levels ensure proper [[authorization_roles|delegation of authority]] for procurement activities.

**Signing Authority Tiers:**
*   **Procurement (Buyer):** Authorized to sign Purchase Orders with a value less than CAD 100,000.
*   **Manager of Procurement:** Authorized to sign Purchase Orders with a value less than CAD 500,000.
*   **Director of Projects:** Authorized to sign Purchase Orders with a value less than CAD 2,000,000.
*   **President:** Authorized to sign Purchase Orders with a value greater than CAD 2,000,000.

All Purchase Orders **must** be signed by personnel with the appropriate authorization level.
