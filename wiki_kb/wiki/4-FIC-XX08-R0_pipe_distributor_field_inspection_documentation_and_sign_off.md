# Pipe Distributor Field Inspection Checklist Documentation and Sign-off (4-FIC-XX08-R0)

This page outlines the essential documentation and sign-off procedures for the Pipe Distributor Field Inspection Checklist, Document ID 4-FIC-XX08-R0. Adherence to these procedures is critical for maintaining robust quality assurance records, tracking compliance, and ensuring all necessary approvals are formally captured.

**Recording Deficiencies and Comments:**
The checklist incorporates a dedicated section for recording any deficiencies or non-conformances identified during the field inspection, along with relevant comments. This section provides 10 distinct slots, allowing for detailed entries regarding observations, corrective actions, or further remarks. It is a mandatory requirement that all identified issues and pertinent comments **must** be documented clearly and concisely to facilitate follow-up and resolution.

**Mandatory Signatures and Dates:**
Formal sign-off is a crucial step to validate the completion and thorough review of the field inspection. Signatures **shall** be obtained from representatives of the following key parties:
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics
*   Sub Contractor
*   Client

For each required signatory, their full name **must** be legibly provided, and the exact date of signature **shall** also be recorded. This comprehensive sign-off process ensures accountability across all involved entities and establishes a clear, auditable timeline for the inspection and approval stages.

This documentation and sign-off process is an integral component of the overall [[4-FIC-XX08-R0_pipe_distributor_field_inspection_checklist_overview]]. For a comprehensive list of the specific items to be inspected, please refer to [[4-FIC-XX08-R0_pipe_distributor_field_inspection_checklist_items]].
