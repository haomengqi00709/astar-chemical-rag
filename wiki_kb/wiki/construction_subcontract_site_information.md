# Construction Subcontract Site Information

This page outlines the "Site Information" section of a Construction Subcontract Agreement, which is a critical component for Subcontractors to accurately prepare their bids. This section **must** provide detailed information about the site conditions that may impact the execution of the works.

### Key Site Information Requirements:
The Site Information section **must** list any and all relevant information about the site that is necessary for the Subcontractor to formulate a comprehensive and accurate bid. This includes, but is not limited to, the following critical details:
*   **Soil conditions:** Comprehensive data regarding the geological and geotechnical characteristics of the site.
*   **Contamination of soil:** Any known or potential soil contamination, including its nature and extent.
*   **Items to be demolished or moved:** A clear inventory and description of any existing structures, equipment, or materials that require demolition or relocation prior to or during the works.
*   **Existence of flood plains:** Information regarding the site's proximity to or location within designated flood plains, including any associated risks or requirements.

### Importance of Detailed Site Information:
It is particularly important that this section thoroughly describes conditions that cannot be readily determined from a visual inspection during a site visit. Providing such detailed information ensures that the Subcontractor has a complete understanding of the site's characteristics, potential challenges, and any specific requirements, thereby minimizing unforeseen issues during project execution. For general information on site conditions and environmental factors, refer to [[environmental_and_site_conditions]].

### Key Site Information Details
The "Site Information" section is crucial for Subcontractors to accurately prepare their bids, as it provides detailed information about site conditions that may impact the execution of the works. This information is particularly important for describing conditions that cannot be determined from a visual inspection during a site visit. Key details to be included are:

*   **Soil conditions:** Comprehensive data on the geological and geotechnical characteristics of the site.
*   **Contamination of soil:** Any known presence of contaminants in the soil.
*   **Items for demolition or movement:** A list of any existing structures or items that need to be demolished or relocated.
*   **Existence of flood plains:** Information regarding the site's susceptibility to flooding.
