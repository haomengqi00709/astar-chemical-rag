# Steam Trap Installation

Steam trap installation involves correctly integrating a steam trap into a steam system to discharge condensate and non-condensable gases without allowing live steam to escape. Proper installation is crucial for system efficiency and preventing water hammer. Key considerations include installing the trap with the correct flow direction, ensuring accessibility for maintenance, and verifying that the type and tag match design specifications as part of [[strainers_check_valves_steam_traps_inspection_5_prc_0007|mechanical checkout]].
