# Vendor Document Requirements (99H10600-4-VDR-4005)

The document 99H10600-4-VDR-4005 R1, titled "Vendor Document Requirements," specifies the comprehensive list of documents that a vendor **must** supply to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for Project 99H10600, particularly concerning the procurement of specific equipment or services (e.g., related to the 4000-series identification numbers). This document is critical for managing the flow of information from vendors and ensuring that all necessary engineering, operational, and maintenance documentation is received.

A Vendor Document Requirements (VDR) list typically includes:
*   **Document Types:** A detailed list of required documents, such as certified drawings (P&IDs, GA drawings, electrical schematics), data sheets, calculation reports, material certificates, inspection and test plans (ITPs), operating and maintenance manuals, spare parts lists, and as-built documentation.
*   **Submission Schedule:** The required dates or milestones for the submission of each document type, often linked to project progress or payment milestones.
*   **Format and Language:** Specifications for the format (e.g., CAD files, PDF, hard copies), language, and revision control of submitted documents.
*   **Review and Approval Process:** Details on how vendor documents will be reviewed, commented on, and approved by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
*   **Number of Copies:** Requirements for the number of electronic and/or hard copies for each document.

Vendors **must** supply documentation in strict accordance with the requirements outlined in this VDR. This document is a key reference in Text Section 2: Documentation of Purchase Orders, ensuring that the project receives all necessary information for design, construction, commissioning, and long-term operation.
