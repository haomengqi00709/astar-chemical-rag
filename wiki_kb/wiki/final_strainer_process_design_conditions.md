# Final Strainer Process Design Conditions

This page details the general process design conditions and operational parameters for the Final Strainer (Tag No. 7842K). These specifications are fundamental for the equipment's design, selection, and safe operation.

**General Specifications:**
*   **Service:** Final Strainer
*   **Quantity:** 2.0 units
*   **Operating Quantity:** 1.0 unit
*   **Installed Spare Quantity:** 1.0 unit

**Major Components:**
The primary component handled by the strainer is 98.14 %w/w H2SO4.

**Maximum Operating Conditions:**
The strainer is designed to operate under the following maximum conditions:
*   **Maximum Flowrate (Volume):** 353.9 m3/h
*   **Maximum Flowrate (Mass):** 633288.9 kg/h
*   **Maximum Temperature:** 70.0 °C
*   **Maximum Pressure:** 117.77004750000002 kPa(g)
*   **Maximum Density:** 1789.4 kg/m3
*   **Maximum Viscosity:** 7.27 cP

These conditions are consistent with the data presented in the [[4-PDS-7842-R1_final_strainer_process_data_sheet]] and are used as a basis for the [[final_strainer_sizing_and_performance]] calculations.
