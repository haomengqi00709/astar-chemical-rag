# Equipment Support Criteria

Equipment Support Criteria documents provide essential information for the design of foundations and supporting structures for various equipment items. These criteria ensure that equipment is safely and correctly integrated into the plant layout.

**Content Elements**
Support Criteria **must** contain the following information:

*   **Dimensioned Footprint:** A clear, dimensioned outline of the equipment's base, indicating a datum location for precise placement.
*   **Quantity and Size of Anchor Bolts:** Specifications for the number and dimensions of anchor bolts required to secure the equipment.
*   **Foundation Cut-outs:** Details of any necessary openings or recesses in the foundation for piping, conduits, or other interfaces.
*   **Support Loading:** The calculated loads exerted by the equipment on its supports, determined at the specified datum.

**Document Preparation and Approval**
The preparation, checking, and approval of Support Criteria follow a defined workflow:

*   **Preparation:** Support Criteria **must** be prepared by Mechanical/Design personnel.
*   **Checking:** The documents **must** be checked by Design personnel.
*   **Approval:** Final approval **must** be granted by Mechanical personnel.

**Responsibilities**
*   **Prepared by:** Mechanical/Design
*   **Checked by:** Design
*   **Approved by:** Mechanical
