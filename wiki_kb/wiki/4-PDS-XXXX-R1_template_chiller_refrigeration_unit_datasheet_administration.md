# Chiller/Refrigeration Unit Datasheet Administration (4-PDS-XXXX-R1)

This document, identified as 4-PDS-XXXX-R1, serves as the template datasheet for Chiller/Refrigeration Units. It is managed by Aker Kvaerner, specifically a division of Aker Kvaerner Canada Inc., for projects titled "[Template - Chiller/Refrigeration Unit]". The client document number for this standard is Standard-4-PDS-XXXX-R1.

The datasheet includes fields for equipment identification, such as [[plant_equipment_identification_4_prc_0001|Tag No.]], and specifies the quantity of units required, categorized as "Operating" and "Installed Spare". The associated entity for the Tag No. field is Chemetics.
