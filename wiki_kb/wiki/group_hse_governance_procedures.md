# Group HSE Governance Procedures

Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' HSE policies and procedures are designed to meet the Group HSE governance procedures approved by the Group President/CEO. These governance procedures include:

*   Pol 2.2 Health, Safety and Environment (HSE) Policy
*   Pol 2.2 PR1 HSE Reporting Procedure
*   Pol 2.2 NO1 Incident Follow-Up Norm
*   Pol 2.2 NO2 Travel Health, Safety and Security Norm
*   Pol 2.2 DI1 Contact List for Emergency Notification of Incident
*   Pol 2.2 DI4 HSE Policy Related Definitions
*   Enet Location of group HSE Documents
