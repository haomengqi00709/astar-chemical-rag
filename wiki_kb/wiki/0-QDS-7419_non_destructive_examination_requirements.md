# Acid Tower Field Erected Shop Component Non-Destructive Examination (0-QDS-7419)

Non-Destructive Examination (NDE) is a mandatory activity for the Acid Tower Field Erected Shop Component to ensure structural integrity without causing damage to the material.

**General NDE Standards:**
All Non-Destructive Testing **will be** in accordance with ASME VIII Div. 1 or an approved equivalent. Furthermore, Non Destructive Examination **will be** in accordance with the ASME Boiler & Pressure Vessel code or an approved equivalent.

**Specific NDE Requirements:**

*   **Radiographic Testing (RT):**
    *   **Type:** Spot radiography.
    *   **Documentation:** Radiographic testing **is required** as FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package).
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Witness.
    *   **Application:** Applies to all pressure envelope butt welds.

*   **Dye Penetrant Testing (PT):**
    *   **100% Dye Penetrant Testing:**
        *   **Documentation:** Dye Penetrant testing (100%) **is required** as FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package).
        *   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Witness.
        *   **Application:** Applies to root & cap passes on dissimilar metal welds.
    *   **0.25 Dye Penetrant Testing:**
        *   **Documentation:** Dye Penetrant testing (0.25) **is required** as FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package).
        *   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Witness.
        *   **Application:** Applies to Candle Plate butt welds and SS pressure envelope butt welds.

For a broader overview of NDE requirements, consult [[0-QDS-7_non_destructive_examination_requirements]].
