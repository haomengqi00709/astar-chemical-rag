# Personnel and Subcontracting (0-SPC-5002)

This page outlines the requirements for personnel and subcontracting arrangements under Purchase Order **0-SPC-5002**.

**Key Personnel:**
*   The *Vendor* **must** either employ each key person named in the Purchase Order or employ a replacement person accepted by the *Project Buyer*.
*   The *Vendor* **must** submit the name, relevant qualifications, and experience of a proposed replacement person to the *Project Buyer* for acceptance.
*   A reason for not accepting a replacement person is that their relevant qualifications and experience are not as good as those of the person to be replaced.
*   The *Project Buyer* may, after stating reasons, instruct the *Vendor* to remove an employee. The *Vendor* **must** ensure that, after one day, the removed employee has no further connection with *Providing the Goods and Services*.

**Cooperation with Others:**
*   The *Vendor* **must** cooperate with *Others* in obtaining and providing information they need in connection with the *Goods and Services*.

**Subcontracting:**
*   If the *Vendor* subcontracts work, they remain responsible for performing this Purchase Order as if they had not subcontracted.
*   This Purchase Order applies as if a *Supplier’s* employees and equipment were the *Vendor's*.
*   The *Vendor* **must** submit the name of each proposed *Supplier* to the *Project Buyer* for acceptance, unless the *Project Buyer* has instructed that no submission is required.
*   A reason for not accepting a *Supplier* is that their appointment will not allow the *Vendor* to *Provide the Goods and Services*.
*   The *Vendor* **must not** appoint a proposed *Supplier* until the *Project Buyer* has accepted them.
*   The *Vendor* **must** submit the proposed conditions of contract for each subcontract to the *Project Buyer* for acceptance, unless the *Project Buyer* has instructed that no submission is required.
*   The *Vendor* **must not** appoint a *Supplier* on the proposed subcontract conditions until the *Project Buyer* has accepted them.
*   Reasons for not accepting proposed subcontract conditions include:
    *   They will not allow the *Vendor* to *Provide the Goods and Services*.
    *   They do not include a statement that the parties to the subcontract **shall** act in a spirit of mutual trust and co-operation.
    *   They do not include requirements for subcontracts imposed by the *Owner*.
*   The *Vendor* **must** obtain approval of their design from *Others* where necessary or required by the [[applicable_law_and_compliance|Applicable Law]].
