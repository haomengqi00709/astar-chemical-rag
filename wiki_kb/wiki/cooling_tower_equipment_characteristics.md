# Cooling Tower Equipment Characteristics

The Equipment Data section of the Template Cooling Tower Datasheet (Document ID: [[4-pds-xxxx-r1_template_cooling_tower_datasheet_administration|4-PDS-XXXX-R1]]) specifies the fundamental design and operational characteristics of the cooling tower and its fan system. These characteristics define the physical configuration and performance capabilities of the unit, influencing its efficiency, footprint, and maintenance requirements.

**Cooling Tower Type:**
*   **Type:** Can be either **Crossflow** or **Counterflow**.
    *   **Crossflow:** Air flows horizontally through the fill while water flows vertically downwards. This design often allows for lower static pressure drop and easier maintenance of the fill.
    *   **Counterflow:** Air flows vertically upwards, counter to the downward flow of water. This design typically offers more efficient heat transfer and a smaller footprint for a given capacity.

**Fill Type:**
*   **Fill Type:** Can be **Film** or **Splash**.
    *   **Film Fill:** Water spreads into a thin film over large surfaces, maximizing the surface area for heat transfer. It is generally more efficient for clean water applications.
    *   **Splash Fill:** Water falls over successive layers of splash bars, breaking into droplets and creating turbulence to enhance heat transfer. It is more robust for water with higher suspended solids.

**Fan Characteristics:**
The fan system is critical for moving air through the tower to facilitate evaporative cooling.
*   **Fan Characteristics:** Can be **Induced Draft** or **Forced Draft**.
    *   **Induced Draft:** The fan is located at the top of the tower, drawing air upwards through the fill. This creates a more uniform airflow and minimizes recirculation of hot, moist air.
    *   **Forced Draft:** The fan is located at the base of the tower, pushing air upwards through the fill. This can be advantageous for maintenance access to the fan, but may be more prone to air recirculation.
*   **Fan Type:** Can be **Axial** or **Centrifugal**.
    *   **Axial Fan:** Propeller-type fan, commonly used in induced draft towers due to its high airflow capacity at low static pressure.
    *   **Centrifugal Fan:** Blower-type fan, often used in forced draft towers, capable of operating against higher static pressures.
*   **Fan Speed:** Can be **One Speed**, **Two Speed**, or **Reverse**.
    *   **One Speed:** Operates at a constant speed.
    *   **Two Speed:** Allows for operation at two distinct speeds, offering flexibility in cooling capacity and energy consumption.
    *   **Reverse:** Capability to reverse fan direction, often used for de-icing in cold climates.

**Fan Features:**
*   **Adjustable Pitch:** Allows for manual or automatic adjustment of fan blade angle to optimize airflow and power consumption.
*   **Integral Louvre:** Louvres integrated into the fan section to control airflow and prevent water splash-out.
*   **Vibration Switch:** A safety device that automatically shuts down the fan motor if excessive vibration is detected, preventing mechanical damage.

These detailed characteristics, in conjunction with the [[fan_datasheet_general_requirements|general fan requirements]], ensure the selection of a cooling tower that is optimized for the specific process and environmental conditions of the project.
