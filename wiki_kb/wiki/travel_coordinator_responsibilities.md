# Travel Coordinator Responsibilities for Business Travel Safety

The Travel Coordinator plays a crucial role in ensuring the health, safety, and security of employees undertaking business travel, particularly to high-risk destinations. Their responsibilities include:

*   **Risk Assessment Verification:** The Travel Coordinator **shall** ensure that a [[travel_risk_assessment_and_approval|risk assessment]] has been conducted when required for any business travel.
*   **Documentation and Compliance:** Risk assessment information **shall** be logged in the Travel Coordinator’s list of travellers. The Travel Coordinator **shall** also ensure that the “Aker Kvaerner Travel, Health and Security Norm” has been followed for all relevant trips.
*   **Traveller Tracking:** The Travel Coordinator **shall** maintain an up-to-date list of all employees and their locations while they are travelling on company business.
*   **Inter-Unit Coordination:** The Travel Coordinator **shall** coordinate with other business units or projects in areas of common concern regarding travel safety and security.
*   **International SOS Liaison:** For travellers to High risk and Extreme risk areas, the Travel Coordinator **shall** send the travellers' contact information and travel details to International SOS.
*   **Security Advisories:** The Travel Coordinator **shall** communicate the International SOS security advisory information to the employee prior to their departure.
*   **Resource Provision:** The Travel Coordinator **shall** inform the traveller about the travel safety resources available on the company Intranet, ensuring they have access to vital information.
*   **Evacuation Planning:** When an employee is travelling to a High or Extreme risk region, the Travel Coordinator **shall** verify the existence of an [[travel_emergency_assistance_and_evacuation|evacuation plan]] for that specific area and **shall** inform the employee of this plan.

These responsibilities are critical components of the overall [[0-prc-8102_travel_health_and_safety|Travel Health, Safety, and Security Overview]], ensuring that employees are well-informed and supported throughout their business journeys.

Risk assessment information will be logged in the Travel Coordinator’s list of travellers.
*   **Traveller Tracking & Coordination:** The Travel Coordinator **shall** maintain a list of all employees and their location while travelling, and coordinate with other business units or projects in areas of common concern.
*   **High/Extreme Risk Support:** For travellers to High or Extreme risk areas, the Travel Coordinator **shall** send the traveller's contact information and travel details to International SOS. They will also communicate the International SOS security advisory to the employee and inform the traveller about the travel safety resources available on the company Intranet.
