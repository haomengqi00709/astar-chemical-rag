# HazOp Study Recording and Deviation Analysis

During a Hazard and Operability (HazOp) Study session, all findings and analyses **must** be meticulously recorded to ensure a comprehensive and traceable review.

**Recording HazOp Study Results:**
*   HazOp study results **shall** be recorded on [[1-haz-0001_hazop_record_overview|1-HAZ-0001: HazOp Record]].
*   1-HAZ-0001 **must** be prepared as described in the [[hazop_study_session_preparation|HazOp Study Session Preparation]] section.

**Deviation Numbering and Identification:**
*   All guide word deviations studied **must** be numbered sequentially.
*   The part number **must** serve as the common element for deviation numbering (e.g., "10.3" refers to the third deviation studied for part 10).
*   Each non-trivial guide word deviation **must** be identified. A trivial deviation is defined as one where the deviation has no context, is impossible, or its consequence is trivial.

**Cause Identification and Recording:**
*   All causes for each non-trivial guide word deviation **must** be identified.
*   Causes that result in potential hazardous situations **must** be recorded.
*   If insufficient information is available to assess whether a guide word deviation is non-trivial, this lack of information **must** be recorded. In such cases, a question will be generated.
*   Deviations that will not result in hazardous situations and are deemed to have no significant consequence (NSC) **must** be recorded as such.
*   Safeguards included in the design or mitigating circumstances **shall** be identified but not recorded for each cause.

**Questions and Problem Assignment:**
*   Questions or problems associated with the cause of the deviation **shall** be written in a concise manner.
*   A discipline **must** be assigned to respond to these questions or problems.

For details on the structure and content of the HazOp Record, refer to [[hazop_record_structure_and_content]]. For general information on guide word deviations, see [[hazop_guideword_deviations]].

*   1-HAZ-0001 **must** be prepared as described in Section 5 of the procedure.
*   The part number **must** be the common element for deviation numbering (e.g., 10.3 refers to the third deviation studied for part 10).
*   A trivial deviation is one in which the deviation has no context, or is impossible, or the consequence is trivial.
*   Causes that result in potential hazardous situations are to be recorded.
*   If insufficient information is available to assess whether a guide word deviation is non-trivial, the lack of information is to be recorded.
*   A question will be generated if there is insufficient information to assess a guide word deviation.

*   Questions or problems associated with the cause of the deviation will be written in a concise manner.
*   A discipline **must** be assigned to respond to questions or problems.
