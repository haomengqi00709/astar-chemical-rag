# Electrical Equipment Testing and Inspection

This page details the procedures for shop inspections and witness testing of electrical equipment, ensuring quality and compliance before and during manufacturing, and prior to shipping.

**Shop Survey Prior to Purchase Order**
Shop inspections and witness testing **will** be done in accordance with Table 2. Refer to procedure [[electrical_bid_evaluation]] (0-PRC-5003) for the necessity of a shop survey prior to issuing a purchase order. A shop survey report **must** be completed, using template Vendor Shop Survey, 0-REP-XXXX. The shop survey report **must** be returned to the designated recipient. The engineering firm **will** identify vendors requiring a shop survey prior to issuing a purchase order. Shop surveys may be done by the engineering firm, the DDE, or an outside inspection agency.

**Shop Inspections During Manufacturing**
Equipment requiring shop visits during manufacturing **will** be identified on the Quality Data Sheets (QDS). Shop inspections during manufacturing are usually conducted at the time of design meetings in the factory. The vendor **will** report any non-conformances to the representative. A Non-conformance report **must** be completed using template 0-NCR-XXXX. The Non-conformance report **must** be submitted to the designated recipient.

**Witness Testing**
Electrical equipment requiring factory witness testing prior to shipping **will** be identified on the QDS sheet. Not all electrical equipment is witness tested by the engineering firm. However, the Transformer/Rectifier (T/R) **is always** witness tested. Witness testing of the T/R is preferably conducted with the transformer(s) connected to the rectifier(s) for a complete unit test. Past experience has enforced the need for a complete unit test for T/R. Refer to Inspection Release form, X-REL-XXXX, for shop release of equipment.
