# Electrical Wiring Details

Electrical wiring details provide specific instructions and component requirements for conductor and cable numbering, marking, bundling, and supporting within enclosures. These details ensure organized, safe, and maintainable wiring installations.

**Content of Wiring Details**
Wiring details **shall** show:
*   The conductor and cable numbering system.
*   Necessary wiring accessories such as spade lugs and closed loop lugs.
*   Conductor and cable marking materials.
*   The locations of identifiers.
*   Methods of bundling the conductors within an enclosure.
*   Methods of supporting the bundle within an enclosure.
*   The terminal block numbering system.
*   Terminal block materials.
