# Cost Tabulation (0-TAB-5) Order Placement Requirements

Before any order can be officially placed, the Cost Tabulation (0-TAB-5) process outlines specific mandatory requirements to ensure financial prudence and technical clarity. Adherence to these steps is critical for effective project procurement.

**Mandatory Requirements Prior to Order Placement:**
*   **Budget Over > 10% Memo:** If the project budget for an item is exceeded by more than 10%, a **Memo must be Issued by the Project Manager (PM)**. This memo, often prepared in accordance with [[project_manager_planning_responsibilities]], serves as a formal notification and justification for the budget variance, requiring appropriate approvals before proceeding.
*   **Clarification and Resolution of Items:** All outstanding items, queries, or discrepancies identified during the bid evaluation process **must be Clarified/Resolved Prior to Order Placement**. This ensures that the scope of work, technical specifications, and commercial terms are fully understood and agreed upon by all parties, minimizing potential disputes or changes post-order.

These requirements are designed to safeguard project finances and ensure that all contractual obligations are clear and unambiguous before committing to a vendor.
