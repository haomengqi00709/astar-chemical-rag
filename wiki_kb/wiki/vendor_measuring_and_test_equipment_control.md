# Vendor Measuring and Test Equipment Control

Accurate and reliable measuring and test equipment (M&TE) is essential for verifying the quality of goods. Specification [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment|0-SPC-7001]] mandates that the Vendor establish and maintain documented procedures for the control of M&TE.

**Control Procedures**
The Vendor **shall** have documented procedures that control the following aspects of measuring and test equipment:
*   **Calibration:** Procedures for the regular calibration of all M&TE to ensure their accuracy against recognized standards.
*   **Certification:** Procedures for the certification of M&TE, confirming their suitability for use and compliance with relevant standards.
*   **Release to Shop Floor:** Procedures governing the release of calibrated and certified M&TE to the shop floor for use in fabrication, inspection, and testing activities. This ensures that only equipment fit for purpose is utilized.

These procedures are critical to ensure that all measurements and tests performed are reliable and provide valid evidence of conformance to specifications.
