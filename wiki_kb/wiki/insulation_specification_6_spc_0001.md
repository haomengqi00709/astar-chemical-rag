# Insulation Specification (6-SPC-0001)

The **Insulation Specification**, assumed to be **6-SPC-0001** based on standard document naming conventions and references, details the technical requirements for insulation materials and their application. This specification is a key document for ensuring quality and consistency in insulation installation across the project.

The Insulation Specification **shall** contain specific requirements for:
*   Surface preparation.
*   Installation procedures.

The preparation of this document is the responsibility of the Mechanical discipline. It is then checked by the Project Engineer and approved by Mechanical.

**Modification and Collaboration:**
If specific statutory or Owner requirements necessitate deviations from the standard, the Mechanical discipline **shall** modify the standard insulation specification accordingly. During this modification process, Mechanical **shall** obtain essential input from the Materials Engineer and other relevant Discipline Leads to ensure all technical aspects are adequately addressed.

For any heat tracing requirements, the Electrical discipline **shall** assist with the development of the necessary technical specifications. This collaborative approach ensures that both insulation and heat tracing systems are integrated and compliant.

This specification works in conjunction with the [[6-prc-0001_insulation_design_criteria]] to provide a complete technical framework for insulation design and implementation, as outlined in the broader [[6-prc-0002_insulation_and_heat_tracing_documentation_procedure]]. It forms the basis for vendor requirements and on-site installation practices.

The Insulation Specification details critical aspects such as surface preparation requirements and installation requirements for insulation.

This document is prepared by the Mechanical discipline, checked by the Project Engineer, and approved by Mechanical.

If applicable, Mechanical will be responsible for modifying the standard insulation specification to incorporate any statutory and Owner requirements. To achieve this, Mechanical will obtain necessary input from the Materials Engineer and other Discipline Leads as required.

Where heat tracing is required, the Electrical discipline will assist with the development of the technical specifications.
