# Component Protection for Shipment (0-SPC-5004)

Specific measures **must** be taken to protect individual components during shipment:

**Flange Faces:**
Flange faces **will** be covered with an adequate shipping gasket. In addition, they **will** be covered with either a 12mm wood cover or a 5mm steel cover. These covers **must** be secured with bolting to ensure they remain in place throughout transit.

**Pipe and Nozzle Openings:**
Pipe or nozzle openings that are prepared for field welding **will** have protective covers. These protective covers **must** be secured by adhesive tape.

**Other Openings:**
All other openings on the goods **will** be plugged, capped, or otherwise closed to prevent ingress of foreign material or damage.

**Internal Support:**
The Vendor **must** provide blocking, bracing, or other necessary support to prevent damage caused by shock loading or vibration during transportation.

These requirements are supplementary to the [[0-SPC-5004_general_packaging_requirements|General Packaging Requirements]]. This page is part of the [[0-SPC-5004_packaging_land_transportation|Packaging and Shipping for Land Transportation Overview]].
