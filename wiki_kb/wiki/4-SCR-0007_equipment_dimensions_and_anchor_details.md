# Skirt Supported Vessel - Equipment Dimensions and Anchor Details (4-SCR-0007)

This page provides critical dimensional and anchor bolt details for the skirt-supported vessel and its concrete pad, as specified in document [[4-SCR-0007_document_administration]]. These specifications are essential for accurate civil and structural design and installation.

**Key Dimensions and Details:**
*   **Underside of Equipment Elevation:** Specified in mm.
*   **Top of Rough Concrete Elevation:** Specified in mm.
*   **Nominal Floor or Grade Elevation:** Specified in mm.
*   **Anchor Bolt Circle Diameter:** Specified in mm.
*   **Anchor Bolt 'AB' Diameter:** Specified in mm.
*   **Number of Bolts:** Specified in ea.
*   **Dimension 'A' - Pad Size:** Specified in mm. This dimension refers to Note 2 within the source document for additional context or specific conditions.
*   **Angle 'E':** Specified in deg.
*   **Angle 'F':** Specified in deg.
*   **Dimension 'H' - Equip. Anchor Height:** Specified in mm.
*   **Dimension 'P' - Bolt Projection:** Specified in mm. This projection includes c/w Double Nut.
*   **Dimension 'Y' - Grout:** Specified in mm.

These dimensions are fundamental for the [[anchor_bolt_installation_requirements]] and [[equipment_grouting_requirements]] for the vessel. Refer to [[4-SCR-0007_support_criteria_basis_and_general_requirements]] for further installation guidelines.

**Key Dimensions and Details:**
*   Dimension 'A' - Pad Size: Refers to Note 2.
*   Dimension 'P' - Bolt Projection: Includes c/w Double Nut.
*   Minimum "Bolt to Edge" Distance: 150 mm
*   Grout Width: Must suit equipment base ring.
