# Gas/Gas Heat Exchanger Fabrication and Installation Requirements

This page details the fabrication and installation requirements for the Gas/Gas Heat Exchanger, as specified in document 4-DST-XXXX-R1.

**Finishing and Location:**
*   **Paint System and Colour:** Refer to [[7-SPC-XXXX]]
*   **Location:** Outdoors
*   **Equipment Base Elevation:** 10 m (above grade)
*   **Height of Building:** N/A m

**Quality Assurance and Testing:**
Quality requirements and nondestructive testing are stated on the associated [[quality_data_sheets_qds]]. Specific requirements for stainless steel heat exchangers can be found in:
*   [[gas_gas_heat_exchanger_ss_final_inspections_and_documentation]]
*   [[gas_gas_heat_exchanger_ss_material_and_welding_certifications]]
*   [[gas_gas_heat_exchanger_ss_non_destructive_examination]]
*   [[gas_gas_heat_exchanger_ss_pressure_and_leak_testing]]
*   [[gas_gas_heat_exchanger_ss_vendor_quality_assurance]]

**Insulation:**
*   **Insulation:** 125mm Mineral Wool (to be supplied and installed by others)

**Vendor Responsibilities:**
*   The Vendor **will submit** fabrication drawings to Chemetics for approval before proceeding with fabrication. This process is governed by the [[vendor_document_review_process]].
*   The Vendor **will supply** all bolts, nuts, and gaskets for openings with blind flanges, in accordance with [[vendor_obligations_and_warranties]].

**Design Considerations:**
*   The outer vessel envelope is designed for blower dead head pressure.

**Marking and Stenciling:**
*   Equipment name, equipment number, and nozzle mark letter **will be clearly stenciled** onto the vessel.
*   Stenciled letters **must be 75 mm high and black**.
