# Commissioning Planning and Management

Aker Kvaerner Chemetics holds primary responsibility for the execution of commissioning and acceptance testing, as well as for the quality of operations from plant turnover through to the completion of Acceptance Testing.

**Commissioning Planning**
Initial planning for commissioning focuses on ensuring sufficient duration is allocated for each stage of the commissioning process as described in this procedure. Detailed planning for commissioning is conducted closer to the actual commissioning period.

Key deliverables from the detailed planning process include:
*   [[0-dat-3001_project_schedule]]: Project Schedule (0-DAT-3001). Data available in SQL: 0-DAT-3001
*   [[operating_manual_1_man_0001]]: Operating Manual (1-MAN-0001).
*   [[analytical_manual_1_man_0002]]: Analytical Manual (1-MAN-0002).
*   [[training_manual_1_man_0003]]: Training Manual (1-MAN-0003).
*   [[water_and_air_trials_1_prc_0006]]: Water and Air Trials (1-PRC-0006).
*   [[performance_testing_1_prc_0008]]: Performance Testing (1-PRC-0008).

All checklists and marked-up Piping and Instrument Diagrams (P&IDs) are to be utilized as guidelines and records throughout the commissioning phases.

**Commissioning Team and Leadership**
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics personnel **will** carry out the commissioning activities under the direct leadership and direction of the Commissioning Manager. The Commissioning Manager serves as the primary point of contact with the Owner on site during commissioning.

The Aker Kvaerner Chemetics commissioning team is responsible for directing the activities of both the construction Subcontractor’s personnel and the Owner’s operating personnel during commissioning. This direction **will** be channeled through the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Site Construction Manager and their established supervisory structures, ensuring clear lines of communication and authority.
