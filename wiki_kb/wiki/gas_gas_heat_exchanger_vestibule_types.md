# Gas/Gas Heat Exchanger Vestibule Types

Radial flow gas/gas heat exchangers utilize various top and bottom vestibule configurations, which are crucial for directing fluid flow and providing structural support. These vestibule types are often specific to certain [[gas_gas_heat_exchanger_shell_types|shell types]].

**Top Vestibule Types:**
*   **Type 1:**
    *   Features a radial flow tube side nozzle located on the side of the vestibule.
    *   Typically used on [[gas_gas_heat_exchanger_shell_types|Shell Types 2 or 3]].
*   **Type 2:**
    *   Features an axial flow tube side nozzle located on the vestibule head.
    *   Typically used on [[gas_gas_heat_exchanger_shell_types|Shell Types 2 or 3]].
*   **Type 3:**
    *   Exclusively used on [[gas_gas_heat_exchanger_shell_types|Shell Type 5]].
    *   This vestibule type is an integral part of the converter core, indicating its use in internal heat exchanger arrangements.
*   **Type 4:**
    *   Features a radial flow tube side nozzle on the side of the vestibule and an axial flow shell side nozzle.
    *   Typically used on [[gas_gas_heat_exchanger_shell_types|Shell Types 1 or 4]].

**Bottom Vestibule Types:**
*   **Type 1:**
    *   Features a radial flow tube side nozzle on the side of the vestibule.
    *   Includes a central core designed for tubesheet support.
    *   Typically used on [[gas_gas_heat_exchanger_shell_types|Shell Types 2 or 4]].
*   **Type 2:**
    *   Features a radial flow tube side nozzle on the side of the vestibule.
    *   Typically used on [[gas_gas_heat_exchanger_shell_types|Shell Types 2 or 4]].
*   **Type 3:**
    *   Exclusively used on [[gas_gas_heat_exchanger_shell_types|Shell Type 5]].
    *   Similar to the top vestibule Type 3, this is an integral part of the converter core.
*   **Type 4:**
    *   Features a radial flow tube side nozzle on the side of the vestibule and a radial flow shell side nozzle on the side of the vestibule.
    *   Shell side flow passes through the vestibule via a central core.
    *   Typically used on [[gas_gas_heat_exchanger_shell_types|Shell Types 1 and 3]].
