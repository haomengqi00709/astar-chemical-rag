# Acid Tower Field Erected Shop Component Inspection Requirements (0-QDS-7419)

Comprehensive inspection activities are critical for the Acid Tower Field Erected Shop Component to verify compliance with design specifications and quality standards. Final Inspection is a required activity.

**Dimensional Inspection:**
*   **Documentation:** Dimensional inspection **is required** as FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package).
*   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Witness and Hold.
*   **Specific Requirement:** All shell course plate actual flat dimensions **must be recorded**.

**Weld Contour and Finish:**
*   **Vendor I & T Plan:** Contour & finish of welds **is required** in the Vendor Inspection & Test Plan.
*   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Witness, Spot, and Hold (0.25).

**Surfaces Free From Defects:**
*   **Vendor I & T Plan:** Surfaces free from defects **is required** in the Vendor Inspection & Test Plan.
*   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Witness, Hold (1.0), and Complete (1.0).

**Cleanliness:**
*   **Vendor I & T Plan:** Cleanliness **is required** in the Vendor Inspection & Test Plan.
*   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Witness.

**Identification Markings:**
*   **Vendor I & T Plan:** Identification markings **is required** in the Vendor Inspection & Test Plan.
*   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Witness.

For general inspection and documentation requirements, refer to documents such as [[0-QDS-7404-1_inspection_and_documentation_requirements]] and [[final_inspections]].
