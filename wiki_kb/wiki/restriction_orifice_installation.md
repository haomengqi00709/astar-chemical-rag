# Restriction Orifice Installation

Restriction orifice installation involves correctly integrating a restriction orifice plate or device into a piping system to control fluid flow rates by creating a pressure drop. Proper installation is essential for achieving the desired flow restriction and ensuring accurate operation. Key aspects include installing the orifice with the correct flow direction, verifying its material, type, and bore, and ensuring adequate upstream and downstream straight runs as part of [[restriction_orifice_inspection_5_prc_0007|mechanical checkout]].
