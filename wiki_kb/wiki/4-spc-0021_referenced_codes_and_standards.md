# Referenced Codes and Standards for Nickel/Copper Welding (4-SPC-0021)

The following codes and standards are referenced within document **4-SPC-0021** and **shall** be adhered to for nickel/copper (UNS N04400 / Monel 400) welded fabrications. The latest edition ruling at the date of contract **shall** apply.

**ASME Codes:**
*   [[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping|ASME B31.3]] - Chemical Plant & Petroleum Refinery Piping
*   [[ASME_bpvc_section_ii_materials|ASME Boiler & Pressure Vessel Code Section II – Parts B&C]] - Materials
*   [[ASME_sfa_5_11_nickel_and_nickel_alloy_electrodes|ASME SFA-5.11]] - Specification for Nickel and Nickel Alloy Covered Welding Electrodes
*   [[ASME_sfa_5_14_nickel_and_nickel_alloy_electrodes_and_rods|ASME SFA-5.14]] - Specification for Nickel and Nickel Alloy Bare Welding Rods and Electrodes
*   [[ASME_sfa_5_12_tungsten_arc_welding_electrodes|ASME SFA-5.12]] - Specification for Tungsten Arc-Welding Electrodes
*   [[ASME_section_v_non_destructive_examination|ASME Section V]] - Non-destructive Examination
*   [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels|ASME Section VIII Div. 1]] - Pressure Vessels
*   [[ASME_section_ix_welding_and_brazing_qualifications|ASME Section IX]] - Welding and Brazing Qualifications

**Other Industry Standards:**
*   [[AWS_a3_0_standard_welding_terms_and_definitions|American Welding Society A3.0]] - Standard Welding Terms & Definitions
*   [[API_620_low_pressure_storage_tanks|API 620]] - Recommended Rules for the Design and Construction of Large Welded Low Pressure Storage Tanks
*   [[API_650_welded_steel_tanks|API 650]] - Welded Steel Tanks for Oil Storage
