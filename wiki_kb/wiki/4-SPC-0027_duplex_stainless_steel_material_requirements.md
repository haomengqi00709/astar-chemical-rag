# Duplex Stainless Steel Material Requirements (UNS S32205)

The selection and verification of duplex stainless steel material, specifically UNS S32205 (Alloy 2205), are critical for ensuring the integrity and performance of fabricated equipment. Specification 4-SPC-0027 mandates several key requirements for all product forms of this material:

*   **Chemical Composition:** All product forms of Alloy 2205 base materials **must** possess a minimum chromium content of 22% and a minimum nitrogen content of 0.15%. These compositional limits are essential for achieving the desired duplex microstructure and corrosion resistance.
*   **Certification:** Only material certified as UNS S32205 is acceptable for use. This ensures that the material meets the specified industry standards for its grade.
*   **Intermetallic Phase Content:** All 2205 material, across all product forms, **must** be tested and found acceptable for intermetallic phase content. This can be achieved either by following the latest edition of [[ASTM_a923_detecting_intermetallic_phases_in_duplex_stainless_steels]] or by certification from the manufacturer that the material is sigma phase free. The test results or certification statements for intermetallic phase content **must** be clearly documented on the material’s mill test certificate.
*   **Mill Test Certificates:** All mill test certificates for the raw materials **must** be made available to the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics inspector for verification prior to fabrication.
*   **Heat Exchanger Tubing:** For welded 2205 heat exchanger tubing, a specific processing step is required: the weld bead **must** be cold worked before annealing. This process is crucial for achieving a fully recrystallized weld structure, which enhances the tubing's mechanical properties and corrosion resistance. The manufacturer’s test certificate for such tubing **must** explicitly show that cold working was performed before annealing, and these certificates **must** also be made available to the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics inspector.
*   **Impact Testing (Plates > 10mm):** 2205 plate materials with a thickness greater than 10mm **must** undergo Charpy V-Notch impact testing by the manufacturer. The lateral expansion results from these tests **must** clearly indicate good impact loading toughness and ductility, which are vital for applications involving dynamic loads or low temperatures. The Charpy V-Notch impact test results **must** be stated on the material's mill test report or another suitable report, and these reports **must** be made available to the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics inspector.
*   **Pressure Envelope Construction:** The process-exposed pressure envelope of the equipment **must** be constructed entirely of Alloy 2205 to ensure consistent corrosion resistance and mechanical properties throughout the critical sections.
*   **Poison Pads:** To prevent galvanic corrosion or other material incompatibilities, 2205 poison pads **must** be used to separate the pressure-containing shell from all carbon steel and other stainless steel welded attachments.

These requirements ensure that the duplex stainless steel used in fabrication meets the highest standards for metallurgical quality and performance. For general material specifications, refer to [[ASME_bpvc_section_ii_materials]]. The overall welding standard is detailed in [[4-SPC-0027_duplex_stainless_steel_welding_standard_overview]].
