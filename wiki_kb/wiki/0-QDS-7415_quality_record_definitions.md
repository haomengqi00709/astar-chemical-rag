# Quality Record Definitions (0-QDS-7415)

This Quality Data Sheet (0-QDS-7415) defines specific requirements for quality records using the following abbreviations:

*   **AP (As Prepared):** Indicates that the quality record is required "As Prepared" by the vendor. This means the document is generated and maintained by the vendor as part of their quality control process. An example of a record requiring AP status is the [[0-QDS-7415_packing_and_shipping_requirements|Packing List]].
*   **FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package):** Indicates that the quality record **must** be included in the final Quality Assurance Documentation Package submitted by the vendor. This signifies that the record is critical for demonstrating compliance and traceability. Examples of records requiring FP status include [[0-QDS-7415_material_verification_and_welding_certifications|Material Test Certificates]], [[0-QDS-7415_filament_winding_and_bond_testing|Bond Testing reports]], [[0-QDS-7415_non_destructive_testing_requirements|Spark Testing reports]], [[0-QDS-7415_coating_and_lining_inspection|External and Internal Coating/Lining Inspection reports]], and various [[0-QDS-7415_final_inspections|Final Inspection reports]].

These definitions ensure clarity in the documentation requirements for the Dual Laminate Tank project. For an overview of the document, refer to [[0-QDS-7415_document_details_and_revisions]].
