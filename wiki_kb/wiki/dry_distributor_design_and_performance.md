# Dry Distributor Design and Performance Parameters

This page outlines the design and performance parameters for the Dry Distributor, as detailed in the [[1-cal-7814-r0_pipe_distributor_calculation_document_details]] and [[4-pds-7814-r0_pipe_distributor_process_data_sheet]]. It covers mechanical specifications, fluid properties, and operational characteristics.

**General Design Information**
*   **Pipe Type Distributors Version:** 3.0
*   **Last update:** June, 2004
*   **Service:** Dry Distributor
*   **Distributor Type:** Single Entry End
*   **Distributor Material:** Saramet
*   **Construction Type:** WELDED
*   **Method of Support:** CORBEL (BRICK RING) / HANGERS
*   **Saramet thickness assumed:** 3 mm
*   **Each arm has:** 2 flanges
*   **Each flange thickness:** 0.19685 inches
*   **Any Feed arrangement to the header:** Excluded

**Dimensions and Physical Properties**
*   **Brick I.D.:** 324 inches (8229.6 mm)
*   **Header Diameter:** 36 inches (914.4 mm)
*   **Header Wall Thickness:** 3 mm
*   **Arm Diameter:** 8 inches (203.2 mm)
*   **Arm Wall Thickness:** 3 mm
*   **Distance From C.L. to Arm Flg.:** 20.0 inches
*   **Distance Arm to Brick:** 2 inches

**Fluid and Operating Conditions**
*   **Fluid Composition %H2SO4:** 98.12
*   **Temperature:** 75.3 °C (75.3 DEG C)
*   **Density:** 1784.1920832866235 KG/M³ (111.28408149599998 lb/ft³)
*   **Viscosity:** 6.50 cP
*   **Pressure:** 1601.0636619175916 mm H2O
*   **Tower Gauge Pressure:** 63.03400243770046 inches H2SO4

**Flow and Velocity Data**
*   **Design Flow:** 9059.781483599998 usgpm (2065.6301782607998 M³/HR)
*   **Calculated Flow:** 9066.482270051443 usgpm
*   **Design Velocity:** 6.5 ft/sec
*   **Calculated Velocity:** 6.459375 ft/sec (1.9812 M/S)
*   **Reynolds No. (Arm):** 9177.97937086056
*   **Header Reynolds No.:** 218408.60795048747
*   **Flow is turbulent in arms.**
*   **Flow is turbulent in header.**
*   **Header is running full.**

**Nozzle Configuration and Performance**
*   **Nozzle Type:** Drilled Orfices
*   **Nozzle Diameter:** 0.6692913385826772 inches (17.0 mm)
*   **Number of Arms per Side:** 16
*   **Number of Arm Nozzles:** 1280.0
*   **Number of Header Nozzles:** 0.0
*   **Total Nozzles:** 1280.0
*   **Nozzles/sq ft:** 2.235598231976833 (24.064566544422313 NOZ/M²)
*   **Flow/Nozzle (Arm):** 7.08318927347769 usgpm
*   **Flow/Nozzle (Head):** 9.255341667053491 usgpm
*   **Arm Nozzle Angle:** 30 degrees (30.0 DEGREES from Horizontal)
*   **Header Nozzle Angle:** 65.0 degrees (65.0 DEGREES from Horizontal)
    *   The angle 65 degrees for nozzles in the header is a weighted average of 4 nozzles at 60 degrees and 1 at 90 degrees.
    *   Typical nozzle arrangement on the header has 4 nozzles at 60 degrees between the arms and 1 at 90 degrees where the arms join the header.
*   **Nozzle P.D.:** 20.23491847222476 inches H2SO4
*   **Pressure Drop:** 513.9669291945089 mm H2SO4
*   **Static Pressure:** 55.57979169431168 inches H2SO4
*   **Header Pressure Ratio:** 0.07910544251168164 (inlet velocity head to nozzle P.D.)

**Weight and Volume Estimates**
*   **Weight of the header:** 1269.312 lbs.
*   **Total internal volume:** 292.433 cub ft.
*   **Acid holdup:** 32543.138 lbs. (14774.584563070373 KG or 8.280825260999999 M³)
*   **Estimated Weight Material:** 0.0 KG

**Mandatory Requirements and Design Considerations**
*   **Mandatory:** Actual nozzle diameter **must** be less than the maximum nozzle diameter given in the detailed layout.
*   **Mandatory:** The header pressure ratio **must** be <=0.10.
*   Arm lengths calculated are only approximate and **must** be confirmed by detailed layout.
*   Acid hold-up and material weights are approximate and **must** be confirmed during detailed design.
*   Minimum nozzle diameter: 0.782 inches.
