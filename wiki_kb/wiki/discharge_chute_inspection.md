# Discharge Chute Inspection

This page outlines the mandatory requirements for ensuring [[discharge_chute_inspection|discharge chutes]] for [[conveying_equipment_mechanical_checkout|conveying equipment]] are clean and free of debris. Discharge chutes **must** be clean and free of debris.
