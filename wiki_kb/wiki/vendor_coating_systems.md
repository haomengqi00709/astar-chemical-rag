# Vendor Coating Systems

For standard equipment and materials, such as Type A and B mechanical equipment and electrical equipment, the use of a Vendor’s standard coating system may be acceptable. However, strict conditions apply to ensure suitability and compliance with project requirements.

**Mandatory Requirements for Vendor Coatings:**
*   Vendor’s standard coating systems must be suitable for the intended service.
*   Vendor’s standard coating systems must be clearly specified by the Vendor in their documentation.
*   Vendor’s standard coating systems must be checked by the responsible Discipline Engineer to confirm their suitability.

In cases where there is doubt regarding the suitability of a Vendor’s standard coating system, the Discipline Engineer shall consult with the Materials Engineer for expert assessment.

It is also important to note that some materials may have specific coating requirements already integrated into their applicable specification (e.g., plating or galvanizing for bolting, electrical conduit). These requirements are defined as part of the material specification itself rather than a separate coating document.
