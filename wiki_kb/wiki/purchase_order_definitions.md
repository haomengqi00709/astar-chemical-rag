# Purchase Order Key Definitions

This section defines key terms and linguistic conventions used within the Purchase Order (PO) document, ensuring clarity and consistent interpretation between Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Vendor.

**General Terms:**
*   **Terms identified in the Purchase Order** are presented in *italics*.
*   **Defined terms** are distinguished by having Capital Initials.
*   **The Parties** refer specifically to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Vendor.
*   **Others** are defined as any individuals or organizations not explicitly identified as Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, the Project Buyer, the Lead Engineer, the Inspector, the Vendor, the Owner, or any employee or Supplier of the Vendor.
*   **A Supplier** is any person or entity with a contract with the Vendor to provide a portion of the Goods and Services.
*   **The Purchase Order Date** is the date on which the Purchase Order officially came into existence.
*   **The Starting Date** is explicitly stated on the Purchase Order.
*   **The Goods and Services** encompass all goods and services purchased from the Vendor.
*   **To Provide the Goods and Services** means to execute all necessary work, including incidental work, materials, services, and actions, to complete the Goods and Services in full accordance with the Purchase Order requirements.
*   **Goods and Services Information** specifies and describes the Goods and Services, including any constraints on how the Vendor Provides them. This information is found in documents referenced in the Purchase Order or in instructions issued under the Purchase Order.
*   **Project Information** describes the [[the_project]] and is contained within the documents referenced in the Purchase Order.
*   **The Project** refers to the facilities being constructed, designed, and/or procured by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for the Owner.
*   **The Owner** is the party with whom Aker Kvaerner Chemetics has contracted for the execution of the Project.
*   **The Applicable Law** includes any binding law enacted, issued, or promulgated by any Government, government agency, or government-established corporation with jurisdiction over the Project or the Goods and Services, as well as any common law or law derived from custom or usage.
*   **Delivery or Deliver** signifies the Vendor's act of delivering the specified parts of the Goods and Services as detailed in the Purchase Order.
*   **The Delivery Date** is the agreed-upon delivery date, subject to changes made in accordance with the Purchase Order.
*   **The Prices** represent the lump sum prices for each item listed in the Purchase Order, unless subsequently modified as per the Purchase Order's terms.
*   **The defined term "Not Used"** is explicitly stated as "Not Used" when referring to clauses that are not applicable.

**Linguistic Conventions:**
*   Any reference or clause pertaining to the Goods and Services, Delivery, and Delivery Date applies to either the whole or any part of the Goods and Services, unless explicitly stated as applying to the whole.
*   Words used in the singular also imply the plural, and vice versa, unless the context clearly indicates otherwise.
*   Words in the masculine gender also encompass the feminine and neuter genders, unless the context clearly indicates otherwise.
*   The term "Person" includes any legal entity, unless the context clearly indicates otherwise.
