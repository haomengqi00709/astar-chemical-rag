# Electrical System I/O List

The Electrical System I/O List details all control inputs and outputs between electrical equipment and the Distributed Control System (DCS) or other control equipment. This list is compiled by the electrical lead and is essential for control system integration.

**Content of the Electrical System I/O List**
The electrical system I/O list **shall** include:
*   All types of control inputs and outputs.
*   I/O from electrical equipment not normally shown on [[piping_and_instrumentation_drawings]].
*   Items such as circuit breaker positions, relay metering from incoming and feeder circuits on switchgear, and alarm and shutdown signals from emergency generators and UPS units.
*   The voltage and current rating of each signal.

**Types of Inputs and Outputs**
*   **Inputs to electrical equipment** include:
    *   Dry isolated contacts.
    *   Analogue signals, powered from the DCS.
    *   Analogue signals, powered from the equipment itself.
*   **Outputs from electrical equipment** include:
    *   Equipment powered output signals.
    *   DCS powered output signals.

Control cables (minimum 120 Vac) are required between electrical equipment and the DCS.
