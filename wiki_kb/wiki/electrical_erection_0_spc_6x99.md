# Electrical Erection (0-SPC-6X99)

This page describes the requirements for Electrical Erection as outlined in document 0-SPC-6X99. The Subcontractor **must** adhere to these specifications for all electrical installation and erection activities related to the construction subcontract. This includes, but is not limited to, power distribution, lighting, grounding, and motor wiring.

Refer to [[0-SUB-6_construction_subcontract_scope_of_works]] for the specific application of this specification within the overall project scope.
