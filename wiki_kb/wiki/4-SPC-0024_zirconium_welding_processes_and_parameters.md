# Zirconium Welding Processes and Parameters (4-SPC-0024)

This page details the mandatory welding processes and parameters for Zirconium (Grade 60702) fabrications as specified in document **4-SPC-0024**.

**Preferred Welding Process:**
*   The Tungsten Inert Gas (TIG) Welding Process is the preferred method of welding Zirconium.
*   Other welding processes **will** be evaluated upon submission of full details, including the procedure and exact location of proposed weldments.

**TIG Welding Specifics:**
*   High frequency (HF) voltage **must** be used to start the arc.
*   Weld metal thickness **must not** exceed 1/8" per pass.
*   Where necessary, a back-up gas **must** be used to prevent root pass oxidation and contamination.
*   Back-up gas is normally 100% argon with 99.9% purity.
*   The use of back-up gas is **mandatory** for root pass welds on piping or other items where access to the internal weld surface is limited or non-existent.

**Preheat and Drying:**
*   Preheat is not required.
*   When adverse weather conditions cause condensation or wetness, material **must** be dried prior to welding.
*   Drying **must** be achieved by heating to a temperature just sufficient to prevent condensation.

**Welding Environment and Surface Preparation:**
*   Suitable enclosures **must** be provided to keep welds and welders free from draughts, rain, or other inclement weather.
*   Welding **must not** be performed on wet surfaces.
*   Fusion faces and adjacent metal (internal and external) **must** be free from moisture, scale, paint, grease, or other foreign matter immediately prior to welding.

**Weld Position:**
*   As far as practical, all welds **must** be made in the downhand (flat) position.
*   Vertical welds **must** be made vertical up (uphand).

**Alignment and Fit-Up:**
*   Alignment of welding edges at butt joints **must** be in accordance with the relevant code.
*   Misalignment for plates up to and including 1/4" in thickness **will** be 1/16".
*   Misalignment for plates above 1/4" in thickness **will** be 25% of the plate thickness or 1/8", whichever is smaller.
*   Misalignment for pipe **will** be in accordance with [[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping|ASME B31.3]].
*   Prior to welding a slip-on flange, the sum of the gaps on any diameter **will not** exceed 3/16" (5mm).
*   Parts to be welded **will** be securely held in position by mechanical means or tack welding to maintain alignment and gap during welding.
*   Reinforcing rods and other shaped items **will** closely conform to their matching curvature.
*   Jig construction **will** allow adequate access for welding and cleaning.
*   Jig construction **will** be such that external loading is not applied to a joint during welding.

**Tack Welds:**
*   Tack welds may form part of the completed weld provided complete fusion is effected at the root and the welding procedure is the same as for the remainder of the root run.
*   Cracked and unsound tack welds **will** be cut out prior to welding.
*   Sound tack welds **must** be thoroughly fused into the root run of the main weld.
*   Tack welds **should** preferably be at least one inch long where thinning down ends is necessary.
*   The use of permanent back-up rings or strips or consumable joint inserts is not permitted unless specifically approved in writing by the QA and Materials Group.

**Arc Strikes and Tungsten Contamination:**
*   Every effort **will** be made to confine arc strikes to the surface on which weld metal is to be deposited.
*   If an arc strike occurs outside the weld metal deposition area, the spot **will** require to be ground, PT examined per Para. 10.3 (see [[4-SPC-0024_zirconium_welding_inspection_and_nde]]), and repaired if necessary.
*   Burn marks from grounding clamps **will** be treated as arc strikes.
*   If a tungsten electrode accidentally makes contact with the work, the section of weld and the tip of the electrode **will** be examined.
*   Where tungsten loss has occurred, the tungsten pick-up in the weld **will** be ground out, and the electrode **will** be reground to the correct profile before re-starting.

**Inter-Pass Cleaning and Peening:**
*   Each run of weld metal **will** be thoroughly cleaned, and all slag and foreign matter removed before the next run is deposited.
*   On re-starting, care **will** be taken to ensure proper fusion with the weld metal previously deposited.
*   Peening of weld runs or hammering of finished welds is not permitted, except for light peening to remove slag.
