# Subcontractor HSE Pre-qualification Requirements

To be considered eligible for Aker Kvaerner Chemetics construction subcontracts, prospective subcontractors **must** meet specific Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) pre-qualification criteria. These requirements ensure a high standard of safety and environmental performance across all projects.

Key pre-qualification requirements include:
*   **Corporate Commitment**: Subcontractors **must** possess a written Corporate Commitment to Health, Safety, and Environment.
*   **Documented HSE Program**: A comprehensive and documented HSE program **must** be in place. This program **must** meet all requirements set forth by government and construction safety organizations with jurisdiction in the project's locale.
*   **Substance Abuse Policy**: Subcontractors **must** have a company substance abuse policy. For more details, refer to [[substance_use_policy_scope_and_definitions]].
*   **Compliance**: Subcontractors **must** ensure strict compliance with all Aker Kvaerner Chemetics construction safety procedures and all applicable local, state, and federal regulations.
*   **Employee Requirements**: Subcontractors **must** agree that all personnel employed by them on Aker Kvaerner Chemetics work, including their own subcontractors, **will** meet the listed requirements. Furthermore, all subcontractor employees **must** communicate with a level of proficiency necessary to ensure safety.
*   **Subcontractor Survey Submission**: Subcontractors **must** submit a response to Aker Kvaerner form [[0-REP-8405-R0_subcontractor_survey]].
*   **Supporting Information**: Subcontractors **must** provide any requested supporting Health, Safety & Environment information. This information is used to verify the subcontractor’s ability to comply with all applicable HSE requirements.
