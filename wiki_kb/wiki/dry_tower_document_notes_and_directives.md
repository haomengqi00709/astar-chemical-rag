# Dry Tower Document Notes and Directives

This page compiles important notes, requirements, specifications, and directives found within the Dry Tower documentation.

**General Requirements**
*   User is to consult with Technology Manager prior to modifying parameters not highlighted in Blue.

**Specifications**
*   For "User provide properties?", a value of 1 means No and 2 means Yes.

**Directives**
*   The instruction "Do not Delete" is present on the Data Input sheet.

**References**
*   Stream data is to "see sketch".

**Design Criteria and Assumptions**
*   Suggested velocity is based on the self venting criteria.
*   Superficial volumetric flux must be < 0.3.
*   A safety factor of 10% must be added when only supplying a tower and there are guarantees on the pressure drop.
*   Max DP must be specified (Note 2).
*   Packing around, on top and 1 ft directly below the distributor is not included in the mass transfer/pressure drop calculations.
