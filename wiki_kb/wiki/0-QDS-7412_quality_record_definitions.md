# Quality Record Definitions for Centrifugal Pumps (0-QDS-7412)

This page defines the terminology used for quality record requirements within the Centrifugal Pump Quality Data Sheet [[0-QDS-7412_centrifugal_pump_quality_data_sheet_overview]]. These definitions specify how required documentation is to be provided and managed.

**Quality Record Requirements:**

*   **AP (As Prepared):**
    This designation indicates that the quality record is required "As Prepared". This means the document **must be provided** in its original, prepared format, typically by the entity responsible for its creation (e.g., the Vendor).

*   **FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package):**
    This designation indicates that the quality record is required to be "Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package". This means the document **must be compiled** as part of the comprehensive Quality Assurance Documentation Package submitted by the Vendor. This package serves as a complete record of all quality-related activities and certifications for the Centrifugal Pump.

These definitions are crucial for ensuring clarity in documentation requirements and facilitating efficient review and archiving of quality records.
