# Equipment Coating Schedule (7-SPC-0001 Table 1)

This page outlines the specific equipment that **must** be coated in accordance with the paint systems detailed in `7-SPC-0001 Protective Coating Specification`. The requirements for coating specific equipment are presented in [[7-SPC-0001_table_1_equipment_coating_schedule]].

The table specifies which paint system, as defined in [[7-SPC-0001_paint_systems_and_temperature_ratings]], is to be applied to each piece of equipment. Adherence to this schedule ensures that all components receive the appropriate level of corrosion protection for their intended service environment.
