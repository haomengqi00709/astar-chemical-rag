# Vendor Quality Reporting and Communication

Effective communication and timely reporting are crucial for managing the quality of goods supplied. Specification [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment|0-SPC-7001]] outlines the Vendor's responsibilities for reporting and communication.

**Immediate Reporting of Significant Events**
The Vendor **shall** immediately report to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics any event that has or could have a significant impact on quality, cost, or schedule. This ensures that potential issues are brought to attention promptly, allowing for timely intervention and mitigation.

**Status Reports**
The Vendor **shall** issue a status report to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics at the frequency stated in the reference documentation (e.g., Purchase Order or [[quality_data_sheets_qds|Quality Data Sheet]]). This status report **must** contain a summary of:
*   Revisions to the [[vendor_quality_plan_requirements|Quality Plan]] or its implementing procedures.
*   The status of preventive actions for [[vendor_non_conformance_management|non-conformances]].
*   Changes to the production schedule.

These reports provide Aker Kvaerner Chemetics with a clear overview of the Vendor's progress, quality performance, and any deviations or changes that may affect the project.
