# Converter Shop Vendor Quality Assurance Requirements (0-QDS-7401)

This Quality Data Sheet (0-QDS-7401) establishes the specific vendor quality assurance requirements for the Converter Shop project.

**Vendor Quality Plan:**
The Vendor **must** prepare a Quality Plan. This plan **shall** be in accordance with [[0-SPC-7002_vendor_quality_assurance_bulk_materials]].

**Compliance:**
The Vendor's Quality Assurance **must** comply with the requirements set forth in this Quality Data Sheet (0-QDS-7401). Additionally, the Vendor's Quality Assurance **must** comply with [[0-SPC-7002_vendor_quality_assurance_bulk_materials]].

**Chemetics Surveillance:**
Surveillance activities conducted by Chemetics for this project **shall** be to Level 2.0. The specific details and scope of this surveillance are as set out within this Quality Data Sheet. For general information on Aker Kvaerner Chemetics surveillance, refer to [[0-QDS-7_aker_kvaerner_surveillance]].

**Chemetics Surveillance:**
Surveillance by Chemetics for this project will be to Level 2.0.
