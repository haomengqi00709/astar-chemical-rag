# Hydraulic Calculation

Hydraulic calculations are essential engineering analyses performed to determine the flow characteristics, pressure drops, and sizing requirements for piping systems. These calculations are generic and also apply to [[pump_calculation_data_sources]]. They ensure that fluids are transported efficiently and safely, meeting process requirements.

**Content Requirements**
A Hydraulic Calculation document **shall** contain the following detailed information:
*   **Calculation Methods and Formulae:** The specific methodologies, equations, and engineering principles used to perform the hydraulic analysis.
*   **Acceptance Parameters or Criteria:** The defined limits or conditions that the calculation results **must** satisfy (e.g., maximum allowable pressure drop, minimum flow velocity).
*   **All Calculation Input Data:** A comprehensive record of all data used as input for the calculation, including fluid properties, pipe dimensions, equipment characteristics, and flow rates.
*   **The Executed Calculation:** The step-by-step execution of the calculation, demonstrating how the input data was processed using the defined methods.
*   **All Output Data:** The complete results generated by the calculation, such as pressure drops, velocities, and required pipe sizes.

**Roles and Responsibilities**
The preparation, checking, and approval of Hydraulic Calculations are assigned to specific disciplines:
*   **Preparation:** The calculation **shall** be prepared by the Mechanical discipline.
*   **Checking:** The calculation **shall** be checked by the Process discipline.
*   **Approval:** The calculation **shall** be approved by the Mechanical discipline.

This rigorous process ensures the accuracy and reliability of hydraulic designs, which are critical for the performance and safety of fluid handling systems.
