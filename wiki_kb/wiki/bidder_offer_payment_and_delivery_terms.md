# Bidder Offer Payment and Delivery Terms

Bidders must clearly define their proposed payment terms and commit to fixed delivery schedules, accounting for all necessary reviews and quality surveillance.

**Proposed Progress Payments:**
If a Bidder proposes payment terms other than full payment upon delivery, the following details **must** be provided:
*   A list of proposed [[milestone_events]] to which payments will be tied.
*   The value of each proposed payment.
*   The estimated duration in weeks after the award of the contract that each milestone will be achieved.
For more information on payment structures, refer to [[progress_payments]].

**Documentation Delivery:**
*   **Mandatory:** Bidders must list all specific documents required by the Request for Quotation, as detailed in the Goods and Services Information, Section 2. For general documentation requirements, see [[vendor_document_requirements]].
*   A Delivery Date **must** be stated for each required document, as specified in Section 9.4 of the RFQ.

**Goods and Services Delivery Dates:**
*   **Mandatory:** Bidders must offer fixed Delivery Dates for each of the Goods and Services.
*   Delivery Dates **must** be stated as weeks after the award of the contract.
*   **Mandatory:** Bidders must qualify their Delivery Dates by explicitly stating any forecast manufacturing shutdown or closure periods that may impact the schedule.
*   **Mandatory:** Bidders must allow for all required documentation reviews by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics in their offered fixed Delivery Date.
*   **Mandatory:** Bidders must also allow for all [[quality_surveillance]] activities by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or other designated parties when offering the fixed Delivery Date.

These requirements ensure that both payment schedules and delivery timelines are transparent, realistic, and account for all necessary project activities.
