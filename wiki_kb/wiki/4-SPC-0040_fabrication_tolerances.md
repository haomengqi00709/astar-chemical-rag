# 4-SPC-0040 Fabrication Tolerances

Specification 4-SPC-0040 establishes mandatory fabrication tolerances for metallic process vessels and components. All equipment **must** satisfy these tolerances, in addition to any requirements from the [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels|ASME Code]].

**Nozzle Tolerances:**
*   **Circumferential location of nozzles:** Within 3mm (1/8”) of the specified axis as measured at the face of the nozzle.
*   **Out of squareness of nozzle faces (measured at the inside of the bolt circle):**
    *   Within 1.5mm (1/16”) for connections DN200 (8”) and under.
    *   Within 2.5mm (3/32”) for connections over DN200 (8”).
*   **Outside diameter of nozzle necks rolled from plate:**
    *   ± 1.5mm (1/16”) for connections DN300 (12”) and under.
    *   ± 3mm (1/8”) for connections over DN300 (12”).

**Flange Tolerances (Shop Fabricated):**
*   **Bolt circle diameter:** ± 1.5mm (1/16”).
*   **Bolt hole circumferential spacing:** ± 1.5mm (1/16”).
*   **Eccentricity of bolt circle with respect to flange bore:** ± 1.5mm (1/16”).
*   **Flange minimum thickness:** + 3mm (1/8”) / -0.

**Slip-on and Lap Joint Flange Clearances:**
*   **Clearance between the inside diameter of the flange and the outside diameter of the cylinder:**
    *   Will **not** exceed 1.5mm (1/16”) for nominal diameter <= DN300 (12”).
    *   Will **not** exceed 3mm (1/8”) for nominal diameter <= DN1200 (48”).
    *   Will **not** exceed 5mm (3/16”) for nominal diameter > DN1200 (48”).

**Lap Ring Tolerances:**
*   **Outside diameter of lap ring:**
    *   ± 1.5mm (1/16”) for connections DN600 (24”) and under.
    *   ± 3mm (1/8”) for connections over DN600 (24”).
*   **Lap ring minimum thickness:** + 1.5mm (1/16”) / -0.

**Skirt and Support Tolerances:**
*   **Datum line to first insulation/vacuum ring:** ± 5mm (3/16”).
*   **Distance between any two adjacent insulation/vacuum rings:** ± 12mm (1/2”).
*   **Radial clearance between outside of head and inside of skirt:** Will **not** exceed 1.5mm (1/16”).
*   **Base of skirt/legs/support lugs (true and square with respect to reference lines):**
    *   Within ± 2.5mm (3/32”) for nominal vessel diameter <= 2150mm (7’-0”).
    *   Within ± 3mm (1/8”) for nominal vessel diameter <= 3050mm (10’-0”).
    *   Within ± 5mm (3/16”) for nominal vessel diameter > 3050mm (10'-0").

**Anchor Bolt Circle Tolerance:**
*   **Anchor bolt circle diameter:** ± 3mm (1/8").
