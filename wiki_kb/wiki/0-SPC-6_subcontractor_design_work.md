# Subcontractor Design Responsibilities under Specification 0-SPC-6

Specification 0-SPC-6 delineates specific design responsibilities assigned to the Subcontractor, ensuring that all design-related activities contribute to the overall project quality and compliance. This page outlines the types of design work expected from the Subcontractor and the mandatory standards for execution.

**Design Work Categories**
The Subcontractor is responsible for carrying out specific design work, which includes:
*   Structural steel shop drawings.
*   Reinforcing Bending Schedules.

It is noted that certain design work is specified as "[None]", indicating areas where the Subcontractor may not have primary design responsibility or where no additional design input is required beyond what is provided by Chemetics or other project entities.

**Compliance with Applicable Documents**
A mandatory requirement is that all design work undertaken by the Subcontractor **shall** comply with the requirements set forth in the Applicable Documents. These documents include, but are not limited to, project drawings, specifications, and other technical instructions. Strict adherence ensures that all design outputs are consistent with the project's engineering standards and operational needs.

For general information on the preparation and validation of design documents, consult [[design_document_preparation_and_software_validation]]. Specific guidelines for structural and architectural design and specification can be found in [[structural_and_architectural_design_and_specification_3_prc_0002]]. The overarching procedures for document control, which govern the Applicable Documents, are detailed in [[document_control_procedure_0_prc_0007]].

The Subcontractor's design responsibilities include, but are not limited to, work specified as "[None]", Structural steel shop drawings, and Reinforcing Bending Schedules.
