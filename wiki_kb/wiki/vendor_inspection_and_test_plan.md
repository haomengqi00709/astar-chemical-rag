# Vendor Inspection and Test Plan (ITP)

The Inspection and Test Plan (ITP) is a critical component of the Vendor's Quality Plan, as described in Section 3 of Specification [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment|0-SPC-7001]]. The Vendor **shall** plan, implement, and document inspections and tests to verify the conformance of materials, components, or the goods to specifications.

**ITP Development and Submission**
This inspection and testing program **must** be provided to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics in the form of an Inspection and Test Plan. As a minimum, the ITP **shall** include all activities listed on the reference Aker Kvaerner Chemetics [[quality_data_sheets_qds|Quality Data Sheet]].

**Documentation and Approval of Results**
All inspection or test results **must** be thoroughly documented. Conformance with specifications **shall** be evaluated and approved by the Vendor’s authorized personnel. This documented evidence provides objective proof that the goods meet the required quality standards.

**Integration with Quality Plan**
The Quality Plan, as detailed in [[vendor_quality_plan_requirements]], **shall** incorporate the Inspection and Test Plan. This ensures that inspection and testing activities are seamlessly integrated into the overall quality management system.
