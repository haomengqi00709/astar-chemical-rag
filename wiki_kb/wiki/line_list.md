# Line List

The Line List is a fundamental document in piping design, providing a comprehensive inventory and detailed information for every pipeline in a project. It serves as a central reference for various engineering disciplines throughout the project lifecycle.

For detailed information on the development and approval process for the Line List, refer to [[5-prc-0004_line_list_development_and_approval]]. This procedure outlines the steps involved in creating, reviewing, and approving the Line List, ensuring its accuracy and completeness for all project stakeholders.
