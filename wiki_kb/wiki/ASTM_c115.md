# ASTM C115 Standard Test Method for Fineness of Hydraulic Cement by the Turbidimeter

[[ASTM_c115|ASTM C115]] is a standard test method primarily used for determining the fineness of hydraulic cement by means of a turbidimeter. This method quantifies the particle size distribution of cement, which is a critical factor influencing its hydration rate, workability, and strength development.

While primarily associated with cement, the principles of particle size analysis and material fineness are also relevant in the broader context of construction and refractory materials. For insulating bricks and mortars, fineness can impact properties such as thermal conductivity, density, and bonding characteristics.

In the context of specification [[4-SPC-0082_refractory_brick_lining_materials_and_supply|4-SPC-0082]], insulating brick **will** conform to the latest edition of [[ASTM_c115|ASTM C115]]. This ensures that the insulating bricks meet specific quality and performance criteria, contributing to the overall integrity and thermal efficiency of the refractory lining system. Adherence to this standard is a mandatory requirement for the Vendor supplying these materials.
