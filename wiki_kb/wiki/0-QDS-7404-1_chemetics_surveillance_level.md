# Chemetics Surveillance Level for 0-QDS-7404-1

This page specifies the level of surveillance to be conducted by Chemetics for the Electrostatic Precipitator Shell & Head Sections-Dual Laminate project, as defined in Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7404-1.

Surveillance by Chemetics **shall** be conducted to **Level 2.0**.

The specific details and scope of Chemetics' surveillance activities **shall** be as set out within this Quality Data Sheet (0-QDS-7404-1). This ensures that all critical stages of manufacturing and inspection are adequately monitored to meet project quality standards.

For a comprehensive understanding of the vendor's quality assurance obligations, refer to [[0-QDS-7404-1_vendor_quality_assurance_requirements]]. For detailed inspection and documentation requirements, including Chemetics' specific surveillance points, consult [[0-QDS-7404-1_inspection_and_documentation_requirements]].
