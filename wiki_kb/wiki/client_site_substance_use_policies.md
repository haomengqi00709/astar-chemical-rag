# Client Site Substance Use Policies

When Chemetics Employees, Consultants, or Agency Subcontractors are working at a Client's site, it is important to recognize that the Client may have their own specific drug and alcohol policies.

### Client Policy Implementation
*   Clients may have their own drug and alcohol policy for work conducted on their own sites.
*   The Client is responsible for implementing and enforcing their own drug and alcohol policy for work on their sites.

### Employee Awareness and Action
*   Employees **MUST** be familiar with Client drug and alcohol policies when working at any Client work site. This is a mandatory requirement to ensure compliance with both Chemetics' policy and the Client's regulations.
*   Should an Employee have concerns regarding Client policies, the Employee **SHOULD** raise such concerns with Human Resources as soon as they become aware of the Client policies. This allows Chemetics to address any potential conflicts or provide clarification.

Adherence to both Chemetics' policy and relevant Client policies is essential for maintaining professional conduct and ensuring safety at all work locations.
