# Water and Air Trials (1-PRC-0006)

This document, 1-PRC-0006, outlines the procedure for conducting water and air trials for the Project. These trials involve operating the plant using water and air in place of process fluids to demonstrate system operability and functionality.

Water and air trials are conducted in accordance with this procedure, which requires the plant to be divided into sub-systems for testing. Marked-up Piping and Instrument Diagrams (P&IDs) **must** be used to identify these sub-systems and any temporary bypasses, jumpers, and hoses. This procedure is a critical part of [[water_and_air_trials_and_safety_protocols]].
