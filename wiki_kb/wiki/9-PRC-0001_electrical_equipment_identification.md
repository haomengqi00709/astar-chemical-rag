# Electrical Equipment Identification (9-PRC-0001)

The identification of all electrical equipment within the plant **shall** be carried out in strict accordance with Procedure 9-PRC-0001: Electrical Equipment Identification. This procedure establishes a standardized system for assigning unique identifiers to every piece of electrical equipment, ensuring clarity, consistency, and ease of reference across all project documentation and operational activities.

Adherence to [[9-PRC-0001_electrical_equipment_identification]] is fundamental for:
*   Accurate tracking and inventory management of electrical assets.
*   Facilitating efficient maintenance, troubleshooting, and repair operations.
*   Ensuring clear communication among engineering, construction, and operations teams.
*   Compliance with safety regulations and operational standards.

This identification system is integrated with the broader [[9-PRC-0021_electrical_numbering_and_identification_procedure|electrical numbering and identification framework]]. For specific details on the identification of electric motors, which are also considered electrical equipment, refer to [[electric_motor_identification]].
