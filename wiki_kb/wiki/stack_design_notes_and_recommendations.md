# Stack Design Notes and Recommendations

This page compiles important design notes and recommendations specific to the stack, which should be considered during detailed engineering and fabrication.

**General Design Considerations:**
*   Free standing stack may require larger diameter at bottom than shown for barrel.
*   Stainless steel lining required at breech.
*   Sample Ports to be 90 degrees apart.
*   Height of base to be confirmed.

**Mandatory Requirement:**
*   User **must** consult with the Technology Manager prior to modifying parameters not highlighted in Blue.

These notes are critical for ensuring the structural integrity, operational efficiency, and maintainability of the stack. They supplement the detailed [[stack_physical_specifications_and_materials]] and [[stack_design_parameters_and_operating_conditions]].
