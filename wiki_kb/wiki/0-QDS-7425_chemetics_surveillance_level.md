# Fan or Blower Chemetics Surveillance Level

For equipment covered by Quality Data Sheet **0-QDS-7425**, surveillance by Chemetics will be conducted at **Level 0.0**.

The specific details and scope of this surveillance level are as set out within this [[0-QDS-7XXX_template_quality_data_sheet|Quality Data Sheet]]. This level defines the extent of Chemetics' involvement in monitoring the Vendor's quality activities and inspections throughout the manufacturing and testing process.
