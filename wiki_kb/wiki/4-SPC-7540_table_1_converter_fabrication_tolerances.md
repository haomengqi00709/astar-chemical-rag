# Converter Fabrication Tolerances Table (4-SPC-7540_table_1)

The table referenced as 4-SPC-7540_table_1 within the 4-SPC-7540 specification provides detailed numerical values and specific criteria for converter fabrication tolerances. This table supplements the general tolerance requirements outlined in [[4-SPC-7540_converter_fabrication_tolerances]].

The table typically includes, but is not limited to, tolerances for:
*   Dimensional accuracy of components (e.g., length, diameter, thickness).
*   Out-of-roundness and concentricity for cylindrical sections.
*   Flatness and perpendicularity of plates and nozzles.
*   Alignment of weld seams and adjacent courses.
*   Fit-up gaps and offsets.

These specific values are critical for quality control and inspection during the [[4-SPC-7540_shop_fabrication_and_assembly]] process.
