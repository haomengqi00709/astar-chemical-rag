# Construction Site Safety Rules

This page outlines the mandatory safety rules for all personnel working on or entering Aker Kvaerner Chemetics [[hse_construction_sites|construction sites]], as specified in procedure [[hse_site_postings_overview]] (0-PRC-8004) and referenced by [[worksite_safety_rules_general]]. Adherence to these rules is critical for maintaining a safe working environment.

**Mandatory Construction Site Safety Rules:**

*   **Personal Protective Equipment (PPE):** Hard hats and safety boots **will** be worn by all workers entering the work site.
*   **Site Entry Protocol:** All personnel entering the work site for the first time **will** report to the site office immediately upon arrival.
*   **Substance Use:** Consuming or possession of illegal drugs or alcohol on this project site is **prohibited**. For more information, refer to the [[prohibited_substances_and_conduct]] policy.
*   **Injury Reporting and First Aid:** First Aid treatment **shall** be obtained immediately for any injury, no matter how minor.
*   **Hazard Reporting:** Unsafe conditions, safety observations, and incidents **will** be reported to your supervisor immediately. For detailed incident reporting procedures, see [[hse_incident_reporting]].
*   **Appropriate Clothing:** Clothing that covers arms and legs (long sleeves and long pants) **will** be worn.
*   **Tool Condition:** Only tools that are in good repair, meet industry standards, and are equipped with all necessary guards and safety devices **will** be used.
*   **Conduct:** Fighting, horseplay, practical jokes, or interference with other workers is **prohibited**.
*   **Company Property:** Theft, vandalism, or abuse or misuse of Company property is **prohibited**.

These rules are prominently posted at the employee entrance of construction sites and **will** be clearly readable from a distance of four meters.
