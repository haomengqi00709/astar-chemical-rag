# Shock Absorber Installation

This page outlines the mandatory requirements for checking the installation and condition of [[shock_absorber_installation|shock absorbers]] for [[rotating_equipment_mechanical_checkout|rotating]] and [[conveying_equipment_mechanical_checkout|conveying equipment]]. Installed shock absorbers **must** be checked for damage, be clean, and move smoothly through their travel range.
