# Project Initiation and Contract Handover

The project initiation phase involves a critical handover of information and documents from the proposal and sales teams to the Project Manager.

**As-Sold Project Documentation:**
The Proposal Manager and the Sales Manager (or Director) are jointly responsible for completing the documents that describe the as-sold project. These documents include:
*   **Contract document:** Completed by the Sales Manager.
*   **Scope Checklists document:** Completed by the Proposal Manager.
*   **Battery Limits Conditions document:** Completed by the Proposal Manager.
*   **Estimate document:** Completed by the Proposal Manager.
*   **Proposal Risk Analysis document:** Completed by the Sales Manager.

**Contract Handover Briefing:**
Immediately upon the initiation of the project (as described in Section 2 of 0-PRC-3001), the Proposal Manager and the Sales Manager (or Director) **must** meet with the Project Manager. The primary objectives of this meeting are:
*   To provide a comprehensive briefing on the requirements of the project.
*   To formally hand over the completed Contract, Scope Checklists, Battery Limits Conditions, Estimate, and Proposal Risk Analysis documents.

If any of these documents have not yet been updated to their final as-sold status, preliminary versions **must** be provided. In such cases, a schedule for the completion of the final documents **must** be agreed upon during the meeting.

The Project Manager **must** prepare minutes of this contract hand-over briefing. These minutes **must** then be checked by all other attendees to ensure their accuracy and completeness.
