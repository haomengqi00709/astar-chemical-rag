# Cooling Tower Design Requirements

The design of cooling towers, as specified in document **4-DST-XXXX-R0**, must adhere to a comprehensive set of requirements to ensure optimal performance, safety, and compliance. These requirements cover various aspects from structural configuration to operational limits.

**General Design Parameters:**
*   **Tower Type**: The specific type of cooling tower (e.g., crossflow, counterflow).
*   **Location**: The intended installation location, influencing environmental factors.
*   **Field Fabrication**: Indication of whether field fabrication is required.
*   **Fan Type**: The type of fan to be used (e.g., axial, centrifugal).
*   **Number of Cells**: The total number of cooling tower cells.
*   **Specification**: General project specifications applicable to the cooling tower.

**Mechanical Design and Standards:**
*   **Mechanical Design**: Specific mechanical design considerations must be followed. Refer to [[general_mechanical_design_requirements_0_spc_0010]] for overarching guidelines.
*   **Design Code**: The applicable design code (e.g., ASME, API) must be specified. Refer to [[design_standards_and_reference_materials]] for a list of approved standards.
*   **Dimensional Limit**: Any dimensional constraints for the cooling tower.

**Performance and Operational Limits:**
*   **Total Noise Limit**: The maximum permissible noise level generated by the cooling tower.
*   **Painting**: Requirements for painting and protective coatings. Refer to [[coatings_design_and_specification_7_prc_0002]] for detailed specifications.
*   **Max. Fan Vibration Displacement**: The maximum allowable fan vibration displacement.
*   **Max. Fan Vibration Limit**: The overall maximum fan vibration limit.
*   **Fan Rotation**: The specified direction of fan rotation.
*   **Fan Speed**: The operational speed of the fan.
*   **Piping Connection**: Details regarding piping connections. Refer to [[piping_design_and_specification_5_prc_0003]] for piping standards.
*   **Max Fan Tip Speed**: The maximum allowable tip speed for the fan blades.

These design requirements are critical for the [[cooling_tower_operating_conditions]] and ensure the cooling tower integrates seamlessly with the overall plant infrastructure.

**General Design Parameters:**
Key design requirements to be specified include:

*   Number of Cells

*   Design Code

*   Total Noise Limit
*   Painting
*   Max. Fan Vibration Displacement

*   Fan Rotation
*   Fan Speed
*   Piping Connection
*   Max Fan Tip Speed
