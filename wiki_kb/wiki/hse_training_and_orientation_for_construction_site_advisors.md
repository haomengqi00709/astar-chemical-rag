# HSE Training and Orientation for Construction Site Advisors

Effective Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) training and orientation are critical for Aker Kvaerner Chemetics construction site advisors. The Aker Kvaerner Chemetics safety manager is responsible for determining the personnel who will provide safety indoctrination and advice to advisors prior to their travel to a construction site. Once on Site, the advisor themselves is responsible for conducting safety indoctrination.

**Mandatory Training Programs**
Advisors who are expected to be on Site for a period exceeding one week **must** complete an HSE training program approved by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. This training **must** be refreshed every two years to ensure continued compliance and up-to-date knowledge. Information regarding these training programs can be obtained directly from the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics safety manager. For an overview of the company's general HSE training framework, refer to [[hse_training_program_overview]].

**Pre-Travel Indoctrination**
Prior to travelling to any construction Site:
*   **Vancouver Office Advisors:** Advisors based in the Vancouver Office **must** receive an HSE indoctrination. This indoctrination **shall** include, at a minimum, a comprehensive review of the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Construction Safety Handbook, safe work practices, and required personal protective equipment (PPE). General information on new employee safety can be found in [[hse_new_employee_orientation]].
*   **Advisors from Other Locations:** Advisors located in other offices or remote locations will receive the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Construction Safety Handbook via email. Their pre-travel review **shall** include safe work practices and required PPE, delivered either verbally or via email.

**Site-Specific Orientation**
Upon arrival at a construction Site, each advisor **must** obtain site-specific HSE indoctrination before entering any construction areas. This orientation is typically provided by the owner of the facility during the advisor's initial arrival at the Site. Advisors **must** familiarize themselves with specific site HSE issues, including the locations of first aid, ambulance availability, and [[evacuation_procedures_akctc]]. For a general understanding of site orientations, see [[hse_orientation_overview]].

The advisor is responsible for safety indoctrination at Site.

**Extended Training Requirements**
Advisors expected to be on site for a period greater than one week will complete an HSE training program approved by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. Applicable advisors will complete these training programs every two years. Information regarding these training programs can be obtained from the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics safety manager. If advisors are not familiar with applicable HSE procedures and specifications, they will obtain training as noted above.

**Indoctrination Content and Delivery**
The person providing safety indoctrination will advise if additional PPE is required to be carried with the advisor.

*   **For Vancouver Office Advisors:** Prior to travelling to Site, advisors from the Vancouver Office will receive an HSE indoctrination. This indoctrination will include a review of the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Construction Safety Handbook, covering safe work practices and required PPE at a minimum.
*   **For Advisors from Other Locations:** Prior to travelling to Site, advisors from Other Locations will receive the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Construction Safety Handbook by email. They will also receive a verbal or email review that will include safe work practices and required personal protective equipment.

**Site-Specific Orientation**
Each site will have its specific HSE orientation. Prior to entering construction areas, the advisor will obtain site-specific HSE indoctrination. This indoctrination is generally given by the owner upon the advisor's initial arrival at site.
