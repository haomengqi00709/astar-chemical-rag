# UNS S30815 Welding Standard Overview

This standard defines the workmanship and quality requirements for heat-resistant UNS S30815 welded fabrications. It is applicable when explicitly referenced on purchase orders and other project documents.

**Scope and Applicability**
The standard covers welding and other fabrication requirements for a variety of equipment, including vessels, heat exchangers, tanks, piping, and miscellaneous welded items. The materials specifically addressed fall under the ASME P8 Material Classification Group 2. All referenced codes and standards are to be interpreted as their latest editions ruling at the date of contract.

**Definitions**
*   **Agreement in Writing**: This term signifies a formal submission by letter, not merely an inclusion in approval drawings.
*   **Inspector**: Refers to a Quality Assurance Inspector or any other authorized representative of Chemetics.

**General Fabricator Responsibilities**
*   **Sub-Contracting**: Sub-contracting of welding is **NOT permitted** without prior written approval from Chemetics.
*   **Supervision**: The Fabricator **MUST** ensure regular and systematic supervision of all work, both in-shop and on-site, by competent staff.
*   **Contamination Prevention**: The Fabricator **MUST** implement necessary controls to prevent contamination of weldments at all stages of fabrication. These controls **SHALL** include the segregation of tools such as wire brushes and grinding wheels for exclusive use on stainless steels.
*   **Drawing Compliance**: All work **SHALL** be carried out in accordance with the "Approved For Construction" shop detail drawing(s). Deviations from the weld geometry shown on approved drawings are **NOT permitted** without the prior approval of Chemetics' Q.A. & Materials Group.
*   **Cleaning**: Upon completion of work, items **WILL** be cleaned internally and externally. All fabrication markings, oxidation, ferroxyl testing, and weld spatter **WILL** be removed prior to shipment.
*   **Passivation**: Where passivation is required by the Purchase Order or referenced documents, it **SHALL** be carried out in accordance with written procedures. These passivation procedures **MUST** be approved by Chemetics prior to use.
