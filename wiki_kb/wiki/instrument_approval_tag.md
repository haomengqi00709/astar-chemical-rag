# Instrument Approval Tag

The Instrument Approval Tag is a critical document used in the tracking and checkout procedure for field instrumentation, as outlined in [[8-prc-0011_field_instrumentation_tracking_and_checkout]]. Its primary purpose is to provide a complete, auditable record of an instrument's lifecycle from receipt through to loop checkout and mechanical completion.

**Application and Responsibility**
*   The subcontractor **must** permanently affix (using Ty-Rap) an Instrument Approval Tag to each instrument upon receipt.
*   Individual tags **are required** for both the primary sensor and its associated transmitter.
*   If a tag becomes detached, the subcontractor **must** immediately replace it and confirm the accuracy of the information.
*   All data recorded on the tag **shall be** printed in a clear and concise manner.

**Tag Structure and Information Fields**
The Instrument Approval Tag is structured into several stages, each corresponding to a phase of the instrument's inspection and commissioning process. An example of the tag is provided in Figure 1 of the source document.

**Stage No. 1: Receipt and Inspection**
This section documents the initial receipt and inspection of the instrument.
*   **Tag No.:** The engraved instrument tag number, which **must** match the [[8-dst-xxxx_instrument_specification_sheets|Instrument Specification Sheet]].
*   **Model No.:** The model number recorded directly from the instrument. Any variance from the [[8-dst-xxxx_instrument_specification_sheets|Instrument Specification Sheet]] **must** be noted in Stage No. 6 (COMMENTS).
*   **Serial No.:** The serial number recorded directly from the instrument.
*   **Inspected by:** Signature and date of the subcontractor upon completion of the physical inspection.

**Stage No. 2: Calibration**
This stage records the instrument's calibration status.
*   **Calibration / Units:** The calibration range or setpoint and units. If a certified manufacturer's certificate is used, "By Manufacturer" **shall be** indicated.
*   **Valve / Xtr. Fail Pos.:** The confirmed fail position for control valves, on/off valves, or transmitters with embedded fail positions, as per [[8-dst-xxxx_instrument_specification_sheets|Instrument Specification Sheets]].
*   **Calibrated by:** Signature and date of the subcontractor upon completion of calibration verification or adjustment.

**Stage No. 3: Installation**
This section confirms the physical installation of the instrument.
*   **Ready for Checkout:** Signature and date of the subcontractor upon completion of installation (including supports, tubing, and wiring).
*   **Checked by:** Signature and date of the Chemetics site supervisor or representative after verifying installation against [[engineering_documentation_requirements]] and manufacturer instructions.

**Stage No. 4: Functional Checkout (Electrical Wiring)**
This stage documents the functional testing of electrically wired instruments.
*   **Ready for Checkout:** Signature and date of the subcontractor upon completion of continuity tests (for wired instruments) or immediately after Stage 3 (for local instruments).
*   **Checked by:** Signature and date of the Chemetics site supervisor or representative after verifying instrument function against [[engineering_documentation_requirements]].

**Stage No. 5: Loop Checkout and Mechanical Completion**
This final stage confirms the completion of loop checks and mechanical readiness.
*   **Ready for Checkout:** Signature and date of the subcontractor after correcting all deficiencies from Stage 4.
*   **Checked by:** Signature and date of the Chemetics site supervisor or representative upon satisfaction that installation and loop check are complete.

**Stage No. 6: Comments and Owner Acceptance**
*   **Comments:** This section, typically on the reverse side of the tag, is used by the subcontractor to record any relevant comments, such as K numbers or calibration numbers for specific instruments (e.g., Mag Flow Meters).
*   **Owner Acceptance:** If required, the owner **shall** sign and date this section to indicate acceptance of individual instruments.

The completion of Stage 5, verified by the Chemetics site supervisor, is visually indicated by attaching a plain green tag, signifying that the instrument is mechanically complete and ready for [[water_batching]].

[[8-prc-0011_field_instrumentation_tracking_and_checkout]] details the full procedure for utilizing this tag.
