# HCl Synthesis Unit Tail Gas Specifications

This page outlines the specifications for the tail gas stream exiting the HCl Synthesis Unit, as detailed in [[4-PDS-XXXX-R1_template_hcl_synthesis_unit_package_datasheet_administration]]. These parameters are critical for ensuring environmental compliance, designing effective downstream gas treatment systems, and evaluating the overall efficiency of the synthesis process.

**Tail Gas Emission Guarantees and Design Parameters:**
The tail gas stream **must** meet the following guaranteed emission limits and design parameters to ensure regulatory compliance and safe operation:
*   **Maximum HCl (Guarantee):** 30.0 mg/Nm3
*   **Maximum Cl2 (Guarantee):** 3.0 mg/Nm3
*   **Maximum HCl (Design):** VTA mg/Nm3
*   **Maximum Cl2 (Design):** VTA mg/Nm3
*   **Flow Rate:** VTA kg/h
*   **Temperature:** VTA oC

The guaranteed maximum concentrations for HCl and Cl2 are mandatory environmental compliance targets that the unit **shall** consistently achieve during operation. These values are legally binding and represent the upper limits for emissions. The design values (VTA) represent the expected performance under normal operating conditions and serve as benchmarks for process optimization. The specified tail gas flow rate and temperature are essential for accurately sizing the [[hcl_synthesis_unit_general_design_features#Tail Gas Scrubber]] and any other required pollution control equipment, ensuring efficient removal of residual components before atmospheric release.

These stringent specifications are vital for ensuring the environmental performance of the HCl Synthesis Unit and maintaining compliance with all applicable regulatory standards for [[emissions_and_effluents]].
