# Group Travel Roles and Responsibilities

Effective management of business travel within the Aker Kvaerner Group relies on clear roles and responsibilities across various levels of the organization. This section outlines the duties of Line Management, Employees, Human Resources, the Group Travel Network, and Travel Agencies.

### Line Management
Line Management is responsible for the application of this travel procedure. Their duties include:
*   Applying the travel procedure consistently.
*   Authorizing travel and related requirements, such as car and hotel bookings, within the limits defined by this procedure.
*   Each Company **shall** specifically name a small number of nominated individuals (no more than 6-8) who are authorized to approve higher costs than those offered by the travel agency when special requirements or circumstances necessitate it.
*   Managers responsible for controlling business costs **shall** follow up on any significant cost discrepancies with individual employees to ensure correct application of the procedure.

### Employees
Every employee has a responsibility and a duty to adhere to the travel procedure and to minimize their Company's travel expenses.
*   Employees **must** follow the travel procedure. Failure to do so may result in the loss of some or all travel expense reimbursement.
*   Employees are requested to provide notice of travel and accommodation requirements as far in advance as possible, ideally no less than seven days prior to travel.
*   Employees **must** make a commercial judgment as to whether the objective of the visit can be met without the need for travel.
*   Only journeys essential for business and properly authorized may be undertaken.

### Group Human Resources (HR)
Group HR plays a central role in the oversight and improvement of the travel procedure:
*   Monitoring the effectiveness of the procedure.
*   Proposing procedure improvements to Group Management.
*   Appointing members to the [[group_travel_roles_responsibilities#the_group_travel_network|Group Travel Network]].

### Business Area and Company HR
Business Area and Company HR are responsible for local implementation and compliance:
*   Ensuring compliance with the procedure.
*   Monitoring the effectiveness of the procedure within their respective areas.
*   Instituting corrective actions as necessary.

### The Group Travel Network
The Group Travel Network is a key strategic body for travel management:
*   **Chair:** Chaired by Group HR.
*   **Composition:** Comprised of key individuals from specialist functions (e.g., Procurement, HR) and line management from across the Group.
*   **Role:**
    *   Reviewing the travel procedure and its effectiveness.
    *   Managing negotiations with main suppliers to maximize purchasing power.

### Travel Agencies
Nominated travel agencies are integral to the travel booking process:
*   Each Company **shall** utilize a minimum number of nominated travel agencies.
*   Travel agencies **must** act as a full-service bureau for all travel needs.
*   They **must** provide a level of service equal to, or better than, that available from any other external source.
*   Travel agencies **shall** play an active role in supporting travel negotiations by providing consolidated volume and location data.
*   The Travel Agency is instructed **not** to book a more expensive travel option unless and until they receive approval from one of the nominated representatives at the Business Area concerned.
*   The travel agency is authorized by the Company to select and recommend the most cost-effective airline and routing to meet the demands of the traveller and to maximize cost-saving opportunities.
