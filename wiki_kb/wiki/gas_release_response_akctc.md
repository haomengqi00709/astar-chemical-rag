# Gas Release Response - AKCTC

This page details the procedures for responding to an accidental gas release at the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Technology Centre (AKCTC).

**Immediate Actions:**
*   AKCTC personnel **shall** alert others in the area of the gas release.
*   Evacuate the area if necessary.
*   Use appropriate protective equipment to remove or turn off the source of the gas release.
*   If possible, isolate the gas release.
*   If possible, neutralize the gas release.
*   If possible, absorb the gas release.

**Life-Threatening Gas Release:**
If a gas release is life-threatening (e.g., HCl, Cl2), evacuate the building immediately. For building evacuation due to a life-threatening gas release, follow the [[fire_emergency_response_akctc#Major_Fire_Response]] procedure, which includes dialing 9-1-1 and proceeding to the [[evacuation_procedures_akctc#Emergency_Assembly_Point]].

**Neighbouring Businesses:**
AKCTC personnel **shall** alert neighbouring businesses if a gas release occurs and evacuate them if necessary.

**Environmental Release:**
*   If a gas release is to the environment and is substantial, report the accidental gas emission to Burnaby’s City Hall (Environmental Emergencies Department at 604-294-7200). The AKCTC Supervisor should preferably report accidental gas emission to Burnaby’s City Hall.
*   Refer to [[akctc_safety_manual_appendix_o_table_1]] in Appendix O, Table 1 “Determination of Materials and Agencies Requiring Notification” (in the AKCTC Safety Manual) to determine which other agency(ies) should be notified for a gas release.
*   The "Spill Reporting Regulation" (B.C. Reg. 166/93), an official guideline from the B.C. Waste Management Act, **must** be complied with in the province of British Columbia for gas releases.

For an overview of emergency response, refer to [[0-PRC-8010_office_emergency_response_overview]].
