# Vendor Quality Assurance Requirements for 0-QDS-7301

For projects governed by Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7301, vendors are subject to specific quality assurance requirements to ensure compliance and product integrity.

**Quality Plan:**
The vendor **must** prepare a Quality Plan in accordance with the guidelines outlined in [[vendor_quality_assurance_bulk_materials_0_spc_7002]]. This Quality Plan is a critical component of the vendor's quality assurance documentation. If a Quality Plan is not required for a specific scope of work, the corresponding cells within the Quality Data Sheet should be deleted.

**Compliance:**
The vendor's Quality Assurance system **must** comply with the requirements specified in this Quality Data Sheet (0-QDS-7301). Additionally, compliance with [[vendor_quality_assurance_bulk_materials_0_spc_7002]] is mandatory. Vendor's Quality Assurance compliance is also **required** to be demonstrated in the Vendor Inspection and Test (I & T) Plan.

**Chemetics Surveillance:**
Surveillance activities conducted by Chemetics will be performed to Level 0.0. The full scope and details of Chemetics' surveillance will be as set out within this Quality Data Sheet. For general vendor quality assurance procedures, refer to [[vendor_quality_assurance_0_prc_7005]]. Further details on vendor quality assurance expectations can be found in [[0-QDS-7_vendor_quality_assurance_requirements]].
