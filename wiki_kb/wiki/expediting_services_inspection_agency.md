# Expediting Services by Third Party Inspection Agencies

When shop expediting visits are requested, the Inspection Agency **shall** utilize experienced shop or field expeditors. This ensures that personnel with the appropriate expertise are deployed for monitoring and accelerating the progress of fabrication or supply.

**Restrictions on Inspectors**
Inspectors **must not** be utilized for expediting activities, unless specifically agreed to by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. This separation of roles helps maintain the impartiality and focus of inspection activities.

**Direction for Expediting**
In all expediting matters, direction and guidance **will** be provided by the [[procurement_and_logistics_strategy]] Department. This ensures alignment with overall project procurement and scheduling objectives.

This service is an optional but important aspect that may be requested under the [[subcontractor_quality_assurance_0_prc_7006]] framework to manage project timelines effectively.
