# Standard Document Roles and Responsibilities

Effective management of standard documents relies on clearly defined roles and responsibilities. This section outlines the duties of key personnel involved in the [[standard_document_management_procedure_0_prc_0001]].

### Standards Administrator
The Standards Administrator, who also serves as the secretary of the President, holds a central role in the management of standard documents. Their responsibilities include:
*   Ensuring that all documents are properly numbered and approved in accordance with established procedures.
*   Updating the [[standard_document_number_index]] to reflect the latest versions and revisions of all standard documents.
*   Placing documents in their correct locations on the Q Drive, ensuring proper organization and accessibility.
*   Maintaining a permanent record of all approved documents on the Q Drive, specifically within the "Approvals" subdirectory.
*   Notifying all employees of the availability of draft standards (Rev 0), approved standards (Rev 1), and subsequent revisions.
*   Collating comments received on draft standards during the designated comment period.
*   Managing write-access to the Q Drive, which is exclusively granted to the Standards Administrator.

### Checker and Approver
The Checker and Approver roles are critical for ensuring the quality, conformity, and compliance of standard documents. Their responsibilities include:
*   Reviewing all first drafts (Rev 0) of standard documents to ensure conformity and compliance with all existing standards.
*   Reviewing all subsequent revisions to standard documents.
*   Digitally signing the PDF version of the approved standard, which is then stored in the "Approvals" subdirectory on the Q Drive.
*   Participating in the review of comments received on draft standards and contributing to the revision process alongside the author.

For the purposes of this procedure, the terms 'checker' and 'approver' are used in their generic dictionary meanings. These definitions are distinct from the more specific definitions provided in [[0-PRC-7004_checking_and_approval_of_design]].

The specific authority for checking and approving standard documents is detailed in [[0_prc_0001_table_2_approval_authority]]. This table outlines which roles are authorized to perform these critical functions for different types of documents.
