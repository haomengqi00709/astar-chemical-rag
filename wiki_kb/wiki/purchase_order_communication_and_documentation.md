# Purchase Order Communication and Documentation

Effective communication and meticulous documentation are critical for the successful execution of a Purchase Order (PO). This section details the requirements for all communications and the impact of acceptance within the PO framework.

**Communication Requirements:**
*   All instructions, certificates, submissions, proposals, records, acceptances, notifications, and replies required by this Purchase Order **must** be communicated in a form that can be read, copied, and recorded.
*   All written communications **must** be in English.
*   Every communication from the Vendor to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or any of its representatives **must** be copied to the [[purchase_order_roles_and_responsibilities#Project Buyer|Project Buyer]].
*   If the Purchase Order requires a representative of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or the Vendor to reply to a communication, they **must** do so within **one week**.

**Acceptance and Responsibility:**
*   The [[purchase_order_roles_and_responsibilities#Project Buyer|Project Buyer]] may withhold acceptance of a submission from the Vendor. Withholding acceptance for a reason explicitly stated in the Purchase Order is **not** considered a [[0-SPC-5002_compensation_events|compensation event]].
*   The acceptance of a communication or work from the Vendor by a representative of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics does **not** alter the Vendor’s fundamental responsibility to [[purchase_order_definitions#To Provide the Goods and Services|Provide the Goods and Services]] in accordance with the Purchase Order.
*   Similarly, such acceptance does **not** change the Vendor’s liability for their design.
