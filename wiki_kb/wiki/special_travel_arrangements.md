# Special Travel Arrangements Policy

The Group Travel Procedure (11.1 PR 2) provides specific guidelines for any special travel arrangements that deviate from standard procedures.

**Justification and Approval:**
Any special travel arrangement, such as the use of a charter aircraft, must be justified on a case-by-case basis. Such arrangements require the prior written approval of the Business Area EVP or local Company President/Managing Director.
