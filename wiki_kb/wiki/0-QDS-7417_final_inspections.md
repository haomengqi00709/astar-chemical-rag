# Shell & Tube Heat Exchanger Final Inspections (0-QDS-7417)

The final inspections for the Shell & Tube Heat Exchanger, as specified in 0-QDS-7417, are crucial steps to ensure that the completed equipment meets all design specifications, quality standards, and is ready for shipment and installation. These inspections cover various aspects of the finished product.

**Final Inspection (Item 22.0):**
This overarching item encompasses a series of checks performed on the heat exchanger after all fabrication, testing, and finishing processes are complete.

**Specific Final Inspection Items:**

*   **Dimensional (Item 23.0):**
    *   **Quality Record Required:** FP ("Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package")
    *   **Surveillance by Chemetics:** Complete
    *   A comprehensive dimensional inspection **must** be performed to verify that all critical dimensions of the heat exchanger conform to the approved drawings and specifications. This includes overall dimensions, nozzle orientations, support locations, and other key measurements. The inspection results **shall** be documented and reviewed completely by Chemetics.

*   **Contour & Finish of Welds (Item 24.0):**
    *   **Quality Record Required:** FP ("Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package")
    *   **Surveillance by Chemetics:** Complete
    *   The contour and finish of all welds **must** be inspected to ensure they meet aesthetic and functional requirements, are free from excessive reinforcement, undercut, or other surface imperfections that could affect performance or lead to stress concentrations. Documentation of this inspection **shall** be provided.

*   **Surfaces Free from Defects (Item 25.0):**
    *   **Quality Record Required:** FP ("Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package")
    *   **Surveillance by Chemetics:** Complete
    *   All accessible surfaces of the heat exchanger **must** be inspected to confirm they are free from defects such as dents, gouges, laminations, or other surface irregularities that could compromise the equipment's integrity or performance.

*   **Cleanliness (Item 26.0):**
    *   **Quality Record Required:** FP ("Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package")
    *   **Surveillance by Chemetics:** Complete
    *   The internal and external cleanliness of the heat exchanger **must** be verified. This is particularly important for process equipment to prevent contamination of the process fluid and ensure efficient operation. All foreign materials, debris, and residues **must** be removed.

*   **Spare Parts (Item 27.0):**
    *   **Quality Record Required:** FP ("Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package")
    *   **Surveillance by Chemetics:** Complete
    *   If spare parts are part of the order, they **must** be inspected to ensure they are correctly identified, complete, and in good condition. Documentation confirming the completeness and quality of spare parts **shall** be included.

*   **Review QA Documentation (Item 28.0):**
    *   **Surveillance by Chemetics:** Witness and Complete
    *   Chemetics will witness and perform a complete review of the Vendor's Quality Assurance Documentation Package. This review ensures that all required quality records, certifications, and reports (including the Manufacturer's Data Report (MDR) if registration is required) are complete, accurate, and compliant with the project specifications.

These final inspections, along with the [[0-QDS-7417_coating_and_lining_inspection|coating and lining inspection]], are critical for the final acceptance of the Shell & Tube Heat Exchanger. For definitions of quality record designations, refer to [[0-QDS-7417_quality_record_definitions]].
