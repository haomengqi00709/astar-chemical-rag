# Business Travel Risk Assessment and Approval

The level of risk associated with a travel destination **shall** be determined based on International SOS recommendations. These recommendations are accessible on the International SOS website at: http://www.internationalsos.com/Members_home/.

Travel to regions classified as High or Extreme risk **must** be closely controlled and approved as outlined in this procedure. While travel to Moderate risk regions, particularly for new travellers to that area, should also follow this procedure, the focus on High and Extreme risk is paramount.

The decision to permit travel **shall** take into consideration:
*   International SOS recommendations.
*   Recommendations from the Government of Canada.
*   Any available local intelligence information.
*   Information shared by other Aker Kvaerner business units.

The Business Unit President, Director of Technology, Director of Projects, Human Resources Manager, and HSE Manager are jointly responsible for understanding the level of risk at any location where the company conducts business.

**Mandatory Approvals for High/Extreme Risk Areas:**
*   Travel to High or Extreme risk areas **must** be approved by the Business Unit President.
*   The Business Area Executive Vice President **must** be informed of all travel to High or Extreme risk areas.
*   The Director of Technology, Director of Projects, and HSE Manager **shall** have joint responsibility for making the decision on whether an employee is permitted to travel to a High or Extreme risk area.
*   These same individuals **shall** also have joint responsibility for determining the necessary actions to minimize risk for such travel.

Travel to Extreme risk areas **should be** avoided whenever possible. Other means of accomplishing the business goal **must** be explored first for these areas. When travel to High or Extreme risk areas is deemed unavoidable, minimum planning and precautions **must** be taken to mitigate risks.

The Travel Coordinator **shall** ensure that a risk assessment has been completed when required and that the "Aker Kvaerner Travel, Health and Security Norm" has been followed. Risk assessment information **shall** be logged in the Travel Coordinator’s list of travellers. This process is integral to the broader [[0-prc-8102_travel_health_and_safety|Travel Health, Safety, and Security Overview]].

Travel to regions classified as High or Extreme risk **shall** be closely controlled and approved as outlined in this procedure. Conversely, travel to regions of **Moderate risk**, especially for new travellers to that region, should also adhere to the guidelines of this procedure.

The decision to permit travel is a comprehensive one, taking into consideration:
*   International SOS recommendations
*   Recommendations from the Government of Canada
*   Any available local intelligence information
*   Information shared by other Aker Kvaerner business units

Key personnel, including the Business Unit President, Director of Technology, Director of Projects, Human Resources Manager, and HSE Manager, are collectively responsible for understanding the level of risk at any location where the company conducts business.

For travel to High or Extreme risk areas, approval is specifically required from the Business Unit President. Additionally, the Business Area Executive Vice President **must** be informed of such travel. The Director of Technology, Director of Projects, and HSE Manager hold joint responsibility for both making the final decision on whether an employee is permitted to travel to a High or Extreme risk area, and for determining the necessary actions to minimize associated risks.

**Extreme Risk Travel Principles:**
Travel to extreme risk areas should generally be avoided. Before approving such travel, other means of accomplishing the business goal **must** be explored. When travel to High or Extreme risk areas is deemed unavoidable, minimum planning and precautions **must** be rigorously implemented.
