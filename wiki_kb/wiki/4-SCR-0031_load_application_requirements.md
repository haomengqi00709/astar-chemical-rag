# 4-SCR-0031 Load Application Requirements

This page specifies the critical point at which all calculated loads for the Plate & Frame Heat Exchanger on Steel must be applied, as defined in document 4-SCR-0031.

**Load Application Point:**
*   All loads are to be applied at **Datum**.

This ensures consistency in structural analysis and design, correlating with the unfactored equipment loads detailed in [[4-SCR-0031_unfactored_equipment_loads]].
