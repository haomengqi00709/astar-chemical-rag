# Clad Steel Cleaning, Passivation, and Ferroxyl Testing

Upon completion of fabrication, rigorous cleaning, passivation, and testing procedures are mandatory for austenitic stainless clad steel items to ensure their corrosion resistance and freedom from contamination prior to shipment.

**Post-Fabrication Cleaning**
On completion of work, items **must** be cleaned internally and externally. All fabrication markings, oxidation, weld spatter, and other foreign matter **must** be removed prior to shipment.

**Passivation Requirements**
Passivation of the stainless steel surface **must** be carried out in accordance with written procedures. This is required where specified by the Purchase Order, referenced documents, or engineering drawings. The written procedures for passivation **must** be approved by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics prior to use. All internal weld surfaces **will** be passivated in accordance with such an approved written procedure. The passivation procedure **should** refer to [[ASTM_a380_cleaning_passivation_stainless_steel]] "Cleaning & Descaling Stainless Steel Parts, Equipment & Systems" for guidance.

**Ferroxyl Testing for Carbon Steel Contamination**
All internal surfaces **will** be given a Ferroxyl Test for Carbon Steel Contamination. This test is crucial for detecting any free iron particles embedded in the stainless steel surface, which could compromise its corrosion resistance. The Ferroxyl Test, like passivation, **must** be carried out in accordance with written procedures approved by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics where required by the Purchase Order, referenced documents, or engineering drawings.
