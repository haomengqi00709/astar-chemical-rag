# Third Party Inspection Agency Initial Responsibilities

Upon receiving a Purchase Order (PO) for inspection services from Aker Kvaerner Chemetics (AKC), the Third Party Inspection Agency assumes several key responsibilities to initiate the inspection process effectively.

The Agency **must** make contact with the equipment vendor promptly. During this initial contact, the Agency **must** advise the vendor that it will represent AKC in all surveillance and inspection matters, establishing its authority and role in the quality assurance process.

A critical responsibility of the Agency is the issuance of an [[inspection_release_process_and_documentation]] covering the equipment once all inspection requirements are satisfied.

Furthermore, the Agency **must** confirm the exact location of manufacture for the equipment. It **must** also establish if the vendor plans any sub-contract of manufacture. If sub-contracting is planned, the Agency **must** advise AKC of these details immediately to ensure appropriate oversight can be arranged.

Any serious problems or non-conformances identified during the inspection process **should** be reported to AKC immediately to allow for timely intervention and resolution.

For comprehensive details regarding reporting procedures and other documentation requirements, the Agency **shall** refer to [[0-SPC-7003_specification_overview]].
