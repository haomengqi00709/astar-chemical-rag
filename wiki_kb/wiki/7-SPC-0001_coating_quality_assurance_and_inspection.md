# Protective Coating Quality Assurance and Inspection (7-SPC-0001)

Quality assurance and inspection are integral to ensuring that protective coatings meet the specified standards outlined in `7-SPC-0001 Protective Coating Specification`.

**Inspection Rights and Vendor Responsibility**
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, or their designated representative, reserves the right to inspect at any time during the coating process. This right to inspect does not relieve the Vendor of their responsibility to provide a properly inspected and acceptable protective coating.

**Visual Inspection and Defect Repair**
Each operation of surface preparation or coating application **must** be visually inspected for uniform quality and coverage. Any defects such as holidays (uncoated areas), variations in gloss or texture, or other imperfections **must** be repaired to provide uniform coverage and appearance.

**Quality Assurance Documentation Package**
The Vendor **must** provide a comprehensive Quality Assurance (QA) documentation package. This package **must** include a paint inspection report covering all painted items.

The paint inspection report **must** include the following details:
*   Total Dry Film Thickness (DFT) above the surface profile.
*   DFT measurements for each individual coat.
*   Number of coats applied.
*   Dates and times of application for each coat.
*   Name of the painting contractor.
*   Identification of the item(s) painted.
*   Substrate material.
*   Surface condition prior to preparation.
*   Details of surface preparation performed (including date and time).
*   Paint system(s) used.
*   Application method.
*   Conditions during application and drying of the paint film.
*   Location of application (e.g., shop/field, inside/outside, heated, etc.).

**DFT Acceptance Criteria**
SSPC PA-2 **will** be used as a guide for the acceptance of Dry Film Thickness (DFT) measurements.

**Pre-Application Requirements**
Copies of paint manufacturer’s data sheets must be provided to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics prior to paint application. When deemed necessary, an adhesion test patch will be carried out before applying paint/coating to a previously painted surface.

**Ongoing Inspection**
Each operation of preparation or coating application must be visually inspected for uniform quality and coverage.

**QA Documentation Package**
Vendor must provide a QA documentation package. The QA documentation package must include a paint inspection report covering the items painted. The paint inspection report must include: Total DFT (above surface profile), DFT measurements for each coat, Number of coats, dates and times of application, Name of painting contractor, Item(s) painted, Substrate material, Surface condition, Surface preparation (including date and time), Paint system(s) used, Application method, Conditions during application and drying of paint film, Location (i.e. shop/field, inside/outside, heated, etc.). SSPC PA-2 will be used as a guide for acceptance of DFT measurements.
