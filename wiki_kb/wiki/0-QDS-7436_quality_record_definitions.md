# Quality Record Definitions for Stainless Steel Tank (0-QDS-7436)

This section defines the abbreviations used for Quality Record requirements within the [[0-QDS-7436_stainless_steel_tank_quality_data_sheet_administration|Stainless Steel Tank Quality Data Sheet (0-QDS-7436)]]. These definitions are crucial for understanding the documentation requirements for various inspection and testing activities.

For general quality record definitions, refer to [[quality_record_definitions]].

**Definitions:**
*   **AP (As Prepared):** This designation means the quality record is required "As Prepared" by the Vendor. It indicates that the Vendor is responsible for preparing and maintaining this record, which will be available for review.
*   **FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package):** This designation means the quality record is required to be "Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package." This implies that the record **must** be part of the final documentation package submitted by the Vendor to Chemetics.
