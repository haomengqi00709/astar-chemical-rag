# Chemetics Surveillance Level for 0-QDS-7404-5

For Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7404-5, pertaining to the Electrostatic Precipitator Shell & Head Sections-FRP, the surveillance level assigned to Chemetics is specified.

Surveillance by Chemetics **shall** be conducted to Level 2.0. This level of surveillance indicates the degree of oversight and inspection Chemetics will perform during the manufacturing and quality control processes.

All surveillance activities undertaken by Chemetics **must** be carried out as explicitly set out within the detailed requirements of this Quality Data Sheet. This ensures that all critical stages of production and testing are monitored according to the project's specific quality objectives.
