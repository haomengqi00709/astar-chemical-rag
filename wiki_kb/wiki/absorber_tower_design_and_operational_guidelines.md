# Absorber Tower Design and Operational Guidelines

This page compiles critical design and operational guidelines for the Absorber Tower, drawing from document [[1-cal-7567-r1_absorber_tower_calculation_document_details]] and [[4-pds-7567-r1_absorber_tower_process_data_sheet_document_details]].

**General Requirements:**
*   **Mandatory Requirement:** User is to consult with Technology Manager prior to modifying parameters not hilited in Blue.
*   The tower operates in a counter-current flow configuration.
*   The primary gas absorbed in the tower is SO3.
*   The standard packing used is 2 in. CERAMIC INTALOX SADDLES.

**Design Criteria and Calculations:**
*   Suggested velocity is based on the self venting criteria.
*   Superficial volumetric flux **must** be < 0.3.
*   A safety factor of 10% **shall** be added when only supplying a tower and there are guarantees on the pressure drop.
*   Maximum Differential Pressure (DP) **must** be specified when adding a 10% safety factor.
*   **Packing Exclusion from Calculations:**
    *   Packing around the distributor is not included in mass transfer/pressure drop calculations.
    *   Packing on top of the distributor is not included in mass transfer/pressure drop calculations.
    *   Packing 1 ft directly below the distributor is not included in mass transfer/pressure drop calculations.

These guidelines are essential for ensuring the safe and efficient operation and modification of the Absorber Tower. For further details on process design, refer to [[process_design_criteria_1_prc_0001]].
