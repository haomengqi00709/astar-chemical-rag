# FRP Vessel Inspection and Testing (4-SPC-0042)

This section outlines the inspection and testing requirements for FRP vessels, primarily following [[ASME_RTP-1_standard_for_reinforced_thermoset_plastic_corrosion_resistant_equipment]] Part 6 - Inspections and Tests, with specific criteria and exceptions. These activities are crucial for ensuring the [[quality_surveillance]] and integrity of the fabricated equipment before it is put into service.

**General Inspection and Testing**:
*   Inspections and tests **shall** substantially be in accordance with RTP-1 Part 6 - Inspections and Tests. This ensures a consistent and recognized standard for evaluating the quality of the FRP fabrication.

**Barcol Hardness Testing**:
*   The minimum number of Barcol test readings in any vessel area **shall be** as recommended in [[ASTM_D_2583_barcol_hardness_test]], or as agreed between the fabricator and Inspector. Barcol hardness testing is a key method for assessing the degree of cure of the resin laminate, indicating proper polymerization and mechanical properties.
*   The locations of the Barcol test readings **shall cover** all major vessel areas and be at the Inspector's discretion, ensuring comprehensive coverage of the vessel's structural integrity.

**Specialized Tests**:
*   Laminate burnout tests, which determine the glass content of the laminate, are required only when specifically requested. This test provides critical data on the structural composition of the FRP.
*   Proof tests, which involve subjecting the vessel to a specified pressure to verify its structural integrity, are required only when specifically requested.
*   [[acoustic_emission_testing]], a non-destructive examination technique used to detect and locate active flaws in materials under stress, are required only when specifically requested. These tests are particularly useful for detecting growing defects in composite structures.

**Visual Defects**:
*   The maximum allowable [[visual_inspection_requirements]] defects **shall be** in accordance with Table 1. “Maximum Allowable Visual Defects Quality Level 2”, and **not** Table 6-1 in RTP-1. This specific deviation from RTP-1 Table 6-1 sets a stricter or different standard for acceptable visual imperfections, reflecting Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' quality expectations.

**Barcol Hardness Testing**
*   The minimum number of Barcol test readings in any vessel area will be as recommended in ASTM D 2583, or as agreed between the fabricator and Inspector.
*   The locations of the Barcol test readings will cover all major vessel areas and be at the Inspector's discretion.

**Specialized Tests**
Laminate burnout tests, proof tests, and acoustic emission tests are required only when specifically requested.

**Visual Defects**
The maximum allowable visual defects will be in accordance with Table 1. “Maximum Allowable Visual Defects Quality Level 2”, which supersedes Table 6-1 in RTP-1.
