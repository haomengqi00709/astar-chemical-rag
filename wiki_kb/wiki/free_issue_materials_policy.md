# Free Issue Materials Policy

The Free Issue Materials Policy, a key aspect of [[order_specific_conditions_for_purchase]], governs the provision and management of materials supplied by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics to a Vendor free of charge for incorporation into Goods and Services. This policy ensures clarity on ownership, responsibility, and risk associated with such materials.

**Definition and Ownership:**
*   Free Issue Materials are defined as materials provided by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics to the Vendor free of charge.
*   These materials are specifically intended for incorporation into the Goods and Services being supplied by the Vendor.
*   Free Issue Materials **remain the property of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics** at all times.

**Vendor Responsibilities:**
Upon receiving Free Issue Materials, the Vendor assumes specific responsibilities:
*   The Vendor **must** protect Free Issue Materials from loss, damage, or unauthorized use.
*   The Vendor **must** maintain Free Issue Materials in the condition in which they were provided.
*   The Vendor **shall** only use Free Issue Materials for their designated purpose: incorporation into the Goods and Services as specified in the order.

**Risk and Liability:**
*   Any loss, waste of, or damage to the Free Issue Materials after they have been provided to the Vendor is at the Vendor's risk. This emphasizes the Vendor's responsibility for safeguarding the materials once in their possession.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ risks include loss of or damage to any Free Issue Materials until the Vendor has received and accepted them. This clarifies the handover point of risk.

**Management of Surplus Materials:**
*   Any surplus Free Issue Materials remaining after the completion of the Goods and Services **shall** be dealt with as instructed by the Project Buyer or Lead Engineer. This ensures proper disposition and accountability for unused materials.

**Insurance Requirements:**
*   The Vendor **must** include the amount advised by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for the replacement of any materials provided by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics in the insurance taken out by the Vendor for loss of or damage to the Goods and Services. This ensures that the value of Free Issue Materials is covered by the Vendor's insurance, protecting Aker Kvaerner Chemetics against financial loss.

Free Issue Materials remain the property of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. The Vendor is responsible for protecting and maintaining Free Issue Materials in the condition in which they were provided, and must only use them for incorporation into the Goods and Services.

Any loss, waste of, or damage to the Free Issue Materials after they have been provided to the Vendor is at the Vendor's risk. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ risks include loss of or damage to any Free Issue Materials until the Vendor has received and accepted them. Any surplus Free Issue Materials will be dealt with as instructed by the Project Buyer or Lead Engineer.

If Aker Kvaerner Chemetics provides free issue materials, the insurance taken out by the Vendor for loss of or damage to Goods and Services must include an amount advised by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics to cover the replacement of any such materials.
