# Shipping Notice Requirements (0-SPC-5004)

Vendors **must** fax or email a shipping notice twenty-four hours in advance of readiness for shipment.

**Required Information in Shipping Notice:**
The shipping notice **will** contain the following details:
*   Address of the factory
*   Contact name
*   Phone number
*   Fax number
*   Email address
*   Number of packages in shipment
*   Weights and dimensions (kg & cm) of each package
*   A brief description of packing (e.g., pallets, box, pails)
*   A brief description of any special handling instructions
*   Material Safety Data Sheets for hazardous goods (refer to [[hazardous_materials_management_overview]])
*   The [[0-SPC-5004_packing_list_requirements|packing list]] for each package

**Submission Details:**
Shipping notices **must** be faxed or emailed to the Logistics Group:
*   **Phone:** 604 734-1200
*   **Fax:** 604 737-4408
*   **Email:** logistics@kvaerner.com

**Shipping Arrangements:**
Upon receipt of the shipping notice, [Company Name] **will** provide the Vendor with the name of the carrier and the date of pickup of the goods. The Vendor **must** allow two business days for [Company Name] to complete shipping arrangements.
