# Conveyor Tensioner Adjustment

This page outlines the mandatory requirements for correctly adjusting the [[conveyor_tensioner_adjustment|take-up roll (tensioner)]] for [[conveying_equipment_mechanical_checkout|conveying equipment]]. The take-up roll **must** be adjusted correctly to maintain proper belt tension.
