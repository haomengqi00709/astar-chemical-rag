# Scrubber Venturi Internals Vendor Quality Assurance Requirements

This section details the mandatory quality assurance requirements for vendors supplying Scrubber Venturi Internals under Quality Data Sheet **0-QDS-7439**. Adherence to these requirements is crucial for ensuring the quality and integrity of the supplied components.

**Vendor Quality Plan:**
The Vendor **will** prepare a Quality Plan. This plan **must** be in accordance with the guidelines specified in [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment|0-SPC-7001: Vendor Quality Assurance]].
*   *Note:* The requirement for the Vendor to prepare a Quality Plan is conditional; the relevant cells should be deleted if a Quality Plan is not required for a specific procurement.

**Compliance:**
The Vendor's Quality Assurance system **will** comply with the specific requirements outlined in this Quality Data Sheet (0-QDS-7439). Furthermore, the Vendor's Quality Assurance system **will** also comply with the general requirements detailed in [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment|0-SPC-7001: Vendor Quality Assurance]].

These requirements ensure that the vendor's quality processes are aligned with Chemetics' standards and project-specific needs, contributing to the overall quality management of the Scrubber Venturi Internals project.
