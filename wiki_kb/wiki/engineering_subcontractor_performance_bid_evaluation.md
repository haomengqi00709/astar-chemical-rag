# Engineering Subcontractor Performance Bid Evaluation

The Performance Evaluation is a crucial step in assessing Engineering Subcontractor bids, particularly for significant engagements. This evaluation **will** be prepared using the template 0-TAB-XXXX-4: [Template Performance Evaluation] and is the responsibility of the [[discipline_lead_and_approver_roles|Discipline Lead]].

A Performance Evaluation is **required** for any subcontract with a value exceeding CAD 50,000. For efficiency, the [[project_engineer_pem_gate_review_responsibilities|Project Engineer]] may, at their discretion, direct that the [[engineering_subcontractor_performance_bid_evaluation|Performance Evaluation]] be conducted only on a pre-selected shortlist of Subcontractors.

The evaluation **will** include a summary of the last two Subcontracts awarded to each Subcontractor for similar services. This summary **will** encompass an assessment of the Subcontractor's quality performance, specifically detailing whether they met requirements concerning the [[0-dat-3001_project_schedule|schedule]] and technical specifications. Any instances of failure to meet these requirements **should** be thoroughly detailed within the evaluation.

For Subcontractors who have not performed work for Aker Kvaerner Chemetics within the preceding three years, a dedicated survey **will** be conducted. This survey **will** be carried out by either the [[discipline_lead_and_approver_roles|Discipline Chief Engineer]] or the [[discipline_lead_and_approver_roles|Discipline Lead]]. The scope and reporting format for this survey **will** adhere to the guidelines set out in 0-REP-XXXX: [Template Engineering Subcontractor Survey]. The survey document itself **will** be numbered using the same identification number as the original requisition. For detailed numbering rules, refer to [[document_number_format_and_components|Document Numbering]] (0-PRC-0002). Surveys conducted for multiple Subcontractors **will** be treated as sheets of a single document. The findings of this Subcontractor Survey **will** then be summarized within the [[engineering_subcontractor_performance_bid_evaluation|Performance Evaluation]].

Based on the performance assessment, the [[discipline_lead_and_approver_roles|Discipline Lead]] **will** rate each Subcontractor as:
*   Performance satisfactory
*   Performance satisfactory with listed qualifications
*   Performance unsatisfactory

Following its completion, the evaluator **will** publish the [[engineering_subcontractor_performance_bid_evaluation|Performance Evaluation]]. For publishing procedures, refer to [[document_publishing_methods|Publishing of Project Documents]] (0-PRC-0005). The final approval of the [[engineering_subcontractor_performance_bid_evaluation|Performance Evaluation]] **will** be provided by the [[discipline_lead_and_approver_roles|Discipline Chief Engineer]].
