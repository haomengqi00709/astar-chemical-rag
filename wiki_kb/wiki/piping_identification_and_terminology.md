# Piping Identification and Terminology

This page defines key terms and numbering conventions used in piping specifications and documentation.

**Pipeline Numbering**
Pipelines **shall** be assigned unique numbers. The structure and basis for these numbers are detailed in [[5-SPC-0001_table_2]]. An example pipeline number is 100-CDG-(V)TT4A-800101.

**Vacuum Service Designation**
Material specifications that are preceded by “(V)” (e.g., (V) TT4) indicate that the corresponding pipeline is in vacuum service.

**Piping Components and Connections**

*   **In-Line Items:** These are components mounted directly within the run of a pipeline. Examples include:
    *   Strainers
    *   Expansion joints
    *   In-line instruments such as magmeters and control valves

*   **HP (High Point Vent):** Refers to a vent connection located at a high point in the piping system, typically used for releasing trapped gases.

*   **LP (Low Point Drain):** Refers to a drain connection located at a low point in the piping system, typically used for draining liquids.

*   **Instrument Connections:** These are branch connections provided from a main pipeline specifically for the mounting of instruments. Examples include connections for pressure gauges and thermowells.

For general piping requirements, refer to [[5-spc-0001_pipe_specification]].

**Vacuum Service Indication**
Material specifications preceded by “(V)” (e.g., (V) TT4) indicate a line in vacuum service.

**Piping Components**
*   **In-Line Items:** Items mounted in the run of a pipeline. Examples include strainers, expansion joints, and in-line instruments such as magmeters and control valves.
*   **HP:** High Point Vent
*   **LP:** Low Point Drain
*   **Instrument Connections:** Connections provided as a branch from a pipeline for mounting an instrument. Examples include connections for pressure gauges and thermowells.
