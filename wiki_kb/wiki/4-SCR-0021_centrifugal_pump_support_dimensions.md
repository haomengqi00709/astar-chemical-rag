# Centrifugal Pump Support Dimensions (4-SCR-0021)

This page provides the detailed dimensional data for the centrifugal pump support system, as specified in document 4-SCR-0021. These dimensions are essential for the accurate fabrication and installation of the concrete foundation, anchor bolts, and grouting, ensuring proper alignment and stability of the pump.

**Elevation Data:**
*   **Underside of Equipment Elevation**: The vertical position of the lowest point of the pump equipment, measured in millimeters (mm). This is a key reference for setting the overall height of the installation.
*   **Top of Rough Concrete Elevation**: The vertical position of the top surface of the unfinished concrete foundation, measured in millimeters (mm). This elevation defines the base for the grout layer.
*   **Nominal Floor or Grade Elevation**: The reference vertical position for the surrounding floor or ground level, measured in millimeters (mm). This helps in integrating the pump foundation with the overall plant layout.

**Anchor Bolt Details:**
*   **Anchor Bolt 'AB' Diameter**: The diameter of the anchor bolts, specified in millimeters (mm). This dimension is critical for selecting appropriate bolts and drilling patterns.
*   **Number of Bolts**: The total count of anchor bolts required for the installation, specified in 'ea' (each).
*   **Anchor Bolt Material**: The specified material for the anchor bolts. This choice is crucial for corrosion resistance and strength.
*   **Nut Material**: The specified material for the nuts used with the anchor bolts, ensuring compatibility and secure fastening.

**Dimensional Layout (in mm):**
These dimensions define the physical layout and positioning of the pump and its anchoring system on the concrete pad. Refer to the associated equipment drawing (4-VEN-XXXX, Revision X) for a visual representation of these dimensions.
*   **Dimension 'A' - Pad Size**: One dimension of the concrete pad, in mm (See Note 2 on the drawing).
*   **Dimension 'B' - Pad Size**: The other dimension of the concrete pad, in mm (See Note 2 on the drawing).
*   **Dimension 'E1' - Bolt Position**: A dimension defining the position of an anchor bolt.
*   **Dimension 'E2' - Bolt Position**: Another dimension defining the position of an anchor bolt.
*   **Dimension 'F' - Bolt Position**: A dimension defining the position of an anchor bolt.
*   **Dimension 'F1' - Bolt Position**: Another dimension defining the position of an anchor bolt.
*   **Dimension 'G' - Datum Offset**: The offset from a primary datum point.
*   **Dimension 'H' - Equip. Anchor Height**: The height of the equipment's anchor point.
*   **Dimension 'P' - Bolt Projection**: The required projection of the anchor bolt above the concrete, including space for a double nut.
*   **Dimension 'Y' - Grout**: The thickness of the grout layer.

These dimensions are to be strictly followed during the [[equipment_erection_0_spc_6x94]] phase. For general guidelines on equipment dimensions and anchor details, refer to [[4-SCR-0004_equipment_dimensions_and_anchor_details]]. The basis for these support criteria is further elaborated in [[4-SCR-0021_centrifugal_pump_support_criteria_and_installation]].
