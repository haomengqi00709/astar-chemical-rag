# Equipment Maintenance and Access Design

All equipment design **will** take into consideration general access requirements for ongoing operation and maintenance.

**Removability and Serviceability:** Equipment and components subject to wear, corrosion, erosion, or any physically depleting effects, **will** be easily removable for changeover. Agitators **will** be removable from tanks without the need for [[0-prc-8502-r1_confined_space_entry|confined space entry]] and with adequate lifting access. Buildings, enclosures, and cabinets housing equipment **will** be designed considering all maintenance operations, including dismantlability and accessibility within.

**Service Facilities:** Permanent facilities for pumpout, steamout, purging, and flushing **will** be included on all equipment where necessary. Equipment **will** be designed so that safe isolation under a [[safe_work_permit_procedure_overview|permit system]] **may** be performed with ease.

**Access Requirements:** Permanent access to mechanical equipment **will** be provided based upon the requirement of regular access by operational and maintenance personnel. Access purposes include plant operation, inspection, maintenance, condition monitoring, isolation under a permit system, overhaul, and cleaning. The minimum size of access manways considered safe and practical for general access requirements is 600 mm. If tanks are too small in diameter to comply with the 600 mm manway size requirement, then top entry **will** be provided via a full diameter removable tank top. Permanent storage tanks **will** be provided with a minimum of one access manway and a roof vent large enough for air ventilation during [[0-prc-8502-r1_confined_space_entry|confined space entry]].

An access stairway **will** be provided to elevated equipment when:
*   Plant operating or maintenance personnel require access on a daily basis, or more regularly.
*   The particular item of equipment is critical to plant operation.
*   Scheduled maintenance or unscheduled repair requirements of the particular item of equipment are labour intensive.
*   Scheduled maintenance or unscheduled repair requirements of the particular item of equipment require heavy tools to be hand carried.

Ladder access **will** be provided when [[plant_layout_review_5_prc_0005|criteria from the Layout Review Procedure]] are met.

**Contaminant Collection:** Provision **will** be made for the collection of potential process contaminants, such as residual process fluids or hydraulic oils, in drip trays. Drip trays **will** have provision for subsequent removal through drain points.

**Guarding:** Guarding for rotating machinery **will** be accessible and designed to facilitate tachometer readings.

**Handling and Lifting:** Equipment Handling requirements are detailed in [[general_equipment_requirements_specification|4-SPC-0001: General Equipment Requirements]]. When necessary, lifting wells and landing platforms **will** be provided to facilitate unimpeded access to grade level. Monorail trolleys of more than 5 tonne capacity **will** have motorised lift and travel motions. Cranes of more than 2 tonne capacity **will** have motorised lift and travel motions.
