# FRP Tank Datasheet Template Administration

This page outlines the administrative details and usage guidelines for the Drawing Insert [Template-FRP Tank], identified by Client Document Number Standard-4-DST-XXXX-R1. This template is specifically designed for use by Chemetics, a Division of Aker Kvaerner Canada Inc., for projects involving Fiberglass Reinforced Plastic (FRP) tanks.

**Document Identification and Revisions**
The template is associated with the project title "Drawing Insert [Template-FRP Tank]" and is used for equipment with a Tag. No. [ ]. The current revision history includes:
*   **Revision 1:** Dated 37716.0, with a "Standard" status. This revision was prepared and checked by G. Schwab and approved by T. Ueki. Key updates in Revision 1 included the addition of hydrotest, location, and Quality Assurance (QA) requirements, while a separate external loads section was deleted.
*   **Revision 0.0:** Dated 37565.0, with a "Comments Requested" status. This initial version was also prepared and checked by G. Schwab and approved by T. Ueki.

For general document revision and status management, refer to [[document_revision_and_status_management_0_spc_6091]].

**Usage and Formatting Guidelines**
This datasheet template is intended for integration into an ACAD OTL (Object-Oriented Technology Library) drawing of the vessel. The contents of this datasheet are inserted directly into the drawing.
*   The OTL drawing, once complete, **must** be published, checked, and approved.
*   The datasheet itself **need not** be separately published, checked, and approved, as its content is incorporated into the approved drawing. For general guidelines on publishing project documents, see [[digital_document_publishing]].
*   **Mandatory Requirement:** Cells within the datasheet **must not** be merged. Merging cells can lead to problems during the insertion process into the ACAD OTL drawing.
*   This template is adaptable and **may be modified** for equipment with similar requirements but different specific details or materials. For information on document templates and their usage, refer to [[document_templates_and_usage]].

**Usage Guidelines:**
The datasheet is intended to be used by inserting its contents into an ACAD OTL drawing of the vessel. The OTL drawing itself is subject to publishing, checking, and approval processes. However, the datasheet itself does not require separate publishing, checking, or approval.

**Template Modification:**
This template is designed to be adaptable and may be modified for equipment with similar requirements but different details or materials.

**Formatting Note:**
It is critical not to merge cells within the datasheet, as merging cells causes problems with inserting the sheet into a drawing.
