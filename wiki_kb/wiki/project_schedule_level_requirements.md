# Project Schedule Level Requirements

The level of detail required for a project schedule is determined by project size and complexity.

For projects exceeding a threshold of CAD 3,000,000 or requiring more than 3,500 home-office hours, the schedule **will** be developed to the detailed levels described in this procedure.

For projects falling below either the CAD 3,000,000 financial threshold or the 3,500 home-office hours threshold, schedule levels **will** be developed at levels determined by the Project Manager. For these smaller projects, the schedules **must** be developed at levels determined by the Project Manager to be fit for the purpose. Furthermore, the schedule levels for projects below these thresholds **must** be approved by the Director of Projects.

The specific levels of detail, denoted by nomenclature, are further described in [[project_schedule_detail_levels]] and [[project_schedule_level_4_work_plans]].
