# 0-REP-8401 Chemical Safety Management

This page details the requirements for chemical safety management on site, as specified in document 0-REP-8401.

**Chemical Documentation and Labeling**
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Client **shall** maintain Material Safety Data Sheets (MSDS) for all chemicals present on site. All chemical containers **shall** be properly labeled and clearly visible. Chemical sampling points **shall** also be labeled correctly to ensure safe handling and identification.
