# Gas Rotating Equipment Process Design Conditions

This page details the process design conditions specified within the 4-PDS-XXXX-R1 "Template Gas Rotating Equipment — Datasheet". These conditions are crucial for the proper design and operation of gas rotating equipment. The datasheet mandates the inclusion of both DESIGN and ALTERNATE operating cases.

**General Process Design Parameters**
Each case (DESIGN and ALTERNATE) requires the following information:
*   **Case Description:** A brief description of the operating scenario.
*   **Design Basis:** The underlying principles or standards used for the design.
*   **Inlet and Outlet Conditions:** Specific parameters for both the inlet and outlet streams for the DESIGN case.
*   **Inlet and Outlet Conditions:** Specific parameters for both the inlet and outlet streams for the ALTERNATE case.

**Fluid Properties and Operating Parameters**
The following fluid properties and operating parameters must be specified for both inlet and outlet streams under DESIGN and ALTERNATE conditions:
*   **Fluid Description:** A general description of the fluid being handled.
*   **Composition Note 1:** Composition of the fluid, expressed in %v/v.
*   **Solids Content/Type:** Any solids present in the fluid and their characteristics.
*   **Flowrate Note 2:** Flowrate of the fluid, specified in kg/h.
*   **Minimum Flowrate:** The minimum expected flowrate, in kg/h.
*   **Flowrate:** Flowrate of the fluid, specified in Am3/h.
*   **Minimum Flowrate:** The minimum expected flowrate, in Am3/h.
*   **Temperature:** Operating temperature, in oC.
*   **Pressure:** Operating pressure, in kPa(a).
*   **Density:** Fluid density, in kg/m3.
*   **Compressibility Factor:** The compressibility factor of the gas.
*   **Viscosity:** Fluid viscosity, in cP.
*   **Specific Heat:** Specific heat capacity of the fluid, in kJ/kg·oC.
*   **Molecular Weight:** Molecular weight of the fluid, in kg/kmol.
*   **cp / cv Ratio:** The ratio of specific heat at constant pressure to specific heat at constant volume.

**Important Notes**
*   **Note 1.0:** States that Composition is given on a (dry/wet) basis, which must be clearly indicated.
*   **Note 2.0:** States that Flowrate is given on a wet basis.

These detailed specifications ensure that the equipment is designed to handle the full range of anticipated process conditions.
