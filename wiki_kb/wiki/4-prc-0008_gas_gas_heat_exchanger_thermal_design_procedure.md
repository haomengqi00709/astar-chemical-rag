# 4-PRC-0008 Gas/Gas Heat Exchanger Thermal Design Procedure

Procedure 4-PRC-0008 outlines the general guidelines for the thermal design of acid plant radial flow gas/gas heat exchangers. This procedure is integral to ensuring the efficient and safe operation of these critical components within chemical engineering processes.

The procedure primarily utilizes Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ in-house [[tgas_2_thermal_design_software|TGAS/2 program]] for its calculations and design functionalities. Basic mechanical data pertinent to gas/gas exchangers is also presented within this document.
