# Quench Venturi Inspection and Documentation Requirements

This page details the specific inspection and documentation requirements for the Quench Venturi, including the necessary quality records and the level of Chemetics surveillance for each activity. All listed items **must** be included in the Vendor's Inspection and Test (I&T) Plan.

**Required Inspections, Documentation, and Surveillance:**

*   **Mill Test Certificates:**
    *   Required as: FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package)
    *   Required in Vendor I&T Plan: Yes
    *   Chemetics Surveillance: Witness
    *   Refer to [[0-QDS-7411_material_and_welding_certifications]] for general requirements.

*   **Certificate of Compliance:**
    *   Required as: FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package)
    *   Required in Vendor I&T Plan: Yes
    *   Chemetics Surveillance: Witness
    *   Refer to [[0-QDS-7411_material_and_welding_certifications]] for general requirements.

*   **External Coating/Lining Inspection:**
    *   Required in Vendor I&T Plan: Yes
    *   Chemetics Surveillance: Witness, Hold
    *   Refer to [[0-QDS-7414_coating_and_lining_inspection]] for general coating and lining inspection guidelines.

*   **Internal Coating/Lining Inspection:**
    *   Required as: FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package)
    *   Required in Vendor I&T Plan: Yes
    *   Chemetics Surveillance: Witness, Hold
    *   Specific Requirement: A spark test **is required** for this inspection.
    *   Refer to [[0-QDS-7414_coating_and_lining_inspection]] for general coating and lining inspection guidelines.

*   **Dimensional Final Inspection:**
    *   Required as: FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package)
    *   Required in Vendor I&T Plan: Yes
    *   Chemetics Surveillance: Witness, Hold
    *   Refer to [[0-QDS-7411_final_inspections]] for general final inspection requirements.

*   **Contour & Finish of Welds:**
    *   Required in Vendor I&T Plan: Yes
    *   Chemetics Surveillance: Witness, Hold
    *   Refer to [[0-QDS-7411_non_destructive_examination_requirements]] for related NDE requirements.

*   **Surfaces Free From Defects:**
    *   Required in Vendor I&T Plan: Yes
    *   Chemetics Surveillance: Witness, Hold

*   **Cleanliness:**
    *   Required in Vendor I&T Plan: Yes
    *   Chemetics Surveillance: Witness, Hold

*   **Item Identification:**
    *   Required in Vendor I&T Plan: Yes
    *   Chemetics Surveillance: Witness, Hold

*   **Review QA Documentation:**
    *   Required in Vendor I&T Plan: Yes
    *   Chemetics Surveillance: Witness, Hold

*   **Packaging:**
    *   Required in Vendor I&T Plan: Yes
    *   Chemetics Surveillance: Witness
    *   Refer to [[0-QDS-7411_packing_and_shipping_requirements]] for general packing and shipping requirements.

*   **Packing List:**
    *   Required as: AP (As Prepared)
    *   Required in Vendor I&T Plan: Yes
    *   Chemetics Surveillance: Witness
    *   Refer to [[0-QDS-7411_packing_and_shipping_requirements]] for general packing and shipping requirements.

For general administrative details, vendor quality assurance, and quality record definitions, refer to [[0-QDS-7421_quality_data_sheet_administration]].
