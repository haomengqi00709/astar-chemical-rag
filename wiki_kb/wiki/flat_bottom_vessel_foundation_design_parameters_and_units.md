# Flat Bottom Vessel Foundation Design Parameters and Units

This page details the specific design parameters and their corresponding units for flat bottom vessel foundations, as typically referenced in specifications like [[flat_bottom_vessel_support_criteria_specification]]. These parameters are crucial for the accurate engineering and construction of vessel foundations.

**Key Dimensions and Notes:**
*   **Dimension 'A' - Pad Size:** Refers to Note 2 for specific details.
*   **Dimension 'P' - Bolt Projection:** Includes provision for c/w Double Nut.

**Mass Parameters:**
The following maximum mass values are specified in kilograms (kg):
*   Empty - Maximum Mass: kg
*   Flooded (Design) - Maximum Mass: kg
*   Operating - Maximum Mass: kg

**Load Parameters:**
Various load parameters are specified in kilonewtons (kN) or kilonewton-meters (kN-m):
*   Uplift due to Internal Pressure: kN (This represents a Total value)
*   Wind Base Shear: kN
*   Wind Base Moment: kN-m
*   Seismic Base Shear: kN
*   Seismic Base Moment: kN-m

**Elevation Parameters:**
Elevations are specified in millimeters (mm):
*   Underside of Equipment Elevation: mm
*   Top of Rough Concrete Elevation: mm
*   Nominal Floor or Grade Elevation: mm

**Anchor Bolt and Foundation Geometry:**
Detailed geometric parameters for anchor bolts and foundation cutouts are provided in millimeters (mm) or degrees (deg):
*   Anchor Bolt Circle Diameter: mm
*   Anchor Bolt 'AB' Diameter: mm
*   Number of Bolts: ea (each)
*   Dimension 'A' - Pad Size: mm
*   Angle 'E': deg
*   Angle 'F': deg
*   Angle 'G': deg
*   Dimension 'D' - Cutout Depth: mm
*   Dimension 'L' - Cutout Backspace: mm
*   Dimension 'M' - Cutout Width: mm
*   Dimension 'H' - Equip. Anchor Height: mm
*   Dimension 'P' - Bolt Projection: mm
*   Dimension 'Y' - Grout: mm

**Material Specifications:**
Specific material requirements are critical for the integrity of the foundation:
*   Anchor Bolt Material: This is a specified data point.
*   Nut Material: This is a specified data point.

For general equipment design criteria, refer to [[equipment_design_criteria_4_prc_0002]]. Further details on vessel mechanical design conditions can be found in [[template_vessel_mechanical_design_conditions]], and information on materials of construction is available in [[template_vessel_materials_of_construction]] and [[material_selection]].
