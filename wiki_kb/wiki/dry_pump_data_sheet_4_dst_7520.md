# Dry Pump Data Sheet (99H10600-4-DST-7520)

The document 99H10600-4-DST-7520 R1, titled "Dry Pump 7520P," is a specific data sheet detailing the technical requirements and specifications for dry pumps designated with tag number 7520P-A/B within Project 99H10600. This data sheet serves as a critical reference for vendors supplying these specific pieces of equipment.

Data sheets like this one are fundamental in defining the technical scope of supply for equipment. They typically include:
*   **Performance Specifications:** Required flow rates, pressure, temperature, power consumption, and other operational parameters.
*   **Material Specifications:** Details on the materials of construction for various components to ensure compatibility with process fluids and environmental conditions.
*   **Design Standards:** Reference to applicable industry codes, standards, and internal engineering specifications that the equipment must comply with.
*   **Dimensions and Connections:** Key physical dimensions, nozzle sizes, flange ratings, and connection types.
*   **Instrumentation and Control Interfaces:** Requirements for integrated instrumentation, control signals, and compatibility with the plant's control system.
*   **Testing and Inspection Requirements:** Specific tests (e.g., hydrostatic, performance) and inspection points required during manufacturing and before shipment.

Vendors supplying dry pumps for Project 99H10600 **must** ensure that their Goods and Services are in strict accordance with the specifications outlined in this data sheet. This document is typically referenced in Text Section 1: Scope of Supply of the relevant Purchase Order.
