# Packed Tower Materials of Construction (4-PDS-XXXX-R2)

The Packed Tower Process Data Sheet (4-PDS-XXXX-R2) specifies the required materials of construction for various components of the packed tower. Proper material selection is crucial for ensuring the longevity, chemical resistance, and structural integrity of the equipment in its intended operating environment.

**Categories for Materials of Construction:**
The datasheet categorizes the materials based on the specific components of the packed tower system:
*   **Pump Tank:** Material for the tank associated with the packed tower's liquid circulation system.
*   **Internals:** General category for internal components not specifically listed elsewhere.
*   **Packing:** The material used for the packing elements within the tower.
*   **Collector Plates:** Material for the plates designed to collect liquid within the tower.
*   **Tower:** The primary material for the main shell of the packed tower.
*   **Distributor:** Material for the liquid distribution system at the top of the packing.
*   **Packing Support:** Material for the structure that supports the packing bed.
*   **Bed Limiter:** Material for the device that restricts the upward movement of the packing.

For general guidelines on material selection in chemical engineering applications, refer to [[material_selection]]. Specific material requirements for vessels can be found in [[template_vessel_materials_of_construction]].
