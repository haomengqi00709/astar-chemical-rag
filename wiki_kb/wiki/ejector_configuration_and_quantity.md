# Ejector Configuration and Quantity

This page describes how the quantity and operational status of ejector packages are specified, as referenced in the Template Ejector Datasheet (Document ID [[template_ejector_datasheet_administration]]).

**Quantity Specification:**
The quantity of ejectors required can be specified as either:
*   **Operating:** Units actively in use during normal operation.
*   **Installed Spare:** Units installed but kept in reserve for backup or maintenance purposes.
