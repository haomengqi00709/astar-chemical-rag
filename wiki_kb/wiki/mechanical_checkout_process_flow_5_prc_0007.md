# Mechanical Checkout Process Flow (5-PRC-0007)

The mechanical checkout process for piping, as outlined in [[mechanical_checkout_piping_5_prc_0007|5-PRC-0007]], involves a structured sequence of reviews and rectifications to ensure piping systems are installed correctly and are ready for commissioning.

**Initial Sub-contractor Review:**
During the construction phase, the sub-contractor is responsible for conducting an initial review, creating punch lists based on isometrics to ensure completeness of their work.

**Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Site Supervisor Review:**
Following the sub-contractor's initial review, the sub-contractor approaches the [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_site_supervisor_review|Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Site Supervisor]] for their review. The Site Supervisor visually inspects the installed piping against the latest isometrics and the contractor's punch list. Once satisfied with the work completion and the correctness of the punch list, the Site Supervisor notifies the Project Manager.

**Final Designer Review:**
A designer from the office then conducts a final, independent review. This involves a visual inspection of the piping against the latest isometrics and [[piping_and_instrumentation_diagram|P&ID]]s to confirm that the installation is in accordance with all construction documents. Any discrepancies or issues identified by the designer are documented as deficiencies on a set of isometrics.

**Deficiency Correction:**
The designer hands these identified deficiencies to the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Site Supervisor. The Site Supervisor then collaborates with a dedicated deficiency crew, supplied by the sub-contractor, to correct all identified deficiencies. This crew is also responsible for addressing any outstanding punch list items from the initial reviews.

**Post-Testing Outstanding Work:**
Outstanding work items, such as painting, insulating, and installing flange guards, are undertaken by the deficiency crew after critical pre-commissioning activities like cleaning, flushing, and hydrotesting have been completed.
