# 4-SPC-0025 Appendix B - Fabrication Tolerances Table

This page describes the content of the table referenced in Specification 4-SPC-0025, Appendix B.

The table, titled "Fabrication Tolerances," provides detailed dimensional and geometric tolerance requirements for various fabricated components made from High Silicon Austenitic Stainless Steel. It is an integral part of the quality control and acceptance criteria for equipment and piping covered by Specification 4-SPC-0025.

Specific sections within this table detail tolerances for:
*   Vessels and Tanks (Pages 18 & 19 of Appendix B)
*   Tower Trays and related internals
*   Shell and Tube Heat Exchangers (Page 20 of Appendix B)

These tolerances are critical for ensuring the proper fit, function, and structural integrity of the fabricated items. Fabricators **must** adhere to these specified limits.

Refer to [[4-SPC-0025_fabrication_tolerances]] for an overview of the general tolerance principles and specific requirements mentioned in the standard.
