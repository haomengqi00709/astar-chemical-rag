# Inline Filter Material Specifications

This page details the material specifications for the Template Inline Filter, as outlined in Document ID [[4-PDS-XXXX-R1_template_inline_filter_datasheet_administration]]. Proper material selection is crucial for ensuring the longevity, chemical compatibility, and structural integrity of the filter in its intended operating environment.

Materials of construction are specified for key components of the inline filter:

*   **Vessel**: The main housing or body of the filter. The material for the vessel must withstand the specified [[template_filter_datasheet_process_design_conditions#Pressure_Range|pressure range]] and [[template_filter_datasheet_process_design_conditions#Maximum_Temperature_Range|temperature range]], as well as be compatible with the fluid composition.
*   **Elements**: The filtration media or internal components responsible for separating solids from the fluid. The material for the elements is selected based on the [[template_filter_datasheet_process_design_conditions#Particle_Retention_Criteria_unit|particle retention criteria]], [[template_filter_datasheet_process_design_conditions#Filter_Media_Pore_Size_unit|filter media pore size]], and chemical resistance to the fluid.

The selection of materials also considers the [[template_filter_datasheet_process_design_conditions#Solids_Type|type of solids]] being handled and potential abrasive or corrosive effects. For a comprehensive understanding of the filter's design, refer to [[equipment_design_and_specification_4_prc_0003]] and [[material_selection]].
