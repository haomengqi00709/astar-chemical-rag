# Level Measurement - Contact and Switches

Contact-based level measurement devices and level switches are utilized for specific applications, including those requiring high reliability for safety functions.

**Capacitance Probes:**
Capacitance probes may be used where no alternative method is satisfactory, offering a solution for challenging applications.

**Magnetic-Coupled Float Level Gauges:**
Magnetic-coupled float level gauges may be used in preference to gauge glasses for fluids where solids build-up can occur, providing a more robust and reliable indication.
*   **Preferred Style:** The preferred style of level indicator used **will be** the sealed float-type.
*   **Construction:** This indicator **will be** enclosed within a stainless steel cage or material suitable for the process fluid. The sealed float **will be** magnetically coupled to the level indication assembly, which is attached to the side of the cage.
*   **Indication:** The indication assembly **will be** field adjustable to enable the scale to be viewed from the most convenient orientation. The indicator **will be** a series of "interlocked" (to prevent over-rotation) rotating flappers, contained within a hermetically sealed case. The indicator **will** range fully between tapping points.
*   **Materials and Connections:** Process wetted materials for magnetic level gauges **will be** 316 stainless steel minimum, unless the process fluid properties require the use of alternative materials. The connections to the vessel for magnetic level gauges **will be** flanged.
*   **Accessories:** Drain and vent valves **will be** fitted to all level gauges. Where magnetic level gauges are to be provided with a 4-20 mA transmitter, the transmitter **will be** located at the bottom of the gauge to allow accessibility.

**Level Switches:**
Level switch devices utilize various measurement methods (refer to [[level_measurement_general|Level Measurement - General]]).
*   **Shutdown Service:** Where switches are used for shutdown service, they **will be** mounted via independent process connections on the vessel to ensure redundancy and safety. Analogue devices are preferred for shutdown service due to their continuous monitoring capabilities.
*   **Electrical Specifications:** Electrical switches **will** have a contact rating of 240 V a.c/24 V d.c., 2A minimum with SPDT (Single Pole Double Throw) contacts.
