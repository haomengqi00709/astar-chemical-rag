# PDMS Catalogue

The Plant Design Management System (PDMS) Catalogue is a comprehensive database that stores detailed information about piping components available for use in a project. This catalogue is fundamental for efficient and accurate 3D modeling and design of piping systems.

**Content Requirements**
The PDMS Catalogue **shall** contain the following information for each piping component:
*   **Available Piping Component According to Material Specifications:** A list of components categorized by their material specifications, ensuring compatibility with project requirements.
*   **Pipe Component Geometry:** Detailed dimensional and geometric data for each component, enabling accurate 3D representation in the PDMS model.
*   **Pipe Component Item Description:** A clear and concise description of each component for identification and procurement purposes.
*   **Pipe Component Material Description:** A detailed description of the material used for each component, including grades and relevant standards.
*   **Connection Arrangement:** Information on how components connect to each other (e.g., flange types, weld preparations, threaded connections).

**Roles and Responsibilities**
The management and approval of the PDMS Catalogue involve specific engineering disciplines:
*   **Preparation:** The catalogue **shall** be prepared by the Computer-Aided Engineering (CAE) team.
*   **Checking:** The catalogue **shall** be checked by the Design discipline.
*   **Approval:** The catalogue **shall** be approved by the Mechanical discipline.

The PDMS Catalogue ensures that designers have access to standardized, accurate, and approved components, facilitating efficient design, material take-offs, and procurement processes.
