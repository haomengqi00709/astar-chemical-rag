# 0-PRC-2001 Procedure Overview

This procedure, identified as 0-PRC-2001, outlines the methodology for assigning personnel to positions of responsibility within a project. It serves as a foundational document for establishing project team structures and defining individual roles.

All project procedures inherently assign execution responsibilities to specific departments or design disciplines, ensuring a clear division of labor and accountability.

For a broader understanding of project execution responsibilities, refer to [[0-prc-2005_project_responsibility_document]]. This document is central to the firm's approach to [[assignment_of_project_responsibilities]].
