# Separation Equipment Mechanical Checkout Requirements

This page outlines the mandatory requirements for the mechanical checkout of separation equipment, designated by suffix 'K' (Filters, Mesh Pads, Candle Filters, Decanters, Settlers, Clarifiers). This process is a key part of the overall [[mechanical_equipment_checkout_procedure]].

**Required Documents:**
The following documents are mandatory for the checkout of separation equipment:
*   [[piping_and_instrumentation_drawings|P&ID]]
*   Vessel Outline drawing
*   [[equipment_data_sheets_4_dst_xxxx|Equipment data sheet]]
*   [[boiler_code_certification_requirements|Boiler code certification]]

**General Installation and Structural Checks:**
*   **Mandatory:** Nameplate data and record for Separation Equipment **must** match the outline drawing/data sheet information.
*   **Mandatory:** Nameplate tag for Separation Equipment **must** match tag drawing.
*   **Mandatory:** Base of Separation Equipment **must** be grouted, and [[equipment_grouting_requirements|grouting]] **must** be good.
*   **Mandatory:** Elevation height of Separation Equipment **must** be consistent with the design.
*   **Mandatory:** All anchor bolts **must** be installed and tight for Separation Equipment.
*   **Mandatory:** All anchor chair connections **must** be installed as per manufacturer information for Separation Equipment.
*   **Mandatory:** Package **must** be accessible on all sides for Separation Equipment.
*   **Mandatory:** Manway **must** be accessible for Separation Equipment.
*   **Mandatory:** Manway gasket material **must** be checked against the outline drawing for Separation Equipment.
*   **Mandatory:** Material **must** be checked against the outline drawing.

**Surface Treatment and Lining:**
*   **Mandatory:** Carbon steel of Separation Equipment **must** be coated per project specifications.
*   **Mandatory:** Check for gouges, cuts, scratches etc. on Separation Equipment paint.
*   **Mandatory:** [[coating_and_lining_inspection|Lining]] of Separation Equipment **must** be continuous.
*   **Mandatory:** Lining inserts of Separation Equipment **must** be sealed.
*   **Mandatory:** Lining seams of Separation Equipment **must** be smooth and continuous.

**Grounding:**
*   **Mandatory:** [[grounding_installation_details|Separation Equipment grounding cable]] connection **must** be tight.
*   **Mandatory:** Separation Equipment grounding cable **must** be connected to the main grounding loop.

**Lubrication (for Filter Presses):**
*   **Mandatory (for Filter Presses):** Reservoir **must** be filled to the line with oil.
*   **Mandatory (for Filter Presses):** Oil **must not** leak.
*   **Mandatory (for Filter Presses):** Oil **must** be the correct specification for the equipment.
*   **Mandatory (where required):** [[bearing_lubrication_and_inspection|Bearings]] of Separation Equipment **must** be lubricated.

**Overflow, Vents, and Drains:**
*   **Mandatory:** Overflow nozzle **must** be connected and bolts **must** be tight for Separation Equipment.
*   **Mandatory:** Overflow piping **must** be connected to ground or trench for Separation Equipment.
*   **Mandatory:** Both vent and drain **must** be clear of debris and open for Separation Equipment.
*   **Mandatory:** Screens **must** be provided where required for Separation Equipment vents/drains.
*   **Mandatory:** Drain valve **must** be connected and lever **must** have unobstructed rotation for Separation Equipment.

**Insulation:**
*   **Mandatory:** Tank [[insulation_design_and_specification_6_prc_0002|insulation material]] for Separation Equipment **must** be checked against specification.
*   **Mandatory:** Insulation thickness for Separation Equipment **must** be checked.
*   **Mandatory:** Insulation sheet **must** cover the surface, and all connections **must** be made for Separation Equipment.

**Internal Components (Plates, Filters, etc.):**
*   **Mandatory:** Correct number of plates, leafs, candles **must** be installed correctly and tightened for Separation Equipment.
*   **Mandatory:** Correct number of filter cloths, bags, cartridges etc. **must** be supplied and installed for Separation Equipment.
*   **Mandatory:** Correct [[gasket_material_selection|gasket]] **must** be installed and it **must** be clean and free of debris for Separation Equipment.
*   **Mandatory:** Dip legs, nozzle inserts, etc. **must** be installed as per outline drawing with correct gasket material and the correct design dimensions for Separation Equipment.
*   **Mandatory:** Internal [[piping_design_and_specification_5_prc_0003|piping]] **must** be installed and all connections **must** be tight for Separation Equipment.
*   **Mandatory:** [[piping_design_and_specification_5_prc_0003|Plastic piping]] **must** be cemented for Separation Equipment.

**Nozzles and Flanges:**
*   **Mandatory:** Nozzle size and orientation **must** match drawing for Separation Equipment.
*   **Mandatory:** Nozzle gasket and thickness **must** match outline drawing/data sheet for Separation Equipment.
*   **Mandatory:** Nozzles **must** be open from the inside of vessel and no foreign debris **must** be present for Separation Equipment.
*   **Mandatory:** Nozzle connection device (instrument, valve, etc) **must** be present for Separation Equipment.
*   **Mandatory:** Valve flange **must** be aligned to piping for Separation Equipment.
*   **Mandatory:** [[flange_bolting_requirements|Flange bolts]] **must** be adequately tightened for Separation Equipment.
*   **Mandatory:** Flange bolts **must not** be overtightened (i.e. damage to gasket) for Separation Equipment.
*   **Mandatory:** The nut has at least three rounds of pipe thread protruding past the nut for Separation Equipment.

**Electrical and Instrumentation:**
*   **Mandatory:** Electrical: See motor installation field check in [[electrical_systems_field_checkout_9_prc_0004|electrical checklist]].
*   **Mandatory:** [[cabinet_air_purge_requirements|Air purge to cabinets]] **must** be checked.
*   **Mandatory:** [[electrical_installation_details|Electrical connections]] **must** be checked and [[electrical_power_specifications|power supply]] confirmed.
*   **Mandatory:** [[instrumentation_and_control_design_criteria_8_prc_0002|Instrumentation]] **must** match the manufacturer’s documentation.
