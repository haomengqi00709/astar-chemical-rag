# Aluminium Bus Weld Quality Requirements

The Aluminium Bus Welding Standard (9-SPC-0001) mandates specific quality criteria for all welds to ensure structural integrity and performance.

**General Weld Appearance and Integrity**
Each weld **shall** be uniform in width and size throughout its full length. Each layer of welding **shall** be smooth and free of slag inclusions, cracks, porosity, lack of fusion, and undercut, except to the extent permitted in the referenced codes. The cover pass **shall** be free of coarse ripples, irregular surfaces, non-uniform bead patterns, high crowns, and deep ridges or valleys between beads.

**Butt Welds**
Butt welds **shall** be slightly convex and of uniform height. They **shall** have full penetration and no lack of sidewall fusion. To minimize distortion, welding of butt joints **shall** alternate from both sides wherever practical.

**Fillet Welds**
Fillet welds **shall** be deposited in not less than two layers, as far as is practicable. Fillet weld surfaces **shall** be free from marked irregularities, and their dimensions **shall** be in accordance with the approved drawing.

**Permissible Porosity**
The following limits for permissible porosity apply to any 6-inch length of weld:
*   4 x 1/16 inch diameter pores in each square inch of weld.
*   15 x 1/32 inch diameter pores in each square inch of weld.
*   60 x 1/64 inch diameter pores in each square inch of weld.
*   A similar proportional combination of the above is also permissible.

These porosity limits are critical for ensuring the structural soundness of the welds and are subject to [[9-SPC-0001_aluminium_bus_welding_inspection_and_nde|Non-destructive Examination]] as per [[ASME_section_v_non_destructive_examination|ASME Section V]].

**General Weld Appearance and Integrity**
Each run of weld metal **shall** be completely fused. The cover pass **shall** be free of coarse ripples, irregular surface, non-uniform bead patterns, high crown, and deep ridges or valleys between beads.

**Buttweld Specifics**
Buttwelds **shall** be slightly convex, of uniform height, have full penetration, and no lack of sidewall fusion.

**Fillet Weld Specifics**
Fillet welds **shall** be deposited in not less than two layers, as far as is practicable. Fillet weld surfaces **shall** be free from marked irregularities. Fillet weld dimensions **shall** be in accordance with the approved drawing.

**Permissible Porosity**
The following permissible porosity limits apply to each square inch of weld, and the porosity chart applies to any 6 inch length of weld:
*   4 x 1/16 inch diameter pores.
*   15 x 1/32 inch diameter pores.
*   60 x 1/64 inch diameter pores.
*   A similar proportional combination of the above is also permissible.
