# Electrical Vendor Document Review

This page outlines the process for reviewing vendor documentation for electrical equipment, including coordination with mechanical and structural reviews, to ensure compliance with project requirements.

**General Vendor Document Review**
The vendor document review **will** be done in accordance with Table 2. The Vendor Document Requirements (VDR) form issued with the purchase order **will** be used to check the documentation submitted for review. Procedure 0-PRC-0007, "Review of Vendor Documentation", **must** be followed for vendor document review.

**Mechanical Vendor Document Review**
Vendor document review – mechanical **will** be done in accordance with Table 2. Vendor documents and the VDR form for mechanical review **will** be the responsibility of others. During mechanical review, vendor documents **will** be checked for electrical information and compliance with project requirements. The electrical department **will** review and comment as per procedure 0-PRC-0007 for mechanical vendor document review.

**Structural Vendor Document Review**
Vendor document review – structure **will** be done in accordance with Table 2. Vendor documents and the VDR form for structure review **will** be the responsibility of others. During structure review, vendor documents **will** be checked for electrical information and compliance with project requirements. An assigned individual in the electrical department **will** review and comment as per procedure 0-PRC-0007 for structure vendor document review.

**Document Metadata**
*   **Document IDs:** 9-VDR-9001 (T/R, CPU), 9-VEN-99XX (ex. T/R, CPU), 4-VEN-1068 (Mechanical), 3-VEN-6328 (Structure)
*   **Primary Usage:** Procurement (T/R, CPU), Design (others)
*   **Roles:** E, QA
