# Standard ANSI Piping Connections for Instrumentation (5-LST-0011)

The Standard ANSI Piping Connections for Instrumentation (5-LST-0011) document provides standardized guidelines for process piping connection sizes for various instrument types, based on the project's [[5-spc-0001_pipe_specification_instrumentation_design]].

**Application and Variances:**
*   This document will provide the PROCESS PIPING connection sizes for the various instrument types.
*   The actual Instrument fitting size may vary from the Standard Process Connection.
*   Where possible, Instruments will be specified to mate to these standards.
*   Where it is impractical to provide instruments that mate to these standards, [[8-dtl-xxxx_instrument_installation_details]] will specify the appropriate adapters required.
*   Where the Field Instrument Engineer (FIE) is unable to provide instruments that mate directly to the sizes as listed in the Standard ANSI Piping Connections for Instrumentation, they **shall immediately inform** the Instrument Engineer of the variance.
*   The Instrument Engineer **shall immediately inform** the Detail Design Engineer (DDE) of any variances.
*   The DDE will provide the necessary adapters on the [[8-dtl-xxxx_instrument_installation_details]] to complete the installation.

**Usage in Design:**
The FIE will use the [[5-spc-0001_pipe_specification_instrumentation_design]] and the Standard ANSI Piping Connections for Instrumentation in the design of the field instruments. Process connection sizes for [[8-dst-xxxx_instrument_specification_sheets]] will be obtained from the Piping Specifications and this spreadsheet.
