# Sulphuric Acid Storage Tank Foundation Requirements (4-SPC-0046)

This section details the requirements for the foundation of sulphuric acid storage tanks, as outlined in specification 4-SPC-0046.

**Foundation Construction**
The foundation for the tank is to be constructed by other parties, in accordance with a separate, dedicated specification. Generally, the foundation **will** consist of well-compacted granular fill. It **will** also incorporate a concrete wall pit located below the bottom outlet nozzles. Copies of the detailed foundation drawings may be obtained upon request.

**Fabricator's Responsibilities Regarding Foundation**
The tank fabricator **will** be responsible for checking the foundation levels both before and during the erection process. Any changes or deviations from the established datum **must** be reported by the tank fabricator. Furthermore, any damage caused to the surface of the foundation **will** be repaired by the tank fabricator at their own expense. These repairs **must** match the original foundation in terms of both design and materials.

**Foundation Tolerances**
All tolerances related to the plumbness, roundness, peaking, banding, and the overall foundations of the tank **will** be in accordance with [[API_650_welded_steel_tanks|API 650 Paragraph 5.5]].
