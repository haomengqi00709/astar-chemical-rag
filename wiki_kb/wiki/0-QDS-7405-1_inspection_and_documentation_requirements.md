# Blower Quality Data Sheet Inspection and Documentation Requirements

This page details the comprehensive inspection, testing, and documentation requirements for the Blower Package, as specified in Document ID 0-QDS-7405-1. These activities are subject to Chemetics' surveillance levels as outlined in [[0-QDS-7405-1_chemetics_surveillance_level]] and require specific quality records as defined in [[0-QDS-7405-1_quality_record_definitions]].

**General Requirements:**
*   **Material Verification:** This is a required activity.

**Specific Inspection and Testing Activities:**

*   **Mill Test Certificates:** Required as FP and for Chemetics Witness.
*   **Vibration Testing:**
    *   Required for 100% of Impeller blades.
    *   Required as FP and for Chemetics Witness and Hold.
*   **Static Balancing:**
    *   Required for Impeller & couplings.
    *   Required as FP and for Chemetics Witness and Hold.
*   **Dynamic Balancing:**
    *   Required for Impeller, rotor & couplings.
    *   Required as FP and for Chemetics Witness and Hold.
*   **Overspeed Testing:**
    *   Required for Impeller.
    *   Required as FP and for Chemetics Witness and Hold.
*   **Hydrostatic Testing of Main Casing:**
    *   **Mandatory Requirement:** **Shall** be Per API-617.
    *   Required as FP and for Chemetics Witness.
*   **Mechanical Run Test:**
    *   **Mandatory Requirement:** **Shall** be Per API-617.
    *   Required as FP and for Chemetics Witness and Hold.
*   **Bearing Inspection:**
    *   Required Post test.
    *   Required as FP and for Chemetics Witness and Hold.
*   **Electrical Testing for Motor:**
    *   Required As specified.
    *   Required as FP and for Chemetics Witness and Hold.
*   **External Coating of CS for Motor:** Required as FP and for Chemetics Witness.
*   **Run Test for Gearbox:**
    *   **Mandatory Requirement:** **Shall** be Per API-613.
    *   Required as FP and for Chemetics Witness.
*   **External Coating of CS for Gearbox:** Required as FP and for Chemetics Witness.

**Final Inspection Activities:**

*   **Dimensional Final Inspection:** Required as FP and for Chemetics Witness and Hold.
*   **Surfaces Free From Defects Final Inspection:** Required as FP and for Chemetics Witness and Hold.
*   **Cleanliness Final Inspection:** Required for Chemetics Witness and Hold.
*   **External Coating of CS Final Inspection:** Required as FP and for Chemetics Witness.
*   **Control Panel Final Inspection:** Required as FP and for Chemetics Witness.
*   **IGV Operation Thru Range Final Inspection:** Required as FP and for Chemetics Witness and Hold.
*   **Sound Attenuation Housing Final Inspection:** Required for Chemetics Witness.

**Documentation Review and Packaging:**

*   **Review QA Documentation:** Required for Chemetics Witness and Hold.
*   **Packaging:** Required for Chemetics Witness and Hold.
*   **Packing List:** Required as AP and for Chemetics Witness.

For Non-Destructive Examination specifics, refer to [[0-QDS-7405-1_non_destructive_examination_requirements]]. The "Witness" and "Hold" points indicate critical stages where Chemetics' presence or approval is mandatory before proceeding. This process is aligned with [[vendor_inspection_release]] procedures.
