# Refractory Brick Lining Materials and Supply (4-SPC-0082)

This section of specification 4-SPC-0082 details the requirements for the supply of materials and consumables for refractory brick linings. The Vendor **shall** supply all necessary materials and consumables, including but not limited to: refractory brick, insulating brick, castable, mortar, ceramic paper, ceramic fibre (e.g., for expansion joints), and anchors.

All materials **will** be tested by the Vendor in accordance with [[ASTM_standards|ASTM]] requirements. The Vendor **will** also supply sufficient extra material to account for typical breakage and wastage during shipping and site installation.

Specific material conformity requirements include:
*   High alumina fire brick **will** conform to the latest edition of [[ASTM_c27|ASTM C27]].
*   Insulating brick **will** conform to the latest edition of [[ASTM_c115|ASTM C115]].

Mortar specifications are critical for the lining system's integrity:
*   Mortar for all fire brick and insulating brick **will** be air setting.
*   This mortar **will** have a minimum temperature limit of 1650ºC.
*   Fire brick mortar **will** be phosphate bonded, high alumina mortar, or an approved equal.
*   Insulating brick mortar **will** be alumina/silica mortar or an approved equal.

Burner inlet nozzle rings **will** be recommended by the Vendor. Additional material requirements may be specified on equipment drawings.

Data regarding specific material properties and quantities may be available in [[4-SPC-0082_table_1]].
