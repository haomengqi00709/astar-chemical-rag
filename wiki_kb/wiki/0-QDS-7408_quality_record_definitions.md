# Preheat Furnace Quality Record Definitions

For the Preheat Furnace Package, specific abbreviations are used to define the requirement for quality records:

*   **AP (As Prepared):** This designation indicates that the quality record **must** be prepared by the Vendor.
*   **FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package):** This designation indicates that the quality record **must** be included as part of the final Quality Assurance Documentation Package submitted by the Vendor.

These quality record definitions are associated with a value of 4.0 in relevant quality documentation. Adherence to these definitions is crucial for maintaining comprehensive and traceable quality records for the project.

For general information on quality records and documentation, refer to [[quality_records_and_documentation]] and [[document_control_procedure_0_prc_0007]].
