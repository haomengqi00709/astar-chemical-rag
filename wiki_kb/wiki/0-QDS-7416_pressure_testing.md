# Pressure Testing (0-QDS-7416)

For the Plate & Frame Heat Exchanger project (**0-QDS-7416**), hydrostatic testing is a mandatory requirement to verify the pressure integrity of the equipment.

*   **Hydrostatic Test:** This test is required as **FP** (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package). Chemetics surveillance for the hydrostatic test is **Complete**.
*   The hydrostatic test **must** be performed on Side 1 & side 2 of the heat exchanger.

For definitions of **FP**, refer to [[0-QDS-7416_quality_record_definitions]]. Details on Chemetics' surveillance level can be found in [[0-QDS-7416_chemetics_surveillance_level]].
