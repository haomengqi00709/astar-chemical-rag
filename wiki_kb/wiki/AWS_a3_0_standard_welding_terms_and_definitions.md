# AWS A3.0 Standard Welding Terms & Definitions

AWS A3.0 is the American Welding Society's standard for welding terms and definitions. This document provides a comprehensive glossary of terms used in the welding industry, ensuring consistent communication and understanding across various welding processes, applications, and documentation.

This standard is referenced in [[4-SPC-0014_referenced_codes_and_definitions]] and implicitly supports the understanding of terminology used throughout [[4-SPC-0014_weld_quality_and_finishing]] and other sections of document 4-SPC-0014, which governs the welding and fabrication of Columbium Stabilized High Nickel Austenitic stainless steel.

For precise definitions of welding-related terms, refer to the latest edition of AWS A3.0.
