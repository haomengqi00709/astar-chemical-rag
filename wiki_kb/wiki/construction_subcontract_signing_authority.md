# Construction Subcontract Signing Authority

The authority to sign Construction Subcontract Agreements is strictly delegated based on the monetary value of the subcontract. This ensures appropriate levels of review and approval for financial commitments made by the firm.

**Delegation of Authority:**
The Construction Subcontract Agreement **must** be signed by personnel authorized under Section 6 of the [[construction_subcontract_issuance_procedure_0_prc_6005]]. The specific authorization levels are as follows:

*   **Manager of Construction and Estimating:** Authorized to sign Construction Subcontract Agreements for values less than CAD 500,000.
*   **Director of Projects:** Authorized to sign Construction Subcontract Agreements for values less than CAD 2,000,000.
*   **President:** Authorized to sign Construction Subcontract Agreements for values greater than CAD 2,000,000.

**Documentation Requirements for Signing:**
When presenting a paper copy of the [[construction_subcontract_agreement_template_and_content]] for signature, the Subcontract Administrator **must** ensure that it is accompanied by specific supporting documentation. The signed paper copy **must** be presented together with a copy of the approved [[construction_subcontractor_evaluation_and_approval_0_prc_6003]]. This ensures that the signing authority has access to the evaluation and approval record of the selected subcontractor, confirming due diligence in the selection process.

**Final Issuance:**
Once the Construction Subcontract Agreement has been signed by the appropriately authorized personnel, the Subcontract Administrator will forward the signed and authorized paper copy to Document Control. Document Control is then responsible for the official issuance of the Construction Subcontract to the Construction Subcontractor, as per the procedures outlined in [[control_of_project_documents_0_prc_0006]].
