# Quality Data Sheets (QDS)

Quality Data Sheets (QDS) are integral documents used to specify and track quality assurance requirements for equipment and materials. They play a crucial role in ensuring that all procured and fabricated items meet the required quality standards.

**Integration into Procedures**
Quality Data Sheets (QDS) were formally added to the 4-PRC-0003 procedure in Revision 1, signifying their importance in the overall equipment documentation and quality management process. For a broader overview of equipment quality assurance, refer to [[equipment_quality_assurance]].
