# Sheet / Section Number Guidelines

The Sheet / Section Number is an optional component of the document numbering system, used to differentiate individual parts of a multi-part document, as specified in [[document_numbering_procedure_0_prc_0002]].

**Usage:**
The Sheet / Section Number is utilized where necessary to break a document into a number of distinct sheets or sections. This is particularly relevant for large drawings, specifications, or reports that are divided for clarity or ease of handling.

**Omission:**
If only a single sheet or section exists for a document, the Sheet / Section Number shall be omitted from the document number. It is not required for documents that are inherently single-part.

**Format:**
In the full document number format (e.g., `98C12300-4-DTL-7801-**5**-R2`), the Sheet / Section Number is the fifth segment.

These guidelines ensure that documents are appropriately segmented and identified, contributing to the overall clarity and organization of the [[document_number_format_and_components]].
