# Chemetics Surveillance Level for 0-QDS-7440

This page specifies the level of surveillance to be conducted by Chemetics for the scope covered by Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7440.

**Surveillance Level:**
Surveillance by Chemetics **shall** be conducted to Level 0.0. This level of surveillance indicates the degree of oversight and inspection Chemetics will perform during the manufacturing and quality control processes.

**Scope of Surveillance:**
The specific activities and details of surveillance by Chemetics **shall** be as set out within this Quality Data Sheet (0-QDS-7440). This ensures that all critical aspects of the project's quality are monitored according to Chemetics' standards.
