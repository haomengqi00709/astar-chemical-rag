# Pipe Support Specification (5-SPC-0006)

The Pipe Support Specification, identified as 5-SPC-0006, is a critical document that defines the technical requirements for the design, fabrication, and installation of all pipe supports. This specification ensures that piping systems are adequately supported to manage loads, vibrations, and thermal expansion, maintaining their structural integrity and operational safety.

**Content Requirements**
The Pipe Support Specification **shall** contain the following detailed information:
*   **Pipe Support Numbering Conventions:** Standardized methods for assigning unique identification numbers to pipe supports.
*   **Pipe Support Design:** Guidelines and requirements for the structural design of pipe supports, including load calculations and material selection.
*   **Pipe Support Fabrication:** Requirements for the manufacturing processes, welding, and quality control of pipe support components.
*   **Pipe Support Installation:** Procedures and guidelines for the proper installation of pipe supports in the field.
*   **Chemetics Approved Manufacturers and Model Numbers:** A list of approved manufacturers and their specific model numbers for standard pipe support components.

**Roles and Responsibilities**
The preparation, checking, and approval of the Pipe Support Specification are assigned to specific disciplines:
*   **Preparation:** The document **shall** be prepared by the Mechanical discipline.
*   **Checking:** The document **shall** be checked by the Design discipline.
*   **Approval:** The document **shall** be approved by the Mechanical discipline.

This structured approach ensures that all pipe supports are correctly specified, designed, fabricated, and installed, contributing significantly to the overall safety and reliability of the piping system.
