# Documentation and Records for Stabilized Austenitic Stainless Steel Fabrication (4-SPC-0014)

This page outlines the mandatory documentation and record-keeping requirements for Columbium Stabilized High Nickel Austenitic stainless steel fabrications, as specified in document 4-SPC-0014.

**Fabricator's Report and Record Sets**
*   The Fabricator shall furnish a report containing all the data pertaining to each vessel or item.
*   Prior to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' final inspection of each unit, the Fabricator shall have prepared in finished form three sets of records, excluding final test data.

**Required Documentation**
The documentation package shall include, but is not limited to:
*   Material mill test reports.
*   Nameplate rubbing.
*   Hydrostatic test report.
*   A copy of all inspection and test reports.

**Assembly and Collection of Documentation**
*   Reports, together with final test data, shall be assembled into three complete sets for each item.
*   In no case shall a documentation package pertain to more than one vessel or item number.
*   The Inspector will collect the appropriate number of documentation packages for each item tested at the time of witnessing the final tests and certifying the correctness of forms.

For general definitions of quality records, refer to [[quality_record_definitions]].
