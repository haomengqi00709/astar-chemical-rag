# Inspection and Documentation Requirements for MV Switchgear & DC Control Power Supply

For the Medium Voltage Switchgear & DC Control Power Supply project, comprehensive documentation review is a critical part of the quality assurance process. This includes reviewing the Vendor's Quality Assurance (QA) documentation and all test reports.

**Documentation Review Requirements:**
*   **Review QA Documentation:**
    *   **Quality Record:** [[0-QDS-7901_quality_record_definitions|AP]]
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance:** [[0-QDS-7901_chemetics_surveillance_level|Witness]], [[0-QDS-7901_chemetics_surveillance_level|Hold]]
*   **Review Test Reports:**
    *   **Quality Record:** [[0-QDS-7901_quality_record_definitions|FP]]
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance:** [[0-QDS-7901_chemetics_surveillance_level|Witness]], [[0-QDS-7901_chemetics_surveillance_level|Hold]], [[0-QDS-7901_chemetics_surveillance_level|Complete]]

These reviews ensure that all required inspections and tests have been properly conducted and documented, and that the results meet project specifications.
