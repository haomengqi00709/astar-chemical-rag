# Anchor Bolt and Grouting Requirements for Plate & Frame Heat Exchanger (4-SCR-0030)

This page outlines the specific requirements for anchor bolts and grouting for the Plate & Frame Heat Exchanger, as detailed in document 4-SCR-0030. Adherence to these specifications is **mandatory** for ensuring the structural stability and longevity of the equipment installation.

**Anchor Bolt Specifications:**
*   **Anchor Bolt Type:** Epoxy Adhesive Anchor System. This specifies the type of chemical anchoring system to be used.
*   **Anchor Bolt Material:** 4.6. This refers to the material grade of the anchor bolts, indicating their tensile strength and yield strength properties.
*   **Nut Material:** 4. This refers to the material grade of the nuts used with the anchor bolts.

**Grouting Requirements:**
*   **Dimension 'Y' - Grout:** 40 mm. This specifies the **mandatory** minimum thickness of the grout layer required between the equipment baseplate and the concrete foundation. Proper grouting ensures uniform load distribution and prevents corrosion.

**Installation Requirements:**
*   **Minimum "Bolt to Edge" distance is 100 mm.** This is a **mandatory** minimum distance from the center of an anchor bolt to the edge of the concrete foundation or pad. This requirement is crucial for preventing concrete breakout failures.
*   For other "Bolt to Edge" distances, consult the Civil/Structural Department for specific guidance and approvals.

For general anchor bolt installation guidelines, refer to [[4-SCR-0004_anchor_bolt_installation_requirements]]. Information on material selection for various components can be found in [[material_selection]].
