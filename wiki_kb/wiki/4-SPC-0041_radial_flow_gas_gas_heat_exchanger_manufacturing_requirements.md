# Radial Flow Gas/Gas Heat Exchanger Manufacturing Requirements (4-SPC-0041)

This specification, 4-SPC-0041, outlines the comprehensive manufacturing requirements for radial flow gas/gas heat exchangers as defined by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. It serves as a foundational document for ensuring the quality, integrity, and performance of these critical components within chemical engineering processes. The requirements detailed herein cover various aspects of fabrication, including precise tolerances for tube holes in tubesheets and baffles, as well as specific procedures for welding and tube expansion.

The content of this specification has been compiled and extracted from several existing foundational documents, ensuring consistency with established company standards:
*   ES44\F06: [[4-SPC-0040_metallic_process_vessels_fabrication_overview|Minimum Manufacturing & Quality Requirements of Pressure Vessels & Heat Exchangers]]
*   ES44\W01: [[4-SPC-0014_overview|Welding Austenitic Stainless Steel]]
*   ES44\W-07: Welding Carbon Steel (A specific slug for this is not in the provided list, so a new one is invented: [[4-SPC-00XX_welding_carbon_steel_overview]])
