# Specific Packaging Methods for Marine Transport

This section details the specific methods for packaging goods for marine transportation, as may be called up on the [[purchase_order_overview|Purchase Order]]. These methods ensure goods are protected and handled appropriately during transit.

**Wooden Crating:**
*   For wooden crating, goods **must** be fully enclosed by the crating.
*   Wooden crating **must** be fastened securely using spiral type galvanised nails.
*   Items within a single crate **must** be strapped to base pallets using steel bands.
*   Steel bands **must** be applied with a stretching tool and secured with crimped steel seals.
*   Items within a single crate **must** be partitioned, braced, blocked, or secured to the base to prevent harmful contact with each other.

**Palletised Packaging:**
*   For palletised packaging, goods **must** be secured to racks, cradles, or saddles.
*   Goods on pallets **must** be protected by draping or wrapping with a water-resistant medium.

**Ocean Freight Containers:**
*   Goods **must** be placed within standard twenty-foot and forty-foot ocean freight containers.
*   Containers **must** be loaded utilizing as much of the interior capacity as possible.
*   Containers **must** be loaded to allow for unloading using standard forklifts where practical.
*   Where items require dragging during loading or unloading, the Vendor **must** mount them on skids, sleds, or runners.
*   Bulk commodities **must** be shipped in bags or boxes, or shrink-wrapped and strapped to pallets or skids.
*   The Vendor **must** block and brace container contents to prevent shifting while in transit.
*   If a Vendor-supplied shipping container is specified on the Purchase Order, it will not be returned to the Vendor. Upon delivery, such a container will be considered part of the packaging supply and become the property of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
*   A Vendor-supplied shipping container **must** have an independent marine surveyor’s certificate of seaworthiness.
*   All marks and numbers not pertaining to the contract **must** be removed from a Vendor-supplied shipping container.

These methods are to be applied in accordance with the general [[packaging_marine_transportation_0_spc_5005]] requirements and [[packaging_materials_and_construction]] standards.
