# Design Deficiency Management

This page outlines the process for managing design deficiencies identified during the [[mechanical_equipment_checkout_procedure|mechanical checkout procedure]].

**Identification and Classification**
*   Other modifications and changes identified during check-out shall be treated as design deficiencies.
*   A design deficiency is not a punch list item.

**Documentation and Resolution**
*   A design deficiency shall be documented and forwarded to the Project Manager for resolution.
