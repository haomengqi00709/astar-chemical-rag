# Converter Shop Fabrication and Assembly (4-SPC-7540)

Specification 4-SPC-7540 mandates specific requirements for the shop fabrication and assembly of converter components, emphasizing maximization of shop work to minimize field activities and ensure quality.

**Maximizing Shop Fabrication:**
*   The vendor **will** provide the largest size pieces that can be economically shipped. Generally, pieces **will** be up to 2.4m x 12m (8’ x 40’) in size.
*   The vendor **is** to consider the most economical alternative for piece sizing and discuss this with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
*   Shop fabricated subassembly sizes **must** be maximized within shipping restrictions.
*   Fabrication of the converter **must** be maximized in the shop to minimize field fabrication/erection efforts.

**Welding and Preparation:**
*   All field weld edge preparations **must** be done in the shop, in accordance with the vendor’s approved weld procedure(s). This ensures consistent quality and reduces field work.
*   All stainless steel to carbon steel welds **must** be done in the shop. This critical requirement minimizes potential contamination and ensures controlled welding environments for dissimilar metals, adhering to standards like [[4-SPC-0061_austenitic_stainless_steel_welding_standard_overview]].

**Component Fabrication and Assembly:**
*   All subassemblies **must** be trial fitted, match marked, and disassembled for shipping. This process verifies fit-up before shipment and facilitates efficient field erection.
*   Shell plates **must** be rolled to the specified curvature.
*   Head plates **must** be formed, trial assembled, and match marked.
*   Baffle and grid plates **must** be rolled, with joining flat bars shop welded to one side.
*   Nozzles **must** be prefabricated complete with their transition sections.
*   Rolled shell courses **must** be trial fitted (complete circle), checked for dimensional and level accuracy, and match marked course by course.
*   The core **must** be completely fabricated in shippable sections.

**Tagging and Documentation:**
*   All parts **must** be tagged as outlined in the [[converter_field_erection_manual]]. This ensures correct identification and assembly sequence during field erection.

Adherence to these detailed fabrication and assembly procedures, along with the specified [[4-SPC-7540_converter_fabrication_tolerances]], is crucial for the successful delivery of the converter components.

*   Generally, pieces will be up to 2.4m x 12m (8’ x 40’) size. The vendor is expected to consider the most economical alternative for piece sizing and discuss it with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
*   All field weld edge preparations will be done in the shop per the Vendor’s approved weld procedure(s).
*   All stainless steel to carbon steel welds must be done in the shop.

**Component-Specific Fabrication:**
*   Shell plates must be rolled.
*   Head plates must be formed, trial assembled, and match marked.
*   Baffle and grid plates must be rolled, and joining flat bars shop welded to one side.
*   Nozzles must be prefabricated complete with their transition section.
*   The core must be completely fabricated in shippable sections.
