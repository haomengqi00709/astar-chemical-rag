# Variable Throat Venturi Sizing Parameters

The sizing of the Variable Throat Venturi is critical for its operational flexibility and performance, as detailed in **1-Cal-6520-R0**. The following parameters define the geometry and operational range of the variable throat mechanism.

**Key Sizing Parameters:**
*   **Angle:** 60.0 degrees
*   **Maximum Area:** 0.8042924366944446 m²
*   **Minimum Area:** 0.4457298366287279 m²
*   **Desired Stroke (h1):** 150.0 mm
*   **Throat Diameter (d1):** 1772.2434725099893 m
*   **Bob Diameter (d2):** 1604.1589919924477 m
*   **d3:** 1945.448553266877 mm
*   **S1:** 147.7827150550508 mm
*   **h2:** 73.89135752752539 mm
*   **d4:** 1860.1261629482697 mm
*   **Opened Area at Maximum Flow:** 0.8041871901308812 m²
*   **FO:** -0.00010524656356336592

These parameters ensure the venturi can adjust its throat area to maintain optimal gas velocity and collection efficiency across varying flow rates. The physical dimensions derived from these sizing parameters are further detailed in [[scrubber_venturi_dimensions]].
