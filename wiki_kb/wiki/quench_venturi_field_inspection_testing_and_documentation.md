# Quench Venturi Field Inspection - Testing and Documentation Requirements

This page details the mandatory testing and documentation requirements for the Quench Venturi, as specified in the Field Inspection Checklist **4-FIC-XX21-R0**. These steps are crucial for verifying the quality of fabrication and installation, ensuring the vessel is safe and ready for operation, and maintaining a comprehensive record of compliance.

**Mandatory Testing and Documentation Checks:**

*   **Non-Destructive Testing (NDT) Completion:** All specified Non-Destructive Testing (NDT) inspections **must** be completed. NDT methods, such as radiographic testing, ultrasonic testing, magnetic particle inspection, or liquid penetrant testing, are used to detect flaws or discontinuities in materials and welds without causing damage. This is critical for ensuring the structural integrity of the Quench Venturi. For general NDT requirements, refer to [[non_destructive_examination_requirements]].
*   **NDT Inspection Records Availability:** All NDT inspection records **must** be available for review. These records provide documented evidence that the required tests were performed, and the results meet the acceptance criteria. They form a vital part of the equipment's quality dossier.
*   **Vessel Cleanliness:** The vessel **must** be clean and empty. Prior to final inspection and commissioning, it is mandatory to ensure that all foreign materials, debris, and construction residues are removed from the interior of the Quench Venturi. This prevents contamination of the process stream and potential damage to internal components.
*   **Final Internal Inspection with Client:** A final internal inspection **must** be completed with the client's representative. This joint inspection confirms that the vessel's interior, including all internals and surface treatments, meets the agreed-upon standards and is ready for closure. This step ensures client satisfaction and sign-off on critical internal conditions.
*   **Closure Certificate for Final Internal Inspection:** A closure certificate for the final internal inspection **must** be attached to the documentation. This certificate formally acknowledges that the internal inspection has been successfully completed and approved by all relevant parties, allowing the vessel to be sealed.

These rigorous testing and documentation procedures are integral to the quality assurance process, providing confidence in the Quench Venturi's construction and readiness for service.
