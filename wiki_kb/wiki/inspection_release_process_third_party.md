# Third Party Inspection Release (IR) Process

The Inspection Release (IR) process is a critical step, signifying the satisfactory completion of inspection activities for equipment. The Inspection Agency **shall** issue Inspection Releases upon satisfactory completion of an inspection.

**IR Numbering**
The IR number is derived from the equipment Purchase Order number. If more than one IR is required for a single Purchase Order, the IRs **shall** be numbered sequentially.

**Conditions for Non-Issuance**
An IR **will not** be issued under the following circumstances:
*   If any equipment requires further work that necessitates another inspection visit.
*   If an outstanding [[non_conformance_report_process_third_party]] exists for the equipment.

**Issuance by Inspector (No Further Inspection Visit Required)**
When the work to be completed does not require another inspection visit, an IR **shall** be issued by the Inspector. The IR issued by the Inspector **must** contain detailed information about the work that needs to be completed.

**Vendor Responsibilities (Post-IR Issuance, No Further Inspection Visit)**
In cases where no further inspection visit is required after IR issuance:
*   The Vendor **shall** be instructed to sign and date the IR, confirming “All noted work completed”.
*   The Vendor **shall** be instructed to forward the signed and dated IR to ChemeticsQA@akerkvaerner.com.
*   The Vendor **shall** complete the work described on the applicable Inspection Release.
*   The Vendor **shall** sign and date sections B and C of the IR.
*   The Vendor **shall** forward the original or a copy of the IR to both the Inspector and Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Endorsed Inspection Releases**
It is permissible for the Inspector to issue a suitably endorsed IR even if the Quality Documentation Manual is not in its final configuration, provided that its contents are complete and acceptable to the Inspector. An example of an endorsed IR is provided in the reference documents.
