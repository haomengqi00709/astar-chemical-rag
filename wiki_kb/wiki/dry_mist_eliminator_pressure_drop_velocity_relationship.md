# Dry Mist Eliminator Pressure Drop and Velocity Relationship

This page details the relationship between vapor phase velocity and pressure drop for the Dry Mist Eliminator, Tag No. **7507K**. This data is crucial for understanding the operational characteristics and efficiency of the mist eliminator.

**Vapor Phase Velocity and Pressure Drop Relationship:**
*   **Velocity range:** 0.2 m/s to 10.0 m/s
*   **Pressure Drop range:** 0.12 cm wg to 70.0 cm wg
