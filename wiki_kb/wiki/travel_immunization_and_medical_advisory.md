# Travel Immunization and Medical Advisory

Medisys is the designated provider for travel health and immunization services, ensuring travelers receive appropriate medical advice and maintain up-to-date immunization records.

**Medisys Services:**
*   All [[travel_authorization_process_0_frm_9002|Travel Authorization Forms]] are now forwarded to Medisys.
*   Medisys contacts travelers directly to advise on recommended immunizations based on their travel destination and itinerary.
*   Medisys maintains a comprehensive database of everyone’s immunization records.

**Medisys Contact Information:**
*   **Address:** 1090 West Pender, Suite 1100, Vancouver, BC
*   **Phone Number:** 604-669-8188

For broader travel health and documentation requirements, refer to [[travel_health_and_documentation_support]]. For emergency medical assistance during travel, see [[travel_emergency_assistance_and_evacuation]].
