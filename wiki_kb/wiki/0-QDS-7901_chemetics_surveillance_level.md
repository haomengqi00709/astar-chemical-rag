# Chemetics Surveillance Level for MV Switchgear & DC Control Power Supply

For the Medium Voltage Switchgear & DC Control Power Supply project, surveillance by Chemetics **shall** be conducted to Level 2.0.

This surveillance level dictates the extent of Chemetics' involvement in the vendor's manufacturing and testing processes, as further detailed throughout this Quality Data Sheet (0-QDS-7901-R0). Specific activities requiring Chemetics' Witness, Hold, or Complete surveillance are outlined in the relevant sections for [[0-QDS-7901_material_verification_and_certifications]], [[0-QDS-7901_equipment_testing]], [[0-QDS-7901_final_inspections]], [[0-QDS-7901_inspection_and_documentation_requirements]], and [[0-QDS-7901_packing_and_shipping_requirements]].
