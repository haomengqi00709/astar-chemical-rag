# Vendor Quality Assurance for Equipment (Specification 0-SPC-7001)

Specification 0-SPC-7001 outlines the mandatory requirements for a Vendor's quality assurance program concerning the supply of equipment. This specification **must** be read in conjunction with the specific [[quality_data_sheets_qds]] relevant to the goods being procured.

**Scope and Application**
The primary purpose of this specification is to ensure that all items, activities, and services provided by the Vendor comply with the requirements of the Purchase Order. It sets forth the minimum quality assurance criteria that Vendors **must** adhere to. These minimum requirements do not absolve the Vendor from considering and complying with any other necessary criteria to achieve an effective Quality Plan.

**Quality Plan Integration**
Where stipulated in the reference [[quality_data_sheets_qds]], the Vendor **shall** establish, implement, and maintain a [[vendor_quality_plan_requirements|Quality Plan]]. This plan **must** be in accordance with the requirements detailed within this specification. Ideally, the Quality Plan will reference the Vendor’s existing Quality Management System. If Aker Kvaerner Chemetics has previously approved the Vendor's Quality Management System and its implementing procedures, the Quality Plan may simply refer to these approved documents and **must** certify that no revisions have been made since their approval.
