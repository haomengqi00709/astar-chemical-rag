# Field Erected CS Tank - Shop Quality Record Definitions

For the "Field Erected CS Tank - Shop" component, specific abbreviations are used to define the requirements for quality records:

*   **AP (As Prepared):** This designation indicates that the quality record is required "As Prepared." This means the document or record should be submitted in its original prepared format.
*   **FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package):** This designation indicates that the quality record is required to be "Included in the Quality Assurance Documentation Package." This means the document or record forms part of the comprehensive set of quality documentation submitted by the Vendor.

These definitions apply to various documentation requirements throughout the project, including material certifications, non-destructive examination reports, and packing lists.
