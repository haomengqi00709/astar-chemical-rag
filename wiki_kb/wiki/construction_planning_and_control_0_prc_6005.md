# Construction Planning and Control (0-PRC-6005)

This document, 0-PRC-6005, outlines the procedures for the planning and control of construction activities for the Project. It covers aspects such as scheduling, resource allocation, and progress monitoring specific to the construction phase.

Initial planning of construction will be undertaken in accordance with this procedure. Subcontracts will also be prepared and issued in accordance with this procedure.
