# Goods and Services Definitions

This page provides definitions related to "Goods and Services" as used in project documentation, particularly within Purchase Order [[0-SPC-5001_purchase_order_overview|0-SPC-5001]].

**Goods and Services**
"Goods and Services" refers to the specific items, equipment, materials, and associated work or activities that are purchased from a Vendor under a Purchase Order. This encompasses both tangible products and intangible services required for the completion of a project.

**To Provide the Goods and Services**
"To Provide the Goods and Services" means to undertake all the necessary work to complete the Goods and Services in accordance with the Purchase Order. This includes all incidental work, materials, services, and actions explicitly or implicitly required by the Purchase Order.

**Goods and Services Information**
"Goods and Services Information" is any data or documentation that either specifies and describes the Goods and Services themselves or states any constraints on how the Vendor **shall** Provide them. This information can be found in:
*   Documents referenced directly in the Purchase Order.
*   Instructions given in accordance with the Purchase Order.

This information is crucial for ensuring that the Vendor understands the scope, technical requirements, and operational limitations for delivering the contracted items and services.
