# Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) for Construction Site Advisors

Aker Kvaerner Chemetics construction site advisors **must** adhere to strict Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) requirements to ensure their safety on construction sites.

**Mandatory PPE**
Advisors **are required** to bring the following essential PPE to Site:
*   **Steel toe safety shoes:** To protect against foot injuries from falling objects or compression. Refer to [[safety_footwear_requirements]] for detailed specifications.
*   **Long sleeve shirts:** To provide protection against sun exposure, minor abrasions, and other environmental hazards.
*   **Long pants:** Similar to long sleeve shirts, these offer protection against physical hazards and environmental elements. For general guidelines on appropriate work attire, see [[work_clothing_requirements]].

**Additional PPE Requirements**
Beyond the mandatory items, advisors **are required** to bring any other PPE as specified by site conditions or task requirements. The individual responsible for providing safety indoctrination will advise the advisor if additional PPE is necessary to be carried or worn. In some instances, certain types of PPE may be available for use directly at the construction Site.

**PPE for Vehicle Operation**
If site conditions necessitate the operation of a motor vehicle by an advisor, they **must** wear proper PPE while operating the vehicle. This ensures continued safety even during transit within the Site.

For a comprehensive understanding of the company's overall policy on protective gear, consult the [[personal_protective_equipment_policy]].

*   **Other PPE as required:** Advisors are also required to bring other PPE as required by specific site conditions or tasks.

**PPE Availability**
In some cases, specific PPE may be available at the construction site.
