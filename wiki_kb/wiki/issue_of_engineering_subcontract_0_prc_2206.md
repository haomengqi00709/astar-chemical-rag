# Issue of Engineering Subcontract (Procedure 0-PRC-2206)

Procedure 0-PRC-2206 outlines the comprehensive steps required for the issuance of an Engineering Subcontract within the firm. This procedure ensures that all aspects of subcontracting, from technical specifications to commercial terms and scheduling, are meticulously managed and documented.

The development of technical requirements for any Engineering Subcontract **must** be in strict accordance with [[engineering_subcontract_scope_preparation_0_prc_2203]]. This ensures that the scope of work is clearly defined and aligns with project objectives. All time-related obligations stipulated within the subcontract **must** be consistent with the master [[0-dat-3001_project_schedule]], providing a unified timeline for project execution. Furthermore, commercial requirements are to be developed following the guidelines set forth in [[engineering_subcontract_commercial_terms_0_prc_2205]], ensuring financial and contractual clarity.

**Administrative and Naming Conventions:**
A name change conversion to "2005 Name Change" has been implemented for this procedure, as detailed in [[0_prc_2005_document_name_change_history]]. This conversion involved changing references from "80XX" to "22XX" within the document numbering scheme. Specifically, the document numbering change instruction is "0--80 to 0--22-R0". It is important to note that no new revision number was used for this name change conversion, and the changes made for this conversion have not been explicitly marked within the document. This approach maintains continuity while updating internal references.

This procedure is critical for maintaining consistency, legal compliance, and efficient project delivery when engaging external engineering services.

The development of technical requirements for subcontracts is guided by 0-PRC-2203: Engineering Subcontract Scope Preparation. All time-related obligations must align with 0-DAT-3001: Project Schedule, and commercial requirements are to be developed in accordance with 0-PRC-2205: Commercial Terms for Engineering Subcontract.
