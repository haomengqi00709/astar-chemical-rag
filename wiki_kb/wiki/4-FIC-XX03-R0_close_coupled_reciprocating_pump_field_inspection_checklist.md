# Close Coupled Reciprocating Pump Field Inspection Checklist (4-FIC-XX03-R0)

This document, 4-FIC-XX03-R0, serves as a template for the field inspection checklist specifically designed for Close Coupled Reciprocating Pumps. It outlines the critical checks and mandatory requirements that must be verified before commissioning and operation, ensuring proper installation and functionality of the equipment. This checklist is a standard document used by [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_company_details]] for project quality assurance.

**Mandatory Inspection Requirements:**
The following items **must** be verified and completed during the field inspection of a Close Coupled Reciprocating Pump:

*   **General Installation:**
    *   The correct Nameplate **must** be installed. Refer to [[plant_equipment_identification_4_prc_0001]] for equipment identification standards.
    *   Pump position **must** be checked for correct elevation, orientation, and leveling.
    *   Foundation / Anchor bolts **must** be secure and tightened. Refer to [[civil_works_design_criteria_2_prc_0001]] for design criteria.
    *   Grouting **must** be completed. Refer to [[civil_construction_and_site_preparation_0_spc_6x92]] for specifications.
    *   Suction/discharge piping **must** be properly aligned and supported, ensuring no stress on pump nozzles. See [[piping_design_and_specification_5_prc_0003]] for piping design.
    *   Auxiliary systems (e.g., seal flush, water cooling) **must** be complete.

*   **Mechanical Components:**
    *   Mechanical seal / packing **must** be installed. Refer to [[horizontal_centrifugal_pump_shaft_seal_system]] for related information on shaft seal systems.
    *   All accessories (e.g., gauges, vents, drain valves, connections) **must** be installed. See [[general_instrument_requirements]] for instrument details.
    *   The Pulsation Damper **must** be installed and filled with the appropriate fluid.
    *   The Relief valve **must** be installed and set at the correct pressure. Refer to [[pressure_relief_equipment_tagging]] for pressure relief equipment.
    *   Check-valves **must** be installed in the correct orientation.

*   **Electrical & Grounding:**
    *   Grounding **must** be completed. Refer to [[grounding_installation_details]] for installation specifics.
    *   Motor rotation **must** be verified by the electrical contractor. A report of this verification **must** be attached. See [[electrical_equipment_testing_and_inspection]] for electrical testing.

*   **Pre-commissioning & Finishing:**
    *   Temporary suction strainers **must** be in place. Refer to [[final_strainer_configuration_and_types]] for strainer information.
    *   External painting **must** be completed with no visible defects. Consult [[coatings_design_and_specification_7_prc_0002]] for coating specifications.
    *   Insulation **must** be complete. Refer to [[insulation_design_and_specification_6_prc_0002]] for insulation design.
    *   Flange Guards **must** be installed.
