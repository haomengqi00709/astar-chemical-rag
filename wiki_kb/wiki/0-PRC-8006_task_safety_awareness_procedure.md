# 0-PRC-8006 Task Safety Awareness Procedure

This procedure, documented as 0-PRC-8006, establishes the framework for involving all employees in the proactive identification and elimination of potentially hazardous conditions within the company's operations. It details the implementation of [[task_safety_awareness_overview]] (TSA), a critical component of the firm's health, safety, and environmental (HSE) management system.

The core objective of 0-PRC-8006 is to foster a safety-conscious culture where employees and supervisors jointly analyze work assignments for hazards and agree upon sound safety practices before any task commences. This document applies to all areas of operations and mandates the application of TSA before starting any new task.
