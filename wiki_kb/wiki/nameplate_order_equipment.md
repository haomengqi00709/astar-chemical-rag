# Equipment Nameplate Requirements

As per document 4-SPC-0001, all equipment **must** be clearly identified with a durable nameplate.

**Nameplate Specifications**
Each piece of equipment **will be provided** with a stamped or engraved stainless steel manufacturer’s nameplate. This nameplate **shall list** the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics equipment number, the Owner’s equipment number, and pertinent manufacturer information. This manufacturer information **includes**, but is not limited to, the make, model number, serial number, and rating of the equipment.

**Language Requirements**
All nameplate information **will be** in the English language. Furthermore, the Vendor **will allow** sufficient space next to all name and instruction plates for the Owner to attach [insert language name] language plates, accommodating local operational requirements.

These requirements ensure clear identification and traceability of all equipment throughout its lifecycle.
