# Template Pump Process Design Conditions

This page outlines the standard process design conditions for a template pump, as specified in document 4-PDS-XXXX-R2. These conditions are critical for the proper selection and operation of the pump, ensuring it is compatible with the fluid characteristics and operational demands of the process. The "PROCESS CONDITIONS" section of the datasheet includes both "DESIGN" and "ALTERNATE" categories to cover various operational scenarios.

Key process parameters to be specified include:

*   **Quantity:**
    *   Operating units
    *   Installed Spare units
*   **Case Description:** A brief description of the operating case, detailing the specific scenario for which the pump is being designed or evaluated.
*   **Design Basis:** The fundamental criteria and assumptions used for the pump's design, including relevant codes, standards, and project-specific requirements.
*   **Fluid Description:** A detailed description of the fluid being pumped, including its chemical name and any special characteristics.
*   **Composition:** Fluid composition in %w/w (weight percentage).
*   **Solids Content:** Percentage of solids in the fluid in %w/w.
*   **Solids Type:** Description of the type of solids present, which can influence pump and impeller selection.
*   **Flow Rate:** The volumetric flow rate in m3/h (cubic meters per hour).
*   **Minimum Flow Rate:** The minimum required volumetric flow rate in m3/h for stable operation or specific process needs.
*   **TDH (estimated):** Total Dynamic Head (estimated) in m (fluid), representing the total energy imparted to the fluid by the pump.
*   **NPSH Available (est.):** Net Positive Suction Head Available (estimated) in m (fluid), which is crucial for preventing cavitation.
*   **Temperature:** Operating temperature in oC (degrees Celsius).
*   **Density:** Fluid density in kg/m3 (kilograms per cubic meter).
*   **Viscosity:** Fluid viscosity in cP (centipoise), affecting friction losses and pump efficiency.
*   **Specific Heat:** Fluid specific heat in kJ/kg·oC (kilojoules per kilogram per degree Celsius).
*   **Vapour Pressure:** Fluid vapour pressure in kPa(a) (kilopascals absolute) at the operating temperature.

These parameters collectively ensure that the pump is adequately sized and constructed for the intended application and fluid characteristics.
