# 0-PRC-5001 Revision History

Procedure 0-PRC-5001, governing the format of Request for Quotations (RFQs) and Purchase Orders (POs), has undergone several revisions to enhance clarity and incorporate updated requirements.

**Revision 2 Changes:**
*   **Mandatory Vendor Contact:** The inclusion of Vendor Contact information in Text Section 7 of the RFQ/PO became a mandatory requirement. Previously, this was optional.
*   **RFQ Delivery Date:** For Request for Quotations, the use of "TBA" (To Be Advised) for the delivery date in Text Section 3 became mandatory.

**Revision 3 Changes:**
*   **Clarification of Responsibilities:** The responsibilities for providing information and documentation across various Line Items and Text Sections were explicitly clarified. This ensures a clear understanding of roles for all involved parties. For a detailed breakdown, refer to [[request_for_quotation_and_purchase_order_responsibilities]].
*   **Form Modification:** The overall form of the RFQ/PO was modified to align with current usage and best practices within the firm.

**2005 Name Change Conversion:**
A name change conversion was implemented in 2005. It is important to note that no new revision number was used to mark these changes, and the specific modifications related to this conversion have not been explicitly flagged within the document.
