# Progressive Cavity Pump Mechanical Design Requirements

The Progressive Cavity Pump Datasheet (Template `4-DST-XXXX-R0`) outlines specific mechanical design requirements to ensure the pump's structural integrity, operational reliability, and compliance with project standards.

**General Mechanical Design:**
*   **Equipment Specification:** Refers to the overarching [[equipment_design_and_specification_4_prc_0003|equipment specification]] governing the pump.
*   **General Equipment Requirements:** Adherence to [[general_mechanical_design_requirements_0_spc_0010|general mechanical design requirements]].
*   **Design Code:** The applicable design code for the pump's construction.
*   **Site Conditions:** Consideration of [[site_design_conditions_1_dst_0001|site-specific environmental and operational conditions]].
*   **Electrical Area Classification:** Compliance with [[hazardous_area_classification_requirements|electrical area classification]] standards for the installation location.
*   **Location:** The specific installation location of the pump.
*   **Connection Type:** Details regarding the type of connections for piping and utilities.
*   **Painting:** Requirements for the pump's protective coating and painting.
*   **Casing Drain:** Specification for the casing drain mechanism.
*   **Total Noise Limit:** The maximum permissible noise level during operation.
*   **Vibration:** Acceptable limits for vibration during pump operation.
*   **Impeller Size:** The specified size of the pump's impeller.
*   **Speed:** The operational speed of the pump.
*   **Base Plate:** Specifications for the pump's base plate.
*   **Spacer Block:** Details regarding any required spacer blocks.

These requirements ensure the pump is mechanically sound and suitable for the specified operating environment.
