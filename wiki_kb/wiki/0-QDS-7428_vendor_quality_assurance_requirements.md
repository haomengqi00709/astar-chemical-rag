# Vendor Quality Assurance Requirements: CS Tubing

This page details the mandatory quality assurance requirements for vendors supplying carbon steel tubing, as specified in Quality Data Sheet (QDS) 0-QDS-7428. Adherence to these requirements is critical for ensuring the quality and compliance of all supplied materials.

**Mandatory Vendor Requirements:**

*   The Vendor **must** prepare a Quality Plan. This plan **shall** be in accordance with [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment|0-SPC-7001: Vendor Quality Assurance]].
*   The Vendor's Quality Assurance system **must** comply with the requirements set forth in this Quality Data Sheet (0-QDS-7428).
*   The Vendor's Quality Assurance system **must** also comply with the general requirements specified in [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment|0-SPC-7001: Vendor Quality Assurance]].

These requirements are explicitly stated as part of the document's foundational principles (associated with statement 0.0). The vendor's Quality Plan serves as a comprehensive document outlining the procedures, resources, and activities necessary to meet the specified quality standards. It ensures that all stages of manufacturing, from material sourcing to final inspection, are conducted under controlled conditions.

For details on the level of surveillance conducted by Chemetics, refer to [[0-QDS-7428_chemetics_surveillance_level]].
