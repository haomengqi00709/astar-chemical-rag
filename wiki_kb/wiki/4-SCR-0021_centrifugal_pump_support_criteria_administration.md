# Centrifugal Pump Support Criteria Document Administration (4-SCR-0021)

This document, identified as 4-SCR-0021, provides the essential support criteria for the installation of a centrifugal pump on a concrete and grout foundation. It is specifically prepared for the project titled 'Centrifugal Pump on Concrete and Grout' and is intended for use by Aker Kvaerner, particularly for Chemetics, a division of Aker Kvaerner Canada Inc. The client document number for this specification is Standard-4-SCR-0021-R2.

The primary purpose of this document is to ensure the structural stability and safe operation of the centrifugal pump by detailing the necessary design parameters for its foundation and anchoring. Adherence to these criteria is crucial for preventing equipment failure, ensuring operational reliability, and complying with relevant engineering standards.
