# Tapecoat T/C Mastic Application for Valves and Flanges (7-SPC-0005)

This section outlines the requirements for applying Tapecoat T/C Mastic to uncoated valves and flanges after their installation. This coating provides protection against corrosion in specific areas not covered by the primary pipe coating systems.

**Application Procedure**
Uncoated valves and flanges **will be coated** with two brush-coats of Tapecoat T/C Mastic after installation.

**Coating Thickness**
*   Each brush-coat of Tapecoat T/C Mastic **will be** 10 mils wet.
*   Each brush-coat of Tapecoat T/C Mastic **will be** 6 mils dry.
*   The total dry thickness of Tapecoat T/C Mastic **will be** 12 mils.

**Operating Temperature Limit**
The maximum continuous operating temperature for Tapecoat T/C Mastic is 120 F.

Prior to application, surfaces **must be cleaned** in accordance with [[7-SPC-0005_surface_preparation_for_coatings]].

**Coating Thickness and Temperature**
*   Each brush-coat of Tapecoat T/C Mastic will be 10 mils wet, resulting in 6 mils dry.
*   Total dry thickness of Tapecoat T/C Mastic will be 12 mils.
*   Maximum continuous operating temperature for Tapecoat T/C Mastic is 120 F.
