# Progressive Cavity Pump Shaft Seal Requirements

The Progressive Cavity Pump Datasheet (Template `4-DST-XXXX-R0`) details specific requirements for the shaft seal system, which is critical for preventing leakage and ensuring reliable pump operation.

**Shaft Seal Specifications:**
*   **Type:** The specific type of shaft seal to be used (e.g., mechanical seal, packing).
*   **Seal Piping Arrangement:** The configuration and layout of piping associated with the shaft seal, often for flush, quench, or buffer fluid.
*   **Seal Fluid:** The type of fluid used with the seal system (e.g., buffer fluid, flush fluid).
*   **Seal Fluid Control System:** The system responsible for managing and controlling the seal fluid.
*   **Seal Chamber:** Specifications for the seal chamber design.
*   **Seal Fluid Connection:** Details regarding the connections for the seal fluid.
*   **Seal Guard:** Requirements for a protective guard around the shaft seal.
*   **Supply by:** Indicates the party responsible for supplying the shaft seal components.
*   **Mount by:** Indicates the party responsible for mounting the shaft seal.
