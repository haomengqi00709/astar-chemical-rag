# DCS Hardware Checkout Procedure Overview (8-PRC-0014)

The DCS Hardware Checkout Procedure, documented as 8-PRC-0014, outlines the minimum requirements for verifying the installation of the Distributed Control System (DCS) in accordance with project drawings and specifications. This procedure ensures that the DCS is properly installed and ready for operation.

The DCS Manufacturer Hardware Checkout procedure(s) will form an integral part of this document. It **must** be used in conjunction with this document where applicable to ensure comprehensive verification.

**Documentation and Deficiency Management:**
The DCS Vendor **will** provide a complete DCS Hardware Checkout sheet. This sheet **must** be completed during the checkout process and will become part of the final DCS Hardware Checkout document. Any deficiencies identified during the checkout are to be recorded on a separate sheet. These deficiency records are then submitted to the [[8-prc-0014_dcs_hardware_checkout_roles_and_prerequisites|site supervisor/commissioning manager]]. The site supervisor or commissioning manager is responsible for working with either the DCS Vendor Representative or the subcontractor to correct all identified deficiencies.

Upon successful completion of the DCS hardware checkout, the DCS **will** be powered and fully operational. For a complete understanding of this procedure and its relationship with other project procedures, refer to the [[control_system_architecture_block_diagram|DCS block diagram]].

This procedure is complemented by specific checkout steps for various DCS components, including [[8-prc-0014_dcs_operator_console_and_highway_device_checkout|operator consoles and highway devices]], [[8-prc-0014_dcs_redundancy_testing|redundancy tests]] for redundant systems, and [[8-prc-0014_dcs_system_integrity_testing|system integrity tests]] for non-redundant systems.

For administrative details regarding this document, refer to [[8-prc-0014_document_administration]].

The DCS Manufacturer Hardware Checkout procedure(s) will form part of this document and must be used in conjunction with this document where applicable.

Upon completion of the DCS hardware checkout, the DCS will be powered and completely operational. To fully understand this procedure and its relationship with other procedures, it is recommended to review the DCS block diagram.
