# Brine Saturator Material Specifications

This page outlines the material specifications for the Brine Saturator and its key components, as detailed in Document ID 4-PDS-XXXX-R1.

**Materials of Construction**

*   **Material of Construction (Brine Distributor):** titanium
*   **Material of Construction (Weir):** CPVC, FRP, or other
*   **Material of Construction (Dip Pipe):** Not specified
*   **Material of Construction (Launder):** Not specified
*   **Material of Construction (Sparger):** Not specified
