# HazOp Study Team Roles and Responsibilities

The Hazard and Operability (HazOp) Study team is carefully constituted to ensure a comprehensive review of the plant design.

**Team Composition:**
The HazOp team **must** consist of, at a minimum:
*   A HazOp Study Leader
*   Representatives from the Process Department
*   Representatives from the Mechanical Department
*   Representatives from the Design Office
*   One study team member assigned the role of recording secretary.

**Assignment of Roles:**
*   The Chief Process Engineer **shall** assign the HazOp Study Leader. This assignment occurs when the Project Engineer advises that all [[hazop_study_prerequisites_and_documentation|supporting documentation]] is ready.
*   The Project Engineer, in consultation with the HazOp Study Leader, **shall** assign other members of the HazOp Study team from among the project team.

**Qualifications and Restrictions:**
*   A HazOp Study Leader **must** be selected from a group of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics employees based on availability.
*   HazOp Study Leaders **must** have participated in a HazOp leaders course.
*   A HazOp Study Leader **must** have experience in the previous three years or have led a HazOp within the previous two years.
*   The HazOp Study Leader **cannot** be a member of the project team.
*   A recording secretary can be a team member, provided the act of recording does not detract from their participation in discussions.
*   A recording secretary **cannot** be the HazOp Study Leader.
*   A recording secretary **cannot** be the representative from the Process Department.

**HazOp Study Leader Qualifications and Selection:**
*   A HazOp Study Leader will be selected on the basis of availability from a group of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics employees.
*   HazOp Study Leaders **must** have participated in a HazOp leaders course.
*   A HazOp Study Leader **must** have experience in the previous three years or have led a HazOp within the previous two years.
*   The HazOp Study Leader **cannot** be a member of the project team.

**Recording Secretary Role:**
*   A recording secretary **can** be a team member.
*   A recording secretary **cannot** be the HazOp Study Leader.
*   A recording secretary **cannot** be the representative from the Process Department.
*   A recording secretary **can** be a team member if the action of recording does not detract from participation in the discussions.
