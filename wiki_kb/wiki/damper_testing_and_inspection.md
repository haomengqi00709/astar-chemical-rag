# Metal Butterfly Damper Testing and Inspection Requirements

All metal butterfly dampers **SHALL** undergo rigorous testing and inspection to ensure their quality, functionality, and compliance with specified requirements. The Vendor is responsible for fully testing the equipment prior to shipment. The list of tests by the Vendor is not limited to the items specified below.

**Non-Destructive Testing (NDT):**
*   Butt welds in the damper body **SHALL** be subject to spot radiographic examination. This examination **MUST** be conducted in accordance with [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels]] (ASME Code Section VIII Division 1, paragraph UW-52).
*   All welds exposed to the process **MUST** be subject to liquid penetrant examination to detect surface-breaking defects.
*   Further non-destructive testing requirements are outlined in the relevant Quality Data Sheet.

**Actuator Performance Tests:**
Actuator performance tests **SHALL** be conducted to verify the functionality and reliability of the actuation equipment.

**Functional Checks:**
The Vendor **SHALL** perform comprehensive functional checks to prove the correct operation of the damper. These checks **MUST** demonstrate the following:
*   The damper blade moves accurately and smoothly over its full range of motion.
*   The speed and direction of movement are correct for the intended application.
*   The damper blade moves to the correct fail-safe position in the event of a supply failure (e.g., loss of air or electrical power).

**Leakage Rate Guarantee:**
The Vendor **SHALL** guarantee the leakage rate of the damper. This guarantee **MUST** be based on the maximum flow rates and maximum inlet pressures specified in [[metal_dampers_process_and_automation_requirements]] (5-LST-[00XX]: Metal Dampers).
