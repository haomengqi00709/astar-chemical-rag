# Subcontractor Specific HSE Responsibilities

Subcontractors working with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **are responsible for** a wide range of specific Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) duties to ensure project safety and compliance.

**Compliance and Training:**
*   Subcontractor **is responsible for** following all regulations, codes, and other legal obligations pertaining to the work.
*   Subcontractor **is responsible for** ensuring that all employees and subcontractors have received appropriate training and are competent to perform their assigned work.

**Equipment and Safety Practices:**
*   Subcontractor **is responsible for** providing [[personal_protective_equipment_policy|Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)]] and other necessary equipment to all employees working in areas or operations requiring PPE.
*   Subcontractor **is responsible for** briefing employees on any unique or unforeseen hazards they may encounter on the job site.
*   Subcontractor **is responsible for** using a permit system to authorize potentially hazardous activities. Refer to [[safe_work_permit_procedure_overview]] for details.
*   Subcontractor **is responsible for** proactively using [[job_hazard_analysis_and_task_safety_awareness|Job Hazard Analysis (JHA)]] and [[task_safety_awareness_overview|Task Safety Awareness (TSA)]] to promote a safety mindset.
*   Subcontractor **is responsible for** supplying copies of Material Safety Data Sheets (MSDS) for all chemicals prior to bringing them on-site. Refer to [[hazardous_materials_management_overview]].
*   Subcontractor **is responsible for** providing all equipment necessary to perform the contracted work and ensuring that this equipment is properly maintained in good working order.
*   Subcontractor **is responsible for** removing and properly disposing of all waste, chemicals, and empty chemical containers brought on-site.

**Audits and Meetings:**
*   Subcontractor **is responsible for** conducting regular safety audits of the work site. See [[hse_audits_and_inspections_overview]].
*   Subcontractor **is responsible for** holding regular safety meetings with a minimum weekly frequency. These meetings **must** have 50% management or supervisory employee representation and 100% non-supervisory trade employee representation.
*   Subcontractor **is responsible for** holding toolbox safety meetings.

**Incident Reporting:**
*   Subcontractor **is responsible for** immediately reporting any hazards, accidents, or incidents to the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Construction Manager. Refer to [[hse_incident_reporting]].
*   Subcontractor **must** follow up with a written report within 7 days of the incident.
*   Subcontractor **must** provide Aker Kvaerner Chemetics with a monthly HSE performance report in a format designated by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
*   Subcontractor **is expected to** report all incidents and submit a monthly HSE report to the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Site Manager.
