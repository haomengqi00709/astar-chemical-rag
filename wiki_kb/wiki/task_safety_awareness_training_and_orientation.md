# Task Safety Awareness (TSA) Training and Orientation

Comprehensive training and orientation are essential to ensure the effective implementation of [[task_safety_awareness_overview]] (TSA) across all project personnel.

**Supervisor Orientation:**
*   Each new supervisor, upon assignment to a project, **will receive** an orientation specifically designed to fully explain the principles and steps of Task Safety Awareness.
*   This critical orientation **will be provided** by HSE Managers, Craft Superintendents, and Department Heads.
*   Upon completion of the TSA orientation, each supervisor **will be required to sign and date** a pledge poster for his or her craft or department. This pledge poster **will be kept** in the respective Craft/Department office as a record of commitment.

**New Employee HSE Orientation:**
*   The core concept and steps of TSA application **will be included** in each project's [[hse_orientation_overview]] for new employees.
*   During this orientation, each employee **will be instructed not to start** any work task without first discussing its potential safety hazards and precautions with their supervisor. This reinforces the employee's [[task_safety_awareness_roles_and_responsibilities]] in the TSA process.

These training initiatives are crucial for embedding a strong safety culture and ensuring compliance with [[task_safety_awareness_implementation_and_requirements]].
