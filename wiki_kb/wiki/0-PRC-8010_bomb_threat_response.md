# Bomb Threat Response

Bomb threats **should always be** taken seriously. This page outlines the procedure to follow if you receive a bomb threat.

**Receiving a Threatening Call**
*   If possible, have a co-worker, relative, or friend listen to the conversation on another phone or extension.
*   **You must** keep the caller on the line as long as you can.
*   **You must** remain calm and concentrate on what you must do.
*   **You must** take notes of what you hear.
*   **You must** have the caller repeat the message clearly while you write the entire message word for word.
*   **You must** ask for the caller's name and location.
*   **You must** ask the caller what the device looks like, where it is, and when it is set to detonate.
*   **You must** always remind the caller that the bomb will most likely harm innocent people, possibly even children.

**Caller Information**
Pay close attention to the caller's voice to determine their sex, accent, overall language ability, etc. Listen for any background noises that could help law enforcement authorities locate the origin of the call.

**Reporting the Threat**
**You must** report the bomb threat immediately to the police by dialling 9-911.
**You must** report the bomb threat immediately to building management at the number on the first page of this document (refer to [[0-PRC-8010_emergency_contacts_and_first_aid]]).
**You must** report the bomb threat immediately to the HSE manager.

**Critical Instructions**
*   **DO NOT PANIC** during a bomb threat.
*   **DO NOT PULL THE FIRE ALARM** during a bomb threat.
*   **You must** follow any additional instructions given after reporting the threat.
