# Material Requirements for High Silicon Austenitic Stainless Steel (4-SPC-0025)

**Base Materials**
Acceptable base materials **shall** be supplied by VEW. The specific acceptable material is designated VEW-Bohler A610, which corresponds to German Werkstoff No. 1.4361 and grade X2 CrNi Si 1815.

All plate, sheet, pipe, tube, and forgings **shall** be purchased in the solution annealed condition. Material specifications **shall** satisfy the following ASME standards:
*   Plate and Sheet: [[ASME_bpvc_section_ii_materials]] SA240
*   Pipe: [[ASME_bpvc_section_ii_materials]] SA312
*   Tube: [[ASME_bpvc_section_ii_materials]] SA268
*   Forgings: [[ASME_bpvc_section_ii_materials]] SA479

Necessary test and certification documents for all materials **shall** be available for inspection by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics prior to the commencement of welding. Corrosion test results (Huey Test) **shall** accompany all mill test certificates.

**Welding Consumables**
All weld filler metals **shall** contain high silicon (4.7 percent maximum). For manual metallic arc welding, filler metals **shall** conform to Bohler Fox EAS-2Si. The use of EASN-2Si for tungsten inert gas welding is **not acceptable** unless specifically agreed upon by an Aker Kvaerner Chemetics QA and materials engineering representative.

Electrodes **shall** have a ferrite content of between 7-11 percent in the undiluted fused filler metal, measured by a Fischer Penescope. Fabricators **shall** ensure and demonstrate that manufacturers' instructions for the storage, drying, and use of weld consumables are fully implemented. All wet electrodes **shall** be discarded and **not** baked and reused.

Prior to the commencement of welding, certification showing results from chemical analysis and other tests (in accordance with relevant material specification) **shall** be supplied to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for all materials, including welding consumables.

VEW-Bohler A610 is the *only* stainless steel covered by this Standard.

Acceptable base and filler materials shall be supplied by VEW.

Sheet material shall satisfy ASME SA240.
