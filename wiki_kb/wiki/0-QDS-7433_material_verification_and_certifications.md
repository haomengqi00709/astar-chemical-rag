# Flocculant Mixer Material Verification and Certifications

Ensuring the correct materials are used and properly certified is a fundamental aspect of quality assurance for the Flocculant Mixer package. Material verification and certifications confirm that the components meet the specified metallurgical and chemical requirements, which is critical for the equipment's structural integrity, corrosion resistance, and overall performance.

**Mandatory Requirements:**

*   **Material Verification:** Material Verification **requires** a Quality Record. This record **must** provide objective evidence that the materials used in the fabrication of the Flocculant Mixer components conform to the specified material grades and standards. This typically involves documentation such as Mill Test Certificates (MTCs) or material traceability reports.
*   **Certificate of Compliance:** A Certificate of Compliance **requires** a Quality Record (FP). This certificate **must** be formally included in the final Quality Assurance Documentation Package. It serves as a declaration by the vendor that the supplied equipment, or specific components thereof, complies with all applicable specifications, codes, and standards.
*   **Chemetics Witness Surveillance for Certificate of Compliance:** The Certificate of Compliance **requires** Chemetics Witness surveillance. This means that a Chemetics representative or designated third-party inspector **must** be present to witness the final review and signing of the Certificate of Compliance, ensuring independent verification of the vendor's declaration.

These stringent requirements for material verification and certification, coupled with Chemetics' witness surveillance, are in place to guarantee the reliability and safety of the Flocculant Mixer package. For general procedures on material verification, refer to [[material_verification]].
