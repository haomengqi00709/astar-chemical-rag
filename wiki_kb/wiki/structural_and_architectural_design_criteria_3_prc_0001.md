# Structural and Architectural Design Criteria (3-PRC-0001)

This document, 3-PRC-0001, specifies the design criteria for all structural and architectural elements of the Project. It establishes the mandatory requirements and standards for the structural integrity, building design, and aesthetic considerations of the plant facilities.

Discipline Leads refer to this document to define the precise requirements for structural and architectural design, ensuring adherence to safety codes, functional needs, and project-specific aesthetic and material standards.
