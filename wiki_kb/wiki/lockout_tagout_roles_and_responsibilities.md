# Lockout and Tagout Roles and Responsibilities

This page details the roles and responsibilities associated with the [[9-prc-0019_lockout_tagout_procedure_overview|Lockout and Tagout Procedure (9-PRC-0019)]].

### Key Roles

*   **Affected Individual**: An employee whose job requires him/her to work on a machine or equipment which is under the lockout/tagout procedure.
*   **Authorised Individual**: A knowledgeable individual to whom the supervisor has given the authority and responsibility to implement a lockout/tagout procedure.
    *   An authorised individual and an affected individual may be the same person when the affected employee’s duties also include work on machines or equipment which **must** be locked and tagged out.

### Subcontractor Responsibilities

*   The Subcontractor **shall** provide affected individuals with his/her own lock and the only key to that lock.
*   The Subcontractor is responsible for seeing that all his employees follow this procedure.
*   The Subcontractor (authorised individual) **shall** instruct all employees in the safety significance of the lockout procedure.
*   The authorised individual **shall** instruct each new or transferred affected individual in the purpose and use of the lockout procedure.

### Individual Duties

*   Individuals authorised to perform lockout **must** be certain as to which switch, valve, or other energy isolating devices applies to the equipment being locked out.
*   More than one energy source (electrical, mechanical or others) may be involved and **shall** be confirmed with the supervisor.
*   The authorised employee **shall** know the type and magnitude of energy that the machine or equipment utilises and **shall** understand the hazards.
*   Each individual requiring to work on the affected machine or equipment **shall** place his/her lock on the device.
*   After work is completed, each affected individual removes his/her lock.

*   The subcontractor is responsible for ensuring all their employees follow this procedure.
*   The subcontractor will provide affected individuals with their own lock and the only key to that lock.
*   An authorised individual and an affected individual may be the same person when the affected employee’s duties also include work on machines or equipment which must be locked and tagged out.
