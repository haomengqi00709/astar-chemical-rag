# Professional Services Subcontract (0-SPC-2201) Administration and History

Document 0-SPC-2201, titled "Administration," serves as the foundational document for professional services subcontracts within Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. It is based on "THE PROFESSIONAL SERVICES CONTRACT," a 2nd Edition New Engineering Contract document published by Thomas Telford for the Institution of Civil Engineers in June 1998. The document contains core clauses, with specific clauses for Option A.

**Document History and Revisions:**
The document has undergone several administrative and structural changes:
*   **June 6, 2004:** The file name was officially changed from 0-CHG-80XX-R0 to 0-CHG-22XX-R0. Consequently, any internal references to the 80XX series **must** be updated to 22XX.
*   **August 2004:** Legal counsel performed a significant revision and update. This involved:
    *   Simplifying the language for clarity and ease of understanding.
    *   Incorporating special conditions that were consistently used into the standard conditions, streamlining the contract structure.
    *   Removing redundant provisions to enhance efficiency and reduce unnecessary complexity.
*   **2005 Name Change Conversion:** A company name change conversion was implemented by 2005. For this specific change, no new revision number was used, and the modifications related to the name change were not explicitly marked within the document.

This document outlines the fundamental principles and procedures governing professional services subcontracts, ensuring clarity and compliance with established legal and operational standards.

**General Principles and Interpretation:**
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Subcontractor shall act in a spirit of mutual trust and cooperation.
*   In this Subcontract, except where the context shows otherwise, words in the singular also mean in the plural and the other way round.
*   In this Subcontract, except where the context shows otherwise, words in the masculine also mean in the feminine and neuter.
*   This Subcontract is governed by the law of the Subcontract.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Subcontractor act in accordance with the health and safety requirements.

**Document Revision History:**
*   On June 6, 2004, the file name was changed from 0-CHG-80XX-R0 to 0-CHG-22XX-R0. Any references to 80XX must change to 22XX.
*   In August 2004, Legal performed a revision and update to simplify language, incorporated always-used special conditions into the standard conditions, and removed redundant provisions.
*   A name change conversion was implemented by 2005. No new revision number was used for the 2005 name change, and changes were not marked.
