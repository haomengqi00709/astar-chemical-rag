# Checking and Approval of Design

This page outlines the procedure for the checking and approval of all project documents, as governed by procedure 0-PRC-7004. Adherence to this procedure is mandatory to ensure the accuracy, compliance, and quality of all project deliverables.

All project documents **must** be checked and approved in accordance with [[0-prc-7004_checking_and_approval_of_design]].

### Roles and Responsibilities

*   **Discipline Lead:** Each design discipline **shall** have an individual assigned as a Discipline Lead, as per [[0-prc-2001_document_overview]]. Discipline Leads **shall** check all work as assigned by the procedures listed in Section 2 of the governing document.
*   **Discipline Approver:** Each design discipline **shall** have an individual assigned as a Discipline Approver, as per [[0-prc-2001_document_overview]]. Discipline Approvers **shall** approve all work as assigned by the procedures listed in Section 2 of the governing document. For more details on these roles, refer to [[discipline_lead_and_approver_roles]].

### Checking Process

Checking **shall** verify that the correct input data has been used in the document's preparation. This responsibility is usually assigned to the discipline responsible for producing the input documentation.

**Example:** The Process discipline is assigned to check [[equipment_proprietary_detail_drawing_prp]] because Process prepares [[process_and_electrical_data_sheets]], which contain the foundational information. Equipment Data Sheets themselves are prepared by Mechanical, based on the information provided in the Process Data Sheets.

### Approval Process

Approval signifies a critical stage in the document lifecycle, confirming several key aspects:

*   Approval **shall** signify that the work has been done accurately in accordance with appropriate procedures.
*   Approval **shall** signify that the work has been done accurately in accordance with the requirements of the Contract.
*   Approval **shall** signify that the work has been checked as required.
*   The approver holds the ultimate responsibility for the accuracy and compliance of the work.
*   If documents are derived from calculations, the approver is further signifying that the supporting calculations have also been checked and approved.

### Revisions

All revisions to documents originally checked and approved in accordance with this procedure **shall** further be checked and approved before re-issue. This ensures that all modifications maintain the same level of scrutiny and compliance as the original document.

Further details regarding specific checking and approval responsibilities are outlined in [[0-prc-7004_table_1_contents]].
