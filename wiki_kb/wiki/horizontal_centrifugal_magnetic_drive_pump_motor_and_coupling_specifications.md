# Horizontal Centrifugal Magnetic Drive Pump Motor and Coupling Specifications

For horizontal centrifugal magnetic drive pumps, motors **will** be appropriately sized to accommodate the run-out condition at the selected impeller size.

Magnetic drive couplings **will** possess torque ratings that are greater than or equal to the maximum motor torque for which the motor is rated.

For general information on pump drive systems, refer to [[horizontal_centrifugal_pump_drive_system]].
