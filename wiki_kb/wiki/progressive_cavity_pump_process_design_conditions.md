# Progressive Cavity Pump Process Design Conditions

The Progressive Cavity Pump Datasheet (Template `4-DST-XXXX-R0`) specifies critical process design conditions to ensure the pump's suitability for its intended application. The header for the second column in this section is "DESIGN / UNIT".

**Key Process Design Parameters:**
*   **Fluid Description:** A detailed description of the fluid being pumped.
*   **Composition:** The chemical composition of the fluid, expressed in %w/w.
*   **Solids Content:** The percentage of solids present in the fluid, expressed in %w/w.
*   **Solids Type:** The nature or type of solids present in the fluid.
*   **Flow Rate:** The nominal operating flow rate, in m3/h.
*   **Minimum Flow Rate:** The lowest acceptable flow rate for operation, in m3/h.
*   **TDH (estimated):** The estimated Total Dynamic Head, in m (fluid).
*   **NPSH Available:** The Net Positive Suction Head available at the pump's suction, in m (fluid).
*   **Temperature:** The operating temperature of the fluid, in oC.
*   **Density:** The density of the fluid, in kg/m3.
*   **Viscosity:** The viscosity of the fluid, in cP.
*   **Pressure at Suction:** The pressure at the pump's suction, in kPa(g).
*   **NPSH Required:** The Net Positive Suction Head required by the pump, in m.
