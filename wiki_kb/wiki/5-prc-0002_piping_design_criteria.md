# Piping Design Criteria (5-PRC-0002)

The Piping Design Criteria document, identified as 5-PRC-0002, serves as a foundational reference for all piping design activities within a project. This document **shall** contain a comprehensive set of requirements and guidelines to ensure consistency, compliance, and quality in piping system design.

**Content Requirements**
The Piping Design Criteria document **shall** include:
*   **Statutory Requirements:** All applicable legal and regulatory requirements pertaining to piping design and installation.
*   **Owner Requirements:** Specific demands and preferences stipulated by the project owner.
*   **Chemetics General Requirements:** Internal standards and best practices established by Chemetics for piping design.
*   **References to Chemetics Specifications:** Links or citations to relevant Chemetics specifications that provide detailed technical requirements for various aspects of piping.

**Roles and Responsibilities**
The preparation, checking, and approval of the Piping Design Criteria document are assigned to specific project personnel:
*   **Preparation:** The document **shall** be prepared by the Mechanical discipline.
*   **Checking:** The document **shall** be checked by the Project Engineer.
*   **Approval:** The document **shall** be approved by the Chief Mechanical Engineer.

This structured approach ensures that the Piping Design Criteria document is thorough, accurate, and officially endorsed, providing a robust framework for subsequent piping design and engineering activities.
