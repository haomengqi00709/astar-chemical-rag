# Subcontractor Sample Documents

Sample documents are provided to the Subcontractor to clarify the expected content and format of various deliverables. This includes both documents that will be provided to the Subcontractor by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and documents that the Subcontractor is required to prepare.

**Listing and Attributes:**
All relevant sample documents will be listed in the [[engineering_scope_checklists_0_lst_22xx|0-LST-8YXX: Engineering Scope Checklist]].

Key attributes for sample documents include:
*   Sample documents will retain the document number of their originating project.
*   They will be published as Revision A.
*   The publication status for sample documents will be “Sample Document (Subcontract [Number])”.

**Approval Process:**
*   Sample documents will be checked by the responsible [[discipline_lead_and_approver_roles|Discipline Lead]].
*   Sample documents will be approved by the [[project_engineer_pem_gate_review_responsibilities|Project Engineer]].
