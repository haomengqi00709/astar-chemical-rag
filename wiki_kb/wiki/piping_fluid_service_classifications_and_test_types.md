# Piping Fluid Service Classifications and Test Types

This section outlines the specific testing requirements based on the fluid service classification of piping systems. The Standard applies to all types of fluid services.

**Special Fluid Service Considerations**
*   **Category D Fluids:** In special cases where fluid service is classified as Category D, specific considerations of [[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping]] **must** apply.
*   **Category M Fluids:** For fluid services classified as Category M, special considerations of [[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping]] **must** apply. Unless otherwise specified, lines handling Category M fluids **must** be leak tested in accordance with B31.3 paragraph M345.1.
*   **Flammable Fluids:** Unless otherwise specified, lines handling flammable fluids **must** be leak tested in accordance with B31.3 paragraph M345.1.
*   **Fluid Service Classification Guide:** [[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping]] Appendix M provides a guide for classifying fluid services.
*   **"Pulp & Paper" Service:** In cases where fluid service is classified as "Pulp & Paper," requirements of this Standard may be relaxed. Such relaxation is described in the supplemental MoDo-Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Standard for Leak Testing of Piping, Spec. No. [[LT1_pulp_and_paper_leak_testing_standard]].

**Specific Test Types by Service**

1.  **Hydrostatic Testing**
    *   The primary steam line from the plant battery limits to the first pressure reducing station **will** be hydrostatically tested.
    *   [[hydrostatic_testing]] of the primary steam line **will** be in accordance with [[ASME_b31_1_power_piping]] paragraph 137.4.

2.  **Initial Service Leak Test**
    *   Lines handling reduced pressure steam (<15 psig) **will** be subjected to an [[initial_service_leak_test]].
    *   Lines handling cooling water **will** be subjected to an initial service leak test.
    *   Lines handling condensate **will** be subjected to an initial service leak test.
    *   Lines handling process water **will** be subjected to an initial service leak test.
    *   Lines handling potable water **will** be subjected to an initial service leak test.
    *   Lines handling instrument air **will** be subjected to an initial service leak test.
    *   Lines handling nitrogen **will** be subjected to an initial service leak test.
    *   Lines handling similar systems handling Category D fluids **will** be subjected to an initial service leak test.
    *   The [[initial_service_leak_test]] for these specified lines **will** be in accordance with [[ASME_b31_1_power_piping]] paragraph 137.7 or [[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping]] paragraph 345.7.

3.  **Pneumatic Leak Test**
    *   Lines not designed to support the weight of a test liquid **will** be subjected to a [[pneumatic_leak_testing]].
    *   Lines handling fluids which, together with residual water from a hydrostatic test, **will** create a highly corrosive environment **will** be subjected to a [[pneumatic_leak_testing]].
    *   [[pneumatic_leak_testing]] **will** be in accordance with [[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping]] paragraph 345.5.
    *   Special procedures for preparation and cleaning may also be specified for pneumatic leak tests.

4.  **Untested Lines**
    *   Lines open to atmosphere (vents, overflows, drains downstream of the last shutoff valve) need not be tested unless specifically required.

The Standard applies to all types of service, as defined in B31.1 para 100.1.1 and B31.3 para 300.1.1.

*   **Category M Fluids:** In special cases where fluid service is classified as Category M, special considerations of B31.3 will apply. Unless otherwise specified, lines handling Category M fluids will be leak tested in accordance with B31.3 para M345.1.
*   **Flammable Fluids:** Unless otherwise specified, lines handling flammable fluids will be leak tested in accordance with B31.3 para M345.1.
*   **Pulp & Paper Service:** In cases where fluid service is classified as "Pulp & Paper", requirements of this Standard may be relaxed. Relaxation for "Pulp & Paper" service is described in supplemental MoDo-Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Standard for Leak Testing of Piping, Spec. No. LT1.

B31.3 Appendix M provides a guide to classifying fluid services.
