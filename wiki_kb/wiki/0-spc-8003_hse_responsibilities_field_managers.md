# HSE Responsibilities of Field Managers

This standard specification (Standard-0-SPC-8003) outlines the specific Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) responsibilities assigned to Field Managers within Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. It serves as a critical reference document for personnel overseeing operations in the field.

**Scope:**
This document details the duties and expectations for Field Managers to ensure compliance with Company HSE objectives, project HSE objectives, and local regulations. It is particularly relevant for HSE Managers at [[hse_construction_sites]] and [[hse_commissioning_sites]].

Field Managers are expected to provide leadership and ensure the implementation of HSE policies and procedures within their areas of administrative control, as part of the broader [[hse_roles_and_responsibilities_company_wide]].
