# DCS Control Function Field Checkout Roles and Responsibilities

The [[8-PRC-0006_dcs_control_function_checkout|DCS Control Function Field Checkout]] requires a collaborative effort involving specialized personnel to ensure the Distributed Control System (DCS) is configured and operating correctly. Clear roles and responsibilities are defined to streamline the process and manage any identified deficiencies.

**Specialist and DCS Programmer**
*   A specialist and a DCS programmer **will** perform the checks outlined in this procedure. Their combined expertise ensures both the functional and programming aspects of the DCS are thoroughly vetted.
*   The specialist is responsible for filling out and signing the [[8-LST-0102_dcs_configuration_checkout_sheet|DCS Configuration Checkout Sheets]] after each object has been checked.

**Subcontractor Assistance**
*   Assistance from the subcontractor **is required** as needed throughout the checkout process. This support may include providing access to equipment, facilitating field operations, or offering specific system knowledge.

**I/O Testing Team**
For I/O testing, a minimum of two (2) specialists **are required** to facilitate the process:
*   **Console Specialist:** One specialist **must** be familiar with DCS software configuration and **must** be located at the operators’ console. This specialist monitors incoming signal status and generates outgoing signals, ensuring real-time verification from the control room perspective.
*   **Field Specialist:** The second specialist **must** be familiar with field devices. This specialist generates required field signals at local instruments/motor controls and monitors the movements of final control elements, verifying physical responses to DCS commands.

**Site Supervisor/Commissioning Manager**
*   The site supervisor/commissioning manager plays a critical role in addressing deficiencies. When deficiencies are recorded, they **are given** to the site supervisor/commissioning manager.
*   The site supervisor/commissioning manager **works with** the specialist and DCS programmer to correct identified deficiencies. This ensures that issues are promptly resolved and re-verified.

**Owner Acceptance**
*   Upon completion of the entire checkout process, the owner **will** indicate acceptance by signing both pages of the [[8-LST-0102_dcs_configuration_checkout_sheet|DCS Configuration Checkout Sheets]]. This formal sign-off signifies the owner's approval of the DCS's readiness for operation.

These defined roles ensure accountability and efficiency in the rigorous process of DCS control function field checkout.
