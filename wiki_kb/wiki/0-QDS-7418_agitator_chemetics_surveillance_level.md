# Agitator Chemetics Surveillance Level

For agitator equipment covered by Quality Data Sheet **0-QDS-7418**, surveillance by Chemetics will be conducted to Level 0.0. This surveillance level will be applied as specifically set out within the requirements of **0-QDS-7418**.

This level dictates the extent of Chemetics' involvement in witnessing inspections and reviewing documentation throughout the manufacturing and testing process.

Refer to [[0-QDS-7418_agitator_quality_data_sheet_administration]] for general information about this QDS.
