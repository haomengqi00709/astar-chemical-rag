# Project Schedule Preparation and Approval

The initial Project Schedule is a foundational document for project execution, and its preparation and approval follow a structured process.

Project Controls **will** be responsible for preparing the initial Project Schedule. This preparation utilizes information generated as part of the project planning phase, specifically drawing from [[0-prc-3001_project_planning]]. The development of the initial schedule is conducted in close consultation with the entire project team to ensure alignment and accuracy.

Checking of the initial Project Schedule **will** be a collaborative group activity, led by the Project Manager. All project team members listed on the [[0-lst-2004_project_responsibility_form]] **will** be involved in this checking process. In instances where project assignments have not yet been finalized, relevant Department Heads **will** sit in on the initial Project Schedule review.

Following this comprehensive review, the schedule **will** be formally published as the base-line for the project. The Project Manager **will** sign the base-line schedule as the checker, signifying their endorsement of its accuracy and completeness. Final approval of the base-line schedule **will** be granted by the Director of Projects.
