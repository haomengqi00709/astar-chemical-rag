# Acid Plant Chemically Resistant Brick Specifications

The selection and quality of acid-resistant bricks are paramount for the durability of linings in sulphuric acid plant equipment, as detailed in [[4-spc-0012_acid_resistant_linings_specification_overview]]. These bricks are used in drying and absorption towers and pump tanks that handle high concentrations of sulphuric acid (92-100%).

**Material Standards and Quality**
Acid resistant bricks **must** conform to ASTM C-279 type III. They **must** be free from blow holes, cracks, chips, voids, and any other defects that could compromise their structural integrity or chemical resistance.

**Acceptable Brick Types and Application**
Both fireclay and red shale acid resistant bricks are acceptable for use. However, specific conditions apply when red shale acid bricks are employed in strong acid vessel linings:
*   Absorber tower gas inlet brickwork **must** be constructed of fireclay acid brick.
*   Associated nozzle to vessel "Bullseye" blocks **must** also be constructed of fireclay acid brick.

**Vendor Proposal Requirements**
Details of the vendor's selected acid brick materials **must** be included in their proposal. This ensures that the materials meet the specified requirements and are appropriate for the intended service conditions.

For a broader understanding of material selection, refer to [[material_selection]]. Information on overall lining responsibilities can be found in [[acid_plant_lining_supplier_contractor_responsibilities]].

---
**Installation Considerations**
The surfaces on which the sidewall skewblock sit must be level to ensure proper brickwork installation and structural integrity.
