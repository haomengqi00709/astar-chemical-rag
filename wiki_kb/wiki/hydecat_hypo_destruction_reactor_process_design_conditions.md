# Hydecat Hypo Destruction Reactor Process Design Conditions

This page details the process design conditions for the Hydecat Hypo Destruction Reactor, as specified in Document ID 4-PDS-XXXX-R1. These conditions form the [[process_design_criteria_document]] for the reactor.

**Case Description**
The primary process design condition case is defined as "Design Basis". These conditions specifically apply to the Hypo Solution.

**Stream Composition**
*   **Measurement Unit:** % w/w, maximum.
*   **Components:** NaOH, NaOCl, NaCl, NaClO3.
*   **Note 3.0:** The excess caustic may be present in carbonate form.

**Flow Rates**
*   **Normal Flow:** Measured in m3/h (refer to Note 6.0 for additional details).
*   **Maximum Flow:** Measured in m3/h.

**Temperature**
*   **Measurement Unit:** oC.

**Density**
*   **Measurement Unit:** kg/m3.

**Pressure**
*   **Measurement Unit:** kPa(g), VTA (Vendor to Advise).
