# Restriction Orifice Inspection (5-PRC-0007)

The inspection of restriction orifices is a key part of the [[mechanical_checkout_piping_5_prc_0007|Piping Mechanical Checkout Procedure (5-PRC-0007)]], ensuring their correct installation and adherence to design specifications for flow control.

**Required Documentation for Restriction Orifice Checkout:**
*   [[piping_and_instrumentation_drawings|P&ID]]
*   [[restriction_orifice_list|Restriction Orifice List]]
*   [[pipe_isometric|Isometric]]

**Restriction Orifice Inspection Steps:**
*   **Flange Checks:** All flange checks for restriction orifices **shall** be performed as described in the [[flange_inspection_requirements_5_prc_0007|Flange Inspection Requirements]].
*   **Tag Number Verification:** Confirm that the restriction orifice tag number matches the [[piping_and_instrumentation_drawings|P&ID]] and [[pipe_isometric|isometric]].
*   **Material, Type, and Bore:** If possible, confirm that the material, type, and bore of the restriction orifice match the [[restriction_orifice_list|restriction orifice list]].
*   **Flow Direction:** Confirm that the [[restriction_orifice_installation|restriction orifice]] has been installed in the correct flow direction.
*   **Straight Runs:** Confirm that adequate upstream and downstream straight runs are present (where applicable) to ensure accurate flow restriction.

These checks are essential for the proper functioning of restriction orifices in controlling fluid flow rates within the piping system.
