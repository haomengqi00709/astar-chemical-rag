# Shutdown and Isolation Valves

Shutdown (SDV) and isolation valves are critical safety components designed to bring a process to a safe state or isolate equipment for maintenance.

**Shutdown Valve Requirements:**
*   All shutdown valves **will** have tight close-off capabilities.
*   All shutdown valves **will** meet the requirements of **ANSI B16.5**, ensuring their structural integrity and performance under specified conditions.
*   All shutdown valves **will** have pre-determined shutdown times to eliminate pipe hammering, protecting the piping system from damage.
*   All shutdown valves **will** have proper access for operation and maintenance.
*   Shutdown valve locations **will be** shown on location drawings.
*   Shutdown valves **WILL NOT BE LOCKED** to ensure immediate access in an emergency.
*   All shutdown valves **will be** supplied with open and closed proximity switches to provide feedback on their position to the control system.
*   Valve position **will be** indicated on a graduated reversible scale with the words OPEN and SHUT or CLOSED at the valve travel limits.

**Isolation Valves:**
For equipment isolation valves (in on/off service) in 25 NB line sizes or smaller where position feedback is not required, direct acting, in-line solenoid valves may be applied.
