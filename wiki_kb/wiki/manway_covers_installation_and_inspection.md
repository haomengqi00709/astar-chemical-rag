# Manway Covers Installation and Inspection

Manway covers are critical components for pressure vessels and tanks, providing access for internal inspection, maintenance, and cleaning while ensuring containment during operation. Proper installation and subsequent inspection are paramount for safety and operational integrity.

**Installation Requirements:**
*   Manway covers **must** be installed in accordance with the equipment design specifications and relevant engineering drawings.
*   Gaskets and sealing surfaces **must** be clean, free from defects, and correctly aligned to ensure a leak-tight seal.
*   Fasteners (bolts, nuts, washers) **must** be of the specified material and grade, and tightened to the manufacturer's recommended torque values using appropriate tools and sequences to ensure even loading.
*   After initial installation, manway covers **must** be seal welded where specified by design. These seal welds provide an additional layer of containment and prevent leakage, especially in critical service applications.

**Inspection and Testing:**
*   Following installation, all manway cover welds **must** be inspected. This inspection typically includes visual examination (VT) for surface defects, and may require additional [[non_destructive_examination_requirements]] such as liquid penetrant testing (PT) or magnetic particle testing (MT) to detect subsurface flaws.
*   Test records for all weld inspections **must** be available and documented as part of the equipment's quality assurance file.
*   The integrity of manway covers and their seals is often verified during [[pressure_and_leak_testing]] of the entire vessel or system.

Adherence to these installation and inspection protocols is essential to prevent leaks, ensure personnel safety, and maintain the operational reliability of the equipment.
