# Procurement Purchase Card Procedure (0-PRC-5006)

This procedure, identified as 0-PRC-5006, outlines the guidelines for the use of the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Procurement Department – Royal Bank Visa, Purchase Card, referred to as "the Card." It assigns responsibilities for information provision, approval, and records keeping related to the Card's usage.

### Purpose and Scope
The primary purpose of the Card is to streamline minor procurement processes by eliminating the need for petty cash, cash advances, and the preparation and issuance of internal purchase orders for approved purchases valued at less than $250.00. The Card is specifically intended for use by and for the [[procurement_and_logistics_strategy|Procurement Department]] and its staff.

**IMPORTANT:** The use of the Card **MUST NOT** be intended to avoid or bypass appropriate [[procurement_and_logistics_strategy|purchasing or payment procedures or policies]]. It is strictly for approved company business and **MUST NOT** be used for any personal purposes.

### Authorized Uses
The Card is designated for project-approved purchases with a value of less than $250.00. Additionally, it can be used for various procurement departmental approved requests, including:
*   Approved Staff Personal Development Programs (e.g., Seminars, Conferences, Accredited Courses).
*   Approved Procurement Subscriptions, Departmental Reference Books, and Magazines.
*   Approved Office Supplies and forms.
*   Approved Catering or dining services (e.g., "lunch and learns").
*   Approved miscellaneous expenditures.

The Card is accepted by any vendor or supplier that accepts Visa, both within Canada and internationally. It can be used for in-store purchases, mail, email, or fax orders. In-store purchases **WILL** require the cardholder's signature.

### Restrictions and Prohibitions
*   The Card **MUST** be used only for approved company business in compliance with this procedure.
*   No single transaction **MAY** exceed $2,500.00 in value, unless explicitly approved by the Vice President of Finance and manually approved by the Royal Bank.
*   Misuse of the Card or any use that does not comply with this procedure **WILL** result in severe consequences, up to and including termination of employment.

### Cardholder Responsibilities
The "cardholder" is identified as the Manager of Procurement. The cardholder **SHALL** be responsible for:
*   The security of the Card.
*   Ensuring the Card's compliance with these procedures.
*   Preparation of any required monthly reporting.
*   The Card's return in accordance with appropriate close-out procedures.

### Approval Authority
The use of the Card **WILL** be in compliance with defined levels of authority:
*   Individual approved purchases less than $2,500.00 **WILL** be approved by the Manager of Procurement.
*   Individual approved purchases greater than $2,500.00 **WILL** be approved by the Vice President of Finance or their approved designate.

### Ordering Process
All orders for a purchase **WILL** be placed by phone/fax, email, mail, or pickup. Orders **WILL** be placed by the cardholder or by approved Procurement Staff designated by the cardholder. Designates **WILL** always be approved Procurement Staff only.

### Documentation and Record Keeping
For non-project expenditures:
*   All proposed non-project expenditures **MUST** be detailed in writing.
*   All proposed non-project expenditures **MUST** be covered by an [[0-prc-5006_expenditure_request_form_appendix_a|Expenditure Request Form]]. This form **WILL** clearly identify the purpose of the expenditure(s) and provide details such as:
    *   Name and address of Vendor.
    *   Description of the purchase.
    *   Quantity to be purchased.
    *   Unit price of each item.
    *   Amount of Sales Tax.
    *   Shipping Charges, if applicable.

For project-related expenditures:
*   If proposed expenditures are for project-related items, the expenditure **MUST** be covered by an approved [[0_prc_5002_requisition_process_overview|project material requisition]].
*   The approved [[0_prc_5002_requisition_process_overview|project material requisition]] **MUST** provide appropriate [[cost_code_structure|cost code allocations]] for all items to be purchased.

The cardholder **WILL** maintain all appropriate records regarding the use of the Card, including all supporting documentation such as receipts and invoices for each transaction. These records **WILL** be subject to internal financial audits as determined by the company’s Finance Department.

### Financial Reporting and Audits
The Royal Bank **WILL** issue a detailed monthly statement of charges against the Card. The cardholder **WILL** review all expenditures to ensure compliance with this procedure and **WILL** assign appropriate [[cost_code_structure|cost code allocations]] to each identified expenditure. The cardholder **WILL** then forward the monthly statement to the Finance Department for payment.

### Purpose and Benefits
The use of the Card offers several benefits, including:
*   Eliminating the need for petty cash.
*   Eliminating the need for cash advances.
*   Reducing the need to prepare and issue internal purchase orders for approved purchases with a value of less than $250.00.

### General Principles and Restrictions
*   The Card is strictly for approved company business in compliance with this procedure and is not intended for any personal use.
*   Its use is not intended to avoid or bypass appropriate purchasing or payment procedures or policies.
*   Misuse of the Card or any use out of compliance with this procedure will result in severe consequences, up to and including termination of employment.
*   All use of the Card must be in compliance with defined levels of authority.

### Scope of Use
The Card is intended for use by and for the Procurement Department and/or its Staff. It will be used for:
*   Project-approved purchases with a value of less than $250.00.
*   Procurement departmental approved requests, which include:
    *   Approved Staff – Personal Development Programs (e.g., Seminars, Conferences, Accredited Courses).
    *   Approved Procurement Subscriptions, Departmental Reference Books and Magazines.
    *   Approved Office Supplies and forms.
    *   Approved Catering or dining services (e.g., “lunch and learns”).
    *   Approved miscellaneous expenditures.
*   The Card can be used for in-store purchases (requiring the cardholder's signature), mail, email, or fax orders.
*   It may be used with any vendor or supplier who accepts Visa throughout Canada or any other country.

### Cardholder Identification and Responsibilities
The "cardholder" for the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Procurement Department – Royal Bank Visa, Purchase Card will be identified as the Manager of Procurement. The cardholder is responsible for:
*   The security of the Card.
*   Ensuring the Card's compliance with these procedures.
*   Preparation of any required reporting on a monthly basis.
*   The Card’s return in accordance with appropriate “close-out” procedures.
*   Maintaining all appropriate records regarding the use of the Card.
*   Collecting and maintaining all supporting documentation attached to each and all transactions, including all receipts and invoices.
*   Reviewing all expenditures to ensure compliance with this procedure.
*   Assigning appropriate cost code allocations against each expenditure identified.
*   Forwarding the monthly statement to the Finance Department for payment.

### Transaction Limits and Approvals
*   No single transaction may exceed $2,500.00 in value, unless specifically approved by the Vice President of Finance and manually approved by the Royal Bank.
*   Individual approved purchases less than $2,500.00 will be approved by the Manager of Procurement.
*   Individual approved purchases greater than $2,500.00 will be approved by the Vice President of Finance or by their approved designate.

### Ordering Procedures
*   All orders for a purchase will be placed by phone/fax, email, mail, or pickup.
*   Orders will be placed by the cardholder, or by designates approved by the cardholder. Designates will always be approved Procurement Staff only.

### Project Expenditures
*   If proposed expenditures are for “Project” related items, the expenditure must be covered by an approved project material requisition.
*   Approved project material requisitions must provide appropriate cost code allocations for all items to be purchased.

### Record Keeping and Audits
*   All records maintained by the cardholder will be subject to internal financial audits as may be determined by the company’s Finance Department.
*   The Royal Bank will issue a detailed statement of charges applied against the Card on a monthly basis.
