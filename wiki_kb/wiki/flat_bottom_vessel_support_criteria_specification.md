# Flat Bottom Vessel Support Criteria Specification (4-SCR-0001)

This specification, identified as Document ID 4-SCR-0001, outlines the support criteria for flat bottom vessels on concrete and grout foundations. It is a standard document for projects undertaken by Chemetics, a division of Aker Kvaerner Canada Inc.

**Document Details:**
*   **Document ID:** 4-SCR-0001
*   **Client Name:** Aker Kvaerner
*   **Project Title:** Flat Bottom Vessel on Concrete & Grout
*   **Equipment Name & Number:** Chemetics
*   **Location:** A Division of Aker Kvaerner Canada Inc.
*   **Client Document Number:** Standard-4-SCR-0001-R2

**General Requirements:**
*   **Mandatory Requirement:** Loads shall be applied at Datum.
*   **Mandatory Requirement:** A minimum "Bolt to Edge" distance of 150 mm must be maintained for all anchor bolts.
*   Support criteria are based on the detailed information provided in the following equipment drawings:
    *   Equipment Drawing Number 4-OTL-XXXX, Revision X
    *   Equipment Drawing Number 4-DTL-XXXX, Revision X
