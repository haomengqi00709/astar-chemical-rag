# Quality Data Sheet (QDS) Template Administration

This wiki page details the administrative framework for the Quality Data Sheet (QDS) template, identified by the client document number Standard-0-QDS-7XXX-R2. This template is a critical component in the quality management system for projects undertaken by Aker Kvaerner, with Chemetics serving as the template provider.

The primary purpose of a Quality Data Sheet is to systematically define and document the quality requirements for specific equipment or components throughout their lifecycle, from manufacturing to installation. It serves as a comprehensive checklist and record for quality control activities, ensuring that all aspects of design, fabrication, inspection, and testing meet the specified standards.

The `7XXX` in the client document number `Standard-0-QDS-7XXX-R2` functions as a placeholder. This allows the template to be adapted for various projects and specific equipment types by replacing `7XXX` with a unique identifier. For instance, in the context of the "Rake & Thickener" project, this template would be customized to reflect the specific quality requirements for the rake and thickener equipment.

A typical QDS covers a broad range of information, including:
*   **Inspection and Test Plan (ITP) requirements:** Detailing hold points, witness points, and review points for client and third-party inspectors.
*   **Material Certifications:** Specifying the required documentation for raw materials, such as Mill Test Certificates (MTCs).
*   **Non-Destructive Examination (NDE) requirements:** Outlining methods (e.g., radiography, ultrasonic testing, magnetic particle testing) and acceptance criteria.
*   **Welding Procedure Specification (WPS) and Welder Qualification Records (WQR):** Ensuring welding activities comply with relevant codes and standards.
*   **Pressure and Leak Testing:** Defining test pressures, mediums, durations, and acceptance criteria.
*   **Surface Preparation and Coating/Lining Inspections:** Verifying adherence to protective coating specifications.
*   **Final Inspections:** Covering dimensional checks, nameplate verification, and overall workmanship.
*   **Packing and Shipping Requirements:** Ensuring equipment is adequately protected for transit.

The consistent application of this QDS template, provided by Chemetics, ensures that all vendors and fabricators adhere to Aker Kvaerner's stringent quality standards, thereby mitigating risks and enhancing the reliability and longevity of installed equipment.
