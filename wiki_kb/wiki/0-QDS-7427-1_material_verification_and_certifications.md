# SS Tubing Tier 1 Material Verification and Certifications

For Stainless Steel Tubing Tier 1, stringent requirements are in place for material verification and associated certifications to ensure compliance with specified standards.

**Material Verification:**
Material verification **must** be conducted in accordance with [ASTM A249-02]. This standard specifies the requirements for welded austenitic steel boilers, superheater, heat-exchanger, and condenser tubes.

**Mill Test Certificates:**
Mill Test Certificates are a required quality record, designated as "FP" (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package). These certificates **must** be provided by the vendor and are subject to Chemetics Witness surveillance. This ensures that the material properties and chemical composition meet the specified requirements directly from the mill.

**Material Testing:**
General material testing is also a required quality record, designated as "FP". All material testing **must** be performed under Chemetics Witness surveillance, confirming that the physical and mechanical properties of the tubing comply with the project specifications.

These measures collectively ensure the integrity and quality of the raw materials used in the fabrication of Stainless Steel Tubing Tier 1.
