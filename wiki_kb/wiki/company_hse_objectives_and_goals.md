# Company HSE Objectives and Goals

Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and Aker Kvaerner are committed to maintaining the highest standards of Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) across all operations. This commitment is reflected in the company's core values, objectives, and specific goals.

**Core Values**
Health, Safety, and Environment is recognized as one of the core values for both Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and Aker Kvaerner. This fundamental principle underscores the importance placed on protecting personnel, the environment, and company assets.

**Employee Rights**
The company firmly believes that all employees have a fundamental right to a safe working environment. This right is a cornerstone of the company's HSE philosophy and guides all related policies and procedures.

**HSE Goals**
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and Aker Kvaerner are dedicated to operating the business without:
*   **Personnel injuries:** Striving for a workplace free from all forms of physical harm to individuals.
*   **Occupational illness:** Aiming to prevent any health conditions arising from or exacerbated by the work environment.
*   **Unplanned discharges to the environment:** Committing to responsible environmental stewardship by eliminating uncontrolled releases of substances.
*   **Zero material damage and zero financial loss:** Protecting company assets and financial stability by preventing incidents that lead to damage or economic detriment.

These objectives and goals represent the company's unwavering commitment to fostering a culture of safety, health, and environmental responsibility.
