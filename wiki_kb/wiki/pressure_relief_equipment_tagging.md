# Pressure Relief Equipment Tagging

This page outlines the requirements for tagging and numbering pressure relief equipment within project documentation, particularly as it pertains to [[pid_development_stages_and_responsibilities#Stage 3 P&ID (Approved for Design - Team P&ID Review, Instrumentation & Control Design)|Stage 3 P&ID development]].

**Instrument Tagging:**
Pressure relief equipment **shall** carry an instrument tag. This ensures proper identification and integration into control and safety systems, facilitating tracking and maintenance.

**Number Assignment:**
Mechanical personnel **shall** consult with an unspecified role (e.g., Process or Instrumentation) to assign a unique number for each piece of pressure relief equipment. This collaborative approach ensures consistency and adherence to project standards for equipment identification.
