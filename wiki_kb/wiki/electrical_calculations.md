# Electrical Calculations

Electrical calculations are essential for designing a safe, reliable, and efficient electrical power system. These calculations determine critical parameters for equipment sizing, protection, and system performance.

**Required Calculations**
Calculations **shall** be required for:
*   Fault levels within the plant downstream of the point of connection of the Owner’s power supply. These power calculations are required for determining short circuit currents available at various voltage levels for specifying switchgear, MCCs, etc.
*   Electrical equipment data sheets.
*   Voltage drops due to motor starting at the various voltage levels. Calculations are required for motor starting voltage drops on the power system. The Owner has set a maximum voltage drop allowable on the incoming power supply.
*   Protective relay settings for all relays in all switchgear.
*   Protective relay settings for all relays in all motor controllers.

**Owner's Requirements and Coordination**
Owner’s power supply parameters, including maximum and minimum MVA available at the point of connection to the incoming switchgear supplied by the engineering firm, **must** be considered.
Protective relay settings **are required** for all protective relays on the project and **must** be coordinated with the Owner’s relay settings.

**Document Numbering**
Calculations **shall** be numbered according to 9-LST-0001, List Electrical Document Numbering Standard.
