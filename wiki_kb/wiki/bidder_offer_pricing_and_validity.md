# Bidder Offer Pricing and Validity

Bidders must adhere to specific requirements regarding the validity and pricing structure of their offers for Goods and Services.

**Offer Validity:**
The Bidder’s offer to supply the Goods and Services will be irrevocable for a period of 60 days following the submission date. This ensures stability in the bidding process for Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
The Bidder's offer will be considered valid for each individual line item for which a separate price is quoted. This validity applies regardless of whether offers for other line items are accepted, allowing for partial acceptance of a bid.

**Pricing Structure:**
*   **Mandatory:** Bidders must complete the Offer Summary form to provide a comprehensive description of their offer.
*   **Mandatory:** A separate fixed price must be quoted for each line item of the [[goods_and_services_definitions|Goods and Services]]. This ensures clarity and allows for detailed cost analysis.
*   **Mandatory:** Bidders must quote prices in single or multiple source currencies for each line item, as specified in the RFQ.
*   **Mandatory:** Bidders must assume no risk for exchange rate fluctuations. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics will manage any such risks.

These conditions are designed to ensure clear, consistent, and financially stable offers, enabling Aker Kvaerner Chemetics to make informed procurement decisions.
