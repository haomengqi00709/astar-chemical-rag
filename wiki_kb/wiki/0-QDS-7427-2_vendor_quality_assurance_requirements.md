# Stainless Steel Tubing Tier 2 Vendor Quality Assurance Requirements

The Vendor's Quality Assurance program for Stainless Steel Tubing Tier 2 **shall** comply with the requirements specified in this Quality Data Sheet and with [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance]].

**Quality Plan:**
The Vendor **shall** prepare a Quality Plan in accordance with [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance]]. If a Quality Plan is not required for a specific scope, the corresponding cells in the Quality Data Sheet **shall** be deleted.

**Compliance:**
The Vendor's overall Quality Assurance system **shall** ensure adherence to all stipulations within this Quality Data Sheet, covering aspects from [[0-QDS-7427-2_material_verification_and_certifications]] to [[0-QDS-7427-2_packing_and_shipping_requirements]].

**Chemetics Surveillance:**
The level of surveillance by Chemetics is detailed in [[0-QDS-7427-2_chemetics_surveillance_level]].
