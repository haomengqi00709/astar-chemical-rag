# Quality Record Definitions for 0-QDS-7440

This page provides specific definitions for quality record requirements as referenced within Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7440. These definitions clarify how documentation for various items and activities should be managed.

**Quality Record Types:**
*   **AP (As Prepared):** This designation means that the quality record is required "As Prepared." The document **must** be prepared by the vendor and submitted as part of the overall documentation.
*   **FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package):** This designation means that the quality record is required to be "Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package." The document **must** be part of the final compilation of quality assurance documentation.

These definitions are crucial for understanding the documentation requirements for items such as [[0-QDS-7440_material_verification_and_documentation]], [[0-QDS-7440_final_inspections]], and [[0-QDS-7440_packing_and_shipping_requirements]]. For broader definitions of quality records, refer to [[quality_record_definitions]].
