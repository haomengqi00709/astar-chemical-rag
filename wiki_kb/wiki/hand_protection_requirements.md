# Hand Protection Requirements

This page outlines the requirements for hand protection, including general and specialized gloves, for various tasks and materials, as specified in Document ID 0-PRC-8015.

**General Hand Protection**
Standard hand protection gloves are recommended when working with rough or unfinished materials to prevent abrasions and minor injuries.

**Specialized Hand Protection**
Special hand protection **is required** when handling solvents or chemicals. For these situations, Material Safety Datasheets **must** be consulted for appropriate glove materials that offer adequate protection against the specific substances being handled.

**Shop Employee Requirements**
Shop employees **are required** to wear leather-palmed gloves for general tasks involving rough materials and potential impact.

**Training**
Employees **will** be provided with and trained in the use of any specific hand protection requirements at the site-specific [[hse_orientation_overview|Health and Safety orientation]].

For general PPE guidelines, refer to [[0-prc-8015_personal_protective_equipment_policy]].
