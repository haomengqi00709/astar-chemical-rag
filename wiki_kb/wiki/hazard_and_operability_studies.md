# Hazard and Operability (HAZOP) Studies

Hazard and Operability (HAZOP) studies are a critical component of risk management for new plant designs and design modifications within Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Purpose of HAZOP Studies:**
The primary purposes of performing a HAZOP study are to:
*   Reduce operability risk to an acceptable level.
*   Reduce risk to human health to an acceptable level.
*   Comply with local environment regulations.
*   Comply with community regulations.

**Methodology and Leadership:**
A HAZOP study **must** be led by a qualified study leader. The study **must** utilize the HAZOP method recognized by the American Society of Chemical Engineers.

Detailed information on the HAZOP method can be found in:
*   [[1-prc-0004_hazard_and_operability_studies_procedure]]
*   [[plant_layout_review]] (Standard-5-PRC-0005 Plant Layout Review)

HAZOP studies are a mandatory requirement for all new plant designs and design modifications, particularly relevant for [[hse_commissioning_sites]].
