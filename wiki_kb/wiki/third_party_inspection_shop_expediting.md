# Third Party Inspection - Shop Expediting

When shop expediting visits are specifically requested by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics (AKC), the Third Party Inspection Agency **will** utilize experienced shop or field expeditors for these assignments.

It is a mandatory requirement that inspectors **shall not** be utilized for shop expediting visits, unless explicit agreement has been obtained from AKC. This distinction ensures that personnel with the appropriate skillset are deployed for each specific task.

In all matters pertaining to expediting, the Agency **will** receive direction and guidance from the [[procurement_document_responsibilities]] within AKC. This ensures alignment with AKC's procurement strategy and project timelines.

In expediting matters, direction will be provided by the Procurement Department.
