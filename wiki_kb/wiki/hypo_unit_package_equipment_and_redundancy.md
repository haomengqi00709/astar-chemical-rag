# Hypo Unit Package Equipment and Redundancy

This page outlines key equipment specifications and redundancy requirements for the Hypo Unit Package.

**Scrubber System:**
*   The unit is designed to have 2 scrubber pumps and 2 scrubber fans.
*   These components are equipped with auto switch-over functionality.
*   All scrubber pumps and fans must be connected to emergency power.

**Bleach Recycle Pump:**
*   The unit may have only 1 bleach recycle pump.
*   This pump does not have to be on emergency power.
