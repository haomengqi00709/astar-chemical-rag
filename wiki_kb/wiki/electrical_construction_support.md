# Electrical Construction Support

Electrical construction support involves providing technical assistance and answering site questions during the construction phase of a project. This ensures that electrical installations proceed smoothly and in accordance with design.

**Roles and Responsibilities**
Table 2 identifies who will be responsible for electrical design office support during construction. The engineering office **will** assist with answering site questions pertaining to the supply of the Transformer/Rectifier (T/R), Central Processing Unit (CPU), and bus bars.

Site questions pertaining to the installation of the T/R, CPU, and bus bars **will** be the responsibility of DDE. Site questions pertaining to the supply and installation of all remaining electrical equipment **will** also be the responsibility of DDE.

The DDE is expected to retain original design personnel to answer questions from the plant site during construction. It is recommended that the DDE assign more than one individual to answer construction questions to ensure availability and familiarity with the design.

**Field Instrument and DCS Support**
Refer to procedure Field Instrument Design and Specification, 8-PRC-0003 for field instrument construction support information.
Refer to procedure Control System Design and Specification, 8-PRC-0004 for DCS construction support information.

**Roles during Construction Support:**
*   Field instrument involves DDE.
*   Electrical equipment is a component type during construction support.
*   DCS involves DDE.
