# Flow Measurement - Other Types

Beyond differential pressure, magnetic, and vortex meters, several other flow measurement technologies are utilized for specific applications requiring high accuracy, mass flow, or particular rangeability.

**Coriolis Flowmeters:**
Coriolis type flow meters **will** be used for mass flow measurement of fluids. They provide highly accurate signals for:
*   Flow
*   Density
*   Temperature

**Installation of Coriolis Meters:**
*   Piping connected to the Coriolis meters **will** be mounted securely.
*   The flow meter **will not** be used for supporting the adjacent piping.
*   Vibration **will not** be transferred through the piping system to the Coriolis meters, as external vibration can affect their accuracy.

Coriolis flowmeters are also used for [[flow_measurement_general|custody transfer metering]] due to their precision.

**Positive Displacement and Turbine Meters:**
These meters are selected for applications demanding superior accuracy or rangeability compared to orifice or vortex flowmeters. They are also employed for [[flow_measurement_general|custody transfer metering]] of process fluids.

**Thermal Dispersion (TD) Mass Flow Measurement:**
The Thermal Dispersion (TD) mass flow measurement technique may be applied for flow measurement, particularly for clean gases, offering a direct mass flow reading.

**Variable Area Meters:**
*   For variable area meters, the normal flowrate **will be** between 50% and 80% of capacity.
*   Variable area flowmeters **will** be selected from the supplier's standard range.
*   If a line size smaller than 50 NB is used, an integral orifice meter or variable area meter **will** generally be used, providing suitable measurement for smaller pipe diameters.
