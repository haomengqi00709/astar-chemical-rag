# Electrical Equipment Data Sheets

Electrical Equipment Data Sheets provide detailed technical information for specific electrical components and systems required for a project. These sheets are essential for procurement and ensuring equipment meets design specifications.

**General Content Requirements**
Equipment data sheets **shall** contain:
*   Design conditions.
*   All applicable design codes and standards.
*   Required materials of construction for enclosures, bus work, wiring, and control devices.
*   Connected AC input power parameters.
*   Output power parameters.
*   Any accessories to be supplied.

Electrical equipment data sheets are **not** required for bulk materials. They **shall** be specific about the equipment required for the project.

**Specific Equipment Data Sheet Requirements**

*   **Transformer/Rectifier Data Sheets:**
    *   Type of transformer.
    *   Type of rectifier.
    *   Type of current measurement.
    *   Type of control package, including alarms and shutdowns, and auxiliary equipment.
    *   Voltage and current output of the rectifier.
*   **CPU Data Sheets:**
    *   Type of controls, including alarms and shutdowns.
    *   Voltage and current output of the rectifier.
*   **Bus Bars Data Sheets:**
    *   Specific requirements.
*   **MV Switchgear Data Sheets:**
    *   Circuit breaker sizes.
    *   Bus bracing information.
    *   Ampere ratings of the buses.
    *   Protective relaying devices.
    *   Type of control for circuit breakers.
    *   Type of load interrupter switches.
    *   [[final_single_line_diagrams]] will be referred to when completing MV Switchgear data sheets.
*   **MCCs Data Sheets:**
    *   Incoming circuit breaker information.
    *   Bus ampere ratings and bracing.
    *   Quantities of motor starters.
    *   NEMA sizes of the motor starters.
    *   Quantities of feeder circuit breakers.
    *   Type of communications requirements in the motor starters.
    *   [[final_single_line_diagrams]] will be referred to when completing MCCs data sheets.
*   **Power Transformers Data Sheets:**
    *   Type of transformer (liquid or dry).
    *   Type of primary and secondary connections.
    *   Protective and auxiliary devices.
*   **LV Power Distribution Centre Data Sheets:**
    *   Bus bracing requirements.
    *   Type of incoming circuit breaker.
    *   Quantities and types of feeder circuit breakers.
    *   Type of protective relaying devices.
    *   Reference to [[final_single_line_diagrams]].
*   **Motors Data Sheets:**
    *   Type of motor (induction, wound rotor, synchronous).
    *   Size (hp or kw) and rpm.
    *   Mounting position.
    *   Location of the terminal box.
    *   Accessories (space heater, winding RTDs, bearing RTDs, vibration switches).
    *   Locations of the terminal boxes for the accessories.
    *   Motor enclosure.
    *   Motor efficiencies.
    *   Power factor.
    *   Motor currents.
    *   Reference to [[motor_list_electrical]].
*   **Emergency Generator Data Sheets:**
    *   Type of engine.
    *   Engine controls requirement.
    *   Generator output circuit breaker.
    *   Engine cooling system.
    *   Fuel storage tank.
*   **Emergency Transfer Switch Data Sheets:**
    *   Automatic controls required on the ‘normal’ and the ‘emergency’ power sides of the switch.
    *   Location of the switch and manual operating provision.
*   **UPS Data Sheets:**
    *   Battery charger type.
    *   Battery type.
    *   Inverter parameters.
    *   Type of bump-less transfer switch (contactor or static).
    *   Provision for bypass, alarms and shutdowns outputs.
*   **Harmonic Filter Equipment Data Sheets:**
    *   Capacitor type.
    *   Reactor type.
    *   Grounding devices.
    *   Enclosure types.
    *   Safety devices.

**Electrical Room HVAC Requirements Data Sheets**
A data sheet **is required** for each electrical room and each control room. These data sheets **shall** list:
*   All electrical and instrumentation equipment located in the room.
*   The amount of heat (kw or Btu) generated by each piece of equipment.
*   Light fixtures.
*   Any other equipment such as computers, test equipment.

**Document Numbering and Usage**
Data sheets **shall** be numbered according to 9-LST-0001, List Electrical Document Numbering Standard. The number for each piece of electrical equipment will be found in 9-PRC-0001, Procedure Electrical Equipment Identification. Equipment data sheets will accompany [[electrical_equipment_specifications]] for electrical equipment requisitioning and purchasing.
