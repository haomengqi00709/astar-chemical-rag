# Welding Nickel/Iron/Chromium Alloy (INCOLOY 825) Overview (4-SPC-0015)

This Standard, identified as 4-SPC-0015, defines the workmanship and quality requirements for welded fabrications utilizing nickel-base alloys, specifically Incoloy 825® (UNS N08825) from Huntington Alloys Inc., which is grouped under No. P45 of the ASME Code. These fabrications are intended for use in aggressively corrosive environments.

**Scope and Application**
The Standard applies when explicitly referenced on purchase orders and other project documents. It comprehensively covers welding and other fabrication requirements for a range of equipment, including:
*   Vessels
*   Heat exchangers
*   Tanks
*   Piping
*   Miscellaneous welded items

**Key Definitions**
*   **Codes, etc.:** Refers to the latest edition of relevant codes and standards ruling at the date of contract.
*   **Agreement in Writing:** **Must** mean a formal submission in writing by letter, not merely an inclusion in approval drawings.
*   **Inspector:** Refers to an Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Quality Assurance Inspector or another Authorized Representative.
