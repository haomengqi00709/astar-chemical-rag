# Engineering Scope Checklists (0-LST-22XX)

The [[engineering_scope_checklists_0_lst_22xx|0-LST-22XX: Engineering Scope Checklists]] are essential documents that itemize the specific work and deliverables to be prepared by both Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Subcontractor within the framework of an engineering subcontract. Data available in SQL: 0-LST-22XX.

**Preparation and Basis:**
Checklists are prepared for each [[major_work_division_codes|Major Work Division]] by the responsible [[discipline_lead_and_approver_roles|Discipline Lead]]. They are developed using [[0_lst_2001_engineering_scope_checklist|0-LST-2001: Engineering Scope Checklist]] as a foundational basis. Data available in SQL: 0-LST-2001.

**Content and Deliverables:**
These checklists are utilized to clearly state critical dates for:
*   Document deliveries from Aker Kvaerner Chemetics to the Subcontractor.
*   Other necessary actions by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics that are required for the Subcontractor to provide the services.
*   Any document deliveries required from the Subcontractor prior to the Completion Date specified in the [[engineering_subcontract_procedure_overview|Subcontract Agreement]].

**Document Numbering:**
Checklists will be assigned an identification number in the format 8YXX.
*   'Y' represents the [[document_numbering_rules|Major Work Division identifier]], as defined in [[document_numbering_rules|0-PRC-0002: Document Numbering]].
*   'XX' is identical to the sequential series used for the [[engineering_subcontract_procedure_overview|Engineering Subcontract Agreement]], as specified in [[engineering_subcontract_procedure_overview|0-PRC-2202]].

**Publication and Approval Process:**
*   The checklist will be published with the status “Approved for Design”.
*   The [[document_publishing_methods|publishing of Project Documents]] is governed by [[document_publishing_methods|0-PRC-0005]].
*   The checklist will be checked by the [[project_engineer_pem_gate_review_responsibilities|Project Engineer]].
*   The checklist will be approved by the [[discipline_lead_and_approver_roles|Discipline Chief Engineer]].

Checklists are prepared, one for each Major Work Division, by the responsible Discipline Lead. They will use [[0-lst-2001_engineering_scope_checklist|0-LST-2001: Engineering Scope Checklist]] as a basis.

**Purpose and Content:** These checklists are used to state the dates for:
*   Document deliveries by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics to the Subcontractor.
*   Other Aker Kvaerner Chemetics actions necessary for the Subcontractor to provide the services.
*   Any document deliveries by the Subcontractor prior to the Completion Date stated in the Subcontract Agreement.

**Document Numbering:** Checklists are numbered with an identification number of 8YXX. 'Y' represents the Major Work Division identifier, and 'XX' is identical to the sequential series used for the Engineering Subcontract Agreement (as defined in [[0-prc-2202_form_of_engineering_subcontract|0-PRC-2202]]). Document numbering is governed by [[0-prc-0002_document_numbering|0-PRC-0002]].

**Publication and Approval:** The checklist will be published with the status “Approved for Design”. Publishing of Project Documents is governed by [[0-prc-0005_publishing_of_project_documents|0-PRC-0005]]. The checklist will be checked by the Project Engineer and approved by the Discipline Chief Engineer.

**Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Deliverables:** Documents prepared by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for the Subcontractor will include all design criteria documents.
