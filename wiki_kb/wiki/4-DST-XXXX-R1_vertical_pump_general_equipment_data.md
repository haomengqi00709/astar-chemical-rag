# Template Vertical Pump General Equipment Data

This page details the general equipment data for the Template Vertical Pump Datasheet (4-DST-XXXX-R1). This information provides a high-level overview of the pump's configuration and identification within the project.

**Equipment Type and Configuration:**

*   **Pump Type:** Specified as "Vertical [Type]". The specific type (e.g., cantilever, sump, line shaft) will be detailed here.
*   **Impeller Type:** Describes the design of the pump's impeller (e.g., open, semi-open, closed, vortex).

**Identification and Quantity:**

*   **Tag No.:** A unique alphanumeric identifier assigned to the pump. Refer to [[plant_equipment_identification_4_prc_0001]] for comprehensive tagging guidelines.
*   **Location:** The designated area or unit where the pump will be installed.
*   **Quantity:**
    *   **Operating:** The number of pumps required for continuous operation.
    *   **Installed Spare:** The number of spare pumps installed and ready for immediate use.

This general data forms the basis for more detailed specifications found in other sections of the datasheet and related [[equipment_data_sheets_4_dst_xxxx]].
