# Field Erected CS Tank - Shop Final Inspections

For the "Field Erected CS Tank - Shop" component, a series of final inspections are required to ensure the quality and conformity of the fabricated items. These inspections are to be detailed in the Vendor's Inspection and Test (I & T) Plan.

**Inspection Requirements and Chemetics Surveillance:**
*   **Dimensional inspection:** Required in Vendor I & T Plan. Chemetics surveillance: **'Witness'**.
*   **Contour & Finish of Welds inspection:** Required in Vendor I & T Plan. Chemetics surveillance: **'Witness'**.
*   **Surfaces Free from Defects inspection:** Required in Vendor I & T Plan. Chemetics surveillance: **'Witness'**.
*   **Cleanliness inspection:** Required in Vendor I & T Plan. Chemetics surveillance: **'Witness'**.
*   **Review QA Documentation:** Required as **FP** (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package). Chemetics surveillance: **'Witness'** and **'Hold'**. The 'Hold' point signifies that Chemetics' approval is required before proceeding to the next stage.

These inspections, coupled with Chemetics' surveillance, ensure that all fabricated components meet the specified quality standards before shipment.
