# Bus Bar Installation Details

Bus bar installation details provide specific instructions and component requirements for supporting bus bars, particularly those located between Transformer/Rectifiers (T/R) and cells, and between cells. These details ensure proper isolation and structural integrity.

**Content of Bus Bar Installation Details**
Bus bar installation details **shall** show:
*   Support brackets required for the bus bars located between the T/R and the cells.
*   Support brackets required for the bus bars between the cells, as necessary.
*   Methods of supporting the brackets from the floors.
*   Methods of supporting the brackets from the walls.
*   Methods of supporting the brackets from the ceiling of the building.
*   The various styles of support brackets required.
*   The quantities of each type of support bracket.

Insulating materials **are required** on the brackets to isolate the bus bars from the bracket materials, ensuring electrical safety and preventing short circuits.

**Project Specificity**
Bus bar details **shall** be project specific and not generic, addressing the unique requirements of each installation.
