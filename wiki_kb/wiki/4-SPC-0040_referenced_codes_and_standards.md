# 4-SPC-0040 Referenced Codes and Standards

Specification 4-SPC-0040 mandates adherence to a comprehensive set of national and international codes, standards, and internal Aker Kvaerner Chemetics specifications for the shop fabrication of metallic process vessels and components. The Vendor **must** ensure compliance with the latest editions of these documents.

**Mandatory Codes and Standards:**
The following codes and standards form an integral part of this specification:
*   [[ASME_bpvc_section_ii_materials]] Part A (Ferrous Material Specifications)
*   [[ASME_bpvc_section_ii_materials]] Part B (Nonferrous Material Specifications)
*   [[ASME_bpvc_section_ii_materials]] Part C (Specifications for Welding Rods, Electrodes, and Filler Metals)
*   [[ASME_section_v_non_destructive_examination]] (Nondestructive Examination)
*   [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels]] (Rules for Construction of Pressure Vessels)
*   [[ASME_section_ix_welding_and_brazing_qualifications]] (Welding and Brazing Qualifications)
*   [[API_620_low_pressure_storage_tanks]] (Recommended Rules for the Design and Construction of Large Welded Low Pressure Storage Tanks)
*   [[API_650_welded_steel_tanks]] (Welded Steel Tanks for Oil Storage)
*   Standards of the Tubular Exchanger Manufacturer’s Association (TEMA)

**Owner Specifications:**
Owner Specifications, as provided by the Owner, **must** be used in conjunction with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics specifications.

**Jurisdictional and Service-Specific Requirements:**
Other national or international codes and/or standards may replace or be used in conjunction with the listed codes where equipment is manufactured or installed in specific countries. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics will provide details to the Vendor if such additional codes or standards are applicable. Furthermore, additional standards may apply depending on the equipment's service, such as for chlorine, hydrogen peroxide, and oxygen applications.
