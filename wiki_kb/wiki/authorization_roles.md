# Authorization Roles

This page defines the key roles and responsibilities associated with authorizing work and financial commitments within Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, as referenced in documents like [[0_chg_6_subcontractor_work_authorization]].

**Authorizing Entity:**
All formal authorizations for work, particularly those involving subcontractors, are issued for and on behalf of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. This signifies that the company, as a legal entity, is the ultimate authority behind the approval, ensuring that all commitments are made under its corporate responsibility.

**Designated Authorizing Role: Site Manager:**
The specific role designated to provide authorization for subcontractor work, as detailed in Document **0-CHG-6**, is the Site Manager. The Site Manager holds the authority to approve the commencement of work, often following the acceptance of a subcontractor's quotation. This role is critical for on-site project execution, ensuring that work proceeds according to plan and within approved parameters.

The Site Manager's authorization is a crucial step in the project lifecycle, linking the administrative and contractual aspects of subcontractor engagement with the practical execution of work. This role is integral to the broader framework of [[roles_and_documentation_management]] and ensures accountability in project operations.
