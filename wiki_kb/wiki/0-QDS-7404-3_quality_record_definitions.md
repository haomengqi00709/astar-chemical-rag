# Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7404-3 Quality Record Definitions

This page defines the terminology used for Quality Record requirements within Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7404-3, pertaining to the Electrostatic Precipitator Polypropylene Piping project.

**Definitions for Quality Record Requirements:**

*   **AP (As Prepared):**
    This designation indicates that the Quality Record is required "As Prepared" by the vendor. This means the document or record is to be submitted in its original form as generated by the vendor.

*   **FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package):**
    This designation signifies that the Quality Record **must** be included as a component of the final Quality Assurance Documentation Package. Records marked "FP" are essential for the comprehensive project documentation and final handover.

Understanding these definitions is crucial for proper documentation management and compliance with project quality standards. For related definitions and requirements, refer to [[0-QDS-7404-2_quality_record_definitions]] and [[0-QDS-7_quality_record_definitions]].
