# Agitator Field Inspection Checklist Items (4-FIC-XX01-R0)

This page details the mandatory inspection items for agitator equipment as specified in the 4-FIC-XX01-R0 Field Inspection Checklist. These items cover various aspects of the agitator's mechanical integrity, installation, and operational readiness.

**Mandatory Inspection Requirements:**
*   Correct Nameplate **must** be installed.
*   Fastening bolts **must** be secure and tightened.
*   Correct gearbox **must** be installed (verification via nameplate is required).
*   Correct agitator **must** be installed (e.g., verifying the number of blades, such as 1 blade or 2 blades).
*   Agitator shaft **must** be straight and free of damage.
*   Agitator lining (e.g., rubber) **must** be completed and free of defects. Refer to [[agitator_coating_and_lining_inspection]] for related requirements.
*   Shaft sleeve **must** be installed.
*   Correct hardware and gaskets **must** be installed.
*   Correct impeller orientation (flow direction up/down) **must** be verified.
*   Correct distance between impellers **must** be verified.
*   Steady bearings **must** be installed.
*   Correct motor **must** be installed.
*   Correct quality and quantity of lubricants (oil & grease) **must** be charged.
*   Agitator **must** be free to turn by hand (when uncoupled).
*   Grounding **must** be completed.
*   Motor rotation **must** be verified by an electrical contractor.
*   Alignment **must** be completed.
*   Coupling and coupling guard **must** be installed.
*   Coupling guard **must** give adequate protection.
*   External painting **must** be completed with no visible defects.

These inspection points contribute to the overall [[agitator_final_inspections]] and ensure compliance with [[4-dst-xxxx-r1_agitator_mechanical_design_vessel_and_material_specifications]] and [[4-dst-xxxx-r1_agitator_shaft_seal_motor_ancillary_equipment_and_notes]].
