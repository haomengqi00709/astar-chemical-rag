# Metal Ductwork Free Issue and Site-Installed Components (5-SPC-0010)

Aker Kvaerner Chemetics will provide certain components as "free issue" items to the Vendor for integration into the ductwork. These include:
*   Butterfly control damper bodies.
*   Expansion joints and hinges.

The Vendor **shall** install components supplied "free issue" by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

Other components, which are supplied by third parties, will be installed at the site by others. These components are clearly indicated on Aker Kvaerner Chemetics drawings and include:
*   Control damper actuators, which will be shipped directly to site for installation by others.
*   Loose expansion joint hinges (supplied separately from the main expansion joint body).
*   Flanged valves and other in-line items.
*   Constant and variable force spring hangers, spring supports, and associated support hardware.
*   Tie-rod assemblies, encompassing all clevises, turnbuckles, pins, cotter pins, split pins, jam nuts, washers, beam welding lugs, or clamps.
*   Consumables, including weld rod material, required for all field joints at the site.

The installation of these site-supplied components is the responsibility of others, not the Vendor. Refer to [[5-SPC-0010_metal_ductwork_vendor_scope_of_supply]] for the Vendor's specific responsibilities.
