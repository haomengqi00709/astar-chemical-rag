# Electrical Document and Drawing Numbering

The numbering of electrical documents and drawings is a critical aspect of project documentation and is governed by established company procedures and specific electrical standards.

All documents **shall** be numbered in accordance with the general company procedure [[0-PRC-0002_document_numbering]]. This ensures a consistent framework for all project documentation.

In addition to the general procedure, electrical documents and drawing numbers **shall** also adhere to the specific guidelines outlined in [[9-LST-0001_electrical_document_numbering_standard]]. This standard provides detailed requirements tailored to electrical documentation.

A range of drawing and document numbers are available for use on a project-specific basis. These ranges are typically detailed in [[9-PRC-0021_table_1_electrical_drawing_and_document_number_ranges]], which specifies the allocated number ranges for each document and drawing type.

The following types of drawings and documents are utilized and numbered as required for each project:
*   **DTL**: Detail Drawings
*   **GAD**: General Arrangement Drawings
*   **LST**: Lists (e.g., equipment lists, cable schedules)
*   **SCM**: Schematics
*   **MAN**: Manuals
*   **CAL**: Calculations

For information regarding the control and authorization of these document numbers, refer to [[electrical_department_document_control]].
