# Product Stripper Design Guidelines

This page outlines general design guidelines and mandatory requirements applicable to the Product Stripper, as referenced in calculation and process data sheet documents.

**User Consultation and Parameter Modification**
*   User is to consult with Technology Manager prior to modifying parameters not highlighted in Blue.

**Velocity Criteria**
*   Suggested velocity is based on flooded and self venting criteria.
*   Flooded criteria is used in the Crossflow stripper for sizing the level controlled acid outlet connected to an Acid Tower.
*   Self venting criteria is used to size the Crossflow stripper Drain and the Product Stripper acid outlet.

**Liquid Level Requirements**
*   Minimum liquid level is assumed to be at least 1 meter.

**Pressure Drop Safety Factor**
*   **A safety factor of 10% must be added when only a tower is supplied and there are guarantees on the pressure drop.**
*   **Max DP must be specified when a safety factor of 10% is added.**

**Packing Calculation Exclusions**
*   Packing around, on top and 1 ft directly below the distributor is not included in the mass transfer/pressure drop calculations.

**Environmental and Operating Conditions**
*   Minimum ambient temperature is a noted parameter.
*   Maximum vacuum at blower suction is a noted parameter.

**Working Volume Criteria**
*   Working Volume Criteria: Centreline pump suction to invert of overflow
*   Working Volume Criteria: Bottom tank to invert of overflow

**Nozzle Sizing Notes**
*   Nozzles attached to process lines will be sized in accordance with [[product_stripper_nozzle_sizing_and_criteria|line sizing criteria]].
*   "Line Sizing" will be entered for nozzles attached to process lines.
*   Overflow nozzles are self venting.
*   Flow and its basis (e.g., 50 m3/h = max flow from feed pump) will be entered for overflow nozzles.
*   For drain nozzles, the minimum time to drain the tank contents will be entered.
