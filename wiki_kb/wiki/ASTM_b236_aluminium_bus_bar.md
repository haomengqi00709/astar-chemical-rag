# ASTM B236 Aluminium Bus Bar

This page provides an overview of ASTM B236, a standard specification for Aluminium bus bar.

**Overview**
ASTM B236 is a standard specification published by ASTM International that covers Aluminium bus bar for electrical conductors. It defines the chemical composition, mechanical properties, dimensions, and tolerances specifically for Aluminium bus bar, ensuring its suitability for electrical applications.

**Application**
In the context of the [[9-SPC-0001_aluminium_bus_welding_standard_overview|Aluminium Bus Welding Standard (9-SPC-0001)]], base materials used for BUS systems **shall** be in accordance with ASTM B236 or [[ASTM_b209_aluminium_and_aluminium_alloy_sheet_and_plate|ASTM B209]]. This ensures that the raw materials meet the specified quality and performance criteria for the fabrication of Aluminium bus systems, particularly for electrical conductivity and structural integrity.
