# Monthly Progress Report Fixed Price Costs Table

This table, 0-REP-2001-1_table_3, is utilized within the [[0-rep-2001_monthly_progress_report_format|Monthly Progress Report]] for projects operating under a fixed price contract. It details the contract price, the cost of agreed change orders, and the estimated costs for pending and expected change orders. All values **must** be presented in CAD.
