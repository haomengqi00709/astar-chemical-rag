# Field Erected CS Tank - Shop Material Certifications

For the "Field Erected CS Tank - Shop" component, specific material certification requirements are in place to ensure compliance with project standards.

**Mill Test Certificates:**
*   Mill Test Certificates are required as **FP** (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package). This means these certificates **must** be part of the comprehensive quality documentation submitted by the Vendor.
*   Chemetics surveillance for Mill Test Certificates is designated as **'Complete'**. This indicates that Chemetics will perform a full review and verification of these certificates.

These certifications are crucial for verifying the material properties and traceability of all components used in the fabrication of the field-erected carbon steel tank.
