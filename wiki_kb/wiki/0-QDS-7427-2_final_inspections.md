# Stainless Steel Tubing Tier 2 Final Inspections

Final inspections for Stainless Steel Tubing Tier 2 are crucial to ensure the product meets all specified quality and dimensional requirements before shipment. Chemetics **shall** witness and hold various stages of these inspections.

**Dimensional Inspection:**
*   Dimensional inspection is required as FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package).
*   Chemetics **shall** Witness and Hold Dimensional inspection.
*   Dimensional Tolerances **shall** be per [A-450].

**Ring Gauge Inspection:**
*   Ring Gauge inspection is required as FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package).
*   Chemetics **shall** Witness and Hold Ring Gauge inspection.
*   Ring Gauge inspection **shall** be performed on 100% of Tubes.

**Visual and Surface Inspections:**
Chemetics **shall** Witness and Hold the following inspections:
*   Contour and finish of welds.
*   Surfaces free from defects.
*   Cleanliness.
*   Item Identification.

**Documentation Review:**
*   Chemetics **shall** Witness and Hold Review QA Documentation.

**Packaging Inspection:**
*   Chemetics **shall** Witness and Hold Packaging inspection.

**Quality Record Requirements for Final Inspections:**
*   Quality Records are required for Dimensional inspection (FP) and Ring Gauge inspection (FP).
*   No Quality Record is required for Contour and finish of welds, Surfaces free from defects, Cleanliness, Item Identification, Review QA Documentation, or Packaging.

These inspections are a critical part of the overall quality assurance process, leading into the [[0-QDS-7427-2_packing_and_shipping_requirements]]. For details on record keeping, refer to [[0-QDS-7427-2_quality_record_definitions]].
