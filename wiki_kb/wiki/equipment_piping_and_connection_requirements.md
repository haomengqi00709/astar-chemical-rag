# Equipment Piping and Connection Requirements

As per document 4-SPC-0001, specific requirements govern the design and installation of piping and connections associated with equipment.

**Piping Standards**
Unless noted otherwise, piping and components **will conform** to ASME B31.1 or B31.3 piping standards as applicable. This ensures consistency and safety in piping system design.

**Drains and Threads**
Drains **will have** plugs, caps, or blind flanges as appropriate to prevent leakage and allow for maintenance. Screwed pipe threads **will be** American standard taper pipe threads (NPT) per ASME B1.20.1.

**Flange Specifications**
Pipe flange sizes and drilling **will be** in accordance with ASME B16.5. All tie points **will be** anchored, flanged connections, ensuring secure and stable interfaces. Thermal expansion **is not allowed** past any tie-in points, which necessitates proper design for thermal movement within piping runs.

**Piping Support**
Piping **will be** adequately supported to prevent stress and vibration. Types and locations of piping supports **will be submitted** to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for approval, ensuring structural integrity and compliance with design standards.

**Plastic Piping**
All plastic piping **must have** an ultra-violet (UV) inhibitor in the base material, or the exterior of the pipe and fittings **shall be** fully coated with a suitable UV inhibitor. This protects plastic components from degradation due to UV exposure, extending their lifespan.

**Drain Connections**

**Thread and Flange Standards**
Screwed pipe threads will be American standard taper pipe threads (NPT) per ASME B1.20.1.
Pipe flange sizes and drilling will be in accordance with ASME B16.5.

**Tie-in Points and Supports**
All tie points will be anchored, flanged connections.
Thermal expansion is not allowed past any tie-in points.
Piping will be adequately supported.
Types and locations of piping supports will be submitted to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for approval.

**Plastic Piping**
All plastic piping will have an ultra-violet (UV) inhibitor in the base material or the exterior of pipe and fittings will be fully coated with a suitable UV inhibitor.
