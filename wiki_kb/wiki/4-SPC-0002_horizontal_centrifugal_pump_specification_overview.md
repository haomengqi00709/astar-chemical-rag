# Horizontal Centrifugal Pump Specification Overview (4-SPC-0002)

This specification, Document ID 4-SPC-0002, outlines the general requirements for the design, fabrication, and supply of horizontal centrifugal pumps. These requirements are supplementary to the [[general_equipment_requirements_specification|General Equipment Requirements (4-SPC-0001)]].

**Vendor Supply Scope**
The Vendor **must** supply equipment fully assembled and **must** provide all items necessary for a complete, functional unit. Major components that **must** be supplied include:
*   Pump wet end and power end.
*   Mechanical seal, complete with a seal guard.
*   Complete coupling, including a coupling guard.
*   Motor.
*   Baseplate.

All supplied components **must** integrate seamlessly to form a fully operational pump system.
