# Field Erected SS Tank Shop Component Final Inspections

This section details the final inspection requirements for the Field Erected Stainless Steel Tank Shop Component (0-QDS-7438). These inspections are crucial to verify the quality and compliance of the fabricated components before shipment.

**Inspection Activities and Chemetics Surveillance:**
All final inspection activities require specific levels of Chemetics surveillance, as outlined in [[0-QDS-7438_chemetics_surveillance_level]].

*   **Dimensional Inspection:**
    *   Chemetics Surveillance: Witness and Hold.
*   **Contour & Finish of Welds Inspection:**
    *   Chemetics Surveillance: Witness and Hold.
*   **Surfaces Free from Defects Inspection:**
    *   Chemetics Surveillance: Witness and Hold.
*   **Cleanliness Inspection:**
    *   Chemetics Surveillance: Witness and Hold.
*   **Review of QA Documentation:**
    *   Quality Record: [[quality_record_definitions|FP]] (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package).
    *   Chemetics Surveillance: Witness and Hold.

The "Hold" surveillance status indicates that work cannot proceed without Chemetics' explicit approval after the inspection. The "Witness" status means Chemetics will observe the inspection process.
