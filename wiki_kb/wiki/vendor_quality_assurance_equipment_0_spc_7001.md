# Vendor Quality Assurance (Equipment) (0-SPC-7001)

This document, 0-SPC-7001, specifies the quality assurance requirements for vendors supplying equipment to the Project. It details the mandatory quality control measures, inspection points, and documentation required from equipment vendors.

Vendors are required to comply with this specification to ensure that all supplied equipment meets the stringent quality and performance standards of the Project. This includes requirements for Inspection and Test Plans (ITPs), testing reports, and surveillance by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
