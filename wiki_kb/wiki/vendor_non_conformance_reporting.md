# Vendor Non-Conformance Reporting

This procedure, as part of [[vendor_quality_assurance_0_prc_7005]], governs the identification, reporting, and resolution of non-conformances by Vendors. It also applies to documenting defect repairs not covered by procedures already approved as part of the Vendor Quality Plan.

**Definition of Non-Conformance**
A non-conformance is defined as the failure by the Vendor to fulfill a requirement of the Purchase Order. The term "non-conformance" is specifically applicable to work that the Vendor considers to be complete but does not meet the stipulated requirements of the Purchase Order.

**Identification and Reporting**
Non-conformances may be identified either by the Vendor themselves or by the Inspector during surveillance activities. For each identified non-conformance, the Inspector **must** prepare a report using the [[0-NCR-7XXX_template_non_conformance_report]].

The non-conformance report **must** contain the following information:
*   A clear description of the non-conformance.
*   The Vendor’s proposed action to correct the non-conformance.
*   The Vendor’s proposed action to prevent the recurrence of the non-conformance.
*   Approval of the proposed actions, including any qualifications.
*   The results of re-inspection after corrective action has been taken.
*   All necessary numbering information.

**Numbering and Revisions**
Non-Conformance Reports **must** be numbered according to [[document_numbering_0_prc_0002]]. The identification number of a Non-Conformance Report **must** be identical to that of the reference [[quality_data_sheets_qds]]. If multiple Non-Conformance Reports are issued for the same goods, they **must** be numbered as multiple sheets or sections of the same document.

Revisions of Non-Conformance Reports will be issued as information is developed. These revisions serve to advise personnel of the current status of the non-conformance and to record the results of re-inspection, providing a final record of disposition.

**Disposition of Non-Conformances**
Revision 2 of [[vendor_quality_assurance_0_prc_7005]] modified language in Section 7.5 to allow a single published version where the disposition of a Non-Conformance is to accept as-is. Non-conformances **must** be dealt with as described in this procedure before an [[vendor_inspection_release]] can be issued, particularly requiring re-inspection if non-conformances were present.

**Non-Conformance Report Numbering and Revisions**
Non-Conformance Reports (NCRs) must contain numbering information and will be numbered according to [[0-PRC-0002_document_numbering]]. The identification numbers for NCRs must be identical to those of the reference Quality Data Sheets. In cases where multiple NCRs are generated for the same goods, they will be numbered as multiple sheets or sections of the same document.

Revisions of Non-Conformance Reports are issued as information is developed, serving to advise personnel of the current status of the non-conformance. These revisions also include the recording of re-inspection results, providing a final record of the disposition of the non-conformance.
