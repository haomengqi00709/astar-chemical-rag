# Cleaning, Passivation, and Ferroxyl Testing for 6% Moly SS (4-SPC-0017)

This section outlines the mandatory requirements for cleaning, contamination prevention, passivation, and Ferroxyl testing for 6 percent Moly austenitic stainless steel fabrications under Standard **4-SPC-0017**.

**Cleaning Before Heating**
The surface and edges of any austenitic steel to be heated **must** be cleaned free from all traces of foreign matter (e.g., marking paint, oil, etc.) by grinding, wire brushing with a stainless steel brush, and/or non-chlorinated solvent cleaning immediately prior to the application of heat.

**Contamination Prevention**
The Fabricator **must** take all necessary controls to prevent contamination of weldments at all stages of fabrication. These controls **must** include the segregation of wire brushes, grinding wheels, and other tools for use on stainless steels only.

**Post-Fabrication Cleaning**
On completion of work, items **must** be cleaned internally and externally. All fabrication markings, oxidation, weld spatter, etc., **must** be removed prior to shipment.

**Passivation and Ferroxyl Testing**
Passivation and Ferroxyl testing, where required by the Purchase Order or referenced documents, **must** be carried out in accordance with written procedures. These passivation and Ferroxyl testing procedures **must** be approved by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics prior to use. Refer to [[ASTM_a380_cleaning_passivation_stainless_steel]] for relevant guidelines.
