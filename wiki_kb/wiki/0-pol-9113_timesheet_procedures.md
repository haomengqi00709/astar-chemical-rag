# Overtime Timesheet Procedures

Employees eligible for compensation for hours worked in excess of the normal hours of work **must** accurately record these hours on their weekly timesheets.

**Recording Overtime**
The proper recording of excess hours is crucial for ensuring fair compensation, whether through direct payment or through the [[0-pol-9113_banked_time_policy]]. This includes hours worked beyond the standard 75 hours per two-week period, as well as specific hours related to [[0-pol-9113_business_travel_compensation]].

For general guidelines on timesheet submission and procedures, refer to [[0-pol-9111_timesheet_procedures]]. This document specifically details the requirements for reporting overtime and banked time under the [[0-pol-9113_overtime_compensation_policy_overview]].
