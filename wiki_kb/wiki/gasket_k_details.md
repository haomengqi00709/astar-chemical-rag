# Gasket K (Ductile Iron Pipe)

Gasket K is supplied by the ductile iron pipe manufacturer and features a unique step ring design for optimal sealing.

### Step Ring Design
The step ring design of Gasket K consists of:
*   A 1.6 mm thick outer ring for centering.
*   A 3.2 mm thick inner ring for sealing.
