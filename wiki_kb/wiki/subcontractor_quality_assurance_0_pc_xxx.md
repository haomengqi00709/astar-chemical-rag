# Subcontractor Quality Assurance (0-PC-XXX)

This document, 0-PC-XXX, specifies the quality assurance requirements for subcontractors involved in the Project. It outlines the mandatory quality control measures, inspection points, and documentation required from construction subcontractors.

The Subcontractor **will** be required to comply with the requirements of this specification. Quality Data Sheets (QDS) **will** be provided for each tagged item and for each class or type of bulk material installed by the Subcontractor, stating requirements for their Inspection and Test Plan, reports, and the level of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics surveillance, including Witness and Hold points.
