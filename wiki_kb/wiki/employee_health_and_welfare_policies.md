# Employee Health and Welfare Policies

Aker Kvaerner Chemetics ("the Company") is committed to fostering a healthy work-life balance for its employees. The Human Resources Department maintains a suite of policies and procedures designed to promote employee health, provide care, and ensure welfare in the event of illness or accident.

Key Employee Health and Welfare policies and procedures include:
*   [[0-pol-8001_drug_and_alcohol_policy]]
*   Policies related to hours of work, including [[0-pol-9106_normal_work_schedule]], [[0-pol-9106_flexible_work_schedule]], [[0-pol-9106_overtime_and_management_responsibilities]], and [[0-pol-9106_site_work_hours_and_rest_periods]].
*   Policies related to vacation, including [[0-pol-9114_vacation_entitlement_and_accrual]], [[0-pol-9114_vacation_scheduling_and_approval]], and [[0-pol-9114_vacation_carryover_and_payout]].
*   [[0-prc-9107_harassment_and_non_discrimination]]
*   [[0-prc-8102_travel_health_and_safety]]
*   [[0-pol-91xx_sick_leave_policy]] (currently not yet available)

In addition to these internal policies, the Company provides comprehensive benefits plans to support employee well-being:
*   Group Benefits Plan with Sun Life (Policy 50769)
*   Employee and Family Assistance Plan (EFAP)

These policies and plans collectively aim to create a supportive and safe working environment for all Company personnel.

**Key Employee Health and Welfare Policies/Procedures:**

*   0-POL-9106 Hours of Work
*   0-POL-9114 Vacation Policy
*   Group Benefits Plan with Sun Life (Policy 50769)
