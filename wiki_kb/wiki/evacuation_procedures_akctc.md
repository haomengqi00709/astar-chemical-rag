# Evacuation Procedures - AKCTC

This page details the mandatory evacuation procedures for the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Technology Centre (AKCTC). For general company-wide evacuation plans, refer to [[0-PRC-8010_building_evacuation_plan]].

**Initiation of Evacuation:**
Evacuation will normally be ordered by the [[emergency_coordinator_roles_and_responsibilities_akctc]] (ECO). The order will be broadcasted throughout the Technology Centre by means of the intercom system.

**Personnel Actions During Evacuation:**
All personnel **must** evacuate the building and proceed directly to the Emergency Assembly Point for headcount and further instructions.

**Emergency Assembly Point:**
The designated Emergency Assembly Point is the postal outlet of the Lake City Industrial Park. This location is at the corner of a unit directly opposing our west back entrance and west from ADT.

**Headcount and Rescue Coordination:**
*   The [[emergency_coordinator_roles_and_responsibilities_akctc]] (ECO) will immediately conduct a headcount at the Emergency Assembly Point.
*   If not all lab personnel (including visitors) are accounted for, the ECO will co-ordinate re-entry and rescue efforts with external emergency services, such as the Fire Department and HAZMAT team.

**Self-Evacuation:**
Every individual **shall** evacuate on his/her own if faced with an uncontrollable situation that poses immediate danger. An individual evacuating on their own should then contact the ECO (e.g., by re-entering the Centre from the office side) and report on the nature of the emergency.

**Mandatory Familiarity:**
*   All personnel **must** be familiar with the exit paths in the building.
*   All personnel **must** be familiar with the safe areas in the building.
*   Familiarity with the evacuation procedures is mandatory.
*   Familiarity with the [[experimental_unit_emergency_shutdown_akctc]] is mandatory.

For an overview of emergency response, refer to [[0-PRC-8010_office_emergency_response_overview]].
