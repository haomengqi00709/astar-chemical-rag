# Weld Quality, Repair, and Finishing for High Silicon Austenitic Stainless Steel (4-SPC-0025)

**Weld Quality and Appearance**
Each weld **shall** be uniform in width and size throughout its full length. Each layer of welding **shall** be smooth and free of slag, inclusions, cracks, porosity, lack of fusion, and undercut, except to the extent permitted in the referenced codes. Buttwelds **shall** be slightly convex, of uniform height, and have full penetration. End grains on tubes and nozzle plates **shall** be fused for nitric acid service. Crevices **shall not** be permitted on the process side of the weld.

The ferrite content of weld deposits **shall** be not less than 4-5 percent. The ferrite content of each batch of electrodes **shall** be checked prior to use.

**Weld Repair Procedures**
Weld repairs **shall not** be carried out on the process side of the weld unless prior approval has been obtained from Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. Consent to carry out repairs **shall** be based on prior acceptance of VEW's repair procedures by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. Weld repair surfaces **shall** be 100% dye penetrant examined as per the requirements outlined in [[4-SPC-0025_inspection_and_testing_requirements]]. Repair areas and the nature of defects found **shall** be identified on a sketch or drawing. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics reserves the right to accept or reject any doubtful or questionable indications.

**Finishing and Post-Weld Treatment**
Each run of weld metal **shall** be carefully examined to ensure it has been thoroughly cleaned and is defect-free. Craters left from the previous pass **shall** be ground out before commencing the next pass. On restarting, care **shall** be taken to ensure proper fusion.

Peening of weld runs is **not permitted**. Hammering of finished welds is **not permitted**. Distortion from welding, solution annealing, and water quenching **shall** be minimized at all times. Tolerances after welding and heat treatment **shall** comply with the requirements detailed in [[4-SPC-0025_fabrication_tolerances]].

Grinding **shall** be carried out in such a manner as not to reduce the adjacent base metal thickness or produce heat tinting. Grinding with water cooling may be necessary to avoid grinding cracks. Wire brushes **shall** be made of austenitic stainless steel and **must not** have been used previously to clean surfaces other than stainless steels.

**Post-Fabrication Cleaning**
On completion of work, items shall be cleaned internally and externally.

**Specific Weld Quality for Nitric Acid Service**
End grains on tubes shall be fused for nitric acid service. End grains on nozzle plate shall be fused for nitric acid service. Crevices shall not be permitted on the process side of the weld.
