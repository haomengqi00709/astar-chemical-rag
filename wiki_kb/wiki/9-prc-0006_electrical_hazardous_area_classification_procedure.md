# Electrical Hazardous Area Classification Procedure (9-PRC-0006)

The Electrical Hazardous Area Classification Procedure, documented as 9-PRC-0006, is a Procedure type document. This Engineering Standard is applicable to all Aker Kvaerner Chemetics projects located in countries that adhere to [[iec_79_10_hazardous_area_classification_standard|IEC standards]] for area classification.

**Purpose and Scope**
The primary purpose of this standard is the area classification of plants constructed in countries following IEC standards. It provides recommendations for special precautions concerning the safe use of electricity in plants designed by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. These plants are facilities where flammable materials are manufactured, processed, handled, or stored. The recommendations specifically address sources of ignition from electricity, including electrical arcs, sparks, and heating effects.

A fundamental requirement is that electrical equipment and systems in hazardous (classified) locations **shall not** provide a means of ignition for an ignitable mixture. A prime requirement in the design and layout of the plant and plant equipment **must** be the avoidance, as far as possible, of the creation of a flammable atmosphere. For an ignition to occur, both a flammable atmosphere and a source of ignition need to exist simultaneously.

**Responsibilities**
The responsibility for the area classification of a plant rests jointly with the process and electrical departments, as well as any other department responsible for plant safety and operation. Practical experience regarding the conditions likely to exist during start-up and normal running **shall** be obtained where possible.

**General Plant Classification**
Based on the [[iec_79_10_hazardous_area_classification_standard|IEC publication # 79-10]] and Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' previous experience, typical Aker Kvaerner Chemetics plants (NaOH, NaClO3, or ClO2) are generally considered non-hazardous.
