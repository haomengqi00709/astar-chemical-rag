# Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Plant Hazardous Areas: General

Aker Kvaerner Chemetics chemical plants, including those producing NaOH, NaClO3, or ClO2, are generally considered non-hazardous with respect to electrical installations, except for specific identified areas. This general classification is based on adherence to codes such as CEC, NEC, and NFPA 497, combined with extensive operational experience.

**Hydrogen Management:**
*   Hydrogen gas is typically contained within vessels and piping systems, rendering these areas non-classified under normal operating conditions. This applies to most areas of typical Aker Kvaerner Chemetics plants.
*   Hydrogen is transported in seamless welded pipes, which contributes to its containment and the non-hazardous classification of the surrounding areas.

**Spark Prevention:**
*   Piping and instrumentation **must** be installed so that no spark-producing device is located closer than one (1) metre (3’-0”) from any hydrogen pipe flange.
*   No spark-producing in-line devices **will be** used in hydrogen service.

**Non-Hazardous Areas:**
*   Boilers, HCl units, and their immediate surrounding areas are classified as non-hazardous with respect to electrical equipment and instruments, as per NFPA 497 (1997) Section 3.3.3.
