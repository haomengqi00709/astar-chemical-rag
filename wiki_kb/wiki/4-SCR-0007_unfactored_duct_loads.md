# Skirt Supported Vessel - Unfactored Duct Loads (4-SCR-0007)

This page outlines the unfactored loads exerted by associated ducting on the skirt-supported vessel, as specified in document [[4-SCR-0007_document_administration]]. These loads are crucial for ensuring the structural integrity of both the vessel and its connections to the ducting system.

**Unfactored Duct Loads:**
*   **Vertical Load:** Specified in kN.
*   **Shear Load:** Specified in kN.
*   **Moment Load:** Specified in kN-m.

These duct loads must be considered in conjunction with the [[4-SCR-0007_unfactored_equipment_loads]] for a comprehensive structural analysis. The application point for these loads is at Datum, as further elaborated in [[4-SCR-0007_support_criteria_basis_and_general_requirements]]. For detailed duct load calculations, refer to documents such as [[4-CAL-0025_gas_gas_hx_duct_combined_base_loads]] and [[4-CAL-0025_gas_gas_hx_duct_connection_loads]].
