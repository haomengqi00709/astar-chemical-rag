# Template Lixator Process Design Conditions

This page details the key process design conditions and fluid characteristics for the Lixator units, as specified in document **4-PDS-XXXX-R1**. These conditions are crucial for the proper operation and design of the Lixator system, ensuring it meets performance requirements for dissolving salt and producing brine.

**Fluid Descriptions:**
The Lixator units are designed to handle the following fluid streams:
*   FEED SALT
*   FEED LIQUOR
*   RAW BRINE

**Measurement Units for Process Parameters:**
To ensure consistency and clarity in design and operation, the following units of measurement **shall** be used for the respective process parameters:
*   **Composition:** Measured in % w/w (weight by weight) and specifically for sodium chloride, in % NaCl.
*   **Normal Flowrate:** Measured in m3/h (cubic meters per hour).
*   **Maximum Flowrate:** Measured in m3/h (cubic meters per hour).
*   **Normal Temperature:** Measured in oC (degrees Celsius).
*   **Temperature Range:** Measured in oC (degrees Celsius).
*   **Density:** Measured in kg/m3 (kilograms per cubic meter).
*   **Viscosity:** Measured in cP (centipoise).
*   **pH:** A unitless value, denoted by (-).

Adherence to these specified units and parameters is essential for accurate engineering calculations and equipment sizing.
