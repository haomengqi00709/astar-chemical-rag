# Job Hazard Analysis (JHA) Roles and Responsibilities

Effective implementation of [[0-PRC-8007_job_hazard_analysis]] requires clear roles and responsibilities across project management and safety personnel.

**Management and Supervisory Responsibilities:**
*   The Site Manager, Site Safety Manager, and their Supervisors **will** cooperate to identify tasks that require a JHA.
*   All Managers and Supervisors **will** demonstrate leadership in the application of JHA principles.
*   The site Safety Manager, or other qualified personnel, **will** ensure the JHA is completed accurately and thoroughly.
*   The site Safety Manager **will** approve all JHAs performed on his job site.
*   The Safety Manager **will** make the completed JHAs available to all supervisory employees.

**Supervisor Orientation and Training:**
*   Each new supervisor upon assignment to the project **must** receive an orientation.
*   This orientation **must** fully explain the principles and steps of [[0-PRC-8007_job_hazard_analysis]].

**Qualified Person Requirements:**
*   Hazard assessments **will** be prepared by a qualified person.
*   A qualified person **must** have adequate training and experience in the recognition, evaluation, and control of hazards.
*   A qualified person **must** also have adequate training and experience in consultation with the person assigned overall responsibility for the administration of Health and Safety on the work site.
*   Hazard assessments may be conducted with employees prior to commencing a task. When done with employees, these assessments **must** be led by a qualified person.

**Critical Activities:**
*   A responsible person **must** be assigned for critical activities identified within the JHA process.
