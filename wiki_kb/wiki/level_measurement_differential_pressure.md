# Level Measurement - Differential Pressure

Differential pressure (DP) transmitters are a preferred method for level measurement, particularly for clean liquids, offering reliable and accurate readings.

**Application in Tanks:**
*   **Atmospheric Tanks:** Where differential pressure transmitters are used on atmospheric tanks, the transmitter **will** be provided with a 3” integrally mounted flange diaphragm on the high-pressure port.
*   **Sealed Tanks:** For sealed tanks, the transmitter **will** be provided with a 3” integrally mounted flange diaphragm on the high-pressure port and a remote mounted 3” flange diaphragm connected to the low-pressure port. This configuration accounts for varying head pressures in sealed systems.

**Manifold Requirements:**
Differential pressure transmitters without diaphragm seals **will** be fitted with 3-valve manifolds. These manifolds serve a dual purpose:
*   They enable isolation of the transmitter from the process, facilitating maintenance and calibration.
*   They allow for the equalization of the high and low pressure sides of the transmitter, which is essential for accurate zero checks.
