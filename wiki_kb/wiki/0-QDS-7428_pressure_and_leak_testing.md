# Pressure and Leak Testing: CS Tubing

This page details the mandatory pressure and leak testing requirements for carbon steel tubing, as specified in Quality Data Sheet (QDS) 0-QDS-7428. These tests are crucial for verifying the structural integrity and leak-tightness of the tubing.

**Mandatory Testing Requirements:**

*   **Hydrostatic Testing:**
    *   Hydrostatic testing is required to be included in the Vendor's Inspection & Test (I & T) Plan (designated as [[0-QDS-7428_quality_record_definitions|FP]]).
    *   Chemetics will **Witness**, **Hold**, and **Complete** surveillance for Hydrostatic testing.
    *   Hydrostatic testing **must** comply with **ASTM A450**. This standard covers general requirements for carbon, ferritic alloy, and austenitic alloy steel tubes.
*   **Air Under Water Testing:**
    *   Air Under Water testing is required to be included in the Vendor's I & T Plan (designated as [[0-QDS-7428_quality_record_definitions|FP]]).
    *   Chemetics will **Witness**, **Hold**, and **Complete** surveillance for Air Under Water testing.
    *   Air Under Water testing **must** comply with **ASTM A450**.

These pressure and leak tests are fundamental for ensuring the safety and operational reliability of the carbon steel tubing. Hydrostatic testing involves filling the tubing with water and pressurizing it to a specified level to detect leaks or structural weaknesses. Air Under Water testing, often used for leak detection, involves pressurizing the tubing with air and submerging it in water to observe for bubbles, indicating leaks.

Chemetics' involvement in witnessing, holding, and completing surveillance for these tests signifies a critical control point. "Witness" means Chemetics personnel will be present during the test. "Hold" implies that the vendor cannot proceed to the next manufacturing step until Chemetics has approved the test results. "Complete" indicates that Chemetics will finalize their surveillance activities for this specific test. This rigorous oversight ensures that all pressure integrity requirements are met before the tubing is accepted for use in the project.
