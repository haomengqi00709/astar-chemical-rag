# Inspection and Documentation Requirements for 0-QDS-7440

This page details the specific inspection and documentation requirements for various items under Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7440, focusing on material verification and initial documentation.

**Item 1.0: Material Verification**
Material verification is a critical step to ensure that the correct materials are used in accordance with specifications.

**Item 2.0: Certificate of Compliance**
A Certificate of Compliance **is required** as an FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package) quality record. This certificate **must** confirm that the materials and components comply with all specified standards and requirements.

**Item 3.0: MSDS Sheets**
Material Safety Data Sheets (MSDS) **are required** as an AP (As Prepared) quality record. These sheets **must** provide essential information on the safe handling, storage, and potential hazards of the materials.

For definitions of AP and FP quality record types, refer to [[0-QDS-7440_quality_record_definitions]].
