# Joint Health and Safety Committee Meetings

Effective operation of the Joint Health and Safety Committee (JHSC) relies on structured and regular meetings to address health, safety, and environmental matters.

**Meeting Protocols:**
*   The joint committee **shall** follow the guidelines of the local Health and Safety Board as mandated by local law, ensuring adherence to [[compliance_and_standards]].
*   The joint committee may establish its own rules of procedure by mutual agreement, allowing for flexibility tailored to specific site needs.

**Meeting Frequency:**
*   The joint committee **shall** meet regularly, at least once per month.
*   At construction sites, the committee **shall** meet at least every two weeks, reflecting the dynamic nature of construction environments.

**Conflict Resolution and Management Response:**
*   If a joint committee is unable to reach agreement on a matter relating to health and safety, one of the co-chairs **shall** report the matter to the [[hse_personnel_definitions_and_responsibilities|Company HSE Manager]]. The co-chair **must** attempt to resolve the issue with the Company HSE Manager.
*   Company management representatives **shall** take action to effectively deal with committee recommendations and **must** report back to the committee at the subsequent meeting.
*   For any matter requiring management authority beyond the managers in the committee, the employer **must** respond in writing to the committee within 21 days. The employer's written response **must** indicate acceptance, or the reasons for not accepting the recommendation.
*   In the event that the Company rejects a joint committee recommendation, the co-chair of the committee may report the issue to the health and safety regulatory body having jurisdiction of the work place, in line with [[legal_compliance_and_policy_conflicts]].

These protocols ensure that JHSC recommendations are given due consideration and that unresolved issues can be escalated appropriately, reinforcing the importance of [[hse_management_and_employee_responsibilities|management commitment]] to safety.

**Meeting Protocols:**
*   The joint committee **shall** follow the guidelines of the local Health and Safety Board as mandated by local law.
*   The joint committee may establish its own rules of procedure by mutual agreement.

**Meeting Attendance:**
*   The Company most senior Management person at the work site will attend all safety meetings.
*   If the Company most senior Management person is not able to attend, they will designate another attendee to act on their behalf.

**Meeting Frequency:**
*   The joint committee will meet regularly at least once per month.
*   At construction sites, the committee will meet at least every two weeks.

**Addressing Disagreements and Recommendations:**
*   If a joint committee is unable to reach agreement on a matter relating to health and safety, one of the co-chairs will report the matter to the Company HSE Manager and attempt to resolve the issue.
*   Company management representatives will take action to effectively deal with committee recommendations and will report back to the committee on the subsequent meeting.
*   For any matter requiring management authority beyond the managers in the committee, the employer must respond in writing to the committee within 21 days. The employer's written response must indicate acceptance, or the reasons for not accepting the recommendation.

**Escalation:**
*   In the event that the Company rejects a joint committee recommendation, the co-chair of the committee may report the issue to the health and safety regulatory body having jurisdiction of the work place.
