# Explosion Response - AKCTC

This page details the procedures to be followed in the event of an explosion at the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Technology Centre (AKCTC).

**Immediate Actions:**
*   AKCTC personnel **shall** alert other personnel in the area of the explosion.
*   AKCTC personnel **shall** assist injured personnel or summon first aid help immediately.
*   Ambulance Service should be summoned if warranted by the nature and severity of injuries.

**Escalation to Fire Emergency:**
If necessary, following an explosion, personnel **shall** announce a fire alarm and follow the [[fire_emergency_response_akctc#Major_Fire_Response]] procedure, which includes dialing 9-1-1 and initiating a building evacuation.

**Evacuation:**
All personnel **must** proceed to the [[evacuation_procedures_akctc#Emergency_Assembly_Point]] for headcount and further instructions once the situation is assessed.

For general emergency response information, refer to [[0-PRC-8010_office_emergency_response_overview]].
