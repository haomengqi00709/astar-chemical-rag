# ASTM C27 Standard Specification for Fireclay and High-Alumina Refractory Brick

[[ASTM_c27|ASTM C27]] is a standard specification that defines the requirements for fireclay and high-alumina refractory brick. This standard is crucial for ensuring the quality and performance of refractory bricks used in high-temperature applications, such as those found in the [[regeneration_furnace_process_and_design_conditions|Regeneration Furnace]].

Key aspects covered by ASTM C27 typically include:
*   **Classification**: Categorization of refractory bricks based on their alumina content and other properties.
*   **Physical Requirements**: Specifications for properties such as bulk density, apparent porosity, cold crushing strength, and modulus of rupture.
*   **Chemical Requirements**: Limits on the chemical composition, particularly alumina (Al2O3) content, to ensure the desired refractory properties.
*   **Workmanship, Finish, and Appearance**: Criteria for the visual quality and dimensional tolerances of the bricks.
*   **Sampling and Testing**: Procedures for selecting samples and conducting tests to verify compliance with the specified requirements.

In the context of specification [[4-SPC-0082_refractory_brick_lining_materials_and_supply|4-SPC-0082]], high alumina fire brick **will** conform to the latest edition of [[ASTM_c27|ASTM C27]] to ensure their suitability for the demanding conditions of refractory linings. Adherence to this standard is mandatory for the Vendor supplying these materials.
