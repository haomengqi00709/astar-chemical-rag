# Form of Engineering Subcontract (0-PRC-02)

This document, 0-PRC-02, specifies the standard form and structure for engineering subcontracts used on the Project. It ensures consistency and compliance in contractual agreements with engineering subcontractors.

The Engineering Subcontract is issued in accordance with this procedure.
