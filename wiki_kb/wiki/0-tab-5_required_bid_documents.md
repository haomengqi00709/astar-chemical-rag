# Cost Tabulation (0-TAB-5) Required Bid Documents

For effective bid evaluation and order placement, the Cost Tabulation (0-TAB-5) process mandates the submission and completion of specific documents. These documents provide critical information for assessing technical and commercial aspects, as well as potential risks.

**Mandatory Bid Documents:**
*   **Technical Bid Tab (0-TAB-xxxx-2):** A detailed tabulation of technical aspects of the bids received. This document is **required** to systematically compare technical specifications across different vendors. If a full Technical Bid Tab is not completed, a **Technical Summary must be completed** instead.
*   **Commercial Bid Tab (0-TAB-xxxx-3):** This document, referencing the [[commercial_bid_tabulation_template]], is **required** to compare the commercial terms and pricing offered by various bidders.
*   **Order Risk Assessment (0-TAB-xxx-4):** An assessment of potential risks associated with placing an order with a particular vendor, referencing the [[order_risk_assessment]] procedure. This document is **required** to identify and mitigate risks early in the procurement process.

**Technical Summary Details:**
In cases where a full Technical Bid Tab is not prepared, the **Technical Summary must include**:
*   Capacity
*   Materials
*   Weight

These documents are integral to the [[0-tab-5_bid_evaluation_criteria|bid evaluation criteria]] and ensure a thorough review before proceeding to [[0-tab-5_order_placement_requirements|order placement]].
