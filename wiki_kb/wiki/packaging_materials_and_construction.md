# Packaging Materials and Construction Standards

The selection and construction of packaging materials for marine transportation **must** adhere to strict quality standards to ensure the integrity of the goods.

**Wooden Packaging:**
*   Wooden packaging **must** be constructed from hardwood or conifer timbers of good quality, free from cracks, loose knots, or other defects.
*   Plywood is an acceptable material for wooden packaging.
*   No other type of manufactured sheeting is permitted for wooden packaging.
*   Conifer timbers **must** be free of bark.
*   Conifer timbers **must** be free of grub holes (holes larger than 3mm across).
*   Conifer timbers **must** show no visible signs of living pests.
*   Conifer timbers **must** have a moisture content of less than 20 percent.

**Prohibited Packing Materials:**
The use of hay, straw, flax combining, palm packing fibre, brown coconut fibre, used hessian bags, or hessian linings in packing is strictly prohibited.

These material specifications are integral to the [[packaging_marine_transportation_0_spc_5005]] guidelines, ensuring durability and compliance with international shipping regulations.
