# Metering Pump Mechanical Design Requirements

This page details the mechanical design requirements for metering pumps, as outlined in Document ID 4-DST-XXXX-R1. These specifications ensure the pump's structural integrity, operational reliability, and compliance with relevant standards.

**General Mechanical Design**
The following mechanical design parameters **must** be specified or adhered to:
*   **Design Maximum Suction Pressure (kPa(g))**: The maximum allowable pressure at the pump's suction inlet.
*   **Design Maximum Temperature (oC)**: The maximum allowable operating temperature for the pump's components.
*   **Equipment Specification**: Detailed technical specifications for the pump.
*   **General Equipment Requirements**: Overarching requirements applicable to all equipment.
*   **Design Code**: The specific industry code or standard (e.g., ASME, API) to which the pump design **must** conform.
*   **Site Conditions**: Environmental and operational conditions at the installation site. Refer to [[site_design_conditions_1_dst_0001]] for details.
*   **Connection Type**: The type and size of process connections (e.g., flanged, threaded).
*   **Electrical Area Classification**: The hazardous area classification where the pump will be installed. Refer to [[electrical_area_classification_requirements]] for guidelines.
*   **Painting**: Specifications for external coatings and finishes.
*   **Total Noise Limit**: The maximum permissible noise level during pump operation.
*   **Vibration**: Acceptable vibration limits for smooth operation.
*   **Speed**: The operating speed of the pump (e.g., RPM).
*   **Discharge Accuracy**: The required precision of the pump's discharge volume.
*   **Control Range**: The range over which the pump's flow rate can be accurately controlled.

**Suction Piping and Performance**
*   **Suction piping length (m)**: The length of the piping on the suction side.
*   **Suction piping diameter (mm)**: The diameter of the piping on the suction side.
*   **Strokes per minute (spm)**: The operating frequency of the pump's reciprocating mechanism.
*   **Acceleration losses (kPa)**: Pressure losses due to fluid acceleration in the suction piping.

**Pump Type**
The pump type is specified as "Metering [Type]", allowing for various metering pump technologies (e.g., diaphragm, plunger) to be selected based on application needs.

**Related Specifications**

Further details on equipment design are available in [[equipment_design_and_specification_4_prc_0003]], and piping design considerations are covered in [[piping_design_and_specification_5_prc_0003]].

**Operational and Physical Parameters**
*   **Pump Type**: Metering [Type]
*   **Strokes per minute**: Measured in spm
*   **Suction Piping Length**: Measured in m
*   **Suction Piping Diameter**: Measured in mm
