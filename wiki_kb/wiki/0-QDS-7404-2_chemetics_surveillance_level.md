# Chemetics Surveillance Level for ESP Lead Lined Components

Chemetics' surveillance activities for the Electrostatic Precipitator Lead Lined Components are defined to ensure adherence to quality standards throughout the manufacturing and inspection processes.

**Surveillance Level:**
*   Surveillance by Chemetics **shall** be conducted to Level 0.0.
*   The specific details and scope of this surveillance **shall** be as set out within this [[0-QDS-7404-2_document_details|Quality Data Sheet (0-QDS-7404-2)]].

This level of surveillance dictates the extent of Chemetics' involvement in witnessing inspections and reviewing documentation, as further detailed in the [[0-QDS-7404-2_inspection_and_documentation_requirements|Inspection and Documentation Requirements]] for these components.
