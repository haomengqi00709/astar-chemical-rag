# Vendor Shop Survey Requirements (Orders > CAD 200k)

For orders valued greater than CAD 200,000 involving custom fabricated Goods and Services, a shop survey **must** be conducted if a short-listed Vendor has not performed any work for Aker Kvaerner Chemetics within the previous three years.

**Survey Execution and Documentation:**
*   An Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Quality Assurance Inspector **must** conduct the shop survey.
*   The scope and report format for the shop survey **must** follow the [[vendor_shop_survey_template]] (0-REP-5XXX).
*   The shop survey document **must** be numbered in accordance with [[document_numbering_and_name_change_conventions]] (0-PRC-0002) and **must** use the same identification number as the original requisition.
*   If surveys are conducted for multiple Vendors, they **must** be considered as sheets of a single document.

**Inspector's Recommendations:**
The Shop Survey **must** include the Inspector's recommendations for:
*   Additional Tests and Inspections.
*   A higher level of Quality Surveillance for the Vendor.

**Approval and Distribution:**
The Inspector **must** publish the Shop Survey. The Manager of Quality Assurance **must** approve the Shop Survey. A copy of the approved shop survey **must** be forwarded to the Requisitioning Discipline.

The Requisitioning Discipline **must** account for any additional cost elements identified in the shop survey within the [[detailed_cost_bid_evaluation|Cost Tabulation]].
