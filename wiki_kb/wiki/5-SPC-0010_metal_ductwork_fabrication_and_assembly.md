# Metal Ductwork Fabrication and Assembly (5-SPC-0010)

**Field Joints**
Field joints are to be executed using the split welding band design, as detailed in the duct detail drawings. Field joints required for construction are shown on the drawings, without regard to shipping limitations. The Vendor may add field joints where required, but the intention is to minimise additional field joints.

**Expansion Joint Bracing**
Duct expansion joints may be supplied with bracing installed to prevent joint movements during shipping and installation. If expansion joints are furnished without braces, or if supplied braces are inadequate for shipping, the Vendor **shall** provide additional braces. For significant lengths of attached ducts, four (4) pieces of 100 x 100 x 10 mm angle welded to the inside of the duct **should** be used as a minimum to brace each expansion joint. Stainless steel angle, or carbon steel angles with stainless steel poison pads, **shall** be used to brace expansion joints in stainless steel ducts. Braces installed on duct expansion joints **must** be left in place for shipping.

The Vendor **shall** ensure that expansion joint hinges are oriented in the duct as shown on the drawings. Expansion joint hinge positions **shall** be clearly marked on the outside of the duct section by the Vendor. Expansion joints are typically provided with internal hinges.

**Workmanship Standards**
Poor workmanship will be reason for rejection. Flaws or defects which cannot be reasonably repaired, excessive repairs, poor fit-up, nozzles requiring relocation, and damaged surfaces will be considered evidence of poor workmanship.

**Attachment Fit-up**
All pads, backing plates, manhole necks, and similar attachments **must** fit closely to and conform with the curvature of the surface to which they are attached.

**Weld Proximity**
Manways, nozzles, and their reinforcement **shall not** be located within 75mm of any weld.

**Alignment Tolerances**
The butting edges of plates at all seams **shall not** be out-of-alignment by more than 20% of the thickness of the plate, or 1.6mm, whichever is less.

**Diameter Tolerances**
The difference between the maximum and minimum diameters as measured against any cross section **shall not** exceed 0.5% of the nominal diameter.
