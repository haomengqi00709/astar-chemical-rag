# Metal Ductwork Vendor Scope of Supply (5-SPC-0010)

The Vendor **shall** furnish all necessary labour, supervision, materials, consumables, and services for the comprehensive fabrication, assembly, inspection, testing, painting, and preparation for delivery of the specified ductwork. The Vendor's supply **must** be in accordance with [[5-SPC-0010_metal_ductwork_fabrication_specification_overview]] and all relevant Aker Kvaerner Chemetics drawings and specifications.

The Vendor's scope of supply explicitly includes:
*   Ductwork supports and guides.
*   Manways, blind flanges, spectacle and blind plates.
*   Bolting and gaskets for all bolted connections.
*   Weld bands for all ductwork field joints.
*   Lifting lugs required for shipping and installation of ductwork spools. The Vendor is responsible for the design and location of all lifting lugs required for shipment and installation. The material of lifting lugs **must** be the same as that of the ductwork spool to which it is attached.
*   Temporary bracing and supports required for shipping and erection of ductwork.
*   Spare parts.

For components supplied by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or other parties, refer to [[5-SPC-0010_metal_ductwork_free_issue_components]].
