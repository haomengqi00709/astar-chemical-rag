# PVN/PCN Program Objectives and Requirements

The Project Variance Notification (PVN) and Project Change Notification (PCN) Program is a critical component of project management, designed to be utilized throughout the Engineering, Procurement, and Construction (EPC) phase of a project.

**Program Objectives:**
*   To serve as a mechanism for identifying, monitoring, rejecting, or authorizing project changes.
*   To function as an "early warning system" for identifying significant changes that are likely to have either a positive or negative influence on the approved project budget and schedule.
*   To provide essential information that ensures a timely and accurate forecast of the total project cost and schedule.

**Program Requirements:**
The Change Management Program, encompassing the PVN/PCN process, **must** satisfy the following requirements:
*   **Early Identification:** The program **must** ensure the early identification of any situation that may impact cost, schedule, execution plan, or cash flow.
*   **Fast Evaluation:** It **must** facilitate a rapid evaluation of the impact on cost, schedule, execution plan, cash flow, and the underlying reasons, along with alternative solutions.
*   **Notification:** Project management **must** be promptly notified of all identified changes.
*   **Regular Meetings:** The program **must** include provisions for regular meetings aimed at the timely resolution of Changes, including approval, rejection, acceptance, and the implementation of corrective or mitigating actions.
*   **Weekly Updates:** Weekly updates of Change reports **must** be provided, accurately recording decisions made, action items, and the current status of the [[pvn_pcn_log_and_documentation_management|Change Log]].
*   **Action Implementation and Tracking:** The program **must** ensure the implementation of approved actions, with ongoing tracking and a minimum of bi-weekly update reporting on the progress of Approved Changes.
*   **Forecast Revisions:** Forecasted cost, schedule, or cash flow revisions **must** be performed when authorized.
*   **Budget and Schedule Revisions:** Revisions to the project budget, baseline schedule, or budgeted cash flow **must** be made when authorized and supplemented by an approved Client’s Change Order.
*   **Funding Source Identification:** The program **must** identify the source of funding, if applicable (e.g., Management Reserve, Allowances).

**Special Considerations for Minor Changes**
*   Changes below $10,000 can be accumulated before a potential trend is raised in some cases to reduce administrative effort.
*   Accumulation of changes below $10,000 would not be done if approval to proceed is required or if the reason for the change is an issue that needs to be managed or communicated.
