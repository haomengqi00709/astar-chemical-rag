# Requisition Content and Structure (0-PRC-5002)

The content of a Requisition is structured to provide a comprehensive description of the required Goods and Services, ensuring clarity for both Request for Quotation (RFQ) and Purchase Order (PO) processes.

Key sections and line items within the Requisition **must** include:
*   **Line Items 1.x:** Detailing the Scope of Supply.
*   **Line Items 2.x:** Specifying Documentation requirements.
*   **Text Section 1:** A narrative description of the Scope of Supply.
*   **Text Section 2:** A narrative description of the Documentation requirements.

The information provided within these sections of the Requisition is intended to be directly duplicated into the corresponding sections of the Request for Quotation or Purchase Order. This ensures consistency and accuracy across procurement documents.

For examples of standard Purchase Order text, refer to [[form_of_purchase_order_0_prc_5001]] (0-PRC-5001).

Restructuring of the Requisition, particularly for the issuance of a Purchase Order (as discussed in Section 5 of 0-PRC-5002), may be necessary. This restructuring ensures that the content is formatted appropriately for direct transfer to the Purchase Order, maintaining a seamless transition from requisition to procurement.
