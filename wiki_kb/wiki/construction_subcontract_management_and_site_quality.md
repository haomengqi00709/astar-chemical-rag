# Construction Subcontract Management and Site Quality

The Director is responsible for ensuring that the plant, whether prefabricated at workshops or constructed at the Site, meets all requirements outlined in the design documents issued for construction.

**Construction Planning**
Initial planning for construction is undertaken in accordance with:
*   [[project_planning_0_prc_3001]]: Project Planning (0-PRC-3001).
*   [[0-prc-3002_project_scheduling_procedure_overview]]: Project Scheduling (0-PRC-3002).
*   [[construction_subcontract_issuance_procedure_0_prc_6005]]: (0-PRC-6005).

Deliverables from the construction planning process include:
*   [[0-prc-2003_project_execution_plan_overview]]: Project Execution Plan (0-PRC-2003).
*   [[0-dat-3001_project_schedule]]: Project Schedule (0-DAT-3001). Data available in SQL: 0-DAT-3001
*   [[special_conditions_for_construction_subcontract_0_spc_6x01]]: Special Conditions for Subcontract (0-SPC-6002). Data available in SQL: 0-SPC-6002

Initial plans **will** be reviewed and revised as necessary, in accordance with [[0-prc-6001_plant_constructability_review_procedure_overview]]: Plant Constructability Review (0-PRC-6001).

**Subcontract Preparation and Issuance**
The Subcontract informs Subcontractors of requirements for material supply, receipt of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics-supplied materials, and project construction. Requirements are typically referenced through attached drawings and specifications. Subcontracts **will** be prepared and issued in accordance with:
*   [[construction_subcontract_agreement_procedure_0_prc_6002]]: (0-PRC-6002).
*   [[construction_subcontractor_evaluation_and_approval_0_prc_6003]]: (0-PRC-6003).
*   [[construction_subcontract_issuance_procedure_0_prc_6005]]: (0-PRC-6005).
*   [[construction_subcontract_preparation_and_issuance_0_prc_6006]]: (0-PRC-6006).

**Subcontractor Selection and Management Plans**
The [[project_quality_objectives]] (Section 1.3 of the original document) **must** be met, and Subcontractors **must** be capable of meeting all requirements in their Subcontracts. Subcontractors **will** be selected in accordance with [[commercial_terms_for_construction_subcontract_0_prc_6004]]: (0-PRC-6004).

The Subcontractor is required to develop management plans in accordance with:
*   [[subcontractor_management_plans_0_spc_600]]: (0-SPC-600). Data available in SQL: 0-SPC-600
*   [[subcontractor_management_plans_0_spc_605]]: (0-SPC-605). Data available in SQL: 0-SPC-605
*   [[subcontractor_environmental_management_0_spc_606]]: Subcontractor Environmental Management (0-SPC-606). Data available in SQL: 0-SPC-606
*   [[subcontractor_industrial_relations_management_0_spc_60]]: Subcontractor Industrial Relations Management (0-SPC-60). Data available in SQL: 0-SPC-60
*   [[subcontractor_material_control_0_spc_60_material_control]]: Subcontractor Material Control (0-SPC-60). Data available in SQL: 0-SPC-60

The Subcontractor **must** have sufficient on-site supervision to effectively manage construction work, in accordance with approved management plans. CVs of key personnel are reviewed as part of 0-PRC-[[0_prc_xxx_cv_review]].

**Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Site Management Team**
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **will** maintain a management team at the site, led by the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Site Construction Manager. This team serves as the primary contact for the Project General Contractor and Owner, and provides front-line subcontract administration.

The site management team **will** audit and monitor Subcontractor performance in:
*   Planning and Control (progress monitoring)
*   Safety Management
*   Environmental Management
*   Industrial Relations
*   Materials Control

Roles and responsibilities of the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site management team, their interaction with Subcontractor supervisors, and with the Project General Contractor **will** be described in the specifications listed in Section 7.5 of the original document, and in [[construction_site_management_roles_0_prc_600]]: (0-PRC-600).

The site management team **will** resolve Subcontractor Technical Queries where possible, and facilitate resolution from the Vancouver design team. Resolution of Technical Queries is in accordance with [[construction_technical_queries_0_prc_xxx]]: Construction Technical Queries (0-PRC-XXX).

**Subcontractor Documentation and Quality Assurance**
Documentation issuance to the Subcontractor **will** be in accordance with [[0-prc-0006_control_of_project_documents]]: Control of Project Documentation. The Subcontractor **will** be required to comply with [[subcontractor_document_supply_0_spc_xxx]]: (0-SPC-XXX). Data available in SQL: 0-SPC-XXX

Vendor Document Requirements (VDR) documents **will** be issued to the Subcontractor as needed, detailing documentation to be supplied, submittal dates, and "hold" documents. "Hold" documents require Aker Kvaerner Chemetics review before fabrication/construction proceeds.

Upon receipt, Subcontractor documentation **will** be scanned, assigned Aker Kvaerner Chemetics document numbers, and placed in the Project electronic file. All Discipline Leads **will** be advised. The Project Engineer **will** assign responsibility for reviewing Subcontractor documentation for compliance with the Subcontract, in accordance with [[review_of_vendor_documentation_0_prc_0007]]: Review of Vendor Documentation (0-PRC-0007). Logging and control **will** follow [[0-prc-0006_control_of_project_documents]]: Control of Project Documentation.

The Subcontractor **will** be required to comply with [[subcontractor_quality_assurance_0_pc_xxx]]: (0-PC-XXX). Data available in SQL: 0-PC-XXX. Quality Data Sheets (QDS) **will** be provided for each tagged item and bulk material class, stating requirements for the Subcontractor’s Inspection and Test Plan, reports, and Aker Kvaerner Chemetics surveillance levels, including Witness and Hold points.

Surveillance of Subcontractor quality **will** be carried out by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Site Quality Assurance Inspectors, in accordance with [[subcontractor_quality_assurance_0_prc_7006]]: Subcontractor Quality Assurance (0-PRC-7006). Owner surveillance **will** generally coincide with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Inspector surveillance.

Initial planning of construction will be undertaken in accordance with 0-PRC-6005. Initial plans will be reviewed and revised as necessary in accordance with 0-PRC-6001: Plant Constructability Review.

**Subcontract Requirements**
The Subcontract informs Subcontractors of requirements for the supply of certain materials, the receipt of materials supplied by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, and the construction of the Project. Subcontract requirements are usually conveyed by reference to attached drawings and specifications.

Project Quality Objectives stated in Section 1.3 **must** be met, and Subcontractors **must** be able to meet the requirements as set out in their Subcontracts.

**On-Site Supervision and Personnel**
The Subcontractor is required to have sufficient on-site supervision to effectively manage the construction work, and this on-site supervision **must** be in accordance with the approved management plans. CV’s of key personnel are reviewed as part of 0-PRC-.

**Site Management Team Roles and Documentation**
Roles and responsibilities of the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site management team, their interaction with the Subcontractor’s supervisors and the interaction of both groups with the Project General Contractor will be described in the specifications listed in Section 7.5.

Issues of documentation to the Subcontractor will be in accordance with 0-PRC-0006: Control of Project Documentation. The Subcontractor will be required to comply with the requirements of 0-SPC-.

**Subcontractor Document Handling**
On receipt of Subcontractor documentation, it will be scanned, assigned Aker Kvaerner Chemetics document numbers, and placed in the Project electronic file. All Discipline Leads will be advised of the receipt of the Subcontractor documentation. The Project Engineer will assign responsibility for review of Subcontractor documentation for compliance with the Subcontract. Review of Subcontractor documentation is in accordance with 0-PRC-0007: Review of Vendor Documentation. Logging and control of Subcontractor documentation will be carried out in accordance with 0-PRC-0006: Control of Project Documentation.

**Subcontractor Quality Data Sheets**
The Subcontractor will be provided with Quality Data Sheets for each tagged item and for each class or type of bulk material installed by the Subcontractor. The Quality Data Sheet will state the requirements for the Subcontractor’s Inspection and Test Plan, the Inspection and Testing Reports to be issued, the level of Surveillance by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, and will identify Witness and Hold points.
