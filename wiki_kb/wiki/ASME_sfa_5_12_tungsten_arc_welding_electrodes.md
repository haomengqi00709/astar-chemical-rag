# ASME SFA-5.12 Specification for Tungsten Arc-Welding Electrodes

ASME SFA-5.12 is a specification that defines the requirements for tungsten arc-welding electrodes. This standard covers the classification, chemical composition, and other characteristics necessary for the proper selection and use of tungsten electrodes in arc welding processes.

This specification is referenced in [[4-SPC-0014_referenced_codes_and_definitions]] and specifically in [[4-SPC-0014_welding_processes_and_parameters]] for the selection of preferred GTAW electrodes for Columbium Stabilized High Nickel Austenitic stainless steel fabrications. The preferred GTAW electrode is thoriated tungsten as per ASME SFA-5.12, Class EWTh-2.

For detailed information on tungsten electrode classification and requirements, refer to the latest edition of ASME SFA-5.12.
