# Brick Lined Equipment Field Inspection Documentation and Sign-off

This page outlines the mandatory documentation and sign-off requirements for the field inspection of brick-lined equipment, as specified in Document ID 4-FIC-XX15-R0. Proper completion of these sections is essential for formal acceptance of the inspection.

**Mandatory Sign-off Requirements:**

*   **Aker Kvaerner Chemetics:**
    *   A signature is **required** from Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
    *   The name of the signatory is also **required**.
    *   The date of signature is **required**.
*   **Sub Contractor:**
    *   A signature is **required** from the Sub Contractor.
    *   The name of the signatory is also **required**.
    *   The date of signature is **required**.
*   **Client:**
    *   A signature is **required** from the Client.
    *   The name of the signatory is also **required**.
    *   The date of signature is **required**.

These signatures and dates serve as formal acknowledgment and acceptance of the inspection findings and the condition of the brick-lined equipment.

For an overview of this checklist, refer to [[4-FIC-XX15-R0_brick_lined_equipment_field_inspection_checklist_overview]].
General requirements for inspection and documentation, including quality record definitions, can be found in [[0-QDS-7431_inspection_and_documentation_requirements]] and [[0-QDS-7431_quality_record_definitions]].
