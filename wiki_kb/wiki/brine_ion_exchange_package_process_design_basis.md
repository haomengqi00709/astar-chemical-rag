# Brine Ion Exchange Package Process Design Basis

This page details the process design conditions and basis for the Brine Ion Exchange Package, as outlined in Document ID 4-PDS-XXXX-R1. These parameters are fundamental for the sizing, selection, and hydraulic design of the system.

**Process Design Conditions**
*   **Case Description:** [[pfd_overview|PFD]] (Process Flow Diagram)

**Design Basis**
The following criteria form the design basis for the Brine Ion Exchange Package:
*   **Resin Volume Sizing/Selection:** Based on the PFD flow rate.
*   **Hydraulics:** Designed for 120% of the PFD flow rate.
*   **Resin Volume:** Utilizes specified average salt impurities as detailed in the salt specification.

For specific feed brine characteristics, refer to [[brine_ion_exchange_package_feed_brine_specifications]]. Performance targets are detailed in [[brine_ion_exchange_package_performance_requirements]].
