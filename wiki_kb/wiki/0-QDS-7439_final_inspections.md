# Scrubber Venturi Internals Final Inspections

This section outlines the final inspection requirements for the Scrubber Venturi Internals, covering critical visual and dimensional checks to ensure the finished product meets design specifications and quality standards.

**Dimensional Inspection:**
*   **Requirement:** Dimensional inspection **is required** as [[0-QDS-7439_quality_record_definitions|Quality Record FP]] (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package).
*   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Witness and Hold. Chemetics **will** witness the dimensional inspection and has the authority to place a hold on the process if discrepancies are found.

**Contour & Finish of Welds:**
*   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Witness and Hold. Chemetics **will** witness the inspection of weld contours and finishes and has the authority to place a hold on the process if discrepancies are found.

**Surfaces Free from Defects:**
*   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Witness and Hold. Chemetics **will** witness the inspection to ensure surfaces are free from defects and has the authority to place a hold on the process if discrepancies are found.
*   **Remarks:** Spot 0.25. (Further details on the nature or extent of spot checks for defects may be found in related specifications).

**Cleanliness:**
*   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Witness and Hold. Chemetics **will** witness the cleanliness inspection and has the authority to place a hold on the process if discrepancies are found.
*   **Remarks:** 1.0, 1.0. (Specific cleanliness standards or levels, e.g., surface roughness or particulate limits, are indicated by these values).

**Review QA Documentation:**
*   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Witness and Hold. Chemetics **will** witness the review of the Quality Assurance Documentation and has the authority to place a hold on the process if discrepancies are found.

These final inspections are crucial steps to verify the physical attributes and documentation compliance of the Scrubber Venturi Internals before they are prepared for shipment.
