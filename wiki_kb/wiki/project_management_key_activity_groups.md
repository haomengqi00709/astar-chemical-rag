# Project Management Key Activity Groups (PEM)

[[0-prc-2101_project_execution_model_gate_reviews_overview|Project Execution Model (PEM) Milestone Gate Reviews]] also evaluate project performance against requirements defined within various Project Management Key Activity Groups. These groups cover the overarching management and control aspects of a project.

**Key Activity Groups**
*   Concept Management
*   Project Execution Strategy
*   HSE Management
*   Cost Estimating / Control
*   Planning & Scheduling
*   Risk Management
*   Weight/Quantity Management
*   Information Management
*   Quality Management
*   Contract / Sub-contract Management
*   Change Management
