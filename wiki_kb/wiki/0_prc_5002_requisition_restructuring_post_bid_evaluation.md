# Requisition Restructuring Post-Bid Evaluation (0-PRC-5002)

The process of [[0_prc_5003_bid_evaluation_and_approval|bid evaluation]] (0-PRC-5003) may reveal that the optimal grouping of items for ordering differs from the initial structure used in the Requisition for a Request for Quotation (RFQ).

In such cases, it may be necessary to restructure the Requisition for the subsequent Purchase Order (PO). This restructuring, as detailed in Section 5 of 0-PRC-5002, is essential to:
*   Align the Requisition with the final commercial and technical agreements reached during bid evaluation.
*   Ensure that the content of the Requisition for the Purchase Order can be directly and accurately copied onto the Purchase Order itself. This streamlines the procurement process and minimizes potential discrepancies.

Restructured Requisitions for the issue of a Purchase Order **shall** be prepared accordingly, reflecting any changes in item groupings or scope as determined by the bid evaluation outcome.
