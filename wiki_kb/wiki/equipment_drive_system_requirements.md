# Equipment Drive System Requirements

As detailed in document 4-SPC-0001, specific requirements govern the design, selection, and installation of equipment drive systems.

**Overload Protection**
Where equipment **may stall** occasionally, suitable overload protection devices **such as** shear pins, zero speed switches, or slip clutches **will be incorporated**. High inertia load devices **will be equipped** with centrifugal clutches or fluid couplings to manage start-up and shutdown stresses.

**Couplings**
Couplings **will be selected** to transmit the design starting torque with a service factor of 1.2. They **shall be manufactured** from SG cast iron, cast, or fabricated steel. Couplings **will be mounted** to their respective shafts using Taper-Lock bushings and rectangular parallel keys for drives up to and including 150 kW.

**V-Belts**
V-belt sizes **will be** 3V, 5V, or 8V wherever possible. Belts **will be supplied** in matched sets. Single groove sheaves **are not acceptable**. The V-belt service factor **will be** a minimum of 1.4 times the motor nameplate HP.

**Drive Guards**
All drive guards (for chains, couplings, belt drives, etc.) **will be supplied** with the equipment. Drive guards **must meet** OSHA requirements. Belt guards **will be** split and **must be** open-mesh, stainless steel, and totally enclosed. Guard openings **must be smaller** than a finger to prevent access to moving parts. Belt guards **will have** a hinged opening to allow for checking belt tension. All guards **will be provided** with a tachometer opening.

**Gear Reducers**
For gear reducer selection, a service factor **will be applied** to the motor nameplate HP rating to determine the mechanical rating.
*   Normal duty service factor for gear reducers: 1.50.
*   Heavy duty service factor for gear reducers: 1.75.
Gear reducers **will be sized** for continuous duty 24 hours per day, 365 days a year. External cooling of gear reducers **is not permitted**.

These specifications ensure robust, safe, and reliable operation of all driven equipment.

**High Inertia Loads**
High inertia load devices will be equipped with centrifugal clutches or fluid couplings.

**Coupling Selection and Materials**
Couplings will be selected to transmit the design starting torque with a service factor of 1.2.
Couplings will be manufactured from SG cast iron, cast or fabricated steel.
Couplings will be mounted to their respective shafts using Taper-Lock bushings and rectangular parallel keys for drives up to and including 150 kW.

**V-Belt Specifications**
V-belt sizes will be 3V, 5V or 8V wherever possible.
Belts will be supplied in matched sets.
Single groove sheaves are not acceptable.
V-belt service factor will be minimum 1.4 times the motor nameplate HP.

**Drive Guards**
All drive guards (for chains, couplings, belt drives, etc.) will be supplied with the equipment.
Drive guards will meet OSHA requirements.
Belt guards will be split.
Guards will be open-mesh, stainless steel, and totally enclosed.
Guard openings must be smaller than a finger.
Belt guards will have a hinged opening to allow for checking belt tension.
All guards will be provided with a tachometer opening.

**Gear Reducer Selection and Design**
For gear reducer selection, a service factor will be applied to the motor nameplate HP rating to determine mechanical rating.
Normal duty service factor for gear reducers: 1.50.
Heavy duty service factor for gear reducers: 1.75.
Gear reducers will be sized for continuous duty 24 hours per day, 365 days a year.
External cooling of gear reducers is not permitted.
