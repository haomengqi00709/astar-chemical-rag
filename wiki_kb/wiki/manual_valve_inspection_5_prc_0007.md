# Manual Valve Inspection (5-PRC-0007)

The inspection of manual valves is a critical component of the [[mechanical_checkout_piping_5_prc_0007|Piping Mechanical Checkout Procedure (5-PRC-0007)]], ensuring their correct installation, accessibility, and functionality.

**Required Documentation for Manual Valve Checkout:**
*   [[piping_and_instrumentation_drawings|P&ID]]
*   [[valve_specification|Valve Specification]]
*   [[pipe_isometric|Isometric]]

**Manual Valve Inspection Steps:**
*   **Flange Checks:** All flange checks for manual valves **shall** be performed as described in the [[flange_inspection_requirements_5_prc_0007|Flange Inspection Requirements]].
*   **Type and Tag Verification:** Confirm that the valve type and tag number match the [[piping_and_instrumentation_drawings|P&ID]] and [[pipe_isometric|isometric]].
*   **Accessibility:** Confirm that the valve installation provides adequate accessibility for both operation and maintenance.
*   **Handle Rotation:** If applicable, confirm that the valve handle can rotate 900 (degrees) without interfering with adjacent equipment or structures.
*   **Handle Orientation:** Confirm that the valve handle orientation (indicating open and close positions) matches the isometric.
*   **Flow Direction:** Confirm that the valve has been installed in the correct flow direction (where applicable).
*   **Body Support:** Confirm that the valve body has been correctly and adequately supported (where applicable).
*   **Butterfly Valve Gasket Interference:** For butterfly valves, confirm that there is no gasket interference with the valve disc when it is fully open.
*   **Operational Check:** Rotate the valve handle on all valves from fully closed to fully open to confirm smooth and proper operation.
*   **Spacers:** Confirm that spacers have been installed where applicable.

These checks are vital for ensuring the reliable and safe operation of manual valves within the piping system.
