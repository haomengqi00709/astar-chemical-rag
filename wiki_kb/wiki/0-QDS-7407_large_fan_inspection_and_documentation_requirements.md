# Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7407: Large Fan Inspection and Documentation Requirements

This page details the comprehensive inspection and documentation requirements for the Large Fan, as specified in Quality Data Sheet (QDS) 0-QDS-7407. These requirements ensure that all components and processes meet the highest quality standards, with specific mandates for quality records and Chemetics' surveillance involvement. For definitions of "AP" and "FP" quality records, refer to [[0-QDS-7407_large_fan_quality_record_definitions]].

**1. Material Verification:**
*   **Mill Test Certificates:**
    *   **Quality Record Required:** FP
    *   **Surveillance by Chemetics:** Complete
    *   *Description:* Documentation confirming the chemical and mechanical properties of materials used, directly from the mill. These certificates are crucial for verifying material compliance with design specifications.
*   **Certificate of Compliance:**
    *   **Quality Record Required:** FP
    *   *Description:* A formal statement from the vendor confirming that the materials and components comply with specified standards and requirements. This document provides an overarching assurance of adherence to project specifications.

**2. Coating / Lining Inspection:**
*   **External Coating/Lining:**
    *   **Quality Record Required:** FP
    *   **Surveillance by Chemetics:** Complete
    *   *Description:* Inspection of the external protective coatings or linings for proper application, thickness, and integrity. This ensures protection against environmental factors and corrosion.
*   **Internal Coating/Lining:**
    *   *Description:* Inspection of internal protective coatings or linings, ensuring they meet specifications for corrosion resistance and finish. This is vital for process integrity and equipment longevity.

**3. Equipment Testing:**
*   **Balancing:**
    *   **Quality Record Required:** FP
    *   **Surveillance by Chemetics:** Complete
    *   *Description:* Testing to ensure the fan's rotating components are properly balanced to minimize vibration and ensure smooth, efficient operation.
*   **Noise Data:**
    *   **Quality Record Required:** FP
    *   **Surveillance by Chemetics:** Complete
    *   *Description:* Measurement and recording of the fan's operational noise levels to ensure compliance with environmental and occupational safety standards.
*   **Motor Test:**
    *   **Quality Record Required:** AP + FP
    *   **Surveillance by Chemetics:** Complete
    *   *Description:* Comprehensive testing of the fan's motor to verify its performance, electrical characteristics, and operational integrity under various conditions.
*   **Run Test:**
    *   *Description:* An operational test of the fan assembly under specified conditions to confirm functionality and identify any immediate issues or anomalies before final acceptance.
*   **Performance Test:**
    *   *Description:* Detailed testing to verify the fan's performance against design specifications, including airflow, pressure, and efficiency. This confirms the equipment meets its intended operational parameters.

**4. Final Inspection (Required in Vendor I & T Plan Witness and Hold Points):**
The following items require both a Witness Point and a Hold Point in the Vendor's Inspection and Test (I&T) Plan, indicating critical stages where Chemetics' presence and approval are mandatory before proceeding:
*   **Dimensional:** Verification of all critical dimensions against approved drawings to ensure manufacturing accuracy.
*   **Contour & Finish of Welds:** Inspection of weld quality, contour, and finish to ensure structural integrity, aesthetic standards, and absence of defects.
*   **Surfaces Free from Defects:** Examination of all surfaces for any defects, imperfections, or damage that could affect performance or appearance.
*   **Cleanliness:** Assessment of the overall cleanliness of the equipment, both internally and externally, prior to packaging, to prevent contamination.
*   **Motor:** Final visual and functional check of the motor, including connections and mounting.
*   **Coupling/Belt:** Inspection of the coupling or belt drive system for correct alignment, tension, and installation.
*   **Guard:** Verification that all safety guards are correctly installed, secure, and meet specifications to protect personnel.
*   **Baseplate:** Inspection of the baseplate for proper fabrication, leveling, and finish, ensuring a stable foundation for the fan.
*   **Silencer:** Final check of any installed silencers for correct assembly, condition, and effectiveness in noise reduction.
*   **Review QA Documentation:** Final review of the complete Quality Assurance Documentation Package to ensure all required records are present and accurate.

**5. Packing/Crating:**
*   **Packaging:**
    *   **Required in Vendor I & T Plan:** Witness
    *   *Description:* Witnessing the packaging process to ensure the fan is adequately protected for shipment, preventing damage during transit.
*   **Packing List:**
    *   **Quality Record Required:** AP
    *   **Surveillance by Chemetics:** Complete
    *   *Description:* A detailed list of all items included in the shipment, required "As Prepared" by the vendor, for inventory and customs purposes.

These inspection and documentation requirements are integral to the overall [[0-QDS-7407_large_fan_vendor_quality_assurance_requirements]] and ensure the delivery of a high-quality Large Fan that meets all project specifications.
