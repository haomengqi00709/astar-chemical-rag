# PO Register

The PO (Purchase Order) Register is a comprehensive log of all purchase orders issued for a project. This register is a key document for tracking project expenditures and material acquisitions, and it is attached to the [[0-rep-2001_monthly_progress_report_format|Monthly Progress Report]].
