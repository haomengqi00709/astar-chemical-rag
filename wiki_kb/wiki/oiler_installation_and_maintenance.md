# Oiler Installation and Maintenance

This page describes the requirements for the installation and checking of [[oiler_installation_and_maintenance|oilers]] for [[rotating_equipment_mechanical_checkout|rotating]] and [[conveying_equipment_mechanical_checkout|conveying equipment]]. Oilers **must** be installed (if required), checked for leakage, and contain the correct specification oil to the fill line.
