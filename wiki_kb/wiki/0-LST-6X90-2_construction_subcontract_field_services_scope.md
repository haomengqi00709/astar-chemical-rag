# 0-LST-6X90-2 Construction Subcontract Field Services Scope

The 0-LST-6X90-2 document outlines the "Construction Subcontract Field Services Scope." This document details the specific field services items that are to be included in the [[0-LST-6X15_construction_subcontract_equipment_schedule]].

The items listed in this scope document are considered part of [[construction_subcontract_indirect_work_costs]] and are essential for the orderly completion of the project, even though they do not become a permanent part of the final installation. Subcontractors **must** refer to this document when preparing their equipment schedules and cost allocations.
