# Aker Kvaerner Safety Policy Manual

The Aker Kvaerner Safety Policy Manual serves as a foundational document for safety procedures within the organization.

**Key Reference:**
Section 7, item 7.25 of this manual contains the base procedure for [[0-PRC-8505-A_line_and_flange_break_procedure|Line and Flange Break operations]], which was issued in January of 2003. This document provides the overarching safety framework upon which specific operational procedures are built.
