# Welding Processes and Parameters for 6% Moly SS (4-SPC-0017)

This section outlines the preferred welding processes and their specific parameters for the fabrication of 6 percent Moly austenitic stainless steel under Standard **4-SPC-0017**.

**Preferred Welding Processes**
The preferred welding processes for 6 percent Moly austenitic steel are Shielded Metal Arc Welding (SMAW), Gas Tungsten Arc Welding (GTAW), and Gas Metal Arc Welding (GMAW). Other welding processes **will** be evaluated upon submission of full details, including the procedure and exact location of proposed weldments.

**Shielded Metal Arc Welding (SMAW)**
*   **Heat Input Control:** Minimum practical amperage, stringer beads rather than weave, and minimum arc length are recommended to minimize heat input.

**Gas Tungsten Arc Welding (GTAW)**
*   **Arc Starting:** High frequency (HF) voltage **must** be used to start the GTAW arc.
*   **Pass Thickness:** GTAW weld metal thickness **must not** exceed 1/8" per pass.
*   **Heat Input Control:** GTAW heat input **must** be kept low by using the stringer bead technique.
*   **Back-up Gas:** Where necessary, a back-up gas **must** be used to prevent root pass oxidation and contamination. This back-up gas is normally 100% argon with 99.9% purity. The back-up gas requirement is **mandatory** for root pass welds on piping or other items where access to the internal weld surface is limited or non-existent.
*   **Guidelines:** Consideration **will** be given to the implementation of guidelines in "TAPPI TI5 GTAW root pass welding of 6% Molybdenum Austenitic Stainless Steel Pipe - open root joint with hand fed filler."

**Gas Metal Arc Welding (GMAW)**
*   **Application:** GMAW **will** only be used for the root pass of butt welds and for fillet welds unless specifically agreed otherwise.
*   **Gas Backing:** Gas backing is required for GMAW where accessibility to the internal weld surface is limited.

**Submerged Arc Welding**
*   The use of Submerged Arc Welding **must** be reviewed and approved in writing by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics prior to the start of fabrication.
*   The Fabricator **must** provide satisfactory evidence of continuous shop use of Submerged Arc Welding in the fabrication of austenitic stainless steel.
