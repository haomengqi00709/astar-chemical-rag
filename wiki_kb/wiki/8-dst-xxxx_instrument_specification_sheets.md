# Instrument Specification Sheets (8-DST-XXXX)

Instrument Specification Sheets (8-DST-XXXX) are detailed documents that capture the technical requirements and characteristics of individual instruments.

**Content Requirements:**
Instrument Specification Sheets (8-DST-XXXX) **shall** contain:
*   Description of Instrument
*   Description of Service
*   Specific Instrument Details
*   Instrument Model Numbers
*   Instrument Code Requirements
*   Normal Operating Conditions (NOC) Reference
*   Operating stream composition and conditions
*   Calibration Data
*   Design stream composition and conditions
*   Wetted Parts Material of construction
*   Connection type, rating, and size
*   Any special dimensional requirements
*   Any special design criteria

**Document Preparation and Approval:**
*   **Prepared by:** Field Instrument Engineer (FIE)
*   **Checked by:** Process
*   **Approved by:** Field Instrument Engineer (FIE)

**Development Process:**
Where required, the FIE will create Instrument Specification Sheets for field-provided instrumentation. Specification sheets **will not** be created for instruments provided with mechanical packages.

These sheets are developed from Stage III [[p_id_development_and_approval_1_prc_0003_instrumentation]], [[8-lst-01xx_instrument_process_data_lists]], Site Design Conditions, and [[5-spc-0001_pipe_specification_instrumentation_design]]. The Process Engineer is responsible for checking the input of data as per the Process Data Lists and the Site Design Conditions. To assist the checking requirements of the Process Engineer, the FIE will provide all applicable information in a spreadsheet format obtained from the instrument database.

Pipe specification and size will be as per the [[5-prc-0004_piping_line_list_procedure_overview]] and P&IDs for Instrument Specification Sheets. Process connections sizes will be obtained from the [[5-spc-0001_pipe_specification_instrumentation_design]] and the [[5-lst-0011_standard_ansi_piping_connections_for_instrumentation]] spreadsheet for Instrument Specification Sheets.

Expedient development of the Instrument Specification sheets is dependent on providing the FIE with complete and accurate feed documents.
