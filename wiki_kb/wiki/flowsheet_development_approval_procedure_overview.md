# Procedure Overview: Flowsheet Development & Approval (1-PRC-0002)

This page provides an overview of Procedure 1-PRC-0002, titled "Flowsheet Development & Approval," which outlines the essential steps and responsibilities for creating and publishing Process Flow Diagrams (PFDs).

*   **Page Count:** The procedure consists of 6 pages, with each page numbered "Page X of 6".

**Purpose and Scope:**
This procedure defines the specific steps required to achieve a published [[pfd_overview|Process Flow Diagram (PFD)]]. It also assigns clear responsibilities for the preparation, checking, and approval of these critical documents.
