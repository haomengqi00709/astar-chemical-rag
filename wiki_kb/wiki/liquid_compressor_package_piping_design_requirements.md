# Liquid Ring Compressor Package Piping Design Requirements

This page details the piping design requirements for the Liquid Ring Compressor Package, as specified in Datasheet 4-PKG-XXXX-R1. These requirements are crucial for ensuring the safe and efficient transfer of fluids to and from the compressor.

**Piping Design Parameters**
The following piping design parameters **must** be specified for each relevant fluid stream:

*   **Fluid:** Identification of the fluid being transported (e.g., Process Fluid, Service Fluid, Seal Fluid, Utilities).
*   **Design Pressure (kPa(g)) maximum:** The maximum design pressure the piping system can safely withstand, in kPa(g).
*   **Design Pressure (kPa(g)) minimum:** The minimum design pressure (or vacuum) the piping system can safely withstand, in kPa(g).
*   **Design Temperature (oC) maximum:** The maximum design temperature the piping system can safely operate at, in °C.
*   **Design Temperature (oC) minimum:** The minimum design temperature the piping system can safely operate at, in °C.
*   **Protection for Overpressure:** Details on the methods or devices used to protect the piping system from overpressure conditions (e.g., relief valves).
*   **Protection for Vacuum:** Details on the methods or devices used to protect the piping system from vacuum conditions (e.g., vacuum breakers).

**Applicability of Requirements**
These piping design requirements apply specifically to the following fluid types associated with the liquid ring compressor package:

*   **Process Fluid:** The main fluid being compressed or handled by the equipment.
*   **Service Fluid:** Fluids used for sealing, cooling, or other auxiliary functions (e.g., seal water). Refer to [[liquid_ring_compressor_package_service_fluid_specifications]] for detailed service fluid specifications.
*   **Seal Fluid:** Any dedicated fluid used for mechanical seals.
*   **Utilities:** Other utility connections such as instrument air, cooling water, etc. Refer to [[utility_fluid_design_conditions]] for general utility specifications.

For overarching piping design guidelines, consult [[piping_design_criteria_5_prc_0002]]. General process design criteria can be found in [[process_design_criteria_1_prc_0001]].
