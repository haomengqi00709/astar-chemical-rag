# Equipment Sketch Requirements for Vessels (4-PDS-XXXX-R2)

The inclusion of an equipment sketch in the Template Vessel Process Data Sheet (4-PDS-XXXX-R2) is a flexible requirement, driven by the need to clearly communicate specific design features that cannot be adequately conveyed through tabular data alone.

**Mandatory Requirement:**
The equipment sketch is **not mandatory** for every vessel datasheet.

**Conditions for Inclusion:**
An equipment sketch **is only required** when it is necessary to communicate special dimensions and features. This includes, but is not limited to:
*   Relative positions of nozzles
*   Placement of baffles
*   Internal configurations
*   Unique geometric aspects

The sketch serves as a visual aid to supplement the detailed information provided in other sections of the PDS, such as the [[template_vessel_equipment_data]] and [[template_vessel_nozzle_table]].

**Consideration of Ancillary Equipment:**
When preparing the equipment sketch for the Vessel PDS, ancillary equipment requirements **should be considered**. This ensures that the sketch accurately reflects the integration points and spatial relationships for components like agitators, coils, or mist eliminators, as detailed in the [[template_vessel_notes_and_ancillary_equipment]] section. The sketch helps visualize how these components fit within the vessel and their impact on overall dimensions and layout.
