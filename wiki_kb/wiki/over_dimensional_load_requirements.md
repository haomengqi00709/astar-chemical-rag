# Over-Dimensional Load Shipping Requirements

For goods that exceed the capacity of a standard forty-foot ocean container, specific procedures and information are required. A standard forty-foot ocean container is approximately 1200 cm in length, 230 cm in width, and 230 cm in height.

The Vendor **must** supply comprehensive transport information four weeks in advance of the scheduled shipment date for any goods exceeding these dimensions. This transport information for over-dimensional loads may include, but is not limited to, detailed transport drawings, specific lifting and handling instructions, and estimated transportation weights and dimensions.

These requirements are a critical component of the overall [[packaging_marine_transportation_0_spc_5005]] guidelines, ensuring safe and compliant transport of large equipment.
