# Document Style and Format (0-PRC-0004)

This procedure, 0-PRC-0004, establishes the mandatory style and format guidelines for drafting all textual documents within Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. These guidelines apply to both internal standards and project-specific documentation, ensuring consistency and clarity across all written materials.

**Document Types and Purpose:**
*   **Procedures:** These documents describe the internal processes and methods by which Aker Kvaerner Chemetics personnel *will* perform work. They outline the step-by-step instructions and responsibilities for various tasks.
*   **Specifications:** These documents outline the requirements that Aker Kvaerner Chemetics imposes on external parties, specifically [[#Key Definitions|Suppliers]] and [[#Key Definitions|Subcontractors]], for the execution of work. They detail the technical, quality, and performance criteria that must be met.

**Company Naming Conventions:**
The full company name, "Aker Kvaerner Chemetics," *must* be used for its initial mention in any document. Subsequent references *may* use the abbreviation "AKC," provided the context clearly identifies it. It is important to note that Aker Kvaerner Chemetics operates as a division of Aker Kvaerner Canada Inc.

**Key Definitions:**
To ensure clarity and consistency across all documentation, the following terms are defined:
*   **Suppliers:** This term specifically refers to entities providing goods.
*   **Subcontractor:** This term encompasses entities providing services, or a combination of both goods and services.
*   **Owner:** This term designates the client, including any of their authorized representatives.

**Document Templates:**
All textual documents *must* utilize the standard template, identified as 0-SPC-0XXX: [Template Textual Document (Portrait)]. Templates are pre-formatted document versions designed to provide a consistent structure for project information, ensuring uniformity in presentation and content organization.
