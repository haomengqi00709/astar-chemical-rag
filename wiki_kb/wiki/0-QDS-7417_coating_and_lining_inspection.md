# Shell & Tube Heat Exchanger Coating and Lining Inspection (0-QDS-7417)

For the Shell & Tube Heat Exchanger specified in 0-QDS-7417, specific requirements are outlined for the inspection of coatings and linings. These inspections are vital to ensure the longevity, corrosion resistance, and overall integrity of the equipment, particularly for external surfaces.

**Coating/Lining Inspection (Item 6.0):**
This item covers the general requirements for inspecting any applied coatings or linings. The purpose of these inspections is to verify that the application process, material quality, and final finish meet the specified standards. This includes checking for proper surface preparation, film thickness, adhesion, and the absence of defects such as holidays, runs, sags, or blistering.

**External Coating/Lining Inspection (Item 7.0):**

*   **Quality Record Required:** FP ("Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package")
*   **Surveillance by Chemetics:** Complete

The external coating or lining of the Shell & Tube Heat Exchanger **must** undergo a thorough inspection. This inspection focuses on the protective layers applied to the exterior surfaces of the equipment, which are crucial for protecting against environmental corrosion and mechanical damage. The Vendor **must** provide documentation of this inspection, and it **shall** be included in the final Quality Assurance Documentation Package. Chemetics will perform a complete review of the inspection records to ensure that all external coating and lining specifications have been met.

This inspection is an integral part of the [[0-QDS-7417_final_inspections|final inspection process]] and contributes to the overall [[0-QDS-7417_vendor_quality_assurance_requirements|Vendor Quality Assurance Requirements]]. For definitions of quality record designations, refer to [[0-QDS-7417_quality_record_definitions]].
