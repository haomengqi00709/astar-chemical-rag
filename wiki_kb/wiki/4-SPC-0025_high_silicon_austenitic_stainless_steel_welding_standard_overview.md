# High Silicon Austenitic Stainless Steel Welding Standard Overview (4-SPC-0025)

This Engineering Standard, 4-SPC-0025, defines the workmanship and quality requirements for welded fabrications utilizing High Silicon Austenitic stainless steel. It applies to fabrications purchased by or on behalf of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, specifically when referenced on purchase orders and other related documents. The scope of this standard encompasses welding and other fabrication requirements for various items, including vessels, heat exchangers, tanks, piping, and miscellaneous welded components.

The only stainless steel covered by this Standard is VEW-Bohler A610. All referenced codes and standards **shall** be interpreted as the latest edition ruling at the date of contract. An "Agreement in Writing" is defined as a formal submission by letter, not merely an inclusion in approval drawings.

This document is a direct copy of the original specification ES44/W14, which was titled "General Requirements for High Silicon Austenitic Stainless Steel Equipment and Piping". Its usage **shall** be in accordance with [[0-prc-0001_standard_document_management_procedure]], titled "Approval and Use of Standards".
