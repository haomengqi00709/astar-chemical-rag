# Stack Field Inspection Sign-off Requirements (4-FIC-XX20-R0)

This page details the mandatory sign-off requirements for the [[4-FIC-XX20-R0_stack_field_inspection_checklist_overview|Stack Field Inspection Checklist (4-FIC-XX20-R0)]]. Proper documentation and authorization are essential for the completion of the inspection process.

**Mandatory Sign-off Parties:**
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **must** provide a name, signature, and date in the designated sign-off section.
*   A Sub Contractor **must** provide a name, signature, and date in the designated sign-off section.
*   The Client **must** provide a name, signature, and date in the designated sign-off section.

These sign-offs confirm the completion and verification of all [[4-FIC-XX20-R0_stack_field_inspection_items|inspection items]] and any noted deficiencies. For general sign-off procedures, refer to [[field_inspection_checklist_sign_off_requirements]].
