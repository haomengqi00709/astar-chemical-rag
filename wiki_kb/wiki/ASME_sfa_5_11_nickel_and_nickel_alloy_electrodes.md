# ASME SFA-5.11 Specification for Nickel and Nickel-Alloy Bare Welding Electrodes and Rods

ASME SFA-5.11 is a specification that establishes the requirements for nickel and nickel-alloy bare welding electrodes and rods. This standard covers the classification, chemical composition, mechanical properties, and other essential characteristics of these consumables used in various welding processes.

This specification is referenced in [[4-SPC-0014_referenced_codes_and_definitions]] and specifically in [[4-SPC-0014_welding_consumables]] for Inconel electrodes/filler wire. All Inconel electrodes/filler wire shall satisfy the requirements of ASME SFA-5.11.

For detailed information on nickel and nickel-alloy bare welding electrodes and rods, refer to the latest edition of ASME SFA-5.11.
