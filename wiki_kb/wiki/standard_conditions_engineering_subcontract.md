# Standard Conditions for Engineering Subcontract (0-SPC-2201)

The standard conditions governing Engineering Subcontracts are defined in 0-SPC-2201: Standard Conditions for Engineering Subcontract. This document is specifically structured in the form of the NEC Professional Services Contract. The NEC (New Engineering Contract) Professional Services Contract is a widely recognized and respected framework, published for the Institution of Civil Engineers by Thomas Telford Publishing, London. Its use provides a robust and standardized contractual basis, promoting good project management and clear communication.

**Copyright and Procurement Requirements:**
The NEC Professional Services Contract document is subject to copyright. To ensure compliance with these copyright requirements, a physical copy of the NEC Professional Services Contract document **must** be purchased through the company library on each occasion that an Engineering Subcontract is issued. This ensures that the firm adheres to intellectual property rights while leveraging a proven contractual standard.

For ease of reference and internal use, an electronic copy of the NEC Professional Services Contract document has been created. However, this electronic copy does not negate the requirement to purchase a physical copy for each new subcontract to satisfy copyright obligations.

**Audit and Record Keeping:**
Upon the award of an Engineering Subcontract, the Subcontract Administrator **must** advise the library of the award. The library, in turn, will record all purchased copies of the NEC Professional Services Contract document against the relevant project number. This meticulous record-keeping is essential for audit purposes, demonstrating compliance with both contractual and copyright requirements.

Adherence to these standard conditions, particularly the proper procurement and use of the NEC Professional Services Contract, is a mandatory step in the [[issue_of_engineering_subcontract_0_prc_2206]] process, ensuring legal soundness and operational consistency.
