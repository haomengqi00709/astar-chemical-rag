# Detailed Technical Bid Evaluation (Orders > CAD 200k)

For orders valued greater than CAD 200,000, a detailed technical evaluation of the short-listed bids **must** be conducted by the Requisitioning Discipline. This evaluation is formally documented in the [[technical_bid_tabulation_template]] (0-TAB-5XXX-2).

**Multi-Discipline Involvement:**
As directed by the Project Manager, the Requisitioning Discipline **must** solicit technical evaluations from other relevant disciplines. Copies of the [[preliminary_cost_and_technical_review|Cost Tabulation]] and the short-listed Vendors’ bids **must** be provided to these participating disciplines.

The technical evaluation process **should** be initiated with a meeting involving all participants. This meeting ensures that no work is duplicated and that all critical evaluation criteria are adequately covered.

**Evaluation Criteria:**
The evaluation **must** consist of a thorough comparison of the technical information provided by each Vendor against the requirements specified in the Scope of Supply. Comparisons **must** be made across as many characteristics as necessary to clearly indicate whether the Goods and Services offered comply with the requirements. Any superior characteristics of a Vendor’s offer **must** be highlighted.

Where appropriate, the evaluation **must** also include:
*   A comparison of the process performance of the Goods and Services offered (e.g., throughput, efficiency, power consumption, other utility consumption).
*   An assessment of the impact on any performance guarantees held by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
*   A summary of observations or evaluations based on previous experience with the Vendors’ Goods and Services.

**Vendor References and Clarifications:**
As directed by the Project Manager, Vendor references **will** be contacted. The evaluation **must** include a summary of the information received from these references.

If clarification of a Vendor’s bid is necessary, the Requisitioning Discipline **must** direct the query to the Vendor. This query **must** be copied to all other technical evaluators and to Procurement.

**Final Technical Bid Tabulation:**
The Requisitioning Discipline **must** prepare the final Technical Bid Tabulation. This document will be a compilation of the evaluations from all participating disciplines and **must** include each discipline’s ranking of the Vendors evaluated.

The Requisitioning Discipline **must** publish the Technical Bid Tabulation using [[publishing_project_documents]] (0-PRC-0005). All other discipline evaluators **must** check the tabulation, and the Requisitioning Discipline Approver **must** approve it.
