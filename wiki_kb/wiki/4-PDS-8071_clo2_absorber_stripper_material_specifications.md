# ClO2 Absorber-Stripper Material Specifications (4-PDS-8071)

This page details the material specifications for the ClO2 Absorber-Stripper (Tag No. 8071U), including column materials, insulation, and packing support.

**Tower Column Materials**
*   **Stripping Section:** FRP (Note 13)
*   **Absorption Section:** FRP (Note 13)

**Tower Column Insulation**
*   **Stripping Section:** CC (EPDM foam Aeroflex)
*   **Absorption Section:** CC (EPDM foam Aeroflex)

**Packing Support Materials**
*   **Stripping Section:** Ti Gr. 2
*   **Absorption Section:** Ti Gr. 2

For general guidelines on material selection, refer to [[material_selection]]. Details on insulation design and specifications can be found in [[insulation_design_and_specification_6_prc_0002]].
