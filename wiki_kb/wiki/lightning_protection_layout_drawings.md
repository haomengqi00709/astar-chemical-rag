# Lightning Protection Layout Drawings

Lightning Protection Layout drawings illustrate the placement of lightning electrodes (air terminals) and grounding conductors on buildings and other tall structures within the plant. These drawings are essential for designing an effective lightning protection system.

**Content of Lightning Protection Layout Drawings**
Lightning protection layout drawings **shall** include:
*   Locations of the lightning electrodes (air terminals) on the buildings.
*   Locations of the lightning electrodes (air terminals) on other tall structures within the project plant.
*   Locations of the lightning grounding conductors.
*   Locations of the lightning electrodes.

The appropriate lightning codes for this project **must** be used. Any required inter-connections with the overall plant grounding system **must** be included. Any [[lightning_protection_installation_details]] showing installation details **must** be referenced.

On some projects, it may be possible to combine the lightning protection layout and details on the overall [[grounding_general_arrangement_drawings]].
