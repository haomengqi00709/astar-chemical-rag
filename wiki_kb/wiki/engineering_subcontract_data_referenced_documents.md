# Engineering Subcontract Data and Referenced Documents

The [[engineering_subcontract_agreement_data_sections|Subcontract Data]] is a crucial section of the [[engineering_subcontract_procedure_overview|Subcontract Agreement]]. It serves as the primary reference point for various standard and project-specific documents that collectively define the terms, conditions, and technical requirements of the engineering subcontract.

**Referenced Specifications:**
The [[engineering_subcontract_agreement_data_sections|Subcontract Data]] will specifically refer to 0-SPC-22XX: [Major Work Division] Engineering. These specifications are prepared uniquely for each Subcontract to detail the engineering requirements pertinent to a specific [[major_work_division_codes|Major Work Division]].

**Standard Documents:**
Additionally, the [[engineering_subcontract_agreement_data_sections|Subcontract Data]] will refer to the following standard documents:
*   [[0-spc-0002_engineering_subcontractor_document_supply|0-SPC-0002: Engineering Subcontractor Document Supply]]. Data available in SQL: 0-SPC-0002
*   [[0-spc-7004_engineering_subcontractor_quality_assurance|0-SPC-7004: Engineering Subcontractor Quality Assurance]]. Data available in SQL: 0-SPC-7004
*   [[0-spc-2204_engineering_subcontractor_coordination|0-SPC-2204: Engineering Subcontractor Coordination]]. Data available in SQL: 0-SPC-2204

**Usage and Approval of Standards:**
These standard documents (0-SPC-0002, 0-SPC-7004, 0-SPC-2204) will be utilized either as-is or modified as necessary to suit the specific requirements of the project. The approval and use of all standards are governed by [[standard_document_management_procedure_0_prc_0001|0-PRC-0001]].

The Subcontract Agreement itself is defined in [[0-prc-2202_form_of_engineering_subcontract|0-PRC-2202: Form of Engineering Subcontract]].

The Subcontract Data will specifically refer to the following documents:
*   [[0-spc-22xx_major_work_division_engineering|0-SPC-22XX: [Major Work Division] Engineering]]
*   [[0-spc-0002_engineering_subcontractor_document_supply|0-SPC-0002: Engineering Subcontractor Document Supply]]
*   [[0-spc-7004_engineering_subcontractor_quality_assurance|0-SPC-7004: Engineering Subcontractor Quality Assurance]]
*   [[0-spc-2204_engineering_subcontractor_coordination|0-SPC-2204: Engineering Subcontractor Coordination]]

These standard documents (0-SPC-0002, 0-SPC-7004, 0-SPC-2204) are to be used either as-is or modified for the project as necessary. The approval and use of standards is governed by [[0-prc-0001_approval_and_use_of_standards|0-PRC-0001]].
