# Major Work Division Engineering Specifications

Engineering specifications are developed for each [[major_work_division_codes|Major Work Division]] to provide detailed engineering requirements for a given subcontract. The preparation of these specifications is the responsibility of the designated [[discipline_lead_and_approver_roles|Discipline Lead]].

**Template and Numbering:**
Specifications **shall** utilize the template 0-SPC-22XX: [Template] [Major Work Division] Engineering as their foundational document. Data available in SQL: 0-SPC-22XX.

Each specification will be assigned an identification number in the format 8YXX-Y.
*   'Y' represents the [[document_numbering_rules|Major Work Division identifier]], as defined in [[document_numbering_rules|0-PRC-0002: Document Numbering]].
*   'XX' is identical to the sequential series used for the [[engineering_subcontract_procedure_overview|Engineering Subcontract Agreement]], as specified in [[engineering_subcontract_procedure_overview|0-PRC-2202]].
The overall [[document_numbering_rules|document numbering]] system is governed by [[document_numbering_rules|0-PRC-0002]].

**Publication and Approval Process:**
*   Specifications will be published with the status “Approved for Design”.
*   Specifications will be checked by the [[project_engineer_pem_gate_review_responsibilities|Project Engineer]].
*   Specifications will be approved by the [[discipline_lead_and_approver_roles|Discipline Chief Engineer]].

**Referenced Checklists:**
The 0-SPC-22XX: [Major Work Division] Engineering specifications will directly reference [[engineering_scope_checklists_0_lst_22xx|0-LST-22XX: Engineering Scope Checklists]].

**Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Documents:**
Documents prepared by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, including all design criteria documents, are an integral part of these specifications.

These specifications (0-SPC-22XX) are prepared specifically for each Subcontract.

Specifications are prepared by the responsible Discipline Lead and will use [[0-spc-22xx_template_major_work_division_engineering|0-SPC-22XX: [Template] [Major Work Division] Engineering]] as a template.

**Document Numbering:** Specifications are numbered with an identification number of 8YXX-Y. 'Y' represents the Major Work Division identifier, and 'XX' is identical to the sequential series used for the Engineering Subcontract Agreement (as defined in [[0-prc-2202_form_of_engineering_subcontract|0-PRC-2202]]). Document numbering in general is governed by [[0-prc-0002_document_numbering|0-PRC-0002]].

**Publication and Approval:** Specifications will be published with the status “Approved for Design”. They will be checked by the Project Engineer and approved by the Discipline Chief Engineer.

**Related Documents:** 0-SPC-22XX: [Major Work Division] Engineering will directly reference [[engineering_scope_checklists_0_lst_22xx|0-LST-22XX: Engineering Scope Checklists]].
