# Review of Vendor Documentation (0-PRC-0007)

This document, 0-PRC-0007, outlines the procedure for reviewing vendor documentation to ensure compliance with Purchase Orders and project requirements.

The Project Engineer assigns responsibility for the review of vendor documentation. This review process is critical for verifying that all supplied documents, including drawings, data sheets, and manuals, meet the technical and quality specifications of the Project. Logging and control of vendor documentation are carried out in accordance with [[0-prc-0006_control_of_project_documents]].

This procedure also applies to the review of subcontractor documentation for compliance with their respective Subcontracts.
