# Gas/Gas Heat Exchanger Maximum Metal Temperatures

This page lists the maximum metal temperatures for critical components of the internal gas/gas heat exchanger, as determined in [[4-CAL-0008_document_details|4-CAL-0008]]. These temperatures are crucial inputs for establishing the [[gas_gas_heat_exchanger_design_temperature_calculations|design temperatures]] of the equipment.

**Component Maximum Metal Temperatures**
*   Maximum metal temperature for Top tubesheet: 330.65 C
*   Maximum metal temperature for Top expansion plate: 330.65 C
*   Maximum metal temperature for Shell: 608.1 C
*   Maximum metal temperature for Bottom seal plate: 514.05 C
*   Maximum metal temperature for Bottom tubesheet: 514.05 C
*   Maximum metal temperature for Tubes: 514.05 C
*   Maximum metal temperature for Bypass seal plate: 384.1 C

These maximum metal temperatures are derived from the [[gas_gas_heat_exchanger_operating_temperatures|operating temperatures]] and are used to ensure the structural integrity and safe operation of the heat exchanger. For details on the calculation methodology and assumptions, refer to the [[gas_gas_heat_exchanger_design_temperature_calculation_commentary|Design Temperature Calculation Commentary]].
