# Coating and Lining Inspection (0-QDS-7415)

This section details the coating and lining inspection requirements for the Dual Laminate Tank project, as specified in 0-QDS-7415. Coating/Lining Inspection is a critical item or activity to ensure protective layers meet specifications.

**External Coating/Lining Inspection:**
*   **Quality Record:** Inspection reports for external coating/lining are mandatory quality records and **must** have a Quality Record status of FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package).
*   **Surveillance:** Surveillance by Chemetics for external coating/lining inspection **must** involve both "Witness" and "Hold" points.
    *   **Witness:** Chemetics personnel **must** be present to observe the external coating/lining inspection.
    *   **Hold:** Work **must** not proceed beyond this point until Chemetics has reviewed and approved the witnessed inspection.

**Internal Coating/Lining Inspection:**
*   **Quality Record:** Inspection reports for internal coating/lining are mandatory quality records and **must** have a Quality Record status of FP.
*   **Surveillance:** Surveillance by Chemetics for internal coating/lining inspection **must** involve both "Witness" and "Hold" points.
    *   **Witness:** Chemetics personnel **must** be present to observe the internal coating/lining inspection.
    *   **Hold:** Work **must** not proceed beyond this point until Chemetics has reviewed and approved the witnessed inspection.

These inspections are vital for the corrosion resistance and overall durability of the dual laminate tank. For general quality record definitions, refer to [[0-QDS-7415_quality_record_definitions]].
