# Structural and Buildings Document Structure (Major Work Subject 3)

The "3 Structural and Buildings" major work subject encompasses all documentation related to the structural design of the plant and any associated buildings. This includes calculations, detailed drawings, specifications, and vendor information for structural components.

The sub-folders and their contents within the 3-Structural-and-Buildings folder are:

*   **CAL:** Structural Calculations. Engineering calculations for all structural elements and building components.
*   **DTL:** Structure Details. Detailed drawings and specifications for structural connections, members, and assemblies.
*   **GAD:** Structure General Arrangements. Overall layout drawings showing the arrangement of structural elements and buildings.
*   **PRC:** Project Specific Procedures. Procedures tailored to the structural design and construction requirements of the project.
*   **REQ:** Requisitions. Documents for requesting bids or proposals for structural materials and services.
*   **SPC:** Specifications. Technical specifications for structural materials, fabrication, and erection.
*   **TAB:** Bid Tabulations. Records of bids received for structural components and services.
*   **VDR:** Vendor Document Requirements. Documents outlining the requirements for vendor-supplied structural documentation.
*   **VEN:** Vendors Steel Shop Drawings. Detailed fabrication drawings provided by steel vendors.

This structure ensures that all structural and building-related documents, from design calculations to vendor shop drawings, are systematically organized and easily retrievable. For an overview of the entire project documentation structure, refer to [[document_control_1_prc_0003]]. The definitions of major work subjects are outlined in [[major_work_division_codes]].
