# Zirconium Post Weld Heat Treatment (4-SPC-0024)

This page outlines the mandatory requirements for Post Weld Heat Treatment (PWHT) of Zirconium (Grade 60702) welding, as specified in document **4-SPC-0024**.

**General Requirements:**
*   Where specified, Zirconium welding **must** be post weld heat treated in a neutral or oxidizing atmosphere.
*   The fabricator **must** submit their PWHT procedure in writing for approval prior to the commencement of work.
*   The procedure **must** include information on the number and location of thermocouples and supports.
*   Furnace recorder charts **must** be supplied as part of the documentation.

**Pre-Heat Treatment Cleaning and Handling:**
*   Prior to heat treatment, all Zirconium components **must** be degreased.
*   Chlorinated solvents are **not allowed** for degreasing.
*   Once degreased, Zr components **must not** be handled or otherwise contaminated with bare hands.
*   Clean gloves or stainless steel tongs **must** be used for handling degreased Zr components.

**Furnace Heat Treatment Parameters:**
*   Where furnace heat-treatment is used, article(s) **must** be loaded into the furnace at a temperature below 315°C (600°F).
*   Heating and cooling rates **must not** exceed 315°C/hour between 315°C and 482°C (600°F and 900°F).
*   Heating and cooling rates **must not** exceed 93°C (200°F)/hour above 482°C (900°F) up to 500°C (1050°F).

**Specific Heat Treatment Cycles:**

**1. Stress Relief Annealing:**
*   Soak at 550°C (1050°F) + 10°C.
*   Soak time is 1 hour/25 mm thickness, with a minimum of 30 minutes.
*   This is followed by furnace cooling to 315°C (600°F).

**2. Recrystallization (after heavy cold work):**
*   Where recrystallization is required (e.g., after heavy cold work), Zirconium **must** be heated to 790°C (1450°F).
*   Soak time is 1 hour/25 mm thickness, with a minimum of 30 minutes.
*   This is followed by furnace cooling to 315°C (600°F).
