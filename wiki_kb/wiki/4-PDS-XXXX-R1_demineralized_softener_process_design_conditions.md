# Demineralized-Softener Process Design Conditions

This page details the standard process design conditions for demineralized and softener systems, as specified in Document ID 4-PDS-XXXX-R1. These parameters define the operational environment and performance expectations for the equipment, forming a critical part of the [[process_design_criteria_document|overall process design criteria]].

**Process Design Conditions**
The primary design basis for this template is designated as **Case Description: I (Design Basis)**. The following parameters are used to define the operational envelope:

*   **Normal Flow (resin):** Specifies the typical operating flow rate through the resin beds, measured in cubic meters per hour (m3/h). This value represents the steady-state flow under normal operating conditions.
*   **Design Flowrate (hydraulic):** Indicates the maximum hydraulic flow rate the system is designed to handle, also in cubic meters per hour (m3/h). This accounts for potential peak demands or upset conditions.
*   **Density:** The density of the process fluid, expressed in kilograms per cubic meter (kg/m3). This is crucial for hydraulic calculations and material balance.
*   **Design Temperature:** The maximum temperature the equipment is designed to withstand, measured in degrees Celsius (oC). This is a critical parameter for material selection and mechanical design.
*   **Normal Temperature:** The typical operating temperature of the process fluid, in degrees Celsius (oC).
*   **Pressure:** The operating pressure of the system, specified in kilopascals gauge (kPa(g)). This is essential for vessel design and pump sizing.
*   **pH:** The pH value of the process fluid, a dimensionless parameter indicating its acidity or alkalinity. This influences resin performance and material corrosion.
*   **Viscosity:** The viscosity of the process fluid, measured in centipoise (cP). This affects fluid flow characteristics and pressure drop calculations.

These process design conditions are fundamental for the accurate sizing, selection, and safe operation of demineralized and softener equipment. They ensure that the system can perform effectively under both normal and specified upset conditions.
