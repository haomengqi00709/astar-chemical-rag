# Security and Payment Guarantees (0-SPC-6001)

This page outlines the security and payment guarantee requirements for the Subcontractor under construction subcontract 0-SPC-6001.

**Security Requirements**
The Subcontractor **shall** provide Aker Kvaerner Chemetics with security. This security **must** be:
*   Provided by a financial or insurance institution accepted by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
*   For the amount stated in the [[standard_conditions_for_construction_subcontract_0_spc_6001#Subcontract Data|Subcontract Data]].
*   In a form acceptable to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
*   Given within 2 weeks of the starting date.

No payment will be made to the Subcontractor until this security has been provided. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics has recourse to the security if a payment to which it is entitled remains unpaid for a period of five days, provided Chemetics has notified the Subcontractor of its intent to have recourse.

**Evidence of Payment and Lien Releases**
The Subcontractor **shall** provide Aker Kvaerner Chemetics with satisfactory evidence that all amounts owing to any person related to the works have been paid.

The Subcontractor **shall** also provide Aker Kvaerner Chemetics with a full and complete release from any person who has filed or registered any notice of lien, security, security interest or charge, UCC or PPSA, or its equivalent in any jurisdiction, or who has provided labour or materials in relation to the works.

If the Subcontractor fails to provide this evidence or these releases, Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may assess an amount to be retained from the Subcontractor as security against any claims by persons who have provided labour or materials for the works.
