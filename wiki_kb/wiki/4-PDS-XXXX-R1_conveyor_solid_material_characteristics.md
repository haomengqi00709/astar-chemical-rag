# Conveyor Solid Material Characteristics (4-PDS-XXXX-R1)

This page outlines the critical physical and chemical characteristics of the solid material to be conveyed, as required by datasheet 4-PDS-XXXX-R1. Accurate characterization is essential for selecting the appropriate conveyor type and ensuring efficient, reliable operation.

**Physical Properties:**
*   **Static Angle of Repose:** Measured in degrees.
*   **Bulk Density:**
    *   **Packed:** kg/m3
    *   **Loose:** kg/m3
    *   **Agitated:** kg/m3
*   **Max Lump Size:** Measured in mm.
*   **Irregular Particle Types:** Examples include fibrous, stringy, cylindrical, slabs, etc.

**Particle Size Distribution:**
The percentage (%) of particles falling within specified size ranges **must** be provided:
*   **Very Fine:** < 0.07mm (%w/w)
*   **Fine:**
    *   0.15mm to 0.07mm (%w/w)
    *   0.4mm to 0.15mm (%w/w)
    *   3.3mm to 0.4mm (%w/w)
*   **Granular:**
    *   13mm to 3.3mm (%w/w)
    *   75mm to 13mm (%w/w)
    *   178mm to 75mm (%w/w)
*   **Lumpy:**
    *   <400mm (%w/w)
    *   >400mm (%w/w)

**Flowability Options:**
The material's flow characteristics **must** be selected from the following options:
*   Very Free Flowing
*   Free Flowing
*   Average flowability
*   Sluggish

**Abrasiveness Options:**
The material's abrasiveness **must** be selected from the following options:
*   Mildly abrasive
*   Moderately abrasive
*   Extremely abrasive

**Other Characteristics:**
Any additional relevant characteristics **must** be identified from the following options or specified in the "Other Characteristics" field:
*   Aerates & Becomes Fluid
*   Becomes Plastic
*   Builds Up & Hardens
*   Contaminable, Affecting Use
*   Decomposes - Deteriorates In Storage
*   Degradable, Affecting Use
*   Elevated Temperature
*   May Contain Explosive Dust
*   Flammable
*   Generates Static Electricity
*   Gives Off Harmful/Toxic Fumes/Gas/Dust
*   Highly Corrosive
*   Hygroscopic
*   Interlocks, Mats Or Agglomerates
*   Mildly Corrosive
*   Oils Present
*   Packs Under Pressure
*   Stickiness
*   Very Dusty
*   Very Light And Fluffy
*   **Other Characteristics field:** (This field **must** be filled if none of the above apply or for additional details).

These characteristics directly influence the selection of conveyor type, material of construction, and operational parameters. For general process design conditions, refer to [[4-PDS-XXXX-R1_conveyor_process_design_conditions]].
