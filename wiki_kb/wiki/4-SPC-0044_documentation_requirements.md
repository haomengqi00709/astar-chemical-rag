# Glass-Lined Vessel Documentation Requirements (4-SPC-0044)

This section outlines the mandatory documentation requirements for glass-lined carbon steel reaction vessels, including submission of drawings, calculations, quality assurance (QA) packages, and inspection records, as specified in 4-SPC-0044.

**Design and Fabrication Submissions**
Vessel type, Engineering Standard, and designation **shall** be provided for each vessel by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. The Fabricator **shall** submit two sepias of detailed approval drawings and one copy of design calculations within three weeks of the receipt of an order. These submissions **shall** be in accordance with the Purchaser's order, 4-SPC-0044, and any standards or drawings the Purchaser may supply as references. Manufacture **shall not** commence until the Purchaser has approved, in writing, the Fabricator’s drawings. The Fabricator **shall** allow two weeks for Purchaser's approval per the vendor data requirements schedule. The Vessel Fabricator **shall** furnish design calculations for supports if agreed previously between Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Supplier.

**Nameplate Requirements**
A stainless steel nameplate attached to each vessel by a bracket projecting beyond any insulation **shall** be furnished by the Manufacturer. The nameplate **shall** be located immediately below the vessel manhole. The nameplate **shall** show the item number, service, and minimum data required by [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels|ASME Code]] and by the appropriate Provincial Department of Labour in Canada or National Board in the US. An Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' supplied nameplate, as shown in Appendix B, **shall** be attached to the same bracket.

**Quality Assurance (QA) Documentation Package**
The Supplier **shall** prepare three sets of QA documentation for each vessel. These packages **shall** consist of:
*   Mill Test Certification for all materials.
*   Calculations (e.g., design calculations for supports).
*   Welding Procedures.
*   Affidavit of Manufacture.
*   Report of major repairs prior to glassing.
*   Spark Testing Report identifying plug locations.
*   Dimensional Report, including glass surface inspection and thickness.
*   Inspection/Shipping Release Form.

The Aker Kvaerner Chemetics inspector or authorized representative **shall** review each QA package for completeness and accuracy prior to acceptance. Test certificates **shall** be supplied to the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ Inspector.

**Glass-Lining Inspection Records**
The Purchaser's Inspector **shall** ascertain from the Fabricator the grade of glass used and record this information on the Glass-lining Inspection Record (Appendix A to this Standard). The thickness of the lining **shall** be recorded in Appendix "A". The Fabricator **shall**, on request, provide the Purchaser with test data for the grade of glass used. This test data **shall** include resistance against impact, hot caustic soda solutions, boiling hydrochloric acid, boiling water/steam, sudden temperature changes or permissible temperature differentials, and Sulphuric and nitric acid mixtures at boiling temperatures.

The Supplier **shall** indicate the positions of all plugs to the satisfaction of the Inspector or Engineer and provide a sketch showing the location, number, and size of plugs used. The number of plugs **shall** be recorded on the Glass-lining Inspection Record (Appendix A). The result of the Visual Examination **shall** be entered on the Glass-lining Inspection Record (Appendix A). The thickness range measured **shall** be recorded, and locations of any areas outside the limits **shall** be specified on the Glass-lining Inspection Record (Appendix A).

The Glass Lining Inspection Record (Appendix A) is to be completed by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' Inspector and used in conjunction with Engineering Standard ES44\F-04. It includes sections for General, Glass Grade, Accessories Glass Grade, High Frequency Spark Testing (Production Shop Test, Test at Supplier's Works), Statiflux Test, Pre-Shipment Inspection, Site Inspection, and Commentary.

The result of the jobsite spark test **shall** be recorded on the Receiving Report. If the jobsite spark test is not carried out, the Receiving Report **shall** be endorsed to this effect.

**Design and Fabrication Submissions**
*   Fabricator’s proposal shall contain the type of glass-lining proposed and the minimum thickness of vessel lining.
*   Fabricator shall submit two sepias of detailed approval drawings and one copy of design calculations within three weeks of the receipt of an order.
*   Submissions shall be in accordance with the Purchaser's order, 4-SPC-0044, and any standards or drawings Purchaser may supply as references.
*   Manufacture shall not commence until the Purchaser has approved, in writing, the Fabricator’s drawings. Fabricator shall allow two weeks for Purchaser's approval per vendor data requirements schedule.
*   When internal baffles are required, Fabricator shall provide information on the number, width and materials required. Baffle information shall be shown on the drawing or data sheet for each vessel. The method of attachment for baffles shall be specified in the quotation.
*   Suitable materials for internal supports shall be shown on the data sheets or drawings.
*   The Vessel Fabricator shall furnish design calculations for supports if agreed previously between Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and Supplier.
*   Vessel fabricator shall provide insulation support details as called for on the vessel drawings.
*   Supplier's latest fabrication tolerance specifications document shall be submitted with the quotation.

**QA Documentation**
*   The Fabricator shall, on request, provide the Purchaser with test data for the grade of glass used. Test data shall include resistance against Impact, hot caustic soda solutions, boiling hydrochloric acid, boiling water/steam and sudden temperature changes or permissible temperature differentials, as well as resistance against Sulphuric and nitric acid mixtures at boiling temperatures.
*   Supplier shall also provide a sketch showing location, number and size of plugs used. The number of plugs shall be recorded on the Glass-lining Inspection Record shown as Appendix A.
*   Test certificates shall be supplied to the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ Inspector.
*   The result of the Visual Examination shall be entered on the Glass-lining Inspection Record (Appendix A).
