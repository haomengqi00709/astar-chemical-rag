# Project Variance Change Notice Procedure

This procedure, identified by Document ID **0-PRC-4003-R1** (Appendix I), outlines the formal process for managing project variances and changes through the use of the Project Variance/Change Notice (PVN/PCN) Form. It is a critical component of the firm's overall [[change_management_roles_and_responsibilities_0_prc_4003]] framework, ensuring that all deviations from the approved project baseline are formally documented, thoroughly evaluated, and appropriately approved before implementation.

The procedure mandates a structured approach to identify the source and basis of any change, assess its comprehensive impact on the project, and secure necessary approvals. This systematic process helps to mitigate risks and maintain control over project scope, schedule, and cost. Key steps include the initiation of the PVN/PCN form, its internal review and approval by relevant project personnel, and subsequent client authorization.

The procedure emphasizes clear communication and distribution of the PVN/PCN to all affected project disciplines, ensuring that all stakeholders are informed and can address the implications of the change. This proactive management of changes is essential for successful project execution and adherence to contractual obligations.

For detailed information on the structure and specific fields of the form utilized in this procedure, refer to [[pvn_pcn_form_initiation_and_details]]. Further details on the broader program objectives and requirements for PVN/PCNs can be found in [[pvn_pcn_program_objectives_and_requirements]], and the process for managing revisions is detailed in [[pvn_pcn_revision_process]]. This procedure also integrates with the firm's [[project_quality_audits]] to ensure compliance and continuous improvement in change management practices.
