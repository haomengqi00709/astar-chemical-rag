# Civil Works Design and Specification (2-PRC-0002)

This document, 2-PRC-0002, details the discipline assignments for the preparation, checking, and approval of civil works design and specifications for the Project. It outlines the roles and responsibilities for developing all civil engineering documentation.

This procedure ensures that all civil design documents, including drawings, calculations, and specifications, are developed, reviewed, and approved in a structured manner, adhering to the [[civil_works_design_criteria_2_prc_0001]] and overall project quality standards.
