# Tower Internals Process Design Conditions

This page details the process design conditions and associated parameters for [[4-DST-XXXX-R0_tower_internals_datasheet_administration|Tower Internals Datasheet (4-DST-XXXX-R0)]]. These conditions are crucial for the design and operation of tower internals, including packing, distributors, and other components.

**General Process Parameters:**
The process design conditions must include comprehensive [[stream_data_calculation_and_integration|Stream data]]. The "Phase" of the fluid can be either vapour or liquid, which influences the design calculations.

**Mandatory Fluid Description:**
*   **Fluid Description**: This is a mandatory field and must accurately describe the process fluid.

**Key Process Variables and Units:**
The following process variables are specified with their respective units:
*   **Flow Rate**: kg/h
*   **Minimum Flow Rate**: kg/h
*   **Temperature**: oC
*   **Density**: kg/m3
*   **Viscosity**: cP
*   **Surface Tension**: mN/m
*   **Composition**: %w/w

**Operational Tendencies:**
*   **Fouling Tendency**: This is a mandatory field, indicating the propensity of the fluid to cause fouling within the tower internals.
*   **Foaming Tendency**: This is a mandatory field, describing the fluid's tendency to foam, which can impact separation efficiency.

**Design Temperature:**
*   **Design Maximum Temperature**: oC. This specifies the maximum temperature the equipment is designed to withstand under operating conditions.

These process conditions directly influence the selection and performance of [[tower_internals_packing_specifications|packing]] and [[tower_internals_support_and_ancillary_components|support and ancillary components]].
