# Subcontractor Audit and Non-Conformance Management

To ensure the quality of services provided by Engineering Subcontractors, regular audits are conducted. These audits are a critical component of the overall quality assurance framework.

**Audit Process**
Services provided by the Subcontractor **will** be audited through a review of the documentation prepared by the Subcontractor. This documentation **must** be properly checked and approved in accordance with the [[subcontractor_quality_plan]].

*   **Audit Execution:** Audits **will** be carried out by the Discipline Lead responsible for the work. The scope of these audits is to the extent necessary to assure the accuracy of the services and their compliance with the requirements of the Subcontract.
*   **Scheduling:** Audits **will** be scheduled to provide early warning to the Subcontractor regarding the necessity for corrective or preventive action.
*   **Document Control for Audits:** Documents submitted by the Subcontractor for audit **will** be logged and published in accordance with [[0-PRC-0006_control_of_project_documents]].

**Defect and Non-Conformance Handling**
*   **Identification of Defects:** If defects are found in the services, they **will** be marked up on Adobe Portable Document Format (PDF) documents within the project electronic file. Copies of these marked-up documents **will** be returned to the Subcontractor.
*   **No Defects Noted:** If no defects are found during an audit, the Adobe PDF documents **will** be marked as "Audited: No Defects Noted." A digital signature **will** be attached by the Discipline Lead to these documents, and copies **will** be returned to the Subcontractor.

**Corrective and Preventive Actions**
Defects in services or other non-conformances may be identified either through audits or the Subcontractor's own internal reviews.

*   **Corrective Action:** The Discipline Lead responsible for the work **will ensure** that the Subcontractor undertakes corrective action as required by the [[subcontractor_quality_plan]]. Additional audits **will** be conducted as deemed necessary by the Discipline Lead to verify the effectiveness of these actions.
*   **Preventive Action:** The Discipline Lead **will also ensure** that the Subcontractor proposes appropriate actions to prevent the recurrence of any identified non-conformance. This proposal **is subject** to approval as required by the Subcontract Quality Plan.
*   **Plan Modifications:** Required modifications to the Subcontract Quality Plan or its referenced documents **are subject** to checking and approval. Further auditing **will** be conducted as necessary to evaluate the effectiveness of the modified Subcontract Quality Plan and its referenced documents. For formal non-conformance reporting, refer to [[0-NCR-7XXX_template_non_conformance_report]].
