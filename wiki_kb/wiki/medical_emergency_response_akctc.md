# Medical Emergency Response - AKCTC

This page details the procedures for responding to medical emergencies at the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Technology Centre (AKCTC), including general first aid and specific laboratory incidents.

**Initial Response:**
The initial responsibility for aid rests with the first person at the accident scene. This individual **shall** act quickly but in a calm and reassuring manner.

**Summoning Help:**
First Aid and/or outside medical help **shall** be summoned immediately. For outside help, call 9-1-1 and ask for an ambulance. Arrange for someone to meet and direct outside help to the scene.

**Handling Injured Persons:**
An injured person should not be moved, except when necessary to prevent further injury. Attending person(s) should be aware of, or look for, emergency medical identification around the victim’s neck, wrist, or in their wallet.

**Anticipated Laboratory Medical Emergencies:**
Personnel should be prepared to respond to the following types of laboratory-specific medical emergencies:
*   Thermal and chemical burns.
*   Cuts and puncture wounds from glass or metal, including possible chemical contamination.
*   Skin irritation by chemicals.
*   Poisoning by ingestion, inhalation, or skin absorption.
*   Asphyxiation (chemical or electrical).
*   Injuries to eyes from splashed chemicals.

**Chemical Exposure Treatment:**
In case of chemical exposure, procedures for treatment of specific medical emergencies can be found under the Emergency and First Aid section in the Material Safety Data Sheet (MSDS) of the chemical in question. If not enough information is available, call the Emergency telephone number located on the MSDS.

**Other Medical Emergencies Requiring Prompt First Aid:**
*   Treatment for Electrical Shock.
*   Cardiac Arrest.
*   Stoppage of Breathing.
*   Heavy Bleeding.
*   Shock (which often accompanies severe injury).

For general first aid and injury reporting requirements, refer to [[first_aid_and_injury_reporting_akctc]]. For an overview of emergency response at AKCTC, refer to [[0-PRC-8010_office_emergency_response_overview]].
