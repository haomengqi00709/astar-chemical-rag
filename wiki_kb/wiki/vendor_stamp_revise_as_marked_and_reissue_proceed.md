# Vendor Stamp: Revise as Marked and Reissue (Proceed)

The "REVISE AS MARKED AND REISSUE" vendor stamp, specifically the version presented on Page 2 of the vendor stamp procedure document, indicates that revisions are required, but the vendor may proceed with the work shown, incorporating the marked changes.

**Mandatory Requirements for Application:**
*   The phrase "PROCEED WITH WORK SHOWN" **must** be clearly indicated.
*   A signature **shall** be applied to the stamp.
*   The date of application **shall** be recorded on the stamp.

This stamp allows for continued progress on the project while ensuring that necessary modifications are addressed in subsequent document revisions. This stamp is associated with [[chemetics_aker_kvaerner_company_details|CHEMETICS]] documentation standards.

For an overview of all vendor stamp options, refer to [[vendor_stamp_procedure_overview]]. For the "REVISE AS MARKED AND REISSUE" stamp that prohibits work, see [[vendor_stamp_revise_as_marked_and_reissue_do_not_proceed]].

**Mandatory Requirements for Application:**
*   The phrase "PROCEED WITH WORK SHOWN" must be included.
*   A signature is required.
*   A date is required.
This stamp is specifically used for Chemetics projects.
