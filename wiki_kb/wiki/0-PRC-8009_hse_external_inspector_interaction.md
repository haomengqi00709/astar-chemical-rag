# HSE External Inspector Interaction Guidelines

This page provides guidelines for interacting with external inspectors, particularly those from Health and Safety Boards, during audits and inspections, as specified in Document ID: 0-PRC-8009.

**General Conduct**
When audits are conducted by representatives of Health and Safety Boards, specific guidelines **ARE RECOMMENDED**. Full cooperation and courtesy **SHALL** be extended to these inspectors at all times.

**Escort Requirements**
The inspector **SHALL** be escorted at all times by the HSE Manager or the senior site manager. The escort **SHOULD NOT** restrict the inspector's movements at the worksite, ensuring they have full access to areas relevant to their inspection.

**Answering Questions**
Questions regarding safety matters of interest to the inspector **SHALL** be answered factually and as directly as possible. It is important to provide only the requested information without offering any additional, unsolicited details.

**Recording Devices and Notes**
*   If the inspector utilizes recording devices (e.g., audio recorders, cameras), the Company **SHOULD** also use similar recording devices to document the proceedings.
*   The Company escort **SHOULD** also take personal notes about discussion items throughout the inspection. These notes can serve as a valuable record for internal review.

**Post-Inspection Review**
At the conclusion of the inspection, the Company escort **SHALL** review any issues of concern with the inspector.
*   It is crucial to ensure common agreement on these concerns with the inspector.
*   The escort **SHALL** also ensure that the inspector’s notes fairly reflect the discussion on those concerns.
*   If possible, the Company **SHOULD** obtain a copy of the inspector’s notes for internal records and follow-up.

These guidelines ensure transparency, compliance, and proper documentation during interactions with external regulatory bodies, complementing the internal [[0-PRC-8009_hse_audits_and_inspections_overview]] and [[0-PRC-8009_hse_audit_program_details]].
