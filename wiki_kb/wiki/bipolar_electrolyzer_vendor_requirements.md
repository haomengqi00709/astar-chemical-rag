# Bipolar Electrolyzer Vendor Requirements

This page outlines the mandatory requirements and information to be provided or confirmed by the vendor for bipolar electrolyzers, as specified in Document ID 4-PDS-XXXX-R1. Adherence to these requirements is crucial for the successful procurement and integration of the electrolyzer system.

**General Vendor Instructions**
*   "VTA" in the datasheet indicates "Vendor to Advise".
*   **Mandatory**: Vendor shall supply or confirm data as noted in the datasheet.
*   **Mandatory**: Vendor shall supply flow sheet and [[mass_balance]] for the cell room with their quotation. This is critical for verifying process integration and material flows, typically developed during [[flows_heet_development_and_approval_1_prc_0002]].
*   **Mandatory**: Vendor shall list materials for electrolyser components. This directly impacts [[material_selection]] and long-term durability.
*   **Mandatory**: Vendor shall specify materials for piping as required. This ensures compatibility and safety within the piping system.
*   **Mandatory**: Vendor shall specify pressure differential H2/Cl2 required with tolerances (refer to Note 4 in [[bipolar_electrolyzer_process_design_conditions]]). This is a critical safety and operational parameter.
*   **Mandatory**: Vendor shall specify gas relief pressure requirements. This is essential for safety and pressure management within the system.
*   **Mandatory**: Vendor shall confirm Power Consumption (refer to Note 5 in [[bipolar_electrolyzer_equipment_specifications]]). This is a key operational cost and performance metric, often derived from [[process_calculations]].

These requirements fall under general [[vendor_quality_assurance]] and are essential for a comprehensive technical and commercial evaluation.
