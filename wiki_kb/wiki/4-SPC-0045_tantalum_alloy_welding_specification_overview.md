# Tantalum Alloy Welding Specification (4-SPC-0045)

This specification, identified as 4-SPC-0045, outlines [[chemetics_aker_kvaerner_company_details|Aker Kvaerner Chemetics']] requirements for welding and associated procedures for vessels and equipment constructed from tantalum alloys. Specifically, it focuses on materials with UNS number R05252. This document was previously numbered 4-SPC-0005-R5.

The scope of this specification includes:
*   [[tantalum_alloy_material_requirements|Material requirements]] for approved tantalum alloys and suppliers.
*   Detailed [[tantalum_alloy_welding_procedures|welding procedures]] and practices.
*   [[tantalum_alloy_ultrasonic_examination|Non-destructive examination (NDE)]] requirements, including ultrasonic, radiographic, and boroscope methods.
*   [[tantalum_alloy_pressure_and_leak_testing|Pressure and leak testing]] protocols.

Referenced codes and standards for general design and fabrication are detailed in [[4-SPC-0040_metallic_process_vessels_fabrication_overview]].

The document also includes specific data in [[4-SPC-0045_table_1]].

This specification also references [[4-SPC-0040_shop_fabricated_metallic_vessels_and_components|4-SPC-0040: Shop Fabricated Metallic Vessels and Metallic Vessel Components]] for codes and standards related to design and fabrication.
Historically, this document was previously identified as 4-SPC-0005-R5.
