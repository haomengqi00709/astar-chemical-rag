# Vessel Fabrication and Surface Preparation for Acid Resistant Lining

The vessel fabricator **must** adhere to all requirements necessary for the subsequent application of acid resistant lining. Coordination of activities with the fabricator is essential to ensure that the vessel is prepared correctly. An inspector **will** be dispatched to the job site to verify that fabrication and surface preparation are proceeding correctly and in accordance with this standard.

**Welding and Surface Finish Requirements:**
Surfaces designated for lining **must** be of fully welded construction. Acceptable weld details include fillet or butt-weld types. The following imperfections and construction methods are strictly prohibited on surfaces to be lined, as they can compromise the integrity of the lining system:
*   Undercuts
*   Crevices
*   Lap-joints
*   Stitch welds
*   Tack welds
*   Riveted construction

All butt welds intended to receive lining **must** be ground flush with the adjacent surfaces upon completion. The following weld and surface irregularities are unacceptable and **must** be rectified:
*   Gouging
*   Weld porosity
*   Blowholes
*   Weld spatter
*   Sharp edges
*   Burrs
*   Projections
*   Any other irregularities

Sharp changes in contour and all corners **will** be rounded by grinding to achieve a minimum radius of 5mm. Similarly, fillet welds in the surfaces to be lined **will** be rounded by grinding to produce a minimum radius of 5mm. These rounding requirements are critical for preventing stress concentrations and ensuring proper adhesion of the lining.

The lining installer **is responsible** for clearly marking the exterior of all vessels as "Lined Vessel Do Not Weld" to prevent accidental damage or compromise to the lining system during subsequent construction phases.

**Abrasive Blasting and Dryness Requirements:**
Internal steel surfaces, with the specific exception of drying tower gas inlets, **will** be abrasive blasted to **SSPC-SP 6 Commercial Blast Cleaning**. Drying tower gas inlets, however, **will** be abrasive blasted to **SSPC-SP 10 Near White Blast Cleaning**. These blasting standards ensure an appropriate surface profile for lining adhesion.

Prior to the commencement of blasting and lining operations, the vessel interiors **must** be completely dry and free from any standing water. This interior dryness **must** be maintained from the time of blasting until the vessels are put into service. If oils, greases, dirt, or rust inhibitors are present on internal steel surfaces and cannot be effectively removed by blasting alone, **SSPC-SP 1** solvent cleaning **must** be applied before blasting.

No blasted surface **will** be left uncoated for more than 8 hours to prevent re-contamination or flash rusting. Blast cleaning **will not** be performed concurrently with the application of the membrane to avoid dust and debris interference.

### Fabrication Requirements
*   Surfaces to be lined **will** be of fully welded construction.
*   Weld details **will** be fillet or butt-weld types.
*   The following are **not permitted** on surfaces to be lined:
    *   Undercuts
    *   Crevices
    *   Lap-joints
    *   Stitch welds
    *   Tack welds
    *   Riveted construction
*   All butt welds to receive lining **will** be ground flush with the adjacent surfaces following completion.
*   The following weld defects and surface irregularities are **not acceptable**:
    *   Gouging
    *   Weld porosity
    *   Blowholes
    *   Weld spatter
    *   Sharp edges
    *   Burrs
    *   Projections
    *   Any other irregularities
*   Sharp changes of contour **will** be rounded by grinding to produce a minimum radius of 5mm.
*   Corners **will** be rounded by grinding to produce a minimum radius of 5mm.
*   Fillet welds in the surfaces to be lined **will** be rounded by grinding to produce a minimum radius of 5mm.

### Surface Preparation
*   Internal steel surfaces, with the exception of drying tower gas inlets, **will** be abrasive blasted to SSPC-SP 6 Commercial Blast Cleaning.
*   Dry tower gas inlets **will** be abrasive blasted to SSPC-SP 10 Near White Blast Cleaning.
*   Before blasting and lining can commence, the vessel interiors **must** be completely dry and free from any standing water.
*   Interior dryness **must** be maintained from the time of blasting to the time the vessels are put into service.
*   If oils, greases, dirt or rust inhibitors are present on internal steel surfaces and cannot be effectively removed by blasting, SSPC-SP 1 **must** be applied prior to abrasive blasting.
*   No blasted surface **will** be left un-coated for more than 8 hours.
*   Blast cleaning **will not** be performed when the membrane is being applied.
