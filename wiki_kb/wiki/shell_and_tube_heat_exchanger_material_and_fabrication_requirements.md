# Shell and Tube Heat Exchanger Material and Fabrication Requirements

This page outlines the mandatory material selections and fabrication requirements for Shell and Tube Heat Exchangers, as specified in the Template S & T Heat Exchanger - Vdr Package Datasheet (4-DST-XXXX-R0). These specifications are critical for ensuring the structural integrity, corrosion resistance, and longevity of the heat exchanger in its intended service.

**Mandatory Material Specifications:**
The following material components **must** be specified:
*   **Tubes material:** The material for the heat exchanger tubes.
*   **Shell material:** The material for the heat exchanger shell.
*   **Channel material:** The material for the channels.
*   **Tubesheet material:** The material for the tubesheets.
*   **Baffles material:** The material for the baffles.
*   **Gasket material:** The material for all gaskets.
*   **Insulation:** The type and specifications of insulation required.

For general guidance on material selection, refer to [[material_selection_guidelines]].

**Mandatory Fabrication and Surface Treatment Specifications:**
The following aspects related to fabrication and surface treatment **must** be specified:
*   **Fabrication:** Detailed requirements for the manufacturing process.
*   **Lining:** Any internal lining specifications.
*   **Welding:** All applicable welding procedures and standards. Refer to [[welding]] for general welding requirements.
*   **Painting:** Painting and coating specifications for external surfaces. Refer to [[coatings_design_and_specification_7_prc_0002]].
*   **Insulation:** Requirements for insulation application. Refer to [[insulation_design_and_specification_6_prc_0002]].

These specifications ensure that the heat exchanger is constructed from appropriate materials and fabricated to the necessary quality standards for its operational environment.

The following material selections and fabrication requirements are mandatory and must be specified:
*   **Fabrication**
*   **Lining**
*   **Welding**
*   **Painting**
*   **Tubes material**
*   **Shell material**
*   **Channel material**
*   **Tubesheet material**
*   **Baffles material**
*   **Gasket material**
*   **Insulation**
