# Personal Travel in High or Extreme Risk Areas

While on a business trip in a High or Extreme risk area, any travel undertaken for pleasure or rest and relaxation (R&R) outside of regular work hours **must** be coordinated. This coordination **shall** involve both the [[travel_coordinator_responsibilities|Travel Coordinator]] and the employee’s direct supervisor at the home office.

This requirement ensures that even during personal time, the company is aware of the employee's movements in potentially hazardous regions, allowing for better oversight and support in case of an emergency. The employee **shall** take all reasonable safety precautions during their travels for pleasure in High or Extreme risk areas, consistent with the general principles outlined in the [[traveller_responsibilities|Employee Responsibilities for Business Travel Safety]] and the broader [[travel_risk_assessment_and_approval|Business Travel Risk Assessment and Approval]] framework.
