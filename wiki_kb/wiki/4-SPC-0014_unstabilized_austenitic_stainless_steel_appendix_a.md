# Unstabilized Austenitic Stainless Steel Requirements (4-SPC-0014 Appendix A)

Appendix A of document 4-SPC-0014 addresses the specific requirements for non-stabilized grades of high nickel austenitic stainless steels.

**Applicability**
This appendix applies to unstabilized P45 alloys such as 904L and 254 SLX, when referenced in purchase orders or other project documents.

**Quality Control and Expertise**
Non-stabilized grades of high nickel austenitic stainless steels demand a similar degree of welder/operator expertise and quality control to that contained in the main body of this Standard. All general requirements for welding procedures, welder qualifications, fabrication, inspection, and documentation, as outlined in the primary sections of 4-SPC-0014, shall apply unless specifically superseded by provisions within this appendix or other referenced documents.

**Welding Consumables**
*   The use of filler metals not listed shall be referred to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics QA & Materials Group for approval.
*   Autogenous (no filler metal) welding of the "904L" type materials is not permitted.

**Inspection**
*   Dye Penetrant Inspection shall satisfy Para 10.3 (refer to [[4-SPC-0014_inspection_and_testing_requirements]]).
*   Dye Penetrant Inspection for piping shall satisfy [[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping]].
