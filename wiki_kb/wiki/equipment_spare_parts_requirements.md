# Equipment Spare Parts Requirements

As outlined in document 4-SPC-0001, the Vendor **must** provide comprehensive spare parts lists to support equipment operation and maintenance.

**Required Spare Parts Lists**
The Vendor **will provide** recommended spare parts lists, with pricing valid for one year, for the following categories:
*   Spare parts required for start-up.
*   Spare parts required for two years of continuous operation.

**Content of Spare Parts Lists**
Each spare parts list **must include** the following essential information:
*   Recommended quantity of spares.
*   Item identification number.
*   Description of the item.
*   Item manufacturer.
*   Unit price.
*   Delivery time.

**Pricing Basis**
The unit price for spares **will be based** on the assumption that the spares **will be ordered** separately from the main equipment purchase.

**Spare Parts Tagging**
All spare parts **will be clearly tagged** with the words "Spare Part For" followed by the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics equipment number. Additionally, spare parts **will be clearly tagged** with a part number which is referenced in the manufacturer's maintenance documentation, if applicable. This ensures easy identification and proper inventory management of critical spares.
