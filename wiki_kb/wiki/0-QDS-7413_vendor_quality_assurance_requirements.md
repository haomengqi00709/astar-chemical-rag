# Acid Tower Distributor Vendor Quality Assurance Requirements (0-QDS-7413)

This document outlines the specific quality assurance requirements for the manufacturing of the Acid Tower Distributor, as defined by Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7413.

**Manufacturing Standards:**
The equipment is manufactured by Chemetics Equipment Division.
*   Manufacturing **must** be in accordance with the CED Quality Control Manual, Latest Edition.
*   Manufacturing **must** be in accordance with this Quality Data Sheet, 0-QDS-7413 [[0-QDS-7413_quality_data_sheet_administration]].

**Surveillance Level:**
*   Surveillance by Chemetics Vancouver will be to Level 1.0. For more information on surveillance levels, refer to [[0-QDS-7411_chemetics_surveillance_level]].

These requirements ensure that the Acid Tower Distributor meets the specified quality and performance standards. For general vendor quality assurance principles, see [[vendor_quality_assurance]].
