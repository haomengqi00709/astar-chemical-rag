# Overtime Compensation Policy Overview (0-POL-9113)

This document, identified as 0-POL-9113, outlines Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' policy on overtime compensation. While the original document title is "Unknown", its purpose is to ensure employees are fairly compensated for work performed in excess of normal hours. The firm's business operations will, at times, necessitate work beyond these standard hours.

**Normal Hours of Work**
The standard definition for normal hours of work is 75 hours per two-week period. This period is adjusted by subtracting 8.25 hours for every local statutory holiday that falls within that timeframe. These normal hours of work are established in accordance with the company's broader policy on hours of work, detailed in [[0-pol-9106_normal_work_schedule]].

**Policy Scope**
This policy details the compensation structure for both "Exempt" and "Non-Exempt" employees, as defined by their positions and statutory provisions. It also covers provisions for [[0-pol-9113_banked_time_policy]] and specific guidelines for [[0-pol-9113_business_travel_compensation]]. Employees eligible for overtime compensation **must** record their excess hours on their weekly timesheets, as further elaborated in [[0-pol-9113_timesheet_procedures]].

Further details regarding specific compensation rates and employee classifications can be found in [[0-pol-9113_exempt_employee_overtime_compensation]] and [[0-pol-9113_non_exempt_employee_overtime_compensation]].

**Normal Hours of Work**
Normal hours of work are defined as 75 hours per two-week period. This period is reduced by 8.25 hours for every local statutory holiday falling within that period. These normal hours are established in accordance with document 0-POL-9106: Hours of Work.

**General Eligibility and Compensation Principles**
Employees in positions evaluated at Hay point 639 or lower will be paid for hours worked in excess of the normal hours of work. Conversely, employees in positions evaluated at Hay point 640 or above will not normally be paid for such excess hours.

For compensation calculations, an employee’s hourly rate of pay is determined by dividing their annual salary by 1,950.
