# Business Travel Authorization Process

Prior to undertaking any business travel, an employee **must** receive authorization. This authorization is obtained by submitting a travel requisition. The comprehensive process for travel authorization is detailed in [[travel_authorization_process_0_frm_9002]].

The travel requisition **must** be approved by the employee’s Department Manager before it is distributed. Once approved, the requisition is distributed to a specific list of individuals and entities for review. This list includes:
*   The Travel Coordinator
*   The HSE Manager
*   The Human Resources Manager
*   The Corporate health services provider (external)

These individuals and the external provider are responsible for reviewing the travel request. Their primary duty is to flag any requests that require further consideration related to Health, Safety, and Security, especially concerning the destination's risk profile. This review process is a critical step in ensuring that all potential risks are identified and addressed before travel commences, aligning with the overall [[0-prc-8102_travel_health_and_safety|Travel Health, Safety, and Security Overview]].

Further details on the approval authorities can be found in [[travel_authorization_approvals_0_frm_9002]].
