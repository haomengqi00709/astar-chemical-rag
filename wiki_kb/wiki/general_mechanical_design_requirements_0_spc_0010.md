# General Mechanical Design Requirements (99H10600-4-SPC-0010)

Data available in SQL: 99H10600-4-SPC-0010

The document 99H10600-4-SPC-0010 R1, titled "General Mechanical Design Requirements," specifies the overarching mechanical design principles, standards, and practices that **must** be adhered to for all mechanical equipment and components within Project 99H10600. This specification ensures consistency, safety, and reliability across all mechanical installations.

This general mechanical design specification typically covers:
*   **Applicable Codes and Standards:** Reference to international and national codes (e.g., ASME, API, EN) for pressure vessels, piping, rotating equipment, and other mechanical items.
*   **Material Selection Guidelines:** General requirements for material grades, corrosion allowances, and fabrication standards.
*   **Welding and NDT Requirements:** Specifications for welding procedures, welder qualifications, and non-destructive testing (NDT) methods and acceptance criteria.
*   **Pressure Testing:** General requirements for hydrostatic or pneumatic testing of equipment and piping systems.
*   **Surface Preparation and Painting:** Standards for surface finish, protective coatings, and insulation requirements.
*   **Safety Features:** General requirements for pressure relief, emergency shutdown, and other safety-critical mechanical design elements.
*   **Vendor Documentation:** General expectations for vendor-supplied drawings, calculations, and manuals.

Vendors supplying any mechanical Goods and Services for Project 99H10600 **must** ensure that their designs and fabrication methods comply with the requirements set forth in this specification. This document is a key reference in Text Section 1: Scope of Supply of Purchase Orders, ensuring that all mechanical equipment meets the project's fundamental design criteria.
