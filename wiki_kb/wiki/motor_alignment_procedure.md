# Motor Alignment Procedure

This page describes the mandatory [[motor_alignment_procedure|motor alignment procedure]] that **must** be completed and signed off for [[rotating_equipment_mechanical_checkout|rotating]] and [[conveying_equipment_mechanical_checkout|conveying equipment]]. Proper alignment is critical for efficient operation and preventing premature wear.
