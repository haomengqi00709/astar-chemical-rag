# Motor Control Schematic Wiring Diagrams

Motor Control Schematic Wiring Diagrams illustrate the control logic and wiring connections for motors, from the motor starter to the motor, local control stations, the DCS, and other control devices. These diagrams are essential for understanding motor operation and troubleshooting.

**Content of Motor Control Schematic Wiring Diagrams**
Motor control schematic wiring diagram drawings **shall** show:
*   The motor control schematic.
*   The wiring required from the motor starter to the motor.
*   The wiring required from the motor starter to the local control station.
*   The wiring required from the motor starter to the [[control_philosophy_and_dcs]].
*   The wiring required from the motor starter to other control devices used.
*   The type of controls located at the motor.

The wiring **shall** show the wiring connections to the local control station, the motor, the DCS, and any other devices.
Motor control schematic wiring diagram drawings **shall** show the cable numbers and conductor numbers for each device.

**References**
Motor control schematic wiring diagram drawings **shall** reference the [[power_and_control_cables_list]], [[final_single_line_diagrams]], and DCS termination drawings (likely [[io_interface_wiring_diagrams]]).
Controls for each motor **will** be shown on [[piping_and_instrumentation_drawings]] (0-PID-XXXX).

**Other Control Devices**
Other control devices that may be included are:
*   Vibration switches.
*   Level switches.
*   Pressure switches.
*   Speed switches.
