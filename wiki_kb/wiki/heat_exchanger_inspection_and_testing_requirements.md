# Inspection and Testing Requirements for CS Gas to Gas Heat Exchangers

This page outlines the mandatory inspection and testing requirements for CS Gas to Gas Heat Exchangers (Vestibules & Shells), as specified in Quality Data Sheet [[0-QDS-7403-1_quality_data_sheet_details]]. These requirements ensure the quality and integrity of the fabricated components.

**Quality Record Definitions**
For the purpose of this document, the following quality record designations apply:
*   **AP**: "As Prepared" - Indicates that the record is to be prepared by the vendor.
*   **FP**: "Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package" - Indicates that the record **must** be included in the final Quality Assurance Documentation Package submitted by the vendor.
For broader context on quality records, see [[0-QDS-7_quality_record_definitions]].

**Chemetics Involvement**
Chemetics' involvement in inspections and testing is designated by "Witness" (Chemetics personnel observe the activity) and "Hold" (Chemetics personnel **must** approve continuation of work after the activity).

**Mandatory Inspection and Testing Points:**

*   **Certificates:**
    *   Mill Test Certificates: Required as FP and for Chemetics Witness.
    *   Weld consumable certificates: Required as FP and for Chemetics Witness.
    *   Head forming certificates: Required as FP and for Chemetics Witness.

*   **Welding Qualifications:**
    *   Vestibule Welding Procedure Qualification: Required as FP, for Chemetics Witness, and Chemetics Hold.
    *   Welder Qualification: Required as FP, for Chemetics Witness, and Chemetics Hold.

*   **Production Activities:**
    *   Production Welding Start: Required for Chemetics Witness.

*   **Pressure and Leak Testing:**
    *   Air/Soap Test for Reinforcing pads: Required as FP, for Chemetics Witness, and Chemetics Hold.

*   **Non-Destructive Examination (NDE):**
    *   NDE **shall** be performed in accordance with ASME Boiler & Pressure Vessel Code or an approved equivalent. Refer to [[0-QDS-7_non_destructive_examination_requirements]] for general NDE requirements.
    *   Spot Radiographic testing: Required for all butt welds, as FP, for Chemetics Witness, and Chemetics Hold.
    *   100% Dye Penetrant testing: Required for root & cap passes on dissimilar alloys, as FP, and for Chemetics Witness.
    *   25% Magnetic Particle testing: Required for all non-butt welds, as FP, and for Chemetics Witness.
    *   25% Magnetic Particle testing: Required for completed nozzle attachment welds, as FP, and for Chemetics Witness.

*   **Dimensional and Visual Inspections:**
    *   Dimensional inspection: Required as FP, for Chemetics Witness, and Chemetics Hold.
    *   Contour & finish of welds inspection: Required as FP, for Chemetics Witness, and Chemetics Hold.
    *   Surfaces free from defects inspection: Required for Chemetics Witness and Chemetics Hold.
    *   Plate Cleanliness inspection: Required for Chemetics Witness and Chemetics Hold.

*   **Documentation and Shipping:**
    *   Review QA Documentation: Required as FP, for Chemetics Witness, and Chemetics Hold.
    *   Preparation for shipping inspection: Required for Chemetics Witness and Chemetics Hold.
    *   Packing List: Required as AP and for Chemetics Witness.

These requirements are critical for ensuring the final product meets the specified quality standards.

**Quality Record Definitions**
*   **AP**: "As Prepared"
*   **FP**: "Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package"

**Certification Requirements**
*   Mill Test Certificates are required as FP and for Chemetics Witness.
*   Weld consumable certs. are required as FP and for Chemetics Witness.
*   Head forming certificates are required as FP and for Chemetics Witness.
*   Packing List is required as AP and for Chemetics Witness.

**Welding Qualifications and Inspections**
*   Vestibule Welding Procedure Qualification is required as FP, for Chemetics Witness, and Chemetics Hold.
*   Welder Qualification is required as FP, for Chemetics Witness, and Chemetics Hold.
*   Production Welding Start is required for Chemetics Witness.

**Non-Destructive Examination (NDE)**
Non Destructive Examination will be in accordance with ASME Boiler & Pressure Vessel Code or approved equivalent.
*   Spot Radiographic testing is required for all butt welds, as FP, for Chemetics Witness, and Chemetics Hold.
*   100% Dye Penetrant testing is required for root & cap passes on dissimilar alloys, as FP, and for Chemetics Witness.
*   25% Magnetic Particle testing is required for all non-butt welds, as FP, and for Chemetics Witness.
*   25% Magnetic Particle testing is required for completed nozzle attachment welds, as FP, and for Chemetics Witness.

**Other Inspections and Milestones**
*   Air/Soap Test for Reinforcing pads is required as FP, for Chemetics Witness, and Chemetics Hold.
*   Dimensional inspection is required as FP, for Chemetics Witness, and Chemetics Hold.
*   Contour & finish of welds inspection is required as FP, for Chemetics Witness, and Chemetics Hold.
*   Surfaces free from defects inspection is required for Chemetics Witness and Chemetics Hold.
*   Plate Cleanliness inspection is required for Chemetics Witness and Chemetics Hold.
*   Review QA Documentation is required as FP, for Chemetics Witness, and Chemetics Hold.
*   Preparation for shipping inspection is required for Chemetics Witness and Chemetics Hold.
