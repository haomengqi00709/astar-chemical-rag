# Construction Non-Conformance Reporting

Construction Non-Conformance Reporting is a formal process for documenting and resolving defects identified in the works supplied by Construction Subcontractors. Defects may be identified either by the Subcontractor themselves or by an Inspector during [[construction_surveillance_and_reporting]].

**Definition of a Defect:**
A Defect is defined as any part of the works that is not in accordance with the Works Information. It is important to note that any part of the works not yet considered complete by the Subcontractor should not be reported as a Defect; rather, it should be addressed through normal construction progress and quality control. However, any part of the works that fails to meet the requirements of the inspections and tests set out in the [[subcontractor_quality_plan]] **will** be reported as a Defect.

**Defect Report Requirements:**
For each identified Defect, the Inspector **must** prepare a report using the form 0-NCR-6XXX: [Template] Construction Non-Conformance Report. This report **must** contain the following critical information:
*   A clear Description of the Defect.
*   The Subcontractor’s proposed action to correct the Defect.
*   The Subcontractor’s proposed action to prevent recurrence of the Defect.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ approval of the proposed actions, including any qualifications or conditions.
*   The Results of re-inspection after the corrective action has been implemented.

**Checking and Approval of Actions:**
The Subcontractor’s proposed actions to address the Defect **will** be checked and approved by the same personnel responsible for the approval of the reference [[quality_data_sheets_qds]]. The checker **must** ensure the acceptance of other relevant disciplines as required. This checking and approval process **will** be formally recorded by digital signatures on the published Construction Non-Conformance Report.

**Document Numbering, Naming, and Publishing:**
Construction Non-Conformance Reports **will** be numbered according to [[document_numbering_and_name_change_conventions]] (0-PRC-0002) and **will** be named identically to the reference Quality Data Sheets. In cases where multiple Non-Conformance Reports are issued for the same item, an incremental number **will** be included in the title to distinguish them.

The publishing of Construction Non-Conformance Reports follows the procedure outlined in [[publishing_project_documents]] (0-PRC-0005). Published NCRs **will** be marked with the status “Approved for Construction”, and their revision numbers **will** be incremented as information is developed.

**Revision and Final Disposition:**
Revisions of Construction Non-Conformance Reports **will** be issued as new information becomes available, primarily to advise personnel of the current status. Generally, two versions of NCRs **will** be formally issued with the status “Approved for Construction”:
1.  The first version denotes Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ approval of the Subcontractor’s proposed corrective and preventive actions.
2.  The second version follows the recording of re-inspection results, providing a final record of the defect’s disposition and closure.

**Definition and Reporting Criteria:**
*   A Defect is defined as any part of the works that is not in accordance with the Works Information.
*   Any part of the works that fails to meet the requirements of the inspections and tests set out in the [[subcontractor_quality_plan|Subcontractor’s Quality Plan]] will be reported as a Defect.
*   It is important to note that any part of the works not yet considered complete by the Subcontractor should *not* be reported as a Defect.

**Review and Approval Process:**
*   The Subcontractor’s proposed actions for correcting and preventing recurrence of defects will be checked and approved by the same personnel responsible for the approval of the reference [[quality_data_sheets_qds|Quality Data Sheet]].
*   The checker is responsible for ensuring the acceptance of other disciplines as required.
*   Checking and approval are formally recorded by digital signatures on the published Construction Non-Conformance Report.
    *   *Reference:* The publishing process follows 0-PRC-0005: Publishing of Project Documents.

**Numbering and Revision Control:**
*   Published Construction Non-Conformance Reports will be marked with the status “Approved for Construction”.
*   The revision number for published Construction Non-Conformance Reports will be incremented with each formal issuance.
*   NCRs are numbered according to 0-PRC-0002: Document Numbering.
*   NCRs will be named identically to their reference [[quality_data_sheets_qds|Quality Data Sheets]].
*   In cases where multiple Non-Conformance Reports are raised for the same item, an incremental number will be included in the title to distinguish them.
*   Revisions of the Construction Non-Conformance Reports are issued as information is developed, primarily to advise personnel of the current status.
*   Generally, two versions of NCRs will be formally issued with the status “Approved for Construction”:
    *   One version denotes the approval by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics of the Subcontractor’s proposed actions.
    *   The subsequent version follows the recording of re-inspection results, providing a final record of the defect's disposition.
