# Commercial Liability Limitations

This page outlines the specific limitations on liability for both Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Vendor within the framework of project contracts, as defined in Purchase Order [[0-SPC-5001_purchase_order_overview|0-SPC-5001]].

**Exclusions from Liability**
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Vendor **will not** be liable under the Purchase Order, whether in law or equity, including for negligence, for the following types of damages or losses:

*   **Loss of Profit:** Neither party can claim compensation for lost profits.
*   **Loss of Use:** Neither party can claim compensation for the loss of use of equipment, facilities, or other assets.
*   **Loss of Production:** Neither party can claim compensation for losses related to a reduction or cessation of production.
*   **Indirect or Consequential Damage:** Neither party is liable for damages that are not a direct result of a breach or fault but arise as a secondary consequence.
*   **Special, Exemplary, or Punitive Damages:** Neither party is liable for damages awarded to punish a party for wrongdoing or to set an example, beyond actual compensatory losses.

These limitations are designed to cap potential financial exposure for both parties, focusing liability on direct and foreseeable damages rather than broader business impacts.
