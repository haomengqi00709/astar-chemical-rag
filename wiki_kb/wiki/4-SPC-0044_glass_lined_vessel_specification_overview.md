# Glass-Lined Carbon Steel Reaction Vessels Specification Overview (4-SPC-0044)

Standard 4-SPC-0044 outlines the comprehensive requirements for the manufacture, testing, inspection, and supply of glass-lined carbon steel reaction vessels. These vessels, intended for Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Inc., encompass various types, including agitated and jacketed or split pipe coil configurations.

**Scope and Application**
This specification applies to all glass-lined carbon steel reaction vessels procured by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Inc. The standard ensures that all supplied equipment meets stringent quality and performance criteria.

**Compliance and Precedence**
All vessels **shall** be manufactured in strict accordance with the following:
*   [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels|ASME Boiler & Pressure Vessel Code, Section VIII, Division 1]] (latest edition).
*   The Manufacturer’s standard procedure for glass-lined vessels.
*   The specific requirements detailed within 4-SPC-0044.

In the event of any conflict between referenced codes and standards, the more stringent requirement **shall** govern. All codes, standards, and other referenced documents **shall** be interpreted as meaning the latest published revisions at the time of quotation.

**Fabricator Responsibilities**
The Fabricator **shall** be responsible for the overall design of the vessel. This includes obtaining the necessary approvals for design and/or fabrication, as well as the registration of pressure equipment by the appropriate jurisdictional authorities. The cost associated with external inspection services required for these approvals **shall** be included in the Fabricator’s proposal.

Aker Kvaerner Chemetics will provide the vessel type, applicable Engineering Standard, and designation for each vessel. The Fabricator’s proposal **shall** clearly state the type of glass-lining proposed and the minimum thickness of the vessel lining.

Related information on packaging and shipping can be found in Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' Packaging and Shipping Standards ES44\S-01 and ES44\S-02, which are applicable to all glass-lined equipment.
