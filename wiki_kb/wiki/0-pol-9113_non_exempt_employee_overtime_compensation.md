# Non-Exempt Employee Overtime Compensation

This policy defines "Non-Exempt" employees and their compensation for hours worked in excess of normal working hours.

**Definition of Non-Exempt Employees**
"Non-Exempt" employees are those in positions subject to the statutory provisions governing the payment of overtime as contained in the Employment Standards Act. At Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, "Non-Exempt" employees comprise all employees not described in Section 3.1 (which defines "Exempt" employees).

**Compensation for Non-Exempt Employees**
Compensation for "Non-Exempt" employees for hours worked in excess of normal hours is structured as follows:
*   **Time above 37.5 hours but under 40 hours per week:** Compensation will be at the employee’s standard hourly rate of pay.
*   **Time above 40 hours per week:** Compensation will be at 1.5 times (1.5x) the employee’s standard hourly rate of pay.
*   **Time in excess of 12 hours in a day:** Compensation will be at 2 times (2x) the employee’s standard hourly rate of pay.

**Flexible Work Schedules**
Where "Non-Exempt" employees are following a [[0-pol-9106_flexible_work_schedule]] in accordance with 0-POL-9106: Hours of Work, overtime will only be paid for hours worked outside the approved flexible work schedule.

Eligible "Non-Exempt" employees may also request that time equivalent to the compensation be [[0-pol-9113_banked_time_policy]].

This policy is part of the broader [[0-pol-9113_overtime_compensation_policy_overview]]. For details on other employee classifications, refer to [[0-pol-9113_exempt_employee_overtime_compensation]].
