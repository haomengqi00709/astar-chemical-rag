# Acid Tower Field Erected Shop Component Vendor Quality Assurance (0-QDS-7419)

For the Acid Tower Field Erected Shop Component, the vendor's quality assurance program **must** adhere to the requirements outlined in this Quality Data Sheet (0-QDS-7419) and the overarching [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment]] specification.

**Vendor Quality Plan:**
The vendor **must** prepare a comprehensive Quality Plan in accordance with [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment]]. This plan **shall** detail the processes and procedures the vendor will implement to ensure all quality requirements are met throughout the fabrication and assembly of the Acid Tower Field Erected Shop Component.

**Chemetics Surveillance Level:**
Surveillance activities conducted by Chemetics for this component **will be** to Level 1.0. This level of surveillance indicates a high degree of involvement from Chemetics in monitoring the vendor's quality activities.

For a broader understanding of vendor quality assurance principles, refer to [[vendor_quality_assurance]]. Details on Chemetics' surveillance levels can be found in documents such as [[0-QDS-7_aker_kvaerner_surveillance]].
