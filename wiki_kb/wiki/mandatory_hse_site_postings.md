# Mandatory HSE Site Postings

To ensure transparency and easy access to critical health, safety, and environmental information, specific documents **must** be prominently displayed at all work sites with a functioning Joint Health and Safety Committee (JHSC). These postings are part of the broader [[hse_site_postings_overview]] and [[mandatory_hse_site_postings]] requirements.

At each work site with a functioning health and safety committee, the Company **shall** post the following:
*   A list of the joint committee members along with their contact information, facilitating direct communication regarding HSE concerns.
*   The reports of the 3 most recent committee meetings, providing employees with up-to-date information on discussions, recommendations, and actions taken by the JHSC.
*   Copies of orders issued by the local health and safety regulatory body over the last 12 months. This ensures that all personnel are aware of any regulatory directives or compliance issues, reinforcing adherence to [[compliance_and_standards]].

These postings are crucial for keeping employees informed and engaged in the site's health and safety program, promoting a culture of awareness and accountability.
