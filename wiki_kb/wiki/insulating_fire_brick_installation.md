# Insulating Fire Brick Installation

The installation of insulating fire brick requires strict adherence to specific quality and workmanship standards to ensure optimal performance of the refractory lining.

**Brick Selection and Mortar Joints**
All insulating fire brick selected for installation **must** be free of cracks and voids. The thickness of mortar joints in insulating fire brick **shall** be kept to a minimum. This minimum thickness is achieved through the strategic use of a combination of standard and special shape bricks. Insulating fire brick **must** be laid up with thin mortar joints, and all mortar joints **shall** be completely filled with mortar.

**Fitting and Cutting**
Any insulating fire brick that requires fitting **must** be accurately laid out. These bricks **shall** be wet cut with a diamond blade; chipping or breaking insulating fire brick with a hammer is strictly not permitted.

**Alignment and Appearance**
Vertical joints in insulating fire brick **shall** be staggered to enhance structural integrity. Courses of insulating fire brick **must** be held straight to line, true to a straight edge, and flush on the surface.

**Inspection**
The installation process, including the application of insulating fire brick, is subject to inspection at various stages to ensure compliance with this specification and maintenance of high workmanship standards. Refer to [[refractory_brick_lining_inspection_and_acceptance]] for detailed inspection requirements.
