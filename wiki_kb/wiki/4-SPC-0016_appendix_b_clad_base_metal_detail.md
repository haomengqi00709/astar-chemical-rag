# 4-SPC-0016 Appendix B: Typical Clad/Base Metal Detail

This page describes the content of Appendix B, which is referenced in the 4-SPC-0016 standard.

Appendix B provides typical clad/base metal details, illustrating the specific welding configurations and preparation requirements for clad steel materials. This appendix serves as a visual and technical guide for Fabricators to ensure adherence to approved weld geometries and material preparation techniques.

Refer to [[4-SPC-0016_clad_steel_material_preparation_and_forming]] and [[4-SPC-0016_clad_steel_weld_preparation_and_assembly]] for detailed requirements related to material preparation, forming, and weld assembly.
