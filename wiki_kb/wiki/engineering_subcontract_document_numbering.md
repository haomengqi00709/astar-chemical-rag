# Engineering Subcontract Document Numbering

Engineering Subcontract Agreements, specifically those using the 0-SUB-22XX template, adhere to a specific numbering convention to ensure unique identification and traceability.

Each Engineering Subcontract Agreement **must** be assigned an identification number in the format of 22XX. The "XX" component represents a sequential series, starting from 01, then 02, 03, and so forth.

An illustrative example of a complete document number for an Engineering Subcontract Agreement is 98C12300-0-SUB-2201-RA. This example demonstrates the integration of the sequential series within the broader document numbering scheme.

For a comprehensive understanding of the overall document numbering system and its components, refer to the [[document_number_format_and_components]] procedure, which is detailed in [[0-prc-0002_table_1]]. The general document numbering procedure is further elaborated in [[0-prc-0002_document_numbering]].
