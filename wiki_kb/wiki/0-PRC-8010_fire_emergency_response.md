# Fire Emergency Response

This page details the immediate actions and responsibilities in the event of a fire emergency within the office.

**Detecting a Fire**
**You must** be alert for signs of a fire. If you see smoke, flames, or smell smoke, **you must** immediately isolate the fire by closing doors.

**Alarm and Reporting**
**You must** pull the Fire Alarm to signal a building evacuation. Alarm pulls are located at stairwell exits.
**You must** report the fire immediately. Refer to the Emergency contacts at the front of this manual (see [[0-PRC-8010_emergency_contacts_and_first_aid]]) for reporting procedures.
The building **is to be** evacuated as per instructions under the [[0-PRC-8010_building_evacuation_plan]].

**Fire Extinguishers**
Fire extinguishers are strategically located throughout the building:
*   At all floor exits.
*   At all coffee stations.
*   At various other locations throughout the building.
*   The locations of fire extinguishers in open areas are marked with a red pyramid mounted high on a building column or wall.

**Using Fire Extinguishers**
A fire is often easy to put out with a fire extinguisher at its initial stage. However, **you must** USE GOOD JUDGEMENT and PUT YOUR PERSONAL SAFETY FIRST if you decide to attempt to put out a small fire using a fire extinguisher.
*   **DO NOT** go out of your way to fight a fire.
*   **DO NOT** enter enclosed spaces to fight a fire. Instead, close doors to isolate the fire.
*   **You must** be sure that your way out is clear if you decide to take action to extinguish a fire.
