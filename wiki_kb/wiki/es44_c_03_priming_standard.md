# Priming Standard ES44\C-03

Standard ES44\C-03 specifies the requirements for priming external surfaces of shop-fabricated tanks.

**Key Requirements:**
*   All external surfaces of shop-fabricated tanks, including the floor, **must** be primed in accordance with this standard.
*   For site-fabricated items, all steel **must** arrive at the site unpainted unless specifically noted otherwise in project documentation.

This standard ensures consistent surface preparation and initial protective coating application for equipment before further painting or installation.
