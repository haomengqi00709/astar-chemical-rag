# Carbon Steel Piping Specific Welding Processes (5-SPC-0122)

This page details specific requirements and restrictions for various welding processes used in carbon steel piping fabrication, as outlined in document 5-SPC-0122.

**Flux Cored Arc Welding (FCAW) and Metal Inert Gas (MIG) Welding**
*   Flux cored arc welding and M.I.G. welding are only permissible where evidence of extensive, satisfactory use is available and prior approval by [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_quality_assurance_and_materials_engineering_group|Aker Kvaerner Chemetics]] has been obtained.
*   Weld deposit chemical analysis **shall** be provided for flux core and submerged arc welding processes.
*   Normally, M.I.G. welding is acceptable for root pass only.

**Preheat and Interpass Temperatures**
*   Preheat and interpass temperatures **shall** be controlled within the range specified on the weld procedure(s) specification.
