# Engineering Subcontractor Quality Plan: Services Provision

The provision of services is a mandatory element of the [[engineering_subcontractor_quality_assurance_0_spc_7004|Engineering Subcontractor Quality Plan]]. The Subcontractor **must** establish and maintain documented procedures for the effective provision and control of all services.

**Key Procedural Requirements:**

*   **Service Provision:** Documented procedures **must** be in place for the actual provision of the services. These procedures **shall** detail the steps and methodologies to be followed to deliver the required outputs.
*   **Review for Completeness:** Procedures **must** exist for reviewing the services to ensure their completeness. This involves verifying that all specified deliverables and tasks have been addressed.
*   **Review for Adequacy:** Procedures **must** also cover the review of services for adequacy. This ensures that the services are sufficient and appropriate for their intended purpose.
*   **Review for Conformance:** The Subcontractor **must** have documented procedures for reviewing services to confirm their conformance with all requirements stipulated in the Subcontract.
*   **Design Change Control:** Procedures for controlling design changes **must** be consistent with the controls applied to the original design. This ensures that any modifications to the design are managed rigorously, maintaining the integrity and quality of the services.

These documented procedures are essential for ensuring a consistent, controlled, and compliant approach to all aspects of service delivery under the Subcontract.
