# 0-POL-9114 Vacation Carryover and Payout

This section of document 0-POL-9114, the Vacation Policy, details the provisions for using, carrying over, and receiving payouts for vacation time, including procedures upon an employee's departure.

**Usage During Earning Year**
Employees are permitted to take their earned vacation time during the same calendar year in which it is accrued. This encourages regular use of vacation benefits for rest and rejuvenation.

**Vacation Carryover Policy**
Any unused vacation time from the current vacation year can be carried forward into the subsequent vacation year. This provision offers employees flexibility in managing their leave, allowing them to save vacation for longer breaks or specific personal needs.

**Payout of Carried-Forward Vacation**
At the employee’s request, any carried-forward vacation time may be paid out. This option provides employees with the choice to receive monetary compensation for accumulated unused vacation, rather than taking the time off.

**Managing Excess Accrual**
Revision 2 of this policy introduced a new requirement aimed at promoting regular vacation usage and preventing excessive accumulation. Employees with excess vacation accrual **must** create a vacation plan to utilize this time. This proactive measure ensures that employees benefit from their earned time off and helps manage company liabilities related to accrued vacation.

**Vacation Payouts Upon Termination**
The policy outlines clear procedures for vacation payouts when an employee leaves Aker Kvaerner Chemetics:
*   **Payment for Unused Earned Vacation:** Employees departing from the company will be compensated for any earned vacation entitlements that have not been taken prior to their last day of employment.
*   **Lump Sum Payment:** This payment for unused earned vacation will be issued as a lump sum, included with the employee’s final pay. This ensures that all outstanding vacation balances are settled promptly upon termination.
*   **Deduction for Excess Vacation Taken:** Conversely, if an employee has taken vacation time in excess of the amount they have earned at the time of their departure, this excess amount will be deducted from their final pay. This ensures a fair and accurate reconciliation of vacation balances upon an employee's termination.

Any carried-forward vacation time may be paid out at the employee’s request.

Upon an employee's departure from Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, any earned vacation entitlements that have not been taken will be paid out. This payment will be issued as a lump sum included with the employee’s final pay. Conversely, if an employee has taken vacation in excess of the amount earned, this excess will be deducted from their final pay.
