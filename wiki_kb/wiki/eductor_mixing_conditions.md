# Eductor Mixing Conditions

When the eductor's [[eductor_equipment_data_and_operation_types]] includes **Mixing** operations, the **Mixing Conditions** section of the Eductor Datasheet (4-PDS-XXXX-R1) provides specific parameters related to the mixing environment. These conditions are applicable if the eductor is used in a tank or vessel for agitation purposes.

The following parameters are specified, with [[engineering_units]] as indicated:
*   **Liquid level:** The liquid level in the tank, specified in m.
*   **Tank diameter:** The diameter of the tank, specified in m.
*   **Liquid Volume (min):** The minimum liquid volume for mixing, specified in m3.
*   **Liquid Volume (max):** The maximum liquid volume for mixing, specified in m.
    *   *Note: The unit for Liquid Volume (max) is specified as 'm' in the source document, which may be a typographical error. For consistency with Liquid Volume (min), 'm3' would typically be expected for volume measurements.*
*   **Mixing turnover rate:** The rate at which the tank volume is turned over by mixing, specified in m3/h.
