# Clad Steel Welding Non-Destructive Examination (NDE)

Non-Destructive Examination (NDE) is a critical component of quality assurance for austenitic stainless clad steel welded fabrications, ensuring the integrity and compliance of all welds.

**Dye Penetrant Testing (PT)**
*   All repairs to clad surfaces **will** be dye penetrant inspected.
*   Unless otherwise specified, weld surfaces **will** be 100 percent PT examined for cracks, porosity, slag inclusions, significant undercutting, or other major defects after grinding smooth.
*   Unless otherwise specified, all welds between dissimilar metals **will** be 100% PT examined on the root pass and on completed weld surfaces.
*   Tube to tubesheet welds **will** be 100% PT examined.
*   The PT exam **will** be carried out in accordance with a written procedure.
*   The limit of acceptability for PT exam **will** satisfy [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels]], Division 1, Appendix VIII. Linear indications **will** be considered unacceptable.
*   All excavated areas **will** be weld built-up flush with the surface of the base material, using the welding procedure previously approved by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for the main welding details. Internal weld repair surfaces **will** be ground smooth and PT examined per the above requirements.
*   Machined surfaces, such as weld overlaid flange faces, **will** also be 100 percent PT examined.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics reserves the right to accept or reject any doubtful or questionable indications, as well as weld surfaces that have not been ground smooth.

**Radiographic Testing (RT)**
*   Carbon steel weld radiography of each seam **will** be complete before starting to weld the stainless side of the seam.
*   Items fabricated in accordance with this Standard and the [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels]] Code **will** be spot radiographed in accordance with Para. UW-52(b) of Section VIII, Division 1 of the ASME Code. This paragraph defines requirements for:
    *   Minimum extent of Spot Radiographic Examination.
    *   Standards for Spot Radiographic Examination.
    *   Evaluation and Retests.
*   The RT Exam **will** be carried out in accordance with a written procedure.
*   RT Exam personnel **must** be qualified to SNTC-1A.
*   Where additional spot radiography is required, as defined in UW-52 Sub-para. (d)(2), the costs of this radiography, all repair costs, and re-radiography costs **will** be to the Fabricator’s account.
*   Where more than one welder or welding operator has been used, spot radiography **will** be performed in such a manner that the welding of each welder or welding operator can be examined.
*   Junctions between circumferential and longitudinal seams **will** be considered as preferred locations for spot radiography.

These NDE requirements are crucial for ensuring the structural integrity and quality of all welded components, aligning with general [[inspection_and_testing_requirements]].

**Ultrasonic Examination (UT) for Repairs and Attachments**
All repairs to clad surfaces **will** be 100 percent scanned with the ultrasonic probe. Ultrasonic scanning **will** include adjacent clad plate to ensure no further delamination has occurred during welding. Repairs **will** be performed in accordance with the ASME code and **will** satisfy the specific requirements of SA578 Level II supplementary requirement S6. A report of these repair areas **will** be provided to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
Where load bearing or stressed attachments are to be welded directly to the cladding, Fabricator **will** ultrasonically check the area concerned for lack of bond prior to welding.

**Chemical Analysis Spot Checks**
A single production check analysis **must** be performed on weld run-off tabs taken from each welding process employed. A spot check analysis **will** be performed on production welds of equipment such as pressure vessels, heat exchangers, and certain proprietary items, and this requirement **will also** apply to non-pressure retaining equipment. The weld(s) to be checked **will** be selected at random by the Inspector or designated representative.
Where results of chemical analysis show that a specific weldment falls outside the approved specification or code requirements, Supplier **will** promptly advise Aker Kvaerner Chemetics in writing of his proposed course of action. Supplier's proposed course of action **must** include details of any additional testing necessary to fully define the extent of the non-conformance. All repairs and retests related to non-conforming chemical analysis **will** be to Supplier’s account.

**Radiographic Testing (RT) Sequence and Locations**
Carbon steel weld radiography of each seam **will** be complete before starting to weld the stainless side of the seam. Where more than one welder or welding operator has been used, spot radiography **will** be performed in such a manner that the welding of each welder or welding operator can be examined. Junctions between circumferential and longitudinal seams **will** be considered as preferred locations for spot radiography.
