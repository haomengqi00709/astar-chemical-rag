# Building Evacuation Plan

This page outlines the general procedures and responsibilities for all employees and visitors during a building evacuation.

**Evacuation Triggers**
In the event that the building needs to be evacuated, the fire alarm **will** ring. Instructions may also be issued over the public address system.

**Evacuation Meeting Area**
Employees **are to** proceed to the meeting area through the nearest emergency exit. In the event of an emergency evacuation of 1818 Cornwall, employees **are to** meet on the large grassy area to the East of the building (Peace Park), along Burrard Street.

**Employee Responsibilities**
All employees have specific responsibilities during a building evacuation. Designated employees, such as Floor Wardens (see [[0-PRC-8010_roles_and_responsibilities_floor_warden]]) and Door Monitors (see [[0-PRC-8010_roles_and_responsibilities_door_monitor]]), have additional responsibilities.
*   Employees **must** know the nearest exit for their location at all times. EXIT signs identify evacuation directions and stairwell locations.
*   Emergency response personnel **will be** those people wearing an orange vest and orange hard hat.

**During Evacuation**
When the FIRE ALARM sounds, employees and visitors **must** leave the building immediately via a designated fire exit.
*   **DO NOT** use the elevators to evacuate.
*   **You must** walk quickly, but **DO NOT RUN**, to the nearest exit stairwell.
*   **You must** evacuate to the "Evacuation Meeting" location.
*   **CAUTION** – Open doors slowly and cautiously. If the exit door is hot or the stairway is smokey, use an alternate emergency exit.
*   **You must** keep to the right in corridors/halls.
*   **You must** hold the handrail when going down the stairs.
*   **You must** merge alternately when two lines meet at various floor landings to keep lines moving.
*   **DO NOT** turn back at any time for any reason.
*   **You must** keep conversation to a minimum.
*   All instructions from emergency personnel **must be** followed. Co-workers and visitors **must** follow during a building evacuation.

**Post-Evacuation at Rally Point**
*   **DO NOT** loiter around the building after an emergency evacuation.
*   **You must** move away from the building as quickly as possible.
*   **You must** go to the "Evacuation Meeting" location.
*   **DO NOT** go to the parking garage and drive away during an emergency evacuation.
*   **You must** gather with your work group at the rally location.
*   **You must** stay with your work group at the rally location until notified it is safe to return to the building.
*   Upon reaching the rally location, **you must** check to see the faces of co-workers known to be in the office.
*   **You must** report anyone found to be missing to the emergency staff.

**Assistance for Individuals with Mobility Challenges**
Those who cannot manage stairs **should** proceed to the stairwell exit and **should** seek assistance from an able person to assist in getting to the exit. The Door Monitor **will** provide assistance or arrange for assistance to anyone requiring assistance descending the stairs.
For specific floor details, refer to [[0-PRC-8010_floor_specific_evacuation_details]].
