# HSE Site Postings Overview

This procedure, documented as 0-PRC-8004, outlines the minimum requirements for job site postings to ensure vital Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) information is effectively communicated to all workers. These requirements are applicable across all Aker Kvaerner Chemetics operations, including design offices, shops, research facilities, and [[hse_construction_sites]], unless specifically noted otherwise.

The **Designated Health and Safety Manager** for each worksite is responsible for ensuring that all required informational items are posted. A copy of each notice described **shall** be posted at every Aker Kvaerner Chemetics work establishment.

**Posting Requirements:**
*   Notices **shall** be prominently displayed.
*   Notices should preferably be placed on a bulletin board.
*   Notices should be located at one or more locations that provide the greatest exposure to all employees, ensuring maximum visibility and accessibility.

For details on specific mandatory postings, refer to [[mandatory_hse_site_postings]]. For general worksite safety rules, see [[worksite_safety_rules_general]].

This specific document (0-PRC-8004) did not receive a new revision number for this name change, and the changes related to the name change have not been explicitly marked within the document itself.
