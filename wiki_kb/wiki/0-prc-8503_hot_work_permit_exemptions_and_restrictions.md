# Hot Work Permit Exemptions and Restrictions (0-PRC-8503)

While the Hot Work Permit procedure (Document ID [[0-prc-8503_hot_work_permit_procedure_overview]]) is broadly applicable, certain conditions allow for exemptions or necessitate immediate work stoppage and permit cancellation. Understanding these is crucial for compliance and safety.

**Areas Without Exemption:**
There is no exception to this regulation for all process, utilities, and chemical storage areas. Hot work in these areas **must** always adhere to the full permit procedure.

**Conditions for Exemption:**
A Hot Work Permit may be exempted under the following specific conditions:
*   Hot work conducted in a laydown yard or an isolated area with zero risk of exposure to or ignition of combustible/flammable materials.
*   Hot work performed in new construction areas with zero risk of exposure to or ignition of combustible/flammable vapour or solid materials.
*   Hot work carried out in areas formally approved as fabrication shops by the company or client. The [[0-prc-8503_hot_work_permit_roles_and_responsibilities|Commissioning HSE Manager]] **shall** sign the approval for these fabrication shops.

These exemptions are applicable to activities within [[hse_commissioning_sites]] and [[hse_construction_sites]].

**Work Stoppage and Permit Re-validation:**
*   Any [[0-prc-8503_hot_work_permit_roles_and_responsibilities|competent authority]] may stop the work covered by this permit at any time if they consider the prevailing conditions or work methods to be unsafe.
*   Anyone stopping work **must** remove the site copy of the permit and return it to the [[0-prc-8503_hot_work_permit_roles_and_responsibilities|Commissioning HSE Manager]], providing reasons for their action.
*   The [[0-prc-8503_hot_work_permit_roles_and_responsibilities|Commissioning HSE Manager]] **will** visit the work site and decide whether or not the permit should be re-validated.
*   Only the [[0-prc-8503_hot_work_permit_roles_and_responsibilities|Commissioning HSE Manager]] can permit a restart after work has been stopped.

**Automatic Permit Cancellation:**
A Hot Work Permit is automatically cancelled under the following circumstances:
*   In the event of a flammable release in the vicinity of the hot work.
*   In the event of the sounding of a fire or gas alarm.

In such cases, all hot work operations **must** cease immediately, and emergency procedures **shall** be followed.

**Restrictions on Exemptions:**
*   There is no exception to this regulation for all process, utilities, and chemical storage areas.
*   The Commissioning HSE Manager signs approval for fabrication shops.

**Work Stoppage and Permit Cancellation:**
*   Anyone stopping work must remove the site copy of the permit and return it to the Commissioning HSE Manager, giving reasons for their action.
*   The Commissioning HSE Manager will visit the work site and decide whether or not the permit should be re-validated.
*   Only the Commissioning HSE Manager can permit a restart after work has been stopped.
