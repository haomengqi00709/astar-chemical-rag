# Inspection and Non-Destructive Examination (NDE) for UNS S30815 Fabrications

This page details the requirements for inspection and Non-Destructive Examination (NDE) of UNS S30815 welded fabrications. It covers inspector access, acceptance criteria, and procedures for various NDE methods, including repair protocols and cost implications.

**Inspector Access and Authority**
*   The Inspector **WILL** have free access to the Vendor's work at all reasonable times. This access extends to Subvendor's work if sublet by approval.
*   The Inspector **WILL** be at liberty to inspect the work during fabrication at any stage and to reject any material or workmanship not conforming to this Standard or the applicable code.
*   Inspection by Chemetics does not relieve the Fabricator of responsibility for providing materials, components, and workmanship in accordance with this Standard, engineering design, and the applicable code.

**Consumable Verification**
*   Supplier **MUST** make available to Chemetics' inspector copies of chemical certification for batches of electrodes and wire coils used.
*   If electrodes or wire cannot be identified to Chemetics' satisfaction, samples **WILL** be removed for chemical analysis. Costs for chemical analysis of unidentified electrodes or wire **WILL** be to the supplier's account.

**Penetrant Testing (PT) Examination**
*   All welds **WILL** be PT examined 100%, as far as is possible, unless otherwise specified in the purchase order, approved drawings, or specifications/codes.
*   The PT exam **WILL** be carried out in accordance with a written procedure.
*   The limit of acceptability for PT exam **WILL** satisfy [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels]], Appendix VIII.
*   All linear indications **WILL** be considered unacceptable for PT exam.
*   All excavated areas **WILL** be weld built-up flush with the surface of the base material using the welding procedure previously approved by Chemetics for the main welding details.
*   Weld repair surfaces **WILL** be PT examined per the above requirements.
*   Chemetics reserves the right to accept or reject any doubtful or questionable indications.

**Radiographic Testing (RT) Examination**
*   Where specified on Chemetics' Engineering drawings, items fabricated in accordance with this Standard and the [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels]] Code **WILL** be spot radiographed.
*   Spot radiography **WILL** be in accordance with Para. UW-52(b) of [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels]]. This paragraph defines the minimum extent, standards, evaluation, and retests for spot radiographic examination.
*   For piping fabricated in accordance with [[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping]] Codes and this Standard, the minimum standard of radiography **WILL** be 5 percent random radiography, as defined in Para. 341.4.1(b) of [[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping]].
*   RT Exam **WILL** be carried out in accordance with a written procedure and by personnel qualified to appropriate national standards.
*   Where additional spot radiography is required per UW-52 Sub-para. (d)(2) of [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels]], the costs of this radiography, all repair costs, and re-radiography costs **WILL** be to the fabricator's account.
*   Where more than one welder or welding operator has been used, spot radiography **WILL** be performed to examine the welding of each welder or welding operator.
*   Junctions between circumferential and longitudinal seams **WILL** be considered as preferred locations for spot radiography.

**Inspector Access and Authority**
*   The Inspector will have free access to Vendor's work at all reasonable times, and to Subvendor's work if sublet by approval, at all reasonable times.
*   The Inspector will be at liberty to inspect the work during fabrication at any stage and to reject any material or workmanship not conforming to this Standard or the applicable code.
*   Inspection by [Company Name] does not relieve the Fabricator of responsibility for providing materials, components, and workmanship in accordance with this Standard, engineering design, and the applicable code.

**Material Verification by Inspector**
*   The Supplier must make available to the purchaser's inspector copies of chemical certification for batches of electrodes and wire coils used.
*   If electrodes or wire cannot be identified to [Company Name]'s satisfaction, samples will be removed for chemical analysis. Costs for chemical analysis of unidentified electrodes or wire will be to the supplier's account.

**Penetrant Testing (PT) Examination**
*   All welds will be PT examined 100%, as far as is possible, unless otherwise specified in the purchase order, approved drawings, specifications/codes.
*   The PT exam will be carried out in accordance with a written procedure.
*   The limit of acceptability for PT exam will satisfy ASME Code Section VIII, Division 1, Appendix VIII. All linear indications will be considered unacceptable for PT exam.
*   All excavated areas will be weld built-up flush with the surface of the base material, using the welding procedure previously approved by [Company Name] for the main welding details. Weld repair surfaces will be PT examined per the above requirements.
*   [Company Name] reserves the right to accept or reject any doubtful or questionable indications.

**Radiographic Testing (RT) Examination**
*   Where specified on [Company Name]'s Engineering drawings, items fabricated in accordance with this Standard and the ASME Code will be spot radiographed.
*   Spot radiography will be in accordance with Para. UW-52(b) of ASME Code Section VIII, Division 1, which defines the minimum extent, standards, evaluation, and retests for Spot Radiographic Examination.
*   For piping fabricated in accordance with ASME Codes and this Standard, the minimum standard of radiography will be 5 percent random radiography, as defined in Para. 341.4.1(b) of ASME B31.3.
*   RT Exam will be carried out in accordance with a written procedure and by personnel qualified to appropriate national standards.
*   Where additional spot radiography is required per UW-52 Sub-para. (d)(2), costs of this radiography, all repair costs, and re-radiography costs will be to the fabricator's account.
*   Where more than one welder or welding operator has been used, spot radiography will be performed to examine the welding of each welder or welding operator.
*   Junctions between circumferential and longitudinal seams will be considered as preferred locations for spot radiography.
