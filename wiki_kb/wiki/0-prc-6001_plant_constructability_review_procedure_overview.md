# Plant Constructability Review Procedure Overview (0-PRC-6001)

This document, Procedure 0-PRC-6001, provides essential guidelines for conducting Plant Constructability Reviews. These reviews are primarily intended for major projects that involve an Engineering, Procurement, and Construction (EPC) scope of supply.

While specifically tailored for large-scale EPC projects, this procedure is adaptable. With minor modifications, it **may** be utilized for smaller projects or those with alternative scopes of supply, ensuring that constructability considerations are integrated across various project types.

**Revision History Notes:**
*   A name change was implemented during Revision 0. No new revision number was used for this change, and changes were not marked.
*   Another name change was implemented in 2005, noted in Revision 1 commentary. Similar to the Revision 0 change, no new revision number was used, and changes were not marked.

For details on the purpose, timing, and methodology of these reviews, refer to [[plant_constructability_review_objectives_and_timing]] and [[plant_constructability_review_process_and_methodology]]. The specific roles and responsibilities involved are outlined in [[plant_constructability_review_roles_and_responsibilities]].
