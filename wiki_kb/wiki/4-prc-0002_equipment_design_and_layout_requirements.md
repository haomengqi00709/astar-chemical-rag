# Equipment Design and Layout General Requirements (4-PRC-0002)

This document, 4-PRC-0002, outlines the general requirements for the design of equipment and layout for the [type of] Plant within the [project name] Project. The design, construction, and operation of the Plant **must** comply with the Statutory Requirements of [city, province, country]. The latest edition of [applicable North American; or list others as applicable] Codes and Standards **shall** be fully complied with in the design, specification, and manufacture of all mechanical equipment.

The [[0-prc-7003_project_quality_management_plan_overview|Quality Plan (0-PRC-7003)]] **will** apply to all aspects of equipment design and layout. Additionally, the [[equipment_design_and_specification_4_prc_0003|Equipment Design and Specification (4-PRC-0003)]] procedure and the [[general_equipment_requirements_specification|General Equipment Requirements (4-SPC-0001)]] specification **will** also apply.

Other design criteria are deliberately not referenced in Section 4.1 of this document; the [[0-prc-7003_project_quality_management_plan_overview|Quality Plan (0-PRC-7003)]] provides references to all other design criteria.
