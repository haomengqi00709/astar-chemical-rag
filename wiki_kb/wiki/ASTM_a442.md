# ASTM A442

ASTM A442 is a standard specification for carbon steel plates, with improved transition properties, for pressure vessels. This specification covers carbon steel plates intended for fusion-welded pressure vessels.

**Key Characteristics:**
*   **Material:** Carbon steel.
*   **Application:** Primarily for pressure vessel applications where improved transition properties (resistance to brittle fracture at lower temperatures) are desired.
*   **Form:** Plates.

**Application in Tank Fabrication:**
*   Materials used in the manufacture of sulphuric acid storage tanks may reference ASTM A442 for plate components, particularly where specific temperature and pressure conditions require enhanced material properties.
