# Respiratory Protection Requirements

This page details the requirements for respiratory protection in environments with airborne hazards, including specific types of respirators and their limitations, as outlined in Document ID 0-PRC-8015.

**Mandatory Respiratory Mask Use**
Respiratory masks **are required** to be worn at all times when employees are working with:
*   Inorganic fibre insulation material.
*   Any other material that may release an airborne hazardous substance.
*   Exposure to welding and cutting fumes.

**Respirator Integrity and Fit**
Facial hair, piercings, or any other facial feature that may compromise the seal between the face and respirator **are prohibited**. Respirators **must not** be modified in any way. Damaged respirators **are to be replaced** immediately.

**3M N95 Particulate Respirators**
The 3M N95 Particulate Respirators are provided by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for all employees and visitors. Standards for the use of 3M N95 Particulate Respirators are set out in CSA standard Z94.4.
*   3M N95 Particulate Respirators **are not** for use when dealing with oxygen levels below 19.5%.
*   3M N95 Particulate Respirators **are not** for use when dealing with contaminants in excess of 10 times the permissible exposure limit.

**Advanced Respiratory Protection**
In cases where particulate respirators are not adequate, oxygen-providing respirators **must** be used. 3M models 8212 and 8214 are particularly recommended for protection against welding fumes.

**Selection Guidance**
Selection of respiratory protection masks is beyond the scope of this document. For selection of masks to protect against specific fumes, consult with the [[hse_roles_and_responsibilities_company_wide|HSE Manager]]. A complete list of CSA approved respiratory devices can be found on the NIOSH Certified Equipment List.

For general PPE guidelines, refer to [[0-prc-8015_personal_protective_equipment_policy]].
