# Construction Subcontract Labour Rates

The Subcontractor **must** complete the [[0-LST-6X12_construction_subcontract_labour_rates]] document. This document is critical for deriving a total cost per hour worked for each classification of personnel engaged in Providing the Works under the subcontract.

Labour rates **shall** comprehensively include the following components:
*   Wages, salaries, premiums, and allowances paid directly to employees.
*   Payments made on behalf of employees to government or other agencies, such as income taxes, social security taxes, pension contributions, benefit premiums, and union dues.
*   Payments made directly to employees or on behalf of employees for travel to the site.
*   Payments made directly to employees or on behalf of employees for living expenses while working at the site.
*   Redistribution of paid non-productive time, including meal breaks, public holidays, leave or vacation, sick leave, and contingency for inclement weather.
*   Consumable supplies such as small tools and consumables, personal protective equipment, and office supplies.

These detailed labour rates form the basis for calculating both [[construction_subcontract_direct_work_costs]] and [[construction_subcontract_indirect_work_costs]] related to personnel.
