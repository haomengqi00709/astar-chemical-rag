# Project Risk Analysis (0-LST-2006)

The Project Risk Analysis (0-LST-2006) is a critical document for identifying and mitigating potential project risks.

**Preparation and Approval Process:**
*   **Preparation:** Prepared by the Project Manager.
*   **Checking:** Checked by the Director of Projects.
*   **Approval:** Approved by the President.

**Basis and Content:**
The foundation for 0-LST-2006 is the original analysis of contract risks and mitigating strategies that were considered during the preparation of estimates and proposals.

The document **must** have the following appended:
*   Final contractual requirements.
*   Any modifications or additions to mitigating strategies deemed necessary after the initial analysis.
