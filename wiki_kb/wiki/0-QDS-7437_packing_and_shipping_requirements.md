# Field Erected CS Tank - Shop Packing and Shipping Requirements

For the "Field Erected CS Tank - Shop" component, specific requirements are in place for the packing and shipping of fabricated items to ensure their safe delivery and integrity.

**Packing Requirements:**
*   **Packaging:** Detailed packaging procedures are required to be included in the Vendor's Inspection and Test (I & T) Plan. This plan **must** outline how components will be protected during transit.
*   **Packing List:** A Packing List is required as **AP** (As Prepared). This list **must** accurately detail all items included in the shipment.
*   Chemetics surveillance for the Packing List is designated as **'Complete'**. This indicates that Chemetics will perform a full review and verification of the packing list to ensure accuracy and completeness.

Adherence to these requirements is essential for logistics and to prevent damage to the components during transportation.
