# Changes to Business Travel Authorizations

Should a traveller need to make changes to an original travel authorization or booking, a specific procedure **must** be followed to ensure proper record-keeping, cost control, and adherence to policy.

**Initiating Changes:**
*   The primary method for making changes to an original travel authorization is to contact the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics designated travel agency.
*   When communicating with the travel agency for changes, the traveller **must** copy their Aker Kvaerner Chemetics travel coordinator on all correspondence.

**Cost and Justification for Changes:**
*   If the traveller is requesting a change to a restricted ticket, they **should** determine the potential cost of that change.
*   For any changes, particularly those involving restricted tickets, the traveller **must** ensure that the change constitutes a justifiable business expense.
*   It is important to note that changes to travel bookings often incur costs that can exceed the price of the original journey. Therefore, all requested changes **should** be carefully evaluated in the context of "behaving as though you were paying for the journey yourself," a core principle of the 11.1 PR 2 Group Travel Procedure (see [[travel_policy_compliance_and_deviations]]).

If a requested change results in a deviation from the approved travel policy or previously authorized deviations, the traveller **must** follow the re-authorization process outlined in [[travel_policy_compliance_and_deviations]] and [[travel_authorization_approvals_0_frm_9002]], which includes revising the [[travel_authorization_form_details_0_frm_9002]] and obtaining new approvals.

**Initiating Changes:**
*   The primary method for making changes to an original travel authorization or booking is to contact the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics designated travel agency.
*   When making changes to original travel authorisation, **always** copy your Aker Kvaerner Chemetics travel coordinator.

**Changes to Itinerary and Restricted Tickets:**
*   If the traveller wants a change to the itinerary that does not comply with the original authorization or agency guidelines, the traveller **must** revise the Travel Request Form, explicitly stating the deviation requested. The revised Travel Request Form **must** then be re-authorised.
*   If the traveller is requesting a change to a restricted ticket, they should determine the cost of that change and **must** ensure that it is a justifiable business expense.
*   Be aware that changes often cost more than the original journey. Changes should be evaluated in the context of “behaving as though you were paying for the journey yourself”.
