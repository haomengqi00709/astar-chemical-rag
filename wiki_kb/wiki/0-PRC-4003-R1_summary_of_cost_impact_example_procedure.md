# Summary of Cost Impact (Example) Procedure

This document, identified as Appendix II, is a Procedure with the document ID **0-PRC-4003-R1**. Its official title is "Summary of Cost Impact (Example) — Procedure". This specific document serves as an illustrative example, providing a template and guidance for how to effectively summarize and present the financial implications of various project changes or unforeseen events.

The core function of a "Summary of Cost Impact" is to quantify the monetary effects of deviations from the original [[project_budgeting_0_dat_4001|project budget]] and [[project_schedule_and_milestones]] within a project. This example procedure is designed to ensure consistency and clarity in reporting these impacts across all projects within the firm. It is an essential component of robust [[project_cost_control]] and [[scope_change_control_0_prc_4003]] mechanisms.

**Purpose and Application**
The primary purpose of this example procedure is to demonstrate the methodology for assessing and documenting the financial consequences of:
*   [[construction_subcontract_compensation_events|Compensation Events]]
*   [[engineering_subcontract_compensation_events|Engineering Subcontract Compensation Events]]
*   [[change_order_documentation_requirements_0_chg_22|Change Orders]]
*   Other project modifications that incur additional costs or savings.

By following the structure and principles outlined in this example, project teams can accurately estimate and communicate the financial standing of a project, facilitating informed decision-making by management. This is particularly critical for managing [[commercial_terms_for_construction_subcontract_0_prc_6003|commercial terms]] and mitigating potential [[delay_damages_commercial_terms]].

**Integration with Project Procedures**
This example procedure is intrinsically linked to the broader [[0-prc-4003_cost_code_procedure_overview|Cost Code Procedure (0-PRC-4003)]], which establishes the overall framework for cost management. It also supports the firm's [[change_management_roles_and_responsibilities_0_prc_4003|change management process]], ensuring that all cost impacts are systematically identified, evaluated, and documented.
