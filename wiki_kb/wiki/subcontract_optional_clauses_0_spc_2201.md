# Professional Services Subcontract (0-SPC-2201) Optional Clauses (X3, X4, X5, X8)

Professional Services Subcontract 0-SPC-2201 includes several optional clauses (Options X3, X4, X5, X8) that are applicable only if explicitly referenced in Section 5 of the Agreement. These clauses provide flexibility for specific project requirements.

**Option X3: Multiple Currencies**
*   **Applicability:** Only if referenced in Section 5 of the Agreement.
*   **Provisions:** Under this option, the Subcontractor may be paid in currencies other than the primary currency of this Subcontract for work listed in the [[subcontract_definitions_0_spc_2201|Subcontract Data]]. Exchange rates are used for conversion from the Subcontract's primary currency to other currencies.
*   **Limitations:** Payments to the Subcontractor in non-Subcontract currencies **must not** exceed the maximum amounts specified in the Subcontract Data. Any payment exceeding these maximum amounts in non-Subcontract currencies **must** be made in the primary currency of this Subcontract.
*   **Related Topics:** [[advanced_payment_terms]]

**Option X4: Parent Company Guarantee**
*   **Applicability:** Only if referenced in Section 5 of the Agreement.
*   **Provisions:** The Subcontractor **must** provide Aker Kvaerner Chemetics with a guarantee from its parent company for the Subcontractor’s performance. The form of this guarantee is set out in the [[subcontract_definitions_0_spc_2201|Scope]].
*   **Timing:** If the parent company guarantee was not provided by the [[subcontract_definitions_0_spc_2201|Subcontract Date]], it **must** be submitted within four weeks of the Subcontract Date.
*   **Related Topics:** [[performance_security_and_guarantees]]

**Option X5: Sectional Completion**
*   **Applicability:** Only if referenced in Section 5 of the Agreement.
*   **Provisions:** Unless explicitly stated as applying to the whole of the services, each reference and clause pertaining to the services, [[subcontract_definitions_0_spc_2201|Completion]], and [[subcontract_definitions_0_spc_2201|Completion Date]] applies to either the entire services or any specific section of the services. This allows for phased completion and handover of different parts of the project.

**Option X8: Collateral Warranty Agreements**
*   **Applicability:** Only if referenced in Section 5 of the Agreement.
*   **Provisions:** The Subcontractor **must** enter into the specified collateral warranty agreements. These agreements typically provide direct contractual rights to third parties (e.g., the ultimate client) who are not direct parties to the main subcontract but have an interest in the quality and performance of the services.

These optional clauses provide a framework for tailoring the subcontract to complex project structures, international payment arrangements, and specific risk management strategies.

**Option X3: Multiple Currencies**
*   **Applicability:** Only if Referenced in Section 5 of the Agreement.
*   **Provisions:** The Subcontractor is paid in currencies other than the currency of this Subcontract for the work listed in the *Subcontract Data*. Exchange rates are used to convert from the currency of this Subcontract to other currencies. Payments to the Subcontractor in currencies other than the currency of this Subcontract must not exceed the maximum amounts stated in the *Subcontract Data*. Any excess payment beyond the maximum amounts in non-Subcontract currencies is paid in the currency of this Subcontract.

**Option X4: Parent Company Guarantee**
*   **Applicability:** Only if Referenced in Section 5 of the Agreement.
*   **Provisions:** The Subcontractor must give to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics a guarantee by the Subcontractor’s parent company of the Subcontractor’s performance in the form set out in the Scope. If the parent company guarantee was not given by the Subcontract Date, it must be given within four weeks of the Subcontract Date.

**Option X5: Sectional Completion**
*   **Applicability:** Only if Referenced in Section 5 of the Agreement.
*   **Provisions:** Unless stated as the whole of the services, each reference and clause relevant to the services, Completion, and Completion Date applies to either the whole of the services or any section of the services.

**Option X8: Collateral Warranty Agreements**
*   **Applicability:** Only if Referenced in Section 5 of the Agreement.
*   **Provisions:** The Subcontractor must enter into the collateral warranty agreements.
