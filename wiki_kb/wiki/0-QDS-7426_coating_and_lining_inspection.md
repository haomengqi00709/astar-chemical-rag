# Conveyors & Mills Coating and Lining Inspection

This page outlines the requirements for coating and lining inspections for Conveyors & Mills, as specified in Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7426. These inspections are critical for ensuring the protective integrity and longevity of the equipment.

**External Coating / Lining Inspection**
*   **Requirement:** External Coating / Lining Inspection is required as FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package). This documentation **shall** confirm that external coatings and linings meet the specified quality and application standards.
*   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Chemetics surveillance for External Coating / Lining Inspection is designated as Witness. A Chemetics representative **shall** be present to observe and verify the inspection process and results.

**Internal Coating / Lining Inspection**
*   **Scope Inclusion:** Internal Coating / Lining Inspection is included within the overall scope of vendor quality assurance for Conveyors & Mills. Specific documentation requirements for internal coatings/linings will be detailed as part of the Quality Assurance Documentation Package.

For general vendor quality assurance requirements, refer to [[0-QDS-7426_vendor_quality_assurance_requirements]]. For definitions of quality record statuses, refer to [[0-QDS-7426_quality_record_definitions]].
