# Water Batching

Water batching is a critical pre-commissioning activity in chemical engineering projects. It involves filling process equipment and piping systems with water to perform initial cleaning, leak testing, and functional checks before introducing process fluids. This procedure helps to remove debris, verify system integrity, and prepare the equipment for safe and efficient operation.

In the context of the [[9-PRC-0004_electrical_field_checkout_procedure_overview|Electrical Field Checkout Procedure (9-PRC-0004)]], electrical systems are verified to be ready for water batching, indicating that the electrical components associated with pumps, valves, and instrumentation required for water circulation and testing are fully installed and functional. This readiness ensures that the mechanical systems can be properly tested and cleaned without electrical impediments.
