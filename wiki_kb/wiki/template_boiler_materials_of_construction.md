# Template Boiler Materials of Construction

This page specifies the materials of construction for various components of a boiler system, as outlined in the [[template_boiler_datasheet_administration|Template Boiler Datasheet (4-PDS-XXXX-R1)]]. Proper [[material_selection]] is critical for ensuring the longevity, safety, and performance of the boiler under its specified [[template_boiler_process_design_conditions|process design conditions]].

**Boiler Internal and Structural Components**
*   **Tubes:** The primary heat transfer surfaces where water is heated and converted into steam.
*   **Downcomer:** Pipes that carry cooler, denser water from the steam drum to the mud drum or lower headers in a watertube boiler.
*   **Tubesheet:** A plate that supports and separates the tubes in a heat exchanger or boiler.
*   **Ferrules:** Short tubes or rings inserted into tubesheets to protect tube ends or facilitate tube replacement.
*   **Shell:** The main pressure-containing vessel of a firetube boiler or the outer casing of a watertube boiler.
*   **Refractory Clips:** Fasteners used to secure refractory materials within the boiler furnace.
*   **Tube Support:** Structures designed to hold boiler tubes in place and prevent sagging or vibration.
*   **Insulating Fire Brick:** A type of refractory brick used for insulation in high-temperature applications.
*   **Steam Drum:** A pressure vessel at the top of a watertube boiler where steam separates from water.
*   **Insulating Castable:** A refractory material that can be cast into shape and used for insulation.
*   **Riser Pipe:** Pipes that carry the steam-water mixture from the heated tubes to the steam drum in a watertube boiler.
