# Engineering Subcontractor Quality Plan: Management and Control

As a mandatory element of the [[engineering_subcontractor_quality_assurance_0_spc_7004|Engineering Subcontractor Quality Plan]], the Subcontractor **must** clearly define the management and control aspects of quality-related activities.

The Quality Plan **shall** include the following:

*   **Responsibility Identification:** The Subcontractor **must** identify the specific groups or persons responsible for the establishment and effective implementation of the Quality Plan.
*   **Compliance Verification:** The Subcontractor **must** also identify the groups or persons responsible for the verification of compliance with the Quality Plan.
*   **Organizational Structure:** A documented organizational structure for all activities affecting quality **must** be provided. This structure clarifies how different parts of the organization contribute to quality.
*   **Functional Responsibilities:** Documented functional responsibilities for all activities affecting quality **must** be established. This ensures that each role understands its specific duties related to quality.
*   **Levels of Authority:** Documented levels of authority for activities affecting quality **must** be defined. This clarifies who has the power to make decisions and approve actions related to quality.
*   **Reporting Lines:** Clear reporting lines for activities affecting quality **must** be documented. This ensures effective communication and escalation channels for quality issues.

These documented elements are crucial for establishing accountability and ensuring a structured approach to quality management throughout the duration of the Subcontract.
