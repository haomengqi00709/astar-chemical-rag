# Chemetics Surveillance Level: CS Tubing

This page specifies the level of surveillance Chemetics will conduct for the supply of carbon steel tubing, as defined in Quality Data Sheet (QDS) 0-QDS-7428. This surveillance ensures that vendor activities and supplied materials meet the stringent quality standards required for the project.

**Surveillance Details:**

*   Surveillance by Chemetics will be to **Level 1.0**.
*   The specific surveillance activities and their scope **shall** be as set out in this Quality Data Sheet (0-QDS-7428).

This surveillance level is a key component of the overall quality assurance strategy (associated with statement 2.0). Level 1.0 surveillance typically indicates a high degree of involvement from Chemetics' quality personnel, including witnessing critical manufacturing and testing activities. This proactive approach allows for early identification and resolution of potential quality issues, minimizing risks and ensuring compliance throughout the production process.

Chemetics' surveillance activities are designed to complement the vendor's own quality control measures, providing an independent verification of adherence to specifications. This includes monitoring of [[0-QDS-7428_material_verification_and_certifications|material verification]], [[0-QDS-7428_pressure_and_leak_testing|pressure and leak testing]], [[0-QDS-7428_non_destructive_examination_requirements|non-destructive examination]], and [[0-QDS-7428_final_inspections|final inspections]]. For the overarching vendor quality assurance requirements, refer to [[0-QDS-7428_vendor_quality_assurance_requirements]].
