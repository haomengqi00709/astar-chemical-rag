# Agitator Vendor Quality Assurance Requirements

For agitator equipment covered by Quality Data Sheet **0-QDS-7418**, the vendor **must** prepare a Quality Plan. This Quality Plan **must** be in accordance with the requirements outlined in [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment]].

The vendor's overall Quality Assurance program **must** comply with the specific requirements detailed in this document, **0-QDS-7418**, as well as the general vendor quality assurance specifications provided in [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment]].

Data available in SQL: 0-SPC-7001

[[vendor_quality_assurance]] provides a broader context for vendor quality expectations.

The vendor's overall Quality Assurance program **must** comply with the requirements of **0-QDS-7418** and **0-SPC-7001: Vendor Quality Assurance**.
