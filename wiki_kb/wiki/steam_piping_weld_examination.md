# Steam Piping Weld Examination

This page details the mandatory weld examination requirements for steam piping, as specified in Specification 4-SPC-0069. These examinations are crucial for verifying the quality and integrity of welded joints.

**Weld Examination Standards**
*   Weld examination **shall** be performed in accordance with [[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping|ASME B31.3]].
*   All butt welds are required to be examined by 100 % RT (Radiographic Testing).

For general non-destructive examination principles, refer to [[ASME_section_v_non_destructive_examination]].

*   Weld examination **shall** be performed in accordance with ASME B 31.3.
