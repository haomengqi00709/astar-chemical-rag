# Purchase Order Governance and Acceptance

This page outlines the legal framework and acceptance criteria for Purchase Orders (PO) issued by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Purchase Order Documentation:**
The documents that collectively form the Purchase Order are explicitly listed within the Purchase Order itself.

**Acceptance of Conditions:**
The Vendor signifies acceptance of the Purchase Order's conditions through one of two actions:
1.  Acknowledging receipt of the Purchase Order.
2.  Commencing to [[purchase_order_definitions#To Provide the Goods and Services|Provide the Goods and Services]].

**Governing Law and International Conventions:**
*   This Purchase Order is governed by the law of **British Columbia, Canada**.
*   The **Incoterms 2000** apply to the Purchase Order, providing internationally recognized rules for the interpretation of trade terms.
*   The **United Nations Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods (CISG)** does **not** apply to this Purchase Order.
