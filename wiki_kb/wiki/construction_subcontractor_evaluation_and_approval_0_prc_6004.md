# Construction Subcontractor Evaluation and Approval (0-PRC-6004)

This document, 0-PRC-6004, outlines the procedure for the evaluation and approval of construction subcontractors. It ensures that selected subcontractors possess the necessary capabilities, experience, and resources to execute the construction work effectively and safely.

Subcontractors will be selected in accordance with this procedure.
