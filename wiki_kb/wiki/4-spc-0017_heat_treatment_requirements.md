# Heat Treatment Requirements for 6% Moly SS Welding (4-SPC-0017)

This section outlines the requirements for preheat, interpass temperature, and postweld heat treatment (PWHT) for 6 percent Moly austenitic stainless steel fabrications under Standard **4-SPC-0017**.

**Preheat**
Preheat is not required. However, when adverse weather conditions give rise to condensation or wetness, the material **must** be dried prior to welding by heating to a temperature just sufficient to prevent condensation.

**Heat Input and Interpass Temperature**
Heat input during welding should be kept to a minimum. The interpass temperature should not exceed 250° F.

**Postweld Heat Treatment (PWHT)**
Postweld Heat Treatment (PWHT) is seldom required for 6 percent Moly austenitic stainless steel. In the event that PWHT is specified, the Fabricator **must** submit their procedure in writing for approval prior to the commencement of work. The PWHT procedure **must** include information on the number and location of thermocouples and supports.

**Minimizing Heat Affected Zone Effects**
Auxiliary methods, such as water spray on the opposite surface to that being welded, **will** be used to minimize heat affected zone effects. These effects could potentially impact the corrosion performance of the material.
