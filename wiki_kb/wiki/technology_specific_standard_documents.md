# Technology-Specific Standard Documents

Within the firm's document management framework, it is recognized that standard documents may require technology-specific versions to address unique process or equipment requirements. The approach to numbering these variations is designed to maintain consistency while allowing for necessary differentiation, as detailed in [[document_numbering_procedure_0_prc_0002]].

**Identification Number Consistency:**
Technology-specific versions of standard documents shall be given the same [[identification_number_rules|identification number]] as their generic counterparts. This ensures that all documents pertaining to a particular standard subject, regardless of the specific technology they address, are grouped under a common identifier.

**Differentiation by Title Wording:**
To distinguish between different technology-specific versions that share the same identification number, the title wording of the document shall be used. The title must clearly indicate the specific technology or application to which the document applies.

**Example:**
If a standard specification for pumps has a generic identification number `0001`, a technology-specific version for "High-Pressure Centrifugal Pumps for Acid Service" would retain `0001` as its identification number, but its title would clearly specify its unique application.

This method allows for the efficient management of a diverse range of standard documents, ensuring that both commonality and specificity are accurately reflected in the document's metadata and title.
