# Clad Steel Weld Quality and Finishing

Achieving superior weld quality and finish is paramount for austenitic stainless clad steel fabrications. This section outlines the requirements for weld appearance, integrity, and post-weld cleaning.

**Weld Pass Control**
*   No increase in thickness of stainless steel root pass(es) is allowed, by comparison with the analysed test piece.
*   No decrease in the number of stainless steel layers is allowed, by comparison with the analysed test piece.

**Arc Strikes and Tungsten Contamination**
Every effort **will** be made to confine arc strikes to the surface on which weld metal is to be deposited. If an arc strike occurs outside the weld metal deposition area, the spot **will** require to be ground, PT examined, and repaired, if necessary. Burn marks from grounding clamps **will** be treated as arc strikes.

If a tungsten electrode accidentally makes contact with the cladding, the section of weld and the tip of the electrode **should** be examined. Where tungsten loss has occurred, the tungsten pick-up in the weld **will** be ground out, and the electrode **will** be reground to the correct profile before re-starting.

**Inter-Run Cleaning and Fusion**
Each run of weld metal **will** be thoroughly cleaned before the next run is deposited. All slag and foreign matter **will** be removed. On re-starting, care **will** be taken to ensure proper fusion with the weld previously deposited.

**Peening Restrictions**
Peening of weld runs, other than light peening for slag removal, is **not permitted** unless specifically shown as a requirement on the "Approved for Construction" fabrication drawings.

**Weld Appearance and Integrity**
*   Each weld **will** be uniform in width and size throughout its full length.
*   Each layer of welding **will** be smooth and free of slag, inclusions, cracks, porosity, lack of fusion, and undercut, except to the extent permitted in the referenced codes.
*   The cover pass **will** be free of coarse ripples, irregular surface, non-uniform bead patterns, high crown, and deep ridges or valleys between beads.
*   Buttwelds **will** be slightly convex, of uniform height, and have full penetration.
*   Crevices **will not** be permitted on the process side of the weld.
*   As far as is practicable, fillet welds **will** be deposited in not less than two layers. Fillet weld surfaces **will** be free from marked irregularities, and their dimensions **will** be in accordance with the approved drawing.

**Grinding and Tooling**
Grinding or gouging **will not** be carried out in such a manner as to reduce the adjacent metal thickness tolerance for clad plate. Grinding wheels **will** be iron-free and of the resin bonded alumina or silica type. Wire brushes or chipping tools **will** be made of austenitic stainless steel and **must not** have been previously used to clean surfaces other than stainless steels. All internal weld surfaces **will** be ground smooth.

**Final Cleaning**
On completion of work, items **must** be cleaned internally and externally. All fabrication markings, oxidation, weld spatter, etc., **must** be removed prior to shipment.

**Arc Strikes and Contamination**
Every effort **will** be made to confine arc strikes to the surface on which weld metal is to be deposited. If an arc strike occurs outside the weld metal deposition area, the spot **will** require to be ground, PT examined, and repaired if necessary. Burn marks from grounding clamps **will** be treated as arc strikes.

**Inter-Pass Cleaning and Fusion**
Each run of weld metal **will** be thoroughly cleaned before the next run is deposited. All slag and foreign matter **will** be removed before the next run is deposited. On re-starting, care **will** be taken to ensure proper fusion with the weld previously deposited.

**Tungsten Contamination**
If a tungsten electrode accidentally makes contact with the cladding, the section of weld and the tip of the electrode should be examined. Where tungsten loss has occurred, the tungsten pick-up in the weld **will** be ground out, and the electrode **will** be reground to the correct profile before re-starting.

**Peening**
Peening of weld runs other than light peening for slag removal is not permitted unless specifically shown as a requirement on the "Approved for Construction" fabrication drawings.

**Weld Appearance and Integrity**
Each weld **will** be uniform in width and size throughout its full length. Each layer of welding **will** be smooth and free of slag, inclusions, cracks, porosity, lack of fusion, and undercut, except to the extent permitted in the referenced codes.
The cover pass **will** be free of coarse ripples, irregular surface, non-uniform bead patterns, high crown, and deep ridges or valleys between beads.
Buttwelds **will** be slightly convex, of uniform height, and have full penetration. Crevices **will not** be permitted on the process side of the weld.
As far as is practicable, fillet welds **will** be deposited in not less than two layers. Fillet weld surfaces **will** be free from marked irregularities. Dimensions of fillet welds **will** be in accordance with the approved drawing.

**Weld Deposits and Special Passes**
Weld deposits **must** be performed in not less than three (3) layers and as shown in [[4-SPC-0016_table_2_manual_metallic_arc_welding_combinations]]. In special cases, interior welds **must** be given a final pass of type 904L SS weld filler, with a 2.5 mm minimum coverage.

**Grinding and Cleaning Tools**
Grinding or gouging **will not** be carried out in such a manner as to reduce the adjacent metal thickness tolerance for clad plate. Grinding wheels **will** be iron-free and of the resin bonded alumina or silica type. Wire brushes or chipping tools **will** be made of austenitic stainless steel and **must not** have been previously used to clean surfaces other than stainless steels. All internal weld surfaces **will** be ground smooth.
