# Field Inspection Checklist Sign-off Requirements

For all field inspection checklists, including the [[4-FIC-XX18-R0_gas_gas_exchanger_field_inspection_checklist_template]], specific sign-off procedures **must** be followed to ensure proper documentation and accountability.

The checklist requires signatures, names, and dates from the following parties:

*   **Aker Kvaerner Chemetics:** The designated representative from [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_company_details]] **must** provide their signature, printed name, and the date of sign-off.
*   **Sub Contractor:** The authorized representative of the Sub Contractor **must** provide their signature, printed name, and the date of sign-off. This ensures their acknowledgment of the inspection findings and compliance with [[subcontractor_document_standards]].
*   **Client:** The Client's representative **must** provide their signature, printed name, and the date of sign-off, indicating their acceptance or acknowledgment of the inspection results.

These sign-off requirements are integral to the [[document_approval_and_revision_process]] and define the [[roles_and_documentation_management]] for quality assurance activities.
