# Electrical Power Specifications

Electrical power specifications are critical for the design, procurement, and installation of all electrical equipment and systems within the Chemetics project. These specifications, including voltage, phase, and frequency requirements, are detailed in [[site_design_conditions_1_dst_0001]].

The following categories of electrical power consumers require specific voltage (V), phase (Ph), and frequency (Hz) specifications:

*   **Motor Power < 0.5 kW (Below 0.5 HP):** Requires V, Ph, Hz specification. This category covers small motors used in various auxiliary equipment.
*   **Motor Power 0.5 to 200 kW:** Requires V, Ph, Hz specification. This range is associated with "Above 200 HP" (Note: 200 kW is approximately 268 HP, indicating a potential discrepancy in the original source's HP conversion or categorization for this specific fact). This category covers a broad range of industrial motors.
*   **Motor Power > 200 kW:** Requires V, Ph, Hz specification. This applies to large-scale motors used for major equipment.
*   **Lighting and Utility Power:** Requires V, Ph, Hz specification. This covers general lighting, receptacles, and other utility power needs throughout the plant.
*   **Instrument Control Power:** Requires V, Ph, Hz specification. Dedicated power supply for control systems and instrumentation.
*   **Instrument Power:** Requires V, Ph, Hz specification. Power for field instruments and other low-power instrumentation.

These detailed electrical power specifications ensure compatibility, safety, and efficient operation of all electrical components throughout the plant. Adherence to these specifications is crucial for the overall [[electrical_design_criteria]] and successful project execution.

The following electrical power specifications, including voltage (V), phase (Ph), and frequency (Hz), are required:
*   Motor Power < 0.5 kW (Below 0.5 HP)
*   Motor Power 0.5 to 200 kW (Above 200 HP)
*   Motor Power > 200 kW
*   Lighting and Utility Power
*   Instrument Control Power
*   Instrument Power
