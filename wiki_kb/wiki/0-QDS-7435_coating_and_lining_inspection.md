# Coating and Lining Inspection for Carbon Steel Tank (0-QDS-7435)

The Coating/Lining Inspection for the Carbon Steel Tank, as specified in Item 19.0 of QDS 0-QDS-7435, is essential to ensure the protective layers meet the required quality and performance standards.

**Scope of Inspection:**
The External Coating/Lining Inspection **shall** include, but is not limited to, the Primer and Top Coat applications. This ensures that all layers of the protective system are thoroughly checked for adherence, thickness, and absence of defects.

**Chemetics Witnessing and Holding Requirements:**
*   Chemetics **must** witness the External Coating/Lining Inspection.
*   Chemetics **must** hold the External Coating/Lining Inspection.
*   The External Coating/Lining Inspection record is required as FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package) as per [[0-QDS-7435_quality_record_definitions]].

For a comprehensive overview of all inspection and documentation requirements, refer to [[0-QDS-7435_inspection_and_documentation_requirements]].
