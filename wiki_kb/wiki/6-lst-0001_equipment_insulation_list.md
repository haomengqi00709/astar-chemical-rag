# Equipment Insulation List (6-LST-0001)

The **Equipment Insulation List**, identified as **6-LST-0001**, is a crucial document used to summarize the insulation requirements for various pieces of equipment within the plant. This list serves as a central reference for insulation scope and details.

The Equipment Insulation List **shall** be issued to provide a concise summary of equipment insulation. It is prepared by the Mechanical discipline, checked by Process, and approved by the Project Engineer.

**Scope and Detail:**
It is important to note that the Equipment Insulation List **shall not** attempt to completely describe the geometry of the insulation. For detailed descriptions of insulation geometry, [[equipment_outline_drawings]] will serve this purpose. This distinction ensures that each document type focuses on its primary function, maintaining clarity and avoiding redundancy.

This list is an integral part of the [[6-prc-0002_insulation_and_heat_tracing_documentation_procedure]], facilitating efficient procurement and installation planning by consolidating key insulation data. The Equipment Insulation List provides a high-level overview, enabling project teams to quickly identify which equipment requires insulation and the general type of insulation specified. This summary is vital for initial planning, cost estimation, and communication with procurement and construction teams, ensuring that all necessary insulation components are accounted for.
