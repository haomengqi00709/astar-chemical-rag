# Field Instrument Design and Specification (8-PRC-0003)

This document, 8-PRC-0003, details the discipline assignments for the preparation, checking, and approval of field instrument design and specifications for the Project. It outlines the roles and responsibilities for developing all field instrumentation documentation.

This procedure ensures that all field instrument design documents, including data sheets, hook-up drawings, and specifications, are developed, reviewed, and approved in a structured manner, adhering to the [[instrumentation_and_control_design_criteria_8_prc_0002]] and overall project quality standards.
