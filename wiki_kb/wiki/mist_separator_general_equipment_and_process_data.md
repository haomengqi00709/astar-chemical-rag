# Mist Separator General Equipment and Process Data

This section of the Mist Separator Datasheet (Document ID [[mist_separator_datasheet_administration]]) specifies the fundamental operational and design parameters for the mist separator. These details are critical for defining the equipment's role within the process and guiding its selection and configuration.

**Equipment Configuration**
*   **Number of Units:** This data field specifies the total quantity of mist separator units required for the application.
*   **Unit Operating:** This data field indicates how many of the total units are intended to be in active operation at any given time.
*   **Installed Spare:** This data field denotes the number of units that are installed but kept as spares, providing redundancy and ensuring continuous operation in case of a primary unit failure.

**Design Basis and Operational Flexibility**
*   **Cell Description:** A data field providing a brief description or identifier for the specific cell or section where the mist separator is installed.
*   **Design Basis:** This data field outlines the primary criteria or conditions upon which the mist separator's design is founded. This could include specific process conditions, regulatory requirements, or performance targets.
*   **Turndown Range:** The specified turndown range for the mist separator is **40% to 110% of design flow**. This indicates the operational flexibility of the unit, allowing it to perform effectively across a range of gas flow rates, from significantly below to slightly above its nominal design capacity.

**Equipment Type and Orientation**
The datasheet allows for the specification of various mist separator types and their physical orientation:
*   **Equipment Type Options:** The selection of the mist separator technology is crucial for effective separation. Available options include:
    *   Cyclone
    *   Interception Candle
    *   Chevron
    *   Single Mesh Pad
    *   Dual Mesh Pad
    *   Impaction Candle
    *   Brownian Diffusion Candle
    *   Other (for specialized or custom designs)
*   **Orientation Options:** The physical installation orientation impacts design, space requirements, and performance. Options include:
    *   Vertical
    *   Horizontal
    *   Candle Standing
    *   Candle Hanging

These parameters collectively define the basic framework for the mist separator's design and operational envelope, linking directly to the detailed process conditions and performance expectations outlined in other sections of the datasheet. For further details on process design, refer to [[mist_separator_process_design_conditions]].

**General Equipment Data Fields**
The following data fields are used to define the equipment and its operational context:
*   **Tag No.:** A unique identifier for the specific equipment unit.
*   **Location:** The physical location or area where the mist separator will be installed.
*   **Number of Units:** Specifies the total quantity of mist separator units.
*   **Unit Operating:** Indicates the number of units actively in operation.
*   **Installed Spare:** Specifies the number of spare units installed for redundancy.
*   **Cell Description:** A descriptive field for individual cells or sections within the separator.
*   **Design Basis:** Outlines the fundamental assumptions and criteria used for the equipment's design.

**Equipment Type and Orientation**
*   **Equipment Type Options:** The datasheet allows for selection from various mist separator technologies, including: Cyclone, Interception Candle, Chevron, Single Mesh Pad, Dual Mesh Pad, Impaction Candle, Brownian Diffusion Candle, or Other (for custom or unlisted types).
*   **Orientation Options:** Specifies the physical orientation of the mist separator, with options such as: Vertical, Horizontal, Candle Standing, or Candle Hanging.
