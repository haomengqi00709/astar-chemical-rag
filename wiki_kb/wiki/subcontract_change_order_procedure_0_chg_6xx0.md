# Subcontract Change Order Procedure (0-CHG-6XX0)

A formal notification, in the form of a Subcontract Change Order (template [[0_chg_6_document_overview]]), is issued when a quotation for a [[construction_subcontract_compensation_events]] is accepted. Change Orders are also issued by the Subcontract Administrator if a Subcontractor's quotation is deemed unacceptable, based on Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' own assessment of the compensation event.

**Preparation and Numbering:**
*   **Preparation:** Change Orders **must** be prepared by the Subcontract Administrator.
*   **Numbering:** Change Orders **must** be numbered as 0-CHG-6X1YYY.
    *   "X" represents the equivalent digit used for the Subcontract Agreement (refer to [[construction_subcontract_agreement_procedure_0_prc_6002]]).
    *   "YYY" represents a sequential addendum.
    *   Further details on document numbering can be found in [[0_prc_0002_table_1]].

**Publication and Approval:**
*   **Status:** Change Orders **must** be published with the status “Approved for Construction” (refer to [[publishing_project_documents]]).
*   **Checking:** Change Orders **must** be checked by the Manager of Construction.
*   **Approval:** Change Orders **must** be approved by the Project Manager.
*   **Signing:** The approved Change Order **must** be printed and physically signed by the same authorised signatory who signed the original Subcontract.

**Issuance:**
The Subcontract Administrator **must** forward the signed and authorised paper copy of the Change Order to [[control_of_project_documents_0_prc_0006]] for official issuance to the Subcontractor.
