# Acid Tower Distributor Final Inspections (0-QDS-7413)

This page details the final inspection requirements for the Acid Tower Distributor, as specified in Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7413. These inspections are crucial to verify the equipment's conformity to design specifications and quality standards before shipment.

**Final Inspection Activities:**
The final inspection process includes the following checks, each **requiring** Chemetics Vancouver surveillance (Witness):
*   **Dimensional Checks:** Verification of all critical dimensions against approved drawings.
*   **Contour & Finish of Welds Checks:** Examination of weld contours and finishes to ensure they meet quality and aesthetic standards.
*   **Surfaces Free from Defects Checks:** Inspection of all surfaces for any defects, imperfections, or damage.
*   **Cleanliness Checks:** Verification that the equipment is clean and free from debris, contaminants, or foreign materials.
*   **Piece Identification Marking Checks:** Confirmation that all components are correctly identified and marked according to specifications.
*   **Review QA Documentation:** A comprehensive review of the entire [[0-QDS-7413_quality_record_definitions|Quality Assurance Documentation Package]] to ensure completeness and compliance.

These rigorous final inspections, with Chemetics Vancouver's witness surveillance, are a critical part of the [[0-QDS-7413_vendor_quality_assurance_requirements|vendor quality assurance requirements]] for the Acid Tower Distributor.
