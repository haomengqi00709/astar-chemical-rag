# Vendor Quality Assurance Requirements for 0-QDS-7802

This page outlines the mandatory quality assurance requirements for vendors supplying equipment and materials covered by Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7802. Adherence to these requirements is crucial for ensuring the quality and compliance of delivered items.

**Mandatory Requirements:**
*   The Vendor **shall** prepare a Quality Plan. This plan **must** be in accordance with the specifications detailed in [[0-SPC-7002_vendor_quality_assurance]].
*   The Vendor's overall Quality Assurance program **shall** comply with all stipulations set forth in this Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7802.
*   The Vendor's Quality Assurance program **shall** also comply with the general requirements specified in [[0-SPC-7002_vendor_quality_assurance]].

**Quality Plan Note:**
*   If a Quality Plan is not required for a specific project, the corresponding cells in the Quality Data Sheet regarding the Quality Plan **should** be deleted before the document is published for project use.

These requirements ensure that all supplied instruments and related components meet the stringent quality standards of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. For details on specific inspection and documentation activities, refer to [[0-QDS-7802_inspection_and_documentation_requirements]]. Information on Chemetics' surveillance levels can be found at [[0-QDS-7802_chemetics_surveillance_level]].

**Quality Plan Usage Note:**
*   If a Quality Plan is not required for a specific project, the corresponding cells in the document regarding the Quality Plan should be deleted.
