# Shop Visit Safety Procedure (0-PRC-8103)

This procedure, identified as 0-PRC-8103, outlines the minimum Health and Safety precautions for Aker Kvaerner Chemetics employees during visits to vendor workshops or fabricating shops.

Aker Kvaerner Chemetics is deeply committed to fostering a safe and healthy workplace for all its employees and to conducting all operations in an environmentally responsible manner. This commitment forms the foundation of the company's [[hse_management_and_employee_responsibilities]] and [[company_hse_objectives_and_goals]].

**Employee Responsibilities**
All Aker Kvaerner Chemetics employees **must** cooperate and contribute actively towards the success of the company's Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) program. Employees **must** perform their jobs in the safest manner prescribed and conduct themselves in a way that enhances their personal safety and the safety of others.

Prior to any shop visit, employees **must** familiarize themselves with the specific safety requirements of the facility. This includes understanding and adhering to all local safety procedures in addition to company standards. Employees may need to carry their own safety equipment to ensure compliance with both company and vendor requirements.

This procedure is complemented by other critical safety documents, including the [[personal_protective_equipment_policy]] (Standard-0-8015) and the [[0-PRC-8502-R1_confined_space_entry]] procedure.

Source document: 0-PRC-8103

Aker Kvaerner Chemetics is also committed to conducting operations in an environmentally sound manner.

**Employee Expectations**
All Aker Kvaerner Chemetics employees are expected to:
*   Cooperate and contribute toward the success of the company's Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) program.
*   Perform jobs in the safest manner prescribed.
*   Conduct themselves in a way that enhances personal safety and that of others.

**Pre-Visit Preparation**
Employees must find out shop safety requirements in advance of their visit.
