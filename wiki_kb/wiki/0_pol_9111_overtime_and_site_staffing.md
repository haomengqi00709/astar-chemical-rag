# 0-POL-9111 Overtime and Site Staffing

Procedure 0-POL-9111 details the guidelines for overtime compensation and the management of staffing levels for employees on site assignments.

**Overtime Compensation:**
Employees [[will]] be eligible for overtime pay while on site. This compensation [[will]] be in accordance with the provisions outlined in [[compensation_of_overtime_0_pol_9113_r2]].
All overtime hours worked [[must]] be approved by the Project Manager or their designated representative prior to being worked.

**Statutory Holidays:**
Employees on short term assignments [[will]] be eligible for paid local statutory holidays observed at the site location.
If an employee works on-site on a home office statutory holiday, they [[will]] be able to bank 8.25 hours in lieu. This provision for banking hours in lieu does not apply if the employee took an equivalent local statutory holiday during the assignment period.

**Site Staffing Levels and Hours of Work:**
Department Managers, Project Managers, Construction Managers, and Commissioning Managers are [[required]] to ensure that appropriate staff levels are maintained on site.
Staff levels on site [[must]] be maintained in accordance with the Site Hours of Work outlined in [[0_pol_9106_site_work_hours_and_rest_periods]]. This ensures compliance with established work schedules and rest periods.
Any exceptions to the prescribed staff levels or hours of work [[must]] be approved in advance by the President or a Director of The Company. This ensures that deviations from standard practice are properly authorized at a senior level.
