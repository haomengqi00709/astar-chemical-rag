# Liquid Ring Compressor Package Mechanical Design Requirements

This page outlines the mechanical design requirements for the Liquid Ring Compressor Package, as specified in Datasheet 4-PKG-XXXX-R1. Adherence to these requirements is critical for the structural integrity, safety, and operational reliability of the equipment.

**Mechanical Design Specification**
The primary specification governing the mechanical design requirements is **4-SPC-1068/0001**. This document provides comprehensive details and standards that **must** be followed. For general mechanical design requirements, refer to [[general_mechanical_design_requirements_0_spc_0010]].

**Key Mechanical Design Considerations**
The following aspects **must** be addressed in the mechanical design:

*   **Equipment Location:** The intended physical location of the compressor package within the plant, which influences environmental factors and accessibility.
*   **Site Conditions and Utilities:** Details regarding the specific site conditions (e.g., seismic, wind loads, ambient temperature range) and available utilities (e.g., power, cooling water, instrument air). For more information on site-specific requirements, see [[liquid_ring_compressor_site_and_fabrication_requirements]].
*   **Electrical Area Classification:** The classification of the area where the equipment will be installed, according to relevant electrical codes (e.g., Class I, Division 1, Group D). This dictates the type of electrical components and wiring required. Refer to [[electrical_area_classification_requirements]] for detailed guidelines.
*   **Dimensional Limit:** Any specific dimensional constraints or limits that the compressor package **must** adhere to for installation and maintenance purposes.

These requirements ensure that the liquid ring compressor package is designed to safely and effectively operate within its intended environment.
