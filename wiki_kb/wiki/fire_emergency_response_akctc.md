# Fire Emergency Response - AKCTC

This page outlines the procedures for responding to fire emergencies, including fire alarms, minor fires, major fires, and fire drill protocols at the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Technology Centre (AKCTC). For general company-wide fire response, refer to [[0-PRC-8010_fire_emergency_response]].

**Fire Alarm Procedures:**
In the event of a fire alarm:
*   If it is safe to do so, turn off heat to any on-going experiments.
*   If necessary, quench on-going experiments with water.
*   If not necessary, turn off water supplies.
*   Lower sashes of fume hoods.
*   Leave via the nearest exit and report to the [[evacuation_procedures_akctc#Emergency_Assembly_Point]].
*   Remain in the area until the “all-clear” signal is given, or unless otherwise instructed by the [[emergency_coordinator_roles_and_responsibilities_akctc]].
*   If you have an outside visitor during a fire alarm, you are responsible for their safety. Visitors **shall** accompany their host to the Emergency Assembly Point.

**Minor Fire Response:**
In the event of a minor fire:
*   AKCTC personnel **shall** alert other personnel in the area.
*   If alone, do not attempt to put out a minor fire.
*   Shut off electrical equipment in use as quickly as possible.
*   Leave all work in progress in a safe condition.
*   With the help of others, attack a minor fire as soon as possible. Get the nearest fire extinguisher (or hose) and attempt to put out the fire.
*   Chlorate fires should be extinguished with water only. Dry chemical extinguishers are deemed suitable for all other types of laboratory fires.

**Major Fire Response:**
In the event of a major fire:
*   AKCTC personnel **shall** activate the fire siren and/or announce the fire through the intercom.
*   AKCTC personnel **shall** Dial 9-1-1.
*   Immediately turn off power to the lab from the outside electrical room.
*   If possible, assist injured personnel.
*   Close all doors to confine/contain the fire before leaving the building.
*   Report to the [[evacuation_procedures_akctc#Emergency_Assembly_Point]].
*   Remain in the area until the “all clear” signal is given, or unless otherwise instructed.

**Fire Drills:**
*   Semi-annual Fire Drills will be conducted to test personnel’s readiness for a real emergency situation.
*   The designated Fire Warden (or his deputy) will organize the fire drill, have the responsibility for conducting the head count, and for calling off the fire drill.
*   Personnel and immediate neighbours will be advised about the date (but not the time) of the fire drill.
*   An audible means (e.g., fire horn) will be used to initiate the fire drill.
*   The date of the fire drill and results **shall** be recorded in the minutes of the following AKCTC Safety Meeting.

**Fire Extinguisher Maintenance and Location:**
*   Multipurpose dry chemical fire extinguishers are available throughout the building.
*   Fire extinguisher locations are properly marked.
*   Fire extinguishers are inspected and serviced yearly by an outside company, Vancouver Fire and Security. A service tag is placed on each extinguisher to mark the next service date.
*   Impromptu Burnaby Fire Hall inspections help ensure a preventative fire safety attitude is maintained year-round.
*   Staff is responsible for familiarizing themselves with the location of fire extinguishers.

For an overview of emergency response at AKCTC, refer to [[0-PRC-8010_office_emergency_response_overview]].
