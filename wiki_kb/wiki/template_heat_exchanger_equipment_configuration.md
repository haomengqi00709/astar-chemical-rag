# Template Heat Exchanger Equipment Configuration

This page details the various configuration options for heat exchangers as specified in document 4-PDS-XXXX-R2. These choices define the fundamental design and operational characteristics of the equipment.

**Equipment Orientation:**
Heat exchangers can be configured in two primary orientations:
*   **Horizontal**
*   **Vertical**

**Flow Configuration:**
The relative direction of fluid flow within the exchanger can be:
*   **Cocurrent:** Fluids flow in the same direction.
*   **Countercurrent:** Fluids flow in opposite directions.

**Exchanger Type:**
The datasheet template accommodates several common heat exchanger types:
*   **Shell and Tube:** A widely used type, often detailed further in [[shell_and_tube_heat_exchanger_design_and_process_requirements]].
*   **Plate and Frame:** Characterized by plates forming channels for heat transfer, with more details in [[plate_heat_exchanger_mechanical_design_requirements]].
*   **Radial Flow:** A specialized type where fluid flows radially.
*   **Other:** For any other specific or proprietary heat exchanger designs.

**Fluid Side Assignment:**
For exchangers with distinct fluid paths (e.g., Shell and Tube), the hot fluid can be designated to flow:
*   On the **Shell Side**
*   On the **Tube Side**

**Bundle Removability (for Shell and Tube Exchangers):**
The design may allow for the removal of the tube bundle for maintenance or cleaning:
*   **Yes:** The bundle is designed to be removable.
*   **No:** The bundle is fixed within the shell.
