# Nickel/Copper Welding Specification Overview (4-SPC-0021)

This document, identified as **4-SPC-0021**, outlines the workmanship and quality requirements for nickel/copper (UNS N04400 / Monel 400) welded fabrications. It is specifically applicable to fabrications purchased by or on behalf of [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_company_details|Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Inc.]] when referenced in purchase orders and other related documents.

The scope of this specification covers welding and other fabrication requirements for a variety of equipment, including vessels, heat exchangers, tanks, piping, and miscellaneous welded items constructed from nickel/copper material.

**Key Definitions:**
*   **Codes, etc.:** Shall mean the latest edition ruling at the date of contract.
*   **Agreement in Writing:** Refers to a formal submission in writing by letter, not merely by inclusion in approval drawings.
*   **Inspector:** Denotes the [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_company_details|Aker Kvaerner Chemetics]] Quality Assurance Inspector or any other Authorized Representative.

**Document Administration:**
This document is a direct copy of the original specification ES44/W09, titled "General Requirements for Equipment Welding Nickel/Copper Alloy." It **shall** be used in accordance with [[0-prc-0001_standard_document_management_procedure|0-PRC-0001 Approval and Use of Standards]].

There are plans to replace this document in the future with a new version that fully complies with the requirements of the [[0_prc_0004_document_style_and_format|0-PRC-0004 style of Textual Documents]].

**Revision History and Name Change:**
A name change conversion, referring to "To Name Change Conversion," has been implemented. A 2005 Name Change Conversion was also implemented. For these conversions, no new revision number has been used, and the changes, including formatting and numbering fixes, have not been marked within the document.
