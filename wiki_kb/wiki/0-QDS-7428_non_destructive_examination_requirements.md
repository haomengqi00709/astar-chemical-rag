# Non-Destructive Examination Requirements: CS Tubing

This page outlines the mandatory Non-Destructive Examination (NDE) requirements for carbon steel tubing, as specified in Quality Data Sheet (QDS) 0-QDS-7428. NDE techniques are essential for detecting internal or surface flaws without damaging the material.

**Mandatory NDE Requirements:**

*   All Non-Destructive Examination **must** be performed in accordance with **ASTM Code** or an approved equivalent standard. This ensures consistency and reliability in defect detection.
*   **Dye Penetrant - Tube Ends Testing:**
    *   This testing is required to be included in the Vendor's Inspection & Test (I & T) Plan (designated as [[0-QDS-7428_quality_record_definitions|FP]]).
    *   Chemetics will **Witness**, **Hold**, and **Complete** surveillance for Dye Penetrant - Tube ends testing. The remark for this activity is "*", indicating specific attention.
*   **Dye Penetrant - Cut Lengths Testing:**
    *   This testing is required to be included in the Vendor's I & T Plan (designated as [[0-QDS-7428_quality_record_definitions|FP]]).
    *   Chemetics will **Witness**, **Hold**, and **Complete** surveillance for Dye Penetrant - Cut Lengths testing. The remark for this activity is "*", indicating specific attention.

Dye penetrant testing is a widely used NDE method for detecting surface-breaking defects in non-porous materials. For carbon steel tubing, it is particularly important for examining tube ends and cut lengths, which are critical areas prone to manufacturing flaws or damage during processing. The "Witness," "Hold," and "Complete" surveillance levels by Chemetics underscore the importance of these examinations. "Witness" ensures Chemetics' presence during the test, "Hold" prevents further processing until Chemetics approves the results, and "Complete" signifies the finalization of Chemetics' oversight for this NDE activity. This comprehensive approach to NDE is vital for ensuring the integrity and reliability of the carbon steel tubing in its intended application.
