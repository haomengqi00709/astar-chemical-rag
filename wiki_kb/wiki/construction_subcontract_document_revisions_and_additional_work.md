# Handling Document Revisions and Additional Work in Construction Subcontracts

This procedure outlines the specific steps for managing changes to construction subcontracts arising from revised documents or the introduction of additional work, both of which may constitute [[construction_subcontract_compensation_events]].

**1. Handling Revised Documents:**
The Subcontract Administrator **must** monitor the [[control_of_project_documents_0_prc_0006]] database for revisions to documents that have already been issued to the Subcontractor with an "Approved for Construction" status.

*   **Issuance of Revised Documents:** The Subcontract Administrator **must** periodically direct Document Control to issue these revised documents to the Subcontractor, the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Site Manager, and the Owner.
*   **Notice to Subcontractor:** Upon the issuance of revised documents, the Subcontract Administrator **must** issue a formal notice to the Subcontractor, with a copy to the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Site Manager. An example of the required notice text is:
    "Our communication KCI-SUB-[024] contains Document Transmittal [KC-SC-0047]. The documents referenced by the Document Transmittal contain revisions to the Works Information and may be a compensation event. The Subcontractor **must** immediately proceed with the work shown on the revised documents. The Subcontractor **must** submit a notice and quotation, including a revised schedule, for any compensation event resulting from the revised documents in accordance with Sections 27 and 28 of [[standard_conditions_for_construction_subcontract_0_spc_6001]]."

**2. Handling Additional Work:**
Additional work is managed separately from revisions to existing documents. It may be possible to assign additional work to a different subcontractor or to agree upon a different Completion Date than that originally stipulated for the subcontract.

*   **Issuance of Additional Work Documents:** The Subcontract Administrator **must** direct Document Control to issue documents describing the additional work to the Subcontractor and the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Site Manager.
*   **Notice to Subcontractor:** Upon the issuance of documents for additional work, the Subcontract Administrator **must** issue a formal notice to the Subcontractor, with a copy to the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Site Manager. An example of the required notice text is:
    "Our communication KCI-SUB-[028] contains Document Transmittal [KC-SC-0053]. The documents referenced by the Document Transmittal contain a proposed revision to the Works Information. The Subcontractor **must** submit a quotation, including a revised schedule, for the work shown on the documents in accordance with Section 28 of [[standard_conditions_for_construction_subcontract_0_spc_6001]]."
