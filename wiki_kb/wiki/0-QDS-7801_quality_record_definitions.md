# Quality Record Definitions for 0-QDS-7801

This Quality Data Sheet (QDS) 0-QDS-7801 defines specific requirements for quality records and the associated Chemetics surveillance levels. Understanding these definitions is crucial for compliance and proper documentation.

**Quality Record Definitions**
*   **AP (As Prepared):** This designation for a Quality Record indicates that the record is required "As Prepared" by the vendor.
*   **FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package):** This designation means the Quality Record **must** be "Included in the Quality Assurance Documentation Package" submitted by the vendor.

**Quality Record Requirements and Chemetics Surveillance**
The following table outlines specific quality records, their required type, and the corresponding Chemetics surveillance level.

| Item or Activity        | Quality Record required: | Surveillance by Chemetics |
| :---------------------- | :----------------------- | :------------------------ |
| Certificate of Compliance | FP                       | Complete                  |
| Packing List            | AP                       | Complete                  |

The "Quality Record" table structure includes columns for "Item or Activity", "Quality Record required:", "Surveillance by Chemetics", and "Remarks". The "Surveillance by Chemetics" column further includes sub-columns for "Required in Vendor I & T Plan", "Witness", "Hold", and "Complete".
