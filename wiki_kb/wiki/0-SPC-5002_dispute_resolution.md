# Purchase Order Dispute Resolution

This page outlines the process for resolving disputes that may arise under or in connection with the Purchase Order (PO).

**Initial Resolution Attempt:**
*   Any dispute arising under or in connection with this Purchase Order **must**, if not resolved within **4 weeks**, be submitted to appropriate senior representatives of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Vendor.
*   These senior representatives **will** then hold a conference within a further **2 weeks** to attempt to resolve the dispute in good faith.

**Arbitration:**
*   If the meeting of the senior representatives fails to resolve the dispute, either Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or the Vendor may refer the dispute for final resolution by arbitration.
*   The arbitration **must** be administered by the British Columbia International Commercial Arbitration Centre pursuant to its Rules.
*   Arbitration proceedings **must not** be initiated before [[purchase_order_definitions#Delivery or Deliver|Delivery]] of the whole of the [[purchase_order_definitions#The Goods and Services|Goods and Services]] or earlier termination of the Purchase Order.
*   One arbitrator **will** be appointed in accordance with the Rules of the British Columbia International Commercial Arbitration Centre.
*   The designated place of arbitration is **Vancouver, British Columbia**.
*   The powers of the arbitrator include the authority to review and revise any action or inaction of the [[purchase_order_roles_and_responsibilities#Project Buyer|Project Buyer]] related to the dispute.
