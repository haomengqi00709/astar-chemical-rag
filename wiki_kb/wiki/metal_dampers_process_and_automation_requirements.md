# Metal Dampers Process and Automation Requirements

This page consolidates the process and automation requirements for metal dampers, as specified in various project documents. These requirements are crucial for the design, fabrication, and operation of the dampers.

**Process Requirements (5-LST-[00XX]):**
The 5-LST-[00XX] document, titled "Metal Dampers," provides detailed process requirements. This includes, but is not limited to:
*   The diameter and thickness of the duct into which the damper body will be welded. This information is vital for ensuring proper fit-up and structural integration of the damper.
*   Maximum flow rates and maximum inlet pressures. These parameters are fundamental for the Vendor to guarantee the leakage rate of the damper, as specified in [[damper_testing_and_inspection]].

Data available in SQL: 5-LST-[00XX]

**Automation Requirements (5-LST-[00XX] and 8-DST-[5XXX]):**
Automation requirements for metal dampers are defined in two primary documents:
*   **5-LST-[00XX]: Metal Dampers:** This document outlines general automation requirements related to the damper's function within the overall process. Data available in SQL: 5-LST-[00XX]
*   **8-DST-[5XXX]: Damper Automation:** This document provides more detailed specifications regarding the automation aspects of the damper. This may include control logic, instrumentation interfaces, and specific operational sequences. Data available in SQL: 8-DST-[5XXX]

These documents collectively ensure that the metal butterfly dampers are designed and supplied to meet both the operational process demands and the integrated control system requirements.
