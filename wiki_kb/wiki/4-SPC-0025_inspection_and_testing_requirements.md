# Inspection and Testing Requirements for High Silicon Austenitic Stainless Steel (4-SPC-0025)

**Inspection Access and Authority**
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Quality Assurance Inspectors or authorized representatives **shall** have free access to the Vendor's work, and to the Subvendor's work (if sublet by approval), at all reasonable times. Inspectors **shall** be at liberty to inspect the work during fabrication at any stage of manufacture and to reject any material or workmanship that does not conform to this specification and/or the applicable code. Inspection by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or its delegated representative **shall not** relieve the fabricator or erector of responsibility in accordance with the requirements of this Standard, engineering design, and the applicable code.

**Material and Consumable Verification**
Supplier **must** make available to purchaser's inspector copies of chemical certification for electrodes used. If electrodes cannot be identified to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' satisfaction, samples **shall** be removed for chemical analysis and other related identity tests. Costs for such tests due to unidentified electrodes **shall** be to the supplier's account. Where the fabricator cannot identify the weld material, chemical analysis and other related tests **shall** be performed on production welds. The weld(s) to be checked **shall** be selected by agreement between the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' representative and the fabricator.

**Corrosion Testing**
Corrosion testing for Intergranular attack **shall** be carried out in accordance with ASTM A262 Practice "C", involving 5 x 48 hours in boiling 65% nitric acid. This testing **shall** have been performed on all heats of parent material, with mill test certification provided.

Production weld corrosion tests **shall** be performed to ensure that production welds by different welders and welds after heat treatment are acceptable for nitric acid service. Each vessel exchanger **shall** be represented by a production test sample removed from a longitudinal seam, covering each welder used. For heat-treated and water-quenched equipment, production test samples **shall** be removed after the water quench operation.

Acceptance criteria for weight loss:
*   No individual weight loss **shall** exceed 1 g/m²h.
*   No individual weight loss **shall** exceed 1.1 mm/yr.

Weight loss results in excess of 1 g/m²h **shall** require the vessel or component to be solution annealed and water quenched, and the corrosion tests repeated. Alternatively, the weld(s) and heat-affected zone(s) **shall** be completely cut out, seams rewelded, and corrosion tests repeated. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **shall** be advised of the extent of the problem and proposed corrective action for excess weight loss. Where possible, corrosion tests **shall** be performed on weld run-off tabs taken from longitudinal seams. Corrosion test coupons **shall** be carefully identified with the welders' symbol and checked prior to leaving the fabrication shop. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics reserves the right to independently test the corrosion resistance of any weld joint.

**Non-Destructive Examination (NDE)**
Items fabricated in accordance with this specification **shall** be 100% dye penetrant tested on all welds, including root passes and dissimilar welds. The PT exam **shall** be carried out in accordance with a written procedure. The limit of acceptability for PT **shall** satisfy [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels]] Section VIII, Division I, Appendix 8. Linear indications **shall** be considered unacceptable for PT.

All butt welds **shall** be spot radiographed in accordance with Para. UW-52 of [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels]] Section VIII, Division I of the ASME Code. Junctions between circumferential and longitudinal seams **shall** be considered preferred locations for spot radiography.

Piping radiography requirements:
*   Piping operating at wall temperatures greater than 70°C **shall** be 100 percent radiographed.
*   Heat-treated and water-quenched piping **shall** be 100 percent radiographed.
*   Piping operating at wall temperatures between 40°C and 70°C **shall** be 25 percent radiographed.
*   Piping operating at wall temperatures less than 40°C **shall** be 10 percent radiographed.

Refer to [[ASME_section_v_non_destructive_examination]] for general NDE requirements.
