# Electrical Field Checkout: Deficiency Management (9-PRC-0004)

Effective [[deficiency_management_process|deficiency management]] is a critical component of the Electrical Field Checkout Procedure (9-PRC-0004). This process ensures that any deviations from specifications or functional issues are systematically identified, documented, and resolved.

**Recording and Reporting Findings:**
During the inspection conducted by the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics specialist, any findings or discrepancies **shall be recorded** on a separate sheet. This sheet **must be attached** to the relevant Electrical System Check Lists. Once recorded, all identified deficiencies **shall be promptly given** to the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site supervisor.

**Correction of Deficiencies:**
The Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site supervisor **shall then work** in close coordination with a dedicated deficiency crew. This crew, supplied by the site sub-contractor, **is responsible for correcting** all identified deficiencies. This includes addressing any outstanding punch list items that may have resulted from the initial review performed by the sub-contractor. The collaborative effort between the site supervisor and the deficiency crew ensures that all electrical systems meet the required standards before final acceptance.
