# Engineering Subcontract Procedure Overview (0-PRC-2202)

This procedure, identified as 0-PRC-2202, outlines the mandatory format and content requirements for all Engineering Subcontracts. It ensures a standardized approach to engaging subcontractors for engineering services.

An Engineering Subcontract is formally established through an Agreement, which **must** be signed by both Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Subcontractor. This Agreement serves as the foundational document, encapsulating all subcontract-specific information and terms.

The Agreement **must** reference various supporting documents that collectively provide a comprehensive description of the services to be rendered and the specific conditions under which these services **must** be performed. This ensures clarity, compliance, and a complete understanding of obligations for both parties involved.

The Agreement **shall** be prepared using the template document 0-SUB-22XX: [Template] Engineering Subcontract Agreement.

Key information that **shall** be explicitly stated within the Agreement includes:
*   The official date of the Agreement.
*   The full legal name and registered address of the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics entity or business name entering into the subcontract.
*   The full legal name and registered address of the Subcontractor.

The detailed scope and conditions of the subcontract are systematically organized into seven distinct sections, collectively referred to as the Subcontract Data. A comprehensive breakdown of these sections and their specific requirements can be found in [[engineering_subcontract_agreement_data_sections]].
