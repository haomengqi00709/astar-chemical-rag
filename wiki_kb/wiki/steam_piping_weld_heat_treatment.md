# Steam Piping Weld Heat Treatment

This page outlines the requirements for preheat, inter-pass temperature control, and post-weld heat treatment (PWHT) for steam piping welds, as defined in Specification 4-SPC-0069. These procedures are critical for ensuring the metallurgical integrity and mechanical properties of the welded joints, especially for carbon steel in ASME P-No. 4 and 5 classifications (1 ¼ Cr ½ Mo and 2 ½ Cr 1 Mo alloy steels).

**Preheat and Inter-pass Temperatures**
*   Preheat and inter-pass temperatures **shall** be in accordance with [[ASME_b31_1_power_piping|ASME B31.1 Para.131]].
*   Temperatures **shall** be controlled by temperature indicating crayons, contact pyrometers, or other suitable means to ensure compliance with specified ranges.

**Post-Weld Heat Treatment (PWHT)**
*   Post-weld heat treatment (PWHT) **shall** be performed on all completed welds.
*   Heat treatment **shall** be conducted according to the requirements specified in [[ASME_b31_1_power_piping|ASME B31.1 Para.132]].
*   No welding is allowed after PWHT has been completed.

**Hardness Requirements after PWHT:**
*   For welds on ASME P-No. 4 material, the final hardness (after PWHT) **shall** be no higher than 2 Brinell.
*   For welds on ASME P-No. 5 material, the final hardness (after PWHT) **shall** be no higher than 2 Brinell.

These requirements ensure that the material achieves the desired properties and stress relief following welding, preventing potential issues such as cracking or reduced service life.

**Preheat and Inter-pass Temperature Control**
*   Preheat and inter-pass temperatures shall be in accordance with ASME B31.1 Para.131.
*   Temperatures shall be controlled by temperature indicating crayons, contact pyrometers or other suitable means.

**Post-Weld Heat Treatment (PWHT)**
*   Post weld heat treatment (PWHT) shall be done on completed welds.
*   No welding is allowed after PWHT.
*   Final hardness (after post-weld heat treatment) of welds on P No.4 material shall be no higher than 2 Brinell.
*   Final hardness (after post-weld heat treatment) of welds on P No. 5 material shall be no higher than 2 Brinell.
