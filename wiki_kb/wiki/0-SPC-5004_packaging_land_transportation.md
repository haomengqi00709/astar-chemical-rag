# Packaging and Shipping for Land Transportation Overview (0-SPC-5004)

This specification, identified as **0-SPC-5004**, outlines the minimum requirements for the pre-shipment preparation and packaging of goods intended for land transportation. It provides general guidelines, with specific packaging requirements potentially detailed within the [[0-SPC-5001_purchase_order_overview|Purchase Order]] itself.

The primary objective of these requirements is to ensure that all goods are adequately protected during transit and storage, preventing damage, loss, or deterioration.
