# Cold Exchanger Function and Flow

The cold exchanger is a critical component in the acid plant, primarily responsible for preheating the main gas stream before it enters the converter. This preheating process utilizes excess heat recovered from the gas stream exiting bed 4 of the converter.

**Shell Side Flow:**
*   Cold process gas, discharged from the main blower, enters the shell side of the cold exchanger at the bottom.
*   Upon exiting the shell side, this preheated gas is directed to the tube side of the [[hot_exchanger_function_and_flow|hot exchanger]] for further heating.

**Tube Side Flow:**
*   Hot gases, originating from bed 4 of the converter, enter the tube side of the cold exchanger at the top.
*   The gas then flows downwards through the tubes and discharges at the bottom.
*   This discharge, which may include any condensed acid, is subsequently sent to the final absorption tower.
