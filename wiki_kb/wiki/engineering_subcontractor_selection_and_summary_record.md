# Engineering Subcontractor Selection and Summary Record

The ultimate objective of Engineering Subcontractor selection is to achieve an optimal balance between minimizing project risk and minimizing overall cost. The ideal selection is the Subcontractor who presents the lowest total cost to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, provided they have received a "satisfactory" rating across all evaluation categories: [[engineering_subcontractor_commercial_bid_evaluation|Commercial]], [[engineering_subcontractor_performance_bid_evaluation|Performance]], and [[engineering_subcontractor_financial_bid_evaluation|Financial]].

The selection process **will** generally necessitate the imposition of additional requirements on the chosen Subcontractor to further reduce identified risks. These requirements are typically highlighted in the evaluations as qualifications associated with a "satisfactory with qualifications" rating. It **will** usually be necessary to solicit modified bids from Subcontractors to ensure their acceptance of these additional [[engineering_subcontract_agreement_data_sections|Subcontract-Specific Conditions]]. For comprehensive details on commercial terms, refer to [[engineering_subcontract_commercial_terms_0_prc_2205|Commercial Terms for Engineering Subcontract]] (0-PRC-2205). The solicitation of modified bids **should** ideally occur during the evaluation process where practical, or during the final negotiation phase with short-listed Subcontractors.

Authorization limits for the final Subcontractor selection are detailed in [[0_prc_2204_table_2]].

The [[project_engineer_pem_gate_review_responsibilities|Project Engineer]] **will** be responsible for preparing a comprehensive summary record of the evaluations for all Subcontractors. This summary record **will** be presented in the form of 0-TAB-XXXX-6: Summary Record. All qualifications identified during the individual evaluations **will** be explicitly listed in this summary record. Furthermore, any bid modifications or [[engineering_subcontract_agreement_data_sections|Subcontract-Specific Conditions]] that have been agreed upon with the selected Subcontractor **will** also be documented in the summary record.

The [[project_engineer_pem_gate_review_responsibilities|Project Engineer]] **will** publish the [[engineering_subcontractor_selection_and_summary_record|Summary Record]]. For guidelines on publishing project documents, refer to [[document_publishing_methods|Publishing of Project Documents]] (0-PRC-0005). The [[engineering_subcontractor_selection_and_summary_record|Summary Record]] **will** be approved by the most senior person listed in Section 7, adhering to the stated authorization limits. In instances where approval is granted by the President, he may include additional instructions to mitigate any remaining risks. The approved [[engineering_subcontractor_selection_and_summary_record|Summary Record]] serves as the official authorization to proceed with the issuance of the Subcontract. For the procedure on issuing engineering subcontracts, refer to [[issue_of_engineering_subcontract_0_prc_2206|Issue of Engineering Subcontract]] (0-PRC-2206).
