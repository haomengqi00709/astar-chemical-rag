# Packing and Shipping Requirements for Centrifugal Pumps (0-QDS-7412)

This page details the packing and shipping requirements for Centrifugal Pumps, as specified in Quality Data Sheet [[0-QDS-7412_centrifugal_pump_quality_data_sheet_overview]]. Adherence to these requirements ensures the safe and documented transit of the equipment.

**Packing/Crating:**
*   **Activity:** Packing/Crating is a required item/activity.
*   **Inspection:** Preparation for shipping is a required item/activity for inspection.
*   **Surveillance:** This inspection is required with Chemetics surveillance (witness). A Chemetics representative **must be present** to observe and verify that the pump is adequately prepared for shipping, including proper crating and protection.

**Packing List:**
*   **Activity:** Packing List is a required item/activity.
*   **Documentation:** A Packing List is required.
*   **Quality Record:** The Packing List **must be provided** as AP (As Prepared).
*   **Inspection:** Packing List inspection is required with Chemetics surveillance (witness). A Chemetics representative **must be present** to verify the accuracy and completeness of the packing list against the actual contents.

These requirements ensure that the Centrifugal Pump is securely packed, protected from damage during transit, and accompanied by accurate documentation for logistics and customs purposes. For definitions of quality record requirements, refer to [[0-QDS-7412_quality_record_definitions]].
