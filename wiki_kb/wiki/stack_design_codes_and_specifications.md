# Stack Design Codes and Specifications

This page outlines the "STACK DESIGN CODE & SPECIFICATION" category as defined in the [[4-dst-xxxx-r0_template_stack_datasheet_administration|Template Stack Datasheet]]. Adherence to these codes and specifications is mandatory to ensure the safety, reliability, and regulatory compliance of stack designs.

**Key Design Codes and Specifications:**
*   **Design Codes:** Refers to the primary engineering design codes applicable to the stack, which may include international standards (e.g., ASME, API, EN) or national codes relevant to the project location. These codes govern aspects such as material selection, fabrication, welding, and structural integrity.
*   **Foundation Design Loading:** Specifies the standards and methodologies for calculating and applying loads to the stack's foundation. This category ensures that the foundation is adequately designed to support the stack's [[stack_design_parameters|empty weight]], [[stack_design_parameters|live load]], [[stack_design_parameters|wind base shear]], [[stack_design_parameters|seismic base shear]], and other forces.
*   **KC Specification:** Refers to specific Chemetics (KC) internal specifications that must be followed. These may cover proprietary design practices, quality requirements, or specific material preferences.
*   **Wind Design Code:** Specifies the particular code or standard used for calculating wind loads on the stack. This is critical for ensuring the stack's stability against [[wind_design_requirements|wind forces]], especially for tall structures.
*   **Seismic Design Code:** Specifies the particular code or standard used for calculating seismic loads on the stack. This is essential for designing the stack to withstand [[seismic_design_requirements|earthquake forces]] in seismically active regions.

Compliance with these codes and specifications is verified throughout the design, fabrication, and construction phases. Any deviations must be formally documented and approved.

**Specific Categories:**
*   Design Codes
*   Foundation Design Loading
*   KC Specification
*   Wind Design Code
*   Seismic Design Code
