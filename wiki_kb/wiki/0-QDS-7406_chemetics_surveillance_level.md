# 0-QDS-7406 Chemetics Surveillance Level

For the Catalyst materials detailed in this Quality Data Sheet, the surveillance level by Chemetics **shall** be Level 0.0.

This surveillance level indicates the degree of oversight and inspection Chemetics will perform. The specific activities and requirements for this surveillance are further detailed within this Quality Data Sheet.

For details on the specific activities and documentation subject to Chemetics surveillance, refer to [[0-QDS-7406_inspection_and_documentation_requirements]].
