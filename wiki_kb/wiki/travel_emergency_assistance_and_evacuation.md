# Travel Emergency Assistance and Evacuation

Ensuring traveler safety and access to medical support is paramount. This page outlines the resources available for emergency assistance and medical expenses during company and personal travel.

**SOS Services (Security and Medical Advisory):**
SOS is the designated company for security and medical advisory and evacuation services for all travelers. Everyone **should** possess an up-to-date SOS card for travel. The program number for the SOS card **should** be **27ACPS000025**.

**Sun Life "Medi-Passport" Card (Medical Expenses):**
The Sun Life "Medi-Passport" Card provides coverage for unexpected medical expenses when traveling for regular employees. If you do not have a Sun Life card, contact Human Resources. Sun Life also extends coverage for medical emergencies and expenses on personal travel for regular employees and their dependents.

**Emergency Contact Information:**
Travelers **should** leave contact numbers for while they are away with someone in the office (e.g., an administrative assistant or Manager).

For general travel health and safety guidelines, refer to [[travel_health_and_safety_requirements]]. For immunization advice, consult [[travel_immunization_and_medical_advisory]].
