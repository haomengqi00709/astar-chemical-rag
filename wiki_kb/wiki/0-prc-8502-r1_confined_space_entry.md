# Confined Space Entry Procedure

The purpose of this procedure, documented as 0-PRC-8502, is to ensure the safety and health of all employees who enter or work in any type of enclosed space. This includes tanks, pipes, pits, vaults, or similar confined spaces where an oxygen-deficient or gaseous atmosphere, or chemicals, may exist, or where flooding may occur, or where emergency egress would be restricted.

This procedure applies to all personnel who may be required to enter tanks and other enclosed spaces as defined herein. It shall be followed by the Commissioning Team (Chemetics personnel, Contractors, and Vendors representatives) after a circuit, system, or equipment is handed over as mechanically completed for commissioning by the Construction Manager to the Commissioning Manager.

**Basis and Compliance:**
This procedure is based on Aker Kvaerner’s Confined Space Entry procedure, issued in January 2003 and found in the [[aker_kvaerner_hse_policy_manual]] section 7, item 7.05. A Client’s regulation may be followed if its requirements and guidelines fully cover those spelled out in Chemetics’ procedures.

**Definition of Confined Space:**
The Occupational Health and Safety Regulation defines a "confined space" as an area, other than an underground working, that is:
*   Enclosed or partially enclosed.
*   Not designed or intended for continuous human occupancy.
*   Has limited or restricted means for entry or exit that may complicate the provision of first aid, evacuation, rescue, or other emergency response services.
*   Large enough and so configured that a worker could enter to perform assigned work.

Examples include vessels, vats, ship holds, sewers, tunnels, and large pipelines.

**General Requirements:**
*   A written [[0-REP-8007b_confined_space_hazard_assessment_form|Hazard Assessment]] must be prepared by the Commissioning HSE Manager for the space of interest before entry.
*   The hazard assessment created for the task **must** be understood by all members of the confined space entry team.
*   All personnel **must** be confined space trained and trained in the use of retrieval equipment and evacuation and rescue procedures prior to entry.
*   All Confined Space Entry incidents and near misses **shall** be thoroughly investigated and documented in accordance with [[0-PRC-8013_hse_incident_reporting]].
