# Pressure and Leak Testing for Centrifugal Pumps (0-QDS-7412)

This page details the pressure and leak testing requirements for Centrifugal Pumps, as specified in Quality Data Sheet [[0-QDS-7412_centrifugal_pump_quality_data_sheet_overview]]. These tests are essential to confirm the structural integrity and leak-tightness of the pump components.

**Pressure/Leak Testing:**
*   **Activity:** Pressure/Leak Testing is a required item/activity.
*   **Specific Test:** Hydrostatic Test is a required item/activity within the Pressure/Leak Testing scope.
*   **Chemetics Surveillance:**
    *   Chemetics surveillance (witness) is required for the Hydrostatic Test. This means a Chemetics representative **must be present** to observe the execution of the test.
    *   Chemetics surveillance (hold) is required for the Hydrostatic Test. This signifies that the manufacturing process **cannot proceed** beyond this point until Chemetics has formally released the hold after satisfactory completion and review of the Hydrostatic Test.

These surveillance requirements ensure that critical pressure integrity tests are performed and verified to Chemetics' standards before further manufacturing or assembly steps are undertaken.
