# Surface Preparation for Protective Coatings (7-SPC-0005)

Proper surface preparation is a mandatory requirement for ensuring the effectiveness and longevity of protective coatings. This section outlines the cleaning and preparation standards for pipe, fittings, and repair areas.

**Initial Cleaning**
Prior to any mechanical cleaning, all mill varnish, oil, grease, paint, and other surface contaminants **will be removed** from the pipe using a suitable solvent.

**Mechanical Cleaning Standards**
*   **Shop-Coated Pipe:** Shop coated pipe **will be mechanically cleaned** to SSPC-SP-6 (NACE 3), Commercial Blast Cleaning. This process **must be** in accordance with the requirements of AWWA Specification No. C209, Cold-Applied Tape Coatings.
*   **Field Joints, Fittings, and Repairs:** Mechanical cleaning to SSPC-SP3, Power-Tool Cleaning, is acceptable for all field joints, fittings, and repairs.

**Surface Imperfection Removal**
Weld spatter, slag, sharp edges, burrs, slivers, and knurls **will be removed** from the pipe surface by filing, rasping, grinding, wire brushing, sanding, or other suitable means.

**Timing of Priming and Wrapping**
Pipe joints and fittings **will be primed and wrapped** on the same day as cleaning is carried out to prevent re-contamination.

**Contaminant Removal**
Any flash rusting, surface oxidation, or other foreign contaminants **will be removed** by reprocessing through the necessary cleaning steps before coating application.

For specific application details, refer to [[7-SPC-0005_polyken_tape_application]] and [[7-SPC-0005_coating_repair_procedures]].

**Mechanical Cleaning Standards**
*   Mechanical cleaning must be in accordance with the requirements of AWWA Specification No. C209, Cold-Applied Tape Coatings.
*   Mechanical cleaning to SSPC-SP3, Power-Tool Cleaning, is acceptable for all field joints and fittings.

**Detailed Surface Preparation Steps**
*   Weld spatter, slag, sharp edges, burrs, slivers and knurls will be removed from pipe surface by filing, rasping, grinding, wire brushing, sanding or other suitable means.
*   Any flash rusting, surface oxidation, or other foreign contaminants will be removed by reprocessing through the necessary cleaning steps.
