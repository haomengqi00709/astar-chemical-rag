# Piping Safety Design Considerations

Personnel safety is paramount in the design, selection, and installation of plant piping, encompassing considerations during construction, ongoing operation, and maintenance.

**Safety Showers and Eye Washes**
Safety showers and eye washes **must** be designed, specified, and strategically located to maximize effectiveness. Design considerations **must** include water temperature, flow rate, coverage, supply pressure, accessibility, and remote indication of use. The water for these units **must** be tepid and **shall not** exceed 40°C. In cases where chemical reactions are accelerated by water temperature, medical advice **must** be sought to determine the optimal temperature for the specific application. The heating effect of sunlight on stagnant lines **must** also be considered.

**Cross-Contamination Prevention**
To prevent cross-contamination of utilities and mitigate associated safety risks, mutually exclusive fittings **must** be specified. Hose couplings unique to the service fluid type **must** be specified.

**Material Restrictions**
Teflon tape **shall not** be used on natural gas piping.

**Fire Protection**
Piping requiring fire protection, such as flanged connections on hydrocarbon lines, **must** be insulated with a suitable medium to provide adequate fire resistance.

**Safeguarding**
Safeguarding, in accordance with [[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping]] Appendix G, **must** be considered in the design of Normal Fluid Service applications, particularly for plastic pipe.

**Emergency Egress**
Piping **must** be arranged to ensure it does not obstruct any exit path from buildings, elevated equipment, or structures during an emergency.

**Safety Showers and Eye Washes**
Safety showers and eye washes **must** be designed, specified, and strategically located to suit the need and maximise their effectiveness. Water temperature, flow rate and coverage, supply pressure, accessibility, and remote indication of use will be considered. The water for safety showers and eye washes will be tepid and will not have a temperature greater than 40°C. In circumstances where the chemical reaction is accelerated by water temperature, medical advice will be sought on the optimum temperature for each application.

**Contamination Prevention**
Cross-contamination of utilities and the safety consequences presented will be avoided by the specification of mutually exclusive fittings. Hose couplings unique to the service fluid type will be specified.

**Fire Protection**
Piping requiring fire protection will be insulated with a suitable medium to provide adequate fire resistance (e.g., at flanged connections on hydrocarbon lines).

**Safeguarding**
Safeguarding in accordance with ASME B31.3 Appendix G will be considered in the design of Normal Fluid Service applications, especially for plastic pipe.

**Vents, Drains, and Emergency Exits**
Piping conveying corrosive or harmful process fluids will have vents, drains, and bleeds routed to a safe location and provided with valves and blinds. Piping will be arranged so that it does not obstruct a way of exit from any point in a building, elevated equipment, or structure in the case of an emergency.
