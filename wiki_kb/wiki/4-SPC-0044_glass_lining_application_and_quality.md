# Glass-Lining Application and Quality Requirements (4-SPC-0044)

This section details the stringent requirements for the application, quality, and repair of glass-linings for carbon steel reaction vessels, as specified in 4-SPC-0044.

**Lining Quality and Application**
The acid resistant glass-lining **shall** be of the highest quality, continuous, and complete, without cracks or flaws. The coating **shall** be applied on a prepared, clean surface. In the case of nozzles, the glass-lining **shall** be continuous over the gasket face.

The lining **shall** consist of a layer or layers (ground coat(s)) to provide a strong metal-to-glass bond, topped with highly corrosion-resistant cover coats. Application of these coatings **shall** be carried out by the Wet Spray Process, the Hot Dust Process, or a combination of both. The lining **shall** be the best grade of chemical and thermal shock-resistant glass currently available.

**Surface Preparation**
Prior to lining, all surfaces to be lined **shall** be smooth, and all corners **shall** conform to the radii specified on the drawing. Welds **shall** be thoroughly inspected and repaired where necessary. The vessel **shall** be normalized prior to lining. Surfaces to be lined **shall** then be grit blasted and visually examined for defects by the Vendor. Surface defects **shall** be ground out or repaired by welding, with weld repairs thoroughly inspected by the non-destructive method used for the original weld. A final grit blasting or mechanical grinding **shall** be carried out prior to lining.

**Glass-Lining Thickness**
The Fabricator’s proposal **shall** contain the minimum thickness of vessel lining. The absolute minimum thickness of glass-lining **shall** be 35 mils (890 microns), achieved by the necessary number of layers (including ground coat) to ensure optimum adhesion and thermal properties. Blind Flanges, Agitators, Baffles, Covers, Dip-pipes, Spargers, Internal Valves, and Instrumentation **shall** have a minimum glass thickness of 30 mils (760 microns).

General vessel surfaces **shall** have glass thickness limits of 35-90 mils (890 - 2300 microns). For agitators, baffles, thermowells, pipes, and radii in vessels up to and including 4" (100mm), glass thickness limits are 30-90 mils (760 - 2300 microns). Ground surfaces (e.g., agitator shaft in way of gland or mechanical seal) **shall** have glass thickness limits of 30-90 mils (700 - 2300mm). The thickness of the lining **shall** be within these limits and free from any sudden variations.

**Operating Temperature Range**
The glass lining **shall** be suitable for an operating temperature range of minus 25°C (-13°F) to 230°C (446°F). The Fabricator **shall** be consulted if operation outside this temperature range is contemplated.

**Defect-Free Locations**
The glass lining **shall** be either free from defects or within the specified plugging standard. The following locations **shall** be defect-free:
*   Covers for nozzles of 24" (600mm) and 20" (500mm) diameter.
*   Covers for 18"x16" (450 x 400mm) manholes.
*   Covers for 18"x14" (450 x 350mm) inspection openings.
*   Covers for 30", 36", 38", 40" and 42" (750, 800, 850, 900, 950mm) diameter openings.
*   Dished heads of two-piece vessels of 420 gal. (1600 Litres) and below.
*   Agitators (standard configuration through 4000 gal. (15,000 Litres) vessel capacity).
*   Baffles.
*   Thermowell nozzles.
*   Flange surfaces.
*   Radii of 3" (75mm) and below.
*   Agitator nozzles.
*   Bottom outlet nozzles.
*   Nozzles 6" (150 mm) nominal size and below.

**Lining Repairs and Plugging**
There **shall** be a minimum of 4" (100mm) of undamaged glass between the edges of adjacent plugs. The number of plugs **shall not** exceed those given in [[4-SPC-0044_table_2_glass_lining_plug_limits]]. Nozzle repairs utilizing a metallic liner are unacceptable and fall outside the scope of this specification.

Before carrying out repairs to the lining that are outside the scope of the agreed Standard, the Supplier **shall** inform Aker Kvaerner Chemetics of the number and position of the defects, and the type of repair proposed, including the composition of any cement to be employed. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics will provide written approval or rejection of the repair proposals.

Plugs will normally be made of tantalum with PTFE sealing washers, which **shall** be either single or three-piece type. Silicate cement **shall** be used in oxidizing environments (e.g., nitric acid) and in conjunction with tantalum plugs for nitrating environments. Silicate cement **should not** be used in alkaline solutions where the pH exceeds 9.

**Lining Quality and Application**
*   35 mils (890 microns) glass-lining shall be achieved by the number of layers (including ground coat) necessary to obtain optimum adhesion and thermal properties.
*   Blind Flanges, Agitators, Baffles, Covers, Dip-pipes, Spargers Internal Valves and Instrumentation shall have a minimum glass thickness of 30 mils (760 microns).
*   The coating shall be applied on a prepared, clean surface.
*   In the case of nozzles, the glass-lining shall be continuous over the gasket face.
*   All surfaces to be lined shall be smooth. All corners shall be to the radii specified on the drawing.
*   Welds shall be thoroughly inspected and repaired where necessary prior to lining.
*   The vessel shall be normalized prior to lining.
*   Surfaces to be lined shall then be grit blasted. Surfaces to be lined shall be visually examined for defects by Vendor after grit blasting.
*   Surface defects shall be ground out. Where necessary, surface defects shall be repaired by welding.
*   Weld repairs shall be thoroughly inspected by the non-destructive method used for the original weld.
*   A final grit blasting or mechanical grinding shall be carried out prior to lining.
*   The lining shall consist of a layer or layers (ground coat(s)) to provide a strong metal to glass bond, and highly corrosion-resistant cover coats on top of ground coat(s).
*   Application of constituent coatings shall be carried out by the Wet Spray Process or the Hot Dust Process or a combination of the two.
*   The lining shall be the best grade of chemical and thermal shock-resistant glass, currently available.
*   The glass lining shall be suitable for an operating temperature range of minus 25C (-13F) to 230C (446F).
*   The glass lining shall be either free from defects or within the specified plugging standard (see Table in Para. 10.4).
*   The Purchaser's Inspector shall ascertain from Fabricator the grade of glass used and record this information on the Glass-lining Inspection Record shown as Appendix A to this Standard.
*   The thickness of the lining shall be within the limits specified in Paragraph 12.3 and recorded in Appendix "A".

**Lining Repair**
*   The number, size and location of any proposed repairs to defects exposed under the spark test and which are outside the scope of the agreed Standard shall be referred to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
*   Nozzle repairs utilizing a metallic liner are unacceptable and fall outside the scope of this specification.
*   Before carrying out repairs to the lining which are outside the scope of the agreed Standard, the Supplier shall inform Aker Kvaerner Chemetics of the number and position of the defects and the type of repair proposed, including the composition of any cement to be employed. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics will indicate approval or otherwise to the repair proposals in writing.
*   Plugs will normally be of tantalum with PTFE sealing washers, which shall be either of the single or three-piece type.
*   Silicate cement shall be used in oxidizing environments (e.g., nitric acid) and in conjunction with the tantalum plug when repairing vessels for nitrating environments. Silicate cement should not be used in alkaline solutions where the pH exceeds 9.
*   There shall be a minimum of 4" (100mm) of undamaged glass between the edges of adjacent plugs.
*   Supplier shall indicate the positions of all plugs to the satisfaction of the Inspector or Engineer.
