# Dry Mist Eliminator Entrained Material Characteristics

This page describes the characteristics of the entrained material and the required collection efficiencies for the Dry Mist Eliminator, Tag No. **7507K**. Understanding these parameters is vital for the effective design and operation of the mist eliminator.

**Entrained Material Size Distribution Categories:**
The entrained acid mist is categorized by particle size distribution, which influences the selection and performance of the mist eliminator element. The specific distribution is defined by the following categories:
*   **Category 1:** % above microns
*   **Category 2:** % between microns
*   **Category 3:** % between microns
*   **Category 4:** % below microns

**Required Collection Efficiency Categories:**
To ensure the desired performance, specific collection efficiencies are mandated for different particle size ranges:
*   **Category 1:** % above microns
*   **Category 2:** % between microns
*   **Category 3:** % between microns
*   **Category 4:** % below microns

The vendor **must** provide curves for predicted collection efficiency versus particle size as part of their deliverables, as specified in [[dry_mist_eliminator_performance_requirements]]. These curves will demonstrate how the mist eliminator performs across the defined particle size ranges.

For a general overview of the Dry Mist Eliminator, refer to [[dry_mist_eliminator_overview]]. Further details on overall performance requirements are available in [[dry_mist_eliminator_performance_requirements]].
