# Electrical Systems Contractor Checkout Procedure (9-PRC-0020)

Procedure 9-PRC-0020, titled "Electrical Systems Contractor Checkout Procedure," details the specific steps and requirements for the initial checkout of electrical systems performed by the site sub-contractor. This procedure is a prerequisite for the broader [[9-PRC-0004_electrical_field_checkout_procedure_overview|Electrical Field Checkout Procedure (9-PRC-0004)]].

The completion of 9-PRC-0020 **must** ensure that the sub-contractor has thoroughly inspected and verified their electrical installations before a final review by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics personnel. Various [[9-PRC-0004_electrical_field_checkout_documentation|Electrical System Check Lists]], including those numbered 9-LST-0701 to 0711, are attached to this procedure, along with [[motor_approval_tag|Motor Approval Tags]]. These documents guide the sub-contractor's checkout process and serve as evidence of their completed work.
