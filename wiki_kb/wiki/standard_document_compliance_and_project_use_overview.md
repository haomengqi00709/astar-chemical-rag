# Standard Document Compliance and Project Use Overview

Adherence to standard documents is a fundamental requirement for all work conducted by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, ensuring consistency, quality, and compliance with established company practices.

### Mandatory Compliance
All work executed by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **must** comply with all applicable standard documents marked **Rev 1 or later**. This ensures that all operations and deliverables align with the most current and approved company standards.

### Non-Compliance
Non-compliance with standard documents will only be permitted with the explicit approval of the applicable Business Director, other than as described in Sections 7.2 and 7.3 of the original 0-PRC-0001 procedure. Any deviation from a standard document **must** be formally justified and approved to maintain control and oversight.

### Application to Templates
Section 7 of the original procedure is not intended to apply to the normal use of standard documents that serve as templates for the insertion of data by the project team (e.g., Lists, Data Sheets). In such cases, the content within the template is project-specific data, not a modification of the standard itself.

However, Section 7 **does** apply to the modification of the *form* of any templates. If the structure, layout, or inherent instructions of a standard template are altered, then the procedures for [[project_use_standard_documents_modified|modified project use]] **must** be followed.

### Project Use of Standard Documents
Standard documents can be integrated into project work in several ways, depending on the project's specific requirements and contractual obligations. The following pages detail the procedures for each scenario:
*   [[project_use_standard_documents_unmodified]]: For using standard documents exactly as they are, without any changes.
*   [[project_use_standard_documents_modified]]: For adapting standard documents with specific alterations to meet project needs.
*   [[project_use_standard_documents_rewritten]]: For situations where a standard document requires a complete overhaul for a project.
