# Job Hazard Analysis (JHA) Review and Updates

To maintain their effectiveness and relevance, existing [[0-PRC-8007_job_hazard_analysis]] (JHA) documents **must** be periodically reviewed and updated. A review of an existing JHA **will be done** under the following circumstances:

*   **Initial Task Completion:** When the first task using the JHA is completed, to assess its initial effectiveness.
*   **Employee Feedback:** When improvements are requested from employees actively performing the task, indicating practical insights or challenges.
*   **New Hazard Identification:** When new hazards are identified that were not previously accounted for in the existing JHA.
*   **Method Changes:** When the method or procedure for performing the task changes, requiring a re-evaluation of associated risks.
*   **Accidents or Near-Misses:** When accidents or near-misses occur on jobs that have been analyzed. In such cases, the causes **should be checked** against the JHA to ensure that established controls were adequate and properly implemented.

These review triggers ensure that JHAs remain dynamic documents that accurately reflect current work conditions and effectively mitigate risks.
