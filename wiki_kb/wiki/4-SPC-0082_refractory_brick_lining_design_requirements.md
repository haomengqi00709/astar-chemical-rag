# Refractory Brick Lining Design Requirements (4-SPC-0082)

This section of specification 4-SPC-0082 details the specific design requirements for refractory brick linings.

Refractory linings **will** be a minimum of 228mm thick, utilizing mullite bonded high alumina firebrick. This primary lining **will** be backed by a 114mm thick layer of insulating firebrick. Any additional requirements for the lining may be specified on the relevant vessel and ducting drawings.

The final lining detail design is the responsibility of the Vendor, who is also solely responsible for the performance of the lining system.

Design considerations for equipment and ductwork include:
*   Spacing between mitres and/or appurtenances **will** allow for at least one 114mm wide brick in the parent lining.
*   Lining in heads **will** be dished or cone keyed, and **will not** consist of flat walls.
*   Nozzle openings **will** be designed using “bulls-eyes” of shaped brick wherever possible to ensure robust and effective lining around penetrations.

A critical aspect of the design is thermal expansion accommodation:
*   The refractory lining system **must** be designed with sufficient expansion joints.
*   These expansion joints **must** accommodate the thermal expansion of the lining system.
*   Thermal expansion **must** be anticipated at the maximum gas operating temperature, as indicated on the equipment drawings (e.g., [[4-OTL-6130_regeneration_furnace_design_conditions|4-OTL-6130: Regeneration Furnace]]).
*   The Vendor **will** submit calculations to demonstrate adequate thermal expansion accommodation.
