# Fabrication Preparation and Forming for Stabilized Austenitic Stainless Steel (4-SPC-0014)

This page outlines the requirements for fabrication preparation and forming of Columbium Stabilized High Nickel Austenitic stainless steel, as specified in document 4-SPC-0014.

**Supervision and Workmanship**
The Fabricator shall ensure regular and systematic supervision of all work, both in-shop and on-site, by competent staff. All work shall be carried out in accordance with the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics "Approved for Fabrication" shop detail drawing(s). Deviations from weld geometry on approved drawing(s) are not permitted without prior approval from Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Cleaning and Contamination Control**
*   The surface and edges of any austenitic steel which is to be heated shall be cleaned free from all traces of foreign matter.
*   Cleaning shall be performed by grinding, wire brushing with a stainless steel brush, and/or non-chlorinated solvent cleaning immediately prior to heat application.
*   The Fabricator shall implement necessary controls to prevent contamination of weldments at all stages of fabrication. These controls shall include the segregation of wire brushes, grinding wheels, etc., for use on stainless steels only.
*   On completion of work, items shall be cleaned internally and externally.
*   All fabrication markings, weld spatter, heat tinting, slag, etc., shall be removed prior to shipment.

**Passivation and Ferroxyl Testing**
*   Passivation, where required by the Purchase Order or referenced documents, shall be carried out in accordance with a Cleaning and Passivation Procedure approved by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
*   Passivation of stainless steel shall correspond to the requirements of [[ASTM_a380_cleaning_passivation_stainless_steel]].
*   Ferroxyl testing, where required, shall necessitate procedural approval by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Material Cutting**
*   Material shall be cut to size by machining, shearing, or thermal cutting.
*   Materials over 3/16" thick shall have extra material left on the sheared edge to allow machining, chipping, etc., to ensure defects are removed.
*   Material over 1" thick shall not be sheared.
*   Thermal cutting is preferably carried out by the Plasma Arc Process. Welding edges after the Plasma Arc Process shall be lightly ground to bright metal.
*   Any other thermal cutting process shall require a minimum of 1/8" of metal to be removed by mechanical means after cutting. This removal ensures all slag, scale, other irregularities, contaminants, and thermal effects are eliminated.
*   For additional grinding requirements, refer to Para 9.9 (covered in [[4-SPC-0014_weld_quality_and_finishing]]).

**Material Forming**
*   Materials shall be cold formed as far as practical.
*   When hot forming is necessary, the procedure shall be agreed to in writing by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics prior to the commencement of work.

**Cleaning and Passivation**
*   Ferroxyl testing, where required, shall require procedural approval by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
