# Packing List Requirements (0-SPC-5004)

The Vendor **must** provide a separate packing list for each package.

**Content of Packing List:**
*   The Vendor **will** follow the line item numbering and equipment descriptions shown on the [Company Name] [[0-SPC-5001_purchase_order_overview|Purchase Order]].
*   If the [Company Name] Purchase Order is less detailed, the Vendor’s more detailed list **will** be used.
*   General line item descriptions such as “one lot assembly nuts and bolts” are **not acceptable** on packing lists.
*   The packing list **will** detail the number and unit type of each item.

**Placement of Packing List:**
*   The Vendor **must** attach the packing slip to the outside of each package.
*   The Vendor **must** place a duplicate packing slip inside the package.
*   The packing slip **will** be in a waterproof package.
*   The waterproof package containing the packing slip **will** be clearly marked "Packing Slip".
*   On large packages, the packing slip **will** be located in a position that permits easy access by a person standing at ground level.

These requirements ensure that contents can be easily verified upon receipt and during customs inspections. This page is part of the [[0-SPC-5004_packaging_land_transportation|Packaging and Shipping for Land Transportation Overview]] and is referenced in [[0-SPC-5004_shipping_notice_requirements]].
