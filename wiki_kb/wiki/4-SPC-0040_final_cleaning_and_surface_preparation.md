# 4-SPC-0040 Final Cleaning and Surface Preparation

Specification 4-SPC-0040 outlines the mandatory requirements for the final cleaning and surface preparation of metallic process vessels and components prior to shipment.

**Cleaning Requirements:**
*   Equipment **must** be drained and thoroughly cleaned inside and outside.
*   Equipment **must** be free from rust, grease, weld spatter, scale, slag and any other foreign matter.

**Surface Preparation:**
*   Interior surfaces **will not** be painted, coated or lined unless otherwise specified.
