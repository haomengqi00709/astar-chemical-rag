# Progressive Cavity Pump Drive System Requirements

The Progressive Cavity Pump Datasheet (Template `4-DST-XXXX-R0`) includes specifications for the pump's drive system, which is essential for powering the pump and achieving the required operational parameters.

**Drive System Details:**
*   **Specification:** Refers to the detailed technical specification for the drive system components.
*   **Size:** The physical dimensions and power rating of the drive system.
*   **Supply by:** Indicates the party responsible for supplying the drive system.
*   **Mount by:** Indicates the party responsible for mounting the drive system.

The drive system typically includes the motor and any associated gearing or variable speed drives. Proper sizing and selection are critical for efficient and reliable pump operation.

*   **Size:** The physical dimensions or capacity of the drive system.
*   **Supply by:** Indicates who is responsible for supplying the drive system.
*   **Mount by:** Indicates who is responsible for mounting the drive system.
