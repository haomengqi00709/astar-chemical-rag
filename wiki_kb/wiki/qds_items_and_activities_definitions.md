# QDS Items and Activities Definitions

This page defines various items and activities commonly referenced within Quality Data Sheets (QDS). Understanding these definitions ensures clarity and consistency in quality control processes.

*   **Material Verification:** An item or activity related to confirming material properties and compliance.
*   **Non-Destructive Testing:** An item or activity involving testing methods that do not damage the material.
*   **Dye Penetrant:** A specific Non-Destructive Testing item or activity.
*   **Coating / Lining Inspection:** An item or activity focused on verifying the application and quality of coatings and linings.
*   **Equipment Testing:** An item or activity related to the functional and performance testing of equipment.
*   **Final Inspection:** An item or activity encompassing the comprehensive inspection of a completed product.
*   **Packing/Crating:** An item or activity related to the preparation of goods for shipment.
