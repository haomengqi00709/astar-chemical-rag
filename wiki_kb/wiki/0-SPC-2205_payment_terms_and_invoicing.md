# Engineering Subcontract Payment Terms and Invoicing (0-SPC-2205)

This page details the payment terms and invoicing procedures for engineering subcontracts under document 0-SPC-2205. It covers the invoicing process, payment timelines, and procedures for handling disputed invoices. For general information on engineering subcontract payment terms, refer to [[engineering_subcontract_payment_terms]].

**Invoicing Process:**
*   The Subcontractor **must** assess the amount due and submit an invoice each month after the starting date of the services.
*   Invoices **must** continue to be submitted until one month after [[0-SPC-2205_general_conditions_and_administration#Key Definitions|Completion]] of the entire services.
*   The amount due is calculated based on the method specified in the [[0-SPC-2205_table_1_subcontract_data_reference|Subcontract Data]].
*   Any value-added tax (VAT), sales tax, or other similar taxes that the law requires Aker Kvaerner Chemetics to pay to the Subcontractor **must** be included in the amount due.

**Payment Timeline:**
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **must** make payment within 45 days of receiving the Subcontractor’s invoice.
*   If a different payment period is specified in the [[0-SPC-2205_table_1_subcontract_data_reference|Subcontract Data]], Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **must** make payment within that stated period.

**Disputed Invoices:**
*   If Aker Kvaerner Chemetics disagrees with an invoice, they **must** notify the Subcontractor of the reason for disagreement before the payment due date.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **must** pay any agreed-upon portion of an invoice.
*   The Subcontractor **must** either correct a disputed invoice to a sum agreed upon by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or provide additional information to justify the original invoice.

**Inspection of Records:**
*   The Subcontractor **must** allow Aker Kvaerner Chemetics to inspect the accounts and records of their expenses at any time during working hours.

**Account Inspection:**
*   The Subcontractor **must** allow Aker Kvaerner Chemetics to inspect the accounts and records of his expenses at any time within working hours.
