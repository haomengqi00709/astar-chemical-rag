# Construction Subcontract Sub-subcontractor Costs

Costs incurred by sub-subcontractors **must** be listed as if they were incurred directly by the Subcontractor. This means that the Sub-subcontractor's costs **must** be broken down into the following categories:
*   [[construction_subcontract_direct_work_costs|Direct Labour]]
*   [[construction_subcontract_direct_work_costs|Direct Materials]]
*   [[construction_subcontract_indirect_work_costs|Indirect Labour]]
*   [[construction_subcontract_indirect_work_costs|Construction Equipment]]
*   [[construction_subcontract_overhead_costs|Overheads]]

This breakdown ensures consistent cost reporting and allocation across all tiers of the project's execution.
