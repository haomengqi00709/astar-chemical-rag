# Acid Tower Distributor Quality Record Definitions (0-QDS-7413)

This page defines the specific quality record designations used within Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7413 for the Acid Tower Distributor. These definitions are crucial for understanding the documentation requirements for various manufacturing and inspection activities.

**Quality Record Designations:**
*   **AP (As Prepared):** This designation indicates that the quality record is required "As Prepared." This means the document or record should be submitted in its original prepared format.
*   **FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package):** This designation signifies that the quality record is required to be "Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package." Such records form a part of the comprehensive quality assurance documentation submitted for the equipment.

These definitions are applied to various activities, including material certifications, non-destructive testing, and packing lists, as detailed in other sections of the Quality Data Sheet. Refer to [[0-QDS-7413_vendor_quality_assurance_requirements]] for an overview of the quality assurance framework.
