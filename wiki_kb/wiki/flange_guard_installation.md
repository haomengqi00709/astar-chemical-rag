# Flange Guard Installation

This page describes the requirements for the installation of [[flange_guard_installation|flange guards]] on piping connections. This task is typically undertaken by the deficiency crew after initial cleaning, flushing, and hydrotesting as part of the [[mechanical_equipment_checkout_procedure|mechanical checkout procedure]], contributing to overall plant safety.
