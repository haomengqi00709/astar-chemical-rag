# Horizontal Centrifugal Magnetic Drive Pump Performance and Impeller Design

Horizontal centrifugal magnetic drive pumps **must** exhibit stable head/capacity curves, which **must** continuously rise to shut-off. The design duty point **will not** be positioned to the right of the head/capacity curve's Best Efficiency Point unless explicit approval is granted by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Impeller Specifications:**
The selected impeller diameter **will neither** be greater than 90% of the maximum size available for the chosen pump casing, nor **will it** be less than 110% of the minimum size without prior approval from Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

Upon selection of a pump casing, the Vendor **will** proceed with the maximum size impeller for that casing. The Vendor **will not** trim the impeller until its final size has been confirmed in writing by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

Impellers **will** be of the closed type unless otherwise specified on individual [[pump_data_sheets]].

The maximum operating speed of the pump **will** be less than the synchronous speed of a two-pole motor.

Equipment **will** be dynamically balanced in accordance with ISO1940: Mechanical vibration - Balance quality requirements of rigid rotors - grade G6.3.
The Vendor **will** provide the estimated noise level of the pumps.
The maximum operating speed **will** be less than the synchronous speed of a two-pole motor.
The Vendor **will** state the length of time that the pumps can run dry.
