# Horizontal Vessel Load Application and Support Conditions

This page details specific rules for load application and support conditions for horizontal vessels, as outlined in document 4-SCR-0032-1. These conditions are vital for accurate structural analysis and design.

**Load Application:**
*   All specified loads are to be applied at the Datum.

**Support Conditions:**
*   Loads acting in the longitudinal direction of the vessel are exclusively resisted by the fixed support.
