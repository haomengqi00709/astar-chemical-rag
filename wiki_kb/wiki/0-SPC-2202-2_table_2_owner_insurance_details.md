# 0-SPC-2202-2 Table 2: Owner's Additional Insurance Details

Table 2, an integral part of the **0-SPC-2202-2** Specification, details the additional insurance provisions made by the Owner. This table is crucial for understanding the comprehensive [[engineering_subcontractor_insurance_requirements|insurance framework]] established for the project.

The Owner’s Insurance Table specifies the types of supplementary insurance coverage provided by the Owner, which may include, but is not limited to, project-specific liability, property damage, or other specialized coverages that extend beyond the standard insurance requirements for subcontractors. It outlines the scope, limits, deductibles, and any specific conditions or exclusions related to these additional policies.

This information is vital for subcontractors to understand the full extent of insurance protection in place, how it interacts with their own insurance policies, and the procedures for making claims or reporting incidents under the Owner's coverage. It ensures clarity regarding risk allocation and financial protection for all parties involved in the project.
