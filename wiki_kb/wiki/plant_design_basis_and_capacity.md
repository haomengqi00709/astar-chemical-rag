# Plant Design Basis and Capacity

The Plant Design Basis and Capacity section, a crucial component of the [[process_design_criteria_document]], outlines the fundamental parameters and performance expectations for the plant. This section ensures that the design is robust, meets operational requirements, and accounts for potential variations and future needs.

**Mandatory Specifications:**
All design capacities, strengths, and concentrations **must** be specified within this document. Furthermore, all guarantee values for the plant's performance **must** also be specified. Beyond these core parameters, other design values relevant to the plant's operation and performance **must** also be specified.

**Capacity Requirements:**
*   Special requirements for Plant capacity **should** be noted. This includes any specific conditions or constraints that influence the plant's throughput or operational limits.
*   All design capacities **must** be specified. This includes, but is not limited to, nameplate capacity and any design margins.
*   **Example:** The plant design **must** handle hydraulically 110% of nameplate capacity. This illustrates a common approach to incorporating safety factors and operational flexibility into the design.

**Strength and Concentration Specifications:**
*   All design strengths **must** be specified. This refers to the structural integrity of materials and components under various operating conditions.
*   All design concentrations **must** be specified. This pertains to the composition of process streams, products, and reagents, ensuring they meet quality and safety standards.

The comprehensive specification of these design values forms the bedrock upon which the entire plant is engineered. It directly influences material selection, equipment sizing, and process control strategies. Adherence to these specified values is critical for achieving the desired plant performance, safety, and reliability, and for meeting the contractual obligations summarized in the [[process_design_criteria_document]]. This section also informs related aspects such as [[plant_availability_and_operation]] and [[emissions_and_effluents]].

Special requirements pertaining to Plant capacity must be explicitly noted. For instance, the plant design might specify the ability to handle hydraulically 110% of nameplate capacity.
Beyond capacities, all design strengths, design concentrations, and guarantee values must be specified. It is also important to specify other design values as applicable.
