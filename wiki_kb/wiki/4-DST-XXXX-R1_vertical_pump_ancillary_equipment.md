# Template Vertical Pump Ancillary Equipment

This page lists the ancillary equipment required for the vertical pump, as specified in the Template Vertical Pump Datasheet (4-DST-XXXX-R1). This equipment supports the main pump unit, ensuring its safe, efficient, and reliable operation.

**Ancillary Equipment Details:**

*   **Supply:** All listed ancillary equipment is supplied by the Vendor.
*   **Mounting:** All listed ancillary equipment is mounted by the Vendor.

**Included Ancillary Equipment:**

*   **Coupling:** Connects the pump shaft to the motor shaft, transmitting power.
*   **Coupling Guard:** A safety device to protect personnel from rotating coupling parts.
*   **Cover Plate:** A plate that covers the top of the pump assembly, often forming part of the mounting structure.
*   **Lubrication system:** Any necessary system for lubricating bearings or other moving parts.
*   **Steam Jacket:** A jacket around pump components (e.g., casing, stuffing box) for heating or cooling purposes, typically using steam.
*   **Strainer:** A device installed in the suction line to remove solid particles from the fluid, protecting the pump.

The supply and mounting of this equipment **MUST** comply with [[vendor_quality_assurance]] and [[vendor_supply_requirements]].

*   **Mounting:** All listed ancillary equipment is mounted by Vendor.
