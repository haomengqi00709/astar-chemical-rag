# FRP Butt and Strap Pipe Installation

Field installation of Butt and Strap FRP (Fiber Reinforced Polymer) pipe requires specific procedures to ensure the integrity of joints and overall system performance.

**Joint Requirements:**
For field installation of Butt and Strap FRP pipe, all joints **will** be made with a corrosion liner and a structural layer, as specified in the [[pipe_specification|pipe material specifications]]. This dual-layer approach ensures both chemical resistance and mechanical strength at each connection.

**Field Trimming and Fitting Size:**
FRP fittings may be field trimmed to accommodate the piping layout where necessary. However, the minimum size of any FRP fitting **will** be such that a full-width strap joint can be applied with the strap centered on the joint, ensuring adequate bonding surface and structural integrity.

Refer to [[piping_manufacturer_data_references|MANUFACTURERS DATA ATTACHED]] for Butt and Strap FRP Pipe and for ABCO FRP Pipe.
