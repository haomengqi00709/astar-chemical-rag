# Plant Modification (1-PRC-0010)

This document, 1-PRC-0010, outlines the procedure for designing and executing plant modifications within the Project. It details the steps for initiating, reviewing, approving, implementing, and documenting changes to the plant design or physical structure.

All plant modifications during commissioning **will** be designed and executed in accordance with this procedure. This ensures that any changes, whether for rectification of deficiencies or optimization, are carefully controlled and regulated to maintain quality and safety standards, as detailed in [[plant_modifications_and_acceptance_testing]].
