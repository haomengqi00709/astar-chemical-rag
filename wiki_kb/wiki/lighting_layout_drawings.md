# Lighting Layout Drawings

Lighting Layout drawings illustrate the placement of various lighting fixtures and utility receptacles across the plant, ensuring adequate illumination and power access in all areas.

**Content of Lighting Layout Drawings**
Lighting layout drawings **shall** show locations of the various types of lighting fixtures within:
*   Process buildings.
*   Pipe racks.
*   Roadways.
*   Electrical rooms.

The latest version of the mechanical general arrangement drawings **will** be used for the backgrounds of the lighting drawings.
Symbols used for each type of lighting fixture **shall** be shown.
Symbols used for utility receptacles **shall** be shown.
Utility receptacles required **must** be shown.

**References and Details**
[[lighting_installation_details]] showing specific ways of installing the various types of lighting fixtures **must** be listed.
[[lighting_installation_details]] showing specific ways of installing receptacles **must** be listed.
The lighting distribution panel board used for powering the lights and receptacles **must** be referenced.
Panel board circuit numbers for each lighting fixture **must** be indicated.
Panel board circuit numbers for each receptacle **must** be indicated.

Mounting elevation for each fixture **will** be identified beside each lighting fixture symbol. If several lighting fixtures are located at the same elevation, a general note may be used for mounting elevation.

Project recommended lighting levels **will** be used for designing the quantity and type of lighting fixtures for various locations.
