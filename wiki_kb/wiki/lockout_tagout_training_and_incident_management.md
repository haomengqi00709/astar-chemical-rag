# Lockout and Tagout Training and Incident Management

This page details the training requirements and procedures for managing incidents related to the [[0-PRC-8504-RA_lockout_and_tagging|Lockout and Tagout Procedure]].

**Training Requirements**
*   Each employee who is [[lockout_tagout_definitions#authorized_individual|authorized]] to perform lockout and tagout **shall** be thoroughly trained in all aspects of these guidelines.
*   Everyone **must** go through [[lockout_tagout_training_and_incident_management|Lockout and Tagout Training]] provided by the [[lockout_tagout_roles_and_responsibilities#commissioning_hse_manager|Commissioning HSE Manager]].
*   Training can be conducted on Site or in an Aker Kvaerner Chemetics office.
*   Training **shall** be documented.
*   Training **shall** be entered into the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics log book that the [[lockout_tagout_roles_and_responsibilities#commissioning_hse_manager|Commissioning HSE Manager]] keeps.

**Incident and Near Miss Management**
*   All Lockout and Tagout incidents and near misses **shall** be thoroughly investigated.
*   All Lockout and Tagout incidents and near misses **shall** be documented.

**Logbook Review**
*   The [[lockout_tagout_roles_and_responsibilities#commissioning_hse_manager|Commissioning HSE Manager]] **will** conduct a monthly review of the tagging system logbook.
*   A more frequent review of the tagging system logbook **will** be performed if deemed necessary.
*   The review **will** consist of checking the active section against the index to ensure accountability of all active tagging orders.
*   The review **will** also include a spot check of active tagging orders to [[lockout_tagout_definitions#verify|verify]] compliance and identify trends of noncompliance with the procedure.
*   Furthermore, a spot check of tags **will** be performed to ensure they are still attached, on the correct equipment, and that the equipment status is as per the tagging order.
