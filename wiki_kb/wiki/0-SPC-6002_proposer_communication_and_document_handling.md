# Proposer Communication and Document Handling for Construction Subcontract Tenders (0-SPC-6002)

Effective communication and meticulous document handling are critical components of the construction subcontract tender process. Proposers **shall** adhere to the following mandatory procedures when interacting with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics regarding a Request for Tender.

**Acknowledgement and Intent to Respond**
Upon receipt of a Request for Tender, the Proposer **must** acknowledge its receipt within five working days. Furthermore, the Proposer **shall** state their intended response (e.g., whether they plan to submit an offer) within five working days of receiving the request. This ensures Aker Kvaerner Chemetics is kept informed of the Proposer's engagement status.

**Agreement Number on Correspondence**
To ensure proper tracking and organization, the relevant [[construction_subcontract_agreement_structure_and_data|Agreement]] number **must** appear prominently on all correspondence and documentation submitted by the Proposer. This requirement applies to all forms of communication and submitted materials throughout the tender process.

**Document Verification and Missing Documents**
The Proposer **shall** verify that they have received all documents referenced in the Agreement. In the event that any referenced documents are found to be missing, the Proposer **must** promptly advise the [[subcontract_administration_and_communication_0_spc_6001|Subcontract Administrator]] in writing. The Subcontract Administrator **will** then be responsible for issuing the missing documents to the Proposer.

**Queries and Clarifications**
All queries and requests for clarification regarding the Request for Tender **must** be submitted in writing to the Subcontract Administrator. This formalizes the communication process and ensures a clear record of all questions and answers. To maintain fairness and transparency among all participants, all proposers **will** be provided with a verbatim copy of each query received, along with the official response to that query. This ensures that all potential subcontractors have access to the same information and clarifications, preventing any informational advantage. Adherence to these communication protocols is essential for a transparent and equitable tender process.
