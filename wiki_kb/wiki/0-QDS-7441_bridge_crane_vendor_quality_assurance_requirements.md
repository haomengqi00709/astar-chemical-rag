# Bridge Crane Vendor Quality Assurance Requirements

This page details the mandatory quality assurance requirements for vendors supplying the Bridge Crane, as specified in Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7441. Adherence to these requirements ensures the quality and compliance of the supplied equipment.

**Vendor Quality Plan:**
The Vendor **must** prepare a Quality Plan. This plan **shall** be in accordance with [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment]]. If a Quality Plan is not required for a specific scope, the related cells in the Quality Data Sheet **shall** be deleted.

**Vendor Quality Assurance Compliance:**
The Vendor's Quality Assurance **shall** comply with the requirements set forth in this Quality Data Sheet (0-QDS-7441). Additionally, the Vendor's Quality Assurance **must** comply with [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment]].

**Chemetics Surveillance Level:**
Surveillance by Chemetics for the Bridge Crane project will be conducted to Level 0.0. All surveillance activities **shall** be as set out in this Quality Data Sheet. This includes witnessing various stages of inspection and testing to ensure compliance.

**Related Quality Activities and Surveillance:**
Chemetics' surveillance activities, typically requiring a "Witness" level, cover a range of critical quality control points throughout the manufacturing and supply process. These include:
*   [[0-QDS-7441_bridge_crane_material_verification_and_certifications]]
*   [[0-QDS-7441_bridge_crane_non_destructive_examination_requirements]]
*   [[0-QDS-7441_bridge_crane_coating_and_lining_inspection]]
*   [[0-QDS-7441_bridge_crane_equipment_testing]]
*   [[0-QDS-7441_bridge_crane_final_inspections]]
*   [[0-QDS-7441_bridge_crane_packing_and_shipping_requirements]]

For administrative details of this Quality Data Sheet, refer to [[0-QDS-7441_bridge_crane_quality_data_sheet_administration]].

**Note on Quality Plan Requirement:** The cells related to the Quality Plan should be deleted if a Quality Plan is not required for a specific project.
