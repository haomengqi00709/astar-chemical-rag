# Form of Purchase Order (0-PRC-5001)

This document, 0-PRC-5001, specifies the standard form and structure for Purchase Orders used on the Project. It ensures consistency and compliance in contractual agreements with vendors for equipment and materials.

Requisitions, Request for Quotation, and Purchase Orders will be issued in accordance with this procedure.
