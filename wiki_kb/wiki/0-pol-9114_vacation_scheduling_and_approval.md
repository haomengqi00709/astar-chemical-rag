# 0-POL-9114 Vacation Scheduling and Approval Process

The process for scheduling and obtaining approval for vacation time is a critical component of document 0-POL-9114, the Vacation Policy. This section outlines the responsibilities and procedures involved.

**Joint Responsibility**
The scheduling of vacation time is a collaborative process, requiring the joint responsibility and agreement of both the employee and their direct supervisor. This approach ensures that employee preferences are considered while also addressing departmental and company needs.

**Operational Requirements**
All vacation scheduling is inherently subject to the operational requirements of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. This stipulation ensures that business continuity, project deadlines, and essential functions are maintained without disruption, even while accommodating employee vacation requests. Supervisors **must** consider these operational needs when reviewing and approving vacation requests.

**Vacation Request Procedure**
Employees **must** submit their vacation requests by email to their supervisor. This formal submission method ensures proper documentation, provides a clear record of the request, and facilitates efficient communication between the employee and their supervisor.

**Mandatory Approval**
An employee **must** obtain explicit approval from their supervisor before any vacation time can be taken. Unauthorized vacation time is strictly not permitted and may lead to disciplinary action. Employees are advised to submit requests well in advance to allow for proper review and approval.

**Record Keeping and Coordination**
When approving requests for time off, the supervisor **should** copy the Office Services Co-ordinator. This action is crucial for updating central absence records, ensuring accurate tracking of employee leave, and facilitating proper resource planning across the Vancouver office. This step helps maintain an up-to-date overview of employee availability.

**Clarification of Line Manager Responsibility**
Revision 2 of this policy specifically clarified the responsibilities of line managers in the vacation scheduling and approval process. This revision aimed to emphasize their critical role in balancing employee needs for rest and rejuvenation with the ongoing operational demands of the company, ensuring a fair and effective vacation management system.

Scheduling is also subject to operational requirements. Employees must submit vacation requests via email to their supervisor and obtain explicit approval before any vacation time can be taken. When approving requests for time off, supervisors are required to copy the Office Services Co-ordinator to ensure central absence records are accurately updated.
