# Mist Eliminator Vendor Quality Assurance Requirements

This page outlines the mandatory quality assurance requirements for vendors supplying Mist Eliminator Mesh Pad Type equipment, as specified in Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7409.

**General Requirements:**
The Vendor's Quality Assurance **must** comply with the requirements set forth in this Quality Data Sheet (0-QDS-7409) and with [[0-SPC-7002_vendor_quality_assurance_bulk_materials]].

**Quality Plan:**
The Vendor **shall** prepare a Quality Plan. This plan **must** be in accordance with [[0-SPC-7002_vendor_quality_assurance_bulk_materials]].
*   **Note:** If a Quality Plan is not required for a specific procurement, the corresponding cells related to the Quality Plan in the source document (0-QDS-7409) **must** be deleted.

These requirements ensure that all supplied materials and equipment meet the specified quality standards and project expectations. Further details on general vendor quality assurance can be found in [[vendor_quality_assurance]] and [[vendor_quality_assurance_0_prc_7005]].
