# Construction Subcontract Scope Preparation (0-PRC-6091)

This procedure, 0-PRC-6091, outlines the methodology for preparing primary work description documents for construction subcontracts. These documents are essential as they are directly referenced by the [[construction_subcontract_agreement_procedure_0_prc_6002|Construction Subcontract Agreement (0-PRC-6002)]].

### Key Documents Prepared by this Procedure

1.  **Major Work Division Specifications (0-SPC-6X9X)**:
    *   A set of specifications, one for each Major Work Division, are prepared by the responsible Discipline. These include:
        *   0-SPC-6X92: Civil Construction and Site Preparation
        *   0-SPC-6X93: Structural and Buildings Construction
        *   0-SPC-6X94: Equipment Erection
        *   0-SPC-6X95: Piping Erection
        *   0-SPC-6X96: Insulation Erection
        *   0-SPC-6X97: Coatings Erection
        *   0-SPC-6X98: Instrumentation and Control Erection
        *   0-SPC-6X99: Electrical Erection
    *   The template for these specifications is "0-SPC-6X9X: [Template] [Major Work Division] Construction".
    *   **Numbering**: These specifications **must** be numbered using [[document_numbering_and_name_change_conventions|0-PRC-0002: Document Numbering]]. The "X" in the specification numbers **shall** be replaced by the same digit as for the [[construction_subcontract_agreement_procedure_0_prc_6002|Subcontract Agreement (0-PRC-6002)]].
    *   **Publication**: Specifications **must** be published with the status “Approved for Construction” using [[publishing_project_documents|0-PRC-0005: Publishing of Project Documents]].
    *   **Approval**: Specifications **must** be checked by the Manager of Construction and approved by the Project Engineer.
    *   **Content**: Each 0-SPC-6X9X specification directly references a [[construction_subcontract_supply_and_erection_scope_checklist|scope checklist]] and includes a "Specific Requirements" section (Section 6) to provide additional work description. Further details are available in [[construction_subcontract_major_work_division_specifications]].

2.  **Construction Subcontract Supply and Erection Scope Checklist (0-LST-6X90-1)**:
    *   This checklist itemizes the supply of equipment and materials, and the erection/construction work to be performed by the Subcontractor or others.
    *   It is prepared by Construction, based on [[supply_and_erection_scope_checklist|0-LST-2002: Supply and Erection Scope Checklist]].
    *   **Numbering**: **Must** be numbered using [[document_numbering_and_name_change_conventions|0-PRC-0002: Document Numbering]]. The "X" **shall** match the digit of the [[construction_subcontract_agreement_procedure_0_prc_6002|Subcontract Agreement (0-PRC-6002)]].
    *   **Publication**: **Must** be published with status “Approved for Construction” via [[publishing_project_documents|0-PRC-0005: Publishing of Project Documents]].
    *   **Approval**: **Must** be checked by responsible Disciplines and approved by the Project Engineer.
    *   Referenced by Section 2.1 of the [[construction_subcontract_agreement_procedure_0_prc_6002|Construction Subcontract Agreement (0-PRC-6002)]].

3.  **Construction Subcontract Field Services Scope Checklist (0-LST-6X90-2)**:
    *   This checklist details field services (e.g., water, power, offices) provided to the Subcontractor by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or the Owner, including any associated charges.
    *   It is prepared by Construction, based on [[field_services_scope_checklist|0-LST-2003: Field Services Scope Checklist]].
    *   **Numbering**: **Must** be numbered using [[document_numbering_and_name_change_conventions|0-PRC-0002: Document Numbering]]. The "X" **shall** match the digit of the [[construction_subcontract_agreement_procedure_0_prc_6002|Subcontract Agreement (0-PRC-6002)]].
    *   **Publication**: **Must** be published with status “Approved for Construction” via [[publishing_project_documents|0-PRC-0005: Publishing of Project Documents]].
    *   **Approval**: **Must** be checked by the Project Engineer and approved by the Manager of Construction.
    *   Referenced by Section 2.6 of the [[construction_subcontract_agreement_procedure_0_prc_6002|Construction Subcontract Agreement (0-PRC-6002)]].

4.  **Construction Facilities Layout Drawing (0-GAD-6X01)**:
    *   This drawing illustrates the location of field service connection points and designated areas for the Subcontractor's temporary facilities (e.g., offices, lunchrooms, parking).
    *   It is prepared by Design.
    *   **Numbering**: **Must** be numbered using [[document_numbering_and_name_change_conventions|0-PRC-0002: Document Numbering]]. The "X" **shall** match the digit of the [[construction_subcontract_agreement_procedure_0_prc_6002|Subcontract Agreement (0-PRC-6002)]].
    *   **Publication**: **Must** be published with status “Approved for Construction” via [[publishing_project_documents|0-PRC-0005: Publishing of Project Documents]].
    *   **Approval**: **Must** be checked by the Manager of Construction and approved by the Project Engineer.
    *   Referenced by Sections 2.5 and 2.6 of the [[construction_subcontract_agreement_procedure_0_prc_6002|Construction Subcontract Agreement (0-PRC-6002)]].

All applicable drawings, specifications, and other documents for a subcontract **must** be listed on an accompanying transmittal, as referenced by Section 2.2 of the [[construction_subcontract_agreement_procedure_0_prc_6002|Construction Subcontract Agreement (0-PRC-6002)]]. Direct referencing of applicable documents within the 0-SPC-6X9X specifications is only necessary if it enhances clarity or provides further description of the works.

---
The procedure also specifies that all drawings, specifications, and other documents applicable to the subcontract are to be listed on an accompanying transmittal. These applicable documents are further referenced by Section 2.2 of the [[construction_subcontract_agreement_procedure_0_prc_6002|Construction Subcontract Agreement (0-PRC-6002)]].
