# Preliminary Cost and Technical Review

The Requisitioning Discipline is responsible for conducting a preliminary evaluation of all bids received from Vendors. This initial assessment is documented using the template [[cost_tabulation_procurement]] (0-TAB-5XXX-1).

**Cost Tabulation Details:**
*   The Cost Tabulation **must** itemize and total the pricing for each line item of every bid received, including any alternates.
*   Pricing will be presented in the currencies quoted by the Vendors.
*   All itemized pricing and totals for each Vendor **must** be converted to Canadian dollars (CAD). This conversion will utilize project hedge rates where applicable, or forward rates when not hedged.
*   The cost associated with collapsing currency hedges **must** be assigned as an additional cost to any bid quoted in a currency different from the budgeted currency.
*   The budget in Canadian dollars, along with a notation of the budgeted currency, **must** be included for each tabulated line item.
*   If not all Vendors have quoted for every line item, a sub-tabulation **must** be prepared for the common items to facilitate an equalized comparison.

**Additional Cost Elements:**
The Requisitioning Discipline **must** list and quantify additional cost elements on the tabulation to ensure a proper comparison of the total cost to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for each bid. These elements may include:
*   Inspection
*   Quality surveillance
*   Freight
*   Associated structures
*   Piping
*   Utility connections
*   Erection costs
The applicability of each additional cost element to each bid **must** be indicated by a checkmark.

**Delivery Duration:**
The Requisitioning Discipline **must** list each Vendor’s quoted delivery duration on the tabulation. This duration will then be compared against the requirements specified in the [[project_schedule_and_milestones]].

**Preliminary Technical Merits and Vendor Experience:**
A preliminary review of the technical merits of each Vendor’s bid **must** be conducted by the Requisitioning Discipline. The acceptability of these technical merits will be indicated on the tabulation. Additionally, the tabulation **must** indicate whether the Vendor has previously supplied similar Goods and Services to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
