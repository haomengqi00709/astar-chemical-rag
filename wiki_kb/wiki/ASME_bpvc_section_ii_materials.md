# ASME Boiler & Pressure Vessel Code Section II - Materials

ASME Boiler & Pressure Vessel Code (BPVC) Section II provides comprehensive specifications for materials used in the construction of boilers, pressure vessels, and nuclear power plant components. It is divided into several parts:
*   **Part A:** Ferrous Material Specifications
*   **Part B:** Nonferrous Material Specifications
*   **Part C:** Specifications for Welding Rods, Electrodes, and Filler Metals
*   **Part D:** Properties (Customary and Metric)

This section of the ASME BPVC is referenced in [[4-SPC-0014_referenced_codes_and_definitions]] as a foundational document for material selection and properties in the fabrication of Columbium Stabilized High Nickel Austenitic stainless steel. It ensures that the materials used meet stringent quality and performance criteria for high-pressure and high-temperature applications.

For specific material requirements, always consult the relevant parts of ASME BPVC Section II.
