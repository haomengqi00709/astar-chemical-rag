# Specialty Item Drawing

A Specialty Item Drawing provides detailed design and installation information for unique or non-standard components within a piping or equipment system. These drawings are essential for ensuring that specialized items, such as [[bellows_inspection_5_prc_0007|bellows]], [[sight_glass_inspection_5_prc_0007|sight glasses]], [[pipe_grounding_ring_inspection_5_prc_0007|pipe grounding rings]], and other [[general_piping_specialty_item_inspection_5_prc_0007|piping specialty items]], are fabricated and installed correctly. They typically include dimensions, material specifications, connection details, and any specific installation instructions.
