# Lamella Clarifier Equipment and Material Specifications

This page outlines the key equipment data, ancillary components, and material selections for the Lamella Clarifier, as detailed in document [[4-PDS-XXXX-R1_template_clarifier_thickener_datasheet]]. These specifications are fundamental for the mechanical design, fabrication, and long-term reliability of the unit.

**Equipment Data:**
A critical performance parameter for the Lamella Clarifier is:
*   **Rise Rate (m/h at Normal Flowrate (VTC)):** This specifies the upward velocity of the liquid in the clarifier at normal operating flowrate, measured in meters per hour. The rise rate is a key design parameter influencing separation efficiency and clarifier sizing.

**Ancillary Equipment Options:**
The Lamella Clarifier can be equipped with various ancillary components to enhance its functionality and operational flexibility:
*   **Single Hopper:** A single collection cone or chamber at the bottom for concentrated solids.
*   **Double Hopper:** Two separate collection cones or chambers, potentially for different solid discharge points or improved solids handling.
*   **Collection Through:** A channel or trough designed to collect the clarified overflow liquid.
*   **Collection Weir:** A barrier over which the clarified liquid flows into the collection trough, ensuring even flow distribution.
*   **Lid:** A cover for the clarifier, which may be required for odor control, heat retention, or to prevent contamination.
*   **Insulation:** Thermal insulation applied to the clarifier body to maintain process temperature or protect personnel.
*   **Removable Plates - Single:** Individual lamella plates designed to be removed one by one for cleaning or maintenance.
*   **Removable Plates - Pack:** Lamella plates grouped into removable packs, facilitating easier and faster maintenance.

**Material of Construction:**
The selection of materials is critical to ensure corrosion resistance and structural integrity, given the process fluids. Key components and their specified materials include:
*   **Tank:** The main body of the clarifier.
*   **Plates:** The inclined lamella plates where separation occurs.
*   **Through:** The collection trough for clarified liquid.
*   **Weir:** The overflow weir.

The specific material for each of these fields **must** be chosen based on the [[lamella_clarifier_process_design_conditions]], particularly the fluid composition, pH, and temperature ranges.

**Equipment Sketch Guidelines:**
While a detailed equipment sketch is not always required, it serves a specific purpose:
*   An **Equipment Sketch is not mandatory** for standard configurations.
*   An **Equipment Sketch is only used to communicate special dimensions and features** that cannot be adequately conveyed through standard data fields or written descriptions. This ensures clarity for fabrication and installation of custom elements.
