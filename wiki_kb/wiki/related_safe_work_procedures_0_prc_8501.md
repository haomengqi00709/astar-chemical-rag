# Related Safe Work Procedures for 0-PRC-8501

The Safe Work Permit Procedure (0-PRC-8501) is part of a broader framework of safety procedures designed to ensure a safe working environment during plant commissioning and operation. Several other procedures provide specific guidelines for particular types of hazardous work that often require a safe work permit.

These related procedures include:
*   [[0-PRC-8002]]: This procedure details requirements for Open Flame and Hot Work, which are activities that generate heat, sparks, or flames and pose a fire hazard.
*   [[0-PRC-8003]]: This procedure covers Confined Space Entry, outlining the necessary precautions, permits, and safety measures for entering enclosed or partially enclosed spaces.
*   [[0-PRC-8004]]: This procedure addresses Lockout and Tagout protocols, which are critical for de-energizing and isolating equipment to prevent unexpected startup during maintenance or repair.
*   [[0-PRC-8005]]: This procedure provides guidelines for Line and Flange Break operations, ensuring safe disconnection of piping systems.

Adherence to these specific procedures, in conjunction with the general safe work permit requirements outlined in [[0-prc-8501_safe_work_permit_procedure_overview]], is mandatory for all personnel involved in these operations.
