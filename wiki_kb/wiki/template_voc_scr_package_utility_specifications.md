# Template VOC-SCR Package Utility Specifications

This page outlines the available utilities for the Template VOC-SCR Package, as specified in document 4-PDS-XXXX-R1. Understanding the available utilities is essential for the vendor to design and integrate the VOC/SCR unit effectively into the plant infrastructure.

**Available Utilities:**

The following utilities are available at the plant site for the operation of the VOC-SCR package:
*   Electrical power
*   Natural gas
*   Ammonia

Vendors **must** design their proposed package to utilize these available utilities. The specific consumption rates and connection requirements for each utility **shall** be detailed in the vendor's proposal. Any additional utility requirements not listed here **must** be clearly identified by the vendor.

For detailed specifications on utility fluids and design conditions, refer to [[utility_fluid_design_conditions]].
