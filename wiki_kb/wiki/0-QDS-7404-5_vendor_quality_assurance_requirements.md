# Vendor Quality Assurance Requirements for 0-QDS-7404-5

For Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7404-5, concerning the Electrostatic Precipitator Shell & Head Sections-FRP, specific requirements are mandated for the Vendor's Quality Assurance (QA) activities.

The Vendor **must** prepare a Quality Plan. This plan **shall** be in accordance with the guidelines specified in [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment]].

Furthermore, the Vendor's overall Quality Assurance program **must** comply with the requirements set forth in this Quality Data Sheet (0-QDS-7404-5). This compliance also extends to the general principles and specific directives outlined in [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment]].

Adherence to these requirements ensures that the fabrication and supply of the Electrostatic Precipitator Shell & Head Sections-FRP meet the necessary quality standards. For a broader understanding of vendor quality expectations, refer to [[vendor_quality_assurance]].
