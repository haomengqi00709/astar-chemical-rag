# Piping Component Classifications

Piping components are categorized into five distinct classifications to standardize design and manufacturing processes. These classifications dictate the specific requirements and practices to be followed for each component type.

**Type A: Standard Piping and Pipe Fittings**
*   **Design:** **Shall** be in accordance with referenced national standards.
*   **Manufacture:** **Shall** be in accordance with referenced national standards.

**Type B: Vendor Standard Design**
This classification includes components such as [[control_valves_general]] and speciality piping, which encompasses types like glass-lined piping (as referenced in Section 2.2 of the source document).
*   **Design:** **Shall** be performed by the vendor in accordance with their standard practices. Additionally, the design **shall** conform to referenced national standards.
*   **Manufacture:** **Shall** be performed by the vendor in accordance with their standard practices. Additionally, the manufacture **shall** conform to referenced national standards.

**Type C: Custom Fabricated Piping Component (Chemetics Design)**
This category covers pipe speciality items and special spools, such as chlorate cell headers and stacks (as referenced in Section 2.3 of the source document), which are specifically designed by Chemetics.
*   **Design:** **Shall** be performed by the vendor in accordance with referenced national standards. The design **shall** also adhere to the vendor’s standard practices.
*   **Manufacture:** **Shall** be performed by the vendor in accordance with referenced national standards. The manufacture **shall** also adhere to the vendor’s standard practices.

**Type D: Pipe Support**
Pipe supports are categorized based on their design origin:
*   **Vendor Standard Design:** Design and manufacture **shall** be by the vendor in accordance with their standard practices.
*   **Chemetics Design:** These are custom-fabricated supports.

These classifications ensure that all piping components meet the necessary technical, safety, and quality standards throughout their lifecycle, from design to installation.

**Type A: Standard Piping and Pipe Fittings**
*   **Design:** Shall be in accordance with referenced national standards.
*   **Manufacture:** Shall be in accordance with referenced national standards.

**Type B: Vendor Standard Design**
*   **Includes:** Valves, speciality piping (e.g., glass lined).
*   **Design:** By vendor in accordance with vendor’s standard practices and referenced national standards.
*   **Manufacture:** By vendor in accordance with vendor’s standard practices and referenced national standards.

**Type C: Custom Fabricated Piping Component; Chemetics Design**
*   **Includes:** Pipe speciality items, special spools (e.g., chlorate cell header and stacks) designed by Chemetics.
*   **Design:** By the vendor in accordance with referenced national standards and vendor’s standard practices.
*   **Manufacture:** By the vendor in accordance with referenced national standards and vendor’s standard practices.

**Type D: Pipe Support**
*   **Vendor Standard Design:** By vendor in accordance with vendor’s standard practices.
*   **Vendor Standard Manufacture:** By vendor in accordance with vendor’s standard practices.
*   **Chemetics Design:** Custom fabricated.
