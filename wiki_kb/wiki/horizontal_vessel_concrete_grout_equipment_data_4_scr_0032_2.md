# Horizontal Vessel on Concrete & Grout Equipment Data (4-SCR-0032-2)

This page details specific equipment data parameters for horizontal vessels supported on concrete and grout foundations, as outlined in document 4-SCR-0032-2. These parameters are crucial for the design and installation of the vessel's support system.

**Equipment Data Parameters:**
The following parameters define key aspects of the vessel's support and anchoring:

*   **Number of Anchor Bolts per Saddle:** This specifies the quantity of anchor bolts required for each saddle supporting the vessel.
    *   *Units:* ea (each)
*   **Dimension 'E3' - Bolt Position:** This dimension indicates a specific bolt position, critical for accurate layout and installation.
    *   *Units:* mm
*   **Dimension 'E4' - Bolt Position:** This dimension indicates another specific bolt position.
    *   *Units:* mm
*   **Dimension 'E5' - Bolt Position:** This dimension indicates a further specific bolt position.
    *   *Units:* mm
*   **Dimension 'E6' - Bolt Position:** This dimension indicates a final specific bolt position.
    *   *Units:* mm

These dimensions and quantities are integral to ensuring the correct fit and functionality of the sliding support system.
