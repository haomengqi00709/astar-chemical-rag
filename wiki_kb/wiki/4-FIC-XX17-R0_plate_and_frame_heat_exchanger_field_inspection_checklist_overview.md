# Plate and Frame Heat Exchanger Field Inspection Checklist Overview (4-FIC-XX17-R0)

This document, identified as 4-FIC-XX17-R0, serves as a template for the field inspection of Plate and Frame Heat Exchangers. It is developed for Aker Kvaerner, specifically for projects under the "Plate & Frame Heat Exchanger" title, originating from Chemetics, a division of Aker Kvaerner Canada Inc.

The main checklist section comprises 24 primary inspection items designed to ensure the proper installation and readiness of the heat exchanger. Mandatory requirements for field inspection include:

*   Nameplate **must** be installed and correct.
*   Foundation/Anchor bolts **must** be secure and tightened.
*   Inlet/outlet piping **must** be properly aligned and supported (no stress on nozzles).
*   Plate pack **must** contain the correct number of plates and gaskets.
*   Exchanger **must** be compressed to the supplier’s specification.
*   Exchanger **must** be clean (all foreign material removed from inlet/outlet ports).
*   Accessories (gauges, vents, drain valves etc.) **must** be installed.
*   Shroud **must** be installed around the plate pack.
*   Grounding **must** be completed.
*   Flange Guards **must** be installed.

The checklist template includes a dedicated section for recording Deficiencies & Comments, providing 10 numbered slots for detailed observations and corrective actions.
