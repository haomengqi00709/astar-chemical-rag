# Emergency Co-ordinator (ECO) Roles and Responsibilities - AKCTC

The Emergency Co-ordinator (ECO) is the designated individual responsible for managing emergency situations at the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Technology Centre (AKCTC).

**Authority and Discretion:**
The ECO will be in charge during emergency situations and may take actions that could deviate from established procedures as warranted by circumstances. This flexibility ensures an effective response to dynamic emergency conditions.

**Designation Priority:**
The ECO role is assigned based on the following priority:
1.  The AKCTC Supervisor (first priority).
2.  Core Members of the Safety Sub-Committee (second priority).

**Key Responsibilities:**
The ECO holds several critical responsibilities during an emergency:
*   The ECO **shall** be responsible for contacting external emergency services if necessary.
*   The ECO **shall** be responsible for contacting relevant government agencies if necessary.
*   The ECO will handle inquiries from the press or other mass media, acting as the sole point of contact for external communications during an emergency.
*   During an [[evacuation_procedures_akctc]], the ECO will immediately conduct a headcount of all personnel, including visitors, at the designated Emergency Assembly Point.
*   If not all personnel are accounted for during an evacuation, the ECO will co-ordinate re-entry and rescue efforts with external emergency services, such as the Fire Department and HAZMAT team.
*   The ECO is also the primary contact for individuals who have self-evacuated due to an uncontrollable situation, requiring them to report the nature of the emergency to the ECO (e.g., by re-entering the Centre from the office side).

For an overview of emergency response procedures at AKCTC, refer to [[0-PRC-8010_office_emergency_response_overview]].
