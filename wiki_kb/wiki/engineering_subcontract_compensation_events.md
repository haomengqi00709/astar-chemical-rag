# Engineering Subcontract Compensation Events (0-SPC-2201)

Under Professional Services Subcontract 0-SPC-2201, [[engineering_subcontract_compensation_events|compensation events]] are defined occurrences that may lead to adjustments in the [[subcontract_definitions_0_spc_2201|Prices]] and/or the [[subcontract_definitions_0_spc_2201|Completion Date]].

**Types of Compensation Events:**
Compensation events include, but are not limited to:
*   An instruction from Aker Kvaerner Chemetics changing the [[subcontract_definitions_0_spc_2201|Scope]].
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics failing to provide something required by the [[subcontract_definitions_0_spc_2201|Accepted Schedule]].
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics changing a previously communicated decision.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics failing to reply to a Subcontractor communication within the required period.
*   An instruction from Aker Kvaerner Chemetics to resolve an ambiguity or inconsistency in the Subcontract conditions or Scope, where Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ interpretation could not have been reasonably anticipated by the Subcontractor.
*   A breach of contract by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics not covered by other compensation events.
*   Services affected by:
    *   War, civil war, rebellion, revolution, insurrection, military, or usurped power.
    *   Strikes, riots, and civil commotion not confined to the employees of the Subcontractor and [[subcontract_definitions_0_spc_2201|Subsubcontractors]].
    *   Ionising radiation or radioactive contamination from nuclear fuel or waste resulting from nuclear fuel combustion.
    *   Radioactive, toxic, explosive, or other hazardous properties of an explosive nuclear device.
    *   Natural disaster.
    *   Fire and explosion.
    *   Impact by aircraft or other aerial devices or objects dropped from them.

**Notification and Quotation Process:**
*   The Subcontractor **must** notify Aker Kvaerner Chemetics of an event as a compensation event within five days of becoming aware of it, and before performing any work in response to the event.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may instruct the Subcontractor to submit quotations for a proposed instruction or changed decision. The Subcontractor **must not** implement such proposals until instructed.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **must** notify its decision or instruct the Subcontractor to submit quotations within one week of the Subcontractor’s notification, or a longer agreed period.
*   The Subcontractor **must** submit required quotations within one week of instruction. Quotations **must** include proposed changes to Prices and any delay to the Completion Date, along with assessment details. If the schedule is affected, a revised schedule **must** be included.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **must** reply within one week of quotation submission, either by instructing a revised quotation, accepting a quotation, notifying that a proposed instruction/decision will not be given, or notifying that Aker Kvaerner Chemetics will make its own assessment.
*   The cost of preparing quotations for compensation events is not included in their assessment.

**Conditions for No Change to Prices/Completion Date:**
The Prices and Completion Date are *not* changed if Aker Kvaerner Chemetics decides that:
*   The event notified by the Subcontractor is described in Clause 24.1 (7) (which refers to events arising from the Subcontractor's fault).
*   The event arises from a fault of the Subcontractor.
*   The event has not happened and is not expected to happen.
*   The event has no effect on the Subcontractor’s costs or planned Completion.
*   The event was notified later than required under Clause 25.1.
*   The event is not one of the compensation events stated in this Subcontract.

**Assessment and Implementation:**
*   Changes to Prices are assessed based on the effect on the [[subcontract_definitions_0_spc_2201|Time Charge]] for work done and forecast. Delay to the Completion Date is assessed as the length of time planned Completion is later due to the event.
*   Assessments assume the Subcontractor reacts competently and promptly, and that additional Time Charge and time are reasonably incurred.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics assesses a compensation event if the Subcontractor fails to submit a required quotation or details within the allowed time, or if there is no Accepted Schedule or a revised schedule has not been submitted.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **must** notify the Subcontractor of its assessment and provide details within two weeks of the need for assessment becoming apparent.
*   Each compensation event **must** be implemented in the form of a [[subcontract_change_order_procedure_0_chg_6xx0|Change Order]]. This Change Order **must** be signed on behalf of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics by the same person who executes the Agreement and **must** include changes to Prices and the Completion Date from the accepted quotation or Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' own assessment.

**Types of Compensation Events:**
Compensation events include, but are not limited to:
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics giving an instruction changing the Scope.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics not providing something he is to provide by the date required by the Accepted Schedule.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics changing a decision he has previously communicated to the Subcontractor.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics not replying to a communication from the Subcontractor within the period required by this Subcontract.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics giving an instruction to resolve an ambiguity or inconsistency in or between the conditions of Subcontract and the Scope, where Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ interpretation is not one that could have been reasonably anticipated by the Subcontractor.
*   A breach of contract by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics which is not one of the other compensation events in this Subcontract.
*   Services affected by war, civil war, rebellion, revolution, insurrection, military or usurped power.
*   Services affected by strikes, riots and civil commotion not confined to the employees of the Subcontractor and SubSubcontractors.
*   Services affected by ionising radiation or radioactive contamination from nuclear fuel or nuclear waste resulting from the combustion of nuclear fuel.
*   Services affected by radioactive, toxic, explosive or other hazardous properties of an explosive nuclear device.
*   Services affected by natural disaster.
*   Services affected by fire and explosion.
*   Services affected by impact by aircraft or other aerial device or thing dropped from them.

**Notification of Compensation Events:**
*   The Subcontractor notifies Aker Kvaerner Chemetics of an event as a compensation event within five days of becoming aware of the event and before doing any work in response to the event.

**Quotations for Compensation Events:**
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may instruct the Subcontractor to submit quotations for a proposed instruction or a proposed changed decision. The Subcontractor does not put a proposed instruction or a proposed changed decision into effect until instructed.
*   The Prices are not changed if the event notified by the Subcontractor is an event described in Clause 24.1 (7).
*   The Prices and the Completion Date are not changed if Aker Kvaerner Chemetics decides that an event arises from a fault of the Subcontractor, has not happened and is not expected to happen, has no effect upon the Subcontractor’s costs or planned Completion, was notified later than required under Clause 25.1, or is not one of the compensation events stated in this Subcontract.
*   If Aker Kvaerner Chemetics decides otherwise, he instructs the Subcontractor to submit quotations for the event. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics notifies his decision or instructs him to submit quotations within one week of the Subcontractor’s notification or a longer period to which the Subcontractor has agreed.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may instruct the Subcontractor to submit alternative quotations based upon different practicable ways of dealing with the compensation event.
*   The Subcontractor submits the required quotations to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics within one week of being instructed. The Subcontractor may also submit quotations for other practicable methods.
*   Quotations for compensation events comprise proposed changes to the Prices and any delay to the Completion Date assessed by the Subcontractor. The Subcontractor submits details of his assessment with each quotation.
*   If the Schedule for remaining work is affected by the compensation event, the Subcontractor includes a revised Schedule in his quotation showing the effect.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics replies within one week of the submission of quotations. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' reply is an instruction to submit a revised quotation, an acceptance of a quotation, a notification that a proposed instruction or a proposed changed decision will not be given, or a notification that he will be making his own assessment.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics instructs the Subcontractor to submit a revised quotation only after explaining his reasons for doing so. The Subcontractor submits the revised quotation within one week of being instructed.
*   The cost of preparing quotations for compensation events is not included in the assessment of compensation events.

**Assessment of Compensation Events:**
*   Changes to the Prices for compensation events are assessed as the effect upon the Time Charge for work already done and the forecast Time Charge for work not yet done.
*   A delay to the Completion Date is assessed as the length of time that planned Completion is later than planned Completion as shown on the Accepted Schedule due to the compensation event.
*   Assessments are based upon the assumptions that the Subcontractor reacts competently and promptly to the compensation event, that the additional Time Charge and time due to the event are reasonably incurred, and that the Accepted Schedule can be changed.
*   The Subcontractor includes proposed staff rates for people for whom there are no staff rates in his quotation for a change to the Scope, consistent with Prices in the activity schedule.
*   The cost of events for which this Subcontract requires the Subcontractor to insure and other costs paid to the Subcontractor by insurers are deducted from the assessment of compensation events.
*   Assessment for changed Prices for compensation events are in the form of changes to the activity schedule.

**Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' Own Assessment:**
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics assesses a compensation event if the Subcontractor has not submitted a required quotation and details of his assessment within the time allowed.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may not instruct the Subcontractor to submit a revised quotation if the Subcontractor has not submitted a Schedule required by this Subcontract or if Aker Kvaerner Chemetics has not accepted the Subcontractor's latest Schedule for reasons stated in this Subcontract.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics assesses a compensation event using its own assessment of the Schedule for the remaining work if there is no Accepted Schedule or if the Subcontractor has not submitted a revised Schedule for acceptance as required by this Subcontract.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics must notify the Subcontractor of its assessment of a compensation event and provide details within two weeks of the need for assessment becoming apparent.

**Implementation of Compensation Events:**
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics implements each compensation event by notifying the Subcontractor of the accepted quotation or its own assessment, or by authorising the Subcontractor to implement the event prior to quotation acceptance or assessment.
*   A compensation event must be implemented in the form of a Change Order. A Change Order must be signed on behalf of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics by the same person who executes the Agreement.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics must include changes to Prices and the Completion Date from the accepted quotation or its own assessment in its notice implementing a compensation event.
