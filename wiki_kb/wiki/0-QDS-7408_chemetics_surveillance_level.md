# Preheat Furnace Chemetics Surveillance Level

For the Preheat Furnace Package, surveillance by Chemetics **shall** be conducted to Level 1.0. The specific details and scope of this surveillance are as set out within this Quality Data Sheet, [[0-QDS-7408_document_details]].

This surveillance level is associated with a value of 2.0 in relevant quality documentation. Chemetics surveillance ensures adherence to specified quality standards throughout the manufacturing and inspection processes.

For general information on Aker Kvaerner Chemetics surveillance levels, refer to [[0-QDS-7_aker_kvaerner_surveillance]].
