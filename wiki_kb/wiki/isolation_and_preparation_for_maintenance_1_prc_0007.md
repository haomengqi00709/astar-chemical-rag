# Isolation and Preparation for Maintenance (1-PRC-0007)

This document, 1-PRC-0007, outlines the procedure for the safe isolation and preparation of plant equipment for maintenance activities. It details the steps required to de-energize, depressurize, and make equipment safe for personnel entry or work.

To control safety risks during [[water_and_air_trials_and_safety_protocols]], this procedure is strictly enforced. It is a critical component of the overall safety management system for the Project.
