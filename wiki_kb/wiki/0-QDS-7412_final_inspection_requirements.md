# Final Inspection Requirements for Centrifugal Pumps (0-QDS-7412)

This page outlines the final inspection requirements for Centrifugal Pumps, as specified in Quality Data Sheet [[0-QDS-7412_centrifugal_pump_quality_data_sheet_overview]]. These inspections are crucial to ensure the pump meets all specified quality, dimensional, and cleanliness standards before shipment.

**Final Inspection Activities:**
Final Inspection is a required item/activity, encompassing several key areas:

*   **Dimensional Inspection:**
    *   **Activity:** Dimensional inspection is a required item/activity.
    *   **Surveillance:** This inspection is required with Chemetics surveillance (witness). A Chemetics representative **must be present** to observe and verify the dimensional accuracy of the pump.

*   **Surfaces Free From Defects Inspection:**
    *   **Activity:** Inspection for surfaces free from defects is a required item/activity.
    *   **Surveillance:** This inspection is required with Chemetics surveillance (witness). A Chemetics representative **must be present** to visually inspect surfaces for any imperfections or defects.

*   **Cleanliness Inspection:**
    *   **Activity:** Cleanliness inspection is a required item/activity.
    *   **Surveillance:** This inspection is required with Chemetics surveillance (witness). A Chemetics representative **must be present** to verify the cleanliness of the pump, both internally and externally.

*   **Review QA Documentation:**
    *   **Activity:** Review of Quality Assurance Documentation is a required item/activity.
    *   **Surveillance:** This review is required with Chemetics surveillance (witness). A Chemetics representative **must be present** to review the compiled Quality Assurance Documentation Package to ensure completeness and compliance.

These comprehensive final inspections, with Chemetics' direct involvement, ensure that the Centrifugal Pump is ready for packaging and shipping, meeting all quality benchmarks.
