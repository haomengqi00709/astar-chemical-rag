# Caustic Concentration Package Process Design Conditions (4-PDS-XXXX-R1)

This page outlines the key process design conditions and performance guarantees for the Caustic Concentration Package, as detailed in document 4-PDS-XXXX-R1. These specifications are critical for ensuring the system operates within defined parameters and meets product quality requirements.

**Product Caustic Specifications:**
*   **Normal Composition**: 50.0 %w/w NaOH
*   **Composition Range**: 49.5 to 51.0 %w/w NaOH
*   **Maximum Temperature**: 40 oC
*   **Composition Guarantee**: The product composition *must* be 50 +/- 0.5 %w/w NaOH.

**Operating Conditions:**
*   **Cooling Water Return Temperature (maximum)**: VTC
*   **General Operating Pressure**: VTA kPa(g)

**Performance Guarantees:**
*   **Steam Flow Rate**: VTA kg/kg NaOH
*   **Cooling Water Flow Rate**: VTA kg/h

These conditions are established in accordance with overall [[process_design_criteria_1_prc_0001]] and contribute to the [[plant_availability_and_operation]].
