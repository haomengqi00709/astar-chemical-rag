# Standard Document Storage and Access

The secure and organized storage of standard documents is crucial for maintaining their integrity and ensuring accessibility across Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. All standard documents are managed through a centralized system on the company's network.

### Storage Location
Standard documents **shall** be stored in their native format on the network in the **Q Drive**. This designated location serves as the single source for all official standard documentation.

### Access Control
*   **Write-Access:** Exclusive write-access to the Q Drive is granted only to the [[standard_document_roles_and_responsibilities#Standards Administrator|Standards Administrator]]. This restriction ensures that documents are properly controlled and unauthorized modifications are prevented.
*   **Read-Access:** All employees of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **shall** have read-access to the Q Drive. This allows employees to view and copy standard documents for their use in accordance with company procedures.

### Version Control
Only the most recent approved version of any standard document **shall** be used for all work. This ensures that employees are always working with the up-to-date and officially sanctioned documentation.

### Permanent Records and Revision History
A permanent record of all approved documents **shall** be maintained by the [[standard_document_roles_and_responsibilities#Standards Administrator|Standards Administrator]] on the Q Drive.
*   **Approved Documents:** The subdirectory named "Approvals" on the Q Drive **shall** contain a PDF version of each approved standard. This PDF version **must** be digitally signed by both the Checker and the Approver, serving as official proof of approval.
*   **Revision History:** A copy of the previous revision of any standard document **shall** be retained for revision history purposes on the Q Drive. This previous revision copy **must** be clearly marked as "obsolete" and stored in the "Old Files" folder on the Q Drive. This practice ensures that a historical record of changes is maintained, supporting [[document_lifecycle_and_metadata]] and audit trails.
