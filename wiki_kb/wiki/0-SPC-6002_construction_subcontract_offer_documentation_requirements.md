# Construction Subcontract Offer Documentation Requirements (0-SPC-6002)

Proposers submitting offers for construction subcontracts **shall** adhere to specific documentation requirements to ensure a comprehensive and compliant submission. The offer to supply the Services **will** be irrevocable for a period of 90 days following the submission date, providing Aker Kvaerner Chemetics sufficient time for evaluation.

**Mandatory Documentation**
The Proposer **must** provide documentation completed in strict accordance with [[construction_subcontract_pricing_unit_price_0_spc_6005|0-SPC-6011: Construction Subcontract Pricing: Lump Sum]]. This includes, but is not limited to, the following specific lists and schedules:
*   [[construction_subcontract_pricing_lists|0-LST-6X11]]: Construction Subcontract Price List. Data available in SQL: 0-LST-6X11
*   [[construction_subcontract_pricing_lists|0-LST-6X12]]: Construction Subcontract Labour Rates. Data available in SQL: 0-LST-6X12
*   [[construction_subcontract_pricing_lists|0-LST-6X13]]: Construction Subcontract Crew Rates. Data available in SQL: 0-LST-6X13
*   [[construction_subcontract_pricing_lists|0-LST-6X14]]: Construction Subcontract Indirect Labour Schedule. Data available in SQL: 0-LST-6X14
*   [[construction_subcontract_pricing_lists|0-LST-6X15]]: Construction Subcontract Equipment Schedule. Data available in SQL: 0-LST-6X15

In addition to pricing documentation, the Proposer **shall** complete the survey provided, identified as [[construction_subcontractor_evaluation_and_approval_0_prc_6003|0-REP-6X0X: Construction Subcontractor Survey]]. This survey is a critical component of the subcontractor evaluation process.

Further mandatory submissions include:
*   A comprehensive list of all proposed sub-subcontractors.
*   A detailed Site Management Organisation Chart, illustrating the proposed structure for managing the works.
*   Resumes or Curriculum Vitae (C.V.'s) for all Key Persons named in the [[construction_subcontract_pricing_lists|0-LST-6X14: Construction Subcontract Indirect Labour Schedule]].
*   Proposed percentages for overhead and profit, which **will** be used by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics in assessing [[compensation_events_and_assessment_0_spc_6001|Compensation Events]].

**Optional and Compliance Documentation**
Proposers **may** supply other documentation they consider necessary to fully describe their offer. Any such additional documentation **should** be concise and **must** clearly indicate its purpose. However, Aker Kvaerner Chemetics is not obliged to consider documentation supplied other than that explicitly listed as mandatory above.

The Proposer’s offer **will** be considered in full compliance with the Agreement and all referenced documents unless any deviations or exceptions are explicitly stated and thoroughly described within the submitted offer. Failure to clearly state deviations **may** result in the offer being deemed non-compliant.
