# HSE Targets and Policy on EPC Projects

Aker Kvaerner Chemetics is committed to achieving specific Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) targets across all its EPC (Engineering, Procurement, and Construction) projects. This commitment is foundational to all operations and is explicitly outlined in the company's overarching HSE policy.

**HSE Targets:**
The company's HSE targets are set with a clear vision for operational excellence and responsibility:
*   Zero injuries.
*   Zero occupational illnesses.
*   Zero material loss.
*   Zero effect on the environment.

**HSE Policy Framework:**
The comprehensive commitment to health, safety, and the environment is formally established in Standard-0-POL-8000, titled "Aker Kvaerner HSE Policy". This document serves as the primary reference for the company's stance and directives on HSE matters. All personnel and subcontractors involved in Aker Kvaerner Chemetics projects **must** adhere to the principles and requirements set forth in this policy.

Related information on the administration of HSE procedures can be found in [[0-prc-8000_administration_of_hse]].
