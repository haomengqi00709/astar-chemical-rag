# Field Erected SS Tank Shop Component Vendor Quality Assurance Requirements

For the Field Erected Stainless Steel Tank Shop Component (0-QDS-7438), the Vendor's Quality Assurance program **must** comply with the requirements outlined in this Quality Data Sheet and with [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment]].

**Quality Plan:**
The Vendor **shall** prepare a Quality Plan in accordance with [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment]]. If a Quality Plan is not required for a specific project, any related cells in the Quality Data Sheet **should** be deleted.

**Chemetics Surveillance:**
Chemetics surveillance for this project **shall** be to Level 1.0, as further detailed in [[0-QDS-7438_chemetics_surveillance_level]]. The surveillance activities **must** be conducted as set out in this Quality Data Sheet.
