# Major Work Division Codes

Major Work Division Codes are used to categorize and group project activities for scheduling and reporting purposes. In the context of [[construction_subcontract_schedule_levels#Level 1 Schedule (Summary Schedule)|Level 1 Schedules]] for Construction Subcontractors, a single activity bar is typically shown for each major work division. This provides a high-level overview of the project's progress across its primary components.

For more detailed breakdowns, these major work divisions are further elaborated into grouped work activities in [[construction_subcontract_schedule_levels#Level 2 Schedule (Control Schedule)|Level 2 Schedules]].
