# HCl Synthesis Unit Nitrogen Utility Specifications

This page outlines the specifications for the nitrogen utility stream used in the HCl Synthesis Unit, as detailed in [[4-PDS-XXXX-R1_template_hcl_synthesis_unit_package_datasheet_administration]]. Nitrogen is an indispensable utility primarily used for purging, inerting, and emergency shutdown procedures, making its availability and characteristics critical for ensuring the safety of personnel and the operational integrity of the plant.

**Nitrogen Utility Parameters:**
The nitrogen supply is characterized by the following design conditions, which **must** be strictly adhered to for safety-critical operations:
*   **Temperature:** Ambient
*   **Required Pressure (Note 10):** VTA kPa(g)
*   **Volume per Purge (Note 8):** VTA Nm3
*   **Time per Purge (Note 8):** VTA Minutes

The ambient temperature requirement for nitrogen simplifies supply logistics and reduces the need for temperature conditioning. The required pressure, specified as VTA kPa(g), is subject to **Note 10**, which may detail the minimum pressure necessary for effective purging and inerting of process lines and vessels. The volume and time per purge, both denoted as VTA and subject to **Note 8**, are crucial parameters for ensuring complete and effective inerting of equipment during maintenance, start-up, shutdown, or emergency situations, thereby preventing the formation of explosive mixtures or hazardous atmospheres.

These parameters are vital for safe start-up, shutdown, and maintenance operations within the HCl Synthesis Unit. For general information on utility fluid design conditions, refer to [[utility_fluid_design_conditions]].
