# Hazardous Materials Packaging and Shipping (0-PRC-8406)

The packaging and shipping of hazardous materials **must** adhere to strict regulations and internal procedures to prevent incidents during transit. This section details the requirements for segregation, packaging, and consultation with shipping agents.

**Segregation and Packaging**
Hazardous items of different composition in a single [[purchase_order_commercial_and_contractual_terms|purchase order]] **will be** segregated and packaged separately. This prevents adverse reactions or confusion if different hazardous materials are shipped together.

**Shipping Agent Consultation**
The [[request_for_quotation_and_purchase_order_responsibilities|Purchasing Agent]] **will** consult with the Shipping Agent to ensure that hazardous items are adequately packaged. This consultation is critical for compliance and safety.

**Compliance with Regulations**
Packaging **must** comply with the following guidelines and regulations for the respective modes of shipment:
*   **Air Shipment:** IATA (International Air Transport Association) guidelines.
*   **Air Shipment:** TDG (Transportation of Dangerous Goods) regulations.
*   **Ground Freight:** TDG regulations.
*   **Ocean Freight:** TDG regulations.

Adherence to these international and national standards is mandatory to ensure the safe transportation of hazardous materials and to avoid legal and safety repercussions.
