# Final Inspections for 0-QDS-7440

This page outlines the requirements for final inspections and associated documentation and surveillance activities under Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7440.

**Item 7.0: Final Inspection**
A comprehensive final inspection **must** be conducted to verify the overall quality and adherence to specifications of the Converter Quartz.

**Item 8.0: Dimensional Record**
A dimensional record **is required** as an FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package) quality record. This record **must** document all critical dimensions of the product. Dimensional surveillance by Chemetics **is required** to be Complete, meaning Chemetics will fully verify the dimensional accuracy.

**Item 9.0: Quantity Record**
A quantity record **is required** as an FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package) quality record. This record **must** accurately state the quantity of items. Quantity surveillance by Chemetics **is required** to be Complete, ensuring full verification of the quantities.

**Item 10.0: Review QA Documentation**
Surveillance by Chemetics for the review of Quality Assurance Documentation **is required** to be Complete. This ensures that all submitted QA documentation is thoroughly checked and approved. No specific Quality Record type (AP or FP) is required for Review QA Documentation itself, as it is an activity of surveillance.

For definitions of AP and FP quality record types, refer to [[0-QDS-7440_quality_record_definitions]].
