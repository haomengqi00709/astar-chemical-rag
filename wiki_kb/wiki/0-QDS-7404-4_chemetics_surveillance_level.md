# 0-QDS-7404-4 Chemetics Surveillance Level

For the Electrostatic Precipitator Miscellaneous Items covered by Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7404-4, the level of surveillance by Chemetics is designated as Level 0.0.

**Surveillance Level 0.0:**
A surveillance level of 0.0 indicates that Chemetics will primarily rely on the Vendor's established Quality Assurance (QA) system and documentation. This level implies minimal direct, on-site inspection or intervention by Chemetics personnel during the manufacturing or fabrication process. Instead, Chemetics' oversight will focus on reviewing the vendor's submitted quality records and certifications to confirm compliance.

**Compliance and Oversight:**
While direct surveillance is minimal, the Vendor's Quality Assurance **shall** still comply fully with the requirements outlined in this Quality Data Sheet (0-QDS-7404-4). Furthermore, the surveillance activities by Chemetics will also be conducted as specifically set out within the various sections of this Quality Data Sheet, particularly concerning the review of mandatory quality records.

This approach ensures that even with a lower surveillance level, the responsibility for quality remains firmly with the vendor, with Chemetics maintaining an oversight role through documentation review. For general information on Chemetics' surveillance levels and vendor quality assurance, refer to [[0-QDS-7_aker_kvaerner_surveillance]] and [[vendor_quality_assurance]].
