# Computer Room Fire Extinguishing System Procedure

This procedure, Document ID 0-PRC-8011, outlines the mandatory actions for employees in the Vancouver computer room during the activation of its automated fire extinguishing system.

**System Overview**
The Vancouver computer room is protected by an automated fire extinguishing system that utilizes FM-200 gas as its fire suppression agent. The system is engineered to flood the room with a sufficient concentration of FM-200 gas to effectively extinguish a fire. FM-200 gas functions by removing heat energy from the fire. A critical safety feature of FM-200 gas is that it does not remove oxygen from the environment, nor does it deplete oxygen in the room, making it considered safe for humans. The system is specifically designed to allow personnel adequate time to evacuate the room before the gas is discharged. Should the system deploy while individuals are still in the room, they can continue to breathe normally.

**System Activation Sequence**
Upon activation, the following events occur:
*   An alarm sounds within the computer room.
*   The building fire alarm is activated.
*   A light on the ceiling outside the computer room door is illuminated.
*   There is a 30-second delay before the FM-200 gas is discharged.

**Actions During System Activation**
In the event of system activation:
*   Everyone in the computer room at the time of activation **MUST** leave the room immediately.
*   Everyone leaving the room **MUST** ensure the door is closed quickly to contain the gas.
*   Personnel leaving the room **MUST** continue to evacuate the building as per [[0-PRC-8010_office_emergency_response_overview|Office evacuation procedures]].

The ABORT BUTTON for the system **SHOULD** only be used if the system is inadvertently activated.

**Entry Procedures**

**Before System Discharge (Light On)**
*   Personnel **MUST** check the light outside the computer room door before attempting to enter.
*   If the light outside the computer room door is on, personnel **MUST NOT** enter the computer room.
*   If the light is on, personnel **MUST NOT** attempt to open the door until 90 seconds have elapsed since the alarm activation.
*   If the light is on and 90 seconds have elapsed, personnel **MUST** use extreme caution and open the door slowly.

**After System Discharge**
*   Personnel **MUST** use extreme caution when attempting to enter the room after a discharge.
*   Personnel **MUST** be sure that 90 seconds have elapsed since the alarm activation before attempting entry.
*   Entry into the room **MUST** be done with extreme caution.
*   Personnel **MUST** check the door for signs of heat on the inside of the room before opening it.
*   If no signs of heat are detected, personnel **MUST** open the door slowly and with caution.

**Post-Discharge Re-entry**
After a discharge, and once the room has been declared safe by the fire marshal, the room **SHOULD** be well ventilated before re-entering.
