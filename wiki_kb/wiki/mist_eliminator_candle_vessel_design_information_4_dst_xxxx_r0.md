# Mist Eliminator Candle Vessel Design Information (4-DST-XXXX-R0)

This page details the mandatory vessel design information required for the Mist Eliminator Candle Datasheet (4-DST-XXXX-R0). This information is crucial for integrating the mist eliminator candles into the larger vessel structure and ensuring proper fit and function.

**Vessel Description and Support:**
*   **Vessel Description:** A description of the vessel in which the candles will be installed is required.
*   **Support Arrangements:** Details regarding the support arrangements for the candles within the vessel are required.

**Vessel and Candle Dimensions:**
*   **Vessel Inside Diameter:** The inside diameter of the vessel in mm is required.
*   **Design Wet Weight of Candle:** The design wet weight of a single candle in kg is required.
*   **Candle Clearance to Vessel:** The clearance between the candle and the vessel in mm is required.
*   **Candle Clearance to Candle:** The clearance between individual candles in mm is required.

**Maintenance and Structural Considerations:**
*   **Device for Candle Removable:** The device or method for candle removal must be specified.
*   **Tubesheet Max Deflection:** The maximum allowable tubesheet deflection in mm is required.

This vessel design information is directly linked to the [[mist_eliminator_candle_physical_specifications_4_dst_xxxx_r0]] and ensures that the candles are properly accommodated and accessible within the overall equipment.
