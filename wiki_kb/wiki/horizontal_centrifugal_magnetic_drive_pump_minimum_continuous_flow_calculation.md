# Horizontal Centrifugal Magnetic Drive Pump Minimum Continuous Flow Calculation

When a [[pump_data_sheets]] specifies a flow rate value in conjunction with ‘Vendor Minimum Continuous Flow’ (MCF), the Vendor **will** clearly state their recommended MCF rate for the pump being selected.

The Vendor **will** then add their recommended MCF to the flow rate value provided on the Data Sheet to determine the final duty point.

**Example:**
If a [[pump_data_sheets]] indicates a flow rate of 10 m3/h + ‘Vendor Minimum Continuous Flow’, and the Vendor's recommended MCF rate for the specific pump is 1 m3/h, then the calculated duty point for the pump **will** become 11 m3/h.

For broader information on minimum flow requirements for pumps, consult [[pump_minimum_flow_requirements]].
