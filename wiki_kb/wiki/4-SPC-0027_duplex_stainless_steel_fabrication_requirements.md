# Duplex Stainless Steel Fabrication Requirements (4-SPC-0027)

Fabrication of duplex stainless steel, specifically Alloy 2205, **must** adhere to stringent quality and workmanship standards as defined in Specification 4-SPC-0027. While recommendations from the alloy producer and fabricator are valuable, the requirements outlined in this specification take precedence.

Key fabrication practices **must** include:
*   **Cleanliness and Handling:** Good stainless steel welding and electrode handling practices **must** be employed. This includes maintaining excellent cleanliness, ensuring electrodes and fillers are dry, meticulously avoiding iron contamination, and achieving proper alignment and fit-up of components.
*   **Weld Cleaning:** All process-exposed surfaces of completed welds **must** be pickled or acid cleaned to thoroughly remove any heat tint or oxides formed during the welding process. For non-process exposed welds, grinding or brushing may be sufficient for cleaning. It is important to note that pickling requirements are in addition to any grinding that might be necessary for surface preparation or finishing.
*   **Surface Finish:** All weld spatter, arc strikes, and slag **must** be completely removed from the finished equipment to ensure optimal surface integrity and corrosion resistance.
*   **End Grain Protection:** To prevent exposure to the chemical process, no end-grain containing surfaces on the pressure envelope of the 2205 material will be exposed. Specifically, nozzle neck pipe end grain **must** be covered. However, vessel internal support ring edges do not require weld capping.
*   **Shielding Gas:** When utilizing inert gas shielded wire welding processes, conservative torch and back-side shielding are extremely important to protect the weld from atmospheric contamination and ensure proper metallurgical properties.
*   **Weld Configuration:** Double-sided welds **must** be used whenever technically feasible to enhance structural integrity and reduce the risk of defects. Any one-sided chemical process exposed welds **must** be clearly highlighted and indicated on the fabricator’s drawings for specific attention during inspection and operation.
