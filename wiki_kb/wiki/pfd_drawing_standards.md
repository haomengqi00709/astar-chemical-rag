# PFD Drawing Standards and Conventions

Adherence to specific drawing standards and conventions is essential for ensuring clarity, consistency, and accuracy in [[pfd_overview|Process Flow Diagrams (PFDs)]]. These guidelines cover everything from equipment representation to line types and drawing formats.

**Templates and Forms:**
*   Standard Form 1-PFD–0001: [Template Process Flow Diagram]
*   Standard Form 1-PFD-0002: [Template: Equipment Shape]

**Clarity and Layout:**
*   Clarity of presentation is important for PFD sketches.
*   All main process and utility lines **must** be shown on PFD sketches.
*   Grouping and crowding equipment at the expense of clarity should be avoided in PFD sketches.
*   Equipment **shall** be drawn approximately in proportion to their actual sizes on PFD drawings.
*   Equipment **shall** be drawn in a sequence of flow, typically from left to right, on PFD drawings.
*   Each equipment item, including those with duplicate sets, is to be shown on PFD drawings.

**Line Types:**
*   **Major Process Lines:** Will be represented by solid thick lines.
*   **Minor Process Lines and Utility Lines:** Will be represented by solid thin lines.
*   **Existing Equipment:** Will be represented by dashed lines.

**Drawing Format:**
*   The first page of the set of drawings will show the Project Design Capacity.
*   A standard drawing border showing the vertical block is **required**.
*   Non-standard borders may be requested by the Client.
*   Standard drawing sizes may be metric “A1” or Imperial “D” size.

**Drawing Numbering:**
For PFD drawing numbers, refer to [[document_numbering|0-PRC-0002: Document Numbering]].

For more information on the graphical components of PFDs, refer to [[pfd_sketch_and_graphics]].
