# Electrical Control Logic Narrative

The Electrical Control Logic Narrative describes the operational logic for various pieces of electrical equipment within the plant. This document is essential for understanding how electrical systems are controlled, including their alarms, trips, and interlocks.

**Content of the Electrical Control Narrative**
The electrical control narrative **shall** describe the logic to be employed for the control of:
*   Transformer/rectifier (T/R) systems.
*   Central Processing Units (CPU).
*   Harmonic filter packages.
*   Circuit breakers feeding the transformer/rectifier.
*   Circuit breakers feeding the harmonic filter package.
*   Process inputs that interact with electrical equipment controls.

This narrative provides a detailed explanation of the sequence of operations, conditions for starting and stopping equipment, and the behavior of the system under various operating scenarios. It also details the integration of alarms, trips, and interlocks to ensure safe and efficient plant operation.
