# Subcontract Documents

Subcontract documents define the specific terms, conditions, and scope of work for services or supplies provided by a subcontractor. These documents are crucial for outlining responsibilities, deliverables, and contractual obligations within a project.

For specific details regarding standard conditions for construction subcontracts, refer to [[0-SPC-6001_standard_conditions_for_construction_subcontract]].
