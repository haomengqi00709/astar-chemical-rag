# Roles and Responsibilities: Chief Warden

A Chief Warden is an employee who serves as the liaison between the building emergency crew and the Chief Incident Commander for the building.

**Initial Actions During an Emergency**
*   The Chief Warden **must** exit the building safely as any other employee.
*   The Chief Warden **must** immediately go to the ground floor storage area.
*   The Chief Warden **must** put on the green vest and red hard hat.
*   The Chief Warden **must then** go to the front entrance door.

**Reporting and Liaison Duties**
*   The Chief Warden **must** take the reports from each of the Floor Wardens as they arrive (see [[0-PRC-8010_roles_and_responsibilities_floor_warden]]).
*   The Chief Warden **must** check that all Floor Wardens and/or the backup Floor Warden have reported.
*   If only one Warden is present, the Chief Warden **must** ascertain the reason for the absence of the other.
*   The Chief Warden **must** use the checklist in Appendix A for a record.
*   The Chief Warden **must** make a report to the Chief Incident Commander for the building.
*   The Chief Warden **must** liaise with the Chief Incident Commander on any matters pertaining to the emergency.

**Evacuation Time and Return Notification**
*   The Chief Warden **must** keep track of the time it took for the evacuation.
*   The Chief Warden **must** help the Chief Incident Commander notify the people in the gathering area when the Vancouver Fire Chief has declared the building safe.
