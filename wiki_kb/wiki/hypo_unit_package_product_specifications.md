# Hypo Unit Package Product Specifications

This page details the specifications for the Sodium Hypochlorite solution produced by the Hypo Unit Package.

**Fluid Description:**
*   The product is Sodium hypochlorite solution.

**Composition (%w/w unless otherwise specified):**
*   **NaOH**: 0.1 - 0.5 %w/w
*   **H2O**: Balance %w/w
*   **NaOCl**: %w/w (Data available in SQL: Sheet1)
*   **NaClO3**: ppm w/w (xx Max.) (Data available in SQL: Sheet1)

**Process Conditions:**
*   **Temperature**: 40 oC (maximum)
*   **Pressure**: kPa(g) (xx (max)) (Data available in SQL: Sheet1)

**Flow Rates:**
*   **Normal Flow Rate**: kg/h (VTA) (Data available in SQL: Sheet1)
*   **Maximum Flow Rate**: kg/h (VTA) (Data available in SQL: Sheet1)

**Operational Notes:**
*   Under normal flow conditions (Note 2), the bleach produced will be dilute with a wide range of possible caustic levels.
*   Under normal flow conditions (Note 2), the bleach and caustic strength will tend to dilute from the water in the feed gas.
*   The bleach is exported on [[level_measurement_general|level control]].
*   Off-spec, dilute bleach is routed to the [[hydecat_hypo_destruction_reactor_datasheet_administration|Hypo Destruct Unit]] directly from the Hypo Tower Pump.

For related information on feed streams, see [[hypo_unit_package_bleach_unit_feed_specifications]] and [[hypo_unit_package_scrubber_unit_feed_specifications]].

**Process Conditions:**
*   **Temperature**: 40 oC (max)

*   **Pressure**: kPa(g) (xx (max)) (Data available in SQL: Sheet1)

**Off-spec Product Handling:**

*   Under normal flow conditions (Note 2), the bleach and caustic strength will tend to dilute from the water in the feed gas.
