# Commercial Terms for Construction Subcontract (0-PRC-6003)

This document, 0-PRC-6003, defines the commercial terms and conditions applicable to construction subcontracts for the Project. It ensures that all financial and contractual aspects are consistently applied and managed during the construction phase.

Subcontracts will be prepared and issued in accordance with this procedure.
