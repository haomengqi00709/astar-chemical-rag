# Employee Responsibilities Regarding Substance Use

All Employees, Consultants, and Agency Subcontractors (**Employees**) have specific responsibilities under Chemetics' Substance Use Policy to ensure a safe and productive work environment.

### Performance Impairment
*   Employees **SHALL** ensure that their work performance is not impaired by alcohol or any substances that may affect their mental or physical abilities.

### Client Policies
*   Employees **MUST** be familiar with [[client_site_substance_use_policies|Client drug and alcohol policies]] when working at any Client work site.
*   Should an Employee have concerns regarding Client policies, the Employee **SHOULD** raise such concerns with Human Resources as soon as they become aware of the Client policies.

### Prescription and Non-Prescription Drugs
*   Employees taking [[substance_use_policy_scope_and_definitions#prescription_drug|Prescription Drugs]] or [[substance_use_policy_scope_and_definitions#non-prescription_drug|Non-Prescription (over-the-counter) Drugs]] are responsible for being aware of any potential effects these drugs may have on their reactions, judgment, or ability to perform their duties.
*   Employees are responsible for ensuring that their performance is not impaired by the use of Prescription Drugs or Non-Prescription (over-the-counter) Drugs.
*   If an Employee suspects that work performance may be impaired by the use of such drugs, they **SHOULD** discuss alternate job duties or other appropriate actions with their manager.

### Conduct During Travel
*   For the duration of travel while on Chemetics business, Employees are expected to act responsibly in their 'off hours' to ensure they can perform their work duties, protect their own safety and the safety of others, and not compromise Chemetics’ interests.
