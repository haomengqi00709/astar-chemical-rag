# Vehicle Use and Driving Safety During Business Travel

Strict guidelines are in place regarding vehicle use and driving safety for employees on business travel, particularly in High or Extreme risk areas. These measures are critical for minimizing risks and ensuring compliance with the [[0-prc-8102_travel_health_and_safety|Travel Health, Safety, and Security Overview]].

**Restrictions in High or Extreme Risk Areas:**
*   In High or Extreme risk areas, employees **are not permitted** to drive a personal vehicle, outside the work compound, for either business or pleasure.
*   Travel within High or Extreme risk areas **should be** pre-arranged with the client or a reliable local transport company to ensure safety and security.

**General Vehicle Operation Requirements:**
*   Employees **are not permitted** to operate any motorized vehicle for which they are not legally licensed.
*   Employees **are not permitted** to operate any motorized vehicle for which they do not possess a certificate of competency in the jurisdiction where they are operating.
*   Leased cars or hire cars **must have** seat belts for the driver and all passengers.
*   Headrests, driver airbags, and ABS brakes **are recommended** where available for leased or hire cars.
*   The driver and all passengers **must** wear seat belts at all times if the car is equipped with them.
*   No driving **is permitted** while impaired by alcohol or drugs. Refer to the [[substance_use_policy_scope_and_definitions|Substance Use Policy]] for further details.
*   The driver **shall not** use a hand-held mobile phone while driving.
*   The use of hands-free mobile phones **is strongly discouraged** while driving due to potential distractions.

**Driving Time and Rest Periods:**
*   Where personal driving is allowed, employees **are expected** to use good judgment regarding the length of time they spend driving.
*   The maximum work time, including driving a vehicle, **should be** limited to 14 hours per day.
*   Overnight accommodation **shall** be provided where necessary for long driving days to ensure adequate rest and prevent fatigue-related incidents.

Adherence to these rules is mandatory to protect employees and maintain safe travel practices.
