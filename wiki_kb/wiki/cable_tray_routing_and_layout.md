# Cable Tray Routing and Layout Drawings

Cable Tray Routing and Layout drawings detail the proposed and actual physical paths of cable trays throughout the plant. These drawings are crucial for planning cable management, ensuring adequate space, and guiding installation.

**Cable Tray Routing Criteria Drawings**
Proposed cable tray routing criteria drawings **shall** show:
*   A proposed routing space on the pipe racks.
*   A proposed routing space in the process buildings.
*   The overall space envelope for the cable trays.

These drawings **will not** indicate the type or sizes of the cable trays, nor **will they** show cable tray routings within the electrical rooms.

**Cable Tray Layout Drawings**
Cable tray layout drawings **shall** show:
*   The actual routing on the pipe racks.
*   The actual routing in the buildings.
*   The branch runs between the pipe racks and the buildings.

Cable tray layouts **shall** be dimensioned to assist the contractor for installation.
The various types of cable trays used **must** be identified.
Accessories such as support brackets **must** be identified.
Materials **will** be identified on the drawings.
Trays requiring covers **must** be identified.
Voltage levels allowed in each tray **must** be identified.
Section views to show the arrangement of the cables **must** be shown.
Grounding conductors **must** be shown where required.

An overall cable tray drawing **shall** show the runs of the cable trays and the estimated cable weights per tray.
