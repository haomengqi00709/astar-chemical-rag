# Ejector Mechanical Design Requirements

This page outlines the mechanical design requirements for ejector packages, as specified in the Template Ejector Datasheet (Document ID [[template_ejector_datasheet_administration]]). Adherence to these requirements ensures the structural integrity, safety, and operational suitability of the ejector.

**Key Mechanical Design Requirements:**
*   The mechanical design shall comply with Specification 4-SPC-1068/0001.
*   **Equipment Location:** This is a mandatory mechanical design consideration.
*   **Site Conditions and Utilities:** These factors are mandatory mechanical design considerations. Refer to [[site_design_conditions_1_dst_0001]] for general site conditions.
*   **Electrical Area Classification:** This is a mandatory mechanical design consideration. Refer to [[electrical_area_classification_requirements]] for classification details.
*   **Dimensional Limit:** This is a mandatory mechanical design consideration.

For broader guidelines on mechanical design, refer to [[general_mechanical_design_requirements_0_spc_0010]].
