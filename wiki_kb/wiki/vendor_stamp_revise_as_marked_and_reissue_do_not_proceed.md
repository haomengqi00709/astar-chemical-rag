# Vendor Stamp: Revise as Marked and Reissue (Do Not Proceed)

The "REVISE AS MARKED AND REISSUE" vendor stamp, specifically the version presented on Page 3 of the vendor stamp procedure document, indicates that revisions are required and the vendor **must not** proceed with the work shown until the revisions are incorporated and reissued.

**Mandatory Requirements for Application:**
*   The phrase "DO NOT PROCEED WITH WORK SHOWN" **must** be clearly indicated.
*   A signature **shall** be applied to the stamp.
*   The date of application **shall** be recorded on the stamp.

This stamp is used when the required revisions are critical and proceeding with the work without addressing them could lead to significant issues. This stamp is associated with [[chemetics_aker_kvaerner_company_details|CHEMETICS]] documentation standards.

For an overview of all vendor stamp options, refer to [[vendor_stamp_procedure_overview]]. For the "REVISE AS MARKED AND REISSUE" stamp that permits work, see [[vendor_stamp_revise_as_marked_and_reissue_proceed]].

**Mandatory Requirements for Application:**
*   The phrase "DO NOT PROCEED WITH WORK SHOWN" must be included.
*   A signature is required.
*   A date is required.
This stamp is specifically used for Chemetics projects.
