# Car Rental Policy

The Group Travel Procedure (11.1 PR 2) outlines the policy for car rental, emphasizing cost efficiency and adherence to corporate agreements.

### Exclusive Use of Avis
The Group has established a global agreement with Avis for car rental services.
*   Avis is to be used exclusively for car rental, unless they do not operate at the specific location where transport is required.
*   If Avis is unavailable at a location, the designated travel agency **will** provide recommendations for the best alternative options.

### Minimizing Car Rental and Size
Car rental **should** be kept to a minimum, and the smallest practical size of car **should** be rented to control costs.
*   If two or more employees are travelling together, a Group B or C (medium size) car **should** be used to accommodate the group efficiently.

### Insurance Coverage in the United States
Travellers renting vehicles for business purposes in the United States through Avis **should** decline all additional insurance coverage offered.
*   Avis rates are inclusive of insurance in the United States, with the exception of Alaska.
*   Travellers **should** be aware of the insurance coverage provided by their Business Area or Company before accepting any additional insurance from the rental company.

### Fuel Policy
To avoid additional administration charges beyond the cost of fuel replacement, the fuel tank **should** be refilled before returning the rental car.
