# 9-PRC-0005 Electrical Safety Precautions

This document, 9-PRC-0005, outlines special precautions for the safe use of electricity within Aker Kvaerner Chemetics designed plants. These plants are facilities where flammable materials are manufactured, processed, handled, or stored.

The primary concern of this procedure is to address potential sources of ignition from electricity, specifically electrical arcs, sparks, and heating effects, in areas where ignitable mixtures may be present.

**Mandatory Requirements:**
*   Electrical equipment and systems installed in hazardous (classified) locations **shall not** provide a means of ignition for an ignitable mixture.
*   A prime requirement **must** be the avoidance, as far as possible, of the creation of a flammable atmosphere in the design and layout of plant and plant equipment.

For an ignition to occur, both a flammable atmosphere and a source of ignition **must** exist simultaneously.

**Referenced Standards:**
Recommendations within this document are based on the following codes and practices:
*   NFPA 70 National Electrical Code (USA) Article 500: Hazardous (Classified) locations.
*   NFPA 70 National Electrical Code (USA) Article 501.
*   CEC Canadian Electrical Code Section 18: Hazardous Locations.
*   NFPA 497 Recommended Practice for the Classification of Flammable Liquids, Gases, or Vapours and of Hazardous (Classified) Locations for Electrical Installations in Chemical Process Areas.
