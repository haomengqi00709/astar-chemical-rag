# Hypo Unit Package Bleach Unit Feed Specifications

This page details the specifications for the gas feed to the Bleach Unit within the Hypo Unit Package.

**Fluid Description:**
*   The Bleach Unit Gas Feed consists of cooled, wet chlorine gas (Note 1 & 2).

**Composition (kg/h):**
*   Cl2 (Data available in SQL: Sheet1)
*   H2O (Data available in SQL: Sheet1)
*   Air (Data available in SQL: Sheet1)
*   HCl (Data available in SQL: Sheet1)

**Process Conditions:**
*   **Temperature**: oC (Note 2) (Data available in SQL: Sheet1)
*   **Minimum Pressure**: kPa(g) (Data available in SQL: Sheet1)
*   **Normal Pressure**: kPa(g) (Note 2) (Data available in SQL: Sheet1)
*   **Maximum Pressure**: kPa(g) (Note 8) (Data available in SQL: Sheet1)

**Operational Notes:**
*   Note 6.0 refers to the maximum possible pressure off the cellroom, which is the relief pressure of the Cl2 pressure seal.
*   The bleach unit **must** be able to process 100% of this specified feed diverted from the HCl plant to the bleach unit, without tripping the unit on high ORP or other process upset, especially under [[hypo_unit_package_operational_modes_and_emergency_conditions|emergency conditions]].

Related information can be found in [[hypo_unit_package_scrubber_unit_feed_specifications]] for the scrubber feed, and [[hcl_synthesis_unit_process_design_basis]] for the HCl plant context.
