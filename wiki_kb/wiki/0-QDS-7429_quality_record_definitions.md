# 0-QDS-7429 Quality Record Definitions

This page defines the abbreviations used for Quality Record requirements within Quality Data Sheet [[0-QDS-7429_document_administration]].

*   **AP (As Prepared)**: This designation indicates that the quality record is required "As Prepared" by the vendor. This means the document is to be submitted in its original prepared format.
*   **FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package)**: This designation indicates that the quality record is required to be "Included in the Quality Assurance Documentation Package". This implies the document forms part of a larger compilation of quality records.
