# Subcontract Quality Management (0-SPC-2201)

Professional Services Subcontract 0-SPC-2201 mandates specific requirements for quality management to ensure the services provided meet the required standards.

**Quality Management System:**
*   The Subcontractor operates a quality management system for Providing the Services as stated in the Scope.
*   The quality management system complies with the requirements stated in the Scope.

**Quality Policy and Plan:**
*   The Subcontractor provides Aker Kvaerner Chemetics with a quality policy statement and a quality plan for acceptance within the period stated in the *Subcontract Data*.
*   The quality policy statement and quality plan comply with the requirements stated in the Scope.

**Compliance and Correction:**
*   The Subcontractor complies with instructions from Aker Kvaerner Chemetics to correct a failure to comply with the quality plan.
