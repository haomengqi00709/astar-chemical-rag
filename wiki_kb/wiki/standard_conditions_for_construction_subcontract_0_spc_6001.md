# Standard Conditions for Construction Subcontract (0-SPC-6001)

This document, 0-SPC-6001, outlines the Standard Conditions for Construction Subcontract. It is a Specification document based on "THE ENGINEERING AND CONSTRUCTION SHORT CONTRACT" 1ST Edition, published by Thomas Telford for the Institution of Civil Engineers in July 1999. Subcontractors familiar with the Standard Form may request a copy of the base contract with tracked amendments.

**Core Principles and Parties**
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Subcontractor **shall** act as stated in this subcontract and **shall** operate in a spirit of mutual trust and co-operation. The parties to this subcontract are Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Subcontractor. The "Agreement" refers to the Construction Subcontract Agreement signed by both Parties.

**Key Definitions**
*   **Owner**: The entity with whom Aker Kvaerner Chemetics has contracted to provide work, including the works.
*   **Head Contract**: The contract between Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Owner related to the works.
*   **To Provide the Works**: To perform all necessary work to complete the works in accordance with this subcontract, including all incidental work, services, and actions required.
*   **Subcontract Data**: Information detailing the commercial terms of the subcontract, found in Section 1 of the Agreement and its referenced documents. Terms identified in the Subcontract Data are presented in italics within these conditions.
*   **Works Information**: Information specifying and describing the works or stating constraints on how the Subcontractor Provides the Works, found in Section 2 of the Agreement and its referenced documents.
*   **Site Information**: Information describing the site and its surroundings, found in Section 3 of the Agreement and its referenced documents.
*   **Equipment and Materials**: Items intended for inclusion in the works.
*   **Construction Equipment**: Items provided and used by the Subcontractor to Provide the Works, but not included in the works.
*   **Completion Date**: The specified completion date, unless subsequently changed per this subcontract.
*   **Completion**: Achieved when the Subcontractor has completed the works according to the Works Information, excluding only notified Defects that do not prevent Aker Kvaerner Chemetics from using the works.
*   **Defect**: Any part of the works not in accordance with the Works Information.
*   **Defects Certificate**: A list of notified Defects not corrected by the defects date, or a statement confirming no such Defects exist.
*   **Prices**: Amounts stated in the Price column of the Price List. For items with a stated quantity, the Price is calculated by multiplying the quantity by the rate.
*   **Price for Work Done to Date**: The sum of the Price for each lump sum item in the Price List multiplied by its percent complete, plus, for quantity-based items, the amount calculated by multiplying the completed quantity by the rate.
*   **Subcontractor’s Cost**: Payments made by the Subcontractor for Providing the Works (excluding recoverable tax) for employees, Equipment and Materials, sub-subcontracted work, and Construction Equipment. This includes amounts paid for hired Construction Equipment and, if applicable, an amount for Subcontractor-owned Construction Equipment as per the Price List. If no amount is specified for owned Construction Equipment, a reasonable amount not exceeding the hypothetical hiring cost is used.

**General Principles**
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Subcontractor **shall** act in a spirit of mutual trust and co-operation.

**Document Conventions**
*   Terms identified in the *Subcontract Data* are in italics.
*   Defined terms have Capital Initials.

**Key Definitions**
*   **The Parties**: Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Subcontractor.
*   **The Agreement**: The document headed Construction Subcontract Agreement signed by the Parties.
*   **The Owner**: The person with whom Aker Kvaerner Chemetics has contracted to provide work including the works.
*   **The Head Contract**: The contract between Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Owner which relates to the works.
*   **To Provide the Works**: To do the work necessary to complete the works in accordance with this subcontract including all incidental work, services and actions which this subcontract requires.
*   **Subcontract Data**: Information which describes the commercial terms of the subcontract and is in Section 1 of the Agreement and the documents referenced in that section.
*   **Works Information**: Information which either specifies and describes the works or states any constraints on how the Subcontractor Provides the Works and is in Section 2 of the Agreement and the documents referenced in that section.
*   **Site Information**: Information which describes the site and its surroundings and is in Section 3 of the Agreement and the documents referenced in that section.
*   **Equipment and Materials**: Items to be included in the works.
*   **Construction Equipment**: Items provided by the Subcontractor, used by the Subcontractor to Provide the Works and not included in the works.
*   **The Completion Date**: The completion date unless later changed in accordance with this subcontract.
*   **Completion**: When the Subcontractor has completed the works in accordance with the Works Information except for correcting notified Defects which do not prevent Aker Kvaerner Chemetics from using the works.
*   **A Defect**: A part of the works which is not in accordance with the Works Information.
*   **The Defects Certificate**: Either a list of notified Defects which the Subcontractor has not corrected at the defects date or a statement that there are no such Defects.
*   **The Prices**: The amounts stated in the Price column of the Price List.
*   **Subcontractor’s Cost**: The amount of payment made by the Subcontractor in Providing the Works (excluding any tax which the Subcontractor can recover) for people who are employed by the Subcontractor, Equipment and Materials, sub-subcontracted work and Construction Equipment.
    *   The amount for Construction Equipment includes amounts paid for hired Construction Equipment.
    *   The amount for Construction Equipment includes an amount for the use of Construction Equipment owned by the Subcontractor which is the amount included in the Price List.
    *   If no amount for Construction Equipment owned by the Subcontractor is included in the Price List, a reasonable amount which is no more than the amount the Subcontractor would have paid if the Construction Equipment had been hired is used.

**Order of Precedence of Contract Documents**
The order of precedence for determining the obligations of the Parties is:
1.  Any Special Conditions for Construction Subcontract.
2.  Standard Conditions for Construction Subcontract.
3.  Other documents referred to in the Construction Subcontract Agreement, read together.

**Governing Law**
This subcontract is governed by the law of the country where the site is.
