# As-Built Instrumentation Documents (8-DTL-XXXX)

As-Built Instrumentation Documents (8-DTL-XXXX) are crucial for reflecting the final installed condition of the plant's instrumentation.

**Documents Provided:**
As-built commissioning documents **will be provided** for:
*   [[8-lop-xxxx_instrument_loop_diagrams]] (as applicable).
*   [[8-dtl-xxxx_instrument_junction_box_drawings]] (as applicable).
*   [[8-lst-0004_instrument_cable_schedule]] (as applicable).
*   [[8-dtl-xxxx_instrument_block_diagram]] (as applicable).

**Document Preparation and Approval:**
*   **Prepared by:** Detail Design Engineer (DDE)
*   **Approved by:** Detail Design Engineer (DDE)

**Site Responsibilities:**
When on the project site, the DDE **shall ensure** the Installation Contractor maintains a clear, concise set of As-Built mark-ups of the engineering drawings. The DDE **shall ensure** the Installation Contractor is prepared to provide As-Built mark-ups to the Site Supervisor in a timely fashion.

The DDE **shall** maintain an additional set of As-Built mark-ups as per the commissioning of the plant. Subsequent to Mechanical Completion, the DDE's additional set of As-Builts **shall** be provided to the Site Supervisor.
