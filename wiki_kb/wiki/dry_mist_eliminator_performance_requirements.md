# Dry Mist Eliminator Performance Requirements

This page outlines the performance requirements for the Dry Mist Eliminator, Tag No. **7507K**, as specified in Client Document #: **Standard-4-PDS-7507-R1 (Data Sheet)**.

**Inlet and Entrained Material Data:**
*   **Inlet Gas Data Description:** Process Gas
*   **Entrained Material Data Description:** Acid Mist
*   **Entrained Material Composition:** 98.04 % w/w H2SO4
*   **Entrained Material Inlet Load:** 500.0 mg/Nm3
*   **Entrained Material Density:** 1785.9 kg/m3
*   **Entrained Material Viscosity:** 6.88 cP
*   **Particle Type:** Solid

**Performance Criteria:**
*   **Overall Collection Efficiency:** VTA (Vendor To Advise)
*   **Outlet Load:** Maximum mg/Nm3
*   **Clean Pressure Drop:** VTA (Vendor To Advise)
*   **Maximum Allowable Pressure Drop:** kPa
*   **Dirty Pressure Drop:** kPa

The unit is designed for a **Turndown** ratio of **40% to 110% of design flow**.

**Mandatory Vendor Requirements:**
*   Vendor **must** provide curves for predicted collection efficiency versus particle size.
*   Vendor **must** guarantee outlet particle load and clean pressure drop.

For an overview of the equipment, refer to [[dry_mist_eliminator_overview]]. Details on the entrained material characteristics, including size distribution and required collection efficiency, are provided in [[dry_mist_eliminator_entrained_material_characteristics]]. Information on the materials of construction can be found in [[dry_mist_eliminator_materials_of_construction]].
