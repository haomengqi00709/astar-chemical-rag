# Thickener Vendor Quality Assurance and Chemetics Surveillance (0-QDS-7423)

This section outlines the vendor's quality assurance obligations and the level of surveillance to be conducted by Chemetics for the Thickener equipment, as specified in Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7423.

**Vendor Quality Assurance Requirements:**
The Vendor **shall** prepare a Quality Plan in accordance with [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment]]. The Vendor's Quality Assurance program **shall** comply with the requirements set forth in this Quality Data Sheet (0-QDS-7423) and with [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment]].

**Chemetics Surveillance Level:**
Surveillance by Chemetics **shall** be to Level 1.0. This surveillance **shall** be conducted as set out in this Quality Data Sheet.

**Quality Plan Documentation:**
Cells within the Quality Data Sheet that indicate a requirement for a Quality Plan **shall** be deleted if a Quality Plan is not required for a specific item or activity.

For general information on vendor quality assurance, refer to [[vendor_quality_assurance]].
