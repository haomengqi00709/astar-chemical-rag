# Lockout and Tagout Key Definitions

This page provides key definitions relevant to the [[0-PRC-8504-RA_lockout_and_tagging|Lockout and Tagout Procedure]].

*   **Safety Tagout:** The placement of a tag accompanied by a lock (as required) on the [[energy_isolating_device]], indicating that the device **will not** be operated until removal of the tag in accordance with an established procedure.
*   **Danger Tag:** A prominent, securely attachable warning device that forbids the operation of an [[energy_isolating_device]] for the purpose of protecting personnel. It identifies the [[authorized_individual|authorized individual]] or authority who has control of the [[clearance]]. Conforms to ANSI Z35.1, Section 5.3.1 Class 1.
*   **Extension Control Tag:** A small RED TAG or label placed on remote operating devices (where applicable) to inform employees that the equipment has been tagged out back at the source, referencing the applicable [[safety_tagout_order|safety tagout order]] number.
*   **Lockout Device:** Utilizes a lock and key to hold an [[energy_isolating_device]] in the safe position, protecting personnel and equipment.
*   **Energy Source:** Includes electrical, mechanical, hydraulic, pneumatic, chemical, nuclear, thermal, or other energy sources that could cause injury to personnel or damage to equipment.
*   **Energy Isolating Device:** A physical device that prevents the transmission or release of energy. Examples include: a manually operated electrical circuit breaker, a disconnect switch, a manually operated switch, a slide gate, slip blind, a line valve, blocks, and similar devices with a visible indication of the position of the device. Push buttons, selector switches, and other control/circuit type devices are **not** energy isolating devices.
*   **Knowledgeable Individual:** One who knows the effect of operating the controls or equipment.
*   **Authorized Individual:** A [[knowledgeable_individual|knowledgeable individual]] to whom the authority and responsibility to perform a specific assignment has been given by the employer.
*   **Commissioning HSE Manager:** The [[authorized_individual|authorized individual(s)]] responsible to administer the safety tagout program.
*   **Affected Employee:** A person whose job includes activities such as erecting, installing, constructing, repairing, adjusting, inspecting, testing, operating, or maintaining the equipment/process.
*   **Safety Tagout Order:** A sequentially numbered form (Form FO1) containing all the necessary information to control, document, audit, and status the safety tagout program at any time.
*   **Clearance:** The term used to identify acceptance of administrative control by an [[authorized_individual|authorized individual]] for an equipment/process which has been safety tagged to allow performance of specific work tasks.
*   **Group Safety Tagout:** Identifies the method by which safety tagout is accomplished in a safe and effective manner so as to permit more than one [[authorized_individual|authorized individual]], independent of others, to accept a [[clearance]] to allow performance of specific work on an equipment/process under one [[safety_tagout_order|safety tagout order]].
*   **Isolated:** Equipment/process is "Isolated" when every [[energy_source|energy source]] has been blocked and/or vented by an [[energy_isolating_device]] to the extent necessary for the work to be performed. Isolated equipment cannot change status and is administratively controlled.
*   **Verify:** Means the manipulation and placement of tags on [[energy_isolating_device]]s and other devices are correct as called for by a tagout order, and ensuring that all physical blocks/grounds/metering have been accomplished, in order to assure zero energy and zero mechanical state, as required for personnel/equipment safety for specific work to be performed or instructions followed.
*   **Accept:** Means to take or assume the duties and responsibilities of administrative control for an equipment/process by an [[authorized_individual|authorized individual]].
*   **Release:** Means to give up the duties and responsibilities of administrative control for an equipment/process by an [[authorized_individual|authorized individual]].
*   **Restored:** A piece of equipment is considered to be "Restored" when all safety tags have been removed, all required [[energy_source|energy sources]] have been established, such that the equipment can perform its intended function, and all administrative controls have been [[release|released]].
