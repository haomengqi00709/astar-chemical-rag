# 0-POL-9111 Home Leave / R&R Considerations

For employees assigned to a project which requires them to be away from home more than 30 days, special leave provisions, referred to as Home Leave or Rest & Recuperation (R&R), are granted as per a predefined schedule. These provisions are detailed within the 0-POL-9111 procedure.

**Scheduling and Approval:**
Every effort [[must]] be made to adhere to the scheduling of these leaves.
Additional time off [[will]] require approval from the Project Manager.
If an R&R leave cannot be taken due to unforeseen situations including technical or client difficulties, the employee [[will]] be granted 2 days off with pay in lieu. Time off in lieu [[must not]] be used for two consecutive R&R leaves.
Additional time off [[must]] be taken as vacation or banked time. This additional time off is not subject to [[0_pol_9111_assignment_uplift]].

**Travel Arrangements and Costs:**
Travel for R&R or Home Leaves [[must]] be booked and approved in accordance with [[travel_authorization_0_prc_9002]].
Travel costs for R&R or Home Leaves, including excursion airfare and other reasonable travel related expenses, [[will]] be paid by The Company.

**Timesheet Requirements:**
Timesheets [[must]] reflect the R&R/Home Leave charge code to ensure proper tracking and compensation.

**Spousal or Family Visits:**
An employee may request that a spouse or family member may visit in lieu of a trip home.
Written approval from Human Resources [[must]] be received before any travel arrangements are made for spousal/family visits.
The Company [[will]] pay for airfare and direct travel expenses for spousal/family visits.
Payment for spousal/family visits is to a maximum equal to the travel expenses that the Company would have paid if the employee had returned home using an excursion class airfare.
