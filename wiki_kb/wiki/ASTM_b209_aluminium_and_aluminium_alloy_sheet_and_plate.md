# ASTM B209 Aluminium and Aluminium Alloy Sheet and Plate

This page provides an overview of ASTM B209, a standard specification for Aluminium and Aluminium alloy sheet and plate.

**Overview**
ASTM B209 is a standard specification published by ASTM International that covers Aluminium and Aluminium alloy sheet and plate for general engineering applications. It defines the chemical composition, mechanical properties, dimensions, and tolerances for various Aluminium alloys in sheet and plate forms.

**Application**
In the context of the [[9-SPC-0001_aluminium_bus_welding_standard_overview|Aluminium Bus Welding Standard (9-SPC-0001)]], base materials used for BUS systems **shall** be in accordance with ASTM B209 or [[ASTM_b236_aluminium_bus_bar|ASTM B236]]. This ensures that the raw materials meet the specified quality and performance criteria for the fabrication of Aluminium bus systems.
