# Piping Mechanical Checkout Procedure (5-PRC-0007)

The 5-PRC-0007 procedure outlines the comprehensive mechanical checkout process for piping systems, ensuring their installation adheres strictly to design drawings and specifications. This critical procedure verifies that all piping is prepared for subsequent [[water_and_air_trials_1_prc_0006|water batching]] and [[commissioning_planning_and_management|commissioning]] activities. All mechanical checkout documents generated during this process will form an integral part of the [[mechanical_completion_testing_and_defect_management|mechanical completion package]].

The primary purpose of this checkout is to confirm that the plant has been constructed precisely in accordance with the approved design documents, including drawings and specifications. Any modifications or changes identified during the checkout process that deviate from the original design are classified as [[design_deficiency_management|design deficiencies]], not as standard punch list items. These design deficiencies **must** be thoroughly documented and promptly forwarded to the [[project_manager_planning_responsibilities|Project Manager]] for formal resolution.

**Checkout Process and Responsibilities:**
The mechanical checkout process involves several key steps and roles:
1.  **Sub-contractor Punch Lists:** The sub-contractor is responsible for compiling punch lists and isometrics to ensure completeness during the construction phase.
2.  **Site Supervisor Review:** The sub-contractor **shall** approach the [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_site_supervisor_review|Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site supervisor]] for an initial review. The site supervisor visually inspects the installed piping against the [[pipe_isometric|isometric]] drawings and the contractor's punch list.
3.  **Project Manager Notification:** Once satisfied with the work completion and the correctness of the punch list, the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site supervisor **must** notify the Project Manager.
4.  **Final Designer Review:** A designer from the engineering office conducts a final visual inspection of the piping against the latest [[pipe_isometric|isometrics]] and [[piping_and_instrumentation_drawings|P&ID]]. The designer confirms that the installation is in accordance with all construction documents.
5.  **Deficiency Identification and Correction:** The designer identifies any deficiencies on a dedicated set of isometrics and hands these to the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site supervisor. The Site Supervisor then collaborates with a deficiency crew, supplied by the sub-contractor, to correct all identified deficiencies. This crew is also responsible for addressing any outstanding punch list items from the initial review.
6.  **Post-Testing Work:** Outstanding work, such as [[coatings_erection_0_spc_6x97|painting]], [[insulation_erection_0_spc_6x96|insulating]], and installing [[flange_guard_installation|flange guards]], is undertaken by the deficiency crew after the piping has undergone cleaning, flushing, and hydrotesting.

**Required Documentation for Piping Checkout:**
For a comprehensive piping checkout, the following documents are essential:
*   [[piping_and_instrumentation_drawings|P&ID]]
*   [[pipe_isometric|Isometrics]]
*   [[line_list|Line List]]
*   [[pipe_specifications|Pipe Material Specification]]
*   [[pipe_support_specification|Pipe Support Specification]]

[[5-PRC-0007_table_1]] provides further details on specific inspection items.

For information on document revisions, refer to [[0-prc-0007_revision_history]].

Upon successful completion, the documentation generated during this checkout process will contribute to the final mechanical completion package.
