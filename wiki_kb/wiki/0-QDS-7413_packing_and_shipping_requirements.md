# Acid Tower Distributor Packing and Shipping Requirements (0-QDS-7413)

This page outlines the packing and shipping requirements for the Acid Tower Distributor, as specified in Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7413. These requirements ensure the safe and secure delivery of the equipment.

**Packing and Crating:**
*   Preparation for shipping, including all necessary packing and crating procedures, **requires** Chemetics Vancouver surveillance (Witness). This ensures that the equipment is adequately protected for transit.

**Documentation:**
*   The Packing List **requires** a Quality Record designation of AP (As Prepared) and Chemetics Vancouver surveillance (Witness). This means the packing list **must** be submitted in its original prepared format and verified by Chemetics Vancouver.

These measures are essential to prevent damage during transportation and to ensure accurate documentation of the shipped items, forming a key part of the [[0-QDS-7413_vendor_quality_assurance_requirements|vendor quality assurance requirements]].
