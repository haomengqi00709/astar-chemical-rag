# Hot Work Permit Procedure Overview (0-PRC-8503)

This procedure, Document ID 0-PRC-8503, outlines the requirements for managing hot work activities during the commissioning phase of projects. Its primary purpose is to eliminate the potential for injury to personnel and damage to equipment due to fire or explosion resulting from cutting, welding, and similar hot work operations.

**Scope and Applicability:**
This regulation **shall** be followed by the entire Commissioning Team, which includes Aker Kvaerner Chemetics personnel, Contractors, and Vendor representatives. It applies specifically after a circuit, system, or equipment has been formally handed over as mechanically completed for commissioning by the Construction Manager to the Commissioning Manager.

The procedure is based on Aker Kvaerner’s Open Flame / Hot Work procedure, originally issued in January of 2003. This foundational document can be found in the Aker Kvaerner’s [[aker_kvaerner_hse_policy_manual]] section 7, item 7.03.

This safety regulation applies to any work wherein sufficient heat or sparks may be generated to serve as a source of ignition in any work area. There is no exception to this regulation for all process, utilities, and chemical storage areas.

**Client Regulations:**
A Client’s regulation may be followed if it fully covers the requirements and guidelines stipulated in Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ procedures.

**Hazard Assessment:**
A critical requirement is that the hazard assessment created for the task **must** be understood by all members of the team involved with the hot work activity.

For detailed roles and responsibilities related to this procedure, refer to [[0-prc-8503_hot_work_permit_roles_and_responsibilities]]. Information on specific training requirements can be found in [[0-prc-8503_hot_work_permit_training_and_documentation]]. The process for permit issuance and validity is detailed in [[0-prc-8503_hot_work_permit_issuance_and_validity]], while [[0-prc-8503_hot_work_permit_exemptions_and_restrictions]] covers conditions under which permits may not be required or may be cancelled. Safety protocols are outlined in [[0-prc-8503_hot_work_safety_protocols]], and the interaction with lockout/tagout procedures is explained in [[0-prc-8503_hot_work_and_lockout_tagout_interface]].

This regulation shall be followed by the Commissioning Team (Aker Kvaerner Chemetics personnel, Contractors and Vendors rep).
The regulation applies after a circuit, system, or equipment is handed over mechanically completed for commissioning by the Construction Manager to the Commissioning Manager.
A Client’s regulation may be followed if it fully covers requirements and guidelines of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ procedures.
All Open Flame/Hot Work incidents and near misses shall be thoroughly investigated and documented.
This regulation is NOT applicable for the execution of hot tap activities. A Hot Work permit is not a substitute for an approved hot tap procedure.
