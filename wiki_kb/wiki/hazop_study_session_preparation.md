# HazOp Study Session Preparation

Thorough preparation is essential before commencing each Hazard and Operability (HazOp) Study session to ensure a structured and effective review.

**Plant Division and Identification:**
*   The Process Department and the HazOp Study Leader **shall** divide the plant into distinct parts for study.
*   The boundaries of these parts **must** be clearly marked on copies of published, checked, and approved [[piping_and_instrumentation_drawings]].
*   A sequential identification number **must** be assigned to each part of the plant.
*   The marked-up copies of the Piping and Instrument Diagrams will be filed in `P:\1-Process-Technology\HAZ`.

**Data Recording in HazOp Record (1-HAZ-0001):**
Prior to the start of each HazOp Study session, specific data **must** be recorded for each plant part using the [[1-haz-0001_hazop_record_overview|[Template] HazOp Record (1-HAZ-0001)]].
*   One Excel worksheet **must** be assigned for each part within 1-HAZ-0001.
*   **Process Department Responsibilities:** The Process Department **shall** record the following data for each part:
    *   Purpose / Design Intent
    *   Materials / Inputs
    *   Activities
    *   Sources
    *   Destination
*   **Mechanical Department Responsibilities:** The Mechanical Department **shall** record Mechanical Design data for each part in 1-HAZ-0001.

*   Discrete parts of the plant are designated on Piping and Instrument Diagrams.
*   Boundaries of parts to be studied **must** be marked on copies of published, checked and approved Piping and Instrument Diagrams.
*   A sequential identification number **must** be assigned to each part of the plant.
*   Marked-up copies of the Piping and Instrument Diagrams will be filed in `P:\1-Process-Technology\HAZ`.

**Data Recording for Each Part (1-HAZ-0001):**
*   One excel worksheet **must** be assigned for each part in 1-HAZ-0001.
*   Prior to commencing each HazOp Study session, Mechanical will record Mechanical Design data for each part using 1-HAZ-0001.
