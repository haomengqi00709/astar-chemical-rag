# Instrument Air System

A dedicated instrument-quality air reticulation system is essential for the reliable operation of pneumatic instrumentation and control valves. This system **will be** separate from the general plant air reticulation system.

**Air Quality and Capacity:**
*   The quality of the instrument air **will** conform to **ANSI/ISA S7.3**.
*   The capacity of the instrument air supply system **will be** such that, in the event of compressor failure, sufficient air is available in reserve to operate the instrumentation, enabling normal plant operation to be maintained for not less than 20 minutes.
*   All pneumatic instrumentation and control valves **will be** designed for service with a plant air supply pressure of 500-750 kPa.
*   Prior to the air receivers, the instrument air **will be** cooled to minus 35 °C dew point to prevent condensation and moisture-related issues.

**Pneumatic Device Connections:**
Each pneumatic device **will be** fitted with a supply air filter, sediment bowl, pressure regulator, and an air isolation ball valve. Filter sediment bowls **will be** fitted with automatic draining devices.

**Air Distribution Network:**
*   Local air distribution manifolds **will** have a minimum of 20% spare outlet capacity and a drain point with valve.
*   The instrument air distribution piping **will be** such that the air take-off points are located on the top of headers and manifolds to prevent moisture ingress.
*   Drain valves **will be** located at the lowest points of instrument air distribution piping to remove accumulated moisture.
*   Each instrument air user **will be** connected via a dedicated isolating ball valve.
*   Each instrument air user **will be** connected via a dedicated in-line filter/water trap with automatic drain.

**Tubing and Fittings:**
*   All valve piping **will be** made in 316 SS.
*   In extreme acid/corrosive environments or on vibrating equipment, instrument air tubing **will be** black flame retarding polyethylene or PTFE, unless environmental conditions require different materials.
*   Special consideration **will be** given to instrument air tubing in areas with chlorine, chlorides, and hydrochloric acid due to their corrosive nature (refer to [[environmental_and_site_conditions|Environmental and Site Conditions]]).
