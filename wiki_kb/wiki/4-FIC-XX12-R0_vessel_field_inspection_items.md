# Vessel Field Inspection Checklist Items (4-FIC-XX12-R0)

This page outlines the mandatory inspection items for tanks and vessels as specified in the 4-FIC-XX12-R0 Field Inspection Checklist. Adherence to these requirements is crucial for ensuring the safe and correct mechanical completion of equipment.

**Mandatory Installation and Completion Requirements:**

The following items **must** be completed or installed as part of the vessel's mechanical completion:

*   **Nameplate:** The correct nameplate **must** be installed on the vessel.
*   **Foundations and Anchoring:** Foundation/Anchor bolts **must** be secure and tightened. For related civil works, see [[civil_construction_and_site_preparation_0_spc_6x92]].
*   **Supports:** Any sliding supports **must** allow for proper movement to accommodate thermal expansion or other operational dynamics.
*   **Vessel Internals:** All vessel internals, including dip pipes, stand pipes, baffles, and vortex breakers, **must** be installed. Refer to [[template_vessel_notes_and_ancillary_equipment]] for general vessel internal details.
*   **Access Structures:** All platforms, handrails, walkways, stairs, and ladders **must** be installed. For structural and building construction standards, see [[structural_and_buildings_construction_0_spc_6x93]].
*   **Safety Gates:** Safety gates **must** be installed and functioning correctly on all access points.
*   **Non-Destructive Testing (NDT):** All NDT inspections **must** be completed. For general NDT requirements, refer to [[non_destructive_examination_requirements]].
*   **Pressure Testing:** The pressure test **must** be completed. For detailed pressure testing procedures, see [[pressure_testing]].
*   **Internal Cleanliness:** The vessel **must** be clean and empty prior to final closure.
*   **Internal Coatings/Linings:** Internal painting or lining **must** be completed. For inspection details, refer to [[coating_and_lining_inspection]].
*   **Isolation Valves:** Isolation valves **must** be installed on all drains. For isolation procedures, refer to [[isolation_and_preparation_for_maintenance_1_prc_0007]].
*   **Manway Covers:** Manway covers **must** be installed and tightened securely.
*   **Venting:** Means of venting **must** be maintained to avoid collapse due to vacuum conditions. This is critical for vessel integrity.
*   **Pressure Relief Devices:** Pressure relief devices **must** be installed. For tagging and general information, refer to [[pressure_relief_equipment_tagging]].
*   **External Painting:** External painting **must** be completed. For coating specifications, see [[coatings_design_and_specification_7_prc_0002]].
*   **Insulation and Cladding:** Insulation and cladding **must** be complete. For design specifications, refer to [[insulation_design_and_specification_6_prc_0002]].
*   **Grounding:** Grounding **must** be completed. This may involve multiple locations. For installation details, refer to [[grounding_installation_details]].
*   **Flange Guards:** Flange guards **must** be installed if applicable, particularly for safety around high-pressure or hazardous fluid flanges.
*   **Grouting:** Grouting **must** be completed for equipment bases and supports. For related civil works, see [[civil_construction_and_site_preparation_0_spc_6x92]].

**Unspecified Items:**
Checklist items 18.0 through 24.0 are not specified in this document. These items may be defined in project-specific addenda or subsequent revisions of the checklist.
