# Hydrostatic Test Applications

This section details specific applications where hydrostatic testing is required.

*   The primary steam line from the plant battery limits to the first pressure reducing station will be hydrostatically tested.
*   Hydrostatic testing of the primary steam line will be in accordance with B31.1 para 137.4.
