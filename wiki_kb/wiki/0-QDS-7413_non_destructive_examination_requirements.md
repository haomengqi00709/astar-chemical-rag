# Acid Tower Distributor Non-Destructive Examination Requirements (0-QDS-7413)

This page outlines the specific Non-Destructive Examination (NDE) requirements for the Acid Tower Distributor, as mandated by Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7413. These examinations are critical for ensuring the structural integrity and quality of the manufactured equipment.

**General NDE Standards:**
*   Non-Destructive Testing **must** be in accordance with ASME VIII Div. 1.
*   Non Destructive Examination **must** be in accordance with ASME Boiler & Pressure Vessel Code Sections V & VIII.

**Dye Penetrant Testing (PT) Requirements:**
*   Dye Penetrant testing **requires** Level 1.0 surveillance, a Quality Record designation of FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package), and Chemetics Vancouver surveillance (Witness).
*   Dye Penetrant testing is specifically required for the root pass of the header, long arms, and short arms of the Acid Tower Distributor.

The "FP" designation for Dye Penetrant testing means that the results **must** be included in the final [[0-QDS-7413_quality_record_definitions|Quality Assurance Documentation Package]]. Chemetics Vancouver's witness surveillance ensures that these critical examinations are performed correctly and to the required standards, aligning with the overall [[0-QDS-7413_vendor_quality_assurance_requirements|vendor quality assurance requirements]].
