# Field Instrumentation Checkout (8-LST-0101)

The Field Instrumentation Checkout (8-LST-0101) document provides a checklist for verifying the correct installation and functionality of field instruments.

**Minimum Confirmations:**
Field Instrumentation Checkout (8-LST-0101) **shall** as a minimum confirm:
*   Primary sensor impulse tubing is complete and installed correctly.
*   Instrument Air Tubing is complete and installed correctly.
*   Instrument is installed correctly.
*   Wire terminations are completed and are correct.
*   All fail actions are as per specifications.
*   Scale and Calibration is as per specifications.
*   DCS Alarms are as per specification and operating correctly.

**Document Preparation and Approval:**
*   **Prepared by:** Detail Design Engineer (DDE)
*   **Approved by:** Detail Design Engineer (DDE)

**Application:**
Checkout sheet 8-LST-0101 **is to be used as a guideline** for Field Instrumentation Checkout. The DDE will provide support for on-site Field Instrumentation Checkout.
