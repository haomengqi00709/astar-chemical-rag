# Instrument Testing Requirements (8-SPC-0133)

The Instrument Testing (8-SPC-0133) document outlines the requirements for the inspection, testing, and calibration of instrumentation, as well as related cabling and wiring.

**Requirements:**
The Instrument Testing (8-SPC-0133) requirement **shall** contain:
*   Common requirements for the inspection, testing, and calibration of instrumentation.
*   Inspection and testing of related cabling and wiring for the project.

**Document Preparation and Approval:**
*   **Prepared by:** Standard
*   **Checked by:** Chief E/I Engineer
*   **Approved by:** Director of Engineering

**Project Application:**
The Instrumentation department will provide the Detail Design Engineer (DDE) with typical Test Specification 8-SPC-0133. The DDE will then provide the project Test Specification using this typical example and their own expertise. The DDE will also provide support for on-site testing and commissioning of the plant as directed by the Site Supervisor.
