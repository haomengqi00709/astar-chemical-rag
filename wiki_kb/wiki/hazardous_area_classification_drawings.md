# Hazardous Area Classification Drawings

Hazardous Area Classification drawings visually represent the classified hazardous zones within the plant, indicating their limits, levels of hazard, and the substances that may be present. These drawings are essential for safe equipment selection and operational planning.

**Content of Hazardous Area Classification Drawings**
Hazardous area classification drawings **shall** show:
*   The entire plant.
*   Process equipment.
*   Pipe racks.
*   Buildings.
*   Tank farms.
*   Electrical rooms.

Areas to be considered classified **must** be indicated by means of a cloud drawn around the equipment, tanks, pumps, etc. Hazardous area clouds **shall** indicate the limits of the clouds from specific equipment by means of dimensions.

Symbols to indicate the various levels of hazardous area classification **must** be shown. Symbols to indicate the substances that may be present within the clouds **must** be shown.

Hazardous area clouds **have to be considered** three-dimensional. Drawings may require additional views to indicate the three dimensions of hazardous area clouds.

**References and Compliance**
The [[hazardous_area_classification_requirements]] specification **must** be referenced. Appropriate codes, standards, and regulations for hazardous areas **must** be referenced. [[piping_and_instrumentation_drawings]] **must** be referenced.
