# Emissions and Effluents

The Emissions and Effluents section, a vital part of the [[process_design_criteria_document]], details the environmental discharge parameters of the plant. This section is crucial for ensuring regulatory compliance, minimizing environmental impact, and defining the plant's operational footprint.

**Mandatory Listing Requirements:**
*   All normal atmospheric emission values **must** be listed. These represent the expected emissions during routine plant operation under design conditions.
*   All guarantee atmospheric emission values **must** be listed. These are the maximum permissible emission levels that the plant is guaranteed not to exceed, often tied to contractual agreements and environmental permits.
*   All liquid effluents **must** be listed. This includes all liquid discharges from the plant, specifying their composition, flow rates, and treatment requirements.
*   All solid effluents **must** be listed. This covers all solid waste streams generated by the plant, detailing their characteristics, quantities, and disposal methods.

The comprehensive listing of emissions and effluents is fundamental for environmental impact assessments, permitting processes, and the design of pollution control systems. It directly influences the selection of technologies for emission reduction, wastewater treatment, and solid waste management. These values are often derived from the [[plant_design_basis_and_capacity]] and are critical for demonstrating the plant's adherence to local and international environmental regulations. Accurate and complete data in this section helps to mitigate environmental risks and ensures the sustainable operation of the facility.

In addition to normal atmospheric emission values, all guarantee atmospheric emission values must be listed.
