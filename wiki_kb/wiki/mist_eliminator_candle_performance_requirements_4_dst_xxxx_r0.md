# Mist Eliminator Candle Performance Requirements (4-DST-XXXX-R0)

This section outlines the mandatory performance requirements for the Mist Eliminator Candle Datasheet (4-DST-XXXX-R0). These specifications define the expected operational efficiency and limitations of the mist eliminator candles.

**Separation Efficiency:**
The separation efficiency must be specified for various particle size ranges:
*   **For Particle Size Above:** The particle size in microns and the corresponding % Efficiency must be provided.
*   **For Particle Size Between:** The particle size range in microns and the corresponding % Efficiency must be provided.
*   **For Particle Size Below:** The particle size in microns and the corresponding % Efficiency must be provided.

**Outlet Particle Load and Overall Efficiency:**
*   **Outlet Particle Load:** The outlet particle load in kg/h must be specified.
*   **Overall Efficiency:** The overall efficiency must be specified.

**Pressure Drop:**
*   **Allowable Pressure Drop:** The allowable pressure drop in kPa must be specified.
*   **Actual Pressure Drop:** The actual pressure drop must be specified.

These performance requirements are directly influenced by the [[mist_eliminator_candle_operating_conditions_4_dst_xxxx_r0]] and dictate the design of the [[mist_eliminator_candle_physical_specifications_4_dst_xxxx_r0]].
