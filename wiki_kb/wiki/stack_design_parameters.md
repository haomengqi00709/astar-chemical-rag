# Stack Design Parameters

This page details the "STACK DESIGN DATA" category from the [[4-dst-xxxx-r0_template_stack_datasheet_administration|Template Stack Datasheet]], outlining the critical parameters used in the mechanical and process design of a stack. These parameters cover various aspects from physical dimensions to structural loads and environmental considerations.

**Physical and Performance Design Data:**
*   **Emission:** Data related to stack emissions, crucial for environmental compliance.
*   **Plume:** Characteristics of the stack plume, including dispersion and visibility.
*   **Stack Height:** Specified in meters (m), a key dimension influencing draft and dispersion.
*   **Stack Diameter:** Specified in meters (m), another critical dimension affecting flow and structural stability.
*   **Natural Draft:** Measured in kilopascals (kPa), indicating the draft created by temperature differences.
*   **Draft Losses:** Measured in kilopascals (kPa), representing pressure losses within the stack.
*   **Available Draft:** Measured in kilopascals (kPa), the net draft available for operation.
*   **Temperature of Exit Fluid:** Specified in degrees Celsius (°C), the temperature of the gas exiting the stack.
*   **Transportation Limitation:** Specified in meters (m), indicating size constraints for shipping the stack components.
*   **Stack Heat Loss:** Measured in kilojoules per hour (kJ/h), the rate of heat dissipation from the stack surface.
*   **Wind Speed for Heat Loss Calc.:** Specified in kilometers per hour (km/h), used in heat loss calculations.
*   **Test Zone:** Specified in meters (m), indicating a designated area for performance testing or monitoring.
*   **Fire Suppression System:** Specifies the type of system, e.g., Steam Injection, for fire safety.

**Mechanical and Structural Design Data:**
*   **Design Pressure:** Measured in kilopascals gauge (kPa(g)), the pressure the stack is designed to withstand.
*   **Corrosion Allowance:** Specified in millimeters (mm), an additional thickness added to account for material loss due to corrosion over the design life.
*   **Max Design Temperature:** Specified in degrees Celsius (°C), the maximum temperature the stack is designed to operate at.
*   **Min Design Temperature:** Specified in degrees Celsius (°C), the minimum temperature the stack is designed to operate at.
*   **Empty Weight:** Specified in kilograms (kg), the weight of the stack structure without contents.
*   **Live Load:** Specified in kilonewtons (kN), the variable load due to maintenance, personnel, or internal components.
*   **Wind Base Shear:** Specified in kilonewtons (kN), the horizontal force at the base due to wind.
*   **Seismic Base Shear:** Specified in kilonewtons (kN), the horizontal force at the base due to seismic activity.
*   **Wind Base Moment:** Specified in kilonewton-meters (kN-m), the overturning moment at the base due to wind.
*   **Seismic Base Moment:** Specified in kilonewton-meters (kN-m), the overturning moment at the base due to seismic activity.
*   **Equipment Base Elevation:** Specified in meters (m), the elevation of the stack's base.
*   **Snow Load:** Specified in kilopascals gauge (Kpa(g)), the load due to accumulated snow.
*   **Maximum Deflection at Top:** Specified in millimeters (mm), the allowable lateral displacement at the top of the stack.

**Other Design Considerations:**
*   **Location:** Specific geographical or plant location details.
*   **Area Classification:** Hazardous area classification (e.g., Class 1, Division 2) for electrical and instrumentation design.
*   **Paint Specification:** Details regarding the paint system to be applied.
*   **Paint Color:** Specific color requirements for the stack.
*   **Vibration Prevention:** Measures or design features to mitigate vibration.

These parameters collectively define the operational and structural requirements for the stack. For further details on stack design, refer to [[stack_design_notes_and_recommendations]] and [[stack_physical_specifications_and_materials]].
