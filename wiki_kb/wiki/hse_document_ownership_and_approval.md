# HSE Document Ownership and Approval

The ownership and approval hierarchy for Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) documents within Aker Kvaerner Chemetics ("the Company") is structured as follows:

**Company Policies:**
*   All Company policies are owned by the Company President.
*   All Company policies **must** be approved by the Company President.

**Standard Procedures (0-PRC-8000 and 0-PRC-8001):**
*   [[0-prc-8000_administration_of_hse]] (Administration of Health, Safety and Environment) is owned by the Company President.
    *   It **must** be checked by the HSE Manager.
    *   It **must** be approved by the Company President.
*   [[0-prc-8001_project_health_and_safety_planning]] (Project Health and Safety Planning) is owned by the Company President.
    *   It **must** be checked by the HSE Manager.
    *   It **must** be approved by the Company President.

**Other Procedures and Specifications:**
*   Other procedures and specifications related to HSE are owned by the Company HSE Manager.
*   These procedures and specifications **must** be checked by the HSE Manager.
*   These procedures and specifications **must** be approved by the Company President.

This structure ensures that all HSE documentation undergoes rigorous review and approval by the highest levels of management, reflecting the Company's commitment to maintaining high HSE standards.
