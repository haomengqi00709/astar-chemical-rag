# Unalloyed Nickel Welding Processes and Parameters

This section details the acceptable welding processes and critical parameters for the fabrication of unalloyed nickel components, as governed by [[4-SPC-0068_unalloyed_nickel_welding_standard_overview]]. Adherence to these guidelines is essential for achieving the required weld quality and mechanical properties.

**Acceptable Welding Processes:**
The following welding processes are deemed acceptable for unalloyed nickel:
*   Shielded Metal Arc Welding (SMAW)
*   Gas Tungsten Arc Welding (GTAW)
*   Gas Metal Arc Welding (GMAW)
*   Submerged Arc Welding (SAW)

**Process-Specific Requirements:**

*   **Gas Tungsten Arc Welding (GTAW)**
    *   The weld metal thickness **must not** exceed 3mm (⅛") per pass.
    *   Heat input during GTAW **must** be kept low by utilizing a stringer bead technique. This minimizes the heat-affected zone and potential for material degradation.

*   **Gas Metal Arc Welding (GMAW)**
    *   The Vendor **must** provide satisfactory evidence of continuous shop use of the GMAW process for unalloyed nickel. This ensures experience and proficiency with the material.

*   **Submerged Arc Welding (SAW)**
    *   The weld metal thickness **must not** exceed 13mm (½") per pass.
    *   Run-on and run-off tabs, made from the same material specification as the base material, **must** be used on all longitudinal seams for SAW. These tabs help ensure full weld penetration and quality at the start and end of the weld.
    *   The Vendor **must** provide satisfactory evidence of continuous shop use of the SAW process for unalloyed nickel.

**General Welding Parameters:**

*   **Preheat:** Preheat is **not** required for welding unalloyed nickel under this specification.
*   **Heat Input:** Heat input during all welding processes **must** be kept to a minimum to control metallurgical changes and distortion.
*   **Inter-pass Temperature:** The inter-pass temperature **must not** exceed 93°C (200°F). Strict control of inter-pass temperature is vital to prevent sensitization and maintain material properties.

For information on welding consumables, refer to [[unalloyed_nickel_welding_consumables]]. All welding procedures and welder qualifications **must** comply with [[ASME_section_ix_welding_and_brazing_qualifications]].
