# Material Verification and Certifications for Carbon Steel Tank (0-QDS-7435)

Material verification and certifications are a fundamental aspect of quality assurance for the Carbon Steel Tank, as outlined in Item 1.0 of QDS 0-QDS-7435. This ensures that all materials used in the fabrication of the tank conform to the specified standards and requirements.

**Mill Test Certificates:**
*   Mill Test Certificates are a mandatory quality record and are required as FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package) as per [[0-QDS-7435_quality_record_definitions]].
*   Chemetics **must** witness these Mill Test Certificates to confirm that the material properties meet the project specifications.

This verification process is critical for ensuring the structural integrity and performance of the Carbon Steel Tank. For a complete list of all witnessing and holding requirements, refer to [[0-QDS-7435_inspection_and_documentation_requirements]].
