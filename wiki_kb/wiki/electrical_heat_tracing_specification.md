# Electrical Heat Tracing Specification

The Electrical Heat Tracing Specification details the requirements for designing and installing electrical heat tracing systems. This specification focuses exclusively on electrical systems, with steam heat tracing systems being addressed by other disciplines.

**Purpose and Scope**
This specification **shall** describe the electrical components required for a complete heat tracing system. It **shall** also describe the purpose of the heat tracing, which is to maintain process temperatures or prevent freezing in pipes, vessels, and pumps. The specification **shall** outline the limitations on the amount of heat that can be applied.

**Application and References**
Temperature limits for heat tracing **shall** be applied to pipes, vessels, pumps, and other equipment as determined by the process requirements and the materials of construction.

The heat tracing specification **must** reference relevant piping, vessel, pump, insulation, and cladding specifications to ensure a coordinated design and installation.
