# Vendor Shop Survey Template (0-REP-5XXX)

This page describes the template 0-REP-5XXX, which is used for documenting Vendor shop surveys.

**Purpose:**
The [[vendor_shop_survey_template]] is a standardized report format used by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Quality Assurance Inspectors to document findings from shop surveys conducted on Vendors. These surveys are crucial for assessing a Vendor's capabilities, quality control, and suitability for fabricating custom Goods and Services.

**Content:**
The template typically includes sections for:
*   Scope of the shop survey.
*   Observations regarding the Vendor's facilities, equipment, and manufacturing processes.
*   Assessment of the Vendor's quality management system.
*   Recommendations for additional tests and inspections.
*   Recommendations for a higher level of quality surveillance for the Vendor.
*   General findings and conclusions regarding the Vendor's suitability.

**Usage:**
This template is primarily used when a shop survey **must** be conducted for custom fabricated Goods and Services, especially if a short-listed Vendor has not performed work for Aker Kvaerner Chemetics in the previous three years. The completed survey report informs the [[vendor_shop_survey_requirements]] process and contributes to the overall Vendor evaluation.

**Document Numbering:**
Shop survey documents created using this template **must** be numbered according to [[document_numbering_and_name_change_conventions]] (0-PRC-0002) and use the same identification number as the original requisition. Surveys for multiple Vendors **must** be treated as sheets within a single document.
