# Construction Site Advisor Required Documents

Aker Kvaerner Chemetics construction site advisors **must** ensure they are equipped with and familiar with a specific set of documents and forms essential for maintaining Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) standards on Site.

**Mandatory Documents to Obtain:**
Prior to commencing work on Site, advisors **shall** obtain and review the following critical documents:
*   **0-MAN-8400 Aker Kvaerner Construction Safety Manual:** This manual serves as a foundational guide for construction safety. It also lists the specifications and procedures typically required at Site, providing a comprehensive reference for various HSE aspects.
*   **0-REP-8400 Construction Site Safety Health and Environmental Analysis:** This report provides a detailed analysis of potential HSE risks and considerations specific to construction sites.
*   **0-REP-8401 Construction Site Safety Checklist:** This checklist is a practical tool for advisors to systematically verify compliance with safety requirements on Site.

**Mandatory Forms to Complete:**
Advisors **must** complete the **0-REP-8402 Personal Data Form for Construction Site Visits**. This form serves a dual purpose:
1.  It provides advisors with essential emergency contact and HSE information relevant to their assignment.
2.  It furnishes Aker Kvaerner Chemetics with critical emergency HSE information about the advisor, ensuring appropriate response in case of an incident.

For a detailed breakdown of specific requirements or additional information, refer to [[0-prc-8400_table_1]].

The 0-MAN-8400 Aker Kvaerner Construction Safety Manual also lists specifications and procedures typically required at Site.

The 0-REP-8402 Personal Data Form for Construction Site Visits serves a dual purpose: it provides advisors with emergency contact HSE information and provides Aker Kvaerner Chemetics with emergency HSE information about the advisor.
