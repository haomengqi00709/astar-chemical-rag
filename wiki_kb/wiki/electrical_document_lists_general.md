# General Electrical Document Lists

This page provides an overview of the various electrical document lists maintained for a project, outlining their general characteristics and purpose.

**General Characteristics of Lists**
All electrical lists **shall** be numbered according to 9-LST-0001, List Electrical Document Numbering Standard. These lists are typically formatted in a table with various headings to organize information effectively. The information contained within these lists is vital for project construction, as well as for plant maintenance records, particularly for equipment replacement.
