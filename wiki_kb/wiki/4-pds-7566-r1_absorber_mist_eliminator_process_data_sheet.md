# Absorber Mist Eliminator Process Data Sheet (4-PDS-7566-R1)

This page provides details from the Absorber Mist Eliminator Process Data Sheet, document 4-PDS-7566-R1. This data sheet specifies the process conditions, performance requirements, and materials of construction for the mist eliminator.

**Process Description**
*   **Inlet Gas Description:** Process Gas
*   **Entrained Material Description:** Acid Mist
*   **Entrained Material Composition % w/w H2SO4:** 98.04
*   **Entrained Material Inlet Load:** 700.0 mg/Nm3
*   **Entrained Material Density:** 1785.9 kg/m3
*   **Entrained Material Viscosity:** 6.88 cP
*   **Entrained Material Particle Type:** Liquid

**Performance Requirements**
*   **Required Collection Efficiency:** VTA (Vendor To Advise)
*   **Overall Collection Efficiency:** VTA
*   **Outlet Load:** 45.0 mg/Nm3 Maximum
*   **Maximum Allowable Pressure Drop:** kPa (blank)
*   **Clean Pressure Drop:** VTA
*   **Dirty Pressure Drop:** kPa (blank)
*   **Turndown:** 40% to 110% of design flow

**Equipment Specifications**
*   **Equipment Type:** Interception Candle
*   **Equipment Orientation:** Vertical, Candle Standing

**Materials of Construction**
*   **Cage / Housing:** 316 L ss
*   **Element:** Glass fibre
*   **Gasket:** Gore-Tex Sealant
*   **Bolting:** 316 L ss

**Vendor Requirements**
1.  Vendor **must** provide curves for predicted collection efficiency versus particle size.
2.  Vendor **must** guarantee outlet particle load and clean pressure drop.
