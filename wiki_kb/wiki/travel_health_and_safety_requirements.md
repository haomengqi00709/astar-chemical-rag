# Travel Health and Safety Requirements

For all Aker Kvaerner Chemetics employees undertaking travel, specific procedures and guidelines **must** be followed to ensure authorization and adherence to health and safety standards.

**Travel Authorization:**
*   Authorization to travel **must** be sought as per procedure Standard-0-PRC-9002, titled "Travel Authorisation". This procedure outlines the necessary steps and approvals required before any business travel can commence. Further details can be found on the [[travel_authorization_process_0_frm_9002]] page.

**Travel Health and Safety Preparations:**
*   Employees **must** make preparations for travel in accordance with Standard-0-PRC-8102, titled "Travel Health and Safety". This document provides essential guidelines for managing health and safety risks associated with business travel, including pre-travel assessments, emergency contacts, and general safety advice. For comprehensive information, refer to the [[0-prc-8102_travel_health_and_safety]] page.
