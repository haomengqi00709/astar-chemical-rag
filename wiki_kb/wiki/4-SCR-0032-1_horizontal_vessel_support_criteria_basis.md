# Horizontal Vessel Support Criteria Basis (4-SCR-0032-1)

This page outlines the foundational documents that serve as the basis for the horizontal vessel support criteria detailed in document 4-SCR-0032-1. The design and engineering of equipment supports rely heavily on accurate and approved equipment drawings, which provide essential dimensional, weight, and interface information.

**Basis for Support Criteria:**
The support criteria for the horizontal vessel are established based on the following key equipment drawings:
*   **Equipment Drawing Number 4-OTL-XXXX, Revision X:** This outline drawing provides the overall dimensions, nozzle orientations, and general arrangement of the horizontal vessel. It is crucial for determining the footprint and spatial requirements of the support structure. Refer to [[outline_drawings]] for more information on this document type.
*   **Equipment Drawing Number 4-DTL-XXXX, Revision X:** This detail drawing provides specific fabrication details, internal components, and precise dimensional data that influence the design of the supports. This type of drawing is essential for understanding the exact points of load application and any unique structural features of the vessel. This is assumed to be a proprietary detail drawing. Refer to [[equipment_proprietary_detail_drawing_prp]] for more information on this document type.

These drawings, along with their specified revisions, form the authoritative source for the physical characteristics and interfaces of the horizontal vessel. Any changes to these basis drawings **must** be reflected in a review and potential revision of the support criteria to maintain design integrity and safety. Adherence to these foundational documents ensures that the [[equipment_support_criteria]] are accurately applied and that the constructed supports are compatible with the vessel's design.
