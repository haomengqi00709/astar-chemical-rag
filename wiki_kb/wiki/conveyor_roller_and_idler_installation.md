# Conveyor Roller and Idler Installation

This page describes the mandatory requirements for the installation of [[conveyor_roller_and_idler_installation|rollers and idlers]] for [[conveying_equipment_mechanical_checkout|conveying equipment]], ensuring they turn freely and their holders are tight. Rollers/idlers **must** be installed, turn freely, and holders **must** be tight.
