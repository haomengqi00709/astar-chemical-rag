# Engineering Subcontract Termination and Liability (0-SPC-2202-1)

This page details the conditions for termination of engineering subcontracts and the limitations of liability for both parties, as specified in document 0-SPC-2202-1.

### Subcontract Termination
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may terminate the Subcontract under the following conditions:
*   **Immediate Termination for Default:** Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may terminate immediately if the Subcontractor has substantially failed to comply with their obligations and has not rectified the default within 2 weeks of receiving notification from Aker Kvaerner Chemetics of the intention to terminate.
*   **Termination for Other Reasons:** Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may terminate the Subcontract with 1 week's notice for any other reason not related to a substantial failure to comply with obligations.

For a comprehensive understanding of subcontract termination, refer to [[subcontract_termination_0_spc_2201]].

### Limitations of Liability
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Subcontractor will not be liable under this Subcontract, in law or equity, including for negligence, in respect of any liability arising in any way in relation to the services for:
*   Loss of profit, loss of use, loss of production, loss of business, or any similar loss.
*   Any indirect or consequential damage.
*   Special, exemplary, or punitive damages.

These limitations define the scope of financial responsibility and protect both parties from certain types of claims. Further details on liability can be found in [[subcontract_liability_0_spc_2201]].
