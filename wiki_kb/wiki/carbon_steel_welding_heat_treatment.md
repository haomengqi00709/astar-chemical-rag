# Carbon Steel Welding Heat Treatment

Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' specification 4-SPC-0060 outlines the requirements for heat treatment procedures during the welding of carbon steel components.

**Preheat and Inter-pass Temperatures**
Preheat and inter-pass temperatures **must** be maintained in accordance with [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels]], Division 1, Appendix R. These temperatures **must** be controlled by using temperature indicating crayons, contact pyrometers, or other suitable and approved means to ensure accuracy and consistency throughout the welding process.

**Post Weld Heat Treatment (PWHT)**
When Post Weld Heat Treatment (PWHT) is required, the PWHT temperature **must not** exceed 649 ºC (1200 ºF). Adherence to this maximum temperature is critical for maintaining the material properties and integrity of the weldment.

Temperatures must be controlled by temperature indicating crayons, contact pyrometers or other suitable means.

**Post Weld Heat Treatment (PWHT)**
PWHT temperature must not exceed 649 ºC (1200 ºF).
