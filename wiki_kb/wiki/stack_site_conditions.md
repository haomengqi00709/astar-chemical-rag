# Stack Site Conditions

This page outlines the "Site Conditions" data category as specified in the [[4-dst-xxxx-r0_template_stack_datasheet_administration|Template Stack Datasheet]]. While the provided facts only indicate the presence of this category, it is understood to encompass critical environmental and geographical data necessary for the structural and operational design of the stack.

**Typical Site Condition Parameters:**
Site conditions generally include, but are not limited to:
*   **Geographical Location:** Latitude, longitude, and elevation.
*   **Ambient Temperature Range:** Minimum, maximum, and average ambient temperatures.
*   **Humidity:** Average and peak humidity levels.
*   **Wind Data:** Prevailing wind speeds, maximum gust speeds, and wind direction data, which are crucial for [[wind_design_requirements]].
*   **Seismic Data:** Seismic zone classification and associated design parameters, essential for [[seismic_design_requirements]].
*   **Rainfall/Snowfall Data:** Average and maximum precipitation levels, including snow loads, which directly impact structural design.
*   **Soil Conditions:** Geotechnical data, including soil bearing capacity and seismic response, relevant for [[stack_design_codes_and_specifications|foundation design loading]].
*   **Corrosivity of Atmosphere:** Presence of corrosive agents in the atmosphere that might affect material selection and coating requirements.
*   **Local Regulations:** Any specific local environmental or building codes that must be adhered to.

These parameters are vital for ensuring the stack's structural integrity, operational efficiency, and compliance with local environmental standards. For broader information on site-specific design, refer to [[site_environmental_design_parameters]] and [[site_design_conditions_1_dst_0001]].
