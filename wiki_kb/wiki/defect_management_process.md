# Defects and Correction Process

This section outlines the definitions, responsibilities, and procedures related to defects in the [[purchase_order_definitions#The Goods and Services|Goods and Services]] provided under the Purchase Order.

**Definition of a Defect:**
A Defect is defined as:
*   A part of the Goods and Services that does not conform with the [[purchase_order_definitions#Goods and Services Information|Goods and Services Information]].
*   A part of the Goods and Services designed by the Vendor that does not comply with the [[purchase_order_definitions#The Applicable Law|Applicable Law]].
*   The Vendor’s design that has not been accepted by the Lead Engineer.

**Defects Correction Period:**
*   The standard **Defects Correction Period** is **4 weeks**. This period begins from the date the Defect is notified to the Vendor.
*   The **Defects Date** is specified in the [[special_conditions_for_purchase]] for the Purchase Order.
*   If no Defects Date is stated in the Special Conditions, it defaults to the earlier of:
    *   52 weeks after the last to occur of [[purchase_order_definitions#Delivery or Deliver|Delivery]] of the whole of the Goods and Services and successful start-up of the Project.
    *   78 weeks after Delivery of the whole of the Goods and Services.
*   The Defects Date will be extended to the end of the last Defects Correction Period if any Defects Correction Period expires after the original Defects Date.
*   For any part of the Goods and Services that is repaired or replaced, the Defects Date will be extended for an additional **52 weeks** following the date of the repair or replacement.

**Vendor Responsibilities for Correction:**
*   The Vendor **must** correct Defects, regardless of whether the Inspector has formally notified them.
*   The Vendor **must** correct all notified Defects before the end of their respective Defects Correction Period.
*   The Project Buyer **must** arrange for Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or the Owner to provide the Vendor with necessary access to and use of any part of the Goods and Services required for correcting a Defect.
*   If the Project Buyer fails to arrange suitable access and use within the Defect Correction Period, the period for correcting that specific Defect will be extended as necessary.

**Cost Assessment for Uncorrected Defects:**
*   If the Vendor fails to correct a notified Defect within its designated Defects Correction Period, the Project Buyer **must** assess the cost incurred by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics to have the Defect corrected by other means.
*   The Vendor **must** pay this assessed amount.
