# Conveyor Trip Wire Installation

This page details the mandatory requirements for the installation of [[conveyor_trip_wire_installation|trip wires]] for [[conveying_equipment_mechanical_checkout|conveying equipment]], as a safety measure. Trip wires **must** be installed.
