# Vendor Roles and Responsibilities

This page outlines the comprehensive roles and responsibilities of the Vendor as defined within Purchase Order [[0-SPC-5001_purchase_order_overview|0-SPC-5001]]. The Vendor is the entity contracted to provide the Goods and Services.

**Core Obligations**
The Vendor's primary responsibility is to Provide the [[goods_and_services_definitions|Goods and Services]] in accordance with the Purchase Order. This includes:

*   **Contract Acceptance:** The Vendor accepts the terms and conditions of the Purchase Order by acknowledging receipt or by commencing to Provide the Goods and Services.
*   **Health and Safety:** The Vendor **must** act in accordance with the health and safety requirements of the [[applicable_law_and_compliance|Applicable Law]] and the Goods and Services Information.
*   **Goods and Services Provision:** The Vendor **must** Provide the Goods and Services in accordance with the Goods and Services Information and the Applicable Law.
*   **Design Responsibility:** The Vendor **shall** design the parts of the Goods and Services specified for Vendor design in the Goods and Services Information. The Vendor **must not** proceed with relevant work until required design submissions are accepted or approved by the [[lead_engineer_roles|Lead Engineer]] or Others.
*   **Intellectual Property:** The Vendor **shall** indemnify Aker Kvaerner Chemetics against claims arising from patent or copyright infringement.
*   **Subcontracting Responsibility:** If the Vendor subcontracts work, they remain fully responsible for performing the Purchase Order. A Supplier's employees and equipment are treated as the Vendor's. See [[subcontractor_obligations_0_spc_2201]].
*   **Quality System:** The Vendor **must** plan, establish, and maintain a quality system conforming to the Purchase Order requirements and **shall** provide access for monitoring and auditing. See [[quality_system_compliance]].
*   **Warranties:** The Vendor provides specific warranties regarding the fitness, suitability, quality, and newness of materials for the Goods and Services.
*   **Access to Work:** The Vendor **must** provide the [[project_buyer_roles|Project Buyer]], [[lead_engineer_roles|Lead Engineer]], and [[owner_roles|Owner]] access to work and stored materials.
*   **Instruction Compliance:** The Vendor **must** obey instructions given by the Project Buyer in accordance with the Purchase Order.
*   **Delivery Schedule:** The Vendor **must** Provide the Goods and Services for Delivery on the [[0-SPC-5001_purchase_order_overview#Delivery Date|Delivery Date]] and **shall not** deliver early unless instructed.
*   **Pre-Delivery Testing:** The Vendor **must not** Deliver tested/inspected Goods and Services until notified by the Project Buyer that they have passed.
*   **Defect Correction:** The Vendor **must** correct notified [[0-SPC-5001_purchase_order_overview#Defect|Defects]] within the [[0-SPC-5001_purchase_order_overview#Defects Correction Period|Defect Correction Period]] and **shall** pay costs for uncorrected Defects.
*   **Invoicing and Payment Evidence:** The Vendor **must** invoice Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and provide evidence of payment to related persons upon request.
*   **Property Protection:** The Vendor **must** properly store, protect, and label Goods and Services as Project property, regardless of title transfer.
*   **Risk Bearing:** The Vendor carries risks not explicitly assigned to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics from the [[0-SPC-5001_purchase_order_overview#Starting Date|Starting Date]] until the [[0-SPC-5001_purchase_order_overview#Defects Date|Defects Date]], including promptly replacing loss and repairing damage.
*   **Indemnification:** The Vendor **shall** indemnify Aker Kvaerner Chemetics against claims due to events at the Vendor's risk.
*   **Insurance:** The Vendor **must** provide the insurances stated in [[0-SPC-5001_table_2]].
*   **Termination Obligations:** Upon termination, the Vendor **must** assign subcontracts if instructed and Deliver all Goods and Services for which payment has been made.

The Vendor's adherence to these responsibilities is critical for the successful execution of the Purchase Order and the overall Project.
