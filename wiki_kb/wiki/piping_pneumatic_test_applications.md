# Pneumatic Leak Test Applications

This section details specific applications where pneumatic leak testing is required.

*   Lines not designed to support the weight of a test liquid will be subjected to a pneumatic leak test.
*   Lines handling fluids which, together with residual water from a hydrostatic test, will create a highly corrosive environment will be subjected to a pneumatic leak test.
*   Pneumatic leak tests will be in accordance with B31.3 para 345.5.
*   Special procedures for preparation and cleaning may also be specified for pneumatic leak tests.
