# Construction Site Staff HSE Responsibilities

All Construction Site Staff Personnel on Aker Kvaerner Chemetics EPC projects bear significant responsibilities for maintaining and promoting a safe work environment. Their active participation and adherence to safety protocols are crucial for achieving the company's HSE targets.

**Key Responsibilities for Construction Site Staff Personnel:**

*   **Policy and Procedure Adherence:**
    *   **Must** read, understand, and carry out the general requirements of the Aker Kvaerner HSE Policy (Standard-0-POL-8000).
    *   **Must** follow planned safe work procedures and methods. Refer to [[safe_work_procedures]].
    *   **Must** ensure that any alteration to the work program does not affect safe working conditions.
    *   **Must** participate in the formulation of safe work procedures and methods.
    *   **Must** ensure that the Job Hazard Analysis procedure (0-PRC-8007) is applied in all areas of operations. See [[0-prc-8007_job_hazard_analysis]] for detailed guidance.

*   **Supervision and Communication:**
    *   **Must** give proper instruction and adequate supervision to employees and others under their control.
    *   **Must** actively encourage employees to report defects or other substandard conditions affecting site safety.
    *   **Must** cooperate with other contractors and with subcontractors to ensure common safety issues are treated appropriately.
    *   **Must** provide subcontractors with necessary information which may affect them.

*   **Personal Protective Equipment (PPE):**
    *   **Must** set a personal example by wearing appropriate personal protective equipment/clothing.
    *   **Must** ensure that all necessary protective equipment and clothing is available for issue as and when required. Refer to [[personal_protective_equipment_policy]].

*   **Site Inspections and Corrective Actions:**
    *   **Must** carry out informal physical condition inspections during the normal work day.
    *   **Must** take corrective action on substandard conditions and unsafe personal acts noted.
    *   Any items noted outside of Construction Site Staff Personnel's control **must** be reported immediately to their supervisor.
