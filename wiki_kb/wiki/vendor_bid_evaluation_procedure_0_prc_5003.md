# Vendor Bid Evaluation Procedure (0-PRC-5003)

This document, 0-PRC-5003, outlines the procedure for evaluating Vendor bids for Goods and Services. The primary purpose of this procedure is to ensure that all Vendors supply Goods and Services in compliance with [[purchase_order_commercial_and_contractual_terms]] and at minimum business risk to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

This procedure describes only the major elements of the work and **must** be read in conjunction with the flowchart 0-SCM-5003, titled "Bid Evaluation and Approval". The evaluation process is comprehensively detailed in this accompanying flowchart.

**Revision History:**
This document is Revision 4. Revision 4 involved a complete rewrite of the procedure. A name change conversion for Aker Kvaerner Chemetics occurred in 2005 and has been implemented throughout the document. No new revision number was assigned for this name change, and the changes related to the name conversion were not specifically marked.
