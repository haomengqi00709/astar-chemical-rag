# Piping Design and Specification (5-PRC-0003)

This document, 5-PRC-0003, details the discipline assignments for the preparation, checking, and approval of piping design and specifications for the Project. It outlines the roles and responsibilities for developing all piping-related documentation.

This procedure ensures that all piping design documents, including P&IDs, layout drawings, and specifications, are developed, reviewed, and approved in a structured manner, adhering to the [[piping_design_criteria_5_prc_0002]] and overall project quality standards.
