# Rotating Equipment Mechanical Checkout Requirements

This page details the mandatory requirements for the mechanical checkout of rotating equipment, which includes items designated by suffixes C (Blowers, Compressors, Fans), G (Grinders, Crushers, Classifiers), P (Pumps), and V (Vacuum pumps). This process is an integral part of the overall [[mechanical_equipment_checkout_procedure]].

**Required Documents:**
The following documents are mandatory for the checkout of rotating equipment:
*   [[piping_and_instrumentation_drawings|P&ID]]
*   Manufacturers documentation
*   [[equipment_data_sheets_4_dst_xxxx|Equipment data sheet]]

**General Installation and Structural Checks:**
*   **Mandatory:** Rotating equipment elevation height **must** be consistent with the design.
*   **Mandatory:** Rotating equipment nameplate data and record **must** match the equipment drawing/data sheet information.
*   **Mandatory:** Rotating equipment nameplate tag **must** match manufacturer information.
*   **Mandatory:** Motor nameplate **must** match the manufacturer information.
*   **Mandatory:** Rotating equipment **must** be horizontal.
*   **Mandatory:** Rotating equipment base **must** be grouted.
*   **Mandatory:** Rotating equipment **must** be bolted to base.
*   **Mandatory:** Installed [[shock_absorber_installation|shock absorbers]] **must** be checked for damage.
*   **Mandatory:** Installed shock absorbers **must** be clean and move smoothly through their travel range.
*   **Mandatory:** [[motor_alignment_procedure|Motor alignment]] **must** have been completed and signed off.
*   **Mandatory:** Motor installation field check is referenced in the [[electrical_systems_field_checkout_9_prc_0004|electrical checklist]].
*   **Mandatory:** [[equipment_grouting_requirements|Grouting]] **must not** obstruct the sliding base, where applicable.
*   **Mandatory:** It **must** be determined if the motor has sealed bearings or whether lubrication is required.

**Surface Treatment and Guards:**
*   **Mandatory:** Equipment **must** have been coated per the job requirements.
*   **Mandatory:** [[coupling_guard_installation|Coupling guard]] **must** have been coated per the job requirements.
*   **Mandatory:** Coupling **must** be installed.
*   **Mandatory:** Shaft **must** rotate freely by hand.
*   **Mandatory:** Guard **must** be installed and bolts tight.

**Belt-Driven Equipment (if applicable):**
*   **Mandatory:** Correct size of [[v_belt_installation_and_tensioning|v-belt]] **must** have been installed.
*   **Mandatory:** V-belt tightness **must** be checked using the installation instruction method.
*   **Mandatory:** V-belt guard **must** have been replaced and be tight.

**Grounding:**
*   **Mandatory:** [[grounding_installation_details|Rotating equipment grounding cable]] connection **must** be tight.
*   **Mandatory:** Rotating equipment grounding cable **must** be connected to the main grounding loop.

**Housing and Materials:**
*   **Mandatory:** All housing nuts and bolts **must** be tight.
*   **Mandatory:** Rotating equipment size and material **must** be checked against manufacturers information and data sheet.

**Nozzles and Piping:**
*   **Mandatory:** Nozzles and piping **must** be free of debris.
*   **Mandatory:** Nozzles **must not** be strained by connected piping.
*   **Mandatory:** Adequate pipe supports **must** be installed for nozzles (may require piping to be disconnected).

**Lubrication and Sealing:**
*   **Mandatory:** [[oiler_installation_and_maintenance|Oilers]] **must** be installed (if required).
*   **Mandatory:** Oiler and [[oil_seal_inspection|oil seal]] **must** be checked for leakage.
*   **Mandatory:** Oil **must** be the correct specification for the equipment.
*   **Mandatory:** Oil casing **must** contain oil to the fill line.
*   **Mandatory:** Manufacturer's installation instructions for [[bearing_lubrication_and_inspection|bearings]] **must** be checked (bearings may have shipping grease).
*   **Mandatory:** Bearings **must** have been lubricated.
*   **Mandatory:** Correct seal **must** have been installed.
*   **Mandatory:** [[seal_flush_system_piping|Seal flush piping]] **must** be connected.

**Ancillary Connections:**
*   **Mandatory:** [[instrument_air_system|Air supply line, regulator, and oiler]] **must** be installed.
*   **Mandatory:** [[drain_plug_installation|Drain plug]] **must** be installed and clear of debris.
*   **Mandatory:** [[start_up_strainer_installation|Start-up strainer]] **must** be installed (if required).

**Compressor-Specific Checks:**
*   **Mandatory (for Compressors):** All valves **must** open and close fully and easily.
*   **Mandatory (for Compressors):** Manufacturer's instructions for lubrication **must** have been followed.
*   **Mandatory (for Compressors):** The oil within the compressor casing may be shipping oil.
*   **Mandatory (for Compressors):** Supplied [[instrumentation_and_control_design_criteria_8_prc_0002|instrumentation]] and seal fluids **must** be checked versus the specifications.

**Installation and Structural Checks**
*   Rotating equipment elevation height must be consistent with the design.

**Motor and Drive Checks**
*   Motor installation field check is referenced in the electrical checklist.
*   It must be determined if the motor has sealed bearings or whether lubrication is required.
*   Equipment must have been coated per the job requirements.
*   [[coupling_guard_installation|Coupling guard]] must have been coated per the job requirements.
*   Coupling must be installed.
*   Shaft must rotate freely by hand.
*   Guard must be installed and bolts tight.
*   Correct size of [[v_belt_installation_and_tensioning|v-belt]] must have been installed.
*   V-belt guard must have been replaced and be tight.

**Grounding**
*   Rotating equipment grounding cable connection must be tight.
*   Rotating equipment grounding cable must be connected to the main grounding loop.

**Housing and Components**
*   All housing nuts and bolts must be tight.
*   Rotating equipment size and material must be checked against manufacturers information and data sheet.
*   Correct seal must have been installed.
*   [[seal_flush_system_piping|Seal flush piping]] must be connected.
*   Air supply line, regulator, and oiler must be installed.
*   [[drain_plug_installation|Drain plug]] must be installed and clear of debris.

**Nozzles and Piping**
*   Nozzles and piping must be free of debris.
*   Nozzles must not be strained by connected piping.
*   Adequate pipe supports must be installed for nozzles (may require piping to be disconnected).

**Compressor Specific Checks**
*   For Compressors, all valves must open and close fully and easily.
*   For Compressors, manufacturer's instructions for lubrication must have been followed.
*   The oil within the compressor casing may be shipping oil.
*   For Compressors, supplied instrumentation and seal fluids must be checked versus the specifications.
