# Gasket Specification (5-SPC-0003)

This specification, document ID 5-SPC-0003, outlines the requirements for gaskets used in piping systems. Unless explicitly listed within this document, the specific gasket style shall be determined by the applicable [[pipe_specification]].

### General Gasket Requirements
*   Gasket X indicates that no gasket is required for the specified application.
*   Gasket U has been added to this specification.

### Gasket Colour Coding
Gasket colour codes are applied by spray painting the outer edge of the gasket in 3 places for easy identification. The following colour codes are assigned to specific gasket materials:
*   **Gasket C**: EPDM, Peroxide Cured - Yellow
*   **Gasket D**: Butyl Rubber - Red
*   **Gasket T**: Viton B - Blue
*   **Gasket V**: Plasticized PVC - Green

### Specific Gasket Types and Dimensions
*   **Gasket F**: This gasket features an IBC Teflon envelope with FF filler.
*   **Gasket Y1**: A spiral wound gasket constructed from 304 SS with graphite filler, and both inner and outer rings made of 304 SS. It has an uncompressed thickness of 4.5 mm and a compressed thickness of 3.2 mm.
*   **Gasket Y2**: A spiral wound gasket made of 316 SS with graphite filler, a 316 SS inner ring, and a carbon steel outer ring. It shares the same thickness specifications as Gasket Y1, with an uncompressed thickness of 4.5 mm and a compressed thickness of 3.2 mm.
*   **Gasket G (for Glass-Lined Steel Pipe)**: This gasket is supplied by the glass-lined steel pipe manufacturer. It is a continuous U-type PTFE envelope, incorporating a non-asbestos acid resistant filler and a stainless steel corrugated metal insert.
    *   For pipe sizes DN250 and less, the uninstalled thickness is 7 mm, and the compressed thickness is 5 mm.
    *   For pipe size DN300, the uninstalled thickness is 10 mm, and the compressed thickness is 7 mm.
*   **Gasket K (for Ductile Iron Pipe)**: This gasket is supplied by the ductile iron pipe manufacturer. It features a step ring design, which includes a 1.6 mm thick outer ring for centering and a 3.2 mm thick inner ring for sealing.

For additional information on gasket material selection, refer to [[gasket_material_selection]].

*   Gasket X indicates that no gasket is required.

### Gasket Colour Coding
Gasket colour codes are applied by spray painting the outer edge of the gasket in 3 places.
