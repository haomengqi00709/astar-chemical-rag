# Product Stripper Nozzle Sizing and Criteria

This page outlines the criteria and specific sizing details for nozzles on the Product Stripper, including inlet, outlet, overflow, and drain connections.

**General Nozzle Sizing Criteria**
*   **Nozzles attached to process lines will be sized in accordance with [[line_list_development_and_approval_5_prc_0004|line sizing criteria]].**
*   "Line Sizing" will be entered for nozzles attached to process lines.
*   **Overflow nozzles are self venting.**
*   Flow and its basis (e.g., 50 m3/h = max flow from feed pump) will be entered for overflow nozzles.
*   For drain nozzles, the minimum time to drain the tank contents will be entered.
*   **Suggested velocity is based on flooded and self venting criteria.**
*   **Flooded criteria is used in the Crossflow stripper for sizing the level controlled acid outlet connected to an Acid Tower.**
*   **Self venting criteria is used to size the Crossflow stripper Drain and the Product Stripper acid outlet.**
