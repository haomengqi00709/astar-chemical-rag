# Wall Penetration Details

Wall penetration details specify the materials and methods required for sealing openings after conduit installation, with particular attention to fire-proof sealants where necessary. These details are crucial for maintaining structural integrity, fire safety, and environmental sealing.

**Content of Wall Penetration Details**
Wall penetration details **shall** identify:
*   The materials required to seal the opening after conduit installation.
*   Specific wall penetrations that require a fire-proof sealant.
*   The materials to be used for fire-proof sealant.
*   The method of installation for fire-proof sealant.
