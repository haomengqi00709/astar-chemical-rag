# HSE Specific Training Requirements

To ensure safety in high-risk operations, specific job training is mandated for personnel engaged in work involving the use or operation of the following equipment:

*   Cranes
*   Lift trucks
*   Mobile equipment
*   Powder-actuated tools
*   Scaffolds
*   Ladders
*   Man-lifts
*   Other high-risk equipment
