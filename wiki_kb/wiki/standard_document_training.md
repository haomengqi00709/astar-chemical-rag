# Standard Document Training

To ensure the effective and compliant use of standard documents across Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, a mandatory training program is in place for all employees.

### Training Requirement
All employees **must** be trained in the use and requirements of standard documents as applicable to their assigned work. This ensures that personnel are familiar with the [[standard_document_management_procedure_0_prc_0001]] and can correctly apply the company's established "Style" and methods.

### Training Methodology
Training **shall** be accomplished by means of group presentations and discussion. This interactive approach facilitates understanding and allows for clarification of any questions regarding the application of standard documents.

### Training Delivery
Training **shall** be delivered by the [[standard_document_roles_and_responsibilities#Approver|Approver]] or a designated delegate. This ensures that the training is conducted by individuals with a comprehensive understanding of the standards and their underlying principles.

### Training Records
Applicable Business Directors or their delegates **shall** ensure that a log of training received by each employee in their department is maintained. This log serves as a record of compliance with the training requirements and supports ongoing professional development.
