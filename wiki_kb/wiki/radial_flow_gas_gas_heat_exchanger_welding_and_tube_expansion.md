# Radial Flow Gas/Gas Heat Exchanger Welding and Tube Expansion

This page outlines the specific requirements for welding and tube expansion procedures applicable to radial flow gas/gas heat exchangers, as defined in [[4-SPC-0041_radial_flow_gas_gas_heat_exchanger_manufacturing_requirements|Specification 4-SPC-0041]]. These procedures are critical for ensuring the structural integrity, leak-tightness, and longevity of the heat exchanger components.

**Welding Requirements**
All welds in tubesheets and expansion plates **must** be radial butt welds. This specific weld type is mandated to ensure optimal strength and resistance to operational stresses. The welding processes **must** adhere to general welding standards such as [[4-SPC-0014_overview|Welding Austenitic Stainless Steel]] and [[4-SPC-00XX_welding_carbon_steel_overview|Welding Carbon Steel]], depending on the materials of construction.

**Tube-to-Tubesheet Joint Preparation and Expansion**
*   **Fit-up Aid:** As an aid to fit-up prior to welding, tubes may be roller expanded into the tubesheet using taper rollers.
*   **Roller Expansion Tooling:** Roller expansion **must** utilize a degreased tool.
*   **Lubricant Prohibition:** No lubricant **must** be used during the roller expansion process. This prevents contamination that could compromise weld quality.
*   **Tack Welding:** Tack welding of tubes to tubesheets is **not permitted**. This restriction is in place to avoid localized stress concentrations or metallurgical issues that could arise from tack welds.
*   **Pre-Weld Degreasing:** Prior to welding, all tubesheet holes, faces, and tube surfaces within 50mm (2”) of the weld **must** be degreased. The degreasing solvent **must** be suitable for the materials, such as acetone or isopropyl alcohol, to ensure a clean surface for welding.
*   **Post-Weld Expansion:** Expansion of the tube to tubesheet joint after welding is **not permitted**. This prevents potential damage to the completed weld or the surrounding material.

These stringent requirements are put in place to ensure the highest quality of fabrication, minimizing the risk of defects and ensuring the reliable performance of the heat exchanger under demanding operating conditions. For further details on material selection and mechanical design, refer to [[gas_gas_heat_exchanger_material_selection]] and [[gas_gas_heat_exchanger_mechanical_calculation_prerequisites]].

### Welding Requirements

All welds in tubesheets and expansion plates must be radial butt welds. Tack welding of tubes to tubesheets is strictly prohibited.

Prior to welding, all tubesheet holes, faces, and tube surfaces within 50mm (2”) of the weld area must be thoroughly degreased. Acceptable degreasing solvents include acetone or isopropyl alcohol.

### Tube Expansion Procedures

As an aid to fit up before welding, tubes may be roller expanded into the tubesheet using taper rollers. When performing roller expansion, a degreased tool must be used, and no lubricant is permitted.

Expansion of the tube to tubesheet joint after welding is not permitted.
