# Field and General Instructions for Construction Subcontracts

This page details the procedures for issuing and managing Field Instructions and other general instructions to subcontractors, which often lead to [[construction_subcontract_compensation_events]].

**1. Field Instructions:**
Field Instructions are typically issued to the Subcontractor in response to [[construction_technical_queries_0_prc_6092]]. These instructions frequently direct the Subcontractor to "Proceed with the work described immediately" and to "Determine the actual cost of the work and submit a claim for compensation."

*   **Claim Approval:** Claims for compensation resulting from Field Instructions **must** be approved by the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Site Manager.
*   **Change Order Compilation:** At each assessment date, the Subcontract Administrator **must** execute a [[subcontract_change_order_procedure_0_chg_6xx0]] that compiles all Field Instructions completed since the previous assessment date and for which cost claims have been approved.

**2. General Instructions:**
Any communication issued by the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Site Manager or by the Subcontract Administrator to the Subcontractor containing an instruction **must** have one of two specified texts appended, depending on the nature of the instruction:

*   **Text 1 (Immediate Proceeding Required):**
    "The foregoing is an instruction to the Subcontractor given by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics which may change the Works Instructions and may be a compensation event. The Subcontractor **must** immediately proceed with the instruction. The Subcontractor **must** submit a notice and quotation, including a revised schedule, for any compensation event resulting from the revised documents in accordance with Sections 27 and 28 of [[standard_conditions_for_construction_subcontract_0_spc_6001]]."
    *   If Text 1 is used, the Subcontractor **must** immediately proceed with the instruction.
    *   If Text 1 is used, the Subcontractor **must** submit a notice and quotation, including a revised schedule, for any compensation event resulting from the revised documents in accordance with Sections 27 and 28 of [[standard_conditions_for_construction_subcontract_0_spc_6001]].

*   **Text 2 (Quotation Required Before Proceeding):**
    "The foregoing instruction is a proposed revision to the Works Information. The Subcontractor **must** submit a quotation, including a revised schedule, for compliance with the instruction in accordance with Section 28 of [[standard_conditions_for_construction_subcontract_0_spc_6001]]."
    *   If Text 2 is used, the Subcontractor **must** submit a quotation, including a revised schedule, for compliance with the instruction in accordance with Section 28 of [[standard_conditions_for_construction_subcontract_0_spc_6001]].
