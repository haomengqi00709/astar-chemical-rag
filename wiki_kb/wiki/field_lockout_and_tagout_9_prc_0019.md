# Field Lockout and Tagout (9-PRC-0019)

This document, 9-PRC-0019, outlines the procedure for implementing Lockout and Tagout (LOTO) in the field for electrical systems within the Project. It details the mandatory steps for de-energizing equipment, isolating energy sources, and applying locks and tags to prevent accidental re-energization during maintenance or testing.

To control safety risks during [[water_and_air_trials_and_safety_protocols]], this procedure is strictly enforced. It is a critical safety protocol for protecting personnel from hazardous energy during plant operations and maintenance.
