# Air Operated Diaphragm Pump Installation Inspection Requirements

This page details the mandatory inspection requirements for the installation of [[aodd_pump_datasheet_administration|Air Operated Diaphragm Pumps]], as outlined in the Field Inspection Checklist **4-FIC-XX02-R0**. Adherence to these requirements is crucial for ensuring the safe and efficient operation of the pump.

**Mandatory Installation Checklist Items:**

The following items **must** be verified during the installation inspection:

*   **Nameplate:** The correct nameplate **must** be installed on the pump. This ensures proper identification and compliance with specifications.
*   **Pump Position:** The pump's position **must** be checked for correct elevation, orientation, and levelness. Proper positioning is vital for operational stability and performance.
*   **Foundation and Anchoring:** The foundation and anchor bolts **must** be secure and properly tightened to prevent movement and vibration during operation.
*   **Grouting:** Grouting around the foundation **must** be completed to provide a solid base and prevent corrosion.
*   **Piping Alignment and Support:** Suction and discharge piping **must** be properly aligned and supported to ensure no undue stress is placed on the pump nozzles. Refer to [[piping_design_and_specification_5_prc_0003|piping design specifications]] for detailed requirements.
*   **Air Supply Piping:** The air supply piping **must** be correctly installed. Details on air supply requirements can be found in [[aodd_pump_air_supply_requirements]].
*   **Air Supply Filter:** An air supply filter **must** be installed to ensure clean air delivery to the pump, protecting its internal components.
*   **Pulsation Damper:** A pulsation damper **must** be installed and filled with the appropriate fluid to mitigate pressure fluctuations and ensure smooth operation.
*   **Check-Valves:** Check-valves **must** be installed in the correct orientation to prevent backflow and ensure proper fluid direction.
*   **Flange Guards:** Flange guards **must** be installed for safety, protecting personnel from potential leaks or sprays at flange connections.

These inspection points are part of a comprehensive [[4-FIC-XX02-R0_aodd_pump_field_inspection_checklist_overview|field inspection process]] designed to identify and address any deficiencies before commissioning.

The following specific requirements must be verified during the field inspection:
*   Correct Nameplate must be installed.
*   Pump position must be checked (elevation, orientation, leveled).
*   Foundation / Anchor bolts must be secure and tightened.
*   Grouting must be completed.
*   Suction/discharge piping must be properly aligned and supported (no stress on pump nozzles).
*   Air supply piping must be installed.
*   Air supply filter must be installed.
*   Pulsation Damper must be installed and filled with appropriate fluid.
*   Check-valves must be installed in correct orientation.
*   Flange Guards must be installed.
