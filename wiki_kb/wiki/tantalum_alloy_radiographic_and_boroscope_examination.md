# Tantalum Alloy Radiographic and Boroscope Examination

This section outlines the requirements for radiographic and boroscope examination of tantalum alloy tubing, to be performed when ultrasonic testing is not feasible or as a supplementary measure, as specified in [[4-SPC-0045_tantalum_alloy_welding_specification_overview|4-SPC-0045]].

*   **Radiographic Examination (Alternative)**: When ultrasonic examination is not available, radiographic examination is **required**. Tubes **must** be 100% radiographed to an [[chemetics_aker_kvaerner_company_details|Aker Kvaerner Chemetics]] approved procedure. The approved radiographic procedure **must** show established defect limits. The radiographic technique used **must** maximize sensitivity to weld defects. A radiographic report **must** be submitted to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics prior to any tube being used for manufacture.
*   **Boroscope Examination**: In addition to radiographic examination, 20% of tubes **will** be internally examined by boroscope. The boroscope examination percentage **will** be increased to 50% upon detection of any defect.
*   **Visual Defect Follow-up**: Visual defects **will** be followed up by exploratory radiography.

For general NDE guidelines, refer to [[non_destructive_examination]].

*   **Boroscope Examination**:
    *   In addition to radiographic examination, 20% of tubes will be internally examined by boroscope.
    *   The boroscope examination percentage will be increased to 50% upon detection of any defect.
    *   Visual defects found during boroscope examination will be followed up by exploratory radiography.
