# Gas/Gas Heat Exchanger Mechanical Calculation Prerequisites

Before commencing mechanical calculations for gas/gas heat exchangers, the following documents and standards **must** be available and referenced:

**Required Documents:**
*   **Exchanger Sizing, Operating Conditions, and Configuration:** These are determined by [[4-CAL-0001_heat_exchanger_performance_calculations]]: TGAS/2. This document provides critical data such as temperatures and pressures required for the mechanical design.
*   **Site Specific Requirements:** Details regarding environmental factors like wind and seismic conditions are provided in [[1-DST-0001_site_design_conditions]]: Site Design Conditions.
*   **Additional Equipment Requirements:** General requirements for equipment are outlined in [[general_equipment_requirements_specification]]: 4-SPC-0001.

**Referenced Codes and Standards:**
The following industry codes and standards are essential for stress evaluation, material properties, and specific component designs:

*   **ASME(1):** Refers to ASME Boiler and Pressure Vessel Code, Section VIII, Division 1, "Rules for Construction of Pressure Vessels," published by the American Society of Mechanical Engineers, New York, New York, 200. This code is used for evaluating primary membrane stresses and for the mechanical design of gas/gas exchanger components in combination with proprietary methods.
*   **ASME(2):** Refers to ASME Boiler and Pressure Vessel Code, Section VIII, Division 2, "Rules for Construction of Pressure Vessels – Alternative Rules," published by the American Society of Mechanical Engineers, New York, New York, 200. This division is used for evaluating local and secondary stresses, including stress combinations (e.g., membrane + bending), specifically referencing Figure 4-130.1.
*   **ASME(3):** Refers to ASME Boiler and Pressure Vessel Code, Section II, Part D, "Materials - Properties," published by the American Society of Mechanical Engineers, New York, New York, 200. This part provides allowable stress values and physical properties for materials used in the design.
*   **TEMA(4):** Refers to "Standards of the Tubular Exchanger Manufacturers Association," 8th Edition, New York, New York, 1998. This standard is specifically referenced for spring rate calculations for expansion joints (Section 8).
*   **Kopp & Sayre(5):** Refers to Kopp, S., and M.F. Sayre, "Expansion Joints for Heat Exchangers," ASME Misc. Papers Vol. 6, No. 211 (1950, ASME annual meeting). This method is used for stress calculations for expansion joints.

These documents and standards **must** be thoroughly understood and applied throughout the mechanical calculation process.

For an overview of the entire procedure, refer to [[4-prc-0009_gas_gas_exchanger_mechanical_calculations]].
