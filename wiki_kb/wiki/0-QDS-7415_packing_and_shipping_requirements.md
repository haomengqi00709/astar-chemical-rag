# Packing and Shipping Requirements (0-QDS-7415)

This section outlines the packing and shipping requirements for the Dual Laminate Tank project, as specified in 0-QDS-7415. Packing/Crating is a critical item or activity to ensure the safe transport of the tank.

**Packaging:**
*   **Surveillance:** Surveillance by Chemetics for packaging **must** be "Complete," indicating a full review and approval of the packaging methods and materials used. This ensures the tank is adequately protected during transit.

**Packing List:**
*   **Quality Record:** The Packing List is a mandatory quality record and **must** have a Quality Record status of AP (As Prepared). This means the vendor is responsible for preparing and maintaining this document.
*   **Surveillance:** Surveillance by Chemetics for the Packing List **must** be "Complete," signifying a full review and approval of the document.

These requirements ensure that the dual laminate tank is properly prepared for shipment, minimizing the risk of damage during transportation.
