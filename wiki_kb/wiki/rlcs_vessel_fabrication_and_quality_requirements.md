# RLCS Vessel Fabrication and Quality Requirements

This page outlines the mandatory fabrication and quality assurance requirements for the Rubber Lined Carbon Steel (RLCS) Vessel, as specified in datasheet 4-DST-XXXX-R1. Adherence to these requirements is essential to ensure the vessel's compliance with design standards, operational safety, and longevity.

**Fabricator Responsibilities and Submissions:**
*   The fabricator **must** submit all design calculations and fabrication drawings to Chemetics for approval before commencing any fabrication work. This ensures that all design aspects are reviewed and validated by Chemetics engineers.
*   The fabricator **must** supply all necessary bolts, nuts, and gaskets for any openings that are to be fitted with blind flanges.

**Marking and Identification:**
*   The equipment name, equipment number, and nozzle mark letters **must** be clearly stenciled on the vessel's exterior.
*   Stenciled letters **must** be 75 mm high and in black color for clear visibility.
*   **Equipment Name:** Hydrochloric Acid Storage Tank
*   **Chemetics Equipment Number:** 7022T

**Painting and Coatings:**
*   **Paint Specification on Carbon Steel (CS):** Painting of carbon steel components **shall** conform to [[7-SPC-0001]].
*   **Paint System and Colour:** The specific paint system and color **shall** be in accordance with [[7-SPC-0006]].

**Quality Assurance and Inspection:**
*   **Quality Assurance Requirements:** All fabrication and inspection activities **must** comply with API-650 standards, Chemetics internal specifications, and the Chemetics Quality Data Sheet (QDS).
*   **Radiography:** Radiographic examination **shall** be performed as per the requirements of API-650.
*   **Leak Test:** A leak test **shall** be conducted by flooding the vessel with water to the overflow level, as specified in [[rlcs_vessel_design_conditions]].

These stringent fabrication and quality requirements ensure that the RLCS vessel is manufactured to the highest standards, meeting all specified design and performance criteria.
