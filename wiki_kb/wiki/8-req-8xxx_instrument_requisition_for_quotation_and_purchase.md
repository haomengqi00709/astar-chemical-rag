# Instrument Requisition for Quotation and Purchase (8-REQ-8XXX)

The Instrument Requisition for Quotation (8-REQ-8XXX) and Request for Purchase (8-REQ-8XXX) are key documents in the procurement process for field instruments and instrument bulk materials. Both refer to [[requisition_for_quotation_or_purchase_0_prc_5002]].

**For Field Instruments:**

**Requisition for Quotation (8-REQ-8XXX):**
*   **Prepared by:** Field Instrument Engineer (FIE)
*   **Content:** Will include individual [[8-dst-xxxx_instrument_specification_sheets]], a single [[8-vdr-8xxx_instrument_vendor_document_requirements]] form per instrument type, and a [[8-qds-8xxx_instrument_quality_data_sheet]] per instrument type.
*   The FIE will provide all field instrument requisitions, and technical responsibility for the specification of the field instruments will remain with the FIE.

**Request for Purchase (8-REQ-8XXX):**
*   **Prepared by:** Field Instrument Engineer (FIE)

**For Instrument Bulk Material:**

**Requisition for Quotation (8-REQ-8XXX):**
*   **Prepared by:** Detail Design Engineer (DDE)
*   **Content:** The DDE will provide Individual [[8-vdr-8xxx_instrument_vendor_document_requirements]] forms and [[8-qds-8xxx_instrument_quality_data_sheet]] forms as required with each item ordered.
*   The DDE will provide all Instrument Bulk Material requisitions, and technical responsibility for the specification of the Instrument Bulk Material will remain with the DDE. Instrument Bulk Material will be taken off of the [[instrument_installation_details_8_dtl_xxxx]] bill of materials.

**Request for Purchase (8-REQ-8XXX):**
*   **Prepared by:** Detail Design Engineer (DDE)

**Finalization and Purchase:**
The Instrument Engineer will advise the FIE/DDE in writing of the successful Vendor. The FIE/DDE will then update 8-REQ-8XXX with the appropriate Vendor details and publish the final document. The FIE/DDE will advise the Buyer that the Requisition is complete and ready for purchase, and the Buyer will process the purchase order accordingly.
