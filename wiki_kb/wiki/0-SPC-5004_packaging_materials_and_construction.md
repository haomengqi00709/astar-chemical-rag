# Packaging Materials and Construction (0-SPC-5004)

The type of packaging utilized **will** be one of the methods described in this specification, as called up on the [[0-SPC-5001_purchase_order_overview|Purchase Order]].

**Wooden Packaging:**
*   Wooden packaging **will** be made of hardwood or conifer timbers of good quality.
*   Wooden packaging timbers **must** be without cracks, loose knots, or other defects that could compromise structural integrity.
*   Plywood is acceptable for wooden packaging. No other manufactured sheeting is permitted.
*   Conifer timbers **must** be free of bark, grub holes (holes larger than 3mm across), and visible signs of living pests.
*   Conifer timbers **must** have a moisture content of less than 20 percent.

**Prohibited Packing Materials:**
The use of the following materials in packing is **not permitted**:
*   Hay
*   Straw
*   Flax combining
*   Palm packing fibre
*   Brown coconut fibre
*   Used hessian bags
*   Hessian linings

**Crating and Securing:**
*   Goods **will** be fully enclosed by wooden crating. Wooden crating **must** be fastened securely using spiral type galvanised nails.
*   Goods **will** be strapped to base pallets using steel bands. These steel bands **must** be applied with a stretching tool and secured with crimped steel seals.
*   Items within a single crate **will** be partitioned, braced, blocked, or secured to the base to prevent harmful contact with each other.
*   Palletised goods **will** be secured to racks, cradles, or saddles and protected by draping or wrapping with a water-resistant medium.

For specialized handling equipment, refer to [[0-SPC-5004_special_shipping_handling_equipment]]. These requirements are part of the [[0-SPC-5004_general_packaging_requirements|General Packaging Requirements]] and the [[0-SPC-5004_packaging_land_transportation|Packaging and Shipping for Land Transportation Overview]].
