# Packaging Land Transportation (0-SPC-5004)

This document, 0-SPC-5004, outlines the mandatory requirements for packaging goods intended for land transportation to the Project site. It specifies the methods, materials, and standards for protecting equipment and materials during transit.

Goods shipped to the site **must** be protected against damage by appropriate packaging, in accordance with this specification. This ensures that materials arrive in an undamaged condition, ready for construction or installation.
