# Standard Document Management Procedure (0-PRC-0001)

This procedure, identified as document ID **0-PRC-0001**, outlines the comprehensive process for the creation, use, and management of standard documents within Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. The primary objective of standard documentation is to provide a common set of tools and define consistent methods for all company work, thereby establishing the distinct Aker Kvaerner Chemetics "Style."

The term "document" is used generically throughout this procedure, encompassing a wide range of materials such as drawings, specifications, procedures, lists, and data sheets.

This procedure is foundational to ensuring consistency and quality across all projects and operations. It details the lifecycle of a standard document from its initial draft through approval, revision, and eventual use in projects.

Key aspects covered by this procedure include:
*   [[standard_document_roles_and_responsibilities]]: Defines the roles of the Standards Administrator, Checker, and Approver, and their specific duties in the document management process.
*   [[standard_document_storage_and_access]]: Specifies where standard documents are stored, how access is controlled, and the protocols for version management.
*   [[standard_document_lifecycle_and_revision]]: Details the steps involved in drafting, reviewing, approving, and revising standard documents, including comment periods and status updates.
*   [[standard_document_training]]: Outlines the mandatory training requirements for all employees regarding the use and application of standard documents.
*   [[standard_document_compliance_and_project_use_overview]]: Establishes the requirements for compliance with standard documents and introduces the different methods for their integration into project work.
*   [[project_use_standard_documents_unmodified]]: Describes the process for incorporating standard documents into projects without any modifications.
*   [[project_use_standard_documents_modified]]: Explains the procedure for adapting standard documents to meet specific project or contractual requirements through modification.
*   [[project_use_standard_documents_rewritten]]: Provides guidelines for situations where a standard document must be completely re-written for a project.
