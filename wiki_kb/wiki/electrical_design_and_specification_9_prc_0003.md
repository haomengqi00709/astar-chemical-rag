# Electrical Design and Specification (9-PRC-0003)

This document, 9-PRC-0003, details the discipline assignments for the preparation, checking, and approval of electrical design and specifications for the Project. It outlines the roles and responsibilities for developing all electrical documentation.

This procedure ensures that all electrical design documents, including single-line diagrams, cable schedules, and equipment specifications, are developed, reviewed, and approved in a structured manner, adhering to the [[electrical_design_criteria]] and overall project quality standards.
