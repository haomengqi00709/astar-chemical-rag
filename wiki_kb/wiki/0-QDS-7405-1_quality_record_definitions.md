# Blower Quality Data Sheet Quality Record Definitions

This page defines the abbreviations used for Quality Record requirements within Document ID 0-QDS-7405-1, pertaining to the Blower Quality Data Sheet. These definitions clarify how documentation related to quality assurance and inspection activities should be managed and submitted.

**Quality Record Definitions:**
*   **AP (As Prepared):** This designation means that the Quality Record is required "As Prepared" by the vendor. It indicates that the document should be submitted in its original form as generated during the manufacturing or testing process.
*   **FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package):** This designation means that the Quality Record is required to be "Included in the Quality Assurance Documentation Package." This implies that the document must be part of the comprehensive set of quality records submitted by the vendor at the completion of the work.

These definitions are crucial for understanding the documentation requirements for various [[0-QDS-7405-1_inspection_and_documentation_requirements]] and ensuring proper [[quality_management_and_compliance]].
