# Electrical Site Plan

The Electrical Site Plan drawing provides an overall view of the plant site, detailing the locations of key electrical infrastructure and other relevant site features. This plan is essential for initial layout and coordination.

**Content of the Electrical Site Plan**
The electrical site plan drawing **shall** show:
*   All process buildings.
*   Pipe racks.
*   Equipment.
*   Roads.
*   Substation yards.
*   The incoming power location.
*   Electrical rooms.
*   Underground duct banks for electrical services.
*   Underground duct banks for water services.
*   Underground duct banks for sewer services.
*   Underground duct banks for storm water services.
*   Underground duct banks for process piping.

The electrical site plan drawing **shall** reference the [[final_single_line_diagrams]].
