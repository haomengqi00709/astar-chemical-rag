# Quality Records and Documentation for 0-QDS-7301

This section outlines the specific quality records and documentation requirements for projects under Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7301. Proper documentation is essential for traceability, verification, and overall quality management.

**Quality Record Abbreviations:**
The following abbreviations are used to denote the status or inclusion of quality records:
*   **AP:** Stands for "As Prepared," indicating that the document is provided in its prepared state.
*   **FP:** Stands for "Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package," meaning the document **must** be part of the final Quality Assurance Documentation Package submitted by the vendor.

**Required Quality Records:**
The following documents are specifically **required** to be included as "FP" (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package):
*   **Mill Test Certificates:** These certificates provide essential material properties and compliance data. [[mill_test_certificates]]
*   **Certificate of Compliance:** This document certifies that the supplied materials or products comply with specified standards and requirements. [[certificate_of_compliance]]
*   **Packing List - Load 1:** This list details the contents of the first truckload for packaging and shipping verification. [[packing_list]]

For a broader understanding of quality record definitions, refer to [[0-QDS-7_quality_record_definitions]].
