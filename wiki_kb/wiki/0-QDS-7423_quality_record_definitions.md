# Thickener Quality Record Definitions (0-QDS-7423)

This page defines the abbreviations used within Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7423 for documenting quality records related to the Thickener equipment.

**Quality Record Definitions:**
*   **AP = "As Prepared"**: This designation indicates that the quality record is required to be prepared by the vendor.
*   **FP = "Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package"**: This designation indicates that the quality record **shall** be included as part of the final Quality Assurance Documentation Package submitted by the vendor.

These definitions ensure clarity in the documentation requirements for all inspections, tests, and certifications related to the Thickener. For a broader understanding of quality record management, refer to [[0-QDS-7411_quality_record_definitions]].
