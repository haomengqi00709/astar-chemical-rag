# Special Conditions for Purchase

Special Conditions for Purchase address unique requirements that are typically project-specific, supplementing the [[standard_conditions_for_purchase]]. These conditions are presented in document 0-SPC-5003: Special Conditions for Purchase.

Data available in SQL: 0-SPC-5003

**Usage and Context:**
*   Special conditions are normally project-specific, designed to cater to the unique demands and risks of individual projects.
*   They **shall** always be used in conjunction with [[standard_conditions_for_purchase]] (0-SPC-5002: Standard Conditions for Purchase (Long Form)), ensuring comprehensive coverage for more complex procurements.

**Structure of 0-SPC-5003:**
The standard version of 0-SPC-5003 is structured to facilitate customization while providing clear guidance:
*   It contains clause titles only, allowing for flexible content development.
*   It includes a description of circumstances under which a particular clause may be required.
*   It provides optional wording for various clauses, offering a starting point for drafting.
*   Descriptive material within 0-SPC-5003 is set out in square brackets `[]`. This material is intended for guidance during preparation and **shall** be deleted before the document is officially issued to vendors.

**Identification and Preparation:**
*   The necessity for Special Conditions for Purchase may be identified as a requirement stemming from the project risk management strategy. This ensures that specific project risks are adequately addressed in the commercial terms. Refer to [[project_risk_analysis_0_lst_2006]] for details on risk management.
*   0-SPC-5003: Special Conditions for Purchase **shall** be prepared specifically for each project by the Procurement department.

**Approval Process:**
To ensure accuracy and legal compliance, Special Conditions for Purchase undergo a stringent review and approval process:
*   They **shall** be **checked** by the Director of Projects.
*   They **shall** be **approved** by Legal.

**Usage and Context:**
Special conditions will always be used in conjunction with 0-SPC-5002: Standard Conditions for Purchase (Long Form). The need for Special Conditions for Purchase may also be identified by the project risk management strategy.

**Structure and Preparation of 0-SPC-5003:**
The standard version of 0-SPC-5003 contains clause titles only, along with a description of circumstances when a clause may be required and optional wording. Descriptive material within 0-SPC-5003 is set out in square brackets and must be deleted before the document is issued.

**Responsibilities:**
0-SPC-5003: Special Conditions for Purchase will be prepared specifically for each project by Procurement. It will then be checked by the Director of Projects and approved by Legal.
