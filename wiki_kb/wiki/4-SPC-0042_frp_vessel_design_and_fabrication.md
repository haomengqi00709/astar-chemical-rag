# FRP Vessel Design and Fabrication (4-SPC-0042)

This section outlines the [[equipment_design_criteria]] and fabrication requirements for FRP vessels, primarily referencing [[ASME_RTP-1_standard_for_reinforced_thermoset_plastic_corrosion_resistant_equipment]] with specific modifications and additions.

**Design Principles**:
*   Design **shall** substantially be in accordance with RTP-1 Part 3 - Design.
*   Design **shall** substantially be in accordance with RTP-1 Non-mandatory Appendices NM-2, NM-3, NM-4, NM-5, NM-12, and NM-13.
*   All calculations for references to wall thickness **shall apply** to the structural layer of the laminate only.
*   The minimum average structural wall thickness of any pressure containing part **shall be** 7mm (9/32”).

**Fabrication Standards**:
*   Fabrication **shall** substantially be in accordance with RTP-1 Part 4 - Fabrication, regardless of vessel size or if Subpart 3B design rules are employed.
*   For tanks, unless otherwise indicated, the bottom head FRP **shall** be in accordance with only Fig. 4-3 (c) or (d) of RTP-1.
*   The number of FRP shell joints **shall be kept** to the absolute minimum practicable for any vessel supplied to this Specification.

**Nozzle and Manway Requirements**:
*   Flanged nozzles **shall have** FRP with integral construction only (as in RTP-1 Fig. 4-7 (b)).
*   Nozzle flange, integral hub, and neck FRP structural layers **shall be constructed** from Type II laminate only.
*   Nozzle attachment and reinforcing pad FRP structural layers **shall be made** from Type II laminate only.
*   Nozzles less than 50mm (2”) dia. **shall have** the same FRP flange and neck structural thickness as for 50mm (2”) dia. flanges.
*   Nozzle gussets **shall be** in accordance with RTP-1 Fig. 4-11 (a), right side sketch only.
*   Nozzles 80mm (3”) dia. and smaller **shall be supported** with four equally spaced gussets.
*   Manway requirements **shall be** the same as for nozzles.

**Tolerances and External Components**:
*   [[fabrication_tolerances]] **shall be** in accordance with RTP-1 Figs. 4-1, 4-5, 4-12, and NM7-1.
*   External pipe support, [[lifting_lugs_design_and_calculations]] or hold-down lugs, and embedded external components **shall be** embedded 300 series stainless steel or solid FRP.
*   Lifting lugs **shall be** the fabricator’s standard design.
*   Each lifting lug **must be capable** of lifting the empty vessel plus internals on its own.
*   Lift lug calculations and details **are to be submitted** for approval.
*   Refer to [[equipment_support_criteria]] for general support requirements.

**Nameplates**
The fabricator will engrave and affix an Aker Kvaerner Chemetics nameplate to each vessel. The nameplate itself will be supplied by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Post Cure**
Post cure requirements are specified in the relevant specifications, drawings, or data sheets.

**Construction Selection**
Unless otherwise specified, the fabricator will select either standard contact moulded or non-standard construction for each vessel.

**Lamination Analysis**
*   Non-standard or Type X laminates, including those with filament, require lamination analyses.
*   The use of commercial software, such as Superlam by Fiberglass Canada Ltd., is permitted for determining laminate properties through Lamination Analysis.
*   Lamination analysis calculations must be submitted for review.

**Design Principles**
*   Design will substantially be in accordance with RTP-1 Non mandatory Appendices NM-2, NM-3, NM-4, NM-5, NM-12, and NM-13.
*   All calculations for references to wall thickness will apply exclusively to the structural layer of the laminate.
*   The minimum average structural wall thickness of any pressure containing part is 7mm (9/32”).

**General Fabrication**
*   Fabrication will substantially be in accordance with RTP-1 Part 4 - Fabrication, regardless of vessel size or if Subpart 3B design rules are employed.
*   For tanks, unless otherwise indicated, the bottom head FRP will be in accordance with only Fig. 4-3 (c) or (d) of RTP-1.
*   The number of FRP shell joints will be kept to the absolute minimum practicable for any vessel supplied to this Specification.

**Nozzle and Manway Design**
*   Flanged nozzles will feature FRP with integral construction only, as depicted in RTP-1 Fig. 4-7 (b).
*   Nozzle flange, integral hub, and neck FRP structural layers will be constructed from Type II laminate only.
*   Nozzle attachment and reinforcing pad FRP structural layers will also be made from Type II laminate only.
*   Nozzles less than 50mm (2”) diameter will have the same FRP flange and neck structural thickness as for 50mm (2”) diameter flanges.
*   Nozzle gussets will be in accordance with RTP-1 Fig. 4-11 (a), specifically the right side sketch only.
*   Nozzles 80mm (3”) diameter and smaller will be supported with four equally spaced gussets.
*   Manway requirements will be the same as those specified for nozzles.

**Fabrication Tolerances**
Fabrication tolerances will be in accordance with RTP-1 Figs. 4-1, 4-5, 4-12, and NM7-1.

**External Components**
External pipe support, lifting or hold down lugs, and embedded external components will be either embedded 300 series stainless steel or solid FRP.

**Lifting Lugs**
*   Lifting lugs will conform to the fabricator’s standard design.
*   Each lifting lug must be capable of lifting the empty vessel plus its internals independently.
*   Lift lug calculations and details are required to be submitted for approval.
