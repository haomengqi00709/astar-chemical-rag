# Purchase Order Compensation Events

This page defines what constitutes a [[0-SPC-5002_compensation_events|compensation event]] under the Purchase Order (PO), the notification requirements, and the actions taken in response.

**Events Considered Compensation Events:**
A compensation event occurs if:
*   The [[purchase_order_roles_and_responsibilities#Project Buyer|Project Buyer]] gives an instruction changing the [[purchase_order_definitions#Goods and Services Information|Goods and Services Information]] (excluding specific exclusions).
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics fails to provide something it is required to provide by the date specified in an [[0-SPC-5002_schedule_management#Accepted Schedule|Accepted Schedule]].
*   The Project Buyer gives an instruction to stop or not to start any work.
*   The Project Buyer, [[purchase_order_roles_and_responsibilities#Inspector|Inspector]], or [[purchase_order_roles_and_responsibilities#Lead Engineer|Lead Engineer]] fails to reply to a communication from the Vendor within the period required by this Purchase Order.
*   The Project Buyer, Inspector, or Lead Engineer changes a decision previously communicated to the Vendor.
*   The Project Buyer withholds an acceptance (other than for an acceleration quotation or for not correcting a [[defect_management_process#Definition of a Defect|Defect]]) for a reason not stated in this Purchase Order.
*   The Inspector instructs the Vendor to search for a Defect and none is found (unless the search is solely due to the Vendor's insufficient notice of work obstructing a required test or inspection).
*   A test or inspection conducted by the Inspector causes unnecessary delay.
*   An Aker Kvaerner Chemetics risk event occurs.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics fails to provide materials, facilities, and samples for tests as stated in the Goods and Services Information.
*   A change in the [[purchase_order_definitions#The Applicable Law|Applicable Law]] occurs after the [[purchase_order_definitions#The Starting Date|Starting Date]] that could not have been reasonably anticipated by the Vendor.
*   A breach of the Purchase Order by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics occurs that is not otherwise defined as a compensation event in this Purchase Order.

**Events Not Considered Compensation Events:**
An event is **not** a compensation event if:
*   A change is made to accept a Defect.
*   A change is a reasonably anticipated development in the [[purchase_order_definitions#The Project|Project]] design.
*   A change to the Goods and Services Information provided by the Vendor for their design is made at their request or to comply with other Goods and Services Information provided by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Vendor Notification Requirements:**
*   The Vendor **must** notify the Project Buyer if they believe an event has occurred or is expected to occur that may increase the cost of [[purchase_order_definitions#To Provide the Goods and Services|Providing the Goods and Services]] or delay [[purchase_order_definitions#Delivery or Deliver|Delivery]].
*   This notification **must** be given within **two weeks** of the Vendor becoming aware of the event.
*   The notification **must** be given before the Vendor has performed any work in response to the potential compensation event.

**Invalid Compensation Event Notifications:**
An event notified by the Vendor is **not** considered a compensation event if:
*   It arises from a fault of the Vendor.
*   It has not happened and is not expected to happen.
*   It is notified more than two weeks after the Vendor became aware of it.
*   It is notified after the Vendor has already performed work in response to it.
*   It has no effect on the cost of Providing the Goods and Services or the Delivery Date.
*   It is not one of the compensation events explicitly stated in this contract.

**Project Buyer Actions in Response to Compensation Events:**
The Project Buyer may:
*   Instruct the Vendor to submit quotations for the event.
*   Accept a quotation for a change.
*   Notify the Vendor of the Project Buyer’s assessment of the change.
*   Instruct the Vendor to proceed with the work, maintaining a separate record of direct costs incurred due to the event.

**Purchase Order Revisions:**
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **must** issue a revision to the Purchase Order with changes to the Delivery Date and the [[purchase_order_definitions#The Prices|Prices]] as soon as practicable.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **must** accept a revised schedule if there is an Accepted Schedule.
