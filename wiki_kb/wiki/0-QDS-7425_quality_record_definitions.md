# Fan or Blower Quality Record Definitions

For Quality Data Sheet **0-QDS-7425**, the following definitions apply to Quality Record types:

*   **AP (As Prepared):** This designation means the document or record is required "As Prepared" by the Vendor. It signifies that the record should be submitted as it is generated during the manufacturing or inspection process.
*   **FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package):** This designation means the document or record is required to be "Included in the Quality Assurance Documentation Package." It indicates that the record forms part of the final compilation of quality documentation submitted by the Vendor.

These definitions guide the submission and retention of quality-related documentation for the Fan or Blower equipment. Refer to the specific inspection and documentation requirements within this [[0-QDS-7XXX_template_quality_data_sheet|Quality Data Sheet]] for where these record types apply.
