# Business Travel Authorization Approvals

The process for authorizing business travel requests involves a clear hierarchy to ensure accountability and adherence to budget and policy. The traveller **must** obtain the necessary approvals before any travel bookings can be made.

**Standard Authorizations:**
The traveller is solely responsible for obtaining the following mandatory authorizations for their [[travel_authorization_form_details_0_frm_9002]]:
*   **Direct Supervisor (Department Manager):** Authorization from the traveller’s direct supervisor is **mandatory**.
*   **Budget Manager:** Authorization from the individual responsible for the budget being charged for the travel is **mandatory**.

**Additional Authorization for Deviations:**
If a deviation from the 11.1 PR 2 Group Travel Procedure is requested (as detailed in [[travel_policy_compliance_and_deviations]]), the traveller **must** obtain an additional level of authorization:
*   **Senior Line Manager (Director or Equivalent):** The traveller’s senior line manager (Director or equivalent) **must** provide authorization for any requested deviations.

These authorization steps ensure that all travel is approved at the appropriate management and financial levels, particularly when special circumstances or policy deviations are involved. Once all required authorizations are secured, the approved form can proceed to [[travel_request_form_submission_and_distribution]].

**Standard Authorizations:**
The traveller is solely responsible for obtaining the following standard authorizations:
*   Authorisation from the traveller’s direct supervisor (Dept. Manager).
*   Authorisation from the person responsible for the budget being charged (Budget Manager).

**Authorizations for Deviations:**
If a deviation from the 11.1 PR 2 Group Travel Procedure is requested, the traveller **must** obtain additional authorisation from the traveller’s senior line manager (Director or equivalent).
