# Confined Space Entry: Training and Documentation

Comprehensive training and meticulous documentation are fundamental to the safe execution of confined space entry operations. This ensures that all personnel are competent, aware of hazards, and that a clear record of compliance and incidents is maintained.

**Training Requirements:**
*   Each employee who is authorized to perform confined space entry **shall** be thoroughly trained in all aspects of these guidelines.
*   Everyone **must** go through Confined Space Entry Training provided by the Commissioning HSE Manager, either on Site or in Chemetics office.
*   The Entry Supervisor **must** ensure that all personnel are confined space trained and trained in the use of retrieval equipment and evacuation and rescue procedures.
*   Authorized entrants **must** understand company policy, procedures, and the nature of the hazards of entry into confined spaces.
*   Attendants **must** understand company policy, procedures, and the nature of the hazards of entry into confined spaces.
*   A safety observer or standby man **should** be well-trained in basic First Aid principles such as rescue breathing techniques.
*   Contact the Chemetics HSE Manager for training video information.
*   Refer to [[0-PRC-8016_hse_training_program_overview]] for general HSE training program details.

**Documentation and Records:**
*   Training **shall** be documented and entered into the Chemetics log book that the Commissioning HSE Manager keeps.
*   The supervisor **must** document the training provided to personnel entering the vessel, including a review of project details and product hazards.
*   All Confined Space Entry incidents and near misses **shall** be thoroughly investigated and documented in accordance with [[0-PRC-8013_hse_incident_reporting]].
*   Before entering a confined space, a written [[0-REP-8007b_confined_space_hazard_assessment_form|Confined Space Hazard Assessment form]] **must** be prepared by the Commissioning HSE Manager.
*   A vessel Entry Permit (form [[DP-802.705-1_confined_space_entry_permit]]) **shall** be issued before entry. This permit certifies and records approval for vessel entry and serves as a checklist for hazard consideration and protective measures.
*   The entry protocol **shall** include written safe work procedures for entry into and work in the confined space.
*   Refer to procedure [[0-PRC-8504-RA_lockout_and_tagging]] for specific lockout and tagging documentation.

Refer to [[0-prc-8502-r1_confined_space_entry]] for the overall procedure.
