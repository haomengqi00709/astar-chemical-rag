# Form of Engineering Subcontract (0-PRC-2202)

0-PRC-2202, titled "Form of Engineering Subcontract," serves as the definitive template and procedural guide for structuring all Engineering Subcontract Agreements within the firm. This document dictates the standardized format, clauses, and essential components that **must** be included in every Engineering Subcontract.

The purpose of 0-PRC-2202 is to ensure consistency, legal compliance, and clarity across all subcontractual engagements. By providing a uniform framework, it streamlines the preparation process for the [[engineering_subcontract_agreement_lifecycle]] and minimizes potential ambiguities or disputes. All Engineering Subcontract Agreements are prepared and revised in strict adherence to the specifications outlined in this procedure, ensuring that they align with the firm's contractual standards and legal requirements.
