# Protective Coating Surface Preparation (7-SPC-0001)

Effective surface preparation is critical for the longevity and performance of protective coatings. This section outlines the mandatory requirements for preparing surfaces prior to paint application, as defined in `7-SPC-0001 Protective Coating Specification`.

**Compressed Air and Abrasive Medium Requirements**
All compressed air utilized for blasting and spray application **must** be completely free of moisture and oil. Suitable air filters and traps **must** be installed on all blasting pots and spray equipment to ensure complete removal of oil and water.

The abrasive medium used for blasting **must** be free of grease. To verify this, samples from each lot of abrasive medium **must** be immersed in clean water. The presence of oil on the water surface is cause for rejection of that entire lot of blasting medium.

**Surface Cleaning and Profile**
Where necessary, all fins at saw cuts and burrs **must** be removed, and sharp edges **must** be ground smooth. Unless otherwise specified, surfaces designated for painting **must** be blast cleaned to NACE No. 3 / SSPC SP-6 "Commercial Blast Clean" standards. If surfaces are contaminated with grease and/or oils, articles **must** first be solvent cleaned in accordance with SSPC SP-1 "Solvent Cleaning".

**Galvanized Surfaces**
Galvanized surfaces that are to be painted **must** be pre-treated with an etchbond primer. The maximum Dry Film Thickness (DFT) for this etchbond primer is 12m (0.5 mil) above the surface profile.

**Surface Preparation Verification**
The Vendor **must** use the SSPC-VIS1 standard to ensure that all surface preparation requirements are satisfied.

**Post-Erection Preparation for Damaged/Unprimed Areas**
After erection or installation, but prior to the application of top-coating, previously primed items **must** be thoroughly examined for any damage or unprimed areas.
*   All oil and grease **must** first be removed in accordance with SSPC SP-1.
*   Following solvent cleaning, all loose dirt, chalk, and other contaminants **must** be removed by high-pressure water wash or other approved techniques.
*   Un-primed and damaged areas **must** be power tool cleaned in accordance with SSPC SP-11.
*   These un-primed and damaged areas **must** be re-coated prior to the application of finish coat(s).
