# Third Party Inspection Reports

Following inspection activities, the Inspection Agency **shall** issue an Inspection Report after each inspection visit. These reports are critical for documenting the findings and progress of equipment fabrication and inspection.

**Issuance and Timing**
Inspection Reports, along with Nonconformance Reports and Shop Survey reports, **shall** be issued within two days of the completion of the activity.

**Content Requirements**
Each Inspection Report **must** detail the Inspector’s assessment of current delivery and inspection findings. Specifically, reports **must** include:
*   The Inspector’s assessment of fabrication status.
*   Whether the fabrication status is in agreement with the current schedule.
*   Details of any delays encountered.
*   Any delays claimed by the vendor to be due to slow approvals by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Mandatory References**
All Inspection Reports **must** clearly reference the following information:
*   The Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Purchase Order (PO) Number on the Agency.
*   The Aker Kvaerner Chemetics PO Number on the Equipment Vendor.
*   The Name of the contact person at the Equipment Vendor.
*   The Date of the visit.
*   Equipment Description(s).
*   Equipment Tag Number(s).

**Format and Submission**
*   Inspection Reports **must** be presented on the Inspection Agency’s official letterhead.
*   A formal inspection report in the English language **shall** be issued.
*   Reports will utilize the Agency’s normal style or format.
*   Accepted digital formats for Inspection Reports are: Microsoft Word (.doc), Microsoft Excel (.xls), and Adobe Acrobat (.pdf).
*   Accepted formats for digital photographs included in reports are: “.jpg”, “.gif”, or “.tif”.
*   All Inspection Reports **must** be sent to ChemeticsQA@akerkvaerner.com.

This reporting process is a key component of the [[subcontractor_quality_assurance_0_prc_7006]] procedure.

**Report Content and Detail**
Each Inspection Report **must** detail the Inspector’s assessment of current delivery and inspection findings. This includes the Inspector’s assessment of fabrication status, whether it aligns with the current schedule, and details of any delays. Any delays claimed by the vendor to be due to slow approvals by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **must** also be noted.

**Issuance and Timing**
Inspection Reports, Nonconformance Reports, and Shop Survey reports **will** be issued within two days of the activity.

**Format and Submission**
Inspection Reports **must** be:
*   On Inspection Agency letterhead.
*   Submitted in English.
*   Sent to ChemeticsQA@akerkvaerner.com.
*   Prepared using the Agency’s normal style or format.
*   Accepted formats for reports are Microsoft Word (.doc), Microsoft Excel (.xls), or Adobe Acrobat (.pdf).
*   Accepted formats for digital photographs included in reports are “.jpg”, “.gif”, or “.tif”.

**Required References**
All Inspection Reports **must** clearly reference the following:
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Purchase Order (PO) Number (both on the Agency and the Equipment Vendor).
*   Name of the contact at the Equipment Vendor.
*   Date of visit.
*   Equipment Description(s).
*   Equipment Tag Number(s).
