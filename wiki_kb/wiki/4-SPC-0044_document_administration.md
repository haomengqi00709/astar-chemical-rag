# Glass-Lined Vessel Specification (4-SPC-0044) Document Administration

This page provides administrative details regarding the 4-SPC-0044 specification, including its history, current status, and future plans.

**Document History and Status**
This document is a direct copy of the original specification ES44/F04 General Requirements for Packaging and Shipping Domestic Products. To Revision 0, the document was identified as No. 4-SPC-0004-R3.

**Compliance and Future Plans**
This document will be used in accordance with [[0-prc-0001_standard_document_management_procedure|0-PRC-0001 Approval and Use of Standards]]. It is planned to replace this document with a new document complying fully with the requirements of [[0_prc_0004_document_style_and_format|0-PRC-0004 style of Textual Documents]]. The style will be updated prior to issue as a Revision 1 standard.

**Name Change Conversion**
Name change conversion has been implemented, but no new revision number has been used, and changes have not been marked (for "To Name Change Conversion" and "To 2005 Name Change Conversion").
