# Head Protection Requirements

This page details the requirements for head protection, specifically hard hats, in various work environments, as per Document ID 0-PRC-8015.

**Mandatory Hard Hat Use**
Approved hard hats **are required** to be worn by all employees working in construction sites. Approved hard hats **are also required** in other work environments if the employee is in danger of sustaining a head injury due to ongoing work above them or other falling hazards.

**Standards and Instruction**
Protective headgear **must** meet the requirements of CSA standard Z94.1 (or ANSI equivalent). Employees **will** be instructed in the proper use of the hard hat at the site-specific [[hse_orientation_overview|health and safety orientation]].

**Unacceptable Head Protection and Practices**
*   Metal hard hats **are not** acceptable head protection.
*   Bump caps **are not** acceptable head protection.
*   It **is unacceptable** to drill holes in headwear for ventilation.
*   Cracked hard hats **will** be replaced with a new one immediately.
*   Hard hats **will not** be worn on top of everyday caps or hats.

**Proper Fit and Adjustment**
The headband **will** be adjusted for proper snugness. The harness **will** be adjusted so that there is at least 1-1/4 inches between the head and the inside shell of the hat, ensuring adequate impact absorption.

For general PPE guidelines, refer to [[0-prc-8015_personal_protective_equipment_policy]].
