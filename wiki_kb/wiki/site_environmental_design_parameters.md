# Site Environmental Design Parameters

The environmental design parameters for the Chemetics project are critical inputs for the structural, mechanical, and process design of plant facilities. These parameters, detailed in [[site_design_conditions_1_dst_0001]], ensure that all equipment and structures are designed to withstand local climatic and geographical conditions.

The following environmental conditions are specified:

*   **Design Wet Bulb Temperature (maximum):** Specified in °C. This parameter is crucial for cooling system design and humidity control.
*   **Design Wet Bulb Temperature (minimum):** Specified in °C. Important for assessing potential freezing conditions and minimum operating temperatures.
*   **Design Dry Bulb Temperature (maximum):** Specified in °C. Used for thermal expansion calculations, material selection, and HVAC design.
*   **Design Dry Bulb Temperature (minimum):** Specified in °C. Essential for insulation requirements, material toughness, and cold weather operation.
*   **Design Snowfall Accumulation:** Specified in mm. This dictates the snow load requirements for roofs and elevated structures.
*   **Rainfall (maximum 1 hour):** Specified in mm. Critical for sizing drainage systems and ensuring adequate waterproofing.
*   **Rainfall (maximum 1 day):** Specified in mm. Provides data for overall site drainage and storm water management.
*   **Barometric Pressure:** Specified in mm. Used in various process calculations, particularly for vacuum and atmospheric pressure systems.
*   **Site Elevation (above sea level):** Specified in metres. Affects atmospheric pressure, boiling points, and pump performance calculations.

These parameters are fundamental for ensuring the safety, reliability, and operational efficiency of the plant under expected environmental conditions.

The following specific environmental parameters are documented in [[site_design_conditions_1_dst_0001]]:
*   Design Wet Bulb Temperature (maximum) in °C
*   Design Wet Bulb Temperature (minimum) in °C
*   Design Dry Bulb Temperature (maximum) in °C
*   Design Dry Bulb Temperature (minimum) in °C

*   Rainfall (maximum 1 hour) in mm
*   Rainfall (maximum 1 day) in mm
*   Barometric Pressure in mm
*   Site Elevation (above sea level) in metres
*   Soil Bearing Pressure in N/m2
