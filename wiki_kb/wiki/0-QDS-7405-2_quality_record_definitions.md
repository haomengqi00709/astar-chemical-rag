# 0-QDS-7405-2 Quality Record Definitions

Within the context of this Quality Data Sheet, 0-QDS-7405-2, and the associated [[0-QDS-7405-2_inspection_and_documentation_requirements]], the following definitions apply to Quality Records:

*   **AP (As Prepared)**: This designation indicates that the specified Quality Record is required to be provided "As Prepared" by the vendor. It signifies that the document or record is generated by the vendor as part of their standard process.

*   **FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package)**: This designation mandates that the specified Quality Record **shall** be "Included in the Quality Assurance Documentation Package" submitted by the vendor. This means the record must be part of the formal documentation package delivered to Chemetics.

*   **FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package)**: This designation indicates that the specified Quality Record is required to be included in the Quality Assurance Documentation Package.
