# Piping and Instrumentation Drawings (P&IDs)

Piping and Instrumentation Drawings (P&IDs) are critical engineering documents that illustrate the interconnectedness of process equipment and instrumentation in a plant. These drawings provide a schematic representation of the process flow, showing piping, valves, equipment, and control instrumentation.

For detailed information on the development and approval process for P&IDs, refer to [[1-prc-0003_p_id_development_and_approval]]. This procedure outlines the stages of P&ID creation, review, and final authorization, ensuring that these vital documents accurately reflect the design intent and operational requirements of the facility.
