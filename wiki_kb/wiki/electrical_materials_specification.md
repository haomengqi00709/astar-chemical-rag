# Electrical Materials Specification

The Electrical Materials Specification outlines the types of materials and protective coatings required for various bulk electrical components used in a project. This ensures consistency, durability, and compliance with design standards.

**Content of the Specification**
The electrical materials specification **shall** detail:
*   **Types of metals** required for components such as cable trays, cables, conduits, and cable connectors. This includes specifying appropriate alloys or grades for different applications.
*   **Protective coatings** required for cable trays, cables, conduits, and cable connectors. These coatings are essential for corrosion resistance, insulation, and overall longevity, especially in chemical plant environments.

This specification ensures that all bulk electrical materials meet the necessary quality and performance standards for the project's operational and environmental conditions.
