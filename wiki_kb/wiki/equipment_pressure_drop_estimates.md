# Equipment Pressure Drop Estimates

When detailed equipment data is not yet available, estimated pressure drop values are used in preliminary hydraulic calculations. These estimates help in initial pump sizing and system design.

The following reference values for estimated pressure drop (at design flow) can be used for various equipment types:

*   **Liquid Control Valves:** 35 kPag
*   **Shell & Tube or Plate Heat Exchangers:** 70 kPag
*   **Filters:** 70 kPag to 175 kPag
*   **In-line Mixers:** 35 kPag
*   **Orifices:** 35 kPag

**Note:** Previous projects may be consulted to refine these equipment pressure drop estimates where specific data is lacking.
