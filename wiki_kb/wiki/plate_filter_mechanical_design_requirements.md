# Plate Filter Mechanical Design Requirements

This page details the mechanical design requirements for the Plate Filter Package, as specified in Document ID 4-PKG-XXXX-R1. All aspects of the mechanical design must adhere to these requirements to ensure structural integrity, operational safety, and compliance with project standards.

**General Mechanical Design**
*   The Mechanical Design must adhere to Requirement Specification 4-SPC-1068/0001.
*   **Equipment Location:** This is a mandatory Mechanical Design Requirement that must be specified.
*   **Dimensional Limit:** This is a mandatory Mechanical Design Requirement that must be adhered to.

**Site Conditions and Utilities**
*   **Site Conditions and Utilities:** This is a mandatory Mechanical Design Requirement, encompassing various environmental and utility considerations.
*   **Electrical Area Classification:** This is a mandatory Site Condition and Utility requirement, which must be clearly defined for safety and compliance.

These requirements are integral to the [[general_mechanical_design_requirements_0_spc_0010]] and align with the [[equipment_design_criteria_4_prc_0002]]. Specific site conditions are further detailed in [[site_design_conditions_1_dst_0001]], and electrical area classifications must comply with [[hazardous_area_classification_requirements]].
