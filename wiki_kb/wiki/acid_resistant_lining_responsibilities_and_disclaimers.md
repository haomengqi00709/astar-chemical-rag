# Responsibilities and Disclaimers for Acid Resistant Lining Standard

This Engineering Standard focuses solely on minimum technical requirements for acid resistant membrane and brick lining systems.

**Standard Scope Limitation:**
*   This Standard **does not** define any minimum safety requirements for the lining installation.

**Installer Responsibilities:**
*   The installer is completely responsible for safety during the lining installation.
*   The installer **will** be responsible for the quality of installation/workmanship.
*   The installer **will** be responsible for compliance with all design documentation.
*   The lining installer is responsible for ensuring that the exterior of the vessels are clearly marked "Lined Vessel Do Not Weld".

**Supplier Obligations:**
*   Review of drawings or acceptance through inspections **does not** relieve the supplier(s) from any obligation to meet the requirements of this Standard.
