# Progressive Cavity Pump Ancillary Equipment

The Progressive Cavity Pump Datasheet (Template `4-DST-XXXX-R0`) lists various ancillary equipment components that are either supplied with the pump or are required for its complete and safe operation.

**Ancillary Equipment List:**
*   **Coupling:** Connects the pump shaft to the drive system.
*   **Coupling Guard:** A safety device to protect personnel from rotating coupling parts.
*   **Base plate:** The structural support on which the pump and motor are mounted.
*   **Bearing:** Components that support rotating shafts and minimize friction.
*   **Lubrication:** The system or method for lubricating bearings and other moving parts.
*   **Inlet Hopper:** A funnel-shaped container at the pump's suction for feeding viscous or solid-laden fluids.
*   **Dry Running protection:** A system or feature designed to prevent damage to the pump if it operates without fluid.
*   **Overpressure Protection:** A device or system to protect the pump from excessive discharge pressure.
*   **Supply by:** Indicates the party responsible for supplying each ancillary equipment item.
*   **Mount by:** Indicates the party responsible for mounting each ancillary equipment item.

These components contribute to the overall functionality, safety, and longevity of the progressive cavity pump system.

*   **Coupling Guard:** A protective cover for the coupling.
*   **Bearing:** Specifications for the pump's bearings.
*   **Lubrication:** Requirements for the lubrication system.
*   **Inlet Hopper:** If applicable, specifications for an inlet hopper.
*   **Dry Running protection:** Features or systems to prevent damage from dry running.
*   **Overpressure Protection:** Devices or systems to protect against overpressure.
*   **Supply by:** Indicates who is responsible for supplying each ancillary equipment item.
*   **Mount by:** Indicates who is responsible for mounting each ancillary equipment item.
