# Vendor Quality Assurance Requirements for 0-QDS-7440

This page outlines the mandatory quality assurance requirements for vendors supplying Converter Quartz under Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7440.

**Vendor Quality Plan:**
The Vendor **must** prepare a Quality Plan. This plan **shall** be in accordance with the requirements specified in [[0-SPC-7002_vendor_quality_assurance]].

**Vendor Quality Assurance Compliance:**
The Vendor's Quality Assurance program **must** comply with all stipulations set forth in this Quality Data Sheet (0-QDS-7440). Furthermore, the Vendor's Quality Assurance program **shall** comply with the overarching requirements detailed in [[0-SPC-7002_vendor_quality_assurance]].

These requirements ensure that all supplied materials and services meet the specified quality standards for the Converter Quartz project.
