# Document Referencing Guidelines

Consistent and accurate referencing is essential for maintaining the integrity and navigability of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' documentation. Specific guidelines *must* be followed when referencing sections within a document or external documents to ensure clarity and prevent ambiguity.

**Internal Document Referencing:**
*   When referring to another part of the same document, references *must* be made by Section number. An example of this is: "as described in Section 5." This method ensures that readers can easily locate the referenced content within the current document, promoting efficient navigation.

**External Document Referencing:**
The method for referencing external documents varies depending on whether the referencing document is a Procedure or a Specification:
*   **For Procedures:** Other documents *must* be referenced indirectly where possible. This approach integrates the reference into the narrative flow without explicitly stating the document type or number as a direct requirement. An example is: "will be sent by the Discipline Lead ([[0_prc_2001_assignment_of_project_responsibilities]]: Assignment of Project Responsibilities)." This method emphasizes the action rather than the document itself.
*   **For Specifications:** Other documents *must* be referenced directly. This method explicitly states the document number and title as a clear requirement for compliance. An example is: "as required by [[0_spc_5004_packaging_land_transport]]: Packaging Land Transport." This direct approach ensures that the required document is unequivocally identified for external parties.

**Reference Format for General Documents:**
*   When referencing general documents, the terms "Procedure" or "Specification," as well as any sheet number or revision number, *must* be omitted from the reference itself. The document number and title are sufficient for identification and maintain a clean, concise reference style.

**Inclusion of Revision Numbers for Commercial Documents:**
*   Despite the general rule to omit revision numbers, they *must* be explicitly included on Requisitions, Purchase Orders, and Subcontract Document Lists. This is a critical requirement in situations where commercial consequences could arise from subsequent revisions, ensuring that all parties are aware of the exact version of the document governing the commercial agreement. This exception safeguards against disputes arising from differing document versions.

**Internal Document References**
Within a document, reference by Section number (e.g., "as described in Section 5.").

**External Document References**
For Procedures, other documents will be referenced indirectly where possible (e.g., "will be sent by the Discipline Lead (0-PRC-2001: Assignment of Project Responsibilities)").
For Specifications, other documents will be referenced directly (e.g., "as required by 0-SPC-5004: Packaging Land Transport").
References omit "Procedure", "Specification", sheet number, or revision number.
