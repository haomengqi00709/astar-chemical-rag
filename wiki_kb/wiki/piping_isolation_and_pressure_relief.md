# Piping Isolation and Pressure Relief

**Equipment Isolation**
Plant equipment and piping **must** be configured to enable mechanical, electrical, and chemical isolation from energy sources. This ensures safe servicing, repair, removal, or replacement of equipment. Isolation **must** be provided in accordance with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics design experience for various fluid services and normal good industry practice.

**System Preparation for Maintenance**
System design **must** include provisions for drainage from the equipment prior to an initial break-in. The process of flushing or purging residual fluid from the isolated system **must** also be considered.

**Isolation Devices**
*   Any single flange valves used for isolation **must** have threaded lugs.
*   Spectacle blinds **must** be accessible from grade or a platform.
*   Blinds located in a pipeway are considered accessible.
*   Blinds weighing over 25 kgs **must** be accessible by mobile equipment; if not possible, davits or hitching points **must** be provided.
*   Spectacle blinds **must** be of the non-lugged type to minimize the effort required for swinging.

**Pressure Relief**
Pressure relief **must** be provided for lines not otherwise protected by pressure relief on equipment. The provision of pressure relief **must** be documented on the [[line_list_5_lst_0001]].

This ensures safe servicing, repair, removal, or replacement of equipment. Isolation **must** be provided in accordance with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics design experience with various fluid services and in accordance with normal good industry practice.
*   System design will provide for drainage from the equipment prior to an initial break-in.
*   The process of flushing/purging of residual fluid from the isolated system will also be considered.
*   Any single flange valves to be used for isolation will have threaded lugs.

**Blinds and Accessibility**
*   Spectacle blinds will be accessible from grade or platform.
*   Blinds located in a pipeway are considered accessible.
*   Blinds that weigh over 25 kgs will be accessible by mobile equipment.
*   Where accessibility by mobile equipment is not possible for blinds over 25 kgs, provide davits or hitching points.
*   Spectacle blinds will be of the non-lugged type so as to minimise the effort required to swing the blind.

**Pressure Relief**
*   Pressure relief will be provided for lines that are not otherwise protected by pressure relief on equipment.
*   Provision of pressure relief will be documented on the line list.
