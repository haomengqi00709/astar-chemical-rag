# Employee Conduct and Ethics

This policy outlines the expected standards of conduct for all employees of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and Aker Kvaerner, ensuring alignment with corporate values and legal obligations.

**General Conduct**
Employees are expected to represent Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and Aker Kvaerner with integrity, honesty, and trustworthiness. This conduct **must** be in accordance with Aker Kvaerner Policy 1.2: Business Ethics.

**Legal and Regulatory Compliance**
Employees **shall** at all times, and in all dealings both internal and external, comply with all applicable laws and regulations of the countries in which they are performing their duties.
*   No employee **will** knowingly commit or condone an illegal act.
*   No employee **will** instruct another employee, supplier, or customer to commit or condone an illegal act.
*   Employees are expected to be sufficiently familiar with any legislation that applies to their work and to recognize situations that present potential corporate liability.
*   If in doubt regarding legislation or potential corporate liability, employees **should** ask for guidance from the appropriate Senior Manager or Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ Legal Counsel.

**Professionalism and Conscientiousness**
Employees are expected to perform their duties conscientiously and with due regard for Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ interests.

**Consequences of Non-Compliance**
Employee conduct not in compliance with this policy may lead to disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment.
