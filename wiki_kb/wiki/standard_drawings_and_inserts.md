# Standard Drawings and Inserts

Standard drawings and inserts are valuable tools for efficiency and consistency in design documentation. While they provide a baseline, they may require modification to suit specific project needs.

**Modification Policy**
Standard drawings and inserts **may** be modified as required for a project. This modification process is a collaborative effort, **requiring** input from both Design and Mechanical disciplines. However, non-essential changes to standard drawings and inserts **should not** be made to maintain standardization and reduce unnecessary rework.

**SCR Revision 1 Format Change**
A significant format change occurred in SCR (Sulfuric Acid Regeneration) Revision 1. The format transitioned from drawings inserted into Excel spreadsheets to Excel spreadsheets inserted into drawings. This change offered several benefits:

*   **Ease of Drawing Changes:** It allows for easier modifications to drawing elements, such as North arrow rotation.
*   **Aspect Ratio Preservation:** It avoids issues with drawing aspect ratio changes that could occur when working directly in Excel.
*   **Revision Clouding:** The new format facilitates the use of revision clouding, making it easier to identify changes between document versions.
