# Refractory Brick Lining Inspection and Acceptance

Inspection is a critical component of the refractory brick lining installation process, ensuring compliance with specifications and high standards of workmanship. Inspections **will** be conducted by a representative of the purchaser at several key stages.

**Inspection Stages:**
1.  **Before Insulating Fire Brick Application:** An inspection **will** be performed before the application of insulating fire brick. The purpose of this inspection is to ensure that metal surfaces have been prepared in accordance with the requirements of this specification, as detailed in [[refractory_brick_lining_surface_preparation]].
2.  **During Brick Work Installation:** Inspections **will** occur at various stages throughout the brickwork installation. These inspections are crucial to ensure that the requirements of this specification are being followed and that high standards of workmanship are being maintained during both [[insulating_fire_brick_installation]] and [[refractory_fire_brick_installation]].
3.  **After Installation is Complete:** A final inspection **will** be conducted after the entire installation is complete. This inspection primarily verifies that the cleanup process, as outlined in [[refractory_brick_lining_post_installation_and_curing]], is in accordance with this specification.

**Rejection Criteria:**
Lining installations that fail to meet the specified requirements **will** be subject to rejection. This includes, but is not limited to, installations:
*   Not in accordance with this specification.
*   Not in accordance with the lining installer’s drawings.
*   Indicating poor workmanship.
*   Indicating injurious defects.
*   Indicating improper application.
*   Indicating excessive repairs.

**Related Quality Assurance**
These inspection activities are aligned with broader [[vendor_quality_assurance]] principles and specific inspection and documentation requirements found in documents such as [[0-QDS-7431_inspection_and_documentation_requirements]] and [[0-QDS-7431_key_items_and_activities]].

**Post-Inspection Curing**
Following the successful completion of all inspections, the lining **must** be cured as per the vendor's instructions. Refer to [[refractory_brick_lining_post_installation_and_curing]] for details on the curing process.
