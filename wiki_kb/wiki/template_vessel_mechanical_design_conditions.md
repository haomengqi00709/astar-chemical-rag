# Template Vessel Mechanical Design Conditions

This section of the Template Vessel Datasheet (Document ID: [[template_vessel_datasheet_administration|4-DST-XXXX-R0]]) outlines the critical mechanical design parameters for the vessel. These conditions ensure the structural integrity, safety, and compliance with relevant engineering standards throughout the vessel's operational life.

**Mechanical Design Parameters:**
The "MECHANICAL DESIGN CONDITIONS" section includes the following specifications:
*   **Design Internal Pressure:** The maximum internal pressure the vessel is designed to withstand, specified in kilopascals gauge (kPa(g)).
*   **Design Vacuum:** The maximum external pressure (vacuum) the vessel is designed to withstand, specified in kilopascals gauge (kPa(g)).
*   **Design Maximum Temperature:** The highest temperature the vessel materials are designed to safely operate at, specified in degrees Celsius (°C).
*   **Design Minimum Temperature:** The lowest temperature the vessel materials are designed to safely operate at, specified in degrees Celsius (°C).
*   **Corrosion Allowance:** The additional thickness added to the vessel walls to account for material loss due to corrosion over its design life, specified in millimeters (mm). Refer to [[corrosion_allowance]] for more details.
*   **Overpressure Protection:** Specifies the method and requirements for protecting the vessel from [[overpressure_protection]] conditions.
*   **Vacuum Protection:** Specifies the method and requirements for protecting the vessel from [[vacuum_protection]] conditions.
*   **Registration:** Details any required regulatory registration or certification for the vessel.
*   **Design Code:** The applicable engineering code or standard used for the vessel's design (e.g., ASME, EN).
*   **Equipment Specification:** References the specific equipment specification document governing the vessel's design and fabrication.
*   **General Equipment Requirements:** Outlines any overarching requirements for the equipment.
*   **Fabrication:** Specifies requirements related to the [[fabrication]] process.
*   **Lining:** Details requirements for internal [[lining]] applications.
*   **Welding:** Specifies applicable [[welding]] procedures and standards.
*   **Site Conditions:** References the [[site_conditions_1_dst_0001]] relevant to the vessel's installation location.
*   **Wind Design Code:** The code used for [[wind_design_requirements]] calculations.
*   **Seismic Design Code:** The code used for [[seismic_design_requirements]] calculations.
*   **Electrical Area Classification:** Specifies the [[hazardous_area_classification_requirements]] for the vessel's vicinity.
*   **Equipment Location:** Provides specific details about the vessel's placement within the plant.
*   **Connection Type:** Describes the types of connections used for nozzles and other attachments.
*   **Painting:** Specifies requirements for external [[painting]] and protective coatings.

These parameters are crucial for ensuring the vessel's long-term reliability and safety under various operating and environmental conditions.
