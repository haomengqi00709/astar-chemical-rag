# Request for Quotation 0-REQ-5 Vendor Supply Requirements

This page details the mandatory requirements for vendors responding to Document ID 0-REQ-5, a Request for Quotation. These requirements pertain to the supply of goods and associated documentation.

**Mandatory Requirements:**

*   The Vendor **shall** supply goods in accordance with specified documents.
*   The Vendor **shall** supply documentation in accordance with specified documents.

**Note on Referenced Documents:**
As of the current revision of 0-REQ-5, no specific documents are explicitly listed for the supply of goods or for the supply of documentation. Vendors are advised to refer to general company standards and procedures for [[vendor_document_requirements]] and [[vendor_quality_assurance]].

Further information on vendor responsibilities in the procurement process can be found in [[request_for_quotation_and_purchase_order_responsibilities]] and [[procurement_and_vendor_documentation]].

*   The Vendor **shall** supply goods in accordance with specified documents.
*   The Vendor **shall** supply documentation in accordance with specified documents.
*   No specific documents are listed for the supply of goods.
*   No specific documents are listed for the supply of documentation.
