# Inter Reheat Exchanger Function and Flow

The inter reheat exchanger serves a critical dual purpose in the acid plant process: it cools the gas stream exiting bed 2 of the converter and provides additional heat to the gas that has passed through the [[cold_reheat_exchanger_function_and_flow|cold reheat exchanger]] before it enters bed 4 of the converter.

**Shell Side Flow:**
*   Gas leaving the shell side of the [[cold_reheat_exchanger_function_and_flow|cold reheat exchanger]] enters the shell side of the inter reheat exchanger.
*   Within the exchanger, the inter reheat exchanger shell side gas flows downwards, receiving additional heat.
*   This reheated gas is then discharged into bed 4 of the converter.

**Tube Side Flow:**
*   Gas from bed 2 of the converter enters the tube side of the inter reheat exchanger.
*   The inter reheat exchanger tube side gas flows upwards, undergoing a cooling process.
*   This cooled gas is subsequently discharged into bed 3 of the converter.
