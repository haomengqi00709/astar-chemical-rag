# Duplex Stainless Steel Welding Procedures and Consumables (4-SPC-0027)

Adherence to specific welding procedures and the correct selection of consumables are paramount for achieving the desired metallurgical properties and performance of duplex stainless steel (Alloy 2205) fabrications. Specification 4-SPC-0027 outlines the following mandatory requirements:

*   **Welding Procedure Qualification:** All welding procedures intended for use on the equipment **must** be submitted by the fabricator to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ Materials and Quality Assurance Group for thorough review and agreement. This submission **must** occur prior to the commencement of any welding work. Each submission **must** include both the Procedure Qualification Record (PQR) and the Welding Procedure Specification (WPS), demonstrating compliance with standards such as [[ASME_section_ix_welding_and_brazing_qualifications]].
*   **Autogenous Welds:** Autogenous welds (welds made without filler metal) are strictly not allowed for duplex stainless steel fabrications under this specification.
*   **Flux Coatings and Fluxes:** For Shielded Metal Arc Welding (SMAW) and Submerged Arc Welding (SAW) processes, only “Basic” type flux coatings and fluxes **must** be used.
*   **Welding Consumables and Filler Metals:**
    *   **Gas Tungsten Arc Welding (GTAW):** Alloy 2209 filler metal **must** be used. Refer to [[ASME_sfa_5_12_tungsten_arc_welding_electrodes]] for relevant standards.
    *   **Shielded Metal Arc Welding (SMAW):** Alloy 2209 Basic Flux type electrodes **must** be used. Refer to [[ASME_sfa_5_4_welding_electrodes]] for relevant standards.
    *   **Submerged Arc Welding (SAW):** Alloy 2209 Basic Flux Type filler metal **must** be used.
    *   **Flux Cored Arc Welding (FCAW):** Alloy 2209 filler metal **must** be used, but FCAW is restricted to non-process contact welds only. FCAW will not be used for any pressure-retaining weld. All FCAW welds **must** be clearly indicated on the fabricator’s drawings.
    *   **Mill Test Certificates:** Mill test certificates for all welding consumables and filler metals **must** be made available to the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics inspector for verification. Relevant standards for nickel and nickel alloy electrodes and rods include [[ASME_sfa_5_11_nickel_and_nickel_alloy_electrodes]], [[ASME_sfa_5_14_nickel_and_nickel_alloy_electrodes_and_rods]], and [[ASME_sfa_5_9_welding_electrodes_and_rods]].
*   **Dissimilar Metal Welding:** For welding Alloy 2205 to carbon steel, and for welding Alloy 2205 to 300 series stainless steel, 309MOL filler material **must** be used.
*   **SAW Flux Recommendation:** If the SAW process is employed, the basic flux **must** be specifically recommended by both the alloy and consumable manufacturers to ensure optimum corrosion resistance. Technical details of the proposed SAW basic flux **must** be provided to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for review and approval.
*   **Welding Technique:** Straight stringer beads **must** be used for all welds.
*   **Interpass Temperature:** The maximum interpass temperature during welding is 150°C. Strict control of interpass temperature is crucial for maintaining the desired duplex microstructure.
*   **Heat Input:** Fabricators **must** use heat inputs in the range of 20-24 kJ/cm as a guiding principle. This range is subject to approval by the alloy and welding consumable supplier. The fabricator **must** also tailor the heat input according to the material thickness; generally, higher heat inputs are more suitable for thicker sections, while lower heat inputs are preferred for thinner sections. Weld heat input is critical to achieving the correct weld metal ferrite/austenite phase balance. Duplex stainless steel alloys typically require more heat for an acceptable weld than other stainless alloys.
*   **Preheat:** Preheat is generally not required. However, work may be heated prior to welding only to a sufficient degree to drive off any moisture.

These detailed requirements ensure that welding operations are performed under controlled conditions, leading to high-quality, corrosion-resistant, and mechanically sound duplex stainless steel fabrications. For general welding terms and definitions, refer to [[AWS_a3_0_standard_welding_terms_and_definitions]].
