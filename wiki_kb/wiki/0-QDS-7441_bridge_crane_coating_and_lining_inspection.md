# Bridge Crane Coating and Lining Inspection

This page outlines the requirements for coating and lining inspection for the Bridge Crane project, as specified in Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7441. These inspections are critical to ensure the protective coatings and linings meet specifications for durability and corrosion resistance.

**External Coating / Lining Inspection:**
External Coating / Lining Inspection is required as "FP" ([[quality_record_definitions|Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package]]). This inspection **must** verify the proper application, thickness, adhesion, and overall integrity of all external coatings and linings.

**Chemetics Surveillance:**
Chemetics surveillance for External Coating / Lining Inspection requires "Witness." A Chemetics representative **shall** be present during these inspections to confirm compliance with project specifications and quality standards.

**Coating / Lining Inspection Activity:**
Coating / Lining Inspection is identified as a key item or activity within the overall quality assurance framework for the Bridge Crane. This activity ensures that protective layers are applied correctly to prevent degradation and extend the lifespan of the equipment.

For an overview of the vendor's general quality assurance obligations, refer to [[0-QDS-7441_bridge_crane_vendor_quality_assurance_requirements]].
