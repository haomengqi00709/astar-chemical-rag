# Engineering Subcontract Intellectual Property and Confidentiality (0-SPC-2205)

This page outlines the intellectual property rights, confidentiality obligations, and indemnification clauses under document 0-SPC-2205. These provisions ensure proper use of materials and protection of sensitive information between Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Subcontractor. For general guidelines on intellectual property and confidentiality in subcontracts, refer to [[subcontract_intellectual_property_and_confidentiality_0_spc_2201]].

**Use of Materials:**
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics has the right to use material provided by the Subcontractor for the purpose stated in the [[0-SPC-2205_general_conditions_and_administration#Key Definitions|Scope]].
*   The Subcontractor **must** obtain equivalent rights from any [[0-SPC-2205_general_conditions_and_administration#Key Definitions|Subsubcontractor]] for Aker Kvaerner Chemetics to use material prepared by that Subsubcontractor.
*   The Subcontractor has the right to use material provided by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics solely for the purpose of Providing the Services.
*   The Subcontractor may make the right to use Aker Kvaerner Chemetics-provided material available to a [[0-SPC-2205_general_conditions_and_administration#Key Definitions|Subsubcontractor]].
*   Upon [[0-SPC-2205_general_conditions_and_administration#Key Definitions|Completion]] of the entire services, the Subcontractor **must** return all material provided by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics to them.

**Confidentiality and Publicity:**
*   The Parties **must not** disclose information obtained in connection with the services, except when such disclosure is necessary to fulfill their duties under this Subcontract.
*   The Subcontractor **may** publicize the services only with the prior written agreement of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Indemnification for Infringement:**
*   The Subcontractor indemnifies Aker Kvaerner Chemetics against any claims, proceedings, compensation, and costs payable arising from an infringement by the Subcontractor of the rights of [[0-SPC-2205_general_conditions_and_administration#Key Definitions|Others]].
*   This indemnity does not apply to an infringement that arose from the Subcontractor's use of materials or things provided by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
