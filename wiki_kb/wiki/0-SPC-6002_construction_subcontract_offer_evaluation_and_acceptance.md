# Construction Subcontract Offer Evaluation and Acceptance (0-SPC-6002)

The evaluation and acceptance of offers for construction subcontracts **will** be conducted with strict adherence to the principles outlined in this section, ensuring a fair yet discretionary selection process.

**Offer Evaluation Process**
Offers **will** be evaluated in the sole discretion of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. The primary [[construction_subcontractor_evaluation_and_approval_0_prc_6003|evaluation criteria]] include, but are not limited to, overall performance capabilities, proposed delivery schedules, and the value offered. Furthermore, the evaluation **will** thoroughly consider any explicitly stated deviations and exceptions included in the offer, assessing them in the manner described by these instructions. Proposers **should** ensure that any such deviations are clearly articulated as per [[0-SPC-6002_construction_subcontract_offer_documentation_requirements|offer documentation requirements]]. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **will** also directly contact the references provided by the Proposer as part of the evaluation process.

**Rights of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics**
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics [[offer_evaluation_and_acceptance_rights|reserves the right]] to:
*   Accept or reject any offer, in whole or in part.
*   Reject all offers, with or without prior notice or stated reasons.
*   Accept an offer other than that having the lowest price, prioritizing overall value and suitability.
*   Accept an offer that is not in full compliance with the Request for Tender, if deemed appropriate.

In situations where no offer is deemed acceptable, the work **may** be abandoned entirely, or it **may** be performed in any alternative manner selected by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Proposer Status and Notification**
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics explicitly makes no representation that any Proposer is pre-qualified or acceptable merely because they have received a Request for Tender. Similarly, receiving a Request for Tender or responding to it does not imply pre-qualification or acceptance. This clarifies that participation in the tender process does not guarantee any specific status.

Unsuccessful Proposers **will** be advised within ten working days following the award of a Subcontract for the works to another party. This notification **will** confirm the conclusion of the tender process for that specific engagement.
