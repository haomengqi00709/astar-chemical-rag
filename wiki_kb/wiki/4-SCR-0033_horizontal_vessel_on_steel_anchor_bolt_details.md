# Horizontal Vessel on Steel: Anchor Bolt Details (4-SCR-0033)

This page provides detailed specifications for the anchor bolts used to secure horizontal vessels to their steel support structures, as outlined in document **4-SCR-0033**. Proper selection and installation of anchor bolts are paramount for the stability and safety of the vessel, especially under extreme loading conditions such as wind and seismic events.

**Anchor Bolt Specifications:**
The following dimensions and quantities are specified for the anchor bolts:
*   **Anchor Bolt 'AB' Diameter:** The diameter of each anchor bolt is specified in millimeters (mm). This dimension is crucial for determining the bolt's tensile and shear strength.
*   **Number of Bolts:** A total of **4 ea.** (four each) anchor bolts are required for securing the horizontal vessel. This quantity is determined based on the anticipated loads and the structural design requirements.
*   **Dimension 'E' - Bolt Position:** This dimension specifies the position of the anchor bolts, measured in millimeters (mm). Dimension 'E' typically refers to a critical spacing or offset that ensures proper load distribution and structural stability.
*   **Dimension 'F' - Bolt Position:** Similar to Dimension 'E', Dimension 'F' also specifies a bolt position, measured in millimeters (mm). Together, 'E' and 'F' define the precise layout and spacing of the anchor bolts on the foundation or support structure.
*   **Dimension 'H' - Equip. Anchor Height:** This dimension indicates the height of the equipment anchor point, measured in millimeters (mm). It is essential for coordinating the anchor bolt projection with the equipment's base plate.
*   **Dimension 'P' - Bolt Length:** The total length of the anchor bolt is specified in millimeters (mm). This length includes the embedded portion within the concrete or steel structure and the exposed threaded portion for securing the nut.

**Material Specifications:**
The materials for the anchor bolts and nuts are critical for their performance and durability:
*   **Anchor Bolt Material:** The anchor bolts **shall** be made from material grade **4.6**. This material designation refers to specific mechanical properties, including tensile strength and yield strength, ensuring the bolts can withstand the design loads.
*   **Nut Material:** The nuts used with the anchor bolts **shall** be made from material grade **4**. This material is selected to be compatible with the anchor bolt material, ensuring proper thread engagement and load transfer without premature failure.

Adherence to these precise specifications for dimensions and materials is a mandatory requirement for all installations to ensure the long-term safety and reliability of the horizontal vessel support system.
