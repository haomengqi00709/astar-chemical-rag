# Piping Layout and Accessibility

**Piping System Sizing**
Piping system sizing **must** conform to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics standard 5-PRC-xxxx (Piping System Sizing).

**Freeze Protection**
Outdoor process lines **must** be protected against freezing by electric heat tracing. Outdoor utility water lines 2” and smaller **must** be freeze protected. Outdoor utility water lines 3” and larger will generally not be freeze protected unless they can be stagnant during normal operation.

**Operational and Maintenance Layout**
Operational and maintenance requirements **must** be integrated into the layout and arrangement of piping. This includes considering access for cranes and vehicles, designated set-down spaces, fire-fighting access and clearances, and emergency routes. Equipment and piping requiring regular cleaning or maintenance **may** necessitate additional space and clearances. Clear overhead access **should** be provided for equipment handling, and removable spools **must** be provided where necessary for maintenance. Condensate lines and process sample points **must** be brought to grade and routed to common locations for operational and maintenance convenience. Commonly used valves and instruments **must** be easily accessible.

**Vents and Drains**
Piping **must** be designed with a minimum number of high and low points. Where these points occur, the piping system **must** be equipped with vent and drain connections of a minimum DN20 (3/4”). Utility piping vent and drain connections **must** be threaded couplings with plugs. Piping conveying corrosive or harmful process fluids **must** have vents, drains, and bleeds routed to a safe location and provided with valves and blinds. Drain valves **must** be positioned to allow rodding out through the valve if required. Drain nipples near the edge of paved areas **must** be positioned to allow draining onto the concrete slab.

**Clearances and Vibration**
Minimum clearances for piping around equipment, such as pumps, **must** be in accordance with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics standard 5-PRC-xxxx (Piping Clearances). To prevent flow-induced vibration, check valves **must** be located a minimum of 6 pipe diameters downstream of the “expander” on pump discharges. Control valve station piping layout **must** follow Aker Kvaerner Chemetics standard 5-PRC-xxxx (Control Valve Layout).

**Dead Ends**
Dead ends **must** be avoided, especially in piping where solids or fluids may congeal or form corrosive condensate.

**Underground Piping**
Underground piping below roads and concrete areas **should** be avoided. Where pipes are laid under roads or areas subject to heavy traffic, they **must** either be laid in suitable unsealed ducts, solidly encased in concrete at least 150 mm thick, or laid at a suitable depth to ensure protection. Underground pipes laid near or across buried electric power cables **must** always be laid at a level lower than the cables. Pipes carrying hot liquids **must** be kept away from cables to prevent adverse effects on plastic sheathing. Valves, meters, and similar fittings on buried lines **must** be housed in a suitable brick chamber or equivalent, with proper access from the surface.

**Utility Stations**
Utility stations **must** be provided such that a single 15 m length of hose can reach the entire working area from the station. Water outlets **must** be provided at grade level only, in pump areas, and near equipment that will be water washed during operation and maintenance. Air outlets **must** be provided in areas where air-driven tools are used (e.g., heat exchangers, air cooler platforms, compressor areas, maintenance platforms on reactors, columns, and tanks), ensuring each manway is within reach of a 15 m hose. Air outlets **must** also be provided adjacent to valves with pneumatic actuators for ease of operation.

**Piping System Sizing**
Piping system sizing **must** conform to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics standard 5-PRC-xxxx (Piping System Sizing).

**Freeze Protection**
Outdoor process lines **must** be protected against freezing by electric heat tracing. Outdoor utility water lines 2” and smaller **must** be freeze protected. Outdoor utility water lines 3” and larger will generally not be freeze protected unless they can be stagnant in normal operation.

**General Layout and Maintenance Considerations**
Operational and maintenance requirements will be incorporated into the layout and arrangement of piping.
*   Crane and vehicle access, set-down space, fire-fighting access and clearances, and emergency routes will all be considered.
*   Special consideration of equipment and piping that requires regular cleaning or maintenance may necessitate additional space and clearances.
*   Clear overhead access should be provided for equipment handling.
*   Removable spools will be provided where necessary.
*   Condensate lines and process sample points will be brought to grade and routed to common locations for operations and maintenance convenience.
*   Commonly used valves and instruments will be easily accessible.
*   Piping will be designed with a minimum number of high points and low points.
*   Where high and low points occur, the piping system will be provided with vent and drain connections of minimum DN20 (3/4”).
*   Utility piping vent and drain connections will be threaded couplings with plugs.
*   Minimum clearances for piping around equipment such as pumps will be in accordance with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics standard 5-PRC-xxxx.
*   Control valve station piping layout will be in accordance with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics standard 5-PRC-xxxx.
*   Dead ends will be avoided, especially for piping where solids or fluids may congeal or form corrosive condensate.

**Access for Operations and Maintenance**
*   Permanent access will be provided for all routine operations (i.e., at sampling points, control valving, and isolation valving).
*   Access by mobile platform is acceptable for isolated valving and piping items requiring infrequent access.
*   Instrumentation and valving will be located to permit operator visibility, maintenance, inspection, repair, and replacement.

**Underground Piping**
*   Underground piping below roads and concrete areas will be avoided.
*   Where pipes are laid under roads or areas subject to heavy traffic, these will either be laid in suitable unsealed ducts, solidly encased in concrete at least 150 mm thick, or laid at suitable depth to ensure protection of the piping.
*   Where underground pipes are laid near to or across buried electric power cables they will always be laid at a level lower than the cables.
*   Pipes carrying hot liquids will be kept away from cables, where the heat dissipated could have an adverse effect on the cable’s plastic sheathing.
*   Where valves, meters, and other similar fittings are used on buried lines, they will be housed in a suitable brick chamber or equivalent, with proper access from the surface.

**Utility Stations and Drains**
*   Provide utility stations such that a single 15 m length of hose can be used to reach the entire working area from the station.
*   Provide water outlets at grade level only, in pump areas, and near equipment that will be water washed during operation and maintenance.
*   Provide air outlets in areas where air-driven tools are used such as at heat exchangers, air cooler platforms, compressor area, maintenance platforms on reactors, on columns and on tanks, so that each manway to be serviced is within the reach of a 15 m hose.
*   Also provide air outlets adjacent to valves fitted with pneumatic actuators for ease of operation.
*   Drain valves will be so positioned as to allow rodding out of the drain through the valve if required.
*   Drain nipples near the edge of paved areas will be positioned to allow draining onto the concrete slab.
