# Subcontractor Management Plans (0-SPC-600)

This document, 0-SPC-600, specifies the general requirements for management plans to be developed by construction subcontractors for the Project. It sets the framework for how subcontractors **must** plan and control their work activities.

The Subcontractor is required to develop management plans in accordance with this specification, ensuring a structured approach to project execution, safety, environmental compliance, and quality.
