# Civil Construction and Site Preparation (0-SPC-6X92)

This page describes the requirements for Civil Construction and Site Preparation as outlined in document 0-SPC-6X92. The Subcontractor **must** adhere to these specifications for all civil works and site preparation activities related to the construction subcontract. This includes, but is not limited to, earthworks, foundations, drainage, and other site infrastructure.

Refer to [[0-SUB-6_construction_subcontract_scope_of_works]] for the specific application of this specification within the overall project scope.
