# Instrument Checkout Procedure Overview (8-PRC-0005)

The Instrument Checkout Procedure, documented as 8-PRC-0005, outlines the steps to verify that instruments are installed and functioning correctly, and are ready for subsequent stages such as water batching and commissioning. This procedure is critical for ensuring the operational integrity of the plant's instrumentation and control systems.

**Procedure Scope and Purpose**
This procedure verifies that all instruments are installed in accordance with engineering drawings and specifications, are functioning correctly, and are prepared for [[water_and_air_trials_1_prc_0006]] and commissioning.

**Roles and Responsibilities**
During the construction phase, the site subcontractor is responsible for checking the completeness of instrumentation installation. The site subcontractor shall then approach the [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_site_supervisor_review]] for a visual review of the installed instruments against the general arrangement and installation instructions.

Once the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site supervisor is satisfied, they shall notify the Project Manager. The equipment is then deemed ready for a final review by an Aker Kvaerner Chemetics specialist. This specialist conducts a visual inspection of the equipment against the engineering documents to confirm that the installation is in accordance with the documents. Each instrument item is inspected according to specific guidelines.

**Deficiency Management**
All findings from the inspections are recorded on the [[8-lst-0101_field_instrumentation_checkout]] list. All identified deficiencies shall be recorded and submitted to the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site supervisor. The site supervisor will then coordinate with a deficiency crew, supplied by the site subcontractor, to correct all deficiencies identified by the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics specialist. This crew is also responsible for correcting any outstanding punch list items from the initial subcontractor review.

It is important to note that modifications and changes identified during the checkout process are treated as design deficiencies, not as standard punch list items. These design deficiencies shall be documented and forwarded to the Project Manager for resolution.

**Prerequisites**
Before commencing instrument checkout, the following conditions **must** be met:
*   [[dcs_field_hardware_checkout_8_prc_0014]] must be fully installed and checked as per Procedure [[8-PRC-0014]].
*   Field wiring, including remote I/O subsystems, **must** be completed.
*   [[dcs_control_function_checkout_8_prc_0006]] **must** be ready to be checked out as per [[STANDARD-8-PRC-0004]].

**Completion Package**
Instrument checkout documents will form part of the instrument completion package, providing a comprehensive record of the installation and verification process.
