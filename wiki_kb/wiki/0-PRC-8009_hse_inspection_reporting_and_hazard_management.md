# HSE Inspection Reporting and Hazard Management

This page details the procedures for reporting findings from HSE inspections and managing identified hazards, as outlined in Document ID: 0-PRC-8009.

**Inspection Methodology**
The general method for inspections is to conduct a walk-about, actively looking for hazards and hazardous conditions on the items listed in the inspection check-list. The inspector **SHOULD** always remain alert to hazardous conditions not explicitly listed in the check-list and **SHOULD** amend the inspection check-list if necessary to accurately reflect current worksite conditions.

**Inspection Report**
The inspection report is the formal document that **MUST** contain a list of hazards noted during the inspection. Each report **SHALL** include:
*   The inspection date.
*   A clear description of the inspected area.
*   A comprehensive list of hazardous conditions identified.
Data available in SQL: [[0-REP-8009_worksite_hse_inspection_report]]

**Hazardous Deviation Management**
When a hazardous deviation is found, it **MUST** be noted, and the following information **SHALL** be assigned:
*   **Location of Hazard**: Specific area where the hazard was identified.
*   **Person Accountable**: Generally, the Manager with responsibility for the affected area.
*   **Risk Level of Item**: Categorized as Low, Medium, High, or Extreme.
*   **Deadline for Action**: The timeframe within which the corrective action **MUST** be completed.

**Risk Level Specific Actions and Deadlines**
*   **Extreme Risk Items**:
    *   The deadline column **SHALL** always state “Complete”.
    *   These items **MUST** be fixed immediately.
    *   The inspector **MUST** stop the inspection (and work, if necessary) and ensure the problem is corrected before proceeding.
*   **High Risk Items**:
    *   **MUST** be corrected within 8 hours.
    *   The area **MUST** be secured and clearly marked to indicate the hazard.
*   **Medium Risk Items**:
    *   **MUST** be corrected within 5 working days.
    *   The area **MUST** be secured and clearly marked to indicate the hazard.
*   **Low Risk Items**:
    *   **MUST** be corrected within 2 weeks.

**Follow-up Procedures**
Follow-up on identified hazards **SHALL** adhere to local statutory regulations if they are more stringent than those outlined here.
*   **High and Extreme Risk Items**: Follow-up **SHALL** occur as per their completion deadline.
*   **Medium and Low Risk Items**: Follow-up **SHALL** occur after 3 working weeks have passed. If the inspection frequency is less than 3 weeks, follow-up **SHALL** occur at the time of the next scheduled inspection.
