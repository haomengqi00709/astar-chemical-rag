# Impulse Tubing and Fittings

Impulse tubing and fittings are critical for connecting process taps to instruments, ensuring accurate pressure and differential pressure measurements.

**Tubing Specifications:**
*   Impulse tubing **will be** 1/2" hard drawn seamless 316 stainless steel, providing durability and corrosion resistance.
*   The maximum length of any impulse tubing **will be** 15 metres to minimize signal lag and potential for clogging.
*   All impulse lines **will be** installed with a minimum gradient of one in twelve to ensure that the entire line is self-draining, preventing accumulation of condensate or process fluid.

**Fittings and Valves:**
*   Stainless steel fittings for impulse tubing **will be** of imperial sizes; metric sizes for stainless steel fittings are not acceptable.
*   Stainless steel valves and compression fittings **will be** used unless the process fluid or the process ambient conditions dictate otherwise.
*   All stainless steel compression fittings **will be** selected from the manufacturer and range approved by the Company for the project.
*   Nylon tubing **will not** be used for impulse lines.
*   All process connections **will be** provided with an isolation cock, allowing for safe maintenance and calibration of instruments.
