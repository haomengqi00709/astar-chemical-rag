# Purchase Order Payment and Invoicing

This page details the procedures and requirements for invoicing, payment, and financial security under the Purchase Order (PO).

**Invoicing Requirements:**
*   The Vendor **must** invoice Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for payment on or before an invoice date.
*   Each invoice **must** include the documents or evidence specified in the Purchase Order.

**Assessment of Amount Due:**
*   The [[purchase_order_roles_and_responsibilities#Project Buyer|Project Buyer]] **must** assess the amount due at each invoice date, provided an invoice has been submitted.
*   The amount due is calculated as the amount stated in the Purchase Order, plus any other amounts payable to the Vendor, minus any amounts payable by or retained from the Vendor.
*   Any value-added tax or sales tax that the law requires Aker Kvaerner Chemetics to pay to the Vendor **must** be included in the amount due.
*   The Project Buyer **must** correct any wrongly assessed amount due in a subsequent assessment.

**Lien Releases and Security:**
*   The Vendor **must** provide the Project Buyer with evidence satisfactory to the Project Buyer that all amounts owing to any person related to the [[purchase_order_definitions#The Goods and Services|Goods and Services]] have been paid.
*   The Vendor **must** provide the Project Buyer with a full and complete release from any person who has filed or registered any notice of lien, security, security interest or charge (UCC or PPSA, or its equivalent in any jurisdiction), or who has provided labor or materials in relation to the Goods and Services.
*   If the Vendor fails to provide this evidence or the required releases, the Project Buyer may assess an amount to be retained from the Vendor as security against any potential claims by persons who have provided labor or materials for the Goods and Services.

**Set-off Rights:**
*   Where an amount is due from the Vendor to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may set that amount off against any payment otherwise due to the Vendor.

**Payment Terms:**
*   Each payment **must** be made within **45 days** of the date upon which Aker Kvaerner Chemetics received the invoice.
*   If a different payment period is specified in the Purchase Order, payment **must** be made within that stated period.
