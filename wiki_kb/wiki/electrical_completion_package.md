# Electrical Completion Package

The Electrical Completion Package is a comprehensive collection of documents and records that certify the successful installation, inspection, and testing of all electrical systems within a project. This package serves as a formal handover document, confirming that the electrical infrastructure meets all design specifications, regulatory requirements, and operational readiness criteria.

Key components of an Electrical Completion Package typically include:
*   Signed-off [[9-PRC-0004_electrical_field_checkout_documentation|Electrical System Check Lists]]
*   Inspection reports and deficiency logs
*   Test certificates (e.g., insulation resistance, continuity)
*   As-built drawings and schematics
*   Vendor manuals for electrical equipment
*   Calibration records for instruments
*   Safety documentation and permits

As outlined in the [[9-PRC-0004_electrical_field_checkout_procedure_overview|Electrical Field Checkout Procedure (9-PRC-0004)]], the Check Lists generated during the electrical field checkout form an integral part of this completion package, demonstrating that all electrical devices are installed correctly, functioning as intended, and ready for subsequent phases like [[commissioning_and_performance_testing]].
