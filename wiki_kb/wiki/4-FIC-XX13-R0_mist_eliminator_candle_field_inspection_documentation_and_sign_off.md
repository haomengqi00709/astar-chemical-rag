# Mist Eliminator Candle Field Inspection Documentation and Sign-off

The Field Inspection Checklist 4-FIC-XX13-R0 includes dedicated sections for documenting observations and formalizing the inspection process.

**Deficiencies and Comments:**
The document provides sections for recording Deficiencies & Comments, with space for 10 distinct items. Any non-conformances identified during the inspection **must** be documented here. For detailed procedures on handling non-conformances, refer to [[0-NCR-7XXX_template_non_conformance_report]].

**Signature Requirements:**
Formal sign-off is a mandatory step to confirm the completion and review of the inspection. Signatures are required from the following parties, including their Name and Date:
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics (refer to [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_company_details]])
*   Sub Contractor (refer to [[construction_subcontract_management_and_site_quality]])
*   Client (refer to [[client_project_progress_reporting]])

These signatures ensure accountability and serve as a formal record of the inspection's outcome. All documentation related to quality records **must** adhere to the [[quality_record_definitions]].
