# Work Schedule and Completion (0-SPC-6001)

This page outlines the requirements for the work schedule and project completion under construction subcontract 0-SPC-6001.

**Work Commencement and Completion Date**
The Subcontractor **shall not** start work until the starting date. The Subcontractor **shall** perform the work such that [[standard_conditions_for_construction_subcontract_0_spc_6001#Completion|Completion]] occurs on or before the [[standard_conditions_for_construction_subcontract_0_spc_6001#Completion Date|Completion Date]].

**Completion Forecasts and Certification**
The Subcontractor **shall** submit a forecast of the date of Completion to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics each week, starting from the starting date until Completion. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics is responsible for deciding the date of Completion and **shall** certify this date to the Subcontractor within one week of its determination.

**Work Instructions**
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may instruct the Subcontractor to stop or not to start any work. Subsequently, Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may instruct the Subcontractor to restart or start work.

**Delay Damages**
The Subcontractor **shall** pay delay damages at the rate specified in the [[standard_conditions_for_construction_subcontract_0_spc_6001#Subcontract Data|Subcontract Data]] for each day:
*   From the date a document is required until the date it is provided.
*   From the Completion Date until Completion.

**Schedule Submissions**
The Subcontractor **shall** submit schedules to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics as stated in the [[standard_conditions_for_construction_subcontract_0_spc_6001#Works Information|Works Information]].
