# Subcontractor Obligations and Design (0-SPC-6001)

This page details the Subcontractor's obligations regarding the provision of works, design, and compliance with legal and quality standards under subcontract 0-SPC-6001.

**Provision of Works**
The Subcontractor **shall** [[standard_conditions_for_construction_subcontract_0_spc_6001#To Provide the Works|Provide the Works]] in accordance with the [[standard_conditions_for_construction_subcontract_0_spc_6001#Works Information|Works Information]].

**Design Responsibilities**
The Subcontractor **shall** design the parts of the works that the Works Information specifies. The Subcontractor **shall** submit the design to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for acceptance. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may reject the design if it does not comply with the Works Information or applicable law. The Subcontractor **shall not** proceed with the relevant work until Aker Kvaerner Chemetics has accepted the design.

**Intellectual Property**
The Subcontractor indemnifies Aker Kvaerner Chemetics against claims, compensation, and costs resulting from the Subcontractor infringing a patent or copyright. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Owner may use and copy the Subcontractor’s design for any purpose related to the construction, use, alteration, or demolition of the works.

**Legal and Regulatory Compliance**
The Subcontractor **shall** comply with all requirements imposed by law in respect of Providing the Works. These requirements include:
*   Obtaining any necessary permit, authority, licence, consent, certificate, and administrative decision.
*   Giving notices or reporting to a government authority.
*   Complying with requirements under any Approvals obtained by the Owner or Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and listed in the Works Information.

The Subcontractor **shall** also comply with all relevant codes and standards for the works.

**Design Liability and Intellectual Property**
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ acceptance of a communication from the Subcontractor or of the Subcontractor’s work does not change the Subcontractor’s liability for the Subcontractor’s design.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Owner may use and copy the Subcontractor’s design for any purpose connected with construction, use, alteration or demolition of the works.

**Site Access**
The Subcontractor **shall** provide access for Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and for others notified by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics to:
*   Work being done for this subcontract.
*   Stored [[standard_conditions_for_construction_subcontract_0_spc_6001#Equipment and Materials|Equipment and Materials]].
