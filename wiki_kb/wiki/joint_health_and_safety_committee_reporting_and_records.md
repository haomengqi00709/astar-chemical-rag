# Joint Health and Safety Committee Reporting and Records

Maintaining accurate and accessible records of Joint Health and Safety Committee (JHSC) activities is essential for transparency, accountability, and continuous improvement in [[workplace_health_and_environmental_practices]].

**Meeting Reports:**
*   After each meeting, the committee **shall** prepare a report of the meeting.
*   A copy of the meeting report **shall** be provided to the [[hse_personnel_definitions_and_responsibilities|Company HSE Manager]].
*   A copy of the meeting report **shall** also be provided to all approved organizations having an interest in the work site health and safety program.
*   Meeting reports **shall** be posted at a location (or locations) that are easily accessible by employees, ensuring broad awareness of discussions and decisions.

**Record Retention:**
*   Copies of meeting reports **shall** be retained for the duration of the project for construction sites.
*   For permanent work sites, copies of meeting reports **shall** be retained for a minimum of 2 years.
*   Retained meeting reports **shall** be readily accessible to all employees and others having appropriate authorization, supporting the company's [[document_control_procedure_0_prc_0007|document control]] and [[hse_data_collection_responsibilities|data collection responsibilities]].

These reporting and record-keeping requirements ensure that JHSC activities are well-documented, communicated, and available for review, contributing to effective [[hse_management_and_employee_responsibilities|HSE management]].
