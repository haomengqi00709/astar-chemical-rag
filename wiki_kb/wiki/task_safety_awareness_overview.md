# Task Safety Awareness (TSA) Overview

Task Safety Awareness (TSA) is a fundamental pre-task review process designed to identify and mitigate risks associated with work assignments. It involves a systematic examination of tools, safety equipment, and best methods to eliminate the potential for injury or environmental damage. The TSA process is collaboratively conducted by employees performing a task, with the active involvement and support of their supervisor and/or the HSE manager.

TSA serves as the cornerstone of an ongoing awareness campaign. This campaign is specifically designed to reinforce and ensure that both employees and supervisors consistently fulfill their joint responsibility to thoroughly analyze each work assignment for potential hazards. Furthermore, it aims to ensure that all parties agree on and commit to sound safety practices before any work assignment begins.

The application of TSA is mandatory across all areas of operations within the company. It **must** be applied before commencing any new task, ensuring that safety considerations are integrated from the outset. For detailed responsibilities, refer to [[task_safety_awareness_roles_and_responsibilities]]. The integration of TSA with [[job_hazard_analysis_and_task_safety_awareness]] is also a key aspect of this process.
