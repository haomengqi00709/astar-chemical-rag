# Mechanical Calculations

Mechanical calculations are a critical component of equipment design, ensuring structural integrity, performance, and compliance with engineering standards. These calculations provide the detailed analysis required for custom-fabricated equipment and other mechanical components.

**Content Elements**
Mechanical calculations **must** contain the following information:

*   **Calculation Methods and Formulae:** The specific methodologies and equations used to perform the analysis.
*   **Acceptance Parameters or Criteria:** The defined limits, conditions, or performance metrics that the calculation results **must** satisfy.
*   **All Calculation Input Data:** A complete and accurate record of all data used as input for the calculations.
*   **The Executed Calculation:** The detailed steps, intermediate results, and final outcomes of the calculation.

**Document Preparation and Approval**
The preparation and approval of Mechanical Calculations follow a defined workflow:

*   **Preparation:** Mechanical Calculations **must** be prepared by Mechanical personnel.
*   **Approval:** Final approval **must** be granted by Mechanical personnel.

**Responsibilities**
*   **Prepared by:** Mechanical
*   **Approved by:** Mechanical
