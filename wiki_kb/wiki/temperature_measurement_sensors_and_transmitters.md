# Temperature Measurement - Sensors and Transmitters

Accurate temperature measurement is vital for process control and safety. The selection of temperature sensors and transmitters depends on the operating temperature range and specific application requirements.

**Temperature Sensor Types:**
*   **Pt100 Resistance Temperature Detectors (RTDs):** **Will be** used for applications where the temperature to be measured is less than 300 degrees Celsius, offering high accuracy and stability in this range.
*   **Type N Thermocouples:** **Will be** used for applications where the temperature to be measured is above 300 degrees Celsius and less than 1200 degrees Celsius, suitable for higher temperature processes.
*   **Type B Thermocouples:** **Will be** used for applications above 1200 degrees Celsius, designed for extremely high-temperature environments.

**General Sensor Specifications:**
*   Except where special requirements apply, temperature sensors **will be** un-grounded, minimizing electrical noise and interference.
*   Except where special requirements apply, temperature sensors **will be** mineral insulated, providing robustness and high-temperature capability.
*   Except where special requirements apply, temperature sensors **will** have 6 mm diameter stainless-steel sheaths, offering mechanical protection and corrosion resistance.

**Transmitter Requirements:**
*   Except where prevented by the process conditions, temperature sensors **will be** supplied with programmable head-mounted transmitters, unless this arrangement is not practical. Head-mounted transmitters reduce wiring complexity and improve signal integrity.
*   Where multiple transmitters are required, such as for Electrolyzer temperature monitoring, the transmitters **will be** DIN rail mounted in a separate junction box, facilitating organized wiring and maintenance.
*   Thermocouple transmitters **will be** configurable for upscale or downscale output on thermocouple failure, providing critical fault indication to the control system.

**Thermowell Integration:**
All temperature sensors **will be** installed with a matching thermowell.
*   Thermowells for temperature sensors **will** have pressure ratings compatible with the line or vessel rating.
*   Thermowells for temperature sensors **will** use materials compatible with the process fluids.
*   Temperature sensors for service in thermowells **will be** spring-loaded to maintain optimal thermal coupling between the sensor tip and the thermowell, ensuring accurate and responsive temperature readings.

For detailed specifications on local temperature gauges and thermowell design, refer to [[temperature_measurement_local_gauges_and_thermowells|Temperature Measurement - Local Gauges and Thermowells]].
