# Mist Eliminator Pad Field Inspection Checklist Administration (4-FIC-XX14-R0)

This document, 4-FIC-XX14-R0, serves as the standard field inspection checklist for Mist Eliminator Pads within projects managed by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, a division of Aker Kvaerner Canada Inc. The checklist ensures that all critical aspects of mist eliminator pad installation and cleanliness are verified before commissioning.

**Purpose and Scope:**
The primary purpose of this checklist is to provide a structured approach for field personnel to inspect the installation of mist eliminator pads. It covers essential checks to confirm that the equipment has been installed correctly, is free from defects, and is ready for subsequent operational phases. This ensures compliance with supplier specifications and internal quality standards, contributing to the overall reliability and performance of the mist eliminator system.

**Documentation and Sign-off Requirements:**
Formal sign-off is a mandatory requirement for this inspection checklist, ensuring accountability and verification at various stages. The document **must** be signed by:
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics personnel.
*   The Sub Contractor responsible for the installation.
*   The Client's representative.

These signatures confirm that all specified inspection items have been thoroughly reviewed and found compliant. This process is integral to the project's quality assurance framework.
