# Design Office HSE Orientation

The Design Office Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) orientation is a critical process for ensuring the safety of all personnel working within or visiting the office environment.

**Responsibilities**
The [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_document_history|Manager of Human Resources]] is responsible for providing a health and safety orientation to all new personnel who will be working in the office for a period exceeding one week. New personnel falling into this category **must** be required to work independently (and unescorted) in the office environment to receive this orientation from the HR Manager.

**Unescorted Personnel Expectations**
Unescorted personnel in the Design Office **are expected** to:
*   Follow all safety policies established by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, Aker Kvaerner, and any relevant Client.
*   Participate fully in the health, safety, and environment awareness programs implemented at the work site.

**Orientation Content and Documentation**
The Design Office health and safety orientation **will** follow the minimum outline provided in Appendix 1 of the source document. All individuals attending this orientation **will** receive a copy of the `0-MAN-8100-RA Office Safety Manual`.
