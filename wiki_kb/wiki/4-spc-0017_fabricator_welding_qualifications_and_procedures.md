# Fabricator Welding Qualifications and Procedures for 6% Moly SS (4-SPC-0017)

This section outlines the mandatory requirements for fabricator qualifications, supervision, and welding procedures when working with 6 percent Moly austenitic stainless steel under Standard **4-SPC-0017**.

**Sub-Contracting**
No sub-contracting of welding is permitted without the prior written approval of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Supervision**
The Fabricator **must** ensure regular and systematic supervision of all work, both in-shop and on-site, by competent staff.

**Welding Procedures**
All welding procedures **must** be submitted by the Fabricator for review and agreement by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Materials and Quality Assurance Group prior to the commencement of any work. The ASME Section IX PQR Form 483 **must** form part of the welding procedure submittal package. A change in essential variables, as outlined in [[ASME_section_ix_welding_and_brazing_qualifications]], **will** require re-qualification and re-submission of the affected procedure(s).

**Welder Qualifications**
All welders employed on Aker Kvaerner Chemetics work **must** be suitably qualified in accordance with relevant codes and standards for every procedure they are to use. Upon request, the Fabricator **must** supply Aker Kvaerner Chemetics with objective evidence of current welder qualifications.

All qualified welders **must** be allocated an identification symbol. This welder identification symbol **must** be identified on the parent material adjacent to their work.
