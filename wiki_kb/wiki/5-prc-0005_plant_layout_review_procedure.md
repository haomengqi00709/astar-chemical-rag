# Plant Layout Review Procedure (5-PRC-0005)

This procedure, identified as 5-PRC-0005, provides comprehensive guidelines for conducting Plant Layout Reviews within projects managed by [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_company_details]]. It outlines the necessary information, checklist items, and administrative considerations to ensure efficient and compliant plant design and layout.

### Purpose and Scope
The primary purpose of this procedure is to establish a standardized approach for reviewing plant layouts, ensuring that all critical aspects of design, safety, operability, and maintainability are thoroughly evaluated. These reviews are crucial for identifying potential issues early in the project lifecycle, thereby minimizing costly rework and schedule delays.

### Required Information for Review
For any Plant Layout Review, the following information **must** be made available:
*   [[plot_plan]]
*   [[equipment_layout_drawings]]
*   [[equipment_list_details]]
*   [[piping_and_instrumentation_drawings]]
*   [[pdms_catalogue]] (specifically the PDMS review model)
*   [[instrument_installation_drawings]] (referred to as Instrument Location Drawings)
*   [[cable_tray_routing_and_layout]]

### Review Checklist
The Plant Layout Review utilizes a detailed checklist to guide the evaluation process. Key areas covered by this checklist include, but are not limited to, considerations for:
*   Personnel
*   Electrical systems

Further details on the specific items within the checklist are provided in [[5-prc-0005_table_1_plant_layout_review_checklist]].
