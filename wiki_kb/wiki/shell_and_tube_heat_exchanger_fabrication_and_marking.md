# Shell and Tube Heat Exchanger Fabrication and Marking

This page outlines the specific fabrication requirements and marking standards for the Shell and Tube Heat Exchanger (Chemetics Equipment Number 10-10H, "Water Heater"). These requirements ensure that the equipment is manufactured to the correct design, quality, and identification standards, facilitating proper installation and maintenance.

For mechanical design requirements, refer to [[shell_and_tube_heat_exchanger_mechanical_design_requirements]].

**Fabrication Documentation and Approval:**
*   The fabricator **must** submit design calculations and fabrication drawings to Chemetics for approval before proceeding with any fabrication work. This ensures compliance with all design specifications and codes, and allows for necessary reviews and adjustments.

**Component Supply:**
*   The fabricator **must** supply all necessary bolts, nuts, and gaskets for any openings that are fitted with blind flanges. This ensures that all components required for sealing these openings are provided as part of the equipment package.

**Equipment Marking (Stenciling):**
Clear and permanent marking of the vessel is mandatory for identification and safety purposes.
*   The equipment name ("Water Heater"), equipment number (10-10H), and all nozzle mark letters **must** be clearly stenciled onto the vessel.
*   **Stenciled Letters Height:** 75 mm
*   **Stenciled Letters Color:** Black

**Civil/Structural Engineering Documentation:**
*   Document 4-SCR-xxxx, which pertains to civil/structural engineering, will be independently prepared internally for use by Chemetics or the Owner. This document provides essential information for the structural integration of the heat exchanger into the plant. For more information, refer to [[4-SCR-xxxx_civil_structural_engineering_document]].

These fabrication and marking standards are essential for quality control, traceability, and safe installation and operation of the heat exchanger.
