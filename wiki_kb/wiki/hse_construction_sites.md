# HSE Management at Construction Sites

Responsibility for Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) management systems at Construction Sites (Section 12 of 0-PRC-8000) is assigned to the Manager of Construction and Estimating. All employees bear responsibility for sound HSE practices at Construction Sites.

**Manager of Construction and Estimating Responsibilities:**
The Manager of Construction and Estimating is responsible for:
*   Designating the HSE manager for the construction works.
*   Establishing site HSE governance to meet Company and Client HSE objectives.
*   Ensuring that all subcontractors, sub-subcontractors, and vendors meet Company HSE objectives, project HSE objectives, and local regulations.
*   Advising construction HSE Managers throughout plant construction.

**Project HSE Planning:**
Responsibilities for ensuring that project HSE meets Aker Kvaerner standards, Client HSE standards, and local occupancy health and safety laws are detailed in [[0-prc-8001_project_health_and_safety_planning]]. This document is owned by the Company President, **must** be checked by the HSE Manager, and **must** be approved by the Company President.

**Site HSE Management Structure:**
The level of health and safety management required on a construction site **will** vary depending on contractual obligations:
*   **Direct Responsibility for Construction:** If the Company has direct responsibility, the Chemetics Manager of Construction and Estimating **will** appoint a full-time HSE Manager. This full-time HSE Manager **will** be responsible for the management of on-site HSE for the duration of the construction works.
*   **Construction Advisory Services:** If the Company provides construction advisory services, the HSE management responsibilities for its employees, direct subcontractors, and vendors **will** be assumed by the Company Site Construction Advisor. Refer to [[aker_kvaerner_construction_site_advisor_responsibilities]] for more details.

**Construction Site HSE Manager Role:**
The construction site HSE Manager is responsible for implementing:
*   The project health, safety, and environmental management plan, detailed in [[project-0-prc-8405_project_hse_plan]] (project specific).
*   The Aker Kvaerner Safety Policy Manual.
*   Client requirements (Client specification).
*   Jurisdictional requirements (Client specification).

Relevant reference documents for the construction site HSE Manager include [[0-spc-8003_hse_responsibilities_field_managers]].

For overall Company HSE policies and responsibilities, refer to [[0-prc-8000_administration_of_hse]] and [[hse_roles_and_responsibilities_company_wide]].

**Construction Site HSE Management:**
*   The level of health and safety management required on a construction site will vary for each worksite depending on the contractual obligations.
*   **Direct Responsibility for Construction:** If the Company has direct responsibility, the Chemetics Manager of Construction and Estimating will appoint a full-time HSE Manager responsible for the management of on-site HSE for the duration of the construction works.
*   **Construction Advisory Services:** If the Company provides Construction Advisory Services, the HSE management responsibilities of the Company for its employees, direct subcontractors, and vendors will be assumed by the Company Site Construction Advisor.
*   The construction site HSE Manager is also responsible for implementing the Aker Kvaerner Safety Policy Manual, Client requirements (Client specification), and Jurisdictional requirements (Client specification).
