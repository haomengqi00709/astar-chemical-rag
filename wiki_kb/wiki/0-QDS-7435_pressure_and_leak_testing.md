# Pressure and Leak Testing for Carbon Steel Tank (0-QDS-7435)

Pressure and leak testing for the Carbon Steel Tank, as specified in Item 8.0 of QDS 0-QDS-7435, are critical procedures to verify the structural integrity and leak-tightness of the tank. Chemetics' involvement in these tests is mandatory.

**Hydrostatic Test (Hydrotest):**
*   The Hydrotest record is required as FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package) as per [[0-QDS-7435_quality_record_definitions]].
*   Chemetics **must** witness the Hydrotest.
*   Chemetics **must** hold the Hydrotest, indicating direct oversight and approval of the test procedure and results.

**Air/Soap Test:**
*   The Air/Soap Test record is required as FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package) as per [[0-QDS-7435_quality_record_definitions]].
*   Chemetics **must** witness the Air/Soap Test.
*   Chemetics **must** hold the Air/Soap Test, ensuring proper execution and evaluation of the leak detection.

These tests are crucial for confirming that the Carbon Steel Tank can safely contain its intended contents without leakage under specified operating conditions. For a comprehensive overview of all inspection and documentation requirements, refer to [[0-QDS-7435_inspection_and_documentation_requirements]].
