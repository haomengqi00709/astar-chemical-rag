# Engineering Subcontract Delay Damages and Defect Correction (0-SPC-2205)

This page outlines the provisions for delay damages and the process for correcting defects under document 0-SPC-2205. These measures ensure accountability for timely completion and quality of services. For a broader understanding of performance security and delay damages in engineering subcontracts, refer to [[0-SPC-2202-1_engineering_subcontract_performance_security_and_delay_damages]].

**Delay Damages:**
*   The Subcontractor **must** pay delay damages at the rate specified in the [[0-SPC-2205_table_1_subcontract_data_reference|Subcontract Data]] for each day that a required document is provided late, calculated from the date the document was due until the date it is actually provided.
*   The Subcontractor **must** also pay delay damages at the rate specified in the [[0-SPC-2205_table_1_subcontract_data_reference|Subcontract Data]] for each day from the [[0-SPC-2205_general_conditions_and_administration#Key Definitions|Completion Date]] until actual [[0-SPC-2205_general_conditions_and_administration#Key Definitions|Completion]] of the services.

**Defect Correction Process:**
*   The Subcontractor **must** correct any [[0-SPC-2205_general_conditions_and_administration#Key Definitions|Defects]] within a timeframe that minimizes any adverse effect on Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or [[0-SPC-2205_general_conditions_and_administration#Key Definitions|Others]].
*   If, after [[0-SPC-2205_general_conditions_and_administration#Key Definitions|Completion]], the Subcontractor fails to correct a [[0-SPC-2205_general_conditions_and_administration#Key Definitions|Defect]] within the time required by this Subcontract, Aker Kvaerner Chemetics will assess the cost of having the Defect corrected by other parties.
*   The Subcontractor **must** pay Aker Kvaerner Chemetics the assessed cost of such Defect correction if the Subcontractor does not perform the correction themselves.
