# 0-LST-6X14 Construction Subcontract Indirect Labour Schedule

The 0-LST-6X14 document, designated as the "Construction Subcontract Indirect Labour Schedule," is a mandatory schedule that the Subcontractor **must** complete. This schedule is essential for detailing the deployment and duration of indirect personnel on the project.

The schedule **must** clearly indicate:
*   The weeks on site for all site-based indirect personnel.
*   The weeks engaged directly on the Subcontract for all home office-based personnel.

This includes various roles such as supervision, administrative staff, material handlers, equipment operators, and health and safety personnel, as detailed in [[construction_subcontract_indirect_work_costs]]. This schedule is a key input for calculating and claiming [[construction_subcontract_indirect_work_costs]] and is referenced when approving [[construction_subcontract_compensation_event_quotations]] related to indirect labour.
