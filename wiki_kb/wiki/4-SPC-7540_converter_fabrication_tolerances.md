# Converter Fabrication Tolerances (4-SPC-7540)

Fabrication tolerances for converter components, as specified in 4-SPC-7540, are critical for ensuring the structural integrity, fit-up, and performance of the final assembly. These tolerances are outlined in Appendix A of the document and are also noted directly within the specification.

**General Tolerance Requirements:**
*   Fabrication tolerances **will** be per Appendix A, titled "Converter Fabrication Tolerances".
*   Fabrication tolerances **will** also be as noted in the main body of the document.
*   In cases of conflict regarding out-of-roundness tolerances, the lowest (most stringent) number **will** govern.
*   Concentricity and out-of-roundness tolerances are explicitly stated as not cumulative.
*   Plate tolerances **are** as per applicable ASTM material tolerances for the purchased plate size.

**Specific Tolerance Guidelines:**
*   Variation in circumferential strapping between adjacent courses **must** be such that the maximum offset **will** be within [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels]] tolerances.

For a detailed breakdown of specific tolerance values, refer to [[4-SPC-7540_table_1_converter_fabrication_tolerances]]. These tolerances are essential for the successful [[4-SPC-7540_shop_fabrication_and_assembly]] of the converter.

**Specific Tolerance Requirements:**
*   Variation in circumferential strapping between adjacent courses must be such that the maximum offset will be within ASME tolerances.
*   If a conflict occurs in the out of roundness tolerances, the lowest number will govern.
*   Concentricity and out of roundness tolerances are not cumulative.
*   Plate tolerances are as per applicable ASTM material tolerances for purchased plate size.
