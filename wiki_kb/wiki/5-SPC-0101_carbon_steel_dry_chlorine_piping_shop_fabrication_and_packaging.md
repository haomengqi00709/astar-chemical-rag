# Carbon Steel Dry Chlorine Piping - Shop Fabrication and Packaging (5-SPC-0101)

Shop fabrication and packaging procedures for carbon steel dry chlorine piping spools are crucial to ensure their cleanliness and integrity before site installation.

**Shop Fabrication and Testing**
Shop fabrication and non-destructive testing **must** comply with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics design criteria. Hydraulic testing to local code requirements **must** be performed on each pipe spool.

**Cleaning and Drying Pipe Spools**
Pipe spools are prepared for "Dry Chlorine" service after hydraulic testing.
*   A solvent such as trichloroethane **must** be used for chemical cleaning (exterior and interior) of pipe spools.
*   Pipe spools **should** be cleaned with steam after chemical cleaning. Low pressure steam **must** be used for steam cleaning, which removes oil, grease, and loose scale.
*   Condensate **must** be able to drain from each pipe section during steam cleaning. Dead-ended sections **must** be opened to permit steam flow.
*   Steam cleaning of each pipe section **should** continue for at least 24 hours or until a white target at the outlet shows no rust particles or millscale.
*   Dry, clean (oil free) air **must** be blown through the hot pipe immediately after steaming to remove moisture. Dry air **should** be heated to 93°C before entering chlorine piping to aid drying.
*   Dry air flow rate **should** start at high volume rates to sweep moisture, then reduce for equilibration.

**Packaging and Shipping**
*   A primer coat of paint **should** be applied to pipe spools prior to packaging. Moisture or contaminants **must not** be reintroduced into pipe spools during primer application. Use of plugs or covers for spool ends is advisable.
*   During crating, use of silica gel bags, shrink wrap coverings, or other means of preventing moisture from entering pipe spools is recommended.
*   Crating material **must** comply with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics requirements for transport protection.
*   Shipments of pre-spooled pipe **should** be kept as intact as possible until installation is required. Removal of protective packaging **will** result in ingress of moisture and contaminants into pipe spools.
*   All pipe spools **should** be kept in dry covered storage until needed.
