# Template Vertical Pump Shaft Seal and Bearings

This page details the specifications for the shaft seal and bearings of the vertical pump, as outlined in the Template Vertical Pump Datasheet (4-DST-XXXX-R1). These components are critical for pump performance, reliability, and containment of the pumped fluid.

**Shaft Seal Requirements:**

*   **Supply:** The shaft seal is supplied by the Vendor.
*   **Mounting:** The shaft seal is mounted by the Vendor.
*   **Shaft Seal Type:** The specific type of shaft seal (e.g., mechanical seal, packing, labyrinth seal) **MUST** be clearly specified by the vendor. This selection is crucial for compatibility with the fluid and operating conditions.

**Bearing Requirements:**

*   **Supply:** The bearings are supplied by the Vendor.
*   **Mounting:** The bearings are mounted by the Vendor.
*   **Bearing Fluid:** The type of fluid used for bearing lubrication or cooling **MUST** be specified.
*   **Bearing Fluid Flow Rate:** The required flow rate of the bearing fluid **MUST** be specified, typically in L/min or GPM.
*   **Bearing Fluid Connection:** The specifications for the connections (e.g., size, type) for the bearing fluid supply and return lines **MUST** be provided.

The vendor's compliance with these specifications is subject to [[vendor_quality_assurance]] and [[vendor_supply_requirements]].

**Shaft Seal Requirements:**
*   **Supply:** Supplied by Vendor
*   **Mounting:** Mounted by Vendor
*   **Shaft Seal Type:** A required specification.

**Bearing Requirements:**
*   **Supply:** Supplied by Vendor
*   **Mounting:** Mounted by Vendor
*   **Bearing Fluid:** A required specification.
*   **Bearing Fluid Flow Rate:** A required specification.
*   **Bearing Fluid Connection:** A required specification.
