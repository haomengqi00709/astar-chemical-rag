# Support Criteria

Support Criteria documents provide essential information for the design and installation of equipment and piping supports. These documents are critical for ensuring the structural stability and safe operation of plant components by detailing the interface requirements with foundations and supporting structures.

**Content Requirements**
A Support Criteria document **shall** contain the following detailed information:
*   **Dimensioned Footprint Showing a Datum Location:** A precise drawing indicating the base dimensions of the equipment or component and a clear reference datum point for alignment.
*   **Quantity of Anchor Bolts:** The specified number of anchor bolts required for securing the support to the foundation.
*   **Size of Anchor Bolts:** The diameter and length specifications for the anchor bolts.
*   **Any Foundation Cut-outs:** Details of any necessary openings or recesses in the foundation to accommodate the support structure or anchor bolts.
*   **Support Loading Calculated at the Datum:** The design loads (e.g., vertical, horizontal, moments) that the support will transmit to the foundation, calculated at the specified datum location.

**Roles and Responsibilities**
The preparation, checking, and approval of Support Criteria documents are assigned to specific disciplines:
*   **Preparation:** The document **shall** be prepared by the Mechanical or Design discipline.
*   **Checking:** The document **shall** be checked by the Design discipline.
*   **Approval:** The document **shall** be approved by the Mechanical discipline.

This structured approach ensures that foundations and support structures are adequately designed to safely accommodate the loads from equipment and piping.
