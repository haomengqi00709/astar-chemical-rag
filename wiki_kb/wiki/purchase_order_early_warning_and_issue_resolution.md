# Purchase Order Early Warning and Issue Resolution

To proactively manage potential issues and maintain project progress, the Purchase Order (PO) mandates an early warning system and a structured approach to resolving ambiguities and inconsistencies.

**Early Warning System:**
Both the Vendor and the [[purchase_order_roles_and_responsibilities#Project Buyer|Project Buyer]] **must** issue an early warning by notifying the other party as soon as they become aware of any matter that could:
*   Increase the total of the [[purchase_order_definitions#The Prices|Prices]].
*   Delay [[purchase_order_definitions#Delivery or Deliver|Delivery]].
*   Impair the quality of the [[purchase_order_definitions#The Goods and Services|Goods and Services]].

**Early Warning Conferences:**
*   Upon an early warning notification, the Project Buyer and the Vendor **must** conduct an early warning conference.
*   The [[purchase_order_roles_and_responsibilities#Lead Engineer|Lead Engineer]] may participate in these conferences.
*   Other individuals may participate by mutual agreement of the Project Buyer and Vendor.
*   During an early warning meeting, participants **must** cooperate in:
    *   Making and considering proposals to avoid or reduce the effect of the notified matter.
    *   Seeking solutions that benefit all affected parties.
    *   Deciding on actions to be taken and identifying who, in accordance with the Purchase Order, will be responsible for those actions.
*   The Project Buyer **must** record the proposals considered and the decisions made during the conference and provide a copy of this record to the Vendor.

**Ambiguity and Inconsistency Resolution:**
*   The Project Buyer or the Vendor **must** notify the other party immediately upon becoming aware of any ambiguity or inconsistency within or between the documents that form part of the Purchase Order.
*   The Project Buyer **must** then issue an instruction to resolve the identified ambiguity or inconsistency.
