# Fan or Blower Packing and Shipping Requirements

Packing/Crating is a mandatory item or activity for the Fan or Blower equipment specified under Quality Data Sheet **0-QDS-7425**.

The following requirements apply to packing and shipping:
*   Chemetics **will Witness** the **Packaging inspection** to ensure proper protection for transit.
*   A **Packing List** **is required** as [[0-QDS-7425_quality_record_definitions|AP]] (As Prepared). Chemetics **will Witness** the Packing List.

These measures ensure that the Fan or Blower equipment is adequately protected during transportation and that all contents are accurately documented.
