# Discipline Lead and Approver Roles

Within each design discipline, specific roles are established to manage and approve technical work, ensuring quality and adherence to standards.

**Discipline Lead Assignment and Responsibilities:**
*   Each design discipline **shall** have a Discipline Lead assigned.
*   Discipline Leads are assigned by the respective Discipline Manager or Chief Engineer.
*   Discipline Leads **shall** execute all assigned work, either directly or by delegating tasks to their discipline team.
*   Discipline Leads **shall** check the assigned work to ensure accuracy and compliance.

The checking and approval process for all design work is governed by the [[0-prc-7004_checking_and_approval_of_design|0-PRC-7004: Checking and Approval of Design]] procedure.

**Discipline Approver Assignment and Responsibilities:**
*   Each design discipline **shall** have a Discipline Approver assigned.
*   Discipline Approvers are assigned by the respective Discipline Manager or Chief Engineer.
*   Discipline Approvers **shall** approve the assigned work, signifying its readiness and compliance.

It is common practice for the Discipline Lead to also be assigned Discipline Approver responsibilities. A Discipline Lead **may** approve her own work, provided she possesses sufficient experience, maturity, and a demonstrated record of accuracy. If the Discipline Lead is not assigned Approver responsibilities, these duties will generally be undertaken by the Discipline Manager or Chief Engineer.

It is possible for multiple Design Discipline Approvers to be assigned for different types of work within a single discipline (e.g., separate approvers for documentation related to different categories of equipment). For further commentary on multiple Discipline Approver assignments, refer to Section 6 of the [[0-prc-2001_document_overview|0-PRC-2001 procedure]].

For general principles governing personnel assignments, see [[general_personnel_assignment_principles]].
