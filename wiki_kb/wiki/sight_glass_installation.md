# Sight Glass Installation

Sight glass installation involves the correct mounting of sight glasses into a piping system or vessel to allow visual inspection of process fluids. This includes ensuring all components, such as shrouds, are properly assembled and that the piping arrangement does not impose undue stress on the sight glass body. For critical services, careful attention to [[sight_glass_inspection_5_prc_0007|piping alignment]] and recommended torque settings is essential to prevent damage and ensure safe operation.
