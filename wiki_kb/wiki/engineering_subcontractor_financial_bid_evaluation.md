# Engineering Subcontractor Financial Bid Evaluation

The Financial Evaluation is an essential part of the Engineering Subcontractor bid assessment process, focusing on the financial stability of potential vendors. This evaluation **will** be prepared in the form of 0-TAB-XXXX-5: [Template Financial Evaluation] and is the responsibility of the Finance department.

A Financial Evaluation is **required** for any subcontract with a value exceeding CAD 100,000. To streamline the process, the [[project_engineer_pem_gate_review_responsibilities|Project Engineer]] may, for expediency, direct that the [[engineering_subcontractor_financial_bid_evaluation|Financial Evaluation]] be conducted only on a shortlist of Subcontractors.

The financial evaluation **will** involve a thorough examination of the Subcontractor's audited operating accounts from the previous fiscal year. This examination ensures that the risk of the Subcontractor suffering a liquidity or credit crisis during the project lifecycle is minimal, thereby safeguarding project continuity.

Based on this financial assessment, Finance **will** assign one of the following ratings to each Subcontractor:
*   Financial status satisfactory
*   Financial status satisfactory with listed qualifications
*   Financial status unsatisfactory

A rating of "satisfactory with listed qualifications" indicates that while the Subcontractor's financial standing is generally acceptable, specific conditions or guarantees may be required to mitigate identified risks during the [[engineering_subcontractor_selection_and_summary_record|subcontractor selection]] process.

Upon completion, the evaluator **will** publish the [[engineering_subcontractor_financial_bid_evaluation|Financial Evaluation]]. For detailed publishing procedures, refer to [[document_publishing_methods|Publishing of Project Documents]] (0-PRC-0005). The Chief Financial Officer **will** provide the final approval for the [[engineering_subcontractor_financial_bid_evaluation|Financial Evaluation]].
