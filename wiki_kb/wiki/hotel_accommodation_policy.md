# Hotel Accommodation Policy

The Group Travel Procedure (11.1 PR 2) provides comprehensive guidelines for hotel accommodation, prioritizing cost-effectiveness and the utilization of negotiated rates.

### Utilizing Company Premises
Whenever possible, employees **should** utilize any Company premises, even if they are not part of the traveller’s specific Business Area. This helps to reduce external accommodation costs.

### Group Preferred Hotel Programmes
The Group has secured negotiated rates with many hotels participating in the Group Preferred Hotel Programmes.
*   A list of these preferred hotels is available on the websites of many Business Areas and **will** also be featured on the Group's central website.
*   The first choice for hotels **must** always be Group preferred hotels.
*   The next choice for hotels **must** be those hotels with which the nominated travel agent has an existing agreement.
*   Travellers **should** make every effort to use the Preferred Hotel Programme whenever it is consistent with business needs.

### Verifying Rates at Check-in
Upon checking in at a hotel, travellers **should** verify that the lowest available rate is being applied. Special promotional rates, which may be lower than the booked rate, might be available directly from the hotel but **will** only be granted if specifically requested by the traveller.

### Long-Term Stays and Project Accommodation
For longer-term stays and accommodation required for projects, arrangements **should** be made by the defined project manager. This **must** be done in cooperation with the Company’s nominated travel coordinator. This ensures that specific project needs and budget constraints are addressed appropriately.

### Hotel Negotiations in New Locations
Negotiations for hotel rates in new locations **must** be referred to the Business Area or Company travel coordinator. Agreement **must** be reached on the best approach for these negotiations, whether locally or through the [[group_travel_roles_responsibilities#the_group_travel_network|Group Travel Network]], to secure the most favorable terms.
