# Design Deficiencies (5-PRC-0007)

During the [[mechanical_checkout_process_flow_5_prc_0007|mechanical checkout process]] for piping, any modifications or changes identified that deviate from the original design intent are classified as design deficiencies.

**Key Characteristics:**
*   A design deficiency is distinct from a standard punch list item, which typically addresses construction quality or completeness issues.
*   Design deficiencies represent fundamental issues with the design documents themselves or significant deviations from them that require design-level resolution.

**Resolution Process:**
All identified design deficiencies are thoroughly documented and subsequently forwarded to the [[project_manager|Project Manager]] for formal review and resolution. This ensures that design-related issues are addressed at the appropriate project level, potentially leading to design revisions or formal approvals for modifications.
