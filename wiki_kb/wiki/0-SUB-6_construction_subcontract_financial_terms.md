# Construction Subcontract Financial Terms (0-SUB-6)

This page details the financial terms, pricing, and compensation event assessment mechanisms for Construction Subcontract Agreement 0-SUB-6.

**Pricing and Payment:**
*   **Total Price:** The total of the Prices for the works is determined by the Subcontractor.
*   **Price List:** The applicable price list for this subcontract is [[0-LST-6X11_construction_subcontract_price_list]]. Data available in SQL: 0-LST-6X11.
*   **Payment Assessment Day:** The assessment day for progress payments is the last Sunday of each month.
*   **Retention:** A retention of 10% of the Price is applied to payments.

**Security and Damages:**
*   **Security:** Security in the amount of 5% of the Price **must** be provided by the Subcontractor. This security **must** be in the form of an irrevocable unconditional bank guarantee.
*   **Delay Damages:** Delay damages are set at AUD10,000 per day.

**Compensation Event Assessment:**
The assessment of [[compensation_events_and_assessment_0_spc_6001]] **shall** be based on the following documents and schedules:
*   [[construction_subcontract_pricing_lump_sum_0_spc_6011]] (0-SPC-6011)
*   [[0-LST-6X12_construction_subcontract_labour_rates]]. Data available in SQL: 0-LST-6X12.
*   [[0-LST-6X13_construction_subcontract_crew_rates]]. Data available in SQL: 0-LST-6X13.
*   [[0-LST-6X14_construction_subcontract_indirect_labour_schedule]]. Data available in SQL: 0-LST-6X14.
*   [[0-LST-6X15_construction_subcontract_equipment_schedule]]. Data available in SQL: 0-LST-6X15.

**Overheads and Profit Percentages:**
The percentages for overheads and profit added to the Subcontractor's costs for compensation events are as follows:
*   For people: by Subcontractor%.
*   For Equipment and Materials: by Subcontractor%.
*   For sub-subcontracted work: by Subcontractor%.
