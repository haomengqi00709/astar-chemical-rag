# Piping Documentation Procedure Overview (5-PRC-0003)

This procedure, identified as 5-PRC-0003, outlines the methodology for project personnel in preparing comprehensive piping documentation. The primary objectives of this documentation are to:
*   Provide a detailed record of pipe sizing and design.
*   Furnish a complete description of technical requirements for issuance to vendors and construction contractors.

The procedure also clearly assigns responsibilities for the preparation and approval of these documents. It defines the specific types of documents that **shall** be prepared during the course of piping design activities.

**Scope Exclusions**
Underground piping is not currently included within the scope of this procedure.
