# Invoicing and Payment for Third Party Inspection Services

This page outlines the mandatory requirements for invoicing and payment related to Third Party Inspection Services provided under [[subcontractor_quality_assurance_0_prc_7006]].

**Wire Payment Authorization**
Where funds other than Canadian Dollars (CDN$) are the form of payment, the Agency **must** send a completed and signed Wire Payment Authorization Form (Figure 5) to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Accounting Communication**
The designated e-mail address for all accounting communication is Accounts.Payable@akerkvaerner.com.

**Invoice Requirements**
All invoices submitted **must** adhere to the following criteria:
*   All invoices **must** be numbered.
*   All invoices **must** be dated.
*   All invoices **must** reference the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Purchase Order (PO) Number issued to the Agency.
*   If an invoice covers more than one Vendor PO, costs **must** be allocated accordingly for each PO.
*   When all work on an Agency PO is complete, the invoice **will** explicitly state that it is the “Final Invoice for Purchase Order Number 0XXXXXXX-07XX”.
*   When work on an Agency PO extends over a period exceeding two months, invoicing **will** be submitted monthly.

**Price Adherence and Change Orders**
The price of services **shall not** exceed the price stated on Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ PO without prior written approval. Any approval for exceeding the PO price **will** be documented on a [[change_order_documentation_requirements_0_chg_22]]. This ensures strict financial control and transparency.

**Payment Delays**
Failure to comply with any of the stipulated invoicing requirements **will** result in a delay in payment until all missing information is supplied and corrected.

**Accounting Communication**
The dedicated e-mail address for all accounting communication is Accounts.Payable@akerkvaerner.com.

**Invoice Requirements**
All invoices **must** adhere to the following:
*   Be numbered and dated.
*   Reference the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Purchase Order (PO) Number on the Agency.
*   If more than one Vendor PO is covered by the invoice, costs **must** be allocated accordingly.
*   When work on an Agency PO is complete, the invoice **will** state that it is the “Final Invoice for Purchase Order Number 0XXXXXXX-07XX”.
*   When work on an Agency PO extends over a period exceeding two months, invoicing **will** be submitted monthly.

**Pricing and Approval**
The price of services **shall not** exceed the price stated on Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ PO without prior written approval. Any approval for exceeding the PO price **will** be documented on a Change Order.

**Consequences of Non-Compliance**
Failure to comply with these invoicing requirements **will** result in a delay in payment until all missing information is supplied.
