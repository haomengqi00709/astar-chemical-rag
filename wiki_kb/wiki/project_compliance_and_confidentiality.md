# Project Compliance and Confidentiality

This page outlines general compliance requirements and confidentiality protocols for all project personnel.

**Compliance:**
*   All work on the Project will be performed in accordance with applicable Corporate Policies.

**Confidentiality and Secrecy:**
*   All personnel are required to comply with the Secrecy Agreement attached as an Appendix to the project documentation.
*   Personnel who regularly communicate with third parties need to be especially vigilant in their disclosure of information.
