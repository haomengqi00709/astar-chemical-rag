# Safe Work Permit Roles and Responsibilities

Effective management of safe work permits is critical for ensuring safety during plant commissioning and operation. This process involves clear responsibilities for the Commissioning HSE Manager, the Commissioning Team, Work Supervisors, and all employees.

**Commissioning HSE Manager Responsibilities**
The Commissioning HSE Manager **will** be appointed for each Site and is responsible for issuing work permits. The Commissioning HSE Manager **shall** ensure that:
*   The Commissioning Team (Aker Kvaerner Chemetics personnel, Contractors, and Vendor representatives) knows and understands the required safety procedures applicable to the work.
*   The Commissioning Team knows and understands the location and the type of work being carried out.
*   The Commissioning Team is familiar with the requirements for obtaining work permits.

In the event the Commissioning HSE Manager is temporarily away from site (e.g., due to [[0-pol-9111_home_leave_and_r_and_r|R&R]], site leave, or [[0-pol-91xx_sick_leave_policy|sick leave]]), the [[commissioning_manager_assignment|Commissioning Manager]] **will** assume administration of all safety-related activities.

**Commissioning Team Responsibilities**
The Commissioning Team **shall**:
*   Become thoroughly familiar with the Client's regulations for obtaining work permits, if available.
*   Coordinate all work with the Commissioning HSE Manager on site.
*   Ensure that a work permit is issued for all applicable tasks.
*   Ensure that the hazards and limitations at the site are identified.
*   Ensure that a joint inspection of the work area is conducted.
*   Ensure that a positive definition of the work to be accomplished is obtained.
*   Ensure that all precautions are taken.
*   Ensure that safe practices are followed.

Familiarization with these procedures is often covered during [[hse_new_employee_orientation]] and [[hse_training_program_details]].

**Work Supervisor Responsibilities**
Before commencement of work, all precautions **shall** be verified by a competent Work Supervisor. Upon issuance of the proper work permit, the Work Supervisor **shall** sign the work permit, verifying that they have reviewed the permit and understand its limitations.

Upon receiving the permit, it is the responsibility of the Work Supervisor to review the permit with their employees/crew. The Work Supervisor **must** explain the precautions and limitations of the proposed task to their employees/crew. Additionally, the Work Supervisor **shall** ensure that employees understand terms that may be used, such as "inert," "hole watch," or "toxic."

**Employee Responsibilities**
All personnel entering operating areas are responsible for obtaining the proper Client authorization and appropriate work permits. Employees **shall** be notified that if any changes in the conditions of the permit take place, the work **shall** stop immediately, and the Client **must** be notified. Adherence to these responsibilities is crucial for maintaining [[safe_work_practices_for_construction_site_advisors]].
