# Metal Butterfly Damper Actuation Equipment

The actuation equipment for metal butterfly dampers is integral to their control and operational functionality. The complete control damper assembly **SHALL** include the supply, mounting, and assembly of this equipment onto the damper body.

**Components of Actuation Equipment:**
The actuation equipment package **SHALL** consist of:
*   The primary actuation mechanism.
*   A declutchable gear box.
*   A hand wheel for manual operation.

**Assembly and Calibration:**
The Vendor is responsible for the mounting and assembly of the actuation equipment onto the damper body. This also includes the necessary instrument calibration to ensure precise control.

**Painting Requirements:**
Actuation equipment **SHALL** be painted according to the Vendor’s standard paint system designed for severe service conditions, ensuring durability and protection in challenging environments.

**Post-Testing Procedures:**
Following the successful completion of functional checks, the actuation equipment **SHALL** be demounted from the damper body.
*   Each piece of actuation equipment **MUST** be clearly tagged with identification that corresponds to its appropriate valve body. This ensures correct re-assembly during installation.
*   For shipping, damper bodies and actuation equipment **SHALL** be shipped separately, as detailed in [[damper_packaging_and_shipping]].
