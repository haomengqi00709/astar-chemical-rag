# Fabricator Welding Qualifications and Procedures for Clad Steel

Fabricators undertaking work for Aker Kvaerner Chemetics involving austenitic stainless clad steel welded fabrications **must** adhere to stringent qualification and procedural requirements to ensure the highest quality and compliance.

**Subcontracting and Supervision**
Subcontracting of welding is **not permitted** without Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' written approval. The Fabricator **must** ensure regular and systematic supervision of all work, both in-shop and on-site, by competent staff.

**Welding Procedures and Qualifications**
All welding procedures, including those for weld overlay, **must** be submitted by the Fabricator for review and agreement by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' Q.A. & Materials group prior to the commencement of any work. The [[ASME_section_ix_welding_and_brazing_qualifications]] PQR Form 483 **must** form part of the welding procedure package. Any change in essential variables, as outlined in [[ASME_section_ix_welding_and_brazing_qualifications]], will require re-submission of the affected procedure(s).

All welders employed on Aker Kvaerner Chemetics work **must** be suitably qualified in accordance with relevant codes and standards for every procedure they are to use. The Fabricator **must**, upon request, supply Aker Kvaerner Chemetics with objective evidence of current welder qualifications. Each qualified welder **must** be allocated an identification symbol, which **must** be stamped on the parent material adjacent to the welder's work.

All welding procedures and testing **must** be agreed upon between the purchaser and Fabricator (refer to Para 5.3 of the source document) before any production welding begins.

**Work Adherence and Deviations**
All work **must** be carried out in accordance with the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics "Approved for Fabrication" shop detail drawing(s). Deviations from the weld geometry as shown on approved drawing(s) are **not permitted** without the prior approval of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Specific Procedural Approvals**
*   **Hot Forming**: If hot forming is necessary, the procedure **must** be agreed to in writing by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics prior to commencement of work.
*   **Postweld Heat Treatment (PWHT)**: If PWHT is specified, the Fabricator **must** submit the procedure in writing for approval prior to commencement of work. The PWHT procedure **must** include information on the number and location of thermocouples and supports.
*   **Passivation**: The passivation procedure **must** be approved by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics prior to its use.
*   **Electrode Selection**: Electrode selection other than those shown in [[4-SPC-0016_table_2_manual_metallic_arc_welding_combinations]] **must** be approved in writing by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
*   **Iron Content Pickup**: Where iron content in alloy clad welding is of consequence (e.g., with Alloy 20 Cb3), the maximum percentage pickup in the last pass **must** be provided by the Fabricator prior to fabrication.

**Non-Conformance Management**
Where results of chemical analysis show that a specific weldment falls outside the approved specification or code requirements, the Supplier **will** promptly advise Aker Kvaerner Chemetics in writing of their proposed course of action. This proposed course of action **must** include details of any additional testing necessary to fully define the extent of the non-conformance. All repairs and retests related to non-conforming chemical analysis **will** be to the Supplier’s account. This process aligns with general [[vendor_quality_assurance]] principles.

**Fabrication Drawings and Deviations**
All work **must** be carried out in accordance with the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics "Approved for Fabrication" shop detail drawing(s). Deviations from the weld geometry as shown on approved drawing(s) are not permitted without the prior approval of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
