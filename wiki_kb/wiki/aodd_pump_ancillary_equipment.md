# AODD Pump Ancillary Equipment

This page details the ancillary equipment associated with the Air Operated Double Diaphragm (AODD) Pump, as specified in document 4-DST-XXXX-R1. Ancillary equipment enhances the pump's functionality, protects it, and ensures its efficient and safe operation. For each item, the responsibility for supply and mounting is clearly assigned.

**Ancillary Equipment Items:**
The following items are considered ancillary equipment for the AODD pump:

*   **Foot Mount:** Provides stable support for the pump, allowing it to be securely bolted to a foundation or skid.
*   **Lubrication:** Refers to any required lubrication system for moving parts, though many AODD pumps are designed for lube-free operation.
*   **Muffler:** Reduces the noise generated by the exhaust air from the pump's air motor, contributing to a safer and more comfortable working environment.
*   **Pulsation Dampener:** Minimizes pressure fluctuations and pulsations in the fluid discharge line, protecting downstream equipment and ensuring a smoother flow.
*   **Strainer:** Installed on the suction side to filter out solid particles from the fluid, protecting the pump's internal components from damage. For more details on strainers, refer to [[strainer_hydrostatic_testing_and_final_inspection]].
*   **Air Filter:** Removes particulates and moisture from the compressed air supply, protecting the pump's air motor and extending its lifespan.
*   **Air Regulator:** Controls and maintains a consistent air pressure to the pump, ensuring stable operation and preventing over-pressurization.
*   **Air Dryer:** Removes moisture from the compressed air supply, preventing condensation and potential damage to the pump's air motor, especially in humid environments.
*   **Air Solenoid Valve:** An electrically operated valve that controls the flow of compressed air to the pump, allowing for remote start/stop or integration into automated control systems. This falls under [[electrical_equipment_specifications]].
*   **Speed Control:** A mechanism (often an air flow control valve) that allows adjustment of the pump's operating speed, thereby controlling the flow rate. This also relates to [[electrical_equipment_specifications]] if electronically controlled.
*   **Leak Detector:** A device or system designed to identify and alert personnel to fluid leaks from the pump, enhancing safety and environmental protection.

**Supply and Mounting Responsibility:**
For all listed ancillary equipment items:
*   **Ancillary Equipment supply responsibility is Vendor.**
*   **Ancillary Equipment mounting responsibility is Vendor.**

This clear assignment of responsibility ensures that the vendor provides and correctly installs all necessary auxiliary components, guaranteeing a complete and functional pump package.
