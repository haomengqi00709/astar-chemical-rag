# Engineering Subcontractor Commercial Bid Evaluation

The Commercial Evaluation is a critical component of the Engineering Subcontractor bid assessment process. This evaluation **will** be prepared using the template 0-TAB-XXXX-3: [Template Commercial Evaluation]. The responsibility for preparing this evaluation lies with the [[discipline_lead_and_approver_roles|Discipline Lead]].

The commercial evaluation **will** involve a detailed comparison of all elements within the received bids against the specific requirements outlined in the Subcontract, the established [[0-dat-3001_project_schedule|project schedule]], and the allocated [[project_financial_and_tax_structure|project budget]].

Regarding financial aspects, all cost evaluations **must** be conducted in Canadian dollars. Where applicable, project hedge rates **must** be utilized for cost evaluations. In instances where hedging is not in place, forward rates **must** be applied. Furthermore, if a bid is submitted in a currency different from that budgeted, the cost associated with collapsing any existing currency hedges **must** be factored into the evaluation.

The commercial evaluation **will** meticulously list all deviations proposed by each Subcontractor from the original bid requirements. These proposed deviations **will** then be compared against the approaches taken by other Subcontractors for similar items to assess their impact and feasibility. Should any clarification be required regarding a Subcontractor’s bid, the [[discipline_lead_and_approver_roles|Discipline Lead]] **will** directly address the query to the respective Subcontractor.

While cost and schedule comparisons are generally straightforward, for other commercial evaluation criteria, the [[discipline_lead_and_approver_roles|Discipline Lead]] **will** assign one of the following ratings to each Subcontractor:
*   Commercially satisfactory as tendered
*   Commercially satisfactory with listed qualifications
*   Commercially unsatisfactory

Upon completion, the evaluator **will** publish the [[engineering_subcontractor_commercial_bid_evaluation|Commercial Evaluation]]. Guidelines for publishing project documents can be found in [[document_publishing_methods|Publishing of Project Documents]] (0-PRC-0005). Final approval of the [[engineering_subcontractor_commercial_bid_evaluation|Commercial Evaluation]] **will** be provided by the [[discipline_lead_and_approver_roles|Discipline Chief Engineer]].
