# Business Travel Compensation

Aker Kvaerner Chemetics is committed to providing reasonable compensation for time spent away from home while employees are travelling on business.

**Recording Travel Time**
Employees travelling on business **must** adhere to the following guidelines for recording hours:
*   A maximum of 8.25 hours per day **shall** be recorded for any days spent in transit.
*   In general, 8.25 hours **should** be recorded for the day employees depart for business travel.
*   In general, 8.25 hours **should** be recorded for the day employees return from business travel.
*   For all days in between the departure and return dates, 8.25 hours **should** generally be recorded, with the exception of any weekend days spent at the away location where no work is performed.

**Work Performed During Travel**
Time actually worked at the away location that is in excess of the normal hours of work will be compensated as set out in the general [[0-pol-9113_overtime_compensation_policy_overview]], specifically according to the employee's classification as [[0-pol-9113_exempt_employee_overtime_compensation]] or [[0-pol-9113_non_exempt_employee_overtime_compensation]].

**Scheduling Travel**
Employees are not expected to schedule business travel outside of normal working hours.

All travel hours and any associated overtime **must** be recorded on weekly [[0-pol-9113_timesheet_procedures]].
