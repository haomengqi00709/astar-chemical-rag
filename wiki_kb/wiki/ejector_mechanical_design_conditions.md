# Ejector Mechanical Design Conditions

This page details the mechanical design considerations and material selection requirements for ejector packages, including their associated condensers. Adherence to these specifications is crucial for the reliability and longevity of the equipment. For general mechanical design requirements, refer to [[general_mechanical_design_requirements_0_spc_0010]].

**Condenser Configuration:**
*   **Interstage Condenser Type:** Options include Surface or Barometric.
*   **Afterstage Condenser Type:** Options include Surface or Barometric.

**Operational Requirements:**
*   The Ejector **must** be stable at Minimum Flow Rate. This is a mandatory operational requirement to ensure consistent performance and prevent surging or instability.

**Equipment Data and Materials of Construction:**
The following information is required for the ejector package:
*   **Number of Stages:** This is a mandatory equipment data point.
*   **Material of Construction for the Ejector Body:** This specifies the material used for the main body of the ejector.
*   **Material of Construction for Condensers (process side):** This specifies the material for components of the condensers that come into contact with the process fluid.
*   **Material of Construction for the Ejector Nozzle:** This specifies the material for the critical nozzle component.

These material selections are vital for corrosion resistance and structural integrity, especially given the process fluids and operating conditions. For detailed process design parameters, refer to [[ejector_process_design_conditions_overview]].
