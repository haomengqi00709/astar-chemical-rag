# Vendor Quality Document Control

Effective document control is a fundamental requirement for Vendor quality assurance, as specified in [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment|0-SPC-7001]]. All Vendor activities that affect the quality of the goods **shall** be executed in conformance with documented instructions, procedures, and drawings.

**Documentation Requirements**
All documents governing quality-related activities **must** include appropriate acceptance criteria. These criteria are essential for objectively determining that the activities have been satisfactorily accomplished and that the goods meet the required specifications.

**Review and Approval**
A rigorous process for document review and approval **shall** be in place. All quality-related documents **must** be:
*   Reviewed for adequacy to ensure they are complete, accurate, and suitable for their intended purpose.
*   Approved for release by authorized personnel, signifying their official endorsement.
*   Distributed appropriately to all relevant personnel and departments to ensure that the most current and approved versions are always used.

**Types of Quality-Related Documents**
Quality-related documents include, but are not limited to:
*   Procedures
*   Instructions
*   Specifications
*   Drawings
*   Procurement documents

**Change Control for Documents**
Changes to any quality-related documents **must** receive the same degree of review and approval as the original documents. This ensures that all modifications are thoroughly vetted and authorized, maintaining the integrity and control of the documentation system.
