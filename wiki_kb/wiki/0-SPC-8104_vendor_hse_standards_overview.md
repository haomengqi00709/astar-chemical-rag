# Vendor Health and Safety Standards Overview (0-SPC-8104)

Specification 0-SPC-8104 establishes the minimum Health and Safety (HSE) standards that [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_name_change_history|Vendor]] employees **must** adhere to when performing work at any Aker Kvaerner Chemetics work site. This specification is crucial for ensuring a consistent and high level of safety across all operations involving external personnel, aligning with the company's overarching safety objectives.

Aker Kvaerner Chemetics is unequivocally committed to providing a safe and healthy workplace for all its employees. This commitment extends to conducting all operations in an environmentally sound manner, forming a core part of the company's [[hse_targets_and_policy|HSE policy]]. These principles are non-negotiable and guide all activities undertaken on company premises or projects.

Every individual working on an Aker Kvaerner Chemetics work site is expected to actively cooperate and contribute toward the success of the company's comprehensive [[aker_kvaerner_hse_policy_manual|HSE program]]. This includes a mandatory expectation to perform all job tasks in the safest manner prescribed by company procedures and industry best practices. Furthermore, all personnel **must** conduct themselves in a way that consistently enhances their personal safety and the safety of others around them. These general [[hse_management_and_employee_responsibilities|HSE responsibilities]] are fundamental to maintaining a secure and compliant operational environment.
