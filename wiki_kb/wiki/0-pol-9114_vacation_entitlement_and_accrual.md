# 0-POL-9114 Vacation Entitlement and Accrual

This section details the framework for how vacation time is earned and allocated under document 0-POL-9114, the Vacation Policy.

**Vacation Year Definition**
The designated vacation year for all eligible employees at Aker Kvaerner Chemetics runs consistently from January 1 to December 31. This standardized period ensures clarity and consistency in vacation planning and accrual calculations across the company.

**Accrual Basis**
Vacation time will accrue on a monthly basis throughout the defined vacation year. This incremental accrual method allows employees to steadily build their vacation balance over time, providing flexibility for planning leave.

**Entitlement Calculation**
Employees earn and receive their vacation entitlements based on a dual criteria system. The entitlement is determined by the greater of two factors:
1.  **Position Hay Point Level:** This reflects the level of responsibility and complexity associated with an employee's role.
2.  **Length of Service:** This considers the cumulative tenure an employee has with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

This dual approach ensures that both the value of an employee's role and their loyalty and experience with the company are recognized in their vacation benefits. Specific vacation entitlements, detailing the exact allocation based on these criteria, are comprehensively detailed in [[0-pol-9114_table_2_contents]].

**Pro-ration of Entitlements**
Vacation entitlements will be pro-rated accordingly based on an employee's promotion dates or their anniversary dates. This ensures that changes in an employee's role or their continued tenure are accurately reflected in their vacation accrual, providing fair and precise allocation of vacation time throughout their career progression and service with the company.

Vacation time accrues on a monthly basis. Entitlements are determined by the greater of an employee's position Hay Point level or their length of service. These entitlements will be pro-rated based on promotion dates or anniversary dates as applicable.
