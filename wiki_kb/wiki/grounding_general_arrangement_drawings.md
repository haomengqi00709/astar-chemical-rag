# Grounding General Arrangement Drawings

Grounding General Arrangement (GA) drawings provide a comprehensive layout of the plant's grounding system, including conductors, electrodes, and test wells. These drawings are critical for ensuring effective and safe grounding across the entire facility.

**Content of Grounding GA Drawings**
Grounding general arrangement drawings **shall** show:
*   The plant main ground grid conductors.
*   Ground electrodes.
*   Ground test wells.
*   Clearly show and identify locations of new buildings.
*   Clearly show and identify locations of new substations.
*   Clearly show and identify existing plant ground grids for existing buildings.
*   Clearly show and identify existing plant ground grids for existing substations.
*   Clearly show and identify existing plant ground grids for existing equipment.
*   Interconnection points between existing and new ground grid systems.
*   Sizes of plant ground grid conductors.
*   Branch conductor sizes.
*   Ground grid conductors.
*   Sizes of the ground grid conductors.
*   Ground electrodes, clearly shown and described.
*   Ground grid conductor locations, dimensionally located from buildings, substations, and equipment.
*   Ground electrode locations, dimensionally located from buildings, substations, and equipment.
*   Symbols used to indicate grounding conductors.
*   Symbols used to indicate electrodes.
*   Symbols used to indicate connections.

All necessary [[grounding_installation_details]] shown on other drawings **must** be referenced.
