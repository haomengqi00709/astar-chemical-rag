# FRP Vessel Material Requirements (4-SPC-0042)

This section details the [[material_selection]] and composition requirements for fibreglass reinforced plastic (FRP) vessels, aligning with [[ASME_RTP-1_standard_for_reinforced_thermoset_plastic_corrosion_resistant_equipment]] Part 2 - Materials, with specific exceptions and additions.

**Laminate Construction**:
*   Materials comprising glass fibre reinforced resin laminates and the laminates **shall** substantially be in accordance with RTP-1 Part 2 - Materials.
*   Formal adherence to Mandatory Appendices M-2 (Reinforcement Materials Receiving Procedures) and M-3 (Matrix Materials Receiving Procedures) is **not a requirement**.
*   [[frp_post_cure_requirements]] are specified in project specifications, drawings, or data sheets.
*   Unless otherwise specified, the fabricator **shall** select either standard contact moulded or non-standard construction for each vessel.
*   Standard contact moulded construction for all major vessel components **shall** be Type II (mat-woven roving-mat) only.
*   Type I (all mat) laminates **shall only** be used when specified.
*   Non-standard or Type X laminates, including filament winding, **require** lamination analyses.

**Inner Surface and Corrosion Liner**:
*   The inner surface exposed to contents **shall include** flange faces and all surfaces of internal components.
*   The surface veil is specified as single or double C-glass or polyester.
*   C-glass **shall** be 0.25mm (0.010 in.) thick grade, chemical resistant veil.
*   Polyester **shall** be 0.38mm (0.015 in.) thick aperture type, Nexus surfacing fabric manufactured by Precision Fabrics Group or an Aker Kvaerner Chemetics approved equal.
*   Unless otherwise specified, the inner surface **shall** consist of one layer of resin saturated surface veil.
*   Only 0.46 kg/m2 (1½ oz/ft2.), 1.1mm (0.043 in.) thick, commercial grade, E type (electrical) or ECR type (electrical-chemical) chopped strand mat is **permitted** in the secondary corrosion liner interior layer.
*   Chopped roving (spray-up) is **not permitted** in the secondary corrosion liner interior layer.

**Structural Armouring**:
*   Unless otherwise agreed, the FRP structural armouring for dual laminate vessels **shall** be Type II (mat-woven roving-mat) laminate only.
*   Only mat and 0.82 kg/m2 (24 oz/yd2) commercial grade, E type woven rovings are **permitted** in the structural layer of Type II laminates.
*   Commercial grade, E type continuous filament strand roving is **permitted** in the structural layer of Type X laminates, in addition to mat and woven roving.
*   Chopped roving (spray-up) is **not permitted** in the structural layer of any laminates.

**Outer Surface and Additives**:
*   The outer surface **shall** be a paraffinated resin coat and a resin rich C-glass surfacing veil applied over the final mat ply of the structural layer.
*   An ultraviolet absorber, a fire retardant synergist, and pigmentation **shall** be incorporated in the outer surface.
*   Pigmentation colour **shall** be specified in project specifications, data sheets, or drawings.
*   All additives **shall** be used as recommended by the resin manufacturer.
*   As a minimum, the final mat ply of the structural laminate **requires** a fire retardant resin system and synergist.
*   If additional laminate areas require fire protection, these **shall** be specified in project specifications, data sheets, or drawings.

**Lamination Analysis**:
*   The use of commercial software such as Superlam by Fiberglass Canada Ltd. is permitted to determine laminate properties using Lamination Analysis.
*   Lamination analysis calculations **shall** be submitted for review.

**Material Receiving Procedures**
Formal adherence to Mandatory Appendices M-2 (Reinforcement Materials Receiving Procedures) and M-3 (Matrix Materials Receiving Procedures) is not a requirement.

**Laminate Types and Construction**
*   Standard contact moulded construction for all major vessel components will be Type II (mat-woven roving-mat) only.
*   Type I (all mat) laminates are permitted only when explicitly specified.
*   For dual laminate vessels, unless otherwise agreed, the FRP structural armouring will exclusively be Type II (mat-woven roving-mat) laminate.

**Inner Surface and Corrosion Liner**
*   The inner surface exposed to contents includes flange faces and all surfaces of internal components.
*   The surface veil is specified as single or double C-glass or polyester.
    *   C-glass veil must be 0.25mm (0.010 in.) thick, chemical resistant grade.
    *   Polyester veil must be 0.38mm (0.015 in.) thick aperture type, Nexus surfacing fabric manufactured by Precision Fabrics Group or an Aker Kvaerner Chemetics approved equal.
*   Unless otherwise specified, the inner surface will consist of one layer of resin saturated surface veil.
*   For the secondary corrosion liner interior layer, only 0.46 kg/m2 (1½ oz/ft2.), 1.1mm (0.043 in.) thick, commercial grade, E type (electrical) or ECR type (electrical-chemical) chopped strand mat is permitted. Chopped roving (spray-up) is explicitly not permitted in this layer.

**Structural Layer Composition**
*   In Type II laminates, only mat and 0.82 kg/m2 (24 oz/yd2) commercial grade, E type woven rovings are permitted in the structural layer.
*   For Type X laminates, commercial grade, E type continuous filament strand roving is permitted in the structural layer, in addition to mat and woven roving.
*   Chopped roving (spray-up) is not permitted in the structural layer of any laminates.

**Outer Surface and Fire Retardancy**
*   The outer surface will consist of a paraffinated resin coat and a resin rich C-glass surfacing veil applied over the final mat ply of the structural layer.
*   An ultraviolet absorber, a fire retardant synergist, and pigmentation will be incorporated into the outer surface. The pigmentation colour will be specified in project specifications, data sheets, or drawings.
*   All additives must be used as recommended by the resin manufacturer.
*   As a minimum, the final mat ply of the structural laminate requires a fire retardant resin system and synergist.
*   If additional laminate areas require fire protection, these requirements will be specified in project specifications, data sheets, or drawings.
