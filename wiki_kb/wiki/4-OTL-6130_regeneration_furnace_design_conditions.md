# Regeneration Furnace Design Conditions (4-OTL-6130)

Drawing [[4-OTL-6130_regeneration_furnace_design_conditions|4-OTL-6130]], titled "Regeneration Furnace," specifies the detailed design conditions for the [[regeneration_furnace_process_and_design_conditions|Regeneration Furnace]]. This drawing serves as a primary reference for the engineering and fabrication of the furnace, providing critical parameters that govern its operation and structural integrity.

Key information typically found in this design drawing includes:
*   **Process Design Conditions**: Detailed parameters such as operating temperatures (e.g., 980 oC to 1100 oC at exit), pressures, flow rates of various feeds (spent sulphuric acid, ammonium bisulphate, molten sulphur, etc.), and gas compositions (e.g., 1.5 % O2 and 5 to 6 vol% SO2 at exit).
*   **Equipment Dimensions**: Overall dimensions of the furnace, including internal and external geometries, and critical clearances.
*   **Material Specifications**: Requirements for the materials of construction for the furnace shell, internal components, and refractory linings.
*   **Nozzle Schedule**: Details of all nozzles and penetrations, including their sizes, locations, orientations, and flange ratings.
*   **Burner Specifications**: Information related to the fuel oil and combination fuel oil/organics burners, including their capacities, injection points, and fuel-to-air ratio ranges.
*   **Thermal Data**: Design temperatures for various sections of the furnace, which are crucial for the design of the [[4-SPC-0082_refractory_brick_lining_design_requirements|refractory lining system]] and external insulation.
*   **External Loads**: Data on wind, seismic, and other external loads that the furnace structure must withstand.

This drawing is essential for ensuring that the furnace is designed and constructed to safely and efficiently perform its function of decomposing spent sulphuric acid and ammonium bisulphate compounds. It also provides the basis for the thermal expansion considerations required for the [[4-SPC-0082_refractory_brick_lining_specification_overview|refractory brick lining]].
