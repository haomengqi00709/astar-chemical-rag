# Travel Health and Documentation Support

Ensuring proper documentation and health preparations are critical for international travel. This page outlines key requirements for visas, immigration letters, and passport validity.

**Visa Requirements:**
For all visa requirements, travelers **must** contact their internal travel coordinator (Anita, Xeniya, or Kellie). The travel coordinator requires the complete charge code before a visa request can be processed. Travelers are advised to give as much notice as possible for visa applications to avoid delays.

**US Immigration Letters:**
An internal travel coordinator will prepare and provide a US Immigration letter. This letter should be carried by the traveler and presented only if specifically requested by immigration officials.

**Passport Validity:**
Passports generally **cannot** expire within 6 months of the intended travel date. Travelers **must** provide a copy of their passport to their internal travel coordinator for retention in the travel file.

For information on medical advisories and immunizations, refer to [[travel_immunization_and_medical_advisory]]. For emergency assistance during travel, see [[travel_emergency_assistance_and_evacuation]]. The overall travel request process is detailed in [[travel_authorization_process_0_frm_9002]].
