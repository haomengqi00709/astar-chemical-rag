# Hot Work Permit Training and Documentation (0-PRC-8503)

Comprehensive training and meticulous documentation are mandatory components of the Hot Work Permit procedure, as outlined in [[0-prc-8503_hot_work_permit_procedure_overview]]. These measures ensure that all personnel involved in hot work activities possess the necessary knowledge and skills to perform their duties safely and effectively.

**Mandatory Training Requirements:**
*   Each employee authorized to perform open flame/hot work **shall** be thoroughly trained in all aspects of these guidelines.
*   Everyone **must** go through Open Flame/Hot Work Training provided by the [[0-prc-8503_hot_work_permit_roles_and_responsibilities|Commissioning HSE Manager]].
*   Training can be provided either on Site or in the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics office.

**Fire Watch Training:**
The [[0-prc-8503_hot_work_permit_roles_and_responsibilities|Commissioning HSE Manager]] **shall** train the [[0-prc-8503_hot_work_permit_roles_and_responsibilities|fire watch]] person. This training **shall** cover:
*   Proper execution of fire watch responsibilities.
*   Proper use of fire extinguishers.
*   Proper use of explosion meters.

**Documentation of Training:**
*   All training **shall** be documented.
*   Training records **shall** be entered into the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics log book, which is maintained by the [[0-prc-8503_hot_work_permit_roles_and_responsibilities|Commissioning HSE Manager]].
*   For general HSE training program details, refer to [[hse_training_program_details]]. Individual training history records may be maintained using forms such as [[form_dp-802_305_safety_training_history_record]].

**Incident and Near Miss Reporting:**
*   All Open Flame/Hot Work incidents and near misses **shall** be thoroughly investigated and documented. This ensures continuous improvement of safety protocols and training programs.

*   Training shall be entered into the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics log book that the Commissioning HSE Manager keeps.
*   Open Flame/Hot Work Training can be provided either on Site or in the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics office.

**Fire Watch Training:**
*   The fire watch person shall be trained by the Commissioning HSE Manager.
*   Training shall cover proper execution of fire watch responsibilities.
*   Training shall include proper use of fire extinguisher.
*   Training shall include proper use of explosion meters.
