# Task Safety Awareness (TSA) Program Monitoring and Improvement

To ensure the sustained effectiveness and continuous improvement of [[task_safety_awareness_overview]] (TSA), each project **will develop** a robust means for monitoring the TSA program. This monitoring mechanism **must ensure** that the TSA program is being fully and consistently implemented across all operations.

**Continuous Reinforcement and Review:**
*   Projects **shall continuously stress** the concepts, principles of application, and overall importance of TSA in all safety topics and meetings. This ongoing emphasis helps embed TSA into the daily safety culture.
*   TSA records, typically documented using the [[0-REP-8006a_task_safety_awareness_form]], **will be reviewed** periodically throughout a project's lifecycle.

**Utilization of Monitoring Results:**
The results derived from the monitoring and follow-up activities serve multiple critical purposes:
*   They **will be used** for rewards in recognition of compliance, encouraging adherence to TSA protocols.
*   They **will be used** to take proactive action in cases of non-compliance, addressing deviations promptly to prevent incidents.
*   They **will be used** as a means of improving the TSA process for subsequent tasks, fostering a cycle of continuous learning and refinement.

**Safety Mindset:**
A safety mindset **will be reinforced** at the site on a continual basis, with TSA being a cornerstone of this ongoing cultural emphasis. This commitment to monitoring and improvement ensures that TSA remains a dynamic and effective tool for hazard prevention.

*   TSA records will be reviewed periodically during a project.
*   Results of follow up will be used for rewards in recognition of compliance, to take proactive action in cases of non-compliance, and as a means of improving the TSA for subsequent tasks.
*   A safety mindset will be reinforced at the site on a continual basis.
