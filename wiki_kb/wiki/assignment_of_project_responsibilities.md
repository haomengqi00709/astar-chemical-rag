# Assignment of Project Responsibilities (0-PRC-2001)

Document **0-PRC-2001: Assignment of Project Responsibilities** outlines the roles and duties assigned to various project personnel and disciplines. This document is referenced for defining the responsibilities of Discipline Approvers, particularly in the context of approving [[vendor_document_requirements_preparation|Vendor Document Requirements (VDRs)]] and marked-up vendor documents during the [[vendor_document_review_process|review process]]. It ensures that appropriate authority and accountability are maintained throughout project documentation workflows.
