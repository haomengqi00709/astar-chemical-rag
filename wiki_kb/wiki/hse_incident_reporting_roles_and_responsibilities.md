# HSE Incident Reporting Roles and Responsibilities

This page outlines the specific responsibilities of employees and management personnel regarding HSE incident reporting, investigation, and follow-up, as defined in [[0-prc-8013_hse_incident_reporting]]. Adherence to these roles is crucial for an effective safety management system.

**Employee Responsibilities**
Employees **must** adhere to the following requirements:
*   **Immediate Reporting:** Employees **must** report all incidents to their supervisor or manager immediately after they occur, or as soon as practicable thereafter. This prompt reporting is crucial for enabling timely investigation and response.
*   **Scene Preservation:** Employees **must** ensure that the incident scene is not disturbed until the situation can be controlled and assessed by appropriate personnel.
*   **Investigation Assistance:** Employees **must** assist in the incident investigation as required, providing accurate accounts and information.
*   **Preventive Measure Cooperation:** Employees **must** assist and co-operate in the implementation of any preventive measure(s) deemed necessary following the investigation(s) to prevent recurrence.

**Management Personnel Responsibilities**
Management Personnel **must** fulfill the following duties:
*   **HSE Department Notification:** Management Personnel **must** report all incidents immediately (or as soon as practicable thereafter) to the HSE Department to initiate the formal response process.
*   **Scene Preservation Oversight:** They **must** ensure the incident scene is not disturbed until the incident is thoroughly investigated, preserving critical evidence.
*   **Investigation Leadership:** Management Personnel **must** take a key role in the accident investigation, actively participating in determining underlying causes and identifying effective corrective and preventive measures.
*   **Implementation of Measures:** Following the investigation, they **must** ensure that the identified preventive measure(s) are implemented in a timely manner across relevant operations.
*   **Staff Notification:** It is mandatory that Management Personnel **must** ensure their staff are notified of investigation results and any preventive measures, fostering a culture of learning and safety awareness.
*   **Procedure Review:** As part of the investigation process, Management Personnel **must** review relevant [[risk_assessments]] and [[safe_work_procedures]] to identify potential systemic issues.
*   **Data Entry:** Management Personnel **must** enter all incident data into the [[aker_kvaerner_synergi_database]], including details on underlying root causes and follow-up actions required to close out the case, ensuring comprehensive record-keeping and trend analysis.
