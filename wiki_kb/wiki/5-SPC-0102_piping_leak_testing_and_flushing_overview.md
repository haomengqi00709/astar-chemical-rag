# Piping Leak Testing and Flushing Overview (5-SPC-0102)

This Standard (5-SPC-0102) defines the requirements for leak testing and flushing of piping systems prior to commissioning. The primary intent of leak testing is to demonstrate that a system is suitable for operation at its design pressure. It is important to note that leak testing does not relieve the contractor of responsibility for quality materials, good workmanship, and effective quality control.

The test requirements outlined in this Standard represent the minimum acceptable criteria. If additional or different tests are deemed necessary, the contractor **must** advise Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Scope and Applicability**
This Standard applies to piping handling all types of fluids and services, as defined in ASME B31.1, paragraph 100.1.1, and ASME B31.3, paragraph 300.1.1. Other definitions relevant to piping systems can be found in ASME B31.1, paragraph 100.2, and ASME B31.3, paragraph 300.2.

Piping should be tested as systems and in sections as the installation work progresses, wherever possible.
