# Flat Bottom Tank on Concrete (No Grout) - Anchor Bolt Installation Requirements

This page outlines specific installation requirements for anchor bolts related to the Flat Bottom Tank on Concrete (no Grout), as specified in document 4-SCR-0004. These requirements are critical for ensuring the secure fastening and stability of the tank.

### Mandatory Installation Requirements
The following installation requirements **must** be adhered to:
*   The minimum "Bolt to Edge" distance **must** be 150 mm.
*   Loads **shall** be applied at Datum.
