# Commercial and Financial Bid Evaluation (Orders > CAD 200k)

For orders valued greater than CAD 200,000, a detailed commercial and financial evaluation of the short-listed bids **must** be conducted.

### Financial Examination

Finance **must** examine the financial information of short-listed Vendors for the previous fiscal year. The purpose of this examination is to ensure minimal risk of Vendor liquidity or credit crisis. For subsidiary Vendors, financial information **must** be obtained from the parent company. If required Vendor financial information is not readily available to Finance, Procurement **must** expedite its acquisition. Finance **must** issue its financial evaluation and recommendations to Procurement via memorandum.

### Commercial Evaluation

Procurement **must** conduct a detailed commercial evaluation of the short-listed bids. This evaluation **must** be documented using the [[commercial_bid_tabulation_template]] (0-TAB-5XXX-3).

The Commercial Bid Tabulation **must** include:
*   A list of commercial deviations proposed by each Vendor.
*   A comparison of Vendor commercial deviations with other Vendors' approaches.
*   The financial evaluation and recommendations provided by Finance.
*   Any outstanding claims or disputes between the Vendor and Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
*   Any previous claims or disputes between the Vendor and Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
*   The total value of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ current Purchase Orders with the Vendor.

If clarification of a Vendor’s bid is necessary, Procurement **must** direct the query to the Vendor. Technical evaluators **must** be copied on all Vendor bid clarification queries.

The evaluator **must** publish the Commercial Bid Tabulation using [[publishing_project_documents]] (0-PRC-0005). The Manager of Procurement **must** approve the Commercial Bid Tabulation.
