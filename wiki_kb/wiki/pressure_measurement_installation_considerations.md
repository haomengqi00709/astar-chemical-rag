# Pressure Measurement - Installation Considerations

Proper installation of pressure instruments is crucial for their accuracy, longevity, and safety.

**Transmitter Mounting:**
*   All pressure transmitters **will be** installed using 2" pipe mounting kits.
*   Mounting brackets **will** support the transmitters; impulse tubing **will not** be used to support transmitters. This ensures the integrity of the impulse lines and prevents stress on the instrument.

**Siphons and Pigtail Condenser Seals:**
*   Siphons or "pigtail" condenser seals **will be** provided for connections to close-mounted gauges or transmitters in steam and other high-temperature (> 60°C) condensing vapor applications. These protect the instrument from direct contact with high-temperature process fluid.
*   Pigtails provided for steam application **will be** Carbon Steel.
*   Fill and drain ports **will not** be provided on siphons or pigtail condensers.
*   To protect the gauges on start-up, the siphon or pigtail **will be** manually water filled prior to start-up.

**Pulsation Dampers:**
Where pulsation is expected, and the process is non-clogging, suitable adjustable dampers **will** be used to stabilize readings and protect the instrument. Dampers **will not** be used in slurry applications, as they can become clogged.

**Diaphragm Seals (General):**
*   The preferred diaphragm seal type is 80 mm RF Flange.
*   Fill liquid for local and remote seals **will be** selected having regard to process conditions. Glycerine or glycol/water are preferred fill liquids for most applications for local and remote seals due to their compatibility and temperature characteristics.

**Capillary Tubing:**
*   All capillary tubing **will be** stainless steel armoured for protection and durability.
*   Equal length capillary tubing **will be** used on differential pressure applications to ensure balanced pressure transmission and accurate readings.
