# Document Publishing Methods

Aker Kvaerner Chemetics permits two distinct methods for publishing project documents: Digital Publishing and Paper Publishing. Both methods aim to create an unalterable record of a document, which can then be certified by authorized personnel through signatures.

### Digital Publishing
Digital Publishing involves the conversion of documents into Adobe Portable Document Format (PDF). This method requires the affixing of digital signatures by authorized personnel. Digitally published and signed documents are stored in the project electronic file, specifically in the same folder as their native format versions. Personnel can view these digital signatures using Adobe Approver or Adobe Acrobat. To verify the authenticity of a digital signature, personnel **must** have received the digital signature key belonging to the signer. All Aker Kvaerner Chemetics personnel responsible for checking or approving project documents **shall** store their digital signature key in the designated network location: `Q:\Signatures\Aker Kvaerner ChemeticsAker Kvaerner Chemetics`.

### Paper Publishing
Paper Publishing involves printing documents on physical paper. Signatures for paper-published documents **must** be affixed using blue ink. After signing, these paper documents **shall** be scanned into Adobe Portable Document Format (PDF). The scanned PDF documents are then stored in the project electronic file, in the same folder as the native format version. The original signed paper document **shall** be stored in the project paper file.

Both methods are integral to maintaining accurate and verifiable project documentation. For detailed procedures on each method, refer to [[digital_document_publishing]] and [[paper_document_publishing]]. The overall context of document publishing is described in [[0_prc_0005_document_overview]].

The structure for both electronic and paper project files is defined in [[document_control_1_prc_0003]].
