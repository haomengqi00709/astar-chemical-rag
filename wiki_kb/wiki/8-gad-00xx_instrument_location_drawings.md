# Instrument Location Drawings (8-GAD-00XX)

Instrument Location Drawings (8-GAD-00XX) are general arrangement drawings that visually depict the placement of instrumentation within the plant.

**Content Requirements:**
Instrument Location Drawings (8-GAD-00XX) **shall** contain:
*   Location of all field mount instrument.
*   Location of all field mounted instrument junction boxes.
*   Instrument Tag Number.
*   Approximate elevation.
*   Instrument signal type (Analog, Discrete).
*   Power supply requirements.
*   Instrument air requirements.

**Document Preparation and Approval:**
*   **Prepared by:** Detail Design Engineer (DDE)
*   **Checked by:** Design Office
*   **Approved by:** Detail Design Engineer (DDE)

**Development and PDMS Integration:**
The DDE will provide Instrument Location Drawings, which will depict the location and elevation of all field mounted instrumentation, along with electrical power supply and instrument air requirements. Plant General Arrangement (GA) drawings and elevations will be utilized in the production of these drawings.

All in-line type instruments and all vessel trim instruments will be input into the PDMS model by the Design Office. All other instruments and all Junction Boxes will be input into the PDMS model by the DDE.
