# Horizontal Centrifugal Pump Design Standards and Impeller Specifications

Horizontal centrifugal pumps **must** be designed in strict accordance with ASME B73.1: Specification for Horizontal End Suction Centrifugal Pump for Chemical Process. This ensures adherence to industry-recognized standards for chemical process pumps.

**Dynamic Balancing**
All equipment **must** be dynamically balanced in accordance with ISO1940: Mechanical vibration - Balance quality requirements of rigid rotors - grade G6.3. This requirement is critical for minimizing [[equipment_vibration_control|vibration]] and ensuring the longevity and reliability of the pump.

**Impeller Diameter Requirements**
*   The selected impeller diameter **must not** be greater than 90% of the maximum size available for the chosen pump casing without prior approval from Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
*   Conversely, the selected impeller diameter **must not** be less than 110% of the minimum size available for the selected pump casing without approval from Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
*   Once a pump casing has been selected, the Vendor **must** proceed with the maximum size impeller for that casing.
*   The Vendor **must not** trim the impeller until the final size is confirmed in writing by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Operating Speed**
The maximum operating speed of the pump **must** be less than the synchronous speed of a two-pole motor.

**Impeller Type**
Impellers **must** be semi-open unless otherwise specified on individual [[template_horizontal_centrifugal_pump_datasheet_administration|Pump Data Sheets]].
