# Instrument Loop Diagrams (8-LOP-XXXX)

Instrument Loop Diagrams (8-LOP-XXXX) are essential engineering drawings that illustrate the complete wiring and functional details of instrument loops.

**Content Requirements:**
The Instrument Loop Diagrams (8-LOP-XXXX) **shall** contain, as a minimum:
*   Instrument tag numbers of all field devices.
*   All wiring details of field installed instruments.
*   Instrument junction box designations.
*   Terminal numbers.
*   Wire numbers.
*   Cable numbers.
*   DCS I/O details.
*   Control Valve Fail Position.
*   Solenoid Valve Shelf Position (de-energized/energized).

**Document Preparation and Approval:**
*   **Prepared by:** Detail Design Engineer (DDE)
*   **Approved by:** Detail Design Engineer (DDE)

**Development and Application:**
The DDE will provide Instrument Loop Diagrams. These diagrams will contain all wiring details of field installed instruments as per the [[8-lst-0002_instrument_index]]. Where Mechanical Packages are supplying instruments shipped loose, the DDE will provide loop diagrams.

When the client maintains DCS responsibility, DCS I/O details may not be shown on loop diagrams. The DDE will determine the DCS contractual requirements from the contract package documents.

**As-Built Documentation:**
As-built commissioning documents **will be provided** for Instrument Loop Diagrams (as applicable).
