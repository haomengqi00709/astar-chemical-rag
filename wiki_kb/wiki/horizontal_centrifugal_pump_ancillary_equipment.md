# Horizontal Centrifugal Pump Ancillary Equipment

Ancillary equipment for horizontal centrifugal pumps, as detailed in Document ID [[template_horizontal_centrifugal_pump_datasheet_administration]], includes various components that support the main pump unit, ensuring its proper installation, operation, and maintenance.

**Vendor Supply and Mounting:**
*   Ancillary Equipment *shall be supplied by Vendor*.
*   Ancillary Equipment *shall be mounted by Vendor*.

**Specific Ancillary Equipment:**

*   **Coupling:** *Shall be specified*. The coupling connects the pump shaft to the motor shaft, transmitting power while accommodating minor misalignments.
*   **Coupling Guard:** *Shall be specified*. This safety device encloses the rotating coupling to prevent accidental contact.
*   **Base plate c/w Spacer Block:** *Shall be specified*. The base plate provides a rigid foundation for mounting both the pump and motor, while the spacer block facilitates maintenance by allowing the pump to be removed without disturbing the motor or piping.
*   **Bearing:** *Shall be specified*. Bearings support the rotating shaft, reducing friction and ensuring smooth operation.
*   **Lubrication:** *Shall be specified*. The type and method of lubrication for bearings and other moving parts are critical for longevity.
*   **Drip Pan:** *Shall be specified*. A drip pan is installed beneath the pump to collect any leakage from seals or drains, preventing contamination of the surrounding area.

This equipment is integral to the overall mechanical design and installation of the pump, complementing the [[horizontal_centrifugal_pump_mechanical_design_requirements]] and ensuring a complete and functional unit.
