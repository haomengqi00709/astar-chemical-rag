# Commercial Control of Construction Subcontracts (0-PRC-6007)

The document 0-PRC-6007 is a Procedure detailing how to maintain commercial control of construction subcontracts. The primary responsibility for the control of these subcontracts rests with the Subcontract Administrator.

**Objectives of the Subcontract Administrator:**
The Subcontract Administrator's role is crucial for ensuring efficient and financially sound project execution. Key objectives include:
*   Timely identification and quantification of all changes in the Works Information.
*   Accurate forecasts of the total cost of the works.
*   Minimal close-out effort due to retrospective cost claims.

This procedure outlines specific steps to be followed, particularly in response to instructions and potential [[construction_subcontract_compensation_events]], to ensure that undue delays in the delivery of the works are avoided, contrasting with potentially lengthier steps described in the general [[standard_conditions_for_construction_subcontract_0_spc_6001]].
