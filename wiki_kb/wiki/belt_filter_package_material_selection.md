# Belt Filter Package Material Selection

This page details the mandatory material selection requirements for the Template Belt Filter Package, as specified in Document ID **4-PDS-XXXX-R1**. Adherence to these specifications is crucial for the durability and operational integrity of the equipment.

**Mandatory Material Requirements:**
*   All wetted parts material of construction **shall** be **304L SS** (Stainless Steel).
*   Filter cloth material of construction **shall** be **PP** (Polypropylene).

These material choices are selected to ensure compatibility with the process fluids and to withstand the operational environment. For general information on material selection, refer to [[material_selection|Material Selection]] or specific equipment material specifications like [[aodd_pump_material_specifications|AODD Pump Material Specifications]].
