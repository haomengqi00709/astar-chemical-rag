# Quality Record Definitions for QDS 0-QDS-7901

This section defines the abbreviations used for Quality Record requirements within Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7901-R0 for the Medium Voltage Switchgear & DC Control Power Supply project. These definitions specify how documentation related to inspections and tests **must** be handled.

**Definitions:**
*   **AP (As Prepared):** Indicates that the Quality Record is required "As Prepared" by the Vendor. This means the document **must** be submitted as a standalone record.
*   **FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package):** Indicates that the Quality Record is required to be "Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package". This means the document **must** be part of the final compilation of quality assurance documentation.

These designations are critical for ensuring proper documentation and traceability throughout the project lifecycle.
