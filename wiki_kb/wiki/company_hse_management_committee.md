# Company HSE Management Committee

An HSE Management Committee will be formed within Aker Kvaerner Chemetics to oversee and review HSE performance.

**Committee Members:**
The committee includes:
*   President of Company (Chair)
*   HSE Manager (Alternate Chair)
*   Administration Services Coordinator (Responsible for Vancouver Office)
*   President of Equipment Division (Responsible for Toronto Workshop)
*   Laboratory Supervisor (Responsible for Burnaby Laboratory)
*   Manager of Construction & Estimating (Responsible for Construction Sites)
*   Manager of Process & Mechanical (Responsible for Commissioning Sites)

**Meetings and Reporting:**
*   The HSE Management Committee will meet monthly to review HSE performance.
*   Attendance by personnel located outside the Vancouver Office may be by teleconference.
*   A record of each meeting will be kept in the form of an Action List.
*   The Action List will include the status of previous actions updated and new actions noted with assignment of responsibility.
*   The Action List will be posted for access by all employees in the Company data network.
