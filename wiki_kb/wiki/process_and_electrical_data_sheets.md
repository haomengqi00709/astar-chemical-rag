# Process and Electrical Data Sheets

This page describes the requirements and content for Process Data Sheets (PDS) and their interaction with electrical equipment design.

**Process Data Sheets (PDS)**
Process Data Sheets are identified by document numbers in the format 4-PDS-XXXX, where XXXX represents the electrical equipment number. These documents are crucial for defining the specific operational parameters that influence electrical equipment selection and design.

The Process Department holds key responsibilities in this area:
*   Determining specific requirements such as DC current and voltage for electrical equipment, including Transformer/Rectifiers (T/R) and Central Processing Units (CPU) for electrolyzer cells.
*   Being responsible for the electrolyzer cell output design.
*   Determining the current and voltage required from a Transformer/Rectifier to achieve their design output.

Process Data Sheets refer to Procedure 4-PRC-0003: Equipment Design and Specification.
