# Austenitic Stainless Steel Welding Parameters and Post-Weld Treatment (4-SPC-0061)

This page details the welding parameters, post-weld cleaning, and hydrostatic testing requirements for austenitic stainless steel, as specified in 4-SPC-0061.

**Welding Parameters**
*   **Preheat:** Preheat is not required for austenitic stainless steel welding under this specification.
*   **Heat Input:** Heat input during welding **will** be kept to a minimum to control metallurgical effects.
*   **Inter-pass Temperature:** The inter-pass temperature **will not** exceed 150°C (300°F).

**Post-Weld Treatment and Cleaning**
*   **Pickling and Passivation:** When specified, pickling and/or passivation **will** be performed in accordance with approved cleaning and passivation procedures. Passivation of stainless steel **will** conform to the requirements of [[ASTM_a380_cleaning_passivation_stainless_steel]].

**Hydrostatic Testing**
*   **Water Quality:** Water used for hydrostatic testing austenitic stainless steel equipment **will** contain less than 50 ppm chlorides to prevent corrosion.
*   **Post-Test Drying:** After hydrostatic testing, equipment **will** be either wiped dry or blown dry with air to remove residual moisture and prevent contamination.
