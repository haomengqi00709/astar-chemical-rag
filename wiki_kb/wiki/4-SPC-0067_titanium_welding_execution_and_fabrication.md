# Titanium Welding Execution and Fabrication (4-SPC-0067)

This page details the procedural and environmental requirements for the welding and fabrication of titanium components, as specified in document 4-SPC-0067.

**Approved Welding Processes**
Welding of titanium components **shall** be performed using one of the following processes:
*   Gas Tungsten Arc Welding (GTAW)
*   Plasma Arc Welding (PAW)

For general [[welding_procedures_and_quality]] requirements, refer to relevant project specifications.

**Shielding Gas Requirements**
Effective gas shielding is critical for titanium welding:
*   The shielding gas **shall** be moisture-free argon, moisture-free helium, or a mixture of argon and helium.
*   Gas handling hoses **must** be made of Tygon® material or vinyl plastic material. Rubber hoses are **not acceptable**.
*   Gas shielding **is required** until the weld zone has cooled to below 426°C (800°F).
*   For pipe and fittings, a complete internal gas purge **is necessary** before starting the root pass.

**Forming and Tooling**
Special precautions **must** be taken during the forming of titanium:
*   Bend tests **shall** be performed on both surfaces of each plate selected for formed head construction. Plates which crack during bend testing **shall not** be used.
*   Forming tools previously used for other materials **must** be cleaned to remove surface contamination.
*   All burrs and indentations on forming tools **shall** be ground smooth.
*   Suitable protective layers **shall** be used between forming equipment and the titanium to minimize metallic pick-up. Iron pick-up is of particular concern for components in nitric acid service.

**Cleaning and Surface Preparation**
Maintaining cleanliness is paramount:
*   Grinding and wire brushes **must** be clean and **shall not** have been previously used on any other material.
*   Acceptable brush materials include titanium, austenitic stainless steel, or alumina.

**Heat Treatment and Temperature Control**
Preheat and inter-pass temperatures **shall** be in accordance with [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels]], Division 1 Appendix R. Temperatures **shall** be controlled by the use of temperature indicating crayons, contact pyrometers, or other suitable means. For general [[post_weld_heat_treatment]] guidelines, refer to relevant procedures.

**Defect Repair**
All excavations of plate or weld defects **must** occur prior to re-welding. For general [[non_destructive_examination]] requirements, refer to relevant project specifications.

**Support Spiders**
Weld-on bridge or support spiders **shall** be made of titanium when in contact with the vessel.

**Shielding Gas Requirements**
The shielding gas used must be moisture-free argon, moisture-free helium, or a mixture of argon and helium.
Gas handling hoses must be made of Tygon® material or vinyl plastic material. Rubber hoses are not acceptable for gas handling.
Gas shielding is required until the weld zone has cooled to below 426°C (800°F).
For pipe and fittings, a complete internal gas purge is necessary before starting the root pass.

**Fabrication Environment**
All titanium welding must be carried out in a clean, segregated area within the facility.

**Forming and Testing**
Bend tests will be performed on both surfaces of each plate selected for formed head construction. Plates which crack during bend testing will not be used in head construction.
Forming tools which have been previously used for other materials must be cleaned to remove surface contamination. All burrs and indentations on forming tools must be ground smooth.
Suitable protective layers will be used between forming equipment and the titanium to minimize metallic pick-up. Iron pick-up is of particular concern where the component is to be used in nitric acid service.

**Tooling and Surface Preparation**
Grinding and wire brushes must be clean and must not have been previously used on any other material. Grinding media will be alumina.
Brushes used must be made of titanium or austenitic stainless steel.

**Temperature Control**
Preheat and inter-pass temperatures will be in accordance with ASME Section VIII, Division 1, Appendix R.
Temperatures will be controlled by the use of temperature indicating crayons, contact pyrometers, or other suitable means.

**Temporary Attachments**
Weld-on bridge or support spiders must be titanium when in contact with the vessel.

**Defect Repair**
All excavations of plate or weld defects must occur prior to re-welding.
