# Radial Flow Gas/Gas Heat Exchanger Tubesheet and Baffle Tolerances

This page details the specific tolerance requirements for tube holes in tubesheets and baffles of radial flow gas/gas heat exchangers, as mandated by [[4-SPC-0041_radial_flow_gas_gas_heat_exchanger_manufacturing_requirements|Specification 4-SPC-0041]]. Adherence to these tolerances is critical for the proper fit-up, structural integrity, and operational efficiency of the heat exchanger.

**General Requirements**
The inside edges of all tube holes in both tubesheets and baffles **must** be free of burrs. This mandatory requirement ensures smooth tube insertion and prevents damage to the tubes during assembly and operation.

**Tubesheet Tube Hole Tolerances**
*   **Diameter Tolerance:** Tubesheet tube hole diameter tolerances **must** comply with the values specified in [[4-SPC-0041_tubesheet_tube_hole_diameter_tolerances]].
    *   For over tolerance, 96% of the holes **must** meet the value specified in row (d) of the table.
    *   The remaining percentage of tubesheet tube holes with over tolerance **must not** exceed the value specified in row (e) of the table.
*   **Location Tolerance:** Tubesheet tube hole location tolerances **must** be in accordance with [[4-SPC-0041_allowable_tolerance_for_tube_hole_locations|Appendix A]].

**Baffle Tube Hole Tolerances**
*   **Diameter Tolerance:** Baffle tube hole diameter tolerance for holes in baffles **must** comply with the values specified in [[4-SPC-0041_baffle_tube_hole_diameter_tolerances]].
    *   For over tolerance, 96% of the holes **must** meet the value specified in row (d) of the table.
    *   The remaining percentage of baffle tube holes with over tolerance **must not** exceed the value specified in row (e) of the table.
*   **Location Tolerance:** Baffle tube hole location tolerances **must** be in accordance with [[4-SPC-0041_allowable_tolerance_for_tube_hole_locations|Appendix A]].

These precise tolerance controls are essential for maintaining the mechanical design integrity of the heat exchanger, particularly concerning [[gas_gas_heat_exchanger_shell_and_baffle_design]] and [[gas_gas_heat_exchanger_tubesheet_design]]. Deviations can lead to issues during assembly, reduced heat transfer efficiency, or premature failure.

### Tube Hole Preparation and Tolerances

All tube holes in tubesheets and baffles must have their inside edges free of burrs.

**Tubesheet Tube Hole Tolerances:**
*   **Diameter Tolerances:** Tubesheet tube hole diameter tolerances must comply with the values specified in 4-SPC-0041_table_2. For over tolerance, 96% of holes must meet the value in row (d), and the remaining holes must not exceed the value in row (e).
*   **Location Tolerances:** Tubesheet tube hole location tolerances will be in accordance with Appendix A.

**Baffle Tube Hole Tolerances:**
*   **Diameter Tolerances:** Baffle tube hole diameter tolerances must comply with the values specified in 4-SPC-0041_table_3. For overtolerance, 96% of holes must meet the value in (d), and the remaining holes must not exceed the value in (e).
*   **Location Tolerances:** Baffle tube hole location tolerances will be in accordance with Appendix A.

**Appendix A** is titled "Allowable Tolerance for Tube Hole Locations".
