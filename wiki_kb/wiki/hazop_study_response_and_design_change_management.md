# HazOp Study Response and Design Change Management

Following the completion of the Hazard and Operability (HazOp) Study, a structured process is implemented to manage responses to identified deviations and integrate necessary design changes.

**Response Log Creation:**
*   At the end of the HazOp Study, the recording secretary **shall** transfer each deviation that generated a question onto a single sheet or section of [[1-haz-0002_hazop_responses_overview|[Template] HazOp Response (1-HAZ-0002)]].
*   The Project Engineer **shall** create a log of these questions using [[1-haz-0001_hazop_record_overview|[Template] Response Log (1-HAZ-0003)]]. This log will indicate the discipline assigned to respond to each question.
*   The Project Engineer **shall** advise the Discipline Leads of the deadline for completing their responses.

**Response Review and Approval:**
*   Discipline Leads **shall** provide the required responses and advise the Project Engineer and Technology Manager upon completion.
*   The Technology Manager **shall** review each response.
*   If a response is acceptable and does not impact the HazOp Study of that part of the plant, the relevant sheet or section of 1-HAZ-0002: HazOp Response will be published. This published sheet or section **shall** be digitally signed by the Technology Manager as approved for implementation.

**Iterative Design Modification for Complex Responses:**
*   If a response necessitates design changes that require further HazOp Study, the Technology Manager **shall** work iteratively with the Discipline Leads to modify the design.
*   The modified design **must** satisfactorily address any remaining HazOp Study questions.
*   The revised sheet or section of [[1-haz-0001_hazop_record_overview|1-HAZ-0001: HazOp Record]] will be published, checked, and approved following the same procedure as the original HazOp study.
*   The resulting final sheet or section of 1-HAZ-0002: HazOp Response will be published and digitally signed by the Technology Manager as approved for implementation.

**Implementation and Verification of Design Changes:**
*   The Project Engineer **shall** direct the implementation of any design changes required by the HazOp Study Responses, following approval by the Technology Manager.
*   Following implementation, the Technology Manager **shall** review the design changes.
*   If the design changes meet the intent of the HazOp Study Responses, the sheet or section of 1-HAZ-0002: HazOp Response will be digitally signed by the Technology Manager as complete.
*   If the Technology Manager believes that the design changes do not meet the intent of the HazOp Study Responses, the Technology Manager will proceed with iterative modification and review until satisfactory.

For details on HazOp question structure, refer to [[1-haz-0002_hazop_question_structure]] and [[1-haz-0002_hazop_question_details]].

**Developing Solutions:**
*   Work assignments are made to develop solutions to remove or contain identified problems.
*   Work assignments are made to develop solutions to produce a competent design.

**Handling Non-Conforming Design Changes:**
*   If the Technology Manager believes that the design changes do not meet the intent of the HazOp Study Responses, then the Technology Manager will proceed as described in the last paragraph of Section 9 of the procedure.
