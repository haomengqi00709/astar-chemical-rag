# Oil Seal Inspection

This page outlines the mandatory requirements for inspecting [[oil_seal_inspection|oil seals]] for leakage in [[rotating_equipment_mechanical_checkout|rotating]] and [[conveying_equipment_mechanical_checkout|conveying equipment]]. This inspection is crucial for preventing lubricant loss and equipment damage.
