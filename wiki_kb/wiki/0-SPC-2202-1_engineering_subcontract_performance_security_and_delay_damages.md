# Engineering Subcontract Performance Security and Delay Damages (0-SPC-2202-1)

This page outlines the requirements for performance security and the application of delay damages within engineering subcontracts, as specified in document 0-SPC-2202-1.

### Performance Security Requirements
The Subcontractor **must** provide Aker Kvaerner Chemetics with security. The conditions for this security are as follows:
*   **Provider:** The security **must** be provided by a financial institution that Aker Kvaerner Chemetics accepts.
*   **Amount:** The security **must** be for the amount stated in the Subcontract Data.
*   **Form:** The security **must** be in a form acceptable to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
*   **Submission Deadline:** The security **must** be given within 2 weeks of the starting date of the subcontract.
*   **Payment Withholding:** No payment will be made to the Subcontractor until the required security has been provided.
*   **Recourse:** Aker Kvaerner Chemetics has recourse to the security if a payment to which it is entitled remains unpaid for a period of five days after Aker Kvaerner Chemetics has notified the Subcontractor of its intention to have recourse to the security.

For more information on performance security, refer to [[performance_security_and_guarantees]].

### Delay Damages
The Subcontractor **must** pay delay damages at the rate stated in the Subcontract Data. These damages are applied under specific circumstances:
*   **Document Delays:** Delay damages are paid for each day from the date by which a document is required until the date on which the document is actually provided.
*   **Completion Delays:** Delay damages are paid for each day from the Completion Date until the actual Completion of the services.

Further details on commercial terms related to delay damages can be found in [[delay_damages_commercial_terms]].
