# Vendor Inspection & Test Plan Exclusions for 0-QDS-7404-1

This page details specific items that are explicitly *not* required to be included in the Vendor's Inspection and Test (I&T) Plan for the Electrostatic Precipitator Shell & Head Sections-Dual Laminate project, as per Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7404-1.

The following items are **not required** in the Vendor I&T Plan:

*   Material Test Certificates
*   Filament winding pattern
*   Bond testing
*   Welder qualification
*   Spark testing
*   External Coating/Lining Inspection
*   Internal Coating/Lining Inspection
*   Dimensional Final Inspection
*   Surfaces free from defects Final Inspection
*   Cleanliness Final Inspection
*   Review QA Documentation Final Inspection
*   Packaging
*   Packing List
