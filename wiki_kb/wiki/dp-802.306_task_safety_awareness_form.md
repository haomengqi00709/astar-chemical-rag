# Task Safety Awareness Form (DP-802.306)

This page describes the Task Safety Awareness (TSA) form, Document ID DP-802.306. This form is a critical tool used to ensure that all personnel involved in a task are aware of the potential hazards and the necessary safety measures.

**Purpose:**
The Task Safety Awareness form facilitates a pre-job hazard assessment and communication process. Its purpose is to ensure that the specific hazards associated with a task are identified, understood, and communicated to all team members, along with the controls required to mitigate those risks. This aligns with the broader principles of [[task_safety_awareness_implementation_and_requirements]].

**Key Sections and Information:**
The form typically includes sections for:
*   **Task Description:** A clear outline of the work to be performed.
*   **Location of Work:** Specific area where the task will take place.
*   **Date and Time:** When the TSA is prepared and valid.
*   **Identified Hazards:** A list of potential hazards associated with the task (e.g., chemical exposure, fall hazards, electrical risks, hot work risks).
*   **Control Measures:** Specific actions or equipment required to mitigate each identified hazard (e.g., PPE, isolation procedures, ventilation, permits).
*   **Emergency Procedures:** Information on what to do in case of an emergency related to the task.
*   **Personnel Involved:** A list of all individuals participating in the task.
*   **Signatures:** Signatures from all personnel involved, indicating their understanding of the hazards and control measures.

**Usage Requirements:**
As specified in [[0-prc-8503_hot_work_permit_issuance_and_validity]]:
*   No hot work permit **will** be issued until the [[0-prc-8503_hot_work_permit_roles_and_responsibilities|Commissioning HSE Manager]] completes a Task Safety Awareness (TSA) review.
*   The completed TSA **must** be posted at the location of the work.
*   The hazard assessment created for the task **must** be understood by all members of the team involved with the hot work activity, as stated in [[0-prc-8503_hot_work_permit_procedure_overview]].

This form is an essential prerequisite for many work permits, including the [[dp-802.702_hot_work_permit]], and contributes to the overall safety management system. It complements other hazard analysis tools such as the [[0-REP-8007a_job_hazard_analysis_worksheet]].
