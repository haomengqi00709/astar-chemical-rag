# Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7407: Large Fan Quality Record Definitions

This page defines the specific abbreviations used for Quality Record requirements within Quality Data Sheet (QDS) 0-QDS-7407, pertaining to the procurement of Large Fans. Understanding these definitions is essential for proper documentation and compliance throughout the project. These definitions are commonly applied across various Quality Data Sheets, such as those referenced in [[0-QDS-7_quality_record_definitions]] and [[0-QDS-7404-2_quality_record_definitions]].

**Quality Record Definitions:**
*   **AP (As Prepared):**
    *   This designation indicates that the quality record is required "As Prepared" by the vendor. It signifies that the document or record should be submitted in its original, completed form as generated during the manufacturing or testing process. These records are typically submitted for review and archiving, providing direct evidence of activities performed.
*   **FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package):**
    *   This designation means that the quality record is required to be "Included in the Quality Assurance Documentation Package." Such records form a part of the final comprehensive documentation package submitted by the vendor, demonstrating overall compliance with the specified quality requirements. This package is a critical deliverable for project handover and future reference, ensuring a complete audit trail.
*   **AP + FP (As Prepared and Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package):**
    *   When both "AP" and "FP" are specified, it means the quality record must be submitted "As Prepared" and subsequently included in the final "Quality Assurance Documentation Package." This ensures that the original record is reviewed and then formally incorporated into the project's quality documentation, providing both immediate verification and long-term traceability.

These definitions guide the vendor in preparing and submitting the necessary documentation for various inspection and testing activities, as detailed in [[0-QDS-7407_large_fan_inspection_and_documentation_requirements]].
