# Conveyors & Mills Final Inspections

This page outlines the mandatory final inspection requirements for Conveyors & Mills, as specified in Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7426. These inspections are critical to ensure the finished product meets all design and quality standards before shipment.

**Chemetics Surveillance for Final Inspections**
For all listed final inspection items, Chemetics surveillance is designated as both Witness and Hold. This means a Chemetics representative **shall** be present to observe the inspection, and the manufacturing process **shall** not proceed to the next stage without their explicit approval.

**Specific Final Inspection Items:**

*   **Dimensional:**
    *   Chemetics Surveillance: Witness, Hold
    *   This inspection verifies that all dimensions of the Conveyors & Mills components conform to the approved drawings and specifications.

*   **Contour & finish of welds:**
    *   Chemetics Surveillance: Witness, Hold
    *   This inspection ensures that all welds meet the specified contour, finish, and quality standards, free from defects.

*   **Surfaces free from defects:**
    *   Chemetics Surveillance: Witness, Hold
    *   This inspection confirms that all accessible surfaces of the equipment are free from manufacturing defects, damage, or imperfections.

*   **Cleanliness:**
    *   Chemetics Surveillance: Witness, Hold
    *   This inspection verifies that the equipment is clean and free from debris, foreign materials, or contaminants, suitable for packaging and shipment.

*   **Motor:**
    *   Chemetics Surveillance: Witness, Hold
    *   Inspection of the motor ensures it is correctly installed, free from visible defects, and meets specified requirements.

*   **Coupling/Belt:**
    *   Chemetics Surveillance: Witness, Hold
    *   Inspection of couplings and belts verifies correct alignment, tension, and installation, ensuring proper power transmission.

*   **Guard:**
    *   Chemetics Surveillance: Witness, Hold
    *   Inspection of safety guards confirms they are properly installed, secure, and provide adequate protection as per safety standards.

*   **Baseplate:**
    *   Chemetics Surveillance: Witness, Hold
    *   Inspection of the baseplate ensures it is level, free from defects, and provides a stable foundation for the equipment.

*   **Review QA Documentation:**
    *   Chemetics Surveillance: Witness, Hold
    *   This final review confirms that all required Quality Assurance Documentation has been completed, compiled, and is accurate and compliant with project specifications.

For a broader understanding of inspection and documentation requirements, refer to [[0-QDS-7411_final_inspections]].
