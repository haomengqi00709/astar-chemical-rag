# Vendor Material Identification and Control

Maintaining strict control over materials and components is essential for ensuring the quality and integrity of the final goods. Specification [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment|0-SPC-7001]] outlines the Vendor's responsibilities for material identification and control.

**Use of Accepted Materials**
Only correct and accepted materials and components (items) **shall** be used in the fabrication of the goods. This ensures that all inputs meet the specified quality standards and are suitable for their intended application.

**Identification and Traceability**
Identification **must** be maintained on the items themselves or in documentation that is clearly identifiable to the items. This identification **shall** be maintained from the initial receipt or fabrication stage until installation or use. Such identification **must** provide clear traceability to:
*   Appropriate specifying documentation (e.g., material specifications, drawings).
*   Quality records associated with the material or component.

This traceability is crucial for verifying the origin, properties, and quality history of each part.

**Prevention of Expired Items**
The Vendor's identification and control procedures **shall** explicitly preclude the use of any items after their useful life has expired. This prevents the incorporation of degraded or unsuitable materials into the goods, safeguarding product quality and reliability.
