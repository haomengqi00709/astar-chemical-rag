# Gas Cooling Tower Separator Section Dimensions

The separator section of the Gas Cooling Tower (GCT), identified by Tag No. **6530-U** in **1-Cal-6520-R0**, is designed with specific dimensions to ensure effective gas-liquid separation.

**Key Dimensions and Parameters:**
*   **Gas Velocity:** 3.0 m/s (9.84249 ft/s)
*   **Area Required for Gas:** 24.514907407407406 m² (263.8746755080234 ft²)
*   **Bottom Tower Diameter:** 5.6 m (18.329639159322657 ft)
*   **Bottom Tower Diameter on Datasheet:** 5600.0 mm
*   **Inlet Gas Nozzle Diameter:** 2300.0 mm (90.55090799999999 in)
*   **Inlet Gas Nozzle Diameter on Datasheet:** 2300.0 mm
*   **Height of Separator Section:** 6900.0 mm (271.652724 in)
*   **Height of Separator Section on Datasheet:** 6900.0 mm
*   **Height of Inlet Nozzle Centre:** 2300.0 mm (90.55090799999999 in)
*   **Height of Inlet Nozzle Centre on Datasheet:** 2300.0 mm
*   **Angle of Bottom:** 45.0 deg

These dimensions are crucial for the mechanical design and fabrication of the separator section, ensuring it meets the required gas velocity and separation area. For overall GCT dimensions, refer to [[gas_cooling_tower_dimensions]]. The process data sheet for the GCT provides additional details at [[4-pds-6530-r0_gas_cooling_tower_process_data_sheet]].
