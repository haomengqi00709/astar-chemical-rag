# HSE Audits and Inspections Roles and Responsibilities

This page details the specific roles and responsibilities for conducting and managing HSE audits and inspections as per Document ID: 0-PRC-8009. These responsibilities are crucial for maintaining a safe and compliant work environment.

**Business Unit President**
The Business Unit President **SHALL** ensure:
*   A comprehensive program of inspections is established and maintained at each business unit worksite.
*   An annual audit of the HSE Operating System is conducted.

**Department Managers / Worksite Managers**
All Department Managers or Worksite Managers **SHALL** ensure:
*   A planned schedule of inspections is in place for their respective area(s) of responsibility.
*   Review of actions arising from inspections takes place, and that all required actions are effectively closed out.

**HSE Manager for the Business Unit**
The HSE Manager for the Business Unit **SHALL** ensure:
*   A program of inspections is implemented at each business unit worksite.
*   Each worksite receives an HSE Operating System Audit.
*   Persons assigned to carry out inspections are competent and adequately trained.
*   A record of each inspection is meticulously kept, and a copy is made available to all employees.

**Joint Health and Safety Committee (JHSC)**
At worksites with an established [[0-PRC-8008_joint_health_and_safety_committee_procedure]], the responsibility for inspections **SHALL** be assigned to member(s) of the JHSC. The JHSC **SHALL** adhere to local statutory requirements when handling items identified through the inspection process. For more on JHSC duties, see [[joint_health_and_safety_committee_duties]].

**Senior Worksite Manager (in absence of HSE Manager)**
In the absence of a designated HSE Manager at a worksite, the senior worksite Manager **SHALL** assume all responsibilities for HSE Management and direction.

**Person(s) Carrying Out the Inspection**
Individuals conducting inspections **SHALL** ensure:
*   All observations made during the inspection are accurately recorded.
*   The owner of an identified action and a reasonable timescale for its completion are clearly identified and mutually agreed upon.
*   A follow-up process is conducted to verify that all agreed actions have been completed.

**Employees**
All employees **SHALL** ensure:
*   Their individual work area is maintained in a clean and safe condition.
*   Weekly inspections of their work area are performed.

These roles collectively contribute to the effectiveness of the [[0-PRC-8009_hse_audits_and_inspections_overview]] and the overall [[hse_management_and_employee_responsibilities]].
