# Refractory Brick Lining Materials List (4-SPC-0082 Table 1)

This page describes the content and purpose of Table 1, as referenced within specification 4-SPC-0082, which details requirements for refractory brick linings.

Table 1 serves as a critical reference, providing a detailed and comprehensive list of all materials and consumables required for the refractory brick lining system. This includes, but is not limited to, various types of refractory brick, insulating brick, castable materials, different grades of mortar, ceramic paper, ceramic fibre (specifically for applications such as expansion joints), and various types of anchors used for securing the lining.

The table is designed to ensure that the Vendor **shall** supply all necessary components, aligning with the obligations outlined in [[4-SPC-0082_refractory_brick_lining_materials_and_supply]]. It typically includes columns for item description, material specification, quantity, unit of measure, and any specific notes or requirements for each component. This level of detail is crucial for procurement, quality assurance, and ensuring the correct materials are available for installation.

For further details on the quality and testing requirements for these materials, refer to the [[4-SPC-0082_refractory_brick_lining_materials_and_supply|materials and supply section]] of the main specification.
