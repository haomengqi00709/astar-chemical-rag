# Engineering Subcontractor Document Supply (0-SPC-0002)

This document, 0-SPC-0002, specifies the requirements for document supply by engineering subcontractors for the Project. It details the types of documents, formats, submittal schedules, and quality standards expected from subcontractors.

Subcontractors are required to comply with this specification to ensure that all necessary engineering documentation is provided in a timely and compliant manner, supporting the overall [[engineering_subcontract_management_and_quality]] and [[project_document_control_and_archiving]].
