# ASME Section VIII, Div. 1 - Pressure Vessels

ASME Boiler & Pressure Vessel Code (BPVC) Section VIII, Division 1, provides rules for the design, fabrication, inspection, testing, and certification of unfired pressure vessels. It is one of the most widely used codes for pressure vessel construction globally, ensuring safety and reliability.

This section of the ASME BPVC is referenced in [[4-SPC-0014_referenced_codes_and_definitions]] and extensively in [[4-SPC-0014_inspection_and_testing_requirements]] for the fabrication and inspection of Columbium Stabilized High Nickel Austenitic stainless steel pressure vessels. Key applications include:
*   Requirements for Dye Penetrant (PT) examination on all welds.
*   Acceptability limits for PT examination (Appendix VIII).
*   Requirements for 100% radiography of butt welds.
*   Requirements for spot radiography for certain plate thicknesses (Para. UW-52(b)).

For comprehensive details on pressure vessel design and construction, refer to the latest edition of ASME Section VIII, Division 1.
