# Subcontractor Pre-qualification Report (0-REP-8405)

This report, identified as 0-REP-8405, serves as a comprehensive survey covering the Safety, Financial, and Performance aspects of potential subcontractors. Its purpose is to gather essential information to assess a subcontractor's capabilities and suitability for participation in Aker Kvaerner Chemetics projects.

**Financial Requirements**
Subcontractors **must** attach a copy of their audited Income Statements and Balance Sheets for the last two years. These financial documents **must** include all accompanying notes and the audit report. This information is crucial for evaluating the subcontractor's financial stability and capacity.

**Project Experience**
Subcontractors **must** provide separate lists detailing their construction experience. These lists **shall** include:
*   Current projects presently under construction.
*   Major projects completed within the last five (5) years.
*   All work previously performed for Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and its former affiliates John Brown or Davy International.

Each entry in these construction experience lists (A, B, C) **shall** include the following details:
1.  Location of the project and the owner.
2.  A clear description of the project.
3.  Details of direct and subcontracted work completed.
4.  The approximate value of the contract.
5.  The percent Complete for ongoing projects.
6.  The duration of work.
7.  The total peak manpower deployed.
8.  A reference name and phone number for verification.

Additionally, a comprehensive Company Owned Equipment List **must** be attached to demonstrate available resources.

**Information Technology (IT) Capabilities**
Subcontractors **must** provide responses to questions regarding their company's IT capabilities, including:
*   Confirmation of high-speed Internet capabilities (e.g., DSL, Cable or other).
*   Ability to download electronic files from an FTP site, especially those exceeding 20MB. If unable, an explanation is required.
*   Ability to receive e-mail attachments exceeding 5MB. If there are size limits, these **must** be provided.
*   Possession of WinZip software or other capabilities for opening and reading compressed (zipped) files.
*   Availability of a Printer or Plotter for printing drawings.
*   Availability of viewing software suitable for opening, viewing, and printing or plotting drawings in DWG, DGN, and PDF formats.

**Quality Assurance/Quality Control (QA/QC)**
A copy of the Table of Contents for the subcontractor's QA/QC procedures and program **must** be provided. This allows Aker Kvaerner Chemetics to assess the subcontractor's quality management systems.

**Evaluation and Future Bidding**
The information gathered through this report, combined with safety performance evaluations, forms the basis for future bidding opportunities. Evaluation results are recorded in the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics database and are used to compile future bidders lists. Only subcontractors demonstrating favourable safety and performance records [[subcontractor_safety_commitment_and_evaluation]] **shall** be allowed to bid on future work.

For details on how financial information is handled, refer to [[confidential_information_policy]]. The overall process for evaluating subcontractors is outlined in procedures such as [[construction_subcontractor_evaluation_and_approval_0_prc_6003]] and [[engineering_subcontractor_evaluation_0_prc_2204]].
