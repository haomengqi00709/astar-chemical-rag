# Packaged Equipment Mechanical Checkout Requirements

This page outlines the mandatory requirements for the mechanical checkout of packaged equipment, which includes items designated by suffixes F (Furnace, Fired Boiler, Incinerator, Kiln, Stack), H (Cooling Tower, Water Chillers, Economizer, Refrigeration), and R (Water Demineralizer, Brine Ion Exchanger). This process is a key part of the overall [[mechanical_equipment_checkout_procedure]].

The checkout of packaged equipment confirms that the equipment is installed according to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics requirements. This checkout is independent of any checks performed by the vendor’s personnel. Any [[rotating_equipment_mechanical_checkout|rotating equipment]] (e.g., pumps, fans, compressors) mounted on packaged equipment **must** be independently checked out in accordance with the relevant procedures.

**Required Documents:**
The following documents are mandatory for the checkout of packaged equipment:
*   [[piping_and_instrumentation_drawings|P&ID]]
*   Vessel Outline drawing
*   [[equipment_data_sheets_4_dst_xxxx|Equipment data sheet]]
*   [[boiler_code_certification_requirements|Boiler code certification]]

**General Installation and Structural Checks:**
*   **Mandatory:** Nameplate data and record for Packaged Equipment **must** match the outline drawing/data sheet information.
*   **Mandatory:** Nameplate tag for Packaged Equipment **must** match tag drawing.
*   **Mandatory:** Base of Packaged Equipment **must** be grouted, and [[equipment_grouting_requirements|grouting]] **must** be good.
*   **Mandatory:** Elevation height of Packaged Equipment **must** be consistent with the design.
*   **Mandatory:** All anchor bolts **must** be installed and tight for Packaged Equipment.
*   **Mandatory:** All anchor chair connections **must** be installed as per manufacturer information for Packaged Equipment.
*   **Mandatory:** Package **must** be accessible on all sides.
*   **Mandatory:** Manway **must** be accessible for Packaged Equipment.
*   **Mandatory:** Manway gasket material **must** be checked against the outline drawing for Packaged Equipment.
*   **Mandatory (if required):** Ladder **must** have an enclosed cage for Packaged Equipment.
*   **Mandatory:** Vessel ladder **must** be adequately supported and all bolts **must** be tight for Packaged Equipment.

**Surface Treatment and Lining:**
*   **Mandatory:** Carbon steel of Packaged Equipment **must** be coated per project specification.
*   **Mandatory:** Check for gouges, cuts, scratches etc. on Packaged Equipment paint.
*   **Mandatory:** [[coating_and_lining_inspection|Lining]] of Packaged Equipment **must** be continuous.
*   **Mandatory:** Lining inserts of Packaged Equipment **must** be sealed.
*   **Mandatory:** Lining seams of Packaged Equipment **must** be smooth and continuous.

**Grounding:**
*   **Mandatory:** [[grounding_installation_details|Packaged Equipment grounding cable]] connection **must** be tight.
*   **Mandatory:** Packaged Equipment grounding cable **must** be connected to the main grounding loop.

**Lubrication:**
*   **Mandatory (where required):** [[bearing_lubrication_and_inspection|Bearings]] of Packaged Equipment **must** be greased.
*   **Mandatory:** Gearbox oil filling for Packaged Equipment **must** follow the installation procedure.
*   **Mandatory:** Gearbox of Packaged Equipment **must** be full to the fill line.
*   **Mandatory:** Gearbox oil of Packaged Equipment **must** be the correct specification.

**Overflow, Vents, and Drains:**
*   **Mandatory:** Overflow nozzle **must** be connected and bolts **must** be tight for Packaged Equipment.
*   **Mandatory:** Overflow piping **must** be connected to ground or trench for Packaged Equipment.
*   **Mandatory:** Both vent and drain **must** be clear of debris and open for Packaged Equipment.
*   **Mandatory:** Screens **must** be provided where required for Packaged Equipment vents/drains.
*   **Mandatory:** Drain valve **must** be connected and lever **must** have unobstructed rotation for Packaged Equipment.
*   **Mandatory:** "See note" (under Overflow) is a requirement.

**Insulation:**
*   **Mandatory:** Tank [[insulation_design_and_specification_6_prc_0002|insulation material]] for Packaged Equipment **must** be checked against specification.
*   **Mandatory:** Insulation thickness for Packaged Equipment **must** be checked.
*   **Mandatory:** Insulation sheet **must** cover the surface, and all connections **must** be made for Packaged Equipment.

**Internal Components (Packing, Distributors, etc.):**
*   **Mandatory:** Support plates **must** be installed correctly and fit for Packaged Equipment.
*   **Mandatory (where material has been previously installed):** Packing, resin or [[converter_catalyst_details|catalyst]] level **must** be at the correct height and correct volume for Packaged Equipment.
*   **Mandatory:** If Packaged Equipment internals have not been installed, confirm they are on site in the correct quantities.
*   **Mandatory:** Distributor tray, piping and collectors **must** be installed and clean, free of debris for Packaged Equipment.
*   **Mandatory:** Dip legs, nozzle inserts, etc. **must** be installed as per outline drawing with correct [[gasket_material_selection|gasket material]] for Packaged Equipment.
*   **Mandatory:** Internal [[piping_design_and_specification_5_prc_0003|piping]] **must** be installed and all connections **must** be tight for Packaged Equipment.
*   **Mandatory:** [[piping_design_and_specification_5_prc_0003|Plastic piping]] **must** be cemented for Packaged Equipment.

**Nozzles and Flanges:**
*   **Mandatory:** Nozzle size and orientation **must** match drawing for Packaged Equipment.
*   **Mandatory:** Nozzle gasket and thickness **must** match outline drawing/data sheet for Packaged Equipment.
*   **Mandatory:** Nozzles **must** be open from the inside of vessel and no foreign debris **must** be present for Packaged Equipment.
*   **Mandatory:** Nozzle connection device (instrument, valve, etc) **must** be present for Packaged Equipment.
*   **Mandatory:** Valve flange **must** be aligned to piping for Packaged Equipment.
*   **Mandatory:** [[flange_bolting_requirements|Flange bolts]] **must** be adequately tightened for Packaged Equipment.
*   **Mandatory:** Flange bolts **must not** be overtightened (i.e. damage to gasket) for Packaged Equipment.
*   **Mandatory:** The nut **must** have at least three rounds of thread protruding past the nut for Packaged Equipment.

**Electrical and Instrumentation:**
*   **Mandatory:** Motor installation field check for Packaged Equipment is in the [[electrical_systems_field_checkout_9_prc_0004|electrical checklist]].
*   **Mandatory:** [[cabinet_air_purge_requirements|Air purge to cabinets]] **must** be checked for Packaged Equipment.
*   **Mandatory:** [[electrical_installation_details|Electrical connections]] **must** be checked and [[electrical_power_specifications|power supply]] confirmed for Packaged Equipment.
*   **Mandatory:** [[instrumentation_and_control_design_criteria_8_prc_0002|Instrumentation]] **must** match the manufacturer’s documentation for Packaged Equipment.

**Final Cleaning:**
*   **Mandatory:** Vessel **must** be [[water_and_air_trials_1_prc_0006|flushed]], walls clean and free of debris for Packaged Equipment.
*   **Mandatory:** Carbon steel tanks should be cleaned of rust for Packaged Equipment.
