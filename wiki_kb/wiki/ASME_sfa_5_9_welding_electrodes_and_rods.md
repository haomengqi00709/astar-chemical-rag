# ASME SFA-5.9 Specification for Corrosion Resisting Chromium and Chromium-Nickel Steel Bare and Composite Metal Cored and Stranded Arc-Welding Electrodes and Welding Rods

ASME SFA-5.9 is a specification that sets forth the requirements for corrosion-resisting chromium and chromium-nickel steel bare and composite metal cored and stranded arc-welding electrodes and welding rods. It covers aspects such as classification, chemical composition, and manufacturing standards.

This specification is referenced in [[4-SPC-0014_referenced_codes_and_definitions]] and specifically in [[4-SPC-0014_welding_processes_and_parameters]] for the selection and use of Gas Tungsten Arc Welding (GTAW) rods for Columbium Stabilized High Nickel Austenitic stainless steel fabrications. GTAW rods used for these applications shall satisfy the requirements of ASME SFA-5.9.

For comprehensive details on welding electrodes and rods, consult the latest edition of ASME SFA-5.9.
