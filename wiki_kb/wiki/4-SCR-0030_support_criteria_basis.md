# Support Criteria Basis for Plate & Frame Heat Exchanger (4-SCR-0030)

This page defines the fundamental basis for applying loads and referencing design documentation for the Plate & Frame Heat Exchanger support criteria, as specified in document 4-SCR-0030.

**Load Application:**
*   **Loads are to be applied at Datum.** This is a **mandatory** requirement, indicating that all specified unfactored equipment loads must be considered as acting at the designated datum point on the equipment or its support structure. This ensures consistency in structural analysis.

**Basis for Support Criteria Documentation:**
The detailed support criteria are derived from and referenced by specific engineering drawings:
*   **Equipment Drawing Number:** The primary drawing number that provides the comprehensive design and layout of the equipment, including its support points.
*   **Revision:** The specific revision of the Equipment Drawing Number that forms the basis for these support criteria.

For a broader understanding of equipment support criteria, refer to [[equipment_support_criteria]]. General equipment design criteria are outlined in [[4-prc-0002_equipment_design_criteria]].
