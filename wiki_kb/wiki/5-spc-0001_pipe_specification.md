# Piping Specification (5-SPC-0001)

This document, 5-SPC-0001, outlines the general requirements for piping systems, ensuring compliance with industry standards and internal specifications.

**General Requirements**
Piping systems **shall** conform to the requirements of [[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping]], latest edition. Materials used in piping construction **shall** be as defined in the listed specifications found in Section 4 of this document.

Nominal metric equivalent pipe diameters for ASME standard pipe sizes are detailed in Section 3. Data regarding ASME Nominal Pipe Size – Metric vs Imperial is available in [[5-SPC-0001_table_3]]. The comprehensive Pipe Specification List is provided in [[5-SPC-0001_table_4]].
