# Agitator Equipment Specifications (4-PDS-XXXX-R2)

This page details the specific equipment and performance specifications for agitators as outlined in the Agitator Process Data Sheet (document ID [[4-PDS-XXXX-R2_agitator_datasheet_administration]]). These specifications are essential for the mechanical design, procurement, and installation of agitator units.

**Quantity and Identification:**
*   **Quantity Field:** The datasheet includes fields for specifying the number of agitators required, differentiating between:
    *   Operating units
    *   Installed Spare units
*   **Tag No.:** Each agitator unit is assigned a unique Tag No. as a primary design parameter for identification and tracking.

**Equipment Type:**
Agitators are categorized by their mounting configuration:
*   **Portable:** Easily moved and repositioned.
*   **Top Entering:** Mounted on the top of the vessel, common for various mixing applications.
*   **Side Entering:** Mounted on the side of the vessel, often used for large tanks or specific mixing patterns.

**Impeller Type:**
The choice of impeller type is critical for achieving the desired mixing action and efficiency. Common types include:
*   **Marine Propeller:** Efficient for high-flow, low-shear applications.
*   **Flat-Blade:** Versatile, provides good radial flow and shear.
*   **Curved-Blade:** Offers improved flow patterns and reduced power consumption compared to flat blades in some applications.
*   **Pitched-Blade:** Generates axial flow, suitable for suspension and heat transfer.
*   **Hydrofoil:** High-efficiency impellers for pumping and blending.
*   **Other:** For specialized or proprietary impeller designs.

**Impeller and Performance Parameters:**
*   **Number of Impellers:** A design parameter indicating how many impellers are on the shaft.
*   **Impeller Diameter:** Measured in mm, directly impacts mixing volume and power draw.
*   **Impeller Speed:** Measured in rpm, determines the shear rate and pumping capacity.
*   **Pumping Rate:** Measured in m3/h, quantifies the volume of fluid moved by the impeller.
*   **Shear Rate:** Measured in s-1, indicates the intensity of mixing and particle dispersion.
*   **Power/Volume:** Measured in W/m3, represents the energy input per unit volume, a key metric for mixing efficiency.
*   **Volume Agitated:** Measured in m3, the total volume of material the agitator is designed to mix.
*   **Max Mixing Time:** Measured in min, the maximum allowable time to achieve a specified level of homogeneity.

These specifications ensure that the agitator is appropriately selected and designed to meet the process requirements. For further details on equipment design and general requirements, refer to [[equipment_data_sheets_4_dst_xxxx]] and [[general_equipment_requirements_specification]].
