# Dry Tower Packing Details

This page details the packing specifications and performance parameters for the Dry Tower (Tag No. 7506U). The tower operates in counter-current mode, with H2O being the gas absorbed.

*   **Packing is 3 in. Ceramic Intalox Saddles.**
