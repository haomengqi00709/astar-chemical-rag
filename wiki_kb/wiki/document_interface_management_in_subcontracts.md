# Document Interface Management in Subcontracts

Effective management of interfaces between different work scopes is crucial for successful project execution, particularly within construction subcontracts. Clear and unambiguous documentation of these interfaces is paramount to prevent misunderstandings, scope gaps, or overlaps that could lead to delays, cost overruns, or quality issues. When any project document defines or implies an interface between work performed by a Subcontractor and work performed by other parties (e.g., Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, the Owner, or other subcontractors), specific requirements **must** be met to ensure clarity and prevent scope gaps or overlaps.

### Interface Documentation Requirements

*   **Clear Location Indication**: Any document containing an interface **shall** clearly show the precise physical or logical location of that interface. This ensures that all involved parties understand the exact boundary and points of interaction between their respective responsibilities, facilitating coordination and handover.
*   **Scope Delineation**: The document **must** explicitly indicate which specific portion of the interface work will be performed by the Subcontractor. This clear delineation prevents ambiguity regarding responsibility for interface-related tasks, such as connections, testing, or final tie-ins.

These requirements apply to all drawings, specifications, and other documents applicable to the subcontract, which are typically listed on an accompanying transmittal and referenced by Section 2.2 of the [[construction_subcontract_agreement_procedure_0_prc_6002|Construction Subcontract Agreement (0-PRC-6002)]]. Adhering to these guidelines is essential for minimizing disputes, ensuring seamless integration of work packages, and maintaining overall project quality and schedule.

---
If any document contains an interface between work done by the Subcontractor and work done by others, the document should clearly show:
*   The location of the interface.
*   Which portion of the interface work will be done by the Subcontractor.
