# FRP Tank Filament Winding Pattern Inspection (0-QDS-7414)

This page details the specific requirements for the filament winding pattern inspection for FRP Tanks, as mandated by Quality Data Sheet **0-QDS-7414**. This inspection is crucial for ensuring the structural integrity and quality of the FRP tank construction.

**Filament Winding Pattern:**
*   The **Filament winding pattern** is a mandatory requirement and **must** be included in the Vendor's Inspection and Test (I & T) Plan. This ensures that the winding process, which dictates the strength and durability of the FRP tank, is properly documented and controlled.
*   The **Filament winding pattern** requires **Chemetics Witness** surveillance. This means that a Chemetics representative **must** be present to observe and verify the filament winding process and its adherence to the approved pattern.

This specific inspection point highlights the importance of the manufacturing process for FRP tanks, where the winding pattern directly impacts the performance characteristics of the final product.
