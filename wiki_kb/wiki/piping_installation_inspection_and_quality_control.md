# Piping Installation Inspection and Quality Control

Thorough inspection and quality control during piping installation are paramount to ensure that all systems conform to design specifications, maintain structural integrity, and operate safely.

**Welded Joint Inspection:**
All welded joints **will** be inspected in accordance with the appropriate fabrication specification. This ensures that welds meet required strength, quality, and non-destructive examination standards.

**Conformity to Drawings and Specifications:**
Each line **will** be inspected by Kvaerner Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' field representative to verify conformity to the approved drawings and specifications. This comprehensive check covers all aspects of the installation.

**Specific Inspection Points:**
Special attention during inspection **will** be given to:
*   **Workmanship:** Ensuring the overall quality and adherence to best practices in installation.
*   **Gasket Location:** Verifying that gaskets are correctly positioned to ensure leak-tight seals.
*   **Bolt Torque:** Confirming that all bolts are tightened to the specified torque values to prevent leaks and structural failures.
*   **Removable Sections:** Sections of pipe at locations such as pump inlets and discharges **will** be inspected to ensure no excessive cold spring or force is transmitted to the equipment, which could lead to premature wear or failure.
*   **Expansion Joints:** Expansion joints **will** be inspected to ensure their cold position is within the allowable value, allowing for proper thermal expansion and contraction during operation.

---
**Line Inspection**
Each line **will** be inspected by Kvaerner Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' field representative for conformity to drawings and specifications. Special attention during inspection **will** be given to workmanship, gasket location, and bolt torque.

**Equipment and Expansion Joint Inspection**
Removable sections of pipe at locations such as a pump inlet and discharge **will** be inspected to ensure no excessive cold spring or force is transmitted to the equipment. Expansion joints **will** be inspected to ensure the cold position is within the allowable value.
