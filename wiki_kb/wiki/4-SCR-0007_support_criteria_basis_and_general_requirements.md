# Skirt Supported Vessel - Support Criteria Basis and General Requirements (4-SCR-0007)

This page outlines the fundamental basis and general requirements for the support criteria of the skirt-supported vessel on a concrete pad, as detailed in document [[4-SCR-0007_document_administration]]. Adherence to these guidelines is mandatory for the structural design and installation.

**Basis for Support Criteria:**
The support criteria are based on the following equipment drawings:
*   Equipment Drawing Number **4-OTL-XXXX**, Revision X.
*   Equipment Drawing Number **4-DTL-XXXX**, Revision X.

**General Requirements:**
*   **Loads Application:** All specified loads, including [[4-SCR-0007_unfactored_equipment_loads]] and [[4-SCR-0007_unfactored_duct_loads]], **must** be applied at Datum.
*   **Minimum Bolt to Edge Distance:** A minimum "Bolt to Edge" distance of 150 mm **must** be maintained. This is critical for ensuring adequate concrete strength around the anchor bolts.
*   **Grout Width:** The width of the grout **must** be suitable to accommodate the equipment base ring. This ensures proper load transfer and stability.

These requirements are integral to the [[equipment_support_criteria]] and overall [[civil_works_design_criteria_2_prc_0001]] for the project.
