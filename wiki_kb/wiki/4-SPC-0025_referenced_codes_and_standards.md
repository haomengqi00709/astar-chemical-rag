# Referenced Codes and Standards for High Silicon Austenitic Stainless Steel Welding (4-SPC-0025)

The following codes and standards are referenced within Specification 4-SPC-0025 and **shall** be interpreted as the latest edition ruling at the date of contract:

*   **ASME Boiler & Pressure Vessel Codes:** General reference for pressure-related equipment.
*   **ASME Boiler and Pressure Vessel Code Case #1953:** Specific code case applicable to certain aspects of pressure vessel design or fabrication.
*   **[[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels]]**: Unfired Pressure Vessels. This section provides rules for the design, fabrication, inspection, testing, and certification of unfired pressure vessels.
*   **[[ASME_section_ix_welding_and_brazing_qualifications]]**: Welding & Brazing Qualifications. This section provides rules relating to the qualification of welders, welding operators, brazers, and brazing operators, and the procedures that they employ in welding and brazing.
*   **[[ASME_bpvc_section_ii_materials]]**: Materials. This section covers material specifications for ferrous and nonferrous materials.
*   **[[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping]]**: Chemical Plant and Petroleum Refinery Piping. This code prescribes requirements for the design, materials, fabrication, erection, examination, inspection, and testing of piping systems.
*   **DIN 1912 - Welding:** A German standard related to welding practices.
*   **ASME Code, Section VII/I:** This code is referenced.
*   **ASTM A262 Practice "C"**: Standard practices for detecting susceptibility to intergranular attack in austenitic stainless steels.
