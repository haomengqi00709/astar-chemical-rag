# Skirt Supported Vessel on Steel: Unfactored Equipment Loads (4-SCR-0008)

This document, **4-SCR-0008**, specifies the unfactored loads for a skirt-supported vessel on steel. These loads are critical inputs for structural design and analysis, ensuring the vessel's stability and integrity under various conditions.

The unfactored equipment loads include:
*   **Empty - Maximum Mass:** The maximum mass of the equipment when empty, measured in kilograms (kg).
*   **Flooded (Design) - Maximum Mass:** The maximum mass of the equipment when flooded to its design capacity, measured in kilograms (kg).
*   **Operating - Maximum Mass:** The maximum mass of the equipment during normal operation, measured in kilograms (kg).
*   **Wind Base Shear:** The horizontal force exerted by wind at the base of the equipment, measured in kilonewtons (kN).
*   **Wind Base Moment:** The rotational force (moment) exerted by wind at the base of the equipment, measured in kilonewton-meters (kN-m).
*   **Seismic Base Shear:** The horizontal force exerted by seismic activity at the base of the equipment, measured in kilonewtons (kN).
*   **Seismic Base Moment:** The rotational force (moment) exerted by seismic activity at the base of the equipment, measured in kilonewton-meters (kN-m).

These loads are used in conjunction with applicable design codes and safety factors to determine the final design loads. For general information on equipment loads, refer to [[unfactored_equipment_loads]]. Specific design considerations for wind and seismic forces are detailed in [[wind_design_requirements]] and [[seismic_design_requirements]], respectively.
