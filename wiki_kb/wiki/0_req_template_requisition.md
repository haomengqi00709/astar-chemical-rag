# Template Requisition (0-REQ-)

This page describes the standard template used for creating Requisitions.

The Requisition form, identified as 0-REQ-, serves as the foundational document for initiating a Request for Quotation (RFQ) or a Purchase Order (PO). It is designed to capture all necessary details regarding the Scope of Supply and Documentation requirements.

The requisitioner **must** utilize this template and clearly indicate whether the Requisition is for an RFQ or a PO. The structure and content of this template are designed to facilitate direct transfer of information to subsequent procurement documents, ensuring consistency.

For details on the content requirements for this template, refer to [[0_prc_5002_requisition_content_and_structure]].
