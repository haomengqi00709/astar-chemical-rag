# Piping Installation Specification (5-SPC-0104)

This specification, Document ID 5-SPC-0104, defines the mandatory requirements for the installation of field-erected piping. It applies to all piping installed by the Piping Subcontractor.

**Applicable Codes and Conflict Resolution**
Piping installations **must** satisfy the requirements of [[ASME_b31_1_power_piping]] or [[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping]] as applicable. In cases of conflict or doubt between Kvaerner Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' documents and ASME codes, the issue **shall** be referred to Kvaerner Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' representative for interpretation. Where welding or fabrication is undertaken in the field as part of pipe erection, [[kvaerner_aker_kvaerner_chemetics_welding_standards_section_6_5]] **shall** apply.

**Piping Documentation**
Kvaerner Aker Kvaerner Chemetics will provide the Piping Subcontractor with detailed piping isometrics for each line, which include overall dimensions, bill of materials, and pipe support references. However, detailed isometrics may not be provided for some small diameter field-run piping. The Piping Subcontractor is responsible for all field adjustments necessary to accommodate equipment and field installation tolerances, as dimensional information on isometrics is nominal.

[[piping_general_arrangement_drawings]] will also be provided, intended to show the general location of piping and supports only. These may or may not be updated to reflect the final isometrics. In cases of conflict, dimensional information shown on the piping isometrics **will** govern.

Additionally, [[piping_and_instrumentation_drawings]] (P&IDs) and a [[piping_line_list]] will be provided. These documents include references for [[pipe_specification|pipe material specifications]], pressure test methods and pressure, insulation, and painting requirements.
