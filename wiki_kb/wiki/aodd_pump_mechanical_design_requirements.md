# AODD Pump Mechanical Design Requirements

This page outlines the mechanical design requirements for the Air Operated Double Diaphragm (AODD) Pump, as specified in document 4-DST-XXXX-R1. These requirements ensure the pump's structural integrity, operational reliability, and compliance with relevant industry standards and site-specific conditions.

**Equipment Data:**
*   **Pump Type:** Air Operated Double Diaphragm. This specifies the fundamental operating principle of the pump.

**Mechanical Design Requirements:**
The following criteria govern the mechanical design and construction of the AODD pump:

*   **Design Maximum Suction Pressure:** The maximum pressure the pump's suction side is designed to withstand, measured in kPa(g) (kilopascals gauge). This is critical for ensuring the pump casing and connections can safely handle system pressures.
*   **Design Maximum Temperature:** The maximum temperature the pump is designed to operate at, measured in oC. This influences the selection of materials of construction and seals to prevent degradation or failure.
*   **Equipment Specification:** Refers to a broader document or standard that provides detailed technical requirements for the equipment.
*   **General Equipment Requirements:** Outlines overarching requirements applicable to all equipment, such as quality, documentation, and testing. These often align with [[general_mechanical_design_requirements_0_spc_0010]].
*   **Design Code:** The specific engineering code or standard (e.g., ASME, API) that the pump design must comply with. This ensures adherence to recognized safety and performance standards.
*   **Site Conditions:** Environmental and operational conditions specific to the installation site, such as ambient temperature, humidity, and seismic requirements. These are typically detailed in [[site_design_conditions_1_dst_0001]].
*   **Connection Type:** Specifies the type and size of process connections (e.g., flanged, threaded) for integration into the piping system.
*   **Electrical Area Classification:** Defines the hazardous area classification (e.g., Class I, Division 1) where the pump will be installed. This dictates the type of electrical components and wiring required to prevent ignition of flammable atmospheres. Refer to [[hazardous_area_classification_requirements]] for more details.
*   **Painting:** Specifies the painting system and finish requirements for corrosion protection and aesthetic purposes. Further details can be found in [[coatings_design_and_specification_7_prc_0002]].
*   **Dry Running:** Indicates whether the pump is capable of operating without fluid for a period without damage. This is an important consideration for intermittent operations or process upsets.
*   **Total Noise Limit:** The maximum permissible noise level generated by the pump during operation, typically measured in dB(A). This is important for occupational health and safety.
*   **Vibration:** Specifies acceptable vibration limits for the pump to ensure smooth operation and prevent premature wear or structural damage.

Adherence to these mechanical design requirements is paramount for the safe, reliable, and long-term performance of the AODD pump in its intended application.
