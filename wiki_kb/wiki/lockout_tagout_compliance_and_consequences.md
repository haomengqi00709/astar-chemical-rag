# Lockout and Tagout Compliance and Consequences

This page details the disciplinary actions for non-compliance and the process for deviations from the [[0-PRC-8504-RA_lockout_and_tagging|Lockout and Tagout Procedure]].

**Disciplinary Action**
*   Disciplinary action for failure to follow proper lockout procedures **will be severe**.
*   Any person who operates a switch or device to which "DANGER" tags and/or locks are attached without authorization **will be subject to immediate termination**.
*   Any person who removes a tag or lock without authorization **will be subject to immediate termination**.
*   Any person who works on power driven equipment without following this procedure **will be subject to immediate termination**.

**Deviations**
*   Prior written approval from the Site Safety Manager **will be the only acceptable way** to deviate from this procedure.
