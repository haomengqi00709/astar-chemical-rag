# Instrumentation Design and Specification Roles and Responsibilities

This page outlines the key roles and their responsibilities within the [[8-prc-0003_field_instrument_design_and_specification_procedure]].

**Field Instrument Engineer (FIE)**
The FIE is responsible for:
*   Project Field Instrument Database and Specifications.
*   Creating and maintaining a current project drawing list for field instruments.
*   Updating the project drawing list and returning it to the Project Engineer.
*   Updating the project drawing list on a regular basis as required.
*   Assignment of client's Instrument Tag/Loop numbers on the [[8-lst-0002_instrument_index]] when required by contract.
*   Cross-referencing client Instrument Tag/Loop numbers to standard numbers as per Stage III P&ID’s.
*   Maintaining the Instrument Tag/Loop number cross-reference for the duration of the project.
*   All Instrument Tag updates to the Instrument Index.
*   Managing dual numbering requirements as per project contract documents.
*   Producing preliminary [[8-lst-01xx_instrument_process_data_lists]].
*   Transferring detailed information from the Final Instrument Process Data Lists to the [[8-dst-xxxx_instrument_specification_sheets]].
*   Using pipe specifications and the [[5-lst-0011_standard_ansi_piping_connections_for_instrumentation]] in the design of field instruments.
*   Immediately informing the Instrument Engineer of any variances where instruments cannot mate directly to standard connection sizes.
*   Creating [[8-dst-xxxx_instrument_specification_sheets]] for field-provided instrumentation (not for instruments with mechanical packages).
*   Producing [[8-cal-xxxx_instrument_sizing_calculations]] for specified instrument types.
*   Reviewing calculation sheets with Process as required.
*   Compiling and incorporating calculations into the final [[8-man-xxxx_project_instrument_manuals]].
*   Providing the [[8-lst-0010_instrument_dimensional_details]] spreadsheet.
*   Providing purchase orders for spare parts as per contractual documents.
*   Determining spare parts requirements.
*   Providing all field instrument requisitions.
*   Maintaining technical responsibility for the specification of field instruments.
*   Providing a "[[8-vdr-8xxx_instrument_vendor_document_requirements]]" form (VDR) for each instrument requisition.
*   Providing a "[[8-qds-8xxx_instrument_quality_data_sheet]]" form (QDS) for each instrument type.
*   Providing the preliminary cost tabulation on 0-TAB-8XXX-1 for each requisition.
*   Tabulating all instruments with their respective Instrument Tag Number and showing the cost.
*   Verifying uncertain cost code assignments with Instrumentation prior to publication of the cost tabulation.
*   Completing the pricing portion of the tabulation if only technical information is provided.
*   Providing the final cost tabulation on 0-TAB-8XXX-1 for each requisition.
*   Propagating "0-TAB-8XXX-3: Commercial Bid Tabulation" with appropriate Instrument Tag references for commercial evaluation.
*   Informing the Project Buyer that the commercial tabulation is ready.
*   Confirming with the Project Engineer that the Financial Evaluation for the proposed supplier is complete and acceptable.
*   Conducting a detailed technical evaluation of bids, documented in "0-TAB-8XXX-2: Technical Bid Tabulation".
*   Coordinating with various engineering disciplines to complete the Technical Evaluation.
*   Completing the evaluation document and obtaining digital signatures.
*   Initiating the Risk Assessment for requisitions greater than CAD 10k.
*   Interfacing directly with the Project Engineer and/or Project Manager for Risk Assessment approval signatures.
*   Advising the Instrument Engineer on completion of all bid evaluation tab forms.
*   Participating in vendor selection meetings as required.
*   Updating [[8-req-8xxx_instrument_requisition_for_quotation_and_purchase]] with vendor details and publishing the final document.
*   Advising the Buyer that the Requisition is complete and ready for purchase.
*   Ensuring vendor documents are processed as per [[review_of_vendor_documentation_0_prc_0007]].
*   Returning signed, marked-up drawings to Document Control.
*   Preparing one hard copy and an equivalent electronic version (CD) of Field Instrument Manuals.
*   Coordinating with the Instrumentation Department to ensure consistency in project manuals.

**Detail Design Engineer (DDE)**
The DDE is responsible for:
*   Preparing [[instrumentation_and_control_design_criteria_8_prc_0002]] (8-PRC-0002).
*   Preparing [[plant_instrument_numbering_8_prc_0001]] (8-PRC-0001).
*   Revising Standard-8-PRC-0001 to include Client numbering procedures if required.
*   Creating and maintaining a current project drawing list for Detail Design Drawings.
*   Updating and maintaining "0-LST-0001: Document List".
*   Providing necessary adapters on [[instrument_installation_details_8_dtl_xxxx]] if FIE cannot provide instruments mating directly to standard connection sizes.
*   Providing [[8-gad-00xx_instrument_location_drawings]].
*   Inputting all other instruments and Junction Boxes into the PDMS model.
*   Providing [[8-dtl-xxxx_instrument_junction_box_drawings]].
*   Determining the optimum number of Instrument Junction Boxes required.
*   Providing [[8-lop-xxxx_instrument_loop_diagrams]].
*   Determining DCS contractual requirements from contract package documents.
*   Providing the [[8-lst-0004_instrument_cable_schedule]].
*   Providing [[8-dtl-xxxx_instrument_block_diagrams]].
*   Providing [[8-dtl-xxxx_instrument_installation_details]].
*   Providing the [[instrument_installation_specification_8_spc_0002]].
*   Providing the [[0-lst-6x90-1_construction_subcontract_supply_and_erection_scope_instrumentation]].
*   Providing support for on-site testing and commissioning as directed by Site Supervisor.
*   Providing the project [[8-spc-0133_instrument_testing_requirements]] using typical examples and expertise.
*   Providing support for on-site [[8-lst-0101_field_instrumentation_checkout]].
*   Providing all Instrument Bulk Material requisitions.
*   Maintaining technical responsibility for the specification of Instrument Bulk Material.
*   Providing Individual VDR forms and QDS forms as required with each item ordered.
*   Providing a "[[8-vdr-8xxx_instrument_vendor_document_requirements]]" form (VDR) for each instrument type (for Instrument Bulk Material).
*   Providing a "[[8-qds-8xxx_instrument_quality_data_sheet]]" form (QDS) for Instrument Bulk Material.
*   Providing the preliminary cost tabulation on 0-TAB-8XXX-1 for each requisition (Instrument Bulk Material).
*   Tabulating all items with reference to their respective Instrument Tag Numbers and showing the cost.
*   Verifying uncertain cost code assignments with Instrumentation prior to publication of the cost tabulation.
*   Providing the final cost tabulation on 0-TAB-8XXX-1 for each requisition (Instrument Bulk Material).
*   Propagating "0-TAB-8XXX-3: Commercial Bid Tabulation" with appropriate items and Instrument Tag Numbers for commercial evaluation.
*   Informing the Project Buyer that the commercial tabulation is ready.
*   Confirming with the Project Engineer that the Financial Evaluation for the proposed supplier is complete and acceptable.
*   Conducting a detailed technical evaluation of bids, documented in "0-TAB-8XXX-2: Technical Bid Tabulation".
*   Coordinating with various engineering disciplines to complete the Technical Evaluation.
*   Completing the evaluation document and obtaining digital signatures.
*   Initiating the Risk Assessment for requisitions greater than CAD 10k.
*   Interfacing directly with the Project Engineer and/or Project Manager for Risk Assessment approval signatures.
*   Advising the Instrument Engineer on completion of all bid evaluation tab forms.
*   Participating in vendor selection meetings as required.
*   Updating [[8-req-8xxx_instrument_requisition_for_quotation_and_purchase]] with vendor details and publishing the final document.
*   Advising the Buyer that the Requisition is complete and ready for purchase.
*   Ensuring vendor documents are processed as per [[review_of_vendor_documentation_0_prc_0007]].
*   Returning signed, marked-up drawings to Document Control.
*   Reviewing Mechanical package vendor drawings.
*   Ensuring Package Instrumentation Design is complete and meets project needs.
*   Preparing Instrument Bulk Material Manuals.
*   Coordinating with the Instrumentation Department to ensure consistency in project manuals.
*   Ensuring the Installation Contractor maintains clear, concise As-Built mark-ups of engineering drawings.
*   Ensuring the Installation Contractor provides As-Built mark-ups to the Site Supervisor in a timely fashion.
*   Maintaining an additional set of As-Built mark-ups as per plant commissioning.
*   Providing the additional set of As-Builts to the Site Supervisor subsequent to Mechanical Completion.
*   Preparing [[8-dtl-xxxx_as_built_instrumentation_documents]].
*   Approving [[8-dtl-xxxx_as_built_instrumentation_documents]].

**Project Engineer**
The Project Engineer is responsible for:
*   Checking [[instrumentation_and_control_design_criteria_8_prc_0002]] (8-PRC-0002).
*   Approving [[instrumentation_and_control_design_criteria_8_prc_0002]] (8-PRC-0002).
*   Checking [[plant_instrument_numbering_8_prc_0001]] (8-PRC-0001).
*   Approving [[plant_instrument_numbering_8_prc_0001]] (8-PRC-0001).
*   Providing an opportunity for the FIE to review and comment on the Instrument Design Criteria prior to final publication.
*   Checking [[0-lst-0001_project_drawing_lists_instrumentation]].
*   Approving [[0-lst-0001_project_drawing_lists_instrumentation]].
*   Updating and maintaining the project file accordingly based on FIE updates to the drawing list.
*   Coordinating critical pathing, scheduling, and expediting activities for long lead instruments requiring hydraulic calculations with FIE and Design Office input.
*   Checking [[0-lst-6x90-1_construction_subcontract_supply_and_erection_scope_instrumentation]].
*   Approving [[0-lst-6x90-1_construction_subcontract_supply_and_erection_scope_instrumentation]].
*   Approving Preliminary Cost Tabulation (< CAD 200k) for Field Instruments.
*   Approving Preliminary Cost Tabulation (> CAD 200k) for Field Instruments.
*   Approving Risk Assessment (< CAD 200k) for Field Instruments.
*   Approving Preliminary Cost Tabulation (< CAD 200k) for Instrument Bulk Material.
*   Approving Preliminary Cost Tabulation (> CAD 200k) for Instrument Bulk Material.
*   Approving Risk Assessment (< CAD 200k) for Instrument Bulk Material.
*   Checking [[8-por-8xxx_instrument_purchase_order_authorization]] (8-POR-8XXX).
*   Identifying and sequencing all disciplines required to review vendor document packages.
*   Forwarding two hard copies of vendor documents to the Project Engineer.

**Instrument Engineer**
The Instrument Engineer is responsible for:
*   Approving [[instrumentation_and_control_design_criteria_8_prc_0002]] (8-PRC-0002).
*   Approving [[plant_instrument_numbering_8_prc_0001]] (8-PRC-0001).
*   Immediately informing the DDE of any variances where FIE is unable to provide instruments that mate directly to standard connection sizes.
*   Advising the FIE on completion of all bid evaluation tab forms.
*   Coordinating through the Project Engineer to meet with appropriate parties to select the successful Vendor.
*   Advising the FIE in writing of the successful Vendor.
*   Advising the DDE in writing of the successful Vendor.
*   Coordinating with the FIE to update the Instrument Index with Installation Detail cross-referencing.
*   Coordinating with the DDE to ensure consistency is maintained with respect to all project manuals.

**Process Department / Process Engineer / Process Lead**
The Process Department / Engineer / Lead is responsible for:
*   Assigning standard Instrument Tag/Loop numbers on Stage I through Stage III P&ID’s.
*   Performing hydraulic calculations.
*   Incorporating results of Hydraulic Calculations in the Process Data List (PDL) as required.
*   Revisions to the PDL once the Instrument Process Data List – Preliminary has been published.
*   Completing and publishing the final [[8-lst-01xx_instrument_process_data_lists]].
*   Compiling and including required process information and Instrument Hydraulic information in the PDL.
*   Ensuring PDL’s are maintained in a current state and relate to the current issue of the P&IDs.
*   Coordinating with Mechanical and Design Office to ensure PDL data is complete.
*   Filling in all fields in the PDL where 3 basis of information (e.g., Minimum/Normal/Maximum) are allowed.
*   Entering N/A if information is not applicable in PDL fields.
*   Entering equivalent data in both fields if information is duplicated between basis in PDL.
*   Checking the input of data as per the Process Data Lists and the Site Design Conditions for Instrument Specification Sheets.
*   Checking [[8-dst-xxxx_instrument_specification_sheets]].

**Quality Assurance**
Quality Assurance is responsible for:
*   Checking [[8-vdr-8xxx_instrument_vendor_document_requirements]] (Field Instruments).
*   Approving [[8-qds-8xxx_instrument_quality_data_sheet]] (Field Instruments).
*   Checking [[8-vdr-8xxx_instrument_vendor_document_requirements]] (Instrument Bulk Material).
*   Approving [[8-qds-8xxx_instrument_quality_data_sheet]] (Instrument Bulk Material).

**Procurement / Project Buyer / Manager of Procurement**
Procurement / Project Buyer / Manager of Procurement is responsible for:
*   Preparing [[0-prc-5005_request_for_quotation_process_instrumentation]] (RFQ) (8-REQ-8XXX).
*   Producing the formal RFQ package after advisement from FIE that documents are ready for issue.
*   All Request for Quotation communications to potential suppliers.
*   Placing copies of critical communications in the Purchase File.
*   Preparing Commercial Evaluation (0-TAB-8XXX-3) for Field Instruments.
*   Approving Commercial Evaluation (0-TAB-8XXX-3) for Field Instruments (Manager of Procurement).
*   Preparing Commercial Evaluation (0-TAB-8XXX-3) for Instrument Bulk Material.
*   Approving Commercial Evaluation (0-TAB-8XXX-3) for Instrument Bulk Material (Manager of Procurement).
*   Processing the purchase order upon advisement that the Requisition is complete and ready for purchase.
*   Issuing a [[8-por-8xxx_instrument_purchase_order_authorization]] (8-POR-8XXX) upon receipt of approved Requisition, Cost Tabulation, and Order Risk Assessment (for orders > CAD 10k).
*   Preparing [[8-por-8xxx_instrument_purchase_order_authorization]] (8-POR-8XXX).

**Finance**
Finance is responsible for:
*   Preparing Financial Evaluation for Field Instruments.
*   Preparing Financial Evaluation for Instrument Bulk Material.

**Project Manager**
The Project Manager is responsible for:
*   Approving Final Cost Tabulation – Field Instruments.
*   Approving Risk Assessment (> CAD 200k) – Field Instruments.
*   Approving Final Cost Tabulation – Instrument Bulk Material.
*   Approving Risk Assessment (> CAD 200k) – Instrument Bulk Material.
*   Approving [[8-por-8xxx_instrument_purchase_order_authorization]] (8-POR-8XXX).

**Director of Projects**
The Director of Projects is responsible for authorizing Purchase Orders < CAD 2,000,000.

**President**
The President is responsible for authorizing Purchase Orders > CAD 2,000,000.

**Site Supervisor**
The Site Supervisor directs DDE support for on-site testing and commissioning.

**Design Office**
The Design Office is responsible for:
*   Checking [[8-gad-00xx_instrument_location_drawings]].
*   Updating the [[p_id_development_and_approval_1_prc_0003]] with client Instrument Tag Numbers.
*   Inputting all in-line type instruments into the PDMS model.
*   Inputting all vessel trim instruments into the PDMS model.

**Electrical**
Electrical will specify all required cable, cable connectors, conduit, and cable tray based on the [[8-lst-0004_instrument_cable_schedule]].

**Document Control**
Document Control is responsible for:
*   Receiving and logging vendor documents.
*   Providing the FIE/DDE with a copy of logged Vendor Documents.
*   Providing the Project Engineer with two hard copies of vendor documents.
*   Receiving signed, marked-up drawings from the FIE/DDE.

**Instrumentation Department**
The Instrumentation Department will:
*   Co-ordinate with the FIE to ensure consistency is maintained with respect to all project manuals.
*   Co-ordinate with the DDE to ensure consistency is maintained with respect to all project manuals.
*   Provide the DDE with typical [[8-spc-0133_instrument_testing_requirements]].
