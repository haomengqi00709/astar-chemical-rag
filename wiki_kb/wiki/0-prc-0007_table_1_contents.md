# 0-PRC-0007 Table 1 Contents - Review Scope Guidelines

Table 1, appended to [[0-prc-0007_document_overview|Document 0-PRC-0007: Vendor Document Control Procedure]], provides essential guidelines for the scopes of reviews for different classes of equipment. This table is a critical reference during the [[vendor_document_review_process|vendor document review process]], helping Discipline Leads understand the specific technical and quality aspects to focus on for various types of vendor-supplied equipment and materials. It ensures that reviews are comprehensive and tailored to the nature of the goods being supplied.
