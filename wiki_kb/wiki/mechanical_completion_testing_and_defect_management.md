# Mechanical Completion Testing and Defect Management

Mechanical completion testing represents the final series of inspections and tests designed to verify that the plant has been constructed in full accordance with the design specifications and is ready to commence commissioning.

**Inspections and Tests for Mechanical Completion**
These inspections and tests are typically conducted by the commissioning team, operating under the direct supervision of the Commissioning Manager. The procedures followed for these critical checks include:
*   [[mechanical_checkout_equipment_4_prc_0004]]: Mechanical Checkout Equipment (4-PRC-0004).
*   [[mechanical_checkout_piping_5_prc_0007]]: Mechanical Checkout Piping (5-PRC-0007).
*   [[instrument_systems_field_checkout_8_prc_0005]]: Instrument Systems Field Checkout (8-PRC-0005).
*   [[dcs_control_function_checkout_8_prc_0006]]: DCS Control Function Checkout (8-PRC-0006).
*   [[instrument_approval_tag_8_prc_0011]]: Instrument Approval Tag (8-PRC-0011).
*   [[dcs_field_hardware_checkout_8_prc_0014]]: DCS Field Hardware Checkout (8-PRC-0014).
*   [[electrical_systems_field_checkout_9_prc_0004]]: Electrical Systems Field Checkout (9-PRC-0004).

Each of these procedures is accompanied by associated checklists, which serve as formal records of the completion of inspections and tests.

**Defect Management**
Any incomplete work identified during mechanical completion testing is formally communicated to the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Site Construction Manager through deficiency lists.

The priority for remedying these identified defects **will** be mutually agreed upon between the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Site Construction Manager and the Commissioning Manager. Furthermore, agreement **must** be reached regarding which outstanding work items can be safely and effectively completed while [[water_and_air_trials_and_safety_protocols]] are in progress, to optimize the project schedule.
