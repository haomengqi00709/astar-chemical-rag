# Equipment Design Safety Considerations

Personnel safety is paramount during the design, selection, and installation of mechanical equipment, encompassing construction, ongoing operation, and maintenance phases.

Protection **will** be provided to prevent operations and maintenance personnel from coming into contact with exposed plant items where the surface temperature is assessed as being likely to cause personal injury. Any exposed surface exceeding 60°C is considered a hazard and **will** be subject to a [[0-prc-8007_hierarchy_of_risk_control|risk assessment]].

The containment and control of dust produced by the normal operation of equipment, such as conveyors, **will** be considered to prevent unwanted ingress into other Plant Areas and equipment.
