# Bridge Crane Equipment Testing

This page details the requirements for equipment testing for the Bridge Crane, as specified in Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7441. Functional testing is essential to confirm the operational readiness and performance of the crane's critical components.

**Functional Test Requirements:**
A Functional Test is required as "FP" ([[quality_record_definitions|Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package]]). This test **must** demonstrate the proper operation of the Bridge Crane's key functionalities.

**Functional Test Remarks:**
Specific remarks for the Functional Test include "Trolley & hoist operation." This indicates that the test **shall** specifically verify the smooth and correct functioning of the trolley movement and the hoist mechanism, including lifting, lowering, and emergency stops.

**Chemetics Surveillance:**
Chemetics surveillance for the Functional Test requires "Witness." A Chemetics representative **shall** be present during the functional testing to observe and confirm that the trolley and hoist operations meet the specified performance criteria.

**Equipment Testing Activity:**
Equipment Testing is a mandatory item or activity within the quality assurance process for the Bridge Crane. This ensures that all mechanical and electrical systems operate as intended before shipment and installation.

For an overview of the vendor's general quality assurance obligations, refer to [[0-QDS-7441_bridge_crane_vendor_quality_assurance_requirements]].
