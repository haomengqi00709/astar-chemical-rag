# Vessel Field Inspection Documentation and Sign-off (4-FIC-XX12-R0)

The 4-FIC-XX12-R0 Vessel Field Inspection Checklist mandates specific documentation and sign-off procedures to ensure the quality and compliance of vessel installation and mechanical completion. This page details the required records and approval steps.

**Required Documentation:**

*   **NDT Inspection Records:** Records for all Non-Destructive Testing (NDT) inspections **must** be available. These records serve as proof that the required examinations have been performed and that the vessel meets specified quality standards. For general NDT requirements, refer to [[non_destructive_examination_requirements]].
*   **Internal Painting or Lining Inspection Reports:** Inspection reports pertaining to internal painting or lining **must** be attached to this checklist. These reports confirm the quality and integrity of protective coatings. For details on coating inspections, refer to [[coating_and_lining_inspection]].
*   **Final Internal Inspection Closure Certificate:** A closure certificate for the final internal inspection **must** be attached. This certificate formally documents the completion and acceptance of the internal inspection, which **must** be completed with the client. For general final inspection requirements, see [[final_inspections]].

**Deficiencies and Comments:**
The document includes dedicated sections for recording up to 10 deficiencies and comments. Any non-conformances identified during the inspection process should be documented here. For formal reporting and management of non-conformances, refer to the [[non_conformance_report_0_ncr_7]] procedure.

**Sign-off Requirements:**
Upon completion of the inspection and verification of all required documentation, the checklist requires formal sign-off from the following parties:

*   **Aker Kvaerner Chemetics:** Representing the client or project management.
*   **Sub Contractor:** The entity responsible for the installation and mechanical completion of the vessel.
*   **Client:** The ultimate owner or operator of the facility.

Each party **must** provide their signature and the date of approval, signifying their acceptance of the inspection results and the vessel's readiness for subsequent project phases. For general guidelines on inspection release processes, refer to [[inspection_release_process_and_documentation]]. Details on required signatories can be found in [[4-FIC-XX02-R0_document_signatories]].
