# Plate & Frame Heat Exchanger Dimensions and Anchor Details (4-SCR-0030)

This page provides specific dimensional data and anchor bolt details for the Plate & Frame Heat Exchanger, as outlined in document 4-SCR-0030. These specifications are essential for the accurate design and installation of the equipment's concrete pad and anchoring system.

**Equipment Data Categories:**
*   **Underside of Equipment Elevation (mm):** Specifies the elevation of the lowest point of the equipment.
*   **Nominal Floor or Grade Elevation (mm):** Indicates the reference elevation for the floor or ground level.
*   **Anchor Bolt 'AB' Diameter (mm):** The specified diameter for the anchor bolts.
*   **Number of Bolts:** 3 ea (three anchor bolts are required).
*   **Dimension 'A' - Pad Size (mm):** A dimension related to the size of the concrete pad. Remarks: See Note 2.
*   **Dimension 'B' - Pad Size (mm):** A dimension related to the size of the concrete pad. Remarks: See Note 2.
*   **Dimension 'C' - Pad Size (mm):** A dimension related to the size of the concrete pad. Remarks: See Note 2.
*   **Dimension 'D' - Pad Size (mm):** A dimension related to the size of the concrete pad.
*   **Dimension 'E' - Bolt Position (mm):** A dimension defining the position of the anchor bolts.
*   **Dimension 'F' - Bolt Position (mm):** A dimension defining the position of the anchor bolts.
*   **Dimension G' - Datum Offset (mm):** The offset from a specified datum point.
*   **Dimension 'H' - Equip. Anchor Height (mm):** The height of the equipment's anchor point.
*   **Dimension 'P' - Bolt Projection (mm):** The required projection length of the anchor bolt above the concrete surface.

**Grout Specification:**
*   **Dimension 'Y' - Grout:** 40 mm. This specifies the required thickness of the grout layer beneath the equipment.

These dimensions are critical for ensuring proper fit-up and structural integrity. For general equipment design and layout requirements, refer to [[4-prc-0002_equipment_design_and_layout_requirements]]. Further details on equipment support criteria can be found in [[equipment_support_criteria]].
