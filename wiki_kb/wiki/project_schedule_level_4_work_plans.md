# Project Schedule Level 4 Work Plans

Level 4 Work Plans represent the most detailed level of project scheduling, developed primarily in the form of checklists. These plans provide granular instructions and verification steps for specific tasks.

Subcontractors **will** be required to develop Level 4 Work Plans as checklists to guide their execution of assigned work. Similarly, for the commissioning phase of a project, Level 4 Work Plans **will** be developed in the form of checklists to ensure thorough and systematic completion of commissioning activities.
