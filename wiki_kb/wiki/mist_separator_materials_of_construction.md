# Mist Separator Materials of Construction

This page specifies the required materials of construction for key components of the mist separator, as detailed in the Template Mist Separator Datasheet (Document ID [[mist_separator_datasheet_administration]]). The selection of appropriate materials is critical for ensuring the equipment's longevity, resistance to corrosion, and mechanical integrity under the specified process conditions.

**Component Material Specifications**
The following components require specific material designations, which are provided as data fields for the vendor to complete:

*   **Materials of Construction for Cage / Housing:** This field specifies the material for the structural cage and/or the outer housing of the mist separator. This material must withstand the external and internal process environment, including temperature, pressure, and corrosive agents.
*   **Materials of Construction for Element:** This field specifies the material for the mist eliminating elements themselves (e.g., mesh pads, candles, chevrons). The element material is crucial for effective separation and must be compatible with the entrained materials and gas stream, as described in [[mist_separator_entrained_material_data]] and [[mist_separator_inlet_gas_data]].
*   **Materials of Construction for Gasket:** This field specifies the material for all gaskets used in the mist separator assembly. Gaskets are vital for maintaining seals and preventing leaks, especially in corrosive or high-pressure environments.
*   **Materials of Construction for Bolting:** This field specifies the material for all bolting components used in the assembly. Bolting materials must provide adequate mechanical strength and corrosion resistance to ensure the structural integrity of the unit.

**Importance of Material Selection**
The choice of materials directly impacts the equipment's reliability and maintenance requirements. Factors such as chemical compatibility, operating temperature, mechanical stress, and cost are all considered during material selection. This section ensures that the mist separator is constructed from materials suitable for the specific application, aligning with general [[material_selection]] guidelines and [[mist_separator_mechanical_design_requirements]].

**Materials of Construction Data Fields**
The datasheet specifies the required materials for critical components:
*   **Cage / Housing:** Material for the main structural enclosure.
*   **Element:** Material for the mist separation elements (e.g., pads, candles).
*   **Gasket:** Material for sealing components.
*   **Bolting:** Material for fasteners used in assembly.
