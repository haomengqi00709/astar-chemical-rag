# Business Travel Class and Ticket Restrictions

To ensure cost-effectiveness and adherence to company policy, specific restrictions apply to travel class and ticket types for business travel. These restrictions are in line with the principles outlined in the 11.1 PR 2 Group Travel Procedure, which emphasizes prudent spending.

**Business Class Travel Restrictions:**
Business class travel is permitted only under specific conditions:
*   For journeys involving more than nine hours of continuous flying.
*   For journeys where the total flying time exceeds twelve hours.

Business class travel is **not allowed** for the following types of travel, regardless of duration:
*   Assignment uplifts (for assignments of 1 month or more). Refer to [[0_pol_9111_assignment_uplift]] for related policies.
*   Employee development activities.
*   Training courses.
*   Home leave.
*   Rest and Recuperation (R&R) trips.

**Ticket Type Considerations:**
*   Refundable tickets are generally not the lowest-cost option. While they offer flexibility, travellers **must** justify their selection if they deviate from the most economic carrier and routing, as per [[travel_policy_compliance_and_deviations]].
*   When considering changes to restricted tickets, travellers **must** determine the cost of such changes and ensure they represent a justifiable business expense. Changes to restricted tickets can often be more expensive than the original journey, reinforcing the need to evaluate them in the context of "behaving as though you were paying for the journey yourself." This is further elaborated in [[travel_authorization_changes]].

**Ticket Types:**
Refundable tickets are not usually the lowest-cost option and should only be requested when justified by potential changes in travel plans, as outlined in the [[travel_policy_compliance_and_deviations]] page regarding deviations.

**Business Class Travel Restrictions:**
Business class travel is only allowed under specific conditions:
*   For journeys of more than nine hours continuous flying.
*   For journeys of more than twelve hours total flying time.

Business class is **not** allowed for the following types of travel:
*   Assignment uplifts (1 month or more).
*   Employee development.
*   Training.
*   Home leave.
*   R&R (Rest and Recuperation).
