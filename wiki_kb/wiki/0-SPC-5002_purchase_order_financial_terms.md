# Purchase Order Financial Terms (0-SPC-5002)

This page outlines the financial terms, including pricing, payments, retention, and security, for Purchase Orders under document **0-SPC-5002**.

**Prices:**
*   The *Prices* are the lump sum prices for each item in the Purchase Order, unless later changed in accordance with this Purchase Order.

**Retention:**
*   The Purchase Order will state if retention does not apply.
*   Until *Completion* of the whole of the *Goods and Services*, an amount of 10% is retained from each payment.
*   A maximum amount retained of 5% of the total *Price* for the *Goods and Services* is reached.
*   The amount retained is halved in the assessment made at *Delivery* of the whole of the *Goods and Services*.
*   The halved amount retained remains at this amount until the *Defects Date*.
*   No amount is retained in assessments made after the *Defects Date*.

**Security:**
*   The *Vendor* **must** provide security.
*   Security **must** be provided by a financial institution accepted by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
*   The security amount is stated in the Purchase Order.
*   The security form **must** be acceptable to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
*   The security **must** be given before the date stated in the Purchase Order.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics has recourse to the security if a payment to which it is entitled remains unpaid for five days after Aker Kvaerner Chemetics has notified the *Vendor* of its intention to have recourse. This is a critical aspect of [[performance_security_and_guarantees]].

**Invoicing and Payment:**
*   The *Vendor* invoices Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for a payment on or before an invoice date.
*   The invoice **must** include the documents or evidence stated in the Purchase Order, aligning with [[invoicing_documentation_requirements]].
*   The *Project Buyer* assesses the amount due at each invoice date if an invoice has been given.
*   The amount due is the amount stated in the Purchase Order plus other amounts to be paid to the *Vendor*, less amounts to be paid by or retained from the *Vendor*.
*   Any value-added tax or sales tax required by law for Aker Kvaerner Chemetics to pay to the *Vendor* is included in the amount due.
*   The *Project Buyer* corrects any wrongly assessed amount due in a later assessment.
*   The *Vendor* **must** provide the *Project Buyer* with evidence satisfactory to the *Project Buyer* that all amounts owing to any person related to the *Goods and Services* have been paid.
*   The *Vendor* **must** provide the *Project Buyer* with a full and complete release from any person who has filed or registered any notice of lien, security, security interest or charge (UCC or PPSA, or equivalent in any jurisdiction) or who has provided labor or materials in relation to the *Goods and Services*.
*   If the *Vendor* does not provide the evidence or releases, the *Project Buyer* may assess an amount to be retained from the *Vendor* as security against any claims by persons who have provided labor or materials.
*   If an amount is due from the *Vendor* to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may set that amount off against any payment otherwise due to the *Vendor*.
*   Each payment is made within 45 days of the date Aker Kvaerner Chemetics received the invoice, or within a different period if stated in the Purchase Order. This is a key aspect of [[subcontract_payment_and_invoicing_0_spc_2201]].
