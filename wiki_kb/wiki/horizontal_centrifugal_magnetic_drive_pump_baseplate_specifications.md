# Horizontal Centrifugal Magnetic Drive Pump Baseplate Specifications

The baseplates for horizontal centrifugal magnetic drive pumps **will** be constructed from painted carbon steel.

These baseplates **will** be designed with sufficient dimensions to accommodate motors that are one size larger than the motor initially selected for the pump.

For general requirements related to equipment grouting, which is often associated with baseplate installation, refer to [[equipment_grouting_requirements]].

For general requirements, refer to [[4-SPC-0001_general_equipment_requirements]].
