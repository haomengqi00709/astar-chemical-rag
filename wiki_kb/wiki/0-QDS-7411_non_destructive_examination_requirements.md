# Acid Cooler Non-Destructive Examination Requirements (0-QDS-7411)

This page details the Non-Destructive Examination (NDE) requirements for the Acid Cooler, as specified in document [[0-QDS-7411_document_details|0-QDS-7411]].

**General Requirements:**
*   Non-Destructive Testing **shall** be in accordance with ASME VIII Div. 1.
*   Non Destructive Examination **shall** be in accordance with ASME Boiler & Pressure Vessel Code Sections V & VIII or approved equivalent.

**Specific NDE Activities:**

*   **Radiographic Testing:**
    *   Type: Spot
    *   Required as [[0-QDS-7411_quality_record_definitions|FP]].
    *   Requires surveillance (witness) by Chemetics Vancouver.
    *   Applies to: Shell & channel sides.

*   **Dye Penetrant Testing:**
    *   Quantity/Level: 1.0
    *   Required as [[0-QDS-7411_quality_record_definitions|FP]].
    *   Requires surveillance (witness) by Chemetics Vancouver.
    *   Applies to: Shell side final weld pass.
    *   Applies to: Tube-tubesheet weld fusion & final passes.

These NDE procedures are critical for detecting internal and surface flaws without damaging the material, ensuring the structural integrity and safety of the Acid Cooler.

For general NDE requirements, refer to [[0-QDS-7_non_destructive_examination_requirements]] or [[0-QDS-7404-2_non_destructive_examination_requirements]].
