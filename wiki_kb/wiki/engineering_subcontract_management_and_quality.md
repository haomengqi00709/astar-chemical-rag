# Engineering Subcontract Management and Quality

The management of engineering subcontracts is a critical component of project execution, ensuring that outsourced design activities meet stringent quality standards.

**Subcontracting Design Activities**
Design activities slated for subcontracting are identified within the [[0-prc-2003_project_execution_plan_overview]]. The Engineering Subcontract serves to inform the Subcontractor of all design requirements, typically by referencing attached drawings and specifications.

Engineering Subcontracts are issued in accordance with the following procedures:
*   [[form_of_engineering_subcontract]]: Form of Engineering Subcontract (0-PRC-02).
*   [[engineering_subcontract_scope_preparation_0_prc_2203]]: Engineering Subcontract Scope Preparation (0-PRC-03).
*   [[engineering_subcontract_commercial_terms_0_prc_2205]]: Commercial Terms for Engineering Subcontract (0-PRC-05).
*   [[issue_of_engineering_subcontract_0_prc_2206]]: Issue of Engineering Subcontract (0-PRC-06).

**Subcontractor Selection and Management**
It is mandatory that the [[project_quality_objectives]] (Section 1.3 of the original document) are met, and the Subcontractor **must** be capable of fulfilling all requirements set out in the Subcontract. Subcontractors are selected in accordance with [[engineering_subcontractor_evaluation_0_prc_2204]]: Engineering Subcontractor Evaluation and Approval (0-PRC-04).

The Project Engineer is responsible for managing the Subcontractor, while the Subcontract Administrator handles the administrative aspects of the Subcontract. The roles and responsibilities of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics personnel and their interaction with the Subcontractor are detailed in [[engineering_subcontract_control_0_prc_07]]: Engineering Subcontract Control (0-PRC-07).

**Documentation and Quality Assurance**
Documentation exchange with the Subcontractor, including issuance and receipt, is managed according to [[0-prc-0006_control_of_project_documents]]: Control of Project Documentation. The Subcontractor **must** comply with:
*   [[engineering_subcontractor_document_supply_0_spc_0002]]: Engineering Subcontractor Document Supply (0-SPC-0002). Data available in SQL: 0-SPC-0002
*   [[engineering_subcontractor_quality_assurance_0_spc_7004]]: Engineering Subcontractor Quality Assurance (0-SPC-7004). Data available in SQL: 0-SPC-7004

Procedure 0-SPC-7004 specifically requires the Subcontractor to prepare a quality plan for their work. This quality plan is reviewed and approved in accordance with [[engineering_subcontractor_quality_assurance_0_prc_7007]]: Engineering Subcontractor Quality Assurance (0-PRC-7007).

While the Subcontractor's work is not subject to direct checking and approval by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, it **will** be audited by the responsible Discipline Lead. These audits are conducted in accordance with [[engineering_subcontractor_quality_assurance_0_prc_7007]]: Engineering Subcontractor Quality Assurance (0-PRC-7007), ensuring compliance with quality standards.

The Engineering Subcontract advises the Subcontractor of design requirements, which are usually conveyed by reference to attached drawings and specifications.

**Subcontractor Requirements and Administration**
Project Quality Objectives stated in Section 1.3 **must** be met by subcontractors. The Subcontractor **must** be able to meet the requirements set out in the Subcontract.

While the Project Engineer manages the Subcontractor, the Subcontract Administrator is responsible for administering the Subcontract. Documentation is issued to and received from the Subcontractor in accordance with 0-PRC-0006: Control of Project Documentation.
