# Structural and Architectural Design and Specification (3-PRC-0002)

This document, 3-PRC-0002, details the discipline assignments for the preparation, checking, and approval of structural and architectural design and specifications for the Project. It outlines the roles and responsibilities for developing all structural and building-related documentation.

This procedure ensures that all structural and architectural design documents, including drawings, calculations, and specifications, are developed, reviewed, and approved in a structured manner, adhering to the [[structural_and_architectural_design_criteria_3_prc_0001]] and overall project quality standards.
