# AODD Pump Air Supply Requirements

This page details the specific air supply requirements for the Air Operated Double Diaphragm (AODD) Pump, as outlined in document 4-DST-XXXX-R1. AODD pumps rely on a clean, regulated air supply for their operation, making these parameters critical for efficient and reliable performance.

**Air Supply Parameters:**
The following specifications define the necessary characteristics of the compressed air supply:

*   **Air Consumption:** The volume of compressed air required by the pump to operate, typically measured in cubic feet per minute (CFM) or normal cubic meters per hour (Nm3/h). This parameter is essential for sizing the air compressor and distribution system.
*   **Air Pressure:** The required operating pressure of the compressed air supply, measured in kPa(g) (kilopascals gauge) or psi(g). Maintaining the correct air pressure is vital for the pump to achieve its specified flow rate and head.
*   **Air Connection:** Specifies the size and type of connection required for the compressed air line to the pump (e.g., NPT, BSP, hose barb).

**Importance of Air Quality:**
While not explicitly listed as a parameter, the quality of the air supply (e.g., dry, filtered, oil-free) is implicitly critical for the longevity and performance of AODD pumps. Contaminants in the air supply can lead to premature wear of internal components, valve sticking, and reduced efficiency. Therefore, appropriate air treatment equipment, such as filters and dryers, is often specified as [[aodd_pump_ancillary_equipment]].

For general information on plant-wide instrument air systems, which often supply AODD pumps, refer to [[instrument_air_system]]. Proper management of the air supply ensures the AODD pump operates effectively and minimizes maintenance needs.
