# FRP Tank Vendor Quality Assurance and Surveillance (0-QDS-7414)

For the supply of FRP Tanks under document **0-QDS-7414**, vendors are subject to specific quality assurance and surveillance requirements to ensure compliance with project standards.

**Mandatory Requirements:**
*   The **Vendor must** prepare a Quality Plan in accordance with [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment]].
*   The **Vendor's Quality Assurance must** comply with this Quality Data Sheet (0-QDS-7414).
*   The **Vendor's Quality Assurance must** comply with [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment]].

**Note on Quality Plan:** The cells related to the Quality Plan should be deleted if a Quality Plan is not required for a specific procurement.

**Chemetics Surveillance Level:**
Surveillance by Chemetics will be conducted at **Level 2.0**. This level of surveillance is further detailed within this Quality Data Sheet and aligns with the general principles outlined in [[0-QDS-7411_chemetics_surveillance_level]]. The surveillance activities ensure that the vendor's manufacturing and inspection processes meet the specified quality criteria.

**Historical Context:**
It is noted that the requirement for "Vendor's Quality Assurance will comply with this Quality Data Sheet and with [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment]]" was specifically associated with Revision 0.0 of this document. This highlights the continuous commitment to vendor quality assurance from the initial stages of the project.

For definitions of quality record types referenced in surveillance activities, refer to [[0-QDS-7414_quality_record_definitions]].
