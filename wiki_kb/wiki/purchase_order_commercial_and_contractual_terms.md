# Purchase Order Commercial and Contractual Terms

Purchase Orders (POs) issued by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics incorporate specific commercial and contractual terms to govern the transaction. These terms cover payment schedules, potential penalties for delays, and adherence to standard and special conditions of purchase.

**Payment Terms:**
*   **Standard Payment Terms:** Payment terms are typically Net 45 days from the invoice date.
*   **Payment Milestone:** The payment milestone for 100% of the order value is set to occur after delivery of the Goods and Services.

**Delay Damages:**
The Vendor **shall** pay delay damages for each part of the Goods and Services that is not delivered by the specified Delivery Date. The delay damages rate applies for each day until the actual delivery occurs.
*   **Delay Damages Rate for Documentation:** CAD 1,000 per day for any item of documentation.
*   **Delay Damages Rate for Other Items:** CAD 10,000 per day for any other item of Goods or Services.

**Order-Specific Conditions:**
Text Section 5 of the PO **must** contain Order-Specific Conditions. These conditions are further defined in [[commercial_terms_for_purchase_0_prc_5004]].

**Compliance with Conditions:**
The Vendor **must** comply with the terms and conditions set out in the following documents:
*   [[standard_conditions_for_purchase]] (Data available in SQL: 0-SPC-5002 - Standard Conditions for Purchase (Long Form))
*   [[special_conditions_for_purchase]] (Data available in SQL: 0-SPC-5003 - Special Conditions for Purchase)

For a comprehensive understanding of the PO structure, refer to [[request_for_quotation_and_purchase_order_structure_and_content]].
