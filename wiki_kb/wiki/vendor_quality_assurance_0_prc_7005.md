# Vendor Quality Assurance Procedure (0-PRC-7005)

This document, identified as 0-PRC-7005, is a Procedure that outlines the roles and responsibilities of personnel involved in assuring the quality of goods supplied by Vendors.

**Purpose and Scope**
The primary purpose of this procedure is to establish the framework for vendor quality assurance activities. The scope of surveillance activities is defined by the [[quality_data_sheets_qds]], [[vendor_quality_assurance_equipment_0_spc_7001]], [[vendor_quality_assurance_bulk_materials_0_spc_7002]], and the guidelines set forth within this procedure (0-PRC-7005).

**Personnel and Surveillance**
Surveillance activities are to be carried out by suitably qualified Inspectors. These Inspectors may be either company staff or personnel from Subcontractor surveillance agencies. They will operate under the direction of [information not provided in source document].

**Related Procedures**
*   **Quality Data Sheets (QDS)**: Vendors are issued [[0-QDS-7XXX_template_quality_data_sheet]] and [[vendor_quality_assurance_equipment_0_spc_7001]] / [[vendor_quality_assurance_bulk_materials_0_spc_7002]]. These documents collectively define the quality requirements for supplied goods. Further details on QDS are available in [[quality_data_sheets_qds]].
*   **Non-Conformance Reporting**: This procedure (0-PRC-7005) also governs the documentation of defect repair not covered by procedures already approved as part of the Vendor Quality Plan. Non-conformances are dealt with as described in [[vendor_non_conformance_reporting]].
*   **Inspection Release**: The process for approving goods for shipment is detailed in [[vendor_inspection_release]].
