# Compliance and Standards

The design, construction, and operation of the Chlor-Alkali Plant **shall** comply with all Statutory Requirements applicable in the Shire of Fitzroy, State of Queensland, Australia.

**Applicable Statutory Requirements include:**
*   Development Approval
*   Environmental Protection Act
*   Queensland Electricity Act
*   Queensland Workplace Health and Safety Act

All equipment and installations **will** comply with the latest revision of applicable Australian Standards. In instances where no Australian Standard exists, work **will** conform to the most current IEC Standard.

**Conflict Resolution:**
Where a conflict exists between statutory regulations, standards, reference documents, drawings, datasheets, and this specification, the most stringent requirement **shall** apply.

**Standard References:**
*   Relevant Australian Standards are listed in [[reference_documents|Appendix B]].
*   Relevant IEC Standards are listed in [[reference_documents|Appendix C]].

**Instrument Approvals:**
Instruments **will** be specified for CENELEC approval to ensure conformity with European safety standards.

Specific compliance requirements for various instrument types are detailed in their respective sections:
*   [[general_instrument_requirements|General Instrument Requirements]] address RFI immunity standards.
*   [[control_valves_general|Control Valves - General]] specifies noise level compliance.
*   [[instrument_air_system|Instrument Air System]] outlines air quality standards.
*   [[flow_measurement_differential_pressure|Flow Measurement - Differential Pressure]] references AS 2360 and BS 1042 for orifice plate design.
*   [[shutdown_and_isolation_valves|Shutdown and Isolation Valves]] require compliance with ANSI B16.5.
*   [[control_valves_actuators_and_accessories|Control Valves - Actuators and Accessories]] mandates compliance with applicable codes for pneumatic buffer vessels.
