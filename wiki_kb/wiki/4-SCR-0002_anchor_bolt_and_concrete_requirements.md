# Anchor Bolt and Concrete Support Requirements for Polyethylene Tank (4-SCR-0002)

This document, 4-SCR-0002, details the specific requirements for anchor bolts and concrete surfaces supporting the Polyethylene Tank on a Concrete and Sand Bed. Adherence to these specifications is **mandatory** for ensuring structural integrity and safety.

**Anchor Bolt and Nut Specifications:**
*   **Anchor Bolt Material:** 4.6
*   **Nut Material:** 4
*   Loads are to be applied at Datum.
*   **Minimum "Bolt to Edge" distance:** 150 mm.
*   The [[civil_works_design_criteria_2_prc_0001|Civil/Structural Department]] **must** be consulted for any "Bolt to Edge" distances other than the specified minimum of 150 mm.

**Concrete Surface Requirements:**
*   Concrete **shall** be poured to a flat surface.
*   Concrete **shall** be free of irregularities, ridges, and depressions.
*   The maximum variation under a 3-meter straight edge in any direction **shall** be 3mm.

The basis for these support criteria includes the [[equipment_documentation_procedure_4_prc_0003|Equipment Drawing Number and Revision]]. For general [[material_selection]] guidelines, refer to relevant specifications.
