# Horizontal Pump Field Inspection Documentation and Sign-off

The completion of the Horizontal Pump Field Inspection Checklist (4-FIC-XX07-R0) requires formal documentation and sign-off by all involved parties to confirm compliance with installation and quality standards.

**Motor Rotation Report:**
A report verifying motor rotation **must** be attached to the checklist. This report is typically provided by the electrical contractor responsible for the motor installation and testing, as part of the broader [[electrical_equipment_testing_and_inspection]] process.

**Sign-off Requirements:**
To finalize the inspection, the following parties **must** provide their Name and Signature, along with the Date:

*   **Aker Kvaerner Chemetics:** A representative from Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **must** provide their Name and Signature, along with the Date, to acknowledge the completion and verification of the inspection items. This aligns with internal [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_obligations_0_spc_2201]] and quality assurance procedures.
*   **Sub Contractor:** The Sub Contractor responsible for the pump installation **must** provide their Name and Signature, along with the Date, confirming that all specified tasks have been executed according to project requirements. This is a critical step in [[subcontractor_quality_assurance_0_prc_7006]] and [[construction_subcontract_management_and_site_quality]].
*   **Client:** The Client **must** provide their Name and Signature, along with the Date, indicating their acceptance of the inspected work. This final sign-off is essential for project handover and adherence to [[client_project_progress_reporting]] and overall [[project_quality_objectives]].

These signatures collectively serve as a record of acceptance for the field inspection, contributing to the overall [[quality_record_definitions]] for the project.
