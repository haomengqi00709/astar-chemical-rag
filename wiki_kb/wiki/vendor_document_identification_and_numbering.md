# Vendor Document Identification and Numbering

While Vendors are permitted to use their own internal document titles and numbering systems, specific requirements are in place to ensure proper identification and integration within Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' document control system.

**Vendor's Own Identification:**
*   Vendors may utilize their own established document titles and numbering conventions for their internal processes.

**Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Identification Block:**
*   Each document supplied by the Vendor **shall** include a dedicated block for Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' numbering. This block **must** measure **75mm x 45mm**.
*   Within this block, the Vendor **shall** insert the following Aker Kvaerner Chemetics-assigned identifiers:
    *   The [[purchase_order_commercial_and_contractual_terms|Purchase Order Number]].
    *   The [[plant_equipment_identification_4_prc_0001|Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Tag Number]] (if applicable to the equipment).
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics will be responsible for inserting all other necessary numbers and identifiers into this block.

This standardized identification block ensures that all vendor documents can be accurately tracked and managed within the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics project documentation system, regardless of the Vendor's internal numbering scheme.
