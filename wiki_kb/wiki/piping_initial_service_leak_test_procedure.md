# Piping Initial Service Leak Test Procedure

Where an [[initial_service_leak_test]] has been specified for a system or line, the test **will** be applied in accordance with [[ASME_b31_1_power_piping]], Section 137.7, with additional requirements as detailed below.

**Preparation for Initial Service Test**
Preparation for the initial service test **will** be the same as for a hydrostatic test, as outlined in [[piping_test_preparation_and_equipment_management]].

**Procedure for Liquid Services**
Prior to service pressure application for liquid services, each line **will** undergo the following steps:
1.  **Flushing:** The line **will** be flushed by opening drains or removing pipe sections near the end of each run. Liquid **must** be allowed to flush at as high a rate as possible.
2.  **Flushing Duration:** Flushing duration **will** be approximately 10 minutes or longer until the discharge appears clear.
3.  **Closure:** After flushing, the flow **will** be stopped, and the line **will** be closed.
4.  **Filling:** The line **will** be slowly filled while venting air.
5.  **Pressurization:** Pressure **will** be slowly applied.
6.  **Inspection:** The line **will** be inspected for leaks.

For more information on this process, refer to [[flushing_liquid_services]].

**Procedure for Vapour (Low Pressure Steam), Air, and Nitrogen Services**
Prior to service pressure application for vapour/gas services, each line **will** undergo the following steps:
1.  **Blowing:** The line **will** be blown free of dirt and debris by opening connections near the end of each run. Gas or vapour **must** be allowed to blow at as high a rate as possible, but **must not** exceed 25 psig.
2.  **Blowing Duration:** Blowing duration **will** be approximately 10 minutes or longer until the discharge appears clear.
3.  **Closure:** After blowing, the flow **will** be stopped, and the line **will** be closed.
4.  **Pressurization:** The line should be slowly pressurized in stages.
5.  **Inspection:** During pressurization, the line should be inspected for leaks.

For more information on this process, refer to [[blowing_vapour_gas_services]].

Where an [[initial_service_leak_test]] has been specified for a system or line, the test **will** be applied in accordance with [[ASME_b31_1_power_piping]], Section 137.7 or B31.3 para 345.7, with additional requirements as detailed below.

**Applications for Initial Service Leak Test**
The following lines will be subjected to an initial service leak test:
*   Lines handling reduced pressure steam (<15 psig)
*   Lines handling cooling water
*   Lines handling condensate
*   Lines handling process water
*   Lines handling potable water
*   Lines handling instrument air
*   Lines handling nitrogen
*   Lines handling similar systems handling Category D fluids

**Liquid Service Procedure**
For liquid services, prior to service pressure application, each line will be flushed.
1.  Flushing involves opening drains or removing pipe sections near the end of each run.
2.  Allow the liquid to flush at as high a rate as possible.
3.  Flushing duration is approximately 10 minutes or longer until the discharge appears clear.
4.  After flushing, the flow will be stopped and the line closed.
5.  The line will then be slowly filled while venting air.
6.  Pressure will be slowly applied.
7.  The line will be inspected for leaks.

**Vapour/Gas Service Procedure**
For vapour (low pressure steam), air, and nitrogen services, prior to service pressure application, each line will be blown free of dirt and debris.
1.  Blowing involves opening connections near the end of each run.
2.  Allow the gas or vapour to blow at as high a rate as possible, but **must not exceed 25 psig**.
3.  Blowing duration is approximately 10 minutes or longer until the discharge appears clear.
4.  After blowing, the flow will be stopped and the line closed.
5.  The line should then be slowly pressurized in stages.
6.  During pressurization, the line should be inspected for leaks.
