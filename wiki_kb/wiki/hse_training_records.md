# HSE Training Records Management

Maintaining accurate and accessible records of all health, safety, and environment (HSE) training is a mandatory requirement. The preferred method for documenting training completion is through attendees signing training attendance records.

All training records **will** clearly indicate the method used to determine trainee comprehension. This may include, but is not limited to, written quizzes, verbal quizzes, or direct observation of task performance.

Every employee **will** complete Form DP-802.305 for each training course undertaken. Upon verification with a copy of a certificate or training card, records of completed courses **will** be added to the employee's work experience documentation. Trained workers **will** receive a Certificate of Completion and a decal to indicate successful training completion.

Construction Managers and Site HSE Managers **will** develop and maintain a dedicated safety-training file for each management and supervisory employee. These files **will** utilize Form DP-802.305, which serves as the Safety Training History Record.

For further details on the specific form, refer to [[form_dp-802_305_safety_training_history_record]]. This record-keeping process is integral to the overall [[0-PRC-8016_hse_training_program_overview]].
