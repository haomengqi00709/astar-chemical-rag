# Engineering Subcontract Compensation Events and Change Orders (0-SPC-2202-1)

This page details the procedures and implications for compensation events and change orders within engineering subcontracts, as specified in document 0-SPC-2202-1.

### Compensation Event Notification
For an event to be considered a compensation event affecting the Subcontractor’s costs or planned Completion, it **must** be notified by the Subcontractor within 2 weeks of becoming aware of the event. An event not notified within this 2-week period is deemed to have no effect upon the Subcontractor’s costs or planned Completion.

Furthermore, the Subcontractor releases and indemnifies Aker Kvaerner Chemetics against any claim in respect of an unnotified compensation event. This emphasizes the critical importance of timely notification. For related procedures, see [[engineering_subcontract_compensation_event_procedure]].

### Change Order Acceptance
Acceptance of a quotation for a change is formalized by a [[engineering_subcontract_change_orders|Change Order]] signed on behalf of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. The Change Order **must** be signed by the same person who executed the original Subcontract. This ensures proper authorization and consistency in contractual amendments. Additional details on change order documentation can be found in [[change_order_documentation_requirements_0_chg_22]].
