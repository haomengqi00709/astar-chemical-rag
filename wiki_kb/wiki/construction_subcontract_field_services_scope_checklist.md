# Construction Subcontract Field Services Scope Checklist (0-LST-6X90-2)

The Construction Subcontract Field Services Scope Checklist (0-LST-6X90-2) is a vital document that details the provision and consumption of field services for construction subcontractors. This checklist is directly referenced by Section 2.6 of the [[construction_subcontract_agreement_procedure_0_prc_6002|Construction Subcontract Agreement (0-PRC-6002)]] and is developed as part of the [[construction_subcontract_scope_preparation_0_prc_6091|Construction Subcontract Scope Preparation (0-PRC-6091)]] procedure.

### Purpose and Content

The primary function of 0-LST-6X90-2 is to itemize:

*   All field services (such as water, power, temporary offices, etc.) that will be supplied to the Subcontractor. These services may be provided by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or directly by the Owner.
*   Any applicable charges or fees for the consumption of these field services by the Subcontractor.

### Preparation and Approval Process

1.  **Preparation**: The checklist **must** be prepared by the Construction department. It is based on [[field_services_scope_checklist|0-LST-2003: Field Services Scope Checklist]].
2.  **Numbering**: The document **must** be numbered in accordance with [[document_numbering_and_name_change_conventions|0-PRC-0002: Document Numbering]]. The "X" in the document number (0-LST-6X90-2) **shall** be replaced by the same digit that corresponds to the specific [[construction_subcontract_agreement_procedure_0_prc_6002|Subcontract Agreement (0-PRC-6002)]] it supports.
3.  **Publication**: 0-LST-6X90-2 **must** be published with the status “Approved for Construction”. The publication process **shall** adhere to the guidelines specified in [[publishing_project_documents|0-PRC-0005: Publishing of Project Documents]].
4.  **Checking**: The checklist **must** be checked by the Project Engineer.
5.  **Approval**: Final approval for 0-LST-6X90-2 **must** be granted by the Manager of Construction.
