# HSE Performance Indicators

HSE performance is measured using a combination of lagging and leading indicators to provide a comprehensive view of safety and environmental performance. These indicators are crucial for monitoring trends, identifying areas for improvement, and ensuring compliance with [[0-prc-8002_hse_performance_statistics|HSE policies]].

**Lagging Indicators**
Lagging indicators measure past performance and outcomes, often related to incidents or negative events. These include:
*   **Number of deaths or permanent disability**: A direct measure of severe outcomes.
*   **Lost Time Injury Frequency (LTIF)**: This indicator tracks the number of lost time injuries per one million man-hours worked. It is calculated based on the sum of exposure hours over the last 12 months (rolling average). Refer to [[hse_performance_reporting_and_incident_management]] for incident reporting.
*   **Total Recordable Injury Frequency (TRIF)**: This indicator tracks the total number of recordable injuries per one million man-hours worked. Like LTIF, it is based on the sum of exposure hours over the last 12 months (rolling average). Refer to [[hse_performance_reporting_and_incident_management]] for incident reporting.
*   **Sick Leave**: Measured as a percentage of worked hours, based on the sum of exposure hours year-to-date. For related policies, see [[0-pol-91xx_sick_leave_policy]].

**Leading Indicators**
Leading indicators are proactive measures that predict future performance and highlight efforts to prevent incidents. These are typically calculated on a monthly basis and then annualized (multiplied by 12) to provide a projected annual rate per employee:
*   **Number of task risk analysis per employee**: Measures the frequency of formal risk assessments conducted. For related procedures, see [[hazard_and_operability_studies_1_prc_0004]].
*   **Number of risk observations per employee**: Tracks the number of identified hazards or unsafe conditions reported. Refer to [[hazard_and_operability_studies]] for risk management.
*   **Number of near-misses per employee**: Records incidents that had the potential to cause harm but did not. Reporting near-misses is a key part of [[hse_performance_reporting_and_incident_management]].
*   **Inspections per employee**: Measures the frequency of safety inspections conducted. This relates to general site [[construction_surveillance_and_reporting]].
*   **Safety Training**: Calculated as a percentage of worked hours, based on the total monthly man-hours. This reflects the commitment to [[project_personnel_competence_and_training|employee training and competence]].

All information required for the calculation of these key measures is to be collected and reported at the end of each month.

**Lagging Indicators** provide a retrospective view of performance and include:
*   Number of deaths or permanent disability.
*   Lost Time Injury Frequency (LTIF).
*   Total Recordable Injury Frequency (TRIF).
*   Sick Leave as a percentage of worked hours.

LTIF and TRIF indicators are calculated based on one million man-hours and represent a rolling average, summing the last 12 months of exposure hours. Sick leave is based on the sum of exposure hours year-to-date.

**Leading Indicators** offer insights into proactive safety measures and include:
*   Number of task risk analysis per employee during the month (multiplied by 12).
*   Number of risk observations per employee during the month (multiplied by 12).
*   Number of near-misses per employee during the month (multiplied by 12).
*   Inspections per employee during the month (multiplied by 12).
*   Safety Training as a percentage of worked hours (based on monthly man-hours total).
