# Coatings Design and Specification Procedure (7-PRC-0002)

Procedure 7-PRC-0002 outlines the methodology for project personnel to prepare comprehensive documentation related to coating selection and technical requirements for Vendors. This ensures that all coating specifications issued to suppliers are clear, complete, and compliant.

The scope of this procedure specifically covers thin film coatings, such as paints and plating. It explicitly excludes thick film coatings, which encompass equipment and floor lining systems. The design and specification of these lining systems are outside the purview of this document.

Project documentation for coating requirements, as detailed in this procedure, will consist of three primary documents:
*   [[coatings_design_criteria_7_prc_0001|Coatings Design Criteria]]
*   [[coating_specification_development_and_standards|Coating Specification]]
*   [[7-SPC-0006_coating_colour_specification|Coating Colour Specification]]

For further details on specific aspects, refer to Section 2.1, Section 2.2, and Section 2.3 within the source document.
