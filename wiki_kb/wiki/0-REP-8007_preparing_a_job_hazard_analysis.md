# Preparing a Job Hazard Analysis

This document, 0-REP-8007, provides essential guidelines and requirements for [[0-PRC-8007_job_hazard_analysis|preparing a Job Hazard Analysis (JHA)]]. A JHA is a fundamental risk management tool designed to systematically identify potential hazards and evaluate associated risks within specific work tasks or processes. It is primarily utilized by work teams to proactively enhance workplace safety and ensure the adoption of effective control measures.

### Purpose and Application

The core purpose of performing a Job Hazard Analysis is to ensure that suitable and robust control measures are identified and implemented to mitigate risks. This proactive approach is critical for preventing incidents and maintaining a safe working environment.

A Job Hazard Analysis **shall** be performed under specific conditions. It is a mandatory requirement if a [[task_safety_awareness_overview|STOP-THINK-GO assessment]] indicates a HIGH risk level for a particular task. This criterion ensures that high-risk activities receive a thorough and structured safety review before commencement. For further details on when a JHA is applicable, refer to [[0-PRC-8007_job_hazard_analysis_application_criteria]].

### Roles and Responsibilities

When a STOP-THINK-GO assessment identifies a HIGH risk, the responsibility for conducting the Job Hazard Analysis falls jointly on the supervisor and the work team involved in the task. Their collaborative effort is crucial for a comprehensive analysis, leveraging their combined experience and knowledge of the task and its environment. This collaborative process is further detailed in [[0-PRC-8007_job_hazard_analysis_roles_and_responsibilities]].
