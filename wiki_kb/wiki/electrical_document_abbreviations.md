# Electrical Document Abbreviations

This page lists common abbreviations used within electrical project documentation. Abbreviations are typically found in the Legend at the end of Table # 2 of relevant documents.

**Common Abbreviations:**
*   **Ci:** Civil
*   **Co:** Control
*   **CV:** Vancouver
*   **D:** Design
*   **DoE:** Director of Engineering
*   **E:** Electrical
*   **EC:** Chief E/I Engineer
*   **F:** Finance
*   **I:** Instrument
*   **M:** Mech.
*   **P:** Process
*   **PE:** Project Engineer
*   **PM:** Project Manager
*   **PR:** Purchasing Representative
*   **MPR:** Manager, Purchasing Representative
*   **OD:** Operations Director
*   **S:** Structural
*   **T:** Technical
