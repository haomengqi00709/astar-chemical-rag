# Press Filter General Equipment Specifications

This page details the general equipment specifications and design requirements for the Press Filter, as outlined in Document ID [[4-DST-XXXX-R0_template_plate_filter_datasheet_administration]].

**Equipment Categorization**
Equipment quantity categories include:
*   **Operating**: Units actively in use during normal plant operation.
*   **Installed Spare**: Units installed but kept in reserve for immediate replacement or backup.

**Mandatory Design Requirements**
The following are **mandatory** design requirements for the Press Filter:
*   **Type**: The specific type or model of the press filter.
*   **Location**: The physical location of the equipment within the plant.
*   **Equipment General Requirement**: General overarching requirements applicable to the equipment.
*   **Equipment Specification**: Detailed technical specifications for the equipment.
*   **Painting Specification**: Requirements for the painting and coating of the equipment.
*   **Process Connection**: Specifications for all connections related to the process fluid.
*   **Utility Connection**: Specifications for all utility connections (e.g., air, water, power).
*   **Motor Specification**: Detailed specifications for the motor driving the equipment.
*   **General Electrical Requirements**: Overarching electrical requirements for the equipment and its associated components.

For specific process parameters, refer to [[plate_filter_operating_details]]. For mechanical design details, see [[plate_filter_mechanical_design_requirements]]. Material specifications are covered in [[plate_filter_material_specifications]], and ancillary components in [[press_filter_ancillary_equipment_and_vendor_supply]].
