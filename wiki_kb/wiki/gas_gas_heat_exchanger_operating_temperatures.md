# Gas/Gas Heat Exchanger Operating Temperatures

This page outlines the specific operating temperatures for the internal gas/gas heat exchanger, as detailed in document [[4-CAL-0008_document_details|4-CAL-0008]]. These temperatures are critical for understanding the thermal conditions within the exchanger, which is positioned between beds 1 and 2 of the process.

**Shellside Temperatures**
*   Shell inlet temperature (Tsi): 608.1 C
*   Shell outlet temperature (Tso): 430.0 C

**Tubeside Temperatures**
*   Tube inlet temperature (Tti): 231.3 C
*   Tube outlet temperature (Tto): 420.0 C
*   Tube bypass temperature (Tb): 160.1 C

**Bed Interface Temperatures**
*   Bed 1 inlet temperature (T1i): 420.0 C
*   Bed 1 outlet temperature (T1o): 608.1 C
*   Bed 2 inlet temperature (T2i): 430.0 C
*   Bed 2 outlet temperature (T2o): 550.5 C

For further details on how these temperatures contribute to derived values and design considerations, refer to [[gas_gas_heat_exchanger_bulk_and_mean_metal_temperatures|Bulk Fluid and Mean Metal Temperatures]], [[gas_gas_heat_exchanger_maximum_metal_temperatures|Maximum Metal Temperatures]], and [[gas_gas_heat_exchanger_design_temperature_calculations|Design Temperatures]]. Important assumptions and limitations regarding these calculations are available in the [[gas_gas_heat_exchanger_design_temperature_calculation_commentary|Design Temperature Calculation Commentary]].
