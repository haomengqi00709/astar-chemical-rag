# 4-SPC-0040 Quality Documentation Package

Specification 4-SPC-0040 mandates the maintenance and submission of a comprehensive Quality Documentation Package throughout the fabrication of metallic process vessels and components.

**Maintenance and Submission:**
*   A quality document package **must** be maintained throughout the fabrication of the equipment.
*   The complete quality document package **must** be submitted (both hard copy and electronic) at the time of shipment.
*   The quality document package **must** be available for review by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics prior to inspection release.

**Required Contents:**
The quality document package **must** consist of the following items, as applicable to the specific equipment being fabricated:
*   Nameplate facsimile(s)
*   Manufacturer’s affidavit
*   Jurisdiction confirmation of equipment design registration
*   Certified “As Built” drawings
*   NDE reports (including RT, PT, MT, UT, etc.)
*   Heat treatment records
*   Air/soap leak test reports
*   Hydrostatic/pneumatic test report
*   Weld map identifying welders and radiograph locations
*   Coating reports
*   Documentation for special materials

**Compliance Reporting:**
*   When an Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or Owner paint specification is applicable, the Vendor **must** include a report demonstrating satisfactory compliance to the specification within the quality assurance documentation package.
