# Piping Speciality Item Specification (5-SPC-0005)

The Piping Speciality Item Specification, identified as 5-SPC-0005, is a crucial document that defines the technical requirements for unique or non-standard piping components. These items, often custom-designed or proprietary, require specific attention to ensure their compatibility and performance within the overall piping system.

**Content Requirements**
The Piping Speciality Item Specification **shall** contain the following detailed information:
*   **Type and Number:** A specific classification and unique identification number for each speciality item.
*   **Description:** A detailed explanation of the item's function, design, and operational characteristics.
*   **Material and Rating:** Required material compositions and pressure-temperature ratings, ensuring suitability for process conditions.
*   **Design Condition:** Specific design parameters, including maximum operating pressure, temperature, and fluid properties.
*   **Chemetics Approved Manufacturers and Model Numbers:** A list of approved manufacturers and their specific model numbers that meet Chemetics' quality and performance standards for these specialized components.

**Roles and Responsibilities**
The preparation, checking, and approval of the Piping Speciality Item Specification are assigned to specific disciplines:
*   **Preparation:** The document **shall** be prepared by the Mechanical discipline.
*   **Checking:** The document **shall** be checked by the Design discipline.
*   **Approval:** The document **shall** be approved by the Mechanical discipline.

This structured approach ensures that all piping speciality items are correctly specified and sourced from approved suppliers, contributing to the safety, functionality, and operational reliability of the piping system.
