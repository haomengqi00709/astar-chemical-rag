# HSE Training Program Details

To ensure comprehensive and effective Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) training, several program details and resources are maintained.

**Training Inventory:**
An inventory of all training programs and resources **will** be maintained by the HSE Manager. This inventory facilitates the identification and provision of training for special needs situations.

**Training Program Materials:**
Where possible, training programs **should** utilize structured lesson plans, comprehensive training materials, visual aids, tests for comprehension assessment, and detailed records of training and qualification.
