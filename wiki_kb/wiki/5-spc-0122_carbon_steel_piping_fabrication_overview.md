# Carbon Steel Piping Fabrication Overview (5-SPC-0122)

This specification, identified as document ID 5-SPC-0122, defines the quality requirements for the fabrication of carbon steel piping. It is a direct copy of the original specification ES44/P02, titled "General Requirements for the Fabrication of Carbon Steel Piping."

**Scope of Application**
This specification applies when referenced on purchase orders, drawings, or other project documents. Its requirements are applicable to both shop and field fabricated piping, specifically covering carbon steel steam, process, and utilities piping. It serves to supplement the requirements outlined in [[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping]] where these are applicable.

**Definitions**
*   **Codes, etc.:** Refers to the latest edition ruling at the date of the contract.
*   **Agreement in Writing:** Means a submission in writing by letter, not merely by inclusion in approval drawings. This definition applies specifically where prior approval in writing is required by this Standard.
*   **Inspector:** Denotes an [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_quality_assurance_and_materials_engineering_group]] Inspector or another Authorized Representative.
