# Engineering Subcontract Change Orders

Upon acceptance of a quotation for a compensation event, a formal notification **shall** be issued as a Subcontract Change Order. This **must** utilize the designated template, [[0_chg_22_document_overview]] (0-CHG-22XX), ensuring consistency in format and content across all project modifications.

Change Orders **shall** be prepared by the Subcontract Administrator, who is responsible for accurately reflecting the agreed-upon changes in scope, price, and schedule. Each Change Order **must** be numbered using the same identification number as the original Subcontract to maintain clear traceability. The specific numbering convention for Change Orders adheres to the guidelines outlined in [[document_numbering_and_name_change_conventions]] (0-PRC-0002), which ensures proper document management within the project framework.

Each specific Change Order is considered a distinct sheet within a single overarching document, allowing for granular tracking of individual modifications. Change Orders **shall** be published with the status "Approved for Design" as per [[document_status_definitions]], indicating their final, authorized state. The publishing process for these documents strictly adheres to the procedures detailed in [[document_publishing_methods]] (0-PRC-0005), ensuring proper dissemination and record-keeping.

Before publication, Change Orders **must** be thoroughly checked by the responsible [[discipline_lead_and_approver_roles]] to verify technical accuracy and compliance. Final approval of Change Orders **shall** be performed by the [[project_engineer_pem_gate_review_responsibilities]]. The approved Change Order **must** then be printed and physically signed by the same authorized signatory who executed the original Subcontract, confirming its legal validity and adherence to [[authorization_roles]].

Following signature, the Subcontract Administrator **shall** forward the signed and authorized paper copy of the Change Order to Document Control for official issuance to the Subcontractor. The comprehensive control of all project documentation, including these critical Change Orders, follows the established procedures in [[control_of_project_documents_0_prc_0006]] (0-PRC-0006).
