# Burner Installation Requirements

The proper installation of burner equipment is critical for operational safety and efficiency. This section outlines the mandatory requirements for burner installation, ensuring compliance with design specifications and safety standards.

**Mandatory Installation Requirements:**
*   **Requirement:** The correct Nameplate must be installed on the burner.
*   **Requirement:** The Burner must be installed securely within the furnace structure. For related furnace inspection details, see [[4-FIC-XX25-R0_furnace_field_inspection_items]].
*   **Requirement:** All Burner bolts must be tightened to the specified torque.
*   **Requirement:** The Burner management system must be fully installed.
*   **Requirement:** The Burner management system must be thoroughly tested to ensure proper functionality and safety interlocks.
*   **Requirement:** The Fuel supply system must be installed. This may involve multiple fuel lines for the pilot light and the main burner, which must all be correctly connected.
*   **Requirement:** The Fuel supply system must be tested for leaks and proper flow.
*   **Requirement:** The Air supply system must be installed.
*   **Requirement:** The Air supply system must be tested to ensure adequate and consistent airflow.
*   **Requirement:** The Ignition system must be installed and verified for reliable operation.
*   **Requirement:** All burner certification documents (from the supplier and local authorities) must be on file and readily accessible.
*   **Requirement:** All necessary Burner accessories must be installed. These accessories typically include gauges, vents, drains, and sight glasses.
*   **Requirement:** Grounding must be completed, if applicable, in accordance with electrical safety standards. Refer to [[grounding_installation_details]] for general grounding installation guidelines.

These requirements are part of the comprehensive [[4-FIC-XX26-R0_burner_field_inspection_checklist_administration]] and are verified during the field inspection process.
