# Instrument Installation Guidelines

Instrument installation **will be** arranged to facilitate ease of operation, inspection, and maintenance, while incorporating all necessary safety measures to protect operating and maintenance personnel.

**Accessibility and Visibility:**
*   All instrumentation **will be** installed to be serviceable or replaceable without the aid of a ladder or scaffolding, promoting efficient maintenance.
*   Local indicator instrumentation (e.g., dial thermometers, pressure gauges) **will be** plainly visible and accessible from grade or platform.

**Isolation for Impulse Tubing:**
*   All instruments connected via process impulse tubing **will be** provided with both primary and secondary isolation.
*   An isolation device **will be** provided as close as possible to the process connection.
*   An isolation device **will be** provided as close as possible to the instrument.
These measures ensure safe isolation for maintenance and calibration.

**Mounting Hardware:**
*   Instrument mounting brackets **will be** 316 SS or epoxy coated mild steel, selected for durability and corrosion resistance.
*   Two-inch (2") pipe brackets **will be** included in the supply of all instruments, with the exception of line/flange mounted instruments.
*   All associated mounting bolts and fasteners **will be** stainless steel.

**Documentation:**
Installation drawings **will be** provided for all instrument types, detailing their placement and connection requirements.

**Manifold Requirements (General):**
"olds" **will be** used with a minimum of 25% spare outlet capacity. "olds" **will** include a drain point with valve.
*(Note: The term "olds" appears to be a typographical error in the source document, likely referring to manifolds or similar distribution points, consistent with requirements for spare capacity and drain points.)*

For specific details on impulse tubing, refer to [[impulse_tubing_and_fittings|Impulse Tubing and Fittings]].
