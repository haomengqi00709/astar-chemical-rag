# Quality Record Definitions for Polypropylene Tank (0-QDS-7424)

This page provides essential definitions for quality records as they apply to the Polypropylene Tank project, governed by Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7424. Understanding these terms is critical for proper documentation and management of quality-related information.

**Key Quality Record Definitions:**
*   **AP (As Prepared):** This designation for Quality Records signifies that the record is to be prepared by the Vendor and submitted as part of the project documentation. It indicates that the document is generated during the manufacturing or inspection process.
*   **FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package):** This designation for Quality Records means that the record **must** be included as a mandatory component of the final Quality Assurance Documentation Package submitted by the Vendor. These are typically critical documents required for project handover and long-term asset management.

These definitions ensure clarity in the documentation requirements, specifying not only what records are needed but also how they are to be managed and submitted. For an overview of the project's quality data sheet administration, see [[0-QDS-7424_polypropylene_tank_quality_data_sheet_administration]].
