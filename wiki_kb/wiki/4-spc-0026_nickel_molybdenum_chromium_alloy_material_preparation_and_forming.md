# Material Preparation and Forming for Nickel/Molybdenum/Chromium Alloys

Proper material preparation and forming are critical for ensuring the integrity and corrosion resistance of nickel/molybdenum/chromium alloy fabrications.

**Cleaning and Contamination Prevention**
*   The surface and edges of material to be heated **shall** be cleaned free from all traces of foreign matter (e.g., marking paint, oil, etc.).
*   Cleaning **shall** be by grinding, wire brushing with a stainless steel brush, and/or non-chlorinated solvent cleaning immediately prior to heat application.
*   The fabricator **shall** take controls to prevent contamination of weldments at all stages of fabrication.
*   Controls **shall** include segregation of wire brushes, grinding wheels, etc., for use on Hastelloy materials only.
*   On completion of work, items **shall** be cleaned internally and externally.
*   All fabrication markings and weld spatter **shall** be removed prior to shipment.

**Marking and Passivation**
*   Sulphur, phosphorous, or lead bearing paints or crayon **shall not** be used for marking or identification purposes.
*   Passivation, where required by Purchase Order/referenced documents, **shall** be carried out in accordance with a written procedure.
*   Passivation procedures **must** be approved by [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_company_details]] prior to use.

**Approved Drawings and Deviations**
*   All work **shall** be carried out in accordance with [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_company_details]] "Approved for Fabrication" shop detail drawing(s).
*   Deviations from weld geometry shown on approved drawing(s) are not permitted without prior approval of [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_company_details]].

**Material Specifications and Condition**
*   Plate, sheet, pipe, and tube **shall** be purchased in accordance with ASME specifications SB574, SB575, SB619, SB622, and SB626.
*   Plate and sheet **shall** be supplied in the solution annealed condition for maximum corrosion resistance unless specified otherwise on the purchase order.

**Material Cutting**
*   Joint design for nickel/molybdenum/chromium alloys is slightly different due to molten metal characteristics.
*   Material thicker than 0.125" **shall** be bevelled to form a 'V', 'U' or 'J' groove.
*   Joint design **shall** follow material suppliers welding practice with respect to root opening and land thickness.
*   Material **shall** be cut to size by Machining or Shearing.
*   Materials over 3/16" thick **shall** have extra material left on sheared edge to allow machining, grinding, etc., to remove defects.
*   Material over 1" thick **shall not** be sheared.
*   Thermal cutting is preferably carried out by the Plasma Arc Process.
*   Welding edges after Plasma Arc cutting **shall** be lightly ground to bright metal where necessary.
*   Any thermal cutting process other than Plasma Arc **shall** require a minimum of 1/16" of metal to be removed by mechanical means.
*   Mechanical removal after thermal cutting (non-Plasma Arc) ensures removal of slag, scale, other irregularities, contaminants, and thermal effects.
*   Silicon carbide wheels, dedicated to Hastelloy Alloys, **shall** be used for grinding.

**Material Forming**
*   Materials **shall** be cold formed as far as practical.
*   When hot forming is necessary, the procedure **shall** be agreed to in writing by [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_company_details]] prior to commencement of work.

**Cleaning and Contamination Prevention**
*   The surface and edges of material to be heated **shall** be cleaned free from all traces of foreign matter (e.g., marking paint, oil, etc.).
