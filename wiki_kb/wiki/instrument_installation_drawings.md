# Instrument Installation Drawings

Instrument Installation Drawings are crucial documents that provide detailed instructions and diagrams for the proper mounting, wiring, and connection of field instruments. These drawings ensure that instruments are installed correctly, safely, and in accordance with design specifications.

For comprehensive information on the design and specification of field instruments, which directly informs the content of these installation drawings, refer to [[8-prc-0003_field_instrument_design_and_specification]]. This procedure outlines the requirements and guidelines that govern the selection and application of instruments, ensuring their effective integration into the control system.
