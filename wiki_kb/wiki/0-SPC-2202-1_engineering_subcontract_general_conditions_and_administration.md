# Engineering Subcontract General Conditions and Administration (0-SPC-2202-1)

This page outlines the general conditions, administrative requirements, and specific clause modifications applicable to engineering subcontracts, as detailed in document 0-SPC-2202-1.

### Contractual Precedence and Definitions
Additional conditions of contract take precedence over the [[standard_conditions_engineering_subcontract]]. The "Agreement" refers to the document executed on behalf of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Subcontractor, formally headed "Agreement".

**Subcontract Data**
Subcontract Data comprises all information necessary to fully ascertain the obligations of both parties under the Subcontract. This data is found within:
*   The Agreement document itself.
*   Documents referenced within the Agreement.
*   The [[special_conditions_engineering_subcontract]].

### Schedule Management
The Subcontractor **must** submit a revised Schedule to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics at maximum intervals of one calendar month. This submission **must** occur whether or not Aker Kvaerner Chemetics has specifically instructed the Subcontractor to do so. For more details on scheduling, refer to [[engineering_subcontract_activity_schedule]] and [[subcontract_schedule_management_0_spc_2201]].

### Early Warning Meetings
An Early Warning Meeting may be conducted by telephone or any other means that effectively enables its objectives to be met.

### Tax and Clause Modifications
*   **Value Added Tax (VAT):** The reference to value added tax in clause 22.3 of the [[standard_conditions_engineering_subcontract]] includes any goods and services tax or other tax of a similar nature.
*   **Excluded Clauses:** Clauses 23.4 and 23.5 of the [[standard_conditions_engineering_subcontract]] do not apply to this subcontract.
*   **Deleted Clause:** Clause 40.3 is deleted from the standard conditions.
*   **Amended Clause:** Clause 41.3 is amended by adding the following at the end: "and does not include any amount in respect of costs incurred by the Subcontractor in expectation of completing the whole of the services whether or not they are costs to which the Subcontractor is committed."

These conditions ensure clarity in the contractual framework and define specific deviations from standard terms.
