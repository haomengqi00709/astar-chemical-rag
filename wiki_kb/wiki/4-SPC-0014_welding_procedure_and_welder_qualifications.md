# Welding Procedure and Welder Qualifications for Stabilized Austenitic Stainless Steel (4-SPC-0014)

This page outlines the mandatory requirements for welding procedures and welder qualifications for Columbium Stabilized High Nickel Austenitic stainless steel fabrications, as per document 4-SPC-0014.

**Subcontracting of Welding**
No subcontracting of welding is permitted without Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' written approval.

**Welding Procedure Requirements**
*   All welding procedures shall be submitted by the Fabricator for review and agreement by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics QA & Materials Group prior to the commencement of any work.
*   The ASME IX PQR Form 483 shall form an integral part of the welding procedure package.
*   The procedure shall be qualified on the same grade of material as that selected for fabrication.
*   A change in essential variables, as outlined in [[ASME_section_ix_welding_and_brazing_qualifications]], will require resubmission of the procedure(s).
*   The welding procedure shall be agreed upon before any production welding is begun.

**Welder Qualification Requirements**
*   All welders to be employed on Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' work shall be suitably qualified in accordance with relevant codes and standards for every procedure they are to use.
*   The Fabricator shall, upon request, supply Aker Kvaerner Chemetics with objective evidence of current welder qualifications.
*   All qualified welders shall be allocated an identification symbol.
*   This identification symbol shall be stamped on the parent material adjacent to their work.
*   A weld map(s) may be substituted for stamping, subject to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' approval.

**Welding Procedure Submission**
*   The ASME IX PQR Form 483 shall form part of the welding procedure package.
