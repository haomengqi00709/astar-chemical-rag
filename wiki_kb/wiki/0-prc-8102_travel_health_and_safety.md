# Travel Health, Safety, and Security Overview (0-PRC-8102)

This document, 0-PRC-8102, outlines the specific requirements for employees to comply with the Aker Kvaerner “Travel Health, Safety and Security” Norm (Norm Number: 2.2 NO 2). Its primary purpose is to ensure the health, safety, and security of all employees while on company business travel.

The procedure covers various aspects of business travel, from initial authorization and risk assessment to on-the-ground safety protocols and emergency response. It emphasizes a proactive approach to managing potential risks associated with travel, particularly to regions identified as high or extreme risk.

Key areas addressed within this procedure include:
*   [[travel_authorization_process_0_frm_9002|Travel Authorization]] requirements and approvals.
*   Detailed procedures for [[travel_risk_assessment_and_approval|assessing and approving travel risk]].
*   Specific [[travel_coordinator_responsibilities|responsibilities of the Travel Coordinator]] in managing travel safety.
*   [[traveller_responsibilities|Employee responsibilities]] before and during business trips.
*   Requirements for [[pre_travel_briefing_and_medical_assessment|pre-travel briefings and medical assessments]].
*   Guidelines for [[travel_vehicle_use_and_driving_safety|vehicle use and driving safety]] while travelling.
*   Protocols for [[travel_emergency_assistance_and_evacuation|emergency assistance and evacuation]].
*   Procedures for [[travel_incident_reporting_and_monitoring|monitoring travel safety and reporting incidents]].
*   Guidance for [[personal_travel_in_high_risk_areas|personal travel]] conducted in high-risk regions during business trips.

Compliance with this procedure is mandatory for all employees undertaking business travel to safeguard their well-being and maintain the company's commitment to health, safety, and security standards.
