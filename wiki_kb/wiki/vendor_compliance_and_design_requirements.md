# Vendor Compliance and Design Requirements

This page outlines the Vendor's obligations regarding compliance with applicable laws and design specifications under the Purchase Order (PO).

**Compliance with Health, Safety, and Law:**
*   The Vendor **must** act in accordance with the health and safety requirements of the [[purchase_order_definitions#The Applicable Law|Applicable Law]].
*   The Vendor **must** also adhere to the health and safety requirements specified in the [[purchase_order_definitions#Goods and Services Information|Goods and Services Information]].
*   The Vendor **must** notify the [[purchase_order_roles_and_responsibilities#Project Buyer|Project Buyer]] immediately upon becoming aware that the Goods and Services Information requires them to perform anything that is illegal or impossible.
*   If the Project Buyer agrees that a requirement is illegal or impossible, they **must** issue an instruction to change the Goods and Services Information appropriately.

**Provision and Design of Goods and Services:**
*   The Vendor **must** [[purchase_order_definitions#To Provide the Goods and Services|Provide the Goods and Services]] in accordance with the Goods and Services Information.
*   The Vendor **must** Provide the Goods and Services in accordance with the Applicable Law.
*   The Vendor is responsible for designing the parts of the Goods and Services that the Goods and Services Information explicitly states they are to design.
*   The Goods and Services Information may require the Vendor to submit particulars of their design to the [[purchase_order_roles_and_responsibilities#Lead Engineer|Lead Engineer]] for acceptance.
*   Reasons for not accepting the Vendor’s design include non-compliance with the Goods and Services Information or non-compliance with the Applicable Law.
*   The Vendor **must not** proceed with the relevant work until the Lead Engineer has accepted their design.
*   The Vendor **must** obtain approval of their design from [[purchase_order_definitions#Others|Others]] where necessary or as required by the Applicable Law.
