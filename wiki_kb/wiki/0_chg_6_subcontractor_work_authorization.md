# Subcontractor Work Authorization (Document 0-CHG-6)

This section of Document **0-CHG-6** outlines the mandatory requirements and procedures for authorizing subcontractor work.

**Work Commencement Instructions:**
Subcontractors **shall** proceed with the work described immediately upon receiving specific authorization. However, it is a critical requirement that the Subcontractor **shall not** proceed with any work for which a quotation has been submitted until explicit instruction to do so is provided. This ensures that all commercial terms are agreed upon before work commences, preventing unauthorized expenditure. This instruction is specifically tied to the work for which a quotation is submitted, establishing a clear sequence of quotation, instruction, and then commencement.

**Authorization Authority:**
All work authorizations under this document are issued for and on behalf of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. The designated individual responsible for providing such authorization is the Site Manager. This role is crucial in managing project timelines, ensuring compliance with contractual agreements, and overseeing the initiation of subcontractor activities. Further details on roles and responsibilities related to project authorization can be found in [[authorization_roles]].

These provisions are designed to ensure controlled and documented initiation of work, aligning with the firm's [[procurement_and_vendor_documentation]] standards and overall project management protocols. The process ensures that work only begins after formal approval, especially when financial commitments are involved, as detailed in [[0_chg_6_subcontractor_quotation_and_compensation]].
