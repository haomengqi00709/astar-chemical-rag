# Electrical Numbering and Identification Procedure (9-PRC-0021)

This procedure, identified as 9-PRC-0021, outlines the comprehensive requirements for the numbering and identification of various electrical components and documentation within the plant. Adherence to this procedure ensures consistency, clarity, and traceability across all project phases and operational activities.

The scope of this procedure specifically covers the identification requirements for:
*   Plant electrical numbering systems.
*   All associated electrical [[electrical_document_and_drawing_numbering|documents and drawings]].
*   Individual [[9-PRC-0001_electrical_equipment_identification|electrical equipment]].
*   [[electrical_cable_identification|Cables]] (both single and multi-conductor).
*   Individual [[electrical_conductor_identification|conductors]] within cables.

For specific details on the numbering and identification of these elements, refer to the respective dedicated procedures and standards as referenced throughout this document. This procedure is critical for maintaining an organized and efficient electrical system infrastructure.

[[electrical_department_document_control|Document control and authorization]] for electrical documents are managed by the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Electrical department.
