# Piping Water Batch Flushing Preparation

Prior to initiating water batch flushing, the plant or section of the plant **must** be prepared to the satisfaction of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' site representative. This comprehensive preparation ensures the system is ready for commissioning and prevents potential issues.

The following items **must** be reviewed and confirmed complete before water batch flushing:
*   **Pressure Testing:** Completion of all required [[pressure_testing_execution]].
*   **Blind Removal:** Removal of all [[blinding_requirements]] used for testing.
*   **Equipment Reinstallation:** Reinstallation of all equipment removed for tests, as detailed in [[post_test_restoration]].
*   **Pipe Support System:** Inspection of the [[pipe_support_system_inspection]] to ensure integrity.
*   **Temporary Strainers:** Installation of all [[temporary_startup_strainer_installation]].
*   **Equipment Cleaning:** Equipment **must** be checked and cleaned of debris. This is part of the broader [[cleaning_and_inspection_pre_test]] process.
*   **Control Valve Verification:** [[control_valve_functions_verification]] **must** be performed.
*   **Seal Fluid Provision:** Adequate provision of [[seal_fluid_provision]] pressure and flow **must** be confirmed.

The [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_site_supervisor_review]] is responsible for approving the readiness of the plant section for water batch flushing.

The following items **must** be reviewed and confirmed prior to water batch flushing:
*   Completion of pressure testing.
*   Removal of all blinds.
*   Reinstallation of equipment removed for tests.
*   Inspection of the pipe support system.
*   Installation of all temporary strainers.
*   Equipment must be checked and cleaned of debris.
*   Control valve functions must be verified.
*   Adequate provision of seal fluid pressure and flow.
