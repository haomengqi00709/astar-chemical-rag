# Polyken Tape Application for Field Joints and Fittings (7-SPC-0005)

This section details the procedures for applying Polyken tape to weld joints and fittings for pipe sizes up to 24 inches, particularly those coated with X-Tru-Coat.

**Pre-Welding Preparation for Joint Cutback**
*   Prior to welding, 6 inches of adhesive **will be removed** from the cut end of field-cut pipe for joint cutback.
*   Prior to welding, 6 inches of plastic outer jacket **will be removed** from the cut end of field-cut pipe for joint cutback.
*   The plastic jacket **will be wire-brushed** at least 3 inches back from the bare pipe on both sides of the joint.

**Post-Welding Primer Application**
*   After welding, the wire-brushed jacket and bare pipe **will be given** a uniform coat of Polyken 927 primer.
*   Fittings **will be similarly coated** with primer after cleaning, as per [[7-SPC-0005_surface_preparation_for_coatings]].
*   The primer **will be one coat** of Polyken No. 927.
*   Polyken 927 primer coverage **will be** approximately 400 square feet per gallon.
*   The primer **will be allowed** to dry until its surface is tacky. Manufacturer’s recommendations for primer drying **will be followed** carefully.
*   Tape **will not be applied** over the primer until the primer is tacky.

**Polyken Tape Application**
*   Before the joint is wrapped, but after the primer is dry, two turns of Polyken 930-35 tape **will be applied** over the weld.
*   Beginning at a point 3 inches back on the plastic jacket, Polyken 930-35 tape **will be applied** with a 50 percent overlap.
*   Polyken 930-35 tape application **must extend** to a point 3 inches past the beginning of the jacket on the adjacent length of pipe.
*   A tension of five to ten pounds per inch width **will be used** when wrapping tape on the pipe.
*   After the last spiral lap, sufficient tape **will be cut** from the roll on the down-side of the pipe to allow the tail portion of the tape to relax from the application tension before it is wrapped onto the coated pipe surface.

**Tape Width Requirements**
*   The width of tape used for spiral wrap **will be** as per [[7-SPC-0005_table_2]].
*   The width of tape used for cigarette wrap **will be** as per [[7-SPC-0005_table_3]].

**Primer Application**
*   Primer will be one coat of Polyken No. 927.
*   Pipe joints and fittings will be primed and wrapped on the same day as cleaning is carried out.
*   The plastic jacket will be wire-brushed at least 3 inches back from the bare pipe on both sides of the joint.
*   Polyken 927 primer coverage will be about 400 square feet per gallon.
*   Fittings will be similarly coated with primer after cleaning.
*   The primer will be allowed to dry until the primer surface is tacky. Manufacturer’s recommendation for primer drying will be followed carefully.
*   Tape will not be applied over the primer until the primer is tacky.

**Tape Application Procedure**
*   Before the joint is wrapped, but after the primer is dry, two turns of Polyken 930-35 tape will be applied over the weld.
*   Beginning at a point 3 inches back on the plastic jacket, apply Polyken 930-35 tape with a 50 percent overlap.
*   Polyken 930-35 tape application must extend to a point 3 inches past the beginning of the jacket on the adjacent length of pipe.
*   A tension of five to ten pounds per inch width will be used in wrapping tape on the pipe.
*   After the last spiral lap, sufficient tape will be cut from the roll on the down-side of the pipe to allow the tail portion of the tape to relax from the application tension before it is wrapped onto the coated pipe surface.
