# Polyethylene Tank Support Criteria (4-SCR-0002-R1)

This document, identified by the client document number Standard-4-SCR-0002-R1, details the specific support criteria for a Polyethylene Tank designed to be installed on a Concrete and Sand Bed. This specification is provided by Chemetics, a division of Aker Kvaerner Canada Inc., to ensure the structural integrity and safe installation of the tank within Aker Kvaerner's projects.

Support Criteria documents are fundamental in civil and structural engineering, providing the necessary design inputs and requirements for foundations, pedestals, and other supporting structures for process equipment. For a polyethylene tank, which can be susceptible to deformation and environmental stresses, proper support design is paramount to prevent structural failure, leakage, and operational issues.

The underlying Support Criteria template, from which `Standard-4-SCR-0002-R1` is derived, includes a mandatory placeholder field for "Equipment Name & Number". For this specific document, this field **must** be populated with the unique identifier for the "Polyethylene Tank on Conc. & Sand Bed," ensuring clear identification and traceability within the project documentation.

Key information typically covered in this Support Criteria document includes:
*   **Equipment Dimensions:** Overall dimensions of the polyethylene tank, including diameter, height, and nozzle locations.
*   **Unfactored Equipment Loads:** Detailed breakdown of all loads acting on the tank, such as empty weight, operating weight (including fluid), test weight, insulation weight, and loads from attached piping and platforms.
*   **Environmental Loads:** Wind loads, seismic loads, and snow loads, calculated according to relevant building codes and site-specific data.
*   **Foundation Design Parameters:** Requirements for the concrete slab (thickness, reinforcement), and specifications for the sand bed (material, compaction, thickness) to ensure uniform support and drainage.
*   **Anchor Bolt Details:** Specifications for anchor bolt material, size, embedment depth, and pattern, if applicable, to secure the tank against uplift or sliding.
*   **Grouting Requirements:** Details for grouting under base plates or support pads to ensure full bearing and load transfer.
*   **Material Specifications:** Requirements for concrete, rebar, sand, and any other materials used in the support structure.
*   **Interface Requirements:** Coordination details with piping, electrical, and instrumentation disciplines for penetrations and connections.

Adherence to these detailed support criteria is crucial for the long-term operational safety and reliability of the polyethylene tank, ensuring it can withstand all anticipated loads and environmental conditions throughout its service life.
