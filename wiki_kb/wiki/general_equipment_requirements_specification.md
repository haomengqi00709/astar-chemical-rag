# General Equipment Requirements Specification

This document, 4-SPC-0001, outlines the general requirements for the design, manufacture, and supply of equipment. Adherence to these specifications is mandatory for all equipment supplied.

**Compliance and Standards**
All equipment manufactured **must** be in accordance with the latest edition of applicable statutes, regulations, codes, standards, and specifications. The Vendor **shall** state the specific codes and regulations on which their tender is based. Codes and Standards required by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **will** be listed on data sheets, drawings, or specifications. It is the Vendor’s responsibility to obtain all relevant codes and standards.

**Design Principles**
Equipment **must** be designed to prevent the accumulation of spilled material, dust, and water. It **shall** be designed for operation under the full range of operating conditions, including non-steady state, start-up, shutdown, and turndown scenarios. Permanent facilities for pump-out, steam-out, purging, and flushing **must** be included on all equipment where necessary to ensure safe and efficient operation and maintenance.

For specific details on design loads, refer to [[equipment_design_loads]]. Information on site-specific conditions and utility data can be found in [[site_and_utility_design_data_reference]].
