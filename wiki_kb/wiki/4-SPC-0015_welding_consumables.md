# Welding Consumables (4-SPC-0015)

All welding filler metals and electrodes used for fabrication under Standard 4-SPC-0015 **must** meet specific requirements to ensure the integrity and performance of welds in aggressively corrosive environments.

**General Requirements**
*   All weld filler metals **must** meet the requirements of Section 8 of this Standard.
*   The Fabricator **must** ensure and demonstrate to the Inspector that manufacturers' instructions regarding storage, drying, and use of weld consumables are fully implemented (in shop and on-site).
*   All wet electrodes **must be discarded** and **not** baked and reused.
*   Prior to commencement of work, the Fabricator **must** furnish Aker Kvaerner Chemetics with the brand name of the filler materials actually to be used.
*   Certification showing results from analysis and other tests (in accordance with relevant material specification) **must** be supplied to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for all materials, including welding consumables.

**Specific Consumables by Process**

**Shielded Metal Arc Welding (SMAW)**
*   SMAW electrodes **must** satisfy the requirements of [[ASME_sfa_5_11_nickel_and_nickel_alloy_electrodes]].
*   The SMAW electrode **must** be Incoloy Welding Electrode 135.

**Gas Tungsten Arc Welding (GTAW)**
*   GTAW rods **must** satisfy the requirements of [[ASME_sfa_5_14_nickel_and_nickel_alloy_electrodes_and_rods]].
*   GTAW filler wire diameter **must** be kept small, preferably 1/16".
*   GTAW filler wire **will normally be** Incoloy Filler Metal 65, designation ER Ni Fe Cr-1.
*   Incoloy 625 or equivalent (designation ER Ni Cr Mo3) may also be used for GTAW, provided prior approval has been granted by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
*   The preferred GTAW electrode is thoriated tungsten as per [[ASME_sfa_5_12_tungsten_arc_welding_electrodes]], Class EWTh-2.

These detailed specifications for welding consumables are critical for achieving the required weld quality and corrosion resistance for nickel-base alloy fabrications.

**General Requirements**
*   Fabricator **must** ensure and demonstrate to the Inspector that manufacturers' instructions regarding storage, drying, and use of weld consumables are fully implemented (in shop and on-site).
*   All wet electrodes **must** be discarded and not baked and reused.
*   Fabricator **must**, prior to commencement of work, furnish Aker Kvaerner Chemetics with the brand name of the filler materials actually to be used.
*   Certification showing results from analysis and other tests (in accordance with relevant material specification) **must** be supplied to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for all materials, including welding consumables.
*   Fabricator **must** maintain full traceability of materials back to original mill certification.
*   Fabricator’s internal controls **must** prevent the use of unspecified materials.
*   Lack of adequate identification or certification **will** be cause for rejection of the specific component or item.
