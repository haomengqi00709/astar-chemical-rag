# Lockout and Tagout Equipment and Devices

This page outlines the mandatory requirements and specifications for equipment and devices used in the [[9-prc-0019_lockout_tagout_procedure_overview|Lockout and Tagout Procedure (9-PRC-0019)]].

### Locks

*   Locks **must** be substantial, durable, and complete with retaining key.
*   Locks **must** be traceable to the [[lockout_tagout_roles_and_responsibilities|affected individual]].
*   A lockout adapter can be used when more than one affected individual is servicing a piece of equipment that **must** be locked out.

### Tags

*   The Subcontractor **shall** provide lockout tags.
*   **DO NOT USE TAGS ALONE.** Tags **must** be used in addition to locks.
*   Tags **must** state the reason for the lockout.
*   Tags **must** state the name of the [[lockout_tagout_roles_and_responsibilities|affected individual]] who is working on the equipment and how that person may be reached.
*   Tags **must** state the date and time the tag was put in place.
*   Tags **shall** be capable of enduring at least 50 pounds of pull.
*   Tags **shall** be non-reusable.

### Blocks

*   Suitable blocks are an important safety device.
*   Blocks **must** be placed under raised dies, lifts, or any equipment that might inadvertently move by sliding, falling, rolling or rotating.
*   Blocks, special brackets, or special stands **must** be available and always used.
*   Coiled springs, spring-loaded devices, or suspended loads **must** be released so that their stored energy will not result in inadvertent movement.
*   Rotating equipment such as fan blades **must** be blocked to prevent rotation in the event of air movement.

### Blinds

*   A blind is a disk of metal placed in a pipe to ensure no air, steam, or other substance will pass through that point if the system is accidentally activated. The placement of a blind is another form of blocking.
*   Before installing blinds or blocks, bleed down steam, air, or hydraulic lines to get rid of any pressure.

Further details on the application of these devices are covered in the [[9-prc-0019_lockout_tagout_procedure_overview|Lockout and Tagout Procedure Overview]]. Refer to [[9-PRC-0019_table_1]] for additional information.
