# Press Filter Material Selection and Vendor Supply

This page outlines the material selection and vendor supply responsibilities for key components of the Press Filter, as detailed in Document ID [[4-DST-XXXX-R0_template_plate_filter_datasheet_administration]]. All listed materials are to be supplied and mounted by the vendor, ensuring consistency and quality.

**Vendor Supplied and Mounted Materials**
The following materials for the Press Filter components **shall** be Supplied & Mounted by the Vendor:
*   **Filter Plate material**: The material used for the filter plates, critical for filtration performance and chemical resistance.
*   **Filter Cloth material**: The fabric material used for filtration, selected based on filtration efficiency and compatibility with the process fluid.
*   **Stationary Head material**: The material for the fixed end of the filter press.
*   **Follower Head material**: The material for the movable end of the filter press.
*   **Side Rails material**: The material for the structural side supports of the filter press.
*   **Gasket and O-ring materials**: Sealing materials chosen for chemical resistance and pressure integrity.
*   **Nozzles materials**: Materials for all inlet and outlet nozzles.
*   **Piping & valves materials**: Materials for all associated piping and valves directly supplied with the filter.
*   **Drip Tray material**: The material for the tray designed to collect any drips or leaks.
*   **Motor**: The motor responsible for operating the filter press mechanism.

**Specific Material Details**
*   **Shifter chain material**: 304 SS (Stainless Steel). This material is specified for the automatic plate shifting system, which is an [[press_filter_ancillary_equipment_and_vendor_supply|ancillary equipment]] component.
