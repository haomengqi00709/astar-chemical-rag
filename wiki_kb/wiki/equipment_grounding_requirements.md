# Equipment Grounding Requirements

As specified in document 4-SPC-0001, proper grounding is mandatory for all equipment to ensure electrical safety.

**Grounding Clips for Small Equipment**
Small individually mounted equipment **will be provided** with one grounding clip of approximate size 50mm x 50mm. These grounding clips **shall have** a 12mm diameter hole for connection.

**Grounding Clips for Skid-Mounted Equipment**
Skid mounted equipment **will be provided** with two grounding clips. These clips **must be located** at opposite ends of the skid to ensure comprehensive grounding.

**Grounding Lugs for Vessels, Tanks, and Heat Exchangers**
Each metallic vessel, tank, and heat exchanger **will be equipped** with two (2) grounding lugs.
*   **Vertical Vessels:** Grounding lugs on vertical vessels **will be located** on vessel supports, positioned 180 degrees apart.
*   **Horizontal Vessels:** Grounding lugs on horizontal vessels **will be located** near each saddle support.

These requirements ensure effective electrical grounding, protecting personnel and equipment from electrical hazards. For general [[grounding_installation_details]], refer to the dedicated page.
