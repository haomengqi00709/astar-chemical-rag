# Template Lixator Equipment Specifications

This page outlines the general physical and dimensional specifications for the Lixator units, as detailed in document **4-PDS-XXXX-R1**. These specifications are fundamental for layout planning, structural design, and fabrication of the Lixator equipment.

**Physical Dimensions:**
The following dimensions are specified for the Lixator equipment:
*   **Equipment Overall Height:** Measured in m (meters).
*   **Equipment Overall Width:** Measured in m (meters).
*   **Equipment Overall Length:** Measured in m (meters).

**Volume Capacities:**
The volumetric capacities of the Lixator units are defined as:
*   **Equipment Working Volume:** Measured in m3 (cubic meters). This represents the volume of fluid or material the Lixator is designed to hold during normal operation.
*   **Equipment Total Volume:** Measured in m3 (cubic meters). This represents the maximum internal volume of the Lixator vessel.

**Equipment Location:**
Lixator units can be installed in one of two primary locations:
*   **In Ground**
*   **Above Ground**
