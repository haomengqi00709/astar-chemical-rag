# PEM Steering Committee Roles and Responsibilities

The Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ Project Execution Model (PEM) Steering Committee holds primary responsibility for the application of the [[0-prc-2101_project_execution_model_gate_reviews_overview|PEM Control Level Milestone Gate Reviews procedure]].

**Composition**
The committee comprises key leadership personnel:
*   The President
*   The Director of Engineering
*   The Director of Projects

**Responsibilities**
The PEM Steering Committee is responsible for:
*   Ensuring that the Project Execution Model (PEM) is consistently applied to all applicable projects.
*   Approving the final reports generated from [[pem_milestone_gate_review_reporting_and_follow_up|Milestone Gate Reviews]].
*   Facilitating the sharing of best practices and lessons learned derived from the application of PEM across projects.
*   Facilitating independent auditing of the results from Milestone Reviews to maintain consistency and accuracy in their findings.

**Composition**

**Responsibilities**
In addition to primary responsibility for procedure application, the PEM Steering Committee is responsible for:
*   Ensuring that PEM is used on all applicable projects.
*   Approving final reports from Milestone Gate Reviews.
*   Facilitating the sharing of best practice and lessons learned from PEM application.
*   Facilitating independent auditing of the Milestone Reviews results to maintain consistency and accuracy.
