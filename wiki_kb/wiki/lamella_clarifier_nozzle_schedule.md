# Lamella Clarifier Nozzle Schedule

This page details the nozzle schedule for the Lamella Clarifier, providing essential information for piping connections and instrumentation, as specified in document [[4-PDS-XXXX-R1_template_clarifier_thickener_datasheet]]. A comprehensive nozzle schedule is critical for accurate piping design, fabrication, and installation, ensuring proper integration of the clarifier into the process system.

**Nozzle Table Fields:**
The nozzle schedule typically includes the following fields for each connection point on the Lamella Clarifier:
*   **Nozzle Description:** A clear, descriptive name for the nozzle, indicating its purpose (e.g., "FEED Inlet," "OVERFLOW Outlet," "UNDERFLOW Discharge," "Drain," "Vent," "Instrument Connection").
*   **Mark:** A unique alphanumeric identifier or tag for the specific nozzle, often correlating with [[line_list_5_lst_0001]] or [[plant_instrument_numbering_8_prc_0001]].
*   **Size (mm):** The nominal diameter of the nozzle connection, specified in millimeters. This size is crucial for piping design and flange selection.
*   **Quantity:** The number of identical nozzles of that description and size on the clarifier.
*   **Sizing Criteria / Accessories / Notes:** This field provides additional critical information, which may include:
    *   **Sizing Criteria:** The basis for determining the nozzle size, such as flow velocity limits, pressure drop considerations, or specific process requirements.
    *   **Accessories:** Any required attachments or components for the nozzle, such as internal dip pipes, vortex breakers, thermal wells, or specific flange types.
    *   **Notes:** Any other pertinent information, such as orientation requirements, special welding procedures, or specific material requirements beyond the general vessel material.

The accurate definition of these nozzle parameters **must** ensure that all necessary connections for process streams, utilities, and instrumentation are correctly designed and installed. This prevents costly rework during construction and commissioning.

For a complete understanding of the process fluids and their characteristics that influence nozzle sizing and material selection, refer to [[lamella_clarifier_process_design_conditions]]. Details on the overall equipment and material specifications can be found in [[lamella_clarifier_equipment_and_material_specifications]].
