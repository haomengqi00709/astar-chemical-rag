# 0-LST-6X15 Construction Subcontract Equipment Schedule

The 0-LST-6X15 document, known as the "Construction Subcontract Equipment Schedule," is a mandatory schedule that the Subcontractor **must** complete. This schedule is vital for planning and tracking the use of Construction Equipment on the project site.

The schedule **must** clearly show the weeks on site for all items of Construction Equipment. This includes:
*   All equipment used for the fabrication, erection, and installation of the Equipment and Materials, such as cranes, welding machines, and scaffolding.
*   Field services items, as specifically listed on [[0-LST-6X90-2_construction_subcontract_field_services_scope]].

This schedule is a key component for calculating and claiming [[construction_subcontract_indirect_work_costs]] related to equipment usage, including costs for rental, fuel, maintenance, and mobilisation/demobilisation.
