# 0-REP-8006a Task Safety Awareness Form

The Standard-0-REP-8006a, titled "Task Safety Awareness Form," is a crucial document used in the implementation of [[task_safety_awareness_overview]] (TSA). This form serves a dual purpose: it acts as a comprehensive checklist during the TSA process and functions as an official record of the completed review.

**Key Factors to Consider During a TSA (as per the form):**
The form guides supervisors and employees through a systematic review of potential hazards and necessary precautions. Important factors to consider and verify include:

*   **Procedures/Permits Required:**
    *   Hot Work (Open Flame)
    *   Confined Space Entry
    *   Excavation
    *   Lock and Tag
    *   Critical Lift Plan
    *   Signs/Barricades
    *   Others

*   **Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) Required:**
    *   Safety Harness & Lanyard
    *   Eye/face protection
    *   Respirator
    *   Foot / Toe protection
    *   Hand protection
    *   Full Body Clothing
    *   Hearing protection
    *   Others

*   **Job Planning and Briefing:**
    *   Confirmation of whether Company personnel are involved in the planning of the job.
    *   Verification that all employees have been briefed on the hazards associated with this job.

*   **Emergency Preparedness:**
    *   Assessment of whether emergency and rescue equipment is located in the immediate work area.

*   **Hazardous Materials:**
    *   Confirmation that Material Safety Data Sheets (MSDS's) have been reviewed with employees for all hazardous chemicals associated with the job.

*   **Personnel Qualification:**
    *   Verification of whether the employee is qualified to perform the task (e.g., fire watch, hole watch, attendant).

*   **Environmental Factors:**
    *   Consideration of whether weather conditions will affect the safe performance of this job.

*   **Specific Instructions:**
    *   Any other specific instructions relevant to the safe execution of the task.

The diligent completion of this form ensures that all critical safety aspects are addressed before work commences, supporting the overall [[task_safety_awareness_program_monitoring_and_improvement]].

The form guides users through important factors to consider during a TSA, including:
*   **Procedures/Permits Required:** Hot Work (Open Flame), Confined Space Entry, Excavation, Lock and Tag, Critical Lift Plan, Signs/Barricades, Others.
*   **Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) Required:** Safety Harness & Lanyard, Eye/face, Respirator, Foot / Toe, Hand, Full Body Clothing, Hearing, Others.
*   **Job Planning & Briefing:**
    *   Are Company personnel involved in the planning of the job?
    *   Have all employees been briefed on the hazards associated with this job?
*   **Emergency Preparedness:** Is emergency and rescue equipment located in the area?
*   **Chemical Hazards:** Have MSDS's been reviewed with employees on all hazardous chemicals associated with the job?

*   **Environmental Factors:** Will weather conditions affect the safe performance of this job?
*   **Other Specific Instructions:** Any other specific instructions relevant to the task.
