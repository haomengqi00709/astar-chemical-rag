# Site Emergency Evacuation Plan Requirements for Subcontractors

The main construction Subcontractor **is responsible for** preparing a comprehensive site emergency evacuation plan. This plan **must be prepared** and submitted to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Owner for review and acceptance prior to sending any trade workers to the work site.

**Mandatory Plan Contents:**
The site emergency evacuation plan **will contain** the following critical elements:
*   **Signals and Alarms**: Clear definitions and procedures for emergency signals and alarms.
*   **Evacuation Routes**: Clearly marked and communicated evacuation routes from all areas of the worksite.
*   **Assembly Points**: Designated and easily accessible assembly points for all personnel.
*   **Responsibilities**: Defined responsibilities for emergency supervisory personnel during an evacuation.

**Emergencies Covered by the Plan:**
The plan **will address** a range of potential emergencies, including but not limited to:
*   Weather emergencies
*   Earthquake scenarios (refer to [[earthquake_response]])
*   Fire emergencies (refer to [[fire_emergency_response]])
*   Hazardous materials handling incidents (refer to [[hazardous_materials_management_overview]])
*   Any other site-specific emergencies identified during risk assessment.

For general guidance on evacuation procedures, refer to [[building_evacuation_plan]].
