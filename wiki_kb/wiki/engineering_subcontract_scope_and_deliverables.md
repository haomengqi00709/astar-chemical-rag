# Engineering Subcontract Scope and Deliverables

The execution of engineering work and the preparation of associated documents for projects involving Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and its Subcontractors are meticulously defined and managed through a structured framework. Both Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Subcontractor **must** execute work and prepare documents as explicitly set out in the [[engineering_scope_checklists_0_lst_22xx|Engineering Scope Checklist]], identified as 0-LST-8[XXX]. This checklist serves as the primary reference for all tasks and deliverables, ensuring a clear understanding of responsibilities and expectations.

The collaborative nature of these projects necessitates precise adherence to established timelines for document and service delivery. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **shall** deliver documents to the Subcontractor no later than the dates specified on the 0-LST-8[XXX] Engineering Scope Checklist. Similarly, Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **shall** take all necessary actions within the deadlines stated on the same checklist. This commitment to timely action and delivery from Aker Kvaerner Chemetics is crucial for enabling the Subcontractor to proceed with their work without delays.

Correspondingly, the Subcontractor **shall** deliver documents to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics no later than the dates stated on the 0-LST-8[XXX] Engineering Scope Checklist. The Subcontractor is also responsible for providing services to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics within the timelines stipulated in the checklist. These mutual obligations ensure that project schedules are maintained, and all engineering phases progress efficiently.

The Engineering Scope Checklist (0-LST-8[XXX]) is a dynamic document that outlines the specific tasks, deliverables, and deadlines for each engineering service. It is instrumental in managing the workflow, tracking progress, and ensuring that all contractual obligations are met. Adherence to this checklist is paramount for maintaining project momentum, facilitating effective communication, and ultimately achieving successful project outcomes. Any deviations from the specified timelines or deliverables **must** be formally addressed and agreed upon by both parties to avoid impacts on the overall project schedule and quality.
