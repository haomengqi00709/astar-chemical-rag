# Electrical Field Checkout: Prerequisites and Tools (9-PRC-0004)

Before commencing the Electrical Field Checkout Procedure (9-PRC-0004), several critical prerequisites **must be met** to ensure an efficient and effective inspection process.

**Prerequisites:**
*   [[8-PRC-0014_dcs_field_hardware_checkout_8_prc_0014|DCS Hardware]] **must be fully installed and checked** in accordance with procedure 8-PRC-0014.
*   Field wiring, including remote I/O sub systems, **must be completed**.
*   [[8-PRC-0004_control_system_design_and_specification|DCS Control Functions]] **must be ready to be checked out**. While the detailed checkout process is typically described in [[8-prc-0006_dcs_control_function_checkout]], the readiness criteria for these functions are specified as per procedure 8-PRC-0004.
*   The [[9-PRC-0020_electrical_systems_contractor_checkout_procedure|Electrical Systems Contractor Checkout Procedure 9-PRC-0020]] **must be completed**.

**Required Tools:**
To perform the electrical field checkout, the following tools **are required**:
*   A Multimeter for various electrical measurements.
*   A Loop signal generator (calibrator) for signal testing and calibration.
