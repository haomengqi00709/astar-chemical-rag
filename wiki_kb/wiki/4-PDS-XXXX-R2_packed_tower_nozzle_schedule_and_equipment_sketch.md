# Packed Tower Nozzle Schedule and Equipment Sketch (4-PDS-XXXX-R2)

The Packed Tower Process Data Sheet (4-PDS-XXXX-R2) includes provisions for a detailed nozzle table and guidelines for providing an equipment sketch. These elements are essential for the mechanical design, fabrication, and installation of the packed tower.

**Nozzle Table:**
The nozzle table within the datasheet is structured with the following columns to provide comprehensive details for each nozzle:
*   **Nozzle Description:** A brief description of the nozzle's purpose (e.g., Inlet, Outlet, Drain, Vent).
*   **Mark:** A unique identifier or tag for the nozzle.
*   **Size (mm):** The nominal diameter of the nozzle in millimeters.
*   **Quantity:** The number of identical nozzles.
*   **Sizing Criteria / Accessories / Notes:** Additional information regarding the nozzle's sizing basis, any required accessories (e.g., flanges, valves), or specific notes relevant to its design or installation.

For general information on vessel nozzle tables, refer to [[template_vessel_nozzle_table]].

**Equipment Sketch Requirements:**
*   **Mandatory Requirement:** An equipment sketch **IS REQUIRED** when it is necessary to communicate special dimensions and features. This includes, but is not limited to, the relative positions of nozzles, internal baffles, or other unique design elements that cannot be adequately conveyed through tabular data alone.
*   **Non-Mandatory Status:** The equipment sketch is otherwise not mandatory if all necessary information can be clearly understood from the data sheet and other referenced documents.

The provision of an equipment sketch ensures that complex geometric details and spatial relationships are accurately communicated to fabricators and installers. For details on proprietary detail drawings, refer to [[equipment_proprietary_detail_drawing_prp]].
