# Metal Ductwork Welding and Non-Destructive Examination (5-SPC-0010)

**Welding Procedures**
All welding operations **shall** be performed per the Vendor’s welding procedures. Specific welding requirements are detailed in:
*   [[4-SPC-0060_carbon_steel_welding_standard_overview]]: Welding Carbon Steel.
*   [[4-SPC-0061_austenitic_stainless_steel_welding_standard_overview]]: Welding Austenitic Stainless Steel.

**Non-Destructive Examination (NDE)**

*   **Radiographic Examination:** Butt welds in the ductwork **shall** undergo spot radiographic examination in accordance with [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels]], Paragraph UW52. Radiography **shall** be performed in accordance with [[ASME_section_v_non_destructive_examination]], Article 2. Weld intersections will be the preferred locations for radiographs. Radiography locations **shall** be selected to examine the welding of each welder/welding operator.

*   **Liquid Penetrant Examination:** Welds between expansion joints, their hinges, and the ducting **shall** be subject to liquid penetrant examination. This examination **shall** be performed in accordance with [[ASME_section_v_non_destructive_examination]], Article 6. Acceptance standards for liquid penetrant examination **shall** be per [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels]], Appendix 8.

*   **Magnetic Particle Examination:** When required or specified, magnetic particle examination **shall** be performed in accordance with [[ASME_section_v_non_destructive_examination]], Article 7. Acceptance standards for magnetic particle examination **shall** be per [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels]], Appendix 6.

Any flaws or defects discovered during NDE that cannot be reasonably repaired, or excessive repairs, may be considered evidence of poor workmanship and grounds for rejection, as per [[5-SPC-0010_metal_ductwork_fabrication_and_assembly]].
