# 9-LST-0724 Welding Receptacle System Installation Field Check List

The 9-LST-0724 Welding Receptacle System Installation Field Check List is a specific document used during the [[9-PRC-0004_electrical_field_checkout_documentation|Electrical Field Checkout Procedure (9-PRC-0004)]] to verify the correct installation of welding receptacle systems. This checklist ensures that all receptacles and associated wiring are installed according to engineering specifications and safety standards.
