# Shell & Tube Heat Exchanger Non-Destructive Examination Requirements (0-QDS-7417)

For the Shell & Tube Heat Exchanger specified in 0-QDS-7417, comprehensive Non-Destructive Examination (NDE) requirements are mandated to ensure the structural integrity and quality of welds and materials without causing damage.

**General NDE Requirements (Item 10.0):**
All Non-Destructive Examination **must** be performed in accordance with the latest editions of the ASME Boiler & Pressure Vessel Code Sections V (Non-Destructive Examination) and VIII (Pressure Vessels). Alternatively, NDE may be conducted in accordance with an approved equivalent standard, provided it offers comparable levels of assurance and is formally approved by Chemetics.

**Specific NDE Methods:**

*   **Radiography (Item 11.0 & 12.0):**
    *   **Titanium or 100% Joint Efficiency Welds (Item 11.0):**
        *   **Requirement:** 100% Radiography
        *   **Quality Record Required:** FP ("Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package")
        *   **Surveillance by Chemetics:** Complete
        *   For welds involving titanium or those requiring 100% joint efficiency, full radiography **must** be performed. This ensures the highest level of inspection for critical welds.
        *   **Alternative:** Spot Radiography (25%) may be specified for certain applications, as indicated in the remarks.
    *   **All Other Butt Welds (Item 12.0):**
        *   **Requirement:** Spot Radiography
        *   **Quality Record Required:** FP ("Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package")
        *   **Surveillance by Chemetics:** Complete
        *   For all other butt welds, spot radiography is required.
        *   **Alternative:** Full Radiography (100%) may be specified for certain applications, as indicated in the remarks.

*   **Dye Penetrant (Item 13.0 & 14.0):**
    *   **Titanium Welds + Root & Final Passes of Dissimilar Metal Welds (Item 13.0):**
        *   **Requirement:** 100% Dye Penetrant
        *   **Quality Record Required:** FP ("Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package")
        *   **Surveillance by Chemetics:** Witness and Complete
        *   All titanium welds, as well as the root and final passes of dissimilar metal welds, **must** undergo 100% dye penetrant testing. Chemetics will witness this examination and perform a complete review of the results.
    *   **Butt Welds Except Carbon Steel (CS) (Item 14.0):**
        *   **Requirement:** 0.25 (25%) Dye Penetrant
        *   **Quality Record Required:** FP ("Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package")
        *   **Surveillance by Chemetics:** Complete
        *   For butt welds, excluding those in carbon steel, a 25% dye penetrant examination is required.

All NDE results and reports **must** be documented and included in the final Quality Assurance Documentation Package. These requirements are crucial for verifying the integrity of the heat exchanger's fabrication and are part of the broader [[0-QDS-7417_vendor_quality_assurance_requirements|Vendor Quality Assurance Requirements]]. For definitions of quality record designations, refer to [[0-QDS-7417_quality_record_definitions]].
