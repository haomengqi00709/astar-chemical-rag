# Appendix 3.05 Decision Logic for Safety Training Program

Appendix 3.05, titled "Decision Logic for Safety Training Program," serves as a crucial reference document for determining and structuring safety training initiatives.

This appendix provides guidance and a logical framework for analyzing training needs and identifying the appropriate safety training programs. It is specifically referenced for guidance in the planning and budgeting of safety training for projects, as detailed in [[project_hse_training_planning]].

The principles and methodologies outlined in this appendix support the comprehensive [[0-PRC-8016_hse_training_program_overview]] by ensuring that training decisions are systematic and aligned with company objectives and regulatory requirements.
