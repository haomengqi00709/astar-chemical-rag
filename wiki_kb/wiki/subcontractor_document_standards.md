# Subcontractor Document Preparation Standards

To ensure consistency, quality, and seamless integration of documentation across projects, Subcontractors working with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **must** adhere to specific document preparation standards. A core requirement is that the Subcontractor **shall** use templates provided by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for all document preparation. These templates are specifically designed to align with the firm's internal documentation protocols, formatting guidelines, and content structure.

The application of these mandatory templates extends to all documents described in the [[engineering_scope_checklists_0_lst_22xx|Engineering Scope Checklist]], identified as 0-LST-8[XXX]. This ensures that whether the document is a technical report, a drawing, or a data sheet, it will conform to a standardized format, making it easier for Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' teams to review, approve, and integrate into the project's overall documentation package. The use of these templates minimizes discrepancies, reduces review cycles, and enhances the overall efficiency of document management.

Furthermore, the Subcontractor **shall** provide document content that is consistent with the Sample Documents provided by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. These sample documents serve as practical examples of the expected level of detail, technical accuracy, and presentation style. By aligning their content with these samples, Subcontractors can ensure that their deliverables meet the qualitative benchmarks set by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. This includes adherence to specific terminology, data presentation formats, and the inclusion of all necessary information as demonstrated in the samples.

Adherence to these document preparation standards is not merely a procedural formality; it is a critical component of quality assurance and project success. It facilitates clear communication, reduces potential misunderstandings, and ensures that all project documentation is professional, accurate, and readily usable by all stakeholders. Failure to comply with these standards may result in document rejections and delays in project timelines.
