# Vendor Cooperation and Subcontracting Management

This page details the Vendor's obligations regarding cooperation with other parties and the management of subcontracted work under the Purchase Order (PO).

**Cooperation with Others:**
*   The Vendor **must** cooperate with [[purchase_order_definitions#Others|Others]] in obtaining information they need in connection with the [[purchase_order_definitions#The Goods and Services|Goods and Services]].
*   The Vendor **must** also cooperate with Others in providing information they need in connection with the Goods and Services.

**Subcontracting Responsibilities:**
*   If the Vendor subcontracts any work, they remain fully responsible for performing this Purchase Order as if they had not subcontracted. This means the Vendor's obligations and liabilities to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics are not diminished by the use of subcontractors.
*   This Purchase Order applies as if a [[purchase_order_definitions#A Supplier|Supplier’s]] employees and equipment were the Vendor's.
