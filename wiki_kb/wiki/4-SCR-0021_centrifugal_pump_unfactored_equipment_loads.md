# Centrifugal Pump Unfactored Equipment Loads (4-SCR-0021)

This page outlines the specific unfactored equipment loads for the centrifugal pump as defined in document 4-SCR-0021, titled 'Centrifugal Pump on Concrete and Grout'. These loads represent the raw, unadjusted forces and masses that the pump assembly exerts, and they are fundamental inputs for the structural design of its foundation and support system. Accurate determination of these unfactored loads is paramount to ensure the long-term stability, safety, and operational integrity of the equipment.

The key unfactored equipment loads are:

*   **Assembled Pump Mass**: This value, expressed in kilograms (kg), encompasses the total weight of the centrifugal pump, its motor, and any other components permanently integrated into the pump assembly. This static load is a primary consideration for the bearing capacity of the concrete foundation.
*   **Power**: The operational power consumption of the pump, measured in kilowatts (kW). While primarily an electrical parameter, it indirectly influences mechanical loads by indicating the work performed and the potential for dynamic forces and vibrations during operation.
*   **Full Load Speed**: The rotational speed of the pump when operating at its maximum design capacity, specified in revolutions per minute (RPM). This dynamic parameter is crucial for analyzing vibrational characteristics, potential resonance with the foundation, and the overall dynamic stability of the pump system.

These unfactored loads are the starting point for all subsequent structural engineering calculations. They are used in conjunction with applicable design codes and safety factors to determine the final design of the concrete pad, anchor bolts, and grouting.
