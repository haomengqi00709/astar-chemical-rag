# Coating Specification Development and Standards

The Coating Specification details the technical requirements for surface preparation and application of coatings across the project. This document is crucial for ensuring the longevity and performance of coated components.

**Content Requirements:**
*   Surface preparation requirements, as applicable, shall be included.
*   Application requirements, as applicable, shall be included.

**Preparation and Approval:**
*   The Coating Specification will be prepared by the Mechanical discipline.
*   It will be checked by the Project Engineer.
*   The approval authority for this document is not specified in the provided facts.

**Standard Coating Specifications:**
In the absence of specific statutory or Owner requirements, Mechanical will issue a standard Coating Specification. The following standard specifications are available for selection:
*   [[7-SPC-0001_protective_coating_specification|7-SPC-0001: Protective Coating Electrochemical & Pulp & Paper]]
*   7-SPC-0002: Protective Coating Acid Plants (Shop Applied)
*   7-SPC-0003: Protective Coating Acid Plants (Field Applied)
*   7-SPC-0004: Protective Coating Calcium Hypochlorite Plants
*   7-SPC-0005: Protective Coating Underground Piping

**Modifications to Standard Specifications:**
*   If applicable, Mechanical will modify the standard coating specification to incorporate specific statutory and Owner requirements.
*   Mechanical shall obtain input from the Materials Engineer and other Discipline Leads as required for any modifications.
*   **Mandatory:** Modified specifications must be re-checked as required by [[0-prc-0001_standard_document_management_procedure|0-PRC-0001: Approval and Use of Standards]].
*   **Mandatory:** Modified specifications must be re-approved as required by [[0-prc-0001_standard_document_management_procedure|0-PRC-0001: Approval and Use of Standards]].
