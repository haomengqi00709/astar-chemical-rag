# Subcontractor Industrial Relations Management (0-SPC-60)

This document, 0-SPC-60, specifies the requirements for industrial relations management plans to be developed by construction subcontractors for the Project. It outlines the mandatory procedures and policies for managing labor relations, site conduct, and dispute resolution.

The Subcontractor is required to develop industrial relations management plans in accordance with this specification, ensuring a harmonious and productive work environment on site.
