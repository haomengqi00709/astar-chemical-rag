# Carbon Steel Welding Processes for Carbon Steel

Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' specification 4-SPC-0060 outlines the acceptable welding processes and specific requirements for each when fabricating carbon steel components. The Vendor **must** give close consideration to root pass quality.

**Acceptable Welding Processes:**
The following welding processes are acceptable:
*   Shielded Metal Arc Welding (SMAW)
*   Gas Tungsten Arc Welding (GTAW)
*   Gas Metal Arc Welding (GMAW)
*   Submerged Arc Welding (SAW)

For process-exposed root passes, the acceptable welding processes are GTAW, GMAW, and SMAW.

**Shielded Metal Arc Welding (SMAW)**
*   SMAW electrodes **must** satisfy [[ASME_sfa_5_4_welding_electrodes|SFA-5.1]] requirements.
*   SMAW of carbon steel **must** be performed using E7018 or E7028 electrodes.
*   For pipe root passes, E6010 or E6011 electrodes may be used.

**Gas Tungsten Arc Welding (GTAW)**
*   The GTAW weld metal thickness **must not** exceed 3mm (⅛") per pass.
*   GTAW heat input **must** be kept low by employing a stringer bead technique.

**Gas Metal Arc Welding (GMAW)**
*   GMAW may be used for the root pass of butt welds.
*   GMAW may be used for fillet welds.
*   GMAW may be used for the complete welding of other joint details.
*   The Vendor **must** provide satisfactory evidence of continuous shop use of the GMAW process.
*   A minimum of two passes is **required** on all thicknesses for GMAW.
*   The short circuit mode of transfer for GMAW **must not** be used unless the specific application receives prior approval from Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Submerged Arc Welding (SAW)**
*   The SAW weld metal thickness **must not** exceed 13mm (½") per pass.
*   Run-on and run-off tabs, made of the same material specification as the base material, **must** be used on all SAW longitudinal seams.
*   Wire and flux combinations recommended for single pass welding **must not** be used for multiple pass welds in SAW.
*   A chemical analysis of the finished deposit **must** be included as part of the SAW weld procedure qualification.

Acceptable welding processes for process exposed root passes are GTAW, GMAW, and SMAW.

**Shielded Metal Arc Welding (SMAW)**
*   SMAW electrodes must satisfy SFA-5.1 requirements.
*   SMAW of carbon steel must be done using E7018 or E7028 electrodes.
*   Pipe root passes may be done using E6010 or E6011 electrodes.

**Gas Tungsten Arc Welding (GTAW)**
*   GTAW weld metal thickness must not exceed 3mm (⅛") per pass.
*   GTAW heat input must be kept low by using stringer bead technique.

**Gas Metal Arc Welding (GMAW)**
*   GMAW may be used for the root pass of butt welds, for fillet welds, and for complete welding of other joint details.
*   The Vendor must provide satisfactory evidence of continuous shop use of the GMAW process.
*   A minimum of two passes is required on all thicknesses for GMAW.
*   The short circuit mode of transfer for GMAW must not be used unless the specific application is approved by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Submerged Arc Welding (SAW)**
*   SAW weld metal thickness must not exceed 13mm (½") per pass.
*   Run-on and run-off tabs of the same material specification as the base material must be used on all SAW longitudinal seams.
*   Wire and flux combinations recommended for single pass welding must not be used for multiple pass welds in SAW.
*   A chemical analysis of the finished deposit must be included as part of the SAW weld procedure qualification.
*   When high carbon or high manganese fluxes are used for low temperature service equipment fabrication, the Vendor must provide sectioned samples (which may be from run-on/off tabs) and satisfactory evidence of continuous shop use of the SAW process. This requirement is due to the possibility of microcracking.
