# Glass-Lined Vessel Design and Fabrication Requirements (4-SPC-0044)

This section details the mandatory design and fabrication requirements for glass-lined carbon steel reaction vessels, ensuring compliance with industry standards and specific project needs.

**General Design Principles**
The Fabricator is responsible for the design of the vessel. The vessel **shall** be designed and fabricated in accordance with [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels|ASME Boiler and Pressure Vessel Code, Section VIII, Division 1]] (latest edition). The use of a design code other than specified is only permitted after written agreement between the Purchaser and Fabricator. No deviation from these requirements **shall** be permitted without the written agreement of the Purchaser.

**Design Conditions**
Unless otherwise stated on the Purchase Order or Vessel Data Sheet:
*   Design temperatures are from -25°C to 230°C (-13°F to 446°F).
*   Design pressures are up to 140 psig (965 kPag) or full vacuum in the vessel.
*   Design pressures are coincident with up to 55 psig (380 kPag) or full vacuum in the jacket.

The Vendor **shall** be consulted if operation outside these specified limits is contemplated. The upper temperature limit **shall** be reduced to the Vendor’s recommended safe design temperature if the maximum continuous temperature can affect the performance of PTFE enveloped gaskets.

**Jacketed Vessels**
For jacketed vessels, both the jacket and vessel compartments **must** be designed to independently withstand their respective design temperatures and pressures as stated on individual vessel drawings. Jacketed vessels **shall** also satisfy the requirements of Appendix IX of [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels|Section VIII, Division 1, ASME Code]]. Cooling or heating jackets **shall** be supplied as specified on the vessel drawings.

Inspection openings **shall** be located in the jackets as required by [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels|ASME Code]], with a minimum size of 2-inch (50mm). These openings **shall** include a flanged cover, suitable gaskets, studbolts, and nuts. Impingement baffles **shall** be located opposite the jacket nozzles, and each jacket **shall** incorporate a spiral baffle or special inlet nozzles to produce a spiraling effect. Water or steam will be circulated through the jackets, with the design flow rate provided in the vessel data sheets or drawings. Jacket closing welds **are to be** in accordance with [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels|ASME Code, Section VIII, Div. 1, Appendix IX]] requirements.

**Corrosion Allowance**
*   For the non-jacketed portion of the vessel shell, the glass-lining provides the corrosion allowance; no extra corrosion allowance is required.
*   For jacketed vessels, a 1/16" (1.5mm) corrosion allowance **shall** be used on the outside of the vessel shell (jacketed section) and on the inside of the jacket.
*   Steam condensate outlet nozzles on jackets **shall** have a 1/8" (3mm) corrosion allowance.
*   A 1/16" (1.5mm) corrosion allowance **shall** be used for all other jacket nozzles.

**Heads and Openings**
The top and bottom heads for all vessels **shall** preferably be ellipsoidal. Openings in the top head **shall** be arranged to permit maintenance access to the mixer and driver. Bottom heads **shall** have a minimum number of openings. Except for the radial manhole nozzle, all nozzles in the vessel heads **shall** be the Fabricator’s standard vertical or radial type. All nozzles or manway openings in the shell or heads **shall** be swaged. For vessels with close-fitting internal parts, all nozzles that may obstruct entrance or assembly **shall** be flush and ground to a smooth curvature suitable for glass-lining application. Each vessel **shall** have an 18" (450mm) circular manhole in the top head, unless otherwise specified on data sheets, including necessary swing-clamps to secure the joint against the design pressure.

**Gaskets**
Gaskets **shall** be provided to match 10K JIS flanges, which may be split or solid type. Gaskets in contact with glass surfaces **shall** be suitable for design pressure service without excessive flange bolt loading. Gaskets **shall** be non-graphite containing. A machined or channel type envelope gasket style is acceptable, but filler (asbestos or Vendor standard) for envelope gaskets **must** be approved by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. Elastomer fillers and slit style gaskets with heat sealed I.D.'s are not acceptable. All gasket material in contact with glass surfaces **shall** be made of Teflon (Virgin PTFE). Teflon enveloped gaskets **shall** satisfy the requirements of ASTM F336 as modified by 4-SPC-0044. All flange bolt holes **shall** straddle the centre line of the vessel.

**Internal Components and Supports**
When internal baffles are required, the Fabricator **shall** provide information on the number, width, and materials required, which **shall** be shown on the drawing or data sheet for each vessel. The method of attachment for baffles **shall** be specified in the quotation. Internal supports (clips, brackets, studs, bolts, plates, nuts, and washers) **shall** be designed and supplied by the Vessel Fabricator Vendor. Suitable materials for internal supports **shall** be shown on the data sheets or drawings.

Normally, each vessel **shall** be supported by either lugs, a continuous ring, or saddles. The Vessel Fabricator **shall** design the support and furnish design calculations for supports if agreed previously between Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Supplier. Vessel fabricator **shall** provide insulation support details as called for on the vessel drawings.

**Loads**
External loads **shall** be kept to an absolute minimum on vessel nozzles. The earthquake and wind loads used for design **shall** conform to the appropriate National Standards. The earthquake zone **shall** be specified on the data sheet and/or vessel drawings. The vessel shell **shall not** be stamped unless otherwise specified.

**General Design Principles**
*   Vessels shall be manufactured in accordance with the Manufacturer’s standard procedure for glass-lined vessels.
*   In the event of conflict between Codes and Standards, the more stringent requirement shall govern.
*   All codes, Standards, etc., referred to herein, shall be interpreted as meaning the latest published revisions at time of quotation.
*   The Fabricator shall be responsible for obtaining the approval of design and/or fabrication and registration of pressure equipment by the appropriate jurisdiction.
*   The cost of external inspection services required for approvals shall be included in Fabricator’s proposal.
*   No deviation from these requirements shall be permitted without the written agreement of the Purchaser.
*   Unless otherwise stated on the Purchase Order/Vessel Data Sheet, design temperatures are from -25°C to 230°C (-13°F to 446°F).
*   Unless otherwise stated on the Purchase Order/Vessel Data Sheet, design pressures are up to 140 psig (965 kPag) or full vacuum in the vessel.
*   Unless otherwise stated on the Purchase Order/Vessel Data Sheet, design pressures are coincident with up to 55 psig (380 kPag) or full vacuum in the jacket.
*   Vendor shall be consulted if operation outside these limits is contemplated.
*   The upper temperature limit shall be reduced to Vendor’s recommended safe design temperature if the upper limit of maximum continuous temperature can affect the performance of the PTFE enveloped gasket.
*   A Design Code other than specified may be used only after written agreement between the Purchaser and Fabricator.

**Jacketed Vessel Design**
*   Jacket and vessel compartments on jacketed vessels must be designed to independently withstand the design temperature and the design pressure stated for the jacket and the vessel on the individual vessel drawings.
*   Jacketed vessels shall also satisfy the requirements of Appendix IX of Section VIII, Division 1, ASME Code.
*   Cooling or heating jackets shall be supplied as called for on the vessel drawings.
*   Inspection openings shall be located in the jackets as required by ASME Code.
*   Inspection openings shall be 2-inch (50mm) minimum size.
*   Inspection openings shall have flanged cover, suitable gaskets, studbolts, and nuts.
*   Impingement baffles shall be located opposite the jacket nozzles.
*   Each jacket shall include a spiral baffle or special inlet nozzles which will produce a spiraling effect.
*   Water or steam will be circulated through the jackets.
*   The design flow rate shall be given in the vessel data sheets or drawings.

**Corrosion Allowance**
*   In the non-jacketed portion of the vessel shell, the glass-lining provides the corrosion allowance. No extra corrosion allowance is required for the non-jacketed portion of the vessel shell.
*   For jacketed vessels, a 1/16" (1.5mm) corrosion allowance shall be used on the outside of the vessel shell (jacketed section).
*   For jacketed vessels, a 1/16" (1.5mm) corrosion allowance shall be used on the inside of the jacket.
*   Steam condensate outlet nozzles on jackets shall have 1/8" (3mm) corrosion allowance.
*   A 1/16" (1.5mm) corrosion allowance shall be used for all other jacket nozzles.

**Loads and Heads**
*   The earthquake and wind loads to be used for design shall conform to the appropriate National Standards.
*   Earthquake zone shall be specified on data sheet and/or vessel drawings.
*   The top and bottom heads for all vessels shall preferably be ellipsoidal.
*   Openings in the top head shall be arranged to permit maintenance access to mixer and driver.
*   Bottom heads shall have a minimum number of openings.
*   Except for the radial manhole nozzle, all nozzles in the vessel heads shall be Fabricator‘s standard vertical or radial type nozzles.
*   Each vessel shall have an 18" (450mm) circular manhole in the top head, unless otherwise called for on data sheets. Necessary swing-clamps to secure the joint against the design pressure shall be included.
*   All nozzles or manway openings in the shell or heads shall be swaged.
*   For vessels equipped with close fitting internal parts, all nozzles that may obstruct entrance or assembly shall be flush and ground to a smooth curvature suitable for glass-lining application.

**Gaskets and Nozzles**
*   Gaskets shall be provided to match 10K JIS flanges. The flanges may be split or solid type.
*   Gaskets in contact with glass surfaces shall be suitable for design pressure service without excessive flange bolt loading.
*   External loads shall be kept to an absolute minimum on vessel nozzles.
*   Gaskets shall be non-graphite containing. A machined or channel type envelope gasket style is acceptable.
*   Filler (asbestos or Vendor standard) for envelope gaskets must be approved by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. Elastomer fillers are not acceptable. Slit style gaskets with heat sealed I.D.'s are not acceptable.
*   All gasket material in contact with glass surfaces shall be made of Teflon (Virgin PTFE).
*   Teflon enveloped gaskets shall satisfy the requirement of ASTM F336 as modified by 4-SPC-0044.
*   All flange bolt holes shall straddle the centre line of the vessel.

**Vessel Supports**
*   Internal supports (clips, brackets, studs, bolts, plates, nuts and washers) shall be designed and supplied by the Vessel Fabricator Vendor.
*   Normally, each vessel shall be supported by either (a) lugs, (b) continuous ring, or (c) saddles.
*   The Vessel Fabricator shall design the support.

**Nameplates**
*   A stainless steel nameplate attached to each vessel by a bracket projecting beyond any insulation shall be furnished by Manufacturer.
*   The nameplate shall be located immediately below the vessel manhole.
*   The nameplate shall show the item number, service and minimum data required by ASME Code.
*   The nameplate shall show minimum data required by the appropriate Provincial Department of Labour in Canada or National Board in US.
*   An Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' supplied nameplate as shown in Appendix B shall be attached to the same bracket.
*   The vessel shell shall not be stamped unless otherwise specified.
