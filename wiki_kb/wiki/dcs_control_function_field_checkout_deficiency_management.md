# DCS Control Function Field Checkout Deficiency Management

Effective management of deficiencies is paramount to the successful completion of the [[8-PRC-0006_dcs_control_function_checkout|DCS Control Function Field Checkout]]. This process ensures that all identified issues are addressed, re-verified, and formally accepted before the Distributed Control System (DCS) is cleared for operation.

**Recording and Reporting Deficiencies**
*   Any deficiencies identified during the checkout process **are recorded**.
*   These recorded deficiencies **are given** to the site supervisor/commissioning manager. This ensures that the appropriate management personnel are aware of outstanding issues.

**Correction and Re-verification**
*   Identified deficiencies **will be rectified** on the spot whenever possible.
*   If immediate rectification is not feasible, the deficiencies **are entered** into the "comments" column of the [[8-LST-0102_dcs_configuration_checkout_sheet|DCS Configuration Checkout Sheets]].
*   The site supervisor/commissioning manager **works with** the specialist and DCS programmer to ensure that all identified and listed deficiencies **must** be corrected.
*   Following correction, the rectified items **must** be rechecked and signed off by the specialist. This step confirms that the corrective actions have been effective.

**Formal Acceptance and Sign-off**
*   The complete DCS **cannot** be signed-off as "Ready for water batching and commissioning" until all identified deficiencies are corrected, rechecked, and formally signed off. This strict requirement ensures that the system is fully compliant and functional before proceeding to the next project phase.

This structured approach to deficiency management guarantees that the DCS meets all engineering specifications and operational requirements, contributing to the overall safety and reliability of the plant.
