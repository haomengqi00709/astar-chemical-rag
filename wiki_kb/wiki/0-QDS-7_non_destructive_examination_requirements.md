# Converter Shop Non-Destructive Examination (NDE) Requirements (0-QDS-7401)

This page details the Non-Destructive Examination (NDE) requirements for the Converter Shop project, as specified in Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7401. All NDE activities **must** be conducted in accordance with ASME Boiler & Pressure Vessel Code Sections V & VIII.

For general NDE requirements, refer to [[non_destructive_examination_requirements]].

**Radiographic Testing (RT):**

*   **Butt Weld Ends Intersecting Field Welds:**
    *   **Scope:** 100% radiographic testing is required.
    *   **Documentation:** Radiographic testing for these welds is required (FP).
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Complete.

*   **Butt Welds of Pressure Envelope & Core:**
    *   **Scope:** Spot radiographic testing is required.
    *   **Documentation:** Radiographic testing for these welds is required (FP).
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Complete.

**Dye Penetrant Testing (PT):**

*   **Butt Welds of Dissimilar Metals (Nozzle Ends):**
    *   **Scope:** 100% dye penetrant testing is required.
    *   **Documentation:** Dye penetrant testing for these welds is required (FP).
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Witness and Hold.

*   **Root Pass of Butt Welds - Pressure Envelope & Core:**
    *   **Scope:** 100% dye penetrant testing is required.
    *   **Documentation:** Dye penetrant testing for these welds is required (FP).
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Witness and Hold.
