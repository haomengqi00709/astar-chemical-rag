# Shell and Tube Heat Exchanger Field Inspection Items

The field inspection of Shell and Tube Heat Exchangers is a critical step to ensure safe and efficient operation. This checklist outlines mandatory items that **must** be verified during installation and pre-commissioning. Adherence to these requirements is essential for the integrity and performance of the heat exchanger.

**General Installation and Cleanliness Requirements:**
*   The **Nameplate must be installed and correct**. Verification of the nameplate ensures that the correct equipment is installed and matches documentation.
*   **Foundation/Anchor bolts must be secure and tightened**. Proper anchoring prevents movement and ensures structural stability.
*   The **Sliding support must be free to move**. This is crucial to accommodate thermal expansion and contraction without inducing stress on the exchanger.
*   **Inlet/outlet piping must be properly aligned and supported**. Misaligned or unsupported piping can lead to excessive loads on the exchanger nozzles. Refer to [[piping_design_and_specification_5_prc_0003]] for detailed piping requirements.
*   **No stress must be on nozzles from piping**. This is a direct consequence of proper piping alignment and support, preventing damage to the exchanger.
*   The **Exchanger must be clean and dry**. Internal cleanliness is vital to prevent contamination of process fluids and ensure optimal heat transfer.
*   **All foreign material must be removed from the exchanger**. This includes debris from fabrication, shipping, or installation.
*   **Inspection ports must be open for verification of cleanliness**. This allows for visual confirmation that the interior is free of foreign material.
*   **Accessories (gauges, vents, drain valves etc.) must be installed**. These components are necessary for monitoring, operation, and maintenance.
*   **Grounding must be completed**. Proper grounding is a safety requirement to prevent electrical hazards. Refer to [[grounding_installation_details]] for specifications.
*   **Inspection ports must be closed with service gasket installed**. Once cleanliness is verified, ports must be sealed to maintain system integrity.
*   **Flange Guards must be installed**. These provide protection for personnel from potential leaks or sprays at flange connections.

For the overall administration and context of this checklist, refer to [[4-FIC-XX16-R0_shell_and_tube_exchanger_field_inspection_checklist_overview]]. For specific requirements related to anodically protected heat exchangers, see [[anodically_protected_heat_exchanger_installation_requirements]].

### General Inspection Points:
*   **Nameplate:** Must be installed and correct.
*   **Foundation/Anchor Bolts:** Must be secure and tightened.
*   **Sliding Support:** Must be free to move.
*   **Piping Alignment:** Inlet/outlet piping must be properly aligned and supported.
*   **Nozzle Stress:** No stress must be on nozzles from piping.
*   **Exchanger Cleanliness:** Exchanger must be clean and dry.
*   **Foreign Material:** All foreign material must be removed from the exchanger.
*   **Inspection Ports (Open):** Must be open for verification of cleanliness.
*   **Accessories:** Gauges, vents, drain valves etc., must be installed.
*   **Grounding:** Must be completed.
*   **Inspection Ports (Closed):** Must be closed with service gasket installed.
*   **Flange Guards:** Must be installed.
