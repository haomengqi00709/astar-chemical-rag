# Procurement and Logistics Strategy

All procurement and expediting activities for the project will be managed from the Vancouver office.

**Delivery Terms:**
Delivery terms for all goods are DDP (Delivered to site, Duty Paid).

**Logistics Services:**
Rohde and Liesenfeld will be responsible for all logistics services, utilizing their in-house personnel. Their services are provided in accordance with an existing Strategic Alliance Agreement. Rohde and Liesenfeld will subcontract to their worldwide offices as required to facilitate global logistics.
*   North American shipments will be consolidated into containers in Vancouver.
*   European shipments will be consolidated in Hamburg.

**Vendor Surveillance:**
*   Vancouver-based personnel will conduct surveillance of North American Vendors.
*   Surveillance of European and Australasian Vendors will be subcontracted to Germanischer-Lloyd.

**Supplier Selection:**
*   Nominated suppliers are explicitly listed in the Contract and indicated as such in the Procurement Plan.
*   Nominated Vendors **must** be included on bid lists.
*   Awarding contracts to other Vendors is possible but requires explicit Owner approval.
*   There is a contractual requirement to provide local (Queensland, Australia) companies with an opportunity to bid for project work.

**Duty Refunds:**
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics will only apply for duty refunds for goods valued at greater than CAD 25,000. [Company used in Orica] will be engaged to assist in obtaining these duty refunds.

Supply responsibilities between Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and Module/Construction Subcontractors are delineated in Data available in SQL: 0-LST-2002.
