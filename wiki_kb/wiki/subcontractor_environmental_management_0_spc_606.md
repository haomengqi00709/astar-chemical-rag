# Subcontractor Environmental Management (0-SPC-606)

This document, 0-SPC-606, specifies the requirements for environmental management plans to be developed by construction subcontractors for the Project. It outlines the mandatory procedures and controls for minimizing environmental impact during construction activities.

The Subcontractor is required to develop environmental management plans in accordance with this specification, ensuring compliance with environmental regulations and project-specific environmental objectives.
