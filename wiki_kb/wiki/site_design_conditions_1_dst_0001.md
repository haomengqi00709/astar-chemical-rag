# Site Conditions Data Sheet (1-DST-0001)

The Site Conditions Data Sheet (1-DST-0001) is a crucial document that compiles all relevant environmental and operational parameters specific to the project site. This data sheet provides essential information for the design of equipment and facilities, ensuring they are suitable for the intended operating environment.

**Content**
The content of the Site Conditions Data Sheet is detailed in 1-DST-0001: Site Design Conditions. It typically includes, but is not limited to, climatic data, seismic requirements, utility availability, and other site-specific design considerations.

**Document Preparation and Approval**
The preparation, checking, and approval of the Site Conditions Data Sheet follow a defined workflow:

*   **Preparation:** The document **must** be prepared by Mechanical personnel.
*   **Checking:** The document **must** be checked by Process, Civil/Structural, and Electrical disciplines to ensure all relevant aspects are covered.
*   **Approval:** Final approval **must** be granted by the Project Engineer.

**Responsibilities**
*   **Prepared by:** Mechanical
*   **Checked by:** Process, Civil/Structural and Electrical
*   **Approved by:** Project Engineer
