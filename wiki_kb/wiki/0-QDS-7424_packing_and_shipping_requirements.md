# Packing and Shipping Requirements for Polypropylene Tank (0-QDS-7424)

This page outlines the mandatory packing and shipping requirements for the Polypropylene Tank project, as specified in Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7424. These requirements ensure that the tank is adequately protected during transit and that all necessary documentation accompanies the shipment.

**Mandatory Packing and Shipping Activities:**
*   **Packaging:** The packaging process **must** be subject to Chemetics Witness surveillance. This ensures that the tank is packed according to specified standards to prevent damage during transportation.
*   **Packing List:** A Packing List **must** be prepared as a Quality Record (AP), meaning it is "As Prepared" by the vendor. This document **shall** also be subject to Chemetics Witness surveillance, verifying its accuracy and completeness.

Packing/Crating is an essential activity listed in the Quality Data Sheet to safeguard the integrity of the polypropylene tank during logistics. The definitions for Quality Records (AP and FP) are provided in [[0-QDS-7424_quality_record_definitions]]. The Chemetics Witness surveillance level for this project is detailed in [[0-QDS-7424_chemetics_surveillance_level]]. For general administrative details of this QDS, refer to [[0-QDS-7424_polypropylene_tank_quality_data_sheet_administration]].

**Specific Packing and Documentation Requirements:**
*   **Packaging:** Requires Chemetics Witness surveillance.
*   **Packing List:** Requires a Quality Record (AP - As Prepared) and Chemetics Witness surveillance.
