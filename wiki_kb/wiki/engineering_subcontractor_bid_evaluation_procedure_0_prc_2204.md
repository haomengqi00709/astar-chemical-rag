# Engineering Subcontractor Bid Evaluation Procedure (0-PRC-2204)

This procedure, identified as 0-PRC-2204, outlines the comprehensive process for evaluating bids submitted by Engineering Subcontractors. Its primary objective is to ensure that all selected Subcontractors are technically and commercially capable of delivering services in full compliance with the Subcontract requirements.

Upon receipt of bids, the [[project_engineer_pem_gate_review_responsibilities|Project Engineer]] **will** direct the appropriate project personnel to commence the bid evaluation process. The [[project_engineer_pem_gate_review_responsibilities|Project Engineer]] **will** also establish clear deadlines for the completion of these evaluations. Each Bid Evaluation **will** be assigned an identification number that matches the original requisition. For detailed guidelines on document numbering, refer to [[document_number_format_and_components|Document Numbering]] (0-PRC-0002).

The evaluation process is structured into four distinct sections:
1.  [[engineering_subcontractor_commercial_bid_evaluation|Commercial Evaluation]]
2.  [[engineering_subcontractor_performance_bid_evaluation|Performance Evaluation]]
3.  [[engineering_subcontractor_financial_bid_evaluation|Financial Evaluation]]
4.  [[engineering_subcontractor_selection_and_summary_record|Summary Record]]

These four sections are considered integral sheets of a single document and **will** be numbered and filed accordingly. For information on the project's file structure, consult [[file_structure|File Structure]] (0-PRC-0003).

The overarching objective of [[engineering_subcontractor_selection_and_summary_record|Subcontractor selection]] is to simultaneously minimize project risk and cost. The ideal Subcontractor is one who offers the lowest total cost to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics while achieving a "satisfactory" rating across all evaluation categories.

The [[project_engineer_pem_gate_review_responsibilities|Project Engineer]] is responsible for orchestrating the entire evaluation process. This involves ensuring that each shortlisted Subcontractor receives a thorough and complete evaluation, while also preventing excessive time expenditure on bids that are clearly unsuitable early in the process. The evaluation is designed as a series of related and parallel activities, allowing for flexibility. Ratings can be adjusted as bids are clarified or modified through communication with Subcontractors. The publishing and final approval of individual evaluation sections **will** typically be completed immediately prior to the approval of the overall [[engineering_subcontractor_selection_and_summary_record|Summary Record]].
