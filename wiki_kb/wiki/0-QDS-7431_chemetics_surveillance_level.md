# 0-QDS-7431 Chemetics Surveillance Level

This page specifies the Chemetics surveillance level for the procurement of Acid Brick & Lining, as defined in [[0-QDS-7431_acid_brick_lining_quality_data_sheet_administration]].

Surveillance by Chemetics will be to Level 0.0. This level of surveillance is further detailed and set out within the provisions of this Quality Data Sheet. This requirement is associated with item 2.0 in the source document.

For general information on Chemetics surveillance levels and their implications across various equipment and materials, refer to other specific QDS documents such as [[0-QDS-7404-1_chemetics_surveillance_level]] or [[0-QDS-7411_chemetics_surveillance_level]].

This statement regarding Chemetics surveillance is associated with item 2.0 in the source document.
