# Related Plant Numbering Systems

The [[plant_equipment_identification_system|plant equipment identification system]] (4-PRC-0001) serves as a foundational basis for the development of other critical numbering systems across the project. This integrated approach ensures consistency and facilitates efficient cross-referencing between different engineering disciplines and project documentation.

**Piping Numbering**
The equipment numbering system provides the essential framework for the [[plant_pipe_numbering_5_prc_0001|Plant Pipe Numbering]] procedure, which is comprehensively described in document 5-PRC-0001. This ensures that piping associated with specific equipment can be easily identified and tracked.

**Instrumentation Numbering**
Similarly, the equipment identification system underpins the [[plant_instrument_numbering_8_prc_0001|Plant Instrument Numbering]] system, as detailed in document 8-PRC-0001. This linkage allows for clear identification of instruments associated with particular pieces of equipment.

**Implementation Requirement**
In the process of assigning standard numbers to equipment, it is **mandatory** to ensure that the intent and requirements of 4-PRC-0001 (Equipment Identification), 5-PRC-0001 (Piping Numbering), and 8-PRC-0001 (Instrument Numbering) are fully realized and harmonized. This integrated application is critical for the overall integrity of the project's numbering schemes.

**Utilities and Electrical Areas**
It is anticipated that the responsible discipline will assign identical numbers to equipment within utilities and electrical areas. This practice further enhances consistency across the entire plant, simplifying operations and maintenance activities.
