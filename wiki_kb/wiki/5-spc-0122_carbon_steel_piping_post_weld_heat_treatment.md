# Carbon Steel Piping Post-Weld Heat Treatment (PWHT) (5-SPC-0122)

This page details the requirements for Post-Weld Heat Treatment (PWHT) for carbon steel piping fabrication, as specified in document 5-SPC-0122.

**PWHT Execution**
*   Where P.W.H.T. is required, it **shall** be carried out in a furnace, unless otherwise approved by [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_quality_assurance_and_materials_engineering_group|Aker Kvaerner Chemetics]].
*   A procedure **shall** be provided showing the location of thermocouples and supports for P.W.H.T.

**Documentation**
*   Furnace charts are **required** to be submitted for records purposes for P.W.H.T.
