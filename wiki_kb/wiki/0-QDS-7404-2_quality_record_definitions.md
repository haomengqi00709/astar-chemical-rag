# Quality Record Definitions for ESP Lead Lined Components

This page defines the abbreviations used for Quality Record requirements within the [[0-QDS-7404-2_document_details|Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7404-2]] for Electrostatic Precipitator Lead Lined Components. These definitions specify how documentation for various inspection and testing activities must be managed.

**Quality Record Abbreviations:**
*   **AP (As Prepared):** This designation means that the quality record is required "As Prepared." The document **must** be submitted in its original prepared format.
*   **FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package):** This designation means that the quality record is required to be "Included in the Quality Assurance Documentation Package." The document **must** be part of the final compilation of quality assurance records.

These definitions are crucial for understanding the documentation requirements outlined in the [[0-QDS-7404-2_inspection_and_documentation_requirements|Inspection and Documentation Requirements]] section of this Quality Data Sheet. For broader definitions, refer to [[0-QDS-7_quality_record_definitions]].
