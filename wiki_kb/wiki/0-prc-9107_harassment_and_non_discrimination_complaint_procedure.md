# Harassment and Discrimination Complaint Procedure

Any employee who believes they are subject to harassment or discrimination is strongly encouraged to immediately bring the issue to the attention of the Human Resources Manager.

Upon receipt of a complaint:
*   Complaints **MUST** be investigated immediately by the Human Resources Manager.
*   The investigation **MUST** be conducted in a manner that is as confidential as is compatible with a thorough investigation of the complaint.
*   The complainant **MUST** be apprised of the outcome of the investigation.

**Disciplinary Actions and Protections:**
*   Any employee determined by impartial investigation to have harassed or discriminated against another employee **MUST** be subject to appropriate disciplinary action, up to and including termination.
*   No prejudicial action **MUST** be taken against any complainant where the complaint is filed in good faith, whether the complaint is upheld or not.
*   A complaint of harassment found to be initiated for malicious, frivolous, or vexatious reasons **MUST** lead to disciplinary action against the complainant.
