# Sulphuric Acid Storage Tank General Requirements (4-SPC-0046)

This section outlines the general requirements and compliance standards for the design, fabrication, and erection of sulphuric acid storage tanks as per specification 4-SPC-0046.

**Applicable Regulations and Standards**
All relevant [[API_650_welded_steel_tanks|API]], [[ASTM_a36|ASTM]], [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels|ASME]], Federal, State, and Local regulations **will** apply where required. The term "Codes, etc." refers to the latest edition of these standards ruling at the date of contract. In cases of conflict between specification 4-SPC-0046 and/or Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' drawings with API and other applicable codes, 4-SPC-0046 and Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' drawings **will** govern. However, Federal, State & Local laws and regulations **must** be maintained in all cases.

**Manufacturer Responsibilities**
The manufacturer **must** thoroughly examine all site conditions, drawings, specifications/Standards, and instructions. Any errors, disagreements, or inconsistencies **must** be reported in writing to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics before any physical work has commenced.

**Subcontracting and Deviations**
No part of the contract **will** be sublet without Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' prior approval. Deviations from this Standard are acceptable only when approved in writing by the designated Aker Kvaerner Chemetics engineer.

**Work Stoppage and Costs**
If the requirements of this Standard are not followed, Aker Kvaerner Chemetics reserves the right to stop work. Any costs incurred due to such work stoppage **will** be at the expense of the manufacturer.

**On-Site Documentation and Contamination Prevention**
The manufacturer **will** ensure that a copy of this Standard **will** be available at all times for consultation in both the shop and the field. All precautions **must** be taken to prevent contamination of plates by oil or other hydrocarbons. The use of vehicles, including cranes and aerial buckets, over floor plates before they are welded is precluded to maintain cleanliness and prevent damage.
