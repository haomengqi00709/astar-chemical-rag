# Electrical Document Numbering Standard (9-LST-0001)

The Electrical Document Numbering Standard, identified as 9-LST-0001, provides specific guidelines and requirements for the numbering of all electrical documents and drawings within a project. This standard supplements the general company document numbering procedure [[0-PRC-0002_document_numbering]] by offering detailed instructions pertinent to electrical engineering documentation.

Adherence to 9-LST-0001 is mandatory for ensuring uniformity and traceability across all electrical deliverables. It defines the structure, components, and allocation rules for document and drawing numbers, facilitating efficient document management and retrieval.

For an overview of how this standard integrates with other electrical numbering procedures, refer to [[9-PRC-0021_electrical_numbering_and_identification_procedure]]. Further details on the ranges of numbers available for different document types can be found in [[9-PRC-0021_table_1_electrical_drawing_and_document_number_ranges]].
