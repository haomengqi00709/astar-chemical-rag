# Plant Layout Review Checklist (5-PRC-0005 Table 1)

This page details the specific items included in the Plant Layout Review Checklist, as referenced by the [[5-prc-0005_plant_layout_review_procedure]]. This checklist is a mandatory tool used during the review process to ensure comprehensive evaluation of various aspects of the plant layout.

The checklist covers critical areas such as:

*   **Personnel:** This section addresses considerations related to human factors in the plant layout, including access and egress for operations and maintenance, emergency escape routes, safety zones, ergonomic design of workstations, and overall personnel safety during routine and emergency conditions. It ensures that the layout supports safe movement, efficient work processes, and adequate space for personnel. [[plant_constructability_review_roles_and_responsibilities]] and [[hse_personnel_definitions_and_responsibilities]] provide further context on personnel-related considerations in project planning and safety.

*   **Electrical:** This section focuses on the electrical infrastructure within the plant layout. It includes checks for the routing and accessibility of [[cable_tray_routing_and_layout]], placement of electrical equipment, compliance with hazardous area classifications, grounding requirements, and overall electrical safety. Considerations for maintenance access to electrical panels, switchgear, and other components are also included. For detailed electrical design criteria, refer to [[electrical_design_criteria]] and [[electrical_installation_specification]].

The full checklist, as outlined in this table, **must** be completed during each Plant Layout Review to ensure all design requirements and safety standards are met.
