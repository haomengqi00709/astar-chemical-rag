# Environmental and Site Conditions for Equipment Design

Mechanical equipment **will** be suitable for operation outdoors, including direct sunlight and exposure to all natural elements. It **will** also be suitable for operation in an environment as described in [[1-dst-0001_site_design_conditions|datasheet 1-DST-0001: Site Design Conditions]] (as applicable). Indoor environments **will** be considered as applicable.

Noise requirements are detailed in [[general_equipment_requirements_specification|4-SPC-0001: General Equipment Requirements]].

Particular plant areas may be subject to abrasive dust-laden atmospheres, corrosive conditions, acidic fluids, hose-down by process water, and direct sunlight and rain. Equipment **will** be designed to prevent the accumulation of spilled material, dust, and water.
