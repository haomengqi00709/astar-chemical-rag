# Instrument Systems Field Checkout (8-PRC-0005)

This document, 8-PRC-0005, outlines the procedure for conducting field checkout inspections and tests for instrument systems within the Project. It details the steps to ensure that all field instruments are correctly installed, calibrated, and wired.

Inspections and tests for [[mechanical_completion_testing_and_defect_management]] are conducted in accordance with this procedure. It includes associated checklists to record the completion of these critical checks.
