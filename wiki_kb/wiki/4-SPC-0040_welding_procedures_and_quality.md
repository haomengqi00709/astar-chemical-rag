# 4-SPC-0040 Welding Procedures and Quality

Specification 4-SPC-0040 sets forth stringent requirements for welding procedures, welder qualifications, and overall weld quality during the fabrication of metallic process vessels and components.

**Welding Location and Qualifications:**
*   All welding **must** be completed at the location specified on the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics purchase order.
*   Alternate welding locations **MUST** be approved by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics prior to commencement of work.
*   All welders **must** be qualified in accordance with the [[ASME_section_ix_welding_and_brazing_qualifications|ASME Code]] or an Aker Kvaerner Chemetics approved alternate.
*   All welding procedures **must** be qualified in accordance with the [[ASME_section_ix_welding_and_brazing_qualifications|ASME Code]] or an Aker Kvaerner Chemetics approved alternate.
*   All weld consumables **must** meet the requirements of [[ASME_bpvc_section_ii_materials|ASME Section II, Part C]].

**Weld Preparation and Alignment:**
*   Butt joint misalignment for plates <= 6mm (1/4”) **must not** exceed 1.5mm (1/16”).
*   Butt joint misalignment for plates > 6mm (1/4”) **must not** exceed the smaller of 3mm (1/8”) or 25% of plate thickness.
*   For slip-on flanges, the sum of gaps on any diameter **must not** exceed 5mm (3/16”).
*   Parts to be welded **must** be securely held in position by mechanical means, welded-on bridge pieces, or by tack welding to maintain alignment and gap.
*   Welded-on bridge pieces or support spiders **must** be of the same material as the component to which they are welded.
*   The equipment surface **must not** be left in a damaged condition after removal of welded-on pieces.
*   Where a jig or other suitable device is used, its construction **must** allow adequate access for welding and cleaning.
*   Jigs **must** be constructed such that external loading is **not** applied to a joint during welding.

**Tack Welds:**
*   Tack welds **must** be performed by [[ASME_section_ix_welding_and_brazing_qualifications|ASME qualified welders]] to an [[ASME_section_ix_welding_and_brazing_qualifications|ASME approved procedure]].
*   Tack welds employed for assembly may form part of the completed weld if complete fusion is achieved at the root and the welding procedure is the same as for the remainder of the root pass.
*   Cracked and unsound tack welds **must** be removed prior to welding.
*   Sound tack welds **must** be completely fused into the root pass of the main weld.
*   Where thinning down ends of tack welds is necessary for fusion, tack welds **must** be at least 25mm (1”) long.

**Weld Execution and Quality:**
*   Vertical welds **must** be made in the up-hand (vertical up) position.
*   Weld surfaces **must** be protected from contamination before and during welding.
*   Use of paste type shielding products (e.g., Solar Flux®) as a substitute for inert gas shielding is **not permitted**.
*   Arc strikes **must** be confined to surfaces on which weld metal is to be deposited. If an arc strike occurs elsewhere, the location **must** be ground smooth, dye penetrant (PT) or magnetic particle (MT) examined, and repaired if necessary. Burn marks from grounding clamps **must** be treated as arc strikes.
*   Each run of weld metal **must** be thoroughly cleaned of slag and foreign matter before the next run is deposited. On restarting, care **must** be taken to ensure proper fusion with the weld metal previously deposited.
*   If a tungsten electrode makes contact with the work, the section of weld and the tip of the electrode **must** be examined. Where tungsten loss has occurred, the tungsten pick up in the weld **must** be ground out, and the electrode **must** be reground to the correct profile.
*   Welds **must** be uniform in width and size throughout their full length.
*   Each pass of weld **must** be smooth and free of slag, inclusions, cracks, porosity, lack of fusion, and undercut, except to the extent permitted by [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels|ASME]].
*   The cover pass **must** be free of coarse ripples, irregular surface, non-uniform bead patterns, high crown, and deep ridges or valleys between beads.
*   Butt welds **must** be convex, uniform in height, and have full penetration.
*   Weld reinforcement **must not** exceed the limits permitted by [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels|ASME]].
*   Crevices and/or undercut are **not permitted** on process exposed welds.
*   Peening of weld runs or hammering finished welds is **not permitted**.

**Chemical Analysis of Weld Consumables:**
*   The Vendor will make available to the Inspector copies of chemical certification for consumables used.
*   If filler metal cannot be identified to the Inspector’s satisfaction, samples will be removed for chemical analysis. The costs for this chemical analysis will be the Vendor’s responsibility if identification is not satisfactory.
*   When evidence of poor electrode control indicates a specific weldment could fall outside the approved specification or Code requirements, the Vendor will complete an analysis of the suspect welds. All costs for repairs and retests resulting from investigation due to poor electrode control will be the Vendor’s responsibility.

**Weld Consumables:**
*   All weld consumables **must** meet the requirements of ASME Section II, Part C.
*   The Vendor **will** make available to the Inspector copies of chemical certification for consumables used.
*   If filler metal cannot be identified to the Inspector’s satisfaction, samples **will** be removed for chemical analysis.
*   Chemical analysis of filler metal samples **will** be at the Vendor’s cost if identification is not satisfactory.

**Joint Requirements:**
*   All category A & B joints (as defined in the ASME Code) **must** be full penetration butt welds.
*   Permanent backing rings are not permitted for category A & B joints.
*   Consumable joint inserts are not permitted for category A & B joints.
*   Longitudinal seams of adjacent courses **must** be offset a minimum of 75 mm (3”) or 5 times the material thickness, whichever is greater.
*   All wall, floor and roof joints **must** be full penetration welds.
*   Nozzle to shell welds **must** be full penetration unless otherwise shown on approved drawings.
*   Floor to shell welds **must** be full penetration unless otherwise shown on approved drawings.

**Support Welds:**
*   All welds on supports **must** be continuous (i.e., not stitch welds).

**Skirt Attachment Welds:**
*   On equipment with support skirts, no tack welds are permitted between the head and skirt on the inside of the skirt.
*   The skirt attachment weld **must not** cover the bottom girth seam.

**Head and Shell Repairs:**
*   Heads with a central hole from manufacture **must** have it completely seal welded and 100% radiographed to the ASME code. This 100% radiography requirement does not apply if a nozzle is to be welded to the head at this location.
*   Shell or head plate cut-outs made at wrong locations **must** be repaired by either installing a nozzle through the plate and performing a full penetration weld or installing a “flush” plate insert with full penetration weld and 100% radiography to the ASME code.

**Welding Technique:**
*   Vertical welds **must** be made in the up-hand (vertical up) position.
*   Weld surfaces **must** be protected from contamination before and during welding.
*   Use of paste type shielding products (i.e. Solar Flux®) as a substitute for inert gas shielding is not permitted.

**Fit-Up and Alignment:**
*   Butt joint misalignment for plates <= 6mm (1/4”) **must not** exceed 1.5mm (1/16”).
*   Butt joint misalignment for plates > 6mm (1/4”) **must not** exceed the smaller of 3mm (1/8”) or 25% of plate thickness.
*   For slip on flanges, the sum of gaps on any diameter **must not** exceed 5mm (3/16”).
*   Parts to be welded **must** be securely held in position by mechanical means, welded-on bridge pieces or by tack welding to maintain alignment and gap.
*   Welded-on bridge pieces or support spiders **must** be of the same material as the component to which they are welded.
*   The Equipment surface **must not** be left in a damaged condition after removal of welded-on pieces.
*   Where a jig or other suitable device is used, its construction **must** allow adequate access for welding and cleaning.
*   Jigs **must** be constructed such that external loading is not applied to a joint during welding.

**Tack Welds:**
*   Tack welds **must** be performed by ASME qualified welders to an ASME approved procedure.
*   Tack welds employed for assembly may form part of the completed weld if complete fusion is achieved at the root and the welding procedure is the same as for the remainder of the root pass.
*   Cracked and unsound tack welds **must** be removed prior to welding.
*   Sound tack welds **must** be completely fused into the root pass of the main weld.
*   Where thinning down ends of tack welds is necessary for fusion, tack welds **must** be at least 25mm (1”) long.

**Arc Strikes and Burn Marks:**
*   Arc strikes **must** be confined to surfaces on which weld metal is to be deposited.
*   If an arc strike occurs elsewhere, the location **must** be ground smooth, dye penetrant (PT) or magnetic particle (MT) examined.
*   If necessary, arc strike locations **must** be repaired.
*   Burn marks from grounding clamps **must** be treated as arc strikes.

**Interpass Cleaning and Restarting:**
*   Each run of weld metal **must** be thoroughly cleaned of slag and foreign matter before the next run is deposited.
*   On restarting, care **must** be taken to ensure proper fusion with the weld metal previously deposited.

**Tungsten Contamination:**
*   If a tungsten electrode makes contact with the work, the section of weld and the tip of the electrode **must** be examined.
*   Where tungsten loss has occurred, the tungsten pick up in the weld **must** be ground out.
*   Where tungsten loss has occurred, the electrode **must** be reground to the correct profile.

**Weld Appearance and Quality:**
*   Welds **must** be uniform in width and size throughout their full length.
*   Each pass of weld **must** be smooth and free of slag, inclusions, cracks, porosity, lack of fusion and undercut, except to the extent permitted by ASME.
*   The cover pass **must** be free of coarse ripples, irregular surface, non-uniform bead patterns, high crown and deep ridges or valleys between beads.
*   Butt welds **must** be convex, uniform in height and have full penetration.
*   Weld reinforcement **must not** exceed the limits permitted by ASME.
*   Crevices and/or undercut are not permitted on process exposed welds.

**Post-Weld Treatment:**
*   Peening of weld runs or hammering finished welds is not permitted.

**Weld Quality Control:**
*   When evidence of poor electrode control indicates a specific weldment could fall outside the approved specification or Code requirements, the Vendor **will** complete an analysis of the suspect welds.
*   All costs for repairs and retests that result from investigation due to poor electrode control **will** be the Vendor’s responsibility.
