# Fan Datasheet Material Specifications

This page outlines the mandatory material specifications for key components of the fan, as required by the [[fan_datasheet_administration|Fan Datasheet Template]] (4-DST-XXXX-R0). Proper material selection is critical for ensuring the fan's durability, corrosion resistance, and suitability for the specified process conditions.

**Mandatory Material Specifications**
The following materials **must** be specified for the respective fan components:
*   **Casing Material**: The material for the fan casing. Refer to [[fan_casing_material]].
*   **Impeller Material**: The material for the fan impeller. Refer to [[fan_impeller_material]].
*   **Lining Material**: The material used for any internal lining. Refer to [[fan_lining_material]].
*   **Wheel Material**: The material for the fan wheel. Refer to [[fan_wheel_material]].
*   **Gaskets Material**: The material for all gaskets. Refer to [[gaskets_material_specification]].
*   **Shaft Material**: The material for the fan shaft. Refer to [[fan_shaft_material]].
*   **Shaft Sleeve Material**: The material for the shaft sleeve. Refer to [[fan_shaft_sleeve_material]].
*   **Seal Metal Parts Material**: The material for the metal components of the shaft seal. Refer to [[seal_metal_parts_material_specification]].
*   **Seal Elastomers Material**: The material for the elastomeric components of the shaft seal. Refer to [[seal_elastomers_material_specification]].
*   **Inlet Filter Material**: The material for the inlet filter. Refer to [[inlet_filter_material_specification]].
*   **Inlet Box Material**: The material for the inlet box. Refer to [[inlet_box_material_specification]].
*   **External Inlet-Vane Damper Material**: The material for the external inlet-vane damper. Refer to [[external_inlet_vane_damper_material_specification]].

These specifications contribute to the overall [[material_selection]] process for the equipment.
