# Purchase Order Title, Risk, and Indemnities

This page outlines the transfer of title, allocation of risks, and indemnification responsibilities between Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Vendor under the Purchase Order (PO).

**Transfer of Title:**
*   Whatever title the Vendor holds to [[purchase_order_definitions#The Goods and Services|Goods and Services]] passes to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics upon [[purchase_order_definitions#Delivery or Deliver|Delivery]].
*   Title to any part of the Goods and Services also passes to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics if Aker Kvaerner Chemetics has made a payment that included the [[purchase_order_definitions#The Prices|Price]] or part of the Price for that specific part of the Goods and Services.
*   A payment of the Price or part of the Price for a part of the Goods and Services before its delivery is considered a part payment, not a deposit.
*   When a part payment is made, the Vendor **must** hold that part of the Goods and Services in trust and as bailee for Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
*   The Vendor **must** keep the part of the Goods and Services free from all claims, charges, and other encumbrances.
*   The Vendor authorizes Aker Kvaerner Chemetics to give notice of or register a charge, lien, security, or security interest, or make a UCC or PPSA filing (or its equivalent in other jurisdictions), to evidence Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' interest in that part of the Goods and Services.
*   The Vendor **must** properly store, adequately protect, and label each part of the Goods and Services as property for the [[purchase_order_definitions#The Project|Project]]. These storage, protection, and labeling requirements apply regardless of whether title has passed to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' Risks:**
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ risks include claims, proceedings, compensation, and costs payable due to:
*   Negligence, breach of statutory duty, or interference with any legal right by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or any person employed by them.
*   A fault of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or a fault in their design.
*   Loss of or damage to parts of the Goods and Services after Delivery, except for loss or damage occurring before the [[defect_management_process#Defects Correction Period|Defects Date]] that is due to:
    *   A [[defect_management_process#Definition of a Defect|Defect]] that existed at Delivery.
    *   An event occurring before Delivery that was not itself an Aker Kvaerner Chemetics risk.
    *   The activities of the Vendor after Delivery.
*   Other Aker Kvaerner Chemetics risks as stated in the Purchase Order.

**Vendor's Risks and Responsibilities:**
*   Risks not explicitly carried by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics are carried by the Vendor from the [[purchase_order_definitions#The Starting Date|Starting Date]] until the Defects Date.
*   The Vendor **must** promptly replace lost Goods and Services and repair damaged Goods and Services until the Defects Date, unless otherwise instructed by the Project Buyer.

**Indemnification:**
*   Each Party **must** indemnify the other against claims, proceedings, compensation, and costs due to an event that is at their respective risk.
*   The liability of each Party to indemnify the other is reduced if events at the other Party’s risk contributed to the claims, proceedings, compensation, and costs.
*   This reduction in liability is proportional to the extent that events at the other Party’s risk contributed, taking into account each Party’s responsibilities under this Purchase Order.
