# Site and Utility Design Data Reference

This page provides references for site-specific and utility design data relevant to equipment design and procurement, as described in document 4-SPC-0001.

**Site Design Conditions**
Site data, including climatic information, **is given** in [[1-DST-0001_site_design_conditions]]. This document provides critical environmental parameters that influence equipment design, such as temperature ranges, humidity, wind loads, and seismic conditions.

**Utility Design Data**
Design data for utilities **is given** in [[1-DST-0001_site_design_conditions]]. This includes specifications for various utilities like water, steam, air, and electricity. Additional utility information **may be** found on individual equipment data sheets in some cases, providing more granular details specific to a particular piece of equipment's requirements.

Vendors **must** ensure their equipment designs are compatible with these specified site and utility conditions.

Source document: 4-SPC-0001 (Equipment)

**Utility Design Data**
Design data for utilities is given in [[1-DST-0001_site_design_conditions]]. Additional utility information may be on equipment data sheets in some cases.
