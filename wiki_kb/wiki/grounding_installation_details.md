# Grounding Installation Details

Grounding installation details provide specific instructions and component requirements for establishing the plant's grounding system. These details are crucial for safety and proper electrical system operation.

**Content of Grounding Details**
Grounding details **shall** show:
*   Grounding conductors used for main ground grids.
*   Grounding conductors used for branches to equipment.
*   Grounding conductors used for branches to buildings.
*   Types of grounding electrodes used.
*   Material types of grounding electrodes.
*   The length of grounding electrodes.
*   The method of fastening grounding electrodes to the grounding conductor.
*   Connectors for fastening grounding conductors to equipment.
*   Connectors for fastening grounding conductors to building columns.
*   Connectors for fastening grounding conductors to ground electrodes.
*   Connectors for fastening grounding conductors to other grounding conductors.
