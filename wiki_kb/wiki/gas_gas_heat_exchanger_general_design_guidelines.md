# Gas/Gas Heat Exchanger General Design Guidelines

These general guidelines apply to the thermal design of radial flow gas/gas heat exchangers, as supported by [[4-prc-0008_gas_gas_heat_exchanger_thermal_design_procedure|Procedure 4-PRC-0008]]. Adherence to these principles ensures optimal performance and reliability.

**Pressure Drop:**
*   Maximum allowable shell and tube side pressure drops **will be** specified on the [[process_data_sheet_overview|process data sheet]].
*   The combined pressure drop **cannot** exceed the sum of the maximum allowable shell and tube side pressure drops.
*   Proportions of pressure drop may be adjusted as necessary to achieve the optimum exchanger design.

**Fouling:**
*   Fouling considerations are covered by the application of a safety factor in the design.
*   Due to the typically high convective resistances in these exchangers, the fouling factor (in the traditional sense) would also be quite high.

**Design Application:**
*   The guidelines provided are primarily intended for new designs.
*   For replacement units, exceptions to these guidelines are permitted to accommodate existing duct sizes and layouts, ensuring compatibility and minimizing modifications to surrounding infrastructure.
