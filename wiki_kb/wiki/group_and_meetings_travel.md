# Group and Meetings Travel

The Group Travel Procedure (11.1 PR 2) provides specific guidelines for managing travel related to groups of employees and meetings, with an emphasis on cost savings and efficient coordination.

### Maximizing Savings with Group Rates
Fares for groups of employees and facilities for meetings can often lead to additional savings. To capitalize on these opportunities:
*   When travel requirements or meetings align with the profile for group rates (especially if meetings are planned in hotels), meeting arrangers **must** coordinate these arrangements through the Company's nominated travel agencies.
*   Meeting arrangers **must** request group or meeting rates directly from these nominated travel agencies. This ensures that the Group's purchasing power is leveraged effectively.

### Discouraging Internal Group Travel
Travel to meetings that involve only Group employees is to be discouraged. The procedure emphasizes the importance of considering alternative communication methods before planning such journeys.
*   Alternative communication methods, such as video conferencing, Net Meeting, or telephone conference calls, **should** be considered for meetings involving only Group employees. This approach helps to minimize unnecessary travel expenses and time away from the office.

By adhering to these guidelines, the Group aims to reduce travel costs associated with internal meetings and maximize savings when group travel is essential.
