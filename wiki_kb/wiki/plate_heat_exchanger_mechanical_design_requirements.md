# Plate Heat Exchanger Mechanical Design Requirements

This page outlines the mandatory mechanical design requirements for plate heat exchangers, as specified in Document ID 4-DST-XXXX-R1. Adherence to these requirements ensures the structural integrity, safety, and operational suitability of the equipment.

**Design Parameters**
The following parameters must be specified for the mechanical design:
*   **Design Internal Pressure:** (kPa(g))
*   **Design Vacuum:** (kPa(g))
*   **Design Maximum Temperature:** (°C)
*   **Design Minimum Temperature:** (°C)

**Protection and Registration**
*   **Overpressure Protection:** Details of the overpressure protection system must be provided.
*   **Vacuum Protection:** Details of the vacuum protection system must be provided.
*   **Registration:** Any required regulatory registration details must be included.

**Codes and Standards**
*   **Design Code:** The applicable design code (e.g., ASME, EN) must be specified.
*   **Equipment Specification:** Reference to the relevant equipment specification document is required.
*   **General Equipment Requirements:** Compliance with general equipment requirements must be ensured. For broader guidelines, refer to [[general_mechanical_design_requirements_0_spc_0010]].

**Fabrication and Construction**
*   **Fabrication:** Specific fabrication requirements must be detailed.
*   **Lining:** Any internal lining requirements must be specified.
*   **Welding:** Welding procedures and qualifications must be outlined.

**Site and Environmental Conditions**
*   **Site Conditions:** The specific site conditions where the heat exchanger will be installed must be considered. Refer to [[site_design_conditions_1_dst_0001]] for general site design parameters.
*   **Wind Design Code:** The applicable wind design code must be specified. See [[wind_design_requirements]].
*   **Seismic Design Code:** The applicable seismic design code must be specified. See [[seismic_design_requirements]].
*   **Connection Type:** The types of connections required for piping and instrumentation must be detailed.
*   **Equipment Location:** The intended location of the equipment within the plant must be specified.
*   **Painting:** Painting and coating specifications must be provided.

**General Design Notes**
*   The heat exchanger frame **must** be sized to allow for the future installation of a minimum of 20% additional plate capacity. This ensures expandability and futureproofing.
*   All piping connections **must** be made to the fixed head of the heat exchanger. This is a mandatory requirement for piping layout and maintenance.

For overall equipment design and specification procedures, consult [[equipment_design_and_specification_4_prc_0003]]. General information on design standards and reference materials can be found in [[design_standards_and_reference_materials]].

**Specific Design Considerations**
*   **Frame Sizing:** The heat exchanger frame must be sized to allow for the future installation of a minimum of 20% additional plate capacity.
*   **Piping Connections:** All piping connections must be made to the fixed head of the heat exchanger.
