# Travel Safety and Security

The Group Travel Procedure (11.1 PR 2) places a strong emphasis on the safety and security of employees during business travel.

### Reputable Suppliers
The Group is committed to dealing only with reputable, safe, and reliable suppliers. All service providers, including airlines (even low-cost carriers), hotels, and other travel service providers, are selected based on their adherence to good industry standards. This ensures that employees are provided with quality services that also prioritize their health and safety needs.

### Automatic Travel Insurance
Everyone travelling on behalf of the Group **will** automatically be provided with travel insurance. This ensures that employees are covered for unforeseen circumstances during their business trips. For more comprehensive details on health and safety during travel, refer to [[travel_health_and_safety_requirements]].

### Personal Responsibility for Documents and Laptops
Travellers bear personal responsibility for their documents and laptops (both hardware and software).
*   Documents and laptops **should** always be carried as hand luggage to minimize the risk of loss, damage, or theft. This also helps maintain the security of sensitive company information.

These measures are in place to safeguard employees and company assets while on business travel.

### Reputable Suppliers
Reservations must be booked with reliable suppliers to ensure quality service and proper regard for travellers’ health and safety needs.

### Flight Risk Management
To mitigate risks associated with group travel, specific restrictions apply to the number of employees on a single flight:
*   No more than three SVPs or VPs and their direct reports may travel on the same aeroplane without prior approval of the Business Area EVP or local Company President/Managing Director.
*   No more than ten employees from the one Company are permitted to fly on the same aircraft without prior approval of the Business Area EVP or local Company President/Managing Director.

### Personal Belongings
The traveller is personally responsible for documents and laptops (hardware and software). Documents and laptops should always be carried as hand luggage.
