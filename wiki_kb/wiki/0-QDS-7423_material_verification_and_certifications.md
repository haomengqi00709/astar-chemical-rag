# Thickener Material Verification and Certifications (0-QDS-7423)

This page details the mandatory requirements for material verification and certifications for the Thickener equipment, as specified in Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7423.

**Material Verification:**
Material Verification is a required item or activity.

**Mill Test Certificates:**
*   Mill Test Certificates are required as FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package).
*   Chemetics surveillance for Mill Test Certificates **shall** require Witness.
*   Mill Test Certificates are specifically required for Stainless steel only.
*   Mill Test Certificates are required to be included in the Vendor's Inspection and Test Plan.

**Certificate of Compliance:**
*   A Certificate of Compliance is required as FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package).
*   The Certificate of Compliance is required to be included in the Vendor's Inspection and Test Plan.

These certifications ensure that the materials used in the Thickener meet the specified quality and composition standards. For general material verification and certification requirements, refer to [[0-QDS-7412_material_verification_and_certifications]].
