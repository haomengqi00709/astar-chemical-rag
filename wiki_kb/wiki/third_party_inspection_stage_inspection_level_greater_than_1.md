# Third Party Inspection - Stage Inspection (Level > 1)

For assignments requiring Stage Inspection (Inspection Level greater than 1), the Third Party Inspection Agency **must** establish a tentative visit plan. This plan **shall** be based on the vendor's current Production Schedule(s) and **must** be designed to enable all requirements specified in the [[quality_data_sheets_qds]] (QDSs) to be met. Crucially, the tentative visit plan **must** also remain within the budget constraints outlined in the Agency's [[purchase_order_overview]] (PO).

The Agency **must** execute inspection visits strictly according to this established plan. However, the plan for inspection visits may be revised as necessary to accommodate any changes in the production schedule or to address quality problems encountered during the manufacturing process.

During these inspection visits, the Agency **must** actively monitor the vendor’s relevant manufacturing and inspection processes. This continuous monitoring ensures ongoing compliance with quality standards. Furthermore, the Agency **must** establish whether the manufacturing progress is in accordance with the current schedule, providing valuable updates to AKC.

Any serious problems or deviations identified during Stage Inspection **should** be reported to AKC immediately. For comprehensive details regarding reporting procedures and other documentation requirements, the Agency **shall** refer to [[0-SPC-7003_specification_overview]].
