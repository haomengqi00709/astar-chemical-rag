# 0-POL-9111 Timesheet Procedures

Accurate and timely submission of timesheets is a critical requirement for all employees on site assignments under procedure 0-POL-9111. These procedures ensure proper tracking of work hours for compensation and project billing.

**Employee Responsibilities:**
Employees [[must]] complete timesheets as fully and accurately as possible, detailing all hours worked and activities performed.
Completed timesheets [[must]] be submitted to the Project Manager or their designated representative on site.

**Approval Process:**
If there is a designated individual on site responsible for approving timesheets, that designate [[will]] forward the approved timesheets to the Department Manager. A copy of the approved timesheet [[will]] also be sent to the Project Manager.
The Department Manager is [[responsible]] to ensure that timesheets are submitted electronically on behalf of the employees working on-site. This streamlines the administrative process and ensures timely payroll processing.

**Client Approval for Reimbursable Work:**
For reimbursable or time and materials (T&M) work, the Project Manager or designate [[will]] ensure that timesheets are approved by the Client representative on site. This step is crucial for accurate client billing and project cost recovery.
