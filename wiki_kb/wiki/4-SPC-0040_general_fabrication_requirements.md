# 4-SPC-0040 General Fabrication Requirements

This page details the general fabrication requirements for metallic process vessels and components as per Specification 4-SPC-0040. Adherence to these requirements is mandatory for all Vendors.

**Skirt Venting:**
Provision **must** be made for skirt venting where no other suitable openings exist.

**Reinforcing Pads:**
Reinforcing pads **must** be provided with a ¼” NPT hole located at the lowest point on the pad. If a split reinforcing pad is necessary, the weld **must** be oriented parallel to the circumferential direction of the shell. Test hole(s) for reinforcing pads/nozzle welds **must** be left open after the [[4-SPC-0040_pressure_and_leak_testing|Air/Soap solution test]] has been completed.

**Corrosion Allowance:**
The corrosion allowance specified on drawings or data sheets **must** be added to both sides of all fixed internals that come in contact with the process fluid. For shell and tube heat exchanger baffles, the corrosion allowance **must** be added to one side. The corrosion allowance **must** also be added to the calculated anchor bolt diameter.
The corrosion allowance will **not** be added to removable internals or flange faces.

**Weld Types and Configuration:**
*   All Category A & B joints (as defined in the [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels|ASME Code]]) **must** be full penetration butt welds.
*   Permanent backing rings are **not permitted** for Category A & B joints.
*   Consumable joint inserts are **not permitted** for Category A & B joints.
*   Longitudinal seams of adjacent courses **must** be offset a minimum of 75 mm (3”) or 5 times the material thickness, whichever is greater.
*   All wall, floor, and roof joints **must** be full penetration welds.
*   All welds on supports **must** be continuous (i.e., not stitch welds).
*   Nozzle to shell and floor to shell welds **must** be full penetration unless otherwise shown on approved drawings.

**Heads and Shell Plate Cut-outs:**
*   On equipment with support skirts, no tack welds are **permitted** between the head and skirt on the inside of the skirt.
*   The skirt attachment weld **must not** cover the bottom girth seam.
*   Heads with a central hole from manufacture **must** have it completely seal welded and 100% radiographed to the [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels|ASME Code]]. This 100% radiography requirement does **not** apply if a nozzle is to be welded to the head at this location.
*   Shell or head plate cut-outs made at wrong locations **must** be repaired by either installing a nozzle through the plate and performing a full penetration weld or installing a “flush” plate insert with full penetration weld and 100% radiography to the [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels|ASME Code]].

**Reinforcing Pads and Attachments:**
*   Reinforcing pads **must** be provided with a ¼” NPT hole located at the lowest point on the pad.
*   Where a split reinforcing pad is necessary, the weld **must** be oriented parallel to the circumferential direction of the shell.
*   Reinforcing pads and other attachments **must** closely match the mating contour.

**Corrosion Allowance:**
*   The corrosion allowance specified on drawings or data sheets **must** be added to both sides of all fixed internals that come in contact with the process fluid.
*   The corrosion allowance specified on drawings or data sheets **must** be added to one side of shell and tube heat exchanger baffles.
*   The corrosion allowance specified on drawings or data sheets **must** be added to calculated anchor bolt diameter.
*   The corrosion allowance **will not** be added to removable internals.
*   The corrosion allowance **will not** be added to flange faces.
