# Chemetics Surveillance Level for 0-QDS-7802

This page details the level of surveillance Chemetics will apply to the vendor's activities for items covered by Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7802.

**Surveillance Level:**
*   Surveillance by Chemetics will be to **Level 0.0**.
*   The specific surveillance activities and requirements **shall** be as set out within this Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7802.

**Types of Surveillance:**
Chemetics may employ various types of surveillance, including but not limited to:
*   **Witness:** This involves Chemetics personnel observing specific tests or activities performed by the vendor.
*   **Hold:** This requires Chemetics approval before the vendor can proceed to the next stage of manufacturing or testing.

**Items Requiring Chemetics Surveillance:**
Chemetics surveillance requires completion for the following items and activities, as detailed in the [[0-QDS-7802_inspection_and_documentation_requirements]] and [[0-QDS-7802_quality_record_definitions]] sections:
*   Certificate of Compliance
*   Calibration Certificate
*   Packaging
*   Packing List

For a comprehensive understanding of vendor quality obligations, refer to [[0-QDS-7802_vendor_quality_assurance_requirements]].

**Types of Surveillance:**
In addition to the general surveillance requirements, Chemetics may apply specific surveillance types, including:
*   **Witness:** Chemetics personnel will witness specified activities.
*   **Hold:** Chemetics personnel must provide approval before proceeding with subsequent activities.
