# Chemetics Surveillance Level

The Chemetics Surveillance Level defines the intensity and frequency of quality surveillance activities performed by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or designated third-party inspectors at the Vendor's facility. The specific level for goods is stated in the reference [[quality_data_sheets_qds|Quality Data Sheet]]. This section details the requirements for each surveillance level, as outlined in Specification [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment|0-SPC-7001]].

**Surveillance Levels:**

*   **Level 0: No Surveillance**
    *   No quality surveillance will be carried out by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or its representatives. The Vendor is solely responsible for ensuring compliance.

*   **Level 1: Final Inspection**
    *   Surveillance will be carried out in a single visit upon completion of the manufacture of the goods.
    *   Alternatively, surveillance can be carried out at some other agreed stage prior to packaging for shipment.

*   **Level 2: Witness and Monitoring Points**
    *   Surveillance of activities noted on the reference [[quality_data_sheets_qds|Quality Data Sheet]] will be carried out upon notification by the Vendor of readiness to inspect.
    *   **Witness Points:** Where activities are denoted as witness points, the Vendor **shall** advise Aker Kvaerner Chemetics in writing at least five (5) working days in advance of the operation. The Vendor **must** advise Aker Kvaerner Chemetics each time the operation is performed. The Vendor **may** proceed with the work past the witness point if Aker Kvaerner Chemetics is not available at the appointed time.
    *   **Hold Points:** Where activities are denoted as hold points, the Vendor **shall** advise Aker Kvaerner Chemetics in writing at least five (5) working days in advance of the operation. The Vendor **must** advise Aker Kvaerner Chemetics each time the operation is performed. The Vendor **must not** proceed with the work beyond the hold point without witness by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. The Vendor **may** proceed beyond a hold point without witness by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics only by written agreement from Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
    *   **Other Activities:** Inspection or test activities not denoted as witness or hold points will be verified or monitored by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics during scheduled shop inspection visits.

*   **Level 3: Enhanced Witness and Monitoring**
    *   This level is similar to Level 2 but includes more identified witness and hold points.
    *   Level 3 also includes continued regular monitoring visits by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics throughout the manufacturing process.

*   **Level 4: Continuous Surveillance**
    *   This level requires continuous surveillance by an Aker Kvaerner Chemetics representative or a designated third-party inspector.
    *   The inspector for Level 4 **will** be resident in the Vendor’s facility for the duration of the work.

**Non-Conformance Reporting during Surveillance:**
*   The Vendor **must** report any non-conformances to the Inspector as soon as they are discovered.
*   Non-conformances identified by the Inspector during surveillance visits **will** be identified to the Vendor in writing.
*   The Vendor **shall** promptly propose corrective and preventive actions, which **must** be approved in writing by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics before implementation. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' approval will indicate if any re-inspection or re-testing will be a witness or hold point.
*   Following completion of corrective action, the Vendor **shall** forward documented results of any inspection or testing, indicating conformance.
