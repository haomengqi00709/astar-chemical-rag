# Standard Document Lifecycle and Revision

The lifecycle of a standard document at Aker Kvaerner Chemetics follows a structured process, from initial drafting and review to approval and subsequent revisions. This ensures that all standard documents are thoroughly vetted, compliant, and up-to-date.

### First Draft (Rev 0)
1.  **Review:** The [[standard_document_roles_and_responsibilities#Checker and Approver|Checker]] and [[standard_document_roles_and_responsibilities#Approver]] **shall** review all first drafts of standard documents. This review ensures conformity and compliance with all existing standards.
2.  **Marking and Status:** Following review, the draft standard **shall** be marked as **Rev 0**. Its status **shall** be designated as "Comments Requested".
3.  **Placement and Notification:** The Rev 0 draft standard **shall** be placed in the [[standard_document_storage_and_access#Storage Location|Q Drive]]. The [[standard_document_roles_and_responsibilities#Standards Administrator|Standards Administrator]] **shall** notify all employees of the availability of this draft standard.

### Comment Period
1.  **Invitation for Comments:** Comments on draft standards **shall** be invited over a one-month period.
2.  **Submission and Collation:** Comments **shall** be e-mailed to the [[standard_document_roles_and_responsibilities#Standards Administrator|Standards Administrator]], who will then collate them.

### Initial Approval (Rev 1)
1.  **Revision based on Comments:** Following the comment period, the author, [[standard_document_roles_and_responsibilities#Checker and Approver|Checker]], and [[standard_document_roles_and_responsibilities#Approver]] **shall** review the collated comments and revise the standard as required.
2.  **Marking and Status:** The revised standard **shall** be marked as **Rev 1**. Its status **shall** be "Standard".
3.  **Placement and Approval Record:** The Rev 1 standard **shall** be placed in the [[standard_document_storage_and_access#Storage Location|Q Drive]]. The signed "Approval" PDF of the standard, digitally signed by the Checker and Approver, **shall** also be stored in the "Approvals" subdirectory.
4.  **Notification and Index Update:** The [[standard_document_roles_and_responsibilities#Standards Administrator|Standards Administrator]] **shall** notify all employees of the availability of the standard (Rev 1). The [[standard_document_number_index]] **shall** be updated for Rev 1.

### Subsequent Revisions
1.  **Flagging and Commentary:** All revisions to standards **shall** be flagged. Authors of revisions **shall** include a note in the commentary briefly stating the details of the revision.
2.  **Review and Increment:** The [[standard_document_roles_and_responsibilities#Checker and Approver|Checker]] and [[standard_document_roles_and_responsibilities#Approver]] **shall** review all revisions. The revision number **shall** be incremented for revisions.
3.  **Status and Placement:** The status of revised documents **shall** remain "Standard". Revised documents **shall** be placed in the [[standard_document_storage_and_access#Storage Location|Q Drive]].
4.  **Approval Record:** The signed "Approval" PDF of the standard, digitally signed by the Checker and Approver, **shall** also be stored for revisions.
5.  **Notification and Index Update:** The [[standard_document_roles_and_responsibilities#Standards Administrator|Standards Administrator]] **shall** notify all employees of the revision of the standard. The [[standard_document_number_index]] **shall** be updated for revisions.
6.  **Revision History:** A copy of the previous revision **shall** be retained for revision history on the Q Drive, marked "obsolete" and stored in the "Old Files" folder.

### Document Cover Sheets
Completed Document Cover Sheets **shall** be retained until the next revision to the standard document.
