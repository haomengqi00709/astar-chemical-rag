# 0-LST-6X90-1 Construction Subcontract Supply and Erection Scope

Document 0-LST-6X90-1 serves as a critical listing that details the specific scope for both the supply of equipment and materials and the erection activities within a construction subcontract. This document is referenced by Specification [[0-SPC-6_document_administration]] to clearly define the division of responsibilities between Chemetics and the Subcontractor.

**Purpose and Content**
This document itemizes the Equipment and Materials that will be supplied by Chemetics, as well as those that the Subcontractor is responsible for providing. It also specifies the particular portions of the plant that the Subcontractor is mandated to construct and erect. The information contained within 0-LST-6X90-1 is essential for clarifying the logistical and construction aspects of the project.

**Application in Subcontracts**
As referenced in [[0-SPC-6_subcontractor_work_scope]] and [[0-SPC-6_material_and_equipment_supply]], the details within 0-LST-6X90-1 are mandatory requirements. All equipment, materials, and construction activities **shall** be executed in strict accordance with the descriptions and quantities specified herein, and in compliance with all other [[document_control_procedure_0_prc_0007|Applicable Documents]]. This ensures a clear understanding of the scope and responsibilities for all parties involved in the construction subcontract.

For related information on supply and erection scope, refer to [[supply_and_erection_scope_checklist_0_lst_2002]].
