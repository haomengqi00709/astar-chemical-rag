# 2.2 NO 4 Just Culture Norm

This page refers to the Aker Kvaerner Group's 2.2 NO 4 Just Culture Norm, a mandatory compliance document for the [[0-prc-8013_hse_incident_reporting]] procedure.

This norm establishes the principles of a "Just Culture" within the organization, promoting an environment where employees are encouraged to report safety concerns and incidents without fear of unfair blame, while still holding individuals accountable for reckless behavior.
