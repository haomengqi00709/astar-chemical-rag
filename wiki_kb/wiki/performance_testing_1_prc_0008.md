# Performance Testing (1-PRC-0008)

This document, 1-PRC-0008, outlines the procedure for conducting performance testing for the Project. Performance tests are designed to demonstrate that the plant meets its contractual performance guarantees.

Acceptance Tests **will** be conducted in accordance with this procedure. Owner's personnel **will** operate the plant during performance testing under the direction of the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics commissioning team. This procedure is a critical part of [[plant_modifications_and_acceptance_testing]].
