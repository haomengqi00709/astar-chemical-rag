# 2.2 PR 1 HSE Reporting Procedure

This page refers to the Aker Kvaerner Group's 2.2 PR 1 HSE Reporting Procedure, a mandatory compliance document for the [[0-prc-8013_hse_incident_reporting]] procedure.

This procedure outlines the detailed steps and requirements for reporting all Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) related incidents within the Group, ensuring consistency and completeness in reporting.
