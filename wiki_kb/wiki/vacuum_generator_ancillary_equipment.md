# Vacuum Generator Ancillary Equipment

This page lists the ancillary equipment and associated details for the Vacuum Generator, as specified in Document ID 4-PDS-XXXX-R1.

**Included Ancillary Equipment**
*   Spray Nozzles
*   Insulation
*   Vortex Breaker
*   External brackets (Refer to Note 2)
*   **Other Ancillary Equipment:** Not specified

**Spray Nozzle Details (Refer to Note 4)**
*   **Total Number of Spray Nozzles:** 5
*   **Distribution:**
    *   One nozzle sprays the relief hatch and gas exit pipe.
    *   Four nozzles spray the gas volume.
*   **Calculations:** Spray arc calculations refer to JP's calculations.

**External Brackets Details (Refer to Note 2 & 3)**
*   **Purpose:** Brackets are for radiation level measurement equipment.
*   **Number of External Brackets:** Refers to Note 3.
*   **Sensor Configuration (Note 3):**
    *   Three brackets are for radiation sensors: two for level switches and one for continuous level indication.
    *   One or more level sources with brackets are dependent on level source radiation spread.
