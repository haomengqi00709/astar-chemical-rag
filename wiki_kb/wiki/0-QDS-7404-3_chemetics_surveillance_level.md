# Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7404-3 Chemetics Surveillance Level

This page details the Chemetics surveillance level applicable to the Electrostatic Precipitator Polypropylene Piping project, as defined in Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7404-3.

**Surveillance Level:**
Surveillance activities conducted by Chemetics for this project **shall** be performed to **Level 0.0**.

**Scope of Surveillance:**
All specific surveillance requirements and procedures for Level 0.0 **must** be as explicitly set out and detailed within this Quality Data Sheet (0-QDS-7404-3). This ensures that all parties are clear on the extent and nature of Chemetics' oversight.

For reference on other Chemetics surveillance levels, consult [[0-QDS-7404-1_chemetics_surveillance_level]] and [[0-QDS-7404-2_chemetics_surveillance_level]].
