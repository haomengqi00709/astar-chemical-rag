# Diaphragm Pump Support Criteria (4-SCR-0020)

This document, 4-SCR-0020, outlines the specific support criteria for a diaphragm pump, including detailed equipment data, unfactored loads, and anchor bolt specifications. It serves as a standard for the installation of diaphragm pumps on concrete and grout foundations.

### Unfactored Equipment Loads

The unfactored loads for the diaphragm pump, which are essential for structural design calculations (see [[4-SCR-0002_unfactored_equipment_loads]] for general information on unfactored loads), include:

*   Assembled Pump Mass (kg) (includes motor, etc.)
*   Power (kW)
*   Full Load Speed (RPM)

### Anchor Bolt and Grouting Specifications

The specifications for the anchor bolts and grouting are as follows:

*   **Number of Bolts:** 4 ea
*   **Anchor Bolt Type:** Epoxy Adhesive Anchor System
*   **Anchor Bolt Material:** 4.6
*   **Nut Material:** 4
*   **Dimension 'Y' - Grout:** 40 mm

For further details on anchor bolt materials, refer to [[4-SCR-0007_anchor_bolt_and_nut_material]].

### Support Criteria and Design Requirements

Adherence to the following mandatory requirements is crucial for the structural integrity and safety of the diaphragm pump installation:

*   Loads **must** be applied at Datum.
*   The minimum "Bolt to Edge" distance **shall** be 100 mm.
*   For any other "Bolt to Edge" distances, the [[structural_and_architectural_design_and_specification_3_prc_0002|Civil/Structural Department]] **must** be consulted.
*   The Basis for Support Criteria **requires** the Equipment Drawing Number and Revision. This information is vital for verifying the design against the actual equipment specifications.
