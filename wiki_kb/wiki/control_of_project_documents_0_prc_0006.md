# Control of Project Documents (Procedure 0-PRC-0006)

This procedure, identified by document ID 0-PRC-0006, outlines the comprehensive methodology for managing project documents within Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. Its primary purpose is to establish a standardized means to accurately record and report on the status of all published project documents.

Furthermore, the procedure details the mechanisms for tracking and reporting on the distribution of these critical project documents. This includes distribution to various external and internal stakeholders such as Owners, Vendors, Subcontractors, and Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' own field supervisors.

The central tool for executing the requirements of this procedure is the [[document_control_database]]. All recording and reporting activities described herein must be performed using this database. Personnel specifically assigned to the [[document_control_department]] are solely responsible for making all entries into the Document Control Database, ensuring data integrity and consistency.

Related procedures cover specific aspects of document management, including [[document_file_naming_conventions]] (0-PRC-0003) and [[0_prc_0005_document_overview]] (0-PRC-0005) for publishing.

Furt...hermore, the procedure details the methods for recording and reporting on the distribution of project documents to various external and internal parties, including Owners, Vendors, Subcontractors, and Aker Kvaerner Chemetics field supervisors.

The [[document_control_database]] serves as the central repository for all recording and reporting activities outlined in this procedure. All entries into the Document Control Database are to be made by personnel of the Document Control Department.
