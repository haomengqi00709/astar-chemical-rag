# Vendor Quality Assurance Requirements for Carbon Steel Tank (0-QDS-7435)

For the Carbon Steel Tank project under QDS 0-QDS-7435, specific vendor quality assurance requirements are mandated to ensure compliance and quality standards.

**Quality Plan:**
The Vendor **must** prepare a Quality Plan. This plan **shall** be in accordance with the requirements outlined in [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment]].

**Vendor Quality Assurance Compliance:**
The Vendor's Quality Assurance program **must** comply with the specifications detailed in this document, 0-QDS-7435. Additionally, the Vendor's Quality Assurance **must** comply with the general requirements set forth in [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment]].

**Chemetics Surveillance Level:**
Surveillance activities conducted by Chemetics for this project **shall** be to Level 2.0. The specific details and scope of this surveillance **shall** be as set out within this 0-QDS-7435 document. This level of surveillance dictates the extent of Chemetics' involvement in witnessing and holding inspections and tests, as further detailed in [[0-QDS-7435_inspection_and_documentation_requirements]].
