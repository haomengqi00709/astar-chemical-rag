# Vendor Quality Surveillance and Access

To ensure compliance with quality requirements, the goods and any included materials or components **will** be subject to quality surveillance as outlined in Specification [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment|0-SPC-7001]].

**Designated Inspectors**
Quality surveillance **may** be carried out by designated Inspectors, who can be:
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics staff.
*   Personnel from Subcontractor surveillance agencies.
*   The Owner or the Owner’s representative.

**Access Rights**
These designated individuals **will** be granted access to any and all parts of the Vendor's and Sub-Vendor's facilities where work is being executed. This access **shall** include, but is not limited to:
*   Manufacturing areas.
*   Quality control departments.
*   Engineering documents.

This comprehensive access ensures that Inspectors can effectively monitor activities and verify conformance at all stages of production.

**Surveillance Level Determination**
The specific level of quality surveillance to be applied **will** be stated in the reference [[quality_data_sheets_qds|Quality Data Sheet]]. Detailed descriptions of the various surveillance levels (Level 0 to Level 4), including requirements for witness points and hold points, are provided in [[chemetics_surveillance_level]].
