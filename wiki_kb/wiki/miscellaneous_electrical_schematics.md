# Miscellaneous Electrical Schematics

Miscellaneous Electrical Schematics are project-specific drawings that detail control logic for equipment or systems not covered by standard schematic types. These schematics address particular needs or unique control requirements.

**Purpose and Content**
Miscellaneous schematics drawing **shall** show schematics for equipment not described by other standard schematic types. These schematics are project-specific for a particular need.

**Examples of Miscellaneous Schematics:**
*   Schematics for a local control panel for motor re-starting.
*   Schematics for circuit breaker control.
