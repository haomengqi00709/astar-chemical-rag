# Brick Lined Equipment Field Inspection Items

This page details the mandatory inspection items for brick-lined equipment, as specified in the Field Inspection Checklist Document ID 4-FIC-XX15-R0. Adherence to these requirements is crucial for ensuring the quality and integrity of the brick lining.

**Mandatory Inspection Requirements:**

*   **Brick Condition:**
    *   Bricks **must** be free from cracks.
*   **Mortar Joints:**
    *   All horizontal mortar joints **must** be level.
    *   All vertical mortar joints **must** be staggered.
    *   All mortar joints **must** be full.
    *   Mortar **must** be free from cracks.
*   **Brick Installation:**
    *   Bricks **must** be installed according to the detailed brick drawing.
    *   Different types of brick may be present and **must** be installed as per the relevant drawings.
*   **Nozzle Liners:**
    *   Nozzle liners **must** be installed as per drawings.
    *   Nozzle liners **must** be packed as per drawings.
*   **Vessel Cleanliness:**
    *   Acid wash **must** be completed.
    *   Vessel **must** be clean.
    *   Debris **must** be removed from the vessel.

These items are critical for the quality assurance of brick-lined equipment. For an overview of this checklist, refer to [[4-FIC-XX15-R0_brick_lined_equipment_field_inspection_checklist_overview]].
Further details on inspection and documentation requirements for acid brick lining can be found in [[0-QDS-7431_inspection_and_documentation_requirements]] and [[0-QDS-7431_key_items_and_activities]].
