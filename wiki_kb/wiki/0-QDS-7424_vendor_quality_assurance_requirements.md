# Vendor Quality Assurance Requirements for Polypropylene Tank (0-QDS-7424)

This section outlines the mandatory quality assurance requirements for vendors involved in the Polypropylene Tank project, as specified in Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7424. Adherence to these requirements is crucial for ensuring the quality and integrity of all supplied materials and services.

**Mandatory Vendor Quality Assurance:**
*   The Vendor **must** prepare a Quality Plan. This plan **shall** be in accordance with the requirements specified in [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment]].
*   The Vendor's Quality Assurance program **must** comply with all stipulations set forth in this Quality Data Sheet (0-QDS-7424).
*   The Vendor's Quality Assurance program **must** also comply with the general requirements detailed in [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment]].

These requirements ensure that the vendor's quality management system is aligned with both project-specific needs and broader company standards for equipment procurement. For a general overview of vendor quality assurance, refer to [[0-SPC-7002_vendor_quality_assurance]]. The administrative details of this QDS can be found at [[0-QDS-7424_polypropylene_tank_quality_data_sheet_administration]].

**Vendor Quality Plan and Compliance:**
*   Vendors are required to prepare a Quality Plan in accordance with document `0-SPC-7001: Vendor Quality Assurance`.
*   Vendor's Quality Assurance processes must comply with the requirements set forth in this Quality Data Sheet (0-QDS-7424) and `0-SPC-7001: Vendor Quality Assurance`.
*   If a Quality Plan is not required for a specific vendor or scope, any related cells or sections in the documentation should be deleted.
