# Final Single Line Diagrams

Final Single Line Diagrams (SLDs) are detailed representations of the electrical distribution system, providing comprehensive information about equipment, protective devices, and system parameters. They are more detailed than [[preliminary_single_line_diagrams]] and serve as a critical reference throughout the project.

**Content of Final Single Line Diagrams**
Final versions of single line diagram drawings **shall** have a legend to describe the equipment, protective relays, and other accessories.
Final single line diagrams **must** include more details about:
*   The equipment.
*   Protective relaying.
*   Other accessories.
*   All bus voltages.
*   All ampere ratings.
*   All bus bracing ratings.
*   All CT (Current Transformer) ratings.
*   All PT (Potential Transformer) ratings.
*   All cable sizes.
*   All bus duct sizes.
*   All power transformer sizes.
*   All motor sizes.

The [[motor_list_electrical]] (9-LST-0200) **will** be used for motor sizes to be shown on single line diagrams.

The battery limit for the incoming power source **must** be indicated. The responsible party to connect the incoming power source to the incoming switchgear **must** be indicated.
