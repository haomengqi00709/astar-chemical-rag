# Mag Drive Pump Mechanical Design Requirements

This page outlines the mechanical design requirements for Mag Drive Pumps, as specified in Document ID 4-DST-XXXX-R1. These requirements ensure the pump's structural integrity, operational reliability, and compliance with relevant standards and site conditions.

**Mechanical Design Requirements**
*   **Design Maximum Suction Pressure**: The maximum pressure the pump's suction side is designed to withstand, in kPa(g).
*   **Design Maximum Temperature**: The maximum temperature the pump is designed to operate at, in oC.
*   **Equipment Specification**: References specific equipment standards or project specifications.
*   **General Equipment Requirements**: Broad requirements applicable to all equipment of this type.
*   **Design Code**: The applicable design code (e.g., ASME, API) governing the pump's construction.
*   **Site Conditions**: Specific environmental and operational conditions at the installation site.
*   **Connection Type**: The type of connections required for piping (e.g., flanged, threaded).
*   **Electrical Area Classification**: The hazardous area classification for electrical components, ensuring compliance with safety standards.
*   **Painting**: Specifications for the external painting of the pump.
*   **Casing Drain**: Requirement for a drain connection on the pump casing.
*   **Total Noise Limit**: The maximum permissible noise level generated by the pump during operation.
*   **Vibration**: Acceptable limits and specifications for pump vibration.
*   **Impeller Size**: The specified diameter of the pump impeller.
*   **Speed**: The operating speed of the pump, typically in RPM.
*   **Base Plate**: Requirement for a base plate to support the pump and motor.
*   **Back Pull Out**: Requirement for a back pull-out design, allowing maintenance without disturbing piping or the motor.

These requirements are critical for the safe and efficient integration of the pump into the plant. Specific details on site conditions can be found in [[site_design_conditions_1_dst_0001]], and electrical safety classifications are detailed in [[electrical_area_classification_requirements]].
