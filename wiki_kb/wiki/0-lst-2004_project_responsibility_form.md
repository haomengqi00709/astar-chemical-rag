# 0-LST-2004 Project Responsibility Form

Form 0-LST-2004, titled "Project Responsibility," is the official document used to record the assignment of key personnel for a project. This form ensures a formal record of who holds which critical roles.

**Mandatory Requirements for Recording and Approval:**
*   The [[project_manager_and_engineer_assignment|Project Manager]] **shall** record the assignment of key personnel on form 0-LST-2004.
*   Form 0-LST-2004 **shall** be checked by both the Director of Engineering and the Director of Projects.
*   Form 0-LST-2004 **shall** be approved by the President.

**Revisions and Reassignments:**
*   The Project Manager **shall** record any new assignments or reassignments of personnel by revising form 0-LST-2004.
*   Revisions to form 0-LST-2004 **shall** be checked by both the Director of Engineering and the Director of Projects.
*   Revisions to form 0-LST-2004 **shall** be approved by the President.

This rigorous checking and approval process ensures that all key personnel assignments and changes are formally documented and endorsed by senior management.

Data available in SQL: 0-LST-2004

For the overarching procedure on personnel assignments, refer to [[0-prc-2001_document_overview]]. This form is a critical component of the firm's approach to [[assignment_of_project_responsibilities]].
