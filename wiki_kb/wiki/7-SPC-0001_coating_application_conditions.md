# Protective Coating Application Conditions (7-SPC-0001)

The application of protective coatings, as specified in `7-SPC-0001 Protective Coating Specification`, requires strict adherence to environmental and procedural conditions to ensure optimal adhesion and durability.

**Environmental Conditions for Application**
*   **Priming Time:** Equipment **must** be primed within 8 hours of blast cleaning. In areas of high humidity, this priming time **will** be reduced accordingly.
*   **Surface Condition:** Under no circumstances **will** equipment showing signs of rust or condensation be painted. Paint **should not** be applied to moist surfaces.
*   **Temperature:** Painting **will not** be done when the surface temperature of the metal substrate is less than 3°C (5°F) above the dew point. Surface temperatures of metal substrates **will not** exceed 38°C (100°F) during the painting operation.
*   **Weather Conditions:** Painting **will not** be attempted during periods of rain, fog, snow, or high winds.

**Application Techniques**
*   **Stripe Coating:** Edges and sharp discontinuities **must** be "stripe coated" to ensure adequate coverage, as these areas are prone to thinner film build-up during general application.
*   **Multiple Topcoats:** Where more than one topcoat is required, the intermediate and final coats **must** have a different color. This helps ensure uniform coverage and allows for visual inspection of each layer.
