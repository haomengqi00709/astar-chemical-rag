# Change Notice Log

The Change Notice Log is a record of all formal change notices issued for a project, documenting potential changes to scope, schedule, or cost. This log is summarized in the [[0-rep-2001_monthly_progress_report_format|Monthly Progress Report]] and included as an attachment for detailed reference.
