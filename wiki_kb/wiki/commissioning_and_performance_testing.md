# Commissioning and Performance Testing

**Commissioning Manager Assignment:**
The Director of Technology will assign the [[commissioning_manager_assignment]] at an appropriate time during the project lifecycle.

**Commissioning Completion:**
Commissioning will be considered complete only when all performance tests have been successfully completed and passed.

**Performance Guarantees:**
Plant performance measures are guaranteed as part of the project contract.

**Performance Test Duration:**
The required performance test for the plant is 72 hours in duration.
