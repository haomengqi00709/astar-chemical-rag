# Template Vertical Pump Motor and Drive System

This page details the requirements for the motor and drive system of the vertical pump, as specified in the Template Vertical Pump Datasheet (4-DST-XXXX-R1). These specifications are essential for ensuring the pump receives adequate power and operates efficiently.

**Motor Supply and Mounting:**

*   Motor supply details **ARE REQUIRED**. This includes information on who is responsible for providing the motor (e.g., Vendor, Client).
*   Motor mounting details **ARE REQUIRED**. This specifies how the motor is to be integrated with the pump assembly.

**Mandatory Motor Specifications:**

*   **Motor Mounting type:** The specific mounting configuration (e.g., C-face, D-flange, vertical solid shaft) **MUST** be specified.
*   **Motor Specification:** A detailed specification for the motor (e.g., NEMA, IEC standards, enclosure type, insulation class, service factor) **MUST** be provided. Refer to [[electrical_equipment_specifications]] for general motor requirements.
*   **Motor Size:** The required power output of the motor (e.g., kW or HP) **MUST** be specified. This is determined by the pump's hydraulic requirements and efficiency.

The motor selected **MUST** be compatible with the pump's operating speed and torque requirements, and adhere to the project's [[motor_list_electrical]] and electrical design standards.

**Motor Supply and Mounting:**
*   Motor supply details are required.
*   Motor mounting details are required.
*   **Motor Mounting Type:** A required specification.

**Motor Specifications:**
*   **Motor Specification:** A required specification.
*   **Motor Size:** A required specification.
