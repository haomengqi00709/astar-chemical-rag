# Vendor Quality Assurance for Bulk Materials (0-SPC-7002)

This specification, Document ID 0-SPC-7002, describes the requirements for a Vendor’s quality assurance system for the supply of bulk goods. It **must** be read in conjunction with the specific [[quality_data_sheets_qds]] for the goods being supplied.

**Quality System and Personnel**
The Vendor **shall** maintain a system of controls to accomplish the specified requirements. This includes following documented procedures for the control of [[subcontractor_selection_and_approval|subcontractor/supplier selection]]. Vendor’s personnel responsible for performing quality-related activities **must** possess the necessary experience, background, and training to perform their assigned functions. Personnel responsible for selecting goods to supply against a customer’s order **shall** be familiar with the goods.

**Material Handling and Identification**
Storage and handling of the goods **shall** be accomplished in a manner that will prevent damage or deterioration. Identification **must** be maintained on the goods or in documentation identifiable to the goods from initial receipt. This identification provides traceability to appropriate [[quality_record_definitions]], such as Material Test Reports.

**Inspection and Marking**
All goods **shall** be inspected for damage and for conformance to [[purchase_order_overview]] requirements. Marking of goods **must** comply with the relevant specification. Markings **shall** be verified and correlated with markings on the goods.

**Non-Conformance and Documentation**
Any non-conforming goods **shall** be returned or quarantined until the non-conformance is rectified. The Vendor **must** retain all documents furnishing evidence of the quality of the goods.

**Reporting**
The Vendor **shall** immediately report to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics any event that has or could have a significant impact on quality.
