# Field Instrumentation Tracking and Checkout Procedure (8-PRC-0011)

This procedure (document ID 8-PRC-0011) outlines the systematic tracking of field instrumentation from receipt through installation and testing, ensuring instruments are ready for [[water_batching]] and [[commissioning]]. The instrument approval tags generated through this process will form a critical part of the [[mechanical_completion_package]].

**Assumptions and Prerequisites**
This procedure assumes that the subcontractor has been provided with all necessary construction-issued documentation, including:
*   [[8-dst-xxxx_instrument_specification_sheets|Instrument Specification Sheets]]
*   [[8-lst-0002_instrument_index|Instrument Index]]
*   [[8-lop-xxxx_instrument_loop_diagrams|Instrument Loop Diagrams]]
*   Chemetics "[[instrument_approval_tag|INSTRUMENT APPROVAL TAGS]]"

**Instrument Approval Tag Management**
The [[instrument_approval_tag]] serves as the primary record, providing a complete history from instrument receipt through loop checkout.
*   The subcontractor **must** permanently affix (using Ty-Rap) an [[instrument_approval_tag]] to each instrument upon receipt.
*   Both the primary sensor and its associated transmitter **require** individual tags.
*   If an [[instrument_approval_tag]] becomes detached, the subcontractor **must** immediately replace it and confirm the accuracy of the information recorded on the replaced tag.
*   All data recorded on the [[instrument_approval_tag]] **shall be** printed in a clear and concise manner.

**Procedure Stages**

**Stage 1: Instrument Receipt and Inspection**
Upon receipt, the subcontractor **shall** inspect each instrument for physical damage.
*   In the event of physical damage, the subcontractor **must** inform the Chemetics site supervisor in writing immediately.
*   The subcontractor **shall** confirm that the instrument matches the [[8-dst-xxxx_instrument_specification_sheets|Instrument Specification Sheet]] and that the engraved instrument Tag No. is equivalent to the Tag No. on the specification sheet.
*   The model number and serial number **shall be** recorded directly from the instrument itself, not from the [[8-dst-xxxx_instrument_specification_sheets|Instrument Specification Sheet]]. Any variance in model numbers **shall be** noted in the comments section (Stage No. 6) of the [[instrument_approval_tag]].
*   On completion of Stage No. 1, the subcontractor **shall** sign and date the [[instrument_approval_tag]] as "Inspected".

**Stage 2: Instrument Calibration**
Following Stage No. 1, the subcontractor **shall** confirm the instrument's calibration.
*   If a certified calibration certificate is provided by the manufacturer, the subcontractor **shall** confirm calibration against the [[8-dst-xxxx_instrument_specification_sheets|Instrument Specification Sheet]]. The calibration range or setpoint and units **shall be** recorded on the [[instrument_approval_tag]], indicating "By Manufacturer" if a certified certificate is used. Certified calibration certificates **shall be** maintained on file by the subcontractor and turned over to the Chemetics site supervisor at [[mechanical_completion_package|mechanical completion]]. Calibration ranges or setpoints indicated directly on field instruments are not considered certified.
*   All other field instruments **shall be** bench checked by the subcontractor to confirm calibration as per the [[8-dst-xxxx_instrument_specification_sheets|Instrument Specification Sheets]]. Necessary calibration adjustments **shall be** made to meet specification requirements. [[calibration_check_sheets]] **shall be** maintained on file for each instrument, noting any adjustments.
*   Control valve and on/off valve fail positions **shall be** confirmed against the [[8-dst-xxxx_instrument_specification_sheets|Instrument Specification Sheets]] and recorded on the [[instrument_approval_tag]] under "Valve/Xtr Fail Pos.". For transmitters with embedded fail high/low positions, the configured fail position **shall be** recorded.
*   Any K numbers or calibration numbers (e.g., for Mag Flow Meters) **shall be** recorded in Stage No. 6 (COMMENTS) of the [[instrument_approval_tag]].
*   On completion of Stage No. 2, the subcontractor **shall** sign and date the [[instrument_approval_tag]] as "Calibrated By".

**Stage 3: Instrument Installation**
After Stage No. 2, the instrument is ready for installation.
*   On completion of installation (including all supports, tubing, and wiring), the subcontractor **shall** sign and date the "Ready for Checkout" section of the [[instrument_approval_tag]].
*   The E&I subcontractor **shall** coordinate the installation of all inline instruments with the piping contractor and is responsible for signing and dating the "Ready for Checkout" for these instruments.
*   The Chemetics site supervisor or representative **shall** verify that the installation meets all [[engineering_documentation_requirements]] and manufacturer instructions, then sign and date the "Checked By" section for installation verification.

**Stage 4: Functional Checkout (Electrical Wiring)**
Following Stage No. 3, the instrument is ready for functional checkout.
*   Local instruments (e.g., pressure gauges, temperature gauges, rotameters) **will be** functionally checked during [[water_batching]]. The subcontractor **shall** sign "Ready for Checkout" on local instruments immediately after completing Stage No. 3.
*   For all instruments with electrical wiring, the subcontractor **shall** check wire continuity using an ohmmeter, buzzer, or equivalent apparatus to confirm termination accuracy as per the [[8-lop-xxxx_instrument_loop_diagrams|Instrument Loop Diagrams]].
*   The subcontractor **shall** sign "Ready for Checkout" upon completion of continuity tests.
*   The Chemetics site supervisor or representative **shall** verify that the instrument function meets all [[engineering_documentation_requirements]], then sign and date the "Checked By" section for instrument function checkout.

**Stage 5: Loop Checkout and Mechanical Completion**
After Stage No. 4 and once all deficiencies are corrected to the satisfaction of the Chemetics site supervisor or representative, the subcontractor **shall** sign and date "Ready for Checkout".
*   The Chemetics site supervisor or representative **shall** sign and date "Checked By" when satisfied that the installation and loop check are complete with respect to both engineering documents and manufacturer instructions.
*   A plain green tag **shall be** attached (using Ty-Rap) by the Chemetics site supervisor or representative to provide a visual indication that the instrument is mechanically complete and ready for [[water_batching]].

**Stage 6: Comments and Owner Acceptance**
*   The subcontractor may record any comments related to the instrument in Stage No. 6 (COMMENTS) of the [[instrument_approval_tag]].
*   If owner acceptance of individual instruments is required, the owner **shall** sign and date the card in Stage No. 6.

Each stage of inspection or check **is to be** performed as per Chemetics Procedure Standard-8-PRC-0011.
