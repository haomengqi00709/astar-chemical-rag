# Around the World Travel Tickets

"Around the World" (RTW) tickets offer a specialized travel option for extensive international itineraries, subject to specific booking and routing rules.

**Booking Requirements:**
*   **Advance Booking:** Reservations and ticketing **must** be completed at least 7 days prior to departure.
*   **Airline Partners:** All flights **must** be with airline partners within a single alliance (e.g., all Star Alliance or all One World).
*   **Minimum Stops:** There **must** be at least three stops, each requiring a minimum of one night's stay.
*   **Minimum Travel Time:** The minimum total trip duration is 10 days.
*   **Maximum Travel Time:** The maximum total trip duration is 12 months.
*   **Class of Travel:** RTW tickets **must** be Business Class, and sufficient availability in Business Class is required to qualify for the RTW discount.

**Routing Rules:**
*   **Ocean Crossings:** Travel **must** include crossings over both the Pacific and Atlantic oceans, with only one crossing allowed for each.
*   **No Backtracking:** Backtracking is not permitted (e.g., flying to Europe, then Asia, then back to Europe again).
*   **Typical Route Example:** A common RTW route might be Vancouver to Perth to Singapore to Frankfurt to Vancouver, fulfilling the minimum three stops and crossing both oceans.

**Changes and Costs:**
*   **Date/Time Changes:** Changes to the date or time of flights are generally free, with the exception that no changes are allowed to the first flight within 7 days of departure.
*   **Routing Changes:** Changes to the routing of the itinerary incur a fee of $150.

**Pricing:**
"Around the World" ticket prices vary based on the total miles traveled, with typical tiers being $8700, $10,000, or $11,700.
