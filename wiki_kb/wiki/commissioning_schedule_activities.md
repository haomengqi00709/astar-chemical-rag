# Commissioning Schedule Activities

The commissioning schedule component of the overall Project Schedule focuses on the activities required to bring the plant or system into operational readiness.

Initial scheduling of commissioning activities **will** be performed at [[project_schedule_detail_levels#level-2-schedule-control-schedule|Level 2]]. This initial scheduling phase is crucial for establishing the required overall duration for the commissioning process. Final scheduling of commissioning **will** be conducted at [[project_schedule_detail_levels#level-3-schedule-detailed-schedule|Level 3]] to provide a comprehensive and granular plan.

For detailed execution, [[project_schedule_level_4_work_plans]] for commissioning **will** be developed in the form of checklists. These checklists serve as a guide for the Commissioning Manager and ensure all necessary steps are completed systematically.

Commissioning Task Lists are prepared to outline activities required for the subsequent reporting period. These lists **will** show early completion dates and identify preceding and succeeding activities, providing a clear roadmap for the Commissioning Manager.

The percentage completion for commissioning activities is derived directly from the field checklists, ensuring accurate tracking of progress during this critical phase.
