# Tantalum Alloy Pressure and Leak Testing

This section details the requirements for pressure and leak testing of tantalum alloy tubes and equipment, as specified in [[4-SPC-0045_tantalum_alloy_welding_specification_overview|4-SPC-0045]].

*   **Hydrostatic Testing**: Hydrostatic tests **must** be completed in accordance with [[ASTM B521]] (latest edition).
*   **Pneumatic Proof Test**: A pneumatic proof test, when specified, **will** be performed after the hydrostatic test has been accepted. The pneumatic proof test pressure **must** be held for 30 seconds minimum.
*   **Helium Leak Test (Alternative)**: Alternatively, a helium leak test of all tubes can be performed. The Vendor **must** have all standard testing equipment and procedures available for the helium leak test. The helium leak test **will** satisfy [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels|ASME Code]] requirements.

For general information on testing, refer to [[pressure_and_leak_testing]].
