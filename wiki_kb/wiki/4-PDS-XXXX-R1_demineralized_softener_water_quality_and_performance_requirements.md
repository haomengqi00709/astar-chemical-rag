# Demineralized-Softener Water Quality and Performance Requirements

This page outlines the critical water quality parameters and system performance requirements for demineralized and softener units, as specified in Document ID 4-PDS-XXXX-R1. These specifications define the desired influent and effluent water characteristics, ensuring the system meets the required purity standards for downstream processes.

**Water Specification/Performance Requirement (IN/OUT Parameters)**
The following parameters are typically specified for both the inlet (IN) and outlet (OUT) streams to define the purification capability of the demineralized/softener system. All concentrations are expressed in parts per million (ppm):

*   **Calcium (ppm):** A key hardness-causing ion, typically removed by softening.
*   **Magnesium (ppm):** Another significant hardness-causing ion, also targeted by softening.
*   **Manganese (ppm):** A common metal impurity that can foul resins and affect water quality.
*   **Sodium (ppm):** An alkali metal ion, typically removed by demineralization.
*   **Potassium (ppm):** Another alkali metal ion, removed during demineralization.
*   **Iron (ppm):** A metal impurity that can cause staining and fouling.
*   **Bicarbonate (ppm):** A component of alkalinity, removed during demineralization.
*   **Carbonate (ppm):** Another component of alkalinity, removed during demineralization.
*   **Hydroxide (ppm):** A strong base, relevant for pH control and alkalinity.
*   **Sulphate (ppm):** An anion typically removed by demineralization.
*   **Chloride (ppm):** Another common anion, removed during demineralization.
*   **M Alkalinity (ppm):** Methyl orange alkalinity, representing total alkalinity.
*   **P Alkalinity (ppm):** Phenolphthalein alkalinity, representing hydroxide and half-carbonate alkalinity.
*   **Turbidity (ppm):** A measure of water clarity, indicating suspended solids. High turbidity can foul resins.
*   **TDS (ppm):** Total Dissolved Solids, a measure of all inorganic and organic substances dissolved in water.
*   **TSS (ppm):** Total Suspended Solids, a measure of insoluble particles suspended in water.
*   **TOC (ppm):** Total Organic Carbon, a measure of organic contaminants.

**System Performance Requirements**
In addition to water quality, the following system-level performance parameters are specified:

*   **System Pressure Drop (kPa):** The total pressure loss across the entire demineralized/softener system, including internal components and associated piping. This is a critical factor for pump sizing and energy consumption. [[pressure_drop_calculations|Pressure drop calculations]] are essential for system design.
*   **Effluent Pressure (kPa(g)):** The required pressure of the treated water exiting the system.
*   **Conductivity (mS/cm):** A measure of the water's ability to conduct electricity, directly related to the concentration of dissolved ions. Lower conductivity indicates higher water purity.

These detailed specifications ensure that the demineralized and softener systems are designed and operated to deliver water of the required quality for industrial applications, protecting downstream equipment and processes.
