# Template VOC-SCR Package Material Specifications

This page details the material selection requirements for the Template VOC-SCR Package, as specified in document 4-PDS-XXXX-R1. Proper material selection is critical to ensure the longevity, integrity, and safe operation of the unit, especially given the corrosive nature of the process streams and the high temperatures involved in VOC/SCR applications.

**Wetted Parts Material:**

*   The Material of Construction for all wetted parts **shall** be stainless steel.

This requirement ensures resistance to corrosion from the process gases and any condensed liquids. The specific grade of stainless steel (e.g., 304L, 316L, duplex stainless steel) will be determined based on detailed process conditions and corrosive species present, in accordance with [[material_selection]] guidelines. Vendors **must** specify the exact grade of stainless steel proposed for all wetted components in their offers.

For general material requirements and quality assurance, refer to [[vendor_quality_assurance]].
