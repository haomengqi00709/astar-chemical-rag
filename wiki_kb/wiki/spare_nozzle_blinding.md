# Spare Nozzle Blinding

This page specifies the requirements for installing [[spare_nozzle_blinding|blinds]] of the correct material on all spare nozzles in [[vessel_and_tank_mechanical_checkout|vessels and tanks]]. This is a mandatory step to ensure system integrity and safety.
