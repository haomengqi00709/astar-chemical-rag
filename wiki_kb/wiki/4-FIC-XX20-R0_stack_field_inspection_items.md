# Stack Field Inspection Checklist Items (4-FIC-XX20-R0)

This page outlines the mandatory inspection items for stack equipment as detailed in the [[4-FIC-XX20-R0_stack_field_inspection_checklist_overview|Stack Field Inspection Checklist (4-FIC-XX20-R0)]]. Adherence to these requirements is critical for ensuring the safe and proper installation and operation of the stack.

**Mandatory Inspection Items:**
*   Correct nameplate **must** be installed.
*   Foundation/anchor bolts **must** be secure and tightened.
*   Stack internals (drain pipes, drip rings etc) **must** be installed.
*   Correct gaskets and hardware **must** be installed.
*   All platforms, hand rails, walkways, stairs & ladders **must** be installed.
*   Safety gates **must** be installed & functioning.
*   All NDT inspections **must** be completed and records available. Refer to [[stack_non_destructive_testing_requirements]] for NDT specifications.
*   Stack **must** be clean and empty.
*   Internal painting or lining **must** be completed (attach inspection reports). Refer to [[coatings_erection_0_spc_6x97]] for coating requirements.
*   Final internal inspection **must** be completed with client (attach closure certificate).
*   Manway covers **must** be installed & tightened. Refer to [[manway_covers_installation_and_inspection]].
*   Stack accessories (sight glasses, sample ports, vents, drains etc) **must** be installed. Refer to [[stack_accessories]].
*   External painting **must** be completed. Refer to [[coatings_erection_0_spc_6x97]].
*   Insulation & cladding **must** be completed. Refer to [[insulation_erection_0_spc_6x96]].
*   Grounding **must** be completed. Refer to [[grounding_installation_details]].

For the overall structure of the checklist, see [[4-FIC-XX20-R0_stack_field_inspection_checklist_structure]]. For sign-off requirements, see [[4-FIC-XX20-R0_stack_field_inspection_sign_off_requirements]].
