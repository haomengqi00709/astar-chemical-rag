# Dual Laminate FRP Equipment Design Requirements (4-SPC-0043)

The design of dual laminate fiberglass reinforced plastic (FRP) equipment under Specification 4-SPC-0043 **shall** substantially adhere to [[ASME_rtp_1_standard_for_reinforced_thermoset_plastic_corrosion_resistant_equipment]] Part 3 - Design, including Non-mandatory Appendices NM-2, NM-3, NM-4, NM-5, NM-12, and NM-13.

**Structural Wall Thickness:**
*   All calculations and references to wall thickness **shall** apply solely to the structural layer of the laminate, as detailed in RTP-1 Appendix NM-13.
*   The thermoplastic liner and the outer surface will be disregarded in design calculations.
*   The minimum average structural wall thickness for any pressure-containing part **shall** be 7mm (9/32”), as specified in RTP-1 Para. 3-200(f).
*   The thermoplastic liner and the outer surface **shall** be in addition to this 7mm (9/32”) minimum structural wall thickness.

**Internal Components and Geometry:**
*   Internal ledges and support rings **shall** be FRP cored. Their conceptual design **shall** follow RTP-1 Mandatory Appendix M-12, Fig. M12D-1.
*   The fabricator **shall** design these ledges and rings to meet the specific service and expected loading conditions.
*   The number of circumferential liner segments within ledges and rings **shall** be minimized.
*   Where feasible, thermoplastic covers for ledges and rings **shall** be vacuum formed in one piece.
*   Internal attachments and reinforcement pads **shall** be located to clear liner shell welds where possible. If intersections are unavoidable, shell welds **shall** be buffed flush, and the reinforcement pad **shall** be continuously welded to the shell.
*   Thermoplastic reinforcing pads at wall attachment points **shall** be fully seal welded against the process.
*   Mixing tank baffles **shall** be fully sealed/encapsulated against the process and **must** prevent any internal deposit build-up.
*   Dip pipe supports **shall** conform to RTP-1 Mandatory Appendix M-12, Fig. M12D-9, unless size constraints dictate otherwise.

**Head and Corner Design:**
*   Dished heads are preferred over conical/toriconical heads.
*   Conical/toriconical head to shell junctions **must** incorporate a radiused knuckle.
*   All corners and contours within the vessel's thermoplastic lining **shall** be radiused by heat forming.
*   90-degree or sharp corners in the liner are prohibited, with the sole exceptions of nozzle-to-shell welds and flange face welds.

**Nozzle Details:**
*   Typical nozzle details **shall** be as provided in RTP-1 Figs. M12D-6 and M12D-7.
*   The "Alternative Weld Design" shown in RTP-1 Fig. M12D-6 **shall not** be used.
*   The flush-mounted bottom drain nozzle detail in the upper part of RTP-1 Fig. M12D-8 is acceptable.
*   Only the FRP structural reinforcement detail for the pump suction nozzle, as depicted in the lower part of RTP-1 Fig. M12D-8, **shall** be used.

**Related Documents:**
*   [[ASME_rtp_1_standard_for_reinforced_thermoset_plastic_corrosion_resistant_equipment]]
*   [[4-SPC-0042_frp_vessel_specification_overview]]

*   The FRP structural wall and outer layers of dual laminate vessels will be designed to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics standard 4-SPC-0042 “Fiberglass Reinforced Plastic (FRP) Equipment.” The inner FRP corrosion liner will be omitted from this design.
*   All calculations for and references to wall thickness will apply to the structural layer of the laminate only (refer to RTP-1 Appendix NM-13). The thermoplastic liner and the outer surface will be ignored in the design calculations.
*   The minimum average structural wall thickness of any pressure containing part is to be 7mm (9/32”) (refer to RTP-1 Para. 3-200(f)). The thermoplastic liner and the outer surface will be additional to this minimum structural wall thickness.
*   Design stress limitations are detailed in RTP-1 Para. M12C-500 (b) and (d).
