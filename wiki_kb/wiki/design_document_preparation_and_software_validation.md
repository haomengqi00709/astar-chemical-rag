**Design Product Approval and Publishing**
All products of design (documents) **must** be checked and approved before they are issued for use by others. Additionally, these design products **must** be published to create an unalterable record prior to their issuance. The procedures governing the checking and approval of design products are:
*   [[publishing_project_documents]]: Publishing of Project Documents (0-PRC-0005).
*   [[0-prc-7004_checking_and_approval_of_design]]: Checking and Approval of Design (0-PRC-7004).

**Software Validation**
A crucial part of design approval involves verifying that any specialist software used for the design has been validated. Software validation can be achieved through several methods:
*   Comparison of a calculated result with a result obtained by an alternate method of calculation.
*   Assessment of the operational performance of a previous design executed using the software.
*   Comparison of a calculated result with a benchmark result provided by the software vendor.

Software verification **must** be thoroughly documented. This documentation is then checked by the user discipline Chief Engineer and subsequently approved by the Director, ensuring robust quality control over design tools.
