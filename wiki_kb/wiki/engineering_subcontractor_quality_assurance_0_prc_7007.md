# Engineering Subcontractor Quality Assurance (Procedure 0-PRC-7007)

Procedure 0-PRC-7007 outlines the roles and responsibilities of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics personnel in assuring the quality of services provided by Engineering Subcontractors. This procedure is critical for maintaining high standards across all outsourced engineering activities.

**Scope and Purpose**
The primary purpose of 0-PRC-7007 is to establish a robust framework for quality assurance concerning services rendered by Engineering Subcontractors. It ensures that all subcontracted work aligns with project requirements and company standards.

**Key Requirements**
Engineering Subcontractors **shall** be issued with [[0-SPC-7004_engineering_subcontractor_quality_assurance]], which details the specific quality requirements for their supplied services. Adherence to 0-SPC-7004 is mandatory for all subcontractors.

**Roles and Responsibilities**
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics personnel, particularly the Discipline Lead and Project Engineer, play crucial roles in the implementation of this procedure. Their responsibilities span from the review and approval of subcontractor documentation to the auditing of services and management of non-conformances, as further detailed in related procedures.
