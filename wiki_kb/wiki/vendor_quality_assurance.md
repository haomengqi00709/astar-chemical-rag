# Vendor Quality Assurance (0-SPC-7001)

Data available in SQL: 0-SPC-7001

The document 0-SPC-7001 R1, titled "Vendor Quality Assurance," establishes the general quality assurance and control requirements that **must** be adhered to by all vendors supplying Goods and Services to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. This specification provides a foundational framework for quality, complementing project-specific Quality Data Sheets (QDS) by setting overarching expectations for vendor quality management systems and practices.

Key aspects covered in this general quality assurance specification include:
*   **Quality Management System (QMS):** Requirements for the vendor to operate an effective QMS, often referencing international standards like ISO 9001.
*   **Quality Control Procedures:** General expectations for in-process quality control, inspection, and testing throughout the manufacturing and fabrication process.
*   **Non-Conformance Management:** Procedures for identifying, documenting, and resolving non-conformances, including corrective and preventive actions.
*   **Documentation and Records:** Requirements for maintaining and submitting quality records, such as material certificates, inspection reports, and test results.
*   **Personnel Qualification:** General requirements for the qualification and certification of personnel involved in quality-critical activities (e.g., welders, NDT technicians).
*   **Sub-Vendor Control:** Expectations for how the vendor manages the quality of goods and services procured from their own sub-vendors.

Vendors **must** comply with the quality assurance requirements set out in this specification, in addition to any project-specific QDS documents like [[quality_data_sheet_4_qds_4520]] and [[quality_data_sheet_4_qds_4540]]. This document is a key reference in Text Section 4: Quality Assurance of Purchase Orders, ensuring a consistent approach to quality across all procurements.

For an overview of how quality assurance requirements are integrated into procurement, refer to [[request_for_quotation_and_purchase_order_structure_and_content]].
