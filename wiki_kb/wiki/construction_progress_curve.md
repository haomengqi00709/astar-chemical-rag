# Construction Progress Curve

The Construction Progress Curve is a visual tool used to represent the cumulative progress of construction activities against the planned schedule. It is a vital component of the [[0-rep-2001_monthly_progress_report_format|Monthly Progress Report]], providing an overview of on-site development and any associated impacts.
