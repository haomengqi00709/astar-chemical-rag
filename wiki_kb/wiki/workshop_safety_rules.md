# Workshop Safety Rules

This page details the specific safety rules that **must** be followed by all personnel, visitors, and contractors within Aker Kvaerner Chemetics [[hse_toronto_workshop|workshop]] facilities, as specified in procedure [[hse_site_postings_overview]] (0-PRC-8004) and referenced by [[worksite_safety_rules_general]]. These rules are designed to ensure a safe and productive workshop environment.

**Mandatory Workshop Safety Rules:**

*   **Personal Protective Equipment (PPE):** Personal protective equipment **shall** be worn in accordance with the Company’s written procedures.
*   **Visitor and Contractor Protocol:** All visitors and contractors are **required** to sign-in and follow safety rules as instructed by the Company.
*   **Substance Use:** Consuming or possession of illegal drugs or alcohol on company premises is **prohibited**. Refer to the [[prohibited_substances_and_conduct]] policy for further details.
*   **Injury Reporting and First Aid:** First Aid treatment **shall** be obtained immediately for any injury.
*   **Hazard Reporting:** Unsafe conditions, safety observations, and incidents are to be reported immediately to your supervisor. For incident reporting guidelines, see [[hse_incident_reporting]].
*   **Work Wear:** Only company-provided work wear **shall** be worn when working in the workshop.
*   **Tool Condition:** Only tools that are in good repair, meet industry standards, and are equipped with all necessary guards and safety devices **will** be used.
*   **Conduct:** Fighting, horseplay, practical jokes, or interference with other workers is **prohibited**.
*   **Company Property:** Theft, vandalism, abuse, or misuse of Company property is **prohibited**.

These rules are prominently posted at the employee entrance of workshop facilities and **will** be clearly readable from a distance of two meters.
