# Task Safety Awareness (TSA) Promotion

The [[task_safety_awareness_overview]] (TSA) concept is actively promoted across all project sites to ensure widespread understanding and adoption. This is achieved through a multi-faceted awareness campaign utilizing various communication tools and materials.

**Promotional Materials and Methods:**
*   **Posters:** Posters illustrating the steps of application **should be used** for general TSA awareness promotion throughout the workplace.
*   **Pledge Posters:** Pledge Posters, signed by supervisors after their [[task_safety_awareness_training_and_orientation]], **should be used** in each craft office to signify commitment and promote awareness.
*   **Banners:** Outdoor banners, measuring 10' x 42", **should be used** for prominent TSA awareness promotion at strategic locations on site.
*   **Awareness Cards:** TSA Awareness Cards, distributed to each supervisor, **should be used** as a handy reference and promotional tool.
*   **Hard Hat Stickers:** TSA Hard Hat Stickers **should be used** to visibly promote the concept among employees.
*   **Forms:** The [[0-REP-8006a_task_safety_awareness_form]] itself, beyond its function as a checklist and record, also serves as a tool for TSA awareness promotion by guiding users through the process.
*   **Application Guidelines:** Comprehensive TSA Application Guidelines are another key source for promoting awareness and understanding of the process.

These promotional efforts are integral to reinforcing a continuous safety mindset and ensuring that the principles of TSA are consistently applied by all personnel, contributing to the overall [[task_safety_awareness_program_monitoring_and_improvement]].

The awareness campaign is specifically designed to re-enforce and ensure that both employees and supervisors execute their joint responsibility to thoroughly analyse each work assignment for potential hazards. It also aims to ensure they agree on sound safety practices before commencing any work. Projects shall continuously stress the concepts, principles of application, and importance of TSA in Safety topics and meetings.
Banners used for promotion are outdoor and measure 10' x 42".
