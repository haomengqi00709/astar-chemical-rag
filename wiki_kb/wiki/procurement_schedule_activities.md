# Procurement Schedule Activities

The procurement schedule component of the overall Project Schedule **will** detail all work activities executed by Vendors and shippers. This sub-schedule is crucial for managing the acquisition and delivery of all necessary project materials and equipment.

Procurement activities **will** be scheduled at [[project_schedule_detail_levels#level-2-schedule-control-schedule|Level 2]]. Work performed by Vendors **will** be grouped by Purchase Order. Within these groupings, Vendor activities **will** be further categorized to show Design, Fabrication, and Testing phases, as applicable. Shipping activities **will** be grouped either by Purchase Order or by consolidated shipment, depending on the logistical arrangements.

Vendors **will** be required to prepare schedules at [[project_schedule_detail_levels#level-3-schedule-detailed-schedule|Level 3]] when specifically requested by the project team. Updates to Vendor schedules **will** be summarized as a percentage completion on the corresponding Level 2 activities within the main procurement schedule.

The percentage completion for procurement activities is derived from Vendor schedules or expediting reports, ensuring accurate progress tracking.
