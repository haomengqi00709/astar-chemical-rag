# Sulphuric Acid Storage Tank Design Requirements (4-SPC-0046)

This section outlines the specific design requirements for sulphuric acid storage tanks, as per specification 4-SPC-0046.

**General Design Responsibility**
The manufacturer is responsible for the overall design of the tank, unless explicitly stated otherwise on the purchase order. The basic design **will** adhere to [[API_650_welded_steel_tanks|API Standard 650 Appendix "D"]], though API approval for this basic design is not required. Overall dimensions and other construction details **will** be as depicted on Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' drawings, which are attached to the request for quotation/purchase order.

**Shell Design**
The design shell thickness **will** be calculated based on the tank being filled with sulphuric acid of a specified specific gravity. A nominal corrosion allowance of 1/8 inch **will** be applied. However, tanks designated for internal coating or protection **will not** require this corrosion allowance. The total shell thickness (combining structural thickness and corrosion allowance) **must not** be less than 3/8 inch and **must not** exceed 1 1/4 inch.

**Annular Ring Plates**
Annular ring plates **will** have a thickness of at least one-half of the nominal design thickness (including corrosion allowance) of the lowest shell course. These plates **must not** be less than 1/2 inch in thickness (including corrosion allowance). In all cases, the annular ring **will** be at least 4 feet wide.

**Bottom Design**
Bottom floor plates **will** have a minimum thickness of 1/2 inch (including corrosion allowance). The tank bottom **will** slope in accordance with the foundation drawings, typically 1 inch per 10 feet, cone up. Floor and first course shell plates containing openings **will** be shop fabricated. Welds in these plates **will** be radiographed as per Section 6 of [[API_650_welded_steel_tanks]], with results and repairs meeting Sections 5 and 6 requirements.

**Nozzles and Manholes**
All nozzles and manholes **will** be seal welded inside the tank. The neck of a side manhole **will** be a minimum 3/4 inch thickness. Roof inlet nozzles **will** project 6 inches inside the roof and **will** be located at least 12 feet from the shell as per referenced drawings. The tank **will** be equipped with an overflow nozzle located in the top of the shell such that the full height of the tank can be utilized. The tank **will** be equipped with a manhole in the first course of the shell. The tank **will** be equipped with nozzles as shown on the drawings.

**Roof Design**
The roof **will** be self-supporting and designed for a minimum load of 25 psf. Roof construction **will** utilize double lap or double butt welding. The joint between the roof plates and the eave angle **will** contain no cavities and **will** be seal welded flush on the inside of the tank. Internal support ribs **will** also be seal welded on the inside of the tank. External support ribs **will** be designed to prevent any accumulation of water, ice, or snow.

**Supporting Columns**
If required, supporting columns **will not** be less than 5/16 inch wall thickness. The bottoms of columns **will** be welded to circular support plates with a continuous seal weld. These support plates **must** be of a sufficient size and thickness to distribute the column load within the bearing capacity of the foundations. Supporting columns and support plates **will** have a nominal corrosion allowance of 1/8 inch minimum.
