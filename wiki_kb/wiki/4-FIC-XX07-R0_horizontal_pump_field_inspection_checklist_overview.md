# Horizontal Pump Field Inspection Checklist Overview

This document, 4-FIC-XX07-R0, serves as a field inspection checklist template for horizontal centrifugal pumps. It is a standard document for Aker Kvaerner, a division of Aker Kvaerner Canada Inc., specifically for projects involving horizontal centrifugal pumps.

The current revision is 0.0, dated 39041.0, with a status of "Comments Requested." The checklist was prepared by R.Dijkstra, checked by T.Caviglia, and approved by D.Shaw.

The checklist comprises 24 distinct inspection items and includes 10 numbered slots for recording deficiencies and comments. It is designed to ensure that the installation of horizontal centrifugal pumps meets specified quality and operational standards.

For detailed specifications of the pump, refer to the [[template_horizontal_centrifugal_pump_datasheet_administration]] and [[template_horizontal_centrifugal_pump_datasheet_structure]]. Specific quality assurance requirements are outlined in [[0-QDS-7412_centrifugal_pump_quality_data_sheet_overview]] and [[0-QDS-7412_final_inspection_requirements]].
