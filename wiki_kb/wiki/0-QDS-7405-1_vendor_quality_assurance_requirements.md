# Blower Quality Data Sheet Vendor Quality Assurance Requirements

This page details the mandatory quality assurance requirements for vendors supplying the Blower Package, as specified in Document ID 0-QDS-7405-1.

**Mandatory Requirements:**
*   The Vendor **shall** prepare a Quality Plan. This plan **must** be in accordance with [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment]].
*   The Vendor's Quality Assurance system **shall** comply with the requirements set forth in this Quality Data Sheet (0-QDS-7405-1).
*   The Vendor's Quality Assurance system **shall** also comply with the general requirements specified in [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment]].

If a Quality Plan is not deemed necessary for a specific scope of work, the corresponding sections and cells related to the Quality Plan within this Quality Data Sheet **shall** be deleted.

For details on the surveillance levels applied by Chemetics, refer to [[0-QDS-7405-1_chemetics_surveillance_level]]. Specific inspection and documentation activities are outlined in [[0-QDS-7405-1_inspection_and_documentation_requirements]].
