# Material Verification and Certifications: CS Tubing

This page outlines the requirements for material verification and certifications for carbon steel tubing, as specified in Quality Data Sheet (QDS) 0-QDS-7428. These measures ensure that all materials used meet the specified metallurgical and performance standards.

**Mandatory Material Requirements:**

*   **Material Verification:**
    *   Material verification **must** comply with **ASTM A178-02**. This standard specifies seamless carbon steel boiler tubes for high-pressure service.
*   **Mill Test Certificates:**
    *   Mill test Certificates are required to be included in the Vendor's Inspection & Test (I & T) Plan (designated as [[0-QDS-7428_quality_record_definitions|FP]]).
    *   Chemetics will **Witness** and **Complete** surveillance for Mill test Certificates, with a remark of "Check/Verify" to ensure thorough review.
*   **Material Testing:**
    *   Material Testing is required to be included in the Vendor's I & T Plan (designated as [[0-QDS-7428_quality_record_definitions|FP]]).
    *   Chemetics will **Witness** and **Complete** surveillance for Material Testing, with a remark of "Check/Verify" to confirm compliance with specifications.

These requirements ensure that the carbon steel tubing possesses the correct chemical composition, mechanical properties, and manufacturing integrity as per the specified ASTM standard. Mill test certificates provide documented evidence from the material manufacturer, detailing the results of chemical analyses, mechanical tests, and other relevant data. Chemetics' surveillance activities, including witnessing and completing the review of these documents, provide an independent layer of assurance that all material specifications are rigorously met before the tubing proceeds to further manufacturing or testing stages. This is a critical step in preventing material non-conformances and ensuring the long-term reliability of the installed equipment.
