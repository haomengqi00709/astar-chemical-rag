# HSE Roles and Responsibilities on EPC Projects

This specification (0-PRC-8401) outlines the Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) responsibilities for site management personnel on Aker Kvaerner Chemetics EPC projects. Site health and safety management **is required** on all Aker Kvaerner Chemetics project work sites.

**Key Roles and Responsibilities:**

*   **Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Construction Services Manager:**
    *   When Aker Kvaerner Chemetics has direct responsibility for construction, this manager **shall** appoint a full-time Health and Safety Manager.
    *   The Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Construction Services Manager is the direct superior for the Site Health and Safety Manager.

*   **Full-time Health and Safety Manager (Construction):**
    *   **Shall** be responsible for health, safety, and the environment on site during construction.

*   **Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Site Construction Manager:**
    *   Has overall responsibility for all activities on the construction site.
    *   **Is responsible** for ensuring that the [[project-0-prc-8405_project_hse_plan]] is implemented in a timely manner.
    *   **Is responsible** for ensuring that the Project Health, Safety and Environment plan is followed by all Aker Kvaerner Chemetics subcontractors.
    *   Specific responsibilities are detailed in [[0-prc-8401_table_1]].

*   **Commissioning Manager:**
    *   When Aker Kvaerner Chemetics is responsible for Commissioning, the Commissioning Manager **shall** fulfill health and safety management responsibilities for its employees.
    *   During commissioning, the Director of Technology may appoint a full-time health and safety manager for the site. See [[commissioning_manager_assignment]] for more details.

*   **Construction Advisors:**
    *   When Aker Kvaerner Chemetics is responsible for providing only construction advisors, health and safety management responsibilities are defined in Standard-0-PRC-8400, [[aker_kvaerner_construction_site_advisor_hse_requirements]].

*   **Project Managers, Project Advisors, Project Controllers, Subcontract Administrators, and Field Engineers:**
    *   **Are responsible** for Safety and Health activities carried out within their respective areas of control.

All personnel **must** seek authorization for travel as per [[travel_authorization_process_0_frm_9002]] and make preparations in accordance with [[0-prc-8102_travel_health_and_safety]].

This specification (0-PRC-8401) outlines the Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) responsibilities for site management personnel on Aker Kvaerner Chemetics EPC projects. Site health and safety management **is required** on all Aker Kvaerner Chemetics project work sites.

**Key Roles and Responsibilities:**

*   **Full-time Health and Safety Manager Appointment:** When Aker Kvaerner Chemetics has direct responsibility for construction, the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Construction Services Manager will appoint a full-time Health and Safety Manager. This manager will be responsible for health, safety, and the environment on site during construction.
*   **Construction Advisors Scenario:** When Aker Kvaerner Chemetics is responsible for providing only construction advisors, health and safety management responsibilities are set in **Standard-0-PRC-8400 HSE Management for Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Construction Site Advisors**.
*   **Commissioning Scenario:** When Aker Kvaerner Chemetics is responsible for Commissioning, health and safety management responsibilities for its employees will be fulfilled by the Commissioning Manager. During commissioning, the Director of Technology may appoint a full-time health and safety manager for the site.
*   **Other Responsible Personnel:** Project Managers, project advisors, project controllers, subcontract administrators, and field engineers are responsible for Safety and Health activities carried out within their area of control.
