# Professional Services Subcontract (0-SPC-2201) Termination

Professional Services Subcontract 0-SPC-2201 details the conditions under which the subcontract may be terminated by either party, along with the subsequent obligations and financial adjustments.

**Grounds for Termination by Either Party (Insolvency/Financial Distress):**
Either Party may terminate the Subcontract by notifying the other if the other Party (individual, company, or partnership) experiences any of the following:
*   **Individual:** Presents a petition for bankruptcy, has a bankruptcy order made against them, has a receiver appointed over their assets, or makes an arrangement with creditors.
*   **Company or Partnership:** Has a winding-up order made against it, has a provisional liquidator appointed, passes a resolution for winding-up (excluding amalgamation or reconstruction), has an administration order made against it, has a receiver/manager/administrative receiver appointed over a substantial part of its undertaking or assets, or makes an arrangement with its creditors.

**Grounds for Termination by Subcontractor:**
*   The Subcontractor may terminate by notifying Aker Kvaerner Chemetics if Aker Kvaerner Chemetics has not paid an amount due within eleven weeks of the Subcontractor issuing a notice that payment is overdue.

**Grounds for Termination by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics:**
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may terminate by notifying the Subcontractor with 1 week's notice for any reason (termination for convenience).
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may terminate immediately if the Subcontractor has substantially failed to comply with obligations and has not rectified the default within two weeks of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' notification.

**Post-Termination Obligations:**
Upon termination, the following **must** occur:
*   The Subcontractor **must** cease all further work necessary to [[subcontract_definitions_0_spc_2201|Provide the Services]].
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may complete the services itself or engage [[subcontract_definitions_0_spc_2201|Others]] to do so.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may use or make available to Others its rights over material prepared by the Subcontractor or a [[subcontract_definitions_0_spc_2201|Subsubcontractor]].
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may require the Subcontractor to assign the benefit of any sub-consultancy or other related contract to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
*   Both Parties **must** continue to comply with the restrictions and obligations in this Subcontract regarding the use of material prepared or obtained by the Subcontractor and publicizing the services.
*   The Subcontractor **must** provide Aker Kvaerner Chemetics with all information resulting from work carried out to date and any information the Subcontractor has obtained and is responsible for providing under this Subcontract.

**Final Payment on Termination:**
*   A final payment **must** be made as soon as possible after termination.
*   The amount due on termination includes:
    *   An amount assessed as for normal payments.
    *   Other direct costs reasonably incurred by the Subcontractor as a direct consequence of the termination.
    *   Any amounts retained by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
*   **Deductions for Default/Insolvency:** If Aker Kvaerner Chemetics terminates due to the Subcontractor's insolvency or substantial failure to comply with obligations, the amount due **must** include a deduction for the forecast additional cost to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics of completing the services. In such cases, the amount due **does not** include any costs incurred by the Subcontractor in expectation of completing the whole of the services.
*   Any dispute following termination **is settled** in the same manner as other disputes under this Subcontract, as outlined in [[subcontract_dispute_resolution_0_spc_2201]].

**Grounds for Termination by Either Party (Insolvency/Financial Distress):**
Either Party may terminate by notifying the other Party if the other Party:
*   (Individual) has presented his petition for bankruptcy, has had a bankruptcy order made against him, has had a receiver appointed over his assets, or has made an arrangement with his creditors.
*   (Company or partnership) has had a winding-up order made against it, has had a provisional liquidator appointed to it, has passed a resolution for winding-up (other than in order to amalgamate or reconstruct), has had an administration order made against it, has had a receiver, receiver and manager, or administrative receiver appointed over the whole or a substantial part of its undertaking or assets, or has made an arrangement with its creditors.

**Other Grounds for Termination:**
*   The Subcontractor may terminate by notifying Aker Kvaerner Chemetics if Aker Kvaerner Chemetics has not paid an amount due to the Subcontractor within eleven weeks of the issue of a notice by the Subcontractor to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics that payment is overdue.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may terminate by notifying the Subcontractor upon 1 week's notice for any reason.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may terminate immediately if the Subcontractor has substantially failed to comply with his obligations and has not put the default right within two weeks of a notification by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Post-Termination Obligations and Rights:**
*   After termination, the Subcontractor must do no further work necessary to Provide the Services.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may complete the services itself or employ other people to do so.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may use or make available to others its rights over material prepared by the Subcontractor or a Subsubcontractor.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may require the Subcontractor to assign the benefit of any subconsultancy or other contract related to performance of this Subcontract to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
*   The Parties must continue to comply with the restrictions and obligations in this Subcontract on the use of material prepared or obtained by the Subcontractor and on publicising the services.
*   Upon termination, the Subcontractor must give to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics information resulting from work carried out to date and information the Subcontractor has obtained which he has a responsibility to provide under this Subcontract.

**Final Payment on Termination:**
*   A final payment must be made as soon as possible after termination.
*   The amount due on termination includes an amount due assessed as for normal payments, other direct costs reasonably incurred by the Subcontractor as a direct consequence of the termination, and any amounts retained by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
*   If Aker Kvaerner Chemetics terminates because of the insolvency of the Subcontractor or the substantial failure of the Subcontractor to comply with his obligations, the amount due on termination includes a deduction of the forecast of the additional cost to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics of completing the services, and does not include any amount in respect of costs incurred by the Subcontractor in expectation of completing the whole of the services.
*   Any dispute following termination is settled in the same way as this Subcontract states any dispute is settled.
