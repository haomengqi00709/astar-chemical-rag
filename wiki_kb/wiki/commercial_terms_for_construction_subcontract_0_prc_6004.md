# Commercial Terms for Construction Subcontract (0-PRC-6004)

Procedure 0-PRC-6004 details the steps and considerations required for developing the commercial terms and conditions applicable to a Construction Subcontract. This procedure ensures that all financial, legal, and administrative commercial aspects are clearly defined, fair, and compliant with company policies and relevant regulations.

**Purpose:**
The primary objective of 0-PRC-6004 is to establish a robust framework for the commercial aspects of construction subcontracts. This includes:
*   Defining payment schedules, invoicing requirements, and financial guarantees.
*   Outlining liabilities, indemnities, and insurance requirements.
*   Specifying terms related to changes, delays, and dispute resolution.
*   Ensuring alignment with the firm's procurement and financial policies.

**Development of Commercial Requirements:**
Commercial requirements for a Construction Subcontract **must** be developed in accordance with this procedure. Key elements typically covered include:
*   Contract price structure (e.g., lump sum, unit price, cost-reimbursable).
*   Payment milestones and terms.
*   Performance bonds, guarantees, and retention policies.
*   Liquidated damages for delays or performance failures.
*   Provisions for variations, claims, and extensions of time.
*   Governing law and dispute resolution mechanisms.

**Integration with Subcontract Issuance:**
The commercial requirements developed under 0-PRC-6004 are a critical input to the overall [[construction_subcontract_issuance_procedure_0_prc_6005]]. They directly inform the content of the [[construction_subcontract_agreement_template_and_content]] and are essential for the preparation of tender documents.

**Related Procedures:**
*   While 0-PRC-6004 focuses on commercial aspects, technical requirements are developed separately in accordance with [[construction_subcontract_scope_preparation_0_prc_6091]].
*   The commercial terms will be reflected in the [[construction_subcontract_pricing_lists]] and other financial schedules.
*   The output of this procedure will be included in the tender package issued to prospective subcontractors, as part of the [[construction_subcontract_tender_process]].
