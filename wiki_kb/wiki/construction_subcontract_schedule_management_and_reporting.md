# Construction Subcontract Schedule Management and Reporting

Effective management and reporting of the construction subcontract schedule are critical for project control, as detailed in [[0-SPC-6021_construction_subcontract_scheduling|0-SPC-6021]]. This includes procedures for schedule submission, acceptance, regular updates, and performance analysis.

### Schedule Submission and Acceptance
The Subcontractor **shall** prepare the initial schedule within two weeks of subcontract award. This schedule **must** then be submitted to [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_obligations_0_spc_2201|Aker Kvaerner Chemetics]] for acceptance.

Within two weeks of receipt, Aker Kvaerner Chemetics will either accept the schedule or provide notification of the reasons for non-acceptance. Reasons for not accepting the schedule include, but are not limited to:
*   The Subcontractor’s plans shown are not practicable.
*   The schedule does not contain the information required by the subcontract.
*   The schedule does not realistically represent the Subcontractor’s plans.
*   The schedule does not comply with the [[construction_subcontract_works_information|Works Information]].

Once accepted, the schedule **shall** be saved as the target schedule, against which all subsequent revisions will be compared.

### Schedule Updates
The schedule **shall** be updated at regular intervals as specified in the [[0-SUB-6X00_construction_subcontract_agreement|Construction Subcontract Agreement]]. Each update **requires**:
*   Adding or deleting activities and dependencies to reflect any revised execution plan.
*   Recording actual activity start and completion dates.
*   Revising resource hours based on the latest count of work quantities.

Percent completion for [[construction_subcontract_schedule_levels#Level 3 Schedule (Detailed Schedule)|Level 3]] Procurement activities is derived from supplier schedules or [[construction_subcontract_schedule_levels#Level 4 Schedule (Work Plan)|Level 4 Work Plans]] for prefabricated items. Similarly, [[construction_subcontract_schedule_levels#Level 3 Schedule (Detailed Schedule)|Level 3]] Construction activity percent completion is derived from [[construction_subcontract_schedule_levels#Level 4 Schedule (Work Plan)|Level 4 Work Plans]].

The Subcontractor **shall** submit the updated schedule to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics concurrently with the monthly application for payment, as per [[construction_subcontract_price_for_work_done_to_date]].

### Acceleration Plan
Should the schedule indicate a delay in Completion, the Subcontractor **shall** submit an acceleration plan for Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' acceptance. This acceleration plan **must** effectively remedy the identified delay.

### Workday Logs
The Subcontractor **shall** maintain daily logs for each workday. These logs **must** accurately record:
*   Headcount and hours worked for each classification of [[construction_subcontract_labour_rates|direct labour]] on site.
*   Headcount and hours worked for all [[construction_subcontract_indirect_labour_schedule|indirect labour]] personnel on site.
*   A comprehensive listing of each item of [[construction_subcontract_equipment_schedule|Construction Equipment]] present on site.

These logs will be submitted to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics weekly.

### Direct Labour Utilisation Analysis
The Subcontractor **shall** prepare an analysis of direct labour utilisation at the same intervals as the schedule update. This analysis **shall** be submitted simultaneously with the updated schedule. The analysis **shall** present cumulative data as a single graph for each classification of direct labour, and **must** clearly show:
*   Target direct labour utilisation.
*   Forecast of direct labour to be used.
*   Actual direct labour used.
*   Earned direct labour based on originally allocated hours for all work completed.
