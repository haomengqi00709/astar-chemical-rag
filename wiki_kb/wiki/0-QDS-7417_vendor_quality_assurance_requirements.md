# Shell & Tube Heat Exchanger Vendor Quality Assurance Requirements (0-QDS-7417)

For the supply of Shell & Tube Heat Exchangers under document 0-QDS-7417, the Vendor's Quality Assurance program is subject to specific requirements and surveillance levels.

**Vendor Quality Plan:**
The Vendor **must** prepare a comprehensive Quality Plan. This plan **shall** be in accordance with the guidelines specified in [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment|0-SPC-7001: Vendor Quality Assurance]]. The Quality Plan outlines the Vendor's approach to ensuring the quality of the heat exchanger throughout the manufacturing process, from material procurement to final inspection and shipment. If a Quality Plan is not deemed necessary for a particular order, the corresponding cells related to the Quality Plan in the Quality Data Sheet **must** be deleted.

**Compliance:**
The Vendor's overall Quality Assurance system and practices **must** comply with the requirements set forth in this Quality Data Sheet (0-QDS-7417). Additionally, the Vendor's Quality Assurance **must** also comply with the broader requirements detailed in [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment|0-SPC-7001: Vendor Quality Assurance]]. This ensures a consistent standard of quality across all equipment supplied.

**Chemetics Surveillance Level:**
Surveillance by Chemetics for Shell & Tube Heat Exchangers will be conducted at **Level 2.0**. This level of surveillance indicates a specific intensity and frequency of inspections and audits performed by Chemetics representatives or their appointed third-party inspectors. The exact scope and activities of this surveillance **shall** be as set out in this Quality Data Sheet (0-QDS-7417). This ensures that critical manufacturing stages and documentation are adequately monitored and verified.

For definitions of quality records referenced throughout this document, refer to [[0-QDS-7417_quality_record_definitions]].
