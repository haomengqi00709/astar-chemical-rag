# Flocculant Mixer Quality Record Definitions

Quality records are essential documents that provide objective evidence of compliance with specified requirements and the effectiveness of the quality management system. For the Flocculant Mixer package, specific abbreviations are used to denote the status and handling of these records. Understanding these definitions is crucial for proper documentation and traceability throughout the project.

**Quality Record Abbreviations:**

*   **AP (As Prepared):** This abbreviation signifies that the quality record is generated and maintained by the entity responsible for the activity (e.g., the vendor). These records are typically part of the internal documentation but may be requested for review.
*   **FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package):** This abbreviation indicates that the quality record **must** be formally included as part of the final Quality Assurance Documentation Package submitted to Chemetics. These records are critical for demonstrating compliance and are subject to formal review and archiving.

These definitions ensure clarity in the documentation requirements for various inspections, tests, and certifications related to the Flocculant Mixer. For a broader context on quality record management, refer to [[quality_record_definitions]].
