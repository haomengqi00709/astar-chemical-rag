# Zirconium Material Requirements and Preparation (4-SPC-0024)

This page outlines the mandatory material requirements and preparation procedures for Zirconium (Grade 60702) fabrications as specified in document **4-SPC-0024**.

**General Material Requirements:**
*   All materials used **must** be as specified in the purchase order and referenced documents.
*   All materials used **must** conform to ASTM/ASME Codes.
*   Mill Test Certification **must** be supplied with all materials, including welding consumables.
*   Fabricator's internal controls **must** prevent the use of unspecified materials. Lack of adequate identification or certification **will** be cause for rejection.

**Specific Zirconium Material Specifications:**
*   All Zirconium plate, sheet & strip **must** satisfy [[ASME_bpvc_section_ii_materials|ASME/ASTM SB551]].
*   All piping **must** conform to [[ASME_bpvc_section_ii_materials|ASME/ASTM SB523]].
*   All tubing **must** conform to [[ASME_bpvc_section_ii_materials|ASME/ASTM SB658]].
*   All fittings **must** conform to [[ASME_bpvc_section_ii_materials|ASME/ASTM SB493]].

**Cleaning and Contamination Control:**
*   The surface and edges of any Zirconium material to be heated **must** be cleaned free from all traces of foreign matter (e.g., marking paint, oil, etc.).
*   Cleaning **must** be performed by disc grinding, wire brushing with a stainless steel brush, and/or non-chlorinated solvent cleaning.
*   Cleaning **must** occur immediately prior to the application of heat.
*   The fabricator **must** implement necessary controls to prevent contamination of weldments at all stages of fabrication.
*   Controls **must** include the segregation of wire brushes, grinding wheels, etc., for use exclusively on Zirconium.
*   On completion of work, items **must** be cleaned internally and externally. All fabrication markings, oxidation, weld spatter, etc., **must** be removed prior to crating/shipment.

**Dedicated Clean Area for Welding:**
*   All Zirconium welding **must** be carried out in a clean area within the shop.
*   The clean area **must** be independent of, or suitably screened from, the rest of the fabrication facilities.
*   The clean area **must** minimize dust ingress and iron contamination.
*   The screened working area **must** be maintained free from drafts.

**Cutting and Edge Preparation:**
*   Material **must** be cut to size by Machining or Shearing.
*   If material over 3/16" thick is sheared, extra material **must** be left on the sheared edge to allow for machining, grinding, etc., to ensure any defects caused by shearing are removed.
*   Material over 1" thick **must not** be sheared.
*   Material **must** also be cut to size by Grinding, utilizing silicon carbide or alumina type discs.
*   Thermal cutting is preferably carried out by the Plasma Arc Process. If used, welding edges **must** be lightly ground to bright metal.
*   Any other thermal cutting process requires a minimum of 1/8" of metal to be removed by mechanical means on completion to ensure all slag, scale, other irregularities, contaminants, and thermal effects have been removed.

**Forming:**
*   Material **must** be cold formed as far as practical.
*   Ideally, all forming tools **will** have been employed only on Zirconium.
*   Tools used for other materials **must** be cleaned to remove surface contamination prior to use with Zirconium.
*   All burns and indentations **must** be removed by grinding to provide smooth surfaces prior to any forming operation.
*   Unless otherwise agreed, suitable protective interlayers **must** be used between tools and the Zirconium to minimize metallic pick-up.

**Drawing Compliance:**
*   All work **must** be carried out in accordance with the "Approved For Construction" shop detail drawing(s).
*   Deviations from weld geometry on approved drawing(s) are not permitted without prior approval of the QA & Materials Group.
