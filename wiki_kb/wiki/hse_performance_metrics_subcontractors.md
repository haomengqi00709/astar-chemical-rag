# Subcontractor HSE Performance Metrics and Targets

Aker Kvaerner Chemetics evaluates subcontractor Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) performance based on historical data and specific targets. Subcontractors **are required** to submit performance values for the preceding five years to gauge their safety record.

**Required Performance Values (for the preceding 5 years):**
*   Total number of construction man-hours for each year.
*   Number of fatalities.
*   Number of recordable injuries or illnesses with lost workdays.
*   Number of recordable injuries or illnesses without lost workdays.
*   Number of days away from work due to non-work related sick leave.

**Incident Rate Calculations:**
*   **Recordable Incident Rate (RIR)**: The RIR **is to be computed** using the formula: (Number of recordable injuries or illness with lost workdays + Number of recordable injuries or illness without lost workdays) x 200,000 / (Total number of construction man-hours for year) = Incident rate.
*   **Lost Time Injury Rate (LTIR)**: The LTIR **is to be computed** using the formula: (Number of recordable injuries or illness with lost workdays) x 200,000 / (Total number of construction man-hours for year) = Incident rate.

Subcontractors **are to submit** these calculated values for the previous five years.

**Performance Targets:**
*   Subcontractors **will** meet or exceed the average health and safety performance of the construction industry in the project's region.
*   Subcontractors **should** aim for a Workers’ Compensation Experience Modification Rate (EMR) of 1.0 or less for the last 3 years. This EMR **must be verified** by the workers’ compensation insurance provider via a letter on their official letterhead.
*   Subcontractors **should** have a Recordable Injury Frequency Rate (RIR) equal to or less than the national average.
*   Subcontractors **should** have a Lost Time Injury Frequency Rate (LTIR) equal to or less than the national average.

**Failure to Meet Criteria:**
If a subcontractor fails to meet these performance criteria, the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Safety Department **will** analyze other aspects of the subcontractor’s safety program. Qualification under such circumstances may necessitate monitoring of the subcontractor’s safety program on other current projects.
