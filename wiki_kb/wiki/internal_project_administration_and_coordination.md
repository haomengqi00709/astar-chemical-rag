# Internal Project Administration and Coordination

Effective internal administration and coordination are vital for project success. The Project Manager is responsible for preparing two key documents that govern these aspects:

**0-PRC-2002: Project Instructions**
This document details internal administrative procedures and guidelines specific to the project.

**0-PRC-2004: Project Co-ordination**
This document outlines the procedures for coordinating activities within the project team and managing communications with the Owner.

**Scope and Approval:**
Both [[project_instructions_0_prc_2002|0-PRC-2002]] and [[project_coordination_0_prc_2004|0-PRC-2004]] cover:
*   Internal administrative details.
*   Owner communications.

These documents **must** be checked by the Manager of Project Controls and subsequently approved by the Director of Projects.
