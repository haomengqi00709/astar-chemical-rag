# Instrument Bid Evaluation and Approval (0-PRC-5003)

The [[vendor_bid_evaluation_procedure_0_prc_5003]] (0-PRC-5003) outlines the process for evaluating and approving bids for both field instruments and instrument bulk materials. This involves preliminary and final cost tabulations, commercial, financial, technical evaluations, and risk assessments.

**Preliminary Cost Tabulation (0-TAB-8XXX-1):**
*   **For Field Instruments (< CAD 10k):** Prepared by FIE, Approved by FIE.
*   **For Field Instruments (< CAD 200k):** Prepared by FIE, Approved by Project Engineer.
*   **For Field Instruments (> CAD 200k):** Prepared by FIE, Approved by Project Engineer.
*   **For Instrument Bulk Material (< CAD 10k):** Prepared by DDE, Approved by DDE.
*   **For Instrument Bulk Material (< CAD 200k):** Prepared by DDE, Approved by Project Engineer.
*   **For Instrument Bulk Material (> CAD 200k):** Prepared by DDE, Approved by Project Engineer.

The FIE/DDE will provide the preliminary cost tabulation on 0-TAB-8XXX-1 for each requisition. They will tabulate all instruments/items with their respective Instrument Tag Number and show the cost. Project cost codes **are to be included** in the tabulation as per [[0-lst-4002_material_cost_codes]]. The FIE/DDE will verify any uncertain cost code assignments with Instrumentation prior to publication. If only technical information is provided, the FIE/DDE will tabulate individual instruments/items by Tag number and list appropriate cost codes, with the pricing portion completed by Instrumentation. Instrumentation will publish the preliminary cost tabulation on completion of pricing.

**Final Cost Tabulation (0-TAB-8XXX-1):**
*   **For Field Instruments:** Prepared by FIE, Approved by Project Manager.
*   **For Instrument Bulk Material:** Prepared by DDE, Approved by Project Manager.

The FIE/DDE will provide the final cost tabulation on 0-TAB-8XXX-1 for each requisition, tabulating all instruments/items with their respective Instrument Tag Number and cost. Project cost codes **are to be included** as per [[0-lst-4002_material_cost_codes]]. The FIE/DDE will verify any uncertain cost code assignments with Instrumentation prior to publication. If only technical information is provided, the FIE/DDE will tabulate by Tag number and list cost codes, with pricing completed by Instrumentation, who will then publish the final cost tabulation.

**Commercial Evaluation (0-TAB-8XXX-3):**
*   **For Field Instruments:** Prepared by Procurement, Approved by Manager of Procurement.
*   **For Instrument Bulk Material:** Prepared by Procurement, Approved by Manager of Procurement.

The Commercial Evaluation is the responsibility of the Project Buyer. To facilitate this, the FIE/DDE will propagate "0-TAB-8XXX-3: Commercial Bid Tabulation" with the appropriate Instrument Tag references/items. The FIE/DDE will inform the Project Buyer when the tabulation is ready. The FIE/DDE **need not** digitally sign "0-TAB-8XXX-3: Commercial Bid Tabulation".

**Financial Evaluation:**
*   **For Field Instruments:** Prepared by Finance.
*   **For Instrument Bulk Material:** Prepared by Finance.

Financial Evaluation is the responsibility of Finance. The FIE/DDE will confirm with the Project Engineer that the Financial Evaluation for the proposed supplier is complete and acceptable.

**Technical Evaluation (0-TAB-8XXX-2):**
*   **For Field Instruments:** Prepared by FIE, Checked by (As assigned by Project Engineer), Approved by FIE.
*   **For Instrument Bulk Material:** Prepared by DDE, Checked by (As assigned by Project Engineer), Approved by DDE.

The FIE/DDE will conduct a detailed technical evaluation of the bids, documented in "0-TAB-8XXX-2: Technical Bid Tabulation". Where required, the FIE/DDE will co-ordinate with various engineering disciplines. The FIE/DDE will complete the evaluation document and obtain digital signatures of the appropriate parties.

**Risk Assessment (0-TAB-8XXX-4):**
*   **For Field Instruments:** Prepared by FIE. Approved by Project Engineer (< CAD 200k), Approved by Project Manager (> CAD 200k).
*   **For Instrument Bulk Material:** Prepared by DDE. Approved by Project Engineer (< CAD 200k), Approved by Project Manager (> CAD 200k).

The FIE/DDE is responsible for initiating the Risk Assessment. This assessment **need only be completed** if the total purchase requisitions to a single supplier are greater than CAD 10k. The FIE/DDE will interface directly with the Project Engineer and/or the Project Manager to obtain the required approval signatures.

**Final Review and Vendor Selection:**
The FIE/DDE will advise the Instrument Engineer on completion of all bid evaluation tab forms. The Instrument Engineer will co-ordinate through the Project Engineer to meet with the appropriate parties to select the successful Vendor. The FIE/DDE will participate in this meeting as required.
