# 0-SPC-6071 Construction Subcontract Quality Assurance Specification

The document 0-SPC-6071, titled "Construction Subcontract Quality Assurance," is a crucial specification issued for the project to define the mandatory quality requirements for all works supplied by Construction Subcontractors. This specification works in conjunction with individual [[quality_data_sheets_qds]] (e.g., 0-QDS-6XYZZ) to establish a comprehensive quality framework that subcontractors **must** adhere to.

**Key Requirements and Scope:**
*   **Quality Requirements:** 0-SPC-6071, alongside the relevant Quality Data Sheets, explicitly sets out the quality requirements that subcontractors **must** meet for all aspects of their work. This includes, but is not limited to, materials, fabrication, installation, and testing.
*   **Subcontractor Quality Plan:** This specification mandates that the Subcontractor **must** prepare a [[subcontractor_quality_plan]]. This plan details how the subcontractor intends to manage and assure the quality of their works in accordance with the project's requirements.
*   **Surveillance Scope:** The scope of [[construction_surveillance_and_reporting]] activities conducted by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' Inspectors is partly defined by the requirements stipulated in 0-SPC-6071. This ensures that surveillance efforts are focused on verifying compliance with the specified quality standards.

**Document Issuance:**
The issuance of 0-SPC-6071 for the project follows the guidelines outlined in [[0_prc_0001_table_1_contents]], which governs the approval and use of standards within Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. This ensures that the specification is formally approved and distributed to all relevant parties, establishing it as a binding document for subcontractor quality control.

By clearly defining expectations and processes, 0-SPC-6071 plays a vital role in mitigating quality risks associated with subcontracted construction work, ensuring that all deliverables meet the stringent standards required for project success.

This specification is issued for the project in accordance with 0-PRC-0001: Approval and Use of Standards.
