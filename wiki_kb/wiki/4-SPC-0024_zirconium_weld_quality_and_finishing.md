# Zirconium Weld Quality and Finishing (4-SPC-0024)

This page outlines the mandatory requirements for weld quality and finishing of Zirconium (Grade 60702) fabrications as specified in document **4-SPC-0024**.

**General Weld Quality:**
*   Each weld **will** be uniform in width and size throughout its full length.
*   Each layer of welding **will** be smooth and free of slag, inclusions, cracks, porosity, lack of fusion, and undercut, except to the extent permitted in the referenced codes.
*   The cover pass **will** be free of coarse ripples, irregular surface, non-uniform bead patterns, high crown, and deep ridges or valleys between beads.
*   Buttwelds **will** be slightly convex, of uniform height, and have full penetration.
*   Crevices **will not** be permitted on the process side of completed weldments.

**Fillet Welds:**
*   As far as is practicable, fillet welds **will** be deposited in not less than two layers.
*   Fillet weld surfaces **will** be free from marked irregularities.
*   Dimensions of fillet welds **will** be in accordance with the approved drawing.

**Grinding and Cleaning Tools:**
*   Grinding or gouging **will not** be carried out in such a manner as to reduce the adjacent base metal thickness.
*   Grinding wheels **will** be iron-free and of the resin bonded alumina or silica/silicon type.
*   Wire brushes or chipping tools **will** be made of austenitic stainless steel.
*   Wire brushes or chipping tools **must not** have been previously used to clean surfaces other than Zirconium.
