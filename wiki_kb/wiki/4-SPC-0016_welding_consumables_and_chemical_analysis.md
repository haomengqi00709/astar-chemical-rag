# Welding Consumables and Chemical Analysis for Clad Steel

The selection, handling, and verification of welding consumables are paramount for maintaining the integrity of austenitic stainless clad steel fabrications. All filler materials **must** conform to the appropriate SFA Specification as referenced in [[4-SPC-0016_welding_standard_referenced_codes_and_standards]].

**Consumable Management**
The Fabricator **must** ensure and demonstrate to the Inspector that manufacturer’s instructions regarding the storage, drying, and use of weld consumables are fully implemented. All wet electrodes **must** be discarded and **not** baked and re-used. For typical stainless weld consumables, refer to Appendix A, which is detailed in [[4-SPC-0016_table_1_typical_stainless_weld_consumables]].

**Certification and Traceability**
Mill test certification, showing results from analysis and other tests in accordance with relevant material specifications, **must** be supplied to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for all materials, including welding consumables. Welding procedures (including weld overlay) **must** provide a chemical analysis of the cover pass on the clad side, and this analysis **must** satisfy the requirements of the [[ASME_section_ix_welding_and_brazing_qualifications]] code.

The Fabricator **must** maintain full traceability of materials back to original mill certification for all pressure-containing or major load-bearing elements, or materials exposed to the process environment. The Fabricator’s internal controls **must**, as a minimum, prevent the use of unspecified materials, including weld consumables. Lack of adequate identification or certification **will** be cause for rejection of the specific component or item.

The Supplier **must** make available to the purchaser's inspector copies of chemical certification for each batch of electrodes or wire coils used. If electrodes, wire, etc., cannot be identified to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' satisfaction, samples **will** be removed for chemical analysis at the Supplier’s account.

**Specific Consumable Requirements**
This document covers combinations for manual metallic arc welding of clad material or overlay welding on carbon steel. A table referenced as [[4-SPC-0016_table_2_manual_metallic_arc_welding_combinations]] is part of this document, outlining specific requirements.
*   A 25CR-20Ni (E-310) electrode **must not** be used under any circumstances.
*   Electrode selection other than those shown in [[4-SPC-0016_table_2_manual_metallic_arc_welding_combinations]] **must** be approved in writing by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
*   Weld deposits **must** be performed in not less than three (3) layers, as shown in [[4-SPC-0016_table_2_manual_metallic_arc_welding_combinations]].

**Production Chemical Analysis**
A single production check analysis **must** be performed on weld run-off tabs taken from each welding process employed. In special cases, interior welds **must** be given a final pass of type 904L SS weld filler, with a minimum coverage of 2.5 mm. This requirement for a final pass of 904L SS weld filler **will** only be carried out if shown on approved drawings, requested in the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Purchase Order, or specified in associated documentation.

**Electrode Restrictions and Approval**
A 25CR-20Ni (E-310) electrode **must not** be used under any circumstances. Electrode selection other than those shown in [[4-SPC-0016_table_2_manual_metallic_arc_welding_combinations]] **must** be approved in writing by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Iron Content in Alloy Clad Welding**
Where iron content in alloy clad welding is of consequence (e.g., with Alloy 20 Cb3), maximum percentage pickup in the last pass **must** be provided by the fabricator prior to fabrication.
