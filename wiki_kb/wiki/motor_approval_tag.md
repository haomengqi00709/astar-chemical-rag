# Motor Approval Tags

Motor Approval Tags are documentation elements associated with the installation and readiness of electric motors within a plant. These tags serve as a record that a motor has undergone the necessary inspections and checks, confirming its suitability for operation.

As part of the [[9-PRC-0020_electrical_systems_contractor_checkout_procedure|Electrical Systems Contractor Checkout Procedure 9-PRC-0020]] and the broader [[9-PRC-0004_electrical_field_checkout_documentation|Electrical Field Checkout Procedure (9-PRC-0004)]], Motor Approval Tags indicate that the motor's installation, wiring, and associated components have been verified against engineering documents and checklists. This includes checks on:
*   Physical installation and alignment.
*   Electrical connections and grounding.
*   Lubrication and cooling systems.
*   Compliance with safety standards.

The presence of an approved tag signifies that the motor is ready for subsequent testing phases, such as [[water_batching]] and [[commissioning_and_performance_testing]].
