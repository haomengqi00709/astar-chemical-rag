# Risk Allocation and Indemnities

This page outlines the allocation of risks and the indemnification responsibilities between parties in project contracts, as detailed in Purchase Order [[0-SPC-5001_purchase_order_overview|0-SPC-5001]].

**Vendor's Risks**
From the [[0-SPC-5001_purchase_order_overview#Starting Date|Starting Date]] until the [[0-SPC-5001_purchase_order_overview#Defects Date|Defects Date]], the Vendor **shall** carry all risks that are not explicitly carried by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. During this period, and unless otherwise instructed by the [[project_buyer_roles|Project Buyer]], the Vendor **must** promptly replace any loss of and repair any damage to the [[goods_and_services_definitions|Goods and Services]].

**Aker Kvaerner Chemetics's Risks**
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics's risks include:
*   Claims, proceedings, compensation, and costs payable due to negligence, breach of statutory duty, or interference with any legal right by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or by any person employed by them.
*   Claims, proceedings, compensation, and costs payable due to a fault of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or a fault in their design.
*   Loss of or damage to the parts of the Goods and Services after Delivery, with specific exceptions:
    *   Loss or damage occurring before the Defects Date which is due to a [[0-SPC-5001_purchase_order_overview#Defect|Defect]] that existed at Delivery.
    *   Loss or damage occurring before the Defects Date which is due to an event occurring before Delivery that was not itself an Aker Kvaerner Chemetics risk.
    *   Loss or damage occurring before the Defects Date which is due to the activities of the Vendor after Delivery.

**Mutual Indemnification**
Each Party **shall** indemnify the other against claims, proceedings, compensation, and costs due to an event that is at their respective risk.

**Reduction in Liability**
The liability of each Party to indemnify the other **shall** be reduced if events at the other Party's risk contributed to the claims, proceedings, compensation, and costs. This reduction **shall** be proportional to the extent that events which were at the other Party's risk contributed, taking into account each Party's responsibilities under this Purchase Order. This ensures a fair allocation of responsibility based on contribution to the event.
