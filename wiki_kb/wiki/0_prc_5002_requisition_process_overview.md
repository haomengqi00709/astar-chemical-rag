# Requisition Process Overview (0-PRC-5002)

This procedure, 0-PRC-5002, outlines the essential steps required for the issuance of a Requisition. A Requisition serves as the initiating document for either a Request for Quotation (RFQ) or a Purchase Order (PO).

The requisitioner **must** indicate on the Requisition form whether it is intended for an RFQ or a PO. The Requisition itself will be prepared using the [[0_req_template_requisition]] (0-REQ-).

The identification number assigned to a Requisition **must** be identical to the number allocated on the [[project_planning_0_prc_3001|Procurement Plan]] (0-PRC-3001) for the subsequent Purchase Order. Requisitions for an RFQ and their subsequent Requisitions for a PO will share the same document number, differing only in their revision numbers.

Once prepared, the requisitioner **shall** publish the Requisition in accordance with the [[publishing_project_documents]] procedure (0-PRC-0005). Following publication, the responsible [[assignment_of_project_responsibilities|Discipline Approver]] (0-PRC-2001) **must** approve the Requisition. The Discipline Lead will then inform Procurement that the Requisition has been published and approved.

Upon receiving this notification, Procurement will proceed with the issuance of the Request for Quotation or the Purchase Order, as detailed in [[issue_of_request_for_quotation_or_purchase_order_0_prc_5005]] (0-PRC-5005).

The approval of a Requisition (as discussed in Section 6 of 0-PRC-5002) is intended to ensure that the text is technically correct and that the reference documents provide a complete technical description of both the Scope of Work and the Technical Documentation.
