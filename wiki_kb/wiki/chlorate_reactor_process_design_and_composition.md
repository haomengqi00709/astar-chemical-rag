# Chlorate Reactor Process Design and Composition

This page outlines the critical process design conditions and detailed chemical compositions for the Chlorate Reactor, ensuring safe and efficient operation according to specified parameters.

**Process Design Conditions**
The reactor is designed for specific operational scenarios and based on established engineering principles, including the overall mass and energy balance.
*   **Case Description**: Normal Operation with °C Cell Room
*   **Design Basis**: ClO2 M&E Balance TPD out and [[1-prc-0002_flowsheet_development_and_approval|PFD's]]
*   **Normal Operating Temperature**: 95.0 [[chlorate_reactor_parameter_units|oC]]
*   **Pressure Notes**:
    *   Note 2 applies to [[chlorate_reactor_parameter_units|kPa (g)]]
    *   Note 3 applies to [[chlorate_reactor_parameter_units|kPa (g)]]
*   **Mandatory Requirement**: Note 2.0: An interlock **MUST** limit pressure to 0 kPag for maloperation of the plant during a cell drain case. This is a critical safety interlock.
*   **Note 3.0**: psig and 10% RD tolerance max.
*   **Working Volume Criteria**: The working volume is defined as the volume from the bottom of the tank to the invert of the liquid overflow. This criterion defines the effective volume for process calculations.

**Major Component Compositions**
Detailed compositions of various streams entering, exiting, and circulating within the reactor are provided below. All percentages are on a weight/weight (%w/w) or volume/volume (%v/v) basis as specified, and are crucial for maintaining the chemical balance and performance of the chlorate reactor.
*   **Liquid In (Weak Chlorate)**:
    *   Type: Weak chlorate
    *   Description: %w/w NaClO3; NaCl; Na2Cr2O7; H2O
*   **Liquid Out (Strong Chlorate)**:
    *   Type: Strong chlorate
    *   Description: %w/w NaClO3; NaCl; Na2Cr2O7; H2O
*   **Gas Out (Cell Gas)**:
    *   Type: Cell Gas
    *   Description: %v/v Cl2; H2; O2; H2O
*   **Circulation In (Cell Chlorate)**:
    *   Type: Cell Chlorate
    *   Description: %w/w Similar composition to CIRC OUT with trace dissolved H2
*   **Circulation Out (Mixed Chlorate)**:
    *   Type: Mixed Chlorate
    *   Description: %w/w NaClO3; NaCl; Na2Cr2O7; H2O
*   **Liquid In (2) (HCl)**:
    *   Type: HCl
    *   Description: %w/w HCl; H2O
*   **Liquid In (3) (Brine)**:
    *   Type: Brine
    *   Description: %w/w NaCl; H2O
*   **Gas In**:
    *   Description: %v/v

These detailed specifications are fundamental for process modeling, control system design, and ensuring the final product quality.
