# Polarization Rectifier (CPU) Specifications

This page details the specific requirements and design parameters for the Polarization Rectifier, also referred to as the Central Processing Unit (CPU), as specified in Document ID 4-PDS-XXXX-R1. The CPU plays a critical role in maintaining optimal electrochemical cell performance.

**CPU Requirement:**
*   **C.P.U Required (Note 4):** This parameter indicates whether a CPU is required for the specific application, with options being "Yes" or "No".

**CPU Design Basis Parameters:**
The following parameters define the design and operational characteristics of the CPU:
*   **Quantity C.P.U.:** Specifies the number of Polarization Rectifier units required.
*   **CPU Design Current:** A (Amperes)
*   **CPU Design Circuit Voltage:** VDC (Volts Direct Current)
*   **CPU Design Circuit Voltage - cells bypassed:** VDC (Volts Direct Current)
*   **CPU Current density:** KA/m2 (Kiloamperes per square meter)

**Terminology Note:**
*   **Note 4.0:** C.P.U. is also referred to as a "Polarization Rectifier."
