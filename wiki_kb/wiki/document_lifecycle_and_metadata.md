# Document Lifecycle and Metadata

This page describes the lifecycle stages and associated metadata for project documents, particularly focusing on their primary usage throughout the project.

**Primary Usage Categories**
Documents are categorized by their primary usage, which indicates the main phase of the project during which they are most relevant:
*   **Design:** Documents used during the engineering and design phase, such as [[electrical_design_criteria]], [[electrical_equipment_data_sheets]], [[electrical_equipment_specifications]], [[electrical_calculations]], and various drawing types.
*   **Procurement:** Documents central to the purchasing process, including [[electrical_procurement_process]] forms, [[electrical_bid_evaluation]] documents, [[cost_tabulation_procurement]], [[order_risk_assessment]], and [[electrical_purchase_orders]].
*   **Construction:** Documents primarily used during the construction and installation phase, such as [[electrical_equipment_manuals]], [[electrical_installation_specification]], [[below_grade_grounding_specification]], [[electrical_field_check_lists]], and [[as_built_documentation]].

**Document Identification and Roles**
Each document is assigned a unique Document ID. Additionally, metadata includes the roles responsible for preparing, checking, and approving the document, ensuring accountability and traceability.
