# Heat Treatment Requirements for High Silicon Austenitic Stainless Steel (4-SPC-0025)

**Furnace Heat Treatment**
When required by the Purchase Order or approved fabrication drawing, furnace heat treatment **shall** be performed between 1100°C - 1150°C. This heat treatment **shall** be followed by rapid water quenching.

The entire heat treatment operation **shall** be covered by a written procedure. This procedure **must** outline the method(s) for controlling distortion, particularly during the critical cooling cycle.

**Solution Treatment for Dished Heads**
All dished heads **shall** be solution treated and water quenched after forming. Hot forming of dished heads **shall not** be performed unless specifically agreed upon by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. After forming and final heat treatment on dished heads, the severely deformed knuckle areas **shall** be dye penetrant inspected both internally and externally around the entire circumference.

Distortion from solution annealing and water quenching **shall** be minimized at all times. Tolerances after heat treatment **shall** comply with the requirements detailed in [[4-SPC-0025_fabrication_tolerances]].
