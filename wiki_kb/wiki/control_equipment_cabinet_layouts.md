# Control Equipment-Cabinet Layouts and Internal Interconnection Wiring

**Control Equipment-Cabinet Layouts and Internal Interconnection Wiring** drawings provide detailed information for the physical arrangement and internal wiring of control equipment within designated cabinets.

**Typical Document Number:** No typical document number
**Usage:** Construction

**Content Requirements:**
These drawings **WILL CONTAIN**:
*   Layout of control equipment in designated cabinets.
*   Control equipment tag and dimension.
*   Field devices interface, service, and type (Analog, Discrete, Digital).
*   Detailed internal Interconnections in schematic and/or wiring format.
*   Hardware configuration settings.
*   Card location layouts.
*   Material takeoff lists.

Specifically, these drawings **WILL SHOW** the special layout for Control Equipment within their designated cabinets. All equipment **WILL BE IDENTIFIED**, **TAGGED**, and **DIMENSIONED**. The drawings **WILL IDENTIFY** where field devices will interface and **WILL INDICATE** the service and type (Analog, Discrete, Digital) of these field device interfaces. Detailed internal interconnections **WILL BE SHOWN** in schematic and/or wiring format. All interface communication connections **WILL BE SHOWN**, and responsibility for the supply of all communications cabling **WILL BE CLEARLY DEFINED**.
