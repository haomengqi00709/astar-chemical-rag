# Rotameter Checkout

This procedure details the specific checkout requirements for Rotameters. These checks supplement the [[instrument_checkout_general_guidelines]] and ensure proper installation and functionality before commissioning.

**Required Documents**
Documents specific to Rotameters include:
*   [[piping_and_instrumentation_drawings]] (P&ID)
*   [[8-lst-0002_instrument_index]] (Instrument Index)
*   [[8-dst-xxxx_instrument_specification_sheets]] (Instrument Specification Sheet)

**Installation and Mechanical Checks**
*   **Nameplate Verification:** Nameplate data and record **must** match the Instrument Specification Sheet information. The Nameplate tag **must** match the Instrument Tag Number.
*   **Impulse Tubed:** N/A
*   **Inst Air Tubed:** N/A
*   **Packing Material:** Packing material **must** be removed from the float.
*   **Flow Tube Direction:** The flow tube **must** be installed with the correct direction of flow.

**Functional Checks**
*   **Field Term Chk:** N/A
*   **DCS Term Chk:** N/A
*   **Fail Action:** N/A
*   **Field Fail Action Checked:** N/A
*   **Range:** The range of the rotameter **must** be in accordance with the [[8-dst-xxxx_instrument_specification_sheets]].
*   **Calibration Check:** N/A
*   **DCS Check:** N/A

**Sign-off**
*   **P&I Ref:** Refers to the [[piping_and_instrumentation_drawings]] where the instrument may be located.
*   **Comments:** General comments during commissioning.
