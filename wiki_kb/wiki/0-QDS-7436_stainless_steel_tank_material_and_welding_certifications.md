# Stainless Steel Tank Material and Welding Certifications (0-QDS-7436)

For the Stainless Steel Tank project (Document ID: 0-QDS-7436), specific requirements are in place for material and welding certifications to ensure compliance with design specifications and quality standards.

**Mill Test Certificates:**
*   **Requirement:** Mill Test Certificates are required to be included in the Vendor's Inspection and Test Plan (I & T Plan) and **must** be part of the [[0-QDS-7436_quality_record_definitions|Quality Assurance Documentation Package (FP)]].
*   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Chemetics requires "Witness" surveillance for Mill Test Certificates. This means a Chemetics representative **must** be present to verify the certificates during the manufacturing process or at a designated review point.

These certifications are critical for verifying the metallurgical properties and quality of the materials used in the fabrication of the Stainless Steel Tank.

*   **Quality Record Requirement:** Mill Test Certificates are designated as "Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package" (FP) within the Vendor's Inspection and Test Plan.
