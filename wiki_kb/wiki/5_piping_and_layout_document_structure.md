# Piping and Layout Document Structure (Major Work Subject 5)

The "5 Piping and Layout" major work subject is dedicated to all documentation related to the design, stress analysis, layout, and installation of piping systems within the plant. This folder is essential for managing the complex network of pipes that transport process fluids.

The sub-folders and their contents within the 5-Piping-and-Layout folder are:

*   **CAL:** Pipe Sizing / Stress Calculations. Calculations for determining optimal pipe sizes and analyzing piping stress.
*   **DAT:** PDMS Model. Data files related to the Plant Design Management System (PDMS) 3D model.
*   **DST:** Pipe Class Sheets. Documents defining the specifications for different piping classes, including materials, ratings, and components.
*   **DTL:** Spool Drawings. Detailed fabrication drawings for piping spools.
*   **GAD:** Piping Plans, Elevations, Perspectives. Drawings showing the overall layout of piping systems, including various views.
*   **ISO:** Piping Isometrics. Isometric drawings providing detailed information for piping fabrication and erection.
*   **LST:** Line List, Valve Lists, Spare Parts Lists. Comprehensive lists of all pipelines, valves, and associated spare parts. Data available in SQL: LST files.
*   **PRC:** Project Specific Procedures. Procedures tailored to piping design, fabrication, and installation.
*   **PSI:** Piping Specialty Items. Documentation for specialized piping components.
*   **REQ:** Requisitions. Documents for requesting bids or proposals for piping materials and services.
*   **SUP:** Piping Support Details. Detailed drawings and specifications for piping supports.
*   **SPC:** Specifications. Technical specifications for piping materials, fabrication, welding, and installation.
*   **TAB:** Bid Tabulations. Records of bids received for piping materials and services.
*   **VDR:** Vendor Document Requirements. Documents outlining the requirements for vendor-supplied piping documentation.
*   **VEN:** Vendors Documents. All documentation provided by piping vendors, such as material certificates and component drawings.

This structure ensures that all piping and layout documents, from design calculations to vendor submissions, are systematically organized and easily retrievable.
