# Acid Plant Packing Support Requirements

Packing supports are a critical component in the design of sulphuric acid plant drying and absorption towers, ensuring the stability and efficiency of the packed bed. The requirements for these supports are detailed in [[4-spc-0012_acid_resistant_linings_specification_overview]].

**General Requirements**
Domed packing supports **are required** in both drying and absorption towers. The surfaces on which the sidewall skewblock sit **must** be level to ensure proper installation and support.

**Preferred Supplier and Alternatives**
Steuler Industriewerke Gmbh is the preferred supplier for packing supports. However, other packing support suppliers and types **will** be considered under specific conditions:
*   The lining supplier **must** be able to prove considerable acid plant experience with the alternative supplier's products.
*   The lining supplier **must** provide demonstrative technical literature and data for evaluation.

**Minimum Information for Evaluation**
To facilitate the evaluation of alternative packing supports, the vendor **must** provide minimum information including:
*   Manufacturer's name
*   Percentage free area
*   Geometry
*   Ceramic details

**Packed Bed Stability**
If a stepped dome packing support is not offered by the vendor, they **must** comment in their proposal on how the stability of the packed bed **will** be maintained. This ensures that alternative designs adequately address the structural integrity of the packing.

**Construction Materials**
Packing supports **must** be constructed of fireclay, porcelain, or stoneware acid brick only, ensuring their resistance to the corrosive environment of the acid plant.

For more information on tower internals and packing specifications, refer to [[tower_internals_packing_specifications]] and [[packing_details]].
