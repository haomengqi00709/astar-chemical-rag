# Inspection and Non-Destructive Examination for Nickel/Molybdenum/Chromium Alloys

This page outlines the comprehensive inspection and non-destructive examination (NDE) requirements for welded fabrications in nickel/molybdenum/chromium alloys.

**Inspector Access and Authority**
*   The Inspector **shall** have free access to the Vendor's work and to his Subvendor's (if work is sublet by approval) at all reasonable times.
*   Access **shall** be given to Authorized Representatives of the End-User.
*   The Inspector **shall** be at liberty to inspect the work during fabrication at any stage in manufacture.
*   The Inspector **shall** be at liberty to reject any material or workmanship which does not conform to this Standard or the applicable code.
*   Inspection by [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_company_details]] does not relieve the fabricator or erector of responsibility in accordance with the requirements of this Standard, engineering design and the applicable code.

**Chemical Analysis**
*   Supplier **must** make available chemical certification for each batch of electrodes or wire coils used.
*   If electrodes, wire, etc., cannot be identified to the Inspector's satisfaction, samples **shall** be removed for chemical analysis.
*   Chemical analysis for unidentified materials **shall** be to supplier's account.
*   A spot check analysis **shall** be performed on production welds of critical equipment (pressure vessels, heat exchangers, pressure piping, proprietary items) when required by purchase order or if the fabricator cannot identify the weld material.
*   Spot check analysis **shall** also include non-pressure retaining equipment in dangerous or demanding services.
*   Welds for spot check analysis **shall** normally be selected at random by the Inspector.
*   If chemical analysis shows non-conformance, the supplier **shall** promptly advise [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_company_details]] in writing of his proposed course of action.
*   Proposed action for non-conformance **shall** include details of any additional testing necessary to fully define the extent of the non-conformance.
*   All repairs and retests for non-conformance **shall** be to the supplier's account.

**Penetrant Testing (PT)**
*   Items fabricated in accordance with [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels]] Code Section VIII, Div. 1, **shall** be PT tested on all welds.
*   Non-coded items **shall** have butt and fillet welds, nozzle attachment welds, etc., 100% PT examined on all surfaces exposed to the process for cracks, porosity, slag inclusions, significant undercutting or other major defects, unless otherwise specified on the purchase order, approved drawings or Standards.
*   [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_company_details]] reserves the right to select the location of PT inspection.
*   All welds between dissimilar metals **shall** be 100% PT examined, unless otherwise specified in the Purchase Order or approved drawings.
*   Tube to tubesheet welds **shall** be 100% PT examined.
*   The PT exam **shall** be carried out in accordance with a written procedure.
*   The limit of PT acceptability **shall** satisfy [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels]] Code Section VIII, Division 1, Appendix VIII.
*   Linear indications **shall** be considered unacceptable for PT exams.
*   All excavated areas **shall** be PT examined prior to building up flush with the surface of the base material.
*   Build-up of excavated areas **shall** use the welding procedure previously approved by [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_company_details]] for the main welding details.
*   All weld repair surfaces **shall** be 100% PT examined per the above requirements.
*   [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_company_details]] reserves the right to accept or reject any doubtful or questionable PT indications.

**Radiographic Testing (RT)**
*   Equipment fabricated to [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels]] Code **shall** have all butt welds 100% radiographed per Para. UW-51 of [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels]] Code Section VIII, Division 1, where plate or shell thickness at the welded joint(s) exceeds 3/8".
*   Equipment fabricated to [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels]] Code, but not exceeding 3/8" plate thickness, **shall** be spot radiographed per Para. UW-52(b) of [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels]] Code Section VIII, Division 1.
*   Spot radiography **shall** be the minimum requirement for equipment.
*   For piping fabricated in accordance with [[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping]] Codes and this Standard, the minimum standard of radiography **shall** be random radiographed as defined in Para. 341.4.1 (b) of [[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping]].
*   For piping fabricated in accordance with [[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping]] Codes and this Standard, 20% of all butt welds **shall** be radiographed.
*   For tankage fabricated in accordance with [[API_620_low_pressure_storage_tanks]] Standard 620 and this Specification, the minimum requirements **shall** be spot radiography.
*   Items other than those described above, fabricated to this Standard only, **shall** be spot radiographed in accordance with UW-52 (b) if required by [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_company_details]] engineering drawings.
*   Items other than those described above, fabricated to this Standard only, **shall** be 20% random radiographed to B31.3 in case of piping, if required by [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_company_details]] engineering drawings.
*   RT Exam **shall** be carried out in accordance with a written procedure.
*   RT Exam personnel **shall** be qualified to SNTC-1A.
*   Where additional spot radiography is required, as defined in UW-52 Sub-para. (d), costs of this radiography, including all repair costs and re-radiography, **shall** be to the fabricator's account.
*   Junctions between circumferential and longitudinal seams **shall** be considered as preferred locations for spot radiography.

**Helium Mass Spectrometer Testing**
*   When required by purchase order or referenced drawings, tube/tube sheet joints and tube bundles **shall** be tested using the helium mass spectrometer (reverse probe method) per [[ASME_section_v_non_destructive_examination]] Code Section V, Article 10.

Spot check analysis shall also include non-pressure retaining equipment in dangerous or demanding services. Welds for spot check analysis shall normally be selected at random by the Inspector.

Proposed action for non-conformance shall include details of any additional testing necessary to fully define the extent of the non-conformance. All repairs and retests for non-conformance shall be to the supplier's account.

**Liquid Penetrant (PT) Examination**
*   Items fabricated in accordance with ASME Code Section VIII, Div. 1, shall be PT tested on all welds.
*   Non-coded items shall have butt and fillet welds, nozzle attachment welds, etc., 100% PT examined on all surfaces exposed to the process for cracks, porosity, slag inclusions, significant undercutting or other major defects, unless otherwise specified on the purchase order, approved drawings or Standards.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics reserves the right to select the location of PT inspection.
*   All welds between dissimilar metals shall be 100% PT examined, unless otherwise specified in the Purchase Order or approved drawings. Tube to tubesheet welds shall be 100% PT examined.
*   The PT exam shall be carried out in accordance with a written procedure.
*   The limit of PT acceptability shall satisfy ASME Code Section VIII, Division 1, Appendix VIII. Linear indications shall be considered unacceptable for PT exams.
*   All excavated areas shall be PT examined prior to building up flush with the surface of the base material. Build-up of excavated areas shall use the welding procedure previously approved by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for the main welding details. All weld repair surfaces shall be 100% PT examined per the above requirements.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics reserves the right to accept or reject any doubtful or questionable PT indications.

**Radiographic Testing (RT) Examination**
*   Equipment fabricated to ASME Code shall have all butt welds 100% radiographed per Para. UW-51 of ASME Code Section VIII, Division 1, where plate or shell thickness at the welded joint(s) exceeds 3/8".
*   Equipment fabricated to ASME Code, but not exceeding 3/8" plate thickness, shall be spot radiographed per Para. UW-52(b) of ASME Code Section VIII, Division 1. Spot radiography shall be the minimum requirement for equipment.
*   The item is not fabricated to the ASME Code.
*   For piping fabricated in accordance with ASME Codes and this Standard, the minimum standard of radiography shall be random radiographed as defined in Para. 341.4.1 (b) of ASME B31.3, meaning 20% of all butt welds shall be radiographed.
*   For tankage fabricated in accordance with API Standard 620 and this Specification, the minimum requirements shall be spot radiography.
*   Items other than those described above, fabricated to this Standard only, shall be spot radiographed in accordance with UW-52 (b) if required by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics engineering drawings.
*   Items other than those described above, fabricated to this Standard only, shall be 20% random radiographed to B31.3 in case of piping, if required by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics engineering drawings.
*   RT Exam shall be carried out in accordance with a written procedure. RT Exam personnel shall be qualified to SNTC-1A.
*   Where additional spot radiography is required, as defined in UW-52 Sub-para. (d), costs of this radiography, including all repair costs and re-radiography, shall be to the fabricator's account.
*   Junctions between circumferential and longitudinal seams shall be considered as preferred locations for spot radiography.

**Helium Mass Spectrometer Testing**
When required by purchase order or referenced drawings, tube/tube sheet joints and tube bundles shall be tested using the helium mass spectrometer (reverse probe method) per ASME Code Section V, Article 10.
