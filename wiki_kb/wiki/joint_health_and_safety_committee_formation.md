# Joint Health and Safety Committee Formation

The establishment of a Joint Health and Safety Committee (JHSC) is a critical component of the company's commitment to [[workplace_health_and_environmental_practices]] and employee well-being. The formation criteria are as follows:

**Mandatory Formation Requirements:**
*   A JHSC **must** be established at any work site where 10 or more workers are regularly employed.
*   A JHSC **must** be established in any workplace where a joint committee is required by local law or regulation. This ensures adherence to [[compliance_and_standards]].
*   A JHSC **must** be established in any workplace where a joint committee is specifically required by the [[hse_personnel_definitions_and_responsibilities|Company HSE Manager]].

**Recommendations for Smaller Workplaces:**
*   For work sites with fewer than 10 employees, it is recommended that at least one employee participates in the owner’s or prime contractor’s existing health and safety committee. This ensures that even smaller teams have a voice in safety matters.
*   The formation of a Joint Health and Safety Committee is highly recommended at all workplaces, regardless of size, to proactively manage risks and promote a safety-conscious culture.

These requirements and recommendations underscore the company's dedication to fostering a collaborative approach to [[hse_management_and_employee_responsibilities|health and safety management]] across all operations.
