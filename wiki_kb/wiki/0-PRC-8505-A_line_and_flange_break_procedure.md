# Line and Flange Break Procedure Overview and Administration

This procedure, documented as 0-PRC-8505, establishes the minimum guidelines for safely opening lines and process equipment that contain or have contained process chemicals potentially hazardous to personnel and/or the environment.

**Purpose and Scope**
The primary purpose is to ensure the safety of all personnel involved in opening process lines and equipment. This regulation **shall** be followed by the Commissioning Team, which includes Aker Kvaerner Chemetics personnel, Contractors, and Vendor representatives. It applies after a circuit, any system, or equipment is handed over as mechanically completed by the Construction Manager to the Commissioning Manager for commissioning activities. A Client’s regulation may be followed if its requirements and guidelines fully cover those specified in Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' procedures.

**Basis and Applicability**
This procedure is based on Aker Kvaerner’s Line and Flange Break procedure issued in January of 2003, which is found in AK’s [[aker_kvaerner_safety_policy_manual|Safety Policy Manual]] section 7, item 7.25. It applies to all personnel who may be required to perform a line break or equipment opening.

**Definitions**
"Line Breaking/Equipment Opening" refers to any job required to open any process line or equipment. This includes, but is not limited to, permanent and/or temporary bypasses, connected fittings, valves, pumps, vessels, or tanks.

**General Hazards**
Hazardous conditions may exist in process lines or equipment that contain or have contained acids, caustics, flammables, or any other hazardous materials in the form of gases, liquids, or solids. Hot materials such as heated water and steam also present a significant hazard. The hazard assessment created for the task **must** be understood by all members of the team involved.

**Roles and Responsibilities**
*   **Commissioning HSE Manager:**
    *   Is responsible for the administration of the Line Breaking and Equipment Opening Permit system.
    *   Is responsible for obtaining permits from the client issuing authority.
    *   **Shall** establish and maintain a program that adequately informs Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ personnel of the existence, purpose, and procedures of the Line and Flange Break system.
    *   **Must** sign a valid permit for this program, along with the Work Supervisor.
*   **Commissioning Manager:**
    *   In case the Commissioning HSE Manager is temporarily away from site, the Commissioning Manager will take over the administration of the Hot Work Permit system.
*   **Work Supervisor:**
    *   **Must** sign a valid permit for this program, along with the Commissioning HSE Manager.

**Training Requirements**
*   Each employee who is authorized to perform first breaks **shall** be thoroughly trained in all aspects of these guidelines.
*   Everyone **must** go through Line Breaking Training provided by the Commissioning HSE Manager.
*   Training can be conducted on Site or in the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics office.
*   Training **shall** be documented and entered into the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics log book that the Commissioning HSE Manager keeps.

**Incident Reporting**
All Line Breaking/Equipment Opening incidents and near misses **shall** be thoroughly investigated and documented. For general incident reporting procedures, refer to [[0-PRC-8013_hse_incident_reporting]].
