# Scrubber Venturi Internals Packing and Shipping Requirements

This page details the requirements for the preparation, packing, and shipping of the Scrubber Venturi Internals, ensuring they are adequately protected during transit and that all necessary documentation accompanies the shipment.

**Preparation for Shipping:**
*   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Complete. Chemetics **will** perform a complete surveillance of the preparation for shipping process, ensuring all necessary protective measures are in place.

**Packing List:**
*   **Requirement:** Packing List **is required** as [[0-QDS-7439_quality_record_definitions|Quality Record AP]] (As Prepared).
*   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Complete. Chemetics **will** perform a complete surveillance of the Packing List, verifying its accuracy and completeness.

These requirements are essential to prevent damage to the Scrubber Venturi Internals during transportation and to ensure that all components are correctly identified and accounted for upon arrival at the destination.
