# Conveyor Material Specifications (4-PDS-XXXX-R1)

This page specifies the materials of construction for various components of the conveyor system, as required by datasheet 4-PDS-XXXX-R1. The selection of appropriate materials is critical to ensure durability, corrosion resistance, and compatibility with the conveyed solid material and operating environment.

**Component-Specific Material Requirements:**
The material of construction **must** be specified for each of the following conveyor components:
*   **Screw:** (Applicable for Screw conveyors)
*   **Trough:** (Applicable for Screw, Drag conveyors)
*   **Cover:** (Applicable for various conveyor types requiring enclosure)
*   **Buckets:** (Applicable for Bucket conveyors)
*   **Casing:** (Applicable for various conveyor types, especially enclosed ones)
*   **Belt:** (Applicable for Belt conveyors)
*   **Bearing:** (For all conveyor types with rotating parts)
*   **Flights:** (Applicable for Screw, Drag conveyors)
*   **Surge Hopper:** (If part of the conveyor system)
*   **Inlet Chute:** (For material entry)
*   **Fluidizer Vessel:** (Applicable for Pneumatic conveyors)
*   **Conveying Line:** (Applicable for Pneumatic conveyors)
*   **Other:** (Any other specific components requiring material specification)

The material selection should consider the [[4-PDS-XXXX-R1_conveyor_solid_material_characteristics]] and the [[4-PDS-XXXX-R1_conveyor_process_design_conditions]]. For general material selection guidelines, refer to [[material_selection]].
