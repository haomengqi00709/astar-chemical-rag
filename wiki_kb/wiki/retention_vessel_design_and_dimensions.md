# Retention Vessel Design and Dimensions

This page details the design parameters and physical dimensions for the Retention Vessel, as calculated and documented in [[1-CAL-6510-R0_quench_venturi_and_retention_vessel_calculation_document_details]]. These specifications are crucial for the vessel's fabrication and integration into the process.

**Design Parameters:**
*   **Gas Residence time 1:** 3.0 sec (used when arsenic is present).
    *   Residence time **should** be between 1.8 - 4.9 seconds.
    *   Default residence time **should** be 3 seconds.
*   **Gas Volume for Residence Time:** 7791.622063206699 ft³ (220.63616297664836 m³)
*   **Gas Velocity 2 (Retention Vessel):** 10.0 ft/s (3.0480091927957256 m/s).
    *   Recommended velocity is 10 ft/s.
*   **Cone angle (Retention Vessel):** 45.0 degrees
*   **Cone volume (Retention Vessel):** 21.778436572541747 m³
*   **Straight wall height** is determined using provided data.
*   **Diameter of vessel** is determined using provided data.
