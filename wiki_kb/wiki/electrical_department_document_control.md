# Electrical Department Document Control and Authorization

The Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Electrical department maintains strict control over the numbering and usage of specific document types to ensure accuracy, consistency, and adherence to project standards.

**Controlled Document Types:**
The following document types, along with their assigned numbers, are under the direct control of the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Electrical department:
*   **PRC** (Procedures)
*   **SPC** (Specifications)
*   **DST** (Data Sheets)

These document numbers **shall not** be used without the explicit authorization of the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Electrical department manager. This mandatory requirement ensures that all critical electrical procedures, specifications, and data sheets are properly managed and approved before implementation.

**Drawing Number Range Changes:**
For other types of electrical drawings, including DTL (Detail Drawings), GAD (General Arrangement Drawings), LST (Lists), SCM (Schematics), MAN (Manuals), and CAL (Calculations), the numbers are typically used on a project-specific basis. However, any proposed changes to the established ranges of these drawing numbers **shall** require the authorization of the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Electrical department manager. This control prevents unauthorized alterations to the numbering system and maintains the integrity of project documentation.

This stringent document control process is an essential part of the overall [[9-PRC-0021_electrical_numbering_and_identification_procedure|electrical numbering and identification framework]], ensuring that all electrical documentation is managed effectively and securely.
