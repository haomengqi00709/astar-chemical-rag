# Construction Subcontractor Document Delivery Methods (0-SPC-6091)

This page specifies the approved methods for the Construction Subcontractor to deliver documents to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, as per document 0-SPC-6091.

Documents **must** be delivered via one of the following methods:

*   **Email**:
    *   Address: chemetics.doccontrol@akerkvaerner.com

*   **File Transfer**:
    *   As described in [[0-SPC-6003_construction_subcontractor_coordination]].

*   **Courier**:
    *   Address: Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, 1818 Cornwall Avenue, Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada
    *   Attention: Document Control Department
    *   Telephone: 604-734-1200

For general information on document control, refer to [[document_control_procedure_0_prc_0007]].
