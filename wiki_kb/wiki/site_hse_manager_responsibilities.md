# Site Health and Safety Manager Responsibilities

The full-time Site Health and Safety Manager plays a critical role in ensuring a safe and healthy work environment on Aker Kvaerner Chemetics EPC project sites. Their responsibilities encompass policy adherence, training, incident management, and proactive safety promotion.

**Core Responsibilities:**

*   **Policy and Procedure Adherence:**
    *   **Must** read, understand, and carry out the general requirements of Aker Kvaerner Health and Safety procedures, as defined in Standard-0-PRC-8000 [[0-prc-8000_administration_of_hse]].
    *   **Must** read, understand, and carry out the general requirements of the project HSE plan, defined in Project-0-PRC-8405 [[project-0-prc-8405_project_hse_plan]].
    *   **Must** ensure all site employees are familiar with the company's HSE Policy (Standard-0-POL-8000) and adhere to its requirements.
    *   **Must** review Client HSE procedures and guidelines with employees.
    *   **Must** review governmental regulations with employees.

*   **Training and Safe Work Practices:**
    *   **Must** perform safety orientations for employees and contractors arriving to work on site. Refer to [[hse_new_employee_orientation]] for general orientation guidelines.
    *   **Must** formulate safe work procedures and methods.
    *   **Must** ensure that safe work procedures and methods are used by the general work force. See [[safe_work_procedures]] for more information.
    *   **Must** ensure that all new or transferred employees are given safety orientation training before assignment to a supervisor.
    *   **Shall** ensure specific job training is carried out for personnel engaged in work involving the use or operation of cranes, lift trucks, mobile equipment, powder-actuated tools, scaffolds, ladders, man-lifts, and other high-risk equipment.

*   **Subcontractor Management:**
    *   **Must** audit Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Subcontractor health, safety, and environment plans and procedures.
    *   **Must** audit the implementation of subcontractor plans and procedures on the site.
    *   **Must** cooperate with non-Aker Kvaerner Chemetics subcontractors to ensure common safety issues are treated appropriately.
    *   **Must** provide subcontractors with necessary information which may affect them.

*   **Personal Protective Equipment (PPE):**
    *   **Must** set a personal example by wearing appropriate personal protective equipment/clothing.
    *   **Must** ensure that all necessary protective equipment and clothing is available for issue as and when required. Refer to [[personal_protective_equipment_policy]].

*   **Incident Management and Reporting:**
    *   **Must** report and investigate all employee injuries, equipment/property damage, and public liability incidents to their superiors immediately.
    *   **Must** take appropriate corrective actions to prevent recurrence of incidents.
    *   Incidents that resulted in an injury that can lead to a permanent disability **must** be reported to the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Vancouver office within one hour of occurrence.
    *   Full incident reporting guidelines are set out in Standard-0-PRC-8013 [[0-prc-8013_hse_incident_reporting]].

*   **Site Inspections and Promotion:**
    *   **Must** carry out informal physical condition inspections during the normal work day.
    *   **Must** take corrective action on substandard conditions and unsafe personal acts noted.
    *   Any items noted outside of the Site Health and Safety Manager's control **must** be reported immediately to his supervisor.
    *   **Must** actively promote Health and Safety on the work site.

*   **HSE Statistics and Reporting:**
    *   The designated Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Health and Safety Manager **will** collect health and safety statistics.
    *   The designated Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Health and Safety Manager **will** prepare a monthly report as required by company procedure Standard-0-PRC-8002 [[0-prc-8002_hse_performance_statistics]].
