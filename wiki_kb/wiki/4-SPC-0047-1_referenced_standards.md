# Referenced Standards for Stainless Steel Heat Exchanger Tubes (4-SPC-0047-2)

This section outlines the mandatory standards and specifications that govern the material, manufacturing, and testing of stainless steel heat exchanger tubes as defined in Specification 4-SPC-0047-2. Adherence to the latest versions of these standards is required to ensure the quality and performance of tubes used in [[gas_gas_heat_exchanger_design_criteria|gas to gas heat exchangers]] within acid plants.

**Primary Material Specification**
The specification applies to ASTM A249/SA249 types 304/304L and 304H.
*   **ASTM A249/SA249 (latest versions)**: This standard is a mandatory requirement for the fabrication of welded austenitic steel boiler, superheater, heat-exchanger, and condenser tubes. All applicable standards to which ASTM A249/SA249 refers are also required.

**Dimensional Tolerances**
*   **ASTM A1016 (latest version)**: Dimensional tolerances for the tubes shall conform to this standard. It is important to note that ASTM A1016 superseded ASTM A450 in 2004.

**General Material Testing and Requirements**
*   Material testing and general requirements as described in **ASTM A249** are mandatory.
*   Material testing and general requirements as described in **ASTM A1016** are mandatory.

These standards ensure that the tubes meet the necessary chemical composition, mechanical properties, and manufacturing quality for their intended application. Specific testing requirements derived from these standards are detailed in [[4-SPC-0047-1_heat_exchanger_tube_testing_requirements]].

For an overview of the entire specification, refer to [[4-SPC-0047-1_stainless_steel_heat_exchanger_tubes_specification_overview]].
