# Professional Services Subcontract (0-SPC-2201) Subcontractor Obligations

Under Professional Services Subcontract 0-SPC-2201, the Subcontractor bears significant responsibilities to ensure the successful and compliant delivery of services.

**General Performance and Compliance:**
*   The Subcontractor **must** [[subcontract_definitions_0_spc_2201|Provide the Services]] in accordance with the [[subcontract_definitions_0_spc_2201|Scope]].
*   The Subcontractor **is to** use the skill and care normally exercised by professionals providing similar services.
*   The Subcontractor **is to** obey all instructions from Aker Kvaerner Chemetics that are in accordance with this Subcontract.
*   Both Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Subcontractor **must** act in accordance with all applicable health and safety requirements.
*   The Subcontractor **must** notify Aker Kvaerner Chemetics immediately upon becoming aware that the [[subcontract_definitions_0_spc_2201|Scope]] requires anything illegal or impossible. If Aker Kvaerner Chemetics agrees, an instruction to change the Scope appropriately **must** be issued.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **must** provide information and resources required by this Subcontract in accordance with the [[subcontract_definitions_0_spc_2201|Accepted Schedule]]. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may also issue instructions to change the Scope.

**Personnel and Cooperation:**
*   The Subcontractor **must** employ each key person named in the [[subcontract_definitions_0_spc_2201|Subcontract Data]] for their stated job or employ a replacement accepted by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
*   For a proposed replacement, the Subcontractor **must** submit their name, relevant qualifications, and experience to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for acceptance. A reason for non-acceptance is if the replacement's qualifications and experience are not equivalent to the person being replaced.
*   The Subcontractor **must** cooperate with [[subcontract_definitions_0_spc_2201|Others]] in obtaining and providing information as specified in the [[subcontract_definitions_0_spc_2201|Scope]].
*   Where necessary, the Subcontractor **must** hold or attend meetings with Others and inform Aker Kvaerner Chemetics of these meetings. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may attend such meetings.
*   The Subcontractor **must** obtain necessary approvals from Others to [[subcontract_definitions_0_spc_2201|Provide the Services]].

**Sub-subcontracting:**
*   If the Subcontractor further subcontracts work, they remain fully responsible for performing this Subcontract as if no sub-subcontracting had occurred. This Subcontract applies as if a [[subcontract_definitions_0_spc_2201|Subsubcontractor]]'s employees were the Subcontractor’s.
*   The Subcontractor **must** submit the name of each proposed Subsubcontractor to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for acceptance and **must not** appoint them until accepted. A reason for non-acceptance is if the Subsubcontractor's appointment would hinder the Subcontractor's ability to Provide the Services.
*   If requested, the Subcontractor **must** submit proposed sub-subcontract conditions to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for acceptance. The Subcontractor **must not** appoint a Subsubcontractor under these conditions until accepted. Reasons for non-acceptance include conditions that would hinder the Subcontractor's ability to Provide the Services or do not include a statement of mutual trust and cooperation between the sub-subcontract parties.

**Quality Management and Defect Correction:**
*   The Subcontractor **must** operate a quality management system for Providing the Services as stated in the [[subcontract_definitions_0_spc_2201|Scope]], ensuring it complies with all specified requirements.
*   The Subcontractor **must** provide Aker Kvaerner Chemetics with a quality policy statement and a quality plan for acceptance within the period stated in the [[subcontract_definitions_0_spc_2201|Subcontract Data]]. Both documents **must** comply with the Scope's requirements.
*   The Subcontractor **must** comply with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' instructions to correct any failure to adhere to the quality plan.
*   The Subcontractor **must** correct any [[subcontract_definitions_0_spc_2201|Defects]] within a timeframe that minimizes adverse effects on Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or Others.
*   If, after [[subcontract_definitions_0_spc_2201|Completion]], the Subcontractor fails to correct a Defect within the required time, Aker Kvaerner Chemetics will assess the cost of having it corrected by other parties. The Subcontractor **must** then pay this assessed cost to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**General Performance and Compliance:**
*   The Subcontractor is to use the skill and normally used by professionals providing services similar to the services.
*   The Subcontractor notifies Aker Kvaerner Chemetics as soon as he becomes aware that the Scope requires him to do anything which is illegal or impossible. If Chemetics agrees that it is illegal or impossible, he gives an instruction to change the Scope appropriately.

**Key Personnel:**
*   The Subcontractor either employs each key person named to do the job for him stated in the *Subcontract Data* or employs a replacement person who has been accepted by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
*   The Subcontractor submits the name, relevant qualifications and experience of a proposed replacement person to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for acceptance.
*   A reason for not accepting the replacement person is that his relevant qualifications and experience are not as good as those of the person who is to be replaced.

**Cooperation with Others:**
*   Where necessary to Provide the Services, the Subcontractor holds or attends meetings with Others. The Subcontractor informs Aker Kvaerner Chemetics of those meetings, and Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may attend them.
*   The Subcontractor obtains approval from Others where necessary to Provide the Services.

**Subsubcontracting:**
*   This Subcontract applies as if a Subsubcontractor's employees were the Subcontractor’s.
*   The Subcontractor submits the name of each proposed Subsubcontractor to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for acceptance.
*   A reason for not accepting the Subsubcontractor is that his appointment will not allow the Subcontractor to Provide the Services.
*   The Subcontractor does not appoint a proposed Subsubcontractor until Aker Kvaerner Chemetics has accepted him.
*   If requested by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, the Subcontractor submits the proposed conditions of contract for each subsubcontract to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for acceptance.
*   When submission is required, the Subcontractor does not appoint a Subsubcontractor on the proposed Subsubcontract conditions submitted until Aker Kvaerner Chemetics has accepted them.
*   Reasons for not accepting proposed Subsubcontract conditions include that they will not allow the Subcontractor to Provide the Services, or that they do not include a statement that the parties to the Subsubcontract shall act in a spirit of mutual trust and co-operation.

**Defect Correction:**
*   The Subcontractor corrects any Defects within a time which minimises the adverse effect on Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or Others.
*   If, after Completion, the Subcontractor does not correct a Defect within the time required by this Subcontract, Aker Kvaerner Chemetics assesses the cost of having the Defect corrected by other people. The Subcontractor pays the assessed cost of correcting Defects by other people to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
