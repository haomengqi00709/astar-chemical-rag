# Final Strainer Materials of Construction

This page specifies the materials of construction for the various components of the Final Strainer (Tag No. 7842K). Material selection is critical for ensuring chemical compatibility, mechanical integrity, and longevity in the intended service.

**Component Materials:**
*   **Body Material of Construction:** SARAMET
*   **Strainer Material of Construction:** SARAMET
*   **Body Flange and Cover Material of Construction:** Carbon Steel (SA-240-316 / SA-516-70)
*   **Nozzles Material of Construction:** SARAMET
*   **Gaskets:** Full Face, Blue Gylon with bolt holes
*   **Bolts and Nuts Material of Construction:** Not specified (to be determined based on project specifications or vendor standards)

The use of SARAMET for the body, strainer, and nozzles indicates a requirement for high corrosion resistance, typical in sulfuric acid service. The carbon steel flanges and covers are likely protected by coatings or linings, or are in a non-corrosive environment.

These material specifications are integral to the overall design and fabrication of the strainer, complementing the [[final_strainer_dimensions]] and [[final_strainer_nozzle_specifications]].

*   **Bolts and Nuts Material of Construction:** Not specified
