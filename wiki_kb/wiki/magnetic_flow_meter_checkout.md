# Magnetic Flow Meter Checkout

This procedure details the specific checkout requirements for Magnetic Flow Meters (Magmeters). These checks supplement the [[instrument_checkout_general_guidelines]] and ensure proper installation and functionality before commissioning.

**Required Documents**
Documents specific to Magnetic Flow Meters include:
*   [[piping_and_instrumentation_drawings]] (P&ID)
*   [[8-lst-0002_instrument_index]] (Instrument Index)
*   [[8-dst-xxxx_instrument_specification_sheets]] (Instrument Specification Sheet)
*   [[instrument_loop_diagrams]] (Loop Diagram)
*   [[instrument_installation_details_8_dtl_xxxx]] (Installation Detail)
*   [[8-lst-0004_instrument_cable_schedule]] (Instrument Cable Schedule)
*   [[alarms_interlocks_and_setpoints]] (Alarms and Interlock List)

**Installation and Mechanical Checks**
*   **Nameplate Verification:** The Magnetic Flow Meter Model No. nameplate data **must** match the Instrument Specification Sheet information. The Nameplate tag **must** match the Instrument Tag Number.
*   **Process Line Location:** The magmeter **must** be located in the process line such that the magnetic flowtube is flooded at all times, including during shutdown.
*   **Vertical Pipeline Flow:** In vertical pipelines, flow **must** travel upwards.
*   **Flow Direction:** Magtube flow direction **must** correspond to the arrow indicated on the flow tube.
*   **Servicing Accessibility:** If the transmitter is integrally mounted to the magtube, accessibility for servicing **must** be confirmed. The magtube **must** be isolatable for servicing.
*   **Impulse Tubed:** N/A
*   **Inst Air Tubed:** N/A

**Electrical and Wiring Checks**
*   **Power Application:** Power **must not** be applied to the Magtube unless it is flooded.
*   **Remote Transmitter Wiring:** If the transmitter is remotely mounted from the magtube, flow tube wiring connections and transmitter wiring connections **must** be in accordance with the [[instrument_loop_diagrams]] and the manufacturers' instructions.
*   **AC Magmeters:** Where AC magmeters are installed, all jumpers **must** be correctly installed for series or parallel coil operation as defined on the [[8-dst-xxxx_instrument_specification_sheets]].
*   **Grounding:** Grounding straps **must** be installed as per the [[instrument_installation_details_8_dtl_xxxx]] and/or the manufacturers' installation instructions. If the pipeline is of non-conductive material (CPVC, PVC, FRP), grounding ring(s) **must** be installed and properly grounded as shown on the [[instrument_installation_details_8_dtl_xxxx]] and/or the manufacturers' installation instructions.

**Calibration and Functional Checks**
*   **Range:** The range of the magmeter **must** be in accordance with the [[8-dst-xxxx_instrument_specification_sheets]].
*   **Calibration Verification:** If the manufacturer has not provided Calibration Certification, liquid drop tests will be performed at the discretion of the site supervisor and/or the start-up engineer to confirm calibration.
*   **Fail Action:** N/A
*   **Field Fail Action Checked:** N/A
*   **Field/DCS Calibration Check:** Refers to the Check - 0% procedure as outlined in [[instrument_checkout_general_guidelines]].

**Sign-off**
*   **Initials KC:** Indicates checkout is complete and ready for water batching and commissioning.
*   **Initials Client:** Indicates acceptance of Instrumentation Checkout completion.
*   **P&I Ref:** Refers to the [[piping_and_instrumentation_drawings]] where the instrument may be located.
*   **Comments:** General comments during commissioning.
