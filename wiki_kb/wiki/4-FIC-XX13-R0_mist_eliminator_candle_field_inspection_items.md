# Mist Eliminator Candle Field Inspection Items

This page outlines the specific inspection requirements for Mist Eliminator Candles as detailed in Field Inspection Checklist 4-FIC-XX13-R0. Adherence to these items is crucial for ensuring proper installation and functionality.

**Mandatory Inspection Requirements:**
*   The Mist Eliminator **must** be installed as per the supplier's instructions. Refer to [[vendor_quality_assurance_requirements]] for general supplier compliance.
*   Candles **must** be checked for bolt tightness on the tubesheet. Torque may be specified for bolt tightness on the tubesheet.
*   Gaskets **must** be installed between the candles and the tubesheet.
*   Correct hardware and gaskets **must** be installed.
*   Tubesheet drain holes **must** be inspected.
*   No blockage **must** be found in the tubesheet drain holes.
*   Tubesheet drain piping **must** be installed.
*   Seal Pots **must** be installed.
*   Candles **must** be checked for tears/damage.
*   Candle restraints **must** be installed on top of candles as per the supplier's instructions (if applicable).
*   Installation **must** be checked from above and below the candle tubesheet.
*   The tubesheet **must** be clean.
*   All debris **must** be removed from the tower.

These inspection items are part of a broader checklist that contains 24 numbered items, though items 12.0 through 24.0 are currently undefined in this template. For general information on mist eliminator candles, refer to [[mist_eliminator_candle_datasheet_administration_4_dst_xxxx_r0]].
