# Commissioning Site Safety Rules

This page details the specific safety rules that **must** be observed by all personnel on Aker Kvaerner Chemetics [[hse_commissioning_sites|commissioning sites]], as outlined in procedure [[hse_site_postings_overview]] (0-PRC-8004) and referenced by [[worksite_safety_rules_general]]. These rules are designed to ensure safety during the critical commissioning phase.

**Mandatory Commissioning Site Safety Rules:**

*   **Personal Protective Equipment (PPE):** Hard hats, safety glasses, and safety boots **will** be worn by all workers entering the job site.
*   **Site Entry Protocol:** All personnel entering the work site for the first time **will** report to the site office immediately upon arrival.
*   **Substance Use:** Consuming or possession of illegal drugs or alcohol on this project site is **prohibited**. Refer to the [[prohibited_substances_and_conduct]] policy for further details.
*   **Injury Reporting and First Aid:** First Aid treatment **shall** be obtained immediately for any injury.
*   **Hazard Reporting:** Unsafe conditions, safety observations, and incidents **will** be reported to your supervisor immediately. For incident reporting guidelines, see [[hse_incident_reporting]].
*   **Appropriate Clothing:** Clothing that covers arms and legs (long sleeves and long pants) **will** be worn.
*   **Client Safety Rules:** If client safety rules and regulations are in place and meet or exceed Aker Kvaerner Chemetics standards, they **will** be followed.
*   **Interlock Management:** By-passing or forcing interlocks **will** only be done with the explicit permission of the [[commissioning_manager_assignment|Commissioning Manager]].
*   **Company Compliance:** All personnel **must** comply with all Aker Kvaerner Chemetics (Aker Kvaerner) safety rules and regulations.

These rules are prominently posted at the employee entrance of commissioning sites.

### Commissioning Site Rules

*   Hard hat, safety glasses, and safety boots will be worn by all workers entering the job site.
*   All personnel entering the work site for the first time will report to the site office first thing.
*   Consuming or possession of illegal drugs or alcohol on this project site is prohibited.
*   First Aid treatment shall be obtained immediately for any injury.
*   Unsafe conditions, safety observations and incidents will be reported to your supervisor immediately.
*   Clothing that covers arms and legs will be worn (long sleeves and long pants).
*   If in place, and if they meet or exceed Aker Kvaerner Chemetics standards, the client’s safety rules and regulations will be followed.
*   By-passing, or forcing, interlocks will only be done with the permission of the Commissioning Manager.
*   Comply with all Aker Kvaerner Chemetics (Aker Kvaerner) safety rules and regulations.
