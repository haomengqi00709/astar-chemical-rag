# Agitator Quality Record Definitions

For agitator equipment covered by Quality Data Sheet **0-QDS-7418**, the following definitions apply to quality records:

*   **AP (As Prepared):** This designation indicates that the quality record is required to be prepared by the vendor.
*   **FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package):** This designation indicates that the quality record **must** be included as part of the final Quality Assurance Documentation Package submitted by the vendor.

These definitions are crucial for understanding the documentation requirements throughout the manufacturing and inspection process.

For general information on quality data sheets and their records, refer to [[quality_data_sheets_qds]].
