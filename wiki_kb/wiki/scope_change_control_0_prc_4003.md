# Scope Change Control (0-PRC-4003)

The document 0-PRC-4003 outlines the procedure for Change Management and Project Variance/Change Notification (PVN/PCN). Its primary purpose is to establish clear policies, responsibilities, and procedures for identifying, tracking, and approving or rejecting potential changes that could impact the Project’s approved budget, schedule, and scope. This procedure also serves to provide trends for the accurate forecasting of project costs and schedule.

This procedure covers the administration of change management processes for both the home office and field construction activities. Its scope encompasses all changes that may affect the project's budget, manpower forecast, schedule, overall scope, and guarantees.

**Mandatory Requirements:**
*   All projects **must** implement a change management procedure.
*   All projects **must** set up a change management program to track, communicate, and document project changes.
*   A change management procedure **must** be reviewed and agreed upon with the client at the project's inception. If the client has an existing change management procedure, an amending procedure **shall** be developed to integrate it with the client's process.
*   Change Management **shall** be a "top priority" in the mindset of all project personnel.

The underlying project philosophy is to deliver a work product that consistently meets the scope, schedule, performance, and warranty requirements stipulated in the contract. Any item identified as different from the original scope, or which may materially affect the scope, cost, schedule, execution plan, or cash flow of the project, **shall** be identified as a Change.

A critical aspect of change management is timeliness. The identification, notification, and resolution of a change **must** occur early enough to enable feasible decisions, minimizing rework and schedule disruption. Where applicable, the probable effects on the approved project parameters **shall** be developed to a comparable level of accuracy to facilitate informed decision-making.

For administrative efficiency, changes below $10,000 can be accumulated before a potential trend is raised. However, this accumulation is not permitted if approval to proceed is required, or if the reason for the change necessitates immediate management or communication.

The Director of Projects is responsible for the approval of this procedure.

**Core Principles and Requirements**
*   It is a REQUIREMENT for all projects to implement a change management procedure and to set up a change management program to track, communicate, and document project changes.
*   A change management procedure must be reviewed and agreed upon with the client at the beginning of a project. If the client has their own change management procedure, an amending procedure should be developed to incorporate it with this procedure.
*   Change Management shall be a “top priority“ in the mindset of all project personnel.
*   The underlying project philosophy is to provide a work product which meets the scope, schedule, performance, and warranty requirements of the contract.
*   Any item found different from the original scope and/or which may materially affect the scope, cost, schedule, execution plan or cash flow of the project shall be identified as a Change.
*   The essential feature of change management is timeliness. Identification, notification, and resolution of a change must occur early enough for decisions to be feasible without (or with minimum) rework and/or schedule disruption to the project.
