# Inspection and Shipping Document Distribution (0-SPC-5004)

**Open-Package Inspection:**
Prior to closing packaging, the Vendor **must** notify [Company Name] that the goods are packaged and ready for an open-package inspection. This inspection **may** be waived at the discretion of [Company Name]. Refer to [[vendor_inspection_release]] for related procedures.

**Shipping Document Distribution:**
The Vendor **must** contact the [Company Name] Logistics Group for instructions on the distribution of shipping documents.

**Required Shipping Documents:**
Shipping documents **include**, but are not limited to:
*   Certificates of Origin
*   Free Trade Certificates
*   Insurance Certificates
*   Fumigation, Heat, or Pressure Treatment Certificates

The list of shipping documents (originally Section 11 of 0-SPC-5004) **should** be amended as applicable to the country of destination.

**Wood Treatment Requirements:**
When packing goods destined for specific jurisdictions (e.g., Australia, European Union), any wood used in packing **must** be treated in accordance with all applicable [named destination] wood treatment and fumigation requirements.
