# Electric Motor Identification

The identification of electric motors follows a specific procedure that integrates with both electrical and mechanical equipment numbering systems. All electric motors **shall** be numbered in accordance with [[4-PRC-0001_plant_equipment_identification]]. This ensures a consistent approach to equipment identification across different engineering disciplines.

Electric motors are identified by a procedure managed by the mechanical department. A key principle of this identification system is that electric motors utilize the same identification number as the mechanical equipment they drive. This linkage simplifies cross-referencing between electrical and mechanical documentation and facilitates maintenance and operational activities.

For example, if a pump (mechanical equipment) is identified as P-101, its associated electric motor will also carry the identification P-101. This approach streamlines the identification process and enhances clarity in plant operations.

This identification method is a component of the broader [[9-PRC-0021_electrical_numbering_and_identification_procedure|electrical numbering and identification framework]].
