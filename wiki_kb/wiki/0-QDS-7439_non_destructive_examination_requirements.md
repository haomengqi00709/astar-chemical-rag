# Scrubber Venturi Internals Non-Destructive Examination Requirements

This page details the mandatory Non-Destructive Examination (NDE) requirements for the Scrubber Venturi Internals, ensuring the structural integrity and quality of welds and materials.

**General NDE Standards:**
*   Non-Destructive Testing **must** be in accordance with **ASME VIII Div. 1**.
*   Non Destructive Examination **will** be in accordance with the **ASME Boiler & Pressure Vessel Code** or an approved alternate standard.

**Dye Penetrant Testing (PT):**
*   **Coverage:** Dye Penetrant testing coverage **is** 100%.
*   **Application:** Dye Penetrant testing applies to all Hastelloy welds.
*   **Quality Record:** Dye Penetrant testing **is required** as [[0-QDS-7439_quality_record_definitions|Quality Record FP]] (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package).
*   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Complete. Chemetics **will** perform a complete surveillance of the Dye Penetrant testing process and results.

These NDE requirements are critical for detecting any surface-breaking defects in welds and materials, especially for components made from Hastelloy, which often require high integrity due to corrosive environments.
