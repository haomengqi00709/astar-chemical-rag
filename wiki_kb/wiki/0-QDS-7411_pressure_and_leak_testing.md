# Acid Cooler Pressure and Leak Testing (0-QDS-7411)

This page outlines the pressure and leak testing requirements for the Acid Cooler, as specified in document [[0-QDS-7411_document_details|0-QDS-7411]].

**Specific Testing Activities:**

*   **Helium Leak Test:**
    *   Required as [[0-QDS-7411_quality_record_definitions|FP]].
    *   Requires surveillance (witness) by Chemetics Vancouver.
    *   Applies to: Tube-tubesheet weld.

*   **Hydrostatic Test:**
    *   Required as [[0-QDS-7411_quality_record_definitions|FP]].
    *   Requires surveillance (witness) by Chemetics Vancouver.
    *   Applies to: Shell & tube sides.

These tests are essential to verify the integrity and leak-tightness of the Acid Cooler's pressure boundaries, ensuring safe and reliable operation.
