# Hydecat Hypo Destruction Reactor Process Guarantees

This page outlines the process guarantees for the Hydecat Hypo Destruction Reactor, as specified in Document ID 4-PDS-XXXX-R1. These guarantees are critical for assessing the [[performance_testing_1_prc_0008]] of the reactor.

**Outlet Process Guarantees**
The following guarantees apply to the reactor's outlet stream:

*   **Maximum Stream Composition for NaOCl:** Must be less than 0.1 % w/w (refer to Notes 4.0 and 5.0 for additional details).
*   **Maximum Outlet Temperature:** Measured in oC, VTA (Vendor to Advise).
