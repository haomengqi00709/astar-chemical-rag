# Vendor Document Status Definitions

All documents supplied by Vendors to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **must** clearly indicate their status prior to issuance. The status designation communicates the document's current stage in the review and approval process and its intended use. The word “Approved” as applied to document status means approved by the Vendor for the stated use in accordance with their [[project_quality_management_plan_overview|Quality Plan]].

The following document statuses are defined:

*   **Approved for Design**:
    *   This status signifies that the information contained within the document may be used by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for the design of ancillary components. This includes items not directly supplied by the Vendor, such as support structures, foundations, associated piping, or power supply systems.

*   **Issued for Review**:
    *   This status is specifically to be used only on a "hold" document. It indicates that Aker Kvaerner Chemetics has not yet given explicit approval to proceed with the work described in Section 12 of the [[vendor_document_supply_0_spc_0001|Vendor Document Supply Specification (0-SPC-0001)]]. This status mandates a review by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics before further work can commence.

*   **Approved for Fabrication**:
    *   Documents with this status contain information that **will** be used to manufacture the goods.
    *   If the document is a "hold" document, this status **shall** only be shown when all revisions requested by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics have been fully incorporated.
    *   Furthermore, for "hold" documents, "Approved for Fabrication" status **shall** only be shown when Aker Kvaerner Chemetics has explicitly given approval to proceed with the work as described in Section 12 of the [[vendor_document_supply_0_spc_0001|Vendor Document Supply Specification (0-SPC-0001)]].

*   **Approved Instruction**:
    *   This status indicates that the information may be used by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or by the Owner for the installation, operation, or maintenance of the supplied goods.

*   **As Built**:
    *   An "As Built" document serves as a final record of the condition of the goods as they were delivered. It reflects any modifications or changes made during fabrication or construction.

Vendors **must** ensure that the correct status is marked on each document before it is issued to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, as this directly impacts the subsequent project activities and approvals.
