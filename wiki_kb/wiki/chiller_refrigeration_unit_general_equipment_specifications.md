# Chiller/Refrigeration Unit General Equipment Specifications

The general equipment specifications for Chiller/Refrigeration Units, detailed in datasheet [[4-PDS-XXXX-R1_template_chiller_refrigeration_unit_datasheet_administration]], cover fundamental aspects of the unit's design and operation.

**Equipment Type:**
The unit can be either:
*   Mechanical
*   Absorption

**Equipment Operation:**
The unit's operation can be:
*   Continuous
*   Intermittent

**Capacity Control:**
Capacity control options include:
*   Continuous
*   Stepwise

**Mandatory Requirements:**
*   **Note 3.0:** The compressor **shall** be a screw type with a sliding vane for capacity control ranging from 0 to 100%. This ensures precise control over the refrigeration capacity.
*   **Note 2.0:** The refrigeration unit **must** be insulated (including piping and equipment in the cold section) to minimize heat gain from ambient air. This is critical for energy efficiency and maintaining desired process temperatures. Refer to [[insulation_design_criteria_6_prc_0001]] for general insulation guidelines and [[piping_design_criteria_5_prc_0002]] for piping design considerations.

**Primary Refrigerant Type:**
The primary refrigerant can be selected from:
*   R404A
*   Ammonia
*   R507
*   Water
*   Lithium Bromide and Water
*   Other (to be specified)

For specific component types, refer to [[chiller_refrigeration_unit_component_types]]. For material selection, see [[chiller_refrigeration_unit_materials_of_construction]].

**Primary Refrigerant Type:**
The datasheet allows for selection from various primary refrigerants, including: R404A, Ammonia, R507, Water, Lithium Bromide and Water, or Other (to be specified).

**Quantity:**
The datasheet allows for specifying the quantity of units, categorized as:
*   **Operating**
*   **Installed Spare**
