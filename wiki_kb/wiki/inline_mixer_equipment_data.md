# Inline Mixer Equipment Data

This page describes the "Equipment Data" section of the Template Inline Mixer Process Data Sheet (document ID [[4-PDS-XXXX-R1_template_inline_mixer_datasheet_administration]]). This section provides critical physical and operational specifications for the inline mixer, complementing the [[equipment_data_sheets_4_dst_xxxx]] standards.

**General Equipment Information:**
*   **Quantity:** Specifies the total number of inline mixers required.
    *   **Operating quantity:** The number of units actively in service.
    *   **Installed Spare quantity:** The number of spare units installed and ready for use.

**Mechanical Design and Operational Parameters:**
The following parameters define the mechanical limits and operational characteristics of the inline mixer, adhering to [[general_mechanical_design_requirements_0_spc_0010]]:
*   **Maximum Pressure:** The maximum allowable operating pressure, measured in kPag (kilopascals gauge).
*   **Maximum Temperature:** The maximum allowable operating temperature, measured in oC (degrees Celsius).
*   **Allowable Pressure Drop:** The maximum permissible pressure drop across the mixer, measured in kPa (kilopascals).
*   **Type of Operation:** Defines the orientation and flow direction of the mixer. Possible types include:
    *   Horizontal
    *   Upward Vertical Flow
    *   Downward Vertical Flow
*   **Does Pipe Run Full with Liquid:** A Yes/No field indicating whether the pipe containing the mixer is expected to be completely filled with liquid during operation.
*   **Nominal Pipe diameter:** The nominal diameter of the pipe in which the inline mixer is installed, measured in mm (millimeters).
*   **Number of Elements:** Specifies the quantity of mixing elements within the inline mixer.

All measurements are provided in standard [[engineering_units]].

**Key Equipment Fields**
This section details critical equipment specifications:
*   **Maximum Pressure:** Measured in kPag.
*   **Maximum Temperature:** Measured in oC.
*   **Allowable Pressure Drop:** Measured in kPa.
*   **Type of Operation:** This field exists and can specify the operational orientation, including:
    *   Horizontal
    *   Upward Vertical Flow
    *   Downward Vertical Flow
*   **Does Pipe Run Full with Liquid:** A Yes/No field indicating the liquid fill status of the pipe.
*   **Nominal Pipe diameter:** This field exists and is measured in mm.
*   **Number of Elements:** Field specifying the quantity of mixing elements.
