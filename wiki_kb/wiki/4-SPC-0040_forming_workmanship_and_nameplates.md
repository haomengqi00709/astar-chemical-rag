# 4-SPC-0040 Forming, Workmanship, and Nameplates

Specification 4-SPC-0040 details requirements for forming operations, acceptable workmanship standards, and the affixing of nameplates to metallic process vessels and components.

**Forming Operations:**
*   Materials **must** be cold formed.
*   Where necessary, cold formed materials **must** be stress relieved or annealed according to the applicable code and Aker Kvaerner Chemetics specifications.
*   When hot forming is necessary, it **must** be carried out in accordance with a written procedure approved by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
*   Operations such as tube rolling and welding **must** also be carried out in accordance with a written procedure.
*   Reinforcing pads and other attachments **must** closely match the mating contour of the equipment.

**Workmanship and Rejection Criteria:**
*   Poor workmanship will be considered a reason for rejection of the fabricated equipment.
*   Evidence of poor workmanship includes, but is not limited to:
    *   Flaws or defects which cannot be reasonably repaired.
    *   Excessive repairs.
    *   Poor fit-up.
    *   Nozzles requiring relocation.
    *   Damaged surfaces.
*   In addition to [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels|ASME Code]] requirements, all equipment **must** satisfy the [[4-SPC-0040_fabrication_tolerances|tolerances given in Section 13]] of this specification.

**Nameplates and Marking:**
*   In addition to any nameplates required by codes (e.g., [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels|ASME]], TEMA), the Vendor **must** affix an Aker Kvaerner Chemetics nameplate to the equipment.
*   All nameplates **must** be securely fastened with stainless steel bolts or rivets to a bracket welded to the vessel shell, as shown on the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics drawing.
*   Nameplate brackets **must** be sized to accommodate the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics nameplate (150mm x 90mm (6” x 3½”)).
*   Nameplate brackets **must** also be sized to accommodate the Vendor’s nameplate where required.
*   For Code stamped equipment where metric pressures and temperatures are required, the Vendor **must** install a supplementary metric nameplate.

**Cleaning and Surface Preparation:**
*   Equipment **must** be drained and thoroughly cleaned inside and outside.
*   Equipment **must** be free from rust, grease, weld spatter, scale, slag, and any other foreign matter.
*   Interior surfaces will **not** be painted, coated, or lined unless otherwise specified.
*   When an Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or Owner paint specification is applicable, the Vendor **must** include a report demonstrating satisfactory compliance to the specification in the [[4-SPC-0040_quality_documentation_package|quality assurance documentation package]].
