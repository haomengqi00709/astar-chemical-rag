# Fabricator Quality Assurance and Welding Procedure Qualification for High Silicon Austenitic Stainless Steel (4-SPC-0025)

**Fabricator Quality Assurance Responsibilities**
The fabricator **shall** ensure regular and systematic supervision of all work, both in-shop and on-site, in accordance with their ASME approved quality control system manual. This includes maintaining full traceability of all materials back to their original mill certification. The fabricator's internal controls **shall** prevent the use of unspecified materials. Substitution of materials **shall not** be permitted without prior agreement in writing by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. Lack of adequate identification or certification **shall** be cause for rejection of the specific component or item.

**Welding Procedure Qualification (WPQ)**
All welding procedures **shall** be submitted by the fabricator for review and agreement by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Q.A. & Materials Group prior to the commencement of any work. The submittal package **shall** include:
*   The ASME IX PQR Form 483 or an equivalent form.
*   100% Radiography test results.
*   100% Dye Check test results.
*   Chemical Analysis (weld metal) test results.
*   Ferrite level in weld metal including root pass test results.
*   Huey Test results.

The Huey Test **shall** be qualified on the same heat of material as that used for fabrication and **shall** identify the method used to rapidly cool the root pass or "process" side of the weld. Refer to [[4-SPC-0025_corrosion_testing_requirements]] for details on production weld corrosion tests.

A change in essential variables, as outlined in [[ASME_section_ix_welding_and_brazing_qualifications]], will require resubmission of the affected procedure(s). Welding parameters (current, voltage, electrode size, etc.) and any special requirements or precautions (e.g., recording interpass temperature, cooling methods) **shall** be documented and monitored during equipment fabrication. Interpass temperature **shall** be monitored and recorded during equipment and piping fabrication.

All work **shall** be carried out in accordance with "Approved for Fabrication" shop detail drawing(s). Deviations from weld geometry shown on approved drawing(s) are **not permitted** without prior approval from Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
