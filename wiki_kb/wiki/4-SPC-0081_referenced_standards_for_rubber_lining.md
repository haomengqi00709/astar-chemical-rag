# Referenced Standards for Rubber Lining (4-SPC-0081)

This specification (4-SPC-0081) incorporates and references several industry codes and standards to ensure the quality and integrity of rubber-lined equipment.

**Key Referenced Standards:**
*   **[[4-SPC-0040_metallic_process_vessels_fabrication_overview]]**: This specification provides the codes and standards governing the design and fabrication of the metallic vessel components that are to be rubber lined.
*   **NACE RP0298-98**: Titled "Sheet Rubber Linings for Abrasion and Corrosion Service," this standard is highly relevant to the selection and application of rubber linings.
*   **NACE RP0178-98**: Titled "Fabrication Details, Surface Finish Requirements and Proper Design Considerations for Tanks and Vessels to be lined for Immersion Service," this standard provides crucial details for preparing equipment for rubber lining.

**Accessing NACE Standards:**
The NACE standards pertaining to rubber lining vessels are integral to this specification. Vendors **must** purchase their own copies of these NACE standards, as copyright laws prohibit Aker Kvaerner Chemetics from attaching paper copies or e-mailing them. NACE publications can be obtained at a nominal cost directly from www.nace.org, either by mail or electronically over the internet.

It is anticipated that the vast majority of Vendors involved in rubber lining work will already be familiar with the cited NACE standards. For those who are not, access to the internet is expected to facilitate their acquisition and review of these essential documents.
