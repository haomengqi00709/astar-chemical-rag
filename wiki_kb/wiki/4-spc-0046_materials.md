# Sulphuric Acid Storage Tank Materials (4-SPC-0046)

This section details the material requirements for the fabrication of sulphuric acid storage tanks under specification 4-SPC-0046.

**Material Standards**
All materials utilized in the manufacture of these tanks **must** conform to [[API_650_welded_steel_tanks]]. Specific material details **must** be clearly indicated on the engineering drawings and/or the purchase order.

**Steel Specifications**
When steel conforming to [[ASTM_a36]] or [[ASTM_a285]] is specified, it **must** be produced using practices other than rimmed or capped steel. This ensures the material properties are suitable for the intended application.

**Mill Test Certificates**
The manufacturer **will** provide Aker Kvaerner Chemetics with one copy of all mill test certificates for plates prior to any fitting and rolling operations. Similarly, one copy of all mill test certificates for other materials **will** be provided before any shop or field work commences. This ensures traceability and verification of material quality.

**Material Compatibility**
It is critical to note that the specified materials are compatible with sulphuric acid only at specific combinations of temperature and concentration. Therefore, consultation with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_quality_assurance_and_materials_engineering_group|Quality Assurance and Materials Engineering Group]] is **necessary** before utilizing this specification for sulphuric acid service to ensure appropriate material selection and prevent corrosion or failure.
