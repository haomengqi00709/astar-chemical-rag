# Electrical Mechanical Check-out and Commissioning

This page outlines the procedures for mechanical check-out and commissioning of electrical equipment and field instruments, ensuring proper installation and functionality before plant startup.

**Electrical Field Check Lists**
There are two types of electrical field check lists to be completed by on-site personnel:
*   The first type **will** be used by the contractor.
*   The second type **will** be used by the (CV Specialist) representative.

Electrical field check lists **will** be completed and forwarded to the office in Vancouver. Electrical equipment **will** be checked-out by the Contractor and the representative in accordance with electrical field check lists, 9-LST-07XX.

**Field Instrument Check-out and Testing**
Refer to procedure Field Instrument Design and Specification, 8-PRC-0003 for field instrument testing information.
Refer to procedure Field Instrument Design and Specification, 8-PRC-0003 for field instrument check-out list information.
Refer to procedure Field Instrument Design and Specification, 8-PRC-0003 for field instrument information.

**DCS Check-out and Commissioning**
Refer to procedure Control System Design and Specification, 8-PRC-0004 for DCS mechanical check-out and commissioning information.

**Roles and Responsibilities**
The DDE is expected to assign personnel from the design team who worked on the plant design for mechanical check-out and commissioning. It is preferable to have the same personnel for mechanical check-out and commissioning who worked on the project and answered questions during the construction phase. DDE personnel assigned to the project **will** report to the commissioning manager for the project.

**Roles during Mechanical Check-out and Commissioning:**
*   Electrical field check list involves CV, EC, and DoE.
*   Field instrument testing involves CV, EC, and DoE.
*   Field instrument check-out list involves DDE.
*   Field instrument involves DDE.
*   Electrical equipment involves CV.
*   DCS involves DDE.
