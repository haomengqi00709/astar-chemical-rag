# Acid Cooler Chemetics Surveillance Level (0-QDS-7411)

This page specifies the surveillance level for the Acid Cooler, as defined in document [[0-QDS-7411_document_details|0-QDS-7411]].

Surveillance by Chemetics Vancouver will be to Level 0.0.

This level dictates the extent of monitoring and oversight required from Chemetics Vancouver during the manufacturing and inspection processes of the Acid Cooler.

For other Chemetics surveillance levels, refer to [[0-QDS-7404-1_chemetics_surveillance_level]] or [[0-QDS-7404-2_chemetics_surveillance_level]].
