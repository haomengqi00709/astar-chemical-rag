# System Isometric Drawings

System Isometric drawings provide a scaled, composite view of an entire piping system, integrating information from multiple individual [[pipe_isometric]] drawings. These drawings are valuable for understanding the overall layout and interconnections of complex piping networks.

**Content and Purpose**
A System Isometric drawing **shall** contain:
*   A scale drawing of a piping system, which is a composite of several individual isometrics. This provides a broader perspective than a single pipe isometric.

**Roles and Responsibilities**
The preparation, checking, and approval of System Isometric drawings are assigned to specific disciplines:
*   **Preparation:** The drawing **shall** be prepared by the Design discipline.
*   **Checking:** The drawing **shall** be checked by the Instrument and Mechanical disciplines.
*   **Approval:** The drawing **shall** be approved by the Process discipline.

**Document Classification and Usage**
System Isometrics are classified as internal engineering QA documents. They **shall not** be issued to the Owner. However, they **may** be issued to Chemetics Construction Management for:
*   Planning purposes.
*   Progress measurement.

The inclusion of System Isometrics was a significant update, added during Revision 1 of the [[5-PRC-0003_piping_documentation_procedure_overview]].
