# Mist Eliminator Candle Operating Conditions (4-DST-XXXX-R0)

This section details the mandatory operating conditions that must be specified for the Mist Eliminator Candle Datasheet (4-DST-XXXX-R0). Accurate input of these parameters is crucial for proper design and performance evaluation of the mist eliminator candles.

**Carrier Fluid at Inlet - Mandatory Information:**
The following properties for the carrier fluid at the inlet must be provided:
*   **Fluid Description:** A detailed description of the carrier fluid is required.
*   **Mass Flow Rate:** The mass flow rate in kg/h must be specified.
*   **Volumetric Flow Rate:** The volumetric flow rate in Am³/h must be specified.
*   **Flow Variation:** The percentage of flow variation must be specified.
*   **Temperature:** The temperature in °C must be specified.
*   **Absolute Pressure:** The absolute pressure in kPa(a) must be specified.
*   **Density:** The density in kg/m³ must be specified.
*   **Viscosity:** The viscosity in cP must be specified.

**Entrained Material at Inlet - Mandatory Information:**
The following properties for the entrained material at the inlet must be provided:
*   **Description:** A detailed description of the entrained material is required.
*   **Mass Flow Rate:** The mass flow rate in kg/h must be specified.
*   **Density:** The density in kg/m³ must be specified.
*   **Viscosity:** The viscosity in cP must be specified.
*   **Plugging Tendency:** The plugging tendency of the entrained material must be specified.
