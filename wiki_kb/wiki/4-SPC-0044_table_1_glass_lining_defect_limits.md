# Glass-Lining Defect Limits (4-SPC-0044 Table 1)

This page describes the content of `[TABLE: 4-SPC-0044_table_1]`, which outlines the permissible limits for defects in glass-linings of carbon steel reaction vessels.

**Table Content:**
The table specifies various types of glass-lining defects and their acceptable sizes or frequencies. It serves as a critical reference for quality control during the inspection of glass-lined equipment, ensuring that any imperfections fall within acceptable manufacturing standards. This table is used in conjunction with the [[4-SPC-0044_glass_lining_application_and_quality|Glass-Lining Application and Quality Requirements]] and [[4-SPC-0044_inspection_and_non_destructive_examination|Inspection and Non-Destructive Examination]] procedures to determine if a vessel's lining meets the required quality standards.
