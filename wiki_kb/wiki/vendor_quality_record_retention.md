# Vendor Quality Record Retention

As a fundamental requirement of quality assurance under Specification [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment|0-SPC-7001]], the Vendor **shall** retain all documents that furnish evidence of the quality of the goods.

**Purpose of Retention**
The retention of quality records is essential for:
*   Demonstrating compliance with specified requirements.
*   Providing objective evidence of quality during audits or reviews.
*   Supporting traceability and accountability throughout the product lifecycle.
*   Facilitating investigation and resolution of any future quality issues.

These records serve as objective evidence that all quality-related activities, inspections, and tests have been performed in accordance with the [[vendor_quality_plan_requirements|Quality Plan]] and Purchase Order requirements. The specific duration and storage methods for these records **should** be defined within the Vendor's [[vendor_quality_document_control|document control procedures]].
