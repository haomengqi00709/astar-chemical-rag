# HSE Management and Employee Responsibilities

Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) is a joint responsibility shared between management and all employees at Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. This collaborative approach ensures a safe and healthy workplace for everyone.

**Management Responsibilities**
Management **must** actively contribute to HSE by:
*   Providing adequate resources for health, safety, and environmental initiatives.
*   Educating employees on safe working practices.
*   Promoting safety and fostering a safe mindset among all employees.
*   Acting promptly to correct unsafe conditions and unsafe working practices.

**Employee Responsibilities**
Employees play a critical role in maintaining a safe environment and **must**:
*   Follow all established safe working practices and work safely at all times.
*   Actively work to ensure their own safety and the safety of others.
*   Report safety violations and hazards promptly to their supervisor.
*   Are empowered and required to take action to correct unsafe conditions under their control.
*   **Must** be aware of other activities around them and **should** look, think, and act to protect their safety and that of others.

**Accident and Hazard Reporting**
*   All accidents and near-miss incidents **must** be reported.
*   Serious accidents **must** be reported immediately.
*   Managers **will** investigate all accidents and near-miss incidents.
*   All observed hazards **must** be reported to your supervisor.

**HSE Questions and Guidance**
For questions regarding health and safety policies and procedures:
*   **Office Environment:** Direct inquiries to your immediate Supervisor, the [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_document_history|HSE Manager]], Safety Coordinator, or Human Resources.
*   **Research Laboratory:** Direct inquiries to your immediate Supervisor, AKCTC Safety Committee Members, the [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_document_history|HSE Manager]], or Human Resources.
