# Project Financial and Tax Structure

Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Australia is the legal entity under which Aker Kvaerner Chemetics conducts business operations in Queensland, Australia.

**Tax Liability Separation:**
A clear distinction between work performed in Australia and work performed outside of Australia **must** be maintained. This separation is crucial for accurately distinguishing income tax liabilities between Canada and Australia.

**Sub-Project Structure:**
To facilitate this distinction, the project will be structured into two separate sub-projects:
*   **Sub-project 02C03001:** Designated for all work performed outside of Australia.
*   **Sub-project 02C03002:** Designated for all work performed within Australia.

**Australian Transactions:**
Purchase Orders and Subcontracts with Australian Vendors and Subcontractors will be placed specifically in the name of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Australia. Furthermore, specific engineering activities performed in Australia will be charged directly to sub-project 02C03002.
