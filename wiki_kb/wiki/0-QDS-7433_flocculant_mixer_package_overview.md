# Flocculant Mixer Package Overview

The Flocculant Mixer is supplied as a comprehensive, skid-mounted package designed for efficient and integrated operation within a larger plant system. This self-contained unit is engineered to perform the critical function of preparing and dispensing flocculant solutions, essential for various chemical processes, particularly in water treatment or mineral processing applications.

**Key Components of the Skid-Mounted Package:**

The Flocculant Mixer package is delivered complete with all necessary sub-components and auxiliary systems, ensuring a ready-to-integrate solution. These include:

*   **Hopper:** For the storage and controlled feeding of dry flocculant material.
*   **Feeder:** A mechanism designed to accurately dose the flocculant from the hopper into the mixing system.
*   **Disperser:** A device or section dedicated to effectively dispersing the flocculant powder into the liquid, preventing agglomeration and ensuring proper dissolution.
*   **Tank c/w Agitator:** A mixing tank equipped with an agitator to ensure thorough and homogeneous mixing of the flocculant solution.
*   **2 Transfer Pumps:** Pumps responsible for transferring the prepared flocculant solution to its point of use within the process.

**Integrated Systems:**

Beyond the core mechanical components, the package is fully equipped with all essential supporting infrastructure:

*   **All Piping:** Complete internal piping for material transfer, water supply, and solution distribution within the skid.
*   **All Instrumentation:** Comprehensive instrumentation for monitoring and controlling process parameters such as flow, level, and concentration. Refer to [[instrumentation_and_control_design_criteria_8_prc_0002]] for general design criteria.
*   **All Wiring:** Complete electrical wiring for power distribution to motors, instruments, and control devices. Refer to [[electrical_design_and_specification_9_prc_0003]] for electrical design specifications.
*   **Control System:** An integrated control system to manage the operation of the hopper, feeder, disperser, agitator, and pumps, ensuring automated and precise flocculant preparation.
*   **Control Panel:** A dedicated control panel housing the necessary human-machine interface (HMI), controls, and indicators for local operation and monitoring of the package.

The design and specification of the equipment components adhere to general guidelines found in [[equipment_design_and_specification_4_prc_0003]], while piping design follows [[piping_design_and_specification_5_prc_0003]]. This comprehensive approach ensures that the Flocculant Mixer package is a robust, reliable, and easily deployable solution.
