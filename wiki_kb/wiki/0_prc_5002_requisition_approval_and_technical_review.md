# Requisition Approval and Technical Review (0-PRC-5002)

The approval process for a Requisition, as outlined in Section 6 of 0-PRC-5002, is a critical step designed to ensure the technical accuracy and completeness of the procurement documentation.

The primary objectives of Requisition approval are to:
*   **Ensure Technical Correctness:** The approval **must** verify that the textual content of the Requisition is technically accurate and free from errors. This includes all descriptions, specifications, and requirements for the Goods and Services.
*   **Verify Complete Scope of Work:** The approval **must** confirm that the referenced documents collectively provide a comprehensive and complete technical description of the entire Scope of Work. This ensures that all necessary technical details are included for the vendor.
*   **Confirm Complete Technical Documentation:** The approval **must** also ensure that the referenced documents offer a complete technical description of the required Technical Documentation. This covers all drawings, data sheets, manuals, and other technical deliverables expected from the supplier.

The responsible [[assignment_of_project_responsibilities|Discipline Approver]] (0-PRC-2001) is tasked with this crucial review and approval, ensuring that the Requisition is technically sound before it proceeds to the next stages of the procurement process.
