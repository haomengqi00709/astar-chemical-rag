# Procurement Progress Curve

The Procurement Progress Curve visually tracks the cumulative progress of procurement activities throughout the project lifecycle. This curve is an important indicator of material and equipment acquisition status and is referenced in the [[0-rep-2001_monthly_progress_report_format|Monthly Progress Report]].
