# Task-Specific HSE Training

Beyond the initial [[hse_new_employee_orientation]], employees **will** receive specialized training tailored to the specific tasks they are assigned and the equipment they will operate. This ensures competence and safety in all operational aspects.

Key requirements for task-specific training include:
*   **Personal Protective Equipment (PPE):** After the standard safety orientation, employees **will** be trained in the correct use of the [[personal_protective_equipment_policy]] provided for their specific tasks.
*   **Equipment Operation:** Employees **must** possess a recognized training certificate for any equipment they operate. Furthermore, they **must** demonstrate proven capability in the operation of such equipment. Training **will** be provided as necessary to achieve the required level of competence.
*   **New or Critical Tasks:** Employees **will** receive all necessary training for the performance of new tasks or those identified as critical due to their inherent risks.

Other specific safety requirements are addressed through targeted, task- and work-group-directed safety-training programs. The HSE Manager maintains a training inventory to facilitate the identification and provision of training for special needs situations, aligning with the overall [[0-PRC-8016_hse_training_program_overview]].

Key requirements for task-specific training include:
*   After the standard safety orientation, employees **will** be trained to use the personal protection equipment (PPE) provided for the tasks they will be performing.
*   Employees **will** possess a recognized training certificate for any equipment they will be operating.
*   Employees **must** demonstrate capability in the operation of the equipment.
*   Training **will** be provided where necessary to achieve the required degree of competence.
