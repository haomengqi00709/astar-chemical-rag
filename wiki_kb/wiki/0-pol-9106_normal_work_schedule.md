# Normal Work Schedule (0-POL-9106)

This page outlines the standard work schedule for employees, as defined by policy 0-POL-9106. This schedule forms the baseline for understanding both flexible work arrangements and specific site assignments.

**Standard Workday Structure:**
Normal hours of work are from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday through Thursday. This daily schedule includes a 45-minute unpaid lunch break. Consequently, the normal working hours for these days amount to 8.25 hours per day.

On Fridays, the normal hours of work are from 8:00 a.m. to 12:30 p.m., totaling 4.5 hours for the day.

**Normal Workweek and Holiday Adjustments:**
The total normal workweek is 37.5 hours. This total workweek is reduced by 8.25 hours for every local Canadian statutory holiday that occurs within that week.

Employees may also be eligible for [[0-pol-9106_flexible_work_schedule]] arrangements, which deviate from these normal hours, or be subject to distinct [[0-pol-9106_site_work_hours_and_rest_periods]] when assigned to project sites.
