# Equipment Part Numbering (4-PRC-0005)

Equipment Part Numbers (EPN) are used to uniquely identify equipment items within a project, particularly as referenced on [[outline_drawings]]. The detailed procedure for assigning and managing these numbers is outlined in 4-PRC-0005.

**Usage**
Outline Drawings **must** contain Equipment Part Numbers (EPN) as required, ensuring consistent identification across project documentation.

**Reference**
For comprehensive details on the Equipment Part Numbering system, refer to 4-PRC-0005.
