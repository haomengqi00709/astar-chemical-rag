# Mechanical Equipment Checkout List

This document is used to record findings and deficiencies during the [[mechanical_equipment_checkout_procedure|mechanical checkout procedure]] for various equipment types. It serves as a critical record for ensuring compliance with design specifications and identifying necessary corrective actions before equipment is deemed ready for commissioning.
