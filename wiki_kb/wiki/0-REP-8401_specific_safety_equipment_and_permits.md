# Specific Site Safety Equipment and Permits (0-REP-8401)

This page outlines the requirements for specific safety equipment and the availability of necessary permits on site, as specified in document 0-REP-8401.

**Gas Detection**
The client **shall** ensure that functioning area gas detectors are present on site. For specific procedures such as [[confined_space_entry_pre_entry_procedures]], the client **shall** provide portable detectors/analyzers.

**Confined Space Entry Equipment**
The site **shall** possess all necessary equipment required to perform [[0-PRC-8502-A_confined_space_entry_procedure]] safely.

**Emergency Wash Stations**
Safety showers and eyewash stations **shall** be functioning correctly and readily accessible throughout the site.

**Permit Stock**
The site **shall** maintain an adequate stock of various permit tags, including [[safe_work_permit_procedure_overview]] tags, warning tags, and [[DP-802.705-1_confined_space_entry_permit]] forms. This ensures that all necessary work authorizations can be issued promptly and safely.

**Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) and Site Safety Gear**
The site **shall** maintain an adequate reserve of PPE equipment to accommodate visitors. Each Aker Kvaerner Chemetics employee and subcontractor **shall** be equipped with personal protection equipment (PPE), including spare cartridges for their respirator. A site wind sock is **required** and **shall** be visible from all plant exits. Safety showers and eyewash stations **shall** be fully functioning.

**Confined Space Entry and Permits**
The site **shall** possess all necessary equipment to perform confined space entry safely. Furthermore, the site **shall** maintain an adequate stock of work permit tags, warning tags, and confined space entry permits.
