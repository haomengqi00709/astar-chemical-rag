# High Silicon Austenitic Stainless Steel Welding Execution (4-SPC-0025)

**Welding Processes**
Shielded Metal Arc Welding (SMAW) is the preferred method for welding High Silicon Austenitic steel. Other welding processes will be evaluated upon submission of full details, including the procedure and exact location of proposed weldments. Shielded metal arc welding electrodes **shall** be Bohler Fox EAS-2Si or an agreed equivalent.

**Welding Parameters and Heat Control**
Welding **should** be performed in the downhand or flat position where possible, in accordance with DIN 1912 Part 2. Stringer beads are preferred, but weaving of the electrode is permitted up to twice the core diameter. When weaving, the interpass temperature of 120°C **shall not** be exceeded. Heat input during welding **shall** be limited to between 4 and 6 K.J. Preheat is **not required**.

Heat generated by welding **shall** be removed quickly by appropriate cooling measures. Welding under intensive water cooling is recommended for heat elimination, especially for layers on the product side. Discolouration produced by welding **shall** be removed by grinding after each pass.

For root pass welding, other suitable measures may be taken to achieve the fastest cooling, such as compressed air for weld joints without gaps or intensive cool shielding gas for pipe seams. The requirement for effective water cooling applies especially to the layers on the product side. To achieve adequate cooling with running water, the weld material **should not** be too thick (maximum 6 mm). Filling of the groove **shall** be carried out while water cooling from the opposite side. For double-sided grooves with wall thicknesses greater than 6 mm, welding **shall** be performed first on the product side. The thickness of weld metal on the product side **shall not** exceed 6 mm to achieve satisfactory cooling rates. Pipe welds **should**, if possible, be welded by multi-pass welding after depositing the root pass using cooling with running water.

**Arc Strikes and Weld Spatter**
Every effort shall be made to confine arc strikes to surfaces on which weld metal is to be deposited. If an arc strike occurs outside the weld area, it shall be ground out to a maximum depth of 0.012" (0.3mm). The area of an arc strike ground out shall be dye penetrant examined. Burn marks from grounding clamps shall be treated as arc strikes. Weld spatter shall also be treated as arc strikes.
