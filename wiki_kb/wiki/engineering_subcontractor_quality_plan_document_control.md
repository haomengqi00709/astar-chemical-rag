# Engineering Subcontractor Quality Plan: Document Control

Effective document control is a mandatory element of the [[engineering_subcontractor_quality_assurance_0_spc_7004|Engineering Subcontractor Quality Plan]]. The Subcontractor **must** establish and maintain procedures for the control of all documents referenced by the Quality Plan. These documents include, but are not limited to, procedures, instructions, specifications, and drawings.

**Requirements for Controlled Documents:**

*   **Review for Adequacy:** Controlled documents **must** be reviewed for adequacy prior to their release. This ensures that the documents are fit for purpose and accurately reflect current requirements.
*   **Approval for Release:** All controlled documents **must** be approved for release by authorized personnel. This ensures that only approved and validated documents are used.
*   **Appropriate Distribution:** Controlled documents **must** be distributed appropriately to ensure that relevant personnel have access to the correct and most current versions.
*   **Change Control:** Any changes made to controlled documents **must** undergo the same degree of review and approval as the original documents. This maintains the integrity and reliability of the documentation.
*   **Retention:** The Subcontractor **must** retain all documents that furnish evidence of the quality of the services provided. These quality records are essential for demonstrating compliance and for audit purposes.

**Submission and Approval of Referenced Documents:**
The Subcontractor **must** submit all documents referenced by the Quality Plan to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for approval. Furthermore, any changes to these referenced documents **require** approval from Aker Kvaerner Chemetics before they can be implemented. This ensures that all supporting documentation aligns with the overall quality requirements and is acceptable to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
