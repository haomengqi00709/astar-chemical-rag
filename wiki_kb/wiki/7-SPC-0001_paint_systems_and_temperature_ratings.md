# Protective Coating Systems and Temperature Ratings (7-SPC-0001 Table 2)

The `7-SPC-0001 Protective Coating Specification` defines several standard paint systems, each with specific temperature suitability. These systems are to be applied to equipment as specified in [[7-SPC-0001_equipment_coating_schedule]].

**Standard Paint Systems**
The following paint systems are specified for general application:
*   **Paint System 1:** Suitable for temperatures up to 93°C (200°F).
*   **Paint System 2:** Also suitable for temperatures up to 93°C (200°F).
*   **Paint System 3:** Designed for higher temperature applications, suitable for temperatures up to 232°C (450°F).
*   **Paint System 4:** For the highest temperature requirements, suitable for temperatures up to 399°C (750°F).

Details regarding the composition and application of these systems are provided in [[7-SPC-0001_table_2_paint_systems]].

**Paint Manufacturer Approval**
It is a mandatory requirement that paint manufacturers not listed in the approved vendor list **must** receive explicit approval from Aker Kvaerner Chemetics prior to their products being used on the project.

The `7-SPC-0001 Protective Coating Specification` defines several standard paint systems, each with specific temperature suitability. These systems are to be applied to equipment as specified in [[7-SPC-0001_equipment_coating_schedule]].

**Standard Paint Systems**
The following paint systems are specified:
*   Paint Systems 1 and 2 are suitable for temperatures up to 93°C (200°F).
*   Paint System 3 is suitable for temperatures up to 232°C (450°F).
*   Paint System 4 is suitable for temperatures up to 399°C (750°F).

**Coating Characteristics**
Several high temperature coatings are extremely soft until they have reached a temperature of 150°C (300F).

**Approved Manufacturers**
Paint manufacturers not listed in the specification require Aker Kvaerner Chemetics approval.
