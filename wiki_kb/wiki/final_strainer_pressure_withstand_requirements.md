# Final Strainer Pressure Withstand Requirements

This page outlines the mandatory pressure withstand requirements for the Final Strainer (Tag No. 7842K). Adherence to these requirements is critical for the safe operation and structural integrity of the equipment.

**Mandatory Requirements:**
*   The strainer body **must** be able to withstand the deadhead pressure of the associated pump at its rated impeller size.
*   The strainer basket **shall** be able to withstand the maximum allowable pressure drop of 100.0 kPa, as specified in [[final_strainer_sizing_and_performance]].

These requirements ensure that the strainer components are robust enough to handle potential overpressure scenarios and normal operating pressure differentials without failure. The design and material selection, detailed in [[final_strainer_materials_of_construction]], must account for these pressure ratings.
