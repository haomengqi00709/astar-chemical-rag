# Discipline Design and Specification Responsibilities

This page details the specific responsibilities assigned to various design disciplines for the development and specification of project deliverables. These assignments ensure clarity of roles and efficient execution of design tasks, aligning with the overall [[project_organizational_structure_and_responsibilities]].

Key discipline responsibilities include:

### Process Discipline
*   **Flowsheet Development and Approval:** Responsibilities are set out in [[flows_development_and_approval_1_prc_0002]].
*   **P&ID Development and Approval:** Responsibilities are set out in [[p_id_development_and_approval_1_prc_0003]].
*   **Checking of Equipment Data Sheets:** The Process discipline is assigned to check [[equipment_proprietary_detail_drawing_prp]] because Process prepares [[process_and_electrical_data_sheets]], which form the basis for Equipment Data Sheets.

### Civil Discipline
*   **Civil Works Design and Specification:** Responsibilities are set out in [[civil_works_design_and_specification_2_prc_0002]].

### Structural and Architectural Discipline
*   **Structural and Architectural Design and Specification:** Responsibilities are set out in [[structural_and_architectural_design_and_specification_3_prc_0002]].

### Mechanical Discipline
*   **Equipment Design and Specification:** Responsibilities are set out in [[equipment_design_and_specification_4_prc_0003]].
*   **Preparation of Equipment Data Sheets:** Mechanical prepares Equipment Data Sheets, which are based on information contained in Process Data Sheets.

### Piping Discipline
*   **Piping Design and Specification:** Responsibilities are set out in [[piping_design_and_specification_5_prc_0003]].
*   **Line List Development and Approval:** Responsibilities are set out in [[line_list_development_and_approval_5_prc_0004]].

### Insulation Discipline
*   **Insulation Design and Specification:** Responsibilities are set out in [[insulation_design_and_specification_6_prc_0002]].

### Coatings Discipline
*   **Coatings Design and Specification:** Responsibilities are set out in [[coatings_design_and_specification_7_prc_0002]].

### Instrumentation and Control Discipline
*   **Field Instrument Design and Specification:** Responsibilities are set out in [[field_instrument_design_and_specification_8_prc_0003]].
*   **Control System Design and Specification:** Responsibilities are set out in [[control_system_design_and_specification_8_prc_0004]].

### Electrical Discipline
*   **Electrical Design and Specification:** Responsibilities are set out in [[electrical_design_and_specification_9_prc_0003]].

These responsibilities are further supported by the roles of [[discipline_lead_and_approver_roles]] who ensure the quality and compliance of all work.
