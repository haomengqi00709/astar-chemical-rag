# Purchase Order Termination and Financial Settlement (0-SPC-5001)

This page outlines the conditions and financial implications related to the termination of Purchase Order **0-SPC-5001**.

**Termination by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics**
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **may** terminate the Purchase Order for any reason by giving the Vendor written notice.

**Vendor Obligations Upon Termination**
In the event of termination, the Vendor **must** comply with the following requirements:
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **may** instruct the Vendor to assign the benefit of any subcontract or other agreement related to the performance of this Purchase Order to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. See [[subcontractor_obligations_0_spc_2201]].
*   The Vendor **must** Deliver all [[goods_and_services_definitions|Goods and Services]], regardless of their state of completion, if Aker Kvaerner Chemetics has made a payment or part payment in respect of those Goods and Services.

**Amount Due on Termination (Not Vendor Default)**
Where Aker Kvaerner Chemetics terminates the Purchase Order for a reason other than the default of the Vendor, the amount due on termination **shall** include:
*   An amount due assessed as for normal payments, covering work completed up to the termination date.
*   The reasonable cost for Goods and Services to which Aker Kvaerner Chemetics has title and of which the Vendor has to accept delivery.
*   Other costs reasonably incurred by the Vendor in expectation of completing the whole of the Goods and Services, which Aker Kvaerner Chemetics is able to recover from the [[owner_roles|Owner]].
*   A deduction of any unwrapped balance of an advanced payment made to the Vendor.

These provisions ensure a structured and fair financial settlement in the event of termination. For general termination procedures, refer to [[termination_procedures]].
