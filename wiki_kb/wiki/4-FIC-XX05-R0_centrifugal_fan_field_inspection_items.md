# Centrifugal Fan Field Inspection Items (4-FIC-XX05-R0)

This page outlines the mandatory inspection items for centrifugal fans as specified in the Field Inspection Checklist **4-FIC-XX05-R0**. These checks are crucial for ensuring the correct installation, mechanical integrity, and operational readiness of the equipment.

All items listed below **must** be verified and completed before the centrifugal fan is considered ready for further stages, such as commissioning.

**Mandatory Inspection Items:**
*   The correct nameplate **must** be installed.
*   Fan position **must** be checked (elevation, orientation, leveled).
*   Foundation / Anchor bolts **must** be secure and tightened.
*   Grouting **must** be completed.
*   Suction/discharge piping **must** be properly aligned and supported (no stress on blower nozzles).
*   Auxiliary systems **must** be complete (Condensate seals, seal flush, air purge etc.).
*   Fan casing **must** be free of foreign material.
*   Mechanical seal / packing **must** be installed.
*   Vibration monitoring **must** be installed.
*   Accessories **must** be installed (instruments, drain valves etc.).
*   Bearings **must** be flushed and filled to correct level.
*   Free rotation of fan **must** be checked by hand.
*   Grounding **must** be completed.
*   Motor rotation **must** be verified by electrical contractor (attach report).
*   Cold alignment **must** be completed.
*   Coupling **must** be installed and lubricated.
*   Coupling guard **must** be installed and secure.
*   Jacking screws **must** be removed.
*   External painting **must** be completed with no visible defects.
*   Thermal insulation **must** be complete.
*   Accoustic insulation **must** be complete.

This checklist contains a total of 24 items. Any deviations or issues found during these inspections **must** be recorded in the designated deficiency slots within the checklist.
