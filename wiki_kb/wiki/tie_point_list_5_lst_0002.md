# 5-LST-0002 Tie Point List

The 5-LST-0002 Tie Point List is a crucial document for the Chemetics project, specifically defining the mechanical requirements for all pipe connections at the battery limit. This list ensures precise coordination and integration of piping systems at critical interfaces.

**Purpose and Content**
The [[tie_point_list_5_lst_0002]] outlines the essential mechanical requirements for each pipe connection at the battery limit, including:
*   **Size:** The nominal diameter of the pipe connection.
*   **Location:** The exact physical coordinates or reference points for the connection.
*   **Pressure Relief:** Any specific pressure relief considerations or requirements associated with the tie-in point.

**Coordination Requirements**
For any fluid supplied from the battery limit, its mechanical design conditions **shall** be coordinated with the specifications provided in the [[tie_point_list_5_lst_0002]]. This ensures seamless integration and adherence to design parameters at the interface with external systems or other plant sections. This coordination is also highlighted in the [[utility_fluid_design_conditions]].

The [[tie_point_list_5_lst_0002]] is one of five primary documents that collectively define the process and mechanical design conditions for piping and equipment within the plant. For a comprehensive overview of this documentation framework, refer to [[process_and_mechanical_design_document_hierarchy]].
