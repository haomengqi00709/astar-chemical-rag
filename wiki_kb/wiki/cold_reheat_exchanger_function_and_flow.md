# Cold Reheat Exchanger Function and Flow

In double absorption acid plants, the 3:1 arrangement is a common configuration where gas is directed to the intermediate absorption tower after passing through bed 3 of the converter. The cold reheat exchanger plays a dual role in this setup: it cools the gas stream leaving bed 3 and subsequently reheats the gas after it exits the intermediate absorption tower.

**Tube Side Flow:**
*   Gas exiting bed 3 of the converter enters the tube side of the cold reheat exchanger.
*   Within the exchanger, the cold reheat exchanger tube side gas flows downwards, undergoing a cooling process.
*   This cooled gas is then discharged directly to the intermediate absorption tower.

**Shell Side Flow:**
*   Gas leaving the intermediate absorption tower enters the shell side of the cold reheat exchanger.
*   The cold reheat exchanger shell side gas flows upwards, where it is reheated.
*   Upon exiting the shell side, this reheated gas is discharged into the shell side of the [[inter_reheat_exchanger_function_and_flow|inter reheat exchanger]] for further processing.
