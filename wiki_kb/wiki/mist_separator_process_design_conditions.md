# Mist Separator Process Design Conditions

This page details the key process design conditions for the Mist Separator, as specified in Document ID **4-DST-XXXX-R0**, "Template Mist Separator Datasheet". These parameters are fundamental for the accurate sizing, selection, and performance evaluation of the mist separator unit.

**Inlet Gas Conditions:**
The operational environment for the mist separator is defined by the following inlet gas parameters:
*   **Inlet Gas Flow Rate:** 30.0 kg/h. This flow rate is critical for determining the required cross-sectional area of the separator and the velocity of the gas through the mist elimination elements.
*   **Inlet Gas Temperature Range:** 10 ~ 60 °C. The temperature range influences the physical properties of the gas and entrained particles, affecting separation efficiency and material selection.
*   **Inlet Gas Pressure Range:** 400 ~ 650 kPa(g). Operating pressure impacts gas density and volumetric flow, which are crucial for calculating gas velocities within the separator.
*   **Inlet Gas Density:** 1230.0 kg/m3. Gas density is a direct input for fluid dynamic calculations, essential for predicting pressure drop and separation performance.
*   **Inlet Gas Molecular Weight:** 1230.0 kg/kmol. Molecular weight, along with temperature and pressure, helps define the gas properties and behavior within the system.

**Entrained Particles:**
*   **Entrained Particles Flow Rate:** 30.0 kg/h. This parameter quantifies the amount of liquid or solid particles that the mist separator is designed to remove from the gas stream. It directly influences the design of the mist elimination elements and the capacity requirements for liquid drainage.

These process parameters are derived from the overall [[process_design_criteria_1_prc_0001]] for the plant and are typically documented in [[equipment_data_sheets_4_dst_xxxx]]. Accurate [[stream_data_calculation_and_integration]] is vital to ensure that the mist separator is designed for the actual operating conditions, contributing to the overall efficiency and reliability of the process.
