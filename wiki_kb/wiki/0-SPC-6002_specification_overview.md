# Construction Subcontract Tender Administration Overview (0-SPC-6002)

This specification, identified as **0-SPC-6002**, outlines the administrative conditions and procedures for a Proposer to submit an offer to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. The primary purpose of this document is to establish the framework under which potential subcontractors (Proposers) **shall** prepare and submit their tenders for works detailed in a specific [[construction_subcontract_agreement_structure_and_data|Agreement]].

The scope of this specification encompasses the general administrative requirements for the entire tender process, ensuring clarity, fairness, and consistency in the submission and subsequent evaluation of offers for construction subcontract works. It sets the foundational rules for engagement between Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and prospective Proposers, covering aspects from initial communication to the final stages of offer submission. Compliance with the conditions described herein is paramount for any Proposer seeking to engage in a subcontract with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Company Name Change Information**
It is important to note that the company name change to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics was implemented in 2005. For documents related to this specification, no new revision number has been issued specifically for this name change. Consequently, any changes reflecting the name change within the document itself have not been individually marked with revision indicators. Users **shall** be aware that references to "Aker Kvaerner Chemetics" reflect this historical name change and that the content remains valid despite the lack of explicit revision marking for this specific administrative update.

This overview serves as a critical guide to the subsequent detailed requirements for communication protocols, mandatory documentation, offer evaluation criteria, and strict confidentiality measures that Proposers **must** adhere to when participating in the tender process. Strict adherence to these administrative conditions is crucial for a compliant and successful offer submission, facilitating a smooth and transparent procurement process.
