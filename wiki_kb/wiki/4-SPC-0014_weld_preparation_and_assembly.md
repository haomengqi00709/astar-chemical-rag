# Weld Preparation and Assembly for Stabilized Austenitic Stainless Steel (4-SPC-0014)

This page outlines the requirements for weld preparation and assembly of Columbium Stabilized High Nickel Austenitic stainless steel fabrications, as specified in document 4-SPC-0014.

**Welding Environment and Surface Cleanliness**
*   As far as practical, all welds shall be made in the downhand (flat) position. Vertical welds shall be made vertical up (uphand).
*   Suitable enclosures shall be provided to keep welds and welders free from draughts, rain, or other inclement weather.
*   Welding shall not be performed on wet surfaces.
*   Fusion faces and the adjacent metal, both internal and external, shall be free from moisture, scale, paint, grease, or other foreign matter immediately prior to welding.

**Alignment and Misalignment**
*   The alignment of welding edges at butt joints shall be in accordance with relevant codes and specifications.
*   Maximum misalignment (High - Low) for plates up to and including 1/4" in thickness shall be 1/16".
*   Maximum misalignment (High - Low) for plates above 1/4" in thickness shall be 25% of the plate thickness or 1/8", whichever is smaller.
*   Alignment for pipe shall be in accordance with [[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping]].
*   Maximum misalignment (High - Low) for Converters and Gas/Gas Exchangers shall be 20% of the plate thickness or 1/16" (1.5mm), whichever is smaller.
*   Prior to welding a slip-on flange, the sum of the gaps on any diameter shall not exceed 3/16" (5mm).

**Holding Parts and Jigs**
*   Parts to be welded shall be securely held in position by mechanical means, welded-on bridge pieces, or by tack welding to maintain specified alignment and gap.
*   Construction of a jig or other suitable device shall allow adequate access for welding and cleaning.
*   Construction of a jig shall be such that external loading is not applied to a joint during welding.
*   Welded-on bridge or support spiders shall be of austenitic steel on the surface welded in contact with the vessel or heat exchanger.
*   The plate surface shall not be left in a damaged condition after welded-on bridge pieces or support spiders are removed.
*   Reinforcing pads and other shaped items shall closely conform to their matching curvature.

**Tack Welds**
*   Tack welds shall be performed by [[ASME_section_ix_welding_and_brazing_qualifications]] code qualified welders.
*   Tack welds employed for assembly purposes may form part of the completed weld, provided that complete fusion is effected at the root of the weld.
*   Tack welds employed for assembly purposes may form part of the completed weld, provided that the welding procedure used is the same as that employed for the remainder of the root run.
*   Cracked and unsound tack welds shall be cut out prior to welding.
*   Sound tack welds must be thoroughly fused into the root run of the main weld.
*   Tack welds should preferably be at least one inch long when thinning down ends is necessary to ensure complete fusion.

**Back-up Rings and Inserts**
The use of permanent back-up rings or strips or consumable joint inserts is not permitted unless specifically approved in writing by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics QA & Materials Group.

**Arc Strikes**
*   Every effort shall be made to confine arc strikes to surfaces on which weld metal is to be deposited.
*   If an arc strike occurs outside the deposition area, the spot will require to be ground.
*   If an arc strike occurs outside the deposition area, the spot will require to be PT examined per Para 10.3 (refer to [[4-SPC-0014_inspection_and_testing_requirements]]).
*   If an arc strike occurs outside the deposition area, the spot will require to be repaired, if necessary.
*   Burn marks from grounding clamps shall be treated as arc strikes.

**Alignment Tolerances**
*   For Converters and Gas/Gas Exchangers, maximum misalignment (High - Low) shall be 20% of the plate thickness or 1/16" (1.5mm), whichever is smaller.
*   Prior to welding a slip-on flange, the sum of the gaps on any diameter shall not exceed 3/16" (5mm).

**Assembly and Fixturing**
*   Reinforcing pads and other shaped items shall closely conform to their matching curvature.

**Tack Welds**
*   It is sometimes necessary to thin down the ends of tack welds to ensure that complete fusion is achieved.
