# Gas/Gas Heat Exchanger Mechanical Calculations Procedure Overview

This procedure, identified as document ID 4-PRC-0009, outlines the specific requirements for performing mechanical calculations for acid plant radial flow gas/gas heat exchangers. It is crucial for ensuring the structural integrity and operational safety of these critical components.

**Scope and Applicability**
This procedure applies exclusively to external gas/gas heat exchangers. Exchangers that are internal to the converter are governed by a separate, distinct procedure.

**Confidentiality**
The contents of this procedure are strictly confidential. Duplication of this document is prohibited, and its contents must not be divulged to individuals outside the employ of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Administrative Notes**
The name change to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics has been implemented within this document. However, no new revision number has been assigned for this administrative change, and the changes related to the name change have not been specifically marked.
