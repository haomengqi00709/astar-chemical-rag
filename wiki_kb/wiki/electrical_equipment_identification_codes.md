# Electrical Equipment Identification Codes

This page provides a detailed list of standard identification codes for various types of electrical equipment, as outlined in procedure 9-PRC-0001. These codes are used in conjunction with the [[electrical_equipment_identification_numbering]] system to ensure consistent and clear identification across all project documentation.

The "Process Area" for electrical distribution equipment is designated as `99`. The following categories and their corresponding identification codes are used:

**High Voltage (HV) Equipment (990XE)**

*   **9901E:** HV Power Transformer
*   **9902E:** HV Outdoor Disconnect Switch
*   **9903E:** HV Switchgear
*   **9908E:** HV Harmonic Filter
*   **9909E:** HV Power Factor Correction

**Medium Voltage (MV) Equipment (991XE)**

*   **9910E:** MV Power Transformer
*   **9911E:** MV Switchgear
*   **9912E:** MV Motor Control Cent. (MCC)
*   **9917E:** MV Variable Frequency Drive
*   **9919E:** MV Power Factor Correction

**Low Voltage (LV) Equipment (<1000V) (992XE)**

*   **9920E:** LV Power Transformer
*   **9921E:** LV Power Distribution Cent.
*   **9922E:** LV Power Distribution Panel
*   **9923E:** LV Motor Control Cent (MCC) – Normal Power
*   **9924E:** LV Motor Control Cent (MCC) – Emergency Power
*   **9925E:** LV Emergency Generator
*   **9926E:** LV Emergency Generator Transfer Switch
*   **9927E:** LV Variable Frequency Drive
*   **9928E:** L.V. Cable Bu.
*   **9929E:** LV Power Factor Correction

**Utility, Lighting, and Instrument Power Equipment (993XE)**

*   **9930E:** Utility Power and Lighting Distribution Transformer
*   **9931E:** Utility Power and Lighting Distribution Panel
*   **9932E:** Instrument Power Distribution Transformer
*   **9933E:** Instrument Power Distribution Panel
*   **9934E:** Un-interruptible Power Supply
*   **9935E:** DC Control Power Supply
*   **9936E:** DC Control Power Distribution Panel

**Electrolytic Power Supply Equipment (994XE)**

*   **9940E:** Electrolytic Power Supply Transformer
*   **9941E:** Electrolyser Power Supply Auto-transformer
*   **9942E:** Electrolytic Power Supply Rectifier
*   **9943E:** Electrolytic Power Supply Bus Bars
*   **9944E:** Electrolytic Power Supply Current Transformer
*   **9945E:** Electrolytic Power Supply Current Shunt
*   **9946E:** Electrolytic Power Supply DC Disconnect Switch
*   **9947E:** Electrolyser Shorting Switch
*   **9948E:** Electrolyser Shorting Cell
*   **9949E:** Electrolyser Cathodic Protection Unit

**DC Motor Power Supply Equipment (995XE)**

*   **9950E:** DC Motor Power Supply Transformer
*   **9951E:** DC Motor Power Supply Rectifier
*   **9952E:** DC Motor Power Supply Battery Bank
*   **9953E:** DC Motor Power Distribution Panel

**CEC Cell Equipment (996XE)**

*   **9960E:** Polarization Rectifier

Standard serial numbers and electrical equipment names are available on the appended list.
