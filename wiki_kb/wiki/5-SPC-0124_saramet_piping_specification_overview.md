# SARAMET Piping Fabrication and NDE Specification (5-SPC-0124)

This specification, identified as 5-SPC-0124, defines the minimum requirements set by [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_company_details]] for the fabrication and associated Non-Destructive Examination (NDE) procedures of piping systems.

**Scope**
The specification applies to piping systems constructed from SARAMET 23 (UNS S30601) and SARAMET 35 (UNS S32615) stainless steels.

**Compliance**
Fabrication and testing of SARAMET piping systems **shall** be in accordance with [[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping]] (latest edition), unless explicitly stated otherwise within this document. Requirements for Normal Fluid Service, as defined by ASME B31.3, will apply.
