# Acid Resistant Membrane and Brick Lining Standard Overview

This Engineering Standard, identified as **4-SPC-0106**, outlines the minimum technical requirements for the installation of acid resistant membrane and brick lining systems specifically for acid plant equipment. Adherence to these specifications is critical for ensuring the longevity, operational integrity, and safety of equipment in highly corrosive environments.

**Scope and Responsibilities:**
The standard focuses exclusively on technical specifications and does not encompass minimum safety requirements for the lining installation. The installer **must** assume complete and unequivocal responsibility for all safety aspects of the work, including but not limited to personnel safety, site conditions, and adherence to all applicable safety regulations.

Furthermore, the installer **shall** be responsible for:
*   The overall quality of the installation and workmanship, ensuring that all procedures are carried out to the highest industry standards.
*   Ensuring full compliance with all design documentation provided, including drawings, specifications, and any supplementary instructions.

It is important to note that the review of drawings or acceptance through inspections by the firm does not relieve the supplier(s) from any of their obligations as defined by this standard. The ultimate responsibility for meeting all technical and quality requirements rests with the installer.
