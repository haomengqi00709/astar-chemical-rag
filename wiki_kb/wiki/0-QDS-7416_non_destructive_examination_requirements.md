# Non-Destructive Examination Requirements (0-QDS-7416)

For the Plate & Frame Heat Exchanger project (**0-QDS-7416**), all Non-Destructive Examination (NDE) activities **shall** be performed in accordance with the **ASME Boiler & Pressure Vessel Code Sections V & VIII**, or an approved equivalent standard.

This requirement ensures that all NDE procedures, personnel qualifications, and acceptance criteria meet recognized industry standards for pressure equipment. For general NDE requirements, refer to [[0-QDS-7411_non_destructive_examination_requirements]].
