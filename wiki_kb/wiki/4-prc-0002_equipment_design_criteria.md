# Equipment Design Criteria (4-PRC-0002)

The 4-PRC-0002 document, titled "Equipment Design Criteria," serves as a foundational reference for all equipment design within a project. It **must** contain a comprehensive set of requirements and guidelines to ensure compliance, safety, and operational efficiency.

**Content Elements**
The Equipment Design Criteria **must** contain the following elements, as applicable:

*   **Statutory Requirements:** All relevant legal and regulatory mandates that govern equipment design and operation.
*   **Owner Requirements:** Specific contractual requirements stipulated by the project owner.
*   **Aker Kvaerner Chemetics General Requirements:** The company's overarching standards and best practices for equipment design.
*   **References to Specifications:** Pointers to appropriate Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Specifications that provide detailed technical requirements.

**Document Preparation and Approval**
The preparation, checking, and approval of the Equipment Design Criteria follow a defined workflow:

*   **Preparation:** The document **must** be prepared by Mechanical personnel.
*   **Checking:** The document **must** be checked by the Project Engineer.
*   **Approval:** Final approval **must** be granted by the Chief Mechanical Engineer.

This document works in conjunction with other design and specification procedures to ensure a consistent and compliant approach to equipment engineering.

**Responsibilities**
*   **Prepared by:** Mechanical
*   **Checked by:** Project Engineer
*   **Approved by:** Chief Mechanical Engineer
