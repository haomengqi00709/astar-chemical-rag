# Acid Tower Distributor Material Verification and Certifications (0-QDS-7413)

This page details the requirements for material verification and certifications for the Acid Tower Distributor, as specified in Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7413. Adherence to these requirements ensures the use of correct and certified materials in the equipment's construction.

**Material Verification:**
*   Material Verification is a listed activity, ensuring that all materials used conform to the specified standards.

**Certification Requirements:**
*   **Mill Test Certificates:** These certificates **require** a Quality Record designation of FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package) and Chemetics Vancouver surveillance (Witness).
*   **Weld Consumable Certificates:** These certificates **require** a Quality Record designation of FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package) and Chemetics Vancouver surveillance (Witness).

The "FP" designation means these documents **must** be included in the final [[0-QDS-7413_quality_record_definitions|Quality Assurance Documentation Package]]. Chemetics Vancouver's witness surveillance ensures compliance during the certification process. These requirements are integral to the overall [[0-QDS-7413_vendor_quality_assurance_requirements|vendor quality assurance requirements]] for the Acid Tower Distributor.
