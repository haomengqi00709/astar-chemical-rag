# Conveyors & Mills Packing and Shipping Requirements

This page outlines the mandatory packing and shipping requirements for Conveyors & Mills, as specified in Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7426. Adherence to these requirements ensures the safe and documented delivery of equipment.

**Packaging**
*   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Chemetics surveillance for Packaging is designated as Witness. A Chemetics representative **shall** be present to observe the packaging process to ensure it meets specified standards for protection during transit.

**Packing List**
*   **Requirement:** The Packing List is required as AP (As Prepared). This document **shall** be prepared by the vendor and accurately detail all items included in the shipment.
*   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Chemetics surveillance for the Packing List is designated as Witness. A Chemetics representative **shall** verify the accuracy and completeness of the packing list against the actual shipment.

For definitions of quality record statuses like AP, refer to [[0-QDS-7426_quality_record_definitions]]. For general packing and shipping guidelines, refer to [[packaging_land_transportation_0_spc_5004]] and [[packaging_marine_transportation_0_spc_5005]].

**Packing List**
*   **Requirement:** A Packing List is required as AP (As Prepared).
