# Shell and Tube Heat Exchanger Datasheet Units of Measurement

This page lists the standard units of measurement used within the Template S & T Heat Exchanger - Vdr Package Datasheet (4-DST-XXXX-R0). Consistent application of these units is essential for accurate data entry, calculations, and communication across engineering disciplines. For general engineering units, refer to [[engineering_units]]. The structure and columns of data sheets are further detailed in [[0_dst_0_data_sheet_structure_and_columns]].

**Process and Performance Units:**
*   **Heat Duty unit:** kW
*   **Total Flowrate unit:** kg/h
*   **Temperature unit:** oC
*   **Pressure unit:** kPa(abs)
*   **Liquid Flow unit:** kg/h
*   **Density unit:** kg/m3
*   **Viscosity unit:** cP
*   **Specific Heat unit:** kJ/kg·oC
*   **Thermal Conductivity unit:** W/m·oC
*   **Bubble Point unit:** oC
*   **Enthalpy unit:** kJ/kg
*   **Vapour Flow unit:** kg/h
*   **Vapour Density unit:** kg/m3
*   **Vapour Viscosity unit:** cP
*   **Vapour Specific Heat unit:** kJ/kg·oC
*   **Vapour Thermal Conductivity unit:** W/m·oC
*   **Molecular Weight unit:** kg/kgmol
*   **Dew Point unit:** oC
*   **Non-Condensable Flow unit:** kg/h
*   **Non-Condensable Density unit:** kg/m3
*   **Non-Condensable Viscosity unit:** cP
*   **Non-Condensable Specific Heat unit:** kJ/kg·oC
*   **Non-Condensable Thermal Conductivity unit:** W/m·oC
*   **Non-Condensable Molecular Weight unit:** kg/kgmol
*   **Excess Area unit:** %

**Design Condition Units:**
*   **Design Internal Pressure (shell) unit:** kPa(g)
*   **Design Vacuum (shell) unit:** kPa(g)
*   **Design Max. Temperature (shell) unit:** °C
*   **Design Min. Temperature (shell) unit:** °C
*   **Design Internal Pressure (tube) unit:** kPa(g)
*   **Design Vacuum (tube) unit:** kPa(g)
*   **Design Max. Temperature (tube) unit:** °C
*   **Design Min. Temperature (tube) unit:** °C
*   **Corrosion Allowance (shellside) unit:** mm
*   **Corrosion Allowance (tubeside) unit:** mm

These units are standardized to ensure clarity and accuracy in all technical documentation related to shell and tube heat exchangers.
