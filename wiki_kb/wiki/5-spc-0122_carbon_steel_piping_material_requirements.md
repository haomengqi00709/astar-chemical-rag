# Carbon Steel Piping Material Requirements (5-SPC-0122)

This section outlines the mandatory requirements for material selection, identification, and traceability for carbon steel piping fabrication, as specified in document 5-SPC-0122.

**Material Control and Identification**
*   **Mandatory:** No deviation from the specified or approved type, grade, or class of material shall be permitted without the written approval of [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_quality_assurance_and_materials_engineering_group|Aker Kvaerner Chemetics]].
*   **Mandatory:** The Fabricator must use an effective system to identify different materials and prevent substitutions.
*   **Mandatory:** All materials must show some form of visual identification, such as stenciling or stamping.
*   Seamless grade of piping is preferred.

**Specific Material Grades**
*   Steam piping will be [[ASTM_a106|ASTM A106]] or [[ASTM_a53_seamless|A53]] unless otherwise requested.

**Material Certification and Traceability**
*   When material certification is specified, or when piping is in accordance with [[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping]], **mandatory:** all materials, including weld consumables, shall be identified and traceable to original mill certification.

**Marking for Cryogenic Service**
*   Carbon steel piping intended for cryogenic service (design temperature -20° F or less) shall be clearly marked or painted for easy on-site identification.
