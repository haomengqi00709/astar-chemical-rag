# Piping Material Selection and Protection

**Material and Component Selection**
Material and component selection **must** adhere to the guidelines outlined in the Piping Service Index (1-PRC-xxxx) and [[5-PRC-0003_piping_design_and_specification]].

**Corrosion Protection**
A corrosion protection system **must** be applied where the rate of external corrosion is assessed to compromise piping integrity over the Plant's design life.

**UV Protection for Plastic Piping**
*   PVC, CPVC, and FRP plastic piping **must** incorporate UV (ultraviolet light) protection through suitable internal pigment.
*   Polypropylene (PP) pipe **must** be painted for UV protection.
*   PVDF pipe **must** be supplied with internal pigment if required to protect liquid contents from UV degradation.
*   Consideration **should** be given to using CPVC instead of PVC in situations where solar gain could elevate the pipe's temperature beyond its design temperature/pressure rating.
*   The specifics of plastic pipe UV protection for Section 4.2 **must** be verified.

**Dissimilar Materials**
Insulating flange sets **must** be provided between dissimilar materials as required to prevent galvanic corrosion.

**Standardization**
The design of piping **must** prioritize the rationalization of pipe material classes and valves. ANSI type flanges **should** be used wherever possible to promote standardization.

**Material and Component Selection**
Material and component selection **must** adhere to the guidelines outlined in the Piping Service Index (1-PRC-xxxx) and [[5-PRC-0003_piping_design_and_specification]]. ANSI type flanges will be used wherever possible. Insulating flange sets will be provided between dissimilar materials as required. Install flat face flanges against flat face white cast iron valves and equipment.

**Corrosion Protection**
A corrosion protection system **must** be applied where the rate of external corrosion is assessed to affect the integrity of the piping over the design life of the Plant.

**UV Protection for Plastic Piping**
*   PVC, CPVC, and FRP plastic piping will have UV (ultraviolet light) protection by way of suitable internal pigment.
*   PP pipe will be painted for UV protection.
*   PVDF pipe will be supplied with internal pigment if required to protect liquid contents from UV.
*   Consideration will be given to using CPVC instead of PVC where solar gain will increase the temperature of the pipe above the design temperature/pressure rating.
*   The heating effect of sunlight on stagnant lines will be considered.

**Specific Material Restrictions**
Teflon tape will not be used on natural gas piping.
