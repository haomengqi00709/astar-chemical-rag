# Control Valves - General

Control valves are essential for modulating process flows and pressures. Their selection, sizing, and material specifications are critical for optimal plant performance and safety.

**Performance and Sizing:**
*   Valve seat leakage **will be** equal to or better than **ANSI B16.104, Class IV**, unless otherwise specified, ensuring tight shut-off capabilities.
*   Data sheets **will** specify service conditions, fluid handled, required rangeability, allowable leakage, allowable noise, and any other special requirements.
*   Control valves **will be** sized to meet the duty and provide a turndown ratio of approximately 10:1. Sizing **will be** in accordance with the manufacturer's recommendations.
*   Valve selection and sizing **must be** done in conjunction to obtain optimum results and ensure cavitation and flashing are minimized or eliminated.
*   **Operating Cv Conditions:**
    *   During maximum flow conditions, modulating control valves **should** operate at approximately 75% of maximum Cv.
    *   During normal flow conditions, modulating control valves **should** operate at approximately 50% of maximum Cv.
    *   During minimum flow conditions, modulating control valves **should** operate at approximately 10% of maximum Cv.
*   It is preferred that no valve **will be** more than one line size smaller than the size of the line.

**Installation and Layout:**
*   Valve bodies **will** have an arrow to indicate the direction of process fluid flow.
*   The need for upstream and downstream block and bypass valves **will be** evaluated during detail design. In general, the bypass arrangement **will not** be provided.

**Valve Types and Materials:**
*   **Globe Valves:** Preferred on clean service up to 100 NB.
*   **Teflon Lined Valves:** **Will be** provided where required for control valves, especially for corrosive services.
*   **Eccentric Disk Valves:** For sizes greater than 80 NB for clean service and slurries with less than 5% solids.
*   **Vee Ball Valves:** Used where a wide range of Cv is required.
*   **Corrosive/Erosive Service:** For corrosive and erosive service, manufacturers' recommendations for control valves **should be** followed.
*   **Toxic/Explosive/Valuable Fluids:** For toxic, explosive, and valuable fluids, "environmental type" zero emission valve stem seals **should be** considered.
*   **Trim and Body Style:** Control valve trim and body style **will be** determined by the application.
*   **End Connections:** All valve body end connections **will be** in accordance with the piping specification.
*   **Body Material:** Valve body material **will generally be** carbon steel. Cast iron **will not** be used for valve bodies.
*   **Trim Material:** Valve trim **will generally be** 316 SS. More suitable valve body/trim material is required if process or environmental conditions dictate.

**Packing:**
*   Packing for operating temperatures up to 230 Deg. C **will be** Teflon composition (non-asbestos base).
*   Packing for operating temperatures above 230 Deg. C **will be** as per the valve manufacturer's recommendation.

**Cavitation and Noise Control:**
*   An appropriate means of cavitation control **will be** used to eliminate cavitation and/or flashing, where necessary.
*   Noise abatement techniques **will be** used, where required, to minimize noise levels generated by control valves within the limits permitted by applicable codes.
*   The maximum acceptable noise level for control valves under normal operation **will be** in accordance with **National Standard for Occupational Noise (NOHSC:1007-1993)**. This level **will not** exceed 82 dB(A) at 1 meter in all directions under all conditions from zero to full flow.

**Nameplates:**
Typical information on control valve nameplates includes:
*   Manufacturer
*   Model
*   Serial number
*   Air supply pressure range
*   Valve design temperature and pressure
*   Body size and material
*   Trim size and material
*   Trim characteristic
*   Cv
*   Actuator spring range and bench set
