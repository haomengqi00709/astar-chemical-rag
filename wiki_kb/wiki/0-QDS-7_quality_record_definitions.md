# Converter Shop Quality Record Definitions and Requirements (0-QDS-7401)

This page outlines the definitions for quality record requirements and specifies the certificates mandated for the Converter Shop project as per Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7401.

**Quality Record Definitions:**
*   **AP ("As Prepared"):** Indicates that the quality record is required to be prepared by the vendor.
*   **FP ("Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package"):** Indicates that the quality record **must** be included as part of the final Quality Assurance Documentation Package.

For a broader understanding of quality record definitions, refer to [[0-QDS-7_quality_record_definitions]].

**Required Certificates and Chemetics Surveillance:**

The following certificates are mandatory quality records for the Converter Shop project:

*   **Mill Test Certificates:**
    *   **Requirement:** Mill Test Certificates are required (AP + FP).
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Complete.

*   **Weld Consumable Certificates:**
    *   **Requirement:** Weld consumable certificates are required (FP).
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Complete.

*   **Head Forming Certificates:**
    *   **Requirement:** Head forming certificates are required.
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Complete.

**Certificates Required:**
*   **Head Forming Certificates:** Required.
