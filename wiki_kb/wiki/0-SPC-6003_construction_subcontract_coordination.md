# Construction Subcontract Coordination (0-SPC-6003)

This page describes the requirements for Construction Subcontract Coordination as outlined in document 0-SPC-6003. The Subcontractor **must** comply with these requirements to ensure effective communication, interface management, and overall coordination of the works under the construction subcontract.
