# Mechanical Checkout Equipment (4-PRC-0004)

This document, 4-PRC-0004, outlines the procedure for conducting mechanical checkout inspections and tests for equipment within the Project. It details the steps to ensure that all equipment has been installed correctly and is ready for commissioning.

Inspections and tests for [[mechanical_completion_testing_and_defect_management]] are conducted in accordance with this procedure. It includes associated checklists to record the completion of these critical checks.
