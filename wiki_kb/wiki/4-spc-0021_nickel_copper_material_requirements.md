# Nickel/Copper Material Requirements (4-SPC-0021)

For fabrications involving nickel/copper (UNS N04400 / Monel 400) under **4-SPC-0021**, strict adherence to material specifications is mandatory.

**Base Materials:**
*   All base materials used **shall** be as specified in the purchase order and referenced documents.
*   All nickel/copper products **shall** be purchased in the annealed condition.
*   Nickel/copper products **shall** satisfy the requirements of [[ASME_bpvc_section_ii_materials|ASME SB 127, 163, 164, and 165]].

**Attachments:**
*   External or internal attachments **shall** be of the same material specification as the parent material. Any deviation from this requirement **must** be agreed upon in writing by the [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_company_details|Aker Kvaerner Chemetics]] QA & Materials Engineering Group.

**Welding Consumables:**
*   All weld filler metals **shall** meet the requirements of the [[ASME_section_ix_welding_and_brazing_qualifications|ASME Code]].
*   Nickel/copper materials **shall** be welded with coated electrode #190 and/or filler wire #60 supplied by International Nickel Co.

**Material Certification:**
*   Certification showing analysis and test results in accordance with the relevant material specification **shall** be supplied for all materials, including welding consumables.
*   The fabricator **must** maintain full traceability of materials back to original mill certification.
*   The fabricator's internal controls **shall** prevent the use of unspecified materials.
*   Lack of adequate identification or certification **shall** be cause for rejection of the specified component or item.
