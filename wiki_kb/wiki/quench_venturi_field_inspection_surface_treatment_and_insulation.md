# Quench Venturi Field Inspection - Surface Treatment and Insulation Requirements

This page outlines the mandatory requirements for surface treatment (painting and lining) and insulation for the Quench Venturi, as specified in the Field Inspection Checklist **4-FIC-XX21-R0**. Proper application of these treatments is crucial for corrosion protection, thermal efficiency, and the longevity of the equipment.

**Mandatory Surface Treatment and Insulation Checks:**

*   **Internal Painting and/or Lining Completion:** Any specified internal painting and/or lining **must** be completed according to design specifications. Internal coatings provide critical corrosion resistance against the process fluids, which are often highly corrosive in a Quench Venturi scrubber. The integrity of this lining directly impacts the operational lifespan of the vessel.
*   **Inspection Reports for Internal Painting and/or Lining:** Inspection reports verifying the completion and quality of the internal painting and/or lining **must** be attached to the inspection documentation. These reports typically include details on surface preparation, coating thickness, adhesion tests, and any detected defects, ensuring that the application meets the required standards.
*   **External Painting Completion:** External painting **must** be completed. This provides protection against atmospheric corrosion, UV degradation, and contributes to the aesthetic appearance of the plant. The paint system used **must** comply with project specifications for durability and environmental resistance.
*   **Insulation and Cladding Completion:** Insulation and cladding **must** be complete. Insulation is essential for maintaining process temperatures, preventing heat loss or gain, and ensuring energy efficiency. Cladding protects the insulation from mechanical damage, weather, and moisture ingress. The installation **must** be continuous and free from gaps or damage that could compromise its effectiveness.

These requirements ensure that the Quench Venturi is adequately protected from its operating environment, both internally and externally, and operates with optimal thermal performance. Any deficiencies **must** be recorded and rectified before final acceptance.
