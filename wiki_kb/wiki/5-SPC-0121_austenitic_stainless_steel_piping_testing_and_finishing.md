# Testing and Finishing Requirements for Austenitic Stainless Steel Piping (5-SPC-0121)

This section details the mandatory requirements for dimensional tolerances, pre-shipping inspection, pressure testing, surface treatment, and marking of austenitic stainless steel piping.

**Dimensional Tolerances and Pre-Shipping Inspection:**
*   Dimensional tolerances for individual spool pieces **must** maintain the accuracy of the overall line as specified on the [[pipe_isometric|isometric drawing]]. The normal dimensional tolerance for overall line accuracy is ± 1/4".
*   The fabricator **must** inspect finished spools prior to shipping. This inspection **must** check materials, workmanship, finish, flange protection, and loading.
*   The fabricator **must** ensure that where specified, MSS type lap joint stub ends have been installed, and that rolled angle stub ends are not installed in their place.
*   If the purchase order specifies "Inspection Required Prior To Shipping", adequate notice **must** be given to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics to allow scheduling of inspection without delaying shipping. A formal release **must** be obtained prior to shipping if "Inspection Required Prior To Shipping" is specified.

**Pressure Testing:**
*   Where pressure testing is required, it **must** be conducted in the field.
*   Pressure testing **must** be conducted in accordance with [[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping|ASME B31.3]], paragraph 345.4, unless otherwise specified.
*   Potable water **must** be used for pressure testing. This potable water **must** have less than 50 ppm chlorides.
*   Piping **must** be drained upon completion of the pressure test.
*   Piping **must** be dried upon completion of the pressure test.
*   The test pressure for each line **will** normally be specified by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
*   All lines **must** be drained and blown dry.
*   If piping supports are not capable of handling flooded weights, a pneumatic soap test **must** be performed in accordance with [[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping|ASME B31.3]] paragraph 345.5.
*   All leaks **must** be rectified.

**Surface Treatment and Marking:**
*   Passivation is performed when specified.
*   Each spool piece **must** have its identity number painted on it. Identity numbers on spool pieces **must** be 1" or larger where possible.
*   Paper or cardboard tags **must not** be used. Tags attached by cord or string **must not** be used.
*   Carbon steel back-up flanges **must** be given a coat of zinc-based epoxy shop primer unless otherwise specified. This primer **must** be applied after any pickling.

**Packaging and Shipping Precautions:**
*   The fabricator **must** take all necessary precautions prior to shipping. These precautions include plugging openings, protecting flange faces, and using shipping supports.
