# Pipe Support Installation Guidelines

The correct installation of pipe supports is essential for maintaining the structural integrity of piping systems, managing loads, and accommodating thermal expansion and contraction.

**Compliance with Drawings and Specifications:**
The supporting of piping **will** be performed in strict accordance with the [[piping_general_arrangement_drawings|general arrangement drawings]], the piping isometrics, the [[pipe_support_specification|Specification for Pipe Supports (5-SPC-0005)]], and the [[field_supporting_of_pipe|Specification for Field Supporting of Pipe (5-SPC-0006)]].

**Engineered vs. Guide Supports:**
Pipe supports have been engineered for all lines of 80 mm (3 inches) and larger. For smaller lines, pipe supports have been indicated on the piping isometrics as a guide to the Piping Subcontractor.

**Installation Sequence:**
Wherever possible, pipe supports, hangers, and guides **should** be installed before the piping itself is erected. Temporary supports **will** be used as required to facilitate safe and accurate piping installation.

**Additional Supports:**
The Piping Subcontractor **should** anticipate and install additional pipe supports not explicitly shown on piping isometrics where required, or as identified and accepted by Kvaerner Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' site representative. These additional supports are particularly crucial to safely accommodate the increased loads experienced during [[piping_hydraulic_test_procedures|hydrostatic testing]].

**Compliance with Drawings and Specifications (Continued)**
The supporting of piping **will** be done in accordance with the Specification for Pipe Supports, 5-SPC-0005, and the Specification for Field Supporting of Pipe, 5-SPC-0006.

**Engineered and Guide Supports**
Pipe supports have been engineered for all lines of 80 mm (3") and larger. For smaller lines, pipe supports have been indicated on the piping isometrics as a guide to the Piping Subcontractor.

**Installation Sequence and Additional Supports**
Pipe supports, hangers, guides, etc. should be installed before piping is erected wherever possible. Temporary support **will** be used where required. The Piping Subcontractor should anticipate and install additional pipe supports not shown on piping isometrics where required and/or as identified or accepted by Kvaerner Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' site representative. Additional pipe supports are particularly required to accept loads during hydrostatic testing.
