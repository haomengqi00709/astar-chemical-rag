# Skirt Supported Vessel - Anchor Bolt and Nut Material (4-SCR-0007)

This page specifies the required materials for anchor bolts and nuts used in the installation of the skirt-supported vessel, as detailed in document [[4-SCR-0007_document_administration]]. Proper material selection is crucial for ensuring the long-term structural integrity and corrosion resistance of the vessel's foundation.

**Material Specifications:**
*   **Anchor Bolt Material:** The specific material for the anchor bolts is defined.
*   **Nut Material:** The specific material for the nuts is defined.

These material specifications **must** comply with relevant project standards and codes for [[material_selection]] and [[anchor_bolt_installation_requirements]]. The choice of materials will depend on the environmental conditions and the design life of the equipment.
