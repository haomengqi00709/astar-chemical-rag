# Metal Butterfly Damper Shafting and Bearings

The shafting and bearing system for metal butterfly dampers are critical components ensuring smooth and reliable operation.

**Shafting Material:**
Each damper **SHALL** be supplied with shafting constructed from 316 stainless steel, providing corrosion resistance and durability in demanding environments.

**Bearings:**
The damper design **SHALL** incorporate self-aligning outboard anti-friction bearings. These bearings are essential for accommodating potential shaft deflections and ensuring efficient operation.

**Grease Insertion System:**
Support bearings **SHALL** be complete with 316 stainless steel tubing and fittings specifically designed for grease insertion.
*   This tubing **SHALL** be routed to an accessible location on the damper body.
*   The routing **MUST** ensure the connections are external to any insulation, or as close to external as practical, to allow for easy maintenance and lubrication.
*   Bearings **SHALL** be greased with Rhotium HT-1000 synthetic grease, a high-performance lubricant suitable for the specified operating conditions.
