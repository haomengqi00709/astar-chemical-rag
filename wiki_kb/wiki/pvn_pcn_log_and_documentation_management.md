# PVN Log Procedure

A Project Variance Notice (PVN) Log is a critical tool for tracking and managing deviations, changes, or variances from the established project baseline. The procedure for managing this log is detailed in Appendix III, identified by document ID **0-PRC-4003-R1**, and titled "PVN Log — Procedure".

This procedure establishes the framework for the systematic recording, review, and control of all project variances, ensuring that all stakeholders are informed and that impacts on scope, schedule, and cost are properly assessed and approved. Effective PVN log management is essential for maintaining project control and facilitating informed decision-making throughout the project lifecycle.

Key aspects typically covered by the PVN Log Procedure include:

*   **Program Objectives and Requirements**: Outlining the overall goals and mandatory requirements for the PVN/PCN program. Refer to [[pvn_pcn_program_objectives_and_requirements]] for detailed objectives.
*   **Form Initiation and Details**: Describing the process for initiating a PVN/PCN form, including the required information and documentation. See [[pvn_pcn_form_initiation_and_details]] for specifics.
*   **Types and Impacts**: Categorizing different types of variances and their potential impacts on the project. Further information is available in [[pvn_pcn_types_and_impacts]].
*   **Impact Analysis and Evaluation**: Procedures for conducting a thorough analysis of the technical, schedule, and cost impacts of a proposed variance. Details can be found in [[pvn_pcn_impact_analysis_and_evaluation]].
*   **Approval Process**: Defining the workflow and authorization levels required for the approval of PVNs/PCNs. Consult [[pvn_pcn_approval_process]] for the full process.
*   **Revision Process**: Managing changes and revisions to existing PVNs/PCNs, ensuring version control and traceability. The revision process is elaborated in [[pvn_pcn_revision_process]].
*   **Documentation Management**: Guidelines for the storage, retrieval, and archiving of all PVN/PCN related documentation. This page, [[pvn_pcn_log_and_documentation_management]], serves as the central reference for this procedure.

This procedure ensures that all project variances are consistently managed, providing transparency and accountability across all project phases. It integrates with broader project management processes, including [[change_management_roles_and_responsibilities_0_prc_4003]] and [[project_cost_control]].
