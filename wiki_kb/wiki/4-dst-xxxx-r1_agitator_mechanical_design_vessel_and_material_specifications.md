# Agitator Mechanical Design, Vessel, and Material Specifications (R1)

This page compiles the mechanical design requirements for the agitator, the descriptive details of its associated vessel, and the material specifications for critical components, as outlined in document 4-DST-XXXX-R1. These specifications are paramount for ensuring the structural integrity, operational safety, and longevity of the agitator system.

**Mechanical Design Requirements:**
The agitator's mechanical design must adhere to the following criteria:
*   Equipment Specification
*   General Equipment Requirements
*   Fabrication
*   Lining
*   Welding
*   Site Conditions
*   Wind Design Code
*   Seismic Design Code
*   Connection Type
*   Electrical Area Classification
*   Painting
*   Steady Bearing
*   Total Noise Limit
*   First Critical Speed (rpm)
*   Shaft Diameter (mm)
*   Shaft Length (mm)
*   Test Requirements
*   Impeller Removable from Shaft

These requirements ensure the agitator is robustly constructed to withstand operational stresses, environmental factors, and comply with relevant engineering standards.

**Vessel Description:**
The vessel in which the agitator operates is characterized by:
*   Vessel reference drawing
*   Vessel liquid level normal (mm)
*   Vessel liquid level high (mm)
*   Vessel liquid level minimum (mm)

These liquid level parameters are essential for proper agitator placement and operation within the vessel.

**Material Specifications:**
The selection of materials for key agitator components is critical for corrosion resistance and mechanical performance:
*   Impeller
*   Shaft
*   Vessel
*   Shaft Sleeve
*   Seal Metal Parts
*   Elastomers
*   Rotating Face
*   Stationary Face

These material choices ensure compatibility with process fluids and environmental conditions, contributing to the agitator's reliability and extended service life.
