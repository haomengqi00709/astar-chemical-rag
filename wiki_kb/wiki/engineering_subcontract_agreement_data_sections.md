# Engineering Subcontract Agreement Data Sections

The Engineering Subcontract Agreement Data is systematically organized into seven distinct parts, each addressing a specific aspect of the subcontract. These parts provide detailed requirements and references to supporting documentation, ensuring a comprehensive and clear agreement.

### Part 1: Services
This section defines the scope of work for the engineering subcontract.
*   Part 1 (Services) **shall** reference one or more of the documents identified as 0-SPC-22XX: [Major Work Division] Engineering. These documents provide the primary definition of the services to be rendered by the Subcontractor.
*   Health and safety requirements **must** be explicitly stated as defined in 0-SPC-2201 Standard Conditions for Engineering Subcontract. These requirements are further identified by reference to any specific document that outlines particular safety rules or standards applicable to the performance of the services.
*   Key personnel involved in the subcontract are identified. Data available in SQL: 0-LST-22XX-2 (Engineering Subcontractor Staff Rates).

### Part 2: Documentation
This section outlines the documentation requirements for the subcontract.
*   Part 2 (Documentation) **shall** reference 0-SPC-0002: Engineering Subcontractor Document Supply, which details the requirements for document submission and management.

### Part 3: Time
This section specifies all critical dates and timeframes related to the subcontract. All time-related parameters are defined in 0-SPC-2201 Standard Conditions for Engineering Subcontract.
*   The starting date of the services **shall** be stated.
*   The completion date for the entirety of the services **shall** be stated.
*   The period for submission of the first Schedule, expressed in weeks after the starting date, **shall** be stated.
*   The period for reply to any communication **shall** be stated.
*   The period for retention of documents **shall** be stated.
*   The defects date, expressed in weeks after Completion, **shall** be stated.

### Part 4: Quality Assurance
This section addresses the quality management aspects of the subcontracted services.
*   Part 4 (Quality Assurance) **shall** reference 0-SPC-7004: Engineering Subcontractor Quality Assurance.
*   The period for submission of the quality plan, expressed in weeks after the starting date, **shall** be stated.

### Part 5: Payment
This section details the financial terms and payment procedures for the subcontract.
*   Part 5 (Payment) **shall** reference 0-LST-22XX-1: Engineering Subcontract Activity Schedule. Data available in SQL: 0-LST-22XX-1.
*   The assessment interval for payments **shall** be stated as defined in 0-SPC-2201 Standard Conditions for Engineering Subcontract.
*   The currency of the subcontract **shall** be stated as defined in 0-SPC-2201 Standard Conditions for Engineering Subcontract.
*   The period within which payments are made **shall** be stated as defined in 0-SPC-2201 Standard Conditions for Engineering Subcontract.
*   The specific expenses to be reimbursed by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **shall** be stated as defined in 0-SPC-2201 Standard Conditions for Engineering Subcontract.
*   The frequency at which the Subcontractor is to submit forecasts of total expenses **shall** be stated as defined in 0-SPC-2201 Standard Conditions for Engineering Subcontract.
*   This section further references 0-LST-22XX-2: Engineering Subcontractor Staff Rates. Data available in SQL: 0-LST-22XX-2.

### Part 6: Commercial
This section lists all documents that define the commercial conditions governing the subcontract.
*   Part 6 (Commercial) **shall** list all documents that define the commercial conditions.
*   It **shall** contain any Order-Specific Conditions, as detailed in 0-PRC-2205: Commercial Terms for Engineering Subcontract.
*   The rate for delay damages **shall** be stated as defined in 0-SPC-2202 Special Conditions for Engineering Subcontract.

### Part 7: Coordination and Communication
This section outlines the procedures for coordination and communication between Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Subcontractor.
*   Part 7 (Coordination and Communication) **shall** reference 0-SPC-2204: Engineering Subcontractor Coordination.
*   It **shall** list the authorized representatives of both parties, ensuring clear lines of communication.
