# Material Requirements for Stabilized Austenitic Stainless Steel (4-SPC-0014)

This section details the material requirements for Columbium Stabilized High Nickel Austenitic stainless steel welded fabrications, as specified in document 4-SPC-0014. These materials are classified under ASME P-45.

**Material Classification and Alloys**
Materials used in these fabrications fall under the ASME P-45 Material Classification (Stabilized). Examples of alloys within this classification include Carpenter 20 Cb-3 and Nicrofer 3620.

**Purchase Condition**
Plate, sheet, pipe, tube, and forging materials shall be purchased in the stabilize-annealed condition (UNS N08020). Base materials shall conform to ASME SB463, ASME SB729, or ASME SB462.

Plate and sheet materials shall be supplied in the hot rolled, annealed, and descaled condition to ensure maximum corrosion resistance, unless otherwise specified on the purchase order.

**Unstabilized Grades**
For requirements pertaining to unstabilized P45 alloys, such as 904L and 254 SLX, refer to [[4-SPC-0014_unstabilized_austenitic_stainless_steel_appendix_a]]. These unstabilized grades demand a similar level of welder/operator expertise and quality control as the stabilized grades covered in the main body of this standard.

For general information on material selection, refer to [[material_selection]].
