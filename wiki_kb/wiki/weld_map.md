# Weld Map Requirements

A weld map is a critical component of the Quality Assurance documentation package for welded fabrications. It provides a visual and documented record of all welds, their characteristics, and associated inspection data.

**Content of a Weld Map**
A comprehensive weld map **MUST** include:
*   **Location of Major Defects**: Clearly indicate the precise location of any major defects identified during inspection.
*   **Welder Identification**: For each weld, the identification symbol of the welder(s) or welding operator(s) responsible for that weld **MUST** be marked. This links the weld to the specific welder's qualifications and performance records.

**Purpose**
The weld map serves as a key reference for:
*   Tracking the quality and integrity of individual welds.
*   Facilitating future maintenance, repairs, or modifications by providing a detailed history of each weld.
*   Ensuring accountability by linking welds to qualified personnel.

**Integration with QA Documentation**
The weld map **MUST** be included as part of the overall [[uns_s30815_quality_assurance_documentation]] package submitted by the fabricator.
