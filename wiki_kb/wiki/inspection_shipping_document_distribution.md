# Inspection and Shipping Document Distribution

Prior to the final closure of packaging, the Vendor **must** notify Aker Kvaerner Chemetics that the goods are packaged and ready for an open-package inspection. This open-package inspection may be waived at the discretion of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Shipping Document Distribution:**
The Vendor **must** contact the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Logistics Group for specific instructions on the distribution of all shipping documents. These documents typically include:
*   Ocean Bills of Lading
*   Certificates of Origin
*   Free Trade Certificates
*   Insurance Certificates
*   Customs Invoices
*   Fumigation, Heat, or Pressure Treatment Certificates

The list of required shipping documents should be amended as applicable to the country of destination.

**Wood Treatment Requirements:**
For goods destined to specific jurisdictions (e.g., Australia, European Union), any wood used in packing **must** be treated in accordance with all applicable destination wood treatment and fumigation requirements.

This process ensures that all necessary inspections are conducted and documentation is correctly handled before the completion of [[packaging_marine_transportation_0_spc_5005]] and shipment.
