# Hot Work Permit Roles and Responsibilities (0-PRC-8503)

Effective administration of the Hot Work Permit system, as outlined in [[0-prc-8503_hot_work_permit_procedure_overview]], relies on clearly defined roles and responsibilities for all personnel involved.

**Commissioning HSE Manager:**
*   **Shall** be responsible for the administration of the Hot Work Permit system.
*   **Shall** issue permits or obtain permits from the client issuing authority.
*   **Will** take over the administration of the Open Flame and Hot Work Permit system if the Commissioning HSE Manager is temporarily away from site.
*   **Shall** establish and maintain a program that adequately informs Chemetic’s personnel of the Hot Work system.
*   **Must** sign a valid permit for this program, along with the Work Supervisor.
*   **Shall** be responsible for testing and certifying in writing that no flammable gases, liquids or solids, oxygen deficiency or toxic materials are present in the location.
*   Is the final authority when conflicting test results are encountered regarding gas testing.
*   Signs approval for areas designated as fabrication shops to obtain hot work permit exemption.
*   **Shall not** issue a Work Permit covering Hot Work while a [[0-PRC-8504-RA_lockout_and_tagging|lock-out, tag-out procedure]] allowing opening of lines and vessels is in existence in an area that could be made hazardous by any release.
*   It is the responsibility of the Commissioning HSE Manager to see that a Work Permit for Hot Work is not issued or the lockout, tag-out procedure for opening is terminated if such a procedure is in existence.
*   If the Commissioning HSE Manager decides sufficient liquid or vapour has been released to create a hazard, a Work Permit covering Hot Work **shall not** be issued until the hazard has been eliminated.
*   **Will** visit the work site and decide whether or not the permit should be re-validated after work has been stopped by a competent authority.
*   Only the Commissioning HSE Manager can permit a restart after work has been stopped.
*   **Shall** train the [[fire_watch|fire watch]] person, covering proper execution of responsibilities, use of fire extinguishers, and use of explosion meters.

**Commissioning Manager:**
*   **Will** take over the administration of the Open Flame and Hot Work Permit system if the Commissioning HSE Manager is temporarily away from site. Refer to [[commissioning_manager_assignment]] for general responsibilities.

**Work Supervisor:**
*   **Must** sign a valid permit for this program, along with the Commissioning HSE Manager.

**Person Executing the Work:**
*   **Will** sign the permit, signifying that they and their crew fully understand and will honor the conditions spelled out on the permit.
*   **Shall** complete the bottom portion of the permit on completion of the work.
*   **Shall** return the completed permit to the point of issue.

**Employees Authorized to Perform Hot Work:**
*   Each employee **shall** be thoroughly trained in all aspects of these guidelines.
*   Everyone **must** go through Open Flame/Hot Work Training.
*   The hazard assessment created for the task **must** be understood by all members of the team involved with the hot work activity.

**Competent Authority:**
*   Any competent authority may stop the work covered by this permit at any time if they consider the prevailing conditions or work methods to be unsafe.
*   Anyone stopping work **must** remove the site copy of the permit and return it to the Commissioning HSE Manager, giving reasons for their action.

**Fire Watch:**
*   **Will** be assigned to the job when indicated on the Hot Work Permit.
*   **Will** have no other duties when carrying out their assignment.
*   **Shall** be trained by the Commissioning HSE Manager.
*   **Shall** be familiar with the location of the hot work and the nature of the hot work.
*   **Shall** remain in constant communication range with the person(s) doing the work.
*   **Shall** be provided with proper fire extinguisher(s) located in the immediate area for quick availability.
*   **Shall** be provided with an explosion meter on jobs that could potentially involve flammable gases or vapours.
*   **Shall** periodically test the area with the explosion meter.
*   If the explosion meter reading indicates a percent concentration at the lower explosive limit, the hot work **shall** be stopped and the potential problem corrected before restart.
*   **Shall** continually survey the work area for combustible materials.
*   If the fire watch leaves, the hot work **will** be disconnected, the welder turned off, and the electric supply turned off until the fire watch returns.
*   If the fire watch cannot extinguish a fire quickly and safely, the [[fire_emergency_response_akctc|emergency evacuation alarm]] **shall** be activated.
*   The fire watch **shall** always know the location of the nearest alarm box.
*   More than one fire watch may be required depending on potential impact on other areas, including floors below the hot work activity.

**Roving Monitor:**
*   If a potential for a spill in an adjacent area could create a hazard, a roving monitor with an explosion meter **shall** be assigned.
*   The roving monitor **shall** patrol the area and immediately stop the hot work if flammable conditions are detected.

These roles are crucial for maintaining safety during hot work operations on EPC projects, as detailed in [[hse_roles_epc_projects]].

*   If the Commissioning HSE Manager is temporarily away from site, the Commissioning Manager will take over the administration of the Open Flame and Hot Work Permit system.
*   The Commissioning HSE Manager is responsible for issuing permits or obtaining permits from the client issuing authority.
*   The Commissioning HSE Manager will establish and maintain a program that adequately informs Chemetic’s personnel of the Hot Work system.
*   The Commissioning HSE Manager is the final authority when conflicting test results are encountered.
