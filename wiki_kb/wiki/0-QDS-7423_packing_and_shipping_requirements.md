# Thickener Packing and Shipping Requirements (0-QDS-7423)

This page details the packing and shipping requirements for the Thickener equipment, as specified in Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7423.

**Packaging and Crating:**
*   Packing/Crating (Packaging) **shall** require Chemetics Witness. This activity is required to be included in the Vendor's Inspection and Test Plan.
*   Packing/Crating (Packing List) is required as AP (As Prepared). This document is required to be included in the Vendor's Inspection and Test Plan.
*   Packing/Crating (Packing List) **shall** require Chemetics Witness.

**Requirements for Sectional Shipments:**
If the Thickener is shipped in more than one section:
*   Trial assembly is required. This activity is required to be included in the Vendor's Inspection and Test Plan.
*   Match-marking is required. This activity is required to be included in the Vendor's Inspection and Test Plan.

If the Thickener is shipped in more than one section for weld assembly and rubber lining in the field:
*   Trial assembly is required.
*   Match-marking is required.

These requirements ensure that the Thickener is adequately protected during transit and that multi-section units can be correctly assembled on-site. For general packing and shipping guidelines, refer to [[0-QDS-7411_packing_and_shipping_requirements]].
