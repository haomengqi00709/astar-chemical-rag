# Engineering Subcontract General Conditions and Administration (0-SPC-2205)

This document, 0-SPC-2205, outlines the general conditions and administrative requirements for engineering subcontracts. It defines key terms, establishes communication protocols, and sets the legal framework for the agreement between Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Subcontractor.

**Key Definitions:**
*   **Parties:** Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Subcontractor.
*   **Others:** Refers to individuals or organizations not directly employed by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, the Subcontractor, or any Subsubcontractor or supplier of the Subcontractor.
*   **Subcontract Date:** The date on which this Subcontract officially came into existence.
*   **To Provide the Services:** Encompasses all work necessary to complete the services as specified in this Subcontract.
*   **Scope:** Specifies and describes the services to be provided, including any constraints on how the Subcontractor Provides the Services. The Scope is detailed in the documents referenced in the [[0-SPC-2205_table_1_subcontract_data_reference|Subcontract Data]] and may also be conveyed through instructions given in accordance with this Subcontract.
*   **Subsubcontractor:** Any person or organization engaged by the Subcontractor under a Subsubcontract to provide a portion of the services.
*   **Completion Date:** The agreed-upon date for completion, subject to changes made in accordance with this Subcontract.
*   **Completion:** Achieved when the Subcontractor has performed all work specified in the Scope by the Completion Date and has corrected any Defects that would prevent Aker Kvaerner Chemetics from utilizing the services.
*   **Defect:** Any part of the services that does not conform to the Scope.
*   **Subcontract Data:** Essential information required to fully define the obligations of both Parties. This data is contained within the Agreement, documents referenced in the Agreement, and the Special Conditions for Engineering Subcontract.

**Interpretation:**
*   Terms identified in the [[0-SPC-2205_table_1_subcontract_data_reference|Subcontract Data]] are presented in *italics*.
*   Defined terms are capitalized.
*   Words in the singular also imply the plural and vice versa, unless the context dictates otherwise.
*   Words in the masculine also imply the feminine and neuter, unless the context dictates otherwise.

**Governing Law:**
This Subcontract **must** be governed by the law of British Columbia.

**Communication Requirements:**
*   All required communications **must** be in a form specified in the Scope.
*   Communications **must** be readable, copyable, and recordable.
*   Written communications **must** be in the English language.
*   A communication takes effect upon receipt at the last address notified by the recipient for communications. If no address is notified, it takes effect upon receipt at the recipient's address stated in the [[0-SPC-2205_table_1_subcontract_data_reference|Subcontract Data]].
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ acceptance or review of a communication or the Subcontractor's work does not alter the Subcontractor’s liabilities or responsibility to Provide the Services.

**Document Retention:**
The Subcontractor **must** retain copies of all drawings, specifications, reports, and other documents that record the services for a period of ten (10) years. These retained copies **must** be in the form specified in the Scope.

**Ambiguities and Inconsistencies:**
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or the Subcontractor **must** notify the other party immediately upon becoming aware of any ambiguity or inconsistency within or between the documents forming part of this Subcontract.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **must** issue an instruction to resolve any identified ambiguity or inconsistency.

**Health and Safety:**
Both Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Subcontractor **must** act in accordance with the applicable health and safety requirements.

**Illegal or Impossible Scope Requirements:**
*   The Subcontractor **must** notify Aker Kvaerner Chemetics as soon as they become aware that the Scope requires anything illegal or impossible.
*   If Aker Kvaerner Chemetics agrees that a Scope requirement is illegal or impossible, they **must** issue an instruction to modify the Scope appropriately.

This page provides an overview of the foundational administrative and legal aspects of the engineering subcontract. For specific details on subcontractor obligations, refer to [[0-SPC-2205_subcontractor_obligations_and_quality]]. For information on broader engineering subcontract procedures, see [[0-SPC-2202-1_engineering_subcontract_general_conditions_and_administration]].

**Key Definitions and Interpretations:**
*   Terms identified in the Subcontract Data are presented in *italics*.
*   Defined terms within the document have Capital Initials.
*   **Others** refers to people or organisations not Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, the Subcontractor, or any employee, Subsubcontractor or supplier of the Subcontractor.
*   The **Subcontract Date** is the date when this Subcontract came into existence.
*   **To Provide the Services** means to do the work necessary to complete the services in accordance with this Subcontract.
*   The **Scope** specifies and describes the services, states any constraints on how the Subcontractor Provides the Services, and is found in the documents which the Subcontract Data states it is in, or in an instruction given in accordance with this Subcontract.
*   A **Subsubcontractor** is a person or an organisation which has a Subsubcontract with the Subcontractor to provide part of the services.
*   The **Completion Date** is the completion date unless later changed in accordance with this Subcontract.
*   **Completion** occurs when the Subcontractor has done all the work which the Scope states he is to do by the Completion Date, and has corrected Defects which would have prevented Aker Kvaerner Chemetics from using the services.
*   A **Defect** is a part of the services which is not in accordance with the Scope.

**General Interpretations:**
*   Words in the singular also mean in the plural and vice versa, except where the context shows otherwise.
*   Words in the masculine also mean in the feminine and neuter, except where the context shows otherwise.

**Governing Law:**
*   This Subcontract is governed by the law of British Columbia.

**Ambiguities and Inconsistencies:**
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or the Subcontractor must notify the other as soon as either becomes aware of an ambiguity or inconsistency in or between the documents which are part of this Subcontract.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics must give an instruction resolving any ambiguity or inconsistency.

**Health and Safety:**
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics must act in accordance with the health and safety requirements.
