# Chain Installation and Tensioning

This page outlines the mandatory requirements for the correct size, installation, and tensioning of [[chain_installation_and_tensioning|chains]] for [[conveying_equipment_mechanical_checkout|conveying equipment]]. The chain **must** be installed and have the correct size and tightness.
