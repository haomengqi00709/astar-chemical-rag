# Fan or Blower Vendor Quality Assurance Requirements

For the supply of Fan or Blower equipment under Quality Data Sheet **0-QDS-7425**, the Vendor's Quality Assurance program **must** comply with the requirements set forth in this Quality Data Sheet.

The Vendor **must** prepare a Quality Plan in accordance with [[0-SPC-7002_vendor_quality_assurance|0-SPC-7002: Vendor Quality Assurance]]. This Quality Plan serves as a critical document outlining the Vendor's approach to ensuring product quality and adherence to specified standards.

Compliance with both this specific [[0-QDS-7XXX_template_quality_data_sheet|Quality Data Sheet]] and the general [[0-SPC-7002_vendor_quality_assurance|Vendor Quality Assurance]] specification is mandatory to ensure the quality and reliability of the supplied equipment.

-SPC-7002: Vendor Quality Assurance]].

Furthermore, the Vendor's Quality Assurance program **will comply** with the requirements of [[0-SPC-7002_vendor_quality_assurance|0-SPC-7002: Vendor Quality Assurance]].
