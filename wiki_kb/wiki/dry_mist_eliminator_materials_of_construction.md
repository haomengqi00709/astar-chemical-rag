# Dry Mist Eliminator Materials of Construction

This page specifies the materials of construction for the Dry Mist Eliminator, Tag No. **7507K**. These material selections are critical for ensuring the longevity and operational integrity of the unit in its intended service.

**Component Materials:**
*   **Cage / Housing:** 316L ss (Stainless Steel)
*   **Element:** VTA (Vendor To Advise)
*   **Gasket:** n/a (Not Applicable)
*   **Bolting:** 316L ss (Stainless Steel)

The selection of 316L stainless steel for the cage, housing, and bolting indicates a requirement for high corrosion resistance, particularly given the presence of acid mist as an entrained material. The element material is to be advised by the vendor, allowing for specialized material selection based on proprietary designs and performance guarantees.

For a general overview of the Dry Mist Eliminator, refer to [[dry_mist_eliminator_overview]]. Performance requirements and vendor guarantees related to the unit's operation are detailed in [[dry_mist_eliminator_performance_requirements]].
