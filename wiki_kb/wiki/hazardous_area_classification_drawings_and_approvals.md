# Hazardous Area Classification - Documentation and Approval

The final stage of the hazardous area classification process involves the production, review, and approval of comprehensive documentation to guide equipment selection and plant operation.

**Area Classification Drawing**
The last step in the classification process is to produce an area classification drawing. This drawing **must** clearly show the different classified areas and their extent. For each zone depicted on the drawing, the gases and/or liquids that may exist due to a leak in that zone **must** be listed on the drawing. If the complexity of the many different zones warrants it, a written document to supplement the drawing **should** be prepared.

**Review and Approval Process**
Once the area classification drawing(s) and any supplementary written documents are complete, these **should** be reviewed with the process, mechanical, civil/structural, instrument, and electrical engineering personnel working on the project. This review ensures that any process changes that may alter the area classification of the plant are identified and addressed.

The plant Owner may forward a copy of the drawing(s) and documents to the insurance company insuring the plant for comments and/or approval, as the area classification of the plant may affect the insurance premiums.

After the area classification drawing(s) and documents have been reviewed and approved by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the plant Owner, then copies **must** be given to the engineering personnel responsible for specifying, selecting, and requisitioning the mechanical, instrumentation, and electrical equipment. This distribution is essential to ensure that all purchased equipment is suitable for the area classification in which it will be located.
