# 0-QDS-7406 Quality Record Definitions

This Quality Data Sheet utilizes specific abbreviations to denote the required status of Quality Records:

*   **AP (As Prepared):** This designation indicates that the Quality Record **is required** to be prepared by the Vendor and submitted for review.
*   **FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package):** This designation indicates that the Quality Record **is required** to be included as part of the Vendor's final Quality Assurance Documentation Package.

These definitions are crucial for understanding the documentation requirements for Catalyst materials. For a list of specific Quality Records and their required status, refer to [[0-QDS-7406_inspection_and_documentation_requirements]].

*   **FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package):** This designation indicates that the Quality Record **is required** to be included in the Vendor's Quality Assurance Documentation Package.
