# Horizontal Centrifugal Pump Process Design Conditions

The process design conditions for a horizontal centrifugal pump, as specified in Document ID [[template_horizontal_centrifugal_pump_datasheet_administration]], define the operational environment and fluid characteristics the pump must handle. These parameters are crucial for proper pump selection and performance.

The following process parameters are measured and specified:

*   **Composition:** Measured in %w/w (weight by weight).
*   **Solids Content:** Measured in %w/w (weight by weight).
*   **Flow Rate:** Measured in m3/h (cubic meters per hour).
*   **Minimum Flow Rate:** Measured in m3/h (cubic meters per hour).
*   **TDH (Total Dynamic Head):** Measured in m (fluid) (meters of fluid).
*   **NPSH Available:** Measured in m (fluid) (meters of fluid).
*   **Temperature:** Measured in oC (degrees Celsius).
*   **Density:** Measured in kg/m3 (kilograms per cubic meter).
*   **Viscosity:** Measured in cP (centipoise).
*   **NPSH Required:** Measured in m (fluid) (meters of fluid).

These conditions are typically detailed within the datasheet structure, which includes fields for "Process Conditions" that can be specified as "DESIGN PER UNIT" or "ALTERNATE PER UNIT," along with "Fluid Description" and "Solids Type." For further details on the datasheet layout, refer to [[template_horizontal_centrifugal_pump_datasheet_structure]].

The datasheet allows for process conditions to be specified as either "DESIGN PER UNIT" or "ALTERNATE PER UNIT". Essential fluid characteristics include a "Fluid Description" and "Solids Type".

**Key Process Parameters and Units:**
*   Composition: %w/w
*   Solids Content: %w/w
*   Flow Rate: m3/h
*   Minimum Flow Rate: m3/h
*   TDH (Total Dynamic Head): m (fluid)
*   NPSH Available: m (fluid)
*   NPSH Required: m (fluid)
*   Temperature: oC
*   Density: kg/m3
*   Viscosity: cP
