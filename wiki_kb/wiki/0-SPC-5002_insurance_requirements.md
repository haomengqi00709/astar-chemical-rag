# Purchase Order Insurance Requirements

This page details the mandatory insurance provisions for the Vendor under the Purchase Order (PO), ensuring adequate coverage for risks.

**Vendor's Insurance Obligations:**
*   The Vendor **must** provide the insurances specified in the [[0-SPC-5007_table_2]] (Insurance Table).
*   The Vendor **must** also provide any additional insurances and minimum limits of indemnity as stated in the [[special_conditions_for_purchase]].
*   The Vendor is **not** required to provide any insurance that the Purchase Order states Aker Kvaerner Chemetics is responsible for providing.
*   All required insurances **must** be in the joint names of the Parties (Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Vendor).
*   These insurances **must** provide coverage for events that are at the Vendor’s risk from the [[purchase_order_definitions#The Starting Date|Starting Date]] until the [[defect_management_process#Defects Correction Period|Defects Date]].

**Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' Right to Insure:**
*   If the Vendor fails to provide evidence of insurance as required by this Purchase Order, Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may, at its discretion, insure the risk that the Vendor is required to insure.
*   Any cost incurred by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for such insurance **must** be paid by the Vendor.
