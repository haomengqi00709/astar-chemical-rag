# 0-QDS-7405-2 Vendor Quality Assurance Requirements

For the Blower Lube Oil System project, the Vendor's Quality Assurance (QA) activities **shall** adhere to the following requirements, as detailed in [[0-QDS-7405-2_document_details]].

**Vendor Quality Plan**
The Vendor **shall** prepare a Quality Plan in accordance with the specifications outlined in [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment]]. This plan serves as a foundational document for all quality-related activities.

**Compliance**
The Vendor's overall Quality Assurance program **shall** comply with all requirements set forth in this Quality Data Sheet (0-QDS-7405-2) and the broader [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment]] document.

**Chemetics Surveillance Level**
Surveillance activities conducted by Chemetics will be performed to Level 1.0. The specific details and requirements for this surveillance level are further elaborated within this Quality Data Sheet, particularly in the [[0-QDS-7405-2_inspection_and_documentation_requirements]] section.

The Vendor **shall** prepare a Quality Plan in accordance with the specifications outlined in 0-SPC-7001: Vendor Quality Assurance.

The Vendor's Quality Assurance activities shall also comply with 0-SPC-7001: Vendor Quality Assurance.

**Chemetics Surveillance**
Surveillance by Chemetics will be conducted to Level 1.0, as set out in this Quality Data Sheet.
