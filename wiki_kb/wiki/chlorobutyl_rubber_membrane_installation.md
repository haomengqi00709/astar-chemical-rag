# Chlorobutyl Rubber Membrane Installation for Drying Tower Gas Inlets

For drying tower gas inlets, a specific acid resistant membrane system utilizing chlorobutyl rubber **will** be employed. This system is distinct from the Pecora/PTFE membrane used in other strong acid equipment, tailored to the specific conditions and requirements of these inlets.

**Membrane Specifications and Application:**
Drying tower gas inlet membranes **will** be 6mm thick chlorobutyl rubber. This robust rubber membrane **will** be applied in either one or two layers, depending on the specific design requirements or the installer's qualified procedure, ensuring adequate thickness for protection.

The application of the chlorobutyl rubber membrane has specific coverage requirements to ensure comprehensive protection:
*   The rubber membrane **will** cover the nozzle flange face, providing a continuous barrier across this critical interface.
*   It **will** extend to the edge of the nozzle to shell junction, ensuring that the entire transition area is protected.

**Installation Sequence:**
The chlorobutyl rubber membrane **will** be applied *before* the Pecora mastic/PTFE vessel membrane. This precise sequencing is critical to ensure proper layering, sealing, and integration of the different lining systems. Following the application of the rubber membrane, the Pecora/PTFE system **will** then cover the rubber in the mouth of the inlet nozzle for a minimum length of 450mm, creating an overlapping and securely sealed transition between the two membrane types. This overlap is vital for preventing any gaps or weak points where corrosive media could penetrate.

Adherence to these specifications ensures robust acid resistance and proper integration of the different lining systems within the drying tower gas inlets, contributing to the overall integrity of the acid plant.

**Membrane Specifications:**

### Application Details
*   Drying tower gas inlet membranes **will** be applied in one or two layers.
*   The rubber membrane **will** cover the nozzle flange face and extend to the edge of the nozzle to shell junction.
*   The rubber membrane **will** be applied before the Pecora mastic/PTFE vessel membrane.
