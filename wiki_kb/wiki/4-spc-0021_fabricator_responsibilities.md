# Fabricator Responsibilities for Nickel/Copper Welding (4-SPC-0021)

Fabricators undertaking work governed by **4-SPC-0021** for [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_company_details|Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Inc.]] **shall** adhere to specific responsibilities concerning subcontracting, supervision, site weld evaluation, material traceability, and documentation.

**Subcontracting and Supervision:**
*   No subcontracting of welding is permitted without the express **written approval** of [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_company_details|Aker Kvaerner Chemetics]].
*   The fabricator **shall** ensure regular and systematic supervision of all work by competent staff.

**Site Weld Evaluation:**
*   The Contractor **shall** evaluate the proposed location of site welds at an early stage. This evaluation is crucial to ensure adequate access for:
    *   Welding
    *   Cleaning
    *   Inspection
    *   Non-destructive testing

**Material Traceability and Control:**
*   The fabricator **must** maintain full traceability of all materials back to their original mill certification.
*   The fabricator's internal controls **shall** prevent the use of unspecified materials.
*   Lack of adequate identification or certification **shall** be cause for rejection of the specified component or item.

**Documentation and Records:**
*   The fabricator **shall** furnish a report containing all data pertaining to each vessel or item.
*   Prior to [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_company_details|Aker Kvaerner Chemetics']] final inspection of each unit, the fabricator **shall** have prepared three sets of records in finished form, excluding final test data.
*   These reports, along with final test data, **shall** be assembled into three complete sets for each item.
*   In no case **shall** a documentation package pertain to more than one vessel or item number.
*   The Inspector will collect the appropriate number of documentation packages for each item tested at the time of witnessing final tests and certifying the correctness of forms.
