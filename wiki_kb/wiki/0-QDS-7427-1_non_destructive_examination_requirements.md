# SS Tubing Tier 1 Non-Destructive Examination Requirements

Non-Destructive Examination (NDE) is a mandatory activity for Stainless Steel Tubing Tier 1, ensuring the structural integrity and absence of defects without damaging the material. All NDE activities **must** be performed in accordance with [ASTM or approved equivalent code].

Specific NDE requirements include:

*   **Non-Destructive Testing (General):** This is a required item or activity within the quality assurance process.
*   **Micrographs of Welds:** These are required as "AP" (As Prepared) quality records. Micrographs of welds **must** undergo Chemetics Witness surveillance and require review after the annealing process. This ensures the metallurgical quality and integrity of the weld joints.
*   **Eddy Current Testing:** This is a required quality record, designated as "FP" (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package). Eddy Current testing **must** be performed under Chemetics Witness surveillance. The testing **shall** be conducted in accordance with [ASTM or approved equivalent code], verifying the absence of surface and near-surface discontinuities in the tubing.

These comprehensive NDE requirements are critical for detecting any potential flaws or inconsistencies in the Stainless Steel Tubing, thereby upholding the highest quality standards.
