# Anchor Bolt and Grouting Requirements for Skid on Concrete & Grout (4-SCR-0050)

This page specifies the material requirements for anchor bolts and nuts, along with critical installation dimensions for grouting, as detailed in document [[4-SCR-0050_document_administration]] for skids supported on concrete and grout.

**Anchor Bolt and Nut Materials:**
*   **Anchor Bolt Material:** 4.6
*   **Nut Material:** 4

**Installation Requirements:**
*   **Minimum "Bolt to Edge" distance:** 150 mm.
*   For other "Bolt to Edge" distances, consult the Civil/Structural Dept.
*   **Grout Dimension 'Y':** 40 mm (as specified in [[4-SCR-0050_equipment_dimensions_and_anchor_details]]).

These specifications are vital for ensuring the structural integrity and longevity of the [[equipment_support_criteria]]. Proper adherence to these material and dimensional requirements is mandatory.
