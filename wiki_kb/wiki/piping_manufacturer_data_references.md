# Piping Manufacturer Data References

This page compiles references to manufacturer-specific data and instructions that are essential for the correct installation, operation, and maintenance of various piping components and systems. Adherence to these manufacturer guidelines is crucial for ensuring product performance, warranty validity, and overall system integrity.

**Referenced Manufacturer Data:**
*   **Teflon Lined Pipe:** Refer to MANUFACTURER’S DATA ATTACHED for specific installation, handling, and maintenance instructions for Teflon lined pipe. These details are critical for ensuring the chemical resistance and mechanical integrity of the lining.
*   **Butt and Strap FRP Pipe:** Refer to MANUFACTURERS DATA ATTACHED for installation procedures, material specifications, and jointing techniques for Butt and Strap FRP Pipe. This includes data for ABCO FRP Pipe.
*   **PVDF Pipe:** Refer to MANUFACTURER’S DATA ATTACHED for detailed information regarding PVDF (Polyvinylidene Fluoride) pipe, including installation guidelines, chemical compatibility, and temperature limits.
*   **Polypropylene Pipe:** Refer to MANUFACTURERS DATA ATTACHED for specific instructions on polypropylene pipe, including butt fusion procedures and requirements for FRP reinforcement.
*   **PVC Pipe:** Refer to MANUFACTURERS DATA ATTACHED for guidelines on PVC (Polyvinyl Chloride) pipe, including solvent cementing procedures and requirements for FRP reinforcement.

It is mandatory that all relevant manufacturer’s data **must** be attached and consulted prior to the installation or maintenance of the respective piping systems.
