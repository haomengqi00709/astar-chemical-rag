# As-Built Documentation for Electrical Systems

As-built documentation for electrical systems involves updating project drawings to reflect the final installed conditions after construction, check-out, and commissioning. This ensures that the Owner receives a complete and accurate record of the plant.

**Process for As-Built Documentation**
As-built documentation **will** be done as shown in Table 2. As-built drawings **will** be generated by updating the latest version of office copies using field mark-ups as the correct version. Field mark-ups **will** be used for updating project drawings for a final record and **will** be done for check-out and commissioning of the plant. Final field mark-ups **should** be done after the plant has successfully started up and problems have been corrected.

As-built drawings **will** be the final copy of drawings provided to the Owner and **will** contain the latest information such as equipment sizes, wiring interconnections, schematics, and electrical equipment arrangement.

**Specific Drawing Updates**
*   **[[final_single_line_diagrams]] (As-built, 9-SCM-0101):**
    *   **Must** be updated to include motor sizes agreeing with installed equipment.
    *   **Must** be updated to include quantity and sizes of motor starters.
    *   **Must** be updated to include protective relays changes such as additions and deletions.
*   **[[electrical_schematic_and_wiring_diagrams]] (As-built, 9-SCM-0400):**
    *   **Must** be updated to include all locations and types of controls for each piece of equipment.
    *   **Must** be updated to include all interlocking controls for each piece of equipment.
    *   Revisions on vendor drawings **will** be done as necessary for schematics.
*   **[[electrical_wiring_diagrams]] (As-built, 9-SCM-0200):**
    *   **Must** be updated to include motor control centres.
    *   **Must** be updated to include interconnecting wiring between pieces of equipment.
    *   **Must** be updated to include switchgear wiring interconnections.
    *   Wiring **will** be updated in conjunction with the schematics.
*   **[[electrical_block_diagrams]] (As-built, 9-SCM-0300):**
    *   **Must** be updated to agree with the revised schematics and wiring diagrams.
    *   Vendor drawings **will** be marked up to agree with the field revisions for block diagrams.
