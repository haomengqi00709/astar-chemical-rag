# Purchase Order Approval and Authorization

This page outlines the specific steps and requirements for the checking, approval, and authorization of Purchase Orders (POs).

**Checking and Approval**
The Project Engineer **shall** check the Purchase Order for accuracy and completeness.
The Project Manager **shall** approve the Purchase Order.
The Project Manager **must** affix [[applying_digital_signatures|digital signatures]] to the Purchase Order as part of the approval process.

**Authorization Process**
Procurement **shall** print a paper copy of the approved Purchase Order. This paper copy **shall** be presented for authorization as set out in Section 13 of the originating procedure.
A copy of the approved [[vendor_bid_evaluation_procedure_0_prc_5003|Cost Tabulation]] **shall** also be presented for authorization.
For orders exceeding CAD 10,000 in value, a copy of the approved [[order_risk_assessment|Order Risk Assessment (0-PRC-5003: Bid Evaluation and Approval)]] **shall** be presented for authorization.

**Post-Authorization**
Following authorization, Procurement **shall** formally advise the Vendor in writing that the order has been awarded.
The official Start Date for the order **shall** be established by Procurement immediately after authorization.
The Purchase Order **must** be signed by personnel with the appropriate [[purchase_order_signing_authority_levels|authorization levels]].
The authorized paper copy of the Purchase Order **shall** be forwarded directly to the Vendor by Procurement.

This process aligns with general [[document_approval_and_revision_process|document approval procedures]] and [[authorization_roles]].
