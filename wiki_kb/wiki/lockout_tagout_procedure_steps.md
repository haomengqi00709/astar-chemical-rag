# Lockout/Tagout Procedure Steps

This page details the step-by-step procedure for implementing lockout/tagout as per [[9-prc-0019_lockout_tagout_procedure_overview|Document ID: 9-PRC-0019]].

### 1. Shutdown Equipment
*   If the machine or equipment is operating, shut it down by the normal stopping procedure.

### 2. Isolate from Energy Source(s)
*   Operate the switch, valve, or other energy isolating device(s) so that the equipment is isolated from its energy source(s).

### 3. Dissipate Stored Energy
*   Stored energy (such as that in springs, elevated machine members, rotating flywheels, hydraulic systems, and air, gas, steam, or water pressure, etc.) must be dissipated or restrained by methods such as repositioning, blocking, bleeding down, etc.
*   Before installing blinds or blocks, bleed down steam, air, or hydraulic lines to get rid of any pressure.
*   Coiled springs, spring-loaded devices, or suspended loads must be released so that their stored energy will not result in inadvertent movement.
*   Rotating equipment such as fan blades must be blocked to prevent rotation in the event of air movement.
*   The placement of a blind is another form of blocking.

### 4. Apply Lockout/Tagout Devices
*   Lockout and tagout the energy isolating devices with assigned individual lock(s) and tag(s).
*   Each individual requiring to work on the affected machine or equipment will place his/her lock on the device.

### 5. Verify Isolation
*   After ensuring that no personnel are exposed, operate the push button or other normal operating controls to make certain the equipment will not operate.
*   Return operating control(s) to neutral or off position after the test.

### 6. Perform Work

### 7. Restore Equipment to Service
*   After the work is complete and the equipment is ready for normal operation, check the area around the machine or equipment to ensure that no one is exposed.
*   After all tools have been removed from the machine or equipment, guards have been reinstalled and employees are in the clear, remove all lockout and tagout devices.
*   Each affected individual removes his/her lock.
*   Operate the energy isolating devices to restore energy to the machine or equipment.
