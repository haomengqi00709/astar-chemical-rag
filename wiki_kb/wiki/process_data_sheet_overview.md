# Process Data Sheets

Process Data Sheets are essential tabular or textual documents that provide detailed process-related information for individual equipment items. These documents are crucial for both equipment design and procurement, ensuring that vendors have a complete understanding of the operational requirements. Sketches are generally not prepared for Process Data Sheets.

**Content Elements**
Process Data Sheets **must** contain the following information:

*   **Operating Case Reference:** Identification of the specific operating scenarios for which the data is provided.
*   **Operating Stream Composition and Conditions:** Detailed information on the chemical composition, flow rates, temperatures, and pressures of process streams during normal operation.
*   **Applied Safety Margins or Design Allowances:** Any safety factors or allowances incorporated into the design parameters.
*   **Design Stream Composition and Conditions:** The most stringent or critical process conditions used for equipment design.
*   **Upset Stream Composition and Conditions:** Data for abnormal or upset conditions, particularly relevant for pressure design considerations.
*   **Nozzle Table:** A comprehensive list of all nozzles, including their sizing criteria.
*   **Special Dimensional Requirements:** Any unique or critical dimensional constraints for the equipment.
*   **Special Design Criteria:** Any specific design considerations or requirements not covered elsewhere.

**Document Preparation and Approval**
The preparation, checking, and approval of Process Data Sheets follow a defined workflow:

*   **Preparation:** Process Data Sheets **must** be prepared by Process personnel.
*   **Checking:** The documents **must** be checked by Technology personnel.
*   **Approval:** Final approval **must** be granted by Process personnel.

**Content Elements**
Process Data Sheets **must** contain the following information:
*   Sketches will not be prepared for Process Data Sheets.
*   Operating case reference.
*   Operating stream composition and conditions.
*   Applied safety margins or design allowances.
*   Design stream composition and conditions.
*   Upset stream composition and conditions (for pressure design).
*   Nozzle table including sizing criteria.
*   Any special dimensional requirements.
*   Any special design criteria.

**Responsibilities**
*   **Prepared by:** Process and Mechanical
*   **Checked by:** Technology
*   **Approved by:** Process
