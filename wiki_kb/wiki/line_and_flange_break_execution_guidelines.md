# Line and Flange Break Execution Guidelines

These guidelines outline the critical steps and safety protocols to be followed during the execution of line and flange breaking operations, ensuring personnel safety and environmental protection.

**General Precautions**
*   Always treat a first line break as a pressured line.
*   Be aware of low points in the line that may contain trapped chemicals. A low point bleed valve **shall** be opened and rodded to assure that no stored energy remains in the line.
*   Atmospheric monitoring should be done to calculate chemical concentrations or residuals that may remain in the line at the bleed point. Continuous atmospheric monitoring is required as needed.

**Bolt Loosening and Flange Opening**
*   Bolts **shall** be loosened slowly.
*   Bolts **shall** be loosened on the opposite side of the employee(s) performing the first break to prevent any escaping chemicals/material from injuring workers and/or co-workers.
*   Flange spreaders **shall** be used for opening flange joints whenever required and/or practical.
*   When flange spreaders are not possible, only standard wedges **shall** be used as an alternative. Steps **shall** be taken to prevent wedges flying in the event it slips from the joint.
*   Whenever flange bolts are severely corroded, the old bolts **shall** be removed one at a time. Corroded bolts **shall** be replaced with new bolts and tightened. New bolts can then be loosened as a wedge is inserted.

**Dismantling with Crane Lifting**
*   When dismantling lines utilizing crane lifting, assure that no chemical is trapped in the line.
*   If the possibility of trapped chemicals exists during crane lifting, the pipe ends **shall** be tightly wrapped with polyurethane and sealed with duct tape prior to lifting to prevent ground contamination or inadvertent chemical contact or exposure to personnel.

**Post-Dismantling Requirements**
Dismantled lines, fittings, pumps, etc., **shall** be washed thoroughly and be free of contaminants before being removed from the jobsite. This washing requirement applies even if the parts are being scrapped.

**Ignition Sources**
No line or equipment containing a flammable material **shall** be opened when there is a “Potential Ignition Source” that may create a hazard from escaping processes.
