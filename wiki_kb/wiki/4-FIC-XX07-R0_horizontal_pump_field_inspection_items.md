# Horizontal Pump Field Inspection Items

This page details the mandatory inspection items for horizontal centrifugal pumps, as outlined in checklist 4-FIC-XX07-R0. Adherence to these requirements is crucial for ensuring proper installation and operational readiness.

The following items **must** be verified during the field inspection:

*   **Nameplate:** The correct nameplate **must** be installed.
*   **Pump Position:** The pump's position (elevation, orientation, leveled) **must** be checked.
*   **Foundation and Anchor Bolts:** Foundation and anchor bolts **must** be secure and tightened.
*   **Grouting:** Grouting **must** be completed.
*   **Piping Alignment:** Suction and discharge piping **must** be properly aligned and supported to prevent stress on pump nozzles. Refer to [[piping_erection_0_spc_6x95]] for general piping installation guidelines.
*   **Auxiliary Systems:** All auxiliary systems, such as seal flush and water cooling, **must** be complete. For details on pump ancillary equipment, see [[horizontal_centrifugal_pump_ancillary_equipment]].
*   **Mechanical Seal/Packing:** The mechanical seal or packing **must** be installed. Refer to [[horizontal_centrifugal_pump_shaft_seal_system]] for specifications.
*   **Accessories:** All accessories, including gauges, vents, drain valves, and connections, **must** be installed.
*   **Oil Rings:** Oil rings **must** be installed and free.
*   **Bearings:** Bearings **must** be flushed and filled to the correct level.
*   **Pump Rotation:** Free rotation of the pump **must** be checked by hand.
*   **Grounding:** Grounding **must** be completed. Refer to [[grounding_installation_details]] for specifications.
*   **Motor Rotation:** Motor rotation **must** be verified by the electrical contractor. A report verifying motor rotation **must** be attached. See [[electrical_erection_0_spc_6x99]] for electrical installation requirements.
*   **Cold Alignment:** Cold alignment **must** be completed.
*   **Coupling:** The coupling **must** be installed and lubricated.
*   **Coupling Guard:** The coupling guard **must** be installed and secure.
*   **Jacking Screws:** Jacking screws **must** be removed.
*   **Temporary Suction Strainers:** Temporary suction strainers **must** be in place.
*   **External Painting:** External painting **must** be completed with no visible defects. Refer to [[coatings_erection_0_spc_6x97]] for painting requirements.
*   **Insulation:** Insulation **must** be complete. Refer to [[insulation_erection_0_spc_6x96]] for insulation requirements.
*   **Flange Guards:** Flange Guards **must** be installed.

These items ensure the pump meets mechanical design and installation standards as per [[horizontal_centrifugal_pump_mechanical_design_requirements]].
