# Weld Preparation and Assembly for 6% Moly SS (4-SPC-0017)

This section details the requirements for weld preparation and assembly procedures for 6 percent Moly austenitic stainless steel fabrications under Standard **4-SPC-0017**.

**Welding Procedure Agreement**
The welding procedure and associated tests **will** be agreed upon before any production welding is begun. Refer to [[4-spc-0017_fabricator_welding_qualifications_and_procedures]] for details on welding procedure submittal.

**Welding Position and Environment**
As far as practical, all welds **will** be made in the downhand (flat) position. Vertical welds **will** be made vertical up (uphand). Suitable enclosures **will** be provided to keep welds and welders free from draughts, rain, or other inclement weather. Welding **will not** be performed on wet surfaces. Fusion faces and adjacent metal (internal and external) **will** be free from moisture, scale, paint, grease, or other foreign matter immediately prior to welding.

**Alignment Requirements**
Alignment of welding edges at butt joints **will** be in accordance with the relevant code or specification.
*   **Plates:**
    *   For plates up to and including 1/4" in thickness, the maximum misalignment (High - Low) **will** be 1/16".
    *   For plates above 1/4" in thickness, the maximum misalignment (High - Low) **will** be 25% of the plate thickness or 1/8", whichever is smaller.
*   **Piping:** Pipe alignment **will** be in accordance with [[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping]].
*   **Slip-on Flanges:** Prior to welding a slip-on flange, the sum of the gaps on any diameter **will not** exceed 3/16" (5mm).

**Holding Parts and Jig Construction**
Parts to be welded **will** be securely held in position by mechanical means, welded-on bridge pieces, or by tack welding to maintain specified alignment and gap. Reinforcing rods and other shaped items **will** closely conform to their matching curvature. Jig construction **will** allow adequate access for welding and cleaning, and **will** be such that external loading is not applied to a joint during welding.

Welded-on bridge pieces or support spiders **will** be of austenitic steel on the surface welded in contact with the vessel or heat exchanger. The plate surface **will not** be left in a damaged condition after removal of welded-on bridge pieces or support spiders.

**Tack Welds**
Tack welds **may** form part of the completed weld, provided complete fusion is effected at the root and the welding procedure is the same as for the remainder of the root run. Cracked and unsound tack welds **will** be cut out prior to welding. Sound tack welds **must** be thoroughly fused into the root run of the main weld. Tack welds should preferably be at least one inch long where thinning down ends is necessary.

**Back-up Rings and Inserts**
The use of permanent back-up rings or strips or consumable joint inserts is not permitted unless specifically approved in writing by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics QA and Materials Group or shown on Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Drawings.
