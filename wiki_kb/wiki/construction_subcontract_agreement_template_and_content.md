# Construction Subcontract Agreement Template and Content

The Construction Subcontract Agreement is a critical document for formalizing engagements with construction subcontractors. Its preparation and content are governed by specific procedures and templates to ensure consistency and compliance.

**Template and Initial Preparation:**
The Construction Subcontract Agreement **must** be based on the standard template 0-SUB-6X00: [Template] Construction Subcontract Agreement. This template serves as the foundational document for all construction subcontracts.

**Modification Requirements:**
*   Information contained within square brackets in template 0-SUB-6X00 **must** be modified to accurately reflect the specific requirements of the project. This ensures that each agreement is tailored while maintaining a standardized structure.
*   Conversely, the specific phrase "by Subcontractor" found within template 0-SUB-6X00 **must not** be modified under any circumstances. This ensures consistency in contractual language.

**Document Status and Approval Workflow (Initial Tender Stage):**
Upon initial preparation, the Agreement will be published in accordance with [[publishing_project_documents]] (0-PRC-0005). At this stage, its status will be "Issued for Quotation". Before issuance, the Agreement **must** undergo a rigorous review and approval process:
*   It **must** be checked by the Project Engineer.
*   It **must** be checked by Legal to ensure compliance with all relevant laws and company policies.
*   It **must** be approved by the Project Manager, signifying project-level endorsement.

**Referenced Documents:**
The Construction Subcontract Agreement directly references several administrative specifications and pricing lists, which provide detailed terms and conditions for various aspects of the subcontract. These include:
*   [[construction_subcontract_administrative_specifications]] (e.g., 0-SPC-6003, 0-SPC-6011, 0-SPC-6012, 0-SPC-6021, 0-SPC-6041, 0-SPC-6051, 0-SPC-6061, 0-SPC-6071, 0-SPC-6081, 0-SPC-6091).
*   [[construction_subcontract_pricing_lists]] (e.g., 0-LST-6X11, 0-LST-6X12, 0-LST-6X13, 0-LST-6X14, 0-LST-6X15).

**Revision and Finalization (Post-Negotiation):**
Following negotiations with the successful Subcontractor, the Agreement **must** be revised by the Subcontract Administrator. This revision process involves:
*   Incorporating all modifications agreed upon during the negotiation phase.
*   Inserting information provided by the Subcontractor as part of their bid, ensuring the final document reflects the agreed scope and terms.
The revised Agreement is then republished (0-PRC-0005) with the final status of "Approved for Construction". This final version also **must** undergo checking by the Project Engineer and Legal, and approval by the Project Manager.

**Special Conditions:**
Any exceptions or deviations from the standard administrative specifications that are agreed upon during negotiation **must** be documented by inserting appropriate text into [[special_conditions_for_construction_subcontract_0_spc_6x01]]. This ensures that all unique terms are formally captured.

**Signing and Issuance:**
A paper copy of the finalized Agreement **must** be printed and signed by the authorized personnel as defined in [[construction_subcontract_signing_authority]]. This signed copy, along with the approved [[construction_subcontractor_evaluation_and_approval_0_prc_6003]], is then forwarded to Document Control for official issuance to the Construction Subcontractor, as per [[control_of_project_documents_0_prc_0006]].
