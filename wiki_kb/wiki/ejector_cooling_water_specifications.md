# Ejector Cooling Water Specifications

This page outlines the required specifications for cooling water used in ejector systems, as detailed in the Template Ejector Datasheet (Document ID [[template_ejector_datasheet_administration]]). These parameters ensure the effective operation and heat removal capabilities of the ejector.

**Cooling Water Parameters and Units:**
*   **Flow Rate:** kg/h
*   **Available Pressure:** kPa(g)
*   **Allowable Pressure Drop:** kPa
*   **Fouling Factor:** m²·°C/W
*   **Excess Area:** %
*   **Temperature:** °C (includes both Supply and Return values)

These specifications are critical for defining the [[utility_fluid_design_conditions]] for the ejector package.

**Cooling Water Parameters and Units:**
*   **Flow Rate:** kg/h
*   **Available Pressure:** kPa(g)
*   **Allowable Pressure Drop:** kPa
*   **Fouling Factor:** m2·oC/W
*   **Excess Area:** %
*   **Temperature:** oC (including "Supply" and "Return" values)
