# Construction Subcontract Environmental Management (0-SPC-6061)

This page describes the requirements for Construction Subcontract Environmental Management as outlined in document 0-SPC-6061. The Subcontractor **must** prepare and execute site environmental management programs in accordance with the guidelines and procedures detailed in this document. This includes, but is not limited to, waste management, pollution control, and compliance with environmental regulations.

Refer to [[0-SUB-6_construction_subcontract_site_management_and_personnel]] for the specific application of this program within the overall project site management.
