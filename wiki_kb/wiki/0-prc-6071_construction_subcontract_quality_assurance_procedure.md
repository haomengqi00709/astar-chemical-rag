# 0-PRC-6071 Construction Subcontract Quality Assurance Procedure

This document, 0-PRC-6071, serves as a critical procedure outlining the roles and responsibilities of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics personnel in ensuring the quality of works supplied by Construction Subcontractors. Its primary objective is to assure that all subcontracted construction activities meet the required quality standards, thereby contributing to the overall integrity and success of the project.

The procedure details the framework for quality assurance, including the preparation and use of [[quality_data_sheets_qds]], the requirements for the [[subcontractor_quality_plan]], and the methodology for [[construction_surveillance_and_reporting]]. It also defines the process for addressing non-conformances through [[construction_non_conformance_reporting]]. The scope of surveillance activities, as described within this procedure, complements the requirements set forth in [[0-spc-6071_construction_subcontract_quality_assurance]] and individual Quality Data Sheets.

Key aspects covered by this procedure include:
*   The issuance and application of quality requirements to subcontractors.
*   The preparation, checking, and approval processes for Quality Data Sheets.
*   The requirements for a Subcontractor's Quality Plan.
*   The execution and reporting of surveillance activities by qualified Inspectors.
*   The identification, reporting, and resolution of defects in subcontracted works.

This procedure is integral to maintaining high quality standards throughout the construction phase of a project, ensuring that all subcontracted work aligns with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' specifications and industry best practices.
