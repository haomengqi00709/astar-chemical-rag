# Piping System Testing and Flushing

Testing and flushing are critical post-installation activities to verify the integrity, cleanliness, and operational readiness of process plant piping systems.

**Procedure and Supervision:**
All testing and flushing operations **will** be performed in the presence of Kvaerner Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' site representative. These activities **must** be conducted in strict accordance with [[piping_leak_testing_and_flushing_overview|Specification 5-SPC-0102, Specification for Testing and Flushing of Process Plant Piping Systems]], and [[5-SPC-0101_dry_chlorine_piping_overview|Specification 5-SPC-0101, Pipe Installation for Dry Chlorine Service]].

**Defect Rectification and Re-testing:**
Any defects, leaks, cleaning issues, or support adjustments identified during the testing process **must** be rectified promptly. Rectified items **will** subsequently be re-tested if required by Kvaerner Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' site representative to ensure full compliance and system integrity.

---

Any defects, leaks, cleaning and support adjustments identified during testing **will** be rectified. Rectified items **will** be subsequently re-tested if required by Kvaerner Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' site representative.
