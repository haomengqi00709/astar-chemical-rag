# Plant Constructability Review Roles and Responsibilities

Effective execution of the Plant Constructability Review relies on clear assignment of roles and responsibilities among key project personnel.

**Review Leader:**
The Project Engineer **shall** act as the Review Leader for the Plant Constructability Review. In projects where a Project Engineer is not assigned, the Project Manager **shall** assume the role of Review Leader (as per Revision 0 commentary to Section 4 of 0-PRC-6001).

**Responsibilities of the Project Engineer (Review Leader):**
The Project Engineer is accountable for several critical aspects of the review:
*   Ensuring that all necessary information required for the review is available to participants. Refer to [[plant_constructability_review_information_requirements]] for details on required documentation.
*   Ensuring that the Plant Constructability Review is conducted in accordance with established guidelines.
*   Ensuring that all identified actions are properly assigned to responsible parties and subsequently followed-up to completion.
*   Interpreting the results and findings of the review into clear, actionable instructions for the [[discipline_lead_and_approver_roles|Discipline Leads]] to guide the detailed design process.

**Review Attendees:**
The following key personnel **must** attend the Plant Constructability Review to provide their expertise and input:
*   The [[project_manager_pem_gate_review_responsibilities|Project Manager]].
*   The Manager of Construction.
*   Relevant [[discipline_lead_and_approver_roles|Discipline Leads]].

These roles ensure a multidisciplinary approach to constructability, integrating perspectives from project management, construction, and various engineering disciplines.

For an overview of the procedure, see [[0-prc-6001_plant_constructability_review_procedure_overview]].
