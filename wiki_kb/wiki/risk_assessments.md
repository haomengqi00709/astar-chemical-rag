# Risk Assessments

Risk assessments are systematic processes used to identify potential hazards, analyze the likelihood and severity of harm, and determine appropriate control measures. As referenced in the [[hse_incident_reporting_roles_and_responsibilities]], management personnel **must** review relevant risk assessments as part of any incident investigation. This review helps to identify if existing controls were adequate, if new hazards have emerged, or if the assessment itself needs updating to prevent future incidents.
