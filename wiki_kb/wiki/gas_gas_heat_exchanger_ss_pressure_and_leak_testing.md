# Gas/Gas Heat Exchanger - SS Pressure and Leak Testing

For the Gas/Gas Heat Exchanger - SS project (Document ID: [[0-QDS-7403-2_gas_gas_heat_exchanger_ss_quality_data_sheet]]), the following pressure and leak testing procedures are mandatory. All listed tests **must** be completed.

**Required Tests:**

*   **Pneumatic Test (2 sides):**
    *   This test is required as "Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package" (FP).
    *   Chemetics requires Witness for the Pneumatic Test (2 sides) when specified.
    *   The Pneumatic Test (2 sides) **must** be completed.

*   **Repad Air/Soap Test:**
    *   This test is required as "Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package" (FP).
    *   Chemetics requires Witness for the Repad Air/Soap Test.
    *   Chemetics requires a Hold point for the Repad Air/Soap Test, meaning work cannot proceed without Chemetics' explicit approval after witnessing.
    *   The Repad Air/Soap Test **must** be completed.

These tests are critical for verifying the pressure integrity and leak-tightness of the heat exchanger components, ensuring compliance with design specifications and safety standards.
