# Purchase Order Tests, Inspections, and Defect Management

This page outlines the requirements for tests and inspections, as well as the procedures for managing [[defect_management_process#Definition of a Defect|Defects]] identified during these processes, under the Purchase Order (PO).

**Scope of Tests and Inspections:**
*   The clause "Tests and Inspections" exclusively governs tests and inspections mandated by the [[purchase_order_definitions#Goods and Services Information|Goods and Services Information]] and the [[purchase_order_definitions#The Applicable Law|Applicable Law]].

**Defect Correction and Costs:**
*   If a test or inspection reveals a Defect in any work, the Vendor **must** correct the Defect.
*   Following the correction of a Defect, the test or inspection **must** be repeated.
*   The [[purchase_order_roles_and_responsibilities#Project Buyer|Project Buyer]] **must** be notified of any defect found.
*   The Project Buyer **must** assess the cost incurred by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for repeating a test or inspection after a Defect is found.
*   The Vendor **must** pay the assessed amount for repeating such tests or inspections.

**Pre-Delivery Inspections:**
*   The Vendor **must not** [[purchase_order_definitions#Delivery or Deliver|Deliver]] the [[purchase_order_definitions#The Goods and Services|Goods and Services]] that the Goods and Services Information specifies are to be tested or inspected before delivery, until the Project Buyer or [[purchase_order_roles_and_responsibilities#Inspector|Inspector]] has notified the Vendor that they have successfully passed the required test or inspection.
