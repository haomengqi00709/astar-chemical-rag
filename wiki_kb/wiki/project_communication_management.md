# Project Communication Management

Effective communication is critical for project success. This section outlines the protocols for communication between Aker Kvaerner and various internal and external parties.

**Owner Communications:**
Communications between Aker Kvaerner and the Owner **shall** be conducted in accordance with [[project_coordination_0_prc_2004]]. The Project Manager **shall** maintain control of all Owner correspondence files.

**Vendor Communications:**
All formal communications between Aker Kvaerner and Vendors **shall** be routed through the Project Buyer. For expedience, technical communications may occur directly between the issuer of the requisition and the Vendor. However, in such cases, the Project Buyer **must** be copied on all outgoing and incoming technical communications. The Project Buyer **shall** maintain the complete Vendor correspondence files.

**Construction Subcontractor Communications:**
Communications between Aker Kvaerner and construction Subcontractors **shall** be routed through the [blank role]. For expedience, technical communications may occur directly between the issuer of the scope of work and the Subcontractor. If technical communications occur directly, the [blank role] **must** be copied on all outgoing and incoming communications. The [blank role] **shall** maintain the complete construction Subcontractor correspondence files. Specific authorities for communications will be delegated to the Aker Kvaerner Site Manager following the mobilization of construction Subcontractors.

**Engineering Subcontractor Communications:**
Communications between Aker Kvaerner and engineering Subcontractors **shall** be routed through the respective [[discipline_lead_and_approver_roles|Discipline Lead]]. For expedience, technical communications may occur directly between the Discipline Engineer and the Subcontractor. If technical communications occur directly, the respective Discipline Lead **must** be copied on all outgoing and incoming communications. The [blank role] **shall** maintain the complete engineering Subcontractor correspondence files.

**Statutory Authority Communications:**
Communications between Aker Kvaerner and Statutory Authorities **shall** be routed through the [[discipline_lead_and_approver_roles|Discipline Lead]]. The Project Engineer **must** be copied on all outgoing and incoming communications with Statutory Authorities. The Discipline Lead **shall** maintain the Statutory Authority correspondence files.

**Confidentiality and Disclosure:**
All personnel are required to comply with the [[secrecy_agreement]] attached as an Appendix. Personnel who regularly communicate with third parties **must** be especially vigilant in their disclosure of information to ensure compliance with confidentiality agreements.
