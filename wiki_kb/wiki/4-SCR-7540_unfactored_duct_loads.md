# 4-SCR-7540 Unfactored Duct Loads

This page outlines the unfactored duct loads for the Acid Plant Converter, as specified in document **4-SCR-7540**. These loads are essential for the structural design of duct supports. All specified loads **must** be applied at the Datum.

The unfactored duct loads include:
*   **Vertical:** Applicable to Skirt / Core (kN)
*   **Shear:** (kN)
*   **Moment:** (kN-m)

For general information on equipment and duct support criteria, refer to [[equipment_support_criteria]].
