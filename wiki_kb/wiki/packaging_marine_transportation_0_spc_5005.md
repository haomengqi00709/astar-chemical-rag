# Marine Transportation Packaging Requirements

This specification defines the minimum requirements for the pre-shipment preparation and packaging of goods intended for marine transportation. The Vendor **must** ensure that all goods are adequately packaged to provide comprehensive protection against various environmental and physical hazards, including physical damage, temperature extremes, corrosion, pilferage, condensation, rain, and salt water spray.

All packaging materials supplied become the property of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and are non-returnable. Packing **must** be sufficient for extended storage periods. The Vendor **must** inform Aker Kvaerner Chemetics of any specific storage or handling requirements at the time of order placement.

**Dangerous Goods and Preparation:**
The Vendor **must** comply with all regulations governing the transportation of dangerous goods and **must** advise Aker Kvaerner Chemetics of any dangerous goods at the time of order placement. Prior to packaging, the Vendor **must** drain, dry, and clean all goods.

**Protection of Openings:**
*   Flange faces **must** be covered with an adequate shipping gasket.
*   Flange faces **must** also be covered with a 12mm wood or 5mm steel cover.
*   Flange covers **must** be securely fastened with bolting.
*   Pipe or nozzle openings designated for field welding **must** have protective covers secured by adhesive tape.
*   All other openings **must** be plugged, capped, or otherwise securely closed.

**Damage Prevention:**
The Vendor **must** provide blocking, bracing, or other necessary support to prevent damage caused by shock loading or vibration during transit. Goods will generally be packaged and shipped fully assembled and ready for their intended use. However, the Vendor may disassemble goods if necessary to prevent damage during shipment or to comply with allowable shipping dimensions or weights.

**Packaging Consistency:**
The type of packaging utilized will be as specified on the [[purchase_order_overview|Purchase Order]] and detailed in subsequent sections. The Vendor **must** package goods in a manner consistent with their size, weight, and characteristics. Packaging **must** allow for an even distribution of weight and enable handling using standard equipment such as cranes, forklift trucks, or bogies/dollies.

For administrative details of this specification, refer to [[0-SPC-5005_document_administration]]. Specific packaging methods are further detailed in [[packaging_methods_marine_transport]], and requirements for oversized items can be found in [[over_dimensional_load_requirements]].
