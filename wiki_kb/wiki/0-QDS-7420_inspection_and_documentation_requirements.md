# Filter Press Quality Data Sheet: Inspection and Documentation Requirements

For the Filter Press (0-QDS-7420), various inspection and documentation activities are required, with specific surveillance and quality record mandates.

**Required Quality Records:**

*   A **Certificate of Compliance** is required and **must** be designated as **FP** (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package).
*   The **Packing List** is required and **must** be designated as **AP** (As Prepared).

**Activities Requiring Chemetics Surveillance Witness:**

The following activities and inspections **must** include a Chemetics Surveillance Witness:

*   **External Coating/Lining Inspection:** This is a critical activity to ensure the integrity and protective qualities of the filter press's surfaces.
*   **Dimensional Final Inspection:** Verification of all critical dimensions against specifications.
*   **Contour & finish of welds Final Inspection:** Assessment of weld quality, including contour and surface finish.
*   **Surfaces free from defects Final Inspection:** Examination of all surfaces to ensure they are free from detrimental defects.
*   **Cleanliness Final Inspection:** Confirmation that the equipment meets specified cleanliness standards prior to packaging.
*   **Functional Test Final Inspection:** Verification of the filter press's operational functionality.
*   **Review QA Documentation Final Inspection:** A final review of all Quality Assurance documentation.
*   **Packaging:** Oversight of the packaging process to ensure proper protection for shipment.
*   **Packing List:** Witnessing of the packing list preparation and verification against packaged items.

**General Activity Classifications:**

The following are recognized as key items or activities within the quality assurance process:

*   **Material Verification**
*   **Coating/Lining Inspection**
*   **Final Inspection**
*   **Packing/Crating**
