# Metal Butterfly Damper Painting and Surface Treatment

Proper painting and surface treatment are essential for the protection and durability of metal butterfly dampers, particularly given their intended outdoor operating environment.

**Carbon Steel Damper Bodies:**
External surfaces of carbon steel damper bodies **SHALL** be shop primed. This initial coating provides a foundational layer of protection against corrosion and prepares the surface for subsequent coatings.

**Actuation Equipment:**
Actuation equipment **SHALL** be painted according to the Vendor’s standard paint system. This system **MUST** be suitable for severe service conditions, ensuring robust protection against environmental factors and operational stresses.

**Loose Components:**
Loose structural components and hardware associated with the damper assembly are also subject to these painting requirements, ensuring consistent protection across all parts of the equipment.
