# Equipment Testing for MV Switchgear & DC Control Power Supply

All production testing for the Medium Voltage Switchgear & DC Control Power Supply **must** be performed in accordance with ANSI/IEEE C37 standards. This includes, but is not limited to, the following tests:

**Medium Voltage Switchgear Tests:**
*   **Dielectric Tests:**
    *   **Quality Record:** [[0-QDS-7901_quality_record_definitions|FP]]
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance:** [[0-QDS-7901_chemetics_surveillance_level|Witness]]
*   **Mechanical Operation Tests:**
    *   **Quality Record:** [[0-QDS-7901_quality_record_definitions|FP]]
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance:** [[0-QDS-7901_chemetics_surveillance_level|Witness]]
*   **Grounding of Instrument Transformer Case Tests:**
    *   **Quality Record:** [[0-QDS-7901_quality_record_definitions|FP]]
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance:** [[0-QDS-7901_chemetics_surveillance_level|Witness]]
*   **Electrical Operation and Control Wiring Test:**
    *   **Quality Record:** [[0-QDS-7901_quality_record_definitions|FP]]
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance:** [[0-QDS-7901_chemetics_surveillance_level|Witness]]
*   **Control Wiring Continuity (MV Switchgear):**
    *   **Quality Record:** [[0-QDS-7901_quality_record_definitions|FP]]
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance:** [[0-QDS-7901_chemetics_surveillance_level|Witness]]
*   **Control Wiring Insulation (MV Switchgear):**
    *   **Quality Record:** [[0-QDS-7901_quality_record_definitions|FP]]
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance:** [[0-QDS-7901_chemetics_surveillance_level|Witness]]
*   **Polarity Verification (MV Switchgear):**
    *   **Quality Record:** [[0-QDS-7901_quality_record_definitions|FP]]
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance:** [[0-QDS-7901_chemetics_surveillance_level|Witness]]
*   **Sequence Tests:**
    *   **Quality Record:** [[0-QDS-7901_quality_record_definitions|FP]]
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance:** [[0-QDS-7901_chemetics_surveillance_level|Witness]]
*   **Operation Test Each Circuit Breaker and Protection Relay:**
    *   **Quality Record:** [[0-QDS-7901_quality_record_definitions|FP]]
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance:** [[0-QDS-7901_chemetics_surveillance_level|Witness]], [[0-QDS-7901_chemetics_surveillance_level|Hold]], [[0-QDS-7901_chemetics_surveillance_level|Complete]]

**DC Control Power Supply Tests:**
*   **Control Wiring Continuity (DC Control Power Supply):**
    *   **Quality Record:** [[0-QDS-7901_quality_record_definitions|FP]]
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance:** [[0-QDS-7901_chemetics_surveillance_level|Witness]]
*   **Control Wiring Insulation (DC Control Power Supply):**
    *   **Quality Record:** [[0-QDS-7901_quality_record_definitions|FP]]
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance:** [[0-QDS-7901_chemetics_surveillance_level|Witness]]
*   **Polarity Verification (DC Control Power Supply):**
    *   **Quality Record:** [[0-QDS-7901_quality_record_definitions|FP]]
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance:** [[0-QDS-7901_chemetics_surveillance_level|Witness]]
*   **Operation Test Complete Unit (DC Control Power Supply):**
    *   **Quality Record:** [[0-QDS-7901_quality_record_definitions|FP]]
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance:** [[0-QDS-7901_chemetics_surveillance_level|Witness]], [[0-QDS-7901_chemetics_surveillance_level|Hold]]

Any [[0-QDS-7901_non_destructive_examination_requirements|Non Destructive Examination]] performed for these tests **must** also be in accordance with ANSI/IEEE standards.
