# Final Inspections for Carbon Steel Tank (0-QDS-7435)

Final Inspections for the Carbon Steel Tank, as specified in Item 24.0 of QDS 0-QDS-7435, are conducted to verify the overall quality, integrity, and readiness of the tank before shipment or installation. Chemetics' involvement in these inspections is mandatory.

**Mandatory Final Inspections and Chemetics' Role:**
*   **Dimensional Final Inspection:**
    *   Chemetics **must** witness Dimensional Final Inspection.
    *   Chemetics **must** hold Dimensional Final Inspection.
*   **Contour & Finish of Welds Final Inspection:**
    *   Chemetics **must** witness Contour & Finish of Welds Final Inspection.
    *   Chemetics **must** hold Contour & Finish of Welds Final Inspection.
*   **Surfaces Free from Defects Final Inspection:**
    *   Chemetics **must** witness Surfaces Free from Defects Final Inspection.
    *   Chemetics **must** hold Surfaces Free from Defects Final Inspection.
*   **Cleanliness Final Inspection:**
    *   Chemetics **must** witness Cleanliness Final Inspection.
    *   Chemetics **must** hold Cleanliness Final Inspection.

These inspections ensure that the tank meets all specified design, fabrication, and cleanliness criteria. For a complete list of all witnessing and holding requirements, refer to [[0-QDS-7435_inspection_and_documentation_requirements]].
