# Electrical Installation Specification

The Electrical Installation Specification describes in detail the requirements for the installation of electrical equipment and systems. This document ensures that all installations comply with applicable codes, standards, and project-specific methods.

**Content and Purpose**
The electrical installation specification **shall** describe in detail requirements for equipment installation. It **shall** identify codes, standards, and regulations applicable to the installation.

This specification **will** supplement the [[construction_subcontract_scope]] document by describing the methods of installation expected of the Contractor. It **will** reference the Construction subcontract supply and erection scope list for clarification of supply of equipment and materials.
