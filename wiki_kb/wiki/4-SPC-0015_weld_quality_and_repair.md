# 4-SPC-0015 Weld Quality and Repair

This page details the requirements for maintaining weld quality, inter-pass cleaning, handling re-starts, addressing contamination, and performing repairs for fabrications under Standard 4-SPC-0015.

**Inter-Pass Cleaning and Re-Starts**
*   Each run of weld metal **will** be carefully examined to ensure that it has been thoroughly cleaned and is defect free.
*   All slag and foreign matter **will** be removed before the next run is deposited. Removal of slag and foreign matter may be achieved by grinding followed by wire brushing.
*   On re-starting, care **will** be taken to ensure proper fusion with the weld metal previously deposited.
*   All re-starts in the root bead **will** be preceded by "feathering" out the area of the stop by grinding or filing, and followed with wire brushing to remove any abrasive residue.

**Contamination During Welding**
*   If a tungsten electrode accidentally makes contact with the work, the section of weld and the tip of the electrode should be examined. Where tungsten loss has occurred, the tungsten pick-up in the weld **will** be ground out, and the electrode **will** be reground to the correct profile before restarting.
*   If the heated end of the filler rod becomes contaminated, it **will** be snipped off and discarded.

**Weld Finishing Restrictions**
*   Peening of weld runs is **not permitted**.
*   Hammering of finished welds is **not permitted**.

**Weld Appearance and Defect Limits**
*   Each weld **will** be uniform in width and size throughout its full length.
*   Each layer of welding **will** be smooth and free of slag, inclusions, cracks, porosity, lack of fusion, and undercut except to the extent permitted in the referenced codes.
*   The cover pass **will** be free of coarse ripples, irregular surface, non-uniform bead patterns, high crown, and deep ridges or valleys between beads.
*   Buttwelds **will** be slightly convex, of uniform height, and have full penetration.
*   Internal reinforcement of pipe welds **will** be subject to the limitations of the governing code.
*   Crevices or undercut **will not** be permitted on the process side of the weld.
*   Fillet welds **will** be deposited in not less than two layers as far as is practicable.
*   Fillet weld surfaces **will** be free from marked irregularities or concavity.
*   Dimensions of fillet welds **will** be in accordance with the approved drawing.

**Grinding and Repair Tools**
*   Grinding or gouging **will not** be carried out in such a manner as to reduce the adjacent base metal thickness.
*   Complete removal of weld defects **will** be verified by non-destructive examination prior to repair welding.
*   Grinding wheels **will** be iron-free and of the resin bonded silicon carbide type.
*   Wire brushes or chipping tools **will** be made of austenitic stainless steel and **must not** have been previously used to clean surfaces other than Incoloy 800 or 825.
