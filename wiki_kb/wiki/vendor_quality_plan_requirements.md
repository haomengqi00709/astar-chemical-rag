# Vendor Quality Plan Requirements

The Vendor's Quality Plan is a critical component of ensuring compliance with the Purchase Order and Specification [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment|0-SPC-7001]]. Where required by the reference [[quality_data_sheets_qds]], the Vendor **shall** establish, implement, and maintain a Quality Plan.

**Purpose and Scope**
The Quality Plan **must** be in accordance with Specification [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment|0-SPC-7001]] and **shall** document the Vendor's commitment to and implementation of quality assurance. It **must** detail the procedures used to ensure that all items, activities, or services comply with the requirements of the Purchase Order.

**Integration with Existing Systems**
Preferably, the Quality Plan will reference the Vendor’s existing Quality Management System (QMS). If the Vendor has previously received approval of their QMS and implementing procedures from Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, the Quality Plan may simply refer to these approved documents. In such cases, the Quality Plan **should** certify that no revisions have been made to the QMS since its approval.

**Minimum Requirements**
The elements outlined in Specification [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment|0-SPC-7001]] are considered the minimum requirements for the Quality Plan. These minimum requirements do not relieve the Vendor from considering and complying with any other criteria deemed necessary to achieve an effective Quality Plan.

**Organizational Structure and Responsibilities**
The Vendor **shall** clearly identify the groups or persons within their organization responsible for:
*   The establishment and effective implementation of the Quality Plan.
*   The verification of Quality Plan compliance.

Furthermore, the Vendor **shall** document:
*   The organizational structure.
*   Functional responsibilities.
*   Levels of authority and reporting lines for all activities affecting quality.

The Quality Plan **must** also incorporate the [[vendor_inspection_and_test_plan|Inspection and Test Plan]] as described in Section 3 of Specification 0-SPC-7001, and procedures for [[vendor_non_conformance_management|handling non-conformances]] as described in Section 5.
