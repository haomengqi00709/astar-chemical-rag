# Vendor Insurance Table (0-SPC-5003)

This page describes the content of the Insurance Table referenced in document **0-SPC-5003**.

The [[0-SPC-5003_insurance_table|Insurance Table]] **must** explicitly state any additional [[insurance_requirements|insurance]] that the Vendor is required to provide under the terms of Specification **0-SPC-5003**. This table serves as a critical reference for the specific types and levels of coverage, ensuring compliance with the [[0-SPC-5003_vendor_insurance_requirements|vendor insurance requirements]]. All insurances listed in this table **shall** be in the joint names of the Parties and provide coverage for events at the Vendor's risk from the starting date until the Defects Certificate has been issued.
