# Horizontal Centrifugal Pump Drive System

The drive system for a horizontal centrifugal pump, specified in Document ID [[template_horizontal_centrifugal_pump_datasheet_administration]], encompasses the motor and its associated characteristics. This system is responsible for providing the necessary power to operate the pump.

**Motor Specifications:**

*   **Motor supply:** *Shall be specified*. This indicates whether the motor is supplied by the pump vendor or by another party.
*   **Motor mounting:** *Shall be specified*. This details how the motor is physically attached to the pump or base plate.
*   **Motor Specification:** *Shall be specified*. This refers to a detailed document outlining the electrical and mechanical requirements for the motor.
*   **Motor Size:** *Shall be specified*. This indicates the power rating of the motor, typically in kilowatts (kW) or horsepower (HP).

The motor's specifications are crucial for matching the pump's power requirements, which are determined by the [[horizontal_centrifugal_pump_process_design_conditions]] and [[horizontal_centrifugal_pump_mechanical_design_requirements]]. The drive system works in conjunction with [[horizontal_centrifugal_pump_ancillary_equipment]] such as couplings and base plates to form a complete and functional pump unit.
