# Purchase Order Overview and General Conditions (0-SPC-5001)

This document, identified as **0-SPC-5001**, outlines the general conditions and administrative framework for Purchase Orders issued by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. It establishes the roles, definitions, and foundational legal aspects governing the procurement of goods and services.

**Parties and Definitions**
The primary parties involved in a Purchase Order are Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Vendor. Other key roles include the Project Buyer, Lead Engineer, and the Owner. Terms identified in the Purchase Order are presented in *italics*, while defined terms begin with capital initials.

*   **Parties:** Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Vendor.
*   **Others:** Individuals or organizations not directly Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, the Project Buyer, the Lead Engineer, the Vendor, the Owner, or any employee or Supplier of the Vendor.
*   **Project:** Refers to the facilities being constructed, designed, and/or procured by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for the Owner.
*   **Owner:** Has a corresponding meaning to the Project definition.
*   **Applicable Law:** Any binding law enacted, issued, or promulgated by any Government, government agency, or corporation established by government with jurisdiction over the Project or the Goods and Services, including common law or law arising by custom or usage. See [[applicable_law_and_compliance]].
*   **Supplier:** A person who has a contract with the Vendor to provide part of the Goods and Services.
*   **Goods and Services:** The specific goods and services purchased from the Vendor.
*   **Goods and Services Information:** Information that specifies and describes the Goods and Services or states constraints on how the Vendor Provides them. This information is found in documents referenced in the Purchase Order or in instructions given in accordance with the Purchase Order. See [[goods_and_services_definitions]].
*   **Purchase Order Date:** The date when the Purchase Order came into existence.
*   **Starting Date:** The date stated on the Purchase Order from which it becomes effective.
*   **Delivery, Deliver, Delivered:** The Vendor delivering those parts of the Goods and Services as described in the Purchase Order.
*   **Delivery Date:** The specified delivery date, unless later changed in accordance with the Purchase Order.
*   **Defect:** A part of the Goods and Services that is not in accordance with the Goods and Services Information, or, if designed by the Vendor, not in accordance with the Applicable Law or the Vendor's design accepted by the Lead Engineer. See [[defect_management_process]].
*   **Defects Correction Period:** A period of 4 weeks for correcting notified Defects.
*   **Defects Date:** The earlier of (1) the date 52 weeks after the last to occur of Delivery of the whole of the Goods and Services and successful start up of the Project, and (2) the date 78 weeks after Delivery of the whole of the Goods and Services.
*   **Prices:** The lump sum prices for each item in the Purchase Order, unless later changed.
*   **Person:** Includes any legal entity.

**Scope and Governance**
Unless stated as the whole of the Goods and Services, each reference and clause relevant to the Goods and Services, Completion, and Completion Date applies to either the whole or any part of the Goods and Services.

This Purchase Order is governed by the law of British Columbia, Canada. [[incoterms_2000_application|Incoterms 2000]] apply, but the [[un_convention_international_sale_of_goods|United Nations Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods]] does not.

**Communication and Administration**
All required communications must be in a form that can be read, copied, and recorded, and must be in English. Any communication from the Vendor to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or its representatives **must** be copied to the [[project_buyer_roles|Project Buyer]]. If a reply is required, it **shall** be provided within one week. Acceptance of Vendor communications or work by an Aker Kvaerner Chemetics representative does not alter the Vendor’s responsibility or liability.

The [[project_buyer_roles|Project Buyer]] and [[lead_engineer_roles|Lead Engineer]] may delegate or cancel delegations of their actions after notifying the Vendor. The Project Buyer may issue instructions to change the Goods and Services Information or the Delivery Date. However, changes to the Price, a date for either party's action, or the Delivery Date only occur when a Revision to the Purchase Order is issued by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and accepted by the Vendor. The Project Buyer **shall** give an instruction to resolve any ambiguity or inconsistency within the Purchase Order documents.

For the order of precedence in interpreting commercial conditions, refer to [[0-SPC-5001_commercial_conditions_precedence]]. Documents referenced in Section 6 of the Purchase Order Form apply only when they do not conflict with other Purchase Order documents.

The Vendor accepts the terms and conditions of the Purchase Order by acknowledging receipt or by commencing to Provide the Goods and Services.
