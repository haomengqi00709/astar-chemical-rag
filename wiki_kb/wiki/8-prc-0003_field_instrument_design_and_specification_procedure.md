# Field Instrument Design and Specification Procedure (8-PRC-0003)

The 8-PRC-0003 document outlines the procedure for the design and specification of field instrumentation within projects. This procedure ensures consistency and adherence to project and company standards.

**Scope of Supply Requirements**
The Field Instrument Engineer (FIE) and Detail Design Engineer (DDE) are responsible for determining which documents apply to the specific scope of supply requirements as defined by project contract documents.

**Document Preparation, Checking, and Approval**
Project engineering and procurement procedures for document preparation, checking, and approval shall remain consistent with established company standards.

**Client Requirements and Standard Practices**
If the client has not provided specific instrument design requirements, the DDE shall provide [[instrumentation_and_control_design_criteria_8_prc_0002]] as per standard 8-PRC-0002 and standard engineering practices. The Project Engineer **must** provide an opportunity for the FIE to review and comment on the Instrument Design Criteria prior to its final publication and issue.

**Plant Instrument Numbering**
[[plant_instrument_numbering_8_prc_0001]] shall follow Standard-8-PRC-0001 where applicable. If project requirements dictate the use of a Client numbering procedure, the DDE **shall** revise Standard-8-PRC-0001 to incorporate the Client's requirements and publish accordingly. This modification to include the Client numbering system **must** be an add-on to the standard document, clearly defining itself as the procedure to be used on the project. The standard portion of Standard-8-PRC-0001 **is not** to be revised.
