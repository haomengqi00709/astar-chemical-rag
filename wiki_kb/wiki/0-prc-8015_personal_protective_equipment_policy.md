# Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) Policy

This specification (Document ID: 0-PRC-8015) describes the minimum requirements for Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) to be used in construction or fabrication activities. The policy ensures the safety and well-being of all personnel on site.

**General Requirements**
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics employees **must** wear the required (or recommended) protective equipment necessary for entry into a work area or for the performance of a specific task. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **shall** provide all protective equipment at no cost to the employee, with some limitations on frequency. Protective equipment **shall** also be provided for visitors where and as necessary.
