# Eductor Material Specification

The **Materials of Construction** section of the Eductor Datasheet (4-PDS-XXXX-R1) specifies the materials required for various components of the eductor to ensure compatibility with the process fluids and operating conditions. This is a critical aspect of [[material_selection]] and adherence to [[general_mechanical_design_requirements_0_spc_0010]].

Specific material requirements are detailed for:
*   **Inlet:** Material for the eductor inlet connection.
*   **Suction:** Material for the suction connection, where the pumped fluid enters.
*   **Nozzle:** Material for the eductor's motive fluid nozzle, which creates the jet action.
*   **Discharge:** Material for the eductor's discharge connection.
