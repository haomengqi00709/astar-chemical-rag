# Construction Subcontractor Design Document Review and Approval Process (0-SPC-6091)

This page outlines the process for the review and approval of design documents prepared by the Construction Subcontractor, as specified in document 0-SPC-6091.

**Work Commencement Restrictions**
The Subcontractor **must not** commence any work shown on a design document they have prepared until the document has been reviewed by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and a formal notice to proceed has been issued. This restriction also applies to any revised portions of a design document; work on such revisions **must not** commence until the revised document has been reviewed and a notice to proceed has been given.

**Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Response**
Upon receipt of a design document, Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **will** respond within 7 working days. The response **will** be provided by returning a marked-up copy of the document in Adobe Portable Document Format to the Subcontractor.

Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' response **will** contain one of the following explicit directions:
1.  No Revisions Required; Proceed with Work Shown.
2.  Revise as Marked and Reissue; Proceed with Work Shown.
3.  Revise as Marked and Reissue; Do Not Proceed with Work Shown.

**Subcontractor Compliance and Reissue**
The Subcontractor **must** comply with the direction provided by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. When directed to reissue documents, the reissue **must** occur within 7 working days.
