# Prohibited Substances and Conduct

Aker Kvaerner Chemetics maintains a strict policy regarding substance use in the workplace to ensure the safety and well-being of all personnel and to comply with all applicable regulations.

**Policy Statement**
Being on-the-job under the influence of alcohol or drugs is strictly **not acceptable** and **will not be tolerated** at any Aker Kvaerner Chemetics work site, including the Design Office and Research Laboratory. This policy applies to all employees, contract employees, indirect employees, Vendor employees, and visitors.

Adherence to this policy is a mandatory requirement for maintaining a safe and productive work environment. Violations of this policy **will** result in disciplinary action as outlined in company procedures.
