# Piping Test Preparation and Equipment Management

Thorough preparation of piping systems and associated equipment is crucial before commencing any leak testing. This involves specific steps for exposing joints, managing in-line components, and ensuring safety.

**General Preparation Requirements**
*   Each line and associated equipment **must** be prepared for testing.
*   Approval of the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site representative **must** be received prior to the application of any test.
*   All threaded, segmented, fusion welded joints, and bolted connections in the system to be tested **will** be exposed.
*   Lines **must not** be painted, insulated, or covered until after the tests have been accepted.
*   [[piping_preparation_for_testing]] for the [[initial_service_leak_test]] **will** be the same as for a [[hydrostatic_testing]].

**Blinding Requirements**
*   The piping contractor **will** provide and install the necessary blinds where lines are installed but not connected to equipment.
*   The piping contractor **will** provide and install the necessary blinds before connections to equipment that are not part of the test.
*   The contractor **will** remove all blinds used for testing after completion of tests.
*   All blinds used **will** be numbered and accounted for at the completion of tests.
*   In-line valves may be used to isolate lines for testing instead of blinds.
*   For more details, refer to [[blinding_requirements]].

**Equipment Exclusion and Management**
Certain equipment and components **must** be managed carefully or excluded from testing:
*   Pumps and other equipment containing pressurized shaft seals **will not** be pressure tested as part of a system. They **must** be blanked off with removable blinds.
*   Glass components should be excluded from testing. If not excluded, they **will** be fully shielded.
*   [[equipment_exclusion_from_testing]] is critical for preventing damage.
*   All piping systems which are hydraulically tested **will** be vented at the high point and purged of air before the test pressure is applied. See [[venting_and_purging_for_hydraulic_tests]].

**Pre-Test Cleaning and Inspection**
*   Prior to the introduction of water, piping and equipment **must** be inspected.
*   All dirt, debris, and loose material **must** be removed prior to the introduction of water. Refer to [[cleaning_and_inspection_pre_test]].

**In-Line Equipment Management**
*   Whenever possible, lines **will** be tested with all in-line equipment (e.g., [[control_valve_installation]], flow transmitters) in place.
*   Control valves **must** be placed in the fully open position unless the valve is being used to terminate a section for test.
*   Branch valves to pressure gauges, differential pressure transmitters, and similar branches **will** be closed to prevent the entry of dirt.
*   The following items should be removed or blanked off for test:
    *   [[expansion_joints_installation]]
    *   [[relief_valve_removal_for_testing]]
    *   [[rupture_disc_installation]]
*   The following items should normally be installed after completion of tests:
    *   [[orifice_plate_installation]]
    *   [[spray_nozzle_installation]]
    *   Similar restrictions
*   Each line **will** be reviewed prior to test with the [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_site_supervisor_review]] to identify items for removal, blanking off, or support.

**General Testing Principles**
*   Piping should be tested as systems and in sections as the installation work progresses, wherever possible.

**Approval**
*   Approval of the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site representative must be received prior to application of the test.

**General Preparation Requirements**
*   Each line and associated equipment **must** be prepared for testing.
*   All threaded, segmented, fusion welded joints and bolted connections in the system to be tested will be exposed.
*   Do not paint, insulate or cover a line until after the tests have been accepted.

**Blinds and Isolation**
*   The piping contractor will provide and install the necessary blinds where lines are installed but not connected to equipment.
*   The piping contractor will provide and install the necessary blinds before the connections to equipment that are not part of the test.
*   The contractor will remove all blinds used for testing after completion of tests.
*   All blinds used will be numbered and accounted for at the completion of tests.
*   In-line valves may be used to isolate lines for testing instead of blinds.

**Equipment Exclusion/Management**
*   Pumps and other equipment containing pressurized shaft seals will not be pressure tested as part of a system. Such equipment must be blanked off with removable blinds.
*   Glass components should be excluded from testing. If glass components are not excluded, they will be fully shielded.

**In-line Equipment Management**
*   Whenever possible, lines will be tested with all in-line equipment (control valves, flow transmitters etc.) in place.
*   Control valves must be placed in the fully open position unless the valve is being used to terminate a section for test.
*   Branch valves to pressure gauges, differential pressure transmitters and similar branches will be closed to prevent the entry of dirt.
*   Expansion joints should be removed or blanked off for test.
*   Relief valves should be removed or blanked off for test.
*   Rupture discs should be removed or blanked off for test.
*   Orifice plates should normally be installed after completion of tests.
*   Spray nozzles should normally be installed after completion of tests.
*   Similar restrictions should normally be installed after completion of tests.

**Review and Approval**
*   Each line will be reviewed prior to test with the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site representative to identify items for removal, blanking off, or support.
