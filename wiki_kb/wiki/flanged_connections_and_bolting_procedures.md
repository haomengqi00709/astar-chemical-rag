# Flanged Connections and Bolting Procedures

Proper execution of flanged connections and bolting is critical for the integrity and leak-tightness of piping systems, especially in chemical processing environments.

**Flange Guards:**
[[flange_guard_installation|Flange guards]] are provided for lines containing strong acids and caustics. Requirements for flange guards are indicated on the [[piping_line_list]].

**Bolting Materials and Tightening:**
Flanged connections **will** be made using bolting as specified in the [[pipe_specification|pipe material specifications]]. All bolting **must** be tightened with suitable wrenches or sockets only; adjustable "crescent" type wrenches **must not** be used. Bolting **will** be tightened in the correct pattern and to the torque specified by the manufacturer or generally accepted industry standards. When tightening joints, care **must** be taken to secure uniform pressure and to avoid over-stressing bolts or dishing flanges. Gaskets **will** be carefully centered on the flange before tightening.

**Bolt Projection:**
Bolt lengths **will** be approximately 6 mm (1/4 inch) past the nut after final tightening. In no case **should** the bolt projection be less than one exposed complete thread.

**Metric and Imperial Bolting for Tapped Components:**
On projects where metric bolting is specified, tapped piping components (including PTFE bellows and lug tapped valves) may require imperial bolting. Bolting materials for tapped components requiring imperial bolting **will** be of an equivalent specification to those listed in the applicable [[pipe_specification|pipe specification]]. Studs **will** be run into tapped components by using a second nut as a jamb nut and threading the stud in using standard wrenches. Under no circumstances **must** studs be run into tapped components using pliers or "Vice-grips".

**Plastic Flanges:**
Particular attention **must** be given to bolting flat face plastic flanges to raised face or lined valves and equipment. Bolting for plastic flanges **must** be tightened in the appropriate sequence and torqued to the plastic flange manufacturer’s specifications to prevent over-stressing or cracking of the plastic flange.

**Galvanized Threaded Rod and Fastener Coating:**
If galvanized threaded rod is supplied and **must** be field cut to length, the cut end **will** be chamfered to allow free run of the nut. Exposed ends of carbon steel fasteners **will** be coated with "Galvacon" or an equivalent cold galvanizing compound.
