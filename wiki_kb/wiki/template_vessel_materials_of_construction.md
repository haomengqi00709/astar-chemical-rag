# Template Vessel Materials of Construction (4-PDS-XXXX-R2)

The "Materials of Construction" section is a critical component of the Template Vessel Process Data Sheet (4-PDS-XXXX-R2). This section explicitly defines the materials to be used for the fabrication of the vessel and its internal components, directly impacting the vessel's integrity, longevity, and suitability for the specified process conditions.

**Section Presence:**
The "Materials of Construction" section **must** be present in the 4-PDS-XXXX-R2 document. It serves as the primary reference for material selection, ensuring compliance with design specifications, process compatibility, and regulatory requirements.

**Importance of Material Selection:**
The selection of appropriate materials is paramount, especially given the diverse range of fluids and operating conditions outlined in the [[template_vessel_process_design_conditions]]. Factors such as corrosion resistance, temperature limits, mechanical strength, and cost are all considered when specifying materials. This section guides fabricators and quality control personnel in verifying that the correct materials are used throughout the manufacturing process.
