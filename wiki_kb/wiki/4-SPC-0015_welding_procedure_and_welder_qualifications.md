# Welding Procedure and Welder Qualifications (4-SPC-0015)

All welding procedures and welder qualifications for work under Standard 4-SPC-0015 **must** strictly adhere to the requirements outlined below, ensuring compliance with relevant codes and standards, particularly [[ASME_section_ix_welding_and_brazing_qualifications]].

**Welding Procedure Requirements**
*   All welding procedures **must** be submitted by the Fabricator for review and agreement by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics QA & Materials Group prior to the commencement of any work.
*   The ASME IX PQR Form 483 **must** form part of the welding procedure package.
*   The welding procedure **must** be qualified on the same grade of material as that selected for fabrication.
*   A change in essential variables, as outlined in [[ASME_section_ix_welding_and_brazing_qualifications]], will require resubmission of the procedure(s).
*   The welding procedure **will be agreed upon** (as per Paragraph 5.3 of the original specification) before any production welding is begun.

**Welder Qualification Requirements**
*   All welders employed on Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' work **must** be suitably qualified in accordance with relevant codes and standards for every procedure they are to use.
*   The Fabricator **must**, upon request, supply Aker Kvaerner Chemetics with objective evidence of current welder qualifications.
*   All qualified welders **must** be allocated an identification symbol. This identification symbol **must** be marked on the parent material adjacent to each welder's work to allow for traceability during fabrication.

These stringent requirements ensure that all welding operations are performed by competent personnel using approved and qualified procedures, contributing to the overall [[4-SPC-0015_fabricator_quality_assurance_and_documentation]].

**Welding Procedure Requirements**
*   The ASME IX PQR Form 483 **must** form part of the welding procedure package.
*   The welding procedure **must** be qualified on the same grade of material as that selected for fabrication.
*   A change in essential variables as outlined in ASME Code Section IX **will** require resubmission of procedure(s).

**Welder Qualifications**
*   Fabricator **must**, upon request, supply Aker Kvaerner Chemetics with objective evidence of current welder qualifications.
*   All qualified welders **must** be allocated an identification symbol.
*   The identification symbol **must** be marked on the parent material adjacent to each welder's work. This marking allows traceability during fabrication.
