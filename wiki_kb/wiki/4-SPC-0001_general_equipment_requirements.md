# 4-SPC-0001 General Equipment Requirements

This document defines the overarching general requirements applicable to the design, fabrication, and supply of various types of equipment. It serves as a foundational specification to ensure consistency and adherence to fundamental engineering and quality standards across different equipment categories.

For specific details regarding horizontal centrifugal magnetic drive pumps, refer to [[4-SPC-0003_horizontal_centrifugal_magnetic_drive_pump_specification_overview]].
