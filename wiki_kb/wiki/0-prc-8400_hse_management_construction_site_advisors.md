# HSE Management for Company Construction Site Advisors

This standard procedure (Standard-0-PRC-8400) details the specific Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) management responsibilities for Company Construction Site Advisors. This applies when Aker Kvaerner Chemetics provides Construction Advisory Services rather than having direct responsibility for construction.

**Advisor Responsibilities:**
In this advisory capacity, the Company Site Construction Advisor assumes the HSE management responsibilities for Company employees, direct subcontractors, and vendors on site. This ensures that even in an advisory role, the Company's personnel and directly managed entities adhere to required HSE standards.

This document is a relevant reference for commissioning HSE Managers, especially when the construction HSE Manager continues through the commissioning phase, as outlined in [[hse_commissioning_sites]]. It complements the general [[0-spc-8003_hse_responsibilities_field_managers]].
