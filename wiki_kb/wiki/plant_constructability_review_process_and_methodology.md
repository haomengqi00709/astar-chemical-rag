# Plant Constructability Review Process and Methodology

The Plant Constructability Review is a collaborative and interactive process guided by the [[0-prc-6001_table_1_plant_constructability_review_checklist]]. While the checklist provides structure, the review encourages open discussion and adaptability.

**Review Conduct:**
*   The Review Leader **shall** determine the specific review method and the time allocated to each item on the checklist.
*   Participants **shall** collectively decide on the applicability of any particular item listed on the checklist to the project's specific context.
*   Participants are encouraged to raise any additional applicable items for review that may not be explicitly listed on the checklist.
*   Open and frank discussion **should** be encouraged throughout the review (Revision 1 commentary to Section 6).
*   No formalized process is contemplated for the review; its success depends heavily on the Review Leader and the interactive participation of attendees, as well as their ability to recognize areas of particular concern (Revision 1 commentary to Section 6).
*   The checklist does not differentiate the importance of listed items, as areas of concern are variable between projects (Revision 1 commentary to Section 6).
*   The majority of review items will typically require a short discussion evaluating the merits of possible alternatives or recording an action for further review (Revision 1 commentary to Section 6).

**Documentation and Follow-up:**
*   Information presented and discussed during the review **must** be recorded under the "Record" section of the [[0-prc-6001_table_1_plant_constructability_review_checklist]].
*   Any follow-up actions identified during the review **must** be recorded on the checklist.
*   Responsibilities for these follow-up actions **must** also be clearly recorded on the checklist.

**Approval and Publication:**
The review process includes two stages of approval and publication:
1.  **Initial Publication:** After the review is completed, the Project Engineer **shall** publish the checklist as "Approved for Design (for Follow-up)". This initial publication **must** also be approved by the Project Manager.
2.  **Final Publication:** Once all follow-up actions have been completed and verified, the Project Engineer **shall** publish the checklist as "Approved for Design (Final)". This final publication **must** also be approved by the Project Manager.

**Checklist Customization:**
The [[0-prc-6001_table_1_plant_constructability_review_checklist]] **should** be modified to suit the specific project scope of supply (Revision 1 commentary to Section 1). This ensures its relevance and effectiveness for each unique project.

For an overview of the procedure, see [[0-prc-6001_plant_constructability_review_procedure_overview]]. The roles involved in this process are detailed in [[plant_constructability_review_roles_and_responsibilities]], and the required information is listed in [[plant_constructability_review_information_requirements]]. The general [[document_approval_and_revision_process]] applies to the checklist publication.
