# Construction Subcontract Scope of Works (0-SUB-6)

This page details the specific scope of works and material responsibilities for Construction Subcontract Agreement 0-SUB-6, concerning the foundations for a hydrochloric acid manufacturing plant.

**Works Overview:**
The Subcontractor, Bob the Builder Pty Ltd, is responsible for the construction and erection of the works at the Stanwell Magnesium Project site.

**Material Supply and Title:**
*   The Subcontractor **must** receive and store free-issue Equipment and Materials provided by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or by other designated parties.
*   The Subcontractor **must** supply all other Equipment and Materials required for the completion of the works.
*   Title to surplus materials remains with the Subcontractor only if those materials were supplied by the Subcontractor and not directly paid for as a [[compensation_events_and_assessment_0_spc_6001]].
