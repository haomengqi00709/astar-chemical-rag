# Refractory Fire Brick Installation

The installation of refractory fire brick demands precision in fitting, cutting, and mortar application to ensure the integrity and longevity of the lining.

**Fitting and Cutting**
Refractory fire bricks that require fitting **must** be accurately laid out. These bricks **shall** be wet cut with a diamond blade.

**Mortar Joint Requirements**
Refractory fire bricks **must** be laid up with thin mortar joints. The average mortar joint thickness for refractory fire brick **shall** be 1.6mm. Joints that are 3mm or thicker are not acceptable and **will** be subject to rejection. Each refractory fire brickwork joint **shall** be completely filled with mortar, with the exception of areas where expansion joints are specifically provided. The inside face of the refractory fire brick **must** be left reasonably free of mortar.

**Alignment and Appearance**
Vertical joints in refractory fire brick **shall** be staggered to ensure structural stability. Courses of refractory fire brick **must** be held straight to line, true to a straight edge, and flush on the surface.

**Inspection**
The installation of refractory fire brick is subject to inspection at various stages to verify adherence to this specification and to ensure high standards of workmanship. For comprehensive inspection details, refer to [[refractory_brick_lining_inspection_and_acceptance]].
