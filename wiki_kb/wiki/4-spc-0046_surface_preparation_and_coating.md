# Sulphuric Acid Storage Tank Surface Preparation and Coating (4-SPC-0046)

This section details the requirements for surface preparation and coating application for sulphuric acid storage tanks, as specified in 4-SPC-0046.

**Interior Surface Preparation**
After erection, all interior steel surfaces, including structural members, **must** be blast cleaned to SSPC-SP6. Immediately prior to making final connections, these surfaces **must** be broom swept and vacuum cleaned to remove all residual scale, slag, rust, and other residues. If all connections are not ready, openings **must** be blind flanged after the vacuum clean-up to prevent re-contamination.

For tanks designated for internal protection with a phenolic coating, the interior surfaces **must** be blast cleaned to SSPC-SP5 (white metal finish).

**Exterior Surface Preparation and Priming**
All external surfaces of shop-fabricated tanks, including the floor, **must** be primed in accordance with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' Standard [[es44_c_03_priming_standard|ES44\C-03]]. For site-fabricated items, all steel **must** arrive at the site unpainted unless specifically noted otherwise in the project documentation.

**Phenolic Coating Requirements**
When phenolic coating of the internal tank surface is required, this **will** be explicitly noted on the engineering drawings. The detailed requirements for phenolic coating **will** be covered in a separate, dedicated document.
