# Construction Subcontractor Document Revision and Status Management (0-SPC-6091)

This page details the requirements for managing document revisions and statuses for documentation supplied by the Construction Subcontractor under document 0-SPC-6091.

**Revision Marking**
The Subcontractor **must** clearly highlight revisions on documents:
*   Drawings: Revisions **must** be indicated by clouding.
*   Textual and Tabular Documents: Revisions **must** be marked with a vertical line in the left-hand margin.

**Revision Process**
Following any revision, the Subcontractor **must** increment the revision number and update the document status accordingly. The status **must** be marked on each document prior to its issuance to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Document Status Definitions**
The following statuses are defined for documents:
*   **Approved for Design**: This status indicates that the information contained within the document may be used for the design of ancillary components not supplied by the Subcontractor. Examples include support structures, foundations, and power supply systems.
*   **Issued for Review**: This status is to be used only if Aker Kvaerner Chemetics has not yet granted approval to proceed with the work described, as detailed in Section 11 of the main specification.
*   **Approved for Construction**: This status signifies that the information will be used for the manufacturing and erection of the work. This status **must** only be applied once all revisions requested by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics have been incorporated and Aker Kvaerner Chemetics has provided approval to proceed, as described in Section 11.
*   **Approved Instruction**: This status indicates that the information may be used by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or the Owner for the operation or maintenance of the work.
*   **As Built**: This status denotes that the information is a record of the final condition of the completed work.

**Status Qualifiers**
Limitations on the use of a document may be specified by adding a qualifier to the status (e.g., “Approved for Design (Layout only)”).

**Definition of "Approved"**
The term “Approved” when applied to document status, means that the document has been approved by the Subcontractor for the stated use, in accordance with their [[subcontractor_quality_plan]].
