# Instrument Sizing Calculations (8-CAL-XXXX)

Instrument Sizing Calculations (8-CAL-XXXX) provide the necessary engineering analysis to ensure instruments are correctly sized for their intended application.

**Content Requirements:**
The Instrument Sizing Calculations (8-CAL-XXXX) **shall** contain:
*   Calculation methods and formulas.
*   Acceptance parameters or criteria.
*   All calculation input data.
*   The executed calculation.

**Instruments Requiring Calculations:**
Calculations **will be provided** as a minimum for:
*   Control Valves
*   Pressure Regulators
*   Pressure Safety Valves
*   Magnetic Flow Meters
*   Mass Flow Meters
*   Vortex Meters
*   Orifice Plates
*   Annubar’s
*   Rotameters

**Document Preparation and Approval:**
*   **Prepared by:** Field Instrument Engineer (FIE)
*   **Approved by:** Field Instrument Engineer (FIE)

**Process and Integration:**
The FIE will produce Instrument Sizing Calculations as a minimum for Analog Control Valves, On/Off Valves, Pressure Relief Valves, Rupture Discs, Magnetic Flow Meters, Mass Flow Meters (Coriolis), Vortex Flow Meters, Orifice Plates, Annubars, and Rotameters, as applicable.

The FIE will review calculation sheets with Process as required to facilitate the smooth completion of the [[8-dst-xxxx_instrument_specification_sheets]]. Calculations will be compiled and incorporated into the final [[8-man-xxxx_project_instrument_manuals]] by the FIE. Calculations may also be created by Instrument Vendors and/or Instrument Manufacturers.
