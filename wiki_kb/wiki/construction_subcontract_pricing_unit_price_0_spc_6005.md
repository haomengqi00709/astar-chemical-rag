# Construction Subcontract Pricing: Unit Price (0-SPC-6005)

Specification 0-SPC-6005, "Construction Subcontract Pricing: Unit Price," provides the standard unit definitions and guidelines for implementing unit price contracts within construction subcontracts. This document ensures consistency and clarity in how quantities are measured and priced for specific units of work.

**Purpose:**
The primary purpose of 0-SPC-6005 is to standardize the definitions of units of work and their associated pricing mechanisms for unit price construction subcontracts. This standardization is crucial for:
*   Ensuring fair and transparent bidding processes.
*   Providing a clear basis for calculating progress payments and final contract value.
*   Minimizing disputes related to quantity measurement and unit rates.

**Standard Unit Definitions:**
This specification contains standard unit definitions that are used in conjunction with the [[construction_subcontract_pricing_lists]], specifically for unit price subcontracts. These definitions detail:
*   The precise scope of work covered by each unit.
*   The method of measurement for each unit (e.g., linear meters, square meters, cubic meters, number of items).
*   Any inclusions or exclusions related to the unit rate.

**Application in Pricing Lists:**
When preparing 0-LST-6X11: Construction Subcontract Price List for a unit price subcontract, the quantities for each unit of the works are set, and these units **must** align with the standard definitions provided in 0-SPC-6005. This ensures that the subcontractor's bid and subsequent claims are based on mutually understood and agreed-upon unit definitions.

**Integration with Subcontract Issuance:**
0-SPC-6005 is an administrative specification that supports the commercial framework of construction subcontracts. It is implicitly referenced by the [[construction_subcontract_agreement_template_and_content]] through its direct link to the pricing lists. Its guidelines are essential for the accurate preparation of tender documents as part of the [[construction_subcontract_issuance_procedure_0_prc_6005]] and the [[construction_subcontract_tender_process]].
