# Thickener Pressure and Leak Testing (0-QDS-7423)

This page details the mandatory pressure and leak testing requirements for the Thickener equipment, as specified in Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7423.

**Water Fill Test:**
*   Pressure/Leak Testing (Water fill test) is required as FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package).
*   Pressure/Leak Testing (Water fill test) **shall** require Chemetics Witness.
*   Pressure/Leak Testing (Water fill test) **shall** require Chemetics Hold.
*   This test is required to be included in the Vendor's Inspection and Test Plan.

**Field Testing for Sectional Shipments:**
If the Thickener is shipped in more than one section for weld assembly and rubber lining in the field, then a water fill test **shall** be performed in the field.

These tests are critical to ensure the structural integrity and leak-tightness of the Thickener. For general pressure and leak testing procedures, refer to [[0-QDS-7411_pressure_and_leak_testing]].
