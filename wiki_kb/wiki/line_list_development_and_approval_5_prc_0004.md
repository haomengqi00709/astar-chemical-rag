# Line List Development and Approval (5-PRC-0004)

This document, 5-PRC-0004, details the discipline assignments for the preparation, checking, and approval of the line list for the Project. It outlines the roles and responsibilities for developing and maintaining this critical piping document.

This procedure ensures that the line list, which contains essential information for each pipeline, is developed, reviewed, and approved in a structured manner, adhering to the [[piping_design_criteria_5_prc_0002]] and overall project quality standards.
