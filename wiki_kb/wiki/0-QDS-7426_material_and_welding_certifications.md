# Conveyors & Mills Material and Welding Certifications

This page details the requirements for material and welding certifications for Conveyors & Mills, as specified in Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7426. These certifications are crucial for verifying the integrity and compliance of major components.

**Mill Test Certificates**
*   **Requirement:** Mill Test Certificates for major components are required as FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package).
*   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Chemetics surveillance for Mill Test Certificates (Major components) is designated as Witness. This means a Chemetics representative **shall** be present to witness the verification of these certificates.

**Certificate of Compliance**
*   **Requirement:** A Certificate of Compliance is required as FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package). This certificate attests that the supplied materials and components meet all specified project and industry standards.

For definitions of quality record statuses like FP, refer to [[0-QDS-7426_quality_record_definitions]].

**Other Certifications**
*   **Certificate of Compliance:** Required as FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package).
