# Order Risk Assessment Procedure

A risk assessment **must** be carried out for the selected Vendor. For orders with a value greater than CAD 10,000, this risk assessment **must** be formally documented using the [[order_risk_assessment]] template (0-TAB-5XXX-4).

**Purpose of the Order Risk Assessment:**
The [[order_risk_assessment]] template serves as a checklist guide for assessing various business risks associated with a Vendor. It also provides a mechanism to instruct Procurement on specific contractual conditions required for risk mitigation during Vendor negotiations.

**Addressing Financial Risks:**
If a Vendor receives an unsatisfactory financial evaluation from Finance, the Requisitioning Discipline and Project Manager **must** consult with the Director of Projects and the V.P. Finance. This consultation **must** determine the necessary contractual conditions to mitigate identified financial risks. The determined contractual conditions **must** then be incorporated into the instructions provided to Procurement.

**Publication and Approval:**
The Requisitioning Discipline **must** publish the Order Risk Assessment.
*   For orders with a value less than CAD 200,000, the Project Engineer **must** approve the Order Risk Assessment.
*   For orders with a value greater than CAD 200,000, the Project Manager **must** approve the Order Risk Assessment.

**Negotiation Challenges and Escalation:**
If, during negotiations, the Vendor does not agree to the commercial conditions stipulated in the Order Risk Assessment, the [[final_vendor_selection_meeting|final review meeting]] (as described in Section 7.7 of 0-PRC-5003) **must** be reconvened. This reconvened meeting **must** include the Directors of Technology, Engineering, and Projects. If these Directors cannot agree on acceptable business risks, the matter **must** be referred to the President for a final decision.
