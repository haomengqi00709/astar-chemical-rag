# Unalloyed Nickel Welding Standard Overview (4-SPC-0068)

This specification, 4-SPC-0068, outlines Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ requirements for welding and associated procedures specifically for unalloyed nickel. It is applicable to vessels and equipment constructed from unalloyed nickel materials.

**Material Classification**
The unalloyed nickel covered by this specification **must** be classified under ASME P-No. 41. This classification is critical for ensuring consistency with recognized welding standards and material properties.

**Referenced Codes and Standards**
For general design and fabrication requirements, this specification refers to [[4-SPC-0040_metallic_process_vessels_fabrication_overview]]. This ensures that the overall construction adheres to established industry practices. The welding procedures and qualifications referenced herein are primarily based on the [[ASME_section_ix_welding_and_brazing_qualifications]] code.

This standard is primarily written for compliance with the ASME code. Should a project necessitate adherence to codes from other jurisdictions, such as Australia or Europe, this standard **will** be modified accordingly to meet those specific requirements.
