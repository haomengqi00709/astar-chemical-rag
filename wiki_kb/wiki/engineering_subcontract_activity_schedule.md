# Engineering Subcontract Activity Schedule (0-LST-22XX-1)

The Engineering Subcontract Activity Schedule, identified as 0-LST-22XX-1, is a critical document for defining the scope and deliverables of an Engineering Subcontract. This schedule provides a detailed breakdown of all activities to be performed by the subcontractor, ensuring clarity and alignment with project requirements.

**Preparation and Content:**
The Subcontract Administrator is responsible for preparing a draft version of 0-LST-22XX-1. This draft schedule will be developed based on the relevant [[engineering_scope_checklists_0_lst_22xx]] (0-LST-8XXX). A separate activity **must** be clearly shown on 0-LST-22XX-1 for each item that has been checked as being the responsibility of the Subcontractor on the corresponding 0-LST-8XXX checklist. This ensures a comprehensive and unambiguous allocation of tasks.

**Document Control and Approval (Issued for Quotation):**
The activity schedule **must** be numbered in accordance with [[document_numbering_rules]] (0-PRC-0002). Its identification number **must** be identical to that used for the main Engineering Subcontract Agreement, as defined by [[form_of_engineering_subcontract]] (0-PRC-2202). Once prepared, the activity schedule will be published in accordance with [[publishing_of_project_documents]] (0-PRC-0005) and assigned the status “Issued for Quotation”. At this stage, the schedule **must** be checked by the relevant [[discipline_lead_and_approver_roles]](s) and subsequently approved by the [[project_engineer_pem_gate_review_responsibilities]].

**Revision and Approval (Approved for Design):**
Following the tender process, the Subcontract Administrator will revise 0-LST-22XX-1 based on the successful Engineering Subcontractor's tender submission. The revised activity schedule will then be republished in accordance with [[publishing_of_project_documents]] (0-PRC-0005), with its status updated to “Approved for Design”. This revised schedule **must** again be checked by the [[discipline_lead_and_approver_roles]](s) and approved by the [[project_engineer_pem_gate_review_responsibilities]], signifying its final acceptance for project execution.

Data available in SQL: 0-LST-22XX-1, 0-LST-8XXX

**Preparation and Initial Status**
The Subcontract Administrator is responsible for preparing a draft version of 0-LST-22XX-1. This draft is based on 0-LST-8XXX: Engineering Scope Checklist(s), with each item checked as the Subcontractor's responsibility on 0-LST-8XXX appearing as a separate activity on 0-LST-22XX-1.

The activity schedule is numbered in accordance with 0-PRC-0002: Document Numbering and shares an identical identification number with the Engineering Subcontract Agreement (0-PRC-2202: Form of Engineering Subcontract). It is published with the status “Issued for Quotation” following 0-PRC-0005: Publishing of Project Documents. At this stage, the schedule is checked by the Discipline Lead(s) and approved by the Project Engineer.

**Revision and Approval for Design**
Following the tender process, the Subcontract Administrator revises 0-LST-22XX-1 based on the successful Engineering Subcontractor's tender. The revised activity schedule is then published in accordance with 0-PRC-0005: Publishing of Project Documents, receiving the status “Approved for Design”. This revised version undergoes checking by the Discipline Lead(s) and requires approval by the Project Engineer.
