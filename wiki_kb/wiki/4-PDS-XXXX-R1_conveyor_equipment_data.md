# Conveyor Equipment Data (4-PDS-XXXX-R1)

This page details the general equipment data and operational specifications for conveyor systems, as outlined in datasheet 4-PDS-XXXX-R1. This information is crucial for the mechanical design and integration of the conveyor into the overall plant layout.

**Conveyor Type:**
The type of conveyor **must** be selected from the following options:
*   Screw
*   Belt
*   Bucket
*   Drag
*   Pneumatic

**Operation Mode:**
The operational mode of the conveyor **must** be specified:
*   Continuous
*   Intermittent

**Sealing Requirements:**
The required level of sealing for the conveyor system **must** be selected:
*   Standard
*   Dust/Vapour Tight
*   Pressure Tight

**Feeding and Discharge:**
*   **Method of Feeding:** This field **must** be filled to describe how material is introduced to the conveyor.
*   **Method of Discharge:** This field **must** be filled to describe how material is removed from the conveyor.

**Conveying Path and Environment:**
*   **Conveying Distance:** Measured in meters (m).
*   **Location:**
    *   Indoors
    *   Outdoors
*   **Direction of Travel:**
    *   Horizontal
    *   Vertical
    *   Vertical inclined (If selected, the specific angle **must** be filled in the "Specify angle for Vertical inclined" field).

**Flow Control:**
The method for controlling the flow rate of material on the conveyor **must** be specified:
*   VSD (Variable Speed Drive)
*   Other (This field **must** be filled to describe any other control method).

For details on the materials of construction for conveyor components, refer to [[4-PDS-XXXX-R1_conveyor_material_specifications]]. General equipment requirements are outlined in [[general_equipment_requirements_specification]].
