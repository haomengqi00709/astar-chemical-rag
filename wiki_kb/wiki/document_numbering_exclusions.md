# Exclusions from Document Numbering Procedure

The Document Numbering Procedure (0-PRC-0002) establishes a comprehensive system for identifying project and standard documents. However, certain types of documents are explicitly excluded from this procedure and are governed by their own specific numbering protocols.

The following document types are not covered by [[document_numbering_procedure_0_prc_0002]]:

*   **Correspondence, Minutes of Meetings, Memoranda, and Project Reports:**
    These documents shall be numbered in accordance with [[project_instructions_0_prc_2002]] and/or [[project_coordination_0_prc_2004]]. These procedures provide guidelines for administrative and communication-related documentation.

*   **Purchase Orders:**
    Purchase Orders shall be numbered in accordance with [[form_of_purchase_order_0_prc_5001]]. This ensures that procurement-related documents follow a dedicated and consistent numbering scheme.

*   **Owner-Issued Documents:**
    Documents issued by the Owner shall retain the Owner’s original document number and revision number. The firm's internal numbering system does not supersede external client documentation.

These exclusions ensure that specialized document types are managed under appropriate, dedicated procedures, preventing conflicts and maintaining clarity in document control.
