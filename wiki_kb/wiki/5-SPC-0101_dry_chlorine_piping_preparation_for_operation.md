# Dry Chlorine Piping - Preparation for Operation (5-SPC-0101)

Following the successful completion of pressure testing, cleaning, drying, and leak testing operations, the dry chlorine piping systems **shall** be prepared for operation.

This preparation **will** include:
*   Adjusting the pipe support systems.
*   Completing the insulation of the pipework and components. Refer to [[insulation_design_and_specification_6_prc_0002]] for insulation requirements.
*   Completion of line identification, marking, and painting. Refer to [[protective_coating_specification]] for painting procedures.
*   Installation of appropriate safeguards where shown on drawings or Engineering Standards/Specifications. Refer to [[equipment_safety_and_maintenance_access]] for safety considerations.

The service at vent and drain points location is to be identified by the Project Group.
