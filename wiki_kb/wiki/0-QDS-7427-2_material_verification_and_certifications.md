# Stainless Steel Tubing Tier 2 Material Verification and Certifications

For Stainless Steel Tubing Tier 2, material verification and certifications **shall** adhere to the following requirements:

**Material Verification:**
*   Material Verification **shall** be in accordance with [ASTM A249-02].

**Required Certifications and Documentation:**
The following documents are required as [[0-QDS-7427-2_quality_record_definitions|Quality Records]]:
*   **Mill Test Certificates:** Required as FP. Chemetics **shall** Witness and Check/Verify these certificates.
*   **Corrosion Test Reports:** Required as FP. Chemetics **shall** Witness and Check/Verify these reports.
*   **Material Testing:** Required as FP. Chemetics **shall** Witness and Check/Verify Material Testing.

**Welding Documentation:**
*   **Micrographs of Welds:** Required as AP. Chemetics **shall** Witness Micrographs of Welds.
*   The review of Micrographs of Welds **shall** be performed after Annealing.

These requirements ensure that all materials used for Stainless Steel Tubing Tier 2 meet the specified standards and quality criteria, aligning with the [[0-QDS-7427-2_vendor_quality_assurance_requirements]].
