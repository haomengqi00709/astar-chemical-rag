# Filter Press Quality Data Sheet: Vendor Quality Assurance Requirements

For the Filter Press (0-QDS-7420), the Vendor's Quality Assurance (QA) program **must** comply with the requirements set forth in this Quality Data Sheet.

The Vendor **shall** prepare a Quality Plan in accordance with document [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment]]. Furthermore, the Vendor's overall Quality Assurance program **must** also comply with the stipulations of [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment]].

**Note:** If a Quality Plan is not required for a specific procurement, the corresponding cells related to the Quality Plan in the Quality Data Sheet **should** be deleted.
