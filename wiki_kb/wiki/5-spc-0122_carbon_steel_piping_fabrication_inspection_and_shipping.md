# Carbon Steel Piping Fabrication Inspection and Shipping (5-SPC-0122)

This page details the requirements for final inspection, identification, and shipping precautions for carbon steel piping spools, as specified in document 5-SPC-0122.

**Dimensional Tolerances**
*   Dimensional tolerances for individual spool pieces **shall** maintain the accuracy of the overall line as specified on the isometric drawing.
*   Normal dimensional tolerance for overall line accuracy on isometric drawings is ± 1/4".

**Final Inspection**
*   The Fabricator **shall** inspect finished spools prior to shipping, checking materials, workmanship, finish, flange protection, and loading.

**Spool Identification**
*   Each spool piece **shall** have its identity number painted on it.
*   Identity numbers **shall** be 1" or larger where possible.
*   **Mandatory:** No paper or cardboard tags, or tags attached by cord or string, shall be used for identification.

**Shipping Precautions**
*   **Mandatory:** All necessary precautions, such as plugging openings, protecting flange faces, and using shipping supports, etc., shall be taken by the fabricator prior to shipping.

**Priming and Surface Treatment**
*   Priming, etc., where required, **shall** be carried out in accordance with [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_quality_assurance_and_materials_engineering_group|Aker Kvaerner Chemetics']] painting Standard.
*   Priming, etc., **shall** only be done after all welding and testing has been completed.
