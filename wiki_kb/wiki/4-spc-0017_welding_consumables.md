# Welding Consumables for 6% Moly SS (4-SPC-0017)

This section outlines the mandatory requirements for welding consumables used in the fabrication of 6 percent Moly austenitic stainless steel under Standard **4-SPC-0017**.

**General Requirements**
All weld filler metals or consumables **must** meet the requirements specified in this standard. The Fabricator **must** ensure and demonstrate to the Inspector that the manufacturer's instructions regarding the storage, drying, and use of weld consumables are being fully implemented. All wet electrodes **must** be discarded and not baked and re-used. Prior to the commencement of welding, the Fabricator **must** furnish Aker Kvaerner Chemetics with the brand name of the filler materials to be used. Autogenous welding (i.e., welding with no filler metal) **must not** be used.

**Certification and Identification**
Mill Test Certification **must** be supplied with all materials, including welding consumables. The Supplier **must** make available to the purchaser's inspector copies of chemical certification for each batch of electrodes or wire coils used. If electrodes, wire, etc., cannot be identified to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' satisfaction, samples **will** be removed for chemical analysis at the Supplier’s account.

**Specific Consumable Requirements by Welding Process**

*   **Shielded Metal Arc Welding (SMAW):**
    *   SMAW electrodes **must** satisfy the requirements of [[ASME_sfa_5_4_welding_electrodes]].
    *   SMAW electrodes **must** contain a minimum of 9% molybdenum.
    *   The use of Type 15 electrodes for normal fillet and butt welds is recommended with a DCRP-Electrode Positive power source.

*   **Gas Tungsten Arc Welding (GTAW):**
    *   Where the root of GTAW welded pipe is exposed to process conditions, a minimum of 9% Molybdenum is required for filler.
    *   Gas tungsten arc welding rods **must** satisfy the requirements of [[ASME_sfa_5_14_nickel_and_nickel_alloy_electrodes_and_rods]].
    *   The preferred GTAW electrode is thoriated tungsten as per [[ASME_sfa_5_12_tungsten_arc_welding_electrodes]], Class EWTh-2.

*   **Gas Metal Arc Welding (GMAW):**
    *   GMAW Welding Electrodes **must** satisfy the requirements of [[ASME_sfa_5_9_welding_electrodes_and_rods]] or SFA-5.22, unless otherwise approved by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

*   **Submerged Arc Welding:**
    *   A chemical analysis of the finished weld deposit **must** be included as part of the Submerged Arc Welding procedure qualification.
    *   Production chemical analysis for Submerged Arc Welding **must** be performed on a random basis (twice per vessel).
