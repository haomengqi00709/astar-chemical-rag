# Quality Data Sheet (QDS) Management for Third Party Inspections

The Inspection Agency plays a critical role in managing Quality Data Sheets (QDS) to ensure compliance and proper documentation throughout the inspection process.

**Agency Responsibilities**
The Inspection Agency **shall** be responsible for maintaining and completing all Quality Data Sheets. Examples of "As Issued" and "Final" revisions of a QDS are available for reference.

**Activity Identification**
All activities listed on the QDS **shall** be identified with one of the following designations, as appropriate:
*   **Witness:** Requires the inspector to be present during the activity.
*   **Hold:** Requires the inspector's explicit approval before proceeding.
*   **Review:** Requires the inspector to examine documentation or procedures.
*   **Verify:** Requires the inspector to confirm compliance with specifications.
*   **Monitor:** Allows for periodic observation rather than continuous presence.

**Witness Point Exemptions and Waivers**
*   Activities designated as Witness points may, by agreement between the Vendor and the Agency, be exempted from the normal five working days advance notification requirement.
*   Where multiple instances of the same activity are being carried out at the same vendor, Witness requirements may be waived by the Agency in favour of Monitoring, subject to agreement.

**Hold Point Restrictions**
Hold points **shall not** be waived without prior written approval from Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. Strict adherence to this requirement is mandatory to maintain quality control.

**Completion and Retention**
*   When an activity has been completed, the Agency inspector **shall** initial and date the appropriate columns in the QDS.
*   Upon completion of the inspection for the item(s) listed in a QDS, the completed QDS **shall** be retained for submittal. It **must** be submitted along with the associated [[inspection_release_process_third_party]] and any relevant [[non_conformance_report_process_third_party]].

For further details on QDS definitions, refer to [[quality_data_sheets_qds]] and [[qds_items_and_activities_definitions]].
