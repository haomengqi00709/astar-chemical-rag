# Post-Test Activities

Upon completion of pressure testing, several critical activities must be performed to ensure the system is ready for operation and all temporary installations are removed.

**Pump Connections**
*   For pump suctions, the line between the pump and the first valve will be dismantled, all debris will be removed, and the temporary startup strainer will be installed.
*   For pump discharges, the line between the pump and the first valve will be dismantled and all debris will be removed.

**System Reinstatement**
*   All blinds used to isolate the system must be removed and accounted for.
*   After pressure testing, all equipment removed or disconnected for the purpose of the test will be replaced or reconnected by the Contractor.
*   All joints will be made good to the satisfaction of the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site representative.
*   Pipes which have been tested will be completely drained.
*   Vents and drains which have been added for the purpose of testing will be sealed by means approved by the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site representative.
