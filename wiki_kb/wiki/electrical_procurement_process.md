# Electrical Procurement Process (REQ & RFQ)

This page outlines the initial stages of the electrical procurement process, focusing on Requisition for Quotation (REQ) and Request for Quotation (RFQ) procedures, including associated documentation.

**Requisition for Quotation (REQ)**
The requisition request for quotation (REQ) **will** be prepared in accordance with Table 2. Procedure 0-PRC-5002, "Requisition for Quotation or Purchase Order", **will** be followed in the preparation of the REQ. The template "Requisition Request for Quotation" **must** be used to create the requisition.

The requisition **will** list the technical documents required for the equipment being requisitioned. The list "Material Cost Codes", 0-LST-4002, **must** be checked to obtain the correct cost code for the request for requisition for quotation.

Documents to be listed on the requisition include:
*   Site design conditions data sheet.
*   [[electrical_equipment_specifications]].
*   [[electrical_equipment_data_sheets]].
*   [[procurement_and_vendor_documentation]] (Vendor data requirements sheet).
*   Quality data sheet for the equipment.
*   Protective coating for chemical plants specification.

**Vendor Document Requirements (VDR)**
Procedure 0-PRC-0007, "Review of Vendor Documentation", **will** be followed when preparing the Vendor Document Requirements (VDR) form. The VDR form **will** be included in the requisition for quotation and purchase packages. The template "Vendor Document Requirements", 0-VDR-0XXX, **must** be used to generate the VDR form. The specification "Vendor Document Supply", 0-SPC-0001, **must** be included in the requisition for quotation and purchase packages.

**Quality Data Sheets (QDS)**
Quality data sheets **will** be prepared in accordance with Table 2. Procedure 0-PRC-7005, "Vendor Quality Assurance", **must** be followed when preparing the quality data sheets. The template "Quality Data Sheet", X-QDS-7XXX, **must** be used to generate the QDS sheet. Specification 0-SPC-7001, "Vendor Quality Assurance (Equipment)", **must** be referred to when creating quality data sheets for equipment. Specification 0-SPC-7002, "Vendor Quality Assurance (Bulk Materials)", **must** be referred to when creating quality data sheets for bulk materials.

**Request for Quotation (RFQ)**
The request for quotation (RFQ) **will** be prepared in accordance with Table 2 and procedure 0-PRC-5005, "Issue of Request for Quotation or Purchase Order".

**Specifics for Transformer/Rectifier (T/R) & CPU**
The requisition request for quotation for the T/R and CPU **will** be prepared and issued by the engineering firm. DDE **will** receive copies of the requisition and attached documents for follow-up communication with bidders during bid evaluation. Requisition request for quotation documents **will** be prepared by the DDE and forwarded to the purchasing department for official issue.

**Exclusions**
Section 3.13 (Requisition – Quotation) specifically excludes T/R & CPU.
