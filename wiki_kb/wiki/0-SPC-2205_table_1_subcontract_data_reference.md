# Subcontract Data Reference (0-SPC-2205_table_1)

This page describes the content of `0-SPC-2205_table_1`, which is referenced within document 0-SPC-2205.

`0-SPC-2205_table_1` contains the **Subcontract Data**. This data is crucial for fully ascertaining the obligations of both Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Subcontractor. It includes specific information that defines various aspects of the subcontract, such as:
*   The documents in which the [[0-SPC-2205_general_conditions_and_administration#Key Definitions|Scope]] is detailed.
*   The recipient's address for communications if no specific address is notified.
*   The form in which retained copies of documents **must** be kept.
*   The specific key personnel to be employed by the Subcontractor.
*   The rate for delay damages for late documents.
*   The rate for delay damages for late [[0-SPC-2205_general_conditions_and_administration#Key Definitions|Completion]].
*   The basis for calculating the amount due for monthly invoices.
*   Any different payment period for invoices (if not 45 days).
*   The specific insurances Aker Kvaerner Chemetics is to provide (if any).
*   The periods for which insurances **must** provide cover.
*   The amount limiting the Subcontractor’s liability to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

Data available in SQL: 0-SPC-2205_table_1

The Subcontract Data is located in the Agreement, in the documents referenced in the Agreement, and in the Special Conditions for Engineering Subcontract.
