# Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7407: Large Fan Non-Destructive Examination Requirements

This page specifies the Non-Destructive Examination (NDE) requirements for the Large Fan, as outlined in Quality Data Sheet (QDS) 0-QDS-7407. NDE is a critical aspect of quality assurance, ensuring the structural integrity and reliability of components without causing damage. These requirements are part of the broader [[0-QDS-7407_large_fan_vendor_quality_assurance_requirements]].

**Mandatory NDE Standards:**
All Non-Destructive Examination (NDE) procedures and applications **shall** be conducted in accordance with ANSI/AWS Structural Welding Code D1.1 or an approved equivalent standard. Adherence to this code ensures that NDE methods, personnel qualifications, and acceptance criteria meet recognized industry benchmarks for structural welding. This is a mandatory requirement to guarantee the safety, durability, and performance of welded components in the Large Fan assembly, minimizing the risk of structural failures.

**Specific Non-Destructive Testing Methods:**
The Non-Destructive Testing (NDT) scope for the Large Fan includes, but is not limited to, the following method:
*   **Dye Penetrant Testing (PT):**
    *   *Description:* Dye Penetrant Testing is a widely used NDT method for detecting surface-breaking discontinuities in all non-porous materials. It involves applying a liquid penetrant to the surface, allowing it to seep into any flaws, and then drawing it out with a developer to make the discontinuities visible. This method is crucial for identifying cracks, laps, seams, porosity, and other surface imperfections that could compromise the integrity of the fan components, particularly in critical weld areas and machined surfaces.

These NDE requirements are integral to the overall [[0-QDS-7407_large_fan_inspection_and_documentation_requirements]] and contribute significantly to the quality and safety of the Large Fan by ensuring hidden defects are identified and addressed.
