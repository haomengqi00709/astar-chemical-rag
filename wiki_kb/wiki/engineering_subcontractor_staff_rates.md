# Engineering Subcontractor Staff Rates (0-LST-22XX-2)

The Engineering Subcontractor Staff Rates document, designated as 0-LST-22XX-2, is an essential component of any Engineering Subcontract, providing a clear and agreed-upon basis for personnel costs. This document details the rates at which the subcontractor's staff will be billed for their services.

**Preparation and Document Control (Issued for Quotation):**
The Subcontract Administrator is responsible for preparing a blank version of 0-LST-22XX-2. This rate list **must** be numbered in accordance with [[document_numbering_rules]] (0-PRC-0002) and **must** bear an identification number identical to that used for the main Engineering Subcontract Agreement, as specified in [[form_of_engineering_subcontract]] (0-PRC-2202). The blank rate list will be published in accordance with [[publishing_of_project_documents]] (0-PRC-0005) and assigned the status “Issued for Quotation”. Notably, at this initial "Issued for Quotation" stage, no formal checking or approval is required for the Staff Rate List, as it is intended to be populated by the tendering subcontractors.

**Revision and Approval (Approved for Design):**
Upon selection of a successful Engineering Subcontractor, the Subcontract Administrator will revise 0-LST-22XX-2 to incorporate the agreed-upon staff rates from the successful tender. The revised rate list will then be republished in accordance with [[publishing_of_project_documents]] (0-PRC-0005), with its status updated to “Approved for Design”. At this critical stage, the revised rate list **must** be checked by the relevant [[discipline_lead_and_approver_roles]](s) and subsequently approved by the [[project_engineer_pem_gate_review_responsibilities]], confirming the final commercial terms for staffing.

This document ensures transparency and agreement on labor costs, which is vital for effective cost control and financial management throughout the subcontract period.

Data available in SQL: 0-LST-22XX-2

**Preparation and Initial Status**
The Subcontract Administrator prepares a blank version of 0-LST-22XX-2. This rate list is numbered in accordance with 0-PRC-0002: Document Numbering and shares an identical identification number with the Engineering Subcontract Agreement (0-PRC-2202: Form of Engineering Subcontract). It is published with the status “Issued for Quotation” following 0-PRC-0005: Publishing of Project Documents. Notably, no checking or approval is required for the Staff Rate List at this "Issued for Quotation" stage.

**Revision and Approval for Design**
After the tender process, the Subcontract Administrator revises 0-LST-22XX-2 based on the successful Engineering Subcontractor's tender. The revised rate list is published in accordance with 0-PRC-0005: Publishing of Project Documents, with the status “Approved for Design”. This revised version is checked by the Discipline Lead(s) and approved by the Project Engineer.
