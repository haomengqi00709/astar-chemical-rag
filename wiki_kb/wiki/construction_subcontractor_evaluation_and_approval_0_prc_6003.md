# Construction Subcontractor Evaluation and Approval (0-PRC-6003)

Procedure 0-PRC-6003 outlines the process for evaluating and approving Construction Subcontractors. This procedure is critical for ensuring that selected subcontractors possess the necessary qualifications, experience, and financial stability to successfully execute their scope of work, thereby mitigating project risks.

**Purpose:**
The primary purpose of 0-PRC-6003 is to establish a systematic and objective method for:
*   Assessing the capabilities of prospective construction subcontractors.
*   Comparing bids and proposals against defined criteria.
*   Making informed decisions regarding subcontractor selection.
*   Documenting the evaluation and approval process for audit and accountability.

**Evaluation Process:**
The evaluation process typically involves:
*   **Technical Evaluation:** Assessing the subcontractor's proposed methodology, technical expertise, resources, and understanding of the project scope as defined in [[construction_subcontract_scope_preparation_0_prc_6091]].
*   **Commercial Evaluation:** Reviewing the subcontractor's pricing, payment terms, and overall commercial proposal against the requirements set out in [[commercial_terms_for_construction_subcontract_0_prc_6004]] and the [[construction_subcontract_pricing_lists]].
*   **Financial Assessment:** Evaluating the subcontractor's financial health and capacity to undertake the project.
*   **Safety and Quality Records:** Reviewing past performance in safety, quality, and environmental management, often informed by the requirements in [[construction_subcontract_administrative_specifications]] (e.g., 0-SPC-6051 for Safety Management, 0-SPC-6071 for Quality Assurance).

**Subcontractor Evaluation Summary Record:**
A key output of this procedure is the Construction Subcontractor Evaluation Summary Record. This document consolidates the findings of the evaluation, highlights the rationale for selection, and formally records the approval of the chosen subcontractor.

**Role in Subcontract Issuance:**
The approved Construction Subcontractor Evaluation Summary Record is a mandatory document required at the final stage of the subcontract issuance process.
*   When a paper copy of the [[construction_subcontract_agreement_template_and_content]] is presented for signing by authorized personnel (as per [[construction_subcontract_signing_authority]]), it **must** be accompanied by a copy of the approved Construction Subcontractor Evaluation Summary Record. This ensures that the signing authority has confirmation of the subcontractor's suitability.
*   The evaluation process is an integral part of the broader [[construction_subcontract_tender_process]], leading to the selection of the successful bidder.

**Related Procedures:**
This procedure works in conjunction with other project management and procurement procedures, including:
*   [[project_execution_plan_overview]] (0-PRC-2003) for initial subcontractor identification.
*   [[instructions_to_construction_subcontractors_for_tender_0_spc_6002]] which guides subcontractors in preparing their bids for evaluation.
