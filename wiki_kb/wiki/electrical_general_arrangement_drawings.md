# General Electrical Arrangement Drawings

General Electrical Arrangement (GA) drawings provide a comprehensive overview of the plant layout, showing the locations and identities of all electrical and relevant non-electrical equipment. These drawings are fundamental for planning and coordinating installations.

**Common Requirements for GA Drawings**
General arrangement drawings **shall** have common requirements, including:
*   The project North arrow.
*   Other parts of an existing plant, if applicable.
*   Symbols for equipment.
*   Legends for equipment.

Symbols used on general arrangement drawings **shall** be either the Owner’s standard symbols or industry standard symbols. GA drawings **shall** copy plant backgrounds from general arrangement drawings produced by the design office.

**Purpose and Content**
General arrangement drawings **shall** show:
*   Locations and identities of all electrical equipment within the plant site.
*   Locations of other non-electrical equipment and any underground services that may pose a problem for electrical installation.
*   Sufficient information for equipment installation.

The equipment layout review will be conducted in accordance with procedure Plant Constructability Review, 0-PRC-6001.

**Document Numbering and Usage**
General arrangement drawings **shall** be numbered according to 9-LST-0001, List Electrical Document Numbering Standard.
