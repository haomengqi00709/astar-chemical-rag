# Material Verification and Certifications for Polypropylene Tank (0-QDS-7424)

This page outlines the mandatory requirements for material verification and certifications for the Polypropylene Tank project, as specified in Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7424. These requirements are essential to confirm that all materials used meet the specified design and safety standards.

**Mandatory Material Documentation:**
*   **Material Test Certificates:** These certificates **must** be provided as a Quality Record (FP). This means they **shall** be included in the final [[0-QDS-7424_quality_record_definitions|Quality Assurance Documentation Package]]. Additionally, Chemetics Witness surveillance is required for the verification of these certificates, ensuring independent oversight of material compliance.
*   **Certificate of Compliance:** A Certificate of Compliance **must** be provided as a Quality Record (FP). This certificate **shall** also be included in the final [[0-QDS-7424_quality_record_definitions|Quality Assurance Documentation Package]], confirming that the supplied materials or components meet all applicable specifications and standards.

Material Verification is a critical activity listed in the Quality Data Sheet to ensure the correct and specified materials are used in the construction of the polypropylene tank. The Chemetics Witness surveillance level for this project is detailed in [[0-QDS-7424_chemetics_surveillance_level]]. For general administrative details of this QDS, refer to [[0-QDS-7424_polypropylene_tank_quality_data_sheet_administration]].

**Specific Certification Requirements:**
*   **Material Test Certificates:** Require a Quality Record (FP - Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package) and Chemetics Witness surveillance.
*   **Certificate of Compliance:** Requires a Quality Record (FP - Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package).
