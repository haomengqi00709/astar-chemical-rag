# Equipment Documentation Procedure (4-PRC-0003)

The 4-PRC-0003 procedure outlines the methodology for project personnel to prepare comprehensive equipment documentation. This documentation serves as a critical record of equipment sizing and design, and provides a complete description of the technical requirements to be issued to vendors. A key aspect of this procedure is the assignment of clear responsibilities for document preparation and approval.

All project documents **must** be published and signed in accordance with [[0_administration_document_structure]].

**Procurement Documentation**
Documents issued for the procurement of equipment **must** be attached to Section 1: Scope of Supply of the Purchase Order. For details on the structure and content of purchase orders, refer to [[0-PRC-5001_purchase_order_overview]].

**Vendor Documentation Review**
Documents received from vendors **must** be reviewed to ensure they meet the intent of the technical requirements issued by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. The process for reviewing vendor documentation is detailed in [[0-PRC-0007_review_of_vendor_documentation]]. Specific requirements for vendor documents are outlined in [[vendor_document_requirements]].

**Revision History**
The procedure has undergone several revisions and updates:

*   **Revision 1:**
    *   The definition of "checking" was updated to include interdiscipline coordination.
    *   Coordination was established with [[1-prc-0002_flows_heet_development_and_approval]] and [[5-prc-0003_piping_design_and_specification]].
    *   New document types were formally added, including Heat Transfer Calculations, Package Data Sheets (PKG), Quality Data Sheets (QDS), and Vendor Document Requirements (VDR).

*   **Name Change (Undated):**
    *   An Aker Kvaerner Chemetics name change was implemented. No new revision number was used, and changes were not marked. Refer to [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_name_change_history]] for more details.

*   **Revision 2:**
    *   The [[4-lst-0001_equipment_list_details]] and [[4-gad-xxxx_equipment_layout_drawing]] were formally added to the procedure.

*   **2005 Name Change:**
    *   A 2005 name change was implemented. No new revision number was used, and changes were not marked. Refer to [[2005_name_change_conversion_details]] for more information.

*   **Revision 3:**
    *   Applicable parts of the commentary were moved to corresponding sections within the main body of the procedure.
    *   SRS (Sulfuric Acid Regeneration System) units were added to the definition of Type E Equipment.
    *   Section 3.5, pertaining to [[process_calculations]], was expanded.
    *   Standard SCR (Sulfuric Acid Regeneration) drawings 0006, 0007, 0008, and 7540 were added.

This procedure also assigns responsibilities for document preparation and approval.
