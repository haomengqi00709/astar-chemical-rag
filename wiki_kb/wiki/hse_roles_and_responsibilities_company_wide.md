# Company-Wide HSE Roles and Responsibilities

Within Aker Kvaerner Chemetics ("the Company"), the President holds overall management responsibility for Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) across all operations. Specific segments of operations have delegated responsibilities as outlined in this procedure.

**General Responsibilities:**
*   All line managers and supervisors are expected to provide HSE leadership and implement the Group Policy and this procedure within their areas of administrative control.
*   All employees are required to take personal responsibility for HSE by focusing on their own behavior.
*   All employees **will** comply with this procedure and all other Company HSE procedures and policies.

**HSE Management Committee:**
An HSE Management Committee **will** be formed with the following members:
*   President of Company (Chair)
*   HSE Manager (Alternate Chair)
*   Administration Services Coordinator (Responsible for Vancouver Office)
*   President of Equipment Division (Responsible for Toronto Workshop)
*   Laboratory Supervisor (Responsible for Burnaby Laboratory)
*   Manager of Construction & Estimating (Responsible for Construction Sites)
*   Manager of Process & Mechanical (Responsible for Commissioning Sites)

The Committee **will** meet monthly to review HSE performance. Attendance for personnel outside the Vancouver Office may be via teleconference. A record of each meeting, in the form of an Action List, **will** be kept. This Action List **will** include updated statuses of previous actions and new actions with assigned responsibilities, and **will** be posted on the Company data network for employee access.

**HSE Manager Duties:**
The Company HSE Manager reports directly to the President and is responsible for:
*   Championing HSE initiatives and promoting ongoing safety awareness.
*   Coaching and mentoring senior management on HSE leadership.
*   Developing HSE policy and procedures, and strategies for their implementation.
*   Maintaining the HSE System documentation.
*   Reviewing supporting procedures and practices to ensure conformity.
*   Conducting health and safety audits.
*   Ensuring follow-up on HSE cases.
*   Gathering HSE performance data.
*   Assisting and/or performing accident investigations, including the initiation and implementation of incident investigations.
*   Evaluating and auditing the HSE Management System and Project HSE Management Plans.

For an overview of the Company's HSE management system, refer to [[0-prc-8000_administration_of_hse]]. Specific HSE responsibilities for different locations are detailed in [[hse_vancouver_office]], [[hse_toronto_workshop]], [[hse_burnaby_laboratory]], [[hse_construction_sites]], and [[hse_commissioning_sites]].

**Employee Compliance:**
*   All employees of the Company will comply with this procedure and all other Company HSE procedures and policies.
