# Personal Vehicle Use Reimbursement (0-PRC-9003)

The use of personal vehicles for business purposes will be reimbursed at a rate of CAD 0.45 per kilometre.

This rate is inclusive of all associated costs, which include, but are not limited to, fuel, maintenance, suitable insurance, and depreciation of the vehicle.
