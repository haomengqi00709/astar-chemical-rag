# Pecora PTFE Membrane Installation

The acid resistant membrane employed on all strong acid equipment **shall** consist of a nominal 3mm layer of Pecora Corporation, Pecora A-103 chemical resistant mastic. This mastic layer **will** be overlaid with sodium etched virgin PTFE sheet, forming a robust barrier against corrosive media.

**Membrane Application Details:**
The Pecora/PTFE membrane **will** be applied as two layers of 0.125mm thick PTFE sheet. These two layers **will** be overlapped by 50% to achieve an effective total thickness of 0.25mm. This layering and overlapping strategy enhances the membrane's integrity and resistance to permeation.

Specific requirements for overlap joints and mastic application include:
*   Overlap joints in the lower layer of 0.125mm PTFE **will** have a minimum width of 150mm.
*   Overlap joints in the lower layer of 0.125mm PTFE **must** be firmly adhered with mastic to prevent disbonding.
*   Pecora A-103 mastic **will** be applied to the entire sandblasted surface of the dished bottoms, ensuring complete coverage and adhesion in these critical areas.
*   For the upper PTFE sheet, it is acceptable to adhere only its edges with mastic, allowing for some flexibility while maintaining a seal.
*   The number of PTFE sheet seams **must** be minimized to reduce potential weak points in the membrane.
*   PTFE sheet seams **must** be staggered between layers to enhance overall integrity and prevent direct pathways for chemical ingress.
*   The PTFE sheet overlap joints **will** be a minimum of 150mm wide, providing ample sealing area.
*   The sheets **will** be arranged to provide a hinging effect, which is crucial for accommodating thermal expansion and contraction of the vessel without compromising the membrane.
*   The number of overlap joints **must** be kept to the absolute minimum to reduce potential failure points.
*   Lapped edges of the PTFE sheets **must** be firmly stuck down with A-103 mastic, ensuring a continuous and secure bond.
*   PTFE sheet seams **will** be staggered, further reinforcing the membrane system.

The membrane system, consisting of Pecora mastic and PTFE sheets, is critical for corrosion protection in strong acid environments. Proper application and strict adherence to these specifications are mandatory to ensure the longevity and performance of the lining system.

**Membrane Specifications:**
The mastic layer **will** be overlaid with 0.125mm (2 layers) or 0.25mm (1 layer) sodium etched virgin PTFE sheet.

### Membrane Application Details
*   The Pecora/PTFE membrane **will** be applied as two layers of 0.125mm thick PTFE sheet.
*   The two layers of 0.125mm thick PTFE sheet **will** be overlapped 50% to give an effective 0.25mm thickness.
*   Overlap joints in the lower layer of 0.125mm PTFE **will** be a minimum of 150mm wide and **must** be firmly stuck down with mastic.
*   Pecora mastic **will** be applied to the entire sandblasted surface of the dished bottoms.
*   It is acceptable to stick down only the edges of the upper PTFE sheet.
*   The number of PTFE sheet seams **will** be minimized.
*   PTFE sheet seams **must** be staggered.
*   The PTFE sheet overlap joints **will** be a minimum of 150mm wide.
*   The sheets **will** be arranged to provide a hinging effect.
*   The number of overlap joints **must** be kept to the absolute minimum.
*   Lapped edges of the PTFE sheets **must** be firmly stuck down with A-103 mastic.
