# Vendor Quality Assurance Requirements for MV Switchgear & DC Control Power Supply

The Vendor's Quality Assurance (QA) program for the Medium Voltage Switchgear & DC Control Power Supply project **must** comply with the requirements set forth in this Quality Data Sheet (0-QDS-7901-R0) and with [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment]].

**Quality Plan:**
The Vendor **shall** prepare a Quality Plan in accordance with [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment]]. If a Quality Plan is not required for this specific scope, the corresponding sections in the Quality Data Sheet **shall** be deleted.

Compliance with these requirements ensures that all aspects of manufacturing, testing, and documentation meet Chemetics' stringent quality standards.
