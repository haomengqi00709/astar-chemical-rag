# Bill of Quantity (Piping)

A Bill of Quantity (BOQ) for piping is a comprehensive list of all materials and components required for the fabrication and installation of a piping system. This document is crucial for procurement, cost estimation, and project planning.

**Content Requirements**
The Bill of Quantity **shall** contain the following detailed information:
*   **Pipe Component Description:** A clear and concise description of each piping component (e.g., pipe, elbow, flange, valve).
*   **Reference to the Applicable Material Specification and/or Detail Drawing:** A cross-reference to the relevant [[5-SPC-0001_pipe_specification]] or [[pipe_support_detail_drawings]] that provides detailed technical requirements for the component.
*   **Quantity Required Including Spares:** The total number of each component needed for the project, explicitly including any specified spare parts.

**Roles and Responsibilities**
The preparation, checking, and approval of the Bill of Quantity are assigned to specific project personnel:
*   **Preparation:** The document **shall** be prepared by the Design discipline.
*   **Checking:** The document **shall** be checked by the Mechanical discipline.
*   **Approval:** The document **shall** be approved by the Project Engineer.

This structured approach ensures that all necessary piping materials are accurately identified and quantified, facilitating efficient procurement and cost control throughout the project.
