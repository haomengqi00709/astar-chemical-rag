# Chiller/Refrigeration Unit Cooling Water Utility Specifications

The cooling water utility specifications for Chiller/Refrigeration Units, as detailed in datasheet [[4-PDS-XXXX-R1_template_chiller_refrigeration_unit_datasheet_administration]], define the requirements for the cooling water supply. These parameters are critical for the design of the unit's condenser and overall thermal management.

Key cooling water parameters and their units are:
*   **Allowable Pressure Drop:** kPa
*   **Temperature (In):** Specified for Minimum and Maximum conditions.
*   **Temperature (Out):** oC (Value: VTA - Vendor to Advise).
*   **Fouling, % xs area:** Note 1.
*   **Fouling resistance:** m2.C/W
*   **Flowrate:** m3/h (Value: VTA - Vendor to Advise).

**Mandatory Requirement:**
*   **Note 1.0:** The vendor **must** recommend excess area based on the fouling nature of fluids and standard industry practice. This ensures the cooling system maintains efficiency despite potential fouling.

For general information on heat transfer properties, refer to [[heat_transfer_coefficients]].

*   **Fouling, % xs area:** This parameter is specified, with a reference to **Note 1** for vendor recommendations.
