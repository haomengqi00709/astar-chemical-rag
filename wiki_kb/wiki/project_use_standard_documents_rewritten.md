# Project Use of Standard Documents (Rewritten)

In instances where a standard document requires complete re-writing for a project to meet specific contractual requirements, the project team **shall** author a new document. This approach is taken when modifications are so extensive that a new document is more appropriate than adapting an existing standard.

### Procedure for Complete Re-writing
1.  **Authoring New Document:** The project team **shall** author the new document from scratch.
2.  **Document Numbering:** The new document **must** be numbered with the project number as a prefix, retaining the same identification number as the replaced standard. This ensures continuity in document identification while clearly marking it as a project-specific rewrite.
3.  **Project Revision Marking:** The document **must** be marked as **Rev A or Rev 1** for the project.
4.  **Status Indication:** The appropriate status **must** be shown on the document.
5.  **Publishing:** The document **must** be published in accordance with [[0-PRC-0005_publishing_of_project_documents]].
6.  **Approval:**
    *   Procedures and templates **must** be checked and approved by the personnel assigned in Section 3 of the original 0-PRC-0001 procedure (referring to the [[standard_document_roles_and_responsibilities|roles and responsibilities]] for standard documents).
    *   Other documents in work areas 1 through 9 **must** be checked and approved by the applicable Chief Engineer.

This process ensures that completely rewritten documents are properly integrated, numbered, and approved within the project's documentation framework, providing a clear and compliant record of project-specific standards.
