# Template Vertical Pump Process Design Conditions

This page outlines the key process design conditions specified in the Template Vertical Pump Datasheet (4-DST-XXXX-R1). These parameters are critical for the proper selection and operation of the vertical pump, aligning with [[process_design_criteria_1_prc_0001]] and utilizing [[engineering_units]].

Process conditions can be specified for "DESIGN PER UNIT" and "ALTERNATE PER UNIT" to cover various operational scenarios.

**Key Process Parameters:**

*   **Fluid Description:** Detailed description of the fluid being pumped.
*   **Composition:** Measured in %w/w. Refer to [[stream_data_calculation_and_integration]] for related data.
*   **Solids Content:** Measured in %w/w.
*   **Solids Type:** Description of the type of solids present in the fluid.
*   **Flow Rate:** Measured in m3/h. This represents the normal operating flow.
*   **Minimum Flow Rate:** Measured in m3/h. Specifies the lowest acceptable flow rate for continuous operation.
*   **TDH (Total Dynamic Head):** Measured in m (fluid). This is the total head the pump must generate.
*   **NPSH Available (Net Positive Suction Head Available):** Measured in m (fluid). This indicates the absolute pressure at the suction side of the pump, minus the vapor pressure of the liquid, converted to meters of liquid.
*   **Temperature:** Measured in oC.
*   **Density:** Measured in kg/m3.
*   **Viscosity:** Measured in cP.
*   **NPSH Required (Net Positive Suction Head Required):** Measured in m (fluid). This is the minimum pressure required at the suction side of the pump to avoid cavitation.
*   **Min Submergence:** Measured in mm. This specifies the minimum liquid level above the pump suction to ensure proper operation and prevent vortexing.

**Process Parameters:**
The datasheet specifies the following process conditions:
