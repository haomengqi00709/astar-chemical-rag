# 0-POL-9114 Document Revision History

This page details the significant revisions and changes made to document 0-POL-9114, the Vacation Policy. Understanding the revision history is crucial for comprehending the evolution of the policy and its current provisions. For general document revision processes, refer to [[document_approval_and_revision_process]].

**Revision 1**
Revision 1 introduced several key changes to the vacation policy:
*   **Carryover Policy Adjustment:** The option for employees to carry over their vacation balance after two full years of accrual was deleted. This change aimed to streamline the carryover policy and encourage more regular use of vacation time.
*   **Entitlement Criteria Enhancement:** Hay Point levels were added to the Entitlement section. This provided an additional factor in determining vacation accrual, alongside an employee's length of service, ensuring a more comprehensive approach to vacation allocation.
*   **Additional Holiday Reference:** A specific reference to the additional days off granted during the Christmas period was added, formalizing this benefit within the policy. This ensures clarity regarding these extra paid days.
*   **Clarity and Simplification:** The language throughout the document was cleaned up, and the sections pertaining to vacation approval and termination procedures were simplified for improved clarity and ease of understanding for all employees and supervisors.

**Revision 2**
Revision 2 further refined the vacation policy with the following updates:
*   **Mandatory Payout Removal:** The requirement for a mandatory pay-out of vacation accrual after two years was removed. This change offered more flexibility in how employees could manage their accumulated vacation balances.
*   **Excess Accrual Management:** A new requirement was introduced, mandating employees to create a vacation plan to utilize any excess accrual. This encourages proactive vacation planning and helps prevent the accumulation of large unused vacation balances.
*   **Line Manager Responsibilities:** The responsibilities of line managers regarding vacation policy implementation and management were clarified. This aimed to ensure better oversight, consistency, and support in the application of the vacation policy across departments.

**2005 Name Change Conversion**
A significant name change conversion occurred in 2005, affecting the company's official designation within the document. It is important to note the following regarding this conversion:
*   **No New Revision Number:** No new revision number was assigned or used to mark these changes. This means the document's revision counter did not increment for this specific update.
*   **Unmarked Changes:** Consequently, the specific changes made during this 2005 name change conversion were not explicitly marked within the document text. Users should be aware that the company name may have been updated without standard revision indicators.
