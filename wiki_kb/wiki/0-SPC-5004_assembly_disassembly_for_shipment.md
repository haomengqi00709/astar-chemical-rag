# Assembly and Disassembly for Shipment (0-SPC-5004)

Goods **will** generally be packaged and shipped fully assembled, ready for their intended use.

However, the Vendor **may** disassemble goods as necessary under the following conditions:
*   To prevent damage during shipment.
*   To comply with allowable shipping dimensions.
*   To comply with allowable shipping weights.

Any disassembly **must** be performed in a manner that facilitates reassembly at the destination and does not compromise the integrity or functionality of the goods.
