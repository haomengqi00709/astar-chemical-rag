# Logistics Information for Procurement

For effective procurement and bid evaluation, especially for large equipment, comprehensive logistics information is essential. This information **should** be established for potential bidders listed in the [[project_procurement_planning_0_lst_5001|Procurement Plan]].

For each shipping route, the following details **should** be noted:
*   **Vendor packaging requirements:** Specific packaging standards or needs from the vendor.
*   **Exporting country shipping method:** The method of transport used from the country of origin.
*   **Port of export:** The specific port from which goods will depart the exporting country.
*   **Consolidation / repackaging requirements:** Any needs for combining shipments or altering packaging during transit.
*   **Port of import:** The specific port where goods will enter the importing country.
*   **Carrier type:** The mode of transport (e.g., sea freight, air freight, road transport).
*   **Importing country shipping method:** The method of transport used within the importing country to reach the final destination.

This detailed logistics information is crucial for accurate cost estimation, scheduling, and risk assessment during the procurement process.
