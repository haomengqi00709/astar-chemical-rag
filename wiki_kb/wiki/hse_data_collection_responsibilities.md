# HSE Data Collection Responsibilities and Process

The accurate and timely collection of HSE performance statistics is a critical component of the [[0-prc-8002_hse_performance_statistics|HSE Performance Statistics Procedure (0-PRC-8002)]]. This process involves specific roles and responsibilities to ensure data integrity and effective reporting.

**Responsibilities for Data Collection**
*   **Work Site Personnel**: The collection and initial reporting of [[hse_performance_indicators|key measures]] are performed by a designated person at each work site. This may include personnel at [[hse_construction_sites]], [[hse_commissioning_sites]], [[hse_burnaby_laboratory]], [[hse_toronto_workshop]], or [[hse_vancouver_office]].
*   **Site HSE Manager/Designate**: The primary responsibility for collecting HSE performance statistics rests with the HSE Manager at the Works site, or their designated representative. At sites without a full-time HSE Manager, the individual responsible for HSE matters will undertake this task.
    *   HSE Managers **must** implement robust [[hse_management_construction_site_advisors|management systems]] to ensure the accurate collection and tallying of all HSE performance information.
*   **Company HSE Manager/Designate**: AK Chemetics HSE managers are responsible for entering [[hse_performance_reporting_and_incident_management|HSE incidents]] into the [[hse_performance_reporting_and_incident_management|Synergi database]] within 7 days of occurrence. They are also responsible for entering monthly exposure hours and HSE performance statistics into the database within 7 days after month-end. If a site HSE manager is unable to directly enter information, the Company HSE Manager or their designate will perform this task. The [[hse_roles_and_responsibilities_company_wide|Company HSE Manager]] oversees the overall process.

**Data Inclusion and Subcontractor Management**
The Company HSE Statistics report **will** encompass the HSE performance of:
*   Direct employees, irrespective of their specific worksite.
*   All employees at worksites where the Company maintains direct control of operations.
*   Subcontractors at worksites where the Company maintains direct control of operations.
*   Indirect subcontractors at worksites where the Company maintains direct control of operations.

Where necessary, the HSE function **will** collect HSE performance data directly from [[subcontractor_quality_assurance_0_prc_7006|subcontractors]]. Furthermore, the HSE function **will** regularly [[subcontractor_audit_and_non_conformance_management|audit subcontractor HSE performance information]] to ensure its accuracy and reliability.

**Reporting Process**
*   All information for key measures calculation **is to be collected** at the end of each month.
*   All information for key measures calculation **is to be reported** at the end of each month.
*   At the end of each month, the site HSE **will report** collated information to the Company using the standard reporting document.
*   Communication of HSE performance data to the Company HSE Manager **shall** be facilitated using the Standard Reporting Spreadsheet.
*   This communication **shall** occur no later than 4 days after the defined month-end day.
*   An Excel spreadsheet is provided to assist with HSE exposure rate calculations. The latest version of this spreadsheet can be acquired by consulting the Company HSE Manager.

Each Business Unit (BU) and Business Area (BA) is mandated to establish and maintain quality checking routines for HSE data.

All information required for key measures calculation is to be collected and reported at the end of each month. This task is performed by a designated person at each worksite.

Each Business Unit shall record required HSE data into the [[aker_kvaerner_synergi_database|Aker Kvaerner Synergi database]]. AK Chemetics HSE managers are responsible for entering HSE incidents into the database within 7 days of occurrence. Monthly exposure hours and HSE performance statistics must be entered within 7 days after month-end. If a site HSE manager is unable to enter information directly, the Company HSE Manager or their designate will perform this task.

The primary responsibility for the collection of HSE performance statistics lies with the HSE Manager at the Works site, or their designate. At sites without a full-time HSE Manager, the person responsible for HSE matters will undertake this collection. The HSE Manager is also responsible for implementing management systems to ensure the accurate collection and tallying of HSE performance information.

HSE Statistics are collected at the end of each month. At month-end, the HSE will report this information to the Company using the standard reporting document.

Where necessary, the HSE will collect HSE performance data directly from subcontractors and will audit subcontractor HSE performance information on a regular basis to ensure accuracy.

The collated HSE performance data for each site is communicated to the Company HSE Manager. This communication must be submitted in the Standard Reporting Spreadsheet no later than 4 days after the month-end day. An Excel spreadsheet is provided to facilitate HSE exposure rate calculations; consult the Company HSE Manager to acquire the latest version of this spreadsheet.
