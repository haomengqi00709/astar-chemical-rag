# Confined Space Hazard Assessment Requirements

Confined space entry hazard assessments are a specialized application of [[0-PRC-8007_job_hazard_analysis]] (JHA) and **will** have specific requirements due to the inherent high risks associated with confined spaces.

A JHA **is required** for all confined space entry or confined space work. This is a mandatory application criterion for JHA.

For detailed procedural guidance on confined space entry, refer to [[0-PRC-8502_confined_space_entry]]. This procedure outlines the comprehensive steps and safety protocols necessary for working in confined spaces.

The record of the JHA for confined space entry **is to be kept** using the specialized form [[0-REP-8007b_confined_space_hazard_assessment_form]]. This form is designed to capture the unique hazards and control measures specific to confined space environments.
