# Warranties and Defect Correction (0-SPC-6001)

This page details the warranties provided for the works and the procedures for defect correction under construction subcontract 0-SPC-6001.

**Warranties**
The works are warranted to be fit and suitable for their intended purposes as described in the [[standard_conditions_for_construction_subcontract_0_spc_6001#Works Information|Works Information]]. The Subcontractor acknowledges that Aker Kvaerner Chemetics relies on the Subcontractor’s skill and judgment regarding the fitness and suitability of the works.

The works are warranted to be of merchantable quality and free of [[standard_conditions_for_construction_subcontract_0_spc_6001#Defect|defects]]. Unless otherwise specified in the Works Information, all materials used in the works are warranted to be new and of best quality. These warranties benefit Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' successors and assigns, as well as the Owner, and are in addition to any warranties arising by operation of law.

**Defect Management**
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may instruct the Subcontractor to search for a Defect. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may notify a Defect to the Subcontractor at any time before the defects date.

The Subcontractor **shall** correct Defects, whether or not Aker Kvaerner Chemetics notifies them. After [[standard_conditions_for_construction_subcontract_0_spc_6001#Completion|Completion]], the Subcontractor **shall** correct notified Defects before the end of the defects correction period. This period begins at the later of Completion or when the Defect is notified.

Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **shall** issue the [[standard_conditions_for_construction_subcontract_0_spc_6001#Defects Certificate|Defects Certificate]] to the Subcontractor on the later of the defects date or the end of the last defects correction period.

**Uncorrected Defects and Damage**
If the Subcontractor has not corrected a notified Defect within its defects correction period, Aker Kvaerner Chemetics will assess the cost of having the Defect corrected by other parties. The Subcontractor **shall** pay this assessed cost of uncorrected Defects.

Until the Defects Certificate has been issued, and unless otherwise instructed by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, the Subcontractor **shall** replace any loss of [[standard_conditions_for_construction_subcontract_0_spc_6001#Equipment and Materials|Equipment and Materials]] and repair any damage to the works, Equipment and Materials.
