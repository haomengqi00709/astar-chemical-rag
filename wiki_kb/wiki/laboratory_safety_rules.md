# Laboratory Safety Rules

This page outlines the specific safety rules that **must** be adhered to by all personnel and guests within Aker Kvaerner Chemetics [[hse_burnaby_laboratory|laboratory]] facilities, as specified in procedure [[hse_site_postings_overview]] (0-PRC-8004) and referenced by [[worksite_safety_rules_general]]. These rules are designed to ensure a safe and compliant laboratory environment.

**Mandatory Laboratory Safety Rules:**

*   **Personal Responsibility:** Personnel are responsible for their own safety, for the safety of others, and for keeping their work area clean.
*   **Personal Protective Equipment (PPE):** Safety glasses **must** be worn in the Laboratory (office area excepted).
*   **Substance Use:** Consuming or possession of illegal drugs or alcohol on company premises is **prohibited**. Refer to the [[prohibited_substances_and_conduct]] policy for further details.
*   **Food and Drink:** Preparation and consumption of food or drink are strictly **forbidden** in the Laboratory working area.
*   **Smoking:** Smoking is strictly **prohibited** inside the building.
*   **Working Alone:** Working alone in the Laboratory is **prohibited** (office area excepted).
*   **Chemical Waste:** Chemical wastes **must** be disposed of safely and promptly.
*   **Chemical Labeling and Storage:** All chemicals are to be correctly and clearly labelled. All chemicals are to be stored in accordance with WHMIS guidelines.
*   **Spill Reporting and Management:** All spills **must** be reported to the CTC Supervisor immediately. All spills **must** be promptly and completely neutralized, absorbed, and disposed of in accordance with the “Hazardous Laboratory Chemicals Disposal Guide”.
*   **Emergency Procedures:** Personnel **must** be familiar with emergency procedures detailed in the CTC Laboratory Safety Manual.
*   **Injury Reporting:** All injuries, no matter how slight, **must** be reported to the CTC Supervisor immediately. For incident reporting guidelines, see [[hse_incident_reporting]].
*   **Guest Access:** Guests are not allowed in the Laboratory working area without the prior permission from the CTC Supervisor or Manager. Permitted guests **will** be accompanied at all times by a Laboratory employee.

These rules are prominently posted within laboratory facilities and **will** be clearly readable from a distance of two meters.
