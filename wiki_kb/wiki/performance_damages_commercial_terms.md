# Performance Damages (Commercial Terms)

Performance damages are a type of [[order_specific_conditions_for_purchase]] designed to address instances where a Vendor's delivered Goods and Services fail to meet specified performance criteria. If a Defect included in the Defects Certificate shows low performance, the Vendor is obligated to pay the amount of low performance damages as stated in the contract.
