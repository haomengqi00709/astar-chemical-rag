# Template Inline Filter Equipment and Operational Specifications

This page outlines the general equipment and operational specifications for the Template Inline Filter, as referenced in Document ID [[4-PDS-XXXX-R1_template_inline_filter_datasheet_administration]]. These specifications guide the selection and configuration of the filter unit to meet the required process conditions.

**Type of Operation**: The desired level of automation for the filter's operation. Options include:

*   **Manual**: Requires direct human intervention for all operational steps.
*   **Semi-Automatic**: Some operational steps are automated, while others require manual input.
*   **Automatic**: The filter operates autonomously with minimal human intervention, typically controlled by a [[control_system_architecture_block_diagram]].

**Filter Type**: The fundamental design and mechanism of the filter. Common types specified include:

*   **Cartridge**: Utilizes disposable or cleanable cartridges for filtration.
*   **Bag**: Employs filter bags, often used for high flow rates and solids loading.
*   **Basket**: Features a reusable basket with a filter mesh, suitable for coarse filtration.

For detailed process parameters and design conditions, refer to [[template_filter_datasheet_process_design_conditions]]. Information regarding the materials used in the construction of the filter can be found in [[inline_filter_material_specifications]].
