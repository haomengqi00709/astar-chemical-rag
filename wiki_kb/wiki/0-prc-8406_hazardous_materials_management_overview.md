# Hazardous Materials Management Overview (0-PRC-8406)

Procedure 0-PRC-8406 outlines the minimum requirements for the management of hazardous materials purchased by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. This procedure ensures that all hazardous materials are handled safely and in compliance with relevant regulations throughout their lifecycle, from procurement to on-site management.

**Scope and Applicability**
The requirements detailed in this procedure apply to all materials purchased by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or its subcontractors, unless explicitly noted otherwise. This includes materials shipped to a work site or directly to a client.

**General Requirements**
Hazardous materials **must** be clearly identified during shipping. Material Safety Data Sheets (MSDS) for hazardous materials **must** be collected, compiled, and distributed to the site where the material will be used.

**Roles and Responsibilities**
It is the joint responsibility of the person making the requisition and the [[request_for_quotation_and_purchase_order_responsibilities|Purchasing Agent]] to flag items being purchased that may be hazardous to people or the environment. This initial identification is crucial for triggering the subsequent safety protocols.

For detailed procurement steps, refer to [[0-prc-8406_hazardous_materials_procurement_and_identification]].
For specific labeling and documentation requirements, see [[0-prc-8406_hazardous_materials_labeling_and_documentation]].
Information on packaging and shipping compliance can be found in [[0-prc-8406_hazardous_materials_packaging_and_shipping]].
On-site handling and management responsibilities are detailed in [[0-prc-8406_hazardous_materials_receiving_and_on_site_management]].

This procedure contributes to the overall [[aker_kvaerner_hse_policy_manual|HSE Policy Manual]] and [[workplace_health_and_environmental_practices|workplace health and environmental practices]].
