# Equipment Type Codes

Within the [[equipment_identification_numbering_format_and_conventions|plant equipment identification system]], a single letter is used to designate the general type of equipment item. These codes provide a concise and standardized way to categorize equipment across all projects. The following codes are mandated for use:

*   **A**: Dispersion (e.g., Agitator, Mixer, Blender, Aerator)
*   **B**: Distribution (e.g., Distributor, Spray Nozzle, Spray Pipe)
*   **C**: Gas Motive (e.g., Blower, Compressor, Fan)
*   **D**: Gas Cleaning (e.g., Cyclone, Precipitator, Dust Collection)
*   **E**: Electrical (e.g., Transformer, Motor Control Center, Switchgear)
*   **F**: Combustion (e.g., Furnace, Fired Boiler, Incinerator, Kiln, Stack)
*   **G**: Diminution (e.g., Grinder, Crusher, Classifier)
*   **H**: Heat Transfer (e.g., Heater, Cooler, Cooling Tower, Economiser)
*   **J**: Evaporation (e.g., Crystalliser, Evaporator)
*   **K**: Separation (e.g., Filter, Decanter, Clarifier, Mesh Pad, Candle Filter)
*   **M**: Material Handling (e.g., Conveyor, Elevator, Bin, Hopper, Crane)
*   **P**: Liquid Motive (e.g., Pump)
*   **R**: Reaction (e.g., Reactor, Converter, Electrolyser, Ion Exchange)
*   **S**: Services (e.g., Safety Shower, Fire Fighting equipment)
*   **T**: Storage (e.g., Tank, Receiver, Drum, Sump)
*   **U**: Gas-Liquid Transfer (e.g., Tower, Column, Stripper, Absorber)
*   **V**: Vacuum (e.g., Vacuum Pump, Ejector)
*   **X**: Miscellaneous (for equipment not fitting into other categories)

These codes are integral to forming the complete equipment identification number, as detailed in the [[equipment_identification_numbering_format_and_conventions|Equipment Identification Numbering Format and Conventions]].
