# HSE Performance Statistics Procedure (0-PRC-8002)

This procedure, 0-PRC-8002, outlines the comprehensive process for the collection, quality assurance, approval, and reporting of Health, Safety, and Environmental (HSE) performance statistics across Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. The primary objective is to ensure strict compliance with the overarching [[hse_roles_and_responsibilities_company_wide|HSE Policy]] of Aker Kvaerner and the specific requirements of [[group_hse_governance_procedures|Group Procedure 2.2 PR 1 "HSE Reporting Procedure"]].

**Definitions**
*   **HSE**: Refers to Health, Safety, and Environment.
*   **Company**: Denotes Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. For historical context, refer to [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_name_change_history]].
*   **Group**: Refers to the Aker Kvaerner Group of Companies.

**HSE Data Recording and Approval**
All Business Units (BUs) and Business Areas (BAs) **must** record the required HSE data into the Aker Kvaerner Synergi database. This database is a critical tool for recording OHS information, facilitating knowledge sharing across the Group, and generating comprehensive HSE performance statistics. The specific data requirements are defined in section 3.2 of the original document. Data available in SQL: 0-PRC-8002.

Key requirements for data management include:
*   HSE data **shall** be quality checked by an HSE function on a monthly basis.
*   HSE data **shall** be approved by the respective BU/BA president each month.
*   Each BU and BA **is required** to establish and maintain robust quality checking routines.
*   Submission dates for verified HSE data **shall** align with the established [[project_financial_and_tax_structure|financial reporting timetable]].

For Business Areas where the Synergi database is not currently in use, the following **mandatory** reporting protocol applies:
*   The BA **shall** submit a Quarterly HSE Report to Group HSE.
*   This Quarterly HSE Report **shall** be complete and duly authorized.
*   The report **shall** be submitted on behalf of all companies operating within their designated area.
*   Submission of the Quarterly HSE Report **shall** occur on a quarterly basis.

**Month-End Definition**
For the purpose of HSE statistics collection and reporting, month-end is officially defined by the Group accounts department. Generally, this is the end of the last Saturday of a given month. The Group accounts department publishes a calendar detailing these month-end dates at the beginning of each accounting year.

The procedure ensures compliance with the Health Safety and Environment Policy of Aker Kvaerner and Group Procedure 2.2 PR 1 “HSE Reporting Procedure”.

For clarity within this document:
*   **HSE** means Health, Safety, and Environment.
*   **Company** means Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
*   **Group** means Aker Kvaerner Group of Companies.

The required HSE data is specifically defined under section 3.2 of the procedure. All collected HSE data undergoes a monthly quality check by an HSE function and must be approved by the respective Business Unit (BU) or Business Area (BA) president each month.

Submission dates for verified HSE data are aligned with the financial reporting timetable. For Business Areas where the [[aker_kvaerner_synergi_database|Synergi database]] is not in use, the BA is required to submit a Quarterly HSE Report to Group HSE. This report must be complete, authorized, and submitted on a quarterly basis, covering all companies within their area.

Month-end for reporting purposes is defined by the Group's accounts department, generally falling on the last Saturday of a given month. A calendar of these month-end dates is published by the Group accounts department at the beginning of each accounting year.

The Company HSE Statistics report encompasses the HSE performance of:
*   Direct employees, regardless of their specific worksite.
*   All employees at worksites where the Company directly controls operations.
*   Subcontractors at worksites where the Company directly controls operations.
*   Indirect subcontractors at worksites where the Company directly controls operations.
