# Press Filter Ancillary Equipment and Vendor Supply

This page details the ancillary equipment and accessories required for the Press Filter, as specified in Document ID [[4-DST-XXXX-R0_template_plate_filter_datasheet_administration]]. All listed accessories are to be supplied and mounted by the vendor, ensuring a complete and integrated system.

**Vendor Supplied and Mounted Accessories**
The following accessories for the Press Filter **shall** be Supplied & Mounted by the Vendor:

*   **Accessory 1.0: Process Automation Package**
    *   Description: A complete process automation package including a control panel and all necessary piping, local instrumentation, control valves, manual valves, motor starters, local single source power disconnect switch, and wiring. This ensures automated and safe operation of the filter.

*   **Accessory 2.0: Core & Corner Blowdown**
    *   Description: System for core and corner blowdown, complete with air connection and isolation & check valve. This is crucial for efficient cake discharge and cleaning.

*   **Accessory 3.0: Automatic Plate Shifting System**
    *   Description: An automatic plate shifting system, equipped with a 304 SS (Stainless Steel) shifter chain. This system automates the opening and closing of filter plates for cake discharge. The [[plate_filter_material_specifications|shifter chain material]] is specifically 304 SS.

*   **Accessory 4.0: Filter Plate Automatic Hydraulic Opening & Closing System**
    *   Description: A hydraulic system for the automatic opening and closing of the filter plates, enhancing operational efficiency and safety.

*   **Accessory 5.0: Launderer Tray**
    *   Description: A launderer tray, designed to collect filtrate or wash liquid.

*   **Accessory 6.0: Bombay Style Drip Trays**
    *   Description: Bombay style drip trays, complete with a hydraulic actuator system and associated control. These trays prevent drips during plate shifting.

*   **Accessory 7.0: Ram Boot**
    *   Description: A ram boot, typically used to protect the hydraulic ram from process fluids or debris.

These accessories are integral to the functionality and automation of the Press Filter. For general equipment specifications, refer to [[plate_filter_general_equipment_fields]].
