# Vendor Document Requirements (VDR)

Vendor Document Requirements (VDR) specify the documentation that vendors **must** provide throughout the project lifecycle. These requirements ensure that all necessary technical, quality, and operational information is submitted in a timely and compliant manner.

**Review Process**
Documents received from vendors **must** be reviewed to ensure they meet the intent of the technical requirements issued by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. The detailed procedure for this review is outlined in [[0-PRC-0007_review_of_vendor_documentation]].

**Revision History**
Vendor Document Requirements (VDR) were formally added to the 4-PRC-0003 procedure in Revision 1, highlighting their importance in managing vendor deliverables.
