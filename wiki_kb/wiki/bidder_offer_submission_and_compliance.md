# Bidder Offer Submission and Compliance

Bidders must ensure their offers are submitted correctly and explicitly address any deviations from the Request for Quotation (RFQ).

**Offer Compliance:**
A Bidder’s offer will be considered to be in full compliance with the [[request_for_quotation_specifics|Request for Quotation]] and all referenced documents unless any deviations or exceptions are explicitly stated and thoroughly described within the offer. It is critical for Bidders to clearly highlight any non-compliance to avoid misinterpretation during evaluation.

**Offer Submission Methods and Deadlines:**
*   **Mandatory:** Bidders must submit their offer on or before the date specified in the covering letter of the Request for Quotation.
*   Offers may be submitted via various methods, including e-mail, telefax, mail, courier, or hand delivery.
*   Submissions made by e-mail or telefax **must** be followed by a paper format submission.
*   The paper format for e-mail or telefax submissions **must** be received by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics within five calendar days of the electronic submission.

Adherence to these submission protocols and clear declaration of compliance or deviations are essential for an offer to be considered valid and properly evaluated.
