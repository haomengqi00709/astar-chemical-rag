# Material Requirements and Handling for Austenitic Stainless Steel Piping (5-SPC-0121)

This section outlines the mandatory requirements for material selection, handling, and quality control during the fabrication of austenitic stainless steel piping.

**Material Selection and Approval:**
*   Only low grade of [material type missing in source] **will** be used for base materials.
*   Fittings that do not require welding may be standard grade.
*   No deviation from the specified or approved type/grade/class of material is permitted without the [[5-SPC-0121_austenitic_stainless_steel_piping_specification_overview#agreement-in-writing|written approval]] of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
*   All base metal **must** be in the solution annealed condition.
*   Filler metal **must** be low carbon 'L' grade. Non-'L' grade electrodes **will** only be used when specifically approved in writing by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Material Segregation and Contamination Prevention:**
*   Effective segregation of stainless steels from, and prevention of contamination by, carbon steel **must** be maintained throughout the fabrication process.
*   No grinding or arc air gouging of carbon steel is permitted nearby stainless steel fabrication areas.
*   Wire brushes and chipping hammers used for stainless steel **must** be made of stainless steel.
*   Grinding wheels used for stainless steel **must** only be used for stainless steel.
*   The surface and edges of any austenitic steel to be heated **must** be cleaned free from all traces of foreign matter (e.g., marking paint, oil) by grinding, wire brushing with a stainless steel brush, and/or non-chlorinated solvent cleaning immediately prior to heat application.
*   The fabricator **must** take all necessary controls to prevent contamination of weldments at all stages of fabrication. These controls **must** include the segregation of wire brushes, grinding wheels, and other tools for use exclusively on stainless steels.

**Material Certification and Traceability:**
*   Mill Test Certification, showing results from analysis and other tests (in accordance with relevant material specifications, e.g., [[ASME_bpvc_section_ii_materials|ASME]], [[ASTM_a380_cleaning_passivation_stainless_steel|ASTM]]), **must** be supplied for all materials, including welding consumables.
*   When required by the purchase order, Standard, or certified drawings, a chemical analysis **must** be performed on production welds made by submerged arc, flux cored arc welding, or weld overlaid surfaces. The weld(s) to be checked **will** be selected at random by the [[5-SPC-0121_austenitic_stainless_steel_piping_specification_overview#inspector|Inspector]].
*   Where chemical analysis results show a weldment falls outside approved specification or code requirements, the supplier **must** promptly advise Aker Kvaerner Chemetics in writing of the proposed course of action. This proposed course of action **must** include details of any additional testing necessary to fully define the extent of the non-conformance. All repairs and retests **will** be to the supplier's account.
*   When required by the purchase order, the fabricator **must** maintain full traceability of materials back to original mill certification.
*   The fabricator's internal controls **must** prevent the use of unspecified materials as a minimum.

**Welding Consumables:**
*   All consumables **must** be readily identifiable. Lack of adequate identification **will** be cause for rejection of the weld(s).
*   Weld consumables **must** be stored in accordance with the manufacturer's recommendations. This storage requirement applies particularly to field welding.
