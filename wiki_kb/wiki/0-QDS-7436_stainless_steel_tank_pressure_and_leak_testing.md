# Stainless Steel Tank Pressure and Leak Testing (0-QDS-7436)

For the Stainless Steel Tank project (Document ID: 0-QDS-7436), rigorous pressure and leak testing procedures are mandated to ensure the integrity and safety of the tank. All testing **must** be documented and subject to Chemetics' surveillance.

**Hydrotest:**
*   **Requirement:** A Hydrotest is required to be included in the Vendor's Inspection and Test Plan (I & T Plan) and **must** be part of the [[0-QDS-7436_quality_record_definitions|Quality Assurance Documentation Package (FP)]].
*   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Chemetics requires both "Witness" and "Hold" surveillance for the Hydrotest.
    *   **Witness:** A Chemetics representative **must** be present during the execution of the Hydrotest.
    *   **Hold:** The Vendor **shall not** proceed with subsequent manufacturing or inspection steps until Chemetics has formally released the "Hold" point after satisfactory completion and review of the Hydrotest.

**Air/Soap Test:**
*   **Requirement:** An Air/Soap Test is required to be included in the Vendor's Inspection and Test Plan (I & T Plan) and **must** be part of the [[0-QDS-7436_quality_record_definitions|Quality Assurance Documentation Package (FP)]].
*   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Chemetics requires both "Witness" and "Hold" surveillance for the Air/Soap Test.
    *   **Witness:** A Chemetics representative **must** be present during the execution of the Air/Soap Test.
    *   **Hold:** The Vendor **shall not** proceed with subsequent manufacturing or inspection steps until Chemetics has formally released the "Hold" point after satisfactory completion and review of the Air/Soap Test.

These tests are essential for confirming the structural integrity and leak-tightness of the Stainless Steel Tank under specified conditions. The dual surveillance requirement (Witness and Hold) ensures direct oversight and approval by Chemetics at critical stages.

*   **Quality Record Requirement:** Hydrotest documentation is designated as "Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package" (FP) within the Vendor's Inspection and Test Plan.
