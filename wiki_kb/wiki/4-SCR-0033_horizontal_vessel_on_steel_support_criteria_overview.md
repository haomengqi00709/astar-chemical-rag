# Horizontal Vessel on Steel: Support Criteria Overview (4-SCR-0033)

This document, identified as **4-SCR-0033**, provides the essential support criteria for horizontal vessels mounted on steel structures. It is a foundational engineering specification developed by Chemetics, a division of Aker Kvaerner Canada Inc., specifically for projects requiring robust and reliable support systems for horizontal vessels. The project title associated with this document is "Horizontal Vessel on Steel."

The primary objective of these criteria is to ensure the structural integrity and operational stability of horizontal vessels throughout their lifecycle. This involves meticulous consideration of various loading conditions and environmental factors. Engineers and designers involved in the structural analysis, civil works, and equipment installation **must** adhere to the guidelines presented in this document to guarantee compliance with safety standards and design specifications.

A critical requirement outlined in this specification is that all loads **must** be applied at Datum. This precise instruction ensures uniformity and accuracy in all structural calculations, preventing discrepancies that could compromise the safety and performance of the vessel and its supporting structure. The consistent application of loads at a defined datum is paramount for reliable engineering analysis.

This document is an integral part of the comprehensive suite of engineering specifications within Chemetics and Aker Kvaerner, reflecting the company's commitment to high-quality design and construction practices. It serves as a key reference for ensuring that all horizontal vessel installations meet stringent performance and safety benchmarks.
