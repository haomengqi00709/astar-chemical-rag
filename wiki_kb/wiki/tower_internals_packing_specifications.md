# Tower Internals Packing Specifications

This page outlines the detailed specifications for the packing used within tower internals, as referenced in the [[4-DST-XXXX-R0_tower_internals_datasheet_administration|Tower Internals Datasheet (4-DST-XXXX-R0)]]. These specifications are critical for ensuring optimal mass transfer and separation efficiency.

**Mandatory Packing Identification:**
*   **Packing Tag No.**: This is a mandatory field for unique identification of the packing section.
*   **Packing Access Area**: This is a mandatory field, specifying the area required for accessing the packing.
*   **Packing Type**: This is a mandatory field, indicating the specific type of packing material used (e.g., random packing, structured packing).
*   **Packing Material**: This is a mandatory field, detailing the material of construction for the packing.

**Physical and Performance Parameters:**
The following parameters define the physical characteristics and expected performance of the packing:
*   **Packing Size**: mm
*   **Packing Height**: m
*   **Pressure Drop Wet**: kPa. This indicates the pressure drop across the packing when wet.
*   **Wetting Rate**: m3/h·m. This measures the liquid flow rate per unit of packing surface area.
*   **Flooding**: %. This represents the percentage of flooding, a critical operational limit.
*   **Loading**: %. This indicates the percentage of hydraulic loading on the packing.
*   **Hold-Up**: %. This refers to the percentage of liquid held up within the packing.
*   **Height Equiv. Theor. Plt. (HETP)**: m. This is a measure of the packing's separation efficiency, representing the height of packing equivalent to one theoretical plate.

These packing specifications are designed to meet the [[tower_internals_process_design_conditions|process design conditions]] and are supported by various [[tower_internals_support_and_ancillary_components|support and ancillary components]] within the tower.
