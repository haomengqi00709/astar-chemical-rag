# Subcontractor Document Templates

Subcontractors will be provided with all necessary document templates to facilitate the preparation of documents listed in the [[engineering_scope_checklists_0_lst_22xx|0-LST-8YXX: Engineering Scope Checklist]].

**Listing and Issuance:**
All applicable templates will be explicitly listed within the [[engineering_scope_checklists_0_lst_22xx|0-LST-8YXX: Engineering Scope Checklist]].

**Mandatory Requirements for Templates:**
*   Document Templates **must** be issued to the Subcontractor in their native file format.
*   The word “[Template]” **must** appear in the document title of all Document Templates.
*   The word “[Template]” **must** appear in the file name of all Document Templates.

Document Templates must be issued to the Subcontractor in their native file format.

**Naming Conventions:** The word “[Template]” must appear in both the document title and the file name of all Document Templates.
