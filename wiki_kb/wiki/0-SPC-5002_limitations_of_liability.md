# Purchase Order Limitations of Liability

This page outlines the limitations of liability for both Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Vendor under the Purchase Order (PO).

**Exclusions from Liability:**
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Vendor **will not** be liable under the Purchase Order, in law or equity (including for negligence), for the following types of damages:
*   Loss of profit.
*   Loss of use.
*   Loss of production.
*   Any indirect or consequential damage.
*   Special, exemplary, or punitive damages.

These limitations apply to both parties, ensuring a defined scope of financial responsibility in the event of a breach or other claim.
