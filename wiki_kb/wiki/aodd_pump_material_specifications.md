# AODD Pump Material Specifications

This page details the material specifications for key components of the Air Operated Double Diaphragm (AODD) Pump, as specified in document 4-DST-XXXX-R1. The selection of appropriate materials is crucial for ensuring chemical compatibility with the pumped fluid, resistance to wear, and overall pump longevity and safety.

**Material Specification:**
The following materials are specified for critical pump components:

*   **Casing:** The main body or housing of the pump. The casing material must be resistant to the corrosive and abrasive properties of the pumped fluid and capable of withstanding the design pressures and temperatures. Common materials include various plastics (e.g., polypropylene, PVDF), stainless steels, or exotic alloys.
*   **Cover:** The component that encloses the diaphragm and air valve mechanism. Its material selection follows similar criteria to the casing, ensuring integrity and chemical resistance.
*   **Diaphragm:** The flexible membrane that moves to displace fluid. Diaphragm material is critical for chemical resistance, flexibility, and fatigue life. Common materials include PTFE, Santoprene, Buna-N, Viton, and EPDM, chosen based on fluid compatibility and temperature.
*   **Ball Check Valve:** The spherical component within the check valve assembly that controls fluid direction. Ball material must be highly resistant to the pumped fluid and wear from solids. Options include PTFE, Santoprene, stainless steel, or ceramic.
*   **Valve Seat:** The component against which the ball check valve seals. The valve seat material must provide a good seal, resist chemical attack, and withstand wear. Materials often match or complement the ball material.
*   **O-Ring:** Sealing components used throughout the pump to prevent leaks. O-ring materials are selected based on their chemical compatibility with the fluid, temperature range, and sealing effectiveness. Common materials include Viton, EPDM, Buna-N, and PTFE encapsulated options.

The careful selection of these materials ensures that the AODD pump can safely and reliably handle the specified process fluid under the given operating conditions, minimizing the risk of corrosion, erosion, and leakage.
