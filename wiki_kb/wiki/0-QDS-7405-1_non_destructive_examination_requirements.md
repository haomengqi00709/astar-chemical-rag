# Blower Quality Data Sheet Non-Destructive Examination Requirements

This page outlines the specific Non-Destructive Examination (NDE) requirements for the Blower Package, as detailed in Document ID 0-QDS-7405-1. These examinations are critical for verifying the integrity and quality of materials and welds without causing damage.

**Mandatory Requirements:**
*   Non-Destructive Testing (NDT) **shall** be performed in accordance with ASME VIII Div. 1.
*   Non-Destructive Examination (NDE) **shall** also be performed in accordance with API-617.

**Specific NDE Activities:**
*   **Dye Penetrant Testing:**
    *   Required for 100% of Impeller blade welds.
    *   Required as FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package) and for Chemetics Witness.

These NDE requirements are part of the broader [[0-QDS-7405-1_inspection_and_documentation_requirements]] and contribute to the overall [[quality_management_and_compliance]] of the Blower Package. For definitions of "FP" and other quality record terms, refer to [[0-QDS-7405-1_quality_record_definitions]].
