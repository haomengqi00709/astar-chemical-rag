# Mechanical Equipment Checkout Procedure

The mechanical checkout procedure for equipment is a critical step in project execution, verifying installation against [[piping_and_instrumentation_drawings|P&ID]] and [[equipment_design_criteria_4_prc_0002|specifications]]. This procedure ensures that equipment is prepared for subsequent stages, including [[water_and_air_trials_1_prc_0006|water batching]] and [[commissioning_planning_and_management|commissioning]]. All documentation generated during this process will form part of the mechanical completion package.

**Checkout Process and Responsibilities:**
During the construction phase, the site sub-contractor is responsible for conducting an initial check for completeness. Following this, the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site supervisor reviews the installed equipment visually against [[4-gad-xxxx_equipment_layout_drawing|general arrangement drawings]] and [[electrical_installation_details|installation instructions]].

Once the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site supervisor is satisfied, he shall notify the [[project_manager_planning_responsibilities|Project Manager]] that the equipment is ready for a final review by a mechanical engineer from the office. This engineer conducts a visual inspection of the equipment against the [[equipment_design_and_specification_4_prc_0003|equipment drawings]] and [[piping_and_instrumentation_drawings|P&ID]], confirming that the installation adheres to all construction documents.

**Documentation and Deficiency Management:**
Each equipment item **must** be inspected in accordance with attached guidelines. Findings from these inspections are recorded on the [[mechanical_equipment_checkout_list]]. All identified deficiencies **shall** be documented and provided to the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site supervisor.

The site supervisor is responsible for working with a deficiency crew, supplied by the site sub-contractor, to correct all identified deficiencies. Outstanding work, such as [[coatings_design_and_specification_7_prc_0002|painting]], [[insulation_design_and_specification_6_prc_0002|insulating]], and installing [[flange_guard_installation|flange guards]], is undertaken by the deficiency crew after initial cleaning, flushing, and [[pressure_and_leak_testing|hydrotesting]].

For [[vessel_and_tank_mechanical_checkout|vessel internals]] (e.g., packing material, size), the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site supervisor may perform the check. If the site supervisor conducts this check, the engineer **shall** obtain a signed form confirming that the internals have been inspected and conform to the design.

**Purpose and Design Deficiencies:**
The primary purpose of the mechanical checkout is to ensure that the plant has been constructed strictly in accordance with the [[design_document_preparation_and_software_validation|design documents]] (drawings and specifications). Any other modifications or changes identified during the checkout process **shall** be treated as design deficiencies.

It is critical to note that a design deficiency is not considered a punch list item. Instead, a design deficiency **shall** be documented and forwarded to the [[project_manager_planning_responsibilities|Project Manager]] for formal resolution.

The mechanical checkout procedure for equipment is a critical step in project execution, verifying installation against [[piping_and_instrumentation_drawings|P&ID]] and [[equipment_design_criteria_4_prc_0002|specifications]]. This procedure ensures that equipment is prepared for subsequent stages, including water batching and commissioning. Mechanical checkout documents will form part of the mechanical completion package.

**Procedure Workflow**
*   During construction, the site sub-contractor checks for completeness before approaching the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site supervisor for review.
*   The Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site supervisor visually reviews installed equipment against general arrangement and installation instructions.
*   When the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site supervisor is satisfied, he notifies the Project Manager that equipment is ready for final review by a mechanical engineer from the office.
*   The engineer from the office conducts a visual inspection of equipment against equipment drawings and [[piping_and_instrumentation_drawings|P&ID]].
*   The engineer's inspection confirms installation is in accordance with construction documents.
*   Each equipment item is inspected in accordance with attached guidelines.
*   Findings are recorded on the [[mechanical_equipment_checkout_list|Mechanical Equipment Check-out List]].
*   All deficiencies are recorded and given to the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site supervisor.
*   The site supervisor works with a deficiency crew supplied by the site sub-contractor to correct all identified deficiencies.
*   Outstanding work (e.g., painting, insulating) is undertaken by the deficiency crew after cleaning, flushing, and hydrotesting. For specific requirements regarding flange guards, refer to [[flange_guard_installation]].

**General Checks**
*   Electrical connections must be checked and power supply confirmed.
*   Instrumentation must match the manufacturer’s documentation.
