# 4-SPC-0015 Definitions

This page provides definitions for key terms used within Standard 4-SPC-0015 to ensure clear understanding and consistent application of its requirements.

*   **Agreement in Writing**: Means submission in writing by letter, not merely by inclusion in approval drawings.
*   **Inspector**: Means Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Quality Assurance Inspector or other Authorized Representative.
