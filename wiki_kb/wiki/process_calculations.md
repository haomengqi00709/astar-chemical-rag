# Process Calculations

Process calculations are fundamental to the design and sizing of equipment, ensuring that all technical requirements are met. This category encompasses various types of calculations, including specialized analyses like Heat Transfer Calculations.

**Heat Transfer Calculations**
Heat Transfer Calculations are a specific type of process calculation, often performed using specialized software such as HTRI (Heat Transfer Research, Inc.) per Section 3.6 commentary of 4-PRC-0003. These calculations **must** include:

*   **Calculation Methods and Formulae:** The specific methodologies and equations used.
*   **Acceptance Parameters or Criteria:** The defined limits or conditions that the calculation results **must** satisfy.
*   **All Calculation Input Data:** A complete record of all data used as input for the calculations.
*   **The Executed Calculation:** The detailed steps and results of the calculation.

**Preparation and Approval of Heat Transfer Calculations**
*   **Preparation:** Heat Transfer calculations **must** be prepared by Mechanical personnel.
*   **Checking:** The calculations **must** be checked by Process personnel.
*   **Approval:** Final approval **must** be granted by Mechanical personnel.

**Revision History**
Heat Transfer Calculations were formally added to the 4-PRC-0003 procedure in Revision 1. In Revision 3, Section 3.5 (Process Calculations) was expanded, indicating an increased emphasis or detail in this area.

*   Heat Transfer Calculations are usually HTRI calculations.
