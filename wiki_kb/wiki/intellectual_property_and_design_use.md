# Intellectual Property and Design Use

This page outlines the intellectual property rights and usage permissions related to the Vendor's design within the scope of the Purchase Order (PO).

**Indemnification for Infringement:**
*   The Vendor **must** indemnify Aker Kvaerner Chemetics against any claims, compensation, and costs arising from the Vendor infringing a patent.
*   The Vendor **must** also indemnify Aker Kvaerner Chemetics against any claims, compensation, and costs arising from the Vendor infringing a copyright.

**Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' Rights to Use and Copy Design:**
Unless otherwise specified in the [[purchase_order_definitions#Goods and Services Information|Goods and Services Information]], Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may use and copy the Vendor’s design for any purpose connected with:
*   Construction of the [[purchase_order_definitions#The Project|Project]].
*   Use of the Project.
*   Alteration of the Project.
*   Demolition of the Project.
*   Other purposes as explicitly stated in the Goods and Services Information.
