# TGAS/2 Thermal Design Software

TGAS/2 is a proprietary thermal design software developed by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. It is a specialized tool used for the thermal design of acid plant radial flow gas/gas heat exchangers, as detailed in [[4-prc-0008_gas_gas_heat_exchanger_thermal_design_procedure|Procedure 4-PRC-0008]].

**Technical Specifications:**
*   **Development:** Coded in Visual Basic.
*   **Interface:** Utilizes Microsoft Excel for both data input and output, providing a familiar user environment.
*   **Foundation:** Based on the original TGAS program, with significant enhancements.
*   **Calculation Methods:** Incorporates new heat transfer and pressure drop calculations derived from HTRI (Heat Transfer Research, Inc.) methods.
*   **Acoustic Analysis:** Includes advanced acoustic resonance calculations based on the work of Dr. H. Goyder, crucial for preventing vibration-induced failures.
*   **Functionality:** Features enhanced capabilities for data input/output and automatic drawing generation, streamlining the design process.

**Confidentiality Requirements:**
The TGAS/2 program is strictly confidential. It **must not** be duplicated or divulged to persons outside the employ of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. This strict confidentiality protects the proprietary algorithms and design methodologies embedded within the software.
