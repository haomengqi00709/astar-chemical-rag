# 0-SPC-2202-2 Table 1: Subcontractor Obligations and Procedures Details

Table 1, referenced within the **0-SPC-2202-2** Specification, provides specific and detailed information regarding the [[0-SPC-2202-2_subcontractor_site_obligations|obligations and procedures]] that subcontractors **must** adhere to when performing services on site.

This table serves as a comprehensive reference, elaborating on the general categories of obligations outlined in Annexure One of the main specification. It is expected to contain granular details such as:
*   Specific legislative acts or standards that require compliance.
*   Detailed requirements for [[hse_management_and_employee_responsibilities|Health and Safety Management Plans]], including risk assessments, emergency procedures, and reporting protocols.
*   Specific [[quality_management_and_compliance|Quality Assurance]] checkpoints, documentation requirements, and inspection procedures.
*   Precise guidelines for [[0-pol-9106_site_work_hours_and_rest_periods|site working hours]], including any restrictions or approval processes for overtime.
*   Detailed instructions for site clean-up, waste management, and environmental protection measures.
*   Specific protocols for [[construction_site_management_roles_0_prc_600|site access]], induction processes, and the use of constructional plant and vehicles.

Subcontractors are advised to consult this table for the exhaustive list of requirements to ensure full compliance with the terms of their subcontract.
