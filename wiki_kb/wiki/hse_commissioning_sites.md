# HSE Management at Commissioning Sites

Responsibility for Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) at Commissioning Sites (Section 13 of 0-PRC-8000) is assigned to the Manager of Process and Mechanical.

**Manager of Process and Mechanical Responsibilities:**
The Manager of Process and Mechanical is responsible for:
*   Maintaining operating plant HSE procedures compliant with Aker Kvaerner HSE guidelines and guidelines of local regulatory authorities.
*   Designating the HSE manager for commissioning works.
*   Supervising commissioning HSE Managers throughout plant commissioning.
*   Ensuring technical changes do not impact safe operation.

**Key HSE Activities for Commissioning:**
*   Workplace and worksite hazard assessments **must** be conducted.
*   A Hazard and Operability (HAZOP) study **must** be performed on all new plant designs and design modifications. Refer to [[hazard_and_operability_studies]] for more details.
*   A Workplace Hazardous Materials Information System (WHMIS) **must** be maintained.
*   HSE management plans for each commissioning works **must** be reviewed.

**Commissioning Site HSE Manager Role:**
Each work site **must** have a designated part-time/full-time HSE Manager for the duration of the commissioning works. Staff responsibilities for health and safety management at a Company commissioning site **must** be fulfilled by the Commissioning Manager. The Manager of Process and Mechanical may:
*   Appoint a full-time HSE Manager for the site.
*   Opt to have the construction HSE Manager continue through the commissioning phase.
*   Opt to have the construction HSE Manager continue through the project.

The commissioning HSE Manager is responsible for implementing:
*   The project health, safety, and environmental management plan, detailed in [[project-0-prc-8405_project_hse_plan]] (project specific).
*   The Aker Kvaerner Safety Policy Manual.

Relevant reference documents for commissioning HSE Manager duties include [[0-spc-8003_hse_responsibilities_field_managers]] and [[0-prc-8400_hse_management_construction_site_advisors]].

For overall Company HSE policies and responsibilities, refer to [[0-prc-8000_administration_of_hse]] and [[hse_roles_and_responsibilities_company_wide]]. Project-specific HSE planning is detailed in [[0-prc-8001_project_health_and_safety_planning]].

**Additional Responsibilities and Requirements:**
*   Workplace and worksite hazard assessments must be done.
*   Workplace Hazardous Materials Information System (WHMIS) must be maintained.
*   HSE management plans for each commissioning works must be reviewed.
*   Each work site must have a designated part-time/full-time HSE Manager for the duration of the commissioning works.
*   Staff responsibilities for health and safety management at a Company commissioning site must be fulfilled by the Commissioning Manager.
*   The Manager of Process and Mechanical may appoint a full-time HSE Manager for the site, or may opt to have the construction HSE Manager continue through the commissioning phase or through the entire project.
*   The project health, safety and environmental management plan is also detailed in the Aker Kvaerner Safety Policy Manual.
