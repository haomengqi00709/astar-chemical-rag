# Internal Project Management Reporting (PMR)

The internal project report, known as the Project Management Review (PMR) report, serves as a crucial tool for internal project oversight and senior management review. This report is primarily prepared by Project Accounting, with essential inputs from the Project Manager and Project Controls.

The PMR report **shall** be produced based on the same reporting period utilized for the overall project. It **must** be issued at least two days prior to the scheduled project review meeting to allow for adequate preparation and review by attendees.

The internal distribution of the PMR report **shall** include, but is not limited to, the following key documents:
*   Master Schedule – Level 1
*   Progress Curves
*   Change Order and Project Change Logs
*   Risk and Opportunity Register

It is important to note that most of the documents included in the internal distribution of the PMR are also prepared as components of the [[client_project_progress_reporting]].

The PMR report **shall** be reviewed with Senior Management on a monthly basis at the dedicated project review meeting. This review process **shall** include a verbal summary provided by the Project Manager, which **must** cover:
*   **Project Status:** A concise overview of current activities and any significant issues.
*   **Changes in Costs, Contingencies, and Margins:** An update on the financial aspects of the project.
*   **Status of Invoices and Payments:** Information regarding billing and payment collection.
*   **Areas of Concern or Opportunity:** Identification of potential problems or advantageous situations.

This structured internal reporting and review process ensures that project performance, financial health, and potential risks or opportunities are regularly assessed and communicated to senior leadership, facilitating informed decision-making and proactive management.
