# Electrical Purchase Orders

This page details the process for preparing and issuing purchase requisitions and purchase orders for electrical equipment, including the required documentation.

**Purchase Requisition (REQ)**
The requisition request for purchase (REQ) **will** be prepared in accordance with Table 2. Procedure 0-PRC-5002, "Requisition for Quotation or Purchase Order", **will** be followed in the preparation of the purchase requisition document.

The purchase requisition **will** have a list of technical documents required for the equipment shown in the request for requisition form. The list "Material Cost Codes", 0-LST-4002, **must** be checked to obtain the correct cost code for the request for requisition for quotation (for purchase requisition).

Documents to be listed on the purchase requisition include:
*   Site design conditions data sheet.
*   [[electrical_equipment_specifications]].
*   [[electrical_equipment_data_sheets]].
*   [[procurement_and_vendor_documentation]] (Vendor data requirements sheet).
*   Quality data sheet for the equipment.
*   Protective coating for chemical plants specification.

**Purchase Order (PO)**
The purchase order **will** be prepared and issued in accordance with Table 2. Procedure 0-PRC-5005, "Issue of Request for Quotation or Purchase Order", **will** be followed to prepare the purchase order.

**Roles and Responsibilities**
The requisition request for purchase documents **will** be prepared by the DDE. The documents **will** be forwarded to the purchasing department for official issue of the purchase order. The purchasing department is responsible for the commercial aspects.

Purchase order involves PR, PE, and PM.

**Exclusions**
Section 3.15 (Requisition – Purchase) specifically excludes T/R & CPU.
