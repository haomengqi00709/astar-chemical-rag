# Fan Datasheet Process Design Conditions

This page outlines the mandatory process design conditions that **must** be specified for the [[fan_datasheet_administration|Fan Datasheet Template]] (4-DST-XXXX-R0). These conditions are critical for accurate fan sizing, selection, and performance evaluation.

**General Requirements**
Process design conditions **must** be provided for both "DESIGN PER UNIT" and "ALTERNATE PER UNIT" scenarios. For each scenario, separate specifications are required for "Inlet" and "Outlet" conditions.

**Mandatory Process Design Conditions**
The following parameters are required:
*   **Fluid Description**: A detailed description of the fluid being handled. Refer to [[fluid_description_requirements]].
*   **Composition**: The composition of the fluid, measured in %v/v. Refer to [[fluid_composition_specification]].
*   **Solids Content/Type**: Description and percentage of any solids present in the fluid. Refer to [[solids_content_specification]].
*   **Flowrate (mass)**: The mass flow rate of the fluid, measured in kg/h. Refer to [[flowrate_specification]].
*   **Minimum Flowrate (mass)**: The minimum mass flow rate, measured in kg/h.
*   **Flowrate (actual volume)**: The actual volumetric flow rate, measured in Am3/h.
*   **Minimum Flowrate (actual volume)**: The minimum actual volumetric flow rate, measured in Am3/h.
*   **Temperature**: The operating temperature, measured in oC. Refer to [[temperature_design_conditions]].
*   **Pressure**: The operating pressure, measured in kPa(g). Refer to [[pressure_design_conditions]].
*   **Density**: The fluid density, measured in kg/m3. Refer to [[fluid_density_specification]].
*   **Compressibility Factor**: The compressibility factor of the fluid. Refer to [[compressibility_factor_specification]].
*   **Viscosity**: The fluid viscosity, measured in cP. Refer to [[fluid_viscosity_specification]].
*   **Specific Heat**: The specific heat of the fluid, measured in kJ/kg·oC. Refer to [[specific_heat_specification]].
*   **Molecular Weight**: The molecular weight of the fluid, measured in kg/kmol. Refer to [[molecular_weight_specification]].
*   **cp / cv Ratio**: The ratio of specific heats (cp/cv) for the fluid. Refer to [[cp_cv_ratio_specification]].

These conditions are essential inputs for the overall [[process_design_criteria_1_prc_0001]].

**Specific Design Conditions**
In addition to the general requirements, process design conditions must be specified for the following specific scenarios:
*   **DESIGN PER UNIT Inlet**
*   **DESIGN PER UNIT Outlet**
*   **ALTERNATE PER UNIT Inlet**
*   **ALTERNATE PER UNIT Outlet**
