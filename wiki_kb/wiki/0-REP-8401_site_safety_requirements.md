# Site Safety Requirements (0-REP-8401)

This page outlines the mandatory safety requirements and practices for site operations, as detailed in document 0-REP-8401. Adherence to these guidelines is crucial for maintaining a safe working environment for all personnel.

**Site Orientation and Training**
All Aker Kvaerner Chemetics employees and subcontractors **shall** undergo a comprehensive site orientation upon their initial arrival. This orientation **must** include a thorough review of site-specific safety issues. Furthermore, each Aker Kvaerner Chemetics employee and subcontractor **shall** be familiar with the client's overall safety program. [[hse_new_employee_orientation]]

**HSE Policy and Awareness**
The site **shall** prominently display the Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) policy and other relevant HSE notices. [[hse_site_postings_overview]] [[task_safety_awareness_overview]] **shall** be an integral part of all work planning meetings to ensure continuous safety awareness.

**Safety Management and Audits**
The client **shall** designate a Safety Officer/Co-Coordinator. The name and contact details of this individual are **required**. Regular [[0-PRC-8009_hse_audits_and_inspections_overview]] of the site are **required** to ensure ongoing compliance and identify potential hazards.

**Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)**
The site **shall** maintain an adequate reserve of [[0-PRC-8015_personal_protective_equipment_policy]] (PPE) to accommodate visitors as needed. Additionally, each Aker Kvaerner Chemetics employee and subcontractor **shall** be equipped with personal protection equipment, including spare cartridges for their respirator.

**Chemical Management**
Both Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Client **shall** possess Material Safety Data Sheets (MSDS) for all chemicals present on site. [[hazardous_materials_management_overview]] Chemical containers **shall** be clearly labeled and visible, and all chemical sampling points **shall** be properly labeled.

**General Site Safety**
The site **shall** ensure that HSE policy and other HSE notices are prominently posted. Task Safety Awareness **shall** be an integral part of all work planning meetings.

**Personnel Familiarization and Oversight**
All Aker Kvaerner Chemetics employees and subcontractors **shall** be familiar with the client's safety program. The client **shall** appoint a Safety Officer/Co-Coordinator, and their name and contact details are **required**.

**Safety Audits**
Regular safety audits of the site **shall** be conducted to ensure ongoing compliance and identify areas for improvement.
