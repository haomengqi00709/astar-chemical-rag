# Gasket Specification (5-SPC-0003)

The Gasket Specification, identified as 5-SPC-0003, is a vital document that defines the technical requirements for all gaskets used in piping systems. This specification ensures that gaskets provide effective sealing under specified operating conditions, preventing leaks and ensuring system integrity.

**Content Requirements**
The Gasket Specification **shall** contain the following detailed information:
*   **Gasket Identification Codes:** Unique codes for easy identification and selection of specific gasket types.
*   **Gasket Dimensions:** Precise dimensional requirements, including outer diameter, inner diameter, and thickness, to ensure proper fit with flanges.
*   **Gasket Materials:** Required material compositions (e.g., PTFE, graphite, spiral wound with specific filler materials) suitable for the process fluid and operating conditions.
*   **Chemetics Approved Manufacturer and Model Number:** A list of approved manufacturers and their specific model numbers that meet Chemetics' quality and performance standards.

**Roles and Responsibilities**
The preparation, checking, and approval of the Gasket Specification are assigned to specific disciplines:
*   **Preparation:** The document **shall** be prepared by the Mechanical discipline.
*   **Checking:** The document **shall** be checked by the Design discipline.
*   **Approval:** The document **shall** be approved by the Mechanical discipline.

This structured approach ensures that all gaskets are correctly specified and sourced from approved suppliers, contributing to the safety and operational reliability of the piping system.
