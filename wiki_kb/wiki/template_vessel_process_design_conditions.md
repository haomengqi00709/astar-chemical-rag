# Template Vessel Process Design Conditions (4-PDS-XXXX-R2)

The "PROCESS DESIGN CONDITIONS" section of the Template Vessel Process Data Sheet (4-PDS-XXXX-R2) is fundamental for defining the operational environment and requirements for the vessel. This section captures critical process parameters under various operating scenarios to ensure the vessel is designed for safe and efficient performance.

**Key Data Fields:**
This section **must** include the following descriptive and quantitative data:
*   **Case Description:** A brief description of the operating scenario (e.g., normal operation, startup, shutdown, upset).
*   **Design Basis:** The underlying principles or standards used for the design calculations.
*   **Contents:** A general description of the fluid(s) contained within the vessel.
*   **Major Components (%w/w):** The primary chemical constituents of the vessel's contents, expressed as weight percentages.
*   **Trace Components:** Any minor components that may impact material selection or process behavior.

**Quantitative Process Parameters:**
For each defined operating case (Maximum, Normal, Minimum), the following parameters **must** be specified:
*   **Temperature (oC):** The operating temperature in degrees Celsius.
*   **Pressure (kPa(g)):** The operating pressure in kilopascals gauge.
*   **Density (kg/m3):** The density of the fluid in kilograms per cubic meter.
*   **Viscosity (cP):** The dynamic viscosity of the fluid in centipoise.
*   **Specific Heat (kJ/kg·oC):** The specific heat capacity of the fluid in kilojoules per kilogram per degree Celsius.
*   **Thermal Conductivity (W/m·oC):** The thermal conductivity of the fluid in Watts per meter per degree Celsius.

These detailed process design conditions are essential for subsequent mechanical design, material selection, and safety analyses of the vessel.
