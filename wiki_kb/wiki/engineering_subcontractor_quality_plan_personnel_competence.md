# Engineering Subcontractor Quality Plan: Personnel Competence and Training

Ensuring the competence of personnel is a mandatory element of the [[engineering_subcontractor_quality_assurance_0_spc_7004|Engineering Subcontractor Quality Plan]]. The Subcontractor **must** ensure that all personnel assigned to perform services are competent.

**Competence Criteria:**
Personnel competence **shall** be based on a combination of:
*   **Education:** Relevant academic or vocational qualifications.
*   **Training:** Specific instruction and development activities.
*   **Skills:** Demonstrated abilities to perform tasks effectively.
*   **Experience:** Practical knowledge gained from previous work.

**Specific Training Requirements:**
In addition to general competence, the Subcontractor **must** provide any necessary specific training related to:
*   The requirements outlined in the Quality Plan itself.
*   The implementing documentation of the Quality Plan (e.g., specific procedures or instructions).

**Training Records:**
The Subcontractor **must** maintain records of all training provided to personnel. These records serve as evidence of competence and compliance with the Quality Plan's requirements.
