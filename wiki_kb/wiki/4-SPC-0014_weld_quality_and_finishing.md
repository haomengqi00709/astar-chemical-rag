# Weld Quality and Finishing for Stabilized Austenitic Stainless Steel (4-SPC-0014)

This page outlines the requirements for weld quality and finishing of Columbium Stabilized High Nickel Austenitic stainless steel fabrications, as specified in document 4-SPC-0014.

**Cleaning Between Weld Runs**
*   Each run of weld metal shall be carefully examined to ensure that it has been thoroughly cleaned.
*   All slag and foreign matter shall be removed before the next run is deposited.
*   Cleaning may be achieved by either grinding or chipping, followed by wire brushing.
*   On restarting, care shall be taken to ensure proper fusion with the weld metal previously deposited.

**Tungsten Pick-up**
*   If a tungsten electrode accidentally makes contact with the work, the section of weld and the tip of the electrode should be examined.
*   Where tungsten loss has occurred, the tungsten pick-up in the weld shall be ground out.
*   Where tungsten loss has occurred, the electrode shall be reground to the correct profile before restarting.

**Peening and Hammering**
Peening of weld runs and hammering of finished welds is not permitted, except for light peening to remove slag.

**Weld Appearance and Defects**
*   Each weld shall be uniform in width and size throughout its full length.
*   Each layer of welding shall be smooth and free of slag, inclusions, cracks, porosity, lack of fusion, and undercut, except to the extent permitted in the referenced codes.
*   The cover pass shall be free of coarse ripples, irregular surface, non-uniform bead patterns, high crown, and deep ridges or valleys between beads.
*   Buttwelds shall be slightly convex, of uniform height, and have full penetration.
*   Crevices shall not be permitted on the process side of the weld.
*   As far as is practical, fillet welds shall be deposited in not less than two layers.
*   Fillet weld surfaces shall be free from marked irregularities.
*   Dimensions of fillet welds shall be in accordance with the approved drawing.

For standard welding terms and definitions, refer to [[AWS_a3_0_standard_welding_terms_and_definitions]].

**Grinding and Tool Requirements**
*   Grinding or gouging shall be carried out in such a manner as not to reduce the adjacent base metal thickness.
*   Grinding wheels shall be iron-free.
*   Grinding wheels shall be of the resin bonded alumina or silica type.
*   Wire brushed or chipping tools shall be made of austenitic stainless steel.
*   Wire brushed or chipping tools must not have been previously used to clean surfaces other than stainless steels.
