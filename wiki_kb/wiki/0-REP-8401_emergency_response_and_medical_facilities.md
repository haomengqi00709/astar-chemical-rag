# Emergency Response and Medical Facilities (0-REP-8401)

This page details the essential emergency response protocols and medical support facilities required on site, as specified in document 0-REP-8401.

**Emergency Contact Information**
A wall-mounted list of emergency phone numbers is **required**. This list **must** include contact details (home and cell numbers) for home office personnel, as well as client contacts. The client **shall** also maintain a list of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics personnel for emergency contact, which **must** include home numbers. [[emergency_contacts_and_first_aid]]

**Site Emergency Planning and Evacuation**
A comprehensive site plan **shall** be in place to address emergencies necessitating a plant evacuation. [[site_emergency_response_plan]] The client **shall** implement a reliable method to alert personnel in the event of an emergency. All employees **shall** be aware of the Emergency signal and the appropriate actions to take during a site emergency.

A regular emergency drill **shall** be a component of the emergency plan, and the recommended frequency for these drills is **required**. A site wind sock is **required** and **shall** be visible from all plant exits to indicate wind direction during emergencies.

SOS International **shall** provide an emergency evacuation plan for the site. Each Aker Kvaerner Chemetics employee **shall** be aware of the site evacuation plan, particularly in scenarios involving medical emergencies or local political unrest. [[travel_health_and_safety_requirements]]

**On-Site Medical and First Aid**
A medical clinic or first aid clinic **shall** be available on site, or the location of the nearest facility **must** be identified. The hours of operation for this medical/first aid clinic are **required**. The clinic **shall** be staffed by trained personnel. Each Aker Kvaerner Chemetics employee and subcontractor **shall** know the location of the site first aid clinic.

The location of the nearest hospital, its distance from the site, and access routes are **required**. Procedures on how to seek medical attention are also **required**. Contact information for the site medical clinic or first aid clinic **shall** be supplied.

**First Aid Equipment**
A fully stocked first aid kit **shall** be maintained in the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site office. The site **shall** also be equipped with an Automated External Defibrillator (AED).

**Emergency Planning and Drills**
A comprehensive site plan **shall** be in place to manage emergencies requiring a plant evacuation. The client **shall** implement a clear method to alert personnel to an emergency. Regular emergency drills **shall** be a component of the emergency plan, and their recommended frequency is **required**. All employees **shall** be knowledgeable about the emergency signal and their actions during a site emergency. SOS International **shall** also maintain an emergency evacuation plan for the site. Furthermore, each Aker Kvaerner Chemetics employee **shall** be aware of the site evacuation plan for medical emergencies or local political unrest.

**Medical Facilities and Access**
A medical clinic or first aid clinic **shall** either be located on site or its nearest location clearly identified. The hours of operation for this clinic are **required**, and it **shall** be staffed by trained personnel. Each Aker Kvaerner Chemetics employee and subcontractor **shall** know the location of the site first aid clinic. The location, distance, and access routes to the nearest hospital are also **required**. Clear procedures on how to seek medical attention **shall** be provided, along with contact information for the site medical or first aid clinic.

**Emergency Contact Lists and Equipment**
Beyond the wall-mounted list, the client **shall** maintain a separate list of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics personnel for emergency contact, which **must** include home numbers. The Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site office **shall** be equipped with a fully stocked first aid kit. Additionally, the site **shall** have an Automated External Defibrillator (AED) readily available.
