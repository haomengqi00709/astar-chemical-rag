# Applicable Law and Compliance

This page defines "Applicable Law" and outlines general compliance requirements within the context of project documentation.

**Definition of Applicable Law**
Applicable Law refers to any binding law enacted, issued, or promulgated by any Government, government agency, or corporation established by government with jurisdiction over a specific Project or the Goods and Services being provided. This definition also encompasses any common law or law arising by custom or usage that is legally binding.

**Compliance Requirements**
All parties involved in a project, including Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, Vendors, and Suppliers, **must** ensure their activities, designs, and provision of goods and services are in full compliance with the Applicable Law. This includes, but is not limited to, adherence to health and safety regulations, environmental standards, and contractual obligations.

For specific project contexts, the Applicable Law may be further detailed within project-specific documentation, such as the [[0-SPC-5001_purchase_order_overview|Purchase Order (0-SPC-5001)]].
