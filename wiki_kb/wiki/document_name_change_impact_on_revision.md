# 2005 Name Change Conversion

A significant administrative event, referred to as the "2005 Name Change Conversion," involved a change in the firm's name. This event had specific implications for document revision control, as outlined in [[document_numbering_procedure_0_prc_0002]].

**Revision Numbering:**
Crucially, no new revision number was used for the 2005 Name Change Conversion. This means that documents affected by the name change did not receive an increment in their revision status solely due to this administrative update. The existing [[revision_numbering_rules]] were not applied for this specific type of change.

**Marking of Changes:**
Furthermore, the changes implemented as part of the 2005 Name Change Conversion have not been marked within the documents themselves. This implies that there are no specific revision clouds, change bars, or other indicators within the document content to highlight where the name change was applied.

This approach was taken to manage the widespread impact of a corporate name change without triggering a formal technical revision for every affected document. Users should be aware that documents predating or spanning this period may reflect the former company name without an explicit revision marker for the change.
