# Electrical Enclosure Installation Details

Electrical enclosure installation details provide specific instructions for installing and supporting boxes, cabinets, and panels, including methods for secure fastening and cable termination. These details ensure robust and accessible installations.

**Content of Enclosure Installation Details**
Installation details for boxes, cabinets, and panels **shall** show:
*   Methods of installing these items.
*   Methods of supporting these items.
*   Methods of securely fastening boxes, cabinets, and panels to floors.
*   Methods of securely fastening boxes, cabinets, and panels to walls.
*   Methods of securely fastening boxes, cabinets, and panels to building columns.
*   Methods of securely fastening boxes, cabinets, and panels on stand-alone support frames.
*   Locations of cables entering boxes, cabinets, and panels.
*   Methods of terminating cables at boxes, cabinets, and panels.

Boxes, cabinets, and panels **shall** be identified. Terminating items for boxes, cabinets, and panels **shall** also be identified.

**Project Specificity**
Details for boxes, cabinets, and panels **shall** be applicable to the project and not generic. They **shall** satisfy all necessary mounting and installation methods required for the project.
