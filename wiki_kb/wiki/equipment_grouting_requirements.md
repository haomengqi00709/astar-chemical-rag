# Equipment Grouting Requirements

This page outlines the specifications and procedures for [[equipment_grouting_requirements|grouting]] equipment bases, ensuring stability and proper installation. Proper grouting is a mandatory requirement for various equipment types during [[mechanical_equipment_checkout_procedure|mechanical checkout]], including [[vessel_and_tank_mechanical_checkout|vessels and tanks]], [[rotating_equipment_mechanical_checkout|rotating equipment]], [[heat_exchanger_mechanical_checkout|heat exchangers]], [[packaged_equipment_mechanical_checkout|packaged equipment]], [[separation_equipment_mechanical_checkout|separation equipment]], and [[conveying_equipment_mechanical_checkout|conveying equipment]]. It is critical that grouting does not obstruct sliding bases where applicable.
