# Business Travel Incident Reporting and Monitoring

A collective responsibility exists among key personnel to continuously monitor global safety and security alerts and to act decisively to protect travelling employees.

**Monitoring and Protective Actions:**
The HSE Manager, Director of Technology, Director of Projects, Travel Coordinator, Human Resources Manager, Project Managers, and Director of Sales **are responsible** for monitoring International SOS situation alerts. These individuals **are also responsible** for taking necessary actions to protect the Health and Safety of Company employees and contractors travelling on business. This includes responding to evolving risks and implementing additional safety measures as required.

**Incident Reporting and Investigation:**
All travel-related Health and Safety incidents **must** be reported to the HSE Manager. This includes injuries, illnesses, and any other incidents that occur during business travel. These incidents **shall** be investigated in accordance with the Aker Kvaerner Incident Follow-up Norm (Norm Number: 2.2 NO 2). The investigation process aims to identify root causes, implement corrective actions, and prevent recurrence, aligning with the principles outlined in the [[2.2_pr_2_incident_investigation_procedure|Incident Investigation Procedure]].

This rigorous approach to monitoring and incident management is a critical component of the [[0-prc-8102_travel_health_and_safety|Travel Health, Safety, and Security Overview]], ensuring that employee well-being is prioritized and lessons learned are integrated into future travel policies.
