# Construction Subcontract Indirect Work Costs

Indirect work comprises all work that does not become a permanent part of the project but is necessary for its orderly completion.

**Indirect Labour:**
*   The Subcontractor **must** complete the [[0-LST-6X14_construction_subcontract_indirect_labour_schedule]].
*   This schedule **must** detail the weeks on site for all site-based indirect personnel.
*   It **must** also show the weeks engaged directly on the Subcontract for all home office-based personnel.

Site-based indirect personnel include, but are not limited to:
*   All levels of supervision.
*   All administrative and support staff.
*   Clerical and janitorial workers.
*   Storemen and material handlers.
*   Installers / removers of field service facilities.
*   Erectors, operators, and maintainers of Construction Equipment.
*   First aid attendants.
*   Health and safety staff and workforce representatives.
*   Union shop stewards.

**Construction Equipment:**
*   The Subcontractor **must** complete the [[0-LST-6X15_construction_subcontract_equipment_schedule]].
*   This schedule **must** show the weeks on site for all items of Construction Equipment.
*   It **must** include all equipment used for the fabrication, erection, and installation of the Equipment and Materials, such as cranes, welding machines, and scaffolding.
*   It **must** also include field services items as listed on [[0-LST-6X90-2_construction_subcontract_field_services_scope]].

**Cost Entry and Calculation:**
*   The Subcontractor **must** enter indirect labour costs in the "Indirect Costs" worksheet within the [[0-LST-6X11_construction_subcontract_price_list]].
*   Indirect labour costs **must** be entered using a separate row for each position or classification identified on the [[0-LST-6X14_construction_subcontract_indirect_labour_schedule]].
*   The Subcontractor **must** show the man-hours of indirect labour required.
*   The Subcontractor **must** use the [[construction_subcontract_labour_rates]] (per Section 2 of 0-SPC-6011) or the [[construction_subcontract_crew_rates]] (per Section 3.1 of 0-SPC-6011) as applicable to calculate the total cost of indirect labour.
*   Data for erectors, operators, and maintainers of Construction Equipment **must** be combined on the same rows as for the Construction Equipment.

**Other Indirect Costs:**
The Subcontractor **must** enter the following costs in the "Indirect Costs" worksheet in [[0-LST-6X11_construction_subcontract_price_list]]:
*   Costs for mobilisation / demobilisation.
*   Costs for rental of Construction Equipment.
*   Costs for fuel for Construction Equipment.
*   Costs for maintenance of Construction Equipment.
*   Construction Equipment costs **must** be entered using a separate row for each item identified on the [[0-LST-6X15_construction_subcontract_equipment_schedule]].
