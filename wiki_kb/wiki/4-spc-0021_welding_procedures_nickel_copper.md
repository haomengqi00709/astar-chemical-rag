# Nickel/Copper Welding Procedures and Consumables (4-SPC-0021)

The welding procedures and consumables for nickel/copper (UNS N04400 / Monel 400) fabrications under **4-SPC-0021** are subject to stringent requirements to ensure quality and compliance.

**Welding Procedure Qualification and Submission:**
*   All welding procedures **shall** be submitted by the fabricator for review and agreement by the [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_company_details|Aker Kvaerner Chemetics]] QA & Materials Group prior to the commencement of work.
*   The [[ASME_section_ix_welding_and_brazing_qualifications|ASME IX]] PQR Form 483 **shall** form part of the welding procedure submittal package.
*   A change in essential variables, as outlined in [[ASME_section_ix_welding_and_brazing_qualifications|ASME Code, Section IX]], will require re-submission of the affected procedure(s).
*   Welding procedures **shall** be agreed upon (refer to Clause 5.2 in the source document) before any production welding is begun.

**Welding Consumables:**
*   All weld filler metals **shall** meet the requirements of the [[ASME_section_ix_welding_and_brazing_qualifications|ASME Code]].
*   Nickel/copper materials **shall** be welded with coated electrode #190 and/or filler wire #60 supplied by International Nickel Co.
*   Fabricators **shall** ensure and demonstrate to the Inspector that manufacturers' instructions for the storage, drying, and use of weld consumables are fully implemented.
*   All wet electrodes **must** be discarded and **not** baked and re-used.

**Preferred Welding Methods and Parameters:**
Preferred welding methods for unalloyed nickel materials include:
*   Shielded Metal Arc Welding (SMAW)
*   Gas Tungsten Arc Welding (GTAW)
*   Gas Metal Arc Welding (GMAW)
*   Submerged Arc Welding (SAW)

Other welding processes will be evaluated upon submission of full details, including the procedure and exact location of proposed weldments.

**Specific Requirements for Welding Processes:**
*   **SMAW:**
    *   Coated electrodes **shall** satisfy [[ASME_sfa_5_11_nickel_and_nickel_alloy_electrodes|SFA-5.11 Class ENiCu7]].
    *   Minimum practical amperage and minimum arc length are recommended.
    *   Stringer beads, rather than weaving, are generally desirable.
*   **GTAW:**
    *   Rods **shall** satisfy [[ASME_sfa_5_14_nickel_and_nickel_alloy_electrodes_and_rods|SFA-A5.14 Class ER NiCu7]].
    *   The preferred electrode is thoriated tungsten as per [[ASME_sfa_5_12_tungsten_arc_welding_electrodes|SFA-A5.12, Class EWTh-2]].
    *   High frequency (HF) voltage **shall** be used to start the arc.
    *   Weld metal thickness **shall not** exceed 1/8" per pass.
    *   Heat input **shall** be kept low by using the stringer bead technique.
    *   An inert backing gas **shall** be used where necessary to prevent root pass oxidation and contamination.
*   **GMAW:**
    *   **Shall** only be used for the root pass of butt welds and for fillet welds unless specifically agreed otherwise.
    *   The fabricator **must** provide satisfactory evidence of continuous shop use of GMAW in nickel component fabrication.
*   **SAW:**
    *   Use of Submerged Arc Welding **must** be reviewed and approved in writing by [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_company_details|Aker Kvaerner Chemetics]] prior to the start of fabrication.
    *   A chemistry check of the finished deposit **must** be included as part of the SAW weld procedure qualification.
    *   An acceptable SAW wire/flux combination is Inco filler metal #60 and Incoflux #5.
    *   The fabricator **must** provide satisfactory evidence of continuous shop use of SAW in nickel material fabrication.

**Preheat and Postweld Heat Treatment (PWHT):**
*   Preheat is not required.
*   When adverse weather conditions cause condensation or wetness, the material **shall** be dried prior to welding by careful heating, just sufficient to prevent condensation.
*   Heat input during welding **should** be kept to a minimum.
*   Interpass temperature **should not** exceed 200F.
*   PWHT is seldom required. If PWHT is necessary, the fabricator **shall** submit a written procedure for approval prior to commencement of work. The PWHT procedure **shall** include information on the number and location of thermocouples and supports.
