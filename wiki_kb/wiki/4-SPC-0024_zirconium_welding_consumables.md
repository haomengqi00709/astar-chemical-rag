# Zirconium Welding Consumables (4-SPC-0024)

This page details the mandatory requirements for welding consumables used in Zirconium (Grade 60702) fabrications, as specified in document **4-SPC-0024**.

**General Requirements:**
*   All weld filler metals or consumables **must** meet the requirements of Section 8 of this Standard.
*   Mill Test Certification **must** be supplied with all materials, including welding consumables.
*   Autogenous welding (i.e., welding with no filler metal) **must not** be used.
*   Prior to the commencement of welding, the fabricator **must** furnish the brand name of the filler materials to be used.

**Storage and Handling:**
*   Filler materials **should** be kept warm, dry, and clean.
*   If dirty, wire **must** be wiped clean with a non-chlorinated solvent such as acetone or alcohol.
*   Only clean cellulose sponges or lint-free cloth **must** be used to apply solvents.
*   Fabricators **must** ensure and demonstrate to the Inspector that manufacturer's instructions regarding the storage, drying, and use of weld consumables are fully implemented. This applies both in the shop and on site.
*   All wet electrodes **must** be discarded and **must not** be baked and re-used.

**Specific Filler Metal Specifications:**
*   Gas tungsten arc welding rods **must** satisfy the requirements of [[ASME_sfa_5_24_zirconium_and_zirconium_alloy_bare_welding_rods_and_electrodes|ASME Boiler & Pressure Vessel Codes SFA-5.24]].
*   Gas tungsten arc welding rods **must** fall within the F-61 Filler Metal Group Classification.
*   The preferred electrode is thoriated tungsten as per [[ASME_sfa_5_12_tungsten_arc_welding_electrodes|SFA-5.12]], Class EWTh-2.
