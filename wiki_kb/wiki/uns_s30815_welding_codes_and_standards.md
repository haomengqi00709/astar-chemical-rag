# Welding Codes and Standards for UNS S30815 Fabrications

This document references several industry codes and standards that **MUST** be adhered to for the welding and fabrication of UNS S30815 materials. Compliance with the latest edition of these codes and standards, ruling at the date of contract, is mandatory.

**Referenced Codes and Standards:**

*   **[[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping]]**: This code is referenced for the fabrication of Chemical Plant & Petroleum Refinery Piping.
*   **[[ASME_bpvc_section_ii_materials]]**: This section of the ASME Boiler & Pressure Vessel Code provides requirements for Materials.
*   **[[ASME_sfa_5_4_welding_electrodes]]**: This specification covers Covered Corrosion Resisting Chromium and Chromium-Nickel Steel Covered Welding Electrodes.
*   **[[ASME_sfa_5_9_welding_electrodes_and_rods]]**: This specification covers Corrosion Resisting Chromium and Chromium-Nickel Steel Bare and Composite Metal Cored and Stranded Arc-Welding Electrodes and Welding Rods.
*   **[[ASME_sfa_5_12_tungsten_arc_welding_electrodes]]**: This specification details requirements for Tungsten Arc-Welding Electrodes.
*   **[[ASME_section_v_non_destructive_examination]]**: This section of the ASME Boiler & Pressure Vessel Code outlines requirements for Non-destructive Examination.
*   **[[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels]]**: This section of the ASME Boiler & Pressure Vessel Code is referenced for Unfired Pressure Vessels.
*   **[[ASME_section_ix_welding_and_brazing_qualifications]]**: This section of the ASME Boiler & Pressure Vessel Code provides rules for Welding and Brazing Qualification.
*   **[[AWS_a3_0_standard_welding_terms_and_definitions]]**: This standard provides definitions for Standard Welding Terms & Definitions.

These codes and standards form the foundational requirements for all aspects of material selection, welding procedures, welder qualifications, and inspection processes detailed in this specification.

**Specific Code Applications:**
*   ASME B31.3 is referenced for Chemical Plant & Petroleum Refinery Piping.
*   ASME Boiler & Pressure Vessel Codes Section II is referenced for Materials.
*   ASME SFA-5.4 is referenced for Specification for Covered Corrosion Resisting Chromium and Chromium-Nickel Steel Covered Welding Electrodes.
*   ASME SFA-5.9 is referenced for Specification for Corrosion Resisting Chromium and Chromium-Nickel Steel Bare and Composite Metal Cored and Stranded Arc-Welding Electrodes and Welding Rods.
*   ASME SFA-5.12 is referenced for Specification for Tungsten Arc-Welding Electrodes.
*   ASME Boiler & Pressure Vessel Codes Section V is referenced for Non-destructive Examination.
*   ASME Boiler & Pressure Vessel Codes Section VIII Div. 1 is referenced for Unfired Pressure Vessels.
*   ASME Boiler & Pressure Vessel Codes Section IX is referenced for Welding and Brazing Qualification.
*   AWS A3.0 is referenced for Standard Welding Terms & Definitions.
