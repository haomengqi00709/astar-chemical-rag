# Shell & Tube Heat Exchanger Packing and Shipping Requirements (0-QDS-7417)

For the Shell & Tube Heat Exchanger specified in 0-QDS-7417, specific requirements are outlined for packing and shipping to ensure the safe and undamaged delivery of the equipment to the project site. Proper packing and documentation are essential to protect the heat exchanger during transit and facilitate efficient receiving.

**Packing/Crating (Item 31.0):**
This item covers the general requirements for how the Shell & Tube Heat Exchanger and its associated components **must** be prepared for transportation. The packing methods **must** be suitable for the mode of transport (e.g., land, sea, air) and the expected environmental conditions during transit, providing adequate protection against physical damage, corrosion, and contamination.

**Specific Packing and Documentation Items:**

*   **Packaging (Item 32.0):**
    *   **Surveillance by Chemetics:** Complete
    *   The actual packaging of the heat exchanger **must** be inspected to ensure it meets the specified standards for protection. This includes verifying the use of appropriate crating, bracing, weatherproofing, and corrosion protection measures. Chemetics will perform a complete review of the packaging methods and their execution.

*   **Packing List (Item 33.0):**
    *   **Quality Record Required:** AP ("As Prepared")
    *   **Surveillance by Chemetics:** Complete
    *   A detailed packing list **must** be prepared by the Vendor. This document **shall** accurately enumerate all items included in the shipment, including the main heat exchanger, spare parts, loose components, and any special tools. The packing list is a critical document for customs clearance, receiving, and inventory management at the project site. It **must** be provided "As Prepared" and will undergo a complete review by Chemetics.

These packing and shipping requirements are the final steps in the [[0-QDS-7417_vendor_quality_assurance_requirements|Vendor Quality Assurance process]] before the equipment leaves the manufacturing facility. Adherence to these requirements helps prevent delays and damage, contributing to the overall success of the project. For definitions of quality record designations, refer to [[0-QDS-7417_quality_record_definitions]].
