# Eye and Face Protection Requirements

This page outlines the mandatory requirements for eye and face protection in various work environments, including construction sites, fabrication facilities, and specific hazardous tasks, as specified in Document ID 0-PRC-8015.

**General Eye Protection**
Protective eyewear **is required** on all construction sites. Protective eyewear **is also required** on all fabrication facilities. In other locations, employees **will** be notified of the requirement for protective eyewear at the site-specific [[hse_orientation_overview|health and safety orientation]].

Standard eye protection for Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **is** ANSI or CSA approved industrial safety glasses with side shields. All protective eyewear worn **must** meet CSA Z94.3 standard (or the equivalent ANSI Z87 standard).

**Maintenance and Restrictions**
It **is imperative** to maintain all protective eyewear with frequent cleaning. Any scratched or damaged safety glasses/goggles **must** be replaced. Contact lenses **are not** to be worn in the shop, laboratory setting, construction or maintenance sites, even when covered with approved goggles. Tinted glasses **are not** to be worn inside buildings or at night.

**Prescription Eyewear**
Employees who wear prescription glasses **must** have eye protection that meets the above standards and includes side shields. Employees may add side shields to present glasses if the glasses with side shields are ANSI or CSA approved. Side shields **will** be of the type approved for the frames, or of universal fit, and **must** be securely fastened to the frame. Employees may choose to wear ANSI or CSA approved mono-goggles that can be worn over non-approved prescription eyewear without disturbing the adjustment of the prescription eyewear. Approved chemical mono-goggles **will** be provided to ensure protection from hazards associated with handling or dispensing liquid chemicals.

**Face Shields**
A full face shield may be worn with goggles. A full face shield **may be required** for certain tasks. A CSA or ANSI approved full face shield **will** be worn to provide face protection to the employee from flying particles, splashes or mist. A face shield provides only limited protection to the face and eyes from direct impact objects. Additional eye protection **must** be worn in conjunction with a face shield (i.e., mono-goggles).

**Tasks Requiring Face Shields (with Goggles underneath)**
Tasks that dictate the use of a face shield include:
*   Blowing
*   Chipping
*   Grinding (especially overhead or in an enclosed/confined space, with abrasive stones/wheels, or using power wire brushes)
*   Hot tar work
*   Liquid metal work
*   Pneumatic tool use
*   Pouring or handling of irritants
*   Sawing metals or masonry
*   Scraping
*   Handling wet cell batteries
*   Handling caustics, acids, and other chemicals where spill or splash hazards exist (chemical goggles required)
*   Operating masonry saws
*   Operating abrasive cut-off saws (gasoline or electric)
*   Using powder actuated tools
*   Operating pneumatic impact wrenches
*   Any operation which subjects the eyes or head to flying particles.

For assistance in eye protection selection, contact the site [[hse_roles_and_responsibilities_company_wide|HSE Manager]].

**Welding and Cutting Eye Protection**
*   Burning goggles with at least a number 6 shade of lens **will** be worn while performing oxy-acetylene, cutting, and welding.
*   A CSA or ANSI approved welding hood with the proper filter lens and an approved hard hat **will** be worn while performing arc-welding.
*   A welding hood with a filtered lens of number 10 shade or darker **will** be used to provide protection from ultra-violet rays produced during electric arc welding.
*   Approved safety glasses with side shields **shall** be worn in conjunction with the welding hood to ensure protection from popping hot slag when the hood is raised. A welding hood with a "flip-up" window and secondary clear lens is recommended.

**Exemptions**
Eyewear **is not required** while working in offices, office trailers, washrooms, or change rooms. Eyewear **is not required** while inside a motor vehicle, or enclosed equipment cab. When sighting through transits, levels or similar instruments, employees may remove safety glasses temporarily for the duration of the sighting.

For general PPE guidelines, refer to [[0-prc-8015_personal_protective_equipment_policy]].
