# Commercial Terms for Purchase (0-PRC-5004)

This document, 0-PRC-5004, outlines the commercial terms and conditions applicable to all purchases for the Project. It ensures consistency in contractual agreements with vendors.

Planning of procurement is undertaken in accordance with this procedure. Requisitions, Request for Quotation, and Purchase Orders will be issued in accordance with this procedure.
