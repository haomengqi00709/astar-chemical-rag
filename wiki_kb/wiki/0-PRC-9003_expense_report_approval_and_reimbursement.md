# Expense Report Approval and Reimbursement (0-PRC-9003)

Upon submission, the Finance Department **shall** check the Expense Report for accuracy and compliance with this procedure and the [[travel_policy_compliance_and_deviations|Group Travel Policy]].

**Discrepancy Resolution**
Any discrepancies identified by Finance will be highlighted for action by the designated approver. The approver **must** discuss and agree upon the treatment of these discrepancies with the employee.

**Approval Authority**
Expense Reports **must** be approved by the person responsible for the budget being charged.
Expenditure for items intended to be retained for future company use (e.g., cameras, library books) requires additional approval:
*   For items valued at less than CAD 500, the employee's senior line manager (Director or equivalent) **must** approve.
*   For items valued at more than CAD 500, the Vice President Finance or the President **must** approve.

**Reimbursement Process**
The employee will be reimbursed in Canadian Dollars (CAD) for all approved expenses. Reimbursement will be issued either by check or by electronic funds transfer directly into the employee’s bank account.

If the reimbursed amount differs from the Expense Report originally submitted, Finance **shall** provide the employee with a marked-up copy of the Expense Report, clearly indicating the changes made.
