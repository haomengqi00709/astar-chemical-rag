# Agitator Material and Compliance Certifications

For agitator equipment covered by Quality Data Sheet **0-QDS-7418**, specific material and compliance certifications are required:

*   **Mill Test Certificates:** These certificates **are required** as [[0-QDS-7418_agitator_quality_record_definitions|FP]] (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package). Chemetics **must** witness Mill Test Certificates if the material is high alloy.
*   **Certificate of Compliance:** This certificate **is required** as [[0-QDS-7418_agitator_quality_record_definitions|FP]] (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package). Chemetics **must** witness the Certificate of Compliance.

These certifications ensure that the materials used and the final product comply with specified standards and requirements.

For general information on material verification and certifications, refer to [[material_verification_and_certifications]].
