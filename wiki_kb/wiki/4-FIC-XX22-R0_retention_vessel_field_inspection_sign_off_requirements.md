# Retention Vessel Field Inspection Sign-off Requirements

The completion of the Retention Vessel Field Inspection Checklist (document 4-FIC-XX22-R0) requires formal sign-off from key project stakeholders. This process ensures that all inspection items have been thoroughly addressed, and any identified deficiencies or comments have been duly noted and acknowledged.

**Mandatory Signatures:**
To validate the inspection and its findings, the following signatures are **required**:
*   Name, Signature, and Date are **required** from Aker Kvaerner Chemetics personnel.
*   Name, Signature, and Date are **required** from the Sub Contractor.
*   Name, Signature, and Date are **required** from the Client.

This checklist provides 10 dedicated slots for recording any deficiencies and comments identified during the inspection process, ensuring a comprehensive record of the vessel's condition. All such records contribute to the overall [[quality_record_definitions]] for the project.

For general administrative details of this checklist, refer to [[4-FIC-XX22-R0_retention_vessel_field_inspection_checklist_overview]].
The specific inspection items that require verification are detailed in [[4-FIC-XX22-R0_retention_vessel_field_inspection_items]].
