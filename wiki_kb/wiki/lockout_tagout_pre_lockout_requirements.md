# Lockout and Tagout Pre-Lockout Requirements

Before initiating any lockout/tagout procedures, specific preliminary steps **must** be completed to ensure safety and proper coordination. These requirements are integral to the overall [[9-prc-0019_lockout_tagout_procedure_overview|Lockout and Tagout Procedure (9-PRC-0019)]].

### Job Authorisation

*   Before lockout commences, job authorisation **shall** be obtained. This ensures that the work is approved and planned, and that all necessary permits and approvals are in place.

### Notification of Affected Individuals

*   Notify [[lockout_tagout_roles_and_responsibilities|affected individuals]] that a lockout or tagout system is going to be utilised. This notification is crucial for preventing unexpected equipment shutdowns and ensuring that all personnel who might be impacted are aware of the impending procedure.

These steps are crucial for ensuring proper coordination and safety before initiating any lockout/tagout procedures.

*   Individuals authorised to perform lockout must be certain as to which switch, valve, or other energy isolating devices applies to the equipment being locked out.
*   More than one energy source (electrical, mechanical or others) may be involved and will be confirmed with the supervisor.
