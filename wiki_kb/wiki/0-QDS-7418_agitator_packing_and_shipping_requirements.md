# Agitator Packing and Shipping Requirements

For agitator equipment covered by Quality Data Sheet **0-QDS-7418**, specific requirements apply to packing and shipping documentation:

*   **Packing List:** The Packing List **is required** as [[0-QDS-7418_agitator_quality_record_definitions|AP]] (As Prepared). Chemetics **must** witness the Packing List.

This ensures that the contents are accurately documented and verified before shipment.

For general guidelines on packaging and transportation, refer to [[packaging_land_transportation_0_spc_5004]] and [[packaging_marine_transportation_0_spc_5005]].
