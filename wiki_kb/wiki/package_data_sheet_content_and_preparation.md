# Package Data Sheets

Package Data Sheets are essential documents for defining the technical requirements of process packages, which are collections of equipment designed to perform a singular overall process duty (Type B Equipment). These sheets consolidate critical design and specification information for vendors.

**Content Elements**
Package Data Sheets **must** contain the following information:

*   **Design Conditions:** All relevant process and environmental conditions under which the package is designed to operate.
*   **Applicable Design Codes and Standards:** References to all national and international codes and standards that the package design and fabrication **must** comply with.
*   **Applicable Specifications:** References to all relevant project-specific or Aker Kvaerner Chemetics specifications.
*   **Description of Protection against Over Pressure/Temperature:** Details of safety devices or design features implemented within the package to protect against overpressure or overtemperature conditions.

**Document Preparation and Approval**
The preparation, checking, and approval of Package Data Sheets follow a defined workflow:

*   **Preparation:** Package Data Sheets **must** be prepared by Mechanical personnel.
*   **Checking:** The documents **must** be checked by Process personnel.
*   **Approval:** Final approval **must** be granted by the Project Engineer.

**Revision History**
Package Data Sheets (PKG) were formally added to the 4-PRC-0003 procedure in Revision 1, signifying their importance in the documentation process for complex equipment packages.

**Responsibilities**
*   **Prepared by:** Mechanical
*   **Checked by:** Process
*   **Approved by:** Project Engineer
