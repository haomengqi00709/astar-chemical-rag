# Vendor Document Revision Standards

Maintaining accurate and clearly marked revisions is a mandatory requirement for all Vendor-supplied documents. Adherence to these standards ensures that Aker Kvaerner Chemetics can easily identify changes and manage document versions effectively.

**Revision Marking:**
*   For drawings, the Vendor **shall** highlight revisions by "clouding" the changed areas.
*   For textual documents, the Vendor **shall** highlight revisions by marking the changed text with a vertical line in the left-hand margin.

**Revision Process:**
*   Following any revision, the Vendor **shall** increment their own internal revision number for the document.
*   If applicable, the Vendor **shall** update the document's [[document_status_definitions|status]] to reflect the nature of the revision (e.g., from "Issued for Review" to "Approved for Fabrication").
*   After incorporating revisions and updating the status, the Vendor **shall** re-issue the document to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

These procedures are critical for maintaining a clear audit trail of document changes and ensuring that all project stakeholders are working with the most current and accurate information.
