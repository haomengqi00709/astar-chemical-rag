# Strainer Hydrostatic Testing and Final Inspection (0-QDS-7402)

The final stages of quality assurance for the Strainer (0-QDS-7402) involve critical hydrostatic testing and a series of comprehensive visual and dimensional inspections, culminating in the preparation for shipping. These activities ensure the Strainer's operational integrity, adherence to specifications, and readiness for delivery.

**Hydrostatic Testing:**
Hydrostatic testing is a crucial procedure to verify the pressure integrity of the Strainer.
*   **Quality Record:** Hydrostatic test is required as FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package).
*   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Chemetics surveillance for Hydrostatic test is "Complete," ensuring full oversight of the testing process and results.
*   **Quality Table Activity:** Pressure/Leak Testing is identified as an item or activity in the Quality table, underscoring its importance in the overall quality framework.
For general information on pressure measurement, refer to [[pressure_measurement_general]].

**Dimensional and Visual Inspection:**
A series of inspections are conducted to verify the physical attributes and surface quality of the Strainer.
*   **Dimensional Inspection:**
    *   **Quality Record:** Dimensional inspection is required as FP.
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Chemetics surveillance for Dimensional inspection is "Witness" and "Hold," allowing for direct observation and intervention if necessary.
*   **Contour & Finish of Welds Inspection:**
    *   **Quality Record:** Contour & finish of welds inspection is required as FP.
    *   **Extent:** This inspection is "Spot," focusing on selected areas.
    *   **Value:** The inspection has a value of 0.25.
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Chemetics surveillance for Contour & finish of welds is "Witness" and "Hold."
*   **Surfaces Free from Defects:**
    *   **Values:** This inspection has associated values of 1.0, 1.0.
    *   **Quality Record:** Quality Record is not required for Surfaces free from defects.
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Chemetics surveillance for Surfaces free from defects is "Witness" and "Hold" (does not include "Complete").
*   **Cleanliness:**
    *   **Quality Record:** Quality Record is not required for Cleanliness.
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Chemetics surveillance for Cleanliness is "Witness" and "Hold" (does not include "Complete").

**Documentation Review and Preparation for Shipping:**
The final steps involve reviewing documentation and preparing the Strainer for shipment.
*   **Review QA Documentation:**
    *   **Quality Record:** Quality Record is not required for Review QA Documentation.
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Chemetics surveillance for Review QA Documentation is "Witness" and "Hold" (does not include "Complete").
*   **Preparation for Shipping:**
    *   **Quality Record:** Quality Record is not required for Preparation for shipping.
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Chemetics surveillance for Preparation for shipping is "Witness" (does not include "Hold" or "Complete").
*   **Quality Table Activities:**
    *   Packing/Crating is an item or activity in the Quality table.
    *   Final Inspection is an item or activity in the Quality table.
    *   Material Verification is also an item or activity in the Quality table.

These comprehensive checks ensure that the Strainer is fully compliant with all specifications and ready for safe and efficient transport. For details on packaging, refer to [[packaging_land_transportation_0_spc_5004]] and [[packaging_marine_transportation_0_spc_5005]]. General information on final inspection processes can be found in [[final_inspection]] and [[inspection_and_surveillance_activities]].

Chemetics surveillance for the **Hydrostatic test** is "Complete".
Chemetics surveillance for **Mill Test Certificates** is "Complete".
Chemetics surveillance for **Weld consumable certs.** is "Complete".

For **Dimensional inspection**, Chemetics surveillance includes "Witness" and "Hold" points.

**Contour & finish of welds inspection** is specified as "Spot" with a value of 0.25. Chemetics surveillance for this inspection includes "Witness" and "Hold" points.

For **Surfaces free from defects**, the associated values are 1.0, 1.0. Chemetics surveillance includes "Witness" and "Hold" points, but explicitly **does not include "Complete"**.

For **Cleanliness**, Chemetics surveillance includes "Witness" and "Hold" points, but explicitly **does not include "Complete"**.

For **Review QA Documentation**, Chemetics surveillance includes "Witness" and "Hold" points, but explicitly **does not include "Complete"**.

For **Preparation for shipping**, Chemetics surveillance is "Witness". It explicitly **does not include "Hold" or "Complete"**.

The **Packing List** is subject to "Complete" surveillance by Chemetics, but explicitly **does not include "Witness" or "Hold"**.

Both **Final Inspection** and **Packing/Crating** are identified as items or activities within the Quality table.
