# Electrical Document Structure (Major Work Subject 9)

The "9 Electrical" major work subject is dedicated to all documentation concerning the design, specification, installation, and operation of the plant's electrical systems. This folder is critical for managing information related to power distribution, lighting, grounding, and electrical equipment.

The sub-folders and their contents within the 9-Electrical folder are:

*   **CAL:** Short Circuit Studies, Harmonics Studies. Calculations and analyses for electrical system performance and safety.
*   **DST:** Electrical Equipment Data Sheets. Detailed technical data sheets for individual electrical equipment. For more information, refer to [[electrical_equipment_data_sheets]].
*   **DTL:** Electrical Installation Details. Detailed drawings for electrical equipment installation, wiring, and connections.
*   **GAD:** Electrical Location Drawings, Tray Layout, Grounding and Lighting Plans. Drawings showing the physical location of electrical equipment, cable tray routes, grounding systems, and lighting layouts.
*   **LST:** Electrical Equipment List, Cable Lists, Spare Parts Lists. Comprehensive lists of all electrical equipment, power and control cables, and associated spare parts. Data available in SQL: LST files.
*   **PRC:** Project Specific Procedures. Procedures tailored to electrical system design, procurement, and installation.
*   **REQ:** Requisitions. Documents for requesting bids or proposals for electrical equipment and services.
*   **SCM:** Single Line Diagrams, Wiring Schematics. Diagrams representing the overall electrical power distribution and detailed wiring connections. For single line diagrams, refer to [[final_single_line_diagrams]].
*   **SPC:** Specifications. Technical specifications for electrical equipment, materials, installation standards, and testing. Data available in SQL: SPC files.
*   **TAB:** Bid Tabulations. Records of bids received for electrical equipment and services.
*   **VDR:** Vendor Document Requirements. Documents outlining the requirements for vendor-supplied electrical documentation.
*   **VEN:** Vendors Documents. All documentation provided by electrical equipment vendors, such as manuals, drawings, and test reports.

This structure ensures that all electrical documents, from design studies to vendor submissions, are systematically organized and easily retrievable. For an overview of the entire project documentation structure, refer to [[document_control_1_prc_0003]]. The definitions of major work subjects are outlined in [[major_work_division_codes]].
