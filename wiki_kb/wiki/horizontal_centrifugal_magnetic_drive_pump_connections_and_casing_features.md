# Horizontal Centrifugal Magnetic Drive Pump Connections and Casing Features

For horizontal centrifugal magnetic drive pumps, the pump suction and discharge connections **will** be flat faced flanged. These connections **will** conform to the DIN PN10 standard. For general information on flange bolting, refer to [[flange_bolting_requirements]].

A casing drain **will** be provided, equipped with an NPT plug.

The pumps **will** be designed for back pull out, facilitating maintenance without disturbing piping connections. Additionally, the pumps **will** be designed for close-coupled assembly with a flange-mounted motor.

A casing drain **will** be provided with an NPT plug.
