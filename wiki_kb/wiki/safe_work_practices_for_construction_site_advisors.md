# Safe Work Practices for Construction Site Advisors

Aker Kvaerner Chemetics construction site advisors **must** uphold rigorous safe work practices to ensure a secure working environment for themselves and others on Site.

**Site Familiarity and Compliance**
Advisors **must** be thoroughly familiar with specific site Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) issues, including the locations of first aid facilities, ambulance availability, and [[evacuation_procedures_akctc]]. They **must** also be familiar with all HSE procedures and specifications applicable to their activities on Site. If an advisor is not familiar with these applicable procedures and specifications, they **must** obtain the necessary training as outlined in [[hse_training_and_orientation_for_construction_site_advisors]]. Advisors **shall** attend the owner's safety meetings if specifically requested by the owner.

**Incident Reporting**
As soon as practical after an occurrence, advisors **must** report any HSE incident that takes place on Site to both the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Vancouver Safety Manager and the Manager of Construction and Estimating. This ensures prompt investigation and corrective action. For detailed procedures on incident reporting, refer to [[hse_incident_reporting]].

**Job Analysis for Hazardous Duties**
Prior to undertaking any potentially hazardous duty, the advisor **must** ensure that a [[job_hazard_analysis]] (JA) report has been completed and thoroughly reviewed. This proactive step is crucial for identifying and mitigating risks associated with specific tasks. For more information on the JHA process, see [[job_hazard_analysis_process_steps]].

**Motor Vehicle Operation**
If site conditions require advisors to operate a motor vehicle, such as an automobile, truck, or scooter, they **must** be properly trained and licensed as applicable for the specific vehicle type. Additionally, advisors **must** wear proper [[personal_protective_equipment_for_construction_site_advisors|PPE]] while operating these vehicles. For general guidelines on vehicle safety, refer to [[travel_vehicle_use_and_driving_safety]].

Advisors **must** be thoroughly familiar with specific site Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) issues such as first aid locations, ambulance availability, and evacuation procedures.

**Meetings and Incident Reporting**
Advisors will attend the owners’ safety meetings if specifically requested by the owner. As soon as practical, advisors will report any HSE incident that occurs on Site to both the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Vancouver Safety Manager and the Manager of Construction and Estimating.

**Hazardous Duties and Job Analysis**
Prior to undertaking any potentially hazardous duty, the advisor will ensure that a job analysis (JA) report has been completed and reviewed.

**Motor Vehicle Operation**
If site conditions require advisors to operate a motor vehicle (such as an automobile, truck, or scooter), advisors will be properly trained and licensed as applicable to operate the vehicle. Advisors will also wear proper PPE while operating vehicles.

**General Safety Principles**
Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) is considered priority above all. No job will be performed without proper HSE plans in place.

**Uncertainty and Refusal to Work**
If unsure about the status of a particular task, advisors will ask a qualified person. If uncertainty persists, advisors will refuse to perform the task until the uncertainty is removed.
