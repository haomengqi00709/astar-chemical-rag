# HazOp Study Special Cases: Utility Subsystems and Identical Plant Parts

Specific guidelines apply to the Hazard and Operability (HazOp) Study of utility subsystems (packages) not designed by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, and for parts of the plant that are identical to previously studied sections.

**Utility Subsystems (Packages):**
*   Portions of utility subsystems (packages) not designed by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics will be studied as a single part.
*   Details of the internals of these packages will not be studied during the HazOp.

**Leveraging Previous HazOp Studies:**
*   Parts of the plant that are identical in form (though not necessarily in size) and have an identical design intent to previously HazOp-studied parts can utilize information from those prior studies.
*   If previous study information is used, relevant sections of specific published documents from the previous HazOp Study will be added as a subfolder within the record of the present HazOp Study. This subfolder will be located in `P:\1-Process-Technology\Haz`.

**Documents to be Added from Previous HazOp Studies:**
The following documents from previous HazOp Studies **shall** be included:
*   1-DST-0001: [[site_design_conditions_1_dst_0001]]
*   1-LST-0001: [[battery_limits_conditions_1_lst_0001]]
*   1-PID-XXXX: [[piping_and_instrumentation_drawings]] (Approved for Design (HazOp))
*   1-PID-XXXX: [[piping_and_instrumentation_drawings]] (As Built)
*   1-PRC-0001: [[process_design_criteria_1_prc_0001]]
*   1-HAZ-0001: [[1-haz-0001_hazop_record_overview|HazOp Record]]
*   1-HAZ-0002: [[1-haz-0002_hazop_responses_overview|HazOp Response]]
*   8-SPC-0020: [[logic_narrative]]

**Confirmation of Identity:**
*   The HazOp Leader and the Technology Manager **shall** be responsible for confirming that the part of the plant is identical in form and design content to the previously studied plant.
*   This confirmation **must** be explicitly noted on the [[1-haz-0001_hazop_record_overview|HazOp Record]].

**Review of Previous Study Information:**
*   Relevant sections of the HazOp Record and HazOp Response from previous studies **shall** be reviewed during the current HazOp session.
*   This review aims to determine whether any questions from previous studies need to be recorded in the current HazOp.

*   The subfolder for previous HazOp Study documents will be located in `P:\1-Process-Technology\Haz`.
