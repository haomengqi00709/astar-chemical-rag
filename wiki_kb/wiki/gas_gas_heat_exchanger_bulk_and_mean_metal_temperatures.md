# Gas/Gas Heat Exchanger Bulk Fluid and Mean Metal Temperatures

This page details the calculated bulk fluid and mean metal temperatures for various components of the internal gas/gas heat exchanger, as documented in [[4-CAL-0008_document_details|4-CAL-0008]]. These values are derived from the [[gas_gas_heat_exchanger_operating_temperatures|operating temperatures]] to provide average thermal conditions within the system.

**Bulk Fluid Temperatures**
Bulk fluid temperatures are based on the average fluid temperatures within the specified sections:
*   Bulk fluid temperature for Shell (inside core): 519.05 C
*   Bulk fluid temperature for Shell (outside core): 502.15 C
*   Bulk fluid temperature for Tubes: 325.65 C

**Mean Metal Temperatures**
Mean metal temperatures represent the average temperature of the metal components:
*   Mean metal temperature for Shellside (Tms): 510.59999999999997 C
*   Mean metal temperature for Tubeside (Tmt): 422.34999999999997 C
*   Mean metal temperature for Bypass duct (Tmb): 339.575 C

For a comprehensive understanding of the calculation methodology and any associated limitations, please consult the [[gas_gas_heat_exchanger_design_temperature_calculation_commentary|Design Temperature Calculation Commentary]].
