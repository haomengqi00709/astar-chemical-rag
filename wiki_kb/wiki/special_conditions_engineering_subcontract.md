# Special Conditions for Engineering Subcontract (0-SPC-2202)

Special Conditions for Engineering Subcontracts are detailed in document 0-SPC-2202: Special Conditions for Engineering Subcontract. These conditions are used to modify the [[standard_conditions_engineering_subcontract]] (0-SPC-2201) and are often project-specific.

**Purpose and Content**
0-SPC-2202 includes the full text for special conditions that **will** always be used to modify the NEC form for use by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. It also contains project-specific special conditions, presented as clause titles only, along with a description of circumstances when a clause may be required, and in some cases, optional wording. Descriptive material enclosed in square brackets within 0-SPC-2202 is deleted before the document is issued to ensure clarity and conciseness in the final contract.

**Preparation, Checking, and Approval**
The preparation of 0-SPC-2202: Special Conditions for Engineering Subcontract **will** be undertaken specifically for each project by the Project Engineer. Following preparation, the document **will** be checked by the Director of Projects. Approval of 0-SPC-2202 **will** be granted by Legal.

**Mandatory Legal Approval**
Any modification to the [[standard_conditions_engineering_subcontract]] **must** be approved by Legal.

**Risk Management Integration**
Special Conditions for Engineering Subcontract may be identified as being required by the project risk management strategy, ensuring that specific project risks are adequately addressed within the contractual terms.

Subcontractors **will** comply with the terms and conditions set out in 02C03000-0-SPC-2202 R1.
