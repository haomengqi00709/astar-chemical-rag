# Instrument Data

Instrument Data refers to the detailed specifications and performance characteristics of various instrumentation components within a process plant. This data is crucial for accurate hydraulic calculations, particularly during the final stages of pump sizing.

**Role in Pump Calculations:**
During the [[pump_sizing_stages|Final Impeller Trim]] stage, the Hydraulic Engineer **will** obtain additional final information from [[instrument_data|Instrument Data]]. This information is used to:
*   **Control Valve Pressure Drops:** Check the Instrument Data for control valve pressure drops. Control valves can introduce significant pressure losses into a system, directly impacting the required Total Dynamic Head (TDH) for a pump.
*   **Significant Instrument Pressure Drops:** Check Instrument Data for pressure drop figures for any other instruments that may have significant pressure drops. Examples include Coriolis meters, which can have measurable pressure losses that need to be accounted for in the hydraulic balance.

Accurate instrument data ensures that all pressure losses within the system are correctly quantified, leading to a precise determination of the pump's required operating point.
