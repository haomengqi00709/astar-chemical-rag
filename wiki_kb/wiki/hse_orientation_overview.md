# Health and Safety Orientation Overview

This procedure establishes a standard format for Health and Safety (HSE) orientations across Aker Kvaerner Chemetics work sites. It outlines the information to be reviewed with new personnel upon hire and extends to all employees, contract employees, indirect employees, and Vendor employees, as well as visitors.

**Scope and General Requirements**
All Aker Kvaerner Chemetics employees and contract employees **must** receive a pre-job orientation. The content of this orientation is determined jointly by Human Resources and the hiring Division. A Health and Safety component **shall** be included as part of the pre-job orientation. This component may be delivered as part of a broader Company orientation or as a separate session, but it **must** be completed before the individual commences work.

Managers **must** ensure that their employees have received an initial Health and Safety orientation prior to starting work. Furthermore, managers **must** conduct an orientation with their employee(s) to review the potential hazards specific to their work area and operations.

**Re-orientation**
Personnel who have been absent from the office for more than one year, but who regularly visit the office, **will** receive a new orientation to ensure their knowledge of current safety procedures is up-to-date.

**Orientation Attendance Log**
All individuals attending a safety orientation **will** sign an orientation attendance log. This log **must** contain the following information as a minimum:
*   Date
*   Name
*   Company
*   Department
*   Social Security number

**Client/Owner Orientation**
Following the completion of the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics / Aker Kvaerner safety orientation, new workers may be required to attend a Client / Owner safety orientation. If a Client / Owner orientation is a project requirement, specific instructions **will** be provided during the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site orientation.

For detailed information on the structure and content of HSE orientations, refer to [[0-PRC-8005_table_1]].
