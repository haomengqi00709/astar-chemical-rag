# Converter Fabrication and Marking Requirements

This page outlines the mandatory fabrication, material handling, and marking requirements for the Converter vessel (Tag No. 7540R). All dimensions are in millimeters unless otherwise specified.

**Fabrication and Documentation:**
*   **Mandatory:** The Vendor **must** submit fabrication drawings to Chemetics for approval before proceeding with fabrication.
*   **Mandatory:** The Vendor **must** supply all bolts, nuts, and gaskets for openings with blind flanges.
*   Maximum (future) loadings are shown on the elevation view.
*   A separate document, 4-SCR-7540, will be independently prepared (internally) for use by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or the Owner for civil/structural engineering.

**Catalyst and Internal Components:**
*   Catalyst volumes and depths are specified per the table below: [[converter_catalyst_details]].
*   **Mandatory:** Wire mesh **must** be woven from 3mm dia. wire.
*   **Mandatory:** Wire mesh pattern **must** be 13mm x 13mm square.
*   **Mandatory:** Minimum open area for wire mesh **must** be 57%.

**Vessel Marking:**
*   **Mandatory:** The equipment name, equipment number, and nozzle mark letter **must** be clearly stenciled to the vessel.
*   **Mandatory:** Stenciled letters **must** be 75 mm high.
*   **Mandatory:** Stenciled letters **must** be black.
*   **Equipment Name:** Converter
*   **Chemetics Equipment Number:** 7540R
*   **Owner Equipment Number:** 42-E-1704

These requirements are crucial for ensuring the quality and proper identification of the fabricated equipment. For general vendor quality assurance, refer to [[vendor_quality_assurance_requirements]]. Final inspections and documentation requirements are also critical, as detailed in documents like [[0-QDS-7411_final_inspections]].
