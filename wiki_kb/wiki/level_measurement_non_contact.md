# Level Measurement - Non-Contact

Non-contact level measurement technologies, such as ultrasonic and radar sensors, are employed for various applications, particularly where direct contact with the process fluid is undesirable or impractical.

**Ultrasonic Level Sensors:**
*   **Applications:** Ultrasonic level sensors may be used for measurement of level in material handling applications and vented vessels, tanks, sumps, etc., containing slurries or liquids.
*   **Limitations and Alternatives:** Other methods may be necessary for ultrasonic level sensors in applications where foam/froth, condensation, dusts, or steam are prevalent, as these can interfere with ultrasonic wave propagation.
*   **Transducer Selection:** Ultrasonic transducers **will** be selected for adequate acoustic power to perform reliably under the most arduous process conditions.
*   **Device Type:** Four-wire ultrasonic devices are preferred.
*   **Temperature Compensation:** Only ultrasonic level instruments with built-in temperature measurement and compensation **will** be used, ensuring accuracy despite temperature fluctuations.

**Radar Level Sensors:**
Radar level sensors offer robust performance in challenging environments.
*   **Applications:** Radar level sensors may be used for measurement of level in material handling. They **will** be used in applications involving pressurised vessels, including vacuum conditions. They are also suitable for applications containing foam/froth, heavy condensation, and where the potential for gas or product vaporisation may occur. Furthermore, radar level sensors may be used in extremely dusty applications and where dust bursts are present during product filling.
*   **Antenna Material:** The radar sensor **will** be fitted with a horn or antenna manufactured from material chemically compatible with the process environment.
*   **Power Supply:** Loop-powered radar level sensors are preferred.
