# Packing and Shipping Requirements for Carbon Steel Tank (0-QDS-7435)

The packing and shipping requirements for the Carbon Steel Tank, as specified in Item 32.0 of QDS 0-QDS-7435, are crucial for ensuring the safe and undamaged delivery of the equipment. Chemetics maintains a mandatory role in witnessing these activities.

**Packing and Documentation:**
*   Chemetics **must** witness Packaging to ensure that the tank is prepared for transport according to specifications, protecting it from damage during transit.
*   A Packing List record is required as AP (As Prepared) as per [[0-QDS-7435_quality_record_definitions]]. This document details the contents of the shipment.
*   Chemetics **must** witness the Packing List to verify its accuracy and completeness before shipment.

For a comprehensive overview of all inspection and documentation requirements, refer to [[0-QDS-7435_inspection_and_documentation_requirements]].
