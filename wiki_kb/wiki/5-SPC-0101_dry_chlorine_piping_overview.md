# Dry Chlorine Piping - Overview and General Requirements (5-SPC-0101)

This Engineering Standard, 5-SPC-0101, defines special requirements for the installation and testing of carbon steel piping specifically for dry chlorine service. It also serves as a guide for preparing the piping system for operation.

**Applicability and Compliance**
This Standard applies when referenced on purchase orders, drawings, or other project documents. All piping **shall** satisfy the requirements of [[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping]] where applicable. In cases of conflict or doubt between Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' drawings and Standards and these codes, clarification **shall** be sought from an Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' engineer.

**Dry Chlorine Characteristics**
Dry chlorine is classified as a toxic irritant. It is generally non-flammable and can exist in both liquid and gaseous states.

**Piping Classification and Materials**
Carbon steel piping for dry chlorine service is generally divided into two classes, which are segregated by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics pipe Project Specifications. For materials other than carbon steel, the project and materials engineer **shall** be consulted. Limited information regarding [[5-SPC-0101_pvc_frp_dry_chlorine_piping_requirements]] is provided in Appendix 2 of the original document.
