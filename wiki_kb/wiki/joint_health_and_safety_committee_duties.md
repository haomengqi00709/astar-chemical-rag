# Joint Health and Safety Committee Duties

The Joint Health and Safety Committee (JHSC) plays a crucial role in maintaining and improving [[workplace_health_and_environmental_practices]]. Its duties encompass a wide range of responsibilities aimed at proactive risk management and continuous improvement.

**Core Responsibilities:**
*   **Hazard Identification and Control:** The JHSC has the duty to identify situations that may be unhealthy or unsafe for workers and advise on effective measures to control exposure. This includes reviewing [[hazard_and_operability_studies]] and other risk assessments.
*   **Complaint Resolution:** The committee has the duty to consider and expeditiously deal with complaints related to health, safety, and the environment.
*   **Recommendation for Improvements:** The JHSC has the duty to make recommendations to the employer for improvements to health, safety, and environment matters at the work site.
*   **Program and Policy Oversight:** The committee has the duty to advise the employer on programs and policies required under regulation and to monitor the effectiveness of those programs.
*   **Rule and Regulation Enhancement:** The JHSC has the duty to plan improvements to existing health and safety rules and regulations.
*   **Change Assessment:** The committee has the duty to assess the implications of changes in work tasks, operations, and processes on health and safety.
*   **Policy Advice:** The JHSC has the duty to advise the employer on proposed changes to health and safety policy.

**Monitoring and Investigation:**
*   **Workplace Inspections:** The committee has the duty to participate in inspections to detect unsafe conditions, unsafe work practices, hazardous materials, and environmental factors. It also **must** perform regular workplace inspections.
*   **Effectiveness Monitoring:** The JHSC has the duty to monitor the effectiveness of health and safety recommendations and procedures.
*   **Accident Investigation:** The committee has the duty to participate in the investigation of [[hse_incident_reporting|workplace accidents]].
*   **Data Analysis:** The JHSC has the duty to study and analyze workplace accident and injury data to identify trends and root causes.

These duties collectively ensure that the JHSC acts as a vigilant and influential body in promoting a safe and healthy work environment, aligning with [[hse_management_and_employee_responsibilities|management and employee responsibilities]] for HSE.
