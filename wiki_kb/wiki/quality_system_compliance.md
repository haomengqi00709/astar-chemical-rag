# Quality System Compliance

This page outlines the requirements for establishing and maintaining a quality system in accordance with project contracts, as specified in Purchase Order [[0-SPC-5001_purchase_order_overview|0-SPC-5001]].

**Vendor's Quality System Obligation**
The Vendor **must** plan, establish, and maintain a quality system that conforms with the quality system described in the Purchase Order. This ensures that all processes, procedures, and controls are in place to deliver [[goods_and_services_definitions|Goods and Services]] that meet the specified requirements and standards.

**Access for Monitoring and Auditing**
To ensure compliance and effectiveness, the Vendor **must** grant the [[project_buyer_roles|Project Buyer]], [[lead_engineer_roles|Lead Engineer]], and [[owner_roles|Owner]] access to the quality system of both the Vendor and any of their [[0-SPC-5001_purchase_order_overview#Supplier|Suppliers]]. This access is for the specific purposes of monitoring and quality auditing. This allows Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Owner to verify that quality standards are being met throughout the supply chain.

For broader vendor quality assurance requirements, refer to [[vendor_quality_assurance]].
