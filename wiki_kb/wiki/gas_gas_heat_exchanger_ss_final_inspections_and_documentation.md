# Gas/Gas Heat Exchanger - SS Final Inspections and Documentation

For the Gas/Gas Heat Exchanger - SS project (Document ID: [[0-QDS-7403-2_gas_gas_heat_exchanger_ss_quality_data_sheet]]), a series of final inspections and documentation reviews are required to ensure the completed unit meets all specifications before shipment. All listed inspections and reviews **must** be completed.

**Required Inspections and Reviews:**

*   **Dimensional Inspection:**
    *   Required as "Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package" (FP).
    *   Chemetics requires Witness for Dimensional inspection.
    *   Chemetics requires a Hold point for Dimensional inspection.
    *   Dimensional inspection **must** be completed.

*   **Contour & Finish of Welds Inspection:**
    *   Required as "Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package" (FP).
    *   Chemetics requires Witness for Contour & finish of welds inspection.
    *   Chemetics requires a Hold point for Contour & finish of welds inspection.
    *   This inspection is designated as 'Spot' with a value of '0.25'.
    *   Contour & finish of welds inspection **must** be completed.

*   **Surfaces Free From Defects Inspection:**
    *   Chemetics requires Witness for Surfaces free from defects inspection.
    *   Chemetics requires a Hold point for Surfaces free from defects inspection.
    *   This inspection has a value of '1.0' in the 'Required in Vendor I & T Plan' column.
    *   This inspection has a value of '1.0' in the 'Location' column.
    *   Surfaces free from defects inspection **must** be completed.

*   **Cleanliness Inspection:**
    *   Chemetics requires Witness for Cleanliness inspection.
    *   Chemetics requires a Hold point for Cleanliness inspection.
    *   Cleanliness inspection **must** be completed.

*   **Review QA Documentation:**
    *   Chemetics requires Witness for Review QA Documentation.
    *   Chemetics requires a Hold point for Review QA Documentation.
    *   Review QA Documentation **must** be completed.

*   **Preparation for Shipping:**
    *   Chemetics requires Witness for Preparation for shipping.
    *   Chemetics requires a Hold point for Preparation for shipping.
    *   Preparation for shipping requires mounting on saddles.
    *   Preparation for shipping **must** be completed.

These final checks ensure that the heat exchanger is fabricated to the correct dimensions, has acceptable weld quality and surface finishes, is clean, and is properly documented and prepared for safe transport.
