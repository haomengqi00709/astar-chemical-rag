# Vertical Pump Field Inspection Items (4-FIC-XX11-R0)

The Vertical Pump Field Inspection Checklist (4-FIC-XX11-R0) outlines 24 main items that **must** be verified during the installation and pre-commissioning phases of a vertical centrifugal pump. These items ensure proper mechanical integrity, alignment, and readiness for operation. Items 21, 22, 23, and 24 are designated as blank for potential additional or project-specific checks.

**Mandatory Inspection Items:**

1.  **Nameplate:** The correct nameplate **must** be installed on the pump.
2.  **Pump Position:** The pump position **must** be checked for correct elevation, orientation, and levelness. This is crucial for proper operation and alignment.
3.  **Permanent Suction Strainer:** A permanent suction strainer **must** be installed.
4.  **Mounting Bolts:** All mounting bolts **must** be secure and tightened to the specified torque.
5.  **Suction/Discharge Piping:** Suction and discharge piping **must** be properly aligned and supported to ensure no undue stress is placed on the pump nozzles. Refer to [[piping_erection_0_spc_6x95]] for piping installation guidelines.
6.  **Auxiliary Systems:** All auxiliary systems, including seal flush, water cooling, and air purge, **must** be complete and functional.
7.  **Accessories:** All necessary accessories, such as gauges, vents, drain valves, and connections, **must** be installed.
8.  **Mechanical Seals/Packing:** Mechanical seals or packing **must** be correctly installed. Refer to [[4-DST-XXXX-R1_vertical_pump_shaft_seal_and_bearings]] for seal specifications.
9.  **Oil Rings:** Oil rings **must** be installed and verified to be free-moving.
10. **Bearings:** Bearings **must** be flushed and filled to the correct lubricant level. Refer to [[4-DST-XXXX-R1_vertical_pump_shaft_seal_and_bearings]] for bearing specifications.
11. **Free Rotation:** Free rotation of the pump **must** be checked by hand to ensure no internal obstructions or binding.
12. **Grounding:** Electrical grounding **must** be completed. Refer to [[grounding_installation_details]] and [[electrical_installation_specification]].
13. **Motor Rotation:** Motor rotation **must** be verified by the electrical contractor, and a report of this verification **must** be attached. Refer to [[4-DST-XXXX-R1_vertical_pump_motor_and_drive_system]] for motor specifications.
14. **Cold Alignment:** Cold alignment of the pump and motor **must** be completed.
15. **Coupling:** The coupling **must** be installed and lubricated as per manufacturer specifications.
16. **Coupling Guard:** The coupling guard **must** be installed and secure to ensure personnel safety.
17. **Temporary Suction Strainers:** Temporary suction strainers **must** be in place for initial startup protection.
18. **External Painting:** External painting **must** be completed with no visible defects. Refer to [[coatings_erection_0_spc_6x97]] for painting requirements.
19. **Insulation:** Insulation **must** be complete where required. Refer to [[insulation_erection_0_spc_6x96]] for insulation guidelines.
20. **Flange Guards:** Flange guards **must** be installed for safety and protection.

These detailed checks are critical to ensure the safe and efficient operation of the vertical pump. Any deficiencies identified **must** be recorded and addressed.
