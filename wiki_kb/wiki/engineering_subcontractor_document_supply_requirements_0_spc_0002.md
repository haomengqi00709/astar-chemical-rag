# Engineering Subcontractor Document Supply Requirements (0-SPC-0002)

This specification, 0-SPC-0002, outlines the mandatory requirements for the supply of documentation by Engineering Subcontractors to Aker Kvaerner. Adherence to these guidelines ensures consistency and compatibility across all project documentation.

**Document Format and Software**
All documents supplied by the Subcontractor **must** be delivered in a native digital format. The primary software platforms mandated for document creation and delivery are:
*   AutoCAD for drawings and technical diagrams.
*   Microsoft Office for textual and tabular documents.

**Document Templates**
Aker Kvaerner will provide specific document templates to Engineering Subcontractors. These templates are designed for use with both AutoCAD and Microsoft Office software and include essential elements such as:
*   Standardized borders.
*   Pre-defined title blocks.
*   Consistent style elements.

Subcontractors are permitted to insert their company name and reference number in the designated locations within these provided templates. However, all other template elements and styles **must** be maintained as provided by Aker Kvaerner.
