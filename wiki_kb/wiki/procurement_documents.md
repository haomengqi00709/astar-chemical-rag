# Procurement Documents and Purchase Orders

**Purchase Order Attachments**
Documents issued for the procurement of equipment will be attached to text section 1: Scope of Supply of the Purchase Order. These attachments ensure that vendors receive all necessary technical and commercial requirements.

For the specific form and structure of the Purchase Order, refer to procedure 0-PRC-5001.
