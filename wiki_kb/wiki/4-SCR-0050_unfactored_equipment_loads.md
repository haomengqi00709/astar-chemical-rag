# Unfactored Equipment Loads for Skid on Concrete & Grout (4-SCR-0050)

This page details the types of unfactored equipment loads specified in document [[4-SCR-0050_document_administration]] for skids supported on concrete and grout. These loads are critical for the structural design of the support system.

**Types of Unfactored Equipment Loads:**
The following load categories must be considered for the equipment:
