# Metering Pump Motor and Ancillary Equipment

This page outlines the specifications for the motor and ancillary equipment associated with metering pumps, as detailed in Document ID 4-DST-XXXX-R1. These components are essential for the pump's operation, control, and safety.

**Motor Specifications**
*   Motor supply and mount **must** be specified to ensure compatibility with the electrical system and mechanical integration.
*   The standard mounting type for the motor is **Foot Mount**.

**Ancillary Equipment and Specifications**
The following specifications and equipment **must** be included:
*   **Lubrication**: Detailed lubrication requirements for moving parts.
*   **Speed Control**: Mechanisms for adjusting the pump's operating speed and, consequently, its flow rate.
*   **Relief Valve**: A safety device designed to protect the pump and system from overpressure.

All ancillary equipment, including the Back Pressure Valve, Pulsation Dampener, and Calibration Column, **shall** be supplied and mounted by the Vendor.

Additional ancillary equipment supplied by the Vendor includes:
*   Foot Mount (for the pump or motor, if applicable)
*   Lubrication system
*   Speed Control mechanism
*   Relief Valve

**Vendor Responsibilities**
The vendor is responsible for the supply and mounting of all specified ancillary equipment, ensuring proper integration and compatibility with the metering pump. For comprehensive vendor quality assurance requirements related to equipment, refer to [[vendor_quality_assurance_equipment_0_spc_7001]]. Further details on electrical equipment specifications can be found in [[electrical_equipment_specifications]].
