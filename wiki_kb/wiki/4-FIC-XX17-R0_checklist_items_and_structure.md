# Plate and Frame Heat Exchanger Field Inspection Checklist: Items and Structure

This page details the main inspection items and structural components of the 4-FIC-XX17-R0 Plate and Frame Heat Exchanger Field Inspection Checklist.

**Main Checklist Items:**
The primary checklist section comprises 24 distinct inspection items. Key requirements include:
*   Nameplate must be installed and correct.
*   Foundation/Anchor bolts must be secure and tightened.
*   Inlet/outlet piping must be properly aligned and supported (no stress on nozzles).
*   Plate pack must contain correct number of plates and gaskets.
*   Exchanger must be compressed to supplier’s specification.
*   Exchanger must be clean (all foreign material removed from inlet/outlet ports).
*   Accessories (gauges, vents, drain valves etc.) must be installed.
*   Shroud must be installed around plate pack.
*   Grounding must be completed.
*   Flange Guards must be installed.

**Deficiencies & Comments Section:**
The document includes a dedicated section for recording Deficiencies & Comments. This section provides 10 numbered slots for detailed notes on any issues identified during the inspection.
