# Substance Use Policy Support and Rehabilitation

Chemetics is committed to supporting its Employees who may be struggling with substance dependency or emerging alcohol or drug problems. The company provides resources for prevention, rehabilitation, and aftercare.

### Seeking Assistance
*   Employees concerned about or experiencing alcohol or drug-related problems are strongly encouraged to seek assistance from Chemetics’ Employee and Family Assistance Program (“EFAP”), their personal physician, or appropriate community services. This proactive approach is encouraged before job performance is affected or violations of this policy occur.
*   Once a problem has been identified, the Employee **SHOULD** contact Human Resources, who can assist in arranging appropriate support.

### Non-Prejudicial Support
*   No prejudicial or disciplinary action will be taken against an Employee solely based on their participation in EFAP or another support program. This encourages Employees to seek help without fear of immediate reprisal for seeking assistance.

### Policy Breaches and Disciplinary Action
*   It is important to note that Employees who breach this policy will still be subject to normal disciplinary action in accordance with the nature and degree of the violation, irrespective of their participation in a support program. Seeking help for a substance problem does not exempt an Employee from the consequences of a policy violation.
