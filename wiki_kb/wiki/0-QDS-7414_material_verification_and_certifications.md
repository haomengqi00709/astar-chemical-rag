# FRP Tank Material Verification and Certifications (0-QDS-7414)

This page outlines the material verification and certification requirements for FRP Tanks as specified in Quality Data Sheet **0-QDS-7414**. These requirements ensure that all materials used in the fabrication meet the specified standards and are properly documented.

**Material Verification:**
*   **Material Verification** is a mandatory requirement and **must** be included in the Vendor's Inspection and Test (I & T) Plan. This ensures that the identity and properties of the materials used are confirmed throughout the manufacturing process.

**Material Test Certificates:**
*   **Material Test Certificates** require a Quality Record **FP** (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package). This means that these certificates **must** be compiled and submitted as part of the final Quality Assurance Documentation Package.
*   **Material Test Certificates** require **Chemetics Witness** surveillance. This indicates that a Chemetics representative **must** be present to witness the verification of these certificates.

**Certificate of Compliance:**
*   A **Certificate of Compliance** requires a Quality Record **FP** (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package). This certificate **must** be included in the final Quality Assurance Documentation Package, confirming that the FRP Tank complies with all specified requirements.

These requirements are critical for maintaining the integrity and safety of the FRP Tanks. For broader context on material and welding certifications, refer to [[0-QDS-7411_material_and_welding_certifications]].
