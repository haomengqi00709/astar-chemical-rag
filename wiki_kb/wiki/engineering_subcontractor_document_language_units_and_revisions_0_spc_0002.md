# Engineering Subcontractor Document Language, Units, and Revisions (0-SPC-0002)

This section of 0-SPC-0002 details the requirements for language, units, and the management of revisions for all documentation supplied by Engineering Subcontractors.

**Language Requirements**
Document content **must** be prepared in the English language. As an alternative, Subcontractors may prepare bilingual documents. If bilingual documents are chosen, they **must** include both English and the Subcontractor’s working language.

**Units of Measure**
All document content **must** be prepared using the International System of Units (SI units). For more information on engineering units, refer to [[engineering_units]].

**Revision Marking and Management**
Subcontractors are required to clearly indicate all revisions made to documents:
*   **Drawings:** Revisions **must** be highlighted by "clouding" the changed areas on the drawings.
*   **Textual and Tabular Documents:** Revisions **must** be marked with a vertical line in the left-hand margin adjacent to the revised text or data.

Following any revision, the Subcontractor **must** increment the revision number and update the document's status. The updated status **must** be clearly marked on each document prior to its issuance to Aker Kvaerner.

For comprehensive guidelines on managing document revisions, refer to [[document_revision_flagging_and_management]]. General standards for document content are also available in [[vendor_document_content_standards]].
