# 0-SPC-2205 Communication and Document Retention

This page outlines the requirements for communications and document retention under document 0-SPC-2205, ensuring clarity, traceability, and proper record-keeping for the engineering subcontract.

**Communication Protocols:**
*   Each required communication **must** be in a form stated in the Scope.
*   All required communications **must** be readable, copyable, and recordable.
*   Writing for communications **must** be in the English language.
*   A communication has effect when it is received at the last address notified by the recipient for receiving communications.
*   If no address is notified, a communication has effect when received at the recipient's address stated in the Subcontract Data.

**Document Retention:**
*   The Subcontractor **must** retain copies of drawings, specifications, reports and other documents which record the services for ten years.
*   Retained copies **must** be in the form stated in the Scope.
