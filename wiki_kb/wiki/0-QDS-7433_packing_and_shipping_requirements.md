# Flocculant Mixer Packing and Shipping Requirements

The proper packing and preparation for shipping of the Flocculant Mixer package are crucial to prevent damage during transit and ensure its safe arrival at the project site. Specific requirements are mandated to ensure that the equipment is adequately protected and correctly documented for logistics.

**Mandatory Requirements:**

*   **Packaging:**
    *   Packaging **requires** Chemetics Witness surveillance. A Chemetics representative **must** be present to observe the packaging process, ensuring that the Flocculant Mixer package is adequately protected against mechanical damage, corrosion, and environmental factors during transportation and storage. This includes verification of crating, wrapping, and securing methods.
*   **Packing List:**
    *   The Packing List **requires** a Quality Record (AP). This record **must** be prepared by the vendor, detailing all items included in the shipment, their quantities, dimensions, weights, and any special handling instructions.
    *   The Packing List **requires** Chemetics Witness surveillance. A Chemetics representative **must** verify the accuracy and completeness of the Packing List against the actual contents of the shipment prior to sealing the package.

These requirements ensure that the Flocculant Mixer package is not only manufactured to high standards but also arrives at its destination in the same condition. For detailed specifications on packaging for different modes of transport, refer to [[packaging_land_transportation_0_spc_5004]] and [[packaging_marine_transport]].
