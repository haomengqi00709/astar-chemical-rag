# Substance Use Policy Breach and Disciplinary Action

Breaches of Chemetics' Substance Use Policy are treated as serious job performance issues and will result in disciplinary action. The response will be determined based on a comprehensive review of all applicable circumstances.

### Disciplinary Actions
Employees who breach this policy will be subject to normal disciplinary action. Chemetics' response to a breach may include, but is not limited to:
*   Requiring counseling or treatment.
*   Increased supervision.
*   Paid leave.
*   Unpaid suspension.
*   Probation.
*   Termination of employment.

### Drug and Alcohol Testing
Chemetics may require an Employee to undergo [[drug_and_alcohol_testing_procedures|drug and alcohol testing]] in certain situations, in accordance with the procedures outlined in Clause 6.2 of the original policy document.

### Determination of Response
Chemetics’ determination of an appropriate response to a policy breach will take into account all applicable circumstances, including:
*   The health and safety of the Employee and others.
*   The specific circumstances of the incident.
*   Any prior violations by the Employee.
*   The supervisory nature of the Employee’s position.
*   Whether the Employee is in a [[safety_sensitive_positions|Safety-Sensitive Position]].
*   Clients’ requirements.
*   Work productivity and schedules.
*   Any other relevant considerations.

### Investigations and Cooperation
Chemetics will carry out investigations into the circumstances surrounding a breach of this policy by an Employee.
*   In post-incident or “near miss” situations, the Employee **SHALL** fully cooperate with the investigation.
*   In post-incident or “near miss” situations, the Employee **SHALL** ensure that they do not ingest any substance or take any action which may affect the results of any substance testing required under Clause 6.2 (referencing [[drug_and_alcohol_testing_procedures]]).
