# Harassment and Discrimination Policy Roles and Responsibilities

The responsibility for creating and maintaining a positive work environment rests with all persons sharing the workplace.

**Employee Expectations:**
*   Managers, supervisors, and co-workers **MUST** recognize and refrain from actions, whether deliberate or unintentional, which offend, embarrass, or humiliate others.

**Manager and Supervisor Responsibilities:**
*   All managers and supervisors **SHALL** be responsible for providing a workplace free from harassment and discrimination.
*   All managers and supervisors **SHALL** be responsible for ensuring the application of this policy within their respective departments.
*   Managers and supervisors have the responsibility to manage and supervise the work of employees. This policy does not diminish the right to manage and supervise, which may include actions such as:
    *   Verbal reminders
    *   Verbal warnings
    *   Written warnings
    *   Discharge of an employee
