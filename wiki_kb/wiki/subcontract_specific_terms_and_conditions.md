# Subcontract-Specific Terms and Conditions

Subcontract-specific terms and conditions are distinct from [[standard_conditions_engineering_subcontract]] and [[special_conditions_engineering_subcontract]] as they are inserted directly into the body of the Engineering Subcontract Agreement. These terms are typically unique to each subcontract.

**Typical Subjects Covered**
Subcontract-specific conditions commonly address the following areas:
*   **Data associated with pricing and payment:** Specific details related to how the subcontractor will be compensated.
*   **Identification of applicable Secondary Option Clauses:** References to specific clauses from [[standard_conditions_engineering_subcontract]] (0-SPC-2201) that are relevant to the subcontract.
*   **Data associated with Secondary Option Clauses:** Detailed information for clauses such as Parent company guarantee, Delay damages, Security, and Limitation of Liability.

**Standard Clauses and Modifications**
Standard clauses for these subjects are appended to the procedure [[engineering_subcontract_commercial_terms_0_prc_2205]]. The text for these standard clauses may be varied by modification of this procedure. Any modification of this procedure **must** be issued for the project in accordance with [[approval_and_use_of_standards]]. The revised document (modified procedure) **will** be approved by Legal.

**Selection and Application**
Subcontract-specific conditions to be applied are typically selected as part of a project risk management strategy. These conditions **will** be indicated on the Project Execution Plan, which is part of [[project_planning_0_prc_3001]]. Additional conditions may also be applied to a subcontract as a result of the [[engineering_subcontractor_bid_evaluation_procedure_0_prc_2204]] process.

**Typical Subjects Covered**
Typical subjects addressed by subcontract-specific conditions include:
*   Data associated with pricing and payment.
*   Identification of applicable Secondary Option Clauses of [[standard_conditions_engineering_subcontract]] (0-SPC-2201).
*   Data associated with specific Secondary Option Clauses, such as Parent company guarantee, Delay damages, Security, and Limitation of Liability.

**Selection Criteria**
Subcontract-specific Conditions to be applied will typically be selected as part of a project risk management strategy.
