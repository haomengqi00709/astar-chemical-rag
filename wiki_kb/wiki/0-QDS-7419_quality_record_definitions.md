# Acid Tower Field Erected Shop Component Quality Record Definitions (0-QDS-7419)

This Quality Data Sheet (0-QDS-7419) specifies the following definitions for quality records related to the Acid Tower Field Erected Shop Component:

*   **AP (As Prepared):** This designation indicates that the quality record is required "As Prepared" by the vendor. These documents are typically generated during the manufacturing process and submitted for review.
*   **FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package):** This designation signifies that the quality record **must** be "Included in the Quality Assurance Documentation Package" submitted by the vendor. These are essential documents that form part of the final quality dossier for the component.

For general quality record definitions and requirements, refer to documents such as [[0-QDS-7404-2_quality_record_definitions]].
