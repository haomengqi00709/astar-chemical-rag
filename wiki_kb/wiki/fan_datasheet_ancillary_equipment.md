# Fan Datasheet Ancillary Equipment

This page outlines the mandatory ancillary equipment items and their specifications required for the fan, as detailed in the [[fan_datasheet_administration|Fan Datasheet Template]] (4-DST-XXXX-R0). These items are crucial for the complete and functional operation of the fan package.

**Mandatory Ancillary Equipment Requirements**
The following ancillary equipment items **must** be specified:
*   **Ancillary Equipment**: General category for auxiliary components. Refer to [[fan_ancillary_equipment_overview]].
*   **Ancillary Equipment Supply by**: The entity responsible for supplying the ancillary equipment. Refer to [[ancillary_equipment_vendor_supply]].
*   **Ancillary Equipment Mount by**: The entity responsible for mounting the ancillary equipment. Refer to [[ancillary_equipment_installation_responsibility]].
*   **Sheaves/V-belt**: Specifications for sheaves and V-belts, if applicable. Refer to [[sheaves_v_belt_specification]].
*   **Base Plate c/w Spacer Block**: Details for the base plate including any spacer blocks. Refer to [[base_plate_and_spacer_block_specification]].
*   **Drive Guard**: Specifications for the drive guard. Refer to [[drive_guard_specification]].
*   **Bearing**: Specifications for the bearings. Refer to [[bearing_specification]].
*   **Lubrication**: Requirements for lubrication systems or methods. Refer to [[lubrication_requirements]].
*   **Vibration Isolator**: Specifications for vibration isolators. Refer to [[vibration_isolator_specification]].
*   **Inlet Filter**: Specifications for the inlet filter. Refer to [[inlet_filter_specification]].
*   **Inlet Box**: Specifications for the inlet box. Refer to [[inlet_box_specification]].
*   **External Inlet-Vane Damper**: Specifications for the external inlet-vane damper. Refer to [[external_inlet_vane_damper_specification]].
*   **Instrument Connection**: Details for all instrument connection points. Refer to [[instrument_connection_details]].
