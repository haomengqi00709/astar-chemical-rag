# API 620 Recommended Rules for the Design and Construction of Large Welded Low Pressure Storage Tanks

API Standard 620 provides recommended rules for the design and construction of large, welded, low-pressure storage tanks. These tanks are typically used for storing liquids at pressures not exceeding 15 psig (103 kPa) and at temperatures not exceeding 250°F (120°C). The standard covers material selection, design, fabrication, erection, and testing requirements.

This standard is referenced in [[4-SPC-0014_referenced_codes_and_definitions]] and specifically in [[4-SPC-0014_inspection_and_testing_requirements]] for the radiographic examination of tankage fabricated from Columbium Stabilized High Nickel Austenitic stainless steel. For such tankage, the minimum radiography requirements shall be spot radiography.

For detailed design and construction rules for low-pressure storage tanks, refer to the latest edition of API 620.
