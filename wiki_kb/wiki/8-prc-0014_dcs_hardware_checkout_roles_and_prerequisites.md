# DCS Hardware Checkout Roles and Prerequisites (8-PRC-0014)

This page outlines the roles and responsibilities, along with the essential prerequisites, for conducting the [[dcs_field_hardware_checkout_8_prc_0014|DCS Hardware Checkout Procedure (8-PRC-0014)]].

**Roles and Responsibilities:**
*   **Site Sub-contractor:** During the construction phase, the site sub-contractor is responsible for checking the completeness of the DCS installation before notifying the site supervisor.
*   **Site Supervisor:** The site supervisor visually reviews the installed DCS systems against general arrangement and installation instructions. They are responsible for notifying the Project/Commissioning Manager when the work is ready for final checkout and all hardware checkout prerequisites are complete. The site supervisor, in conjunction with the commissioning manager, **will** advise the DCS vendor that the DCS is ready for hardware checkout.
*   **DCS Vendor Representative(s):** DCS Vendor Representative(s) **will be required** to perform this checkout procedure. They **will** verify the DCS Hardware installation against Engineering Documents, DCS Vendor drawings and procedures, and the project's [[dcs_field_hardware_checkout_8_prc_0014|DCS Hardware Checkout Procedure (8-PRC-0014)]].
*   **Specialist:** A specialist **will** witness this checkout procedure.
*   **Project/Commissioning Manager:** Receives notification from the site supervisor regarding readiness for final checkout and advises the DCS vendor.

**Checkout Prerequisites:**
Before initiating the DCS hardware checkout, the following prerequisites **must** be met:
*   All cables are installed, properly sized, and terminated.
*   [[uninterruptible_power_supply|Uninterruptible Power Supply (UPS)]] is installed and operational.
*   All relevant Engineering Documents are available.
*   The [[control_system_architecture_block_diagram|DCS Block Diagram]] is available.
*   [[control_equipment_cabinet_layouts|DCS Cabinet/Marshalling Panel Layout and Termination Drawings]] are available.
*   DCS Interconnection Wiring/Drawings are available.
*   [[equipment_grounding_requirements|DCS Grounding Drawing]] is available.
*   [[rack_room_layout_drawings]] (new slug) are available.
*   [[control_room_layout_drawings]] (new slug) (showing operator consoles) are available.
*   [[dcs_performance_specifications]] (new slug) by Vendor are available.
*   [[dcs_hardware_testing_procedures]] (new slug) by Vendor are available.
*   [[dcs_installation_manuals]] (new slug) by Vendor are available.

**Specific Installation Requirements:**
*   All grounding cables **must** be properly sized and terminated according to grounding drawings. Verification **must** ensure that the grounding meets the DCS Manufacturer Grounding Requirement(s).
*   All cabinet interconnect cables **must** be properly tagged, sized, and terminated.
*   The [[dcs_communication_network]] (new slug) **will** be tested to ensure it meets the DCS Manufacturer’s Performance Requirement(s).

**Roles and Responsibilities:**
*   **Site Sub-contractor:** During the construction phase, the site sub-contractor checks for completeness before approaching the site supervisor.
*   **Site Supervisor:** Visually reviews installed DCS systems against general arrangement and installation instructions. The site supervisor notifies the Project/Commissioning Manager when work is ready for final checkout and all hardware checkout prerequisites are complete. The site supervisor/commissioning manager will also advise the DCS vendor that the DCS is ready for DCS hardware checkout.
*   **Project/Commissioning Manager:** Receives notification from the site supervisor regarding readiness for final checkout. Advises the DCS vendor. Works with either the DCS Vendor Representative or the subcontractor to correct identified deficiencies.
*   **DCS Vendor Representative(s):** Will be required to perform this checkout procedure. They will verify DCS Hardware installation against Engineering Documents, DCS Vendor drawings and procedures, and this procedure. The DCS Vendor will provide a complete DCS Hardware Checkout sheet. They will also work with the site supervisor/commissioning manager to correct identified deficiencies.
*   **Specialist:** A specialist will witness this checkout procedure.

**DCS Hardware Checkout Sheet:**
The DCS Hardware Checkout sheet must be completed and will form part of the DCS Hardware Checkout final document.

**Deficiency Handling:**
Deficiencies identified during the checkout are recorded on a separate sheet. These deficiency sheets are then given to the site supervisor/commissioning manager, who will work with either the DCS Vendor Representative or the subcontractor to correct the identified deficiencies.

**Checkout Prerequisites:**
Before commencing the hardware checkout, the following prerequisites must be complete and available:
*   All cables
*   Uninterruptible Power Supply (UPS)
*   Engineering Documents
*   DCS Block Diagram
*   DCS Cabinet/Marshalling Panel Layout and Termination Drawings
*   DCS Interconnection Wiring/Drawings
*   DCS Grounding Drawing
*   Rack Room Layout Drawings
*   Control Room Layout Drawings (showing operator consoles)
*   DCS Performance Specification(s) by Vendor
*   DCS Hardware Testing Procedure(s) by Vendor
*   DCS Installation Manual(s) by Vendor

**Specific Hardware Verification Points:**
*   All grounding cables must be properly sized and terminated according to grounding drawings. Verification must ensure the grounding meets the DCS Manufacturer Grounding Requirement(s).
*   All cabinet interconnect cables must be properly tagged, sized, and terminated.
