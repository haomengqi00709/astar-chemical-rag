# Process Analysers

Process analysers are critical for monitoring and controlling process parameters. Specific requirements for process analysers are detailed on the individual instrument data sheets.

**Probe and Sampling System Design:**
*   pH, ORP (Oxidation-Reduction Potential), and Conductivity Probes **will** be self-cleaning where practically achievable to minimize maintenance and ensure accuracy.
*   Non-extractive sampling methods of analysis **will** be employed wherever possible to simplify design and reduce sample handling.
*   The design **will** allow for simple, automated calibration procedures where applicable, enhancing operational efficiency.

Process analysers **will** be supplied complete with any necessary sampling systems and sample conditioning systems. The sampling and sample conditioning system **will** be designed, supplied, and commissioned by the analyser Vendor to ensure optimal performance and compatibility with the specific analyser application.

**Calibration and Materials:**
*   Calibration gases and/or liquids **will** normally be provided on site due to limitations in shelf life.
*   Field mounted analysers **will** be constructed from corrosion-resistant material and be of weatherproof construction, making them suitable for outdoor installation in the plant's challenging environment (refer to [[environmental_and_site_conditions|Environmental and Site Conditions]]).

**Output Signals:**
*   For single component analysis, the output signal **will** be isolated 4-20 mA (linear) or digital fieldbus.
*   For multiple component analysis, a serial output may be considered as the method of data transmission to the Process Control System.
*   All analyser transmitters **will** incorporate an integral mounted indicator for local visibility.

**Sample System Requirements:**
Sample and sample conditioning systems **will** be constructed from materials that suit the process conditions. The sample **will** be metered, pressure regulated, cooled, cleaned, or treated as required to obtain a satisfactory sample for the analyser under all process conditions. The sampling system **will** be designed for reliability and ready access for maintenance and service.

**Emissions Monitoring:**
Process emissions **will** be identified and monitored to ensure environmental compliance.
