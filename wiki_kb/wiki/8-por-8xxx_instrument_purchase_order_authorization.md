# Instrument Purchase Order Authorization (8-POR-8XXX)

The Purchase Order (8-POR-8XXX) is the formal document for procuring instruments and bulk materials. Its issuance and authorization follow specific procedures and approval thresholds.

**Issuance Requirements:**
*   **Prepared by:** Procurement
*   **Checked by:** Project Engineer
*   **Approved by:** Project Manager

Procurement will proceed with the issue of a Purchase Order (8-POR-8XXX) upon receipt of the approved [[requisition_for_quotation_or_purchase_0_prc_5002]]. For orders of value over CAD 10k, Procurement will also require receipt of the approved [[0-prc-5003_instrument_bid_evaluation_and_approval]] (Cost Tabulation and Order Risk Assessment).

**Authorization Levels:**
*   Purchase Order < CAD 100,000 **will be authorised** by Project Buyer.
*   Purchase Order < CAD 500,000 **will be authorised** by Manager of Procurement.
*   Purchase Order < CAD 2,000,000 **will be authorised** by Director of Projects.
*   Purchase Order > CAD 2,000,000 **will be authorised** by President.
