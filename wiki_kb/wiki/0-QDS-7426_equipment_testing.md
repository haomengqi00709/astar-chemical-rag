# Conveyors & Mills Equipment Testing

This page details the specific equipment testing requirements for Conveyors & Mills, as outlined in Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7426. These tests ensure the operational integrity and performance of the equipment.

**Rotation Test**
*   **Requirement:** A Rotation Test is required as FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package).
*   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Chemetics surveillance for the Rotation Test is designated as both Witness and Hold. This means a Chemetics representative **shall** be present to observe the test, and the process **shall** not proceed without their explicit approval after the test.
*   **Remarks:** During the Rotation Test, the run-out and freedom to rotate **shall** be checked.

**Additional Equipment Tests**
The scope of vendor quality assurance for Conveyors & Mills also includes the following tests:
*   Balancing
*   Run Test
*   Performance Test
*   Noise Data measurement
*   Motor Test

These tests contribute to the overall [[equipment_design_and_specification_4_prc_0003|equipment design and specification]] and ensure compliance with operational parameters. For general vendor quality assurance requirements, refer to [[0-QDS-7426_vendor_quality_assurance_requirements]].

**Rotation Test**
*   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Witness, Hold
*   **Remarks:** Check run-out & freedom to rotate.
