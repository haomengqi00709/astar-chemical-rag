# Pre-Travel Briefing and Medical Assessment

To enhance the safety and preparedness of employees, specific briefings and medical assessments are mandated for certain travel scenarios. These measures are crucial, especially when travelling to higher-risk regions, and form an integral part of the [[0-prc-8102_travel_health_and_safety|Travel Health, Safety, and Security Overview]].

**Pre-Travel Briefing Requirements:**
Employees with little or no experience travelling in High or Extreme risk areas **must** receive a pre-travel briefing. This briefing **shall** be conducted by the HSE Manager and **must** include, at a minimum, the following:
*   General safe travel precautions.
*   A situation update on possible risks in the area of travel, along with strategies to minimize risk exposure.
*   The best safe-travel advice offered by International SOS.
*   Details of [[travel_emergency_assistance_and_evacuation|evacuation plan arrangements]] and how to trigger the plan if necessary.
*   Information on intranet resources available for travellers.

**Medical Assessments and Vaccinations:**
The Aker Kvaerner Chemetics designated travel medical services provider **shall** assess whether any vaccinations are recommended for the specific travel destination(s).
*   If vaccinations are recommended, the travel medical services provider **shall** contact the traveller directly to inform them of these recommendations and arrange an appointment as needed.
*   If the planned duration of the trip is longer than three months, the travel medical services provider **shall** contact the traveller to schedule a foreign posting medical assessment and briefing.
*   Employees with existing medical conditions **are advised** to seek medical advice from their healthcare provider prior to any business travel.

These briefings and assessments ensure that employees are well-informed, medically prepared, and aware of the necessary precautions to take, thereby minimizing health and safety risks during their business trips.
