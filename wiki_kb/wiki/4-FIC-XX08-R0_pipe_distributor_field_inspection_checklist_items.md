# Pipe Distributor Field Inspection Checklist Items (4-FIC-XX08-R0)

This page details the specific inspection items for the Pipe Distributor Field Inspection Checklist, Document ID 4-FIC-XX08-R0. These items are crucial for verifying the correct installation and integrity of pipe distributor components during field inspections.

**Mandatory Inspection Requirements:**
The following items **must** be thoroughly checked to ensure compliance with design and safety standards:

1.  Distributor header and arm hangers **must** be properly adjusted, bolted, and tightened.
2.  The distributor **must** be leveled according to the provided engineering drawings.
3.  Gussets on piping **must** be checked for proper installation and integrity.
4.  Correct hardware and gaskets **must** be installed for all connections.
5.  All flanges **must** be checked for tightness. Flange torque values may be specified in relevant documentation and **shall** be adhered to if provided.
6.  Holes in the distributor arms **must** be pointing down to ensure correct flow distribution.
7.  The header and arms **must** be free from debris prior to installation to prevent blockages and operational issues.

The checklist currently contains 24 items in total. Items 8.0 through 24.0 are presently undefined. These sections are reserved for additional, project-specific inspection criteria that may be added as required.
