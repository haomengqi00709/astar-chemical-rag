# 0-SUB-6X00 Construction Subcontract Agreement

The 0-SUB-6X00 document represents the "Construction Subcontract Agreement." This agreement contains the contractual terms and conditions governing the relationship between the main contractor and the subcontractor for construction works.

A key aspect of this agreement, as referenced in the cost allocation specifications, is the determination of rates for Overhead and Profit. These rates, as stipulated in 0-SUB-6X00, **must** be used by the Subcontractor when presenting quotations for [[construction_subcontract_compensation_event_quotations]]. This ensures that all financial aspects of compensation events, including profit margins and overhead recovery, align with the overarching contractual terms.
