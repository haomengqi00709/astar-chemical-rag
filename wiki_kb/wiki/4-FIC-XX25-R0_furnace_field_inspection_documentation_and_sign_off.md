# Furnace Field Inspection Documentation and Sign-off (4-FIC-XX25-R0)

This page outlines the essential documentation and sign-off requirements for the Furnace Field Inspection Checklist (4-FIC-XX25-R0), ensuring proper record-keeping and accountability for the installation and quality verification of furnace equipment.

**Documentation Requirements:**
*   **NDT Inspection Records:** Records of all Non-Destructive Testing (NDT) inspections **must** be available. These records provide evidence of compliance with quality standards for welds and material integrity.
*   **Refractory/Brick Lining Inspection Report:** The completed refractory or brick lining inspection report **must** be attached to this checklist. This report verifies the proper application and integrity of the furnace lining.
*   **Final Internal Inspection Closure Certificate:** A closure certificate for the final internal inspection **must** be attached. This certificate confirms that the internal components of the furnace have been inspected and approved by the client prior to final closure.

**Deficiency Reporting:**
The checklist includes a dedicated section for recording up to 10 deficiencies and comments. Any identified non-conformances during the inspection **must** be documented here. For detailed procedures on managing and reporting non-conformances, refer to [[0-NCR-7XXX_template_non_conformance_report]].

**Sign-off Requirements:**
Formal sign-off is a mandatory step to confirm the completion and acceptance of the furnace field inspection. Signatures **must** be obtained from the following parties:
*   **Aker Kvaerner Chemetics:** A representative from [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_company_details]] **must** provide their name, signature, and the date of sign-off.
*   **Sub Contractor:** A representative from the Sub Contractor **must** provide their name, signature, and the date of sign-off. For general guidelines on subcontractor documentation, refer to [[subcontractor_document_standards]].
*   **Client:** A representative from the Client **must** provide their name, signature, and the date of sign-off.

The provision of names and dates for all required signatures is mandatory to validate the inspection process.

**Documentation Requirements:**
*   **NDT Inspection Records:** NDT inspection records must be available and attached.
*   **Refractory / Brick Lining Inspection Report:** The refractory / brick lining inspection report must be attached.
*   **Closure Certificate:** A closure certificate for the final internal inspection must be attached.

**Sign-off Requirements:**
The sign-off section requires signatures from Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, Sub Contractor, and Client. In addition to signatures, the names and dates for each party must also be provided.
