# Gas/Gas Heat Exchanger Stress Analysis and Material Selection

The stress analysis and material selection for gas/gas heat exchangers are critical aspects of the mechanical design, ensuring the structural integrity and longevity of the equipment.

**Stress Evaluation:**
*   **Primary Membrane Stresses:** These stresses **shall** be evaluated in accordance with [[gas_gas_heat_exchanger_mechanical_calculation_prerequisites#ASME(1)|ASME(1)]] Section VIII, Division 1.
*   **Local and Secondary Stresses:** This includes stress combinations such as membrane plus bending. These **shall** be evaluated per [[gas_gas_heat_exchanger_mechanical_calculation_prerequisites#ASME(2)|ASME(2)]] Section VIII, Division 2 (Figure 4-130.1).

**Material Selection:**
*   Generic shellside and tubeside materials are initially specified on the process data sheet.
*   The designer is solely responsible for specifying the particular material specification for each individual component of the heat exchanger. This selection **must** consider the operating conditions, corrosive environment, and stress levels.

**Design Methodology and Reference Data:**
*   The mechanical design of gas/gas exchanger components is a combination of proprietary methods developed by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the established guidelines of [[gas_gas_heat_exchanger_mechanical_calculation_prerequisites#ASME(1)|ASME(1)]] Section VIII, Division 1.
*   **Allowable Stress Values:** These **shall** be obtained from [[gas_gas_heat_exchanger_mechanical_calculation_prerequisites#ASME(3)|ASME(3)]] Section II, Part D.
*   **Physical Properties:** Relevant physical properties of materials **shall** also be taken from [[gas_gas_heat_exchanger_mechanical_calculation_prerequisites#ASME(3)|ASME(3)]] Section II, Part D.

Adherence to these standards and a meticulous approach to material selection are paramount for the safe and efficient operation of the heat exchanger.

For an overview of the entire mechanical calculation procedure, refer to [[4-prc-0009_gas_gas_exchanger_mechanical_calculations]].
