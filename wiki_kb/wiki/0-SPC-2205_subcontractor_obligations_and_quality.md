# Engineering Subcontractor Obligations and Quality (0-SPC-2205)

This page details the core obligations of the Subcontractor under document 0-SPC-2205, focusing on service provision, quality management, and adherence to instructions.

**Service Provision:**
*   The Subcontractor **must** Provide the Services strictly in accordance with the [[0-SPC-2205_general_conditions_and_administration#Key Definitions|Scope]].
*   The Subcontractor **must** exercise the skill and care typically expected from professionals providing similar services.
*   The Subcontractor **must** comply with all instructions issued by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, including those that modify the [[0-SPC-2205_general_conditions_and_administration#Key Definitions|Scope]].

**Personnel and Subcontracting:**
*   The Subcontractor **must** employ each key person named for the job specified in the [[0-SPC-2205_table_1_subcontract_data_reference|Subcontract Data]] or an Aker Kvaerner Chemetics-accepted replacement.
*   The Subcontractor **may not** further subcontract work without the explicit approval of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
*   If work is further subcontracted, the Subcontractor remains fully responsible for performing this Subcontract as if no further subcontracting had occurred.
*   For the purposes of this Subcontract, a [[0-SPC-2205_general_conditions_and_administration#Key Definitions|Subsubcontractor]]'s employees are considered the Subcontractor’s employees.

**Approvals and Completion:**
*   The Subcontractor **must** obtain necessary approvals from [[0-SPC-2205_general_conditions_and_administration#Key Definitions|Others]] to Provide the Services.
*   The Subcontractor **must** ensure that all work leads to [[0-SPC-2205_general_conditions_and_administration#Key Definitions|Completion]] on or before the [[0-SPC-2205_general_conditions_and_administration#Key Definitions|Completion Date]].

**Quality Management:**
*   The Subcontractor **must** operate a quality management system for Providing the Services as specified in the [[0-SPC-2205_general_conditions_and_administration#Key Definitions|Scope]].
*   This quality management system **must** comply with all requirements stated in the [[0-SPC-2205_general_conditions_and_administration#Key Definitions|Scope]]. For general quality assurance requirements, refer to [[0-SPC-7002_vendor_quality_assurance]].

**Defect Correction:**
*   The Subcontractor **must** correct any [[0-SPC-2205_general_conditions_and_administration#Key Definitions|Defects]] within a timeframe that minimizes adverse effects on Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or [[0-SPC-2205_general_conditions_and_administration#Key Definitions|Others]].
*   If, after [[0-SPC-2205_general_conditions_and_administration#Key Definitions|Completion]], the Subcontractor fails to correct a [[0-SPC-2205_general_conditions_and_administration#Key Definitions|Defect]] within the required time, Aker Kvaerner Chemetics will assess the cost of having the Defect corrected by other parties.
*   The Subcontractor **must** pay Aker Kvaerner Chemetics the assessed cost for such Defect correction if not performed by the Subcontractor.

For details on delay damages related to completion and documentation, see [[0-SPC-2205_delay_damages_and_defect_correction]].

**Health and Safety:**
*   The Subcontractor **must** act in accordance with the health and safety requirements.
*   The Subcontractor **must** notify Aker Kvaerner Chemetics as soon as he becomes aware that the Scope requires him to do anything which is illegal or impossible. If Aker Kvaerner Chemetics agrees, he **must** give an instruction to change the Scope appropriately.

**Standard of Care:**
*   The Subcontractor **must** use the skill and care normally used by professionals providing services similar to the services.

**Personnel and Subcontracting:**
*   The Subcontractor **must** either employ each key person named to do the job for him stated in the Subcontract Data or employ a replacement person who has been accepted by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
*   The Subcontractor may not further subcontract work without approval of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
*   If work is further Subcontracted, the Subcontractor is responsible for performing this Subcontract as if he had not further subcontracted.
*   This Subcontract applies as if a Subsubcontractor's employees were the Subcontractor’s.

**Approvals and Completion:**
*   The Subcontractor **must** obtain approval from Others where necessary to Provide the Services.
*   The Subcontractor **must** do the work so that Completion is on or before the Completion Date.
