# Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7404-3 Inspection and Documentation Requirements

This page outlines the specific inspection activities and associated Quality Record requirements for the Electrostatic Precipitator Polypropylene Piping project, as detailed in Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7404-3. Chemetics will witness several key stages of inspection and documentation.

**Required Inspections and Quality Records:**

*   **Material Test Certificates**
    *   **Quality Record Requirement:** FP ([[0-QDS-7404-3_quality_record_definitions|Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package]])
    *   **Chemetics Witness:** Yes. Chemetics **will** Witness the verification of Material Test Certificates.

*   **Hydrostatic Test of Piping**
    *   **Quality Record Requirement:** FP ([[0-QDS-7404-3_quality_record_definitions|Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package]])
    *   **Chemetics Witness:** Yes. Chemetics **will** Witness the Hydrostatic Test of Piping.

*   **Dimensional Inspection**
    *   **Quality Record Requirement:** FP ([[0-QDS-7404-3_quality_record_definitions|Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package]])
    *   **Chemetics Witness:** Yes. Chemetics **will** Witness the Dimensional Inspection.

*   **Contour & Finish of Welds Inspection**
    *   **Chemetics Witness:** Yes. Chemetics **will** Witness the inspection of weld contour and finish.

*   **Cleanliness Inspection**
    *   **Chemetics Witness:** Yes. Chemetics **will** Witness the Cleanliness Inspection.

*   **Review QA Documentation**
    *   **Chemetics Witness:** Yes. Chemetics **will** Witness the review of the Quality Assurance Documentation.

*   **Packaging**
    *   **Chemetics Witness:** Yes. Chemetics **will** Witness the Packaging process.

*   **Packing List**
    *   **Quality Record Requirement:** AP ([[0-QDS-7404-3_quality_record_definitions|As Prepared]])
    *   **Chemetics Witness:** Yes. Chemetics **will** Witness the Packing List.

These requirements are critical for ensuring the quality and integrity of the polypropylene piping components. For related inspection and documentation guidelines, refer to [[0-QDS-7404-1_inspection_and_documentation_requirements]], [[0-QDS-7404-2_inspection_and_documentation_requirements]], and [[0-qds-7402_strainer_hydrostatic_testing_and_final_inspection]].
