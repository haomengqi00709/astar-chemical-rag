# Professional Services Subcontract (0-SPC-2201) Communication and Documentation

Effective communication and meticulous documentation are critical under Professional Services Subcontract 0-SPC-2201. All instructions, certificates, submissions, proposals, records, acceptances, notifications, and replies required by this Subcontract **must** be communicated in a form that can be read, copied, and recorded. All written communications **must** be in the English language.

**Communication Protocols:**
*   A communication takes effect upon its receipt at the last address notified by the recipient for receiving communications. If no specific address is notified, it takes effect at the recipient's address stated in the [[subcontract_definitions_0_spc_2201|Subcontract Data]].
*   If this Subcontract requires a reply, the responding party **must** reply within the stipulated period, or within any other period specified in the Subcontract.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **must** reply to any communication submitted or resubmitted by the Subcontractor for acceptance. If the reply is not an acceptance, Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **must** state the reasons for non-acceptance.
*   The Subcontractor **must** resubmit the communication within the reply period, addressing Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' stated reasons. A valid reason for withholding acceptance may be the need for more information to fully assess the Subcontractor’s submission.
*   A notification required by this Subcontract **must** be communicated separately from other communications.

**Documentation and Record Keeping:**
*   The Subcontractor **must** retain copies of all drawings, specifications, reports, and other documents that record the services for a period of ten years. These copies **must** be retained in the form specified in the [[subcontract_definitions_0_spc_2201|Scope]].
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' acceptance of a communication or checking of the Subcontractor's work does not alter the Subcontractor’s liabilities or his responsibility to [[subcontract_definitions_0_spc_2201|Provide the Services]]. Withholding acceptance for a reason stated in this Subcontract is not considered a [[engineering_subcontract_compensation_events|compensation event]].

**Early Warning and Issue Resolution:**
*   Both Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Subcontractor **must** give an early warning by notifying the other as soon as either becomes aware of any matter that could:
    *   Increase the total of the [[subcontract_definitions_0_spc_2201|Prices]].
    *   Change the [[subcontract_definitions_0_spc_2201|Accepted Schedule]].
    *   Impair the usefulness of the services to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
*   Either party **must** notify the other immediately upon becoming aware of an ambiguity or inconsistency within or between the documents forming part of this Subcontract. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **must** then issue an instruction to resolve such ambiguity or inconsistency.

**Communication Standards:**
*   Writing is in the English language.
*   A communication has effect when it is received at the last address notified by the recipient for receiving communications. If no address is notified, a communication has effect at the address of the recipient stated in the *Subcontract Data*.
*   A notification which this Subcontract requires is communicated separately from other communications.

**Reply and Acceptance Procedures:**
*   If this Subcontract requires Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or the Subcontractor to reply to a communication, the reply must be within the period for reply, unless another period is stated in this Subcontract.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics replies to a communication submitted or resubmitted to him by the Subcontractor for acceptance.
*   If Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' reply is not acceptance, he states his reasons.
*   The Subcontractor resubmits the communication within the period for reply taking account of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' reasons.
*   A reason for withholding acceptance is that more information is needed in order to assess the Subcontractor’s submission fully.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may withhold acceptance of a submission by the Subcontractor. Withholding acceptance for a reason stated in this Subcontract is not a compensation event.

**Documentation Retention:**
*   The Subcontractor retains copies of drawings, specifications, reports and other documents which record the services for ten years. The copies are retained in the form stated in the Scope.

**Disclaimer on Acceptance and Checking:**
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’s acceptance of a communication from the Subcontractor does not change the Subcontractor’s liabilities or his responsibility to Provide the Services.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’s checking of the Subcontractor's work does not change the Subcontractor’s liabilities or his responsibility to Provide the Services.

**Early Warning and Ambiguity Resolution:**
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Subcontractor give an early warning by notifying the other as soon as either becomes aware of any matter which could increase the total of the Prices, change the Accepted Schedule, or impair the usefulness of the services to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or the Subcontractor notifies the other as soon as either becomes aware of an ambiguity or inconsistency in or between the documents which are part of this Subcontract. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics gives an instruction resolving the ambiguity or inconsistency.
