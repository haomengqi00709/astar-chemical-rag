# Equipment Safety and Maintenance Access

As outlined in document 4-SPC-0001, specific provisions **must** be made for the safe operation and maintenance of all equipment.

**Safety Devices and Connections**
The supply of all devices required for the safe operation of the equipment **shall be** the responsibility of the Vendor. The Vendor **will** provide Aker Kvaerner Chemetics with detailed information concerning any required connections, such as relief valve discharges to drain, to ensure proper integration into the plant's safety systems. This ensures that all necessary safety measures are in place and correctly interfaced.

**Lifting and Handling**
Provision **must** be made for the assisted lifting of all items of equipment, plant, and products weighing more than 25 kg (55 lb). All equipment and major sub-assemblies **shall have** designated lifting points clearly marked to facilitate safe handling during installation and maintenance.

**Maintenance Accessibility**
All items of equipment which **may require** removal for regular maintenance (once or more per year) and weighing more than 50 kg (110 lb) **must** be readily retrievable from its installed position. This retrieval **shall be** achievable by wheeled trolley, forklift, monorail trolley hoist, or crane, allowing transport onto road transport at grade level or to a designated position where maintenance can be performed safely and efficiently. This requirement minimizes downtime and enhances worker safety during routine servicing.

For general equipment design principles, refer to [[general_equipment_requirements_specification]].
