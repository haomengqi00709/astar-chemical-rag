# 0-QDS-7404-4 Vendor Quality Assurance Requirements

For the procurement of Electrostatic Precipitator Miscellaneous Items under Document ID 0-QDS-7404-4, strict vendor quality assurance (QA) requirements are in place to ensure compliance with project standards.

**Mandatory Requirements:**
*   The Vendor **shall** prepare a Quality Plan. This plan **must** be in accordance with the guidelines specified in [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment]]. The Quality Plan serves as a comprehensive document outlining the vendor's approach to quality management throughout the manufacturing and supply process.
*   The Vendor's Quality Assurance system **shall** comply with the specific requirements detailed in this Quality Data Sheet (0-QDS-7404-4).
*   The Vendor's Quality Assurance system **shall** also comply with the overarching requirements set forth in [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment]]. This ensures a consistent level of quality across all procured equipment and bulk materials.

**Quality Plan Requirement:**
The requirement for a Quality Plan is contingent on the project's needs. If a Quality Plan is not deemed necessary for a particular scope of supply, the corresponding cells in the Quality Data Sheet related to the Quality Plan **shall** be deleted. This flexibility allows for tailoring QA requirements to the complexity and criticality of the supplied items.

Adherence to these requirements is crucial for maintaining the integrity and performance of the Electrostatic Precipitator Miscellaneous Items. For a broader understanding of vendor quality assurance processes, refer to [[vendor_quality_assurance]].
