# Electrical Junction Box/Marshalling Cabinet Detail Drawings

Electrical Junction Box and Marshalling Cabinet Detail Drawings provide comprehensive information regarding the internal layout, wiring, and component arrangement within junction boxes and marshalling cabinets. These drawings are critical for the correct installation, termination, and troubleshooting of electrical and instrumentation signals.

Key details typically found in these drawings include:
*   Terminal block arrangements
*   Internal wiring diagrams
*   Component identification (e.g., relays, fuses, barriers)
*   Cable entry and exit points
*   Grounding connections
*   Enclosure specifications

During the [[9-PRC-0004_electrical_field_checkout_documentation|Electrical Field Checkout Procedure (9-PRC-0004)]], these drawings (e.g., [[instrument_junction_box_drawings]]) are used to verify that field wiring is correctly connected to the appropriate terminals, ensuring proper signal integrity and system functionality. They are essential for confirming that the physical installation matches the engineered design.
