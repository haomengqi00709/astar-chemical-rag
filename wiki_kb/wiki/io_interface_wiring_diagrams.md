# Process Control – I/O Interface Wiring Diagrams

**Process Control – I/O Interface Wiring Diagrams** provide essential details for the termination of field devices to specific I/O cards within the control system.

**Typical Document Number:** No typical document number
**Usage:** Construction

**Content Requirements:**
These wiring diagrams **WILL CONTAIN**:
*   Termination of field devices to the specific I/O cards.
*   I/O card type and location within the control layout.
*   Wire numbers.
*   Cable numbers.
*   Field device tag.
