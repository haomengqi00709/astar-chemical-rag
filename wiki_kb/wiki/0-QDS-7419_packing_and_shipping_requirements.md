# Acid Tower Field Erected Shop Component Packing and Shipping (0-QDS-7419)

Proper packing and shipping procedures are essential for the Acid Tower Field Erected Shop Component to prevent damage during transit and ensure all documentation is correctly managed. Packing/Crating is a required activity.

**Preparation for Shipping:**
*   **Vendor I & T Plan:** Preparation for shipping **is required** in the Vendor Inspection & Test Plan.
*   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Witness.

**Packing List:**
*   **Documentation:** A Packing List **is required** as AP (As Prepared).
*   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Witness.

These requirements ensure that the component is adequately protected and accounted for during its journey from the vendor's facility.

For general packing and shipping requirements, refer to documents such as [[0-QDS-7411_packing_and_shipping_requirements]].
