# Vendor Quality Assurance and Warranties

This page outlines the Vendor's responsibilities for maintaining a quality system and providing warranties for the [[purchase_order_definitions#The Goods and Services|Goods and Services]] supplied under the Purchase Order (PO).

**Quality System Requirements:**
*   The Vendor **must** plan, establish, and maintain a quality system that conforms with the quality system described in the Purchase Order.
*   The Vendor **must** grant the [[purchase_order_roles_and_responsibilities#Project Buyer|Project Buyer]], [[purchase_order_roles_and_responsibilities#Inspector|Inspector]], [[purchase_order_roles_and_responsibilities#Lead Engineer|Lead Engineer]], and [[purchase_order_definitions#The Owner|Owner]] access to the quality systems of both the Vendor and any [[purchase_order_definitions#A Supplier|Suppliers]] for monitoring and quality auditing purposes.

**Vendor Warranties:**
The Vendor provides the following warranties to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics:
*   The Goods and Services are fit and suitable for the purposes for which they are to be used, as described in the [[purchase_order_definitions#Goods and Services Information|Goods and Services Information]] and the [[purchase_order_definitions#Project Information|Project Information]].
*   The Vendor acknowledges that Aker Kvaerner Chemetics relies on the Vendor’s skill and judgment regarding the fitness and suitability of the Goods and Services.
*   The Vendor acknowledges that Aker Kvaerner Chemetics relies on the Vendor’s agreement to complete the whole of the Goods and Services by the [[purchase_order_definitions#The Delivery Date|Delivery Date]].
*   The Vendor’s usual business involves supplying goods and services of a similar nature to the Goods and Services provided under this PO.
*   The Goods and Services are of merchantable quality and are free of [[defect_management_process#Definition of a Defect|defects]].
*   Unless otherwise stated in the Goods and Services Information, all materials used in the Goods and Services are new and of the best quality.

**Benefit and Scope of Warranties:**
*   These warranties benefit Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ successors and assigns, as well as the Owner.
*   The warranties provided are in addition to any warranties that arise by operation of law.
