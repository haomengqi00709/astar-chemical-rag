# Progress Payments

Progress payments refer to a payment schedule where a contractor or vendor receives partial payments as specific milestones or stages of a project are completed, rather than a single lump sum upon final delivery. This method helps manage cash flow for the vendor and provides a mechanism for the client to monitor project advancement.

In the context of a Request for Quotation (RFQ), if a Bidder proposes payment terms other than full payment upon delivery, they **must** clearly state their proposed progress payment schedule. This schedule **must** include:
*   A detailed list of [[milestone_events]] to which each payment will be tied.
*   The specific value of each proposed payment.
*   The estimated duration in weeks after the contract award by which each milestone is expected to be achieved.

This structured approach to progress payments ensures transparency and allows Aker Kvaerner Chemetics to evaluate the financial implications and project timeline associated with the Bidder's offer.
