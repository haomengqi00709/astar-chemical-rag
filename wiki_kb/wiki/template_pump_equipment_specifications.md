# Template Pump Equipment Specifications

This page details the essential equipment specifications for a template pump, as outlined in document 4-PDS-XXXX-R2. These specifications guide the selection and design of the pump to ensure it meets operational requirements and integrates correctly into the process.

**General Equipment Data:**

*   **Pump Type:** The datasheet allows for selection from various pump types, accommodating diverse process needs. Available options include:
    *   Centrifugal Horizontal
    *   Centrifugal Vertical
    *   Axial Flow
    *   Screw
    *   Lobe
    *   Peristaltic
    *   Diaphragm
    *   Gear
    *   Piston
    *   Other (for types not explicitly listed)
*   **Impeller Type:** The type of impeller specified for the pump, which significantly impacts pump performance and handling of solids. Options include:
    *   Open
    *   Semi-Open
    *   Closed
    *   Recessed
    *   Other (for types not explicitly listed)
*   **Estimated Maximum Pressure at Suction:** The estimated maximum pressure at the pump's suction, specified in kPa(g) (kilopascals gauge). This is important for mechanical design and seal selection.
*   **Maximum Temperature for Mechanical Design:** The maximum temperature the pump's mechanical components are designed to withstand, in oC (degrees Celsius). This ensures structural integrity under extreme conditions.
*   **Vertical Pump Length:** For vertical pump types, the length of the pump in meters (m). This dimension is crucial for installation and layout planning.
*   **Seal Type:** The type of shaft seal used to prevent leakage of process fluid. Options include:
    *   Stuffing Box
    *   Single mechanical
    *   Double mechanical
    *   Magnetic Drive
    *   Lip
    *   Other (for types not explicitly listed)
*   **Seal Fluid:** The fluid used for the pump's seal, if applicable (e.g., for double mechanical seals).
*   **Emergency Motor Power:** Indicates whether the pump motor requires an emergency power supply (Yes/No) for critical applications or safety shutdowns.

These specifications are crucial for ensuring the pump's compatibility with the [[template_pump_process_design_conditions]] and overall system requirements.
