# Piping General Arrangement Drawings (5-GAD-XXXX)

Piping General Arrangement (GA) Drawings, identified as 5-GAD-XXXX, are essential layout drawings that provide a comprehensive overview of piping and equipment within a plant area. These drawings are crucial for visualizing the spatial relationships between components and for coordinating various engineering disciplines.

**Content Requirements**
Piping General Arrangement Drawings **shall** contain the following detailed information:
*   **Plan View of Piping and Equipment:** A top-down view illustrating the layout of piping and equipment, complete with bay line references and piping identification.
*   **Section View of Piping and Equipment:** Cross-sectional views providing vertical relationships of piping and equipment, also including bay line references and piping identification.
*   **Major Pipe Support Steel:** Identification and location of structural steel elements that support more than one pipe, indicating primary support structures.
*   **Location of Pipe Supports (Untagged):** General locations of individual pipe supports, even if they are not yet fully tagged or detailed on separate drawings. It is noted that no separate pipe support GA is typically provided.

**Roles and Responsibilities**
The preparation, checking, and approval of Piping General Arrangement Drawings are assigned to specific disciplines:
*   **Preparation:** The drawing **shall** be prepared by the Design discipline.
*   **Checking:** The drawing **shall** be checked by the Mechanical discipline.
*   **Approval:** The drawing **shall** be approved by the Design discipline.

These drawings are fundamental for overall plant layout, constructability reviews, and ensuring adequate access and maintenance space for piping systems.

**Responsibilities**
*   **Prepared by:** Design
*   **Checked by:** Mechanical
*   **Approved by:** Design
