# Horizontal Centrifugal Pump Process and Performance Requirements

Horizontal centrifugal pumps **must** meet the specific process requirements detailed in their individual [[template_horizontal_centrifugal_pump_datasheet_administration|Pump Data Sheets]].

**Minimum Continuous Flow (MCF)**
If a Pump Data Sheet specifies a flow rate value along with 'Vendor Minimum Continuous Flow' (MCF), the Vendor **must** state their recommended MCF rate. This recommended MCF rate **must** then be added by the Vendor to the flow rate value provided on the Data Sheet for design considerations.

**Head/Capacity Curves**
Pumps **must** exhibit stable head/capacity curves. These curves **must** continuously rise to shut-off. It is a mandatory requirement that pumps **must not** have a design duty point located to the right of the head/capacity curve's Best Efficiency Point (BEP) unless explicit approval is granted by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Noise Levels**
The Vendor **must** provide the estimated noise level of the pumps. This information is crucial for compliance with [[equipment_noise_limits]] and overall plant safety planning.

**Motor Sizing**
Motors **must** be appropriately sized for the run-out condition at the selected impeller size to ensure reliable operation across the pump's performance envelope.
