# Strainer Vendor Quality Assurance and Surveillance by Chemetics (0-QDS-7402)

Vendor Quality Assurance (QA) is a cornerstone of project success, ensuring that all supplied equipment and materials meet the stringent quality standards set by the client and engineering firm. For the Strainer specified in 0-QDS-7402, the Vendor's Quality Assurance program is subject to specific mandatory requirements and oversight by Chemetics.

**Vendor Quality Assurance Requirements:**
The Vendor **shall** adhere to the following mandatory requirements for Quality Assurance:
*   The Vendor **shall** prepare a Quality Plan in accordance with [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment]]. This plan outlines the specific practices, resources, and sequence of activities relevant to the quality of the Strainer.
*   The Vendor's Quality Assurance program **shall** comply with the requirements set forth in this Quality Data Sheet (0-QDS-7402).
*   The Vendor's Quality Assurance program **shall** also comply with the general guidelines and specifications detailed in [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment]].
*   The compliance of the Vendor's Quality Assurance with both this Quality Data Sheet and 0-SPC-7001 is associated with a value of 0.0.
*   A Quality Plan is not required if the cells related to it are deleted, indicating a streamlined process for certain scenarios.

For further information on general vendor quality assurance, refer to [[vendor_quality_assurance]].

**Chemetics Surveillance:**
Chemetics implements a structured surveillance program to monitor and verify the quality activities performed by vendors. For the Strainer (0-QDS-7402), this surveillance is mandated at Level 1.0, as detailed within this Quality Data Sheet. The overall surveillance approach is associated with a value of 2.0, indicating a comprehensive oversight strategy.

Chemetics surveillance **shall** be conducted as set out in this Quality Data Sheet, encompassing various stages of manufacturing and inspection. Specific surveillance activities by Chemetics include:
*   **Mill Test Certificates:** "Complete" surveillance.
*   **Weld consumable certs.:** "Complete" surveillance.
*   **Hydrostatic test:** "Complete" surveillance.
*   **Radiographic testing:** "Complete" surveillance.
*   **Dye Penetrant testing:** "Complete" surveillance.
*   **Dimensional inspection:** "Witness" and "Hold" surveillance.
*   **Contour & finish of welds inspection:** "Witness" and "Hold" surveillance.
*   **Surfaces free from defects:** "Witness" and "Hold" surveillance (does not include "Complete").
*   **Cleanliness:** "Witness" and "Hold" surveillance (does not include "Complete").
*   **Review QA Documentation:** "Witness" and "Hold" surveillance (does not include "Complete").
*   **Preparation for shipping:** "Witness" surveillance (does not include "Hold" or "Complete").
*   **Packing List:** "Complete" surveillance (does not include "Witness" or "Hold").

This detailed surveillance ensures that all critical aspects of the Strainer's production meet the specified quality benchmarks. For more information on Chemetics' surveillance protocols, refer to [[0-QDS-7_aker_kvaerner_surveillance]] and [[inspection_and_surveillance_activities]].

The Vendor is required to prepare a **Quality Plan** in accordance with document **0-SPC-7001: Vendor Quality Assurance**. Vendor's Quality Assurance must also comply with this specific 0-SPC-7001 document. It is important to note that a Quality Plan is not required if the related cells are deleted from the document. The compliance of Vendor's Quality Assurance with both the Quality Data Sheet and 0-SPC-7001 is associated with the value 0.0.

Chemetics' surveillance for this project is set to **Level 1.0**. This surveillance, as detailed in the Quality Data Sheet, is associated with the value 2.0.
