# Acid Cooler Final Inspections (0-QDS-7411)

This page details the final inspection requirements for the Acid Cooler, as specified in document [[0-QDS-7411_document_details|0-QDS-7411]]. All listed final inspection activities require surveillance (witness) by Chemetics Vancouver.

**Final Inspection Activities:**
*   **External Coating/Lining Inspection:** Applies to Carbon Steel.
*   **Dimensional Final Inspection**
*   **Contour & finish of welds Final Inspection**
*   **Surfaces free from defects Final Inspection**
*   **Cleanliness Final Inspection**
*   **Review QA Documentation Final Inspection**

These final inspections are crucial to ensure that the Acid Cooler meets all specified quality, dimensional, and surface finish requirements before shipment.
