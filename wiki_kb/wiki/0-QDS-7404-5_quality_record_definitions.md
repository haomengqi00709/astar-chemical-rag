# Quality Record Definitions for 0-QDS-7404-5

This page defines the specific quality record designations used within Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7404-5 for the Electrostatic Precipitator Shell & Head Sections-FRP project. These definitions clarify the required documentation status for various inspection and testing activities.

**Quality Record Designations:**

*   **"AP" (As Prepared):** This designation means the quality record **must** be provided "As Prepared" by the Vendor. It indicates that the document is generated by the Vendor and submitted as part of the quality documentation.
*   **"FP" (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package):** This designation means the quality record **must** be "Included in the Quality Assurance Documentation Package." It signifies that the document is a mandatory component of the final quality assurance dossier.

These definitions are crucial for managing [[quality_data_sheets_management]] and ensuring that all required documentation is correctly categorized and submitted.
