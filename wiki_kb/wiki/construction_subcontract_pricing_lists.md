# Construction Subcontract Pricing Lists

Construction Subcontract Pricing Lists are essential documents that define the financial basis of a construction subcontract, whether it's a lump sum or unit price agreement. These lists are directly referenced by the [[construction_subcontract_agreement_template_and_content]] and are crucial for bid evaluation, progress claims, and overall cost control.

**Key Pricing Lists:**

1.  **0-LST-6X11: Construction Subcontract Price List**
    *   **Purpose:** This list defines how the subcontract price is structured.
        *   For a lump sum subcontract, it details the breakdown of the total price by the Subcontractor.
        *   For a unit price subcontract, it sets the quantities for each unit of the works. This allows for standard unit definitions contained in [[construction_subcontract_pricing_unit_price_0_spc_6005]].
    *   **Function:** The Price List will form the basis for the Subcontractor’s progress claims throughout the project.
    *   **Numbering:** This list **must** be numbered according to [[document_numbering]] (0-PRC-0002). Its identification number **must** be identical to that used for the Construction Subcontract Agreement, as specified in [[construction_subcontract_agreement_procedure_0_prc_6002]].
    *   **Initial Status & Approval:** It will be published (0-PRC-0005: [[publishing_project_documents]]) with status “Issued for Quotation”. It **must** be checked by the Project Engineer and approved by the Project Manager.
    *   **Revision & Final Approval:** The Subcontract Administrator will revise this list based on the successful Subcontractor's tender and any negotiated modifications. The revised list will be published (0-PRC-0005) with status “Approved for Construction”, checked by the Project Engineer, and approved by the Project Manager.
    Data available in SQL: 0-LST-6X11

2.  **Other Pricing Lists for Subcontractor Input:**
    The following lists are designed for information insertion by the Subcontractor to better define the basis for their bid:
    *   **0-LST-6X12: Construction Subcontract Labour Rates:** Details the proposed labor rates. Data available in SQL: 0-LST-6X12
    *   **0-LST-6X13: Construction Subcontract Crew Rates:** Specifies rates for various work crews. Data available in SQL: 0-LST-6X13
    *   **0-LST-6X14: Construction Subcontract Indirect Labour Schedule:** Outlines costs associated with indirect labor. Data available in SQL: 0-LST-6X14
    *   **0-LST-6X15: Construction Subcontract Equipment Schedule:** Provides details on equipment usage and rates. Data available in SQL: 0-LST-6X15

    *   **Templates:** The standard templates for these lists contain sample data demonstrating how they are to be completed by the Subcontractor.
    *   **Instructions:** [[instructions_to_construction_subcontractors_for_tender_0_spc_6002]] contains instructions on the proper use and completion of these lists.
    *   **Numbering:** These lists **must** be numbered according to [[document_numbering]] (0-PRC-0002). The "X" in their identification numbers **must** be replaced by the same digit as used for the Subcontract Agreement (as per [[construction_subcontract_agreement_procedure_0_prc_6002]]).
    *   **Initial Status & Approval:** They will be published (0-PRC-0005) with status “Issued for Quotation”. No checking or approval is required for these lists at this initial stage.
    *   **Revision & Final Approval:** The Subcontract Administrator will revise these lists based on the successful Subcontractor's tender and any negotiated modifications. The revised lists will be published (0-PRC-0005) with status “Approved for Construction”, checked by the Project Engineer, and approved by the Project Manager.
