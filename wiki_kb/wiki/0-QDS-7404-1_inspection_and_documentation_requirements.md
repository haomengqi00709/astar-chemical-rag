# 0-QDS-7404-1 Inspection and Documentation Requirements

This page details the specific quality record requirements and Chemetics' surveillance levels for various inspection and testing activities related to the Electrostatic Precipitator Shell & Head Sections-Dual Laminate, as outlined in Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7404-1.

**Quality Record Definitions:**
*   **AP**: "As Prepared" - The record is prepared by the vendor.
*   **FP**: "Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package" - The record must be part of the final QA documentation package.

**Inspection and Documentation Requirements:**

*   **Material Test Certificates**
    *   **Quality Record**: FP
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance**: Complete
    *   **Note**: Not required in the Vendor Inspection & Test (I & T) Plan.

*   **Filament Winding Pattern**
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance**: Witness
    *   **Note**: Not required in the Vendor I & T Plan.

*   **Bond Testing**
    *   **Quality Record**: FP
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance**: Complete
    *   **Note**: Not required in the Vendor I & T Plan.

*   **Welder Qualification**
    *   **Quality Record**: FP
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance**: Complete
    *   **Note**: Not required in the Vendor I & T Plan.

*   **Spark Testing**
    *   **Quality Record**: FP
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance**: Witness, Hold
    *   **Note**: Not required in the Vendor I & T Plan.

*   **External Coating/Lining Inspection**
    *   **Quality Record**: FP
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance**: Witness, Hold
    *   **Note**: Not required in the Vendor I & T Plan.

*   **Internal Coating/Lining Inspection**
    *   **Quality Record**: FP
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance**: Witness, Hold
    *   **Note**: Not required in the Vendor I & T Plan.

*   **Dimensional Final Inspection**
    *   **Quality Record**: FP
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance**: Witness, Hold
    *   **Note**: Not required in the Vendor I & T Plan.

*   **Surfaces free from defects Final Inspection**
    *   **Quality Record**: FP
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance**: Witness, Hold
    *   **Note**: Not required in the Vendor I & T Plan.

*   **Cleanliness Final Inspection**
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance**: Witness, Hold
    *   **Note**: Not required in the Vendor I & T Plan.

*   **Review QA Documentation Final Inspection**
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance**: Witness, Hold
    *   **Note**: Not required in the Vendor I & T Plan.

*   **Packaging**
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance**: Witness
    *   **Note**: Not required in the Vendor I & T Plan.

*   **Packing List**
    *   **Quality Record**: AP
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance**: Witness
    *   **Note**: Not required in the Vendor I & T Plan.

These requirements ensure comprehensive quality control throughout the manufacturing and inspection process. For general vendor quality assurance requirements, refer to [[0-QDS-7404-1_vendor_quality_assurance_requirements]]. The overall Chemetics surveillance level is detailed in [[0-QDS-7404-1_chemetics_surveillance_level]].
