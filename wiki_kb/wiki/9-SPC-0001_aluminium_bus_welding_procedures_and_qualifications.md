# Aluminium Bus Welding Procedures and Qualifications

Adherence to established and qualified welding procedures is critical for all Aluminium Bus welding activities under Standard 9-SPC-0001.

**Welding Procedure Requirements**
Welding procedure(s) **shall** be provided by the fabricator for review and agreement by the [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_quality_assurance_and_materials_engineering_group|Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Q.A. & Materials Group]] prior to the commencement of any work. Once procedure parameters are established, changes in essential variables, as defined by [[ASME_section_ix_welding_and_brazing_qualifications|ASME Code, Section IX]] or [[csa_w47_2_aluminium_welding_qualification_code|CSA W47.2]], **shall not** be permitted. Each procedure **shall** clearly show the joint preparation and a weld bead sketch.

Weld procedures are recommended to be organized as set out in [[ASME_section_ix_welding_and_brazing_qualifications|Section IX of the ASME Code]]. The specific AWS filler metal grade **must** be shown on every weld procedure.

**Welder Qualifications**
All welders employed on Aker Kvaerner Chemetics work **shall** be experienced in welding Aluminium. Furthermore, all welders **shall** be suitably qualified in accordance with relevant codes and standards for every procedure to be used. For field welding, any uncertified welder **shall** pass Qualification Tests as laid out in [[csa_w47_2_aluminium_welding_qualification_code|CSA W47.2]] prior to commencing production welding.

Upon request, the fabricator **shall** supply Aker Kvaerner Chemetics with objective evidence of current welder qualifications. All qualified welders **shall** be allocated an identification symbol for traceability.

**Welding Procedure Requirements**
Change in essential variables (ASME Code, Section IX or CSA W47.2) **shall not** be permitted once procedure parameters are established. The procedure **shall** show joint preparation and weld bead sketch. Weld procedures are recommended to be organized as set out in Section IX of the ASME Code.

**Welder Qualifications**
All welders employed on Aker Kvaerner Chemetics work **shall** be experienced in welding Aluminum. For field welding, any uncertified welder **shall** pass Qualification Tests laid out in CSA W47.2 prior to production welding. All qualified welders **shall** be allocated an identification symbol.

**Filler Metal Specification in Procedures**
A specific AWS filler metal grade **must** be shown on every weld procedure.
