# Electrical Cable Schedules

Electrical cable schedules are essential engineering documents that provide detailed information about all cables used within a plant or project. These schedules are crucial for procurement, installation, and maintenance, ensuring that the correct cables are specified and installed according to design requirements.

A typical electrical cable schedule includes:
*   Cable tag numbers
*   Source and destination equipment
*   Cable type (e.g., power, control, instrumentation)
*   Conductor size and number
*   Insulation and jacket material
*   Voltage rating
*   Length
*   Routing information

During the [[9-PRC-0004_electrical_field_checkout_documentation|Electrical Field Checkout Procedure (9-PRC-0004)]], electrical cable schedules (e.g., [[power_and_control_cables_list]]) are referenced to verify that cables have been installed correctly, terminated properly, and match the specified design. This ensures the integrity and functionality of the entire electrical system.
