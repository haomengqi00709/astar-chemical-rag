# SARAMET Piping Non-Destructive Examination (NDE) and Testing Requirements

This page details the Non-Destructive Examination (NDE) and testing requirements for SARAMET 23 (UNS S30601) and SARAMET 35 (UNS S32615) piping systems, as specified in document 5-SPC-0124.

**General NDE Requirements**
*   NDE requirements specified herein are in addition to those outlined in [[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping]] (latest edition).
*   These requirements also supersede random X-ray (RT) testing typically specified for Normal Fluid Service.
*   The primary objective of NDE is to ensure that the piping system is supplied free of significant defects.
*   All NDE requirements applicable to SARAMET 23 also apply to SARAMET 35, with additional specific requirements for SARAMET 35 as noted below.

**SARAMET 23 (UNS S30601) NDE and Hydrotesting**
*   **Radiographic Testing (RT):** RT **shall** be performed in accordance with [[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping]].
    *   A minimum of 5% radiography is required for long seams.
    *   A minimum of 1% radiography is required for circumferential seams.
    *   Weld tees are preferred locations for RT.
    *   Film used for spot RT **shall** be a minimum of 430 mm (17 inches) long.
*   **Liquid Penetrant Testing (PT):** Final condition liquid penetrant testing (PT) of all weld surfaces (inside and outside) is required.
*   **Hydrotesting:** Hydrotesting is required.
    *   Hydro-test water **shall** be drained immediately after the final hydrotest.
    *   The piping system and components **shall** be blown dry with air immediately after the final hydrotest.

**SARAMET 35 (UNS S32615) NDE and Hydrotesting**
*   **Liquid Penetrant Testing (PT):**
    *   PT **shall** be performed using solvent removable penetrant only.
    *   The minimum dwell time (soak time) for the penetrant is 1 hour.
    *   All interpretation of PT results **shall** be performed by certified inspectors only. Certified inspectors **must** be equivalent to CGSB level 2 minimum.
*   **Radiographic Testing (RT):** All weld tees **shall** be RT tested along the long axis of the pipe.
*   **Hydrotesting:** The hold time for hydrotesting **shall** be a minimum of 24 hours.
