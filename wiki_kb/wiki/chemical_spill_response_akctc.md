# Chemical Spill Response - AKCTC

This page details the procedures for responding to an accidental release of a chemical at the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Technology Centre (AKCTC).

**Immediate Actions:**
*   AKCTC personnel **shall** alert other personnel of the potential hazard.
*   Turn off any heat sources.
*   Ventilate the area as necessary.
*   Use proper protective equipment (e.g., gloves and personal respirators, face shields, etc.).
*   Attempt to contain the release in order to counteract, neutralize, or absorb the chemical.
*   Refer to the Material Safety Data Sheet (MSDS) for specific cleanup methods for the chemical involved.

**Escalation and External Services:**
*   Depending on the severity of the spill, call 9-1-1 (Fire Department).
*   Depending on the severity of the spill, follow the [[fire_emergency_response_akctc#Major_Fire_Response]] procedure, which may include evacuation.

**Reporting Requirements:**
*   Refer to [[akctc_safety_manual_appendix_o_table_1]] in Appendix O of the AKCTC Safety Manual to determine if a spill is reportable and which agency(ies) should be notified.
*   The "Spill Reporting Regulation" (B.C. Reg. 166/93), an official guideline from the B.C. Waste Management Act, **must** be complied with in the province of British Columbia.

**Environmental Release:**
*   If a release is to the environment (i.e., sewer or outside the building) and is substantial, cordon-off the spill area.
*   Call the City of Burnaby Chemical Spill/Sewer Emergency 24-hour line at (604) 294-7200. The AKCTC Supervisor should preferably make this call.
*   Be prepared to provide the following information:
    *   Your name and contact phone number.
    *   Name and telephone number of the person who caused the spill.
    *   Location and time of the spill.
    *   Type and quantity of the substance spilled.
    *   Cause and effect of the spill.
    *   Details of action taken or proposed.
    *   Description of the spill location and surrounding area.
    *   Names of agencies on scene.
    *   Names of other persons or agencies advised concerning the spill.
*   If unsure of the answer to any requested questions, respond by saying "I do not know at this time".

**Company Notification for External Agency Involvement:**
If outside agencies were notified, contact the Company’s president. If the president is unavailable, contact the HSE Manager. If the HSE Manager is unavailable, contact Legal Counsel. If Legal Counsel is unavailable, contact the VP-Finance.

For an overview of emergency response, refer to [[0-PRC-8010_office_emergency_response_overview]].
