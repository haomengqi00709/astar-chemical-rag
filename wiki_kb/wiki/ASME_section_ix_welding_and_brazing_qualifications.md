# ASME Section IX - Welding and Brazing Qualifications

ASME Boiler & Pressure Vessel Code (BPVC) Section IX provides rules relating to the qualification of welders, welding operators, brazers, and brazing operators, and the procedures that they employ in welding and brazing. It establishes the essential variables that, if changed, require requalification of a welding or brazing procedure.

This section of the ASME BPVC is referenced in [[4-SPC-0014_referenced_codes_and_definitions]] and specifically in [[4-SPC-0014_welding_procedure_and_welder_qualifications]] for:
*   Defining essential variables that necessitate resubmission of welding procedures.
*   Ensuring welders are suitably qualified in accordance with relevant codes and standards.
*   Requiring tack welds to be performed by ASME code qualified welders.

For detailed requirements on welding and brazing qualifications, consult the latest edition of ASME Section IX.
