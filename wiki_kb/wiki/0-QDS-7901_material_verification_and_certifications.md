# Material Verification and Certifications for MV Switchgear & DC Control Power Supply

For the Medium Voltage Switchgear & DC Control Power Supply project, specific material verification and certification requirements apply, particularly for critical components.

**Copper Bus Bars:**
Material Test Certificates are **required** for all Copper bus bars. These certificates **must** meet the following Quality Record and surveillance requirements:
*   **Quality Record:** [[0-QDS-7901_quality_record_definitions|AP (As Prepared)]]
*   **Chemetics Surveillance:** [[0-QDS-7901_chemetics_surveillance_level|Witness]]
*   **Chemetics Surveillance:** [[0-QDS-7901_chemetics_surveillance_level|Hold]]

These requirements ensure that the materials used comply with the specified standards and project requirements, with appropriate oversight from Chemetics.
