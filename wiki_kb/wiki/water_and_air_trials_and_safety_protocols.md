# Water and Air Trials and Safety Protocols

Water and air trials are a crucial phase in commissioning, involving the operation of the plant using water and air instead of actual process fluids. During these trials, all utilities are energized.

**Purpose of Water and Air Trials**
These trials are designed to demonstrate several key aspects of plant functionality:
*   Operability of utility systems and their controls.
*   Hydraulic function of process piping and equipment.
*   Operability of process controls.
*   Functionality of interlocks and emergency shutdown systems.

Water and air trials are conducted in strict accordance with [[water_and_air_trials_1_prc_0006]]: Water and Air Trials (1-PRC-0006). This procedure mandates that the plant be divided into sub-systems for the trials, with these sub-systems clearly identified in marked-up Piping and Instrument Diagrams (P&IDs). These marked-up P&IDs **must** also include any requirements for temporary bypasses, jumpers, and hoses. Records, including checklists and marked-up P&IDs, are meticulously maintained throughout the water and air trials.

During these trials, utility systems are energized, and water is pumped through process piping and equipment at elevated pressures and temperatures to simulate operational conditions.

**Safety Protocols During Trials**
Controlling safety risks during water and air trials is paramount. The following safety procedures are strictly enforced under the direction of the Commissioning Manager:
*   [[isolation_and_preparation_for_maintenance_1_prc_0007]]: Isolation and Preparation for Maintenance (1-PRC-0007).
*   [[safe_work_permits_1_prc_0009]]: Safe Work Permits (1-PRC-0009).
*   [[field_lockout_and_tagout_9_prc_0019]]: Field Lockout and Tagout (9-PRC-0019).

**Transition to Chemical Commissioning**
Upon successful completion of the water and air trials, agreement **must** be reached between the Owner and the Commissioning Manager that the plant is complete and ready for the introduction of raw materials and treatment chemicals. This agreement **will** be formally made and recorded in accordance with the protocols set out in the Contract, marking the transition to [[chemical_commissioning_and_production_energization]].
