# Project Change Management Roles and Responsibilities

Effective execution of the [[0-PRC-4003-R1_project_change_management_flow_diagram|Project Change Management Flow Diagram]] relies on clear roles and responsibilities across various project stakeholders and departments. Key entities involved in the process include:

*   **Client:** Initiates change requests and provides approval for significant project modifications.
*   **Project Manager:** Accepts client change requests, initiates PCNs, and reviews the change log with the project team for pending changes.
*   **Project Team Member:** Identifies potential changes and initiates PVNs/PCNs.
*   **Project Control Specialist:** Registers and distributes PVNs/PCNs, incorporates impact assessments, issues variation reports, and updates project control systems (cost, schedule, progress) and the change log.
*   **Discipline Leads:** Evaluate man-hours and schedule impacts of proposed changes.
*   **Estimator:** Estimates the total cost of changes when required.
*   **Project Risk Management:** Reviews changes, assesses risks, and determines contingency requirements, updating the risk register as necessary.
*   **Project Engineer:** Coordinates the evaluation of man-hours and schedule impacts, and provides approval for changes.
*   **Client Project Manager:** Provides approval for changes.
*   **Director of Projects:** Provides approval for changes as required.
*   **Project Controls Manager:** Provides approval for changes.
*   **Engineering Management:** An entity involved in the overall Project Change Management Flow Diagram.
