# Inspection and NDE Requirements (4-SPC-0015)

Inspection and Non-Destructive Examination (NDE) for fabrications under Standard 4-SPC-0015 **must** adhere to the following stringent requirements to ensure quality and compliance.

**Inspector Access and Authority**
*   The Inspector **will have free access** to the Vendor's work and to his Subvendor's (if work is sublet by approval) at all reasonable times. Access **will also be given** to Authorized Representatives of the End-User.
*   The Inspector **will be at liberty** to inspect the work during fabrication at any stage in manufacture and to reject any material or workmanship that does not conform to this Standard or the applicable code.
*   Inspection by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **does not relieve** the Fabricator of responsibility in accordance with the requirements of this Standard, engineering design, and the applicable code.

**Material and Weld Chemical Analysis**
*   The Supplier **must make available** to the Inspector copies of chemical certification for each batch of electrodes or wire coils used.
*   If electrodes, wire, etc., cannot be identified to the Inspector's satisfaction, samples **will be removed** for chemical analysis at the supplier's account.
*   A spot check analysis **will be performed** on production welds of critical equipment (pressure vessels, heat exchangers, pressure piping, and certain proprietary items) when required by purchase order or where the fabricator cannot identify the weld material. This also includes non-pressure retaining equipment in dangerous or demanding services.
*   The weld(s) to be checked **will normally be selected** at random by the Inspector.
*   Where results of chemical analysis show that a specific weldment falls outside of the approved specification or code requirements, the supplier **will promptly advise** Aker Kvaerner Chemetics in writing of his proposed course of action. This **must** include details of any additional testing necessary to fully define the extent of the non-conformance. All repairs and retests **will be** to the supplier's account.

**Liquid Penetrant (PT) Examination**
*   Items fabricated in accordance with [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels]] **will be PT tested** on all welds.
*   Non-coded items **will have** butt and fillet welds, nozzle attachment welds, etc., 100 percent PT examined on all surfaces exposed to the process, for cracks, porosity, slag inclusions, significant undercutting, or other major defects, unless otherwise specified on the purchase order, approved drawings, or Standards.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics reserves the right to select the location of the above inspection.
*   All welds between dissimilar metals **will be 100% PT examined**, unless otherwise specified in the Purchase Order or approved drawings.
*   Tube to tubesheet welds **will be 100% PT examined**.
*   The PT exam **will be carried out** in accordance with a written procedure.
*   The limit of acceptability for PT **will satisfy** [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels]], Appendix VIII, except that linear indications **will be considered unacceptable**.
*   All excavated areas **will be PT examined** prior to building up flush with the surface of the base material. Building up excavated areas **must** use the welding procedure previously approved by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for the main welding details.
*   All weld repair surfaces **will be 100% PT examined** per the above requirements.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics reserves the right to accept or reject any doubtful or questionable indications.

**Radiographic Testing (RT) Examination**
*   RT Examination **must** be carried out in accordance with a written procedure and by personnel qualified to appropriate national standards.
*   **ASME Code Section VIII, Div. 1 Equipment:**
    *   Equipment with plate or shell thickness at the welded joint(s) exceeding 3/8" **will have** all butt welds 100% radiographed in accordance with Para. UW-51.
    *   Equipment not exceeding 3/8" plate thickness **will be spot radiographed** in accordance with Para. UW-52(b).
*   **Non-ASME Coded Equipment:**
    *   Spot radiography **will be the minimum requirement**.
    *   Items fabricated to this Standard only (not ASME, etc.) **will be spot radiographed** in accordance with UW-52 (b) if required by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics engineering drawings.
*   **Piping:**
    *   For piping fabricated in accordance with [[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping]] and this Standard, the minimum standard of radiography **will be random radiography** as defined in Para. 341.4.1 (b).
    *   20% of all butt welds **will be radiographed**.
    *   Items fabricated to this Standard only (not ASME, etc.) **will be 20% random radiographed** per B31.3 if required by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics engineering drawings.
*   **Tankage:**
    *   For tankage fabricated in accordance with [[API_620_low_pressure_storage_tanks]], minimum requirements for this Specification **will be spot radiography**.
*   Junctions between circumferential and longitudinal seams are preferred locations for spot radiography.
*   If additional spot radiography is required as defined in UW-52 Sub-para. (d), costs of this radiography, all repair costs, and re-radiography **will be** to the Fabricator’s account.

**Helium Mass Spectrometer Testing**
*   Tube/tube sheet joints and tube bundles **will be tested** using the helium mass spectrometer (reverse probe method) per [[ASME_section_v_non_destructive_examination]], Article 10, when required by purchase order or referenced drawings.

These detailed NDE requirements ensure that all welds meet the necessary quality standards for the intended service.

**Chemical Analysis and Material Identification**
*   Supplier **must** make available to the Inspector copies of chemical certification for each batch of electrodes or wire coils used.
*   If electrodes, wire, etc., cannot be identified to the Inspector's satisfaction, samples **will** be removed for chemical analysis at supplier's account.
*   A spot check analysis **will** be performed on production welds of critical equipment (pressure vessels, heat exchangers, pressure piping and certain proprietary items) when required by purchase order or where the fabricator cannot identify the weld material. Spot check analysis **will** also include non-pressure retaining equipment in dangerous or demanding services.
*   The weld(s) to be checked **will** normally be selected at random by the Inspector.
*   Where results of chemical analysis show that a specific weldment falls outside of the approved specification or code requirements, the supplier **will** promptly advise Aker Kvaerner Chemetics in writing of his proposed course of action. Proposed course of action **must** include details of any additional testing necessary to fully define the extent of the non-conformance.
*   All repairs and retests **will** be to the supplier's account.

**Liquid Penetrant Testing (PT)**
*   Items fabricated in accordance with the ASME Code Section VIII, Div. 1, **will** be PT tested on all welds.
*   Non-coded items **will** have butt and fillet welds, nozzle attachment welds, etc., 100 percent PT examined on all surfaces exposed to the process, for cracks, porosity, slag inclusions, significant undercutting or other major defects, unless otherwise specified on the purchase order, approved drawings or Standards. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics reserves the right to select the location of the above inspection.
*   All welds between dissimilar metals **will** be 100% PT examined, unless otherwise specified in the Purchase Order or approved drawings.
*   Tube to tubesheet welds **will** be 100% PT examined.
*   The PT exam **will** be carried out in accordance with a written procedure.
*   The limit of acceptability for PT **will** satisfy the ASME Code Section VIII, Division 1, Appendix VIII, except that linear indications **will** be considered unacceptable.
*   All excavated areas **will** be PT examined prior to building up flush with the surface of the base material. Building up excavated areas **must** use the welding procedure previously approved by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for the main welding details.
*   All weld repair surfaces **will** be 100% PT examined per the above requirements.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics reserves the right to accept or reject any doubtful or questionable indications.

**Radiographic Testing (RT)**
*   Equipment fabricated in accordance with the ASME Code **will** have all butt welds 100% radiographed in accordance with Para. UW-51 of Section VIII, Division 1 of the ASME Code where the plate or shell thickness at the welded joint(s) exceeds 3/8".
*   Equipment fabricated to the ASME Code, but not exceeding 3/8" plate thickness, **will** be spot radiographed in accordance with Para. UW-52(b) of Section VIII, Division 1.
*   Spot radiography **will** be the minimum requirement for equipment not fabricated to the ASME Code.
*   For piping fabricated in accordance with ASME Codes and this Standard, the minimum standard of radiography **will** be random radiography as defined in Para. 341.4.1 (b) of ASME B31.3, with 20% of all butt welds radiographed.
*   For tankage fabricated in accordance with API Standard 620, minimum requirements for this Specification **will** be spot radiography.
*   Items fabricated to this Standard only (not ASME, etc.) **will** be spot radiographed in accordance with UW-52 (b) if required by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics engineering drawings. For piping, if required by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics engineering drawings, 20% random radiography per B31.3 **will** be performed.
*   RT Examination **must** be carried out in accordance with a written procedure and by personnel qualified to appropriate national standards.
*   If additional spot radiography is required as defined in UW-52 Sub-para. (d), costs of this radiography, all repair costs, and re-radiography **will** be to Fabricator’s account.
*   Junctions between circumferential and longitudinal seams are preferred locations for spot radiography.

**Helium Mass Spectrometer Testing**
*   Tube/tube sheet joints and tube bundles **will** be tested using the helium mass spectrometer (reverse probe method) per ASME Code Section V, Article 10 when required by purchase order or referenced drawings.

**Documentation Requirements**
*   Fabricator **must** furnish a report containing all data pertaining to each vessel or item.
*   Prior to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' final inspection, Fabricator **must** have prepared three sets of records (except final test data) in finished form for each unit.
*   Documentation **must** include Material mill test reports, Nameplate rubbing, Hydrostatic test report, and a copy of all inspection and test reports.
*   Reports and final test data **must** be assembled into three complete sets for each item.
*   A documentation package **must not** pertain to more than one vessel or item number.
*   The Inspector **will** collect the appropriate number of documentation packages for each item tested at the time of witnessing final tests and certifying correctness of forms.
