# 0-POL-9114 Vacation Policy Overview

This document, identified as 0-POL-9114, outlines the comprehensive guidelines and procedures for vacation time for eligible employees at Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. It serves as the primary reference for understanding vacation entitlements, accrual, scheduling, and related policies within the company.

The policy references [[0-pol-9114_table_1_contents]] for additional supplementary details that support the overall framework of the vacation policy.

**Policy Alignment and Scope**
This vacation policy is established in accordance with Aker Kvaerner Policy 11.1 Group People Policy. This ensures that the local vacation guidelines are consistent with and uphold the broader corporate human resources principles and standards set by the Aker Kvaerner Group.

**Applicability:** This policy applies to all regular employees working at Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ Vancouver office. It does not extend to temporary staff, contractors, or employees in other office locations unless explicitly stated otherwise by specific local policies.

**Company Philosophy on Vacation**
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics firmly believes that vacation time is necessary and highly desirable for the physical and mental health of its employees. The company recognizes that regular breaks from work are crucial for rejuvenation, stress reduction, and overall well-being.

Furthermore, Aker Kvaerner Chemetics actively supports the creation of an appropriate balance between professional responsibilities and life outside of work. This commitment to work-life balance is a core principle, aiming to foster a healthy, productive, and sustainable work environment for all its regular employees in the Vancouver office.

This policy is rooted in the belief that vacation time is essential for the physical and mental well-being of employees, and Aker Kvaerner Chemetics actively supports a healthy work-life balance. The policy aligns with Aker Kvaerner Policy 11.1 Group People Policy and is specifically applicable to all regular employees based in Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ Vancouver office.
