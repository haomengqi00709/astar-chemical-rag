# Site Work Hours and Rest Periods (0-POL-9106)

Employees required to work on site are subject to distinct regular hours of work during site assignments, which differ from the standard [[0-pol-9106_normal_work_schedule]] and [[0-pol-9106_flexible_work_schedule]] arrangements. These regular site hours will be set on a case-by-case basis to accommodate project-specific demands.

**Regular Site Hours and Rest Requirements:**
Regular site hours may extend up to 60 hours per week. Crucially, these regular site hours **must** include a minimum of one day of rest per week.

**Additional Hours and Maximum Limits:**
Staff may be required to work additional hours beyond the established regular site hours. However, strict maximum limits are enforced to ensure employee well-being and compliance:
*   The maximum work hours in any one week period is 75 hours.
*   The maximum work hours in any one day is 16 hours.
*   A maximum of 16 hours in any one day **must** never be on consecutive days.
*   A minimum rest period between work periods is 8 hours.
*   A minimum of 1 rest day in any two-week period **must** be provided.

These regulations are critical for managing fatigue and ensuring safety during intensive site assignments.
