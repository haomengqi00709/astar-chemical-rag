# Material Verification and Welding Certifications (0-QDS-7415)

This section outlines the requirements for material verification and welding activities for the Dual Laminate Tank project as specified in 0-QDS-7415.

**Material Verification:**
Material Verification is a critical item or activity that **must** be performed.
*   **Material Test Certificates:** These certificates are mandatory quality records and **must** have a Quality Record status of FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package). Additionally, Chemetics surveillance for Material Test Certificates **must** be "Complete," meaning Chemetics will fully review and approve these documents.

**Welding:**
Welding is identified as a key item or activity requiring specific quality controls.
*   **Welder Qualification:** Documentation of welder qualification is a mandatory quality record and **must** have a Quality Record status of FP. Chemetics surveillance for Welder Qualification **must** be "Complete," indicating a full review and approval by Chemetics.

These requirements ensure that all materials used and welding performed meet the stringent quality standards for the Dual Laminate Tank. For general quality record definitions, refer to [[0-QDS-7415_quality_record_definitions]]. For overall vendor quality assurance, see [[0-QDS-7415_vendor_quality_assurance_requirements]].
