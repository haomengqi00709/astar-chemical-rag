# SS Tubing Tier 1 Final Inspections

Final Inspections are a critical required activity for Stainless Steel Tubing Tier 1, encompassing a range of visual and dimensional checks to ensure the product meets all specified requirements before shipment. All final inspection activities are subject to Chemetics Witness surveillance.

Key aspects of the final inspections include:

*   **Dimensional Inspection:** This is a required quality record, designated as "FP" (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package). Dimensional inspection **must** be performed under Chemetics Witness surveillance. Dimensional tolerances **must** adhere to [SA-450] standards.
*   **Ring Gauge Inspection:** This is a required quality record, designated as "FP". Ring Gauge inspection **must** be performed on 100% of tube ends and is subject to Chemetics Witness surveillance. This ensures the correct diameter and roundness of the tube ends for proper fitting.
*   **Contour and Finish of Welds:** Chemetics Witness surveillance is required to verify the acceptable contour and finish of all welds, ensuring they meet aesthetic and functional specifications and are free from defects.
*   **Surfaces Free from Defects:** Chemetics Witness surveillance is required to confirm that all surfaces of the tubing are free from defects, such as scratches, dents, or other imperfections that could compromise performance or appearance.
*   **Cleanliness:** Chemetics Witness surveillance is required to ensure the tubing meets specified cleanliness standards, which is vital for preventing contamination in process systems.
*   **Item Identification:** Chemetics Witness surveillance is required to verify correct item identification, ensuring that each piece of tubing is properly marked and traceable.
*   **Review QA Documentation:** Chemetics Witness surveillance is required for the review of the Quality Assurance Documentation. This final review ensures that all required quality records and certifications are complete and accurate, forming a comprehensive quality package for the Stainless Steel Tubing.

These comprehensive final inspections are essential to confirm the overall quality, compliance, and readiness of the Stainless Steel Tubing Tier 1 for delivery and installation.
