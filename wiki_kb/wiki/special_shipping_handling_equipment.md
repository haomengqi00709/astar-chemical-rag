# Special Shipping and Handling Equipment Requirements

To ensure the safe and secure transportation of goods, the Vendor **must** design and supply all necessary items for shipment and handling. This includes, but is not limited to, supports, special bracing, and spreader bars.

**Design and Approval:**
The Vendor **must** provide detailed drawings of all such items for the approval of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics before proceeding with their manufacture.

**Shipping Saddles:**
*   Shipping saddles **must** be designed to withstand all imposed loads.
*   Shipping saddles **must** be designed to prevent damage or permanent distortion to the goods.
*   The minimum contact arc length for shipping saddles will be 120°.

**Securing Goods:**
Tensioned steel bands, straps, wire rope, or other suitable means **must** be used to keep goods securely attached to supports during handling and transporting. Protection **must** be provided at band locations to prevent damage to painted items.

These requirements are crucial for maintaining the integrity of goods during [[packaging_marine_transportation_0_spc_5005]] and transit, especially for large or sensitive equipment.
