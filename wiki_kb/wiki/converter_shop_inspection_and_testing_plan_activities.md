# Converter Shop Inspection and Testing Plan Activities (0-QDS-7401)

This page outlines the mandatory inspection and testing activities for the Converter Shop project, as specified in Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7401. These activities **must** be included in the Vendor's Inspection and Testing (I & T) Plan.

**Trial Fit-up Requirements:**

*   **Top Head Segments:**
    *   **Requirement:** Trial Fit-up of Top head segments is required in Vendor I & T Plan.
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Witness and Hold.

*   **Intermediate Head Segments:**
    *   **Requirement:** Trial Fit-up of Intermediate head segments is required in Vendor I & T Plan (If present).
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Witness and Hold (If present).

*   **Bottom Head Segments:**
    *   **Requirement:** Trial Fit-up of Bottom head segments is required in Vendor I & T Plan.
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Witness and Hold.

**Match Marking:**

*   **Requirement:** Match marking heads is required in Vendor I & T Plan.
*   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Witness.

**Final Inspection Activities:**

The following final inspection activities are required and **must** be included in the Vendor I & T Plan, with specified Chemetics surveillance levels:

*   **Dimensional Final Inspection:**
    *   **Requirement:** Including full check on all shell & core plates (FP).
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Witness and Hold.

*   **Contour & Finish of Welds Final Inspection:**
    *   **Requirement:** Required in Vendor I & T Plan.
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Witness and Hold.

*   **Surfaces Free From Defects Final Inspection:**
    *   **Requirement:** Required in Vendor I & T Plan.
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Witness and Hold.

*   **Cleanliness Final Inspection:**
    *   **Requirement:** Required in Vendor I & T Plan.
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Witness and Hold.

*   **Item Identification Final Inspection:**
    *   **Requirement:** Required in Vendor I & T Plan.
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Witness and Hold.

*   **Review QA Documentation Final Inspection:**
    *   **Requirement:** Required in Vendor I & T Plan.
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Witness and Hold.

*   **Packaging:**
    *   **Requirement:** Required in Vendor I & T Plan.
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Witness and Hold.

**Packing List:**

*   **Requirement:** A Packing List is required (AP).

**Trial Fit-up Requirements:**
*   **Top Head Segments:** Required in Vendor I & T Plan. Chemetics surveillance is Witness and Hold.
*   **Intermediate Head Segments:** Required in Vendor I & T Plan (If present). Chemetics surveillance is Witness and Hold (If present).
*   **Bottom Head Segments:** Required in Vendor I & T Plan. Chemetics surveillance is Witness and Hold.

**Other Inspection Activities:**
*   **Match Marking Heads:** Required in Vendor I & T Plan. Chemetics surveillance is Witness.

**Final Inspection Activities:**
The following final inspection activities are required and must be included in the Vendor's I & T Plan:
*   **Dimensional Final Inspection:** Required (FP), including a full check on all shell & core plates. Chemetics surveillance is Witness and Hold.
*   **Contour & Finish of Welds Final Inspection:** Required. Chemetics surveillance is Witness and Hold.
*   **Surfaces Free From Defects Final Inspection:** Required. Chemetics surveillance is Witness and Hold.
*   **Cleanliness Final Inspection:** Required. Chemetics surveillance is Witness and Hold.
*   **Item Identification Final Inspection:** Required. Chemetics surveillance is Witness and Hold.
*   **Review QA Documentation Final Inspection:** Required. Chemetics surveillance is Witness and Hold.

**Packaging and Documentation:**
*   **Packaging:** Required in Vendor I & T Plan. Chemetics surveillance is Witness and Hold.
*   **Packing List:** Required (AP).
