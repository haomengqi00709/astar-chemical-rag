# Template Heat Exchanger Material Specifications

This page outlines the material selection requirements for various components of heat exchangers, as specified in document 4-PDS-XXXX-R2. Proper material selection is critical for ensuring the longevity, corrosion resistance, and structural integrity of the equipment under specified operating conditions.

The following components require detailed material specification:
*   **Plates:** Material of construction for the heat transfer plates (e.g., in plate and frame exchangers).
*   **Shell:** Material of construction for the outer shell or casing of the exchanger.
*   **Frame:** Material of construction for the structural frame supporting the heat exchanger components.
*   **Tubes:** Material of construction for the heat transfer tubes (e.g., in shell and tube exchangers).
*   **Spray Shield:** Material of construction for any internal spray shields.
*   **Tubesheet:** Material of construction for the plates that support and separate the tubes from the shell side fluid.

For general guidance on material selection, refer to [[material_selection]]. Specific material requirements for different exchanger types can be found in [[shell_and_tube_heat_exchanger_material_selection]] and [[plate_heat_exchanger_material_specifications]].
