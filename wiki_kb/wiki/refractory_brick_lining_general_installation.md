# Refractory Brick Lining General Installation Requirements

The installation of refractory brick linings **must** adhere strictly to the instructions provided by the refractory Vendor. This includes compliance with the refractory Vendor’s drawings and their detailed installation instructions. Adherence to these vendor-specific guidelines is critical for ensuring the integrity and performance of the lining.

**Material and Component Restrictions**
No metallic shims **shall** be used in the lining. This mandatory requirement prevents potential material incompatibilities or structural weaknesses that could arise from the inclusion of metallic components within the refractory system.
