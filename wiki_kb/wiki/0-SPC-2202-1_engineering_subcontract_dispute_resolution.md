# Engineering Subcontract Dispute Resolution (0-SPC-2202-1)

This page outlines the procedures for dispute resolution under engineering subcontracts, as specified in document 0-SPC-2202-1. These mechanisms are designed to provide a structured approach to resolving disagreements between Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Subcontractor.

### Adjudication Process
Either party may submit a dispute to an Adjudicator by following the established procedure under the contract. Adjudication is a formal process for resolving disputes quickly.

### Reference to Tribunal
It is important to note that adjudication is not a pre-condition to the reference of a dispute to the tribunal. This means that parties are not required to complete the adjudication process before escalating a dispute to a higher authority.

If neither party submits a dispute to the Adjudicator, either party may at any time refer the dispute directly to the tribunal.

### Appointment of Adjudicator and Tribunal
*   **Adjudicator Appointment:** Unless a dispute relates to the Head Contract, the Adjudicator will be appointed by agreement between the parties involved in the subcontract.
*   **Tribunal Appointment:** Unless a dispute relates to the Head Contract, the tribunal for dispute resolution will be arbitration. This arbitration will be conducted by a single Arbitrator appointed by the British Columbia International Arbitration Centre under its established Rules.
