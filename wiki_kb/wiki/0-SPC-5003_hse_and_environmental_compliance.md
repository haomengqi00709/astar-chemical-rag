# HSE and Environmental Compliance (0-SPC-5003)

Under Specification **0-SPC-5003**, both Environment Management and Health and Safety Management are designated as mandatory areas of [[compliance_and_standards|compliance]]. Vendors **must** ensure full adherence to all relevant environmental and [[hse_management_and_employee_responsibilities|Health and Safety]] regulations and company policies.

To facilitate oversight and ensure ongoing adherence, access to records **is required** for the purpose of reviewing, monitoring, and auditing compliance with all Industrial Relations, [[subcontractor_environmental_management_0_spc_606|Environment]], and Health and Safety obligations. This includes allowing for [[hse_audit_program_details|audits]] and inspections to verify that all [[emissions_and_effluents|environmental]] and safety protocols are being followed.
