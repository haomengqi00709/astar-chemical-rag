# Equipment Design and Specification (4-PRC-0003)

This document, 4-PRC-0003, details the discipline assignments for the preparation, checking, and approval of equipment design and specifications for the Project. It outlines the roles and responsibilities for developing all equipment-related documentation.

This procedure ensures that all equipment design documents, including data sheets, drawings, and specifications, are developed, reviewed, and approved in a structured manner, adhering to the [[equipment_design_criteria_4_prc_0002]] and overall project quality standards.
