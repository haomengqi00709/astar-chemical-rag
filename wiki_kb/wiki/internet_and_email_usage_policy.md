# Internet and Email Usage Policy

This policy governs the use of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ Internet access and email systems, which are primarily provided for official business use.

**Permitted Use**
Occasional personal use of email/Internet is permitted, provided it does not interfere with business operations or violate any other aspect of this policy. Personal messages **will** be treated no differently under this policy from business messages.

**Prohibited Uses**
Email/Internet **may not** be used for:
*   Illegal, unethical, or immoral uses.
*   Sending, receiving, or accessing offensive, objectionable, abusive, pornographic (including nudity), obscene, sexist, racist, harassing, or provocative messages, images, or other materials, including adult–oriented Web sites or news groups.
*   Defamatory, derogatory, or false messages.
*   Political activities.
*   Other commercial or business uses other than in pursuit of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics business.
*   Unauthorized access to other user’s email, data, or communications.
*   Uses that infringe on copyright or other intellectual property rights.
*   Unsecured disclosure of confidential or privileged information.
*   Uses that may compromise system integrity or degrade system performance.
*   Deliberately propagating viruses.

**Monitoring and Control**
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics reserves the right to access, monitor, and control all employee usage of the Internet and any message sent or received over Aker Kvaerner Chemetics email system.
