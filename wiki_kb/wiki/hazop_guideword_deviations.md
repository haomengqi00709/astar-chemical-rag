# HAZOP Guideword Deviations

HAZOP (Hazard and Operability) studies utilize a set of guidewords combined with process parameters to systematically identify potential deviations from design intent. These guideword deviations prompt the HAZOP team to explore "Causes," "Consequences," "Safeguards," and "Actions" related to each deviation, as outlined in the [[hazop_record_structure_and_content|HAZOP record structure]].

The following guideword deviations are documented within HAZOP Record 1-HAZ-0001:

*   **1.01:** No material
*   **1.02:** No activity
*   **1.03:** More material
*   **1.04:** More activity
*   **1.05:** More temperature
*   **1.06:** More pressure
*   **1.07:** More concentration
*   **1.08:** More minor components
*   **1.09:** Less material
*   **1.1:** Less activity
*   **1.11:** Less temperature
