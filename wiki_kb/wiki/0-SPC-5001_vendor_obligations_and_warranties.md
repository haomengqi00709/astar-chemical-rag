# Vendor Obligations, Design, Quality, and Warranties (0-SPC-5001)

This page details the mandatory obligations, design responsibilities, quality system requirements, and warranties that the Vendor **must** adhere to under Purchase Order **0-SPC-5001**.

**Health and Safety**
The Vendor **must** act in accordance with the health and safety requirements of the [[applicable_law_and_compliance|Applicable Law]] and as stated in the [[goods_and_services_definitions|Goods and Services Information]]. See also [[hse_key_document_references]].

**Provision of Goods and Services**
The Vendor **shall** Provide the Goods and Services in accordance with the [[goods_and_services_definitions|Goods and Services Information]] and the [[applicable_law_and_compliance|Applicable Law]].

**Design Responsibilities**
The Vendor **shall** design the parts of the Goods and Services that the Goods and Services Information specifies for Vendor design. If the Goods and Services Information or Applicable Law requires the Vendor to submit design particulars to the [[lead_engineer_roles|Lead Engineer]] or Others for acceptance or approval, the Vendor **must not** proceed with the relevant work until such acceptance or approval has been granted.

**Intellectual Property**
The Vendor **shall** indemnify Aker Kvaerner Chemetics against claims, compensation, and costs arising from the Vendor infringing a patent or copyright. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may use and copy the Vendor's design for any purpose connected with the construction, use, alteration, or demolition of the Project, unless otherwise stated in the Goods and Services Information. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may also use and copy the Vendor's design for other purposes as stated in the Goods and Services Information. See [[intellectual_property_and_design_use]].

**Subcontracting**
If the Vendor subcontracts any work, the Vendor remains fully responsible for performing the Purchase Order as if no subcontracting had occurred. This Purchase Order applies as if a Supplier's employees and equipment were those of the Vendor. See [[subcontractor_obligations_0_spc_2201]].

**Quality System**
The Vendor **must** plan, establish, and maintain a quality system that conforms with the quality system described in the Purchase Order. The Vendor **shall** grant the [[project_buyer_roles|Project Buyer]], [[lead_engineer_roles|Lead Engineer]], and [[owner_roles|Owner]] access to the quality system of both the Vendor and any Suppliers for monitoring and quality auditing purposes. See [[quality_system_compliance]] and [[vendor_quality_assurance]].

**Warranties**
The Vendor provides the following warranties to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, which also benefit Aker Kvaerner Chemetics's successors, assigns, and the Owner. These warranties are in addition to any warranties arising by operation of law:
*   The Goods and Services are fit and suitable for their intended purposes as described in the Goods and Services Information.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics relies on the Vendor's skill and judgment regarding the fitness and suitability of the Goods and Services.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics relies on the Vendor’s agreement to complete the whole of the Goods and Services by the Delivery Date.
*   The Vendor's usual business involves supplying goods and services of a similar nature to the Goods and Services.
*   The Goods and Services are of merchantable quality and free of defects.
*   Unless otherwise stated in the Goods and Services Information, all materials used in the Goods and Services **must** be new and of the best quality.

**Access and Instructions**
The Vendor **must** provide access to work being done or goods and materials being stored for this Purchase Order to the Project Buyer, Lead Engineer, and other parties notified by the Project Buyer. The Vendor **shall** obey any instruction given by the Project Buyer that is in accordance with this Purchase Order.
