# Acid Resistant Lining Quality Control and Inspection

Rigorous quality control and inspection are paramount for acid resistant lining systems to ensure their long-term performance, structural integrity, and safety in highly corrosive environments.

**Inspector Access and Authority:**
Inspectors **will** have free access to the installation job site at all times during the lining process. This unrestricted access allows for continuous monitoring and verification of compliance. They **will** be entitled to inspect the work in progress at any stage, from surface preparation to final brick placement and membrane application. Furthermore, inspectors **will** be entitled to reject any material or workmanship that does not conform to this Standard or to normal industry practice, ensuring that only high-quality components and techniques are used.

**Inspection Schedule and Requirements:**
*   During installation work in progress, at the end of each shift (approximately every 8 hours), the finished brickwork **will** be thoroughly visually inspected. This regular inspection helps to identify and rectify issues promptly.
*   Upon completion of the lining work in each vessel, the finished brickwork **will** undergo a thorough visual inspection to confirm overall compliance and quality.
*   The requirements of this Standard **must** be complied with without exception. Any deviation **must** be addressed immediately.

**Defect Rectification and Acceptance Criteria:**
*   Mortar joints **will** be 100% full, including the bedding layer and the backside of the brick. If mortar joints are not 100% full, touch-up is required to ensure a complete and impermeable barrier.
*   Any tears identified in the PTFE sheet **must** be repaired with overlap patches to maintain the integrity of the membrane and prevent chemical penetration.
*   All partially disbonded joints in the PTFE/Pecora membrane **are to be** firmly re-stuck with Pecora mastic and flattened to ensure complete adhesion and continuity of the protective layer.

**Final Appearance and Workmanship:**
Completed lining and membrane sections **will** exhibit a uniform and neat appearance. This includes:
*   Consistent joint widths throughout the brickwork, indicating careful and precise installation.
*   Consistent workmanship across all installed sections, reflecting a high standard of quality.
*   Level brick courses, demonstrating proper installation and alignment, which is critical for structural stability and even load distribution.

These stringent quality control measures ensure that the acid resistant lining systems meet the required technical specifications and provide reliable, long-lasting protection against corrosive media, thereby safeguarding plant assets and operational continuity.

### Inspection Frequency
*   During installation work in progress, at the end of each shift (approximately every 8 hours), the finished brick work **will** be thoroughly visually inspected.
*   At the completion of the lining work in each vessel, the finished brick work **will** be thoroughly visually inspected.

### Acceptance Criteria
*   Mortar joints **will** be 100% full (including the bedding layer/back side of the brick).
*   Completed lining and membrane sections **will** be uniform in appearance.
*   Completed lining and membrane sections **will** be neat in appearance.
*   Completed lining and membrane sections **will** have consistent joint widths.
*   Completed lining and membrane sections **will** have consistent workmanship.
*   Completed lining and membrane sections **will** have level brick courses.

### Rework and Repair
*   If mortar joints are not 100% full, touch up is required.
*   Any tears in the PTFE sheet **must** be repaired with overlap patches.
*   All partially disbonded joints in the PTFE/Pecora membrane are to be firmly re-stuck with Pecora mastic and flattened.
