# Business Travel Policy Compliance and Deviation

All business travel **must** strictly comply with the requirements outlined in the 11.1 PR 2 Group Travel Procedure. This procedure is a mandatory reference for all travellers and can be accessed through the eNet.

Key mandatory requirements from the 11.1 PR 2 Group Travel Procedure include:
*   Travellers **must** "behave as though you were paying for the journey yourself." This principle guides all decisions related to travel expenses and choices.
*   The use of the most cost-effective airlines and routing is **mandatory**.
*   Refundable tickets are generally not considered the lowest-cost option and should be justified if requested.

**Deviations from Policy:**
If a traveller requests bookings that deviate from the 11.1 PR 2 Group Travel Procedure, the following **must** be adhered to:
*   The traveller **must** clearly state the specific deviation requested on the [[travel_authorization_form_details_0_frm_9002]].
*   The traveller **must** provide detailed reasons for the requested deviation. A common justification for deviation is when travel plans are subject to potential changes, requiring the flexibility of a refundable ticket, despite it not being the most economic choice.
*   If the traveller wishes to change an itinerary after initial approval in a way that no longer complies with the policy or previously authorized deviations, they **must** revise the [[travel_authorization_form_details_0_frm_9002]].
*   The revised Travel Request Form **must** explicitly state the new deviation requested.
*   The revised Travel Request Form **must** be re-authorised through the appropriate channels, as detailed in [[travel_authorization_approvals_0_frm_9002]].

Adherence to these compliance and deviation protocols ensures that all business travel remains aligned with company policy while allowing for necessary flexibility under justified circumstances.

*   The use of the most cost-effective airlines and routing is required.

**Deviations from Policy:**
While strict compliance is expected, there are instances where deviations may be necessary. A common reason for requesting travel bookings that deviate from the 11.1 PR 2 Group Travel Procedure is when the traveller’s plans are subject to possible change and a refundable ticket is required. Any requested deviation **must** be clearly stated and justified on the Travel Request Form.
