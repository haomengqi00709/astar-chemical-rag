# Order Risk Assessment

This page details the process for conducting an order risk assessment, which categorizes procurement risks based on the value of the order.

**Risk Assessment Levels**
The order risk assessment has three levels based on the value of the order:
*   Risk assessment – value < CAD 10 K.
*   Risk assessment – value < CAD 200 K.
*   Risk assessment – value > CAD 200 K.

The order risk assessment **will** be prepared in accordance with Table 2. The form "Order Risk Assessment", 0-TAB-5XXX, **must** be completed.

**Roles and Responsibilities**
For order risk assessment, value less than CAD 200 K involves PE, and value greater than CAD 200 K involves PM.
