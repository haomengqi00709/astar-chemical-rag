# Metal Butterfly Damper Body Design and Fabrication

The design and fabrication of the metal butterfly damper body are critical to its performance and longevity. The complete control damper assembly **SHALL** include a fabricated damper body and a reinforced blade.

**Welding Requirements:**
All welding procedures **SHALL** be in accordance with the Vendor’s established welding procedures. These procedures **MUST** comply with either [[carbon_steel_welding_standard_overview]] (4-SPC-0060) for carbon steel components or [[austenitic_stainless_steel_welding_standard_overview]] (4-SPC-0061) for austenitic stainless steel components, as applicable.
*   All welds on the damper body **MUST** be continuous and gas tight.
*   Damper body longitudinal seams **SHALL** be full penetration welds.

**Damper Body Dimensions and Thickness:**
The diameter and thickness of the duct into which the damper body will be welded are specified in [[metal_dampers_process_and_automation_requirements]] (5-LST-[00XX]: Metal Dampers). It is important to note that the duct thickness is not to be taken as the required damper body thickness.
*   The damper body thickness **SHALL** be determined by the Vendor.
*   The Vendor **MUST** give consideration to all specified process and climatic conditions when determining the damper body thickness.
*   The Vendor **MUST** also give consideration to the effect of operating forces inherent to the equipment when determining the damper body thickness.
*   The body thickness **SHALL NOT** be less than the duct thickness.
*   The damper body outside diameter **SHALL** match the outside diameter of the duct.
*   The inside wall of the damper body **SHALL** be tapered at a 3:1 ratio to match the duct thickness.
*   The damper body **SHALL** be designed with sufficient thickness to prevent distortion under all operating conditions.

**Damper Body Length and Lifting:**
*   Damper body length **SHALL NOT** exceed 500mm (20”). Longer body lengths require explicit approval by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
*   Lifting lugs **SHALL** be provided on the damper body to facilitate its installation and removal.

**Blade Position Indication:**
Each damper **SHALL** be supplied with a local indicator for blade position. This indicator **SHALL** be located at the “dead” end of the shaft.
*   The indicator **SHALL** be 300mm (12”) long.
*   The indicator **SHALL** be painted yellow.
