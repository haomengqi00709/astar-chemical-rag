# 0-QDS-7429 Packing and Crating Requirements

This page details the requirements for packing, crating, and associated documentation for SS Plate, as specified in Quality Data Sheet [[0-QDS-7429_document_administration]].

**Packing/Crating** is a mandatory item or activity.

### Packaging
*   **Surveillance**: Chemetics **requires Witness surveillance** for the packaging process to ensure compliance with specifications.

### Packing List
*   **Quality Record Requirement**: The Packing List is required as [[0-QDS-7429_quality_record_definitions|AP]] (As Prepared).
*   **Surveillance**: Chemetics **requires Witness surveillance** for the Packing List to verify its accuracy and completeness.
