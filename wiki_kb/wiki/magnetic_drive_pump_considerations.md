# Magnetic Drive Pump Considerations

Magnetic drive pumps require specific considerations during the sizing and procurement process due to their unique design and operational characteristics.

**Procurement and Sizing:**
*   Magnetic drive pump Vendors **will** typically purchase the magnet assembly at the same time as the casing.
*   For magnetic drive pumps, the TDH (Total Dynamic Head) **will** have to be finalised at least to within a motor size at the time of purchase. This requirement may limit their use to very well-defined applications that change little from project to project.

**Power Requirements:**
*   Ensure that the Vendor has factored in seal drag when calculating power requirements. Many applications employ SiC vs. SiC inboard seal faces with an external flush, which results in significant seal drag that can impact the overall power consumption of the pump.

**Procurement and Sizing:**
The requirement to finalize TDH for magnetic drive pumps **may** limit their use to very well defined applications that change little from project to project.

**Baseplate Sizing:**
If the motor frame size is the same for next larger motor size and the selected motor size, the baseplate **can** be sized for the selected motor size.
