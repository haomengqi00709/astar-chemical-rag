# Equipment Classification Types

Equipment is categorized into one of five classifications based on the level of engineering required for its design and manufacture. The assignment of an equipment classification **must** be made by Mechanical personnel and recorded on the [[4-lst-0001_equipment_list_details]].

The five equipment classifications are:

*   **Type A Equipment: Vendor Standard Equipment**
    *   **Description:** This category includes common, off-the-shelf items such as pumps, fans, agitators, and plate heat exchangers.
    *   **Design & Manufacture:** The vendor is responsible for the design and manufacture of Type A Equipment. This **must** be in accordance with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics documentation, referenced national standards, and the vendor’s standard practices.

*   **Type B Equipment: Process Packages; Vendor Proprietary Design**
    *   **Description:** A "process package" refers to a collection of equipment designed to perform a singular overall process duty. Examples include water or brine demineralizers, chlorine refrigeration units, and acid plant blowers.
    *   **Design & Manufacture:** The vendor is responsible for the design and manufacture of Type B Equipment. This **must** be in accordance with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics documentation, referenced national standards, and the vendor’s standard practices. The vendor also determines the duty and sizing of individual equipment items within the package.

*   **Type C Equipment: Custom Fabricated Equipment; Vendor Design**
    *   **Description:** This category includes custom-built items such as tanks, pressure vessels, and shell and tube heat exchangers where the detailed mechanical design is performed by the vendor.
    *   **Design & Manufacture:** The vendor is responsible for the detailed mechanical design, producing shop drawings and cutting plans, and the manufacture of Type C Equipment. This **must** be in accordance with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics documentation, referenced national standards, and the vendor’s standard practices.

*   **Type D Equipment: Custom Fabricated Equipment; Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Design**
    *   **Description:** This category also includes custom-built items like tanks, pressure vessels, shell and tube heat exchangers, and reactors. However, the design is performed by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, often utilizing specialized or proprietary methods.
    *   **Design & Manufacture:** Aker Kvaerner Chemetics is responsible for the design. The vendor will generally produce any required shop drawings and cutting plans and **must** manufacture the equipment in accordance with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics documentation and referenced national standards.

*   **Type E Equipment: Proprietary Equipment**
    *   **Description:** Type E Equipment consists of items with proprietary designs that hold significant after-market commercial value. Details of the design are typically not disclosed to the user. SRS (Sulfuric Acid Regeneration System) units were added to this category in Revision 3 of 4-PRC-0003.
    *   **Approval:** Classification of items as Type E **requires** the approval of the President.
    *   **Design & Manufacture:** The vendor will generally produce any required shop drawings and cutting plans and **must** manufacture the equipment in accordance with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics documentation and referenced national standards.

**Type A Equipment: Vendor Standard Equipment**
*   Includes pumps, fans, agitators, and plate heat exchangers.
*   Design and manufacture are performed by the vendor.
*   Work is in accordance with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics documentation, referenced national standards, and the vendor’s standard practices.

**Type B Equipment: Process Packages; Vendor Proprietary Design**
*   Design and manufacture are performed by the vendor.
*   Work is in accordance with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics documentation, referenced national standards, and the vendor’s standard practices.
*   The vendor determines the duty and sizing of equipment items within a Type B package.
*   Examples include water or brine demineralizers, chlorine refrigeration units, and acid plant blowers.

**Type C Equipment: Custom Fabricated Equipment; Vendor Design**
*   Includes tanks, pressure vessels, and shell and tube heat exchangers.
*   Detailed mechanical design, shop drawings, cutting plans, and manufacture are performed by the vendor.
*   Work is in accordance with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics documentation, referenced national standards, and the vendor’s standard practices.

**Type D Equipment: Custom Fabricated Equipment; Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Design**
*   Includes tanks, pressure vessels, shell and tube heat exchangers, and reactors.
*   Designed by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, often using specialised or proprietary methods.
*   The vendor generally produces any required shop drawings and cutting plans and manufactures the equipment.
*   Manufacture is in accordance with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics documentation and referenced national standards.

**Type E Equipment: Proprietary Equipment**
*   Includes items of proprietary design with significant after-market commercial value.
*   Details of Type E Equipment design are not disclosed to the user.
*   Classification of items as Type E requires the approval of the President.
*   The vendor generally produces any required shop drawings and cutting plans and manufactures the equipment.
*   Manufacture is in accordance with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics documentation and referenced national standards.
