# Through Distributor Field Inspection Items

This page outlines the specific inspection items for Through Distributors, as detailed in checklist 4-FIC-XX10-R0. These inspections are critical for ensuring proper installation and functionality of the distributor components in accordance with design specifications and quality standards.

**Mandatory Inspection Requirements:**
The following items **must** be verified during the field inspection:
*   All Distributor headers, throughs, and arm hangers **must** be properly adjusted, bolted, and tightened. This ensures structural integrity and correct positioning.
*   Distributor throughs **must** be leveled to ensure even liquid distribution across the packing surface. Refer to [[trough_distributor_design_guidelines]] for design specifics.
*   Gussets on piping **must** be checked for proper installation and structural integrity.
*   All flanges **must** be checked for tightness. Flange torque values may be specified in relevant engineering documents.
*   Correct hardware and gaskets **must** be installed for all connections to prevent leaks and ensure system integrity.
*   Orifice plates **must** be installed in downcomers as per design specifications. See [[trough_distributor_downcomer_details]] for related information.
*   Throughs **must** be empty and clean before operation to prevent contamination or blockage.
*   Candle drain dip pipes **must** be installed in the through. Generally, two candle drain dip pipes are installed per through.
*   Downcomers **must** penetrate into the top layer of packing to ensure proper liquid distribution and prevent bypassing. For details on packing installation, refer to [[converter_catalyst_and_packing_installation]].
