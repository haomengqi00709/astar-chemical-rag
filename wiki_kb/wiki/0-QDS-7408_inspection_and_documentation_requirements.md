# Preheat Furnace Inspection and Documentation Requirements

This page details the mandatory inspection, testing, and documentation requirements for the Preheat Furnace Package, including the involvement of Chemetics personnel for witnessing and holding points. The definitions for "AP" (As Prepared) and "FP" (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package) are provided in [[0-QDS-7408_quality_record_definitions]].

**General Requirements:**
The Quality Data Sheet for the Preheat Furnace Package identifies the following key activities:
*   Material Verification
*   Pressure/Leak Testing
*   Coating/Lining Inspection
*   Final Inspection

**Documentation Requirements:**
*   **Material Test Certificates:** **Must** be provided as FP and **require** Chemetics Witness.
*   **Certificate of Conformance:** **Must** be provided as FP and **require** Chemetics Witness.
*   **Packing List:** **Must** be provided as AP and **require** Chemetics Witness.

**Testing Requirements:**
*   **Hydrostatic Test Fuel Train:** **Must** be provided as FP and **require** Chemetics Witness. For general pressure testing guidelines, refer to [[gas_gas_heat_exchanger_ss_pressure_and_leak_testing]].

**Coating and Lining Inspections:**
*   **External Coating/Lining Inspection:** **Requires** Chemetics Witness.
*   **Refractory Coating/Lining Inspection:** **Requires** Chemetics Witness.

**Final Inspection Activities:**
All final inspection activities listed below **require** Chemetics Witness and are designated as Chemetics Hold points, meaning work cannot proceed without Chemetics' approval.
*   **Dimensional Final Inspection:** **Must** be provided as FP, **requires** Chemetics Witness, and **requires** Chemetics Hold.
*   **Contour & finish of welds Final Inspection:** **Requires** Chemetics Witness and **requires** Chemetics Hold.
*   **Surfaces free from defects Final Inspection:** **Requires** Chemetics Witness and **requires** Chemetics Hold.
*   **Cleanliness Final Inspection:** **Requires** Chemetics Witness and **requires** Chemetics Hold.
*   **Bought out items Final Inspection:** **Must** be provided as FP, **requires** Chemetics Witness, and **requires** Chemetics Hold.
*   **Item identification Final Inspection:** **Requires** Chemetics Witness and **requires** Chemetics Hold.
*   **Review QA Documentation Final Inspection:** **Requires** Chemetics Witness and **requires** Chemetics Hold.

**Packing and Crating Requirements:**
*   **Packing/Crating Surveillance:** Chemetics surveillance for packing and crating is "Spot" with a value of 0.25.
*   **Packaging:** **Requires** Chemetics Witness and **requires** Chemetics Hold. It also requires a value of 1.0 in the "Required in Vendor I & T Plan" column and 1.0 in the "Complete" surveillance column.
*   **Blocking of refractory:** **Must** be provided as FP, **requires** Chemetics Witness, and **requires** Chemetics Hold.

For broader context on vendor quality assurance, refer to [[vendor_quality_assurance]].
