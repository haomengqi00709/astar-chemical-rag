# Sick Leave Policy

This policy (0-POL-91xx), currently under development, will define the terms and conditions for sick leave for employees of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. It will outline eligibility, notification procedures, documentation requirements, and compensation related to employee absence due to illness or injury.

The Sick Leave Policy is being developed by the Human Resources Department as part of its commitment to providing comprehensive [[employee_health_and_welfare_policies]] and ensuring employee care and welfare.
