# Emergency Response Equipment

This page details the standard equipment required for designated emergency response personnel and individual responsibilities for its maintenance.

**Standard Equipment for Floor Wardens**
The standard equipment for a Floor Warden (see [[0-PRC-8010_roles_and_responsibilities_floor_warden]]) is:
*   Hard hat
*   Safety vest
*   Flashlight

**Standard Equipment for Door Monitors**
The standard equipment for a Door Monitor (see [[0-PRC-8010_roles_and_responsibilities_door_monitor]]) is:
*   Hard hat
*   Safety vest
*   Flashlight
*   Ear plugs

**Individual Responsibility**
Each individual **is responsible** to ensure that their assigned emergency equipment is in working order.
Each individual **is responsible** to test their flashlight once per month to ensure it is functional.
