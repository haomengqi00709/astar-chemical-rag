# Risk Allocation, Indemnities, and Insurance for Purchase Orders (0-SPC-5001)

This page details the allocation of risks, indemnification responsibilities, and insurance requirements under Purchase Order **0-SPC-5001**. It also outlines limitations on liability for both parties.

**Title Transfer**
Whatever title the Vendor has to the [[goods_and_services_definitions|Goods and Services]] passes to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics once the Goods and Services have been Delivered. The Vendor **must** properly store, adequately protect, and label as property for the Project each part of the Goods and Services, regardless of whether title has passed to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Risk Allocation**
From the [[0-SPC-5001_purchase_order_overview#Starting Date|Starting Date]] until the [[0-SPC-5001_purchase_order_overview#Defects Date|Defects Date]], the risks not explicitly carried by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics are carried by the Vendor. Until the Defects Date, and unless otherwise instructed by the [[project_buyer_roles|Project Buyer]], the Vendor **must** promptly replace any loss of and repair any damage to the Goods and Services.

Aker Kvaerner Chemetics's risks include:
*   Claims, proceedings, compensation, and costs payable due to negligence, breach of statutory duty, or interference with any legal right by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or its employees.
*   Claims, proceedings, compensation, and costs payable due to a fault of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or a fault in its design.
*   Loss of or damage to parts of the Goods and Services after Delivery, with the following exceptions:
    *   Loss or damage occurring before the Defects Date which is due to a [[0-SPC-5001_purchase_order_overview#Defect|Defect]] that existed at Delivery.
    *   Loss or damage occurring before the Defects Date which is due to an event occurring before Delivery that was not itself an Aker Kvaerner Chemetics risk.
    *   Loss or damage occurring before the Defects Date which is due to the activities of the Vendor after Delivery.

**Indemnification**
Each Party **shall** indemnify the other against claims, proceedings, compensation, and costs due to an event that is at their respective risk. The liability of each Party to indemnify the other **shall** be reduced if events at the other Party's risk contributed to the claims, proceedings, compensation, and costs. This reduction in liability **shall** be proportional to the extent that events at the other Party's risk contributed, taking into account each Party's responsibilities under this Purchase Order. See [[risk_allocation_and_indemnities]].

**Insurance Requirements**
The Vendor **must** provide the insurances stated in the Insurance Table. Data available in SQL: [[0-SPC-5001_table_2]].
If the Vendor does not provide evidence that the required insurance is taken out, Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **may** insure the risk. The cost incurred by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for such insurance **shall** be paid by the Vendor. See [[insurance_requirements]].

**Limitations of Liability**
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Vendor **will not** be liable under the Purchase Order, in law or equity (including for negligence), for:
*   Loss of profit, loss of use, or loss of production.
*   Any indirect or consequential damage.
*   Special, exemplary, or punitive damages.

These limitations apply to both parties. See [[liability_limitations_commercial]].
