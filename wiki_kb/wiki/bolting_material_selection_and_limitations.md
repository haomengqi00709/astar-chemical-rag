# Bolting Material Selection and Limitations

When considering revisions to bolting materials, it is **mandatory** to review the requirements outlined in [[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping]] and [[ASME_b16_5_pipe_flanges_and_flanged_fittings]].

Any new material selected **shall** be ensured to be suitable for the specified service limits of the application.

A specific material limitation to note is that ASTM A307 Gr. B cannot be used above 204°C.

Related topics:
*   [[5-spc-0002_bolting_specification]]

Specifically, ASTM A307 Grade B bolting material is not suitable for service temperatures exceeding 204°C.
