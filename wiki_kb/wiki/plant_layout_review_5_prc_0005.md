# Plant Layout Review (5-PRC-0005)

This document, 5-PRC-0005, outlines the procedure for conducting plant layout reviews for the Project. Plant layout reviews are critical for optimizing space utilization, ensuring safe access and egress, facilitating maintenance, and verifying constructability and operational efficiency.

Design reviews for the Project are conducted in accordance with this procedure, ensuring that the physical arrangement of equipment and facilities meets all functional, safety, and logistical requirements.
