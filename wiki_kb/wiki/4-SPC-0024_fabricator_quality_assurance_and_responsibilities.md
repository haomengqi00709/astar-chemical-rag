# Zirconium Fabrication Quality Assurance and Responsibilities (4-SPC-0024)

This page outlines the mandatory quality assurance and responsibility requirements for fabricators undertaking Zirconium (Grade 60702) welded fabrications under document **4-SPC-0024**.

**Sub-Contracting:**
*   Sub-Contracting of welding is **not permitted** without explicit written approval from the company.

**Supervision:**
*   The fabricator **must** ensure regular and systematic supervision of all work by competent staff.
*   This supervision applies to both in-shop and on-site activities.

**Material Traceability and Control:**
*   The fabricator **must** maintain full traceability of all materials, including welding consumables, back to their original mill certification.
*   The fabricator's internal controls **must** prevent the use of unspecified materials.
*   Lack of adequate identification or certification **will** be cause for rejection of the specific component or item.

**Inspection Access and Authority:**
*   The Inspector (Quality Assurance Inspector or other Authorized Representative) **shall** have free access to the Vendor's work and, if work is sublet with approval, to the Subvendor's work at all reasonable times.
*   The Inspector **shall** be at liberty to inspect the work during fabrication at any stage of manufacture.
*   The Inspector **shall** be at liberty to reject any material or workmanship that does not conform to this Standard or the applicable code.
*   Inspection by the company does not relieve the fabricator or erector of their responsibility for providing materials, components, and workmanship in accordance with the requirements of this Standard, engineering design, and the applicable code.

**Chemical Analysis of Consumables:**
*   The supplier **must** make available to the purchaser's inspector copies of chemical certification for each batch of electrodes or wire coils used.
*   If electrodes, wire, etc., cannot be identified to the company's satisfaction, samples **will** be removed for chemical analysis.
*   Costs for chemical analysis of unidentified electrodes/wire **will** be to the supplier's account.

**Non-Conforming Weldments:**
*   Where results of chemical analysis show that a specific weldment falls outside of the approved specification or code requirements, the supplier **must** promptly advise the company in writing of their proposed course of action.
*   The supplier's proposed course of action for non-conforming weldments **must** include details of any additional testing necessary to fully define the extent of the non-conformance.
*   All repairs and retests for non-conforming weldments **will** be to the supplier's account. Non-conformance issues should be documented using the [[0-NCR-7XXX_template_non_conformance_report|Non-Conformance Report template]].
