# Professional Services Subcontract (0-SPC-2201) Schedule Management

Effective schedule management is a core requirement under Professional Services Subcontract 0-SPC-2201, ensuring that the Subcontractor's work progresses efficiently towards the [[subcontract_definitions_0_spc_2201|Completion Date]]. The Subcontractor **must** perform the work such that [[subcontract_definitions_0_spc_2201|Completion]] occurs on or before the Completion Date.

**Initial Schedule Submission:**
If a Schedule is not explicitly identified in the [[subcontract_definitions_0_spc_2201|Subcontract Data]], the Subcontractor **must** submit a first Schedule to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for acceptance within the period specified in the Subcontract Data.

**Required Schedule Content:**
Each Schedule submitted for acceptance **must** clearly show the following information:
*   The starting date.
*   The [[subcontract_definitions_0_spc_2201|Completion Date]].
*   Dates stated in the [[subcontract_definitions_0_spc_2201|Scope]] that constrain the timing of the Subcontractor’s work.
*   Planned Completion.
*   The start and finish dates for each activity on the activity schedule.
*   The planned order and timing of operations the Subcontractor intends to perform to [[subcontract_definitions_0_spc_2201|Provide the Services]].
*   The planned order and timing of work by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and [[subcontract_definitions_0_spc_2201|Others]], either as stated in the Scope or as subsequently agreed with them by the Subcontractor.
*   Dates when the Subcontractor plans to complete work necessary for Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and Others to perform their tasks.
*   Dates when the Subcontractor will require access to a person, place, or thing to Provide the Services in accordance with the Schedule.
*   Dates when the Subcontractor will require information and items to be provided by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics to Provide the Services in accordance with the Schedule.
*   Dates when the Subcontractor will require approval from Others to Provide the Services in accordance with the Schedule.
*   For each operation, a method statement identifying the resources the Subcontractor plans to use.
*   Any other information that the Scope requires the Subcontractor to show on a Schedule.

**Schedule Acceptance and Revisions:**
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **must** either accept the Schedule or notify the Subcontractor of reasons for non-acceptance within two weeks of the Subcontractor's submission.
*   The Subcontractor **must** submit a revised Schedule to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for acceptance each month after the starting date. Each revised Schedule **must** detail:
    *   The actual progress achieved on each operation and its impact on the timing of remaining work.
    *   The effects of implemented [[engineering_subcontract_compensation_events|compensation events]] and notified early warning matters.
    *   Plans to address any delays and correct notified [[subcontract_definitions_0_spc_2201|Defects]].
    *   Any other proposed changes to the [[subcontract_definitions_0_spc_2201|Accepted Schedule]].

**Initial Schedule Submission:**
*   If a Schedule is not identified in the *Subcontract Data*, the Subcontractor submits a first Schedule to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for acceptance within the period stated in the *Subcontract Data*.

**Schedule Content:**
On each Schedule submitted for acceptance, the Subcontractor **must** show:
*   The starting date and the Completion Date.
*   The dates stated in the Scope which constrain the timing of the Subcontractor’s work.
*   Planned Completion.
*   The start and finish of each activity on the activity schedule.
*   The order and timing of the operations which the Subcontractor plans to do in order to Provide the Services.
*   The order and timing of the work of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and Others either as stated in the Scope or as later agreed with them by the Subcontractor.
*   The dates when the Subcontractor plans to complete work needed to allow Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and Others to do their work.
*   The dates when, in order to Provide the Services in accordance with his Schedule, the Subcontractor will need access to a person, place or thing.
*   The dates when, in order to Provide the Services in accordance with his Schedule, the Subcontractor will need information and things to be provided by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
*   The dates when, in order to Provide the Services in accordance with his Schedule, the Subcontractor will need approval from Others.
*   For each operation, a method statement which identifies the resources which the Subcontractor plans to use.
*   Other information which the Scope requires the Subcontractor to show.

**Schedule Acceptance:**
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics either accepts the Schedule or notifies the Subcontractor of his reasons for not accepting it within two weeks of the Subcontractor submitting a Schedule for acceptance.

**Revised Schedule Submission and Content:**
*   The Subcontractor submits a revised Schedule to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for acceptance each month after the starting date.
*   On each revised Schedule, the Subcontractor **must** show:
    *   The actual progress achieved on each operation and its effect upon the timing of the remaining work.
    *   The effects of implemented compensation events and of notified early warning matters.
    *   How he plans to deal with any delays and to correct notified Defects.
    *   Any other changes he proposes to make to the Accepted Schedule.
