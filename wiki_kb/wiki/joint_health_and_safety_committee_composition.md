# Joint Health and Safety Committee Composition

The Joint Health and Safety Committee (JHSC) is structured to ensure balanced representation and effective collaboration between management and non-management personnel.

**Membership Requirements:**
*   The committee **shall** be composed of an equal number of management employees and non-management employees. This ensures a fair and balanced perspective on [[workplace_health_and_environmental_practices]].
*   The committee **shall** have a minimum of 4 members. The number of members **shall** be greater than 4 where required by regulation.
*   At a construction site, the committee **shall** include one management employee from each [[subcontracting_and_site_management|subcontractor]] and one worker employed by each subcontractor.

**Representation and Selection:**
*   Non-management employee representatives **shall** be elected by their peers through a democratic election process, particularly where multiple nominations exist.
*   The [[hse_personnel_definitions_and_responsibilities|Company most senior Management person]] at the work site **shall** attend all safety meetings. If they are unable to attend, they **must** designate another attendee to act on their behalf.

**Co-Chairs:**
*   The committee **shall** have 2 co-chairs.
*   One co-chair **shall** be selected by the worker representatives of the committee.
*   The other co-chair **shall** be selected by the management representatives of the committee.

This composition ensures that the JHSC benefits from diverse perspectives and expertise, facilitating comprehensive discussions and decision-making on health, safety, and environmental matters.

*   The committee will have 2 co-chairs.
*   One co-chair will be selected by the worker representatives of the committee.
*   The other co-chair will be selected by the management representatives of the committee.
