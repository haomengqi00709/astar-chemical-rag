# Welder Qualifications and Identification for Nickel/Copper Welding (4-SPC-0021)

For all nickel/copper (UNS N04400 / Monel 400) welding activities under **4-SPC-0021**, strict adherence to welder qualification and identification protocols is mandatory.

**Welder Qualifications:**
*   All welders **shall** be suitably qualified in accordance with relevant codes and standards for every procedure they are to use.
*   The fabricator **shall**, upon request, supply objective evidence of current welder qualifications to the Inspector.

**Welder Identification:**
*   All qualified welders **shall** be allocated an identification symbol.
*   Welder identification symbols **shall** be marked on the parent material adjacent to each welder's work. These symbols **shall** allow traceability during fabrication.
*   Fully marked-up weld maps may be substituted for stamping, subject to [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_company_details|Aker Kvaerner Chemetics]] approval.
