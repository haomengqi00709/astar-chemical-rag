# Project Document Control and Standards

This page outlines the requirements for document control, standardization, and storage within the project. Adherence to these standards ensures consistency, traceability, and efficient management of all project documentation.

**Document Standardization:**
Documents issued to the Owner or third parties **must** utilize a standard numbering system, format, and project title.
*   **Document Numbering:** Requirements for document numbering are detailed in [[document_numbering_rules]] (referencing 0-PRC-0002: Document Numbering).
*   **Project Title and Number:** The Project Title and Project Number are specified in [[project_coordination_0_prc_2004]].

**Document Processing:**
*   **External Documents (Issued):** Documents issued to the Owner or third parties **shall** be published, logged, and issued by [[document_control_1_prc_0003]].
*   **External Documents (Received):** Documents received from the Owner or third parties **shall** be logged and distributed by [[document_control_1_prc_0003]].
*   **Internal Documents:** Documents issued for internal use do not require formal publishing.

**Overall Document Control:**
Comprehensive requirements for documentation control are outlined in [[control_of_project_documents_0_prc_0006]].

**Document Storage:**
All project documentation **shall** be stored in either paper or electronic format, in accordance with the guidelines specified in [[file_structure]].

**Standard Procedure Modification:**
Where required by Contract, the Project Engineer **shall** modify standard procedures and standard templates to incorporate specific Owner requirements, ensuring project-specific compliance while maintaining a foundation of established practices.

**Document Standardization:**
*   Where required by Contract, the Project Engineer will modify standard procedures and standard templates to incorporate Owner requirements.
*   Document numbering requirements are detailed in [[0-PRC-0002_document_numbering]].
*   Project Title and Project Number are specified in [[0-PRC-2004_project_coordination]].

**Document Issuance and Receipt:**
*   Documents issued to the Owner or third parties will be published, logged, and issued by Document Control.
*   Documents received from the Owner or third parties will be logged and distributed by Document Control.
*   Documents issued for internal use do not require publishing.
*   Requirements for documentation control are shown in [[0-PRC-0006_control_of_project_documentation]].

**Document Storage:**
*   Project documentation will be stored in paper or electronic format in accordance with [[0-PRC-0003_file_structure]].
