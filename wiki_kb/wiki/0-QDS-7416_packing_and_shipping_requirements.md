# Packing and Shipping Requirements (0-QDS-7416)

For the Plate & Frame Heat Exchanger project (**0-QDS-7416**), specific requirements are in place for packing and shipping documentation.

*   **Packing List:** A Packing List is required as **AP** (As Prepared). Chemetics surveillance for the Packing List is **Complete**.
*   **Packaging:** Chemetics surveillance for the overall Packaging process is **Complete**.

These requirements ensure proper documentation and quality control during the preparation and shipment of the equipment. For definitions of **AP**, refer to [[0-QDS-7416_quality_record_definitions]]. Details on Chemetics' surveillance level can be found in [[0-QDS-7416_chemetics_surveillance_level]]. General packing and shipping guidelines can be found in [[0-QDS-7411_packing_and_shipping_requirements]].
