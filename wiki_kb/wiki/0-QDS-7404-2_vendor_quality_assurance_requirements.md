# Vendor Quality Assurance Requirements for ESP Lead Lined Components

For the Electrostatic Precipitator Lead Lined Components, the Vendor's Quality Assurance (QA) program **must** adhere to specific requirements to ensure compliance and product integrity.

**Mandatory Requirements:**
*   The Vendor **shall** prepare a Quality Plan. This plan **must** be in accordance with [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment|0-SPC-7001: Vendor Quality Assurance]].
*   The Vendor's Quality Assurance program **shall** comply with the requirements set forth in this [[0-QDS-7404-2_document_details|Quality Data Sheet (0-QDS-7404-2)]].
*   The Vendor's Quality Assurance program **shall** also comply with [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment|0-SPC-7001: Vendor Quality Assurance]].

**Conditional Requirement:**
*   Cells related to the Quality Plan **should** be deleted if the Quality Plan is not required for a specific procurement or project phase.
