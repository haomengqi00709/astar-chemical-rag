# Carbon Steel Welding Inspection and Testing

Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' specification 4-SPC-0060 details the mandatory inspection and testing requirements for carbon steel weldments.

**Low Temperature Service Requirements**
Weldments intended for low temperature service **must** meet the specific requirements outlined in [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels]], Division 1, paragraph UCS-67. For these applications, butt welds on materials in low temperature service **must** undergo 100% Radiographic Testing (RT) examination.

**Piping Radiography**
Piping **must** have a minimum of 5% random radiography, as defined in [[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping]], paragraph 341.4.1(b). For shop fabricated pipe made from sheet or plate, one spot radiograph **must** be performed at each longitudinal seam. No additional radiography of the longitudinal seam is required unless defects are revealed when the pipe is subsequently cut into smaller lengths.

**Magnetic Particle Examination (MT)**
When Radiographic Testing (RT) results are inconclusive, welds **must** be magnetic particle examined. If magnetic particle examination is required or specified, it **must** be performed in accordance with [[ASME_section_v_non_destructive_examination]], Article 7. The acceptance standards for magnetic particle examination **must** be in accordance with [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels]], Division 1, Appendix 6. After magnetic particle examination, all surfaces **must** be demagnetised.

**Quality Assurance Documentation**
All other inspection-related requirements are now covered in the appropriate [[vendor_quality_assurance]] documents.

**Radiographic Examination (RT)**
*   Butt welds on materials in low temperature service must be 100% RT examined.
*   Piping must have 5% random radiography as defined in ASME B31.3 paragraph 341.4.1(b) as a minimum.
*   Shop fabricated pipe made from sheet or plate must have one spot radiograph at each longitudinal seam. No additional radiography of the longitudinal seam is required unless defects are revealed when the pipe is cut into smaller lengths.

**Magnetic Particle Examination (MT)**
*   When RT results are inconclusive, welds must be magnetic particle examined.
*   When required or specified, magnetic particle examination must be performed in accordance with ASME Section V, Article 7.
*   Magnetic particle examination acceptance standards must be in accordance with ASME Section VIII Division 1, Appendix 6.
*   Surfaces must be demagnetised after magnetic particle examination.
