# SARAMET Piping Fabrication and Welding Requirements

This page outlines the general and material-specific fabrication and welding requirements for SARAMET 23 (UNS S30601) and SARAMET 35 (UNS S32615) piping systems, as detailed in specification 5-SPC-0124.

**General Fabrication Requirements**
*   Elbows and fittings **shall** be smooth flow wherever possible. Mitred elbows and fittings will only be accepted after written approval from [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_company_details]].
*   The vendor **shall** supply as-built drawings for all SARAMET piping systems.
*   The fabricator **must** perform fit-up (prior to welding) and dimensional checks on all spools/pieces.
*   Spools/pieces may be manufactured in-house or procured from third-party suppliers.

**SARAMET 23 (UNS S30601) Welding and Traceability**
*   All welding procedures and qualifications (WPS's and PQR's) **shall** conform to [[ASME_section_ix_welding_and_brazing_qualifications]] requirements.
*   All welding procedures and qualifications (WPS's and PQR's) **must** be verified by a third party.
*   Full traceability is required for all materials and welds. This includes:
    *   Mill test certificates for the material.
    *   Weld maps that clearly identify the welder and the welding process used for each weld.
*   Only SARAMET 23 run-off tabs and fit-up pieces are permitted for use with SARAMET 23 materials.

**SARAMET 35 (UNS S32615) Welding and Fabrication**
*   Welding for SARAMET 35 materials **shall** be kept to an absolute minimum.
*   All fabrication requirements specified for SARAMET 23 also apply to SARAMET 35.
*   Only SARAMET 35 run-off tabs and fit-up pieces are permitted for use with SARAMET 35 materials.
*   All temporary weld attachment points **must** be blended and subsequently Liquid Penetrant (PT) examined.
