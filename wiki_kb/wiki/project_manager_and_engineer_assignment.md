# Project Manager and Project Engineer Assignment

The assignment of key leadership roles for a project follows a defined process to ensure appropriate oversight and execution.

**Mandatory Requirements:**
*   The Director of Projects **shall** assign the Project Manager.
*   The Director of Projects and the Project Manager **shall** jointly decide whether a project requires a Project Engineer. This decision is based on the overall size and complexity of the project.

If a Project Engineer is not assigned to a project, their relevant responsibilities **shall** be distributed among other members of the project team. Specific guidance on this distribution can be found in Section 2 of the [[0-prc-2001_document_overview|0-PRC-2001 procedure]].

Responsibilities may be assigned or shared between the Project Manager and the Project Engineer to optimize workload and leverage expertise. In cases where no Project Engineer is assigned, responsibilities may be distributed among the Project Manager and one or more [[discipline_lead_and_approver_roles|Discipline Leads]].

For further details on the reassignment of responsibilities, refer to Sections 2 and 4 of the [[0-prc-2001_document_overview|0-PRC-2001 procedure]].

General principles for personnel assignments are outlined in [[general_personnel_assignment_principles]].
