# Plant Equipment Identification System

The 4-PRC-0001 procedure outlines the mandatory requirements for establishing standard identification numbers and names for all plant equipment. This system is crucial for maintaining consistency across all project documentation and operational phases.

**Purpose**
The primary purpose of this procedure is to define a standardized method for identifying and naming plant equipment. This standardization ensures clarity, reduces ambiguity, and facilitates efficient communication and data management throughout the project lifecycle.

**Responsibilities**
Each responsible discipline **must** develop and maintain standard identification numbers and names for plant equipment specific to their assigned technologies. This ensures that specialized equipment within different technological areas adheres to a consistent framework while allowing for discipline-specific nuances where necessary.

**Application and Principles**
Standard identification numbers **must** be used as the foundation for developing all related numbering systems, including [[cost_code_structure|cost codes]], [[document_numbering_0_prc_0002|document numbers]], and other project-specific identifiers.

The established standard identification numbers and names **must** be applied to all projects. However, if specific project requirements or client mandates dictate otherwise, the project team **shall** maintain a dual system on the project equipment list. This dual system **must** provide a clear cross-reference between the project-specific identifiers and the company's standard identification numbers, ensuring traceability and compliance.
