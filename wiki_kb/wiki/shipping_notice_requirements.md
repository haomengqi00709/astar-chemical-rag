# Shipping Notice Procedures

Timely and accurate shipping notices are critical for coordinating logistics and receipt of goods.

**Two-Week Shipping Notice:**
Vendors **must** fax or email a shipping notice two weeks in advance of readiness for shipment. This notice **must** contain the following information:
*   Address of factory, contact name, phone, fax, and email address.
*   Estimated number of packages in the shipment.
*   Estimated weights and dimensions (kg & cm) of each package.
*   Type of packing used.
*   Special handling instructions.
*   Material safety data sheets for any hazardous goods included in the shipment.

**Final Shipping Notice:**
Vendors **must** fax or email a final shipping notice twenty-four hours in advance of readiness for shipment. This final notice **must** contain:
*   The final number of packages in the shipment.
*   The final weights and dimensions (kg/cm) of each package.
*   The final packing list for each package.

**Logistics Group Contact Information:**
All shipping notices **must** be faxed or emailed to the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Logistics Group:
*   Phone: 604 734-1200
*   Fax: 604 737-4408
*   Email: logistics@kvaerner.com

The Vendor **must** allow two business days for Aker Kvaerner Chemetics to complete shipping arrangements after receiving the final shipping notice. These procedures are vital for the efficient management of [[packaging_marine_transportation_0_spc_5005]] and subsequent transportation.
