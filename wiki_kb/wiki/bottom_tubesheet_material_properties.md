# Bottom Tubesheet Material Properties

The selection of materials for the bottom tubesheet in heat exchangers, such as the Gas/Gas Heat Exchanger, is crucial for ensuring structural integrity and performance under operational conditions.

**Material Options:**
The primary material options for the bottom tubesheet include:
*   Stainless Steel
*   Carbon Steel

**Poisson's Ratio:**
For stainless steel, a key mechanical property considered in stress and deflection calculations is Poisson's Ratio (n).
*   Poisson's Ratio (n) for stainless steel: 0.305

Poisson's Ratio is a measure of the Poisson effect, which describes the expansion or contraction of a material in directions perpendicular to the direction of loading. This property is directly utilized in the calculation of geometry constants and shear forces for load distribution analysis, as detailed in [[4-cal-0011_gas_gas_hx_load_distribution_calculation_details]].

For comprehensive information on material and welding certifications for stainless steel components in Gas/Gas Heat Exchangers, refer to [[gas_gas_heat_exchanger_ss_material_and_welding_certifications]].
