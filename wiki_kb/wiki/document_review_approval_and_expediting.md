# Document Review, Approval, and Expediting

This page details the processes for document review and approval, including provisions for deemed approval, and outlines responsibilities for expediting documents to maintain project schedules.

**Owner Document Review and Approval:**
Documents provided to the Owner for review or approval are listed in the Project Document List. The Owner is required to review or approve these documents within [XX] calendar days of receipt.

**Deemed Approval Process:**
If a document or an approval notification is not received from the Owner within XX calendar days, [[document_control_1_prc_0003]] **shall** send a notification of deemed approval to the Owner. The necessity for issuing a deemed approval notice **shall** be established for each project individually. Owners who are chronically late in approving documents **must** be made aware that such delays may result in project milestones being impacted.

**Document Expediting Responsibilities:**
*   **Vendor Documents:** [[document_control_1_prc_0003]] **shall** issue Document Status Reports that list Vendor documents requiring expediting and return.
*   **Expediting Vendor Documents:** The Project Buyer is responsible for expediting Vendor documents from Vendors to ensure timely submission.
*   **Expediting Internal Review:** The Project Engineer is responsible for expediting the internal review and subsequent return of Vendor documents, ensuring that internal stakeholders provide feedback promptly.

For more general information on expediting vendor documents, refer to [[vendor_document_expediting]].

**Owner Document Review and Approval:**
*   The need for issuance of a deemed approval notice will be established for each project.
*   Owners who are chronically late in approving documents need to be made aware that project milestones may be delayed as a result.

**Vendor Document Expediting:**
*   Document Control will issue Document Status Reports listing the Vendor documents for expediting and return.
*   The Project Buyer is responsible for expediting Vendor documents from Vendors.
*   The Project Engineer is responsible for expediting the internal review and return of Vendor documents.
