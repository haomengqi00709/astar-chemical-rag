# SRS Filtration Unit Process Design Conditions

This page details the process design conditions for the SRS Filtration Unit, as specified in Document ID 4-PDS-XXXX-R1. These conditions are critical for the design and operation of the filtration unit, covering various fluid properties and operational parameters.

**Process Design Cases:**
The process design conditions are evaluated for two distinct cases:
*   **Case Description:** NEW
*   **Case Description:** END OF LIFE

**Fluid Descriptions and Composition:**
The filtration unit handles three primary fluid categories:
*   Feed
*   Permeate
*   Reject

The fluid composition is measured in **%w/w** and includes the following components:
*   NaCl
*   Na2SO4
*   NaClO3

**Process Parameters and Units:**
The following process parameters are specified with their respective units:
*   **Fluid Flow Rate unit:** m3/h
*   **Temperature unit:** °C
*   **Pressure unit:** kPa(g)
*   **Fluid Density unit:** kg/m3
*   **Fluid Viscosity unit:** cP
*   **pH:** pH is a critical process parameter.
*   **Recirculation Rate unit:** m3/h

These parameters are essential for understanding the operational envelope and performance requirements of the SRS Filtration Unit. For a general overview of process data sheets, refer to [[process_data_sheet_overview]]. Detailed process design criteria can be found in [[process_design_criteria_document]], and information on standard measurement units is available in [[engineering_units]].
