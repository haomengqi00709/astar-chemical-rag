# Agitator Coating and Lining Inspection

For agitator equipment covered by Quality Data Sheet **0-QDS-7418**, specific coating and lining inspections are mandated:

*   **External Coating / Lining Inspection:** This inspection **is required** as [[0-QDS-7418_agitator_quality_record_definitions|FP]] (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package). Chemetics **must** witness the External Coating / Lining Inspection.
*   **Spark Test:** A Spark Test **is required** as [[0-QDS-7418_agitator_quality_record_definitions|FP]] (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package) if the agitator is rubber lined. Chemetics **must** witness the Spark Test if the agitator is rubber lined.

These inspections ensure the integrity and quality of any applied coatings or linings, which are critical for corrosion protection and operational longevity.

For general information on coating design and specifications, refer to [[coatings_design_and_specification_7_prc_0002]].
