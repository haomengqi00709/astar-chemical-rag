# Floor-Specific Evacuation Details

This page provides specific details regarding evacuation assignments and designated areas for different floors within the office.

**References**
For detailed visual information, refer to floor maps and Appendix A of the 0-PRC-8010 procedure.

**Floor Wardens and Designated Areas**
Floor Wardens are listed in [[0-PRC-8010_table_5]].
Designated areas for Floor Wardens include:
*   3rd Floor
*   4th Floor

**Door Monitors and Exit Instructions**
Door Monitors are listed in [[0-PRC-8010_table_6]].
Specific instructions for exits on the 4th Floor:
*   Exit 4-4 **is not to be** used in an Emergency if exit 4-5 is clear and available.
*   The Door Monitor **will** redirect people to the nearest safe exit, 4-5.
