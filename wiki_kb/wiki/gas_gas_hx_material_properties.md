# Gas/Gas Heat Exchanger Material Properties

This page details the material selections and associated density properties for various components of the Gas/Gas Heat Exchanger, as referenced in the [[4-CAL-0026_total_load_calculation]]. These properties are essential for accurate weight and load calculations.

**Top Vestibule:**
*   Material options for the top vestibule include stainless steel and carbon steel.
*   Density (r1) for stainless steel (when used for the Top vestibule): 7999.0 kg/m3

**Shell:**
*   Shell material: stainless steel
*   Density (r2) for shell material: 7999.0 kg/m3

**Tubes:**
*   Tube material: stainless steel
*   Density (r3) for tube material: 7999.0 kg/m3

**Insulation:**
*   Insulation density (r4): 0.0 kg/m3
