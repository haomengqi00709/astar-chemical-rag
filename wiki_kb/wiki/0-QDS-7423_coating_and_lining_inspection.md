# Thickener Coating and Lining Inspection (0-QDS-7423)

This page outlines the requirements for coating and lining inspections for the Thickener equipment, as specified in Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7423.

**External Coating/Lining Inspection:**
*   External Coating/Lining Inspection **shall** require Chemetics Witness.
*   External Coating/Lining Inspection **shall** require Chemetics Hold.
*   This inspection is required to be included in the Vendor's Inspection and Test Plan.

**Internal Coating/Lining Inspection:**
*   Internal Coating/Lining Inspection is required as FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package).
*   Internal Coating/Lining Inspection **shall** require Chemetics Witness.
*   Internal Coating/Lining Inspection **shall** require Chemetics Hold.
*   A Spark test is required for Internal Coating/Lining Inspection.
*   This inspection is required to be included in the Vendor's Inspection and Test Plan.

**Field Inspection for Sectional Shipments:**
If the Thickener is shipped in more than one section for weld assembly and rubber lining in the field, then a spark test **shall** be performed in the field.

These inspections ensure the integrity and quality of all coatings and linings applied to the Thickener. For general coating and lining inspection procedures, refer to [[0-QDS-7414_coating_and_lining_inspection]].
