# Construction Subcontract Communication Protocol

This specification, 0-SPC-6003, outlines the mandatory communication and coordination procedures between Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Subcontractor during a construction subcontract. Adherence to these protocols ensures efficient information exchange and project progress.

**General Communication**
All communication **shall** be conducted via e-mail. E-mail communication **shall** conform to the guidelines specified in [[standard_conditions_for_construction_subcontract_0_spc_6001]].

**Communication Numbering**
*   **From Aker Kvaerner Chemetics to Subcontractor**: Communications will be sequentially numbered using the format CHM-[SUB]-XXX, where XXX is a sequential number.
*   **From Subcontractor to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics**: Communications will be sequentially numbered using the format [SUB]-CHM-XXX, where XXX is a sequential number.

The subject line of all e-mails **shall** contain the communication number, followed by a brief description of the content.

**Meetings**
*   **Progress Review Meetings**: These **will** be held weekly at the site to review project progress.
*   **Other Meetings**: Additional meetings **will** be held as requested by either party to address specific issues or discussions.
*   **Meeting Minutes**: Minutes for all meetings **shall** be kept, transcribed, and issued by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

For further details on meeting procedures, refer to [[subcontract_meeting_procedures]].

**Email Communication**
All communication shall be conducted via e-mail, in accordance with the guidelines set out in 0-SPC-6001: Standard Conditions for Construction Subcontracts.

E-mail communications from Aker Kvaerner Chemetics to the Subcontractor will be sequentially numbered using the format CHM-[SUB]-XXX, where XXX represents a sequential number. Similarly, communications from the Subcontractor to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics will use the format [SUB]-CHM-XXX, with XXX also being a sequential number.

The subject line of all e-mails must include the communication number, followed by a concise description of the content.

**Meetings**
Progress review meetings will be held weekly at the construction site. Additional meetings may be convened as requested by either Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or the Subcontractor. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics is responsible for keeping, transcribing, and issuing minutes for all meetings.
