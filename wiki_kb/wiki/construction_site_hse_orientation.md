# Construction Site HSE Orientation

Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) orientation for construction sites is a comprehensive process designed to ensure the safety of all personnel entering the work site.

**Responsibilities**
The [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_document_history|Site Health and Safety Manager]] is responsible for conducting HSE orientations for all personnel entering a work site. The [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_document_history|Construction Manager]] **will** ensure that each employee receives an initial Health and Safety orientation prior to starting work. They **will** also conduct an orientation with their employee(s) to review the potential hazards of specific operations in the work area. Furthermore, the Construction Manager **will** supervise the instruction and training of new employees, either personally or by delegating to experienced employees, until the new employee can satisfactorily demonstrate their ability to perform work safely.

**Pre-Travel Orientation for Site Personnel**
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics office employees or contract employees traveling to a project site **will** receive a safety orientation prior to departure, as outlined in section 4.2.1 of the source document. This orientation **will** be provided by the [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_document_history|Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Health and Safety Manager]]. In the absence of the HSE Manager, this responsibility **will** be transferred to the [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_document_history|Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Manager or Construction Services]] (for construction personnel) or the [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_document_history|Director of Technology]] (for commissioning personnel). The safety orientation prior to travel to site **will** follow the minimum outline given in Appendix 2 of the source document.

**Unescorted Personnel Expectations**
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics employees on a construction site **will** be considered "Unescorted Personnel" and **will** undergo the same orientation procedures as other unescorted individuals (section 4.4 of the source document). Unescorted personnel on a construction site **are expected** to act independently and, therefore, **will** receive a full safety orientation. They **are expected** to follow all Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, Aker Kvaerner, and Client safety policies, and **will** participate fully in the health, safety, and environment awareness programs used at the site.

**Orientation Delivery and Documentation**
The Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site safety orientation may include audio/visual presentations and oral discussions. The exact means of delivery **is left to be decided** by the Site Health and Safety Manager. Every worker entering the site for an unescorted visit **will** sign the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics safety orientation log, which **must** contain the following minimum information: Date, Name, Company, Department, and Social Security number. All employees attending the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics safety orientation **will** receive a copy of the "Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Construction Safety Handbook". The worksite health and safety orientation **will** follow the outline given in Appendix 3.03 of the Aker Kvaerner HSE Manual.
