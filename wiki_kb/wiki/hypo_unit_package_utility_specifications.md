# Hypo Unit Package Utility Specifications

This page details the required specifications for utilities supplied to the Hypo Unit Package.

**Demineralized Water:**
*   **Flow Rate**: m3/h (VTA) (Data available in SQL: Sheet1)
*   **Temperature**: oC (Data available in SQL: Sheet1)
*   **Pressure**: kPa(g) (Data available in SQL: Sheet1)

**Supply Caustic (NaOH):**
*   **Strength**: % w/w (Data available in SQL: Sheet1)
*   **Temperature**: oC (Data available in SQL: Sheet1)
*   **Pressure**: kPa(g) (Data available in SQL: Sheet1)
*   **Flow Rate**: m3/h (VTA) (Data available in SQL: Sheet1)

**Cooling Water:**
*   **Supply Pressure**: kPa(g) (Data available in SQL: Sheet1)
*   **Supply Temperature**: oC (Data available in SQL: Sheet1)
*   **Flow Rate**: m3/h (VTA) (Data available in SQL: Sheet1)
*   **Return Pressure**: kPa(g) (Data available in SQL: Sheet1)
*   **Return Temperature**: oC (Data available in SQL: Sheet1)

These utility specifications are crucial for the proper functioning and design of the Hypo Unit Package, impacting various aspects of [[plant_design_basis_and_capacity]] and [[utility_fluid_design_conditions]].
