# Refractory Brick Lining Surface Preparation

Thorough surface preparation is a mandatory prerequisite for the successful installation of refractory brick linings. The lining installer **shall** ensure that all interior metal surfaces designated for lining are meticulously cleaned.

**Cleaning Requirements**
All loose scale, rust, dirt, grease, and any other foreign material **must** be completely removed from these interior metal surfaces. This rigorous cleaning process is essential to ensure proper adhesion and long-term performance of the refractory lining.

**Inspection**
The effectiveness of this surface preparation is subject to inspection by a representative of the purchaser before the application of insulating fire brick. This pre-application inspection is specifically to ensure that metal surfaces have been prepared in accordance with this specification. For more details on inspection stages, refer to [[refractory_brick_lining_inspection_and_acceptance]].
