# Roles and Responsibilities: Receptionist

The Receptionist plays a critical role in communication and initial response during an emergency evacuation or fire drill.

**During Fire Alarm or Emergency Requiring Evacuation**

*   **If in immediate danger:**
    *   The Receptionist **must** put the answering machine onto the nightline.
    *   The Receptionist **must** unplug the receiver.
    *   The Receptionist **must** vacate the building through the nearest exit.

*   **Otherwise (if not in immediate danger but evacuation is required):**
    *   The Receptionist **must** inform any incoming external calls on the Switchboard: "A fire alarm has been activated. We are required to vacate the building. Could you please call back in 20 minutes?” The Receptionist **must be** polite but firm when informing callers of an emergency.
    *   The Receptionist **must** make the following announcement twice, with a brief pause in between: "Attention all employees - Due to a possible fire condition in the building, please evacuate your area by the nearest stairwell. Do not use the elevators." The Receptionist **must** speak calmly and clearly during the announcement.
    *   If time permits, the Receptionist **must** change the message on the answering machine (or by inserting the "Fire Alarm" tape) to say: "We are having a potential fire situation. Sorry for the inconvenience. Please call back in 20 minutes".
    *   The Receptionist **must** put the answering machine onto the nightline.
    *   The Receptionist **must** unplug the receiver.
    *   The Receptionist **must** vacate the building through the nearest exit.

**After Returning to the Switchboard**
*   The Receptionist **must** resume normal procedures.
*   The Receptionist **must** reset the answering machine message when time permits.

**During a Fire Drill**
*   Advanced notice **will be** provided to the receptionist for all drills.
*   Receptionist procedures are the same as above, except **do not** evacuate the building.
*   The Receptionist **must** continue to operate the switchboard.
*   The Receptionist **must** notify callers using: " is having a fire drill. Could you please call back in 20 minutes?"
