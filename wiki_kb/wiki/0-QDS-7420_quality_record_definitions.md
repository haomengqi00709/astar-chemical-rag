# Filter Press Quality Data Sheet: Quality Record Definitions

For the Filter Press Quality Data Sheet (0-QDS-7420), the following abbreviations define the requirements for Quality Records:

*   **AP (As Prepared):** This designation indicates that the Quality Record is required "As Prepared" by the Vendor.
*   **FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package):** This designation indicates that the Quality Record **must** be included as part of the comprehensive Quality Assurance Documentation Package submitted by the Vendor.
