# Aker Kvaerner Construction Site Advisor Responsibilities

Construction Site Advisors play a critical role in ensuring the safety and operational efficiency of project sites. Their responsibilities encompass various aspects of site management, safety compliance, and resource identification. This page outlines key mandatory requirements and recommendations for personnel operating in this capacity, drawing from administrative guidelines such as 0-REP-8400.

**Safety and Compliance Requirements**
Contractor personnel **must** obtain copies of all client safety rules, procedures, or manuals relevant to the project. This mandatory requirement ensures that all site activities are conducted in strict adherence to established safety protocols and client-specific guidelines. Familiarity with these documents is fundamental to maintaining a safe working environment and preventing incidents on [[hse_construction_sites]]. This also ties into the broader [[aker_kvaerner_construction_site_advisor_hse_requirements]].

**Emergency Preparedness**
Emergency action plans, procedures, and alarms **must not** be overlooked during site setup and ongoing operations. Construction Site Advisors are responsible for ensuring that these critical safety measures are in place, clearly communicated, and regularly reviewed. This proactive approach is vital for effective [[emergency_procedures_and_first_aid]] and response in unforeseen circumstances, contributing to the overall safety of all personnel on site.

**Site Utilities Information**
For effective site planning and operations, a comprehensive understanding of available infrastructure is essential. Utilities available at or near the site **must** be listed. This mandatory requirement facilitates efficient resource management, safety planning, and coordination with external service providers. Furthermore, it is recommended that names and contact information of area utility representatives should be obtained if possible. This proactive measure enhances communication channels and allows for rapid response in case of utility-related issues or emergencies, supporting overall [[construction_planning_and_control_0_prc_6005]] and [[construction_site_management_roles_0_prc_600]]. Accurate and up-to-date utility information is a cornerstone of robust site management.

Key mandatory requirements for site management include ensuring that contractor personnel obtain copies of all client safety rules, procedures, or manuals. Furthermore, emergency action plans, procedures, and alarms must be thoroughly developed and implemented. For effective resource identification, all utilities available at or near the site must be listed, and the names and contact information of area utility representatives should be obtained if possible.
