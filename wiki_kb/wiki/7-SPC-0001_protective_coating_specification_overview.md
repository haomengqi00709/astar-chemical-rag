# Protective Coating Specification Overview (7-SPC-0001)

The `7-SPC-0001 Protective Coating Specification` defines the minimum painting requirements for assemblies and components intended for use in corrosive environments, such as acid plants and electrochemical facilities. This specification consolidates and replaces previous documents, specifically `7-SPC-0002` and `7-SPC-0003`, and is now the sole governing coatings specification.

**Scope and Responsibilities**
The Vendor **must** be responsible for all painting requirements of process equipment, piping, pumps, tanks, and structural steel, including any non-galvanized items. Selected paint systems, as detailed in this specification, are not suitable for immersion in acid or alkaline environments. In the event of spills, affected areas **must** be washed down with water immediately.

**Unpainted Surfaces**
Certain surfaces are explicitly excluded from painting requirements. These include:
*   Nameplates
*   Shaft seals
*   Stainless steels and other high alloys (unless specifically stated otherwise)
*   Rubber, plastic, and glass components
*   Valve stems
*   Movable linkage on equipment
*   Grease fittings
*   Compressor or pump shafts
*   Pressure gauges and instrument glasses
*   Other similar items
*   Machined surfaces (e.g., flange gasket faces)
*   Any surfaces on equipment normally lubricated or with close working tolerances
*   Structural steel that has been galvanized or is designated for fireproofing

**Paint Manufacturer Data and Approval**
Copies of paint manufacturer’s data sheets **must** be provided to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics prior to paint application. Paint manufacturers not explicitly listed in this specification **require** prior approval from Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
