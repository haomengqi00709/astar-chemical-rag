# Refractory Brick Lining Specification Overview (4-SPC-0082)

This specification (4-SPC-0082) defines the [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_obligations_0_spc_2201|Aker Kvaerner Chemetics]] requirements for refractory brick linings, which are installed in steel vessels and ducting.

The Vendor **shall** provide the detail design of the refractory brick lining system. This detail design includes, but is not limited to, the mechanical design of the brick lining. The mechanical design **shall** consider the radial thermal expansion of both the lining and the steel shell, as well as the interaction between them.

The Vendor **shall** design longitudinal expansion joints within the brick lining and **shall** design thermal insulation external to the vessel. Furthermore, the Vendor **shall** design criteria for the vessel in which the lining will be installed.

The Vendor **shall** provide comprehensive installation and curing instructions, along with dry-out (curing) curves for the refractory lining system.

The final lining detail design is the sole responsibility of the Vendor, who is also responsible for the performance of the lining. The Vendor **will** clearly state where their recommendations differ from [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_obligations_0_spc_2201|Aker Kvaerner Chemetics]] basic design criteria.

The refractory lining system **must** be designed with sufficient expansion joints to accommodate thermal expansion, which **must** be anticipated at the maximum gas operating temperature specified on the equipment drawings. The Vendor **will** submit calculations to demonstrate this thermal expansion accommodation.

The Vendor **will** guarantee that the lining system is correct for its intended [[regeneration_furnace_process_and_design_conditions|application]] and **will** require no repairs for a period of 5 years. Additionally, the Vendor **will** guarantee a life of 20 years for the lining system with minimal maintenance repairs.

For specific material requirements, refer to [[4-SPC-0082_refractory_brick_lining_materials_and_supply]]. For detailed design parameters, see [[4-SPC-0082_refractory_brick_lining_design_requirements]]. Installation and curing responsibilities are further detailed in [[4-SPC-0082_refractory_brick_lining_installation_and_curing]].
