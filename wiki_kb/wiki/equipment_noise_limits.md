# Equipment Noise Limits

As per document 4-SPC-0001, all equipment supplied **must** comply with specified noise limits to ensure a safe working environment.

**Operational Noise Limits**
All equipment **shall meet** a noise limit of 85 dB(A) when measured one metre from the equipment. This standard applies to equipment operating under normal conditions.

**Excursion-Specific Noise Limits**
Equipment that operates only during plant excursions (e.g., relief valves) **must** be designed to minimize noise levels, with particular regard for personnel safety. Noise from equipment operating during plant excursions **shall comply** with a limit of LC, peak 140 dB(C) (a C-weighted sound pressure level of 140 dB(C), referenced to 20 micropascals) measured at any location that has personnel access. This higher limit acknowledges the infrequent and emergency nature of such operations while still ensuring a maximum safe exposure level.

**Vendor Responsibilities**
The Vendor **will** submit expected noise levels and supporting data for the equipment with their quotation. If the expected noise levels are anticipated to exceed the specified requirements, the Vendor **must** propose a noise reduction system, complete with cost implications, for Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ consideration. This proactive approach ensures that potential noise issues are addressed during the procurement phase.

**Noise Limits for Plant Excursion Equipment**
Equipment that operates during plant excursions only (such as relief valves) will be designed to minimize noise levels, having particular regard for personnel safety.
Noise from equipment operating during plant excursions will comply with a limit of LC, peak 140 dB(C) (a C-weighted sound pressure level of 140 dB(C), referenced to 20 micropascals) measured at any location that has personnel access.

**Vendor Noise Information and Mitigation**
The Vendor will submit expected noise levels and data for the equipment with the quotation.
If noise levels are expected to exceed specified requirements, the Vendor will propose a noise reduction system, complete with cost, for Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ consideration.
