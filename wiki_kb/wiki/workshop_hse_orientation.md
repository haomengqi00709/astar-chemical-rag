# Workshop HSE Orientation

Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) orientations for the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Equipment Division workshops are a critical component of ensuring a safe working environment.

**Responsibility**
Workshop Orientations for the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Equipment Division **are to be done** by Bob Vanderland. This ensures that all personnel working within or visiting the workshops receive consistent and accurate safety information specific to the workshop environment.
