# Stack Nozzle Sizing Criteria

This page outlines the sizing criteria applied to the various nozzles on the stack, ensuring optimal performance and accessibility.

**Nozzle Sizing Criteria:**
*   **Nozzle Gas Inlet (Mark A) Sizing Criteria:** 25.0 m/s Gas Velocity
*   **Nozzle Gas Outlet (Mark B) Sizing Criteria:** 15.0 m/s Minimum Gas Velocity
*   **Nozzle Drain (Mark C) Sizing Criteria:** Standard
*   **Nozzle Sample ports (Mark S1-S4) Sizing Criteria:** Standard
*   **Nozzle Manway (Mark M) Sizing Criteria:** Standard

These criteria are used in conjunction with the [[stack_physical_specifications_and_materials]] to determine the final dimensions and placement of each nozzle.
