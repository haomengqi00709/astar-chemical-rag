# Vortex Breaker Installation

This page details the requirements for the installation of [[vortex_breaker_installation|vortex breakers]] in vessels and tanks, as indicated on [[piping_and_instrumentation_drawings|P&IDs]]. Correct gasket position **must** comply with special item drawings to ensure proper functionality and prevent vortex formation at outlets.
