# Vendor Quality Assurance Requirements for 0-QDS-7404-1

This page outlines the mandatory quality assurance requirements for the vendor supplying the Electrostatic Precipitator Shell & Head Sections-Dual Laminate, as specified in Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7404-1.

The vendor **must** prepare a Quality Plan. This plan **shall** be in accordance with the requirements specified in [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment]]: Vendor Quality Assurance.

The vendor's overall Quality Assurance program **shall** comply with the specific requirements detailed in this Quality Data Sheet (0-QDS-7404-1) and with the general guidelines provided in [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment]]: Vendor Quality Assurance.

**Mandatory Requirement:** If a Quality Plan is not required for a specific aspect, the corresponding cells in the documentation **shall** be deleted to reflect this.

For details on Chemetics' surveillance level, refer to [[0-QDS-7404-1_chemetics_surveillance_level]]. For specific inspection and documentation requirements, see [[0-QDS-7404-1_inspection_and_documentation_requirements]].

The vendor **must** prepare a Quality Plan. This plan **shall** be in accordance with the requirements of 0-SPC-7001: Vendor Quality Assurance. The vendor's Quality Assurance processes **must** comply with both this Quality Data Sheet and 0-SPC-7001: Vendor Quality Assurance.

**Note**: If a Quality Plan is not required for a specific item, the corresponding cells in the document should be deleted.
