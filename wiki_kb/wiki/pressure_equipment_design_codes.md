# Pressure Equipment Design Codes and Certifications

As per document 4-SPC-0001, all pressure-containing equipment **must** be designed, fabricated, and tested in accordance with specific industry codes and standards.

**Pressure Vessels and Heat Exchangers**
*   Pressure vessels **will be designed**, fabricated, and tested in accordance with ASME Section VIII, Division 1.
*   Heat exchangers **will be** in accordance with ASME Section VIII, Division 1 and in accordance with TEMA Class “C”.
*   All pressure vessels and heat exchangers **will be “U” stamped**, signifying compliance with ASME Boiler and Pressure Vessel Code.

**Boilers, Superheaters, and Economisers**
*   Boilers (and the tube side of superheaters and economisers) **will be designed**, fabricated, and tested in accordance with ASME Section I.
*   All boilers, superheaters, and economisers **will be “S” stamped**, indicating compliance with ASME Boiler and Pressure Vessel Code for power boilers.

**Low-Pressure Metallic Vessels and Tanks**
*   Metallic vessels (other than flat bottom tanks) operating at less than 100 kPag (15 psig) **will be designed** and fabricated in accordance with ASME Section VIII, Division 1.
*   Flat bottom metallic tanks operating at less than 100 kPag (15 psig) but greater than 17 kPag (2½ psig) **will be designed** and fabricated to API 620.
*   Flat bottom metallic tanks operating at less than 17 kPag (2½ psig) **will be designed** and fabricated to API 650.

These mandatory requirements ensure the highest standards of safety and reliability for all pressure equipment.

**Heat Exchanger Standards**
Heat exchangers will be in accordance with ASME Section VIII, Division 1 and in accordance with TEMA Class “C”.

**Stamping Requirements**
All pressure vessels and heat exchangers will be “U” stamped.

**Boilers and Related Equipment**
Boilers (and the tube side of superheaters and economisers) will be designed, fabricated and tested in accordance with ASME Section I.
All boilers, superheaters, and economisers will be “S” stamped.

**Low-Pressure Metallic Vessels and Tanks**
Metallic vessels (other than flat bottom tanks) operating at less than 100 kPag (15 psig) will be designed and fabricated in accordance with ASME Section VIII, Division 1.
Flat bottom metallic tanks operating at less than 100 kPag (15 psig) but greater than 17 kPag (2½ psig) will be designed and fabricated to API 620.
Flat bottom metallic tanks operating at less than 17 kPag (2½ psig) will be designed and fabricated to API 650.
