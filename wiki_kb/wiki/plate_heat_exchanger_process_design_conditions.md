# Plate Heat Exchanger Process Design Conditions

This page details the required process design conditions for plate heat exchangers, as specified in Document ID 4-DST-XXXX-R1. These parameters are crucial for the accurate sizing, selection, and operation of the equipment.

**General Process Parameters**
*   **Quantity:** Operating, Installed Spare
*   **Heat Duty:** (kW)
*   **Fluid Description:** Hot Side, Cold Side
*   **Component Flows:** (Inlet, Outlet) for Hot Side and Cold Side
*   **Total Flowrate:** (kg/h)
*   **Temperature:** (°C)
*   **Pressure:** (kPa(abs))

**Fluid-Specific Properties**

For each fluid (liquid, vapour, non-condensable), the following properties must be specified:

*   **Liquid Phase:**
    *   Liquid Flow (kg/h)
    *   Density (kg/m3)
    *   Viscosity (cP)
    *   Specific Heat (kJ/kg·°C)
    *   Thermal Conductivity (W/m·°C)
    *   Bubble Point (°C)
    *   Enthalpy (kJ/kg)

*   **Vapour Phase:**
    *   Vapour Flow (kg/h)
    *   Density (kg/m3)
    *   Viscosity (cP)
    *   Specific Heat (kJ/kg·°C)
    *   Thermal Conductivity (W/m·°C)
    *   Molecular Weight (kg/kgmol)
    *   Dew Point (°C)
    *   Enthalpy (kJ/kg)

*   **Non-Condensable Phase:**
    *   Non-Condensable Flow (kg/h)
    *   Density (kg/m3)
    *   Viscosity (cP)
    *   Specific Heat (kJ/kg·°C)
    *   Thermal Conductivity (W/m·°C)
    *   Molecular Weight (kg/kgmol)

**Allowable Pressure Drop**
The allowable pressure drop for each side of the heat exchanger is specified as:
*   Hot Side Inlet: 23 kPa
*   Hot Side Outlet: 23 kPa
*   Cold Side Inlet: 23 kPa
*   Cold Side Outlet: 23 kPa
*   An additional allowable pressure drop of 23 kPa is specified.

For general information on calculating pressure drops in process equipment, refer to [[pressure_drop_calculations]].

**Excess Area**
*   **Excess Area:** A required parameter (%), indicating the percentage of surface area provided beyond the minimum calculated requirement.

For related information on heat exchanger performance and design, see [[heat_transfer_coefficients]] and the [[plate_and_frame_heat_exchanger_quality_data_sheet_overview]].

**Pressure Drop and Area Requirements**
*   **Allowable Pressure Drop:**
    *   Hot Side Inlet: 23 kPa
    *   Hot Side Outlet: 23 kPa
    *   Cold Side Inlet: 23 kPa
    *   Cold Side Outlet: 23 kPa
    *   General: 23 kPa
*   **Excess Area:** Required parameter (%).
