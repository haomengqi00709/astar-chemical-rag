# Acid Cooler Vendor Quality Assurance Requirements (0-QDS-7411)

This page details the vendor quality assurance requirements for the Acid Cooler, as specified in document [[0-QDS-7411_document_details|0-QDS-7411]].

The equipment is manufactured by the Chemetics Equipment Division.

**Mandatory Requirements:**
*   Equipment manufacturing **shall** be in accordance with the CED Quality Control Manual, Latest Edition.
*   Equipment manufacturing **shall** be in accordance with this Quality Data Sheet [[0-QDS-7411_document_details|0-QDS-7411]].

These requirements ensure that the manufacturing process adheres to established quality standards and specific project specifications.

For general vendor quality assurance information, refer to [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment]] or [[0-SPC-7002_vendor_quality_assurance]].
Other Chemetics-specific vendor quality assurance requirements can be found in [[0-QDS-7404-1_vendor_quality_assurance_requirements]].
