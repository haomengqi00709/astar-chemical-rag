# Metal Butterfly Damper Packaging and Shipping

The packaging and shipping of metal butterfly dampers **SHALL** adhere to specific requirements to ensure their safe delivery and integrity.

**General Packaging and Shipping Standards:**
All packaging and shipping procedures **SHALL** be in accordance with either [[packaging_land_transportation]] (0-SPC-5004: Packaging Land Transport) or [[packaging_marine_transport]] (0-SPC-5005: Packaging Marine Transport), depending on the mode of transportation.

**Separate Shipment of Components:**
After the completion of functional checks, the actuation equipment **SHALL** be demounted from the damper body.
*   Actuation equipment **MUST** be clearly tagged with identification corresponding to the appropriate valve body to facilitate correct re-assembly at the installation site.
*   Damper bodies and actuation equipment **SHALL** be shipped separately. This separation helps prevent damage to the more delicate actuation components during transit and simplifies handling.

**Protection of Connections:**
All grease and air purge connections on the damper body **SHALL** be securely plugged prior to packaging and shipping. This prevents ingress of contaminants and protects the internal systems during transit.
