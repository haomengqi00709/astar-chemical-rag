# Owner Roles and Responsibilities

This page outlines the general roles and responsibilities of the Owner within the context of projects and procurement, particularly as referenced in Purchase Order [[0-SPC-5001_purchase_order_overview|0-SPC-5001]].

**Definition and Relationship**
The Owner is the entity for whom Aker Kvaerner Chemetics is constructing, designing, and/or procuring the Project facilities. The Owner is a key stakeholder whose interests are paramount to the project's success.

**Key Interactions and Rights**
While Aker Kvaerner Chemetics directly manages the Vendor through the Purchase Order, the Owner has specific rights and benefits:

*   **Project Definition:** The Project is defined as the facilities being constructed and/or designed and procured by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for the Owner.
*   **Warranties Benefit:** The warranties provided by the Vendor for the [[goods_and_services_definitions|Goods and Services]] **shall** benefit the Owner, in addition to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics's successors and assigns.
*   **Quality System Access:** The Owner **shall** be granted access to the Vendor's and any Suppliers' quality systems for monitoring and quality auditing purposes.
*   **Access to Work:** The Vendor **must** provide the Owner with access to work being done or goods and materials being stored for the Purchase Order.
*   **Termination Costs:** In the event Aker Kvaerner Chemetics terminates the Purchase Order for a reason other than the Vendor's default, the amount due on termination **may** include other costs reasonably incurred by the Vendor which Aker Kvaerner Chemetics is able to recover from the Owner.

The Owner's involvement ensures that the ultimate recipient of the project assets has oversight and protection regarding the quality and compliance of the procured goods and services.
