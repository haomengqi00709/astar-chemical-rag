# RFQ and Purchase Order Contact Information

This page specifies the required contact information to be included in Requests for Quotation (RFQs) and Purchase Orders (POs), and outlines communication protocols.

**Required Contact Details:**
Procurement **shall** insert their own particulars to serve as the commercial contact for the RFQ or PO.
The particulars of the responsible [[discipline_lead_and_approver_roles|Discipline Lead]] **shall** be inserted as the technical contact.
The particulars of the responsible Inspector **shall** be included.
The particulars of the Vendor’s responsible personnel **shall** be inserted.

**Communication Protocol:**
Any communication related to the RFQ or PO **must** be copied to both parties involved, as further detailed in Section 10 of the originating procedure.

This ensures clear lines of communication and accountability, aligning with [[purchase_order_contact_information_and_query_protocol]].
