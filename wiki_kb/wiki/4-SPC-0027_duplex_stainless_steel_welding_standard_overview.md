# Duplex Stainless Steel Welding Standard Overview (4-SPC-0027)

Specification 4-SPC-0027 establishes the fundamental minimum workmanship and quality requirements for welded fabrications utilizing duplex stainless steel. This comprehensive standard is specifically designed for application to fabrications purchased by or on behalf of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, ensuring consistent quality across all relevant projects. Its provisions become mandatory when explicitly called for on official purchase orders and other referenced project documentation.

The scope of this specification is broad, covering essential welding and fabrication requirements for a diverse array of equipment. This includes critical components such as pressure vessels, heat exchangers, storage tanks, intricate piping heater coils, and various other miscellaneous welded items. The primary material addressed by this standard is duplex stainless steel with UNS designation S32205, commonly referred to as Alloy 2205.

A crucial prerequisite for the application of this specification is that the minimum design metal temperature (MDMT) for the fabricated equipment **must** be maintained above -30°C. This temperature constraint is vital for ensuring the structural integrity and performance of the material under the specified operating conditions, particularly concerning its metallurgical properties and resistance to brittle fracture. Adherence to these guidelines is paramount for the safety and longevity of the fabricated components.
