# HCl Synthesis Unit Cooling Water Specifications

This page details the specifications for the cooling water utility stream required by the HCl Synthesis Unit, as documented in [[4-PDS-XXXX-R1_template_hcl_synthesis_unit_package_datasheet_administration]]. Cooling water is an indispensable utility for heat removal from various process sections, ensuring stable operating temperatures, protecting heat-sensitive equipment, and maintaining the overall thermal balance of the synthesis unit.

**Cooling Water Utility Parameters:**
The cooling water supply is characterized by the following design conditions, which **must** be consistently met for reliable operation:
*   **Flow Rate:** VTA kg/h
*   **Design Temperature:** VTA oC
*   **Supply Pressure (Note 4):** kPa(g) (value not specified)
*   **Pressure Drop (Note 7):** VTA kPa(g)

The specified flow rate and design temperature are critical for the effective heat transfer performance of the unit's heat exchangers and coolers. These parameters directly impact the ability to maintain process temperatures within safe and optimal ranges. The supply pressure of the cooling water is subject to **Note 4**, which may include minimum or maximum pressure requirements necessary for adequate distribution throughout the system. The allowable pressure drop across the cooling water system, specified as VTA kPa(g), is a key factor for pump sizing, piping design, and ensuring efficient circulation. This parameter is further clarified by **Note 7**, which may provide additional design considerations or operational guidelines.

Maintaining these parameters is crucial for the thermal management, operational stability, and overall efficiency of the HCl Synthesis Unit. For general information on utility fluid design conditions, refer to [[utility_fluid_design_conditions]].
