# HAZOP Record Structure and Content

The HAZOP (Hazard and Operability) record is a structured document used to systematically identify potential hazards and operability problems in a process design. Each record typically references a specific process node, such as Node 1.0, to focus the analysis.

Key fields within the HAZOP record include:
*   **PID Reference:** Linking the analysis to specific [[p_id_development_and_approval_1_prc_0003|Piping and Instrumentation Diagrams]].
*   **Equipment/Line No.:** Identifying the specific equipment or pipeline being analyzed. Relevant data can be found in [[equipment_data_sheets_4_dst_xxxx]] and [[line_list_5_lst_0001]].
*   **Function/Design Intent:** Describing the intended operation and purpose of the system under review.

The analysis is organized into several categories to ensure comprehensive coverage:
*   **Materials/Inputs:** Examining the properties and flow of substances entering the system.
*   **Mechanical Design:** Reviewing the physical construction and integrity of components.
*   **Activities:** Assessing operational steps and procedures.
*   **Sources:** Identifying origins of materials or energy.
*   **Destinations:** Determining where materials or energy are directed.

Items within the HAZOP record are further distinguished as either "Continuous or Primary" or "Intermittent / Secondary" to categorize their operational nature. A dedicated section, "Items Raised in HAZOP," compiles all identified issues and concerns.

The core of the HAZOP record is presented in a tabular format, with the following columns:
*   **Ref.:** A unique reference number for each identified deviation.
*   **Guideword Deviation:** The specific deviation from design intent, often derived from standard HAZOP guidewords. For a comprehensive list, see [[hazop_guideword_deviations]].
*   **Causes:** Potential reasons for the deviation to occur.
*   **Consequences:** The potential outcomes or impacts of the deviation.
*   **Safeguards:** Existing protective measures or controls in place.
*   **Questions:** Further inquiries or points for clarification.
*   **Action By:** The individual or department responsible for addressing the identified action item.

**HAZOP Record Categories:**
The HAZOP record categorizes items under the following headings:
*   Materials/Inputs
*   Mechanical Design
*   Activities
*   Sources
*   Destinations

**Item Classification:**
The record distinguishes between "Continuous or Primary" and "Intermittent / Secondary" items for detailed analysis.

**HAZOP Findings Section:**
A dedicated section is included for "Items Raised in HAZOP" to document findings and recommendations.

**HAZOP Table Columns:**
The main HAZOP record table includes the following columns:
*   Ref.
*   Guideword Deviation
*   Causes
*   Consequences
*   Safeguards
*   Questions
*   Action By
