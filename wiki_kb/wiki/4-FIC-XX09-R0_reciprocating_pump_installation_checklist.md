# Reciprocating Pump Mechanical and Electrical Installation Checklist (4-FIC-XX09-R0)

This checklist outlines the mandatory requirements for the mechanical and electrical installation of reciprocating pumps, as specified in the Field Inspection Checklist document 4-FIC-XX09-R0. Adherence to these items is crucial for ensuring the safe and efficient operation of the pump.

**Mandatory Installation Requirements (Items 1.0 to 23.0):**
*   Correct Nameplate **must** be installed.
*   Pump position **must** be checked (elevation, orientation, leveled).
*   Foundation / Anchor bolts **must** be secure and tightened.
*   Grouting **must** be completed.
*   Suction/discharge piping **must** be properly aligned and supported (no stress on pump nozzles).
*   Auxiliary systems **must** be complete (seal flush, water cooling, etc.).
*   Mechanical seal / packing **must** be installed.
*   Accessories **must** be installed (gauges, vents, drain valves, connections, etc.).
*   Pulsation Damper **must** be installed and filled with appropriate fluid.
*   Relief valve **must** be installed & set at correct pressure.
*   Check-valves **must** be installed in correct orientation.
*   Grounding **must** be completed.
*   Bearings **must** be flushed and filled to correct level.
*   Free rotation of pump **must** be checked by hand.
*   Motor rotation **must** be verified by electrical contractor (attach report required).
*   Cold alignment **must** be completed.
*   Coupling **must** be installed and lubricated.
*   Coupling guard **must** be installed and secure.
*   Jacking screws **must** be removed.
*   Temporary suction strainers **must** be in place.
*   External painting **must** be completed with no visible defects.
*   Insulation **must** be complete.
*   Flange Guards **must** be installed.
