# Gas/Gas Hx Load Distribution Calculation Overview (4-CAL-0011)

This document, 4-CAL-0011-R1, details the calculation for the distribution of axial load between the bottom vestibule and core of a Gas/Gas Heat Exchanger. The calculation was prepared by Chemetics, a Division of Aker Kvaerner Canada Inc., for the Aker Kvaerner Gas/Gas Hx Load Distribution project.

The primary purpose of this spreadsheet-based calculation is to determine the resultant axial load on the core (f2). This value is critical for checking the cone-to-cylinder junction within the bottom vestibule. Further details on these mechanical checks can be found in [[4-prc-0009_gas_gas_exchanger_mechanical_calculations]].
