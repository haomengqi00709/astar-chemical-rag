# Offer Evaluation and Acceptance Rights

Aker Kvaerner Chemetics retains full discretion in the evaluation and acceptance of offers, with several key rights reserved throughout the procurement process.

**Evaluation Process:**
Offers will be evaluated at the sole discretion of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, considering overall performance, delivery capabilities, and value. The evaluation process will explicitly take into account any deviations and exceptions included in the submitted offer. For more details on the evaluation process, refer to [[bid_evaluation_and_approval_0_prc_5003]], [[commercial_and_financial_bid_evaluation]], [[detailed_cost_bid_evaluation]], [[detailed_technical_bid_evaluation]], and [[vendor_bid_evaluation_procedure_0_prc_5003]].

**Rights Reserved by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics:**
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics reserves the right to accept or reject any offer, either in whole or in part.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics reserves the right to reject all offers, with or without notice or stated reasons.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics reserves the right to accept an offer that is not the lowest priced.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics reserves the right to accept an offer that is not in full compliance with the Request for Quotation.
*   If no offer is deemed acceptable, the work may be abandoned or performed in any manner selected by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Bidder Status and Costs:**
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics makes no representation that any Bidder is pre-qualified or acceptable merely by virtue of having received or responded to a Request for Quotation.
Unsuccessful Bidders will be formally advised within ten working days of the award of a [[purchase_order_approval_and_authorization|Purchase Order]] for the Goods and Services to another party.
No costs incurred by the Bidder, any Supplier, or other parties in preparing the Bidder’s offer will be reimbursed by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

These reserved rights ensure flexibility and optimal decision-making for Aker Kvaerner Chemetics in securing the best value and performance for its projects.
