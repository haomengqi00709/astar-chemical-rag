# Vendor Personnel Competence and Training

The competence of personnel performing quality-related activities is paramount to achieving the quality objectives outlined in Specification [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment|0-SPC-7001]]. The Vendor **shall** ensure that all personnel involved in such activities are adequately qualified and trained.

**Experience and Background**
Vendor’s personnel responsible for performing quality-related activities **must** possess the necessary experience or background to effectively perform their assigned functions. This ensures that tasks are carried out by individuals with the requisite knowledge and practical skills.

**Training, Certification, and Qualification**
Personnel **shall** receive appropriate training, certification, or qualification relevant to their roles. This includes:
*   **Training:** Providing instruction on specific procedures, equipment, and quality standards.
*   **Certification:** Obtaining formal recognition of competence from an authorized body, where applicable (e.g., welding certifications, NDE certifications).
*   **Qualification:** Demonstrating the ability to perform specific tasks to a required standard.

**Familiarity with Purchase Order Requirements**
All personnel involved in quality-related activities **must** be familiar with the requirements of the Purchase Order. This ensures that they understand the specific quality standards, specifications, and expectations for the goods being supplied.
