# Shop Visit Safety Orientation Requirements

Upon arrival at any vendor workshop or fabricating shop, Aker Kvaerner Chemetics employees **must** request and participate in a safety orientation provided by the host facility. This orientation is crucial for understanding the specific hazards and safety protocols unique to that site.

**Mandatory Orientation Topics**
The minimum topics that **must** be covered during the safety orientation include:
*   **Personal Protection Equipment (PPE) Requirements:** Specific types of PPE mandated by the vendor or shop owner, which may supplement or differ from standard company requirements. Refer to [[shop_visit_personal_protective_equipment]] for general company guidelines.
*   **Emergency Signals and Safe Meeting Location:** Understanding the facility's emergency alarm systems and designated assembly points in case of an evacuation. This aligns with general [[emergency_procedures_and_first_aid]].
*   **Location of Emergency Wash Stations:** Identification of eyewash stations and safety showers for chemical exposure.
*   **How to Summon First Aid:** Procedures for reporting injuries or illnesses and accessing first aid services.
*   **Typical Shop Hazards and Ongoing Work:** Awareness of common hazards present in the shop environment, such as moving machinery, hot work, or specific ongoing operations like radiography or hydro testing, which may present immediate risks.

**Pre-Visit Preparation**
Employees **must** endeavor to find out the shop safety requirements in advance of their visit. This proactive approach allows for better preparation and ensures that all necessary safety measures can be taken. Additionally, employees **must** ask their host about any specific safety procedures they are required to observe throughout their visit.
