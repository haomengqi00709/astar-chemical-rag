# 0-POL-9114 Statutory and Additional Holidays

This section of document 0-POL-9114, the Vacation Policy, outlines the company's observance of public holidays and additional paid days off for eligible employees.

**British Columbia Statutory Holidays**
All statutory holidays recognized by the province of British Columbia are observed at Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ Vancouver office. Employees are entitled to these days off with pay as per provincial regulations. These holidays are mandated by law and provide employees with designated days of rest throughout the year. For a broader understanding of company policies regarding holidays, refer to [[0_pol_9111_statutory_holidays]].

**Additional Christmas Period Days Off**
In addition to the standard statutory holidays, employees are granted 3 additional days off with pay. These days are specifically allocated between the Christmas statutory holiday and the New Year's statutory holiday. This provision aims to provide employees with an extended and cohesive break during the festive season, promoting rest and family time.

**Policy Revisions Regarding Additional Days**
The inclusion of these additional days off during the Christmas period was explicitly added as a reference in Revision 1 of this policy. This formalization ensured that this benefit was clearly documented and communicated to all eligible employees, reinforcing the company's commitment to employee well-being during the holiday season. Details of this and other revisions can be found in [[0-pol-9114_document_revision_history]].
