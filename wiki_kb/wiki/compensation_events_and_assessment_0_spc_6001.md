# Compensation Events and Assessment (0-SPC-6001)

This page details the definition, notification, and assessment process for compensation events under construction subcontract 0-SPC-6001.

**Definition of Compensation Events**
A compensation event is defined by various occurrences, including:
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics instructing a change to the [[standard_conditions_for_construction_subcontract_0_spc_6001#Works Information|Works Information]] (unless to make a [[standard_conditions_for_construction_subcontract_0_spc_6001#Defect|Defect]] acceptable).
*   A difference between the final total quantity of work done and the quantity stated for an item in the Price List.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics instructing to stop or not to start any work.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics changing a previously communicated decision.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics instructing a search for a Defect, and none is found.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics not allowing access to and use of the site as necessary for the work.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics not replying to a communication within the required period.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics not providing something it is required to provide by the stated date.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics notifying a correction to a previously stated assumption about a compensation event.
*   Loss of [[standard_conditions_for_construction_subcontract_0_spc_6001#Equipment and Materials|Equipment and Materials]] or damage to the works, Equipment and Materials, not due to the Subcontractor's fault or responsibility.
*   The Subcontractor being prevented by weather from carrying out all work on site for periods totaling more than one-seventh of the total days between the starting date and the [[standard_conditions_for_construction_subcontract_0_spc_6001#Completion Date|Completion Date]]. Only working days exceeding the one-seventh limit and where work is prevented solely by weather are considered.
*   The Subcontractor encountering physical conditions on site (not weather) that an experienced subcontractor would have reasonably judged, at the date of signing the Agreement, to have a negligible chance of occurring.
*   An event delaying [[standard_conditions_for_construction_subcontract_0_spc_6001#Completion|Completion]] by more than two weeks, which the Subcontractor could not have prevented or controlled, and which an experienced subcontractor would have judged... (document cut off). Only the delay in excess of two weeks is considered.

**Subcontractor's Assumptions for Site Conditions**
The Subcontractor is assumed to have considered the [[standard_conditions_for_construction_subcontract_0_spc_6001#Site Information|Site Information]], publicly available information referenced therein, information obtainable from a visual site inspection, and other information an experienced subcontractor could reasonably be expected to have or obtain.

**Notification and Assessment Process**
The Subcontractor **shall** notify Aker Kvaerner Chemetics of an event as a compensation event if they believe it is one, and if it is less than two weeks since they became aware of it. An event not notified within two weeks of awareness is deemed to have no effect on the Subcontractor’s Costs or Completion. The Subcontractor releases and indemnifies Aker Kvaerner Chemetics against any claim, cost, or loss from an un-notified event.

The [[standard_conditions_for_construction_subcontract_0_spc_6001#Prices|Prices]] and Completion Date are not changed if Aker Kvaerner Chemetics decides the event:
*   Arises from the Subcontractor's fault.
*   Has not happened or is not expected to happen.
*   Has no effect on the Subcontractor’s Cost or Completion.
*   Is not one of the stated compensation events.

If Aker Kvaerner Chemetics decides otherwise, it **shall** instruct the Subcontractor to submit a quotation for the event within one week of the Subcontractor’s notification. If Aker Kvaerner Chemetics believes the Subcontractor did not give an early warning, it **shall** notify this decision when instructing a quotation. If the effects are too uncertain, Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **shall** state assumptions for the assessment, which will be corrected if later found wrong. A compensation event cannot be notified after the defects date.

**Quotation Submission and Reply**
A quotation for a compensation event includes proposed changes to Prices or rates and any delay to the Completion Date assessed by the Subcontractor, with supporting details. The Subcontractor **shall** submit a quotation within one week of instruction, or within two weeks of notification if no instruction is received.

Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may request a quotation for a proposed instruction or changed decision, which the Subcontractor **shall not** implement until accepted. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **shall** reply within one week, either by:
*   Notifying that the instruction/decision will not be given.
*   Notifying the instruction/decision as a compensation event and accepting the quotation.
*   Notifying the instruction/decision as a compensation event and disagreeing with the quotation.
*   For other compensation events, accepting or disagreeing with the quotation.

If Aker Kvaerner Chemetics disagrees, the Subcontractor may submit a revised quotation within one week. If no agreement is reached or no revised quotation is received, Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **shall** assess and notify the assessment. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may also instruct alternative quotations.

**Assessment Methodology**
*   **Quantity-based events**: Changes to Prices are assessed by multiplying changed quantities by appropriate rates in the Price List.
*   **Other events**: Changes to Prices are assessed by forecasting the effect on the Subcontractor’s Cost. If the event has occurred, assessment is based on incurred costs. Effects on Subcontractor’s Cost are assessed separately for people, Equipment and Materials, sub-subcontracted work, and Construction Equipment. The Subcontractor **shall** detail these effects in each quotation.
*   **Overheads and Profit**: Percentages stated in the [[standard_conditions_for_construction_subcontract_0_spc_6001#Subcontract Data|Subcontract Data]] are applied to the assessed cost effect.
*   **Cost Basis**: Assessed at open market or competitively tendered prices, with deductions for all recoverable discounts, rebates, and taxes. Costs covered by Subcontractor's insurance or paid by insurers are deducted.
*   **Delay Assessment**: Assessed as the forecast length of delay to Completion due to the event. If an early warning was not given, the event is assessed as if it had been.
*   **Assumptions**: Assessments include risk allowances for cost and time for matters at the Subcontractor’s risk, assuming competent and prompt reaction and reasonably incurred additional cost and time.
*   **Ambiguity Resolution**: A compensation event involving an instruction to change Works Information to resolve ambiguity is assessed as if Prices and Completion Date favored the Party not providing the Works Information.

**Subcontractor's Assumptions for Site Information**
When considering physical conditions as a compensation event, the Subcontractor is assumed to have taken into account:
*   The [[standard_conditions_for_construction_subcontract_0_spc_6001#Site Information|Site Information]].
*   Publicly available information referred to in the Site Information.
*   Information obtainable from a visual inspection of the site.
*   Other information which an experienced subcontractor could reasonably be expected to have or to obtain.

**Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' Decisions and Quotation Process**
*   The Prices and Completion Date are not changed if Aker Kvaerner Chemetics decides an event:
    *   Arises from a fault of the Subcontractor.
    *   Has not happened and is not expected to happen.
    *   Has no effect upon the Subcontractor’s Cost or upon Completion.
    *   Is not one of the compensation events stated in this subcontract.
*   If Aker Kvaerner Chemetics decides otherwise, it **shall** instruct the Subcontractor to submit a quotation for the event.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **shall** notify its decision or instruct the Subcontractor to submit a quotation within one week of the Subcontractor’s notification.
*   If Aker Kvaerner Chemetics decides the Subcontractor did not give an early warning, it **shall** notify that decision when instructing a quotation.
*   If Aker Kvaerner Chemetics decides the effects of a compensation event are too uncertain to be forecast reasonably, it **shall** state assumptions about the event when instructing a quotation. The assessment of the event is based on these stated assumptions. If any assumptions are later found to have been wrong, Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **shall** notify a correction.
*   A compensation event is not notified after the defects date.
