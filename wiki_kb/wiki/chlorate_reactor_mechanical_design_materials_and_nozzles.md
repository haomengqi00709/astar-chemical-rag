# Chlorate Reactor Mechanical Design, Materials, and Nozzles

This page details the mechanical design specifications, material selections, ancillary equipment, and nozzle schedule for the Chlorate Reactor. These aspects are critical for the structural integrity, operational efficiency, and longevity of the equipment, especially given the corrosive nature of the process fluids involved in chlorate production.

**Mechanical Design and Equipment Data**
The physical configuration and design parameters of the reactor are specified to meet operational requirements and safety standards.
*   **Equipment Orientation**: Horizontal. This dictates the overall layout and support structure for the reactor.
*   **Roof Type (Vertical)**: Cone. (Note: This specification might be a template placeholder as the reactor is specified as horizontal. Project-specific verification is required.)
*   **Floor Type (Vertical)**: Flat. (Note: Similar to roof type, this may be a template placeholder.)
*   **Head Type (Horizontal) Options**: Dished, Cone, Flat, Other. The final selection for horizontal vessels will depend on pressure requirements, fabrication considerations, and specific process needs.

**Materials of Construction**
The selection of materials is paramount for corrosion resistance and operational safety in a highly corrosive chlorate environment.
*   **Shell**: Gr. 2 Titanium. This material is chosen for its excellent resistance to corrosive media encountered in chlorate production processes.
*   **Internals**: Gr. 2 Titanium. Consistent material selection for internal components ensures uniform corrosion resistance throughout the reactor.
*   **Nozzles**: Gr. 2 Titanium with Gr. 7 flange rings. The use of Gr. 7 flange rings provides enhanced strength and corrosion resistance at critical connection points, ensuring robust sealing and longevity.
For more information on general material selection principles and guidelines, refer to [[material_selection]].

**Ancillary Equipment**
The reactor includes several ancillary components essential for its function, process control, and maintenance.
*   **Other Ancillary Equipment**: Internal seal, baffle, (note 1) dip tubes, injection insert. These components are integral to the reactor's operation.
*   **Mandatory Requirement**: Note 1.0: A 25 [[chlorate_reactor_parameter_units|mm]] diameter hole **MUST** be present in the top of the internal baffle. This is a mandatory design feature for proper fluid dynamics or gas venting.
*   **Ancillary Equipment Options**: Jacket, Coil, Baffles, Cooling, Heating, Tracing, Mist Separator, Agitation, Insulation. Specific project requirements will determine which of these options are implemented to optimize reactor performance and safety.
