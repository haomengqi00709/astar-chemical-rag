# Advanced Payment Terms

Advanced payment terms, as part of [[order_specific_conditions_for_purchase]], outline the conditions under which Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may provide an upfront payment to a Vendor. These terms are designed to support Vendor cash flow while ensuring appropriate security measures are in place for Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Advanced Payment Provision:**
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics makes an advanced payment to the Vendor for the amount stated in the Purchase Order.
*   The advanced payment is made either:
    *   Within four weeks of the Purchase Order date, OR
    *   Within four weeks of the later of the Purchase Order Date and when Aker Kvaerner Chemetics receives the advanced payment security (if required).

**Security for Advanced Payment:**
*   The Vendor **must** give Aker Kvaerner Chemetics security for the amount of the advanced payment. This security is crucial to protect Aker Kvaerner Chemetics in case the Vendor fails to deliver the Goods and Services after receiving the advance.
*   Security for advanced payment **must** be provided by a financial institution accepted by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
*   Security for advanced payment **must** be in a form acceptable to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or in the form annexed to the Purchase Order or relevant procedure.

**Repayment in Instalments (Optional):**
*   The advanced payment can optionally be repaid to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics by the Vendor in instalments.
*   If repaid in instalments, the advanced payment instalment amount is specified as "$………. ".
*   If repaid in instalments, an instalment is deducted from each payment due after the [specify] payment until the advanced payment has been fully repaid. This provides a structured mechanism for the Vendor to return the advance over the course of the project.
