# Skirt Supported Vessel on Steel: Equipment Data (4-SCR-0008)

Document **4-SCR-0008** provides specific equipment data crucial for the structural design and installation of the skirt-supported vessel on steel. This data includes key dimensions and material specifications for the vessel's base and anchoring system.

**Equipment Data:**
*   **Underside of Equipment Elevation:** The elevation of the underside of the equipment, measured in millimeters (mm). This is a critical reference point for civil and structural interfaces.
*   **Anchor Bolt Circle Diameter:** The diameter of the circle on which the anchor bolts are arranged, measured in millimeters (mm).
*   **Anchor Bolt 'AB' Diameter:** The nominal diameter of the anchor bolts, measured in millimeters (mm).
*   **Number of Bolts:** The total count of anchor bolts used (ea).
*   **Angle 'E':** A specific angle related to the equipment's geometry or anchor bolt layout, measured in degrees (deg).
*   **Angle 'F':** Another specific angle related to the equipment's geometry or anchor bolt layout, measured in degrees (deg).
*   **Dimension 'H' - Equip. Anchor Height:** The height of the equipment's anchor point, measured in millimeters (mm).
*   **Dimension 'P' - Bolt Projection:** The projection length of the anchor bolt, including a double nut, measured in millimeters (mm).
*   **Dimension 'W' - Equip. Base Ring Width:** The width of the equipment's base ring, measured in millimeters (mm).
*   **Dimension 'X' - Edge Distance:** The distance from the center of an anchor bolt to the edge of the concrete foundation or support structure, measured in millimeters (mm).
*   **Dimension 'Z' - Grating Clearance:** The minimum clearance required for grating around the equipment base, measured in millimeters (mm).
*   **Anchor Bolt Material:** The specified material for the anchor bolts.
*   **Nut Material:** The specified material for the nuts used with the anchor bolts.

This detailed information is vital for ensuring proper fit-up, structural integrity, and compliance with design standards. For general information on equipment dimensions, refer to [[equipment_dimensions]].
