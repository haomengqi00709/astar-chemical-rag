# FRP Tank Design Conditions

This page outlines the critical design parameters and conditions for the Fiberglass Reinforced Plastic (FRP) tank, ensuring its safe and efficient operation. These conditions form the basis for all subsequent engineering calculations and fabrication processes.

**Design Codes and Specifications**
*   **Vessel Design Codes:** The tank design **must** comply with ASME RTP-1, which specifies requirements for reinforced thermoset plastic corrosion resistant equipment.
*   **Specifications:** Fabrication of the FRP tank **must** adhere to Specification 4-SPC-0042: Fiberglass Equipment Fabrication.
*   **Equipment Design and Specification Reference:** General procedures for equipment design and specification are outlined in [[equipment_design_and_specification_4_prc_0003]].

**Process and Environmental Conditions**
*   **Contents:** The tank is designed to hold a "25% Brine Solution."
*   **Specific Gravity Contents:** The specific gravity of the contents is 1.17. This value is crucial for calculating hydrostatic loads.
*   **Maximum Design Temperature:** The tank is designed for a maximum operating temperature of 90.0 °C.
*   **Minimum Design Temperature:** The minimum design temperature is "Ambient," indicating it is suitable for typical environmental conditions without specific low-temperature requirements.
*   **Liquid Level:** The tank is designed to be "Flooded to overflow" under normal operating conditions.
*   **Corrosion Allowance:** A corrosion allowance is "Not specified (mm)," which is typical for FRP materials due to their inherent corrosion resistance.

**Pressure and Protection**
*   **Design Internal Pressure:** The design internal pressure is 0.0 kPa(g). This indicates the tank is designed for atmospheric or very low internal pressure.
*   **Design Vacuum:** The design vacuum is 0.0 kPa(g), meaning it is not designed to withstand significant internal vacuum.
*   **Overpressure Protection:** Overpressure protection is provided by a "Vent connection." This connection serves as a safety device to prevent internal pressure buildup beyond design limits.
*   **Vacuum Protection:** Similarly, vacuum protection is also provided by a "Vent connection," preventing collapse due to internal vacuum. For more on pressure relief equipment, refer to [[pressure_relief_equipment_tagging]].

**External Loads and Testing**
*   **External Loads (Wind, Seismic, Snow) Reference:** Information regarding external loads, including wind, seismic, and snow, is referenced in [[site_design_conditions_1_dst_0001]].
*   **Fabricator Responsibility:** The fabricator **shall** calculate these external loads as part of their design submission.
*   **Hydrotest:** The hydrotest procedure requires filling the tank with water to the overflow level. This test verifies the structural integrity and leak-tightness of the vessel. For general pressure testing requirements, refer to [[0-QDS-7414_pressure_and_leak_testing]].

**Regulatory and Design Parameters**
*   **Vessel Registration:** Not required
*   **External Loads Reference:** External loads (wind, seismic, snow) are referenced in document 1-DST-0001: Site Design Conditions.
*   **Equipment Design and Specification Reference:** 4-PRC-000x

**Operating Conditions**
*   **Design Internal Pressure:** 0.0 kPa(g)
*   **Design Vacuum:** 0.0 kPa(g)
*   **Overpressure Protection:** Vent connection
*   **Vacuum Protection:** Vent connection
*   **Max. Design Temperature:** 90.0 °C
*   **Min. Design Temperature:** Ambient
*   **Liquid Level:** Flooded to overflow
*   **Corrosion Allowance:** Not specified (mm)
*   **Insulation:** Not Required
