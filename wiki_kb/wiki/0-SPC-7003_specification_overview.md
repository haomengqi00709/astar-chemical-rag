# Third Party Inspection Services Specification Overview

The document, titled "0-SPC-7003 — Specification," outlines the requirements for Agencies providing Third Party Inspection Services to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Purpose**
The primary purpose of this specification is to ensure that Agencies supplying Third Party Inspection Services submit and complete all required documentation in accordance with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' standards.

**Scope**
This document details the specific documents that Agencies providing Third Party Inspection Services are required to submit and/or complete as part of their service delivery.

**General Document Instructions**
Any text displayed within square brackets on Aker Kvaerner Chemetics forms is instructional in nature and **shall** be deleted when the form is being used for official documentation.
