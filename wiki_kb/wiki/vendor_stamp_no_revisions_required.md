# Vendor Stamp: No Revisions Required

The "NO REVISIONS REQUIRED" vendor stamp is used to indicate that a submitted document has been reviewed and no further changes are necessary. This stamp is typically found on Page 1 of the vendor stamp procedure document.

**Mandatory Requirements for Application:**
*   The phrase "PROCEED WITH WORK SHOWN" **must** be clearly indicated.
*   A signature **shall** be applied to the stamp.
*   The date of application **shall** be recorded on the stamp.

This stamp signifies that the vendor can proceed with the work as depicted in the document without any modifications. This stamp is associated with [[chemetics_aker_kvaerner_company_details|CHEMETICS]] documentation standards.

For an overview of all vendor stamp options, refer to [[vendor_stamp_procedure_overview]].

**Mandatory Requirements for Application:**
*   The phrase "PROCEED WITH WORK SHOWN" must be included.
*   A signature is required.
*   A date is required.
This stamp is specifically used for Chemetics projects.
