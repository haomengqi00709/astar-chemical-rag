# NEC Secondary Option Clauses for Engineering Subcontracts

The conditions of contract for Engineering Subcontracts may include specific Secondary Option Clauses from the NEC Professional Services Contract, as referenced in [[standard_conditions_engineering_subcontract]] (0-SPC-2201). These options allow for customization of the contract to suit particular project requirements.

**Common Secondary Option Clauses:**
*   **Option X3: Multiple currencies**
    *   If Option X3 is used, payment for the work listed **will** be made in the currencies stated.
    *   The exchange rates used for Option X3 are those published in [specify source] on [specify date].
*   **Option X4: Parent company guarantee**
    *   If Option X4 is used, the form of Parent Company Guarantee is attached as [specify attachment number].
*   **Option X5: Sectional Completion**
    *   If Option X5 is used, the [[engineering_subcontract_delay_damages]] for the sections of the services are [specify].

The identification of applicable Secondary Option Clauses and their associated data (e.g., Parent company guarantee, Delay damages, Security, Limitation of Liability) are typical subjects covered by [[subcontract_specific_terms_and_conditions]].

**Specific Option Clauses**
The conditions of contract may include clauses for specific NEC Secondary Options, such as:

*   **Option X3: Multiple Currencies**
    *   If Option X3 is used, payment for the work listed will be made in the currencies stated within the contract.
    *   The exchange rates used for Option X3 are those published in [specify source] on [specify date].
*   **Option X4: Parent Company Guarantee**
    *   If Option X4 is used, the specific form of Parent Company Guarantee required is attached as [specify attachment number].
*   **Option X5: Sectional Completion**
    *   If Option X5 is used, the delay damages for the individual sections of the services are [specify].
