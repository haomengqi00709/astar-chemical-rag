# Joint Health and Safety Committee Procedure (0-PRC-8008)

This document, 0-PRC-8008, outlines the procedure for establishing and operating Joint Health and Safety Committees (JHSCs) within the company. Its primary purpose is to foster cooperation between management personnel and employees to collectively fulfill their joint duties regarding workplace safety and environmental practices.

The procedure aims to ensure that the highest standards of [[workplace_health_and_environmental_practices]] are consistently applied at all work sites. Furthermore, it seeks to actively [[task_safety_awareness_promotion|promote and maintain a strong safety mindset]] among the entire workforce, contributing to a safer and healthier working environment.

This procedure is a key component of the company's overall [[0-prc-8000_administration_of_hse|HSE administration]] framework, emphasizing a proactive approach to hazard identification, risk control, and continuous improvement in health, safety, and environmental performance.
