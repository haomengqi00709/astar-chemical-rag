# ASME B31.3 Chemical Plant & Petroleum Refinery Piping

ASME B31.3 is a widely recognized industry standard that provides requirements for the design, materials, fabrication, erection, examination, inspection, and testing of piping systems typically found in chemical plants and petroleum refineries.

This code is referenced in [[4-SPC-0014_referenced_codes_and_definitions]] for specific applications related to the welding and fabrication of Columbium Stabilized High Nickel Austenitic stainless steel.

Key areas where ASME B31.3 is applied include:
*   **Piping Alignment:** Alignment requirements for pipe during welding.
*   **Radiographic Examination:** Minimum standards for random radiography for piping.
*   **Dye Penetrant Inspection:** Requirements for dye penetrant inspection in piping.

For detailed requirements on piping systems, refer to the latest edition of ASME B31.3.
