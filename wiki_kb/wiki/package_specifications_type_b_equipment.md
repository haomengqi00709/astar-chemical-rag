# Package Specifications (Type B Equipment)

Package Specifications are dedicated documents for defining the detailed requirements of [[equipment_classification_types|Type B Equipment]] (Process Packages). These specifications go beyond the general data sheets to provide comprehensive information on the package's functionality, components, and control.

**Content Elements**
For Type B Equipment (Process Packages), Package Specifications **must** contain the following:

*   **Scope:** A clear definition of what the package includes and its boundaries.
*   **Function:** A detailed description of the overall process duty and performance expected from the package.
*   **Control:** Specifications related to the control philosophy, instrumentation, and automation of the package.
*   **Component Requirements:** Detailed requirements for individual equipment items and components within the package.
*   **Pointers to Other Documents:** References to other relevant project documents, such as Process Data Sheets, P&IDs, or specific vendor documentation.

**Document Preparation and Approval**
The preparation, checking, and approval of Package Specifications follow a defined workflow:

*   **Preparation:** Package Specifications **must** be prepared by Mechanical personnel.
*   **Checking:** The documents **must** be checked by Process personnel.
*   **Approval:** Final approval **must** be granted by the Project Engineer.

**Responsibilities**
*   **Prepared by:** Mechanical
*   **Checked by:** Process
*   **Approved by:** Project Engineer
