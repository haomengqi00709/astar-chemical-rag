# Glass-Lined Vessel Tolerances and Dimensions (4-SPC-0044)

This section outlines the mandatory tolerances and dimensional requirements for glass-lined carbon steel reaction vessels, including vessel, nozzle, and flange specifications, as detailed in 4-SPC-0044.

**General Vessel Tolerances**
Vessel tolerances **shall** be in accordance with [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels|ASME Code, Section VIII, Div. 1]] and as outlined in Paragraph 13 of 4-SPC-0044. In case of conflict between [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels|ASME Code]] and Paragraph 13, the contents of Paragraph 13 **shall** govern. It is mandatory that Aker Kvaerner Chemetics has the opportunity to review and comment on the Supplier's latest fabrication tolerance specifications prior to order placement. The Supplier's latest fabrication tolerance specifications document **shall** be submitted with the quotation. Tolerances outside the specified limits **shall** be referred to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for decision.

Vessel and jacket diameters **shall** be in accordance with [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels|Para. UG-80 (a) of ASME Code, Section VIII, Division I]], as modified by Code Cases 1854. Openings required for manholes **shall** be in accordance with the requirements of the [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels|ASME Code, Section VIII, Division I]].

**Nozzle and Flange Tolerances**
The dimensions and concentricity of the mechanical seal housing components before assembly **shall** be to the tolerances shown on the appropriate drawing. Nozzles, with the exception of the agitator nozzle, **shall** be in the correct angular relationship to each other and to the centre lines of the vessel to within ¼" (6mm).

Vessel nozzle flange faces **shall** be flat to within 1/16" (1.5mm) circumferentially. Joint flatness **shall** be measured at both the inside and outside edge of the flange using a flat template reference. Flange face of nozzles **shall** be parallel with the indicated plane in any direction to within tolerances specified in [[4-SPC-0044_table_3_nozzle_flange_flatness_tolerances]].

**General Vessel Tolerances**
*   Vessel tolerances shall be as outlined in Paragraph 13 of 4-SPC-0044. In case of conflict between ASME Code and Paragraph 13, the contents of Paragraph 13 shall govern.
*   It is mandatory that Aker Kvaerner Chemetics has the opportunity to review and comment on Supplier's latest fabrication tolerance specifications prior to order placement.
*   Tolerances outside the specified limits shall be referred to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for decision.
*   Openings required for manholes shall be in accordance with the requirements of the ASME Code, Section VIII, Division I.
*   Vessel and jacket diameters shall be in accordance with Para. UG-80 (a) of ASME Code, Section VIII, Division I, as modified by Code Cases 1854.

**Nozzle and Flange Tolerances**
*   The dimensions and concentricity of the mechanical seal housing components before assembly shall be to the tolerances shown on the appropriate drawing.
*   Nozzles with the exception of the agitator nozzle shall be in the correct angular relationship to each other and to the centre lines of the vessel to within ¼" (6mm).
*   Vessel nozzle flange faces shall be flat to within 1/16" (1.5mm) circumferentially. Joint flatness shall be measured at both the inside and outside edge of the flange using a flat template reference.
