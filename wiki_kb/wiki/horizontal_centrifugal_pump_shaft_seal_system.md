# Horizontal Centrifugal Pump Shaft Seal System

The shaft seal system for a horizontal centrifugal pump, as specified in Document ID [[template_horizontal_centrifugal_pump_datasheet_administration]], is a critical component designed to prevent leakage of the process fluid along the pump shaft. Its design and material selection are paramount for safety and operational efficiency.

**Supply and Mounting:**
*   Shaft Seal *shall be supplied by Vendor*.
*   Shaft Seal *shall be mounted by Vendor*.

**Seal System Specifications:**

*   **Shaft Seal Type:** *Shall be specified*. This defines the fundamental design of the seal, e.g., mechanical seal, packing, etc.
*   **Seal Piping Arrangement:** *Shall be specified*. This refers to the piping plan for supplying flush fluid, quench fluid, or other services to the seal.
*   **Seal Fluid:** *Shall be specified*. The type of fluid used for flushing, quenching, or barrier fluid in the seal system.
*   **Seal Fluid Control System:** *Shall be specified*. This describes the instrumentation and controls governing the flow and pressure of the seal fluid.
*   **Seal Chamber:** *Shall be specified*. The design and dimensions of the chamber where the seal components are housed.
*   **Seal Fluid Connection:** *Shall be specified*. The type and size of connections for the seal fluid lines.
*   **Seal Guard:** *Shall be specified*. A protective cover around the seal area to prevent injury from rotating parts or fluid spray.

The materials used in the shaft seal system are detailed in [[horizontal_centrifugal_pump_material_selection]], including the rotating and stationary faces, metal parts, and elastomers. The proper functioning of the shaft seal system is directly influenced by the [[horizontal_centrifugal_pump_process_design_conditions]], particularly the fluid properties and operating temperatures.
