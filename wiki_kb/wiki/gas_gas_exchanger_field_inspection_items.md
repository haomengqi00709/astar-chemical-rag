# Gas-Gas Exchanger Field Inspection Items

This page outlines the mandatory field inspection items for Gas-Gas Exchangers, as specified in the 4-FIC-XX18-R0 template. Adherence to these requirements is crucial for ensuring the quality, safety, and operational integrity of the installed equipment.

All items listed below **must** be verified as complete and satisfactory during the field inspection:

*   **Nameplate Installation:** A correct nameplate **must** be installed on the equipment.
*   **Foundation/Anchor Bolts:** Foundation and anchor bolts **must** be secure and properly tightened.
*   **Non-Destructive Testing (NDT):** All [[non_destructive_examination_requirements]] **must** be completed, and their corresponding records **must** be available for review.
*   **Access Structures:** All platforms, handrails, walkways, stairs, and ladders **must** be fully installed.
*   **Safety Gates:** Safety gates **must** be installed and fully functioning.
*   **Internal Components:** All internal components, including [[thermowells_installation]], **must** be correctly installed.
*   **Hardware and Gaskets:** Correct hardware and gaskets **must** be installed throughout the exchanger.
*   **Vestibule Cleanliness:** All vestibules **must** be clean and dry.
*   **Dampers in Piping:** All dampers in piping connected to the exchanger **must** move freely. This is noted as the "last chance to check these" (Item 9.0). Refer to [[dampers_inspection]] for related procedures.
*   **Expansion Joints:** All [[expansion_joints_installation]] **must** be installed with the correct orientation, and hinge locations **must** be checked.
*   **Final Internal Inspection:** A final internal inspection **must** be completed with the client's representative.
*   **Closure Certificate:** A closure certificate **must** be attached for the final internal inspection.
*   **Manway Covers:** [[manway_covers_installation_and_inspection]] **must** be installed and seal welded.
*   **Manway Cover Welds:** Manway cover welds **must** be inspected, and test records **must** be available.
*   **External Painting:** External painting **must** be completed according to specifications. Refer to [[coatings_erection_0_spc_6x97]].
*   **Insulation & Cladding:** Insulation and cladding **must** be complete. Refer to [[insulation_erection_0_spc_6x96]].
*   **Grounding:** [[grounding_installation_details]] **must** be completed, potentially at multiple locations.
*   **Isolation Valves:** [[isolation_and_preparation_for_maintenance_1_prc_0007]] **must** be installed on all drains.

These inspection points are crucial for the [[gas_gas_heat_exchanger_fabrication_and_installation_requirements]] and overall project quality.
