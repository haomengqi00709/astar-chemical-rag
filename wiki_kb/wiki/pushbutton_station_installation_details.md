# Pushbutton Station Installation Details

Pushbutton station installation details provide specific instructions and component requirements for supporting and attaching pushbutton stations in various plant locations. These details ensure accessibility and secure mounting.

**Content of Pushbutton Station Details**
Pushbutton station details **shall** show:
*   Methods of supporting the pushbutton station on walls.
*   Methods of supporting the pushbutton station on building columns.
*   Methods of supporting the pushbutton station at motor bases where a wall or building column do not exist.
*   The location of the cable entry to the pushbutton station.
*   Cable terminating components required to fasten the cable to the pushbutton station.
*   The various types of pushbutton stations.
*   Any installation differences for pushbutton stations based on type or location.

Mounting brackets and support steel **are required** for the three possible pushbutton station locations. Each component required to attach the pushbutton station to the mounting bracket or support steel **shall** be identified.
