# Vendor Document Expediting

The expediting of vendor documents is a critical process to ensure timely delivery and adherence to project schedules. This process is primarily driven by the contents of the [[vendor_document_requirements_preparation|Vendor Document Requirements (VDRs)]].

**Weekly Review and Reporting:**
A Vendor Document Expediting Report is reviewed weekly by the Project Engineer. This report, detailed in [[control_of_project_documents_0_prc_0006|Document 0-PRC-0006: Control of Project Documentation]], lists vendor documents that are overdue or scheduled to be received in the upcoming period.

**Expediting Actions:**
Upon reviewing the report, the Project Engineer will consult with the responsible Discipline Leads to verify whether documents have indeed not been received. If documents are confirmed as missing or overdue, the Project Engineer will direct Procurement to expedite their delivery from the Vendor. This proactive approach helps maintain project timelines and prevents delays caused by missing documentation.
