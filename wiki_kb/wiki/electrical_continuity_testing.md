# Electrical Continuity Testing

Electrical continuity testing is a procedure used to verify that an electrical path is continuous and unbroken. This test is performed to ensure that components are properly connected and that there are no open circuits or unintended resistances. In the context of piping, it is used to confirm the effective grounding of systems, such as [[pipe_grounding_ring_inspection_5_prc_0007|pipe grounding rings]], by checking that there is a low-resistance path to ground. This testing is typically performed by [[electrical_personnel|qualified electrical personnel]].
