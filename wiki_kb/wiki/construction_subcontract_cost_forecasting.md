# Construction Subcontract Cost Forecasting

The Subcontract Administrator is responsible for preparing forecasts of the total cost of the Subcontract. These forecasts are generated as requested by the Project Manager.

**Forecasting Requirements:**
*   Forecasts **must** accurately account for any instructions issued to the Subcontractor for which quotations have not yet been received or accepted. This ensures that potential [[construction_subcontract_compensation_events]] and their financial impacts are considered in the overall cost projection, even if formal [[subcontract_change_order_procedure_0_chg_6xx0]] have not yet been issued.
