# Welding Consumables and Processes for Nickel/Molybdenum/Chromium Alloys

This section outlines the requirements for welding consumables and the approved welding processes for nickel/molybdenum/chromium alloys.

**Welding Consumables**
*   All weld filler metals **shall** meet the requirements of Section 8 of this Standard.
*   Fabricators **shall** ensure and demonstrate to the Inspector that manufacturers' instructions regarding storage, drying, and use of weld consumables are fully implemented.
*   All wet electrodes **shall** be discarded and not baked and reused.
*   The fabricator **shall**, prior to commencement of work, furnish [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_company_details]] with the brand name of the filler materials to be used.

**Preferred Welding Methods**
Preferred welding methods for nickel-base alloys are:
*   Shielded Metal Arc Welding (SMAW)
*   Gas Tungsten Arc Welding (GTAW)
*   Gas Metal Arc Welding (GMAW)

Other welding processes will be evaluated upon submission of full details, including the procedure and exact location of proposed weldments. Submerged arc welding is not recommended.

**General Welding Parameters**
General welding parameters (current, voltage, electrode size, etc.) and special requirements should be obtained from the electrode supplier.

**Shielded Metal Arc Welding (SMAW)**
*   SMAW electrodes **shall** satisfy the requirements of [[ASME_sfa_5_11_nickel_and_nickel_alloy_electrodes]] (SFA-5.11).
*   SMAW electrode for C-276 **shall** be Designated AWS Classification ENiCrMo-4.
*   SMAW electrode for C-22 **shall** be Designated AWS Classification ENiCrMo-10.
*   Minimum practical amperage and minimum arc length are recommended for SMAW to control the weld puddle.
*   A straight stringer bead is generally desirable for SMAW.

**Gas Tungsten Arc Welding (GTAW)**
*   GTAW rods **shall** satisfy the requirements of [[ASME_sfa_5_14_nickel_and_nickel_alloy_electrodes_and_rods]] (SFA-5.14).
*   GTAW filler wire diameter **shall** be kept small, preferably 1/16".
*   GTAW filler wire for C-276 **shall** be designated AWS Classification ERNiCrMo-4.
*   GTAW filler wire for C-22 **shall** be designated AWS Classification ERNiCrMo-10.
*   Preferred GTAW electrode is thoriated tungsten as per [[ASME_sfa_5_12_tungsten_arc_welding_electrodes]] (SFA-5.12), Class EWTh-2.
*   GTAW electrode **shall** have a tapered tip for good arc stability and penetration.
*   GTAW power supply **shall** incorporate a high frequency (HF) voltage to start the arc.
*   GTAW power supply **shall** incorporate a current decay unit with foot control to eliminate or minimize crater cracking.
*   Direct current straight polarity (D.C.S.P. - Electrode negative) **shall** be used for both manual or machine GTAW.
*   GTAW weld metal thickness **shall not** exceed 1/8" per pass.
*   A back-up gas **shall** be used for GTAW to prevent root pass oxidation and contamination (e.g., piping or where root pass is inaccessible).
*   The GTAW Tungsten Arc Torch **shall** have a gas diffuser screen for optimum gas coverage.
*   The GTAW gas cup size should be as large as practical.

**Gas Metal Arc Welding (GMAW)**
*   GMAW **shall only** be used for the root pass of butt welds and for fillet welds unless specifically agreed otherwise.
*   Short-circuiting transfer or the Pulsed Arc transfer process **shall** be used for GMAW.
*   Direct current reverse polarity (D.C.R.P. - Electrode positive) **shall** be used for GMAW.
*   The GMAW gas cup size **shall** be as large as practical.
*   Fabricator **must** provide satisfactory evidence of continuous shop use of GMAW in the fabrication of nickel-base alloys.

**Submerged Arc Welding**
*   Use of Submerged Arc Welding **must** be reviewed and approved in writing by [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_company_details]] prior to the start of fabrication.
*   Fabricator **must** provide sectioned samples and satisfactory evidence of continuous shop use of Submerged Arc Welding in the fabrication of Hastelloy C-276 & C-22.

*   All weld filler metals **shall** meet the requirements of Section 8 of this Standard.
