# Converter Fabrication Items Supplied by Others (4-SPC-7540)

For the converter fabrication project governed by specification 4-SPC-7540, several key components and materials are designated to be supplied by parties other than the primary fabrication vendor. These items are critical for the complete assembly and operation of the converter but fall outside the vendor's direct scope of supply.

The following items are supplied by Others:
*   Ceramic Saddles
*   Quartz pebbles
*   Wire mesh for grid plates
*   Catalyst
*   Thermo wells
*   Standard field fit up lugs, strong backs, and spreader bars
*   Insulation support hardware, insulation, and cladding
*   Field welding consumables

The primary vendor's responsibilities for supply are detailed in [[4-SPC-7540_converter_fabrication_vendor_scope]].
