# Welding Processes and Parameters for Stabilized Austenitic Stainless Steel (4-SPC-0014)

This page details the approved welding processes and their specific parameters for Columbium Stabilized High Nickel Austenitic stainless steel fabrications, as specified in document 4-SPC-0014.

**Approved Welding Processes**
Preferred methods of welding Columbium stabilized austenitic steels to ASME P45 classification are Shielded Metal Arc Welding (SMAW), Gas Tungsten Arc Welding (GTAW), and Gas Metal Arc Welding (GMAW). Other welding processes will be evaluated upon submission of full details, including the procedure and exact location of proposed weldments. General welding parameters (current, voltage, electrode size, etc.) and special requirements should be obtained from the electrode supplier.

**Shielded Metal Arc Welding (SMAW)**
*   SMAW electrodes shall satisfy the requirements of [[ASME_sfa_5_4_welding_electrodes]].
*   SMAW electrodes shall be Columbium stabilized Grade E320 or E320 LR to ASME F5 classification.
*   Use of Type 15 is recommended for normal fillet and butt welds.
*   Use of Type 16 is recommended for weld overlay.
*   Minimum practical amperage, stringer beads rather than weave, and minimum arc length are recommended for SMAW to minimize heat input.
*   Single sided SMAW welds shall have the root protected by gas backing during welding.

**Gas Tungsten Arc Welding (GTAW)**
*   GTAW rods shall satisfy the requirements of [[ASME_sfa_5_9_welding_electrodes_and_rods]].
*   GTAW filler wire diameter shall be kept small, preferably 1/16".
*   GTAW filler wire shall be Columbium stabilized Grade ER320 or ER320 LR to ASME F6 classification.
*   Preferred GTAW electrode is thoriated tungsten as per [[ASME_sfa_5_12_tungsten_arc_welding_electrodes]], Class EWTh-2.
*   GTAW electrode shall be ground to a sharp point.
*   High frequency (HF) voltage shall be used to start the GTAW arc.
*   Direct current straight polarity (D.C.S.P. - Electrode negative) shall be used for GTAW.
*   GTAW weld metal thickness shall not exceed 1/8" per pass.
*   GTAW heat input shall be kept low by using stringer bead technique.
*   Single sided GTAW welds shall have the root protected by backing gas during welding.

**Gas Metal Arc Welding (GMAW)**
*   GMAW shall only be used for the root pass of butt welds and for fillet welds unless specifically agreed otherwise.
*   Direct current reverse polarity (D.C.R.P. - Electrode positive) shall be used for GMAW.
*   The Fabricator must provide satisfactory evidence of continuous shop use of the GMAW process in the fabrication of austenitic stainless steel.
*   The use of ER320 LR filler metal is mandatory for GMAW.
*   The mode of transfer for GMAW must be stated.

**Submerged Arc Welding (SAW)**
*   Use of the Submerged Arc Welding process must be reviewed and approved in writing by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics prior to the start of fabrication.
*   A chemistry check of the finished deposit must be included as part of the SAW weld procedure qualification.
*   The Fabricator must provide sectioned samples and satisfactory evidence of continuous shop use of the SAW process in the fabrication of the austenitic stainless steel.

**Heat Treatment and Control**
*   Preheat is not required. When adverse weather conditions cause condensation or wetness, material shall be dried prior to welding by heating to a temperature just sufficient to prevent condensation.
*   Heat input during welding should be kept to a minimum.
*   Interpass temperature should not exceed 200°F (93°C).
*   Postweld Heat Treatment (PWHT) is seldom required. In the event that PWHT is specified, the Fabricator shall submit their procedure in writing for approval prior to the commencement of work. The PWHT procedure shall include information on the method of water quenching, as well as the number and location of thermocouples and supports.
