# Professional Services Subcontract (0-SPC-2201) Key Definitions

This document, 0-SPC-2201, establishes key definitions essential for understanding the obligations and responsibilities within a professional services subcontract. Terms identified in the *Subcontract Data* are presented in italics, while all other defined terms begin with capital initials.

**Key Definitions:**

*   **Parties:** Refers collectively to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Subcontractor. Both parties **shall** act as stated in this Subcontract and **shall** operate in a spirit of mutual trust and cooperation.
*   **Subcontractor:** A person or an organisation contracted by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics to provide services.
*   **Others:** Individuals or organisations that are not Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, the Subcontractor, or any employee, Subsubcontractor, or supplier of the Subcontractor.
*   **Subcontract Date:** The specific date on which this Subcontract officially came into existence.
*   **To Provide the Services:** Encompasses all work necessary to complete the services as required by this Subcontract.
*   **Scope:** Information that specifies and describes the services to be provided, including any constraints on how the Subcontractor **Provides the Services**. The Scope is detailed in documents referenced in the [[engineering_subcontract_agreement_data_sections|Subcontract Data]] and any instructions given in accordance with this Subcontract.
*   **Subsubcontractor:** A person or organisation holding a Subsubcontract with the Subcontractor to deliver a portion of the services.
*   **Completion Date:** The agreed-upon date for completion, subject to changes made in accordance with this Subcontract.
*   **Completion:** Achieved when the Subcontractor has performed all work specified in the Scope by the Completion Date and has corrected any Defects that would prevent Aker Kvaerner Chemetics from using the services.
*   **Accepted Schedule:** The Schedule identified in the [[engineering_subcontract_agreement_data_sections|Subcontract Data]] or the latest Schedule accepted by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. The latest accepted Schedule supersedes all previous Accepted Schedules.
*   **Defect:** Any part of the services that does not conform to the requirements of the Scope.
*   **Time Charge:** The total sum derived from multiplying each applicable staff rate by the total staff time properly expended on the services at that rate.
*   **Prices:** The lump sum prices assigned to each activity within the activity schedule, unless subsequently modified in accordance with this Subcontract.
*   **Price for Services Provided to Date:** The cumulative total of the Prices for all activities that have been completed.
*   **Subcontract Data:** Essential information required to fully define the obligations of both Parties. This data is contained within The Agreement, documents referenced in The Agreement, and the [[special_conditions_engineering_subcontract]].

**Additional Key Definitions:**
*   **Others:** People or organisations who are not Chemetics, the Subcontractor, or any employee, Subsubcontractor or supplier of the Subcontractor.
*   **Subcontract Date:** The date when this Subcontract came into existence.
*   **To Provide the Services:** To do the work necessary to complete the services in accordance with this Subcontract.
*   **Scope:** Information which specifies and describes the services, states any constraints on how the Subcontractor Provides the Services, and is found in the documents which the *Subcontract Data* states it is in, or in an instruction given in accordance with this Subcontract.
*   **Subsubcontractor:** A person or an organisation which has a Subsubcontract with the Subcontractor to provide part of the services.
*   **Completion Date:** The completion date unless later changed in accordance with this Subcontract.
*   **Completion:** When the Subcontractor has done all the work which the Scope states he is to do by the Completion Date, and has corrected Defects which would have prevented Aker Kvaerner Chemetics from using the services.
*   **Accepted Schedule:** The Schedule identified in the *Subcontract Data* or the latest Schedule accepted by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. The latest Accepted Schedule supersedes previous Accepted Schedules.
*   **Defect:** A part of the services which is not in accordance with the Scope.
*   **Time Charge:** The sum of the products of each of the staff rates multiplied by the total staff time appropriate to that rate properly spent on the services.
*   **Prices:** The lump sum prices for each of the activities in the activity schedule unless later changed in accordance with this Subcontract.
*   **Price for Services Provided to Date:** The total of the Prices for the activities which have been completed.
*   **Subcontract Data:** Information required to fully ascertain the obligations of the Parties, found in The Agreement, the documents referenced in the Agreement, and The Special Conditions for Engineering Subcontract.
