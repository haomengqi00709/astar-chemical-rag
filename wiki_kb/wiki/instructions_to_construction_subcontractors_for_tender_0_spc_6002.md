# Instructions to Construction Subcontractors for Tender (0-SPC-6002)

The document 0-SPC-6002, titled "Instructions to Construction Subcontractors for Tender," is a critical standard that guides prospective subcontractors through the tendering process for construction subcontracts. It ensures clarity, consistency, and compliance in bid submissions.

**Status as a Standard:**
0-SPC-6002 exists as a company standard. As such, it **must** be used as-is or modified for specific project requirements as necessary, in accordance with the [[approval_and_use_of_standards]] (0-PRC-0001) procedure. This flexibility allows for adaptation to unique project contexts while maintaining a baseline of standardized instructions.

**Mandatory Legal Approval for Changes:**
Any changes made to Sections 8 and 9 of 0-SPC-6002 **must** be approved by Legal. This requirement underscores the importance of these sections, which typically contain critical legal, contractual, or commercial terms that require expert review to mitigate risks and ensure compliance.

**Role in the Tender Process:**
*   **Referencing in Covering Letters:** When inviting Construction Subcontractors to tender, the Subcontract Administrator **must** prepare covering letters that explicitly reference 0-SPC-6002. This ensures that all invited parties are directed to the official instructions for preparing their bids.
*   **Guidance for Pricing Lists:** 0-SPC-6002 contains essential instructions on the proper use and completion of the various [[construction_subcontract_pricing_lists]], such as 0-LST-6X12 (Labour Rates), 0-LST-6X13 (Crew Rates), 0-LST-6X14 (Indirect Labour Schedule), and 0-LST-6X15 (Equipment Schedule). These instructions are vital for subcontractors to accurately define the basis for their bids, ensuring that submitted information is consistent and comparable.

**Issuance to Subcontractors:**
As part of the [[construction_subcontract_tender_process]], the Subcontract Administrator and the Project Engineer will forward a list of all required documents, including 0-SPC-6002, to Document Control for official issuance to the Construction Subcontractors. This process is managed under [[control_of_project_documents_0_prc_0006]].
