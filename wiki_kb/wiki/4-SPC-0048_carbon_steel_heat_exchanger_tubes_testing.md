# Carbon Steel Heat Exchanger Tubes Testing Requirements (4-SPC-0048)

To ensure the quality and integrity of carbon steel heat exchanger tubes procured under [[4-SPC-0048_carbon_steel_heat_exchanger_tubes_specification|Specification 4-SPC-0048]], a series of mandatory tests and general requirements **must** be met. These testing protocols are critical for verifying material properties, dimensional accuracy, and overall fitness for service in demanding [[acid_plant_chemically_resistant_brick_specifications|acid plant]] applications.

**Mandatory Testing and General Requirements:**
*   Material testing as described in ASTM A178 is **mandatory**.
*   General requirements as described in ASTM A178 are **mandatory**.
*   Material testing as described in ASTM A450 is **mandatory**.
*   General requirements as described in ASTM A450 are **mandatory**.

**Non-Destructive Examination (NDE):**
All tubes, regardless of their source, **shall** undergo specific NDE tests to confirm their structural soundness and dimensional compliance.
*   **Hydrostatic Test:** This test is **mandatory** for all tubes.
*   **Ring Gauge Test:** This test is **mandatory** and **shall** be performed on 100% of the tubes.
    *   A 50 mm long ring gauge **shall** be used.
    *   The ring gauge's internal diameter (ID) **must** precisely match the maximum allowable outer diameter (OD) of the tube.
    *   The ring gauge **shall** be passed over the entire length of all tubes.
    *   The primary purpose of this test is to ensure that the tube is not oversized and will fit correctly into the tube-sheet hole during assembly, preventing potential fabrication issues and ensuring a proper seal.

**Supplier Qualification Testing:**
For tubes supplied by vendors who have not previously supplied tubes to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, additional tests (referred to as "Section 4" in the source document, though the specific content of Section 4 is not provided here) **shall** be performed. These additional tests serve as a critical part of the supplier qualification process, establishing confidence in new vendors' manufacturing capabilities and quality control.

**Waiver of Section 4 Requirements:**
The requirements outlined in "Section 4" **may** be waived for subsequent orders placed with the same supplier, provided their performance on previous orders has been satisfactory. Any such waiver, however, **requires** explicit consultation with and approval from the [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_quality_assurance_and_materials_engineering_group|Materials and QA group]]. This ensures that any decision to waive testing is made with appropriate technical oversight and maintains the required quality standards.
