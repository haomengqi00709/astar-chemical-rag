# Mist Eliminator Candle Physical Specifications (4-DST-XXXX-R0)

This page details the mandatory physical specifications and dimensional requirements for the Mist Eliminator Candle Datasheet (4-DST-XXXX-R0). These specifications are essential for the fabrication and installation of the mist eliminator candles.

**Mist Eliminator Candle Configuration:**
The following characteristics of the mist eliminator candle must be specified:
*   **Type:** The type of mist eliminator candle is required.
*   **Flow Direction:** The intended flow direction must be specified.
*   **Number of Candles:** The total number of candles required must be specified.
*   **Position:** The position of the candles within the vessel must be specified.

**Dimensional Requirements:**
Precise dimensions are critical and must include:
*   **Candle Outside Diameter:** The outside diameter of the candle in mm is required.
*   **Candle Inside Diameter:** The inside diameter of the candle in mm is required.
*   **Candle Height:** The height of the candle in mm is required.
*   **Minimum Flange Thickness:** The minimum flange thickness in mm is required.
*   **External Flange Outside Dia.:** The external flange outside diameter in mm is required.

**Mounting and Handling:**
Details related to mounting and handling must be provided:
*   **Mounting Bolts - No/Dia:** The number and diameter of mounting bolts are required.
*   **Lifting Lugs per Candle:** The number of lifting lugs per candle is required.
*   **Mounting:** The mounting method must be specified.
