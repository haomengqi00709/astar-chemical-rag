# Gas/Gas Heat Exchanger PVElite Modeling and Nozzle Reinforcement

The mechanical design of gas/gas heat exchangers extensively utilizes specialized software for modeling and analysis, particularly for pressure-retaining components and critical connections.

**PVElite® Pressure Vessel Design Software:**
*   Main pressure parts of the heat exchanger, including the shell, vestibules, and heads, are modeled using the PVElite® pressure vessel design software. This software aids in ensuring compliance with design codes and standards.
*   The weight of the tube bundle, which includes acoustic baffles, is accurately accounted for within the PVElite model by incorporating a packed section with the appropriate density.

**Nozzle Reinforcement:**
*   For nozzle reinforcement calculations, the required shell thickness **shall** be determined based on the greater value derived from either the internal pressure requirements or the longitudinal bending stresses. This conservative approach ensures adequate strength at these critical connection points.

This combination of advanced modeling software and rigorous design principles for nozzle reinforcement contributes to the overall safety and reliability of the gas/gas heat exchanger.
