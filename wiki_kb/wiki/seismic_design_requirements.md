# Seismic Design Requirements

Seismic design is a critical aspect of plant engineering for the Chemetics project, ensuring the structural integrity and operational safety of facilities in the event of an earthquake. All seismic design parameters and requirements are comprehensively documented in [[site_design_conditions_1_dst_0001]].

The following parameters **must** be specified for seismic design:

*   **Seismic Design Code:** The specific building code or standard governing seismic design for the project location **must** be identified. This ensures compliance with local regulations and best practices.
*   **Type of Analysis:** The methodology used for seismic analysis (e.g., equivalent static, response spectrum, time history) **must** be clearly defined.
*   **Structure Classification:** The classification of the structure (e.g., essential, hazardous, standard) **must** be specified, as this influences the required performance level during a seismic event.
*   **Acceleration Coefficient:** The design ground acceleration coefficient **must** be provided, representing the expected peak ground acceleration at the site.
*   **Site Factor:** A site-specific factor that accounts for local soil conditions and their influence on ground motion amplification **must** be specified.
*   **Importance Factor:** An importance factor, reflecting the consequences of failure for the structure, **must** be specified. Higher importance factors lead to more stringent design requirements.
*   **Soil Profile:** The geological characteristics of the soil at the site **must** be specified, as this directly impacts seismic wave propagation and structural response.
*   **Soil Bearing Pressure:** The allowable soil bearing pressure is specified in N/m². This value is essential for foundation design, ensuring that the foundations can safely support the structure's weight and seismic loads.

Adherence to these specified seismic design requirements is mandatory to mitigate risks and ensure the long-term resilience of the plant infrastructure.

The following parameters **must** be specified for seismic design:
*   Seismic Design Code
*   Type of Analysis
*   Structure Classification
*   Acceleration Coefficient
*   Site Factor
*   Importance Factor
*   Soil Profile
