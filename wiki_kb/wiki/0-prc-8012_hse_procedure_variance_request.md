# HSE Procedure Variance Request

The `0-PRC-8012` procedure outlines the guidelines for requesting a variance to an approved Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) procedure or guideline. This ensures that any deviations from established HSE protocols are properly reviewed, justified, and managed to maintain or enhance safety standards.

Approved HSE procedures and guidelines are typically found in Aker Kvaerner or [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_document_history|Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Health and Safety manuals]], which serve as the primary source documents for HSE protocols.

**Responsibilities**
The responsibility for initiating a variance request primarily lies with the designated work site Health and Safety Manager. The work site Manager is also responsible for completing the variance request. In cases where a specific Aker Kvaerner Chemetics HSE Manager is not designated for a particular work site, the senior Aker Kvaerner Chemetics employee present at the site will make the request.

The responsible party **must** provide any information required to support the case for a variance. This includes a comprehensive justification demonstrating why the variance is necessary and how it will impact health, safety, and environmental protection.

**Approval Criteria**
Variances will only be approved if the variance request results in equally effective or better protection of health, safety, and environment compared to the original procedure. This is a mandatory criterion for all variance approvals.

**Approval Process**
Required variances to established HSE procedures **should** be discussed in advance with the [[hse_roles_and_responsibilities_company_wide|Company HSE Manager]]. The final approval for all variances rests with the Company HSE Manager.

**Documentation and Review**
Upon approval, any new HSE procedure resulting from a variance **will** be documented prior to its implementation. This ensures that the modified procedure is formally recorded and communicated. Furthermore, all variance requests are subject to consideration for permanent inclusion in the established [[standard_document_lifecycle_and_revision|HSE policies and procedures]]. This systematic review process allows for continuous improvement and adaptation of company-wide HSE standards based on practical site-specific needs and lessons learned.

**Variance Request Form**
The official form for submitting a variance request is DP-802.322. This form is available in the Aker Kvaerner OGPE HSE manuals.
