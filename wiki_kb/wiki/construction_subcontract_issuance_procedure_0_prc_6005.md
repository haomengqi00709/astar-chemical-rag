# Construction Subcontract Issuance Procedure (0-PRC-6005)

Procedure 0-PRC-6005 outlines the comprehensive steps required for the issuance of a Construction Subcontract. This procedure covers the development of both technical and commercial requirements, the preparation and revision of the Construction Subcontract Agreement, and the final authorization and issuance process.

**Key Stages and Responsibilities:**

1.  **Requirement Development:**
    *   Technical requirements for the subcontract **must** be developed in accordance with [[construction_subcontract_scope_preparation_0_prc_6091]].
    *   Commercial requirements **must** be developed following [[commercial_terms_for_construction_subcontract_0_prc_6004]].

2.  **Agreement Preparation (Initial Tender Stage):**
    *   The Subcontract Administrator is responsible for preparing the [[construction_subcontract_agreement_template_and_content]], which is based on the standard template 0-SUB-6X00.
    *   Information enclosed in square brackets within template 0-SUB-6X00 **must** be modified to reflect specific project requirements.
    *   The phrase "by Subcontractor" in template 0-SUB-6X00 **must not** be modified.
    *   The Agreement will be published according to [[publishing_project_documents]] (0-PRC-0005) with an initial status of "Issued for Quotation".
    *   The Agreement **must** be checked by the Project Engineer and Legal, and approved by the Project Manager.

3.  **Referenced Administrative Specifications:**
    *   The Construction Subcontract Agreement directly references several administrative specifications, detailed further in [[construction_subcontract_administrative_specifications]]. These include:
        *   0-SPC-6003: Construction Subcontract Coordination
        *   0-SPC-6011: Construction Subcontract Pricing: Lump Sum
        *   0-SPC-6012: Construction Subcontract Pricing: Unit Price
        *   0-SPC-6021: Construction Subcontract Scheduling
        *   0-SPC-6041: Construction Subcontractor Materials Control
        *   0-SPC-6051: Construction Subcontract Safety Management
        *   0-SPC-6061: Construction Subcontract Environmental Management
        *   0-SPC-6071: Construction Subcontract Quality Assurance
        *   0-SPC-6081: Construction Subcontract Industrial Relations Management
        *   0-SPC-6091: Construction Subcontractor Document Supply
    *   These administrative specifications exist as standards and **must** be used as-is or modified for the project as necessary, in accordance with [[approval_and_use_of_standards]] (0-PRC-0001).
    *   These documents **should** be published with a status of "Approved for Construction" and **should** be checked and approved as per [[approval_and_use_of_standards]].

4.  **Referenced Pricing Lists:**
    *   The Subcontract Administrator prepares [[construction_subcontract_pricing_lists]], including 0-LST-6X11: Construction Subcontract Price List.
    *   For lump sum subcontracts, the Price List defines the breakdown of the price. For unit price subcontracts, it sets quantities for each unit, referencing standard unit definitions in [[construction_subcontract_pricing_unit_price_0_spc_6005]].
    *   The Price List forms the basis for Subcontractor progress claims.
    *   The Price List **must** be numbered according to [[document_numbering]] (0-PRC-0002), with its identification number identical to that of the Construction Subcontract Agreement (as per [[construction_subcontract_agreement_procedure_0_prc_6002]]).
    *   It will be published (0-PRC-0005) with status "Issued for Quotation", checked by the Project Engineer, and approved by the Project Manager.
    *   Other pricing lists (0-LST-6X12, 0-LST-6X13, 0-LST-6X14, 0-LST-6X15) are also prepared, numbered, and published with status "Issued for Quotation" but do not require initial checking or approval. These lists are for Subcontractor input to define their bid basis, with sample data provided in their templates.

5.  **Tender Process:**
    *   Construction Subcontractors invited to tender are identified by the project team (refer to [[project_execution_plan_overview]] (0-PRC-2003)).
    *   The Subcontract Administrator prepares covering letters referencing [[instructions_to_construction_subcontractors_for_tender_0_spc_6002]].
    *   Letters of invitation and a list of all required documents are forwarded to Document Control for issuance to Subcontractors (refer to [[control_of_project_documents_0_prc_0006]]).

6.  **Agreement Revision (Post-Negotiation):**
    *   The Subcontract Administrator revises the Construction Subcontract Agreement to incorporate modifications agreed during negotiation and information provided in the successful Subcontractor's bid.
    *   The revised Agreement is published (0-PRC-0005) with status "Approved for Construction".
    *   It **must** be checked by the Project Engineer and Legal, and approved by the Project Manager.
    *   Any exceptions or deviations agreed during negotiation **must** be handled by inserting appropriate text into [[special_conditions_for_construction_subcontract_0_spc_6x01]].
    *   Administrative specifications listed in Section 4.2 (refer to [[construction_subcontract_administrative_specifications]]) will not be further modified.

7.  **Pricing List Revision (Post-Negotiation):**
    *   The Subcontract Administrator revises 0-LST-6X11: Construction Subcontract Price List based on the successful tender and negotiated modifications.
    *   The revised Price List is published (0-PRC-0005) with status "Approved for Construction", checked by the Project Engineer, and approved by the Project Manager.
    *   Other pricing lists (0-LST-6X12, 0-LST-6X13, 0-LST-6X14, 0-LST-6X15) are also revised based on the successful tender and negotiations, published (0-PRC-0005) with status "Approved for Construction", checked by the Project Engineer, and approved by the Project Manager.

8.  **Final Issuance:**
    *   A paper copy of the Agreement **must** be printed and signed by the person authorized under Section 6 (refer to [[construction_subcontract_signing_authority]]).
    *   The signed paper copy **must** be presented with a copy of the approved [[construction_subcontractor_evaluation_and_approval_0_prc_6003]].
    *   The signed and authorized Construction Subcontract is forwarded to Document Control for issuance to the Subcontractor (0-PRC-0006).
    *   A list of all required documents is forwarded to Document Control by the Subcontract Administrator and Project Engineer for issuance to the Construction Subcontractor (0-PRC-0006).
