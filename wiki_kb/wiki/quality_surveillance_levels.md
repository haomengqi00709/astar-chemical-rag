# Quality Surveillance Levels

Aker Kvaerner Chemetics performs [[quality_surveillance]] to monitor the quality of supplied goods. This system defines different levels of surveillance based on the criticality and nature of the goods.

**Level 0 Quality Surveillance**
This level indicates that no quality surveillance will be carried out by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Level 1 Quality Surveillance**
This is defined as Final Quality Surveillance. It will be carried out in a single visit by an Inspector. This visit typically occurs upon completion of the manufacture of the goods, or it can occur at some other agreed stage prior to packaging for shipment.
