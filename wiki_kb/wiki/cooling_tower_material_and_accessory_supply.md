# Cooling Tower Material and Accessory Supply

For cooling towers specified under document **4-DST-XXXX-R0**, the vendor is solely responsible for the supply and mounting of all specified materials and accessories. This ensures that components are correctly integrated and meet the required quality and performance standards. This aligns with general [[vendor_quality_assurance_requirements]].

**Mandatory Material Supply and Mounting by Vendor:**
The vendor **shall** supply and mount the following materials:
*   **Fill**: The heat transfer media within the cooling tower.
*   **Framework**: The structural support system of the tower.
*   **Cold Water Basin**: The basin collecting cooled water.
*   **Casing & Partition**: The external enclosure and internal cell dividers.
*   **Fan**: The primary air movement device.
*   **Stairways**: Access structures for maintenance and inspection.
*   **Bolts, Nuts, Washer & Nail**: All necessary fasteners for assembly.
*   **Fan Deck**: The platform above the fan.
*   **Mist Eliminator**: Components designed to reduce water droplet escape.
*   **Louvers**: Air inlet and outlet screens.
*   **Water Manifolds**: Distribution systems for hot water.

**Mandatory Accessory Supply and Mounting by Vendor:**
The vendor **shall** supply and mount the following accessories:
*   **Drive System Specification**: The complete drive system, including motor and gearbox.
*   **Fan with gearbox**: The fan assembly integrated with its gearbox.
*   **Mist Eliminator**: As specified for performance.
*   **Water Manifolds**: For efficient water distribution.
*   **Fan discharge stack**: The stack for discharging air from the fan.
*   **Access ladder and Platform**: For safe access to various parts of the tower.
*   **Hot water basin cover**: To prevent contamination and evaporation.
*   **Lubrication**: All necessary lubrication systems and initial fill.
*   **Vibration cut out switch**: Safety device to shut down the fan in case of excessive vibration.
*   **Bearing**: All bearings required for rotating equipment.
*   **Cold Water Basin Heater**: For freeze protection in cold climates.

The comprehensive supply and mounting responsibility by the vendor is critical for maintaining the integrity and performance of the cooling tower, as detailed in the [[cooling_tower_design_requirements]] and [[cooling_tower_operating_conditions]].

**Vendor-Supplied Materials:**
The vendor is responsible for supplying and mounting the following materials:
*   Fill
*   Framework
*   Cold Water Basin
*   Casing & Partition
*   Fan
*   Stairways
*   Bolts, Nuts, Washer & Nail
*   Fan Deck
*   Mist Eliminator
*   Louvers
*   Water Manifolds

**Vendor-Supplied Specifications and Accessories:**
The vendor is also responsible for supplying and mounting the following specifications and accessories:
*   Drive System Specification
*   Fan with gearbox
*   Mist Eliminator
*   Water Manifolds
*   Fan discharge stack
*   Access ladder and Platform
*   Hot water basin cover
*   Lubrication
*   Vibration cut out switch
*   Bearing
*   Cold Water Basin Heater
