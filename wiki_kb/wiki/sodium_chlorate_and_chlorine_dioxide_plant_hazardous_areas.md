# Sodium Chlorate and Chlorine Dioxide Plant Hazardous Areas

Sodium Chlorate (NaClO3) and Chlorine Dioxide (ClO2) plants, while generally considered non-hazardous, contain specific areas designated as hazardous due to the potential presence of flammable gases like hydrogen and combustible dust. Adherence to these classifications is crucial for electrical safety.

**Specific Hazardous Locations (Class I, Division 1 or 2):**

*   **Hydrogen Sample Points:** These are hazardous areas, normally isolated with blind flanges or double valves.
*   **Main Vent Stack:** This is a hazardous area where hydrogen is released continuously and is piped outside. The area around the top of the main vent stack is classified as Class I, Division 1 for a 1.5m (5’-0”) radius sphere around the vent.
*   **Tail Gas Scrubber Vent:** If an HCl unit is attached, this vent is a hazardous area and is piped outside. The area around the top of the tail gas scrubber vent (HCl plant) is classified as Class I, Division 1 for a 1.5m (5’-0”) radius sphere around the vent.
*   **Hydrogen Vent after Hydrogen Scrubber:** This is a hazardous area where hydrogen is released continuously. The area around the top of the hydrogen scrubber vent is classified as Class I, Division 1 for a 1.5m (5’-0”) radius sphere around the vent.
*   **Rupture Disks:** These are hazardous areas, normally intact.
*   **Vents classified as Class I, Division 1 must be located outdoors.**

**Degasifier and Reactor Areas:**
*   The area from the degasifier and reactor rupture disc elevations to the building roof is classified as Class I, Division 2.
*   Except for lighting fixtures, no electrical equipment **will be** located in Class I, Division 2 areas (from degasifier/reactor rupture disc elevations to building roof).

**Rupture Disc Event Procedures:**
*   In the event of a rupture disc blowing and releasing hydrogen gas, the plant **will** automatically shutdown.
*   Following a rupture disc event, the hydrogen system **will be** purged with inert nitrogen to atmosphere.

**Lighting Requirements:**
*   Lighting on the degasifier floor in a Chlorate plant **should be** vapour-tight.
*   Lighting on the degasifier floor in a Chlorate plant **must** be installed with the top of the fixture 300 mm min. (1’-0”) spacing from the roof.

**Chlorate Dust Hazards (Class II):**
*   In chlorate plants, chlorate crystal and dust are flammable.
*   In areas where chlorate dust is present, equipment **must** be dust-tight.

**Ventilation Requirements:**
*   Good natural building ventilation **must** be ensured at all times the Chlorate cell room is in operation (i.e., the transformer/rectifier is energized).
*   The roof ridge ventilation and wall louvres **must** be open when the Chlorate cell room is in operation.
*   If roof vents can be closed, an interlock to the process **should be** provided to prevent the Transformer / Rectifier from operating.
