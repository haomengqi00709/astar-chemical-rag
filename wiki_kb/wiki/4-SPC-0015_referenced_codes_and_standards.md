# Referenced Codes and Standards (4-SPC-0015)

This Standard (4-SPC-0015) for welding nickel-base alloys references several industry codes and standards to ensure compliance and quality in fabrication. All referenced codes and standards **must** be interpreted as their latest edition ruling at the date of contract.

The following documents are referenced:

*   [[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping]]: Chemical Plant & Petroleum Refinery Piping.
*   [[ASME_bpvc_section_ii_materials]]: Boiler & Pressure Vessel Code Section II, Parts A, B & C - Materials Specifications.
*   [[ASME_sfa_5_11_nickel_and_nickel_alloy_electrodes]]: Specification for Nickel and Nickel-Alloy Covered Welding Electrodes.
*   [[ASME_sfa_5_12_tungsten_arc_welding_electrodes]]: Specification for Tungsten Arc-Welding Electrodes.
*   [[ASME_sfa_5_14_nickel_and_nickel_alloy_electrodes_and_rods]]: Specification for Nickel and Nickel-Alloy Bare Welding Rods and Electrodes.
*   [[ASME_section_v_non_destructive_examination]]: Non-destructive Examination.
*   [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels]]: Pressure Vessels.
*   [[ASME_section_ix_welding_and_brazing_qualifications]]: Welding and Brazing Qualifications.
*   [[AWS_a3_0_standard_welding_terms_and_definitions]]: Standard Welding Terms & Definitions.
*   [[API_620_low_pressure_storage_tanks]]: Recommended Rules for the Design and Construction of Large Welded Low Pressure Storage Tanks.

These references provide the foundational requirements for material selection, welding procedures, welder qualifications, and inspection methods applicable to the fabrication of equipment using Incoloy 825®.
