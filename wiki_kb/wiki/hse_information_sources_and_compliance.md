# HSE Information Sources and Compliance

Adherence to Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) policies and procedures is mandatory for all personnel at Aker Kvaerner Chemetics work sites. Access to comprehensive and up-to-date information is crucial for maintaining a safe and compliant working environment.

**Key Information Sources**
Personnel **must** be familiar with and refer to the following sources for HSE information:

*   **Occupancy Health and Safety Regulations:** These are external regulatory documents, typically from the Workmen’s Compensation Board or Worker’s Compensation Board, which outline legal requirements for workplace safety.
*   **Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Health and Safety Policies:** These internal policies are available on the company's Q: drive.
*   **Aker Kvaerner ENET:** This is an internal Aker Kvaerner resource that provides a wide range of company-specific information, including HSE guidelines.
*   **Aker Kvaerner Health and Safety Policy Manual:** A comprehensive manual detailing Aker Kvaerner's overarching HSE policies.
*   **AKC Technology Centre Safety Manual:** This manual is specifically for the Technology Centre and is posted under "Lab_Data" on the U: drive.

**Compliance Requirements**
*   All health and safety policies and procedures **are to be followed** without exception.
*   When working on a client's premises, client policies **will** be followed in addition to, or in conjunction with, company policies.

For any questions regarding health and safety policies and procedures, personnel **are to direct** their inquiries to their immediate Supervisor, the [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_document_history|HSE Manager]], Safety Coordinator, Human Resources, or, for the Research Laboratory, AKCTC Safety Committee Members.
