# Absorber Pump Data Sheet (99H10600-4-DST-7540)

The document 99H10600-4-DST-7540 R1, titled "Absorber Pump 7540P," provides the detailed technical requirements and specifications for absorber pumps identified with tag numbers 7540P-A/B/C within Project 99H10600. This data sheet is a crucial reference for vendors responsible for supplying these specific pumps.

Similar to other equipment data sheets, this document typically contains:
*   **Process Conditions:** Operating and design conditions such as fluid properties, temperatures, pressures, and flow rates specific to the absorber service.
*   **Hydraulic Performance:** Required head, capacity, NPSH (Net Positive Suction Head) requirements, and efficiency expectations.
*   **Mechanical Design:** Specifications for casing, impeller, shaft, bearings, seals, and coupling, including material selections.
*   **Motor Specifications:** Power, voltage, frequency, enclosure type, and hazardous area classification requirements for the pump motor.
*   **Ancillary Equipment:** Requirements for baseplates, guards, lubrication systems, and any other auxiliary components.
*   **Quality Control and Documentation:** Specific inspection points, testing procedures, and vendor documentation deliverables.

Vendors supplying absorber pumps for Project 99H10600 **must** ensure that their Goods and Services strictly comply with the specifications detailed in this data sheet. This document is typically referenced in Text Section 1: Scope of Supply of the relevant Purchase Order.
