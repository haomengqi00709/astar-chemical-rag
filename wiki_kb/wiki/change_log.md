# Change Log

The Change Log is an essential tracking document used throughout the [[0-PRC-4003-R1_project_change_management_flow_diagram|Project Change Management Flow Diagram]]. Its purpose is to maintain a comprehensive and chronological record of all proposed and approved changes to a project.

During the [[project_change_implementation|Project Change Implementation]] phase, the [[change_management_roles_and_responsibilities_0_prc_4003|Project Control Specialist]] **must** update the Change Log. This update includes details of the implemented change, its status (e.g., "Progress/Approved"), and the date of formal approval.

Key functions of the Change Log include:
*   **Centralized Record**: It provides a single, auditable source of truth for all Project Variation Notices (PVNs), Project Change Notices (PCNs), and [[formal_change_order|Formal Change Orders]].
*   **Status Tracking**: It allows for monitoring the current status of each change, from initiation through evaluation, approval, and implementation.
*   **Historical Data**: It serves as a historical record of all modifications made to the project, which is invaluable for post-project analysis, lessons learned, and future project planning.

The [[change_management_roles_and_responsibilities_0_prc_4003|Project Manager]] **must** regularly review the Change Log with the project team. This review is crucial for:
*   Monitoring the progress of pending changes.
*   Ensuring that all approved changes are being effectively managed.
*   Identifying any bottlenecks or issues in the change management process.

For detailed procedures on managing and updating the Change Log, refer to [[0-PRC-4003-R1_pcn_log_procedure]] and [[pvn_pcn_log_and_documentation_management]]. The Change Log is a fundamental component of the [[change_management_document_types]] suite, ensuring robust control over project modifications.

During the [[project_change_implementation|Project Change Implementation]] phase, the Change Log is updated to reflect the final status of approved changes, including their implementation details and any resulting adjustments to project baselines. The Project Manager regularly reviews the Change Log with the project team to monitor pending changes and ensure timely resolution.
