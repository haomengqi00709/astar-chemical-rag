# Nickel/Copper Welding Execution and Quality (4-SPC-0021)

The execution and quality of nickel/copper (UNS N04400 / Monel 400) welding under **4-SPC-0021** **shall** meet specific standards to ensure the integrity of the fabricated items.

**General Workmanship and Adherence to Drawings:**
*   All work **shall** be carried out in accordance with [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_company_details|Aker Kvaerner Chemetics]] "Approved for Fabrication" shop detail drawing(s).
*   Deviations from weld geometry on approved drawing(s) are **not permitted** without prior approval of [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_company_details|Aker Kvaerner Chemetics]].

**Welding Environment and Surface Preparation:**
*   As far as practical, all welds **shall** be made in the downhand (flat) position. Vertical welds **shall** be made vertical up (uphand).
*   Suitable enclosures **shall** be provided to keep welds and welders free from draughts, rain, or other inclement weather.
*   Welding **shall not** be performed on wet surfaces.
*   Fusion faces and adjacent metal, both internal and external, **shall** be free from moisture, oxide, paint, grease, or other foreign matter immediately prior to welding.

**Joint Alignment and Fit-Up:**
*   Alignment of welding edges at butt joints **shall** be in accordance with the relevant code, specification, etc.
*   Maximum misalignment (High-Low) for plates up to and including 1/4" in thickness **shall** be 1/16".
*   Maximum misalignment (High-Low) for plates above 1/4" in thickness **shall** be 25% of the plate thickness or 1/8", whichever is smaller.
*   Misaligned surfaces **shall** be chamfered accordingly.
*   Alignment of butt joints for pipe **shall** be in accordance with [[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping|ASME B31.3]].
*   Run-off pads, in the same material as the parent plate, **shall** be used at the beginning and end of each plate weld and longitudinal pipe weld.

**Assembly and Tack Welding:**
*   Parts to be welded **shall** be securely held in position by mechanical means, welded-on bridge pieces, or tack welding to maintain specified alignment and gap during welding.
*   Jigs or suitable devices used for assembly **shall** allow adequate access for welding and cleaning, and **shall** be such that external loading is not applied to a joint during welding.
*   Welded-on bridge or support spiders **shall** be of nickel material/weld metal where in contact with the vessel, heat exchanger, or pipework. The plate surface **shall not** be left in a damaged condition after removal.
*   Tack welds employed for assembly purposes may form part of the completed weld provided complete fusion is effected at the root of the weld and the welding procedure used is the same as that employed for the remainder of the root run.
*   Cracked and unsound tack welds **shall** be cut out prior to root pass or full seam welding.
*   Tack welds **should** preferably be at least one inch long when feathering is necessary.
*   Sound tack welds **must** be thoroughly fused into the root of the main weld.
*   The use of permanent back-up rings or strips or consumable joint inserts is **not permitted** unless specifically approved in writing by [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_company_details|Aker Kvaerner Chemetics]] QA & Materials Engineering.

**Arc Strikes and Weld Cleaning:**
*   Every effort **shall** be made to confine arc strikes to surfaces on which weld metal is to be deposited.
*   If an arc strike occurs outside the weld area, the spot **shall** be ground, PT examined per Para. 10.3 (refer to [[4-spc-0021_inspection_and_nde_nickel_copper|Inspection and NDE for Nickel/Copper Welds]]), and repaired if necessary.
*   Burn marks from grounding clamps **shall** be treated as arc strikes.
*   Each run of weld metal **shall** be thoroughly cleaned and all slag and foreign matter removed before the next run is deposited.
*   On re-starting, care **shall** be taken to ensure proper fusion with the weld metal previously deposited.
*   All restarts in the root bend **shall** be preceded by "feathering" out the area of the stop by grinding or filing, followed by wire brushing to remove any abrasive residue.

**Tungsten and Filler Rod Contamination:**
*   If a tungsten electrode accidentally makes contact with the work, the section of weld and the tip of the electrode **should** be examined. Where tungsten loss has occurred, the tungsten pick-up in the weld **shall** be ground out, and the electrode **shall** be reground to the correct profile before re-starting.
*   If the heated end of the filler rod becomes contaminated (i.e., falls outside of the inert atmosphere at the weld puddle), it **shall** be snipped off and discarded.

**Weld Appearance and Defects:**
*   Peening of weld runs is **not permitted**. Hammering of finished welds is **not permitted**.
*   Each weld **shall** be uniform in width and size throughout its full length.
*   Each layer of welding **shall** be smooth and free of slag, inclusions, cracks, porosity, lack of fusion, and undercut, except to the extent permitted in the referenced codes.
*   The cover pass **shall** be free of coarse ripples, irregular surface, non-uniform bead patterns, high crown, and deep ridges or valleys between beads.
*   Buttwelds **shall** be slightly convex, of uniform height, and have full penetration.
*   Internal reinforcement for pipe welds **shall** be subject to the limitations of the governing code.
*   Crevices or undercut **shall not** be permitted on the process side of the weld.
*   As far as is practicable, fillet welds **shall** be deposited in not less than two layers.
*   Surfaces of fillet welds **shall** be free from marked irregularities.
*   Dimensions of fillet welds **shall** be in accordance with the approved drawing or specified size, with uniform legs and full throat.

**Grinding and Wire Brushes:**
*   Grinding or gouging **shall** be carried out in such a manner as not to reduce the adjacent base metal thickness.
*   Complete removal of weld defects **shall** be verified by non-destructive examination prior to repair welding.
*   Rubber bonded grinding wheels or abrasive discs **shall not** be used.
*   Wire brushes **shall** be made of austenitic stainless steel and **must not** have been previously used on other metals.
