# 0-POL-9111 Assignment Uplift

The Assignment Uplift provision within procedure 0-POL-9111 is designed to compensate employees for specific conditions encountered during extended site assignments.

**Purpose and Coverage:**
The Assignment Uplift compensates for site location and conditions, as well as for additional home and living expenses incurred due to extended periods of absence from the employee's primary residence.
Examples of expenses intended to be covered by the Assignment Uplift premium include:
*   Child care arrangements
*   Home maintenance expenses such as lawn mowing
*   Pet care arrangements including kennel boarding fees
*   Entertainment expenses such as fitness or theatre costs during assignment
*   Costs associated with mail forwarding or mail pick-up
*   Local membership fees such as gym memberships
Expenses covered by Assignment Uplift [[will not normally]] be reimbursed as part of travel expenses under [[business_expense_reimbursement_0_prc_9003]].

**Eligibility Criteria:**
Assignment Uplift [[will only]] be paid if the time on site is in excess of 30 consecutive days, excluding travel time.
*   Should the planned trip be expected to be more than 30 days, Assignment Uplift [[will]] commence immediately.
*   If the planned trip is for less than 30 days and the trip is extended for more than 30 days, Assignment Uplift [[will]] be retroactively paid from the day the employee arrived on site.
Travel for less than 30 days [[will]] be treated as a business trip. Expenses for travel less than 30 days [[will]] be reimbursed in accordance with [[business_expense_reimbursement_0_prc_9003]].
As outlined in the [[0_pol_9111_site_compensation_policy_overview]], Assignment Uplift provisions apply only for employees of The Company in positions below Hay Point 640. This limitation may be waived by the President under exceptional circumstances.
The Assignment Uplift does not apply during normal leaves in accordance with the [[0_pol_9111_home_leave_and_r_and_r]] section of this policy.

**Determination and Calculation:**
An Assignment Uplift [[will]] be determined in accordance with Group norms and the International Assignment Procedure. This determination includes obtaining uplift factors from Employment Conditions Abroad ("ECA"). ECA is a consulting firm that specialises in measuring living standard differences between countries and work locations.
The Assignment Uplift [[will]] be applied to base salary for the duration of the assignment, excluding travel time.

**Tax and Benefits Implications:**
Payments made under the Assignment Uplift section are taxable earnings.
However, these payments are not considered earnings for pension purposes.
Similarly, payments made under the Assignment Uplift section are not considered earnings for benefits purposes.

**Responsibilities:**
*   It is the responsibility of the employee and the Department Manager to ensure that the travel requisition requests Assignment Uplift if the planned trip is more than 30 days.
*   The Project Manager [[must]] approve the travel requisition.
*   Once the approved travel requisition is received, this [[will]] trigger communication with Human Resources and Finance to apply this procedure.
*   If the planned trip is less than 30 days but the assignment is extended beyond 30 days, the Project Manager [[must]] advise Human Resources of this change.

**Eligibility and Conditions**
Assignment Uplift provisions apply only for employees of The Company in positions below Hay Point 640. This limitation may be waived by the President under exceptional circumstances.

Assignment Uplift will only be paid if the time on site is in excess of 30 consecutive days (excluding travel time).
*   Should the planned trip be expected to be more than 30 days, Assignment Uplift will commence immediately.
*   If the planned trip is for less than 30 days and the trip is extended for more than 30 days, Assignment Uplift will be retroactively paid from the day the employee arrived on site.

**Determination Process**
An Assignment Uplift will be determined in accordance with Group norms and the International Assignment Procedure. This determination includes obtaining uplift factors from Employment Conditions Abroad (“ECA”), a consulting firm that specialises in measuring living standard differences between countries and work locations. The Assignment Uplift will be applied to base salary for the duration of the assignment, excluding travel time.

**Administrative Process**
It is the joint responsibility of the employee and the Department Manager to ensure that the travel requisition requests Assignment Uplift if the planned trip is more than 30 days. The Project Manager must approve the travel requisition. Once the approved travel requisition is received, this will trigger communication with Human Resources and Finance to apply this procedure. If a planned trip is less than 30 days but the assignment is extended beyond 30 days, the Project Manager must advise Human Resources of this change.

**Financial and Tax Implications**
Expenses covered by Assignment Uplift will not normally be reimbursed as part of travel expenses under 0-PRC-9003 Business Expense Reimbursement. Payments made under the Assignment Uplift section are considered taxable earnings. However, these payments are not considered earnings for pension purposes or for benefits purposes.
