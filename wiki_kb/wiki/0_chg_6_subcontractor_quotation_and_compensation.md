# Subcontractor Quotation and Compensation (Document 0-CHG-6)

Document **0-CHG-6** establishes the mandatory procedures for subcontractor quotations and subsequent compensation. These guidelines ensure transparency and proper financial management for all subcontracted work.

**Quotation Submission:**
The Subcontractor **shall** submit a comprehensive quotation for the work described. This quotation forms the basis for agreement on the scope and cost of services. The instruction to not proceed with work until instructed, as detailed in [[0_chg_6_subcontractor_work_authorization]], is directly linked to the acceptance of this quotation.

**Cost Determination and Compensation Claims:**
The Subcontractor **shall** determine the actual cost of the work performed. Following the completion of the work or agreed-upon milestones, the Subcontractor **shall** submit a claim for compensation. Compensation will be provided strictly in accordance with the quotation received and accepted by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. This ensures that all payments are aligned with pre-agreed terms, supporting accurate [[cost_tabulation_procurement]].

**Compensation Event Assessment:**
In instances where the Subcontractor’s quotation is not accepted by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, the firm reserves the right to assess the compensation event independently. This provision allows for a fair evaluation of work performed or costs incurred up to the point of non-acceptance, ensuring equitable treatment while maintaining financial control. This process is part of the broader [[procurement_and_vendor_documentation]] framework, designed to manage contractual relationships effectively.
