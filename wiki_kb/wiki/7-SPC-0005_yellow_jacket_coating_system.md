# Yellow Jacket Coating System (7-SPC-0005)

The Yellow Jacket coating system, also known as X-Tru-Coat, is a shop-applied protective coating for pipes. It consists of an extruded yellow polyethylene jacket over a butyl rubber adhesive.

**Application and Components**
The pipe **will be shop-coated** with X-Tru-Coat. This application is typically performed by Standard Pipe Protection or a similar approved system. The complete coating system comprises several critical steps and components:
*   Pipe cleaning by shot blasting.
*   Flame preheat and degreasing.
*   Application of an undercoat sealant.
*   Application of the corrosion-resistant yellow jacket.
*   Controlled cooling.
*   Electronic holiday inspection.
*   Ends cut back for welding.
*   Thickness check.

**Material Specifications and Dimensions**
*   The butyl rubber adhesive thickness **will be** 10 mils.
*   The polyethylene jacket thickness over the 10-mil adhesive **will be** the manufacturer’s standard for pipe diameters below 4 inches.
*   For pipe diameters 4 inches through 24 inches, the polyethylene jacket thickness over the 10-mil adhesive **will be** 60 mils.
*   Shop-coated pipe **must have** a 6-inch cutback at the ends to facilitate welding.

**Operating Temperature Limits**
*   The maximum continuous operating temperature for the X-Tru-Coat (yellow polyethylene) is 150 F.
*   An optional orange polypropylene copolymer jacket is available, suitable for a maximum continuous operating temperature of 190 F.

For details on surface preparation, refer to [[7-SPC-0005_surface_preparation_for_coatings]]. Inspection and testing procedures are outlined in [[7-SPC-0005_coating_inspection_and_holiday_testing]].

**Coating System Components and Specifications**
The complete coating system comprises several key stages and materials:
*   Pipe cleaned by shot blasting.
*   Flame preheat and degreasing.
*   Undercoat sealant.
*   Corrosion resistant jacket (yellow jacket).
*   Controlled cooling.
*   Electronic holiday inspection.
*   Ends cut back, for welding.
*   Thickness check.

**Thickness Specifications**
*   The butyl rubber adhesive thickness will be 10 mils.
*   Polyethylene jacket thickness over a 10-mil thickness of adhesive will be manufacturer’s standard for pipe diameters below 4”.
*   Polyethylene jacket thickness over a 10-mil thickness of adhesive will be 60 mils for pipe 4” through 24”.

**Optional Coatings**
*   Maximum continuous operating temperature for optional orange polypropylene copolymer jacket is 190 F.
