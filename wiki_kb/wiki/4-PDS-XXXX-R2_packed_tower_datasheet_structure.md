# Packed Tower Datasheet Structure (4-PDS-XXXX-R2)

The Packed Tower Process Data Sheet (4-PDS-XXXX-R2) is structured to capture comprehensive information for the design, procurement, and operation of packed towers. The document contains 24 distinct data columns, ensuring all critical parameters are specified.

**Key Fields and Sections:**

The datasheet includes the following essential fields to identify and describe the packed tower and its operational context:
*   **Tag No.:** Unique identifier for the equipment.
*   **Location:** Physical placement of the packed tower within the plant.
*   **Case Description:** A brief description of the specific operating scenario or design case.
*   **Design Basis:** The fundamental criteria and assumptions used for the tower's design.
*   **Stream:** Refers to a specific process stream, often linked to a process flow diagram or sketch.
*   **Fluid Description:** Details about the process fluids handled by the tower.

**Internals and Materials Specific Fields:**
The datasheet also includes dedicated fields for the tower's internal components and materials of construction:
*   **Packing Type:** The specific type of packing used (e.g., random, structured).
*   **Packing Material:** The material from which the packing is constructed.
*   **Packing Support Type:** The design of the support structure for the packing.
*   **Packing Support Material:** The material of construction for the packing support.
*   **Bed Limiter Type:** The type of device used to limit the height of the packed bed.
*   **Bed Limiter Material:** The material of construction for the bed limiter.
*   **Redistributor/Distributor Type:** The design of the liquid redistributor or distributor.
*   **Redistributor/Distributor Material:** The material of construction for the redistributor/distributor.
*   **Collector Plates Type:** The type of plates used for liquid collection.
*   **Collector Plates Material:** The material of construction for the collector plates.

For a general understanding of datasheet structures and column definitions, refer to [[0_dst_0_data_sheet_structure_and_columns]]. An overview of process data sheets can be found in [[process_data_sheet_overview]].
