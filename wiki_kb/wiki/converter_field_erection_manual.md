# Converter Field Erection Manual

The Converter Field Erection Manual provides detailed instructions and procedures for the on-site assembly and erection of converter components. This manual is a crucial reference document for field personnel, ensuring that all parts are correctly installed according to design specifications.

As referenced in [[4-SPC-7540_shop_fabrication_and_assembly]], all fabricated parts **must** be tagged as outlined in this manual. This tagging system facilitates accurate identification and sequencing during the erection process.

The manual typically covers:
*   Detailed assembly sequences.
*   Match marking and tagging conventions.
*   Installation procedures for various components (e.g., shell courses, heads, internals, nozzles).
*   Requirements for field welding and inspection.
*   Safety protocols specific to converter erection.
*   Dimensional checks and alignment procedures.

This manual works in conjunction with shop fabrication specifications to ensure a seamless transition from manufacturing to field installation.
