# Pressure Measurement - General

Pressure instruments are fundamental for monitoring and controlling process pressures. General requirements ensure their suitability and reliability.

**Materials and Range Selection:**
*   Unless otherwise specified, the process wetted parts for pressure instruments **will be** 316 stainless steel.
*   Pressure ranges **should be** selected so that the normal operating pressure **will be** between 50% and 70% of full scale or instrument span. Pressure range selection **should have** due regard for the maximum pressure expected.

**Pressure Gauges:**
*   **Construction:** Pressure gauges **will** have stainless steel type 316 cases and clear 3 mm thick shatter-resistant window glass.
*   **Fill Liquid:** Pressure gauges for Process Air or Instrument Air service, not subject to pulsation, do not require liquid fill. All other gauges **will** have liquid (glycerine) fill to dampen pulsations and reduce wear.
*   **Safety:** Pressure gauges **will** incorporate blowout protection devices on the rear of each gauge.
*   **Size and Connections:** Case diameters **will be** 100 mm for process indications and 50 mm for gauges monitoring instrumentation (e.g., filter regulators, positioners). Pressure gauges **will** be provided with ½” NPT process connections.
*   **Scaling:** Scaling for pressure gauges **will be** in kilopascals (kPa). Dial scales **will be** marked in black on a white background.

**Diaphragm Seals with Pressure Gauges:**
Diaphragm seals provided with pressure gauges **will be** 40 mm flange mount type. The fill solution for these seals **will be** compatible with the process fluids.

**Pressure Switches:**
*   **Measuring Element:** Pressure switch measuring elements may be Bourdon tube, bellows, or diaphragm type, depending upon the service and pressure.
*   **Connections:** Connections for process pressure switches **will be** ½” NPT.
*   **Diaphragm Seals:** When process conditions require, pressure switches **will be** provided with diaphragm seals. The diaphragm seal for pressure switches **will be** 40 mm flange mount type, and its fill solution **will be** compatible with the process fluid.
*   **Safety:** Pressure switches **will** incorporate over-pressure protection to prevent pressurization of the case in the event of failure of the measuring device.
*   **Electrical Specifications:** Electrical switches **will** have a contact rating of 240 V a.c/24 V d.c., 2 A minimum with SPDT contacts.

**Pressure Transmitters:**
Pressure transmitters **will be** of materials suitable for the process applications. Except where remote diaphragm seals are used, the process connection of all transmitters **will be** 1/2" NPT female threads.
