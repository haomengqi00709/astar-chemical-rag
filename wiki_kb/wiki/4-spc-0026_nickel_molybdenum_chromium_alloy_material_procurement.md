# Material Procurement and Specifications

This section details the requirements for the procurement and handling of materials used in nickel/molybdenum/chromium alloy fabrications.

**Material Procurement**
Plate, sheet, pipe, and tube shall be purchased in accordance with ASME specifications SB574, SB575, SB619, SB622, and SB626. Plate and sheet shall be supplied in the solution annealed condition for maximum corrosion resistance unless specified otherwise on the purchase order.

**Material Control and Rejection**
The fabricator's internal controls shall prevent the use of unspecified materials. Lack of adequate identification or certification shall be cause for rejection of the specific component or item.
