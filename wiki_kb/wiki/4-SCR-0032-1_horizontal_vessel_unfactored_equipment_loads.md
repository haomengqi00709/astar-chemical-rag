# Horizontal Vessel Unfactored Equipment Loads (4-SCR-0032-1)

This page details the unfactored loads for horizontal vessels, as specified in document 4-SCR-0032-1. These loads are crucial for the structural design of their supports and foundations. Unfactored loads represent the maximum expected forces and masses under various operating and environmental conditions, prior to the application of any safety factors. Accurate assessment of these unfactored loads is fundamental to ensuring the long-term stability and safety of the vessel.

**Unfactored Load Specifications:**
The following unfactored loads are specified for the horizontal vessel:
*   **Unfactored Empty - Maximum Mass:** Specified in kg. This represents the mass of the vessel when empty, including all permanent attachments.
*   **Unfactored Flooded (Design) - Maximum Mass:** Specified in kg. This accounts for the mass of the vessel when completely filled with its design fluid, used for hydrostatic testing and specific design scenarios.
*   **Unfactored Operating - Maximum Mass:** Specified in kg. This is the mass of the vessel under normal operating conditions, including process fluids and internal components.
*   **Unfactored Wind Base Shear:** Specified in kN (Long. / Trans., Note 2). This refers to the horizontal force exerted by wind at the base of the support structure, broken down into longitudinal and transverse components.
*   **Unfactored Wind Base Moment:** Specified in kN-m (Long. / Trans.). This is the overturning moment caused by wind forces at the base, also specified for longitudinal and transverse directions.
*   **Unfactored Seismic Base Shear:** Specified in kN (Long. / Trans., Note 2). This represents the horizontal force at the base due to seismic activity, in both longitudinal and transverse directions.
*   **Unfactored Seismic Base Moment:** Specified in kN-m (Long. / Trans.). This is the overturning moment at the base due to seismic forces.

**Load Application Notes:**
*   Loads are to be applied at Datum.
*   Loads in the longitudinal direction are resisted by the fixed support only.

These load specifications are integral to the [[equipment_support_criteria]] and are used in conjunction with [[seismic_design_requirements]] and [[wind_design_requirements]] to ensure robust structural design.
