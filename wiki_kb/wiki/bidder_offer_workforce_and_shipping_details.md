# Bidder Offer Workforce and Shipping Details

Bidders are required to provide specific information regarding their workforce and proposed shipping methods to ensure compliance and logistical planning.

**Workforce Information:**
*   **Mandatory:** Bidders must state the trade union affiliations of their workforce. This information is crucial for understanding potential labor relations impacts.
*   **Mandatory:** Bidders must state the expiry dates of their collective agreements. This provides insight into potential labor negotiations or disruptions during the project timeline.

**Shipping and Packaging:**
*   **Mandatory:** Bidders must state the method of shipping proposed for delivery to the specified point of delivery. This includes details such as land, sea, or air transport. For detailed packaging requirements, refer to [[packaging_land_transportation_0_spc_5004]], [[shipping_marks_and_labeling]], [[packing_list_requirements]], and [[over_dimensional_load_requirements]].
*   **Mandatory:** Bidders must complete the Packaging Summary form. This form requires a detailed description of the size and weight of all packages, which is essential for logistics planning and cost estimation.

Providing these details allows Aker Kvaerner Chemetics to assess the logistical feasibility and potential industrial relations considerations associated with each offer.
