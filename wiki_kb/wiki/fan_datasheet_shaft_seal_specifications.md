# Fan Datasheet Shaft Seal Specifications

This page details the mandatory specifications for the shaft seal system of the fan, as required by the [[fan_datasheet_administration|Fan Datasheet Template]] (4-DST-XXXX-R0). These specifications are crucial for ensuring containment and operational integrity.

**Mandatory Shaft Seal Requirements**
The following information regarding the shaft seal system **must** be provided:
*   **Shaft Seal Requirements**: General specifications for the shaft seal. Refer to [[fan_shaft_seal_requirements]].
*   **Shaft Seal Supply by**: The entity responsible for supplying the shaft seal. Refer to [[shaft_seal_vendor_supply]].
*   **Shaft Seal Mount by**: The entity responsible for mounting the shaft seal. Refer to [[shaft_seal_installation_responsibility]].
*   **Shaft Seal Type**: The specific type of shaft seal to be used. Refer to [[shaft_seal_types]].

**Purge System Requirements**
If a purge system is required for the shaft seal, the following details **must** be specified:
*   **Purge Piping Arrangement**: The required arrangement for the purge piping. Refer to [[purge_piping_arrangement_details]].
*   **Purge Fluid**: The type of fluid used for purging. Refer to [[purge_fluid_specification]].
*   **Purge Fluid Pressure**: The required pressure of the purge fluid, measured in kPa(g). Refer to [[purge_fluid_pressure_requirements]].
*   **Purge Fluid Flow Rate**: The required flow rate of the purge fluid, measured in m3/h. Refer to [[purge_fluid_flow_rate_requirements]].
*   **VTA**: This field is required, likely referring to a specific valve or component in the purge system. Refer to [[vta_specification]].
*   **Purge Fluid Connection**: Details regarding the connection point for the purge fluid. Refer to [[purge_fluid_connection_details]].

**Purge System Requirements**
If a purge system is utilized for the shaft seal, the following details are mandatory:
*   **Purge Piping Arrangement**: Required field.
*   **Purge Fluid**: Required field.
*   **Purge Fluid Pressure**: Required field, measured in kPa(g).
*   **Purge Fluid Flow Rate**: Required field, measured in m3/h.
*   **VTA**: Required field.
*   **Purge Fluid Connection**: Required field.
