# Non-Destructive Examination Requirements for Carbon Steel Tank (0-QDS-7435)

Non-Destructive Examination (NDE) for the Carbon Steel Tank, as specified in Item 14.0 of QDS 0-QDS-7435, is a critical component of the quality assurance process. These examinations are performed to detect defects or discontinuities without damaging the material.

**Compliance Standards:**
All Non-Destructive Examination procedures **must** be conducted in accordance with the applicable standards:
*   API 650 (Welded Tanks for Oil Storage)
*   API 620 (Design and Construction of Large, Welded, Low-Pressure Storage Tanks)

Approved equivalents to these standards may be used, provided they receive prior approval from Chemetics.

**Required NDE Records and Witnessing:**
*   **Radiography Record:** This record is required as FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package) as per [[0-QDS-7435_quality_record_definitions]]. Chemetics **must** witness Radiography and **must** hold Radiography.
*   **Dye Penetrant Record:** This record is required as FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package) as per [[0-QDS-7435_quality_record_definitions]]. Chemetics **must** witness Dye Penetrant and **must** hold Dye Penetrant.

For a comprehensive list of all witnessing and holding requirements by Chemetics, refer to [[0-QDS-7435_inspection_and_documentation_requirements]].
