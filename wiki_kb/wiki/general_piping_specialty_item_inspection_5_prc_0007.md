# General Piping Specialty Item Inspection (5-PRC-0007)

Beyond specific components, the [[mechanical_checkout_piping_5_prc_0007|Piping Mechanical Checkout Procedure (5-PRC-0007)]] includes a general inspection for other piping specialty items to ensure their correct installation and material compliance.

**Required Documentation for General Piping Specialty Item Checkout:**
*   [[piping_and_instrumentation_drawings|P&ID]]
*   [[specialty_item_drawing|Specialty Item Drawing]]
*   [[pipe_isometric|Isometric]]

**General Inspection Steps for Piping Specialty Items:**
*   **Flange Checks:** Where applicable, all flange checks for piping specialty items **shall** be performed as described in the [[flange_inspection_requirements_5_prc_0007|Flange Inspection Requirements]].
*   **Material and Installation Compliance:** Confirm that the materials and installation of the specialty item are in accordance with the [[specialty_item_drawing|specialty item drawing]].

This general category ensures that all unique or less common piping components are also subjected to a verification process, maintaining the overall quality and safety of the piping system.
