# Gas/Gas Heat Exchanger Design Conditions

This page outlines the design conditions and codes applicable to the Gas/Gas Heat Exchanger, as specified in document 4-DST-XXXX-R1.

**Vessel Registration and Design Codes:**
*   **Vessel Registration:** No
*   **Vessel Design Codes:** Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Proprietary and ASME Section VIII, Division 1
*   **Chemetics Specifications:** 4-SPC-0040, 4-SPC-0041 & 4-SPC-0061

**Process Contents:**
*   **Shell Side Contents:** SO2 Gas (exit interpass tower)
*   **Tube Side Contents:** SO3 Gas (exit bed 2)
*   **Shell Side Specific Gravity Contents:** N/A
*   **Tube Side Specific Gravity Contents:** N/A

**Pressure Design:**
*   **Shell Side Design Internal Pressure:** 70 kPa(g)
*   **Tube Side Design Internal Pressure:** 70 kPa(g)
*   **Shell Side Overpressure Protection:** Note 4
*   **Tube Side Overpressure Protection:** Note 4
*   **Shell Side Design External Pressure:** N/A kPa(g)
*   **Tube Side Design External Pressure:** N/A kPa(g)
*   **Shell Side Vacuum Protection:** N/A
*   **Tube Side Vacuum Protection:** N/A

**Test Pressure:**
*   **Shell Side Test Pressure:** N/A kPa(g)
*   **Tube Side Test Pressure:** N/A kPa(g)

**Design Temperatures:**
The following maximum design temperatures are specified for various components:
*   **Top Vestibule (Shell Side):** 455 °C
*   **Top Vestibule (Tube Side):** 455 °C
*   **Top Tubesheet (Shell Side):** 420 °C
*   **Top Tubesheet (Tube Side):** 420 °C
*   **Top Expansion Plate (Shell Side):** 405 °C
*   **Shell (Shell Side):** 390 °C
*   **Btm. Expansion Plate (Shell Side):** 320 °C
*   **Bottom Tubesheet (Shell Side):** 140 °C
*   **Bottom Tubesheet (Tube Side):** 140 °C
*   **Bottom Vestibule (Shell Side):** 110 °C
*   **Bottom Vestibule (Tube Side):** 175 °C
*   **Tubes (Tube Side):** 420 °C
*   **Base Ring (Shell Side):** 110 °C

**Mean Metal Temperatures:**
*   **Design Mean Metal Temp. (Shell Side):** 223 °C
*   **Design Mean Metal Temp. (Tube Side):** 256 °C
*   **Alt. Mean Metal Temp. (Shell Side):** 32 °C
*   **Alt. Mean Metal Temp. (Tube Side):** 80 °C

**Corrosion Allowance:**
*   **Shell Side:** 0 mm
*   **Tube Side:** 0 mm

**External Loads and Temperature Calculations:**
*   External loads (wind, seismic, snow) reference: [[1-DST-0001_site_design_conditions]].
*   External loads (4-SCR-xxxx) will be independently prepared (internally) for use by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or Owner for civil/structural engineering.
*   External loads (civil/structural engineering) reference: [[4-PRC-0003_equipment_design_and_specification]].
*   Design temperatures and mean metal temperatures are calculated using [[4-CAL-0002_gas_gas_hx_design_temperatures]].
