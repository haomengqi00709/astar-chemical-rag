# Electrical Materials List

The Electrical Materials List identifies all bulk electrical materials required for a project, including quantities, descriptions, and materials of construction. This list is essential for procurement and inventory management.

**Content of the Electrical Materials List**
The electrical materials list **shall** include:
*   All bulk materials required for the project.
*   All quantities, full descriptions, and materials of construction for each item.

**Examples of Bulk Electrical Materials:**
*   Lighting fixtures.
*   Receptacles.
*   Push button switches.
*   Lighting panel boards.
*   Lighting distribution transformers.
*   Miscellaneous power distribution panel boards.
*   Miscellaneous power distribution transformers.
*   Conduits.
*   Conduit accessories.
*   Grounding materials.
*   Wiring materials (e.g., lugs, tyraps, wire markers, tape).
*   Cable trays.
*   Cable tray accessories.

**Purpose and Usage**
The electrical materials list is used for purchasing bulk materials for the project. It ensures that all necessary components are identified and procured efficiently.
