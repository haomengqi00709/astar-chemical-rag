# Converter Nozzle Schedule

This schedule provides detailed specifications for all nozzles on the Converter vessel (Tag No. 7540R). All dimensions are in millimeters unless otherwise specified.

**General Notes:**
*   Nozzle diameter will be specified as OD (Outside Diameter), ID (Inside Diameter), or Nom. (Nominal) in the case of pipe.
*   Nozzle thickness will be specified as actual thickness or schedule in the case of pipe.
*   The Flange column specification standard is ASME B16.5.
