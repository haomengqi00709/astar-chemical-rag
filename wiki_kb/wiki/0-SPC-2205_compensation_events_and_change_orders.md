# Engineering Subcontract Compensation Events and Change Orders (0-SPC-2205)

This page outlines the types of compensation events, the notification process, assessment criteria, and the implementation of changes through Change Orders under document 0-SPC-2205. This process is critical for managing unforeseen circumstances and scope adjustments in the engineering subcontract. For a broader context on engineering subcontract compensation, refer to [[0-SPC-2202-1_engineering_subcontract_compensation_events_and_change_orders]].

**Types of Compensation Events:**
The following events are classified as compensation events:
*   An instruction from Aker Kvaerner Chemetics that changes the [[0-SPC-2205_general_conditions_and_administration#Key Definitions|Scope]].
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics failing to provide something by the date it is reasonably required, provided the Subcontractor has advised Aker Kvaerner Chemetics of that date.
*   An instruction from Aker Kvaerner Chemetics to resolve an ambiguity or inconsistency in the Subcontract conditions or [[0-SPC-2205_general_conditions_and_administration#Key Definitions|Scope]], where Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ interpretation could not have been reasonably anticipated by the Subcontractor.
*   A breach of contract by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics that is not otherwise defined as a compensation event in this Subcontract.
*   Services being affected by:
    *   War, civil war, rebellion, revolution, insurrection, military, or usurped power.
    *   Strikes, riots, and civil commotion not confined to the employees of the Subcontractor and [[0-SPC-2205_general_conditions_and_administration#Key Definitions|Subsubcontractors]].
    *   Ionizing radiation or radioactive contamination from nuclear fuel or waste resulting from nuclear fuel combustion.
    *   Radioactive, toxic, explosive, or other hazardous properties of an explosive nuclear device.
    *   Natural disaster.
    *   Fire and explosion.
    *   Impact by aircraft or other aerial device, or anything dropped from them.

**Notification of Compensation Events:**
*   The Subcontractor **must** notify Aker Kvaerner Chemetics of a compensation event within five (5) days of becoming aware of the event.
*   The Subcontractor **must** notify Aker Kvaerner Chemetics of a compensation event *before* undertaking any work in response to that event.

**Impact on Price and Completion Date:**
The Price and [[0-SPC-2205_general_conditions_and_administration#Key Definitions|Completion Date]] are **not** changed under the following circumstances:
*   If the compensation event is described in Clause 2.1 (5) (war, civil war, etc.).
*   If the compensation event arises from a fault of the Subcontractor.
*   If the compensation event has not occurred and is not expected to occur.
*   If the compensation event has no effect on the Subcontractor’s costs or planned [[0-SPC-2205_general_conditions_and_administration#Key Definitions|Completion]].
*   If the compensation event was notified to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics later than required by the Subcontract.

**Claim Submission and Assessment:**
*   The Subcontractor **must** submit a claim for proposed changes to the Price and [[0-SPC-2205_general_conditions_and_administration#Key Definitions|Completion Date]] within one (1) week of being instructed to do so by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **must** reply within one (1) week of receiving the claim submission.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' reply may include: an instruction to submit a revised claim, an acceptance of the claim, a notification not to proceed with a proposed change, or a notification that Aker Kvaerner Chemetics will conduct its own assessment.
*   The cost of preparing claims for compensation events **must** be borne by the Subcontractor.
*   Changes to the Prices are assessed based on the effect of the compensation event on the change in the cost of the work required to Provide the Services, using the Price as a basis.
*   A delay to the [[0-SPC-2205_general_conditions_and_administration#Key Definitions|Completion Date]] is assessed as the duration by which [[0-SPC-2205_general_conditions_and_administration#Key Definitions|Completion]] is delayed due to the compensation event.
*   Assessments are based on the assumption that the Subcontractor reacts competently and promptly to the compensation event, and that additional costs and time are reasonably incurred.
*   The cost of events for which this Subcontract requires the Subcontractor to insure, and other costs paid to the Subcontractor by insurers, **must** be deducted from the assessment of compensation events.

**Implementation of Compensation Events:**
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics implements each compensation event by notifying the Subcontractor of the claim's acceptance or of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ own assessment.
*   Alternatively, Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may authorize the Subcontractor to implement the compensation event prior to a claim being accepted or an assessment being made.
*   A compensation event **must** be implemented in the form of a Change Order.
*   The Change Order **must** be signed on behalf of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics by the same person who executed the Agreement.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **must** include the changes to the Prices and the [[0-SPC-2205_general_conditions_and_administration#Key Definitions|Completion Date]] in its notice implementing a compensation event.
