# Project Change Implementation

The Project Change Implementation phase is the stage in the [[0-PRC-4003-R1_project_change_management_flow_diagram|Project Change Management Flow Diagram]] where approved changes are formally integrated into the project. This phase involves executing the necessary actions, updating project documentation, and adjusting project control systems to reflect the modifications.

Upon receiving all required approvals, the following actions **must** be taken:

*   A [[formal_change_order|formal change order]] is issued. This document legally formalizes the approved change, providing official authorization for its execution.
*   The [[project_scope_adjustment|project scope]] is adjusted to incorporate the modifications. This ensures that all project activities, deliverables, and objectives are aligned with the new requirements.
*   The [[contract_adjustment|contract]] is adjusted as necessary to reflect the revised scope, schedule, and cost implications of the change.
*   The [[change_management_roles_and_responsibilities_0_prc_4003|Project Team]] takes action and implements the approved change, carrying out the necessary design, procurement, construction, or other project activities.

To maintain accurate project records and control, the [[change_management_roles_and_responsibilities_0_prc_4003|Project Control Specialist]] performs critical updates:
*   Changes are incorporated into the project's cost system, ensuring that the budget reflects the revised financial plan.
*   Changes are incorporated into the project's schedule system, updating timelines and milestones to account for the modifications.
*   Changes are incorporated into the project's progress system, allowing for accurate tracking and reporting of project advancement against the new baseline.
*   The [[change_log]] is updated to record the details of the implemented change, including its status, date of approval, and impact.

The [[change_management_roles_and_responsibilities_0_prc_4003|Project Manager]] **must** regularly review the change log with the project team to monitor pending changes and ensure that all approved modifications are being effectively managed and integrated.

For detailed procedures on managing the change log, refer to [[0-PRC-4003-R1_pcn_log_procedure]] and [[pvn_pcn_log_and_documentation_management]]. These procedures ensure that all changes are meticulously tracked from initiation through implementation, providing a clear audit trail and supporting effective project control.

This phase involves executing the necessary actions, updating project documentation, and formally adjusting the project scope, schedule, and budget as per the approved change.
During this phase, the Project Control Specialist incorporates the approved changes into the project's cost, schedule, and progress systems to ensure accurate tracking and reporting.
