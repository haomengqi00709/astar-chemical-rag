# Flow Measurement - General

Various technologies are employed for flow measurement, selected based on the fluid properties, accuracy requirements, and process conditions.

**Preferred and Common Flow Meter Types:**
*   **Magnetic Flow Meters:** Preferred for liquid applications. For liquids with suspended solids, an ideal velocity through the flow tube of 1.5 to 3 meters per second is maintained to prevent sedimentation of solids and minimize wear on the liner. Specific requirements for magnetic flow meters are detailed in [[flow_measurement_magnetic_and_vortex|Flow Measurement - Magnetic and Vortex]].
*   **Vortex Shedding Meters:** May be used for clean or low conductivity liquids and steam. Also suitable for gas flow measurement. Specific considerations for vortex meters are in [[flow_measurement_magnetic_and_vortex|Flow Measurement - Magnetic and Vortex]].
*   **Averaging Pitot Tubes:** May be used as the primary measuring element on large diameter clean liquid pipelines and for gas flows.
*   **Orifice Plates:** May be used as the primary measuring element on large diameter clean liquid pipelines and for gas flows. Detailed specifications are in [[flow_measurement_differential_pressure|Flow Measurement - Differential Pressure]].
*   **Positive Displacement Meters:** Used where greater accuracy or rangeability is required than that obtainable with orifice or vortex flowmeters.
*   **Turbine Meters:** Used where greater accuracy or rangeability is required than that obtainable with orifice or vortex flowmeters.
*   **Coriolis Flowmeters:** Used for mass flow measurement of fluids. Also used for custody transfer metering. Specifics are in [[flow_measurement_other_types|Flow Measurement - Other Types]].
*   **Thermal Dispersion (TD) Mass Flow Measurement:** May be applied for clean gas flows. Details in [[flow_measurement_other_types|Flow Measurement - Other Types]].
*   **Flow Nozzles or Venturis:** May be used where high capacity or high-pressure recovery is required.

**Custody Transfer Metering:**
Custody transfer metering **will** be accomplished using positive displacement meters or Coriolis flowmeters on the process fluid to ensure high accuracy for commercial transactions.

**Installation Guidelines:**
*   Instruments for clean gases **will** be flange-mounted, with the flange marked to indicate the correct orientation of the device.
*   When flow is controlled by a variable speed pump, the flowmeter **is to be** installed after and as close as practical to the pump.
*   When flow is controlled by a control valve, the flowmeter **is to be** installed upstream to and as close as practical to the valve.
