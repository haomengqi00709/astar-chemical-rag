# Subcontract Quality Plan Management

Engineering Subcontractors **must** prepare and submit a comprehensive Subcontract Quality Plan for the services they provide. This plan is a foundational document for ensuring quality throughout the subcontracted work.

**Submission Requirements**
The Subcontractor **shall** submit the Subcontract Quality Plan along with copies of all referenced documents. These documents are integral to understanding the subcontractor's quality management approach.

**Document Control**
Upon submission, the Subcontract Quality Plan and its referenced documents **will** be logged and published in accordance with [[0-PRC-0006_control_of_project_documents]]. This ensures proper record-keeping and accessibility within the project's document control system.

**Review and Approval Process**
The submitted Subcontract Quality Plan and all referenced documents **will** undergo a thorough review by the Discipline Lead responsible for the specific work and the [[project_engineer_pem_gate_review_responsibilities]].

*   **Mark-up and Feedback:** Any required changes or revisions **will** be marked up directly on Adobe Portable Document Format (PDF) versions of the documents within the project's electronic file. These marked-up documents **will** then be returned to the Subcontractor for revision.
*   **Approval Status:** Once approved, the Adobe PDF versions of the documents **will** be officially marked as "Approved for Design."
*   **Digital Signatures:** Approved documents **shall** have [[applying_digital_signatures]] attached. The Discipline Lead **will** attach their digital signature as the Checker, and the Project Engineer **will** attach their digital signature as the Approver. These digital signatures signify formal acceptance of the plan and its contents.

**Modifications to the Plan**
Any subsequent modifications to the Subcontract Quality Plan or its referenced documents **are subject** to the same rigorous checking and approval process as described above. This ensures that any changes maintain the required quality standards and are formally documented.
