# Cooling Tower Vendor Information Requirements

The Template Cooling Tower Datasheet (Document ID: [[4-pds-xxxx-r1_template_cooling_tower_datasheet_administration|4-PDS-XXXX-R1]]) includes mandatory requirements for information that vendors **must** provide. These details are essential for a comprehensive technical and commercial evaluation, enabling accurate system integration, operational planning, and cost analysis. Adherence to these requirements is a key aspect of [[vendor_quality_assurance]] and [[vendor_document_requirements]].

**Mandatory Vendor Information:**
The vendor **must** advise the following information:

*   **Normal and maximum drift rate as a % of the water circulation rate:** This specifies the amount of water lost from the tower as fine droplets carried out by the airflow. It's crucial for environmental compliance and water balance calculations.
*   **Normal and maximum rate of evaporation as a % of the water circulation rate:** This indicates the primary mechanism of heat rejection and is vital for calculating water consumption and make-up requirements.
*   **Expected windage losses as a % of the water circulation rate:** This refers to water losses due to wind blowing water out of the tower, distinct from drift. Important for overall water balance.
*   **Minimum practical cooling water outlet temperature, assuming low winter wet bulb temperature:** This provides insight into the tower's performance under cold ambient conditions, critical for preventing freezing and optimizing winter operation.
*   **Required pressure at water inlet nozzle:** This specifies the minimum pressure needed at the tower's water inlet for proper distribution and operation, impacting pump sizing and hydraulic design.
*   **BHP requirement per fan:** The Brake Horsepower (BHP) required for each fan motor, essential for electrical load calculations and energy consumption estimates.
*   **Supplied fan motor rating:** The actual electrical power rating of the motor supplied with each fan, typically in kW or HP.
*   **Required blowdown rate:** The rate at which a portion of the circulating water is discharged to control the concentration of dissolved solids. This is critical for water treatment and make-up calculations.
*   **Required water make-up rate:** The total rate at which fresh water must be added to the system to compensate for losses due to evaporation, drift, windage, and blowdown.

These detailed performance and operational data points are fundamental for the project's [[procurement_and_vendor_documentation|procurement process]] and for ensuring the selected cooling tower meets all specified design and operational criteria.
