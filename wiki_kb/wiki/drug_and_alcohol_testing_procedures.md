# Drug and Alcohol Testing Procedures

Chemetics may require an Employee to undergo drug or alcohol testing in specific situations to ensure a safe and unimpaired workforce. These procedures are conducted in accordance with Clause 6.2 of the original policy document.

### Situations Requiring Testing
**Drug Testing may be required:**
*   **Post-incident or “near miss”**: Where there is [[substance_use_policy_scope_and_definitions#reasonable_cause|Reasonable Cause]] to believe that drug impairment contributed to the incident circumstances. This testing will only be part of a larger assessment and investigation.
*   **Impaired Work Performance**: Where there is [[substance_use_policy_scope_and_definitions#reasonable_cause|Reasonable Cause]] to believe that an Employee’s work performance is impaired by drugs. This testing will only be part of a larger assessment and investigation.
*   **Post-reinstatement**: Only as part of a larger assessment of the Employee.

**Alcohol Testing may be required:**
*   **Post-incident or “near miss”**: Where there is [[substance_use_policy_scope_and_definitions#reasonable_cause|Reasonable Cause]] to believe that alcohol impairment contributed to the incident circumstances.
*   **Impaired Work Performance**: Where there is [[substance_use_policy_scope_and_definitions#reasonable_cause|Reasonable Cause]] to believe that an Employee’s work performance is impaired by alcohol.
*   **Post-reinstatement**: Only as part of a larger assessment of the Employee.
*   **Random Testing**: For Employees in [[safety_sensitive_positions]], random alcohol testing may be required by a method which indicates impairment.

### Testing Process
All drug and alcohol testing will adhere to the following protocol:
*   Testing will be performed by a medical doctor.
*   The process will involve a physical examination.
*   Blood and/or urine samples will be taken for testing to support the medical observations.
*   Samples will be analyzed by an accredited laboratory, as directed by the examining medical doctor.
*   The examining medical doctor **SHALL** make a report to Chemetics on their findings.
*   The examining medical doctor **SHALL** express a professional opinion of the degree of impairment found.

Employees **SHALL** cooperate fully with investigations and **SHALL** ensure they do not ingest any substance or take any action that may affect the results of required substance testing.
