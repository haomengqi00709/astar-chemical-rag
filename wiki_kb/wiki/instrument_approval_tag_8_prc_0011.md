# Instrument Approval Tag (8-PRC-0011)

This document, 8-PRC-0011, outlines the procedure for the application and management of instrument approval tags within the Project. It details the process for formally approving and tagging instruments after successful inspection and testing.

Inspections and tests for [[mechanical_completion_testing_and_defect_management]] are conducted in accordance with this procedure, which culminates in the application of these approval tags. It includes associated checklists to record the completion of these critical checks.
