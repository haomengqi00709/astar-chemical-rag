# Mack Iron Strainer Data Table Units

This page specifies the units of measurement for the various columns in the Mack Iron Strainer Data table. This information is crucial for accurate interpretation and application of the data, which is sourced from the Mack Iron Catalogue and referenced in [[1-CAL-7842-R1_final_strainer_calculation_document_details]].

The units for the detailed column headers are as follows:

*   **Line Size:** inches | mm
*   **ID:** inches | mm
*   **A:** inches | mm
*   **B(ID):** inches | mm
*   **C (ID):** inches | mm
*   **D(ID):** inches | mm
*   **H (OD):** inches | mm
*   **Q:** inches | mm
*   **R (OD):** inches | mm
*   **O (OD):** inches | mm
*   **P:** inches | mm
*   **Base Filter Area:** m2
*   **Lateral Filter Area:** m2
*   **L/B Ratio:** (unitless ratio)
*   **Total Filter Area:** m2
*   **Pipe Area:** m2
*   **Ratio:** (unitless ratio)
*   **Flow:** (unitless flow factor, or specific flow unit depending on context)
*   **Annular Area:** (unitless area factor, or specific area unit depending on context)

Additional sub-headers and their units:
*   **THK:** inches | mm
*   **E:** (unitless)
*   **OD:** (unitless)
*   **D/H:** (unitless ratio)

For a description of the headers themselves, refer to [[mack_iron_strainer_data_table_headers]]. A summary of the data contained within this table can be found on the [[mack_iron_strainer_data_table_summary]] page.
