# Shell & Tube Heat Exchanger Quality Record Definitions (0-QDS-7417)

This page defines the terminology used for Quality Record requirements within the Shell & Tube Heat Exchanger Quality Data Sheet (0-QDS-7417). These definitions ensure clarity and consistency in the documentation and submission of quality records throughout the project lifecycle.

**Quality Record Designations:**

*   **AP = "As Prepared"**
    *   This designation indicates that the quality record is required to be submitted "As Prepared" by the Vendor. These documents are typically generated during the manufacturing or inspection process and are submitted in their original, unedited form as they become available. Examples might include internal inspection reports or raw data sheets.

*   **FP = "Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package"**
    *   This designation signifies that the quality record **must** be included as part of the final Quality Assurance Documentation Package. This package is a comprehensive compilation of all required quality records, certifications, and reports, submitted upon completion of the fabrication and inspection activities. These documents are crucial for demonstrating compliance with all specified requirements and for future reference during the operational life of the equipment.

These definitions are applied consistently across all inspection and testing items detailed in the [[0-QDS-7417_vendor_quality_assurance_requirements|Vendor Quality Assurance Requirements]] and subsequent sections of the 0-QDS-7417 document.
