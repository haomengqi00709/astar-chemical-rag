# Vendor Document Supply Specification (0-SPC-0001)

This specification, identified as Document ID **0-SPC-0001**, outlines the mandatory requirements for the supply of documentation by Vendors to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. It serves as a foundational document to ensure consistency, quality, and proper management of all vendor-supplied information throughout project execution.

The specification details various aspects of document management, including content standards, revision control, status definitions, identification, delivery formats, transmittal procedures, and the critical review and approval processes. Adherence to these guidelines is essential for seamless integration of vendor documentation into Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' project workflows.

Key areas covered by this specification include:
*   **[[vendor_document_requirements]]**: Details on the content and purpose of Vendor Document Requirements (VDRs).
*   **[[vendor_document_content_standards]]**: Requirements for language and units used in documentation.
*   **[[document_control_revisions]]**: Procedures for marking and managing document revisions.
*   **[[document_status_definitions]]**: Comprehensive definitions for all document statuses.
*   **[[vendor_document_identification_and_numbering]]**: Guidelines for document numbering and identification blocks.
*   **[[vendor_document_delivery_formats_and_quantities]]**: Specifications for document formats, sizes, storage media, and required quantities.
*   **[[outgoing_document_issuance_and_transmittals]]**: Requirements for document transmittals.
*   **[[vendor_document_delivery_addresses]]**: Designated contact information and methods for document submission.
*   **[[vendor_document_review_process]]**: Procedures for the review and approval of both "hold" and "non-hold" documents.

This specification is critical for all Vendors engaged with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics to ensure that all supplied documentation meets the required standards for design, fabrication, installation, operation, and maintenance.
