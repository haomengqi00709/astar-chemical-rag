# Sexual Harassment Definition

Sexual harassment is defined as any conduct, comment, gesture, or contact of a sexual nature that is likely to cause offense or humiliation to any employee or prospective employee. It might also be perceived by an employee as placing a condition of a sexual nature on employment, or on any opportunity for training or promotion.

Sexual harassment can be verbal or physical, deliberate, unsolicited, or unwelcome. It may consist of a single incident or a series of incidents, and can be directed towards males or females.

Examples of sexual harassment, which are not exhaustive, may include:
*   Verbal abuse or threats of a sexual nature.
*   Unwelcome remarks, jokes, innuendoes, or taunting about a person’s body or sex.
*   Displaying pornographic or other offensive or derogatory pictures of a sexual nature in the workplace.
*   Unwelcome invitations or requests, whether indirect or explicit.
*   Intimidation or any other comments that might be construed as a demand for sexual favors.
*   Unnecessary physical contact such as touching, patting, pinching, or punching.
*   Physical assault that is sexually related.
