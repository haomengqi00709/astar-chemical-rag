# Piping Documentation References

Kvaerner Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **will** provide the Piping Subcontractor with a detailed piping isometric for each line. Piping isometrics show overall dimensions, bill of materials, and pipe support references. Detailed piping isometrics may not be provided for some small diameter field run piping. Dimensional information shown on the piping isometric drawings is nominal.

Kvaerner Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **will** also provide piping general arrangements. Piping general arrangements are intended to show the general location of piping and supports only and may or may not be updated to reflect the final isometrics. Dimensional information shown on the piping isometrics **will** govern in cases of conflict with general arrangements.

Additionally, Kvaerner Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **will** provide piping and instrumentation drawings (P&ID's) and a piping line list. P&ID's and the piping line list include references for pipe material specifications, pressure test methods and pressure, insulation, and painting requirements.
