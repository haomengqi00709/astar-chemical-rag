# Carbon Steel Piping Pressure Testing (5-SPC-0122)

This page outlines the requirements for pressure testing of carbon steel piping, as specified in document 5-SPC-0122.

**Scope of Testing**
*   Unless specifically excluded, pressure testing of all pipe spools, except those which require field welding, **shall** be carried out by the fabricator.

**Testing Standard**
*   Pressure testing **shall** be in accordance with [[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping]], paragraph 345.4.
