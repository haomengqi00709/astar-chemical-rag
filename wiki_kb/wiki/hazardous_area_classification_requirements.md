# Hazardous Area Classification Requirements

The Hazardous Area Classification Requirements specification provides the methodology for determining and classifying hazardous areas within the plant. This is critical for ensuring the safe selection and installation of equipment in environments where flammable substances may be present.

**Determination of Hazardous Areas**
The specification **shall** explain how to determine the hazardous area classification. This classification depends on several factors:
*   The types of chemicals and gases present within equipment.
*   The frequency with which chemicals and gases may be present outside equipment.
*   Whether their presence occurs as part of normal operations or due to an accident.

Hazardous area classification applies to equipment such as vessels, pumps, valves, electrolyzer cells, and instrument connections.
