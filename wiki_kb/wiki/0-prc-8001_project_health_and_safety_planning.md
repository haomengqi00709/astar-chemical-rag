# Project Health and Safety Planning

This standard procedure (0-PRC-8001) details the responsibilities and processes for ensuring that Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) in a project meets the stringent standards set by Aker Kvaerner, Client HSE standards, and all applicable local occupancy health and safety laws.

**Key Responsibilities:**
This document outlines the specific roles and duties for project personnel to integrate HSE considerations from the initial planning stages through to project completion. It serves as a foundational reference for HSE management on all projects.

**Document Ownership and Approval:**
This standard is owned by the Company President. It **must** be checked by the HSE Manager and **must** be approved by the Company President.

This planning document is crucial for the effective HSE management at both [[hse_construction_sites]] and [[hse_commissioning_sites]], providing the framework for project-specific HSE plans. For the overall HSE document ownership and approval hierarchy, refer to [[hse_document_ownership_and_approval]].
