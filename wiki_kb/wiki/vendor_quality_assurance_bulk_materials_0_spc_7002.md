# Vendor Quality Assurance (Bulk Materials) (0-SPC-7002)

This document, 0-SPC-7002, specifies the quality assurance requirements for vendors supplying bulk materials to the Project. It details the mandatory quality control measures, inspection points, and documentation required for bulk material procurement.

Vendors are required to comply with this specification to ensure that all supplied bulk materials meet the required quality and specification standards of the Project. This includes requirements for material certification, testing reports, and surveillance by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
