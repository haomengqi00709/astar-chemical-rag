# IEC International Standard # 79–10, 1995

The [[9-prc-0006_electrical_hazardous_area_classification_procedure|Electrical Hazardous Area Classification Procedure (9-PRC-0006)]] bases its recommendations on the **IEC International Electrotechnical Commission, International Standard # 79–10, 1995**. This standard is a foundational document for classifying hazardous areas in industrial plants.

**Scope of IEC 79-10:**
Part 79 of IEC # 79–10, 1995, is specifically concerned with the classification of hazardous areas where flammable gas or vapours may exist. It provides the framework and methodology for identifying, defining, and delineating such areas to ensure the safe application of electrical equipment.

**Key Concepts and References:**
*   **Definitions:** All definitions related to hazardous and non-hazardous areas, as well as the classification into [[hazardous_area_classification_definitions_iec_79_10|Zone 0, Zone 1, and Zone 2]], are as per the IEC publication # 79–10.
*   **Classification of Hazardous Areas:** For the classification of hazardous areas (for gases and vapours), refer to IEC publication # 79–10, clause # 3, titled, ‘Safety and area classification’.
*   **Extent of Hazardous Areas:** For determining the extent of hazardous areas, refer to IEC publication # 79–10, clause # 4, titled, ‘Area classification procedure’. The extent of classified locations may be defined by applying, with sound engineering judgement, the distances recommended in the diagrams shown in this publication.

The principles outlined in IEC 79-10 are critical for preventing ignition of explosive mixtures by guiding the selection and installation of electrical equipment in potentially hazardous environments.
