# FRP Tank Coating and Lining Inspection (0-QDS-7414)

This page outlines the specific requirements for coating and lining inspections for FRP Tanks, as mandated by Quality Data Sheet **0-QDS-7414**. These inspections are vital for ensuring the protective layers of the tank meet design specifications and provide adequate corrosion resistance.

**Coating/Lining Inspection:**
*   **Coating/Lining Inspection** is a mandatory requirement and **must** be included in the Vendor's Inspection and Test (I & T) Plan. This ensures that all protective coatings and linings are applied and inspected according to specified procedures.

**External Coating/Lining Inspection:**
*   **External Coating/Lining Inspection** requires a Quality Record **FP** (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package). The inspection results **must** be compiled and submitted as part of the final Quality Assurance Documentation Package.
*   **External Coating/Lining Inspection** requires **Chemetics Witness** surveillance. A Chemetics representative **must** be present to observe the external coating/lining inspection.
*   **External Coating/Lining Inspection** requires **Chemetics Hold** surveillance. Work cannot proceed beyond this point until Chemetics has reviewed and approved the external coating/lining inspection results.

**Internal Coating/Lining Inspection:**
*   **Internal Coating/Lining Inspection** requires a Quality Record **FP** (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package). The inspection results **must** be compiled and submitted as part of the final Quality Assurance Documentation Package.
*   **Internal Coating/Lining Inspection** requires **Chemetics Witness** surveillance. A Chemetics representative **must** be present to observe the internal coating/lining inspection.
*   **Internal Coating/Lining Inspection** requires **Chemetics Hold** surveillance. Work cannot proceed beyond this point until Chemetics has reviewed and approved the internal coating/lining inspection results.

These detailed inspection requirements ensure that both the internal and external protective layers of the FRP tanks are applied correctly and meet the necessary quality standards for long-term performance and safety.
