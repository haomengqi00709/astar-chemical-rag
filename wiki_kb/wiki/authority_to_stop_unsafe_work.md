# Authority to Stop Unsafe Work

This policy outlines the critical authority and responsibility of all individuals to intervene and stop unsafe work practices immediately, ensuring a proactive approach to workplace safety.

**Individual Authority and Obligation**
Every individual within the organization possesses the inherent authority to halt any work deemed unsafe. This is a fundamental aspect of our [[safe_work_procedures|safe work procedures]] and [[hse_audits_and_inspections_overview|HSE management system]].
*   Individuals have the authority to stop unsafe work immediately. This empowers all personnel to act decisively when faced with potential hazards.
*   Individuals **shall** use their judgment to stop unsafe practices. This emphasizes personal responsibility and the application of safety knowledge in real-time situations.

**Mandatory Action for Unsafe Conditions**
The immediate cessation of unsafe activities is a mandatory requirement to prevent incidents and protect personnel.
*   Unsafe work **shall** be stopped immediately upon identification. There is no requirement for prior approval or consultation when an immediate hazard is present.
*   Unsafe practices **shall** be stopped. This applies to any method or action that deviates from established [[task_safety_awareness_overview|task safety awareness]] or [[0-PRC-8007_job_hazard_analysis|Job Hazard Analysis]] protocols and poses a risk.

This policy reinforces the company's commitment to safety, placing the power and responsibility to prevent harm directly with its workforce.
