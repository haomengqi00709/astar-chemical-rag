# Titanium Welding Specification Overview (4-SPC-0067)

This specification, 4-SPC-0067, defines Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ mandatory requirements for welding and associated procedures specifically for vessels and equipment constructed from titanium.

**Scope and Material Classifications**
The specification covers titanium materials classified under [[ASME_bpvc_section_ii_materials]] P-No. 51 and [[ASME_bpvc_section_ii_materials]] P-No. 52.

Specifically, it applies to titanium with the following UNS numbers:
*   R50250 (Grade 1)
*   R50400 (Grade 2)
*   R52400 (Grade 7)
*   R52402 (Grade 16)
*   R53400 (Grade 12)

For general design and fabrication codes and standards, refer to [[4-SPC-0040_metallic_process_vessels_fabrication_overview]].

**Fabrication Environment**
All titanium welding **must** be carried out in a clean, segregated area within the facility to prevent contamination.

**Jurisdictional Modifications**
This standard **shall** be modified as required when a project necessitates compliance with codes from other jurisdictions (e.g., Australia, Europe).
