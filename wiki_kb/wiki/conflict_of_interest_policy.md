# Conflict of Interest Policy

This policy addresses situations where an employee's personal interests may conflict with the interests of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Avoiding Conflicts of Interest**
Employees are expected not to put themselves in a position in which their personal interests and those of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics might conflict. A conflict of interest exists for employees who use their position at Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or business information of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for personal gain or benefit to themselves, friends, or families, whether directly or indirectly.

Employees **must** avoid any situation in which there is, or appears to be, a potential conflict that could interfere with the employee’s judgment in making decisions in Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ best interests.

**Disclosure Requirements**
Employees **must** disclose all circumstances that constitute an actual or potential conflict of interest to the appropriate Senior Manager or Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ Legal Counsel.

**Outside Employment**
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics employees may hold outside jobs or engage in modest self-employment activities in their own time, using their own resources, provided the performance of their duties for Aker Kvaerner Chemetics is not adversely affected. However, employees **should not** become involved in any activity that would affect their objectivity when carrying out their normal duties.

*   A conflict of interest exists for employees who use their position at Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or business information of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for personal gain or benefit to themselves, friends or families, whether directly or indirectly.
*   Employees must avoid any situation in which there is, or appears to be, potential conflict which could interfere with the employee’s judgement in making decisions in Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ best interests.

*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics employees may hold outside jobs or engage in modest self-employment activities in their own time, using their own resources, provided the performance of their duties for Aker Kvaerner Chemetics is not adversely affected.
*   Employees should not become involved in any activity that would affect their objectivity when carrying out their normal duties.
