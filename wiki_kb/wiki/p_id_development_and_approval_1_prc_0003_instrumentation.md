# P&ID Development and Approval - Instrumentation Context

[[p_id_development_and_approval_1_prc_0003]] is the overarching procedure for the development and approval of Piping and Instrumentation Diagrams (P&IDs). Within the context of instrumentation, P&IDs are critical for defining the process and control requirements.

**Instrument Tagging on P&IDs:**
The Process department will assign standard Instrument Tag/Loop numbers on the Stage I through to Stage III P&ID’s. This initial tagging is fundamental for the subsequent development of the [[8-lst-0002_instrument_index]] and other instrumentation documents.

**Client Instrument Tag Numbers:**
When required by contract, the Field Instrument Engineer (FIE) will be responsible for the assignment of the client's Instrument Tag/Loop numbers on the Instrument Index. The FIE will cross-reference client Instrument Tag/Loop numbers to standard numbers as per Stage III P&ID’s. The Design Office will be responsible for updating the P&ID’s with the client Instrument Tag Numbers.

**Accuracy and Related Documents:**
The accuracy of the preliminary [[8-lst-01xx_instrument_process_data_lists]] is directly dependent on the accuracy of the P&ID’s. Process Data Lists **must** relate to the current issue of the P&IDs.
