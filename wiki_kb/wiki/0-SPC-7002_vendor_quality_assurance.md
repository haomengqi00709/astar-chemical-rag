# Vendor Quality Assurance

This document, 0-SPC-7002, outlines the general requirements for Vendor Quality Assurance (QA). It serves as a foundational specification for vendors to develop and implement their Quality Plans, ensuring compliance with project standards and client expectations.

Vendors **must** prepare their Quality Plan in accordance with the provisions detailed in this specification. The Vendor's overall Quality Assurance program **must** comply with the requirements of this document, as well as any project-specific Quality Data Sheets (QDS) that may apply.

For specific applications, such as bulk materials or equipment, additional or more detailed QA requirements may be specified. For example, [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment]] addresses QA for equipment, while [[0-SPC-7002_vendor_quality_assurance_bulk_materials]] provides specific guidelines for bulk materials.

Adherence to this specification is critical for maintaining the overall quality and integrity of supplied goods and services.
