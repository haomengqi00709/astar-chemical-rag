# Gas Rotating Equipment Technical Specifications

This page compiles the technical specifications for gas rotating equipment, as outlined in the 4-PDS-XXXX-R1 "Template Gas Rotating Equipment — Datasheet". It covers various aspects including equipment types, general operating data, ancillary components, flow control methods, and materials of construction. These details are essential for the selection, procurement, and installation of rotating machinery.

**Equipment Types**
The datasheet allows for the specification of various types of gas rotating equipment, including:
*   Centrifugal Radial
*   Centrifugal Axial
*   Lobe
*   Liquid Ring [[liquid_ring_compressor_design_and_process_parameters]]
*   Screw
*   Sliding Vane
*   Reciprocating
*   Other (for equipment not explicitly listed)

**General Equipment Data**
Key operational and design parameters for the equipment include:
*   **Quantity:** Can be specified as Operating or Installed Spare.
*   **Number of Stages:** An equipment data parameter.
*   **Operation:** Can be Continuous or Intermittent.
*   **Seal Type:** Options include Stuffing Box, Single mechanical, Double mechanical, Sealless, or Other.
*   **Seal Fluid:** An equipment data parameter.
*   **Liquid Ring Fluid:** Specific parameters for liquid ring type equipment:
    *   Pressure, in kPa(g)
    *   Temperature, in oC
    *   Vapour Pressure, in kPa(a)

**Ancillary Equipment**
The datasheet provides for the inclusion of various ancillary components that may be required with the main rotating equipment:
*   Separator
*   Mist Eliminator [[mist_eliminator_candle_material_selection_4_dst_xxxx_r0]]
*   Cooler
*   Suction Pot
*   Intercooler
*   Receiver
*   Filter [[filter_field_installation_and_inspection_requirements]]
*   Silencer
*   Inlet Vanes
*   Air Dryer
*   Prefilter
*   After Filter
*   Others (for additional components)

**Method of Controlling Flow**
The datasheet specifies the method used for controlling the flow of gas through the equipment:
*   Control Valve [[control_valves_general]]
*   VSD (Variable Speed Drive)
*   Other (for alternative control methods)

**Materials of Construction**
Detailed material specifications are required for various components of the rotating equipment:
*   Casing
*   Impeller
*   Cone
*   Seal
*   Shaft
*   Screw
*   Rotor
*   Idler
*   Piston
*   Vanes
*   Wetted Parts
*   Separator
*   Cooler
*   Other (for additional components)

These specifications are critical for ensuring compatibility with process fluids, operating conditions, and overall equipment longevity. For broader information on equipment specifications, refer to [[equipment_data_sheets_4_dst_xxxx]] and [[general_equipment_requirements_specification]]. General mechanical design requirements are detailed in [[general_mechanical_design_requirements_0_spc_0010]], and overall material selection guidance can be found in [[material_selection]].
