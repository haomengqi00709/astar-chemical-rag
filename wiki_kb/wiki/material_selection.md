# Material Selection for Equipment

As per document 4-SPC-0001, the selection of materials for equipment **must** meet stringent quality and compatibility criteria.

**General Material Quality**
All materials **will be** new and of merchantable quality, ensuring high standards and reliability.

**Environmental Compatibility**
The Vendor **shall consider** both internal and external environments, fugitive emissions, and potential plant fluid spills when selecting materials and protective coatings. The components of all equipment that **is not** permanently sealed from the atmosphere **will be reviewed** and selected for compatibility with the environment, including all fugitive emissions. This comprehensive approach minimizes corrosion, degradation, and environmental impact.

**Prohibited and Restricted Materials**
*   Aluminium alloys **will not be used** without the explicit approval of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, except for standard items such as panels and instrument housings. Standard aluminium alloy items with a protective coating **will be permitted**.
*   Asbestos containing materials **are prohibited** in all equipment.
*   Galvanic action via contact of dissimilar metals **will be prevented** through careful material selection and design, avoiding accelerated corrosion.

These requirements ensure the long-term integrity, safety, and environmental performance of all equipment.
