# Inspection and Documentation Requirements for 0-QDS-7802

This page details the various inspection activities and documentation requirements for items covered by Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7802. These requirements ensure that all supplied instruments meet specified quality and safety standards.

**Key Items and Activities:**
The following are specific items or activities for which inspection and documentation are required:
*   **Hazardous Area Certificate:** This certificate is a required item or activity, confirming compliance with hazardous area classifications.
*   **O2 Cleaning Certificate:** This certificate is a required item or activity, verifying that equipment has undergone oxygen cleaning procedures.
*   **H2O2 Cleaning Certificate:** This certificate is a required item or activity, verifying that equipment has undergone hydrogen peroxide cleaning procedures.
*   **Certificate of Compliance:** This is a critical item or activity, further detailed under [[0-QDS-7802_quality_record_definitions]].
*   **Calibration Certificate:** This is a critical item or activity, further detailed under [[0-QDS-7802_quality_record_definitions]].
*   **Packaging:** This activity requires Chemetics surveillance and specific documentation, as outlined in [[0-QDS-7802_chemetics_surveillance_level]].
*   **Packing List:** This document is a required item or activity, further detailed under [[0-QDS-7802_quality_record_definitions]].
*   **Item 6.0:** This is an item or activity number with no further specified details in the source document.
*   **Items 9.0 through 38.0:** These are item or activity numbers with no further specified details in the source document.

**Documentation and Reporting:**
Vendors **shall** adhere to the following reporting and documentation standards:
*   **"Vendor will issue reports as prepared":** This column header indicates that vendors are responsible for issuing reports as they are completed.
*   **"Required in Vendor I & T Plan":** This column header specifies items that **must** be included in the Vendor's Inspection and Test Plan.
*   **"I & T Plan":** This column header refers to the Inspection and Test Plan itself, which outlines the sequence and details of inspections and tests.
*   **"Initials / Date":** This column header is for recording the initials and date of personnel who have completed or verified specific activities.

For definitions of quality record statuses (AP, FP), refer to [[0-QDS-7802_quality_record_definitions]]. Vendor quality assurance requirements are detailed in [[0-QDS-7802_vendor_quality_assurance_requirements]].

The following items and activities have specific requirements:
*   **Certificate of Compliance:**
    *   Required as an **FP** (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package) Quality Record.
    *   Surveillance by Chemetics requires completion for this item.
*   **Calibration Certificate:**
    *   Required.
    *   Surveillance by Chemetics requires completion for this item.
*   **Hazardous Area Certificate:** This is a required item or activity.
*   **O2 Cleaning Certificate:** This is a required item or activity.
*   **H2O2 Cleaning Certificate:** This is a required item or activity.
*   **Packaging:** Surveillance by Chemetics requires completion for this activity.
*   **Packing List:**
    *   Required as an **AP** (As Prepared) Quality Record.
    *   Surveillance by Chemetics requires completion for this item.

**Additional Items/Activities (Details Not Specified):**
The document also lists the following items or activities without further specific details in this context:
*   Item 6.0
*   Items 9.0 through 38.0
