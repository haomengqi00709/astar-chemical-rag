# Subcontracting and Site Management

**Modularization Subcontracts:**
To minimize Industrial Relations impact on site, unionized Australasian subcontractors will perform the modularization work. Separate modularization subcontracts will be issued for:
*   Structural steel fabrication.
*   Mechanical work (including equipment setting, pipe spooling and installation, and instrument installation).
*   Electrical work.

The work of the Electrical Modularisation Subcontractor will be performed at the Mechanical Modularisation Subcontractor’s yard and will be coordinated by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Site Subcontracts:**
Multiple site subcontracts will be let to specialized contractors for various on-site activities. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics will maintain overall coordination of these site subcontracts. Site subcontracts will cover:
*   Civil works, including buildings.
*   Mechanical erection.
*   Electrical erection.

The detailed work distribution among all subcontracts is shown on Data available in SQL: 0-LST-2002.

**Site Management Teams:**
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics will maintain dedicated Site Management teams at both the modularizer’s yard(s) and at the project site. The assignment of the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site management team, along with their specific roles and responsibilities, will be described in [[site_management_0_prc_6007]] (0-PRC-6007: Site Management).
