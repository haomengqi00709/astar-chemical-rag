# Converter Fabrication Specification Overview (4-SPC-7540)

Specification 4-SPC-7540 outlines Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' comprehensive requirements for the shop fabrication of converter components. This document serves as a critical reference for vendors, ensuring adherence to specific manufacturing and quality standards.

This document (4-SPC-7540) is a new specification. Its content has been compiled and extracted from two foundational documents:
*   ES44\F06: [[4-SPC-0040_metallic_process_vessels_fabrication_overview]]
*   ES44\W01: [[4-SPC-0061_austenitic_stainless_steel_welding_standard_overview]]

The specification details various aspects of converter fabrication, including the vendor's scope of supply, items provided by other parties, specific fabrication tolerances, and detailed requirements for shop fabrication and assembly processes. Adherence to these requirements is mandatory for all fabrication activities related to converter components.
