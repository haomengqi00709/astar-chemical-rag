# HSE Management at Burnaby Laboratory

Responsibility for Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) at the Burnaby Laboratory (Section 10 of 0-PRC-8000), also referred to as Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Technology Centre, is assigned to the Laboratory Supervisor.

**Laboratory Joint Health and Safety Committee (JHSC):**
A Laboratory Joint Health and Safety Committee (JHSC) **will** be formed in accordance with the OHS regulations of British Columbia. The JHSC **will** have equal representation from management and non-management employees, with non-management members elected by their peers. The management representative **will** be, at minimum, the Laboratory Supervisor.

The Laboratory JHSC **will** meet monthly to review HSE performance within the Division. An Action List, including updated statuses of previous actions and new actions with assigned responsibilities, **will** be maintained and posted on the Company data network for employee access.

**Workplace Environment and Risk Minimization:**
The Technology Centre **will** provide a workplace environment designed to minimize any deleterious impact on employee health. This includes:
*   Premises that **must** comply with B.C.’s codes and regulations for heating, ventilation, air conditioning, and sanitary facilities.
*   A smoke-free working environment that **must** be provided.
*   Appropriate levels of ambient and task lighting that **must** be provided.
*   Appropriate means for food storage (e.g., refrigerators) that **must** be provided.
*   Office and laboratory furniture designed to minimize repetitive strain injury that **must** be provided.
*   Protective clothing and equipment that **must** be provided when working in pilot areas or adjacent labs.
*   Promotion of employee health and fitness initiatives that **must** occur.

To minimize risk to employees working at the Burnaby Laboratory:
*   Health and safety guidelines of the Workers Compensation Board of B.C. **must** be followed.
*   All rules and internal safety procedures highlighted in the Center’s Safety Manual **must** be implemented and enforced.
*   Appropriate instruction and training in the proper handling and usage of laboratory chemicals and equipment **must** be provided.
*   First Aid facilities **must** be maintained.
*   All employees **must** hold valid First Aid Certificates.
*   An effective laboratory emergency response plan **must** be maintained and tested on a regular basis.
*   An effective Workplace Hazardous Materials Information System (WHMIS) program **must** be maintained, with its database updated annually.
*   Risk assessments or process audits **must** be performed for all new experimental setups.
*   Safety inspections/audits of safety equipment and operations **must** be performed on a minimum monthly basis.
*   Employee awareness of ergonomic factors **must** be maintained.
*   Employees **must** be engaged in reporting near-misses and risk observations.
*   Proactive actions **must** be taken to eliminate unsafe conditions.
*   Proactive actions **must** be taken to reduce clutter and accumulation of unnecessary materials.

**Hazardous Chemical and Waste Management:**
*   Hazardous chemicals **will** be stored in compliance with Workers Compensation Board WHMIS guidelines and local municipal guidelines.
*   Waste chemical discharges **will** be monitored and **must** fall within local municipal discharge limits.
*   Waste chemicals that cannot be discharged safely **will** be safely stored and disposed periodically using an approved chemical disposal services provider.
*   All accidental releases or spills **will** be controlled immediately and cleaned up by personnel knowledgeable of the hazards involved.
*   Spill control and clean-up **must** be done in accordance with all applicable regulations, including WCB and/or the “Spill Reporting Regulation” of the Waste Management Act, if applicable.

**Environmental Care:**
The Technology Centre **will** take a leading role in ensuring the principles of environmental care embodied in the slogan "Reduce Recycle Reuse" are implemented and adhered to in a responsible manner.
