# Construction Subcontract Supply & Erection Scope

The Construction Subcontract Supply & Erection Scope document defines the responsibilities for material supply and installation within a construction subcontract. This document is crucial for clarifying roles and preventing overlaps or gaps in project execution.

**Purpose and Content**
The construction subcontract supply & erection scope document is a list showing material supply and installation responsibilities. It **will** indicate equipment purchased by the engineering firm and materials supplied by a contractor.

This document requires input from all disciplines (e.g., Electrical, Mechanical, Civil, Structural) to list equipment and materials, supply responsibility, and installation responsibility.
