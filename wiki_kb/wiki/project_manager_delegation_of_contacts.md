# Project Manager Delegation of Contacts (0-PRC-2004)

Within the framework of [[project_coordination_0_prc_2004]], Project Managers are authorized to delegate specific contacts for various project activities, as detailed in Section 3.6 of the procedure. This delegation is crucial for efficient project management and specialized oversight.

**Delegation for Technical Issues:**
Project Managers may delegate contacts for specific technical issues. Any technical contact delegated for specific technical issues **must** be capable of recognizing the commercial impact of technical decisions. This requirement ensures that technical choices are not only sound from an engineering perspective but also align with the project's financial objectives and overall commercial strategy.

**Delegation for Construction and Commissioning:**
Project Managers are also permitted to delegate contacts for construction and commissioning activities. This allows for dedicated personnel to manage these critical phases, ensuring smooth execution and adherence to project timelines and quality standards.

**EPC Scope Site Contact:**
For projects executed under an Engineering, Procurement, and Construction (EPC) scope, the designated site contact may be an Aker Kvaerner Chemetics representative. It is noted that the original document's section reference for this specific EPC scope site contact information is incomplete, stated as "To Section :".

This delegation process is a fundamental aspect of effective [[project_organizational_structure_and_responsibilities]] and supports the overall [[assignment_of_project_responsibilities]] within a project.
