# Shell and Tube Heat Exchanger Mechanical Design Requirements

This page details the mechanical design and fabrication requirements for the Shell and Tube Heat Exchanger (Chemetics Equipment Number 10-10H, "Water Heater"), focusing on structural integrity, welding quality, corrosion protection, and thermal expansion management. These specifications are crucial for the safe and reliable operation of the equipment.

For process-related design conditions, refer to [[shell_and_tube_heat_exchanger_process_design_conditions]]. Material specifications can be found on the [[shell_and_tube_heat_exchanger_material_selection]] page.

**External Load Considerations:**
*   External loads, including wind, seismic, and snow loads, **shall** be calculated by the fabricator.
*   Reference for site design conditions: [[1-DST-0001_site_design_conditions]].

**Welding and Joint Efficiency:**
*   **Radiography:** Spot radiography is required for welds.
*   **Joint Efficiency Shell Side:** 0.7
*   **Joint Efficiency Tube Side:** 0.85

**Corrosion Allowance:**
*   **Corrosion Allowance on Carbon Steel (Shell Side):** 1.5 mm
*   **Corrosion Allowance (Tube Side):** 0.0 mm

**Hydrostatic Testing:**
*   **Shell Side Hydrotest Pressure:** Code kPa(g). This pressure **must** comply with the applicable design code.
*   **Tube Side Hydrotest Pressure:** Code kPa(g). This pressure **must** comply with the applicable design code.

**Expansion Joint Requirements:**
The fabricator **shall** determine the necessary expansion joint requirements based on the specified normal and upset metal temperatures.
*   **Minimum Cycle Life for Normal Conditions:** 10,000 cycles
*   **Minimum Cycle Life for Upset Conditions:** 100 cycles

These mechanical design parameters are crucial for ensuring the long-term reliability and safety of the heat exchanger under various operating and environmental stresses.

**General Design & Fabrication Requirements:**
*   **Vessel Registration:** Required in the Province of B.C., Canada.
*   **Design Codes:** The vessel must be designed in accordance with ASME Section VIII, Division 1 and TEMA "C".
*   **Chemetics Specifications:** Adherence to Chemetics specifications 4-SPC-0040, 4-SPC-0060, and 4-SPC-0061 is mandatory.
*   **External Loads:** External loads (wind, seismic, snow) are to be referenced from 1-DST-0001: Site Design Conditions. The fabricator is responsible for calculating these loads.
*   **Radiography:** Spot radiography is required.
*   **Joint Efficiency:** Shell Side Joint Efficiency: 0.7; Tube Side Joint Efficiency: 0.85.
*   **Corrosion Allowance:** Corrosion Allowance on Carbon Steel (Shell Side): 1.5 mm; Corrosion Allowance (Tube Side): 0.0 mm.
*   **Quality Assurance:** In addition to ASME Code and Chemetics specifications, Chemetics Quality Data Sheet requirements must be met.

**Thermal Expansion and Expansion Joints:**
*   **Normal Mean Metal Temperatures:** Shell: 32.0 deg °C; Tubes: 60.0 deg °C.
*   **Upset Mean Metal Temperatures:** Shell: 32.0 deg °C; Tubes: 80.0 deg °C.
*   The fabricator will determine expansion joint requirements based on the specified normal and upset metal temperatures.
*   **Expansion Joint Cycle Life:** Minimum cycle life for normal conditions: 10,000 cycles; Minimum cycle life for upset conditions: 100 cycles.

**Site Conditions:**
*   **Equipment Base Elevation:** 10.0 m
*   **Height of Building:** 15.0 m
