# Purchase Order Termination Conditions and Procedures

This page outlines the conditions and procedures for the termination of a Purchase Order (PO) by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, including the Vendor's obligations and the calculation of amounts due upon termination.

**Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' Right to Terminate:**
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may terminate the Purchase Order for any reason by providing the Vendor with written notice.

**Vendor Obligations Upon Termination:**
*   In the event of termination, Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may instruct the Vendor to assign the benefit of any subcontract or other agreement related to the performance of this Purchase Order to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
*   The Vendor **must** [[purchase_order_definitions#Delivery or Deliver|Deliver]] all [[purchase_order_definitions#The Goods and Services|Goods and Services]], regardless of their state of completion, if Aker Kvaerner Chemetics has made a payment or part payment in respect of those Goods and Services.

**Amount Due on Termination (Non-Default):**
Where Aker Kvaerner Chemetics terminates the Purchase Order for a reason other than the default of the Vendor, the amount due on termination **must** include:
*   An amount due assessed as for normal payments.
*   The reasonable cost for Goods and Services to which Aker Kvaerner Chemetics has title and of which the Vendor has to accept delivery.
*   Other costs reasonably incurred by the Vendor in expectation of completing the whole of the Goods and Services, which Aker Kvaerner Chemetics is able to recover from the [[purchase_order_definitions#The Owner|Owner]].
*   A deduction of any advanced payment made to the Vendor.
