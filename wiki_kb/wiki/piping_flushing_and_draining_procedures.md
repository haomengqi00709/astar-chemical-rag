# Piping Flushing and Draining Procedures

Effective flushing and draining are essential steps before and after piping system tests to ensure cleanliness and prevent damage.

**Pre-Test Flushing**
*   With the drains open, water should be flushed through the system at the maximum rate available from the supply until the water leaving the drains appears to be clean.
*   After flushing, drains may be closed, and the system filled for testing.
*   For detailed flushing guidelines, refer to [[flushing_procedures]].

**Post-Test Draining**
*   After completion of testing, the system **will** be drained at the fastest rate possible through the drain connections.
*   The system **will** be inspected for debris and cleaned after completion of testing.
*   The timing and manner of draining the test fluid **will** be agreed with the [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_site_supervisor_review]].
*   Care **will** be taken when draining the test fluid to avoid damage to other items of equipment.
*   Attention **will** be paid to opening top vents when draining test fluid.
*   For comprehensive draining instructions, see [[draining_procedures]].

**Pump Suction and Discharge Preparation**
Specific procedures apply to pump connections:
*   For pump suctions, the line between the pump and the first valve **will** be dismantled. All debris **will** be removed, and the [[temporary_startup_strainer_installation]] **will** be installed.
*   For pump discharges, the line between the pump and the first valve **will** be dismantled, and all debris **will** be removed.
*   These steps are crucial for [[pump_suction_and_discharge_preparation]].

**Pre-Test Flushing**
*   Prior to the introduction of water, piping and equipment must be inspected.
*   Prior to the introduction of water, all dirt, debris and loose material must be removed.
*   With the drains open, water should be flushed through the system at the maximum rate available from the supply until the water leaving the drains appears to be clean.
*   After flushing, drains may be closed and the system filled for testing.

**Post-Test Draining**
*   After completion of testing, the system will be drained at the fastest rate possible through the drain connections.
*   After completion of testing, the system will be inspected for debris and cleaned.
*   The timing and manner of draining the test fluid will be agreed with the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site representative.
*   Care will be taken when draining the test fluid to avoid damage to other items of equipment.
*   Attention will be paid to opening top vents when draining test fluid.
