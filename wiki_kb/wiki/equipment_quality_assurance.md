# Equipment Quality Assurance

Ensuring the quality of equipment, both during supply and installation, is paramount for project success and operational reliability. Quality assurance (QA) processes are implemented to verify compliance with specifications and standards.

**Equipment Supply Quality Assurance**
For quality assurance related to equipment supply, project personnel **must** refer to [[0-PRC-7005_vendor_quality_assurance]]. This procedure outlines the requirements and processes for ensuring vendor quality throughout the manufacturing and delivery stages.

**Installation Quality Assurance**
For quality assurance related to equipment installation, project personnel **must** refer to [[0-PRC-6071_construction_subcontract_quality_assurance]]. This procedure details the quality requirements and inspection protocols for construction subcontractors.

**Quality Data Sheets (QDS)**
Quality Data Sheets (QDS) were formally added to the 4-PRC-0003 procedure in Revision 1, indicating their role in documenting and tracking quality-related information for equipment. For general information on QDS, refer to [[quality_data_sheets_qds]].
