# Cooling Tower Operating Conditions

This page outlines the mandatory operating conditions and performance parameters required for the design and specification of cooling towers, as detailed in document **4-DST-XXXX-R0**. These conditions are crucial for ensuring the cooling tower meets project-specific performance and safety standards.

**Quantity Data:**
The datasheet requires detailed quantity information, including:
*   Operating units
*   Installed Spare units

**Operating Conditions:**
All operating conditions must specify both **Maximum** and **Minimum** values to define the operational envelope of the cooling tower. Mandatory parameters include:
*   **Fluid Description**: A clear description of the fluid being cooled.
*   **Dissolved Solid information**: Data on dissolved solids in the circulating water.
*   **Water Circulation Rate**: Must be provided in m3/h.
*   **Drift Loss**: Must be provided in m3/h.
*   **Blowdown**: Must be provided in m3/h.
*   **Evaporation Loss**: Must be provided in m3/h.
*   **Make Up Water Requirements**: Must be provided in m3/h.
*   **Water Temperature In**: Must be provided in °C.
*   **Water Temperature Out**: Must be provided in °C.
*   **Design Wet Bulb Temperature**: Must be provided in °C.
*   **Design Wind Velocity - Process**: Must be provided in kM/Hr.

These detailed operating conditions are essential inputs for the [[cooling_tower_design_requirements]] and contribute to the overall [[plant_design_basis_and_capacity]]. Accurate data ensures the cooling tower is appropriately sized and configured for its intended application.

**Required Operating Parameters:**
For each operating condition, both **Maximum** and **Minimum** values must be provided. Specific parameters required include:
*   Fluid Description
*   Dissolved Solid information
*   Water Circulation Rate (in m3/h)
*   Drift Loss (in m3/h)
*   Blowdown (in m3/h)
*   Evaporation Loss (in m3/h)
*   Make Up Water Requirements (in m3/h)
*   Water Temperature In (in °C)
*   Water Temperature Out (in °C)
*   Design Wet Bulb Temperature (in °C)
*   Design Wind Velocity - Process (in kM/Hr)
