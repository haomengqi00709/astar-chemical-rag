# Liability and Indemnities (0-SPC-6001)

This page details the limitations of liability and indemnity obligations for both Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Subcontractor under construction subcontract 0-SPC-6001.

**Limitations of Liability**
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Subcontractor **will not** be liable under this Subcontract, in law or equity (including for negligence), for:
*   Loss of profit, loss of use, loss of production, loss of business, or any similar loss.
*   Any indirect or consequential damage.
*   Special, exemplary, or punitive damages.

**Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' Indemnities**
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics indemnifies the Subcontractor against claims, proceedings, compensation, and costs payable that are:
*   The unavoidable result of the works or of [[standard_conditions_for_construction_subcontract_0_spc_6001#To Provide the Works|Providing the Works]].
*   Arising from fault by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or its employees.
*   Arising from negligence by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or its employees.
*   Arising from breach of statutory duty by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or its employees.
*   Arising from infringement of intellectual property by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or its employees.
*   Arising from interference with a legal right by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or its employees.

**Subcontractor's Indemnities**
The Subcontractor indemnifies Aker Kvaerner Chemetics against:
*   Death of or injury to a person arising from or in connection with the Subcontractor’s Providing the Works.
*   Loss of and damage to property (excluding the works, [[standard_conditions_for_construction_subcontract_0_spc_6001#Equipment and Materials|Equipment and Materials]]) arising from or in connection with the Subcontractor’s Providing the Works.
*   Claims, proceedings, compensation, and costs payable arising from or in connection with the Subcontractor’s Providing the Works.

**Reduction of Indemnity Liability**
The liability of one Party to indemnify the other is reduced to the extent that events which are the other Party’s responsibility contributed to the losses, claims, proceedings, compensation, and costs.
