# Equipment Lubrication Requirements

As specified in document 4-SPC-0001, proper lubrication design and provision are essential for the longevity and reliable operation of equipment.

**Accessibility and Operation**
Equipment **must** be designed such that lubrication points are easily accessible. Lubrication **must be** possible while the equipment is operating, minimizing downtime and ensuring continuous protection of moving parts.

**Vendor Responsibilities**
The Vendor **will** recommend appropriate lubricants for the equipment. Additionally, the Vendor **shall provide** a comprehensive lubrication schedule, detailing types, quantities, and frequencies of lubrication.

**Shipping and Initial Fill**
Gearboxes **will be** drained of oil prior to shipping to prevent leaks and damage during transit. Sufficient lubricant for a first fill **will be** provided and shipped with the equipment, ensuring it is ready for initial operation upon arrival. Greased bearings **will be** greased at the factory, reducing on-site preparation.

**Lubrication System Preferences and Features**
Splash lubrication **is preferred** over forced circulation oil lubrication due to its simplicity and reliability. All gearboxes **shall include** the following features:
*   A replaceable breather
*   A magnetic sump element
*   A dust-tight dipstick or sight glass
*   A plugged oil drain point

**Shaft Protection**
Rotating shaft packings and seals subject to dust and process loading **will be protected** by additional coarse sealing, such as labyrinth seals, in addition to any primary mechanical seal. This dual protection enhances the lifespan of seals and prevents ingress of contaminants.

For information on [[equipment_bearings_and_seals]], refer to the dedicated page.

**Lubricant Recommendations and Supply**
The Vendor will recommend lubricants.
The Vendor will provide a lubrication schedule.
Sufficient lubricant for a first fill will be provided and shipped with the equipment.
Greased bearings will be greased at the factory.

**Gearbox Design and Preparation**
Splash lubrication is preferred over forced circulation oil lubrication.
All gearboxes will include a replaceable breather, magnetic sump element, dust-tight dipstick or sight glass, and plugged oil drain point.
Gearboxes will be drained of oil prior to shipping.
