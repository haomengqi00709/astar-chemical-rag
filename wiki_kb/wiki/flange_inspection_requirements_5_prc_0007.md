# Flange Inspection Requirements (5-PRC-0007)

As part of the [[mechanical_checkout_piping_5_prc_0007|Piping Mechanical Checkout Procedure (5-PRC-0007)]], a thorough inspection of flanges is conducted to ensure their correct installation and compliance with design specifications.

**Required Documentation for Flange Checkout:**
*   [[piping_and_instrumentation_drawings|P&ID]]
*   [[pipe_specifications|Pipe Specification]]
*   [[pipe_isometric|Isometric]]
*   [[gasket_colour_code_chart|Gasket Colour Code Chart]]

**Flange Inspection Steps:**
*   **Gasket Material:** Visually confirm that the installed [[gasket_specification|gasket material]] matches the [[pipe_isometric|isometric]] using a flashlight.
*   **Gasket Centering:** Visually confirm that the gasket is correctly centred within the flange joint.
*   **Overtightening Check:** Visually confirm that the flange has not been overtightened, which could cause the gasket to be forced out.
*   **Flange Type and Material:** Visually confirm that the flange type and material conform to the [[pipe_specifications|pipe specification]].
*   **Flange Alignment:** Visually confirm that the flange alignment is satisfactory, ensuring no undue stress on the piping system.
*   **Flange Tightness:** Check flange tightness by attempting to hand-turn nuts and gently shaking the pipe.
*   **Flange Clearance:** Confirm that adequate flange clearance is maintained for operational access and future maintenance activities.
*   **Bolting Material:** Visually confirm that the [[bolting_specification|bolting material]] is in accordance with the specification.
*   **Bolt Length:** Confirm that the bolt length is adequate for the flange and gasket combination.
*   **Washers:** Confirm that washers are present when required by the design.

These detailed checks ensure the integrity and safety of all flanged connections within the piping system.
