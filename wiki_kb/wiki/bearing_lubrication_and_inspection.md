# Bearing Lubrication and Inspection

This page details the mandatory requirements for [[bearing_lubrication_and_inspection|bearing lubrication]] and inspection for [[rotating_equipment_mechanical_checkout|rotating]], [[packaged_equipment_mechanical_checkout|packaged]], and [[conveying_equipment_mechanical_checkout|conveying equipment]]. Manufacturer's installation instructions **must** be checked for shipping grease, and bearings **must** be lubricated.
