# Insulation Document Structure (Major Work Subject 6)

The "6 Insulation" major work subject is dedicated to all documentation related to the design, specification, and installation of insulation systems for piping, equipment, and other plant components. This folder is crucial for managing information that ensures thermal efficiency and personnel protection.

The sub-folders and their contents within the 6-Insulation folder are:

*   **CAL:** Insulation Thickness Calculations. Calculations to determine the required thickness of insulation for various applications.
*   **DTL:** Insulation Details. Detailed drawings and specifications for insulation application, including specific components and methods.
*   **LST:** Insulation Lists. Comprehensive lists of all insulated items, including type and thickness of insulation. Data available in SQL: LST files.
*   **PRC:** Project Specific Procedures. Procedures tailored to insulation design, material selection, and installation.
*   **REQ:** Requisitions. Documents for requesting bids or proposals for insulation materials and services.
*   **SPC:** Specifications. Technical specifications for insulation materials, application methods, and finishes.
*   **TAB:** Bid Tabulations. Records of bids received for insulation materials and services.

This structure ensures that all insulation-related documents, from thickness calculations to bid tabulations, are systematically organized and easily retrievable. For an overview of the entire project documentation structure, refer to [[document_control_1_prc_0003]]. The definitions of major work subjects are outlined in [[major_work_division_codes]].
