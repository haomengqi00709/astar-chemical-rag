# Review of Vendor Documentation (0-PRC-0007)

The Review of Vendor Documentation procedure (0-PRC-0007) establishes the process for evaluating documents submitted by vendors. This critical step ensures that all vendor-provided documentation aligns with the technical requirements issued by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Purpose**
Documents received from vendors **must** be reviewed to ensure they meet the intent of the technical requirements issued by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. This review process is essential for maintaining project quality, compliance, and design integrity.

**Reference**
For detailed instructions on the review process for vendor documentation, refer to 0-PRC-0007. This procedure works in conjunction with [[vendor_document_requirements]] to manage vendor deliverables effectively.
