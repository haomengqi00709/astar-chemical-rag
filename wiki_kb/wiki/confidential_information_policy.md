# Confidential Information Policy (Subcontractor Data)

Aker Kvaerner Chemetics is committed to protecting the confidentiality of sensitive information provided by subcontractors during the pre-qualification and bidding processes, particularly as outlined in the [[subcontractor_pre_qualification_report_0_rep_8405]].

**Confidentiality Commitment**
The Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Finance Department **shall** treat all information provided in the subcontractor pre-qualification form, including audited financial statements, as strictly confidential.

**Non-Disclosure**
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **will not** disclose confidential information, nor make it available to any other person within Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, except with the subcontractor's prior written consent. This ensures that sensitive data is only accessible to authorized personnel directly involved in the evaluation process.

**Protection Measures**
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **shall** take all reasonable precautions necessary to protect confidential information from unauthorized disclosure or access by others. This includes implementing appropriate security measures for data storage and access.

**Limited Use**
Confidential information, or any modification or derivation thereof, **shall not** be used by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for any purpose other than to assess the financial capabilities of the subcontractor organization to provide the quoted Goods and Services. This policy strictly limits the scope of use for all submitted confidential data.

This policy aligns with the broader [[confidential_information_policy]] of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, ensuring ethical and legal handling of proprietary data.
