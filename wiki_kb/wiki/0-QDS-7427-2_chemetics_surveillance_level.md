# Stainless Steel Tubing Tier 2 Chemetics Surveillance Level

For the Stainless Steel Tubing Tier 2 project, surveillance by Chemetics will be conducted to Level 1.0.

This surveillance level indicates the degree of oversight and involvement Chemetics will maintain throughout the manufacturing and inspection processes, as further detailed within this Quality Data Sheet. The specific activities and hold points for Chemetics are outlined in the respective sections, such as [[0-QDS-7427-2_material_verification_and_certifications]], [[0-QDS-7427-2_pressure_and_leak_testing]], [[0-QDS-7427-2_non_destructive_examination_requirements]], [[0-QDS-7427-2_final_inspections]], and [[0-QDS-7427-2_packing_and_shipping_requirements]].

The Vendor's Quality Assurance program, as described in [[0-QDS-7427-2_vendor_quality_assurance_requirements]], **shall** accommodate this surveillance level.
