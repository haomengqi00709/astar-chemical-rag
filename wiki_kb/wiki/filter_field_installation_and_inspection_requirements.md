# Filter Field Installation and Inspection Requirements

This page outlines the critical installation and inspection requirements for filter equipment, typically verified using a [[4-FIC-XX19-R0_filter_field_inspection_checklist_overview|Field Inspection Checklist]] such as document 4-FIC-XX19-R0. Adherence to these requirements is mandatory to ensure the safe, reliable, and compliant operation of the filter.

**General Installation Requirements:**
These items cover the foundational and structural aspects of the filter installation:
*   The correct Nameplate **must** be installed, verifying equipment identification.
*   Foundation/Anchor bolts **must** be secure and tightened to specified torque values.
*   All Platforms, hand rails, walkways, stairs & ladders **must** be installed, ensuring safe access and egress.
*   Safety gates **must** be installed and functioning correctly at all access points.
*   Grounding **must** be completed. Grounding may be required at multiple locations as per [[electrical_installation_specification]] and [[below_grade_grounding_specification]].
*   Filter accessories (gauges, vents, drains etc.) **must** be installed.
*   Isolation valves **must** be installed on drains to allow for safe maintenance and operation, as per [[isolation_and_preparation_for_maintenance_1_prc_0007]].
*   Flange Guards **must** be installed if applicable, particularly for flanges handling hazardous materials or operating at high temperatures/pressures.

**Non-Destructive Testing (NDT) and Documentation:**
*   All NDT inspections **must** be completed as per project specifications and relevant codes.
*   NDT inspection records **must** be available for review, demonstrating compliance with quality standards. These records are crucial for [[vendor_inspection_and_test_plan]] verification.

**Internal Components and Cleanliness:**
The internal integrity and cleanliness of the filter vessel are paramount for its performance:
*   Internal painting or lining **must** be completed according to specifications.
*   Internal painting or lining **must** be inspected to ensure full coverage and integrity, preventing corrosion or contamination. Refer to [[0-QDS-7414_coating_and_lining_inspection]] for related requirements.
*   Filter medium **must** be installed according to the manufacturer's drawing and specifications.
*   Filter internals (hold downs, supports, piping, etc.) **must** be installed and properly supported to prevent movement or damage during operation.
*   Correct hardware **must** be installed for all internal components.
*   Correct gaskets **must** be installed at all sealing surfaces to ensure leak-tightness.
*   The filter vessel **must** be clean and free from debris, foreign objects, or construction residue.
*   The filter vessel **must** be empty of any liquids or temporary packing materials.

**Final Closure and External Finishes:**
*   A final internal inspection **must** be completed with the client's representative to confirm all internal requirements are met.
*   A closure certificate **must** be attached for the final internal inspection, formally documenting its completion.
*   Manway covers **must** be installed and securely bolted tight after the final internal inspection, as detailed in [[manway_covers_installation_and_inspection]].
*   External painting **must** be completed according to project coating specifications. Refer to [[coatings_erection_0_spc_6x97]].
*   Insulation **must** be complete, providing thermal protection and energy efficiency. Refer to [[insulation_erection_0_spc_6x96]].
*   Cladding **must** be complete, protecting the insulation and providing a finished appearance.

These requirements collectively ensure that the filter equipment is mechanically complete, safe for operation, and ready for subsequent pre-commissioning and commissioning activities. Deviations from these requirements **must** be documented and resolved through the project's [[defect_management_process]] or [[non_conformance_report_0_ncr_7]].
