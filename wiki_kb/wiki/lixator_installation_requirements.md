# Lixator Installation Requirements

This page details critical installation requirements and considerations for Lixator units, derived from document **4-PDS-XXXX-R1**. Adherence to these requirements is essential for the safe, efficient, and long-term operation of the Lixator system.

**Inter-Unit Overflow:**
*   Either Lixator unit **will** overflow to the other unit. This design feature ensures redundancy and prevents overfilling of a single unit by directing excess flow to the alternate Lixator. Proper interconnections and level monitoring **must** be in place to facilitate this overflow mechanism, ensuring continuous operation and preventing potential spills.

**Piping Support for Salt Dumps:**
*   Piping **must** be well supported to withstand the weight of a salt dump. This is a mandatory requirement to prevent structural failure or damage to piping due to the significant static and dynamic loads imposed during salt loading operations. Adequate structural supports, foundations, and pipe racks **shall** be designed and installed to accommodate these loads, considering the maximum possible weight of salt.

**Fine Salt Compaction Prevention:**
*   For applications involving fine salt, a "graded gravel bed" **is required** to avoid salt compaction. Salt compaction can lead to reduced dissolution rates, channeling, and operational inefficiencies within the Lixator. The graded gravel bed provides a permeable layer that prevents the fine salt from consolidating into an impermeable mass, thereby ensuring consistent brine production and optimal performance. The design of this gravel bed **shall** consider the specific particle size distribution and characteristics of the fine salt being used.
