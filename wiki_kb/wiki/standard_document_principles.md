# Standard Document Principles

The development of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' standard documents is guided by several core principles designed to maximize their effectiveness, clarity, and utility for their intended audience. Adherence to these principles ensures that standards are practical, focused, and easily applicable.

**Singular Purpose:**
*   Each standard document *must* be written for a singular purpose only. This principle ensures that the document remains focused and avoids scope creep, which can lead to confusion and reduced applicability. Writing standards with a singular purpose directly reflects the typical procurement methods employed for materials and services, where specific requirements are often isolated and addressed individually. This approach simplifies both the creation and application of standards.

**End-User Focus:**
*   Standards *must* be targeted directly at the end-user of the document. This means the language, level of detail, and organization of content *must* be tailored to meet the practical needs and understanding of those who will be applying the standard in their daily work. Documents should be intuitive and provide actionable information without requiring extensive interpretation.

**Practical Coverage:**
*   Standards *must* be written to cover approximately 95% of circumstances normally encountered. This pragmatic approach acknowledges that while comprehensive coverage is desirable, attempting to address every conceivable edge case can lead to overly complex and unwieldy documents. The focus is on providing robust guidance for the vast majority of situations, with unusual cases handled through specific project instructions or approved deviations, ensuring efficiency without sacrificing essential control.

**Brevity and Clarity:**
*   All textual documents *must* be as brief as possible while rigorously preserving clarity. Unnecessary jargon, verbose explanations, and redundant information *must* be avoided. The goal is to convey information efficiently and effectively, ensuring that the message is understood without requiring excessive reading or interpretation. Concise documents are more likely to be read and followed.

**Template Instructions:**
*   Instructions intended for users within a template body that *must* be removed before final document issuance *will* be clearly enclosed in square brackets (e.g., `[Insert project-specific details here]`). This visual cue helps authors identify and delete placeholder text, ensuring that only relevant, finalized content remains in the published document, preventing the accidental inclusion of drafting notes.

**Singular Purpose and Audience**
Standards will be written for a singular purpose only. Writing standards for a singular purpose reflects the typical procurement method for material and services.
Standards will be targeted at the end-user of the document.
Standards will be written to cover 95% of circumstances normally encountered.
