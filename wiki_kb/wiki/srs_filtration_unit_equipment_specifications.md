# SRS Filtration Unit Equipment Specifications

This page outlines the key equipment specifications for the SRS Filtration Unit, as detailed in Document ID 4-PDS-XXXX-R1. These specifications cover the physical and operational characteristics of the unit's components.

**General Equipment Data:**
*   **Quantity:** 1.0 unit of the SRS Filtration Unit is specified.

**Equipment Model and Operation:**
*   **Equipment Model Number options:** The unit may utilize models 111.0, 211.0, 321.0, 321.0, or 432.0.
*   **Type of Operation options:** The filtration unit can operate in Manual, Semi-Automatic, or Automatic modes.

**Filtration Stages and Components:**
The SRS Filtration Unit is designed with multiple stages:
*   **Equipment Stages:** Stage 1, Stage 2, Stage 3, Stage 4

Key internal components and their parameters include:
*   **Number of Elements:** This is a critical equipment parameter.
*   **Number of Housings:** This is also a key equipment parameter.
*   **Housing Diameter unit:** inch

**Membrane and Spacer Details:**
*   **Type of Membrane:** This is an essential equipment parameter, defining the filtration technology.
*   **Type of Wrap:** This is an equipment parameter related to membrane construction.
*   **Spacer unit:** mils

**Material of Construction:**
*   **Housing Material of Construction:** FRP (Fiber Reinforced Polymer) is specified for the housing, chosen for its corrosion resistance and structural integrity. For general guidelines on material selection, refer to [[material_selection]].
