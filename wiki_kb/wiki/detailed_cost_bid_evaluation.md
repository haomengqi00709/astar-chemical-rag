# Detailed Cost Bid Evaluation (Orders > CAD 200k)

For orders valued greater than CAD 200,000, the Requisitioning Discipline **must** conduct a detailed cost evaluation of the short-listed bids. This evaluation is documented as a revision of the previously prepared [[preliminary_cost_and_technical_review|Cost Tabulation]] (0-TAB-5XXX-1).

**Revision and Total Cost Comparison:**
*   Pricing of line items **must** be revised as necessary to provide a complete and accurate record of each Vendor’s offer. These revisions **must** reflect any changes made in response to requests for clarification or modification.
*   The Cost Tabulation **must** compare the total cost to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for each short-listed bid. This total cost **must** include an estimate of any additional cost elements such as inspection, quality surveillance, freight, associated structures, piping, utility connections, and erection costs.

**Content Refinement:**
*   Sections of the previously prepared Cost Tabulation that dealt with the preliminary technical merits of each Vendor’s bid **must** be deleted.
*   Sections of the previously prepared Cost Tabulation that dealt with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' experience with each Vendor **must** also be deleted.
*   These aspects (technical merits and Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' experience) will be addressed more thoroughly and in detail within the [[detailed_technical_bid_evaluation|Technical Bid Tabulation]].

**Final Identification and Approval:**
Following the [[final_vendor_selection_meeting|final review meeting]], the selected Vendor **must** be identified on the Cost Tabulation. The Requisitioning Discipline **must** publish the revised Cost Tabulation using [[publishing_project_documents]] (0-PRC-0005). For orders of value greater than CAD 200k, the Project Manager **will** approve the Cost Tabulation during this final cost evaluation stage.

The approved Cost Tabulation authorizes the issuance of the Purchase Order, provided that the conditions described in the [[order_risk_assessment_procedure|Order Risk Assessment]] are met.
