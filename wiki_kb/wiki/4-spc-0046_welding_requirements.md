# Sulphuric Acid Storage Tank Welding Requirements (4-SPC-0046)

This section details the mandatory welding requirements for sulphuric acid storage tanks under specification 4-SPC-0046, emphasizing quality, procedure adherence, and welder qualifications.

**General Welding Standards**
Welding quality **must** adhere rigidly to specified procedures. All welds **must** be free of slag and defects that could cause leaks, as welding slag is incompatible with sulphuric acid. Poor fit-up, inadequate dryness, or cleanliness that adversely affects welding **will not** be compromised. Manufacturers **must** select welders qualified per paragraph 10.4.2, who **must** consistently produce high-quality work to the satisfaction of the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' inspector.

**Welding Processes and Consumables**
Preferred welding processes are Shielded Metal Arc Welding (SMAW), Gas Tungsten Arc Welding (GTAW), Gas Metal Arc Welding (GMAW), and Submerged Arc Welding (SAW). Manufacturer's welding procedures and qualification records **must** be submitted for review prior to construction.

*   **SMAW:** Carbon steel SMAW **will** use low hydrogen electrodes (e.g., E7018) conforming to [[ASME_sfa_5_4_welding_electrodes|SFA-5.1]]. Direct current, reverse polarity is preferred.
*   **GTAW:** Filler rods **will** satisfy [[ASME_sfa_5_9_welding_electrodes_and_rods|SFA-5.18]] requirements. Preferred electrode is thoriated tungsten per [[ASME_sfa_5_12_tungsten_arc_welding_electrodes|SFA-5.12]], Class EWTh-2, **must** be sharply ground. Weld metal thickness **will not** exceed 1/8"/pass. Low heat input via stringer bead technique is recommended. Back-up gas **will** be used to prevent root pass oxidation/contamination where necessary.
*   **GMAW:** Solid wire electrodes **must** conform to [[ASME_sfa_5_9_welding_electrodes_and_rods|SFA-5.18]] ER70S-2 or ER70S-6. Flux cored electrodes **must** conform to SFA 5.20 (E60T-8, E70-Tl, E70-T5, or E70T-6). A chemical analysis of weld metal **must** accompany the P.Q.R. Fabricator **must** demonstrate continuous GMAW use. A minimum of two passes is required for all thicknesses. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **will** be formally advised of flux core welded seam locations. GMAW **will** only be used with written approval.
*   **SAW:** Electrodes and fluxes **must** satisfy [[ASME_sfa_5_17_carbon_steel_electrodes_and_fluxes_for_submerged_arc_welding|SFA-5.17]] requirements. Maximum weld deposit per pass is 1/2". Starting/run-off pads of base material **must** be used on all longitudinal seams. Production flux/wire combination **must** match qualification. Wire spools **must** be tagged. Single-pass welding combinations **must not** be used for multiple-pass welds. Chemical analysis of finished deposit **must** be part of weld procedure qualification. Fabricator **must** provide evidence of continuous shop use for Low Temperature, high carbon, or high Manganese steels.

**Preheat, Interpass, and Electrode Management**
Preheat and interpass temperatures **must** conform to applicable codes and reviewed procedures, controlled by crayons, pyrometers, or similar. Preheat **must** remove condensation from the weld joint area (2"-3" adjacent material). Electrode preheating and storage **must** follow manufacturer requirements. Fabricator **must** ensure low hydrogen electrodes are dry and warm using portable heated containers.

**Welder Qualifications and Workmanship**
All welders **must** be qualified per [[ASME_section_ix_welding_and_brazing_qualifications]] for all position welding on plate up to maximum tank thickness prior to fabrication. Welds **must** be clearly identified with the welder's ID on the tank exterior. Welding surfaces **must** be smooth, uniform, and free from defects, dry, and protected from weather. If metal surface temperature is below 350F (20C), base metal within 3 inches of weld start **must** be preheated to hand-warm temperature per [[API_650_welded_steel_tanks|API Standard 650, Section 5.2]].

**Joint Specifics**
*   **Annular/Bridge Plates:** Radial joints **must** be double butt welded, full penetration. If thickness differs, joint **must** be double butt welded, full penetration, with flush inside surface.
*   **Bottom Plates:** **Must** be joined by double butt welds with full penetration OR butt welded with full penetration using 2" wide, 1/4" thick backing strips. Backing strips **must** be firmly in contact, centered, and field tack welded to one plate before fitting the second. No gaps between adjacent backing strips. Bottom plates **must** have single "V" grooves, wire brushed, and vacuum cleaned before welding. All bottom plate welds **must** have a minimum of four layers. Root gap **must** be 1/8 inch minimum, 1/4 inch maximum.
*   **Bottom-to-Shell:** **Must** have full penetration welds with reinforcing fillets, balanced to minimize cocking/tilting.
*   **Shell Plates:** Horizontal and vertical joints **must** have full penetrating butt welds. Shell plates 5/8" or over **must** have double V-grooves. Thinner plates **must** have single V-grooves, completed with back gouge and final pass from inside. Reinforcement **must** be within welding procedure and API specifications.

**Post-Welding Cleanup**
All erection lugs, brackets, arc strikes, craters, etc. **must** be removed to leave a uniform, full thickness surface, free from stress raisers or crevices. This removal is also **mandatory** to prevent crevices where dilute acid condensation can collect.
