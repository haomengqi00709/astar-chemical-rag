# Acid Cooler Packing and Shipping Requirements (0-QDS-7411)

This page outlines the packing and shipping requirements for the Acid Cooler, as specified in document [[0-QDS-7411_document_details|0-QDS-7411]].

**Packing and Crating Activities:**
*   Preparation for shipping Packing/Crating requires surveillance (witness) by Chemetics Vancouver.
*   Packing List Packing/Crating requires surveillance (witness) by Chemetics Vancouver.

These requirements ensure that the Acid Cooler is properly prepared, packed, and documented for safe transportation, preventing damage and ensuring accurate delivery.
