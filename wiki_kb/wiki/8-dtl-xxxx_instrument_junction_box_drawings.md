# Instrument Junction Box Drawings (8-DTL-XXXX)

Instrument Junction Box Drawings (8-DTL-XXXX) provide detailed information for the supply and fabrication of instrument junction boxes.

**Content Requirements:**
Instrument Junction Box Drawings (8-DTL-XXXX) **shall** contain:
*   Box assembly details.
*   Junction Box ID Tag (Size, Material, Colours, Engraving).
*   Enclosure specification.
*   Terminal quantity and specification.
*   Circuit breaker quantity and specification.
*   Fuse quantity and specification.
*   Terminal rail size and specification.
*   Grounding Details.
*   Purge Requirements where applicable.
*   Heating Requirements where applicable.

**Document Preparation and Approval:**
*   **Prepared by:** Detail Design Engineer (DDE)
*   **Approved by:** Detail Design Engineer (DDE)

**Quantity and Signal Segregation:**
The quantity of Instrument Junction boxes is dependent on the plant layout and instrument locations as per the [[8-gad-00xx_instrument_location_drawings]]. The DDE will determine the optimum number of Instrument Junction Boxes required for the project.

When Instrumentation discrete signals are 120 VAC or 220 VAC, separate junction boxes will be allowed for. These separate junction boxes will segregate Analog signals from discrete signals. When Instrumentation discrete signals are a low voltage DC signal (e.g., 24 VDC), separate junction boxes separating power supplies from analog signals is preferred. However, if there is a small quantity of low voltage DC signals, a single box with segregated terminal strips will be permitted. This segregation or single box with segregated terminal strips will allow for separate multiconductor cables to the plant control system.
