# Group Travel Policy Principles and Scope

The Aker Kvaerner Policy System, including this Group Travel Procedure (11.1 PR 2), is designed for internal use within Aker Kvaerner ASA and its subsidiaries and affiliates (the "Aker Kvaerner Group"). Its establishment aims to achieve a consistently high level of operations across the entire Group.

**Compliance and Interpretation**
The Policy System **shall** be fully complied with, always subject to local statutory laws and regulations. It **shall not** be interpreted as constituting any form of representation or warranty by Aker Kvaerner ASA or any Group member regarding performance levels. Furthermore, it **shall not** be interpreted as forming a contract or any part thereof (including an employment contract) unless expressly confirmed in writing by a duly authorised representative of a Group member.

**Purpose and General Principles**
The core purpose of this travel procedure is twofold:
1.  To ensure effective management of travelling costs.
2.  To ensure employees travel in a safe manner.

Employees **should** exercise commercial judgment to determine if the objective of a visit can be met without the need for physical travel. Only journeys that are essential for business may be undertaken, and these **must** be properly authorised. This applies universally to journeys by road, rail, or air.

To minimize costs and risks, the number of employees involved in any journey **must** be kept to a minimum. Travel to meetings involving only Group employees is strongly discouraged, and alternative communication methods such as video conferencing, Net Meeting, or telephone conference calls **should** be considered.

All reservations **must** only be booked with reliable suppliers to ensure quality service and proper regard for travellers’ health and safety needs.

**Application Scope**
This procedure is applicable to all Operating Businesses and Corporate Staff Departments within the Aker Kvaerner Group. It also extends to joint ventures, partnerships, and companies in which the Group has a participating interest, to the extent that the Group can enforce its application.

For details on how to handle conflicts with local regulations, refer to [[group_travel_policy_compliance_deviations]].

**Section 4.1: General Travel Principles**
This section covers the general guidelines for initiating travel. Before planning any journey, employees **should** ask if the journey is truly necessary and consider alternative communication methods.

### Purpose and Scope
The core purposes of this travel procedure are to ensure the effective management of travelling costs and to ensure employees travel in a safe manner.

This procedure applies to all Operating Businesses and Corporate Staff Departments. It also extends to joint ventures, partnerships, and companies in which the Group has a participating interest, to the extent the Group can enforce its application.

It is recognized that business travel represents a huge item of expenditure for the Group, underscoring the importance of adherence to these guidelines.
