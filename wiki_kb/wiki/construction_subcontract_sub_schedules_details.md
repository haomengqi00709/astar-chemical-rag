# Construction Subcontract Sub-Schedule Details

As part of the overall [[0-SPC-6021_construction_subcontract_scheduling|Construction Subcontract Scheduling]] requirements (Document ID: 0-SPC-6021), the Subcontractor's schedule **shall** be structured into three distinct sub-schedules: Design, Procurement, and Construction. Each sub-schedule has specific requirements for its preparation and content.

### Design Sub-schedule
The bar chart scheduling for design activities **shall** be prepared at [[construction_subcontract_schedule_levels#Level 3 Schedule (Detailed Schedule)|Level 3]].
*   This sub-schedule **shall** explicitly detail all design work activities executed by the Subcontractor’s personnel.
*   It **shall** also include activity durations for review and approval by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

### Procurement Sub-schedule
The bar chart scheduling for procurement activities **shall** be prepared at [[construction_subcontract_schedule_levels#Level 3 Schedule (Detailed Schedule)|Level 3]].
*   The Procurement sub-schedule **shall** represent the manufacture and shipment of each item or type of [[equipment_materials_and_title_0_spc_6001|Equipment and Materials]] supplied by the Subcontractor as single activities.
*   It **shall** show as [[project_schedule_and_milestones|milestones]] the delivery to the Subcontractor of all Equipment and Materials supplied by [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_obligations_0_spc_2201|Aker Kvaerner Chemetics]] or by the Owner.
*   Off-site prefabrication of sub-assemblies (e.g., structural steelwork, piping spools) **shall** also be detailed at [[construction_subcontract_schedule_levels#Level 3 Schedule (Detailed Schedule)|Level 3]].
*   Procurement activities **shall** have trade responsibility and [[construction_subcontract_labour_rates|direct labour hours]] entered. This data enables the preparation of resource loading histograms for Procurement.
*   The Subcontractor **shall** also develop [[construction_subcontract_schedule_levels#Level 4 Schedule (Work Plan)|Level 4 Work Plans]] specifically for Procurement activities.

### Construction Sub-schedule
The bar chart scheduling for construction activities **shall** be prepared at [[construction_subcontract_schedule_levels#Level 3 Schedule (Detailed Schedule)|Level 3]].
*   [[construction_subcontract_schedule_levels#Level 4 Schedule (Work Plan)|Level 4 Work Plans]] **shall** also be developed for Construction activities.
*   Construction activities **shall** include [[construction_subcontract_labour_rates|direct labour assignments]] and man-hours entered.
*   Construction activities **shall** also detail [[construction_subcontract_equipment_schedule|Construction Equipment]] requirements.
*   The entered data facilitates the preparation of resource loading histograms for Construction.
*   Dates for access to the site or specific portions of the site for tie-ins or other activities **shall** be clearly shown as [[project_schedule_and_milestones|milestones]].
