# Plant Communication Systems Layout Drawings

Plant Communication Systems Layout drawings detail the locations and types of plant telephones, public address (PA) speakers, and associated accessory equipment. These drawings also describe the interconnections with overall plant communication systems.

**Content of Communication Systems Layout Drawings**
Drawings **shall** indicate:
*   Locations of the plant telephones.
*   The type of telephone.
*   Locations of the plant public address one-way speakers.
*   Locations of the plant public address two-way speakers.
*   Types of speakers, identified and described.
*   Any accessory equipment associated with speakers, identified and described.
*   Main switchboards (area specific) for both telephone and PA systems.

Interconnections required between these area main switchboards and the overall plant telephone systems **will** be identified and described on the drawings. Similarly, interconnections required between these area main switchboards and the overall plant PA systems **will** be identified and described on the drawings.

Drawings **shall** include a complete bill of materials for both telephones and PA systems.
