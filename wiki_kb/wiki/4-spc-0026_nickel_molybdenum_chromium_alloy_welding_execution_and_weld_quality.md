# Welding Execution and Weld Quality for Nickel/Molybdenum/Chromium Alloys

This section details the mandatory requirements for welding execution and ensuring the quality of welds in nickel/molybdenum/chromium alloy fabrications.

**Preheat, Postweld Heat Treatment, and Interpass Temperature**
*   Preheat and postweld heat treatment are not recommended.
*   Material **shall** be dried prior to welding by heating to a temperature just sufficient to prevent condensation when adverse weather conditions cause condensation or wetness.
*   Interpass temperatures **shall not** exceed 200F.

**Welding Environment and Position**
*   The welding procedure **shall** be agreed upon (see Para 5.3 in [[4-spc-0026_nickel_molybdenum_chromium_alloy_fabricator_requirements]]) before any production welding is begun.
*   All welds **shall** be made in the downhand (flat) position, as far as practical.
*   Vertical welds **shall** be made vertical up (uphand).
*   Suitable enclosures **shall** be provided to keep welds and welders free from draughts, rain or other inclement weather.
*   Welding **shall not** be performed on wet surfaces.
*   Welding **shall not** be performed with wet or damp electrodes.
*   Fusion faces and adjacent metal (internal and external) **shall** be free from moisture, scale, paint, grease or other foreign matter immediately prior to welding.

**Alignment**
*   Alignment of welding edges at butt joints **shall** be in accordance with relevant code, specification, etc.
*   Maximum misalignment (High - Low) for plates up to and including 1/4" thickness is 1/16".
*   Maximum misalignment (High - Low) for plates above 1/4" thickness is 25% of the plate thickness or 1/8", whichever is smaller.
*   Maximum misalignment (High - Low) for pipe **shall** be in accordance with [[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping]].

**Holding Parts and Tack Welds**
*   Parts to be welded **shall** be securely held in position by mechanical means, welded-on bridge pieces or by tack welding to maintain alignment and gap during welding.
*   Jig construction **shall** allow adequate access for welding and cleaning.
*   Jig construction **shall** ensure external loading is not applied to a joint during welding.
*   Welded-on bridge or support spiders **shall** be of austenitic steel on the surface welded in contact with the vessel or heat exchanger.
*   Plate surface **shall not** be left in a damaged condition after removal of welded-on bridge pieces or support spiders.
*   Tack welds **shall** be performed by qualified welders.
*   Tack welds may form part of the completed weld if complete fusion is effected at the root and the welding procedure is the same as for the remainder of the root run.
*   Cracked and unsound tack welds **shall** be cut out prior to welding.
*   Sound tack welds **must** be thoroughly fused into the root run of the main weld.
*   Tack welds **must** be crack free when thinning down ends is necessary.
*   Tack welds should preferably be at least one inch long when thinning down ends is necessary.

**Back-up Rings, Run-off Pads, and Arc Strikes**
*   The use of permanent back-up rings or strips or consumable joint inserts is not permitted unless specifically approved in writing by [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_company_details]] Q.A. & Materials Group.
*   Run-off pads, in the same material as the parent plate, **shall** be used at the beginning and end of each plate weld.
*   Every effort **shall** be made to confine arc strikes to surfaces on which weld metal is to be deposited.
*   Arc strikes outside the weld area **shall** be ground.
*   Arc strikes outside the weld area **shall** be PT examined per Para 10.3 (see [[4-spc-0026_nickel_molybdenum_chromium_alloy_inspection_and_non_destructive_examination]]).
*   Arc strikes outside the weld area **shall** be repaired if necessary.
*   Burn marks from grounding clamps **shall** be treated as arc strikes.

**Weld Run Examination and Rework**
*   Each run of weld metal **shall** be carefully examined to ensure it is thoroughly cleaned and defect free.
*   All slag and foreign matter **shall** be removed before the next weld run is deposited.
*   On re-starting, care **shall** be taken to ensure proper fusion with the weld metal previously deposited.
*   All re-starts in the root bead **shall** be preceded by "feathering" out the area of the stop by grinding or filing.
*   Feathering **shall** be followed with wire brushing to remove any abrasive residue.
*   If tungsten loss occurs from an electrode contacting the work, tungsten pick-up in the weld **shall** be ground out.
*   If tungsten loss occurs from an electrode contacting the work, the electrode **shall** be reground to the correct profile before restarting.
*   If the heated end of the filler rod becomes contaminated, it **shall** be snipped off and discarded.
*   Peening of weld runs is not permitted.
*   Hammering of finished welds is not permitted.

**Weld Appearance and Quality**
*   Each weld **shall** be uniform in width and size throughout its full length.
*   Each layer of welding **shall** be smooth and free of slag, inclusions, cracks, porosity, lack of fusion, and undercut (except as permitted by referenced codes).
*   The cover pass **shall** be free of coarse ripples, irregular surface, non-uniform bead patterns, high crown, and deep ridges or valleys between beads.
*   Buttwelds **shall** be slightly convex.
*   Buttwelds **shall** be of uniform height.
*   Buttwelds **shall** have full penetration.
*   Internal reinforcement of pipe welds **shall** be subject to the limitations of the governing code.
*   Crevices or undercut **shall not** be permitted on the process side of the weld.
*   Fillet welds **shall** be deposited in not less than two layers, as far as practicable.
*   Fillet weld surfaces **shall** be free from marked irregularities or concavity.
*   Dimensions of fillet welds **shall** be in accordance with the approved drawing.
*   Grinding **shall not** be carried out in such a manner as to reduce the adjacent base metal thickness.
*   Complete removal of weld defects **shall** be verified by non-destructive examination prior to repair welding.

**Tools for Cleaning and Grinding**
*   Grinding wheels **shall** be iron-free.
*   Grinding wheels **shall** be of the resin bonded silicon carbide type.
*   Wire brushes or chipping tools **shall** be made of austenitic stainless steel.
*   Wire brushes or chipping tools **must not** have been previously used to clean surfaces other than Hastelloy Alloys.

**Preheat, Postweld Heat Treatment, and Interpass Temperature**
*   Preheat and postweld heat treatment are not recommended.
*   Material shall be dried prior to welding by heating to a temperature just sufficient to prevent condensation when adverse weather conditions cause condensation or wetness.
*   Interpass temperatures shall not exceed 200F.

**Welding Procedure and Position**
The welding procedure shall be agreed upon (see Para 5.3) before any production welding is begun. All welds shall be made in the downhand (flat) position, as far as practical. Vertical welds shall be made vertical up (uphand).

**Environmental Controls and Surface Preparation**
*   Suitable enclosures shall be provided to keep welds and welders free from draughts, rain or other inclement weather.
*   Welding shall not be performed on wet surfaces or with wet or damp electrodes.
*   Fusion faces and adjacent metal (internal and external) shall be free from moisture, scale, paint, grease or other foreign matter immediately prior to welding.

**Alignment**
Alignment of welding edges at butt joints shall be in accordance with relevant code, specification, etc.
*   Maximum misalignment (High - Low) for plates up to and including 1/4" thickness is 1/16".
*   Maximum misalignment (High - Low) for plates above 1/4" thickness is 25% of the plate thickness or 1/8", whichever is smaller.
*   Maximum misalignment (High - Low) for pipe shall be in accordance with ASME B31.3.

**Fixturing and Jigs**
*   Parts to be welded shall be securely held in position by mechanical means, welded-on bridge pieces or by tack welding to maintain alignment and gap during welding.
*   Jig construction shall allow adequate access for welding and cleaning and shall ensure external loading is not applied to a joint during welding.
*   Welded-on bridge or support spiders shall be of austenitic steel on the surface welded in contact with the vessel or heat exchanger. Plate surface shall not be left in a damaged condition after removal of welded-on bridge pieces or support spiders.

**Tack Welds**
*   Tack welds shall be performed by qualified welders.
*   Tack welds may form part of the completed weld if complete fusion is effected at the root and the welding procedure is the same as for the remainder of the root run.
*   Cracked and unsound tack welds shall be cut out prior to welding. Sound tack welds must be thoroughly fused into the root run of the main weld.
*   Tack welds must be crack free when thinning down ends is necessary and should preferably be at least one inch long in such cases.

**Back-up Rings, Inserts, and Run-off Pads**
The use of permanent back-up rings or strips or consumable joint inserts is not permitted unless specifically approved in writing by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Q.A. & Materials Group. Run-off pads, in the same material as the parent plate, shall be used at the beginning and end of each plate weld.

**Arc Strikes and Burn Marks**
Every effort shall be made to confine arc strikes to surfaces on which weld metal is to be deposited. Arc strikes outside the weld area shall be ground, PT examined per Para 10.3, and repaired if necessary. Burn marks from grounding clamps shall be treated as arc strikes.

**Interpass Cleaning and Restarts**
*   Each run of weld metal shall be carefully examined to ensure it is thoroughly cleaned and defect free. All slag and foreign matter shall be removed before the next weld run is deposited.
*   On re-starting, care shall be taken to ensure proper fusion with the weld metal previously deposited. All re-starts in the root bead shall be preceded by "feathering" out the area of the stop by grinding or filing. Feathering shall be followed with wire brushing to remove any abrasive residue.

**Contamination During Welding**
*   If tungsten loss occurs from an electrode contacting the work, tungsten pick-up in the weld shall be ground out, and the electrode shall be reground to the correct profile before restarting.
*   If the heated end of the filler rod becomes contaminated, it shall be snipped off and discarded.

**Post-Weld Treatment Restrictions**
Peening of weld runs is not permitted. Hammering of finished welds is not permitted.

**Weld Quality Requirements**
*   Each weld shall be uniform in width and size throughout its full length.
*   Each layer of welding shall be smooth and free of slag, inclusions, cracks, porosity, lack of fusion, and undercut (except as permitted by referenced codes).
*   The cover pass shall be free of coarse ripples, irregular surface, non-uniform bead patterns, high crown, and deep ridges or valleys between beads.
*   **Butt Welds**: Shall be slightly convex, of uniform height, and have full penetration. Internal reinforcement of pipe welds shall be subject to the limitations of the governing code.
*   **Process Side**: Crevices or undercut shall not be permitted on the process side of the weld.
*   **Fillet Welds**: Shall be deposited in not less than two layers, as far as practicable. Fillet weld surfaces shall be free from marked irregularities or concavity. Dimensions of fillet welds shall be in accordance with the approved drawing.

**Grinding and Repair**
*   Grinding shall not be carried out in such a manner as to reduce the adjacent base metal thickness.
*   Complete removal of weld defects shall be verified by non-destructive examination prior to repair welding.

**Tooling Requirements**
*   Grinding wheels shall be iron-free and of the resin bonded silicon carbide type.
*   Wire brushes or chipping tools shall be made of austenitic stainless steel and must not have been previously used to clean surfaces other than Hastelloy Alloys.
