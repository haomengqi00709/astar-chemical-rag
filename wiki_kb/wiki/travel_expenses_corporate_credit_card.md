# Travel Expenses and Corporate Credit Card

The Group Travel Procedure (11.1 PR 2) outlines the policies for managing travel expenses and the use of corporate credit cards to ensure financial accountability and efficiency.

### Travel Expenses and Allowances
Daily travel and expense allowances are established by each Company. These allowances are set in accordance with local custom and practice, typically within the limits imposed by relevant tax legislation.
*   A schedule of these allowances, broken down by country, **will** be provided by the Business Area or Company travel department, or another nominated department.
*   In all other circumstances where specific allowances are not provided, expenses **will** only be recoverable based on valid receipts. Employees **must** retain and submit receipts for all recoverable expenses.

### Corporate Credit Card Usage
The Group has designated American Express as its global corporate credit card provider.
*   All company expenses, including but not limited to airline tickets, hotel accommodation, car hire, meals, and local transportation, are to be charged to individual corporate American Express cards.
*   An exception applies if the American Express card is not accepted by the establishment; in such cases, alternative payment methods may be used, and receipts **must** be obtained.
*   In some specific locations, airline travel **will** be booked via a central billing American Express card, which simplifies the process for certain types of bookings.

### Corporate Credit Card Issuance
Corporate credit cards **should** be issued to all employees who incur Company expenses exceeding Euro 400 per annum. This threshold ensures that employees with regular business travel or expense needs have access to the corporate payment system.

Managers responsible for controlling business costs **will** follow up on any significant cost discrepancies with individual employees to ensure that the procedure is being correctly applied and that expenses are justified.
