# Coatings Document Structure (Major Work Subject 7)

The "7 Coatings" major work subject encompasses all documentation related to the specification, application, and inspection of protective coatings for plant structures, equipment, and piping. This folder is essential for managing information that ensures corrosion protection and aesthetic requirements.

The sub-folders and their contents within the 7-Coatings folder are:

*   **COR:** Correspondence. All correspondence related to coating selection, application, and issues.
*   **GAD:** Coating Plans. Drawings or documents outlining the areas to be coated, coating types, and application zones.
*   **PRC:** Project Specific Procedures. Procedures tailored to coating selection, surface preparation, application, and quality control.
*   **SPC:** Specifications. Technical specifications for coating materials, surface preparation standards, application methods, and inspection criteria.

This structure ensures that all coating-related documents, from correspondence to technical specifications, are systematically organized and easily retrievable. For an overview of the entire project documentation structure, refer to [[document_control_1_prc_0003]]. The definitions of major work subjects are outlined in [[major_work_division_codes]].
