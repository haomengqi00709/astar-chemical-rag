# Agitator Final Inspections

For agitator equipment covered by Quality Data Sheet **0-QDS-7418**, Chemetics **must** witness the following final inspections to ensure compliance with quality standards:

*   **Balancing:** Verification of proper balancing of rotating components.
*   **Run Test:** Operational testing of the agitator to confirm performance.
*   **Dimensional Inspection:** Confirmation that all critical dimensions meet specifications.
*   **Contour & Finish of Welds Inspection:** Examination of weld quality, contour, and finish.
*   **Surfaces Free from Defects Inspection:** Visual inspection to ensure surfaces are free from detrimental defects.
*   **Cleanliness Inspection:** Verification of internal and external cleanliness.
*   **Motor Inspection:** Inspection of the agitator's motor for proper installation and condition.
*   **Coupling/Belt/Gearbox Inspection:** Examination of power transmission components.
*   **Guard Inspection:** Verification of safety guard installation and integrity.
*   **Spare Parts Inspection:** Inspection of any supplied spare parts for completeness and condition.
*   **Review QA Documentation:** Final review of all Quality Assurance documentation.
*   **Packaging:** Inspection of the packaging to ensure suitability for shipment.

These witnessed inspections are a critical part of the [[0-QDS-7418_agitator_chemetics_surveillance_level|Chemetics surveillance level]] and the overall [[0-QDS-7418_agitator_vendor_quality_assurance_requirements|vendor quality assurance requirements]].

*   **Dimensional inspection:** Verification of critical dimensions.
*   **Contour & finish of welds inspection:** Examination of weld quality and finish.
*   **Surfaces free from defects inspection:** Ensuring surfaces are free from imperfections.
*   **Cleanliness inspection:** Verification of internal and external cleanliness.
*   **Motor inspection:** Inspection of the agitator motor.
*   **Coupling/Belt/Gearbox inspection:** Inspection of power transmission components.
*   **Guard inspection:** Verification of safety guards.
*   **Spare parts inspection:** Inspection of any supplied spare parts.
*   **Review QA Documentation:** Review of the vendor's Quality Assurance documentation package.
*   **Packaging:** Inspection of the final packaging for shipment.
