# Glass-Lining Plug Limits (4-SPC-0044 Table 2)

This page describes the content of `[TABLE: 4-SPC-0044_table_2]`, which specifies the maximum allowable number of plugs for repairs in the glass-lining of carbon steel reaction vessels.

**Table Content:**
The table details the permissible number of plugs based on factors such as vessel size, component type, or specific zones within the vessel. This is crucial for managing repairs to glass-linings, ensuring that the structural integrity and corrosion resistance are not compromised by an excessive number of repairs. The information in this table is directly referenced by the [[4-SPC-0044_glass_lining_application_and_quality|Glass-Lining Application and Quality Requirements]] to guide repair decisions.
