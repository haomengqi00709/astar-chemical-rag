# Travel Financial Support and HR Benefits

This page outlines processes related to financial support, such as cash advances, and HR-managed benefits like Assignment Uplift, both initiated by the Travel Authorization Form.

**Cash Advances**
*   If the cash advance portion is marked on your Travel Authorization Form, Emma Large will receive a copy of your travel request.
*   Emma Large will then generate the cash advance.
*   For short notice cash advances, contact Emma Large directly by telephone or e-mail.
*   The digitally signed Travel Authorization Form will replace the originally signed form for the purpose of processing cash advances.

**Assignment Uplift**
*   Assignment Uplift will be looked after by Human Resources (HR) according to what is indicated on your travel request form.
