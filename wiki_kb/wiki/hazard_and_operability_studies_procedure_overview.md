# Hazard and Operability Studies (HazOp) Procedure Overview

Procedure 1-PRC-0004 outlines the comprehensive methodology and timing for conducting Hazard and Operability (HazOp) Studies for projects. This procedure is critical for ensuring the safety and operability of plant designs by systematically identifying potential hazards and operational issues.

The procedure also details the mechanisms for controlling changes to the design throughout the project lifecycle, ensuring that the integrity of the HazOp study is maintained.

For a general understanding of HazOp studies, refer to [[hazard_and_operability_studies]].
