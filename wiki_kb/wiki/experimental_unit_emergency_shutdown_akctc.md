# Experimental Unit Emergency Shutdown Procedures - AKCTC

This page outlines the mandatory requirements for emergency shutdown procedures for experimental units at the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Technology Centre (AKCTC).

**Documented Operating Procedures:**
Each specific experimental unit (e.g., ClO2 generator) **shall** have a documented operating procedure.

**Emergency Shut Down Instructions:**
The documented operating procedure for experimental units **shall** include clear and concise “Emergency Shut Down” instructions. These instructions are critical for safely de-energizing or deactivating experimental processes in the event of an emergency, preventing escalation of incidents, and protecting personnel and property.

Familiarity with these emergency shutdown procedures is mandatory for all personnel operating or working near experimental units.

For general emergency response information, refer to [[0-PRC-8010_office_emergency_response_overview]].
