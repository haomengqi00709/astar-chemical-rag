# First Aid and Injury Reporting - AKCTC

This page outlines the procedures for administering first aid and reporting injuries at the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Technology Centre (AKCTC).

**Injury Reporting:**
All injuries, regardless of how minor, **must** be reported to a certified first aider as soon as possible.

**First Aid Personnel and Equipment:**
*   Only personnel with a valid First Aid (level 1) Certificate **shall** be administering first aid.
*   Only personnel with a valid First Aid (level 1) Certificate are permitted to remove items from the first aid cabinet.
*   The first aid cabinet is located in the AKCTC lunchroom.
*   A list of personnel holding a valid First Aid certificate **shall** be posted on the first aid cabinet.

**External Medical Assistance:**
If outside help is required, personnel **must** follow the procedure outlined in [[medical_emergency_response_akctc]].

**HSE Management Committee Notification:**
All injuries **shall** be reported to the [[company_hse_management_committee]].

**Injury Assessment Guidelines:**
*   Appendix G in the Lab Safety Manual contains an overview of the OSHA criteria for recording work-related injuries and illnesses. These criteria could be used as a guide by the first aider to assess accidents and/or injuries.
*   In British Columbia, WCB (Workers' Compensation Board) regulations **shall** prevail for reporting and recording accidents and/or injuries.

**WCB Reporting Forms:**
WCB-issued report forms **shall** be used when reporting and recording any accidents and/or injuries.
*   Form 6A (Worker’s Report of Injury or Occupational Disease to Employer) **shall** be filled out and submitted according to WCB regulations.
*   Form 7 (Employer’s Report of Injury or Occupational Disease) **shall** be filled out and submitted according to WCB regulations.
*   Form 7A (First Aid Report) **shall** be filled out and submitted according to WCB regulations.
*   Copies of these WCB forms are attached in Appendix H of the AKCTC Safety Manual.

For general incident reporting procedures, refer to [[0-PRC-8013_hse_incident_reporting]]. For an overview of emergency response at AKCTC, refer to [[0-PRC-8010_office_emergency_response_overview]].
