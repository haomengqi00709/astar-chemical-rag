# Preheat Furnace Package Components

The Preheat Furnace Package, as detailed in [[0-QDS-7408_document_details]], comprises several key components essential for its operation. These components are subject to the quality assurance and inspection requirements outlined in the associated Quality Data Sheet.

The package specifically includes:
*   Preheat Furnace 7590F
*   Combustion Air Fan 7592C
*   Dilution Air Fan 7593C
*   Fuel Oil Pumps 9252P-A/B
*   Fuel Oil Strainer 9253K
*   Burner & Fuel Trains, mounted on a common support frame
*   Combustion & Dilution Air Ducting
*   A free-standing Control Panel

For detailed design and specification information regarding equipment, refer to [[equipment_design_and_specification_4_prc_0003]]. Information on control systems can be found under [[control_system_design_and_specification_8_prc_0004]].
