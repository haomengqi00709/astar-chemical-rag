# Structural and Buildings Construction (0-SPC-6X93)

This page describes the requirements for Structural and Buildings Construction as outlined in document 0-SPC-6X93. The Subcontractor **must** adhere to these specifications for all structural elements and building construction activities related to the construction subcontract. This includes, but is not limited to, steel structures, concrete buildings, and architectural finishes.

Refer to [[0-SUB-6_construction_subcontract_scope_of_works]] for the specific application of this specification within the overall project scope.
