# Quality Record Definitions for Carbon Steel Tank (0-QDS-7435)

This page defines the terminology used for quality records within the scope of the Carbon Steel Tank project, as specified in 0-QDS-7435. These definitions dictate how records are prepared and managed as part of the Quality Assurance Documentation Package.

**Definitions:**
*   **AP (As Prepared):** This designation means the quality record is prepared by the Vendor and is available for review.
*   **FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package):** This designation means the quality record **must** be included as part of the final Quality Assurance Documentation Package submitted by the Vendor.

**Required Quality Records and Their Status:**
The following quality records are required for the Carbon Steel Tank, with their respective statuses:

*   **Mill Test Certificates:** Required as FP. These certificates are crucial for [[0-QDS-7435_material_verification_and_certifications|material verification]].
*   **Hydrotest Record:** Required as FP. This record documents the [[0-QDS-7435_pressure_and_leak_testing|hydrostatic pressure test]].
*   **Air/Soap Test Record:** Required as FP. This record documents the [[0-QDS-7435_pressure_and_leak_testing|air/soap leak test]].
*   **Radiography Record:** Required as FP. This record pertains to [[0-QDS-7435_non_destructive_examination_requirements|non-destructive examination]].
*   **Dye Penetrant Record:** Required as FP. This record pertains to [[0-QDS-7435_non_destructive_examination_requirements|non-destructive examination]].
*   **External Coating/Lining Inspection Record:** Required as FP. This record documents the [[0-QDS-7435_coating_and_lining_inspection|coating and lining inspection]].
*   **Review QA Documentation Record:** Required as FP. This record confirms the review of the overall Quality Assurance documentation.
*   **Packing List Record:** Required as AP. This record is essential for [[0-QDS-7435_packing_and_shipping_requirements|packing and shipping]].

For details on Chemetics' witnessing and holding requirements for these records, refer to [[0-QDS-7435_inspection_and_documentation_requirements]].
