# Gas/Gas Heat Exchanger - SS Non-Destructive Examination (NDE)

For the Gas/Gas Heat Exchanger - SS project (Document ID: [[0-QDS-7403-2_gas_gas_heat_exchanger_ss_quality_data_sheet]]), Non-Destructive Testing (NDT) and Non-Destructive Examination (NDE) **must** be performed in strict accordance with the specified industry codes and project requirements. All listed NDE activities **must** be completed.

**Applicable Standards:**
*   Non-Destructive Testing **must** be in accordance with ASME VIII Div. 1.
*   Non-Destructive Examination **shall** be in accordance with ASME Boiler & Pressure Vessel Code Sections V & VIII.

**Radiographic Testing (RT) Requirements:**

*   **Tubesheet & Expansion Joint Butt Welds:**
    *   Radiographic testing is required for **100%** of these welds.
    *   Required as "Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package" (FP).
    *   Chemetics requires Witness for Radiographic testing of Tubesheet & expansion joint butt welds.
    *   Radiographic testing for Tubesheet & expansion joint butt welds **must** be completed.

*   **All Other Pressure Envelope Butt Welds:**
    *   Radiographic testing is required as **Spot** examination for these welds.
    *   Required as "Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package" (FP).
    *   Chemetics requires Witness for Radiographic testing of all other pressure envelope butt welds.
    *   Radiographic testing for all other pressure envelope butt welds **must** be completed.

**Dye Penetrant Testing (PT) Requirements:**

*   **Tubesheet & Expansion Joint Butt Welds:**
    *   Dye Penetrant testing is required for **100%** of these welds.
    *   Required as "Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package" (FP).
    *   Chemetics requires Witness for Dye Penetrant testing of Tubesheet & expansion joint butt welds.
    *   Dye Penetrant testing for Tubesheet & expansion joint butt welds **must** be completed.

*   **Other Applications:**
    *   Dye Penetrant testing applies to the **Root pass** of all other pressure envelope welds.
    *   Dye Penetrant testing applies to the **Root pass** of tube to tubesheet welds.
    *   Dye Penetrant testing applies to both **Root & cap passes** on dissimilar metal welds.
    *   Dye Penetrant testing applies to **Lifting/tailing lug attachment welds**.

These comprehensive NDE requirements are crucial for detecting any subsurface or surface discontinuities in the welds and materials, ensuring the structural integrity and safety of the heat exchanger.
