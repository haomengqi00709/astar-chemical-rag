# SS Tubing Tier 1 Vendor Quality Assurance Requirements

For Stainless Steel Tubing Tier 1, the vendor's quality assurance program **must** comply with the requirements outlined in this Quality Data Sheet (0-QDS-7427-1) and the overarching [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment]] specification.

The vendor **shall** prepare a Quality Plan that is in accordance with [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment]]. This plan will detail the procedures and practices the vendor will implement to ensure the quality of the Stainless Steel Tubing.

Surveillance by Chemetics for this project will be conducted at Level 0.0, as specified within this Quality Data Sheet. This surveillance level dictates the extent of Chemetics' involvement in monitoring the vendor's quality activities throughout the manufacturing and inspection processes.

Compliance with these requirements is essential to ensure that the Stainless Steel Tubing meets all specified quality and performance criteria.
