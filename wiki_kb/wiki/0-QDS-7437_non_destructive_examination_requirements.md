# Field Erected CS Tank - Shop Non-Destructive Examination Requirements

For the "Field Erected CS Tank - Shop" component, Non-Destructive Examination (NDE) procedures are critical for ensuring the structural integrity of the fabricated parts.

**Radiography:**
*   The specified radiography type is **'Spot'**.
*   Radiography is required as **FP** (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package). This means all radiography reports **must** be included in the Vendor's Quality Assurance Documentation Package.
*   Chemetics surveillance for Radiography is designated as **'Complete'**. This indicates that Chemetics will perform a full review and verification of all radiography results.
*   Radiography specifically applies to **pressure envelope butt welds**.

All Non-Destructive Examination activities **must** be conducted in accordance with the applicable standards of **API 650 / API 620** or an approved equivalent standard.
