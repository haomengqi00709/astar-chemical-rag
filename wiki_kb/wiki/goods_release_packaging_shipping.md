# Goods Release for Packaging and Shipping

The Vendor **shall not** release the goods for packaging or shipping until all the activities specified in the relevant [[quality_data_sheets_qds]] have been satisfactorily completed. Additionally, the quality documentation pertinent to the goods **must** have been sent to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

For goods designated with [[quality_surveillance_levels|Surveillance Level 1]], the Vendor **must** receive an [[vendor_inspection_release|Inspection Release]] from the Inspector before proceeding with packaging or shipping.
