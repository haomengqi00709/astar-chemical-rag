# Documentation and Records for Nickel/Molybdenum/Chromium Alloy Welding

Comprehensive documentation and record-keeping are mandatory for all fabrications involving nickel/molybdenum/chromium alloys to ensure compliance and traceability.

**Required Documentation Package**
*   The fabricator **shall** furnish a report containing all the data pertaining to each vessel or item.
*   Prior to [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_company_details]]' final inspection of each unit, the fabricator **shall** have prepared in finished form three sets of records, excluding final test data.
*   Documentation **shall** include:
    *   Material mill test reports.
    *   Nameplate rubbing.
    *   Hydrostatic test report.
    *   A copy of all inspection and test reports.
*   These reports, together with final test data, **shall** be assembled into three complete sets for each item.
*   In no case **shall** a documentation package pertain to more than one vessel or item number.
*   The Inspector will collect the appropriate number of documentation packages for each item tested at the time of witnessing the final tests and certifying correctness of forms.

**Required Documentation Package**
The fabricator shall furnish a report containing all the data pertaining to each vessel or item. Prior to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' final inspection of each unit, the fabricator shall have prepared in finished form three sets of records, except final test data.

Documentation shall include:
*   Material mill test reports.
*   Nameplate rubbing.
*   Hydrostatic test report.
*   A copy of all inspection and test reports.

These reports, together with final test data, shall be assembled into three complete sets for each item. In no case shall a documentation package pertain to more than one vessel or item number. The Inspector will collect the appropriate number of documentation packages for each item tested at the time of witnessing the final tests and certifying correctness of forms.

**Material Certification and Traceability**
Certification showing results from analysis and other tests carried out in accordance with the relevant material specification shall be supplied to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for all materials, including welding consumables. Fabricator shall maintain full traceability of materials back to original mill certification.
