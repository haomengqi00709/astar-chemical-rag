# Mag Drive Pump Process Design Conditions

This page details the critical process design conditions specified for Mag Drive Pumps, as outlined in Document ID 4-DST-XXXX-R1. These parameters are essential for the correct selection and operation of the pump.

**Process Conditions**
The following process conditions are specified for the fluid handled by the pump:
*   **Fluid Description**: General description of the fluid.
*   **Composition**: The chemical composition of the fluid, expressed in %w/w.
*   **Solids Content**: The percentage of solids present in the fluid, expressed in %w/w.
*   **Solids Type**: Description of the type of solids in the fluid.
*   **Flow Rate**: The nominal operating flow rate, in m3/h.
*   **Minimum Flow Rate**: The minimum allowable flow rate for safe operation, in m3/h.
*   **TDH (Total Dynamic Head)**: The total dynamic head the pump must overcome, in m (fluid).
*   **NPSH Available (Net Positive Suction Head Available)**: The available NPSH at the pump suction, in m (fluid).
*   **Temperature**: The operating temperature of the fluid, in oC.
*   **Density**: The density of the fluid, in kg/m3.
*   **Viscosity**: The viscosity of the fluid, in cP.
*   **NPSH Required (Net Positive Suction Head Required)**: The NPSH required by the pump for cavitation-free operation, in m (fluid).

**Process Condition Categories**
The datasheet categorizes process conditions to distinguish between primary design and alternative operating scenarios:
*   **DESIGN PER UNIT**: Represents the primary design conditions for the pump.
*   **ALTERNATE PER UNIT**: Represents alternative or secondary operating conditions.

These process conditions are crucial for ensuring the pump meets the specific demands of the chemical engineering process. For general guidelines on pump process design, refer to [[horizontal_centrifugal_pump_process_design_conditions]].
