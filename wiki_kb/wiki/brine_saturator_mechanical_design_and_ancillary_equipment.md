# Brine Saturator Mechanical Design and Ancillary Equipment

This page details the mechanical design requirements, dimensional specifications, and ancillary equipment for the Brine Saturator, as outlined in Document ID 4-PDS-XXXX-R1.

**Key Dimensions and Volumes**
*   **Dry Salt Volume unit:** m3 (note 2)
*   **Working Volume unit:** m3 (note 3)

**Ancillary Equipment**
The Brine Saturator includes the following ancillary equipment:
*   Brine Distributor
*   Dip Pipe
*   Launder
*   Sparger
*   Other Equipment: weir for outlet nozzle (note 6)

**Design Requirements and Notes**

*   **Upflow Velocity (note 1.0):** Upflow velocity **must** be less than x m/h based on normal flow.
*   **Hopper Design (note 2.0):**
    *   The hopper **must** be xxxx mm (min) wide to accommodate.
    *   The angle of the hopper wall **must** be 45 degrees min.
*   **Salt Working Volume (note 3.0):**
    *   Total salt working volume **must** include x m3 in hopper plus x m3 in vessel.
    *   A minimum of 1500 mm **must** be allowed from the bottom of the vessel to the minimum operating salt level.
*   **Distributor Placement (note 4.0):** The distributor may lie on the bottom of the vessel.
*   **Outlet Nozzle Location (note 5.0):**
    *   The outlet nozzle should be at least 500 mm from the top of the vessel.
    *   The outlet nozzle location **must** provide 500 mm (min) free board for liquid.
    *   The outlet nozzle needs to be at least at the height of the inlet on the Brine Treatment Tank so raw brine can flow by gravity from the Saturator to the Treatment tank.
*   **Outlet Nozzles and Weir (note 6.0):**
    *   Outlet nozzles (2 off) **must** have a cover or weir.
    *   The cover or weir **must** extend from the top of the vessel to 1000 mm below the nozzle.
    *   The cover or weir is to prevent salt crystals from flowing into the nozzle.
    *   Two outlet nozzles are needed to prevent channelling.
*   **Hopper and Ramp Dimensions (note 7.0):**
    *   The top of the hopper "front wall" should be 2000 mm (max) above the ramp for loader access.
    *   The back wall may be to suit loader reach and angle of repose for salt.
    *   The angle of repose for salt to use is 30 degrees.
    *   Vessel height **must** provide salt working volume as per note 3.
    *   Vessel height **must** accommodate outlet nozzle location as per note 5.
    *   Ramp height should be kept to 2500 mm (max).
*   **Design Consultation (note 8.0):**
    *   Consult with process when designing the outline drawing.
    *   The hopper could be inserted into the vessel to create an annular zone of liquid above the salt to help minimise channelling.
