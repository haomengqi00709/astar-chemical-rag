# New Employee HSE Orientation

All new employees **must** undergo a comprehensive Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) orientation prior to commencing any job duties. This orientation is a fundamental step in integrating new personnel into the company's safety culture and ensuring immediate awareness of critical HSE protocols.

The HSE orientation **will** cover several key areas:
*   The Company’s unwavering commitment to Health, Safety, and the Environment.
*   Specific local health and safety information pertinent to the work location.
*   The "must obey" safety rules applicable to the worksite, emphasizing non-negotiable safety behaviors.

All engineering and construction employees **will** be provided with a copy of the construction safety handbook at the conclusion of their orientation session.
