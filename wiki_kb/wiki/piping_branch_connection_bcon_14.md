# Piping Branch Connection Specification BCON-14

BCON-14 specifies the requirements for branch connections applicable to piping specifications TT3 and TT4.

**Applicability:** Branch Connections for TT3 & TT4.

**Design Parameters:**
*   Design temperature: 149°C
*   Design pressure: Full Vacuum to 621 kPag

**Legend for Branch Connection Types:**
*   **R:** Reducing Tee
*   **T:** Straight Tee
*   **S:** Stub-in, unreinforced
*   **SX:** Stub-in with X mm wide reinforcement (cut from header pipe)

The allowable branch connections are defined in the [[5-SPC-0001_table_9]].

**Specific Fabrication Requirements for DN25 and Smaller Stub-in Connections:**
*   DN25 and smaller fabricated stub-in connections **shall** be reinforced by two gussets.
*   These gussets **shall** be positioned 90° apart.
*   Gussets **shall** be made from 40mm wide by 3mm thick strip.
*   Gussets **shall** extend 75mm along the run and the branch at 45°.

**Vacuum Service Requirements:**
For vacuum service, stiffening rings are required as specified in [[5-SPC-0001_table_10]].

**Mandatory Requirements:**
*   All branch connections **shall** be in accordance with [[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping]].
*   In the event of a conflict between B31.3 and this specification, B31.3 **shall** govern.
*   This specification applies to branch connections made at 90° only.
*   Branch connections **shall not** intersect with the longitudinal weld of the run pipe.
*   Substitutions are subject to prior approval by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

For general requirements applicable to all branch connections, refer to [[piping_branch_connection_general_requirements]].

**General Requirements**
*   The allowable branch connections are defined in the BCON-14 TABLE.
*   Substitutions are subject to prior approval by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
*   All branch connections will be in accordance with ASME B31.3.
*   In the event of a conflict between B31.3 and this specification, B31.3 will govern.
*   This TABLE applies to branch connections made at 90° only.
*   Branch connections will not intersect with the longitudinal weld of the run.

**Fabricated Stub-in Connections**
DN25 and smaller fabricated stub-in connections will be reinforced by two gussets positioned at 90° apart. These gussets will be made from 40mm wide by 3mm thick strip and will extend 75mm along the run and the branch at 45°.

**Vacuum Service**
For vacuum service, stiffening rings are required as follows: [[5-SPC-0001_table_10]].
