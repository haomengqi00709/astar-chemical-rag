# Vendor Document Review - Instrumentation (0-PRC-0007)

The [[review_of_vendor_documentation_0_prc_0007]] procedure (0-PRC-0007) governs the review process for vendor documents related to instrumentation.

**General Process for Vendor Documents:**
Vendor documents **shall** be received and logged by Document Control. Document Control **shall** forward two hard copies of vendor documents to the Project Engineer. The Project Engineer **shall** identify and sequence all disciplines required to review the document package.

The primary review of the document package **shall** be by the Field Instrument Engineer (FIE). On completion of the primary review, the FIE **shall** co-ordinate all other review comments with the assigned disciplines. On completion of all discipline reviews, the FIE **shall** compile all relevant mark-ups onto a single Red Lined mark-up set. The FIE **shall** provide the single Red Lined mark-up set to the Project Engineer.

**For Field Instruments:**
Vendor documents will be received and logged by Document Control, who will provide the FIE with a copy. The FIE **will ensure** that the documents are processed as per 0-PRC-0007. The FIE **will return** the SIGNED marked up set of drawings to Document Control.

**For Instrument Bulk Material:**
Vendor documents will be received and logged by Document Control, who will provide the Detail Design Engineer (DDE) with a copy. The DDE **will ensure** that the documents are processed as per 0-PRC-0007. The DDE **will return** the SIGNED marked up set of drawings to Document Control.

**For Mechanical Packages:**
Mechanical package Vendor Drawings received **will be made available** to the DDE for review as per 0-PRC-0007. The DDE **will be responsible** for ensuring that the Package Instrumentation Design is complete and meets the needs of the project.
