# PEM Milestone Gate Review Reporting and Follow-Up

Following a [[pem_milestone_gate_review_process|Project Execution Model (PEM) Milestone Gate Review]], a structured reporting and follow-up process is essential to document findings, assign actions, and ensure project progression.

**Reporting Timelines**
*   The Summary of Findings **must** be issued within 2 days of the conclusion of the Review.
*   The Action Plan and Risk Mitigation Plan **must** be issued within 3 days of the Summary of Findings.
*   The Report to the [[pem_steering_committee_roles|PEM Steering Committee]] **must** be issued within 7 days of the Review.

**Review Objectives**
*   Reviews **shall** be carried out to establish project performance relative to the Applicable Milestone requirements as specified in the Corporate Common PEM for each applicable [[project_execution_key_activity_groups|Project Execution Key Activity Group]].
*   Reviews **shall** be carried out to establish project performance relative to the Applicable Milestone requirements as specified in the Corporate Common PEM for each applicable [[project_management_key_activity_groups|Project Management Key Activity Group]].
*   Reviews **shall** also be carried out to establish project performance relative to any approved changes to these requirements made by the [[pem_steering_committee_roles|PEM Steering Committee]].

**Report to PEM Steering Committee - Minimum Requirements**
The report **must** include, at a minimum:
*   **Executive Summary:**
    *   Significant PEM Milestone requirements which have not been achieved.
    *   A Risk Mitigation Plan addressing identified risks, costs, and schedule impacts.
    *   Recommendations and timescales for corrective action.
*   **Detailed Findings:**
    *   A detailed report, by exception, for each [[project_execution_key_activity_groups|Project Execution]] and [[project_management_key_activity_groups|Project Management Key Activity Group]].
    *   A detailed Action Plan with nominees and agreed dates for completion within the project team.

**Action Tracking and Gate Clearance**
*   The status of the actions resulting from the milestone gate review **must** be tracked to ensure completion by the agreed date.
*   The [[project_engineer_pem_gate_review_responsibilities|Project Engineer]] **will** maintain the Action Plan current and **will** update it as actions are completed.
*   Completion of all actions documented in the Action Plan constitutes the clearing of the PEM gate.

**Report Content (Minimum Requirements)**
The Report to the PEM Steering Committee must include:
*   **Executive Summary:**
    *   Significant PEM Milestone requirements which have not been achieved.
    *   Risk Mitigation Plan addressing risk / cost / schedule.
    *   Recommendations and timescales for corrective action.
*   **Detailed Findings:**
    *   A detailed report, by exception, for each Project Execution and Project Management Key Activity Group.
    *   A detailed Action Plan with nominees and dates for completion agreed within the project team.

**Follow-up**
*   The status of the actions from the milestone gate review must be tracked to ensure completion by the agreed date.
*   Completion of all actions constitutes clearing of the PEM gate.
