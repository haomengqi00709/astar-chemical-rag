# Inspection and Documentation Requirements for 0-QDS-7801

This Quality Data Sheet (QDS) 0-QDS-7801 specifies the key items and activities requiring inspection and documentation, along with Chemetics' surveillance requirements for Type 1 Instruments.

**Key Items and Activities**
The following items and activities are subject to specific documentation and surveillance:
*   Calibration Certificate
*   Hazardous Area Certificate
*   O2 Cleaning Certificate
*   H2O2 Cleaning Certificate
*   Packaging

**Chemetics Surveillance for Items and Activities**
For the listed items and activities, Chemetics' surveillance requirements are detailed as follows:

| Item or Activity        | Vendor will issue reports as prepared | Surveillance by Chemetics | Remarks |
| :---------------------- | :------------------------------------ | :------------------------ | :------ |
| Calibration Certificate | (Not specified)                       | (Not specified)           |         |
| Hazardous Area Certificate | (Not specified)                       | (Not specified)           |         |
| O2 Cleaning Certificate | (Not specified)                       | (Not specified)           |         |
| H2O2 Cleaning Certificate | (Not specified)                       | (Not specified)           |         |
| Packaging               | (Not specified)                       | Complete                  |         |

The "Item or Activity" table includes columns for "Item or Activity", "Vendor will issue reports as prepared", "Surveillance by Chemetics", and "Remarks". The "Surveillance by Chemetics" column further includes sub-columns for "Required in Vendor I & T Plan", "Witness", "Hold", and "Complete".

Note that for Calibration Certificate, Hazardous Area Certificate, O2 Cleaning Certificate, and H2O2 Cleaning Certificate, the specific "Quality Record required" type and "Surveillance by Chemetics" requirements were not explicitly provided in the source document.

For general definitions of items and activities in Quality Data Sheets, refer to [[qds_items_and_activities_definitions]]. Further details on packaging and shipping requirements can be found in [[0-QDS-7411_packing_and_shipping_requirements]].

**Table Structures**
The document utilizes two primary tables for detailing requirements:

*   **"Item or Activity" Table:** This table includes the following columns: "Item or Activity", "Vendor will issue reports as prepared", "Surveillance by Chemetics", and "Remarks".
    *   The "Surveillance by Chemetics" column is further divided into sub-columns: "Required in Vendor I & T Plan", "Witness", "Hold", and "Complete".

*   **"Quality Record" Table:** This table includes the following columns: "Item or Activity", "Quality Record required:", "Surveillance by Chemetics", and "Remarks".
    *   The "Surveillance by Chemetics" column is further divided into sub-columns: "Required in Vendor I & T Plan", "Witness", "Hold", and "Complete".

**Key Items and Activities (Continued)**
*   **Calibration Certificate:** Identified as an Item or Activity. The Quality Record required type is not specified, and the Surveillance by Chemetics requirements are not specified.
*   **Hazardous Area Certificate:** Identified as an Item or Activity. The Quality Record required type is not specified, and the Surveillance by Chemetics requirements are not specified.
*   **O2 Cleaning Certificate:** Identified as an Item or Activity. The Quality Record required type is not specified, and the Surveillance by Chemetics requirements are not specified.
*   **H2O2 Cleaning Certificate:** Identified as an Item or Activity. The Quality Record required type is not specified, and the Surveillance by Chemetics requirements are not specified.
*   **Packaging:** Identified as an Item or Activity. The Quality Record required type is not specified. Surveillance by Chemetics requires "Complete".
