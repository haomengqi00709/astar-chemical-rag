# Quality Record Definitions for 0-QDS-7802

This page defines the terminology and requirements for quality records associated with Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7802. These definitions ensure clarity and consistency in documentation provided by vendors.

**Quality Record Status Definitions:**
*   **AP (As Prepared):** This designation for a Quality Record indicates that the document is required to be prepared by the vendor. It signifies that the record is generated as part of the manufacturing or inspection process.
*   **FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package):** This designation means that the Quality Record **must** be included as part of the final Quality Assurance Documentation Package submitted by the vendor.

**Required Quality Records:**
The following quality records are specifically required for items covered by 0-QDS-7802:
*   **Certificate of Compliance:** This document is required as an FP Quality Record, meaning it **must** be included in the final Quality Assurance Documentation Package. Chemetics surveillance requires completion for this certificate [[0-QDS-7802_chemetics_surveillance_level]].
*   **Calibration Certificate:** This certificate is required as a Quality Record. Chemetics surveillance requires completion for this certificate [[0-QDS-7802_chemetics_surveillance_level]].
*   **Packing List:** This document is required as an AP Quality Record, meaning it **must** be prepared by the vendor. Chemetics surveillance requires completion for the Packing List [[0-QDS-7802_chemetics_surveillance_level]].

The column header "Quality Record required:" in the Quality Data Sheet indicates the specific status (AP or FP) for each required record. For a full list of inspection and documentation requirements, refer to [[0-QDS-7802_inspection_and_documentation_requirements]].

*   **FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package):** This designation indicates that the quality record must be included as part of the vendor's overall Quality Assurance Documentation Package.
