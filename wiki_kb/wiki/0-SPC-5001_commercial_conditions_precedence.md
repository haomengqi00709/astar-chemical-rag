# Commercial Conditions Precedence for Purchase Orders

When interpreting the commercial conditions of a Purchase Order (PO), a specific order of precedence **must** be followed to resolve any ambiguities or inconsistencies between the various documents that constitute the PO.

The established order of precedence for interpreting commercial conditions is as follows:

1.  **Conditions set out within Section 5 of the Purchase Order Form.** These are the primary conditions and take precedence over all other commercial terms.
2.  **The Special Conditions for Purchase.** These conditions address specific requirements or deviations for a particular Purchase Order and rank second in precedence.
3.  **The Standard Conditions for Purchase.** These are the general terms and conditions that apply to most Purchase Orders and are third in the hierarchy.

Documents referenced in Section 6 of the Purchase Order Form apply only when they do not conflict with the conditions established in Section 5, the Special Conditions for Purchase, or the Standard Conditions for Purchase. In the event of a conflict, the higher-ranking document's conditions will prevail.
