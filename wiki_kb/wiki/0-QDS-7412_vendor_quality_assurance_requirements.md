# Vendor Quality Assurance and Chemetics Surveillance for Centrifugal Pumps (0-QDS-7412)

This page details the specific vendor quality assurance requirements and Chemetics surveillance levels applicable to Centrifugal Pumps as defined in Quality Data Sheet [[0-QDS-7412_centrifugal_pump_quality_data_sheet_overview]].

**Vendor Quality Assurance Compliance:**
The Vendor's Quality Assurance program **must comply** with the requirements set forth in this Quality Data Sheet (0-QDS-7412). Additionally, the Vendor's Quality Assurance **must comply** with the overarching standard [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance]].

**Quality Plan:**
The Vendor **shall prepare** a Quality Plan. This plan **must be in accordance** with [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance]].

**Chemetics Surveillance Level:**
Surveillance activities conducted by Chemetics for Centrifugal Pumps will be performed to **Level 0.0**. The specific surveillance activities **shall be as set out** in this Quality Data Sheet. This level of surveillance indicates the degree of involvement and oversight Chemetics will exercise during the manufacturing and inspection processes.

**Vendor Quality Assurance Compliance:**
*   The Vendor's Quality Assurance will comply with this Quality Data Sheet.
*   The Vendor's Quality Assurance will comply with 0-SPC-7001: Vendor Quality Assurance.
*   The Vendor will prepare a Quality Plan in accordance with 0-SPC-7001: Vendor Quality Assurance.
    *   *Note: The cells related to the Quality Plan should be deleted if a Quality Plan is not required.*

**Chemetics Surveillance:**
*   Surveillance by Chemetics will be to Level 0.0.
*   Surveillance by Chemetics will be as set out in this Quality Data Sheet.
