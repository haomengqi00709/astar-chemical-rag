# Termination of Subcontract (0-SPC-6001)

This page details the conditions and procedures for termination of the construction subcontract 0-SPC-6001, including the consequences and calculation of amounts due upon termination.

**Termination Process**
If a Party wishes to terminate, that Party **shall** notify the other, providing details of the reason. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **shall** promptly issue a termination certificate if the reason complies with this subcontract. Once a termination certificate has been issued, the Subcontractor **shall** perform no further work to complete the works.

**Reasons for Termination**
Either Party may terminate if the other Party:
*   Has become insolvent or its equivalent (Reason 1).
*   Has assigned this subcontract (Reason 2).

Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may terminate if it has notified the Subcontractor of a default in one of the following ways, and the Subcontractor has not ceased defaulting within two weeks of the notification:
*   Failed to comply with this subcontract (Reason 3).
*   Substantially hindered Aker Kvaerner Chemetics (Reason 4).
*   Substantially broken a health or safety regulation (Reason 5).
*   For any other reason (Reason 6).

The Subcontractor may terminate if Aker Kvaerner Chemetics:
*   Has not made a payment within ten weeks of the assessment day following receipt of the Subcontractor’s application for it (Reason 7).
*   Has instructed the Subcontractor to stop or not to start any substantial work or all work for a reason not attributable to the Subcontractor’s fault, and an instruction allowing the work to restart or start has not been given within eight weeks (Reason 8).

**Consequences of Termination**
Upon termination, Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may complete the works or employ other parties to do so. The Subcontractor **shall** leave the site. If the Subcontractor terminates for Reason 1, 2, 7, or 8, the Subcontractor **shall** remove the [[standard_conditions_for_construction_subcontract_0_spc_6001#Construction Equipment|Construction Equipment]].

**Amount Due on Termination**
The amount due on termination includes:
*   An amount assessed as for normal payments.
*   The cost of [[standard_conditions_for_construction_subcontract_0_spc_6001#Equipment and Materials|Equipment and Materials]] provided by the Subcontractor that are on site or for which the Subcontractor **must** accept delivery.
*   Any amounts retained by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Specific Adjustments for Termination Reasons:**
*   **Aker Kvaerner Chemetics terminates for Reason 1, 2, 3, 4, or 5**: The amount due on termination also includes a deduction for the forecast additional cost to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics of completing the works. It **does not** include any amount for costs incurred by the Subcontractor in expectation of completing the whole of the works, whether or not committed.
*   **Subcontractor terminates for Reason 1, 2, 7, or 8, or Aker Kvaerner Chemetics terminates for Reason 6**: The amount due on termination also includes 5% of any excess of a forecast of the amount due at Completion (had there been no termination) over the amount due on termination assessed as for normal payments.
