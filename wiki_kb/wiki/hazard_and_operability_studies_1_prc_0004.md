# Hazard and Operability Studies (1-PRC-0004)

This document, 1-PRC-0004, outlines the procedure for conducting Hazard and Operability (HAZOP) studies for the Project. HAZOP studies are a systematic and structured examination of a planned or existing process or operation in order to identify and evaluate potential problems that may represent risks to personnel or equipment, or prevent efficient operation.

Design reviews for the Project are conducted in accordance with this procedure, ensuring that potential hazards and operational issues are thoroughly identified and addressed early in the design phase.
