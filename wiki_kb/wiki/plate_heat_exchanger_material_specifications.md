# Plate Heat Exchanger Material Specifications

This page details the mandatory material specifications for various components of a plate heat exchanger, as outlined in Document ID 4-DST-XXXX-R1. Proper material selection is critical for ensuring the longevity, chemical compatibility, and operational safety of the equipment.

**Required Material Specifications**
Material specifications **must** be provided for the following components:

*   **Hot Side:** Materials for components exposed to the hot process fluid.
*   **Cold Side:** Materials for components exposed to the cold process fluid.
*   **Nozzle Liners:** Materials used for lining the nozzles to prevent corrosion or erosion.
*   **Gaskets:** Materials for all gaskets, ensuring chemical resistance and sealing integrity.
*   **Frame:** Material for the main structural frame of the heat exchanger.
*   **Plates:** Material for the heat transfer plates, which are the primary heat exchange surfaces.
*   **Spray Shield:** Material for any spray shields, if applicable.
*   **Nozzle Studs:** Material for the studs used in nozzle connections.
*   **Tie-bars:** Material for the tie-bars that compress the plate pack.

For general guidance on material selection processes, refer to [[material_selection]]. Examples of material specifications for other heat exchanger types can be found in [[shell_and_tube_heat_exchanger_material_selection]].
