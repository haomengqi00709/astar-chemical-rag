# Bellows Installation

Bellows installation refers to the process of correctly integrating expansion joints (bellows) into a piping system. Proper installation is crucial to allow for thermal expansion and contraction, absorb vibration, and accommodate misalignment, thereby preventing stress on the piping. Key aspects include ensuring correct installed length, proper alignment, setting of limit bolts on tie rods, and confirming adequate clearance for [[bellows_inspection_5_prc_0007|bellows expansion or contraction]] without interference.
