# Personal Harassment Definition

Personal harassment is defined as objectionable conduct or comment directed towards a specific person that serves no legitimate work purpose and ought to be reasonably known to be inappropriate. Such conduct has the effect of creating an intimidating, humiliating, hostile, or offensive work environment.

Examples of personal harassment, which are not exhaustive, may include:
*   Threats, bullying, or coercion.
*   Actual or threatened physical assault.
*   Verbal assault.
*   Malicious gestures or actions.
