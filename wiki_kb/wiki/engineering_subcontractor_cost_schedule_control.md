# Engineering Subcontractor Cost and Schedule Control

The procedure 0-PRC-2207 outlines the comprehensive methodology for controlling the cost and schedule of services provided by Engineering Subcontractors. This critical process ensures that subcontracted work aligns with project objectives and financial parameters.

The Subcontract Administrator is explicitly assigned responsibility for the control of both cost and schedule for all engineering subcontract services. This role is central to monitoring performance and ensuring compliance with contractual agreements.

The primary objectives of effectively controlling engineering subcontract cost and schedule include:
*   Ensuring the timely delivery of services as required by the overall [[project_schedule_and_milestones]].
*   Generating accurate and reliable forecasts for the total cost of the services provided under the subcontract.
*   Minimizing the effort required for close-out activities, particularly in relation to cost claims, by proactively managing potential issues.

The Subcontract Administrator **shall** prepare forecasts of the total cost of the Subcontract and of the delivery of the services, as requested by the [[project_manager_and_engineer_assignment]]. These forecasts are vital tools for project financial planning and risk management. The forecasts **must** take into account any instructions issued to the Subcontractor for which quotations have not yet been received or accepted, providing a realistic view of potential future costs and schedule impacts.
