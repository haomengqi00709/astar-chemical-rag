# Equipment Piping and Nozzle Connection Standards

Piping connections **will** adhere to ANSI standards or the equipment manufacturer’s standard. Steel bolts for tanks and vessels **will** be heavy hex hot dip galvanized. The minimum size for vessel and tank nozzles **will** be DN25.
