# Austenitic Stainless Steel Piping Fabrication Specification Overview (5-SPC-0121)

Document 5-SPC-0121 defines the quality requirements for both shop and field fabrication of austenitic stainless steel piping (300 series). This specification is primarily intended for low pressure corrosive service applications, such as those found in pulp and paper mills and chemical plants. Appendix "A" of this document extends its coverage to include Alloy 20 (UNS N08020) stainless steel piping fabrication.

This Standard **must** be applied when referenced on purchase orders, drawings, or other project documents, and is applicable to both shop and field fabricated piping and fittings.

**Applicability of ASME B31.3:**
*   For piping with a design pressure of 15 psig or greater, [[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping|ASME B31.3]] requirements **must** be satisfied in addition to this Standard.
*   For piping with a design pressure of up to 15 psig, [[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping|ASME B31.3]] paragraph 300.1 will determine the applicability of [[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping|ASME B31.3]].

**Definitions:**
*   **Codes, etc.:** Refers to the latest edition ruling at the date of contract.
*   **Agreement in Writing:** Signifies submission in writing by letter, not merely by inclusion in approval drawings.
*   **Inspector:** Means Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Quality Assurance Inspector or other Authorized Representative.
