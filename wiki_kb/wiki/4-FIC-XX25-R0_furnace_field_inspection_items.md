# Furnace Field Inspection Checklist Items (4-FIC-XX25-R0)

This page details the mandatory inspection items for furnaces as specified in the 4-FIC-XX25-R0 Field Inspection Checklist. Adherence to these requirements is crucial for ensuring the quality and safety of furnace installations.

**Mandatory Inspection Items:**
The following items **must** be verified during the field inspection:

1.  **Nameplate:** The correct nameplate **must** be installed.
2.  **Foundation/Anchor Bolts:** Foundation and anchor bolts **must** be secure and tightened.
3.  **Sliding Support:** Any sliding support mechanisms **must** be free to move as designed.
4.  **Furnace Position:** The furnace position **must** be checked for correct elevation, orientation, and levelness.
5.  **Platforms and Access:** All platforms, handrails, walkways, stairs, and ladders **must** be installed.
6.  **Safety Gates:** Safety gates **must** be installed and functioning correctly.
7.  **Non-Destructive Testing (NDT):** All NDT inspections **must** be completed. For general NDT requirements, refer to [[non_destructive_examination_requirements]].
8.  **NDT Records:** NDT inspection records **must** be available for review.
9.  **Refractory/Brick Lining:** Refractory or brick lining **must** be completed. For related inspection and documentation requirements, see [[0-QDS-7431_inspection_and_documentation_requirements]].
10. **Refractory/Brick Lining Report:** The refractory/brick lining inspection report **must** be attached.
11. **Vessel Internals:** Vessel internals, including baffles and nozzles, **must** be installed.
12. **Burners:** Burners **must** be installed.
13. **Burner Protrusion:** The protrusion of burners into the furnace **must** be checked.
14. **Vessel Cleanliness:** The vessel **must** be clean and empty prior to final closure.
15. **Final Internal Inspection:** The final internal inspection **must** be completed with the client.
16. **Closure Certificate:** A closure certificate for the final internal inspection **must** be attached.
17. **Manway Covers:** Manway covers **must** be installed and tightened (applicable for multiple covers). Refer to [[manway_covers_installation_and_inspection]] for detailed procedures.
18. **Furnace Accessories:** Furnace accessories, such as gauges, vents, drains, and sight glasses, **must** be installed.
19. **External Painting:** External painting **must** be completed. Refer to [[coatings_erection_0_spc_6x97]] for painting specifications.
20. **Insulation & Cladding:** Insulation and cladding **must** be complete. See [[insulation_erection_0_spc_6x96]] for insulation requirements.
21. **Grounding:** Grounding **must** be completed, potentially at multiple locations. For general grounding installation details, consult [[electrical_installation_details]].

**Undescribed Items:**
Items 17.0 through 24.0 are listed in the checklist but do not have specific descriptions provided in the source document. These items would typically cover additional mechanical, electrical, or instrumentation checks depending on the specific furnace design and project requirements.

**Mandatory Inspection Items:**
The following items **must** be verified as part of the field inspection:
*   Correct Nameplate must be installed.
*   Foundation/anchor bolts must be secure and tightened.
*   Sliding support must be free to move.
*   Furnace position must be checked (elevation, orientation, leveled).
*   All Platforms, hand rails, walkways, stairs & ladders must be installed.
*   Safety gates must be installed & functioning.
*   All NDT inspections must be completed.
*   Refractory / brick lining must be completed.
*   Vessel internals (baffles, nozzles etc) must be installed.
*   Burners must be installed.
*   Protrusion of burners into furnace must be checked.
*   Vessel must be clean and empty.
*   Final internal inspection must be completed with client.
*   Manway covers must be installed & tightened (multiple).
*   Furnace accessories must be installed (gauges, vents, drains, sight glasses etc).
*   External painting must be completed.
*   Insulation & cladding must be complete.
*   Grounding must be completed (possible multiple locations).
