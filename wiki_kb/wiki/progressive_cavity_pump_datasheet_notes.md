# Progressive Cavity Pump Datasheet Notes

The Progressive Cavity Pump Datasheet (Template `4-DST-XXXX-R0`) includes a dedicated section for "NOTES". This section is used to convey additional information, clarifications, or specific instructions that are not covered in the structured fields of the datasheet.

Notes may include:
*   References to other relevant documents or specifications.
*   Specific testing requirements beyond standard procedures.
*   Clarifications on material substitutions or alternatives.
*   Special handling or installation instructions.
*   Information regarding vendor-specific options or features.
*   Any deviations from standard practices that have been approved.

This section is crucial for ensuring all parties involved in the procurement, fabrication, and installation of the pump have a complete understanding of its requirements.
