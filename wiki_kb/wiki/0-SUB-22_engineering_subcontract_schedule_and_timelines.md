# Engineering Subcontract 0-SUB-22 Schedule and Timelines

This page outlines the key dates, deadlines, and scheduling requirements for Engineering Subcontract 0-SUB-22.

**Key Dates:**
*   **Starting Date:** 5 December 2002.
*   **Completion Date for the whole of the services:** 31 March 2003.

**Submission Deadlines:**
*   The period for submission of the first Schedule **shall** be two weeks after the starting date.
*   The period for submission of the quality plan **shall** be two weeks after the starting date.

**Communication and Response Times:**
*   The period for reply to a communication **shall** be 10 days.

**Document Retention:**
*   The period for retention of documents **shall** be 12 years following Completion or earlier termination of the subcontract.

**Defects Liability Period:**
*   The defects date is 52 weeks after Completion of the services. This period defines the duration during which the Subcontractor remains liable for rectifying defects.

**Activity Schedule and Assessment:**
*   The activity schedule for this subcontract is identified as 02C03000-0-LST-22XX-1-RA: [[engineering_subcontract_activity_schedule]].
*   The assessment interval for progress and performance **shall** be 1 month.

Effective management of these timelines is crucial for the successful execution of the subcontract. For broader information on project scheduling, refer to [[project_scheduling_and_resource_management]].
