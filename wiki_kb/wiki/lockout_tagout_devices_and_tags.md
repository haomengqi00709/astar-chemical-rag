# Lockout and Tagout Devices and Tags

This page provides details on the various devices and tags used in the [[0-PRC-8504-RA_lockout_and_tagging|Lockout and Tagout Procedure]].

**Danger Tags**
*   [[lockout_tagout_definitions#danger_tag|Danger tags]] block and prohibit the operation of equipment.
*   A [[lockout_tagout_definitions#danger_tag|danger tag]] **will** be placed on each properly positioned [[lockout_tagout_definitions#energy_isolating_device|energy-isolating device]] to warn employees that the device **will not** be operated.
*   A [[lockout_tagout_definitions#danger_tag|danger tag]] **may** be used without a lock.
*   A [[lockout_tagout_definitions#danger_tag|danger tag]] conforming to ANSI Z35.1, Section 5.3.1 Class 1, is used for this purpose.

**Lockout Devices**
*   [[lockout_tagout_definitions#lockout_device|Lockout devices]] **will** be used in conjunction with [[lockout_tagout_definitions#danger_tag|Danger tags]] in all cases where lockout is possible.
*   Exceptions to the lockout device requirement can only be waived by the [[lockout_tagout_roles_and_responsibilities#commissioning_hse_manager|Commissioning HSE Manager]].
*   Exceptions to the lockout device requirement are only allowed if equivalent measures and procedures have been initiated to provide for the safety of all [[lockout_tagout_definitions#affected_employee|affected personnel]].
*   A lock **will never** be used without a [[lockout_tagout_definitions#danger_tag|danger tag]].

**Extension Control Tags**
*   [[lockout_tagout_definitions#extension_control_tag|Extension control tags]] **will** be used in conjunction with [[lockout_tagout_definitions#danger_tag|danger tags]] as required.
*   [[lockout_tagout_definitions#extension_control_tag|Extension control tags]] are placed on remote operating devices to advise [[lockout_tagout_definitions#affected_employee|affected employees]] of the presence of a safety tagout at the source.
*   [[lockout_tagout_definitions#extension_control_tag|Extension control tags]] are used for informational purposes only.
*   [[lockout_tagout_definitions#extension_control_tag|Extension control tags]] **will** contain the [[safety_tagout_order_and_documentation#safety_tagout_order|safety tagout order number]] and the name of the [[lockout_tagout_definitions#authorized_individual|authorized individual]] as it appears on the [[safety_tagout_order_and_documentation#safety_tagout_order|safety tagout order]].

**General Rules for Devices and Tags**
*   A [[lockout_tagout_definitions#danger_tag|danger tag]]/lockout **will not** be placed or maintained on a piece of [[lockout_tagout_definitions#restored|restored]] equipment.
*   A piece of equipment **will not** be [[lockout_tagout_definitions#restored|restored]] until all appropriate [[lockout_tagout_definitions#danger_tag|danger tags]]/[[lockout_tagout_definitions#lockout_device|lockout devices]] have been properly [[lockout_tagout_definitions#release|released]].
*   [[lockout_tagout_definitions#lockout_device|Lockout/tagout devices]] **will** be durable.
*   [[lockout_tagout_definitions#lockout_device|Lockout/tagout devices]] **will** be standardized for each site, which can be achieved by marking, color code, or dyeing.
*   [[lockout_tagout_definitions#lockout_device|Lockout/tagout devices]] **will** be used exclusively for safety tagout.
