# Employee Responsibilities for Business Travel Safety

Employees undertaking business travel have specific responsibilities to ensure their own health, safety, and security, as well as to uphold the company's reputation. These responsibilities are integral to the [[0-prc-8102_travel_health_and_safety|Travel Health, Safety, and Security Overview]].

Before and during travel, the employee **shall**:
*   **Itinerary Familiarity:** Become familiar with their itinerary, including the route and key contacts along the route.
*   **Proactive Precautions:** Take appropriate actions and precautions, such as obtaining recommended vaccinations and implementing risk management actions as advised during the [[pre_travel_briefing_and_medical_assessment|pre-travel briefing]].
*   **Professional Conduct:** Act in a manner that does not negatively affect the company’s reputation.
*   **Legal Compliance:** Take care not to engage in any activities that may contravene local laws in the destination country.
*   **Emergency Contacts:** Be in possession of International SOS contact information and other essential emergency contact details.
*   **Maintain Contact:** Stay in regular contact with the home office (or other designated regional contact) as directed by the [[travel_coordinator_responsibilities|Travel Coordinator]].

Adherence to these responsibilities is crucial for the employee's personal safety and for ensuring compliance with company policies and local regulations.
