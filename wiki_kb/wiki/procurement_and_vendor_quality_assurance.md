# Procurement and Vendor Quality Assurance

The procurement entity is responsible for ensuring that all goods procured and delivered to the Site meet the requirements specified in the Purchase Orders. This involves meticulous planning, vendor selection, document control, and quality surveillance.

**Procurement Planning**
Planning for procurement activities is conducted in accordance with:
*   [[project_planning_0_prc_3001]]: Project Planning (0-PRC-3001).
*   [[0-prc-3002_project_scheduling_procedure_overview]]: Project Scheduling (0-PRC-3002).
*   [[commercial_terms_for_purchase_0_prc_5004]]: Commercial Terms for Purchase (0-PRC-5004).

Key deliverables from the procurement planning process include:
*   [[0-dat-5001_purchasing_and_logistics_database]]: Purchasing and Logistics Database (0-DAT-5001). Data available in SQL: 0-DAT-5001
*   [[0-dat-3001_project_schedule]]: Project Schedule (0-DAT-3001). Data available in SQL: 0-DAT-3001
*   [[special_conditions_for_purchase]]: Special Conditions for Purchase (0-SPC-5002). Data available in SQL: 0-SPC-5002

**Request for Quotation (RFQ) and Purchase Order (PO) Issuance**
The RFQ and PO documents inform Vendors of the requirements for purchased equipment and materials, typically by referencing attached drawings and specifications. Requisitions, RFQs, and POs **will** be issued in accordance with:
*   [[form_of_purchase_order_0_prc_5001]]: Form of Purchase Order (0-PRC-5001).
*   [[0_prc_5002_requisition_process_overview]]: Requisition for Quotation or Purchase (0-PRC-5002).
*   [[commercial_terms_for_purchase_0_prc_5004]]: Commercial Terms for Purchase (0-PRC-5004).
*   [[issue_of_request_for_quotation_or_purchase_order_0_prc_5005]]: Issue of Request for Quotation or Purchase Order (0-PRC-5005).

**Vendor Selection and Compliance**
The [[project_quality_objectives]] (Section 1.3 of the original document) **must** be met, and Vendors **must** be capable of meeting all requirements set out in their Purchase Orders. Vendors **will** be selected in accordance with [[vendor_bid_evaluation_procedure_0_prc_5003]]: Bid Evaluation and Approval (0-PRC-5003).

Vendors **will** be required to comply with [[vendor_document_supply_0_spc_0001]]: Vendor Document Supply (0-SPC-0001).

**Vendor Document Requirements (VDR)**
For each type of equipment or material in an RFQ/PO, the Vendor **will** be issued a Vendor Document Requirements document. This document **will** specify:
*   The documentation to be supplied by the Vendor.
*   The dates for submittal.
*   Designation of certain documents as “hold” documents. “Hold” documents require review by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics before the Vendor can proceed with fabrication.

**Vendor Document Processing and Review**
Upon receipt, Vendor documentation **will** be scanned, assigned Aker Kvaerner Chemetics document numbers, and placed in the Project electronic file. All Discipline Leads **will** be advised of the receipt. The Project Engineer **will** assign responsibility for reviewing Vendor documentation for compliance with the Purchase Order, in accordance with [[review_of_vendor_documentation_0_prc_0007]]: Review of Vendor Documentation (0-PRC-0007). Logging and control of Vendor documentation **will** be carried out per [[0-prc-0006_control_of_project_documents]]: Control of Project Documentation.

**Vendor Quality Assurance and Surveillance**
Vendors **will** be required to comply with:
*   [[vendor_quality_assurance_equipment_0_spc_7001]]: Vendor Quality Assurance (Equipment) (0-SPC-7001), as applicable. Data available in SQL: 0-SPC-7001
*   [[vendor_quality_assurance_bulk_materials_0_spc_7002]]: Vendor Quality Assurance (Bulk Materials) (0-SPC-7002), as applicable. Data available in SQL: 0-SPC-7002

A Quality Data Sheet (QDS) **will** be provided for each equipment item or material class in the Purchase Enquiry/Order. The QDS specifies:
*   Requirements for the Vendor’s Inspection and Test Plan.
*   Inspection and Testing Reports to be issued.
*   The level of Surveillance by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
*   Witness and Hold points.

Surveillance of Vendor quality may be conducted by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics staff or subcontracted to local surveillance agencies. Surveillance **will** be conducted in accordance with [[vendor_quality_assurance]]: Vendor Quality Assurance (0-PRC-7005). Owner surveillance **will** generally be scheduled to coincide with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Inspector surveillance.

**Procurement Planning**
Planning of procurement is undertaken in accordance with 0-PRC-3001: Project Planning and 0-PRC-3002: Project Scheduling.

**Vendor Requirements and Selection**
The Request for Quotation / Purchase Order informs Vendors of requirements for purchased equipment and materials, which are usually conveyed by reference to attached drawings and specifications. Project Quality Objectives stated in Section 1.3 **must** be met, and Vendors **must** be able to meet the requirements as set out in their Purchase Orders.

**Vendor Document Requirements (VDR)**
The Vendor will be issued a Vendor Document Requirements document for each type of equipment or material in the Request for Quotation / Purchase Order. The VDR will state the documentation to be supplied by the Vendor, the dates for submittal, and will designate certain documents as “hold”. “Hold” documents require review by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics before the Vendor proceeds with fabrication.

**Vendor Document Handling**
On receipt of Vendor documentation, it will be scanned, assigned Aker Kvaerner Chemetics document numbers, and placed in the Project electronic file. All Discipline Leads will be advised of the receipt of the Vendor documentation. Logging and control of Vendor documentation will be carried out in accordance with 0-PRC-0006: Control of Project Documentation.

**Quality Data Sheets and Surveillance**
The Vendor will be provided with a Quality Data Sheet for each equipment item or class of material in the Purchase Enquiry / Order. The Quality Data Sheet states the requirements for the Vendor’s Inspection and Test Plan, the Inspection and Testing Reports to be issued, the level of Surveillance by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, and identifies Witness and Hold points.

Surveillance of Vendor quality may be carried out by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics staff or subcontracted to surveillance agencies local to Vendor’s workshops. Surveillance by Owner will generally be scheduled to coincide with surveillance by the assigned Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Inspector.
