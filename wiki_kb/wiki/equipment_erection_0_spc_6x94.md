# Equipment Erection (0-SPC-6X94)

This page describes the requirements for Equipment Erection as outlined in document 0-SPC-6X94. The Subcontractor **must** adhere to these specifications for all equipment installation and erection activities related to the construction subcontract. This includes, but is not limited to, the placement, alignment, and securing of process equipment.

Refer to [[0-SUB-6_construction_subcontract_scope_of_works]] for the specific application of this specification within the overall project scope.
