# Agitator Process and Equipment Data (R1)

This page consolidates the critical process design conditions and specific equipment data for the agitator template, as detailed in document 4-DST-XXXX-R1. These parameters are fundamental for defining the agitator's operational environment and its performance capabilities.

**Quantity Categories:**
Agitators are typically categorized for procurement and installation purposes into:
*   Operating units
*   Installed Spare units

**Process Design Conditions:**
The following conditions define the process environment in which the agitator will operate:
*   Feed Material
*   Quantity (%w/w)
*   State
*   Composition
*   Temperature (oC)
*   Density (kg/m3)
*   Viscosity (cP)
*   Final Mixture
*   Type of Operation
*   Foaming Tendency
*   Degree of Agitation
*   Nature of Solids
*   Particle Size
*   Particle Settling Velocity (m/s)
*   Particle Density (kg/m3)

These conditions are crucial for the accurate sizing and selection of the agitator to ensure efficient mixing and processing outcomes.

**Equipment Data:**
The specific performance and physical characteristics of the agitator are defined by:
*   Type
*   Pumping Rate (m3/h)
*   Impeller Type
*   Shear Rate (s-1)
*   Number of Impellers
*   Power/Volume (W/m3)
*   Impeller Diameter (mm)
*   Volume Agitated (m3/h)
*   Speed (rpm)
*   Max Mixing Time (min)

This comprehensive data set ensures that the agitator's design aligns with the required process performance. For a broader understanding of process design criteria, refer to [[process_design_criteria_document]]. General guidelines for equipment data sheets can be found in [[equipment_data_sheets_4_dst_xxxx]], and specific vessel process design conditions are detailed in [[template_vessel_process_design_conditions]]. Further details on process calculations are available in [[process_calculations]].
