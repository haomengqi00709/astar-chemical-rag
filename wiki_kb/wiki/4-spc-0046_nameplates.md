# Sulphuric Acid Storage Tank Nameplates (4-SPC-0046)

This section outlines the requirements for nameplates on sulphuric acid storage tanks, as specified in 4-SPC-0046.

**Nameplate Affixing**
The fabricator **must** affix a nameplate, which **will** be supplied by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, to all equipment.

**Nameplate Specifications**
The maximum size of the nameplate is 6½ inches wide by 4 inches high. All nameplates **must** be securely fastened using stainless steel bolts or stainless steel rivets.

**Mounting Location**
Nameplates **must** be fastened to a bracket that is welded to the vessel. The specific location and details of this bracket **will** be shown on Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' drawings.
