# Plant Availability and Operation

The Plant Availability and Operation section, integral to the [[process_design_criteria_document]], defines the expected operational uptime and annual operating schedule of the facility. Specifying these parameters is critical for economic evaluations, maintenance planning, and overall project feasibility.

**Mandatory Specifications:**
*   Plant availability **must** be specified. This includes the percentage of time the plant is expected to be operational and capable of producing its intended output.
*   Other aspects of plant availability beyond percentage and days of operation per year **must** also be specified. This ensures a comprehensive understanding of what constitutes "available" and may include factors like planned downtime for maintenance, unplanned outages, or specific operational modes.

**Examples of Availability and Operation Parameters:**
*   **Example Plant Availability:** 99%. This indicates that the plant is designed to be operational for 99% of the total possible operating hours in a given period.
*   **Example Plant Operation:** 350 days per year. This specifies the typical number of days the plant is expected to run annually, accounting for scheduled shutdowns for maintenance, turnarounds, or other non-operational periods.

These specifications are directly linked to the [[plant_design_basis_and_capacity]] and influence various design decisions, including redundancy requirements for critical equipment, spare parts inventory, and maintenance strategies. A high availability target often necessitates more robust equipment, redundant systems, and efficient maintenance procedures to minimize downtime. Conversely, a lower availability target might allow for simpler designs but could impact production output and profitability. Clear definition of these parameters ensures that the plant is designed to meet its intended operational profile and economic objectives.

For example, plant availability might be specified as 99%, with plant operation expected for 350 days per year. Other aspects of plant availability beyond percentage and days of operation per year must also be specified.
