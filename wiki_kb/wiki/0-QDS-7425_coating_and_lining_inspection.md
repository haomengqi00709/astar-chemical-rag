# Fan or Blower Coating and Lining Inspection

Coating / Lining Inspection is a required item or activity for the Fan or Blower equipment specified under Quality Data Sheet **0-QDS-7425**.

Specific requirements include:
*   **External Coating / Lining Inspection:** This **is required** as [[0-QDS-7425_quality_record_definitions|FP]] (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package). Chemetics **will Witness** this inspection.
*   **Internal Coating / Lining Inspection:** This is also an item or activity that **must** be performed.

These inspections ensure that all protective coatings and linings are applied correctly and meet the specified quality standards, providing corrosion resistance and extending the equipment's lifespan.
