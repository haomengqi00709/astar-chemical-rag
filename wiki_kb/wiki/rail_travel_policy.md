# Rail Travel Policy

The Group Travel Procedure (11.1 PR 2) outlines specific requirements for rail travel to ensure cost-effectiveness and consistency across the Aker Kvaerner Group.

### Standard Class Requirement
For all rail journeys undertaken in European and Western countries, standard class travel **will** be utilized. This is the default and expected class of service for business travel by rail in these regions.

### Exceptions for Underdeveloped Countries
In underdeveloped countries, alternative levels of rail travel may be necessary due to varying standards of service. In such cases, alternative levels of rail travel, similar to standard class in European and Western countries, may only be utilized with appropriate approval from a nominated person. This ensures that any deviations from the standard policy are justified and formally approved.

These guidelines aim to balance cost efficiency with practical considerations for travel in different global regions.
