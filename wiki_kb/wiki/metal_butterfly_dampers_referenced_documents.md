# Metal Butterfly Dampers Referenced Documents

This page lists key documents referenced in Specification 5-SPC-0014 for metal butterfly dampers. Adherence to these documents is mandatory for the design, fabrication, assembly, and testing of the equipment.

**Process and Automation Requirements:**
*   Process requirements for metal dampers are specified in [[metal_dampers_process_and_automation_requirements]] (5-LST-[00XX]: Metal Dampers). Data available in SQL: 5-LST-[00XX]
*   Automation requirements for metal dampers are specified in [[metal_dampers_process_and_automation_requirements]] (5-LST-[00XX]: Metal Dampers). Data available in SQL: 5-LST-[00XX]
*   Detailed damper automation requirements are provided in [[metal_dampers_process_and_automation_requirements]] (8-DST-[5XXX]: Damper Automation). Data available in SQL: 8-DST-[5XXX]

**Environmental Conditions:**
*   The outdoor environment conditions suitable for equipment installation and continuous operation are specified in [[site_design_conditions]] (1-DST-0001: Site Design Conditions).

**Welding Standards:**
*   Welding procedures for carbon steel components **MUST** be in accordance with [[carbon_steel_welding_standard_overview]] (4-SPC-0060: Equipment Welding Carbon Steel).
*   Welding procedures for austenitic stainless steel components **MUST** be in accordance with [[austenitic_stainless_steel_welding_standard_overview]] (4-SPC-0061: Equipment Welding Austenitic Stainless Steel).

**Non-Destructive Examination (NDE) Codes:**
*   Spot radiographic examination of butt welds in the damper body **MUST** be performed in accordance with [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels]] (ASME Code Section VIII Division 1, paragraph UW-52).

**Packaging and Shipping Standards:**
*   Packaging and shipping procedures **MUST** comply with either [[packaging_land_transportation]] (0-SPC-5004: Packaging Land Transport) or [[packaging_marine_transport]] (0-SPC-5005: Packaging Marine Transport), as applicable.
