# Refractory Brick Lining Installation and Curing (4-SPC-0082)

This section of specification 4-SPC-0082 outlines the responsibilities and requirements for the installation and curing of refractory brick linings.

The installation of refractory materials in process vessels and ducting **shall** be performed by parties other than the Vendor (referred to as "Others"). This includes the physical placement and assembly of the refractory brick, insulating brick, castable, and mortar.

Similarly, the curing process for the completed refractory lining, often referred to as dry-out, **shall** also be carried out by Others.

Despite the installation and curing being performed by other parties, the Vendor **shall** provide comprehensive installation instructions to ensure proper application of the lining system. This includes detailed guidance on preparation, application techniques, and quality control during installation.

Furthermore, the Vendor **shall** provide specific dry-out (curing) curves for the refractory lining system supplied. These curves are essential for guiding the curing process, ensuring the refractory reaches its optimal properties and preventing damage due to improper drying.

Regarding site services, the Vendor is generally not required to provide any direct site services. However, any potential requirements for site services from the Vendor are subject to explicit confirmation by the Owner.

For a complete understanding of the refractory brick lining requirements, including material specifications and design details, refer to the [[4-SPC-0082_refractory_brick_lining_specification_overview|specification overview]].
