# Payment and Retention (0-SPC-6001)

This page details the payment and retention procedures under construction subcontract 0-SPC-6001.

**Price and Tax**
Unless otherwise stated, the price is exclusive of any value-added tax, goods and services tax, or other similar taxes.

**Assessment and Application for Payment**
The Subcontractor **shall** assess the amount due as of each assessment day and **shall** apply to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for payment. An assessment day occurs monthly, from the starting date until the month following the issuance of the [[standard_conditions_for_construction_subcontract_0_spc_6001#Defects Certificate|Defects Certificate]].

The Subcontractor’s application for payment **shall** include details of how the amount has been assessed. The first application is for the total amount due, while subsequent applications are for the change in the amount due since the previous payment.

**Amount Due Calculation**
The amount due is calculated as the [[standard_conditions_for_construction_subcontract_0_spc_6001#Price for Work Done to Date|Price for Work Done to Date]] plus other amounts payable to the Subcontractor (including any legally required taxes Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **must** pay to the Subcontractor), minus amounts payable by or retained from the Subcontractor.

Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **shall** correct any wrongly assessed amount due and **shall** notify the Subcontractor of the correction before making payment.

**Retention Mechanism**
An amount is retained from the Subcontractor in the assessment of each amount due until [[standard_conditions_for_construction_subcontract_0_spc_6001#Completion|Completion]]. This retained amount is the retention applied to the Price for Work Done to Date.

The retained amount is halved in the first assessment made after Completion. This halved amount remains until the assessment day following the issuance of the Defects Certificate. No amount is retained in assessments made after the Defects Certificate has been issued.

If Aker Kvaerner Chemetics requires a schedule to be submitted, one quarter (25%) of the Price for Work Done to Date is retained in assessments of the amount due until the Subcontractor has submitted a first schedule to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics showing the information required by the [[standard_conditions_for_construction_subcontract_0_spc_6001#Works Information|Works Information]].

**Payment Terms**
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **shall** pay within the later of:
*   Four weeks after receipt of the application for payment by the Subcontractor.
*   Two weeks after the next assessment day following receipt of an application for payment by the Subcontractor.

**Price Calculation**
*   Where a quantity is stated for an item in the Price List, the Price is calculated by multiplying the quantity by the rate.
*   The Price for Work Done to Date is the total of the Price for each lump sum item in the Price List multiplied by the percent complete of that lump sum item.
*   The Price for Work Done to Date is also the total of, where a quantity is stated for an item in the Price List, an amount calculated by multiplying the quantity which the Subcontractor has completed by the rate.

**Specific Retention Conditions**
If Aker Kvaerner Chemetics requires a schedule to be submitted, one quarter of the Price for Work Done to Date is retained in assessments of the amount due until the Subcontractor has submitted a first schedule to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics showing the information which the Works Information requires.
