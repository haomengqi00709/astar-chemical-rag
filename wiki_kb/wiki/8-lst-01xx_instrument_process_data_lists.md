# Instrument Process Data Lists (8-LST-01XX)

The Instrument Process Data Lists (PDL) are crucial documents for capturing and maintaining process-related data for instrumentation. They are developed in preliminary and final stages.

**Preliminary Instrument Process Data List (8-LST-01XX)**

**Content Requirements:**
The Preliminary PDL **shall** contain:
*   Instrument Tag Number
*   Instrument Service
*   P&ID Reference

**Preparation:**
*   **Prepared by:** Field Instrument Engineer (FIE)

The FIE will produce the preliminary process data lists, which will be seeded only with Instrument Tag Numbers, Service Description, and P&ID reference. The FIE is intended to provide the preliminary PDL document only once, and the record information provided is intended to remain unchanged. The accuracy of the preliminary PDL’s is dependent on the accuracy of the [[p_id_development_and_approval_1_prc_0003_instrumentation]].

**Revisions:**
Revisions to the PDL, once the Instrument Process Data List – Preliminary has been published and provided, will be the responsibility of the Process Engineer.

**Final Instrument Process Data List (8-LST-01XX)**

**Content Requirements:**
The Final PDL **shall** be produced for each type of field instrument and **shall** contain:
*   Instrument Tag Number
*   Instrument Service
*   P&ID Reference
*   Minimum/Normal/Maximum Operating conditions where applicable
*   Operating stream composition and conditions
*   Calibration Data
*   Design stream composition and conditions
*   Wetted parts Material of construction
*   Connection type, rating, and size
*   Any special dimensional requirements
*   Any special design criteria
*   In-line Instrument hydraulic performance requirements

**Preparation and Approval:**
*   **Prepared by:** Process
*   **Approved by:** Process

**Development and Maintenance:**
The Process Lead is responsible for completing and publishing the final Instrument PDL’s. Required process information and Instrument Hydraulic information will be compiled by Process and included in the PDL to meet the needs of the Instrument Specification. The Process department is responsible to ensure the PDL’s are maintained in a current state and **must** relate to the current issue of the P&IDs. Process will co-ordinate with Mechanical and Design Office to ensure that PDL data is complete.

**Data Entry Guidelines:**
*   Where the PDL allows for 3 basis of information (e.g., Minimum/Normal/Maximum), the Process Engineer **is required** to fill in all fields.
*   If information is not applicable in PDL fields, the Process Engineer will enter N/A.
*   If information is duplicated between basis in PDL, the Process Engineer will enter the equivalent data in both fields.
*   Pipe specification and size will be as per [[5-prc-0004_piping_line_list_procedure_overview]] and P&IDs, and **is not** to be duplicated in the Instrument PDL’s.
