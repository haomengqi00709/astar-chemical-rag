# Tie Point List (5-LST-0002)

The Tie Point List, identified as 5-LST-0002, is a critical document for managing interfaces between new and existing plant facilities or different project scopes. This list provides essential details for each tie-in point, ensuring seamless integration and proper design coordination.

**Content Requirements**
The Tie Point List **shall** contain the following information for each tie point:
*   **Tie Point Number:** A unique identifier for each tie-in location.
*   **Line Number:** The corresponding line number from the [[line_list]].
*   **Pressure Relief Requirements:** Details on any necessary pressure relief provisions at the tie point.
*   **Tie Point Fluid Conditions:** The process conditions (e.g., temperature, pressure, composition) of the fluid at the tie point.
*   **Tie Point Connection Size, Type, Rating:** Specifications for the physical connection, including nominal pipe size, connection type (e.g., flanged, welded), and pressure rating.
*   **Tie Point Location:** Precise geographical or plant coordinates for the tie point.
*   **Tie Point Pipe Support Requirements:** Specific requirements for supporting the piping at the tie-in location.

**Roles and Responsibilities**
The preparation, checking, and approval of the Tie Point List are assigned to specific project disciplines:
*   **Preparation:** The document **shall** be prepared by the Design discipline.
*   **Checking:** The document **shall** be checked by both the Process and Mechanical disciplines.
*   **Approval:** The document **shall** be approved by the Project Engineer.

This structured approach ensures that all tie points are thoroughly documented and reviewed, facilitating effective coordination of pipe stress requirements across battery limits and minimizing potential interface issues.
