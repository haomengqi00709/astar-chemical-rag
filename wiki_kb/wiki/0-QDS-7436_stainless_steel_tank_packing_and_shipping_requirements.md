# Stainless Steel Tank Packing and Shipping Requirements (0-QDS-7436)

For the Stainless Steel Tank project (Document ID: 0-QDS-7436), specific requirements are in place for packing and shipping to ensure the safe and secure delivery of the equipment. These activities are subject to Chemetics' surveillance.

**Packaging:**
*   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Chemetics requires "Witness" surveillance for Packaging. This means a Chemetics representative **must** be present during the packaging process to ensure it meets the specified standards for protection during transit.

**Packing List:**
*   **Requirement:** The Packing List is required to be included in the Vendor's Inspection and Test Plan (I & T Plan) and **must** be part of the [[0-QDS-7436_quality_record_definitions|Quality Assurance Documentation Package (FP)]].
*   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Chemetics requires "Witness" surveillance for the Packing List. A Chemetics representative **must** verify the accuracy and completeness of the packing list against the actual packaged items.

These requirements ensure that the Stainless Steel Tank is adequately protected and correctly documented for shipment, minimizing risks of damage or discrepancies during transportation.

**Packing List:**
*   **Requirement:** A Packing List is required as part of the Vendor's Inspection and Test Plan (I&T Plan) and is designated as "Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package" (FP).
*   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Chemetics requires "Witness" surveillance for the Packing List.
