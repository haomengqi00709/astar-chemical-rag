# Conveyors & Mills Quality Record Definitions

This page defines the required quality record statuses for documentation related to Conveyors & Mills, as specified in Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7426. These definitions ensure clarity in the submission and inclusion of quality documentation.

**Quality Record Status Definitions**
*   **AP (As Prepared):** This status indicates that the quality record is required "As Prepared" by the vendor. These documents are typically submitted independently or as part of a general documentation package.
*   **FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package):** This status indicates that the quality record **shall** be "Included in the Quality Assurance Documentation Package" submitted by the vendor. This package typically contains critical documents verifying compliance and quality.

**Required Quality Records**
The following quality records are required with the specified status:

*   **Mill Test Certificates (Major components):** Required as FP. These certificates provide essential information about the material properties of major components.
*   **Certificate of Compliance:** Required as FP. This certificate confirms that the supplied equipment or materials comply with all specified requirements and standards.

For further details on documentation requirements, refer to [[0-QDS-7426_material_and_welding_certifications]].
