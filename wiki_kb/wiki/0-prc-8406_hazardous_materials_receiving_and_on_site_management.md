# Hazardous Materials Receiving and On-Site Management (0-PRC-8406)

Upon arrival at the field, the Receiving Agent plays a critical role in verifying, handling, and managing hazardous materials to ensure site safety and compliance.

**Receiving Agent Responsibilities**
The Receiving Agent at the field **will** check all [[purchase_order_commercial_and_contractual_terms|purchase orders]] for hazardous goods identifications.
The Receiving Agent at the field **will** also check all crates for hazardous goods identifications.

The Receiving Agent **must** ensure that the goods are handled and managed according to:
*   Material Safety Data Sheet (MSDS) handling instructions.
*   Specific vendor recommendations.
*   [[safe_work_practices_for_construction_site_advisors|Safe work practices]].

**MSDS Management and HSE Notification**
The Receiving Agent is responsible for collecting a copy of the Material Safety Data Sheets attached to any crates. These collected MSDS **must be** placed in a reference binder, which **is to be** kept in the field office for easy access by all personnel.

Furthermore, the Receiving Agent is responsible for alerting the [[hse_roles_and_responsibilities_company_wide|HSE manager]] of the nature of the hazardous goods received. This notification ensures that the HSE manager is aware of potential risks and can implement any necessary additional controls or training.

Effective on-site management of hazardous materials is a cornerstone of [[company_hse_management_committee|company HSE management]] and contributes to a safe working environment.

For an overview of hazardous materials management, refer to [[0-prc-8406_hazardous_materials_management_overview]].
For details on hazardous materials labeling and documentation, see [[0-prc-8406_hazardous_materials_labeling_and_documentation]].
