# Rubber Lined Equipment Marking (4-SPC-0081)

All equipment that has undergone rubber lining, as per specification 4-SPC-0081, **shall** be clearly and permanently marked on its exterior. These markings are crucial for ensuring proper handling and preventing damage during subsequent fabrication, installation, or maintenance activities.

**Mandatory Marking Requirements:**
*   **Content**: Rubber lined equipment **shall** be clearly marked on the outside with the phrase: "Rubber Lined - Handle With Care - Do Not Weld". This explicit warning communicates the special nature of the equipment and the precautions required.
*   **Method**: The marking **shall** be applied using a paint stencil to ensure legibility and durability.
*   **Size**: The letters used for the marking **shall** have a minimum height of 75mm. This ensures that the warning is easily visible and recognizable.

These marking requirements are in addition to the general [[nameplate_order_equipment]] specifications.
