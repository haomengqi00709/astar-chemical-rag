# Pipe Support Detail Drawings

Pipe Support Detail Drawings provide specific, dimensioned designs and installation instructions for individual pipe supports. These drawings are essential for the accurate fabrication and erection of supports, ensuring they meet the structural and functional requirements of the piping system.

**Content Requirements**
Pipe Support Detail Drawings **shall** contain the following detailed information:
*   **Chemetics Approved Manufacturers and Model Numbers for Pipe Support Components:** A list of approved suppliers and their specific product identifiers for standard components used in pipe supports (e.g., clamps, hangers, spring supports).
*   **Installation Details:** Step-by-step instructions and diagrams for the proper assembly and installation of the pipe support in the field.
*   **Dimensional Details (for Custom Design Supports):** Precise dimensions and fabrication requirements for supports that are custom-designed rather than standard components.

**Roles and Responsibilities**
The preparation and approval of Pipe Support Detail Drawings are assigned to specific disciplines:
*   **Preparation:** The drawing **shall** be prepared by the Design discipline.
*   **Approval:** The drawing **shall** be approved by the Mechanical discipline.

These drawings are critical for ensuring that pipe supports are correctly constructed and installed, contributing to the overall integrity and safety of the piping system.
