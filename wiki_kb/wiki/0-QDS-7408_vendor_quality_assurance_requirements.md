# Preheat Furnace Vendor Quality Assurance Requirements

For the Preheat Furnace Package, the Vendor's Quality Assurance (QA) program **must** comply with the requirements specified in this Quality Data Sheet (0-QDS-7408) and with **0-SPC-7001: Vendor Quality Assurance**.

The Vendor **shall** prepare a Quality Plan in accordance with [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment]]. This Quality Plan is a critical component of the overall [[vendor_quality_assurance]] framework.

**Note:** A Quality Plan is not required if its corresponding cells within the Quality Data Sheet are explicitly deleted, indicating that the requirement has been waived for specific items.

Vendor's Quality Assurance compliance is associated with a value of 0.0 in relevant documentation.
