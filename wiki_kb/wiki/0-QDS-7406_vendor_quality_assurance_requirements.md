# 0-QDS-7406 Vendor Quality Assurance Requirements

For the Catalyst materials covered by this Quality Data Sheet, the Vendor's Quality Assurance program **must** adhere to specific requirements.

The Vendor **must** prepare a Quality Plan in accordance with the guidelines set forth in [[0-SPC-7002_vendor_quality_assurance_bulk_materials]].

Furthermore, the Vendor's overall Quality Assurance program **shall** comply with the stipulations outlined in this Quality Data Sheet (0-QDS-7406) and with [[0-SPC-7002_vendor_quality_assurance_bulk_materials]]. This ensures a consistent and documented approach to quality throughout the procurement and supply process.

Related topics include [[0-QDS-7406_chemetics_surveillance_level]] and [[0-QDS-7406_inspection_and_documentation_requirements]].

Furthermore, the Vendor's Quality Assurance program **must** comply with the requirements set out in this Quality Data Sheet.

**Note on Quality Plan:** If a Quality Plan is not required for a specific scope, any related cells or sections within this document should be deleted.
