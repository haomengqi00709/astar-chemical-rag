# 4-SCR-0022 Centrifugal Pump on Steel Unfactored Equipment Loads

This section details the unfactored equipment loads for the centrifugal pump on steel, as specified in document 4-SCR-0022. These loads represent the inherent characteristics of the assembled equipment without applying any safety or design factors.

**Unfactored Equipment Loads:**
*   **Assembled Pump Mass:** Includes the motor and other associated components, measured in kg.
*   **Power:** The operational power requirement, measured in kW.
*   **Full Load Speed:** The rotational speed of the pump at full load, measured in RPM.

All specified loads **must** be applied at the designated Datum point for structural analysis.

For the basis of these support criteria, refer to [[4-SCR-0022_centrifugal_pump_support_criteria_basis]]. General information on equipment support criteria can be found in [[equipment_support_criteria]].

*   **Power:** in kW
*   **Full Load Speed:** in RPM
