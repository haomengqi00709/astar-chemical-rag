# General Personnel Assignment Principles

Beyond specific leadership roles, the assignment of other project personnel and the management of responsibilities adhere to several guiding principles.

**Mandatory Requirements:**
*   Respective Department Managers **shall** assign other project personnel as required to meet project demands.

Responsibilities may be reassigned throughout a project's lifecycle to achieve various objectives, including balancing individual workloads, fostering personal development, or accommodating specific project needs and changes.

**Mandatory Requirements:**
*   All assignments **shall** be made on the basis of an individual's experience, maturity, and demonstrated accuracy in their field.

For detailed commentary regarding the basis for assignments, refer to Section 5 of the [[0-prc-2001_document_overview|0-PRC-2001 procedure]].

It is permissible for a single individual to be assigned multiple responsibilities within their discipline, provided they possess adequate experience and the project's size and scope allow for such consolidation of duties.

These principles ensure that project teams are staffed effectively and responsibilities are managed dynamically to support project success.
