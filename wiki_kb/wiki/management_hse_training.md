# Management and Supervisory HSE Training

Management and supervisory personnel play a critical role in upholding the company's Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) standards. Therefore, specific training and orientation programs are mandated for these roles.

**Senior Management Orientation:**
Senior Managers **will** receive an orientation specifically covering their HSE responsibilities. This orientation includes:
*   Reference to major program elements and standards.
*   Emphasis on HSE leadership.
*   The manager’s specific program responsibilities.
*   The critical HSE activities of their subordinates.

New members of senior management **will** be given 4 hours of orientation to the safety and health/loss control program. This Senior Management HSE orientation **will** cover all elements of the company's HSE program as identified in the company's HSE policy document. It **will** also cover the purpose and practices of the program, providing an overview of the executive's specific accountability and responsibilities. This briefing **should** occur during the first week of the senior manager's assignment and **will** be delivered by the senior manager's superior or their representative. The senior manager **will** also be provided with a copy of the company HSE Manual.

**Senior Management Review Training:**
Ten hours of formal review training **will** be provided to Senior Management personnel every three years, following the date of their original formal training.

**Supervisory/Middle Management Initial Training:**
Formal initial training for Supervisory and Middle Management **will** emphasize the applied aspects of:
*   On-the-job orientation of employees.
*   Task instruction.
*   Safety contacts.
*   Job risk analysis.
*   Employee training.
*   Group meetings.
*   Inspections.
*   Investigations.
*   Task observation.
This training **will** also specifically emphasize how to effectively use the HSE Manual.

**Supervisory/Middle Management Review Training:**
Ten hours of formal review training **will** be given to Supervisory and Middle Management every three years, following the date of their original formal training.

These training requirements are integral to [[project_hse_training_planning]] and the broader [[0-PRC-8016_hse_training_program_overview]].

Construction Managers and Site HSE Managers are responsible for developing and maintaining a dedicated safety-training file for each management/supervisory employee. These files **will** utilize Form DP-802.305, the Safety Training History Record.
