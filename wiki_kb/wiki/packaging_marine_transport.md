# Packaging Marine Transport (0-SPC-5004)

Data available in SQL: 0-SPC-5004

The document 0-SPC-5004 R1, titled "Packaging Marine Transport," specifies the mandatory requirements for packaging goods that are to be transported by marine vessel for Project 99H10600. This specification is crucial for ensuring the safe and secure delivery of equipment and materials, protecting them from damage during transit, handling, and storage in potentially harsh marine environments.

Key requirements typically covered in this packaging specification include:
*   **Packaging Materials:** Specifications for types of crating (e.g., wooden crating), pallets, wrapping materials, and cushioning.
*   **Protection Against Elements:** Requirements for moisture barriers, desiccants, and corrosion protection to safeguard against humidity, salt spray, and temperature fluctuations.
*   **Handling and Lifting:** Provisions for adequate lifting points, slings, and handling instructions to facilitate safe loading, unloading, and movement.
*   **Marking and Labeling:** Clear and durable marking requirements for package contents, destination, weight, dimensions, and special handling instructions (e.g., "Fragile," "This Way Up").
*   **Securing and Bracing:** Methods for securing items within packages and bracing packages within containers or on vessels to prevent movement and damage.
*   **Dangerous Goods:** Specific packaging and labeling requirements for any hazardous materials in accordance with international marine transport regulations.

Vendors **must** supply packaging in accordance with the requirements set out in this specification. This document is a key reference in Text Section 3: Delivery of Purchase Orders, ensuring that all goods are adequately prepared for marine transport.
