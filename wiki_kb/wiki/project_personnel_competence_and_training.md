# Project Personnel Competence and Training

Ensuring the competence and proper training of all personnel assigned to the Project is a critical aspect of quality management.

**Personnel Competence and Assignment**
Personnel assigned to work on the Project **must** be competent, demonstrating the necessary education, training, skills, and experience for their roles. Specific responsibilities for project personnel are detailed in Section 5.4 of the original document and are summarized in the [[0-prc-2005_project_responsibility_document]].

Assignments of personnel to positions of responsibility for the Project are formally recorded. This process is carried out in accordance with:
*   [[assignment_of_project_responsibilities]]: Assignment of Project Responsibilities (0-PRC-2001).
*   [[0-lst-2004_project_responsibility_form]]: Project Responsibility (0-LST-2004). Data available in SQL: 0-LST-2004

**Training Requirements**
All employees involved in the Project **must** be trained in the use and requirements of standard documents. This training is conducted in accordance with [[standard_document_management_procedure_0_prc_0001]]: Approval and Use of Standards. Chief Engineers and Department Managers are responsible for maintaining a log of training received by each employee within their respective departments.

In addition to standard document training, all personnel assigned to the Project **must** receive specific training on:
*   The project-specific requirements outlined in this procedure (0-PRC-7003).
*   All other applicable referenced procedures.

This additional training is typically delivered through group presentations and discussions to ensure comprehensive understanding and consistent application across the team.

The Project Engineer is responsible for maintaining a register that includes details of all project personnel, their work assignments, and the training they have received. This register serves as a vital record for competence assurance and compliance.
