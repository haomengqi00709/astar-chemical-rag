# ASME B31.1 Power Piping

ASME B31.1 is a widely recognized standard for the design, fabrication, installation, examination, and testing of power piping systems. This code specifically applies to piping systems typically found in:
*   Electric power generating stations.
*   Industrial and institutional plants.
*   Geothermal heating systems.
*   Central and district heating systems.

The standard covers various aspects including material selection, component design, fabrication methods (such as welding and bending), inspection requirements, and pressure testing procedures to ensure the safe and reliable operation of power piping.
