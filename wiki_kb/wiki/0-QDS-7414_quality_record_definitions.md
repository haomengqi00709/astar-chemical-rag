# FRP Tank Quality Record Definitions (0-QDS-7414)

This page defines the specific quality record designations used within the FRP Tank Quality Data Sheet, document **0-QDS-7414**. These definitions are crucial for understanding the documentation requirements for various inspection and testing activities.

**Quality Record Definitions:**

*   **AP (As Prepared):** This designation means that the quality record is required to be "As Prepared" by the vendor. It signifies that the document or record must be generated and maintained by the vendor as part of their quality control process.
    *   *Example:* The [[0-QDS-7414_packing_and_shipping_requirements#Packing List]] requires a Quality Record AP.

*   **FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package):** This designation means that the quality record is required to be "Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package". It signifies that the document or record must be compiled and submitted as part of the final Quality Assurance Documentation Package provided by the vendor.
    *   *Example:* [[0-QDS-7414_material_verification_and_certifications#Material Test Certificates]] require a Quality Record FP.

These definitions align with general quality record management practices, which are further elaborated in documents such as [[0-QDS-7411_quality_record_definitions]]. Understanding these designations is essential for ensuring proper documentation and traceability throughout the FRP tank manufacturing and supply process.
