# Project Schedule Detail Levels (Level 1, 2, and 3)

The level of detail provided in a project schedule is systematically denoted by specific nomenclature, ranging from high-level summaries to granular work activities.

### Level 1 Schedule (Summary Schedule)
The Level 1 Schedule, also known as a Summary Schedule, provides a high-level overview of the project. It features:
*   One activity bar for each major work division for engineering.
*   One activity bar for each major work division for procurement.
*   One activity bar for each major work division for construction.
*   A single activity bar for commissioning.
*   All major project milestones.

Activities presented in a Level 1 Schedule are derived as summaries of corresponding activities detailed in a Level 2 Schedule.

### Level 2 Schedule (Control Schedule)
The Level 2 Schedule, also known as a Control Schedule, offers a more detailed view than Level 1. Key characteristics include:
*   Grouped work activities for items such as equipment, pipes, and instruments, organized either by type or by functional area.
*   Typically, up to 20 activities are shown for each major work division.
*   All project milestones are explicitly shown.

Activities in a Level 2 Schedule are derived as summaries of corresponding activities in a Level 3 Schedule. This level of detail is mandated for external reporting: the Level 2 Schedule is the designated format for reporting to the Owner and to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics senior management.

### Level 3 Schedule (Detailed Schedule)
The Level 3 Schedule, referred to as a Detailed Schedule, provides the most granular view of project activities. It includes:
*   All individual work activities for items such as equipment, pipes, and instruments, grouped by type or by functional area.
*   All project milestones are explicitly shown.
