# Demineralized-Softener Utility Specifications

This page details the utility requirements for the demineralized and softener systems, as outlined in Document ID 4-PDS-XXXX-R1. These specifications are crucial for the proper operation, regeneration, and control of the water treatment package.

**Hydrochloric Acid (HCl) Supply Specifications**
Hydrochloric acid is typically used as a regenerant for cation exchange resins in demineralization systems. The specifications for its supply are:

*   **HCl Supply Concentration:** The concentration of the hydrochloric acid solution, expressed in percent weight by weight (%w/w). This affects the amount of regenerant required and the dilution process.
*   **HCl Supply Pressure:** The pressure at which the HCl is supplied to the system, measured in kilopascals gauge (kPa(g)). This is important for ensuring adequate flow for regeneration.
*   **HCl Supply Temperature:** The temperature of the incoming HCl solution, in degrees Celsius (oC). Temperature can influence reaction rates and material compatibility.

**Sodium Hydroxide (NaOH) Supply Specifications**
Sodium hydroxide (caustic soda) is commonly used as a regenerant for anion exchange resins in demineralization systems. The specifications for its supply are:

*   **NaOH Supply Concentration:** The concentration of the sodium hydroxide solution, expressed in percent weight by weight (%w/w). Similar to HCl, this impacts regenerant dosage and dilution.
*   **NaOH Supply Pressure:** The pressure at which the NaOH is supplied to the system, measured in kilopascals gauge (kPa(g)). Adequate pressure is needed for effective regeneration.
*   **NaOH Supply Temperature:** The temperature of the incoming NaOH solution, in degrees Celsius (oC).

**Instrument Air Specifications**
Instrument air is vital for the operation of pneumatic control valves and instrumentation within the demineralized/softener package.

*   **Instrument Air:** The required supply pressure for instrument air, specified in kilopascals gauge (kPa(g)). Consistent and clean instrument air is essential for reliable [[control_philosophy_and_dcs|control system]] operation.

These utility specifications ensure that the necessary chemical regenerants and control media are available at the correct conditions for the efficient and effective functioning of the demineralized and softener units.
