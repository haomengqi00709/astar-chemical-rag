# Stack Quality Data Sheet Non-Destructive Examination Requirements

Non-Destructive Examination (NDE) requirements for the Stack component (Item 8.0) are critical for verifying material integrity and weld quality without causing damage. All NDE **must** be performed in accordance with ANSI/AWS Structural Welding Code D1.1 or an approved equivalent standard.

**Radiographic Testing (Item 9.0):**
*   **Method:** Spot radiography.
*   **Coverage:** 1 spot in every 50 feet of each butt weld category.
*   **Quality Record:** Radiographic testing **requires** Quality Record FP ([[0-QDS-7410_quality_record_definitions]]).
*   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Radiographic testing **requires** Chemetics surveillance to Witness (ü).

**Dye Penetrant Testing:**

*   **100% Dye Penetrant Testing (Item 10.0):**
    *   **Coverage:** 100% for dissimilar metal butt welds, specifically for the root and final passes.
    *   **Quality Record:** This testing **requires** Quality Record FP ([[0-QDS-7410_quality_record_definitions]]).
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance:** This testing **requires** Chemetics surveillance to Witness (ü).

*   **25% Dye Penetrant Testing (Item 11.0):**
    *   **Coverage:** 25% for Stainless Steel (SS) butt welds, specifically for the cover pass.
    *   **Quality Record:** This testing **requires** Quality Record FP ([[0-QDS-7410_quality_record_definitions]]).
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance:** This testing **requires** Chemetics surveillance to Witness (ü).

These NDE requirements ensure that critical welds and material interfaces meet the specified quality standards. For general NDE guidelines, refer to [[non_destructive_examination_requirements]]. Specific NDE requirements for other heat exchangers can be found in [[gas_gas_heat_exchanger_ss_non_destructive_examination]].
