# Chemetics Surveillance Level for Polypropylene Tank (0-QDS-7424)

This page defines the Chemetics surveillance level applicable to the Polypropylene Tank project, as specified in Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7424. The surveillance level dictates the extent of oversight and inspection activities performed by Chemetics personnel or their designated representatives.

**Surveillance Level Details:**
*   Surveillance by Chemetics **shall** be conducted to Level 0.0.
*   The specific details and scope of this surveillance **shall** be as explicitly set out within this Quality Data Sheet (0-QDS-7424).

A Level 0.0 surveillance typically indicates a minimal level of direct Chemetics involvement in routine inspections, often relying on the vendor's established quality control processes, but with specific witness points as defined in the QDS. This approach ensures that critical quality checkpoints are still observed while optimizing resource allocation.

For further context on the overall quality administration of this project, please refer to [[0-QDS-7424_polypropylene_tank_quality_data_sheet_administration]].

**Specific Surveillance Activities:**
*   **Review QA Documentation:** Requires Chemetics Witness surveillance.
