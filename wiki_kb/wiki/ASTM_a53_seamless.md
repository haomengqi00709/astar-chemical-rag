# ASTM A53 (Seamless)

ASTM A53 is a standard specification for pipe, steel, black and hot-dipped, zinc-coated, welded and seamless. This specification covers seamless and welded black and hot-dipped galvanized steel pipe in NPS 1/8 to NPS 26.

**Key Characteristics (Seamless):**
*   **Seamless Pipe:** Produced without a weld seam, typically by hot working or cold drawing.
*   **Material:** Carbon steel.
*   **Applications:** Suitable for pressure applications, mechanical applications, and general purpose use for conveying steam, water, gas, and air.

**Application in Tank Fabrication:**
*   Materials used in the manufacture of sulphuric acid storage tanks may reference ASTM A53 (Seamless) for various piping or structural components.
