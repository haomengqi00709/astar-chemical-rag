# HCl Synthesis Unit Product Acid Specifications

This page details the quality and operational specifications for the product acid generated by the HCl Synthesis Unit, as outlined in [[4-PDS-XXXX-R1_template_hcl_synthesis_unit_package_datasheet_administration]]. These specifications are crucial for ensuring the product meets client requirements and for downstream processing.

**Product Acid Parameters:**
The hydrochloric acid produced by the unit **must** meet the following specifications:
*   **Composition:** 32 +/- 0.5 %w/w HCl
*   **Capacity as 100% HCl:** xx MTPD (refer to [[hcl_synthesis_unit_process_design_basis]])
*   **Maximum Cl2 Content:** 5.0 ppm (v/v)
*   **Turndown Capacity:** 25% of Design or less, VTA
*   **Maximum Temperature:** 45.0 oC
*   **Pressure (Note 2):** VTA kPa(g)

The product acid composition of 32 +/- 0.5 %w/w HCl is a critical quality parameter, ensuring consistency for end-use applications. The maximum chlorine content of 5.0 ppm (v/v) is a strict limit to prevent product contamination. The turndown capacity allows for flexible operation. The maximum temperature of 45.0 oC is important for material integrity and safety. The product acid pressure is subject to **Note 2**, which may provide further details on operating conditions or system constraints.

Adherence to these specifications is mandatory for the acceptance and usability of the synthesized HCl.
