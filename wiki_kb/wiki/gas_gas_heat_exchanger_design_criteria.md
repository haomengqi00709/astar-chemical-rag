# Gas/Gas Heat Exchanger Design Criteria

The establishment of robust design criteria is a mandatory initial step for the mechanical calculations of gas/gas heat exchangers. The designer **must** adhere to the following principles to ensure the safety and reliability of the equipment.

**Operating Conditions:**
The designer **must** consider all potential operating conditions, including:
*   Startup phases
*   Shutdown procedures
*   Plant upset conditions

The primary objective is to determine the worst-case combination of pressure and temperature that the equipment will experience. Operating conditions for exchanger sizing, configuration, temperatures, and pressures are determined by [[4-CAL-0001_heat_exchanger_performance_calculations]].

**Pressure Boundary Design:**
*   Components that form the pressure boundary **shall** be designed for the maximum pressure anticipated.
*   Components not on the pressure boundary **shall** be designed for the maximum differential pressure present. This maximum differential pressure **must** account for all operating conditions, including startup and shutdown scenarios.

**Design Criteria Establishment:**
Design criteria **must** be established as outlined in this procedure. This involves a comprehensive analysis of all factors that could impact the mechanical integrity of the heat exchanger.

For detailed information on how design and mean metal temperatures are determined, refer to [[gas_gas_heat_exchanger_design_metal_temperatures]]. The overall mechanical calculation procedure is outlined in [[4-prc-0009_gas_gas_exchanger_mechanical_calculations]].
