# Ejector Mechanical Checkout Requirements

This page outlines the mandatory requirements for the mechanical checkout of ejectors, designated by suffix V. This process is a key part of the overall [[mechanical_equipment_checkout_procedure]].

**Required Documents:**
The following documents are mandatory for the checkout of ejectors:
*   [[piping_and_instrumentation_drawings|P&ID]]
*   Vessel Outline drawing
*   [[equipment_data_sheets_4_dst_xxxx|Equipment data sheet]]

**General Installation and Structural Checks:**
*   **Mandatory:** Ejector nameplate data and record **must** match the outline drawing/data sheet information.
*   **Mandatory:** Ejector nameplate tag **must** match tag drawing.
*   **Mandatory:** Ejector and adjacent piping **must** be adequately supported.
*   **Mandatory:** Ejector **must** be accessible on all sides.

**Surface Treatment and Lining:**
*   **Mandatory:** Carbon steel vessels **must** have been primed and painted.
*   **Mandatory:** Vessels **must** be checked for gouges, cuts, scratches etc.
*   **Mandatory:** [[coating_and_lining_inspection|Lining]] **must** be continuous.
*   **Mandatory:** Lining inserts **must** be sealed.
*   **Mandatory:** Lining seams **must** be smooth and continuous.

**Grounding:**
*   **Mandatory:** [[grounding_installation_details|Grounding cable]] connection **must** be tight.
*   **Mandatory:** Grounding cable **must** be connected to the main grounding loop.

**Insulation:**
*   **Mandatory:** Ejector [[insulation_design_and_specification_6_prc_0002|insulation material]] **must** be checked against specification.
*   **Mandatory:** Insulation thickness **must** be checked.
*   **Mandatory:** Insulation sheet **must** cover the surface and all connections **must** be made.

**Nozzles and Flanges:**
*   **Mandatory:** Nozzle size and orientation **must** match drawing, particularly the flow direction.
*   **Mandatory:** Nozzle gasket and thickness **must** match outline drawing/data sheet.
*   **Mandatory:** Nozzles **must** be open from the inside of vessel and no foreign debris **must** be present.
*   **Mandatory:** Ejector flange **must** be aligned to piping.
*   **Mandatory:** [[flange_bolting_requirements|Flange bolts]] **must** be adequately tightened.
*   **Mandatory:** Flange bolts **must not** have been overtightened (i.e. damage to gasket).
*   **Mandatory:** The nut **must** have at least three rounds of pipe thread protruding past the nut.
