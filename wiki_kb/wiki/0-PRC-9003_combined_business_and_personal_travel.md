# Combined Business and Personal Travel (0-PRC-9003)

Combining business travel with personal time (e.g., vacation, [[0-pol-9113_banked_time_policy|banked time]], weekend days) is permissible, provided it receives prior approval from the employee’s direct supervisor.

**Financial Offsets and Documentation**
Financial offsets **must** be identified and agreed upon before the personal portion of the trip commences.
*   Any additional travel costs incurred by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics that exceed the amount payable for business travel alone **must** be shown as a credit on the employee’s Expense Report.
*   Conversely, any reduction in travel costs incurred by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics below the amount payable for business travel alone may be used to offset the employee’s costs for hotels and meals during personal time. To the extent these costs are reimbursed, they **must** be shown on the employee’s Expense Report as for any other business travel expense.
*   Receipts **must** be provided for all reimbursed costs incurred during personal time, as described in [[0-PRC-9003_expense_report_submission_and_documentation|Section 5]] of this procedure.

The Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Travel Arranger **shall** provide fare quotations for both the base business travel case and the travel actually booked. These fare quotations **must** be attached to the employee’s Expense Report to support any additional costs credited or costs reimbursed while on personal time.
