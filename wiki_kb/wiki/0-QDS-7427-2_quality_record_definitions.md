# Stainless Steel Tubing Tier 2 Quality Record Definitions

This section defines the requirements for Quality Records pertaining to Stainless Steel Tubing Tier 2.

**Quality Record Status Definitions:**
*   **AP = "As Prepared":** Indicates that the quality record is required to be prepared by the Vendor.
*   **FP = "Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package":** Indicates that the quality record **shall** be included as part of the final Quality Assurance Documentation Package submitted by the Vendor.

**Specific Quality Record Requirements:**

**Records Required (FP or AP):**
*   Mill Test Certificates (FP)
*   Corrosion Test Reports (FP)
*   Material Testing (FP)
*   Micrographs of Welds (AP)
*   Hydrostatic testing (FP)
*   Air Under Water testing (FP)
*   Dye Penetrant - Tube ends testing (FP)
*   Dye Penetrant - Cut Lengths testing (FP)
*   Dimensional inspection (FP)
*   Ring Gauge inspection (FP)
*   Packing List (AP)

**Records Not Required:**
No Quality Record is required for the following inspection activities:
*   Contour and finish of welds
*   Surfaces free from defects
*   Cleanliness
*   Item Identification
*   Review QA Documentation
*   Packaging
*   Truck Loading
*   Ship Loading

For general quality record definitions, refer to [[0-QDS-7_quality_record_definitions]]. All quality records **shall** be managed as part of the [[0-QDS-7427-2_vendor_quality_assurance_requirements]].
