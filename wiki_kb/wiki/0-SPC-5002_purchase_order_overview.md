# Purchase Order Overview and General Conditions (0-SPC-5002)

This document, identified as **0-SPC-5002**, serves as a Specification outlining the general conditions for Purchase Orders. It defines the roles, responsibilities, and foundational terms governing the relationship between Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and its Vendors.

**Parties and Definitions:**
The primary *Parties* to this Purchase Order are Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the *Vendor*. Other key entities include:
*   **Aker Kvaerner Chemetics:** The purchasing entity.
*   **Vendor:** The party providing the *Goods and Services*.
*   **Project Buyer:** A representative of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics responsible for commercial aspects.
*   **Inspector:** A representative of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics responsible for quality verification.
*   **Lead Engineer:** A representative of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics responsible for technical design acceptance.
*   **Project:** The facilities being constructed, designed, and/or procured by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for the *Owner*.
*   **Owner:** The party with whom Aker Kvaerner Chemetics has contracted to execute the *Project*.
*   **Others:** Any persons or organizations not explicitly defined as one of the above parties or their employees/suppliers.
*   **Supplier:** A person or entity contracted by the *Vendor* to provide a part of the *Goods and Services*.

All parties, including Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, the *Vendor*, the *Project Buyer*, the *Inspector*, and the *Lead Engineer*, **must** act in a spirit of mutual trust and cooperation.

**Key Dates:**
*   **Purchase Order Date:** The date the Purchase Order came into existence.
*   **Starting Date:** The date specified on the Purchase Order, from which the Purchase Order is effective.

**General Interpretation:**
*   Terms identified in the Purchase Order are in *italics*.
*   Defined terms have capital initials.
*   Words in the singular also mean in the plural and vice versa, unless the context dictates otherwise.
*   Words in the masculine also mean in the feminine and neuter, unless the context dictates otherwise.
*   "Person" includes any legal entity, unless the context dictates otherwise.

**Document Formation and Acceptance:**
The documents forming part of the Purchase Order are specified within the Purchase Order itself. The *Vendor* accepts the conditions of the Purchase Order by acknowledging receipt or by commencing to *Provide the Goods and Services*.

**Governing Law and International Conventions:**
This Purchase Order is governed by the law of British Columbia, Canada. The [[incoterms_2000_application|Incoterms 2000]] apply to the Purchase Order. The [[un_convention_international_sale_of_goods|United Nations Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods]] does not apply to this Purchase Order.

**Document Precedence:**
The order of precedence for interpreting commercial conditions is as follows:
1.  Conditions set out within Section 5 of the Purchase Order Form.
2.  The [[special_conditions_for_purchase_0_spc_5002|Special Conditions for Purchase]].
3.  The [[standard_conditions_for_purchase|Standard Conditions for Purchase]].

Documents referenced in Section 6 of the Purchase Order Form apply only when they do not conflict with other documents that are part of the Purchase Order.
