# Metal Ductwork Leak Testing (5-SPC-0010)

Unless otherwise specified, all ducting will be leak tested by others, after installation, under initial service conditions.

The Vendor **shall** be responsible for the remediation of any defects in the fabricated duct spools discovered during the initial service leak test. This responsibility underscores the importance of quality fabrication as outlined in [[5-SPC-0010_metal_ductwork_fabrication_and_assembly]] and [[5-SPC-0010_metal_ductwork_welding_and_nde]].
