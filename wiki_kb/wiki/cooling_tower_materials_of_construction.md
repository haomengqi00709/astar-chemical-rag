# Cooling Tower Materials of Construction

The Materials of Construction section in the Template Cooling Tower Datasheet (Document ID: [[4-pds-xxxx-r1_template_cooling_tower_datasheet_administration|4-PDS-XXXX-R1]]) specifies the materials to be used for various components of the cooling tower. This is a critical aspect of the design, directly impacting the equipment's durability, corrosion resistance, and overall lifespan, especially given the harsh operating environment involving water, air, and potential chemical treatments. Proper [[material_selection]] is essential to prevent premature failure and ensure reliable operation.

**Specified Components and Material Requirements:**
*   **Tower:** This refers to the main structural components and casing of the cooling tower. Common materials include fiberglass reinforced plastic (FRP), treated timber, galvanized steel, or stainless steel, chosen based on environmental conditions, water chemistry, and structural integrity requirements.
*   **Fill:** The fill media is where the primary heat and mass transfer between water and air occurs. Materials must be resistant to degradation from water, chemicals, and UV radiation. Common choices include PVC (polyvinyl chloride), polypropylene, or treated wood.
*   **Eliminator:** Mist eliminators are designed to capture water droplets entrained in the airflow, preventing drift losses. Materials must be corrosion-resistant and durable, typically PVC, polypropylene, or stainless steel.
*   **Fan:** The fan blades and housing require materials that can withstand continuous operation in a moist, potentially corrosive environment. Materials often include FRP, aluminum, or stainless steel for blades, and galvanized or stainless steel for the fan stack and housing.
*   **Other specifications:** This field allows for the inclusion of additional material requirements for other critical components not explicitly listed, such as nozzles, piping, support structures, or fasteners. These specifications ensure that all parts of the cooling tower are compatible with the operating conditions and expected lifespan.

The selection of appropriate materials is paramount for the long-term performance and maintenance of the cooling tower, directly influencing its resistance to corrosion, scaling, and biological fouling.
