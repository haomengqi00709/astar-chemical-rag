# Acid Plant Nozzle Lining and Transition Requirements

The design and installation of nozzle linings and their transitions to vessel linings are critical for maintaining the integrity of acid-resistant systems in sulphuric acid plants. These requirements are specified in [[4-spc-0012_acid_resistant_linings_specification_overview]].

**Nozzle Details and Transition Blocks**
Nozzle details for the acid towers and tanks are provided in a referenced document (document ID missing). All nozzle lining to vessel lining transitions **must** be constructed with one-piece shaped "Bullseye" blocks. The use of field-cut standard bricks in these transitions **is forbidden** to ensure consistent quality and chemical resistance.

**Two-Layer Acid Brick Areas**
If a nozzle is located in an area of the vessel that features two layers of acid brick, each layer **requires** its own set of nozzle transition blocks. These nozzle transition blocks in two-layer acid brick areas **must** be arranged so that the layer-to-layer brick joints are staggered. This staggering enhances the overall structural integrity and chemical resistance of the lining system.

For general nozzle requirements in absorber towers, refer to [[absorber_tower_nozzle_requirements]]. Detailed nozzle schedules and equipment sketches can be found in [[4-PDS-XXXX-R2_packed_tower_nozzle_schedule_and_equipment_sketch]].
