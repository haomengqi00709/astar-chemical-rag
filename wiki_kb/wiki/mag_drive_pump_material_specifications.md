# Mag Drive Pump Material Specifications

This page details the material specifications for key components of Mag Drive Pumps, as outlined in Document ID 4-DST-XXXX-R1. The selection of these materials is critical for ensuring chemical compatibility, mechanical integrity, and longevity in the intended service.

**Material Specifications**
The following components have specific material requirements:
*   **Front Casing**: Material specified for the front section of the pump casing.
*   **Rear Casing**: Material specified for the rear section of the pump casing.
*   **Front Casing Cover**: Material specified for the cover of the front casing.
*   **Rear Casing Cover**: Material specified for the cover of the rear casing.
*   **Impeller**: Material specified for the rotating impeller, which imparts energy to the fluid.
*   **Bearing**: Material specified for the bearings, which support the rotating shaft.
*   **Spindle**: Material specified for the spindle, a critical internal shaft component.
*   **Base Plate**: Material specified for the base plate, which provides structural support for the pump and motor.
*   **Body Gasket**: Material specified for the gaskets used to seal the pump body sections.
*   **O-Ring**: Material specified for the O-rings, used for sealing various static and dynamic interfaces.

These material selections are crucial for preventing corrosion, erosion, and ensuring the overall reliability of the pump in its operational environment. For broader guidance on material selection for centrifugal pumps, refer to [[horizontal_centrifugal_pump_material_selection]].
