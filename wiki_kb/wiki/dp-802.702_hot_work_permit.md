# Hot Work Permit Form (DP-802.702)

This page describes the Hot Work Permit form, Document ID DP-802.702. This form is a sample document used to authorize and control hot work activities, ensuring that all necessary safety precautions are identified and implemented before work commences.

**Purpose:**
The Hot Work Permit form serves as a formal authorization document for any work involving equipment, tools, or apparatus capable of generating heat, sparks, or flames in restricted areas. Its purpose is to ensure that a thorough risk assessment is conducted, and all mandatory safety measures are in place to prevent fires, explosions, and injuries.

**Key Sections and Information:**
The form typically includes sections for:
*   **Permit Number and Date/Time of Issue:** Unique identification and timestamp for the permit.
*   **Work Location:** Specific area where hot work will be performed.
*   **Description of Work:** Details of the hot work activity (e.g., welding, cutting, grinding).
*   **Equipment/System to be Worked On:** Identification of the affected equipment.
*   **Hazard Assessment Checklist:** Questions regarding potential hazards (e.g., presence of flammables, combustibles, confined space, overhead work).
*   **Precautions Required:** Specific safety measures to be implemented (e.g., isolation, ventilation, fire watch, gas testing, PPE).
*   **Gas Test Results:** Documentation of flammable gas, oxygen, and toxic gas readings.
*   **Fire Watch Assignment:** Details of the assigned [[0-prc-8503_hot_work_permit_roles_and_responsibilities|fire watch]] and their responsibilities.
*   **Authorization Signatures:** Signatures from the [[0-prc-8503_hot_work_permit_roles_and_responsibilities|Commissioning HSE Manager]], [[0-prc-8503_hot_work_permit_roles_and_responsibilities|Work Supervisor]], and the [[0-prc-8503_hot_work_permit_roles_and_responsibilities|person executing the work]].
*   **Permit Validity:** Start and end times, and conditions for invalidation.
*   **Work Completion Confirmation:** Section to be completed upon cessation of work.

**Usage Requirements:**
As specified in [[0-prc-8503_hot_work_permit_issuance_and_validity]]:
*   Every question on the permit form **must** be answered in ink.
*   Where any item is not applicable, it **will** be marked thus (e.g., N/A) and not left blank.
*   The form **will** be completed in duplicate, with a hard copy displayed at the work site and a soft copy retained in the commissioning office.
*   The form **will** be completed in the English language and, if necessary, in the local language.

This form is an integral part of the [[0-prc-8503_hot_work_permit_procedure_overview|Hot Work Permit Procedure (0-PRC-8503)]] and is used in conjunction with other safety documents like the [[dp-802.306_task_safety_awareness_form]].
