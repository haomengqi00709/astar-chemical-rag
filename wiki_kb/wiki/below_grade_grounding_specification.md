# Below Grade Grounding Specification

The Below Grade Grounding Specification details the installation requirements for grounding components that are buried during the early stages of construction. This specification is critical for establishing the foundational grounding system of the plant.

**Purpose and Scope**
The below grade grounding specification **will** be prepared in accordance with Table 2. It **will** describe below grade grounding installation required during early stages of construction, specifically while building concrete footings, equipment concrete pads, and other equipment concrete pads are being poured.

Ground rods, the main plant grounding grid, and all buried connections **must** be described in the below grade grounding specification. The specification **will** instruct the contractor on how to supply and install all grounding connection tails above grade for future connection to equipment.

Connections from the buried grounding grid **must** be readily available to the above grade grounding contractor and **must** be clearly identified in the specification.

**Timing and Coordination**
The below grade grounding specification and drawings **will** be required early in the project. Below grade grounding may be installed during the civil work, requiring close coordination with civil construction activities.
