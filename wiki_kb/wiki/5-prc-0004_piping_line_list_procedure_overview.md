# Piping Line List Procedure Overview (5-PRC-0004)

This procedure, 5-PRC-0004, outlines the process for developing and managing the Piping Line List. The Line List serves as a critical document for documenting piping design conditions and requirements, and for providing essential fabrication and testing specifications to construction sub-contractors.

The Line List is developed concurrently with the [[1-PRC-0003_p_id_development_and_approval|Piping and Instrumentation Diagrams (P&ID)]]. This procedure specifically assigns responsibilities for the preparation, checking, and approval of the Line List throughout its various stages.

The project Line List is based on the template provided in [[line_list|5-LST-0001: Piping Line List]]. The Line List template also functions as a calculation worksheet to assist in determining appropriate line sizes.

All stages of the Line List **must** be published in accordance with [[0-PRC-0005_publishing_of_project_documents]].
