# Spray Tower Materials of Construction (4-PDS-XXXX-R1)

The Template Spray Tower Process Data Sheet (document ID 4-PDS-XXXX-R1) includes a dedicated section for specifying the **Materials of Construction**. This is a critical aspect of the design, ensuring chemical compatibility, mechanical integrity, and longevity of the equipment in its intended service.

Materials of construction are explicitly specified for the following key components of the spray tower system:

*   **Tower**: The main body of the spray tower, including its shell and internal structures.
*   **Pump Tank**: The tank associated with the spray system, typically holding the liquid for circulation.
*   **Spray Nozzles**: The components responsible for atomizing and distributing the liquid within the tower.

The selection of these materials is crucial for resisting corrosion, erosion, and other forms of degradation caused by the process fluids and operating conditions. For general guidelines on material selection, refer to [[material_selection]].
