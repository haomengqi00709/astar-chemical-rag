# RFQ Offer Conditions and Bidder Acknowledgement

This specification details the conditions under which a Bidder must provide an offer for Goods and Services as described in a [[request_for_quotation_specifics|Request for Quotation (RFQ)]] issued by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Bidder Forms and Initial Response**
Bidders will be provided with specific forms to facilitate their response, including an Acknowledgement form, an Offer Summary form, and a Packaging Summary form.

**Mandatory Requirements for Acknowledgement:**
*   **Mandatory:** The Bidder must complete and return the Acknowledgement form within five working days of receiving the Request for Quotation.
*   **Mandatory:** The Acknowledgement form must clearly state the Bidder's intended response to the RFQ.
*   **Mandatory:** The Request for Quotation number must appear prominently on all correspondence and documentation submitted by the Bidder.

**Document Verification:**
*   **Mandatory:** Bidders must verify the receipt of all documents referenced in the Request for Quotation.
*   If any referenced documents are missing, the Bidder must promptly advise the [[project_buyer_roles|Project Buyer]]. The Project Buyer is responsible for issuing any missing documents to ensure the Bidder has all necessary information to prepare a comprehensive offer.

This initial phase ensures that all parties are aligned on the scope and requirements of the tender process, setting the stage for a compliant and well-informed offer submission.
