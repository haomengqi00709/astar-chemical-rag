# Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7404-3 Vendor Quality Assurance Requirements

This Quality Data Sheet (0-QDS-7404-3) outlines the specific Quality Assurance (QA) requirements for vendors involved in the Electrostatic Precipitator Polypropylene Piping project.

**Vendor Quality Assurance Compliance:**
The Vendor's Quality Assurance program **must** comply with all requirements specified in this Quality Data Sheet (0-QDS-7404-3). Additionally, the Vendor's QA program **shall** adhere to the comprehensive guidelines provided in [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment]].

**Quality Plan Requirement:**
The Vendor **shall** prepare a Quality Plan. This plan **must** be developed in accordance with the stipulations of [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment]].

**Note on Quality Plan Deletion:**
If a Quality Plan is not deemed necessary for a particular scope of work, the corresponding sections or cells related to the Quality Plan within this Quality Data Sheet **should** be deleted to reflect the project's specific requirements.

For broader context on vendor quality assurance, refer to [[vendor_quality_assurance]] and other project-specific vendor QA documents such as [[0-QDS-7404-1_vendor_quality_assurance_requirements]] and [[0-QDS-7404-2_vendor_quality_assurance_requirements]].
