# Final Strainer Configuration and Types

This page describes the various configurations, types, and available nominal sizes for the Final Strainer (Tag No. 7842K). This information is essential for selecting the appropriate strainer for specific process requirements.

**Equipment Orientation and Head Types:**
*   **Equipment Orientation:** Horizontal
*   **Top Type (Vertical Strainers):** Dished
*   **Bottom Type (Vertical Strainers):** Cone
*   **Head Type (Horizontal Strainers):** Dished

**Ancillary Equipment:**
*   The strainer is equipped with a basket featuring 6 mm square openings.

**Working Volume Criteria:**
The working volume can be defined based on the following criteria:
*   Centerline Pump Suction to Invert of Overflow
*   Bottom of Tank to Invert of Overflow
*   Other (as specified per project)
