# Flocculant Mixer Equipment Testing

A series of critical tests are mandated for the Flocculant Mixer package to validate its mechanical integrity, operational functionality, and performance characteristics before shipment and commissioning. These tests ensure that the equipment meets design specifications and is ready for service.

**Mandatory Testing Requirements:**

*   **Static Balancing (Impeller Assembly):**
    *   Static balancing **requires** a Quality Record (FP). This record **must** document the results of the static balancing performed on the impeller assembly, confirming it meets the specified balance tolerances.
    *   Static balancing **requires** Chemetics Witness surveillance. A Chemetics representative **must** be present to observe the static balancing procedure and verify the results for the Impeller assembly. This ensures independent confirmation of the balancing quality.
    *   This test specifically applies to the **Impeller assembly** within the Flocculant Mixer package.

*   **Water Fill Test:**
    *   The water fill test **requires** a Quality Record (FP). This record **must** document the successful completion of the water fill test, verifying the tank's integrity and absence of leaks under hydrostatic conditions.
    *   The water fill test **requires** Chemetics Witness surveillance. A Chemetics representative **must** be present to observe the water fill test, ensuring proper execution and verification of results.

*   **Operational Test:**
    *   The operational test **requires** a Quality Record (FP). This record **must** document the successful completion of the operational test, demonstrating the functional performance of the Flocculant Mixer package, including its various components, under simulated operating conditions.

These tests are crucial for confirming the mechanical and operational readiness of the Flocculant Mixer. For general information on equipment testing, refer to [[factory_acceptance_test]] and [[commissioning_and_performance_testing]].
