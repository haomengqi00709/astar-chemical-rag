# Instrument Dimensional Details (8-LST-0010)

The Instrument Dimensional Details (8-LST-0010) document provides specific dimensions and installation requirements for various field instruments, crucial for mechanical and piping design.

**Content Requirements:**
Instrument Dimensional Details (8-LST-0010) **shall** contain:
*   Specific dimensions related to installation of instruments.
*   Specific installation requirements.
*   Individual worksheets for appropriate field instruments (e.g., Control/On-Off Valves, Mag Flow Meters, Orifice Plates, Thermowells) as a minimum.
*   Instrument connection type, size and rating as a minimum.
*   Specific instrument dimensional details which may have an affect on mechanical/piping design details as a minimum.
*   Instrument Weight (where appropriate) as a minimum.
*   Special installation requirements (e.g., Straight Pipe Lengths) as a minimum.

**Document Preparation and Approval:**
*   **Prepared by:** Field Instrument Engineer (FIE)
*   **Approved by:** Field Instrument Engineer (FIE)

**Development and Purpose:**
The FIE will provide the Instrument Dimensional Details spreadsheet. This spreadsheet is intended to provide dimensional and special installation requirements to the Design Office Group. This information **is to be provided as early in the project as possible**. Appropriate sheets in the Instrument Dimensional Details spreadsheet **should be completed immediately upon issue of purchase orders**.

A single Excel workbook will be provided for all instruments for dimensional details. Each instrument type will be provided with a dedicated worksheet in the Excel workbook, and each worksheet will include specific parameters defined for the particular instruments.

**Specific Instrument Requirements:**

**Control Valves and On/Off Valves:**
*   Connection size, type, and rating.
*   Face to Face dimension.
*   Centreline of valve to top of actuator dimension.
*   Centreline of valve to greatest point of obtrusion dimension.
*   Diameter of actuator bonnet.
*   Weight of Control Valve Assembly.
*   Recommended working envelope space **must** be defined.
*   Rotary actuator orientation **must** be defined with Design Office agreement.

**Magnetic Flow Tube:**
*   Connection size, type, and rating.
*   Face to Face dimension.
*   Inlet Straight Pipe length requirement.
*   Outlet Straight Pipe length requirement.
*   Grounding Ring Material.
*   Grounding Ring Thickness.
*   Flow tube weight.
*   Centreline of flow tube to greatest point of obtrusion.
*   Recommended working envelope space **must** be defined.

**Orifice Plate:**
*   Inlet Straight Pipe length requirement.
*   Outlet Straight Pipe length requirement.
*   Connection size, type, and rating.
*   Orifice Plate Thickness.

**Vortex Flow Meter:**
*   Connection size, type, and rating.
*   Face to Face dimension.
*   Inlet Straight Pipe length requirement.
*   Outlet Straight Pipe length requirement.
*   Flow tube weight.
*   Centreline of flow tube to greatest point of obtrusion.
*   Recommended working envelope space **must** be defined.

**Mass Flow Meter (Coriolis):**
*   Connection size, type, and rating.
*   Face to Face dimension.
*   Inlet Straight Pipe length requirement.
*   Outlet Straight Pipe length requirement.
*   Flow tube weight.
*   Centreline of flow tube to greatest point of obtrusion.
*   Recommended working envelope space **must** be defined.

**Rotameter:**
*   Connection size, type, and rating.
*   Face to Face dimension.
*   Inlet Horizontal or Vertical orientation.
*   Horizontal inlet at left, right or rear.
*   Outlet Horizontal or Vertical orientation.
*   Horizontal outlet at left, right or rear.
