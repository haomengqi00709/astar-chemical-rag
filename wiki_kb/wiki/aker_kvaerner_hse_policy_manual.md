# Aker Kvaerner HSE Policy Manual

Standard-0-MAN-8400, titled "Aker Kvaerner Health and Safety Policy," serves as a key reference document defining the company's health and safety procedures.

This manual outlines the general requirements that site management personnel, including the Site Health and Safety Manager, **must** read, understand, and carry out. It complements the overarching Aker Kvaerner HSE Policy (Standard-0-POL-8000) by providing detailed procedural guidance for maintaining a safe and healthy work environment on all EPC projects.

For a summary of the company's HSE targets and policy, refer to [[hse_targets_and_policy]].
For the administration of general HSE procedures, see [[0-prc-8000_administration_of_hse]].
