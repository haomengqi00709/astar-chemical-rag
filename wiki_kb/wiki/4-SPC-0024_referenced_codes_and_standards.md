# Zirconium Welding Referenced Codes and Standards (4-SPC-0024)

This page details the industry codes and standards referenced in document **4-SPC-0024**, which govern the welding and fabrication of Zirconium (Grade 60702) materials. Adherence to the latest editions of these documents, ruling at the date of contract, is mandatory.

**Key Referenced Documents:**
*   [[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping|ASME B31.3]] - Chemical Plant & Petroleum Refinery Piping: This code provides comprehensive requirements for the design, materials, fabrication, erection, examination, testing, and inspection of piping systems in chemical plants and petroleum refineries.
*   [[ASME_bpvc_section_ii_materials|ASME Boiler & Pressure Vessel Codes Section II]] - Materials: This section covers material specifications for both ferrous and nonferrous materials, ensuring that Zirconium materials meet specified properties.
*   [[ASME_sfa_5_12_tungsten_arc_welding_electrodes|ASME Boiler & Pressure Vessel Codes SFA-5.12]] - Specification for Tungsten Arc-Welding Electrodes: This specification details the requirements for tungsten electrodes, which are critical for the Tungsten Inert Gas (TIG) welding process preferred for Zirconium.
*   ASME Boiler & Pressure Vessel Codes SFA-5.24 - Specification for Zirconium and Zirconium Alloy Bare Welding Rods and Electrodes: This standard specifically addresses the filler metals used in Zirconium welding, ensuring their chemical composition and mechanical properties are suitable.
*   [[ASME_section_v_non_destructive_examination|ASME Boiler & Pressure Vessel Codes Section V]] - Non-destructive Examination: This section provides the requirements and methods for various non-destructive examination (NDE) techniques, crucial for verifying weld integrity.
*   [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels|ASME Boiler & Pressure Vessel Codes Section VIII Div. 1]] - Pressure Vessels: This code provides rules for the design, fabrication, inspection, testing, and certification of pressure vessels, which often involve Zirconium fabrications.
*   [[ASME_section_ix_welding_and_brazing_qualifications|ASME Boiler & Pressure Vessel Codes Section IX]] - Welding and Brazing Qualification: This section sets forth the rules for qualifying welding and brazing procedures, as well as the welders, brazers, and welding/brazing operators involved in Zirconium fabrication.
*   [[AWS_a3_0_standard_welding_terms_and_definitions|AWS A3.0]] - Standard Welding Terms & Definitions: This standard provides a comprehensive list of welding terms and definitions, ensuring consistent terminology across all project documentation.
