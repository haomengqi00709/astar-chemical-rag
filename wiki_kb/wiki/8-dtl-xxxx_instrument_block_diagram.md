# Instrument Block Diagram (8-DTL-XXXX)

Instrument Block Diagrams (8-DTL-XXXX) provide a pictorial representation of the overall instrument cabling architecture, showing the interconnections between various instrument components.

**Content Requirements:**
Instrument Block Diagram (8-DTL-XXXX) **shall** contain:
*   Field Instruments.
*   Instrument junction boxes.
*   Marshalling cabinets.
*   DCS cabinets.
*   PLC cabinets.
*   Motor control center cabinets.
*   Switchgear cabinets.
*   Cable identification.
*   Cable size and type.
*   Identification of all spare cables and conductors.

**Document Preparation and Approval:**
*   **Prepared by:** Detail Design Engineer (DDE)
*   **Approved by:** Detail Design Engineer (DDE)

**Development and Purpose:**
The DDE will provide Instrument Block Diagrams. These diagrams will pictorially depict all field installed instrument cables and identify each cable as per project numbering requirements. Cable type, size, and conductors will be identified for each cable, along with the quantity of spare conductors in each cable.

**As-Built Documentation:**
As-built commissioning documents **will be provided** for Instrument Block Diagrams (as applicable).
