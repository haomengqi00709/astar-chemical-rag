# Group Travel Policy Compliance and Deviations

The Group Travel Procedure (11.1 PR 2) outlines the framework for compliance and addresses situations requiring deviations from the standard policy.

### Compliance with Local Statutory Laws
The Aker Kvaerner Policy System, including this travel procedure, **shall** be fully complied with, always subject to local statutory laws and regulations.
*   Local statutory laws and regulations **shall** always be complied with.
*   If any part of this Procedure conflicts with local statutory laws or regulations, the latter **shall** prevail.
*   In instances of conflict, the Business Unit Manager and the relevant Policy Owner **shall** be notified in writing.
*   Necessary deviations **must** be made from the Procedure to comply with local statutory laws or regulations.

### Business Area EVP Discretion
Business Area EVPs have the discretion to implement more restrictive travel procedures within their respective areas. However, they **may not** exceed the levels or allowances set forth in this Group Travel Procedure. This ensures a baseline standard across the Group while allowing for stricter controls where deemed necessary.

### Statutory Requirements and Interfaces
As of the revision date (31.12.05), there are no specific statutory requirements noted within this procedure that directly dictate its content beyond the general principle of complying with local laws.

Furthermore, this Group Travel Procedure has no direct interfaces with other Group Procedures, Group Norms, or Group Directories, indicating it is a standalone policy in terms of its internal document relationships.
