# Conveying Equipment Mechanical Checkout Requirements

This page outlines the mandatory requirements for the mechanical checkout of conveying equipment, designated by suffix M (Material Handling). This process is a key part of the overall [[mechanical_equipment_checkout_procedure]].

**Required Documents:**
The following documents are mandatory for the checkout of conveying equipment:
*   [[piping_and_instrumentation_drawings|P&ID]]
*   Manufacturers documentation
*   [[equipment_data_sheets_4_dst_xxxx|Equipment data sheet]]

**General Installation and Structural Checks:**
*   **Mandatory:** Conveying equipment nameplate data and record **must** match the conveying drawing/data sheet information.
*   **Mandatory:** Conveying equipment nameplate tag **must** match manufacturer information.
*   **Mandatory:** Motor nameplate **must** match the manufacturer information.
*   **Mandatory:** Foundation elevation height **must** be consistent with the design.
*   **Mandatory:** Base **must** have been grouted.
*   **Mandatory:** Equipment **must** be bolted to base.
*   **Mandatory:** Installed [[shock_absorber_installation|shock absorbers]] **must** be checked for damage.
*   **Mandatory:** Conveyor **must** have been aligned with motor.
*   **Mandatory:** [[equipment_grouting_requirements|Grouting]] **must not** obstruct the sliding base, where applicable.
*   **Mandatory:** It **must** be determined if the motor has sealed bearings or whether lubrication is required.
*   **Mandatory:** Motors, drives and lubrication points **must** have sufficient access.

**Surface Treatment and Guards:**
*   **Mandatory:** Conveyor **must** have been primed, where required.
*   **Mandatory:** [[coupling_guard_installation|Coupling guard]] **must** have been primed and painted.
*   **Mandatory:** Coupling **must** be installed.
*   **Mandatory:** Shaft **must** rotate freely by hand.
*   **Mandatory:** Guard **must** be installed and bolts tight.

**Drive System (Chains/Belts):**
*   **Mandatory:** [[chain_installation_and_tensioning|Chain]] **must** be installed and correct size and tightness.
*   **Mandatory:** Correct size of [[v_belt_installation_and_tensioning|v-belt]] **must** have been installed.
*   **Mandatory:** V-belt tightness **must** be checked using the installation instruction method.
*   **Mandatory:** V-belt guard **must** have been replaced and be tight.
*   **Mandatory:** [[conveyor_belt_and_bucket_installation|Belt/bucket]] **must** be installed.
*   **Mandatory:** [[conveyor_belt_splicing|Splice]] **must** be correct, belt aligned and fasteners embedded tightly.
*   **Mandatory:** [[conveyor_roller_and_idler_installation|Rollers/idlers]] **must** be installed, turn freely and holders **must** be tight.
*   **Mandatory:** [[conveyor_tensioner_adjustment|Take up roll (tensioner)]] **must** be adjusted correctly.

**Safety and Discharge:**
*   **Mandatory:** [[conveyor_trip_wire_installation|Trip wires]] **must** be installed.
*   **Mandatory:** [[discharge_chute_inspection|Discharge chutes]] **must** be clean and free of debris.

**Grounding:**
*   **Mandatory:** [[grounding_installation_details|Conveying equipment grounding cable]] connection **must** be tight.
*   **Mandatory:** Conveying equipment grounding cable **must** be connected to the main grounding loop.

**Housing and Hoppers:**
*   **Mandatory:** All housing nuts and bolts **must** be tight.
*   **Mandatory:** [[hopper_design_and_material|Hopper]] size and material **must** be checked against manufacturers information and data sheet.
*   **Mandatory:** Hopper and belts **must** have adequate support.

**Lubrication:**
*   **Mandatory:** [[oiler_installation_and_maintenance|Oilers]] **must** be installed.
*   **Mandatory:** Oiler and [[oil_seal_inspection|oil seal]] **must** be checked for leakage.
*   **Mandatory:** Oil **must** be the correct specification for the equipment.
*   **Mandatory:** Oil casing **must** contain oil to the fill line.
*   **Mandatory:** [[bearing_lubrication_and_inspection|Bearings]] may have shipping grease; manufacturer's installation instructions **must** be checked.
*   **Mandatory:** Bearings **must** have been lubricated.

**Electrical:**
*   **Mandatory:** Electrical for conveying equipment: See motor installation field check in [[electrical_systems_field_checkout_9_prc_0004|electrical checklist]].

**Coupling and Guards**
*   [[coupling_guard_installation|Coupling guard]] must have been primed and painted.

**Grounding**
*   Conveying equipment grounding cable connection must be tight.
*   Conveying equipment grounding cable must be connected to the main grounding loop.

**Housing and Components**
*   All housing nuts and bolts must be tight.

**Lubrication**
*   [[oiler_installation_and_maintenance|Oilers]] must be installed.
