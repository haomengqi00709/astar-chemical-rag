# Roles and Responsibilities: Door Monitor

A Door Monitor is an employee who ensures an orderly exit during the emergency evacuation of a floor area. Each Door Monitor is assigned to a specific emergency exit.

**Equipment**
During a building evacuation, Door Monitors **must** put on a fire vest and hard hat. They **must** also carry a flashlight. For details on standard equipment, refer to [[0-PRC-8010_emergency_response_equipment]].

**Duties During Evacuation**
*   Door Monitors **must** go to their designated exit door during a building evacuation.
*   Door Monitors **must** supervise the orderly evacuation of all employees and visitors to the rally location outside the building (see [[0-PRC-8010_building_evacuation_plan]]).
*   Door Monitors **must** provide assistance to anyone requiring assistance descending the stairs.

**Assistance for Individuals with Mobility Challenges**
*   If providing assistance descending stairs is not possible, the Door Monitor **will** send for help.
*   If providing assistance descending stairs is not possible, the Door Monitor **will** stay with the physically disabled person in the refuge area at the exit until the Fire Department Rescue team arrives.

**Post-Clearance and Reporting**
*   Once evacuation of the floor to the stairwell is complete, Door Monitors **must** ensure door(s) to the exit stairwell are closed (not left propped open).
*   Once evacuation of the floor to the stairwell is complete, Door Monitors **must** exit.
*   Door Monitors **must** report any unusual incident to the Chief Warden at the front door to the building on Cornwall Street (see [[0-PRC-8010_roles_and_responsibilities_chief_warden]]).
*   If no unusual incident occurs, Door Monitors **do not** need to go to the front door.

**Assignments and Specific Exit Instructions**
Door Monitors are listed in [[0-PRC-8010_table_6]].
Regarding specific exits, Exit 4-4 **is not to be** used in an Emergency if exit 4-5 is clear and available. The Door Monitor **will** redirect people to the nearest safe exit, 4-5. For more details, refer to [[0-PRC-8010_floor_specific_evacuation_details]].
