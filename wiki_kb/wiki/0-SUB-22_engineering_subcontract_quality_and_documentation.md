# Engineering Subcontract 0-SUB-22 Quality and Documentation

This page details the quality assurance and documentation requirements for Engineering Subcontract 0-SUB-22.

**Quality Assurance Requirements:**
The Subcontractor **shall** comply with the requirements for quality assurance as set out in document ID 02C03000-0-SPC-7004-RA: [[engineering_subcontractor_quality_assurance_0_spc_7004]]. This document specifies the quality management system, processes, and standards that the Subcontractor **must** adhere to throughout the project lifecycle.

*   **Quality Plan Submission:** The Subcontractor **shall** submit the quality plan within two weeks after the starting date of the subcontract. This plan outlines how the Subcontractor intends to meet the specified quality requirements.

**Documentation Requirements:**
The Subcontractor **shall** supply all documentation in the form and manner described in document ID 02C03000-0-SPC-0002-RA: [[engineering_subcontractor_document_supply_0_spc_0002]]. This specification covers aspects such as document formats, content, numbering, and submission procedures.

*   **Document Retention:** The period for retention of documents **shall** be 12 years following Completion or earlier termination of the subcontract. This ensures that all project records are maintained for future reference and compliance.

Adherence to these quality and documentation standards is mandatory for the Subcontractor to ensure the integrity and traceability of all deliverables.
