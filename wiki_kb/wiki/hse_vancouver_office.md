# HSE Management at Vancouver Office

Responsibility for Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) at the Vancouver Office (Section 8 of 0-PRC-8000) is assigned to the Administration Services Coordinator.

**Joint Health and Safety Committee (JHSC):**
A Vancouver Office Joint Health and Safety Committee (JHSC) **will** be formed in accordance with the OHS regulations of British Columbia. The JHSC **will** have equal representation from management and non-management employees, with non-management members elected by their peers. Management representatives **will** include, at minimum, the Administration Services Coordinator, the HSE Manager, and the Human Resources Manager. The JHSC **will** assist the Administration Services Coordinator in fulfilling all HSE responsibilities for the Vancouver Office.

The Vancouver Office JHSC **will** meet monthly to review HSE performance. An Action List, documenting the status of previous actions and new actions with assigned responsibilities, **will** be maintained and posted on the Company data network for employee access.

**Workplace Environment and Risk Minimization:**
The Company **will** provide a workplace environment designed to minimize any deleterious impact on employee health. This includes:
*   Heating, Ventilation, and Air Conditioning that **must** comply with local codes and regulations.
*   Sufficient and sanitary facilities that **must** be provided.
*   Appropriate levels of ambient and task lighting that **must** be provided.
*   Refrigerators for food storage that **must** be provided.
*   Furniture designed to minimize repetitive strain injury that **must** be provided.
*   Promotion of employee health and fitness that **must** be provided.

To minimize risk to employees at the engineering office:
*   Health and safety guidelines of the Workers Compensation Board of B.C. **must** be followed.
*   First Aid facilities **must** be maintained.
*   Trained first aid attendants **must** be maintained.
*   An effective office emergency response plan **must** be maintained and tested on a regular basis.
*   Employee awareness of ergonomic factors **must** be maintained.
*   Employees **must** be engaged in reporting near-misses and risk observations.
*   Proactive actions **must** be taken to eliminate unsafe conditions.
*   Proactive actions **must** be taken to reduce clutter and accumulation of unnecessary materials.

**Environmental Care:**
The Company **will** take a leading role in adhering to the principles of environmental care embodied in the slogan "Reduce Recycle Reuse."

For overall Company HSE policies and responsibilities, refer to [[0-prc-8000_administration_of_hse]] and [[hse_roles_and_responsibilities_company_wide]].

*   The JHSC will assist the Administration Services Coordinator to ensure that all responsibilities for HSE at the Vancouver Office are fulfilled.
