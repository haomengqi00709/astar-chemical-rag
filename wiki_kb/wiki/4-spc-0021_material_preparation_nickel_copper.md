# Material Preparation for Nickel/Copper Welding (4-SPC-0021)

Proper material preparation, including cleaning, cutting, and forming, is critical for nickel/copper (UNS N04400 / Monel 400) fabrications under **4-SPC-0021**.

**Cleaning Procedures:**
Surfaces and edges of nickel material to be heated for thermal cutting, forming, or welding **shall** be cleaned in two stages:
1.  **Stage 1 Cleaning:** All surfaces and edges to be heated **shall** be lightly ground to white metal. If grinding is not practical (e.g., pipe bore), the surface **shall** be chemically cleaned using an agreed procedure.
2.  **Stage 2 Cleaning:** Immediately prior to heat application, ground surfaces **shall** be cleaned with a suitable organic solvent. These solvents **shall** effectively remove lubricants, greases, oils, marking crayons, paints, dye penetrants, and other contaminants.

**Contamination Control:**
*   The fabricator **shall** implement controls to prevent contamination of weldments at all stages of fabrication.
*   These controls **shall** include the segregation of wire brushes for exclusive use on nickel materials.
*   Upon completion of work, items **shall** be cleaned internally and externally.
*   All fabrication markings, weld spatter, and other residues **shall** be removed prior to shipment.
*   Sulphur, phosphorous, or lead-bearing paints or crayons **shall not** be used for marking or identification.

**Material Cutting:**
*   Material **shall** be cut to size by machining or shearing.
*   Material over 3/16" thick that is sheared **shall** have extra material left on the sheared edge for subsequent machining, chipping, or grinding to ensure the removal of defects caused by shearing.
*   Material over 1" thick **shall not** be sheared.
*   Thermal cutting is preferably carried out by the Plasma Arc Process. Welding edges after the Plasma Arc Process **shall** be lightly ground to bright metal.
*   Any other thermal cutting process requires a minimum of 1/8" of metal to be removed by mechanical means. This mechanical removal ensures all slag, scale, other irregularities, contaminants, and thermal effects are eliminated.
*   Silicon carbide wheels **shall** be used for grinding.

**Material Forming:**
*   Materials **shall** be cold formed as far as practical.
*   When hot forming is necessary, the procedure **must** be agreed to in writing by [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_company_details|Aker Kvaerner Chemetics]] prior to the commencement of work. The hot forming procedure reference is Para. 5.5.1.
*   Pipe bends **shall** be in accordance with [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_company_details|Aker Kvaerner Chemetics]] Drawings.
*   Low melting point metals or alloys **shall not** be utilized for filling pipe for bending.
