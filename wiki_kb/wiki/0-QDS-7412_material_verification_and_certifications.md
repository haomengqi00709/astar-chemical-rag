# Material Verification and Certifications for Centrifugal Pumps (0-QDS-7412)

This page outlines the mandatory material verification and certification requirements for Centrifugal Pumps, as specified in Quality Data Sheet [[0-QDS-7412_centrifugal_pump_quality_data_sheet_overview]]. These activities and documents are critical for ensuring the integrity and compliance of materials used in pump construction.

**Material Verification:**
*   **Activity:** Material Verification is a required item/activity.
*   **Documentation:** Mill Test Certificates are required for Material Verification.
*   **Quality Record:** Mill Test Certificates **must be provided** as FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package).
*   **Scope:** Mill Test Certificates are only required for high alloys.
*   **Surveillance:** Chemetics surveillance (witness) is required for Mill Test Certificates.

**Certificate of Compliance:**
*   **Activity:** Certificate of Compliance is a required item/activity.
*   **Documentation:** A Certificate of Compliance is required.
*   **Quality Record:** The Certificate of Compliance **must be provided** as FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package).
*   **Surveillance:** Chemetics surveillance (witness) is required for the Certificate of Compliance.

These requirements ensure that all materials meet the specified standards and that proper documentation is available for traceability and quality assurance. For definitions of quality record requirements, refer to [[0-QDS-7412_quality_record_definitions]].
