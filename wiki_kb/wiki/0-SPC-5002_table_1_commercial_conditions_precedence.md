# Purchase Order Commercial Conditions Precedence (0-SPC-5002 Table 1)

This page describes the content of Table 1 within document **0-SPC-5002**, which specifies the order of precedence for interpreting commercial conditions in a Purchase Order.

The table outlines a hierarchy to resolve any ambiguities or inconsistencies between different commercial documents forming part of the Purchase Order. The order of precedence is as follows, from highest to lowest:

1.  Conditions set out within Section 5 of the Purchase Order Form.
2.  The [[special_conditions_for_purchase_0_spc_5002|Special Conditions for Purchase]].
3.  The [[standard_conditions_for_purchase|Standard Conditions for Purchase]].

Documents referenced in Section 6 of the Purchase Order Form apply only when they do not conflict with the other documents that are part of the Purchase Order.
