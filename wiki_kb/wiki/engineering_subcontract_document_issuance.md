# Engineering Subcontract Document Issuance

The issuance of documents related to Engineering Subcontracts is a critical process managed through Document Control, ensuring that all parties receive the correct and latest versions of essential project information. This process covers the initial invitation to tender through to the final signed agreement.

**Issuance of Invitation Letters:**
Once prepared, the Subcontract Administrator **must** forward the covering letters of invitation to Document Control for formal issuance to prospective Engineering Subcontractors. The issuance of these letters **must** strictly follow the procedures outlined in [[control_of_project_documents_0_prc_0006]]. These letters formally initiate the tendering process, referencing key documents like [[instructions_to_engineering_subcontractors_for_tender]].

**Issuance of Tender Document Packages:**
In addition to the invitation letters, the Subcontract Administrator and the relevant [[discipline_lead_and_approver_roles]](s) **must** forward a published copy of all required tender documents to Document Control. These comprehensive document packages are then issued to the Engineering Subcontractors, and their issuance **must** also adhere to [[control_of_project_documents_0_prc_0006]]. This ensures that subcontractors receive a complete and controlled set of documents necessary for preparing their bids.

**Issuance of the Signed Agreement:**
After the successful completion of negotiations and the final signing of the Engineering Subcontract Agreement, the Subcontract Administrator **must** forward the signed and authorized paper copy of the agreement to Document Control. The issuance of this signed agreement to the Engineering Subcontractor **must** strictly follow [[control_of_project_documents_0_prc_0006]]. This final step formally executes the contract and provides the subcontractor with their official contractual document.

**Issuance of Supporting Documents for Execution:**
Concurrently with the signed agreement, the Subcontract Administrator and the Discipline Lead(s) **must** forward copies of all other required documents (e.g., final activity schedules, staff rates, technical specifications) to Document Control for issuance to the Engineering Subcontractor. The issuance of this complete document package **must** also comply with [[control_of_project_documents_0_prc_0006]], ensuring the subcontractor has all necessary information to commence work.

Adherence to these issuance procedures is paramount for maintaining document integrity, legal compliance, and effective communication throughout the Engineering Subcontract lifecycle.

**Tender Invitation Package**
The Subcontract Administrator prepares covering letters for each Engineering Subcontractor invited to tender. These letters must explicitly reference 0-SPC-2203: Instructions to Engineering Subcontractors for Tender and 0-REP-8XXX: [Template Engineering Subcontractor Survey]. The Subcontract Administrator forwards these letters of invitation to Document Control for formal issue, which must adhere to 0-PRC-0006: Control of Project Documentation. Additionally, the Subcontract Administrator and Discipline Lead(s) are responsible for forwarding a published copy of all required documents to Document Control for issue to the Engineering Subcontractors as part of the tender package.

**Final Subcontract Package**
Following the award, the Subcontract Administrator and Discipline Lead(s) forward copies of all required documents to Document Control for issue to the successful Engineering Subcontractor. The Subcontract Administrator also forwards the signed and authorized paper copy of the Engineering Subcontract to Document Control for issue. Both these final document issues must follow 0-PRC-0006: Control of Project Documentation.
