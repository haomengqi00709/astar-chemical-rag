# Packing and Shipping Requirements: CS Tubing

This page outlines the mandatory packing and shipping requirements for carbon steel tubing, as specified in Quality Data Sheet (QDS) 0-QDS-7428. Proper packing and shipping procedures are essential to prevent damage during transit and ensure the safe delivery of materials to the project site.

**Mandatory Packing and Shipping Requirements:**

*   **Packing List:**
    *   A Packing List is required to be included in the Vendor's Inspection & Test (I & T) Plan (designated as [[0-QDS-7428_quality_record_definitions|AP]]).
    *   Chemetics will **Witness** surveillance for the Packing List. Chemetics will not "Hold" or "Complete" surveillance for this item.
*   **Truck Loading:**
    *   Chemetics will **Witness** surveillance for Truck Loading. No specific Quality Record is required, and Chemetics will not "Hold" or "Complete" surveillance for this activity.
*   **Ship Loading:**
    *   Chemetics will **Witness** surveillance for Ship Loading. No specific Quality Record is required, and Chemetics will not "Hold" or "Complete" surveillance for this activity.

These requirements ensure that the carbon steel tubing is correctly prepared for transportation and handled appropriately during loading operations. The Packing List, designated as "As Prepared" (AP), is a crucial document that details the contents of each shipment, aiding in inventory management and verification upon receipt. Chemetics' role in witnessing the Packing List ensures its accuracy and completeness.

For both Truck Loading and Ship Loading, Chemetics' "Witness" surveillance indicates their presence during these critical logistical operations. While Chemetics does not "Hold" the process or "Complete" the surveillance for loading activities, their observation helps ensure that proper handling procedures are followed, and the tubing is secured to prevent damage during transit. This oversight contributes to the overall quality assurance by extending control beyond the manufacturing floor to the point of dispatch, safeguarding the integrity of the delivered materials.
