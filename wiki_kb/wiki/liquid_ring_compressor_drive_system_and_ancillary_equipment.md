# Liquid Ring Compressor Drive System and Ancillary Equipment

This page details the mandatory requirements for the drive system and ancillary equipment of the Liquid Ring Compressor, as specified in document 4-DST-XXXX-R0. All listed fields **must** be completed to ensure the correct integration and operation of the compressor package.

**Drive System:**
The drive system powers the liquid ring compressor.
*   **Drive System:** The type of drive system (e.g., motor, turbine) **must** be specified.
*   **Drive System Supply by:** The entity responsible for supplying the drive system **must** be identified.
*   **Drive System Mount by:** The entity responsible for mounting the drive system **must** be identified.
*   **Motor Specification:** The detailed specification for the motor **must** be provided. Refer to [[electrical_equipment_specifications]] and [[motor_list_electrical]] for related documentation.
*   **Motor Size:** The size or power rating of the motor **must** be specified.

**Ancillary Equipment:**
Various auxiliary components are required for the complete and functional operation of the compressor.
*   **Ancillary Equipment:** A general description of ancillary equipment **must** be provided.
*   **Ancillary Equipment Supply by:** The entity responsible for supplying ancillary equipment **must** be identified.
*   **Ancillary Equipment Mount by:** The entity responsible for mounting ancillary equipment **must** be identified.
*   **Sheaves/V-belt:** If applicable, details for sheaves and V-belts **must** be provided.
*   **Base plate c/w Spacer Block:** Specifications for the base plate complete with spacer block **must** be provided.
*   **Drive guard:** Details for the drive guard **must** be provided.
*   **Bearing:** Specifications for the bearings **must** be provided.
*   **Lubrication:** Requirements for the lubrication system **must** be specified. For related information, see [[blower_lube_oil_system_components]].
*   **Vibration Isolator:** Details for vibration isolators **must** be provided. Refer to [[vibration_analysis]] for further context.
