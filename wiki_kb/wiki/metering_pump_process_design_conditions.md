# Metering Pump Process Design Conditions

This page outlines the critical process design conditions and fluid characteristics for metering pumps, as specified in Document ID 4-DST-XXXX-R1. These parameters are fundamental for accurate pump sizing and selection.

**Process Condition Categories**
Process conditions are categorized into two primary sets:
*   **DESIGN PER UNIT**: Represents the maximum or most stringent operating conditions for which the pump is designed.
*   **ALTERNATE PER UNIT**: Specifies alternative or less frequent operating conditions that the pump may encounter.
These categories allow for the specification of operational envelopes and alternative scenarios for pump performance.

**Fluid Description Parameters**
The following fluid characteristics **must** be specified:
*   **Composition (%w/w)**: The weight percentage of each component in the fluid.
*   **Solids Content (%w/w)**: The weight percentage of any solids present in the fluid.
*   **Solids Type**: A description of the type of solids (e.g., abrasive, crystalline, fibrous).

**Flow Rate Parameters**
*   **Flow Rate (m3/h)**: The required volumetric flow rate of the fluid.
*   **Minimum Flow Rate (m3/h)**: The lowest acceptable volumetric flow rate for stable operation.

**Performance and Physical Properties**
The following performance and physical properties are measured and specified:
*   **TDH (m fluid)**: Total Dynamic Head, representing the total equivalent height the pump must overcome.
*   **NPIP Available (kPa(a))**: Net Positive Suction Head Available, indicating the absolute pressure at the suction side of the pump.
*   **Temperature (oC)**: The operating temperature of the fluid.
*   **Density (kg/m3)**: The mass per unit volume of the fluid.
*   **Viscosity (cP)**: The resistance of the fluid to shear or flow.
*   **NPIP Required (kPa(a))**: Net Positive Suction Head Required, which is a characteristic of the pump itself.

**Units of Measurement**
All measurements adhere to the specified [[engineering_units]] to ensure consistency and accuracy in design and operation. For general process design guidelines, refer to [[process_design_criteria_1_prc_0001]].

**Quantity Categories**
Equipment quantities are specified under the following categories:
*   Operating
*   Installed Spare

**Fluid Description Parameters**
Key parameters for fluid description include:
*   Composition (%w/w)
*   Solids Content (%w/w)
*   Solids Type

**Flow Rate Parameters**
Flow rate specifications include:
*   Flow Rate (m3/h)
*   Minimum Flow Rate (m3/h)

**Hydraulic and Process Parameters**
Critical hydraulic and process parameters and their units are:
*   TDH: m (fluid)
*   NPIP Available: kPa (a)
*   Temperature: oC
*   Density: kg/m3
*   Viscosity: cP
*   NPIP Required: kPa (a)
*   Acceleration losses: kPa
