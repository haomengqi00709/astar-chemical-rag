# Protective Coating Specification (7-SPC-0001)

This specification (7-SPC-0001) outlines the requirements for protective coating applications.

For metal ductwork, the Vendor **shall** paint all carbon steel surfaces in accordance with this specification. This includes external support brackets, lugs, support saddles, and similar attachments welded to the ductwork, as well as loose structural components and hardware.

Specific instructions for ductwork painting include:
*   Lifting lugs and temporary expansion joint bracing **shall** be painted yellow.
*   Ductwork designated to be covered with insulation **shall not** be painted.
*   Stainless steel components **shall not** be painted.
*   Internal carbon steel surfaces **shall not** be painted.

For carbon steel ducting supplied from overseas, or where cosmetic requirements dictate, a weldable red oxide primer may be applied.

Refer to [[5-SPC-0010_metal_ductwork_painting_requirements]] for specific application of this specification to metal ductwork.
