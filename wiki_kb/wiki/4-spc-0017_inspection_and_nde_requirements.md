# Inspection and NDE Requirements for 6% Moly SS (4-SPC-0017)

This section details the mandatory inspection and Non-Destructive Examination (NDE) requirements for 6 percent Moly austenitic stainless steel fabrications under Standard **4-SPC-0017**.

**Inspector Access and Authority**
The Inspector **will** have free access to the Vendor's work and Subvendor's work (if sublet by approval) at all reasonable times. The Inspector **will** be at liberty to inspect work during fabrication at any stage and to reject material or workmanship not conforming to this Standard or applicable code. Inspection by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics does not relieve the Fabricator of responsibility for providing materials, components, and workmanship in accordance with this Standard, engineering design, and applicable code.

**Chemical Certification and Analysis**
The Supplier **must** make available to the purchaser's inspector copies of chemical certification for the batch of electrodes or wire coils used. If electrodes, wire, etc., cannot be identified to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' satisfaction, samples **will** be removed for chemical analysis at the Supplier’s account.

A spot check analysis **will** be performed on production welds of critical equipment (pressure vessels, heat exchangers, pressure piping, certain proprietary items) and non-pressure retaining equipment in dangerous or demanding services. The weld(s) to be checked **will** be selected at random by the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' Inspector.

Where chemical analysis results show a weldment falls outside approved specification or code, the Supplier **will** promptly advise Aker Kvaerner Chemetics in writing of the proposed course of action. The Supplier's proposed action **will** include details of any additional testing necessary to fully define the extent of non-conformance. All repairs and retests due to non-conformance **will** be to the Supplier’s account. For non-conformances, refer to [[non_conformance_report_0_ncr_7]].

**Penetrant Testing (PT)**
Unless otherwise specified in the purchase order, approved drawings, or specifications/codes:
*   Nozzle attachment weld surfaces **will** be 100% PT examined for their complete length.
*   All butt weld surfaces, including skirt to shell welds, **will** be 100% PT examined for their complete length.
*   All welds between dissimilar metals **will** be 100% PT examined on the root pass and completed weld surfaces.
*   Tube to tubesheet welds **will** be 100% PT examined.

PT examination **will** check for cracks, porosity, slag inclusions, significant undercutting, or other major defects. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics reserves the right to select the location of PT inspection. The PT exam **will** be carried out in accordance with a written procedure. The limit of acceptability for PT **will** satisfy [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels]], Division I, Appendix VIII, except that linear indications **will** be considered unacceptable.

All excavated areas **will** be weld built-up flush with the surface of the base material using the welding procedure previously approved by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for the main welding details. Weld repair surfaces **will** be PT examined per the above requirements. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics reserves the right to accept or reject any doubtful or questionable indications.

**Radiographic Testing (RT)**
Where specified on Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' Engineering drawings, items fabricated to this Standard and ASME Code **will** be spot radiographed in accordance with Para. UW-52(b) of [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels]], Division 1 of the ASME Code. Para. UW-52(b) defines the minimum extent of Spot Radiographic Examination, Standards for Spot Radiographic Examination, and Evaluation and Retests.

For piping fabricated in accordance with ASME Codes and this Standard, the minimum standard of radiography **will** be 5 percent random radiography as defined in Para. 341.4.1(b) of [[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping]]. The RT Exam **will** be carried out in accordance with a written procedure. RT personnel **must** be qualified to SNTC-lA.

Where additional spot radiography is required, as defined in UW-52 Sub-para. (d)(2), costs (including repair and re-radiography) **will** be to the Fabricator's account. Where more than one welder or welding operator has been used, spot radiography **will** be performed to examine the welding of each. Junctions between circumferential and longitudinal seams **will** be considered as preferred locations for spot radiography.

**Helium Mass Spectrometer Testing**
When required by purchase order or referenced drawings, tube/tubesheet joints and tube bundles **will** be tested using the helium mass spectrometer (reverse probe method) per [[ASME_section_v_non_destructive_examination]], Article 10.
