# Fan or Blower Final Inspections

Final Inspection is a mandatory item or activity for the Fan or Blower equipment specified under Quality Data Sheet **0-QDS-7425**.

Chemetics **will Witness** the following aspects of the final inspection:
*   **Dimensional inspection:** Verification of critical dimensions against drawings.
*   **Contour & finish of welds inspection:** Assessment of weld quality and appearance.
*   **Surfaces free from defects inspection:** Examination for any surface imperfections or damage.
*   **Cleanliness inspection:** Confirmation that the equipment is clean and free from debris.
*   **Motor inspection:** Verification of the motor's condition and proper installation.
*   **Coupling/Belt inspection:** Inspection of the coupling or belt drive system.
*   **Guard inspection:** Confirmation of proper installation and integrity of safety guards.
*   **Baseplate inspection:** Assessment of the baseplate's condition and alignment.
*   **Silencer inspection:** This is an item or activity that **must** be performed and witnessed by Chemetics.
*   **Review QA Documentation:** Chemetics **will Witness** the review of the complete Quality Assurance Documentation Package.

These comprehensive final inspections ensure that the Fan or Blower equipment meets all specified quality, safety, and design requirements before shipment.
