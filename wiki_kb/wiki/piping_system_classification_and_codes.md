# Piping System Classification and Applicable Codes

Piping systems are generally categorized into three distinct classes, each governed by specific ASME codes to ensure appropriate design and installation standards.

**Piping Classes:**
*   **Power Piping:** This class is defined as per [[ASME_b31_1_power_piping]]. It typically covers piping systems found in power generation facilities, industrial and institutional plants, geothermal heating systems, and central and district heating systems.
*   **Plant Services and Utilities Piping:** This category applies to piping systems handling fluids classified as [[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping|ASME B31.3 Category D]]. Category D fluids are non-flammable, non-toxic, and not damaging to human tissue, and their design pressure does not exceed 150 psig (1035 kPa) and design temperature does not exceed 366°F (186°C).
*   **Process Piping:** This class is designated for fluids categorized as [[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping|ASME B31.3 Category N]]. Category N fluids are those that are extremely hazardous, requiring the most stringent design, fabrication, examination, and testing requirements to minimize the risk of release.
