# Aluminium Bus Welding Material Requirements

The Aluminium Bus Welding Standard (9-SPC-0001) specifies strict requirements for all materials used in the fabrication of Aluminium Bus systems.

**Base Material Specifications**
The material classification for BUS systems is **ASME P-21**. All base materials used **shall** be as specified in the purchase order and referenced documents. Base material **shall** be in accordance with [[ASTM_b209_aluminium_and_aluminium_alloy_sheet_and_plate]] or [[ASTM_b236_aluminium_bus_bar]]. Type 1100 or 1350 materials are considered acceptable for BUS systems, with H112 temper normally used. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **must** be advised in writing regarding the proposed use of alternate materials.

**Weld Filler Metal Specifications**
All weld filler metals **shall** be selected from and meet the requirements of The American Welding Society and [[ASME_bpvc_section_ii_materials|ASME Code]]. All filler wire/electrodes **shall** be in accordance with [[ASME_sfa_5_10_aluminium_and_aluminium_alloy_welding_rods_and_bare_electrodes|AWS A5.10]]. Recommended filler wire types are 4043 or 1100, with Type 4043 being the preferred choice. The filler wire/electrode used on production welding **shall** be the same as that used in the Procedure Qualification Test.

**Material Traceability and Certification**
Certification showing results from analysis and other tests (in accordance with relevant material specifications) **shall** be supplied to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for all materials, including welding consumables. When required by the purchase order, the fabricator **shall** maintain full traceability of materials back to original mill certification. Lack of adequate identification or certification **shall** be cause for rejection of the specific component or item. The run-off pad material **shall** be of the same material designation as the parent material.

For general guidance on material selection, refer to [[material_selection]].

**Base Material Specifications**
Type 1100 or 1350 materials are considered acceptable for BUS systems. H112 temper is normally used for BUS systems. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **must** be advised in writing regarding proposed use of alternate materials.

**Weld Filler Metal Requirements**
All weld filler metals **shall** be selected from, and meet requirements of, The American Welding Society and ASME Code. Recommended filler wire: Type 4043 or 1100. Type 4043 is the preferred filler wire choice. Fabricators **shall** ensure and demonstrate to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics representative that manufacturer's instructions for storage, drying, and use of weld consumables are fully implemented. Filler wire/electrode used on production welding **shall** be the same as that used in the Procedure Qualification Test.

**Material Certification and Traceability**
Certification showing results from analysis and other tests (in accordance with relevant material specification) **shall** be supplied to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for all materials, including welding consumables. When required by purchase order, fabricator **shall** maintain full traceability of materials back to original mill certification. Fabricator's internal controls **shall** prevent the use of unspecified materials. Lack of adequate identification or certification **shall** be cause for rejection of the specific component or item.
