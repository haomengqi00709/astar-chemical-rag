# Chemetics Standards Usage Guidelines

This page outlines the current status and mandatory usage requirements for standards published under the Chemetics brand. These guidelines are crucial for ensuring consistency, accuracy, and compliance when utilizing or modifying any Chemetics standard documents.

**Current Status of Standards**
All standards are currently published under the Chemetics Logo. This branding is maintained due to existing internal and external links that reference Chemetics-branded documentation. It is important to note that these standards are actively being developed and refined. Users should be aware that the standards will not receive official updates until a final product version is formally released. For more information on Chemetics as a company, refer to [[chemetics_aker_kvaerner_company_details]].

**Mandatory Usage Requirements**
To ensure proper application and control of Chemetics standards, all users **must** adhere to the following requirements:
*   Users **must** thoroughly read and understand the content of any standard document before using or attempting to update it.
*   For any further updates, clarifications, or inquiries regarding these standards, users **must** contact Tyler Caviglia.
*   When these standards are adapted or utilized for a specific job or project, the headers and footers of the document **must** be updated to reflect the project-specific information and branding. Guidance on document templates and revision control can be found in [[document_templates_and_usage]] and [[document_revision_and_status_management_0_spc_6091]].

Adherence to these guidelines is essential for maintaining the integrity and applicability of Chemetics' technical documentation. For general information on document management, refer to [[standard_document_management_procedure_0_prc_0001]]. For details on document numbering, see [[document_numbering_0_prc_0002]].

All standards are currently under the Chemetics Logo, primarily due to existing links and historical context. These standards are currently being actively worked on. Please note that these standards will not receive further updates until a final product version is officially available. For any further updates or inquiries regarding these standards, please contact Tyler Caviglia.

**Specific Usage Requirements**
When utilizing these standards for a specific job or project, it is mandatory to update all headers and footers within the document to reflect the current project details.
