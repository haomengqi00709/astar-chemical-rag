# Stainless Steel Tank Vendor Quality Assurance Requirements (0-QDS-7436)

For the Stainless Steel Tank project (Document ID: 0-QDS-7436), the Vendor's Quality Assurance (QA) program **must** comply with the requirements outlined in this Quality Data Sheet and the general [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment|Vendor Quality Assurance (0-SPC-7001)]] specification.

**Key Requirements:**
*   The Vendor **shall** prepare a Quality Plan in accordance with [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment]].
*   The Vendor's Quality Assurance activities **must** comply with all stipulations set forth in this [[0-QDS-7436_stainless_steel_tank_quality_data_sheet_administration|Quality Data Sheet (0-QDS-7436)]].
*   The Vendor's Quality Assurance activities **must** comply with the requirements of [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment]].

These requirements ensure that the fabrication and supply of the Stainless Steel Tank meet the specified quality standards and client expectations.
