# Construction Subcontract Tender Process

The Construction Subcontract Tender Process outlines the systematic steps for identifying, inviting, and managing bids from potential construction subcontractors. This process ensures that qualified subcontractors are engaged and provided with all necessary documentation to submit comprehensive and compliant tenders.

**1. Subcontractor Identification:**
*   Construction Subcontractors invited to tender will be identified by the project team. This identification process is typically guided by the [[project_execution_plan_overview]] (0-PRC-2003), which defines the overall project strategy and resource requirements.

**2. Preparation of Invitation Documents:**
*   The Subcontract Administrator is responsible for preparing covering letters addressed to each identified Construction Subcontractor.
*   These covering letters **must** explicitly reference [[instructions_to_construction_subcontractors_for_tender_0_spc_6002]]. This ensures that all prospective bidders are directed to the official guidelines for preparing their tender submissions.

**3. Issuance of Tender Documents:**
*   **Invitation Letters:** The Subcontract Administrator will forward the prepared letters of invitation to Document Control. Document Control is then responsible for the official issuance of these letters to the Construction Subcontractors, following the procedures outlined in [[control_of_project_documents_0_prc_0006]].
*   **Required Documents:** Concurrently, the Subcontract Administrator and the Project Engineer will compile and forward a comprehensive list of all required tender documents to Document Control. These documents, which include the [[construction_subcontract_agreement_template_and_content]] (with status "Issued for Quotation"), [[standard_conditions_for_construction_subcontract_0_spc_6001]], [[construction_subcontract_administrative_specifications]], and [[construction_subcontract_pricing_lists]], are then issued to the Construction Subcontractors by Document Control, also under [[control_of_project_documents_0_prc_0006]].

**4. Subcontractor Bid Preparation:**
*   Subcontractors utilize the provided documents, including the [[instructions_to_construction_subcontractors_for_tender_0_spc_6002]], to prepare their bids. This includes populating the various [[construction_subcontract_pricing_lists]] with their proposed rates and schedules.

**5. Post-Tender Negotiation and Revision:**
*   Following the submission and evaluation of tenders (refer to [[construction_subcontractor_evaluation_and_approval_0_prc_6003]]), negotiations are conducted with the successful Subcontractor.
*   The [[construction_subcontract_agreement_template_and_content]] and [[construction_subcontract_pricing_lists]] are then revised by the Subcontract Administrator to incorporate all agreed-upon modifications and information provided in the successful bid. These revised documents are then published with an "Approved for Construction" status.

**6. Final Issuance:**
*   Upon final approval, a signed paper copy of the Construction Subcontract Agreement, along with the approved [[construction_subcontractor_evaluation_and_approval_0_prc_6003]], is forwarded to Document Control for official issuance to the successful Construction Subcontractor, as detailed in [[construction_subcontract_issuance_procedure_0_prc_6005]].
*   A final list of all required documents is also forwarded to Document Control for issuance to the Construction Subcontractor.
