# Scrubber Venturi Internals Quality Record Definitions

This page defines the specific abbreviations used for Quality Record requirements within the [[0-QDS-7439_scrubber_venturi_internals_qds_administration|Scrubber Venturi Internals Quality Data Sheet (0-QDS-7439)]]. These definitions are crucial for understanding the documentation requirements for various inspection and testing activities.

For a broader understanding of quality record management, refer to the general [[0-QDS-7_quality_record_definitions|Quality Record Definitions]] page.

**Definitions:**
*   **AP (As Prepared):** This designation indicates that the Quality Record is required "As Prepared." This means the document should be submitted in its original, prepared format, typically by the vendor or responsible party, without necessarily being part of a larger compiled package.
*   **FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package):** This designation indicates that the Quality Record is required to be "Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package." This means the document **must** be compiled and submitted as part of the final Quality Assurance documentation package for the Scrubber Venturi Internals.

These definitions ensure clarity in the submission and compilation of all necessary quality documentation for the project.
