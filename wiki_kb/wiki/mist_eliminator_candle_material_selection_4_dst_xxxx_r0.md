# Mist Eliminator Candle Material Selection (4-DST-XXXX-R0)

This section specifies the mandatory material requirements for the Mist Eliminator Candle Datasheet (4-DST-XXXX-R0). The selection of appropriate materials is critical for ensuring the longevity, chemical resistance, and structural integrity of the mist eliminator candles under specified operating conditions.

**Component Material Requirements:**
The material for each of the following components must be clearly specified:
*   **Mist Eliminator Material:** The material for the mist eliminator itself is required.
*   **Outside Cage Material:** The material for the outside cage is required.
*   **Inside Cage Material:** The material for the inside cage is required.
*   **Cover Bolts & Nuts Material:** The material for the cover bolts and nuts is required.
*   **Mounting Bolts & Nuts Material:** The material for the mounting bolts and nuts is required.
*   **Mounting Gasket Material:** The material for the mounting gasket is required.

Material selection must consider the [[mist_eliminator_candle_operating_conditions_4_dst_xxxx_r0]] to ensure compatibility with the carrier fluid and entrained material, as well as the [[mist_eliminator_candle_physical_specifications_4_dst_xxxx_r0]] for structural integrity.
