# Control Valves - Actuators and Accessories

Actuators and their accessories are integral to the operation of control valves, ensuring precise and reliable valve positioning.

**Actuator and Accessory Materials:**
Materials of manufacture for the actuator, electro-pneumatic positioner, and all accessories **will be** selected to withstand process and/or ambient conditions. Special corrosion and ultra-violet resistant materials may be necessitated for certain applications, especially given the [[environmental_and_site_conditions|site conditions]].

**Fail-Safe Mechanisms:**
Where fail-safe operation is required and mechanical (spring) motive force is not available, accumulator backup ancillary devices **will be** used to ensure the valve moves to a safe position upon power or air failure.

**Actuator Types and Operation:**
*   Air operated actuators in combination with electro-pneumatic positioner units **will be** used in all applications unless specified otherwise. This rule does not apply to self-regulating valves.
*   An actuator shaft extension arrangement **will be** used, if necessary, to ensure that the temperature limit of the actuator and accessories is not exceeded.

**Actuator Fittings and Tubing:**
*   Valve actuators **will be** fitted with a 316 stainless steel or epoxy coated aluminium filter-regulator (whichever is appropriate).
*   Valve actuators **will be** fitted with a stainless steel air supply gauge.
*   All tubing and fittings for actuators **will be** 316 stainless steel and **will be** pre-assembled as far as is practicable.
*   Mounting brackets and bolting accessories for actuators **will be** provided in 316 SS.
*   316 SS vent bug screens **will be** provided for actuators.

**Actuator Sizing and Performance:**
*   The actuator **will be** specifically sized to satisfy process conditions such as shut-off pressure, operating time, “water-hammer" problems, and solids build-up (in slurries).
*   Actuators **will be** sized for operation at the minimum instrument air supply of 500 kPag.
*   Actuators and all related accessories **will be** rated for a maximum working pressure and differential pressure (where applicable) of at least 1000 kPag.
*   All on/off actuators **will be** sized and selected on an individual basis to best satisfy the specific process and operating conditions.
*   Actuator torque **will be** between 1.8 and 2.5 times the maximum breakout torque at minimum specified air pressure.

**Valve Travel Time:**
*   Maximum allowable valve travel time for Body Size < DN100 is 0.3 seconds.
*   Maximum allowable valve travel time for Body Size > 100 NB is equal to the normal body size in inches.
*   Pneumatic "buffer vessels" may be required to achieve the specified valve stroking times or where an air fail static design is prescribed. The design and construction of pneumatic "buffer vessels" **will be** in accordance with the applicable codes and standards.
*   Instrument air relays, quick vent valves, or volume boosters **will be** used as required.

**Positioners:**
*   Positioners **will be** provided on all modulating valves. Electro-pneumatic positioners **will be** used.
*   Direct or reverse action **will be** attainable on all positioners by simple field adjustments.
*   Valve positioners **will be** provided with pressure gauges (stainless steel casing) to indicate supply pressure and output pressure to the diaphragm operator.
*   Valve positioner cases **will be** IP65 rated and constructed of stainless steel, fibreglass, or other corrosion-resistant material.

**Hand Wheels:**
Generally, hand wheels **will not** be fitted to control valves or actuated on/off valves unless nominated on the data sheet. If hand wheels are fitted, they **will be** either top or side mounted and de-clutchable.

**Accessory Mounting:**
Valve accessories such as limit switches, positioners, transducers, solenoid valves, and relays **shall** be mounted on the valve if possible, provided that vibration and temperature do not affect operability. If direct mounting is not possible, accessories **will be** prewired to a terminal strip mounted within an IP65 (minimum) enclosure. Where beacon enclosures are provided on discrete valves (On/Off), the position switches and solenoid valve **will be** provided prewired to a terminal strip inside the beacon enclosure.

**Solenoid Valves:**
*   Solenoid valves **will be** non I.S. (Intrinsically Safe).
*   Solenoid valves **will be** 24 V d.c. and rated at less than 10 watt.
*   Direct acting solenoid operated valves are preferred.
*   Internal air pilot-operated spool valves **will be** used as required.
