# Chiller/Refrigeration Unit Design Notes

This page compiles specific design notes and requirements for Chiller/Refrigeration Units, as referenced in datasheet [[4-PDS-XXXX-R1_template_chiller_refrigeration_unit_datasheet_administration]].

**Note 1.0:** Vendor to recommend excess area based on fouling nature of fluids, and standard industry practice.

**Note 2.0:** The refrigeration unit must be insulated (piping & equipment in the cold section) to minimize heat gain from ambient air.

**Note 3.0:** Compressor to be screw type with sliding vane for capacity control from 0 to 100%.
