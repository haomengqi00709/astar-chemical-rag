# Stack Quality Data Sheet Quality Record Definitions

This section defines the specific codes used within the Stack Quality Data Sheet (0-QDS-7410) to denote requirements for quality records. These definitions ensure clarity and consistency in documentation and reporting.

**Quality Record Codes:**

*   **AP (As Prepared):** This designation indicates that a document or record **must** be prepared by the Vendor. It signifies that the creation of the record is a mandatory deliverable from the Vendor.
*   **FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package):** This designation indicates that a document or record **must** be included as part of the formal Quality Assurance Documentation Package submitted by the Vendor. This ensures that critical quality information is compiled and delivered for review and archiving.

These definitions are crucial for managing project documentation and ensuring that all necessary quality records are generated and submitted appropriately. For general information on quality management and documentation, refer to [[quality_management_and_compliance]] and [[document_control_periodic_reports]].
