# Final Strainer Aspen Phase Properties

This page outlines the vapor and liquid phase properties for the Final Strainer (Tag No. 7842K) as determined by Aspen simulations. These properties are critical for detailed engineering calculations and for understanding the fluid dynamics within the strainer.

**Aspen Vapor Phase Properties:**
*   **Density:** 1.15 kg/m3
*   **Viscosity:** 0.0235 cP
*   **Heat Capacity:** 0.979 kJ/kg-K
*   **Mole Fraction SO2:** 0.0032
*   **Mole Fraction SO3:** 0.0704

**Aspen Liquid Phase Properties:**
*   **Density:** 1789.4 kg/m3
*   **Viscosity:** 7.27 cP
*   **Heat Capacity:** 1.502 kJ/kg-K
*   **Weight Fraction H2SO4:** 0.9814
*   **Weight Fraction SO2:** 0.0
*   **Weight Fraction SO3:** 0.0

These phase properties are used in conjunction with the [[final_strainer_aspen_inlet_stream_data]] and [[final_strainer_aspen_outlet_stream_data]] to perform detailed hydraulic and mass transfer calculations for the strainer.
