# Site Objects and Surplus Materials (0-SPC-6001)

This page outlines the handling of objects of value, historical interest, and surplus materials on site under construction subcontract 0-SPC-6001.

**Objects of Value or Historical Interest**
The Subcontractor has no title to any object of value or of historical or other interest found within the site. The Subcontractor **shall not** move such an object unless instructed to do so by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Surplus Materials**
The Subcontractor has title to other surplus materials, except as specifically stated in the [[standard_conditions_for_construction_subcontract_0_spc_6001#Works Information|Works Information]]. The Subcontractor **shall** take all surplus materials away from the site.
