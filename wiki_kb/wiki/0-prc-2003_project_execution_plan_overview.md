# Project Execution Plan Overview (0-PRC-2003)

This document, Procedure 0-PRC-2003, outlines the comprehensive project requirements and details how Aker Kvaerner Chemetics will execute the project. It serves as a foundational guide for project implementation.

The overall scope of work encompasses a full Engineering, Procurement, and Construction (EPC) scope, including commissioning activities.

Key provisions of the Contract are summarised in Data available in SQL: 0-LST-2005.

The project execution plan is further detailed in [[0-prc-2003_table_1_project_execution_plan_details]].

Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Australia is the designated entity for conducting business in Queensland, Australia. To ensure proper separation of income tax liabilities between Canada and Australia, a clear distinction **must** be maintained between work performed in Australia and work performed outside of Australia. This distinction is managed through a specific project financial structure detailed in [[project_financial_and_tax_structure]].
