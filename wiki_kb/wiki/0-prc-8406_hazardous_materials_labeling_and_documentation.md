# Hazardous Materials Labeling and Documentation (0-PRC-8406)

Proper labeling and comprehensive documentation are mandatory for all hazardous materials to ensure safe handling, storage, and use. This section outlines the specific requirements for tagging, labeling, and providing Material Safety Data Sheets (MSDS).

**Labeling Requirements**
All hazardous goods **are to be** clearly tagged and labeled in accordance with the Workplace Hazardous Materials Information System (WHMIS) guidelines.
Furthermore, all hazardous goods **are to be** clearly labeled in:
*   English.
*   The language of the country of destination.

Hazardous goods that may pose a danger if improperly handled **will be** properly marked and identified with the appropriate labels.

**Documentation Attached to Goods**
For all hazardous goods, instructions for the proper handling and storage of the package **will be** attached on the outside in a secure manner.

Additionally, a copy of the Material Safety Data Sheets (MSDS) or handling instructions **will be** included in a suitable pouch attached to the goods inside the shipping crate. These internal instructions **will be** written in:
*   English.
*   The language of the destination country.
*   At a minimum, they **will** include the Material Safety Data Sheet for the material.

For goods that only pose a hazard when spent (expended), Material Safety Data Sheets or handling instructions **will** also be provided in a suitable pouch attached to the goods inside the shipping crate.

These measures ensure that critical safety information is readily available to personnel at all stages of transport and handling.
