# Third Party Inspection Non-Conformance Report (NCR) Process

The Non-Conformance Report (NCR) process is essential for documenting and managing deviations from specified requirements during third-party inspections. The Inspection Agency **shall** issue NCRs as required.

**Issuance Criteria**
An NCR **shall** be issued when equipment fails to comply with the requirements of the Purchase Order and cannot be made to comply by further work.

**NCR Numbering**
The NCR number is derived from the associated [[quality_data_sheets_management]] number.

**Nonconformance Identification**

*   **Vendor-Identified Nonconformances:** If a nonconformance is first identified by the Equipment Vendor, the Vendor’s own system and documentation **shall** be used. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **must** be included in the review and approval process for these vendor-identified nonconformances.
*   **Agency-Identified Nonconformances:** If a nonconformance is first identified by the Agency inspector, the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics NCR form [[0-NCR-7XXX_template_non_conformance_report]] **shall** be used. This form **shall** be completed by the Agency inspector.

**Submission and Documentation**
*   The completed Aker Kvaerner Chemetics NCR form **shall** be forwarded to ChemeticsQA@akerkvaerner.com.
*   Any Vendor proposal(s) to correct the nonconformance **shall** be forwarded along with the NCR.
*   The Vendor’s NCR reference number **shall** be clearly shown on the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics NCR.

**Timing**
Nonconformance Reports **shall** be issued within two days of the activity. An example of the initial revision of an NCR is provided in the reference documents.

For related information, see [[inspection_report_requirements]] and [[inspection_release_process_third_party]].
