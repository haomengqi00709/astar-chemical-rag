# Flow Measurement - Magnetic and Vortex

Magnetic and vortex flowmeters are specialized instruments suitable for particular fluid types and process conditions.

**Magnetic Flowmeters:**
Magnetic flow meters are preferred for liquid applications, especially those with suspended solids.
*   **Velocity for Suspended Solids:** For liquid applications with suspended solids, the ideal velocity through the flow tube **will be** in the range of 1.5 to 3 meters per second. This velocity is crucial to prevent sedimentation of solids and to minimize wear on the liner.
*   **High Magnetic Field Applications:** Foxboro AC type magnetic flow tubes **will** be provided for service in the proximity of the cells, specifically due to the high magnetic fields present in these areas (refer to [[environmental_and_site_conditions|Environmental and Site Conditions]] and [[signal_transmission_and_power|Signal Transmission and Power]]).
*   **Material Selection:** Magnetic flowmeter tubes, liners, and electrodes **will** be of materials to suit the application. The effects of pressure, chemical corrosion, abrasiveness, magnetic content, and conductivity of the measured fluid **will** be taken into consideration when specifying magnetic flowmeters.
*   **Installation:** Where practically possible, magnetic flow meters **will** be installed in vertical lines with an upward flow. If vertical installation is not practical, the piping arrangement **will** be designed to ensure the flow meter is maintained full of process fluid at all times.
*   **Transmitter Integration:** Where possible, the transmitter **will** be integral to the magnetic flowmeter body.
*   **Body Types:** Wafer body flowmeters **will** be used for applications where the line size is 200 mm or less in diameter, if appropriate. Flanged flowmeters **will** be used where the line size is 250 mm diameter or greater.

**Vortex Flowmeters:**
Vortex shedding meters are suitable for clean fluids but have specific limitations.
*   **Applications:** Suitable applications for vortex flowmeters are clean gas or steam services with non-pulsating flow. They may also be used for clean or low conductivity liquids.
*   **Limitations:** Vortex meters **will not** be used where the piping may be subjected to vibration, as vibration can interfere with their operation. They **will not** be used on scaling services. Vortex meters require a minimum flow to produce a signal, which must be considered during selection.
*   **Body Types:** Wafer body flowmeters **will** be used for vortex flowmeter applications where the line size is 200 mm or less in diameter.
