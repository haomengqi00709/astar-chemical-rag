# Proposer Costs and Confidentiality for Construction Subcontract Tenders (0-SPC-6002)

This section outlines the Proposer's responsibilities regarding costs incurred during the tender process and the strict confidentiality requirements for all provided documentation.

**Proposer's Responsibility for Costs**
All costs incurred by the Proposer in preparing their offer **are** the sole responsibility of the Proposer. This includes, but is not limited to, costs incurred by any suppliers, sub-subcontractors, or any other parties involved in assisting the Proposer with their offer submission. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **will not** reimburse the Proposer for any costs associated with the preparation of the offer, regardless of the outcome of the tender process. Proposers **should** factor these costs into their business operations when deciding to participate in a tender.

**Confidentiality Requirements**
The Proposer **must** take all reasonable measures to maintain the strict [[rfq_confidentiality_and_document_ownership|confidentiality]] of the Request for Tender and all referenced documents provided by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. This obligation extends to all parties associated with the Proposer's offer preparation.

When engaging suppliers or sub-subcontractors, the Proposer **must** ensure that these parties are only given sufficient information to enable them to provide their specific contribution to the Proposer's offer. No more information than is strictly necessary **shall** be disclosed.

**Document Ownership and Return/Destruction**
The Request for Tender and all documents provided to the Proposer by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **remain** the exclusive property of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. The Proposer **must** return all originals and copies of the Request for Tender and all associated documents to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics upon demand. Alternatively, upon demand, the Proposer **must** destroy all originals and copies of these documents. This ensures the protection of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' [[intellectual_property_and_design_use|intellectual property]] and proprietary information throughout and after the tender process. Adherence to these confidentiality and document ownership clauses is mandatory.
