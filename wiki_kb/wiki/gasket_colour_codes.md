# Gasket Colour Codes

The following colour codes are used to identify specific gasket types:

*   **Gasket C (EPDM, Peroxide Cured):** Yellow
*   **Gasket D (Butyl Rubber):** Red
*   **Gasket T (Viton B):** Blue
*   **Gasket V (Plasticized PVC):** Green
