# Safety Report

The Safety Report is a detailed document that provides comprehensive information on project safety performance. It is referenced in the [[0-rep-2001_monthly_progress_report_format|Monthly Progress Report]] and typically includes details on key safety activities, incidents, and compliance with [[hse_management_and_employee_responsibilities|HSE policies]].
