# Field Erected SS Tank Shop Component Material and Welding Certifications

This section outlines the requirements for material and welding certifications for the Field Erected Stainless Steel Tank Shop Component (0-QDS-7438).

**Mill Test Certificates:**
*   Mill Test Certificates **must** be designated as a [[quality_record_definitions|Quality Record FP]].
*   Chemetics surveillance for Mill Test Certificates **must** be "Complete", as detailed in [[0-QDS-7438_chemetics_surveillance_level]].

These requirements ensure that all materials used in the fabrication of the tank components meet the specified quality and metallurgical standards.
