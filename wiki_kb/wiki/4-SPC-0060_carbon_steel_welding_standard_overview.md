# Carbon Steel Welding Standard Overview (4-SPC-0060)

Document 4-SPC-0060 defines Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ requirements for welding and associated procedures for carbon steel. This specification applies to vessels, equipment, and pipe spools made of carbon steel in the ASME P-No. 1 classification.

This specification is a complete re-write of the previous document, 4-SPC-0019-R7: Equipment Welding Carbon Steel. Content not directly related to welding or already covered in the ASME code has been removed from this specification to streamline its focus.

The standard is primarily written for the [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels]] code. For projects requiring codes from other jurisdictions (e.g., Australia, Europe), this standard will be modified as necessary to ensure compliance.

General codes and standards for design and fabrication are listed in [[4-SPC-0040_metallic_process_vessels_fabrication_overview|4-SPC-0040]], titled "Shop Fabricated Metallic Vessels and Metallic Vessel Components."
