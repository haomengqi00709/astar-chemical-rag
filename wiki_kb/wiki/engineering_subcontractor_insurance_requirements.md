# Engineering Subcontractor Insurance Requirements (0-SPC-2201)

Under Professional Services Subcontract 0-SPC-2201, specific insurance requirements are mandated for the Subcontractor to mitigate risks associated with the provision of services.

**Mandatory Insurances:**
*   The Subcontractor **must** provide the insurances detailed in [[0-SPC-2201_table_2]].
*   Additionally, the Subcontractor **must** provide any other insurances stated in the [[subcontract_definitions_0_spc_2201|Subcontract Data]], unless the Subcontract Data explicitly states that Aker Kvaerner Chemetics is responsible for providing a particular insurance.
*   These insurances **must** provide continuous coverage from the [[subcontract_definitions_0_spc_2201|Subcontract Date]] until the end of the periods specified in the Subcontract Data.

**Verification of Insurance:**
*   Upon request by either Party, the other Party **must** provide a certificate from its insurer or broker confirming that all required insurances are currently in force.

**Limitation of Liability:**
*   The Subcontractor’s liability to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, arising from any failure to [[subcontract_definitions_0_spc_2201|Provide the Services]], is limited to the amount explicitly stated in the [[subcontract_definitions_0_spc_2201|Subcontract Data]]. This limitation helps define the financial exposure of the Subcontractor in the event of performance issues.

These insurance and liability provisions are crucial for establishing a secure contractual framework and ensuring that both parties are adequately protected against potential financial and operational risks throughout the duration of the subcontract.
