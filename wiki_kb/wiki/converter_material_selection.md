# Converter Material Selection

The following materials are specified for the construction of the Converter vessel (Tag No. 7540R), ensuring compliance with the design conditions outlined in [[converter_vessel_design_conditions]]. All dimensions are in millimeters unless otherwise specified.

**Primary Components:**
*   **Shell & Heads:** A-240-304H
*   **Core:** A-240-304H
*   **Grid Plates:** A-240-304H
*   **Baffle Plates:** A-240-304H
*   **Core Base Plate:** A-240-304H

**Structural and Support Components:**
*   **Base Ring:** A-240-304
*   **Anchor Chairs:** A-240-304
*   **Anchor Bolts:** A-307-B
*   **Grillage Beams:** A-36

**Nozzle Components:**
*   **Nozzle Necks (plate):** A-240-304H
*   **Nozzle Necks (pipe):** A-312-TP304H
*   **Nozzle Flanges:** A-182-F304H
*   **Nozzle Bolts:** A-193-B7
*   **Nozzle Nuts:** A-194-2H
*   **Nozzle Gaskets:** Garlock HTC 9800
*   **Repads:** A-240-304H

**Insulation:**
*   **Insulation:** 200mm Mineral Wool (supplied by others)

For detailed specifications of each nozzle, refer to the [[converter_nozzle_schedule]]. Material and welding certifications are critical for quality assurance and are detailed in documents such as [[0-QDS-7411_material_and_welding_certifications]].
