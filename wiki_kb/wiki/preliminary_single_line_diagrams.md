# Preliminary Single Line Diagrams

Preliminary Single Line Diagrams (SLDs) are used in the early stages of a project for initial equipment sizing and pricing. They provide a high-level overview of the electrical distribution system without extensive detail.

**Purpose and Content**
Preliminary single line diagrams **shall** show the electrical distribution equipment included in the bid proposal. The electrical distribution equipment is not detailed in preliminary single line diagrams and is subject to change as the detailed design progresses.

These diagrams are used for:
*   Preliminary equipment sizing and pricing.
*   Determining major changes to the electrical equipment.
*   Determining major changes to the arrangement.
*   Recording costs for any changes.

Preliminary single line diagram drawings **will** be used as a guide for creating the new [[final_single_line_diagrams]].
