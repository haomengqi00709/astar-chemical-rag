# Non-Conformance Report (NCR) 0-NCR-7

**Description Section Requirements:**
The Description section of the NCR **must** clearly describe the Non-Conformance itself. It **must** also detail how the Non-Conformance was determined and by whom it was identified.

**Vendor's Proposal (Corrective Action):**
This section **must** describe the specific work proposed by the Vendor to correct the identified non-conformance. This proposal **must** also include details of any further inspection or testing deemed necessary to verify the correction. The Vendor **may not** proceed with any proposed corrective action work until formal approval has been granted by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Approval (Corrective Action):**
Upon review, Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **must** formally approve the Vendor's Corrective Action Proposal. This approval **must** include an instruction to proceed with the work. Furthermore, the approval **must** clearly indicate any required witness points or hold points during the execution of the corrective actions. This ensures proper oversight and quality control.

**Inspection and Testing Results:**
Following the implementation of corrective actions, this section **must** document the success of these actions. It **must** refer to the Vendor's official Inspection and Test Reports as evidence. In instances where the corrective action is not successful, this section **must** describe the final disposition of the non-conforming item.

**Vendor's Proposal (Preventive Action):**
Beyond correcting the immediate non-conformance, this section **must** describe any proposed modifications to existing procedures by the Vendor. These modifications are intended to prevent the recurrence of the non-conformance in the future. Similar to corrective actions, the Vendor **may not** proceed with implementing these proposed procedural changes until formal approval is received from Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Approval (Preventive Action):**
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **must** review and approve the Vendor's Preventive Action Proposal. The approval **must** include an instruction to proceed with the implementation of the proposed changes. Any necessary witness points or hold points related to the implementation of preventive actions **must** also be indicated in this approval.
