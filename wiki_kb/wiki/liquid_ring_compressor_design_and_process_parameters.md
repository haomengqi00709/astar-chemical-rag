# Liquid Ring Compressor Package Process Design Conditions

This page outlines the mandatory process design conditions for the Liquid Ring Compressor Package, as specified in Datasheet 4-PKG-XXXX-R1. These conditions are critical for the proper sizing, selection, and operation of the compressor. The datasheet requires detailed information for both DESIGN (Inlet, Outlet) and ALTERNATE (Inlet, Outlet) operating scenarios.

**Mandatory Process Design Conditions**
For both DESIGN and ALTERNATE conditions, the following parameters **must** be specified for both inlet and outlet streams:

*   **Fluid Description:** A clear description of the process fluid.
*   **Composition:** The composition of the fluid, expressed in %v/v (percent by volume).
*   **Solids Content/Type:** Any solids present in the fluid, including their content and type.
*   **Flowrate:** The normal operating flowrate in kg/h.
*   **Minimum Flowrate:** The minimum operating flowrate in kg/h.
*   **Flowrate (Actual Volume):** The actual volumetric flowrate in Am3/h.
*   **Minimum Flowrate (Actual Volume):** The minimum actual volumetric flowrate in Am3/h.
*   **Temperature:** The operating temperature in °C.
*   **Pressure:** The operating pressure in kPa(g) (kilopascals gauge).
*   **Density:** The fluid density in kg/m3.
*   **Compressibility Factor:** The compressibility factor of the gas.
*   **Viscosity:** The fluid viscosity in cP (centipoise).
*   **Specific Heat:** The specific heat capacity of the fluid in kJ/kg·°C.
*   **Molecular Weight:** The molecular weight of the fluid in kg/kmol.
*   **cp / cv Ratio:** The ratio of specific heat at constant pressure (cp) to specific heat at constant volume (cv).

These parameters are essential for ensuring the compressor meets the required performance under various operating conditions. Refer to [[process_design_criteria_1_prc_0001]] for general process design guidelines.
