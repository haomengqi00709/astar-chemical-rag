# Nozzle Flange Flatness Tolerances (4-SPC-0044 Table 3)

This page describes the content of `[TABLE: 4-SPC-0044_table_3]`, which specifies the flatness tolerances for nozzle flanges on glass-lined carbon steel reaction vessels.

**Table Content:**
The table provides specific dimensional tolerances for the flatness and parallelism of nozzle flange faces relative to the indicated plane. These tolerances are critical for ensuring proper sealing and assembly of components connected to the vessel. This table is directly referenced by the [[4-SPC-0044_tolerances_and_dimensions|Tolerances and Dimensions]] section of the specification to ensure manufacturing precision.
