# General Packaging Requirements (0-SPC-5004)

The Vendor **must** ensure that all goods are adequately packaged to withstand the rigors of land transportation and extended storage. Packaging **must** provide comprehensive protection against various environmental and physical hazards, including:
*   Physical damage
*   Temperature extremes
*   Corrosion
*   Pilferage
*   Condensation
*   Rain

All packaging materials supplied become the property of [Company Name] and are non-returnable.

**Preparation of Goods:**
Prior to packaging, the Vendor **must** drain, dry, and clean all goods.

**Storage and Handling:**
Packaging **must** be adequate for extended storage. The Vendor **shall** advise [Company Name] of any particular storage or handling requirements at the time of order placement.

**Dangerous Goods:**
The Vendor **must** comply with all regulations governing the transportation of dangerous goods. Furthermore, the Vendor **shall** advise [Company Name] of any dangerous goods at the time of order placement. Refer to [[hazardous_materials_management_overview]] for related procedures.

**Packaging Design:**
The Vendor **must** package goods in a manner consistent with their size, weight, and characteristics. Packaging **must** allow for an even distribution of weight and enable packages to be handled safely using standard equipment such as cranes, forklift trucks, or bogies/dollies.

For specific details on packaging materials and construction, refer to [[0-SPC-5004_packaging_materials_and_construction]]. For protection of individual components, see [[0-SPC-5004_component_protection_for_shipment]].
