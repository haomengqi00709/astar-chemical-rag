# HSE Audits and Inspections Overview

This procedure (Document ID: 0-PRC-8009) outlines the requirements for Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) audits and inspections within the Company. It **SHALL** apply to all locations where the Company maintains full control over the operation of health and safety at the worksite.

**Purpose**
HSE audits and inspections **SHALL** be utilized for the following key objectives:
*   To identify and correct unsafe work practices and hazards.
*   To ensure compliance with Aker Kvaerner, Company, and statutory health and safety requirements and standards.
*   To measure the level of adherence to Aker Kvaerner procedures, Company procedures, and statutory HSE regulations.
*   To guide continuous improvement in HSE performance.

**Definitions**
*   **Audit**: An in-depth review of HSE procedural requirements and the level of their implementation at a specific worksite.
*   **Company**: Refers to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. For more information, see [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_name_change_history]].
*   **HSE**: Stands for Health, Safety, and Environment.
*   **Inspection**: A systematic and predetermined review of the worksite and working environment, aimed at identifying hazards and conditions that could negatively impact health, safety, and the environment.
