# Bridge Crane Non-Destructive Examination Requirements

This page details the requirements for Non-Destructive Examination (NDE) for the Bridge Crane project, as specified in Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7441. NDE is crucial for ensuring the structural integrity and quality of welded components without causing damage to the material.

**NDE Standards:**
All Non-Destructive Examination **must** be performed in accordance with ANSI/AWS Structural Welding Code D1.1 or an approved equivalent standard. The vendor **shall** ensure that all NDE procedures and personnel are qualified to these standards.

**NDE Activities:**
Non-Destructive Testing (NDT) is a mandatory item or activity within the quality assurance process. Specific NDT methods that may be required include:
*   **Dye Penetrant:** Dye Penetrant testing is an item or activity used to detect surface-breaking defects in non-porous materials.

The results of all NDE activities **must** be documented and included in the Quality Assurance Documentation Package as required.

For an overview of the vendor's general quality assurance obligations, refer to [[0-QDS-7441_bridge_crane_vendor_quality_assurance_requirements]].
