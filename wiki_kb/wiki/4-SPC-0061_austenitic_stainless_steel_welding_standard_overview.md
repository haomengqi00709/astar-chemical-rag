# Austenitic Stainless Steel Welding Standard Overview (4-SPC-0061)

This specification, 4-SPC-0061, defines Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ requirements for welding and associated procedures for austenitic stainless steel. It is a complete re-write of the previous standard, 4-SPC-0013-R7: Equipment Welding Austenitic Stainless Steel.

**Scope of Application**
The requirements outlined in this specification apply to vessels, equipment, and pipe spools fabricated from austenitic stainless steel, specifically those classified under ASME P-No. 8.

**Referenced Standards and Codes**
For the design and fabrication of shop-fabricated metallic vessels and metallic vessel components, refer to [[4-SPC-0040_metallic_process_vessels_fabrication_overview]]. All weld filler metals **shall** meet the requirements of [[ASME_bpvc_section_ii_materials]], Part C. Passivation of stainless steel **shall** conform to the requirements of [[ASTM_a380_cleaning_passivation_stainless_steel]].

**Jurisdictional Requirements**
When a project necessitates compliance with codes from other jurisdictions, such as Australia or Europe, this standard **will** be modified as required to ensure adherence to local regulations.
