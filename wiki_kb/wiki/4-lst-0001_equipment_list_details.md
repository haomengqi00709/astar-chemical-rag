# Equipment List (4-LST-0001)

The Equipment List (4-LST-0001) is a critical project document that records and tracks all equipment items. It serves as a central repository for key information, including the assigned [[equipment_classification_types]] for each piece of equipment.

**Content**
The contents of the Equipment List are detailed in the 4-LST-0001 [Template].
Data available in SQL: 4-LST-0001

**Document Preparation and Approval**
The preparation, checking, and approval of the Equipment List follow a defined workflow:

*   **Preparation:** The Equipment List **must** be prepared by Mechanical personnel.
*   **Checking:** The document **must** be checked by Process and Technology.
*   **Approval:** Final approval **must** be granted by the Project Engineer.

**Revision History**
The Equipment List was formally added to the 4-PRC-0003 procedure in Revision 2, highlighting its importance in the overall equipment documentation process.
