# Contractor Responsibilities for 7-SPC-0005

The contractor holds primary responsibility for various aspects related to the protective coating system as defined by this Engineering Standard.

**Contractor Responsibilities:**
*   The contractor will be responsible for all requirements relating to the supply of coated pipe.
*   The contractor will be responsible for all requirements relating to the installation of coated pipe.
*   The contractor will be responsible for all requirements relating to the welding of coated pipe.
*   The contractor will be responsible for all requirements relating to the testing of coated pipe.
*   The contractor will be responsible for all requirements relating to the coating of fittings.
*   The contractor will be responsible for all requirements relating to the testing of fittings.
*   The contractor will be responsible for all requirements relating to backfilling.

**Delegated Responsibilities:**
*   Excavation may be the responsibility of others, depending upon project requirements.
*   Supply of fittings may be the responsibility of others, depending upon project requirements.
