# Electrical Block Diagrams

Electrical Block Diagrams provide a high-level representation of electrical equipment and their interconnections using simplified boxes and lines. These diagrams are useful for visualizing system architecture and referencing detailed documentation.

**Content of Block Diagrams**
Block diagrams **shall** show:
*   A box to represent various pieces of electrical equipment.
*   Electrical equipment in block diagrams, which **will** be interconnected by cables.
*   The box for each piece of electrical equipment, which **will** include the equipment number.
*   The vendor drawing numbers for that piece of equipment.
*   Boxes interconnected with lines to represent the necessary cabling.
*   Cable numbers shown on the lines.

**References and Usage**
Block diagrams **shall** reference the [[electrical_installation_details]] to show the installation method. They **shall** also reference the [[power_and_control_cables_list]] for a detailed description of the cables.

Block diagrams **will** have notes about the vendor drawings being marked up to show the terminations of the cables.
