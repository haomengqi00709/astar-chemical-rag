# Power and Control Cables List

The Power and Control Cables List provides a comprehensive record of all cables required for a project, including their descriptions, specifications, and routing information. This list is critical for procurement and installation.

**Content of the Cable List**
The power and control cables list **shall** include:
*   All required power and control cables and their descriptions.
*   HV (High Voltage) power cables and associated control cables.
*   MV (Medium Voltage) power cables and associated control cables.
*   LV (Low Voltage) power cables and associated control cables.
*   Power and control cables associated with all motors.
*   Power cables required for DCS control equipment.
*   Power cables required for auxiliary equipment.
*   The voltage rating of the insulation.
*   The quantity and size of the conductors per cable.
*   The color of the internal conductors.
*   The color of the outer jacket.
*   The cable number.
*   Equipment names at each end of the cable.
*   The length of the cable.
*   The routing of the cable.

Cable descriptions on the power and control cable list **shall** agree with the [[electrical_design_criteria]].

**Purpose and Usage**
The power and control cable list is used for purchasing cables for the project. It ensures that all necessary cables are identified, specified correctly, and procured efficiently.
