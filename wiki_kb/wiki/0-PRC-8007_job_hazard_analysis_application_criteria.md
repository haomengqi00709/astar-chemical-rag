# Job Hazard Analysis (JHA) Application Criteria

A [[0-PRC-8007_job_hazard_analysis]] (JHA) is a mandatory requirement under specific conditions to ensure the systematic identification and control of hazards. A JHA **is required** for the following:

*   **Confined Space Entry:** All confined space entry or confined space work. Refer to [[confined_space_hazard_assessment_requirements]] for specific details.
*   **Work Permit/Order Requirement:** If the Work Permit or Work Order explicitly states that a JHA is a requirement.
*   **High-Risk Jobs:** Any other jobs identified as high-risk.
*   **History of Incidents:** Jobs with a history of, or potential for, injury, illness, or environmental impact.
*   **Severe Accident Potential:** High-risk jobs where a single human error could lead to a severe accident or injury.
*   **Unassessed Risks:** Jobs that have not yet been assessed for risk.
*   **Process/Procedure Changes:** Jobs that have undergone changes in processes and procedures.
*   **New Employees:** Jobs where new employees are performing the task.

These criteria ensure that JHA is applied proactively to mitigate risks and maintain a safe working environment.
