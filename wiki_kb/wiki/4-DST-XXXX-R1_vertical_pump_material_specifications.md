# Template Vertical Pump Material Specifications

This page details the material specifications for the various components of the vertical pump, as outlined in the Template Vertical Pump Datasheet (4-DST-XXXX-R1). Proper [[material_selection]] is crucial for corrosion resistance, mechanical strength, and overall longevity of the pump in its intended service.

**Component Material Requirements:**

*   **Casing:** Material specified for the pump housing.
*   **Impeller:** Material specified for the rotating component that imparts energy to the fluid.
*   **Impeller Rings:** Materials for wear rings associated with the impeller.
*   **Casing Rings:** Materials for wear rings associated with the pump casing.
*   **Shaft:** Material specified for the main rotating shaft.
*   **Shaft Cover:** Material for any protective cover over the shaft.
*   **Cover Plate:** Material for the pump's cover plate.
*   **O-Rings & Gaskets:** Materials for sealing components, selected for chemical compatibility and temperature resistance.
*   **Discharge Pipe:** Material for the pipe connecting to the pump's discharge.
*   **Packing:** Material for the packing used in the shaft seal area (if applicable).
*   **Bearings:** Materials for the pump's bearings.

All specified materials **MUST** meet the project's [[vendor_quality_assurance]] requirements and relevant industry standards.
