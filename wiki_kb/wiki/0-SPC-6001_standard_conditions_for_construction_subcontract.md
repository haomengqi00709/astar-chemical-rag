# 0-SPC-6001 Standard Conditions for Construction Subcontract

The 0-SPC-6001 document outlines the "Standard Conditions for Construction Subcontract." This specification establishes the fundamental contractual terms and definitions that govern construction subcontracts.

Specifically, Section 24.4 of this document states that the amount due to the Subcontractor is the "Price for Work Done to Date." This crucial term is further defined in Section 2.2 (16) of 0-SPC-6001. The definitions provided in this document are essential for understanding how payment claims are calculated and validated, as detailed in [[construction_subcontract_price_for_work_done_to_date]]. All subcontractors **must** adhere to these standard conditions for the administration and execution of their work.
