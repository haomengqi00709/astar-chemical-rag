# Equipment, Materials, and Title (0-SPC-6001)

This page details the requirements for the delivery, title, and handling of Equipment and Materials under construction subcontract 0-SPC-6001.

**Delivery and Title Transfer**
The Subcontractor **shall** deliver the [[standard_conditions_for_construction_subcontract_0_spc_6001#Equipment and Materials|Equipment and Materials]] to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics as described in the [[standard_conditions_for_construction_subcontract_0_spc_6001#Works Information|Works Information]]. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics retains title to any [[free_issue_materials_policy|free issue materials]].

Title to any Equipment and Materials held by the Subcontractor passes to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics upon delivery. Title also passes to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for any part of the Equipment and Materials for which Aker Kvaerner Chemetics has made a payment that included the Price or part of the Price.

**Part Payments and Encumbrances**
A payment of the Price or part of the Price for Equipment and Materials before delivery is considered a part payment, not a deposit. When a part payment is made, the Subcontractor **shall** hold that part of the Equipment and Materials in trust and as bailee for Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. The Subcontractor **shall** keep this part of the Equipment and Materials free from all claims, charges, and other encumbrances.

The Subcontractor authorizes Aker Kvaerner Chemetics to give notice of or register a charge, lien, security or security interest, make a UCC or PPSA filing, or its equivalent in any jurisdiction, to evidence Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' interest in the Equipment and Materials.

**Storage, Protection, and Testing**
The Subcontractor **shall** properly store, adequately protect, and label as property for the Project each part of the Equipment and Materials, regardless of whether title is retained by or has passed to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

The Subcontractor **shall not** deliver any Equipment and Materials that the Works Information states are to be tested or inspected before delivery, until Aker Kvaerner Chemetics has notified the Subcontractor that they have passed the test or inspection.
