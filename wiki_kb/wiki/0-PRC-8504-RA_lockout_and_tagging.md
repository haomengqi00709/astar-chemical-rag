# Lockout and Tagout Procedure Overview

This document, identified as 0-PRC-8504, outlines the Procedure for Lockout and Tagout. Its primary purpose is to ensure the safety and health of personnel and to protect equipment from unexpected energy release.

**Scope and Applicability**
This procedure defines the minimum requirements for safety Lock and Tag out, applying to all situations where work is performed on or around equipment capable of having an [[lockout_tagout_definitions#energy_source|energy source]] applied. This includes precautions required prior to servicing or repairing power-driven equipment.

While not the primary focus of this document, it is noted that precautions are also required before entering an enclosed space. For detailed procedures on confined space entry, refer to [[0-PRC-8502-A_confined_space_entry_procedure]] or [[0-PRC-8502-r1_confined_space_entry]].

The procedure **will** be followed by the Commissioning Team, which includes Aker Kvaerner Chemetics personnel, Contractors, and Vendor representatives. It **will** be implemented after a circuit, any system, or equipment is handed over mechanically completed for commissioning by the Construction Manager to the Commissioning Manager. A Client’s regulation **may** be followed if its requirements and guidelines fully cover those spelled out in Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ procedures.

**Foundational Basis**
This procedure is based on Aker Kvaerner’s Energy Isolation (Lock, Tag, Test and Try) procedure, issued in January 2003. This foundational document can be found in the [[aker_kvaerner_hse_policy_manual|AK’s Safety Policy Manual]], section 7, item 7.10.

**Hazard Assessment**
The hazard assessment created for the task **must** be understood by all members of the team involved with Lock and Tag out activity.

**Terminology**
The word "will" denotes a mandatory requirement. The word "should" denotes an advisory recommendation. A slash (/) denotes "and/or". For a comprehensive list of terms, refer to [[lockout_tagout_definitions]].

**General Applicability and Site-Specific Conditions**
The requirements of this procedure apply to all situations where the unexpected application of power, start-up, or release of stored energy of the equipment/process would be likely to endanger personnel or damage equipment. Any safety lock & tag out procedure prepared for a site-specific or task-specific condition **will** conform to the minimum standards for personnel or equipment protection as set forth within this procedure. When work is being performed on an existing facility, the procedure **should** be guided by the client’s active “in plant” procedure. However, the procedure in use **will** conform to the minimum safety standards for personnel and equipment protection as set forth within this procedure.

**Work Permit Requirement**
A work permit **is required** to perform work on equipment in a plant under commissioning or operation. For general safe work permit procedures, refer to [[0-prc-8501_safe_work_permit_procedure_overview]].

**Pre-Work Isolation Requirements**
Work **may only begin** after shutting off and locking out all electrical power sources to the equipment. Work **may only begin** after shutting off and locking out any other sources of energy that can provide motive power to the equipment. Work **may only begin** after shutting off and locking out valves controlling flows to the equipment. If possible, blinding line between equipment and controlling valve **is required**. On pressurized systems, pressure between the equipment and the locked out controlling valve **must** be released. A [[lockout_tagout_devices_and_tags#danger_tags|DANGER TAG]] **will** be placed at the power sources of all equipment being serviced. A [[lockout_tagout_devices_and_tags#danger_tags|DANGER TAG]] **will** be placed at all valves controlling flows into the equipment.
