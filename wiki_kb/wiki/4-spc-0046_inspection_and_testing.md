# Sulphuric Acid Storage Tank Inspection and Testing (4-SPC-0046)

This section outlines the inspection and testing requirements for sulphuric acid storage tanks as per specification 4-SPC-0046.

**Inspection Access and Responsibility**
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or its representative **must** have access to the work at all times for inspection purposes. The manufacturer **must** provide adequate notice and facilities for inspection. It is important to note that Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' inspection does not relieve the manufacturer of their overall responsibility for quality and compliance. After bottom plates are fitted and tack welded, no further welds on the bottom **must** be carried out until accepted by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or its representative.

**Radiographic Inspection**
The manufacturer **must** provide and schedule radiographic inspection. The following welds **must** be radiographed:
*   All vertical welds in all courses **must** be 100% radiographed.
*   All T-joints between horizontal and vertical shell welds **must** be radiographed.
*   Horizontal welds between shell courses **must** be spot radiographed as specified in [[API_650_welded_steel_tanks|API Standard 650, Paragraph 6.1.2]]. At least eight radiographs per horizontal seam are required.
*   All radial welds on annular ring and bridge plates **must** be 100% radiographed.
*   All T-joints on double butt welded floors (if used) **must** be radiographed.
*   5% of the remaining floor welds (if double butt welded floors are used) **must** be radiographed.
*   At least one radiograph **must** be made randomly of the work of each welder after completing their first 10 feet of welding in each position.
*   Tanks to be internally protected with a phenolic coating **will** be subject to spot radiography requirements as outlined in Section 6 of [[API_650_welded_steel_tanks]].

**Radiograph Records and Retention**
The manufacturer **must** retain all radiographic results for a period of at least 5 years, unless a longer retention period is required by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. After 5 years, the manufacturer **must** ascertain if Aker Kvaerner Chemetics wishes to have the radiographs for its files. Radiograph locations **must** be marked temporarily on the tank (e.g., by paint or crayon), and a permanent record of these locations **must** be provided to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Other Testing**
Hydrostatic testing of the tank is not required. However, other tests as specified under Section 5.3 of [[API_650_welded_steel_tanks|API Standard 650]] **must** be carried out. Non-destructive examination (NDE) procedures **must** conform to [[ASME_section_v_non_destructive_examination|ASME Boiler & Pressure Vessel Codes Section V]].
