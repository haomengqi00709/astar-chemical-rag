# Inspection and Testing Requirements for Stabilized Austenitic Stainless Steel (4-SPC-0014)

This page details the inspection and testing requirements for Columbium Stabilized High Nickel Austenitic stainless steel fabrications, as specified in document 4-SPC-0014.

**Inspector Access and Authority**
*   The Inspector has free access to the Vendor's work and to their Subvendor's (if work is sublet by approval) at all reasonable times.
*   The Inspector shall be at liberty to inspect the work during fabrication at any stage in manufacture.
*   The Inspector shall be at liberty to reject any material or workmanship which does not conform to this Standard or the applicable code.
*   Inspection by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics does not relieve the Fabricator of responsibility.
*   Material found to be unsatisfactory shall be rejected or repaired to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' satisfaction, at the Vendor’s cost.

**Dimensional Tolerances**
Dimensional tolerances shall be as stated on Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' drawing or equipment specification. In the absence of dimensional tolerance reference, Aker Kvaerner Chemetics ESDO/001 shall apply.

**Material and Consumable Certification**
*   Supplier must make available to the purchaser's inspector copies of chemical certification for each batch of electrodes or wire coils used.
*   If electrodes, wire, etc., cannot be identified to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' satisfaction, samples shall be removed for chemical analysis at the Supplier’s account.

**Production Weld Chemical Analysis**
*   When required by purchase order, a chemical analysis shall be performed on production welds of critical equipment. Critical equipment includes pressure vessels, heat exchangers, pressure piping, and certain proprietary items. Chemical analysis shall also include non-pressure retaining equipment in dangerous or demanding services.
*   The weld(s) to be checked shall be selected at random by the Inspector.
*   Where results of chemical analysis show that a specific weldment falls outside of the approved specification or code requirements, the Supplier shall promptly advise Aker Kvaerner Chemetics in writing of their proposed course of action. This proposed action shall include details of any additional testing necessary to fully define the extent of the non-conformance.
*   All repairs and retests related to chemical analysis non-conformance shall be to the Supplier’s account.

**Dye Penetrant (PT) Examination**
*   Items fabricated in accordance with [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels]] shall be 100% PT examined on all welds.
*   Unless otherwise specified, non-coded items shall have butt and fillet welds, nozzle attachment welds including skirt to shell welds, etc., PT examined for at least 25% of each welder/process used.
*   In the case of piping, 25% PT examination refers to 100% PT examination of 25% of the production welds in each pipe size.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics shall reserve the right to select the location of PT inspection.
*   Unless otherwise specified, all pressure welds between dissimilar metals shall be 100% PT examined.
*   Tube to tubesheet welds shall be 100% PT examined.
*   The PT exam shall be carried out in accordance with a written procedure.
*   The limit of acceptability for PT shall satisfy [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels]], Appendix VIII, except that linear indications shall be considered unacceptable.
*   All excavated areas shall be weld built-up flush with the surface of the base material using the welding procedure previously approved by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for the main welding details.
*   All weld repair surfaces shall be 100% PT examined per the above requirements.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics reserves the right to accept or reject any doubtful or questionable PT indications.
*   Dye Penetrant Inspection shall satisfy Para 10.3.
*   Dye Penetrant Inspection for piping shall satisfy [[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping]].

**Radiographic (RT) Examination**
*   Equipment fabricated in accordance with [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels]] shall have all butt welds 100% radiographed.
*   Radiographic inspection shall be in accordance with Para. UW-51 of [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels]].
*   Equipment fabricated to [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels]], but not exceeding 3/8" plate thickness, shall be spot radiographed in accordance with Para. UW-52(b) of [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels]].
*   For piping fabricated in accordance with ASME Codes and this Standard, the minimum standard of radiography shall be random radiography as defined in Para. 334.4.1 (b) of [[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping]]. 20% of all butt welds shall be radiographed.
*   For tankage fabricated in accordance with [[API_620_low_pressure_storage_tanks]] and [[API_650_welded_steel_tanks]] and this Standard, the minimum requirements shall be spot radiography.
*   Non-Code items fabricated to this Standard only shall, if required by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics engineering drawings, be spot radiographed in accordance with UW-52 (b).
*   Non-Code piping fabricated to this Standard only shall, if required by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics engineering drawings, be 20% random radiographed to [[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping]].
*   RT Exam shall be carried out in accordance with a written procedure.
*   RT Exam personnel shall be qualified to appropriate national standards.
*   Costs of additional spot radiography (as typically defined in UW-52 Sub-para. (d2)), including all repair costs and re-radiography, shall be to the Fabricator’s account.
*   Junctions between circumferential and longitudinal seams shall be considered as preferred locations for spot radiography.

**Helium Mass Spectrometer Testing**
Tube/tube sheet joints and tube bundles shall be tested using the helium mass spectrometer (reverse probe method) per [[ASME_section_v_non_destructive_examination]], Article 10, when required by purchase order or referenced drawings.

**Dimensional Tolerances**
*   In the absence of specific dimensional tolerance references, Aker Kvaerner Chemetics ESDO/001 shall apply.
