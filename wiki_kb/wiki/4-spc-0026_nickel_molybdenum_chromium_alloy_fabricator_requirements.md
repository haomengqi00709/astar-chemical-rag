# Fabricator Requirements for Nickel/Molybdenum/Chromium Alloy Welding

Fabricators undertaking work involving nickel/molybdenum/chromium alloys **must** adhere to stringent quality assurance and qualification requirements.

**Subcontracting and Supervision**
No subcontracting of welding is permitted without [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_company_details]] written approval. The fabricator **shall** ensure regular and systematic supervision of all work, both in-shop and on-site, by competent staff.

**Welding Procedures and Qualifications**
*   All welding procedures **shall** be submitted by the fabricator for review and agreement by [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_company_details]] Q.A. & Materials Group prior to commencement of any work.
*   The [[ASME_section_ix_welding_and_brazing_qualifications]] Code Section IX PQR Form 483 **shall** form part of the welding procedure submittal package.
*   Welding procedures **shall** be qualified on the same grade of material as that selected for fabrication.
*   A change in essential variables as outlined in [[ASME_section_ix_welding_and_brazing_qualifications]] Code Section IX will require resubmission of procedure(s).
*   All welders employed on [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_company_details]]' work **shall** be suitably qualified in accordance with relevant codes and standards for every procedure they are to use.
*   The fabricator **shall**, upon request, supply [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_company_details]] with objective evidence of current welder qualifications.
*   All qualified welders **shall** be allocated an identification symbol, which **shall** be marked on the parent material adjacent to each welder's work.

**Material Traceability and Controls**
*   Certification showing results from analysis and other tests carried out in accordance with the relevant material specification **shall** be supplied to [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_company_details]] for all materials, including welding consumables.
*   Fabricator **shall** maintain full traceability of materials back to original mill certification.
*   The fabricator's internal controls **shall** prevent the use of unspecified materials.
*   Lack of adequate identification or certification **shall** be cause for rejection of the specific component or item.

**Subcontracting and Supervision**
No subcontracting of welding is permitted without [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_company_details]] written approval. The fabricator shall ensure regular and systematic supervision of all work, both in-shop and on-site, by competent staff.

**Welding Procedure Qualifications**
All welding procedures shall be submitted by the fabricator for review and agreement by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Q.A. & Materials Group prior to commencement of any work. The ASME code section IX PQR Form 483 shall form part of the welding procedure submittal package. Welding procedures shall be qualified on the same grade of material as that selected for fabrication. A change in essential variables as outlined in ASME Code Section IX will require resubmission of procedure(s).

**Welder Qualifications**
All welders employed on Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' work shall be suitably qualified in accordance with relevant codes and standards for every procedure they are to use. The fabricator shall, upon request, supply Aker Kvaerner Chemetics with objective evidence of current welder qualifications. All qualified welders shall be allocated an identification symbol, which shall be marked on the parent material adjacent to each welder's work.

**Work Adherence and Deviations**
All work shall be carried out in accordance with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics "Approved for Fabrication" shop detail drawing(s). Deviations from weld geometry shown on approved drawing(s) are not permitted without prior approval of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
