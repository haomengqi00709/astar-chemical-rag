# HCl Synthesis Unit Plant Air Utility Specifications

This page details the specifications for the plant air utility stream used in the HCl Synthesis Unit, as outlined in [[4-PDS-XXXX-R1_template_hcl_synthesis_unit_package_datasheet_administration]]. Plant air is utilized for various operational functions, including purging and potentially as a start-up medium.

**Plant Air Utility Parameters:**
The plant air supply is characterized by the following design conditions:
*   **Composition (Note 11):**
    *   H2: XX%
    *   O2: XX%
    *   N2: XX%
*   **Temperature (Note 11):** Ambient
*   **Required Pressure (Note 10, Note 11):** VTA kPa(g)
*   **Volume per Purge (Note 8, Note 11):** VTA Nm3
*   **Time per Purge (Note 8, Note 11):** VTA Minutes

All parameters related to plant air, including its composition, temperature, required pressure, volume per purge, and time per purge, are subject to **Note 11**. Additionally, the required pressure is subject to **Note 10**, and the purge parameters are subject to **Note 8**. These notes provide essential clarifications or specific requirements for the plant air utility. The composition of plant air is critical, especially regarding H2 and O2 content, to prevent unintended reactions or hazards.

For general information on utility fluid design conditions, refer to [[utility_fluid_design_conditions]] and for instrument air systems, refer to [[instrument_air_system]].
