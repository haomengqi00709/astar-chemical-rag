# Mag Drive Pump Motor and Mounting Details

This page provides details regarding the motor and mounting arrangements for Mag Drive Pumps, as specified in Document ID 4-DST-XXXX-R1. These specifications are essential for proper integration and operation of the pump unit.

**Motor Details**
*   **Motor**: General specification or type of electric motor required.
*   **Supply by**: Indicates whether the motor is supplied by the pump vendor or by another party (e.g., Client, EPC contractor).
*   **Mount by**: Specifies who is responsible for mounting the motor onto the pump (e.g., Vendor, Site Contractor).

**Mounting Details**
*   **Mounting**: Describes the general mounting configuration of the pump (e.g., horizontal, vertical).
*   **Specification**: References any specific standards or requirements for the mounting arrangement.
*   **Size**: Relevant dimensions or sizing information for the mounting interface.

The coordination of motor supply and mounting is critical for project execution. For general information on drive systems for horizontal centrifugal pumps, refer to [[horizontal_centrifugal_pump_drive_system]].
