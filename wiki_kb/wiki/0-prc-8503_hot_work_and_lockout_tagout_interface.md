# Hot Work and Lockout/Tagout Interface (0-PRC-8503)

The coordination between Hot Work Permits and [[0-PRC-8504-RA_lockout_and_tagging|Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) procedures]] is a critical safety interface within the [[0-prc-8503_hot_work_permit_procedure_overview|Hot Work Permit Procedure]]. Strict adherence to these guidelines is essential to prevent hazardous situations arising from simultaneous operations.

**Prohibition of Concurrent Permits:**
*   The [[0-prc-8503_hot_work_permit_roles_and_responsibilities|Commissioning HSE Manager]] **shall not** issue a Work Permit covering Hot Work while a [[0-PRC-8504-RA_lockout_and_tagging|lock-out, tag-out procedure]] allowing opening of lines and vessels is in existence in the area that could be made hazardous by any release.

**Defining Hazardous Areas:**
*   All openings from which releases occur **shall** be considered hazardous.
*   The area of concern **shall** cover the limits where there is a remote possibility that sparks from Hot Work could fall into any vapours which could be released from the opening or could flow to a point where sparks might ignite them.

**Responsibility for Coordination:**
*   If a lockout, tag-out procedure for opening is in existence, it is the responsibility of the [[0-prc-8503_hot_work_permit_roles_and_responsibilities|Commissioning HSE Manager]] to ensure that a Work Permit for Hot Work is not issued, or that the lockout, tag-out procedure for opening is terminated before a Hot Work Permit is issued.
*   If the [[0-prc-8503_hot_work_permit_roles_and_responsibilities|Commissioning HSE Manager]] decides sufficient liquid or vapour has been released to create a hazard, a Work Permit covering Hot Work **shall not** be issued until the hazard has been eliminated.

This strict protocol ensures that potential ignition sources from hot work are not present in areas where flammable or hazardous materials could be released due to ongoing LOTO-controlled operations.
