# Absorber Tower Nozzle Requirements

This page details the requirements for nozzles and associated components of the Absorber Tower, as specified in document [[4-pds-7567-r1_absorber_tower_process_data_sheet_document_details]].

**Nozzle Sizing and Design:**
*   Nozzles attached to process lines **shall** be sized in accordance with [[line_sizing_criteria]].
*   Overflow nozzles are self venting.
*   Flow and its basis **shall** be entered (e.g., 50 m3/h = max flow from feed pump).
*   For drain nozzles, the minimum time to drain the tank contents **shall** be entered.
*   Accessories will describe any accessories associated with the nozzle.

**Materials:**
*   Carbon Steel
*   Saramet
*   CS

**Notes:**
*   **Note 1.0 (Data Sheet):** Blower's dead head
*   **Note 2.0 (Data Sheet):** Minimum ambient temperature

For general nozzle design principles, refer to [[nozzle_requirements]].
