# Construction Subcontractor Document Standards (0-SPC-6091)

This specification outlines the mandatory requirements for documentation supplied by the Construction Subcontractor under document 0-SPC-6091.

**Document Content and Language**
Document content **must** be prepared in the English language. The Subcontractor may, however, prepare bilingual documents that include both English and their primary working language.

**Units of Measure**
All document content **must** utilize SI units.

**Document Identification**
The Subcontractor is permitted to use their own document titles and numbering system. However, each document **must** include a designated 75mm x 45mm block for the insertion of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' internal numbering. Within this block, the Subcontractor **must** insert the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Subcontract Number and the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Tag Number. All other numbers within this block will be inserted by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

For further details on document control and numbering, refer to [[document_numbering_0_prc_0002]] and [[subcontractor_document_templates]].
