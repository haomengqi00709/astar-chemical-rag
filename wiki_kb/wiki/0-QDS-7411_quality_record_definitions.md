# Acid Cooler Quality Record Definitions (0-QDS-7411)

This page provides definitions for quality record requirements specific to the Acid Cooler, as outlined in document [[0-QDS-7411_document_details|0-QDS-7411]].

*   **AP (As Prepared):** Indicates that the quality record is required "As Prepared."
*   **FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package):** Indicates that the quality record is required to be "Included in the Quality Assurance Documentation Package."

These definitions clarify the documentation and submission requirements for various quality records throughout the project lifecycle.

For general quality record definitions, see [[0-QDS-7_quality_record_definitions]] or [[0-QDS-7404-2_quality_record_definitions]].
