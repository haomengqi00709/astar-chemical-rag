# Acid Plant Lining Supplier and Contractor Responsibilities

For sulphuric acid plant drying and absorption towers and pump tanks, which contain 92-100% sulphuric acid, clear responsibilities are defined for both the lining supplier and the installation contractor/applicator. These responsibilities are critical to ensure the quality and compliance of chemically resistant brick linings as per Specification 4-SPC-0012.

**Supplier Responsibilities**
The lining supplier **shall** assume full responsibility for the lining design and the quality of all materials supplied. This includes ensuring that all raw materials necessary for the lining system are provided.

**Installation Contractor/Applicator Responsibilities**
The installation contractor/applicator **shall** be responsible for the quality of the installation and workmanship. This responsibility extends to ensuring full compliance with all design documentation. The contractor/applicator **must** also supply all specialized installation tools and equipment required for the job, such as diamond tip brick saws, mortar mixers, and trowels.

**Shared Requirements and Obligations**
Both the supplier and the installation contractor/applicator **must** have demonstrated experience in the design, supply, and/or installation of acid plant chemically resistant brick linings.

It is important to note that any review of drawings or acceptance through inspections by the client/owner does not relieve the supplier or contractor from their obligations regarding design, material quality, or workmanship.

**Deviation and Consultation**
Any deviations from the requirements specified in the purchase order documentation or [[4-spc-0012_acid_resistant_linings_specification_overview]] **must** be approved in writing. Furthermore, if the vendor identifies potential technical difficulties with a particular lining design, the client/owner **must** be consulted before any work commences.

For details on specific lining components, refer to [[acid_plant_chemically_resistant_membrane_specifications]] and [[acid_plant_chemically_resistant_brick_specifications]]. General vendor quality assurance requirements can be found in [[vendor_quality_assurance_requirements]] and [[0-SPC-5001_vendor_obligations_and_warranties]].

---
**Detailed Responsibilities and Obligations**

The lining supplier holds full responsibility for the lining design and the quality of all materials supplied. The installation contractor/applicator is responsible for the quality of installation and workmanship, as well as ensuring compliance with all design documentation.

It is important to note that any review of drawings or acceptance through inspections by the client/owner does not relieve the supplier or contractor from any of their specified obligations.

**Supplier/Contractor Qualifications and Supply:**
Suppliers and contractors must demonstrate proven experience in the design, supply, and/or installation of acid plant chemically resistant brick linings. They are responsible for supplying all necessary raw materials, including specialized installation tools and equipment such as diamond tip brick saws, mortar mixers, and trowels.

**Procedural Requirements:**
Any deviations from the requirements outlined in the purchase order documentation or Document 4-SPC-0012 must receive written approval. Should the vendor anticipate technical difficulties with a particular lining design, the client/owner must be consulted before any work commences.

Furthermore, technical details for any proposed rubber lining system must be included in the vendor's proposal. Descriptions and areas of application for all types of membranes used in the equipment must be clearly defined on the project drawings.
