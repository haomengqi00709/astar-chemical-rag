# Shell and Tube Heat Exchanger Process Design Conditions

This page outlines the critical process design parameters for the Shell and Tube Heat Exchanger (Chemetics Equipment Number 10-10H, "Water Heater"). These conditions define the operational environment and safety requirements for both the shell and tube sides of the heat exchanger, ensuring its performance under various process scenarios.

For related mechanical design aspects, refer to [[shell_and_tube_heat_exchanger_mechanical_design_requirements]].

**Fluid Contents and Properties:**
*   **Shell Side Contents:** Cooling Water
*   **Tube Side Contents:** Demineralized Water
*   **Shell Side Specific Gravity Contents:** 1.0
*   **Tube Side Specific Gravity Contents:** 1.0

**Pressure Design Conditions:**
*   **Shell Side Design Internal Pressure:** 1000 kPa(g)
*   **Tube Side Design Internal Pressure:** 1000 kPa(g)
*   **Shell Side Design External Pressure:** -100.0 kPa(g)
*   **Tube Side Design External Pressure:** 0.0 kPa(g)

**Overpressure and Vacuum Protection:**
Appropriate protection mechanisms are specified for both sides to ensure safe operation under abnormal pressure conditions.
*   **Shell Side Overpressure Protection:** PSV-XXXX
*   **Tube Side Overpressure Protection:** PSV-YYYY
*   **Shell Side Vacuum Protection:** FV design
*   **Tube Side Vacuum Protection:** Vent

**Temperature Design Conditions:**
The maximum and minimum design temperatures define the operational limits, while mean metal temperatures are crucial for thermal stress analysis and expansion joint design.
*   **Shell Side Max. Design Temp.:** 150 deg °C
*   **Shell Side Min. Design Temp.:** -28 deg °C
*   **Tube Side Max. Design Temp.:** 150 deg °C
*   **Tube Side Min. Design Temp.:** -28 deg °C

**Mean Metal Temperatures:**
*   **Normal Mean Metal Temp. Shell:** 32.0 deg °C
*   **Normal Mean Metal Temp. Tubes:** 60.0 deg °C
*   **Upset Mean Metal Temp. Shell:** 32.0 deg °C
*   **Upset Mean Metal Temp. Tubes:** 80.0 deg °C

These parameters are essential for the detailed mechanical design and fabrication of the heat exchanger, ensuring its integrity and performance under specified operating conditions.
