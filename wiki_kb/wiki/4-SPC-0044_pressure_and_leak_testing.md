# Glass-Lined Vessel Pressure and Leak Testing (4-SPC-0044)

This section details the mandatory pressure and leak testing requirements for glass-lined carbon steel reaction vessels, including hydrostatic tests, pneumatic tests, and mixer operation during testing, as specified in 4-SPC-0044.

**Hydrostatic Testing**
The hydrostatic test pressure for the glassed vessel **shall** be the maximum allowable working pressure in accordance with [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels|ASME Code, Section VIII, Paragraph UG-99 (k) of Division 1]]. The jacket **shall** be hydrotested at its design pressure with the vessel empty. No vessel or its component parts **shall** be given a proof hydrostatic test (refer Paragraph UG 101) unless approval is obtained in writing from Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. If a proof test is necessary, it **shall** be witnessed in its entirety by the Project Engineer or his designate.

All cover plate hinge pins **shall** be in place during the test and **shall not** bind on the hinges. Gaskets and bolting supplied with the vessel may be used for pressure testing. The use of shellac, glue, compounds, lead, etc., on gaskets is not permitted. The temperature of water used for hydrostatic tests **shall** be maintained at 60°F (15°C) throughout the testing cycle, in accordance with the intent of the [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels|ASME Code]].

All hydrostatic tests **shall** be made in the presence and with the approval of an Authorized Inspector. The release of a vessel by the Inspector **shall not** relieve the Fabricator of responsibility or alter the conditions of the guarantee.

**Pneumatic Testing**
Welded attachments provided with tell-tale holes **shall** be pneumatically tested with 10 psig (68 kPag) air prior to heat treatment and/or hydrostatic test. Tell-tale holes **shall not** be plugged during the final hydrostatic test of the vessel.

**Mixer Operation During Hydrotest**
In the case of vessels containing mixers, the Fabricator **shall** assemble the vessel mixer and drive system for final hydrotest at the design pressure. The mixer **shall** be operated during the hydrotest for a continuous period of one hour. The mixer **shall** be operated at a speed that will not overload any part of the mixer system (preferably maximum agitator speed).

**Post-Test Inspection**
Upon completion of the hydrotest and running test, the vessel lining **shall** be Holiday-tested prior to shipment to site, as detailed in [[4-SPC-0044_inspection_and_non_destructive_examination|Inspection and Non-Destructive Examination]].
