# Electrical Equipment Identification Numbering Procedure Administration (9-PRC-0001)

This procedure, identified as 9-PRC-0001, outlines the requirements for establishing standard identification numbers and names for electrical equipment within projects. It serves as a foundational document for ensuring consistency across various project-related numbering systems, including cost codes and other document numbers.
