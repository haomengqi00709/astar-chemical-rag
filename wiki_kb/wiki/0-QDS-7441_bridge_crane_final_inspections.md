# Bridge Crane Final Inspections

This page outlines the mandatory final inspection requirements for the Bridge Crane, as specified in Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7441. These inspections are conducted to ensure the completed unit meets all design, fabrication, and quality standards before release.

**Chemetics Surveillance for Final Inspections:**
Chemetics surveillance requires "Witness" for all listed final inspection items. A Chemetics representative **shall** be present to verify compliance for each of the following:

*   **Dimensional Final Inspection:** Verification of all critical dimensions against approved drawings.
*   **Contour & finish of welds Final Inspection:** Assessment of weld quality, including contour, finish, and absence of defects.
*   **Surfaces free from defects Final Inspection:** Examination of all surfaces for any imperfections, damage, or non-conformances.
*   **Cleanliness Final Inspection:** Confirmation that the equipment is free from debris, foreign materials, and contaminants.
*   **Trolley Final Inspection:** Specific inspection of the trolley assembly for proper construction, alignment, and component installation.
*   **Coupling/Belt Final Inspection:** Inspection of all couplings and belts for correct installation, tension, and alignment.
*   **Guard Final Inspection:** Verification that all safety guards are correctly installed and provide adequate protection.
*   **Silencer Final Inspection:** Inspection of any silencer components for proper installation and integrity.
*   **Review QA Documentation Final Inspection:** A final review of the complete Quality Assurance Documentation Package to ensure all required records are present and accurate.

**Final Inspection Activity:**
Final Inspection is a critical item or activity in the quality assurance process, serving as the last checkpoint before the Bridge Crane is prepared for shipment.
