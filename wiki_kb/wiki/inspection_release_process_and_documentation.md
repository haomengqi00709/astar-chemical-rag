# Inspection Release Process and Documentation

This page outlines the procedures for issuing and completing the [[0-REL-7XXX_template_inspection_release]] document, detailing the responsibilities of the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Inspector and the vendor representative.

**Inspector's Issuance Criteria**
The Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Inspector will only issue an Inspection Release if the goods do not require sufficient subsequent work that would necessitate another inspection.

**Scenario 1: No Further Work Required**
If no further work is required on the goods:
*   The Inspector **must** complete Section 2 of the Inspection Release form.
*   The Inspector **must** delete Sections B & C from the form.
*   An electronic copy of the completed Inspection Release **must** be sent to ChemeticsQA@akerkvaerner.com.
*   Upon issuance of this release, the vendor may proceed with packaging the goods and arranging for their shipment in accordance with [[purchase_order_commercial_and_contractual_terms]] requirements.

**Scenario 2: Further Work Required (Not Requiring Re-inspection)**
If further work is required on the goods, but this work does not necessitate another inspection by the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Inspector:
*   The Inspector **must** complete Section 2 of the Inspection Release form.
*   The Inspector **must** delete Section A from the form.
*   The Inspector **must** detail all required vendor actions in Section B of the form that **must** be completed before the goods may be shipped.
*   The Inspector **must** obtain the vendor representative's signature in Section B.
*   An electronic copy of the Inspection Release **must** be sent to ChemeticsQA@akerkvaerner.com.

**Vendor Responsibilities for Further Work**
Upon receiving an Inspection Release indicating further work:
*   The vendor representative **must** satisfactorily perform all corrections and/or complete the activities noted in Section B prior to packaging the goods and arranging for their shipment.
*   Upon satisfactory completion of the work detailed in Section B, the vendor representative **must** sign Section C of the form.
*   The vendor representative **must** then send an electronic copy of the completed Inspection Release to ChemeticsQA@akerkvaerner.com.

**General Shipment Requirements**
In all cases, any noted corrections or completions **must** be satisfactorily performed prior to packaging the goods and arranging for their shipment. An electronic copy or fax of the completed form **must** be sent to the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Inspector noted in the [[purchase_order_commercial_and_contractual_terms]].
