# Quench Venturi Field Inspection - Mechanical Installation Requirements

This page details the mandatory mechanical installation requirements for the Quench Venturi, as outlined in the Field Inspection Checklist **4-FIC-XX21-R0**. Adherence to these requirements is critical for the structural integrity, operational efficiency, and long-term reliability of the equipment.

**Mandatory Mechanical Installation Checks:**

*   **Nameplate Installation:** The correct nameplate **must** be installed on the Quench Venturi. This is essential for accurate equipment identification, compliance with regulatory standards, and facilitating future maintenance and spare parts procurement.
*   **Foundation and Anchor Bolts:** All foundation and anchor bolts **must** be secure and properly tightened. This ensures the stable mounting of the Quench Venturi, preventing movement, vibration, and potential structural damage during operation. Proper torque specifications, as per design documents, **must** be followed.
*   **Sliding Supports:** Any sliding supports present on the equipment **must** allow for unrestricted movement. This is crucial to accommodate thermal expansion and contraction of the vessel, preventing stress buildup and potential cracking in the material.
*   **Vessel Internals:** All specified vessel internals **must** be correctly installed. This includes components such as dip pipes, stand pipes, baffles, and vortex breakers. The proper installation of these internals is vital for the process performance of the Quench Venturi, ensuring correct fluid distribution, mixing, and separation.
*   **Spray Nozzles:** The correct type and quantity of spray nozzles **must** be installed both above and below the venturi throat. These nozzles are critical for the efficient scrubbing action of the venturi, ensuring proper liquid atomization and contact with the gas stream. Verification of nozzle type, orientation, and secure fitting is mandatory.
*   **Manway Covers:** All manway covers **must** be installed and securely tightened. This ensures the integrity of the vessel, prevents leaks, and maintains internal pressure conditions. Refer to [[manway_covers_installation_and_inspection]] for detailed procedures.
*   **Accessories Installation:** Essential accessories **must** be installed. This includes gauges (pressure, temperature), sight glasses, sample ports, vents, and drains. These components are necessary for monitoring process conditions, safe operation, and maintenance activities.
*   **Isolation Valves on Drains:** Isolation valves **must** be installed on all drains. This allows for safe and controlled draining of the vessel, facilitating maintenance, cleaning, and emergency shutdown procedures without affecting the main process.

These mechanical checks are fundamental to the safe and effective operation of the Quench Venturi. Any deviations **must** be documented and addressed through the appropriate non-conformance reporting procedures, such as [[non_conformance_report_0_ncr_7]].
