# Chemetics Surveillance Level (0-QDS-7416)

For the Plate & Frame Heat Exchanger project, Chemetics surveillance will be conducted at **Level 0.0**. This surveillance level indicates the extent of Chemetics' involvement in monitoring the vendor's quality activities.

The specific surveillance activities and their corresponding Chemetics involvement are detailed throughout this Quality Data Sheet, **0-QDS-7416**. Key areas of surveillance include:

*   **Material Certifications:** Complete surveillance for [[0-QDS-7416_material_certifications|Mill Test Certificates]] and [[0-QDS-7416_material_certifications|Certificates of Compliance]].
*   **Inspections:** Complete surveillance for [[0-QDS-7416_final_inspections|External Coating / Lining Inspection]], [[0-QDS-7416_final_inspections|Dimensional Final Inspection]], [[0-QDS-7416_final_inspections|Contour & finish of welds Final Inspection]], [[0-QDS-7416_final_inspections|Surfaces free from defects Final Inspection]], [[0-QDS-7416_final_inspections|Cleanliness Final Inspection]], [[0-QDS-7416_final_inspections|Spare parts Final Inspection]], and [[0-QDS-7416_final_inspections|Review QA Documentation]].
*   **Testing:** Complete surveillance for [[0-QDS-7416_pressure_testing|Hydrostatic test]].
*   **Documentation:** Complete surveillance for [[0-QDS-7416_packing_and_shipping_requirements|Packing List]].
*   **Packaging:** Complete surveillance for [[0-QDS-7416_packing_and_shipping_requirements|Packaging]].

The vendor's Quality Plan and overall Quality Assurance **shall** comply with these surveillance requirements, as outlined in [[0-QDS-7416_vendor_quality_assurance_requirements]].
