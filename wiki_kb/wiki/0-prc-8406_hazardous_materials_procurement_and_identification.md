# Hazardous Materials Procurement and Identification (0-PRC-8406)

The procurement process for hazardous materials involves specific responsibilities for identification and documentation to ensure safety from the outset. This section details the requirements for requisitioning and purchasing agents.

**Requisition and Purchasing Agent Responsibilities**
It is the joint responsibility of the person making the [[requisition_for_quotation_or_purchase_0_prc_5002|requisition]] and the [[request_for_quotation_and_purchase_order_responsibilities|Purchasing Agent]] to flag items being purchased that may be hazardous to people or the environment.

Every [[request_for_quotation_and_purchase_order_responsibilities|Purchasing Agent]] **shall** request information from the supplier about the properties of items being bought. This information is critical for assessing potential risks.

The [[request_for_quotation_and_purchase_order_responsibilities|Purchasing Agent]] **must** identify possible hazards posed by the material during:
*   Shipping, handling, and storage.
*   Actual use or installation.
*   Improper disposal.

**Purchase Order Requirements**
Material Safety Data Sheets (MSDS) or material safety handling instructions **will** be a separate line item in the [[purchase_order_commercial_and_contractual_terms|purchase order]]. The [[purchase_order_commercial_and_contractual_terms|purchase order]] **will** clearly flag hazardous goods so that it becomes apparent to anyone looking at the purchase order.

The [[purchase_order_commercial_and_contractual_terms|purchase order]] **must** make it apparent under which conditions the goods pose a hazard, specifically detailing risks during:
*   Shipping/handling/storage.
*   During actual use or installation.
*   Disposal.

This proactive identification and documentation in the [[purchase_order_commercial_and_contractual_terms|purchase order]] are essential for informing all parties involved in the material's journey.
