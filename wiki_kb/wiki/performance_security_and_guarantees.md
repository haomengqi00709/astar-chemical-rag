# Performance Security and Guarantees

Performance security and guarantees are essential components of [[order_specific_conditions_for_purchase]], providing Aker Kvaerner Chemetics with financial protection against a Vendor's failure to fulfill contractual obligations. These mechanisms ensure that the project remains protected even in unforeseen circumstances.

**Specification of Security:**
*   The amount of the security **must** be specified clearly within the commercial terms. This amount is crucial for determining the extent of protection provided.
*   The date the security is given **must** be specified from the following options:
    *   Within 2 weeks of the Purchase Order date.
    *   Prior to the payment due upon Delivery.
    *   Prior to the payment due after successful start-up.
    Selecting the appropriate timing ensures that security is in place when most critical for project phases.

**Parent Company Guarantees:**
*   A form of Parent Company Guarantee **must** be attached if one is required for the order. This provides an additional layer of assurance, particularly when dealing with smaller entities or those with limited financial history.

**Retention:**
*   It **must** be specified if retention does not apply to the order. Retention is a portion of the payment withheld until certain conditions (e.g., successful commissioning, warranty period expiry) are met. Clearly stating its applicability or non-applicability prevents misunderstandings.

The amount of the security must be clearly specified. The date the security is given must also be specified from the following options: within 2 weeks of the Purchase Order date, prior to the payment due upon Delivery, or prior to the payment due after successful start-up.

If required, a form of Parent Company Guarantee must be attached. It must also be explicitly specified if retention does not apply to the order.
