# Piping Design and Specification - Instrument Hydraulic Requirements

The [[piping_design_and_specification_5_prc_0003]] procedure outlines the requirements for piping design and specification, which directly impacts instrument hydraulic requirements.

**Hydraulic Calculations:**
Hydraulic calculations will be performed by the Mechanical or Process department. The results of these calculations **shall** be incorporated into the [[8-lst-01xx_instrument_process_data_lists]] as required.

**Long Lead Instruments:**
For long lead instruments, such as control valves, hydraulic information is required as soon as practicable to facilitate procurement and design. Critical pathing, scheduling, and expediting of activities for long lead instruments requiring hydraulic calculations **should** be coordinated by the Project Engineer with input from the Field Instrument Engineer (FIE) and the Design Office.
