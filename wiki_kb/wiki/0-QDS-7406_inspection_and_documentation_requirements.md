# 0-QDS-7406 Catalyst Inspection and Documentation Requirements

This section outlines the specific Quality Records and activities required for Catalyst materials, along with the necessity for Chemetics surveillance. Compliance with these requirements **is mandatory** for the Vendor.

**Required Quality Records and Surveillance:**
The following Quality Records **are required** and **must** undergo Chemetics surveillance:

*   **Chemical Analysis Report:** Required as "As Prepared" (AP).
*   **MSDS Sheets:** Required as "As Prepared" (AP).
*   **Dimensional record:** Required as "Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package" (FP).
*   **Quantity record:** Required as "Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package" (FP).
*   **Packing List:** Required as "As Prepared" (AP).

**Activities Requiring Chemetics Surveillance:**
The following activities **must** also undergo Chemetics surveillance:

*   Review QA Documentation
*   Packaging/Tagging
*   Material Verification
*   Final Inspection
*   Packing/Crating

For definitions of "AP" and "FP" as they apply to Quality Records, refer to [[0-QDS-7406_quality_record_definitions]]. The overall surveillance level by Chemetics is detailed in [[0-QDS-7406_chemetics_surveillance_level]].

The following Quality Records **are required** and necessitate Chemetics surveillance:
*   **Chemical Analysis Report:** Required as AP (As Prepared).
*   **MSDS Sheets:** Required as AP (As Prepared).
*   **Dimensional Record:** Required as FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package).
*   **Quantity Record:** Required as FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package).
*   **Packing List:** Required as AP (As Prepared).

**Required Activities and Surveillance:**
The following activities **are required** and necessitate Chemetics surveillance:
*   **Review QA Documentation**
*   **Packaging/Tagging**

**Other Key Activities:**
The following are recognized as key items or activities within the scope of this document:
*   **Material Verification**
*   **Final Inspection**
*   **Packing/Crating**
