# Construction Site Advisor HSE Requirements Overview (0-PRC-8400)

This procedure, identified as 0-PRC-8400, outlines the minimum Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) requirements for Aker Kvaerner Chemetics construction site advisors operating at various construction sites (referred to as "Site"). Its primary application is to construction sites where Aker Kvaerner Chemetics is providing construction advisory services for the erection of process plants designed by the company.

**General HSE Principles**
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics prioritizes HSE above all other considerations. Advisors **must** ensure that no job is performed without proper HSE plans in place. If an advisor is uncertain about the safety status of a particular task, they **must** consult a qualified person. Should uncertainty persist, the advisor **must** refuse to perform the task until all safety concerns are adequately addressed. Furthermore, advisors have a responsibility to notify involved parties immediately if they witness an unsafe situation.

**Scope and Applicability**
The requirements detailed in this procedure are specifically tailored for Aker Kvaerner Chemetics personnel acting as construction site advisors. It ensures a consistent standard of HSE conduct and awareness across all applicable projects. For a broader understanding of advisor roles, refer to [[aker_kvaerner_construction_site_advisor_responsibilities]].
