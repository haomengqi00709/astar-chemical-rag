# Ejector Process Design Conditions

This page details the key process design conditions for ejector systems, covering parameters for the pumped fluid, motive fluid, and cooling water. These conditions are critical for the proper sizing, selection, and operation of the ejector.

**Pumped Fluid Design Conditions**
The following parameters define the characteristics and requirements for the fluid being pumped by the ejector:
*   Flow Rate (kg/h)
*   Minimum Flow Rate (kg/h)
*   Maximum Flow Rate (kg/h)
*   Flow Rate (Am3/h)
*   Inlet Temperature (oC)
*   Inlet Pressure (kPa(a))
*   Discharge Pressure (kPa(a))
*   Inlet Density (kg/m3)
*   Viscosity (cP)

**Motive Fluid Design Conditions**
The motive fluid drives the ejector's operation. Its design conditions include:
*   Flow Rate (kg/h)
*   Operating Temperature (oC) or Superheat (oC)
*   Maximum Temperature (oC)
*   Operating Pressure (kPa(g))
*   Maximum Pressure (kPa(g))
*   Density (kg/m3)

**Cooling Water Design Conditions**
For ejector systems requiring cooling, the following cooling water parameters are specified:
*   Flow Rate (kg/h)
*   Available Pressure (kPa(g))
*   Allowable Pressure Drop (kPa)
*   Fouling Factor (m2·oC/W) or Excess Area (%)
*   Supply Temperature (oC)
*   Return Temperature (oC)
