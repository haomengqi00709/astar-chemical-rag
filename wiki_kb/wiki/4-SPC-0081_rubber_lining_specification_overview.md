# Rubber Lining Specification Overview (4-SPC-0081)

This specification, identified as 4-SPC-0081, outlines the comprehensive requirements established by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for fabricated steel components intended for rubber lining. It details the specific criteria for the selection, application, curing, testing, and repair of sheet rubber linings.

This document (4-SPC-0081) represents a complete rewrite of the previous specification, 4-SPC-0008-R6, which was titled "Equipment Lining Rubber".

[[4-SPC-0081_document_administration]] provides details on the revision history and name change conversions applied to this document.

**Scope of Requirements:**
The specification covers critical aspects to ensure the integrity and performance of rubber-lined equipment, including:
*   Requirements for fabricated steel components that will be rubber lined.
*   Guidelines for sheet rubber selection.
*   Procedures for rubber application and curing.
*   Protocols for testing the rubber lining.
*   Methods for repairing rubber lining defects.

For related information on the design and fabrication of metallic vessels, refer to [[4-SPC-0040_metallic_process_vessels_fabrication_overview]].
