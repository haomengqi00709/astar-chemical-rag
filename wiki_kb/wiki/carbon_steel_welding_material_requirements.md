# Carbon Steel Welding Material Requirements

This section outlines the mandatory requirements for materials used in the welding of carbon steel components, including filler metals and fluxes, as defined by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' specification 4-SPC-0060.

**Filler Metals**
All weld filler metals **must** meet the requirements specified in [[ASME_bpvc_section_ii_materials]], Part C.

For welds between carbon steels and alloy steels, specific weld consumables **must** be used as detailed in [[4-SPC-0060_table_2_dissimilar_weld_filler_metals]]. Any deviations from these specified filler material requirements **require** written approval by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Fluxes for Low Temperature Service**
When high carbon or high manganese fluxes are utilized for the fabrication of equipment intended for low temperature service, the Vendor **must** provide sectioned samples. These samples may be taken from run-on/off tabs. This requirement is critical due to the potential for microcracking when such fluxes are used in low-temperature applications.

Furthermore, in these specific low-temperature service applications, the Vendor **must** provide satisfactory evidence of continuous shop use of the Submerged Arc Welding (SAW) process to ensure consistent quality and mitigate risks associated with these materials.

Deviations from filler material requirements require written approval by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
