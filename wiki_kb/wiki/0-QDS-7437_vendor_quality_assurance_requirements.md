# Field Erected CS Tank - Shop Vendor Quality Assurance Requirements

For the "Field Erected CS Tank - Shop" component, the Vendor **must** adhere to specific quality assurance requirements as detailed in this Quality Data Sheet (0-QDS-7437) and related specifications.

The Vendor **shall** prepare a Quality Plan. This plan **must** be developed in accordance with the guidelines set forth in [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment]]. The Vendor's overall Quality Assurance program **must** comply with both this Quality Data Sheet (0-QDS-7437) and [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment]].

**Note:** If a Quality Plan is not deemed necessary for a particular scope, the corresponding sections or cells related to the Quality Plan within the documentation should be deleted.
