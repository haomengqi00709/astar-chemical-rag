# Order-Specific Conditions for Purchase

Order-Specific Conditions are tailored commercial terms that are directly inserted into the body of a Request for Quotation (RFQ) or Purchase Order (PO). These conditions are typically unique to a particular order, addressing specific requirements, risks, or commercial arrangements that are not covered by [[standard_conditions_for_purchase]] or [[special_conditions_for_purchase]].

**Key Characteristics:**
*   Order-Specific Conditions are normally order-specific, meaning they are developed and applied based on the unique context of each individual procurement.
*   Standard clauses for common Order-Specific Conditions subjects are appended to the main procedure, [[commercial_terms_for_purchase_0_prc_5004]], to provide a consistent starting point.

**Typical Subjects Covered:**
Order-Specific Conditions often address critical commercial aspects, including but not limited to:
*   Terms of payment.
*   [[delay_damages_commercial_terms]] for delay.
*   Liquidated damages for performance.
*   [[performance_security_and_guarantees]] or bank guarantees.

**Customization and Approval:**
The text for Order-Specific Conditions may be varied by modifying the [[commercial_terms_for_purchase_0_prc_5004]] procedure itself and then issuing the revised procedure for the specific project. This process **shall** reference [[standard_document_management_procedure_0_prc_0001]] (0-PRC-0001: Approval and Use of Standards) to ensure compliance with document control standards. Any revised document for Order-Specific Conditions **shall** be approved by Legal.

**Application and Risk Assessment:**
Order-Specific Conditions **shall** be applied to an order as directed by the [[order_risk_assessment]] (referencing 0-PRC-5003: Bid Evaluation and Approval). This ensures that all identified risks and specific project needs are adequately addressed in the contractual terms.

**Insurance Requirements:**
*   Order-Specific Conditions **must** specify any insurances or minimum limits of indemnity that are unique to the order.
*   The minimum limit of indemnity for any [specify] insurance **must** be clearly stated.
*   The Vendor **must** take out insurance for [specify] with a minimum limit of indemnity of [$specify] in addition to other required insurances.
*   If Aker Kvaerner Chemetics provides [[free_issue_materials_policy]], the insurance against loss or damage to Goods and Services **must** include an amount of [$specify] to cover these free issue materials.

**Low Performance Damages:**
If a Defect included in the Defects Certificate demonstrates low performance, the Vendor **shall** pay the amount of low performance damages as stated in the Order-Specific Conditions.

**Typical Subjects:**
Order-Specific Conditions commonly cover subjects such as:
*   Terms of payment
*   Liquidated damages for delay
*   Liquidated damages for performance
*   Performance bonds or bank guarantees

**Modification and Approval:**
The text for Order-Specific Conditions may be varied by modification of this procedure and issued for the project, referencing 0-PRC-0001: Approval and Use of Standards. Any revised document for Order-Specific Conditions will require approval by Legal.

**Application and Insurance:**
Order-Specific Conditions will be applied to an order as directed by the Order Risk Assessment (referencing 0-PRC-5003: Bid Evaluation and Approval). These conditions must also specify any order-specific insurances or minimum limits of indemnity, with the minimum limit of indemnity for any specified insurance clearly stated. The Vendor must take out insurance for specified items with a minimum limit of indemnity of [$specify] in addition to other required insurances.
