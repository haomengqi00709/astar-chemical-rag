# FRP Tank Fabrication and Quality Requirements

This page outlines the mandatory fabrication procedures, quality assurance protocols, and specific requirements for the Fiberglass Reinforced Plastic (FRP) tank. Adherence to these guidelines is essential to ensure the tank's structural integrity, performance, and compliance with project standards.

**Fabrication Responsibilities and Approvals**
*   **Design Calculations and Drawings:** The fabricator **must** submit all design calculations and fabrication drawings to Chemetics for approval. Fabrication **shall not** proceed until this approval is granted. This process is critical for ensuring that the design meets all specified requirements and safety standards. For detailed procedures on design document review and approval, refer to [[design_document_review_and_approval_process_0_spc_6091]].
*   **External Loads Calculation:** The fabricator **shall** be responsible for calculating external loads (e.g., wind, seismic, snow) as part of their design submission. This ensures that the tank is adequately designed for its specific site conditions.
*   **Supply of Fasteners:** The fabricator **must** supply all necessary bolts, nuts, and gaskets for openings equipped with blind flanges or covers. This ensures compatibility and proper sealing of all connections.

**Marking and Identification**
*   **Equipment Stenciling:** The equipment name, equipment number, and nozzle mark letters **must** be clearly stenciled onto the vessel. These markings **shall** be 75 mm high black letters, ensuring clear identification on-site.

**Painting and Insulation**
*   **Paint Specification:** Painting is "Not applicable" for this FRP tank, as the external resin coat provides the necessary protection and finish.
*   **Paint System and Colour Reference:** While painting is not required for the tank itself, general guidelines for paint systems and colors for other project components can be found in [[coatings_design_and_specification_7_prc_0002]].
*   **Insulation:** Insulation is "Not Required" for this tank, indicating that process temperature maintenance or personnel protection from surface temperatures is not a design concern.

**Quality Assurance (QA) and Civil/Structural Coordination**
*   **Quality Assurance Requirements:** All quality assurance requirements **must** comply with Specification 4-SPC-0042: Fiberglass Equipment Fabrication and the Chemetics Quality Data Sheet. These documents define the inspection points, hold points, and documentation required throughout the fabrication process. For general vendor quality assurance requirements, refer to [[vendor_quality_assurance]] and specifically for FRP tanks, [[0-QDS-7414_vendor_quality_assurance_and_surveillance]].
*   **Civil/Structural Engineering:** Separate civil/structural engineering documents, identified as 4-SCR-xxxx, **will be independently prepared** (internally) for use by Chemetics or the Owner. These documents will address the foundation and structural support requirements for the tank, coordinating with the tank's design. For related structural design specifications, refer to [[structural_and_architectural_design_and_specification_3_prc_0002]].

**Fabrication Responsibilities**
*   The fabricator is responsible for calculating external loads.
*   The fabricator will submit design calculations and fabrication drawings to Chemetics for approval before proceeding with fabrication.
*   The fabricator will supply all bolts, nuts, and gaskets for openings with blind flanges or covers.

**Testing and Finishing**
*   **Hydrotest:** The vessel shall be filled with water to overflow for hydrotesting.
*   **Paint Specification:** Not applicable
*   **Paint System and Colour Reference:** 7-SPC-0006

**Marking and Identification**
Equipment name, equipment number, and nozzle mark letters will be clearly stenciled to the vessel with 75 mm high black letters.
