# Gas Cooling Tower Acid Holdup Volume and Packing Details

The design of the Gas Cooling Tower (GCT), Tag No. **6530-U**, includes specific considerations for acid holdup volume within the pump well and the characteristics of the packing material, as detailed in **1-Cal-6520-R0**.

**Cooling Tower Dimensions (for Holdup Calculation):**
*   **Cooling Tower Diameter:** 7.7 m (25.262391 ft)
*   **Cooling Tower Diameter on Datasheet:** 7700.0 mm
*   **Packed Height:** 4.88 m (16.0104504 ft)
*   **Packed Height on Datasheet:** 4880.0 mm

**Packing Details:**
*   **Type of Packing:** 3.5" Tri-Packs
*   **Packing Material:** Polypropylene
*   **Holdup Fraction:** 0.05

**Acid Holdup Volume Calculations:**
*   **Holdup in Packing:** 11.362166734311671 m³ (401.24750094691325 ft³)
*   **Pipe Diameter:** 600.0 mm (1.9684979999999999 ft)
*   **Pipe Length:** 30.0 m (98.4249 ft)
*   **Holdup in Piping:** 8.482300164692441 m³ (299.54689303110456 ft³)
*   **Total Holdup in Packing and Piping:** 19.84446689900411 m³ (700.7943939780178 ft³)
*   **Volume to Use:** 39.6 m³ (1398.4481489357725 ft³)
*   **Volume to Use on Datasheet:** 39.6
*   **Safety:** 99.55184586987981 %

**Pump Well Dimensions:**
