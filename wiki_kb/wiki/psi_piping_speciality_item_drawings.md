# PSI (Piping Speciality Item) Drawings

Piping Speciality Item (PSI) Drawings provide detailed technical information for unique or custom-designed components within a piping system. These drawings are essential for the accurate fabrication, procurement, and installation of specialized items that may not be covered by standard specifications.

**Content Requirements**
PSI Drawings **shall** contain the following detailed information:
*   **Dimensions of PSI:** Precise dimensional data for the speciality item, including overall size, connection dimensions, and critical tolerances.
*   **Materials of PSI:** Specific material compositions required for the item, ensuring compatibility with process conditions and adherence to material standards.
*   **References to Chemetics Specifications:** Citations to relevant Chemetics internal specifications that govern the design, fabrication, or quality of the speciality item.
*   **References to National Standards:** Citations to applicable national or international standards (e.g., ASME, ASTM) that the item **must** comply with.

**Roles and Responsibilities**
The preparation, checking, and approval of PSI Drawings are assigned to specific disciplines:
*   **Preparation:** The drawing **shall** be prepared by the Design discipline.
*   **Checking:** The drawing **shall** be checked by the Process discipline.
*   **Approval:** The drawing **shall** be approved by the Mechanical discipline.

These drawings ensure that specialized piping components are accurately designed and manufactured, contributing to the overall integrity and functionality of the piping system.
