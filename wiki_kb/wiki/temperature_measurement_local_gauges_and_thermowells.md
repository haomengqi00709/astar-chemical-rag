# Temperature Measurement - Local Gauges and Thermowells

Local temperature gauges provide immediate visual indication of process temperatures, while thermowells ensure the protection and proper installation of temperature sensors.

**Local Temperature Gauges (Indicators):**
*   **Construction:** Local temperature gauges **will** have stainless steel type 316 cases and clear 3 mm thick shatter-resistant window glass for durability and visibility.
*   **Size and Adjustability:** Case diameters for local temperature gauges **will be** 100 mm. Dials **will be** free to rotate around the thermowell axis, and the dial tilt angle (normal to the thermowell axis) **will be** adjustable through at least 90 degrees, allowing for optimal viewing.
*   **Sensing Elements:** Sensing elements for local temperature gauges **will be** bimetallic helix type unless otherwise approved. All sensing elements **will be** installed in thermowells.
*   **Stem Length:** The stem length for sensing elements **will be** a minimum of 50 mm above the compression or gland nut when the stem is fully seated in the thermowell.
*   **Scaling and Connections:** Scaling for local temperature gauges **will be** in degrees Celsius, with dial scales marked in black on a white background. The stem connection thread for local temperature gauges **will be** 1/2" NPT.

**Thermowell Design and Materials:**
*   **Material:** Thermowell material **will be** 316 stainless steel unless process conditions dictate the use of other materials.
*   **Bore and Marking:** The bore diameter of the thermowell **will** match the element. The thermowell material and tag number **will be** stamped onto the thermowell so that this information is clearly visible when the thermowell is installed.
*   **Construction:** Thermowells **will be** machined from bar stock in a single piece, except for the flange. They **will** have a stepped stem with a maximum Root OD of 0.75” unless otherwise specified on the [[instrument_calibration_and_testing|Instrument Specification Sheets]].
*   **Testing:** Thermowells **will be** pressure tested. Flange mounted thermowells **will** have the pressure sealing weld dye penetration tested to ensure integrity.

**Thermowell Process Connections:**
*   For air and water services, process connections for thermowells **will be** 1" NPT (screwed into flange).
*   For all other services, process connections for thermowells **will be** DN40 flange (refer to piping specification for flange details).

**Thermowell Immersion Depth:**
*   **In Pipes (≥ DN80):** Immersion depth for thermowells in pipe size ≥ DN80 **will be** between one third to one half of the internal pipe diameter.
*   **In Pipes (< DN80):** Immersion depth for thermowells in pipe size < DN80 **will be** one third of the internal pipe diameter, installed on a pipe elbow, facing into the flow.
*   **In Vessels:** Immersion depth for thermowells in vessels **will be** the lesser of 450 mm or 50% of the vessel diameter.

These specifications ensure accurate temperature measurement and robust installation. For sensor and transmitter details, refer to [[temperature_measurement_sensors_and_transmitters|Temperature Measurement - Sensors and Transmitters]].
