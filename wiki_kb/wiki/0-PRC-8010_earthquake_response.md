# Earthquake Response - AKCTC

This page outlines the procedures for responding to an earthquake at the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Technology Centre (AKCTC). This supplements the general company earthquake response guidelines.

**Pre-Earthquake Preparedness:**
*   All top-heavy shelving, cabinets, and computers **shall** be properly secured during initial installation.
*   Regular inspections will reveal any deficiencies in securing equipment.

**During an Earthquake:**
*   It is recommended that all personnel move away from windows and tall shelves and/or structures.
*   Take shelter under a strong desk or stand in a corner or doorway.
*   Watch for falling fixtures.

**After an Earthquake:**
*   First check for injuries.
*   Do not move seriously injured persons unless they are in immediate danger.
*   Apply first aid if required.
*   Report to the [[evacuation_procedures_akctc#Emergency_Assembly_Point]] for roll call and directions.

For general emergency response information, refer to [[0-PRC-8010_office_emergency_response_overview]].
