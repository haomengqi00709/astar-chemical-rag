# Filament Winding and Bond Testing (0-QDS-7415)

For the Dual Laminate Tank project, specific inspection and testing requirements apply to the filament winding pattern and bond testing, as detailed in 0-QDS-7415.

**Filament Winding Pattern:**
The filament winding pattern is a critical aspect of dual laminate tank fabrication.
*   **Inspection:** Surveillance by Chemetics for the filament winding pattern **must** be "Complete," signifying a full review and approval of the pattern by Chemetics. This ensures the structural integrity and adherence to design specifications.

**Bond Testing:**
Bond testing is a mandatory activity to verify the adhesion between the liner and the FRP laminate.
*   **Quality Record:** Bond testing results are mandatory quality records and **must** have a Quality Record status of FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package).
*   **Surveillance:** Surveillance by Chemetics for bond testing **must** be "Complete," indicating a full review and approval of the test results.

These measures are crucial for ensuring the long-term performance and safety of the dual laminate tank.
