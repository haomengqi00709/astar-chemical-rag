# Delivery, Work Stoppage, and Defect Management (0-SPC-5001)

This page outlines the procedures and requirements for the delivery of Goods and Services, instructions for work stoppage, and the process for managing and correcting Defects under Purchase Order **0-SPC-5001**.

**Delivery Schedule**
The Purchase Order is effective from the [[0-SPC-5001_purchase_order_overview#Starting Date|Starting Date]]. The Vendor **must** Provide the [[goods_and_services_definitions|Goods and Services]] so that Delivery occurs on the [[0-SPC-5001_purchase_order_overview#Delivery Date|Delivery Date]]. The Vendor **shall not** deliver the Goods and Services before the Delivery Date unless specifically instructed by the [[project_buyer_roles|Project Buyer]].

**Work Stoppage and Restart**
The Project Buyer may instruct the Vendor to stop or not to start any work. Subsequently, the Project Buyer may instruct the Vendor to re-start or start work. If the Project Buyer issues an instruction to stop or not to start work, the Delivery Date **will** be adjusted accordingly.

**Pre-Delivery Testing and Inspection**
The Vendor **must not** Deliver Goods and Services that the [[goods_and_services_definitions|Goods and Services Information]] states are to be tested or inspected before delivery, until the [[project_buyer_roles|Project Buyer]] has notified the Vendor that they have successfully passed the required test or inspection.

**Defect Correction**
A [[0-SPC-5001_purchase_order_overview#Defect|Defect]] is defined as a part of the Goods and Services not in accordance with the Goods and Services Information, or, if designed by the Vendor, not in accordance with the Applicable Law or the Vendor's accepted design.

The Vendor **must** correct any notified Defects before the end of their respective [[0-SPC-5001_purchase_order_overview#Defects Correction Period|Defect Correction Period]]. The Defect Correction Period commences when the Defect is formally notified to the Vendor.

**Costs of Uncorrected Defects**
If the Vendor fails to correct a notified Defect within its designated Defect Correction Period, the [[project_buyer_roles|Project Buyer]] **shall** assess the cost of having that Defect corrected by other means. The Vendor **must** then pay the assessed cost of having the Defect corrected. This ensures that any deficiencies are rectified promptly and at the Vendor's expense if they fail to meet their obligations. See [[defect_management_process]].
