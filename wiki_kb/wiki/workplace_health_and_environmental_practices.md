# Workplace Health and Environmental Practices

Aker Kvaerner Chemetics is committed to fostering a healthy and safe workplace while promoting environmental stewardship. This commitment is reflected in various initiatives and practices across all company locations.

**General Workplace Safety**
*   Work areas **must** be kept clean and organized to prevent hazards.
*   Local specific safe work practices and/or procedures exist and **must** be followed.

**Health Promotion Initiatives**
The company supports employee well-being through various health promotion initiatives, including:
*   General health promotion programs.
*   Specific initiatives such as "MVMT" (mentioned in the Research Laboratory context).
*   Availability of on-site and local exercise facilities.
*   Access to health resources, including online platforms like www.ecarewise.com and www.lifeworks.com.

**Environmental Programs and Conservation**
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics encourages environmental responsibility through several programs and conservation efforts:
*   **Recycling:** Local recycling programs are in place.
*   **3R Program:** The environmental program includes the "3R" principles: Reduce, Re-use, and Recycle.
*   **Energy and Water Conservation:** Initiatives include turning off workstations, monitors, computers, lights, and water before leaving for home.
*   **Paper Reduction:** Employees are encouraged to reduce paper usage where possible.
*   **Sustainable Commuting:** "Go Green" initiatives encourage employees to ride the bus or cycle to work.

These practices collectively contribute to a healthier workforce and a more sustainable operational footprint.
