# Discriminatory Harassment Definition and Grounds

Discriminatory harassment occurs when verbal or physical conduct or gestures relating to specific protected grounds interfere with a person’s work performance or create an unwelcome or intimidating work environment.

In accordance with the BC Human Rights Code, no person shall refuse to employ or discriminate against an individual with respect to employment, or any term or condition of employment, based on the following grounds:
*   Race
*   Colour
*   Ancestry
*   Place of origin
*   Political belief
*   Religion
*   Marital status
*   Family status
*   Physical or mental disability
*   Sex
*   Sexual orientation
*   Age
*   Criminal or summary conviction offense unrelated to employment
