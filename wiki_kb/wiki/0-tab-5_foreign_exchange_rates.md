# Cost Tabulation (0-TAB-5) Foreign Exchange Rates

The Cost Tabulation (0-TAB-5) document utilizes specific foreign exchange rates for converting costs into Canadian Dollars (CAD), which is the standard currency for all cost expressions. These rates are crucial for accurate financial comparisons and calculations, especially when dealing with international vendors.

**Standard Foreign Exchange Rates (as of document date 39338.0):**
*   **CAD (Canadian Dollar):** 1.0
*   **EUR (Euro):** 1.4474 CAD
*   **GBP (British Pound Sterling):** 2.1415 CAD
*   **USD (United States Dollar):** 1.0565 CAD

**Application of Exchange Rates:**
Foreign Exchange Rates can be applied in two primary ways:
*   **Project Hedged:** Rates may be fixed or hedged for the duration of a project to mitigate currency fluctuation risks.
*   **Current (with date):** Alternatively, current market rates, along with their effective date, may be used for conversions.

These rates directly influence the [[0-tab-5_cost_tabulation_details|Total Quoted Cost]] and [[0-tab-5_cost_tabulation_details|Equalization Costs]] within the cost tabulation, ensuring a standardized financial baseline for all bids.
