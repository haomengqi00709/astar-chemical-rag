# RLCS Vessel Nozzle Schedule

This schedule details the specifications for all nozzles on the Rubber Lined Carbon Steel (RLCS) Vessel, as outlined in datasheet 4-DST-XXXX-R1. Accurate fabrication and installation of these nozzles are crucial for the vessel's functionality and connection to the process system.

Each nozzle is identified by a unique mark and includes details on its description, quantity, nominal size, schedule, connection type, and projection from the vessel centerline.

| Nozzle Mark | Description       | Quantity | Nominal Size (mm) | Schedule | Connection | Projection from Centerline (mm) | Special Feature |
| :---------- | :---------------- | :------- | :---------------- | :------- | :--------- | :------------------------------ | :-------------- |
| A           | Acid Inlet        | 1.0      | 50.0              | By fab   | PN 10      | see elevation                   |                 |
| D           | Drain             | 1.0      | 50.0              | By fab   | PN 10      | 1750                            |                 |
| F           | Overflow          | 1.0      | 100.0             | By fab   | PN 10      | 1750                            |                 |
| L           | Level Instrument  | 1.0      | 80.0              | By fab   | PN 10      | 1750                            |                 |
| M           | Manway            | 1.0      | 600.0             | By fab   | Custom     | 1800                            | Detail 1        |
| P           | Pump Suction      | 1.0      | 50.0              | By fab   | PN 10      | 1750                            |                 |
| V           | Vent              | 1.0      | 80.0              | By fab   | PN 10      | see elevation                   |                 |

**Notes on Nozzle Specifications:**
*   **Nominal Size:** All sizes are provided in millimeters (mm).
*   **Schedule:** "By fab" indicates that the specific pipe schedule for the nozzle neck will be determined by the fabricator, adhering to the overall vessel design and pressure requirements.
*   **Connection:** "PN 10" refers to the nominal pressure rating of the flange connection. "Custom" for the Manway indicates a specialized connection design.
*   **Projection from Centerline:** This dimension specifies the distance from the vessel's vertical centerline to the face of the nozzle flange. For nozzles marked "see elevation," this dimension will be detailed in the associated elevation drawings.
*   **Manway Special Feature:** "Detail 1" indicates that specific design details for the manway are provided in a separate drawing or section.

This comprehensive nozzle schedule ensures that all necessary connections for process flow, instrumentation, and access are clearly defined for fabrication. For further details on vessel design, refer to [[rlcs_vessel_design_conditions]].
