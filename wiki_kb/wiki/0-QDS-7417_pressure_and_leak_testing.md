# Shell & Tube Heat Exchanger Pressure and Leak Testing (0-QDS-7417)

For the Shell & Tube Heat Exchanger specified in 0-QDS-7417, rigorous pressure and leak testing procedures are mandated to ensure the integrity and leak-tightness of the equipment. These tests are critical for verifying the safety and operational reliability of the heat exchanger before it is put into service.

**Pressure/Leak Testing (Item 17.0):**
This section outlines the specific tests required to confirm that the heat exchanger can safely withstand its design pressures and that all pressure-containing boundaries are free from leaks.

**Specific Testing Methods:**

*   **Hydrostatic Test (Item 18.0):**
    *   **Quality Record Required:** FP ("Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package")
    *   **Surveillance by Chemetics:** Witness and Complete
    *   A hydrostatic test **must** be performed on both the shell side and the tube side of the heat exchanger. This test involves filling the component with a liquid (typically water) and pressurizing it to a specified test pressure, which is typically higher than the design pressure. The purpose is to detect any structural weaknesses or leaks under pressure. Chemetics will witness this test and perform a complete review of the test results and documentation.

*   **Air/Soap Test (Item 19.0):**
    *   **Quality Record Required:** FP ("Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package")
    *   **Surveillance by Chemetics:** Complete
    *   An air/soap test **must** be conducted on repads and tube-to-tubesheet joints. This test involves applying air pressure to the area and then applying a soap solution to detect any leaks, which would manifest as bubbles. This method is particularly effective for identifying small leaks in critical joint areas. The Vendor **must** document the results, and these **shall** be included in the Quality Assurance Documentation Package. Chemetics will perform a complete review of the test records.

These pressure and leak tests are essential components of the [[0-QDS-7417_vendor_quality_assurance_requirements|Vendor Quality Assurance Requirements]] and are performed after [[0-QDS-7417_non_destructive_examination_requirements|Non-Destructive Examination]] to ensure the overall integrity of the fabricated heat exchanger. For definitions of quality record designations, refer to [[0-QDS-7417_quality_record_definitions]].
