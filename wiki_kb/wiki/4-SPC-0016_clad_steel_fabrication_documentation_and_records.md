# Clad Steel Fabrication Documentation and Records

Comprehensive documentation and accurate record-keeping are essential for all austenitic stainless clad steel fabrications, providing a complete history of materials, fabrication processes, and quality assurance activities.

**Fabricator's Report and Record Sets**
The Fabricator **will** furnish a report containing all the data pertaining to each vessel or item. Prior to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' final inspection of each unit, the Fabricator **will** have prepared in finished form three (3) sets of records, excluding the final test data.

**Mandatory Records**
The records **must** include, but are not limited to, the following:
*   Mill test reports, including reference to shear and ultrasonic testing.
*   Nameplate rubbing.
*   A copy of all inspection and test reports (e.g., Ultrasonic Testing (UT), Dye Penetrant Testing (PT), X-Ray, Chemical Analysis, Passivation, Ferroxyl Test).
*   Affidavit of Manufacture.
*   Hydrostatic Test reports.

These reports, together with the final test data, **will** be assembled into three complete sets for each item. In no case **will** a documentation package pertain to more than one vessel or item number, ensuring clear traceability for individual components.

**Documentation Collection**
The Inspector **will** collect the appropriate number of documentation packages for each item tested at the time of witnessing the final tests and certifying the correctness of forms. This ensures that all necessary records are compiled and verified at the point of final acceptance. This process is a key part of [[documentation_and_records]] management.

**Material and Consumable Certification**
Supplier **must** make available to the purchaser's inspector copies of chemical certification for each batch of electrodes or wire coils used, as well as for plate material etc. If electrodes, wire, etc. cannot be identified to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' satisfaction, samples **will** be removed for chemical analysis, with the cost of analysis to Supplier’s account.
