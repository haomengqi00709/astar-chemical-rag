# Plant Modifications and Acceptance Testing

During the commissioning phase, plant design deficiencies may be discovered, generally requiring rapid rectification. Any plant modifications made during commissioning **must** be subject to careful control and regulation to ensure both quality and safety.

**Plant Modifications During Commissioning**
All plant modifications undertaken during commissioning **will** be designed and executed in accordance with [[plant_modification_1_prc_0010]]: Plant Modification (1-PRC-0010). This procedure ensures that changes are properly documented, reviewed, and implemented, maintaining the integrity of the plant design and operational safety.

**Acceptance Testing**
Plant performance guarantees, as stipulated in the Contract, **will** be demonstrated through one or more Acceptance Tests. These tests **will** be conducted in accordance with [[performance_testing_1_prc_0008]]: Performance Testing (1-PRC-0008).

During performance testing, the Owner's personnel **will** operate the plant. They **will** do so under the direction of the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics commissioning team, as assigned by the Commissioning Manager, ensuring that the tests are conducted according to established protocols and under expert guidance.

**Post-Acceptance Test Procedures**
Upon successful completion of Acceptance Testing, agreement **must** be reached between the Owner and the Commissioning Manager that the plant has passed the Acceptance Tests and is formally Accepted. This acceptance agreement **will** be made and recorded in accordance with the protocols set out in the Contract.

If the plant does not pass the Acceptance Tests and Aker Kvaerner Chemetics determines that modifications are necessary, these modifications **will** require the same stringent controls as described in Section 8.9 (Plant Modification) of the original document. Any modifications made after failed Acceptance Tests **will** also require Owner approvals as set out in the Contract.
