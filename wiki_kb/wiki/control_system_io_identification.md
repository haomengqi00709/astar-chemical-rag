# Control System Input/Output (I/O) Identification

The identification of inputs and outputs (I/O) between electrical equipment and the Distributed Control System (DCS) is a critical aspect of plant instrumentation and control. Proper identification ensures accurate system configuration, efficient troubleshooting, and reliable operation.

All I/O points connecting electrical equipment to the DCS **shall** be clearly identified. The comprehensive information and detailed procedures for this identification are provided in [[8-PRC-0001_plant_instrument_numbering]]. This document outlines the specific numbering conventions and methodologies to be applied for all control system I/O.

Adherence to [[8-PRC-0001_plant_instrument_numbering]] is essential for:
*   Maintaining consistency in control system documentation.
*   Facilitating the integration of electrical and control system designs.
*   Streamlining the commissioning and testing of I/O points.
*   Enabling quick and accurate fault diagnosis during operation.

This identification process is a key component of the broader [[9-PRC-0021_electrical_numbering_and_identification_procedure|electrical numbering and identification framework]], ensuring seamless communication and control within the plant.
