# Converter External Installation Checklist

This checklist outlines the mandatory requirements for the external installation and structural integrity of the converter equipment. Adherence to these items is crucial for operational safety and long-term reliability.

**Mandatory External Installation Requirements:**

*   **Nameplate:** The correct Nameplate **must** be installed. This ensures proper identification and compliance with [[converter_fabrication_and_marking_requirements]].
*   **Foundation/Anchor Bolts:** All Foundation/Anchor bolts **must** be secure and tightened. This is critical for the structural stability of the converter, considering its [[converter_vessel_design_conditions]].
*   **Access Structures:** All Platforms, hand rails, walkways, stairs & ladders **must** be installed. These provide safe access for operation and maintenance personnel.
*   **Safety Gates:** Safety gates **must** be installed & functioning. These are essential for preventing unauthorized access to hazardous areas and ensuring personnel safety.
*   **Grounding:** Grounding **must** be completed (possible multiple locations). Proper grounding is vital for electrical safety and protection against lightning strikes. Refer to [[grounding_installation_details]] for detailed specifications.
*   **Insulation & Cladding:** Insulation & cladding **must** be complete. This is necessary for thermal efficiency, personnel protection, and corrosion prevention. Refer to [[insulation_erection_0_spc_6x96]] and [[coatings_erection_0_spc_6x97]] for specific requirements.

These external checks are part of a broader field inspection process to ensure that the converter is installed correctly and safely before internal components are finalized and commissioned.

**Mandatory External Installation Requirements:**
*   **Foundation/Anchor Bolts:** Must be secure and tightened.
*   **Hardware and Gaskets:** Correct hardware and gaskets must be installed.
*   **Access Structures:** All Platforms, hand rails, walkways, stairs & ladders must be installed.
*   **Safety Gates:** Must be installed & functioning.
*   **Grounding:** Must be completed (possible multiple locations).
*   **Insulation & Cladding:** Must be complete.
