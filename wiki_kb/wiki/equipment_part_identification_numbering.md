# Equipment Part Identification Numbering

This procedure, identified as 4-PRC-0005, outlines the requirements for establishing standard identification numbers for detachable equipment parts. These standard identification numbers **must** be used for the development of all related numbering systems, such as drawing numbers. Their application is mandatory across all projects unless specific client requirements explicitly dictate otherwise.

### Definition of Equipment Part
An equipment part is defined as any component of equipment that is detachable and typically replaced after a period of service. These parts often include items not originally purchased from the main equipment vendor.

**Examples of Equipment Parts:**
*   **Gaskets:** This includes manway gaskets, body flange gaskets, and special types used on explosion hatches or sight glasses.
*   **Nozzles:** Such as spray nozzles, sleeves (e.g., PTFE, Titanium), spargers, dip tubes, and vent goose necks.
*   **Support Plates:** Including packing support plates, distributor plates, and mist eliminator support plates.
*   **Internals:** This category covers packing, distributors, mist eliminators, and floating roofs.
*   **Miscellaneous Items:** Examples include spacers, rupture discs, stacks, insulators, slide pads, grounding devices, and pressure relief assemblies.
*   **Bolting:** Specifically, special fasteners like bolts, studs, washers, and nuts.
*   **Vendor-Independent Components:** Items not purchased from the main vendor, such as plastic components in a metal tank or metal components in a plastic tank.

### Numbering Format
The standard numbering format for an equipment part is **EPN1234-02**.
*   **EPN**: Stands for "Equipment Part".
*   **1234**: Represents the Associated Equipment Number. This is the standard equipment number, which refers to the specific piece of equipment to which the part belongs. Details on the standard equipment number are described in [[4-PRC-0001_plant_equipment_identification]].
*   **02**: Denotes the Serial Number. A unique two-digit number is assigned as the Serial Number to each distinct equipment part that belongs to the same piece of equipment.
