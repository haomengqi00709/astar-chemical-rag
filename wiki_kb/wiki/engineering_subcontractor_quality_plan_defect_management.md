# Engineering Subcontractor Quality Plan: Defect and Non-Conformance Management

Managing defects and non-conformances is a mandatory element of the [[engineering_subcontractor_quality_assurance_0_spc_7004|Engineering Subcontractor Quality Plan]]. The Subcontractor **must** establish and maintain documented procedures for the identification, documentation, correction, and prevention of recurrence of defects and non-conformances.

**Key Procedural Requirements:**

*   **Identification of Defects:** Procedures **must** be in place for identifying Defects that occur in the services provided.
*   **Documentation of Defects:** Procedures **must** detail how identified Defects in the services are documented, ensuring a clear record of the issue.
*   **Identification of Non-Conformances:** Procedures **must** also cover the identification of non-conformances with the requirements of the Quality Plan itself.
*   **Documentation of Non-Conformances:** Procedures **must** specify how non-conformances with the Quality Plan are documented.
*   **Corrective Action:** Documented procedures **must** outline the steps for taking appropriate corrective action for both Defects in services and non-conformances with the Quality Plan.
*   **Preventive Action (Quality Plan Changes):** Procedures **must** include provisions for enacting necessary changes to the Quality Plan to prevent the recurrence of identified Defects or non-conformances.
*   **Preventive Action (Referenced Document Changes):** Procedures **must** also cover enacting necessary changes to any referenced documents (e.g., procedures, instructions) to prevent the recurrence of Defects or non-conformances.

**Response to Audited Documents with Defects:**
Upon receipt of audited documents from Aker Kvaerner Chemetics where Defects have been noted (as described in [[engineering_subcontractor_quality_audit_process]]), the Subcontractor **must** follow the steps for correction and prevention of recurrence as set out in their Quality Plan or its referenced documents. This ensures a systematic approach to addressing and learning from identified issues.
