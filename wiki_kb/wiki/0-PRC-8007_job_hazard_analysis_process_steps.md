# Job Hazard Analysis (JHA) Process Steps

A [[0-PRC-8007_job_hazard_analysis]] (JHA) is systematically broken down into 7 major steps to ensure thorough hazard identification and control. This procedure requires the assistance of a safe, experienced worker (domain expert) throughout the process.

**1. Define the Job Description:**
*   The job description, located at the top of the JHA form, is a concise explanation of the job.
*   It **should not** be overly complex.
*   Sufficient detail **must** be provided to properly identify the job and differentiate it from other similar jobs.

**2. Break the Job into a Sequence of Steps:**
*   Divide the job into a logical sequence of individual steps.
*   Each step **should** describe *what* is being done, not *how* it is being done.
*   Care **must** be taken not to be excessively detailed when listing job steps.
*   When recording a step, begin with an action word (e.g., "remove," "carry," "open").
*   End a recorded step by identifying the object to which the action was applied (e.g., "remove the die," "carry to bench").
*   After observing and recording the steps, the observed employee **should** verify all steps and explain any deviations that may occur.

**3. Identify Potential Hazards for Each Step:**
*   For each step identified, determine what could go wrong and potentially result in an accident, injury, illness, or environmental impact.

**4. Develop Recommended Safe Job Procedures:**
*   Develop a recommended safe job procedure that effectively eliminates or controls the identified hazardous conditions or operations.
*   When developing controls, apply the [[0-PRC-8007_hierarchy_of_risk_control]] in order from most effective to least effective.
*   Seek the help of the experienced employee (domain expert) for practical suggestions in developing effective controls.

**5. Complete the JHA Record:**
*   Ensure that the Job Step/Activity, Potential Hazards, and Control Measures are clearly listed in the JHA record.
*   The primary form for this is [[0-REP-8007a_job_hazard_analysis_worksheet]]. For confined space entry, use [[0-REP-8007b_confined_space_hazard_assessment_form]].

**6. Review the Recorded JHA:**
*   Thoroughly review the completed JHA.
*   Assign a risk ranking to each task activity to prioritize control efforts.
*   Assign a responsible person for critical activities identified within the JHA.

**7. Integrate and Brief:**
*   Integrate the risk control measures with the job steps to form the job’s standard operating procedures.
*   Use the JHA as the guide to brief employees during their pre-job [[0-PRC-8006_task_safety_awareness_procedure]]. The [[0-REP-8006a_task_safety_awareness_form]] can be used for this briefing.
