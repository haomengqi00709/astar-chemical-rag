# Strainer Quality Record Requirements (0-QDS-7402)

Accurate and comprehensive quality records are essential for demonstrating compliance and traceability throughout the manufacturing and inspection process. For the Strainer (0-QDS-7402), specific requirements for quality record submission are defined, utilizing two primary classifications: 'As Prepared' (AP) and 'Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package' (FP). The overall definition of these record types is associated with a value of 4.0.

**Quality Record Definitions:**
*   **AP ("As Prepared"):** This designation indicates that the quality record is required to be prepared by the Vendor and submitted.
*   **FP ("Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package"):** This designation signifies that the quality record must be included as part of the comprehensive Quality Assurance Documentation Package submitted by the Vendor.

For general definitions of quality records, refer to [[0-QDS-7_quality_record_definitions]].

**Specific Quality Record Requirements:**
The following items require specific quality records as indicated:
*   **Mill Test Certificates:** Required as FP. These certificates provide crucial information about the material properties.
*   **Weld consumable certs.:** Required as FP. Documentation for welding consumables ensures material compatibility and quality.
*   **Hydrostatic test:** Required as FP. Records of hydrostatic tests confirm the pressure integrity of the Strainer.
*   **Radiographic testing:** Required as FP. Documentation of radiographic examinations provides evidence of internal weld quality.
*   **Dye Penetrant testing:** Required as FP. Records of dye penetrant tests confirm surface integrity of welds.
*   **Dimensional inspection:** Required as FP. Documentation of dimensional checks verifies adherence to design specifications.
*   **Contour & finish of welds inspection:** Required as FP. Records of weld contour and finish inspections ensure aesthetic and functional quality.
*   **Packing List:** Required as AP. This document details the contents of the shipment for logistical and verification purposes. It is not required as FP.

**Activities Not Requiring Quality Records:**
For certain inspection and verification activities, a formal Quality Record is explicitly not required:
*   **Surfaces free from defects:** Quality Record is not required.
*   **Cleanliness:** Quality Record is not required.
*   **Review QA Documentation:** Quality Record is not required.
*   **Preparation for shipping:** Quality Record is not required.

These specifications ensure that only essential documentation is generated and maintained, streamlining the quality assurance process while maintaining necessary traceability. For broader information on quality records, consult [[quality_records_and_documentation]].

The definition for 'FP' (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package) clarifies that these records must be part of the final QA documentation. The overall definition of Quality Record requirements (AP and FP) is associated with the value 4.0.

Specific quality records required as 'FP' include:
*   Mill Test Certificates
*   Weld consumable certificates
*   Hydrostatic test reports
*   Radiographic testing reports
*   Dye Penetrant testing reports
*   Dimensional inspection reports
*   Contour & finish of welds inspection reports

The **Packing List** is required as 'AP' (As Prepared), and it is explicitly noted that it is not required as 'FP'.

Conversely, Quality Records are **not required** for the following activities:
*   Surfaces free from defects
*   Cleanliness
*   Review QA Documentation
*   Preparation for shipping
