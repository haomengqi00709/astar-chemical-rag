# Quality Record Definitions

This page defines common abbreviations used in Quality Data Sheets (QDS) to specify the required documentation and its inclusion in the Quality Assurance Documentation Package. These definitions ensure clarity and consistency in quality record management across projects.

**Quality Record Requirements:**

*   **AP (As Prepared):** This designation indicates that the specified quality record is required "As Prepared." This means the document **must** be submitted as it is generated during the manufacturing or inspection process. An example of a record required "AP" is the [[0-QDS-7441_bridge_crane_packing_and_shipping_requirements#Packing List|Packing List]].

*   **FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package):** This designation indicates that the specified quality record is required to be "Included in the Quality Assurance Documentation Package." This means the document **must** be compiled and submitted as part of the comprehensive quality documentation for the project. Examples of records required "FP" include:
    *   [[0-QDS-7441_bridge_crane_material_verification_and_certifications#Mill Test Certificates|Mill Test Certificates]]
    *   [[0-QDS-7441_bridge_crane_material_verification_and_certifications#Certificate of Compliance|Certificate of Compliance]]
    *   [[0-QDS-7441_bridge_crane_coating_and_lining_inspection#External Coating / Lining Inspection|External Coating / Lining Inspection]]
    *   [[0-QDS-7441_bridge_crane_equipment_testing#Functional Test|Functional Test]]

These definitions are critical for understanding the documentation deliverables for projects such as the Bridge Crane, as detailed in [[0-QDS-7441_bridge_crane_quality_data_sheet_administration]].
