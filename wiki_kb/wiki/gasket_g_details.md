# Gasket G (Glass-Lined Steel Pipe)

Gasket G is specifically designed for glass-lined steel pipe applications and is supplied directly by the glass-lined steel pipe manufacturer.

### Specifications
*   **Construction:** Continuous U-type PTFE envelope with non-asbestos acid resistant filler and a stainless steel corrugated metal insert.

### Thickness
*   **For DN250 and less:**
    *   Uninstalled thickness: 7 mm
    *   Compressed thickness: 5 mm
*   **For DN300:**
    *   Uninstalled thickness: 10 mm
    *   Compressed thickness: 7 mm
