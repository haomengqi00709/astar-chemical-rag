# Control System Wiring Standards

This page outlines the typical wiring standards and types used for both control and electrical systems within Aker Kvaerner Chemetics projects. These standards ensure compatibility and proper functioning of instrumentation and control devices.

**Typical Control System Wiring:**
Control system wiring **WILL CONFORM** to the following types:
*   **Analog Inputs:** 2-wire/4-wire 4-20 mA.
*   **Discrete Inputs:** Isolated, Dry 120 VAC or 24 VDC.
*   **Discrete Outputs:** Isolated, High Side 120 VAC or 24 VDC.
*   **Digital Bus:** Profibus, Fieldbus.

**Typical Electrical System Wiring:**
Electrical system wiring **WILL CONFORM** to the following types:
*   **Analog:**
    *   Analog inputs are 2-wire/4-wire 4-20 mA.
    *   Analog outputs are 4-20 mA.
*   **Discrete:**
    *   Discrete inputs are Isolated, Dry 120 VAC or 24 VDC.
    *   Discrete outputs are Isolated, High Side 120 VAC or 24 VDC.

These standards are critical for the design of [[control_equipment_cabinet_layouts]] and [[io_interface_wiring_diagrams]], ensuring correct interconnection and operation of all field devices and control system components.
