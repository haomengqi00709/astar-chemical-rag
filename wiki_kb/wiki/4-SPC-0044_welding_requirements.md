# Glass-Lined Vessel Welding Requirements (4-SPC-0044)

This section details the mandatory welding procedures, processes, consumables, and quality control measures for the fabrication of glass-lined carbon steel reaction vessels, as outlined in 4-SPC-0044.

**Welding Procedures and Qualifications**
All welding procedures for pressure retaining parts **shall** be reviewed by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics prior to fabrication. The [[ASME_section_ix_welding_and_brazing_qualifications|ASME IX P.Q.R. Form 483]] **shall** form part of the welding procedure package. Fabricator's typically used welding procedures **shall** be provided to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for record purposes. Internal structural parts of non-pressure retaining parts in alloy materials which require welding **shall** be welded to an approved welding procedure by qualified personnel.

**Welding Execution**
Welding **shall** be performed in dry, clean surroundings. All longitudinal and circumferential seams **shall** be double butt welded with full penetration. Heads with a central hole resulting from manufacture **shall** have it completely welded and radiographed to [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels|ASME Code, Section VIII, Div. 1 Paragraph UW-51]]. Welding and radiography are not required if a nozzle is to be welded to the head at the central hole location.

Plate edges for welding **shall** be prepared by shearing, machining, grinding, or flame cutting. Plate thickness greater than 1¼" (31mm) **shall** be preheated prior to flame cutting. The maximum thickness of non-bevelled plate **shall not** exceed 5/16" (6mm). Shell sections **shall** be completely welded longitudinally and corrected for out-of-roundness and peaking of the weld seam prior to assembly. All rerolling or forming of shell sections **shall** be completed prior to any radiographic examination. Jacket closing welds **are to be** in accordance with [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels|ASME Code, Section VIII, Div. 1, Appendix IX]] requirements.

**Welding Processes and Restrictions**
Backing strips **shall not** be used, except as allowed on jacket closing welds. Shield electric arc processes **shall** be used with restrictions.

For Automatic Submerged Arc Welding:
*   Maximum weld deposit per pass **shall** be limited to ½" (12mm).
*   Starting and run-off pads of the same material as the base material **shall** be used on all longitudinal seams.
*   The flux and wire combination used on production welds **shall** be the same as used in the procedure qualifications.
*   Submerged arc welding procedures **shall** utilize the consumable manufacturer's recommended wire and flux combinations.
*   Consumable manufacturer's recommended wire and flux combinations **shall not** result in excessive manganese or silicon build-up in the weld.
*   Wire and flux combinations recommended for single pass welding **shall not** be used for multiple pass welds.
*   Wire spools **shall** be tagged for easy, in-shop, identification.

For Shield Metal Arc Welding:
*   The electrode used **shall** be the same type and brand as used in the qualification procedure.
*   Once electrode cans are opened, electrodes **shall** be stored in a cabinet heated in accordance with the manufacturer's recommendations.
*   Wet electrodes **shall** be discarded.

Back welding of screwed fittings is not permitted. Electroslag, Electrogas, Short Arc & Fluxcore processes **shall not** be used unless approved in writing by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. The Pfaudler and De Dietrich organizations have approval for these processes.

**Welding Consumables**
Certification is required for Welding Consumables.

**General Welding Requirements**
*   Jacket closing welds are to be in accordance with ASME Code, Section VIII, Div. 1, Appendix IX, requirements.
*   Heads which have a central hole as a result of manufacture, shall have it completely welded and radiographed to ASME Code, Section VIII, Div. 1 Paragraph UW-51. Welding and radiography are not required if a nozzle is to be welded to the head at the central hole location.
*   Plate edges for welding shall be prepared by shearing, machining, grinding or flame cutting. Plate thickness greater than 1¼" (31mm) shall be preheated prior to flame cutting.
*   The maximum thickness of non-bevelled plate shall not exceed 5/16" (6mm).
*   Shell sections shall be completely welded longitudinally and corrected for out of roundness and peaking of the weld seam prior to assembly.
*   All rerolling or forming of shell sections shall be completed prior to any radiographic examination.
*   All longitudinal and circumferential seams shall be double butt welded with full penetration.
*   Internal structural parts of non-pressure retaining parts in alloy materials which require welding shall be welded to an approved welding procedure by qualified personnel.
*   Welding shall be performed in dry, clean surroundings.
*   Backing strips shall not be used, except as allowed on jacket closing welds.

**Welding Procedures and Qualifications**
*   The ASME IX P.Q.R. Form 483 shall form part of the welding procedure package.
*   Fabricator's typically used welding procedures shall be provided to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for record purposes.

**Welding Processes**
*   Shield electric arc processes shall be used with restrictions.
*   For Automatic Submerged Arc Welding, maximum weld deposit per pass shall be limited to ½" (12mm).
*   Starting and run-off pads of same material as base material shall be used on all longitudinal seams.
*   Back welding of screwed fittings is not permitted.
*   Electroslag, Electrogas, Short Arc & Fluxcore processes shall not be used unless approved in writing by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. The Pfaudler and De Dietrich organizations have approval for these processes.

**Welding Consumables**
*   The flux and wire combination used on production welds shall be the same as used in the procedure qualifications.
*   Submerged arc welding procedures shall utilize the consumable manufacturer's recommended wire and flux combinations. These combinations shall not result in excessive manganese or silicon build-up in the weld.
*   Wire and flux combinations recommended for single pass welding shall not be used for multiple pass welds.
*   Wire spools shall be tagged for easy, in shop, identification.
*   For Shield Metal Arc Welding, the electrode used shall be the same type and brand as used in the qualification procedure.
*   Once electrode cans are opened, electrodes shall be stored in a cabinet heated in accordance with the manufacturer's recommendations. Wet electrodes shall be discarded.
