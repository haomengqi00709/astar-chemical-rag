# Metal Ductwork Packaging and Shipping (5-SPC-0010)

**General Requirements**
All packaging and shipping operations **shall** be conducted in accordance with:
*   [[0-SPC-5004_packaging_land_transportation]] (0-SPC-004: Packaging Land Transport).
*   [[0-SPC-5005_packaging_marine_transport]] (0-SPC-005: Packaging Marine Transport).
*   Packaging and shipping **must** also comply with additional requirements listed in this section.

**Weight Verification and Marking**
The Vendor **shall** verify all spool weights prior to lifting. Verification of spool weight **must** include the weight of components supplied free issue and assembled into the ductwork. The Vendor **shall** mark the weight on the outside of each duct spool.

**Internal Dryness and Sealing**
The Vendor **shall** maintain a dry condition inside of all ductwork spools throughout fabrication. All openings in the ductwork spools **shall** be covered and sealed prior to shipment to prevent the entry of unwanted material or water.

**Spool Marking**
The spool number **shall** be clearly marked on each duct spool in two places, at opposite sides of each end.

**Surface Protection**
Exposed machine surfaces, including flange faces, **shall** be coated with commercial rust preventative.

**Roundness Maintenance**
Duct spools **shall** be adequately braced at the open ends to maintain roundness tolerance during shipping.

**Flange and Connection Protection**
*   Flanges **shall** be blanked with bolted or steel-strapped wood or metal covers.
*   Tapped connections **shall** be fitted with threaded steel pipe plugs.
*   Threaded ends **shall** be provided with steel thread protectors.
*   All other openings **shall** be suitably covered to prevent the entry of unwanted material or water.

These requirements are integral to the [[5-SPC-0010_metal_ductwork_vendor_scope_of_supply]].
