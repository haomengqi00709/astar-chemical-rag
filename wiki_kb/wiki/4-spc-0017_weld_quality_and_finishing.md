# Weld Quality and Finishing for 6% Moly SS (4-SPC-0017)

This section outlines the requirements for achieving acceptable weld quality and the finishing procedures for 6 percent Moly austenitic stainless steel fabrications under Standard **4-SPC-0017**.

**Arc Strikes and Burn Marks**
Every effort **will** be made to confine arc strikes to the surface on which weld metal is to be deposited. If an arc strike occurs outside the weld metal deposition area, the spot **will** require to be ground, [[4-spc-0017_inspection_and_nde_requirements|PT examined]] per Para. 10.3, and, if necessary, repaired. Burn marks from grounding clamps **will** be treated as arc strikes.

**Cleaning Between Weld Runs**
Each run of weld metal **will** be thoroughly cleaned, and all slag and foreign matter removed before the next run is deposited. On re-starting, care **will** be taken to ensure proper fusion with previously deposited weld metal.

**Tungsten Contamination**
If a tungsten electrode accidentally makes contact with the work, the section of weld and the tip of the electrode **will** be examined. Where tungsten loss has occurred, the tungsten pick-up in the weld **will** be ground out. The electrode **will** be reground to the correct profile before re-starting after tungsten loss.

**Peening**
Peening of weld runs or hammering of finished welds is not permitted, except for light peening to remove slag.

**Weld Appearance and Integrity**
*   Each weld **will** be uniform in width and size throughout its full length.
*   Each layer of welding **will** be smooth and free of slag, inclusions, cracks, porosity, lack of fusion, and undercut, except to the extent permitted in referenced codes.
*   The cover pass **will** be free of coarse ripples, irregular surface, non-uniform bead patterns, high crown, and deep ridges or valleys between beads.
*   Buttwelds **will** be slightly convex, of uniform height, and have full penetration.
*   Crevices **will not** be permitted on the process side of completed weldments.

**Fillet Welds**
As far as is practicable, fillet welds **will** be deposited in not less than two layers. Fillet weld surfaces **will** be free from marked irregularities. Dimensions of fillet welds **will** be in accordance with the approved drawing.

**Grinding and Cleaning Tools**
Grinding or gouging **will not** be carried out in such a manner as to reduce the adjacent base metal thickness. Grinding wheels **will** be iron-free and of the resin bonded alumina or silica type. Wire brushes or chipping tools **will** be made of austenitic stainless steel and **must not** have been previously used to clean surfaces other than stainless steels.
