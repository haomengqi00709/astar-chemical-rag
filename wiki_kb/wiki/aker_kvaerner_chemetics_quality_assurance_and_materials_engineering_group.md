# Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Quality Assurance and Materials Engineering Group

The Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Quality Assurance and Materials Engineering Group is a critical internal resource for ensuring the integrity and compliance of materials and designs within projects.

**Role and Responsibilities:**
*   **Consultation:** This group **must** be consulted for material compatibility assessments, especially when dealing with hazardous fluids like sulphuric acid, to ensure materials are suitable for specific combinations of temperature and concentration.
*   **Specification Review:** They are involved in reviewing specifications and drawings to ensure adherence to quality and material engineering standards.
*   **Deviation Approval:** Deviations from established standards and specifications require written approval from the designated Aker Kvaerner Chemetics engineer, often involving input from this group.
*   **Inspection Oversight:** The group's inspectors play a key role in ensuring welding quality and overall fabrication meets specified standards.
*   **Documentation Review:** They review welding procedures, qualification records, design calculations, and quality records to ensure compliance.

Their expertise is vital in preventing material failures, ensuring safety, and maintaining the high quality of fabricated equipment.
