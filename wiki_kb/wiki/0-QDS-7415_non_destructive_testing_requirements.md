# Non-Destructive Testing Requirements (0-QDS-7415)

This section outlines the Non-Destructive Testing (NDT) requirements for the Dual Laminate Tank project, as specified in 0-QDS-7415. NDT is a critical item or activity to ensure the integrity of the tank without causing damage.

**Spark Testing:**
Spark testing is a mandatory NDT method used to detect pinholes or defects in the non-conductive lining of the dual laminate tank.
*   **Quality Record:** Spark testing results are mandatory quality records and **must** have a Quality Record status of FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package).
*   **Surveillance:** Surveillance by Chemetics for spark testing **must** involve both "Witness" and "Hold" points.
    *   **Witness:** Chemetics personnel **must** be present to observe the spark testing procedure.
    *   **Hold:** Work **must** not proceed beyond this point until Chemetics has reviewed and approved the witnessed testing.

These requirements ensure thorough inspection and verification of the tank's lining integrity. For other specific testing requirements related to the tank's construction, refer to [[0-QDS-7415_filament_winding_and_bond_testing]].
