# Quality Surveillance

Quality surveillance refers to the systematic monitoring and verification activities performed by a client or a designated third party to ensure that a vendor's processes, products, or services comply with specified quality requirements and standards throughout the project lifecycle. This can include inspections, audits, and reviews at various stages of manufacturing, fabrication, or service delivery.

In the context of a Bidder's offer for Goods and Services, particularly when proposing fixed delivery dates, the Bidder **must** allow for all necessary quality surveillance activities. This includes surveillance conducted by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics personnel or by other designated third-party inspectors.

The allowance for quality surveillance **must** be factored into the fixed delivery date offered by the Bidder. This ensures that adequate time is allocated for all required quality checks without causing delays to the overall project schedule. Effective quality surveillance is crucial for mitigating risks, ensuring compliance, and ultimately delivering a high-quality product or service.
