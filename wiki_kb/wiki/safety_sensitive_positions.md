# Safety-Sensitive Positions

A **Safety-Sensitive Position** is defined by the potential for impaired performance to result in direct and significant risk. This designation is crucial for identifying roles where substance use policies and testing procedures are particularly critical.

### Definition of a Safety-Sensitive Position

A position is considered Safety-Sensitive if:
1.  It involves limited or no supervision, and impaired performance in this role could result in direct and significant risk of injury to the Employee, others, or the environment.
2.  It is a supervisory position where the Employee is supervising individuals in positions with limited or no supervision, and impaired performance by the supervisor could result in direct and significant risk of injury to the Employee, others, or the environment.

### Temporary Assignments
A temporary assignment of an Employee may be classified as a Safety-Sensitive Position, even if the Employee’s regular job position is not typically considered Safety-Sensitive.

### Typical Locations
Safety-Sensitive Positions will normally include only positions or assignments located at, or directly controlling activities at, a plant site.
