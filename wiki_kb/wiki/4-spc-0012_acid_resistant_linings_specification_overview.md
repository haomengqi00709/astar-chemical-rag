# 4-SPC-0012 Acid Resistant Linings Specification Overview

Document 4-SPC-0012, titled "Specification", outlines the minimum technical requirements for acid-resistant linings in towers and tanks within sulphuric acid plants. This specification is crucial for ensuring the integrity and longevity of equipment handling 92-100% sulphuric acid, specifically drying and absorption towers and their associated pump tanks.

**Scope**
This document defines the technical standards for the design, materials, and installation of chemically resistant brick linings. It serves as a foundational reference for suppliers and contractors involved in these specialized applications.

**Document Administration and History**
A name change conversion occurred in 2005, affecting this document. Notably, no new revision number was assigned for this conversion, and the changes made were not explicitly marked within the document. For general document administration procedures, refer to [[0-PRC-0002_document_numbering]] and [[2005_name_change_conversion_details]].

**Compliance**
Any deviations from the requirements stipulated in the purchase order documentation or this specification (4-SPC-0012) **must** be approved in writing by the client/owner. Vendors are also obligated to consult the client/owner before commencing any work if technical difficulties are anticipated with a particular lining design.
