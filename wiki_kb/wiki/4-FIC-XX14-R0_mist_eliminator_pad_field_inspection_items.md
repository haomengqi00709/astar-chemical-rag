# Mist Eliminator Pad Field Inspection Items (4-FIC-XX14-R0)

This page details the specific installation and cleanliness requirements for Mist Eliminator Pads, as outlined in the field inspection checklist 4-FIC-XX14-R0. Adherence to these items is crucial for ensuring the proper function and longevity of the mist eliminator system.

**Installation Requirements:**
The following mandatory requirements **must** be verified during the installation of the mist eliminator pad:
*   The Mist Eliminator **must** be installed as per supplier's instructions. This includes all manufacturer-specific guidelines and procedures.
*   All segments **must** be checked for bolt tightness. Proper torque application is essential for structural integrity and sealing.
*   Torque may be specified for bolt tightness. If specified, these values **must** be strictly followed.
*   Correct hardware **must** be installed. This includes ensuring that all bolts, nuts, washers, and other fasteners match the specifications.
*   Correct gaskets **must** be installed. Gaskets are critical for preventing leaks and ensuring a tight seal between sections.
*   Mist eliminator sections **must** fit tightly together. Any looseness can compromise performance.
*   No gaps **must** be visible between mist eliminator sections. Gaps can lead to bypass and reduced efficiency.
*   A Stainless Steel Drip Ring **must** be installed. This component is vital for collecting and directing liquid runoff.
*   Seal Pots **must** be installed. These are necessary for maintaining liquid seals and preventing gas bypass.

**Inspection and Cleanliness:**
Upon completion of the installation, the following inspection points **must** be thoroughly checked:
*   The installation **must** be checked from above the mist eliminator to verify alignment, fit, and overall integrity.
*   The installation **must** be checked from below the mist eliminator to ensure proper seating, sealing, and drip ring/seal pot connections.
*   The Mist Eliminator **must** be clean and free from any foreign materials.
*   All debris **must** be removed from the tower before closing it up. This prevents contamination and potential operational issues.
