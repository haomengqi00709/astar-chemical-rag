# Ejector Pumped Fluid Operating Conditions

This page details the standard units and parameters for specifying the pumped fluid operating conditions for ejectors, as outlined in the Template Ejector Datasheet (Document ID [[template_ejector_datasheet_administration]]). These parameters are crucial for the proper design and selection of ejector systems.

**Pumped Fluid Parameters and Units:**
*   **Flow Rate:** kg/h (also specified in Am3/h)
*   **Minimum Flow Rate:** kg/h
*   **Maximum Flow Rate:** kg/h
*   **Temperature:** °C
*   **Inlet Pressure:** kPa(a)
*   **Discharge Pressure:** kPa(a)
*   **Density:** kg/m³
*   **Viscosity:** cP

These specifications contribute to the overall [[process_design_criteria_1_prc_0001]] and are typically captured in the [[process_data_sheet_overview]] for the equipment.

**Pumped Fluid Parameters and Units:**
*   **Flow Rate:** kg/h, Am3/h
*   **Minimum Flow Rate:** kg/h
*   **Maximum Flow Rate:** kg/h
*   **Temperature:** oC
*   **Inlet Pressure:** kPa(a)
*   **Discharge Pressure:** kPa(a)
*   **Density:** kg/m3
*   **Viscosity:** cP
