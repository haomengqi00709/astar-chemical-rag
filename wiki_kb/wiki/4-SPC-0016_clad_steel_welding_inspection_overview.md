# Clad Steel Welding Inspection Overview

Inspection activities are integral to ensuring the quality and compliance of austenitic stainless clad steel welded fabrications. The "Inspector" (an Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Quality Assurance Inspector or other Authorized Representative) plays a crucial role throughout the fabrication process.

**Inspector Access and Authority**
The Inspector **will** have free access to the Vendor's work at all reasonable times. If work is sublet by approval, the Inspector **will** also have free access to the Subvendor's work at all reasonable times. The Inspector **will** be at liberty to inspect the work during fabrication at any stage in manufacture and **will** be at liberty to reject any material or workmanship that does not conform to this Standard or the applicable code.

It is important to note that inspection by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics does not relieve the Fabricator of responsibility for providing materials, components, and workmanship in accordance with the requirements of this Standard, engineering design, and the applicable code.

**Clad Plate Delamination and Repair**
Evidence of lack of bond revealed by nozzle cut-outs, edge preparation, etc., **will** require that the suspect areas be ultrasonically examined. The extent of delamination **must** be determined for these suspect areas. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **will** be advised of the extent of the delaminated surfaces and the repair method proposed by the Fabricator.

All repairs to clad surfaces **will** be ground smooth. These repairs **will** be performed in accordance with the [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels]] code and **will** satisfy the specific requirements of SA578 Level II supplementary requirement S6. A report of these repair areas **will** be provided to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. Where load-bearing or stressed attachments are to be welded directly to the cladding, the Fabricator **will** ultrasonically check the area concerned for lack of bond prior to welding.

**Chemical Analysis and Spot Checks**
The Supplier **must** make available to the purchaser's inspector copies of chemical certification for each batch of electrodes or wire coils used, as well as for plate material. If electrodes, wire, etc., cannot be identified to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' satisfaction, samples **will** be removed for chemical analysis at the Supplier’s account.

A spot check analysis **will** be performed on production welds of equipment such as pressure vessels, heat exchangers, and certain proprietary items. This spot check analysis requirement **will** also apply to non-pressure retaining equipment. The weld(s) to be checked **will** be selected at random by the Inspector or designated representative.

Where results of chemical analysis show that a specific weldment falls outside the approved specification or code requirements, the Supplier **will** promptly advise Aker Kvaerner Chemetics in writing of their proposed course of action. This proposed course of action **must** include details of any additional testing necessary to fully define the extent of the non-conformance. All repairs and retests related to non-conforming chemical analysis **will** be to the Supplier’s account. This process is crucial for [[vendor_quality_assurance]].

**Inspection of Clad Plate Integrity**
Nozzle cut-outs **must** be examined by the fabricator for lack of bond and **must** be retained for customer inspection. Evidence of lack of bond revealed by nozzle cut-outs, edge preparation, etc., **will** require that the suspect areas be ultrasonically examined. The extent of delamination **must** be determined for suspect areas. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **will** be advised of the extent of the delaminated surfaces and the repair method proposed by the fabricator.
