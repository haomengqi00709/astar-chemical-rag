# Refractory Brick Lining Post-Installation and Curing

Upon completion of the refractory brick lining installation, specific procedures for cleanup and curing **must** be followed to ensure the final quality and readiness of the equipment.

**Cleanup Requirements**
The completed lining **must** present a clean, workmanlike appearance. The lining installer **shall** be responsible for removing all loose mortar, loose bricks, scaffolding, and any other debris from the inside of the equipment and from the general work area. This thorough cleanup is a mandatory step before final acceptance.

**Curing Process**
After the installation is complete and has undergone the necessary inspections, the lining **shall** be cured. This curing process **must** be performed strictly in accordance with the instructions provided by the Vendor of the lining. Adherence to the Vendor's curing guidelines is essential for the proper setting and long-term performance of the refractory material.

**Inspection and Acceptance**
The cleanup process is subject to inspection after installation is complete to verify compliance with this specification. For full details on inspection stages and acceptance criteria, refer to [[refractory_brick_lining_inspection_and_acceptance]].
