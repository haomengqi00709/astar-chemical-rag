# Electrical Personnel

Electrical personnel are qualified individuals responsible for the installation, maintenance, inspection, and testing of electrical systems and components. Their expertise is crucial for ensuring the safety and functionality of all electrical installations, including tasks such as checking the [[electrical_continuity_testing|electrical continuity]] of [[pipe_grounding_ring_inspection_5_prc_0007|pipe grounding rings]] as part of mechanical checkout procedures. They ensure compliance with electrical codes, standards, and project-specific requirements.
