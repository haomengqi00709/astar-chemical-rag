# 4-SPC-0040 Material Sourcing and Segregation

Specification 4-SPC-0040 outlines strict requirements for material sourcing, identification, and segregation to ensure the quality and integrity of metallic process vessels and components.

**Material Sourcing Restrictions:**
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may impose restrictions on the country of origin and/or specific suppliers of metallic materials, including plate, sheet, tube, pipe, and fittings.
The following materials and suppliers are **not acceptable**:
*   Materials from Enlin Steel (Taiwan)
*   Materials from Highveld Steel and Vanadium Corporation (South Africa)
*   Materials from Heavy Metal and Tubes Ltd. (India)
*   Materials from Vitkovice Steel (Czech Republic)

**Titanium Product Sourcing:**
Titanium products are only **permitted** from the following countries/suppliers:
*   U.S.A.
*   Canada
*   European Union (EU) countries
*   Japan
*   Australia
*   VSMPO (Russia)
*   Baoji (China)

**Material Certification and Traceability:**
*   Mill test certificates are **required** for all materials, including weld consumables.
*   Mill test certificates **must** clearly show the country of origin of the primary material.
*   Identification and traceability are **required** to meet the minimum requirements of the [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels|ASME Code]].

**Attachment Materials:**
*   Attachments welded to equipment **must** be of a Code approved material of the same P-No. as the equipment.
*   Where attachments are of a different P-No., a poison pad of the same P-No. as the equipment **must** be used.

**Material Segregation and Contamination Control:**
*   Fabrication of carbon steel **must** be completed in an area segregated from stainless steel and other alloys.
*   The Vendor **must** use an effective system to identify and segregate different materials to prevent inadvertent use.
*   Contamination of stainless steels, nickel-based alloys, titanium, zirconium, and tantalum alloys by carbon steel or other incompatible materials is **not permitted**.
*   Contamination of specified alloys will be cause for rejection.
*   Grinding wheels, wire brushes, and other hand tools **must not** be used on more than one type of material.
