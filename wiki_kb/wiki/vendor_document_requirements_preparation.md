# Vendor Document Requirements (VDR) Preparation

Vendor Document Requirements (VDRs) are crucial documents issued to Vendors to clearly define the documentation expectations for goods supplied. These requirements are prepared for each equipment type and each type or class of bulk materials listed in the [[form_of_purchase_order_0_prc_5001|Purchase Order]].

**Mandatory Content of VDRs:**
*   VDRs **must** contain a list of documents or document packages to be supplied.
*   VDRs **must** contain the required delivery dates of the documents (packages).
*   VDRs **must** contain identification of hold documents (packages), which are critical documents requiring Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' approval before the Vendor can proceed with related work.
*   VDRs **must** contain instructions for documents to be bundled as manuals.
*   Date entries within VDRs **must** be in the correct format (e.g., 12 Jan 03) to facilitate cutting and pasting.

**Preparation and Approval Process:**
VDRs are prepared by the requisitioner of the goods. They are then checked by Quality Assurance and approved by the responsible Discipline Approver. For details on Discipline Approver responsibilities, refer to [[assignment_of_project_responsibilities|Document 0-PRC-2001: Assignment of Project Responsibilities]].

**Numbering Convention:**
VDRs are numbered according to [[document_numbering|Document 0-PRC-0002: Document Numbering]]. The identification number of a VDR will be identical to that of the [[form_of_purchase_order_0_prc_5001|Purchase Order]]. In cases where multiple VDRs are required for the same Purchase Order, they will be numbered as multiple sheets of the same document. The sheet title for these multiple VDRs will describe the specific type of equipment or bulk material to which it applies.

**Revisions to VDRs:**
VDRs **must** be revised to incorporate any changes agreed with the Vendor during bid evaluation. These revisions **must** include converting document delivery dates from "Weeks ARO (After Receipt of Order)" to fixed calendar dates.
