# Line and Flange Break Isolation and Safety Preparation

Thorough isolation and preparation are critical steps to ensure safety before commencing any line or equipment opening. This page outlines the mandatory procedures for identifying, isolating, and preparing the work area.

**Positive Identification**
Piping and equipment that has been in service **must** be positively identified by an individual familiar with that specific system. This ensures that the correct line or equipment is being worked on and that its contents and history are accurately known.

**Isolation Procedures**
Any piping or equipment to be opened or disassembled **shall** be isolated by having the appropriate upstream and downstream valve(s) closed, disconnected, blinded, or by having a mechanical restraining device affixed. A low point bleed valve should be open to assure no stored energy or chemicals remain in the line.

**Lockout and Tagout (LOTO)**
*   “DO NOT OPERATE” tags, “ENERGY ISOLATION” tags, or equivalent **shall** be placed at all isolation points.
*   It is mandatory that all valves be blinded, locked, tagged out, or chained and tagged when employees are working downstream or upstream.
*   Where potential for leaks exists, it is mandatory that all valves be blinded, locked, and tagged out.
*   Switches and/or push buttons that operate pumps, blowers, or any other electrical, pneumatic, or hydraulic equipment **must** be locked and tagged per the [[0-PRC-8504-RA_lockout_and_tagging|Lockout and Tagging procedure]].
*   Pumps, motors, or other devices that are activated by mechanical devices such as floats, switches, etc., **shall** be operated manually to assure they are properly isolated.

**Barricades**
Barricades **shall** be erected to warn other workers of the possible hazard(s). If the line breaking/equipment opening is at an elevated location, all levels below where workers may walk **shall** be barricaded if a hazard may exist.

**Work Coordination**
Coordination between Aker Kvaerner Chemetics personnel and Client operations personnel **shall** be such that no other process work, maintenance, or sampling is being performed on the line being opened. If other work is being performed, it **shall** be evaluated to assure the other activities will not endanger the personnel performing the line breaking operation.

**Emergency Facilities**
*   Where eyewash/safety shower facilities are not readily available and the potential for hazardous materials exists, a potable water hose with a quick opening valve **shall** be located at the work site.
*   If a shower facility is readily available, it **shall** be tested before starting the job.
*   The location of a readily available shower facility **shall** be known by all employees performing the first line break.
