# Template Heat Exchanger Mechanical Design Requirements

This page details specific mechanical design requirements for heat exchangers, as specified in document 4-PDS-XXXX-R2. These requirements ensure the structural integrity, maintainability, and operational flexibility of the equipment.

**Frame Capacity:**
*   The Frame **must** accept a minimum of ____% additional plates. This is a mandatory requirement to allow for future capacity expansion or to facilitate maintenance and replacement of plates without requiring a complete frame overhaul. The specific percentage must be filled in the datasheet.

For broader mechanical design considerations, refer to [[general_mechanical_design_requirements_0_spc_0010]]. Specific mechanical design requirements for different heat exchanger types can be found in [[shell_and_tube_heat_exchanger_mechanical_design_requirements]] and [[plate_heat_exchanger_mechanical_design_requirements]].
