# Electrical Installation Criteria

Electrical Installation Criteria define the preferred methods and materials for installing specific electrical components and systems. These criteria are typically presented in various drawings and are crucial for maintaining consistency and quality across the project.

**Purpose and Content**
Electrical installation criteria drawings **shall** show the installation method and materials required. The details in these criteria represent the preferred methods of installing specific materials.

**Key Areas Covered:**
*   **Grounding:**
    *   Grounding bars in the electrolyzer cell rooms.
    *   Grounding rings in the electrolyzer piping runs.
    *   Cabling required for interconnecting the grounding rings.
    *   Cabling required for interconnecting the grounding bars.
    *   Cabling required for interconnecting the plant main ground grid.
*   **Support Systems:**
    *   Methods and material of supporting lighting transformers.
    *   Methods and material of supporting distribution panel boards.
