# HSE Coordinator Training

Formal training **should** be provided to all Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) Coordinators. This specialized training is crucial for equipping them with the necessary knowledge and skills to effectively manage and promote HSE within the company.

The training for HSE Coordinators **should** specifically focus on HSE Management concepts. A key emphasis of this training **should** be on proactive methods for controlling accidents and losses, enabling coordinators to implement preventative strategies rather than solely reactive measures.

This dedicated training ensures that HSE Coordinators are well-prepared to support the overall [[0-PRC-8016_hse_training_program_overview]] and contribute to a robust safety culture.
