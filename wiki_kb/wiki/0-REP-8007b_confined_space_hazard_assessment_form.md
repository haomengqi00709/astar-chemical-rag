# Confined Space Hazard Assessment Form (Form 0-REP-8007b)

Form Standard-0-REP-8007b is the official **Confined Space Hazard Assessment Form**. This specialized form is used to document the specific hazards and control measures required for confined space entry or work, as part of the [[confined_space_hazard_assessment_requirements]]. It is a critical component of the [[0-PRC-8007_job_hazard_analysis]] process when applied to confined spaces.
