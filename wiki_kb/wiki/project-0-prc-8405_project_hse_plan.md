# Project Health, Safety and Environmental Plan

This document (Project-0-PRC-8405) represents a project-specific Health, Safety, and Environmental (HSE) Plan. It details the specific strategies, procedures, and responsibilities for managing HSE risks and ensuring compliance on a particular project.

**Implementation:**
The construction site HSE Manager is responsible for implementing this project-specific plan during [[hse_construction_sites]]. Similarly, the commissioning HSE Manager is responsible for its implementation during [[hse_commissioning_sites]]. This plan works in conjunction with the Aker Kvaerner Safety Policy Manual and incorporates Client and Jurisdictional requirements.

This project-specific plan is developed based on the overarching principles and responsibilities outlined in [[0-prc-8001_project_health_and_safety_planning]].
