# Project Buyer Roles and Responsibilities

This page outlines the key roles and responsibilities of the Project Buyer within the context of project procurement and contract administration, particularly as defined in Purchase Order [[0-SPC-5001_purchase_order_overview|0-SPC-5001]].

**Core Responsibilities**
The Project Buyer acts as a central point of contact and authority for commercial and contractual matters related to Purchase Orders. Their responsibilities include:

*   **Communication Management:** All communications from the Vendor to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or its representatives **must** be copied to the Project Buyer.
*   **Delegation of Actions:** The Project Buyer, after notifying the Vendor, **may** delegate any of their actions or cancel any delegation.
*   **Instruction Issuance:** The Project Buyer **may** give instructions to the Vendor that change the [[goods_and_services_definitions|Goods and Services Information]] or the [[0-SPC-5001_purchase_order_overview#Delivery Date|Delivery Date]].
*   **Ambiguity Resolution:** The Project Buyer **shall** give an instruction to resolve any ambiguity or inconsistency in or between the documents that are part of the Purchase Order.
*   **Work Stoppage and Restart:** The Project Buyer **may** instruct the Vendor to stop or not to start any work, and later instruct them to re-start or start work.
*   **Delivery Authorization:** The Vendor **shall not** deliver Goods and Services before the Delivery Date unless the Project Buyer instructs otherwise.
*   **Testing and Inspection Notification:** The Project Buyer **must** notify the Vendor when tested or inspected Goods and Services have passed, before the Vendor can Deliver them.
*   **Defect Cost Assessment:** If the Vendor fails to correct a notified [[0-SPC-5001_purchase_order_overview#Defect|Defect]] within its [[0-SPC-5001_purchase_order_overview#Defects Correction Period|Defect Correction Period]], the Project Buyer **shall** assess the cost of having the Defect corrected.
*   **Payment Assessment:** The Project Buyer **shall** assess the amount due at each invoice date if an invoice has been given.
*   **Payment Correction:** The Project Buyer **may** correct any wrongly assessed amount due in a later assessment.
*   **Evidence of Payment:** The Project Buyer **may** request evidence from the Vendor that all amounts owing to any person related to the Goods and Services have been paid.
*   **Retention Assessment:** If the Vendor does not provide satisfactory evidence of payment, the Project Buyer **may** assess an amount to be retained from the Vendor as security against claims.
*   **Access to Work:** The Vendor **must** provide the Project Buyer with access to work being done or goods and materials being stored for the Purchase Order.
*   **Instruction Compliance:** The Vendor **must** obey an instruction given by the Project Buyer that is in accordance with the Purchase Order.
*   **Quality System Access:** The Project Buyer **shall** be granted access to the Vendor's and any Suppliers' quality systems for monitoring and auditing.

The Project Buyer plays a critical role in ensuring contractual compliance, managing changes, and overseeing financial aspects throughout the lifecycle of a Purchase Order.
