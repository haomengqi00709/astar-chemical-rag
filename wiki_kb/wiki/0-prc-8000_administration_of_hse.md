# Administration of Health, Safety and Environment (HSE)

This procedure, documented as 0-PRC-8000, outlines the comprehensive management system employed by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics ("the Company") to ensure strict compliance with the Aker Kvaerner Group's Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) Policy, Group Policy Number 2.2. HSE is defined as Health, Safety, and Environment and is considered a core value within Aker Kvaerner.

**Aker Kvaerner's HSE Mindset and Objectives:**
The Company operates under the fundamental HSE mindset that all incidents can be prevented. Aker Kvaerner continuously strives for zero harm to personnel, material, and non-material assets. Key objectives include:
*   Focusing on employee health and continuously improving the work environment.
*   Conducting operations with efficient use of materials and energy, minimizing waste and environmental damage.
*   Designing products and services to have no undue environmental impact, to be safe, and to be efficient in consuming energy and natural resources.
*   Ensuring products can be recycled or disposed of safely.

**Mandatory HSE Principles:**
*   Every employee **must** take personal responsibility for HSE by focusing on their own behavior.
*   A systematic and measurable approach **must** be applied to continually improve HSE culture and performance.
*   High HSE standards **must** be maintained wherever business is conducted.
*   Complying with applicable laws and regulations is the minimum standard.
*   HSE issues and performance **must** be openly communicated.
*   HSE best practices **must** be shared and learned from, both internally and externally.
*   HSE performance **must** be included in the selection, appraisal, and reward of staff.
*   HSE **must** be integrated into all business processes.
*   Line management is required to provide HSE leadership and implement the policy.

**Compliance and Jurisdictional Requirements:**
HSE protection is achieved by supporting and enforcing:
*   The Occupancy Health and Safety (OHS) regulations of the Workers Compensation Board of British Columbia (WCB).
*   The Aker Kvaerner Group Health and Safety Manual.
*   Company-specific safety policies and procedures.

Company safety policies and procedures **must** meet or exceed the requirements of both Aker Kvaerner and WCB policies. In case of conflict, the higher standard **will** be used. When working in foreign jurisdictions, the Company **will** comply with applicable legislated law, Client HSE Standards, and contract documents, while striving toward higher standards.

**Group HSE Governance and Precedence:**
AK Chemetics HSE policies and procedures **will** meet the Group HSE governance procedures approved by the Group President/CEO. These include:
*   Pol 2.2 Health, Safety and Environment (HSE) Policy
*   Pol 2.2 PR1 HSE Reporting Procedure
*   Pol 2.2 NO1 Incident Follow-Up Norm
*   Pol 2.2 NO2 Travel Health, Safety and Security Norm
*   Pol 2.2 DI1 Contact List for Emergency Notification of Incident
*   Pol 2.2 DI4 HSE Policy Related Definitions
*   Enet Location of group HSE Documents

In the absence of a Company procedure addressing a specific HSE component, the policies and procedures of the Oil, Gas, Process and Energy Division **will** prevail. If those are also absent, Aker Kvaerner group policies and procedures **will** prevail.

**HSE Management System Elements:**
Group Policy 2.2 outlines seven elements of an effective HSE Management System, which **will** be applied throughout the organization. Each element includes sub-elements with clear expectations and a list of minimum enabling arrangements that **must** be in place. The Operating System performance of each Business Unit is evaluated annually through self-assessment and peer review.

**Document Ownership and Approval:**
This procedure (0-PRC-8000) is owned by the Company President and **must** be approved by the Company President. It **must** also be checked by the HSE Manager.

**Company Definition and Core Values:**
*   "Company" refers to the corporate entity Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
*   HSE is a core value in Aker Kvaerner.
*   Aker Kvaerner's HSE Mindset states: All incidents can be prevented.
*   Aker Kvaerner strives continuously for zero harm to personnel, material and non-material assets.
*   Aker Kvaerner focuses on employee health and on continuously improving the work environment.
*   Aker Kvaerner conducts operations through efficient use of materials and energy, with minimum waste and damage to the environment.
*   Aker Kvaerner designs products and services to have no undue environmental impact, to be safe and to be efficient in consuming energy and natural resources.
*   Aker Kvaerner seeks to ensure that its products can be recycled or disposed of safely.

**HSE Management Principles:**
*   A systematic and measurable approach must be applied to continually improve HSE culture and performance.
*   High HSE standards must be brought wherever business is done.
*   Complying with applicable laws and regulations is the minimum standard.
*   HSE issues and performance must be openly communicated.
*   HSE best practices must be shared and learned from, internally and externally.
*   HSE performance must be included in the selection, appraisal and reward of staff.
*   HSE must be integrated in all business processes.

**Protection of Health, Safety, and Environment:**
*   Health, Safety, and the Environment will be protected by supporting and enforcing the Occupancy Health and Safety (OHS) regulations of the Workers Compensation Board of British Columbia (WCB).
*   Health, Safety, and the Environment will be protected by supporting and enforcing the Aker Kvaerner Group Health and Safety Manual.
*   Health, Safety, and the Environment will be protected by supporting and enforcing Company specific safety policies and procedures.
*   Company safety policies and procedures will meet or exceed the requirements of Aker Kvaerner policies and procedures.
*   Company safety policies and procedures will meet or exceed the requirements of WCB policies and procedures.
*   In case of conflict, the policy or procedure deemed to the higher standard will be used.

**Foreign Jurisdictions and Policy Hierarchy:**
*   When performing work in foreign jurisdictions, the Company will comply with applicable legislated law, Client HSE Standards, and contract documents. The Company will also strive toward higher standards.
*   AK Chemetics HSE policies and procedures will meet the Group HSE governance procedures approved by the Group President/CEO.
*   In the absence of a Company procedure addressing a specific HSE component, the policies and procedures of the Oil, Gas, Process and Energy Division will prevail.
*   In the absence of Oil, Gas, Process and Energy Division policies, Aker Kvaerner group policies and procedures will prevail.

**HSE Management System Elements:**
*   Group Policy 2.2 outlines seven elements of an effective HSE Management System, which will be applied throughout the organization.
*   Each management element has sub-elements with clearly stated expectations.
*   Each management element has a list of minimum enabling arrangements that must be in place to achieve the expectations.
