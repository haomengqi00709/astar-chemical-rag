# Purchase Order Schedule Management

Effective schedule management is crucial for the timely [[purchase_order_definitions#Delivery or Deliver|Delivery]] of [[purchase_order_definitions#The Goods and Services|Goods and Services]] under the Purchase Order (PO). This page details the requirements and procedures for managing the project schedule.

**Key Dates and Instructions:**
*   The Purchase Order is effective from the [[purchase_order_definitions#The Starting Date|Starting Date]].
*   The Vendor **must** [[purchase_order_definitions#To Provide the Goods and Services|Provide the Goods and Services]] such that Delivery occurs on the [[purchase_order_definitions#The Delivery Date|Delivery Date]].
*   The Vendor **must not** deliver the Goods and Services before the Delivery Date unless instructed by the [[purchase_order_roles_and_responsibilities#Project Buyer|Project Buyer]].
*   The Project Buyer may instruct the Vendor to stop or not to start any work. The Project Buyer may later instruct the Vendor to re-start or start work.

**Accepted Schedule:**
*   The **Accepted Schedule** is either the schedule referenced in the Purchase Order or the latest schedule formally accepted by the Project Buyer.
*   The latest schedule accepted by the Project Buyer supersedes all previous Accepted Schedules.

**Schedule Submission and Updates:**
*   If a schedule is required, the Vendor **must** submit a first schedule to the Project Buyer for acceptance within **two weeks** of the Starting Date.
*   The Vendor is **not** entitled to receive any payment until a required schedule is accepted.
*   The Vendor **must** include the information specified in the [[purchase_order_definitions#Goods and Services Information|Goods and Services Information]] on each schedule submitted for acceptance.
*   The Vendor **must** submit a monthly progress update of the Accepted Schedule.
