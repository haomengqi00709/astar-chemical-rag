# Rupture Disc Inspection (5-PRC-0007)

The inspection of rupture discs is a crucial safety-focused activity within the [[mechanical_checkout_piping_5_prc_0007|Piping Mechanical Checkout Procedure (5-PRC-0007)]]. This ensures that these passive overpressure protection devices are correctly installed and configured.

**Required Documentation for Rupture Disc Checkout:**
*   [[piping_and_instrumentation_drawings|P&ID]]
*   [[piping_speciality_item_specification|Specialty Item Specification]]
*   [[pipe_isometric|Isometric]]

**Rupture Disc Inspection Steps:**
*   **Flange Checks:** All flange checks for rupture discs **shall** be performed as described in the [[flange_inspection_requirements_5_prc_0007|Flange Inspection Requirements]].
*   **Type and Tag Verification:** Confirm that the rupture disc type and tag number match the [[piping_and_instrumentation_drawings|P&ID]] and [[pipe_isometric|isometric]].
*   **Vendor Tag Details:** Confirm that the vendor tag has the correct model number, burst pressure, material etc.
*   **Accessibility:** Confirm that the [[rupture_disc_installation|installation]] is accessible for maintenance, including replacement.
*   **Flow Direction:** Confirm that the rupture disc has been installed in the correct flow direction.
*   **Holder Materials:** Confirm the materials of construction for rupture disc holders (where applicable).

These inspections are vital for ensuring the correct functioning of rupture discs, which provide instantaneous overpressure relief in critical applications.
