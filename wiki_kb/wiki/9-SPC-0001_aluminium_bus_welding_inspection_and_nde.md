# Aluminium Bus Welding Inspection and NDE

The Aluminium Bus Welding Standard (9-SPC-0001) details comprehensive requirements for inspection and Non-Destructive Examination (NDE) to verify weld quality and compliance.

**Inspector Access and Authority**
The Inspector, defined as an [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_quality_assurance_and_materials_engineering_group|Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Quality Assurance Inspector]] or other Authorized Representative, **shall** have free access to the Vendor's work and, if approved, the Subvendor's work at all reasonable times. The Inspector **shall** be at liberty to inspect the work during fabrication at any stage of manufacture and to reject any material or workmanship that does not conform to this Standard or the applicable code.

**Material Certification and Analysis**
The supplier **must** make available to the Inspector copies of chemical certification for batches of electrodes or wire coils used. If electrodes, wire, etc., cannot be identified to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' satisfaction, samples **shall** be removed for chemical analysis, which **shall** be to the supplier's account. When required by the purchase order, a spot check analysis **shall** be performed on production welds, with the weld(s) selected at random by the Inspector.

**Radiography Requirements**
Test Pieces **shall** be radiographed in accordance with [[csa_w47_2_aluminium_welding_qualification_code|CSA W47.2]] to ascertain the quality of the welds prior to the commencement of shop or site welding. The quality of the production welds **shall** be monitored by the use of spot radiography and/or dye penetrant inspection. Welds **shall** be rejected if radiographs reveal:
*   Lack of fusion.
*   Cracks.
*   Other linear defects as outlined in the attached sketch - Page 14.
*   Gross porosity as outlined in the attached sketch - Page 14.

Accessible field butt welds **shall** be spot radiographed (25 percent). Rejection of accessible field butt welds may be cause for dye penetrant inspection in addition to the mandatory extra radiography.

**Dye Penetrant Inspection (PT)**
Welds **shall** be rejected if dye penetrant inspection reveals linear defects such as cracking or lack of fusion. Welds which cannot be radiographed (e.g., fillet welds) **shall** be dye penetrant inspected. If an arc strike occurs outside the weld area, the spot **will require** PT examined per Para. 10.3 (referencing this section).

**Defect Notification and Repair**
It **shall** be the responsibility of the fabricator to notify the purchaser immediately upon the discovery of any unacceptable defects. Repair of unacceptable defects **shall** be to the fabricator's account. All repairs and retests for non-conforming weldments **shall** be to the supplier's account.

All NDE activities **shall** conform to [[ASME_section_v_non_destructive_examination|ASME Section V]].

**Material and Consumable Verification**
Supplier **must** make available to the Inspector copies of chemical certification for batch of electrodes or wire coils used. If electrodes, wire, etc., cannot be identified to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' satisfaction, samples **shall** be removed for chemical analysis. Chemical analysis of unidentified electrodes/wire **shall** be to supplier's account.

**Production Weld Analysis**
When required by purchase order, a spot check analysis **shall** be performed on production welds. The weld(s) to be checked for spot check analysis **shall** be selected at random by the Inspector.

**Non-Conformance and Repair**
Where results of chemical analysis show that a specific weldment falls outside of the approved specification or code requirements, the supplier **shall** promptly advise Aker Kvaerner Chemetics in writing of his proposed course of action. Proposed course of action for non-conforming weldment **shall** include details of any additional testing necessary to fully define the extent of the non-conformance. All repairs and retests for non-conforming weldments **shall** be to the supplier's account.

It **shall** be the responsibility of the fabricator to notify the purchaser immediately upon the discovery of any unacceptable defects. Repair of unacceptable defects **shall** be to the fabricators' account.

**Non-Destructive Examination (NDE)**
Test Pieces **shall** be radiographed in accordance with CSA W47.2 to ascertain quality of the welds prior to commencement of shop or site welding. The quality of the production welds **shall** be monitored by the use of spot radiography and/or dye penetrant inspection.

**Rejection Criteria (Radiography)**
Welds **shall** be rejected if radiographs reveal:
*   Lack of fusion.
*   Cracks.
*   Other linear defects as outlined in the attached sketch - Page 14.
*   Gross porosity as outlined in the attached sketch - Page 14.

**Rejection Criteria (Dye Penetrant Inspection)**
Welds **shall** be rejected if dye penetrant inspection reveals linear defects such as cracking or lack of fusion.

**Field Welding NDE**
Accessible field butt welds **shall** be spot radiographed (25 percent). Rejection of accessible field butt welds may be cause for dye penetrant inspection in addition to the mandatory extra radiography. Welds which cannot be radiographed (e.g. fillet welds) **shall** be dye penetrant inspected.
