# Motor Installation Details

Motor installation details provide specific instructions and component requirements for connecting power and control cables, as well as grounding conductors, to motors and associated equipment. These details ensure proper and safe motor integration.

**Content of Motor Installation Details**
Motor installation details **shall** show:
*   Power cable connection to the motor junction box.
*   Control cable connection to the local control station located at the motor.
*   Grounding conductor connection to the motor.
*   Grounding conductor connection to the pump.
*   Grounding conductor connection to the common frame.
*   Cabling to other motor junction boxes for accessories such as space heaters, winding & bearing RTDs, and vibration and speed switches.
*   The various components needed to complete each type of installation.
