# Steam Piping Welding Specification Overview (4-SPC-0069)

Specification **4-SPC-0069** defines Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ requirements for welding and associated procedures for steam piping.

The specification applies to steam piping constructed from carbon steel in ASME P-No. 4 and 5 classifications. Specifically, it covers 1 ¼ Cr ½ Mo and 2 ½ Cr 1 Mo alloy steels. This specification is primarily intended for use in [[sulphuric_acid_storage_tank_specification_overview|sulphuric acid plants]].

For detailed requirements regarding specific welding processes, refer to [[steam_piping_welding_processes]]. For information on preheat, inter-pass, and post-weld heat treatment, consult [[steam_piping_weld_heat_treatment]]. Requirements for weld examination are outlined in [[steam_piping_weld_examination]].

The specification was written for steam piping in sulphuric acid plants.
