# Plant Constructability Review (0-PRC-6001)

This document, 0-PRC-6001, outlines the procedure for conducting Plant Constructability Reviews for the Project. These reviews are essential to ensure that the plant design can be efficiently and safely constructed, identifying potential issues early in the design phase.

Design review is conducted in accordance with this procedure.
