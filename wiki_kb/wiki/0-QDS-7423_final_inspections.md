# Thickener Final Inspections (0-QDS-7423)

This page outlines the various final inspections required for the Thickener equipment, as specified in Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7423. All listed final inspections **shall** be included in the Vendor's Inspection and Test Plan.

**Dimensional Inspection:**
*   Final Inspection (Dimensional) is required as FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package).
*   Dimensional inspection **shall** require Chemetics Witness.
*   Dimensional inspection **shall** require Chemetics Hold.

**Contour and Finish of Welds:**
*   Inspection of the Contour & finish of welds **shall** require Chemetics Witness.
*   Inspection of the Contour & finish of welds **shall** require Chemetics Hold.

**Surfaces Free From Defects:**
*   Inspection of Surfaces free from defects **shall** require Chemetics Witness.
*   Inspection of Surfaces free from defects **shall** require Chemetics Hold.

**Cleanliness:**
*   Inspection for Cleanliness **shall** require Chemetics Witness.
*   Inspection for Cleanliness **shall** require Chemetics Hold.

**Item Identification:**
*   Inspection of Item identification **shall** require Chemetics Witness.
*   Inspection of Item identification **shall** require Chemetics Hold.

**Review of QA Documentation:**
*   Review of QA Documentation **shall** require Chemetics Witness.
*   Review of QA Documentation **shall** require Chemetics Hold.

These final inspections ensure that the Thickener meets all specified quality, dimensional, and finish requirements before shipment. For general final inspection guidelines, refer to [[0-QDS-7411_final_inspections]].
