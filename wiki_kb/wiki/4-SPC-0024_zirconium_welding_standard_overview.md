# Zirconium Welding Standard Overview (4-SPC-0024)

This document, identified as **4-SPC-0024**, serves as a comprehensive standard defining the workmanship and quality requirements for Zirconium (Grade 60702) welded fabrications.

**Purpose and Application:**
The primary purpose of this Standard is to ensure high-quality fabrication of Zirconium (Grade 60702) components. It applies specifically when referenced on purchase orders and other related documents for fabrications purchased by or on behalf of the company. The scope of this Standard encompasses welding and other fabrication requirements for various items, including vessels, heat exchangers, tanks, piping, and miscellaneous welded components. These items are classified under the ASME P-61 & P-62 Material Classification.

**Governing Codes and Definitions:**
All referenced codes and standards within this document shall be interpreted as the latest edition ruling at the date of contract. Key definitions for clarity are:
*   **Agreement in Writing**: This term signifies a formal submission in writing by letter, explicitly excluding mere inclusion in approval drawings.
*   **Inspector**: Refers to the designated Quality Assurance Inspector or any other Authorized Representative.
