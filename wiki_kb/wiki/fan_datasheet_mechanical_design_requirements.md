# Fan Datasheet Mechanical Design Requirements

This page details the mandatory mechanical design requirements that **must** be specified for the [[fan_datasheet_administration|Fan Datasheet Template]] (4-DST-XXXX-R0). These specifications ensure the structural integrity, operational reliability, and compliance with relevant standards for the fan equipment.

**Mandatory Mechanical Design Requirements**
The following mechanical design parameters are required:
*   **Design Code**: The applicable design code for the equipment. Refer to [[design_standards_and_reference_materials]].
*   **Equipment Specification**: Specific technical requirements for the fan. Refer to [[equipment_design_and_specification_4_prc_0003]].
*   **General Equipment Requirements**: General mechanical design requirements applicable to all equipment. Refer to [[general_mechanical_design_requirements_0_spc_0010]].
*   **Fabrication Requirements**: Specific requirements for the manufacturing and assembly of the fan. Refer to [[equipment_fabrication_requirements]].
*   **Lining Requirements**: Details regarding any internal lining specifications. Refer to [[coatings_design_and_specification_7_prc_0002]].
*   **Welding Requirements**: Applicable welding procedures and standards. Refer to [[welding_requirements]].
*   **Site Conditions**: Environmental and operational conditions specific to the installation site. Refer to [[site_design_conditions_1_dst_0001]].
*   **Wind Design Code**: The code governing wind load calculations. Refer to [[wind_design_requirements]].
*   **Seismic Design Code**: The code governing seismic design considerations. Refer to [[seismic_design_requirements]].
*   **Electrical Area Classification**: Classification of the area for electrical equipment. Refer to [[hazardous_area_classification_requirements]].
*   **Equipment Location**: Specific location details for the fan within the plant. Refer to [[equipment_location_specification]].
*   **Connection Type**: Specifications for all process and utility connections. Refer to [[equipment_connection_types]].
*   **Casing Drain Requirements**: Details for the fan casing drainage system. Refer to [[fan_casing_drain_requirements]].
*   **Painting Requirements**: Specifications for external painting and protective coatings. Refer to [[coatings_design_and_specification_7_prc_0002]].
*   **Corrosion Allowance**: The specified corrosion allowance, measured in mm. Refer to [[corrosion_allowance_specification]].
*   **Total Noise Limit**: The maximum permissible noise level for the equipment. Refer to [[equipment_noise_limit]].
*   **Vibration Requirements**: Acceptable vibration limits and monitoring specifications. Refer to [[vibration_analysis]].
*   **Testing Requirements**: Details of required mechanical and performance tests. Refer to [[equipment_testing_requirements]].
*   **Speed Requirements**: Operational speed specifications for the fan. Refer to [[fan_speed_requirements]].
*   **Base Plate Requirements**: Specifications for the fan's base plate. Refer to [[equipment_base_plate_requirements]].
*   **Spacer Block Requirements**: Details for any required spacer blocks. Refer to [[equipment_spacer_block_requirements]].
*   **Arrangement Requirements**: General layout and arrangement specifications. Refer to [[equipment_arrangement_requirements]].
*   **Motor Position Requirements**: Specific positioning requirements for the fan motor. Refer to [[fan_motor_position_requirements]].
*   **Operating Curve Requirements**: Specifications for the fan's operating performance curve. Refer to [[fan_operating_curve_requirements]].
*   **Rotation/Discharge Requirements**: Direction of rotation and discharge orientation. Refer to [[fan_rotation_discharge_requirements]].
