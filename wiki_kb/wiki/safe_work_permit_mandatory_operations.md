# Mandatory Operations Requiring Safe Work Permits

Safe work permits are essential for controlling work in areas under commissioning or operation, ensuring that all personnel involved (operator, maintenance, and construction) are aware of and adhere to safe work practices. These permits are issued by the [[commissioning_hse_manager_safe_work_permit_responsibilities|Commissioning HSE Manager]] and are generally mandatory for a range of potentially hazardous operations.

**Mandatory Operations:**
A safe work permit **shall** be obtained for the following operations:
*   Line breaking operations.
*   Pump repairs operations.
*   Making tie-ins to existing equipment operations.
*   Working in hazardous area operations.
*   Work on equipment used for corrosives operations.
*   Hydro-blasting operations.
*   Flame or electric welding operations.
*   Electric power tools or motor operations.
*   Open flame devices operations.
*   Excavation operations.
*   Tank entry and/or lockout operations. For specific requirements, refer to [[0-prc-8502-r1_confined_space_entry]].
*   Tank entry into a hazardous area operations.

All personnel entering operating areas **must** obtain the proper Client authorization and appropriate work permits. This ensures adherence to both company and client safety standards, as detailed in the [[0-prc-8501_safe_work_permit_procedure_overview]].
