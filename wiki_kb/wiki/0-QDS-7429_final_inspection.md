# 0-QDS-7429 Final Inspection Requirements

This page outlines the final inspection requirements and associated surveillance activities for SS Plate, as specified in Quality Data Sheet [[0-QDS-7429_document_administration]].

**Final Inspection** is a mandatory item or activity. During the final inspection, Chemetics **requires Witness surveillance** for the following aspects:

*   **Surfaces & Edges**: Verification that surfaces and edges are free from rejectable defects.
*   **Cleanliness**: Assessment of the cleanliness of the SS Plate.
*   **Plate Identification**: Confirmation of correct plate identification markings.
*   **Dimensional Check**: Verification of dimensions against specified requirements.
*   **Review QA Documentation**: Review of the Quality Assurance Documentation to ensure completeness and compliance.
