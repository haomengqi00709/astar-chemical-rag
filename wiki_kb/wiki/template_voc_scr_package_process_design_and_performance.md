# Template VOC-SCR Package Process Design and Performance

This page outlines the key process design parameters and performance guarantees for the Template VOC-SCR Package, as specified in document 4-PDS-XXXX-R1. These parameters are fundamental for the design, operation, and evaluation of the VOC/SCR unit, ensuring it meets environmental and operational objectives.

These values serve as a baseline for initial calculations and vendor proposals. Actual project-specific values will be incorporated during detailed engineering. For further details on process calculations, refer to [[process_calculations]].

**Performance Guarantees:**

The VOC-SCR package is designed to meet stringent performance criteria:
*   **VOC Destruction Guarantee:** The unit **must** achieve a VOC destruction efficiency of 0.99 (99%).
*   **NOx Destruction Guarantee:** The unit **must** achieve a NOx destruction efficiency of 92% of the NOx present at the exit of the VOC catalyst bed.
*   **NH3 Slip:** Ammonia (NH3) slip **must** be less than 10 ppm.

These guarantees are critical for compliance with environmental regulations and ensuring the effectiveness of the pollution control system. Vendors **shall** demonstrate their ability to meet these guarantees in their proposals. Performance testing to verify these guarantees will be conducted as per [[performance_testing_1_prc_0008]].
