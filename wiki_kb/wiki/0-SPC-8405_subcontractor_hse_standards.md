# Subcontractor HSE Standards (0-SPC-8405)

Document 0-SPC-8405 outlines the minimum Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) standards that apply to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics construction subcontractors. Adherence to these standards **is required** for eligibility for any Aker Kvaerner Chemetics construction subcontracts.

Aker Kvaerner Chemetics is deeply committed to fostering a safe and healthy workplace for all its employees, subcontractors, and surrounding communities. This commitment extends to conducting all operations in an environmentally sound manner. Consequently, only subcontractors who demonstrate strong management leadership and possess systems that consistently result in excellent environmental, safety, and health performance will be considered for work.

Compliance monitoring of the subcontractor's safety program **will be** conducted against the applicable Health and Safety standards for the specific worksite.
