# Hot Work Safety Protocols (0-PRC-8503)

Adherence to stringent safety protocols is paramount for all hot work activities conducted under the [[0-prc-8503_hot_work_permit_procedure_overview|Hot Work Permit Procedure]]. These protocols are designed to mitigate risks associated with heat, sparks, and flames, ensuring the safety of personnel and integrity of equipment.

**Isolation and Venting:**
*   All lines leading to and from the equipment or line to be worked on **shall** be isolated, blinded off, or disconnected.
*   Refer to [[0-PRC-8505-A_line_and_flange_break_procedure]] for detailed Line and Flange Break procedures.
*   Before burning into or heating any hollow vessel or equipment which has been in service, the vessel or equipment **shall** be properly vented, unless the operation is performed under controlled conditions to avoid any over-pressuring.

**Gas Testing and Monitoring:**
*   The [[0-prc-8503_hot_work_permit_roles_and_responsibilities|Commissioning HSE Manager]] is responsible for testing and certifying in writing that no flammable gases, liquids or solids, oxygen deficiency or toxic materials are present in the location.
*   Gas testing **shall** be in accordance with approved methods and equipment.
*   Tests **should** indicate, and the Hot Work Permit certifies, that there are no flammable gases present.
*   Contractors **should** be encouraged to own, maintain, and operate their own oxygen and gas detection devices.
*   The [[0-prc-8503_hot_work_permit_roles_and_responsibilities|Commissioning HSE Manager]] is the final authority when conflicting test results are encountered.
*   Flammable gas and oxygen deficiency testing **will only** be undertaken using an approved explosive meter or oxygen meter.
*   The [[0-prc-8503_hot_work_permit_roles_and_responsibilities|fire watch]] **shall** periodically test the area with the explosion meter.
*   If the explosion meter reading indicates a percent concentration at the lower explosive limit, the hot work **shall** be stopped, and the potential problem corrected before hot work can restart.
*   If a potential for a spill in an adjacent area could create a hazard, a [[0-prc-8503_hot_work_permit_roles_and_responsibilities|roving monitor]] with an explosion meter **shall** be assigned to patrol the area and immediately stop hot work if flammable conditions are detected.

**Ventilation and Respiratory Protection:**
*   Adequate ventilation **shall** be provided when working in a closed container or enclosed space.
*   Adequate ventilation means a minimum of 12 changes of air per hour at the point or points where work is being performed.
*   If adequate ventilation is not possible, suitable [[respiratory_protection_requirements|respiratory protection]] **shall** be used.
*   Refer to [[0-PRC-8502-r1_confined_space_entry]] for the Confined Space Entry procedure, which includes requirements for permits such as [[DP-802.705-1_confined_space_entry_permit]].

**Fire Watch Requirements:**
*   A [[0-prc-8503_hot_work_permit_roles_and_responsibilities|competent fire watch]] **will** be assigned to the job when indicated on the Hot Work Permit.
*   The fire watch **will** have no other duties when carrying out their assignment.
*   A fully charged fire extinguisher of a type and size designated as suitable **shall** be provided where Hot Work is to be done, located in the immediate area for quick availability.
*   Fire extinguishers for fire watch **should not** be taken from safety stations and are to be used for emergencies only.
*   The fire watch **shall** continually survey the work area for combustible materials.
*   More than one fire watch may be required depending on potential impact on other areas, including floors below the hot work activity.
*   If the fire watch cannot extinguish a fire quickly and safely, the [[fire_emergency_response_akctc|emergency evacuation alarm]] **shall** be activated, and the fire watch **shall** always know the location of the nearest alarm box.

**Equipment Handling and Setup:**
*   Gas-burning and welding torches and hose **shall** be removed from all vessels or closed containers at the end of any shift, at lunch time, or at any other time when work is discontinued.
*   Gas-burning and welding hose **shall** be disconnected at the regulators at the end of any shift, at lunch time, or at any other time when work is discontinued.
*   Cylinders of any flammable material **shall never** be placed in a container where Hot Work is to be done.
*   When Hot Work is done in overhead locations, a method for catching hot metal, electrode stubs, etc., **must** be provided to prevent a fire and personnel hazard.
*   When electric welding is to be performed, the ground connection **shall** be attached to the work within the area covered by the permit.
*   The welding machine **shall** be in the area covered by the permit.
*   The ground and the stringer **should** be as close together as possible and on the same equipment.
*   Ground connections, except under approved "Hot Tap" conditions, **must not** be attached to "live" process lines or vessels.

**Pre-Work Procedures:**
*   A job-specific written procedure must be prepared and approved before any work starts that requires welding connections or additions to piping, vessels, and tanks containing flammable liquids, gas, or combustible materials under pressure.
*   All lines leading to and from the equipment or line to be worked on shall be isolated, blinded off, or disconnected.
*   Before burning into or heating any hollow vessel or equipment which has been in service, the vessel or equipment shall be properly vented (unless the operation is performed under controlled conditions so that any over pressuring will be avoided).

**Gas Testing and Ventilation:**
*   The Commissioning HSE Manager is responsible for testing and certifying in writing that no flammable gases, liquids or solids, oxygen deficiency or toxic materials are present in the location.
*   Gas testing shall be in accordance with approved methods and equipment.
*   Tests should indicate, and the Hot Work Permit certifies, that there are no flammable gases present.
*   Contractors should be encouraged to own, maintain, and operate their own oxygen and gas detection devices.
*   Flammable gas and oxygen deficiency testing will only be undertaken using an approved explosive meter or oxygen meter.
*   Adequate ventilation shall be provided when working in a closed container or enclosed space. Adequate ventilation means a minimum of 12 changes of air per hour at the point or points where work is being performed.
*   If adequate ventilation is not possible, suitable respiratory protection shall be used.

**Equipment Handling:**
*   Gas-burning and welding torches and hose shall be removed from all vessels or closed containers at the end of any shift, at lunch time, or at any other time when work is discontinued.
*   Gas-burning and welding hose shall be disconnected at the regulators at the end of any shift, at lunch time, or at any other time when work is discontinued.
*   Cylinders of any flammable material shall never be placed in a container where Hot Work is to be done.
*   When Hot Work is done in overhead locations, a method for catching hot metal, electrode stubs, etc., must be provided to prevent a fire and personnel hazard.

**Electric Welding Specifics:**
*   When electric welding is to be performed, the ground connection shall be attached to the work within the area covered by the permit.
*   The welding machine shall be in the area covered by the permit.
*   The ground and the stringer should be as close together as possible and on the same equipment.
*   Ground connections, except under approved "Hot Tap" conditions, must not be attached to "live" process lines or vessels.

**Fire Watch Responsibilities:**
*   A competent fire watch will be assigned to the job when indicated on the Hot Work Permit.
*   The fire watch shall be familiar with the location and nature of the hot work.
*   The fire watch will have no other duties when carrying out his assignment.
*   If the fire watch leaves, the hot work will be disconnected (welder turned off, electric supply turned off) until the fire watch returns.
*   The fire watch shall remain in constant communication range with person(s) doing work.
*   A fully charged fire extinguisher of a type and size designated as suitable shall be provided where Hot Work is to be done. The fire watch shall be provided with proper fire extinguisher(s), located in the immediate area for quick availability. Fire extinguishers for fire watch should not be taken from safety stations and are to be used for emergencies only.
*   The fire watch shall be provided with an explosion meter on jobs that could potentially involve flammable gases or vapours. The area shall be periodically tested with the explosion meter.
*   If the explosion meter reading indicates a percent concentration at the lower explosive limit, the hot work shall be stopped, the potential problem corrected, and hot work can only be restarted after the potential problem is corrected.
*   The fire watch shall continually survey the work area for combustible materials.
*   More than one fire watch may be required depending on potential impact on other areas, including floors below the hot work activity.
*   If a potential for a spill in an adjacent area could create a hazard, a roving monitor with an explosion meter shall be assigned. The roving monitor shall patrol the area and immediately stop the hot work if flammable conditions are detected.
*   If the fire watch cannot extinguish a fire quickly and safely, the emergency evacuation alarm shall be activated. The fire watch shall always know the location of the nearest alarm box.
