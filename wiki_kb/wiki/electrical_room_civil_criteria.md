# Electrical Room Civil Criteria Drawings

Electrical Room Civil Criteria drawings provide essential information for civil and structural design related to electrical rooms, including equipment arrangement, weights, and requirements for floor and wall openings.

**Content of Electrical Room Civil Criteria Drawings**
Electrical room civil criteria drawings **shall** include:
*   Electrical equipment arrangement in an electrical room.
*   The weights of each piece of electrical equipment.
*   The requirement for floor and/or wall openings for cables, cable trays, etc.
*   The sizes of floor and/or wall openings.
*   Floor and wall openings, which **will** be dimensioned and located.
*   The locations for embedded steel in the floors for anchoring the equipment.
*   The descriptions for embedded steel in the floors for anchoring the equipment.
*   The area for additional steel work for cable tray and conduit supports above the equipment.
*   The approximate weights of the cable tray and conduits to be supported from the overhead steelwork.
