# Engineering Subcontractor Document Status Definitions (0-SPC-0002)

The 0-SPC-0002 specification defines a series of mandatory document statuses that Engineering Subcontractors **must** apply to all deliverables. These statuses indicate the purpose and permissible use of the information contained within each document.

**Defined Document Statuses:**
*   **Issued for Owner Review:** This status signifies that the document has been published specifically for the Owner's review and comment.
*   **Issued for Owner Approval:** This status indicates that the document has been published specifically for the Owner's formal approval.
*   **Issued for Quotation:** This status means the document has been published specifically for the purpose of soliciting quotations.
*   **Approved for Design:** Documents with this status contain information that may be used for further design activities.
*   **Approved for Fabrication:** This status indicates that the information provided within the document may be used to manufacture the items shown or described.
*   **Approved for Construction:** Documents bearing this status contain information that may be used to construct the items shown or described.
*   **Approved Instruction:** This status means the information provided may be used by the Owner for the operation or maintenance of the items shown or described.
*   **As Built:** This status designates the document as a definitive record of the plant's condition as it was constructed.

**Status Qualifiers and Approval:**
Limitations on the use of a document may be specified by adding a qualifier to its status. An example of such a qualifier is “Approved for Design (Layout only)”.

The term “Approved,” when applied to a document status, specifically means that the document has been approved by the Subcontractor for the stated use. This approval by the Subcontractor **must** be in accordance with their established [[subcontractor_quality_plan]].
