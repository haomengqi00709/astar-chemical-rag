# Process Design Criteria (1-PRC-0001)

This document, 1-PRC-0001, outlines the specific design criteria for all process engineering aspects of the Project. It establishes the mandatory requirements and standards for process design, including mass and energy balances, process flow, and equipment sizing.

Discipline Leads utilize this document to set out the precise process requirements for the Project.
