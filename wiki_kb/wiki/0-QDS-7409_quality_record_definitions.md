# Mist Eliminator Quality Record Definitions

This page defines the terminology and specific quality records required for Mist Eliminator Mesh Pad Type equipment, as outlined in Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7409.

**Quality Record Terminology:**
The following abbreviations are used to denote the requirement for quality records:
*   **AP (As Prepared):** Indicates that the quality record is required "As Prepared" by the vendor.
*   **FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package):** Indicates that the quality record **must** be included as part of the final Quality Assurance Documentation Package submitted by the vendor.

**Specific Quality Records:**
For the Mist Eliminator Mesh Pad Type equipment, the following specific quality records are required:
*   **Certificate of Compliance:** This document is a Quality Record required FP, meaning it **must** be included in the Quality Assurance Documentation Package.
*   **Packing List:** This document is also a Quality Record required FP, meaning it **must** be included in the Quality Assurance Documentation Package.

These definitions and requirements ensure consistent documentation and traceability for all quality-related aspects of the Mist Eliminator. For other equipment, similar definitions and requirements can be found in pages such as [[0-QDS-7404-2_quality_record_definitions]] and [[0-qds-7301_quality_records_and_documentation]].
