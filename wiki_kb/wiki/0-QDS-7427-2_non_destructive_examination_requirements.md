# Stainless Steel Tubing Tier 2 Non-Destructive Examination Requirements

Non-Destructive Examination (NDE) for Stainless Steel Tubing Tier 2 **shall** be performed to verify material integrity without causing damage. All NDE activities **shall** be conducted in accordance with [ASTM or approved equivalent code].

**Dye Penetrant Testing:**
*   **Tube Ends Testing:**
    *   Dye Penetrant testing on tube ends is required as FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package).
    *   Chemetics **shall** Witness and Hold Dye Penetrant - Tube ends testing.
*   **Cut Lengths Testing:**
    *   Dye Penetrant testing on cut lengths is required as FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package).
    *   Chemetics **shall** Witness and Hold Dye Penetrant - Cut Lengths testing.

All NDE results and reports **shall** be submitted as [[0-QDS-7427-2_quality_record_definitions|Quality Records]]. These requirements are in line with general [[0-QDS-7_non_destructive_examination_requirements]] for quality assurance.
