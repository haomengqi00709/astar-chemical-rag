# ASME SFA-5.4 Specification for Covered Corrosion Resisting Chromium and Chromium-Nickel Steel Covered Welding Electrodes

ASME SFA-5.4 is a specification that establishes the requirements for covered corrosion-resisting chromium and chromium-nickel steel welding electrodes. This standard covers classification, chemical composition, mechanical properties, and other essential characteristics of these electrodes.

This specification is referenced in [[4-SPC-0014_referenced_codes_and_definitions]] and specifically in [[4-SPC-0014_welding_processes_and_parameters]] for the selection and use of Shielded Metal Arc Welding (SMAW) electrodes for Columbium Stabilized High Nickel Austenitic stainless steel fabrications. SMAW electrodes used for these applications shall satisfy the requirements of ASME SFA-5.4.

For detailed information on electrode classification and requirements, refer to the latest edition of ASME SFA-5.4.
