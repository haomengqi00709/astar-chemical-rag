# AWS A4.2 - Standard Procedures for Calibrating Magnetic Instruments to Measure the Delta Ferrite Content of Austenitic Stainless Steel Weld Metal

AWS A4.2 is a standard that outlines the procedures for calibrating magnetic instruments used to measure the delta ferrite content in austenitic stainless steel weld metal.

This standard is referenced in the [[5-SPC-0121_austenitic_stainless_steel_piping_specification_overview|Austenitic Stainless Steel Piping Fabrication Specification (5-SPC-0121)]] as a key document for ensuring the quality and metallurgical integrity of welds in austenitic stainless steel piping. Accurate measurement of delta ferrite content is crucial for predicting and controlling the mechanical properties and corrosion resistance of stainless steel welds.
