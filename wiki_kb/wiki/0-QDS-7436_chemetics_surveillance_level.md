# Chemetics Surveillance Level for Stainless Steel Tank (0-QDS-7436)

For the Stainless Steel Tank project, Chemetics' surveillance activities **shall** be conducted to Level 2.0.

This surveillance level indicates the degree of oversight and inspection Chemetics will apply to the Vendor's manufacturing and quality control processes. The specific surveillance points and requirements are detailed throughout this [[0-QDS-7436_stainless_steel_tank_quality_data_sheet_administration|Quality Data Sheet (0-QDS-7436)]].

For each required inspection or documentation item, Chemetics' surveillance may include "Witness" or "Hold" points, as specified in the relevant sections of this document.
