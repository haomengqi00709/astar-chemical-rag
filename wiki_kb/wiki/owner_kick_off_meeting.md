# Owner Kick-off Meeting

A formal kick-off meeting with the Owner is a crucial step in establishing clear project parameters and fostering effective collaboration.

**Purpose of the Meeting:**
The Project Manager **must** arrange a kick-off meeting with the Owner. The primary objectives of this meeting are:
*   To ensure that the respective responsibilities of the Project Team and the Owner are clearly understood.
*   To ensure that the scope delineation between all parties is thoroughly understood and agreed upon.

**Coordination Procedure:**
Both parties, the Project Team and the Owner, **must** follow the [[project_coordination_0_prc_2004|0-PRC-2004: Project Co-ordination Procedure]] to ensure consistent and effective communication throughout the project lifecycle.
