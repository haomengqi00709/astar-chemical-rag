# Packing and Shipping Requirements for MV Switchgear & DC Control Power Supply

The packing and shipping of the Medium Voltage Switchgear & DC Control Power Supply **must** adhere to specific requirements to ensure the safe delivery of the equipment. Chemetics surveillance is required for these activities.

**Requirements:**
*   **Packaging:**
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance:** [[0-QDS-7901_chemetics_surveillance_level|Witness]], [[0-QDS-7901_chemetics_surveillance_level|Hold]]
*   **Packing List:**
    *   **Quality Record:** [[0-QDS-7901_quality_record_definitions|AP]]
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance:** [[0-QDS-7901_chemetics_surveillance_level|Witness]]

These measures ensure that the equipment is adequately protected during transit and that all necessary documentation for shipment is prepared and verified.
