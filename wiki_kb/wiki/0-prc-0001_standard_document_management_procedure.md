# Standard Document Management Procedure (0-PRC-0001)

The Standard Document Management Procedure (0-PRC-0001) governs the creation, approval, and use of standard documents across projects. This procedure ensures consistency, quality, and efficiency in documentation practices.

**Application to Specifications and Drawings**
It is anticipated that standards will exist for all [[general_equipment_requirements_specification|Specifications]] as well as for all [[equipment_proprietary_detail_drawing_prp|Detail Drawings and Proprietary Drawings]]. For information regarding the approval and proper use of these standards, project personnel **must** refer to 0-PRC-0001.
