# Language and Directive Guidelines

Adherence to specific language and directive guidelines is crucial for ensuring clarity, precision, and consistency across all Aker Kvaerner Chemetics textual documents. These guidelines aim to eliminate ambiguity and facilitate straightforward communication, making documents easy to understand and act upon.

**Expression of Imperatives:**
*   Within procedures and specifications, the imperative *must* be expressed using "will" instead of "shall." While "will" and "shall" are often considered interchangeable in legal contexts, the consistent use of "will" is mandated for all directives within company documentation to maintain a uniform tone and style.

**Voice and Style:**
*   Directives *must* be expressed in the active voice as much as possible. This promotes clarity by clearly identifying the actor performing the action, making instructions easier to understand and follow. For example, "The engineer will review the drawing" is preferred over "The drawing shall be reviewed by the engineer."
*   The use of legalistic or archaic language *must* be avoided. Documents *must* be written in clear, contemporary language accessible to the intended audience, preventing misinterpretation and improving readability for all users, regardless of their legal background.

**Avoiding Repetition:**
*   Directives *must not* be repeated within a single document or across cross-referenced documents. Repetition significantly increases the opportunity for errors, inconsistencies, or ambiguity, especially during revisions. Instead, documents *must* rely on clear [[document_referencing_guidelines|referencing]] to avoid redundancy and ensure a single source of truth for each directive. This principle helps to streamline document maintenance and reduce potential conflicts.

**Consistent Terminology for External Parties:**
*   When issuing directives to external parties, the terms "[[0_prc_0004_document_style_and_format#Key Definitions|Supplier]]" or "[[0_prc_0004_document_style_and_format#Key Definitions|Subcontractor]]" *must* be used consistently. This applies regardless of whether the directive is intended for a primary supplier/subcontractor or any sub-supplier/sub-subcontractor in the chain. This ensures a unified approach to addressing all external entities involved in the provision of goods or services, preventing confusion about the intended recipient of a directive.

**Company and Role Naming Conventions**
"Aker Kvaerner Chemetics" will be used for the company's full name. After its first use, "AKC" can be used.
"Suppliers" refers to suppliers of goods.
"Subcontractor" refers to suppliers of services or of goods and services.
"Owner" refers to the client including his representatives.
"Supplier" or "Subcontractor" will be used for all directives, regardless of sub-supplier/sub-subcontractor.

**Directive Language**
The imperative will be expressed as "will" instead of "shall". In law, "will" and "shall" are interchangeable.
Directives will be expressed in the active voice as much as possible.
Legalistic or archaic language will not be used.
Directives will not be repeated within a document or in cross-referenced documents. Repetition provides the greatest opportunity for errors or ambiguity in a document.
