# Acid Plant Chemically Resistant Membrane Specifications

The chemically resistant membrane system for strong acid equipment in sulphuric acid plants, as defined in [[4-spc-0012_acid_resistant_linings_specification_overview]], consists of a multi-layered application designed for maximum protection.

**Pecora/PTFE Membrane System**
The primary acid-resistant membrane on all strong acid equipment **will** be a nominal 3mm layer of Pecora A-103 chemical resistant mastic. This mastic layer **will** then be overlaid with either two layers of 0.125mm or one layer of 0.25mm sodium etched virgin PTFE sheet.

**Dished Tower and Tank Bottoms**
For dished tower and tank bottoms, including associated acid outlet and drain nozzles, the Pecora/PTFE membrane **will** employ two layers of 0.125mm thick PTFE sheet. These sheets **must** be overlapped by 50%, resulting in an effective thickness of 0.25mm at the overlaps. Overlap joints in the lower 0.125mm PTFE layer **must** be a minimum of 150mm wide and **must** be firmly adhered with mastic. Pecora mastic **must** be applied to the entire sandblasted surface of the dished bottoms.

**Minimizing and Staggering Seams**
The number of PTFE sheet seams **must** be minimized, and all seams **must** be staggered to enhance membrane integrity.

**Drying Tower Gas Inlets and Dished Bottom Areas**
In dished bottom areas and drying tower gas inlets, the membrane **will** primarily consist of Pecora mastic. PTFE sheet overlap joints in these areas **will** also be a minimum of 150mm wide. The PTFE sheets **will** be arranged to provide a hinging effect, and the number of overlap joints **must** be kept to the absolute minimum. Lapped edges of the PTFE sheets in these specific areas **must** be firmly stuck down with A-103 mastic.

**Chlorobutyl Rubber Membrane for Drying Tower Gas Inlets**
Drying tower gas inlet membranes **will** additionally feature a 6mm thick chlorobutyl rubber layer, applied in one or two layers. This rubber membrane **must** cover the nozzle flange face and extend to the edge of the nozzle-to-shell junction. The chlorobutyl rubber membrane **must** be applied before the Pecora mastic/PTFE vessel membrane. The Pecora/PTFE membrane **must** then cover the chlorobutyl rubber in the mouth of the inlet nozzle for a minimum length of 450mm.

**Documentation Requirements**
Technical details for the proposed rubber lining system **must** be included in the vendor's proposal. Descriptions and areas of application for the various types of membranes employed in the equipment **must** be clearly defined on the drawings.

For related information on lining responsibilities, see [[acid_plant_lining_supplier_contractor_responsibilities]].

**Pecora/PTFE Membrane System - Application Details**
For dished tower and tank bottoms, and associated acid outlet and drain nozzles, PTFE sheets are to be overlapped by 50%, resulting in an effective thickness of 0.25mm in these overlapped areas. Overlap joints in the lower layer of 0.125mm PTFE must be a minimum of 150mm wide and firmly stuck down with mastic. Pecora mastic must be applied to the entire sandblasted surface of the dished bottoms.

To ensure optimal performance, the number of PTFE sheet seams must be minimized and all seams must be staggered.

**Chlorobutyl Rubber Membrane for Drying Tower Gas Inlets**
The membrane for drying tower gas inlets will consist of 6mm thick chlorobutyl rubber, applied in either one or two layers. This rubber membrane must cover the nozzle flange face and extend to the edge of the nozzle to shell junction. It is critical that the chlorobutyl rubber membrane is applied before the Pecora mastic/PTFE vessel membrane.
