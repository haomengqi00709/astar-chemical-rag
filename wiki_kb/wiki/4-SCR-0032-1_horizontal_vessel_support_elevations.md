# Horizontal Vessel Support Elevations (4-SCR-0032-1)

This page outlines the critical elevation specifications for the installation of horizontal vessels on concrete and grout supports, as detailed in document 4-SCR-0032-1. Precise elevation data is essential for ensuring proper alignment, drainage, and seamless integration with surrounding plant infrastructure and piping systems. These measurements define the vertical positioning of the vessel and its supporting elements relative to established benchmarks, playing a key role in both the civil and mechanical aspects of the project.

**Elevation Specifications:**
All specified elevations are provided in millimeters (mm) and are fundamental for construction and installation accuracy:
*   **Underside of Equipment Elevation:** This specifies the elevation of the lowest point of the vessel's structural components, providing a baseline for vertical positioning.
*   **Top of Rough Concrete Elev. (Fixed):** This indicates the elevation of the top surface of the rough concrete foundation at the fixed support end of the vessel. The fixed end typically resists longitudinal loads.
*   **Top of Rough Concrete Elev. (Sliding):** This indicates the elevation of the top surface of the rough concrete foundation at the sliding support end of the vessel. Sliding supports are designed to accommodate thermal expansion and contraction of the vessel, preventing undue stress on the structure.
*   **Nominal Floor or Grade Elevation:** This provides a general reference elevation for the surrounding floor or ground level, which is critical for overall plant layout, access planning, and ensuring compliance with site grading requirements.

These elevation details are crucial for the [[civil_works_design_criteria_2_prc_0001|civil works design criteria]] and are directly referenced in procedures for [[equipment_grouting_requirements]] to ensure correct installation and alignment. Accurate adherence to these elevations is vital for the operational efficiency, maintainability, and safety of the horizontal vessel.
