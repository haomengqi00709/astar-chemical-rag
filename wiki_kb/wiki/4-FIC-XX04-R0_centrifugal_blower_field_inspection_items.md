# Centrifugal Blower Field Inspection Items (4-FIC-XX04-R0)

This page details the 25 mandatory inspection items for [[centrifugal_blower]] equipment, as outlined in checklist 4-FIC-XX04-R0. Adherence to these requirements is crucial for ensuring the safe and efficient operation of the blower.

### General Installation and Positioning

*   **Nameplate Installation:** The correct nameplate **must** be installed.
*   **Blower Position:** Blower position **must** be checked for elevation, orientation, and levelness.
*   **Foundation and Anchor Bolts:** [[foundation_anchor_bolts|Foundation]] / Anchor bolts **must** be secure and tightened. Refer to [[civil_works_design_criteria_2_prc_0001]] for general civil design criteria.
*   **Grouting:** Grouting **must** be completed.
*   **Piping Alignment and Support:** Suction/discharge piping **must** be properly aligned and supported, ensuring no stress on blower nozzles. Consult [[piping_design_and_specification_5_prc_0003]] for piping design standards.
*   **Inlet Guide Vanes (IGV):** Inlet guide Vanes (IGV) **must** be installed and moving without interference.

### Mechanical Systems and Components

*   **Auxiliary Systems:** Auxiliary systems (e.g., Condensate seals, seal flush, air purge, water cooling, [[lube_oil_system_components|lube oil]]) **must** be complete.
*   **Blower Casing Cleanliness:** Blower casing **must** be free of foreign material.
*   **Mechanical Seal / Packing:** [[mechanical_seal_packing_installation|Mechanical seal / packing]] **must** be installed.
*   **Lube Oil System Flushing:** The [[lube_oil_system_components|lube oil system]] **must** be flushed.
*   **Lube Oil System Level:** The [[lube_oil_system_components|lube oil system]] **must** be filled to the correct level.
*   **Bearings Flushing and Filling:** Bearings **must** be flushed and filled to the correct level.
*   **Free Rotation Check:** Free rotation of the blower **must** be checked by hand.
*   **Cold Alignment:** [[coupling_installation|Cold alignment]] **must** be completed.
*   **Coupling Installation and Lubrication:** [[coupling_installation|Coupling]] **must** be installed and lubricated.
*   **Coupling Guard:** [[coupling_installation|Coupling guard]] **must** be installed and secure.
*   **Jacking Screws:** Jacking screws **must** be removed.

### Electrical and Instrumentation

*   **Local Control Panel:** Local control panel **must** be installed. Refer to [[control_equipment_cabinet_layouts]] for layout standards.
*   **Vibration Monitoring:** [[vibration_analysis|Vibration monitoring]] **must** be installed.
*   **Accessories:** Accessories (e.g., [[instrument_installation_specification|instruments]], drain valves) **must** be installed.
*   **Grounding:** [[grounding_general_arrangement_drawings|Grounding]] **must** be completed.
*   **Motor Rotation Verification:** [[motor_rotation_verification|Motor rotation]] **must** be verified by the electrical contractor, with an attached report. Refer to [[electrical_installation_specification]] for electrical installation standards.

### Coatings and Insulation

*   **External Painting:** [[external_painting|External painting]] **must** be completed with no visible defects. General coating and lining inspection requirements are detailed in [[0-QDS-7414_coating_and_lining_inspection]].
*   **Thermal Insulation:** [[thermal_insulation|Thermal insulation]] **must** be complete. Refer to [[insulation_design_and_specification_6_prc_0002]] for design specifications.
*   **Acoustic Insulation:** [[acoustic_insulation|Acoustic insulation]] **must** be complete.
