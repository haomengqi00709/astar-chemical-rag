# V-Belt Installation and Tensioning

This page outlines the mandatory requirements for the correct size, installation, and tensioning of [[v_belt_installation_and_tensioning|v-belts]] for belt-driven [[rotating_equipment_mechanical_checkout|rotating]] and [[conveying_equipment_mechanical_checkout|conveying equipment]]. V-belt tightness **must** be checked using the installation instruction method, and the v-belt guard **must** be replaced and be tight.
