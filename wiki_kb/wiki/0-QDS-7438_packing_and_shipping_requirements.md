# Field Erected SS Tank Shop Component Packing and Shipping Requirements

This page outlines the packing and shipping requirements for the Field Erected Stainless Steel Tank Shop Component (0-QDS-7438), including necessary documentation and Chemetics surveillance.

**Packaging:**
*   Packaging activities require Chemetics surveillance to be "Witness", as detailed in [[0-QDS-7438_chemetics_surveillance_level]]. This ensures that components are adequately protected for transit.

**Packing List:**
*   The Packing List **must** be designated as a [[quality_record_definitions|Quality Record AP]] (As Prepared).
*   The Packing List requires Chemetics surveillance to be "Witness", as detailed in [[0-QDS-7438_chemetics_surveillance_level]]. This ensures accuracy and completeness of shipping documentation.

These requirements are in place to ensure the safe and documented delivery of the tank components to the project site.
