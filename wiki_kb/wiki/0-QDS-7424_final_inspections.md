# Final Inspections for Polypropylene Tank (0-QDS-7424)

This page specifies the mandatory final inspection requirements for the Polypropylene Tank project, as detailed in Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7424. These inspections are performed to ensure the completed tank meets all specified quality, dimensional, and aesthetic standards prior to shipment or handover.

**Mandatory Final Inspection Activities:**
*   **Dimensional Inspection:** A dimensional inspection **must** be conducted. The results **shall** be recorded as a Quality Record (FP), requiring inclusion in the final [[0-QDS-7424_quality_record_definitions|Quality Assurance Documentation Package]]. Chemetics Witness surveillance is also required for this inspection.
*   **Surfaces Free From Defects Inspection:** An inspection to ensure surfaces are free from defects **must** be performed. The findings **shall** be documented as a Quality Record (FP), to be included in the final [[0-QDS-7424_quality_record_definitions|Quality Assurance Documentation Package]]. Chemetics Witness surveillance is required for this inspection.
*   **Cleanliness Inspection:** A cleanliness inspection **must** be carried out. The documentation of this inspection **shall** be a Quality Record (FP), included in the final [[0-QDS-7424_quality_record_definitions|Quality Assurance Documentation Package]]. Chemetics Witness surveillance is required for this inspection.
*   **Review QA Documentation:** A review of the Quality Assurance Documentation **must** be performed, with Chemetics Witness surveillance required for this activity.

Final Inspection is a critical activity listed in the Quality Data Sheet to ensure the product's compliance with all specifications. The Chemetics Witness surveillance level for this project is detailed in [[0-QDS-7424_chemetics_surveillance_level]]. For general administrative details of this QDS, refer to [[0-QDS-7424_polypropylene_tank_quality_data_sheet_administration]].

**Specific Final Inspection Requirements:**
*   **Dimensional Inspection:** Requires a Quality Record (FP - Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package) and Chemetics Witness surveillance.
*   **Surfaces Free From Defects Inspection:** Requires a Quality Record (FP - Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package) and Chemetics Witness surveillance.
*   **Cleanliness Inspection:** Requires a Quality Record (FP - Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package) and Chemetics Witness surveillance.
