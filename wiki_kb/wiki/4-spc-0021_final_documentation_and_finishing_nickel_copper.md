# Final Documentation and Finishing for Nickel/Copper Fabrications (4-SPC-0021)

The final stages of fabrication for nickel/copper (UNS N04400 / Monel 400) items under **4-SPC-0021** involve specific requirements for painting and comprehensive documentation.

**Painting Restrictions:**
*   No paint **shall** be applied either externally or internally to any item or component manufactured to this Standard unless agreed to in writing by [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_company_details|Aker Kvaerner Chemetics]].

**Required Documentation and Assembly:**
*   The fabricator **shall** furnish a report containing all data pertaining to each vessel or item.
*   Prior to [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_company_details|Aker Kvaerner Chemetics']] final inspection of each unit, the fabricator **shall** have prepared in finished form three sets of records, excluding final test data.
*   Required records **include**:
    *   Material mill test reports
    *   Nameplate rubbing
    *   Hydrostatic test report
    *   A copy of all inspection and test reports
*   These reports, together with final test data, **shall** be assembled into three complete sets for each item.
*   In no case **shall** a documentation package pertain to more than one vessel or item number.
*   The Inspector will collect the appropriate number of documentation packages for each item tested at the time of witnessing final tests and certifying the correctness of forms.
