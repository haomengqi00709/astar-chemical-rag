# Conveyors & Mills Vendor Quality Assurance Requirements

This page outlines the mandatory quality assurance requirements for vendors supplying Conveyors & Mills, as specified in Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7426.

**General Requirements**
The Vendor's Quality Assurance **shall** comply with this Quality Data Sheet and with [[0-SPC-7002_vendor_quality_assurance]].

**Quality Plan**
The Vendor **shall** prepare a Quality Plan in accordance with [[0-SPC-7002_vendor_quality_assurance]]. If a Quality Plan is not required for a specific project, the related cells in the Quality Data Sheet **shall** be deleted.

**Chemetics Surveillance Level**
Surveillance by Chemetics will be to Level 0.0 and as set out in this Quality Data Sheet. For more details on Chemetics surveillance levels, refer to [[0-QDS-7411_chemetics_surveillance_level]].

**Scope of Vendor Quality Assurance**
The vendor's quality assurance scope for Conveyors & Mills includes, but is not limited to, the following activities:
*   [[material_verification_and_certifications|Material Verification]]
*   [[0-QDS-7426_non_destructive_examination_requirements|Non-Destructive Testing]]
*   Dye Penetrant testing
*   [[0-QDS-7426_coating_and_lining_inspection|Internal Coating / Lining Inspection]]
*   [[0-QDS-7426_equipment_testing|Equipment Testing]]
*   Balancing
*   Run Test
*   Performance Test
*   Noise Data collection
*   Motor Test

For a comprehensive understanding of vendor quality assurance, refer to [[vendor_quality_assurance]].

**General Requirements**
*   The Vendor shall prepare a Quality Plan in accordance with [[0-SPC-7002_vendor_quality_assurance|0-SPC-7002: Vendor Quality Assurance]].
*   Surveillance by Chemetics will be to Level 0.0.
*   Surveillance by Chemetics will be as set out in this Quality Data Sheet.
*   **Note:** If a Quality Plan is not required, the related cells shall be deleted.
