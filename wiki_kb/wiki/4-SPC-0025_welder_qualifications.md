# Welder Qualifications and Identification for High Silicon Austenitic Stainless Steel (4-SPC-0025)

**Welder Experience and Certification**
All welders employed on Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' work **shall** be experienced in welding high silicon austenitic steel. Furthermore, all welders **shall** be qualified in accordance with each specific welding procedure they are to use and in compliance with [[ASME_section_ix_welding_and_brazing_qualifications]].

The fabricator **shall**, upon request, supply Aker Kvaerner Chemetics with objective evidence of current welder qualifications.

**Welder Identification**
All qualified welders **shall** be allocated a unique identification symbol. This welder identification symbol **shall** be etched on the parent material adjacent to their work. An alternative method of welder identification that has been successfully used in the past will also be considered, subject to approval.
