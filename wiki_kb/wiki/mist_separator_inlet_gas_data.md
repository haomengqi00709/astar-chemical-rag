# Mist Separator Inlet Gas Data

This page details the characteristics of the gas stream entering the mist separator, as specified in the Template Mist Separator Datasheet (Document ID [[mist_separator_datasheet_administration]]). Accurate and comprehensive inlet gas data is fundamental for the correct sizing, selection, and design of the mist separator to ensure optimal performance.

**Inlet Gas Data Parameters**
The following parameters are provided as data fields to characterize the inlet gas stream:

*   **Description:** A data field for a brief description or identifier of the inlet gas stream.
*   **Composition:**
    *   Measured in **Vol%**: Specifies the volumetric percentage of each component in the gas mixture.
    *   Measured in **% w/w**: Specifies the weight percentage of each component in the gas mixture.
*   **Flow Rate:**
    *   Measured in **Am3/h**: Actual cubic meters per hour, representing the volumetric flow rate at operating conditions.
    *   Measured in **Nm3/h**: Normal cubic meters per hour, representing the volumetric flow rate at standard conditions (typically 0°C and 1 atm).
*   **Temperature:**
    *   Measured in **ºC**: Temperature in degrees Celsius.
    *   Measured in **oC**: (Duplicate entry, but preserved as given) Temperature in degrees Celsius.
*   **Total Flowrate:** Measured in **kg/h**: The total mass flow rate of the gas stream in kilograms per hour.
*   **Pressure:** Measured in **kPa(g)**: The gauge pressure of the inlet gas stream in kilopascals.
*   **Density:** Measured in **kg/m3**: The density of the gas mixture in kilograms per cubic meter at operating conditions.
*   **Molecular Weight:** Measured in **kg/kmol**: The average molecular weight of the gas mixture in kilograms per kilomole.
*   **Viscosity:** Measured in **cP**: The dynamic viscosity of the gas mixture in centipoise.

**Importance of Inlet Gas Data**
The detailed specification of these parameters allows vendors to accurately model the gas flow dynamics, predict pressure drops, and select the most appropriate mist separator technology and design to handle the specific process conditions. Variations in gas composition, temperature, pressure, and flow rate directly impact the efficiency and operational stability of the mist elimination process.

This data is closely related to the [[mist_separator_general_equipment_and_process_data]] and directly influences the [[mist_separator_performance_requirements]].

**Inlet Gas Data Fields and Units**
The following parameters characterize the gas stream entering the mist separator:
*   **Description:** A general descriptive field for the inlet gas.
*   **Composition:** Measured in Vol% or % w/w.
*   **Flow Rate:** Measured in Am3/h (Actual cubic meters per hour) or Nm3/h (Normal cubic meters per hour).
*   **Temperature:** Measured in ºC or oC.
*   **Total Flowrate:** Measured in kg/h.
*   **Pressure:** Measured in kPa(g) (kilopascals gauge).
*   **Density:** Measured in kg/m3.
*   **Molecular Weight:** Measured in kg/kmol.
*   **Viscosity:** Measured in cP (centipoise).
