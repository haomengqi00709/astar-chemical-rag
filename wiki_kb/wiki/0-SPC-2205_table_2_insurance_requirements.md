# Engineering Subcontract Insurance Requirements Table (0-SPC-2205_table_2)

This page describes the content of `0-SPC-2205_table_2`, which is referenced within document 0-SPC-2205 as the "Insurance Table".

`0-SPC-2205_table_2` specifies the mandatory insurances that the Subcontractor **must** provide as part of the engineering subcontract. This table details the types of insurance coverage required, including policy limits, deductibles, and any specific endorsements or conditions. These requirements are essential for managing risks associated with the services provided by the Subcontractor.

For further details on general insurance requirements, refer to [[0-SPC-2205_insurance_and_liability]].
