# Subcontractor Survey Form (0-REP-8405-R0)

The Subcontractor Survey Form, identified as 0-REP-8405-R0, is a mandatory document for all construction subcontractors seeking to work with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

Subcontractors **must** submit their response to Aker Kvaerner using this form as part of the pre-qualification process. This survey is crucial for Aker Kvaerner Chemetics to assess a subcontractor's Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) capabilities and commitment.

For more information on the overall pre-qualification requirements, refer to [[subcontractor_hse_pre_qualification]].
