# Travel Request Form (0-FRM-9002) Details

Each traveller **must** complete the 0-FRM-9002: Travel Request form for all business travel. This form serves as the primary document for initiating and detailing travel requirements, enabling the company to assess and arrange necessary provisions.

The traveller **must** provide the following information on the form for each destination:
*   Latest required arrival dates and times.
*   Earliest required departure dates and times.
*   Preferred travel class (Business or Economy).
*   Requirement for a hotel booking.
*   Requirement for a rental car.
*   The purpose of the trip.
*   The cost-code(s) to be charged.

Additionally, the traveller **must** indicate the need for specific support or entitlements, which the Company will then assess:
*   **US Immigration Letter:** The Company will assess the requirements for a US Immigration letter based on the traveller's indication.
*   **Business or Visitors Visa:** The Company will assess the requirements for a Business or visitors visa. For more details on obtaining these, refer to [[travel_health_and_documentation_support]].
*   **Assignment Uplift Entitlement:** The traveller **must** indicate if they require Assignment Uplift entitlement. The Company will assess these requirements in accordance with the [[0_pol_9111_assignment_uplift]] policy.
*   **Cash Advance:** The traveller **must** indicate if a cash advance is needed. The Company will assess these requirements.

If requesting travel bookings that deviate from the 11.1 PR 2 Group Travel Procedure, the traveller **must** explicitly state the requested deviation and provide clear reasons for it. A common reason for such deviations is when the traveller’s plans are subject to possible change, necessitating a refundable ticket, which is typically not the lowest-cost option. This process is further detailed in [[travel_policy_compliance_and_deviations]].

The traveller **must** provide the following information on the 0-FRM-9002: Travel Request form:
*   Latest required arrival dates and times for each destination.
*   Earliest required departure dates and times for each destination.
*   Indication of Business or Economy Class for each destination.
*   Requirement for a hotel booking for each destination.
*   Requirement for a rental car for each destination.
*   Purpose of the trip.
*   Cost-code(s) to be charged.
*   Indication of the need for a US Immigration letter.
*   Indication of the need for a Business or visitors visa.
*   Indication of the need for Assignment Uplift entitlement.
*   Indication of the need for a Cash advance.

**Requesting Deviations:**
If requesting travel bookings that deviate from the 11.1 PR 2 Group Travel Procedure, the traveller **must** clearly state the specific deviation requested and provide the reasons for it on the form.
