# Fan or Blower Non-Destructive Examination Requirements

Non-Destructive Testing (NDT) is a required item or activity for the Fan or Blower equipment under Quality Data Sheet **0-QDS-7425**.

**Dye Penetrant** testing is specifically identified as an item or activity to be performed.

All Non-Destructive Examination (NDE) **will be** conducted in accordance with **ANSI/AWS Structural Welding Code D1.1**. An approved equivalent standard may be used for NDE, provided it receives prior approval. These standards ensure the structural integrity and quality of welds and materials without causing damage to the component.
