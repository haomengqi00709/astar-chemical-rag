# Line and Flange Break Permit System and Personal Protective Equipment

This page details the requirements for the Line Breaking Permit system and the associated Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) necessary for safely performing line and flange break operations.

**Line Breaking Permit System**
Prior to beginning any first break, the Line Breaking Permit (ON HOLD) **shall** be completed by the Commissioning HSE Manager. The permit serves as a critical control document to ensure all hazards are identified and mitigated.

The Line Breaking Permit **must** include the following information:
*   Identification of the last known chemical(s) contained in the line, equipment, vessel, or tank. If the line or equipment contained a mixture of chemicals, the Material Safety Data Sheet (MSDS) for each **shall** be reviewed, and the necessary precautions observed.
*   Health hazards associated with the chemical(s).
*   Method of detection associated with the chemical(s).
*   The specific [[line_and_flange_break_personal_protective_equipment|PPE]] required per MSDS and Aker Kvaerner work procedure(s) (e.g., respiratory protection, body protection, head/face protection, hand protection, fall protection).
*   Type of containment or container needed to prevent environmental contamination.

**Task Safety Awareness (TSA) Review**
No line and flange break permit will be issued until the Commissioning HSE Manager completes a [[task_safety_awareness_overview|Task Safety Awareness (TSA)]] review. The TSA review **must** be posted at the location of the work. Reference Form [[dp-802.306_task_safety_awareness_form|DP-802.306 (Task Safety Awareness form)]] for the required documentation.

**Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) Requirements**
[[0-PRC-8015_personal_protective_equipment_policy|PPE]] **shall** be worn (refer to MSDS/task procedure) to protect employees from the type of chemical(s) that have been contained. The level of PPE required to carry out a first line break will depend on the relative hazards associated with the chemical(s) and the task being performed. The minimum PPE to be worn for first break **shall** protect the employees from the last known chemical(s) that have been contained.

*   **Minimum PPE for Potable Water or Process Airlines:** For potable water or process airlines, the minimum PPE to be worn for first break **shall** be gloves and a face shield worn over safety goggles.
*   **PPE Training:** Employees performing the first break **shall** be trained on the proper use and limitations of the PPE required for the break.
*   **Permit Reference:** Specific “PPE Requirements” are identified on the [[line_and_flange_break_permit_and_ppe|Line Breaking Permit]].

**Line Breaking Permit System**
Prior to beginning any first break, the Line Breaking Permit (ON HOLD) shall be completed by the Commissioning HSE Manager. The Commissioning HSE Manager is responsible for the administration of the Line Breaking and Equipment Opening Permit system. In the temporary absence of the Commissioning HSE Manager from site, the Commissioning Manager will assume administration of the Line Breaking and Equipment Opening Permit system. The Commissioning HSE Manager is also responsible for obtaining permits from the client issuing authority and for establishing and maintaining a program that adequately informs Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ personnel of the existence, purpose, and procedures of the Line and Flange Break system. A valid permit for this program must be signed by both the Commissioning HSE Manager and the Work Supervisor.

The Line Breaking Permit must include:
*   Identification of the last known chemical(s) contained in the line, equipment, vessel, tank, etc. If a mixture of chemicals was present, the MSDS for each shall be reviewed, and all necessary precautions observed.
*   Health hazards associated with the chemical(s).
*   Method of detection associated with the chemical(s).
*   Specific PPE required per MSDS and Aker Kvaerner work procedure(s) (e.g., respiratory protection, body protection, head/face protection, hand protection, fall protection).
*   Type of containment or container needed to prevent environmental contamination.

No line and flange break permit will be issued until the Commissioning HSE Manager completes a Task Safety Awareness (TSA) review. The TSA review must be posted at the location of the work.

**PPE Requirements**
PPE shall be worn, referring to the MSDS and task procedure, to protect employees from the type of chemical(s) that have been contained. The level of PPE required for a first line break will depend on the relative hazards associated with the chemical(s) and the task being performed. The minimum PPE worn for a first break shall protect employees from the last known chemical(s) that have been contained. For potable water or process airlines, the minimum PPE for a first break shall be gloves and a face shield worn over safety goggles. Always refer to the “PPE Requirements” identified on the permit.
