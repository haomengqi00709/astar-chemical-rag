# Tower Field Inspection Checklist Items (4-FIC-XX23-R0)

This page outlines the mandatory inspection items for tower equipment, as detailed in the Tower Field Inspection Checklist document **4-FIC-XX23-R0**. Adherence to these requirements is crucial for ensuring the quality, safety, and operational integrity of tower installations.

**Mandatory Inspection Items:**

The following items **must** be verified during the field inspection:

*   **Nameplate:** The correct nameplate **must** be installed.
*   **Foundation/Anchor Bolts:** Foundation and anchor bolts **must** be secure and tightened.
*   **Sliding Supports:** Any sliding supports **must** allow for proper movement.
*   **Vessel Internals:** All vessel internals, including dip pipes, stand pipes, baffles, and vortex breakers, **must** be installed. Refer to [[vessel_internals_installation_requirements]] for detailed specifications.
*   **Platforms, Hand Rails, Walkways, Stairs & Ladders:** All platforms, hand rails, walkways, stairs, and ladders **must** be installed.
*   **Safety Gates:** Safety gates **must** be installed and functioning correctly.
*   **Non-Destructive Testing (NDT):** All NDT inspections **must** be completed, and their records **must** be available. General inspection requirements are detailed in [[0-QDS-7419_inspection_requirements]].
*   **Distributor:** The distributor **must** be installed and checked. A report for the distributor installation and check **must** be attached. [[tower_distributor_installation_report]]
*   **Packing Support:** Packing support **must** be installed according to vendor instructions. [[tower_packing_support_installation_guidelines]]
*   **Packing:** The correct type and amount of packing **must** be installed.
*   **Vessel Cleanliness:** The vessel **must** be clean and empty prior to final closure.
*   **Internal Painting or Lining:** Internal painting or lining **must** be completed. Inspection reports for internal painting or lining **must** be attached. [[tower_internal_coating_inspection_reports]]
*   **Final Internal Inspection:** A final internal inspection **must** be completed with the client. A closure certificate for this final internal inspection **must** be attached. [[tower_final_internal_inspection_closure_certificate]]
*   **Isolation Valves:** Isolation valves **must** be installed on all drains.
*   **Manway Covers:** All manway covers (multiple, if applicable) **must** be installed and tightened. Refer to [[manway_covers_installation_and_inspection]] for proper procedures.
*   **External Painting:** External painting **must** be completed. Refer to [[coatings_erection_0_spc_6x97]] for erection specifications.
*   **Insulation & Cladding:** Insulation and cladding **must** be complete. Refer to [[insulation_erection_0_spc_6x96]] for erection specifications.
*   **Grounding:** Grounding **must** be completed, potentially at multiple locations. Refer to [[grounding_installation_details]] for installation specifics.
*   **Overflow / Seal Loops:** Overflow and seal loops **must** be at the correct level.
*   **Flange Guards:** Flange guards **must** be installed, if applicable. [[flange_guards_installation_requirements]]

This checklist ensures a comprehensive review of the tower's physical installation and readiness for operation. For administrative details of this checklist, refer to [[4-FIC-XX23-R0_tower_field_inspection_checklist_administration]].
