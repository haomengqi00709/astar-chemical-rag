# Zirconium Welding Procedure and Welder Qualifications (4-SPC-0024)

This page details the mandatory requirements for welding procedures and welder qualifications for Zirconium (Grade 60702) fabrications as per document **4-SPC-0024**.

**Welding Procedures:**
*   All welding procedures **must** be submitted by the fabricator for review and agreement by the Materials and Quality Assurance Group.
*   Welding procedure submission **must** occur prior to the commencement of any work.
*   The [[ASME_section_ix_welding_and_brazing_qualifications|ASME Section IX]] PQR Form 483 **must** form part of the welding procedure package.
*   A change in essential variables, as outlined in [[ASME_section_ix_welding_and_brazing_qualifications|ASME Code Section IX]], **will** require re-qualification of the procedure.
*   A change in essential variables, as outlined in [[ASME_section_ix_welding_and_brazing_qualifications|ASME Code Section IX]], **will** also require re-submission of the procedure(s).
*   Welding procedures and associated tests **must** be agreed upon before any production welding is commenced.

**Welder Qualifications:**
*   All welders employed on the work **must** be suitably qualified in accordance with relevant codes and standards for every procedure they are to use.
*   The fabricator **must**, upon request, supply objective evidence of current welder qualifications.
*   All qualified welders **must** be allocated an identification symbol.
*   The welder identification symbol **must** be identified on the parent material adjacent to their work.
