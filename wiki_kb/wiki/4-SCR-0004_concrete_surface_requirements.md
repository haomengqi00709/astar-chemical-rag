# Flat Bottom Tank on Concrete (No Grout) - Concrete Surface Requirements

This page specifies the mandatory surface finish requirements for the concrete foundation supporting the Flat Bottom Tank on Concrete (no Grout), as detailed in document 4-SCR-0004. Adherence to these specifications is crucial for proper tank seating and structural integrity.

### Mandatory Concrete Surface Requirements
The following requirements **must** be met for the concrete surface:
*   The concrete **must** be poured to a flat surface.
*   The concrete **shall** be free of irregularities.
*   The concrete **shall** be free of ridges.
*   The concrete **shall** be free of depressions.
*   The maximum variation under a 3m straight edge in any direction **shall** be 3mm.
