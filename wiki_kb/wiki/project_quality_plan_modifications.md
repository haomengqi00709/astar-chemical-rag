# Project Quality Plan Modifications

The Project Manager is responsible for evaluating the specific quality requirements of each project and proposing any necessary modifications to the standard Quality Plan.

**Evaluation and Proposal:**
The Project Manager **must** evaluate the quality requirements of the project. Based on this evaluation, the Project Manager will propose modifications to the standard 0-PRC-7003: Quality Plan.

**Approval Process:**
Any proposed modifications to the Quality Plan **must** undergo a multi-level approval process to ensure comprehensive review and endorsement. The Quality Plan **must** be approved by:
*   The Director of Engineering.
*   The Director of Projects.
*   The Director of Technology.
*   The President.

This rigorous approval process ensures that the project's quality objectives are met while maintaining alignment with company standards and strategic goals.
