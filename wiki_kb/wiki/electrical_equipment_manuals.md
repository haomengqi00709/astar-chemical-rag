# Electrical Equipment Manuals

Electrical Equipment Manuals are comprehensive compilations of documentation related to electrical equipment, assembled for operational and maintenance purposes.

**Minimum Content Requirements**
Project Electrical Manuals **will** contain, as a minimum:
*   Table of Contents.
*   [[electrical_equipment_specifications]] Sheets.
*   [[electrical_purchase_orders]].
*   Electrical Vendor Manuals (Operation and Maintenance).

Electrical equipment manuals **will** be assembled as per Table 2.
