# Professional Services Subcontract (0-SPC-2201) Dispute Resolution

Professional Services Subcontract 0-SPC-2201 outlines a structured process for resolving disputes between Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Subcontractor, emphasizing initial attempts at resolution before escalating to formal arbitration.

**Escalation to Senior Representatives:**
*   Any dispute that remains unresolved within four weeks **must** be submitted to appropriate senior representatives of both Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Subcontractor.
*   These senior representatives **must** hold a conference within a further two weeks to actively attempt to resolve the dispute. This step encourages direct negotiation and resolution at a higher management level before external intervention.

**Arbitration Proceedings:**
*   If the meeting of the senior representatives fails to resolve the dispute, either Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or the Subcontractor may refer the dispute for final resolution by arbitration.
*   The arbitration **must** be administered by the British Columbia International Commercial Arbitration Centre, in accordance with its established Rules.
*   A single arbitrator will be appointed in accordance with these Rules.
*   The designated place of arbitration is Vancouver, British Columbia.
*   It is a mandatory requirement that arbitration proceedings **must not** be initiated before the Delivery of the whole of the Goods and Services or an earlier termination of the Subcontract. This ensures that arbitration is pursued only after the primary contractual obligations related to delivery are met or the contract is formally ended.

This dispute resolution mechanism is designed to provide a clear pathway for addressing disagreements, starting with internal resolution efforts and progressing to a formal, binding arbitration process if necessary, all within a defined legal framework.
