# Construction Subcontract Administrative Specifications

A suite of administrative specifications directly referenced by the [[construction_subcontract_agreement_template_and_content]] provides detailed guidelines and requirements for various aspects of construction subcontract execution. These specifications ensure standardized practices across projects and cover critical areas from coordination to quality assurance.

**List of Administrative Specifications:**
The following administrative specifications are integral to the Construction Subcontract Agreement:
*   **0-SPC-6003: Construction Subcontract Coordination:** Defines procedures and responsibilities for coordinating activities between the firm and the subcontractor. Data available in SQL: 0-SPC-6003
*   **0-SPC-6011: Construction Subcontract Pricing: Lump Sum:** Outlines the specific requirements and methodologies for lump sum pricing within a construction subcontract. Data available in SQL: 0-SPC-6011
*   **0-SPC-6012: Construction Subcontract Pricing: Unit Price:** Details the framework for unit price contracts, including definitions and application. Data available in SQL: 0-SPC-6012
*   **0-SPC-6021: Construction Subcontract Scheduling:** Sets forth the requirements for project scheduling, progress reporting, and adherence to timelines. Data available in SQL: 0-SPC-6021
*   **0-SPC-6041: Construction Subcontractor Materials Control:** Specifies procedures for the control, handling, and accountability of materials supplied by or to the subcontractor. Data available in SQL: 0-SPC-6041
*   **0-SPC-6051: Construction Subcontract Safety Management:** Establishes mandatory safety protocols, management systems, and reporting requirements to ensure a safe working environment. Data available in SQL: 0-SPC-6051
*   **0-SPC-6061: Construction Subcontract Environmental Management:** Defines environmental protection measures, compliance requirements, and waste management procedures. Data available in SQL: 0-SPC-6061
*   **0-SPC-6071: Construction Subcontract Quality Assurance:** Outlines the quality management system, inspection, testing, and documentation requirements to ensure work meets specified quality standards. Data available in SQL: 0-SPC-6071
*   **0-SPC-6081: Construction Subcontract Industrial Relations Management:** Addresses policies and procedures related to labor relations, workforce management, and dispute resolution. Data available in SQL: 0-SPC-6081
*   **0-SPC-6091: Construction Subcontractor Document Supply:** Specifies the requirements for document submission, format, and timelines from the subcontractor. Data available in SQL: 0-SPC-6091

**Usage and Modification of Standards:**
These administrative specifications exist as company standards. They **must** be used as-is or modified for specific project requirements as necessary, in accordance with the [[approval_and_use_of_standards]] (0-PRC-0001) procedure.

**Document Status and Approval:**
*   These documents **should** be published with a status of "Approved for Construction" to indicate their readiness for implementation.
*   They **should** be checked and approved following the guidelines outlined in [[approval_and_use_of_standards]] (0-PRC-0001).

**Handling Exceptions and Deviations:**
Administrative specifications listed above will generally not be further modified after their initial approval for a project. Any exceptions or deviations from these standards that are agreed upon during the negotiation phase with a subcontractor **must** be formally handled by inserting appropriate text into [[special_conditions_for_construction_subcontract_0_spc_6x01]]. This ensures that all project-specific variations are clearly documented and legally binding.
