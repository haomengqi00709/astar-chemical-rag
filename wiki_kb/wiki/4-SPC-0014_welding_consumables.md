# Welding Consumables for Stabilized Austenitic Stainless Steel (4-SPC-0014)

This page details the requirements for welding consumables used in Columbium Stabilized High Nickel Austenitic stainless steel fabrications, as specified in document 4-SPC-0014.

**General Requirements**
*   All weld filler metals shall meet the requirements of Section 8 of this Standard. For non-stabilized grades, refer to [[4-SPC-0014_unstabilized_austenitic_stainless_steel_appendix_a]].
*   The Fabricator shall ensure and demonstrate that manufacturers' instructions regarding the storage, drying, and use of weld consumables are fully implemented.
*   All wet electrodes are discarded and shall not be baked and re-used.
*   Prior to the commencement of work, the Fabricator shall furnish Aker Kvaerner Chemetics with the brand name of the filler materials actually to be used.
*   Certification showing results from analysis and other tests in accordance with relevant material specifications shall be supplied to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for all materials, including welding consumables.
*   The Fabricator shall maintain full traceability of materials back to original mill certification.
*   The Fabricator’s internal controls shall prevent the use of unspecified materials.
*   Lack of adequate identification or certification shall be cause for rejection of the specific component or item.

**Specific Filler Metal Requirements**
*   **Inconel Electrodes/Filler Wire:** All Inconel electrodes/filler wire shall satisfy the requirements of [[ASME_sfa_5_11_nickel_and_nickel_alloy_electrodes]] and [[ASME_sfa_5_14_nickel_and_nickel_alloy_electrodes_and_rods]] respectively.
*   **Unlisted Filler Metals:** The use of filler metals not listed shall be referred to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics QA & Materials Group for approval.
*   **Autogenous Welding:** Autogenous (no filler metal) welding of "904L" type materials is not permitted.
