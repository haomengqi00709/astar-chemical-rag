# Safety Footwear Requirements

This page outlines the mandatory requirements for safety footwear in construction and workshop environments, as specified in Document ID 0-PRC-8015.

**Mandatory Requirements**
Safety boots **are required** for all employees and visitors in construction sites. Safety boots **are also required** for all employees and visitors in workshops. Company-approved footwear **is limited** to CSA Grade 1 boots. All company-approved footwear **must** meet the CSA standard Z195 (or equivalent ANSI standard).

**CSA Approval and Protection**
A green CSA patch, in the shape of either a rectangle or triangle, indicates CSA approval. The triangular patch designates sole puncture protection, in addition to impact protection. All CSA-approved boots provide protection against falling and sharp objects in a heavy industrial work environment.

**Special Purpose Footwear**
Whenever required, special purpose footwear **shall** be selected to provide maximum protection for the employee. This special purpose footwear **will** at minimum incorporate a steel shank and steel toe.
