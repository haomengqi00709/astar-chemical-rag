# Over-dimensional Load Requirements (0-SPC-5004)

For any goods exceeding the capacity of a standard forty-foot ocean container, the Vendor **must** supply transport information four weeks in advance of the scheduled shipment date.

**Standard Forty-Foot Ocean Container Dimensions (Approximate):**
*   Length: 1200 cm
*   Width: 230 cm
*   Height: 230 cm

**Required Transport Information for Over-dimensional Loads:**
The transport information provided for over-dimensional loads **may** include:
*   Detailed transport drawings.
*   Lifting and handling instructions.
*   Estimated transportation weights and dimensions.

This advance notification and detailed information are crucial for [Company Name] to make necessary arrangements for specialized transport and handling.
