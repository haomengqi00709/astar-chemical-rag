# Confined Space Entry Procedure (0-PRC-8502-A)

This page describes the Confined Space Entry Procedure, Document ID 0-PRC-8502-A. This procedure outlines the requirements and guidelines for safely entering and working in confined spaces.

**Purpose:**
The purpose of this procedure is to ensure the safety of personnel entering confined spaces by establishing clear protocols for hazard identification, risk assessment, atmospheric monitoring, ventilation, rescue planning, and permit-to-work systems.
