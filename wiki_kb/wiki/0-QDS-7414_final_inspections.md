# FRP Tank Final Inspections (0-QDS-7414)

This page details the final inspection requirements for FRP Tanks, as specified in Quality Data Sheet **0-QDS-7414**. These inspections are the conclusive steps to ensure the finished product meets all specified quality, dimensional, and cleanliness standards before shipment.

**Mandatory Final Inspection Requirements:**
*   **Final Inspection** is a mandatory requirement and **must** be included in the Vendor's Inspection and Test (I & T) Plan. This encompasses a comprehensive review of the completed FRP tank.

The following specific final inspection activities require Chemetics surveillance:

*   **Dimensional Inspection:**
    *   Requires Quality Record **FP** (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package).
    *   Requires **Chemetics Witness** surveillance.
    *   Requires **Chemetics Hold** surveillance.

*   **Acetone Wipe Test:**
    *   Requires Quality Record **FP** (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package).
    *   Requires **Chemetics Witness** surveillance.
    *   Requires **Chemetics Hold** surveillance.

*   **Surfaces Free From Defects:**
    *   Requires **Chemetics Witness** surveillance.
    *   Requires **Chemetics Hold** surveillance.

*   **Cleanliness:**
    *   Requires **Chemetics Witness** surveillance.
    *   Requires **Chemetics Hold** surveillance.

*   **Review QA Documentation:**
    *   Requires **Chemetics Witness** surveillance.
    *   Requires **Chemetics Hold** surveillance.

These rigorous final inspections, including dimensional checks, surface integrity tests like the acetone wipe test, and overall cleanliness, are crucial to confirm the FRP tank's readiness for service. The Chemetics Witness and Hold points ensure that critical aspects are verified by the client before final acceptance. For general final inspection guidelines, refer to [[0-QDS-7411_final_inspections]].
