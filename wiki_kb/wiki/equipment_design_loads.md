# Equipment Design Loads

As per document 4-SPC-0001, equipment **must** be designed to withstand the most adverse combination of loadings. These design loads **shall** include, but are not limited to, the following categories:

*   **Dead Loads:** The weight of the equipment itself, including all permanent attachments and internals.
*   **Live Loads:** Variable loads such as personnel, tools, and temporary equipment.
*   **Wind Loads:** Forces exerted by wind, which **must** accommodate the design wind loading as specified in [[1-DST-0001_site_design_conditions]].
*   **Earthquake Loads:** Seismic forces, which **must** accommodate the earthquake loading as specified in [[1-DST-0001_site_design_conditions]].
*   **Pressure Induced Loads:** Forces resulting from internal or external pressure differentials.
*   **Machine Action Loads:** Loads generated by the operation of the machine, such as torque.
*   **Acceleration/Deceleration (Inertia) Loads:** Forces due to changes in velocity.
*   **Impact Loads:** Forces resulting from sudden impacts.
*   **Expansion/Contraction Loads:** Forces due to thermal expansion or contraction of materials.
*   **Material Spillage/Abnormal Operation Loads:** Loads resulting from unexpected events like material spills or deviations from normal operating conditions.
*   **Plant Maintenance Loads:** Loads imposed during maintenance activities, such as lifting or temporary support.

**Fatigue Failure Analysis**
For applications where fatigue failure analysis is applicable, design loads **shall** be determined from loads applied to, or produced by, a machine whilst operating at its maximum rated capacity, assuming the machine's prime mover(s) are operating at their maximum continuous rating.

**Load Combinations**
Load combinations **must** be in accordance with the applicable codes. Anchor bolts **will** be designed in accordance with the applicable codes.

For general requirements for equipment design, refer to [[general_equipment_requirements_specification]].

**Types of Design Loads**
Design loads will include, but not be limited to: Dead, live, wind, earthquake, pressure induced, machine action (e.g., torque), acceleration/deceleration (inertia), impact, expansion/contraction, material spillage/abnormal operation, and plant maintenance loads.

**Fatigue Failure Analysis**
Design loads for fatigue failure analysis (where applicable) will be determined from loads applied to, or produced by, a machine whilst operating at its maximum rated capacity assuming the machine's prime mover(s) are operating at their maximum continuous rating.

**Environmental Load References**
Equipment will be designed to accommodate the design wind loading and earthquake loading as specified in [[1-DST-0001_site_design_conditions]].

**Load Combinations**
Load combinations will be in accordance with the applicable codes.
