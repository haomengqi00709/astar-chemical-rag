# Sulphuric Acid Storage Tank Specification Overview (4-SPC-0046)

This specification, identified as 4-SPC-0046, outlines the comprehensive design, fabrication, erection, and quality requirements for the procurement of shop-fabricated sulphuric acid storage tanks. It specifically addresses welded steel storage tanks intended for sulphuric acid service within the 93-98% concentration range.

Sulphuric acid is recognized as a hazardous fluid. Consequently, the materials specified within this standard are compatible with sulphuric acid only under precise combinations of temperature and concentration. Prior to utilizing this specification for sulphuric acid service, consultation with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_quality_assurance_and_materials_engineering_group|Quality Assurance and Materials Engineering Group]] is **necessary**.

This document is a direct copy of the original specification ES44/F08, titled "General Requirements for Design, Fabrications, Erection & Quality of Sulphuric Acid Storage Tanks." Its use **must** be in accordance with the [[0-prc-0001_standard_document_management_procedure|Approval and Use of Standards (0-PRC-0001)]]. Historically, this document was known as 4-SPC-0007-R5.
