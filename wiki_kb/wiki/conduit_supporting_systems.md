# Conduit Supporting Systems

Conduit supporting systems details provide specific instructions and material requirements for installing and supporting conduits in various plant locations. These details ensure the secure routing and protection of electrical wiring.

**Content of Conduit Supporting Systems Details**
Conduit supporting systems details **shall** show:
*   Conduit supporting systems along pipe racks.
*   Conduit supporting systems for drop-offs.
*   Conduit supporting systems for wall penetrations.
*   Necessary supporting materials for conduit systems.
