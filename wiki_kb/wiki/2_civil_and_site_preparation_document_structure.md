# Civil and Site Preparation Document Structure (Major Work Subject 2)

The "2 Civil and Site Preparation" major work subject contains all documentation pertaining to the civil engineering aspects of a project, including foundation design, site layouts, and related specifications. This folder is essential for managing the infrastructure development of the plant.

The sub-folders and their contents within the 2-Civil-and-Site-Preparation folder are:

*   **CAL:** Foundation Calculations. Detailed engineering calculations for all types of foundations.
*   **DTL:** Foundation Details. Drawings and specifications providing specific details for foundation construction.
*   **GAD:** Foundation General Arrangements. Overall layout drawings showing the arrangement of foundations on the site.
*   **PRC:** Project Specific Procedures. Procedures tailored to the civil and site preparation requirements of the project.
*   **SPC:** Specifications. Technical specifications for civil works, materials, and construction methods.
*   **VDR:** Vendor Document Requirements. Documents outlining the requirements for vendor-supplied civil-related documentation.
*   **VEN:** Vendors Reinforcing Bending Schedules. Schedules provided by vendors for the bending of reinforcing steel.

This structure ensures that all civil and site preparation documents, from calculations to vendor-specific details, are systematically organized and easily retrievable. For an overview of the entire project documentation structure, refer to [[document_control_1_prc_0003]]. The definitions of major work subjects are outlined in [[major_work_division_codes]].
