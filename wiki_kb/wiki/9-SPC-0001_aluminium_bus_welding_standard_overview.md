# Aluminium Bus Welding Standard Overview (9-SPC-0001)

This document, 9-SPC-0001, defines the workmanship and quality requirements for Aluminium and Aluminium alloy welded BUS and Associated Connections. It is a direct copy of the original specification ES44/F01, "General Requirements for Welding Aluminium Bus and Associated Connections."

**Scope and Application**
This standard applies to connections purchased by or on behalf of [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_company_details]] and covers welding and other fabrication requirements for both shop and field-fabricated BUS systems. It **shall** be applied when explicitly called for on purchase orders and referenced documents.

**Document Administration and Future Plans**
This document will be used in accordance with [[0-PRC-0001_approval_and_use_of_standards]]. There are plans to replace this document in the future with a new version that fully complies with the [[0-PRC-0004_document_style_and_format]] for textual documents.
