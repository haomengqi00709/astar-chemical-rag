# Hearing Protection Requirements

This page outlines the requirements for hearing protection based on noise exposure levels in various work environments, as specified in Document ID 0-PRC-8015.

**Mandatory Hearing Protection**
Employees who are regularly exposed to hazardous noise levels **will** wear hearing protection. Hazardous noise levels are defined as an 8-hour Time-Weighted Average (TWA) greater than 85 dbA, or a 12-hour TWA greater than 82 dbA.

Hearing protection **is required** in workshop areas. Hearing protection **is also required** in construction areas where the employee is exposed to noise levels above the previously stated guidelines. It is recommended that hearing protection be worn during any exposure to persistent noise levels above a TWA of 85 dbA.

**Types and Standards**
Hearing protection may consist of foam ear inserts, moulded ear inserts, and muff-type hearing protectors, depending on the noise level. All hearing protection worn **must** meet the requirements set out by CSA Standard Z94.2-02 "Hearing Protection Devices" (or equivalent ANSI standard). Consult CSA Standard Z94.2-02 for the class of hearing protection device (Class A is generally the best).

**Workplace Monitoring and Identification**
The Company **will** monitor the workplace for hazardous noise levels. Areas with hazardous noise levels **will** be identified with signs posted in conspicuous places to caution personnel of the potentially hazardous exposure.
