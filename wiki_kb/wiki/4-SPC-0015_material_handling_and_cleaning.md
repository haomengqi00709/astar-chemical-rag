# 4-SPC-0015 Material Handling and Cleaning

This page outlines the critical requirements for material handling, cleaning, and contamination control during the fabrication of nickel-base alloys under Standard 4-SPC-0015.

**Pre-Fabrication Cleaning**
*   The surface and edges of material to be heated **must** be cleaned free from all traces of foreign matter (e.g., marking paint, oil, etc.).
*   Cleaning methods include grinding, wire brushing with a stainless steel brush, and/or non-chlorinated solvent cleaning.
*   Cleaning **must** be done immediately prior to the application of heat.

**Contamination Control**
*   Fabricator **must** take controls to prevent contamination of weldments at all stages of fabrication.
*   Controls **must** include segregation of wire brushes, grinding wheels, etc., for use on nickel-base materials only.
*   Sulphur, phosphorous or lead bearing paints or crayon **must not** be used for marking or identification purposes.

**Post-Fabrication Cleaning and Passivation**
*   On completion of work, items **must** be cleaned internally and externally.
*   All fabrication markings, weld spatter, etc., **must** be removed prior to shipment.
*   Passivation, where required by Purchase Order/referenced documents, **must** be carried out in accordance with a written procedure.
*   The passivation procedure **must** be approved by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics prior to its use.
