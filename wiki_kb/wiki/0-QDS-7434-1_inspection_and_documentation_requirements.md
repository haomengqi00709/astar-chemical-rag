# Inspection and Documentation Requirements for Lime Handling System (0-QDS-7434-1)

This page details the specific inspection and documentation requirements for key components of the Lime Handling System, as outlined in Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7434-1. The Vendor's Quality Assurance **shall** comply with these requirements, with Chemetics surveillance set at Level 1.0.

[[quality_record_definitions|Quality Record abbreviations]] used in this document:
*   **AP:** As Prepared
*   **FP:** Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package

Chemetics surveillance levels indicate the extent of Chemetics' involvement:
*   **Complete:** Chemetics **must** perform a full review or inspection of the activity.
*   **Witness:** Chemetics **must** be present to observe the activity being performed.
*   **Hold:** Chemetics **must** provide explicit approval before the Vendor can proceed to the next stage of work.

### Lime Storage Bin

**Material Verification:**
*   **Material Test Certificates:** Required FP. Chemetics surveillance: Complete.
*   **Certificate of Compliance:** Required FP. Chemetics surveillance: Complete.

**Coating/Lining:**
*   **External coating of CS:** Required FP. Chemetics surveillance: Complete.

**Final Inspection:**
*   **Dimensional inspection:** Required FP. Chemetics surveillance: Complete. This inspection **must** include a trial fit of the handrail, ladder, and platform.
*   **Surfaces free from defects:** Required FP. Chemetics surveillance: Complete.
*   **Cleanliness:** Required FP. Chemetics surveillance: Complete.

**QA Documentation Review:**
*   **Review QA Documentation:** Chemetics surveillance: Witness and Hold.

**Packing/Crating:**
*   **Packaging:** Chemetics surveillance: Witness.
*   **Packing List:** Required AP.

### Bin Filter

**Material Verification:**
*   **Certificate of Compliance:** Required FP. Chemetics surveillance: Complete.

**Coating/Lining:**
*   **External coating of CS:** Required FP. Chemetics surveillance: Complete.

**Final Inspection:**
*   **Dimensional inspection:** Required FP. Chemetics surveillance: Complete.
*   **Cleanliness:** Required FP. Chemetics surveillance: Complete.

**Packing/Crating:**
*   **Packaging:** Chemetics surveillance: Witness.

### Discharger

**Material Verification:**
*   **Certificate of Compliance:** Required FP. Chemetics surveillance: Complete.

**Coating/Lining:**
*   **External coating of CS:** Required FP. Chemetics surveillance: Complete.

**Final Inspection:**
*   **Dimensional inspection:** Required FP. Chemetics surveillance: Complete. This inspection **must** verify NEMA 4X construction.
*   **Cleanliness:** Required FP. Chemetics surveillance: Complete.

**Packing/Crating:**
*   **Packaging:** Chemetics surveillance: Witness.
