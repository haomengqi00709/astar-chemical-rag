# Compensation Events in Construction Subcontracts

Compensation Events are defined as occurrences that typically result in a change (generally an increase) to the Prices, and/or a change (generally an extension) to the Completion Date of a construction subcontract. The handling of these events is detailed in Sections 26 to 29 of [[standard_conditions_for_construction_subcontract_0_spc_6001]]. Data available in SQL: 0-SPC-6001.

**Common Compensation Events:**
Several scenarios are commonly recognized as compensation events:
*   Instructions issued by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics that change the Works Information.
*   Changes in the quantity of works caused by revisions to documents issued by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics between the "Issued for Quotation" and "Approved for Construction" statuses.
*   Extensions to the Completion Date resulting from late delivery of Equipment and Materials by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
*   Modification of work already completed due to changes in documents issued by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics with an "Approved for Construction" status.
*   Field Orders providing instructions to rectify an error or omission in documentation issued by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
*   Explicit or implicit instructions issued by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics via email messages or as minutes of meetings.

**Handling Compensation Event Claims:**
The Subcontract Administrator is responsible for responding to any notice from the Subcontractor regarding the occurrence of a compensation event. The response will either be an acceptance or a rejection of the claim.

If the claim is accepted as a valid compensation event, the Subcontract Administrator will request a quotation from the Subcontractor. Section 28.3 of [[standard_conditions_for_construction_subcontract_0_spc_6001]] outlines the possible actions Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may take upon receiving such a quotation.

Should a quotation be accepted, a formal notification will be issued in the form of a [[subcontract_change_order_procedure_0_chg_6xx0]].

**Unacceptable Quotations:**
If the Subcontractor fails to provide an acceptable quotation for a compensation event, Aker Kvaerner Chemetics will assess the compensation event internally, in accordance with Section 28.4 of [[standard_conditions_for_construction_subcontract_0_spc_6001]]. In such cases, the Subcontract Administrator will determine the increase in cost and extension of time resulting from the compensation event and issue a [[subcontract_change_order_procedure_0_chg_6xx0]] accordingly. The Subcontractor's only recourse under these circumstances is to put the matter into dispute.
