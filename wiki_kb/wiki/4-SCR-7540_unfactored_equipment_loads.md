# 4-SCR-7540 Unfactored Equipment Loads

This page details the unfactored equipment loads for the Acid Plant Converter, as specified in document **4-SCR-7540**. These loads are critical for the structural design of equipment supports and foundations. All specified loads **must** be applied at the Datum.

The unfactored equipment loads include:
*   **Empty - Maximum Mass:** This refers to the bare vessel mass, excluding any insulation or catalyst.
    *   Applicable to Skirt / Core (t)
*   **Operating - Maximum Mass:**
    *   Applicable to Skirt / Core (t)
*   **Uplift due to Internal Pressure:**
    *   Applicable to Skirt / Core (kN)
*   **Wind Base Shear:** (kN)
*   **Wind Base Moment:** (kN-m)
*   **Seismic Base Shear:** (kN)
*   **Seismic Base Moment:** (kN-m)

For related information on equipment support design, refer to [[equipment_support_criteria]]. Specific design requirements for wind and seismic conditions can be found in [[wind_design_requirements]] and [[seismic_design_requirements]], respectively.
