# Change Management Document Types

Effective project change management relies on a suite of standardized documents to track, evaluate, approve, and implement modifications. These documents ensure transparency, accountability, and a clear audit trail throughout the [[0-PRC-4003-R1_project_change_management_flow_diagram|Project Change Management Flow Diagram]].

Key document types used in the change management process include:

*   **Change Request**: This is the initial document used to formally propose a change to the project. It can be initiated by the Client or an internal Project Team member, outlining the proposed modification and its rationale. See also: [[project_change_initiation]].

*   **PVN (Project Variation Notice)**: A document initiated by a Project Team member to formally identify a potential change or variation to the project scope, schedule, or cost. It serves as an internal notification and trigger for the [[project_change_evaluation|evaluation process]]. Details on its initiation can be found in [[pvn_pcn_form_initiation_and_details]].

*   **PCN (Project Change Notice)**: Similar to a PVN, a PCN is also initiated for potential changes, often used interchangeably with PVN depending on internal project conventions. It triggers the formal evaluation of impacts.

*   **Variation Report**: This document is generated by the [[change_management_roles_and_responsibilities_0_prc_4003|Project Control Specialist]] during the [[project_change_evaluation|change evaluation]] phase. It consolidates all assessed impacts (man-hours, schedule, cost, risks) of a proposed change and is issued for approval.

*   **Formal Change Order**: Once a change has been thoroughly evaluated and approved by all necessary stakeholders, a formal change order is issued. This is a legally binding document that officially modifies the project contract and scope, authorizing the [[project_change_implementation|implementation]] of the change.

*   **Change Log**: The change log is a comprehensive record that tracks all PVNs, PCNs, and formal change orders throughout the project lifecycle. It provides a centralized repository for monitoring the status, impacts, and approval dates of all changes. The management of this log is detailed in [[0-PRC-4003-R1_pcn_log_procedure]] and [[pvn_pcn_log_and_documentation_management]].

*   **Risk Register**: While not exclusively a change management document, the [[risk_register]] is a critical tool that is updated during the [[project_change_evaluation|change evaluation]] phase. It records identified risks, their assessment, and proposed mitigation strategies, ensuring that new risks introduced by changes are managed.

These documents collectively form the backbone of a controlled and auditable change management system, vital for successful project delivery.

These documents ensure transparency, accountability, and a clear audit trail throughout the [[0-PRC-4003-R1_project_change_management_flow_diagram|Project Change Management Flow Diagram]]. Key document types include:
*   **Project Change Notice (PCN):** A formal document initiated by the Project Manager or Project Team to propose and evaluate a change.
*   **Project Variation Notice (PVN):** Similar to a PCN, often used by Project Team members to identify and initiate potential changes.
