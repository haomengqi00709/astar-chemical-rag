# Aluminium Bus Fabrication and Surface Preparation

The Aluminium Bus Welding Standard (9-SPC-0001) outlines specific requirements for fabrication and surface preparation to ensure the integrity of welded Aluminium Bus systems.

**Cleaning and Contamination Control**
Surfaces and edges of Aluminium or Aluminium alloy to be heated **shall** be cleaned free from all traces of foreign matter. The cleaning method for surfaces to be heated is solvent cleaning with acetone. Before welding, all weld surfaces and base metal one inch from the weld joint **shall** be solvent cleaned with acetone and then thoroughly wire brushed with a stainless steel wire brush. On completion of work, items **shall** be cleaned internally and externally. All fabrication markings, weld spatter, etc., **shall** be removed prior to shipment.

**Cutting, Forming, and Adherence to Drawings**
Plate and pipe **shall** be cut to size by mechanical methods only. Bevel preparation by machining, shearing, or grinding is acceptable. Material **shall** be cold formed as far as practical. When hot forming is necessary, the procedure **shall** be agreed to in writing by [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_quality_assurance_and_materials_engineering_group|Aker Kvaerner Chemetics]] prior to commencement of work. All work **shall** be carried out in accordance with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics "Approved For Fabrication" shop detail drawing(s). Deviations from weld geometry on approved drawing(s) are not permitted without prior approval of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Fit-Up and Tack Welding**
Fusion faces and adjacent metal (internal and external) **shall** be free from moisture, scale, paint, grease, or other foreign matter immediately prior to welding. Misalignment of welding edges at butt joints **shall** be held to a minimum. Fit-up **must** be accurate and checked frequently to minimize possible distortion and weld penetration difficulties. Considerable care is essential in joint preparation; bevel angles and thicknesses **shall** be accurate and true to facilitate good welding.

Parts to be welded **shall** be securely held in position by mechanical means, welded-on bridge pieces, or by tack welding to maintain specified alignment and gap. Tack welds may form part of the completed weld if complete fusion is effected at the root and the welding procedure is the same as for the remainder of the root run. Sound tack welds **must** be thoroughly fused into the root run of the main weld. Where thinning of tack weld ends is necessary, tack welds should preferably be at least one inch long. Cracked and unsound tack welds **shall** be cut out prior to welding. The use of permanent back-up rings or strips or consumable joint inserts is not permitted unless specifically approved in writing by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' Q.A. and Materials Engineering.

**Inter-Pass Cleaning and Tooling**
Back gouging of root passes of butt welds **shall** be performed whenever possible. Grinding or gouging **shall** be carried out in such a manner as not to reduce the adjacent base metal thickness. Welds **shall** be thoroughly cleaned between passes by wire brushing, with power brushing being preferred. Grinding wheels **shall** be iron-free and of the resin bonded alumina or silica type. Wire brushes and chipping tools **shall** be made of austenitic stainless steel and **must not** have been previously used to clean other surfaces.

**Cleaning and Contamination Control**
Surfaces and edges of Aluminium or Aluminium alloy to be heated **shall** be cleaned free from all traces of foreign matter. The cleaning method for surfaces to be heated is solvent cleaning with acetone.

Fusion faces and adjacent metal (internal and external) **shall** be free from moisture, scale, paint, grease or other foreign matter immediately prior to welding.

**Post-Fabrication Cleaning**
On completion of work, items **shall** be cleaned internally and externally. All fabrication markings, weld spatter, etc., **shall** be removed prior to shipment.

**General Fabrication Requirements**
All work **shall** be carried out in accordance with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics "Approved For Fabrication" shop detail drawing(s). Deviations from weld geometry on approved drawing(s) are not permitted without prior approval of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Material Preparation**
Plate and pipe **shall** be cut to size by mechanical methods only. Bevel preparation by machining, shearing, or grinding is acceptable. Material **shall** be cold formed as far as practical. When hot forming is necessary, the procedure **shall** be agreed to in writing by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics prior to commencement of work.

**Tooling Requirements**
Grinding wheels **shall** be iron-free and of the resin bonded alumina or silica type. Wire brushes and chipping tools **shall** be made of austenitic stainless steel and **must not** have been previously used to clean other surfaces.
