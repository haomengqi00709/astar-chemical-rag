# 0-POL-9111 Site Compensation Policy Overview

This procedure, 0-POL-9111, outlines the guidelines for compensating employees performing site work under specific conditions and for costs associated with extended periods away from home. The policy aims to ensure employees are fairly compensated for performing site work under specific conditions and for costs associated with being away from home for extended periods.

**Purpose and Exclusions:**
The procedure 0-POL-9111 is not designed to provide compensation in lieu of adequate on-site health and safety precautions or work practices. Furthermore, it is not designed to provide compensation for insurable risks covered by the Company's group health and insurance benefits.

**Scope and Application:**
The procedure 0-POL-9111 applies to all Regular employees of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, Canadian Shared Services, and AK Chemetics Offshore Services Inc., which are collectively referred to as "The Company."
Assignment Uplift provisions apply only for employees of The Company in positions below Hay Point 640. This limitation may be waived by the President under exceptional circumstances.
The procedure 0-POL-9111 does not apply to independent consultants contracted by The Company. It does not normally apply to temporary employees engaged primarily for the purpose of site work; for such employees, their rate [[should]] be set to provide fair compensation. However, the procedure 0-POL-9111 [[will]] be applied to all other temporary employees required to travel on behalf of The Company.

This policy applies to short-term assignments that are less than 6 months. For international assignments that are longer than 6 months, the [[11_1_pr_1_group_international_assignment_procedure]] [[will]] apply.

The procedure 0-POL-9111 applies to various categories of site assignments, including but not limited to:
*   Construction site services
*   In-plant technical service and customer training assignments
*   Plant commissioning
*   Quality assurance and expediting assignments
*   Assignments to other Engineering offices

**Compliance and Precedence:**
To the extent that any statutory requirements provide higher overall compensation than the aggregate provisions of this Policy and [[compensation_of_overtime_0_pol_9113_r2]], the statutory requirements [[shall]] prevail.

**Document History:**

**Definitions**
For the purpose of this procedure, Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, Canadian Shared Services and AK Chemetics Offshore Services Inc. are collectively referred to as "The Company".

**Scope and Application**
This procedure applies to all Regular employees of The Company. It is specifically designed for short-term assignments, defined as those less than 6 months in duration. For international assignments exceeding 6 months, the 11.1 PR 1 Group International Assignment Procedure will apply.

The procedure applies to various short-term site assignment categories, including but not limited to:
*   Construction site services
*   In-plant technical service and customer training assignments
*   Plant commissioning
*   Quality assurance and expediting assignments
*   Assignments to other Engineering offices

**Exclusions**
This procedure does not apply to independent consultants contracted by The Company. It also does not normally apply to temporary employees engaged primarily for the purpose of site work; for such temporary employees, their rate should be set to provide fair compensation. However, 0-POL-9111 will be applied to all other temporary employees required to travel on behalf of The Company.

Furthermore, this procedure is not designed to provide compensation in lieu of adequate on-site health and safety precautions or work practices, nor is it intended to provide compensation for insurable risks already covered by the Company's group health and insurance benefits.

**Short-Term Travel (Less than 30 Days)**
Travel for less than 30 days will be treated as a business trip. Expenses for such travel will be reimbursed in accordance with 0-PRC-9003 Business Expense Reimbursement.

**General Principles**
The Company will make efforts to ensure compliance with Canada Revenue Agency guidelines to avoid personal tax liability for employees. To the extent that any statutory requirements provide higher overall compensation than the aggregate provisions of this Policy and 0-POL-9113-R2 Compensation of Overtime, the statutory requirements shall prevail.

**Policy History**
This Procedure 0-POL-9111 replaces the previous Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Site Compensation Policy.
