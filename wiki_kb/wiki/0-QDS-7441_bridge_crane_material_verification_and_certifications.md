# Bridge Crane Material Verification and Certifications

This page outlines the requirements for material verification and certifications for the Bridge Crane project, as specified in Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7441. These requirements ensure that all materials used comply with project specifications and industry standards.

**Required Certifications:**

*   **Mill Test Certificates:** Mill Test Certificates are required as "FP" ([[quality_record_definitions|Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package]]). These certificates **must** provide verifiable data on the chemical composition and mechanical properties of the materials, directly from the mill.

*   **Certificate of Compliance:** A Certificate of Compliance is required as "FP" ([[quality_record_definitions|Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package]]). This certificate **must** affirm that the supplied materials and components meet all specified requirements of the project.

**Chemetics Surveillance for Certifications:**
Chemetics surveillance for the Certificate of Compliance requires "Witness." This means a Chemetics representative **shall** be present to verify the compliance documentation.

**Material Verification Activity:**
Material Verification is a key item or activity within the quality assurance process for the Bridge Crane. This activity ensures that the materials received match the specifications and certifications provided by the vendor.

For an overview of the vendor's general quality assurance obligations, refer to [[0-QDS-7441_bridge_crane_vendor_quality_assurance_requirements]].

*   **Certificate of Compliance:** Required as FP. Chemetics surveillance for the Certificate of Compliance requires **Witness**.
