# Outline Drawings

Outline Drawings are crucial design documents that provide a high-level visual representation of equipment, facilitating the design of surrounding and supporting facilities. These drawings evolve through the project lifecycle, from initial dimensional criteria to detailed outlines.

**Initial Content and Purpose**
Initially, Outline Drawings **must** contain:
*   Dimensional criteria in the form of equipment elevations and plan views.
*   Itemized references to any required standard details provided on [[equipment_proprietary_detail_drawing_prp]].

The primary purpose of Outline Drawings is to provide sufficient information to allow the design of surrounding and supporting facilities.

**Detailed Content**
As the design progresses, Outline Drawings **must** contain:
*   Dimensioned outlines in the form of equipment elevations and plan views.
*   A virtual copy of the equivalent Equipment Data Sheet.
*   Internal components in outline.
*   Itemized references to [[equipment_proprietary_detail_drawing_prp]].
*   Plant elevation reference.
*   Gasket dimensions.
*   Equipment Part Numbers (EPN) as required. For EPN details, refer to [[equipment_part_numbering]].

**Type D Equipment Drawings**
For Type D equipment, drawings **must** contain:
*   Fully dimensioned equipment elevations and plan views.
*   Itemized references to any required fabrication details provided on [[equipment_proprietary_detail_drawing_prp]].

**Control and Maintenance**
Outline drawings are considered a "controlling document" for Aker Kvaerner Chemetics plant design. Dimensions on Outline Drawings **must** be kept updated with any changes from vendor detail design or fabrication considerations.

**Usage**
Outline Drawings **must** be used for Equipment manuals. Vendor drawings **must not** be used for Equipment manuals.

**Document Preparation and Approval**
The preparation, checking, and approval of Outline Drawings follow a defined workflow:
*   **Preparation:** Outline Drawings **must** be prepared by Design personnel.
*   **Checking:** The drawings **must** be checked by Process personnel.
*   **Approval:** Final approval **must** be granted by Mechanical personnel.

**Content Elements (Evolving Content)**
In addition to initial content, Outline Drawings will evolve to include:
*   A virtual copy of the equivalent Equipment Data Sheet.
*   Internal components in outline.
*   Plant elevation reference.
*   Gasket dimensions.

**Type D Equipment Drawings**
Drawings specifically for [[equipment_classification_types|Type D Equipment]] will contain fully dimensioned equipment elevations and plan views, along with itemised references to any required fabrication details provided on Detail Drawings.

**Usage and Control**
*   Outline drawings are considered a “controlling document” for Aker Kvaerner Chemetics plant design.
*   Dimensions on Outline Drawings will be kept updated with any changes from vendor detail design or fabrication considerations.
*   Outline Drawings will be used for Equipment manuals; vendor drawings will not be used for this purpose.

**Responsibilities**
*   **Prepared by:** Design
*   **Checked by:** Process
*   **Approved by:** Mechanical
