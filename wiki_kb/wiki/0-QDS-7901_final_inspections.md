# Final Inspections for MV Switchgear & DC Control Power Supply

A series of final inspections are required for the Medium Voltage Switchgear & DC Control Power Supply to ensure compliance with quality standards before shipment. These inspections **must** include Chemetics surveillance as specified.

**Inspection Requirements:**
*   **Visual Inspection:**
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance:** [[0-QDS-7901_chemetics_surveillance_level|Witness]], [[0-QDS-7901_chemetics_surveillance_level|Hold]], [[0-QDS-7901_chemetics_surveillance_level|Complete]]
*   **Cabinet Seam Welding:**
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance:** [[0-QDS-7901_chemetics_surveillance_level|Witness]], [[0-QDS-7901_chemetics_surveillance_level|Hold]]
*   **Dimensional Inspection:**
    *   **Quality Record:** [[0-QDS-7901_quality_record_definitions|FP]]
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance:** [[0-QDS-7901_chemetics_surveillance_level|Witness]], [[0-QDS-7901_chemetics_surveillance_level|Hold]]
*   **Surfaces Free from Defects:**
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance:** [[0-QDS-7901_chemetics_surveillance_level|Witness]], [[0-QDS-7901_chemetics_surveillance_level|Hold]]
*   **Physical Damage Inspection:**
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance:** [[0-QDS-7901_chemetics_surveillance_level|Witness]], [[0-QDS-7901_chemetics_surveillance_level|Hold]]
*   **Surface Hardware Inspection:**
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance:** [[0-QDS-7901_chemetics_surveillance_level|Witness]], [[0-QDS-7901_chemetics_surveillance_level|Hold]]
*   **Cleanliness Inspection:**
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance:** [[0-QDS-7901_chemetics_surveillance_level|Witness]], [[0-QDS-7901_chemetics_surveillance_level|Hold]]
*   **Nameplates Inspection:**
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance:** [[0-QDS-7901_chemetics_surveillance_level|Witness]]

These inspections are crucial for verifying the physical quality and readiness of the equipment.
