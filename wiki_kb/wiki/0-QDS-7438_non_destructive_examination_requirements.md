# Field Erected SS Tank Shop Component Non-Destructive Examination Requirements

This page details the Non-Destructive Examination (NDE) requirements for the Field Erected Stainless Steel Tank Shop Component (0-QDS-7438).

**General NDE Requirements:**
Non-Destructive Examination **shall** be performed in accordance with applicable API 650 codes, API 620 codes, or approved equivalents.

**Specific NDE Methods:**

*   **Radiography:**
    *   Applies to: Pressure envelope butt welds.
    *   Quality Record: [[quality_record_definitions|FP]] (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package).
    *   Chemetics Surveillance: Witness (Spot) and Complete, as detailed in [[0-QDS-7438_chemetics_surveillance_level]].

*   **Dye Penetrant:**
    *   Quality Record: [[quality_record_definitions|FP]] (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package).
    *   Chemetics Surveillance: Witness (25%) and Complete, as detailed in [[0-QDS-7438_chemetics_surveillance_level]].

These NDE procedures are critical to ensure the structural integrity and quality of the tank components, particularly for critical weld areas.
