# RLCS Vessel Materials of Construction

This page specifies the materials of construction for various components of the Rubber Lined Carbon Steel (RLCS) Vessel, as detailed in datasheet 4-DST-XXXX-R1. The selection of these materials is critical for ensuring the vessel's structural integrity, corrosion resistance, and longevity, especially given its service with 32% HCl.

**Primary Vessel Components:**
*   **Shell Material:** A-36 (Carbon Steel)
*   **Lining Material:** 6mm Bromo-Butyl Rubber. This lining is essential for corrosion protection against the acidic contents. For un-lined vessels, the material specification **shall** be "none".
*   **Roof Material:** A-36 (Carbon Steel)
*   **Floor Material:** A-36 (Carbon Steel)

**Nozzle and Connection Components:**
*   **Nozzle Flange Material:** A-105 (Forged Carbon Steel)
*   **Nozzle Neck Material:** A-53-E/B (Carbon Steel Pipe)
*   **Body Flange Material:** Not applicable for this vessel design.
*   **Coupling Material:** A-105 (Forged Carbon Steel)

**Internal and External Attachments:**
*   **Reinforcement Pad Material:** A-36 (Carbon Steel)
*   **Anchor Chair / Chair Plate Material:** A-36 (Carbon Steel)
*   **Vacuum Ring Material:** N/A (Not applicable for this vessel, as vacuum protection is via vent connection).
*   **Internal Attachment Material:** A-36 (Carbon Steel)
*   **Gasket Material:** Hold (Material to be determined or specified later, pending final design or vendor selection).

**Bolting Materials:**
*   **Anchor Bolts Material:** A-36 (Carbon Steel)
*   **Bolts Material:** A-307-B hot dip galv. (Hot-dip galvanized Carbon Steel bolts for corrosion resistance in outdoor environments).
*   **Nuts Material:** A-563-Gr.A hot dip galv. (Hot-dip galvanized Carbon Steel nuts, Grade A).

The combination of carbon steel for structural components and bromo-butyl rubber lining for internal surfaces provides a robust solution for the specified operating conditions. For detailed nozzle specifications, refer to [[rlcs_vessel_nozzle_schedule]].
