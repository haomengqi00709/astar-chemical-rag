# Protective Coating Repair Procedures (7-SPC-0005)

This section outlines the mandatory procedures for repairing damaged protective coatings on pipes and fittings. Effective repair is crucial to maintain the long-term integrity of the coating system.

**Surface Preparation for Repair**
*   All dirt, scale, and damaged coating **will be removed** from the affected area using wire brushes, grinders, or other suitable means.
*   Adjacent coating areas **will be feathered out** to ensure a smooth transition with the new repair material.
*   All dust and debris **will be wiped** from the pipe surface to ensure proper adhesion of the repair coating.

**Repair Application**
Repairs **will be** in accordance with the weld joint material and application requirements previously described in [[7-SPC-0005_polyken_tape_application]]. This ensures consistency in materials and application techniques for optimal performance.

**Post-Repair Testing**
After the repair material has been applied and cured as per manufacturer's recommendations, the repair areas **will be holiday tested** to ensure the integrity of the repaired coating. This re-testing confirms that no new voids or pinholes have been introduced and that the protective barrier is fully restored.

For initial surface preparation guidelines, refer to [[7-SPC-0005_surface_preparation_for_coatings]].

**Surface Preparation for Repair**
*   Mechanical cleaning to SSPC-SP3, Power-Tool Cleaning, is acceptable for all repairs.
*   Adjacent coating areas will be feathered out.
*   All dust will be wiped from the pipe prior to repair application.

**Repair Application**
*   Repairs will be in accordance with the weld joint material and application requirements previously described in this standard.
