# Aker Kvaerner Synergi Database

The Aker Kvaerner Synergi database is a central Group-wide system used for managing and reporting Health, Safety, and Environmental (HSE) information.

Key functions of the Synergi database include:
*   Recording Occupational Health and Safety (OHS) information.
*   Facilitating knowledge sharing across the entire Aker Kvaerner Group.
*   Producing comprehensive HSE performance statistics.
