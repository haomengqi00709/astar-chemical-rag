# Gas/Gas Heat Exchanger Design Temperature Calculations

This page details the calculated design temperatures for various components of the internal gas/gas heat exchanger, as specified in [[4-CAL-0008_document_details|4-CAL-0008]]. These design temperatures are critical for the safe and reliable operation of the equipment.

**Design Temperature Calculation Methodology**
The design temperature for each component is determined based on its [[gas_gas_heat_exchanger_maximum_metal_temperatures|maximum metal temperature]].
*   Design temperature **must** be calculated as the maximum metal temperature plus 25 C.
*   Design temperature results **must** be rounded to the nearest 5 degrees.

**Component Design Temperatures**
*   Design temperature for Top tubesheet: 355.0 C
*   Design temperature for Top expansion plate: 355.0 C
*   Design temperature for Shell: 635.0 C
*   Design temperature for Bottom seal plate: 540.0 C
*   Design temperature for Bottom tubesheet: 540.0 C
*   Design temperature for Tubes: 540.0 C
*   Design temperature for Bypass seal plate: 410.0 C

For a complete understanding of the assumptions and limitations applied during these calculations, refer to the [[gas_gas_heat_exchanger_design_temperature_calculation_commentary|Design Temperature Calculation Commentary]].
