# Dry Chlorine Piping - Assembly and Erection (5-SPC-0101)

Proper assembly and erection procedures are essential for the safe and efficient operation of dry chlorine piping systems.

**Piping Installation**
Piping **shall** be assembled and erected to minimize unintended cold springing and cold forcing. If designed layout dimensions differ from field dimensions, requiring cold springing or cold forcing even after field trim allowances, the erector **shall** make field modifications only after receiving instructions and approval from the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' site representative.

Piping **shall** be installed to permit free expansion and contraction, except where drawings specifically indicate an anchor or guide. The standard tolerance for pipe installation is ± 1/2 inch, unless approved by the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' site representative. Where a slope is indicated without dimensional values, the line is intended to be generally horizontal, without visible sags or reverse slopes, thus aiding drainage.

**Pipe Supports**
Pipe supports, hangers, and guides **should** be installed before the piping is erected wherever possible. Temporary supports **shall** be used where required. The pipe erector **should** anticipate and **shall** install some additional pipe supports, not shown on the drawings, as identified by the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' site representative, particularly to accept loads during [[5-SPC-0101_carbon_steel_dry_chlorine_piping_pressure_and_leak_testing]].

Refer to [[pipe_support_specification]] and [[field_supporting_of_pipe]] for more details.

**Cleanliness During Erection**
The pipe erector **shall** ensure that no debris, weld rod stubs, paper and plastic products, or any other material remains in any piping system. Each line **shall** be inspected and cleaned before any closing joint is assembled. Temporary covers **shall** be installed over open connections when work is not being immediately continued.

**Flange and Bolt Assembly**
All bolts **shall** be tightened with suitable wrenches only; hammering and bumping **shall not** be permitted for tightening bolts. Care **shall** be taken to ensure uniform pressure and avoid overstressing bolts or dishing flanges when tightening joints. Gaskets **shall** be carefully centred on the flange before tightening. Bolt lengths **shall** be approximately 1/4 inch past the nut after final tightening of the assembled joint. Bolt projection less than one exposed complete thread **shall not** be accepted.

Refer to [[bolting_specification]] and [[gasket_specification]] for material and installation specifics.

**Temporary Components**
"Dummy" sections **shall** be installed at selected locations for valves and other items as shown on the piping isometric drawings. These temporary items **shall** be removed and replaced with the final item at a time indicated in Appendix 1 of the original document.
