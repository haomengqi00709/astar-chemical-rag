# Blower Quality Data Sheet Chemetics Surveillance Level

This page specifies the surveillance levels to be applied by Chemetics for the Blower Package, as outlined in Document ID 0-QDS-7405-1. These levels dictate the extent of monitoring and inspection activities performed by Chemetics on vendor operations.

**Mandatory Requirements:**
*   Surveillance by Chemetics **shall** be to Level 2.0.
*   All surveillance activities by Chemetics **shall** be conducted as set out in this Quality Data Sheet.

**Specific Surveillance Levels:**
*   **Spot Surveillance:** Level 0.25
*   **Packing/Crating Surveillance:** Levels 1.0, 1.0

These surveillance levels are integral to ensuring the vendor's adherence to the [[0-QDS-7405-1_vendor_quality_assurance_requirements]] and the overall [[quality_management_and_compliance]] for the project. Further details on specific inspection and testing requirements can be found in [[0-QDS-7405-1_inspection_and_documentation_requirements]].
