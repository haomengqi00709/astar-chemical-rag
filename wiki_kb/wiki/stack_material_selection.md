# Stack Material Selection

This page outlines the "MATERIALS" category as specified in the [[4-dst-xxxx-r0_template_stack_datasheet_administration|Template Stack Datasheet]]. Proper material selection is critical for ensuring the long-term integrity, corrosion resistance, and structural performance of the stack under various operating and environmental conditions.

**Key Components and Their Material Designations:**
Materials are specified for the following primary stack components:
*   **Shell:** The main cylindrical or conical body of the stack. Material selection for the shell considers factors such as operating temperature, pressure, corrosive environment (internal and external), and structural loads. Common materials include carbon steel, stainless steel, or specialty alloys, often with internal linings or coatings.
*   **Platform, Ladders, Cages, Stairs:** These access structures require materials that provide adequate strength, corrosion resistance, and safety features. Galvanized carbon steel or stainless steel are common choices, depending on the environment.
*   **Base Plate:** The plate that connects the stack to its foundation. This material must be robust enough to transfer all structural loads to the foundation and resist corrosion at the interface.
*   **Grating:** Used for platforms and walkways, grating material must provide slip resistance and structural support. Galvanized steel or FRP (Fiber Reinforced Polymer) are typical options.
*   **Skirt:** The conical or cylindrical support structure at the base of the stack. Material selection is similar to the shell, considering structural loads and environmental exposure.
*   **Guy Wires:** If the stack is guyed, the wires require high tensile strength and corrosion resistance. Galvanized steel cables or stainless steel cables are typically used.
*   **Structure Steel:** Refers to any supporting structural elements, such as beams, columns, or bracing. Standard structural steel grades are common, with appropriate corrosion protection.
*   **Anchor Bolts:** Used to secure the base plate to the foundation. These must be high-strength, corrosion-resistant bolts, often specified with specific coatings or materials (e.g., stainless steel, galvanized steel).
*   **Insulation:** Material used to reduce heat loss from the stack or protect personnel from hot surfaces. Types vary based on operating temperature and environmental exposure (e.g., mineral wool, ceramic fiber).
*   **Washers:** Used with anchor bolts and other fasteners, washers must be compatible with the bolt material and provide adequate load distribution.

The selection process for these materials involves considering the [[stack_design_parameters|design temperatures]], [[stack_design_parameters|corrosion allowance]], and the specific chemical environment. For general material selection guidelines, refer to [[converter_material_selection]] (as an example for process equipment).
