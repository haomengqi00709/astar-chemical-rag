# Vertical Pump Field Inspection Documentation and Sign-off (4-FIC-XX11-R0)

The Vertical Pump Field Inspection Checklist (4-FIC-XX11-R0) includes specific requirements for documentation, recording deficiencies, and obtaining necessary approvals to ensure accountability and quality control.

**Deficiencies and Comments:**
The checklist provides 10 numbered slots for recording any deficiencies identified during the inspection, along with corresponding comments. It is **mandatory** to document all non-conformances or observations in these sections. This allows for a clear record of issues that require rectification before the pump can be accepted for operation. For managing non-conformances, refer to the [[0-NCR-7XXX_template_non_conformance_report]].

**Required Signatures and Dates:**
Formal sign-off is a critical step in the inspection process, indicating that the inspection has been completed and the findings acknowledged by all relevant parties. Signatures and dates **must** be obtained from the following entities:

*   **Aker Kvaerner Chemetics:** Represents the client or project management team, confirming their review and acceptance of the inspection results.
*   **Sub Contractor:** The installing subcontractor **must** sign off, acknowledging the completion of their work and the inspection findings. This aligns with [[subcontractor_quality_assurance_0_prc_7006]] and general [[vendor_quality_assurance]] requirements.
*   **Client:** The ultimate client or owner's representative **must** provide their signature, signifying final acceptance of the pump installation.

The requirement for multiple signatories ensures a comprehensive review and agreement on the status of the vertical pump installation, promoting transparency and shared responsibility across the project stakeholders. This documentation is essential for project records and future maintenance reference.
