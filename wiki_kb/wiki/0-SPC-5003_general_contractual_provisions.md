# General Contractual Provisions (0-SPC-5003)

Specification **0-SPC-5003** includes several general contractual provisions governing the execution of work and information management.

A dedicated "Site Works" section **shall** be utilized in instances where the Vendor is responsible for providing a portion of the Goods and Services directly on site. This section will detail specific requirements pertinent to [[construction_subcontract_site_information|on-site operations]].

Provisions are in place to regulate access for persons authorized to interact with the Goods and Services.

Strict provisions also exist concerning [[confidential_information_policy|Confidentiality]], Publicity, Photographs, and Sketches, ensuring proprietary and sensitive information is protected.

Compliance with all applicable [[applicable_law_and_compliance|Legislative Requirements]] **is mandatory** for the Vendor.

Furthermore, the Vendor **is required** to have access to up-to-date [[document_control_periodic_reports|construction documents]] to ensure accurate and compliant execution of the work.
