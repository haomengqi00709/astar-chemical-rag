# Contract Administration and Risk Management

**Contract Administration:**
Contract administration for the project will be the direct responsibility of the Project Manager.

**Contract Provisions and Risk Analysis:**
*   Key provisions of the Contract are summarised in Data available in SQL: 0-LST-2005.
*   Contract risks and their corresponding mitigating strategies are summarised in Data available in SQL: 0-LST-2006: Project Risk Analysis.

**Scope Change Control:**
Any deviation from the described risk mitigation strategies will be considered a change in project scope. All scope changes **must** be handled strictly in accordance with [[scope_change_control_0_prc_4003]] (0-PRC-4003: Scope Change Control).
