# Standard 0-PRC-8006 New Employee Orientation

Standard 0-PRC-8006 provides the framework and specific requirements for New Employee Orientation within the company. This document serves as the primary reference for conducting the initial Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) briefing for all new hires.

The content and procedures outlined in this standard are integral to ensuring that new employees are fully informed of the company's HSE policies, local safety information, and mandatory worksite safety rules before they begin their assignments.

This standard is directly referenced by the [[hse_new_employee_orientation]] procedure.
