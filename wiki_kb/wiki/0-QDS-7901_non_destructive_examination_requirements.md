# Non-Destructive Examination (NDE) for MV Switchgear & DC Control Power Supply

All Non-Destructive Examination (NDE) activities performed for the Medium Voltage Switchgear & DC Control Power Supply project **must** be conducted in accordance with ANSI/IEEE standards.

This requirement applies specifically if NDE is performed in conjunction with the following tests outlined in [[0-QDS-7901_equipment_testing]]:
*   Dielectric tests
*   Mechanical operation tests
*   Grounding of instrument transformer case tests
*   Electrical operation and control wiring test
*   Control wiring continuity (MV Switchgear)
*   Control wiring insulation (MV Switchgear)
*   Polarity verification (MV Switchgear)
*   Sequence tests
*   Operation test each circuit breaker and protection relay

Adherence to ANSI/IEEE standards for NDE ensures the integrity and reliability of the equipment.
