# Vessel and Heat Exchanger Design Details

As specified in document 4-SPC-0001, various design details for vessels and heat exchangers **must** adhere to specific standards to ensure safety, accessibility, and structural integrity.

**Access Manways**
The minimum size of access manways considered safe and practical for general access requirements **is** 600 mm diameter. If equipment is too small in diameter to comply with the 600 mm manway size requirement, then top entry **will be provided** via a full diameter removable tank top. This ensures adequate access for inspection and maintenance. For more on manways, see [[manway_covers_installation_and_inspection]].

**Lifting Lugs and Trunnions**
Vessels, heat exchangers, and tanks **will be fitted** with lifting lugs and/or trunnions. Each individual lug or trunnion **shall be designed** to support the total vessel weight. The weight of the vessel, including all internals, ladders, platforms, etc., and the maximum weight of the specified liquid contents, **will be calculated** and shown on the general arrangement drawing.

**Flanges**
Flanges **will be** in accordance with ASME B16.5. Flange ratings **will be specified** on the data sheets and general arrangement drawings. Flanges beyond the limit of ASME B16.5 or where the rating is not specified **will be designed** to meet the specified design code requirements. All flange faces for pressure connections **must have** a surface finish compatible with the selected gaskets. Flange bolt-holes **will straddle** vessel natural centerlines. For more on flanges, see [[flange_bolting_requirements]].

**Drains and Vents**
Liquid-filled vessels or sections of vessels **will be** either self-draining and venting or fitted with dedicated drain and vent connections. This facilitates complete drainage and safe venting during operation and maintenance.

**Anchor Bolts**
Anchor bolts will be designed in accordance with the applicable codes.

**Access Manways (Continued)**
If equipment is too small in diameter to comply with the 600 mm manway size requirement, then top entry will be provided via a full diameter removable tank top.

**Lifting Provisions**
Vessels, heat exchangers and tanks will be fitted with lifting lugs and/or trunnions.
Each individual lug or trunnion will be designed to support the total vessel weight.
The weight of the vessel including all internals, ladders, platforms, etc. and the maximum weight of the specified liquid contents, will be calculated and shown on the general arrangement drawing.

**Flange Specifications**
Flanges will be in accordance with ASME B16.5.
Flange ratings will be specified on the data sheets and general arrangement drawings.
Flanges beyond the limit of ASME B16.5 or where the rating is not specified will be designed to meet the specified design code requirements.
All flange faces for pressure connections will have a surface finish compatible with the selected gaskets.
Flange bolt-holes will straddle vessel natural centerlines.

**Draining and Venting**
Liquid filled vessels or sections of vessels will be either self draining and venting or fitted with drain and vent connections.
