# Plant Equipment Identification (4-PRC-0001)

The procedure for Plant Equipment Identification, designated as 4-PRC-0001, defines the system for assigning unique 4-digit standard equipment numbers to all equipment items within a plant. This procedure is critical for consistent equipment tagging and documentation across projects.

**Purpose:**
This procedure establishes the methodology for creating and managing the 4-digit standard equipment numbers. These numbers serve as a primary identifier for individual pieces of equipment, facilitating their tracking, maintenance, and integration into various engineering documents.

**Application:**
The 4-digit standard equipment number is a mandatory component for documents associated with single equipment items or primary equipment within an equipment package. It is directly referenced by the [[identification_number_rules]] within the [[document_numbering_procedure_0_prc_0002]].

**Key Aspects:**
*   **Unique Identification:** Ensures each equipment item has a distinct identifier.
*   **Cross-Referencing:** Enables seamless cross-referencing of equipment across drawings, data sheets, specifications, and other project documentation.
*   **Standardization:** Promotes a standardized approach to equipment identification throughout the firm's operations.

Understanding this procedure is essential for anyone involved in equipment design, procurement, installation, or maintenance, as it underpins the entire equipment documentation structure.
