# Confined Space Entry Permit (DP-802.705-1)

This page describes the Confined Space Entry Permit form, Document ID DP-802.705-1. This form is used to authorize and control entry into confined spaces, ensuring that all necessary safety precautions are identified and implemented before entry commences.

**Purpose:**
The Confined Space Entry Permit form serves as a critical checklist and authorization document to manage the risks associated with confined space entry. It ensures that atmospheric conditions are safe, rescue plans are in place, and all personnel involved are aware of the hazards and control measures.
