# Coated Pipe Handling, Storage, and Backfilling (7-SPC-0005)

Careful handling, storage, and backfilling procedures are essential to prevent damage to the protective coating on pipes and fittings, ensuring its long-term effectiveness.

**Handling Requirements**
*   Coated and wrapped pipe **will be handled** at all times with wide, nonabrasive slings, belts, or other equipment specifically designed to prevent damage to the coating.
*   The use of ropes, hooks, chains, or cables of any kind **is not permitted** directly on the coating.
*   End hooks may only be used on full-length pipe that has a 6-inch cutback at the ends, ensuring no contact with the coated surface.

**Transportation Guidelines**
During transport by rail, truck, or ship, pipe **will be supported** at the ends on padded cradles or sawdust-filled bags of adequate strength to prevent coating damage from contact or vibration.

**Storage Requirements**
*   Wrapped pipe **will not be allowed** to rest directly on the ground or on unpadded hard surfaces.
*   Coated and wrapped pipe **will be stored** on skids or on wide blocks equipped with resilient pads.
*   Storage methods **must prevent** any damage to the coating from contact points or environmental factors.
*   Stack heights for both storage and transportation **will be** in accordance with the recommendations provided by the pipe coater.

**Backfilling Procedures**
Backfill material placed adjacent to the coated and wrapped pipe **will be free** of scraps, sticks, rocks, or any other hard debris that could potentially damage the coating during placement or over time.

Adherence to these guidelines is crucial for maintaining the integrity of the [[7-SPC-0005_yellow_jacket_coating_system]] and [[7-SPC-0005_polyken_tape_application]].

**Handling Restrictions**
*   Use of ropes of any kind is not permitted on the coating.
*   Use of hooks, chains, or cables is not permitted on the coating.
*   End hooks may be used only on full-length pipe with 6-inch cutback at the ends.

**Transport and Storage**
*   During transport by rail, truck or ship, pipe will be supported at the ends on padded cradles or sawdust-filled bags of adequate strength.
*   Wrapped pipe will not be allowed to rest directly on the ground or on unpadded hard surfaces.
*   Stack heights for storage and transportation will be in accordance with recommendations of the pipe coater.
