# Protective Coating Curing and Repair Procedures (7-SPC-0001)

Proper curing and timely repair of protective coatings are essential for maintaining the integrity and performance of painted components, as outlined in `7-SPC-0001 Protective Coating Specification`.

**Curing Requirements**
A minimum curing time of 2 days at 21°C (70°F) and 50% relative humidity is recommended before exposing newly painted parts to service conditions, unless otherwise stated on the coating manufacturer's data sheet. It is important to note that several high-temperature coatings are extremely soft until they have reached a temperature of 150°C (300°F).

**Protection During Construction**
Painted surfaces **must** be protected from damage throughout the construction period. Any damaged or abraded areas **must** be promptly repaired to prevent degradation of the underlying material.

**Repair Procedures**
*   **Preparation for Welding:** Surfaces previously painted and subsequently welded **must** have all paint coatings completely removed from weld areas for a minimum of 50mm (2”) on either side of the repair area.
*   **Adhesion Test:** When deemed necessary, an adhesion test patch **will** be carried out before applying paint/coating to a previously painted surface.
*   **Repair Material and Application:** Material and application for all repairs **must** be as specified for new coatings. Repairs **must** provide protection equal to the undamaged areas.

Specific touch-up procedures are as follows:
*   **Shop and Field Touch-Up (No Rusting, Prime Coat Exposed):** Apply one or two finish coats to regain the specified thickness.
*   **Shop and Field Touch-Up (No Rusting, Prime Coat Damaged):** Clean the area to the base material and reapply the specified coating system.
*   **Shop and Field Touch-Up (Rust Areas Present):** After cleaning to the original specified standard of surface preparation, reapply the specified coating system.
*   **Shop and Field Touch-Up (Field Welds):** Power tool clean (or spot sandblast) the area and apply the specified paint system.

**Curing Requirements**
A minimum curing time of 2 days at 21°C (70°F) and 50% relative humidity is recommended before exposing newly painted parts to service conditions, unless stated otherwise on the coating manufacturers data sheet.

**Post-Erection Inspection and Initial Repair Steps**
After erection/installation but before top-coating, previously primed items must be examined for damage and unprimed areas.

**Repair Surface Preparation**
All oil and grease must first be removed in accordance with SSPC SP-1. Un-primed and damaged areas must be power tool cleaned in accordance with SSPC SP-11. Surfaces previously painted and subsequently welded must have all paint coatings completely removed from weld areas for a minimum of 50mm (2”) on either side of the repair area.

**Repair Application**
Un-primed and damaged areas must be re-coated prior to application of finish coat(s).

**Defect Repair**
Holidays, variations in gloss or texture, or other defects must be repaired to provide uniform coverage.

**Protection of Painted Surfaces**
Painted surfaces must be protected from damage throughout the construction period.

**General Repair Principles**
Damaged or abraded areas must be promptly repaired. Material and application for repairs must be as specified for new coatings. Repairs must provide protection equal to the undamaged areas.

**Specific Repair Procedures**
*   **For shop and field touch up where no rusting but prime coat exposed:** Apply one or two finish coats to regain specified thickness.
*   **For shop and field touch up where no rusting but prime coat damaged:** Clean area to base material and reapply the specified coating.
*   **For shop and field touch up where rust areas are present:** After cleaning to the original specified standard of surface preparation, reapply the specified coating.
*   **For shop and field touch up of field welds:** Power tool clean (or spot sandblast) and apply the specified paint system.
