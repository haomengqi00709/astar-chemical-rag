# PEM Milestone Gate Review Process

The [[0-prc-2101_project_execution_model_gate_reviews_overview|Project Execution Model (PEM) Milestone Gate Review]] process is structured into distinct preparation and review phases, designed to systematically assess project performance and identify necessary actions.

**Preparation Phase**
*   **Typical Duration:** The typical preparation time for a Gate Review ranges from 1 to 3 days, depending on the overall size and complexity of the project.
*   **Activities:**
    *   The [[pem_gate_review_facilitator_roles|PEM Gate Review Facilitator]] prepares the template for the control object status checklist.
    *   [[discipline_lead_and_approver_roles|Discipline Leads]] report the actual status of their respective control objects on the checklist.
    *   Validation of these checklists is performed by the Discipline Manager.
    *   Updates are made to key project documents, including the Project Manager's Report, [[project_schedule_and_milestones|Project Schedule]], Cost Reports, Risk Reports, Change Notifications, HSE Status, and the Action Plan from the previous gate review.

**Review Phase**
*   **Typical Duration:** The typical review time for a Gate Review ranges from 1 to 2 days, depending on the project size.
*   **Participants:** Reports (Project Manager's Report, Project Schedule, Cost Reports, Risk Reports, Change Notifications, HSE Status, and Action Plan from the previous gate review) are reviewed by the [[project_manager_pem_gate_review_responsibilities|Project Manager]], [[project_engineer_pem_gate_review_responsibilities|Project Engineer]], and the [[pem_gate_review_facilitator_roles|PEM Gate Review Facilitator]]. Other relevant personnel **will** be requested to attend as appropriate.
*   **Activities:**
    *   Selective probing and spot checks are conducted to verify information.
    *   Discussion and agreement on major findings and required actions take place among the review participants.
    *   Preparation and issuance of the final report occurs as described in the [[pem_milestone_gate_review_reporting_and_follow_up|reporting requirements]].

**Preparation Phase**
*   **Template Preparation:** The PEM Gate Review Facilitator prepares the template of the control object status check list.
*   **Status Reporting:** Discipline Leads report the actual status of the control objects on the check list.
*   **Validation:** Validation of the check lists by the Discipline Manager occurs.
*   **Document Updates:** Updates of the Project Manager's Report, Project Schedule, Cost Reports, Risk Reports, Change Notifications, HSE Status, and Action Plan from the previous gate review occur.

**Review Phase**
*   **Report Review:** During the Review, reports (Project Manager's Report, Project Schedule, Cost Reports, Risk Reports, Change Notifications, HSE Status, and Action Plan from the previous gate review) are reviewed by the Project Manager, Project Engineer, and PEM Gate Review Facilitator.
*   **Participant Attendance:** Others will be requested to attend the Review as appropriate.
*   **Assessment:** Selective probing and spot checks occur during the Review.
*   **Findings and Actions:** Discussion and agreement of major findings and actions required occur during the Review.

**Typical Timelines**
*   **Preparation Time:** Typically 1 – 3 days, depending upon project size.
*   **Review Time:** Typically 1 – 2 days, depending upon project size.
