# Inline Mixer Mechanical Design Requirements

The mechanical design of the Inline Mixer, as detailed in [[4-DST-XXXX-R0_template_inline_mixer_datasheet_administration]], encompasses a range of critical specifications and mandatory requirements to ensure safe and efficient operation.

**General Mechanical Design Features:**
*   The mixer's elements **can be removable**, allowing for maintenance or replacement.
*   **Corrosion Allowance** is a critical parameter, measured in millimeters (mm). The datasheet provides two distinct fields for specifying corrosion allowance.
*   **Shell material specification** **shall** be provided, detailing the material used for the main body of the mixer.
*   **Elements material specification** **shall** also be provided, outlining the materials for the internal mixing components.

**Key Design Parameters:**
*   **Design Internal Pressure:** Measured in kPa(g) (kilopascals gauge), defining the maximum pressure the equipment can safely withstand internally.
*   **Design Vacuum:** Measured in kPa(g), specifying the maximum vacuum conditions the equipment can safely endure.
*   **Design Maximum Temperature:** Measured in °C, indicating the highest temperature the equipment is designed to operate at.
*   **Design Minimum Temperature:** Measured in °C, indicating the lowest temperature the equipment is designed to operate at.

**Mandatory Mechanical Design Requirements:**
The following aspects are explicitly designated as mandatory mechanical design requirements for the inline mixer:
*   **Overpressure Protection:** Systems or features to prevent internal pressure from exceeding design limits.
*   **Vacuum Protection:** Systems or features to prevent internal vacuum from exceeding design limits.
*   **Registration:** Compliance with relevant regulatory registration requirements.
*   **Design Code:** Adherence to specified engineering design codes (e.g., ASME, API).
*   **Equipment Specification:** Conformance to detailed equipment specifications.
*   **General Equipment Requirements:** Fulfillment of overarching equipment standards.
*   **Fabrication:** Standards and procedures for manufacturing the mixer.
*   **Lining:** Specifications for any internal linings required for corrosion resistance or other purposes.
*   **Welding:** Requirements for welding procedures, qualifications, and inspection.
*   **Site Conditions:** Design considerations based on the specific environmental and operational conditions of the installation site.
*   **Wind Design Code:** Compliance with codes governing resistance to wind loads. See [[wind_design_requirements]].
*   **Seismic Design Code:** Compliance with codes governing resistance to seismic activity. See [[seismic_design_requirements]].
*   **Connection Type:** Specifications for how the mixer connects to piping or other equipment.
*   **Equipment Location:** Design considerations related to the physical placement of the equipment.
*   **Painting:** Requirements for external coatings and finishes.
