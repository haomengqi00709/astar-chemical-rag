# Special Shipping and Handling Equipment (0-SPC-5004)

The Vendor **must** design and supply all items required for shipment and handling, such as supports, special bracing, and spreader bars.

**Drawings and Approval:**
The Vendor **must** provide drawings detailing all such items for the approval of [Company Name] before their manufacture.

**Shipping Saddles:**
*   Shipping saddles **will** be designed to withstand all imposed loads.
*   Shipping saddles **will** be designed to prevent damage or permanent distortion to the goods.
*   The minimum contact arc length for shipping saddles **will** be 120°.

**Securing Goods:**
Tensioned steel bands, straps, wire rope, or other suitable means **will** be used to keep goods securely attached to supports during handling and transporting. Protection **must** be provided at band locations to prevent damage to painted items.

These requirements are crucial for the safe transport of [[0-SPC-5004_over_dimensional_load_requirements|over-dimensional loads]] and are part of the [[0-SPC-5004_packaging_materials_and_construction|Packaging Materials and Construction]] guidelines.
