# ASME B16.5 Pipe Flanges and Flanged Fittings Standard

ASME B16.5 is a standard that covers pressure-temperature ratings, materials, dimensions, tolerances, marking, testing, and methods of designating openings for pipe flanges and flanged fittings. This standard is crucial for ensuring the integrity and compatibility of piping components in various industrial applications.

When revising bolting materials, it is **mandatory** to consult the requirements specified in this standard to ensure compliance and suitability for the intended service.
