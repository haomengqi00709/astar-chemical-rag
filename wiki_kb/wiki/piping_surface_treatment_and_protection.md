# Piping Surface Treatment and Protection

Proper surface treatment, including insulation and protective coatings, is vital for the longevity, operational efficiency, and safety of piping systems.

**Insulation Requirements:**
Piping **will** be insulated in accordance with the [[piping_line_list]] (5-LST-0001) and the [[insulation_specification_6_spc_0001|Specification for Insulation (6-SPC-0001)]]. Insulation helps maintain process temperatures, prevents heat loss or gain, and provides personnel protection.

**Painting Requirements:**
*   **Carbon Steel and Lined Carbon Steel Pipe:** These pipes **will** be painted in accordance with the [[piping_line_list]] (5-LST-0001) and the [[protective_coating_specification]] (7-SPC-0001). This provides corrosion protection and visual identification.
*   **Plastic Pipe:** Plastic pipe located outdoors or in areas exposed to direct sunlight **will** be painted with an UV inhibiting latex paint. This painting **will** also conform to the [[piping_line_list]] (5-LST-0001) and the [[protective_coating_specification]] (7-SPC-0001). UV protection is essential to prevent material degradation from solar radiation.

**Touch-Up Responsibility:**
The Piping Subcontractor **will** be responsible for the touch-up of paint on equipment, piping, and structural steel where damage has occurred during piping installation activities. This ensures that protective coatings remain intact across the entire installation.

---
**Painting Requirements**
Carbon steel pipe and lined carbon steel pipe **will** be painted in accordance with Piping Line List, 5-LST-0001, and Specification for Protective Coating, 7-SPC-0001.

Plastic pipe located outdoors or in locations where direct sunlight can occur **will** be painted with an UV inhibiting latex paint. Plastic pipe painting **will** be in accordance with Piping Line List, 5-LST-0001, and Specification for Protective Coatings, 7-SPC-0001.

**Subcontractor Responsibility for Touch-up**
The Piping Subcontractor **will** be responsible for touch up of paint on equipment, piping and structural steel where damage has occurred during piping installation.
