# Subcontractor Quality Assurance (Procedure 0-PRC-7006)

This document, identified as 0-PRC-7006, is a Procedure outlining the requirements for Agencies supplying Third Party Inspection Services to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. Its primary purpose is to ensure that these agencies submit and complete all necessary documentation as per company standards. The procedure details the specific documents that Third Party Inspection Agencies are required to submit and/or complete during their service.

This procedure forms an integral part of the broader [[vendor_quality_assurance]] framework and ensures compliance with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' quality standards for external inspection services.

The procedure describes the documentation requirements for Third Party Inspection Agencies, covering various stages of inspection activities, reporting, and financial administration.
