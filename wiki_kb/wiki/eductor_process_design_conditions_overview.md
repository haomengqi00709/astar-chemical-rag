# Eductor Process Design Conditions

The **PROCESS DESIGN CONDITIONS** section of the Eductor Datasheet (4-PDS-XXXX-R1) outlines the critical parameters for the eductor's operation, aligning with [[process_design_criteria_1_prc_0001]].

Key fields within this section include:
*   **Case Description:** Describes the specific operating scenario or design case for which the eductor is being specified.
*   **Design Basis:** Specifies the foundational criteria, assumptions, and standards used for the eductor's design.

Detailed conditions are provided for both the [[eductor_pumped_fluid_operating_conditions]] and the [[eductor_motive_fluid_operating_conditions]], ensuring a comprehensive understanding of the fluid dynamics and properties relevant to the eductor's performance.
