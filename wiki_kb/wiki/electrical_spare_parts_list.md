# Electrical Spare Parts List

The Electrical Spare Parts List details recommended spare parts for major electrical equipment, crucial for plant maintenance and operational continuity.

**Content of the Spare Parts List**
The spare parts list **shall** contain:
*   The equipment name.
*   The tag number.
*   Catalogue numbers for each part.
*   A full description of the part.
*   The manufacturer’s name of each part.
*   Quantities of each spare part.

**Procurement and Review**
Vendors **shall** submit priced recommended spare parts lists with major electrical equipment at the time of quotation. Depending on project instructions, spare parts may or may not be included with the equipment purchase order; they may be purchased separately. The spare parts list **shall** be checked by the Project Engineer to ensure it aligns with the project Contract.
