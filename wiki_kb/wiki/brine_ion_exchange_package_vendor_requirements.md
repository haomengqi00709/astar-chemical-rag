# Brine Ion Exchange Package Vendor Requirements

This page consolidates the specific requirements and obligations for vendors supplying the Brine Ion Exchange Package, as derived from Document ID 4-PDS-XXXX-R1. Adherence to these requirements is mandatory for all proposals and supplied equipment.

**Regeneration Schedule**
*   **Note 2:** Vendor **will supply** a complete regeneration schedule with their proposal. This regeneration schedule **must** include chemical dilution and usage rates.

**Pressure Drop Information**
*   **Note 3:** Vendor **must** provide pressure drop at both normal and design flow for the entire system (including piping) with both columns on line.
*   **Note 3:** Vendor **must** confirm the required inlet pressure to achieve the specified outlet pressure at design flow.
*   **Note 3:** Vendor **should** keep the maximum system pressure drop below 150 kPa.

**Operating Temperature Guarantees**
*   **Note 4:** Vendor **must** confirm the operating temperature range for which the performance is guaranteed.
*   **Note 4:** Vendor **must** provide the maximum allowable operating temperature for resin integrity.

**Outlet Brine Specifications Confirmation**
*   **Note 8:** Outlet brine specifications **are to be confirmed** when the final quote from the cell supplier and final membrane selection are obtained. Vendors **must** acknowledge this requirement and be prepared to adjust their proposals accordingly.

These requirements are critical for ensuring the [[brine_ion_exchange_package_performance_requirements|performance]] and [[brine_ion_exchange_package_equipment_and_operating_data|operational integrity]] of the Brine Ion Exchange Package. Vendors should also refer to [[brine_ion_exchange_package_feed_brine_specifications]] for detailed feed brine characteristics.
