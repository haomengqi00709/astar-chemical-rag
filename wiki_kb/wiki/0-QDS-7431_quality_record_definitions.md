# 0-QDS-7431 Quality Record Definitions

This page defines the terminology used for quality record requirements within [[0-QDS-7431_acid_brick_lining_quality_data_sheet_administration]]. These definitions are associated with item 4.0 in the source document.

*   **AP ("As Prepared"):** This designation indicates that a quality record is required to be submitted "As Prepared" by the vendor.
*   **FP ("Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package"):** This designation indicates that a quality record is required to be "Included in the Quality Assurance Documentation Package" submitted by the vendor.

These definitions are crucial for understanding the documentation requirements outlined in [[0-QDS-7431_inspection_and_documentation_requirements]]. For a broader understanding of quality record management, refer to [[quality_data_sheets_management]] and other specific QDS documents like [[0-QDS-7404-2_quality_record_definitions]].
