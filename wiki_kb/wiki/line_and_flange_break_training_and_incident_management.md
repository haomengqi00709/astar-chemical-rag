# Line and Flange Break Training and Incident Management

This page outlines the mandatory training requirements for personnel involved in line and flange break operations, as well as procedures for incident and near-miss management.

**Training Requirements**
Each employee who is authorized to perform first breaks shall be thoroughly trained in all aspects of these guidelines. Everyone must go through Line Breaking Training provided by the Commissioning HSE Manager. Training can be conducted on Site or in the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics office. All training shall be documented and entered into the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics log book maintained by the Commissioning HSE Manager. Employees performing the first break shall also be trained on the proper use and limitations of the Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) required for the break.

**Incident and Near Miss Management**
All Line Breaking/Equipment Opening incidents and near misses shall be thoroughly investigated and documented to prevent recurrence and improve safety procedures.
