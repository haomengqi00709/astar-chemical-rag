# Quality Record Definitions (0-QDS-7416)

For the Plate & Frame Heat Exchanger project (**0-QDS-7416**), specific abbreviations are used to define the requirements for quality records:

*   **AP (As Prepared):** This designation indicates that the quality record is required to be prepared by the vendor.
*   **FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package):** This designation signifies that the quality record **shall** be included as part of the comprehensive Quality Assurance Documentation Package submitted by the vendor.

These definitions apply to various documents and reports throughout the project lifecycle, including [[0-QDS-7416_material_certifications|material certifications]], [[0-QDS-7416_final_inspections|final inspections]], [[0-QDS-7416_pressure_testing|pressure testing]], and [[0-QDS-7416_packing_and_shipping_requirements|packing documentation]].
