# Cost Tabulation (0-TAB-5) Financial Details

The Cost Tabulation document (0-TAB-5) provides a structured overview of project-related costs, facilitating financial evaluation and decision-making. All costs within this document are expressed in **CAD (Canadian Dollars)**.

**Cost Components:**
*   **Cost Tabulation ID:** xxxxxx
*   **Total Quoted Cost in CAD:** 0.0 (This figure is subject to [[0-tab-5_foreign_exchange_rates|Equalization Costs]] adjustments).
*   **Subtotal Additional Items:** 0.0. It is important to note that **Additional Items are not included in the Grand Total** calculation. These represent supplementary costs that may be considered separately.
*   **Subtotal Equalization Costs:** 0.0. [[0-tab-5_foreign_exchange_rates|Equalization Costs]] can be modified or added as required to account for various financial adjustments, such as currency fluctuations or specific project incentives.
*   **Grand Total Cost Quoted/Converted in CAD:** 0.0. This represents the final calculated cost after all applicable adjustments.

**Mandatory Requirements:**
*   **Recommended Price(s) must be highlighted in the Grand Total Cost Index** to clearly indicate preferred options or benchmarks during the evaluation process.

This structured approach ensures transparency and accuracy in financial reporting for project procurement. For further details on cost control, refer to [[project_cost_control]].
