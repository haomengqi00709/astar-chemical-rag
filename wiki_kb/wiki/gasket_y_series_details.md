# Gasket Y Series (Spiral Wound)

This section details the specifications for Gasket Y1 and Gasket Y2, both of which are spiral wound gasket types.

### Gasket Y1
*   **Construction:** Spiral wound 304 SS with graphite filler, featuring 304 SS inner and outer rings.
*   **Uncompressed Thickness:** 4.5 mm
*   **Compressed Thickness:** 3.2 mm

### Gasket Y2
*   **Construction:** Spiral wound 316 SS with graphite filler, featuring a 316 SS inner ring and a carbon steel outer ring.
*   **Uncompressed Thickness:** 4.5 mm
*   **Compressed Thickness:** 3.2 mm
