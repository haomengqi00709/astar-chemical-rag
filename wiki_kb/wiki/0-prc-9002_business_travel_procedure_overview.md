# Business Travel Procedure Overview (0-PRC-9002)

This procedure, identified as 0-PRC-9002, outlines the comprehensive process for requesting, authorizing, and booking business travel within the company. It details the necessary steps for travellers to initiate travel requests, the required authorizations, and the mechanisms for making travel bookings.

All business travel undertaken must comply with the requirements stipulated in the 11.1 PR 2 Group Travel Procedure. This foundational document, accessible via the eNet, mandates that travellers "behave as though you were paying for the journey yourself" and requires the use of the most cost-effective airlines and routing available. Adherence to these principles is a mandatory requirement for all business travel.

The procedure ensures that all aspects of business travel, from initial request to final booking, are managed efficiently and in line with company policies. It also provides guidelines for handling deviations from standard travel policies, ensuring proper justification and authorization are obtained.

Key aspects covered by this procedure include:
*   Completing the [[travel_authorization_form_details_0_frm_9002]].
*   Understanding [[travel_policy_compliance_and_deviations]] with the Group Travel Procedure.
*   Adhering to [[travel_class_and_ticket_restrictions]].
*   Navigating the [[travel_authorization_approvals_0_frm_9002]] process.
*   The [[travel_request_form_submission_and_distribution]] protocols.
*   The [[travel_booking_process_and_agency_guidelines]].
*   Accessing [[travel_health_and_documentation_support]].
*   Procedures for [[travel_authorization_changes]].

This document serves as a central reference for all employees involved in business travel, ensuring consistency, compliance, and cost-effectiveness.
