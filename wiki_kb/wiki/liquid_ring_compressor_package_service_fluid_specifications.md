# Liquid Ring Compressor Package Service Fluid Specifications

This page details the mandatory service fluid specifications for the Liquid Ring Compressor Package, as outlined in Datasheet 4-PKG-XXXX-R1. The service fluid, often a sealant or cooling medium, is crucial for the operation and performance of the liquid ring compressor.

**Mandatory Service Fluid Requirements**
The following parameters **must** be specified for the service fluid:

*   **Fluid Description:** A clear description of the service fluid (e.g., water, oil).
*   **Supply Pressure:** The pressure at which the service fluid is supplied, in kPa(g).
*   **Supply Temperature:** The temperature at which the service fluid is supplied, in °C.
*   **Vapour Press. @ Supply Temp.:** The vapour pressure of the service fluid at its supply temperature, in kPa(a).
*   **Mass Flow Rate:** The required mass flow rate of the service fluid, in kg/h.
*   **Max quantity in exit gas stream:** The maximum allowable quantity of service fluid in the exit gas stream, in %w/w (percent by weight).
*   **VTA (Vapour Temperature at Ambient?):** This field requires clarification regarding the vapour temperature at ambient conditions.
*   **Once Through:** Indication if the service fluid is used in a once-through system or recirculated.
*   **Temperature at Discharge:** The temperature of the service fluid at discharge, in °C.
*   **VTA (Vapour Temperature at Ambient?):** This field requires clarification regarding the vapour temperature at ambient conditions.

**Mandatory Connection Requirement**
*   The **Service Liquid Connection** is a required field and **must** be specified.

These specifications ensure compatibility and optimal performance of the service fluid within the compressor system. For general utility fluid design conditions, refer to [[utility_fluid_design_conditions]].
