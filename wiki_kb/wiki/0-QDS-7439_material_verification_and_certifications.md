# Scrubber Venturi Internals Material Verification and Certifications

This section outlines the requirements for material verification and certifications for the Scrubber Venturi Internals, ensuring that all materials used meet the specified quality and composition standards.

**Material Verification:**
*   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Complete. Chemetics **will** perform a complete surveillance of the material verification process.

**Mill Test Certificates:**
*   **Requirement:** Mill Test Certificates **are required** as [[0-QDS-7439_quality_record_definitions|Quality Record FP]] (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package).
*   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Complete. Chemetics **will** perform a complete surveillance of the Mill Test Certificates.

**Weld Consumable Certificates:**
*   **Requirement:** Weld consumable certs. **are required** as [[0-QDS-7439_quality_record_definitions|Quality Record FP]] (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package).
*   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Complete. Chemetics **will** perform a complete surveillance of the weld consumable certificates.

These stringent requirements for material verification and certifications are in place to guarantee the integrity and performance of the Scrubber Venturi Internals, particularly given the critical nature of their application.
