# General Instrument Requirements

All instruments **shall** meet specific design and construction criteria to ensure reliability, safety, and compatibility with the plant environment.

**Electrical Housing:**
The electrical housing of all instruments **shall** be, as a minimum, Epoxy coated Aluminium or a suitably UV stabilised and chemically resistant non-metallic material. This ensures durability in potentially corrosive or harsh environments, as described in [[environmental_and_site_conditions|Environmental and Site Conditions]].

**Process Alarm and Trip Applications:**
Transmitters **shall** be considered for process alarm and trip applications. This consideration **shall** take into account minimum effective design principles to ensure safety and operational integrity.

**Nameplates and Identification:**
Instruments **shall** be identified with permanently attached stainless steel nameplates. These nameplates **will** be provided as per vendor standard. Wherever possible, the means of attaching nameplates **shall** be by stainless steel screws or rivets to ensure longevity and resistance to environmental factors.

**Typical information on instrument vendor nameplates includes:**
*   Manufacturer
*   Model number
*   Serial number
*   Size
*   Rating
*   Date of manufacture

For specific nameplate requirements for control valves, refer to [[control_valves_general|Control Valves - General]].

**Explosion Protection:**
All explosion-protected (Ex) items of equipment **shall** be fitted with permanently attached stainless steel labels, clearly indicating their certification and suitability for hazardous areas.

**RFI Immunity:**
Instruments **will** be RFI (Radio Frequency Interference) immune. They **will** comply with SAMA PMC 33.1C (IEC 801-3) or an equivalent standard for RFI immunity. This standard applies in the frequency range from 20 to 1000 MHz, for field intensity up to 10 mV/m. This ensures reliable operation in environments with potential electromagnetic disturbances.
