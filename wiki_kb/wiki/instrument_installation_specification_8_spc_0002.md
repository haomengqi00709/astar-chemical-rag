# Instrument Installation Specification (8-SPC-0002)

The [[instrument_installation_specification_8_spc_0002]] (8-SPC-0002) defines the general and technical requirements for the installation of instrumentation equipment.

**Content Requirements:**
Instrument Installation Specification (8-SPC-0002) **shall** contain, as a minimum:
*   General and technical requirements for installation of Instrumentation equipment.
*   Reference to the required Codes and Regulations which are to be adhered to.
*   Reference to the Scope of Work including the [[8-lst-0002_instrument_index]] complete with cross-referencing to [[instrument_installation_details_8_dtl_xxxx]].
*   Definition of supply of bulk materials as indicated in the [[instrument_installation_details_8_dtl_xxxx]].

**Document Preparation and Approval:**
*   **Prepared by:** Detail Design Engineer (DDE)
*   **Approved by:** Detail Design Engineer (DDE)

**Purpose and Related Documents:**
The Instrument Installation Specification will describe the standard of work that is required. It **is to be read in conjunction with** document [[0-lst-6x90-1_construction_subcontract_supply_and_erection_scope_instrumentation]], which is the applicable site-specific Construction Subcontract Supply and Erection Scope document.
