# Instrument Cable Schedule (8-LST-0004)

The Instrument Cable Schedule (8-LST-0004) is a comprehensive list detailing all cables used for instrumentation within a project.

**Content Requirements:**
Instrument Cable Schedule (8-LST-0004) **shall** contain, as a minimum:
*   Cable Number.
*   Type of cable (power, control, instrumentation).
*   Cable Specification.
*   Approximate Length.
*   Point of Origin.
*   Point of termination.
*   Cable fitting specification and quantity of fittings.
*   Remarks (as required).

**Document Preparation and Approval:**
*   **Prepared by:** Detail Design Engineer (DDE)
*   **Approved by:** Detail Design Engineer (DDE)

**Development and Coordination:**
The DDE will provide the Instrument Cable Schedule. This schedule **shall** include all cables depicted in the [[8-lop-xxxx_instrument_loop_diagrams]] and all cables depicted in control system interconnection drawings.

The Instrument Cable Schedule will be provided to Electrical. Electrical will specify all required cable, cable connectors, conduit, and cable tray. The schedule will clearly define any specific colour requirements for the varying cable types.

**As-Built Documentation:**
As-built commissioning documents **will be provided** for the Instrument Cable Schedule (as applicable).
