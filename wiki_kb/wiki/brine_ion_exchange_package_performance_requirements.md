# Brine Ion Exchange Package Performance Requirements

This page details the performance criteria and output specifications for the Brine Ion Exchange Package, as specified in Document ID 4-PDS-XXXX-R1. Adherence to these requirements is mandatory for the successful operation of the system.

**Outlet Brine Quality Specifications**
The treated brine exiting the system **must** meet the following impurity limits:
*   **Outlet Brine Ca and Mg:** 20 max ppb as Ca
*   **Outlet Brine Sr:** 50 max ppb
*   **Outlet Brine Ba:** 500 max ppb
*   **Outlet Brine Fe:** 200 max ppb
*   **Outlet Brine Mn:** 10 max ppb

**System Pressure and Flow Requirements**
*   **System Pressure Drop:** 150 max kPa
    *   **Note 3:** Vendor **must** provide pressure drop at both normal and design flow for the entire system (including piping) with both columns on line. Vendor **must** confirm required inlet pressure to achieve required outlet pressure at design flow. Vendor **should** keep max pressure drop below 150 kPa.
