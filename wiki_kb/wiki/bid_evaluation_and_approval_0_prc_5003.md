# Bid Evaluation and Approval (0-PRC-5003)

This document, 0-PRC-5003, outlines the procedure for the evaluation of bids received from vendors and the subsequent approval process for vendor selection. It ensures a fair and transparent selection process based on technical and commercial criteria.

Vendors will be selected in accordance with this procedure.
