**Inspection Requirements:**
The following items represent mandatory requirements that **must** be verified during the field inspection of the Electrostatic Precipitator. Completion of these items ensures compliance with design specifications and operational safety.

*   **Item 1.0:** Correct Nameplate installed.
*   **Item 2.0:** Foundation/Anchor bolts secure and tightened. Refer to [[civil_works_design_criteria_2_prc_0001]] for foundation requirements.
*   **Item 3.0:** Vessel internals (dip pipes, stand pipes, baffles, nozzles, vortex breakers etc.) installed. See [[vessel_internals_installation_requirements]].
*   **Item 4.0:** All Platforms, hand rails, walkways, stairs & ladders installed. Refer to [[structural_and_architectural_design_criteria_3_prc_0001]].
*   **Item 5.0:** Safety gates installed & functioning. Adherence to [[worksite_safety_rules_general]] is mandatory.
*   **Item 6.0:** All [[non_destructive_examination_requirements|NDT inspections]] completed and records available.
*   **Item 7.0:** Control panel (in MCC) installed and ready for service. Refer to [[control_equipment_cabinet_layouts]] or [[mcc_switchgear_room_layouts]].
*   **Item 8.0:** Internal inspection completed by vendor representative (attach report). This report **must** be attached.
*   **Item 9.0:** Vessel is clean and empty.
*   **Item 10.0:** Internal painting or lining completed (attach inspection reports). Inspection reports **must** be attached. Refer to [[coating_and_lining_inspection]].
*   **Item 11.0:** Final internal inspection completed with client (attach closure certificate). A closure certificate **must** be attached. See [[final_inspections]].
*   **Item 12.0:** Manway covers installed & tightened (multiple). Refer to [[manway_covers_installation_and_inspection]].
*   **Item 13.0:** External painting completed. Refer to [[coatings_erection_0_spc_6x97]].
*   **Item 14.0:** Insulation & cladding complete. Refer to [[insulation_erection_0_spc_6x96]].
*   **Item 15.0:** Grounding completed (possible multiple locations). Refer to [[grounding_installation_details]].
*   **Item 16.0:** Electrical insulation verified (High voltage system). Refer to [[electrical_installation_specification]] and [[electrical_systems_field_checkout_9_prc_0004]].
*   **Item 17.0:** External inspection completed by vendor representative (attach report). This report **must** be attached. See [[vendor_quality_assurance]].
*   **Item 18.0:** Safety Signs installed as per supplier's instructions. Refer to [[hse_site_postings_overview]].
