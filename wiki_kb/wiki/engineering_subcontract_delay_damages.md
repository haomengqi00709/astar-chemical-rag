# Engineering Subcontract Delay Damages

This page outlines the provisions for delay damages in Engineering Subcontracts, as defined within the commercial terms.

**Calculation and Rate**
*   Delay damages for the whole of the services are [specify rate] per day.
*   The rate of delay damages may be expressed as a percentage (%) of the Price.
*   The percentage (%) of Price for delay damages **must** be a genuine estimate of the damage suffered by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
*   A fixed monetary sum for delay damages is preferable if different rates **will** apply to documents and Completion.
