# Industrial Relations Management (0-SPC-5003)

Effective Industrial Relations Management is a mandatory area of [[compliance_and_standards|compliance]] under Specification **0-SPC-5003**, particularly for Vendors providing Goods and Services. The Vendor **must** adhere to the following requirements:

*   **Compliance with Labour Agreements:** All applicable labour agreements **shall** be strictly observed.
*   **Payment of Employees Wages and Benefits:** Timely and accurate payment of all employee wages and benefits **is required**.
*   **Workers Compensation Insurance:** Adequate Workers Compensation Insurance **must** be maintained for all employees.
*   **Compliance with Site Rules:** Strict adherence to all established [[worksite_safety_rules_general|Site Rules]] **is mandatory**.
*   **Compliance with Site Industrial Relations Strategy:** The Vendor **shall** comply with the overall [[subcontractor_industrial_relations_management_0_spc_60|Site Industrial Relations Strategy]].
*   **Compliance with Labour Mobilisation/Induction Requirements:** All requirements related to labour mobilisation and induction **must** be met.

These provisions ensure a stable and compliant working environment, aligning with the broader [[subcontractor_management_plans_0_spc_600|subcontractor management plans]].
