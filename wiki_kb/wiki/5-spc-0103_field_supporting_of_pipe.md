# Field Supporting of Pipe (5-SPC-0103)

The Field Supporting of Pipe specification, identified as 5-SPC-0103, provides essential guidelines for the installation of pipe supports that are not explicitly detailed on engineering drawings. This document ensures that all piping, regardless of whether its support is pre-engineered or field-routed, receives adequate and appropriate structural support.

**Content Requirements**
The specification **shall** contain:
*   **Guidelines for Field Supporting:** Instructions and criteria for designing and installing pipe supports in the field when specific details are not provided on formal drawings. This includes considerations for load distribution, thermal expansion, vibration control, and accessibility.

**Roles and Responsibilities**
The preparation, checking, and approval of this specification are assigned to specific disciplines:
*   **Preparation:** The document **shall** be prepared by the Mechanical discipline.
*   **Checking:** The document **shall** be checked by the Design discipline.
*   **Approval:** The document **shall** be approved by the Mechanical discipline.

This procedure is crucial for maintaining the structural integrity and operational safety of piping systems, especially in complex installations where field adjustments to pipe support may be necessary.
