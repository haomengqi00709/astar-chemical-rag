# Pipe Rack Design Guidelines

**Standard Parameters**
Standard parameters for pipe rack design include 6-meter baylines with 2-meter intermediate bents.

**Layout on Racks**
*   Process piping on pipe racks **must** be placed on the bottom level.
*   Utility piping **must** be located above process piping.
*   Cables on pipe racks **must** be on the top level.

**Clearances**
A minimum of 1.5 meters **must** be maintained between parallel levels (decks) on pipe racks.

**Design Rule**
Flat turns **must** be avoided in pipe rack design.
