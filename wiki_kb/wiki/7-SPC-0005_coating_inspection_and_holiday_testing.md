# Protective Coating Inspection and Holiday Testing (7-SPC-0005)

Comprehensive inspection and holiday testing are critical to ensure the integrity of the protective coating system, both in the shop prior to shipment and in the field during installation.

**Shop Inspection and Testing (Prior to Shipment)**
*   **Vendor Responsibility:** The shop-coated vendor **is responsible** for visually inspecting and electrically testing the pipe prior to shipment.
*   **Visual Inspection:** Visual inspection **will ensure** the pipe coating is free from defects such as surface blisters, craters, laminations, and pinholes. It **will also ensure** proper coating and wrapping according to these specifications.
*   **Holiday Testing:** Coated pipe **will be 100 percent holiday-tested** in addition to visual inspection.
    *   Holiday testing **will use** approved electrical holiday detection equipment.
    *   Testing **will be** in accordance with NACE Standard RP-02-74.
    *   The detector **will be set** to acceptable voltages as recommended by the manufacturer.
    *   The detector **will travel** at a rate not to exceed 1 foot per second.
    *   The detector **will not be allowed** to remain stationary while the power is on.
    *   All voids, pinholes, and defects **will be clearly marked**, repaired, and subsequently holiday-tested to restore the integrity of the coating system.

**Field Inspection and Testing (In Trench)**
*   **Visual Inspection:** The protective coating system **will be visually inspected** in the trench prior to the placement of pipe bedding material and backfill.
*   **Holiday Testing:** Coated and wrapped pipe **will be 100 percent holiday-tested** in addition to visual inspection during field inspection.
    *   Field holiday testing **will use** approved electric holiday detection equipment.
    *   Field holiday testing **will be** in accordance with NACE Standard RP-02-74.
    *   The detector **will be set** to acceptable voltages as recommended by the manufacturer for field holiday testing.
    *   The detector **will travel** at a rate not to exceed 1 foot per second during field holiday testing.
    *   The detector **will not be allowed** to remain stationary while the power is on during field holiday testing.

Any identified defects during these inspections **must be addressed** according to [[7-SPC-0005_coating_repair_procedures]].

**Visual Inspection Details**
*   Visual inspection will ensure freedom of pipe coating from defects such as surface blisters, craters, laminations, and pinholes.
*   Visual inspection will also ensure proper coating and wrapping of the pipe according to these specifications.

**Post-Detection Actions**
*   All voids, pinholes, and defects identified during holiday testing will be clearly marked.
*   All marked voids, pinholes, and defects will be repaired.
*   Repaired areas will be holiday-tested again to restore the integrity of the coating system.

**Field Inspection**
*   The protective coating system will be visually inspected in the trench prior to placement of pipe bedding material and backfill.
