# Spray Tower Equipment Data (4-PDS-XXXX-R1)

The Template Spray Tower Process Data Sheet (document ID 4-PDS-XXXX-R1) features an **EQUIPMENT DATA** section that details the physical and operational characteristics of the spray tower and its associated components. This section is structured to capture essential information for procurement, fabrication, and installation.

Key equipment data points include:

*   **Zones**: The spray tower structure is divided into numbered zones from 1.0 to 6.0, allowing for detailed specification of different sections.
*   **Location**: Specifies the physical location of the equipment within the plant.
*   **Column Inside Diameter**: The internal diameter of the spray tower column, measured in meters (m).
*   **Pump Tank Working Volume**: The operational volume of the pump tank, measured in cubic meters (m3).
*   **Pump Tank Diameter**: The diameter of the pump tank, measured in meters (m).
*   **Pump Tank SWH**: The "Side Wall Height" of the pump tank, measured in meters (m).

**Quantity Data**
The datasheet also specifies the required quantities for the equipment, differentiating between:
*   **Operating**: The number of units required for normal operation.
