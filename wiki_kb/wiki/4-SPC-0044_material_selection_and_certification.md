# Glass-Lined Vessel Material Selection and Certification (4-SPC-0044)

This page outlines the mandatory requirements for material selection, identification, and certification for glass-lined carbon steel reaction vessels and their components, as specified in 4-SPC-0044.

**Material Requirements**
The materials of construction **shall** be those defined in Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' approved vessel engineering drawings. No substitution of materials, including internals, is permitted without explicit written approval from Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. Fabricator’s suggested alternative materials **shall** be approved in writing by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics prior to use.

**Material Identification**
All materials **must** be clearly identified in accordance with [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels|ASME Code Section VIII, Div. 1]]. Lack of adequate identification for components such as flanges and fittings **shall** be cause for rejection.

**Certification**
Certification is a mandatory requirement for all materials used in the manufacture of vessels and agitators. This ensures traceability and compliance with specified material properties. Welding consumables also require certification, as detailed in [[4-SPC-0044_welding_requirements|Welding Requirements]]. Agitator drive components are explicitly exempted from these certification requirements.
