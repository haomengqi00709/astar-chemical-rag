# Inline Mixer Materials of Construction

This page details the "Materials of Construction" section of the Template Inline Mixer Process Data Sheet (document ID [[4-PDS-XXXX-R1_template_inline_mixer_datasheet_administration]]). This section is vital for ensuring the chemical compatibility and mechanical integrity of the inline mixer components, in line with [[material_selection]] guidelines and [[general_mechanical_design_requirements_0_spc_0010]].

**Key Material Specifications:**
The following fields specify the materials used for the primary components of the inline mixer:
*   **Shell material of construction:** Defines the material used for the outer casing or housing of the inline mixer. This material must be selected to withstand the process fluid's corrosive properties and the operating pressure and temperature.
*   **Elements material of construction:** Specifies the material for the internal mixing elements. These elements are in direct contact with the process fluids and are crucial for achieving the desired mixing performance, thus requiring materials resistant to erosion and corrosion.

Proper material selection is paramount for the longevity, safety, and operational efficiency of the inline mixer in its intended service.

**Key Material Fields**
This section specifies the materials for critical components:
*   **Shell material of construction:** Field for the material used for the mixer shell.
*   **Elements material of construction:** Field for the material used for the mixing elements.
