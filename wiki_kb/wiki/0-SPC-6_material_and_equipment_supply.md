# Material and Equipment Supply for Specification 0-SPC-6

Specification 0-SPC-6 details the responsibilities for the supply of equipment and materials, distinguishing between items provided by Chemetics and those supplied by the Subcontractor. This ensures clarity in procurement and accountability for all project components.

**Chemetics-Supplied Equipment and Materials**
Chemetics will supply specific Equipment and Materials as itemized in the document [[0-LST-6X90-1_construction_subcontract_supply_and_erection_scope]]. The items supplied by Chemetics **shall** be precisely as described and in the quantities listed within the Applicable Documents. This ensures that all free-issue materials meet the required specifications and are available for the Subcontractor's use. For general policies regarding free-issue materials, refer to [[free_issue_materials_policy]].

**Subcontractor-Supplied Equipment and Materials**
Conversely, the Subcontractor will be responsible for supplying other Equipment and Materials, also as set out in [[0-LST-6X90-1_construction_subcontract_supply_and_erection_scope]]. All Equipment and Materials supplied by the Subcontractor **shall** conform exactly to the descriptions and specifications provided in the Applicable Documents. This ensures that all components integrated into the plant meet the necessary quality and technical standards.

**Compliance and Documentation**
For both Chemetics and Subcontractor supplied items, strict adherence to the Applicable Documents is a mandatory requirement. These documents include drawings, specifications, and other relevant project instructions. For comprehensive information on vendor supply requirements, consult [[vendor_supply_requirements_0_req_5]]. General procedures for managing and controlling all project documents, including the Applicable Documents, are outlined in [[document_control_procedure_0_prc_0007]].

**Chemetics-Supplied Equipment and Materials**
Chemetics will supply equipment and materials as specified in 0-LST-6X90-1: Construction Subcontract Supply and Erection Scope. These items will be as described and in the quantities listed in the Applicable Documents.

**Subcontractor-Supplied Equipment and Materials**
The Subcontractor is responsible for supplying equipment and materials as outlined in 0-LST-6X90-1: Construction Subcontract Supply and Erection Scope. All such equipment and materials must conform to the descriptions and specifications in the Applicable Documents.
