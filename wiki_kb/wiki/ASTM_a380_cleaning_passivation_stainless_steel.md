# ASTM A380 Standard Practice for Cleaning, Descaling, and Passivation of Stainless Steel Parts, Equipment, and Systems

ASTM A380 is a standard practice that provides guidelines for cleaning, descaling, and passivation of stainless steel parts, equipment, and systems. This practice is crucial for maintaining the corrosion resistance and surface integrity of stainless steel components, especially after fabrication processes that may introduce contaminants or alter surface chemistry.

This standard is referenced in [[4-SPC-0014_referenced_codes_and_definitions]] and specifically in [[4-SPC-0014_fabrication_preparation_and_forming]] for the passivation of Columbium Stabilized High Nickel Austenitic stainless steel. Passivation of stainless steel shall correspond to the requirements of ASTM A380.

For detailed procedures on cleaning, descaling, and passivation of stainless steel, refer to the latest edition of ASTM A380.
