# Emergency Procedures and First Aid

Aker Kvaerner Chemetics maintains robust emergency procedures and readily available first aid services to ensure the safety and well-being of all personnel across its work sites. Familiarity with these procedures and locations is critical for effective response during an emergency.

**First Aid Procedures and Contacts**
*   Information on first aid procedures, contacts, and the location of first aid services **must** be known by all personnel.
*   This information, including a brief description of procedures, contacts, and the location of first aid kits, is prominently posted at various locations. Personnel **must** identify where and what first aid information is posted.
*   The location and use of Automated External Defibrillators (AEDs) for medical emergencies **must** also be known.

**Emergency Response Plan**
A comprehensive Building Emergency Response plan exists for all facilities. Key elements of this plan include:
*   **Alarm Signal:** Understanding the specific alarm signals for various emergencies.
*   **Types of Emergencies:** Procedures for responding to spills, fires, explosions, and earthquakes.
*   **Emergency Assembly Point:** Knowing the designated safe locations for assembly during an evacuation.
*   **Equipment Locations:** Knowledge of the location of alarm pulls, fire-extinguishers, emergency showers, and eye-wash stations **must** be known.

In the event of an emergency, all personnel **are expected** to follow established procedures and the guidance of designated emergency responders. Escorting personnel **must** ensure their guests follow emergency procedures and provide assistance as required.
