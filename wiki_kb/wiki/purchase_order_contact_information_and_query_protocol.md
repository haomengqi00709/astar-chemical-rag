**Query Protocol:**
All queries regarding PO execution, whether directed to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or the Vendor, **must** adhere to the following protocol:
*   **Reference:** All queries **must** reference the Purchase Order Number.
*   **Written Communication:** Queries **must** be directed in writing.
*   **Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Queries:** Directed to the Project Buyer. Copies are required to be sent to the Lead Engineer and the Inspector where required.
*   **Vendor Queries:** Directed to the Commercial Manager.

For a comprehensive understanding of the PO structure, refer to [[request_for_quotation_and_purchase_order_structure_and_content]].
