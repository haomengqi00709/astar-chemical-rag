# Zirconium Welding Inspection and Non-Destructive Examination (NDE) (4-SPC-0024)

This page outlines the mandatory inspection and Non-Destructive Examination (NDE) requirements for Zirconium (Grade 60702) welded fabrications, as specified in document **4-SPC-0024**.

**General Inspection:**
*   The company reserves the right to select the location of any inspection.
*   Production Weld Testing **will** satisfy the requirements of para UNF 95 of the [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels|ASME Code Section VIII Div. 1]].

**Dye Penetrant (PT) Examination:**
*   Items fabricated in accordance with the [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels|ASME Code Section VIII, Div. 1]], **will** be PT tested on all welds prior to Heat Treatment.
*   Unless otherwise specified, non-coded items **will** have butt and fillet welds, nozzle attachment welds, etc., 100 percent PT examined on all surfaces exposed to the process prior to heat treatment, for cracks, porosity, slag inclusions, significant undercutting, or other major defects.
*   Tube to tubesheet welds **will** be 100% PT examined.
*   Heat treated welds **will** receive a 10% dye penetrant inspection. This 10% PT inspection for heat-treated welds **will** be representative of each type of heat-treated weld detail.
*   The 10% PT inspection for heat-treated welds **will** be increased to 100% coverage upon revelation of any rejectable defects.
*   The PT exam **will** be carried out in accordance with a written procedure.
*   The limit of acceptability for PT exams **will** satisfy [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels|ASME Code Section VIII, Division 1, Appendix VIII]].
*   Linear indications **will** be considered unacceptable for PT exams.
*   All excavated areas **will** be PT examined prior to building up flush with the surface of the base material. Build-up of excavated areas **must** use the welding procedure previously approved by the company for the main welding details.
*   All weld repair surfaces **will** be 100% PT examined per the above requirements.
*   The company reserves the right to accept or reject any doubtful or questionable PT indications.

**Radiographic (RT) Examination:**
*   Equipment fabricated in accordance with the [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels|ASME Code]] **will** have all butt welds 100% radiographed in accordance with Para. UW-51 of [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels|Section VIII, Division 1 of the ASME Code]].
*   For piping fabricated in accordance with [[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping|ASME Codes and this Standard]], the minimum standard of radiography **will** be random radiography as defined in Para. 341.4.1 (b) of [[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping|ASME B31.3]].
*   20% of all butt welds in piping fabricated in accordance with [[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping|ASME Codes and this Standard]] **will** be radiographed.
*   For tankage fabricated in accordance with [[API_620_low_pressure_storage_tanks|API Standard 620]] and this Standard, the minimum requirements **will** be spot radiography.
*   Non-ASME items fabricated to this Standard only, if required by engineering drawings, **will** be spot radiographed in accordance with UW-52 (b) or 20% random radiographed B31.3 in case of piping.
*   RT Exam **will** be carried out in accordance with a written procedure.
*   RT Exam personnel **must** be qualified to SNTC-1A.
*   Where additional radiography is required, as defined in UW-52 Sub-para. (d), costs of this radiography, including all repair costs and re-radiography, **will** be to the fabricator's account.
*   Junctions between circumferential and longitudinal seams **will** be considered as preferred locations for spot radiography.

**Helium Mass Spectrometer Testing:**
*   Tube/tube sheet joints and tube bundles **will** be tested using the helium mass spectrometer (reverse probe method) per [[ASME_section_v_non_destructive_examination|ASME Code Section V, Article 10]].
