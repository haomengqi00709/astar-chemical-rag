# Shop Visit Hazard Avoidance and Safe Practices

During any shop visit, Aker Kvaerner Chemetics employees **must** adhere to strict safety practices to avoid common industrial hazards. These practices are essential for personal safety and for maintaining a safe environment for others. For general guidelines, refer to [[workshop_safety_rules]].

**General Conduct and Movement:**
*   Employees **must** stay within designated walkways at all times.
*   Employees **must** enter work areas only when guided by their host.
*   Employees **must** not assume that workers within the plant are aware of their presence and will act to protect them. Constant vigilance is required.
*   If unsure about any safety aspect, employees **must** ask their host for clarification.

**Machinery and Grinding Areas:**
*   Employees **must** be extra cautious in machine shop areas.
*   Employees **must** stay clear of boring mills, lathes, drills, or any other moving machinery.
*   Employees **must** avoid grinding areas.
*   Employees **must** not stand in direct line with grinding or cutting operations to avoid grinding dust and the risk of being struck by a broken stone or disk.

**Welding and Arc Cutting Operations:**
*   Employees **must** not look directly at welding or cutting arcs, as this can cause severe eye damage.
*   Employees **must** avoid areas where arc cutting is being used. Arc cutting operations generate hot flying metal and are very noisy, posing significant hazards.
*   If exposed to dust, FRP resin vapors, or paint fumes, employees **must** wear an appropriate [[respiratory_protection_requirements|respirator]].

**Overhead Cranes and Radiography:**
*   Employees **must** watch for overhead crane movements at all times.
*   Employees **must** never be under a load being moved by an overhead crane.
*   Employees **must** not enter areas where radiography is being conducted, even if told it is safe, unless specifically authorized and with full understanding of the safety protocols.

These practices, combined with the [[shop_visit_safety_orientation]] and appropriate [[shop_visit_personal_protective_equipment]], are critical for ensuring a safe shop visit.

**General Safety Practices**
*   Employees must ask their host about the specific safety procedures they are required to observe.
*   Always stay within designated walkways.
*   Enter work areas only when guided by your host.
*   Be extra cautious in machine shop areas. Stay clear of boring mills, lathes, drills, or any other moving machinery.
*   Avoid grinding areas. Do not stand in direct line with grinding or cutting operations to avoid grinding dust or being struck by a broken stone or disk.
*   Do not look directly at welding or cutting arcs. Avoid areas where arc cutting is being used, as these operations generate hot flying metal and are very noisy.
*   Watch for overhead crane movements and never be under a load.
*   Do not enter areas where radiography is being conducted, even when told it is safe.
*   Never assume that workers within the plant are aware of your presence and will act to protect you.
*   If unsure about any safety aspect, always ask your host.
