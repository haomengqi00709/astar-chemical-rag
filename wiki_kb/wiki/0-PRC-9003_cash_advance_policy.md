# Cash Advance Policy (0-PRC-9003)

Employees may request a cash advance prior to travelling under specific circumstances:
*   If they do not possess a credit card for which the annual fee has been reimbursed by the company.
*   If they are travelling to a location where credit card acceptance is limited.

**Requesting a Cash Advance**
Employees **must** indicate any requirements for a cash advance on their [[travel_authorization_form_details_0_frm_9002|Travel Request Form]] (0-PRC-9002 Travel Authorisation). A copy of the authorised Travel Request Form **must** be provided to the Finance Department, who will then arrange for the cash advance.

**Cash Advance Payment and Limits**
Cash advances will be paid either by check or by electronic funds transfer into the employee’s bank account.
*   For travel within Canada, cash advances are limited to CAD 200 per day away.
*   For travel outside Canada, cash advances are limited to USD 200 or CAD 300 per day away.
