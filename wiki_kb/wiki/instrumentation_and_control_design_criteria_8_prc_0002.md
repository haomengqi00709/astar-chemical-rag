# Instrument and Control Design Criteria (8-PRC-0002)

The [[instrumentation_and_control_design_criteria_8_prc_0002]] document (8-PRC-0002) outlines the fundamental requirements for the design of instrumentation and control systems within a project.

**Content Requirements:**
The 8-PRC-0002 document **shall** contain:
*   Statutory requirements.
*   Owner requirements.
*   Reference to the appropriate specification.
*   Any additional general requirements for Plant field instrument design.

**Document Preparation and Approval:**
*   **Prepared by:** DDE
*   **Checked by:** Project Engineer
*   **Approved by:** Instrument Engineer

**Integration with Project Procedures:**
If the customer has not provided instrument design requirements, the DDE **shall** provide the Instrument and Control Design Criteria as per standard 8-PRC-0002 and standard engineering practices. The Project Engineer **must** provide an opportunity to the Field Instrument Engineer (FIE) to review and comment on the Instrument Design Criteria prior to its final publication and issue.
