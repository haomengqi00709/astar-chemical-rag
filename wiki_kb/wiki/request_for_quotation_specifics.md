# Request for Quotation Specifics

While a Request for Quotation (RFQ) shares a largely identical format and structure with a Purchase Order (PO), there are key distinctions that differentiate the two documents. These differences primarily serve to clarify the non-binding nature of an RFQ and provide specific instructions for bidders.

**Title and Watermark**
An RFQ will appear identical to a PO in its overall layout and content, with the exception of its title. To clearly indicate its status, an RFQ **shall** have the text "This Is Not An Order" watermarked across the body of the document.

**Delivery Date in Text Section 3**
In an RFQ, Text Section 3, which typically specifies delivery dates, **must** state "TBA" (To Be Advised) for the delivery date. This requirement became mandatory as of Revision 2 of Procedure [[form_of_purchase_order_0_prc_5001]].

**References in Text Section 6**
For an RFQ, Text Section 6, which lists pertinent vendor documents, **shall** refer the Vendor to [[instructions_to_bidders]]. Data available in SQL: 0-SPC-5006. This ensures that potential vendors receive all necessary guidance for preparing their bids.

For details on the general structure and content of both RFQs and POs, refer to [[request_for_quotation_and_purchase_order_structure_and_content]].
