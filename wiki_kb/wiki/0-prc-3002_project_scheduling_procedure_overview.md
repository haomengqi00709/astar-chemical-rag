# Project Scheduling Procedure Overview (0-PRC-3002)

This procedure, documented as 0-PRC-3002, outlines the methods for effectively scheduling projects, measuring their progress, and accurately forecasting outcomes. A schedule **will** be prepared and consistently updated for every project undertaken by the firm. All project schedules **will** be presented in the form of a bar chart.

The Project Schedule **will** be structured into four distinct sub-schedules: Engineering, Procurement, Construction, and Commissioning. Activities both within and between these sub-schedules **will** be linked by logical dependencies. These dependencies are crucial for establishing critical paths within the project timeline and enabling accurate forecasting of the effects of potential delays or changes.

The level of detail incorporated into a project schedule is systematically denoted by specific nomenclature, as further detailed in [[project_schedule_detail_levels]].
