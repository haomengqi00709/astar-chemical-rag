# Vendor Quality Assurance and Chemetics Surveillance for 0-QDS-7801

This Quality Data Sheet (QDS) 0-QDS-7801 outlines the specific requirements for vendor quality assurance and Chemetics' surveillance activities for Type 1 Instruments.

**Vendor Quality Assurance Requirements**
*   The Vendor **must** prepare a Quality Plan in accordance with [[0-SPC-7002_vendor_quality_assurance]].
*   The Vendor's Quality Assurance program **must** comply with the requirements set forth in this Quality Data Sheet.
*   The Vendor's Quality Assurance program **must** also comply with the general requirements specified in [[0-SPC-7002_vendor_quality_assurance]].

Data available in SQL: 0-SPC-7002

**Chemetics Surveillance Level**
*   Surveillance by Chemetics for items covered by this QDS will be conducted to Level 0.0.
*   The specific surveillance activities and requirements for Chemetics will be as detailed within this Quality Data Sheet.

For a broader understanding of vendor quality assurance principles, refer to [[vendor_quality_assurance]].
