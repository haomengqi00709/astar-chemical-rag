# Tantalum Alloy Welding Procedures

This specification outlines the mandatory welding procedures and practices for tantalum alloys, particularly UNS R05252, as defined in [[4-SPC-0045_tantalum_alloy_welding_specification_overview|4-SPC-0045]].

*   **Filler Metals**: Matching filler metals from the alloy suppliers **must** be used for all welds. Refer to [[welding_consumables]] for general guidelines.
*   **Welding Process**: Only [[ASME_sfa_5_12_tungsten_arc_welding_electrodes|gas tungsten arc welding (GTAW)]] is acceptable.
*   **Shielding Gas**: The shielding gas **shall** be moisture-free argon, helium, or a mixture of the two.
*   **Gas Handling Hoses**: Gas handling hoses **must** be made of Tygon® or vinyl plastic material. Rubber hoses **shall not** be used for gas handling.
*   **Work Area**: All tantalum welding **must** be completed in a clean, segregated area within the Vendor’s facility.
*   **Contamination Prevention**: Provision **must** be made to prevent oxidation or contamination of **all** tantalum surfaces during welding and cooling operations.
*   **Tool Cleaning**: Forming tools previously used for other materials **must** be cleaned to remove surface contamination.
*   **Surface Preparation**: All burrs and indentations **must** be ground smooth.
*   **Grinding Equipment**: Grinding wheels and wire brushes **must** be clean and **shall not** have been previously used on any other material. Grit for abrasive materials **must** be alumina only. Brushes **must** be fabricated from austenitic stainless steel.
*   **Stress Relief**: Stress relief of formed parts **shall not** be performed unless specifically requested in writing by [[chemetics_aker_kvaerner_company_details|Aker Kvaerner Chemetics]].

For general welding requirements, consult [[welding_procedures_and_quality]] and [[welding_procedure_and_welder_qualifications]].

*   **Welding Method**: Only Gas Tungsten Arc Welding (GTAW) is acceptable.
*   **Shielding Gas**: The shielding gas must be moisture-free argon, helium, or a mixture of the two.
*   **Gas Handling Hoses**: Gas handling hoses must be made of Tygon® or vinyl plastic material. Rubber hoses are explicitly prohibited for gas handling.
*   **Welding Environment**: All tantalum welding must be completed in a clean, segregated area within the Vendor’s facility. Provisions must be made to prevent oxidation or contamination of all tantalum surfaces during welding and cooling operations.
*   **Tooling and Preparation**:
    *   Forming tools previously used for other materials must be thoroughly cleaned to remove surface contamination.
    *   All burrs and indentations must be ground smooth.
    *   Grinding wheels and wire brushes must be clean and must not have been previously used on any other material.
    *   Grit for abrasive materials must be alumina only.
    *   Brushes must be fabricated from austenitic stainless steel.
*   **Stress Relief**: Stress relief of formed parts will not be performed unless specifically requested in writing by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
