# Instrument Electrical Layout Drawings

Instrument Electrical Layout drawings are derived from instrument location drawings and specifically identify instruments requiring electrical power, along with their circuit numbers and distribution panel board references.

**Content of Instrument Electrical Layout Drawings**
Instrument electrical layout drawings **shall** be copies of the instrument location drawings.
Instruments requiring electrical power, such as 120 vAC, 220 vAC, or 240 vAC, **must** be identified.
Distribution panel board circuit numbers for each instrument **must** be shown.
The distribution panel board **will** be referenced on these drawings.
Any [[instrument_installation_guidelines]] showing the installation method **must** be referenced.

Drawings **will** have a symbol legend shown on each drawing or on a cover sheet for a group of drawings.
