# Construction Subcontract Scheduling

This procedure (Document ID: 0-SPC-6021) describes the methods and requirements for Construction Subcontractors to schedule works, set activity sequence, measure progress, and forecast outcomes.

**General Requirements**
The Subcontractor **must** prepare and update a schedule in the form of a bar chart. The schedule **must** utilize SureTrak Version 5.0 or later software.

For works exceeding 3,500 direct labour hours, the schedule **shall** be developed to the specific levels outlined in this procedure. For works below 3,500 direct labour hours, the schedule levels will be determined by the [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_obligations_0_spc_2201|Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Site Manager]].

**Schedule Structure**
The schedule for the works **shall** be structured into three distinct sub-schedules: Design, Procurement, and Construction. Activities within and between these sub-schedules **shall** be linked by logical dependencies. These dependencies are crucial for establishing critical paths and enabling accurate forecasting of the effects of delays or changes.

Linkage between sub-schedules **shall** utilize intermediate milestones. These milestones allow for independent assessment and control of float within each individual sub-schedule.

For detailed information on the different schedule levels, refer to [[construction_subcontract_schedule_levels]]. Specific requirements for each sub-schedule can be found in [[construction_subcontract_sub_schedules_details]]. Management and reporting aspects of the schedule are detailed in [[construction_subcontract_schedule_management_and_reporting]].
