# PVN/PCN Revision Process

The revision process for Project Variance Notifications (PVN) and Project Change Notifications (PCN) is strictly controlled to maintain document integrity and ensure accurate tracking of changes.

**Revision Identification:**
*   Any revision to an original PVN/PCN will be clearly identified by the Project Controls Specialist.
*   Revisions are assigned an "Alpha" character, commencing with "A" for the first revision, "B" for the second, and so on.

**Mandatory Revision Rules:**
*   A revision **cannot** be made to an already approved PVN/PCN. Once a PVN or PCN has received final approval, its content is considered final for that specific notification number.
*   If changes are required for an already approved PVN/PCN, a new PVN/PCN number **must** be assigned. This ensures that each approved change or subsequent modification is uniquely identifiable and traceable.

**Documentation of Revisions:**
*   The reasons for a PVN/PCN revision **must** be briefly described as part of the detailed description of the variance within the revised form. This provides a clear audit trail for why a particular notification was updated.

This structured approach to revisions helps prevent confusion, ensures that all stakeholders are working with the most current information, and maintains the integrity of the change management process.
