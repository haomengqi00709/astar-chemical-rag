# Rubber Lining Application Requirements (4-SPC-0081)

This section details the mandatory requirements and responsibilities for the application of rubber linings to fabricated steel components, as specified in 4-SPC-0081.

**Vendor Responsibilities:**
*   **Agreement on Lining Details**: The type and thickness of the rubber lining **shall** be agreed upon between the Vendor and Aker Kvaerner Chemetics prior to the commencement of any lining work. This ensures that the selected lining meets the specific operational and chemical resistance requirements of the equipment.
*   **Fabricator Precautions**: It is the responsibility of the Vendor to ensure that the vessel or component fabricator has implemented all necessary precautions to facilitate a high-quality rubber lining installation. This includes adherence to surface preparation and design considerations outlined in standards such as NACE RP0178-98 (see [[4-SPC-0081_referenced_standards_for_rubber_lining]]).
*   **Lining Applicator**: It is anticipated that the Vendor responsible for the completed equipment will also serve as the lining applicator. This streamlines accountability and ensures consistent quality control throughout the fabrication and lining process.
*   **Steel Surface Acceptability**: The Vendor **must** ensure that all component steel surfaces designated for rubber lining are acceptable for the application. This involves verifying surface finish, cleanliness, and any other preparatory requirements to achieve optimal adhesion and longevity of the rubber lining.

For specific marking requirements for rubber-lined equipment, refer to [[4-SPC-0081_rubber_lined_equipment_marking]].
