# Vendor Bid Evaluation Document Structure

The evaluation of Vendor bids is compiled into a single document, which is structured into four distinct sections. These sections collectively provide a comprehensive assessment of each bid.

The four mandatory sections are:
1.  **Cost Tabulation:** Details the pricing and associated costs of the Goods and Services.
2.  **Technical Bid Tabulation:** Compares the technical aspects of the bids against project requirements.
3.  **Commercial Bid Tabulation:** Evaluates the commercial terms and conditions proposed by Vendors.
4.  **Order Risk Assessment:** Identifies and assesses business risks associated with placing an order with a specific Vendor.

**Document Numbering:**
All sections of the bid evaluation document **must** be numbered in accordance with [[document_numbering_and_name_change_conventions]] (0-PRC-0002). Each section will utilize the same Major Work Area code as the Goods and Services being ordered. Furthermore, the Identification Number assigned to the evaluation document **must** match that of the intended [[form_of_purchase_order_0_prc_5001]].

**Example Document Number:**
An illustrative example of a document number for a bid evaluation section is 99C10500-4-TAB-4005-1-R2.
