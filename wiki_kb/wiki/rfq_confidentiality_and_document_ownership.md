# RFQ Confidentiality and Document Ownership

Maintaining confidentiality and respecting document ownership are critical aspects of the Request for Quotation (RFQ) process.

**Confidentiality Requirements:**
*   **Mandatory:** Bidders must take reasonable measures to maintain the confidentiality of the Request for Quotation and all referenced documents. This includes preventing unauthorized disclosure of sensitive project information. For broader guidelines, refer to the [[confidential_information_policy]].
*   Bidder’s Suppliers will only be provided with sufficient information to enable them to prepare an offer to the Bidder. This ensures that sensitive project details are restricted to a need-to-know basis.

**Document Ownership:**
The Request for Quotation and all documents provided to the Bidder by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics remain the exclusive property of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. This includes any intellectual property or design information contained within. For related policies, see [[intellectual_property_and_design_use]].
*   Originals and all copies of these documents **must** be returned to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or destroyed upon demand. This ensures control over proprietary information and prevents its unauthorized retention or use.

Adherence to these confidentiality and ownership clauses is paramount to protecting Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' proprietary information and ensuring the integrity of the procurement process.
