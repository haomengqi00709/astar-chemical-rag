# Austenitic Stainless Steel Welding Consumables (4-SPC-0061)

This page details the requirements for welding filler metals and consumables as specified in 4-SPC-0061 for austenitic stainless steel.

**General Filler Metal Requirements**
All weld filler metals **shall** meet the requirements of [[ASME_bpvc_section_ii_materials]], Part C. Type 300 stainless steels **will** be welded with matching filler metal. Specific weld consumables **will** be used when making welds between austenitic stainless steels and other alloys, including carbon steels. Deviations from filler material requirements **require** written approval by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Electrode Specifications by Process**

*   **Shielded Metal Arc Welding (SMAW):** SMAW electrodes **will** satisfy the requirements of [[ASME_sfa_5_4_welding_electrodes]].
*   **Gas Tungsten Arc Welding (GTAW):** GTAW rods **will** satisfy the requirements of [[ASME_sfa_5_9_welding_electrodes_and_rods]].
*   **Gas Metal Arc Welding (GMAW):** GMAW electrodes **will** satisfy the requirements of [[ASME_sfa_5_9_welding_electrodes_and_rods]] or [[ASME_sfa_5_22_flux_cored_arc_welding_electrodes]].
*   **Submerged Arc Welding (SAW):** SAW electrodes **will** satisfy the requirements of [[ASME_sfa_5_9_welding_electrodes_and_rods]]. Filler metal types ER310 and ER320 **will not** be used with the SAW process. Any change in SAW flux **will require** that a chemical analysis of the finished deposit be performed and submitted to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
*   **Plasma Arc Welding (PAW):** PAW electrodes **will** satisfy the requirements of [[ASME_sfa_5_9_welding_electrodes_and_rods]].
*   **Flux Core Arc Welding (FCAW):** FCAW electrodes **will** satisfy the requirements of [[ASME_sfa_5_22_flux_cored_arc_welding_electrodes]]. Only FCAW electrodes supplied by Kobe, ESAB, Sandvik, Lincoln, and Air Liquide are acceptable. FCAW electrodes from other producers may only be used if written approval is given by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics before work is started.
