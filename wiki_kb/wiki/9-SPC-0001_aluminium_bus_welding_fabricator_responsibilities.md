# Aluminium Bus Welding Fabricator Responsibilities

Fabricators undertaking work governed by the Aluminium Bus Welding Standard (9-SPC-0001) have several mandatory responsibilities to ensure quality and compliance.

**General Supervision and Quality Control**
The fabricator **shall** ensure regular and systematic supervision of all work, both in-shop and on-site, by competent staff. Controls **shall** be implemented to prevent contamination of weldments at all stages of fabrication. These controls **shall** include the segregation of wire brushes, grinding wheels, and other tools for exclusive use on Aluminium and its alloys.

**Welding Process Evidence**
For Gas Metal Arc Welding (GMAW), the fabricator **must** provide satisfactory evidence of continuous shop use of the GMAW process in the fabrication of Aluminium or its alloys.

**Consumable Management**
Fabricators **shall** ensure and demonstrate to the [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_quality_assurance_and_materials_engineering_group|Aker Kvaerner Chemetics representative]] that the manufacturer's instructions for the storage, drying, and use of weld consumables are fully implemented. The fabricator's internal controls **shall** prevent the use of unspecified materials.

**Defect Management and Non-Conformance**
It **shall** be the responsibility of the fabricator to notify the purchaser immediately upon the discovery of any unacceptable defects. Repair of unacceptable defects **shall** be to the fabricator's account. If chemical analysis results show that a specific weldment falls outside of the approved specification or code requirements, the supplier **shall** promptly advise Aker Kvaerner Chemetics in writing of their proposed course of action. This proposed course of action **shall** include details of any additional testing necessary to fully define the extent of the non-conformance. All repairs and retests for non-conforming weldments **shall** be to the supplier's account.

**Overall Responsibility**
Inspection by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics does not relieve the fabricator or erector of responsibility for providing materials, components, and workmanship in accordance with the requirements of this Standard, engineering design, and the applicable code.

**General Supervision and Quality Control**
The fabricator **shall** ensure regular and systematic supervision of all work, both in-shop and on-site, by competent staff.
