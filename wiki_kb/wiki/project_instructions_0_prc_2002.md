# Project Instructions (0-PRC-2002)

The Project Instructions procedure, identified as 0-PRC-2002, governs the numbering and management of specific administrative and communication-related documents within projects. This procedure is distinct from the general document numbering system and applies to documents not covered by [[document_numbering_procedure_0_prc_0002]].

**Scope:**
This procedure specifically addresses the numbering of:
*   Correspondence
*   Minutes of meetings
*   Memoranda
*   Project reports

These document types are essential for project communication, record-keeping, and progress tracking, but they do not follow the structured technical document numbering format.

**Purpose:**
The purpose of 0-PRC-2002 is to ensure a consistent and traceable system for these administrative documents, facilitating efficient project communication and historical record-keeping. It provides guidelines for their creation, distribution, and archiving.

**Relationship to Other Procedures:**
This procedure is referenced by [[document_numbering_exclusions]] as one of the specific protocols for documents outside the main technical numbering scheme. It may also be used in conjunction with [[project_coordination_0_prc_2004]] for broader project communication and coordination efforts.

Adherence to 0-PRC-2002 is vital for maintaining clear and organized administrative records throughout the lifecycle of a project.
