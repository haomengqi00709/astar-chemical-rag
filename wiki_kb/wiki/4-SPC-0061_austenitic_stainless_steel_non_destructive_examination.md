# Austenitic Stainless Steel Non-Destructive Examination (4-SPC-0061)

This page outlines the non-destructive examination (NDE) requirements for austenitic stainless steel welds, as specified in 4-SPC-0061.

**Important Note on Inspection Documentation:**
It is important to note that, as per internal directives, anything related to inspection **must now** be covered in the appropriate Quality Assurance (QA) document. While these requirements are listed in 4-SPC-0061, users **should** also consult relevant QA documents such as [[0-QDS-7_non_destructive_examination_requirements]] for the most current and comprehensive inspection protocols.

**Radiographic Testing (RT) Requirements**
*   **Piping:** Piping **will** have 5% random radiography as defined in [[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping]] paragraph 341.4.1(b) as a minimum.
*   **Shop Fabricated Pipe:** Shop fabricated pipe made from sheet or plate **will** have one spot radiograph at each longitudinal seam. No additional radiography of the longitudinal seam is required unless defects are revealed when the pipe is cut into smaller lengths.
*   **Inconclusive RT Results:** When RT results are inconclusive, welds **will** be liquid penetrant or ultrasonic examined.

**Liquid Penetrant Examination (LPE) Requirements**
*   **Nozzle Attachment Welds:** The cover pass of nozzle attachment welds **will** be liquid penetrant examined for 25% of the total weld length for each welder or welding process or both.
*   **Butt Welds:** The cover pass of butt welds **will** be liquid penetrant examined for 25% of the total weld length for each welder or welding process or both.
*   **Skirt to Shell Welds:** The cover pass of skirt to shell welds **will** be liquid penetrant examined for 25% of the total weld length for each welder or welding process or both.
*   **Dissimilar Metal Welds:** The root pass and cover pass of dissimilar metal welds **will** be liquid penetrant examined for 100% of their length.
*   **Tube to Tubesheet Welds:** Tube to tubesheet welds **will** be liquid penetrant examined for 100% of their length.
*   **Weld Repair Surfaces:** Weld repair surfaces, including weld built-up excavated areas, **will** be liquid penetrant examined for 100% of their length.
*   **Production Welds (Piping):** For each pipe size, ¼ of the production welds **will** be 100% liquid penetrant examined.
