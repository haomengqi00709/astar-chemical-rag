# Research Laboratory HSE Orientation

Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) orientation for the Research Laboratory, specifically at the Technology Centre, is tailored to address the unique hazards and operational requirements of a laboratory environment.

**Pre-Job Orientation**
All employees assigned to the Research Laboratory **will** receive a pre-job orientation. The content of this orientation is determined by Human Resources. The Health and Safety component **will** be an integral part of this pre-job orientation and **will** be delivered at the Technology Centre as a separate orientation before the employee commences work.

**Responsibilities for Orientation**
It is the joint responsibility of the AKCTC Manager or Supervisor to provide a health and safety orientation to all new personnel coming into the Technology Centre for a period exceeding two days. New personnel in this category **must** be required to work independently (and unescorted) in the laboratory environment to receive their orientation from the AKCTC Manager or Supervisor.

**Re-orientation**
Personnel who have not visited the Technology Centre for one year or more **will** receive a new orientation to ensure their knowledge of current safety procedures and laboratory-specific protocols is up-to-date.

**Orientation Content and Documentation**
The Technology Centre health and safety orientation **will** follow the minimum outline given in Appendix 3 of the source document. All individuals receiving this safety orientation **will** sign the Acknowledgement Form, which can be found in Appendix D of the AKCTC Safety Manual. For unescorted personnel in the Research Laboratory, the orientation **will** also follow the minimum outline given in Appendix 3, and individuals receiving this orientation **will** sign the standard AKCTC Acknowledgement Form.

**Information and Contacts**
*   Sources of Information include the AKC Technology Centre Safety Manual (posted under Lab_Data on U: drive).
*   Questions regarding health and safety policies and procedures are to be directed to your immediate Supervisor, AKCTC Safety Committee Members, HSE Manager, or Human Resources.

**Emergency Preparedness**
*   The Technology Centre's emergency response plan includes procedures for spills, fires, explosions, and earthquakes.
*   Key elements of the emergency response plan include alarm signals and designated Emergency Assembly Points.
*   Personnel **must** know the location of emergency showers, eye-wash stations, and the Automated External Defibrillator (AED) for medical emergencies, and how to use them.

**Health and Environmental Initiatives**
*   Health promotion initiatives at the Technology Centre include MVMT.
*   The environmental program emphasizes the 3R principles (reduce, re-use, recycle).
*   Electricity and water conservation initiatives include turning off computers, lights, and water before leaving for home.
*   "Go Green" initiatives encourage employees to ride the bus or cycle to work.
