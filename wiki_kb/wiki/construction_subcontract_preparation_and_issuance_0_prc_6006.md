# Construction Subcontract Preparation and Issuance (0-PRC-6006)

This document, 0-PRC-6006, outlines the procedure for the preparation and issuance of construction subcontracts for the Project. It details the steps involved in drafting, reviewing, and formally issuing subcontracts to selected construction subcontractors.

Subcontracts **will** be prepared and issued in accordance with this procedure, ensuring that all contractual terms, scope of work, and quality requirements are clearly defined and legally binding. This is a key part of [[construction_subcontract_management_and_site_quality]].
