# General Welding Requirements (4-SPC-0015)

This page outlines the general requirements for welding nickel-base alloys under Standard 4-SPC-0015, covering aspects from process selection to weld quality and post-weld cleaning. For specific process details, refer to [[4-SPC-0015_specific_welding_processes]].

**Welding Processes**
*   Preferred welding processes for nickel-base alloys are Shielded Metal Arc Welding (SMAW), Gas Tungsten Arc Welding (GTAW), and Gas Metal Arc Welding (GMAW).
*   Other welding processes **will be evaluated** upon submission of full details, including procedure and exact location of proposed weldments.
*   General welding parameters (current, voltage, electrode size, etc.) and special requirements/precautions **should be obtained** from the electrode supplier.

**Preheat and Postweld Heat Treatment**
*   Preheat is **not required**.
*   Postweld heat treatment is **not required**.

**Environmental Conditions**
*   When adverse weather conditions cause condensation or wetness on component surfaces, the material **must** be dried.
*   Prior to welding, heating to a temperature just sufficient to prevent condensation is required.
*   Suitable enclosures **will be provided** to keep welds and welders free from draughts, rain, or other inclement weather.
*   Welding **will not be performed** on wet surfaces.
*   Welding **will not be performed** with wet or damp electrodes.

**Welding Position**
*   All welds **will be made** in the downhand (flat) position as far as practical.
*   Vertical welds **will be made** vertical up (uphand).

**Inter-Pass Cleaning and Re-Starts**
*   Each run of weld metal **will be carefully examined** to ensure that it has been thoroughly cleaned and is defect-free.
*   All slag and foreign matter **will be removed** before the next run is deposited. Removal may be achieved by grinding followed by wire brushing.
*   On re-starting, care **will be taken** to ensure proper fusion with the weld metal previously deposited.
*   All re-starts in the root bead **will be preceded** by "feathering" out the area of the stop by grinding or filing, and **will be followed** with wire brushing to remove any abrasive residue.

**Tungsten Contamination and Filler Rods**
*   If a tungsten electrode accidentally makes contact with the work, the section of weld and the tip of the electrode **should be examined**.
*   Where tungsten loss has occurred, the tungsten pick-up in the weld **will be ground out**, and the electrode **will be reground** to the correct profile before restarting.
*   If the heated end of the filler rod becomes contaminated, it **will be snipped off** and discarded.

**Weld Finishing**
*   Peening of weld runs is **not permitted**.
*   Hammering of finished welds is **not permitted**.
*   Grinding or gouging **will not be carried out** in such a manner as to reduce the adjacent base metal thickness.
*   Complete removal of weld defects **will be verified** by non-destructive examination prior to repair welding. Refer to [[4-SPC-0015_inspection_and_nde_requirements]].
*   Grinding wheels **will be iron-free** and of the resin bonded silicon carbide type.
*   Wire brushes or chipping tools **will be made** of austenitic stainless steel and **must not** have been previously used to clean surfaces other than Incoloy 800 or 825.

**Weld Appearance and Quality**
*   Each weld **will be uniform** in width and size throughout its full length.
*   Each layer of welding **will be smooth** and free of slag, inclusions, cracks, porosity, lack of fusion, and undercut, except to the extent permitted in the referenced codes.
*   The cover pass **will be free** of coarse ripples, irregular surface, non-uniform bead patterns, high crown, and deep ridges or valleys between beads.
*   Buttwelds **will be slightly convex**, of uniform height, and have full penetration.
*   Internal reinforcement of pipe welds **will be subject** to the limitations of the governing code.
*   Crevices or undercut **will not be permitted** on the process side of the weld.
*   Fillet welds **will be deposited** in not less than two layers as far as is practicable.
*   Fillet weld surfaces **will be free** from marked irregularities or concavity.
*   Dimensions of fillet welds **will be in accordance** with the approved drawing.

**General Welding Parameters and Precautions**
*   General welding parameters (current, voltage, electrode size, etc.) and special requirements/precautions should be obtained from the electrode supplier.

**Preheat and Postweld Heat Treatment**
*   Preheat is **not required**.
*   Postweld heat treatment is **not required**.

**Environmental Conditions and Pre-Welding Preparation**
*   When adverse weather conditions cause condensation or wetness on component surfaces, the material **must** be dried.
*   Prior to welding, heating to a temperature just sufficient to prevent condensation is required.
*   The welding procedure **will** be agreed upon (see Para 5.3) before any production welding is begun.
*   All welds **will** be made in the downhand (flat) position as far as practical. Vertical welds **will** be made vertical up (uphand).
*   Suitable enclosures **will** be provided to keep welds and welders free from draughts, rain or other inclement weather.
*   Welding **will not** be performed on wet surfaces or with wet or damp electrodes.
*   Fusion faces and adjacent metal (internal and external) **will** be free from moisture, scale, paint, grease or other foreign matter immediately prior to welding.
