# Construction Subcontractor Document Delivery Formats and Quantities (0-SPC-6091)

This page outlines the acceptable formats and required quantities for documents delivered by the Construction Subcontractor under document 0-SPC-6091.

**Document Formats**
The Subcontractor **must** deliver documents in one of the following formats:
*   **Native Digital**: This includes files in AutoCAD, Microstation, or Word formats.
*   **Digital PDF**: Portable Document Format files.
*   **Printed Paper Originals**.

**Maximum Document Size**
The maximum allowable document size is D (22” x 34”) or A1 (594mm x 841mm).

**Required Quantities for Printed Paper Documents**
*   Documents delivered during the course of project execution: 1 quantity.
*   Operating and Maintenance Manuals: 10 quantities.
*   Quality Manuals: 5 quantities.

**Digital Document Storage and Compression**
Digital documents may be stored on:
*   CD-ROMs that are readable on a PC running Microsoft Windows 95/NT.
*   Floppy disks (either 1.44 MB or LS-120).

Files **may** only be compressed using the PKZIP format.

For general document publishing guidelines, refer to [[publishing_of_project_documents]].
