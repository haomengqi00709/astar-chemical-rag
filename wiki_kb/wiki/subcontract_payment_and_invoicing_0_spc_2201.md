# Professional Services Subcontract (0-SPC-2201) Payment and Invoicing

This section outlines the procedures for payment and invoicing under Professional Services Subcontract 0-SPC-2201.

**Invoice Submission and Assessment:**
*   The Subcontractor **must** assess the amount due and submit an invoice at each assessment date.
*   Unless otherwise specified in the [[subcontract_definitions_0_spc_2201|Subcontract Data]], assessment dates occur at monthly intervals, starting from the commencement date until one month after [[subcontract_definitions_0_spc_2201|Completion]] of the entire services.
*   Invoices submitted by the Subcontractor **must** include details, as stated in the [[subcontract_definitions_0_spc_2201|Scope]], demonstrating how the amount due has been assessed.
*   The first invoice **must** be for the initial amount due, while subsequent invoices **must** reflect the change in the amount due since the previous invoice.

**Calculation of Amount Due:**
The amount due is calculated as:
*   The [[subcontract_definitions_0_spc_2201|Price for Services Provided to Date]].
*   Plus payment for expenses incurred in [[subcontract_definitions_0_spc_2201|Providing the Services]], as specified in the Subcontract Data.
*   Plus other amounts to be paid to the Subcontractor.
*   Less any amounts to be paid by or retained from the Subcontractor.
*   Any value-added tax, sales tax, or similar taxes that the law requires Aker Kvaerner Chemetics to pay to the Subcontractor **must** be included in the amount due.

**Payment Terms:**
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **must** make payment within 45 days of receiving the Subcontractor’s invoice. If a different period is stated in the Subcontract Data, payment **must** be made within that specified period.
*   Each payment **must** be the amount due, minus any previous payments.
*   Payments **must** be made in the currency of this Subcontract, unless otherwise stated in the Subcontract Data.

**Disputed Invoices:**
*   If Aker Kvaerner Chemetics disputes an invoice, they **must** notify the Subcontractor of the reason for disagreement before the payment becomes due.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **must** pay any agreed portion of the invoice.
*   The Subcontractor **must** either correct the invoice to a sum agreed upon by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or provide additional information to justify the original invoice amount.

**Record Keeping and Inspection:**
*   The Subcontractor **must** maintain accounts and records of all expenses.
*   The Subcontractor **must** allow Aker Kvaerner Chemetics to inspect these accounts and records at any time during working hours.

**Invoice Submission and Assessment:**
*   Unless otherwise stated in the *Subcontract Data*, assessment dates are at monthly intervals after the starting date until one month after Completion of the whole of the services.
*   Invoices submitted by the Subcontractor include the details stated in the Scope to show how the amount due has been assessed.
*   The first invoice is for the amount due. Other invoices are for the change in the amount due since the previous invoice.
*   Any value added tax or sales tax or other tax of a similar nature which the law requires Aker Kvaerner Chemetics to pay to the Subcontractor is included in the amount due.

**Payment Terms:**
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics makes payment within 45 days of receiving the Subcontractor’s invoice, unless a different period is stated in the *Subcontract Data*.
*   Each payment is the amount due less any previous payments.
*   Payments are in the currency of this Subcontract unless otherwise stated in the *Subcontract Data*.

**Invoice Disagreement and Resolution:**
*   If Aker Kvaerner Chemetics does not agree with an invoice, he notifies the Subcontractor of the reason for his disagreement before the payment becomes due. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics pays any agreed part of the invoice.
*   The Subcontractor either corrects the invoice to a sum agreed by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, or provides further information to justify the invoice.

**Records and Inspection:**
*   The Subcontractor keeps accounts and records of his expenses.
*   The Subcontractor allows Aker Kvaerner Chemetics to inspect the accounts and records which he is required to keep at any time within working hours.
