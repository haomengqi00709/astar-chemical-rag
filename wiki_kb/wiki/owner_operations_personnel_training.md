# Owner Operations Personnel Training

Training of the Owner’s operations personnel is the foundational first step in the overall [[commissioning_planning_and_management]] process, ensuring the Owner's team is fully prepared for plant operation.

**Training Manuals and Lesson Plans**
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **will** prepare comprehensive manuals to support the training program:
*   [[operating_manual_1_man_0001]]: Operating Manual (1-MAN-0001).
*   [[analytical_manual_1_man_0002]]: Analytical Manual (1-MAN-0002).
*   [[training_manual_1_man_0003]]: Training Manual (1-MAN-0003).

A series of detailed lesson plans are prepared, covering essential operational scenarios:
*   Normal start-up and shutdown procedures.
*   Routine operation control and monitoring.
*   Troubleshooting of commonly encountered problems.
*   Upset response and recovery.
*   Emergency response and shutdown procedures.
*   Plant purging and preparation for maintenance.

**Training Delivery and Assessment**
Lessons are delivered primarily in the form of lectures given by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics process engineers. It is preferable for these lessons to be conducted at the site, allowing for plant walk-throughs and hands-on familiarization with the actual facility.

Trainees **will** be given a written test to assess their understanding. Additional training **will** be provided to address any common areas of misunderstanding identified through these assessments.

The Owner is responsible for preparing detailed operator instructions and checklists, and for delivering training on these specific instructions, checklists, and site safety procedures. Continuing hands-on training is integrated and delivered throughout the remaining phases of plant commissioning, reinforcing learned concepts with practical application.
