# Engineering Subcontract Scope Preparation (0-PRC-2203)

This procedure, 0-PRC-2203, outlines the methodology for preparing the Scope to be included in an Engineering Subcontract.

The Scope is formally defined in [[0-spc-2201_standard_conditions_for_engineering_subcontract|0-SPC-2201: Standard Conditions for Engineering Subcontract]]. It encompasses all information that specifies and describes the services to be rendered by the Subcontractor. Furthermore, it details any constraints or limitations on how the Subcontractor is to provide these services.

The information constituting the Scope is primarily found within the documents referenced by the [[engineering_subcontract_agreement_data_sections|Subcontract Data]]. It may also be conveyed through specific instructions issued in accordance with the Subcontract.

The [[engineering_subcontract_agreement_data_sections|Subcontract Data]] itself is a dedicated section within the [[engineering_subcontract_procedure_overview|Subcontract Agreement]]. The [[engineering_subcontract_procedure_overview|Subcontract Agreement]] is comprehensively defined in [[engineering_subcontract_procedure_overview|0-PRC-2202: Form of Engineering Subcontract]].

... It encompasses all information that specifies and describes the services, and states any constraints on how the Subcontractor Provides the Services. The Scope can be found in the documents referred to by the Subcontract Data, or in an instruction given in accordance with the Subcontract.
