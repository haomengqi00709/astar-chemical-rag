# 2.2 NO 1 Incident Follow-up Norm

This page refers to the Aker Kvaerner Group's 2.2 NO 1 Incident Follow-up Norm, which is a mandatory compliance document for the [[0-prc-8013_hse_incident_reporting]] procedure.

This norm establishes the requirements and guidelines for the systematic follow-up of all incidents, ensuring that corrective and preventive actions are identified, implemented, and tracked to completion. It also defines the reporting timeframe for incidents.
