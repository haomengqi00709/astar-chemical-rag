# Subcontract Meeting Procedures

To ensure effective collaboration and progress tracking throughout the engineering subcontract, specific procedures are established for meetings between Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Subcontractor. These procedures cover meeting frequency, logistics, and documentation responsibilities.

**Meeting Schedule and Types**
*   **Progress Review Meetings:** Biweekly progress review meetings **shall** be held to discuss project status, upcoming tasks, and any potential issues.
*   **Other Meetings:** Additional meetings may be convened as requested by either Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or the Subcontractor, as deemed necessary for project execution or issue resolution.

**Meeting Logistics**
*   **Teleconference Preference:** Whenever feasible, meetings will be conducted via teleconference to optimize efficiency and reduce travel requirements.
*   **Physical Meetings:** If a physical meeting is required, it will be held at either Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' or the Subcontractor's office, as directed by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Meeting Minutes**
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics holds the responsibility for all aspects of meeting minutes:
*   **Keeping Minutes:** Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **shall** be responsible for keeping accurate records of all meeting discussions and decisions.
*   **Transcription:** The minutes will be transcribed by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
*   **Issuance:** The official meeting minutes will be issued by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics to all relevant parties.

**Subcontractor Attendance**
Attendance by Subcontractor personnel at all scheduled and requested meetings is considered an integral part of the services provided under the Subcontract. No additional compensation will be provided for meeting attendance, as it is factored into the overall subcontract agreement.

**Meeting Frequency and Logistics**
Progress review meetings will be held biweekly. Other meetings will be held as requested by either party.
Meetings will be held by teleconference whenever possible. When teleconference is not feasible, meetings will be held at either party's office as directed by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Meeting Documentation**
Minutes will be kept, transcribed, and issued by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Subcontractor Attendance**
Attendance at meetings by Subcontractor personnel is considered to be part of the services of the Subcontract.
