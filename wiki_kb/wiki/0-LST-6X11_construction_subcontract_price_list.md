# 0-LST-6X11 Construction Subcontract Price List

The 0-LST-6X11 document serves as the Construction Subcontract Price List. It is a critical document for cost allocation and payment claims within a construction subcontract.

This list is used by the Subcontractor to allocate costs for:
*   [[construction_subcontract_direct_work_costs|Direct Labour]]
*   [[construction_subcontract_direct_work_costs|Direct Materials]]
*   [[construction_subcontract_indirect_work_costs|Indirect Labour]]
*   [[construction_subcontract_indirect_work_costs|Construction Equipment]]
*   [[construction_subcontract_overhead_costs|Overheads]]

For [[construction_subcontract_compensation_event_quotations]], the Price List provides the basis for insurance, financing, and security rates.

The Price List is also fundamental to calculating the [[construction_subcontract_price_for_work_done_to_date]]. Prior to the first assessment day, the Subcontractor **must** submit a proposed revision to 0-LST-6X11, subdividing line items to correspond with the accepted project schedule. [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_obligations_0_spc_2201|Aker Kvaerner Chemetics]] **will approve** and re-issue this document, which then **must** be used by the Subcontractor for payment claims.
