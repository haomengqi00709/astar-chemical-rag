# Commercial Bid Tabulation Template (0-TAB-5XXX-3)

This page describes the template 0-TAB-5XXX-3, which is used for documenting the detailed commercial evaluation of Vendor bids.

**Purpose:**
The [[commercial_bid_tabulation_template]] is a standardized form used by Procurement to conduct a detailed commercial evaluation of short-listed Vendor bids. It ensures a comprehensive review of commercial terms, financial standing, and historical interactions with Vendors.

**Content:**
The template typically includes sections for:
*   Listing commercial deviations proposed by each Vendor.
*   Comparing commercial approaches across different Vendors.
*   Incorporating the financial evaluation and recommendations provided by Finance.
*   Documenting any outstanding claims or disputes between the Vendor and Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
*   Recording any previous claims or disputes with the Vendor.
*   Stating the total value of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ current Purchase Orders with the Vendor.
*   Documenting clarification queries and Vendor responses related to commercial terms.

**Usage:**
This template is primarily used during the [[commercial_and_financial_bid_evaluation]] process for orders valued greater than CAD 200,000. The completed Commercial Bid Tabulation provides a critical commercial perspective for the final Vendor selection.
