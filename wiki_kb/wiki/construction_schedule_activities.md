# Construction Schedule Activities

The construction schedule component of the overall Project Schedule **will** detail all work activities executed by construction Subcontractors. This sub-schedule is vital for managing the physical build-out of the project.

Construction activities **will** be scheduled at [[project_schedule_detail_levels#level-2-schedule-control-schedule|Level 2]]. Construction work **will** be grouped by physical area, or by equipment or material class or type, to provide a logical breakdown of tasks. Major equipment items **will** be shown as individual activities within the construction schedule.

For each construction activity, trade responsibility **will** be entered, along with direct labour hours. This detailed input allows for the preparation of resource loading histograms, which are essential for resource management and forecasting. Activities **will** be assigned to Subcontracts as set out in [[0-prc-2003_project_execution_plan_overview]].

Subcontractors **will** be required to schedule their work at [[project_schedule_detail_levels#level-3-schedule-detailed-schedule|Level 3]]. Furthermore, Subcontractors **will** develop [[project_schedule_level_4_work_plans]] in the form of checklists to ensure thorough execution and quality control. Subcontractor’s schedule updates **will** be summarized as a percentage completion on the corresponding Level 2 activities in the main construction schedule.

The percentage completion for construction activities is derived from Subcontractor schedules, providing a clear measure of physical progress.
