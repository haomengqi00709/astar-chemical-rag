# Work Clothing Requirements

This page details the mandatory and recommended clothing requirements for employees working in non-office environments, particularly in construction or fabrication settings, as outlined in Document ID 0-PRC-8015.

**General Clothing**
Appropriate clothing for employees in a non-office environment **shall** consist of long-sleeved shirts and long-sleeved pants of proper fit. Shirts **must** be tucked in at all times. Excessively loose-fitting clothing is considered a hazard and **must** be avoided.

**Protection Against Moving Machinery and Tools**
Employees working with power tools or in close proximity to rotating machines **will** take care that loose clothing, long hair, ties, jewellery, accessories, etc., do not pose a danger.
*   Long hair **must** be tied up.
*   Jewellery **must** be removed or covered.
*   Ties **must not** be worn.
*   Clothing **must** be of proper fit to prevent entanglement.

**Specialized Clothing**
In situations where the skin or body of an employee may be in danger, special clothing **must** be worn (e.g., rainwear, chemical suits, flash suits). The company provides appropriate work wear to employees. Company-approved work wear with weekly laundry service **will** be provided for all shop employees.
