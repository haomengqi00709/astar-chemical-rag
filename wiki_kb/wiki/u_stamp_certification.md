# U-Stamp Certification

This page provides details regarding [[u_stamp_certification|U-stamp certification]] requirements, which **must** match manufacturer information and equipment data sheets for [[heat_exchanger_mechanical_checkout|heat exchangers]] and other pressure vessels. This certification is crucial for verifying compliance with ASME Boiler and Pressure Vessel Code standards.
