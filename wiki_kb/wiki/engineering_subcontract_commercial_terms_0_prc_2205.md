# Engineering Subcontract Commercial Terms (0-PRC-2205)

Procedure 0-PRC-2205 outlines the methodology for presenting payment terms and commercial conditions within Engineering Subcontracts. This procedure is critical for ensuring clarity and consistency in contractual agreements with engineering subcontractors.

**Scope and Purpose**
This procedure specifically describes how payment terms and commercial terms **will** be presented for Engineering Subcontracts. It assigns responsibility for the preparation, checking, and approval of these payment and commercial terms, ensuring a structured approach to contract development.

**Related Documents and Procedures**
*   [[standard_conditions_engineering_subcontract]]: Details the standard conditions that form the basis of engineering subcontracts.
*   [[special_conditions_engineering_subcontract]]: Addresses any necessary modifications to the standard conditions.
*   [[subcontract_specific_terms_and_conditions]]: Covers terms inserted directly into the body of the agreement.
*   [[approval_and_use_of_standards]]: Governs the approval and use of all standards, including those referenced in this procedure.
*   [[issue_of_engineering_subcontract_0_prc_2206]]: Sets out the conditions for the use of copyright documents.
*   [[engineering_subcontractor_bid_evaluation_procedure_0_prc_2204]]: Describes how additional conditions may be applied based on bid evaluation.
*   [[project_planning_0_prc_3001]]: Subcontract-specific conditions are indicated on the Project Execution Plan, which is part of Project Planning.

**Modification of Standard Clauses**
