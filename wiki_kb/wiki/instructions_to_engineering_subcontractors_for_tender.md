# Instructions to Engineering Subcontractors for Tender (0-SPC-2203)

The tendering process for Engineering Subcontracts is guided by 0-SPC-2203: Instructions to Engineering Subcontractors for Tender. This standard document provides prospective subcontractors with all the necessary information and guidelines to prepare and submit their bids effectively.

**Usage and Modification:**
0-SPC-2203 exists as a standard document and can be used as-is for projects. However, if project-specific requirements necessitate modifications, the document may be altered. Any modifications to 0-SPC-2203 **must** strictly follow the procedures outlined in [[approval_and_use_of_standards]] (0-PRC-0001), ensuring that all changes are properly reviewed, approved, and documented. This maintains the integrity of the tendering process while allowing for necessary project-specific adaptations.

**Subcontractor Identification:**
The identification of Engineering Subcontractors invited to tender is a critical initial step in the procurement process. The project team is responsible for this identification, and the selection **must** adhere to the guidelines and procedures detailed in the [[project_execution_plan_overview]] (0-PRC-2003). This ensures that only qualified and suitable subcontractors are invited to participate.

**Covering Letters and Required References:**
The Subcontract Administrator is responsible for preparing covering letters for each Engineering Subcontractor invited to tender. These covering letters serve as the formal invitation and **must** explicitly reference 0-SPC-2203: Instructions to Engineering Subcontractors for Tender, directing the subcontractors to the comprehensive tender guidelines. Additionally, the covering letters **must** reference 0-REP-8XXX: [Template Engineering Subcontractor Survey], which is likely used to gather essential information about the subcontractor's capabilities and experience.

The preparation and issuance of these instructions are fundamental to a fair, transparent, and efficient tendering process, ensuring that all potential subcontractors receive consistent and complete information.

**Usage and Modification**
While 0-SPC-2203 can be used as-is, it may be modified for specific project needs. Any modifications to 0-SPC-2203 must strictly follow the guidelines outlined in 0-PRC-0001: Approval and Use of Standards.
