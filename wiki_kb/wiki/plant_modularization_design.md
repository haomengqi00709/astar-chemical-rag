# Plant Modularization Design

The plant will be modularised at off-site fabrication yards. The modules will be delivered to the site with all equipment, piping, instrumentation, cable tray, conduit, and instrument cabling pre-installed.

**Module Dimensions:**
*   **Preferred Dimensions:** Module dimensions should correspond to standard 20 ft or 40 ft shipping containers.
*   **Maximum Oversized Dimensions:** Maximum oversized module dimensions are limited to standard container lengths x 14 ft wide x 13 ft high.

**Structural Requirements:**
Module frames **must** be designed to provide sufficient strength and rigidity for:
*   Handling during transportation.
*   Setting and installation at the site.
*   Withstanding operational loads throughout the plant's lifecycle.

**Site Integration:**
The modules will be designed to sit on concrete plinths, which **must** be a minimum of 100 mm above the finished slab elevation.
