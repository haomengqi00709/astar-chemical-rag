# Metal Ductwork Painting Requirements (5-SPC-0010)

**Carbon Steel Surfaces**
The Vendor **shall** paint all carbon steel surfaces in accordance with [[7-SPC-0001_protective_coating_specification]] (7-SPC-0001: Protective Coating). This includes:
*   External support brackets, lugs, support saddles, etc., attached by welding to any ductwork **must** be painted.
*   Loose structural components and hardware **must** be painted.

**Specific Painting Instructions**
*   Lifting lugs and temporary expansion joint bracing **shall** be painted yellow.
*   Ductwork to be covered with insulation **shall not** be painted.
*   Stainless steel **shall not** be painted.
*   Internal carbon steel surfaces **shall not** be painted.

**Primer for Overseas Supply**
For ducting supplied from overseas, or where cosmetic requirements dictate, carbon steel ducting may be supplied with a weldable red oxide primer.

These requirements are part of the overall [[5-SPC-0010_metal_ductwork_vendor_scope_of_supply]].
