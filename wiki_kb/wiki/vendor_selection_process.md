# Vendor Selection Process

This page describes the final review and vendor selection process, which culminates in the choice of a successful bidder for electrical equipment.

**Final Review and Vendor Selection**
The final review and vendor selection **will** be a meeting by the participants shown in Table 2. This meeting **will** be guided by procedure 0-PRC-5003, "Bid Evaluation and Approval".

**Roles and Responsibilities**
The final review and vendor selection involves E, PE, PM, and PR.
