# Risk Register

The Risk Register is a dynamic document used in project management to identify, assess, and track project risks. Within the context of the [[0-PRC-4003-R1_project_change_management_flow_diagram|Project Change Management Flow Diagram]], the Risk Register plays a crucial role during the [[project_change_evaluation|Change Evaluation]] phase.

During this phase, the [[change_management_roles_and_responsibilities_0_prc_4003|Project Risk Management]] team reviews proposed changes to identify any new risks introduced by the change or any existing risks whose likelihood or impact might be altered. They also assess the [[project_risk_analysis_0_lst_2006|contingency requirements]] associated with these risks.

It is a mandatory requirement that the Project Risk Management team **must** update the Risk Register if necessary, to reflect:
*   Newly identified risks stemming from the proposed change.
*   Changes in the assessment (likelihood, impact, severity) of existing risks due to the modification.
*   Updates to mitigation strategies or contingency plans.

Maintaining an up-to-date Risk Register ensures that project management has a clear understanding of the risk landscape, enabling informed decision-making and proactive risk mitigation. This contributes to the overall health and stability of the project, especially when undergoing significant modifications. For general information on risk assessment methodologies, refer to [[risk_assessments]].

Within the context of the [[0-PRC-4003-R1_project_change_management_flow_diagram|Project Change Management Flow Diagram]], the Risk Register plays a crucial role during the [[project_change_evaluation|Project Change Evaluation]] phase, where Project Risk Management reviews proposed changes to identify and assess associated risks, including the assessment of contingency requirements. If a change is approved, the Risk Register is updated to reflect any new or modified risks and their corresponding mitigation strategies.
