# HSE Performance Reporting and Incident Management

Monitoring of Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) performance within Aker Kvaerner Chemetics ("the Company") follows the Aker Kvaerner Group guidelines. The Company procedure for collecting HSE performance measures is Standard-0-PRC-8002 HSE Performance Reporting.

**HSE Performance Indicators:**

**Lagging Indicators** (based on a 12-month worked hours moving average):
*   Lost Time Injury Frequency (LTIF)
*   Total Recordable Injury Frequency (TRIF)
*   Sick Leave as a percentage of worked hours

**Leading Indicators:**
*   Number of task risk analysis per employee
*   Number of risk observations per employee
*   Number of near-misses per employee
*   Inspections per employee
*   Safety Training as a percentage of worked hours

**Incident Management and Reporting:**
Synergi is the Group database utilized across the Aker Kvaerner Group of companies for:
*   Recording occupancy health and safety incidents and statistics.
*   Facilitating knowledge sharing.
*   Producing HSE performance statistics.

The Company **will** adhere to Group guidelines for entering occupancy health and safety information into the Synergi database.

**Reporting Requirements:**
*   All employees **will** report injuries, incidents, and near-misses to their Supervisor or workplace HSE Manager.
*   Management personnel **will** report incidents up the Company and Group Management as specified in [[0-prc-8013_hse_incident_reporting]].

For an overview of the Company's HSE management system, refer to [[0-prc-8000_administration_of_hse]].

**Operating System Performance Evaluation:**
*   The Operating System performance of each Business Unit is evaluated on an annual basis, using a self-assessment and a peer review.

**Additional Performance Indicators:**
*   Lagging Indicators are based on 12 months of worked hours moving average and include:
    *   Sick Leave as a percentage of worked hours.
*   Leading Indicators include:
    *   Number of task risk analysis per employee.
    *   Number of risk observations per employee.
    *   Number of near-misses per employee.
    *   Inspections per employee.
    *   Safety Training as a percentage of worked hours.

**Synergi Database:**
*   Synergi is the Group database used to record occupancy health and safety incidents and statistics.
*   It is also used for knowledge sharing across the Aker Kvaerner Group of companies and for producing HSE performance statistics.
*   The Company will follow Group guidelines to enter occupancy health and safety information into the Synergi database.
