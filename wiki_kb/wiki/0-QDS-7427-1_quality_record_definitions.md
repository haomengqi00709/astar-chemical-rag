# SS Tubing Tier 1 Quality Record Definitions

For Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7427-1, specific abbreviations are used to define the status and inclusion of quality records:

*   **AP (As Prepared):** This designation indicates that the quality record is required to be prepared by the vendor. These documents are generated during the manufacturing and inspection process.
*   **FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package):** This designation signifies that the quality record **must** be included as part of the final Quality Assurance Documentation Package submitted by the vendor. This package serves as a comprehensive record of all quality-related activities and certifications for the Stainless Steel Tubing Tier 1.

These definitions ensure clarity in the documentation requirements for [[0-QDS-7427-1_material_verification_and_certifications]], [[0-QDS-7427-1_non_destructive_examination_requirements]], [[0-QDS-7427-1_final_inspections]], and [[0-QDS-7427-1_packing_and_shipping_requirements]].
