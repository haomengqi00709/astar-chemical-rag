# Construction Subcontract Safety Management (0-SPC-6051)

This page describes the requirements for Construction Subcontract Safety Management as outlined in document 0-SPC-6051. The Subcontractor **must** prepare and execute site safety management programs in accordance with the guidelines and procedures detailed in this document. This includes, but is not limited to, hazard identification, risk assessment, safety training, and incident reporting.

Refer to [[0-SUB-6_construction_subcontract_site_management_and_personnel]] for the specific application of this program within the overall project site management.
