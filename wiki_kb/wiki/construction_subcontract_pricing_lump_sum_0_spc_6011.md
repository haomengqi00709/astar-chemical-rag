# Construction Subcontract Pricing - Lump Sum (0-SPC-6011)

This page describes the methodology for Construction Subcontract Pricing based on a Lump Sum model, as detailed in document 0-SPC-6011. This specification outlines the principles and procedures for assessing compensation events when the subcontract is structured as a lump sum agreement.

The assessment of [[compensation_events_and_assessment_0_spc_6001]] under the lump sum model **shall** adhere to the guidelines provided in this document, in conjunction with other relevant pricing schedules.

Refer to [[0-SUB-6_construction_subcontract_financial_terms]] for the application of this pricing method within the specific construction subcontract.
