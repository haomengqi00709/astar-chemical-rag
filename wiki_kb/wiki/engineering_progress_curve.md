# Engineering Progress Curve

The Engineering Progress Curve is a graphical representation illustrating the cumulative progress of engineering activities over time. It is a key metric for tracking project development and is referenced within the [[0-rep-2001_monthly_progress_report_format|Monthly Progress Report]] to show progress achieved and any impacts.
