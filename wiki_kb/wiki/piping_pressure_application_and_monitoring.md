# Piping Pressure Application and Monitoring

The safe and accurate application and monitoring of test pressure are critical for successful piping system testing.

**Pressure Application**
*   The Erector **will** ensure that the operator responsible for applying the test pressure is absolutely clear as to the required test pressure.
*   When pumping up by hand, the required test pressure **must not** be exceeded.
*   If the final application of pressure is by a mechanical pump or from a higher pressure system, a [[pressure_relief_device_requirements]] **will** be fitted. This device **will** be set to ensure that the required test pressure is not exceeded.

**Pressure Gauges**
*   Accurate gauges **will** be provided by the Erector.
*   Gauges **will** be submitted to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' site representative for checking prior to the start of pressure testing and at any time thereafter.
*   Gauges **will** be of a minimum face size of 150mm (6 in) diameter.
*   Gauges **will** be ranged to approximately twice the test pressure.
*   For more details on specifications, refer to [[pressure_gauge_specifications]].

**Test Execution and Monitoring**
*   Testing **will** be carried out in the presence of and to the satisfaction of the [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_site_supervisor_review]].
*   Test pressures may be taken at any point on the system being tested. A correction for static head **must** be agreed with the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site representative. Where static head is significant, the datum elevation **will** be the lowest point being tested. See [[static_head_correction]].
*   The test pressure **will** be maintained for a minimum period of 30 minutes. It **will** also be maintained for a time sufficient to allow the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site representative to complete his inspection, when necessary. This duration is part of [[test_duration_and_inspection]].
*   Non-return or [[check_valve_testing]] **will** have the test pressure applied simultaneously on both sides of the valves. On completion of the test, the Erector **will** ensure the release of the pressure on the downstream side of these valves.

**Leak Repair and Retesting**
*   Any leaks **will** be made good to the satisfaction of the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site representative.
*   The pressure test **will** be re-applied if any repairs have been carried out. This is part of the [[leak_repair_and_retesting]] process.

**Thermal Expansion and Cold Weather Precautions**
*   When the test has to be maintained for a period during which thermal expansion of the test medium might occur, the Erector **will** ensure that the test system is not subjected to a pressure greater than the specified test pressure. Refer to [[thermal_expansion_management_during_testing]].
*   During frosty or potentially frosty weather, the greatest care **will** be taken to prevent damage to the pipework by the formation of ice.
*   Under frosty conditions, the system **will** be completely drained before the end of work each day, even if testing of the system is not complete. These are critical [[cold_weather_testing_precautions]].

For an overview of the entire process, see [[pressure_testing_execution]].

**Pressure Application**
*   The Erector **will** ensure that the operator responsible for applying the test pressure is absolutely clear as to the required test pressure.
*   When pumping up by hand, the required test pressure must not be exceeded.
*   If final application of pressure is by a mechanical pump or from a higher pressure system, a pressure relief device will be fitted. The pressure relief device will be set to ensure that the required test pressure is not exceeded.

**Gauge Requirements**
*   Accurate gauges will be provided by the Erector.
*   Gauges will be submitted to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' site representative for checking prior to the start of pressure testing and at any time thereafter.
*   Gauges will be of a minimum face size of 150mm (6 in) diameter.
*   Gauges will be ranged to approximately twice the test pressure.

**Test Supervision**
*   Testing will be carried out in the presence of and to the satisfaction of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' site representative.

**Pressure Monitoring**
*   Test pressures may be taken at any point on the system being tested.
*   A correction for static head must be agreed with the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site representative. Where static head is significant, the datum elevation will be the lowest point being tested.

**Test Duration**
*   The test pressure will be maintained for a minimum period of 30 minutes, or for a time sufficient to allow the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site representative to complete his inspection, when necessary.

**Valve Handling During Test**
*   Non-return or check valves will have the test pressure applied simultaneously on both sides of the valves.
*   On completion of the test, the Erector will ensure the release of the pressure on the downstream side of the non-return/check valves.

**Leak Resolution**
*   Any leaks will be made good to the satisfaction of the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site representative.
*   The pressure test will be re-applied if any repairs have been carried out.

**Safety Considerations**
*   When the test has to be maintained for a period during which thermal expansion of the test medium might occur, the Erector will ensure that the test system is not subjected to a pressure greater than the specified test pressure.
*   During frosty or potentially frosty weather, the greatest care will be taken to prevent damage to the pipework by the formation of ice. Under frosty conditions, the system will be completely drained before the end of work each day, even if testing of the system is not complete.
