# Engineering Design and Outsourcing Strategy

All design work for the project will primarily be carried out in the Vancouver office, with specific exceptions for outsourced disciplines.

**Outsourced Design Disciplines:**
The following detailed design activities will be outsourced to qualified Australasian engineering contractors:
*   Detailed Civil and Site Preparation Design
*   Detailed Electrical Design
*   Detailed Instrument Design

The precise split in scope between Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and any engaged engineering contractors is clearly delineated in Data available in SQL: 0-LST-2001.

**In-house Design and Augmentation:**
Detailed structural design will be performed in-house by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics personnel. To augment existing staff for this specific task, contract personnel will be brought in as required.

**Specialized Instrument Specification:**
Instrument Specification preparation will be executed by Norpac Controls in Vancouver. This work falls under the existing Aker Kvaerner Chemetics / Norpac Strategic Supply Agreement.

**Document Review and Approval Requirements:**
*   The requirement for document review and approval by the Owner is explicitly set out in Data available in SQL: 0-LST-2001.
*   Any contractually required submittal dates for documents are also noted in Data available in SQL: 0-LST-2001.
*   A specific requirement for document review and approval by an Australian registered engineer is mandated and detailed in Data available in SQL: 0-LST-2001. These approvals **shall** be obtained from the engineering contractor(s) engaged for detail engineering or from other qualified entities for this specific purpose.
