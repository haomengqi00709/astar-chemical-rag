# Vendor Document Delivery Formats and Quantities

Vendors **must** deliver documents in specified formats and quantities to ensure compatibility and proper archiving within Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' systems. Adherence to these requirements, as detailed in Section 8 of the [[vendor_document_supply_0_spc_0001|Vendor Document Supply Specification (0-SPC-0001)]], is mandatory.

**Accepted Document Formats:**
Vendors **shall** deliver documents in one of the following formats:
*   **Native Digital Formats**: This includes original editable files such as AutoCAD, Microstation, or Microsoft Word documents.
*   **Digital PDF**: Portable Document Format files.
*   **Printed Paper Originals**: Hard copies of documents.

**Document Size Limitations:**
*   The maximum allowable document size for any delivered document **shall** be **D (22” x 34”)** or **A1 (594mm x 841mm)**.

**Required Quantities for Printed Paper Documents:**
*   Documents delivered in printed paper format during the course of project execution **shall** be supplied in **one (1)** quantity.
*   Operating, Erection, and Maintenance Manuals delivered in printed paper format **shall** be supplied in **ten (10)** quantities.
*   Quality Manuals delivered in printed paper format **shall** be supplied in **five (5)** quantities.

**Digital Storage Media and Compression:**
*   Digital documents may be stored on **CD-ROMs** that are capable of being read on a PC running Microsoft Windows 95/NT.
*   Digital documents may also be stored on **Floppy disks**, specifically 1.44 MB or LS-120 formats.
*   If files require compression, it **shall** only be done using the **PKZIP format**. No other compression formats are permitted.

These specifications ensure that all documentation is received in a usable and manageable form, facilitating efficient project execution and long-term record keeping.
