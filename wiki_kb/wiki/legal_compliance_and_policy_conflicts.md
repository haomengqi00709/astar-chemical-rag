# Legal Compliance and Policy Conflicts

Chemetics' Substance Use Policy (0-POL-8001) is developed with due consideration for applicable legal frameworks and is intended to operate in harmony with them.

### Compliance with Legislation
*   Nothing in this policy is intended to contradict the BC Human Rights Code or other applicable legislation. Chemetics is committed to upholding all relevant laws and regulations.

### Resolution of Conflicts
*   To the extent that any clause or portion of a clause within this policy conflicts with any applicable law, such conflicting portion of this policy **SHALL NOT** apply. In such cases, the applicable law will take precedence.

This ensures that the policy remains legally sound and adaptable to varying jurisdictional requirements.
