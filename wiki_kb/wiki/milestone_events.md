# Milestone Events

Milestone events are significant points or achievements within a project timeline that mark the completion of a major phase or a critical deliverable. These events are often used as benchmarks for tracking project progress, triggering payments, or conducting formal reviews.

In the context of a Bidder's offer for Goods and Services, especially when proposing [[progress_payments]], the Bidder **must** clearly list the specific milestone events to which proposed payments will be tied. For each milestone, the Bidder **must** also specify:
*   The value of the payment associated with its completion.
*   The estimated duration in weeks after the contract award by which the milestone is expected to be achieved.

Defining clear milestone events is essential for effective project management, enabling both the client and the vendor to monitor progress, manage expectations, and ensure timely completion of key project stages.
