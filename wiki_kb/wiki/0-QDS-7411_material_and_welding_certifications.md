# Acid Cooler Material and Welding Certifications (0-QDS-7411)

This page outlines the requirements for material verification and welding certifications for the Acid Cooler, as specified in document [[0-QDS-7411_document_details|0-QDS-7411]].

**Material Verification:**
Material Verification is a required item or activity.

**Mandatory Certification Requirements:**
*   Mill Test Certificates are required as [[0-QDS-7411_quality_record_definitions|FP]].
*   Mill Test Certificates require surveillance (witness) by Chemetics Vancouver.
*   Weld consumable certs. are required as [[0-QDS-7411_quality_record_definitions|FP]].
*   Weld consumable certs. require surveillance (witness) by Chemetics Vancouver.

These certifications and verification activities ensure that all materials and welding consumables used in the Acid Cooler's fabrication meet the specified quality and safety standards.
