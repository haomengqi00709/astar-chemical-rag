# Nickel/Molybdenum/Chromium Alloy Welding Standard Overview

This Standard, titled "4-SPC-0026 — Specification," defines the workmanship and quality requirements for welded fabrications utilizing nickel/molybdenum/chromium alloys. These alloys are specifically intended for use in aggressively corrosive environments.

**Scope and Application**
The Standard applies to various welded fabrications, including vessels, heat exchangers, tanks, piping, and other miscellaneous welded items, when explicitly called for on purchase orders and referenced documents. The materials covered by this standard are grouped under No. P44 of the [[ASME_section_ix_welding_and_brazing_qualifications]] Code. Specifically, it addresses Hastelloy C-276, which has the UNS designation N10276, and Hastelloy C-22, with the UNS designation N06022.
