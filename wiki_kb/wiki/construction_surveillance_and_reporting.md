# Construction Surveillance and Reporting

Construction surveillance is a critical process implemented to monitor and verify the quality of works supplied by Construction Subcontractors. This activity is carried out by suitably qualified Inspectors under the direct supervision of the Site Quality Supervisor.

**Scope of Surveillance:**
The scope of surveillance activities is comprehensively defined by three key documents:
*   The specific requirements detailed in the [[quality_data_sheets_qds]] issued for various equipment, instruments, electrical components, and bulk materials.
*   The overarching quality assurance requirements stipulated in [[0-spc-6071_construction_subcontract_quality_assurance]].
*   The procedural guidelines outlined in [[0-prc-6071_construction_subcontract_quality_assurance_procedure]].

**Surveillance Execution and Documentation:**
Quality Data Sheets are specifically formatted to facilitate surveillance, allowing Inspectors to directly check off completed activities. The Inspector **will** update surveillance activities directly within the native format version of the Quality Data Sheet, which is maintained in the project site file. To ensure traceability and accurate record-keeping:
*   The original document numbering of the Quality Data Sheet **will** be retained.
*   The document title (and corresponding file name) **will** be amended to specifically refer to the item inspected, incorporating relevant tag numbers, line numbers, or other unique identifiers.

**Surveillance Reporting:**
Surveillance reports are generated by publishing these updated Quality Data Sheets. The publishing process adheres to the guidelines specified in [[publishing_project_documents]] (0-PRC-0005).
*   **Status Marking:** Initially, the status of published surveillance reports **will** be marked as “As Built (In Progress)”.
*   **Revision Control:** Revision numbers for these published reports **will** be incremented with each update.
*   **Final Status:** The final version of surveillance reports, indicating completion of all checks, **will** have its status marked as “As Built (Complete)”.
*   **Approval:** All published surveillance documents **will** be approved by the Site Quality Supervisor, signifying official acceptance of the surveillance findings.

This systematic approach to construction surveillance and reporting ensures that all subcontracted works are continuously monitored for quality compliance, and that a comprehensive, auditable record of these checks is maintained throughout the project lifecycle.

**Surveillance Documentation and Reporting:**
The process of documenting surveillance activities is integrated with the [[quality_data_sheets_qds|Quality Data Sheets]]:
*   Inspectors update surveillance activities directly in the native format version of the Quality Data Sheet, which is maintained in the project site file.
*   The original document numbering of the QDS is retained for these updated versions.
*   The document title (and file name) of the updated QDS will be amended to refer to the specific item inspected, incorporating tag numbers, line numbers, or other relevant identifiers.
*   Surveillance reports are formally prepared by publishing these updated Quality Data Sheets.
    *   *Reference:* The publishing process follows 0-PRC-0005: Publishing of Project Documents.
*   The status of published surveillance reports will initially be marked as “As Built (In Progress)”.
*   Revision numbers for published surveillance reports are incremented with each update.
*   The final version of surveillance reports will have its status marked as “As Built (Complete)”.
*   All published surveillance documents must be approved by the Site Quality Supervisor.
