# Electrical Equipment in Hazardous Locations

When electrical equipment is required within a classified hazardous area, its selection and approval are subject to stringent requirements to prevent it from becoming an ignition source.

**Mandatory Approval:**
*   Where electrical equipment is required inside a classified area, it **must** be approved for the specific gas or vapour that **will be** present.

**Gas and Vapour Group Classifications:**
For the purpose of electrical equipment approval, gases and vapours are categorized into groups:
*   **Hydrogen:** Classified as Group B.
*   **TRS gases (Reduced Sulphur Gases):** Classified as Group D.

For comprehensive details on electrical equipment approval for specific gases or vapours, refer to [[NFPA_70_National_Electrical_Code]] (USA) or [[CEC_Canadian_Electrical_Code]]. These codes provide the necessary guidelines and specifications to ensure compliance and safety.

For an understanding of how hazardous locations are classified, refer to [[hazardous_location_classification_types]].
