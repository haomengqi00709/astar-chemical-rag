# Other HSE Committees

While the establishment of Joint Health and Safety Committees (JHSCs) is a primary focus for ensuring [[workplace_health_and_environmental_practices]], this procedure (0-PRC-8008) does not preclude the formation of additional Health, Safety, and Environmental (HSE) committees.

**Formation of Additional Committees:**
*   Other HSE committees may be formed as required by [[hse_management_and_employee_responsibilities|Company Management]] to address specific organizational or project needs.
*   Additional HSE committees may also be mandated by local regulations, ensuring adherence to [[compliance_and_standards]] beyond the scope of the standard JHSC.

**Transparency and Accessibility:**
*   Minutes of any other HSE committees **shall** be posted in a publicly accessible location. This ensures transparency and allows all employees and stakeholders to be informed of discussions and decisions made by these committees.

The flexibility to establish additional committees allows the company to tailor its HSE governance structure to specific operational contexts, complementing the work of the JHSC and the [[company_hse_management_committee]].
