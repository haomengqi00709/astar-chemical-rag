# Aluminium Bus Welding Definitions and Roles

This page outlines key definitions and roles pertinent to the Aluminium Bus Welding Standard (9-SPC-0001).

**Definitions**
*   **Agreement in Writing:** This term **shall** mean a submission provided in writing by letter, not merely by inclusion in approval drawings.
*   **Codes, etc.:** This term **shall** mean the latest edition of the relevant codes and standards ruling at the date of the contract.

**Roles and Responsibilities**
*   **Inspector:** The Inspector refers to an [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_quality_assurance_and_materials_engineering_group|Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Quality Assurance Inspector]] or another Authorized Representative. The Inspector's role includes monitoring work during fabrication and rejecting non-conforming materials or workmanship.
*   **Fabricator:** The Fabricator is responsible for ensuring regular and systematic supervision of all work (both in-shop and on-site) by competent staff. The Fabricator also bears responsibility for providing materials, components, and workmanship in accordance with this Standard, engineering design, and applicable codes, even with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' inspection.
*   **Supplier:** The Supplier (which may be the Fabricator) **must** make available to the Inspector copies of chemical certification for batches of electrodes or wire coils used. The supplier **shall** promptly advise Aker Kvaerner Chemetics in writing of any proposed course of action for non-conforming weldments, including details of additional testing. All repairs and retests for non-conforming weldments **shall** be to the supplier's account.

*   **Inspector:** Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Quality Assurance Inspector or other Authorized Representative.
