# Strainers, Check-Valves, and Steam Traps Inspection (5-PRC-0007)

The inspection of strainers, check-valves, and steam traps is an integral part of the [[mechanical_checkout_piping_5_prc_0007|Piping Mechanical Checkout Procedure (5-PRC-0007)]], verifying their correct type, tag, accessibility, and flow direction.

**Required Documentation for Checkout:**
*   [[piping_and_instrumentation_drawings|P&ID]]
*   [[piping_speciality_item_specification|Specialty Item Specification]]
*   [[pipe_isometric|Isometric]]

**Inspection Steps for Strainers, Check-Valves, and Steam Traps:**
*   **Flange Checks:** All flange checks for these components **shall** be performed as described in the [[flange_inspection_requirements_5_prc_0007|Flange Inspection Requirements]].
*   **Type and Tag Verification:** Confirm that the component type and tag number match the [[piping_and_instrumentation_drawings|P&ID]] and [[pipe_isometric|isometric]].
*   **Accessibility:** Confirm that the [[strainer_installation|strainer]], [[check_valve_installation|check-valve]], or [[steam_trap_installation|steam trap]] installation is accessible for both operation and maintenance.
*   **Flow Direction:** Confirm that the component has been installed with the correct flow direction, which is critical for their proper functioning.

These checks ensure that these essential piping components are correctly installed to protect downstream equipment, prevent backflow, and efficiently remove condensate.
