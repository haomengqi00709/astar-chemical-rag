# Worksite Safety Rules (General)

This page provides general guidelines for the display and content of [[mandatory_hse_site_postings|Work Site Safety Rules]] across Aker Kvaerner Chemetics facilities, as outlined in procedure [[hse_site_postings_overview]] (0-PRC-8004). These rules are essential for maintaining a safe working environment and are tailored to specific operational contexts.

For all worksites, with the exception of the engineering office environment, the most important site rules **will** be posted near the employee entrance.

**Readability Requirements:**
*   On a [[hse_construction_sites|construction site]], worksite safety rules **will** be clearly readable from a distance of four meters.
*   In a [[hse_burnaby_laboratory|laboratory]] or [[hse_toronto_workshop|shop]] setting, worksite safety rules **will** be clearly readable from a distance of two meters.

Section 5 of 0-PRC-8004 details the minimum safety rules required for various environments. Specific rules for different types of worksites are detailed in the following pages:
*   [[construction_site_safety_rules]]
*   [[commissioning_site_safety_rules]]
*   [[workshop_safety_rules]]
*   [[laboratory_safety_rules]]

All personnel are expected to familiarize themselves with and adhere to the rules applicable to their specific work environment.

### General Requirements for Work Site Safety Rules

*   Work Site Safety Rules must be posted near the employee entrance for worksites (with the exception of the engineering office environment).
*   Section 5 of the procedure 0-PRC-8004 outlines the minimum safety rules requirement.
*   Worksite safety rules will be clearly readable from a distance of four meters on a construction site.
*   Worksite safety rules will be clearly readable from a distance of two meters in a laboratory or shop setting.
