# Flowchart for Procedure at the RFQ Stage

This page serves as a placeholder for a flowchart detailing the procedural steps and decision logic during the Request for Quotation (RFQ) stage of pump sizing. The flowchart visually represents the sequence of activities, information gathering, and preliminary calculations performed by the Hydraulic Engineer and Requisition Originator.

**Key elements typically illustrated in the flowchart include:**
*   Initiation of the RFQ process.
*   Identification and collection of necessary data from sources like [[4-PDS-XXXX-R2_template_pump_datasheet_administration|PDS]], [[site_design_conditions_1_dst_0001|Site Conditions]], [[pipe_specifications|Pipe Specifications]], [[4-gad-xxxx_equipment_layout_drawing|Preliminary Equipment General Arrangement]], [[piping_and_instrumentation_drawings|Preliminary P & IDs]], and [[battery_limits_conditions_1_lst_0001|Battery Limit List]].
*   Estimation of pump kickback and its application to design flow.
*   Calculation of preliminary TDH, NPSHa, and Maximum Suction Pressure.
*   Preparation of the [[4-DST-XXXX-R2_template_pump_datasheet_administration|DST]] with preliminary data.
*   Interaction points between the Hydraulic Engineer and Requisition Originator.

This flowchart provides a quick reference and ensures a consistent approach to preliminary pump sizing.
