# Exempt Employee Overtime Compensation

This policy defines "Exempt" employees and their compensation for hours worked in excess of normal working hours.

**Definition of Exempt Employees**
"Exempt" employees are those in positions not subject to the statutory provisions governing the payment of overtime as contained in the Employment Standards Act. At Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, "Exempt" positions include:
*   Supervisors at Hay point 450 or higher.
*   Managers.
*   Engineers.
*   "Engineers in Training".
*   Employees in equivalent professional positions to engineers.

**Compensation for Exempt Employees**
Employees in positions evaluated at Hay point 639 or lower will be paid for hours worked in excess of the normal hours of work. However, employees in positions evaluated at Hay point 640 or above will not normally be paid for hours worked in excess of the normal hours of work.

For "Exempt" employees who are eligible for compensation, payment will be at the employee’s hourly rate of pay. This hourly rate is calculated by dividing the employee's annual salary by 1,950.

Eligible "Exempt" employees may also request that time equivalent to the compensation be [[0-pol-9113_banked_time_policy]].

This policy is part of the broader [[0-pol-9113_overtime_compensation_policy_overview]]. For details on other employee classifications, refer to [[0-pol-9113_non_exempt_employee_overtime_compensation]].
