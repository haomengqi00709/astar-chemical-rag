# Carbon Steel Dry Chlorine Piping - Welding, NDE, and Low Temperature Service (5-SPC-0101)

Welding and Non-Destructive Examination (NDE) for carbon steel dry chlorine piping **must** adhere to stringent standards to ensure system integrity and safety.

**Non-Destructive Examination (NDE)**
Dry liquid or gaseous chlorine piping systems installed within the plant **shall** be subjected to 20 percent radiography of all circumferential butt welds, fabricated laps, and branch connections. This radiography **shall** be in accordance with [[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping]] paragraph 341.4. This **shall** be interpreted to mean 100 percent radiography requirements as specified in [[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping]] Table 341.3.2A.

**Low Temperature Service Requirements**
Where the metal temperature is less than -29°C (-20°F), Charpy testing of piping and welds **shall** be necessary.

**Shop Fabrication Compliance**
Shop fabrication and non-destructive testing **must** comply with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics design criteria.

Refer to [[ASME_section_v_non_destructive_examination]] for general NDE principles and [[carbon_steel_welding_inspection_and_testing]] for specific carbon steel welding inspection details.
