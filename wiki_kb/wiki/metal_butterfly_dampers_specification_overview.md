# Metal Butterfly Dampers Specification Overview (5-SPC-0014)

Specification 5-SPC-0014 outlines Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' comprehensive requirements for the design, fabrication, assembly, and testing of metal butterfly dampers. This document serves as a critical reference for vendors supplying these components, ensuring adherence to stringent quality and performance standards.

The scope of this specification encompasses the complete control damper, including the fabricated damper body, reinforced blade, drive and tail shafts, stuffing boxes, and the supply, mounting, and assembly of actuation equipment. It also covers essential activities such as instrument calibration and functional testing.

The Vendor is responsible for the full shop assembly and testing of the equipment. This includes ensuring that the damper is suitable for installation and continuous operation in an outdoor environment, as further detailed in [[site_design_conditions]] (1-DST-0001).

**Vendor Scope and Exclusions:**
While the Vendor is responsible for the supply and assembly of the damper and its actuation equipment, certain activities are explicitly excluded from the Vendor's scope:
*   Installation of damper bodies into ductwork is to be performed by Others.
*   Supply and installation of insulation and cladding are to be performed by Others.

**Sourcing Assumptions:**
This specification assumes that the damper bodies and actuation equipment are sourced together from a single vendor. If actuation equipment is to be sourced separately, this specification **MUST** be modified accordingly to reflect the revised scope and responsibilities.

**General Requirements:**
The list of goods and services provided by the Vendor is not limited to the items explicitly specified herein. Vendors are expected to provide all necessary components and services to deliver a fully functional and compliant metal butterfly damper.
