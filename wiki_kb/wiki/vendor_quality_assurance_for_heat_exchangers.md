# Vendor Quality Assurance for CS Gas to Gas Heat Exchangers

This page details the vendor quality assurance requirements specifically for the fabrication of CS Gas to Gas Heat Exchangers (Vestibules & Shells), as outlined in Quality Data Sheet [[0-QDS-7403-1_quality_data_sheet_details]].

**Quality Plan and Compliance**
The Vendor **must** prepare a Quality Plan. This plan **shall** be in accordance with the general [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment|Vendor Quality Assurance]] specification. The Vendor's overall Quality Assurance program **must** comply with both this specific Quality Data Sheet (0-QDS-7403-1) and the overarching [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment|Vendor Quality Assurance]] specification.

**Surveillance Level**
Surveillance activities conducted by Chemetics for this project will be at **Level 2.0**. This level of surveillance indicates a specific degree of oversight and involvement from Chemetics personnel during the fabrication process. Details on surveillance levels can be found in [[0-QDS-7_aker_kvaerner_surveillance]].

For a comprehensive understanding of quality records and their definitions, refer to [[0-QDS-7_quality_record_definitions]]. Specific inspection and testing requirements for these heat exchangers are detailed in [[heat_exchanger_inspection_and_testing_requirements]].

Surveillance by Chemetics will be to Level 2.0.
