# Purchase Order Price Adjustment Proportions (0-SPC-5003 Table 2)

This page describes the content of Table 2 within document **0-SPC-5003**.

[[0-SPC-5003_table_2]] outlines the proportions associated with each index used in the calculation of the [[0-SPC-5003_price_adjustment|Price Adjustment Factor]]. These proportions, as stated in the [[purchase_order_commercial_and_contractual_terms|Purchase Order]], are multiplied by the (L - B)/B ratio for their respective linked indexes to determine the total Price Adjustment Factor. This table is essential for the detailed methodology of price adjustments as described in the [[0-SPC-5003_price_adjustment]] section.
