# HSE Audit Program Details

This page outlines the requirements for the HSE audit program, including scheduling, process, and self-assessment, as specified in Document ID: 0-PRC-8009.

**Audit Announcements**
Audits conducted by the Business Unit HSE management as part of the HSE quality assurance process **SHALL** be announced to the worksite management no less than 7 days prior to the visit.

**Audit Frequency**
*   **Permanent Work Sites**: Work sites with an average of more than 25 employees **SHALL** be audited annually.
*   **Temporary Construction Work Sites**:
    *   **SHALL** be audited at the 15% construction completion stage.
    *   Following the initial audit, they **SHALL** then be audited every 6 months.
    *   Minimally, a construction site **SHALL** be audited at the 15% completion stage and at the 50% completion stage.

**Audit Process**
The audit process **SHALL** consist of two basic steps:
1.  **HSE Operating System Audit**: An in-depth review of the HSE management system.
2.  **Worksite Inspection**: A physical inspection of the worksite, similar to those described in [[0-PRC-8009_hse_inspection_program_details]].

**Annual Self-Assessment**
The Company **SHALL** conduct an annual self-assessment of the Business Unit's overall HSE operations.
*   This annual self-assessment **SHALL** utilize a consensus process, led by the HSE Manager of the Business Unit.
*   The self-assessment methodology is described in section 3.3 of Group Policy 2.2 PR 3 “HSE Operating System Procedure”.
*   Assessment results **SHALL** be submitted to Aker Kvaerner Group auditors for their information and follow-up.

**Worksite Assessment and Scoring**
Company worksites **SHALL** be assessed and scored using the HSE Operating System Assessment tool.
*   Assessment and scoring **SHALL** be performed by the HSE Manager of the Business Unit.
*   The methodology for assessment and scoring is detailed in section 3.3 of Group Policy 2.2 PR 3 “HSE Operating System Procedure”.
*   The Excel-based assessment tool is identified as 2.2 DI 4 HSE Operating System Assessment.
*   As per section 3.3.5 of the “HSE Operating System Assessment”, improvement actions identified during these assessments **SHALL** be managed to completion as an integral part of the Business Unit’s local management system.

**Annual Learning Review**
The Company as a whole **SHALL** conduct an annual “Learning Review” of its assessment with the HSE Manager of another Aker Kvaerner Business Unit. This fosters cross-organizational learning and continuous improvement in HSE practices.

For guidelines on interacting with external inspectors, refer to [[0-PRC-8009_hse_external_inspector_interaction]].
