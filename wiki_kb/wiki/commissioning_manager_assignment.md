# Commissioning Manager Assignment

The assignment of the Commissioning Manager is a critical step in project staffing, ensuring dedicated leadership for the commissioning phase.

**Mandatory Requirements:**
*   The Director of Technology **shall** assign the Commissioning Manager.
*   The assignment of the Commissioning Manager **requires** the explicit agreement of that individual's Discipline Manager or Chief Engineer.

This ensures alignment between the project's commissioning needs and the availability and expertise within the relevant discipline.

For an overview of the procedure governing all project responsibility assignments, refer to [[0-prc-2001_document_overview]]. This role is a key part of the overall [[assignment_of_project_responsibilities]].
