# Business Expense Reimbursement Policy (0-PRC-9003)

This procedure details the process for employees to request reimbursement for travel or other business expenses that they have paid directly.

**General Principles**
All business travel expenses **must** comply with the requirements of the [[travel_policy_compliance_and_deviations|Group Travel Policy]], which can be accessed through the eNet. Employees **shall** act reasonably when incurring expenses while travelling on company business and **must** frequent establishments consistent with their travel purpose and the Group Travel Policy. The Group Travel Policy explicitly states that travellers **must** "behave as though you were paying for the journey yourself."

Employees will be reimbursed for all reasonable expenses that they would otherwise not have incurred if they were not travelling. When in doubt regarding the reasonableness of intended expenses, employees **should** consult with their supervisor. Compliance with the Group Travel Policy for business travel expenses is "as applicable."

**Specific Allowances**
*   **Luggage and Briefcase Wear and Tear:** Employees may claim CAD 20 for each trip away from their home base to cover wear and tear on luggage and briefcases.
*   **Credit Card Annual Fee:** Employees who normally pay in excess of CAD 500 per year directly for travel or other business expenses will be reimbursed for the annual fee of a credit card. The maximum reimbursement for a credit card annual fee is CAD 55.

**Exclusions**
[[0-pol-9111_site_compensation_policy_overview|Site compensation]] (0-POL-9111: Site Compensation) is intended to reimburse employees for most expenses they would not have incurred if not travelling. Therefore, site compensation **should not** be claimed as a business expense.
