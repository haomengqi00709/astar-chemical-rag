# Equipment Data Sheets

Equipment Data Sheets are detailed technical documents that specify the mechanical design and fabrication requirements for individual equipment items. These sheets are crucial for communicating precise technical information to vendors and ensuring that manufactured equipment meets all project standards.

**Content Elements**
Equipment Data Sheets **must** contain the following information:

*   **Design Conditions:** All relevant process and environmental conditions under which the equipment is designed to operate.
*   **Pressure Test Requirements:** Specific parameters and procedures for pressure testing the equipment.
*   **Applicable Design Codes and Standards:** References to all national and international codes and standards that the equipment design and fabrication **must** comply with.
*   **Applicable Specifications:** References to all relevant project-specific or Aker Kvaerner Chemetics specifications.
*   **Required Materials of Construction:** Detailed specifications for all materials used in the equipment's fabrication.
*   **Accessories to be Supplied:** A list and description of any ancillary components or fittings that **must** be provided with the main equipment.
*   **Description of Protection against Over Pressure/Temperature:** Details of safety devices or design features implemented to protect against overpressure or overtemperature conditions.
*   **Nozzle Tables:** Comprehensive tables detailing all nozzles, including their sizes, ratings, and locations.
*   **Request for Specific Vendor Information:** A clear request for particular information or documentation that the vendor **must** provide in subsequent submittals.

**Document Preparation and Approval**
The preparation, checking, and approval of Equipment Data Sheets follow a defined workflow:

*   **Preparation:** Equipment Data Sheets **must** be prepared by Mechanical personnel.
*   **Checking:** The documents **must** be checked by Process personnel.
*   **Approval:** Final approval **must** be granted by Mechanical personnel.

**Content Elements**
Equipment Data Sheets **must** contain:
*   Design conditions.
*   Pressure test requirements.
*   All applicable design codes and standards.
*   All applicable specifications.
*   Required materials of construction.
*   Any accessories to be supplied.
*   Description of protection against over pressure/temperature.
*   Nozzle tables.
*   Request for specific information to be provided by the Vendor on subsequent document submittals.

**Responsibilities**
*   **Prepared by:** Mechanical
*   **Checked by:** Process
*   **Approved by:** Mechanical
