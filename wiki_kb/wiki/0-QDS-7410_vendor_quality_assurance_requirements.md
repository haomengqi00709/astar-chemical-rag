# Stack Quality Data Sheet Vendor Quality Assurance and Surveillance

For the Stack component, the Vendor's Quality Assurance (QA) program **must** adhere to specific requirements outlined in this Quality Data Sheet and referenced standards.

**Vendor Quality Assurance Compliance (Item 0.0):**
The Vendor **must** prepare a Quality Plan in accordance with [[0-SPC-7002_vendor_quality_assurance]]. The Vendor's Quality Assurance program **must** comply with the requirements set forth in this Quality Data Sheet (0-QDS-7410) and with [[0-SPC-7002_vendor_quality_assurance]].

**Chemetics Surveillance Level:**
Chemetics' surveillance activities for this project **will** be conducted to Level 2.0. The specific details and scope of this surveillance **will** be as set out within this Quality Data Sheet.

For broader context on vendor quality assurance, refer to [[vendor_quality_assurance]] and [[subcontractor_quality_assurance_0_prc_7006]]. Specific requirements for equipment-related QA can be found in [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment]]. Information on Aker Kvaerner's general surveillance levels is available in [[0-QDS-7_aker_kvaerner_surveillance]].
