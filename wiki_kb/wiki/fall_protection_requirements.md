# Fall Protection Requirements

This page details the mandatory requirements for fall protection devices and systems for employees working at elevated heights, as outlined in Document ID 0-PRC-8015.

**Mandatory Fall Protection Use**
Any employee engaging in work more than 2.4 metres above safe ground **must** use a fall protection device.

**Standards and Devices**
Fall protection devices **must** meet all requirements outlined in CSA standard Z259 (or ANSI equivalent). Fall protection devices include safety belts, lanyards, personnel lowering devices, lifelines, body belts, and safety straps.

**Performance Criteria for Fall Arresting Devices**
*   Fall arresting devices **must** prevent the protected employee from enduring a fall force of more than 8 kN.
*   Fall arresting devices **must** prevent the protected employee from free-falling more than 1.2 metres.

**Anchor Requirements**
The anchor of a fall protection system **must** be able to withstand a force of 17.8 kN.

**Inspection and Maintenance**
All fall prevention equipment **must** be inspected before and after each use for possible damage.

**Ladder Safety**
When ladders are used, they **must** be tied off or held by a spotter to ensure stability and prevent falls.
