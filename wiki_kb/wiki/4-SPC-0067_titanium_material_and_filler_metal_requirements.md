# Titanium Material and Filler Metal Requirements (4-SPC-0067)

This page details the specific material properties and filler metal selection criteria for titanium welding, as defined in specification 4-SPC-0067.

**Titanium Grades Covered**
The specification applies to titanium with the following UNS numbers, which correspond to specific grades:
*   UNS R50250 (Grade 1)
*   UNS R50400 (Grade 2)
*   UNS R52400 (Grade 7)
*   UNS R52402 (Grade 16)
*   UNS R53400 (Grade 12)

For general [[material_selection]] guidelines, refer to relevant project specifications.

**Nitric Acid Service Requirements**
For titanium components intended for nitric acid service, specific chemical composition limits **must** be adhered to:
*   Any grade of titanium **shall not** contain more than 0.05% iron.
*   Any grade of titanium **shall not** contain more than 60 ppm hydrogen.

**Welding Filler Metal Specifications**
All weld filler metals **must** meet the requirements of [[ASME_bpvc_section_ii_materials]], Part C.

*   **Grades 1, 2, 7, and 16**: Filler metals for these grades **shall** satisfy the requirements of [[ASME_sfa_5_16_titanium_and_titanium_alloy_electrodes_and_rods]] and fall within the F-No. 51 filler metal group.
*   **Grade 12**: Filler metals for Grade 12 **shall** satisfy the requirements of [[ASME_sfa_5_16_titanium_and_titanium_alloy_electrodes_and_rods]] and fall within the F-No. 52 filler metal group.

**Filler Metal Matching and Specific Applications**
*   The grade of filler metal **shall** generally match that of the base metal being welded.
*   Grade 7 filler metal **shall** be used to join Grade 16 to Grade 16.
*   Grade 7 filler metal **shall** also be used to join Grades 7 or 16 to Grades 1 or 2.
*   Grade 2 filler metal **may** be used to join Grades 7 or 16 to Grades 1 or 2, provided the completed weld is openly exposed to the process and does not form part of a crevice or a gasketted joint.

**Filler Metal for Nitric Acid Service**
For nitric acid service, filler metal **shall not** contain more than 0.05% iron and **shall not** contain more than 60 ppm hydrogen.

**Material Requirements for Nitric Acid Service**
For components intended for nitric acid service, any grade of titanium used must not contain more than 0.05% iron and not more than 60 ppm hydrogen.

**General Filler Metal Requirements**
All weld filler metals must meet the requirements of ASME Section II, Part C.

**Specific Filler Metal Selection**
*   Filler metals for Grades 1, 2, 7, and 16 must satisfy the requirements of SFA-5.16 and fall within the F-No. 51 filler metal group.
*   Filler metals for Grade 12 must satisfy the requirements of SFA-5.16 and fall within the F-No. 52 filler metal group.
*   Generally, the grade of filler metal will match that of the base metal.
*   Grade 7 filler metal will be used to join Grade 16 to Grade 16.
*   Grade 7 filler metal will be used to join Grades 7 or 16 to Grades 1 or 2.
*   Grade 2 filler metal may be used to join Grades 7 or 16 to Grades 1 or 2, provided the completed weld is openly exposed to the process and does not form part of a crevice or a gasketted joint.

**Filler Metal Requirements for Nitric Acid Service**
For nitric acid service, filler metal must not contain more than 0.05% iron and not more than 60 ppm hydrogen.
