# Substance Use Policy Scope and Definitions

This policy applies to all individuals providing services to Chemetics, including:
*   **Employees**
*   **Consultants**
*   **Agency Subcontractors**

For the purposes of this policy, the term "Employees" encompasses Consultants and Agency Subcontractors.

### Key Definitions

*   **Employee**: Any individual on the payroll of Chemetics working on a permanent/temporary and/or full-time/part-time basis whose pay is quoted hourly, weekly, bi-weekly, semi-monthly, monthly or annually.
*   **Consultant**: Any independent contractor hired by Chemetics to provide a specific service for a specific period of time.
*   **Agency Subcontractor**: Any individual under Chemetics’ direction and control whose services are retained through a third party for a contracted period of time, or for assignments of short duration, or for a specific service.
*   **Client**: Any individual, company, organisation, or other entity for whom Chemetics is performing work.
*   **Chemetics Work Site**: Any Chemetics, Client, or vendor work site location where Chemetics is performing work. This includes, but is not limited to, offices, construction sites, plant sites, and other places where business is being conducted.
*   **Illegal Drug**: Any drug not legally obtained or legally obtainable. This can include prescription drugs obtained illegally or not being used for their prescribed purposes.
*   **Prescription Drug**: Any drug which is legally prescribed to an individual and which is being used for the purposes for which it was prescribed or manufactured.
*   **Non-Prescription Drug**: Any drug which may be legally bought without a medical prescription and which is not illegal to possess.
*   **Controlled Substance**: Any (a) [[substance_use_policy_scope_and_definitions#illegal_drug|Illegal Drug]] or (b) any [[substance_use_policy_scope_and_definitions#prescription_drug|Prescription Drug]] obtained and used without a prescription or which is used in excess of the therapeutic dosage prescribed by a doctor.
*   **Reasonable Cause**: A belief based on observed, specific and objective facts.
*   **Safety-Sensitive Position**: Refer to [[safety_sensitive_positions]] for a detailed definition.
