# Aluminium Bus Welding Referenced Codes and Standards

The Aluminium Bus Welding Standard (9-SPC-0001) mandates adherence to the latest editions of the following codes and standards ruling at the date of contract:

*   **ASME Boiler & Pressure Vessel Codes:**
    *   [[ASME_bpvc_section_ii_materials|Section II - Parts B & C (Materials)]]
    *   [[ASME_sfa_5_10_aluminium_and_aluminium_alloy_welding_rods_and_bare_electrodes|SFA-5.10 (Specification for Aluminium and Aluminium Alloy welding rods and bare electrodes)]]
    *   [[ASME_sfa_5_12_tungsten_arc_welding_electrodes|SFA-5.12 (Specification For Tungsten Arc-Welding Electrodes)]]
    *   [[ASME_section_v_non_destructive_examination|Section V (Non-destructive Examination)]]
    *   [[ASME_section_ix_welding_and_brazing_qualifications|Section IX (Welding and Brazing Qualification)]]

*   **American Welding Society (AWS):**
    *   [[AWS_a3_0_standard_welding_terms_and_definitions|A3.0-80 (Welding Terms & Definitions)]]

*   **Canadian Standards Association (CSA):**
    *   [[csa_w47_2_aluminium_welding_qualification_code|C.S.A. W 47.2 (Aluminium Welding Qualification Code)]]

Fabricators and suppliers **shall** ensure all work, materials, and procedures comply with these referenced codes and standards.
