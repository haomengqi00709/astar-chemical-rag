# Carbon Steel Heat Exchanger Tubes Specification (4-SPC-0048)

This technical specification, Document ID 4-SPC-0048, outlines the mandatory requirements set forth by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for the procurement and use of carbon steel heat exchanger tubes. These tubes are specifically intended for the fabrication of [[gas_gas_heat_exchanger_fabrication_and_installation_requirements|gas to gas heat exchangers]] utilized within [[acid_plant_chemically_resistant_brick_specifications|acid plants]]. The rigorous conditions within these industrial settings necessitate strict adherence to material and manufacturing standards to ensure operational safety and longevity.

The specification applies exclusively to ASTM A178/SA178 Grade A tube material. Compliance with the latest version of ASTM A178/SA178 is a **mandatory** requirement, encompassing all other applicable standards referenced within it. This ensures that the fundamental properties and manufacturing processes of the tubes meet established industry benchmarks. Furthermore, dimensional tolerances for these tubes **shall** conform to the latest version of ASTM A450, which is critical for proper fit-up and assembly during heat exchanger fabrication.

The scope of this specification is strictly limited to the procurement of electric resistance welded (ERW) carbon steel tubing for gas/gas heat exchanger applications. This focus on ERW tubing highlights a specific manufacturing method, ensuring consistency in the quality and characteristics of the supplied tubes. Adherence to these material and dimensional standards is paramount for maintaining the integrity and performance of the heat exchangers in demanding acid plant environments, directly impacting the reliability and efficiency of the overall process.

For comprehensive details on the required inspections and tests, refer to [[4-SPC-0048_carbon_steel_heat_exchanger_tubes_testing]].
