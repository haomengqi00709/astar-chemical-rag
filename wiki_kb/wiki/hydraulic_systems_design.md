# Hydraulic Systems Design Requirements

Hydraulic systems **will** be designed and manufactured to be fully sealed, preventing any ingress of air or foreign matter into the system.

For general service conditions, the hydraulic fluid **will** be mineral-based. For high-temperature systems, the generation of hazardous areas **will** be avoided by using synthetic phosphate ester fluids.

Test points **will** be provided in all actuator lines, in pressure supply lines to directional control valves, and at points necessary to test the fluid pressure in hydraulic accumulators. Hydraulic test points **will** also be incorporated into air valving manifold blocks for testing respective pressures.
