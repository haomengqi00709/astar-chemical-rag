# Task Safety Awareness (TSA) Implementation and Requirements

The implementation of [[task_safety_awareness_overview]] (TSA) is a critical step in ensuring workplace safety. Adherence to the following requirements is mandatory for all tasks:

**General Requirements:**
*   No job is to start until the employees involved in the task complete a Task Safety Awareness review. This is a **mandatory** prerequisite.
*   The completed TSA **must** be posted at the location of the work for visibility and reference.
*   TSA **will be applied** in all areas of operations.
*   TSA **will be applied** before commencing a new task.

**Relationship with Job Hazard Analysis (JHA):**
*   A TSA is not generally required for jobs where a [[0-PRC-8007_job_hazard_analysis]] (JHA) has been prepared immediately prior to the job in conjunction with the work team.
*   However, if a generic JHA is available, a TSA is **necessary** to ensure all specific hazards likely to be present during the task are thoroughly considered.
*   A TSA **will be necessary** if conditions change during a task or if new workers become involved in the task, even if a prior JHA or TSA was conducted.
*   For new or complicated tasks, the HSE Manager **will determine** if a full Job Hazard Analysis is required. Where a full JHA is required, employees **will be led** by the site HSE Manager (or other qualified personnel) in performing it.

**Task Analysis:**
*   Employees **will** use the TSA method to analyze common tasks performed repeatedly, ensuring consistent safety practices.
*   Employees **will** give more consideration to the TSA of tasks performed less frequently, recognizing the increased potential for unfamiliar hazards.

The [[0-REP-8006a_task_safety_awareness_form]] serves as the official checklist and record for all TSA activities.

TSA will be applied in all areas of operations.
