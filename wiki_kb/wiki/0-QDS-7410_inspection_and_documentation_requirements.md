# Stack Quality Data Sheet Inspection and Documentation Requirements

This page details the various inspection and documentation requirements for the Stack component, covering material verification, coatings, final inspections, and packing. Compliance with these requirements is essential for ensuring the quality and integrity of the delivered item.

**Material Verification (Item 1.0) and Mill Test Certificates (Item 2.0):**
*   Mill Test Certificates **must** include Quality Record FP ([[0-QDS-7410_quality_record_definitions]]).
*   Chemetics surveillance for Mill Test Certificates **requires** to Complete (ü).

**Coating / Lining Inspection (Item 15.0) and External Coating / Lining Inspection (Item 16.0):**
*   Coating / Lining Inspection **must** include Quality Record FP ([[0-QDS-7410_quality_record_definitions]]).
*   Chemetics surveillance for Coating / Lining Inspection **requires** to Witness (ü).
*   Chemetics surveillance for Coating / Lining Inspection **requires** to Hold (ü).
For related information on coatings, refer to [[coatings_design_and_specification_7_prc_0002]] and [[coatings_design_criteria_7_prc_0001]].

**Final Inspection (Item 20.0):**
The following aspects of the final inspection **must** meet Chemetics surveillance requirements:

*   **Dimensional (Item 21.0):**
    *   Chemetics surveillance **requires** to Witness (ü).
    *   Chemetics surveillance **requires** to Hold (ü).
*   **Contour & finish of welds (Item 22.0):**
    *   Chemetics surveillance **requires** to Witness (ü).
    *   Chemetics surveillance **requires** to Hold (ü).
*   **Surfaces free from defects (Item 23.0):**
    *   Chemetics surveillance **requires** to Witness (ü).
    *   Chemetics surveillance **requires** to Hold (ü).
*   **Cleanliness (Item 24.0):**
    *   Chemetics surveillance **requires** to Witness (ü).
    *   Chemetics surveillance **requires** to Hold (ü).
*   **Item identification (Item 25.0):**
    *   Chemetics surveillance **requires** to Witness (ü).
    *   Chemetics surveillance **requires** to Hold (ü).
*   **Review QA Documentation (Item 26.0):**
    *   Chemetics surveillance **requires** to Witness (ü).
    *   Chemetics surveillance **requires** to Hold (ü).

**Packing/Crating (Item 33.0):**

*   **Packaging (Item 34.0):**
    *   Chemetics surveillance for Packaging **requires** to Witness (ü).
*   **Packing List (Item 35.0):**
    *   Packing List **must** include Quality Record AP ([[0-QDS-7410_quality_record_definitions]]).
    *   Chemetics surveillance for Packing List **requires** to Witness (ü).

These inspection points, combined with the specified documentation, ensure that the Stack meets all design and quality standards prior to shipment. For details on inspection release processes, refer to [[inspection_release_process_third_party]] and [[vendor_inspection_release]]. Related documentation includes [[third_party_inspection_reports]].
