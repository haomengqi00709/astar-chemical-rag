# Vendor Document Content Standards

To ensure clarity, consistency, and usability across all project documentation, Vendors **must** adhere to specific content standards for all documents supplied to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Language Requirements:**
*   Document content **shall** be prepared in the English language.
*   As an alternative, Vendors may prepare bilingual documents, presenting content in both English and their own working language. This ensures accessibility for both Aker Kvaerner Chemetics personnel and the Vendor's internal teams.

**Units of Measurement:**
*   Document content **shall** be prepared using [[engineering_units|SI units]] (International System of Units). This standardization is critical for technical accuracy and interoperability across different engineering disciplines and project phases.

These requirements, as specified in Section 3 of the [[vendor_document_supply_0_spc_0001|Vendor Document Supply Specification (0-SPC-0001)]], are mandatory to facilitate effective communication and prevent misinterpretations in technical documentation.
