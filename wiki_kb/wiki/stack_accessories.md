# Stack Accessories

This page outlines the "ACCESSORIES" category as specified in the [[4-dst-xxxx-r0_template_stack_datasheet_administration|Template Stack Datasheet]]. These accessories are integral to the safe operation, maintenance, and regulatory compliance of the stack.

**Key Stack Accessories:**
*   **Platform:** Provides safe working areas for inspection, maintenance, and access to instruments or other stack components. Platforms are typically designed with [[stack_material_selection|grating]] and handrails.
*   **At False Bottom:** Indicates accessories or features located at a false bottom within the stack, often for specific process functions or internal support.
*   **Aviation Light:** Mandatory lighting installed at specified heights on tall stacks to ensure visibility for aircraft, complying with aviation safety regulations.
*   **Lighting Protection:** A system designed to protect the stack from lightning strikes, typically including air terminals, down conductors, and grounding electrodes. Refer to [[lightning_protection_installation_details]] for installation specifics.
*   **Inspection Ports:** Openings in the stack shell or internal structures to allow visual inspection of internal conditions, linings, or components without extensive disassembly.
*   **Test & Instrument Ports:** Dedicated openings for inserting test probes, sensors, or instruments to monitor process conditions (e.g., temperature, pressure, gas composition) or conduct performance tests.
*   **Test Zone:** A designated section of the stack, often equipped with specific ports, for conducting performance tests or continuous emissions monitoring.
*   **False Bottom:** An internal structural element that supports packing, mist eliminators, or other internals, while allowing gas flow.
*   **False Bottom Access Door:** A dedicated access point to the area below or within a false bottom for inspection or maintenance.
*   **Drain in False Bottom:** A drainage point to remove accumulated liquids from the false bottom area.
*   **Access Ladder or Stairs:** Provides safe vertical access to platforms, inspection ports, or the top of the stack. Ladders often include safety cages.
*   **Painter's Trolley Truck & Trolley:** Equipment installed at the top of the stack to facilitate external painting or maintenance work, allowing a trolley to traverse the stack circumference.
*   **Rain Caps:** Covers installed at the top of the stack outlet to prevent rainwater ingress, which can affect internal components or process conditions.
*   **Gin Pole or Davits:** Lifting devices permanently installed on the stack to assist with the installation or removal of internal components, instruments, or maintenance equipment.

These accessories are selected and designed based on operational requirements, safety standards, and regulatory compliance.
