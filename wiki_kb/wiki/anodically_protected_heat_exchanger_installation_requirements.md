# Anodically Protected Heat Exchanger Installation Requirements

For heat exchangers equipped with anodic protection systems, specific installation requirements **must** be met to ensure the system's effectiveness in preventing corrosion. These requirements are in addition to the general field inspection items applicable to all shell and tube heat exchangers.

**Mandatory Anodic Protection System Component Installation:**
*   The **Controller / Power Supply (C/PS) must be installed** for anodically-protected heat exchangers. This unit provides the necessary electrical current to maintain the anodic protection.
*   **Cathodes and reference electrodes must be installed** for anodically-protected heat exchangers. Cathodes are the sacrificial components that complete the electrical circuit, while reference electrodes monitor the potential of the protected metal.
*   The **Remote Display Unit (RDU) must be installed** for anodically-protected heat exchangers. The RDU allows for monitoring and control of the anodic protection system from a convenient location.

Proper installation of these components is crucial for the functionality and longevity of the anodic protection system. All electrical installations **must** comply with relevant specifications, as detailed in [[electrical_installation_specification]]. For general field inspection items applicable to all shell and tube heat exchangers, refer to [[shell_and_tube_heat_exchanger_field_inspection_items]].

### Anodic Protection System Components:
*   **Controller / Power Supply (C/PS):** Must be installed for anodically-protected heat exchangers.
*   **Cathodes and Reference Electrodes:** Must be installed for anodically-protected heat exchangers.
*   **Remote Display Unit (RDU):** Must be installed for anodically-protected heat exchangers.
