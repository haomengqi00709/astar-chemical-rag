# Project HSE Training Planning and Budgeting

Effective planning and budgeting for Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) training are critical responsibilities for Project Managers and Construction Managers. These roles **must** integrate safety training into all project plans and budgets.

A key aspect of this planning is ensuring that management and supervisory training and education are carried out in a timely manner, aligning with project schedules and milestones. This proactive approach helps to embed safety leadership and competence throughout the project lifecycle.

Training needs **will** be analyzed and identified as part of the project planning process, ensuring that all personnel receive the appropriate and necessary instruction. Guidance for this process can be found in [[appendix_3_05_decision_logic_for_safety_training_program]].

This planning is a fundamental component of the broader [[0-PRC-8016_hse_training_program_overview]] and directly supports the effectiveness of [[management_hse_training]] on projects.

A key aspect of this planning is ensuring that management/supervisory training and education are carried out in a timely manner for all projects.
