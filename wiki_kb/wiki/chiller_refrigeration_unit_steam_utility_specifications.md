# Chiller/Refrigeration Unit Steam Utility Specifications

The steam utility specifications for Chiller/Refrigeration Units, as outlined in datasheet [[4-PDS-XXXX-R1_template_chiller_refrigeration_unit_datasheet_administration]], are relevant for absorption-type refrigeration units or those requiring steam for auxiliary functions.

Key steam utility parameters and their units are:
*   **Pressure:** kPa(g)
*   **Max. Pressure:** kPa(g)
*   **Temperature:** oC
*   **Flowrate:** kg/h (Value: VTA - Vendor to Advise).

These specifications ensure that the steam supply meets the operational demands of the unit.
