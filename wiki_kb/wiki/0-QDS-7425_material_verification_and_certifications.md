# Fan or Blower Material Verification and Certifications

Material Verification is a critical item or activity for the Fan or Blower equipment specified under Quality Data Sheet **0-QDS-7425**.

The following certifications are mandatory:
*   **Mill Test Certificates:** These **are required** as [[0-QDS-7425_quality_record_definitions|FP]] (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package). Chemetics **will Witness** the review of Mill Test Certificates.
*   **Certificate of Compliance:** This **is required** as [[0-QDS-7425_quality_record_definitions|FP]] (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package).

These requirements ensure that all materials used in the fabrication of the Fan or Blower meet the specified standards and project requirements.
