# Austenitic Stainless Clad Steel Welding Standard Overview

This standard, a direct copy of the original specification ES44/W04 "General Requirements for Welding Stainless Clad Steel," defines the workmanship and quality requirements for austenitic stainless clad steel welded fabrications. It applies when explicitly called for on purchase orders and referenced documents issued by or on behalf of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Scope and Application**
The standard covers welding and other fabrication requirements for a range of equipment, including vessels, heat exchangers, piping, and various miscellaneous welded items. All items **must** utilize 300 series stainless steel cladding. Other austenitic stainless steel cladding, such as Alloy 20-Cb3 (UNS N08020), is also covered. Acceptable cladding methods include roll bonded or explosion clad plate. All codes and specifications referenced within this document are to be interpreted as the latest edition ruling at the date of contract.
