# Packed Tower Pump Tank and Working Volume Criteria (4-PDS-XXXX-R2)

The Packed Tower Process Data Sheet (4-PDS-XXXX-R2) includes specific requirements for the associated pump tank and defines the criteria for its working volume. These specifications are critical for ensuring proper liquid management and pump operation within the packed tower system.

**Pump Tank Dimensions and Volume (with units):**
*   **Pump Tank Working Volume:** m3
*   **Pump Tank Diameter:** m
*   **Pump Tank SWH (Straight Wall Height):** m

**Working Volume Criteria:**
The datasheet specifies the following criteria for determining the effective working volume of the pump tank:
*   **Centreline pump suction to invert of overflow:** This criterion defines the usable liquid volume between the pump's suction intake and the overflow level.
*   **Bottom tank to invert of overflow:** This criterion specifies the total liquid volume from the tank's bottom to the overflow level.
*   **Other:** An "Other" field is provided to accommodate any additional or specific working volume criteria not covered by the standard options.

These criteria ensure that the pump tank is adequately sized and configured to maintain continuous liquid flow to the pump, prevent cavitation, and manage overflow conditions effectively.
