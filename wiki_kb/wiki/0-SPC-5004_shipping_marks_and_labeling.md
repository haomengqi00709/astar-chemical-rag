# Shipping Marks and Labeling (0-SPC-5004)

All packages **must** be clearly identified by painting or dye stencilling on the top and two sides of the exterior.

**Marking Specifications:**
*   Lettering for shipping marks **will** be black.
*   Lettering for shipping marks **will** be at least 8 centimetres high.
*   When palletised packaging is used, marks **will** be applied to a securely mounted plywood placard.
*   An example shipping mark format is: `96W11100-4006-1/6`.

**Handling and Storage Instructions:**
Handling, lifting, and storage instructions or restrictions, supplemented by standard international pictorial symbols, **will** be clearly stencilled on two sides of each package.

**Center of Gravity Marking:**
All packages exceeding 2,000 kg **will** have the centre of gravity marked on the projected location on three sides. The letters "C.G." and the shipping weight **must** be adjacent to the centre of gravity marks.

These labeling requirements ensure proper identification and safe handling of all shipments. This page is part of the [[0-SPC-5004_packaging_land_transportation|Packaging and Shipping for Land Transportation Overview]].
