# Fabrication Documentation, Nameplates, Packaging, and Shipping for High Silicon Austenitic Stainless Steel (4-SPC-0025)

**Required Documentation**
Three sets of the following documentation **shall** be provided to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics prior to the release of the fabricated items:
*   Material Test Certificates (covering parent material).
*   Material Test Certificates (covering welding consumables).
*   Corrosion test records.
*   Affidavit of manufacture.
*   Nameplate rubbing or facsimile.
*   Copies of approved welding procedures.
*   List of approved welders and their symbols.
*   A copy of test charts.
*   N.D.T. reports showing repair locations.
*   Post weld heat treatment charts.
*   "As Built" drawing marked up to show actual recorded dimensions.

**Nameplate Requirements**
The fabricator **shall** affix an Aker Kvaerner Chemetics nameplate to equipment where shown on the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics drawing. This is in addition to any nameplates required by codes such as ASME/TEMA. Details of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' nameplates are shown in Appendix A to this Standard.

All nameplates **shall** be securely fastened with stainless steel bolts or stainless steel rivets to a bracket welded to the vessel shell, as shown on the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics drawing. The Aker Kvaerner Chemetics nameplate will be supplied to the fabricator by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, normally with markings already engraved.

Nameplates are to be engraved or stamped. Letters and figures **shall** be not less than 1/8" high for 3"x4" size nameplates, with 5/32" high preferred. For 4"x6" size nameplates, letters and figures **shall** be not less than 3/16" high. All spaces on nameplates are to be completed.

The spaces on the nameplate designate the following information:
*   **Space A:** Equipment Tag Number
*   **Space B:** Equipment Description
*   **Space C:** Project Number
*   **Space D:** Year of Manufacture

Refer to [[nameplate_order_equipment]] for general nameplate ordering information.

**Packaging and Shipping**
All equipment **shall** be packed and shipped in accordance with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' Engineering Standards ES44\S-01 or S-02. For general requirements, refer to [[0-SPC-5004_general_packaging_requirements]] and [[0-QDS-7429_packing_and_crating]].
