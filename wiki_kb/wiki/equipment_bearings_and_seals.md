# Equipment Bearings and Seals

As specified in document 4-SPC-0001, the design and selection of bearings and seals for mechanical equipment **must** adhere to stringent quality and performance standards.

**Bearings**
Bearings for all mechanical equipment **will be** ball or roller anti-friction type. The minimum basic rating bearing life **shall be** L10h = 100,000 hours, unless otherwise specified on the equipment data sheet or relevant documentation. This ensures a long and reliable operational life for critical rotating components.

**Mechanical Seals**
*   **Single Mechanical Seals:** Single mechanical seals **will have** either a bypass flush or an internal flush system to maintain optimal operating conditions and extend seal life.
*   **Double Mechanical Seals:** Double mechanical seals **will be** of a balanced, self-aligning, double-seal type, providing enhanced sealing integrity and reliability, particularly in demanding applications.

**Shaft Assemblies and Fits**
Assemblies having rotating shafts or loose fittings with set screws **are not acceptable**, as these can lead to imbalance, wear, and potential failure. Surfaces **will be machined** for an interference fit in accordance with ANSI B4.1 or in accordance with ISO or DIN standards, ensuring precise component assembly and stability. Taper-lock bushings **are permitted** for shaft mounting applications.

**Shaft Sealing and Protection**
Rotating shaft packings and seals subject to dust and process loading will be protected by additional coarse sealing, e.g., labyrinth seals in addition to any mechanical seal.

**Mechanical Seal Types**
Single mechanical seals will have bypass flush or internal flush.
Double mechanical seals will be a balanced, self-aligning, double-seal type.

**Assembly and Fit Standards**
Assemblies having rotating shafts or loose fittings with set screws are not acceptable.
Surfaces will be machined for an interference fit in accordance with ANSI B4.1 or in accordance with ISO or DIN standards.
Taper-lock bushings are permitted.
