# Cost Tabulation (0-TAB-5) Bid Evaluation Criteria

The Cost Tabulation document (0-TAB-5) incorporates several key criteria for the comprehensive evaluation of bids, ensuring that all aspects of a proposal—from documentation to technical compliance and quality—are thoroughly assessed before an order is placed.

**Key Evaluation Criteria:**
*   **Supply of Documents per VDR:** This criterion assesses the vendor's adherence to the specified [[vendor_document_requirements]] (VDR) regarding the provision of necessary documentation.
*   **Document Delivery Schedule Acceptability:** Evaluates whether the proposed schedule for document delivery aligns with project timelines and requirements.
*   **Quality Assurance per QDS:** Assesses the vendor's quality assurance plan and its compliance with the project's [[quality_data_sheets_qds]] (QDS) specifications. This ensures that the delivered goods or services meet the required quality standards.
*   **Shop Inspection:** Determines if a shop inspection is **Required**, **Completed**, or **N/A** for the specific item. This helps verify manufacturing quality and progress.
*   **Meeting RFQ Technical Requirements:** Evaluates the extent to which the bid technically complies with the specifications outlined in the Request for Quotation (RFQ).
*   **Technical Acceptability (Y/N/Qualifications):** A final assessment of the technical proposal, indicating whether it is acceptable (Y), not acceptable (N), or acceptable with specific qualifications.

These criteria are essential for a robust [[bid_evaluation_and_approval_0_prc_5003|bid evaluation process]], ensuring that all aspects of a vendor's offer are systematically reviewed. For specific documents required during the bid process, refer to [[0-tab-5_required_bid_documents]].
