# Shell & Tube Heat Exchanger Material Verification and Certifications (0-QDS-7417)

For the Shell & Tube Heat Exchanger specified in 0-QDS-7417, stringent requirements are in place for material verification and the provision of associated certifications. These measures are critical to ensure that all materials used in the fabrication meet the specified standards and are traceable.

**Material Verification (Item 1.0):**
Material verification is a fundamental step in the quality assurance process. It involves confirming that the materials received and used for fabrication match the specifications and order requirements. This process helps prevent the use of incorrect or substandard materials, which could compromise the integrity and performance of the heat exchanger.

**Required Certifications:**

*   **Mill Test Certificates (Item 2.0):**
    *   **Quality Record Required:** FP ("Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package")
    *   **Surveillance by Chemetics:** Complete
    *   Mill Test Certificates (MTCs) are official documents issued by the material manufacturer, certifying the chemical composition, mechanical properties, and other relevant characteristics of the material. The Vendor **must** provide these certificates, and they **shall** be included in the final Quality Assurance Documentation Package. Chemetics will perform a complete review of these certificates to ensure compliance.

*   **Certificate of Compliance (Item 3.0):**
    *   **Quality Record Required:** FP ("Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package")
    *   **Surveillance by Chemetics:** Complete
    *   A Certificate of Compliance (CoC) is a document issued by the Vendor, stating that the supplied materials or components conform to the specified requirements of the purchase order and relevant standards. This certificate **must** be provided and included in the Quality Assurance Documentation Package. Chemetics will conduct a complete review of the CoC to verify adherence to all contractual and technical specifications.

These certifications, along with the material verification process, form a crucial part of the [[0-QDS-7417_vendor_quality_assurance_requirements|Vendor Quality Assurance Requirements]] for the Shell & Tube Heat Exchanger, ensuring full traceability and quality of materials. For definitions of quality record designations, refer to [[0-QDS-7417_quality_record_definitions]].
