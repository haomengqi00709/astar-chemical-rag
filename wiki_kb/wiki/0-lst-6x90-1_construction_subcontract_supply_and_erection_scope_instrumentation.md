# Construction Subcontract Supply and Erection Scope - Instrumentation (0-LST-6X90-1)

The [[0-lst-6x90-1_construction_subcontract_supply_and_erection_scope]] document (0-LST-6X90-1) defines the construction supply and erection scope of work for a project, including a specific subsection for instrumentation.

**Content Requirements:**
The Construction Subcontract Supply and Erection Scope (0-LST-6X90-1) **shall** contain, as a minimum:
*   Item Description.
*   Identify responsibility for supply of material (e.g., , Other, Subcontractor).
*   Identify responsibility for installation (e.g., , Other, Subcont).

**Document Preparation and Approval:**
*   **Prepared by:** Detail Design Engineer (DDE)
*   **Checked by:** Project Engineer
*   **Approved by:** Detail Design Engineer (DDE)

**Instrumentation Scope:**
Document 0-LST-6X90-1 indicates the full multidiscipline scope of the job. The instrumentation scope is a subsection within this document. The [[instrument_installation_specification_8_spc_0002]] **is to be read in conjunction with** document 0-LST-6X90-1.
