# Stainless Steel Heat Exchanger Tubes Specification Overview (4-SPC-0047-2)

Specification 4-SPC-0047-2 outlines Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ mandatory requirements for stainless steel heat exchanger tubes. These tubes are specifically intended for the fabrication of [[gas_gas_heat_exchanger_design_criteria]] in acid plants. The specification applies to ASTM A249/SA249 types 304/304L and 304H.

The primary purpose of this document is to ensure the procurement of high-quality tubing that meets stringent performance and safety standards for critical applications within acid production facilities. Adherence to these requirements is crucial for the structural integrity and operational efficiency of the [[gas_gas_heat_exchanger_design_criteria|gas to gas heat exchangers]].

**Scope of Application**
This specification is solely for the procurement of stainless steel gas/gas heat exchanger tubing. It details the material, manufacturing, and testing criteria that suppliers must meet.

**First-Time Suppliers**
Additional requirements, as detailed in [[4-SPC-0047-2_additional_testing_first_time_suppliers|Section 4]], are mandatory for suppliers who have not previously supplied tubes to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. These additional tests require consultation with and approval from the Materials and QA group to ensure compliance and maintain quality standards.
