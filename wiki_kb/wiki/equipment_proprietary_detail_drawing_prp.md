# Detail and Proprietary Drawings

Detail Drawings and Proprietary Drawings both provide fully dimensioned details of all components of equipment. While they contain the same technical information, their distribution and ownership differ significantly.

**Content Elements**
Detail drawings and proprietary drawings **must** contain the following elements:

*   Fully dimensioned details of all components of the equipment.

**Proprietary Drawings**
Proprietary Drawings are never issued to the Owner due to their sensitive nature and commercial value. These drawings typically contain intellectual property that Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or its vendors wish to protect.

**Standards Compliance**
It is anticipated that standards will exist for all Detail Drawings and Proprietary Drawings (per Section 3.14 commentary of 4-PRC-0003). For information on the approval and use of these standards, refer to [[0-PRC-0001_standard_document_management_procedure]].

**Document Preparation and Approval**
The preparation, checking, and approval of Detail Drawings follow a defined workflow:

*   **Preparation:** Detail Drawings **must** be prepared by Design personnel.
*   **Checking:** The drawings **must** be checked by DesignMechanical personnel.
*   **Approval:** Final approval **must** be granted by Mechanical personnel.

**Distribution**
*   Proprietary Drawings are never issued to the Owner.

**Responsibilities**
*   **Prepared by:** Design
*   **Checked by:** DesignMechanical
*   **Approved by:** Mechanical
