# Vendor Site Health and Safety Requirements

When [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_name_change_history|Vendor]] employees are scheduled to work at an Aker Kvaerner Chemetics construction site, specific Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) requirements **must** be met to ensure compliance and safety.

Prior to any travel to construction sites, all potential [[vendor_roles|Vendor]] safety concerns **must** be thoroughly considered and addressed. Vendor employees **shall** review all applicable construction HSE specifications prior to their site visit to familiarize themselves with site-specific protocols. Upon arrival at the worksite, Vendor employees **shall** attend an Aker Kvaerner Chemetics [[construction_site_hse_orientation|site safety briefing]]. This briefing provides essential information regarding site hazards, emergency procedures, and specific safety rules.

While on an Aker Kvaerner Chemetics work site, Vendor employees **shall** strictly adhere to all Aker Kvaerner Chemetics [[aker_kvaerner_hse_policy_manual|HSE policies and procedures]]. Any work procedure proposed by the Vendor **must** be approved by the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site manager prior to the commencement of any work activity. This ensures that all tasks are performed in accordance with established safety protocols.

Vendor employees **shall** use the required minimum [[minimum_personal_protective_equipment_requirements|personal protection equipment (PPE)]] at all times within the worksite. They are also responsible for arriving at the worksite already wearing the appropriate clothing and footwear as per specified standards. The Vendor **shall** ensure that their employees are fully aware of all Aker Kvaerner Chemetics minimum PPE requirements. Specialized PPE, specifically required by the Vendor for their employees to perform the contracted work, remains the sole responsibility of the Vendor.

All safety infractions and incidents **must** be reported immediately by Vendor employees to the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics [[hse_roles_and_responsibilities_company_wide|HSE Manager]] on the worksite. This ensures prompt investigation and corrective action, contributing to overall [[hse_incident_reporting|incident management]].
