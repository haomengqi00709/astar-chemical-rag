# Subcontractor Safety Commitment and Performance Evaluation

Aker Kvaerner Chemetics places paramount importance on the safety performance record of its subcontractors. A continuous emphasis on safety is maintained throughout all project phases, from the initial bid process through construction and contract execution, and extending to post-contract completion.

**Commitment to Safety**
Subcontractors **must** demonstrate a full commitment to safety and **shall** meet specific performance criteria to be eligible for participation in Aker Kvaerner Chemetics construction projects. This commitment is integral to the company's overall [[hse_targets_and_policy]] and [[aker_kvaerner_hse_policy_manual]].

**Safety Requirements in the Bid Process**
During the Pre-Bid meeting, Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **will** clearly explain all specific contractual safety and health requirements to potential subcontractors. Prior to any contract award, a safety preconstruction checklist **will** be reviewed with the Subcontractor's Field Supervisor to ensure alignment and understanding of expectations.

**Statement of Safety Commitment**
A critical requirement is that an officer of the Subcontractor **must** execute the "STATEMENT OF SAFETY COMMITMENT" at the end of the [[subcontractor_pre_qualification_report_0_rep_8405]]. By signing this statement, the officer **shall** personally assume responsibility for the safety and health of their own employees and all lower-tier sub-subcontractors. Furthermore, the signatory agrees that all employees **shall** at all times observe and comply with relevant legislation and Company procedures/rules.

**Safety Performance Evaluation**
Upon the completion of a subcontract, the safety performance of the subcontractor **will** be rigorously evaluated. This evaluation **will** assess the subcontractor's attitude towards safety, compliance with regulations and company procedures, responsiveness to safety concerns, and the occurrence of any injuries or incidents.

**Impact on Future Bidding**
The results of this safety performance evaluation **will** be recorded in the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics database. These results are a fundamental basis for compiling future bidders lists. Only subcontractors with favourable safety and performance records **shall** be allowed to bid on future work, reinforcing Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' commitment to a safe working environment. This process contributes to [[hse_performance_reporting_and_incident_management]].

**Worker's Compensation Board (WCB) Rates**
Subcontractors **must** provide a copy of their WCB assessment rates for each of the last five years as part of their pre-qualification, offering an objective measure of their historical safety performance.
