# Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7407: Large Fan Vendor Quality Assurance Requirements

This page outlines the mandatory requirements for Vendor Quality Assurance (QA) as specified in Quality Data Sheet (QDS) 0-QDS-7407 for Large Fan procurement. Adherence to these requirements is crucial for ensuring the quality and reliability of the supplied equipment.

**Vendor Quality Plan:**
The Vendor **shall** prepare a Quality Plan. This plan **must** be in accordance with the guidelines set out in [[0-SPC-7002_vendor_quality_assurance_bulk_materials]]. The Quality Plan serves as a comprehensive document detailing the vendor's approach to quality management throughout the manufacturing and supply process, ensuring all stages meet the required standards.

**Vendor Quality Assurance Compliance:**
The Vendor's overall Quality Assurance system **shall** comply with the specific requirements detailed in this Quality Data Sheet [[0-QDS-7407_document_details]]. Furthermore, the Vendor's Quality Assurance **shall** also comply with the broader framework established in [[0-SPC-7002_vendor_quality_assurance_bulk_materials]]. This dual compliance ensures both project-specific and general quality standards are met, providing a robust framework for quality control.

**Chemetics Surveillance Level:**
Surveillance by Chemetics for the Large Fan project will be conducted to Level 1.0. The specific surveillance activities and their requirements are detailed within this Quality Data Sheet, ensuring that Chemetics' oversight is applied consistently across all critical stages of production and inspection. This level of surveillance dictates the extent of Chemetics' involvement in witnessing and holding points during the vendor's manufacturing and testing processes, thereby maintaining strict quality control.

For definitions of quality record requirements, refer to [[0-QDS-7407_large_fan_quality_record_definitions]]. Details on specific inspection and documentation requirements are available in [[0-QDS-7407_large_fan_inspection_and_documentation_requirements]].
