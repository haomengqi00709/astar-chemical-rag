# Metal Butterfly Damper Stuffing Boxes and Packing

The stuffing boxes and packing system for metal butterfly dampers are designed to ensure a gas-tight seal and prevent leakage during operation.

**Stuffing Box Configuration:**
Stuffing boxes **SHALL** be supplied with two sets of packing installed in series, along with two lantern rings also installed in series.
*   Each set of packing **MUST** have a minimum of two rings of packing material.
*   The packing material **SHALL** be Garlock 1300 or an approved equal, ensuring high performance and chemical resistance.
*   Lantern rings **SHALL** be constructed from 316 stainless steel. Spring rings are explicitly **NOT ACCEPTABLE** for use as lantern rings.

**Stuffing Box Follower:**
The stuffing box follower **SHALL** incorporate a spring/bolt tightening design. This design is crucial to prevent over-tightening of the packing, which can lead to premature wear or damage.

**Stuffing Box Connections:**
Each stuffing box **SHALL** be provided with two distinct connections:
1.  One connection **SHALL** be used for the insertion of grease.
2.  The other connection **SHALL** be available for air purging. It is important to note that the air purging connection **SHALL NOT** be used under normal operating conditions.

Both stuffing box connections **SHALL** be routed to accessible locations on the damper body. These locations **MUST** be external to any insulation, or as close to external as practical, to facilitate maintenance. The stuffing box **SHALL** be greased with Rhotium HT-1000 synthetic grease.

**Welding Requirements:**
All welds on the packing gland **MUST** be continuous and gas tight, ensuring the integrity of the seal.
