# Chiller/Refrigeration Unit Process Fluid Specifications

The process fluid specifications for Chiller/Refrigeration Units, referenced in datasheet [[4-PDS-XXXX-R1_template_chiller_refrigeration_unit_datasheet_administration]], detail the characteristics of the fluid to be chilled. These specifications are essential for accurate equipment design and material selection. The "Fluid Description" field identifies the specific fluid.

Key process fluid parameters and their units are:
*   **Composition:** %w/w
*   **Flow Rate:** kg/h
*   **Inlet Temperature:** oC
*   **Outlet Temperature:** oC
*   **Max. Inlet Temperature:** oC
*   **Min. Inlet Temperature:** oC
*   **Density:** kg/m3
*   **Viscosity:** cP
*   **Specific Heat:** kJ/kg·oC
*   **Thermal Conductivity:** W/m·K
*   **Inlet Pressure:** kPa(g)
*   **Allowable Pressure Drop:** kPa
*   **Fouling resistance:** m2.C/W
*   **Fouling, % xs area:** % xss area

**Mandatory Requirement:**
*   **Note 1.0:** The vendor **must** recommend excess area based on the fouling nature of the fluids and standard industry practice. This ensures adequate heat transfer performance over time, considering potential fouling.

For general information on heat transfer properties, refer to [[heat_transfer_coefficients]].

*   **Fouling, % xss area:** Specified in **% xss area**.
