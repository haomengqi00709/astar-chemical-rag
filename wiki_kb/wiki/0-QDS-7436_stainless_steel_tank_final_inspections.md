# Stainless Steel Tank Final Inspections (0-QDS-7436)

For the Stainless Steel Tank project (Document ID: 0-QDS-7436), a series of final inspections are mandated to ensure the completed tank meets all specified quality and design criteria before shipment. These inspections are subject to Chemetics' "Witness" and "Hold" surveillance.

**Dimensional Final Inspection:**
*   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Chemetics requires both "Witness" and "Hold" surveillance for the Dimensional final inspection.
    *   **Witness:** A Chemetics representative **must** be present during the verification of all critical dimensions.
    *   **Hold:** The Vendor **shall not** proceed with subsequent steps (e.g., packaging) until Chemetics has formally released the "Hold" point after satisfactory completion and review of the dimensional inspection.

**Contour & Finish of Welds Final Inspection:**
*   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Chemetics requires both "Witness" and "Hold" surveillance for the Contour & Finish of Welds final inspection.
    *   **Witness:** A Chemetics representative **must** be present during the inspection of weld contours and finishes.
    *   **Hold:** The Vendor **shall not** proceed until Chemetics has formally released the "Hold" point after satisfactory completion and review of the weld inspection.

**Surfaces Free from Defects Final Inspection:**
*   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Chemetics requires both "Witness" and "Hold" surveillance for the Surfaces Free from Defects final inspection.
    *   **Witness:** A Chemetics representative **must** be present during the inspection of all surfaces for defects.
    *   **Hold:** The Vendor **shall not** proceed until Chemetics has formally released the "Hold" point after satisfactory completion and review of the surface inspection.

**Cleanliness Final Inspection:**
*   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Chemetics requires both "Witness" and "Hold" surveillance for the Cleanliness final inspection.
    *   **Witness:** A Chemetics representative **must** be present during the inspection to verify the cleanliness of the tank.
    *   **Hold:** The Vendor **shall not** proceed until Chemetics has formally released the "Hold" point after satisfactory completion and review of the cleanliness inspection.

**Review QA Documentation:**
*   **Requirement:** Review of the Quality Assurance Documentation is required to be included in the Vendor's Inspection and Test Plan (I & T Plan) and **must** be part of the [[0-QDS-7436_quality_record_definitions|Quality Assurance Documentation Package (FP)]].
*   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Chemetics requires both "Witness" and "Hold" surveillance for the Review of QA Documentation.
    *   **Witness:** A Chemetics representative **must** be present during the review of the complete QA documentation package.
    *   **Hold:** The Vendor **shall not** proceed with shipment until Chemetics has formally released the "Hold" point after satisfactory completion and approval of the QA documentation.

These comprehensive final inspections and documentation review processes ensure that the Stainless Steel Tank is fully compliant and ready for delivery.

**Review QA Documentation:**
*   **Requirement:** Review of QA Documentation is required as part of the Vendor's Inspection and Test Plan (I&T Plan) and is designated as "Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package" (FP).
*   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Chemetics requires "Witness" surveillance for Review QA Documentation.
*   **Chemetics Hold Point:** Chemetics requires a "Hold" surveillance point for Review QA Documentation.
