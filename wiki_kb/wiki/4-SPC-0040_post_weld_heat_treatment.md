# 4-SPC-0040 Post Weld Heat Treatment (PWHT)

Specification 4-SPC-0040 outlines the mandatory requirements for Post Weld Heat Treatment (PWHT) during the fabrication of metallic process vessels and components.

**PWHT Procedure and Approval:**
*   When PWHT is required, the Vendor **must** submit a detailed PWHT procedure to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for review and approval before PWHT is completed.
*   The PWHT procedure **must** satisfy the requirements of all applicable codes and standards.

**Heating Methods:**
*   The use of direct flame impingement heating, exothermic heat treatment, or localized heat treatment is **not permitted** without Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ prior written approval.

**Timing of Welding Operations:**
*   All welding, including clips, structural parts, and any other attachments, **must** be completed prior to the commencement of PWHT.

**Soaking Period:**
*   The soaking period for PWHT will be based on the heaviest welded section, which **must** include any reinforcing pads.

**Flange Protection:**
*   Flange faces **must** be suitably protected from oxidation during PWHT to prevent damage or degradation of the sealing surfaces.

**Temperature Monitoring and Records:**
*   Thermocouples **must** be attached to the equipment to accurately indicate the metal temperature during PWHT.
*   A sufficient number of thermocouples **must** be used and distributed strategically to ensure that the full range of metal temperatures is recorded throughout the PWHT cycle.
*   Comprehensive temperature records are **mandatory** for all PWHT operations.

**Post-PWHT Testing:**
*   Any required hydrostatic/pneumatic testing, air/soap testing, or magnetic particle inspection **must** be carried out *after* PWHT has been completed. This ensures that the tests reflect the final metallurgical state of the material.
