# Horizontal Centrifugal Pump Material Selection

Material selection for horizontal centrifugal pumps, as detailed in Document ID [[template_horizontal_centrifugal_pump_datasheet_administration]], is critical for ensuring compatibility with the process fluid, resistance to corrosion and erosion, and overall longevity of the equipment. Each component's material *shall be specified* to meet the demanding operational conditions.

**Key Components and Their Material Requirements:**

*   **Casing material:** *Shall be specified*. The casing forms the main pressure boundary of the pump.
*   **Impeller material:** *Shall be specified*. The impeller is responsible for imparting kinetic energy to the fluid.
*   **Wear Rings material:** *Shall be specified*. These provide a renewable sealing surface between the impeller and casing.
*   **Impeller O-Ring/Body Gasket material:** *Shall be specified*. These ensure static sealing within the pump.
*   **Shaft material:** *Shall be specified*. The shaft transmits power from the motor to the impeller.
*   **Shaft Sleeve material:** *Shall be specified*. This protects the shaft from wear and corrosion in the seal area.

**Shaft Seal System Materials:**
The materials for the shaft seal components are particularly important for preventing leakage and ensuring reliable operation. For more details on the seal system, refer to [[horizontal_centrifugal_pump_shaft_seal_system]].

*   **Seal Rotating Face - Inboard material:** *Shall be specified*.
*   **Seal Stationary Face - Inboard material:** *Shall be specified*.
*   **Seal Rotating Face - Outboard material:** *Shall be specified*.
*   **Seal Stationary Face - Outboard material:** *Shall be specified*.
*   **Seal Metal Parts material:** *Shall be specified*.
*   **Seal Elastomers material:** *Shall be specified*.

Careful consideration of these material specifications is essential for the safe and efficient operation of the pump, especially when handling corrosive or abrasive fluids as described in [[horizontal_centrifugal_pump_process_design_conditions]].
