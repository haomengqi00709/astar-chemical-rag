# FRP Reinforced PVC and CPVC Pipe Installation

FRP (Fiber Reinforced Polymer) reinforcement for PVC (Polyvinyl Chloride) and CPVC (Chlorinated Polyvinyl Chloride) pipe serves to enhance mechanical properties without altering the pressure or temperature ratings of the base pipe system.

**Purpose of Reinforcement:**
The primary purposes of FRP reinforcement for PVC and CPVC pipe are:
*   To increase allowable pipe support spacing.
*   To provide additional mechanical strength to resist breakage or damage from external loadings.
It is important to note that FRP reinforcement for PVC and CPVC pipe is **not** considered to increase the pressure rating or the temperature rating of the pipe system.

**Material Supply:**
CF1 is supplied as CP3 loose fittings, and VF6 is supplied as PV6 loose fittings. Both CF1 and VF6 are also supplied as bulk FRP wrap material. Materials for FRP wrapped PVC and CPVC pipe **will** be as specified in the [[pipe_specification|pipe specification]].

**Surface Preparation:**
The outer surface of the PVC or CPVC piping **will** be roughened with a grinder where required. All grinding dust **must** be removed with dry compressed air where required. The roughened surface **must** then be coated with a catalyzed bonding resin as per the manufacturer’s installation instructions.

**Fibreglass Wrapping Process:**
Wrapping **will** be done using rolls of fibreglass reinforcement. The fibreglass reinforcement **must** be wound spirally, with a slight overlap at the edges. Each layer of mat **must** be completely covered in resin and rolled to remove all entrained air pockets. Reinforcement **will** be applied to the piping to the thickness specified in the [[pipe_specification|pipe specification]]. Reinforcement **will** be applied in several sessions, with no more than six layers of mat applied per session to avoid an excessive exotherm. The surface finish of the reinforcement **must** be smooth, free of sags, drips, and wrinkles, and **must** contain no exposed glass fibres. The final two layers of mat **will** be applied with a fire retardant resin. When the final wrap has hardened completely, a final pigmented hot coat containing ultraviolet inhibitors **will** be applied.

**Wrapping of Straight Pipe:**
Wrapping of straight pipe **will** be done under clean, controlled conditions, preferably with the pipe on a mandrel. Piping can be "dry fit" in the field to the required length. If dry fit, pipe **will** then be wrapped up to 2 inches (50 mm) of the intended socket depth at joints. Alternatively, piping can be cut to the theoretical cut length plus 2 inches (50 mm) field trim each end, in which case wrapping **will** be extended to within 3 inches (80 mm) of the theoretical cut length each end. The unwrapped ends of each pipe **must** be protected during the wrapping process and kept clean and free from any resin, dirt, or other contaminants that might interfere with making good solvent-cemented joints.

**Joint Assembly and Post-Testing Overlay:**
Pre-wrapped pipe and unwrapped fittings **will** be installed. All joints **must** be solvent cemented using the manufacturer's recommended materials and procedures. FRP wrapping of the joints is to be done only after the [[piping_system_testing_and_flushing|pressure testing]] of the completed spool. Bolts of flanged joints **will** be tightened in accordance with the manufacturer's recommended sequence and bolt torques for plain PVC or CPVC flanges.

Following pressure testing and inspection of the piping system, a double layer of chopped strand mat and resin **will** be overlaid over all pipe fittings, over the hubs only of all flanges, and over the adjacent portions of pipe reinforcement to a length equal to the OD of the reinforced pipe (minimum one inch). Prior to application of this overlay, the affected surfaces of pre-applied pipe reinforcement **will** be ground to remove the surface finish. The fittings, bare pipe, and flange hubs **will** be treated with bonding resin. The reinforcement of fittings and flanges **will** be applied in a neat, workmanlike manner, presenting an outer surface of smooth contour, free from sags, drips, and wrinkles, and containing no exposed glass fibres. When the final wrap has hardened completely, a final pigmented hot coat containing ultraviolet inhibitors **will** be applied.

Refer to [[piping_manufacturer_data_references|MANUFACTURER’S DATA ATTACHED]] for PVDF Pipe, Polypropylene Pipe, and PVC Pipe.
