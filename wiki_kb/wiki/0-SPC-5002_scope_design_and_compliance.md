# Scope of Work, Design, and Compliance (0-SPC-5002)

This page details the *Vendor's* obligations regarding the scope of work, design responsibilities, and compliance with applicable standards and laws under Purchase Order **0-SPC-5002**.

**Goods and Services:**
*   The *Goods and Services* are the goods and services purchased from the *Vendor*.
*   To *Provide the Goods and Services* means to perform all work necessary to complete the *Goods and Services* in accordance with this Purchase Order, including all incidental work, materials, services, and actions required.
*   *Goods and Services Information* specifies and describes the *Goods and Services*, and states any constraints on how the *Vendor* *Provides the Goods and Services*. This information is found in documents referenced in the Purchase Order or in instructions given in accordance with the Purchase Order.
*   *Project Information* describes the *Project* and is contained in the documents referenced in the Purchase Order.

**Vendor's Obligations:**
*   The *Vendor* **must** *Provide the Goods and Services* in accordance with the *Goods and Services Information*.
*   The *Vendor* **must** *Provide the Goods and Services* in accordance with the [[applicable_law_and_compliance|Applicable Law]].
*   The *Vendor* **must** design the parts of the *Goods and Services* that the *Goods and Services Information* specifies as their design responsibility.

**Design Submission and Acceptance:**
*   If required by the *Goods and Services Information*, the *Vendor* **must** submit particulars of their design to the *Lead Engineer* for acceptance.
*   Reasons for the *Lead Engineer* not accepting the *Vendor’s* design include:
    *   It does not comply with the *Goods and Services Information*.
    *   It does not comply with the *Applicable Law*.
*   The *Vendor* **must not** proceed with the relevant work until the *Lead Engineer* has accepted their design.
*   The *Vendor* may submit their design for acceptance in parts if each part's design can be fully assessed.

**Health and Safety Compliance:**
*   The *Vendor* **must** act in accordance with the health and safety requirements of the [[applicable_law_and_compliance|Applicable Law]].
*   The *Vendor* **must** act in accordance with the health and safety requirements stated in the *Goods and Services Information*.
*   The *Vendor* **must** notify the *Project Buyer* as soon as they become aware that the *Goods and Services Information* requires them to do anything that is illegal or impossible.
*   If the *Project Buyer* agrees that a requirement is illegal or impossible, they will issue an instruction to change the *Goods and Services Information* appropriately.

**Intellectual Property:**
*   The *Vendor* indemnifies Aker Kvaerner Chemetics against claims, compensation, and costs arising from the *Vendor* infringing a patent or copyright.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may use and copy the *Vendor’s* design for any purpose connected with the construction, use, alteration, or demolition of the *Project*, unless otherwise stated in the *Goods and Services Information*. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may also use and copy the design for other purposes as stated in the *Goods and Services Information*. This constitutes an aspect of [[intellectual_property_and_design_use]].
