# Quality Record Definitions: CS Tubing Source Inspected

This page defines the abbreviations used for Quality Records within Quality Data Sheet (QDS) 0-QDS-7428, which specifies the quality requirements for carbon steel tubing. These definitions are essential for understanding the documentation requirements throughout the project lifecycle.

**Quality Record Abbreviations:**

*   **AP = "As Prepared"**
    *   This designation indicates that a document or record is required to be prepared by the vendor and submitted as part of their documentation. It signifies that the record is generated during the process and reflects the actual conditions or results.
*   **FP = "Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package"**
    *   This designation means that the specified document or record **must** be included as a part of the comprehensive Quality Assurance Documentation Package submitted by the vendor. This package typically consolidates all critical quality records for review and archiving.

These definitions are fundamental to the documentation and record-keeping processes outlined in the QDS (associated with statement 4.0). Proper categorization and submission of quality records are vital for demonstrating compliance with project specifications, regulatory requirements, and industry standards. They provide an auditable trail of all quality-related activities, from initial material certifications to final inspection reports.

These quality record requirements apply to various stages of the manufacturing and inspection process, including [[0-QDS-7428_material_verification_and_certifications|material verification]], [[0-QDS-7428_pressure_and_leak_testing|pressure and leak testing]], [[0-QDS-7428_non_destructive_examination_requirements|non-destructive examination]], [[0-QDS-7428_final_inspections|final inspections]], and [[0-QDS-7428_packing_and_shipping_requirements|packing and shipping]].
