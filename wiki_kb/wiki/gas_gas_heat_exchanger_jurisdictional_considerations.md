# Gas/Gas Heat Exchanger Jurisdictional Considerations

While gas/gas heat exchangers are not typically classified as pressure vessels under [[gas_gas_heat_exchanger_mechanical_calculation_prerequisites#ASME(1)|ASME]] rules, it is imperative to consider local jurisdictional requirements.

**ASME Classification:**
*   Gas/gas exchangers are generally not considered pressure vessels by ASME rules. This distinction impacts the specific application of ASME codes.

**Local Jurisdiction Impact:**
*   When equipment is considered a pressure vessel by local jurisdiction (e.g., in Australia or Europe), the design methods **must** be modified to reflect these particular jurisdictional requirements. This ensures compliance with regional safety standards and regulations, which may impose additional design constraints or require different certification processes.

Designers **must** be aware of and comply with all applicable local codes and regulations to ensure the legality and safety of the installed equipment.

For an overview of the entire mechanical calculation procedure, refer to [[4-prc-0009_gas_gas_exchanger_mechanical_calculations]].
