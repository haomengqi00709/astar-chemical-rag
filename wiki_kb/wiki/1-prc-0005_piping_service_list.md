# Piping Service List (1-PRC-0005)

The Piping Service List, identified as 1-PRC-0005, is an essential internal document that consolidates critical information for each piping service within a project. This document is strictly for internal Chemetics use and **shall not** be issued outside the company. A separate project-specific service list may be developed for future projects, if deemed necessary.

**Content Requirements**
The Piping Service List **shall** contain the following detailed information for each service:
*   **Fluid Code:** A unique identifier for the fluid being transported.
*   **Approximate Design Conditions:** Key operational parameters such as temperature and pressure.
*   **B31.3 Classification:** The classification of the piping system in accordance with [[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping]].
*   **Pressure Test Method:** The specified method for pressure testing the piping system.
*   **Flange Guard:** Indication of whether a flange guard is required.
*   **Pipe Material (Generic):** A general description of the pipe material.
*   **Corrosion Allowance:** The specified allowance for corrosion.
*   **Gasket Material Code:** A code identifying the required gasket material.
*   **Valve Selection:** Details regarding the selection of valves for the service.
*   **Instrument Connections:** Information on connections required for instrumentation.

**Roles and Responsibilities**
The preparation, checking, and approval of the Piping Service List are assigned to specific disciplines:
*   **Preparation:** The document **shall** be prepared by the Mechanical discipline.
*   **Checking:** The document **shall** be checked by the Technology discipline.
*   **Approval:** The document **shall** be approved by the Process discipline.

This rigorous process ensures that the Piping Service List is accurate and comprehensive, providing vital data for detailed piping design and material selection.
