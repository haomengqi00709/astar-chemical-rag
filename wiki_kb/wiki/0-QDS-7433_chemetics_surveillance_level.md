# Flocculant Mixer Chemetics Surveillance Level

Chemetics surveillance defines the level of oversight and inspection that Chemetics personnel or their designated representatives will exercise over the vendor's manufacturing and quality control activities for the Flocculant Mixer package. This surveillance ensures that the vendor's processes and products consistently meet the specified quality requirements.

For the Flocculant Mixer package, surveillance by Chemetics will be to **Level 0.0**.

This surveillance level indicates a specific degree of involvement, which is further detailed and governed by the requirements set out within this Quality Data Sheet (0-QDS-7433). The specific activities and hold points for Chemetics' witness or review are explicitly stated in the relevant sections of this document, such as during material verification, testing, and final inspections.

For a broader understanding of Chemetics' surveillance methodology and different levels, refer to [[0-QDS-7_aker_kvaerner_surveillance]].
