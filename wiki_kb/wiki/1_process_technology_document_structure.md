# Process Technology Document Structure (Major Work Subject 1)

The "1 Process Technology" major work subject encompasses all documentation related to the core process design, calculations, and operational aspects of a project. This folder is critical for storing information that defines the plant's functionality and performance.

The sub-folders and their contents within the 1-Process-Technology folder are:

*   **CAL:** Mass and Energy Balance Calculations. These calculations are fundamental to process design and optimization.
*   **HAZ:** HazOp Records. Documentation related to Hazard and Operability studies, identifying potential hazards and operational problems.
*   **LST:** Battery Limit List. This list defines the boundaries of the process unit. Data available in SQL: LST files.
*   **MAN:** Operating Manual, Analytical Manual. Comprehensive guides for plant operation and analytical procedures.
*   **PFD:** Process Flow Diagram. Graphical representations of the overall process, showing major equipment and flow paths. For more information, see [[pfd_overview]].
*   **PID:** Piping and Instrument Diagrams. Detailed diagrams showing piping, instrumentation, and control systems. Refer to [[piping_and_instrumentation_drawings]] for further details.
*   **PRC:** Project Specific Procedures, Piping Service. Procedures tailored to the project's process requirements and piping service specifications.

This structure ensures that all process-related documentation, from initial calculations to operational manuals, is systematically organized and readily accessible. For an overview of the entire project documentation structure, refer to [[document_control_1_prc_0003]]. The definitions of major work subjects are outlined in [[major_work_division_codes]].
