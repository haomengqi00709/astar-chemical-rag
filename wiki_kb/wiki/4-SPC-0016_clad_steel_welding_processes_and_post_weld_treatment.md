# Clad Steel Welding Processes and Post-Weld Treatment

The selection of appropriate welding processes and careful control of parameters, including post-weld treatments, are critical for achieving high-quality austenitic stainless clad steel fabrications.

**Approved Welding Processes**
Preferred methods of welding stainless clad steels include:
*   Shielded Metal Arc Welding (SMAW)
*   Gas Tungsten Arc Welding (GTAW)
*   Gas Metal Arc Welding (GMAW)
*   Submerged Arc Welding (SAW)

Other welding processes **will** be evaluated upon submission of full details, including the procedure and exact location of proposed weldments. SMAW is the recommended weld process for the process side of welds. For recommended welding electrodes, refer to Appendix A, detailed in [[4-SPC-0016_table_1_typical_stainless_weld_consumables]].

**Preheat and Interpass Temperature Control**
Preheat is generally **not required**. However, when adverse weather conditions cause condensation or wetness, the material **must** be dried prior to welding by heating to a temperature just sufficient to prevent condensation.

Heat input during welding **should** be kept to a minimum to control metallurgical changes.
*   Interpass temperature **should not** exceed 300°F (149°C) on 300 series stainless steel.
*   Interpass temperature **should not** exceed 200°F (93°C) on Alloy 20-Cb3.

**Postweld Heat Treatment (PWHT)**
Postweld Heat Treatment (PWHT) is **not generally a requirement**. If PWHT is specified, the Fabricator **must** submit the procedure in writing for approval prior to the commencement of work. The PWHT procedure **must** include information on the number and location of thermocouples and supports.

**Weld Positioning and Sequence**
As far as practical, all welds **must** be made in the downhand (flat) position. Vertical welds **must** be made vertical up (uphand). The external side of each weld seam **must** be completed before starting the internal (process) side.

**Stress Relief**
Welds **must** be stress relieved by shot peening using glass beads only if this requirement is explicitly shown on approved drawings, requested in the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Purchase Order, or specified in associated documentation.

**Welding Positions and Sequence**
As far as practical, all welds **must** be made in the downhand (flat) position. Vertical welds **must** be made vertical up (uphand).
Substantially all carbon steel welding **will** be completed in each joint before stainless welding on the joint is started. The external side of each weld seam **must** be completed before starting the internal (Process) side.

**Special Post-Weld Treatments**
Welds **must** be stress relieved by shot peening using glass beads if shown on approved drawings, requested in the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics P.O., or requested in associated documentation. The requirement for a final pass of 904L SS weld filler is also subject to these conditions.
