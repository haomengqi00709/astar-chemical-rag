# Job Hazard Analysis (JHA) Documentation and Records

Accurate and consistent documentation is crucial for the effective management and review of [[0-PRC-8007_job_hazard_analysis]] (JHA). All JHA activities **must** be formally recorded using designated forms.

**JHA Record Keeping:**
*   A record of the JHA **is to be kept** using form [[0-REP-8007a_job_hazard_analysis_worksheet]].
*   For confined space entry, the JHA record **is to be kept** using form [[0-REP-8007b_confined_space_hazard_assessment_form]].

**Associated Forms and References:**
The following forms and reference documents are integral to the JHA process:

*   **[[0-REP-8007a_job_hazard_analysis_worksheet]]**: This is the primary worksheet used for recording general job hazard analyses.
*   **[[0-REP-8007b_confined_space_hazard_assessment_form]]**: This specialized form is used for hazard assessments specifically related to confined space entry.
*   **[[0-REP-8007c_job_hazard_analysis_reference_card]]**: A reference card providing quick access to JHA principles and steps.
*   **[[0-PRC-8006_task_safety_awareness_procedure]]**: This procedure outlines the requirements for task safety awareness, which utilizes the JHA as a briefing guide.
*   **[[0-REP-8006a_task_safety_awareness_form]]**: The form used for documenting task safety awareness briefings.

Each project **will** maintain a comprehensive record of all JHA reports, ensuring they are readily accessible for review, updates, and compliance purposes.
