# Eductor Quantity and Scope

The Template Ejector Datasheet (Document ID: [[4-DST-XXXX-R0_template_eductor_datasheet_administration]]) provides fields to define the quantity of ejectors required for a project, along with details regarding operational and spare units.

**Quantity Fields Present:**
- **Quantity:** The total number of ejector units.
- **Operating quantity:** The number of ejector units intended for continuous operation.
- **Installed Spare quantity:** The number of spare ejector units that are installed and ready for use, typically for redundancy or quick replacement.

These fields are essential for procurement, installation planning, and ensuring adequate operational capacity and reliability for the project. The quantity specified here directly impacts the overall [[procurement_plan]] and [[project_budgeting_0_dat_4001]].
