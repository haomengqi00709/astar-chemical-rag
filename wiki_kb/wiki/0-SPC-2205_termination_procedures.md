# Engineering Subcontract Termination Procedures (0-SPC-2205)

This page details the conditions and procedures for termination of engineering subcontracts under document 0-SPC-2205, including grounds for termination, post-termination obligations, and final payment calculations. For a general overview of engineering subcontract termination, refer to [[0-SPC-2202-1_engineering_subcontract_termination_and_liability]].

**Grounds for Termination:**

*   **Subcontractor's Right to Terminate:** The Subcontractor **may** terminate by notifying Aker Kvaerner Chemetics if Aker Kvaerner Chemetics has not paid an amount due to the Subcontractor within eleven (11) weeks of the Subcontractor issuing a notice that payment is overdue.
*   **Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' Right to Terminate (for convenience):** Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **may** terminate by notifying the Subcontractor upon one (1) week's notice for any reason.
*   **Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' Right to Terminate (for default):** Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **may** terminate by notifying the Subcontractor immediately if the Subcontractor has substantially failed to comply with their obligations and has not rectified the default within two (2) weeks of a notification from Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Post-Termination Obligations:**
Upon termination:
*   The Subcontractor **must** cease all further work necessary to Provide the Services.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **may** complete the services themselves or engage other parties to do so.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **may** use or make available to other parties their rights over material prepared by the Subcontractor or a [[0-SPC-2205_general_conditions_and_administration#Key Definitions|Subsubcontractor]].
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **may** require the Subcontractor to assign the benefit of any subconsultancy or other contract related to the performance of this Subcontract to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
*   Both Parties **must** continue to comply with the restrictions and obligations in this Subcontract regarding the use of material prepared or obtained by the Subcontractor.
*   Both Parties **must** continue to comply with the restrictions and obligations in this Subcontract regarding publicizing the services.
*   The Subcontractor **must** provide Aker Kvaerner Chemetics with all information resulting from work carried out to date and any information the Subcontractor has obtained which they are responsible to provide under this Subcontract.

**Final Payment on Termination:**
*   A final payment **must** be made as soon as possible after termination.
*   The amount due on termination includes:
    *   An amount due assessed as for normal payments.
    *   Other direct costs reasonably incurred by the Subcontractor as a direct consequence of the termination.
    *   Any amounts retained by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
*   If Aker Kvaerner Chemetics terminates due to the insolvency of the Subcontractor or a substantial failure of the Subcontractor to comply with their obligations:
    *   The amount due on termination **must** include a deduction for the forecast additional cost to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics of completing the services.
    *   The amount due on termination **does not** include any amount for costs incurred by the Subcontractor in expectation of completing the entire services.

**Dispute Resolution Post-Termination:**
Any dispute arising after termination **must** be settled using the same dispute resolution process outlined in [[0-SPC-2205_dispute_resolution]].
