# Equipment Document Structure (Major Work Subject 4)

The "4 Equipment" major work subject is dedicated to all documentation concerning the design, specification, layout, and procurement of major plant equipment. This folder is crucial for managing information related to the physical components that constitute the process facility.

The sub-folders and their contents within the 4-Equipment folder are:

*   **CAL:** Equipment Sizing / Design Calculations. Calculations performed for the sizing and design of various equipment.
*   **DST:** Equipment Data Sheets. Detailed technical data sheets for individual pieces of equipment. For more information, refer to [[process_and_electrical_data_sheets]].
*   **DTL:** Equipment Details. Detailed drawings and specifications for specific equipment components or features.
*   **GAD:** Plot Plan, Equipment Layouts, Elevations, Perspectives. Drawings showing the overall arrangement of equipment on the site, including various views.
*   **LST:** Equipment List, Spare Parts Lists. Comprehensive lists of all equipment and their associated spare parts. Data available in SQL: LST files.
*   **OTL:** Equipment Outline Drawings. Drawings showing the external dimensions and connection points of equipment.
*   **PDS:** Process Data Sheets. Data sheets containing process-specific information for equipment.
*   **PKG:** Package Data Sheets. Data sheets for complete equipment packages supplied by vendors.
*   **PRC:** Project Specific Procedures. Procedures tailored to equipment design, procurement, and installation.
*   **PRP:** Proprietary Detail Drawings. Drawings containing proprietary design details for specific equipment. Refer to [[equipment_proprietary_detail_drawing_prp]].
*   **REQ:** Requisitions. Documents for requesting bids or proposals for equipment.
*   **SCR:** Equipment Plinth and Loading Drawings. Drawings detailing foundations (plinths) and loading requirements for equipment.
*   **SPC:** Specifications. Technical specifications for equipment design, materials, fabrication, and testing.
*   **TAB:** Bid Tabulations. Records of bids received for equipment.
*   **VDR:** Vendor Document Requirements. Documents outlining the requirements for vendor-supplied equipment documentation.
*   **VEN:** Vendors Documents. All documentation provided by equipment vendors, such as manuals, drawings, and certifications.

This structure ensures that all equipment-related documents, from initial design calculations to vendor submissions, are systematically organized and easily retrievable. For an overview of the entire project documentation structure, refer to [[document_control_1_prc_0003]]. The definitions of major work subjects are outlined in [[major_work_division_codes]].
