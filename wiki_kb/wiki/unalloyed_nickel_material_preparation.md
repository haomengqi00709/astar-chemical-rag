# Unalloyed Nickel Material Preparation for Welding

Proper material preparation is crucial for ensuring the integrity of welds in unalloyed nickel components, as defined by [[4-SPC-0068_unalloyed_nickel_welding_standard_overview]]. This specification mandates a two-stage cleaning process for the surface and edges of any material intended for heat application, including fusion cutting, forming, or welding.

**Stage 1 Cleaning: Mechanical or Chemical Preparation**
All surfaces and edges that will be subjected to heat **must** be lightly ground to achieve a clean, white metal finish. This mechanical grinding removes oxides, contaminants, and surface imperfections that could compromise weld quality.

In instances where grinding is not practical, such as within a pipe bore, the surface **must** be chemically cleaned. This chemical cleaning **must** be performed using an approved procedure to ensure effective contaminant removal without detrimental effects on the base material.

**Stage 2 Cleaning: Organic Solvent Application**
Immediately prior to any heat application, all ground surfaces **must** be cleaned with a suitable organic solvent. This step ensures the removal of any residual oils, grease, or other organic contaminants that may have accumulated after the initial cleaning stage, preventing their introduction into the weld pool.
