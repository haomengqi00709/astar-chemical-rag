# Insulation Design Criteria (6-PRC-0001)

The **Insulation Design Criteria**, identified as **6-PRC-0001**, serves as a foundational document for all insulation selection and design within the plant. This document is prepared by the Mechanical discipline, checked by the Project Engineer, and ultimately approved by the Chief Mechanical Engineer.

The Insulation Design Criteria **shall** contain the following information, as applicable to the project:
*   Statutory requirements.
*   Owner requirements.
*   Reference to the appropriate [[insulation_specification_6_spc_0001]].
*   Any additional general requirements pertinent to insulation selection for the plant.

In instances where specific statutory or Owner requirements are not present, the Design Criteria **shall** reference the standard [[insulation_specification_6_spc_0001]]. This ensures that a consistent and approved standard is always applied.

This document is a critical component of the overall [[6-prc-0002_insulation_and_heat_tracing_documentation_procedure]], ensuring that all insulation designs meet regulatory, client, and internal technical standards. Its clear definition of requirements guides subsequent detailed design and procurement activities. The rigorous preparation and approval process for the Insulation Design Criteria ensures that all insulation solutions are robust, compliant, and optimized for the specific operational environment of the plant.

This document is crucial as it will contain all applicable statutory and Owner requirements related to insulation. It will also reference the appropriate specifications and include any additional general requirements for insulation selection specific to the plant.

In the absence of specific statutory or Owner requirements, the Insulation Design Criteria will reference the standard Insulation Specification.
