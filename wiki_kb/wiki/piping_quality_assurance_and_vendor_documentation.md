# Piping Quality Assurance and Vendor Documentation

Ensuring quality in piping projects involves rigorous quality assurance (QA) processes for both material supply and installation, as well as meticulous review of vendor documentation. These processes are critical for meeting technical requirements and project standards.

**Quality Assurance for Material Supply**
Vendor Quality Assurance (QA) for material supply is governed by [[0-PRC-7005_vendor_quality_assurance]]. This procedure outlines the requirements for vendors to ensure that all materials supplied for piping systems meet specified quality standards. The [[0-QDS-7XXX_template_quality_data_sheet]] was added in Revision 1 of [[5-PRC-0003_piping_documentation_procedure_overview]] to standardize quality data requirements.

**Quality Assurance for Installation**
Construction Subcontract Quality Assurance for installation activities is covered by [[0-PRC-6071_construction_subcontract_quality_assurance_procedure]]. This ensures that the physical installation of piping systems adheres to design specifications and quality benchmarks.

**Review of Vendor Documentation**
The process for reviewing documents received from vendors is detailed in [[0-PRC-0007_review_of_vendor_documentation]]. All documents received from vendors **shall** be reviewed to ensure they meet the intent of the technical requirements issued by Chemetics. The [[0-VDR-0_vendor_document_requirements_administration]] was added in Revision 2 of [[5-PRC-0003_piping_documentation_procedure_overview]] to further standardize vendor document requirements.

**Procurement Documentation**
Documents issued for the procurement of equipment **shall** be attached to Section 1: Scope of Supply of the Purchase Order. The general framework for purchase orders is outlined in [[0-PRC-5001_purchase_order_overview]].

This integrated approach to quality assurance and vendor documentation management is vital for the successful execution of piping projects, ensuring compliance, reliability, and safety.
