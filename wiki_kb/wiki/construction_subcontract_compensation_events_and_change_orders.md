# Construction Subcontract Compensation Events and Change Orders

This section, derived from 0-SPC-6003, outlines the procedures for managing compensation events and the issuance of Change Orders within a construction subcontract.

**Compensation Event Notification and Quotation**
The Subcontractor **shall** promptly notify Aker Kvaerner Chemetics of any compensation events. This notification **shall** be in accordance with Section 27 of [[standard_conditions_for_construction_subcontract_0_spc_6001]].
Following notification, the Subcontractor **shall** submit a quotation for each compensation event. This quotation **shall** be in accordance with Section 28 of [[standard_conditions_for_construction_subcontract_0_spc_6001]].

For further details on compensation events, refer to [[construction_subcontract_compensation_events]] and [[compensation_events_and_assessment_0_spc_6001]].

**Change Order Issuance**
A Change Order **will** be issued by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics to acknowledge or advise on compensation events. A Change Order can serve several purposes:
*   Instruct the Subcontractor to proceed with a previously proposed instruction.
*   Acknowledge acceptance of the Subcontractor’s quotation for a compensation event.
*   Advise the price and extension of time assessed by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for a compensation event.

**Change Order Validity**
A Change Order is only valid when signed by the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics signatory to the original Construction Subcontract Agreement.

For general documentation requirements related to change orders, see [[change_order_documentation_requirements_0_chg_22]].

**Compensation Event Notification and Quotation**
The Subcontractor **shall** promptly notify Aker Kvaerner Chemetics of any compensation events, in accordance with Section 27 of 0-SPC-6001: Standard Conditions for Construction Subcontract. Following notification, the Subcontractor shall submit a quotation for each compensation event, adhering to the requirements of Section 28 of 0-SPC-6001: Standard Conditions for Construction Subcontract.

**Change Orders**
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics will issue a Change Order to formally acknowledge or advise on matters related to compensation events. A Change Order may:
*   Instruct the Subcontractor to proceed with a previously proposed instruction.
*   Acknowledge acceptance of the Subcontractor’s quotation for a compensation event.
*   Advise the price and extension of time assessed by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for a compensation event.

A Change Order is only considered valid when it has been signed by the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics signatory to the original Construction Subcontract Agreement.
