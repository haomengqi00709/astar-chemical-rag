# Glass-Lined Vessel Packaging, Shipping, and Site Protection (4-SPC-0044)

This section outlines the mandatory requirements for packaging, shipping, and on-site protection of glass-lined carbon steel reaction vessels, ensuring their integrity during transit and storage, as specified in 4-SPC-0044.

**General Protection Requirements**
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' Packaging and Shipping Standards ES44\S-01 and ES44\S-02 are applicable to all glass-lined equipment. The Supplier **shall** ensure that the completed items are adequately protected against damage in transit and are suitable for possible long periods of storage in the open.

**Surface Preparation and Coatings**
Unlined surfaces **shall** have all loose scale and rust removed, and a protective primer coating **shall** be applied. All machined surfaces **shall** be coated with temporary metal protective, after necessary cleaning. Set screws and washers or plastic protective caps **shall** be fitted in tapped holes for corrosion protection. Installed vessels **shall** have a suitable external top or finish coating to prevent the ingress of corrodents, particularly acids, to the metal surface. The type of external coating **shall** be agreed between the Vendor and Purchaser.

**Specific Packaging and Shipping Details**
Jackets **shall** be completely emptied to prevent damage due to freezing in cold weather. Vessels with open tops **shall** be adequately protected in store by fitting a robust wooden cover. All joint faces and parts such as agitators, stuffing boxes, mechanical seals, and thermowells **shall** be provided with adequate protection to prevent damage in transit. Loose backing flanges **shall** either be secured to their respective nozzles (van stone type) or shipped separately. All nozzle flanges **shall** be protected by caps of rubber, plastic, or gasketted plywood.

A spare set of gaskets for all flanges **shall** be supplied with the vessel.
