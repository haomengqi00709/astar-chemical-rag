# Shipping Marks and Labeling Standards

Proper identification and labeling of packages are mandatory for marine transportation.

**Identification Marks:**
*   Packages **must** be clearly identified by painting or dye stencilling on the top and two sides of the exterior.
*   Lettering for shipping marks **must** be black.
*   Lettering for shipping marks **must** be at least 8 centimetres high.
*   When palletised packaging is used, marks **must** be applied to a securely mounted plywood placard.

**Example Shipping Mark Format:**
The example shipping mark format is `96W11100-4006-1/6`. For Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Purchase Order Number 96W11100-4006, delivered in a total of six packages, each package **must** be identified sequentially as: 96W11100-4006-1/6, 96W11100-4006-2/6, and so on.

**Handling and Storage Instructions:**
Handling, lifting, and storage instructions or restrictions, supplemented by standard international pictorial symbols, **must** be clearly stencilled on both sides of each crate.

**Centre of Gravity Marking:**
All packages exceeding 2,000 kg **must** have the centre of gravity marked on the projected location on three sides. The letters "C.G." and the shipping weight **must** be adjacent to these centre of gravity marks.

These marking requirements are essential for efficient logistics, safe handling, and compliance with [[packaging_marine_transportation_0_spc_5005]] standards.
