# 7-SPC-0001 Surfaces Not to Be Painted

The `7-SPC-0001 Protective Coating Specification` explicitly lists surfaces that are not to be painted to ensure proper equipment function and maintain material integrity.

**Exclusions from Painting**
Surfaces not painted include:
*   Nameplates
*   Shaft seals
*   Stainless steels and other high alloys (unless specifically stated otherwise)
*   Rubber, plastic, glass
*   Valve stems
*   Movable linkage on equipment
*   Grease fittings
*   Compressor or pump shafts
*   Pressure gauges, instrument glasses, and other similar items
*   Machined surfaces (e.g., flange gasket faces)
*   Any surfaces on equipment normally lubricated or with close working tolerances
*   Structural steel which has been galvanized or is to be fireproofed
