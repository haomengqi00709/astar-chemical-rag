# Engineering Subcontract Compensation Event Procedure

The [[standard_conditions_engineering_subcontract]] (0-SPC-2201) outlines potentially lengthy steps for review and quotation in response to instructions that may constitute a compensation event. To ensure no undue delays in the delivery of services, the procedures described in Sections 5 ("Issue of Documents") and 6 ("Issue of Communications") of 0-PRC-2207 **shall** be followed.

Immediately following the issue of documents by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics to the Subcontractor, the Subcontract Administrator **shall** issue a communication. An example of such a communication is: "Our communication KCI-SUB-[024] contains Document Transmittal [KC-SC-0047]." The communication **must** explicitly state: "The documents referenced by the [[document_transmittal_document_type]] contain revisions that may be considered by Subcontractor to be instructions given by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics changing the [[engineering_subcontract_scope_preparation_0_prc_2203]]."

The Subcontractor **must** immediately proceed with the services based on the instructions contained in the revised documents. Concurrently, the Subcontractor **must** keep a separate record of the direct costs applicable to any change in Scope. The Subcontractor **shall** submit a notice and quotation, including a revised schedule, for any compensation event resulting from the instructions contained in the revised documents. This submission **must** be in accordance with clause 27 of [[standard_conditions_engineering_subcontract]] (0-SPC-2201).

Any communication issued by the [[discipline_lead_and_approver_roles]] or by the Subcontract Administrator to the Subcontractor containing an instruction **shall** have the following specific text appended:
"The foregoing is an instruction to Subcontractor given by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics which may change the Scope. Subcontractor will immediately proceed with the services based on the instruction, keeping a separate record of the direct costs applicable to any change in Scope. Subcontractor will submit a notice and quotation, including a revised schedule, for any compensation event resulting from the instruction in accordance with clause 27 of 0-SPC-2201: Standard Conditions for Engineering Subcontract."

The Subcontract Administrator **shall** respond to any notice from the Subcontractor regarding the occurrence of a compensation event within one week. The response **must** clearly indicate either acceptance or rejection of the claim that the instruction constitutes a compensation event. If the decision is made to accept the claim, the Subcontract Administrator **shall** then request a formal quotation from the Subcontractor. Clause 27.3 of [[standard_conditions_engineering_subcontract]] (0-SPC-2201) describes the possible actions by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics following receipt of a quotation for a compensation event.

To ensure no undue delays in the delivery of services, the procedures described in Sections 5 ("Issue of Documents") and 6 ("Issue of Communications") will be followed.

Immediately following the issue of documents by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics to the Subcontractor, the Subcontract Administrator will issue a communication. An example communication is: “Our communication KCI-SUB-[024] contains Document Transmittal [KC-SC-0047].” This communication states: “The documents referenced by the Document Transmittal contain revisions that may be considered by Subcontractor to be instructions given by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics changing the Scope.”

Upon receiving such instructions, the Subcontractor will immediately proceed with the services based on the instructions contained in the revised documents. The Subcontractor must keep a separate record of the direct costs applicable to any change in Scope. Subsequently, the Subcontractor will submit a notice and quotation, including a revised schedule, for any compensation event resulting from the instructions contained in the revised documents. This submission must be in accordance with clause 27 of 0-SPC-2201: Standard Conditions for Engineering Subcontract.

Any communication issued by the Discipline Lead or by the Subcontract Administrator to the Subcontractor containing an instruction will have specific text appended. The appended text is: “The foregoing is an instruction to Subcontractor given by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics which may change the Scope. Subcontractor will immediately proceed with the services based on the instruction, keeping a separate record of the direct costs applicable to any change in Scope. Subcontractor will submit a notice and quotation, including a revised schedule, for any compensation event resulting from the instruction in accordance with clause 27 of 0-SPC-2201: Standard Conditions for Engineering Subcontract.”

The Subcontract Administrator will respond to any notice from the Subcontractor of the occurrence of a compensation event within one week. The response will be either acceptance or rejection of the claim that the instruction is a compensation event. If the decision is made to accept the claim, the Subcontract Administrator will request a quotation. Clause 27.3 of 0-SPC-2201: Standard Conditions for Engineering Subcontract describes the possible actions by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics following receipt of a quotation for a compensation event.
