# Strainer Non-Destructive Examination (NDE) Requirements (0-QDS-7402)

Non-Destructive Examination (NDE) is a critical component of the quality assurance process for the Strainer (0-QDS-7402), ensuring the integrity of materials and welds without causing damage. All NDE activities **shall** be conducted in strict accordance with recognized industry codes and standards.

**General NDE Standards:**
*   Non-Destructive Testing **shall** be performed in accordance with ASME VIII Div. 1.
*   Non-Destructive Examination **shall** be performed in accordance with ASME Boiler & Pressure Vessel Code Sections V & VIII. These sections provide detailed methodologies and acceptance criteria for various NDE techniques.
*   Non-Destructive Testing is recognized as a key item or activity within the overall Quality table, highlighting its importance in the quality control framework.

For general NDE requirements, refer to [[0-QDS-7_non_destructive_examination_requirements]] and [[non_destructive_testing]].

**Specific NDE Techniques:**

**Radiographic Testing:**
*   **Extent:** Radiographic testing is specified as "Spot," meaning it is applied to selected areas rather than the entire component.
*   **Quality Record:** Radiographic testing is required as FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package).
*   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Chemetics surveillance for Radiographic testing is "Complete," indicating full oversight of the process and results.

**Dye Penetrant Testing:**
*   **Extent:** Dye Penetrant testing is specified as 100%, meaning it is applied to all relevant surfaces.
*   **Quality Record:** Dye Penetrant testing is required as FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package).
*   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Chemetics surveillance for Dye Penetrant testing is "Complete," ensuring thorough verification.
*   **Application:**
    *   Dye Penetrant testing is specifically required for the **Root pass** of welds.
    *   Dye Penetrant testing is also required for the **Cap** when the weld is made by the Shielded Metal Arc Welding (SMAW) process. This ensures the integrity of critical weld areas.

These detailed NDE requirements are designed to identify any potential defects and ensure the structural integrity and safety of the Strainer.

All Non-Destructive Testing (NDT) shall specifically adhere to **ASME VIII Div. 1**. Furthermore, Non-Destructive Examination (NDE) shall be in accordance with **ASME Boiler & Pressure Vessel Code Sections V & VIII**. NDE is recognized as an item or activity within the overall Quality table.

Specific NDE requirements and Chemetics surveillance details are as follows:
*   **Radiographic testing**: Specified as "Spot" and required as 'FP'. Chemetics surveillance for this activity is "Complete".
*   **Dye Penetrant testing**: Required at "100%" coverage and as 'FP'. Chemetics surveillance for this activity is "Complete". This testing is specifically required for the **Root pass** and for the **Cap** when the weld is made by the SMAW (Shielded Metal Arc Welding) process.
