# Sulphuric Acid Storage Tank Documentation and Records (4-SPC-0046)

This section outlines the documentation and quality record submission requirements for sulphuric acid storage tanks under specification 4-SPC-0046.

**Pre-Construction Document Submission**
Prior to the commencement of work, the manufacturer **must** submit the following documents to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for review:
*   Copies plus sepias of manufacturer's shop and erection drawings (as required by purchase orders).
*   Two copies of the welding procedures.
*   Two copies of the design calculations.

One reviewed copy of each (drawings, procedures, and calculations) **will** be returned to the manufacturer. It is crucial to understand that review by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics does not relieve the manufacturer of responsibility for proper and efficient design or for checking drawings.

**Final Certified Drawings**
Upon completion of the work, the manufacturer **must** supply copies plus sepias of final certified drawings in a form suitable for micro-filming.

**Quality Records Submission**
On completion of work, the manufacturer **must** supply Aker Kvaerner Chemetics with two sets (in booklet form) of comprehensive quality records. These required quality records include:
*   Mill Test Reports (as detailed in Paragraph 7.2 of the specification).
*   Non-Destructive Examination (NDE) reports, including precise locations of all radiographs.
*   Procedure Qualification Record (P.Q.R.) form (QW484) for all welders utilized in the project.
*   Certificate of tests carried out per [[API_650_welded_steel_tanks|API 650, Section 5.3]].

Additionally, a permanent record of radiograph locations **must** be provided to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
