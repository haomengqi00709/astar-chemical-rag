# Conveyor Belt Splicing

This page outlines the mandatory requirements for correct [[conveyor_belt_splicing|belt splicing]], alignment, and tightly embedded fasteners for [[conveying_equipment_mechanical_checkout|conveying equipment]]. The splice **must** be correct, the belt aligned, and fasteners embedded tightly.
