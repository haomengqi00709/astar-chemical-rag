# Inspection and Documentation Requirements for Carbon Steel Tank (0-QDS-7435)

This page outlines the mandatory inspection and documentation requirements for the Carbon Steel Tank project, as specified in 0-QDS-7435. Chemetics' involvement in witnessing and holding various tests and inspections is detailed below, in line with the [[0-QDS-7435_vendor_quality_assurance_requirements|Chemetics Surveillance Level 2.0]].

**Material Verification (Item 1.0):**
*   Chemetics **must** witness Mill Test Certificates. These certificates are required as FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package) as per [[0-QDS-7435_quality_record_definitions]]. For more details, see [[0-QDS-7435_material_verification_and_certifications]].

**Pressure/Leak Testing (Item 8.0):**
*   Chemetics **must** witness Hydrotest.
*   Chemetics **must** hold Hydrotest.
*   The Hydrotest record is required as FP.
*   Chemetics **must** witness Air/Soap Test.
*   Chemetics **must** hold Air/Soap Test.
*   The Air/Soap Test record is required as FP.
For more details, see [[0-QDS-7435_pressure_and_leak_testing]].

**Non-Destructive Examination (Item 14.0):**
*   Chemetics **must** witness Radiography.
*   Chemetics **must** hold Radiography.
*   The Radiography record is required as FP.
*   Chemetics **must** witness Dye Penetrant.
*   Chemetics **must** hold Dye Penetrant.
*   The Dye Penetrant record is required as FP.
For more details, see [[0-QDS-7435_non_destructive_examination_requirements]].

**Coating/Lining Inspection (Item 19.0):**
*   Chemetics **must** witness External Coating/Lining Inspection.
*   Chemetics **must** hold External Coating/Lining Inspection.
*   The External Coating/Lining Inspection record is required as FP. This inspection includes Primer / Top Coat.
For more details, see [[0-QDS-7435_coating_and_lining_inspection]].

**Final Inspections (Item 24.0):**
*   Chemetics **must** witness Dimensional Final Inspection.
*   Chemetics **must** hold Dimensional Final Inspection.
*   Chemetics **must** witness Contour & Finish of Welds Final Inspection.
*   Chemetics **must** hold Contour & Finish of Welds Final Inspection.
*   Chemetics **must** witness Surfaces Free from Defects Final Inspection.
*   Chemetics **must** hold Surfaces Free from Defects Final Inspection.
*   Chemetics **must** witness Cleanliness Final Inspection.
*   Chemetics **must** hold Cleanliness Final Inspection.
For more details, see [[0-QDS-7435_final_inspections]].

**Quality Assurance Documentation Review:**
*   Chemetics **must** witness Review QA Documentation.
*   Chemetics **must** hold Review QA Documentation.
*   The Review QA Documentation record is required as FP.

**Packing/Crating (Item 32.0):**
*   Chemetics **must** witness Packaging.
*   Chemetics **must** witness Packing List.
*   The Packing List record is required as AP (As Prepared).
For more details, see [[0-QDS-7435_packing_and_shipping_requirements]].
