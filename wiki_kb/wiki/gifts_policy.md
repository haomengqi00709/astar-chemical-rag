# Gifts Policy

This policy establishes guidelines for the purchase and acceptance of gifts in a business context, ensuring ethical conduct and transparency.

**Purchasing Gifts for Clients**
*   The purchase of gifts for clients is restricted to Sales and Project Managers.
*   Any gifts with a value of over $200 **must** have the prior approval of the appropriate Senior Manager.
*   All gifts **must** be reported on an expense report.
*   Expense reports for gifts **must** indicate the recipient, position, and organization.
*   Expense reports for gifts **must** indicate the prior approval obtained where required.
*   For general expense reporting guidelines, refer to [[0-PRC-9003_expense_report_submission_and_documentation]].

**Acceptance of Gifts and Entertainment**
Employees and members of their immediate families **may not** accept gifts, cash payments, free services, privileges, entertainment, or favors of more than a nominal value from customers, suppliers, or other business contacts.
*   Entertainment and/or gifts **should** be reasonable.
*   Entertainment and/or gifts **should never** lead to a sense of obligation upon the employee.
*   Entertainment and/or gifts **should not** be of such a type that they could be construed by an impartial observer as a bribe, payoff, or improper incentive.
*   Employees **should not** accept any entertainment that could not be justified on an Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ expense report were they offering it rather than receiving it.
*   All gifts and entertainment **must** be disclosed to the appropriate Senior Manager or Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ Legal Counsel.
