# Vendor Design Control Procedures

As part of the overall quality assurance framework outlined in Specification [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment|0-SPC-7001]], the Vendor **shall** maintain robust procedures for the design of goods. These procedures are crucial to ensure that all design activities meet the specified requirements and standards of the Purchase Order.

**Design Procedures**
The Vendor **must** have documented procedures that govern the entire design process for the goods. These procedures **shall** ensure that the design is systematically developed and controlled.

**Design Review**
In addition to design procedures, the Vendor **must** have documented procedures for the review of the design. This review process **shall** verify the completeness, adequacy, and conformance of the design with all requirements stipulated in the Purchase Order. Design reviews are a critical step to identify and address potential issues early in the development cycle.

**Design Change Control**
Procedures for controlling design changes are equally important. These design change control procedures **must** be consistent with the controls applied to the original design. This ensures that any modifications to the design undergo the same rigorous review and approval process as the initial design, maintaining the integrity and quality of the goods throughout their lifecycle.
