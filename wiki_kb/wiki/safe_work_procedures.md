# Safe Work Procedures

Safe Work Procedures (SWPs) are detailed, step-by-step instructions that guide workers on how to perform tasks safely, minimizing risks to health and safety. As referenced in the [[hse_incident_reporting_roles_and_responsibilities]], management personnel **must** review relevant Safe Work Procedures as part of any incident investigation. This review helps to determine if the procedures were followed, if they were adequate for the task, or if they require revision based on lessons learned from the incident.
