# Project Quality Audits

To ensure ongoing compliance and adherence to quality standards, both internal and external audits are conducted throughout the project lifecycle.

**Internal Audits**
The Manager is responsible for conducting periodic audits of project documentation to verify compliance with established procedures. These audits specifically check:
*   The percentage compliance of checking and approval procedures for all published documents.
*   The status of corrective actions undertaken to ensure compliance with this procedure (0-PRC-7003).
*   The status of preparation for all procedures referenced or required by this procedure.
*   The status of training received by personnel assigned to the Project.

These internal audits are crucial for identifying areas for improvement and ensuring that the [[0-prc-7003_project_quality_management_plan_overview]] is effectively implemented.

**Owner Audits**
The Owner reserves the right to audit the work of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for compliance at any time. Such Owner audits may be conducted either by the Owner's own staff or by a subcontracted inspection agency, providing an independent verification of project quality and adherence to contractual obligations.
