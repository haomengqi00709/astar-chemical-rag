# 0-SPC-6011 Specification Overview

Specification 0-SPC-6011 outlines the methodology for the Subcontractor to allocate costs within the project. This includes the detailed breakdown of expenses in the [[0-LST-6X11_construction_subcontract_price_list]] and the structure for cost allocation in quotations related to [[construction_subcontract_compensation_event_quotations]].
