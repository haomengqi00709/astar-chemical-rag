# Engineering Subcontract Dispute Resolution (0-SPC-2205)

This page outlines the structured process for resolving disputes arising under or in connection with engineering subcontracts, as defined in document 0-SPC-2205. This procedure aims to facilitate resolution at appropriate levels before resorting to arbitration. For a broader overview of engineering subcontract dispute resolution, refer to [[0-SPC-2202-1_engineering_subcontract_dispute_resolution]].

**Escalation to Senior Representatives:**
*   Any dispute arising under or in connection with this Subcontract that is not resolved within four (4) weeks **must** be submitted to appropriate senior representatives of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Subcontractor.
*   These senior representatives **must**, within a further two (2) weeks of submission, hold a conference in good faith and attempt to resolve the dispute.

**Arbitration:**
*   If the meeting of the senior representatives fails to resolve the dispute, either Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or the Subcontractor **may** refer the dispute to be finally resolved by arbitration.
*   The arbitration **must** be administered by the British Columbia International Commercial Arbitration Centre pursuant to its Rules.
*   Arbitration proceedings **must not** be initiated before [[0-SPC-2205_general_conditions_and_administration#Key Definitions|Completion]] of the Services or earlier termination of the Subcontract.
*   One arbitrator will be appointed in accordance with the Rules of the British Columbia International Commercial Arbitration Centre.
*   The designated place of arbitration is Vancouver, British Columbia.
