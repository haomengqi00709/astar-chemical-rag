# Roles and Responsibilities: Floor Warden

A Floor Warden is an employee who oversees the evacuation of a designated area during an emergency.

**Primary Responsibilities**
The primary responsibility of Floor Wardens is the evacuation process. Floor Wardens **are not responsible** for extinguishing fires.

**Equipment**
During an evacuation, Floor Wardens **must** put on a fire vest and hard hat. They **must** also carry a flashlight. For details on standard equipment, refer to [[0-PRC-8010_emergency_response_equipment]].

**Duties During Evacuation**
*   Floor Wardens **must** supervise the evacuation of all employees and visitors from the designated area on their floor.
*   Floor Wardens **must** "sweep" all areas, including corridors, meeting rooms, and washrooms.
*   Floor Wardens **must** firmly, but politely, insist that all employees and visitors leave immediately.
*   Floor Wardens **must** ensure employees walk, rather than run.
*   Floor Wardens **must** check for any disabled person who may need assistance in exiting the building.

**Post-Clearance and Reporting**
*   After all employees and visitors have been cleared from their area, Floor Wardens **must** leave the building through the nearest safe exit.
*   After exiting the building, Floor Wardens **must** report the status of their floor area to the Chief Warden at the front entrance to the building (see [[0-PRC-8010_roles_and_responsibilities_chief_warden]]).
*   Floor Wardens **must** report the presence of any disabled persons who may need assistance.
*   In the absence of the Building Fire Safety Director, Floor Wardens **must** report directly to the Fire Department.

**Backup Floor Wardens**
Backup Floor Wardens are designated to provide redundancy should the primary Floor Warden be out of the office.
*   The duties of Backup Floor Wardens are identical to those of the Floor Warden.
*   Backup Floor Wardens' duties **must be** performed even if both the Floor Warden and backup are present in the office.
*   In case of an event requiring intervention causing a delay in clearing the building, the backup Warden **is to** proceed to the front door to the building on Cornwall Street and report the event to the Chief Warden.

**Assignments**
Floor Wardens are listed in [[0-PRC-8010_table_5]]. Designated areas for Floor Wardens include the 3rd Floor and 4th Floor. For more details, refer to [[0-PRC-8010_floor_specific_evacuation_details]].
