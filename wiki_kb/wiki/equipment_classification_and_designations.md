# Equipment Classification and Designations

Equipment within the plant is categorized and designated using specific suffixes to facilitate identification and management. These designations are crucial for referencing equipment in [[piping_and_instrumentation_drawings|P&IDs]], [[equipment_data_sheets_4_dst_xxxx|equipment data sheets]], and during [[mechanical_equipment_checkout_procedure|mechanical checkout procedures]].

The primary equipment classifications are as follows:

*   **Suffix 'A' - Agitation and Mixing Equipment:** This category includes [[agitation_and_mixing_equipment_checkout|Agitators]], Mixers, Blenders, and Aerators.
*   **Suffix 'C' - Rotating Equipment (Gas/Air):** This designation covers [[rotating_equipment_mechanical_checkout|Blowers]], Compressors, and Fans.
*   **Suffix 'D' - Vessels (Separation/Collection):** Equipment such as Cyclones, Precipitators, and Dust Collectors fall under this category. These are typically covered under [[vessel_and_tank_mechanical_checkout]].
*   **Suffix 'F' - Packaged Equipment (Thermal/Combustion):** This suffix is used for [[packaged_equipment_mechanical_checkout|Furnaces]], Fired Boilers, Incinerators, Kilns, and Stacks.
*   **Suffix 'G' - Rotating Equipment (Solids Handling):** This category includes [[rotating_equipment_mechanical_checkout|Grinders]], Crushers, and Classifiers.
*   **Suffix 'H' - Heat Transfer Equipment:** This suffix covers [[heat_exchanger_mechanical_checkout|Heat Exchangers]] (Plate, Spiral, or Shell and Tube). It also designates [[packaged_equipment_mechanical_checkout|Packaged Equipment]] such as Cooling Towers, Water Chillers, Economizers, and Refrigeration units.
*   **Suffix 'J' - Vessels (Crystallization/Evaporation):** This category includes Crystallizers and Evaporators, typically covered under [[vessel_and_tank_mechanical_checkout]].
*   **Suffix 'K' - Separation Equipment (Liquid/Solid):** This suffix covers [[separation_equipment_mechanical_checkout|Decanters]], Settlers, Clarifiers, Filters, Mesh Pads, and Candle Filters.
*   **Suffix 'M' - Material Handling Equipment:** This designation is for [[conveying_equipment_mechanical_checkout|Conveying Equipment]], including various types of material handling systems.
*   **Suffix 'P' - Rotating Equipment (Pumps):** This category specifically covers [[rotating_equipment_mechanical_checkout|Pumps]].
*   **Suffix 'R' - Vessels (Reactors/Converters):** This suffix is used for [[vessel_and_tank_mechanical_checkout|Reactors]] and [[converter_shop_overview|Converters]]. It also designates [[packaged_equipment_mechanical_checkout|Packaged Equipment]] such as Water Demineralizers and Brine Ion Exchangers.
*   **Suffix 'T' - Vessels (Storage/Receiving):** This category includes [[vessel_and_tank_mechanical_checkout|Tanks]], Receivers, Drums, and Sumps.
*   **Suffix 'U' - Vessels (Towers/Columns):** This suffix is used for [[vessel_and_tank_mechanical_checkout|Towers]], Columns, Strippers, and Absorbers.
*   **Suffix 'V' - Rotating Equipment (Vacuum):** This designation covers [[rotating_equipment_mechanical_checkout|Vacuum Pumps]] and [[ejector_mechanical_checkout|Ejectors]].
