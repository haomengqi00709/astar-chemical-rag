# Fabrication Tolerances for High Silicon Austenitic Stainless Steel (4-SPC-0025)

All tolerances not specifically indicated in this specification **shall** be in accordance with the unspecified standard. Tolerances after welding and after heat treatment **shall** comply with the requirements of Appendix B of Specification 4-SPC-0025.

**General Tolerance Principles:**
*   Tolerances shown are intended to apply to both vertical and horizontal tanks.
*   Tolerances on angular diameter expressed in degrees **shall** be based upon the equivalent inches of arc.
*   Thickness specified for machined parts are minimum.
*   Tolerances on consecutive dimensions may not be cumulative where prohibited by the tolerance on an overall dimension, or where interference between parts would result.

**Specific Tolerances:**
*   **Vessel & Tank Tolerances:** Refer to Appendix ‘B’, Page 18 & 19 of Specification 4-SPC-0025.
*   **Tower Trays, Etc.:** Tolerances are 1/8 inch, except as noted below:
    *   Tray Support Rings **shall** be Flat to 1/32 inch on any 12 inch chord.
    *   Tray Support Rings **shall** be Level to 3/16 inch for 4-7 feet diameter.
    *   Tray Support Rings **shall** be Level to 1/4 inch over 7 feet diameter.
    *   Top of Weir Plate elevation above tray **shall** be to 1/16 inch.
    *   Top of Weir Plate **shall** be Level to 3/16 inch for 7-13 feet diameter.
    *   Top of Weir Plate **shall** be Level to 1/4 inch over 13 feet diameter.
*   **Shell & Tube Heat Exchanger Tolerances:** Refer to Appendix ‘B’, Page 20 of Specification 4-SPC-0025.

For a detailed breakdown of specific tolerance values, refer to [[4-SPC-0025_table_1]].
