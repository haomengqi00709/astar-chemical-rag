**Purpose and Scope:**
The primary purpose of this checklist is to provide a systematic and comprehensive framework for conducting field inspections of centrifugal blowers. It outlines all mandatory inspection items across various disciplines, including mechanical, electrical, and civil aspects, to ensure compliance with project specifications, safety standards, and manufacturer guidelines. The checklist is designed to identify and document any deviations or deficiencies that could impact the blower's performance or safety.

**Checklist Structure and Usage:**
The checklist is structured to cover 25 distinct inspection items, each requiring verification by the inspection team. For each item, inspectors are required to confirm its satisfactory completion. In cases where issues or non-conformances are identified, the checklist provides 10 dedicated slots for recording [[non_conformance_report_0_ncr_7|deficiencies and comments]]. This structured documentation is essential for tracking corrective actions and ensuring all identified problems are addressed.
