# Construction Subcontract Site Management and Personnel (0-SUB-6)

This page details the requirements for site management programs, personnel, and facilities under Construction Subcontract Agreement 0-SUB-6.

**Site Definition:**
The site is defined as the area within the Stanwell Magnesium Project designated for the hydrochloric acid manufacturing plant, as identified on the site plan included in the Works Information.

**Site Management Programs:**
The Subcontractor **must** prepare and execute site management programs in accordance with the following documents:
*   [[construction_subcontract_safety_management_0_spc_6051]] (0-SPC-6051)
*   [[construction_subcontract_environmental_management_0_spc_6061]] (0-SPC-6061)
*   [[0-SPC-6071_construction_subcontract_quality_assurance]] (0-SPC-6071)
*   [[subcontractor_industrial_relations_management_0_spc_60]] (0-SPC-6081)

**Personnel Requirements:**
*   The Subcontractor **must** maintain the named Key Personnel, along with other necessary management and supervision personnel, at the site as set out in [[construction_subcontract_supervision_schedule_0_lst_6x16]] (0-LST-6X16). Data available in SQL: 0-LST-6X16.
*   The Subcontractor **must** also maintain other management and supervision personnel as needed to provide the Works and to conduct the required management programs effectively.

**Temporary Facilities:**
*   The Subcontractor **must** locate temporary offices and other facilities in the locations shown on the [[construction_facilities_layout_drawing]] (0-GAD-6X01).

**Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Provided Services:**
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **shall** provide the services designated on [[0-LST-6X90-2_construction_subcontract_field_services_scope]]. Data available in SQL: 0-LST-6X90-2. These services will be provided at the locations shown on the [[construction_facilities_layout_drawing]] (0-GAD-6X01).

**Site Information for Bid:**
*   Site information necessary for the Subcontractor to prepare their bid **must** be listed within the subcontract documentation.
