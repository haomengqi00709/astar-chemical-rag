# Dual Laminate FRP Equipment Specification Overview (4-SPC-0043)

This Specification (4-SPC-0043) outlines the requirements for shop-fabricated dual laminate (thermoplastic lined) fiberglass reinforced plastic (FRP) equipment, specifically for liquid storage tanks or vessels. It details Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' specific requirements, which serve as exceptions and additions to the 1999 Edition of ASME RTP-1: Standard for Reinforced Thermoset Plastic Corrosion Resistant Equipment, particularly its Mandatory Appendix M-12, "Dual Laminate Vessels."

**Key Principles and Exclusions:**
*   RTP-1 accreditation is not required for fabricating or supplying equipment under this Specification.
*   The User's Basic Requirements Specification (UBRS), as detailed in RTP-1 Para. 1-200, will not be completed.
*   This Specification is not to be used for vessels in critical service, as defined in RTP-1 Para. 1-210.
*   The FRP structural wall and outer layers of dual laminate vessels **shall** be designed to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics standard [[4-SPC-0042_frp_vessel_specification_overview]] ("Fiberglass Reinforced Plastic (FRP) Equipment").
*   The inner FRP corrosion liner, typically specified in [[4-SPC-0042_frp_vessel_specification_overview]], will be omitted from the design.

**Design and Fabrication Adherence:**
Design and fabrication activities **shall** substantially conform to [[ASME_rtp_1_standard_for_reinforced_thermoset_plastic_corrosion_resistant_equipment]] Part 3 - Design and Part 4 - Fabrication, respectively. This includes adherence to RTP-1 Non-mandatory Appendices NM-2, NM-3, NM-4, NM-5, NM-12, and NM-13, as well as Mandatory Appendix M-12, "Dual Laminate Vessels." The fabricator **must** apply fabrication details from RTP-1 Part 4 and Mandatory Appendix M-12, regardless of vessel size or if Subpart 3B design rules are employed.

**Revision History:**
*   **Revision 1:** The specification was completely rewritten.
*   **Revision 2:** RTP-1 Appendix M-14 was corrected to M-12 to align with the latest revision.
*   **2005 Name Change Conversion:** A name change was implemented without a new revision number or marked changes. Refer to [[2005_name_change_conversion_details]] for more information.

This Specification also provides Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' specific requirements as exceptions and additions to RTP-1 Mandatory Appendix M-12, Dual Laminate Vessels.

**General Requirements and Restrictions:**
*   RTP-1 accreditation is not required to fabricate equipment to this Specification (refer to RTP-1 Para. 1-520 for general accreditation requirements).
*   The User's Basic Requirements Specification (UBRS) will not be completed (refer to RTP-1 Para. 1-200 for UBRS requirements).
*   This Specification will not be used for vessels in critical service (refer to RTP-1 Para. 1-210 for service restrictions).
