# Insulation Design and Specification (6-PRC-0002)

This document, 6-PRC-0002, details the discipline assignments for the preparation, checking, and approval of insulation design and specifications for the Project. It outlines the roles and responsibilities for developing all insulation-related documentation.

This procedure ensures that all insulation design documents, including material specifications and application details, are developed, reviewed, and approved in a structured manner, adhering to the [[insulation_design_criteria_6_prc_0001]] and overall project quality standards.
