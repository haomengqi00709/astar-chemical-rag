# Engineering Subcontract Insurance and Liability (0-SPC-2205)

This page details the insurance requirements and liability limitations for engineering subcontracts as specified in document 0-SPC-2205. These provisions are crucial for risk management and financial protection for both Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Subcontractor. For general engineering subcontractor insurance requirements, refer to [[engineering_subcontractor_insurance_requirements]]. For general liability considerations, see [[engineering_subcontractor_liability_and_security]].

**Insurance Requirements:**
*   The Subcontractor **must** provide the insurances specified in [[0-SPC-2205_table_2_insurance_requirements|0-SPC-2205_table_2]].
*   Additionally, the Subcontractor **must** provide any other insurances stated in the [[0-SPC-2205_table_1_subcontract_data_reference|Subcontract Data]], with the exception of any insurance that Aker Kvaerner Chemetics is explicitly designated to provide.
*   All required insurances **must** provide continuous cover from the [[0-SPC-2205_general_conditions_and_administration#Key Definitions|Subcontract Date]] until the end of the periods specified in the [[0-SPC-2205_table_1_subcontract_data_reference|Subcontract Data]].
*   Upon request by either Party, the other Party **must** provide a certificate from their insurer or broker confirming that their insurances are in force.

**Limitation of Liability:**
*   The Subcontractor’s liability to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics resulting from a failure to Provide the Services is limited to the amount stated in the [[0-SPC-2205_table_1_subcontract_data_reference|Subcontract Data]].

**Limitation of Liability:**
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ acceptance of a communication from the Subcontractor does not change the Subcontractor’s liabilities or responsibility to Provide the Services.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ review of the Subcontractor's work does not change the Subcontractor’s liabilities or responsibility to Provide the Services.
