# Construction Subcontract Crew Rates

The Subcontractor **must** complete the [[0-LST-6X13_construction_subcontract_crew_rates]] document. This document is utilized to present an average total cost per hour worked for each direct labour work crew involved in Providing the Works.

Crew rates **shall** be calculated using the individual [[construction_subcontract_labour_rates]] determined as per the guidelines in Section 2 of the governing specification (0-SPC-6011). This ensures consistency and accuracy in cost estimation for crew-based activities.
