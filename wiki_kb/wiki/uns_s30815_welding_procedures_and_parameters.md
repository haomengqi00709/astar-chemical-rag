# Welding Procedures and Parameters for UNS S30815 Fabrications

This page details the requirements for welding procedures, welder qualifications, and specific parameters for welding UNS S30815 materials. Strict adherence to these guidelines is essential for achieving the required quality and integrity of welded fabrications.

**Welding Procedure Qualification and Approval**
*   All welding procedures **MUST** be submitted by the Fabricator for review and agreement by Chemetics' Materials and Quality Assurance Group prior to commencement of any work.
*   The [[ASME_section_ix_welding_and_brazing_qualifications]] PQR Form 483 **WILL** form part of the welding procedure package.
*   A change in essential variables, as outlined in [[ASME_section_ix_welding_and_brazing_qualifications]], **WILL** require re-qualification and re-submission of the affected procedure(s).
*   The welding procedure **MUST** be agreed upon before any production welding is begun.

**Welder Qualifications**
*   All welders employed on the work **MUST** be suitably qualified in accordance with relevant codes and standards for every procedure they are to use.
*   The Fabricator **WILL**, upon request, supply objective evidence of current welder qualifications.
*   All qualified welders **WILL** be allocated an identification symbol. This symbol **MUST** be identified on the parent material adjacent to their work.

**Preferred Welding Methods and Electrodes**
*   The preferred welding methods are Gas Tungsten Arc Welding (GTAW) and Gas Metal Arc Welding (GMAW).
*   Other welding processes **WILL** be evaluated upon submission of full details, including the procedure and exact location of proposed weldments.
*   The preferred GTAW electrode is thoriated tungsten as per [[ASME_sfa_5_12_tungsten_arc_welding_electrodes]], Class EWTh-2.
*   High frequency (HF) voltage **WILL** be used to start the arc for GTAW.

**Welding Parameters and Gas Requirements**
*   Weld metal thickness **WILL NOT** exceed pass.
*   Heat input **WILL** be kept low by using a stringer bead technique.
*   Back-up gas (normally 100% argon with 99.9% purity) **WILL** be used to prevent root pass oxidation and contamination.
*   Back-up gas requirement is **MANDATORY** for root pass welds on piping or other items where access to the internal weld surface is limited.
*   GMAW **WILL** only be used for the root pass of butt welds and for fillet welds unless specifically agreed otherwise.
*   Gas backing (argon) is **REQUIRED** for GMAW root pass welds where accessibility to the internal weld surface for grinding, etc., is not possible.

**Preheat and Postweld Heat Treatment (PWHT)**
*   Preheat is **NOT** required.
*   When adverse weather conditions cause condensation or wetness, the material **WILL** be dried prior to welding by heating. Drying temperature **MUST** be just sufficient to prevent condensation.
*   Heat input during welding **SHOULD** be kept to a minimum.
*   Interpass temperature **SHOULD NOT** exceed .
*   Postweld Heat Treatment (PWHT) is seldom required. In the event that PWHT is specified, the fabricator **WILL** submit their procedure in writing for approval prior to commencement of work. The PWHT procedure **WILL** include information on the number and location of thermocouples and supports.
