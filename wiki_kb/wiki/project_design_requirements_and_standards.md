# Project Design Requirements and Standards

Establishing clear design requirements and adhering to appropriate standards are crucial for the Project's success and compliance.

**Defining Project Requirements**
Each Discipline Lead is responsible for reviewing the Contract to ascertain the Owner’s specific requirements for the Project. Concurrently, the Project Engineer surveys all statutes and regulations applicable to the Project and advises each Discipline Lead of any additional requirements stemming from these legal and regulatory frameworks.

These comprehensive requirements are then formally documented by the Discipline Leads in various design criteria documents, including:
*   [[site_design_conditions_1_dst_0001]]: Site Design Conditions (1-DST-0001).
*   [[battery_limits_conditions_1_lst_0001]]: Battery Limits Conditions (1-LST-0001). Data available in SQL: 1-LST-0001
*   [[process_design_criteria_document]]: Process Design Criteria (1-PRC-0001).
*   [[civil_works_design_criteria_2_prc_0001]]: Civil Works Design Criteria (2-PRC-0001).
*   [[structural_and_architectural_design_criteria_3_prc_0001]]: Structural and Architectural Design Criteria (3-PRC-0001).
*   [[equipment_design_criteria_4_prc_0002]]: Equipment Design Criteria (4-PRC-0002).
*   [[piping_design_criteria_5_prc_0002]]: Piping Design Criteria (5-PRC-0002).
*   [[insulation_design_criteria_6_prc_0001]]: Insulation Design Criteria (6-PRC-0001).
*   [[coatings_design_criteria_7_prc_0001]]: Coatings Design Criteria (7-PRC-0001).
*   [[instrumentation_and_control_design_criteria_8_prc_0002]]: Instrumentation and Control Design Criteria (8-PRC-0002).
*   [[electrical_design_criteria]]: Electrical Design Criteria (9-PRC-0002).

**Standard Document Application and Conflict Resolution**
Discipline Leads are also responsible for determining which existing Aker Kvaerner Chemetics standard documents are applicable to the Project's [[project_scope_and_deliverables]]. They **must** identify any instances where these standard documents conflict with the Owner's requirements as specified in the Contract.

Aker Kvaerner Chemetics standard documents are modified as necessary to align with project-specific requirements. These modified standard documents are then issued for the Project in accordance with [[standard_document_management_procedure_0_prc_0001]]: Approval and Use of Standards.

Any conflicts or ambiguities found within the Contract requirements or applicable statutes/regulations **must** be identified to the Owner. Similarly, any requirements that prevent the achievement of the [[project_quality_objectives]] (as stated in Section 1.3 of the original document) **must** also be identified to the Owner. Such identifications are formally communicated through a request for a Variation under the Contract.

**Managing Conflicts and Variations**
Conflicts or ambiguities in Contract requirements or applicable statutes/regulations **must** be identified to the Owner. Similarly, any requirements preventing the meeting of Project Quality Objectives (as stated in Section 1.3) **must** also be identified to the Owner. The identification of such conflicts, ambiguities, or preventing requirements is done by way of a request for a Variation under the Contract.
