# HSE Incident Reporting Procedure Overview

This procedure, identified as document 0-PRC-8014, provides comprehensive guidelines for the reporting, investigation, and follow-up of all accidents and near-miss incidents within the Company's operations.

**Purpose and Aims**
The primary aims of this procedure are to:
*   Establish an effective system for personnel to notify the [[hse_department]] and appropriate line management in the event of an accident or near-miss.
*   Ensure suitable arrangements are in place for prompt reporting of accidents and near-miss incidents to relevant persons, Senior Management, and external authorities as required.
*   Facilitate effective communication to enable a prompt response to incidents and ensure a thorough investigation.
*   Comply with Aker Kvaerner Group HSE Norms and procedures.

**Scope of Application**
This procedure applies to all work sites where the Company is in control of operations. It also extends to other work sites to the extent that the Company can enforce the application of its HSE management processes.

**Definitions**
For the purpose of this procedure:
*   **Company:** Refers to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
*   **Group:** Refers to Aker Kvaerner Group companies.
*   **HSE:** Stands for Health, Safety, and Environment.
*   **Near Miss:** Defined as a discharge of energy (or release of hazardous chemical) that had the potential to cause injury to an employee, damage to the environment, or a loss.
*   **Accident:** Defined as an event that affected the Health or Safety of employees, caused damage to the environment, or resulted in property loss.
*   **Incident:** Encompasses both an Accident and a Near Miss incident.

**Incident Reporting Process**
All incidents **must** be reported to the HSE Manager of the Business Unit using the Standard-0-REP-8014 “Incident Notification Form”, which is detailed in [[0_frm_8014_document_overview]]. This reporting **must** occur within the timeframe established in the [[2.2_no_1_incident_follow_up_norm]]. The overall incident follow-up process is also specified in the [[2.2_no_1_incident_follow_up_norm]].

For detailed roles and responsibilities related to incident reporting, refer to [[hse_incident_reporting_roles_and_responsibilities]]. Specific instructions for managing the incident scene are provided in [[incident_scene_quarantine_and_preservation]].
