# Insulation Erection (0-SPC-6X96)

This page describes the requirements for Insulation Erection as outlined in document 0-SPC-6X96. The Subcontractor **must** adhere to these specifications for all insulation installation activities related to the construction subcontract. This includes, but is not limited to, the application of thermal and acoustic insulation materials to equipment and piping.

Refer to [[0-SUB-6_construction_subcontract_scope_of_works]] for the specific application of this specification within the overall project scope.
