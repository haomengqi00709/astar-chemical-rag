# Plant Constructability Review Objectives and Timing

The Plant Constructability Review is a critical process designed to integrate construction considerations early in the project lifecycle. This review **must** be conducted at the earliest opportunity to maximize its impact and enable design concepts to be incorporated without incurring major cost expenditures later in the project.

**Timing of the Review:**
The review **shall** be held during the development of the [[pfd_drawing_standards|Plot Plan]] and [[equipment_proprietary_detail_drawing_prp|Equipment Layouts]]. It is crucial that this review occurs prior to the commencement of detailed design activities.

**Key Objectives of the Review:**
The primary objectives of the Plant Constructability Review include ensuring that the detailed design is carried out with a comprehensive understanding of:
*   How equipment will be installed.
*   How equipment will be accessed for operational purposes.
*   How equipment will be maintained throughout its lifecycle.
*   How the overall Plant will be constructed efficiently and safely.

By addressing these aspects proactively, the review aims to prevent costly rework, improve project schedules, and enhance the overall operability and maintainability of the plant.

For an overview of the procedure, see [[0-prc-6001_plant_constructability_review_procedure_overview]]. The methodology for conducting the review is detailed in [[plant_constructability_review_process_and_methodology]].
