# Electrical Wiring Diagrams

Electrical Wiring Diagrams illustrate the interconnecting wiring between various pieces of electrical equipment, detailing terminal numbers, cable numbers, conductor numbers, and internal jumpers. These diagrams are created by following control schematics and block diagrams.

**Content of Wiring Diagrams**
Wiring diagrams **shall** show:
*   The equipment terminal numbers.
*   The interconnecting cable numbers.
*   The cable conductor numbers.
*   The necessary internal jumpers on the terminal blocks.

**Creation and References**
Wiring diagrams **shall** show the interconnecting wiring between pieces of equipment. [[motor_control_schematic_wiring_diagrams]] **will** be referenced and followed in creating the wiring diagrams. [[electrical_block_diagrams]] **will** also be referenced and followed in creating the wiring diagrams.
