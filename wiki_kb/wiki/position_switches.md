# Position Switches

Position switches are used to detect the physical position of equipment, such as valves or mechanical components.

**Preferred Type:**
Inductive Proximity Switches **will be** used in preference to traditional limit switches due to their reliability, non-contact operation, and reduced maintenance requirements.

**Power Supply:**
Unless otherwise specified, position switches **will be** suitable for operation on a 24 V d.c. power supply, aligning with common plant control system voltages (refer to [[signal_transmission_and_power|Signal Transmission and Power]]).

**Environmental Rating:**
All position switch devices **will** have corrosion-resistant, weatherproof IP65 rated cases. They **will** also be suitable for outdoor mounting, ensuring their durability and functionality in the plant's environmental conditions (refer to [[environmental_and_site_conditions|Environmental and Site Conditions]]).
