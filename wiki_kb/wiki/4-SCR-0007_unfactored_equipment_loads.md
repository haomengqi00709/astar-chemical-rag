# Skirt Supported Vessel - Unfactored Equipment Loads (4-SCR-0007)

This page details the unfactored equipment loads specified in document [[4-SCR-0007_document_administration]], which pertains to a skirt-supported vessel on a concrete pad. These loads represent the maximum anticipated forces and masses under various operating and environmental conditions, prior to the application of any safety factors.

**Unfactored Equipment Loads:**
*   **Empty - Maximum Mass:** Specified in kg.
*   **Flooded (Design) - Maximum Mass:** Specified in kg.
*   **Operating - Maximum Mass:** Specified in kg.
*   **Wind Base Shear:** Specified in kN.
*   **Wind Base Moment:** Specified in kN-m.
*   **Seismic Base Shear:** Specified in kN.
*   **Seismic Base Moment:** Specified in kN-m.

These loads are critical inputs for the structural design of the concrete pad and the vessel's support structure. All loads are to be applied at Datum, as further detailed in [[4-SCR-0007_support_criteria_basis_and_general_requirements]]. For related load calculations, refer to documents such as [[4-CAL-0026_total_load_calculation]].
