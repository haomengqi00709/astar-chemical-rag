# Construction Subcontract Major Work Division Specifications (0-SPC-6X9X)

The Construction Subcontract Major Work Division Specifications (0-SPC-6X9X series) are a critical component of the [[construction_subcontract_scope_preparation_0_prc_6091|Construction Subcontract Scope Preparation (0-PRC-6091)]] procedure. These specifications provide the primary description of the works for various major work divisions within a construction subcontract, directly referenced by Section 2.1 of the [[construction_subcontract_agreement_procedure_0_prc_6002|Construction Subcontract Agreement (0-PRC-6002)]].

### Specific Major Work Division Specifications

*   **0-SPC-6X92**: Civil Construction and Site Preparation
*   **0-SPC-6X93**: Structural and Buildings Construction
*   **0-SPC-6X94**: Equipment Erection
*   **0-SPC-6X95**: Piping Erection
*   **0-SPC-6X96**: Insulation Erection
*   **0-SPC-6X97**: Coatings Erection
*   **0-SPC-6X98**: Instrumentation and Control Erection
*   **0-SPC-6X99**: Electrical Erection

Each specification is prepared by the responsible Discipline using the template "0-SPC-6X9X: [Template] [Major Work Division] Construction". The "X" in the document number **must** correspond to the digit used for the specific [[construction_subcontract_agreement_procedure_0_prc_6002|Subcontract Agreement (0-PRC-6002)]]. These documents are numbered according to [[document_numbering_and_name_change_conventions|0-PRC-0002: Document Numbering]] and published with an "Approved for Construction" status via [[publishing_project_documents|0-PRC-0005: Publishing of Project Documents]]. They are checked by the Manager of Construction and approved by the Project Engineer.

### Section 6: Specific Requirements

A key section within each 0-SPC-6X9X specification is "Section 6: Specific Requirements". This section is designed to elaborate on the work scope beyond what is detailed in referenced drawings, other specifications, and documents. Its primary objective is to enable a generally experienced Subcontractor to accurately quantify the works involved.

Topics typically covered in Section 6 include, but are not limited to:

*   Assembly of items that have been disassembled for shipping purposes.
*   Installation of equipment internals or components that are supplied separately from the main equipment.
*   Any specific requirements, procedures, or methodologies that are not adequately described in other referenced specifications or documents.

Each 0-SPC-6X9X specification also directly references a [[construction_subcontract_supply_and_erection_scope_checklist|scope checklist]], which further clarifies the division of supply and erection responsibilities.

---
Section 2.1 of the [[construction_subcontract_agreement_procedure_0_prc_6002|Construction Subcontract Agreement (0-PRC-6002)]] refers directly to these specific specifications.

These specifications are prepared as required for each Subcontract, with one for each Major Work Division, by the responsible Discipline. The template for these specifications is `0-SPC-6X9X: [Template] [Major Work Division] Construction`. The "X" in the specification numbers will be replaced by the same digit as for the Subcontract Agreement (0-PRC-6002).

The series includes the following specific specifications:
*   **0-SPC-6X92:** Civil Construction and Site Preparation
*   **0-SPC-6X93:** Structural and Buildings Construction
*   **0-SPC-6X94:** Equipment Erection
*   **0-SPC-6X95:** Piping Erection
*   **0-SPC-6X96:** Insulation Erection
*   **0-SPC-6X97:** Coatings Erection
*   **0-SPC-6X98:** Instrumentation and Control Erection
*   **0-SPC-6X99:** Electrical Erection

**Document Control:**
*   **Numbering:** The specifications are numbered using [[document_numbering_0_prc_0002|0-PRC-0002: Document Numbering]].
*   **Status:** They will be published with status “Approved for Construction”.
*   **Checking:** Checked by the Manager of Construction.
*   **Approval:** Approved by the Project Engineer.

**Section 6: Specific Requirements**
Section 6 of each [Major Work Division] Specification is entitled “Specific Requirements”. This section is intended to provide additional description of the work over and above that provided on referenced drawings, specifications, and other documents. Its primary aim is to enable a generally experienced Subcontractor to quantify the works.

Topics typically covered in Section 6 include:
*   Assembly of items disassembled for shipping.
*   Installation of equipment internals or components supplied separately.
*   Any requirements or procedures not described in a specification.

**Scope Checklist Reference:**
Each 0-SPC-6X9X: [Major Work Division] Construction directly references a scope checklist. Reference to applicable documents by 0-SPC-6X9X is not necessary unless it accompanies some further description of the works to improve clarity.
