# Vendor Stamp: Reviewed

The "REVIEWED" vendor stamp indicates that a document has undergone a review process. This stamp is typically found on Page 4 of the vendor stamp procedure document.

**Mandatory Requirements for Application:**
*   A signature **shall** be applied to the stamp.
*   The date of application **shall** be recorded on the stamp.

This stamp confirms that the document has been examined by the appropriate personnel, though it does not explicitly state approval for work to proceed or require revisions in the same manner as other stamps. It primarily serves as an acknowledgment of review. This stamp is associated with [[chemetics_aker_kvaerner_company_details|CHEMETICS]] documentation standards.

For an overview of all vendor stamp options, refer to [[vendor_stamp_procedure_overview]].

**Mandatory Requirements for Application:**
*   The date of application **shall** be included.
This stamp is specifically used for Chemetics projects.
