# Instrument Vendor Document Requirements (8-VDR-8XXX)

The Vendor Document Requirements (VDR) form (8-VDR-8XXX) specifies the documentation expected from vendors for both field instruments and instrument bulk materials. It refers to [[review_of_vendor_documentation_0_prc_0007]].

**For Field Instruments:**
*   **Prepared by:** Field Instrument Engineer (FIE)
*   **Checked by:** Quality Assurance
*   **Approved by:** Field Instrument Engineer (FIE)

The FIE will provide a "8-VDR-8XXX: Vendor Documentation Requirements" form (VDR) for each instrument requisition. VDR forms will generally be quite typical. Exceptions may be required to facilitate Hazardous Area Certificates or Calibration Certificates. Calibration Certificates are required wherever it is possible to have the vendors provide this certification. Exceptions for Calibration Certificates will generally be simple devices such as pressure gauges and temperature gauges. The Project Engineer **is to be advised** when Calibration Certificates may increase the cost of the Purchase Order by an excessive amount. If Calibration Certificates are waived, the site installation costs will increase due to instrument calibration requirements on site for instruments without calibration certificates.

**For Instrument Bulk Material:**
*   **Prepared by:** Detail Design Engineer (DDE)
*   **Checked by:** Quality Assurance
*   **Approved by:** Detail Design Engineer (DDE)

The DDE will provide a "8-VDR-8XXX: Vendor Documentation Requirements" form (VDR) for each instrument type (for Instrument Bulk Material). VDR forms for Instrument Bulk Material will generally be quite typical. Exceptions may be required to facilitate Hazardous Area Certificates or Calibration Certificates. Calibration Certificates are required wherever it is possible to have the vendors provide this certification for Instrument Bulk Material. Exceptions for Calibration Certificates for Instrument Bulk Material will generally be simple devices such as pressure gauges and temperature gauges.
