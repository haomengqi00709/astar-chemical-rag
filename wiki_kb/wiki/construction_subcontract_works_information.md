# Construction Subcontract Works Information

The Construction Subcontract Works Information refers to the comprehensive set of documents and specifications that define the scope, requirements, and conditions for the works to be executed by a Construction Subcontractor.

Compliance with the Works Information is a mandatory requirement. For instance, if a submitted schedule does not comply with the Works Information, it may be a reason for [[construction_subcontract_schedule_management_and_reporting#Schedule Submission and Acceptance|Aker Kvaerner Chemetics to not accept the schedule]].
