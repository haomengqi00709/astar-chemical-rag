# Personal Protective Equipment for Shop Visits

Aker Kvaerner Chemetics employees visiting a fabricating shop are **required** to wear minimum personal protection equipment (PPE) to ensure their safety. These requirements are in addition to any specific PPE mandated by the vendor or shop owner, which **must** also be adhered to. For general recommendations on equipment types and conditions of use, refer to [[personal_protective_equipment_policy]] (Standard-0-8015).

**Required Minimum PPE:**
*   **Clothing:** Long sleeves and long pants are **required** to cover arms and legs, providing protection against cuts, abrasions, and minor burns.
*   **Work Boots:** Work boots that meet the CSA Z195 Standard are **required**. Refer to [[safety_footwear_requirements]] for more details.
*   **Protective Eyewear:** Glasses with side shields or goggles are **required**. All protective eyewear **must** meet the CSA Z94.3 Standard. The CSA Logo indicates compliance with this standard. Refer to [[eye_and_face_protection_requirements]] for further information.

**Recommended and Situational PPE:**
*   **Hard Hat:** A hard hat meeting CAN/CSA Standard Z94.1-92 is recommended and **required** under specific conditions:
    *   If a bumping hazard is present.
    *   If fork lift operations are present in the vicinity.
    *   If there is any possibility for falling objects.
    *   If people are working above the employee's location.
    Refer to [[head_protection_requirements]] for more details.
*   **Hearing Protection:** **Required** if exposed to noisy environments. Refer to [[hearing_protection_requirements]].
*   **Respirator:** **Required** if exposed to paint fumes, FRP resin vapors, or dust. Employees **must** wear an appropriate respirator in these conditions. Refer to [[respiratory_protection_requirements]].
*   **Gloves:** Recommended for general hand protection. Refer to [[hand_protection_requirements]].

Other safety equipment may be required by the vendor or shop owner, and employees **must** comply with these additional requirements as identified during the [[shop_visit_safety_orientation]].

**Carrying Personal Equipment**
Employees may need to carry their own safety equipment.

**Minimum Required PPE**
The minimum personal protection equipment required includes:
*   Clothing that covers arms and legs (long sleeves and long pants).
*   Work boots which meet CSA Z195 Standard.
*   Glasses with side shields, or goggles. All protective eyewear must meet CSA Z94.3 Standard, indicated by the CSA Logo.

**Specific Recommendations and Requirements**
For specific recommendations on the type of equipment and conditions of use, refer to specification Standard-0-8015 Personal Protection Equipment.
*   **Hard Hat:** A hard hat which meets CAN/CSA Standard Z94.1-92 is recommended. It is required to be worn if a bumping hazard is present, if forklift operations are present, if there is any possibility for falling objects, or if people are working above.
*   **Hearing Protection:** Required if exposed to noisy environments.
*   **Respirator:** Required if exposed to paint fumes, FRP resin vapors, or dust. Employees must wear an appropriate respirator under these conditions.
*   **Gloves:** Recommended.
