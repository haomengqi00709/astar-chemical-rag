# 0-QDS-7402 Quality Table Activities

Beyond the detailed Non-Destructive Examination, Hydrostatic Testing, and Final Inspection, the Quality Data Sheet (0-QDS-7402) also identifies other key activities within its Quality table. These include:

*   **Material Verification**: Ensuring that all materials used comply with specified requirements.
*   **Pressure/Leak Testing**: Verifying the integrity and leak-tightness of the Strainer.
