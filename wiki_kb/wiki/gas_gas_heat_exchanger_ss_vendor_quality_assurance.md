For the Gas/Gas Heat Exchanger - SS project (Document ID: [[0-QDS-7403-2_gas_gas_heat_exchanger_ss_quality_data_sheet]]), the Vendor **must** adhere to specific quality assurance requirements.

The Vendor **shall** prepare a comprehensive Quality Plan. This plan **must** be developed in strict accordance with the guidelines and specifications outlined in [[vendor_quality_assurance_equipment_0_spc_7001]].

**Compliance Requirements:**
The Vendor's overall Quality Assurance system and practices **shall** comply fully with:
*   The specific requirements detailed in this Quality Data Sheet (0-QDS-7403-2).
*   The general Vendor Quality Assurance specifications provided in [[vendor_quality_assurance_equipment_0_spc_7001]].

**Chemetics Surveillance:**
Chemetics will conduct surveillance activities for this project at **Level 2.0**. The scope and methodology of this surveillance **shall** be as explicitly set out within the [[0-QDS-7403-2_gas_gas_heat_exchanger_ss_quality_data_sheet]] document. This level of surveillance ensures that critical manufacturing and inspection stages are monitored by Chemetics personnel.
