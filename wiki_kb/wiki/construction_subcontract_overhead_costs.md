# Construction Subcontract Overhead Costs

Overheads are defined as cost items that are proportional to the total Price of the subcontract. These are typically corporate-based cost items, and their recoveries are derived from all projects that the Subcontractor undertakes, not solely from the specific project.

**Cost Entry and Calculation:**
*   The Subcontractor **must** enter overhead costs in the "Cover Sheet" worksheet within the [[0-LST-6X11_construction_subcontract_price_list]].
*   Overhead costs **must** be expressed as percentages of the combined total of [[construction_subcontract_direct_work_costs|Direct Work Items]] and [[construction_subcontract_indirect_work_costs|Indirect Work Items]].
