# Mist Eliminator Inspection and Documentation Requirements

This section details the mandatory inspection points and documentation requirements for Mist Eliminators, specifying the required quality records and the level of Chemetics' surveillance. These requirements ensure the quality and compliance of all components and the final product.

**Required Quality Records and Surveillance**
The following inspections and documentation **are required** for Mist Eliminators:

*   **Certificate of Compliance:**
    *   **Required as:** FP ([[0-QDS-7422_quality_record_definitions]])
    *   **Surveillance:** Chemetics Witness

*   **Material Test Certificates (Metallic Components):**
    *   **Required as:** FP ([[0-QDS-7422_quality_record_definitions]])
    *   **Surveillance:** Chemetics Witness

*   **Material Certification (Mesh Pad Fibre):**
    *   **Required as:** FP ([[0-QDS-7422_quality_record_definitions]])
    *   **Surveillance:** Chemetics Witness

*   **Weld Quality:**
    *   **Surveillance:** Chemetics Witness and Hold

*   **Dimensional Checks:**
    *   **Required as:** FP ([[0-QDS-7422_quality_record_definitions]])
    *   **Surveillance:** Chemetics Witness and Hold

*   **Flatness of Flange Faces:**
    *   **Required as:** FP ([[0-QDS-7422_quality_record_definitions]])
    *   **Surveillance:** Chemetics Witness and Hold

*   **Quantities:**
    *   **Surveillance:** Chemetics Witness and Hold

*   **QA Documentation Review:**
    *   **Surveillance:** Chemetics Witness and Hold

*   **Packaging/Tagging:**
    *   **Surveillance:** Chemetics Witness

*   **Packing List:**
    *   **Required as:** AP ([[0-QDS-7422_quality_record_definitions]])
    *   **Surveillance:** Chemetics Witness

These requirements are integral to the [[0-QDS-7422_vendor_quality_assurance_requirements|Vendor Quality Assurance]] process and are subject to the [[0-QDS-7422_chemetics_surveillance_level|Chemetics Surveillance Level]] specified for this equipment.
