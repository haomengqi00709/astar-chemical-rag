# Gas/Gas Heat Exchanger Design Metal Temperatures

The accurate determination of design and mean metal temperatures is a critical step in the mechanical design of gas/gas heat exchangers. These temperatures are calculated using [[4-CAL-0002_gas_gas_hx_design_temperature_calculations]]: Gas/Gas Hx Design Temperatures.

**Calculation Requirements:**
*   This calculation **must** be performed for all operating cases, considering various gas strengths.
*   The calculation **must** identify the worst-case scenario for the design metal temperature of each individual component.

**Components for Temperature Calculation:**
Design and mean metal temperatures are specifically calculated for the following key components of the gas/gas heat exchanger:
*   Top vestibule
*   Top tubesheet
*   Top expansion plate
*   Shell
*   Bottom expansion plate
*   Bottom tubesheet
*   Bottom vestibule (tubeside)
*   Bottom vestibule (shellside)
*   Tubes
*   Base ring

These temperature values are essential inputs for subsequent stress analysis and material selection processes, ensuring that each component is designed to safely withstand its operational thermal environment.

For an overview of the entire mechanical calculation procedure, refer to [[4-prc-0009_gas_gas_exchanger_mechanical_calculations]]. General design principles are covered in [[gas_gas_heat_exchanger_design_criteria]].
