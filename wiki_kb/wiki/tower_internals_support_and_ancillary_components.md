# Tower Internals Support and Ancillary Components

This page details the specifications for various support and ancillary components within tower internals, as documented in the [[4-DST-XXXX-R0_tower_internals_datasheet_administration|Tower Internals Datasheet (4-DST-XXXX-R0)]]. These components are essential for the proper functioning, stability, and maintenance of the tower's internal structure, particularly in relation to [[tower_internals_packing_specifications|packing]].

**Packing Support Specifications:**
*   **Packing Support Tag No.**: This is a mandatory field for unique identification of the packing support.
*   **Packing Support Access Area**: This is a mandatory field, specifying the area required for accessing the packing support.
*   **Packing Support Type**: This is a mandatory field, indicating the design type of the support structure.
*   **Packing Support Size**: This is specified as "% min open area", with the unit being %. This ensures adequate liquid and vapor flow through the support.
*   **Packing Support Material**: This is a mandatory field, detailing the material of construction for the packing support.

**Bed Limiter Specifications:**
*   **Bed Limiter Tag No.**: This is a mandatory field for unique identification of the bed limiter.
*   **Bed Limiter Access Area**: This is a mandatory field, specifying the area required for accessing the bed limiter.
*   **Bed Limiter Type**: This is a mandatory field, indicating the design type of the bed limiter.
*   **Bed Limiter Material**: This is a mandatory field, detailing the material of construction for the bed limiter. Bed limiters prevent packing from fluidizing or migrating upwards.

**Redistributor/Distributor Specifications:**
*   **Redistributor/Distributor Tag No.**: This is a mandatory field for unique identification of the redistributor or distributor.
*   **Redistributor/Distributor Access Area**: This is a mandatory field, specifying the area required for accessing the redistributor/distributor.
*   **Redistributor/Distributor Type**: This is a mandatory field, indicating the design type of the liquid redistributor or distributor.
*   **Redistributor/Distributor Material**: This is a mandatory field, detailing the material of construction for the redistributor/distributor, which ensures uniform liquid distribution over the packing.

**Collector Plates Specifications:**
*   **Collector Plates Tag No.**: This is a mandatory field for unique identification of the collector plates.
*   **Collector Plates Access Area**: This is a mandatory field, specifying the area required for accessing the collector plates.
*   **Collector Plates Type**: This is a mandatory field, indicating the design type of the collector plates.
*   **Collector Plates Material**: This is a mandatory field, detailing the material of construction for the collector plates, which are used to collect and redistribute liquid or separate phases.

All these components are designed to operate effectively under the [[tower_internals_process_design_conditions|process design conditions]] specified for the tower.
