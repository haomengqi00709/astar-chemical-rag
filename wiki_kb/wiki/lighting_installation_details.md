# Lighting Installation Details

Lighting installation details provide specific instructions and component requirements for supporting and installing various types of lighting fixtures and associated hardware.

**Content of Lighting Details**
Lighting details **shall** show:
*   Lighting fixtures.
*   The method of supporting lighting fixtures from ceilings.
*   The method of supporting lighting fixtures from walls.
*   The method of supporting lighting fixtures along walkways.
*   The method of supporting lighting fixtures under walkways.
*   The method of supporting lighting fixtures along roadways.
*   Various ways of installing fixtures according to the reflectors used.

**Mounting Hardware for Lighting**
Mounting hardware for lighting **shall** include:
*   Junction boxes.
*   Fixture hangers.
*   Conduit boxes.
*   Cable fittings.
*   Nuts.
*   Bolts.
