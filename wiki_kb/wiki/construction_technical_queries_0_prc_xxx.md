# Construction Technical Queries (0-PRC-XXX)

This document, 0-PRC-XXX, outlines the procedure for the resolution of Technical Queries (TQs) arising during the construction phase of the Project. It defines the process for submitting, reviewing, and responding to technical questions from subcontractors.

The Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site management team is responsible for providing resolution to Subcontractor’s Technical Queries where possible, and for facilitating resolution from the design team in Vancouver when necessary. This procedure ensures timely and accurate technical support for construction activities.
