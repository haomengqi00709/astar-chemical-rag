# Electrical Field Checkout Procedure Overview (9-PRC-0004)

The Electrical Field Checkout Procedure, documented as 9-PRC-0004, outlines the systematic verification of electrical systems and devices. This procedure **must** ensure that all electrical devices have been installed in strict accordance with the relevant engineering drawings and specifications.

Beyond installation compliance, the procedure also verifies that all electrical devices are functioning correctly, ensuring operational integrity. A key objective is to confirm that all electrical systems are ready for subsequent project phases, specifically [[water_batching]] and [[commissioning_and_performance_testing]].

The comprehensive [[electrical_completion_package]] for the project will include the various Check Lists utilized during this electrical field checkout process. The installation **must** be in accordance with the documents, and each electrical system item **is inspected and signed-off** in accordance with the attached Electrical System Check Lists.
