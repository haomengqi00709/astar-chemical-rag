# Chloralkali Plant Hazardous Areas

While Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Chloralkali plants are generally considered non-hazardous, specific locations within these facilities are designated as hazardous due to the potential presence of hydrogen gas. These classifications are critical for ensuring appropriate electrical safety measures.

**Specific Hazardous Locations (Class I, Division 1 or 2):**

*   **Hydrogen Sample Points:** These are classified as hazardous areas. They are normally isolated with blind flanges or double valves to minimize risk.
*   **Seal Leg Drains:** These are hazardous areas, typically sealed with water.
*   **Electrolyzer Cells Connecting Flanges:** The flanges connecting electrolyzer cells are hazardous areas (the electrolyzer body itself is not included in this classification).
*   **PRV Valve on Catholyte Tank:** The Pressure Relief Valve (PRV) on top of the catholyte tank is a hazardous area. It is piped outside the building, normally closed, and functions similarly to a rupture disk.
*   **Main Vent Stack:** This is a hazardous area where hydrogen is released continuously. It is piped outside the building. The area around the top of the main vent stack (chloralkali) is classified as Class I, Division 1 for a 1.5m (5’-0”) radius sphere around the vent.
*   **Tail Gas Scrubber Vent:** If an HCl unit is attached, this vent is a hazardous area and is piped outside. The area around the top of the tail gas scrubber vent (HCl plant) is classified as Class I, Division 1 for a 1.5m (5’-0”) radius sphere around the vent.

**Ventilation Requirements:**
*   Good natural building ventilation **must** be ensured at all times the Chlor-Alkali cell room is in operation (i.e., the transformer/rectifier is energized).
*   The roof ridge ventilation and wall louvres **must** be open when the Chlor-Alkali cell room is in operation.
*   If roof vents can be closed, an interlock to the process **should be** provided to prevent the Transformer / Rectifier from operating.
