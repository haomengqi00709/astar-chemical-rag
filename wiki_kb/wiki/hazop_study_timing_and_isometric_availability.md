# HazOp Study Timing and Isometric Availability

The timing of the Hazard and Operability (HazOp) Study is critical and dependent on the completion and availability of key project documentation.

**Prerequisites for HazOp Commencement:**
*   The HazOp Study **shall** only be conducted after the [[line_list_5_lst_0001|Line List (Stage 5)]] has been completed.
*   More than 80% of the isometrics **must** have been completed and be available to the HazOp team when the HazOp is conducted.
