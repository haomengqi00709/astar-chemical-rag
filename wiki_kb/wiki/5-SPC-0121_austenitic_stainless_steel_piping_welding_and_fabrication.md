# Welding and Fabrication Requirements for Austenitic Stainless Steel Piping (5-SPC-0121)

This section details the mandatory requirements for welding procedures, welder qualifications, and general fabrication practices for austenitic stainless steel piping.

**Subcontracting and Supervision:**
*   No subcontracting of welding is permitted without Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' [[5-SPC-0121_austenitic_stainless_steel_piping_specification_overview#agreement-in-writing|written approval]].
*   The fabricator **must** ensure regular and systematic supervision of all work (in-shop and on-site) by competent staff.
*   Fabrication should be undertaken by an experienced pipe fabricating contractor. Fabrication premises should be devoted to this type of work and have adequate supervision and inspection.

**Welding Procedures and Welder Qualifications:**
*   All welding procedures **must** be submitted by the fabricator for review and [[5-SPC-0121_austenitic_stainless_steel_piping_specification_overview#agreement-in-writing|agreement]] by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics prior to commencement of any work.
*   The [[ASME_section_ix_welding_and_brazing_qualifications|ASME IX]] PQR Form 483 **must** form part of the welding procedure package.
*   A change in essential variables as outlined in [[ASME_section_ix_welding_and_brazing_qualifications|ASME Code Section IX]] requires re-submission of procedure(s).
*   All welding **must** be in accordance with [[ASME_section_ix_welding_and_brazing_qualifications|ASME Code]] qualified procedures.
*   Welding procedures **must** be made available for review by the [[5-SPC-0121_austenitic_stainless_steel_piping_specification_overview#inspector|Inspector]] prior to production use.
*   Welding procedures **will** generally satisfy the requirements of [[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping|ASME B31.3]] for Chemical Plant and Petroleum Refinery Piping.
*   Weld procedure(s) **must** state permissible variations in weld preparation dimensions, alignment, etc.
*   All welders employed on Aker Kvaerner Chemetics work **must** be suitably qualified in accordance with relevant codes and standards for every procedure they are to use.
*   The fabricator **must** supply Aker Kvaerner Chemetics with objective evidence of current welder qualifications upon request.
*   All qualified welders **must** be allocated an identification symbol. This identification symbol **must** be stamped on parent material adjacent to the welder's work. Fully marked-up [[weld_map|weld maps]] may be substituted for stamping, subject to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics approval.
*   All welding **must** be carried out by experienced welders in possession of current appropriate certification or who have been qualified directly on the procedure to be used.
*   Repeated testing of a welder who fails a test is not permissible.
*   All welds **must** be traceable to the welder responsible.

**Weld Preparation and Execution:**
*   On completion of work, all weld splatter, heat tinting, tack welds, etc., **must** be removed.
*   All butt and groove welds **must** be full penetration.
*   For one-sided welds, the root pass **must** be TIG with gas shielding on the inside of the piping. Solar flux **will not** be used.
*   Suitable enclosures **must** be provided to keep welds and welders free from draughts, rain, or other inclement weather.
*   Welding **will not** be performed on wet surfaces.
*   The use of backing rings is not permitted.
*   Consumable inserts may only be used with prior [[5-SPC-0121_austenitic_stainless_steel_piping_specification_overview#agreement-in-writing|written approval]] of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Piping Drawings and Fabrication:**
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **will** provide the Fabricator with an [[pipe_isometric|isometric drawing]] for each line. Each drawing **will** show Aker Kvaerner Chemetics [[pipe_specifications|Piping Specification]], fluid service, design pressure/temperature, and [[bill_of_quantity_piping|bill of material]]. Quality control requirements **will** be identified on drawings where necessary.
*   The Fabricator **will** normally provide the required shop spool drawings, which **must** include spool identification numbers and location of field welds. Spool drawings may alternatively be provided by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics as shown on the purchase order.
*   Where field welds are shown on Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' drawings or determined by the Fabricator, at least one field weld on any pipe run **must** have a 3 inch trim allowance added.
*   When mitred bends are shown on Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' drawings, each section **must not** exceed 30 degrees unless specifically approved in writing.
*   Fittings **must not** be formed from pipe by the piping fabricator unless specifically approved by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
*   The fabricator (and site representative where appropriate) **must** inspect work in progress to ensure satisfactory fit-up and adherence to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' approved weld procedures.
