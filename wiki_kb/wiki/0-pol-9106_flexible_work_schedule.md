# Flexible Work Schedule (0-POL-9106)

Aker Kvaerner Chemetics supports an appropriate balance between work and life outside work by allowing a flexible work schedule, as described in policy 0-POL-9106. This policy permits employees to establish a work schedule that allows them to arrive and leave earlier or later than the [[0-pol-9106_normal_work_schedule]].

**Flexible Schedule Requirements:**
Employees **must** work 75 hours per two-week period for a flexible schedule. This 75-hour requirement per two-week period is reduced by 8.25 hours for every local Canadian statutory holiday occurring within that period.

A flexible work schedule **must** allow the employee to fulfill all operational requirements of their position. Additionally, the schedule **must** adhere to the following specific guidelines:
*   It **must** include the core hours of 9:30 a.m. to 3:30 p.m., Monday through Thursday.
*   It **must** include a minimum 30-minute unpaid lunch break.
*   It **must** include no more than 10 hours worked on any single day.

**Request and Approval Process:**
Employees may request a flexible work schedule by sending an e-mail addressed to their supervisor. The supervisor **must** ensure that both departmental and project needs are considered when evaluating the operational requirements of the position.

The supervisor will approve the request by return e-mail if the proposed flexible work schedule meets the guidelines outlined in Section 3 of policy 0-POL-9106. Upon approval, the supervisor **must** copy Finance and Human Resources on the approval e-mail. This ensures proper record-keeping and coordination across relevant departments, aligning with general [[authorization_roles]] for such approvals.
