# HSE Personnel Definitions and Responsibilities

This page defines different categories of personnel within Aker Kvaerner Chemetics work sites and outlines the specific Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) responsibilities associated with each category, particularly concerning orientation and escort duties.

**Personnel Definitions**

*   **Employees:** This category includes permanent employees, contract employees, or temporary employees directly engaged by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
*   **Escorted Personnel (Visitors):** These are visiting personnel who are not direct employees of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
    *   They are typically on site for more than one week.
    *   They are expected to have minimal exposure to construction or operating hazards.
    *   They **will** be escorted at all times by an Aker Kvaerner Chemetics employee.
*   **Unescorted Personnel:** This category includes Subcontractor employees, indirect employees, or visiting personnel who are not direct employees of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
    *   They are typically on site for one week or less.
    *   They are expected to work independently.
    *   For orientation purposes, all Aker Kvaerner Chemetics employees (whether in the office or on a construction site) **will** be considered "Unescorted Personnel" and **will** undergo the same orientation procedures as other unescorted individuals.

**Responsibilities for Escorted Personnel**

**Design Office Escorted Personnel:**
The safety of escorted personnel in the Design Office is the direct responsibility of the primary Aker Kvaerner Chemetics employee receiving the visitor(s).
*   The employee **will** present the visitor with the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics office Health and Safety Card.
*   The employee **will** answer any health and safety questions the visitor may have.
*   Any health and safety questions the employee cannot answer **will** be directed to the [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_document_history|Aker Kvaerner Chemetics HSE Manager]].
*   In the event of an emergency, the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics employee **will** act to safeguard the safety of their guest.
*   The employee **will** ensure the guest follows Aker Kvaerner Chemetics emergency procedures and **will** provide assistance in case of an emergency.

**Construction Site Escorted Personnel:**
The orientation for visitors on a construction site **will** consist of, as a minimum:
*   Providing the guest with necessary Personal Protection Equipment (PPE) and instruction on its proper use.
*   An overview of the site emergency response plan.
*   First aid contact information.
*   Identification of site-specific high-risk hazards.
In the event of an emergency, the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics escort **will** act to safeguard the safety of their guest, ensure the guest follows Aker Kvaerner Chemetics emergency procedures, and provide assistance as required.

**Research Laboratory Escorted Personnel:**
The safety of escorted personnel in the Research Laboratory is the responsibility of the primary Aker Kvaerner Chemetics employee receiving the visitor(s).
*   The employee **will** brief the visitor on the Technology Centre emergency plan and basic safety precautions for the visit.
*   The employee **will** answer any questions the visitor may have.
*   Questions that the employee cannot answer **will** be directed first to the AKCTC supervisor, and then to the [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_document_history|Aker Kvaerner Chemetics HSE Manager]].
*   In the event of an emergency, the AKCTC employee **will** act to safeguard the safety of their guest, ensure the guest follows all emergency procedures, and provide assistance as required.
