# ASME SFA-5.10 Specification for Aluminium and Aluminium Alloy Welding Rods and Bare Electrodes

This page provides an overview of ASME SFA-5.10, a key reference standard for welding Aluminium and Aluminium alloys.

**Overview**
ASME SFA-5.10 is a specification that outlines the requirements for Aluminium and Aluminium alloy welding rods and bare electrodes. It covers classifications, chemical composition, mechanical properties, and other technical requirements for these welding consumables.

**Application**
In the context of the [[9-SPC-0001_aluminium_bus_welding_standard_overview|Aluminium Bus Welding Standard (9-SPC-0001)]], all filler wire/electrodes used **shall** be in accordance with the requirements of AWS A5.10 (which is equivalent to ASME SFA-5.10). This ensures that the welding consumables meet the necessary quality and performance criteria for Aluminium bus systems.
