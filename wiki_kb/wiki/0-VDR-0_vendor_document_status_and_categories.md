# Vendor Document Requirements (0-VDR-0) Document Statuses and Categories

This page outlines the various statuses and categories that may be assigned to vendor documents as specified in **0-VDR-0**, the Vendor Document Requirements document. These classifications are crucial for managing the [[vendor_document_review_process|vendor document review process]] and ensuring proper record-keeping.

**Document Statuses:**
Vendor documents may be assigned the following statuses during their lifecycle:
*   **Required with Proposal:** Documents that **must** be submitted concurrently with the vendor's proposal.
*   **"HOLD" for Review:** Documents that are temporarily on hold, pending further review or information.
*   **Issued as Completed for Record:** Documents that have completed the review and approval process and are officially recorded.

**Document Categories:**
Vendor documents may also be categorized based on their content and purpose:
*   **OEM Manual Content:** Refers to content derived from Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) manuals.
*   **QA Manual Content:** Refers to content related to Quality Assurance (QA) procedures and documentation. These are considered [[quality_record_definitions|quality records]].

Understanding these statuses and categories is essential for all parties involved in the submission and review of vendor documentation. For general administrative information about this document, refer to [[0-VDR-0_vendor_document_requirements_administration]].
