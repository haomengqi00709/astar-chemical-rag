# Harassment and Non-Discrimination Policy Overview

This policy, documented as 0-PRC-9107, is established in accordance with Aker Kvaerner Policy 11.1: [[aker_kvaerner_group_people_policy]]. Its primary purpose is to emphasize the importance of a workplace free from harassment and discrimination, and to recognize the fundamental right of all employees to work in such an environment.

The policy also serves to establish a clear procedure for reporting complaints in the event of harassment or discrimination.
