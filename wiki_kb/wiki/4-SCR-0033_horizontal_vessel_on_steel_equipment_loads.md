# Horizontal Vessel on Steel: Equipment Loads (4-SCR-0033)

This page details the various equipment loads and key dimensional data pertinent to the design of support structures for horizontal vessels, as specified in document **4-SCR-0033**. These loads are critical inputs for structural engineers to ensure the safe and stable operation of the vessel under all anticipated conditions.

**Maximum Mass Specifications:**
The maximum mass values for the horizontal vessel are provided for different operational states, all measured in kilograms (kg):
*   **Empty Mass:** The mass of the vessel when it contains no process fluids or materials. This value is essential for transportation, erection, and initial structural checks.
*   **Flooded (Design) Mass:** The maximum mass of the vessel when completely filled with water or a similar fluid, representing a worst-case scenario for hydrostatic testing or accidental flooding. This is a critical design load.
*   **Operating Mass:** The typical mass of the vessel during normal operation, including the weight of process fluids, internal components, and insulation.

**External Environmental Loads:**
The design also accounts for significant environmental forces, which are crucial for ensuring the vessel's stability against natural phenomena:
*   **Wind Base Shear:** The total horizontal force exerted by wind on the vessel and its support structure, measured in kilonewtons (kN). This force is applied at the base of the structure.
*   **Wind Base Moment:** The overturning moment caused by wind forces acting on the vessel, measured in kilonewton-meters (kN-m). This moment is also calculated at the base.
*   **Seismic Base Shear:** The total horizontal force exerted by seismic activity (earthquakes) on the vessel and its support structure, measured in kilonewtons (kN). This is a critical load for regions prone to seismic events.
*   **Seismic Base Moment:** The overturning moment resulting from seismic forces, measured in kilonewton-meters (kN-m). This moment is also calculated at the base.

**Equipment Elevation:**
*   **Underside of Equipment Elevation:** This critical dimension specifies the elevation of the underside of the equipment, measured in millimeters (mm). This value is fundamental for determining the overall height of the support structure and ensuring proper clearance and integration with other plant components.

All these load and elevation specifications **must** be rigorously applied in the structural design process to comply with the project's safety and engineering standards. For an overview of the support criteria, refer to [[4-SCR-0033_horizontal_vessel_on_steel_support_criteria_overview]].
