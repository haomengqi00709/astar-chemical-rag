# Chiller/Refrigeration Unit Process Design Conditions

The process design conditions for Chiller/Refrigeration Units, as outlined in datasheet [[4-PDS-XXXX-R1_template_chiller_refrigeration_unit_datasheet_administration]], specify critical parameters for equipment sizing and performance. These conditions are detailed under specific "Case Description" and "Design Basis" fields.

Key process design parameters include:
*   **Heat Duty:** Measured in kW.
*   **Operating Range:** Measured in kW.

These parameters are crucial for defining the operational envelope of the chiller/refrigeration unit and ensuring it meets the required cooling capacity under various scenarios. Further details on the specific fluid properties and requirements are available in [[chiller_refrigeration_unit_process_fluid_specifications]].
