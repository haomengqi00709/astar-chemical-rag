# Subcontractor Management Plans (0-SPC-605)

This document, 0-SPC-605, specifies additional requirements for management plans to be developed by construction subcontractors for the Project. It complements general requirements by detailing specific aspects of project management, such as scheduling, resource allocation, and progress reporting.

The Subcontractor is required to develop management plans in accordance with this specification, ensuring comprehensive planning and control over their work activities.
