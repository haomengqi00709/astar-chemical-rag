# Piping Specialty Item Identification Procedure Overview

This procedure, identified as 5-PRC-0006, outlines the requirements for establishing standard identification numbers for piping specialty items. The primary objective is to ensure consistency and clarity across all project documentation and related numbering systems, including [[0-PRC-0002_document_numbering|drawing numbers]].

**Application Scope**
Standard identification numbers **must** be utilized for the development of all related numbering systems. These standard identification numbers **shall** be applied to all projects unless specific client requirements explicitly dictate an alternative numbering scheme.

**Historical Context**
A name change conversion was implemented in 2005. It is important to note that no new revision number was issued for this conversion, and the changes resulting from the 2005 name change conversion have not been explicitly marked within the document.
