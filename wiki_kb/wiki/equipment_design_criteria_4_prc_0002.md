# General Equipment Design Criteria (4-PRC-0002)

Equipment **will** be heavy-duty and industrial standard. It **will** be designed for operation under the full range of operating conditions, including non-steady state, start-up, shutdown, and turndown. Loadings requirements are detailed in [[general_equipment_requirements_specification|4-SPC-0001: General Equipment Requirements]].
