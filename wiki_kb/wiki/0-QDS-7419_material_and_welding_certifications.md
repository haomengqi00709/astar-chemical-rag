# Acid Tower Field Erected Shop Component Material and Welding Certifications (0-QDS-7419)

For the Acid Tower Field Erected Shop Component, strict adherence to material and welding certification requirements is mandatory to ensure the integrity and safety of the final product.

**Material Verification:**
Material Verification is a critical activity that **must** be performed.

**Required Certifications:**
*   **Mill Test Certificates:** These certificates **are required** as FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package). Chemetics surveillance for Mill Test Certificates is **Witness**.
*   **Weld Consumable Certificates:** These certificates **are required** as FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package). Chemetics surveillance for Weld consumable certificates is **Witness**.

These certifications provide documented evidence that the materials and welding consumables used meet the specified standards and project requirements.

For further details on material and welding certification requirements, refer to documents such as [[0-QDS-7411_material_and_welding_certifications]].
