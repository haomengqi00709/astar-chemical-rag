# Dry Tower Materials of Construction

This page details the specified materials of construction (MOC) for the Dry Tower (Tag No. 7506U) and its components, as outlined in calculation document [[1-cal-7506-r1_dry_tower_calculation_document_overview|1-CAL-7506-R1]] and process data sheet [[4-pds-7506-r1_dry_tower_process_data_sheet|4-PDS-7506-R1]]. Proper material selection is crucial for ensuring the chemical resistance, structural integrity, and longevity of the equipment in its operating environment.

**Primary Structural Materials**
*   **Shell Material of Construction:** Carbon Steel
*   **Top Material of Construction:** 316L SS
*   **General Materials Mentioned:** Carbon Steel, Alloy C22
*   The "Other Materials of Construction" field on the data sheet is present but unspecified, indicating that additional materials may be used for specific components not explicitly listed.
*   Sketches in Revision 1 of [[1-cal-7506-r1_dry_tower_calculation_document_overview|1-CAL-7506-R1]] were specifically modified to show more clearly the transition to stainless steel, highlighting a key design consideration for corrosion resistance.

**Internal Lining Materials**
The internal surfaces of the tower are protected by a robust combination of lining materials to withstand the corrosive process fluids:
*   3 mm pecora mastic
*   0.25 mm PTFE (Polytetrafluoroethylene)
*   Acid Brick

**Packing and Support Materials**
The packing elements and their support structures within the tower are specified to be chemically inert and mechanically stable under operating conditions:
*   **Packing Material (all zones):** Ceramic
*   **Packing Support Material:** Ceramic

These material specifications are critical for [[equipment_design_and_specification_4_prc_0003|equipment design]], [[procurement_and_vendor_documentation|procurement]], and ensuring compliance with [[process_design_criteria_1_prc_0001|process design criteria]].
