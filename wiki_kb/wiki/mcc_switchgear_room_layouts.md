# MCC/Switchgear Room Layouts

MCC/Switchgear Room Layout drawings illustrate the general arrangement of electrical components within an electrical room, including dimensions for working space requirements. These layouts are crucial for ensuring safe and compliant equipment placement.

**Content of MCC/Switchgear Room Layout Drawings**
MCC/switchgear room layout drawings **shall** show:
*   General arrangement of the electrical components within an electrical room.
*   Dimensions between the various pieces of electrical equipment to show working space requirements.

The project electrical codes, standards, and regulations **will** be followed for arranging the electrical equipment. Equipment **will** be identified with equipment numbers as shown on the [[final_single_line_diagrams]].
