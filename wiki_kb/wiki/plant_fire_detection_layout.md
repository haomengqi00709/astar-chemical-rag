# Plant Fire Detection Layout Drawings

Plant Fire Detection Layout drawings illustrate the locations and descriptions of all fire detection equipment and associated control panels across the plant. These drawings are crucial for designing a comprehensive fire safety system.

**Content of Fire Detection Layout Drawings**
Plant fire detection layout drawings **shall** show:
*   Locations and descriptions of all fire detection equipment, including smoke detectors, rate of rise temperature detectors, and ultra-violet detectors. This equipment is used for fire detection in chemical and acid plants.
*   Locations and descriptions of the area fire detection control panels. The main master control panel may be located elsewhere by the plant Owner.

Drawings **must** include all process areas, all buildings, all electrical switchyards, and all electrical rooms. The overall main plant fire detection system **must** be referenced.

**Interconnections and Cabling**
Interconnections required between the area control panels and the main master control panel **will** be described, along with the materials required for these interconnections. The battery limits of the fire detection system **must** be indicated. Cabling and installation methods required between the area control panel(s) and the various detection sensors **will** be described.
