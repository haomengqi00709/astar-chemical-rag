# Construction Subcontract Schedule and Progress Management (0-SUB-6)

This page outlines the scheduling, progress measurement, and material delivery requirements for Construction Subcontract Agreement 0-SUB-6.

**Project Key Dates:**
*   **Starting Date:** 5 December 2002.
*   **Completion Date:** 31 March 2003.
*   **Defects Date:** 52 weeks after Completion.
*   **Defects Correction Period:** 2 weeks.

**Planning and Scheduling:**
*   The Subcontractor **must** plan and schedule the works in accordance with [[construction_subcontract_scheduling]] (0-SPC-6021).

**Progress Measurement and Reporting:**
*   The Subcontractor **must** measure progress in accordance with [[construction_subcontract_scheduling]] (0-SPC-6021).
*   The Subcontractor **must** measure progress and update the schedule monthly.

**Material Delivery:**
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **shall** deliver Equipment and Materials to the site at the dates stated in the [[expediting_report_0_rep_6x01]] (0-REP-6X01).

For general information on work schedule and completion, refer to [[work_schedule_and_completion_0_spc_6001]].
