# Carbon Steel Piping Non-Destructive Examination (NDE) (5-SPC-0122)

This section outlines the requirements for Non-Destructive Examination (NDE), specifically radiography, for carbon steel piping fabrication as per document 5-SPC-0122.

**Radiography Requirements**
*   Where radiography is specified or required by code, the minimum standard **shall** be spot radiography (5 percent) as defined in para. 341.1.1(b) of [[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping]].
*   Butt welds in low temperature service (design temperature -20° F or less) **shall** be 20% radiographed.
*   Spot radiography, where required, **shall** cover the welding of each welder and operator.
*   Spot radiography **shall** be performed on the first-off butt welds completed by each welder or operator as early as possible during production.
*   **Mandatory:** The requirement for spot radiography is mandatory for welds performed by M.I.G. or flux cored arc welding.

**Referenced Standard**
*   NDE procedures and acceptance criteria should align with [[ASME_section_v_non_destructive_examination]].
