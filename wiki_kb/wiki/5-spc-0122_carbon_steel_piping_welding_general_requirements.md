# Carbon Steel Piping General Welding Requirements (5-SPC-0122)

This page details the general welding requirements for carbon steel piping fabrication, as per document 5-SPC-0122, covering aspects from subcontracting to welder qualifications and in-progress inspection.

**Fabricator and Subcontracting**
*   Fabrication shall be undertaken by an experienced pipe fabricating Contractor.
*   Fabrication premises **must** be devoted to this type of work and have adequate supervision and inspection.
*   **Mandatory:** No subcontracting of welding is permitted without [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_quality_assurance_and_materials_engineering_group|Aker Kvaerner Chemetics]]' written approval.

**Welding Procedures**
*   **Mandatory:** All welding shall be in accordance with [[ASME_section_ix_welding_and_brazing_qualifications|ASME qualified procedures]].
*   Weld procedures **must** be made available for review by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics prior to production use.
*   Weld procedures **must** state permissible variations in weld preparation dimensions, alignment, etc.
*   For low temperature service (design temperature -20° F or less), weld procedure qualification **shall** include impact testing of weld metal coupons to [[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping]].
*   Where specified by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, the weld procedure **shall** be qualified on the same grade of material as the piping. This same-grade qualification **shall** apply to low temperature service piping, such as liquid chlorine service.
*   **Mandatory:** All Butt/Groove welds in pipe, including branch connections, shall be full penetration.

**Welder Qualifications and Traceability**
*   **Mandatory:** Only experienced welders who are qualified by certification or recent test shall be used.
*   Repeated testing of a welder who fails a test is not permissible.
*   **Mandatory:** All welds shall be traceable to the welder responsible.
*   The Fabricator **must** maintain a list of welders and their qualifications.

**In-Progress Inspection**
*   The Fabricator **shall** systematically inspect work in progress to check fit-up, workmanship, and ensure that correct procedures are being followed.
