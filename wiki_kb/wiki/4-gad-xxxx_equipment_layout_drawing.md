# Equipment Layout Drawings (4-GAD-XXXX)

Equipment Layout Drawings (4-GAD-XXXX) are fundamental engineering documents that depict the physical arrangement of equipment and facilities within a plant. These drawings provide essential spatial information for construction, operations, and maintenance.

**Content Elements**
Equipment Layout Drawings **must** contain the following elements, as applicable:

*   **Building Location, Orientation, Size, and Layout:** Comprehensive details of all buildings, including their position, alignment, dimensions, and internal arrangement.
*   **Equipment General Outline and Location:** Plan and section views showing the general outline and precise location of all major equipment items.
*   **Baylines:** Reference lines used for structural and layout coordination.
*   **Access Platforms and Stairs:** Details of all platforms, walkways, and staircases for personnel access.
*   **Pipe Rack Location, Size, and Layout:** The arrangement, dimensions, and routing of pipe racks.
*   **Ducting Location, Size, and Layout:** The arrangement, dimensions, and routing of all ductwork.
*   **Bunded Areas Location and Size:** The location and dimensions of containment areas for spill prevention.
*   **Trench, Pit, and Sump Location and Size:** The location and dimensions of all trenches, pits, and sumps.
*   **Suspended Floors Elevation, Location, and Layout:** Details of elevated floor structures, including their height, position, and arrangement.
*   **Safety Stations:** The location of emergency showers, eyewash stations, and other safety equipment.
*   **Utility Stations:** The location of utility connections and distribution points.

**Document Preparation and Approval**
The preparation, checking, and approval of Equipment Layout Drawings follow a defined workflow:

*   **Preparation:** Equipment Layout Drawings **must** be prepared by Design personnel.
*   **Checking:** The drawings **must** be checked by Process and Mechanical personnel.
*   **Approval:** Final approval **must** be granted by the Project Engineer.

**Revision History**
