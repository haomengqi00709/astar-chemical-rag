# Vendor Subcontractor and Supplier Management

To ensure the overall quality of the goods supplied, Specification [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment|0-SPC-7001]] mandates that the Vendor implement robust procedures for managing subcontractors and suppliers.

**Subcontractor/Supplier Selection and Evaluation**
The Vendor **shall** have documented procedures for:
*   **Subcontractor/Supplier Selection:** A systematic process for choosing qualified subcontractors and suppliers.
*   **Bid Evaluation:** Procedures for evaluating bids from potential subcontractors and suppliers to ensure they meet technical, commercial, and quality requirements.
*   **Quality Plan Review and Approval:** Procedures for reviewing and approving the Quality Plans of subcontractors and suppliers, ensuring alignment with the overall project quality objectives.

**Notification and Flow-Down Requirements**
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **must** be advised of any sub-contracted items or equipment. This ensures transparency and allows for appropriate oversight. Furthermore, all applicable requirements of Specification [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment|0-SPC-7001]] **shall** be included in the procurement documents issued to subcontractors and suppliers, ensuring that quality standards are consistently applied throughout the supply chain.

**Audits, Surveillance, and Inspections**
The Vendor **shall** perform periodic audits or surveillance of its subcontractor/supplier quality plans. This proactive approach helps to verify ongoing compliance and effectiveness. Additionally, the Vendor **shall** perform source or receipt inspections to verify the conformance of purchased items with specified requirements before they are incorporated into the goods.
