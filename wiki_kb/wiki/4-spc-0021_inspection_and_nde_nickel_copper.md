# Inspection and NDE for Nickel/Copper Welds (4-SPC-0021)

Inspection and Non-Destructive Examination (NDE) for nickel/copper (UNS N04400 / Monel 400) welds under **4-SPC-0021** are critical for ensuring compliance and quality.

**Inspector Access and Authority:**
*   The Inspector **shall** have free access to the Vendor's work at all reasonable times.
*   If work is sublet by approval, the Inspector **shall** also have free access to the Subvendor's work at all reasonable times.
*   Access **shall** also be given to Authorized Representatives of the End-User.
*   The Inspector **shall** be at liberty to inspect the work during fabrication at any stage in manufacture.
*   The Inspector **shall** be at liberty to reject any material or workmanship which does not conform to this Standard or the applicable code.
*   Inspection by [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_company_details|Aker Kvaerner Chemetics]] does not relieve the fabricator or contractor of responsibility for providing materials, components, and workmanship in accordance with the requirements of this Standard, engineering design, and the applicable code.

**Chemical Analysis and Non-Conformance:**
*   The supplier **must** make available to the Inspector copies of chemical certification for each batch of electrodes or wire coils used.
*   If electrodes, wire, etc., cannot be identified to the Inspector's satisfaction, samples **shall** be removed for chemical analysis at the supplier's account.
*   When required by purchase order, a spot check analysis **shall** be performed on production welds of critical equipment (e.g., pressure vessels, heat exchangers, pressure piping, proprietary items) and non-pressure retaining equipment in dangerous or demanding services.
*   The weld(s) to be checked for chemical analysis **shall** be selected at random by the Inspector.
*   Where results show a weldment falls outside approved specifications, the supplier **shall** promptly advise [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_company_details|Aker Kvaerner Chemetics]] in writing of the proposed course of action, including details of any additional testing.
*   All repairs and retests for non-conforming weldments **shall** be to the supplier's account.

**Liquid Penetrant (PT) Examination:**
*   Unless otherwise specified, nozzle attachment weld surfaces and butt weld surfaces (including skirt to shell welds) **shall** be PT examined for 25% of each welder/process used for cracks, porosity, slag inclusions, significant undercutting, or other major defects.
*   For piping, 25% PT examination refers to 100% PT examination of 25% of the production welds.
*   [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_company_details|Aker Kvaerner Chemetics]] **shall** reserve the right to select the location of PT inspection.
*   Unless otherwise specified, all pressure welds between dissimilar metals and tube to tube sheet welds **shall** be 100% PT examined.
*   The PT exam **shall** be carried out in accordance with a written procedure.
*   The limit of acceptability for PT exam **shall** satisfy [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels|ASME Code Section VIII, Division 1, Appendix VIII]], except that linear indications **shall** be considered unacceptable.
*   All excavated areas **shall** be weld built-up flush with the surface of the base material using the previously approved welding procedure.
*   All excavated areas and weld repair surfaces **shall** be 100% PT examined per the above requirements.
*   [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_company_details|Aker Kvaerner Chemetics]] reserves the right to accept or reject any doubtful or questionable PT indications.

**Radiographic Testing (RT) Examination:**
*   Where access is limited (e.g., process side of small piping), spot radiography **shall** be performed in accordance with the relevant code.
*   Items fabricated in accordance with this Standard and the [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels|ASME Code]] **shall** be spot radiographed in accordance with Para. UW-52 (b) of [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels|Section VIII Division 1 of the ASME Code]] where specified on [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_company_details|Aker Kvaerner Chemetics]] Engineering drawings. This paragraph defines minimum extent, standards, evaluation, and retests for spot radiographic examination.
*   For piping fabricated in accordance with [[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping|ASME Codes]] and this Standard, the minimum standard of radiography **shall** be random radiography defined in Para. 341.4.1 (b) of [[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping|ASME B.31.3]]. 20% of all butt welds **shall** be radiographed.
*   For tankage fabricated in accordance with [[API_620_low_pressure_storage_tanks|API Standards 620]] and [[API_650_welded_steel_tanks|650]] and this Standard, the minimum requirements **shall** be spot radiography as required by these standards.
*   Items requiring Radiography **shall** meet the requirements of Paras 10.4.1 or 10.4.2 as appropriate.
*   RT Exam **shall** be carried out in accordance with a written procedure.
*   RT Exam personnel **shall** be qualified to SNTC-1A.
*   Where additional spot radiography is required, as defined in UW-2 Sub-para. (d) (2), costs of this radiography, including all repair costs and re-radiography, **shall** be to the fabricator's account.
*   Junctions between circumferential and longitudinal seams **shall** be considered as preferred locations for spot radiography.

**Helium Mass Spectrometer Testing:**
*   When required by purchase order or referenced drawings, tube/tubesheet joints and tube bundles **shall** be tested using the helium mass spectrometer (reverse probe method).
*   Helium mass spectrometer testing **shall** be performed per [[ASME_section_v_non_destructive_examination|ASME Code Section V, Article 10]].
