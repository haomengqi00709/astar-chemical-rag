# HCl Synthesis Unit Mixed Chlorine Specifications

This page provides the key specifications for the mixed chlorine stream, which is formed by combining the strong and weak chlorine feeds prior to the burner inlet in the [[hcl_synthesis_unit_process_design_basis]]. These parameters are detailed in [[4-PDS-XXXX-R1_template_hcl_synthesis_unit_package_datasheet_administration]] and are essential for the accurate design, sizing, and safe operation of the burner system and associated upstream equipment.

**Mixed Chlorine Stream Parameters:**
The combined chlorine stream entering the burner is characterized by the following critical parameters:
*   **Pressure @ Burner Inlet:** VTA kPa(g)
*   **Molecular Weight (MW):** VTA kg/kg mol

The pressure of the mixed chlorine stream at the burner inlet is a critical design parameter, as it directly influences the flow dynamics, mixing efficiency within the burner, and overall reaction stability. The molecular weight is equally important for accurate mass flow calculations, burner performance modeling, and ensuring the correct stoichiometry for the HCl synthesis reaction. These values are a direct result of the blending of the [[hcl_synthesis_unit_strong_chlorine_specifications]] and [[hcl_synthesis_unit_weak_chlorine_specifications]] streams. Proper control of these parameters is vital for consistent and safe operation of the synthesis unit.

For further details on the individual chlorine streams that contribute to this mixed stream, refer to their respective specification pages.
