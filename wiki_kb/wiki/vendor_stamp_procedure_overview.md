# Vendor Stamp Procedure Overview

This document outlines the procedure for applying vendor stamps to documentation, detailing the four distinct stamp options available. These stamps are crucial for indicating the review status and subsequent actions required for vendor-supplied documents within Chemetics projects.

The four primary vendor stamp options are:
*   [[vendor_stamp_no_revisions_required|No Revisions Required]] (presented on Page 1 of the source document)
*   [[vendor_stamp_revise_as_marked_and_reissue_proceed|Revise as Marked and Reissue (Proceed with Work Shown)]] (presented on Page 2 of the source document)
*   [[vendor_stamp_revise_as_marked_and_reissue_do_not_proceed|Revise as Marked and Reissue (Do Not Proceed with Work Shown)]] (presented on Page 3 of the source document)
*   [[vendor_stamp_reviewed|Reviewed]] (presented on Page 4 of the source document)

Each stamp option carries specific mandatory requirements for its application, including necessary actions, signatures, and dates, all associated with [[chemetics_aker_kvaerner_company_details|CHEMETICS]] standards. Adherence to this procedure ensures proper document control and communication regarding vendor submissions.

The document is officially titled "Vendor Stamp — Procedure".
