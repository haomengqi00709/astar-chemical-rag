# Kvaerner Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Welding Standards (Section 6.5)

Where welding or fabrication is undertaken in the field as part of pipe erection, Kvaerner Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Standards in Section 6.5 **will** apply. These standards provide specific requirements and guidelines for welding procedures, welder qualifications, material preparation, inspection, and quality control to ensure the integrity and safety of all field-welded piping components. Adherence to these standards is mandatory for all subcontractors performing welding or fabrication work on Kvaerner Aker Kvaerner Chemetics projects.
