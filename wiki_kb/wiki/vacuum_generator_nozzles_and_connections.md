# Vacuum Generator Nozzles and Connections

This page details the various nozzles, connections, and instrumentation points on the Vacuum Generator, as specified in Document ID 4-PDS-XXXX-R1.

**Nozzles and Connections**

*   **Recirculation Inlet (Mark A)**
    *   **Quantity:** 1.0 (Note 5)
    *   **Size:** Not specified
    *   **Details:** Refer to Generator Volume Calculations and Generator Nozzles and Circulation Loop sketch (Note 5).

*   **Gas Outlet (Mark B)**
    *   **Quantity:** 1.0
    *   **Sizing Criteria:** To minimize liquid carryover at nozzle corner.
    *   **Size:** Not specified

*   **Liquor Outlet (Mark C)**
    *   **Quantity:** 1.0 (Note 5)
    *   **Size:** Not specified
    *   **Details:** Refer to Generator Volume Calculations and Generator Nozzles and Circulation Loop sketch (Note 5).

*   **Quench Water Nozzle @ Gas Outlet (Mark D1)**
    *   **Size:** 100.0 mm
    *   **Quantity:** 1.0
    *   **Feature:** Allows for insert removal.

*   **Quench Water Nozzle @ Vessel Walls (Mark D2)**
    *   **Size:** 100.0 mm
    *   **Quantity:** 4.0
    *   **Feature:** Allows for insert removal.

*   **Relief Hatch (Mark E)**
    *   **Quantity:** 1.0
    *   **Sizing:** Sized for pressure per Note 7.
    *   **Size:** Not specified

*   **Sight Glass - Gas Phase (Mark F)**
    *   **Size:** 100.0 mm
    *   **Quantity:** 2.0
    *   **Details:** Refer to sightglass used on Degasifier.

*   **Sight Glass - Liquid Level (Mark G)**
    *   **Size:** 100.0 mm
    *   **Quantity:** 3.0
    *   **Details:** Refer to sightglass used on Degasifier.

*   **Air Intake (Mark H)**
    *   **Quantity:** 1.0

*   **Level (Mark L)**

*   **Temperature - Gas (Mark T1)**
    *   **Size:** 40.0 mm
    *   **Quantity:** 1.0
    *   **Type:** Typical for thermowell.

*   **Temperature - Liquid (Mark T2)**
    *   **Size:** 40.0 mm
    *   **Quantity:** 1.0
    *   **Type:** Typical for thermowell.

*   **Manway (Mark M)**

*   **Quench Water Insert @ Gas Outlet (Mark N1)**
    *   **Size:** 25 or 40

*   **Quench Water Insert @ Vessel Walls (Mark N2)**
