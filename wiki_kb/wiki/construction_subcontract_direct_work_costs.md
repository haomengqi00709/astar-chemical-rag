# Construction Subcontract Direct Work Costs

Direct work encompasses all activities and materials that become a permanent part of the project. This includes:
*   The supply of all permanently installed materials.
*   The labour required to fabricate, erect, and install permanently installed materials.

**Direct Labour Costs:**
Direct labour refers specifically to the effort required to fabricate, erect, and install the Equipment and Materials.
*   The Subcontractor **must** enter direct labour costs in the "Direct Costs" worksheet within the [[0-LST-6X11_construction_subcontract_price_list]].
*   The work breakdown for direct labour costs **must** be as entered by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
*   The Subcontractor **must** clearly show the man-hours of direct labour required.
*   To calculate the total cost of direct labour, the Subcontractor **must** use the [[construction_subcontract_crew_rates]] determined per Section 3.1 of 0-SPC-6011.

**Direct Materials Costs:**
Direct materials costs include:
*   The delivered cost of Equipment and Materials supplied by the Subcontractor.
*   The delivered costs of any materials, such as formwork, consumed in the fabrication, erection, and installation of the Equipment and Materials.
*   The Subcontractor **must** enter direct materials costs in the "Direct Costs" worksheet within the [[0-LST-6X11_construction_subcontract_price_list]].
*   The work breakdown for direct materials costs **must** be as entered by [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_obligations_0_spc_2201|Aker Kvaerner Chemetics]].
