# Roles, Responsibilities, and Communication (0-SPC-5002)

This section outlines the specific roles and communication protocols governing the execution of Purchase Orders under document **0-SPC-5002**.

**Key Roles and Responsibilities:**
*   **Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, the Vendor, the Project Buyer, the Inspector, and the Lead Engineer** **must** act in a spirit of mutual trust and cooperation.
*   The *Project Buyer*, *Inspector*, and *Lead Engineer* may delegate any of their actions after notifying the *Vendor*. They may also cancel any delegation after notifying the *Vendor*. A reference to an action includes an action by a delegate.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may replace the *Project Buyer*, *Inspector*, or *Lead Engineer* after notifying the *Vendor* of the replacement's name.

**Communication Requirements:**
*   All instructions, certificates, submissions, proposals, records, acceptances, notifications, and replies required by this Purchase Order **must** be communicated in a form that can be read, copied, and recorded.
*   All written communication **must** be in English.
*   Each communication from the *Vendor* to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or any of its representatives **must** be copied to the *Project Buyer*.
*   If this Purchase Order requires a representative of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or the *Vendor* to reply to a communication, they **must** reply within one week.

**Acceptance of Submissions:**
*   The *Project Buyer* may withhold acceptance of a submission by the *Vendor*. Withholding acceptance for a reason stated in this Purchase Order is not considered a [[0-SPC-5002_compensation_events|compensation event]].
*   The acceptance of a communication from the *Vendor* or of their work by a representative of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics does not alter the *Vendor’s* responsibility to *Provide the Goods and Services* or their liability for their design.

**Early Warning System:**
*   The *Vendor* and the *Project Buyer* **must** give an early warning by notifying the other as soon as either becomes aware of any matter which could:
    *   Increase the total of the *Prices*.
    *   Delay *Delivery*.
    *   Impair the quality of the *Goods and Services*.
*   The *Project Buyer* and the *Vendor* **must** conduct an early warning conference. The *Lead Engineer* may participate, and other people may participate by agreement.
*   At an early warning meeting, participants **must** cooperate in:
    *   Making and considering proposals to avoid or reduce the effect of the notified matter.
    *   Seeking solutions that benefit all affected parties.
    *   Deciding upon actions and who will take them in accordance with this Purchase Order.
*   The *Project Buyer* records the proposals considered and decisions taken in an early warning conference and provides a copy to the *Vendor*.

**Ambiguity and Inconsistency Resolution:**
*   The *Project Buyer* or the *Vendor* **must** notify the other as soon as either becomes aware of an ambiguity or inconsistency in or between the documents that are part of the Purchase Order.
*   The *Project Buyer* will then issue an instruction resolving the ambiguity or inconsistency.
