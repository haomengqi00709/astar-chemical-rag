This document, Specification 4-SPC-0041, is a new document that consolidates content from several existing specifications.

The content of 4-SPC-0041 has been extracted from the following existing specifications:
*   ES44\F06: Minimum Manufacturing & Quality Requirements of Pressure Vessels & Heat Exchangers
