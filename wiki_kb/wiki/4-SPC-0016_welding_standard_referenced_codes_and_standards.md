# Welding Standard Referenced Codes and Standards

This standard for austenitic stainless clad steel welded fabrications references several key industry codes and specifications to ensure quality and compliance. Fabricators **must** adhere to the latest editions of these documents as ruling at the date of contract.

**Materials and Welding Qualifications**
*   [[ASME_bpvc_section_ii_materials]]: ASME Boiler & Pressure Vessel Codes Section II Materials.
*   [[ASME_section_ix_welding_and_brazing_qualifications]]: ASME Boiler & Pressure Vessel Codes Section IX - Welding and Brazing Qualifications.
*   [[ASME_sfa_5_1_carbon_steel_electrodes_for_shielded_metal_arc_welding]]: ASME SFA-5.1 - Carbon Steel Electrodes for Shielded Metal Arc Welding.
*   [[ASME_sfa_5_4_welding_electrodes]]: ASME SFA-5.4 - Specification for Corrosion Resisting Chromium-Nickel Steel Covered Electrodes.
*   [[ASME_sfa_5_9_welding_electrodes_and_rods]]: ASME SFA-5.9 - Specification for Corrosion Resisting Chromium & Chromium-Nickel Steel Bare and Composite Metal Cored and Stranded Arc-Welding Electrodes and Welding Rods.
*   [[ASME_sfa_5_12_tungsten_arc_welding_electrodes]]: ASME SFA-5.12 - Specification for Tungsten Arc-Welding Electrodes.
*   ASME SFA-5.17 - Carbon Steel Electrodes and Fluxes for submerged Arc Welding.
*   ASME SFA-5.18 - Carbon Steel Filler Metals for Gas Shielded Arc Welding.
*   ASME SFA-5.20 - Carbon Steel Electrodes for Flux Cored Arc Welding.

**Non-Destructive Examination and Pressure Vessels**
*   [[ASME_section_v_non_destructive_examination]]: ASME Boiler & Pressure Vessel Codes Section V - Non-destructive Examination.
*   [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels]]: ASME Boiler & Pressure Vessel Codes Section VIII Div. 1 - Pressure Vessels.

**Welding Terminology**
*   [[AWS_a3_0_standard_welding_terms_and_definitions]]: AWS A3.0 - Standard Welding Terms & Definitions.

**Cleaning and Passivation**
*   [[ASTM_a380_cleaning_passivation_stainless_steel]]: ASTM A380 "Cleaning & Descaling Stainless Steel Parts, Equipment & Systems" is referenced for passivation procedures.

These referenced documents form the foundational requirements for all fabrication and welding activities under this standard.
