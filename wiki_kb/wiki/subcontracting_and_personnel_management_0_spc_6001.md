# Subcontracting and Personnel Management (0-SPC-6001)

This page outlines the requirements for subcontracting and personnel management under construction subcontract 0-SPC-6001.

**Sub-subcontracting**
If the Subcontractor further subcontracts work, the Subcontractor remains fully responsible for [[standard_conditions_for_construction_subcontract_0_spc_6001#To Provide the Works|Providing the Works]] as if the work had not been further subcontracted. This subcontract applies as if a sub-subcontractor’s employees and Construction Equipment were those of the Subcontractor.

**Acceptance of Sub-subcontractors and Conditions**
The Subcontractor **shall** submit the name of each proposed sub-subcontractor to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for acceptance. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may reject a proposed sub-subcontractor if their appointment would prevent the Subcontractor from Providing the Works.

The Subcontractor **shall** also submit the proposed conditions of contract for each sub-subcontract to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for acceptance, unless submission is explicitly agreed not to be required. The Subcontractor **shall not** appoint a sub-subcontractor on proposed conditions until those conditions are accepted. Reasons for not accepting sub-subcontract conditions include:
*   They would prevent the Subcontractor from Providing the Works.
*   They do not include a statement that the parties to the sub-subcontract **shall** act in a spirit of mutual trust and co-operation.

**Employee Management**
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may, after stating reasons, instruct the Subcontractor to remove an employee. If such an instruction is given, the Subcontractor **shall** arrange for that employee to immediately have no further connection with the work included in this subcontract.

**Access for Inspection**
The Subcontractor **shall** provide access for Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, and for others notified by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, to work being done for this subcontract. This access also extends to stored [[standard_conditions_for_construction_subcontract_0_spc_6001#Equipment and Materials|Equipment and Materials]].
