# General Electrical Schematic and Wiring Diagrams

This page provides an overview of the general requirements for electrical schematic and wiring diagrams, which are fundamental for understanding electrical control logic and interconnections.

**General Requirements**
Schematic drawings **shall** be numbered according to 9-LST-0001, List Electrical Document Numbering Standard. Schematics **will** be drawn in the format requested by the Owner or the engineering firm. All symbols used to represent electrical components **shall** have a clear description.
