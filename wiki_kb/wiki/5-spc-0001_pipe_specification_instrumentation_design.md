# Pipe Specification (5-SPC-0001) - Instrumentation Design

The [[5-spc-0001_pipe_specification]] document (5-SPC-0001) defines the requirements for piping materials and design, which is directly referenced and utilized in the design of instrumentation.

**Application in Instrumentation Design:**
*   Pipe specification and size for instruments will be as per the [[5-prc-0004_piping_line_list_procedure_overview]] and [[p_id_development_and_approval_1_prc_0003_instrumentation]]. This information **is not** to be duplicated in the [[8-lst-01xx_instrument_process_data_lists]].
*   The project pipe specifications **shall** be provided to the Field Instrument Engineer (FIE). In the event that the pipe specifications are revised, they **are to be immediately reissued** to the FIE.
*   The FIE will use the pipe specifications and the [[5-lst-0011_standard_ansi_piping_connections_for_instrumentation]] in the design of the field instruments.
*   Process connection sizes for [[8-dst-xxxx_instrument_specification_sheets]] will be obtained from the Piping Specifications and the [[5-lst-0011_standard_ansi_piping_connections_for_instrumentation]] spreadsheet.
