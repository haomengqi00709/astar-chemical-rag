# Electrical Bid Evaluation

This page details the process for evaluating bids received for electrical equipment, including technical and financial assessments, and the roles involved.

**Bid Evaluation Process**
Bid evaluations **will** be done in accordance with Table 2. Procedure 0-PRC-5003, "Bid Evaluation and Approval", **must** be followed for completing the bid evaluation forms.

**Financial Evaluation**
The financial evaluation **will** be done in accordance with Table 2.

**Technical Bid Tabulation**
The technical bid tabulation **will** be prepared in accordance with Table 2. The form "Technical Bid Tabulation", 0-TAB-5XXX-2, **must** be completed.

**Specifics for Transformer/Rectifier (T/R) & CPU**
Quotations for the T/R and CPU **will** be forwarded to DDE for technical review and compliance with specifications and data sheets. DDE **will** discuss technical issues with bidders for resolution and prepare bid evaluation documents in preparation for purchasing. DDE **will** identify important technical items from specifications and data sheets to be added to the Technical Bid Tabulation prior to receiving quotes. Each bidder **must** supply information for each technical item on the Technical Bid Tabulation. The Technical Bid Tabulation **will** be very important for determining the successful bidder.

**General Bid Evaluation (excluding T/R & CPU)**
Bid evaluations **will** be prepared by the DDE and forwarded to the relevant party for completion of forms where necessary.

**Exclusions**
Section 3.14 (Bid Evaluation) specifically excludes T/R & CPU.
