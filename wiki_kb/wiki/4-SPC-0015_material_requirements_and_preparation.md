# Material Requirements and Preparation (4-SPC-0015)

Material selection, handling, and preparation for fabrication under Standard 4-SPC-0015 **must** comply with the following requirements to ensure the integrity and corrosion resistance of nickel-base alloy components.

**Material Specifications**
*   Plate, sheet, pipe, and tube **must** be purchased in the stabilized condition.
*   Plate and sheet **must** conform to [[ASME_bpvc_section_ii_materials]] SB424.
*   Pipe **must** conform to [[ASME_bpvc_section_ii_materials]] SB423.
*   Tube **must** conform to [[ASME_bpvc_section_ii_materials]] SB163.
*   Plate and sheet **must** be supplied in the hot rolled, annealed, and descaled condition for maximum corrosion resistance, unless specified otherwise on the purchase order.
*   Pipe and tube **must** be seamless grade and supplied in the annealed and pickled condition.

**Cleaning and Marking**
*   The surface and edges of material to be heated **must** be cleaned free from all traces of foreign matter (e.g., marking paint, oil, etc.).
*   Cleaning methods include grinding, wire brushing with a stainless steel brush, and/or non-chlorinated solvent cleaning.
*   Cleaning **must** be done immediately prior to the application of heat.
*   On completion of work, items **must** be cleaned internally and externally. All fabrication markings, weld spatter, etc., **must** be removed prior to shipment.
*   Sulphur, phosphorous, or lead bearing paints or crayon **must not** be used for marking or identification purposes.
*   Fusion faces and adjacent metal (internal and external) **will be free** from moisture, scale, paint, grease, or other foreign matter immediately prior to welding.

**Passivation**
*   Passivation, where required by the Purchase Order or referenced documents, **must** be carried out in accordance with a written procedure.
*   The passivation procedure **must** be approved by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics prior to its use. Refer to [[ASTM_a380_cleaning_passivation_stainless_steel]] for general guidelines.

**Cutting and Edge Preparation**
*   Material thicker than 0.1" **must** be bevelled to form a 'V', 'U', or 'J' groove.
*   Material **must** be cut to size by Machining or Shearing.
    *   For shearing, materials over 3/16" thick **must** have extra material left on the sheared edge to allow machining, grinding, etc., to remove defects.
    *   Material over 1" thick **must not** be sheared.
*   Material **must** also be cut to size by Thermal Cutting, preferably by the Plasma Arc Process.
    *   Welding edges from the Plasma Arc Process should be lightly ground to bright metal where necessary.
    *   For other thermal cutting processes, a minimum of 1/16" of metal **must** be removed by mechanical means after cutting. This removal ensures all slag, scale, other irregularities, contaminants, and thermal effects are removed.
*   For grinding, Silicon carbide wheels, dedicated to nickel-base alloys, **must** be used.

**Forming**
*   Materials **must** be cold formed as far as practical.
*   When hot forming is necessary, the procedure **must** be agreed to in writing by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics prior to commencement of work.

These detailed requirements ensure that the base materials are correctly prepared for the welding processes, contributing to the overall quality and longevity of the fabricated components.
