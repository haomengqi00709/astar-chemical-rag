# Construction Subcontract Supply and Erection Scope Checklist (0-LST-6X90-1)

The Construction Subcontract Supply and Erection Scope Checklist (0-LST-6X90-1) is a crucial document for defining the division of responsibilities within a construction subcontract. This checklist is directly referenced by Section 2.1 of the [[construction_subcontract_agreement_procedure_0_prc_6002|Construction Subcontract Agreement (0-PRC-6002)]] and is prepared as part of the [[construction_subcontract_scope_preparation_0_prc_6091|Construction Subcontract Scope Preparation (0-PRC-6091)]] procedure.

### Purpose and Content

The primary purpose of 0-LST-6X90-1 is to itemize and clearly delineate:

*   Which party is responsible for the supply of specific equipment and materials.
*   What erection and construction work will be undertaken by the Subcontractor.
*   What erection and construction work will be performed by other parties (e.g., Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, Owner, or other subcontractors).

### Preparation and Approval Process

1.  **Preparation**: The checklist **must** be prepared by the Construction department. It uses [[supply_and_erection_scope_checklist|0-LST-2002: Supply and Erection Scope Checklist]] as its foundational basis.
2.  **Numbering**: The document **must** be numbered in accordance with [[document_numbering_and_name_change_conventions|0-PRC-0002: Document Numbering]]. The "X" in the document number (0-LST-6X90-1) **shall** be replaced by the same digit that corresponds to the specific [[construction_subcontract_agreement_procedure_0_prc_6002|Subcontract Agreement (0-PRC-6002)]] it supports.
3.  **Publication**: 0-LST-6X90-1 **must** be published with the status “Approved for Construction”. The publication process **shall** follow the guidelines outlined in [[publishing_project_documents|0-PRC-0005: Publishing of Project Documents]].
4.  **Checking**: The checklist **must** be checked by the responsible Disciplines to ensure accuracy and completeness.
5.  **Approval**: Final approval for 0-LST-6X90-1 **must** be granted by the Project Engineer.
