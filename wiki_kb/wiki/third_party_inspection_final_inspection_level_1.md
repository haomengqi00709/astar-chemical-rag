# Third Party Inspection - Final Inspection (Level 1)

When Final Inspection (Inspection Level 1) is a requirement for equipment, the Third Party Inspection Agency has specific responsibilities to ensure all conditions are met before release.

The Agency **must** arrange for the vendor to provide advance notification of a date when all equipment will be available for inspection. Concurrently, the Agency **must** ensure the vendor notifies them in advance of a date when all [[quality_data_sheets_qds]] (QDS) inspection requirements can be fully met.

QDS inspection requirements encompass a thorough review of all relevant Quality Assurance (QA) documentation. Additionally, where specified in the QDS, these requirements include witnessing [[pressure_and_leak_testing]], such as hydrostatic testing, to verify the integrity of the equipment.

During the visit for Final Inspection (Inspection Level 1), a brief review of the vendor’s relevant manufacturing and inspection processes **should** be conducted. This review helps confirm adherence to quality standards. However, this brief process review is not mandatory if it has already been performed within the past year.

Any serious problems or deviations discovered during the Final Inspection **should** be reported to AKC immediately. For detailed reporting protocols and other documentation requirements related to Final Inspection, the Agency **shall** refer to [[0-SPC-7003_specification_overview]].

The Agency **must** arrange for the vendor to provide advance notification of a date when all equipment will be available and when all Quality Data Sheet (QDS) inspection requirements can be met. QDS inspection requirements include review of all QA documentation and, where specified, witnessing hydrostatic testing. A brief review of vendor’s relevant manufacturing and inspection processes should be carried out during the visit for Final Inspection (Inspection Level 1). This brief review of vendor’s processes is not required if it has already been done within the past year.
