# Shop Equipment Lockout Rules

This page outlines specific lockout rules applicable to shop equipment, as part of the broader [[0-PRC-8504-RA_lockout_and_tagging|Lockout and Tagout Procedure]].

*   The qualified operator of shop equipment **must** lock out his equipment to change tools, chucks, blades, and perform similar tasks.
*   A power disconnect switch **must** be provided for shop equipment for lockout purposes at or near the equipment, unless the equipment can be unplugged.
*   Push-button or butterfly controls **will not** be used for purposes of lockout on shop equipment.
*   A lock without a tag **may** be used for lockout on shop equipment.
*   Shop equipment lockout rules **do not apply** to maintenance or repair work done by other than the [[lockout_tagout_definitions#authorized_individual|authorized operator]].
*   A master series of locks to be used specifically for lockout **may** be provided to each craft that requires them.
*   Only individual keyed locks **will** be used for lockout.
*   The key **will** remain in the possession of the person placing the lock.
