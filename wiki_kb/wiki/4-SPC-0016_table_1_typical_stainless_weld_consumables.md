# 4-SPC-0016 Table 1: Typical Stainless Weld Consumables

This page describes the content of Table 1, which is referenced as Appendix A in the 4-SPC-0016 standard.

Table 1 provides a list of typical stainless weld consumables recommended for use in the fabrication of austenitic stainless clad steel. This table serves as a guide for Fabricators in selecting appropriate electrodes and filler materials for various welding processes and applications within the scope of the standard.

Refer to [[4-SPC-0016_welding_consumables_and_chemical_analysis]] for general requirements regarding welding consumables.
