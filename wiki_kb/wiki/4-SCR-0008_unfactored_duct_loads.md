# Skirt Supported Vessel on Steel: Unfactored Duct Loads (4-SCR-0008)

Document **4-SCR-0008** outlines the unfactored loads specifically for ducts associated with the skirt-supported vessel on steel. These loads are essential for the structural design of ducting systems and their supports.

The unfactored duct loads include:
*   **Vertical:** The vertical force acting on the duct, measured in kilonewtons (kN). This typically accounts for the weight of the duct, its contents, and any insulation or external attachments.
*   **Shear:** The transverse force acting perpendicular to the duct's axis, measured in kilonewtons (kN). This can arise from wind, seismic activity, or differential movement.
*   **Moment:** The rotational force (bending moment) acting on the duct, measured in kilonewton-meters (kN-m). Moments are critical for designing connections and supports to prevent excessive bending stresses.

These unfactored loads serve as a basis for further engineering calculations, where appropriate load factors and combinations are applied according to relevant design codes. For a broader understanding of duct loading, consult [[unfactored_duct_loads]].
