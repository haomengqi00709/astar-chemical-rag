# Stainless Steel Tubing Tier 2 Pressure and Leak Testing

All Stainless Steel Tubing Tier 2 **shall** undergo rigorous pressure and leak testing as specified below. These tests are critical for ensuring the integrity and safety of the tubing.

**Hydrostatic Testing:**
*   Hydrostatic testing is required as FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package).
*   Chemetics **shall** Witness and Hold Hydrostatic testing.
*   Hydrostatic testing **shall** be in accordance with [ASTM A1016].

**Air Under Water Testing:**
*   Air Under Water testing is required as FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package).
*   Chemetics **shall** Witness and Hold Air Under Water testing.
*   Air Under Water testing **shall** be in accordance with [ASTM A1016].

All required documentation for these tests **shall** be provided as [[0-QDS-7427-2_quality_record_definitions|Quality Records]]. These tests complement the [[0-QDS-7427-2_non_destructive_examination_requirements]] to ensure comprehensive quality assurance.
