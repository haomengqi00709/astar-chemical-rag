# Group Safety Tagout Procedure

[[lockout_tagout_definitions#group_safety_tagout|Group Safety Tagout]] is acceptable when work by more than one [[lockout_tagout_definitions#authorized_individual|authorized individual]] on a given equipment/process can safely and effectively be accomplished by a single [[safety_tagout_order_and_documentation|safety tagout order]].

**Procedure for Group Safety Tagout**
*   For [[group_safety_tagout_procedure|Group Safety Tagout]], the [[lockout_tagout_roles_and_responsibilities#commissioning_hse_manager|Commissioning HSE Manager]] **will** Lock Out Specific [[lockout_tagout_definitions#energy_isolating_device|energy isolating devices]].
*   The [[lockout_tagout_roles_and_responsibilities#commissioning_hse_manager|Commissioning HSE Manager]] **will** place his keys into a Lock Box with a clear lid.
*   Each [[lockout_tagout_definitions#affected_employee|affected employee]] **will** then place a lock on the box and keep their key.
*   A list **will** be kept of each individual participating in “Group Lock Out”.

**Restoration**
Restoration of equipment/process after [[group_safety_tagout_procedure|Group Safety Tagout]] **will** be as outlined in the [[lockout_tagout_procedure_steps#restoration_of_equipment_process|Restoration of Equipment/Process]] section of the main procedure.
