# Travel Booking Process and Agency Guidelines

All company travel bookings are facilitated through FCM Travel, which handles travel routes, pricing, and itinerary management.

**FCM Travel Contact Information:**
*   **Telephone:** (604) 718-8813
*   **Hours:** 9 AM to 5 PM
*   **Contact Person:** Ask for Jolene Johnson at FCM Travel.

**After-Hours Service:**
For changes or inquiries when traveling, an after-hours service is available 24 hours a day:
*   **Toll-Free (North America):** 1-866-245-6871
*   **Collect Call (International):** 1-303-876-4190
These numbers, along with toll-free lines for various countries, will be noted on your travel itinerary. If you do not keep your itinerary with you, **make a note of the after-hours numbers**.

**Invoice and Expense Reporting:**
FCM attaches an invoice to all travel itineraries. A copy of this invoice **should** be attached to your [[0-PRC-9003_expense_report_submission_and_documentation|expense report]]. This invoice does not need to be forwarded to anyone else, as Accounting will automatically receive a copy.

**Ticket Delivery:**
*   **Paper Tickets:** If paper tickets are expected, Reception will contact your travel coordinator to arrange delivery.
*   **Urgent Tickets:** If tickets are required urgently, please inform Reception.

**Office Contact Information:**
Travelers **should** leave contact numbers for while they are away with someone in the office (e.g., an administrative assistant or Manager).

For specific details on "Around the World" tickets, refer to [[around_the_world_travel_tickets]]. The initial process for requesting travel is outlined in [[travel_authorization_process_0_frm_9002]].

**After-Hours Service Details**
*   For changes or inquiries when you are away, the after-hours number is 1-866-245-6871.
*   Alternatively, you can call 1-303-876-4190 collect for after-hours service.
*   This after-hours number is a 24-hour call service.
*   The after-hours number will be noted on your itinerary. If you do not keep your itinerary with you, make a note of these numbers separately.

**Toll-Free Lines**
*   FCM will note toll-free lines for various countries on your itinerary.
*   A list of these toll-free lines for various countries is also attached to your itinerary.

**Itinerary & Invoice Management**
*   FCM attaches an invoice to all travel itineraries.
*   A copy of the invoice attached to the itinerary should be attached to your expense report.
*   The invoice attached to the itinerary does not need to be forwarded to anyone else.
*   Accounting will automatically receive a copy of the invoice.

**Ticket Delivery**
*   If you are expecting paper tickets, Reception will contact your travel coordinator to arrange for their delivery.
*   If you are expecting tickets urgently, please let Reception know so they can prioritize the delivery.
