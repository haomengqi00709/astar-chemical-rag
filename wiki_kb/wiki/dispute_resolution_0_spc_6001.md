# Dispute Resolution (0-SPC-6001)

This page outlines the procedures for resolving disputes under construction subcontract 0-SPC-6001.

**Referral to Tribunal**
Any dispute arising under or in connection with this subcontract that the Parties have not resolved **shall** be referred to the tribunal.

**Arbitration for Non-Head Contract Disputes**
Unless otherwise stated in the [[standard_conditions_for_construction_subcontract_0_spc_6001#Subcontract Data|Subcontract Data]], if the dispute does not relate to the [[standard_conditions_for_construction_subcontract_0_spc_6001#Head Contract|Head Contract]], the tribunal **shall** be arbitration by a single Arbitrator appointed by the British Columbia International Arbitration Centre under its Rules.

**Continuation of Work During Dispute**
Unless and until the Arbitrator has given a decision on the dispute, the Parties **shall** proceed as if the matter was not disputed.

**Jurisdiction for Head Contract Related Disputes**
If a dispute arises between the Subcontractor and Aker Kvaerner Chemetics that relates to the Head Contract, the Subcontractor irrevocably and unconditionally submits to the non-exclusive jurisdiction of the tribunal stated in the Subcontract Data.

Similarly, if a dispute arises between Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the [[standard_conditions_for_construction_subcontract_0_spc_6001#Owner|Owner]] that relates to the works, the Subcontractor irrevocably and unconditionally submits to the non-exclusive jurisdiction of the tribunal stated in the Subcontract Data.
