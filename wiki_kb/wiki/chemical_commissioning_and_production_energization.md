# Chemical Commissioning and Production Energization

Following successful [[water_and_air_trials_and_safety_protocols]], the plant proceeds to chemical commissioning and subsequent production energization, marking a critical transition in the project lifecycle.

**Chemical Commissioning**
At the commencement of chemical commissioning, responsibility for safety management of the plant is formally transferred to the Owner. All of the Owner's safety regulations **will** be in force during this phase.

Raw materials and treatment chemicals are introduced into the plant in a sequential and systematic manner. During chemical commissioning, as many process unit operations as possible are fully or partially operated, troubleshooted, and their controls tuned to optimize performance.

Upon completion of chemical commissioning, agreement **must** be reached between the Owner and the Commissioning Manager that the plant is complete and ready for energization and commencing production. This agreement **will** be formally made and recorded in accordance with the protocols set out in the Contract.

**Production Energization**
For production energization, DC Power **will** be applied to the electrolysers. This application **will** immediately initiate the production of chlorine gas, hydrogen gas, and sodium hydroxide.

Initially, the plant **will** be operated at minimum allowable loads to continue operation, troubleshooting, and tuning of controls in downstream process unit operations. DC Power **will** then be applied to the electrolysers in increasing increments. This phased approach to production energization demonstrates the operation and stability of all process unit operations across all rates, up to full production capacity.
