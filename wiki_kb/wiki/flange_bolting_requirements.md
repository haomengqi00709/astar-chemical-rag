# Flange Bolting Requirements

This page outlines the mandatory requirements for [[flange_bolting_requirements|flange bolting]], including adequate tightening, prevention of overtightening (to avoid gasket damage), and ensuring at least three rounds of pipe thread protrude past the nut. These requirements apply to various equipment types, including [[vessel_and_tank_mechanical_checkout|vessels and tanks]], [[heat_exchanger_mechanical_checkout|heat exchangers]], [[packaged_equipment_mechanical_checkout|packaged equipment]], [[separation_equipment_mechanical_checkout|separation equipment]], and [[ejector_mechanical_checkout|ejectors]].
