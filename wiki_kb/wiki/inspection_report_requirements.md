# Third Party Inspection Report Requirements

Following the completion of any inspection activities, the Inspection Agency **shall** issue an Inspection Report after each inspection visit. These reports are crucial for documenting findings and progress.

**Issuance and Timing**
Inspection Reports, along with [[non_conformance_report_process_third_party]] and Shop Survey reports, **shall** be issued within two days of the activity.

**Format and Language**
*   Inspection Reports **shall** be presented on the Inspection Agency’s official letterhead.
*   A formal inspection report **shall** be issued in the English language.
*   Reports **shall** utilize the Agency’s normal style or format.
*   Accepted digital formats for inspection reports include: Microsoft Word (.doc), Microsoft Excel (.xls), and Adobe Acrobat (.pdf).
*   When digital photographs are included as part of the report, accepted formats are: “.jpg”, “.gif”, or “.tif”.

**Distribution**
All Inspection Reports **shall** be sent by e-mail to ChemeticsQA@akerkvaerner.com.

**Mandatory Content**
Each Inspection Report **must** include the following details:
*   The Inspector’s assessment of current delivery and inspection findings.
*   Reference to the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Purchase Order (PO) Number (both on the Agency's records and the Equipment Vendor's records).
*   The name of the contact person at the Equipment Vendor.
*   The date of the inspection visit.
*   A clear description of the equipment(s) inspected.
*   The equipment tag number(s).
*   The Inspector’s assessment of the fabrication status, including whether it aligns with the current schedule.
*   Details of any delays encountered, specifically noting any delays claimed by the vendor to be due to slow approvals by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

For related documentation, refer to [[quality_data_sheet_management]] and [[inspection_release_process_third_party]].
