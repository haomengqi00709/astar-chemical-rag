# Spray Tower Working Volume Criteria (4-PDS-XXXX-R1)

The Template Spray Tower Process Data Sheet (document ID 4-PDS-XXXX-R1) specifies important **Working Volume Criteria** for the associated pump tank. These criteria define the operational liquid levels and capacities within the tank, which are essential for stable and efficient pump operation and overall system performance.

The working volume criteria include:

*   **Pump Suction to invert of overflow**: This criterion defines the minimum liquid level required to ensure continuous pump suction, preventing cavitation, up to the point where liquid would begin to overflow.
*   **Bottom tank to invert of overflow**: This criterion specifies the total liquid volume from the very bottom of the tank up to the invert (lowest point) of the overflow connection, indicating the maximum safe operating volume.
*   **Other**: An "Other" option is provided to accommodate any additional or specific working volume criteria that may be unique to a particular project or design.

These criteria are vital for designing the pump tank and its control system to maintain appropriate liquid levels during various operating conditions, preventing both dry running of pumps and tank overfilling.
