# Discipline Reporting Requirements

As a critical input to the overall [[client_project_progress_reporting]], each discipline **shall** prepare a monthly "Discipline Report." These reports are essential for providing detailed, discipline-specific updates on project activities and concerns.

Each Discipline Report **shall** cover the following key areas:
*   **Achievements this month:** A summary of significant accomplishments and milestones reached within the reporting period.
*   **Planned activities for next month:** An outline of the work scheduled for the upcoming month.
*   **Areas of Concern:** Identification of any issues, items on hold, information required from other parties, manpower constraints, or other potential risks impacting the discipline's progress.

All items within the Discipline Reports **shall** be presented in bullet point form for clarity and conciseness.

Submission of these reports is mandatory and **shall** be completed prior to the preparation of the client's monthly progress report. Discipline Reports **shall** be submitted via email to the Project Manager, Project Engineer, and Project Controls personnel. This ensures that all necessary information is consolidated in a timely manner for both internal review and external client communication, contributing to accurate and comprehensive project oversight.
