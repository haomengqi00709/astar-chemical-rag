# Procurement Plan

The Procurement Plan outlines the strategy and procedures for acquiring all necessary goods and services for a project. This document is included as an attachment to the [[0-rep-2001_monthly_progress_report_format|Monthly Progress Report]], providing a detailed roadmap for [[procurement_and_logistics_strategy|procurement activities]].
