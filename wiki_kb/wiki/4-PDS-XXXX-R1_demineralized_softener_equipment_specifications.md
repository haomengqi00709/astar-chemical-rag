# Demineralized-Softener Equipment Specifications

This page details the general equipment specifications for demineralized and softener systems, as outlined in Document ID 4-PDS-XXXX-R1. These parameters define the physical configuration, operational characteristics, and critical design limits of the water treatment units.

**General Equipment Data**
*   **Number of Columns:** The system is designed with **2.0** columns. This indicates a multi-column configuration, often used for continuous operation (one in service, one regenerating or on standby).
*   **Mode of Operation:** The specific operational sequence and control logic for the columns. For detailed information on the control philosophy, refer to [[4-PDS-XXXX-R1_demineralized_softener_notes_and_vendor_requirements|Note 1]] on the related notes page, which directs to equipment specification 4-DST-XXXX.
*   **Service Time Between Regenerations:** The expected operational duration of a column before regeneration is required, specified in hours (h). The placeholder "XX Hours" indicates that a specific value will be provided for a given project.
*   **Cation Resin Type:** The type of cation exchange resin to be used. The placeholder "VTA" indicates that this will be "Vendor To Advise" or a specific type will be specified per project. Cation resins are responsible for removing positively charged ions like calcium, magnesium, and sodium.
*   **Anion Resin Type:** The type of anion exchange resin to be used. The placeholder "VTA" indicates "Vendor To Advise" or a specific type will be specified. Anion resins remove negatively charged ions such as sulphate, chloride, and bicarbonate.
*   **Resin Volume per Column:** The volume of resin contained within each column. The placeholder "VTA" indicates "Vendor To Advise" or a specific volume will be specified. This is a critical parameter for sizing and regeneration calculations.

**Mechanical Design Limits**
These parameters define the maximum conditions the equipment is mechanically designed to withstand, considering potential process upsets:

*   **Maximum Pressure:** The maximum possible process pressure upset for mechanical design considerations, such as a pump dead head condition. This value is specified in kilopascals gauge (kPa(g)). Refer to [[4-PDS-XXXX-R1_demineralized_softener_notes_and_vendor_requirements|Note 6]] for further context.
*   **Maximum Temperature:** The maximum possible process temperature upset for mechanical design considerations. This value is specified in degrees Celsius (oC). Refer to [[4-PDS-XXXX-R1_demineralized_softener_notes_and_vendor_requirements|Note 5]] for further context.

**Materials of Construction**
*   **Materials:** The specific materials of construction for the equipment. For detailed material specifications, refer to [[4-PDS-XXXX-R1_demineralized_softener_notes_and_vendor_requirements|Note 4]] on the related notes page, which directs to equipment specification 4-DST-XXXX. [[material_selection|Material selection]] is critical for corrosion resistance and longevity.
