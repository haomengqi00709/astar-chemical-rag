# Stainless Steel Tubing Tier 2 Packing and Shipping Requirements

Proper packing and shipping procedures are essential to ensure the Stainless Steel Tubing Tier 2 arrives at its destination without damage and with all necessary documentation.

**Packing List:**
*   A Packing List is required as AP (As Prepared).
*   Chemetics **shall** Witness the Packing List.
*   Chemetics **shall not** Hold the Packing List.

**Loading Inspections:**
*   Chemetics **shall** Witness Truck Loading.
*   Chemetics **shall not** Hold Truck Loading.
*   Chemetics **shall** Witness Ship Loading.
*   Chemetics **shall not** Hold Ship Loading.

**Quality Record Requirements for Packing and Shipping:**
*   A Quality Record is required for the Packing List (AP).
*   No Quality Record is required for Truck Loading or Ship Loading.

For detailed specifications on land transportation, refer to [[packaging_land_transportation_0_spc_5004]]. For marine transport, consult [[packaging_marine_transportation_0_spc_5005]]. All documentation related to packing and shipping **shall** align with the [[0-QDS-7427-2_quality_record_definitions]].
