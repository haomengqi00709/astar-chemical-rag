# Horizontal Centrifugal Magnetic Drive Pump General Supply and Components

For horizontal centrifugal magnetic drive pumps, the Vendor **must** supply equipment fully assembled, ensuring it includes all items necessary for a complete and functional unit.

Major components that **must** be included are:
*   Pump assembly
*   Motor
*   Power monitor
*   Baseplate

Pumps **must** meet the process requirements detailed in their individual [[pump_data_sheets]].

**Performance and Protection:**
The Vendor **will** provide the estimated noise level of the pumps. For further details on noise limits, refer to [[equipment_noise_limits]].

All equipment **will** be dynamically balanced in accordance with ISO1940: Mechanical vibration - Balance quality requirements of rigid rotors - grade G6.3. Information on vibration control can be found in [[equipment_vibration_control]].

The Vendor **will** state the length of time that the pumps can run dry.

A power monitor **will** be supplied by the Vendor for pump protection.

Pumps **must** meet process requirements detailed in individual Pump Data Sheets.
Impellers **will** be closed unless noted otherwise on individual Pump Data Sheets.
