# Control System Project Roles and Responsibilities

This page defines key roles and their general responsibilities within the context of the Control System Design and Specification Procedure ([[control_system_design_procedure_overview]]). Specific preparation, checking, and approval responsibilities for individual documents are detailed on their respective wiki pages.

**Key Roles:**

*   **Detail Design Engineer (DDE):**
    *   The DDE is responsible for the project's Control System detailed Engineering.
    *   The DDE is frequently involved in the preparation and approval of various design, specification, and procurement documents.

*   **Field Instrument Engineer (FIE):**
    *   The FIE is responsible for the project's Field Instrument Database and Specifications.
    *   The FIE plays a key role in the preparation and approval of the [[control_system_io_list]].

**Other Involved Disciplines and Roles:**

*   **Project Engineer:** Involved in checking and approving various documents, particularly those related to design criteria, spare parts, and construction scope. Also approves purchase orders for values < CAD 200 K and risk assessments for values < CAD 200 K.
*   **Instrument:** Approves the [[control_system_design_criteria]] and checks the [[basic_control_system_specification]]. Audits the [[control_system_architecture_block_diagram]]. Involved in the final review and vendor selection.
*   **Process:** Prepares and approves various logic and configuration documents, including the [[process_logic_narrative]], [[dcs_configuration_parameters]], [[alarms_interlocks_and_setpoints]], and [[control_system_graphics_sketches]]. Checks the [[electrical_control_logic_narrative]], [[control_system_io_list]], and [[control_system_graphics]].
*   **Technology:** Checks the [[process_logic_narrative]], [[dcs_configuration_parameters]], and [[alarms_interlocks_and_setpoints]].
*   **Electrical:** Prepares and approves the [[electrical_control_logic_narrative]]. Involved in the preparation and approval of [[process_control_application_software_configuration]].
*   **Quality Assurance:** Checks and approves [[vendor_document_requirements]] and approves [[quality_documentation_sheets]].
*   **Procurement:** Prepares [[request_for_quotation]] and [[commercial_bid_tabulation]]. Involved in the final review and vendor selection. Prepares and approves [[purchase_order]].
*   **Finance:** Prepares [[financial_evaluation]].
*   **Project Manager:** Approves [[final_cost_tabulation]], [[risk_assessment]] for values > CAD 200 K, and [[purchase_order]]. Involved in the final review and vendor selection.
*   **Manager of Procurement:** Approves [[commercial_bid_tabulation]].
*   **Vendor/Manufacturer (Vendor/Mfr):** Responsible for preparing the [[dcs_operating_system_configuration]].

This procedure assigns specific preparation, checking, and approval responsibilities to these project personnel for various documents and activities throughout the control system design and implementation lifecycle.
