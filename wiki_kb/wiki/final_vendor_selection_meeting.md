# Final Vendor Selection Meeting (Orders > CAD 200k)

For orders valued greater than CAD 200,000, the Requisitioning Discipline **must** convene a meeting for the final review and Vendor selection.

**Mandatory Attendees:**
The meeting attendees **must** include:
*   The Project Engineer
*   The Project Manager
*   The Project Buyer
*   The Discipline Leads involved in the [[detailed_technical_bid_evaluation|Technical Bid Tabulation]]

**Meeting Objectives:**
During this meeting, participants **must**:
*   Review the [[detailed_cost_bid_evaluation|Cost Tabulation]] and the [[detailed_technical_bid_evaluation|Technical Bid Tabulation]], as well as the [[commercial_and_financial_bid_evaluation|Commercial Bid Tabulation]].
*   Conduct a risk assessment as described in the [[order_risk_assessment_procedure]].
*   Select the Vendor to receive the order.

The overarching objective of Vendor selection is to choose the lowest cost bid that presents acceptable business risks to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Escalation for Unacceptable Risks:**
If meeting participants identify unmitigated unacceptable business risks, they may request reconvening the meeting to include the Directors of Technology, Engineering, and Projects. If these Directors cannot agree on acceptable business risks, the matter **must** be referred to the President for a final decision.

**Order Splitting:**
The Project Manager **must** direct that the order be split among two or more Vendors if doing so results in an overall lower project cost. If an order is split, the [[detailed_cost_bid_evaluation|Cost Tabulation]] and all Bid Tabulations **must** be copied and re-numbered for each individual order. Furthermore, a separate [[order_risk_assessment_procedure|Risk Assessment]] **must** be completed for each split order.
