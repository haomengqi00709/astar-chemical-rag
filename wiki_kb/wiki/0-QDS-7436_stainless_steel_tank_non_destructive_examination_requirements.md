# Stainless Steel Tank Non-Destructive Examination Requirements (0-QDS-7436)

For the Stainless Steel Tank project (Document ID: 0-QDS-7436), Non-Destructive Examination (NDE) is a critical component of quality assurance, ensuring the structural integrity of welds and materials without causing damage.

**General NDE Compliance:**
*   All Non-Destructive Examination **shall** be performed in accordance with applicable API 650 codes.
*   All Non-Destructive Examination **shall** be performed in accordance with applicable API 620 codes.
*   Approved equivalents to API 650 or API 620 codes may be used, provided they receive prior approval from Chemetics.

**Specific NDE Requirements and Surveillance:**

**Radiography:**
*   **Requirement:** Radiography is required to be included in the Vendor's Inspection and Test Plan (I & T Plan) and **must** be part of the [[0-QDS-7436_quality_record_definitions|Quality Assurance Documentation Package (FP)]].
*   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Chemetics requires both "Witness" and "Hold" surveillance for Radiography.
    *   **Witness:** A Chemetics representative **must** be present during the execution of radiography or the review of radiographic films.
    *   **Hold:** The Vendor **shall not** proceed with subsequent manufacturing or inspection steps until Chemetics has formally released the "Hold" point after satisfactory completion and review of the Radiography results.

**Dye Penetrant:**
*   **Requirement:** Dye Penetrant testing is required to be included in the Vendor's Inspection and Test Plan (I & T Plan) and **must** be part of the [[0-QDS-7436_quality_record_definitions|Quality Assurance Documentation Package (FP)]].
*   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Chemetics requires both "Witness" and "Hold" surveillance for Dye Penetrant testing.
    *   **Witness:** A Chemetics representative **must** be present during the execution of dye penetrant testing or the review of its results.
    *   **Hold:** The Vendor **shall not** proceed with subsequent manufacturing or inspection steps until Chemetics has formally released the "Hold" point after satisfactory completion and review of the Dye Penetrant test results.

These NDE methods, coupled with Chemetics' stringent surveillance, ensure that critical areas of the Stainless Steel Tank meet the highest quality and safety standards.

**Specific NDE Requirements:**

**Radiography:**
*   **Requirement:** Radiography is required as part of the Vendor's Inspection and Test Plan (I&T Plan) and is designated as "Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package" (FP).
*   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Chemetics requires "Witness" surveillance for Radiography.
*   **Chemetics Hold Point:** Chemetics requires a "Hold" surveillance point for Radiography.

**Dye Penetrant:**
*   **Requirement:** Dye Penetrant examination is required as part of the Vendor's Inspection and Test Plan (I&T Plan) and is designated as "Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package" (FP).
*   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Chemetics requires "Witness" surveillance for Dye Penetrant.
*   **Chemetics Hold Point:** Chemetics requires a "Hold" surveillance point for Dye Penetrant.
