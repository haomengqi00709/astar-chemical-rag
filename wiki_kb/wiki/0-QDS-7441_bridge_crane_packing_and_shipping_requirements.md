# Bridge Crane Packing and Shipping Requirements

This page details the packing and shipping requirements for the Bridge Crane, as specified in Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7441. These requirements ensure that the equipment is adequately protected during transit and that all necessary documentation accompanies the shipment.

**Packing and Crating Activity:**
Packing/Crating is a mandatory item or activity within the quality assurance process. The vendor **must** ensure that the Bridge Crane and its components are packed and crated appropriately to prevent damage during handling and transportation.

**Chemetics Surveillance for Packaging:**
Chemetics surveillance for Packaging requires "Witness." A Chemetics representative **shall** be present to observe the packing process, ensuring that all items are secured and protected according to specifications.

**Packing List Requirements:**
A Packing List is required as "AP" ([[quality_record_definitions|As Prepared]]). The Packing List **must** accurately detail all contents of the shipment, including quantities, descriptions, and any special handling instructions.

**Chemetics Surveillance for Packing List:**
Chemetics surveillance for the Packing List requires "Witness." A Chemetics representative **shall** verify the accuracy and completeness of the Packing List against the actual packed items.
