# Project Variance/Change Notice Form Details

This page details the structure and fields of the **PROJECT VARIANCE/CHANGE NOTICE (PVN/PCN) FORM**, which serves as Appendix I to the [[pvn_pcn_approval_process]] (Document ID: 0-PRC-4003-R1). This form is a crucial tool for documenting and managing project changes and variances, aligning with the [[pvn_pcn_program_objectives_and_requirements]] and supporting the [[pvn_pcn_revision_process]].

### SECTION 1 - JOB INFORMATION
This section captures essential project identification details:
*   **Job No.:** Unique identifier for the project.
*   **PVN NO.:** Project Variance Notice number.
*   **PCN NO.:** Project Change Notice number.
*   **Rev.:** Revision number of the form.
*   **Client:** Name of the client.
*   **Date:** Date of form initiation.
*   **Project Title:** Full title of the project.
*   **Location:** Project geographical location.

### SECTION 2 - CHANGE INFORMATION
This section details the specifics of the proposed change or variance:
*   **Source of Change:** Origin of the change (e.g., client request, design error, field condition).
*   **Title:** A concise title for the change.
*   **Basis of Change:** Justification or reason for the change.
*   **Impact:** Description of impacts on various project aspects, including Scope, Engineering, Construction, Procurement, and others. For detailed analysis, refer to [[pvn_pcn_impact_analysis_and_evaluation]].
*   **Schedule Impact:** Assessment of the change's effect on the [[project_schedule_and_milestones]].
*   **Cost Impact:** Financial implications of the change, affecting [[project_cost_control]].
*   **Originator:** Name of the person initiating the form.
*   **Date:** Date the change information was recorded.

### SECTION 3 - APPROVALS AND ACTIONS
This section documents the internal approvals required for the PVN/PCN:
*   **Project Control Manager:** Signature for approval.
*   **Date:** Date of Project Control Manager's approval.
*   **Project Manager:** Signature for approval.
*   **Project Engineer:** Signature for approval.
*   **Date:** Date of Project Engineer's approval.
*   **Project Director:** Signature for approval.
*   **Recommended Action:** Proposed course of action.
*   **Comments:** Any additional remarks or justifications.

### SECTION 4 - CLIENT AUTHORIZATION
This section is reserved for formal client approval:
*   **Project Engineer:** Signature for client submission.
*   **Date:** Date of client submission by Project Engineer.
*   **Project Manager:** Signature for client submission.
