# Flow Measurement - Differential Pressure

Differential pressure (DP) techniques, primarily using orifice plates, are widely applied for flow measurement, especially in large diameter pipelines.

**Primary Elements and Taps:**
*   The standard primary element **will** be a square edge concentric orifice plate in accordance with **AS 2360**.
*   A quadrant edge orifice, in accordance with **BS 1042**, **will** be used when the Reynolds number is below 20,000.
*   Flange taps **will** be used. D-D/2 taps may be used if the line size is 400 NB or larger.

**Orifice Plate Material and Marking:**
*   The material of orifice plates **as minimum will be** type 316 stainless steel, unless special materials are required by the process fluid.
*   Orifice plates **will** have the instrument tag number, material type, flow direction identification, and bore size clearly stamped on the upstream side of the plate handle.

**Installation Requirements:**
*   Straight-run lengths of pipe upstream and downstream of orifice plate installations **will** conform to the requirements defined in **AS 2360.1** and as required to meet the specified accuracy.
*   Orifice plates **will** be installed in a manner such that they **will** always measure a full pipe flow.
*   The orifice meter differential range **will** be selected so that the d/D ratio exceeds 0.2 and is less than 0.7.
*   Square root extraction for flow applications **will** be configured in the transmitter.

**DP Transmitter Installation:**
*   Seals and condensate pots **will** be used for DP transmitter installation where necessary, especially for steam and high-temperature applications (refer to [[pressure_measurement_installation_considerations|Pressure Measurement - Installation Considerations]]).
*   All differential pressure transmitters for flow measurement applications **will** be fitted with 3-Valve manifolds. These manifolds enable isolation of the transmitter from the process and equalization of the high and low pressure sides for zero checks.
*   Where safety concerns exist with the potential of mixing two dissimilar fluids, the 3-valve manifold **will not** be provided. In such cases, isolation valves **will** be provided to enable isolation of the process.

**Flow Rate and Rangeability:**
*   For differential pressure flowmeters, the normal flowrate **will** be between 70% and 80% of the instrument span.
*   Anticipated minimum and maximum flowrates **will** be between 30% and 95% of capacity.
*   If a rangeability larger than 30% to 95% is required, two differential pressure transmitters **will** be considered to connect to the DP element, or an alternative flow measurement method **will** be used.

**Multivariable Transmitters:**
Multivariable transmitters with automatic temperature and pressure compensation may be used for mass flow measurement on gas flows using pressure differential techniques, offering enhanced accuracy and data.
