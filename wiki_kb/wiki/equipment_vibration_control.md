# Equipment Vibration Control

As per document 4-SPC-0001, the control of vibration is a critical aspect of equipment design and installation.

**Vendor Information Requirements**
The Vendor **will** provide relevant information on inertial loads and any theoretical dynamic loads caused by an imbalance in the equipment. This data is essential for assessing potential vibration issues and designing appropriate mitigation strategies.

The Vendor **shall specify** installation requirements for vibration control or isolation. This includes recommendations for mounting, foundations, and any necessary isolation components. If required, the Vendor **will** provide vibration absorption pads as part of the equipment supply.

**Vibration Limits**
Vibration limits for fans and pumps **must conform** to ISO standard 10816-1, zone A. This standard defines criteria for assessing machine vibration by measurements on non-rotating parts.

**Installation Requirements**
The Vendor **will** provide comprehensive installation information for mounting and foundations that **will be required** to achieve the specified vibration limits. Proper installation is crucial to ensure that the equipment operates within acceptable vibration parameters throughout its service life.

For related information on equipment noise limits, refer to [[equipment_noise_limits]].
