# Business Entertainment and Expenses

This policy outlines the guidelines for incurring business entertainment expenses and seeking reimbursement, ensuring sound business judgment and adherence to corporate principles.

**Expense Reimbursement Principles**
Employees are expected to exercise sound business judgment in incurring expenses for which reimbursement is expected from Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. For general expense reimbursement procedures, refer to [[0-PRC-9003_business_expense_reimbursement_policy]] and [[0-PRC-9003_expense_report_submission_and_documentation]].

**Business Entertainment Requirements**
*   Before any business entertainment expenses are incurred, a clear potential for business development **must** exist.
*   When arranging business entertainment, employees **should** exercise good judgment in selecting the individuals to attend. A group for business entertainment **should** consist of a balanced mixture of employees and clients.
*   Spouses/partners **should not** be included in business entertainment unless the occasion warrants, such as when a client is traveling with their spouse/partner.
*   The selected business entertainment **must not** negatively impact Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ corporate image or principles.
*   Examples of appropriate business entertainment include lunches, dinners, theatre, golf, and other sporting events.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **will not** tolerate any form of “adult” entertainment.
*   If an employee has any doubt as to whether a proposed form of entertainment is in accordance with this policy, he or she **should** first seek guidance from the appropriate Senior Manager or Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ Legal Counsel.

**Alcohol Consumption and Transportation**
*   When entertaining clients where alcohol is served, employees **should** exercise good judgment in terms of how much alcohol is offered or consumed.
*   Employees **must not** drive while impaired when entertaining clients where alcohol is served.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **will** cover the cost of alternate transportation in circumstances where alcohol is served and employees **must not** drive while impaired.
