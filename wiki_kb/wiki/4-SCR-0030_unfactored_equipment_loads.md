# Unfactored Equipment Loads for Plate & Frame Heat Exchanger (4-SCR-0030)

This page details the unfactored equipment load categories for the Plate & Frame Heat Exchanger as specified in document 4-SCR-0030. These loads are critical for the structural design and analysis of the equipment's support system.

**Unfactored Equipment Load Categories:**
*   **Empty - Maximum Mass (kg):** Represents the maximum mass of the equipment when it is empty, typically used for transportation and erection loads.
*   **Flooded (Design) - Maximum Mass (kg):** Represents the maximum mass of the equipment when it is completely filled with water or another test medium, used for hydrostatic testing and design verification.
*   **Operating - Maximum Mass (kg):** Represents the maximum mass of the equipment under normal operating conditions, including process fluids and internal components.
*   **Wind Base Shear (kN):** The horizontal force exerted by wind on the equipment at its base. This is a critical load for assessing lateral stability. Refer to [[wind_design_requirements]] for detailed wind load calculations.
*   **Wind Base Moment (kN-m):** The overturning moment exerted by wind on the equipment at its base. This is used to design foundations and anchorages against overturning.
*   **Seismic Base Shear (kN):** The horizontal force exerted by seismic activity on the equipment at its base. This is a critical load for assessing structural integrity in earthquake-prone regions. Refer to [[seismic_design_requirements]] for detailed seismic load calculations.
*   **Seismic Base Moment (kN-m):** The overturning moment exerted by seismic activity on the equipment at its base. This is used to design foundations and anchorages against seismic overturning.

These unfactored loads serve as inputs for further structural calculations, where appropriate load factors and combinations are applied according to relevant design codes and standards. For general information on equipment support criteria, see [[equipment_support_criteria]].
