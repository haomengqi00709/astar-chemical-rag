# Vendor Non-Conformance Management

Effective management of non-conformances is a critical aspect of Vendor quality assurance, as detailed in Section 5 of Specification [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment|0-SPC-7001]]. The Vendor's Quality Plan **shall** incorporate procedures for handling non-conformances.

**Reporting Non-Conformances**
The Vendor **must** report any non-conformances to the Inspector as soon as they have been discovered. Non-conformances identified by the Inspector during [[vendor_quality_surveillance_and_access|surveillance visits]] **will** be identified to the Vendor in writing.

**Corrective and Preventive Actions**
Upon identification of a non-conformance, the Vendor **shall** promptly propose corrective action to remedy the non-conformance. This corrective action **may** include any necessary inspection or testing. Furthermore, the Vendor **shall** propose action to prevent the recurrence of the non-conformance.

**Approval of Actions**
The Vendor **must not** proceed with the proposed corrective or preventive actions until Aker Kvaerner Chemetics approves them in writing. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' approval **will** indicate whether any subsequent inspection or testing related to the non-conformance will be designated as a [[chemetics_surveillance_level|witness or hold point]].

**Documentation of Results**
Following the completion of the corrective action, the Vendor **shall** forward to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics documented results of any inspection or testing performed. These documented results **must** clearly indicate conformance with specifications, as evaluated and approved by the Vendor’s authorized personnel.
