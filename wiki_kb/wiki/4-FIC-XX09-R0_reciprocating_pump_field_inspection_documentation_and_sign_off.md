# Reciprocating Pump Field Inspection Documentation and Sign-off (4-FIC-XX09-R0)

The field inspection checklist for reciprocating pumps (document 4-FIC-XX09-R0) mandates a structured approach to documenting any identified deficiencies and securing formal approvals from all relevant parties. This ensures accountability and facilitates a clear record of the pump's readiness for service.

**Deficiencies and Comments:**
A dedicated section within the document allows for the detailed recording of up to 10 numbered Deficiencies & Comments. This section is vital for capturing any non-conformances, deviations from specifications, or observations made during the inspection. Each entry should clearly describe the issue, enabling effective tracking and resolution through the [[construction_non_conformance_reporting]] process. The use of a [[0-NCR-7XXX_template_non_conformance_report]] may be required for significant issues.

**Sign-off Requirements:**
Formal sign-off is a mandatory step, ensuring that all stakeholders acknowledge the inspection findings and agree on the subsequent actions. Signatures and dates **are required** from:
*   **Aker Kvaerner Chemetics:** This signature confirms that the company's internal quality and engineering standards have been met, and that their representatives have reviewed and accepted the inspection findings.
*   **Sub Contractor:** The Sub Contractor's signature acknowledges their awareness of the inspection results, including any identified deficiencies for which they may be responsible. This is a key part of [[subcontractor_sample_documents]] and project accountability.
*   **Client:** The Client's signature represents the final acceptance of the reciprocating pump's installation status, signifying their approval for progression to subsequent project phases, such as commissioning. This is often linked to [[client_project_progress_reporting]] and project milestones.
