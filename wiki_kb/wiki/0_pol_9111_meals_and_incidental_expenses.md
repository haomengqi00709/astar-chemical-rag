# 0-POL-9111 Meals and Incidental Expenses

The 0-POL-9111 procedure outlines the provisions for covering meals and incidental expenses for employees on short-term site assignments.

**Per Diem System:**
A per diem [[will]] be set to cover meals and incidental expenses in accordance with reasonable local costs. This per diem aims to provide adequate compensation for daily living expenses while employees are away from their primary residence on assignment.
The per diem [[will]] take into consideration whether meals and housing are already provided with the assignment. This ensures that compensation is adjusted based on the support already offered.

**Housing:**
Housing costs associated with site assignments [[will]] be considered a Business Expense, separate from the per diem for meals and incidentals.

**Fallback Procedure:**
If a specific per diem is not established for a particular Short Term Assignment, the provisions of [[business_expense_reimbursement_0_prc_9003]] [[will]] apply for the reimbursement of meals and incidental expenses.

**Tax Compliance:**
The Company [[will]] make efforts to ensure compliance with Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) guidelines regarding per diem and expense reimbursements. This proactive approach aims to avoid personal tax liability for employees related to these payments.

**Housing**
Housing will be considered a Business Expense.
