# Variation Report

The Variation Report is a key document generated during the [[project_change_evaluation|Project Change Evaluation]] phase of the [[0-PRC-4003-R1_project_change_management_flow_diagram|Project Change Management Flow Diagram]]. Its primary purpose is to consolidate and present a comprehensive assessment of the impacts associated with a proposed project change.

Prepared by the [[change_management_roles_and_responsibilities_0_prc_4003|Project Control Specialist]], the Variation Report incorporates all findings from the detailed impact evaluations conducted by various project disciplines. This includes:
*   **Man-hours impacts**: Evaluations from [[change_management_roles_and_responsibilities_0_prc_4003|Discipline Leads]] on the additional or reduced labor required.
*   **Schedule impacts**: Assessments from [[change_management_roles_and_responsibilities_0_prc_4003|Discipline Leads]] on how the change affects project timelines and milestones.
*   **Cost estimates**: Financial assessments provided by the [[change_management_roles_and_responsibilities_0_prc_4003|Estimator]] for the total cost of the change.
*   **Risk assessments**: Reviews and contingency requirements identified by [[change_management_roles_and_responsibilities_0_prc_4003|Project Risk Management]].

The Project Control Specialist **must** incorporate all these impact assessments into the Variation Report. Once compiled, the report is issued for approval, serving as the primary document for stakeholders during the [[project_change_approval|Project Change Approval]] phase. It provides the necessary data for informed decision-making regarding whether to approve, reject, or revise the proposed change.

As a critical component of the [[change_management_document_types]], the Variation Report ensures that all potential consequences of a change are clearly understood before any formal commitment is made.
