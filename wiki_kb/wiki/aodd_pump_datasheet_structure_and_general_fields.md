# AODD Pump Datasheet Structure and General Fields

This page describes the general structure and common fields found within the Air Operated Double Diaphragm (AODD) Pump Datasheet, document 4-DST-XXXX-R1. These fields provide essential organizational and quantitative information, facilitating equipment identification, procurement, and operational planning.

**General Datasheet Fields:**

*   **Item Numbering:** The document lists items numbered from 1.0 to 24.0. This systematic numbering helps in organizing and referencing specific details within the datasheet.
*   **Tag No.:** A unique alphanumeric identifier assigned to each piece of equipment within a plant. The datasheet includes a field for "Tag No." to ensure proper identification and integration into the plant's asset management system. For more information on equipment tagging, refer to [[plant_equipment_identification_4_prc_0001]].
*   **Quantity:** Specifies the number of pumps required for the project, broken down into two categories:
    *   **Operating:** The number of pumps actively in service during normal plant operation.
    *   **Installed Spare:** The number of identical pumps installed but kept in standby, ready to be brought online in case of failure or maintenance of an operating unit. This ensures continuous plant operation and reduces downtime.
*   **Process Conditions Categories:** The datasheet organizes process conditions under distinct categories to accommodate different operational scenarios:
    *   **DESIGN PER UNIT:** This section specifies the primary design conditions for a single pump, representing the most common or critical operating point.
    *   **ALTERNATE PER UNIT:** This section allows for the specification of alternative or less frequent operating conditions that the pump must also be capable of handling. This is useful for scenarios like turndown, startup, or different process modes.

These structural elements and general fields are fundamental to any equipment datasheet, providing a clear and comprehensive overview of the AODD pump's requirements and deployment within a project. The consistent use of such fields across [[equipment_data_sheets_4_dst_xxxx]] ensures uniformity and ease of information retrieval.
