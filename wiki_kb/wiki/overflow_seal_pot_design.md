# Overflow Seal Pot Design

This page provides information on the design and dimensions of [[overflow_seal_pot_design|overflow seal pots]], which are critical for ensuring proper overflow piping connections in [[vessel_and_tank_mechanical_checkout|vessels and tanks]]. Correct dimensions **must** be verified during mechanical checkout.
