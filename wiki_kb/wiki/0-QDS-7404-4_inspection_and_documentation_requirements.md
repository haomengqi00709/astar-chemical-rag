# 0-QDS-7404-4 Inspection and Documentation Requirements

This page details the specific inspection and documentation requirements for the Electrostatic Precipitator Miscellaneous Items, as specified in Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7404-4. These requirements ensure that critical aspects of the items' quality, manufacturing, and supply are verified and properly documented. Chemetics' surveillance for all listed items is designated as "Complete," indicating a thorough review of the vendor's documentation and, where applicable, direct inspection.

**Mandatory Quality Records and Surveillance:**

1.  **Material Verification (Item 1.0 in Quality Record table):**
    *   **Requirement:** Verification of materials used against specifications.
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Complete.
    *   **Significance:** Ensures that the correct materials, with the specified properties, are used in the fabrication of components, which is critical for performance and longevity. This often involves reviewing material certificates and traceability records.

2.  **Certificate of Compliance:**
    *   **Required Quality Record Type:** FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package).
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Complete.
    *   **Significance:** A formal document issued by the vendor certifying that the supplied items comply with all specified requirements, standards, and regulations. This is a crucial final document for project handover.

3.  **Exterior Coating/Lining Inspection (Item 5.0 in Quality Record table):**
    *   **Requirement:** Inspection of all exterior coatings and linings.
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Complete.
    *   **Significance:** Ensures that protective coatings and linings are applied correctly and meet specified thickness, adhesion, and finish requirements. This is vital for corrosion protection and operational lifespan. For more information on coating specifications, refer to [[coatings_design_and_specification_7_prc_0002]].

4.  **Packaging (Item 10.0 in Quality Record table):**
    *   **Requirement:** Inspection of packaging methods.
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Complete.
    *   **Significance:** Verifies that items are packaged appropriately to prevent damage during transit and storage. Proper packaging is essential for ensuring the integrity of the delivered goods. Refer to [[packaging_land_transportation_0_spc_5004]] for general packaging guidelines.

5.  **Packing List:**
    *   **Required Quality Record Type:** AP (As Prepared).
    *   **Chemetics Surveillance:** Complete.
    *   **Significance:** A detailed list of all items included in a shipment. While an "As Prepared" record, its complete surveillance by Chemetics ensures accuracy for logistics and receipt verification.

These detailed inspection and documentation requirements, coupled with "Complete" Chemetics surveillance, are designed to provide comprehensive assurance of the quality and compliance of all Electrostatic Precipitator Miscellaneous Items. For general vendor quality assurance processes, refer to [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment]] and [[vendor_quality_assurance]].
