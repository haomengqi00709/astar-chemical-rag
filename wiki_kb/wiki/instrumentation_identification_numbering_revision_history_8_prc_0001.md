# Instrumentation Identification Numbering Revision History (8-PRC-0001)

This page documents the revision history for the [[plant_instrument_numbering_8_prc_0001]] procedure.

*   **Revision 3:** The "PRV" (Pressure Relief Valve) designation was added to the standard character assignments. This specific addition was not explicitly flagged within the document at the time of its implementation.
