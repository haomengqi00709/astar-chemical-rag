# Lamella Clarifier Process Design Conditions

This page details the essential process design conditions and stream data for the Lamella Clarifier, as specified in document [[4-PDS-XXXX-R1_template_clarifier_thickener_datasheet]]. Understanding these parameters is crucial for the correct operation and integration of the clarifier within the overall plant process.

**Process Design Conditions:**
The design of the Lamella Clarifier is based on specific operational scenarios and criteria:
*   **Case Description:** Defines the particular operating scenario or condition for which the clarifier is designed. This could include normal operation, maximum capacity, or specific upset conditions.
*   **Design Basis:** Outlines the fundamental assumptions and principles used in the engineering design, ensuring the clarifier meets performance requirements under specified conditions.

**Process Streams:**
The Lamella Clarifier handles three primary process streams:
*   **FEED:** The incoming stream containing the liquid and suspended solids that require separation.
*   **OVERFLOW:** The clarified liquid stream exiting the top of the clarifier, typically with a reduced concentration of suspended solids.
*   **UNDERFLOW:** The concentrated sludge or solids stream discharged from the bottom of the clarifier.

**Detailed Process Data Fields:**
For each of the process streams (FEED, OVERFLOW, UNDERFLOW), the following data fields provide critical information:
*   **Fluid Description:** A qualitative description of the fluid being processed.
*   **Composition (%w/w):** The weight percentage of various components within the fluid, essential for material balance and chemical compatibility.
*   **Suspended Solids (Max) (%w/w):** The maximum allowable weight percentage of suspended solids, a key parameter for clarifier performance.
*   **Coagulant Content (ppm):** The concentration of any coagulants or flocculants added to aid in solid-liquid separation, measured in parts per million.
*   **Normal Flowrate (m3/h):** The typical volumetric flow rate of the stream during standard operation.
*   **Maximum Flowrate (m3/h):** The peak volumetric flow rate the clarifier is designed to handle, crucial for sizing and surge capacity.
*   **Temperature (oC):** The operating temperature of the fluid in degrees Celsius.
*   **Density (kg/m3):** The mass per unit volume of the fluid, in kilograms per cubic meter.
*   **Viscosity (cP):** The resistance to flow of the fluid, measured in centipoise.
*   **pH:** The acidity or alkalinity of the fluid, a critical parameter for chemical reactions and material selection.
*   **Pressure (kPa(g)):** The gauge pressure of the fluid in kilopascals.
*   **Fluid Temperature Minimum (oC):** The lowest expected operating temperature of the fluid.
*   **Fluid Temperature Normal (oC):** The typical operating temperature of the fluid.
*   **Fluid Temperature Maximum (oC):** The highest expected operating temperature of the fluid, vital for thermal design and material limits.

These detailed process parameters ensure that the Lamella Clarifier is designed to effectively handle the specific characteristics of the process fluids, contributing to efficient solid-liquid separation.
