# Document Administration and Revisions (4-SPC-0068)

This document, 4-SPC-0068, represents a complete re-write of its predecessor, 4-SPC-0020-R5, which was titled "Equipment Welding Unalloyed Nickel." The re-write aimed to streamline the specification by focusing exclusively on welding-related requirements.

**Scope Changes and Content Relocation:**
*   Any content not directly related to welding or already covered by the ASME code has been removed from this specification.
*   Requirements pertaining to inspection **must** now be addressed in the appropriate Quality Assurance (QA) documents, such as a [[0-QDS-7XXX_template_quality_data_sheet]] or other project-specific QA plans.

**Jurisdictional Compliance:**
This standard is primarily developed to align with the ASME code. However, for projects requiring compliance with codes from other jurisdictions (e.g., Australia, Europe), this standard **will** be modified as necessary to meet those specific regulatory requirements.

Consequently, no new revision number has been assigned, and changes have not been explicitly marked due to this administrative update. For more details on the company's name change history, refer to [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_name_change_history]].
