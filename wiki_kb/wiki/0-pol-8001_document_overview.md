# 0-POL-8001 Substance Use Policy Overview

This document, identified as **0-POL-8001**, outlines Chemetics' policy regarding substance use. The policy is a core administrative document reflecting Chemetics' fundamental commitments.

Chemetics is committed to providing safe workplaces for its employees and maintaining high standards of conduct to ensure health, safety, and productivity across all operations.

The primary purpose of this policy is to minimize the risk of impaired performance due to substance use. By doing so, it aims to ensure a safe, healthy, and productive workplace environment for all individuals associated with Chemetics.
