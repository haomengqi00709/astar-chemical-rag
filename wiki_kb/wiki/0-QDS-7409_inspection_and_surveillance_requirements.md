# Mist Eliminator Inspection and Surveillance Requirements

This page details the inspection and surveillance requirements for Mist Eliminator Mesh Pad Type equipment, as defined in Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7409.

**Chemetics Surveillance Level:**
Surveillance by Chemetics for this equipment **shall** be conducted to Level 0.0. The specific surveillance activities are further detailed within this Quality Data Sheet. For general information on Chemetics surveillance levels, refer to pages such as [[0-QDS-7404-1_chemetics_surveillance_level]].

**Vendor Inspection and Test Plan (I&T Plan) Requirements:**
The Vendor's Inspection and Test Plan **must** include the following activities:
*   **Material Verification:** Verification of materials used in the Mist Eliminator.
*   **Final Inspection:** A comprehensive final inspection of the completed equipment.
*   **Packing/Crating:** Procedures for proper packing and crating of the equipment.

**Chemetics Witness Surveillance Requirements:**
Chemetics requires witness surveillance for the following inspection and documentation activities:
*   **Dimensional Inspection:** Verification of critical dimensions.
*   **Quantity Inspection:** Confirmation of the quantity of items.
*   **Review QA Documentation:** Review of the Vendor's Quality Assurance documentation package.
*   **Packaging/Tagging:** Inspection of packaging and tagging procedures.
*   **Packing List:** Verification of the packing list.

These requirements ensure thorough quality control throughout the manufacturing and preparation process for the Mist Eliminator equipment. Related inspection and documentation requirements for other equipment can be found in pages like [[0-QDS-7404-1_inspection_and_documentation_requirements]].
