# Project Variance and Change Notification (PVN/PCN) Types and Impacts

Within the framework of project change management, two distinct types of changes are recognized: external changes, documented as Project Change Notifications (PCN), and internal changes, documented as Project Variance Notifications (PVN). Both utilize the same form but are registered in separate logs to facilitate tracking their respective impacts on the project budget.

**External Change: Project Change Notification (PCN)**
A PCN documents a scope change. This includes:
*   Any additional work or rework requested by the client that was not part of the original scope documents.
*   Additional work or rework resulting from a client's change in their project intent, execution plan, standards and specifications, or the delivery of key information and decisions.
*   Any change in intent to better align with project objectives, even if it deviates from the original project scope, design basis, and execution plan.

**Mandatory Requirement:** The PCN **must** be approved by the Client prior to its implementation or execution. An approved PCN will impact both the project's "Approved Budget" and its "Forecast." A Project Change Order is typically requested by the Project Manager from the Client to incorporate one or more approved PCNs, thereby adjusting the contract value or project targets.

**Internal Change: Project Variance Notification (PVN)**
A PVN documents a non-scope change. This includes:
*   Any additional work or rework necessitated by errors or omissions.
*   Additional work or rework due to an internal change in the execution plan.
*   Productivity loss not attributable to client impact.
*   Internal budget transfers for improved tracking of cost elements, resulting in a $0 cost change to the overall project.

**Mandatory Requirement:** The PVN **must** be approved before any associated work commences. Upon approval of a PVN, the project's "Revised Budget" and "Forecast" will be adjusted. If a cost system does not feature a "Revised Budget" function, PVN/PCN will solely impact the Forecast.

**General Principles:**
*   When a change notification is initiated, it **must** first be identified as either a scope change (PCN) or a non-scope change (PVN).
*   PVNs generated for internal changes may or may not be provided to the Client, depending on the type of contract and existing agreements. For Lump Sum contracts, PVNs will generally not be provided to the Client. In reimbursable contracts, PVNs may be shared with Clients.
*   Project forecast trending is based on both approved PCN/PVNs and "Pending" PCN/PVNs.
