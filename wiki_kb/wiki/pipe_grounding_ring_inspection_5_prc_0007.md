# Pipe Grounding Ring Inspection (5-PRC-0007)

The inspection of pipe grounding rings is an important safety and electrical integrity check within the [[mechanical_checkout_piping_5_prc_0007|Piping Mechanical Checkout Procedure (5-PRC-0007)]]. This ensures proper grounding and prevents stray currents or static build-up.

**Required Documentation for Pipe Grounding Ring Checkout:**
*   [[piping_and_instrumentation_drawings|P&ID]]
*   [[specialty_item_drawing|Specialty Item Drawing]]
*   [[pipe_isometric|Isometric]]

**Pipe Grounding Ring Inspection Steps:**
*   **Flange Checks:** All flange checks for pipe grounding rings **shall** be performed as described in the [[flange_inspection_requirements_5_prc_0007|Flange Inspection Requirements]].
*   **Location and Orientation:** Confirm that the [[pipe_grounding_ring_installation|grounding ring]] is installed in the correct location and orientation as per the [[specialty_item_drawing|specialty item drawing]].
*   **Grounding Wire Connection:** Confirm that the grounding wires are firmly bolted to the grounding ring, ensuring a secure electrical connection.
*   **Electrolytic Cellroom Piping Isolation:** Confirm that electrolytic cellroom piping and structure is not grounded or touching any other structure.
*   **Electrical Continuity:** Check with [[electrical_personnel|electrical personnel]] for checkout and [[electrical_continuity_testing|electrical continuity]] of the pipe grounding ring.

These checks are crucial for maintaining the electrical safety and integrity of the piping system, especially in environments where static electricity or stray currents pose a risk.
