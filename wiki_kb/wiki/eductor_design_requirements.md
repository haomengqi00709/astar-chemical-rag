# Eductor Design Requirements

This page details the general design requirements for eductor units, as outlined in datasheet **4-DST-XXXX-R0**. These specifications are crucial for ensuring the eductor's suitability for its intended application, operational environment, and compliance with safety and performance standards.

**General Design Parameters:**
*   **Location:** The eductor is designated for **Indoor** installation. This implies specific considerations for ventilation, lighting, and protection from external weather elements.
*   **Area Classification:** This is a mandatory parameter and **must** be specified according to relevant hazardous area classification standards (e.g., NEC, IECEx). This dictates the type of electrical equipment and instrumentation required.
*   **Number of Stages:** The "Type of Unit" for the eductor **must** explicitly specify the number of stages. The number of stages directly impacts the eductor's performance characteristics, such as suction lift and discharge pressure capabilities.
*   **Stability at Shut Off:** The eductor design **must** ensure operational stability under shut-off conditions, preventing cavitation, vibration, or other detrimental effects when the pumped fluid flow is interrupted.
*   **Painting:** Specific painting requirements **must** be specified, including paint type, surface preparation, and color codes, for corrosion protection and aesthetic purposes.
*   **Connection - Pumped Fluid:** Detailed connection specifications for the pumped fluid inlet and outlet **must** be provided, including size, flange rating, and material.
*   **Connection - Motivated Fluid:** Detailed connection specifications for the motivated fluid inlet **must** be provided, including size, flange rating, and material.

**Pressure and Temperature Design Conditions:**
*   **Design Pressure:** Two distinct values for design pressure **must** be specified, with units in kPa (g) (kilopascals gauge). These values typically represent the maximum allowable working pressure and a test pressure. The [[site_design_conditions_1_dst_0001]] are highly relevant for determining the appropriate design pressure, considering factors like altitude and ambient conditions.
*   **Minimum Design Temperature:** Two values for minimum design temperature **must** be specified, with units in °C. These values define the lowest temperatures the eductor can safely withstand, crucial for material selection and preventing brittle fracture.
*   **Maximum Design Temperature:** Two values for maximum design temperature **must** be specified, with units in °C. These values define the highest temperatures the eductor can safely withstand, impacting material selection, gasket choices, and insulation requirements.

These comprehensive design requirements are closely linked to the [[eductor_material_specification]] and are developed in consideration of the specified [[eductor_pumped_fluid_operating_conditions]] and [[eductor_motive_fluid_operating_conditions]] to ensure a robust and reliable eductor system.

### Pressure and Temperature Specifications
*   **Design Pressure:** While the unit is kPa (g), it requires two distinct values to be specified. Site conditions are relevant and must be considered when determining the Design Pressure.
*   **Minimum Design Temperature:** While the unit is °C, it requires two distinct values to be specified.
*   **Maximum Design Temperature:** While the unit is °C, it requires two distinct values to be specified.
