# 4-SPC-0040 Non-Destructive Examination (NDE)

Specification 4-SPC-0040 outlines the mandatory requirements for Non-Destructive Examination (NDE) during the fabrication of metallic process vessels and components.

**Radiography (RT):**
*   When required or specified, radiography **must** be performed in accordance with:
    *   [[ASME_section_v_non_destructive_examination|ASME Section V, Article 2]] for vessels fabricated to [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels|ASME]].
    *   [[API_620_low_pressure_storage_tanks|API 620 paragraph 7.15.1]] for tanks fabricated to [[API_620_low_pressure_storage_tanks|API 620]].
    *   [[API_650_welded_steel_tanks|API 650 paragraph 6.1]] for tanks fabricated to [[API_650_welded_steel_tanks|API 650]].
*   Radiography will **not** be performed until all work required to correct out-of-roundness and peaking has been completed.
*   Location of spot radiographs will normally be selected by the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Inspector. Junctions between circumferential and longitudinal seams will be considered as preferred locations.
*   Where more than one welder/welding operator has been used, spot radiography **must** be performed in such a manner that the welding of each welder/welding operator is examined.
*   When additional radiography is required as defined in [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels|ASME Section VIII, Division 1, paragraph UW-52(d)(2)]], all associated costs will be the responsibility of the Vendor.
*   For radiography of repairs, all costs will be the responsibility of the Vendor.
*   Radiographic inspection reports **must** include a sketch or map of the locations of the radiographs.
*   Personnel performing radiographic examination **must** be qualified to SNTC-1A.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics reserves the right to accept or reject any detected indications from radiography.

**Liquid Penetrant Examination (PT):**
*   When required or specified, liquid penetrant examination **must** be performed in accordance with [[ASME_section_v_non_destructive_examination|ASME Section V, Article 6]].
*   Acceptance standards for liquid penetrant examination will be per [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels|ASME Section VIII Division 1, Appendix 8]].
*   Linear indications will be considered unacceptable for liquid penetrant examination.
*   Welds **must**, as a minimum, be dressed sufficiently so as to permit proper evaluation of defects for liquid penetrant examination.
*   Where internal welds are specified to be ground smooth, any required liquid penetrant examination **must** be carried out after grinding.
*   Liquid penetrant examination **must** be performed on both surfaces of completed butt welds.
*   Liquid penetrant examination **must** be performed on all welds exposed to the process.

**Magnetic Particle Examination (MT):**
*   When required or specified, magnetic particle examination **must** be carried out in accordance with [[ASME_section_v_non_destructive_examination|ASME Section V, Article 7]].
*   Acceptance standards for magnetic particle examination will be per [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels|ASME Section VIII, Division 1, Appendix 6]].
