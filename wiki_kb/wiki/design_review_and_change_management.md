# Design Review and Change Management

The design of the Project is subject to systematic review processes to ensure its quality, safety, and constructability. Following these reviews, a rigorous change management process is implemented for all subsequent design modifications.

**Systematic Design Reviews**
Design reviews are conducted in accordance with established procedures to identify potential issues and optimize the design:
*   [[hazard_and_operability_studies_1_prc_0004]]: Hazard and Operability Studies (1-PRC-0004).
*   [[0-prc-6001_plant_constructability_review_procedure_overview]]: Plant Constructability Review (0-PRC-6001).
*   [[plant_layout_review_5_prc_0005]]: Plant Layout Review (5-PRC-0005).

**Design Change Control**
Following the completion of these initial design reviews, the Project Engineer is responsible for monitoring all subsequent design changes. Any subsequent design changes **must** be reviewed in accordance with the requirements of the listed procedures to maintain design integrity.

Changes to previously published documents are clearly identified:
*   For drawings, changes are indicated by "clouding."
*   For textual documents, changes are marked by lines in the left margins.

Changed documents **must** be checked and approved before re-issue. They **must** also be published as for the originals before re-issue, ensuring an unalterable record of the revised design.

The Project Engineer is responsible for communicating all design changes to every Discipline Lead. This communication ensures that all affected design elements are revised consistently and accurately across all disciplines.

**Owner Review and Approval**
Design documents that require review and approval by the Owner are explicitly identified in the Contract. Such documents **must** be published, checked, and approved by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics before they are issued to the Owner for their review.
