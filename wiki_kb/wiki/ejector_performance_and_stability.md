# Ejector Performance and Stability

This page outlines critical performance and stability requirements for ejector systems, as specified in the Template Ejector Datasheet (Document ID [[template_ejector_datasheet_administration]]). These criteria ensure the reliable and efficient operation of the ejector under various conditions.

**Performance Requirements:**
*   The ejector **must** be stable at the specified Minimum Flow Rate for the pumped fluid. This is a mandatory operational requirement to prevent surging or unstable operation.

**Ejector Quantity Specification:**
When specifying ejectors, the quantity can be designated as either "Operating" or "Installed Spare." This distinction is important for procurement and operational planning, influencing redundancy and maintenance strategies.

These performance and quantity specifications are integral to the [[equipment_design_and_specification_4_prc_0003]] process.

**Performance and Stability Criteria:**
*   Ejector must be stable at Minimum Flow Rate.
