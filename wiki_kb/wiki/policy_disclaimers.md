# Group Travel Procedure Policy Disclaimers

The Policy System, nor any parts thereof, shall not be interpreted as constituting any form of representation or warranty by Aker Kvaerner ASA or any of the members of the Aker Kvaerner Group as to any level of performance or otherwise.

**Not a Contract:**
The Policy System, nor any parts thereof, shall not be interpreted as forming a contract or any part thereof (including an employment contract) entered into by a member of the Aker Kvaerner Group, unless otherwise expressly confirmed in writing by a duly authorised representative of such member of the Aker Kvaerner Group.
