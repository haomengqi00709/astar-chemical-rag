# ASME SFA-5.17 Carbon Steel Electrodes and Fluxes for Submerged Arc Welding

ASME SFA-5.17 is a specification that outlines the requirements for carbon steel electrodes and fluxes used in Submerged Arc Welding (SAW).

**Key Requirements:**
*   SAW electrodes and fluxes **must** satisfy the requirements of SFA-5.17.
*   The flux and wire combination used on production welds **must** be the same as that used for weld procedure qualification.
*   Wire spools **must** be tagged for easy shop identification.
*   Wire and flux combinations recommended for single pass welding **must not** be used for multiple pass welds.
*   A chemical analysis of the finished deposit **must** be included as part of the weld procedure qualification.

This standard ensures the quality and consistency of welding consumables for SAW processes, which is critical for the structural integrity of welded components.
