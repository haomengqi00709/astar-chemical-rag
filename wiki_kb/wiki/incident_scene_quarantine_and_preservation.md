# Incident Scene Quarantine and Preservation

Effective management of an incident scene is critical for thorough investigation and preventing further harm. This page outlines the mandatory requirements for incident scene quarantine and preservation, as detailed in [[0-prc-8013_hse_incident_reporting]].

**Quarantine Requirements**
In the event of any incident causing personal injury requiring greater attention beyond [[emergency_procedures_and_first_aid]] (i.e., a Recordable Incident) or a [[near_miss]] with the potential for such, the Area Supervisor/Manager **must** ensure the affected area is immediately quarantined. This quarantine is essential to preserve the scene for thorough investigation and to prevent further harm or contamination.

The quarantined area **must** remain undisturbed awaiting the arrival of specific personnel and authorities:
*   A member of the [[hse_department]].
*   Relevant Authorities and emergency services (e.g., Police, Paramedics, etc.) where appropriate, to conduct their assessments.

**Permissible Disturbance**
Disturbance of the accident site is only permissible in strictly defined circumstances. Specifically, disturbance is allowed only when it is absolutely necessary to prevent further harm to personnel, mitigate environmental damage, or prevent deterioration from unstable circumstances, such as a risk of explosion or structural collapse. Any such necessary disturbance **must** be documented thoroughly.
