# Emergency Response Overview - AKCTC Technology Centre

This procedure (Document ID: 0-PRC-8200) outlines the process for an orderly response to emergencies at the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Technology Centre (AKCTC), located at 8515 Eastlake Drive, Burnaby, B.C., V5A 4T7. The primary objective is to ensure the safety and well-being of all personnel and visitors during unforeseen events.

**General Preparedness Requirements:**
*   It is mandatory that all AKCTC personnel **must** become familiar with the location of all emergency equipment and facilities. This includes:
    *   Fire Extinguishers
    *   Safety Showers
    *   Eye-Wash Stations
    *   Respirators
    *   First Aid Cabinets
    *   First Aid Personnel
    *   Emergency Exits
*   Personnel **must** also know how to dial for outside help, specifically by dialing 9-1-1 directly for the Fire Department, Ambulance, and Police services.
*   The location of emergency items can be found on the AKCTC Floor Plan, which is prominently posted at the entrance to the Fax/Xerox nook.
