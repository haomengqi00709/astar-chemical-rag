# Piping Material Management and Identification

Effective management and identification of piping materials are crucial for preventing damage, loss, substitution, and ensuring efficient project execution.

**Material Storage and Control:**
All materials received at the construction site **must** be stored in a manner that prevents damage, loss, or pilferage. Storage practices **must** also facilitate easy identification of materials. The Piping Subcontractor **shall** implement an effective system to identify different materials and components to prevent unintended substitution during installation. Furthermore, the Subcontractor **must** institute effective practices to control the issue of piping materials from storage and to recover unused components (e.g., high alloy bolting, gaskets, spacers, fittings) issued to the work site.

**Material Identification:**
Specific identification methods are employed for various piping materials:
*   **Carbon Steel Piping:** Identified with a painted line extending the length of the pipe. The specific colors for carbon steel piping are detailed in the [[pipe_specification|pipe material specifications]].
*   **Fabricated Titanium Stub Ends:** These components are color-coded as indicated in the [[pipe_specification|pipe specification]].
*   **Gaskets:** Gaskets **will** be color-coded on their outside diameter surface to facilitate easy identification in the field. The specific [[gasket_colour_codes|gasket color coding]] is identified on the [[gasket_specification]]. Gaskets may also be pre-marked by the Supplier.

**Material Shortages:**
Pipe materials are supplied with sufficient quantities of construction and commissioning spares. The Piping Subcontractor **will** be solely responsible for any shortages of materials resulting from loss, theft, or fabrication error.

**Material Storage and Control (Continued)**
All materials received at the construction site **will** be stored to facilitate easy identification. The Piping Subcontractor **will** have an effective system to identify different materials and components to prevent substitution and **will** institute effective practices to control the issue of piping materials from storage. Furthermore, the Piping Subcontractor **will** institute effective practices to recover components issued to the work site (e.g., high alloy bolting, gaskets, spacers, fittings) which are not utilised.

**Material Identification**
*   **Carbon Steel Piping:** Identified with a painted line extending the length of the pipe. Colours for carbon steel piping are identified in the pipe material specifications.
*   **Titanium Stub Ends:** Fabricated titanium stub ends have been colour coded as indicated in the pipe specification.
*   **Gaskets:** Gaskets **will** be colour coded on the outside diameter surface to facilitate easy identification in the field. Gasket colour coding is identified on the gasket specification. Gaskets may be pre-marked by the Supplier.

**Material Supply and Responsibility**
Pipe materials have been supplied with sufficient quantities of construction and commissioning spares. The Piping Subcontractor **will** be solely responsible for shortages of materials due to loss, theft and fabrication error.
