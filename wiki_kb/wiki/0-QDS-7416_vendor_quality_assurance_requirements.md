# Vendor Quality Assurance Requirements (0-QDS-7416)

For Plate & Frame Heat Exchangers under document **0-QDS-7416**, the vendor's Quality Assurance (QA) program must adhere to specific requirements.

The vendor **shall** prepare a Quality Plan in accordance with [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment]].
The vendor's overall Quality Assurance program **shall** comply with the stipulations outlined in this Quality Data Sheet, **0-QDS-7416**, as well as with [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment]].

Chemetics surveillance for this equipment will be conducted at Level 0.0, as further detailed within this Quality Data Sheet. For specific surveillance points, refer to [[0-QDS-7416_chemetics_surveillance_level]].
