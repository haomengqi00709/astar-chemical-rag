# Bellows Inspection (5-PRC-0007)

The inspection of bellows is an essential part of the [[mechanical_checkout_piping_5_prc_0007|Piping Mechanical Checkout Procedure (5-PRC-0007)]], focusing on their correct installation, alignment, and integrity to accommodate thermal expansion and contraction.

**Required Documentation for Bellows Checkout:**
*   [[piping_and_instrumentation_drawings|P&ID]]
*   [[piping_speciality_item_specification|Specialty Item Specification]]
*   [[pipe_isometric|Isometric]]

**Bellows Inspection Steps:**
*   **Flange Checks:** All flange checks for bellows **shall** be performed as described in the [[flange_inspection_requirements_5_prc_0007|Flange Inspection Requirements]].
*   **Type and Tag Verification:** Confirm that the bellows type and tag number match the [[piping_and_instrumentation_drawings|P&ID]] and [[pipe_isometric|isometric]].
*   **Installed Length:** Measure the installed length of the bellows and confirm it against the specification.
*   **Misalignment Check:** Check visually for any misalignment of the bellows.
*   **Misalignment Limits:** Confirm that any observed bellows misalignment does not exceed the vendor's specified limits.
*   **Limit Bolt Settings:** Measure the limit bolt settings on tie rods and confirm that they match the specification.
*   **Expansion/Contraction Clearance:** Confirm that there is no interference that would restrict the [[bellows_installation|bellows expansion or contraction]].
*   **Sleeves:** Confirm that sleeves are present in the bellows, if applicable.
*   **Damage Assessment:** Confirm that the bellows do not have obvious damage such as punctures or cuts.
*   **Adjacent Pipe Support:** Confirm that the pipe is adequately supported adjacent to the bellows to prevent undue stress.

These inspections ensure the proper functioning of bellows as critical components for managing pipe movement and stress.
