# Standard Conditions for Purchase

Standard Conditions for Purchase are foundational commercial terms that are not specific to any particular project or order. These conditions provide a baseline for all procurement activities and are presented in one of two standardized forms: 0-SPC-5001 or 0-SPC-5002.

**Forms of Standard Conditions:**
*   **0-SPC-5001: Standard Conditions for Purchase (Short Form)**
*   **0-SPC-5002: Standard Conditions for Purchase (Long Form)**

Data available in SQL: 0-SPC-5001, 0-SPC-5002

**Application of Short Form (0-SPC-5001):**
The Short Form (0-SPC-5001) may be utilized under specific circumstances to streamline the procurement process for less complex or lower-value orders. These conditions include:
*   If the Order value is less than CAD 200,000.
*   If no specific conditions are imposed by the [[order_risk_assessment]] (referencing 0-PRC-5003: Bid Evaluation and Approval).
*   If no site work is involved in the scope of the order.

**Approval Process:**
All Standard Conditions documents undergo a rigorous approval process to ensure legal and procedural compliance:
*   Standard conditions are **checked** by the Director of Projects.
*   Standard conditions are **approved** by Legal.

**Document Issuance:**
Standard condition documents are typically issued for a project without modification. This practice aligns with the firm's policy on the approval and use of standards, as detailed in [[standard_document_management_procedure_0_prc_0001]] (referencing 0-PRC-0001: Approval and Use of Standards).

For project-specific or order-specific requirements, these standard conditions are supplemented by [[special_conditions_for_purchase]] and [[order_specific_conditions_for_purchase]].

**Forms of Standard Conditions:**
*   **0-SPC-5001: Standard Conditions for Purchase (Short Form)**: This form may be used if the Order value is less than CAD 200,000, if no conditions are imposed by the Order Risk Assessment (referencing 0-PRC-5003: Bid Evaluation and Approval), and if no site work is involved.
*   **0-SPC-5002: Standard Conditions for Purchase (Long Form)**

**Preparation and Approval:**
Standard conditions are checked by the Director of Projects and approved by Legal. These documents will be issued for the project without modification, in accordance with 0-PRC-0001: Approval and Use of Standards.
