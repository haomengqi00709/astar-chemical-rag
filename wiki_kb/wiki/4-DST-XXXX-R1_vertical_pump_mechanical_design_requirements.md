# Template Vertical Pump Mechanical Design Requirements

This page outlines the mechanical design requirements for the Template Vertical Pump Datasheet (4-DST-XXXX-R1). These specifications ensure the pump's structural integrity, operational reliability, and compliance with relevant standards under various [[site_design_conditions_1_dst_0001]]. The overall [[general_equipment_requirements_specification]] also applies.

**Key Mechanical Design Parameters:**

*   **Design Maximum Suction Pressure:** Measured in kPa(g). This defines the maximum pressure the pump's suction casing can withstand.
*   **Design Maximum Temperature:** Measured in oC. This specifies the maximum temperature the pump components are designed to tolerate.
*   **Design Code:** The applicable industry code or standard (e.g., ASME, API) governing the design and fabrication of the pump.
*   **Site Conditions:** Environmental and operational conditions specific to the installation site, including considerations for [[seismic_design_requirements]].
*   **Discharge Connection:** Specifications for the pump's discharge nozzle, including size, flange rating, and orientation.
*   **Electrical Area Classification:** The hazardous area classification (e.g., Class I, Division 1) for the pump's location, influencing electrical component selection. Refer to [[hazardous_area_classification_requirements]].
*   **Painting specification:** Details the required coating system for corrosion protection and aesthetic purposes, as per [[coatings_design_and_specification_7_prc_0002]].
*   **Total Noise Limit:** The maximum permissible noise level (e.g., in dB(A)) generated by the pump during operation.
*   **Vibration specification:** Defines acceptable vibration levels for the pump, often requiring [[vibration_analysis]] during testing.
*   **Impeller Size:** The diameter of the pump's impeller.
*   **Speed:** The rotational speed of the pump shaft, measured in RPM.
*   **Cover Plate to Tank Bottom:** This dimension, measured in mm, specifies the distance from the pump's cover plate to the bottom of the tank. Two values may be provided for different configurations or operating scenarios.
*   **Suction Port to Tank Bottom:** This dimension, measured in mm, specifies the distance from the pump's suction port to the bottom of the tank. Two values may be provided.

All mechanical design aspects **MUST** adhere to the [[general_mechanical_design_requirements_0_spc_0010]] and project-specific standards.
