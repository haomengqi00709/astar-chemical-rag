# Welding and Fabrication Requirements for Stabilized High Nickel Austenitic Stainless Steel - Overview

This document, identified as 4-SPC-0014, outlines the workmanship and quality requirements for welded fabrications made from Columbium Stabilized High Nickel Austenitic stainless steel. These fabrications are procured by or on behalf of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Scope and Applicability**
The standard applies when explicitly referenced in purchase orders and other related documents. It encompasses welding and other fabrication requirements for a wide range of equipment, including vessels, heat exchangers, tanks, piping, and various miscellaneous welded items. For unstabilized alloys, specific requirements are detailed in [[4-SPC-0014_unstabilized_austenitic_stainless_steel_appendix_a]].

**Document History and Administration**
This document is a direct copy of the original specification ES44/W02, titled "General Requirements for Welding High Nickel Austenitic Stainless Steels." Its usage must comply with [[0-PRC-0001_standard_document_management_procedure]]. There are plans to replace this document with a new version that fully adheres to the [[0-PRC-0004_document_style_and_format]].

Administrative changes, including a name change and the implementation of the [[2005_name_change_conversion_details]], have been applied without a new revision number. These changes have not been explicitly marked within the document.

**Definitions**
*   **Codes, etc.:** Shall mean the latest edition ruling at the date of contract.
*   **Agreement in Writing:** Means submission in writing by letter, not merely by inclusion in approval drawings.
*   **Inspector:** Refers to an Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Quality Assurance Inspector or another Authorized Representative.
