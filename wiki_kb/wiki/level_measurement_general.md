# Level Measurement - General

Level measurement is a critical aspect of process control, with various technologies available to suit different applications and fluid characteristics.

**Preferred and Alternative Methods:**
*   The preferred level measurement device for clean liquids **will be** Differential Pressure Level measurement. Detailed requirements for this method are found in [[level_measurement_differential_pressure|Level Measurement - Differential Pressure]].
*   Ultrasonic, radar, magneto-restrictive, or capacitance level transmitters **will** be used as applications require, especially for challenging fluids or vessel conditions. Specifics for non-contact methods are in [[level_measurement_non_contact|Level Measurement - Non-Contact]], and for contact methods and switches in [[level_measurement_contact_and_switches|Level Measurement - Contact and Switches]].

**Level Switch Devices:**
Level switch devices **will** utilize one or more of the following methods of measurement, depending on the application:
*   Side-entry float
*   Vibrating fork
*   Capacitance
*   Ultrasonic
*   Conductivity
*   Rotary type
*   Microwave

For specific requirements regarding level switches, particularly for shutdown service, refer to [[level_measurement_contact_and_switches|Level Measurement - Contact and Switches]].
