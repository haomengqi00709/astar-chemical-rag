# Task Safety Awareness (TSA) Roles and Responsibilities

Effective [[task_safety_awareness_overview]] (TSA) relies on clear roles and responsibilities for both supervisors and employees.

**Supervisor Responsibilities:**
*   A supervisor assigning a task **shall** identify necessary instructions and precautions derived from any prior [[0-PRC-8007_job_hazard_analysis]]. These instructions and precautions **shall** provide the maximum level of safety for each employee.
*   The site HSE manager or supervisor **will** review existing Job Hazard Analysis documents with employees prior to the start of a task.
*   Supervisors are explicitly responsible for **teaching** the employee how to perform a task safely.
*   In performing a TSA, the supervisor **must** demonstrate appropriate safety protocols.
*   The supervisor **will ensure** each employee thoroughly understands each safety instruction provided on tasks.
*   The supervisor **must ensure** employees have understood the safe working plan before commencing work.

**Employee Responsibilities:**
*   Employees **must** participate in Task Safety Awareness before beginning a new task.
*   Employees **will** proactively ask for a TSA review and instruction from their supervisor if such instruction was not given.
*   Employees **will** follow recommended safe practices.
*   Employees **will** perform work with due care and attention.
*   Employees **will** wear appropriate personal protection equipment (PPE) at all times.
*   Employees **will** analyze the task to be performed for hazards using the TSA method.
*   Each employee **will be instructed not to start** any work task without first discussing its potential safety hazards and precautions with their supervisor.

These responsibilities are reinforced through [[task_safety_awareness_training_and_orientation]] and are integral to [[task_safety_awareness_implementation_and_requirements]].

**Supervisor Responsibilities (continued):**
*   Instructions and precautions identified shall provide the maximum level of safety for each employee.
*   Supervisors are responsible to **teach** the employee how to perform a task safely.
*   In performing a TSA, the supervisor demonstrates appropriate safety protocols.

**Employee Responsibilities:**
*   Employees will ask for TSA review and instruction from their supervisor if such instruction was not given.
*   Employees will follow recommended safe practices.
*   Employees will perform work with due care and attention.
*   Employees will wear appropriate personal protection equipment at all times.
