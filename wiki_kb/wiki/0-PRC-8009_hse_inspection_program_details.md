# HSE Inspection Program Details

This page outlines the requirements for the HSE inspection program as defined in Document ID: 0-PRC-8009, covering types of inspections, scheduling, and frequency.

**Inspection Program Requirements**
Each worksite **SHALL** establish and maintain a program of regular inspections. This program **MUST** include:
*   A comprehensive check-list of items to be inspected for compliance with HSE regulations.
*   An inspection schedule that is appropriate for the specific risk level of the work being conducted.

**Types of Inspections**
Three primary types of inspections are mandated:
1.  **Specific Equipment Inspections**: These focus on individual pieces of equipment.
    *   Construction sites and research laboratories **SHALL** have regular inspection schedules for specific power equipment.
    *   Check-lists and schedules for equipment inspections **SHALL** be detailed in specific worksite safety procedures or equipment operating manuals.
    *   Equipment inspections **SHALL** be performed by the equipment operator or a specialized technician.
2.  **Worksite or Work Area Inspections**: These are conducted by worksite personnel to assess the general working environment.

**Establishing Inspection Frequency**
The frequency of inspections **SHALL** be determined by the [[0-PRC-8008_joint_health_and_safety_committee_procedure]] or by the person responsible for HSE at the worksite. Key considerations for establishing frequency **SHALL** include:
*   The risk level associated with the operation.
*   The number of personnel present at the location.
*   The established inspection frequency **MUST** meet or exceed the requirements set by the local Workers Compensation board or any other statutory regulations.

**Minimum Recommended Inspection Frequencies**
The following are the minimum recommended frequencies for worksite inspections:
*   **Construction Sites**: Once per week. For related safety information, see [[construction_site_safety_rules]].
*   **Workshops and Laboratories**: Once per month. For related safety information, see [[workshop_safety_rules]] and [[research_laboratory_safety_rules]].
*   **Design or Working Office Environment**: Every three months.
*   **Equipment Inspections**: The most frequent of that required by the equipment operating manual, local statutory regulations, or workplace safety rules.

The general method for conducting inspections involves a walk-about to identify hazards and hazardous conditions, guided by the inspection check-list. For details on reporting findings, refer to [[0-PRC-8009_hse_inspection_reporting_and_hazard_management]].
