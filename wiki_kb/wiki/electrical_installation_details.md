# General Electrical Installation Details

This page provides an overview of the general requirements and purpose of electrical installation detail drawings. These drawings serve as a guide for contractors during the installation of electrical equipment and components.

**Purpose and Content**
Detail drawings **shall** show the methods of installing electrical equipment and indicate the various components required for the overall installation. The details shown on these drawings are intended as a guide during the installation process. They **shall** include a full description of the materials and components necessary for the installation.

All information presented on detail drawings **shall** be useful and provide sufficient information for equipment installation. A small quantity of specific detail drawings, illustrating preferred methods of supply and installation, will be issued.

**Document Numbering and Usage**
Detail drawings **shall** be numbered according to 9-LST-0001, List Electrical Document Numbering Standard. Electrical installation details will be required for items not already identified and described, and for specific project-related instances.
