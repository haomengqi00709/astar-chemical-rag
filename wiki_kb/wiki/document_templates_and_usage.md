# Document Templates and Usage

Templates are essential tools for ensuring consistency and efficiency in document creation. This page outlines their definition and specific usage guidelines.

**Definition**
Templates are versions of documents that provide a fixed format within which project information will be placed.

**Standard Template**
The standard template for textual documents is 0-SPC-0XXX: [Template Textual Document (Portrait)].
