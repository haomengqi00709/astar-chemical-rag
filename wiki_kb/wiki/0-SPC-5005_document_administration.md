# Specification 0-SPC-5005 Administration

Specification 0-SPC-5005 outlines the minimum requirements for pre-shipment preparation and packaging of goods for marine transportation. This document describes the general requirements, while specific packaging details may be further elaborated on individual [[purchase_order_overview|Purchase Orders]].
