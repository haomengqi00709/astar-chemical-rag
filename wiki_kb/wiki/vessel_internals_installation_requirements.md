# Vessel Internals Installation Requirements

This page outlines general requirements and best practices for the installation of vessel internals in process equipment. Proper installation of these components is critical for the efficient operation, safety, and longevity of the vessel.

Vessel internals can include a variety of components such as dip pipes, stand pipes, baffles, vortex breakers, trays, packing supports, distributors, and mist eliminators. The specific requirements will vary depending on the vessel type, process conditions, and design specifications.

**General Installation Requirements:**
*   **Pre-Installation Inspection:** All vessel internals **must** be inspected for damage, cleanliness, and conformity to design specifications prior to installation. Any discrepancies **must** be reported and resolved.
*   **Cleanliness:** The vessel interior and all internals **must** be thoroughly cleaned and free from debris, foreign objects, and contaminants before and during installation.
*   **Alignment and Orientation:** All internals **must** be installed with correct alignment and orientation as specified in the engineering drawings and vendor manuals. Misalignment can lead to reduced performance or mechanical failure.
*   **Secure Fastening:** Components **must** be securely fastened using appropriate hardware (bolts, nuts, washers) and torque specifications. Locking mechanisms or anti-vibration measures **must** be applied where required.
*   **Welding and Bolting Procedures:** All welding and bolting operations **must** adhere to approved procedures and relevant codes. Weld inspections, including Non-Destructive Testing (NDT), **must** be completed as per project specifications.
*   **Clearances:** Adequate clearances between internals and the vessel wall, as well as between different internal components, **must** be maintained to allow for thermal expansion, maintenance, and proper fluid flow.
*   **Access and Maintenance:** Installation **must** consider future access for inspection, cleaning, and maintenance of internals.
*   **Material Compatibility:** Ensure that all installation materials (e.g., gaskets, fasteners) are compatible with the process fluids and vessel materials.
*   **Documentation:** Detailed records of the installation, including inspection reports, torque logs, and NDT results, **must** be maintained.

**Specific Considerations:**
*   **Dip Pipes and Stand Pipes:** Ensure correct length, orientation, and secure attachment to nozzles or internal supports.
*   **Baffles and Vortex Breakers:** Install according to design to prevent swirling and improve mixing or separation efficiency.
*   **Spray Nozzles:** Verify correct type, size, and orientation for optimal spray pattern and coverage.
*   **Mist Eliminators:** Install carefully to prevent damage to the delicate elements, ensuring proper sealing and support.
