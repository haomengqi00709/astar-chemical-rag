# Engineering Subcontract Agreement Preparation and Approval

The Engineering Subcontract Agreement is the foundational legal document for engaging external engineering services. Its preparation, revision, and final authorization follow a rigorous multi-stage process to ensure accuracy, compliance, and proper delegation of authority.

**Initial Preparation and Approval (Issued for Quotation):**
The Subcontract Administrator, with assistance from the Discipline Lead, is responsible for preparing the initial draft of the Engineering Subcontract Agreement. This agreement **must** be prepared strictly in accordance with [[form_of_engineering_subcontract]] (0-PRC-2202). Once drafted, the agreement will be published following [[publishing_of_project_documents]] (0-PRC-0005) and assigned the status “Issued for Quotation”. At this stage, the agreement **must** undergo a thorough review process, including checking by the [[project_engineer_pem_gate_review_responsibilities]] and Legal counsel. Final approval for the "Issued for Quotation" status is granted by the [[project_manager_pem_gate_review_responsibilities]].

**Revision and Approval (Approved for Design):**
Following negotiations with the successful Engineering Subcontractor, the Subcontract Administrator will revise the Engineering Subcontract Agreement as necessary. During this revision, it **must** be ensured that all references to other project documents are correct, including their revision numbers. Furthermore, the revisions **must** comply with any modifications agreed upon during the negotiation phase with the successful Engineering Subcontractor. The revised agreement will then be republished in accordance with [[publishing_of_project_documents]] (0-PRC-0005), with its status updated to “Approved for Design”. Similar to the initial draft, the revised agreement **must** be checked by the [[project_engineer_pem_gate_review_responsibilities]] and Legal, and then approved by the [[project_manager_pem_gate_review_responsibilities]].

**Final Authorization and Signing:**
Once the agreement reaches "Approved for Design" status, the Subcontract Administrator will print a paper copy. The Subcontract Administrator is responsible for arranging for this paper copy to be signed by the person authorized under Section 6 of the company's internal policies. The signed agreement **must** be presented together with a copy of the approved [[engineering_subcontractor_evaluation_0_prc_2204]] (0-PRC-2204: Engineering Subcontractor Evaluation and Approval) to ensure all necessary approvals are documented.

**Signing Authority:**
The authority for signing Engineering Subcontract Agreements is tiered based on the contract value:
*   For agreements with a value less than CAD 1,000,000, the Director of Engineering is authorized to sign.
*   For agreements with a value greater than CAD 1,000,000, the President is authorized to sign.

This structured approval and signing process ensures that all Engineering Subcontracts are legally sound, commercially viable, and properly authorized at the appropriate management level.

**Initial Preparation and Quotation Stage**
The Subcontract Administrator, with assistance from the Discipline Lead, prepares the Engineering Subcontract Agreement. This agreement is published in accordance with 0-PRC-0005: Publishing of Project Documents and assigned the status “Issued for Quotation”. At this stage, the agreement is checked by the Project Engineer and Legal, and subsequently approved by the Project Manager.

**Revision and Approval for Design**
The Subcontract Administrator revises the Engineering Subcontract Agreement as needed, ensuring all references to other documents are correct as to revision number and that the agreement complies with any modifications agreed during negotiations with the successful Engineering Subcontractor. The revised agreement is published in accordance with 0-PRC-0005: Publishing of Project Documents, with the status “Approved for Design”. It undergoes checking by the Project Engineer and Legal, and requires approval by the Project Manager.

**Finalization and Signing**
For finalization, the Subcontract Administrator prints a paper copy of the agreement and arranges for it to be signed by the person authorized under Section 6. The signed agreement must be presented alongside a copy of the approved Engineering Subcontractor Evaluation Summary Record (0-PRC-2204: Engineering Subcontractor Evaluation and Approval). The Subcontract Administrator then forwards the signed and authorized paper copy of the Engineering Subcontract to Document Control for issue to the Engineering Subcontractor.

Authority for signing agreements is tiered: for agreements under CAD 1,000,000, the Director of Engineering signs; for agreements exceeding CAD 1,000,000, the President signs.
