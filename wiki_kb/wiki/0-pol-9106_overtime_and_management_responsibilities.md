# Overtime Compensation and Management Responsibilities (0-POL-9106)

This section of policy 0-POL-9106 outlines the guidelines for overtime compensation and the specific responsibilities of management personnel in upholding the policy's provisions, particularly concerning staff levels on site.

**Overtime Compensation:**
All time worked in excess of 37.5 hours per week will be paid in accordance with the separate policy document, 0-POL-9113-R2 Compensation of Overtime. This ensures that employees are appropriately compensated for hours worked beyond the standard workweek, as defined in the [[0-pol-9106_normal_work_schedule]].

**Management Responsibilities:**
Department Managers, Project Managers, Construction Managers, and Commissioning Managers are explicitly **required** to manage in accordance with the provisions of policy 0-POL-9106. Their responsibilities include, but are not limited to, ensuring compliance with [[0-pol-9106_flexible_work_schedule]] guidelines and the specific rules for [[0-pol-9106_site_work_hours_and_rest_periods]].

Furthermore, these managers are **required** to ensure that appropriate staff levels are maintained on site. This responsibility is crucial for project execution, safety, and adherence to the maximum work hour limits and minimum rest periods stipulated for site assignments. These roles fall under the broader framework of [[authorization_roles]] within the firm.
