# Vendor Data Requirements (4-VDR-7540)

Document 4-VDR-7540 specifies the comprehensive list of data and documentation that the vendor **shall** provide for the fabrication of converter components. This document is referenced in [[4-SPC-7540_converter_fabrication_vendor_scope]] and is essential for project management, quality assurance, and record-keeping.

The vendor data requirements typically include, but are not limited to:
*   Fabrication drawings and detailed engineering designs.
*   Material certifications and traceability records.
*   Welding procedure specifications (WPS) and welder qualification records (PQR/WPQ).
*   Non-destructive examination (NDE) reports.
*   Inspection and test plans (ITP).
*   Hydrostatic test reports and other testing documentation.
*   Quality control records.
*   Packing lists and shipping documentation.
*   Final documentation packages, including "as-built" drawings.

Adherence to 4-VDR-7540 ensures that all necessary technical and quality information is submitted by the vendor in a timely and compliant manner.
