# Cooling Tower Water Specifications

The Water Specification section of the Template Cooling Tower Datasheet (Document ID: [[4-pds-xxxx-r1_template_cooling_tower_datasheet_administration|4-PDS-XXXX-R1]]) details the chemical and physical properties of the water circulating within the cooling tower system. These specifications are crucial for assessing the water's corrosive and scaling potential, guiding [[material_selection]] for tower components, and designing an effective water treatment program. Maintaining water quality is vital for the longevity and efficient operation of the cooling tower.

**Detailed Water Parameters (all in ppm unless otherwise specified):**
*   **Calcium (Ca):** Concentration of calcium ions. High levels contribute to calcium carbonate scaling.
*   **Chloride (Cl):** Concentration of chloride ions. High chloride levels can increase the corrosivity of the water, particularly for stainless steel components.
*   **Chlorine Residual:** The amount of free chlorine remaining in the water after disinfection. Important for controlling biological growth but can also be corrosive at high concentrations.
*   **Magnesium (Mg):** Concentration of magnesium ions. Contributes to hardness and can form magnesium silicate scale.
*   **Potassium (K):** Concentration of potassium ions. Generally less problematic than calcium or magnesium, but contributes to total dissolved solids.
*   **Sulphate (as SO4):** Concentration of sulphate ions. Can contribute to calcium sulphate scaling and increase corrosivity.
*   **B.O.D. (Biochemical Oxygen Demand):** A measure of the amount of oxygen required by aerobic microorganisms to decompose organic matter in a water sample. High B.O.D. indicates significant organic contamination, promoting biological growth and fouling.
*   **pH:** A measure of the acidity or alkalinity of the water. The pH range is critical for corrosion control, scale inhibition, and biocide effectiveness.
*   **Saturation Index (as pH - pHs):** The Langelier Saturation Index (LSI) or similar index, which predicts whether water is scale-forming (positive index), corrosive (negative index), or balanced (near zero).
*   **Conductivity:** The ability of water to conduct an electric current, measured in mS/cm. It is an indicator of the total concentration of dissolved ionized solids in the water.
*   **Total Hardness (as CaCO3):** The sum of calcium and magnesium concentrations, expressed as calcium carbonate equivalent. High hardness leads to scaling.
*   **Hardness (non-carbonate):** The portion of total hardness not associated with carbonate or bicarbonate ions. This can indicate the presence of other scale-forming ions.
*   **Dissolved Solids – Total:** The total concentration of all dissolved inorganic and organic substances in the water. High levels can lead to scaling and increased corrosivity.
*   **Suspended Solids – Total:** The total concentration of insoluble particles suspended in the water. High suspended solids can cause fouling, erosion, and act as sites for biological growth.
*   **Total alkalinity (as CaCO3):** The capacity of water to neutralize acid, primarily due to bicarbonate, carbonate, and hydroxide ions, expressed as calcium carbonate equivalent. Important for pH buffering and scaling potential.
*   **Total residue:** The total amount of solid material remaining after evaporation of a water sample.

Understanding and controlling these water specifications are paramount for the efficient and reliable operation of any cooling tower system, minimizing maintenance costs and maximizing equipment lifespan.
