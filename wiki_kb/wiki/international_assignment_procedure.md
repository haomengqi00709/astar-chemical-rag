# International Assignment Procedure

The International Assignment Procedure provides detailed guidelines for employees undertaking worldwide travel for mobilisation, demobilisation, and holidays related to overseas assignments and secondments. This procedure is referenced by the Group Travel Procedure (11.1 PR 2) for specific travel arrangements under these circumstances.

While the general [[air_travel_policy]] mandates economy class for most business travel, this dedicated procedure elaborates on the specific provisions and requirements for international assignments. It covers aspects such as travel class, allowances, and logistical support tailored to the unique needs of employees on long-term international postings.

Employees involved in international assignments or secondments **should** consult this procedure for comprehensive information regarding their travel entitlements and obligations.
