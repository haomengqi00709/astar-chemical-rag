# Electrical Equipment Identification Numbering System

The Electrical Equipment Identification Numbering System, as defined in procedure 9-PRC-0001, establishes a standardized approach for assigning identification numbers and names to all electrical equipment. This system is crucial for maintaining consistency across various project documentation and related numbering systems, such as cost codes and other document identifiers.

**General Requirements**

*   Standard identification numbers and names **shall** be utilized for all projects.
*   If specific Owner requirements necessitate an alternative numbering system, the project team **must** implement and maintain a dual system on the project equipment list to provide a clear cross-reference between the two systems.

**Numbering Format**

The electrical equipment number format is structured similarly to the general plant equipment identification system described in [[4-PRC-0001_plant_equipment_identification]]. The standard format is `XXYYE`, where:

*   `XX` represents the **Process Area**.
*   `YY` represents the **Serial Number** within that process area.
*   `E` is the **Identifier for Electrical Equipment**.

**Association with Plant Equipment**

For electrical equipment that is directly associated with a specific piece of plant equipment (e.g., motors, resistance heaters), the following rules apply:

*   The Process Area and Serial Number (`XXYY`) **shall** be identical to those assigned to the corresponding plant equipment. Refer to [[4-PRC-0001_plant_equipment_identification]] for plant equipment numbering.
*   The electrical equipment name **shall** be derived from the name of the associated plant equipment.

**Examples:**

*   Lixator Pump Motor 1016E is associated with Lixator Pump 1016P.
*   Brine Heater Element 1070E is associated with Brine Heater 1070H.

**Electrical Distribution Equipment**

For electrical distribution equipment that is not directly tied to a specific process area, the "Process Area" identifier **shall** be `99`.

**Identification of Duplicate Equipment**

When multiple pieces of identical equipment are installed, a suffix is used to differentiate them:

*   **Numeric Suffix (-1, -2):** Used for duplicate equipment that forms part of parallel processing systems.
*   **Letter Suffix (-A, -B):** Used for duplicate equipment located within a single processing system.
*   **Combined Suffixes (-1A, -1B):** May be used where appropriate to denote both parallel system and within-system duplication.

For a comprehensive list of specific electrical equipment identification codes, refer to [[electrical_equipment_identification_codes]]. The benefits of this consistent numbering system are further detailed in [[benefits_of_electrical_equipment_numbering]].
