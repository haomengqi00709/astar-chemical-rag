# Progressive Cavity Pump Material Specifications

The Progressive Cavity Pump Datasheet (Template `4-DST-XXXX-R0`) specifies the materials of construction for various critical components to ensure compatibility with the pumped fluid and operational longevity.

**Component Material Specifications:**
*   **Stator:** Material for the stationary part of the pump.
*   **Rotor:** Material for the rotating helical screw.
*   **Joint:** Material used for joints within the pump assembly.
*   **Shaft:** Material for the pump shaft.
*   **Lining:** Material for any internal lining required for corrosion or abrasion resistance.
*   **Seal Rotating Face - Inboard:** Material for the inboard rotating face of the shaft seal.
*   **Seal Stationary Face - Inboard:** Material for the inboard stationary face of the shaft seal.
*   **Seal Rotating Face - Outboard:** Material for the outboard rotating face of the shaft seal.
*   **Seal Stationary Face - Outboard:** Material for the outboard stationary face of the shaft seal.
*   **Seal Metal Parts:** Materials for metallic components of the shaft seal assembly.
*   **Seal Elastomers:** Materials for elastomeric components (e.g., O-rings, gaskets) of the shaft seal.

These material selections are crucial for the pump's resistance to corrosion, erosion, and chemical attack, ensuring reliable operation in the specified process conditions.
