# Shell and Tube Heat Exchanger Design and Process Requirements

This page details the mandatory design and process parameters that **must** be specified for Shell and Tube Heat Exchangers, as outlined in the Template S & T Heat Exchanger - Vdr Package Datasheet (4-DST-XXXX-R0). Adherence to these requirements ensures proper design, fabrication, and operation of the equipment.

**Mandatory Design and Process Specifications:**
The following parameters **must** be specified in the datasheet:
*   **Fluid Description:** Detailed description of the fluids on both hot and cold sides.
*   **Component Flows:** Individual component flow rates for each fluid stream.
*   **Hot Side Conditions:** All relevant process conditions for the hot fluid.
*   **Cold Side Conditions:** All relevant process conditions for the cold fluid.
*   **Orientation:** The physical orientation of the heat exchanger (e.g., horizontal, vertical).
*   **Flow Configuration:** The flow arrangement within the heat exchanger (e.g., counter-current, co-current).
*   **TEMA Style:** The specific TEMA (Tubular Exchanger Manufacturers Association) designation for the heat exchanger type.
*   **Hot Fluid:** Identification of the fluid flowing through the hot side.
*   **Shellside Flow:** Specification of the flow characteristics on the shell side.
*   **Removable Bundle:** Indication if the tube bundle is designed to be removable.
*   **Overpressure Protection (shell):** Details of overpressure protection for the shell side.
*   **Vacuum Protection (shell):** Details of vacuum protection for the shell side.
*   **Overpressure Protection (tube):** Details of overpressure protection for the tube side.
*   **Vacuum Protection (tube):** Details of vacuum protection for the tube side.
*   **Registration:** Any required regulatory registration details.
*   **Design Code:** The applicable design code (e.g., ASME, TEMA).
*   **Equipment Specification:** Reference to the relevant equipment specification document.
*   **General Equipment Requirements:** Any overarching requirements for the equipment.
*   **Site Conditions:** Specific environmental and operational conditions at the installation site. Refer to [[site_design_conditions_1_dst_0001]] for general site design parameters.
*   **Wind Design Code:** The code governing wind load design. Refer to [[wind_design_requirements]].
*   **Seismic Design Code:** The code governing seismic load design. Refer to [[seismic_design_requirements]].
*   **Electrical Area Classification:** Classification of hazardous electrical areas. Refer to [[electrical_area_classification_requirements]].
*   **Equipment Location:** The precise location where the equipment will be installed.
*   **Connection Type:** Types of connections required for piping and instrumentation.

**Additional Design Parameters:**
*   **Allowable Pressure Drop:** 23 kPa
*   **Quantity:** Can be specified as Operating or Installed Spare.

These requirements are crucial for ensuring the heat exchanger meets performance, safety, and regulatory standards. For broader mechanical design considerations, refer to [[general_mechanical_design_requirements_0_spc_0010]]. Quality assurance aspects related to shell and tube heat exchangers are detailed in [[0-QDS-7417_shell_and_tube_heat_exchanger_quality_data_sheet_administration]].
