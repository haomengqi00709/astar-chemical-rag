# ASTM A516

ASTM A516 is a standard specification for pressure vessel plates, carbon steel, for moderate- and lower-temperature service. This specification covers carbon steel plates primarily intended for service in welded pressure vessels where improved notch toughness is important.

**Key Characteristics:**
*   **Material:** Carbon steel.
*   **Application:** Specifically designed for pressure vessels operating at moderate and lower temperatures, requiring good notch toughness.
*   **Form:** Plates.
*   **Grades:** Available in various grades (e.g., 55, 60, 65, 70) indicating minimum tensile strength.

**Application in Tank Fabrication:**
*   Materials used in the manufacture of sulphuric acid storage tanks may reference ASTM A516 for plate components, especially for parts of the tank that might experience lower operating temperatures or require enhanced toughness.
