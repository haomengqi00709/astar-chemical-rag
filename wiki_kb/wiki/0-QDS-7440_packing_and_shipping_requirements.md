# Packing and Shipping Requirements for 0-QDS-7440

This page details the packing, crating, and shipping requirements, including associated documentation and surveillance, for items under Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7440.

**Item 15.0: Packing/Crating**
All items **must** be properly packed and crated to ensure their safe transport and delivery, protecting them from damage during transit.

**Item 16.0: Packaging/Tagging**
Surveillance by Chemetics for Packaging/Tagging **is required** to be Complete. This ensures that all packaging and tagging procedures comply with specified standards. No specific Quality Record type (AP or FP) is required for Packaging/Tagging itself, as it is an activity of surveillance.

**Item 17.0: Packing List**
A Packing List **is required** as an AP (As Prepared) quality record. This list **must** accurately detail the contents of each package. Packing List surveillance by Chemetics **is required** to be Complete, ensuring full verification of the packing list accuracy.

For definitions of AP and FP quality record types, refer to [[0-QDS-7440_quality_record_definitions]]. For general packing and shipping guidelines, refer to [[packing_land_transportation_0_spc_5004]] and [[packaging_marine_transportation_0_spc_5005]].
