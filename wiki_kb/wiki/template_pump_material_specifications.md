# Template Pump Material Specifications

This page details the required materials of construction for various components of a template pump, as specified in document 4-PDS-XXXX-R2. Proper material selection is critical to ensure the pump's durability, corrosion resistance, and compatibility with the process fluid and operating conditions outlined in the [[template_pump_process_design_conditions]].

The datasheet includes fields for specifying materials for the following key components:

*   **Casing:** The main body or housing of the pump, which contains the fluid and internal components.
*   **Impeller:** The rotating component that imparts velocity and pressure to the fluid.
*   **Shaft:** The rotating element that transmits power from the motor to the impeller or other pumping elements.
*   **Seal:** Components of the sealing system (e.g., mechanical seal faces, packing, O-rings) that prevent leakage along the shaft.
*   **Bushing:** A removable cylindrical lining used to reduce friction and wear, often found in bearings or shaft supports.
*   **Bearing:** Components that support the shaft and allow smooth rotation, reducing friction.
*   **Diaphragm:** For diaphragm pumps, the flexible membrane that moves the fluid.
*   **Valve ball:** For certain pump types (e.g., diaphragm pumps), the material of the check valve balls.
*   **Valve seat:** For certain pump types, the material of the valve seats, which provide a sealing surface for the valve ball or disc.
*   **Screw:** For screw pumps, the material of the helical screws that move the fluid.
*   **Gear:** For gear pumps, the material of the intermeshing gears that trap and displace fluid.
*   **Rotor:** The rotating part of a positive displacement pump (e.g., progressive cavity, lobe, gear pumps).
*   **Idler:** For gear pumps, the non-driven gear that meshes with the driven gear.
*   **Piston:** For piston pumps, the reciprocating component that displaces fluid.
*   **Hose/Tube:** For peristaltic pumps, the flexible hose or tube that is compressed to move fluid.
*   **Stator:** For progressive cavity pumps, the stationary helical component that forms cavities with the rotor.
*   **Wetted Parts:** A general category for all parts of the pump that come into direct contact with the process fluid.
*   **Other:** Any other specific components requiring material specification that are not covered by the above categories.

The selection of these materials is directly influenced by the [[template_pump_process_design_conditions]] and the [[template_pump_equipment_specifications]].
