# Hypo Unit Package Scrubber Unit Feed Specifications

This page outlines the specifications for the gas feed to the Scrubber Unit within the Hypo Unit Package.

**Fluid Description:**
*   The Scrubber Unit Gas Feed consists of Storage Tank Vent Sweeps.

**Composition (kg/h):**
*   Cl2 (Data available in SQL: Sheet1)
*   H2O (Data available in SQL: Sheet1)
*   Air (Data available in SQL: Sheet1)

**Process Conditions:**
*   **Temperature**: oC (Data available in SQL: Sheet1)
*   **Minimum Pressure**: kPa(g) (Data available in SQL: Sheet1)
*   **Normal Pressure**: kPa(g) (Data available in SQL: Sheet1)
*   **Maximum Pressure**: kPa(g) (Data available in SQL: Sheet1)

**Operational Notes:**
*   The Scrubber system will normally be exposed to non-Cl2 vent gases and caustic only.
*   During hypo production, gas from the bleach tower will contain Cl2 and produce some bleach in the Scrubber.
*   Allowances **must** be made to address [[material_selection|materials of construction]] in the Scrubber, or a small kickback **must** be added from the bleach unit to provide an oxidation passivator.

For details on the product output from the scrubber, refer to [[hypo_unit_package_vent_gas_specifications]]. The overall operational context is described in [[hypo_unit_package_operational_modes_and_emergency_conditions]].
