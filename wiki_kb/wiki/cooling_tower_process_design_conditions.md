# Cooling Tower Process Design Conditions

The Process Design Conditions section of the Template Cooling Tower Datasheet (Document ID: [[4-pds-xxxx-r1_template_cooling_tower_datasheet_administration|4-PDS-XXXX-R1]]) specifies the critical operational parameters required for the design, sizing, and performance evaluation of a cooling tower. These conditions are fundamental for ensuring the cooling tower meets the project's thermal and hydraulic requirements under various operating scenarios.

**Key Process Design Parameters:**
*   **Case Description:** A brief description of the specific operating scenario or design case being considered (e.g., normal operation, peak load, summer design, winter design).
*   **Design Basis:** The underlying assumptions and criteria used for the design calculations, referencing the overall [[process_design_criteria_document|process design basis]] for the plant.
*   **Quantity:**
    *   **Operating:** The number of cooling tower cells or units actively in service during normal operation.
    *   **Installed Spare:** The number of additional cells or units installed but kept in reserve for redundancy, maintenance, or future expansion.
*   **Duty:** The total heat rejection capacity required from the cooling tower, specified in kW. This is a primary indicator of the tower's thermal load.
*   **Water Flow Rate:**
    *   **Water Flow Rate:** The nominal volume of water circulated through the cooling tower, specified in m3/h. This dictates the hydraulic load on the system.
    *   **Minimum Flow Rate:** The lowest acceptable water flow rate (in m3/h) to maintain efficient operation and proper water distribution across the fill media, preventing dry spots or uneven cooling.
*   **Water Inlet Temperature:** The temperature of the hot water entering the cooling tower from the process, specified in oC.
*   **Water Outlet Temperature:** The target temperature of the cooled water exiting the tower, specified in oC. The difference between inlet and outlet temperatures defines the cooling range.
*   **Air Wet Bulb Temperature:**
    *   **Summer:** The design wet bulb temperature of the ambient air (in oC) for summer conditions. This is a critical parameter as it represents the theoretical lowest temperature to which water can be cooled by evaporation and significantly impacts tower sizing.
    *   **Winter:** The design wet bulb temperature of the ambient air (in oC) for winter conditions. This helps in evaluating potential for freezing and minimum achievable cooling water temperatures.

These parameters collectively define the performance envelope of the cooling tower and are essential inputs for vendor proposals and detailed engineering calculations.
