# Electrical Systems Field Checkout (9-PRC-0004)

This document, 9-PRC-0004, outlines the procedure for conducting field checkout inspections and tests for electrical systems within the Project. It details the steps to ensure that all electrical equipment, wiring, and connections are correctly installed and safe for operation.

Inspections and tests for [[mechanical_completion_testing_and_defect_management]] are conducted in accordance with this procedure. It includes associated checklists to record the completion of these critical checks.
