# Purchase Order Roles and Responsibilities

Within the framework of this Purchase Order (PO), specific roles are defined with corresponding responsibilities to ensure the smooth execution of the agreement. The primary parties involved are Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Vendor, who **must** act in a spirit of mutual trust and cooperation as stated in the Purchase Order.

**Key Roles and Their Actions:**
*   **Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, the Vendor, the Project Buyer, the Inspector, and the Lead Engineer** **must** act as specified in this Purchase Order.
*   **The Parties** are Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Vendor.
*   **Others** are defined as individuals or organizations outside of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, the Project Buyer, the Lead Engineer, the Inspector, the Vendor, the Owner, or any employee or Supplier of the Vendor.
*   **A Supplier** is a person or entity contracted by the Vendor to provide a portion of the [[purchase_order_definitions#The Goods and Services|Goods and Services]]. The Purchase Order applies as if a Supplier’s employees and equipment were the Vendor's.

**Delegation and Replacement:**
*   The **Project Buyer, Inspector, and Lead Engineer** may delegate any of their actions after notifying the Vendor. They also retain the right to cancel any such delegation. A reference to an action includes an action performed by a delegate.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may replace the **Project Buyer, Inspector, or Lead Engineer** after notifying the Vendor of the replacement's name.

**Supplier Management:**
*   The **Vendor** **must** submit the name of each proposed Supplier to the Project Buyer for acceptance, unless the Project Buyer has explicitly instructed that no submission is required.
*   The **Project Buyer** may reject a proposed Supplier if their appointment is deemed to prevent the Vendor from [[purchase_order_definitions#To Provide the Goods and Services|Providing the Goods and Services]] in accordance with the Purchase Order.
*   The **Vendor** **must not** appoint a proposed Supplier until the Project Buyer has formally accepted them.

**Not Used Clauses:**
*   Clause 15.3 is explicitly stated as "Not Used" within this Purchase Order.
