# Subcontractor Site Obligations and Compensation Events (0-SPC-2202-2)

This section details the mandatory obligations and procedures that subcontractors **must** comply with when performing services on site, as stipulated in Annexure One of Specification **0-SPC-2202-2**. Adherence to these requirements is fundamental to the successful and compliant execution of subcontracted work.

**Mandatory Subcontractor Obligations**

The Subcontractor **shall** comply with all obligations and procedures detailed in Annexure One, which specifically outlines the requirements for performing services on site. These obligations encompass a wide range of operational and administrative aspects, ensuring a structured and safe working environment. Key areas of compliance include:

*   **Compliance with all Legislative Requirements:** Subcontractors **must** adhere to all applicable laws, regulations, and statutory provisions relevant to the services performed on site.
*   **Management Plans/Industrial Relations Strategy/Health and Safety Management:** This includes the implementation and adherence to approved management plans, industrial relations strategies, and comprehensive [[hse_management_and_employee_responsibilities|Health and Safety Management]] protocols. For detailed HSE requirements, refer to [[hse_construction_sites]].
*   **Quality Assurance:** Subcontractors **must** maintain and demonstrate robust [[quality_management_and_compliance|Quality Assurance]] processes throughout the execution of services.
*   **Site Working Hours:** Compliance with established [[0-pol-9106_site_work_hours_and_rest_periods|Site Working Hours]] is mandatory to ensure coordination and minimize disruption.
*   **Clean Up:** Subcontractors are responsible for maintaining a clean and organized work area and performing necessary clean-up operations.
*   **Cultural Heritage Requirements:** Adherence to procedures and requirements related to the protection and management of [[environmental_and_site_conditions|cultural heritage]] on site.
*   **Community Relations:** Maintaining positive [[project_communication_management|community relations]] and adhering to established protocols for interaction with local communities.
*   **Security:** Compliance with site [[construction_site_management_roles_0_prc_600|security]] procedures and regulations.
*   **Construction Area:** Adherence to rules and guidelines pertaining to the designated [[construction_site_management_roles_0_prc_600|construction area]].
*   **Constructional Plant and Vehicles:** Proper management, operation, and maintenance of [[construction_site_management_roles_0_prc_600|constructional plant and vehicles]] on site.
*   **Induction:** All subcontractor personnel **must** complete the required [[hse_new_employee_orientation|site induction]] processes before commencing work.
*   **Access to Works on Site:** Compliance with established procedures for [[construction_site_management_roles_0_prc_600|access to works on site]].

For a more detailed breakdown of these obligations, refer to [[0-SPC-2202-2_table_1_subcontractor_obligations_details]].

**Site Access and Compensation Events**

Any delay experienced by the Subcontractor in obtaining access to the Site is explicitly stated as **not** constituting a breach of the Subcontract by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. However, such a delay **is** recognized as a [[engineering_subcontract_compensation_events|compensation event]]. This distinction is crucial for managing project timelines and financial implications, allowing for appropriate adjustments without implying a contractual default by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
