# Final Inspections (0-QDS-7415)

This section outlines the final inspection requirements for the Dual Laminate Tank project, as specified in 0-QDS-7415. Final Inspection is a critical item or activity to ensure the completed tank meets all specifications before shipment.

**Dimensional Inspection:**
*   **Quality Record:** Dimensional inspection reports are mandatory quality records and **must** have a Quality Record status of FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package).
*   **Surveillance:** Surveillance by Chemetics for dimensional inspection **must** involve both "Witness" and "Hold" points.
    *   **Witness:** Chemetics personnel **must** be present to observe the dimensional inspection.
    *   **Hold:** Work **must** not proceed beyond this point until Chemetics has reviewed and approved the witnessed inspection.

**Surfaces Free From Defects Inspection:**
*   **Quality Record:** Inspection reports confirming surfaces are free from defects are mandatory quality records and **must** have a Quality Record status of FP.
*   **Surveillance:** Surveillance by Chemetics for this inspection **must** involve both "Witness" and "Hold" points.
    *   **Witness:** Chemetics personnel **must** be present to observe the inspection for surface defects.
    *   **Hold:** Work **must** not proceed beyond this point until Chemetics has reviewed and approved the witnessed inspection.

**Cleanliness Inspection:**
*   **Surveillance:** Surveillance by Chemetics for cleanliness inspection **must** involve both "Witness" and "Hold" points.
    *   **Witness:** Chemetics personnel **must** be present to observe the cleanliness inspection.
    *   **Hold:** Work **must** not proceed beyond this point until Chemetics has reviewed and approved the witnessed inspection.

**Review QA Documentation:**
*   **Surveillance:** Surveillance by Chemetics for the review of Quality Assurance Documentation **must** involve both "Witness" and "Hold" points.
    *   **Witness:** Chemetics personnel **must** be present during the review of the QA documentation package.
    *   **Hold:** Final release **must** not be granted until Chemetics has reviewed and approved the documentation.

These final inspections are essential for verifying the overall quality and readiness of the dual laminate tank. For general quality record definitions, refer to [[0-QDS-7415_quality_record_definitions]].
