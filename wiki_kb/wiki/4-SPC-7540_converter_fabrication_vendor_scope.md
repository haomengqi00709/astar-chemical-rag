# Converter Fabrication Vendor Scope (4-SPC-7540)

The vendor's scope of supply and responsibilities for the fabrication of converter components, as defined by specification 4-SPC-7540, are detailed below.

**Mandatory Supply Items:**
*   Vendor **shall** supply all stainless steel and carbon steel components of the converter, including the shell, heads, nozzles, internals, and other specified parts.
*   Vendor **shall** supply non-standard field erection items. These include, but are not limited to, grid/baffle installation braces, tier bracing beams, and temporary lifting lugs.
*   Vendor **shall** supply material for field weld run off tabs. This material **shall** be of the same specification and grade as the parent material to ensure compatibility and weld integrity.

**Mandatory Services and Documentation:**
*   Vendor **shall** perform trial fitting, match marking, and tagging of components as described in the document. This ensures proper alignment and identification during field erection.
*   Vendor **shall** perform all necessary packaging and preparation for shipment, adhering to specified standards to protect components during transit.
*   Vendor **shall** provide all documentation listed on [[4-VDR-7540_vendor_data_requirements]]. This includes, but is not limited to, fabrication records, material certifications, and inspection reports.

For details on items not included in the vendor's supply, refer to [[4-SPC-7540_converter_fabrication_items_supplied_by_others]]. Fabrication tolerances and assembly requirements are further elaborated in [[4-SPC-7540_converter_fabrication_tolerances]] and [[4-SPC-7540_shop_fabrication_and_assembly]], respectively.

In addition to the primary converter components, the vendor's supply scope includes:
*   Non-standard field erection items such as grid / baffle installation braces, tier bracing beams, and temporary lifting lugs.
*   Material for field weld run off tabs. This material shall be of the same specification and grade as the parent material.

The vendor is also responsible for performing packaging and preparation for shipment.
