# Template Boiler Ancillary Equipment

This page lists the various ancillary equipment options that can be specified for a boiler system, as detailed in the [[template_boiler_datasheet_administration|Template Boiler Datasheet (4-PDS-XXXX-R1)]]. This equipment supports the main boiler unit, ensuring its efficient and safe operation.

**Ancillary Equipment Options**
The following components can be included as part of the boiler package:
*   **Fan:** Used for providing combustion air (forced draft) or exhausting flue gases (induced draft). For general fan specifications, refer to [[fan_datasheet_administration]].
*   **Deaerator:** Removes dissolved gases (primarily oxygen and carbon dioxide) from boiler feed water to prevent corrosion.
*   **Blowdown Receiver:** Collects and cools blowdown water from the boiler before disposal or recovery.
*   **Economizer:** A heat exchanger that recovers waste heat from the boiler flue gases to preheat the boiler feed water, improving efficiency.
*   **Stack:** The chimney or exhaust duct for discharging flue gases to the atmosphere. For general stack specifications, refer to [[stack_design_parameters]].
*   **Feed Pumps:** Pumps responsible for delivering feed water to the boiler at the required pressure.
*   **Sample Coolers:** Used to cool boiler water samples to a safe temperature for analysis.
*   **Chemical Dosing System:** Introduces chemicals into the boiler water to control pH, prevent scale, and inhibit corrosion.
*   **Other:** Any additional equipment not explicitly listed above.

For details on the materials of construction for these components, refer to [[template_boiler_materials_of_construction]].
