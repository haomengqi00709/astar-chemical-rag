# Quality Management and Compliance

The project **must** be executed in strict accordance with 0-PRC-7003: Quality Plan. All documents referenced by this Quality Plan are comprehensively listed in Data available in SQL: 0-LST-700.

**Oversight and Training:**
The Manager of Project Controls holds a critical oversight role, responsible for:
*   Conducting periodic audits and reporting on project compliance.
*   Ensuring that all project personnel receive proper and adequate training relevant to their roles.

**Project Responsibilities:**
Personnel assigned to positions of responsibility within the project are listed in Data available in SQL: 0-LST-2004. This document details the specific roles and accountability for various project functions.
