# Delay Damages in Commercial Terms

Delay damages are a critical component of [[order_specific_conditions_for_purchase]], designed to compensate Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for losses incurred due to late delivery of Goods and Services by the Vendor. These damages are applied to ensure timely project execution and mitigate financial impact from delays.

**Application of Delay Damages:**
*   The Vendor **shall** pay delay damages for each part of the Goods and Services that is not delivered by the agreed-upon Delivery Date.
*   The delay damages rate is stated from the Delivery Date for each day until actual Delivery is achieved.

**Calculation of Delay Damages:**
*   The rate of delay damages may be expressed as a percentage (%) of the Price of the Goods and Services.
*   If expressed as a percentage, the % of Price for delay damages **must** be a genuine estimate of the damage suffered by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. This ensures that the damages are a fair reflection of actual losses and are not punitive.
*   A fixed monetary sum is generally preferable for delay damages, as it provides greater clarity and avoids potential disputes over percentage calculations.
