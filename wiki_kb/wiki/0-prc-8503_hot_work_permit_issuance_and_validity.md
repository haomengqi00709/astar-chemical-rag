# Hot Work Permit Issuance and Validity (0-PRC-8503)

The issuance and management of Hot Work Permits are critical steps in ensuring safety during commissioning activities, as detailed in [[0-prc-8503_hot_work_permit_procedure_overview]]. This page outlines when a permit is required, the process for its completion, and its validity conditions.

**When a Permit is Required:**
A "Hot Work Permit" is required when using any equipment, tools, or apparatus capable of generating heat, sparks, or flames in any restricted areas. Examples of hot work requiring a permit include:
*   Welding
*   Burning
*   Cutting
*   Sawing
*   Grinding
*   Drilling
*   Open flame operations
*   Use of non-explosion proof electrical equipment
*   Sandblasting operation of internal combustion engines
*   Hot tap operations

Form [[dp-802.702_hot_work_permit]] is a sample Hot Work Permit form.

**Specific Requirements for Welding and Hot Taps:**
*   A job-specific written procedure **must** be prepared and approved before any work starts that requires welding connections or additions to piping, vessels, and tanks containing flammable liquids, gas, or combustible materials under pressure.
*   This regulation is NOT applicable for the execution of hot tap activities as a standalone procedure. A Hot Work permit is not a substitute for an approved hot tap procedure. However, all hot taps **require** a Hot Work permit.
*   For detailed discussion of hot taps, refer to API publication 2201: Procedures for Welding or Hot Tapping on Equipment Containing Flammables.

**Pre-Permit Requirements:**
*   No hot work permit **will** be issued until the [[0-prc-8503_hot_work_permit_roles_and_responsibilities|Commissioning HSE Manager]] completes a [[task_safety_awareness_implementation_and_requirements|Task Safety Awareness (TSA)]] review.
*   The completed TSA **must** be posted at the location of the work. Form [[dp-802.306_task_safety_awareness_form]] is the Task Safety Awareness form.

**Permit Completion and Documentation:**
*   Every question on the permit form **must** be answered in ink.
*   Where any item is not applicable, it **will** be marked thus (e.g., N/A) and not left blank.
*   The person executing the work **will** sign the permit.
*   The form **will** be completed in duplicate.
*   The form **will** be completed in the English language and, if necessary, in the local language.
*   The hard copy of the permit is to be displayed at the work site.
*   The soft copy of the permit is to be retained in the commissioning office until the work has been completed.
*   On completion of the work, the bottom portion of the permit **shall** be completed by the person executing the work.
*   The completed permit **shall** be returned to the point of issue.

**Permit Validity:**
*   The permit becomes invalid if work does not start within one hour of issue.
*   The permit becomes invalid if there is a break in the work duration.
*   A permit is not valid for more than one shift, unless authorized by the [[0-prc-8503_hot_work_permit_roles_and_responsibilities|Commissioning HSE Manager]].
*   The duration of the Hot Work Permit **shall not** exceed 12 hours.
*   A new permit is to be issued if the job/task duration exceeds 12 hours.

This procedure is closely related to the general [[0-PRC-8501_safe_work_permit_procedure_overview]].

**Permit Requirements:**
*   The Commissioning HSE Manager and the Work Supervisor must sign a valid permit for this program.
*   Examples of hot work requiring a permit include welding, burning, cutting, sawing, grinding, drilling, open flame operations, non-explosion proof electrical equipment, sandblasting operation of internal combustion engines, and hot tap operations.
*   All hot taps require a Hot Work permit.
*   No hot work permit will be issued until the Commissioning HSE Manager completes a Task Safety Awareness (TSA) review.

**Permit Completion:**
*   Every question on the permit form must be answered in ink. Where any item is not applicable, it will be marked thus and not left blank.
*   The person executing the work will sign the permit. By signing the permit, the person executing the work signifies that he and his crew fully understand and will honor the conditions spelled out on the permit.
*   The form will be completed in duplicate.
*   The form will be completed in the English language and if necessary in the local language.
*   The hard copy of the permit is to be displayed at the work site.
*   The soft copy of the permit is to be retained in the commissioning office until the work has been completed.
*   On completion of the work, the bottom portion of the permit shall be completed by the person executing the work.
*   The completed permit shall be returned to the point of issue.
