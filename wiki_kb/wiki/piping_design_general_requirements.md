# Piping Design General Requirements (5-PRC-0002)

This document (5-PRC-0002) outlines the general requirements for the design of piping systems within the [type of] Plant of the [project name] Project.

**Compliance and Standards**
All piping design, construction, and operation **must** comply with the Statutory Requirements of [city, province, country]. The design, specification, and manufacture of all piping **must** adhere to the latest edition of applicable North American Codes and Standards, with [[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping]] being most commonly applicable.

**Related Procedures**
The following procedures are applicable to piping design:
*   [[0-PRC-7003_project_quality_management_plan_overview]]
*   [[5-PRC-0003_piping_design_and_specification]]

**Standards Updates**
Applicable Aker Kvaerner Chemetics standard specifications **must** be updated to incorporate all requirements of these design criteria.
