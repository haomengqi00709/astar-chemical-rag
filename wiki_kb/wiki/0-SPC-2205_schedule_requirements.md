# Engineering Subcontract Schedule Requirements (0-SPC-2205)

This page details the requirements for the Subcontractor's schedule under document 0-SPC-2205, ensuring clear planning and progress tracking for the engineering services. For general information on engineering subcontract activity schedules, refer to [[engineering_subcontract_activity_schedule]].

**Schedule Submission:**
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **may** require the Subcontractor to submit a Schedule.
*   If requested by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, the Subcontractor **must** submit a revised Schedule to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for acceptance each month after the starting date of the services.

**Mandatory Schedule Content:**
The Schedule **must** clearly show the following information:
*   The starting date of the services.
*   The [[0-SPC-2205_general_conditions_and_administration#Key Definitions|Completion Date]].
*   All dates stated in the [[0-SPC-2205_general_conditions_and_administration#Key Definitions|Scope]] that constrain the timing of the Subcontractor’s work.
*   The planned [[0-SPC-2205_general_conditions_and_administration#Key Definitions|Completion]] of the services.
*   The planned order and timing of the operations that the Subcontractor intends to perform to Provide the Services.
*   The planned order and timing of the work of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and [[0-SPC-2205_general_conditions_and_administration#Key Definitions|Others]], either as specified in the [[0-SPC-2205_general_conditions_and_administration#Key Definitions|Scope]] or as subsequently agreed with them by the Subcontractor.
*   The dates when the Subcontractor plans to complete work necessary to enable Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and [[0-SPC-2205_general_conditions_and_administration#Key Definitions|Others]] to perform their respective tasks.
*   The dates when the Subcontractor will require access to a person, place, or thing to Provide the Services in accordance with their Schedule.
*   The dates when the Subcontractor will require information and items to be provided by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics to Provide the Services in accordance with their Schedule.
*   The dates when the Subcontractor will require approval from [[0-SPC-2205_general_conditions_and_administration#Key Definitions|Others]] to Provide the Services in accordance with their Schedule.
*   Any other information that the [[0-SPC-2205_general_conditions_and_administration#Key Definitions|Scope]] requires the Subcontractor to include on a Schedule submitted for acceptance.
