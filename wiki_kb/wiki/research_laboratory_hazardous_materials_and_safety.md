# Research Laboratory Hazardous Materials and Safety

Safety in the Research Laboratory, particularly at the Technology Centre, requires stringent protocols for handling hazardous materials, managing experiments, and ensuring equipment safety.

**Hazardous Materials Communication Plan**
A comprehensive Hazardous Materials Communication Plan is in place. This plan includes:
*   **Workplace Hazardous Materials Information System (WHMIS):** WHMIS is an integral part of the plan, providing information on hazardous products used in the workplace.
*   **Material Safety Data Sheets (MSDS):** MSDS documents, and their readily accessible location, are a key component of communicating hazard information.

**Hazardous Materials Management**
Effective management of hazardous materials involves:
*   **Spill Management and Prevention:** Procedures for preventing and managing chemical spills.
*   **Safe Storage:** Guidelines for the safe storage of chemicals.
*   **Waste Disposal:** Protocols for the proper disposal of chemical waste.

**Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)**
The requirements, proper use, and care of Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) **must** be known by all laboratory personnel.

**Working Alone Procedures**
*   Procedures for working alone in the laboratory **are required**.
*   A minimum staffing rule exists to ensure adequate support and safety in the laboratory environment.

**Experiment Safety Management**
Rigorous safety management is applied to experiments, including:
*   **HAZOP (Hazard and Operability Studies):** Conducted to identify and mitigate potential hazards.
*   **Decommissioning:** Safe procedures for decommissioning experiments and equipment.
*   **Unattended Experiments:** Specific protocols for managing experiments that run unattended.
*   **Pressure and Vacuum Operations:** Safety considerations and procedures for operations involving pressure and vacuum.

**Equipment Safety**
Safety protocols extend to all equipment used in the laboratory, covering:
*   **Glassware:** Safe handling and use of glassware.
*   **Flexible Tubing:** Proper selection, installation, and maintenance of flexible tubing.
*   **Electrical Equipment:** Safe use and maintenance of electrical apparatus.
*   **Workshop Tools:** Safe operation of workshop tools used within the laboratory context.
