# Tantalum Alloy Material Requirements

This section details the specific tantalum alloys and suppliers approved for use in equipment and vessel fabrication, as per specification [[4-SPC-0045_tantalum_alloy_welding_specification_overview|4-SPC-0045]].

*   **Approved Alloys**: Only tantalum 2.5% tungsten alloy with UNS number R05252 (Ta 2.5% W) is approved for use. Other tantalum alloy grades listed in this specification are also approved for use, provided they meet the specified requirements.
*   **Approved Suppliers**: The approved suppliers for UNS R05252 are H.C. Starck and Cabot Corporation.
*   **Alternative Alloys/Suppliers**: Any alternative alloys or suppliers **must** receive prior written approval from [[chemetics_aker_kvaerner_company_details|Aker Kvaerner Chemetics]].
*   **Tube Conformance**: All tubes **must** conform to [[ASTM B521]] (latest edition).

For general guidance on [[material_selection]], refer to relevant project specifications.
