# Ejector Process Design Conditions

This page details the key process design conditions for ejector packages, encompassing both pumped and motive fluid parameters. These conditions are critical for the proper sizing, selection, and operation of ejector systems. For specific cooling water requirements, refer to [[ejector_cooling_water_specifications]], and for evacuation parameters, see [[ejector_evacuation_requirements]].

**Pumped Fluid Conditions:**
The following parameters define the characteristics and flow requirements of the fluid being pumped by the ejector:
*   **Flow Rate:** Measured in kg/h or Am3/h.
*   **Minimum Flow Rate:** Measured in kg/h.
*   **Maximum Flow Rate:** Measured in kg/h.
*   **Temperature:** Measured in oC.
*   **Inlet Pressure:** Measured in kPa(a).
*   **Discharge Pressure:** Measured in kPa(a).
*   **Density:** Measured in kg/m3.
*   **Viscosity:** Measured in cP.

**Motive Fluid Conditions:**
The motive fluid, typically steam or another high-pressure gas, drives the ejector. Its conditions are specified as:
*   **Flow Rate:** Measured in kg/h.
*   **Operating Temperature:** Measured in oC.
*   **Superheat:** Measured in oC.
*   **Maximum Temperature:** Measured in oC.
*   **Operating Pressure:** Measured in kPa(g).
*   **Maximum Pressure:** Measured in kPa(g).
*   **Density:** Measured in kg/m3.

For details on the mechanical design and materials of construction for ejectors, refer to [[ejector_mechanical_design_conditions]].
