# Protective Coating Specification (7-SPC-0005) Overview

This Engineering Standard, 7-SPC-0005, defines the minimum requirements for the shop application of Yellow Jacket coatings and the field application of Polyken tape to joints and fittings. It applies when explicitly called for on a purchase order or referenced in other project documents.

In cases where end-user or consultant specifications are also applicable, the more restrictive condition **will apply**.

**Contractor Responsibilities**
The contractor **will be responsible** for all requirements related to:
*   The supply of coated pipe.
*   The installation of coated pipe.
*   The welding of coated pipe.
*   The testing of coated pipe.
*   The coating of fittings.
*   The testing of fittings.
*   Backfilling operations.

**Potential Exclusions from Contractor Scope**
Depending on specific project requirements, the responsibility for excavation and the supply of fittings may lie with other parties.
