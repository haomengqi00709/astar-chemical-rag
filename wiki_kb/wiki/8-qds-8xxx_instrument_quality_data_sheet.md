# Instrument Quality Data Sheet (8-QDS-8XXX)

The Quality Data Sheet (QDS) (8-QDS-8XXX) specifies the quality assurance and documentation requirements for instruments. It refers to [[vendor_quality_assurance_0_prc_7005]].

**For Field Instruments:**
*   **Prepared by:** Field Instrument Engineer (FIE)
*   **Approved by:** Quality Assurance

The FIE will provide a "8-QDS-8XXX: Quality Data Sheet" form (QDS) for each instrument type (for Field Instruments). The QDS (for Field Instruments) **shall** as a minimum include all pertinent Calibration, Testing, Compliance, Hazardous Area certificates, and Cleaning certificates as applicable. Any special testing will be included on the QDS.

**For Instrument Bulk Material:**
*   **Prepared by:** Detail Design Engineer (DDE)
*   **Approved by:** Quality Assurance

The DDE will provide a "8-QDS-8XXX: Quality Data Sheet" form (QDS) (for Instrument Bulk Material). The QDS (Quality Data Sheet) **must** include all pertinent Calibration, Testing, Compliance, Hazardous Area certificates, and Cleaning certificates as applicable. Any special testing will be included on the QDS.
