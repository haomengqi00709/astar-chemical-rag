# Dry Chlorine Piping - Material Handling and Storage (5-SPC-0101)

Effective material handling and storage practices are critical for maintaining the integrity and safety of dry chlorine piping components.

**General Storage Requirements**
All materials received at the plant site **shall** be stored in a manner that prevents damage and facilitates identification. The erector **shall** use an effective system to identify different materials and components, and to prevent substitution. This is particularly important when dealing with Low Temperature Carbon Steel piping.

The erector **shall** institute effective practices to:
*   Control the issue of piping materials from storage.
*   Recover components issued to the work site that are not utilized for the work in hand.

**Storage of Pipe Spools and Components**
Careful storage of any pipe spools not yet installed in the piping system **must** be observed. All pipe stubs **should** be covered with plastic sheets to minimize ingress of contaminants. All pipes, fittings, and inline items **should** be stored in a covered dry area prior to installation.

For instruments, valves, and other inline items designated for "Dry Chlorine" piping:
*   All vendor-supplied protective packaging **must** be maintained upon receipt at the jobsite.
*   Vendor-supplied vapour tight packaging and internal drying aids **must** be left in place until the item is to be installed.
*   Valve bodies **should** be enclosed in plastic bags.
*   Diaphragm transmitter faces **should** be covered with plastic sheets.
*   All items **should** be warehoused in an enclosed, dry, storage area.
*   Careful storage **must** be observed before installation in and after removal from Chlorine piping.

**Pipe and Fitting Protection**
Upon receipt of piping and fittings, all pipe stubs, flanges, and exposed process surfaces **must** be covered with a moisture-resistant material (e.g., plastic). Storage in a dry covered area **must** be provided for piping and fittings.

**Packaging for Transport**
During crating, the use of silica gel bags, shrink wrap coverings, or other means of preventing moisture from entering pipe spools is recommended. Crating material **must** comply with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics requirements for transport protection. Shipments of pre-spooled pipe **should** be kept as intact as possible until installation is required. Removal of protective packaging **will** result in the ingress of moisture and contaminants into pipe spools. All pipe spools **should** be kept in dry covered storage until needed.

Refer to [[packaging_materials_and_construction]] and [[packing_and_shipping_requirements]] for general guidelines.
