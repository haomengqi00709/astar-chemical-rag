# Electrical Motor List

The Electrical Motor List provides specific technical and operational information for all motors used on a project. This list is crucial for design, procurement, and maintenance.

**Content of the Motor List**
The motor list **shall** contain specific information related to each motor, including:
*   Motor hp (horsepower).
*   Motor kW (kilowatt).
*   Motor RPM (revolutions per minute).
*   Motor voltage.
*   Motor phase.
*   Enclosure type.
*   Power factor.
*   Service factor.
*   Efficiency.
*   Full load currents.
*   ¾ load currents.
*   ½ load currents.
*   No load currents.
*   Weight.
*   Motor terminal box location.
*   Vertical or horizontal mounting position (incomplete in source, assumed).

**Maintenance and References**
The motor list **shall** show all motors on the project. It is updated according to 4-LST-0001, Equipment List, and 4-DST-XXXX, Equipment Data Sheet. The motor list **shall** be kept up to date with each revision of the Equipment List and Equipment Data Sheets. This ensures that the information remains current throughout the project lifecycle.
