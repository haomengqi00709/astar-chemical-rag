# Valve Specification (5-SPC-0004)

The Valve Specification, identified as 5-SPC-0004, is a comprehensive document that defines the technical requirements for all valves utilized in piping systems. This specification ensures that valves meet the necessary functional, material, dimensional, and performance standards for safe and efficient operation.

**Content Requirements**
The Valve Specification **shall** contain the following detailed information:
*   **Valve General Description and Number:** A general overview and unique identification number for each valve type.
*   **Valve Type:** Specific types of valves (e.g., ball, gate, globe, check, butterfly) suitable for different applications.
*   **Valve Material:** Required material compositions for valve bodies, trims, and internal components, compatible with process fluids and operating conditions.
*   **Valve Rating and Specification:** Pressure-temperature ratings and relevant industry specifications (e.g., API, ASME, ANSI).
*   **Valve Size Range:** The range of nominal pipe sizes for which the valve type is applicable.
*   **Chemetics Approved Manufacturers and Model Numbers:** A list of approved manufacturers and their specific model numbers that meet Chemetics' quality and performance standards.

**Roles and Responsibilities**
The preparation, checking, and approval of the Valve Specification are assigned to specific disciplines:
*   **Preparation:** The document **shall** be prepared by the Mechanical discipline.
*   **Checking:** The document **shall** be checked by the Design discipline.
*   **Approval:** The document **shall** be approved by the Mechanical discipline.

This structured approach ensures that all valves are correctly specified and sourced from approved suppliers, contributing to the safety, control, and operational reliability of the piping system.
