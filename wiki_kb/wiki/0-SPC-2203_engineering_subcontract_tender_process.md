# Engineering Subcontract Tender Process (0-SPC-2203)

This specification, 0-SPC-2203, outlines the comprehensive tender process for engineering subcontracts, detailing requirements for proposers, evaluation criteria, and administrative procedures. It serves as a foundational document for managing vendor interactions and ensuring compliance throughout the tender and contract lifecycle.

### Document Overview and History
The document title is **0-SPC-2203 — Specification**. Its administrative history includes a significant name change event. On June 6, 2004, the file name was changed from 0-CHG-80XX-R0 to 0-CHG-22XX-R0. Consequently, all internal references to "80XX" were updated to "22XX". This name change conversion was implemented in 2005. It is important to note that no new revision number was used for this conversion, and the specific changes related to this administrative update have not been individually marked within the document. Users should be aware of this historical context when referencing older versions or related documents. For more details on document naming conventions and revision impacts, refer to [[aker_kvaerner_name_change_documentation_impact_0_spc_0002]] and [[document_name_change_and_revision_impact]].

### Proposer Communication and Submission Requirements
Proposers **must** acknowledge receipt of the request within five working days and **shall** state their intended response within five working days of receipt of the request. The Agreement number **must** appear prominently on all correspondence and documentation submitted by the Proposer. Proposers **shall** verify that they have received all documents referenced in the Agreement. If any referenced documents are missing, the Proposer **must** promptly advise the Subcontract Administrator, who **shall** then issue the missing documents. All queries and requests for clarification **must** be submitted in writing to the Subcontract Administrator. For transparency, all proposers **will** be provided with a verbatim copy of both the query and its response.

A Proposer’s offer to supply the Services **will** be irrevocable for a period of 60 days following the submission date. Proposers **must** provide documentation completed in accordance with instructions. Required documentation includes:
*   **0-LST-22XX-1**: Engineering Subcontract Activity Schedule (Data available in SQL: 0-LST-22XX-1)
*   **0-LST-22XX-2**: Engineering Subcontractor Staff Rates (Data available in SQL: 0-LST-22XX-2)
*   **0-REP-22XX**: Engineering Subcontractor Survey

Additional documentation that may assist in the evaluation includes:
*   Explicitly stated and described deviations or exceptions to any part of the Subcontract or referenced documents.
*   Resumes or C.V.’s for nominated Key Persons.
*   A preliminary schedule.
*   Quality Management Manuals.

Proposers may supply other documentation they consider necessary to fully describe their offer, provided it is concise and clearly indicates its purpose. However, Aker Kvaerner Chemetics is not obliged to consider documentation supplied other than that explicitly listed. For general guidelines on subcontractor document supply, refer to [[engineering_subcontractor_document_supply_requirements_0_spc_0002]].

### Pricing Structure and Staff Rates
The Subcontractor **will** be paid a lump sum for the services provided. The [[engineering_subcontract_activity_schedule]] (0-LST-22XX-1) serves as a detailed list of activities the Subcontractor **will** carry out. The lump sum for each activity represents the price paid by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for that specific activity. The total of these lump sum prices on the activity schedule constitutes the Subcontractor’s lump sum price for the entirety of the services. Proposers **must** complete the provided activity schedule (0-LST-22XX-1), listing quantities for activities not already specified and providing lump sum prices for each. Any part of the services not specifically identified as an activity **will** be considered included within the other activities.

Proposers **must** include staff rates in their tender, which **must** be consistent with the priced activity schedule. These staff rates **will** be utilized to value [[engineering_subcontract_compensation_events]]. Proposers **must** complete the provided list (0-LST-22XX-2: Engineering Subcontractor Staff Rates), detailing categories of employees and the hourly rate to be charged for each. These hourly rates **must** be inclusive of all overhead charges and profit. Furthermore, Proposers **must** nominate key persons who **will** be engaged to provide the Services and **must** list their names on the 0-LST-22XX-2 form.

### Tender Evaluation and Award
Proposers **must** complete the provided survey (0-REP-22XX: Engineering Subcontractor Survey), and Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **will** contact references directly. An offer to supply services **will** be considered in full compliance with the Agreement and all referenced documents unless deviations or exceptions are explicitly stated and described. Offers **will** be evaluated at the sole discretion of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, considering overall performance, delivery, and value. The evaluation **will** also consider any explicitly stated deviations and exceptions in the manner described by these instructions.

Aker Kvaerner Chemetics reserves the right to accept or reject any offer in whole or in part, and to reject all offers with or without notice or reasons. The company also reserves the right to accept an offer other than that having the lowest price, or an offer not in full compliance with the Request for Tender. If no offer is acceptable, the work may be abandoned or performed in any manner selected by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. Receiving or responding to a Request for Tender does not imply that any Proposer is pre-qualified or acceptable. Unsuccessful Proposers **will** be advised within ten working days of the award of a Subcontract for the services to another party. For further details on evaluation procedures, refer to [[engineering_subcontractor_bid_evaluation_procedure_0_prc_2204]] and [[bid_evaluation_and_approval_0_prc_5003]].

### Tender Costs and Confidentiality
All costs incurred by the Proposer, any Supplier, Subsubcontractor, or Others in preparing the offer are the sole responsibility of the Proposer and **will not** be reimbursed by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. Proposers **must** take reasonable measures to maintain the confidentiality of the Request for Tender and all referenced documents. Proposer’s Suppliers or Subsubcontractors **must** only be given sufficient information to provide an offer to the Proposer. The Request for Tender and all documents provided by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics remain the property of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. Originals and all copies of these documents **must** be returned to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or destroyed on demand. For broader guidelines on information security, see [[confidential_information_policy]].

### Proposer Responsibilities and Communication

*   **Acknowledgement and Response:** Proposers must acknowledge receipt of the Request for Tender within five working days and state their intended response within five working days of receipt.
*   **Agreement Number:** The Agreement number must appear prominently on all correspondence and documentation submitted by the Proposer.
*   **Document Verification:** Proposers are required to verify receipt of all documents referenced in the Agreement. If any referenced documents are missing, the Proposer must promptly advise the Subcontract Administrator, who will then issue the missing documents.
*   **Queries and Clarifications:** All queries and requests for clarification must be submitted in writing to the Subcontract Administrator. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics will provide all proposers with a verbatim copy of both the query and its response.
*   **Offer Validity:** The Proposer’s offer to supply the Services will be irrevocable for a period of 60 days following the submission date.

### Required Documentation

Proposers must provide documentation completed in accordance with instructions.

*   **Mandatory Documents:**
    *   0-LST-22XX-1: Engineering Subcontract Activity Schedule
    *   0-LST-22XX-2: Engineering Subcontractor Staff Rates
    *   0-REP-22XX: Engineering Subcontractor Survey
*   **Additional Documentation for Evaluation:** To assist in the evaluation, proposers may supply:
    *   Deviations to any part of the Subcontract.
    *   Resumes or C.V.’s for nominated Key Persons.
    *   A preliminary schedule.
    *   Quality Management Manuals.
    *   Proposers may also supply any other documentation they consider necessary to fully describe their offer.
*   **Documentation Standards:** All documentation supplied should be concise and clearly indicate its purpose. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics is not obliged to consider documentation other than that explicitly listed as required.

### Offer Content and Pricing

*   **Lump Sum Payment:** The Subcontractor will be paid a lump sum for the services.
*   **Activity Schedule (0-LST-22XX-1):**
    *   The activity schedule lists the activities the Subcontractor will carry out.
    *   The lump sum for each activity represents the price paid by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for that activity.
    *   The total of the lump sum prices on the activity schedule constitutes the Subcontractor’s lump sum price for the entire services.
    *   Proposers must complete the provided activity schedule (0-LST-22XX-1), listing quantities for activities not already specified and providing lump sum prices for each activity.
    *   Any part of the services not specifically identified as an activity will be included within the scope of other activities.
*   **Staff Rates (0-LST-22XX-2):**
    *   Proposers must include staff rates in their tender, consistent with the priced activity schedule. These rates will be used to value compensation events.
    *   Proposers must complete the provided list (0-LST-22XX-2), listing categories of employees and the hourly rate to be charged for each category.
    *   Hourly rates must be inclusive of all overhead charges and profit.
    *   Proposers must nominate key persons to provide the Services and list their names on 0-LST-22XX-2.
*   **Subcontractor Survey (0-REP-22XX):** Proposers must complete the provided survey (0-REP-22XX). Aker Kvaerner Chemetics will contact the references directly.
*   **Compliance and Deviations:** Proposer’s offer will be considered in full compliance with the Agreement and all referenced documents unless deviations or exceptions are explicitly stated and described.

### Evaluation Process

*   **Discretionary Evaluation:** Offers will be evaluated in the sole discretion of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
*   **Evaluation Criteria:** Evaluation criteria include overall performance, delivery, and value.
*   **Consideration of Deviations:** The evaluation will consider any deviations and exceptions included in the offer in the manner described by these instructions.
*   **Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' Rights:** Aker Kvaerner Chemetics reserves the right to:
    *   Accept or reject any offer in whole or in part.
    *   Reject all offers with or without notice or reasons.
    *   Accept an offer other than that having the lowest price.
    *   Accept an offer not in full compliance with the Request for Tender.
*   **Unacceptable Offers:** If no offer is acceptable, the work may be abandoned or performed in any manner selected by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
*   **No Representation of Qualification:** Aker Kvaerner Chemetics makes no representation that any Proposer is pre-qualified or acceptable merely because they have received or responded to a Request for Tender.
*   **Notification:** Unsuccessful Proposers will be advised within ten working days of the award of a Subcontract for the services to another party.

### Costs and Confidentiality

*   **Proposer's Costs:** Costs incurred by the Proposer, any Supplier, Subsubcontractor, or Others in preparing the Proposer’s offer are the sole responsibility of the Proposer and will not be reimbursed by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
*   **Confidentiality:** Proposers must take reasonable measures to maintain the confidentiality of the Request for Tender and all referenced documents. Proposer’s Suppliers or Subsubcontractors must only be given sufficient information to provide an offer to the Proposer.
*   **Property of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics:** The Request for Tender and all documents provided to the Proposer by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics remain the property of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. Originals and all copies of these documents must be returned to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or destroyed on demand.
