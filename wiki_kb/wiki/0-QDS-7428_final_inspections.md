# Final Inspections: CS Tubing

This page details the mandatory final inspection requirements for carbon steel tubing, as specified in Quality Data Sheet (QDS) 0-QDS-7428. These inspections are conducted to verify the physical characteristics, dimensions, and overall quality of the finished tubing.

**Mandatory Final Inspection Requirements:**

*   **Dimensional Inspection:**
    *   Dimensional inspection is required to be included in the Vendor's Inspection & Test (I & T) Plan (designated as [[0-QDS-7428_quality_record_definitions|FP]]).
    *   Chemetics will **Witness**, **Hold**, and **Complete** surveillance for Dimensional inspection.
    *   Dimensional tolerances **must** be in accordance with **SA-450**.
*   **Ring Gauge Inspection:**
    *   Ring Gauge inspection is required to be included in the Vendor's I & T Plan (designated as [[0-QDS-7428_quality_record_definitions|FP]]).
    *   Chemetics will **Witness**, **Hold**, and **Complete** surveillance for Ring Gauge inspection.
    *   Ring Gauge inspection **must** be performed on **100%** of Tubes.
*   **Visual and Surface Inspections (Chemetics Witness & Hold):**
    *   Chemetics will **Witness** and **Hold** surveillance for the following aspects, with no specific Quality Record required and no "Complete" surveillance by Chemetics:
        *   Contour and finish of welds
        *   Surfaces free from defects
        *   Cleanliness
        *   Item Identification
        *   Review QA Documentation
        *   Packaging

The final inspections are a critical step in the quality assurance process, ensuring that the carbon steel tubing meets all specified physical and aesthetic criteria. Dimensional inspection verifies that the tubing conforms to the required sizes and tolerances, which are crucial for proper fit-up and assembly. The 100% Ring Gauge inspection is particularly stringent, ensuring that the internal diameter of every tube meets the specified dimensions, which is vital for flow characteristics and subsequent processing.

For visual and surface aspects, Chemetics' "Witness" and "Hold" surveillance ensures that these critical attributes are checked before the product is released. While Chemetics does not require a specific Quality Record for these items, nor does it "Complete" the surveillance, its presence and the ability to "Hold" the process provide a strong control point. This ensures that any observed non-conformances in weld contour, surface finish, cleanliness, identification, or packaging are addressed by the vendor before shipment.
