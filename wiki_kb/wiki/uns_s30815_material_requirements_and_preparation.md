# Material Requirements and Preparation for UNS S30815 Fabrications

This section outlines the mandatory requirements for material selection, handling, cutting, and forming of UNS S30815 for welded fabrications. Adherence to these specifications is critical for ensuring the integrity and quality of the final product.

**Base Materials and Consumables**
*   All base materials used **SHALL** be as specified in the purchase order and referenced documents.
*   All base materials used **MUST** conform to [[ASME_bpvc_section_ii_materials]] and [[ASTM_a380_cleaning_passivation_stainless_steel]] Codes.
*   All weld filler metals or consumables **SHALL** be Avesta 253 MA®.
*   Mill Test Certification **MUST** be supplied with all materials, including welding consumables.
*   The fabricator **MUST** maintain full traceability of materials back to original mill certification.
*   The fabricator's internal controls **MUST** be such as to prevent the use of unspecified materials.
*   Lack of adequate identification or certification **WILL** be cause for rejection of the specific component or item.
*   Supplier **MUST** make available to Chemetics' inspector copies of chemical certification for batches of electrodes and wire coils used. If electrodes or wire cannot be identified to Chemetics' satisfaction, samples **WILL** be removed for chemical analysis, with costs borne by the supplier.

**Material Cutting**
Material **SHALL** be cut to size by Machining, Shearing, or Grinding.
*   Material over thick **WILL** have extra material left on the sheared edge to allow for machining, grinding, etc.
*   Material over thick **WILL NOT** be sheared.
*   Thermal cutting is preferable by the Plasma Arc Process. Welding edges after plasma arc cutting **SHOULD** be lightly ground to bright metal.
*   If any other thermal cutting process is used, a minimum of of metal **MUST** be removed by mechanical means. This mechanical removal after thermal cutting ensures that all slag, scale, other irregularities, and contaminants, including thermal effects, have been removed.

**Material Forming**
*   Material **WILL** be cold formed as far as practical.
*   When hot forming is necessary, the procedure to be adopted **MUST** be agreed to in writing by Chemetics prior to commencement of work.
*   A solution anneal **WILL** be necessary after any hot working operation.
