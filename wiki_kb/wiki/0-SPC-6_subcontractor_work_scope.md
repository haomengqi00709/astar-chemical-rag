# Subcontractor Work Scope for Specification 0-SPC-6

Specification 0-SPC-6 defines the comprehensive work scope for subcontractors, particularly focusing on the [Major Work Division] activities required for project execution. This page details the general obligations and responsibilities of the Subcontractor concerning the construction and erection of plant components.

**General Work Requirements**
The Subcontractor **shall** perform all [Major Work Division] work in strict accordance with the requirements stipulated in the Applicable Documents. These documents encompass a range of project-specific materials, including drawings, specifications, and other relevant instructions, exemplified by document 02C03000-4-GAD-0022. Adherence to these documents is mandatory to ensure consistency and quality throughout the project lifecycle.

**Plant Construction and Erection**
A key responsibility of the Subcontractor, as outlined in this specification, is the construction and erection of designated portions of the plant. These specific portions are detailed in the document [[0-LST-6X90-1_construction_subcontract_supply_and_erection_scope]]. The entire process of plant construction and erection **shall** be carried out precisely as described and specified within the Applicable Documents. This ensures that the final plant configuration meets all design and operational criteria.

For a broader understanding of construction subcontract scopes, refer to [[construction_subcontract_scope]]. General procedures for managing project documents, including Applicable Documents, can be found in [[document_control_procedure_0_prc_0007]].

All [Major Work Division] work performed by the Subcontractor must strictly adhere to the requirements of the Applicable Documents. The specific portions of the plant to be constructed and erected by the Subcontractor are detailed in 0-LST-6X90-1: Construction Subcontract Supply and Erection Scope. The construction and erection of the plant must be carried out precisely as described and specified in the Applicable Documents.
