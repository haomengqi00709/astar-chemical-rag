# Construction Facilities Layout Drawing (0-GAD-6X01)

The Construction Facilities Layout Drawing (0-GAD-6X01) is a critical graphical document that provides essential site information for construction subcontractors. This drawing is directly referenced by Sections 2.5 and 2.6 of the [[construction_subcontract_agreement_procedure_0_prc_6002|Construction Subcontract Agreement (0-PRC-6002)]] and is developed as part of the [[construction_subcontract_scope_preparation_0_prc_6091|Construction Subcontract Scope Preparation (0-PRC-6091)]] procedure.

### Purpose and Content

The primary purpose of 0-GAD-6X01 is to visually communicate key logistical and operational details on the construction site, including:

*   The precise location of connection points for various field services supplied to the Subcontractor, such as water, power, and other utilities.
*   Designated areas where the Subcontractor is permitted to locate temporary facilities, including temporary offices, lunchrooms, employee parking, and other necessary infrastructure.

### Preparation and Approval Process

1.  **Preparation**: The drawing **must** be prepared by the Design department.
2.  **Numbering**: The document **must** be numbered in accordance with [[document_numbering_and_name_change_conventions|0-PRC-0002: Document Numbering]]. The "X" in the document number (0-GAD-6X01) **shall** be replaced by the same digit that corresponds to the specific [[construction_subcontract_agreement_procedure_0_prc_6002|Subcontract Agreement (0-PRC-6002)]] it supports.
3.  **Publication**: 0-GAD-6X01 **must** be published with the status “Approved for Construction”. The publication process **shall** adhere to the guidelines specified in [[publishing_project_documents|0-PRC-0005: Publishing of Project Documents]].
4.  **Checking**: The drawing **must** be checked by the Manager of Construction.
5.  **Approval**: Final approval for 0-GAD-6X01 **must** be granted by the Project Engineer.

---
The drawing will show:
*   The location of connection points for field services (water, power, offices, etc.) supplied to the Subcontractor.
*   Where the Subcontractor may locate temporary offices, lunchrooms, employee parking, etc.

**Preparation and Control:**
*   **Preparation:** Prepared by Design.
*   **Numbering:** Numbered using [[document_numbering_0_prc_0002|0-PRC-0002: Document Numbering]]. The "X" in its number will be replaced by the same digit as for the Subcontract Agreement (0-PRC-6002).
*   **Publishing:** Published using [[publishing_of_project_documents_0_prc_0005|0-PRC-0005: Publishing of Project Documents]].
*   **Status:** Published with status “Approved for Construction”.
*   **Checking:** Checked by the Manager of Construction.
*   **Approval:** Approved by the Project Engineer.
