# Expenditure Request Form (0-SPC-5006-1)

This page outlines the requirements for the Expenditure Request Form, as detailed in document 0-SPC-5006-1, titled "Procedure". This form is an essential tool for [[project_cost_control]] administration, designed to ensure proper tracking, allocation, and authorization of project expenditures.

**Mandatory Information:**
The Expenditure Request Form **shall** include the following details to ensure comprehensive record-keeping and financial transparency:
*   **Cost Code Allocation:** A clear and accurate assignment of the expenditure to the relevant [[cost_code_structure]]. This ensures costs are attributed to the correct project segments.
*   **Expenditure Total Costs:** The precise total monetary value of the expenditure.
*   **Invoice/Statement Received:** A clear indication that the corresponding invoice or statement has been received.
*   **Invoice/Statement Number:** The unique identifier associated with the received invoice or statement.
*   **Sent to Accounting for Payment Date:** The specific date on which the completed form, along with all necessary supporting documentation, was forwarded to the accounting department for payment processing.

**Approval Requirements:**
All expenditure requests **must** adhere to a defined approval hierarchy to maintain financial governance:
*   **Manager of Procurement Approval:** The form **requires** the signature of the Manager of Procurement. The date of this approval **must** also be clearly recorded on the form.
*   **V.P. of Finance Approval:** If the "Expenditure Total Costs" are greater than $2,500.00, then the approval signature of the V.P. of Finance **is required**. The date of this approval **must** also be recorded. This conditional approval ensures higher-value expenditures receive appropriate senior management oversight.

This structured process ensures financial accountability, adherence to budgetary guidelines, and proper documentation for all project-related expenses.

**Related Information:**
Further details regarding specific items and activities related to this form can be found by referencing [[0-spc-5006-1_expenditure_request_form_table_1]].

**Required Fields and Approvals**
The Expenditure Request Form requires the following information and approvals:
*   **Cost Code Allocation:** To properly categorize expenses.
*   **Expenditure Total Costs:** The total amount of the expenditure.
*   **Manager of Procurement Approval:** Required for all requests, including the date of approval.
*   **V.P. of Finance Approval:** Required if Expenditure Total Costs are greater than $2,500.00, including the date of approval.

**Payment Tracking**
The form also includes sections for tracking payment status:
*   Recording of Invoice/Statement Received.
*   The Invoice/Statement Number.
*   The date the form was Sent to Accounting for Payment.
