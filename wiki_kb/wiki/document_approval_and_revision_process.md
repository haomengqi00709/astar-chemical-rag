# Document Approval and Revision Process

This page outlines the standard procedures for document approval and revision, ensuring quality control and proper record-keeping throughout the project lifecycle.

**Document Approval Process**
Procedure 9-PRC-0003 assigns responsibilities for document preparation, checking, and approval. This structured approach ensures that all documentation undergoes thorough review before finalization.

**Document Revisions**
Revisions are tracked with specific dates, statuses, and responsible personnel. For example:
*   **Document Revision 1:** Date 15-June-04, Status Standard. Prepared By A. Mews, Checked By D. Clark, Approved By T. Ueki.
*   **Document Revision 0:** Date 27-Aug-02, Status Comment requested. Prepared By A. Mews, Checked By D. Clark, Approved By T. Ueki.

A name change was implemented for the 2005 Name Change Conversion. Notably, no new revision number was used for this conversion, and changes were not marked. This highlights the importance of understanding specific project conventions for document control.
