# Requisition for Quotation or Purchase (0-PRC-5002)

This document, 0-PRC-5002, outlines the procedure for preparing and issuing requisitions for quotations or purchases for the Project. It ensures that all requests for equipment and materials are properly documented and authorized.

Requisitions, Request for Quotation, and Purchase Orders will be issued in accordance with this procedure.
