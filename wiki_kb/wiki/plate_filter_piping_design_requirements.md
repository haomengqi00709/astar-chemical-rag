# Plate Filter Piping Design Requirements

This page outlines the piping design requirements for the Plate Filter Package, as specified in Document ID 4-PKG-XXXX-R1. Adherence to these requirements is mandatory for all associated piping systems to ensure safe and efficient operation.

**Applicability of Piping Design Requirements**
Piping design requirements must be applied to the following streams and utilities:
*   Fluid
*   Precoat
*   Filter Aid
*   Utilities

**Piping Design Conditions**
*   **Design Pressure:** This is a mandatory Piping Design Requirement, measured in kPa(g). It must specify both maximum and minimum values.
*   **Design Temperature:** This is a mandatory Piping Design Requirement, measured in °C. It must specify both maximum and minimum values.

**Piping Protection**
*   **Protection:** This is a mandatory Piping Design Requirement. It must include provisions for:
    *   Overpressure
    *   Vacuum

These requirements are critical for the overall [[piping_design_criteria_5_prc_0002]] and are further elaborated in the [[piping_design_and_specification_5_prc_0003]]. Considerations for overpressure and vacuum protection are also linked to [[pressure_relief_equipment_tagging]].
