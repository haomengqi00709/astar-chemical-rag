# Pipe Fabrication and Installation Specifications

The Pipe Fabrication and Installation Specifications are comprehensive documents that detail the specific requirements for the construction and setup of chemical and acid piping systems. These specifications are crucial for ensuring the integrity, safety, and performance of critical process infrastructure.

**Key Requirements**
These specifications **shall** include special requirements for:
*   **Fabrication:** Detailed procedures and quality control measures for the manufacturing of piping components, including welding, bending, and forming. This ensures that components meet design tolerances and material integrity.
*   **Installation:** Guidelines for the assembly and erection of piping systems on-site, covering aspects such as alignment, fit-up, and connection methods.
*   **Testing:** Protocols for various tests, including pressure testing, leak testing, and non-destructive examination, to verify the structural integrity and leak-tightness of the installed system.
*   **Cleaning:** Procedures for internal and external cleaning of piping to remove contaminants, scale, and debris, which is particularly critical for chemical and acid services.
*   **Flushing:** Requirements for flushing the piping system with appropriate fluids to ensure cleanliness and prepare it for commissioning.

**Roles and Responsibilities**
The development and approval of these specifications involve a collaborative effort between key engineering disciplines:
*   **Preparation:** The specifications **shall** be prepared by the Mechanical discipline.
*   **Checking:** The specifications **shall** be checked by the Design discipline.
*   **Approval:** The specifications **shall** be approved by the Mechanical discipline.

This rigorous process ensures that all aspects of pipe fabrication and installation for chemical and acid piping systems adhere to the highest standards of quality and safety.

These specifications **shall** contain special requirements for:
*   The fabrication of chemical / acid piping systems.
*   The installation of chemical / acid piping systems.
*   The testing of chemical / acid piping systems.
*   The cleaning of chemical / acid piping systems.
*   The flushing of chemical / acid piping systems.
