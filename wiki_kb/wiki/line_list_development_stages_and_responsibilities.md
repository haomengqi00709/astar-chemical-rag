# Line List Development Stages and Responsibilities

The Piping Line List is developed through four distinct stages, with specific data requirements and assigned responsibilities for preparation, checking, and approval at each stage. The development of the Line List is closely integrated with the evolution of the [[1-PRC-0003_p_id_development_and_approval|Piping and Instrumentation Diagrams (P&ID)]].

**Stage 1 Line List**
This initial stage focuses on capturing fundamental information for all process and utility lines.
*   **Data Included**: P&ID reference, Line origin and destination, Fluid code, Line identifier, Preliminary material code, Estimated line size, and Type of insulation and heat tracing.
*   **Data Source (from Stage 2 P&ID)**: P&ID reference, Line origin and destination, Fluid code, Line identifier, Preliminary material code, Estimated line size, Type of insulation and heat tracing.
*   **Prepared by**: Design
*   **Checked by**: Process
*   **Approved by**: Design

**Stage 2 Line List**
This stage expands upon the initial data with more detailed process conditions.
*   **Data Included**: Design flow rate, Fluid density, Velocity criteria, Estimated operating pressure (Maximum and Minimum), and Operating temperature (Maximum and Minimum).
*   **Data Source**: All data for Stage 2 is provided by Process.
*   **Prepared by**: Process
*   **Checked by**: Technology
*   **Approved by**: Process

**Stage 3 Line List**
This stage incorporates mechanical design parameters and final material selections.
*   **Data Included**: Final line size, Final gasket material selection, Design pressure (Maximum and Minimum), Design temperature (Maximum and Minimum), B31.3 fluid code, Pressure relief device, Pressure relief setting, and Insulation thickness.
*   **Data Source**: All data for Stage 3 is provided by Mechanical.
*   **Prepared by**: Mechanical
*   **Checked by**: Process
*   **Approved by**: Mechanical

**Stage 4 Line List**
The final stage details testing and finishing requirements.
*   **Data Included**: Test pressure, Test method, NDT requirements, and Paint system.
*   **Data Source**: All data for Stage 4 is provided by Mechanical.
*   **Prepared by**: Mechanical
*   **Checked by**: Project Engineer
*   **Approved by**: Mechanical

**Chronology of Line List and P&ID Development:**
The overall development sequence is as follows:
1.  Stage 1 P&ID
2.  Stage 2 P&ID
3.  Stage 3 Line List
4.  Stage 3 P&ID
5.  Stage 4 P&ID
6.  Stage 5 P&ID
7.  Stage 4 Line List

**Stage 1 Scope:** Stage 1 Line List will contain data for all process lines and all utility lines.

**Chronology of Line List and P&ID Development:**
The development of the Line List is closely integrated with the P&ID development, following this chronology: Stage 1 P&ID, Stage 2 P&ID, Stage 3 Line List, Stage 3 P&ID, Stage 4 P&ID, Stage 5 P&ID, Stage 4 Line List.
