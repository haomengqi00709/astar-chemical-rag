# Electrical Field Checkout: Roles and Responsibilities (9-PRC-0004)

The Electrical Field Checkout Procedure (9-PRC-0004) defines clear roles and responsibilities to ensure thorough verification of electrical system installations.

**Site Sub-contractor:**
The site sub-contractor **shall check** for the completeness of the electrical system installation. Following this initial check, a deficiency crew supplied by the site sub-contractor **shall correct** any outstanding punch list items identified during their initial review or subsequent inspections.

**Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Site Supervisor:**
The Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site supervisor **shall visually review** all installed electrical systems. This review is conducted against general arrangement and installation instructions to ensure compliance. Upon satisfaction that the sub-contractor has completed their scope of work to the furthest extent possible at that time, the site supervisor **shall sign** the applicable Electrical Contractor Check Lists. Subsequently, the site supervisor **shall notify** the [[project_manager_planning_responsibilities|Project Manager]] that the equipment is ready for a final review by an Aker Kvaerner Chemetics specialist. The site supervisor also plays a crucial role in [[deficiency_management_process|deficiency management]], working directly with the sub-contractor's deficiency crew to ensure all recorded deficiencies are corrected.

**Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Specialist:**
The Aker Kvaerner Chemetics specialist **shall accept** the signed Check Lists from the site supervisor. Following this, the specialist **shall conduct an inspection** of the electrical systems. This inspection involves cross-referencing the installation against engineering documents and the applicable Electrical System Check List. Any findings from this inspection are recorded and provided to the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site supervisor for resolution.
