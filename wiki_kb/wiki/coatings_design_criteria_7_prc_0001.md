# Coatings Design Criteria (7-PRC-0001)

The Coatings Design Criteria, documented under 7-PRC-0001, serves as a foundational document for all coating selection within a project. This document outlines the essential requirements and guidelines that govern the choice and application of coatings for the plant.

**Content Requirements:**
*   **Mandatory:** Statutory requirements, as applicable, shall be included.
*   **Mandatory:** Owner requirements, as applicable, shall be included.
*   **Mandatory:** A reference to the appropriate [[coating_specification_development_and_standards|Coating Specification]], as applicable, shall be provided.
*   Any additional general requirements for coating selection specific to the Plant, as applicable, shall be detailed.

**Preparation and Approval:**
*   This document will be prepared by the Mechanical discipline.
*   It will be checked by the Project Engineer.
*   It will be approved by the Chief Mechanical.

**Guidance and Standards:**
In the absence of specific statutory or Owner requirements, the Design Criteria will reference the standard [[coating_specification_development_and_standards|Coating Specifications]] applicable to the Plant. The Design Criteria will also provide guidance on scenarios such as the acceptability of [[vendor_coating_systems|Vendor's standard coating systems]].
