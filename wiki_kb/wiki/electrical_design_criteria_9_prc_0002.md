# Electrical Design Criteria (9-PRC-0002)

This document, 9-PRC-0002, details the specific design criteria for all electrical systems within the Project. It sets forth the mandatory requirements and standards for power distribution, motor control, lighting, grounding, and electrical safety.

Discipline Leads utilize this document to establish the precise electrical requirements for the Project.
