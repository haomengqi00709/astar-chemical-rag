# Stack Non-Destructive Testing Requirements

This page details the "NON DESTRUCTIVE TESTING" (NDT) requirements for stacks, as specified in the [[4-dst-xxxx-r0_template_stack_datasheet_administration|Template Stack Datasheet]]. NDT methods are crucial for verifying the integrity and quality of materials and welds without causing damage to the component.

**Mandatory NDT Categories:**
The following NDT methods are typically specified for stack components:
*   **Radiography:** This method uses X-rays or gamma rays to detect internal flaws such as cracks, voids, or inclusions in welds and materials. The extent of radiography (e.g., percentage of welds) must be specified according to applicable design codes and project requirements.
*   **Postweld Heat Treatment (PWHT):** While not strictly an NDT method, PWHT is a critical process often specified in conjunction with NDT. It involves heating and cooling a welded component to reduce residual stresses and improve material properties, particularly for thick sections or certain alloys. The requirements for PWHT are typically driven by material type, thickness, and service conditions.
*   **Liquid Penetrant (PT):** This surface inspection method is used to detect surface-breaking discontinuities in non-porous materials. It involves applying a liquid penetrant to the surface, allowing it to seep into flaws, and then using a developer to draw the penetrant out, making the flaws visible. PT is commonly applied to welds, nozzle connections, and other critical surfaces.
*   **Impact Test:** This test measures the material's ability to absorb energy before fracturing, particularly at low temperatures. It is crucial for materials used in cold environments or for components subjected to dynamic loads. The specific temperature and energy absorption requirements for impact tests are defined by material specifications and design codes.

These NDT requirements are integral to the overall quality assurance program for stack fabrication. For general NDE requirements, refer to [[0-QDS-7_non_destructive_examination_requirements]].

**Specific NDT Methods:**
*   Radiography
*   Postweld Heat Treatment
*   Liquid Penetrant
*   Impact Test
