# RLCS Vessel Design Conditions

This document outlines the specific design conditions and parameters for Rubber Lined Carbon Steel (RLCS) Vessels, as referenced in datasheet 4-DST-XXXX-R1. Adherence to these conditions is critical for the safe and effective operation of the vessel.

**General Design Parameters:**
*   **Vessel Registration:** Not required.
*   **Vessel Design Code:** The vessel **shall** be designed in accordance with API 650 standards.
*   **Vessel Specifications:** Detailed specifications are provided in documents [[4-SPC-0040]], [[4-SPC-0060]], and [[4-SPC-0081]].
*   **External Loads:** External loads, including wind, seismic, and snow, are referenced in [[site_design_conditions_1_dst_0001]]. The fabricator **shall** be responsible for calculating these external loads as part of their design submission. Civil/structural engineering calculations for these loads are independently prepared in [[structural_calculation_report_4_scr_xxxx]].
