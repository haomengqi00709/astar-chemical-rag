# Form DP-802.305 Safety Training History Record

Form DP-802.305 is designated as the Safety Training History Record. This form is a critical document used to systematically record an individual's participation and completion of various health, safety, and environment (HSE) training courses.

All employees **will** complete Form DP-802.305 for each training course they attend. Furthermore, Construction Managers and Site HSE Managers **will** utilize this form to maintain comprehensive safety-training files for all management and supervisory personnel.

The form is referenced within the OGPE Safety Manual, underscoring its importance in the company's safety documentation framework. Its proper completion and maintenance are essential for effective [[hse_training_records]] management and compliance with the broader [[0-PRC-8016_hse_training_program_overview]].

All employees **will** complete Form DP-802.305 for each training course taken.
