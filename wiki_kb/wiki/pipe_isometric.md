# Pipe Isometric (5-ISO-XXXX)

A Pipe Isometric drawing, identified as 5-ISO-XXXX, is a single-line representation of a pipeline in three dimensions. It provides a schematic routing of a pipe line and is crucial for fabrication, installation, and material take-offs.

**Content Requirements**
Each Pipe Isometric drawing **shall** contain the following detailed information:
*   **Schematic Routing of a Pipe Line:** A clear, three-dimensional representation of the pipeline's path.
*   **Co-ordinates of Piping Components:** Precise spatial coordinates for all components along the pipeline, facilitating accurate placement.
*   **Bay Line Reference:** References to plant grid lines or bay lines for easy location within the overall plant layout.
*   **Pipe Support Location and Type Identifier:** Indication of where pipe supports are located and their specific type (e.g., hanger, spring support).
*   **Pipe Component Bill of Quantity:** A list of all components required for the specific pipeline, including quantities.
*   **Insulation Type and Thickness:** Details on the type and thickness of insulation required for the pipeline.
*   **Spool Numbers:** Identification numbers for individual pipe spools, which are pre-fabricated sections of pipe.

**Roles and Responsibilities**
The preparation, checking, and approval of Pipe Isometric drawings are assigned to specific disciplines:
*   **Preparation:** The drawing **shall** be prepared by the Design discipline.
*   **Checking:** The drawing **shall** be checked by the Mechanical discipline.
*   **Approval:** The drawing **shall** be approved by the Design discipline.

Pipe Isometrics are indispensable tools for ensuring accurate fabrication, efficient installation, and effective material management for piping systems.
