# Underground Cable Installation Details

Underground cable installation details provide specific instructions for installing cables in trenches, including backfill materials, warning markers, and clearances. These details are essential for the safety and longevity of buried electrical infrastructure.

**Content of Underground Cable Installation Details**
Underground cable installation details **shall** show:
*   The method of installing the cable in a trench.
*   The type of material to be used to backfill the trench.
*   The location of the warning marker tape.
*   The location of the concrete or wood barrier between the cable and the top of the trench.
*   The method of supporting the cables and maintaining distances.
*   The depth of burial.
*   The distance between the cable and all other items located in the trench.
*   All clearances required between cables.
*   The method of grouping cables of different voltages within a cable tray system.
*   The method of dropping cables out of a cable tray to motors.
*   The method of dropping cables out of a cable tray to pushbutton stations.
*   The method of dropping cables out of a cable tray to instruments.
