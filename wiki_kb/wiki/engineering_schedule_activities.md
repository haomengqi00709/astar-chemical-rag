# Engineering Schedule Activities

The engineering schedule component of the overall Project Schedule **will** detail all work activities executed by home-office personnel. This sub-schedule is critical for managing the design and development phases of a project.

For each department involved in engineering, Engineering Task Lists are prepared. These lists serve as a guide for the Project Engineer to manage engineering work effectively. Each Engineering Task List **will** include:
*   A comprehensive list of engineering activities required to be completed over the subsequent reporting period.
*   Early completion dates for each activity.
*   Identification of preceding and succeeding activities to illustrate logical dependencies.

The percentage completion for engineering activities is derived directly from the Project Resource Management System, providing a real-time measure of progress.
