# Lead Engineer Roles and Responsibilities

This page outlines the key roles and responsibilities of the Lead Engineer within the context of project procurement and technical oversight, particularly as defined in Purchase Order [[0-SPC-5001_purchase_order_overview|0-SPC-5001]].

**Core Responsibilities**
The Lead Engineer primarily focuses on the technical aspects of the Goods and Services, ensuring compliance with design specifications and quality standards. Their responsibilities include:

*   **Design Acceptance/Approval:** The [[goods_and_services_definitions|Goods and Services Information]] or [[applicable_law_and_compliance|Applicable Law]] may require the Vendor to submit particulars of their design to the Lead Engineer (or Others) for acceptance or approval. The Vendor **must not** proceed with the relevant work until such acceptance or approval has been given.
*   **Defect Definition:** A [[0-SPC-5001_purchase_order_overview#Defect|Defect]] includes a part of the Goods and Services designed by the Vendor which is not in accordance with the Vendor's design that has been accepted by the Lead Engineer.
*   **Delegation of Actions:** The Lead Engineer, after notifying the Vendor, **may** delegate any of their actions or cancel any delegation.
*   **Quality System Access:** The Lead Engineer **shall** be granted access to the Vendor's and any Suppliers' quality systems for monitoring and quality auditing purposes.
*   **Access to Work:** The Vendor **must** provide the Lead Engineer with access to work being done or goods and materials being stored for the Purchase Order.

The Lead Engineer's role is crucial for maintaining the technical integrity and quality of the Goods and Services throughout the project lifecycle.
