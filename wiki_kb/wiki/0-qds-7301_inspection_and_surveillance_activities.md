# Inspection and Surveillance Activities for 0-QDS-7301

For projects governed by Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7301, Chemetics conducts various inspection and surveillance activities to ensure adherence to quality standards. The surveillance level by Chemetics is designated as "Complete" for several critical items and activities.

**Chemetics Surveillance Status (Complete):**
Chemetics' surveillance is "Complete" for the following documentation and inspection points:
*   [[mill_test_certificates]]
*   [[certificate_of_compliance]]
*   External [[coating_lining_inspection]]
*   [[dimensional_inspection]] / Sizes Final Inspection
*   Contour & finish of welds Final Inspection (refer to [[weld_inspection]])
*   Surfaces free from defects Final Inspection (refer to [[surface_defect_inspection]])
*   [[cleanliness_inspection]] Final Inspection
*   [[piece_identification_inspection]] Final Inspection
*   QA Documentation Final Inspection (refer to [[qa_documentation_inspection]])
*   [[packaging_inspection]] - Load 1
*   [[packing_list]] - Load 1

**Mandatory Surveillance Requirement:**
The surveillance for Packaging - Load 1 **must** be repeated for each subsequent truckload to ensure consistent quality throughout the shipment process.

**General Inspection and Testing Activities:**
The following are recognized as standard inspection and testing activities that may be subject to surveillance:
*   [[material_verification]]
*   [[non_destructive_testing]] (NDT), which includes:
    *   [[radiographic_testing]]
    *   [[dye_penetrant_testing]]
    *   [[magnetic_particle_testing]]
*   [[coating_lining_inspection]]
*   [[final_inspection]]
*   [[packing_crating]]

For more information on Chemetics' general surveillance procedures, consult [[0-QDS-7_aker_kvaerner_surveillance]]. Details on non-destructive examination requirements can be found in [[0-QDS-7_non_destructive_examination_requirements]].
