# 4-SPC-0040 Metallic Process Vessels Fabrication Overview

Specification 4-SPC-0040 outlines Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ mandatory requirements for the shop fabrication of metallic process vessels and metallic vessel components. This includes, but is not limited to, pressure vessels, shell and tube heat exchangers, and atmospheric storage tanks.

**Scope and Exemptions:**
This specification applies to the fabrication of the aforementioned metallic process vessels and components. Structural components such as skids, bases, ladders, and platforms are explicitly exempt from the requirements of 4-SPC-0040. Fabrication of these structural components shall be in accordance with the structural welding code specified in the relevant equipment requisition or purchase order.

**Compliance:**
The Vendor is solely responsible for meeting all applicable federal, provincial or state, and local regulations pertaining to the fabrication process. This specification is primarily written for the [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels]] Code. However, when equipment is manufactured or installed in countries requiring adherence to other jurisdictional codes (e.g., Australia, Europe), this specification **must** be modified to reflect those particular codes. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics will provide specific details to the Vendor if other national or international codes and standards are applicable.

Additional standards may also apply depending on the specific equipment service, such as for chlorine, hydrogen peroxide, or oxygen applications.

**Document History:**
This document is a complete re-write of the original specification ES44/F06, which was previously titled "Minimum Manufacturing & Quality Requirements of Pressure Vessels & Heat Exchangers". Significant portions of QA-related wording have been removed from this document, as these are now covered by individual [[quality_data_sheets_qds]]. Details specific to gas/gas exchangers and converters have also been moved to separate specifications.

A name change was implemented in 2005 and again later, without a new revision number or marked changes for these conversions. For Revision 1, QA and materials requirements were temporarily moved to [[0-SPC-7040_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment]]. For Revision 2, these QA and materials requirements were reinserted into this specification. Revision 3 saw the addition of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics reference specifications, an expansion of Section 4 (Materials), and modifications to Section 11.1 to include pneumatic and hydrostatic testing, along with the addition of Section 12 (Quality Documentation).
