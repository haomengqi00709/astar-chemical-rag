# Pump Calculations Procedure (4-PRC-0007)

This procedure, 4-PRC-0007, outlines the methodology for preparing pump calculations, ensuring a clear, checkable, and technically adequate record of pump sizing throughout a project's lifecycle. The process involves several key stages, from initial quotation requests to final impeller trim, with distinct information requirements and responsibilities.

**Purpose**
The primary purpose of this procedure is to standardize the calculation of pump hydraulic requirements, including Total Dynamic Head (TDH), Net Positive Suction Head Available (NPSHa), and Maximum Suction Pressure. These calculations are critical for proper pump selection and design.

**Roles and Responsibilities**
*   **Project Personnel:** Responsible for preparing pump calculations.
*   **Process Engineer:** Responsible for creating the Process Data Sheet (PDS) and confirming that temperature rise from minimum flow does not cause process problems.
*   **Hydraulic Engineer:** Utilizes 'Pipe-Flo' software to calculate pump hydraulic requirements at various stages. This role is generally performed by a Mechanical or Process Engineer.
*   **Requisition Originator:** Enters applicable information from the PDS and hydraulic calculation results into the Data Sheet (DST). They also prepare Requisitions for Purchase Enquiry / Order and perform final checks on motor, coupling, and baseplate sizing. The Requisition Originator **must** ensure that calculations are completed to allow the pump vendor to meet the delivery schedule. They **must** also ensure that the pump and piping can handle the temperature rise from minimum flow.

**Pump Sizing Stages**
Pump sizing is performed at three distinct stages:
1.  **Request for Quotation (RFQ) Stage:** Preliminary sizing based on estimated data.
2.  **Request for Purchase (RFP) Stage:** Recalculation using vendor-supplied data and updated design information.
3.  **Final Impeller Trim:** Final adjustments based on detailed design data.

Each stage has specific information requirements and calculation steps, detailed further in [[pump_sizing_stages]].

**Documentation and Storage**
All calculations are stored electronically and in hard copy. Electronic files are located in the project directory under `4-Equipment/Cal/Hydraulic`. Hard copies are maintained in a calculation binder. For detailed requirements, refer to [[pump_calculation_documentation_and_storage]].

*   Setting restriction orifice plate sizing criteria.
