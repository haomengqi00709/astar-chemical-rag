# Electrical Systems Contractor Checkout Procedure (9-PRC-0020)

This procedure, document ID 9-PRC-0020, outlines the verification process for the installation of all electrical devices, ensuring they comply with project drawings and specifications. Electrical devices **must** be ready for the subsequent electrical systems field checkout by an Aker Kvaerner Chemetics specialist. The various [[electrical_systems_contractor_check_lists]] referenced within this procedure will form a critical part of the overall [[electrical_completion_package]].

### Contractor Responsibilities
During the construction phase, the site sub-contractor is responsible for checking the completeness of the electrical system installation. Each electrical system item **must** be inspected and signed-off in accordance with the attached Electrical Systems Contractor Check Lists. The site sub-contractor **must** approach the [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_site_supervisor_review|Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site supervisor]] for review of the completed checklists.

### Deficiency Management
All findings, including any deficiencies, **must** be recorded on a separate sheet that is attached to the respective Check Lists. These recorded deficiencies **must** then be submitted to the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site supervisor. The site supervisor will work in conjunction with a deficiency crew, supplied by the site sub-contractor, to ensure all identified deficiencies are corrected. This includes issues identified by both the sub-contractor's own checks and those noted by the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site supervisor.

### Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Site Supervisor Role
The Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site supervisor is responsible for visually reviewing the installed electrical systems. This review is conducted against the relevant [[electrical_general_arrangement_drawings|general arrangement]] and [[electrical_installation_details|installation instructions]]. Once satisfied with the installation and the resolution of any deficiencies, the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site supervisor **must** sign the completed Electrical Contractor Check Lists. Following this, the site supervisor **must** notify the Project Manager that the equipment is ready for a final review by an Aker Kvaerner Chemetics specialist.

### Prerequisites and Tools
A fundamental prerequisite for initiating this checkout procedure is the completion of the electrical installation. The tools required for these checks are "As required," depending on the specific inspection tasks.

The procedure details a multi-step verification process. Check Lists, which are referenced in a section titled "Electrical Systems Field Check Lists for Contractor", will form part of the electrical completion package. A key prerequisite for the checkout is a completed electrical installation. Tools required for the process are "As required".

**Verification Process Flow:**
1.  **Sub-contractor Initial Check:** During construction, the site sub-contractor performs an initial check for the completeness of the electrical system installation.
2.  **Supervisor Review Request:** The site sub-contractor must then approach the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site supervisor for his review.
3.  **Item Inspection & Sign-off:** Each electrical system item is inspected and signed-off in accordance with the attached Electrical Systems Contractor Check Lists.
4.  **Findings & Deficiencies:** Any findings are recorded on a separate sheet attached to the Check Lists. All identified deficiencies are recorded and subsequently given to the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site supervisor.
5.  **Supervisor Visual Review:** The Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site supervisor visually reviews the installed electrical systems against the general arrangement and installation instructions.
6.  **Supervisor Sign-off:** When satisfied, the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site supervisor signs the completed Electrical Contractor Check Lists.
7.  **Deficiency Correction:** The site supervisor works with a deficiency crew, supplied by the site sub-contractor, to correct all deficiencies identified by either the sub-contractor or the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site supervisor.
8.  **Project Manager Notification:** Once the supervisor is satisfied, they notify the Project Manager that the equipment is ready for a final review.
9.  **Final Review:** This final review is conducted by an Aker Kvaerner Chemetics specialist.
