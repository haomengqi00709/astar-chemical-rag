# HSE Training Program Overview

Procedure 0-PRC-8016 establishes the minimum training requirements for Chemetics employees concerning company health, safety, and environment (HSE) procedures and standards. Employee education is considered an essential component of the overall Chemetics HSE program.

Training types are categorized based on their origin:
*   **Regulatory Requirements:** Some training is mandated by health and safety regulatory agencies.
*   **Management Determination:** Other training types are identified and determined by Management on an as-needed basis.

Training needs are subject to analysis and identification to ensure relevance and effectiveness. Project Managers and Construction Managers **must** plan and budget for safety training within all projects, ensuring that management and supervisory training is carried out in a timely manner.

A comprehensive training inventory **will** be maintained by the HSE Manager to facilitate the identification and addressing of special needs situations. Training programs, where possible, **should** incorporate lesson plans, appropriate training materials, visual aids, tests, and robust records of training and qualification.

Employees are actively encouraged to enhance their HSE skills and awareness through external training and educational opportunities. However, prior to attending any external HSE courses, approval **must** be obtained from the company’s HSE Manager.
