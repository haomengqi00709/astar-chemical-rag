# 5-LST-0001 Line List

The 5-LST-0001 Line List is a critical engineering document for the Chemetics project, serving as the definitive source for mechanical design conditions related to piping systems. This list is essential for the accurate selection of pipe materials, components, and associated specifications.

**Purpose and Content**
The [[line_list_5_lst_0001]] defines all mechanical design conditions required for piping within the plant. These conditions include, but are not limited to:
*   **Temperature:** Process and mechanical design temperatures.
*   **Pressure:** Process, mechanical design, and relief pressures.
*   **Relief Requirements:** Specifications related to pressure relief devices.
*   **Insulation:** Requirements for thermal or acoustic insulation.
*   **Material Selection:** Data necessary for selecting appropriate pipe materials, fittings, and valves.

**Coordination Requirements**
A key requirement for the [[line_list_5_lst_0001]] is the mandatory coordination of its mechanical design conditions with the utility fluid design conditions detailed in the [[site_design_conditions_1_dst_0001]] data sheet. This ensures that piping systems are designed consistently with the overall plant utility specifications.

The [[line_list_5_lst_0001]] is one of five primary documents that collectively define the process and mechanical design conditions for piping and equipment throughout the plant. For a comprehensive understanding of this documentation framework, refer to [[process_and_mechanical_design_document_hierarchy]].

**Purpose and Content**
The 5-LST-0001 Line List defines all mechanical design conditions (including temperature, pressure, relief, and insulation requirements) necessary for the accurate selection of pipe materials and components. Mechanical design conditions on the Line List shall be coordinated with utility fluid design conditions specified on the [[site_design_conditions_1_dst_0001]] data sheet.
