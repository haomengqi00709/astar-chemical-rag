# 0-LST-6X13 Construction Subcontract Crew Rates

The 0-LST-6X13 document, known as "Construction Subcontract Crew Rates," is a form that the Subcontractor **must** complete. This document is used to present an average total cost per hour worked for each direct labour work crew that will be engaged in "Providing the Works" for the project.

The rates detailed in 0-LST-6X13 **must** be calculated using the individual [[construction_subcontract_labour_rates]] established in [[0-LST-6X12_construction_subcontract_labour_rates]]. This ensures that crew rates are consistently derived from the approved individual labour cost components. These crew rates are then utilized in calculating the total cost of [[construction_subcontract_direct_work_costs|direct labour]].
