# Vendor Inspection Release and Shipping Requirements

The final stage of the Vendor's quality assurance process, as defined in Specification [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment|0-SPC-7001]], involves the formal release of goods for packaging and shipping. Strict conditions **must** be met before this stage can proceed.

**Pre-Release Conditions**
The Vendor **must not** release the goods for packaging or shipping until the following conditions have been satisfactorily met:
*   All activities specified in the approved [[quality_data_sheets_qds|Quality Data Sheet]] and [[vendor_inspection_and_test_plan|Inspection and Test Plan]] have been satisfactorily completed.
*   All required quality documentation is available and acceptable to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Inspection Release Requirement**
For goods subject to [[chemetics_surveillance_level|Surveillance Level 1, 2, 3, or 4]], the Vendor **must** receive an Inspection Release from the Inspector before proceeding with packaging or shipping. This formal release signifies that the goods have passed all necessary inspections and documentation reviews.

**Scope of Inspection Release**
The Inspection Release applies only to the specific goods stated in its title. It **will** also list any remaining work required by the Vendor prior to the final packaging of the goods. This ensures that any minor outstanding items are clearly identified and addressed.
