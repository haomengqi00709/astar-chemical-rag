# Invoicing Documentation Requirements (0-PRC-2004)

As specified in Section 8.2 of the [[project_coordination_0_prc_2004]] document, specific documentation **must** be included with invoices. This mandatory requirement ensures transparency, facilitates verification, and supports proper record-keeping for all financial transactions related to the project.

Examples of documentation that **must** accompany invoices include:

*   **Ocean Bills of Lading:** These documents are required for equipment and materials shipments. They serve as proof of shipment and receipt of goods, which is essential for verifying material costs and ensuring that all delivered items are accounted for.
*   **Transmittals for Document Deliveries:** These confirm the successful delivery of required project documentation. The inclusion of transmittals with invoices can be crucial, especially when payments are linked to the completion or delivery of specific project documents.

Adherence to these documentation requirements is vital for maintaining smooth [[procurement_and_vendor_documentation]] processes and ensuring compliance with the overall [[project_financial_and_tax_structure]]. Proper documentation supports auditing, dispute resolution, and accurate financial reporting.
