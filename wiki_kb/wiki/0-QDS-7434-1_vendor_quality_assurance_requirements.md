# Vendor Quality Assurance Requirements for Lime Handling System (0-QDS-7434-1)

This section outlines the mandatory quality assurance requirements for the Vendor supplying the Lime Handling System, as specified in Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7434-1.

**Vendor Quality Plan:**
The Vendor **must** prepare a Quality Plan. This plan **shall** be in accordance with the requirements set forth in [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment|0-SPC-7001: Vendor Quality Assurance]].

**Vendor's Quality Assurance Compliance:**
The Vendor's overall Quality Assurance program **shall** comply with all requirements specified within this Quality Data Sheet (0-QDS-7434-1). Additionally, it **must** adhere to the general Vendor Quality Assurance specifications detailed in [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment|0-SPC-7001: Vendor Quality Assurance]].

**Chemetics Surveillance Level:**
Surveillance activities conducted by Chemetics for this project will be to Level 1.0. The specific surveillance activities and their associated requirements **shall** be as set out in this Quality Data Sheet.

**Note on Quality Plan Requirement:**
Cells related to the Quality Plan **should** be deleted from the Quality Data Sheet if a Quality Plan is not explicitly required for a specific procurement or component.
