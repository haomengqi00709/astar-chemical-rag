# Ejector Piping Design Requirements

This page details the piping design requirements for ejector systems, covering pumped fluid, motive fluid, and cooling water, as specified in the Template Ejector Datasheet (Document ID [[template_ejector_datasheet_administration]]). These requirements are essential for ensuring the safe and reliable operation of the piping system connected to the ejector.

**Piping Design Requirements by Fluid:**

**Pumped Fluid:**
*   **Design Pressure:** Maximum kPa(g), Minimum kPa(g)
*   **Design Temperature:** Maximum °C, Minimum °C
*   **Overpressure Protection:** Required
*   **Vacuum Protection:** Required

**Motive Fluid:**
*   **Design Pressure:** Maximum kPa(g), Minimum kPa(g)
*   **Design Temperature:** Maximum °C, Minimum °C
*   **Overpressure Protection:** Required
*   **Vacuum Protection:** Required

**Cooling Water:**
*   **Design Pressure:** Maximum kPa(g), Minimum kPa(g)
*   **Design Temperature:** Maximum °C, Minimum °C
*   **Overpressure Protection:** Required
*   **Vacuum Protection:** Required

These requirements align with general [[piping_design_criteria_5_prc_0002]] and are implemented through the [[piping_design_and_specification_5_prc_0003]] procedures.
