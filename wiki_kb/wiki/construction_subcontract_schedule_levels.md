# Construction Subcontract Schedule Levels

Construction Subcontractors are required to develop schedules to specific levels, particularly for works exceeding 3,500 direct labour hours, as outlined in [[0-SPC-6021_construction_subcontract_scheduling|0-SPC-6021]]. For smaller works, the [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_obligations_0_spc_2201|Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Site Manager]] determines the appropriate schedule levels.

The procedure defines four distinct schedule levels:

### Level 1 Schedule (Summary Schedule)
The Level 1 Schedule, also known as a Summary Schedule, provides a high-level overview of the project.
*   It **shall** show one activity bar for each [[major_work_division_codes|major work division]].
*   Key [[project_schedule_and_milestones|major project milestones]] **shall** be clearly indicated.
*   Activities presented in a Level 1 Schedule are derived as summaries of corresponding activities detailed in a [[#Level 2 Schedule (Control Schedule)|Level 2 Schedule]].

### Level 2 Schedule (Control Schedule)
The Level 2 Schedule, or Control Schedule, offers a more granular view than Level 1.
*   It **shall** show grouped work activities for various items (e.g., equipment, pipes, instruments) organized by type and/or functional area.
*   Typically, this level provides up to 20 activities for each [[major_work_division_codes|major work division]].
*   All [[project_schedule_and_milestones|project milestones]] **shall** be included in a Level 2 Schedule.
*   Activities at this level are derived as summaries of corresponding activities from a [[#Level 3 Schedule (Detailed Schedule)|Level 3 Schedule]].

### Level 3 Schedule (Detailed Schedule)
The Level 3 Schedule, known as a Detailed Schedule, provides a comprehensive breakdown of all work activities.
*   It **shall** show all work activities for all items (e.g., equipment, pipes, instruments) grouped by type and/or functional area.
*   All [[project_schedule_and_milestones|project milestones]] **shall** be shown in a Level 3 Schedule.
*   The status of activities are derived as summaries of the status of corresponding activities in a [[#Level 4 Schedule (Work Plan)|Level 4 Schedule]].

### Level 4 Schedule (Work Plan)
The Level 4 Schedule, or Work Plan, represents the most detailed level of scheduling.
*   It **shall** show all work activities for all items (e.g., equipment, pipes, instruments).
*   While it may be prepared as a bar chart, it is more commonly presented as a tabular checklist.
*   Work actually completed **shall** be checked off directly on the list.
*   Percent completions in a Level 4 Schedule are derived by counting completed work and considering the scheduled resources allocated to that work.
