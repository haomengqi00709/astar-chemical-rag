# HCl Synthesis Unit Process Design Basis

This page details the fundamental process design basis for the Template HCl Synthesis Unit Package, as outlined in document [[4-PDS-XXXX-R1_template_hcl_synthesis_unit_package_datasheet_administration]]. Understanding this basis is crucial for all subsequent engineering, procurement, and operational activities related to the unit.

**Process Description:**
The primary function of this unit is the combustion-based synthesis of HCl, specifically engineered to produce 32% HCl. This process utilizes a combination of strong chlorine, weak chlorine, and cell gas hydrogen as primary reactants. The controlled combustion reaction facilitates the efficient production of hydrochloric acid, a critical chemical in various industrial applications.

**Design Capacity:**
The unit's design basis specifies a production capacity of **XX MTPD** (Metric Tons Per Day) of 100% HCl. This capacity represents the maximum continuous output the unit is engineered to achieve under optimal operating conditions. The design ensures robust performance to meet project requirements.

**Turndown Capacity:**
The HCl Synthesis Unit is designed with a significant turndown capability to provide operational flexibility. It **must** be capable of operating at **25% of Design or less**, with the specific turndown capacity denoted as VTA. This allows the unit to adjust production rates efficiently in response to market demand or upstream/downstream process variations, without compromising product quality or operational stability.
