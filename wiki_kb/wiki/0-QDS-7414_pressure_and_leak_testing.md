# FRP Tank Pressure and Leak Testing (0-QDS-7414)

This page outlines the mandatory pressure and leak testing requirements for FRP Tanks, as specified in Quality Data Sheet **0-QDS-7414**. These tests are essential to confirm the structural integrity and leak-tightness of the tanks before commissioning.

**Mandatory Testing Requirements:**
The following tests are mandatory and **must** be included in the Vendor's Inspection and Test (I & T) Plan:

*   **Pressure/Leak Testing:** General pressure and leak testing procedures **must** be defined and executed to verify the containment capabilities of the FRP tank.
*   **Hydrotest:** A hydrostatic test **must** be performed, involving filling the tank with water and pressurizing it to a specified level to check for leaks and structural soundness.
*   **Vacuum Test:** A vacuum test **must** be conducted to assess the tank's ability to withstand negative pressure conditions without deformation or leakage.

These tests are critical steps in the quality assurance process for FRP tanks, ensuring they can safely operate under their intended service conditions. For general guidelines on pressure and leak testing, refer to [[0-QDS-7411_pressure_and_leak_testing]].
