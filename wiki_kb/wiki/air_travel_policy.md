# Air Travel Policy

The Group Travel Procedure (11.1 PR 2) establishes clear guidelines for air travel to ensure cost efficiency and employee safety.

### Class of Travel
For all domestic and international journeys, **ALL** travel **will** be Economy Class by default.

**Business Class Exceptions:**
Business class seating **will** be permitted under specific conditions:
*   For journeys involving more than nine hours of continuous flying.
*   For journeys where the total flying time exceeds twelve hours.

When business class is permitted, it **must** be booked via the most economic route available. Transatlantic flights may be booked in the most economic business class.

An exception to the class of travel rule may be made if the cost difference between a return economy/coach class ticket and a business class ticket is insignificant, typically defined as around NOK 500. In such cases, the higher fare may be booked.

### Special Travel Circumstances
For worldwide travel related to mobilisation, demobilisation, and holidays associated with overseas assignments and secondments, economy class travel **will** be utilized. Further details on these specific travel arrangements can be found in the [[international_assignment_procedure]].

### Connecting Flights
Travel agencies are authorized to recommend flights that include a connection if it helps achieve the lowest cost airfares. However, the stopover time for such recommended connecting flights **will not** normally exceed 2 hours.

### Group Travel Limits on Aircraft
To manage risk and ensure business continuity, specific restrictions apply to the number of senior personnel and employees traveling on the same aircraft:
*   No more than three SVPs or VPs and their direct reports may travel on the same aeroplane without prior approval from the Business Area EVP or local Company President/Managing Director.
*   No more than ten employees from a single Company are permitted to fly on the same aircraft without prior approval from the Business Area EVP or local Company President/Managing Director.

### Central Billing for Airline Travel
In some specific locations, airline travel **will** be booked using a central billing American Express card, streamlining the payment process.
