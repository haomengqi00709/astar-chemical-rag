# Carbon Steel Piping Welding Consumables and Environmental Controls (5-SPC-0122)

This page outlines the requirements for welding consumables and environmental controls during carbon steel piping fabrication, as specified in document 5-SPC-0122.

**Filler Materials**
*   Filler materials **shall** conform to the appropriate [[ASME_sfa_5_4_welding_electrodes|AWS/ASME SFA-5 series specification]].
*   Filler materials **shall** be stored in accordance with the manufacturer's recommendations.
*   **Mandatory:** All shielded metal arc welding shall use [[AWS_a3_0_standard_welding_terms_and_definitions|E 7018 electrodes]].
*   Root pass welding may use other classifications than E 7018.

**Low Hydrogen Electrodes Handling**
*   **Mandatory:** All low hydrogen electrodes shall be kept dry at all times, particularly during field work.
*   Low hydrogen electrodes **shall** be stored in accordance with manufacturers' recommendations.
*   Wet shielded metal arc electrodes **shall not** be oven baked or dried for reuse.
*   Wet shielded metal arc electrodes **shall** be discarded, and new electrodes obtained.

**Field Welding Protection**
*   Adequate protection from the weather **shall** be provided during field welding.
