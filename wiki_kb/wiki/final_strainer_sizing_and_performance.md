# Final Strainer Sizing and Performance

This page outlines the sizing criteria, calculated performance metrics, and operational requirements for the Final Strainer (Tag No. 7842K). These parameters ensure the strainer operates effectively within the specified process conditions.

**Velocity Criteria:**
*   **Suggested Acid Inlet Velocity:** 2.5 m/s
*   **Selected Acid Inlet Velocity/Diameter:** 250.0 (likely mm diameter)
*   **Suggested Acid Outlet Velocity:** 2.5 m/s
*   **Selected Acid Outlet Velocity/Diameter:** 250.0 (likely mm diameter)
*   **Suggested Drain Velocity:** 50.0
*   **Selected Drain Velocity:** 50.0
*   **Suggested Vent Velocity:** 25.0
*   **Selected Vent Velocity:** 25.0

**Calculated Performance:**
*   **Calculated Inlet Velocity:** 2.002664332807885 m/s
*   **Clean Pressure Drop:** 11.634063609488368 kPa
*   **Maximum Allowable Pressure Drop:** 100.0 kPa

**Mandatory Performance Requirements:**
*   The acid velocity through the basket openings **must not** exceed 0.6 m/s.
*   The basket **shall** be able to withstand the maximum allowable pressure drop.

**Nominal Sizes:**
*   **Nominal Line Size:** 250.0 mm
*   **Dry Strainer Nominal Size:** 150.0
*   **Absorber Strainer Nominal Size:** 200.0
*   **Final Strainer Nominal Size:** 250.0

Further details on the physical dimensions and nozzle configurations can be found in [[final_strainer_dimensions]] and [[final_strainer_nozzle_specifications]]. The materials of construction are specified in [[final_strainer_materials_of_construction]].

*   **Selected Acid Inlet Velocity/Diameter:** 250.0 (likely mm diameter)
*   **Selected Acid Outlet Velocity/Diameter:** 250.0 (likely mm diameter)
*   **Suggested Drain Velocity:** 50.0
*   **Selected Drain Velocity:** 50.0
*   **Suggested Vent Velocity:** 25.0
*   **Selected Vent Velocity:** 25.0

**Operational Limits:**
*   Acid velocity through basket openings is not to exceed 0.6 m/s.
