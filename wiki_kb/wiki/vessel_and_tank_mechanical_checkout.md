# Vessel and Tank Mechanical Checkout Requirements

This page outlines the mandatory requirements for the mechanical checkout of vessels and tanks, which are designated by suffixes T (Tanks, Receivers, Drums, Sumps), D (Cyclones, Precipitators, Dust collectors), J (Crystallizers, Evaporators), K (Decanters, Settlers, Clarifiers), R (Reactors, Converters), and U (Towers, Columns, Strippers, Absorbers). This process is part of the overall [[mechanical_equipment_checkout_procedure]].

**Required Documents:**
The following documents are mandatory for the checkout of vessels and tanks:
*   [[piping_and_instrumentation_drawings|P&ID]]
*   Vessel drawing
*   [[equipment_data_sheets_4_dst_xxxx|Equipment data sheet]]
*   [[boiler_code_certification_requirements|Boiler code certification]]

**General Installation and Structural Checks:**
*   **Mandatory:** Vessel nameplate data and record **must** match the outline drawing/data sheet information.
*   **Mandatory:** Vessel nameplate tag **must** match tag drawing.
*   **Mandatory:** Vessel base **must** be grouted, and [[equipment_grouting_requirements|grouting]] **must** be good.
*   **Mandatory:** Vessel elevation height **must** be consistent with the design.
*   **Mandatory:** All anchor bolts **must** be installed and tight.
*   **Mandatory:** All anchor chair connections **must** be installed as per outline drawing.
*   **Mandatory:** Nuts of any slide pads **must** be loose, allowing for expansion.
*   **Mandatory:** Slide pads **must not** be grouted.
*   **Mandatory:** Manway **must** be accessible and openable without obstruction.
*   **Mandatory:** Manway gasket material **must** be checked against the outline drawing.
*   **Mandatory:** Vessel ladder **must** be adequately supported and all bolts tight.
*   **Mandatory:** Vessel ladder **must** have an enclosed cage.

**Surface Treatment and Lining:**
*   **Mandatory:** Carbon steel vessels **must** have been primed and painted.
*   **Mandatory:** Vessels **must** be checked for gouges, cuts, scratches, etc.
*   **Mandatory:** [[coating_and_lining_inspection|Lining]] **must** be continuous.
*   **Mandatory:** Lining inserts **must** be sealed.
*   **Mandatory:** Lining seams **must** be smooth and continuous.

**Grounding and Connections:**
*   **Mandatory:** [[grounding_installation_details|Grounding cable]] connection **must** be tight.
*   **Mandatory:** Grounding cable **must** be connected to the main grounding loop.
*   **Mandatory:** Overflow nozzle **must** be connected and bolts tight.
*   **Mandatory:** Overflow piping **must** be connected to ground, trench, or [[overflow_seal_pot_design|seal pot]].
*   **Mandatory:** Both vent and drain **must** be clear of debris and open.
*   **Mandatory:** Screens **must** be provided where required for vents/drains.
*   **Mandatory:** Drain valve **must** be connected and lever **must** have unobstructed rotation.

**Insulation:**
*   **Mandatory:** Tank [[insulation_design_and_specification_6_prc_0002|insulation material]] **must** be checked against specification.
*   **Mandatory:** Insulation thickness **must** be checked.
*   **Mandatory:** Insulation sheet **must** cover the surface, and all connections **must** be made.

**Internal Components (Packing, Distributors, Mist Eliminators):**
*   **Mandatory:** Support plates **must** be installed correctly and fit.
*   **Mandatory:** Correct material and size of [[tower_internals_packing_specifications|packing]] **must** be supplied.
*   **Mandatory:** Packing or [[converter_catalyst_details|catalyst]] level **must** be at the correct height and correct volume installed.
*   **Mandatory:** Bed limiter plate **must** be the correct material, installed, and bolted down.
*   **Mandatory:** Distributor tray or piping **must** be the correct material, installed, clean, and free of debris.
*   **Mandatory:** [[mist_eliminator_candle_datasheet_administration_4_dst_xxxx_r0|Mesh pads or mist eliminators]] **must** be the correct materials, clean, and free of debris.
*   **Mandatory:** Mesh pads and mist eliminators **must** be supported and bolted in place.
*   **Mandatory:** [[vortex_breaker_installation|Vortex breakers]] **must** be installed where [[piping_and_instrumentation_drawings|P&ID]] indicates such.
*   **Mandatory:** Vortex breaker gasket position **must** comply with special item drawing.
*   **Mandatory:** Dip legs, nozzle inserts, etc. **must** be installed as per outline drawing with correct [[gasket_material_selection|gasket material]] and correct design dimensions.
*   **Mandatory:** Internal [[piping_design_and_specification_5_prc_0003|piping]] **must** be installed and all connections tight.
*   **Mandatory:** [[piping_design_and_specification_5_prc_0003|Plastic piping]] **must** be cemented.
*   **Mandatory:** Overflow seal pot dimensions **must** be correct.

**Nozzles and Flanges:**
*   **Mandatory:** Nozzle size and orientation **must** match outline drawing.
*   **Mandatory:** Nozzle gasket and thickness **must** match outline drawing/data sheet.
*   **Mandatory:** Nozzles **must** be open from the inside of the vessel and free of foreign debris.
*   **Mandatory:** Items connected to nozzle (instrument, valve, etc.) **must** be present.
*   **Mandatory:** Valve flange **must** be aligned to piping.
*   **Mandatory:** [[flange_bolting_requirements|Flange bolts]] **must** be adequately tightened.
*   **Mandatory:** Flange bolts **must not** be overtightened (i.e., no damage to gasket).
*   **Mandatory:** The nut **must** have at least three rounds of pipe thread protruding past the nut.
*   **Mandatory:** All [[spare_nozzle_blinding|spare nozzles]] **must** have installed blinds of the correct material.
*   **Mandatory:** "See notes on nozzle" is a requirement.

**Final Cleaning and Testing:**
*   **Mandatory:** Vessel **must** be [[water_and_air_trials_1_prc_0006|flushed]], walls clean, and free of debris.
*   **Mandatory:** Carbon steel tanks should be cleaned of rust.
*   **Mandatory:** [[pressure_and_leak_testing|Hydro test]] **must** have been carried out and signed as acceptable.

*   Nuts of any slide pads must be loose, allowing for expansion.

**Accessibility and Safety**
*   Manway must be accessible and openable without obstruction.
*   Manway gasket material must be checked against the outline drawing.
*   Vessel ladder must be adequately supported and all bolts tight.
*   Vessel ladder must have an enclosed cage.

**Coating and Lining**
*   Carbon steel vessels must have been primed and painted.
*   Vessels must be checked for gouges, cuts, scratches, etc.
*   Lining must be continuous (where applicable).
*   Lining inserts must be sealed (where applicable).
*   Lining seams must be smooth and continuous (where applicable).

**Grounding**
*   Grounding cable connection must be tight.
*   Grounding cable must be connected to the main grounding loop.

*   Overflow nozzle must be connected and bolts tight.
*   Overflow piping must be connected to ground, trench, or [[overflow_seal_pot_design|seal pot]].
*   Both vent and drain must be clear of debris and open.
*   Screens must be provided where required for vents/drains.
*   Drain valve must be connected and lever must have unobstructed rotation.
*   Dip legs, nozzle inserts, etc. must be installed as per outline drawing with correct [[gasket_material_selection|gasket material]] and correct design dimensions.
*   Nozzle size and orientation must match outline drawing.
*   Nozzle gasket and thickness must match outline drawing/data sheet.
*   Nozzles must be open from the inside of the vessel and free of foreign debris.
*   Items connected to nozzle (instrument, valve, etc.) must be present.
*   Valve flange must be aligned to piping.
*   [[flange_bolting_requirements|Flange bolts]] must be adequately tightened.
*   [[flange_bolting_requirements|Flange bolts]] must not be overtightened (i.e., no damage to gasket).
*   The nut must have at least three rounds of pipe thread protruding past the nut, as per [[flange_bolting_requirements]].
*   All [[spare_nozzle_blinding|spare nozzles]] must have installed blinds of the correct material.
*   "See notes on nozzle" is a requirement.

**Insulation**
*   Tank insulation material must be checked against specification.
*   Insulation thickness must be checked.
*   Insulation sheet must cover the surface and all connections must be made.

**Internals**
*   The Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site supervisor may carry out the check for vessel internals (packing material, size, etc.). If the site supervisor checks internals, the engineer will obtain a signed form stating internals have been checked and conform to design.
*   Support plates must be installed correctly and fit.
*   Correct material and size of packing must be supplied.
*   Packing or catalyst level must be at the correct height and correct volume installed.
*   Bed limiter plate must be the correct material, installed, and bolted down.
*   Distributor tray or piping must be the correct material, installed, clean, and free of debris.
*   Mesh pads or mist eliminators must be the correct materials, clean, and free of debris.
*   Mesh pads and mist eliminators must be supported and bolted in place.
*   Internal piping must be installed and all connections tight.
*   Plastic piping must be cemented.
*   [[overflow_seal_pot_design|Overflow seal pot]] dimensions must be correct.

**Post-Installation Checks**
*   Vessel must be flushed, walls clean, and free of debris.
*   Carbon steel tanks should be cleaned of rust.
*   Hydro test must have been carried out and signed as acceptable.
