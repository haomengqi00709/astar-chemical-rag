# Chlorate Reactor Parameter Units

This page serves as a comprehensive reference for the standard units of measurement used throughout the Chlorate Reactor Process Data Sheet (4-PDS-XXXX-R1). Consistent application of these units is essential for accurate engineering calculations, unambiguous equipment specifications, and clear communication across all project documentation. Adherence to these units ensures compatibility between different engineering disciplines and avoids misinterpretation of critical design and operating parameters.

For broader company standards on engineering units and their application, refer to [[engineering_units]].

**Mass Flow**
*   Unit: kg/hr (kilograms per hour)

**Operating Conditions**
*   Pressure Unit: kPa (g) (kilopascals gauge)
*   Temperature Unit: oC (degrees Celsius)

**Property Measurements**
*   Density Unit: kg/m3 (kilograms per cubic meter)
*   Reference Temperature Unit: °C (degrees Celsius)
*   Viscosity Unit: cP (centipoise)
*   Specific Heat Unit: kJ/kg·oC (kilojoules per kilogram per degree Celsius)
*   Thermal Conductivity Unit: W/m·oC (watts per meter per degree Celsius)

**Dimensional Measurements**
*   Side Wall Height / Length Unit: m (meters)
*   Inside Diameter Unit: m (meters)
*   Height Unit: m (meters)
*   Width Unit: m (meters)
*   Depth Unit: m (meters)

**Volume and Time**
*   Residence Time Unit: min (minutes)
*   Working Volume Unit: m3 (cubic meters)

These units are applied consistently across all relevant sections of the Chlorate Reactor PDS to maintain clarity and precision in technical documentation, facilitating effective project execution and operational understanding.

**Flow and Quantity Units**
*   **Mass Flow Unit:** kg/hr

**Operating Condition Units**
*   **Operating Conditions Pressure Unit:** kPa (g)
*   **Operating Conditions Temperature Unit:** oC

**Property Units**
*   **Property Density Unit:** kg/m3
*   **Property Reference Temperature Unit:** °C
*   **Property Viscosity Unit:** cP
*   **Property Specific Heat Unit:** kJ/kg·oC
*   **Property Thermal Conductivity Unit:** W/m·oC

**Dimension and Volume Units**
