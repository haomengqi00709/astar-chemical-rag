# Construction Subcontract Insurance and Security (0-SUB-6)

This page details the insurance provisions and security requirements for Construction Subcontract Agreement 0-SUB-6.

**Insurance Provisions:**
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **shall** provide insurance against specified applicable insurances as detailed in the subcontract.
*   The minimum amount of cover for the third insurance stated in the Insurance Table is AUD 5,000,000.

**Security Requirements:**
*   Security in the amount of 5% of the Price **must** be provided by the Subcontractor.
*   This security **must** be in the form of an irrevocable unconditional bank guarantee.

For further details on general insurance requirements, refer to [[insurance_requirements_0_spc_6001]]. For information on security and payment guarantees, see [[security_and_and_payment_guarantees_0_spc_6001]].
