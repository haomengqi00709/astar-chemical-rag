# Coupling Guard Installation

This page details the mandatory requirements for the installation and coating of [[coupling_guard_installation|coupling guards]] for [[rotating_equipment_mechanical_checkout|rotating]] and [[conveying_equipment_mechanical_checkout|conveying equipment]], ensuring safety and compliance with job requirements. Guards **must** be installed and their bolts tight.
