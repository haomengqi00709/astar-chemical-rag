# Burner Field Inspection Items

The Burner Field Inspection Checklist (4-FIC-XX26-R0) is a template designed to guide the inspection of burner installations. This checklist comprises a total of 24 items, each requiring verification during the inspection process.

**Checklist Structure and Content:**
*   The checklist includes a dedicated 'Checked' column for each item, where inspectors mark completion or compliance.
*   Items 12.0 through 24.0 are currently blank in this template, indicating placeholders for project-specific or additional inspection points as required.
*   The document features a "Deficiencies & Comments" section, providing 10 numbered slots for recording any identified issues, observations, or necessary corrective actions during the inspection.

**Mandatory Inspection and Documentation:**
*   **Requirement:** The inspection must be completed by a qualified vendor representative to ensure specialized expertise is applied.
*   **Requirement:** A comprehensive Vendor inspection report must be attached to this checklist upon completion. For details on vendor inspection reports, refer to [[vendor_inspection_report_requirements]].
*   **Requirement:** All Insulation around the burner and associated components must be complete. For general insulation erection specifications, see [[insulation_erection_0_spc_6x96]].
*   **Requirement:** All Cladding must be complete, providing necessary protection and finish. Related specifications for coatings and erection can be found in [[coatings_erection_0_spc_6x97]].

The completion of these inspection items is crucial for confirming the safe and correct installation of the burner, as detailed in [[burner_installation_requirements]]. The overall administration and sign-off procedures for this checklist are outlined in [[4-FIC-XX26-R0_burner_field_inspection_checklist_administration]].
