# 4-SCR-0022 Centrifugal Pump on Steel Support Criteria Basis

This page defines the foundational basis for the centrifugal pump support criteria, as specified in document 4-SCR-0022.

The basis for this support criteria refers to **Equipment Drawing Number 4-VEN-XXXX**, Revision 'X'. This drawing provides the detailed design and layout information from which the support requirements are derived.

All unfactored equipment loads, as detailed in [[4-SCR-0022_centrifugal_pump_unfactored_equipment_loads]], **must** be applied at the designated Datum point. The specific dimensions and materials for the support are outlined in [[4-SCR-0022_centrifugal_pump_support_dimensions_and_materials]].

For general information on equipment support criteria, refer to [[equipment_support_criteria]]. The referenced equipment drawing is considered a [[equipment_proprietary_detail_drawing_prp]].

Loads are to be applied at Datum.
