# Non-Destructive Examination Requirements for ESP Lead Lined Components

Non-Destructive Examination (NDE) for the Electrostatic Precipitator Lead Lined Components **must** adhere to recognized industry standards to ensure structural integrity and quality.

**Mandatory Requirements:**
*   Non-Destructive Examination **shall** be performed in accordance with ANSI/AWS Structural Welding Code D1.1.
*   Alternatively, NDE may be performed in accordance with an approved equivalent to ANSI/AWS Structural Welding Code D1.1, provided such equivalent is formally approved by Chemetics.

These requirements ensure that all welding and critical structural elements of the lead-lined components meet the necessary safety and performance criteria. For general NDE requirements, refer to [[0-QDS-7_non_destructive_examination_requirements]].
