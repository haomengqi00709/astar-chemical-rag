# SS Tubing Tier 1 Packing and Shipping Requirements

For Stainless Steel Tubing Tier 1, specific packing and shipping requirements **must** be adhered to, ensuring the safe and undamaged delivery of the materials. Packing/Crating is a required activity within the quality assurance process.

All packing and shipping activities are subject to Chemetics Witness surveillance, ensuring compliance with specified procedures. Key requirements include:

*   **Packaging:** Chemetics Witness surveillance is required for the packaging process, ensuring that the tubing is adequately protected against damage during transit.
*   **Packing List:** A Packing List is a required quality record, designated as "AP" (As Prepared). This list **must** be prepared by the vendor and is subject to Chemetics Witness surveillance, verifying accuracy and completeness of shipped items.
*   **Container Loading:** Chemetics Witness surveillance is required during container loading to ensure proper handling, securing, and arrangement of the tubing within the shipping containers, minimizing the risk of damage.
*   **Truck Loading:** Similarly, Chemetics Witness surveillance is required during truck loading, ensuring that the tubing is safely loaded and secured for overland transport.

These requirements are crucial for maintaining the quality and integrity of the Stainless Steel Tubing from the point of manufacture to its final destination.
