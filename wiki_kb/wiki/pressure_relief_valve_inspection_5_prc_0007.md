# Pressure Relief Valve Inspection (5-PRC-0007)

The inspection of pressure relief valves (PRVs) is a critical safety-related activity within the [[mechanical_checkout_piping_5_prc_0007|Piping Mechanical Checkout Procedure (5-PRC-0007)]]. This ensures that these vital safety devices are correctly installed and configured to protect equipment from overpressure.

**Required Documentation for Pressure Relief Valve Checkout:**
*   [[piping_and_instrumentation_drawings|P&ID]]
*   [[valve_specification|Valve Specification]]
*   [[pipe_isometric|Isometric]]

**Pressure Relief Valve Inspection Steps:**
*   **Flange Checks:** All flange checks for pressure relief valves **shall** be performed as described in the [[flange_inspection_requirements_5_prc_0007|Flange Inspection Requirements]].
*   **Type and Tag Verification:** Confirm that the valve type and [[pressure_relief_equipment_tagging|tag number]] match the [[piping_and_instrumentation_drawings|P&ID]] and [[pipe_isometric|isometric]].
*   **Vendor Tag Details:** Confirm that the vendor valve tag displays the correct model number, set pressure etc.
*   **Accessibility:** Confirm that the valve installation is accessible for both operation and maintenance, including any required testing.
*   **Flow Direction:** Confirm that the valve has been installed in the correct flow direction.
*   **Body and Discharge Piping Support:** Confirm that the valve body and discharge piping have been adequately supported (where applicable) to manage reaction forces during relief.
*   **Testing Lever Operation:** For valves equipped with a testing lever, confirm that the lever operates freely and is accessible.

These rigorous checks are paramount for ensuring the integrity and reliability of pressure relief systems, which are fundamental to plant safety.
