# Banked Time Policy

Employees eligible for compensation for hours worked in excess of the normal hours of work have the option to request that time equivalent to their overtime compensation be "banked."

**Purpose of Banked Time**
Banked time is intended to be taken as time off at a future date, providing flexibility for employees who accrue excess hours.

**Banked Time Limits**
Employees **shall not** hold more than 75 hours in their time bank at any given time. If an employee requests time to be banked on their [[0-pol-9113_timesheet_procedures]] and this request would cause their time bank to exceed the 75-hour cap, any time in excess of this limit **will be paid out** to the employee.

This policy is an integral part of the [[0-pol-9113_overtime_compensation_policy_overview]].

**Banked Time Limits**
Employees are not permitted to hold more than 75 hours in their time bank at any given time. Any time requested to be banked on timesheets that exceeds this 75-hour cap will be paid out to the employee.
