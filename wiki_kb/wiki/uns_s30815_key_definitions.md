# UNS S30815 Welding Standard - Key Definitions

This page provides definitions for key terms used throughout the UNS S30815 Welding Standard to ensure consistent understanding and application.

*   **Agreement in Writing**: Refers to a submission in writing by letter, and not merely by inclusion in approval drawings.
*   **Inspector**: Means Quality Assurance Inspector or other Authorized Representative.
