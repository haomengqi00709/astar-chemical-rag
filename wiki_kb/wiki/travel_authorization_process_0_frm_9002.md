# Travel Authorization Process

The Travel Authorization Form is a mandatory document for initiating company travel. It serves as the primary record for travel requests, approvals, and the subsequent activation of various support services. Travelers **must** use the latest version of the form, which is available in PDF format with fillable fields at Q:\\0 Admin\\9 Corporate\\90 General\\0-FRM-9002-R4 Travel Request Form.pdf.

**Submission and Approval Flow:**
1.  The traveler fills in the required sections of the form.
2.  The completed form is then emailed to the Department Manager for review and signature.
3.  Upon approval, the Department Manager forwards the signed form to the Budget Manager.
4.  The Budget Manager **must** sign the form and email the PDF to the "DL-ECVAN-TRAVEL" distribution list. The Budget Manager **must** also copy the traveler on this email.
5.  A copy of the approved form **must** be retained by the traveler to attach to their [[0-PRC-9003_expense_report_submission_and_documentation|expense report]].

**Required Information on the Form:**
*   **Dates and Times:** Travelers should specify the latest required arrival dates and times, and the earliest required departure dates and times.
*   **Employee Status:** Travelers **must** check one Employee Status box.
*   **Trip Type:** Indicate whether the trip is Business or Economy, in accordance with the Group Travel Procedure.
*   **Charge Codes:** Charge Codes **must** be correctly and completely filled in for the form to be accepted and processed.
*   **Special Sections:** Travelers **must** complete sections for US Immigration letters, site compensation, [[visa_requirements_for_travel|visa requirements]], and [[0-PRC-9003_cash_advance_policy|cash advances]] if they apply.
*   **Policy Deviations:** If deviating from company policy, travelers **must** fill in the "requested deviation from policy" section with a clear reason. The traveler’s senior line manager (Director or equivalent) **must** also be indicated for such requests.

The bottom area of the form is reserved for Travel Office use only.

**Initiated Actions Upon Submission:**
The submission of a Travel Authorization Form initiates several key actions:
*   **Travel Booking:** FCM begins looking into travel routes and prices.
*   **Medical Advisory:** [[travel_immunization_and_medical_advisory|Medisys]] contacts the traveler regarding recommended immunizations.
*   **Documentation Support:** Preparation of [[travel_health_and_documentation_support|Immigration letters]] and assessment of [[travel_health_and_documentation_support|Visa requirements]].
*   **Compensation:** Processing of [[assignment_uplift|Assignment Uplift]] by HR.
*   **Financial Support:** Arrangement of [[0-PRC-9003_cash_advance_policy|cash advances]].
*   **Special Approvals:** Initiation of any necessary special approvals.

For information on how to sign the form digitally, refer to [[digital_signatures_for_travel_authorization]]. For details on expense reimbursement, consult the [[0-PRC-9003_business_expense_reimbursement_policy]].
