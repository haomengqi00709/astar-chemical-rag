# Agitator Field Inspection Documentation and Sign-off (4-FIC-XX01-R0)

The completion of the Agitator Field Inspection Checklist (4-FIC-XX01-R0) requires specific documentation and formal sign-off to ensure compliance and record-keeping.

**Mandatory Documentation and Reporting:**
*   A report of motor rotation verification **must** be attached to this checklist.
*   Test Reports **must** be completed.
*   A copy of Test Reports **must** be attached to this checklist.
*   All required documentation forms part of the [[agitator_quality_record_definitions]].

**Mandatory Sign-off Requirements:**
*   Signatures are required from [[aker_kvaerner_chemetics_company_details|Aker Kvaerner Chemetics]], the Sub Contractor, and the Client.
*   Dates are required for signatures from Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, the Sub Contractor, and the Client.
*   Names are required for Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, the Sub Contractor, and the Client in the signature section.

Compliance with these sign-off procedures ensures proper handover and acceptance of the agitator equipment. Subcontractors should adhere to [[subcontractor_document_standards]] for all submitted documentation.
