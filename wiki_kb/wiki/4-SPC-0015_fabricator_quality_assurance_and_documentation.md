# Fabricator Quality Assurance and Documentation (4-SPC-0015)

The Fabricator **must** adhere to stringent quality assurance and documentation requirements for all work performed under Standard 4-SPC-0015.

**Subcontracting and Supervision**
*   Sub-Contracting of welding is **not permitted** without Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' written approval.
*   The Fabricator **must** ensure regular and systematic supervision of all work (both in-shop and on-site) by competent staff.

**Welder Qualifications and Traceability**
*   The Fabricator **must**, upon request, supply Aker Kvaerner Chemetics with objective evidence of current welder qualifications.
*   All qualified welders **must** be allocated an identification symbol, which **must** be marked on the parent material adjacent to each welder's work to allow traceability during fabrication. For detailed requirements, see [[4-SPC-0015_welding_procedure_and_welder_qualifications]].

**Material Control and Contamination Prevention**
*   The Fabricator **must** take controls to prevent contamination of weldments at all stages of fabrication. These controls **must** include the segregation of wire brushes, grinding wheels, and other tools for use exclusively on nickel-base materials.
*   The Fabricator **must** ensure and demonstrate to the Inspector that manufacturers' instructions regarding the storage, drying, and use of weld consumables are fully implemented (in shop and on-site).
*   The Fabricator **must** maintain full traceability of materials back to original mill certification.
*   The Fabricator’s internal controls **must** prevent the use of unspecified materials. Lack of adequate identification or certification will be cause for rejection of the specific component or item.

**Documentation Requirements**
The Fabricator **must** furnish a report containing all data pertaining to each vessel or item. Prior to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' final inspection, the Fabricator **must** have prepared three sets of records (excluding final test data) in finished form for each unit. This documentation **must** include:
*   Material mill test reports
*   Nameplate rubbing
*   Hydrostatic test report
*   A copy of all inspection and test reports

Reports and final test data **must** be assembled into three complete sets for each item. A documentation package **must not** pertain to more than one vessel or item number. The Inspector will collect the appropriate number of documentation packages for each item tested at the time of witnessing final tests and certifying the correctness of forms.

**Non-Conformance and Repair Costs**
*   Where results of chemical analysis show that a specific weldment falls outside of the approved specification or code requirements, the supplier **will promptly advise** Aker Kvaerner Chemetics in writing of his proposed course of action. This proposed course of action **must** include details of any additional testing necessary to fully define the extent of the non-conformance. Refer to [[0-NCR-7XXX_template_non_conformance_report]] for non-conformance reporting procedures.
*   All repairs and retests, including any additional spot radiography required as defined in UW-52 Sub-para. (d) of ASME Section VIII, Div. 1, will be to the Fabricator’s account.

These requirements ensure adherence to [[vendor_quality_assurance]] principles throughout the fabrication process.
