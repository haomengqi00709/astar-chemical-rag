# Request for Quotation and Purchase Order Responsibilities

The preparation and management of Request for Quotations (RFQs) and Purchase Orders (POs) involve clear assignment of responsibilities across various project functions. This ensures that all necessary information is accurately compiled and reviewed by the appropriate personnel. These responsibilities were clarified in Revision 3 of Procedure [[form_of_purchase_order_0_prc_5001]].

The following outlines the specific responsibilities for each section and line item within an RFQ or PO:

**Requisitioner Responsibilities:**
The Requisitioner is primarily responsible for defining the technical scope and documentation requirements.
*   **Line Items 1.x (Scope of Supply):** The Requisitioner is responsible for detailing the descriptions, tag numbers, cost codes, and pricing for all goods and services.
*   **Line Items 2.x (Documentation):** The Requisitioner is responsible for specifying all documentation deliverables required from the Vendor.
*   **Text Section 1: Scope of Supply:** The Requisitioner is responsible for listing all documents that define the technical requirements of the Goods and Services.
*   **Text Section 2: Documentation:** The Requisitioner is responsible for listing all documents that define the requirements for documentation to be supplied by the Vendor.

**Logistics Responsibilities:**
The Logistics department is responsible for all aspects related to delivery and packaging.
*   **Line Items 3.x (Delivery):** Logistics is responsible for detailing packaging and shipping requirements.
*   **Text Section 3: Delivery:** Logistics is responsible for listing all documents that define packaging requirements and for stating the Delivery Date(s).

**Quality Assurance Responsibilities:**
The Quality Assurance department is responsible for ensuring that vendor quality standards are met.
*   **Text Section 4: Quality Assurance:** Quality Assurance is responsible for listing all documents that define the requirements for the Vendor’s quality assurance, as well as the requirements for review, inspection, and verification.

**Project Buyer Responsibilities:**
The Project Buyer manages the commercial aspects, references, and contact information for the procurement.
*   **Text Section 5: Commercial:** The Project Buyer is responsible for listing all documents that define the commercial conditions and for including Order-Specific Conditions.
*   **Text Section 6: References:** The Project Buyer is responsible for listing all pertinent Vendor documents, such as the original quotation, clarifications, or modifications.
*   **Text Section 7: Contact:** The Project Buyer is responsible for stating the names and details of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics contact persons (Project Buyer, Lead Engineer, and Inspector) and Vendor contact persons, including any Vendor Key Personnel.

For a detailed breakdown of the content required in each section, refer to [[request_for_quotation_and_purchase_order_structure_and_content]].
