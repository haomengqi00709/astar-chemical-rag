# Safety Tagout Order and Documentation

This page describes the [[lockout_tagout_definitions#safety_tagout_order|Safety Tagout Order]] and other essential documentation requirements for the [[0-PRC-8504-RA_lockout_and_tagging|Lockout and Tagout Procedure]].

**Safety Tagout Order (Form FO1)**
The [[lockout_tagout_definitions#safety_tagout_order|Safety Tagout Order]] is utilized to control, document, audit, and status the safety tagout of equipment/processes. All information pertaining to safety tagout necessary to satisfy these objectives **will** be recorded on the safety tagout order form. The form includes the means to [[lockout_tagout_definitions#accept|accept]] and [[lockout_tagout_definitions#release|release]] administrative control for an equipment/process.

The safety tagout order form has three parts and **will** include:
*   An index of all safety tagout orders.
*   An individual sequential order number.
*   The field site.
*   An accurate description of the equipment/process.
*   An accurate description of the work to be performed.
*   A complete listing of all [[lockout_tagout_definitions#energy_isolating_device|energy isolating devices]], sequential order of manipulation, device position, and locks/tags.
*   Any special instructions.
*   The placement of protective grounds **must** be recorded.

Required signatures, dates, and times to be recorded:
*   Of the [[lockout_tagout_definitions#authorized_individual|authorized individual]] ([[lockout_tagout_roles_and_responsibilities#commissioning_hse_manager|Commissioning HSE Manager]]) reviewing and approving the order.
*   Of the [[lockout_tagout_definitions#authorized_individual|authorized individual]] ([[lockout_tagout_roles_and_responsibilities#commissioning_hse_manager|Commissioning HSE Manager]]) performing the safety tagout on the equipment/process.
*   Name, signature, date, and time of the [[lockout_tagout_definitions#authorized_individual|authorized individual]] [[lockout_tagout_definitions#accept|accepting a clearance]] or [[lockout_tagout_definitions#release|releasing]] the equipment/process.
*   Of the [[lockout_tagout_definitions#authorized_individual|authorized individual]] ([[lockout_tagout_roles_and_responsibilities#commissioning_hse_manager|Commissioning HSE Manager]]) ordering the equipment/process [[lockout_tagout_definitions#restored|restored]] to service.
*   Of the [[lockout_tagout_definitions#authorized_individual|authorized individual]] removing the tags/locks and [[lockout_tagout_definitions#restored|restoring]] the equipment/process to service.

**Authorized List (Form FO2)**
An Authorized list (Form FO2) **will** be prepared and maintained by the [[lockout_tagout_roles_and_responsibilities#commissioning_hse_manager|Commissioning HSE Manager]] for use on the site. This list **will** include only [[lockout_tagout_definitions#knowledgeable_individual|knowledgeable appointed individuals]] to perform required actions.

**Tag Information**
All information required on the tags **must** be filled in before the tags are attached. Minimum information on tags includes: an order number, the date, the name of the [[lockout_tagout_definitions#authorized_individual|authorized individual]] for whom the tagout is performed, an accurate identification of the equipment/process, and the work to be performed. An order number designates the year and the sequential number of the respective order (example 98-001).

**Multiple Authorized Individuals**
When more than one [[lockout_tagout_definitions#authorized_individual|authorized individual]] requests safety tagout on the same equipment/process, a separate safety tagout order **will** be prepared for each person, and separate lock & tags **will** be applied for each person, except when [[group_safety_tagout_procedure|"Group Safety Tagout is Approved"]].

**Records Maintenance**
The [[lockout_tagout_roles_and_responsibilities#commissioning_hse_manager|Commissioning HSE Manager]] **will** keep and maintain a complete set of records pertaining to the safety tagout program. Records **will** consist of sequentially numbered records of all safety tagout orders and a list of [[lockout_tagout_definitions#knowledgeable_individual|knowledgeable appointed individuals]] to perform the required actions of the safety tagout program.

**Forms**
*   Form DP-802.710 is the Safety Tagout Order Index (F01a).
*   The Safety Tagout Order is designated (F01b).
*   The Safety Tagout Order Supplemental Sheet is designated (F01c).
*   The Authorized List is designated (F02).
