# Quench Venturi Quality Data Sheet Administration

This Quality Data Sheet (QDS), identified as **0-QDS-7421**, outlines the quality assurance and inspection requirements for the Quench Venturi. The document is prepared for Aker Kvaerner, a division of Aker Kvaerner Canada Inc., under the project title "Quench Venturi". The template used for this QDS is Chemetics. The client's internal document number for this standard is Standard-0-QDS-7421-R1.

**Vendor Quality Assurance and Surveillance:**
The Vendor **must** prepare a Quality Plan in accordance with [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance]]. The Vendor's Quality Assurance program **shall** comply with the requirements specified in this Quality Data Sheet and [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance]]. Note that cells within the QDS pertaining to a Quality Plan are to be deleted if a Quality Plan is not required for a specific item or project.

Chemetics surveillance for the Quench Venturi will be conducted at **Level 1.0**, as further detailed within this Quality Data Sheet. For general information on Chemetics surveillance levels, refer to [[0-QDS-7411_chemetics_surveillance_level]].

**Quality Record Definitions:**
The following abbreviations are used to denote quality record requirements:
*   **AP (As Prepared):** Indicates that the quality record is required to be submitted as prepared by the Vendor.
*   **FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package):** Indicates that the quality record **must** be included as part of the final Quality Assurance Documentation Package submitted by the Vendor.

For further details on specific inspection and documentation requirements, refer to [[0-QDS-7421_inspection_and_documentation_requirements]].
