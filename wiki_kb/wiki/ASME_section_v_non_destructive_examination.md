# ASME Section V - Non-destructive Examination

ASME Boiler & Pressure Vessel Code (BPVC) Section V provides requirements and methods for non-destructive examination (NDE) of materials and components. It covers various NDE techniques, including radiographic, ultrasonic, liquid penetrant, magnetic particle, and visual examination, used to detect surface and subsurface discontinuities without damaging the material.

This section of the ASME BPVC is referenced in [[4-SPC-0014_referenced_codes_and_definitions]] and specifically in [[4-SPC-0014_inspection_and_testing_requirements]] for the helium mass spectrometer testing of tube/tube sheet joints and tube bundles.

For detailed procedures and acceptance criteria for non-destructive examinations, consult the latest edition of ASME Section V.
