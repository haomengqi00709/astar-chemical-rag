# 0-POL-9111 Statutory Holidays

This section of procedure 0-POL-9111 outlines the provisions for statutory holidays for employees on short-term site assignments.

**Eligibility**
Employees on short-term assignments will be eligible for paid local statutory holidays.

**Working on Home Office Statutory Holidays**
If an employee works on-site on a home office statutory holiday, they will be able to bank 8.25 hours in lieu. This banking provision does not apply if the employee took an equivalent local statutory holiday during the assignment.
