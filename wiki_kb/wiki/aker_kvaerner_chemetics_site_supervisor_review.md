# Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Site Supervisor Review

The Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Site Supervisor plays a critical role in overseeing and verifying construction activities, particularly during the [[mechanical_checkout_piping_5_prc_0007|piping mechanical checkout procedure]]. The site supervisor is responsible for visually reviewing installed piping against [[pipe_isometric|isometric drawings]] and contractor punch lists to ensure work completion and adherence to specifications. When satisfied with the quality of work and the accuracy of the punch list, the site supervisor **must** notify the [[project_manager_planning_responsibilities|Project Manager]]. This role is essential for maintaining quality control and ensuring compliance with project standards on site.
