# Engineering Subcontractor Quality Assurance Requirements

This specification (Document ID 0-SPC-7004) details the mandatory quality assurance requirements for Engineering Subcontractors.

**Quality Plan Establishment**
The Subcontractor **must** establish, implement, and maintain a Quality Plan. This plan **shall** ensure that all services provided comply with the requirements of the Subcontract. While the Quality Plan may preferably reference the Subcontractor’s existing Quality Management System, it **must** include the minimum elements specified below. These minimum requirements do not relieve the Subcontractor from complying with any other necessary criteria for an effective Quality Plan.

If the Subcontractor's Quality Management System has been previously approved by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, the Quality Plan may refer to these approved documents. In such cases, the Quality Plan **should** certify that no revisions have been made since the prior approval.

**Quality Plan Submission and Approval**
The Subcontractor **must** submit the Quality Plan, along with all referenced documents, for approval by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. Submission details are specified in the [[engineering_subcontract_agreement_data_sections|Subcontract Data]]. Any changes to the Quality Plan or its referenced documents **require** approval from Aker Kvaerner Chemetics before implementation.

**Minimum Elements of the Quality Plan:**
The Quality Plan **must** address the following key areas:

*   **Management and Control:** Details on organizational structure, responsibilities, authority, and reporting lines for quality-related activities. Refer to [[engineering_subcontractor_quality_plan_management_and_control]].
*   **Documents:** Procedures for controlling, reviewing, approving, distributing, and retaining all quality-related documents. Refer to [[engineering_subcontractor_quality_plan_document_control]].
*   **Resources:** Provisions for ensuring personnel competence through education, training, skills, and experience, including specific training related to the Quality Plan. Refer to [[engineering_subcontractor_quality_plan_personnel_competence]].
*   **Services:** Documented procedures for the provision and review of services to ensure completeness, adequacy, and conformance with Subcontract requirements, including design change control. Refer to [[engineering_subcontractor_quality_plan_services_provision]].
*   **Defects/Non-Conformances:** Procedures for identifying, documenting, correcting, and preventing the recurrence of defects and non-conformances. Refer to [[engineering_subcontractor_quality_plan_defect_management]].

**Auditing and Compliance**
The services provided by the Subcontractor will be subject to audits by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics to ensure accuracy and compliance with the Subcontract. For details on the audit process, refer to [[engineering_subcontractor_quality_audit_process]].
