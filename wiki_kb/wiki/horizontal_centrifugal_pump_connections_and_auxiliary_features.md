# Horizontal Centrifugal Pump Connections and Auxiliary Features

This page details the mandatory requirements for connections and auxiliary components of horizontal centrifugal pumps as per specification 4-SPC-0002.

**Process Connections**
*   Pump suction and discharge connections **must** be flat-faced flanged.
*   These connections **must** match the DIN PN10 standard to ensure compatibility with piping systems.

**Drains and Seal Fluid Connections**
*   A casing drain **must** be provided and fitted with an NPT plug.
*   The seal fluid connection **must** be provided with a temporary NPT plug.

**Baseplates and Couplings**
*   Baseplates and couplings **must** be sized sufficiently large to accommodate motors one size larger than the motor initially selected for the pump. This provides flexibility for future upgrades or changes.
*   Spacer blocks **must** be provided to facilitate the installation of a larger motor without requiring modifications to the pump's process connections.
*   Baseplates **must** be constructed from polymer concrete, offering enhanced durability and vibration dampening.

**Seal Chamber Design**
*   Pump seal chamber bores **must** be enlarged.
*   Pump seal chamber bores **must** also be tapered. These design features are critical for optimal mechanical seal performance and reliability.

**Seal Fluid Control Systems**
The seal fluid control systems themselves **will** be supplied by other parties. However, the Vendor **must** clearly specify the required seal fluid pressure and flow requirements to ensure proper integration and operation of the pump's sealing system.
