# Cable System Installation Field Check List (9-LST-0726)

The Cable System Installation Field Check List (9-LST-0726) is a specific document used during the [[9-PRC-0004_electrical_field_checkout_documentation|Electrical Field Checkout Procedure (9-PRC-0004)]] to verify the correct installation of all cabling within a project. This checklist ensures that cable systems meet design specifications, safety standards, and are ready for operation.

Items typically covered in the 9-LST-0726 checklist include:
*   Verification of correct cable routing as per [[cable_tray_installation_details|Cable Tray Layouts]] and [[electrical_cable_schedules|Cable Schedules]].
*   Confirmation of proper cable support and securing.
*   Inspection of cable terminations at equipment and junction boxes.
*   Verification of correct cable tagging and identification.
*   Checks for physical damage to cables.
*   Confirmation of proper grounding and shielding where required.

This checklist is attached to the [[9-PRC-0020_electrical_systems_contractor_checkout_procedure|Electrical Systems Contractor Checkout Procedure 9-PRC-0020]] and is signed off by the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site supervisor upon satisfactory completion of the inspection.
