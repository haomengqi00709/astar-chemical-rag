# Lockout/Tagout Key Definitions

This page provides definitions for key terms used within the [[9-prc-0019_lockout_tagout_procedure_overview|Lockout and Tagout Procedure (9-PRC-0019)]].

*   **Activation/Energisation**: To set machinery into motion by starting, switching, pushing, moving, or otherwise engaging power sources for such equipment.
*   **Authorised Individual**: A knowledgeable individual to whom the supervisor has given the authority and responsibility to implement a lockout/tagout procedure.
*   **Hazardous Motion**: Motion of equipment under mechanical stress or gravity that may abruptly release and cause injury. Hazardous motion may result even after power sources are disconnected. Examples include coiled springs, raised hydraulic equipment, and any sources of potential energy that may cause injury.
*   **Lockout**: Practice of using keyed security devices ("locks") to prevent unwanted activation of mechanical or electrical equipment.
*   **Tagout**: Practice of using tags in conjunction with locks to increase visibility and awareness that equipment is not to be energised or activated until such devices are removed.
