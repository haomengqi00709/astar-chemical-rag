# 0-QDS-7431 Vendor Quality Assurance Requirements

This page outlines the specific quality assurance requirements for vendors supplying Acid Brick & Lining, as detailed in [[0-QDS-7431_acid_brick_lining_quality_data_sheet_administration]].

The vendor **shall** prepare a Quality Plan. This plan **must** be in accordance with [[0-SPC-7002_vendor_quality_assurance]]. Data available in SQL: 0-SPC-7002.

The vendor's Quality Assurance program **shall** comply with the requirements set forth in this Quality Data Sheet (0-QDS-7431) and with [[0-SPC-7002_vendor_quality_assurance]]. Data available in SQL: 0-SPC-7002. This requirement is associated with item 0.0 in the source document.

**Mandatory Requirement:** The cells regarding the Quality Plan **should be deleted** if a Quality Plan is not required for a specific procurement.

For general vendor quality assurance principles and procedures, refer to [[vendor_quality_assurance]] and [[0-SPC-7002_vendor_quality_assurance]].

Vendor's Quality Assurance shall comply with this Quality Data Sheet and with [[0-SPC-7002_vendor_quality_assurance]]. This requirement is associated with item 0.0 in the source document.

**Quality Plan Management:**
The cells regarding the Quality Plan should be deleted if the Quality Plan is not required for a specific procurement.
