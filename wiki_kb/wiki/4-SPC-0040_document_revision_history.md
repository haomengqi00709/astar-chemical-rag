# 4-SPC-0040 Document Revision History

This page provides a detailed revision history for Specification 4-SPC-0040, which defines Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ requirements for shop fabrication of metallic process vessels and components.

**Original Specification (ES44/F06):**
*   This document is a complete re-write of the original specification ES44/F06.
*   The original specification ES44/F06 was titled "Minimum Manufacturing & Quality Requirements of Pressure Vessels & Heat Exchangers".

**Initial Re-write and Content Relocation:**
*   Much QA related wording has been removed from this document, as it is now covered by individual [[quality_data_sheets_qds]].
*   Details related to gas/gas exchangers have been moved to separate specifications.
*   Details related to converters have been moved to separate specifications.

**Name Change Conversions:**
*   A name change was implemented in 2005. No new revision number was used for this 2005 name change conversion, and changes were not marked.
*   Another name change was implemented at a later date. No new revision number was used for this name change conversion, and changes were not marked.

**Revision 1:**
*   QA requirements were moved to [[0-SPC-7040_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment]].
*   Materials requirements were moved to [[0-SPC-7040_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment]].

**Revision 2:**
*   QA requirements were reinserted into this specification.
*   Materials requirements were reinserted into this specification.

**Revision 3:**
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics reference specifications were added.
*   Section 4 (Materials) was expanded.
*   Section 5.6 (Chemical Analysis) was added.
*   Section 11.1 was modified to include pneumatic testing.
*   Section 11.1 was modified to include hydrostatic testing.
*   Section 12 (Quality Documentation) was added.
