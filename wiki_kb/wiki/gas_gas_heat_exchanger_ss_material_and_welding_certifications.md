# Gas/Gas Heat Exchanger - SS Material and Welding Certifications

For the Gas/Gas Heat Exchanger - SS project (Document ID: [[0-QDS-7403-2_gas_gas_heat_exchanger_ss_quality_data_sheet]]), specific certifications and qualifications related to materials and welding are mandatory. All listed items **must** be completed.

**Required Certifications and Qualifications:**

*   **Mill Test Certificates:**
    *   Required as "Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package" (FP).
    *   Chemetics requires Witness for Mill Test Certificates.
    *   Mill Test Certificates **must** be completed.

*   **Weld Consumable Certificates:**
    *   Required as "Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package" (FP).
    *   Chemetics requires Witness for Weld consumable certificates.
    *   Weld consumable certificates **must** be completed.

*   **Head Forming Certificates:**
    *   Required as "Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package" (FP).
    *   Chemetics requires Witness for Head forming certificates.
    *   Head forming certificates **must** be completed.

*   **Procedure Qualification for Tube-Tubesheet Welding:**
    *   Required as "As Prepared" (AP).
    *   Chemetics requires Witness for Procedure Qualification.
    *   Chemetics requires Hold for Procedure Qualification.
    *   Procedure Qualification **must** be completed.

*   **Welder Qualification:**
    *   Chemetics requires Witness for Welder Qualification.
    *   Welder Qualification **must** be completed.

*   **Production Welding Start:**
    *   Chemetics requires Witness for Production Welding start.
    *   Production Welding start **must** be completed.

These requirements ensure the quality and integrity of all materials and welding processes used in the fabrication of the Gas/Gas Heat Exchanger - SS.
