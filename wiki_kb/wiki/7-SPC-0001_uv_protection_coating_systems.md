# Protective Coating UV Protection Systems (7-SPC-0001 Table 3)

For specific applications requiring protection against ultraviolet (UV) radiation, `7-SPC-0001 Protective Coating Specification` mandates the use of specialized coating systems. These systems are to be applied only where explicitly indicated for UV protection.

The details of these UV protection systems are provided in [[7-SPC-0001_table_3_uv_protection_systems]]. It is crucial to consult this table to ensure the correct application of coatings for areas exposed to significant UV radiation, thereby preventing material degradation and maintaining aesthetic integrity.
