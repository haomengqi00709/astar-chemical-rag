# Fastener Specifications

As outlined in document 4-SPC-0001, specific standards **must** be followed for all fasteners used in equipment.

**Thread Standards**
Fasteners **will be** imperial in accordance with the American National Unified thread standard. They **shall be** in either Unified National Coarse (UNC) or Unified National Fine (UNF) sizes, ensuring compatibility and interchangeability.

**Nut and Bolt Requirements**
Heavy Hex nuts **will be used** for all applications. Bolts and studs **must have** a minimum of 2 threads projection beyond the nut, and a maximum of 4 threads projection. This ensures adequate thread engagement and prevents excessive protrusion.
