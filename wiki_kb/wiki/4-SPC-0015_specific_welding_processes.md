# Specific Welding Processes (4-SPC-0015)

This page details the specific requirements and parameters for various welding processes when fabricating nickel-base alloys under Standard 4-SPC-0015. These requirements supplement the [[4-SPC-0015_general_welding_requirements]].

**Shielded Metal Arc Welding (SMAW)**
*   Minimum practical amperage is recommended for SMAW.
*   Minimum arc length is recommended for SMAW to control the weld puddle.
*   A weave pattern is generally desirable for SMAW.
*   SMAW weave pattern **should not be wider** than 3x electrode core diameter.
*   SMAW electrodes **must** satisfy requirements of [[ASME_sfa_5_11_nickel_and_nickel_alloy_electrodes]]. The electrode **must** be Incoloy Welding Electrode 135.

**Gas Tungsten Arc Welding (GTAW)**
*   GTAW rods **must** satisfy requirements of [[ASME_sfa_5_14_nickel_and_nickel_alloy_electrodes_and_rods]].
*   GTAW filler wire diameter **must** be kept small, preferably 1/16".
*   GTAW filler wire **will normally be** Incoloy Filler Metal 65, designation ER Ni Fe Cr-1. Incoloy 625 or equivalent (designation ER Ni Cr Mo3) may also be used, provided prior approval has been granted by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
*   The preferred GTAW electrode is thoriated tungsten as per [[ASME_sfa_5_12_tungsten_arc_welding_electrodes]], Class EWTh-2.
*   GTAW electrode **must** have a tapered tip for good arc stability and penetration.
*   GTAW power supply **must** incorporate a high frequency (HF) voltage to start the arc.
*   GTAW power supply **must** incorporate a current decay unit.
*   Direct current straight polarity (D.C.S.P. - Electrode negative) **must** be used for GTAW (manual or machine welding).
*   GTAW weld metal thickness **must not exceed** 1/8" per pass.
*   A back-up gas **must** be used for GTAW to prevent root pass oxidation and contamination (e.g., for piping or where the root pass is inaccessible).

**Gas Metal Arc Welding (GMAW)**
*   GMAW **will only be used** for the root pass of butt welds and for fillet welds unless specifically agreed otherwise.
*   Short-circuiting transfer **must** be used for GMAW on material up to 1/8" thick.
*   Spray or pulsed arc transfer **must** be used for GMAW on material thicker than 1/8".
*   Direct current reverse polarity (D.C.R.P. - Electrode positive) **must** be used for GMAW.
*   The Fabricator **must** provide satisfactory evidence of continuous shop use of GMAW in the fabrication of nickel-base alloys.

**Submerged Arc Welding (SAW)**
*   Use of Submerged Arc Welding **must** be reviewed and approved in writing by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics prior to the start of fabrication.
*   The Fabricator **must** provide sectioned samples and satisfactory evidence of continuous shop use of Submerged Arc Welding in the fabrication of nickel-base alloys.

**Shielded Metal Arc Welding (SMAW)**
*   The SMAW electrode **must** be Incoloy Welding Electrode 135.

**Gas Metal Arc Welding (GMAW)**
*   Fabricator **must** provide satisfactory evidence of continuous shop use of GMAW in the fabrication of nickel-base alloys.

**Submerged Arc Welding (SAW)**
*   Use of Submerged Arc Welding **must** be reviewed and approved in writing by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics prior to start of fabrication.
*   Fabricator **must** provide sectioned samples and satisfactory evidence of continuous shop use of Submerged Arc Welding in the fabrication of nickel-base alloys.
