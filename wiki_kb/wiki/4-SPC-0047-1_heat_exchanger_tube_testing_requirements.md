# Heat Exchanger Tube Testing Requirements (4-SPC-0047-2)

Specification 4-SPC-0047-2 mandates several critical tests for stainless steel heat exchanger tubes to ensure their integrity and suitability for use in [[gas_gas_heat_exchanger_design_criteria|gas to gas heat exchangers]] in acid plants. These tests are in addition to the general material testing and requirements outlined in [[4-SPC-0047-1_referenced_standards|ASTM A249/SA249]] and [[4-SPC-0047-1_referenced_standards|ASTM A1016]].

**Mandatory Tests for All Tubes:**

1.  **Air-Under-Water Pressure Test (ASTM A249 Supplementary Requirement S3)**
    *   This test is **required** on 100% of the tubes.
    *   It refers to ASTM A1016 paragraph 27.

2.  **Hydrostatic Test (ASTM A1016 Paragraph 26)**
    *   This test is **required** on 100% of the tubes.

3.  **Weld Decay Test (ASTM A249 Supplementary Requirement S7)**
    *   This test is **required** on each heat of tube.
    *   The corrosion ratio for the weld decay test **must** be 1.2 or less.

4.  **Reverse Bend Test (ASTM A249 Paragraph 11)**
    *   This test **will** be performed a minimum of once every 100 tubes.

5.  **Ring Gauge Test**
    *   A ring gauge test **will** be performed on 100% of the tubes.
    *   The ring gauge **must** be 50 mm long.
    *   The ring gauge ID **must** match the maximum allowable OD of the tube.
    *   The ring gauge **must** be passed over 100% of the length of all tubes.
    *   The purpose of the ring gauge test is to ensure that the tube is not too large to fit into the hole in the tube-sheet of the heat exchanger.

These tests are crucial for verifying the quality of the welds, the absence of leaks, and the dimensional accuracy of the tubes, which are vital for the safe and efficient operation of the heat exchangers.

For specific additional testing requirements applicable to first-time suppliers, refer to [[4-SPC-0047-2_additional_testing_first_time_suppliers]].

General material testing and requirements as described in ASTM A1016 are also mandatory.

**Specific Test Requirements:**
*   **Air-Under Water Pressure Test:** ASTM A249 supplementary requirement S3 (air-under water pressure test) is required on 100% of the tubes. This test refers to A1016 para. 27.
*   **Hydrostatic Test:** The Hydrostatic test (A1016 para. 26) is required on 100% of the tubes.
*   **Weld Decay Test:** ASTM A249 supplementary requirement S7 (weld decay test) is required on each heat of tube. The weld decay test corrosion ratio must be 1.2 or less.
*   **Reverse Bend Test:** The reverse bend test (A249 para.11) will be done a minimum of once every 100 tubes.
*   **Ring Gauge Test:** A ring gauge test will be done on 100% of the tubes. The ring gauge must be 50 mm long, and its ID must match the maximum allowable OD of the tube. It must be passed over 100% of the length of all tubes. This test ensures that the tube is not too large to fit into the hole in the tube-sheet of the heat exchanger.
*   **Penetrant Testing (PT) of Tube Ends:** All tube ends (both ends of each tube) will be PT tested on the inside diameter, extending a minimum of 50mm inside the tube bore. PT testing will be done in batches of 50, starting as soon as the first 50 tubes are completed. If indications are found on any of the 50 tubes, they will be investigated by slitting the tube longitudinally, and the entire tube length will be PT tested to investigate the severity of the problem. Indications will be reported immediately.
*   **Periodic Destructive and PT Testing:** Every 100th tube, starting at tube 1, will be cut lengthwise and PT tested. Indications from this test will be reported immediately.
