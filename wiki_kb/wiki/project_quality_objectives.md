# Project Quality Objectives

This procedure mandates specific quality objectives for the Project, designed to ensure successful delivery and operational excellence. Adherence to these objectives is critical for meeting contractual and performance requirements.

The key quality objectives are:

*   **Mechanical Checkout:** No modifications **must** be required during [[mechanical_completion_testing_and_defect_management]] to meet contractual or operational requirements. This ensures that the plant is built correctly the first time, minimizing rework and delays.
*   **Practical Completion:** Practical Completion **must** be achieved by the date stipulated in the Contract. This objective emphasizes efficient project execution and adherence to the [[0-dat-3001_project_schedule]].
*   **Acceptance Tests:** No modifications **must** be required following Practical Completion in order to successfully pass the [[plant_modifications_and_acceptance_testing]]. This objective highlights the importance of robust design and construction that meets performance guarantees.
*   **Defects Liability Period Expenditure:** Expenditure during the Defects Liability Period **must** be less than 0.3% of the total Contract Price. This objective aims to minimize post-completion issues and ensure the long-term reliability and quality of the delivered plant.

These objectives are foundational to the overall [[0-prc-7003_project_quality_management_plan_overview]] and guide all project activities from design through commissioning.
