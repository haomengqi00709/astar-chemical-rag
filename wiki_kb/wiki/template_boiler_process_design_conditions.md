# Template Boiler Process Design Conditions

This page details the standard process design conditions specified in the [[template_boiler_datasheet_administration|Template Boiler Datasheet (4-PDS-XXXX-R1)]]. These conditions are crucial for the proper design and operation of boiler systems.

**General Process Design Conditions**
*   **Case Description:** Specifies the operational scenario for which the boiler is being designed.
*   **Design Basis:** Outlines the fundamental assumptions and criteria used for the boiler's design. For more information on design criteria, refer to [[process_design_criteria_document]].

**Steam Parameters**
The boiler system is designed for steam generation. Key parameters for steam include:
*   **Maximum Flowrate:** (kg/h)
*   **Minimum Flowrate:** (kg/h)
*   **Normal Flowrate:** (kg/h)
*   **Pressure:** (kPag)
*   **Temperature:** (oC)
*   **Steam Quality:** (%)

**Feed Water Parameters**
The quality and conditions of the feed water are critical for boiler performance and longevity. Specified feed water parameters include:
*   **Temperature:** (oC)
*   **Pressure:** (kPag)
*   **Copper:** (ppm)
*   **Conductivity:** (mS/cm)
*   **Iron:** (ppm)
*   **Total Hardness as CaCO3:** (ppm)
*   **Silica:** (ppm)
*   **Dissolved O2:** (ppb)
*   **Calcium (Ca):** (ppm)
*   **pH:**
*   **Chloride (Cl):** (ppm)
*   **Total Dissolved Solids:** (ppm)
*   **Chloride residual:** (ppm)
*   **Total suspended Solids:** (ppm)
*   **Total alkalinity:** (ppm)
*   **Maximum Flowrate (VTA):** (kg/h) - *Vendor to Advise*
*   **Minimum Flowrate (VTA):** (kg/h) - *Vendor to Advise*
*   **Normal Flowrate (VTA):** (kg/h) - *Vendor to Advise*
*   **% Blowdown (VTA):** - *Vendor to Advise*

**Fuel Parameters and Firing Options**
The datasheet specifies various fuel options and associated parameters:
*   **Firing Options:** Single, Dual, or Either/Or.
*   **Fuel Options:** Natural gas, Fuel oil #2, Fuel oil #4, Fuel oil #5, Fuel oil #6, Propane, Hydrogen, Waste gas, or Other.
*   **Fuel Parameters:**
    *   **Viscosity:** (cP)
    *   **Temperature:** (oC)
    *   **Density:** (kg/m3)
    *   **Available Pressure:** (kpag)
    *   **Specific Heat:** (kJ/kg.C)
    *   **Maximum Flowrate (Vendor to advise):** (kg/h)
    *   **Thermal conductivity:** (W/m.C)
    *   **Normal Flowrate (VTA):** (kg/h) - *Vendor to Advise*
    *   **Heating value:** (kJ/kg)
    *   **Minimum Flowrate (VTA):** (kg/h) - *Vendor to Advise*
