# Gas/Gas Heat Exchanger Datasheet Administration (4-DST-XXXX-R1)

This document, identified as 4-DST-XXXX-R1, serves as a template datasheet for the Gas/Gas Heat Exchanger within the "Template-Gas/Gas Heat Exchanger" project. It is prepared for Aker Kvaerner, specifically Chemetics, A Division of Aker Kvaerner Canada Inc. The client document number for this standard is Standard-4-DST-XXXX-R1.

**Revision History:**
*   **Revision 1.0:** Dated 39107.0, with a status of "Standard". Prepared by T. Wacker, checked by J. Wright, and approved by D. Shaw.
*   **Revision 0.0:** Dated 38133.0, with a status of "Comments Requested". Prepared by T. Wacker, checked by J. Purdy, and approved by T. Ueki.

**Datasheet Usage and Approval:**
This datasheet is primarily used by inserting its contents into an ACAD OTL drawing of the vessel. Once integrated into the OTL drawing, the drawing itself is published, checked, and approved. The datasheet itself **need not** be separately published, checked, and approved. This template is designed to be flexible and **may be modified** for exchangers with specific project requirements or different materials of construction.

**Equipment Identification:**
*   **Equipment Name:** Cold Interpass Heat Exchanger
*   **Chemetics Equipment Number:** 7557H
*   **Owner Equipment Number:** 42-E-1704

**General Notes:**
All dimensions specified within this datasheet are in millimeters. Equipment name, equipment number, and nozzle mark letters **must be clearly stenciled** onto the vessel. Stenciled letters **must be 75 mm high and black**.
