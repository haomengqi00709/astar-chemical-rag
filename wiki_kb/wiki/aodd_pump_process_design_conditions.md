# AODD Pump Process Design Conditions

This page details the critical process conditions for the Air Operated Double Diaphragm (AODD) Pump, as specified in document 4-DST-XXXX-R1. These parameters are essential for the correct selection, sizing, and operation of the pump within a chemical engineering process. The datasheet includes fields for both "DESIGN PER UNIT" and "ALTERNATE PER UNIT" conditions, allowing for flexibility in operational scenarios.

**Process Condition Parameters:**
The following parameters define the fluid characteristics and operational requirements for the AODD pump:

*   **Fluid Description:** A general description of the fluid being pumped.
*   **Composition:** The chemical makeup of the fluid, measured in %w/w (weight by weight). This is crucial for material compatibility and process safety.
*   **Solids Content:** The percentage of solid particles present in the fluid, measured in %w/w. High solids content can impact pump wear and selection.
*   **Solids Type:** Description of the type of solids present, which influences material selection and pump design to prevent clogging or abrasion.
*   **Flow Rate:** The nominal volumetric flow rate the pump is designed to handle, measured in m3/h.
*   **Minimum Flow Rate:** The lowest acceptable volumetric flow rate for stable pump operation, measured in m3/h.
*   **TDH (Total Dynamic Head):** The total equivalent height the pump must lift the fluid, including static head, friction losses, and pressure head, measured in m (fluid). This is a key parameter for pump power requirements.
*   **NPSH Available (Net Positive Suction Head Available):** The absolute pressure at the suction side of the pump, converted to meters of fluid, minus the vapor pressure of the liquid. This value, measured in m (fluid), indicates the energy available to push the liquid into the pump.
*   **Temperature:** The operating temperature of the fluid, measured in oC. Temperature affects fluid properties like density and viscosity, and also impacts material selection.
*   **Density:** The mass per unit volume of the fluid, measured in kg/m3. Density is critical for calculating pump power and head.
*   **Viscosity:** A measure of the fluid's resistance to flow, measured in cP (centipoise). High viscosity fluids require different pump designs and can significantly impact pump performance.
*   **Dry Suction Lift:** The vertical distance the pump can lift fluid from a source below the pump's inlet without priming, measured in m. This is a specific capability of AODD pumps.
*   **NPSH Required (Net Positive Suction Head Required):** The minimum NPSH necessary at the pump's suction port to prevent cavitation. This value, measured in m (fluid), is provided by the pump manufacturer.

Understanding and accurately specifying these process conditions is fundamental to ensuring the selected AODD pump meets the operational demands of the plant.
