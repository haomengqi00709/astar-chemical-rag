# Vacuum Generator Process Design Conditions

This page details the process design conditions and stream compositions for the Vacuum Generator, as specified in Document ID 4-PDS-XXXX-R1.

**Operational Quantities**
*   **Quantity Operating:** 2.0 units
*   **Quantity Installed Spare:** N/A (originally 2.0, later corrected)

**Operating Temperatures**
*   **Minimum Operating Temperature:** ambient
*   **Maximum Operating Temperature:** 90.0 oC

**Operating Pressures**
*   **Minimum Operating Pressure:** kPa (g) (Note 6)
    *   *Note 6:* Refers to the estimated lowest operating pressure for the vacuum pump.
*   **Upset Condition Pressure:** kPa (g) (Note 7)
    *   *Note 7:* This pressure is a critical factor for sizing the [[vacuum_generator_equipment_specifications#Relief Hatch (Mark E)|Relief Hatch (Mark E)]].

**Process Stream Compositions**
*   **Gas Out Composition:**
    *   xx ClO2
    *   xx Cl2
    *   xx H2O
    *   TRACE inerts
*   **Circulation In Composition:**
    *   Composed of Circulation Out + Gas Out (Note 9).
*   **Circulation Out Composition:**
    *   xx NaClO3
    *   xx HCl
    *   xx H2O
    *   xx NaCl
    *   xx Na2Cr2O7
    *   TRACE ClO2+Cl2

For general guidelines on process design conditions, refer to [[template_vessel_process_design_conditions]]. Specific component compositions can be referenced in documents like [[4-PDS-8071_clo2_absorber_stripper_composition_components]].

**Stream Compositions**
*   **Circulation In:** Circn Out + Gas Out (Note 9)

**Notes**
*   **Note 6:** Refers to the estimated lowest operating pressure for the vacuum pump.
