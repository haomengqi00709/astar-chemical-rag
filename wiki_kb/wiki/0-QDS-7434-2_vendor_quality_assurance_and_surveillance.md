# Lime Handling System Quality Data Sheet 2 Vendor Quality Assurance and Surveillance

For the Lime Handling System project, the Vendor **must** prepare a comprehensive Quality Plan. This plan **shall** be developed in strict accordance with the requirements outlined in [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment]].

The Vendor's Quality Assurance program **shall** comply fully with the stipulations of this Quality Data Sheet (0-QDS-7434-2) and also with the broader requirements specified in [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment]]. Adherence to these documents ensures that all quality standards and procedures are met throughout the project lifecycle.

Surveillance activities conducted by Chemetics for this project **will be** to Level 1.0. This level of surveillance indicates a specific degree of oversight and inspection by Chemetics personnel to ensure compliance with quality standards. The detailed scope and methodology for this surveillance **will be** as set out within this Quality Data Sheet itself.

For additional context on vendor quality assurance requirements, refer to [[vendor_quality_assurance]] and the specific requirements for Sheet 1 of this system: [[0-QDS-7434-1_vendor_quality_assurance_requirements]].
