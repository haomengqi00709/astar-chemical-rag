# Bidder Offer Documentation and Queries

Bidders are required to adhere to specific protocols for submitting queries and providing documentation to support their offer. This ensures a transparent and equitable evaluation process.

**Query Submission:**
*   **Mandatory:** All queries or requests for clarification regarding the [[request_for_quotation_specifics|Request for Quotation (RFQ)]] must be submitted in writing to the [[project_buyer_roles|Project Buyer]].
*   To maintain fairness and provide equal information to all participants, all bidders will receive a verbatim copy of any query submitted, along with the official response from Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Documentation Requirements:**
*   **Mandatory:** Bidders must supply documentation that assists in the evaluation of their offer, as specifically required by the Request for Quotation and the Goods and Services Information, Section 2. For general requirements, refer to [[vendor_document_requirements]].
*   Bidders may also supply any other documentation they deem necessary to fully describe their offer.
*   All documentation provided should be concise and clearly indicate its purpose.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics is not obligated to consider documentation supplied beyond what is explicitly required by the Request for Quotation.

Adherence to these guidelines is crucial for a smooth and efficient tender process, facilitating a thorough evaluation of all submitted offers.
