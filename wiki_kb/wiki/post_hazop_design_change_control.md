# Post-HazOp Design Change Control

To maintain the integrity of the Hazard and Operability (HazOp) Study, any design changes made after the study's conclusion, and unrelated to its direct responses, **must** undergo a specific review and approval process.

**Approval for Subsequent Design Changes:**
*   Following the HazOp Study, any changes to the design that are unrelated to the study responses **shall** require the approval of the Technology Manager. This approval ensures that the integrity of the HazOp Study is maintained.
*   A "change to design" is interpreted as any action necessitating a revision to the content of one of the documents listed in [[hazop_study_prerequisites_and_documentation|HazOp Study Prerequisites and Documentation]].

**Drafting and Reviewing Changes:**
*   Draft changes to the design **shall** be prepared under the direction of the responsible Discipline Lead.
*   These draft changes are prepared by marking up the published version of the documents listed in [[hazop_study_prerequisites_and_documentation|HazOp Study Prerequisites and Documentation]], or any subsequent published versions after revision to incorporate the HazOp Study responses.
*   Draft changes to the design **shall** be submitted to the Technology Manager for review.
*   The Technology Manager **shall** review each draft change.

**Approval and Further HazOp for Draft Changes:**
*   If a draft change is acceptable and does not impact the HazOp Study of that part of the plant, the document describing the draft change will be digitally signed by the Technology Manager as approved for implementation.
*   If a draft change will require further HazOp Study, the Technology Manager **shall** advise the Project Engineer. The Project Engineer will then arrange for the necessary personnel to conduct the study for these draft changes.
*   For further HazOp Studies due to draft changes, the procedures outlined in [[hazop_study_record_publishing|HazOp Study Record Publishing]], [[hazop_study_response_and_design_change_management|HazOp Study Response and Design Change Management]], and this [[post_hazop_design_change_control|Post-HazOp Design Change Control]] section will be followed.

**Project Engineer's Responsibility:**
*   The Project Engineer **is responsible for ensuring** that no changes of design are implemented following a HazOp Study without the review and approval described in this section.

**Definition of Design Change:**
*   A change to design is interpreted to mean any action that requires a revision to the content of one of the documents listed in Section 3 of the procedure.

**Process for Post-HazOp Design Changes:**
*   Draft changes to design will be prepared under the direction of the responsible Discipline Lead.
*   Draft changes are prepared by marking-up the published version of the documents listed in Section 3, or any subsequent published versions after revision to incorporate the HazOp Study responses.
*   Draft changes to design are submitted to the Technology Manager for review.
*   The Technology Manager will review each draft change to design.
*   If a draft change is acceptable and does not impact the HazOp Study of that part of the plant, then the document describing the draft change will be digitally signed by the Technology Manager as approved for implementation.
*   If a draft change will require further HazOp Study, the Technology Manager will advise the Project Engineer.
*   The Project Engineer will arrange for the necessary personnel to conduct the study for draft changes.
*   The procedures of Sections 8, 9 and 10 of the procedure will be followed for further HazOp Study due to draft changes.
*   The Project Engineer is responsible for ensuring that no changes of design are implemented following HazOp Study without review and approval as described in Section 11 of the procedure.
