# Professional Services Subcontract (0-SPC-2201) Intellectual Property and Confidentiality

Professional Services Subcontract 0-SPC-2201 establishes clear guidelines regarding the use of material, intellectual property rights, and confidentiality obligations for both Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the Subcontractor.

**Rights to Use Material:**
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics holds the right to use material provided by the Subcontractor for the purpose stated in the [[subcontract_definitions_0_spc_2201|Scope]].
*   The Subcontractor **must** ensure that equivalent rights are obtained from any [[subcontract_definitions_0_spc_2201|Subsubcontractor]] for material they prepare, allowing Aker Kvaerner Chemetics to use it.
*   The Subcontractor's right to use material provided by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics is strictly limited to [[subcontract_definitions_0_spc_2201|Providing the Services]].
*   The Subcontractor may extend the right to use Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' material to a Subsubcontractor, where necessary for the services.
*   Upon [[subcontract_definitions_0_spc_2201|Completion]] of the entire services, the Subcontractor **must** return all material provided by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Confidentiality and Publicity:**
*   Both Parties **must not** disclose any information obtained in connection with the services, except when such disclosure is necessary to fulfill their duties under this Subcontract.
*   The Subcontractor may publicize the services only with the prior written agreement of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Indemnification for Infringement:**
*   The Subcontractor **must** indemnify Aker Kvaerner Chemetics against any claims, proceedings, compensation, and costs payable that arise from an infringement by the Subcontractor of the rights of [[subcontract_definitions_0_spc_2201|Others]].
*   This indemnity, however, does not cover infringements that result from the Subcontractor's use of materials or items provided by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

These provisions ensure that intellectual property is protected, confidential information is maintained, and both parties are aware of their rights and responsibilities concerning the use and disclosure of project-related materials and information.

**Rights to Use Material:**
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics has the right to use material provided by the Subcontractor for the purpose stated in the Scope.
*   The Subcontractor must obtain from a Subsubcontractor equivalent rights for Aker Kvaerner Chemetics to use material prepared by the Subsubcontractor.
*   The Subcontractor has the right to use material provided by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics only to Provide the Services. The Subcontractor may make the right to use Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' material available to a Subsubcontractor.
*   On Completion of the whole of the services, the Subcontractor must return the material provided by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Confidentiality and Publicity:**
*   The Parties must not disclose information obtained in connection with the services except when necessary to carry out their duties under this Subcontract.
*   The Subcontractor may publicise the services only with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’s written agreement.

**Indemnity for Infringement:**
*   The Subcontractor indemnifies Aker Kvaerner Chemetics against claims, proceedings, compensation, and costs payable arising out of an infringement by the Subcontractor of the rights of Others. This indemnity does not cover an infringement which arose out of the use by the Subcontractor of things provided by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
