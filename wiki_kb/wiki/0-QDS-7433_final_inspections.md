# Flocculant Mixer Final Inspections

Prior to packaging and shipment, a comprehensive series of final inspections **must** be conducted on the Flocculant Mixer package. These inspections are critical to verify that the completed unit meets all specified requirements, is free from defects, and is ready for delivery. Chemetics' witness surveillance is mandated for several of these key inspection points to ensure independent verification of quality.

**Mandatory Final Inspection Requirements:**

*   **Dimensional Inspection:**
    *   Dimensional inspection **requires** a Quality Record (FP). This record **must** document the verification of critical dimensions against approved drawings and specifications.
    *   Dimensional inspection **requires** Chemetics Witness surveillance. A Chemetics representative **must** be present to observe the dimensional inspection, confirming accuracy and adherence to design.
*   **Surfaces Free From Defects:**
    *   Inspection for surfaces free from defects **requires** a Quality Record (FP). This record **must** confirm that all accessible surfaces are free from unacceptable defects, damage, or imperfections.
    *   Inspection for surfaces free from defects **requires** Chemetics Witness surveillance. A Chemetics representative **must** visually inspect the surfaces to ensure they meet the specified quality criteria.
*   **Cleanliness:**
    *   Cleanliness inspection **requires** a Quality Record (FP). This record **must** confirm that the Flocculant Mixer package, both internally and externally, meets the specified cleanliness standards, free from debris, contaminants, or foreign materials.
    *   Cleanliness inspection **requires** Chemetics Witness surveillance. A Chemetics representative **must** verify the cleanliness of the equipment prior to final packaging.
*   **Review QA Documentation:**
    *   Review of QA Documentation **requires** Chemetics Witness surveillance. A Chemetics representative **must** conduct a final review of the complete Quality Assurance Documentation Package to ensure all required records are present, accurate, and complete before acceptance.

These final inspections, with mandatory Chemetics witness points, are essential to guarantee the overall quality, integrity, and readiness of the Flocculant Mixer package for its intended service. For general guidelines on final inspections, refer to [[final_inspection]].
