# Final Strainer Nozzle Specifications

This page details the specifications for the various nozzles on the Final Strainer (Tag No. 7842K). Proper nozzle sizing and configuration are essential for efficient fluid transfer and operational flexibility.

**Inlet Nozzle:**
*   **Equipment Inlet Nozzle Size:** 267.05 mm
*   **Equipment Inlet Velocity:** 2.002664332807885 m/s
*   **Nozzle A (Acid Inlet) Size:** 250.0 mm
*   **Nozzle A (Acid Inlet) Quantity:** 1.0
*   **Nozzle A (Acid Inlet) Sizing Criteria:** 2.5 m/s

**Outlet Nozzle:**
*   **Nozzle B (Acid Outlet) Size:** 250.0 mm
*   **Nozzle B (Acid Outlet) Quantity:** 1.0
*   **Nozzle B (Acid Outlet) Sizing Criteria:** 2.5 m/s

**Drain Nozzle:**
*   **Nozzle C (Drain) Size:** 50.0 mm
*   **Nozzle C (Drain) Quantity:** 1.0
*   **Nozzle C (Drain) Sizing Criteria:** Standard

**Vent Nozzle:**
*   **Nozzle D (Vent) Size:** 25.0 mm
*   **Nozzle D (Vent) Quantity:** 1.0
*   **Nozzle D (Vent) Sizing Criteria:** Standard

All nozzles are constructed from [[final_strainer_materials_of_construction|SARAMET]] to ensure compatibility with the process fluid. These specifications are directly linked to the [[final_strainer_sizing_and_performance]] calculations and the overall [[final_strainer_dimensions]].
