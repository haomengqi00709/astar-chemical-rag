# Steam Piping Welding Processes

This page details the acceptable welding processes and their specific requirements for steam piping, as defined in Specification 4-SPC-0069.

All welding **shall** be in accordance with the [[ASME_b31_1_power_piping]] code. All weld filler metals **shall** meet the requirements of [[ASME_bpvc_section_ii_materials|ASME Section II, Part C]].

**Acceptable Welding Processes:**
The following welding processes are acceptable:
*   Shielded Metal Arc Welding (SMAW)
*   Gas Tungsten Arc Welding (GTAW)
*   Gas Metal Arc Welding (GMAW)
*   Submerged Arc Welding (SAW)

Other welding processes will be considered if proposed by the Vendor and approved in writing by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Specific Process Requirements:**

*   **Gas Tungsten Arc Welding (GTAW)**
    *   GTAW **shall** be used for root passes.
    *   GTAW electrodes **shall** satisfy the requirements of [[ASME_sfa_5_28_welding_electrodes_and_rods|SFA-5.28]].
    *   Weld metal thickness **shall not** exceed 3mm (⅛") per pass.
    *   Heat input **shall** be kept low by using a stringer bead technique.

*   **Shielded Metal Arc Welding (SMAW)**
    *   SMAW electrodes **shall** satisfy the requirements of [[ASME_sfa_5_5_welding_electrodes|SFA-5.5]].

*   **Gas Metal Arc Welding (GMAW)**
    *   GMAW electrodes **shall** satisfy the requirements of [[ASME_sfa_5_28_welding_electrodes_and_rods|SFA-5.28]].
    *   GMAW may be used for fillet welds.
    *   The Vendor **must** provide satisfactory evidence of continuous shop use of the GMAW process.
    *   A minimum of two passes is required for GMAW on all thicknesses.
    *   The short circuit mode of transfer for GMAW will not be used unless the specific application is approved by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

*   **Submerged Arc Welding (SAW)**
    *   SAW electrodes **shall** satisfy the requirements of [[ASME_sfa_5_23_welding_electrodes_and_fluxes_for_submerged_arc_welding|SFA-5.23]].
    *   Weld metal thickness **shall not** exceed 13mm (½") per pass.
    *   Run-on and run-off tabs of the same material specification as the base material **shall** be used on all longitudinal seams for SAW.
    *   Wire and flux combinations recommended for single pass welding **shall not** be used for multiple pass welds with SAW.
    *   A chemical analysis of the finished deposit **must** be included as part of the SAW weld procedure qualification.

**Acceptable Welding Processes**
Acceptable welding processes include Shielded Metal Arc Welding (SMAW), Gas Tungsten Arc Welding (GTAW), Gas Metal Arc Welding (GMAW), and Submerged Arc Welding (SAW). Other welding processes will be considered if proposed by the Vendor and approved in writing by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Shielded Metal Arc Welding (SMAW)**
*   SMAW electrodes shall satisfy the requirements of SFA-5.5.

**Gas Tungsten Arc Welding (GTAW)**
*   GTAW shall be used for root passes.
*   GTAW electrodes shall satisfy the requirements of SFA-5.28.
*   Weld metal thickness shall not exceed 3mm (⅛") per pass.
*   Heat input shall be kept low by using stringer bead technique.

**Gas Metal Arc Welding (GMAW)**
*   GMAW electrodes shall satisfy the requirements of SFA-5.28.
*   GMAW may be used for fillet welds.
*   The Vendor must provide satisfactory evidence of continuous shop use of the GMAW process.
*   A minimum of two passes is required for GMAW on all thicknesses.
*   The short circuit mode of transfer for GMAW will not be used unless the specific application is approved by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Submerged Arc Welding (SAW)**
*   SAW electrodes shall satisfy the requirements of SFA-5.23.
*   Weld metal thickness shall not exceed 13mm (½") per pass.
*   Run-on and run-off tabs of the same material specification as the base material shall be used on all longitudinal seams.
*   Wire and flux combinations recommended for single pass welding shall not be used for multiple pass welds.
*   A chemical analysis of the finished deposit must be included as part of the SAW weld procedure qualification.
