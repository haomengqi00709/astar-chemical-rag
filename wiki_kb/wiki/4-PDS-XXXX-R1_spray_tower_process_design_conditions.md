# Spray Tower Process Design Conditions (4-PDS-XXXX-R1)

The Template Spray Tower Process Data Sheet (document ID 4-PDS-XXXX-R1) includes a comprehensive section dedicated to **PROCESS DESIGN CONDITIONS**. This section outlines critical parameters and characteristics necessary for the design and operation of the spray tower.

Key process design conditions and their specified units are:

*   **Case Description**: Provides a brief overview of the specific operating scenario.
*   **Design Basis**: Details the fundamental assumptions and criteria guiding the design.
*   **System Description**: Describes the overall system in which the spray tower operates.
*   **Stream**: References a specific stream from an associated equipment sketch, indicating its properties.
*   **Phase**: Specifies the phase of the fluid, with "vapour/liquid" as an example.
*   **Fluid Description**: Provides a detailed description of the fluid being processed.
*   **Design Mass Flow Rate**: Measured in kg/h.
*   **Design Volumetric Flow Rate**: Measured in Am3/h.
*   **Temperature**: Measured in oC.
*   **Pressure**: Measured in Kpa(g).
*   **Density**: Measured in kg/m3.
*   **Viscosity**: Measured in cP.
*   **Surface Tension**: Measured in mN/m.
*   **Composition**: Can be expressed in %w/w (weight/weight) or %v/v (volume/volume).
*   **Fouling Tendency**: Categorized as High, Medium, or Low.
*   **Foaming Tendency**: Categorized as High, Medium, or Low.
*   **Maximum Operating Temperature**: Measured in oC.
*   **Maximum Operating Pressure**: Measured in kPa(g).
*   **Minimum Operating Temperature**: Measured in oC.
*   **Maximum Operating Pressure (associated with Minimum Operating Temperature)**: Measured in kPa(g).

These detailed specifications are crucial for ensuring the spray tower is designed to safely and efficiently handle the intended process fluids under various operating scenarios. For general information on process design criteria, refer to [[process_design_criteria_1_prc_0001]].

The section also details various quantitative parameters and their units:
*   **Quantity data** includes categories for "Operating" and "Installed Spare".
*   **Design Mass Flow Rate** is measured in kg/h.
*   **Design Volumetric Flow Rate** is measured in Am3/h.
*   **Temperature** is expressed in oC.
*   **Pressure** is expressed in Kpa(g).
*   **Density** is expressed in kg/m3.
*   **Viscosity** is expressed in cP.
*   **Surface tension** is expressed in mN/m.
*   **Composition** can be expressed in either %w/w (weight by weight) or %v/v (volume by volume).
*   **Fouling Tendency** and **Foaming Tendency** are categorized as High, Medium, or Low.
*   **Maximum Operating Temperature** is in oC.
*   **Maximum Operating Pressure** is in kPa(g).
*   **Minimum Operating Temperature** is in oC.
*   A specific field captures the **Maximum Operating Pressure** associated with the **Minimum Operating Temperature**, also in kPa(g).
