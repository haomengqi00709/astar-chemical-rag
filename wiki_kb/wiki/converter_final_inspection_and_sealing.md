# Converter Final Inspection and Sealing

This page details the mandatory final inspection, cleanliness, and sealing procedures for the converter, ensuring it is ready for commissioning and operation. These steps are crucial for verifying the quality of installation and preventing contamination or leaks.

**Mandatory Final Inspection and Sealing Requirements:**

*   **Cleanliness and Dryness:** All areas of the converter **must** be clean and dry. This prevents contamination of the catalyst and internal components.
*   **Water Removal:** No water **must** be left in sealed-off sections of the converter. Residual water can cause corrosion, interfere with process chemistry, or lead to operational issues.
*   **Non-Destructive Testing (NDT):** All NDT inspections **must** be completed and records available. NDT ensures the structural integrity of welds and materials without causing damage. Refer to [[non_destructive_examination_requirements]] and specific NDT requirements like [[0-QDS-7404-2_non_destructive_examination_requirements]] for details.
*   **Final Internal Inspection:** A final internal inspection **must** be completed with the client. This joint inspection confirms that all internal components are correctly installed and the vessel is ready for closure. This is a key activity in the [[converter_shop_inspection_and_testing_plan_activities]].
*   **Closure Certificate:** A closure certificate for the final internal inspection **must** be attached. This document formally certifies the completion and acceptance of the internal inspection.
*   **Manway Covers:** Manway covers **must** be installed and seal welded. Proper sealing of manways is essential for maintaining the integrity of the converter's internal environment.
*   **Manway Weld Inspection:** Manway cover welds **must** be inspected. This inspection verifies the quality and integrity of the seal welds, preventing potential leaks.

All inspection records and certificates contribute to the project's [[quality_record_definitions]]. Any deviations or issues identified during these final inspections **must** be documented using a [[0-NCR-7XXX_template_non_conformance_report]].

**Mandatory Final Inspection and Sealing Procedures:**
*   **Cleanliness and Dryness:** All areas of the converter must be clean and dry.
*   **Water Removal:** No water must be left in sealed-off sections of the converter.
*   **Final Internal Inspection:** Must be completed with the client.
*   **Closure Certificate:** A closure certificate for the final internal inspection must be attached.
*   **Manway Covers:** Must be installed and seal welded.
*   **Manway Cover Welds:** Must be inspected.
