# Vendor Quality Assurance Requirements (0-QDS-7415)

For the Dual Laminate Tank project, the vendor **must** prepare a Quality Plan in accordance with the specifications outlined in [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment]]. The vendor's overall Quality Assurance program **shall** comply with the requirements detailed in this Quality Data Sheet (0-QDS-7415) and also with [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment]].

It is a mandatory requirement that cells related to the Quality Plan are deleted if a Quality Plan is not required for a specific scope of work.

For details on the Chemetics surveillance level for this project, refer to [[0-QDS-7415_chemetics_surveillance_level]].
For definitions of quality records, see [[0-QDS-7415_quality_record_definitions]].
An overview of this Quality Data Sheet can be found at [[0-QDS-7415_document_details_and_revisions]].
