# 6% Moly Stainless Steel Welding Standard Overview (4-SPC-0017)

This Standard, identified as **4-SPC-0017**, defines the workmanship and quality requirements for welded fabrications utilizing 6 percent Moly austenitic stainless steel (UNS S31254). It is applicable when explicitly called for on purchase orders and other referenced project documents.

**Scope of Application**
The Standard covers welding and other fabrication requirements for a range of equipment, including vessels, heat exchangers, tanks, piping, and various miscellaneous welded items. These materials fall under the ASME P-8 Material Classification Group 4. An example of such stainless steel is Avesta 254 SMO®.

**Definitions**
*   **Codes, etc.:** Refers to the latest edition of relevant codes and standards ruling at the date of contract.
*   **Agreement in Writing:** Signifies a formal submission in writing via letter, not merely an inclusion in approval drawings.
*   **Inspector:** Denotes an Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Quality Assurance Inspector or another duly Authorized Representative.

**Document History and Usage**
This document is a direct copy of the original specification ES44/W05, which was titled "General Requirements for Equipment Welding 6% Moly Stainless Steel." The material specification for equipment welding covered by this standard is 6% Moly Stainless Steel.
