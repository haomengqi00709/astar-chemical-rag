# Subcontract Administration and Communication (0-SPC-6001)

This page details the administrative and communication protocols governing the construction subcontract, as outlined in 0-SPC-6001.

**Communication Requirements**
All communications required by this subcontract take effect upon written receipt. Each communication **must** be received at the last address notified by the recipient for receiving communications. If the subcontract requires a reply from Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or the Subcontractor, the responder **shall** reply within the specified period, unless otherwise stated in this subcontract.

**Instructions and Acceptance**
The Subcontractor **shall** obey any instruction given by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics that is in accordance with this subcontract. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may issue instructions that change the [[standard_conditions_for_construction_subcontract_0_spc_6001#Works Information|Works Information]].

Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' acceptance of a communication or work from the Subcontractor does not alter the Subcontractor’s responsibility to [[standard_conditions_for_construction_subcontract_0_spc_6001#To Provide the Works|Provide the Works]] or the Subcontractor’s liability for its design.

**Delegation of Authority**
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may delegate any of its actions after notifying the Subcontractor. Any such delegation may also be cancelled after notifying the Subcontractor. A reference to an action by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics in this subcontract includes an action performed by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ delegate.

**Site Access and Services**
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **shall** allow the Subcontractor access to and use of the site as necessary for the work included in this subcontract. Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **shall** also provide services and other items as specified in the [[standard_conditions_for_construction_subcontract_0_spc_6001#Works Information|Works Information]].

**Early Warning System**
Both the Subcontractor and Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **shall** give an early warning by notifying the other as soon as either becomes aware of any matter that could:
*   Increase the total of the [[standard_conditions_for_construction_subcontract_0_spc_6001#Prices|Prices]].
*   Delay [[standard_conditions_for_construction_subcontract_0_spc_6001#Completion|Completion]].
*   Impair the performance of the works in use.

The Parties **shall** co-operate in making and considering proposals to avoid or reduce the effect of any matter notified as an early warning. They **shall** also co-operate in deciding and recording the actions to be taken for such early warnings.

**Instructions and Acceptance**
*   The Subcontractor **shall** obey an instruction which is in accordance with this subcontract and is given by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics may give an instruction to the Subcontractor which changes the Works Information.
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ acceptance of a communication from the Subcontractor or of the Subcontractor’s work does not change the Subcontractor’s responsibility to [[standard_conditions_for_construction_subcontract_0_spc_6001#To Provide the Works|Provide the Works]].

**Delegation**
*   Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, after notifying the Subcontractor, may delegate any of its actions or cancel any delegation.
*   A reference to an action of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics in this subcontract includes an action by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ delegate.

**Early Warning System**
Both the Subcontractor and Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **shall** give an early warning by notifying the other as soon as either becomes aware of any matter which could:
*   Increase the total of the Prices.
*   Delay [[standard_conditions_for_construction_subcontract_0_spc_6001#Completion|Completion]].
*   Impair the performance of the works in use.

The Parties **shall** co-operate in making and considering proposals for how the effect of each matter notified as an early warning can be avoided or reduced, and in deciding and recording actions to be taken.
