# Confined Space Entry Permit System

The Confined Space Entry Permit system is a mandatory control measure designed to formalize procedures, ensure all hazards are considered, and verify that appropriate protective measures are in place before any personnel enter a confined space.

**Entry Permit (Form DP-802.705-1):**
*   A vessel Entry Permit (form [[DP-802.705-1_confined_space_entry_permit]]) **shall** be issued before entry.
*   The Entry Permit certifies and records approval for vessel (enclosed or confined space) entry.
*   It serves as a method of formalizing agreed-upon procedures.
*   It serves as a checklist to ensure that all existing hazards are considered, evaluated, and correct protective measures taken.
*   **No employee shall enter any vessel** without reporting to and securing written approval from the immediate supervisor and the Commissioning HSE Manager.
*   The Commissioning HSE Manager and the Entry Supervisor **must** sign a valid permit for this program.

**Permit Validity and Reissuance:**
*   An Entry Permit is **not a work permit**.
*   An Entry Permit is valid only for entry and inspection of a vessel or confined space.
*   **No authorization shall be valid** except for the job, location, persons, and time specified.
*   The Entry Supervisor **must** terminate entry upon completion of work, expiration of permit, or if a condition arises in or near the permit space that places occupants at risk.
*   The Entry Supervisor **must** ensure permits are reissued or updated before authorizing re-entry into the confined space.
*   Commencement of a new shift **will always** require reinvestigation and endorsement of a new authorization by new shift supervision.

**Interaction with Other Permits:**
*   Should it be necessary to perform Hot Work inside a vessel, both an entry permit and a Hot Work Permit **will be required** prior to commencing work. This highlights that the Confined Space Entry Permit focuses specifically on the safety aspects of entry and presence, while other permits address the hazards of specific tasks.

**Supervisor Responsibilities for Permits:**
*   Appropriate supervisors **must** ascertain by personal investigation immediately before vessel entry that the entry and incidental work will be safe.
*   Appropriate supervisors **must** ascertain by personal investigation before signing the authorization that the entry and incidental work will be safe.
*   To ensure continuation of safe conditions if a break of appreciable duration occurs during the job, the authorizing supervisors **must** reinvestigate the site before the job can be allowed to resume.
