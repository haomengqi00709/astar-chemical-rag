# Isometrics

Isometrics, or isometric drawings, are a type of technical drawing used extensively in piping design and construction to represent three-dimensional piping systems in a two-dimensional format. They provide a detailed, scaled representation of individual pipe runs, including dimensions, fittings, valves, and other components.

**Role in Pump Calculations:**
During the [[pump_sizing_stages|Final Impeller Trim]] stage of pump sizing, the Hydraulic Engineer **will** obtain additional final information from [[isometrics|Isometrics]] from the design office. This is crucial for:
*   **Accurate Pipe Lengths:** Isometrics provide precise lengths of pipe runs, which are essential for calculating friction losses in the piping system.
*   **Exact Fitting Counts:** They detail the exact number and type of fittings (elbows, tees, reducers, etc.) in a pipe run, allowing for accurate determination of minor losses.
*   **Elevation Changes:** Isometrics clearly show all changes in elevation, which are critical for calculating the static head component of the Total Dynamic Head (TDH).

By using information from isometrics, the Hydraulic Engineer can finalize hydraulic calculations with a high degree of accuracy, ensuring the pump is correctly sized for the installed piping system.
