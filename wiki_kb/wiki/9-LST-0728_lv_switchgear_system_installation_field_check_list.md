# 9-LST-0728 LV Switchgear System Installation Field Check List

The 9-LST-0728 LV Switchgear System Installation Field Check List is a specific document used during the [[9-PRC-0004_electrical_field_checkout_documentation|Electrical Field Checkout Procedure (9-PRC-0004)]] to verify the correct installation of Low Voltage (LV) switchgear systems. This checklist ensures that all LV switchgear components are installed according to engineering specifications, safety regulations, and operational requirements.
