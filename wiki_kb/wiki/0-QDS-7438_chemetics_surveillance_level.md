# Field Erected SS Tank Shop Component Chemetics Surveillance Level

For the Field Erected Stainless Steel Tank Shop Component (0-QDS-7438), Chemetics surveillance **shall** be conducted to Level 1.0. This level of surveillance dictates the extent of Chemetics' involvement in various inspection and documentation activities throughout the fabrication process.

The specific surveillance requirements for different activities are detailed in the respective sections:

*   **Material and Welding Certifications:** Mill Test Certificates require Complete surveillance. See [[0-QDS-7438_material_and_welding_certifications]].
*   **Non-Destructive Examination (NDE):**
    *   Radiography requires Witness (Spot) and Complete surveillance.
    *   Dye Penetrant requires Witness (25%) and Complete surveillance.
    See [[0-QDS-7438_non_destructive_examination_requirements]].
*   **Final Inspections:**
    *   Dimensional inspection requires Witness and Hold surveillance.
    *   Contour & Finish of Welds inspection requires Witness and Hold surveillance.
    *   Surfaces Free from Defects inspection requires Witness and Hold surveillance.
    *   Cleanliness inspection requires Witness and Hold surveillance.
    *   Review of QA Documentation requires Witness and Hold surveillance.
    See [[0-QDS-7438_final_inspections]].
*   **Packing and Shipping:**
    *   Packaging requires Witness surveillance.
    *   Packing List requires Witness surveillance.
    See [[0-QDS-7438_packing_and_shipping_requirements]].

For general information on Chemetics surveillance levels, refer to other specific QDS documents such as [[0-QDS-7436_chemetics_surveillance_level]].
