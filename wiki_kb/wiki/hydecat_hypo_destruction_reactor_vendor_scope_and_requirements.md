# Hydecat Hypo Destruction Reactor Vendor Scope and Requirements

This page details the vendor's scope of supply and specific requirements for the Hydecat Hypo Destruction Reactor, as outlined in Document ID 4-PDS-XXXX-R1. Adherence to these requirements is essential for [[vendor_quality_assurance]] and successful project execution.

**Vendor Scope of Supply (Note 1.0)**
The vendor's scope of supply **shall** include:
*   Catalyst
*   Design package
*   Annual license fee

**Items Supplied by Others (Note 2.0)**
The following components **shall** be supplied by other parties:
*   Reactor housing
*   Piping
*   Instrumentation
*   Filters
*   Pump
*   Other ancillary equipment

**Vendor Verification and Catalyst Performance (Notes 4.0 & 5.0)**
The vendor **must** advise (VTA - Vendor to Advise) on:
*   The heavy metal loss rate of the catalyst (applicable for both new and aged catalyst, with or without post-filtration).
*   The catalyst efficiency over its lifetime and the assumptions used when calculating the residual hypo level of the effluent.

**Dilution Water and Pump Sizing (Note 6.0)**
*   The feed solution may require dilution with demineralized water to meet the vendor's specific requirements.
*   The vendor **must** specify the required dilution water quantity.
*   The Mechanical discipline **shall** size the pump to accommodate the total flow exiting the Hypo Destruction Reactor (HDR).

For general vendor documentation requirements, refer to [[vendor_document_requirements]].
