# HCl Synthesis Unit General Design Features

This page describes the general design features and key equipment considerations for the HCl Synthesis Unit, as specified in [[4-PDS-XXXX-R1_template_hcl_synthesis_unit_package_datasheet_administration]]. These features encompass critical aspects of operation, material selection, and safety.

**Key Design Features:**
*   **Ignition System:** The unit's ignition system can be either **Automatic or Manual**, providing flexibility in start-up procedures.
*   **Materials of Construction:** The selection of materials for the unit's components **shall** refer to the specific [[material_selection]] requirements detailed in the Equipment Specification. This is crucial due to the highly corrosive nature of HCl and chlorine.
*   **Start-Up Fan:** For initial start-up procedures, an **Ejector or Plant air** system **must** be available. This ensures proper purging and establishment of initial flow conditions before combustion.
*   **Flame Arrestors:**
    *   **Cl2 Flame Arrestor Pressure Drop:** VTA kPa (subject to **Note 9**)
    *   **H2 Flame Arrestor Pressure Drop:** VTA kPa (subject to **Note 9**)
    Flame arrestors are critical safety devices designed to prevent the propagation of flames into upstream piping. The specified pressure drops are important for system hydraulic design and ensuring effective flame quenching. **Note 9** provides further details on these requirements.
*   **Tail Gas Scrubber:** The unit's tail gas scrubber system **must** include a **Density Pot**. This component is essential for monitoring and controlling the density of the scrubbing liquid, which is vital for efficient removal of residual HCl and Cl2 from the tail gas, as per [[hcl_synthesis_unit_tail_gas_specifications]] and [[emissions_and_effluents]].

These general design features contribute significantly to the safety, reliability, and environmental performance of the HCl Synthesis Unit. For broader equipment requirements, refer to [[general_equipment_requirements_specification]].
