# Digital Signatures for Travel Authorization Forms

The Travel Authorization Form supports digital signatures, streamlining the approval process. When using digital signatures, travelers should type the name of their direct Supervisor (Department Manager) and the Budget Manager (person responsible for the budget) into the designated fields. It is crucial to ensure that both the Department Manager and Budget Manager are available in the office or have designated a delegate to sign in their place to avoid delays.

**Digital Signature Process:**
1.  Fill in the Travel Authorization Form electronically.
2.  For digital signatures, type in the names of the direct Supervisor (Department Manager) and the Budget Manager.

**Using Adobe Reader (Non-Acrobat Users):**
If you are using Adobe Reader instead of the full Adobe Acrobat software, follow these steps to "print to file" for digital signature processing:
1.  After filling in the form, select **FILE** then **PRINT**.
2.  Choose the **PRINT TO FILE** option.
3.  Enter the file path as `i:\ACROBATQ\OFFICE\IN\filename` (e.g., `i:\ACROBATQ\OFFICE\IN\Traveler,Joe_Houston_Feb16`).
4.  The processed file will then be available in the directory `i:\ACROBATQ\OFFICE\OUT`.

**Alternative to Digital Signatures:**
If digital signing is not feasible, a hard copy of the form can be printed and circulated for manual signatures.

**Impact on Cash Advances:**
A digitally signed Travel Authorization Form will replace the need for an originally signed hard copy when requesting [[0-PRC-9003_cash_advance_policy|cash advances]].

For the overall travel request process, refer to [[travel_authorization_process_0_frm_9002]].

**Digital Signature Best Practices**
For digital signatures, ensure that the direct Supervisor (Department Manager) and Budget Manager are available in the office to sign, or indicate a delegate to sign in their place.

**Alternative Signature Methods**
*   **Adobe Reader Instructions:** If you are not using Adobe Acrobat (only Adobe Reader), fill in the form and then select `FILE` then `PRINT`. From the print dialog, select `PRINT TO FILE`. Enter the destination path as `i:\ACROBATQ\OFFICE\IN\filename` (e.g., `Traveler,Joe_Houston_Feb16`). The processed file can then be found in the directory `i:\ACROBATQ\OFFICE\OUT`.
*   **Hard Copy:** Alternatively, a hard copy of the form can be sent around for physical signatures.
