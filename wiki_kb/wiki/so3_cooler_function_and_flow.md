# SO3 Cooler Function and Flow

The SO3 cooler is designed to cool an SO3-rich gas stream using air as the cooling medium. This exchanger plays a vital role in managing the temperature of the SO3 stream within the process.

**Flow Configuration:**
*   The SO3 stream **is always placed on the tube side** of the exchanger.
*   The SO3 stream enters the top of the exchanger and flows downwards through the tubes.
*   This specific flow configuration (SO3 on the tube side, entering at the top, and flowing downwards) is critical. It ensures that any condensed acid, which may form during the cooling process, drains effectively from the exchanger, preventing accumulation and potential corrosion issues.

**Operational Considerations:**
*   The SO3 cooler can be subjected to extreme cycling during plant operation.
*   To minimize differential thermal expansion stresses, particularly due to the potential for severe cycling, the SO3 cooler unit **should be kept as short as practical**.
