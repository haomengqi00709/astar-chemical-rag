# Engineering Subcontractor Quality Audit Process

The services provided by the Engineering Subcontractor are subject to a formal audit process by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. This process is a critical component of ensuring the accuracy and compliance of services with the requirements of the Subcontract, as outlined in [[engineering_subcontractor_quality_assurance_0_spc_7004]].

**Audit Objectives:**
Audits are conducted for two primary purposes:
*   To assure the accuracy of the services provided.
*   To assure the compliance of the services with all requirements stipulated in the Subcontract.

**Documentation Transmission and Review:**
Upon request, the Subcontractor **must** transmit relevant documentation to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics for audit purposes. It is a mandatory requirement that all transmitted documentation **must** be properly checked and approved in accordance with the Subcontractor’s own [[engineering_subcontractor_quality_plan_document_control|Quality Plan]] prior to submission.

Following review by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, the documents will be returned to the Subcontractor as Adobe Portable Document Format (PDF) copies. These returned documents will either have identified Defects clearly marked-up, or they will be marked as “Audited: No Defects Noted”. Each returned document **will** be digitally signed by the person who conducted the audit, providing an official record of the review.

**Subcontractor Obligations Post-Audit:**
It is crucial to note that the failure of an audit to identify a Defect in the services **will not** relieve the Subcontractor of their obligations to perform services strictly in accordance with the Subcontract.

Upon receipt of audited documents where Defects have been noted, the Subcontractor **must** follow the established steps for correction and prevention of recurrence. These steps **shall** be detailed within the Subcontractor's [[engineering_subcontractor_quality_plan_defect_management|Quality Plan]] or its referenced implementing documents.
