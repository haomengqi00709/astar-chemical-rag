# Subcontractor Material Control (0-SPC-60)

This document, 0-SPC-60, specifies the requirements for material control plans to be developed by construction subcontractors for the Project. It outlines the mandatory procedures for receiving, storing, handling, and tracking materials on site.

The Subcontractor is required to develop material control plans in accordance with this specification, ensuring efficient and accurate management of all project materials, minimizing waste and loss.
