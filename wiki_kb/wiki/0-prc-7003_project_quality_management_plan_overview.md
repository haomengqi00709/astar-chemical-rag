**Commitment to Quality**
Aker Kvaerner Chemetics is fully committed to the quality objectives of this project. Both the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Director and the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Director Project are committed to upholding these standards. The operational line management structure of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, as depicted in the attached organization chart, supports this commitment.

All Aker Kvaerner Chemetics project team members are responsible for the quality of their individual work and **must** comply with this procedure. Specific responsibilities for quality are further detailed in Sections 4, 5.1, 6.1, 7.1, 8.1, and 9.1 of the original document, with additional responsibilities outlined in Section 4.2. The Manager Project holds overall management responsibility.
