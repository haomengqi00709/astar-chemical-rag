# Project Scope and Deliverables

The project encompasses a full Engineering, Procurement, and Construction (EPC) scope, including commissioning.

**Battery Limits and Material Flow:**
Battery limits for the project are defined in Data available in SQL: 1-LST-0001.
*   Salt is delivered to the plant via trucks.
*   Treatment chemicals are delivered to the plant in bulk containers.
*   Utilities are delivered to designated piping tie-points.
*   Liquid effluents are delivered to designated piping tie-points.
*   Hydrochloric Acid is produced as a 32% solution to Battery Limits.
*   Export Chlorine is produced as gas to Battery Limits.
*   Sodium Hypochlorite is produced as a 15% solution in trucks.
*   Sodium Hydroxide is produced as a 50% solution in trucks.

**Scope of Supply Checklists:**
A complete description of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' scope of supply is detailed in the following checklists:
*   Engineering Scope: Data available in SQL: 0-LST-2001
*   Supply and Erection Scope: Data available in SQL: 0-LST-2002
*   Field Services Scope: Data available in SQL: 0-LST-2003

The split in scope between Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and any engaged engineering contractors is also outlined in Data available in SQL: 0-LST-2001. The work distribution among various subcontracts is further detailed in Data available in SQL: 0-LST-2002.
