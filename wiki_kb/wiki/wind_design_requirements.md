# Wind Design Requirements

Wind design is a fundamental consideration for the structural integrity and safety of all plant components and structures within the Chemetics project. The comprehensive wind design requirements are documented in [[site_design_conditions_1_dst_0001]].

The following parameters **must** be specified for wind design:

*   **Wind Design Code:** The applicable wind design code or standard **must** be identified to ensure compliance with regulatory requirements and industry best practices.
*   **Region:** The geographical region for which the wind design is being performed **must** be specified, as wind characteristics vary by location.
*   **Terrain Category:** The terrain category (e.g., open country, suburban, urban) **must** be specified, influencing the wind speed profile and turbulence intensity.
*   **Exposure Category:** The exposure category **must** be specified, which further refines the wind pressure calculations based on the roughness of the surrounding terrain.
*   **Basic Wind Speed:** The basic wind speed, typically a 3-second gust speed with a specific return period, **must** be specified.
*   **Structure Importance Multiplier:** An importance multiplier for the structure **must** be specified, reflecting the consequences of its failure and influencing the design wind loads.

In addition to these mandatory specifications, the following wind speeds are specified for various load conditions:

*   **Serviceability Wind Speed:** Specified in m/s. This speed is used for design checks related to occupant comfort and prevention of minor damage.
*   **Permissible Loads Wind Speed:** Specified in m/s. This speed is used for design checks under normal operating conditions.
*   **Ultimate Loads Wind Speed:** Specified in m/s. This speed represents extreme wind events and is used for ultimate strength design checks, ensuring structural survival.

Strict adherence to these wind design requirements is essential to safeguard personnel, equipment, and the overall plant infrastructure against wind-related hazards.

The following parameters **must** be specified for wind design:
*   Wind Design Code
*   Region
*   Terrain Category
*   Exposure Category
*   Basic Wind Speed
*   Structure Importance Multiplier
*   Serviceability wind speed in m/s
*   Permissible Loads wind speed in m/s
*   Ultimate Loads wind speed in m/s
