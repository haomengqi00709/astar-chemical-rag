# FRP Tank Quality Data Sheet Overview (0-QDS-7414)

This Quality Data Sheet (QDS), identified as **0-QDS-7414**, outlines the specific quality assurance and inspection requirements for the fabrication and supply of Fiberglass Reinforced Plastic (FRP) Tanks. It serves as a critical reference for both the vendor and Chemetics, a division of Aker Kvaerner Canada Inc., ensuring compliance with established quality standards.

This document is part of a broader framework of quality data sheets, which typically follow the [[0-QDS-7XXX_template_quality_data_sheet]] structure. For detailed revision history and administrative information, refer to [[0-QDS-7414_document_revisions]].

The requirements detailed within this QDS are mandatory for all vendors involved in the supply of FRP Tanks for projects under the purview of Aker Kvaerner. Compliance ensures that all materials, manufacturing processes, and final products meet the stringent quality and performance criteria necessary for chemical engineering applications.
