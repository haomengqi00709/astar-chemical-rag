# Heat Exchanger Mechanical Checkout Requirements

This page outlines the mandatory requirements for the mechanical checkout of heat exchangers, designated by suffix H (Plate, Spiral, or Shell and Tube). This process is a key part of the overall [[mechanical_equipment_checkout_procedure]].

**Required Documents:**
The following documents are mandatory for the checkout of heat exchangers:
*   [[piping_and_instrumentation_drawings|P&ID]]
*   Manufacturers documentation
*   [[equipment_data_sheets_4_dst_xxxx|Equipment data sheet]]

**General Installation and Structural Checks:**
*   **Mandatory:** Heat exchanger nameplate data and record **must** match the heat exchanger drawing/data sheet information.
*   **Mandatory:** Heat exchanger nameplate tag **must** match tag drawing.
*   **Mandatory:** [[u_stamp_certification|U-stamp]] information **must** match the manufacturers information and equipment data sheets.
*   **Mandatory:** Hot pressure and temperature rating **must** be checked.
*   **Mandatory:** Cold pressure and temperature rating **must** be checked.
*   **Mandatory:** Heat exchanger **must** be installed with correct alignment.
*   **Mandatory:** Heat exchanger base **must** be grouted.
*   **Mandatory:** Heat exchanger **must** be bolted to base.
*   **Mandatory:** A sliding base, if applicable, **must** be loose.
*   **Mandatory:** Heat exchanger elevation height **must** be consistent with the design.
*   **Mandatory:** Piping **must** be correctly aligned with heat exchanger nozzles.
*   **Mandatory:** Heat exchanger **must** be coated per the project specifications.

**Grounding:**
*   **Mandatory:** [[grounding_installation_details|Grounding cable]] connection **must** be tight.
*   **Mandatory:** Grounding cable **must** be connected to the main grounding loop.

**Nozzles and Piping:**
*   **Mandatory:** Nozzle size, inlet outlet locations and material **must** be checked against manufacturers information and data sheet.
*   **Mandatory:** Nozzles and piping **must** be free of debris.
*   **Mandatory:** Nozzles **must not** be strained by connected piping.
*   **Mandatory:** Adequate pipe supports **must** be installed for nozzles.
*   **Mandatory:** [[drain_plug_installation|Drain valve]] **must** be installed and clear of debris.

**Valves and Safety Devices:**
*   **Mandatory:** [[steam_trap_installation|Steam trap]] **must** have correct orientation and flow direction.
*   **Mandatory:** [[pressure_relief_equipment_tagging|Relief valve]] **must** be installed, where applicable.
*   **Mandatory:** Relief valve discharge **must** be directed to a safe location.
*   **Mandatory:** Relief valve tag number **must** match the equipment number and relief valve data sheet.

**Maintenance Access and Plate Exchangers:**
*   **Mandatory:** No obstructions **must** prevent the removal of heads, tubes, plates etc.
*   **Mandatory:** Piping **must** have adequate spool pieces for tube or head removal.
*   **Mandatory (for plate heat exchangers):** [[plate_heat_exchanger_safety_shields|Safety shield]] **must** be installed.
*   **Mandatory (for plate heat exchangers):** Safety shield bolts **must** be tight.
*   **Mandatory:** Plate material **must** be checked versus specification.
*   **Mandatory:** [[gasket_material_selection|Gasket material]] **must** be checked versus specification.
