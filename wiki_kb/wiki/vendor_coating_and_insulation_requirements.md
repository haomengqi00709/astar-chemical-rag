# Vendor Coating and Insulation Requirements

As per document 4-SPC-0001, specific requirements apply to vendor-supplied coating and insulation systems for equipment.

**Coating Systems**
For Vendor standard equipment, including pumps, fans, agitators, plate heat exchangers, and bulk materials, the Vendor’s standard coating system for chemical service **will be used**. The Vendor **shall submit** details of the proposed coating system with their quotation for Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ approval. This ensures that the coating provides adequate protection against the specific chemical environments encountered in the plant.

**Insulation Systems**
Insulation for equipment **will conform** to the applicable Aker Kvaerner Chemetics specification, ensuring thermal efficiency and personnel safety. For packaged equipment where insulation of equipment or piping is required, the Vendor’s standard insulation system **will be used**. Similar to coatings, the Vendor **shall submit** details of the proposed insulation system with their quotation for Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ approval. This review process guarantees that the insulation meets performance and safety standards.

For general information on [[coatings_design_and_specification_7_prc_0002]] and [[insulation_design_and_specification_6_prc_0002]], refer to their respective pages.

**Coating System Approval**
The Vendor will submit details of the coating system with their quotation for Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ approval.

**Insulation Requirements**
Insulation will conform to the applicable Aker Kvaerner Chemetics specification.
For packaged equipment, where insulation of equipment or piping is required, the Vendor’s standard insulation system will be used.
The Vendor will submit details of the proposed insulation system with their quotation for Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ approval.
