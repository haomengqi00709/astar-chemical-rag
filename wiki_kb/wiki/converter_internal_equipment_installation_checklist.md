# Converter Internal Equipment Installation Checklist

This checklist details the mandatory requirements for the installation and support of internal components within the converter. Proper installation of these items is critical for the converter's process performance and structural integrity.

**Mandatory Internal Equipment Installation Requirements:**

*   **Internal Equipment Support:** Converter internal equipment **must** be installed and supported. This includes various components essential for the converter's operation.
*   **Internal Structures:** Converter internals (ladders, piping, blooper plates etc) **must** be installed and supported. These structures facilitate access, material flow, and process distribution within the vessel.
*   **Hardware and Gaskets:** Correct hardware and gaskets **must** be installed. The use of specified hardware and gaskets is crucial for maintaining pressure integrity and preventing leaks, especially at [[converter_nozzle_schedule]] points. Material selection for these components should align with [[converter_material_selection]].
*   **Blooper Plate Mesh:** Mesh **must** be installed on blooper plates. This is often required for catalyst retention or gas distribution purposes.
*   **Thermowells:** All Thermowells **must** be installed. Thermowells are essential for accurate temperature measurement within the converter, providing critical process data.
*   **Instrumentation:** All instrumentation **must** be installed. This includes various sensors and devices necessary for monitoring and controlling the converter's operation. Refer to [[instrument_installation_guidelines]] and [[instrument_installation_specification]] for detailed requirements.
*   **Isolation Valves:** Isolation valves **must** be installed on sample/pressure points. These valves are vital for safe sampling, pressure verification, and maintenance activities, allowing for [[isolation_and_preparation_for_maintenance_1_prc_0007]].

**Documentation Requirements:**

*   Reports for individual internal equipment items **must** be attached. These reports provide detailed records of the installation and inspection of each component, contributing to the overall [[quality_record_definitions]].

Adherence to these internal installation requirements ensures the converter operates efficiently and safely according to its design specifications.

**Mandatory Internal Equipment Installation Requirements:**
*   **Internal Equipment Reports:** Reports for individual internal equipment items must be attached.
*   **Specific Internals:** Converter internals, including ladders, piping, and blooper plates, must be installed and supported.
*   **Blooper Plate Mesh:** Mesh must be installed on blooper plates.
