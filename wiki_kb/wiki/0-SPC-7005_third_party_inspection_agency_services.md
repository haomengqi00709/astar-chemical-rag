# Third Party Inspection Agency Services Overview (0-SPC-7005)

Specification 0-SPC-7005 outlines the expectations for Agencies providing Third Party Inspection Services to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics (AKC). Its primary purpose is to ensure that these agencies have a clear and consistent understanding of the services normally required.

This specification details the minimum extent of activities that Third Party Inspection Agencies are expected to provide. While these activities form the standard baseline, the specific range of services may be adjusted for individual assignments based on instructions issued by AKC. Any modifications to the scope of activities **must** be confirmed in writing, typically via E-mail, to ensure clarity and record-keeping.

Agencies **shall** read this specification in conjunction with AKC Standard Specification [[0-SPC-7003_specification_overview]], which details the documentation requirements for Third Party Inspection Agencies.

Upon receiving a [[purchase_order_overview]] (PO) from AKC for Inspection Services, the Agency **shall** follow the sequence of activities outlined in this specification, unless explicitly advised otherwise by AKC. This structured approach ensures consistent quality and adherence to project requirements.

This specification details the minimum extent of activities normally expected to be provided by Third Party Inspection Agencies. The range of these activities may be modified for a particular assignment by instructions issued by AKC, provided such modifications are confirmed in writing, usually by E-mail. This specification should be read in conjunction with AKC Standard Specification 0-SPC-7003 Third Party Inspection Agency Documentation Requirements. For reporting and other documentation requirements, refer to 0-SPC-7003.
