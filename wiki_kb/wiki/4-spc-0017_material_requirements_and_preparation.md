# Material Requirements and Preparation for 6% Moly SS Welding (4-SPC-0017)

This section details the mandatory material specifications and preparation procedures for 6 percent Moly austenitic stainless steel fabrications under Standard **4-SPC-0017**.

**Material Specifications**
All materials used **must** be as specified in the purchase order and referenced documents, and **must** conform to applicable ASTM/ASME Codes.
*   All austenitic steel plate **must** be in the hot-rolled, annealed, and descaled condition per ASTM A240.
*   All piping **must** be in the annealed condition and conform to ASTM A312.
*   All fittings **must** be in the annealed condition and conform to ASTM A182.
*   All tubing **must** conform to ASTM A269.

**Material Traceability and Control**
Mill Test Certification **must** be supplied with all materials. The Fabricator **must** maintain full traceability of materials back to original mill certification. The Fabricator's internal controls **must** prevent the use of unspecified materials. Lack of adequate identification or certification **will** be cause for rejection of the specific component or item.

**Material Cutting**
Material **can** be cut to size by machining, shearing, or thermal cutting.
*   **Shearing:** Material over 3/16" thick **must** have extra material left on the sheared edge to allow for machining, grinding, etc., ensuring that any defects caused by shearing are removed. Material over 1" thick **must not** be sheared.
*   **Thermal Cutting:** The Plasma Arc Process is preferable. Welding edges after the Plasma Arc Process should be lightly ground to bright metal. If any thermal cutting process other than Plasma Arc is used, a minimum of 1/8" of metal **must** be removed by mechanical means. This 1/8" metal removal is to ensure all slag, scale, other irregularities, and contaminants, including thermal effects, have been removed.
*   **Grinding:** Material **can** also be cut to size by grinding.

**Material Forming**
Material **must** be cold formed as far as practical. When hot forming is necessary, the procedure to be adopted **must** be agreed to in writing by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics prior to the commencement of work. A solution anneal **will** be necessary after the hot working operation.
