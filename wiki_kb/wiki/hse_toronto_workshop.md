# HSE Management at Toronto Workshop

Responsibility for Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) at the Toronto Workshop (Section 9 of 0-PRC-8000) is assigned to the President of the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Equipment Division.

**Equipment Division HSE Management Committee:**
An Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Equipment Division HSE Management Committee **will** be formed, comprising the Division President, the Plant Manager, and the Manager responsible for HSE documentation and reporting. This committee **will** assist the Division President in ensuring all HSE responsibilities for the Chemetics Equipment Division are fulfilled.

The Equipment Division HSE Management Committee **will** meet monthly to review HSE performance within the Division. An Action List, including updated statuses of previous actions and new actions with assigned responsibilities, **will** be maintained and posted on the Company data network for employee access.

**Equipment Division Joint Health and Safety Committee (JHSC):**
An Equipment Division Joint Health and Safety Committee (JHSC) **will** be formed in accordance with the requirements of the Occupational Health and Safety Act of Ontario and the Collective Agreement with unionized employees. The JHSC **will** have equal representation from management and non-management employees, with non-management members elected by their peers. The Equipment Division JHSC **will** meet monthly.

Minutes of all Equipment Division JHSC meetings **must** be prepared and submitted to the HSE Management Committee. Reports covering safety inspections, accident investigations, and any other activities the JHSC deems appropriate **must** also be prepared and submitted to the HSE Management Committee.

**Workplace Environment and Risk Minimization:**
The Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Equipment Division **will** provide a workplace environment designed to minimize any deleterious impact on employee health. This includes:
*   Heating, Ventilation, and Air Conditioning that **must** comply with local codes and regulations.
*   Sufficient and sanitary facilities that **must** be provided.
*   Appropriate levels of ambient and task lighting that **must** be provided.
*   Refrigerators for food storage that **must** be provided.
*   Furniture designed to minimize repetitive strain injury that **must** be provided.
*   Promotion of employee health and fitness that **must** occur.

To minimize risk to employees working at the Equipment Division:
*   Health and safety guidelines of the Ontario Occupational Health and Safety Act **must** be followed.
*   First Aid facilities **must** be maintained.
*   Trained first aid attendants **must** be maintained.
*   An effective facilities emergency response plan **must** be maintained and tested on a regular basis.
*   Employee awareness of ergonomic factors **must** be maintained.
*   Risk assessments **must** be performed for higher risk activities.
*   Safety inspections/audits of shop operations **must** be performed on a minimum monthly basis.
*   Employees **must** be engaged in reporting near-misses and risk observations.
*   Proactive actions **must** be taken to eliminate unsafe conditions.
*   Proactive actions **must** be taken to reduce clutter and accumulation of unnecessary materials.

**Environmental Care:**
The Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Equipment Division **will** take a leading role in ensuring the principles of environmental care embodied in the slogan "Reduce Recycle Reuse."

For overall Company HSE policies and responsibilities, refer to [[0-prc-8000_administration_of_hse]] and [[hse_roles_and_responsibilities_company_wide]].
