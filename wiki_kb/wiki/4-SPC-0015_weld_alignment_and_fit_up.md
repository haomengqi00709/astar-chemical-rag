# Weld Alignment and Fit-Up (4-SPC-0015)

Accurate weld alignment and fit-up are critical for achieving high-quality welds in nickel-base alloy fabrications under Standard 4-SPC-0015. All work **must** be carried out in accordance with the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics "Approved for Fabrication" shop detail drawing(s).

**Drawing Compliance**
*   Deviations from the weld geometry as shown on approved drawing(s) are **not permitted** without the prior approval of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Alignment Tolerances**
*   Alignment of welding edges at butt joints **will be in accordance** with relevant code, specification, etc.
*   Maximum misalignment (High - Low) for plates up to and including 1/4" in thickness is 1/16".
*   Maximum misalignment (High - Low) for plates above 1/4" in thickness is 25% of the plate thickness or 1/8", whichever is smaller.
*   Pipe alignment **will be in accordance** with [[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping]].

**Holding and Support**
*   Parts to be welded **will be securely held** in position by mechanical means, welded-on bridge pieces, or tack welding to maintain specified alignment and gap during welding.
*   Jigs or suitable devices **must** allow adequate access for welding and cleaning.
*   Jigs **must** be constructed such that external loading is **not applied** to a joint during welding.
*   Welded-on bridge or support spiders **will be** of austenitic steel on the surface welded in contact with the vessel or heat exchanger.
*   The plate surface **will not be left** in a damaged condition after removal of welded-on bridge pieces or support spiders.

**Tack Welds**
*   Tack welds **will be performed** by qualified welders.
*   Tack welds employed for assembly purposes may form part of the completed weld, provided that complete fusion is effected at the root of the weld and that the welding procedure used is the same as that employed for the remainder of the root run.
*   Cracked and unsound tack welds **will be cut out** prior to welding.
*   Sound tack welds **must** be thoroughly fused into the root run of the main weld.
*   Tack welds **must** be crack-free where thinning down is necessary and **should preferably be** at least one inch long in such cases.

**Back-up Rings and Run-off Pads**
*   The use of permanent back-up rings or strips or consumable joint inserts is **not permitted** unless specifically approved in writing by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics QA & Materials Group.
*   Run-off pads, in the same material as the parent plate, **will be used** at the beginning and end of each plate weld.

**Arc Strikes and Burn Marks**
*   Arc strikes **should be confined** to surfaces on which weld metal is to be deposited.
*   If an arc strike occurs outside the deposition area, the spot **will require** to be ground and PT examined per Paragraph 10.3 of the original specification. It **will be repaired** if necessary.
*   Burn marks from grounding clamps **will be treated** as arc strikes.

These requirements ensure proper preparation and stability of components during the welding process, which is fundamental to achieving the [[4-SPC-0015_general_welding_requirements]].
