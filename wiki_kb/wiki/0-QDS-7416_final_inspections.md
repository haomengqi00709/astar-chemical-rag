# Final Inspections (0-QDS-7416)

For the Plate & Frame Heat Exchanger project (**0-QDS-7416**), a series of final inspections are required to ensure the quality and integrity of the equipment. All listed inspections are required as **FP** (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package), and Chemetics surveillance for each is **Complete**.

The required final inspections include:

*   **External Coating / Lining Inspection:** This inspection verifies the quality and application of any external coatings or linings.
*   **Dimensional Final Inspection:** This inspection confirms that the equipment dimensions comply with the specified drawings and tolerances.
*   **Contour & finish of welds Final Inspection:** This inspection assesses the quality, contour, and finish of all welds.
*   **Surfaces free from defects Final Inspection:** This inspection ensures that all surfaces are free from defects that could impact performance or longevity.
*   **Cleanliness Final Inspection:** This inspection verifies the cleanliness of the equipment, both internally and externally, prior to shipment.
*   **Spare parts Final Inspection:** This inspection confirms the correct identification, quantity, and quality of any spare parts supplied with the equipment.
*   **Review QA Documentation:** This review ensures the completeness and accuracy of the vendor's Quality Assurance documentation. This review **must** include the Manufacturer's Data Report (MDR) if registration is required for the equipment.

For definitions of **FP**, refer to [[0-QDS-7416_quality_record_definitions]]. Details on Chemetics' surveillance level can be found in [[0-QDS-7416_chemetics_surveillance_level]].
