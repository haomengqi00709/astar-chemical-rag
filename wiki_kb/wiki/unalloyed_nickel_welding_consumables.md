# Unalloyed Nickel Welding Consumables

For welding unalloyed nickel, all weld filler metals **must** strictly adhere to the requirements outlined in [[ASME_bpvc_section_ii_materials]], Part C. This ensures that the consumables possess the necessary chemical composition and mechanical properties for the intended application, as specified in [[4-SPC-0068_unalloyed_nickel_welding_standard_overview]].

Specific requirements for various welding processes are as follows:

*   **Shielded Metal Arc Welding (SMAW)**
    *   SMAW consumables **must** satisfy [[ASME_sfa_5_11_nickel_and_nickel_alloy_electrodes]], specifically classification ENi-1.
    *   The use of SMAW consumables other than Special Metals welding electrode 141 **requires** prior approval from Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

*   **Gas Tungsten Arc Welding (GTAW)**
    *   GTAW consumables **must** satisfy [[ASME_sfa_5_14_nickel_and_nickel_alloy_electrodes_and_rods]], specifically classification ERNi-1.
    *   The use of GTAW consumables other than Special Metals filler metal 61 **requires** prior approval from Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

*   **Gas Metal Arc Welding (GMAW)**
    *   GMAW consumables **must** satisfy [[ASME_sfa_5_14_nickel_and_nickel_alloy_electrodes_and_rods]], specifically classification ERNi-1.
    *   The use of GMAW consumables other than Special Metals filler metal 61 **requires** prior approval from Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

*   **Submerged Arc Welding (SAW)**
    *   An acceptable wire/flux combination for SAW is Special Metals filler metal 61 and Special Metals Incoflux 6.
    *   As part of the weld procedure qualification for SAW, a chemical analysis of the finished weld deposit **must** be included.
