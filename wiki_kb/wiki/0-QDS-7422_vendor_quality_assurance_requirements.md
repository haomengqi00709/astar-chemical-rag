# Mist Eliminator Vendor Quality Assurance Requirements

For the supply of Mist Eliminators, the Vendor's Quality Assurance (QA) program **must** adhere to specific requirements outlined in this Quality Data Sheet and related specifications.

**Quality Plan**
The Vendor **shall** prepare a Quality Plan in accordance with [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance]]. This plan details the procedures and practices the Vendor will implement to ensure the quality of the Mist Eliminators.

**Compliance**
The Vendor's Quality Assurance program **must** comply with:
*   This Quality Data Sheet ([[0-QDS-7422_quality_data_sheet_administration]]).
*   [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance]]: Vendor Quality Assurance.

**Quality Plan Requirement**
If a Quality Plan is not explicitly required for a specific order, the corresponding sections or cells related to the Quality Plan within the documentation **shall** be deleted. This ensures that only relevant quality requirements are maintained for each procurement.
