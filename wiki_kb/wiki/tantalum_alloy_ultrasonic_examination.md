# Tantalum Alloy Ultrasonic Examination

This section details the non-destructive examination (NDE) requirements for tantalum alloy tubing, with a focus on ultrasonic testing, as specified in [[4-SPC-0045_tantalum_alloy_welding_specification_overview|4-SPC-0045]].

*   **General NDE**: Each length of tubing **must** be examined as per the NDE requirements.
*   **Visual Examination**: A 100% external visual examination for surface irregularities and damage is **required**. Out-of-tolerance dimensions **will** be cause for rejection.
*   **Preferred Method**: Ultrasonic examination (immersion type) is the preferred method for tubing inspection.
*   **Alternative Notification**: When ultrasonic examination cannot be performed, [[chemetics_aker_kvaerner_company_details|Aker Kvaerner Chemetics]] **must** be advised at the quotation stage.
*   **Approved Agency**: Ultrasonic examination **must** be completed by an Aker Kvaerner Chemetics approved agency.
*   **Calibration Standard**: The calibration standard **must** be of the same diameter, wall thickness, and grade of material as the tube being tested.
*   **Notch Dimensions**: Internal and external longitudinal and transverse notches **must** be 6mm (1/4”) maximum in length and 0.125mm (0.005”) nominal width. Notch depth **shall not** exceed 10% of the nominal wall thickness.
*   **Indication Settings**: The indication from the calibration defects **must** be set at 80% of full screen height. The alarm level **must** be set at not less than 50% of the minimum indication from the calibration defects.
*   **Defect Investigation**: All indications causing an alarm **must** be investigated and manually maximized. Indications exceeding 75% of the minimum calibration indication **will** be cause for rejection.
*   **Recording**: An appropriate multi-channel strip chart recorder **must** be used for all ultrasonic examination.
*   **Documentation**: Copies of all strip chart recordings and examination reports **must** be submitted to [[chemetics_aker_kvaerner_company_details|Aker Kvaerner Chemetics]] prior to any tube being used for manufacture.
*   **Procedure Submission**: The ultrasonic examination procedure **must** be submitted to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics prior to commencement of the test.
*   **Proficiency Demonstration**: The Vendor or N.D.E. contractor **must** demonstrate adequate proficiency using the proposed procedure prior to commencement of the test.
*   **Rejected Tubes**: Rejected tubes **must** be classified as to defect type.

For general information on [[non_destructive_examination]], refer to [[ASME_section_v_non_destructive_examination]].

*   **Visual Examination**: A 100% external visual examination is required for surface irregularities and damage. Out of tolerance dimensions will be cause for rejection.
*   **Preferred Method**: Ultrasonic examination (immersion type) is the preferred NDE method. If ultrasonic examination cannot be performed, Aker Kvaerner Chemetics must be advised at the quotation stage.
*   **Approved Agency**: Ultrasonic examination must be completed by an Aker Kvaerner Chemetics approved agency.
*   **Calibration Standards**:
    *   The calibration standard must be of the same diameter, wall thickness, and grade of material as the tube being tested.
    *   Internal and external longitudinal and transverse notches will be 6mm (1/4”) maximum in length.
    *   Notches will be 0.125mm (0.005”) nominal width.
    *   Notch depth will not exceed 10% of the nominal wall thickness.
*   **Acceptance Criteria**:
    *   The indication from the calibration defects will be set at 80% of full screen height.
    *   The alarm level will be set at not less than 50% of the minimum indication from the calibration defects.
    *   All indications causing an alarm must be investigated and manually maximized.
    *   Indications exceeding 75% of the minimum calibration indication will be cause for rejection.
*   **Documentation and Procedure**:
    *   An appropriate multi-channel strip chart recorder must be used for all ultrasonic examination.
    *   Copies of all strip chart recordings and examination reports must be submitted to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics prior to any tube being used for manufacture.
    *   The ultrasonic examination procedure must be submitted to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics prior to commencement of the test.
    *   The Vendor or N.D.E. contractor must demonstrate adequate proficiency using the proposed procedure prior to commencement of the test.
*   **Defect Classification**: Rejected tubes must be classified as to defect type.
