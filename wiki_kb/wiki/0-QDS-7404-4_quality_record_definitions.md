# 0-QDS-7404-4 Quality Record Definitions

This page defines the specific types of quality records required for the Electrostatic Precipitator Miscellaneous Items, as outlined in Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7404-4. Quality records are essential documents that provide objective evidence of activities performed, results achieved, or compliance with specified requirements. They are a fundamental part of any robust quality management system, ensuring traceability, accountability, and verification of quality.

**Quality Record Types:**
Two primary types of quality records are defined within this document:

*   **AP (As Prepared):**
    *   This designation means "As Prepared".
    *   An AP quality record is a document that is generated and maintained by the Vendor during the manufacturing, inspection, or testing process. These records are typically internal working documents or reports that capture the immediate results or status of an activity.
    *   Examples might include internal inspection reports, raw test data, or manufacturing checklists. While prepared by the vendor, these records may be subject to review by Chemetics or the client.

*   **FP (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package):**
    *   This designation means "Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package".
    *   An FP quality record is a document that **must** be formally compiled and submitted as part of the final Quality Assurance Documentation Package. These records represent the conclusive evidence of compliance and are typically required for client review and project handover.
    *   FP records are often summaries, certificates, or final reports derived from the AP records, demonstrating that all specified quality requirements have been met.

The clear distinction between AP and FP records helps streamline the documentation process, ensuring that all necessary evidence is available for review and archiving.
