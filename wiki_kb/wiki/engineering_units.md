# Engineering Units

All process data **will** be specified using the engineering units listed below. Preferred units **will** be used for local gauges, transmitter indicators, and throughout the Plant-Wide Control System (PWCS).

It is important to note that 'Normal' base conditions for gases are not to be confused with “Standard” conditions.

**Normal Base Conditions for Gases:**
*   **Temperature:** 0°C
*   **Pressure:** 101.325 kPa(A)

**Specific Engineering Units:**

*   **Flow (Gases):** Nm3/h, Nm3/s
*   **Flow (Liquids):** m3/h, l/h, l/s
*   **Flow (Steam):** t/h, kg/h
*   **Flow (Solids):** t/h
*   **Pressure (Gauge):** kPa(G), Pa(G)
*   **Pressure (Absolute):** kPa(A)
*   **Pressure (Vacuum):** Pa(A)
*   **Pressure (Differential):** kPa(A)
*   **Level:** %
*   **Temperature:** ºC
*   **Density:** kg/m3, %
*   **Torque:** %, Nm
*   **Conductivity:** µS/cm
*   **Weight:** T, kg
*   **Viscosity:** cP
*   **Current:** A, mA
*   **Voltage:** V, kV
*   **Energy:** kWh, MJ
*   **Power:** kW, MW
*   **Magnetic Flux Density:** T

For information on how these units are applied in specific instruments, refer to pages such as [[flow_measurement_general|Flow Measurement - General]], [[level_measurement_general|Level Measurement - General]], [[pressure_measurement_general|Pressure Measurement - General]], and [[temperature_measurement_sensors_and_transmitters|Temperature Measurement - Sensors and Transmitters]].
