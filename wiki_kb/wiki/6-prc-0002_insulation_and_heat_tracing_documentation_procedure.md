# Insulation and Heat Tracing Documentation Procedure (6-PRC-0002)

This procedure, identified as **6-PRC-0002**, outlines the requirements for the preparation and approval of documentation related to insulation and heat tracing design. Project personnel **shall** prepare all documentation as described within this procedure.

The primary purpose of this procedure is to:
*   Provide a comprehensive record of the insulation and heat tracing design.
*   Furnish a complete description of the technical requirements that **shall** be issued to Vendors.
*   Clearly assign responsibilities for the preparation and approval of these critical documents.

This procedure ensures that all insulation and heat tracing designs are thoroughly documented, providing a clear technical basis for procurement and installation, and maintaining a consistent record throughout the project lifecycle.

Key documents governed by this procedure include the [[6-prc-0001_insulation_design_criteria]], the [[insulation_specification_6_spc_0001]], and the [[6-lst-0001_equipment_insulation_list]]. Other related document types, such as a Line List, may also be referenced within the scope of insulation design.
