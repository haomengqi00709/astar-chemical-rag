# Engineering Subcontract 0-SUB-22 Financial Terms

This page outlines the financial terms, payment conditions, expense reimbursement, and delay damages applicable to Engineering Subcontract 0-SUB-22.

**Currency:**
The currency of this subcontract is **Australian Dollars (AUD)**.

**Payment Terms:**
*   The period within which payments are made **shall** be 45 days from the date of invoice. For general information on payment terms, refer to [[engineering_subcontract_payment_terms]].

**Expense Reimbursement:**
*   Listed expenses will be reimbursed at cost by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics upon submission of copies of itemised receipts.
*   The Subcontractor **shall** submit forecasts of the total expenses at intervals of 1 month.

**Staff Rates:**
*   The staff rates for key personnel are given in document ID 02C03000-0-LST-22XX-2-R1: [[engineering_subcontractor_staff_rates]]. The Subcontractor **shall** use the key persons for the stated positions as listed in this document.
    *   *Note:* Data available in SQL: 02C03000-0-LST-22XX-2-R1

**Delay Damages:**
*   The rate for delay damages is **AUD 1,000** per day. This amount will be applied in cases where the Subcontractor fails to meet agreed-upon deadlines, as specified in the subcontract. For more details on delay damages, refer to [[engineering_subcontract_delay_damages]].

These financial provisions are critical for managing the commercial aspects of the subcontract and ensuring timely compensation and accountability.
