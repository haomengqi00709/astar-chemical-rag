# Plant Instrument Numbering (8-PRC-0001)

The [[plant_instrument_numbering_8_prc_0001]] document (8-PRC-0001) establishes the conventions for identifying plant instrumentation.

**Content Requirements:**
The 8-PRC-0001 document **shall** contain:
*   Standard identification numbers for plant instrumentation.
*   Client's alternate identification number for plant instrumentation.

**Document Preparation and Approval:**
*   **Prepared by:** DDE
*   **Checked by:** Project Engineer
*   **Approved by:** Instrument Engineer

**Application and Customization:**
Plant Instrument Numbering **shall** follow Standard-8-PRC-0001 where applicable. If project requirements dictate the use of a Client numbering procedure, the DDE **shall** revise Standard-8-PRC-0001 to include the Client's requirements and publish accordingly. This modification **must** be an add-on section to the standard document, clearly defining itself as the procedure to be used on the project. The standard portion of Standard-8-PRC-0001 **is not** to be revised.

**Instrument Tag/Loop Numbering on P&IDs:**
The Process department will assign standard Instrument Tag/Loop numbers on the Stage I through to Stage III [[p_id_development_and_approval_1_prc_0003]]. When required by contract, the Field Instrument Engineer (FIE) will be responsible for the assignment of the client's Instrument Tag/Loop numbers on the [[8-lst-0002_instrument_index]]. The FIE will cross-reference client Instrument Tag/Loop numbers to standard numbers as per Stage III P&ID’s. Refer to 8-PRC-0001 for detailed information on Instrument Tag Numbering.
