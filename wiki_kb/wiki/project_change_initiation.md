# Project Change Initiation

The Project Change Initiation phase is the first step in the [[0-PRC-4003-R1_project_change_management_flow_diagram|Project Change Management Flow Diagram]], focusing on the identification and formal documentation of any potential modifications to the project. This phase ensures that all proposed changes, regardless of their origin, are captured and processed systematically.

Changes can be initiated from two primary sources:

1.  **Client-Initiated Change Requests**:
    *   A Client initiates a [[change_request|change request]] when they require a modification to the project scope, specifications, or deliverables.
    *   The [[change_management_roles_and_responsibilities_0_prc_4003|Project Manager]] is responsible for accepting these change requests from the Client.
    *   Upon acceptance, the Project Manager **must** initiate a Project Change Notice (PCN) for evaluation. This ensures that the client's request is formally entered into the change management system.

2.  **Internal Project Team Identified Changes**:
    *   A [[change_management_roles_and_responsibilities_0_prc_4003|Project Team member]] may identify a potential change due to various factors, such as design optimization, unforeseen site conditions, regulatory updates, or internal process improvements.
    *   When a potential change is identified, the Project Team member **must** initiate either a Project Variation Notice (PVN) or a Project Change Notice (PCN) to formally document the proposed modification.

The initiation of a PCN or PVN marks the formal entry of a proposed change into the project's change management process. These documents serve as the foundational record for all subsequent evaluation and approval steps. The specific details and structure for initiating these documents can be found in [[pvn_pcn_form_initiation_and_details]]. For a comprehensive understanding of these and other related documents, refer to the [[change_management_document_types]] page.

This phase ensures that all proposed changes are formally identified and documented, whether they originate from the Client or internal project team members.
A change request can be initiated by the Client, which is then accepted by the Project Manager. Upon acceptance, the Project Manager initiates a Project Change Notice (PCN) for further evaluation. Alternatively, a Project Team member may identify a potential change and initiate either a Project Variation Notice (PVN) or a PCN to formally document it.
