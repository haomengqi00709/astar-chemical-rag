# 4-SCR-7540 Support Criteria Basis

This page outlines the foundational documents used as the basis for the support criteria for the Acid Plant Converter, as specified in document **4-SCR-7540**.

The support criteria are based on the following equipment drawings:
*   Equipment Drawing Number **4-OTL-7540**, Revision X
*   Equipment Drawing Number **4-DTL-7540-X**, Revision X

These drawings provide essential information regarding the equipment configuration, dimensions, and interfaces necessary for the design of supporting structures.

For general information on equipment support criteria, refer to [[equipment_support_criteria]]. Related drawing types can be found under [[equipment_layout_drawing]] and [[outline_drawings]].
