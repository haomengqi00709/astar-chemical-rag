# Sight Glass Inspection (5-PRC-0007)

The inspection of sight glasses is a key part of the [[mechanical_checkout_piping_5_prc_0007|Piping Mechanical Checkout Procedure (5-PRC-0007)]], ensuring their correct installation, component integrity, and proper alignment within the piping system.

**Required Documentation for Sight Glass Checkout:**
*   [[piping_and_instrumentation_drawings|P&ID]]
*   [[piping_speciality_item_specification|Specialty Item Specification]]
*   [[pipe_isometric|Isometric]]

**Sight Glass Inspection Steps:**
*   **Flange Checks:** All flange checks for sight glasses **shall** be performed as described in the [[flange_inspection_requirements_5_prc_0007|Flange Inspection Requirements]].
*   **Type and Tag Verification:** Confirm that the sight glass type and tag number match the [[piping_and_instrumentation_drawings|P&ID]] and [[pipe_isometric|isometric]].
*   **Component Installation:** Confirm that all components, such as shrouds, have been installed correctly as per [[sight_glass_installation|installation guidelines]].
*   **Piping Misalignment (Critical Services):** For critical services in metal lines, it **may be necessary to** disassemble piping around glass sight glasses to check for piping misalignment.
*   **Stress on Body:** Confirm that the piping arrangement does not put undue stress on the sight glass body.
*   **Torque Settings:** Confirm which sight glasses have recommended torque settings and verify that this torque is correctly applied.

These checks are crucial for the safe and reliable operation of sight glasses, particularly in applications where process visibility is critical.
