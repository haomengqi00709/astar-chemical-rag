# 0-LST-6X12 Construction Subcontract Labour Rates

The 0-LST-6X12 document, titled "Construction Subcontract Labour Rates," is a mandatory form that the Subcontractor **must** complete. Its primary purpose is to establish and document the total cost per hour worked for each distinct classification of personnel involved in "Providing the Works" under the construction subcontract.

This document forms the basis for the detailed [[construction_subcontract_labour_rates]] calculations, which include wages, benefits, taxes, travel, living expenses, non-productive time redistribution, and consumable supplies. It is a foundational document for cost estimation and tracking for both [[construction_subcontract_direct_work_costs]] and [[construction_subcontract_indirect_work_costs]].
