# Lightning Protection Installation Details

Lightning protection installation details provide specific instructions and hardware requirements for installing lightning electrodes and grounding conductors on various plant structures. These details are critical for protecting personnel and equipment from lightning strikes.

**Content of Lightning Protection Details**
Lightning protection details **shall** show:
*   The lightning electrode mounting hardware required when the electrode is located on a building roof.
*   The lightning electrode mounting hardware required when the electrode is located on an outdoor vessel.
*   The lightning electrode mounting hardware required when the electrode is located on a vent pipe.
*   The lightning electrode mounting hardware required when the electrode is located on other tall structures.
*   The method of installing the grounding conductor from the lightning electrode to the buried grounding electrode.
*   The type of grounding electrode required.
*   Any interconnection with other grounding systems.
