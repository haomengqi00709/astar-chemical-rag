# Project Change Management Flow Diagram

The Project Change Management Flow Diagram, documented as Appendix V to Standard 0-PRC-4003-R1, outlines the systematic procedure for managing changes within a project. This critical document, titled "Project Change Management Flow Diagram — Procedure," ensures that all project modifications are initiated, evaluated, approved, and implemented in a controlled manner, maintaining project integrity and objectives.

The flow diagram delineates five primary stages:
1.  **[[project_change_initiation|Change Initiation]]**: The process begins with the identification and formal submission of a potential change.
2.  **[[project_change_evaluation|Change Evaluation]]**: This stage involves a comprehensive assessment of the change's impacts across various project aspects.
3.  **[[project_change_approval|Change Approval]]**: Formal authorization is sought from relevant stakeholders based on the evaluation.
4.  **[[project_change_implementation|Change Implementation]]**: Approved changes are put into action, and project documentation is updated.
5.  **Project Control**: Continuous monitoring and updating of project baselines to reflect implemented changes.

Throughout these stages, the process incorporates several key states or actions:
*   **Revision**: Indicates that a change request or related document is undergoing modifications or further review.
*   **Rejected**: Signifies that a proposed change has not been approved and will not proceed.
*   **Progress/Approved**: Denotes that a change has received the necessary approvals and is moving forward with implementation.

This structured approach is essential for effective [[scope_change_control_0_prc_4003|scope change control]] and minimizing adverse impacts on project cost, schedule, and quality. The diagram serves as a visual guide for all project personnel involved in managing modifications. Further details on the objectives and requirements for managing project variations can be found in [[pvn_pcn_program_objectives_and_requirements]]. The process also defines procedures for [[pvn_pcn_revision_process|revising PVNs/PCNs]] and categorizing [[pvn_pcn_types_and_impacts|different types of PVN/PCN impacts]].

For a summary of cost impact examples related to this procedure, refer to [[0-PRC-4003-R1_summary_of_cost_impact_example_procedure]].

The diagram encompasses key phases such as Change Initiation, Change Evaluation, Change Approval, Change Implementation, and Project Control, ensuring a structured approach to managing project modifications. The process also defines various states for changes, including "Revision," "Rejected," and "Progress/Approved," to track their lifecycle effectively.
