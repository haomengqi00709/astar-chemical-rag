# Large Diameter Gasket Requirements

This page outlines specific gasket requirements for large diameter flanges, particularly those exceeding DN600. These requirements are critical for ensuring proper sealing and operational integrity in larger piping systems.

### Metallic Flanges (Above DN600)
For metallic flanges with pipe sizes above DN600, GORE™ GR **must** be used. This material is specified to meet the demanding sealing requirements for these larger applications.

### Non-Metallic Flanges (Above DN600)
For non-metallic flanges with pipe sizes above DN600, elastomer gaskets appropriate for the specific service **must** be used. The selection of the appropriate elastomer gaskets for these applications is detailed in the [[5-LST-0003_piping_service_list]]. This ensures that the gasket material is compatible with the process fluid and operating conditions.

### Non-Metallic Flanges (Above DN600)
For non-metallic flanges with pipe sizes above DN600, elastomer gaskets appropriate for the service must be used. The specific appropriate elastomer gaskets are detailed in 5-LST-0003: Piping Service List.
