# Piping Degreasing and Alkaline Wash

When degreasing of lines is required to remove cutting oils and greases, and solvent washing is not a viable option, an alkaline wash should be used. This method is effective for cleaning internal piping surfaces.

**Alkaline Wash Solution Composition**
The alkaline wash solution should be based on (but not restricted to) the following chemicals, forming a 3% solution (in water):
*   38% Sodium Hydroxide
*   36% Sodium carbonate
*   12% Sodium metasilicate (as anhydrous chemical)
*   9% Tetrasodium Pyrophosphate
*   3% Fatty Acid Esters
*   2% Ethoxylated Alkylphenol

**Function of Components**
*   The first three components (Sodium Hydroxide, Sodium carbonate, Sodium metasilicate) act as caustics to saponify the oils and greases.
*   The last three components (Tetrasodium Pyrophosphate, Fatty Acid Esters, Ethoxylated Alkylphenol) function as emulsifiers and wetting agents, aiding in the removal and suspension of contaminants.

**Application Procedure**
*   Washing efficiency **will** be enhanced by using warm water.
*   After washing with the alkaline solution, the lines **must** be rinsed with clean water.
*   Following rinsing, the lines **must** be air blown dry to prevent residual moisture.
