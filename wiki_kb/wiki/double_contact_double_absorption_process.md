# Double Contact Double Absorption (DCDA) Process

The Double Contact Double Absorption (DCDA) process is a method used in sulfuric acid production to achieve higher sulfur dioxide (SO2) conversion rates and reduce [[emissions_and_effluents|SO2 emissions]] to the atmosphere. This process involves passing the sulfur dioxide gas through absorption towers twice, with an intermediate absorption step.

### Process Overview
In a conventional single absorption process, SO2 gas is converted to sulfur trioxide (SO3) over a catalyst bed, and then SO3 is absorbed in sulfuric acid. The DCDA process enhances this by:
1.  **First Contact**: SO2 gas is passed through a series of catalyst beds (e.g., 3 beds) where it is partially converted to SO3.
2.  **Intermediate Absorption**: The gas stream, now containing a mixture of SO2 and SO3, is cooled, and the SO3 is absorbed in an intermediate absorption tower using concentrated sulfuric acid. This removes a significant portion of the SO3.
3.  **Second Contact**: The remaining gas, which is now lean in SO3 but still contains unconverted SO2, is reheated and passed through additional catalyst beds (e.g., the 4th bed). This allows for further conversion of SO2 to SO3, shifting the equilibrium towards higher conversion.
4.  **Final Absorption**: The gas from the second contact stage is again cooled, and the newly formed SO3 is absorbed in a final absorption tower.

### Advantages
The primary advantages of the DCDA process include:
*   **Higher Conversion Efficiency**: Typically achieving SO2 conversion rates of 99.8% or higher, compared to 99.5% for single absorption plants.
*   **Reduced Emissions**: Significantly lower SO2 emissions to the atmosphere, meeting stringent environmental regulations.
*   **Increased Acid Production**: The higher conversion efficiency can lead to increased sulfuric acid production capacity.

### Application
The DCDA process is widely adopted in modern sulfuric acid plants due to its environmental and economic benefits. It is a critical component in achieving high-purity acid production and minimizing environmental impact.
