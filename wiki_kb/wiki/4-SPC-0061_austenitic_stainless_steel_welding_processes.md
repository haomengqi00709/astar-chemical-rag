# Austenitic Stainless Steel Welding Processes (4-SPC-0061)

This page outlines the acceptable welding processes and specific procedural requirements for austenitic stainless steel fabrication, as defined in 4-SPC-0061.

**Acceptable Welding Processes**
The following welding processes are acceptable:
*   Shielded Metal Arc Welding (SMAW)
*   Gas Tungsten Arc Welding (GTAW)
*   Gas Metal Arc Welding (GMAW)
*   Submerged Arc Welding (SAW)
*   Plasma Arc Welding (PAW)
*   Flux Core Arc Welding (FCAW)

**Process-Specific Requirements**

*   **Shielded Metal Arc Welding (SMAW):** Heat input **will** be minimized by using the stringer bead technique for SMAW.
*   **Gas Tungsten Arc Welding (GTAW):** GTAW weld metal thickness **will not** exceed 3mm (⅛") per pass. Heat input **will** be minimized by using the stringer bead technique for GTAW. Gas backing **is required** for GTAW where second side welding is not carried out.
*   **Gas Metal Arc Welding (GMAW):** GMAW **will** be used for the root pass of butt welds only when fill passes are to be made from both sides. Gas backing of the root pass **is required** when GMAW is used to fill a butt weld from one side. The Vendor **must** provide satisfactory evidence of continuous shop use of the short circuiting arc process if it is to be used.
*   **Submerged Arc Welding (SAW):** SAW weld metal thickness **will not** exceed 13mm (½") per pass. Run-on and run-off tabs of the same material specification as the base material **will** be used on all longitudinal seams for SAW. The Vendor **must** provide satisfactory evidence of continuous shop use of the SAW process. A chemical analysis of the finished deposit **must** be included as part of the weld procedure qualification for SAW.
*   **Plasma Arc Welding (PAW):** No additional process-specific requirements beyond electrode specifications are noted in this document.
*   **Flux Core Arc Welding (FCAW):** A chemical analysis of the finished deposit **must** be included as part of the weld procedure qualification for FCAW. The Vendor **must** provide satisfactory evidence of continuous shop use of the FCAW process.
