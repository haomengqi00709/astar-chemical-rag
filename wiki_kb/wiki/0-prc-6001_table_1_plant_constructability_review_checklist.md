# Plant Constructability Review Checklist (0-PRC-6001 Table 1)

This page describes the contents and usage of the Plant Constructability Review Checklist, which is referenced as `0-PRC-6001_table_1` in the source document.

The checklist serves as a primary guidance tool for the [[plant_constructability_review_process_and_methodology]]. It is designed to facilitate a structured yet flexible review of project designs from a constructability perspective.

**Key Features and Requirements:**
*   **Guidance:** The checklist provides a framework for discussions and evaluations during the Plant Constructability Review.
*   **Information Requirements:** It indicates, typically with an (X), the specific information required for the review, as detailed in [[plant_constructability_review_information_requirements]].
*   **Record Keeping:** Information presented during the review, along with any identified follow-up actions and the responsibilities for completing these actions, **must** be recorded directly on this checklist.
*   **Customization:** The checklist **must** be modified to suit the specific scope of supply for each project. This ensures its relevance and effectiveness for diverse project requirements.
*   **Flexibility:** It is important to note that the checklist does not differentiate the importance of its listed items. This is because areas of concern can vary significantly between different projects, requiring participants to exercise judgment on applicability and priority.

The successful application of this checklist contributes significantly to achieving the objectives outlined in [[plant_constructability_review_objectives_and_timing]].
