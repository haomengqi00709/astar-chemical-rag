# FRP Tank Non-Destructive Testing Requirements (0-QDS-7414)

This page details the Non-Destructive Testing (NDT) requirements for FRP Tanks, as specified in Quality Data Sheet **0-QDS-7414**. NDT methods are crucial for evaluating the integrity of the FRP structure without causing damage.

**Non-Destructive Testing (NDT):**
*   **Non-Destructive Testing** is a mandatory requirement and **must** be included in the Vendor's Inspection and Test (I & T) Plan. This ensures that appropriate NDT methods are applied to detect any internal or surface defects.

**Barcol Hardness Test:**
*   The **Barcol hardness test** requires a Quality Record **FP** (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package). The results of this test **must** be compiled and submitted as part of the final Quality Assurance Documentation Package.
*   The **Barcol hardness test** requires **Chemetics Witness** surveillance. A Chemetics representative **must** be present to observe the execution of this test.
*   The **Barcol hardness test** requires **Chemetics Hold** surveillance. This means that work cannot proceed beyond this point until Chemetics has reviewed and approved the test results.

The Barcol hardness test is a common NDT method for FRP materials, used to assess the degree of cure and overall hardness of the laminate. For general information on non-destructive examination requirements, refer to [[0-QDS-7411_non_destructive_examination_requirements]].
