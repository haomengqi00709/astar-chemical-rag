# Mag Drive Pump Ancillary Equipment

This page details the ancillary equipment specified for Mag Drive Pumps, as outlined in Document ID 4-DST-XXXX-R1. Ancillary equipment provides additional functionality, protection, or support for the main pump unit.

**Ancillary Equipment Details**
*   **Ancillary Equipment**: General category for additional components.
*   **Supply by Vendor**: Indicates whether the ancillary equipment is supplied by the pump vendor.
*   **Mount by Vendor**: Specifies whether the ancillary equipment is mounted by the pump vendor.
*   **Dry Running Protection**: Requirement for a system to protect the pump from damage due to dry running conditions.
*   **Baseplate**: Requirement for a baseplate, often supplied as an ancillary item, to ensure proper alignment and support for the pump and motor.

The inclusion and proper integration of ancillary equipment are vital for the pump's safe and reliable operation. For a broader overview of ancillary equipment for horizontal centrifugal pumps, refer to [[horizontal_centrifugal_pump_ancillary_equipment]].
