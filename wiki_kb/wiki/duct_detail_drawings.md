# Duct Detail Drawings

Duct Detail Drawings are crucial engineering documents that provide fully dimensioned details for all components of ducting systems, particularly for applications such as Acid Plant Ducting. These drawings are essential for the accurate fabrication and installation of complex ductwork.

**Content Requirements**
A Duct Detail Drawing **shall** contain:
*   **Fully Dimensioned Details:** Comprehensive dimensional information for all individual components of the ducting system, ensuring precise manufacturing and assembly.

**Roles and Responsibilities**
The preparation, checking, and approval of Duct Detail Drawings are assigned to specific disciplines:
*   **Preparation:** The drawing **shall** be prepared by the Design discipline.
*   **Checking:** The drawing **shall** be checked by the Process discipline.
*   **Approval:** The drawing **shall** be approved by the Mechanical discipline.

**Vendor Involvement**
For Acid Plant Ducting, vendors will generally produce any necessary shop drawings. The manufacture of Acid Plant Ducting hardware **shall** be in accordance with Chemetics documentation.

The inclusion of Acid Plant Ducting requirements and associated documentation was added during Revision 3 of the [[5-PRC-0003_piping_documentation_procedure_overview]].
