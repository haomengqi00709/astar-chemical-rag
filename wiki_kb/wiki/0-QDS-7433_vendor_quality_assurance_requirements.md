# Flocculant Mixer Vendor Quality Assurance Requirements

For the Flocculant Mixer package, stringent vendor quality assurance is paramount to ensure that all supplied components and the integrated system meet Chemetics' specified quality standards and performance criteria. The vendor's commitment to quality is a critical factor in the successful delivery and operation of the equipment.

**Mandatory Requirements:**

*   The Vendor **must** prepare a Quality Plan in accordance with the requirements set forth in [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment]]. This plan **shall** detail the vendor's quality management system, inspection points, and control measures throughout the manufacturing and assembly process.
*   The Vendor's Quality Assurance program **shall** comply fully with all stipulations outlined in this Quality Data Sheet (0-QDS-7433).
*   The Vendor's Quality Assurance program **shall** also comply with the general requirements specified in [[0-SPC-7001_vendor_quality_assurance_equipment]].

Compliance with these requirements ensures that the Flocculant Mixer package is manufactured to the highest standards, minimizing risks of defects, operational issues, and non-conformances. The vendor's adherence to these quality protocols is subject to review and surveillance by Chemetics.

Further details on general vendor quality assurance principles can be found in [[vendor_quality_assurance]].
