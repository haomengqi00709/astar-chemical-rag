# Purchase Order Security and Access

This page outlines provisions related to financial security, retention, and access rights under the Purchase Order (PO).

**Retention and Security:**
*   The Purchase Order will explicitly state if retention applies to payments.
*   The Purchase Order will also specify if the Vendor is required to provide Aker Kvaerner Chemetics with any form of security.

**Access to Work and Materials:**
*   The Vendor **must** provide access to work being done or goods and materials being stored for this Purchase Order to the [[purchase_order_roles_and_responsibilities#Project Buyer|Project Buyer]], [[purchase_order_roles_and_responsibilities#Inspector|Inspector]], and [[purchase_order_roles_and_responsibilities#Lead Engineer|Lead Engineer]].
*   Access **must** also be provided to [[purchase_order_definitions#Others|others]] as notified to the Vendor by the Project Buyer.

**Vendor Compliance with Instructions:**
*   The Vendor **must** obey any instruction given by the Project Buyer that is in accordance with this Purchase Order.
