# PVC/FRP Piping for Dry Chlorine Service (5-SPC-0101)

This section outlines the minimum requirements for FRP wrapped PVC piping used in dry chlorine service.

**Design and Manufacturing Standards**
[[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping]] and Chlorine Institute requirements for non-metallic piping **shall** apply where appropriate. Piping manufacture **shall** be in accordance with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Engineering Standard/Specifications. The design pressure for PVC/FRP piping **shall not** exceed 5 psig.

**Material Handling and Site Preparation**
Piping for Dry Chlorine Service **shall** be received on site in an oil and grease-free condition. Open ends of piping for Dry Chlorine Service **shall** be protected by flanges or covers from ingress of rainwater, debris, etc. Installed piping **shall** be blown clean of any debris prior to pneumatic leak testing.

**Leak Testing Procedures**
Properly selected and calibrated low pressure gauges **shall** be installed during leak testing of PVC/FRP piping. Leak testing of the completed PVC/FRP piping system **shall** use air or nitrogen and an external soapy water solution, in accordance with [[ASME_b31_3_chemical_plant_and_petroleum_refinery_piping]] paragraph 345.5.

The leak testing procedure involves:
1.  A preliminary leak test **shall** be carried out at 3 psig.
2.  After the preliminary test, pressure **shall** be increased to 5.5 psig and held for 10 minutes.
3.  Pressure **shall** then be reduced to the design pressure of 5 psig, and the inspection repeated.

**Chlorine Leak Detection**
*   Internal pressure of the PVC/FRP piping system **shall** be reduced to 2 psig, then a small volume of chlorine gas introduced.
*   Sufficient quantities of chlorine **shall** be added for external inspection of all joints with ammonia vapour. The presence of a chlorine leak **will** be evident by a white cloud.
*   Masking tape can be used to wrap exposed flanges to improve leak detection. The masking tape can be pierced at one point to provide the check location.
*   All leaks **shall** be repaired, and the system re-tested.

**Prohibited Cleaning Methods**
PVC/FRP piping system **shall** at no time be exposed to steam cleaning or purging. Cleaning of PVC/FRP piping with solvents is not permitted unless specifically approved in writing by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.
