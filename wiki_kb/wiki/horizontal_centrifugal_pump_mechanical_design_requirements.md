# Horizontal Centrifugal Pump Mechanical Design Requirements

The mechanical design requirements for a horizontal centrifugal pump, as outlined in Document ID [[template_horizontal_centrifugal_pump_datasheet_administration]], ensure the pump's structural integrity, operational reliability, and compliance with relevant standards.

**General Specifications:**
*   **Pump Type:** End Suction Horizontal Centrifugal.
*   **Impeller Type:** *Shall be specified*.
*   **Design Maximum Suction Pressure:** Measured in kPa(g) (kilopascals gauge).
*   **Design Maximum Temperature:** Measured in oC (degrees Celsius).
*   **Equipment Specification:** General Equipment Requirements.
*   **Design Code:** *Shall be specified*. This code dictates the manufacturing and testing standards.
*   **Site Conditions:** *Shall be specified*. These include environmental factors relevant to the pump's operation.
*   **Connection Type:** *Shall be specified*. This refers to the type of connections for piping.
*   **Electrical Area Classification:** *Shall be specified*. This classification is critical for safety in hazardous environments.
*   **Painting requirements:** *Shall be specified*. These define the coating and finishing standards.
*   **Casing Drain requirements:** *Shall be specified*. These detail the provisions for draining the pump casing.
*   **Total Noise Limit:** *Shall be specified*. This sets the maximum allowable noise level during operation.
*   **Vibration requirements:** *Shall be specified*. These define acceptable vibration levels for smooth operation and longevity.
*   **Impeller Size:** *Shall be specified*.
*   **Speed:** *Shall be specified*.
*   **Base Plate:** *Shall be specified*.
*   **Spacer Block:** *Shall be specified*.

These mechanical specifications are complemented by details on [[horizontal_centrifugal_pump_material_selection]], [[horizontal_centrifugal_pump_drive_system]], [[horizontal_centrifugal_pump_ancillary_equipment]], and [[horizontal_centrifugal_pump_shaft_seal_system]].
