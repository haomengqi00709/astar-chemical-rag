# Piping Installation Verification (5-PRC-0007)

As part of the [[mechanical_checkout_piping_5_prc_0007|Piping Mechanical Checkout Procedure (5-PRC-0007)]], a detailed verification of piping installation is conducted to ensure compliance with design documents. This involves a series of visual and dimensional checks across various aspects of the piping system.

**General Piping Checks:**
*   **Material Verification:** Visually confirm that line materials match the specified [[pipe_specifications|pipe material specification]].
*   **Routing Confirmation:** Visually confirm that the pipe routing precisely matches the [[pipe_isometric|isometric drawings]].
*   **Branch and Take-off Location:** Visually confirm that all piping branches and take-offs are in the correct locations as identified on both the [[pipe_isometric|isometric]] and [[piping_and_instrumentation_drawings|P&ID]].
*   **Branch Connection Material/Type:** Confirm that the material and type of branch connections align with the isometric.
*   **Pipe Slope:** Visually confirm that the pipe slope is in the correct direction as specified.
*   **Support Shims/Supports:** Visually confirm the presence of shims or supports on pipe bridges.
*   **Joint Clearance:** Visually confirm that piping joints (e.g., couplings) are not resting on supports, which could cause the piping to bend upwards.
*   **Critical Dimensions:** Measure and confirm critical dimensions identified on the [[piping_and_instrumentation_drawings|P&ID]], such as seal legs, pots, and internals.
*   **Straight Runs:** Visually confirm that straight runs in pipe length are correct to ensure proper operation of orifice plates and flowmeters.

**Pipe Support Checks:**
*   **Support Existence and Location:** Confirm that supports exist as identified on the [[pipe_isometric|isometric]] (correct type and correct location).
*   **Support Adequacy:** Confirm the adequacy of supports by attempting to move or shake the pipe.
*   **Support Connection:** Check the support connection to the pipe for correct tightness of bolts and proper installation of wear pads.
*   **Anchor Supports and Guides:** Visually confirm that anchor supports and guides appear to be adequately sized and correctly located.
*   **Thermal Expansion:** Visually confirm that hot lines have sufficient room to expand and that U-clamps are not overly tight, restricting movement.
*   **Spring Hangers:** Confirm that travel stops are removed from spring hangers.

**Ancillary Installation Checks (Punch List Items):**
*   **Insulation:** Visually confirm the [[insulation_erection_0_spc_6x96|insulation]] material, thickness, and cladding (if installed). If not completed, it **shall** be added to the punch list.
*   **Painting:** Visually confirm [[coatings_erection_0_spc_6x97|painting]] (where required on the [[line_list|line list]]). If not completed, it **shall** be added to the punch list.
*   **Flange Guards:** Confirm that [[flange_guard_installation|flange guards]] are installed (where required on the [[line_list|line list]]). If not completed, it **shall** be added to the punch list.

**Required Documentation for Piping Installation Verification:**

*   [[isometric_drawing|Isometrics]]
*   [[line_list|Line List]]
*   [[pipe_material_specification|Pipe Material Specification]]
*   [[pipe_support_specification|Pipe Support Specification]]
