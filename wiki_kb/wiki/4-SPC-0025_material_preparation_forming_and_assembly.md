# Material Preparation, Forming, and Assembly for High Silicon Austenitic Stainless Steel (4-SPC-0025)

**Material Preparation**
Material **shall** be cut or prepared by mechanical means such as machining or shearing, with close attention to cleanliness and accuracy. Plasma cut surfaces **shall** have the area affected by heat completely removed, except for underwater plasma cutting where this requirement does not apply. Other methods of thermal cutting high silicon stainless steel **shall not** be permitted.

Single or Double "V" grooves **shall** have a maximum depth of 6 mm on the process side. Exceptions to this groove depth requirement **shall** be reviewed between VEW and Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Contamination Control and Cleaning**
The fabricator **shall** take all necessary controls to prevent contamination of weldments at all stages of fabrication. These controls **shall** include the segregation of wire brushes, grinding wheels, and other tools for use exclusively on stainless steels. On completion of work, items **shall** be cleaned internally and externally. All fabrication markings, weld spatter, and other extraneous materials **shall** be removed prior to shipment.

**Forming Requirements**
Plate, sheet, pipe, and tubes **shall** be cold formed at all times. Hot forming for alignment when welding or assembling components or adjusting for distortion is **not permitted** and may result in rejection of the part or component.

All dished heads **shall** be solution treated and water quenched after forming. Hot forming of dished heads **shall not** be performed unless specifically agreed upon by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. After forming and final heat treatment on dished heads, the severely deformed knuckle areas **shall** be dye penetrant inspected both internally and externally around the entire circumference.

**Assembly and Tack Welding**
When aligning parts for welding, the use of clamping devices is preferred to tacking. Tack welds **should** be avoided. If necessary, tack welds **shall** be small and performed by qualified welders. Tack welds employed for assembly purposes **shall not** form part of the completed weld and **must** be ground out as the main weld progresses. Tacking from the outside of the groove is advantageous if accessible, as tacks can be completely removed by grinding before welding the outside surface. Cracked and unsound tack welds **shall** be cut out prior to installing the root pass.

**Arc Strikes and Surface Imperfections**
Every effort **shall** be made to confine arc strikes to surfaces on which weld metal is to be deposited. If an arc strike occurs outside the weld area, it **shall** be ground out to a maximum depth of 0.012" (0.3mm). The area of an arc strike ground out **shall** be dye penetrant examined. Burn marks from grounding clamps and weld spatter **shall** be treated as arc strikes.

**General Fabrication Requirements**
All work shall be carried out in accordance with "Approved for Fabrication" shop detail drawing(s). Deviations from weld geometry as shown on approved drawing(s) are not permitted without prior approval of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Weld Groove Preparation**
Single or Double "V" grooves shall have a maximum depth of 6 mm on the process side. Exceptions to this 6 mm groove depth requirement shall be reviewed between VEW and Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Alignment and Tack Welding**
When aligning parts for welding, the use of clamping devices is preferred to tacking. Tack welds should be avoided. If necessary, tack welds shall be small and performed by qualified welders. Tack welds employed for assembly purposes shall not form part of the completed weld and must be ground out as the main weld progresses. Tacking from the outside of the groove is advantageous if accessible, as tacks can be completely removed by grinding before welding the outside surface. Cracked and unsound tack welds shall be cut out prior to installing the root pass.
