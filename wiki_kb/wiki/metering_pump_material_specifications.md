# Metering Pump Material Specifications

This page details the mandatory material specifications for various components of metering pumps, as required by Document ID 4-DST-XXXX-R1. Proper material selection is crucial for ensuring chemical compatibility, longevity, and safe operation of the pump in its intended service.

**Mandatory Material Requirements**
Material specifications **are required** for the following key components:
*   **Pump Head**: The primary wetted part that contains the fluid being pumped.
*   **Diaphragm**: A flexible membrane used in diaphragm-type metering pumps to isolate the process fluid from the drive mechanism.
*   **Valve Seat**: The component against which the valve ball or disc seals.
*   **Valve Ball**: A spherical component used in check valves to control fluid flow direction.
*   **Valve Gasket**: A sealing component used between static parts of the pump.
*   **O-Ring**: A toroidal seal used to prevent fluid leakage.
*   **Drive Components**: Parts of the mechanism that transmit power to the pumping element.
*   **Plunger packing**: Sealing elements used around the plunger in plunger-type metering pumps.
*   **Plunger Seal**: A seal specifically designed for the plunger.
*   **Pulsation Dampener**: A device used to reduce pulsations in the discharge flow.
*   **Back Pressure Valve**: A valve that maintains a minimum pressure on the discharge side of the pump.

These material selections **must** align with the fluid description and process conditions to prevent corrosion, erosion, and degradation, thereby ensuring the reliability and safety of the metering pump.
