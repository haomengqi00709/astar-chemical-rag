# Third Party Inspection Invoicing and Payment Requirements

Adherence to specific invoicing and payment procedures is mandatory for Agencies supplying Third Party Inspection Services to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Wire Payment Authorization**
Where funds are to be paid in a currency other than Canadian Dollars (CDN$), the Agency **must** send a completed and signed [[wire_payment_authorization_form]] to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Accounting Communication**
All accounting-related communications, including invoices and payment queries, **shall** be directed to Accounts.Payable@akerkvaerner.com.

**Invoice Requirements**
All invoices **must** comply with the following:
*   **Numbering and Dating:** All invoices **must** be numbered and dated.
*   **Purchase Order Reference:** All invoices **must** clearly reference the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics Purchase Order (PO) Number issued to the Agency.
*   **Cost Allocation:** If an invoice covers more than one Vendor PO, costs **must** be allocated accordingly for each PO.
*   **Final Invoice Designation:** When all work on an Agency PO is complete, the invoice **shall** explicitly state that it is the “Final Invoice for Purchase Order Number 0XXXXXXX-07XX”.
*   **Monthly Invoicing:** For work on an Agency PO that extends over a period exceeding two months, invoicing **shall** be submitted on a monthly basis.

**Price Limits and Change Orders**
The price of services **shall not** exceed the price stated on Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ Purchase Order without prior written approval. Any prior written approval for exceeding the PO price **will** be formally documented on a [[change_order_documentation_requirements_0_chg_22]].

**Consequences of Non-Compliance**
Failure to comply with these invoicing requirements **will** result in a delay in payment until all missing or incorrect information is supplied and rectified.

For general invoicing guidelines, refer to [[invoicing_documentation_requirements]]. For purchase order approval, see [[purchase_order_approval_and_authorization]].
