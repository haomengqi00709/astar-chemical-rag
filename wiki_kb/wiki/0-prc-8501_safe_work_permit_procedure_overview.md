# Safe Work Permit Procedure (0-PRC-8501) Overview

Procedure 0-PRC-8501 outlines the requirements for properly authorizing work on a plant under [[commissioning_planning_and_management]] or operation, ensuring all necessary safety precautions are taken before work commences.

**Purpose and Applicability**
The primary purpose of this procedure is to ensure that any work on a plant under commissioning or operation is properly authorized and that all safety precautions have been taken prior to initiating the work. This regulation applies after a circuit, any system, or equipment is handed over mechanically completed for commissioning by the Construction Manager to the Commissioning Manager.

The procedure **shall** be followed by the Commissioning Team, which includes Aker Kvaerner Chemetics personnel, Contractors, and Vendor representatives.

**Basis and Client Regulations**
This procedure is based on Aker Kvaerner’s Work Permits procedure, issued in January 2003, which is detailed in the [[aker_kvaerner_hse_policy_manual]] section 7, item 7.02.

Client regulations may be followed if their requirements and guidelines fully cover those spelled out in Aker Kvaerner Chemetics’ procedures. However, Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' work permit procedures **will** be used when clients have inadequate permit programs, ensuring a consistent standard of safety.
