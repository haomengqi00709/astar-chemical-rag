# Additional Testing for First-Time Suppliers (Stainless Steel Heat Exchanger Tubes) (4-SPC-0047-2)

Specification 4-SPC-0047-2 includes mandatory additional testing requirements for tubes supplied by vendors who have not previously provided stainless steel heat exchanger tubes to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. These requirements are designed to ensure rigorous quality control and mitigate risks associated with new suppliers. These additional tests **must** be conducted in consultation with and approval from the Materials and QA group.

**Liquid Penetrant (PT) Testing Requirements:**

1.  **Tube End PT Testing:**
    *   All tube ends (both ends of each tube) **will** be PT tested on the inside diameter.
    *   This PT testing on the inside diameter **must** extend a minimum of 50mm inside the tube bore.

2.  **Batch Testing and Investigation:**
    *   PT testing **will** be performed in batches of 50 tubes.
    *   Testing **starts** as soon as the first 50 tubes are completed.
    *   If indications are found on any of the 50 tubes within a batch, the entire tube length **will** be PT tested.
    *   The entire tube length **will** be PT tested to investigate the severity of the problem.
    *   Any indications found **will** be reported immediately.

3.  **Longitudinal Cut and PT Testing (Every 100th Tube):**
    *   Every 100th tube, starting at tube 1, **will** be cut lengthwise.
    *   Every 100th tube, starting at tube 1, **will** also be PT tested after being cut lengthwise.
    *   Any indications found from this test **will** be reported immediately.

These additional tests provide an enhanced level of scrutiny for new suppliers, ensuring that their manufacturing processes and quality control measures are robust and meet the high standards required for [[gas_gas_heat_exchanger_design_criteria|gas to gas heat exchanger]] components.

For general testing requirements applicable to all suppliers, refer to [[4-SPC-0047-1_heat_exchanger_tube_testing_requirements]].

These additional tests (Section 4) require consultation with and approval from the Materials and QA group.
