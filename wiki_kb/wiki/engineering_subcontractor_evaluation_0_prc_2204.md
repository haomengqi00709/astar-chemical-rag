# Engineering Subcontractor Evaluation and Approval (0-PRC-2204)

Procedure 0-PRC-2204, titled "Engineering Subcontractor Evaluation and Approval," outlines the process for assessing and approving engineering subcontractors.

**Impact on Subcontract Conditions**
Additional conditions may be applied to a subcontract as a direct result of the bid evaluation process described in this procedure. This ensures that contractual terms can be tailored based on the specific findings and requirements identified during the subcontractor evaluation.
