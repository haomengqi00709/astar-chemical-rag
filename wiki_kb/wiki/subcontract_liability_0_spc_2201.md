# Subcontractor Liability (0-SPC-2201)

Professional Services Subcontract 0-SPC-2201 defines the limits of the Subcontractor's liability to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics.

**Limitation of Liability:**
*   The Subcontractor’s liability to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics resulting from a failure to Provide the Services is limited to the amount stated in the *Subcontract Data*.
