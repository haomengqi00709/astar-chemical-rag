# Product Stripper Material Selection and Lining

This page details the materials of construction (MOC) for the Product Stripper's shell, top, internal lining, and packing components.

**Major Equipment Materials**
*   **Shell Material of Construction:** Carbon Steel
*   **Top Material of Construction:** Carbon Steel
*   **Equipment Sketch Material:** Carbon Steel.

**Internal Lining**
*   **Lining Material of Construction:** 3 mm pecora mastic, 0.25 mm PTFE, Acid Brick

**Packing Materials**
*   **Packing Material (Zones 1-6):** Ceramic
*   **Detail tower packing data Bottom Packing MOC:** Ceramic
*   **Detail tower packing data Zone 1 Packing MOC:** Ceramic
*   **Detail tower packing data Zone 2 Packing MOC:** Ceramic
*   **Detail tower packing data Zone 3 Packing MOC:** Ceramic
*   **Detail tower packing data Zone 4 Packing MOC:** Ceramic
*   **Detail tower packing data Top Packing MOC:** Ceramic

**Packing Support Material**
*   **Packing Support Material:** Ceramic

**Revision Notes**
*   In R1, the sketch with meshpad was changed to show the correct split between carbon and stainless steel.
