# Instructions to Bidders (0-SPC-5006)

Data available in SQL: 0-SPC-5006

The document 0-SPC-5006, titled "Instructions to Bidders," provides comprehensive guidelines and requirements for vendors responding to Request for Quotations (RFQs) issued by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. This specification is crucial for ensuring that all bids are submitted in a consistent format, contain all necessary information, and meet the company's expectations.

Key information typically covered in the Instructions to Bidders includes:
*   **Submission Requirements:** Details on how, when, and where bids must be submitted, including deadlines and preferred submission methods.
*   **Bid Format:** Guidelines on the structure and content of the bid proposal, ensuring all required sections (technical, commercial, legal) are addressed.
*   **Technical Specifications:** Clarification on how vendors should address the technical requirements outlined in Text Section 1 of the RFQ.
*   **Commercial Terms:** Instructions regarding pricing, payment terms, and any specific financial conditions to be included in the bid.
*   **Documentation Requirements:** What supporting documentation (e.g., company profiles, certifications, references) must accompany the bid.
*   **Clarification Process:** Procedures for vendors to ask questions or seek clarifications regarding the RFQ.
*   **Evaluation Criteria:** An overview of how bids will be evaluated, helping vendors to tailor their proposals effectively.

Text Section 6 of a Request for Quotation **shall** refer the Vendor to this document, ensuring that all potential suppliers have access to the necessary information to prepare a compliant and competitive bid.

For more information on the specific characteristics of RFQs, refer to [[request_for_quotation_specifics]].
