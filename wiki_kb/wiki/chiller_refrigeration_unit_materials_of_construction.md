# Chiller/Refrigeration Unit Materials of Construction

The selection of materials of construction for the Chiller/Refrigeration Unit, as outlined in datasheet [[4-PDS-XXXX-R1_template_chiller_refrigeration_unit_datasheet_administration]], is critical for ensuring the longevity, reliability, and chemical compatibility of the equipment with the process fluids and refrigerants.

The datasheet requires specifying the materials for the following key components:
*   **Compressor**
*   **Expansion Valve**
*   **Evaporator**
*   **Concentrator**
*   **Solution Pump**
*   **Purge Pump**
*   **Condenser**
*   **Receiver**
*   **Absorber**
*   **Recuperator**
*   **Refrigerant Spray Pump**
*   **Compressor Suction Surge Drum**
*   **Other** (for any additional components not explicitly listed)

Material selection **must** consider the operating temperatures, pressures, and corrosive properties of the fluids involved, as detailed in [[chiller_refrigeration_unit_process_fluid_specifications]] and [[chiller_refrigeration_unit_cooling_water_utility_specifications]]. General guidelines for material selection can be found in [[material_selection]].

The datasheet specifies materials for a comprehensive list of components, including but not limited to:
*   Expansion Valve
*   Solution Pump
*   Receiver
*   Refrigerant Spray Pump
*   Compressor Suction Surge Drum
