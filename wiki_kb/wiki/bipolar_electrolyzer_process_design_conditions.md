# Bipolar Electrolyzer Process Design Conditions

This page details the process design conditions for bipolar electrolyzers, as specified in Document ID 4-PDS-XXXX-R1. These parameters are critical for the design and operation of the electrolyzer system, ensuring product quality and operational efficiency.

**General Operating Conditions**
*   **Operating Case Reference**: Normal Cell Production
*   **Production Rate**: VTA mtpd (as 100% NaOH)
*   **Design Allowance**: 100% of PFD normal flow rate
*   **Turndown Ratio**: VTA %
*   **On-line Stream Factor**: VTA days/year

**Caustic Product Specifications**
*   **Composition NaOH**: 32.0 % w/w
*   **Composition NaOH Tolerance**: +/- 0.5 % w/w
*   **Composition NaCl**: <35 ppm
*   **Composition Na2CO3**: <350 ppm
*   **Composition Fe**: <0.3 ppm
*   **Flow Rate**: VTA kg/h
*   **Temperature**: VTA oC
