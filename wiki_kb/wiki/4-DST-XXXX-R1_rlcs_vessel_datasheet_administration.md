**Usage Guidelines:**
This datasheet is intended for integration into an ACAD OTL (Object-Oriented Technology Library) drawing of the vessel. The contents of this datasheet are inserted directly into the drawing.
*   The OTL drawing, once compiled with this datasheet, **must** be published, checked, and approved according to standard procedures.
*   The datasheet itself **need not** be separately published, checked, and approved.
*   Cells within the datasheet **must not** be merged, as merging cells can cause issues during the insertion process into the drawing.
*   This datasheet is primarily configured for an API style tank, specifically adhering to [[rlcs_vessel_design_conditions|API 650]] design code.
*   It may be adapted for an ASME vessel with minor modifications.
*   The template is flexible and may be modified for other equipment types that have similar requirements but different specific details or materials.
*   For vessels that are not lined, the entry for "lining" **shall** be specified as "none".

**Related Procedures and Documents:**

*   Civil/structural engineering calculations for external loads (wind, seismic, snow) are independently prepared in [[structural_calculation_report_4_scr_xxxx]].

This administrative overview ensures consistent application and management of vessel datasheets across projects.
