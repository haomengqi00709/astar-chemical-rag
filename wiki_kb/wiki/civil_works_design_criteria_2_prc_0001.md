# Civil Works Design Criteria (2-PRC-0001)

This document, 2-PRC-0001, outlines the specific design criteria for all civil works related to the Project. It provides the foundational requirements and standards that **must** be adhered to during the design and construction of civil infrastructure.

Discipline Leads utilize this document to establish the detailed requirements for civil engineering aspects of the Project, ensuring compliance with project specifications, industry standards, and regulatory mandates.
