# HAZOP Question Structure (1-HAZ-0002)

The HAZOP Responses Document **1-HAZ-0002** defines a comprehensive structure for recording [[hazard_and_operability_studies]] questions and their associated responses. This structure ensures all critical aspects of a potential deviation are captured, from identification to resolution and approval.

The following fields **must** be completed for each HAZOP question entry:

*   **Node:** Identifies the specific section or point in the process being analyzed. For example, Question 1.0 is associated with Node 1.
*   **Equipment/Line No:** Specifies the relevant equipment or line number.
*   **Document Ref:** References supporting documentation.
*   **Ref.:** An additional reference field for cross-referencing.
*   **Guideword:** The specific [[hazop_guideword_deviations]] used (e.g., No, More Of, Less Of, Part Of, Reverse, Other Than).
*   **Deviation:** The specific deviation from design intent identified using the guideword.
*   **Causes:** The potential reasons or events that could lead to the deviation.
*   **Consequences:** The potential outcomes or impacts of the deviation.
*   **Safeguards:** Existing protective measures or systems in place to prevent or mitigate the deviation.
*   **Question:** The specific question formulated during the HAZOP review.
*   **Action By:** The individual or department responsible for addressing the HAZOP question.
*   **Response:** The proposed action or resolution to the HAZOP question.
*   **Approved for Implementation By:** The individual who approves the proposed response for implementation.
*   **Implementation Approved Date:** The date when the implementation of the response was approved.
*   **Notes:** Any additional relevant information or comments.

This structured approach facilitates thorough analysis and ensures clear accountability for addressing identified hazards. For an overview of the document, see [[1-haz-0002_hazop_responses_overview]]. Specific examples of recorded questions can be found in [[1-haz-0002_hazop_question_details]]. Further details on HAZOP record structures can be found in [[hazop_record_structure_and_content]].
