# FRP Reinforced Polypropylene Pipe Installation

FRP (Fiber Reinforced Polymer) reinforcement for polypropylene pipe is utilized to enhance its mechanical properties, specifically to increase allowable working pressure at a given temperature, allow greater pipe support spacing, and provide additional mechanical strength against external loadings.

**Material Supply and Fabrication:**
PF1 (Polypropylene, FRP Reinforced) is typically provided as factory-fabricated spools, manufactured according to Kvaerner Aker Kvaerner Chemetics isometrics. Polypropylene pipe materials **will** be as specified in the [[pipe_specification|pipe specification]]. Pre-cut field joint kits **will** be provided for PF1 to facilitate on-site connections.

**Field Joints and Butt Fusion:**
Field joints are permitted for PF1 and involve butt welding of the liner, followed by FRP lining over the joint. Piping **will** be assembled using the standard tools and procedures for butt fusion of polypropylene pipe. Refer to Appendix PP6 for detailed butt fusion procedures. Care **must** be taken during butt welds to ensure that the exterior fibreglass does not become embedded in the face of the weld. The exterior bead formed by the fusion process **will** be ground flush prior to wrapping.

**Post-Testing FRP Wrapping:**
FRP wrapping of the joints for polypropylene pipe is to be done only after the [[piping_system_testing_and_flushing|pressure testing]] of the completed spool.

Refer to [[piping_manufacturer_data_references|MANUFACTURER’S DATA ATTACHED]] for polypropylene pipe.

**Factory Fabrication and Field Joints (PF1)**
PF1 is provided as factory fabricated spools according to Kvaerner Aker Kvaerner Chemetics isometrics. Field joints are allowed for PF1, involving butt welding of the liner and FRP lining over the joint.

Polypropylene pipe **will** be a factory spooled FRP wrapped piping system. Polypropylene pipe materials are as specified in the pipe specification. Pre-cut field joint kits **will** be provided for PF1.

**Butt Fusion and Wrapping**
Piping **will** be assembled using the standard tools and procedures for butt fusion of polypropylene pipe. Refer to Appendix PP6 for butt fusion of polypropylene pipe.

Care **must** be taken in performing butt welds to ensure that the exterior fibreglass does not get embedded in the face of the weld. The exterior bead formed by the fusion process **will** be ground flush prior to wrapping.

FRP wrapping of the joints for polypropylene pipe is to be done only after the pressure testing of the completed spool.
