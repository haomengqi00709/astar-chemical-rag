# Flocculant Mixer Coating and Lining Inspection

The external coating of carbon steel (CS) components within the Flocculant Mixer package is vital for corrosion protection and ensuring the longevity of the equipment in its operational environment. A thorough inspection of this coating is therefore a mandatory quality control step.

**Mandatory Requirements:**

*   **External Coating of CS:** The external coating of carbon steel components **requires** a Quality Record (FP). This record **must** document the details of the coating application, including surface preparation, coating thickness measurements, adhesion tests, and any visual inspections, confirming compliance with the specified coating system.
*   **Chemetics Witness Surveillance for External Coating:** The external coating of CS **requires** Chemetics Witness surveillance. A Chemetics representative or designated third-party inspector **must** be present to observe key stages of the coating application and inspection, ensuring that the process is executed according to specifications and that the final coating quality is acceptable.

These requirements ensure that the protective coating applied to the Flocculant Mixer's carbon steel elements provides the necessary durability and resistance against environmental degradation. For detailed specifications on coatings, refer to [[coatings_design_and_specification_7_prc_0002]].
