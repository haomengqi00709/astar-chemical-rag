# Subcontractor General HSE Obligations

Subcontractors engaged with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics **are bound** by a set of general Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) obligations to ensure a secure and compliant work environment.

**Cooperation and Compliance:**
*   The Subcontractor **will**, at its own expense, cooperate with Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and the owner in all reasonable safety precautions.
*   The Subcontractor **shall comply** with all requirements of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or the owner concerning the secrecy of any part or aspect of the work or project.
*   The Subcontractor **shall comply** with all requirements of any governmental authority having an interest in the work concerning secrecy.
*   The Subcontractor **will not permit** any person to enter premises except in accordance with the safety and security requirements of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, the owner, or any governmental authority.
*   The Subcontractor **will strictly adhere** to the Owner’s Environment, Health, and Safety requirements.
*   The Subcontractor **will strictly adhere** to the corporate Safety Manual of Aker Kvaerner Chemetics, which is issued by Aker Kvaerner. A compact disk version of this manual **will be made available** to the Subcontractor. Refer to [[aker_kvaerner_safety_policy_manual]] for more information.

**Procedure Alignment:**
*   The Subcontractor **will ensure** its own safety procedures are updated to incorporate all requirements listed under this Subcontract.
*   In instances where Subcontractor safety procedures are less stringent than this Specification, the more stringent requirements **will apply**.
