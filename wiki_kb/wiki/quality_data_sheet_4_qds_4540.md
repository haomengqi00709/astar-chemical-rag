# Quality Data Sheet (99H10600-4-QDS-4540)

The document 99H10600-4-QDS-4540 R1, titled "Quality Data Sheet," specifies the quality assurance and control requirements pertinent to the procurement of specific equipment or services (e.g., related to the 4000-series identification numbers, such as Absorber Pumps 7540P) for Project 99H10600. This data sheet outlines the necessary inspections, tests, and documentation required to ensure the quality and integrity of the supplied items.

Similar to other Quality Data Sheets, this document typically details:
*   **Inspection and Test Plan (ITP):** Requirements for the vendor to submit an ITP, outlining all planned inspections and tests throughout the manufacturing process.
*   **Hold and Witness Points:** Specific stages in manufacturing where Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or its designated inspector requires notification to hold production or witness tests.
*   **Material Traceability:** Requirements for material certificates and traceability records for critical components.
*   **Non-Destructive Testing (NDT):** Specific NDT methods (e.g., radiography, ultrasonic testing, magnetic particle inspection) and acceptance criteria.
*   **Performance Testing:** Requirements for functional or performance tests, including parameters to be measured and acceptance criteria.
*   **Documentation:** Specific quality-related documentation to be submitted, such as test reports, inspection records, and calibration certificates.
*   **Vendor Quality Management System:** Requirements for the vendor's quality management system (e.g., ISO 9001 certification).

Vendors **must** comply with the quality assurance requirements set out in this Quality Data Sheet. This document is a key reference in Text Section 4: Quality Assurance of Purchase Orders, ensuring that all supplied items meet the project's stringent quality standards.
