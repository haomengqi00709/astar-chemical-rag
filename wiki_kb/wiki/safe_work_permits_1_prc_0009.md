# Safe Work Permits (1-PRC-0009)

This document, 1-PRC-0009, outlines the procedure for the issuance and management of Safe Work Permits for activities conducted on the Project site. It details the requirements for assessing risks, defining precautions, and authorizing work to proceed safely.

To control safety risks during [[water_and_air_trials_and_safety_protocols]], this procedure is strictly enforced. It is a fundamental element of the Project's safety management system, ensuring that all hazardous work is properly controlled and authorized.
