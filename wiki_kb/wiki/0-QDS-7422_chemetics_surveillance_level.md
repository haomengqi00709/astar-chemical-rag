# Mist Eliminator Chemetics Surveillance Level

For Mist Eliminators, Chemetics' surveillance activities are defined to ensure compliance with quality standards throughout the manufacturing and supply process.

**Surveillance Level**
Surveillance by Chemetics **shall** be conducted to Level 0.0. This level of surveillance indicates the degree of involvement and oversight Chemetics will exercise.

**Compliance**
All surveillance activities performed by Chemetics **shall** be as set out in this Quality Data Sheet ([[0-QDS-7422_quality_data_sheet_administration]]), ensuring consistency and adherence to the specified quality requirements for Mist Eliminators.
