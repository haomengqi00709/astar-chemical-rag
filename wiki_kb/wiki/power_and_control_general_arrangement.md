# Power and Control General Arrangement Drawings

Power and Control General Arrangement drawings illustrate the physical locations of all major electrical equipment, control stations, and panels within the plant. These drawings are derived from mechanical general arrangement drawings and are essential for electrical layout and coordination.

**Content of Power and Control Drawings**
Power and control drawings **shall** show locations of:
*   All substations.
*   All electrical rooms.
*   All power transformers.
*   All motors.
*   All control stations.
*   All local control panels.
*   Any other electrical equipment.

Electrical equipment **shall** have the equipment number located next to the symbol used to identify it. Symbols used for electrical equipment **shall** be shown.

**References and Backgrounds**
Power and control drawings **shall** be derived from the latest version of the mechanical general arrangement drawings.
All [[electrical_installation_details]] pertaining to the installation of the electrical equipment **must** be referenced.
[[final_single_line_diagrams]] **must** be referenced.
[[electrical_schematic_and_wiring_diagrams]] **must** be referenced.
[[power_and_control_cables_list]] **must** be referenced.
The mechanical general arrangement drawings **must** be referenced.
Civil/structural drawings **must** be referenced.
