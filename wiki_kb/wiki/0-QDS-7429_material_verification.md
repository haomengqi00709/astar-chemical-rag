# 0-QDS-7429 Material Verification Requirements

This page details the requirements for material verification, including documentation and surveillance, as specified in Quality Data Sheet [[0-QDS-7429_document_administration]] for SS Plate.

**Material Verification** is a mandatory item or activity.

### Mill Test Certificates
*   **Quality Record Requirement**: Mill Test Certificates are required as [[0-QDS-7429_quality_record_definitions|FP]] (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package).
*   **Surveillance**: Chemetics **requires Witness surveillance** for Mill Test Certificates.
*   **Applicable Standards**: The current edition of ASTM or any other referenced specification **shall** apply for Mill Test Certificates.

### Certificate of Compliance
*   **Quality Record Requirement**: A Certificate of Compliance is required as [[0-QDS-7429_quality_record_definitions|FP]] (Included in Quality Assurance Documentation Package).
*   **Surveillance**: Chemetics **does not require Witness surveillance** for the Certificate of Compliance.

### General Surveillance Notes
For any item or activity listed in this Quality Data Sheet, Chemetics **does not require** the following surveillance types:
*   "Required in Vendor I & T Plan" surveillance.
*   "Hold" surveillance.
*   "Complete" surveillance.
