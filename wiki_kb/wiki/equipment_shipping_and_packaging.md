# Equipment Shipping and Packaging

As specified in document 4-SPC-0001, stringent requirements are in place for the shipping and packaging of equipment to ensure its safe delivery and integrity.

**Special Handling Requirements**
*   **Large Tanks:** Internal bracing **is required** for large tanks to prevent deformation during transit.
*   **Heavy Equipment:** High lifting capacity cranes and spreader bars **are required** for heavy equipment, and these specifications **must** be detailed in shipping plans.
*   **Temperature Sensitive Equipment:** Climate-controlled containers or routing through warm climates **are required** for temperature-sensitive equipment to prevent damage from extreme temperatures.
*   **Fragile Equipment:** Vibration/shock isolation packaging, including "no shunting" instructions when shipped by rail, **is required** for fragile equipment to protect it from mechanical stress.

**Shipping Configuration Drawings**
Shipping configuration drawings for large, heavy, temperature-sensitive, or fragile equipment **must be submitted** for review. These drawings **shall detail** supports, lifting attachments, weights, dimensions, and any shipping restrictions. The review of these drawings **must be completed** before packaging such equipment to ensure compliance with all safety and handling protocols.

For general packaging requirements, refer to [[packaging_land_transportation_0_spc_5004]] and [[packaging_marine_transportation_0_spc_5005]].
