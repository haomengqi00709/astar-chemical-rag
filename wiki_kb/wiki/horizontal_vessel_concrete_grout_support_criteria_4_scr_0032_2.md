# Horizontal Vessel on Concrete & Grout Support Criteria (4-SCR-0032-2)

This document, 4-SCR-0032-2, specifies the support criteria for horizontal vessels mounted on concrete and grout foundations, focusing on sliding support mechanisms.

**General Support Requirements:**
The support system detailed in this document is exclusively for **sliding support** applications. This design allows for thermal expansion and contraction of the vessel relative to its foundation.

**Mandatory Installation Requirements:**
*   The anchor bolt head **must** be welded to the underside (u/s) of the steel support structure. This is critical for ensuring the sliding functionality of the support system.
*   Sufficient clearance **must** be maintained between the nut and the vessel base plate. This clearance is essential to permit the support to slide freely, accommodating any movement without binding or damage.
