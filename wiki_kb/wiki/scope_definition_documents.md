# Project Scope Definition Documents

Project scope definition is critical for successful execution, relying on several key documents that detail the boundaries and deliverables of the project. These documents are generally based on preliminary versions from the proposal team but are verified and expanded for accuracy. They are ultimately issued with the status "Approved for Design."

**1-LST-0001: Battery Limits Conditions**
This document defines the interfaces and boundaries of the project.
*   **Preparation:** Prepared by Process.
*   **Checking:** Checked by the Project Engineer.
*   **Approval:** Approved by the Director of Technology.
Data available in SQL: 1-LST-0001

**0-LST-2001: Engineering Scope Checklist**
This checklist details the engineering deliverables and responsibilities.
*   **Preparation:** Prepared by the Project Engineer.
*   **Checking:** Checked by the Project Manager.
*   **Approval:** Approved by the Director of Projects.
*   **Content Requirements:** This checklist **must** include:
    *   The requirement for document review and approval by the Owner.
    *   The split in scope between Aker Kvaerner Chemetics and any engineering contractor(s).
    *   Any contractually required document submission dates.
    *   The requirement for document review and approval by a locally registered engineer.
Data available in SQL: 0-LST-2001

**0-LST-2002: Supply and Erection Scope Checklist**
This checklist outlines the scope related to supply and erection activities.
*   **Preparation:** Prepared by the Project Engineer.
*   **Checking:** Checked by the Project Manager.
*   **Approval:** Approved by the Director of Projects.
*   **Content Requirements:** This checklist **must** include:
    *   Delineation of supply by Aker Kvaerner Chemetics or by Subcontractors.
    *   The work distribution amongst Subcontracts.
Data available in SQL: 0-LST-2002

**0-LST-2003: Field Services Scope Checklist**
This checklist details the scope of field services required for the project.
*   **Preparation:** Prepared by the Project Engineer.
*   **Checking:** Checked by the Project Manager.
*   **Approval:** Approved by the Director of Projects.
Data available in SQL: 0-LST-2003
