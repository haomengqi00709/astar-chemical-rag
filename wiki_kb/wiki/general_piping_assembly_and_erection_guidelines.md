# General Piping Assembly and Erection Guidelines

Proper assembly and erection of piping systems are critical to ensure operational integrity, safety, and adherence to design specifications.

**Cold Springing and Expansion:**
Piping **will** be assembled and erected to minimize unintended cold springing and cold forcing. If isometric dimensions differ from field dimensions, requiring cold springing or forcing after utilizing field trim allowances or spacers, the Piping Subcontractor **must** only make field modifications after receiving explicit instructions and approval from Kvaerner Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' site representative. Piping **will** be installed to permit free expansion and contraction, unless drawings specifically indicate an anchor or guide pipe support.

**Installation Tolerances and Slopes:**
The standard tolerance for pipe installation is ± 13 mm (± ½ inch). Any deviation from this standard tolerance **must** be approved by Kvaerner Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' site representative. Where a slope is indicated but not dimensioned, the line is intended to be generally horizontal, without visible sags or reverse slopes, primarily to aid drainage in the direction indicated.

**Cleanliness and Temporary Protection:**
The Piping Subcontractor **must** ensure that no debris, weld rod stubs, paper and plastic products, or any other foreign material remains in any piping system. Each line **will** be inspected and cleaned before any closing joint is assembled. Temporary covers **will** be installed over open connections when work is not immediately continued to prevent contamination.

**Connection Types:**
*   **Socket Weld Fittings:** When using socket weld fittings, the pipe **will** be spaced in the socket to prevent stresses on the weld due to "bottoming" of the pipe.
*   **Screwed Connections:** For screwed connections in steam service, joints **will** be made with a suitable anti-seize compound. For other services, joints **will** be made using Teflon tape. Teflon tape wrapping **will** start approximately two threads from the end of the pipe and **must** be applied so that it tightens when the joint is screwed together. If a screwed joint is broken, all old tape **must** be removed before re-assembly.

**Pre-spooled Piping and Equipment Installation:**
Pre-spooled piping **should** be installed before other connecting piping and before small equipment, such as pumps, are anchored. This allows for minor adjustments of equipment and connecting piping to fit the pre-spooled pipe. Where expansion bellows are provided for misalignment of pre-spooled piping, the installed bellows **will** be checked to ensure that the maximum allowable combined axial, parallel, and angular displacement has not been exceeded. Bellows **will** be installed with the face-to-face dimension specified on the isometric and manufacturer’s drawing. Compression or expansion of bellows may be required to meet installation dimension requirements.

**Instrument and Valve Installation:**
Where instruments or valves cannot be installed at the time of pipe installation, the Piping Subcontractor **will** fabricate spool pieces of the appropriate face-to-face dimensions to allow assembly and testing of the pipe. On screwed piping systems, the Piping Subcontractor **should** anticipate the need to install unions, in addition to those shown on the piping isometrics, to facilitate ease of fabrication and maintenance.

**Start-up Strainers:**
Where specified by Kvaerner Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' site representative, Kvaerner Aker Kvaerner Chemetics-supplied start-up strainers **will** be installed on pump suctions. These strainers **will** typically be removed after water batching but prior to plant start-up.

**Wafer Type Valves and Instruments:**
When installing wafer type valves and instruments, care **must** be taken to ensure the item is centered on the flanges. Butterfly valves **will** be opened to ensure the valve does not bind on the ID of the pipe. Wafer check valves with a ‘loose’ flapper disc **will** be installed with the proper spacers or checked to ensure the pipe ID is within the manufacturer’s stated tolerance.

**Field Adjustments and Tolerances**
The Piping Subcontractor is responsible for all field adjustments necessary to accommodate equipment and field installation tolerances. Piping **will** be installed to permit free expansion and contraction, except where drawings specifically indicate an anchor or guide pipe support. The standard tolerance for pipe installation is ± 13 mm (± ½ inch) unless otherwise approved by Kvaerner Aker Kvaerner Chemetics' site representative.

**Slopes and Drainage**
Where a slope is indicated but not dimensioned, the line is intended to be generally horizontal, without visible sags or reverse slopes, and intended to aid drainage in the direction indicated.

**Cleanliness and Protection**
The Piping Subcontractor **will** ensure that no debris, weld rod stubs, paper and plastic products or any other material remains in any piping system. Each line **will** be inspected and cleaned before any closing joint is assembled. Temporary covers **will** be installed over open connections when the work is not being immediately continued.

**Socket Weld and Screwed Connections**
Where socket weld fittings are used, the pipe **will** be so spaced in the socket that no stresses **will** be imparted to the weld due to "bottoming" of the pipe.

For screwed connections in steam service, joints **will** be made with a suitable anti-seize compound. For screwed piping in other services, joints **will** be made using Teflon tape. Teflon tape wrapping **will** start approximately two threads from the end of the pipe and **will** be applied so that the tape **will** tighten when the joint is screwed together. If a screwed joint is broken, all old tape **will** be removed before re-assembly.

**Pre-spooled Piping and Bellows**
Pre-spooled piping should be installed before other connecting piping and before small equipment such as pumps are anchored. This allows minor adjustment of equipment and connecting piping to fit the pre-spooled pipe.

Where expansion bellows are provided for misalignment of pre-spooled piping, the installed bellows **will** be checked to ensure that the maximum allowable combined axial, parallel and angular displacement has not been exceeded. Bellows **will** be installed with the face to face dimension as specified on the isometric and manufacturer’s drawing. Compression or expansion of bellows may be required to meet installation dimension requirements.

**Temporary Spool Pieces and Unions**
Where instruments or valves cannot be installed at time of pipe installation, the Piping Subcontractor **will** fabricate spool pieces of the appropriate face to face dimensions to allow assembly and testing of the pipe.

On screwed piping systems, the Piping Subcontractor should anticipate the need to install unions, in addition to those shown on the piping isometrics, to facilitate ease of fabricating and maintaining.

**Start-up Strainers**
Where specified by Kvaerner Aker Kvaerner Chemetics site representative, Kvaerner Aker Kvaerner Chemetics supplied start up strainers **will** be installed on pump suctions. Start up strainers **will** typically be removed after water batching but prior to plant start-up.

**Valve and Instrument Installation**
When installing wafer type valves and instruments, care **must** be taken to ensure that the item is centred on the flanges.
