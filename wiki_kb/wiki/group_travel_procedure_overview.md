# Group Travel Procedure Overview

This document outlines the Group Travel Procedure, identified as Procedure Number 11.1 PR 2, a People Policy for internal use within the Aker Kvaerner Group. It was issued on 12.12.03 with a revision date of 31.12.05 (Revision Number 2). The policy owner is Group COS, and the content provider is SVP Group HR. The controlled document version is maintained on eNet under the Group Operating System.

The primary purpose of this procedure is to ensure the effective management of travelling costs and to ensure employees travel in a safe manner. It applies to all Operating Businesses and Corporate Staff Departments, as well as to joint ventures, partnerships, and companies where the Group can enforce its application.

The procedure is structured into several key sections:
*   **Section 1: Procedure Purpose** (Page 2) - Details the objectives of the travel policy.
*   **Section 2: Procedure Application** (Page 2) - Defines the scope and applicability of the policy.
*   **Section 3: Responsibilities** (Page 2) - Outlines the roles and duties of various stakeholders:

    *   [[group_travel_roles_responsibilities#employees|Employees]] (Page 2)
    *   [[group_travel_roles_responsibilities#group_human_resources_hr|Group Human Resources (HR)]] (Page 3)
    *   [[group_travel_roles_responsibilities#business_area_and_company_hr|Business Area and Company HR]] (Page 3)
    *   [[group_travel_roles_responsibilities#the_group_travel_network|The Group Travel Network]] (Page 3)
    *   [[group_travel_roles_responsibilities#travel_agencies|Travel Agencies]] (Page 3)
*   **Section 4: Procedure Description** (Page 3) - Provides detailed guidelines for different aspects of travel:
    *   [[group_travel_policy_principles_scope#general_travel_principles|General]] (Page 3)
    *   [[air_travel_policy|Air Travel]] (Page 4)
    *   [[group_and_meetings_travel|Group and Meetings Travel]] (Page 4)
    *   [[rail_travel_policy|Rail Travel]] (Page 4)
    *   [[hotel_accommodation_policy|Hotels]] (Page 4)

*   **Section 5: Statutory Requirements** (Page 6) - Addresses compliance with local laws.

This procedure is a key component of the Aker Kvaerner Group's internal operating system, aiming for consistent and high-level operations across all entities.
