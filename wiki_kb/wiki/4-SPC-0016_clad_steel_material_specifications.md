# Clad Steel Material Specifications for Welded Fabrications

The selection and specification of materials for austenitic stainless clad steel welded fabrications are critical to ensuring the integrity and performance of equipment supplied to Aker Kvaerner Chemetics. All materials **must** be as specified in the purchase order and referenced documents.

**Clad Plate Requirements**
All clad plate **must** be in accordance with SA264 or SA265. The base metal, typically carbon steel, **must** satisfy specification SA516. The alloy cladding **must** have a minimum thickness of 1/8" (3mm).
*   For 300 series stainless steel, the alloy cladding **must** conform to specification SA240.
*   For Alloy 20-Cb3 (UNS N08020), the alloy cladding **must** conform to specification SB463.

**Nozzle and Flange Requirements**
*   Weld overlay **must** be applied to carbon steel flange faces exposed to the process environment.
*   Small diameter nozzle necks **must** be solid seamless stainless steel, Schedule 80 minimum.
*   Small diameter flanges **must** be slip-on or lap joint type.

**Material Inspection and Traceability**
Mill ultrasonic inspection **must** be performed in accordance with [[ASME_bpvc_section_ii_materials]] A578 Level II. Repairs to clad plate **must** be in accordance with supplementary requirement S6. All clad plate **must** be subjected to the shear test outlined in material specification SA264 or SA265.

Nozzle cut-outs **must** be examined by the Fabricator for lack of bond and **must** be retained for customer inspection.

The Fabricator **must** maintain full traceability of materials back to original mill certification for all pressure-containing or major load-bearing elements, or materials exposed to the process environment. The Fabricator’s internal controls **must**, as a minimum, prevent the use of unspecified materials, including weld consumables. Lack of adequate identification or certification **will** be cause for rejection of the specific component or item.

Where load-bearing or stressed attachments are to be welded directly to the cladding, the Fabricator **will** ultrasonically check the area concerned for lack of bond prior to welding. The Supplier **must** make available to the purchaser's inspector copies of chemical certification for plate material and other relevant documentation. This ensures compliance with [[material_selection]] guidelines.

**Cladding Methods and Component Specifications**
Roll bonded or explosion clad plate **will** be acceptable cladding methods.
Weld overlay **must** be applied to carbon steel flange faces exposed to the process environment. Small diameter nozzle necks **must** be solid seamless stainless steel Sch. 80 min. Small diameter flanges **must** be slip-on or lap joint type.

**Material Testing and Repairs**
Mill ultrasonic inspection **must** be performed in accordance with ASME A578 Level II. Repairs to clad plate **must** be in accordance with supplementary requirement S6. All clad plate **must** be subjected to the shear test outlined in material specification SA264 or SA265.
