# Rupture Disc Installation

Rupture disc installation involves correctly integrating a rupture disc into a piping system or pressure vessel as a passive overpressure protection device. Proper installation is critical for safety, ensuring the disc bursts at its specified pressure to relieve excess pressure. Key aspects include installing the disc with the correct flow direction, verifying the vendor tag details (model, burst pressure, material), and ensuring accessibility for [[rupture_disc_inspection_5_prc_0007|maintenance and replacement]].
