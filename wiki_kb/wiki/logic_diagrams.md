# Logic Diagrams (8-SCM-XXXX)

**Logic Diagrams** (8-SCM-XXXX) are intended to provide a detailed expansion of the [[process_logic_narrative]] into technically specific language details required by the control system used.

**Document ID:** 8-SCM-XXXX
**Usage:** Design

**Purpose and Status:**
Logic drawings are based on the [[process_logic_narrative]] and are used in the specific configuration of the control system. They represent the Logic Narrative in another form.

**Note on Redundancy and Future Production:**
Logic drawings are considered redundant due to the trend in control systems where the software configuration (Operating System) directly produces the control configuration documentation package. Consequently, logic drawings **WILL NO LONGER BE PRODUCED** within the scope of projects. However, they **MAY BE PRODUCED** if precedent dictates deliverables for a specific project.

**Content Requirements:**
Logic Diagrams **MUST** contain a detailed expansion of the [[process_logic_narrative]] into technically specific language details required by the control system used.
