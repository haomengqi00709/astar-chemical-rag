# Duplex Stainless Steel Inspection and Testing (4-SPC-0027)

Comprehensive inspection and testing are integral to ensuring the quality and integrity of duplex stainless steel (Alloy 2205) fabrications. Specification 4-SPC-0027 outlines the following mandatory requirements for non-destructive examination (NDE) and material testing:

*   **Liquid Penetrant Examination (LPE):** All welds **must** undergo 100% Liquid Penetrant Examination (LPE) in accordance with the requirements of [[ASME_section_v_non_destructive_examination]] and [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels]]. This ensures the detection of surface-breaking discontinuities in the weldments.
*   **Radiographic Testing (RT):** All pressure-retaining welds will be subject to a minimum of spot radiography, as specified by [[ASME_section_v_non_destructive_examination]] and [[ASME_section_viii_div_1_pressure_vessels]]. This method is crucial for detecting internal flaws such as porosity, slag inclusions, and cracks within the weld metal.
*   **Magnetic Ferrite Testing:** Magnetic ferrite testing **must** be performed on a representative sample of pressure-retaining welds. The weld ferrite content **must** fall within the acceptable range of 30% to 60%. This range is critical for ensuring the optimal balance of austenite and ferrite phases in the duplex microstructure, which directly impacts the material's corrosion resistance and mechanical properties. The exact number and specific locations for these magnetic ferrite tests are to be mutually agreed upon with the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics inspector prior to execution.
*   **Welded Test Coupons:** The fabricator **must** provide Aker Kvaerner Chemetics with welded test coupons during the fabrication process. These coupons **must** be produced in a manner identical to the primary pressure envelope welds of the actual equipment, replicating the welding procedures, consumables, and heat input conditions. The use of run-out tabs for these coupons is preferred to ensure representative weld quality.
    *   **Purpose of Coupons:** These weld coupons will be utilized for subsequent corrosion and metallurgical evaluations, providing a representative sample of the actual weld quality and material response under the applied welding conditions.
    *   **Agreement on Coupons:** The specific type and number of weld coupons required are to be agreed upon with the Aker Kvaerner Chemetics inspector.

These rigorous inspection and testing protocols are designed to verify that all duplex stainless steel fabrications meet the specified quality standards, ensuring their reliability and longevity in service. For detailed information on welding procedures and consumables, refer to [[4-SPC-0027_duplex_stainless_steel_welding_procedures_and_consumables]].
