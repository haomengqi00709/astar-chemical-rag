# Conveyors & Mills Non-Destructive Examination Requirements

This page details the mandatory Non-Destructive Examination (NDE) requirements for Conveyors & Mills, as specified in Quality Data Sheet 0-QDS-7426. These examinations are crucial for verifying the structural integrity of components without causing damage.

**General NDE Standard**
Non Destructive Examination **shall** be in accordance with ANSI/AWS Structural Welding Code D1.1 or an approved equivalent standard. The vendor **shall** ensure that all NDE procedures and personnel comply with this standard.

**Scope of NDE**
The scope of vendor quality assurance for Conveyors & Mills includes:
*   [[non_destructive_examination_requirements|Non-Destructive Testing]]
*   Dye Penetrant testing

These methods are applied to critical components and welds to detect surface and subsurface discontinuities.

For a broader overview of NDE requirements, refer to [[0-QDS-7411_non_destructive_examination_requirements]].
