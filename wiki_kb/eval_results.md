### [+] Q02 — What are the publishing stages for Process Flow Diagrams (PFDs)?
**Difficulty:** easy | **Source:** 1-PRC-0002 | **Verdict:** HIT (100%)

**Expected:** Stage 1: Issued for Internal Design. Stage 2: Issued for Hazop. Stage 3: Approved for Operating Manual.

**Answer:**
> The publishing stages for Process Flow Diagrams (PFDs) involve three distinct stages, each with specific content, production, checking, and approval responsibilities, as described in the "pfd_development_process" wiki page:
> 
> 1.  **Stage 1 PFD (Issued for Internal Design):**
>     *   **Content:** Incorporates Stream Data and PFD Graphics.
>     *   **Production:** Produced by Design, by merging the Stream Data File with the PFD Graphics.
>     *   **Checking:** Checked by Process.
>     *   **Approval:** Approved by the Process Approver.
> 
> 2.  **Stage 2 PFD (Issued for Hazop):**
>     *   **Content:** Incorporates the Stage 1 PFD, along with vendor information and confirmed utility requirements.
>     *   **Production:** Produced by Design.
>     *   **Checking:** Checked by Process.
>     *   **Approval:** Approved by the Process Approver.
> 
> 3.  **Stage 3 PFD (Approved for Operating Manual):**
>     *   **Content:** Incorporates the Stage 2 PFD, along with final vendor and HAZOP (Hazard and Operability Study) information.
>     *   **Production:** Produced by Design.
>     *   **Checking:** Checked by Process.
>     *   **Approval:** Approved by the Process Approver.
> 
> The overall PFD checking, approval, and publishing process must be in accordance with "0-PRC-7004: Checking and Approval of Design" (Source: pfd_development_process).
